> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Roadmap to the Future is a community planning initiative whose team develops shared principles, priorities, needs assessments, and strategic actions for youth, families, and communities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ground community needs assessment and planning in guiding principles of youth and family voice, access and equity, and alignment and coherence**: The Roadmap to the Future initiative developed three guiding principles—Youth and Family Voice, Access and Equity, and Alignment and Coherence—alongside four shared priorities, during Phase 0 convenings. The report states these "informed the Roadmap to the Future team's work to develop the needs assessment" and will continue to guide the long-term strategic plan. Recommendations and strategic actions were filtered through these principles, and the shared priorities span quality childcare and TK–12, mental health and wellness, welcoming beacons of community life, and thriving in young adulthood. # Ground community needs assessment and planning in guiding principles of youth and family voice, access and equity, and alignment and coherence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The Roadmap to the Future initiative developed three guiding principles—Youth and Family Voice, Access and Equity, and Alignment and Coherence—alongside four shared priorities, during Phase 0 convenings. The report states these "informed the Roadmap to the Future team's work to develop the needs assessment" and will continue to guide the long-term strategic plan. Recommendations and strategic actions were filtered through these principles, and the shared priorities span quality childcare and TK–12, mental health and wellness, welcoming beacons of community life, and thriving in young adulthood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Principles developed collaboratively with elected officials and community leaders before the assessment, and applied as filters for recommendations
#### Constraints
- Principles are specific to the Roadmap to the Future initiative's vision and phases in Yolo County

### Target Learners
- Children, youth, and families in Yolo County, including youth in foster care, experiencing homelessness, and those who are socioeconomically disadvantaged

### Learning Goals
- Healthy development and educational advantage for every child and youth in the county
