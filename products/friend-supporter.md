# Friend Supporter

## Description
A five-week web-based experiential learning programme developed by Tsuchiya Miyako et al. for adults without cancer experience to practise empathetic listening and supportive responses to cancer disclosure through narratives, role-plays, worksheets, and psychologist feedback.
