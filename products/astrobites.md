## Description
Astrobites is a not-for-profit website run by graduate students in astrophysical sciences that publishes accessible summaries of current astrophysics research for undergraduates.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Science Bites network: 17 graduate-student-led summary sites across 15 scientific topics and multiple languages**: Since 2010 the Astrobites model has expanded into a network of "Science Bites" websites led by graduate students and postdocs in different fields and languages. "The Science Bites family encompasses 17 sites and covers 15 different scientific topics", including Softbites, particlebites, PERbites, Oceanbites, and Oncobites, plus sites in Spanish (astrobitos), Portuguese (astropontos), and Persian/Farsi (staryab). The authors argue this growth demonstrates the model filled a gap in how researchers communicate with broader communities. (Astrobites as a Community-led Model for Education, Science Communication, and Accessibilit)
- **Astrobites and AAS Nova paper-summary websites as entry points to the literature**: Astrobites is a daily-updated website hosting graduate-student-written summaries of astronomy papers and their results, offering short, focused reviews from a student perspective; AAS Nova is a similar site hosted by the American Astronomical Society that often reposts Astrobites articles of noteworthy papers. The guide positions both as starting locations for undergraduates and graduates who lack the time or comfort to read papers in full, and notes Astrobites accepts guest authors seeking writing experience. (Cooke et al. (2020))
