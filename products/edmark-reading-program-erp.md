# Edmark Reading Program (ERP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A whole-word beginning-reading curriculum developed for emergent readers and students with disabilities, using errorless learning, sight-word instruction, matching, writing, games, and story reading.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-paced levelled sight-word lesson sequence with errorless word recognition and phrase matching**: The ERP's instructional pattern moves students through levelled lessons at their own pace: each word-recognition lesson has the student hear, see, point to, and read a new word, first choosing the target word from a row of similar-looking words, then from very similar-looking words, followed by a phrase-match activity pairing phrase cards with pictures. Because "the ERP allows students to move at their own pace, students were often at different levels at the same time." Level progression appeared to drive motivation and engagement in this study. (Meeks et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [The Edmark Reading Program produced significantly larger gains in estimated oral reading fluency than regular classroom reading instruction in third-grade students with disabilities](../claims/edmark-program-increased-orf-gains-third-grade-disabilities.md) [+M]
- [Students in the Edmark Reading Group showed more positive attitudes toward reading than students in the regular reading instruction group](../claims/edmark-group-more-positive-reading-attitudes.md) [+M]
- [Students in the Edmark Reading Group were more engaged in reading instruction than students in the regular reading instruction group](../claims/edmark-group-more-engaged-reading-instruction.md) [+M]
