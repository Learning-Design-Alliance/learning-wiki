# Digital Virtual Reality Simulator (DVRS)

## Description
A multifunctional virtual-reality platform developed for dental surgical skills training, comprising a PC-based oral teaching platform and a force-feedback oral surgery simulator.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Digital Virtual Reality Simulator (DVRS) for Dental Surgical Skills Training**: The DVRS is China's first multifunctional VR platform for dental surgical skill training, comprising the Oral Virtual PC-based Teaching Platform (Unidraw Stomatology Digital Virtual Training System) and the Oral Surgery Simulator (Unidraw Virtual Classic Edition). It builds "a complete 3D patient oral model", lets students practice the full diagnosis-and-treatment process online or with a simulator using "dual-hand force feedback technology", and its evaluation system "provides real-time scoring and generates feedback of each student's operation, supporting replay for review". In the study it replaced manikin-based preclinical training for the 2018 cohort. (Ren Jian-gang et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Students trained with the DVRS scored significantly higher in the dental surgery course than students trained with traditional manikins](../claims/dvrs-higher-dental-surgery-scores-than-manikin.md) [+M]
