# Approaches to Teaching Inventory-Revised (ATI-R)

## Description
A course-specific 22-item self-report inventory developed by Trigwell and Prosser for measuring academics' teacher/content-focused and student/learning-focused approaches to teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Approaches to teaching inventory framework: teacher/content versus student/learning approaches (ATI-R)**: The article organizes teaching approaches using Trigwell and Prosser's distinction between teacher/content-oriented and student/learning-oriented approaches, measured with the Approaches to Teaching Inventory-Revised (ATI-R). Teacher/content-oriented academics "concentrate on what they 'do' in teaching to transmit particular information," viewing students as passive recipients; student/learning-oriented academics concentrate on facilitating understanding and conceptual change, utilizing students' experiences. The ATI-R contains 11 ITTF items and 11 CCSF items rated on a 5-point scale, and the study uses it to predict email advice-giving behavior. (Lena Päuler-Kuppinger et al. (2018))
- **Approaches to Teaching Inventory—Revised (ATI-R) two-scale framework for describing teaching approaches**: The ATI-R is a 22-item inventory with two 11-item scales measuring approaches to teaching: the Information Transfer/Teacher-focused (ITTF) scale and the Conceptual Change/Student-focused (CCSF) scale, each scored as the mean of 5-point item responses. The ITTF approach focuses on "transmitting information to their students based on their own knowledge" with little interaction, while the CCSF approach aims to change students' conceptions and encourages students to construct their own knowledge. This study used the ATI-R to assess genetic counselors' classroom teaching approaches and to test whether approaches relate to teaching experience and student populations taught. (Doxey et al. (2026))
- **HowUTeach survey instrument measuring approaches to teaching in higher education**: HowUTeach is a revised and further-developed version of the Approaches to Teaching Inventory, measuring approaches to teaching through 12 items and four dimensions: interactive, transmissive, unreflective, and organized approach. In this study it is supplemented by a new 4-item constructive-approach scale developed from the HowULearn student instrument and modified to measure teachers' strategies to support students' deep approach to learning. The full survey in this protocol contains 78 items from 11 scales answered on a 5-point agreement scale, administered three times across the study. (Schwartz A et al. (2025))
- **Approaches to Teaching Inventory framework: teacher-focused (ITTF) versus student-focused (CCSF) approaches and five qualitatively different approaches**: The study uses Prosser and Trigwell's classification in which teaching approaches divide into two main types, the "Information Transmission/Teacher-Focused (ITTF) approach" and the "Conceptual Change/Student-Focused (CCSF) approach", with five qualitatively different approaches (A–E) ranging from teacher-focused information transmission to student-focused conceptual change. The ATI operationalises this as a 22-item, two-scale instrument rated on a 5-point Likert scale from "only rarely" to "almost always". In this study it measured the extent to which 93 Karachi medical and dental educators adopt teacher-centred or student-centred strategies. (Sukkurwalla et al. (2024))
