Dataset of 69 Qassim University PBL course offerings with coded forum interactions¶

Description¶

A dataset compiled from 69 Qassim University problem-based learning course offerings, containing coded Moodle forum interactions, student participation, and course-assessment information for studying online collaborative learning.

Claims¶

  • Multigraph indegree centrality is consistently and positively correlated with student performance across 12 PBL courses, with stronger correlations than other configurations in eight courses [+W]
  • Explicit group regulation in online PBL was infrequent, emerged in response to challenges, and often involved teacher support [+W]
