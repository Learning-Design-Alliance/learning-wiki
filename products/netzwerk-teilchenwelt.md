## Description
A German network of particle- and astroparticle-physics institutes, scientists, schools, laboratories, and museums that provides students and teachers with authentic research experiences and training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Netzwerk Teilchenwelt network enabling authentic research experiences in particle and astroparticle physics**: Netzwerk Teilchenwelt is a network of 24 German research institutes of astroparticle and particle physics whose goal is to enable students to authentically experience modern physics research. More than 100 young scientists provide students and teachers with insights into their research at workshops in schools, student laboratories, or museums all over Germany. Students analyse real LHC data in a masterclass and perform their own measurements in cosmic particle projects, with discussion with scientists encouraged. The CosMO detectors are deployed within this network, and teachers receive training to incorporate the experiment into their classes. (R. Franke et al. (2013))
