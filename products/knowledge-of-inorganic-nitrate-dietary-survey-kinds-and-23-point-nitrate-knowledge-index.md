## Description
A custom online questionnaire and evidence-based scoring index developed by Shannon Oliver and colleagues to assess nutrition professionals’ knowledge and beliefs about dietary inorganic nitrate.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KINDS online questionnaire and 23-point Nitrate Knowledge Index for assessing nitrate knowledge and beliefs**: The Knowledge of Inorganic Nitrate Dietary Survey (KINDS) is a custom online questionnaire developed to evaluate knowledge of and beliefs about dietary inorganic nitrate among nutrition professionals. It contains 12 key questions covering five areas: health and performance effects, current and recommended intake values, dietary sources, methods of evaluating intake, and nitrate metabolism, plus demographic items. The article reports that "we derived a 23-point index of nitrate knowledge", awarding one point for correct responses on questions with clear evidence-based answers and excluding belief questions, informed by recent systematic reviews and meta-analyses. The authors state it may be used in future investigations in other professional groups or countries. (Shannon Oliver M et al. (2019))
