## Description
The Ex-Lap App is a smartphone-based learning and management application developed with BoYi Times Medical Technology for the STALS laparoscopic training course.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ex-Lap smartphone application with learning and management terminals**: The Ex-Lap App is the online component of the STALS course, developed with BoYi Times Medical Technology. The article states "This app is divided into a learning terminal and a management terminal", with the learning terminal offering a Course Center and Appointment Center that provide theory explanations and demonstrations of operational techniques, skills, common mistakes, and solutions. Theoretical tests are graded automatically through the App, and trainees use it to preview standardized operations during fragmented time. (Liang Yao et al. (2024))
