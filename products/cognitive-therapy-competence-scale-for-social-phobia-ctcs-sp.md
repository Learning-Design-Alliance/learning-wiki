# Cognitive Therapy Competence Scale for Social Phobia (CTCS-SP)

## Description
The CTCS-SP is a 16-item, seven-point observational rating scale developed from the Cognitive Therapy Scale to assess psychotherapists’ competence in delivering cognitive therapy for social phobia.
