# Parental Engagement of Families from Latino Backgrounds / Participación Educativa de Familias Latinas (PEFL)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 65-item Spanish- or English-language parent-report assessment developed by McWayne et al. to measure culturally contextualized home- and school-based engagement among low-income Latino families of Head Start preschool children.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEFL parent-report measure of Latino family engagement with four dimensions**: The Parental Engagement of Families from Latino Backgrounds / Participación Educativa de Familias Latinas is a 65-item parent-report measure developed from qualitative focus-group data with low-income Latino Head Start families. "The first three factors described below represent home-based engagement and are believed to reflect cultural values and socio-economic realities of low-income, Latino families living in the United States": Foundational Education (20 items, α = 0.73), Supplemental Education (12 items, α = 0.77), and Future-oriented Teaching (3 items, α = 0.63), plus School Participation (8 items, α = 0.77). Items are rated on a four-point Likert-type scale from Never to Frequently. (McWayne et al. (2016))
