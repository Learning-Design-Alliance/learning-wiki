# Engagement Classroom Model (ECM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A classroom implementation framework described by McWilliam et al. that combines routines-based goals, incidental teaching, integrated therapy, zone defense scheduling, and Reggio Emilia-inspired environments to promote engagement among preschool children with disabilities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Engagement Classroom Model with five components: RBI goals, incidental teaching, integrated therapy, zone defense schedule, and Reggio Emilia concepts**: The Engagement Classroom Model (ECM) is the RBM's set of procedures for running classrooms to promote child engagement. Implementers focus on five components: conducting an RBI to establish functional routines-based goals; "Incidental teaching to address all goals in all routines, by following the child's lead"; integrated therapy, meaning specialists work with teachers in the classroom and never pull the child out; the zone defense schedule to organize adults and decrease non-engagement time during transitions; and Reggio Emilia concepts to make the environment provocative and beautiful. (McWilliam R. A. et al. (2020))
