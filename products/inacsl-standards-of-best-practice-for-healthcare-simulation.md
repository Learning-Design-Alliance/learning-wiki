# INACSL Standards of Best Practice for healthcare simulation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A standards framework developed by the International Nursing Association for Clinical Simulation and Learning (INACSL) to guide the design, facilitation, evaluation, and psychologically safe implementation of healthcare simulations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **INACSL Standards of Best Practice as the theoretical-methodological framework for designing biosafety simulation**: The study is "a descriptive qualitative study based on the International Nursing Association for Clinical Simulation and Learning (INACSL) theoretical-methodological framework(14), which provides best practice standards for the development of clinical simulation." The framework structured the simulation into planning, learning objectives, expected conduct, briefing, scenario, debriefing, and assessment stages. It guided a scenario replicating a two-bed hospital ward with trained actors and a venipuncture arm simulator. (Canever Bruna Pedroso; de Oliveira Saionara Nunes; Peiter Caroline Cechinel; Suplici Samara Eliane Rabelo; Gomes Diana Coelho; Bellaguarda Maria Lígia dos Reis; da Costa Diovane Ghignatti; Magalhães Aline Lima Pestana; de Oliveira Fernanda Marques (2026))
