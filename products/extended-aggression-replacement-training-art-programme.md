# Extended Aggression Replacement Training (ART) programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A manualized group intervention for children and adolescents with behavioural problems that combines social-skills training, anger-control training, and moral-reasoning or character-education training, delivered by trained ART facilitators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Extended Aggression Replacement Training (ART) programme for children**: ART is a multi-focused intervention consisting of "three equal components: social skills t raining, anger control training and moral reasoning training", each taught weekly over a 10-week period. The version evaluated here was an extended version incorporating character education in the moral reasoning component, setting events in anger control training, and rehearsals with pedagogical techniques from Gundersen and Moynahan. It makes considerable use of role-playing and exercises and includes transfer and maintenance strategies. (Koposov et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [The 30-hour ART programme is associated with a significant increase in children's self-reported overall social competence, with no change in the control group](../claims/art-self-report-social-competence-increase.md) [+M]
- [ART effects on self-reported social competence are moderated by age, with reliable effects in the 6-9 and 10-14 groups but not among children aged 15 and older](../claims/art-age-moderator-younger-children.md) [+M]
