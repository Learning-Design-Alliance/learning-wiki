## Description
A web-based infection-control training platform developed and reviewed by hospital infection-control staff for nursing students beginning clinical internships.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Infection Control Workspace web-based learning platform**: A web-based learning platform developed by hospital infection control staff and implemented as the first phase of the experimental group's training. Its content was similar to that of traditional lectures, covering laws and regulations on work-related biological exposure, clinical situations and associated risks, and prevention and control strategies. It served as the standardized content-delivery component before peer-facilitated discussion sessions. (Chen Lulin et al. (2026))
