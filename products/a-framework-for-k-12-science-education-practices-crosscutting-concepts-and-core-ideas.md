## Description
A National Research Council framework that defines science and engineering practices, crosscutting concepts, and disciplinary core ideas to guide K–12 science standards, curriculum, instruction, and assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **K12 Science Education Framework practices as an organizing frame for becoming like physicists with Tracker**: The article adopts the K12 Science Education Framework (Quinn, Schweingruber & Keller, 2012) as a comprehensive tool to promote and guide students as scientists, situating Tracker use within three practices: Practice 1 asking questions, Practice 2 using models, and Practice 5 mathematical and computational thinking. The author reflects on how "these 3 practices below (IV.A, IV.B, and IV.C) can be used to further deepen becoming like physicists" in the Grade 9 performance task. (Loo Kang WEE (2014))
