## Description
The Nurse Competence Scale (NCS) is a validated 73-item visual-analogue self-assessment instrument developed for measuring nurses’ perceived competence across seven professional domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nurse Competence Scale (NCS) as a longitudinal self-assessment tool for newly graduated nurses' transition**: The Nurse Competence Scale is a validated instrument with "73 items divided into seven dimensions": helping role, teaching–coaching, diagnostic functions, managing situations, therapeutic interventions, quality assurance, and working role. It measures perceived competence on a visual analogue scale from 0 to 100, with scores below 60 indicating insufficient competence. In this study it was administered at four time points over 5 months with high internal consistency (Cronbach's α 0.983–0.992). (Mesaglio et al. (2026))
