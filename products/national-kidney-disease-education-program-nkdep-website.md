# National Kidney Disease Education Program (NKDEP) website

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based patient education resource sponsored by the National Kidney Disease Education Program that provides elementary-reading-level kidney disease information for patients and provider–patient interactions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NKDEP website: web-based kidney disease education for patients at elementary reading level**: The National Kidney Disease Education Program (NKDEP) sponsors kidney disease education via digital media; its website contains links to educational topics targeted at patients rather than providers. Materials are available for download and print as educational supplements during provider-patient interactions that patients can take home. The review notes the content "is directed at an elementary school level reading capability, and has been modified based on an iterative process of review". (Diamantidis Clarissa J (2014))
