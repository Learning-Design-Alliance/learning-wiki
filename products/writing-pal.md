## Description
Writing Pal is an intelligent tutoring system developed to teach persuasive-writing strategies through explicit instruction, game-based practice, and automated writing evaluation for college-aged learners and students preparing for standardized writing assessments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Writing Pal intelligent tutoring system provides explicit writing strategy instruction with game-based practice and automated writing evaluation**: The Writing Pal is an intelligent tutoring system designed to provide explicit instruction on writing strategies and opportunities to practice writing and receive feedback, specifically targeting persuasive essays similar to standardized test tasks. Instruction includes "a series of nine strategy instruction modules, covering freewriting, planning, introduction building, body building, conclusion building, cohesion building, and revision", delivered via 5-10 minute lesson videos with animated characters and checkpoint quizzes. Students practice through over a dozen game-based activities plus an automated writing evaluation (AWE) component that provides summative and formative feedback on drafted essays. (Weston-Sementelli et al. (2016))
