## Description
SPARK is a family–school–community early-literacy partnership programme that combines in-school tutoring, afterschool enrichment, and family engagement for early-grade students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SPARK family–school–community partnership model for sustained early literacy**: SPARK is a literacy development model combining "in-school tutoring, afterschool enrichment, and family engagement in concert" to build early-grade literacy skills while engaging families to support literacy at home. It was developed in response to mixed evidence that skill-based program impacts fade after students leave a program; it therefore works to build an environment around students conducive to learning to read and maintaining literacy development beyond participation. Literacy deficits are treated as a result of ecological processes (Bronfenbrenner, 1979) across school, family, and community microsystems rather than individual deficits. (Curtis Jones (2018))

### Claims
- [The SPARK program produced a small but statistically significant positive impact on reading achievement of 0.12 standard deviations in a two-year RCT](../claims/spark-rct-reading-impact-012-sd.md) [+M]
