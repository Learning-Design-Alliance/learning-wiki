## Description
A seven-category, 31-indicator rating instrument developed by Kulgemeyer and Peters and applied by Bitzenbauer et al. to assess the richness and variety of explanatory videos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Category-based measure of explaining quality (category points, CP) for explanatory videos**: A process-oriented, category-based rating instrument adopted from Kulgemeyer and Peters (2016) and based on the communication model for explaining physics. "The category system to evaluate explanatory videos' explaining quality (cf. appendix) consists of seven main categories (content, structure, use of language, contexts and examples, mathematics, interrogation, non-verbal elements) comprising a total of 31 different categories." Each assigned category scores one point, four negative subcategories score -1, and the sum yields category points (upper limit 28 CP) as the video's explaining-quality score. In this study two independent raters coded the videos with substantial inter-rater reliability (κ = 0.79). (Bitzenbauer et al. (2022))
