Early Warning Intervention and Monitoring System (EWIMS)¶

Description¶

A school-based programme developed for schools to use staff teams, student indicators, targeted interventions, and progress monitoring to support students at risk of not graduating on time.

Components¶

  • Early Warning Intervention and Monitoring System (EWIMS) seven-step process and early warning data tool: EWIMS is a systematic approach to identifying students at risk of not graduating on time, assigning them to interventions, and monitoring their progress. Schools implement a seven-step process—establishing a team, using the early warning data tool, reviewing and interpreting data, assigning and providing interventions, monitoring students, and evaluating and refining the process—supported by an early warning data tool that "automatically flags students as at risk using thresholds based on prior research". The tool's indicators include chronic absence, course failure, low GPA, behavior, and an off-track flag. EWIMS does not prescribe specific interventions; schools inventory available interventions and match them to flagged students' needs. (Faria et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • EWIMS reduced the percentage of students who were chronically absent after one year of implementation [+M]
  • EWIMS reduced the percentage of students who failed one or more courses after one year [+M]
