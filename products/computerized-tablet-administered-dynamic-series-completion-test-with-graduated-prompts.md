# Computerized tablet-administered dynamic series completion test with graduated prompts

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A tablet-based visual-spatial series-completion assessment developed by Touw et al. for individually testing inductive reasoning in children aged 6–8, with automated scoring, feedback, and graduated prompts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Computerized tablet-based dynamic test of series completion with animated agent Lisa and automated scoring**: A newly constructed computerized test measuring inductive reasoning via geometric series-completion items on an Acer Aspire Switch 10 tablet. An animated figure, named Lisa, "appeared on the left side of the screen and gave the children verbal instructions", children dragged figures into an empty seventh box, and the tablet provided visual enlargements and auditory feedback (a high 'pling' for correct answers). The tablet "automatically scored children's performance during the pre-test, training, and post-test by producing log files". The test used 18 items per session with five possible transformations (shape, colour, size, quantity, position). (Touw et al. (2020))
