## Description
Google Forms is Google's web-based form and survey application, used here to collect student answers, score responses automatically, and provide instructor-entered explanations for review.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Google Forms for real-time scoring of student answers**: Google Forms is a free tool the authors use to collect students' answers in class. Its stated advantage is that "students’ answers are automatically scored and counted in real time," saving instructor time, while learners can "immediately check whether the answer is correct" and review questions with pre-entered instructor explanations asynchronously at their own pace. The article presents it as a low-barrier assessment tool requiring no special cost. (Park Jung-Chul et al. (2021))
