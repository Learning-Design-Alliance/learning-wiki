Schema-based instruction (SBI) for proportional reasoning¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 21-lesson mathematics intervention curriculum developed by Jitendra and colleagues that teaches proportional problem structures, schematic representations, flexible procedures, and metacognitive problem-solving strategies to middle-school students.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • SBI significantly improves seventh-grade students' proportional problem solving at immediate posttest and at 11-week delayed posttest relative to business-as-usual instruction [+M]
  • For students with mathematics difficulties, SBI significantly improves proportional problem solving at immediate and 11-week delayed posttests but not general mathematical problem solving [+M]
