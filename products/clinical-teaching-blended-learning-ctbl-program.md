# Clinical Teaching Blended Learning (CTBL) program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A nurse-educator professional-development programme developed by Wu XV et al. that combines an eight-module web-based clinical pedagogy course with a face-to-face case-based learning workshop for nurse preceptors.

### Claims
- [Nurse preceptors' self-rated clinical teaching competency scores, in total and in all four CTCI subdomains, were higher after the CTBL program than before it, in a single-group study](../claims/ctbl-increased-clinical-teaching-competency.md) [+M]
- [Nurse preceptors' self-rated self-efficacy scores in clinical teaching and assessment rose significantly from pre-test to post-test after the CTBL program, in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/ctbl-increased-preceptor-self-efficacy.md) [+M]

- [The CTBL program significantly improved blended learning outcomes on the LEQ, except the workload subdomain, which showed no significant change](../claims/ctbl-improved-blended-learning-outcomes.md) [+M]
