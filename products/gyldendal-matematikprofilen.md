# Gyldendal: Matematikprofilen

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Gyldendal: Matematikprofilen is a standardized, Rasch-validated mathematics skills assessment for Danish primary-school students, developed and published by Gyldendal.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Matematikprofilen standardized mathematics test with Rasch-validated psychometric properties**: The study measured students' mathematical skills with a standardized test (Gyldendal: Matematikprofilen) covering "number and algebra; geometry and measurement; statistics and probability". The test "is validated using psychometric analysis" with a Rasch analysis checking local independence, targeting, midpoint, location, item fit, and ordered response categories. It provides an overall score classifying students into five categories from "started" to "much longer than expected", with 54 criterion-referenced questions in three parts. (Kaup et al. (2023))
