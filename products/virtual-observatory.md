# Virtual Observatory

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An international interoperable infrastructure coordinated by the International Virtual Observatory Alliance that organizes astronomical archives and provides tools for researchers, educators, students, amateurs, and the public to access and analyze real astronomical data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Virtual Observatory as integrated, interoperating access infrastructure for astronomical archives**: The Virtual Observatory is an international infrastructure that collects and organizes astronomical data of many kinds and provides tools to access it. The article states: "The Virtual Observatory (VO) is an international attempt to collect astronomical data (images, simulation, mission-logs, etc), organize it and develop tools that let astronomers access this huge amount of information." The International Virtual Observatory Alliance, established in 2002, coordinates this work, and the article frames the VO as valuable not only for researchers but also for education and public outreach. (Freistetter et al. (2011))
