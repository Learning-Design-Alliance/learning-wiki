# Squid, handyCalc, and ChampCalc

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A trio of Android smartphone applications from their respective developers that support handwritten note-taking and scientific calculation for coursework.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Free smartphone apps for exact-science coursework: Squid, handyCalc, and ChampCalc**: The article identifies free smartphone applications meeting two selection criteria: usable during the course and free of cost to the student. Squid is a note-taking app whose functions include "Toma apuntes naturalmente con el lápiz y borra con el dedo" and exporting to PDF, PNG or JPEG. handyCalc is suggested for students who have difficulty entering information into traditional calculators or visualizing the algorithm for an operation. ChampCalc resembles traditional scientific calculators but, as an app, offers more problem-solving power; combined with handyCalc it forms a learning tool going beyond either alone. The recommended device runs Android with at least 32 gigabytes. (Navarro del Toro (2021))
