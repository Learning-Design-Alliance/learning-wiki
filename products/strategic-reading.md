## Description
In Strategic Reading, students work in small, interdependent cooperative learning groups. Activities include paired reading for fluency and comprehension, practice with new vocabulary related to novels and plays, and structured partner discussion guides that provide background, vocabulary, and comprehension questions to help students identify characters, plots, and informational content. Students also get time for self-selected reading and writing activities.
