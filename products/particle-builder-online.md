## Description
Particle Builder Online is a Unity-based web card game developed by M. Attar and colleagues in which high-school students learn Standard Model particle-physics concepts through tutorial play, AI opponents, and peer competition.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Particle Builder online browser-based version with an AI opponent**: A browser-based adaptation of the board game, developed by students at the Australian National University, which maintains the structure of the original game and provides a tutorial to help students learn to play. Students play "against a basic Artificial Intelligence (AI)". The authors note it is "significantly less effort for teachers" because the cards and game mats need not be printed and cut out. (Lachlan McGinness et al. (2025))
- **Seven-level progression mapping gameplay rules onto Standard Model concepts**: Particle Builder organises gameplay into seven levels with different rules, from level one, the simplest, to level seven, which contains the most analogies to particle physics concepts. The higher levels introduce "the fundamental forces and exchange particles (level 2), color charge (level 3), randomness and probability (level 4)", followed by W transformations, neutrino oscillations and spontaneous decay. Baryons, mesons and systems such as positronium and hydrogen serve as build targets. (Lachlan McGinness et al. (2025))
