## Description
A machine-scorable situational judgement test developed for medical school admissions that assesses prospective applicants' integrity through ten critical-incident scenarios and 50 rated response items.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrity-based situational judgement test for medical school admissions (10 scenarios, 50 items)**: A machine-scorable SJT designed to measure the construct of integrity in medical school selection, consisting of "50 scorable items pertaining to ten scenarios involving medical student integrity", elicited via critical incident interviews with 12 faculty members. Candidates rated response options on a four-point likert scale from most to least appropriate, with an even number of points to deter neutral responding. Scenarios were presented in written, video or transcript formats with identical content and scoring, and were keyed using the Chan and Schmitt method against 13 subject matter experts. (Husbands Adrian et al. (2015))
