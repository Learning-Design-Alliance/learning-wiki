# Epistemological Beliefs Assessment for Physical Sciences (EBAPS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EBAPS is a 30-item assessment instrument developed to measure physical-science students' epistemological beliefs across five non-orthogonal dimensions using prescribed weighted axis scores.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EBAPS five-dimension framework of epistemological beliefs in physical sciences**: The Epistemological Beliefs Assessment for Physical Sciences is a 30-item questionnaire of Likert-style and multiple-choice items, each scored from 0 (least sophisticated) to 4 (most sophisticated). "The questionnaire probes students' beliefs across the following five non-orthogonal dimensions": structure of scientific knowledge (10 items), nature of knowing and learning (8), real-life applicability (4), evolving knowledge (3), and source of ability to learn (5). The study uses weighted percentage scores per axis to compare belief sophistication across chatbot preference groups. (Amogh Sirnoorkar et al. (2026))
