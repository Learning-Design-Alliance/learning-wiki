# SPLIT (Six Progressive Levels in Testing) instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 36-item multiple-choice assessment developed by Buljan et al. to measure evidence-based medicine knowledge across six cognitive levels and six EBM topics in undergraduate medical students and EBM experts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SPLIT instrument: a 36-item multiple-choice test assessing EBM knowledge across six cognitive levels and six EBM topics**: The SPLIT (Six Progressive Levels in Testing) instrument consists of 36 multiple-choice items measuring EBM knowledge at six cognitive levels (Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analysing, Evaluating, Creating) across six EBM topics (Evidence-based practice, Internal validity, Clinical importance, Study design, Sources of evidence, Diagnostic studies). Each cognitive level had six questions, one per topic, except the Creating level, whose questions spanned multiple topics. The test was developed per Anderson et al.'s revised Bloom taxonomy, randomized for item order, and piloted with second-year students before administration to students and EBM experts. (Buljan Ivan et al. (2021))
