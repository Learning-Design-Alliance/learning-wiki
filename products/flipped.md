## Description
FlippED is a teacher dashboard developed by Mejia-Domenzain et al. that uses machine-learning analyses of flipped-classroom log data to visualize students' self-regulated-learning profiles and behavioral patterns for instructional monitoring and support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FlippED teacher dashboard for monitoring students' SRL behavior**: FlippED is a teacher dashboard that visualizes ML-based SRL profiles and behaviors from flipped-classroom log data. Its navigation menu has "an overview displaying a Summary and Student Profiles and a detailed view illustrating students' behavioral patterns in the different dimensions", with course and week selection and help buttons. It was iterated with seven pilot teachers and evaluated with ten university teachers through think-aloud exploration and semi-structured interviews. (Mejia-Domenzain et al. (2023))
