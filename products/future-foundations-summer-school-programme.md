# Future Foundations summer school programme

## Description
Future Foundations is a four-week summer programme providing literacy and numeracy catch-up lessons, small-group mentoring, and enrichment activities for disadvantaged Year 5 and 6 pupils.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Future Foundations summer school programme (four-week literacy and numeracy catch-up with enrichment activities)**: A four-week summer holiday programme for Year 5 and 6 pupils eligible for free school meals or below Level 4 in English or maths, piloted in 2012 and evaluated by randomised trial in 2013 across Brighton, Enfield and Islington. "Pupils attending the four-week programme followed a specially designed curriculum involving regular literacy and numeracy lessons taught by trained primary and secondary school teachers." Mornings delivered two 75-minute lessons to small tuition groups supported by mentors and peer-mentors; afternoons offered sports and enrichment activities, closing with a graduation ceremony. (Stephen Gorard et al. (2014))

### Claims
