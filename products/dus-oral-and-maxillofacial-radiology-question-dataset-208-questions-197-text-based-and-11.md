# DUS oral and maxillofacial radiology question dataset (208 questions, 197 text-based and 11 image-based, across 14 subject areas)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A publicly available dataset compiled by Geduk Gediz et al. containing 208 Turkish Dentistry Specialty Examination questions for evaluating LLM knowledge of oral and maxillofacial radiology.

### Claims

- [ChatGPT 4.0 achieved the highest accuracy (91.3%) on DUS oral and maxillofacial radiology questions and significantly outperformed all other evaluated LLMs](../claims/chatgpt-4-highest-accuracy-dus-radiology.md) [+M]
- [All seven LLMs showed markedly lower accuracy on image-based than text-based DUS questions, with significant text-image gaps for five models but not Gemini or Claude](../claims/llms-lower-accuracy-image-based-dus-questions.md) [+M]
- [LLM accuracy varied by subject area, with oral pathology (94%) and salivary gland diseases (91.8%) highest and radiological anatomy (67.3%) lowest; only Copilot showed a statistically significant across-subject difference](../claims/subject-area-accuracy-variation-dus-llms.md) [+M]
- [Pairwise chi-square comparisons found no significant difference only between Grok and ChatGPT 4.5, while all other model pairs differed significantly](../claims/pairwise-llm-comparisons-grok-chatgpt45-nonsignificant.md) [+M]
- [ChatGPT 4.0 was more consistent on a question-by-question basis than other models, whose error rates were considerably higher on items ChatGPT 4.0 answered correctly](../claims/chatgpt-4-question-level-consistency-advantage.md) [+M]
