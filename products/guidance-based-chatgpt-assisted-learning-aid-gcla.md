# Guidance-based ChatGPT-assisted Learning Aid (GCLA)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
GCLA is a ChatGPT-assisted learning aid developed by Lee et al. that uses Apple Shortcuts to require students' proposed answers before providing hint-based guidance and logging interactions for reflection.
