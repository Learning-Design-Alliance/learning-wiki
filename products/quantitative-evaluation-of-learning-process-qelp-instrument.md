## Description
QELP is a multi-component pharmacology experimental-course assessment developed and administered by the college's Experimental (Training) Teaching Center to combine online, midterm, experimental-report, and final scores.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantitative evaluation of learning process (QELP) instrument for pharmacology experimental courses**: QELP is a multi-component evaluation developed by the college's Experimental (Training) Teaching Center, "divided into 4 aspects: online scores, midterm scores, experimental report scores, final scores, and composite scores (the average score of the above 4 items)." Each evaluation is scored to a maximum of 100 points, with final scores covering 10 teaching projects averaged across key experimental-operation scoring points rated by the guiding teacher during class. The article uses QELP to compare TPETM and RFPETM groups across the learning process. (Miao C et al. (2026))
