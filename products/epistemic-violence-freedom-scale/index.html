Epistemic violence-freedom scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 29-item Likert-scale instrument developed by Kartal et al. to measure adults' acceptance of, use of, past exposure to, and tendency toward epistemic violence or freedom in education.
