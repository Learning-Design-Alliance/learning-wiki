# GEAS laboratory resources

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
GEAS is a set of online general-astronomy laboratory projects and data-analysis tools developed by its authors for college courses, distance learning, and outreach.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three usage modes for the GEAS laboratory resources: full semester sequence, single standalone project, and tool-only access**: The article defines three modes of adopting the GEAS resources. The first is a semester- or term-long class sequence in which students complete up to eight two-week projects with full online access; the second deploys one or more single projects in stand-alone mode as class or homework assignments or outreach, with alternative versions that circumvent individual plotting-tool use; the third is direct use of the plotting, imaging, or spectrum analysis tools for independent data-analysis projects. The article notes "All projects can be conducted in-class, via distance learning, or through a hybrid model." (Nicole P. Vogt et al. (2012))
