## Description
Pulsar Search Collaboratory is a research-participation programme run by its organising science-education partners that engages high-school students and teachers in authentic pulsar-data analysis through online training, mentoring, clubs, seminars, and related research experiences.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PSC II program design: online training, near-peer mentoring, Capstone seminars, and summer camp around authentic research**: PSC II organizes authentic research participation through recurring components: a six-week online workshop with certification tests, an online community forum, annual Capstone seminars, and a summer camp at the Green Bank Observatory. The article lists innovations over PSC I including "mentoring by near-peer undergraduate students to build strong bonds with high school students" and an online learning and discovery environment, while retaining core elements such as school-based clubs and pulsar data analysis. (Blumer et al. (2019))
