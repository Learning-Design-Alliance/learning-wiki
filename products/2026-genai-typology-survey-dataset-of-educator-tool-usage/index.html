2026 GenAI Typology survey dataset of educator tool usage¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

An internationally collected survey dataset of 211 educators across nine countries and education levels, released by Matt Bower et al., documenting the generative-AI tools educators reported using across nine typology categories.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A 2025-2026 survey of 211 educators in nine countries identified 50 unique generative AI tools meeting inclusion criteria across nine categories [+W]
  • Typology tools are ordered by educator-reported frequency of use within each category [+W]
  • Many contemporary generative AI tools serve multiple educational purposes, with several tools appearing in multiple typology categories [+W]
