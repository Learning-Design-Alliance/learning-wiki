# Accelerate, TN ALL Corps, and Vetted Texas Tutor Corps

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
These are statewide intensive-tutoring programmes run by Louisiana, Tennessee, and Texas education agencies, respectively, with dedicated public funding, approved or supported providers, tutor training, instructional materials, and implementation assistance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Statewide intensive tutoring programs with dedicated funding and vetted vendor hubs (Accelerate, TN ALL Corps, Vetted Texas Tutor Corps)**: The brief profiles named statewide tutoring systems built with relief funds: Louisiana's Accelerate program (part of $90 million in academic and social-emotional recovery spending, with a year-long math and ELA tutoring curriculum developed with the teacher-leader community and a vendor guide of state-approved private providers); Tennessee's TN ALL Corps (an estimated $200 million initiative under the Learning Loss Remediation and Student Acceleration Act, with matching grants to LEAs); and Texas's Vetted Texas Tutor Corps, which "provides districts free access to instructional materials for tutoring" and TEA-subsidized tutor training with ongoing technical assistance via Regional Education Service Centers. (Promising Practices: Smart State Strategies for Building Intensive Tutoring Systems (2022))
