# Self-Assessment of Implementation Survey (SAIS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SAIS is an online multi-informant survey developed to assess implementation of systems-of-care strategies across governance, management, service delivery, geographic coverage, and continuous quality improvement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-Assessment of Implementation Survey (SAIS) with five SOC implementation themes**: The SAIS is a multi-informant online survey assessing "the degree to which strategies have been implemented in the service of developing or expanding the grantee's system of care." It covers five themes: SOC governance; management, policies, and procedures; support of local service delivery; geographic area covered; and continuous quality improvement. Respondents rate strategies from 1 ("not planned") to 6 ("extensively implemented"), and mean theme scores are aggregated by grantee for analysis at the system rather than individual level. (Olson Jonathan R. et al. (2020))
