EPIC Charter School Consortium school-and-teacher-effectiveness measurement system¶

Description¶

A Mathematica Policy Research measurement system used by the EPIC charter school consortium to identify effective schools and teachers across its member charter schools.

Components¶

  • EPIC Charter School Consortium school-and-teacher-effectiveness measurement system (Year 2): A measurement system developed by Mathematica Policy Research to identify effective schools and teachers for the EPIC charter school consortium. In its second year it covered "a consortium of over 140 charter schools in 17 states and the District of Columbia." The system's purpose is to support the consortium in recognizing effective schools and teachers, though the available page does not print the technical details of the measures. (Liz Potamites et al. (2009))
