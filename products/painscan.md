## Description
PainScan is a visualization-based digital training platform developed for PICU nurses to practise pain assessment through standardized clinical cases, instructional materials, visualization, deliberate practice, and immediate feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PainScan visualization-based training platform for PICU nurses' pain assessment**: PainScan is a visualization-based digital training platform for pain assessment in critically ill children, built on a multimodal standardized dataset. It integrates four components—"theoretical learning, case‐based practice, visualization and deliberate practice"—through a Learning Materials Module and a Pain Assessment Training Module with 104 standardized real-world ICU cases, immediate feedback with explanations, individualized accounts, and support for up to 300 users with 24/7 accessibility. (Rong Xinyi et al. (2026))
