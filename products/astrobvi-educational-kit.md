## Description
The Universidad de Antofagasta's AstroBVI educational kit uses 3D-printed tactile galaxy reliefs with complementary digital images to support astronomy workshops for learners who are blind or have low vision.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AstroBVI educational kit of 3D tactile galaxy images at different wavelengths**: The AstroBVI educational kit is a set of 3D-printed tactile relief images of galaxies used as the main workshop element. The article states it "is made up of the 3D tactile images of different galaxies and at different wavelengths", listing M100 (spiral, B band), M109 (inclined barred spiral), M51 (spiral–elliptical fusion, R and B bands), M105 (elliptical, R band), NGC5866 (lenticular, R band), plus digital images for low-vision comparison. It was distributed to Latin American countries by the Universidad de Antofagasta with IAU-OAD financing. (Alexis Rodriguez Quiroz et al. (2021))

### Claims
