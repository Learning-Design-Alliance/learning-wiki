Economics Achievement Test (EAT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A researcher-used economics achievement test developed to measure senior secondary students' economics learning outcomes, including pre- and post-treatment assessment of students with hearing impairment.

Components¶

  • Economics Achievement Test (EAT) outcome instrument: The Economics Achievement Test (EAT) is the instrument the study used to measure learning outcomes of students with hearing impairment in economics, with "reliability coefficient of 0.72." It was administered in pre- and post-treatment assessments during a treatment period in which the experimental group received ICT-based training and the control group conventional teaching. Scores from the EAT supplied the ANCOVA dependent measure and the group means reported in the results. (Egaga et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • ICT-based instruction produces a significant main effect on the economics learning outcomes of students with hearing impairment [+M]
