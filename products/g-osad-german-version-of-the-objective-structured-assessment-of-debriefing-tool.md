# G-OSAD (German version of the Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing tool)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A German-language behavioural rating instrument developed by Abegglen et al. for trained raters to assess simulation-debriefing quality across eight categories and provide instructor feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **G-OSAD: a validated German version of the Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing tool**: The G-OSAD is the German translation of the OSAD, a behavioural rating tool comprising eight categories of effective high-quality debriefing, each rated on a 5-point scale yielding a global score from 8 to 40. The article developed it via translation, back-translation and cross-cultural adaptation with expert input, and validated it against the 'Standards of Educational and Psychological Measurement'. Its stated purposes include assessing debriefing quality, providing feedback to instructors, and guiding novice instructors. (Abegglen S et al. (2020))
