## Description
A publicly available Ed Trust data and rating tool that compares states' progress on educator-diversity policies using evidence-based criteria and three-level ratings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EdTrust policy scan rating methodology for state educator-diversity policies**: A rating instrument the brief uses to score each state's progress on the six diversity strategies. Each criterion is scored on a three-level scale — "Meets all or most of the requirements within the goal," "Meets some of the requirements within the goal," or "Meets one or no requirements within the goal" — with printed rating methodologies listing the evidence thresholds for each level (e.g., publicly available school-level versus district-level versus no demographic data). The brief applies it criterion-by-criterion to North Carolina and names comparison states (Massachusetts, Illinois, Tennessee, Arkansas, New Jersey, Florida, Alabama, Virginia, Oregon, New York, California, Minnesota, Delaware, Ohio, Texas) as examples. (EdTrust (2022))
