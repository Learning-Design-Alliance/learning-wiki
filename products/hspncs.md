## Description
A study-developed hospital security personnel narrative-competency framework organized around self-reflection, empathy, and professionalism, with items adapted to security work and educational levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-dimension framework of narrative competency: self-reflection, empathy, professionalism**: The article proposes a theory-driven framework extending narrative medicine to non-clinical staff. It states: "the preliminary framework of the HSPNCS comprised three higher-order dimensions: self-reflection, empathy, and professionalism." Self-reflection covers introspective processing, empathy covers listening and perspective-taking, and professionalism covers occupational identity and value. Professionalism was added as a context-specific extension beyond the classical narrative medicine definition. (Fang Binru et al. (2026))
