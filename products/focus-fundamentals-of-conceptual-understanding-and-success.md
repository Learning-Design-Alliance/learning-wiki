## Description
A university co-requisite developmental mathematics programme that combines concurrent developmental and credit-bearing coursework with mandatory learning and academic support services.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The FOCUS model as a three-legged stool of interdependent program components**: The FOCUS (Fundamentals of Conceptual Understanding and Success) model visualizes the credit-bearing course as the seat of a three-legged stool supported equally by developmental mathematics, learning support, and academic support services, with research-based best practices forming the rim. The article states "the credit-bearing course, is supported equally by Developmental Mathematics, Learning Support, and Academic Support Services". The model frames a co-requisite program in which components are interdependent rather than optional add-ons. (Mireles et al. (2014))
