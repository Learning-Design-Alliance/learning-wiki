## Description
A suite of seven GenAI-powered research-support tools developed by Soroush Sabbaghan for graduate students' literature exploration, research planning, information retrieval, document interaction, manuscript outlining, and academic copyediting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **COREI: a seven-application GenAI-powered research support suite for graduate students**: COREI (Collaborative Research Environment and Intelligence) is a suite of seven GenAI-powered tools developed to support academic research: LEO (literature exploration and research-gap identification), NORA (research-plan guidance with pre-set methodologies), OMAR (manuscript outline generation), SARA (citation-backed literature search), VIDA (web-based information retrieval), SADI (context-aware document interaction), and CESAR (APA 7th edition-compliant copyediting). Built with Python and OpenAI's GPT-4o and deployed via Streamlit, it integrates external APIs including Elsevier Scopus, SerpAPI, and Crossref. The authors describe it as "a comprehensive suite of GenAI-powered tools developed by the first author to support various aspects of academic research." (Soroush Sabbaghan et al. (2025))
