## Description
3DAM is a QMUL-developed cross-platform iOS and Android application that provides interactive 3D facial models for planning botulinum toxin treatments and injection patterns in aesthetic-medicine training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **3DAM cross-platform mobile app for interactive 3D botulinum toxin injection simulation**: A cross-platform iOS and Android application developed at QMUL that "incorporates interactive visual simulation in 3D" for aesthetic medicine training. It "allows users to include botulinum toxin planning and injection patterns into 3D facial models, replicating real clinical scenarios", covering female and male models at rest and in animation for frontalis, lateral canthus, and glabella treatment targets. Links to the 3D app and a 2D format were randomly distributed to first- and second-year aesthetic medicine students via email. (Hassan Haidar et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Postgraduate aesthetic medicine trainees overwhelmingly rate a 3D simulation app as visually similar to 2D models and superior for understanding the 3D structure of facial aging](../claims/3d-simulation-app-facial-aging-visualization-perceptions.md) [+M]
- [Trainees report that 3D simulation training improved their skin analysis, injection treatment planning, and perceived skills for injecting real patients](../claims/3d-simulation-app-clinical-skill-enhancement-perceptions.md) [+M]
