# Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire (MSLQ)

## Description
The MSLQ is Pintrich and colleagues' self-report assessment of students' motivation and learning-strategy use, comprising motivation and self-regulated-learning scales for college and high-school learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MSLQ-based self-regulation measure and self-developed ten-question geometry achievement test**: The study used "a part of the Pintrich and de Groot's motivated strategies for learning questionnaire (MSLQ)" together with a self-developed achievement test. The MSLQ version contained 22 questions on a five-point Likert scale measuring self-efficacy beliefs, beliefs about goals, and beliefs about values and interests. The achievement test consisted of ten questions, five measuring low levels of the cognitive domain and five the upper levels. Cronbach's alpha was used to assess validity of the results. (Ali Hatami (2015))
