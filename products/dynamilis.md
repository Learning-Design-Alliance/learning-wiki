## Description
Dynamilis is a tablet-and-digital-pen application that rapidly assesses children's handwriting using static, kinematic, pressure, and tilt data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Dynamilis digital application for rapid in-class handwriting assessment**: Dynamilis is a digital application that assesses the handwriting of 5–12 year old children using a consumer tablet and digital pen, scoring static, kinematic, pressure and tilt components from high-frequency pen data normalized by age and combined via PCA into a weighted total score clipped between 0 and 100. The article demonstrates its use for "rapid and in-class" assessment with 1361 second graders, capturing dynamics "not directly observable by teacher through simple observation". (Denis Pasco et al. (2025))
