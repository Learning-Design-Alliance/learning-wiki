## Description
A Python method developed by K L Cristiano et al. that calculates and visualizes the electric field produced by multiple point charges for university electromagnetism learning and laboratory use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Python method field_punctual_charge for calculating and visualising the electric field of n point charges**: The article describes a Python software tool built around a method named field_punctual_charge that implements a seven-step algorithm for computing the electric field at a point from n charges. The method uses a while loop responding to the number of charges, and plots loads, position vectors and relative position vectors with matplotlib.pyplot. Positive charges are drawn in red, negative in blue, the calculation point in black, position vectors in blue and relative position vectors in magenta. The terminal also displays intermediate quantities and the superposed field vector. (K L Cristiano et al. (2019))
