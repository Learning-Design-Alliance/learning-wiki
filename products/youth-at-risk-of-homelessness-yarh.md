## Description
YARH is a multiphase program funded by the Children's Bureau within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Administration for Children and Families. "The program funded communities interested in preventing homelessness among youth and young adults with child welfare histories." The funder, grantees, and ETA provider call it Youth At-Risk of Homelessness (YARH), and it received evaluation technical assistance from liaisons as part of the case study.
