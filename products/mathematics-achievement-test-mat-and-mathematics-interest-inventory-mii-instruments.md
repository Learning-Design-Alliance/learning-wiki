# Mathematics Achievement Test (MAT) and Mathematics Interest Inventory (MII) instruments

## Description
A researcher-developed 30-item mathematics achievement test and 20-item mathematics-interest inventory created by Stephen Akinyemi Omotayo et al. for senior secondary school students in Nigeria.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mathematics Achievement Test (MAT) 20-item instrument**: The major instrument in this study was a mathematics achievement test (MAT), a 20-item instrument with full marks of 50, drawn from Bangladeshi secondary algebra and geometry sources (Kalimullah, 1996; Nurunnabi and Kuddus, 1996). It was used as both pre-test and post-test, and its content validity was established through consensus of expert panels and pilot testing with 40 secondary students to ensure reliability in the Bangladesh context. (Hossain et al. (2012))
- **MAT 1 and MAT 2 mathematics achievement instruments**: Two researcher-developed instruments measured entry ability and final performance: "MAT 1 had five items that tested linear programming prerequisites" (constructing linear graphs, forming and graphing inequalities, shading unwanted regions, solving simultaneous linear equations graphically), and MAT 2 had five items on linear programming and optimization. Reliability was determined by the split half method, acceptable at indices of 0.6, with Pearson's r values of 0.9211 and 0.9131 (MAT 1) and 0.8786 and 0.8896 (MAT 2). (Shikuku Beatrice Nakhanu (2015))
