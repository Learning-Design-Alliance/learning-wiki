## Description
The 95 Phonics Core Program (PCP) is "a whole-class, supplemental phonics curriculum" designed as a Tier I program for students in grades K-5 to address and prevent reading gaps. It provides "explicit, structured phonics instruction for 20-30 minutes per day" based on a scope and sequence, with grade-level kits including a teacher's edition, student workbooks, manipulatives, and digital presentation files.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Initial 3-hour training, with follow-up one-hour question and answer sessions each semester and onsite coaching recommended; minimum is 3-hour initial training
#### Constraints
- Digital presentation files are complimentary only for the initial year of implementation

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-5 in whole-class (Tier I) settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonemic awareness, decoding, phonics skills, oral reading fluency, and reading comprehension
