# Six-construct AI literacy framework

## Description
A framework synthesized by Almatrafi et al. that organizes AI literacy into Recognize, Know and Understand, Use and Apply, Evaluate, Create, and Navigate Ethically.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-construct AI literacy framework (Recognize, Know and Understand, Use and Apply, Evaluate, Create, Navigate Ethically)**: The article applies a framework from Almatrafi et al.'s systematic review, which "synthesized six key constructs of AI literacy: Recognize, Know and Understand, Use and Apply, Evaluate, Create, and Navigate Ethically." The authors use it as an analytic lens to rate the prevalence (high, medium, low, none) of each construct across the three workplace teams, mapping observed knowledge, use, evaluation, creation, and ethical concerns onto the constructs. (Aditya Johri et al. (2024))
