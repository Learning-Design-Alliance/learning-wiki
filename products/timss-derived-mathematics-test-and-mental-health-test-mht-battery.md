# TIMSS-derived mathematics test and Mental Health Test (MHT) battery

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A study-administered assessment battery combining a TIMSS-derived mathematics proficiency test with the 100-item Mental Health Test (MHT) for measuring rural primary students’ mathematics performance and anxiety risk.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TIMSS-based mathematics test and Mental Health Test (MHT) measures used in the rural China commuting study**: The study measures academic performance with a 25-minute TIMSS-derived mathematics test, described as "a global benchmark for assessing primary school students’ proficiency in science and mathematics", and mental health with the Mental Health Test (MHT), a 100-item yes/no questionnaire variant of the Children's Manifest Anxiety Scale with a reported total-scale Cronbach's Alpha of 0.9. MHT scores above 65 signal high risk, and eight anxiety subcategories are scored, with learning anxiety used for robustness checking. These instruments operationalize the two dependent variables in the 12,394-student analysis sample. (Guan Hongyu et al. (2025))
