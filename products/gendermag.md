## Description
GenderMag is an evidence-based framework and method for identifying and fixing inclusivity barriers in software through personas, facet values, and specialized cognitive walkthroughs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GenderMag method for finding and fixing inclusivity bugs in software**: GenderMag is "an existing method for avoiding, finding, and fixing inclusivity "bugs" in software", chosen by the authors because it is evidence-based and used in practice by technologists. "At the core of GenderMag are five facets, each of which has a range of facet values an individual could have": motivations, information processing style, computer self-efficacy, learning style (process vs. tinkering), and attitude toward risk. Three personas (Abi, Pat, Tim) bring the facets to life, and the method integrates them into a specialized cognitive walkthrough of use-case steps. (Lara Letaw et al. (2021))
