PERMA-based AI well-being scale for faculty in AI-integrated teaching¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A five-item Likert-scale instrument developed by Liu et al. to measure university faculty's positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment in AI-supported teaching and academic work.

Related Products and Programmes¶

  • 22-item self-report instrument measuring contextual support, trust calibration, collaborative regulation, and perceived transfer gains
  • 31-item five-scale questionnaire measuring AI support, mediators, and innovative competence
  • Teacher enjoyment scale developed for this study (5 class-referenced items)
