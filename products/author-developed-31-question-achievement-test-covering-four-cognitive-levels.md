# Author-developed 31-question achievement test covering four cognitive levels

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An author-developed 31-item multiple-choice and true-or-false achievement test for pre-service teachers, measuring remembering, understanding, applying, and analyzing course content.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Author-developed 31-question achievement test covering four cognitive levels with reliability of 0.84**: The study prepared an achievement test in the form of multiple choice and true or false questions to measure students' abilities in remembering, understanding, analyzing and applying the course content. The final test included 31 questions distributed as 9 for remembering, 8 for understanding, 7 for application, and 7 for analysis, with one point per correct answer (maximum 31, minimum 0). The reliability coefficient reached 0.84 and intrinsic validity 0.89, which the article describes as acceptable. (Ibrahim (2017))
