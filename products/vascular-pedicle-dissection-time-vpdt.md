## Description
A video-based operative technical-skill assessment metric that measures the time from vascular-pedicle retraction through medial dissection completion for evaluating laparoscopic colorectal surgery trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vascular pedicle dissection time (VPDT) as a video-derived surgical skill assessment measure**: The VPDT is a simple, video-observable operative metric defined as "the time taken from retraction of the vascular pedicle until completing the medial dissection and proceeding to the next step of the operation". It was measured from recorded laparoscopic colorectal resections and benchmarked against the validated CAT score, with which it was inversely correlated. The authors position it as a skill measure with "the potential for computer vision automation", and a study is in progress to automate its measurement using a three-dimensional convolutional neural network. (de Burlet K et al. (2025))
