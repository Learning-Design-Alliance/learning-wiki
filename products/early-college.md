# Early College

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A whole-school dual-enrollment programme run through district-college partnerships that combines college-level coursework with academic, social, and transition supports for high-school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Early College four-pronged theory of change for underrepresented students' postsecondary success**: The article describes Early College as a dual-enrollment model whose design rests on a "four-pronged approach": whole-school enrollment, added academic and social supports, early exposure to college-level rigor, and credit accumulation. As the authors state, "EC aims to improve postsecondary success via a four-pronged approach: (1) it eliminates self-selection issues commonly experienced in dual-enrollment programs through whole-school enrollment" and three further aims covering supports, transition, and an academic head start. The study evaluates this model for English Learners. (Johnson et al. (2020))

### Claims
- [Early College participation increases college credits earned by English Learners in 12th grade by 10.5 credits, equivalent to two semester-long courses](../claims/ec-increases-el-12th-grade-college-credits.md) [+M]
- [Early College participation significantly decreases the probability that English Learners attend a four-year college after high school](../claims/ec-decreases-el-four-year-college-attendance.md) [-M]
