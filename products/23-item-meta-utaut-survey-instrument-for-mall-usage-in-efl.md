## Description
A questionnaire measuring MALL acceptance constructs among Vietnamese EFL learners, distributed via Google Forms with a 5-point Likert scale from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree. The article states: "In total, the survey integrated 23 items across seven conceptual domains, offering a multifaceted view of the topic." Scales were adapted from prior studies for UB, SI, PE, ATT, EE, PBC and BI, with bidirectional translation for cultural relevance and demographic variables included as controls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bidirectional translation process for linguistic and cultural relevance to the Vietnamese context
#### Constraints
- Convenience sampling via social networking platforms; the sample was predominantly female (69.45%) and aged 18–25 (87.47%), which the article says may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- Vietnamese EFL learners in blended learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring attitudes, intentions and usage behavior toward MALL
