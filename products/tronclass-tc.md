## Description
Tronclass is a visual online teaching platform developed by a university for distributing learning resources, administering tests, releasing scores, and supporting grading through web and mobile applications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tronclass (TC), a visual online teaching platform used to deliver resources, tests and anonymous grading**: Tronclass (TC) is the online learning platform through which the authors delivered team-based learning in a pediatric nursing course; the article calls it a “visual teaching platform,” developed by their university, and states the study “primarily used the platform to upload teaching resources, time-limited tests, anonymous grading, etc.” It runs on PCs and a mobile application, releases scores after tests, and calculates the error rate and distribution of options for each question. (Hu Longyi et al. (2024))
