## Description
ESR eGUIDE is an online evidence-based imaging-appropriateness and clinical decision-support learning platform developed and operated by the European Society of Radiology.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ESR eGUIDE online e-learning clinical decision support platform for appropriate imaging test selection**: ESR eGUIDE is an online e-learning tool developed by the European Society of Radiology, available since 2018, that supports choosing imaging modalities for clinical scenarios. "The ESR eGUIDE allows to review the evidence-based appropriateness of different modalities, and their radiation exposure and costs." Appropriateness is scored on a 1–9 scale with radiation (0–5) and cost indicators; users step through patient age and gender, anatomic region, clinical indication, and scenario. This evaluation used the first platform version. (Diekhoff Torsten et al. (2020))
