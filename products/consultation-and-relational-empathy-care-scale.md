## Description
A 10-item, 5-point Likert patient-rating instrument designed by Mercer et al. to assess experienced clinician empathy during consultations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CARE scale as an external patient-rated empathy assessment instrument**: The Consultation and Relational Empathy (CARE) scale, designed by Mercer et al., is used in this study as the external behavioural measure of empathy, rated by the group-associated patient immediately after the encounter. The article describes it as "an external assessment tool for empathy rated by an involved person" containing 10 items on a 5-point Likert scale, where a low score indicates high experienced empathy. It complemented the self-report JSPE and IRI instruments, which detected no group differences. (Britz Vanessa et al. (2024))
