retrievalpractice.org¶

Description¶

A free science-communication resource initiative created by Pooja K. Agarwal that provides teacher practice guides, a website, and an email newsletter on retrieval practice and related learning strategies.

Components¶

  • retrievalpractice.org website, downloadable practice guides, and email newsletter for teachers: A suite of free science-communication resources developed by the author from 2012 onward: a 10-page practice guide on retrieval practice refined through teacher focus groups and classroom observation, six additional practice guides on interleaving, transfer, spaced retrieval, metacognition, early childhood, and interpreting meta-analyses, the retrievalpractice.org website (created 2014), and a Mailchimp email newsletter begun around 2016. All materials are "freely available for download under Creative Commons Licensing," with translations in Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, and Mandarin. (Pooja K. Agarwal (2024))

Claims¶

  • Dissemination data from the author's resources show approximately 15,000 monthly website visitors, at least 250,000 practice-guide downloads, and a newsletter with nearly 18,000 subscribers and a 50% open rate [+W]
  • A newsletter issue featuring a specific teaching activity yielded a higher email open rate than previous research-focused content, and teaching-activity content drove engagement [+W]
