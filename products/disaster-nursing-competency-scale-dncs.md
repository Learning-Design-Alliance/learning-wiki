## Description
The DNCS is a 50-item Likert self-assessment developed by Hu for measuring undergraduate nursing students’ disaster-nursing knowledge, professional competence, and comprehensive qualities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Disaster Nursing Competency Scale (DNCS) as a self-assessment instrument for evaluating disaster nursing education**: The DNCS developed by Hu is a self-assessment instrument theoretically grounded in the ICN/WHO core-competency framework for disaster nursing. It comprises three first-level indicators (knowledge system, professional competence, and comprehensive qualities), 11 second-level indicators, and 50 third-level indicators, using a 5-point Likert scale with total scores ranging from 50 to 250. In the original development study, the scale demonstrated a Scale-level Content Validity Index of 0.95 and Cronbach α of 0.984; in the current sample, Cronbach α was 0.927. (Li P et al. (2026))
