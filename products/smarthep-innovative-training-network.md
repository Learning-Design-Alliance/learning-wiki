## Description
A Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral training network that supports early-stage researchers through cross-sector supervision, research placements, and coordinated academic–industry training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SMARTHEP network structure for doctoral training in ML and real-time analysis**: The SMARTHEP Innovative Training Network was a Marie Sklodowska-Curie funded doctoral training programme running 2021-2025 that "supported twelve ESRs across eight European nations, all enrolled in doctoral programs". ESRs conducted their PhD within the four major LHC collaborations or at industrial partners such as IBM and Verizon Connect, received "dual supervision by experts from both academia and the private sector", and took part in annual network meetings, training events, monthly management meetings, and an affiliated-student cohort fully integrated into training sessions and annual meetings. (Albrecht et al. (2026))
