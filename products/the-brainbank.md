## Description
The BrainBank is a free online collection of neuroscience teaching activities, demonstrations, and supporting notes created by the Christmas Lectures authors for school learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The BrainBank free online teaching resource of 60 activities and demonstrations**: A free online resource of teaching materials and notes created by the authors to support the Christmas Lectures content, hosted at www.thebrainbank.org.uk. The article states that interest and memory survey data "was subsequently used to select a series of 60 activities and demonstrations that were included in a free online resource of teaching materials and notes". It includes a section describing the Deese-Roediger-McDermott memory illusion demonstration used in the lecture. (Gjersoe NL (2013))
