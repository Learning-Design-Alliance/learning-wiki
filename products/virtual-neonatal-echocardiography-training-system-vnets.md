# Virtual Neonatal Echocardiography Training System (VNETS)

## Description
VNETS is a hybrid neonatal and pediatric echocardiography simulator that uses tracked transducer interaction, 3D heart models, real echocardiographic datasets, automated image-quality scoring, and physiological-mode simulations for clinical training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Virtual Neonatal Echocardiography Training System (VNETS), a hybrid neonatal and pediatric echocardiography simulator**: VNETS is one of two simulators with real images dedicated to echocardiography training in neonates and infants. It uses 3D echocardiographic datasets sliced into cut planes displayed as 2D images, with a 3D heart model showing the scanning plane; "the system feedback mechanism notifies the trainee when optimal cut plane is obtained". It simulates color flow Doppler, spectral Doppler and M-mode, supports hemodynamic measurements and automated image-quality scoring in a student mode, and has been used since 2012 in courses and workshops internationally. (Singh Yogen et al. (2025))
