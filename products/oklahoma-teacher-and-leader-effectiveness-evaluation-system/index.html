Description¶

Oklahoma's statewide system for evaluating teachers and school leaders, including value-added measures estimated from student test scores and related teacher and student information.

Components¶

  • Oklahoma teacher value-added measures for the 2013-2014 school year: The article describes value-added measures delivered to Oklahoma teachers and administrators as part of the state's Teacher and Leader Effectiveness Evaluation System. The report states that "In the spring and summer of 2015, teachers and administrators will receive value-added measures based on instruction provided in the 2013–2014 school year." The measures were produced by estimating value added from test scores and other information about teachers and students. (Elias Walsh et al. (2015))
