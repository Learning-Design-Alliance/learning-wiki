# SimulHeart®

## Description
A custom 3D-printed coronary-procedure simulator made by 3D CardioSolutions that provides realistic vascular anatomy, haptic catheter practice, pulsatile flow, and radiation-free or fluoroscopic training and assessment for medical learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SimulHeart 3D-printed coronary simulator**: SimulHeart® (3D CardioSolutions, Coimbra, Portugal) is a custom-made 3D-printed simulator in which "a clear acrylic tank filled with water" holds "standard 3D-printed vascular anatomical structures" including the aorta and coronaries, connected to a simulated radial access and a pulsatile pumping system. It enables the use of standard diagnostic and interventional devices with realistic haptics feedback, was used radiation-free for training and under fluoroscopy for assessment, and had been validated for content and appearance in simulated coronary procedures. (Oliveira-Santos Manuel et al. (2026))
