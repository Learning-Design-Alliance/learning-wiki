# AIxSuture dataset of 314 expert-rated open suturing videos with OSATS-based three-level skill labels

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A publicly released corpus of 314 standardized open-suturing videos from medical and dental students, annotated by independent raters using eight OSATS categories and three-level skill labels for surgical-skill assessment research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Public open-surgery suturing video dataset with blinded OSATS annotations (Zenodo)**: The authors released the study's video dataset (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7940583) to enable development of automated AI-based surgical performance assessment. "The dataset includes 314 videos with a total length of 26 h and blinded OSATS annotated by three qualified investigators." It also includes the number of sutures performed, and the authors state it is, to their knowledge, the first public dataset of its kind for open surgical suturing and one of the largest video datasets. (Peters Philipp et al. (2023))
