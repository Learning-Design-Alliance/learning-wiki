## Description
OCRA is a supplementary interactive multimedia mobile application developed by Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman and colleagues for visualizing and manipulating organic reaction mechanisms on smartphones and tablets.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OCRA: an interactive multimedia mobile learning prototype for organic reaction mechanisms**: OCRA (Organic Chemistry Reaction Application) is a mobile learning prototype for smartphones and tablets that lets students create and visualize the mechanistic steps of organic reactions. It "uses a touch screen feature to demonstrate and conceptually visualize the mechanistic steps in an organic reaction mechanism", letting students form and break chemical bonds by sliding electrons or atoms, and covers addition, substitution, and elimination reactions. It is positioned as a supplementary tool, not a prime classroom teaching tool, and was developed based on the revised Bloom's taxonomy. (Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman et al. (2017))
