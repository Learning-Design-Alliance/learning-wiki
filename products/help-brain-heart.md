## Description
Help-Brain-Heart is the Swedish Resuscitation Council's interactive web-based cardiovascular-disease and BLS-preparation education for adult lay learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Help-Brain-Heart (HBH) interactive web course**: The HBH web course was developed and published online by the Swedish Resuscitation Council in 2013 and "deals with the symptoms, causes and actions for stroke, AMI and cardiac arrest and includes information about lifestyle factors". It is led by an animated storyteller, with narrative interspersed with videos, animations and ten interactive questions designed to "engage the students and force them to use decision-making skills". It lasts 20–30 min and was undertaken individually or as a class within 7 days before practical CPR training. (Nord Anette et al. (2017))
