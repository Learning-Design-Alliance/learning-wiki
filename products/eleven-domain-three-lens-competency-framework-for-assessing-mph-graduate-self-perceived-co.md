# Eleven-domain, three-lens competency framework for assessing MPH graduate self-perceived competencies

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-administered questionnaire developed by Zweigenthal et al. to assess MPH graduates’ self-perceived competencies across 11 domains through usefulness-at-work, MPH-program attainment, and application-confidence lenses.

### Claims
