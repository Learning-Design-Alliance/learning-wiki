## Description
P-scribe is a web-based e-learning platform developed for pharmacy and medical education, used by Dutch and Belgian medical centres to deliver case-based rational pharmacotherapy and prescribing training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **P-scribe e-learning platform for pharmacotherapy education**: P-scribe is a Dutch web-based e-learning platform for pharmacy and medical students, used at all Dutch university medical centres and several Belgian medical centres. E-learning modules can be built adapted to curriculum needs and are based around the WHO 6-step model for rational pharmacotherapy. Students are "guided through different life‐like cases in which a change in pharmacotherapy is needed", each module focusing on a different aspect such as side-effects or polypharmacy. At Erasmus MC it is used in the medical master's curriculum as part of flipped classroom teaching. (Kalfsvel L et al. (2022))
- **Case-based web-based e-learning module on antibiotic prescribing (Pscribe, endocarditis case)**: The authors developed an e-learning module in Pscribe, a web-based programme based on the WHO Guide to Good Prescribing, built around a case of endocarditis interspersed with antibiotic information. It includes questions with direct feedback to increase interactivity, provides local and national guidelines to mimic real prescribing, tracks participation, takes 60–90 minutes, and permits stopping and resuming. Content validity was assessed by one physician-researcher and three infectious diseases physicians. (Caris MG et al. (2018))
