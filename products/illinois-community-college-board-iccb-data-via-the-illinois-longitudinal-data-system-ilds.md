# Illinois Community College Board (ICCB) data via the Illinois Longitudinal Data System (ILDS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A statewide administrative dataset maintained by the Illinois Community College Board and accessed through the Illinois Longitudinal Data System, containing enrollment, credential, program, and credit records for students at Illinois public two-year colleges.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Illinois Community College Board (ICCB) data via the Illinois Longitudinal Data System**: ICCB data, accessed through the ILDS, is a state administrative dataset covering all public two-year colleges in Illinois. The article states that "Students cannot opt out of the data, making it a more comprehensive source of information on CPS graduates who enroll in two-year colleges than NSC data." It classifies credentials as associate degrees, short-term certificates, or long-term certificates, and includes program types, credits attempted and earned, and early college enrollment. Records link across state agencies via the CDDA ID without transferring personal identifying information. (Jenny Nagaoka et al. (2020))

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A data sharing agreement through the ILDS and CDDA IDs linking ISBE K-12 records to ICCB records
#### Constraints
- Covers only two-year colleges in Illinois
- Less research and guidance exist for working with and interpreting ICCB data in external research
- No consistent data system and decision rules between IBHE and ICCB for overall postsecondary outcomes

### Target Learners
- CPS high school graduates enrolling in Illinois two-year colleges
