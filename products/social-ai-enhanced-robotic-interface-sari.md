# Social AI-Enhanced Robotic Interface (SARI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
SARI is a study-developed virtual-patient interface combining an LLM with a Furhat social robot for spoken clinical-encounter practice, automatic transcription, and AI-generated feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Social AI-Enhanced Robotic Interface (SARI) for virtual patient encounters**: The SARI is a virtual patient modality that "combines AI using LLMs with a social robot", pairing GPT-4o-mini with a Furhat Robotics back-projected robot head capable of humanlike facial expressions, natural head movements, and lip-synchronized speech. Students engage through natural speech dialogue; interactions are audio-recorded and transcribed automatically to enable AI-powered feedback. In this study students completed 9 VP cases with the SARI within a rheumatology virtual outpatient clinic. (Alexander Borg et al. (2026))
