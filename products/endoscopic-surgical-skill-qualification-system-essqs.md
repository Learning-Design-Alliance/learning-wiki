# Endoscopic Surgical Skill Qualification System (ESSQS)

## Description
A Japan Society for Endoscopic Surgery (JSES) certification and video-based assessment system for accrediting laparoscopic surgeons as skilled operators and instructors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Endoscopic Surgical Skill Qualification System (ESSQS) of the Japan Society for Endoscopic Surgery**: The ESSQS is a certification system established by the Japan Society for Endoscopic Surgery to maintain and improve the quality of laparoscopic surgery in Japan. Applicants submit documents including practice certification, society membership, seminar attendance, a bibliography, an operative list, and an unedited video; video evaluation by two judges uses a score sheet in which "Checking points are divided into 2 parts consisting of: "common criteria" for basic endoscopic techniques commonly used for all procedures, and "organ-specific criteria" for special endoscopic surgical techniques for individual organs." Marks are allotted 60 and 40 points respectively, with a pass mark of 70 points focused on surgical technique and camera work. (Aoyama Shota et al. (2019))
