# MT-ITS (embedded moving-target marksmanship tutoring system)

## Description
MT-ITS is a software-based intelligent tutoring system embedded in an instrumented rifle's digital sight for training novice shooters in moving-target marksmanship using the Virtual Immersive Soldier Simulator.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MT-ITS: embedded moving-target marksmanship tutoring system**: MT-ITS is a software-based tutor embedded into the digital sight of an instrumented rifle, operating in the Virtual Immersive Soldier Simulator. Its two feedback mechanisms are "a real-time correction of aiming" delivered by the Smart Sight System, which presents the optimal point of aim as a red rectangle on the target, and an after-action feedback showing each shot sequentially plus a statistical summary. It incorporates domain, tutor, and user-interface components of a standard ITS; its learner model is largely undeveloped, and the system is tuned for novice shooters. (Vladimir Zotov et al. (2023))
