## Description
NVivo is qualitative research software made by Lumivero for organizing, coding, querying, and analyzing diverse qualitative data such as interviews, observations, journals, videos, and artifacts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NVivo qualitative software for organizing and coding action research data**: The teacher-researchers were trained in the uses of NVivo qualitative software to assist them in organizing and coding their participatory observation field-notes, daily reflective journals, video tape-log summaries where applicable, recorded interviews, recorded focus group discussions, and material artifacts. The tool supported the iterative nature of action research across the two-year period. (Razfar (2011))
