# Mental Health First Aid (MHFA)

## Description
Mental Health First Aid is a structured mental-health-literacy and first-aid training programme developed and licensed by Mental Health First Aid International, delivered here as a self-paced six-module eLearning course.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The MHFA eLearning course: six self-paced modules teaching the ALGEE action plan**: The MHFA eLearning course is a structured online programme teaching mental health literacy and first aid skills. It "comprises six modules completed consecutively", covering depression, anxiety, eating disorders, psychosis and substance use problems, each with a disorder-specific action plan. "Content is delivered through text, images, audio, videos and interactive activities, and completed at the user's own pace over approximately 6–8 h." Central to MHFA is the five-step ALGEE action plan: approach the person, assess and assist with any crisis; listen non-judgementally; give support and information; encourage appropriate professional help; and encourage other support strategies. Participants also received the MHFA Manual and a supplementary medical students' booklet. (Davies E. Bethan et al. (2018))
- **Consider Mental Health First Aid training as a standard component of pre-service or in-service teacher training**: The article argues that, given the high prevalence of adolescent mental health problems and teachers' obligation to provide safe environments, "Mental Health First Aid training needs to be considered as a standard component of pre-service or in-service teacher training", analogous to first aid and child protection training. It rests on the trial's demonstrated gains in teacher knowledge, attitudes, confidence and policy awareness. (Jorm Anthony F et al. (2010))
- **Modified 7-hour Youth Mental Health First Aid course for high school teachers**: A shortened adaptation of the 14-hour Youth Mental Health First Aid course, organized into "two one-day parts of seven hours each": Part 1 for all education staff covering departmental policy, common adolescent disorders and the ALGEE action plan; Part 2 for welfare staff covering crises and less common problems. Delivered at schools by paired instructors trained in a one-week program, with lesson plans, the Youth MHFA manual and factsheets as documentation. (Jorm Anthony F et al. (2010))
