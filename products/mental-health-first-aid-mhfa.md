## Description
Mental Health First Aid is a structured mental-health-literacy and first-aid training programme developed and licensed by Mental Health First Aid International, delivered here as a self-paced six-module eLearning course.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The MHFA eLearning course: six self-paced modules teaching the ALGEE action plan**: The MHFA eLearning course is a structured online programme teaching mental health literacy and first aid skills. It "comprises six modules completed consecutively", covering depression, anxiety, eating disorders, psychosis and substance use problems, each with a disorder-specific action plan. "Content is delivered through text, images, audio, videos and interactive activities, and completed at the user's own pace over approximately 6–8 h." Central to MHFA is the five-step ALGEE action plan: approach the person, assess and assist with any crisis; listen non-judgementally; give support and information; encourage appropriate professional help; and encourage other support strategies. Participants also received the MHFA Manual and a supplementary medical students' booklet. (Davies E. Bethan et al. (2018))
