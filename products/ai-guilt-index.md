## Description
A four-item Likert-scale instrument developed by Diane Vassallo to measure university academic staff's guilt-related moral distress about using AI in academic practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI guilt as a psychological phenomenon of moral discomfort from using AI tools**: AI guilt is defined as "a psychological phenomenon wherein individuals feel guilt or moral discomfort when use AI tools and feel that their reliance on these technologies might be perceived negatively by others or feel disingenuous." The article frames it as most relevant "in academic, professional, or creative contexts where originality and individual effort are highly valued," and interprets it through impostor syndrome (Clance & Imes) and cognitive dissonance (Festinger) alongside technology-acceptance frameworks (TAM, UTAUT). (Chan (2024))
