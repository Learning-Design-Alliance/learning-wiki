## Description
The Expository Reading and Writing Course is a college-preparatory English language arts curriculum developed by the California State University system for secondary students, with modules that integrate rhetorical reading, writing, revision, and language study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ERWC Assignment Template: scaffolded module structure with six strands across three domains**: The ERWC curriculum's 12 modules all follow the Assignment Template, "which presents a scaffolded process to help students read, comprehend, and respond to nonfiction and literary texts." Each module spans three domains — Reading Rhetorically (Prereading, Reading, Postreading strands), Connecting Reading to Writing (Discovering What You Think), and Writing Rhetorically (Entering the Conversation, Revising and Editing). Strands contain elements with activities, such as Surveying the Text and Making Predictions and Asking Questions. The template also embeds formative assessment recommendations and rhetorical grammar activities using curriculum readings and students' own writings. (Anthony B. Fong et al. (2015))
