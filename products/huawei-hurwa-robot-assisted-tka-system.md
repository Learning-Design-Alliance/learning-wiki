# Huawei HURWA robot-assisted TKA system

## Description
Huawei HURWA is a robotic total-knee-arthroplasty system that uses CT-based three-dimensional planning, optical registration, and mechanical-arm-assisted osteotomy for surgical teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Huawei HURWA robot-assisted TKA system with manufacturer standardized training**: The HURWA system is the robot-assisted TKA platform evaluated in the study. It is "based on preoperative hip-knee-ankle CT" and its standard procedure comprises "preoperative CT three-dimensional planning, intraoperative optical tracker registration, and mechanical arm-assisted osteotomy". All participating doctors "completed the manufacturer's standardized training for the used robot system (Huawei HURWA) (the first 5 RA-TKAs performed)", ensuring initial adaptation of the surgeon and surgical team. The mechanical-alignment strategy targeted an HKA of 180°, with deviation of up to 3° allowed. (Song Xunzhou et al. (2026))
