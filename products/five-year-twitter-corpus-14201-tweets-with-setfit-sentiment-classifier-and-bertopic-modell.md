# Five-year Twitter corpus (14,201 tweets) with SetFit sentiment classifier and BERTopic modelling pipeline

## Description
A researcher-created dataset and analysis pipeline comprising 14,201 English-language U.S. tweets about AI, education, and ethics, classified with SetFit and modelled with BERTopic by Bagale et al.
