# EDUCE teaching and learning materials

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EDUCE is an open-source modular data-science curriculum and materials package developed by the University of British Columbia for undergraduate life-sciences learners and adaptation by instructors elsewhere.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Open-source EDUCE teaching materials on GitHub with a workshop portfolio and Course Compliler app**: All EDUCE teaching and learning materials are released on GitHub (https://educe-ubc.github.io), with the stated intention of "developing an open-source community of practice that extends beyond UBC". Released materials include self-assessment tools, lecture slides, notes/handouts, in-class and at-home tutorials, captioned screen capture videos, individual assignments, and team projects. The site also hosts a workshop portfolio (github.com/EDUCE-UBC/workshops_access) of 32 hours of R content and a "Course Compliler" app (educe-ubc.github.io/compiler.html) with additional module examples. (Dill-McFarland KA et al. (2021))
