## Description
A 10-question context-modified version of the Force Concept Inventory created by John Stewart and colleagues to diagnose introductory mechanics students’ Newtonian-force concepts and misconceptions while reducing recognition and answer-recall effects.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The FCIT: a 10-question context-modified version of the Force Concept Inventory**: The FCIT (FCI transformed) is a 10-question test created by applying eight restrictive transformation types to selected FCI questions: making the system abstract, changing the physical system, removing redundant distractors, adding or removing a figure, reordering responses, restructuring question groups, and making responses symbolic. Multiple transformations were applied to most questions "to insure that the question was not recognizable to the students and could not be answered by recalling their previous answer." Original FCI wording was maintained so only the listed transformations affected responses. (John Stewart et al. (2007))

### Claims
