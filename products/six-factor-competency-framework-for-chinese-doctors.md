# Six-factor competency framework for Chinese doctors

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A work-analysis-based framework defining six competency domains for Chinese doctors—technical procedural skills, diagnosis and management, teamwork and administration, communication, professional behavior, and professional values—to guide outcomes-based medical education and training reform.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-factor competencies framework for Chinese doctors**: The article establishes a competencies model for doctors in China built from work analysis rather than expert consensus. It comprises six elements: "(a) technical procedural skills; (b) diagnosis and management; (c) teamwork and administration; (d) communication; (e) professional behavior; and (f) professional values." The GWA questionnaire captures elements above the competency "iceberg" and the WS questionnaire those beneath it. The framework is intended to drive outcomes-based reform of Chinese medical education. (Zhao Li et al. (2015))
