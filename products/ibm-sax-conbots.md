# IBM SAX Conbots

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open-source virtual teacher–learner interaction environment and GAIL teacher model developed by IBM for training and evaluating automated motor-skill teaching systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Open-source teacher-learner interaction environment and GAIL teacher model (IBM SAX Conbots)**: The authors "implemented an adaptable environment for training and assessing a GAIL model to teach a motor skill", a continuous 60×60 virtual board on which a target cursor moves via sine functions and a teacher and learner agent interact through connected or unconnected force-based dynamics. The environment serves as a testbed platform using RL to simulate teacher-learner interactions and evaluate teaching effectiveness. The article states, "We open source both the model and the environment to the community", available at https://github.com/IBM/SAX/tree/main/Conbots/EAAI24. (Hadar Mulian et al. (2024))
