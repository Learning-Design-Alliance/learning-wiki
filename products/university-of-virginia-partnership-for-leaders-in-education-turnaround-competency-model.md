# University of Virginia Partnership for Leaders in Education turnaround competency model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A seven-competency, four-level proficiency framework developed by the University of Virginia Partnership for Leaders in Education to assess and develop school leaders in turnaround contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **University of Virginia Partnership for Leaders in Education turnaround competency model with four proficiency levels across seven competencies**: An evidence-based competency model developed from analysis of the actions of successful turnaround principals, identifying competencies the analysis linked to improvements in student achievement. Table 1 presents seven competencies — including "Focuses on Sustainable Results," "Engages the Team," "Impact and Influence," and "Commitment to Student Learning" — each described across four levels of proficiency from basic to sustained, advanced behavior. (Center on School Turnaround at WestEd (2018))
