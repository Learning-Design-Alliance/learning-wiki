## Description
A freely available upper-division electrodynamics curriculum package developed by Charles Baily and colleagues at the University of Colorado, containing clicker questions, activities, homework, exams, implementation guides, learning goals, and editable source files.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Set of 13 in-class electrodynamics tutorials plus nine short activities used in roughly 40% of lectures**: A set of curricular materials developed for the CU Boulder E&M 2 course: "The FA11 interviews resulted in a set of 13 tutorials that were used in the SP12 semester; another nine short activities were added to cover review material and other basics". Approximately 40% of lectures incorporated small-group work facilitated by a room with tables, with completion times ranging from 10 to 45 minutes and tutorials oriented by concept tests and discussion. (Baily et al. (2013))
