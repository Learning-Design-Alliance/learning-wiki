# Big Ideas in Cosmology online college curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A web-based 15-week introductory astronomy curriculum developed by McLin et al. for general-education undergraduates, organized into three thematic modules with integrated interactive activities and progress tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Big Ideas in Cosmology web curriculum: three thematic modules of five chapters each for general-education undergraduates**: The Big Ideas in Cosmology curriculum is a web-based, 15-week course for general-education undergraduate students that makes recent cosmological advances its primary focus. It is "organized into three thematic modules, and each module is divided into five chapters," covering size and time scales, gravity and dark matter, and cosmic expansion, the early Universe, the CMB, large-scale structure, and dark energy. Interactives are built into the flow of the text rather than treated as supplementary, and students give feedback to the computer learning system so they and their instructor have a running tally of progress. (McLin et al. (2013))
