# Physics and Astronomy New Faculty Workshop (NFW)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A national four-day professional-development workshop run for new tenure-track physics and astronomy faculty, featuring sessions led by experienced physics and astronomy education researchers on research-based instructional strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Physics and Astronomy New Faculty Workshop with Faculty Online Learning Communities as implementation support for RBIS use**: The New Faculty Workshop (NFW) is a dissemination effort attended by approximately 40% of new US physics faculty each year. Beginning in 2015, attendees could join a Faculty Online Learning Community (FOLC): "virtual meetings with a small group of NFW attendees and afacilitator approximately every other week for approximately one year after the NFW." FOLC discussions center on implementation difficulties and successes, with peer support supplemented by outside experts, and FOLCs have also been used in other settings to implement RBISs in physics. (Melissa Dancy et al. (2022))
