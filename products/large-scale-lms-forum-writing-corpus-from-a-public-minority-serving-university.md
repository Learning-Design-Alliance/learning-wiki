# Large-scale LMS forum writing corpus from a public minority-serving university

## Description
A deidentified corpus of undergraduate LMS discussion-forum posts linked to anonymized student linguistic and socioeconomic records for studying academic writing quality and equity trends, assembled by Renzhe Yu and colleagues.
