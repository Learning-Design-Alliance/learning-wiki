## Description
The Campaign for Learning's Mind the Gap family-learning programme uses the 5Rs framework—Readiness, Resourcefulness, Resilience, Responsibility and Reflectiveness—to structure metacognitive learning activities for primary-aged children and their families.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The 5Rs learning dispositions framework: Readiness, Resourcefulness, Resilience, Responsibility and Reflectiveness**: The 5Rs is "a learning dispositions framewor k" developed by the Campaign for Learning, comprising "Readiness, Resourcefulness, Resilience, Responsibility and Reflectiveness". In Mind the Gap it structures the animation course: each session of the five-session programme was matched to one of the 5Rs, so that session 1 focused on Readiness, session 2 on Resourcefulness, session 3 on Responsibility, session 4 on Resilience and session 5 on Reflection. The progression through the programme was mapped so that metacognitive elements and the animation process were closely associated, maximising opportunities for transfer. (Dorsett et al. (2014))
