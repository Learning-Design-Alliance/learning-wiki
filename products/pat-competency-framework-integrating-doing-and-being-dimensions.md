## Description
A quality-improvement-developed competency framework for psychedelic-assisted therapy training that integrates observable practice with relational presence across five domains for postgraduate learners, educators, supervisors, and programme designers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Competence framework built on three interdependent learning pillars**: The article's competence framework treats competence as the holistic manifestation of an individual, encompassing knowledge, capacities, skills, attitudes and principles in an ethical and practical context. Learning rests on three interdependent pillars, 'knowing how to be', 'knowing how to do' and 'knowing how to know', with the teacher acting as mediator and catalyst and the student as active agent. (Eng. Fernanda Zapata Bascuñán et al. (2021))
