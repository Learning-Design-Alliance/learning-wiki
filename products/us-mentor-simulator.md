# US Mentor® simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
US Mentor® is a Simbionix® computer-based ultrasonography simulator for standardized hands-on training, offering anatomical visualization, procedural exercises, clinical cases, and performance tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **US Mentor ultrasonography simulator (Simbionix) used for standardized POCUS skills training**: The US Mentor® simulator from Simbionix® was the training platform for the pilot's simulator sessions. "This simulator allows the students to perform an ultrasonography with the aid of anatomical legends and three-dimensional diagrams", and its software standardizes exercise difficulty while "the total exercise time (in seconds) and distance covered with the probe (in meters) were recorded". It delivered hand–eye coordination games, image-setting exercises, and clinical cases for the abdominal POCUS procedure. (Borderie Baptiste et al. (2026))
