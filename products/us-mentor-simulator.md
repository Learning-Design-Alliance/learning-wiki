# US Mentor® simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
US Mentor® is a Simbionix® computer-based ultrasonography simulator for standardized hands-on training, offering anatomical visualization, procedural exercises, clinical cases, and performance tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **US Mentor ultrasonography simulator (Simbionix) used for standardized POCUS skills training**: The US Mentor® simulator from Simbionix® was the training platform for the pilot's simulator sessions. "This simulator allows the students to perform an ultrasonography with the aid of anatomical legends and three-dimensional diagrams", and its software standardizes exercise difficulty while "the total exercise time (in seconds) and distance covered with the probe (in meters) were recorded". It delivered hand–eye coordination games, image-setting exercises, and clinical cases for the abdominal POCUS procedure. (Borderie Baptiste et al. (2026))
- **Simbionix US Mentor Lung Module simulator for self-guided lung ultrasound practice**: A simulator-based educational tool used in the course's hands-on workshops: "The Simbionix Lung Module is an educational tool that offers simulated ultrasound imaging for various lung conditions, allowing for self-guided practice." It depicts normal lung anatomy, atelectasis, pneumonia, empyema, the Lung Point of pneumothorax, and B-lines, and integrates the BLUE Protocol decision tree for diagnosing lung pathologies. (Shitrit Itamar Ben et al. (2024))
- **US Mentor ultrasound simulator with virtual randomly moving fetus and scaffolded Task mode for fetal echocardiography training**: The 3D Systems Simbionix US Mentor consists of a mannequin, sham ultrasound probes, a touchscreen with built-in PC, and an electronic box; a magnetic transmitter on the mannequin lets the simulator locate the probe position and render a real-time image of a virtual fetus generated from MRI-volume data. The article states "The utilization of a virtual fetus enables the simulator to simulate fetal movements while scanning", a feature it calls unique to the US Mentor. Its Fetal Echo Module provides an aided "Task mode" for beginners (3D map, anatomic labels, written instructions, live feedback) and an examination-like "Case mode" where all aids can be deactivated, plus pathologic virtual fetuses with HLHS, TGA and Tetralogy of Fallot. (Janzing Paul et al. (2023))
- **Simbionix US Mentor ultrasound simulator with a virtual, randomly moving fetus for second-trimester training**: The Simbionix US Mentor is a high-end ultrasound simulator consisting of a mannequin, sham probes, touchscreen, footswitch and computing device; magnetic tracking of the probes lets it "render a real-time image of a simulated fetus" generated from MRI volume data. Its virtual fetus simulates fetal movements while scanning, a feature the authors call unique, and supports simulation of artifacts and malformations. The second-trimester module comprised six modules including a healthy randomly moving fetus and four fetuses with different malformations, with 23 standard views per ISUOG guidelines. (Freundt Paula et al. (2023))
