## Description
A six-domain competency framework proposed by Hertlein and colleagues for training and guiding practicing couple and family therapists in responsible AI-integrated relational practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AICMFT: a six-domain competency framework for AI in couple and family therapy**: The article introduces the AI Competencies for Couple and Family Therapy (AICMFT), a framework of "six proposed domains for AI Competencies for CMFT" that provide "a comprehensive skill set for therapists seeking to responsibly engage with AI in relational contexts." Specific competencies under each domain are worded using Bloom's taxonomy and mapped in a logic model to three informing frameworks. (Hertlein Katherine M. et al. (2026))
