# GREET checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 17-item reporting guideline developed by Phillips and colleagues for transparent reporting of evidence-based-practice educational interventions, used with its explanation-and-elaboration paper.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-stage development process for reporting guidelines: systematic review, Delphi consensus, and pilot psychometric testing**: The GREET checklist was developed following the Guidance for Developers of Health Research Reporting Guidelines through three broad stages: "1) systematic review, 2) consensus processes including Delphi survey; and 3) development and pilot testing". The systematic review identified 25 items relevant for reporting EBP educational interventions; a four-round Delphi survey expanded these to 39 items; three international consensus teleconferences retained 26 items, refined to 14 in the first draft; expert review added three more, yielding the final 17-item checklist. The TIDieR framework was adopted as a starting point to ensure consistency with intervention reporting. (Phillips Anna C. et al. (2016))
