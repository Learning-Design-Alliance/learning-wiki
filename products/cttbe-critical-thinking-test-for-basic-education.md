# CTTBE: Critical Thinking Test for Basic Education

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 48-item visual- and text-based test developed by Silva et al. to assess Portuguese 4th-grade students' observation, interpretation, analysis, and argumentation skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CTTBE: Critical Thinking Test for Basic Education (48-item researcher-built instrument)**: Because no Portuguese test assessed critical thinking for basic school students, the authors built the CTTBE from the national Profile of Students at the End of Compulsory Schooling, with items reviewed by two experts for relevance and clarity. It comprises "48 items with visual stimuli printed in color" and verbal stimuli (short authentic texts), assessing Observation (15 restricted-response items), Interpretation (14 multiple-choice), Analysis (18 multiple-choice), and Argumentation (one long-answer item). Reliability is acceptable (α = 0.72). (Silva et al. (2022))
