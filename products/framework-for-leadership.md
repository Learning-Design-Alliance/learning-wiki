## Description
The Framework for Leadership (FFL) is described as "Pennsylvania's tool for evaluating school leaders' effectiveness." It was piloted statewide over two school years, 2012/13 and 2013/14, and its measurement properties were examined across score variation, internal consistency, score stability, and concurrent validity. The element is the evaluation instrument itself, studied during pilot implementation rather than at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used within Pennsylvania's system for evaluating school leaders' effectiveness, as piloted in 2012/13 and 2013/14
#### Constraints
- Evidence about the tool comes from its pilot implementation period, not full-scale use

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
