> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A GitHub collection of Python-based Jupyter Notebooks created by Eugenio Tufino for introducing learners to word embeddings and GPT-2 through Google Colab.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Introduction-to-LLM Jupyter Notebook collection on GitHub**: A set of Python-based Jupyter Notebooks hosted in the GitHub folder https://github.com/etufino/Introduction-to-LLM, used in a three-hour module on AI in a Master's course on Teaching and Learning Physics at the University of Padua with approximately twenty students. The notebooks guide students through Word2Vec embeddings (similarity, analogies, PCA visualization, training with CBOW and Skip-Gram) and GPT-2 text generation (temperature, top-k, model size). The article notes that "An introductory notebook is also available for students with limited knowledge of Python and JNs." (Eugenio Tufino (2025))
