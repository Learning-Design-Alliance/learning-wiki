# International Young Physicists' Tournament (IYPT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An international high-school physics competition run by the IYPT community in which teams investigate open-ended problems, develop and test explanations, and defend their results in discussion.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **YPT as authentic inquiry-based learning aligned with Authentic Science and ISLE**: The article positions YPT within modern educational concepts, seeing it as closest to Authentic Science and the Investigative Science Learning Environment (ISLE), a well-proven learning system based on engaging students in a scientific reasoning process. YPT was born before these concepts became popular and developed without direct intent to apply specific didactic strategies. Its problems engage students in hypothetico-deductive reasoning: proposing explanations, building models, testing predictions against outcomes and explaining discrepancies. IYPT remains the only major international competition evaluating high-school students' inquiry-problem skills in physics. (Sergej Faletič et al. (2022))
