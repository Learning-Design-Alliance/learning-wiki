# Rocky Mountain Prep Family Advocacy Support Team (FAST)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Rocky Mountain Prep's Family Advocacy Support Team (FAST) is a referral-based family-support programme providing emergency basic-needs assistance and connections to longer-term resources for students' families.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rocky Mountain Prep Family Advocacy Support Team (FAST) emergency family supports**: FAST is Rocky Mountain Prep's referral-based team of teachers, social workers, and operations staff that responds to family hardship affecting student well-being. Staff with deep family relationships submit referrals; the team provides stopgap supports, including up to $300 in cash assistance for food, laundry, clothing, or other needs, while connecting families to long-term resources. Each campus has approximately $20,000 in flexible funds; in 2020-2021 FAST supported "nearly 125 families with resources for basic needs around food, shelter, mental health, and more." (National Charter School Resource Center et al. (2022))
