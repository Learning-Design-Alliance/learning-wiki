## Description
A weighted competency framework for Chinese cardiovascular physicians and residents, organizing clinical knowledge, practice-based skills and abilities, and motivation, values, and professionalism into layered indicators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Onion Model layering of the Chinese cardiovascular physician competency model into knowledge, skills/abilities, and motivation/values layers**: The article structures its final competency model on the Onion Model, dividing it "into three layers: knowledge, skills/abilities, and motivation/values". The outermost layer holds professional knowledge competencies (Clinical Skills and Medical Services, Disease Prevention and Health Promotion, Information and Management Skills); the middle layer holds skills acquired through practice or training (Medical Knowledge and Lifelong Learning Ability, Interpersonal Communication Skills, Teamwork Skills, Scientific Research Skills); the innermost layer is Core Values and Professionalism, described as "the driving force behind their commitment to their work". (Sun Shilin et al. (2025))
