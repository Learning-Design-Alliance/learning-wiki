# Tool #3: Assessing Schools for Indicators of Distress rating scales

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Aimee Evan et al.'s rating-scale assessment helps charter school authorizers and schools evaluate organizational indicators of school distress using school-level evidence and reflection prompts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tool #3: Assessing Schools for Indicators of Distress rating scales**: Tool #3 "provides an easy-to-use assessment to determine how schools fare on each of the indicators and the extent to which they are showing signs of distress." For each of the seven categories it presents 0–5 rating scales contrasting healthy and distressed poles, such as a leader who "Has the respect and trust of the staff" versus one who "Is not respected by the staff," plus additional user-defined measures and reflection prompts on strengths, weaknesses, and needed resources. (Aimee Evan et al. (2021))
