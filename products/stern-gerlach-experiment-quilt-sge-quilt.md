# Stern-Gerlach Experiment QuILT (SGE QuILT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A research-based quantum-mechanics tutorial package developed by Guangtian Zhu and colleagues that combines guided prediction, computer simulations, conceptual questions, warm-up activities, and homework for learning the Stern–Gerlach experiment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Stern-Gerlach Experiment QuILT (Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial)**: The SGE QuILT is a research-based tutorial for advanced undergraduate quantum mechanics that builds on students' prior knowledge and difficulties. "It uses a guided approach to help students build a knowledge structure about the SGE" and adapts the Open Source Physics SPINS program, with students predicting before each simulation and reconciling differences from observations. It covers measurement, state preparation, superposition versus mixture, physical versus Hilbert space, and choice of basis, and comes with a warm-up exercise and homework. (Guangtian Zhu et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [In a preliminary class evaluation, average post-test performance after the SGE QuILT (92%) improved over pre-test performance (53%) among 22 undergraduates with traditional instruction](../claims/sge-quilt-preliminary-gains.md) [+M]
- [Many advanced students incorrectly predict no beam splitting in an SGE whose field gradient is orthogonal to the spin state, connecting physical-space field direction with Hilbert-space state vectors](../claims/sge-orthogonal-gradient-no-splitting-difficulty.md) [+M]
