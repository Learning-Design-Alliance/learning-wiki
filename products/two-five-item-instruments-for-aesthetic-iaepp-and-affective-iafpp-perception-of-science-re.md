# Two five-item instruments for aesthetic (IaePP) and affective (IafPP) perception of science-related pictures

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed two-part six-point rating instrument by Zähringer et al. for assessing lower-secondary students' aesthetic and affective perceptions of science-related pictures.
