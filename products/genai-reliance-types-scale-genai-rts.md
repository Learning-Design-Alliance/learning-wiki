# GenAI Reliance Types Scale (GenAI-RTS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 20-item self-report scale developed by Hossain et al. to measure undergraduate students' reliance on generative AI in academic writing across strategic, instrumental, dependent, and dialogic dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GenAI Reliance Types Scale (GenAI-RTS), a 20-item instrument measuring four reliance types across five facets**: The GenAI-RTS is a 20-item self-report instrument measuring reliance on generative AI in academic writing, with all items rated on 7-point Likert scales (1 = Strongly Disagree, 7 = Strongly Agree). It operationalizes four theoretically derived types across five measurement facets: "four Deliberate Use items informed by metacognitive awareness theory" and four Critical Evaluation items adapted from critical AI literacy competencies, plus four items each for Instrumental, Dependent, and Dialogic reliance. Subscale scores are item means (range 1–7), and the Strategic composite weights its two facets equally. (Hossain et al. (2026))
- **LLM Reliance Scale: 20-item, four-subscale instrument distinguishing reliance type from reliance intensity**: The LLM Reliance Scale is a 20-item instrument developed in this study across four subscales: Strategic (8 items, with Deliberate Use and Critical Evaluation facets), Instrumental (4 items on bounded mechanical use), Dialogic (4 items on iterative co-construction with AI as a thinking partner), and Dependent (4 items measuring "cognitive abdication; wholesale outsourcing of writing tasks with minimal critical oversight"). All items use 7-point Likert scales. It was developed through construct definition, item generation, expert content review, pilot administration (n=143), and main-study psychometric evaluation, and is designed to measure reliance type rather than frequency of use. (Shahin Hossain (2026))
