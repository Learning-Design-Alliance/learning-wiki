## Description
A digital training prototype and accompanying manual developed by the SCriPT project to support supervision, skills development, and crisis-assessment and intervention training for CAMHS crisis-team clinicians.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SCriPT digital training prototype for CAMHS crisis team clinicians**: The SCriPT (Strengthening Crisis Team Provision for Young People via Technology-enhanced Assessment and Training) project developed a "digital training prototype to support CAMHS crisis team clinicians through supervision and skills development." The prototype is publicly viewable online with an associated manual hosted on OSF. It was informed by the Delphi consensus findings on core competencies and outcomes, and aims to support workforce sustainability by strengthening training, supervision and skills development. (Clau N et al. (2026))
