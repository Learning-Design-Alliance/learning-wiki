## Description
A 32-item, four-point Likert questionnaire developed to measure teachers' beliefs about reformed science teaching and learning across four subscales.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BARSTL questionnaire for measuring reformed science teaching and learning beliefs**: The Beliefs about Reformed Science Teaching and Learning (BARSTL) questionnaire measures teachers' beliefs about reformed science teaching and learning. It consists of "32 questionnaire items, which had 4-point Likert scale" plus 5 demographic questions, and "four subscales including (a) the nature of the science curriculum (NOSC), (b) characteristics of teachers and the learning environment (CTLE), (c) lesson design and implementation (LDI), and (d) how people learn about science (HPLS)". This study administered it online via Qualtrics to US and Turkish teachers. (Burhan Ozfidan et al. (2017))
