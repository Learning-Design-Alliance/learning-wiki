## Description
EdNet is a large-scale dataset of student interactions, problems, lectures, and skill tags collected by Santa for TOEIC preparation and released for educational data-mining and knowledge-tracing research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EdNet dataset of student-system interactions from Santa**: EdNet is a publicly released dataset of student-system interactions collected over more than two years by Santa, a multi-platform AI tutoring service for TOEIC preparation with approximately 780,000 students in South Korea. It provides "a total of 131,441,538 interactions collected from 784,309 students" plus 13,169 problems and 1,021 lectures tagged with 293 types of skills. The data are released under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license for research purposes only. (Choi et al. (2020))
