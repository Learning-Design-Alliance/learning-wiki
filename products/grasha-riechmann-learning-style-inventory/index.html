Grasha-Riechmann Learning Style Inventory¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 60-item Likert-scale assessment developed by Anthony Grasha to measure students' social-interaction learning-style preferences across independent-dependent, avoidant-participant, and collaborative-competitive dimensions; this study used a Turkish adaptation.

Components¶

  • Grasha-Riechmann Learning Style Inventory (Turkish adaptation) as the study's learning style measure: The study used the Grasha-Riechmann Learning Style Inventory, a 60-item five-point Likert scale adapted by Sarıtaş and Süral (2010) with six dimensions of ten items each, with a printed Cronbach Alpha of .802. The article describes it as "a social interaction scale" positioning styles as independent-dependent, avoidant-participant, and collaborative-competitive on three major lines, each representing two opposite directions. Styles are scored at low, medium, and high levels using printed cut-off ranges (Table 2). (Balta (2018))
