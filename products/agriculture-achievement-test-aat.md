# Agriculture Achievement Test (AAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 20-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by the researchers to measure senior secondary students' learning of maize husbandry in agricultural science.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Agriculture Achievement Test (AAT), a 20-item multiple-choice instrument**: The AAT is a measurement instrument self-developed by the researchers, consisting of "20 multiple choice test items based on the topics in this study (maize husbandry)". It was validated by experts in Educational Evaluation and Agricultural Science for face and content validity, and subjected to test-retest, yielding a reliability coefficient of 0.85 using the Kuder Richardson (KR 20) method. It was administered for one hour as both pre-test and post-test to both groups. (Abiola Lateef Olori et al. (2016))
