## Description
LASSO is a free web-based platform hosted by the Learning Assistant Alliance for faculty to administer online research-based STEM assessments and obtain response and pre/post summary reports.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LASSO platform for administering research-based concept inventories online**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform is a free online tool hosted on the Learning Assistant Alliance website that allows faculty to administer research-based concept inventories as pre- and posttests to their students online. Faculty provide course-level information, select assessments, and upload student names and emails; students receive unique links to complete tests at the beginning and end of instruction. The platform provides downloadable student responses and a summary report with plots of pre- and posttest scores, normalized learning gains (g), and effect sizes (Cohen's d). (Jayson M. Nissen et al. (2018))
