# LASSO platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
LASSO is a free web-based platform hosted by the Learning Assistant Alliance for faculty to administer online research-based STEM assessments and obtain response and pre/post summary reports.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LASSO platform for administering research-based concept inventories online**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform is a free online tool hosted on the Learning Assistant Alliance website that allows faculty to administer research-based concept inventories as pre- and posttests to their students online. Faculty provide course-level information, select assessments, and upload student names and emails; students receive unique links to complete tests at the beginning and end of instruction. The platform provides downloadable student responses and a summary report with plots of pre- and posttest scores, normalized learning gains (g), and effect sizes (Cohen's d). (Jayson M. Nissen et al. (2018))
- **LASSO (Learning About STEM Student Outcomes) platform and research database**: The LASSO platform "hosts, administers, scores, and analyzes student pre- and posttest assessments online to provide instructors with objective feedback on their courses". Data from participating courses are anonymized, aggregated, and made available to researchers with approved IRB protocols. This study drew three years of introductory physics course data from the LASSO database, yielding pre- and posttest scores for 4,365 students in 93 courses using the FCI or FMCE. (Daniel Caravez et al. (2017))
- **LASSO platform for administering, scoring, and interpreting research-based assessments online**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform, developed by the Learning Assistant Alliance, hosts RBAs online so they can be administered outside of class, "to be promptly and automatically scored," with a summary report to help instructors interpret results. It automatically aggregates all data and makes it available for research. In this study it delivered the Force Concept Inventory, Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism, and Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey, and produced data equivalent to paper administration. (Jayson M. Nissen et al. (2017))
- **LASSO platform for online administration, scoring, and analysis of research-based assessments**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform is a free online system that "hosts, administers, scores, and analyzes RBAs online". Instructors set up a course by answering questions, selecting assessments, and uploading a roster with student emails; students receive an email with a unique link to their assessment page. Instructors can track participation in real time and generate reminder emails, receive a post-posttest report, and access all student responses; participating-course data are anonymized, aggregated, and made available to researchers with approved IRB protocols. (Nissen et al. (2017))
- **LASSO platform and national database of science course outcomes**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform is an online platform that collects large-scale, multi-institution data by administering, scoring, and analyzing pretest and posttest research-based assessments. It makes an anonymized version of its database of course and student data available to researchers for those who consent to share it. In this study the LASSO database supplied data from 13,857 students in 187 first-semester college physics courses across 31 institutions, including the Force Concept Inventory and Force and Motion Conceptual Evaluation scores. (Van Dusen et al. (2019))
- **LASSO platform for administering and analyzing research-based pre/post assessments**: The Learning About STEM Student Outcomes (LASSO) platform is an online assessment database that "hosts, administers, scores, and analyzes student pre- and post tests online to provide instructors with objective feedback on student learning in their courses." When creating a course, instructors answer questions about their course, such as whether students engage in collaborative learning and the primary use of LAs, and receive reports on class performance. The database records student responses and course data, which are anonymized, aggregated, and made available to researchers. This study drew three years of introductory physics course data from LASSO. (Xochith Herrera et al. (2018))
- **LASSO (Learning About STEM Student Outcomes) platform**: The LASSO platform is an online system hosting research-based assessments that supplied the data for this study. The article describes it: "The LASSO platform administers, scores, and analyzes research-based assessments online to build a multi-institution database." The database is anonymized and filtered to students who consented to share data, and it is free to instructors and students. The study drew 4673 students in 95 first-semester introductory physics courses at 18 institutions from it, with the CLASS administered as a pretest in the first week and post-test in the last week. (Jayson M. Nissen et al. (2021))
