## Description
Emberio is a custom web-based gamification platform developed by medical-education experts, embryology faculty, and software developers for delivering timed, progressive, multimedia-supported course activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A custom web-based gamification platform for the embryology course with timed question sets, progression thresholds, leaderboards, badges, and avatars**: The platform at https://emberio.ir/ was "a custom-designed, web-based application developed specifically for this study by a collaboration between medical education experts, embryology faculty members, and a professional software development team", built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (React framework), PHP, and MySQL. Content was delivered as multiple-choice, matching, and fill-in-the-blank question sets of 5 to 15 items, each timed at 30 seconds, and students were required to attain "at least 75% of the total score for each step to advance to the next level", with leaderboards, achievement badges, avatars, stars, and medals tracking progress. (Rahimipour Marzieh et al. (2025))
