# Goal Net and Multi-Agent Development Environment (MADE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A visual AI knowledge-representation and agent-development environment developed by Yu et al. for modelling goal-and-activity networks and constructing trackable learning paths.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Goal Net methodology and MADE: AI knowledge representation tool for modelling learning paths**: Goal Net, with its associated Multi-Agent Development Environment (MADE), is an AI knowledge representation tool designed to model logic flows guiding software agents visually without lengthy code. Based on goal setting theory, it "allows users to express a complex process (e.g. organizing a set of learning tasks) as a network of goals and activities", tracking transitions and goal achievement. The authors illustrate it with a learning path for an HCI course built from micro-MOOCs, quizzes and forum discussions. (Yu et al. (2017))
