Zirkus Empathico¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Zirkus Empathico is a touchscreen application developed for socio-emotional competence training through emotion-recognition, empathy, and prosocial-behavior activities.

Components¶

  • Zirkus Empathico touchscreen application for socio-emotional competence training: Zirkus Empathico is a touchscreen application that "targets awareness and differentiation of own and others ' emotions, empathy, and prosocial behaviors through interactions with naturalistic video sequences of facial expressions and social situations". It comprises four modules: a virtual emotion manikin for specifying own emotional states on arousal and valence dimensions, facial expression labeling, identifying emotions from emotion-eliciting video contexts, and choosing prosocial reactions to others' emotions. Developed from autism-specific behavior therapy principles, it was evaluated here with typically developing preschoolers at home over 6 weeks. (Naumann et al. (2023))

Claims¶

  • Six-week Zirkus Empathico training increased parent-rated empathy (EMK 3-6) but not GEM-rated or child-assessed empathy in preschoolers [+M]
  • Zirkus Empathico improved child-assessed emotion recognition but not parent-rated emotion recognition in preschoolers [+M]
  • Zirkus Empathico increased parent-rated prosocial behavior but not child-assessed prosocial behavior [+M]
  • Zirkus Empathico reduced parent-rated behavioral problems in preschoolers [+M]
  • Training group showed larger P3 amplitudes to happy versus neutral and angry faces post-training; early ERP components were unaffected [+M]
  • Exploratory 3-month follow-up suggested a sleeper effect on parent-rated empathy, but no maintained gains in other outcomes [~W]
