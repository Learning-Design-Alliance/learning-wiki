# Custom 3D electrostatics assessment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A researcher-designed pretest/posttest multiple-choice assessment suite created by C. D. Porter and colleagues to measure introductory physics students’ understanding of three-dimensional electric fields.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Custom 3D electrostatics assessment with Cronbach's alpha of 0.91**: A researcher-designed assessment suite for three-dimensional understanding of electric fields, consisting of 13 pretest and 11 posttest multiple-choice questions, some posed on a 2D computer screen and some posed in VR with answers recorded on a computer. The authors report that "a reliability analysis in SPSS reveals a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.91" and that a factor analysis revealed a single factor with eigenvalue 4.8 explaining 43% of the variance. Items address determining the direction of the electric field at non-coplanar locations and features of the vector field such as divergence. (C. D. Porter et al. (2020))
