CPMP-Tools¶

Description¶

CPMP-Tools is mathematical software developed for the Core-Plus Mathematics Project to support graphing, problem solving, modeling, and inquiry-oriented mathematics instruction.

Components¶

  • CPMP-Tools mathematical software and graphing calculators for Core-Plus Mathematics: The review lists the technology requirements for implementing Core-Plus Mathematics as graphing calculators and CPMP-Tools, or similar mathematical software tools. These tools support the curriculum's problem-based, inquiry-oriented instruction, which focuses on problem solving and mathematical modeling with a balanced treatment of concepts and procedures. (Core-Plus Mathematics evidence review (2019))
