## Description
A named aquatic-education teaching methodology introduced by Moreno and Gutiérrez that uses play, exploration, problem-solving, and learner choice to develop water competence and autonomy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Comprehensive Aquatic Method (MAC) as a constructivist, student-centred approach to aquatic education**: The MAC, introduced by Moreno and Gutiérrez in 1998, is described as "a constructivist approach that centers on student interests and needs, promoting autonomy and interaction". It employs play as a medium, encourages problem-solving and exploration in aquatic environments, and develops knowledge, skills and attitudes contributing to student autonomy and personal growth. In the study it is measured with a validated 26-item scale and tested as a predictor of, and predictor by, teaching self-efficacy. In the authors' reviewer response they clarify MAC is an integrative approach combining constructivism, cognitivism and student-centred elements rather than solely constructivist. (Fonseca-Pinto R et al. (2024))
