Basic Science Achievement Test (BSAT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The BSAT is a 30-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by Ukpai Patricia Ogonnaya and colleagues to measure Nigerian junior secondary students' learning of three basic-science topics.

Components¶

  • Basic Science Achievement Test (BSAT), a 30-item multiple-choice instrument: The researchers developed the Basic Science Achievement Test (BSAT) as "a 30 item multiple choice test" built from the content covered in the study, with items drawn using a table of specification. It covered three topics in the lesson note plan: the living components of the environment, grouping of living things, and classification of living things. The instrument was subjected to face and content validation, and its Kuder Richardson coefficient of internal consistency is 0.97. (Ukpai Patricia Ogonnaya et al. (2016))
