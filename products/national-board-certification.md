## Description
A rigorous professional certification programme run by the National Board for Professional Teaching Standards in which teachers demonstrate advanced practice through a portfolio and assessment-centre examinations.

- **National Board for Professional Teaching Standards (NBPTS) certification process**: NBPTS was "established to elevate the teaching profession by providing an advanced, rigorous level of certifica- tion," beginning in 1995. Certification involves an extensive portfolio with unedited classroom videotapes, analysis of student work, and evidence of work with families and colleagues, plus six assessment-center examinations scored by trained teachers; Educational Testing Service administers the assessments. The report evaluates Chicago NBCTs and candidates on professional development, commitment, leadership roles, and principal ratings. (Penny Bender Sebring et al. (2004))
