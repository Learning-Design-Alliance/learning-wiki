# COAMFTE Standards Version 12.5

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
COAMFTE Standards Version 12.5 is an accreditation standards framework issued by the Commission on Accreditation for Marriage and Family Therapy Education that specifies entry-level training and competency requirements for marriage and family therapy programs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **COAMFTE Standards Version 12.5 mandating entry-level teletherapy training**: COAMFTE Standards Version 12.5 are accreditation standards for marriage and family therapy training programs that, per the article, "explicitly set parameters for the inclusion of entry‐level training and experience in teletherapy practice," shifting telebehavioral health from an optional skill to a required professional competency and normalizing virtual and on-campus site visits. (Hertlein Katherine M. et al. (2026))
