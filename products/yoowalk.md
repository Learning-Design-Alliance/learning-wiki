## Description
Yoowalk is a browser-based virtual-world platform provided and operated by the Yoowalk service for text-based interaction with virtual-world residents.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Yoowalk browser-based virtual world used for second conditional practice**: Yoowalk (http://www.yoowalk.com/) is a browser-based virtual world that the article describes as providing access to online virtual worlds for less resourced schools without downloading and installing software. In the study, treatment students logged on to Yoowalk, used example second conditional questions to talk to residents, and conducted a questionnaire among its residents. The article notes browser-based virtual environments offer only text-based chat communication and lack voice, unlike more sophisticated worlds such as Second Life. (Mariusz Kruk (2012))

### Claims
