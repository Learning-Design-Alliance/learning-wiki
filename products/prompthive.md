# PromptHive

## Description
PromptHive is an open-source collaborative prompt-authoring interface developed by Mohi Reza et al. for subject-matter experts to create, test, share, and track prompt variations used to generate hints in OATutor.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PromptHive generative AI authoring tool for instructor material generation and refinement**: PromptHive is a generative AI authoring tool cited by the article as early evidence that embedding AI-assisted design capabilities into instructor workflows is viable. The article states that "the generative AI authoring tool PromptHive, which supports instructors in generating and refining instructional materials, achieved a usability score of 89 out of 100 among subject matter experts." It is offered as an example of minimizing instructor friction by supporting low-effort generation and refinement of materials. (Kwak et al. (2026))

### Claims

- [Subject matter experts rated PromptHive as highly usable (SUS 89/100) with a large reduction in perceived cognitive workload versus manual authoring](../claims/prompthive-high-usability-reduced-cognitive-load.md) [+M]
- [Hints generated with PromptHive produced learning gains on par with hints manually authored by subject matter experts without generative AI](../claims/prompthive-hints-learning-gains-on-par-with-human.md) [+M]
- [Experts iterated extensively on prompts, averaging 30 executions and 10 library commits, with most reaching their final textbook-level prompt in 3 to 6 iterations](../claims/prompthive-prompt-iteration-behavior.md) [+W]
- [Experts perceived AI-assisted hints as more consistent than human-only hints and reported retaining control over generation](../claims/experts-perceived-consistency-and-control.md) [+W]
