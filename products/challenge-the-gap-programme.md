## Description
A year-long school collaboration programme run in England that supports disadvantaged pupils through research-informed workshops, in-school interventions, cross-school practice development, and transfer of expertise from Lead to Accelerator schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Challenge the Gap programme components and trio structure**: Challenge the Gap is a school collaboration programme with four main components: after-school workshops drawing on published research and evidenced practice, focused in-school interventions with a selected cohort of disadvantaged pupils, cross-school collaboration and practice development, and practical tools and resources for use in schools. Schools work in 'trios' comprising one 'Lead' school and two 'Accelerator' schools, targeting 12–15 disadvantaged pupils per school with a view to rolling out best practice more widely. The programme lasts for a year, during which expertise is transferred from Lead to Accelerator schools. (West et al. (2017))
