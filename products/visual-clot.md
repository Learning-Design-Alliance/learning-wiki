# Visual Clot

## Description
Visual Clot is a 3D-animated visualization software tool that translates viscoelastic coagulation test results into an interpretable schematic blood-clot display for clinical teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Situation awareness-oriented, user-centered design as the conceptual basis of Visual Clot**: The article grounds Visual Clot in Endsley's user-centered design principles, Wittgenstein's philosophy of logical representation, and NASA's guidance on organization of information. Situation awareness "breaks down into three separate phases (1) the perception of environmental elements in the current situation within a volume of time and space, (2) understanding their meaning, and (3) their projection in the near future." Based on this, Visual Clot data are visually represented, preprocessed, and simplified, with the goal of providing situational information quickly and with minimal cognitive load. (Gasciauskaite G et al. (2024))
