## Description
Grammar for Writing is a contextualised grammar-and-writing curriculum developed for teachers to improve Year 6 pupils' writing by linking linguistic choices to their effects on readers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Grammar for Writing intervention (15 contextualised grammar sessions for Year 6)**: Grammar for Writing is a literacy curriculum intervention aiming to improve the writing of Year 6 pupils through contextualised grammar teaching. It "encourages pupils to improve how their writing communicates with the reader by making connections between a linguistic feature and the effect it has on writing, rather than by focusing on grammatical inaccuracies." In this trial it comprised 15 sequential guided writing sessions delivered over four weeks, with teachers receiving three days of training, and was tested as both a universal and targeted intervention at an estimated cost of £20 per pupil. (Torgerson et al. (2014))
