## Description
P3 is a federal initiative supported by six agencies: "the U.S. Departments of Education, Health and Human Services, and Labor, the Corporation for National and Community Service, and the Institute for Museum and Library Services." Like YARH, it focused on disconnected youth and building evidence, and its grantees received evaluation technical assistance from the same liaisons who served both the grantee/service providing group and the local evaluator.
