# New General Service List (new-GSL)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A corpus-derived English word list compiled by Brezina and Gablasova from four large language corpora for materials development and lexical-difficulty analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New General Service List (new-GSL) as the corpus basis for materials and testing**: The new-GSL is the specific corpus-derived word list the study applies both pedagogically and analytically. It is described as "a robust comparison of four language corpora (LOB, BNC, BE06, and EnTenTen12) of a total size of over 12 billion running words," compiled by Brezina and Gablasova. The article used it to select and edit the experimental group's teaching materials, to evaluate the lexical difficulty of pretest and posttest articles, and to design the tests. (Jing Shi (2017))
