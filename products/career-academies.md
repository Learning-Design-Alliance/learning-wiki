# Career Academies

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Career Academies is a school-within-a-school programme model that combines small learning communities, career-themed academic and career courses, work experience, and partnerships with employers, higher education, and the community.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Career Academies program element**: Career Academies are a dropout prevention program for high school youth organized as a school-within-a-school with a career theme such as health care, finance, technology, communications, or public service. Students "take both career-related and academic courses and acquire work experience through partnerships with local employers." The National Career Academy Coalition's standards of practice identify "three basic features" as key elements: small learning communities, a focused career-themed curriculum, and partnerships with employers, higher education institutions, and the community. ## Description
Career Academies are a dropout prevention program for high school youth organized as a school-within-a-school with a career theme such as health care, finance, technology, communications, or public service. Students "take both career-related and academic courses and acquire work experience through partnerships with local employers." ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A school-within-a-school structure with a career theme, academic and career-oriented courses, and work-based learning activities inside and outside of school
#### Constraints
- The report focuses on Career Academies with a school-within-a-school structure; cost was estimated in 2004 at $600 per pupil more than a district's average per-pupil expenditure, and more recent cost information was not available

### Target Learners
- High-school aged youth at risk of dropping out, including students entering ninth or tenth grade from low-income or single-parent households

### Target Learning Goals
- High school completion and dropout prevention
- Career awareness and understanding of the world of work
- Meeting core academic requirements for graduation and college preparation
