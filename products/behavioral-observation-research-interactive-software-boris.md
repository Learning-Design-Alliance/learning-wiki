# Behavioral Observation Research Interactive Software (BORIS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
BORIS is open-source software for coding behavioral states and events in video and audio recordings, developed for observational research and used across research settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BORIS video coding for speaking time**: Behavioral Observation Research Interactive Software (BORIS) is an open source video and audio coding software originally designed to analyze animal behaviors, repurposed here to code when individual students start and stop speaking. The article states it "allows for coding of states, e.g. when a student is talking, and events, e.g. when a student says a specific word." Coding rules treated interjections as speaking when adjacent to other words and pauses shorter than 0.3 seconds as talking. (Ben Archibeque et al. (2017))
