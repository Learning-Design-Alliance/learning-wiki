## Description
A collaborative online practice assessment document produced by 28 UK universities for assessing pre-registration nursing students against the NMC Future Nurse standards through practice-, supervisory-, and academic-assessor judgments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MYEPAD online practice assessment document for UK student nurses**: MYEPAD (Midlands, Yorkshire and East Practice Assessment Document) is an online practice assessment document produced collaboratively between 28 universities, used in this study as the data source. The article states it "was developed to ensure that student nurses are prepared to successfully meet the Future Nurse: Standards of Proficiency for Registered Nurses" at registration, and it details the level of performance students must demonstrate at the end of each year of study. Sections are structured so practice assessors comment on students' 'knowledge, skills, attitudes and values'. (Bond et al. (2025))
