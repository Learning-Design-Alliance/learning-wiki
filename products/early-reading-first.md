## Description
A U.S. federal grant programme that supported preschools in improving teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments for young children's language and literacy development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Early Reading First grant program for preschool language and literacy**: Early Reading First was a federal grant program for preschools, evaluated in a national report to Congress. Grants were "designed to enhance teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments in preschools" so that young children, especially those from low-income families, begin school with the skills needed for academic success. (Jackson et al. (2007))
- **Enhance preschool teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments to prepare children for school**: The Early Reading First program embodies the principle that improving preschool quality on three fronts — teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments — helps children start school ready.
