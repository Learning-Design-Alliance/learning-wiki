# Particle physics masterclass model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (3 associational, 2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
A CERN- and Fermilab-associated outreach programme in which high school students analyze real collider data with physicist mentors and compare findings through an inter-institute videoconference.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **International Masterclasses**: International Masterclasses are all-day events in which students analyze event displays from LHC data and build statistical results including mass histograms, experiencing being "particle physicists for a day." The paper states that in International Masterclasses "students have the opportunity to analyze event displays from LHC data and use these to build statistical results, including mass histograms." Participants who have done this would likely find a discovery mass plot accessible. (Kenneth Cecire (2015))
- **International Masterclasses in particle physics (Pisa INFN implementation)**: The International Masterclasses for Particle Physics give high-school students the opportunity to "be particle physicists for a day": they attend introductory lectures on the standard model, accelerators and detectors, make measurements on real data collected at CERN by the LHC experiments, and present findings in a video linkup with CERN or Fermilab and student groups in other countries. The Pisa INFN unit has participated since 2005, hosting more than a hundred students aged 18-19 each year, selected by schools with attention to the school's gender distribution. A questionnaire given after the videoconference assesses the day's response. (Sandra Leone (2016))
- **The International Masterclass day: presentations, paired data analysis, tutor-supported measurement, group discussion, and a videoconference**: International Masterclasses follow a recurring one-day structure for high school students. Students attend presentations on experimental particle physics and the measurement they will analyze, then examine authentic event displays event-by-event, categorizing events and combining results. The article states that "students work in pairs—two to a computer and data set—to check each other as they work as well as make the analysis more efficient." The day ends with a discussion with tutors and a videoconference with up to four other institutions and physicist moderators at CERN, Fermilab, or TRIUMF. (K. Cecire (2017))
- **One-day particle physics masterclass: authentic data analysis in pairs with circulating physicist mentors**: A one-day event at a university or laboratory in which high-school students analyze authentic LHC event displays with physicist mentors. The day includes an ice-breaker, lessons on the Standard Model and data analysis, and a facility tour; students then "work in pairs (two students at one computer) to analyze events to help and challenge each other", relying on two-fold coincidence of opinions, while physicists circulate to help, and the day ends with a CERN-moderated videoconference. (K. Cecire et al. (2017))
- **CMS WZH masterclass measurement incorporating four-lepton and diphoton Higgs candidate events**: The CMS masterclass evolved from a J/Ψ measurement (2010) to a W and Z measurement including low-mass mesons, and after the Higgs discovery to the current CMS WZH measurement when 'a small number of four -lepton and diphoton Higgs candidate events became available and were incorporated.' 'In 2018, nearly 300 four -lepton events became available, showing evidence of Z γ and ZZ events as well as a far smaller sample close to the Higgs mass.' Work is underway so students can make meaningful two- and four-lepton mass plots. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
- **International Day of Women and Girls in Science masterclasses for young women with women as role models**: Since 2016, special masterclasses for women and girls have been held on and around February 11, 'united by CERN-moderated videoconferences which focus not only on the physics but also on issues and opportunities for women in science.' The IMC effort emphasizes a friendly physics environment for young women and women as role models: most participants are high school girls, women are well represented among tutors and organizers, and the moderators at CERN are women. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
- **Masterclass day structured around introductory presentation, laboratory tour, authentic data analysis, and videoconference**: The article defines the International Masterclass day as built from four fixed components: 'The International Masterclass day is structured around the following elements: Introductory presentation, Tour of laboratory facilities, Analyzing authentic HEP data, Videoconference.' Teachers prepare students with short QuarkNet activities, a physicist mentor introduces the physics before students make a measurement based on authentic data from a contemporary experiment, and the day closes with a videoconference joining participants at other locations, often in other countries. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
- **MicroBooNE detector commissioning masterclass using liquid argon TPC data**: Fermilab MicroBooNE physicists are collaborating with QuarkNet on a detector commissioning masterclass built on the liquid argon time-projection chamber (TPC) technology, which the article calls sophisticated and sensitive. 'students can, for example, examine cosmic ray muon tracks in the detector to determine the purity of the argon and the overall response of the detector.' The MicroBooNE masterclass will be tested in International Masterclasses 2020, and MicroBooNE uses and enables students to test the key liquid argon TPC technology on which DUNE will rely. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
- **Particle therapy masterclass using the open source matRad toolkit for treatment planning**: Pioneered by GSI Darmstadt and the Heidelberg Ion Therapy Center, the particle therapy masterclass is positioned for students more oriented toward life sciences or motivated by applications of science to society. 'Student participants examine cancer s cenarios and plan treatment with photon, carbon nucleus , and proton therapies , calculating optimal doses using the open source matRad toolkit.' The masterclass will be introduced into International Masterclasses in 2020. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
- **International Masterclasses (IMC)**: International Masterclasses invite high school teachers to bring their students to universities or labs to be "particle physicists for a day", where they learn about the standard model, problems in particle physics, and specific experiments from which they analyze authentic data. Students combine and share results in final video conferences hosted by CERN, Fermilab, KEK, GSI or TRIUMF. IMC happen in and around March each year, worldwide, and are organized by IPPOG; example masterclasses include ATLAS, CMS, ALICE, LHCb, MINERvA, Belle II, Particle Therapy and Darkside. (Bardeen M G et al. (2022))
- **International Masterclasses: authentic-data particle physics events where students become scientists for a day**: International Masterclasses (IMC) are experimental particle physics education and outreach events for high school students aged 15 to 19 years, hosted at universities and research institutes. In the program, "students become “scientists for a day”": they attend introductory talks on the Standard Model, detectors and accelerators, then analyze authentic data from experiments such as the LHC, Belle II, Fermilab neutrino experiments and GSI particle therapy. The program is coordinated internationally and includes institutes in Egypt, Algeria, Morocco, Sao Tomé and Principe, and South Africa. (Uta Bilowa (2022))
- **Masterclass day: talks, tour, measurement introduction, data analysis, and international videoconference**: The masterclass day follows a structured sequence: registration and welcome, an introduction to particle physics, a second talk or tour, an introduction to the measurement, lunch with physicists, a two-hour data analysis block, local combination of results and discussion, and an international videoconference. The article prints this as a Sample Agenda, noting that "The masterclass day varies depending on the institute and time zone but generally follows a plan similar to this Sample Agenda". The design moves learners from orientation, to hands-on measurement, to combining and discussing results with peers at other institutes. (Uta Bilowa (2022))
- **ALICE masterclass program based on analysis of real data, updated into fully web-based formats including strangeness analysis and RAA measurement via python notebooks**: The ALICE particle physics masterclass program has long been based on analysis of real data with a customised ROOT framework. These were updated into web-based formats already in 2019, including "a fully web-basedanalysisofstrangeparticlesin ALICE" and "the measurement of the nuclear modiﬁcation factor via python notebooks". The web formats allow participants over wide areas with high accessibility to take part in masterclasses remotely. (Christian Klein-Bösing (2022))
- **International Particle Physics Masterclasses: one-day hands-on outreach events at research institutes**: The International Particle Physics Masterclasses are one-day non-formal learning events for upper secondary pupils held at universities or research institutes running active particle physics research. The day includes introductory lectures, a roughly two-hour hands-on analysis of data from a major particle physics experiment, and an international videoconference in which up to five institutes discuss results with moderators from a major laboratory. The article reports that "each year about 13,000 pupils participate in IMC that take place at some 220 institutes from 55 countries", with coordination by IPPOG since 2005. (Júlia Kekeláková et al. (2023))
- **Hands-on analysis of authentic experiment data with pooled results**: The core Masterclass design is a precisely formulated data-analysis task performed on pre-processed data from a major HEP experiment, using provided software tools. "An important feature of the activity is that it is performed on (pre-processed) data that indeed come from some major HEP experiment." Participants individually analyse events (e.g., 50 collision events with HYPATIA), then results are merged across participants and institutions so the Z-boson peak at 91 GeV gains clarity as more data are included, teaching the value of statistics and collaboration. (Boris Tomašík et al. (2024))
- **International Particle Physics Masterclasses (IMC)**: An annual, globally coordinated outreach event since 2005 in which upper-secondary pupils visit research institutions, hear introductory lectures on the Standard Model and experimental techniques, perform a hands-on data-analysis exercise, and join a closing videoconference. "Annually, about 13 000 pupils participate in some of the events that are hosted at more than 200 research institutions worldwide." Coordination is ensured by IPPOG, hosted by CERN. In Slovakia and the Czech Republic coverage is good, with physicists travelling to institutions without HEP groups to provide authentic experience. (Boris Tomašík et al. (2024))
- **Masterclass pattern mirroring professional experimental workflow: build the detector, take and quality-check data, model, measure, and compare**: This pattern organises an outreach or classroom activity around the complete workflow of an experimental scientist rather than a single demonstration. The article states the Masterclass "provides a comprehensive, hands-on learning experience that mirrors the procedures used in professional physics experiments, such as those conducted at the experiments operating at the CERN LHC," spanning detector design and construction, data collection, quality control, modelling, analysis, and interpretation. The Cosmic Piano makes this feasible where cloud chambers are impractical. (Guillermo Tejeda Munoz et al. (2024))
- **Original ROOT-based ALICE Masterclass with R_AA and strangeness enhancement exercises**: The original ALICE Masterclass, part of the International Masterclasses in Particle Physics program, requires participants to install the ROOT analysis package and offers two exercises: measurement of the nuclear modification factor R_AA and strangeness enhancement. The article calls the program 'a cornerstone of the collaboration's educational outreach, offering students a unique opportunity to engage with real data from the ALICE experiment.' (Ragoni et al. (2024))
- **Web-based ALICE Masterclass accessible through a browser**: The web-based ALICE Masterclass, accessible through the ALICE Masterclass website, offers the strangeness enhancement exercise without any software installation. The article presents it as 'a new, web-based version of the Masterclass' with 'a vastly improved accessibility and a more immediate experience with the respect to the original Masterclass.' (Ragoni et al. (2024))
