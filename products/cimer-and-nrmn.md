## Description
CIMER and the National Research Mentoring Network (NRMN) are national programmes that provide evidence-based, culturally responsive mentorship education, networking, and resources for mentors and mentees in STEM and biomedical research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CIMER and NRMN national mentorship-education infrastructures**: The paper describes two national programs supporting cultural change in mentorship. CIMER "develops, implements, and evaluates mentor and mentee training using theoretically-grounded, evidence-based, and culturally-responsive training interventions", facilitating training at all career stages and promoting cultural change that values research mentorship. NRMN develops, implements, assesses, and disseminates approaches to engaging, training, and mentoring individuals underrepresented in biomedical research, offering a virtual mentorship program and a mentoring network platform. Together they gather evidence and provide guidelines across the STEM ecosystem. (2030STEM Collaboration (2022))
