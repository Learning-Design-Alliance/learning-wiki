# BasqueARA, Agrega2-Es and Agrega2-En+ graded science-text corpora

## Description
Three independent, grade-annotated secondary-education science-text corpora released by Uçar et al. for matching Basque, Spanish, and English reading materials to learners' language competence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BasqueARA, Agrega2-Es and Agrega2-En+ graded science-text corpora for secondary education**: The article compiles and releases three independent, document-level, grade-annotated science corpora for ESO secondary education: BasqueARA (329 documents), Agrega2-Es (401 documents) and Agrega2-En+ (251 documents), with English texts written for non-native learners. Texts were extracted from the Agrega2 repository and other sources, split by headings into self-contained reading passages, and 'each document was assigned a level 1-4 according to the original ESO grade for which the material was developed.' (Uçar et al. (2024))
