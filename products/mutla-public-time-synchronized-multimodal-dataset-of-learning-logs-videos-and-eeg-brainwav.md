# MUTLA public time-synchronized multimodal dataset of learning logs, videos, and EEG brainwaves from real-world SAIL after-school sessions

> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MUTLA is a publicly accessible dataset created by Fangli Xu et al. containing time-synchronized SAIL learning logs, webcam video, and EEG recordings from Chinese primary and middle school students in after-school tutoring sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MUTLA: a public time-synchronized multimodal dataset of learning logs, videos, and EEG brainwaves from real-world SAIL after-school sessions**: MUTLA is a large-scale, publicly accessible multimodal dataset for teaching and learning analytics. It bundles three time-synchronized data sources per student: question-level user records from the SAIL learner record store, EEG brainwave data from BrainCo headsets stored via FocusEDU, and webcam video captured with Debut software. The authors state it includes "time-synchronized multimodal data recordings (learning logs, videos, EEG brainwaves)" gathered during real after-school tutoring rather than in a controlled lab, aiming to support engagement prediction and adaptive learning research. (Fangli Xu et al. (2019))
