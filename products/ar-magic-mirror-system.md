# AR Magic Mirror system

## Description
A screen-based augmented-reality anatomy learning system developed at the Technical University of Munich that superimposes radiological images and 3D anatomical models on a tracked learner's body.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AR Magic Mirror system for in-situ radiology learning**: The AR Magic Mirror is a screen-based augmented reality system "employing the mirror metaphor", in which users see a reflection of themselves with virtual anatomical information superimposed on a large display. A Kinect camera tracks the user's pose, and intuitive hand gestures select the modality and move through CT, MRI, or photographic cross-section images displayed at the height of the user's hand, with a 3D model superimposed on the body. It was used in gross anatomy tutorial sessions and in the elective self-directed learning session. (Bork F et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [In the elective course, posttest scores improved significantly overall and for the Magic Mirror and Theory groups, but no significant pre-post difference was recorded for the Anatomage group.](../claims/mm-anatomage-elective-prepost-improvement.md) [+M]
