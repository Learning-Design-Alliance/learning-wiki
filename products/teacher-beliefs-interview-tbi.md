# Teacher Beliefs Interview (TBI)

## Description
An interview-based instrument that classifies teachers' teaching beliefs across five ordered categories from teacher-focused to student-focused orientations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher Beliefs Interview (TBI) five-category classification of teaching beliefs from teacher focused to student focused**: The TBI classifies teaching beliefs into five ordered categories: "traditional (teacher focused), instructive (teacher focused), transitional, responsive (student focused), or reform based (student focused)". Traditional beliefs emphasize transferring information via lecturing; instructive beliefs focus on providing experiences directed by the teacher; transitional beliefs stress the teacher–student relationship and supportive environment; responsive beliefs emphasize collaborative peer learning; reform-based beliefs center on students driving their own learning. The study adapted TBI descriptions as a coding rubric for concept-map terms, categorizing each term by the explanation GTAs gave during map presentations. (Star W. Lee (2019))

### Claims
