# Physical Therapists' Perceptions of Motor Learning (PTP-ML) questionnaire and adapted Brazilian version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A standardized questionnaire developed by Vaz et al. for measuring physical therapists' motor-learning self-efficacy, implementation, and workplace factors, including a Brazilian Portuguese adaptation for identifying self-efficacy gaps before and after education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Physical Therapists' Perceptions of Motor Learning (PTP-ML) questionnaire and its adapted Brazilian version**: A standardized questionnaire with "adequate construct validity, internal consistency, and test-retest reliability" that assesses PTs' self-efficacy, self-reported ML implementation, and environmental workplace factors with three separate scales. The study translated the self-efficacy scale into Portuguese and created an adapted version with Part A (12 original self-efficacy statements) and Part B (8 new statements on the Kleynen framework content), used to map self-efficacy gaps before and after the course. (Vaz DV et al. (2021))
