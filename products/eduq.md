# Eduq+

## Description
Eduq+ is a Movva-run message-based nudge programme that sends structured SMS or audio messages to parents and teachers to support primary students' learning and engagement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eduq+ message-based nudge program**: Eduq+ is a low-cost message-based nudge intervention implemented by Movva with the Ivorian Ministry of Education, sending "two SMS-messages per week in simpli ﬁed French" to parents and/or teachers of primary students. Each two-week core module follows a behavioral-economics-based sequence: "a motivating fact (message 1), a suggested activity (message 2), an interactive message (message 3), and a growth message (message 4)". Parent messages addressed social-emotional development and educational engagement; teacher messages included pedagogical tips and motivational support. Costs were 796 USD per school or 6.68 USD per student. (Sharon Wolf et al. (2023))
