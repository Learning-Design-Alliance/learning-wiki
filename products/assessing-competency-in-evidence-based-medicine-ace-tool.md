# Assessing Competency in Evidence-Based Medicine (ACE) tool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A clinical-scenario-based assessment developed by Ilic et al. that measures evidence-based medicine competency in question formulation, evidence searching, critical appraisal, and application of evidence to patient care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Assessing Competency in Evidence-Based Medicine (ACE) tool, Persian version**: The ACE tool, developed by Ilic et al., was used to assess EBM competency. It "consists of a clinical scenario followed by 15 dichotomous questions assessing four domains of EBM competency: clinical question formulation, evidence searching, critical appraisal, and application of evidence to patient care". Each item was scored as correct (1) or incorrect (0), yielding a total score ranging from 0 to 15. The Persian version has previously demonstrated acceptable validity and reliability (Cronbach's alpha = 0.79). Assessments were independently scored by two trained researchers blinded to group allocation, with excellent inter-rater agreement (Cohen's kappa > 0.90). (Zand Vahid et al. (2026))
- **ACE tool: a 15-item dichotomous assessment of EBM competency across steps 1-4**: The ACE tool presents users with a patient scenario, a search strategy and a hypothetical article extract, then asks 15 yes/no questions covering question formulation, literature searching, critical appraisal and application of evidence. Items 1-11 assess knowledge and skills, while items 12-15 assess attitudes toward implementing EBM in clinical practice. The authors state it "provides a novel assessment that measures user performance across the four main steps of EBM" and is the first such instrument with a dichotomous outcome measure. (Ilic Dragan et al. (2014))
