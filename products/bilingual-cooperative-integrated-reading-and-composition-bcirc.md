# Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition (BCIRC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
BCIRC is a bilingual adaptation of the Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition program that organizes Spanish- and English-language reading, writing, vocabulary, and comprehension instruction in cooperative student teams for Spanish-speaking learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BCIRC before-, during-, and after-reading activity structure**: BCIRC organizes instruction as a recurring cycle of fifteen activities occurring before, during, and after reading. As the report describes, "Fifteen activities occur before, during, and after reading, including using BCIRC materials to develop vocabulary, making predictions of a story's content based on its title, and reading with a partner followed by silent reading". Teachers model reading strategies such as making and confirming a prediction before, during, and after reading, and cooperative groups then apply the demonstrated strategy while attempting to comprehend stories from their classroom text. The pattern embeds vocabulary, prediction, partner reading, and writing within cooperativ (Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading et al. (2007))

## Description
BCIRC is an adaptation of the Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition (CIRC) program designed to help Spanish-speaking students succeed in reading Spanish and then transition to English reading. In the adaptation, "students complete tasks that focus on reading, writing, and language activities in Spanish and English, while working in small cooperative learning groups". Students are assigned to heterogeneous four-member teams; activities include partner reading, story-component recognition, vocabulary development, creative writing, and comprehension tasks, with teachers modeling strategies such as making and confirming predictions. It targets grades 2-5 and is now offered as Reading Wings on the Success for All website.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers received extensive staff development on using a constructivist framework to facilitate cooperative learning discussions
#### Constraints
- Developed for use with students whose primary home language is Spanish

### Target Learners
- Spanish-speaking English language learners in grades 2-5

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension in Spanish and English
- English language development
- Transition from Spanish to English reading instruction
