## Description
An adapted Spanish-language version of the National Assessment of Educational Progress mathematics assessment, administered as a trial to public school students in grades 4 and 8 in Puerto Rico in 2003 and again in 2005. The 2003 administration's preliminary analyses prompted changes in administration and translation procedures for 2005, and the assessments were the basis for item misfit analyses and a special validity analysis.
