## Description
A cloud-based educational media-service system attributed to Jeong, Kim, and Yoo that enables educators to author, manage, exchange, and deliver text, images, video, 3D objects, and AR/VR scenes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cloud-based content-oriented smart education system enabling AR and VR content authoring (Jeong, Kim and Yoo)**: The article describes, citing Jeong, Kim and Yoo, a system that "provides the delivery and exchange of various applications of educational content by integrating a number of functions necessary for the deployment of a learning media service environment in the cloud". Teachers and lecturers can create text, images, videos, 3D objects, and VR- and AR-based virtual scenes using a cloud-driven author's tool in a common format, for use in schools, universities and institutes. The article says the system may be adapted to each learner's perception benefits, learning styles and content usage templates. (Popel et al. (2018))
