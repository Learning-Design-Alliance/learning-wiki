## Description
The GENESISS project’s conceptual model organizes healthcare in-situ simulation around assessment, system readiness and safety culture, clinical skills and patient outcomes, and non-technical skills, knowledge and confidence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Conceptual model of in-situ simulation in healthcare proposing four main ISS functions**: The GENESISS project's phase-one conceptual model of ISS in healthcare proposes four main ISS functions, depicted in Figure 1 of this review. The model underpins the review's aim to develop evidence-based standards for healthcare professionals, educators and managers implementing ISS interventions in clinical practice. The review states that "A conceptual model of ISS was developed in phase one [33] which proposed four main ISS functions (Fig. 1)", and the mapping review's four evidence categories reflect these functions. (Evans Kerry et al. (2022))
