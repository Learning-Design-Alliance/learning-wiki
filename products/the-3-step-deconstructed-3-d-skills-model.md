# The 3-step deconstructed (3-D) skills model

## Description
A near-peer teaching framework for undergraduate clinical examination skills that integrates Peyton's comprehension and self-practice steps with guided teaching around a formative OSCE.

### Claims
- [Third-year medical students taught with the 3-D skills model at a formative OSCE achieved significantly higher summative OSCE station pass rates than the rest of the year cohort, with a higher median grade banding of educational significance.](../claims/cl-3d-skills-summative-osce-improvement.md) [+M]
- [The short-term formative impact of the 3-D skills model was replicated with a larger sample, showing high student satisfaction, improved confidence and a mean increase of ~15% in checklist scores.](../claims/cl-3d-skills-short-term-formative-replication.md) [+W]
