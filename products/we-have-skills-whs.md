## Description
A video-based social-skills instructional programme for K–3 students developed by Marquez et al. and collaborators, combining student lessons, the Elementary Social Behavior Assessment, and professional-development materials for teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **We Have Skills! three-module social skills program**: We Have Skills! (WHS) is a video-based social skills instructional program for early elementary students comprising three modules: Module 1 delivers student instructional materials including "eight 5-minute video lessons" covering seven core skills; Module 2 offers the online Elementary Social Behavior Assessment (ESBA) for screening and progress monitoring; Module 3 provides professional development materials covering delivery of instruction and more intensive student support. The program blends PBIS, RtI, and explicit instruction principles. (Marquez et al. (2014))
