## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the EarthTime interactive visualization technology and internet-connected devices for viewing time-lapse imagery
#### Constraints
- The deck frames the tool for guided observation of geographic change over time, with research questions about causes handled through outside research such as the National Park Service

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Observing and describing change over time in data visualizations
- Generating questions from data patterns
