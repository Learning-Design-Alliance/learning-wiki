## Description
A 33-item, six-competency, five-point Likert self-report questionnaire developed to assess teaching competencies among nurse educators and identify professional-development needs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Salus-Ed questionnaire for measuring teaching competencies among nurse educators**: The Salus-Ed questionnaire is a validated self-report instrument measuring teaching competencies among nurse educators. It comprises 33 items scored on a 5-point Likert scale, organized into six competencies: relationship and communication, self-management and teaching planning, methodological, teamwork and collaborative management, disciplinary, and ethical and reflective competence. The article reports that the questionnaire 'is a valid and reliable tool that can be used to assess teaching competences among nurse educators.' (Quintana-Alonso R et al. (2026))
