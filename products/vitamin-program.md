## Description
Vitamin Program is an internet-based curriculum-aligned educational software service developed by SEBİT in cooperation with the Turkish Ministry of National Education and Turk Telekom, providing multimedia lessons, interactive activities, simulations, experiments, worked examples, assessments, and supplementary resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vitamin Program internet-based education software**: Vitamin Program is a computer software education support service used over the internet, developed by SEBİT according to the Turkish Ministry of National Education curriculum and offered through cooperation among SEBİT, Turk Telekom and the Ministry. It provides prepared presentations, book contents, "interactive activities with 3D model type", animations, simulations, experiments, worked examples, question-and-answer activities, screening and pilot tests, and additional resources. In this study it delivered the triangles lesson content to the experiment group over the internet in classroom settings. (Deniz KAYA et al. (2013))
