# Lumify handheld ultrasound system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Philips Lumify is a handheld ultrasound system comprising a transducer, mobile-device application, and cloud-connected imaging workflow for acquiring and sharing diagnostic images.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lumify handheld ultrasound probe with Android tablet interface for focused cardiac imaging**: The Lumify probe (Philips) is a 1–4-MHz single-phased array cardiac transducer with scanning software connected to an Android tablet interface, in which "the transducer performs most of the beam forming, image acquisition and reconstruction processing". The touchscreen supports tap, pinch-to-zoom and swipe functions, and images can be wirelessly transferred to a picture-archiving and communication system. It served as the imaging hardware for the entire training programme. (Acheampong Benjamin MD et al. (2022))
