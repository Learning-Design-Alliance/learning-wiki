Description¶

Experience Corps is a community-based programme that places adults aged 55 and older in elementary schools to tutor and mentor at-risk students, assist teachers, and lead enrichment activities.

Components¶

  • Experience Corps (EC) program: older adult volunteers tutoring and mentoring at-risk elementary students: Experience Corps is a program that "brings older adults ages 55 and up into public elementary schools to tutor and mentor children who are at risk of academic failure." EC members also "help teachers in the classroom and lead after-school enrichment activities." The program was evaluated for its effects on student reading outcomes. (Morrow-Howell et al. (2009))

Claims¶

  • The Experience Corps program was found to be a low burden to teachers [+W]
  • Students receiving Experience Corps tutoring and mentorship showed significant improvements in reading and comprehension skills [+W]
  • Experience Corps had grown to 23 sites with nearly 2,000 tutors serving approximately 20,000 students [+W]
  • A funded evaluation of Experience Corps effects on student reading outcomes was conducted by Washington University's Center for Social Development [+W]
