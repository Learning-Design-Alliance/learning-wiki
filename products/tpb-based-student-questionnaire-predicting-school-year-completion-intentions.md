# TPB-based student questionnaire predicting school-year completion intentions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A questionnaire developed by Davis et al. to measure attitudes, subjective norms, perceived behavioral control, and intentions concerning high-school year completion among students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TPB-based student questionnaire administered early in the second year of high school**: A questionnaire grounded in the theory of planned behavior was completed by male and female high-school students aged 14 to 17 (N=166) early in their second year. It measured the TPB constructs predicting intentions to complete the year, which the article reports were "accurately predicted from attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control". It functions as an early assessment instrument for completion-relevant beliefs in this longitudinal study. (Davis et al. (2002))
