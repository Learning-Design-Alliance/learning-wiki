## Description
AstroTutor is a Gemini-based multi-agent retrieval-augmented tutoring chatbot developed for astronomy education, combining course notes, textbook, and research-literature knowledge sources with moderated Socratic dialogue.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AstroTutor constrained GenAI tutor**: AstroTutor is a custom GPT-based tutor configured around course materials, the optical photometry activity and an instructional script specifying domain, purpose, response modes and limits. Its design was "intentionally conservative": it supported conceptual clarification, planner interpretation, diagnostic feedback and controlled operational help, but "was not designed to provide summative evaluation, certify the correctness of work or produce final reports for students". It was introduced as an optional support tool on the familiar ChatGPT platform, with students informed they remained responsible for verifying outputs and authoring reports. (Matteo Tuveri (2025))
