# CSTPP cloud-based student, teacher, and parent platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A cloud-based platform developed with Han Lin Publishing Co., Ltd. that provides EFL practice, progress tracking, self-evaluation, diagnostic analysis, remedial videos, and student–teacher–parent communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CSTPP cloud-based student, teacher, and parent platform for EFL learning**: The CSTPP is a cloud-based platform built with Han Lin Publishing Co., Ltd. that operates on any PC or mobile device and records students' learning progress. Its student end provides self-evaluation with over 100,000 test questions per topic — "the CSTPP contained over 100,000 test questions for each teaching topic of every field and unit" — instant transcripts, test question analysis, remedial videos, and a database of personal learning weaknesses; teacher and parent ends provide communication logs, study progress, and learning progress modules. It is available at www.ehanlin.com.tw. (Fu-Yuan Chiu (2014))
