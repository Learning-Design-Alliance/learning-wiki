# ER-VIPE (Emergency Room-Virtual Interprofessional Education)

## Description
ER-VIPE is a 3D virtual interprofessional emergency-room simulation platform developed for healthcare education and used by medical, nursing, pharmacy, radiological technology, and medical technology learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ER-VIPE: a 3D virtual interprofessional emergency-room simulation platform with TeamSTEPPS-aligned communication features**: ER-VIPE (Emergency Room-Virtual Interprofessional Education) is a VR platform for medical interprofessional education in Thailand, used here as a 30- to 60-minute scenario of a 70-year-old ED patient with acute dyspnea and critical hyperkalemia. Each scenario involves 6 participants in 5 professional roles communicating via "computer microphones (non-VR) or VR headsets, using direct conversation, individual calls, and a group call function (limited to 3 uses per scenario)." Features include a morale-boosting "plus" button restoring a Health Point bar, wristband-based patient identification, and 1-time laboratory test ordering. (Khuansiri Narajeenron et al (2025))
