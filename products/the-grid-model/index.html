The Grid Model¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A differentiated curriculum framework that organizes objectives, content, processes, and products through eleven depth-and-complexity indicators for use by educators in curriculum design.

Components¶

  • The Grid Model as a differentiated curriculum framework based on depth and complexity: The Grid Model is a differentiated curriculum model that restructures the existing general education curriculum according to the principles of depth and complexity without changing its scope. Learning objectives, content, process and products are organized by themes, and eleven indicators are defined: "discipline's language, details, patterns, trends, unanswered questions, rules, ethics, great ideas, timing, perspective, and interdisciplinary". In this study it was operationalized as a 7-week, 42-hour differentiated Light and Sound science unit integrating disciplines such as archeology, astronomy, chemistry and design, implemented in GATE-style settings with gifted and non-gifted 4th graders. (Neşe Kutlu Abu (2018))

Claims¶

  • Grid Model-based science curriculum raises 4th graders' internal goal orientation post-test scores relative to the general curriculum [+M]
  • Grid Model curriculum raises task value post-test scores of public-school students relative to controls [+M]
  • Grid Model curriculum raises self-efficacy post-test scores of public-school students relative to controls [+M]
  • Grid Model curriculum shows no significant post-test effect on external goal orientation, learning control belief, or test anxiety [~M]
