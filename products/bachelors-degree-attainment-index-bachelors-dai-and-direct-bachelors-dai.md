# Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index (Bachelor's DAI) and Direct Bachelor's DAI

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
These are UChicago Consortium measurement metrics estimating the proportion of a ninth-grade cohort that earns a bachelor's degree, distinguishing direct four-year pathways from pathways involving delayed entry or two-year colleges.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index and Direct Bachelor's DAI metrics**: The UChicago Consortium developed two metrics to estimate students' progression to and through college: the Bachelor's DAI and the Direct Bachelor's DAI. The Direct Bachelor's DAI counts students taking "the straight-forward path of graduating high school in four years, enrolling immediately in a four-year college, and earning a four-year college degree within six years of starting college," estimated at 16 percent for 2015. The Bachelor's DAI additionally counts students who first enroll in a two-year college or delay entry before earning a four-year degree, yielding 18 percent. (Nagaoka et al. (2016))
