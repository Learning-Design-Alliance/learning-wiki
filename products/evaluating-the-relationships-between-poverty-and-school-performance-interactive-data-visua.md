## Description
An interactive data visualization released by NWEA Research alongside the 2018 report. "The gallery below presents our findings and gives you the chance to explore how your school site compares to schools from the sample." It is listed under the topics Equity and High-growth schools & practices, and serves as the public interface for exploring the report's findings on poverty and school performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users need a school site to compare against the schools in the study sample.
#### Constraints
- The gallery compares a user's school site only to schools from the report's sample.

### Target Learners
- Educators and school leaders evaluating school performance

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding relationships between poverty and school performance using growth and achievement data
