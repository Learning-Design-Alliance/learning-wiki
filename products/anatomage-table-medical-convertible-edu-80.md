# Anatomage Table (Medical Convertible, EDU 8.0)

## Description
A touchscreen virtual-dissection and medical-imaging platform made by Anatomage, Inc. that provides interactive digital cadaver exploration and integration of radiological examinations for anatomy and clinical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Anatomage virtual dissection table (Medical Convertible, EDU 8.0)**: The Anatomage Table (Medical Convertible, EDU 8.0) is one of two virtual dissection tables used for hands-on sessions in the postgraduate oral anatomy course. The article states that the "Anatomage® table incorporates advanced imaging technologies such as CT scan, X-ray, ultrasound, and MRI, perfectly integrated into a user-friendly touchscreen interface". Like the SECTRA table, it uses Visible Human Project data to let users explore digital cadavers, dissect, rotate and zoom anatomical models in real time, and import radiological exams such as CBCT during the student-led clinical-application hour. (Gerardi Davide et al. (2025))
- **Anatomage Table 7.0 virtual dissection table**: The Anatomage Table 7.0 is a life-size touch screen virtual dissection table on which four digitalized cadavers are loaded, including "an Asian woman with a gastric cancer, an Asian man with acute leukemia, a Caucasian women with cardiac disease, and a Caucasian man with a brainstem tumor". Users can visualize three section planes, rotate, zoom, isolate organs, highlight vessels and nerves with colored filters, and switch to preinstalled or uploaded DICOM radiological images for 3D reconstruction of normal and pathological anatomy. (Bartoletti-Stella Anna et al. (2021))
