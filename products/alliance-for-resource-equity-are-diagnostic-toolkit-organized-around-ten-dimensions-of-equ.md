# Alliance for Resource Equity (ARE) diagnostic toolkit organized around ten dimensions of equity

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A toolkit developed by the Education Trust and Education Resource Strategies that helps district and school leaders diagnose inequities across ten dimensions of student experience and select guidebook actions for improvement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Alliance for Resource Equity toolkit with ten dimensions of equity**: The Alliance for Resource Equity (ARE), a partnership between Ed Trust and Education Resource Strategies, offers a toolkit that "categorizes the student experience into 10 'dimensions of equity.'" It includes a diagnostic tool organized around key questions for identifying problem areas, plus practical guidebooks offering actions to address those problems. The brief highlights relevant ARE key questions and guidebook sections under each SEAD policy area for district leaders and advocates seeking local improvement. (The Education Trust (2023))
