## Description
A Flash-based interactive virtual-patient simulation developed by the National University of Singapore for nursing refresher training in assessing and managing clinical deterioration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **e-RAPIDS virtual patient simulation for refresher training in assessing and managing clinical deterioration**: e-RAPIDS is a single-user interactive multimedia virtual patient simulation developed at the National University of Singapore using Flash software, designed as a refresher course reinforcing the RAPIDS mannequin-based program. Learners play the role of a nurse in five scenarios of acute medical conditions, assessing and managing deterioration "by clicking on the actions from the ABCDE control menus" and reporting via SBAR menus, with immediate programmed feedback, physiological monitor displays, and a debriefing screen with reflection questions, an evaluation checklist, and an automatically calculated performance score. (Liaw SY et al. (2014))
