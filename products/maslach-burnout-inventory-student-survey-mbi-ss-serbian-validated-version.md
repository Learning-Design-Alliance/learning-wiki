# Maslach Burnout Inventory – Student Survey (MBI-SS), Serbian validated version

## Description
A licensed 15-item student burnout assessment measuring emotional exhaustion, cynicism, and academic efficacy, translated and validated in Serbian by Ilić IM (2023).

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Maslach Burnout Inventory – Student Survey (MBI-SS), Serbian validated version**: The MBI-SS is a 15-item student version of the Maslach Burnout Inventory measuring burnout via three subscales: emotional exhaustion (5 items), cynicism (4 items) and academic efficacy (6 items), each rated on a 7-point frequency scale from 0 (never) to 6 (daily). The authors licensed it from Mind Garden, translated it into Serbian, and validated it; the three subscales explained 64.9% of variance with Cronbach's α of 0.869, 0.856 and 0.852 and good test-retest reliability. (Ilić IM (2023))
