Description¶

Khan Academy MAP Accelerator is a mastery-based mathematics learning platform developed by Khan Academy in partnership with NWEA for students in grades 3–8.

Components¶

  • Mathematics mastery learning platform (Khan Academy MAP Accelerator) used by over half a million U.S. students in grades 3–8: A technology-enhanced, mastery-based mathematics learning platform used at scale by U.S. students in grades 3–8. The article states it is "used by over half a million U.S. students in grades 3–8" and evaluates its impact on standardized math test scores. The reference list identifies the platform as Khan Academy MAP Accelerator in a cited prior proceedings paper by the same research team. (Weatherholtz et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • Platform treatment effects did not vary across demographic subgroups, suggesting equal benefit regardless of racial background [+W]
  • Longitudinal within-student analysis of a mathematics mastery learning platform replicates positive effects on standardized math test scores [+W]
  • Prior cross-sectional between-group research on the platform found a positive usage–test-score relationship but could not rule out unobserved confounding [+W]
