# Super Cyber Kids framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 30-field matrix developed by Nicolai B. Plintz that organizes actionable cybersecurity skills for children aged 8–13 across adapted NIST dimensions and six threat-related categories.
