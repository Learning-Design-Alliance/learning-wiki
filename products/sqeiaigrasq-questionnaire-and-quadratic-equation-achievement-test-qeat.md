# SQEIAIGRASQ questionnaire and Quadratic Equation Achievement Test (QEAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pair of validated instruments developed by Oladayo et al. to measure Senior Secondary School I students' ethical perspectives and self-efficacy regarding AI-generated responses and their achievement in solving quadratic equations.

### Claims
