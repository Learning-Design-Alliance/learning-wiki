# Schema-based instruction (SBI) for proportional reasoning

## Description
A 21-lesson mathematics intervention curriculum developed by Jitendra and colleagues that teaches proportional problem structures, schematic representations, flexible procedures, and metacognitive problem-solving strategies to middle-school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Schema-based instruction (SBI): a multicomponent intervention emphasizing the mathematical structure of problems through schematic diagrams with problem solving and metacognitive strategy instruction**: Schema-based instruction (SBI) is a multicomponent intervention rooted in schema theory of cognitive psychology and cognitive models of mathematical problem solving. The article describes SBI as "a multicomponent intervention that emphasizes the mathematical structure of problems through the use of schematic diagrams and incorporates problem solving and metacognitive strategy instruction." The SBI program comprises 21 lessons across two units (Ratio/Proportion and Percent) and rests on four instructional practices: modeling problem solving and metacognitive strategies, activating the mathematical structure of problems, visually mapping information using schematic diagrams, and developing procedural flexibility. (Jitendra et al. (2016))
