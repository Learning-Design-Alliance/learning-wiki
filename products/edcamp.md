# Edcamp

## Description
Edcamp is a participant-driven professional-learning programme and event model, supported by the Edcamp Foundation, in which educators choose topics, lead peer sessions, and collaboratively share resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Edcamp participant-driven professional learning model**: Edcamp is an "un-conference" model of professional development created by teachers for teachers, in which educators collaboratively build session boards from participant-submitted topics and choose breakout rooms led by peers, with a notetaker documenting key points in a public note repository such as Padlet. The report describes it as "participatory professional development events where educators lead their learning and professional networking through collaboration and shared experiences, rather than traditional sit-and-get professional development presentations." It cites Gates Foundation research that teachers who choose their professional learning are more than twice as satisfied. (Edcamp Design for Learning Series: A New Bridge Between Research and Professional Learning)

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher volunteers to organize
- Participant-submitted topics collated into a session board
- Notetakers and a public note repository
#### Constraints
- In-person Edcamps are specific to regions and districts; online Edcamps remove the geographic limit

### Target Learners
- educators across career stages, from pre-service to veteran teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher-driven professional learning, networking, and leadership development
