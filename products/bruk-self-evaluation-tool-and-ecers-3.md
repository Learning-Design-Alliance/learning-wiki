# BRUK self-evaluation tool and ECERS-3

## Description
BRUK is a Swedish National Agency for Education self-evaluation tool for systematic preschool quality work, while ECERS-3 is an established observational rating scale used by trained observers to assess preschool process quality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BRUK self-evaluation tool as the active control condition, and ECERS-3 as the preschool process-quality measure**: The trial used BRUK, a self-evaluative tool administered by the Swedish National Agency for Education, as an active control: control units continued business as usual while staff used BRUK, which covers four areas of investigation (each preschool's development; norms, values, and influence; knowledge, development, and learning; transit, collaboration, and the surrounding world) with indicators rated on a four-point agreement scale. Preschool quality for all units was estimated with ECERS-3, an internationally established rating scale of process quality consisting of 35 items in 6 subscales rated from 1 to 7, rated by trained observers from the University of Gothenburg. (Gerholm Tove et al. (2018))
