## Description
QTris is a tic-tac-toe-based educational game developed by Amabile et al. in which high-school learners play through board-game phases that simulate quantum processes on nine qubits.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QTris as a formally well-defined sequential quantum game within Quantum Game Theory**: The article situates QTris within Quantum Game Theory (QGT), an extension of Classical Game Theory in which classical information carriers are replaced by quantum systems. QTris satisfies the criteria identifying a quantum game: the board represents a quantum register defining the Hilbert space, the preparation phase corresponds to the initial quantum state, the cards define the admissible quantum operations, and the final measurement determines payoffs. The authors conclude that "QTris is a formally well defined sequential quantum game whose dynamics can be analyzed within the general framework of QGT." (Amabile et al. (2026))
