## Description
A 31-item questionnaire developed by Lyu Jie et al. to assess clinical nurses' frailty-management competency across knowledge, attitude, and practice domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Knowledge-Attitude-Practice (KAP) model applied to nurses' frailty management, with attitudes mediating the translation of knowledge into practice**: The KAP model, first proposed in the 1960s, is a behavioral-change framework the article applies to nurses' frailty management. The article states that "This theoretical model classifies changes in human behavior into three stages: acquiring knowledge, generating beliefs, and forming behaviors", where knowledge is understanding and cognition, attitude is beliefs and approaches, and practice is behaviors. The article hypothesizes that "attitudes mediate translation of knowledge into practice" and tests this with structural equation modeling, finding a partial mediating effect of attitude on the knowledge-to-practice pathway. (Huang Xueyan et al. (2025))
