# QuaRCS numeracy assessment instrument

## Description
QuaRCS is an online low-stakes assessment developed by Follette et al. for measuring quantitative literacy and mathematics attitudes among general-education college science students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QuaRCS: a numeracy assessment instrument for general education college science students combining 25 demographic/attitudinal items with 25 real-world quantitative items**: The QuaRCS is a numeracy assessment designed for college-level general education science students, developed over eight semesters between 2010 and 2015 and administered to over 4,000 students in more than 40 courses. It contains "an equal mixture of demographic/attitudinal questions ( N=25) and \"real world\" quantitative questions (N=25)", each quantitative question followed by a confidence ranking, and ends with a self-reported effort question used for motivation filtering. Skills assessed were selected from an educator survey of Astronomy and Mathematics educators. (Follette et al. (2015))
