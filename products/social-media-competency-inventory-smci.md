# Social Media Competency Inventory (SMCI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An 82-item web-based inventory developed by Alber et al. to measure social-media competency among Certified and Master Certified Health Education Specialists across six scales.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Social Media Competency Inventory (SMCI) for Certified Health Education Specialists**: The SMCI is an 82-item self-report Web-based inventory measuring social media competency of CHES and MCHES across six scales: Social Media Self-Efficacy (n=50), Social Media Experience (n=20), Effort Expectancy (n=3), Performance Expectancy (n=3), Facilitating Conditions (n=3), and Social Influence (n=3). It was developed because 'there are no existing standardized measures of social media competency among health education specialists.' Administration, scoring, and interpretation guidelines accompany the final inventory. (Alber JM et al. (2015))
