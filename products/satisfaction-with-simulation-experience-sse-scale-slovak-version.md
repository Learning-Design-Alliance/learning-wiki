# Satisfaction with Simulation Experience (SSE) scale, Slovak version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An 18-item, five-point Likert questionnaire translated and validated by Polónyiová et al. for assessing medical students’ satisfaction with simulation-based education, including debriefing and reflection, clinical reasoning, and clinical learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Slovak version of the Satisfaction with Simulation Experience (SSE) scale**: An 18-item questionnaire rated on a 5-point Likert scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree, measuring "general student satisfaction with simulation experience and its three components: debriefing and reflection, clinical reasoning, and clinical learning." This study produced the first validated Slovak translation and the first administration of the SSE to medical students rather than nursing students. Raw data, R code and scale items are shared on OSF. (Polónyiová K. et al. (2025))
