# Virtual Patient (VP): Simulator of Clinical Cases

## Description
A web-based low-fidelity virtual-patient simulator developed by Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá and SENA that lets medical educators author clinical cases and lets students practice history taking, examination, diagnosis, and treatment decisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Virtual Patient: Simulator of Clinical Cases — a web-based low-fidelity clinical-case simulator**: The VP is "a Web-based tool that allows professors to easily design clinical cases with teaching purposes". Students type questions identified by keywords, access physical exam findings via a human figure icon (vital signs, sounds, videos, images), request laboratory tests and diagnostic images, propose a diagnosis hypothesis and formulate a treatment, and the software includes "an algorithm to assess the students' performance". Cases default to a healthy patient that professors modify to fit teaching goals and can later enrich. It was developed by Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá and SENA and used as the core tool of the elective course. (Isaza-Restrepo Andrés et al. (2018))
