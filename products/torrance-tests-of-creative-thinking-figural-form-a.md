## Description
Figural Form A is a standardized figural creativity assessment developed by E. Paul Torrance that uses picture-forming, picture-completion, and parallel-lines activities to score fluency, flexibility, originality, and elaboration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking, Figural Form A, as the creativity outcome measure**: Figural Form A of the Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking (TTCT) was the data collection instrument, comprising three activities: forming pictures, picture completion, and parallel lines (repeated lines). It "yields the fluency, flexibility, originality, and elaboration su bcomponents of creativity scores and the creativity total score." The test was rated by two raters with an inter-rater reliability coefficient of .75. The subdimensions are defined as fluency (creating various ideas in response to an open-ended question), flexibility (different approaches to a problem), elaboration (detailing the proposed idea), and originality. (Tulin KARACA (2017))
