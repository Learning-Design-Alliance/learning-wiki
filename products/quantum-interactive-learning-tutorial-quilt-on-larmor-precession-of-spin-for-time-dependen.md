# Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on Larmor precession of spin for time dependence of expectation values

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A research-based guided-inquiry tutorial developed by Emily Marshman and colleagues to support advanced undergraduate and graduate physics students' understanding of time-dependent expectation values through Larmor precession.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on Larmor precession of spin**: The QuILT is a research-based learning tool that helps upper-level undergraduates learn about time dependence of expectation values using Larmor precession of a spin-1/2 two-state system. It "builds on students' prior knowledge" and "strives to help them develop a good knowledge structure of relevant concepts," using a guided sequence of questions in which later questions build on preceding ones. It includes pre-/posttests, computer simulations, and was validated with five physics faculty and fifteen think-aloud interview participants. (Benjamin R. Brown et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [Upper-level students' scores on time-dependence of expectation values improve significantly after working on the Larmor precession QuILT](../claims/quilt-larmor-precession-pre-post-improvement.md) [+M]
