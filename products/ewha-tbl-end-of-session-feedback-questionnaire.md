# Ewha TBL end-of-session feedback questionnaire

## Description
A four-point end-of-session questionnaire developed by the Ewha Womans University College of Medicine TBL Board to gather medical students’ perceptions of TBL session management, participation, and satisfaction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ewha TBL end-of-session feedback questionnaire measuring perceived systematic management, active participation, and satisfaction**: A three-variable feedback questionnaire administered at the end of each TBL session at Ewha Womans University College of Medicine, developed by the College's TBL Board of four clinical professors "with an average of over 15 years of educational experience" and validated by content experts. It measures perception of systematic TBL class management (4 items, Cronbach's α=0.871), active class participation (2 items, α=0.861), and class satisfaction, all on a 4-point agreement scale. The study used it to measure its primary variables. (Jeong Somi et al. (2025))
