# Three-dimensional framework for assessing problem posing ability: number, category, and quality

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Dai et al. (2026) framework and coded rubric that assesses primary students’ science problem-posing questions by number, category breadth, and cognitive quality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-dimensional framework for assessing problem posing ability: number, category, and quality**: The article operationalizes students’ problem posing ability in science into three coded dimensions, defining them so that "category was used to reflect the breadth of question types and the characteristics of their knowledge sources; and quality was used to reflect the depth of cognitive processing" demonstrated in students’ questions, with number reflecting how many questions were posed. The framework draws on English (1997), Silver and Cai (2005), and Yuan and Sriraman (2011). Number is coded in five levels, category into six question types, and quality into five cognitive levels from the revised Bloom’s taxonomy. The dimensions feed a 10-point total score weighted 3, 2, and 5 points for number, category, and quality. (Dai et al. (2026))
