# Whole Child Approach

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework for organizing education around developmental relationships, supportive environments, rich learning, skill and mindset development, and integrated supports, developed from the science of learning and development literature.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five key elements of a whole child approach**: The guide enumerates five key elements that constitute a whole child approach: "positive developmental relationships; environments filled with safety and belonging; rich learning experiences and knowledge development; development of skills, habits, and mindsets; and integrated support systems." The fifth element, integrated support systems, is the guide's central focus, operationalized as integrated, school-based systems of care built through cross-sector collaboration. The list is grounded in the science of learning and development literature the guide cites. (Breaking Barriers et al. (2022))
