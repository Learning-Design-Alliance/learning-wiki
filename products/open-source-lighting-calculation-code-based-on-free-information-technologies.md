# Open-source lighting-calculation code based on Free Information Technologies

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open-source code developed by the GEA group for modeling sky glow and performing lighting calculations related to light pollution, with accompanying didactic material for university teachers, engineering students, and interdisciplinary professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Open-source lighting-calculation code based on free information technologies, and didactic material addressing inconsistent radiometric conventions**: Among its contributions to university teaching on light pollution, the GEA group reports the "Realización de un código abierto basado en Tecnologías Informáticas Libres para modelos y cálculos luminotécnicos relacionados a la CL", including evaluation of a luminaire's upper hemispheric flux and estimation of lumens per capita from sky brightness. The group also prepared didactic material (in editing phase), noting a problem: concepts like intensity, flux or luminosity are defined differently in standard, astronomical or health-science usage, so definitions should be posed from physics and particular uses clarified. The material is expected to be available through the UTN-FRSN website. (Quaranta et al. (2006))
