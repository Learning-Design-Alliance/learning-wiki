Step One¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Step One is a free age-friendly pre-beginning ESL and U.S. citizenship curriculum developed and field-tested through a USCIS-funded USC project for older immigrant learners.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In one practitioner's report on her own older-beginner Step One class, with no comparison group, students gained an average of 128 BEST Plus points over 6 months and two former absolute beginners passed the citizenship test [+M]
  • Federally-funded adult ESL programs and citizenship classes largely exclude older beginner immigrants because instruction is designed for workforce-bound younger adults [+W]
  • Common language teaching methods such as oral drills, memorization, and fast-paced competitive activities disadvantage older learners [+M]
  • Instructional modifications that lower barriers are optional supports for younger resilient learners but may be necessary for older beginners, pre-literate students, and learners dealing with trauma or disabilities [+W]
