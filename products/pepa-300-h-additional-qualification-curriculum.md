# PEPA 300-h additional qualification curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 300-hour, university-led additional-qualification curriculum for registered nurses in long-term care, combining classroom/online teaching, self-regulated study, and on-the-job training with practice supervision.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEPA 300-h additional qualification curriculum for registered nurses**: The key implementation strategy of the PEPA intervention is a 300-h additional qualification program led by university lecturers to prepare registered nurses to apply the intervention components. It carries 10 European Credit Transfer System points and "consists of two modules" covering role development, evidence-based practice and communication, and medical knowledge of chronic diseases, geriatric assessment and case management. The program "is split into 100 h each of classroom/online teaching, self-regulated study time, and training on the job in the long-term care facility", with a handbook documenting participation and learning objectives reviewed through practice supervision. (Silies K et al. (2025))
