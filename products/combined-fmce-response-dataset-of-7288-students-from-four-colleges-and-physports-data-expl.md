# Combined FMCE response dataset of 7,288 students from four colleges and PhysPort's Data Explorer

## Description
A research dataset compiled by Trevor I. Smith et al. from Force and Motion Conceptual Evaluation responses collected at four colleges and through PhysPort's Data Explorer, cleaned for inappropriate, blank, and unscorable responses and used to study Newtonian-mechanics conceptual understanding.
