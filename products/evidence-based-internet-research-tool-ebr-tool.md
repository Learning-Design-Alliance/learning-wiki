# Evidence-based internet research tool (EBR tool)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based tool developed to teach undergraduate students across disciplines how to formulate evidence-based questions, search for information, and critically appraise online sources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Evidence-based internet research tool (EBR tool) for teaching critical appraisal skills across disciplines**: Long et al.'s intervention was an "Evidence based internet research tool for teaching students across disciplines, about EBP (particularly critical appraisal skills)". Participants viewed a 30-min training video on how to use the tool, and case-study based questions were included for students to apply question formulation and critical appraisal skills. The tool was designed to be an adjunct resource to texts and classroom education, and its use was grounded in Cognitive Apprenticeship Theory. (Ramis Mary-Anne et al. (2019))
