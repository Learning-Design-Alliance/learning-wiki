1997 Improving Chicago's Schools Individual School Report¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A school-level reporting instrument developed by Anthony Bryk and colleagues that synthesizes student and teacher survey responses for comparison, self-analysis, and school-improvement planning.

Components¶

  • 1997 Improving Chicago's Schools Individual School Report (survey-based school report instrument): This artifact is an individual school report built from two surveys — Improving Chicago's Schools: The Student Survey, 1997 and Improving Chicago's Schools: The Teacher Survey, 1997 — administered to sixth, eighth, and tenth grade students and to elementary and high school teachers in spring 1997. Its stated purpose was "to provide individual schools with useful information about themselves to help in their planning and self-analysis, and to guide their improvement efforts." Part 1 gives ten profile graphs comparing the school to the citywide average and to schools with similar ITBS scores; Part 2 gives two pages per scale showing item-level responses and frequency distributions against bott (Anthony Bryk et al. (1997))
