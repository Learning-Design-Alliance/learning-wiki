# Measurement battery for the Australian TPSR trial: PSRQ, TARE, and PACES-PE

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A measurement battery used by the Australian Teaching Personal and Social Responsibility trial to assess students’ personal and social responsibility, observed responsibility-based teaching, and enjoyment of physical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Measurement battery for the Australian TPSR trial: PSRQ, TARE, and PACES-PE**: The trial uses three named instruments. The Personal and Social Responsibility Questionnaire (PSRQ) measures the primary outcomes and "consists of 14 items split into 2 categories (personal responsibility and social responsibility)". The Tool for Assessing Responsibility-Based Education (TARE) is an observational tool aligned with the TPSR model, collected through lesson observations three times during the intervention. The Physical Activity Enjoyment Scale–Physical Education version (PACES-PE) is an 11-item Likert-scale tool for enjoyment in adolescents. (Sonja Maric et al. (2025))
