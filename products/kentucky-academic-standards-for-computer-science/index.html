Kentucky Academic Standards for Computer Science¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A framework issued by the Kentucky Department of Education that defines K–8 computer-science learning standards, including Data & Analysis and Impacts of Computing strands.

Components¶

  • Kentucky Data & Analysis standards organize learning into storage, collection/visualization/transformation, inference & models, and data ethics strands: The Kentucky Academic Standards for Computer Science structure Data & Analysis for K-8 into named strands: Storage (E-DA-01, appropriately store and modify digital files), Collection, Visualization & Transformation (E-DA-02, collect and visually display data; E-DA-03, "Analyzing data for trends and relationships"), plus an Impacts of Computing strand on proper use of data (E-IC-04). The session maps its activities to these standards. (Computational Thinking Boosters: Data et al. (2020))
