# GUIDE (GenAI Units In Digital Design Education)

## Description
An open-source repository created by Weihua Xiao and colleagues containing runnable Google Colab labs and other materials for teaching LLM-aided digital design, including RTL generation, verification, and hardware security.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GUIDE open courseware repository for GenAI-driven digital design education**: GUIDE (GenAI Units In Digital Design Education) is an open-source courseware repository with runnable Google Colab labs for teaching LLM-aided digital design. The article states it is "an open courseware repository with runnable Google Colab labs and other materials", available at a public GitHub URL, and open for community contributions. It turns research results into adoptable, updatable class material. (Weihua Xiao et al. (2026))
- **Design courseware units to be runnable from scratch with grading evidence to avoid solutions that only look correct**: GUIDE imposes quality requirements so units are teaching-ready and reusable across courses: labs must run in a clean environment without private paths, include at least one end-to-end runnable example, specify what students must submit as evidence (compilation/simulation logs, waveform screenshots, or reports), follow stable interfaces and file layout, and use clear explanations. The article notes evidence requirements "reduces the risk of solutions that 'look correct'" in a domain where small RTL issues can cause subtle bugs. (Weihua Xiao et al. (2026))
