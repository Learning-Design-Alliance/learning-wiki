# Ottawa CanMEDS Competency Assessment Tool (OCCAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 29-item, patient-perspective questionnaire developed by Esteves et al. to assess postgraduate medical trainees' communicator, professional, and health advocate CanMEDS competencies using a five-point scale with a Not Assessable option.
