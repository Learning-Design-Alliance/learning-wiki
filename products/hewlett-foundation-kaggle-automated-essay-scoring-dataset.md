# Hewlett Foundation Kaggle Automated Essay Scoring dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A public Kaggle dataset released through the Hewlett Foundation Automated Essay Scoring Competition containing 12,976 English student essays across eight prompts or essay sets with human ratings for developing and evaluating automated essay-scoring models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hewlett Foundation Kaggle Automated Essay Scoring dataset (12976 essays across eight essay sets)**: A public dataset from the Automated Essay Scoring Competition hosted by the Hewlett Foundation on Kaggle, consisting of 'eight essay sets with overall 12976 essays written in English.' Each essay set was written by human students based on a specific prompt, and all essays were rated by at least two human raters. The article splits it into training (7783, 60%), validation (2596, 20%), and test (2597, 20%) sets stratified by essay topic. (Debelak Rudolf (2026))
