# Research-based pre/post survey of divergence and curl statements

## Description
A researcher-developed selected-response survey for assessing undergraduate students’ conceptual understanding and graphical interpretation of divergence and curl in vector fields before and after electromagnetism instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Research-based pre/post survey of divergence and curl statements developed from interviews and free responses**: A pre- and post-instruction survey whose first two questions present lists of statements about the curl and divergence, asking students to "[I]ndicate which statements, formulas or properties are always correct when describing the curl [divergence of a vector field." The twelve curl statements (C1–C12) and twelve divergence statements (D1–D12) were assembled from written free responses and eight semi-structured interviews, and assess conceptual understanding, interpretation of graphical representations of vector fields, and connections between the two. (Charles Baily et al. (2014))
