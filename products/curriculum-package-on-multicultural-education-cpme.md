## Description
A multicultural-education curriculum package developed by Ajitoni Sunday Olukayode et al., guided by NCSS multiculturalism and diversity guidelines and containing instructional content plus teacher and student activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Researcher-designed 32-hour multicultural education programme using interactive methods**: A multicultural education programme designed and prepared by the researcher, delivered to the experimental group of high school students using interactive teaching methods. The article states lessons were prepared "using different interactive methodologies like co-operative learning, role play, debates, slogan, lecture cum discussion, poster, competition" and that "the program comprised of 32 hours". Content was developed through in-depth discussion with the literature and with colleges belonging to different cultures. (Shaheen Perveen (2014))
