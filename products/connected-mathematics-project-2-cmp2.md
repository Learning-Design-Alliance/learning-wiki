# Connected Mathematics Project 2 (CMP2)

## Description
A middle-school mathematics curriculum developed for inquiry-based, active, and collaborative learning through problem solving and teacher-facilitated classroom discussion.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CMP2 inquiry-based instructional model of active, collaborative student learning**: CMP2 embodies an instructional model in which "CMP2 allows students to be responsible for their learning by exploring different solution pathways, sharing their ideas with other students, listening to the ideas of others, and questioning each other." Teachers ensure lesson goals are met by asking questions, encouraging students to share and compare their thinking, and connect representations of problems and solutions. The report identifies four elements of effective mathematics instruction it incorporates: active student participation in building knowledge, motivation and engagement through contexts connected to student experience, collaborative learning through group problem solving, and development of conceptual understanding and procedural knowledge. (Martin et al. (2012))
