## Description
A Dutch national competency framework that organizes medical-leadership competencies across the dimensions Me, Others, and Society for use in physician education, professional development, and curriculum design.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The DML framework v1.0 organizes 12 ML competency domains under three overarching dimensions: Me, Others, and Society**: The Dutch Medical Leadership framework is a national taxonomy of medical leadership built through Grounded Theory synthesis of Dutch literature, interviews, focus groups and surveys. Its final version maps interrelations between 12 competency domains and "three overarching dimensions: 'Me'; 'Others'; and 'Society'", each domain entailing four distinct competencies, alongside a compact ML definition. The article positions the framework as a canonical 'knowledge artefact' created by a community of practice, intended as a contextualized 'leadership lens' for curriculum redesign and ML training in the Netherlands. (Keijser Wouter A. et al. (2019))
