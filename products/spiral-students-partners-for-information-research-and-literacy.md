# SPIRaL (Students Partners for Information Research and Literacy)

## Description
SPIRaL is Purdue's two-semester students-as-partners undergraduate research programme in which information-studies students investigate societal information challenges while developing information-literacy and research skills.

- **SPIRaL: a two-semester undergraduate research program in which students investigate societal information challenges**: SPIRaL (Students Partners for Information Research and Literacy) is one of three educational programs developed at Purdue leveraging a students as partners approach. It is described as "a two-semester undergraduate research program in which students investigate societal information challenges." It pairs undergraduate research with information literacy development. (Maybee et al. (2025))
