Mathematics Formative Assessment System (MFAS-CCSS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A mathematics formative-assessment system developed by Laura B. Lang and colleagues that provides Common Core–aligned tasks and rubrics, together with professional-development and implementation supports, for elementary teachers.

Components¶

  • Mathematics Formative Assessment System (MFAS) with five key formative assessment strategies: The MFAS-CCSS is a system of tasks and rubrics designed to help teachers enact formative assessment as an ongoing process rather than a test. It was designed to provide teachers with tasks and rubrics to employ five key strategies: assessing understanding during instruction, identifying misconceptions and errors, examining student work, posing additional questions to elicit thinking, and obtaining guidance on next steps for instruction. The system was implemented with online professional development modules, weekly communities of instructional practice, and principal support. (Laura B. Lang et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • MFAS use produced a statistically significant positive effect of 0.26 on teacher mathematics content knowledge in Grades K-1 [+M]
  • MFAS produced a statistically significant positive effect of 0.20 on Kindergarten student mathematics achievement [+M]
  • MFAS produced a statistically significant positive effect of 0.24 on Grade 1 student mathematics achievement [+M]
