# Web-based nurse-to-physician non-clinical performance survey instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based five-point Likert survey developed by Alameddine et al. for nurses to evaluate physicians’ communication, professionalism, and leadership performance in an emergency-department setting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Web-based nurse-to-physician non-clinical performance survey instrument (communication, professionalism, leadership)**: A short web-based questionnaire developed through multiple phases and reviewed by an expert panel for content validity and alignment with ACGME competencies. It asked nurses to "rate physicians' non-clinical performance on a five-point Likert scale" covering communication (two questions), professionalism (six questions) and leadership (four questions), plus one open-ended question. It was pilot tested with five randomly selected nurses and administered anonymously to nurses with at least two years of ED experience working at least two shifts per month with the evaluated physician. (Alameddine M et al. (2015))
