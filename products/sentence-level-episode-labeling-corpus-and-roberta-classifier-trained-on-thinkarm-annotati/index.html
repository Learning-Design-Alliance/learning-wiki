Sentence-level episode-labeling corpus and RoBERTa classifier trained on ThinkARM annotations¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A sentence-level corpus of ThinkARM-annotated Omni-MATH reasoning traces with a RoBERTa classifier for automatically labeling problem-solving episodes, developed by Chenguang Wang et al.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Episode-composition and transition features contribute predictive process evidence beyond semantics, with Epi.+Tran. reaching 0.136 permutation importance versus 0.084 for Sem. in the Both (Role) setting [+W]
  • Harder SAT Math items are associated with more implementation-centered and iterative episode dynamics — stronger Implement counts and Analyze–Implement transitions — rather than a proportional increase in all reasoning activity [+W]
