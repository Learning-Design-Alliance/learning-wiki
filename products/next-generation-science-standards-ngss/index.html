Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A U.S. K–12 science education standards framework developed by states and partner organizations that integrates scientific and engineering practices, crosscutting concepts, and disciplinary core ideas.

Components¶

  • NGSS three-dimensional framework integrating scientific and engineering practices, crosscutting concepts, and core ideas: The Next Generation Science Standards are a K-12 curriculum standard of the United States combining skills, understanding, and content. The article states it "combines aspects of skills, understanding, and content into three dimensions: scientific and engineering practice, crosscutting concept, and core idea". The study operationalises this framework in Indonesian grade 8 science learning by building lesson plans, worksheets, and assessment around one practice (developing and using models), one core idea (the human respiratory system), and one crosscutting concept (structure and function). (Rachmawati et al. (2019))
