# Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
NWEA's Spanish-language early-grades reading assessment measures foundational skills across kindergarten through grade 3 and provides achievement and growth norms for interpreting scores over time.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish Foundational Skills achievement and growth norms tables (grades K-3)**: Twelve snapshot tables (Tables 3.3-3.14) present achievement and growth norms for each domain in grades K-3, released in Fall 2026. Grey columns give percentile ranks from 5 to 95, blue columns give achievement norm scores per term, green columns give expected between-term growth and SDs for each percentile, and yellow and mixed-colored cells support normative evaluation of a student's actual gain between terms. Achievement norms cover fall, winter, and spring; growth norms cover fall-to-winter, winter-to-spring, and fall-to-spring growth. (Wei He et al. (2026))

### Claims
- [Mean foundational-skills scores rise from fall to spring with the largest gains in kindergarten and grade 1, and growth flattens by grades 2-3](../claims/spanish-foundational-skills-fall-to-spring-gain-pattern.md) [+W]
- [Norming-sample testing volume is concentrated in kindergarten and grade 1, which together account for more than 70% of test events](../claims/spanish-foundational-skills-testing-volume-concentration.md) [+W]
