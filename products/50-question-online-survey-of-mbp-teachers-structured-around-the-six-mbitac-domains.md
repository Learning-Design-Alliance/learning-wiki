# 50-question online survey of MBP teachers structured around the six MBI:TAC domains

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research-team-developed online questionnaire for adult mindfulness-based-programme teachers, assessing demographics, online teaching experience, practical delivery issues, participant interaction, and MBI:TAC-informed pedagogy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **50-question online survey of MBP teachers structured around the six MBI:TAC domains**: A 50-question online survey designed by the research team to capture demographics, teaching experience, practical online teaching matters, and interaction with participants. Pedagogical questions "were based on the six domains of the Mindfulness Based Interventions: Teaching Assessment Criteria (MBI:TAC)". It included 24 qualitative questions, was usability-tested with 18 experienced MBP teachers (eight in a focus group), and took around 20–30 minutes to complete; responses were analysed in SPSS with qualitative content analysis. (Burton Alison M. et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Most online MBP teachers found online teaching as satisfactory as, or better than, in-person teaching](../claims/online-mbp-teaching-satisfaction-comparable.md) [+M]
