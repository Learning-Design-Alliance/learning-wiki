# TUG-K related PCK task

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A weighted-scorе pedagogical-content-knowledge assessment developed by Alexandru Maries and colleagues in which physics graduate teaching assistants predict introductory students’ incorrect answers to TUG-K questions, supplemented by think-aloud interviews.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TUG-K related PCK task with weighted scoring and think-aloud interviews**: The article's instrument is the "TUG-K related PCK task", in which graduate students, for each TUG-K question, identified "which one of the four incorrect answer choices in their view, would be most commonly selected by introductory physics students after instruction". PCK scores equal the fraction of introductory students selecting the chosen distractor, summed over the 21 questions, with a maximum of 6.70. Qualitative data came from semi-structured audio-recorded think-aloud interviews with six graduate students on the four lowest-scoring questions. (Alexandru Maries et al. (2014))
