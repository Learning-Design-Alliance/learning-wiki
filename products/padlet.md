## Description
Padlet is a collaborative digital bulletin-board platform made by Wallwisher, Inc. that supports posting, commenting, discussion, and voting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Padlet as a pre-class question-and-voting tool supporting interaction and feedback**: Padlet was used for posting questions about pre-class content, which students answered in real time via voting and comments. In the discussion the author states "Padlet proved to be an indispensable tool for stimulating discussion and ensuring all students understood the lesson before engaging in practice -oriented group work," and credits it with enabling peer and instructor feedback and a safe, inclusive sharing environment. Communication on it was limited to writing. (Alizadeh (2024))
