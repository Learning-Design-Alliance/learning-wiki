# UCL competency framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive, hierarchically organized online competency framework published by University College London that links broad areas of knowledge, skills, and attitudes to more detailed competence items for acute mental health care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interactive online competence map hosted on the UCL competency framework website**: The framework is published as an interactive online 'map' on the UCL competency framework website, giving an overview of key areas of knowledge, skills and attitudes. The article states "The map is designed to be viewed online in an interactive format so that each competency can be linked to a further list of more detailed competences", allowing users to drill down from broad areas to specific competence items. The full list of competences is freely available as an open-access document. (Wood et al. (2025))
