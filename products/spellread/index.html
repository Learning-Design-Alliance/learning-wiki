SpellRead™¶

Description¶

SpellRead™ is a structured literacy programme for struggling readers in grade 2 and above, delivered through systematic explicit auditory and visual reading instruction in small groups and supported by an instructor monitoring system.

Components¶

  • SpellRead™ literacy program for struggling readers: SpellRead™ (formerly SpellRead Phonological Auditory Training®) is described as "a literacy program for struggling readers in grades 2 or above, including special education students, English language learners, and students more than two years below grade level in reading". It integrates auditory and visual aspects of reading through systematic, explicit instruction in 140 lessons completed over five to nine months. Instruction occurs in small groups of five students with one instructor in 60–90 minute classes, and includes a web-based Instructor Support System for monitoring progress. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
  • SpellRead™ small-group literacy program with three instructional phases: SpellRead™, formerly SpellRead Phonological Auditory Training®, is a small-group literacy program for struggling readers in grades 2–12 distributed through PCI Education. It "consists of 105 lessons implemented in three distinct phases that interweave phonemics, phonetics, and instruction in language-based reading and writing". Phase A (50 lessons) trains students to hear and manipulate the 44 sounds of English, Phase B (30 lessons) covers secondary vowel spelling, consonant blends, and two-syllable decoding, and Phase C (25 lessons) covers words of three or more syllables, clusters, and verb forms. The program takes five to nine months and is delivered in small groups of five students with (SpellReadâ"¢)
  • Three-phase sequential skill-mastery structure for remedial reading instruction: SpellRead™ organizes instruction as "140 lessons implemented in three distinct phases that interweave phonemics, phonetics, and instruction in language-based reading and writing". Phase A (50 lessons) trains the auditory process to hear and manipulate the 44 sounds of English; Phase B (30 lessons) covers secondary vowel spelling, consonant blends, and two-syllable syllabication; Phase C (25 lessons) addresses decoding words of three or more syllables, clusters, and verb forms. Each lesson includes activities for phonemic, phonetic, semantic, syntactic, comprehension/vocabulary, and fluency skills. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))

Claims¶

  • In the Rashotte et al. (2001) study, the WWC confirmed statistically significant SpellRead™ effects on four of seven alphabetics outcomes [+W]
  • Comprehension effects of SpellRead™ differed between the two studies: Torgesen et al. found no significant effects while Rashotte et al. found significant effects on both outcomes [~W]
  • SpellRead™ has potentially positive effects on reading comprehension, with an average improvement index of +20 percentile points [+W]
  • SpellRead™ has positive effects on alphabetics for struggling beginning readers, with an average improvement index of +18 percentile points [+W]
  • SpellRead™ has potentially positive effects on reading fluency, with an average improvement index of +9 percentile points [+W]
  • In the Torgesen et al. (2006) RCT, SpellRead™ showed indeterminate effects for fifth graders, with no statistically significant or substantively important effects in any domain after six months [~W] — (also cited [-M])
  • In the Rashotte, MacPhee, and Torgesen (2001) RCT, SpellRead™ produced statistically significant positive effects on alphabetics and reading fluency for fifth- and sixth-grade struggling readers after eight weeks [+M]
  • SpellRead™ shows potentially positive effects on alphabetics for adolescent readers, with an average improvement index of +23 across two studies [+W]
  • One year after the intervention ended, SpellRead™ effects were not statistically significant on the supplemental Torgesen et al. (2007) outcomes [-W]
  • SpellRead™ shows potentially positive effects on comprehension for adolescent readers, with an average improvement index of +11 [+W]
  • SpellRead™ shows potentially positive effects on reading fluency for adolescent readers, with an average improvement index of +14 [+W]
  • One-year follow-up and LiPS-vs-RWT supplemental comparisons showed mostly non-significant differences, with some follow-up alphabetics effects remaining significant [~W]
