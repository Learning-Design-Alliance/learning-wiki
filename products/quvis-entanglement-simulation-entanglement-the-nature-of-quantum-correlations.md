# QuVis Entanglement simulation (Entanglement: The nature of quantum correlations)

## Description
An interactive quantum-mechanics visualization developed by the QuVis Quantum Mechanics Visualization Project to help undergraduate students explore entanglement, measurement outcomes, and correlations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QuVis Entanglement simulation (Entanglement: The nature of quantum correlations)**: An interactive simulation using two-particle entangled spin states, developed as part of the QuVis Quantum Mechanics Visualization Project. It "shows a source of particle pairs in the middle of two Stern -Gerlach apparatuses" that can be jointly rotated along orthogonal X and Z axes, displays individual and paired measurement outcomes and the correlation coefficient, and lets students choose input states and create their own states. It does not require tensor-product formalism and is aimed at introductory and advanced undergraduate levels. A revised second version added X and Z basis display, a create-your-own-state option, and two notation choices. (Antje Kohnle et al. (2015))
