## Description
An international project producing free, illustrated, plain-language astronomy booklets for children and general audiences, developed, translated, and revised by professional astronomers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TUIMP free downloadable astronomy booklets**: TUIMP (www.tuimp.org) is an international project producing "small 16-page booklets folded from one sheet of paper, free to download from the internet". The booklets are written in simple language with numerous illustrations, revised and translated by professional astronomers, and can be read online on a mobile phone if no printer is available. They are intended for children from nine years old and anyone curious about astronomy. (Grażyna STASIŃSKA (2018))
