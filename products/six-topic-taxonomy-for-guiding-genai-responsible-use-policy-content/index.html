Description¶

A RAPID GenAI Working Group framework that organizes responsible generative-AI policy guidance into AI literacy, safety, ethics, transparency, implementation guidelines, and evaluation and impact.

Components¶

  • Student-centred, first-person responsible use policy: ten or fewer points written as "I am responsible for…", with policy modules, a glossary, a quiz and student-made announcements
