## Description
TIM is an open-source, MIT-licensed document-based learning and MOOC platform developed and maintained by the University of Jyväskylä.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TIM document-based MOOC platform supporting interactive blocks, learning analytics, and reusable course material**: TIM (Interactive Material) is an open-source, MIT-licensed MOOC learning environment developed at the University of Jyväskylä since 2014, in which everything is document-based: documents consist of blocks that may be text paragraphs or interactive elements such as multiple-choice, drawing, or programming tasks. Instructors can see all student attempts on a task, write feedback, and communicate via comments, so "TIM provides a lot of data for learning analytics". Documents can borrow blocks from one another, and macros centralize year- or version-dependent parts to speed maintenance. (Juha Reinikainen et al. (2024))
