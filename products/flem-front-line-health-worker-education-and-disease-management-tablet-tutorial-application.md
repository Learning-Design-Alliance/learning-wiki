# FLEM (Front Line health worker Education and disease Management) tablet tutorial application

## Description
A tablet-based tutorial application developed by Instrat and Anadach on the Vecna Cares CliniPAK system to provide frontline health workers with real-time Ebola education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FLEM tablet tutorial application for Ebola awareness delivered via the CliniPAK system**: The FLEM (Front Line health worker Education and disease Management) application is a tablet computer tutorial application developed by Instrat and Anadach on the Vecna Cares CliniPAK system, leveraging the CommCare Open Data Kit platform. It was "designed by a multidisciplinary group of ICT and infectious diseases experts and health system researchers to extend and enhance existing CliniPAK electronic health information systems to disseminate critical information to FHWs in real time via tablet computers". It requires minimal instruction after download and was used to deliver the Ebola awareness tutorial over a 2-week period with multiple views allowed. (Otu Akaninyene et al. (2016))
