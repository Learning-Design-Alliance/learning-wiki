16-item paper-and-pencil test of inquiry skills¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 16-item paper-and-pencil assessment for upper-secondary students that measures hypothesis formulation, experiment design, data representation, variable relationships, and error identification.

Components¶

  • 16-item paper-and-pencil test of inquiry skills (Cα=.63): The research instrument is a test of inquiry skills updated from Ješková et al. (2016) and developed from existing tests such as ScInqLiT, TISP, TOSLS and TGSA. As the article states, "The test consisted of 16 items, it was administered in the paper-and-pencil form, and took 45 minutes." Items cover formulating hypotheses, designing experiments, transforming data into tables and graphs, determining relationships between variables, and identifying sources of errors, with at least two items per skill and a maximum of 14 points. (Mária Ganajová et al. (2021))

Claims¶

  • Teaching with FACTs produces significantly better inquiry-skill post-test results than teacher-centred teaching without FACTs in upper-secondary science [+M]
