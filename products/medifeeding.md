## Description
Medifeeding is an iOS and Android peer-feedback application developed for medical students to upload clinical-skills videos and exchange audio, video, and text feedback.

### Claims
- [In a pilot with 31 second-year medical students, 90% reported that Medifeeding was educationally beneficial and the overall rating averaged 4.13/5](../claims/medifeeding-pilot-90-percent-educational-benefit.md) [+M]
