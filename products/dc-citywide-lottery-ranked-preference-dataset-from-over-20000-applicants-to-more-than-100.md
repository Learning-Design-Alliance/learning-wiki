## Description
The evidence base for the brief is a dataset of ranked school-preference lists submitted by families to a Washington, DC citywide school lottery. As the brief states, "The findings are based on lists of preferred schools submitted by over 20,000 applicants to a citywide lottery for more than 100 traditional and charter public schools in Washington, DC." The dataset spans both traditional and charter public schools and was collected as part of the associated 2013-2015 project for the Walton Family Foundation.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
- Covers applicants to a single city's (Washington, DC) lottery during the 2013-2015 project time frame

### Target Learners
