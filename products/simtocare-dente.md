## Description
SIMtoCARE B.V.'s SIMtoCARE Dente® is a VR-based dental simulator with haptic feedback, 3D visualization, sensor-based dexterity assessment, and standardized tooth-preparation feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SIMtoCARE Dente VR haptic dental simulation system**: The SIMtoCARE Dente® system (SIMtoCARE B.V., Vreeland, The Netherlands) is a VR-based dental simulator used as the intervention in this trial. The article states it "offers real time force feedback and 3D visualization, and is equipped with sensors designed to assess manual dexterity". Its tooth preparation modules let students compare preparations with predefined ideal models, providing color-coded visual feedback and standardized assessment of taper, occlusal reduction, and finish line standardization. (Kızılırmak Burcu et al. (2025))
- **SIMtoCARE Dente HVRS with a custom pulpotomy exercise on a primary molar (tooth #85)**: The SIMtoCARE Dente is the haptic virtual reality simulator used in this study, providing "multimodal feedback, particularly haptic (touch) feedback" through a physical handpiece with virtual tip, a force-feedback robotic arm replicating tactile resistance, a real foot pedal and simulated aerator sounds. For the study, a custom pulpotomy exercise on a non-carious primary molar (tooth #85) was developed with dental technicians, modifying the resin tooth to replicate internal anatomy with a realistic pulp chamber and tactile response. The conventional comparator was a Frasaco Pedodontal Model AK-6/2 DAK mannequin head with a replaceable resin primary tooth. (Aura‐Tormos Juan Ignacio et al. (2025))
