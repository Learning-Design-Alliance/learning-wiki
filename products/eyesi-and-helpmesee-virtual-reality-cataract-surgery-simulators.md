# EyeSi and HelpMeSee virtual reality cataract surgery simulators

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (3 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
EyeSi and HelpMeSee are virtual-reality cataract-surgery simulation systems developed and operated by their respective commercial and training organizations for ophthalmology residents and other surgical trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EyeSi Surgical Simulator for vitreoretinal training and assessment**: The EyeSi Surgical Simulator (VRMagic, Mannheim, Germany) is a virtual reality simulator for ophthalmic microsurgical training. Five of the seven studies included in the review "all used the same virtual reality simulator, the EyeSi® Surgical Simulator," training skills subcategorized as complex procedures, intraocular navigation and instrument handling. The review concludes it can be used to assess and possibly improve basic and complex VRS skills, with expert groups showing no performance improvement across sessions. (Rasmussen Rasmus Christian et al. (2019))
- **Eyesi Surgical virtual reality ophthalmic surgery simulator**: The Eyesi Surgical (VRmagic, Mannheim, Germany) is "a high-fidelity virtual reality simulator designed for practising intraocular procedures". It combines a mannequin head housing a model eye, an operating microscope, instrument tracking sensors, and software modules for cataract and vitreoretinal surgery steps; the system records performance metrics, enabling scores and feedback to be generated. It is the most extensively assessed ophthalmic simulator in the review, with 33 validity studies. (Lee Roxanne et al. (2020))
- **EyeSi Surgical Simulator with 3D heads-up visualization system for microsurgical training**: The EyeSi® Surgical Simulator (Haag-Streit, Köniz, Switzerland) is a virtual reality simulator providing validated training modules for cataract and vitreoretinal surgery with objective performance metrics and the possibility of repeated practice. In this study it was equipped with both a conventional ocular microscope and a 3D heads-up display, and automatically generated objective performance metrics including overall performance scores, precision parameters, error rates, and task completion times. (Eckardt F et al. (2026))
- **Eyesi Ophthalmology high-fidelity virtual reality surgical simulator for refractive surgery training**: The HFS system was a high-fidelity ophthalmic surgical simulator (Eyesi Ophthalmology, VRmagic GmbH, Germany) integrating "virtual reality display, a microscopic surgical interface, and a force-feedback tactile system." For refractive surgery training it "simulates the complete procedure of femtosecond laser-assisted laser in situ keratomileusis (FS-LASIK) from a first-person surgeon's view." Its built-in software "automatically generated objective performance reports for each procedure" and provided guidance. The authors note it does not involve patient-specific digital twin technology based on image fitting or clinical data fusion. (Yang K et al. (2026))
- **EyeSi Surgical Simulator vitreoretinal training module**: The EyeSi Surgical Simulator (VRmagic, Mannheim, Germany) is a virtual reality platform for ophthalmic surgical training, offering "an immersive and risk-free environment where trainees can safely develop and refine their surgical skills". Its vitreoretinal module replicates posterior segment surgery tasks such as membrane peeling, retinal detachment repair, and laser photocoagulation, using tactile feedback, a stereoscopic display, a BIOM-like optical system, and foot pedal controls. Trainees in this study completed Module-B, Level 2 on Navigation and Instruments Laser Coagulation, and the simulator reports a performance score (maximum 100) based on five criteria: target achievement, efficiency, instrument handling, microscope handling, and tissue treatment. (Giannuzzi Federico et al. (2025))
- **Eyesi Surgical virtual reality microsurgery simulator with graded Navigation Training curriculum**: The Eyesi Surgical simulator from Haag-Streit Simulation is a virtual reality tool that "enables trainees to practice microscopic intraocular surgery in a realistic simulated environment." It uses "a binocular operating microscope, surgical instruments, a patient with mechanical eye, and foot pedals" and its curriculum "begins with basic skills practice and progressively advances users through anterior or posterior segment surgical tasks." In this study students completed Navigation Training Level 1, an abstract targeting task with 19 spheres, scored 0–100 across five criteria categories. (Deuchler Svenja et al. (2022))
