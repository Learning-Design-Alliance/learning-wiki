## Description
A 20-item multiple-choice questionnaire and 30-item dichotomous observational checklist developed and validity- and reliability-checked by the study authors to assess undergraduate nursing students’ BCLS knowledge and skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BCLS knowledge questionnaire and BCLS skills checklist**: Two measurement instruments used to assess outcomes in the trial. The knowledge questionnaire contains 20 four-choice items scored 0–20 (low = 0–9, moderate = 10–15, high = 16–20), and the skills checklist "consisted of 30 items to measure BCLS skills" scored dichotomously over a 0–30 range (low = 0–9, moderate = 10–20, high = 21–30). Both were updated based on the 2020 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPCR and their validity and reliability were reconfirmed in this study. (Mohsenzadeh Habibeh et al. (2025))
