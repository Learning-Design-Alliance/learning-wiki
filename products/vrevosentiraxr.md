## Description
VREvo/SentiraXR is a stand-alone virtual-reality paediatric tracheostomy emergency training package developed by VREvo Ltd for unsupervised use on a Meta Quest 3 headset.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A stand-alone VR paediatric tracheostomy emergency training package (VREvo/SentiraXR) hosted on a Meta Quest 3 HMD, usable without direct supervision**: The educational tool is a stand-alone software package hosted entirely on a VR head-mounted display, developed by VREvo Ltd, designed to be used without direct supervision by a technician or educator. It models the long-running NTSP paediatric tracheostomy safety course and teaches learners to recognise that paediatric tracheostomy emergencies require oxygenation via the mouth and nose, recall critical equipment, and follow the NTSP paediatric emergency tracheostomy algorithm, including suction to determine patency, emergency tube change, and five rescue breaths via mouth or stoma. The package "takes around 20–35 min to complete and has four distinct activities within the virtual environment" and runs on a Meta Quest 3 HMD loaned to participants. (Abbas Jonathan R. et al. (2025))
