Imagine IM¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Imagine IM is a print and digital K–12 core mathematics curriculum developed by Imagine Learning and certified by Illustrative Mathematics for problem-based, student-centered instruction.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In a Maryland rural fringe district study of over 700 students in grades K-5, Imagine IM showed significant impacts on Acadience Math (+0.26) and Grade 2 NWEA MAP Growth Math (+0.39), with positive but non-significant effects on the state MCAP assessment [+W]
  • District leaders report that building a shared instructional vision before curriculum adoption lays the foundation for successful IM implementation [+W]
  • Districts use multiple forms of data—learning walks, state assessment items, dashboards, and diagnostics—to give teachers feedback and refine IM implementation over time [+W]
