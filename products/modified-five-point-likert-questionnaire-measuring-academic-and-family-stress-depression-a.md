# Modified five-point Likert questionnaire measuring academic and family stress, depression, and academic performance

## Description
A researcher-modified self-report questionnaire administered to Pakistani higher-education students to measure academic and family stress, depression, and academic performance using five-point Likert items.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified five-point Likert questionnaire with SEM analysis of stress, depression, and academic performance**: The study's instrument and analysis pipeline: a modified questionnaire with a five-point Likert scale, with items taken from Maajida Aafreen et al.'s study and partially modified, administered to students in management sciences, engineering, and computer science. Of 721 questionnaires given out, 186 responses were useful. The analysis combined confirmatory factor analysis with structural equation modeling; Cronbach's alpha was 0.889, and fit indices met recommended thresholds (e.g., measurement model CMIN/DF 1.886, RMSEA 0.047). (Jiang Zuopeng et al. (2022))
