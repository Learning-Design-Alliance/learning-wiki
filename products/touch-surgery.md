# Touch Surgery

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Touch Surgery is a mobile surgical simulation application developed by Touch Surgery to help surgical trainees and practicing surgeons rehearse procedural steps and motor skills on compatible smartphones or devices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Touch Surgery mobile simulation application**: Touch Surgery is a smart application that aims to provide motor skill simulation and surgical step rehearsal by helping professionals familiarize and prepare for particular procedures. The review reports that one study demonstrated participants using the app "demonstrated better initial performance based on prior surgical experience and improved performance of a simulated surgical procedure with practice". It is also recommended in the review's checklist as an affordable platform for low-resource settings, and is noted as enabling practice on smartphones even in remote areas. (Anyinkeng A et al. (2025))
- **Touch Surgery™ app-based training module for chest tube insertion used as standardized pre-assessment training**: Before performing the assessed insertion, each participant completed an initial training session using "a mobile training device and the validated surgical training app Touch Surgery™ (TS)". The TS-module "Chest Tube Insertion" served as both an initial training to this intervention and a compact recapitulation for versed participants. The article cites validation work for Touch Surgery for cognitive training in laparoscopic cholecystectomy, positioning the app as a standardized cognitive preparation step prior to hands-on performance. (Friedrich Mirco et al. (2018))
- **Touch Surgery assessment tool: a validated cognitive interactive VR application scoring femoral intramedullary nailing across four modules**: The Touch Surgery application (Kinosis, London, UK) is "a cognitive, interactive VR application that utilizes computer animation to explain the operative steps of a surgical procedure", free on mobile and smart devices. Its assessment tool, validated by Sugand et al. (2015b), was used here across 4 modules: patient positioning and preparation, femoral canal preparation, proximal locking, and distal locking and closure. Scores are automatically calculated percentages with no negative marking. (Bhattacharyya R et al. (2018))
