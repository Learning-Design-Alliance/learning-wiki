# Number Sets Test

## Description
A speeded assessment developed by Moore et al. that measures preschool and early-elementary children's fluent processing and manipulation of symbolic and nonsymbolic numerical magnitudes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Number sets test as a measure of fluent magnitude processing predicting later mathematics outcomes**: The number sets test asks children to quickly circle domino-like combinations of objects, Arabic numerals, or both that sum to a target value, with a 60-second limit and signal-detection (d-prime) scoring. The article reports that first-grade performance "correctly identified 2 out of 3 children who were diagnosed with MLD several years later and 9 out of 10 children who received no such diagnosis", with sensitivity and specificity better than first-grade achievement scores and no prediction of reading achievement. In this study it served as the kindergarten outcome measuring fluent processing of symbolic and nonsymbolic magnitudes. (Moore et al. (2016))
