# Fundamentals of Laparoscopic Surgery (FLS) program

## Description
A SAGES- and American College of Surgeons-sponsored programme combining box-trainer practice and a proctored examination to prepare and assess general-surgery residents in foundational laparoscopic knowledge, judgment, and technical skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FLS pegboard transfer and pattern cutting tasks on a laparoscopic box trainer as standardized assessment of surgical skill and novel instruments**: The Fundamentals of Laparoscopic Surgery (FLS) manual skills component, based on the MISTELS program, consists of five basic tasks performed on a laparoscopic box trainer. This study used two of them: the pegboard transfer task, in which the operator grasps six small pegs, transfers them to the other hand and places them on a second pegboard, and the pattern cutting task, cutting a predrawn 5 cm circle from gauze. Scores were computed as "cutofftime–completiontime–penaltyscore", with higher scores reflecting better performances, and the authors note simulators "can reduce the impact of biases associated to patients' variability" when assessing novel instruments. (Giannotti Domenico et al. (2015))
