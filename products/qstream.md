## Description
QStream is an online spaced-education platform developed by Harvard Medical School that delivers case-based questions, repeated retrieval, and corrective feedback at scheduled intervals for professional learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QStream spaced education model: case-based questions emailed at spaced intervals with succinct corrective feedback**: QStream (previously spaced education), developed at Harvard Medical School, delivers case-based multiple-choice questions electronically over several weeks. 'Upon answering a question, participants are given succinct feedback and links to further resources.' It exploits the finding that 'educational encounters which are spaced and repeated over time result in more efficient learning and improved learning retention'. Typical programs repeat cases in cycles of 8 to 45 days; this study used only three case scenarios. (Robinson Tracy et al. (2017))
