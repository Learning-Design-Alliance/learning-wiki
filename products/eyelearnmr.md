## Description
EyelearnMR is a mixed-reality HoloLens 2 application developed by Tan CJM et al. for practicing and assessing ocular motility examination with simulated patients and standardized clinical signs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EyelearnMR mixed reality simulation application for ocular motility examination on HoloLens 2**: EyelearnMR is a mixed reality simulation application "designed to teach and assess ocular examination via a wearable headset," consisting of a hologram 3D patient, a Lang fixation cube for ocular motility examination, and animation logic modeling eye movements during fixation. Deployed on Microsoft HoloLens 2, it superimposes simulated patients in physical space and offers six simulated ocular motility scenarios that students practice in guided 2-hour sessions, enabling repeated, standardized practice with reproducible clinical signs. (Tan CJM et al. (2026))
