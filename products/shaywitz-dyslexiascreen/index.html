Shaywitz DyslexiaScreen¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Shaywitz DyslexiaScreen is a brief teacher-completed screening assessment for identifying young children at risk for dyslexia, developed for use with students in kindergarten through second grade.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Nearly half of screened K-2 children in two New Orleans charter schools screened at risk for dyslexia, and most tested at-risk children met dyslexia criteria [+M]
