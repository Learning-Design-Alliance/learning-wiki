# Bright Beginnings: a child-centered, literacy-focused preschool curriculum with nine thematic units and parent involvement

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Bright Beginnings is an early childhood curriculum based in part on the High/Scope and The Creative Curriculum models, with additional emphasis on building early language and literacy skills. It consists of nine thematic units (language and literacy, mathematics, social and personal development, healthful living, scientific thinking, social studies, creative arts, physical development, and technology), each including "concept maps, literacy lessons, center activities, and home activities." Active exploration and interaction with children, adults, and materials are key components, teachers continually monitor children's progress, and parents sign a parent-school partnership agreement affirming active participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parents are required to sign a parent-school partnership agreement affirming their active participation in their child's education.
#### Constraints
- The curriculum as examined in PCER Consortium (2008) currently is not being used in preschool programs or supported by any organization, and its implementation cost is not available.

### Target Learners
- preschool children (pre-kindergarten, average age 4.5 years in the evaluated study)

### Target Learning Goals
- early language and literacy skills
- cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development
- school readiness in oral language, print knowledge, phonological processing, and math
