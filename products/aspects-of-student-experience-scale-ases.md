## Description
The ASES is a 35-item Likert-style survey developed with the American Physical Society Bridge Program to assess physics graduate students' reported experiences of social integration, academic support, mentorship, research, professional skills, and financial well-being.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASES instrument and dataset of physics graduate student responses**: The Aspects of Student Experience Scale (ASES) is a Likert-style survey of 35 items with five response options, "designed to capture physics graduate student experiences with various support structures that may exist within graduate programs." Developed in partnership with the American Physical Society Bridge Program, it aligns with recommendations covering social integration, academic success, mentorship, research experiences, professional skills, and financial well-being. The study dataset comprises 381 fully complete responses collected in Spring 2019 across 20 physics departments. (Robert P. Dalka et al. (2024))
