# Quarked! interactive website

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Quarked! is an interactive educational website that provides videos, games, science questions, downloadable activities, lesson plans, and a glossary for introducing subatomic physics to youth, educators, parents, and the general public.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quarked! interactive website with videos, games, and educator resources**: The Quarked! interactive website (www.quarked.org) launched in 2006 and introduces visitors to subatomic physics through 3D video clips, a Game Zone for youth aged 7 and up, an "Ask Mr. Marks" science question page, downloadable activities, lesson plans for teachers and parents, and a glossary. "The website has attracted over 100,000 unique visitors from over 60 countries" and continues to draw over 5,000 unique visitors per month. (A. Bean (2011))
