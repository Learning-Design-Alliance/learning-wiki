## Description
CALLIOPE is a browser-based source-grounded oral-assessment application developed by Nizam Kadir that manages assessment configuration, learner recordings and transcription, adaptive questioning, rubric-based AI scoring, educator review, and evidence export.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CALLIOPE source-grounded oral assessment workflow**: CALLIOPE is a browser-based oral assessment application that separates learner, educator and administrative responsibilities and integrates versioned instructional material, learner-turn recording and transcription, lexical retrieval of source chunks, adaptive follow-up questions, two-provider rubric scoring across six dimensions, educator review and exportable evidence. The article describes it as addressing "the engineering continuity between assessment configuration, spoken evidence, machine evaluation and educator review". Educators configure question, time allowance, rubric weights and descriptors, required concepts, terminology and versioned source material; published sources are versioned so later teaching-material changes do not silently change the evidential basis of an existing assessment. (Nizam Kadir (2026))
