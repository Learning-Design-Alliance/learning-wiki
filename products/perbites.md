## Description
PERbites is an online discipline-based education research summary blog in the ScienceBites family, created and maintained by graduate students for DBER researchers and STEM education practitioners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PERbites, a graduate-student-run DBER summary blog in the ScienceBites family**: PERbites is a graduate-student organization that publishes an online DBER literature blog, part of the larger ScienceBites project. Since 2018 it has published summaries of nearly 100 peer-reviewed papers, posting 1-2 summaries monthly for four years; "Each article takes around 5-6 hours to prepare and write" and posts should be readable in about 5 minutes. It uniquely targets education practitioners outside DBER research as well as early career researchers. (Nicholas T. Young et al. (2022))
- **The "bites" format: a standard five-part structure for short plain-language research summaries**: Each PERbites post follows a standard model, the "bites" format, producing an accessible summary of a recent paper readable in approximately 5 minutes. The structure states the article title, authors, affiliations, and where to access the full text; describes the overarching problem and why it matters; explains the new progress; concludes with the takeaway and what we still do not know; and briefly explains the implications. The article notes that "Communicating uncertainty can positively inﬂuence readers' perception of trustworthiness of results in the long-term". (Nicholas T. Young et al. (2022))
