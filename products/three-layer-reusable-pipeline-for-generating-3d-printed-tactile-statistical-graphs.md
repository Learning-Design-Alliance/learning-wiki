# Three-layer reusable pipeline for generating 3D-printed tactile statistical graphs

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An open-source JavaScript pipeline by Lawrence Obiuwevwi et al. that generates 3D-printable tactile statistical graphs from typed data or chart images for blind and low-vision statistics learners.
