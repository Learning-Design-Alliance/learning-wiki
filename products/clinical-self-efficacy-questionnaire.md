## Description
A 37-item self-report and 15-item case-based multiple-choice assessment developed with nursing-faculty collaboration to measure undergraduate nursing students' clinical self-efficacy before and after an intervention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Clinical Self-Efficacy Questionnaire (37 items, 0–100 five-point Likert scale) as the outcome measure**: The study's data-collection instrument for clinical self-efficacy. As printed, it is "the Clinical Self-Efficacy Questionnaire, comprising 37 items rated on a five-point Likert scale ranging from 0 to 100", with items drawn from nursing students' expected clinical skills and NCLEX Review textbooks. The instrument also included five case scenarios across five nursing domains each with three questions, yielding 15 multiple-choice items; higher scores indicated greater clinical self-efficacy. It was administered before the intervention and three months afterward. (Fallah-Karimi Samaneh et al. (2025))
