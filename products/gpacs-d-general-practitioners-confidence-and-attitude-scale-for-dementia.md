# GPACS-D: General Practitioners Confidence and Attitude Scale for Dementia

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The GPACS-D is a validated Likert-scale survey instrument developed by Mason Ron et al. to assess general practitioners' confidence, attitudes, and engagement regarding dementia care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GPACS-D: General Practitioners Confidence and Attitude Scale for Dementia**: The GPACS-D is a validated survey instrument for measuring GPs' confidence and attitudes towards dementia before and after targeted education. The article states "The GPACS-D comprises three subscales: Confidence in Clinical Abilities (six items), Attitude to Care (six items) and Engagement (three items)", validated using confirmatory factor analysis. It employs a Likert scale scored from 1 (strongly agree) to 5 (strongly disagree), with subscale scores standardised to a minimum of 1 and maximum of 5 so subscales can be compared. In this study a research assistant not associated with workshop delivery administered it immediately before (T1) and after (T2) each workshop. (Mason Ron et al. (2020))
