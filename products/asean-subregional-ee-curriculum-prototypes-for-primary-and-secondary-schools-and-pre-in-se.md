# ASEAN subregional EE curriculum prototypes for primary and secondary schools and pre-/in-service teacher training

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The UNESCO-UNEP International Environmental Education Programme, with Malaysia's Curriculum Development Centre and the University of the Philippines' ISMED, developed adaptable EE curriculum prototypes for the ASEAN subregion. The article reports that "Prototype EE curricula for primary and secondary schools as well as for the pre-service training of prima- ry and secondary school teacheis have thus been devel- oped for thc sub-region which can be easily adapted to the needs of each country." The primary prototype is built on four goal levels and thematic content; the secondary prototype uses a multidisciplinary approach with five themes (land, water, air, energy resources, life) infused into Science, Social studies, and Values and Moral education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adaptation to each country's ecological and socio-cultural characteristics, goals, objectives, methods and existing curricular framework, as the prototypes provide
#### Constraints
- Some prototypes were still in the process of final updating in view of the UNCED recommendations

### Target Learners
- ASEAN primary and secondary school students
- pre-service and in-service primary and secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- ecological foundations
- conceptual awareness
- investigation and evaluation
- issue resolution skill
- interdisciplinary integration of EE concepts
