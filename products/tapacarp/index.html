TapaCarp¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TapaCarp is a tangible user-interface-based CSCL environment developed for classroom training of carpenter apprentices by Cuendet, Dehler-Zufferey, Ortoleva, and Dillenbourg.

Components¶

  • A tangible interface used for a small part of a classroom activity: the multi-step activity design in which TapaCarp is used only for one portion

Claims¶

  • The multi-step activity proved usable, fostered workplace connections, and engaged teacher and students [+W]
  • Students' performance improved from day 1 to day 2 of the TapaCarp activity [+W]
  • The tangible nature of TapaCarp helped integrate it in the classroom environment [+W]
  • Pre-test/post-test learning gains showed no significant difference compared to a control group [-W]
