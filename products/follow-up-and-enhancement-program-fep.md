# Follow-Up and Enhancement Program (FEP)

## Description
A Nepal National Health Training Center and Nick Simons Institute programme in which certified trainers assess and coach skilled birth attendants at their worksites and report findings to health-facility management committees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Follow-Up and Enhancement Program (FEP) on-site assessment and coaching tool for skilled birth attendants**: FEP is a supportive supervision program devised in 2011 by Nepal's National Health Training Center in collaboration with the Nick Simons Institute, in which the knowledge, clinical skills and working environment of in-service trained skilled birth attendants are assessed directly at their worksites. Its core instrument is a paper FEP tool administered by SBA trainers, covering background information, knowledge and clinical assessment, clinical decision-making, infection prevention, and an enabling environment checklist, taking approximately 5 hours per SBA. Results are fed back to Health Facility Operation and Management Committees at district and central levels. (Thapa R. et al. (2023))
