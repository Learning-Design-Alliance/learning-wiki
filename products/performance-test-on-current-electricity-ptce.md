# Performance Test on Current Electricity (PTCE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A validated 10-item pretest/posttest developed by Olaniyan et al. to measure senior secondary students’ knowledge and problem solving in current electricity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Performance Test in Current Electricity (PTCE)**: The Performance Test in Current Electricity (PTCE) is the instrument used to pre-test and post-test both groups in this study. It was administered as a two-hour test at pre-test, with questions retrieved from students immediately afterwards, and a two-hour post-test was administered in the sixth week. Post-test PTCE scores served as the dependent variable in the ANCOVA analyses, with terminal results used beforehand to group students into three scoring levels. (Olaniyan et al. (2015))
