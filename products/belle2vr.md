## Description
Belle2VR is a Virginia Tech-developed virtual-reality application that lets learners explore and manipulate simulated events inside the Belle II particle detector.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Belle2VR virtual-reality application for exploring the Belle II particle detector**: Belle2VR, developed by Virginia Tech, lets users "virtually enter the particle physics detector of the Belle II experiment" at KEK in Tsukuba, Japan, which studies heavy quarks and leptons in searches for new physics. It reconstructs the detector interior, shows "realistic simulations of particles interacting with each other and with the detector elements", and lets users navigate the detector and control the time evolution of events, moving back and forth or stopping particles at a specific time. In the school activity, a researcher wears the helmet while a large screen shows the class what the researcher sees, then students enter the detector in first person with researcher guidance. (Ilaria De Angelis et al. (2023))
