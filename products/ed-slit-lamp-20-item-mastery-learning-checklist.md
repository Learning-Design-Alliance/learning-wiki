# ED SLIT LAMP 20-item mastery learning checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 20-item checklist developed to assess emergency physicians' performance of a comprehensive slit-lamp examination, with mastery defined as completing at least 18 items.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ED SLIT LAMP 20-item mastery learning checklist for the comprehensive slit-lamp exam**: A 20-item checklist scoring a complete slit-lamp examination, from identifying slit-lamp anatomy and applying a face shield through anterior chamber evaluation, cells-and-flare assessment, fluorescein application, and cobalt blue filter use. The minimum passing score was "determined to be 90%", and participants at Time 2 "were required to complete a minimum 18 of 20 checklist items to achieve mastery." Checklist performance at baseline and the final in-person assessment formed the study's primary outcome measure, with individual items analyzed by exact McNemar's test. (Hamou Samara BA et al. (2024))
