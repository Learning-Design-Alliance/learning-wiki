SDT-based Chinese-language ESP motivation questionnaire (adapted LLOS-IEA)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 23-item Chinese-language Likert questionnaire adapted by Liu (2016) from Noels's LLOS-IEA to measure seven self-determined motivation subtypes among Chinese college students studying English for Specific Purposes.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Introjected regulation was low among Chinese ESP learners except for an identity-maintenance item [+W]
  • An SDT-based questionnaire yields seven distinct, reliable motivation subtypes for Chinese college ESP learners [+W]
