# Breastfeeding: Essential Support Skills DVD (Mandarin version)

## Description
A short UK-developed breastfeeding-support training DVD with Mandarin voice-over, covering positioning and attachment, satisfying feeds, hand expression, and baby-led feeding for maternity-service healthcare professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Breastfeeding: Essential Support Skills DVD (Mandarin version)**: A short video training intervention, "Breastfeeding: Essential Support Skills DVD", developed by UK researchers and dubbed into Mandarin for this trial. It covers four educational sessions: "optimal P & A, the process of a satisfying feed shown by a baby of three months, hand expression demonstrated by a mother, and baby-led feeding demonstrated by an experienced baby of ten months", with a total length of 14 min 32 s. It was tested against a vaginal delivery DVD of similar duration in the randomised controlled trial. (Ma Yuan Ying et al. (2018))

### Claims
- [A short breastfeeding training DVD significantly increases healthcare professionals' total knowledge of positioning and attachment and hand expression immediately after viewing](../claims/dvd-training-increases-breastfeeding-knowledge.md) [+M]
- [The DVD significantly increases healthcare professionals' confidence in supporting positioning and attachment and hand expression, with no change in controls](../claims/dvd-training-increases-breastfeeding-confidence.md) [+M]
