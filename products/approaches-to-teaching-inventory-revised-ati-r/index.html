Approaches to Teaching Inventory-Revised (ATI-R)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A course-specific 22-item self-report inventory developed by Trigwell and Prosser for measuring academics' teacher/content-focused and student/learning-focused approaches to teaching.

Components¶

  • Approaches to teaching inventory framework: teacher/content versus student/learning approaches (ATI-R): The article organizes teaching approaches using Trigwell and Prosser's distinction between teacher/content-oriented and student/learning-oriented approaches, measured with the Approaches to Teaching Inventory-Revised (ATI-R). Teacher/content-oriented academics "concentrate on what they 'do' in teaching to transmit particular information," viewing students as passive recipients; student/learning-oriented academics concentrate on facilitating understanding and conceptual change, utilizing students' experiences. The ATI-R contains 11 ITTF items and 11 CCSF items rated on a 5-point scale, and the study uses it to predict email advice-giving behavior. (Lena Päuler-Kuppinger et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Academics with a higher student focus (ATI-R CCSF) advise more deep learning strategies in out-of-class email advice [+M]
  • Teacher-focused reflection instructions produced a greater proportion of teacher/content-oriented reflection extracts; no effect on student/learning-oriented extracts [~M]
