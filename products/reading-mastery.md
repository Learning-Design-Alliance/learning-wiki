# Reading Mastery: a Direct Instruction reading curriculum for grades K–6 in three versions

## Description
Reading Mastery is "one of several curriculum components that constitute the Direct Instruction curriculum from SRA/McGraw-Hill," designed to provide systematic reading instruction to students in grades K–6. It can serve as an intervention for struggling readers, a supplement to a core reading program, or a stand-alone program. It is available in three versions: Reading Mastery Classic (pre-K–3), Reading Mastery Plus (K–6), and Reading Mastery Signature Edition (K–5), the last with Reading, Oral Language/Language Arts, and Literature strands. Students are grouped by reading level based on program placement tests, and the program includes a continuous monitoring component.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are grouped with other students at a similar reading level, based on program placement tests
- The program typically spans one academic year
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- Elementary-age children above, at, or below grade level in reading performance
- English language learners
- Special education students

### Target Learning Goals
- Letter sounds, segmenting and blending words, vocabulary, and comprehension strategies
- Phonemic awareness, phonics, word analysis, fluency, spelling, decoding, and word recognition
- Oral language, communication, and writing skills
