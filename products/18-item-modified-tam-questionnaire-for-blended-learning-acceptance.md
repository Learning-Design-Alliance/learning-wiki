# 18-item modified TAM questionnaire for blended learning acceptance

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An 18-item, six-construct Likert questionnaire developed by Sun Xiaofen et al. to measure undergraduate medical imaging students’ perceived usefulness, ease of use, influencing factors, social support, satisfaction, and behavioral intention toward blended learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) as a framework for designing and evaluating blended learning platforms**: The article adopts the technology acceptance model, developed by Davis and based on reasoned action and planned behavior theories, which "explores the predictors of a user's potential acceptance/rejection of new technology and reflects their willingness to use the technology." The original TAM encompassed perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, attitude toward using, intention to use, and actual use; the study used a 31-item, six-dimension questionnaire adding external variables of interaction quality, computer self-efficacy, and perceived playfulness. The authors used TAM to design GC teaching materials and assess students' acceptance and use. (Huang Tzu-Hao et al. (2021))
