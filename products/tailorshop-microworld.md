## Description
Tailorshop is a semantically rich factory-management computer simulation developed for studying and assessing complex problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tailorshop microworld for assessing complex problem solving**: Tailorshop is a semantically rich computer-simulated microworld first published in 1981: "Tailorshop is a microworld, which realizes the production conditions of a small factory. The participant takes on the role of a manager of a small shirt factory." Participants manipulate ten exogenous input variables (workers, machines, raw material, salaries, social security contributions, maintenance, advertising, shop location, utility van, sales price) across a 24-variable connection diagram, with monthly reports and delayed effects. It stands for the broad interpretation of CPS but has weak psychometric properties. (Joachim Funke (2009))
