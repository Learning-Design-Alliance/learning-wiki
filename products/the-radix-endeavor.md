## Description
The Radix Endeavor is a multiplayer online learning game developed by the MIT Education Arcade for exploring secondary-level science and mathematics concepts through game-based quests and player interaction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Radix Endeavor multiplayer online learning game**: The Radix Endeavor is a multiplayer online game used in the study for "student exploration of high school level Mendelian genetics". It provides an open-ended quest context in which students make gameplay choices, and it generates the log data analyzed through the three GLA approaches. The article uses it as the setting for identifying content-specific gameplay decisions in genetics. (Rosenheck et al. (2021))
- **Trait examiner and trait decoder tools in the Radix genetics quest**: Within the Radix genetics quest, players can use tools including the trait examiner and the trait decoder. The study found that "use of the trait examiner tool was most likely to lead to success, though a greater number of trait decoder tool uses could also lead to success." These tools support different pathways to quest completion. (Cheng et al. (2017))
