## Description
A Heidelberg University multiple-mini-interview admission procedure using video-recorded simulated-patient interviews and trained-rater emotional-availability scoring to assess applicants’ interactional competencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IC-MD: MMI-based admission procedure assessing interactional skills via Emotional Availability scales rated from video-recorded simulated patient interviews**: The IC-MD (Interactional Competencies Medicine) is a multiple-mini-interview-based selection procedure used since 2023 in Heidelberg's Additional Aptitude Quota 1. Applicants take part in "a series of short interviews with actors as patients" in which "Clinical interactions are simulated, whereby interactional skills are assessed and not medical expertise." Interactions are video-recorded and rated in a standardized manner by trained raters with medical or psychological backgrounds using a manual on emotional availability with four main scales, each with five subscales, jointly yielding the IC-MD score. (Schütte C et al. (2025))
