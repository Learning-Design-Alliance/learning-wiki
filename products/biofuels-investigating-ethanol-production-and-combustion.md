# Biofuels: Investigating Ethanol Production and Combustion

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Lab-Aids classroom laboratory investigation kit in which students investigate ethanol production and combustion, including comparisons of fuel energy and related experimental-design concepts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lab-Aids Biofuels: Investigating Ethanol Production and Combustion classroom kit (Investigation 2)**: The agriscience laboratory experiment employed a kit designed by Lab-Aids titled Biofuels: Investigating Ethanol Production and Combustion. Specifically, students completed investigation two, titled Comparing the Energy Stored in Two Fuels, in which they compared the energy levels of ethanol and kerosene. Students formulated hypotheses, completed the experimental investigation, and employed mathematical formulae and calculations for testing their hypotheses. Major concepts included the chemical make-up of fuels, pollutants, experimental design and control, converting units of measurement, and averaging. (Blackburn et al. (2015))
