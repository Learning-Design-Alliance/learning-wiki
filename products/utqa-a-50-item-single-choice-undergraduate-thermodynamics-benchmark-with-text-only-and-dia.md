# UTQA: a 50-item single-choice undergraduate thermodynamics benchmark with text-only and diagram-based items

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
UTQA is an expert-developed 50-item single-choice benchmark assessment created by Anna Geißler and colleagues for evaluating undergraduate thermodynamics competence, released for reuse through Hugging Face.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UTQA: a 50-item single-choice undergraduate thermodynamics benchmark with text-only and diagram-based items**: UTQA is a 50-item single-choice benchmark for evaluating LLM competence in undergraduate thermodynamics, with "33 text-only questions and 17 diagram-based questions". Items target constructs such as state vs. path variables, sign conventions, and reversibility, with distractors reflecting common misconceptions. The benchmark is released at huggingface.co/datasets/herteltm/UTQA. (Anna Geißler et al. (2025))
