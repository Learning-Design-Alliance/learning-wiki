# To&Through Project

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A public online data tool developed and maintained by the To&Through Project that lets users explore educational attainment rates by Chicago Public Schools school, district, and community area.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **To&Through Community Milestones Tool**: A publicly available online data resource built on the earlier To&Through High School Milestones Tool, providing data on "five key milestones for college success—high school enrollment, high school graduation, college enrollment, college persistence, and college completion" for CPS students organized by the community area they live in. It was inspired by the Little Village Education Collaborative and developed from the belief that communities possess knowledge to solve their own challenges. The report serves as its accompanying research resource. (McKoy et al. (2021))

# To&Through Community Milestones Tool

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly available online data resource built on the earlier To&Through High School Milestones Tool, providing data on "five key milestones for college success—high school enrollment, high school graduation, college enrollment, college persistence, and college completion" for CPS students organized by the community area they live in. It was inspired by the Little Village Education Collaborative and developed from the belief that communities possess knowledge to solve their own challenges. The report serves as its accompanying research resource.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires coupling with local relationships and deep understanding of a community area's context to be useful to practitioners
#### Constraints
- Data come from CPS administrative records only and exclude private school students, about 12.4 percent of Chicago high school students per the 2018 ACS estimate

### Target Learners
- CPS high school students and the community-based practitioners, educators, and leaders serving them

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding and improving community-level high school and college attainment
