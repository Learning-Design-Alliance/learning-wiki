## Description
A University of Melbourne PEAK trial blended professional-development program combining asynchronous knee-osteoarthritis and telehealth modules with quizzes, simulated-patient consultation, and audited practice consultations for physiotherapists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEAK trial e-learning training program for physiotherapists**: The PEAK trial e-learning program is a blended asynchronous-plus-synchronous training package for physiotherapists covering best-practice knee OA management and telehealth delivery. It involved "self-directed learning modules, a mock video consultation with a researcher (simulated patient), and 4 audited practice video consultations with pilot patients with chronic knee pain". Modules ran on the University of Melbourne Canvas LMS, included quizzes, and the modules have since been released for wider clinician use. The program was evaluated qualitatively by interviews with all 15 trial physiotherapists. (Sarah E Jones et al. (2021))
