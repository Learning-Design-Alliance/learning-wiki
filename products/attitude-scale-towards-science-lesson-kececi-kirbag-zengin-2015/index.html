Attitude Scale towards Science Lesson (Kececi & Kirbag Zengin, 2015)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 31-item five-point Likert assessment developed by Kececi and Kirbag Zengin to measure students' attitudes toward science lessons.

Components¶

  • Attitude Scale towards Science Lesson (Kececi & Kirbag Zengin, 2015) used as pretest, posttest and monitoring test: The study measured attitudes with the 31-item, five-point Likert "Attitude Scale towards Science Lesson" developed by Kececi & Kirbag Zengin (2015), ranging from "Completely Agree" to "Completely Disagree". It was applied as pretest, posttest and as a monitoring test three months after the research process. Validated on 318 sixth-grade students, it yielded a Cronbach Alpha reliability coefficient of 0.87; scores were averaged across the 31 items to give each student an attitude score from 1 to 5. (Halil Ibrahim Yildirim (2018))
