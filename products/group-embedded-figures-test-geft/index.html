Group Embedded Figures Test (GEFT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A paper-and-pencil embedded-figures assessment developed by Oltman, Raskin, and Witkin to measure field dependence–independence through identification of simple forms hidden in complex geometric figures.

Components¶

  • Group Embedded Figures Test (GEFT) for classifying field dependence-independence: The Group Embedded Figures Test, developed by Oltman, Raskin and Witkin (1971), is a paper-and-pencil instrument with three sections in which participants identify simple forms hidden in complex geometric figures; the maximum score in this study was 18. The article calls it "the most common instrument applied by researchers" for assessing field-dependence/independence and notes it requires "only a minimum level of language proficiency". The authors report the instrument's published reliability as .89 and validity as .82, and computed a study reliability of .79. (Elaheh Hamed Mahvelati et al. (2012))
