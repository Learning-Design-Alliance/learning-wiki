## Description
OPS-English is a Ministry of Education textbook-based English curriculum programme for Malaysian Form One students, providing ten units of task-based listening, speaking, and vocabulary activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OPS-English programme textbook and lesson materials**: OPS-English is a Ministry of Education textbook-based programme for Malaysian Form One students, organised into ten units of task-based activities integrating listening and speaking. The article used "a series of 10 lesson plans (5 lesson plans for the experimental group and 5 lesson plans for the control group)" drawn from the textbook, with activities designed to give all students opportunities to use English for oral communication in a fun way. (Manesha Kaur Rajendra Singh et al. (2015))
