## Description
Digital Promise's portfolio of an evidence-gathering framework, a collaborative analytics workspace, and a survey-based improvement initiative that supports schools and education organizations conducting rapid-cycle edtech testing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Data infrastructure tools supporting rapid-cycle testing: Edtech Pilot Framework, TeamSpace, and Dynamic Learning Project**: The report describes three tools from Digital Promise's portfolio that provide infrastructure for piloting and data analytics. The Edtech Pilot Framework guides educators and product developers through an eight-step evidence gathering process with free online tools and videos. TeamSpace is a cloud-based analytics workspace helping educational organizations securely and collaboratively analyze complex data from one or more digital sources. The Dynamic Learning Project uses a Qualtrics platform with snapshot pre/post surveys, giving schools analyzed and visualized data at the end of each eight-week cycle. (Angevine et al. (2019))

# Digital Promise Edtech Pilot Framework

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Edtech Pilot Framework is a free, open-source Digital Promise resource built from a 2015 study with the University of California at Davis to help education leaders and educators build skills in conducting, interpreting, and using research for edtech decisions. It "includes nearly 150 open-source tools and resources to support educators throughout the process of conducting pilot studies." The 2018 survey examined who accesses the Framework, their motivations, and its usefulness, and the report suggests it has supported League members in more regularly accessing and using evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intended for education leaders and educators conducting edtech pilot studies to inform procurement and adoption decisions
#### Constraints
- The survey could only uncover potential correlations between self-reported evidence use and the Framework

### Target Learners
- K-12 education leaders
- educators
- coaches and instructional specialists

### Target Learning Goals
- Building capacity to conduct, interpret, and use research and evidence in edtech pilot studies and procurement decisions
