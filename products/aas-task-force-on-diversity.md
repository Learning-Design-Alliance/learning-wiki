# AAS Task Force on Diversity

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An American Astronomical Society task-force framework for advancing diversity and inclusion in astronomy graduate education through coordinated top-down incentives and bottom-up departmental action.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Theory of change: combining top-down incentives from funding agencies and the AAS with bottom-up departmental action**: The paper's underlying framework holds that large, distributed organizations change through coordinated pressure at two levels. Top-down, funding agencies should use "the central role funding has in the structure and function of graduate astronomy departments" to fund diversity and inclusion activities, while the AAS measures program climate, invests in evidence-based practices, and recognizes adopting departments. Bottom-up, departments are given goals and recommendations for admissions, retention, and data collection. The paper argues diversity and inclusion should be both goals and "principles to embody in the change process." (AAS Task Force on Diversity et al. (2019))
