# MyOpenMath/IMathAS

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MyOpenMath, powered by the open-source IMathAS backend, is an online learning and assessment platform developed for automated grading, dynamic question authoring, practice, and feedback, especially in higher-education STEM courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MyOpenMath/IMathAS open-source assessment platform**: MyOpenMath is a free-to-use online LMS whose backend is the fully open-source IMathAS software, based on PHP, which allows automated grading of student responses. Beyond multiple choice and matching, it supports numbers, strings, drawing objects, dynamic content, randomizing macros, conditional feedback, flexible grading schemas, practice mode, and LTI export of assessments or entire courses into a home institution's LMS. (Joshua Tan et al. (2021))
