## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Helpful Responses Questionnaire (HRQ) as empathy outcome measure**: The HRQ is "a six-item free-response questionnaire measuring therapeutic empathy" used to assess learner outcomes in MI training. Participants respond to vignettes open-endedly; two blinded external reviewers score each response 1–5 using standard criteria (a 1 is a roadblock, a 5 a complex reflection), giving total scores from 6 to 30, averaged across coders. (Simper Trevor et al. (2020))
