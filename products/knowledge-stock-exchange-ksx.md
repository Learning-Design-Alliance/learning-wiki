# Knowledge Stock Exchange (KSX)

## Description
A virtual stock-market platform in which learners trade, revise, and collaboratively evaluate editable knowledge artifacts using virtual currency and an automated market maker.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Knowledge Stock Exchange: a virtual stock market variant for incentive-compatible knowledge sharing**: The KSX is a new type of Virtual Stock Market in which virtual stocks are editable knowledge artifacts rather than fixed contracts, traded with play money (Virt$) via Hanson's LMSR automated market maker. The article distinguishes it from prediction, idea and preference markets by four characteristics: an incentive-compatible learning mechanism over months, editable stocks whose price changes give "prompt feedback on the current quality of a knowledge artifact", incentivized continuous revaluation and arbitrage through improvement, and crowd-sourced rather than authority-proposed stocks. (Heusler Andreas et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [KSX participation is associated with substantially higher MOOC completion rates (64.9% vs 6.4%), persisting after propensity score matching](../claims/ksx-participation-higher-mooc-completion.md) [+M]
