# Four-element framework for family engagement: Capacity, Process, Infrastructure, and Outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The playbook organizes family engagement improvement around four interrelated elements. Capacity "develop[s] skills, confidence, and disposition for authentic home and school partnerships" through professional learning; Process covers selecting and implementing evidence-based practices; Infrastructure allocates time, resources, staff, and policies; Outcomes monitor, evaluate, and report progress. The elements form "a dynamic cycle of continuous improvement" and are presented through illustrative tables populated from needs-assessment findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A needs assessment identifies key areas requiring focused attention and resources before strategies are selected
#### Constraints
- The framework is developed for the middle school context and school and district leaders implementing family engagement plans

### Target Learners
- middle school students and their families

### Learning Goals
- strengthening school-family partnerships to support student attendance, social-emotional development, and academic outcomes
