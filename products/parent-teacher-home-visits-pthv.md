# Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) is a family-engagement programme run by the Parent Teacher Home Visits organization in which educators and families build relationships through structured voluntary home visits.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) family engagement model**: PTHV is a family engagement program co-created by parents and educators using community organizing principles of shared leadership. Teachers conduct 30-40 minute home visits in which they listen, ask questions, and make observations to improve instruction. The model is structured by five non-negotiables, including that "Visits are voluntary and arranged in advance" and "Educators go in pairs and reflect." (Academic Performance — Parent Teacher Home Visits (2021))

Teachers conduct 30-40 minute home visits in which they listen, ask questions, and make observations to improve instruction. ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers are trained and compensated for visits
- Visits focus on hopes and dreams
- Educators visit a cross-section of students (not a targeted intervention)
#### Constraints
- No additional staffing required
- None required for professional development or technology

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 1-5 and their families

### Target Learning Goals
- Family engagement, student attendance, academic performance, and social-emotional outcomes

## Description
PTHV is a family engagement program co-created by parents and educators using community organizing principles of shared leadership. Participating teachers conduct "30-40 minute home visits in which educators listen, ask questions, and make observations that they can take back to their classrooms to improve instruction for the learner." The model was evaluated in District of Columbia Public Schools in grades 1–5.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers are trained and compensated for visits
- Visits are voluntary and arranged in advance
#### Constraints
- No additional staffing required; no professional development or technology beyond the model's own training is required

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades 1–5 and their families

### Target Learning Goals
- Family engagement and improved instruction informed by home-visit observations
