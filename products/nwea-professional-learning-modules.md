# NWEA professional learning modules

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NWEA's professional-learning module suite trains educators to use MAP Growth reports, interpret assessment information, set growth goals, and apply broader formative-assessment practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NWEA professional learning modules supporting MAP Growth implementation and formative practice**: Table 1 of the article lists professional learning modules that support instructional uses of MAP Growth: Responsive Teaching and Learning (assessment-empowered classrooms, not MAP Growth specific), MAP Growth: Basics, Applying Reports, Informing Instruction, and Focusing on Growth. Modules cover "formative assessment practices that are independent of MAP Growth and are based on principles of assessment literacy" as well as MAP Growth-specific training on interpreting score reports and setting growth goals. (Meyer et al. (2022))
