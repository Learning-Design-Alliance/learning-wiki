## Description
The air:bit project’s cloud-based platform provides air-quality data storage, retrieval, visualization, programming and assembly guides, video lessons, and open educational resources for schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **air:bit online platform and open educational resources**: The air:bit platform is a cloud-based system for storing, exploring and visualizing air quality data from air:bit kits and external sources, consisting of three parts: a web application with educational resources and interactive visualizations, a service for retrieving and uploading datasets, and a backend storage service hosted on Google Cloud Platform to scale to more schools. It integrates student data with data from NILU and MET retrieved through their open APIs, making it easier for students to explore heterogeneous data. Guides on soldering, assembly and programming each sensor, plus video lectures on air pollution, are openly available at airbit.uit.no. (Bjørn Fjukstad et al. (2019))
