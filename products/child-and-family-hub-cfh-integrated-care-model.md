# Child and Family Hub (CFH) integrated care model

## Description
A co-designed model run by participating Child and Family Hubs that integrates health and social care through family-engagement supports, practitioner training, referral pathways, learning collaboratives, and co-location.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Child and Family Hub integrated care model with eight components including practitioner training and referral pathways**: The CFH is "a co-designed model of care which integrates health and social care through eight separate components". Four components aim to improve family engagement and access to care, including a "no-wrong-door" approach, parenting support, partnership with families, and a wellbeing coordination program. Four practitioner-directed components include training using Family Partnership Model principles, the Parent Engagement Resource, mapped referral pathways providing a community directory of local services, monthly learning collaborative meetings forming a community of practice, and co-location enabling 'warm referral' pathways. (Loveday Sarah et al. (2024))
