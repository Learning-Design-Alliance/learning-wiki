## Description
A structured 11-cycle classroom-talk intervention developed by the Cambridge Primary Review Trust and the University of York for Year 5 primary teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Dialogic Teaching intervention programme (11 cycles, mentoring, video, handbooks)**: A Year 5 classroom-talk intervention developed by the Cambridge Primary Review Trust and the University of York, entailing "a structured programme of 11 cycles arranged in two school terms, preceded by induction and training days". Phase 1 expanded talk repertoires; phase 2 applied them in English, maths, science, and a non-core subject. Each cycle had a compulsory directed focus and an adaptable responsive focus. Schools received handbooks, recording equipment, and mentor support; cost was estimated at £52 per pupil per year. (Tim Jay et al. (2017))

### Claims
- [Dialogic Teaching raised attainment in English and science (two additional months' progress) and maths (one additional month) in Year 5 pupils compared to control schools](../claims/dialogic-teaching-two-months-english-science-one-month-maths.md) [+M]
- [FSM pupils in Dialogic Teaching schools made two additional months' progress in English, science, and maths compared to FSM children in control schools](../claims/dialogic-teaching-fsm-two-months-all-subjects.md) [+M]
