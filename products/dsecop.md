# DSECOP

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
DSECOP is a free, open-source collection of short data-science instructional modules for undergraduate physics, created by fellows of the DSECOP group and supplied with lesson plans, exercises, and instructor solutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DSECOP free and open-source data science modules for undergraduate physics**: DSECOP provides "a collection of free and open-source modules created by the group's fellows who are Ph.D. students and recent Ph.D. graduates." Each module introduces a specific data science topic applied to a physics concept, consists of "a lesson plan, exercises, and solutions for the instructor," and is designed to be short enough to fit into a pre-existing course without significant alterations. At publication, 21 posted modules span common undergraduate physics courses, organized by a 100/200/300-level numbering system with suggested prerequisites, and all modules run on Google Colab in a browser or locally with Conda. (Karan Shah et al. (2024))
