## Description
LapApp is a structured laparoscopic appendectomy training and evaluation programme developed and implemented by Stavanger University Hospital for surgical trainees and their trainers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LapApp structured training programme for laparoscopic appendectomy**: The LapApp programme is a structured training programme for laparoscopic appendectomy developed and implemented at Stavanger University Hospital, in which "the training programme consisted of an introductory course for the trainees and a train the trainer-course for the trainers, before implementing structured training and evaluation of each learning situation". Each real-time supervised surgery is evaluated by the trainer on 8 predefined procedural steps plus overall performance on a 6-point scale from 1 (unable to perform) to 6 (expert performance), with proficiency defined as scoring 5 or 6 overall. The present cohort study drew its 142 evaluation forms from this programme. (Skjold-Ødegaard B et al. (2024))
