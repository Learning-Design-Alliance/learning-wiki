# Open annotated dataset of 2,348 teacher utterances labeled for funneling and focusing questions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An open-source dataset released by Sterling Alic et al. containing 2,348 annotated teacher–student exchanges from fourth- and fifth-grade mathematics classrooms, labeled for funneling, focusing, or neither questioning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Turn-level annotations for dialogic discourse moves (on/off task, uptake, focusing questions, student reasoning)**: Expert turn-level annotations accompanying the transcripts for five discourse features: student and teacher on-task instruction, teachers' uptake of student ideas, focusing questions, and student reasoning. Experts annotated "a sample of 2,348 utterance pairs" for on-task instruction, uptake and focusing questions, and separately coded 2,000 student utterances for reasoning based on the MQI item "Student Provide Explanations", with "Inter-rater agreement on calibration set (n=200) was 90%". The coding scheme is released with the dataset. (Dorottya Demszky et al. (2022))
