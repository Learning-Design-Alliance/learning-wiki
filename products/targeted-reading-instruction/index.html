Targeted Reading Instruction¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Targeted Reading Instruction (TRI) is a one-to-one tutoring programme for struggling kindergarten and first-grade readers that provides brief daily instruction in foundational literacy skills with webcam-supported teacher coaching.

Components¶

  • Targeted Reading Instruction (TRI): one-to-one tutoring model for struggling K-1 readers: TRI is a one-to-one tutoring model "designed in particular for isolated rural schools." Teachers work individually with struggling kindergarten and first-grade readers for 15 minutes a day, using "re-reading, word work, and guided oral reading" during sessions. Instruction focuses on "oral language, decoding, writing, comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency skills." Webcam technology connects teachers with TRI coaches for training and follow-up. (Targeted Reading Instruction (TRI) (2025))
  • Targeted Reading Intervention (TRI) framework: diagnostic, teacher-delivered one-on-one instruction with remote webcam coaching: The TRI is a diagnostic early reading intervention and professional development program delivered by the classroom teacher in daily 15-minute one-on-one sessions, supported by a remote literacy coach using webcam technology for real-time interaction and feedback. Grounded in an ecological systems perspective, automaticity theory, and construction–integration theory, it aims to help teachers individualize instruction for struggling readers. The article states the TRI "uses a diagnostic teaching approach to help classroom teachers improve the reading skills of students who are struggling with reading acquisition". It was designed for the general education classroom, not specifically for ELs. (Steven J. Amendum et al. (2018))
  • TRI 15-minute session structure of Rereading for Fluency, Word Work, and Guided Oral Reading across four instructional levels: Each TRI session is composed of three instructional activities that build on one another so the student reaches fluent reading with beginning comprehension. "Three instructional activities compose each 15- minute TRI session: Rereading for Fluency, Word Work, and Guided Oral Reading." Strategies vary across four progressively more challenging instructional levels (pink, blue, green, purple), and teachers use the TRI Diagnostic Map to plan and chart daily progress and select texts matched to the student's instructional reading level. (Steven J. Amendum et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Struggling English learners in TRI classrooms significantly outperformed control struggling ELs on spring Letter-Word Identification (g = .43) [+M]
  • Struggling English learners in TRI classrooms significantly outperformed control struggling ELs on spring Word Attack (g = .45) [+M]
  • TRI treatment did not significantly improve spring Passage Comprehension for struggling English learners [~M]
