# ICILS 2023 Flanders instrument measuring teachers’ computational thinking content knowledge

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 16-item conventional-format test developed for ICILS 2023 in Flanders to measure lower-secondary teachers’ and senior pre-service teachers’ computational-thinking content knowledge across five framework aspects.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ICILS 2023 Flanders instrument measuring teachers’ computational thinking content knowledge (16 multiple-choice and short constructed response items)**: A direct, conventional-format test of lower secondary teachers’ computational thinking content knowledge developed as part of ICILS 2023 in Flanders. The final tool consists of “16 multiple-choice and short constructed response questions”, deliberately excluding items requiring familiarity with a particular programming language. It covers the five aspects of the Fraillon et al. (2023) framework and was calibrated on 352 pre-service teachers, yielding “favorable item and overall instrument characteristics”. (Monteyne S. et al. (2024))
