## Description
ResuscitationVR is an immersive virtual-reality pediatric emergency simulation program developed by i3 Simulations in London, in which medical and nursing learners manage interactive emergency scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ResuscitationVR pediatric emergency simulation with immersive audio and character interaction**: ResuscitationVR (i3 Simulations, London, UK) is a VR simulation program, localized to Italian for this pilot, in which the student plays the emergency department physician managing pediatric scenarios. "Immersive audio provides sounds typical of the setting (monitor alarms, patient’s moans, the mother’s vocalizations, and intercom announcements), which adds stress and creates a realistic environment." The student interacts with characters (paramedic, nurse, respiratory therapist, a distraught mother as a potential distraction) and with equipment and medications in response to changing patient states; medication doses or age-based equipment sizing are not required. Sessions ran on Oculus Quest 2 headsets. (Savino Sandro et al. (2023))
