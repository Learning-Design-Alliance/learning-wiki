# Quantum puzzle visualization tool representing quantum computation exactly in visual form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Quarks Interactive educational software tool in which learners construct quantum circuits visually by arranging matrix puzzle pieces and solving quantum-computation problems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hello Qiskit — a puzzle game providing game-based intuition for qubits and gates**: Hello Qiskit is a variant of the Hello Quantum app, included in the textbook as an interactive, game-based way for readers to become familiar with basic principles of quantum computing without linear algebra. It is based on a visualization of two qubit states and gates; the game itself does not teach quantum computing directly but creates a context that helps people learn, with explanations interspersed as intros and outros around puzzles. Topics mirror the first two chapters, from bits to a universal gate set, ending with Bell's inequalities. (Wootton et al. (2020))
