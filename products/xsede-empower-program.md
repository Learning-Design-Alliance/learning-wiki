# XSEDE-EMPOWER program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A National Computational Science Institute programme supported by Shodor that places undergraduates in mentored computational research positions and develops them through learner, apprentice, and intern participation tiers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **XSEDE-EMPOWER three-tier undergraduate research participation ladder: learner, apprentice, intern**: The XSEDE-EMPOWER program, run under the National Computational Science Institute with Shodor support, creates funded undergraduate research positions in faculty mentors' research groups. It organizes participation into three tiers by skill level: "learner, apprentice, and intern". A learner with no scientific computing experience builds skills through online tutorials, workshops, and self-learning; an apprentice begins transforming knowledge into skills with additional training in debugging and performance tools; after completing these two levels in two semesters, students are accepted as interns. (Tulin Kaman et al. (2022))
