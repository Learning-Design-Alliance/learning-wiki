## Description
A five-year, teacher-led professional-development programme for secondary physics and physical-science teachers in high-needs districts, run through classroom action research, graduate study, conference presentation, collaboration, and leadership development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learner-driven professional development modeled on induction: teachers collaboratively extract general principles of teaching and learning from evidence**: The article frames teacher learning through a heuristic contrasting expert-provided principles with a model in which teacher participants extract principles of teaching and learning collaboratively through evidence-based activities such as conducting their own educational research. Induction is described as "a complex and iterative form of reasoning" moving from specific instances toward general rules, analogous to the work of communities of scientists. Streamline to Mastery operationalizes this via lesson-sharing, self-initiated research, and conference presentation, with the additional goal of teachers becoming agents of broader educational change. (Mike Ross et al. (2014))
