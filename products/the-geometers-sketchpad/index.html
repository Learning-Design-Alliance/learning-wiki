The Geometer's Sketchpad¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Geometer's Sketchpad is dynamic geometry software developed by Key Curriculum Press for constructing, manipulating, and exploring geometric figures and relationships.

Components¶

  • Geometer's Sketchpad (GSP) dynamic geometry software with Angles and Polygons worksheets: An instructional setup combining Geometer's Sketchpad dynamic geometry software with researcher-developed worksheets for the 7th-grade unit Angles and Polygons. Worksheets contained "open-ended and gap-filling types of questions" with clearly stated GSP operations, and were described as "kinds of browser and inquisitive". Students drew, dragged, and changed features of shapes to discover concepts, with the teacher guiding and directing with questions. (Kesan et al. (2013))

Claims¶

  • Seventh graders learning geometry with GSP dynamic geometry software scored significantly higher on a geometry achievement posttest than peers taught by traditional methods [+M]
  • GSP-taught students retained significantly more geometry learning one month after instruction than traditionally taught students [+M]
