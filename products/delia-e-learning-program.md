## Description
DeliA is a German-language asynchronous Moodle-based delirium education programme developed for nurses and other nursing-home staff, combining expert videos, case vignettes, interactive questions, drag-and-drop activities, and pocket cards.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-module competence-based e-learning curriculum on delirium for nurses and GPs in nursing homes**: A competence-based curriculum for interdisciplinary e-learning developed in the DeliA project, built on the German Qualifications Framework competence concept and focused on knowledge, skills and attitudes. The article states: "The competencies were grouped into six modules and encompassed 120 competencies in total", covering definitions and consequences, causes and risk factors, symptoms and diagnostics, therapy and management, prevention, and terminal delirium. Competencies were formulated from an exploratory literature search in PubMed, CINAHL and grey literature. (Molitor Vincent et al. (2025))

### Claims

- [In the pilot study, nurses' subjective confidence in recognizing delirium increased from a median of 5 to 7 on a 10-point scale (r = 0.92)](../claims/elearning-delirium-recognition-confidence-gain.md) [+M]
