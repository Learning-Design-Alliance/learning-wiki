# ICO-OCEX (International Council of Ophthalmology-Ophthalmic Clinical Evaluation Exercise)

## Description
A 34-item formative workplace-based assessment tool developed by the International Council of Ophthalmology to evaluate ophthalmology residents’ non-procedural patient-encounter skills using behaviorally descriptive Dreyfus-based rating levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ICO-OCEX: a 34-item workplace-based assessment tool with four constructs and a three-point Dreyfus rubric**: The ICO-OCEX (International Council of Ophthalmology-Ophthalmic Clinical Evaluation Exercise) is a workplace-based formative assessment tool consisting of 34 items measuring four constructs (Interview Skills, Examination Skills, Interpersonal Skills, Case Presentation Skills) on a three-point Dreyfus scale ('novice, beginner, competent'). It employs 'behaviorally descriptive milestones based on the Dreyfus model to address inconsistent grading interpretations.' The finalized instrument with behavioral descriptors and rating rubric is provided as supplementary material. (Gull Ambreen et al. (2026))
- **The Dreyfus Model of Skill Acquisition structures the ICO-OCEX assessment framework**: The article uses the Dreyfus Model of Skill Acquisition as its conceptual framework, guiding the development and validation of the ICO-OCEX formative assessment tool. The model maps three training levels (early, mid, senior) onto three Dreyfus stages (novice, beginner, competent), which correspond to the tool's three-point rating rubric. The authors state the tool 'ensures the systematic progression of residents through the Dreyfus stages, enabling them to acquire the competencies needed for autonomy and patient safety.' (Gull Ambreen et al. (2026))
