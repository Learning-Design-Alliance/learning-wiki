## Description
aiPlato is an AI-enabled homework and tutoring platform developed by Atharva Dange and colleagues that supports open-ended symbolic physics problem solving through stepwise feedback, hints, solution disclosure, AI tutor chat, and human-tutor escalation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Artificial Intelligent Tutoring Systems (AITS): combining ITS scaffolding with AI adaptability, centered on productive struggle**: The authors propose Artificial Intelligent Tutoring Systems (AITS) as an emerging category of platform that "combine the structured scaffolding of ITS with the adaptability, personalization, and real-time feedback enabled by artificial intelligence." Unlike correctness-focused homework systems, AITS are "centered on conceptual understanding": they highlight misconceptions, acknowledge partial progress, and offer guidance tailored to the student's approach during problem solving. A distinguishing feature is their focus on productive struggle, with feedback addressing root causes of mistakes, and they aim to interpret symbolic, multi-step reasoning in ways general-purpose LLM tutors cannot consistently replicate. aiPlato is presented as a pilot instance of this category. (Atharva Dange et al. (2026))
