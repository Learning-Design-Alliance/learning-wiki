## Description
A written situational assessment instrument developed and used by language-education researchers to elicit and evaluate learners' pragmatic speech-act production.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Discourse Completion Test (DCT) for assessing apology speech act appropriateness**: The study used a Discourse Completion Test as its pre- and post-test measure. The DCT is described as "a kind of questionnaire which contains series of concisely defined situations used to elicit special speech acts". The pre-test consisted of four apology scenarios varying social status and social distance, and the post-test comprised 12 situations. Responses were scored on a five-point analytic Likert appropriateness scale following Farahian, Rezaee and Gholami (2012), where "scale of 5 indicates 'completely appropriate'". (Shima Rajabi et al. (2015))
