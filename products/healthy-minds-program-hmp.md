## Description
Healthy Minds Innovations' Healthy Minds Program is a free smartphone-based meditation app that delivers structured audio lessons and guided practices for cultivating well-being skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Healthy Minds Program (HMP) four-week smartphone meditation course**: The Healthy Minds Program is a free smartphone-based meditation app constructed around the ACIP model. The four-week Foundations course studied here "includes two introductory audio lessons and two introductory guided meditations... followed by one week of content for each pillar of well-being," with two brief podcast lessons (5 to 7 minutes each) and three guided meditations per week. "In total, the intervention contains 10 lessons and 14 guided meditations." Participants choose between two guides, active or sitting practices, and practice lengths of 5 to 30 minutes, requiring only minutes a day. (Hirshberg Matthew J. et al. (2022))
