# Healthy Minds Program (HMP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Healthy Minds Innovations' Healthy Minds Program is a free smartphone-based meditation app that delivers structured audio lessons and guided practices for cultivating well-being skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Healthy Minds Program (HMP) four-week smartphone meditation course**: The Healthy Minds Program is a free smartphone-based meditation app constructed around the ACIP model. The four-week Foundations course studied here "includes two introductory audio lessons and two introductory guided meditations... followed by one week of content for each pillar of well-being," with two brief podcast lessons (5 to 7 minutes each) and three guided meditations per week. "In total, the intervention contains 10 lessons and 14 guided meditations." Participants choose between two guides, active or sitting practices, and practice lengths of 5 to 30 minutes, requiring only minutes a day. (Hirshberg Matthew J. et al. (2022))
- **Awareness, Connection, Insight, and Purpose (ACIP) training-based model of well-being**: The ACIP model, proposed by Dahl and colleagues, holds that well-being rests on four trainable pillars: "awareness, connection, insight, and purpose (ACIP)". Awareness comprises attention and meta-awareness skills; connection emphasizes prosocial dispositions such as gratitude and kindness; insight targets clarity about the nature of one's identity and experience; and purpose aims to clarify values and apply them in daily life. The model claims these pillar skills "demonstrate plasticity" and that this plasticity "can be harnessed through intentional practice," specifically meditation training. The Healthy Minds Program app and this trial's hypotheses were constructed around this framework. (Hirshberg Matthew J. et al. (2022))

### Claims
- [A four-week smartphone meditation app reduces psychological distress in school system employees at post-intervention and three-month follow-up](../claims/hmp-reduces-psychological-distress-school-employees.md) [+M]
- [Most participants assigned to the app downloaded it, but fewer engaged, averaging about 11 days and 128 minutes of practice over four weeks](../claims/hmp-app-engagement-adherence-pattern.md) [~M]
- [The meditation app improves school employees' self-reported well-being skills (self-compassion, loneliness, cognitive defusion, perseverative thinking, well-being, meaning), persisting at follow-up except for mindful action](../claims/hmp-improves-well-being-skills-persisting.md) [+M]
