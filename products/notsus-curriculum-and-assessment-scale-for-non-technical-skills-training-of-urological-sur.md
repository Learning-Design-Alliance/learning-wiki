# NoTSUS curriculum and assessment scale for non-technical skills training of urological surgeons

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Delphi-refined, simulation-based non-technical-skills training programme for urological surgeons using ureteroscopy scenarios, faculty supervision, structured debriefing, and the NoTSUS assessment scale.

### Claims

- [Participants' NoTSUS scores improved significantly across the four simulation scenarios in each session, with the largest gain between sessions 1 and 3](../claims/notsus-improvement-across-scenarios.md) [+M]
- [The NoTSUS rating scale correlates strongly with the validated NoTSS scale and shows inter-rater agreement between the two NTS-trained expert raters](../claims/notsus-scale-correlates-with-notss.md) [+M]
- [Participants rated the NoTSUS course and full immersion simulation highly, with a mean Likert score of 4.5 out of 5 across survey parameters](../claims/notsus-course-positive-educational-value.md) [+M]
- [Participants' ureteroscopy experience, overall clinical experience, and prior NTS training did not significantly affect NoTSUS scores](../claims/experience-did-not-affect-notsus-scores.md) [+W]
- [NoTSS-trained expert raters scored participants significantly differently from non-trained raters, which the authors cite as highlighting the value of training the trainers](../claims/trained-vs-non-trained-rater-score-difference.md) [+W]
