## Description
A monthly International Astronomical Search Collaboration programme run with PACS e-Lab in which African teams analyze supplied astronomical image datasets and submit asteroid-candidate reports for expert validation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PACS e-Lab: Pan-African Citizen Science e-Laboratory platform for hands-on astronomy and space science participation**: PACS e-Lab is described as "a non-profit organization/platform dedicated to advancing STEM education, promoting citizen science, and enabling entry-level research participation through hands-on activities in astronomy and space science across Africa". It uses collaborations, digital technologies, e-learning/e-science, volunteers and innovative projects to democratise astronomy and space science, and has run science campaigns in about 50 countries. Its programmes include asteroid searches with IASC, exoplanet observations with NASA Exoplanet Watch, SkyMapper collaboration, astrophotography, double-star research, robotic telescope access, telescope distribution and free astronomy lectures. (Barbosa D et al. (2026))
