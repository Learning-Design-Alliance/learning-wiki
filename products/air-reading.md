## Description
Air Reading is an assessment-driven virtual tutoring programme that provides frequent small-group foundational reading instruction through qualified live tutors and is operated by Air Tutors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Air Reading assessment-driven virtual tutoring program**: Air Reading is "an assessment-driven virtual tutoring program designed to improve students' foundational reading skills in Kindergarten through 8th grade". Students receive live virtual instruction from consistent tutors throughout the school year, with comprehensive one-on-one diagnostics informing group placement and bi-weekly progress assessments. Tutors deliver explicit, skill-based instruction using a systematic reading curriculum, and all sessions are tracked and monitored on the program's proprietary platform. (Neitzel et al. (2025))
- **Consistent small-group live virtual tutoring with embedded assessment**: The Air Reading model pairs small groups of students with the same tutor over time and embeds assessment into placement and progress monitoring. "Small groups of up to four students are paired with the same highly-qualified tutor in 30-minute sessions four times each week." Diagnostics identify learning needs and inform group placement, while bi-weekly assessments track progress, and sessions are monitored on a proprietary platform to maintain consistent standards. (Neitzel et al. (2025))
