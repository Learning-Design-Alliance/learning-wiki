Embodied simulated-patient training application for ACT-oriented psychotherapy practice with spoken dialogue¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Unity application developed by Pascal Riachi et al. that provides spoken, embodied virtual-patient simulations and automated ACT-consistency evaluation for psychotherapist training and research.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • GPT-4o mini achieved the lowest ACT balance MAE (6.12) in replicating human supervisor ratings across 49 full transcripts [+W]
  • Expert evaluation found the virtual patient realistic and immediate ACT feedback increased therapists' awareness of intervention choices [+W]
  • GPT-4o mini showed progressive turn-level convergence with accumulating context while larger models showed increasing or stable error [+W]
