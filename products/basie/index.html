Description¶

A downloadable software tool released by John Deke et al. to perform the probabilistic computations required for implementing the BASIE framework.

Components¶

  • BASIE probability tool (downloadable software): A downloadable software artifact released with the guide. The page instructs readers to "Download the BASIE probability tool", making the framework's probabilistic computations accessible without researchers building their own tooling. It accompanies the practical guide as one of two released computational resources supporting BASIE implementation. (John Deke et al. (2022))
