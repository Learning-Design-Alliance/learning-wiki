# Concept Cat programme

## Description
A 30-week whole-class early-years intervention developed by the Concept Cat team that teaches one early conceptual vocabulary word per week through whole-class instruction, play, home activities, and review.

The report states "The average cost of delivering Concept Cat was £1,206.80 per setting or £30.94 per pupil per year over three years", assuming all children aged three to four in a setting participate each year. Costs include direct training costs, staff cover for training, physical resources, and Concept Cat coach visits. (Miguel Subosa et al. (2025))
