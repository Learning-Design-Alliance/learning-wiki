# DigCompEdu six-area framework of educators' digital competence

## Description
The European Commission's framework organizes educators' digital competence into six areas—professional engagement, digital resources, teaching and learning, assessment, empowering learners, and facilitating learners' digital competence—for professional development, evaluation, certification, and accreditation.
