## Description
The Ripple Tank Model is an open-source Easy Java Simulations computer simulation created by Loo Kang Wee and collaborators for A-level physics learners to explore two-source interference, coherence, displacement, intensity, and three-dimensional wave representations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ripple tank interference simulation applet**: A Physlet simulation usable as a traditional ripple tank that demonstrates interference effects without the mess of a physical tank and can be made accessible to students outside of class. Users can create any number of wave sources, place them at any location, and specify amplitude and phase; all sources share one settable wavelength. The authors use it to create problems such as determining the relative phase or separation of sources from the generated pattern, giving students practice analyzing interference effects separate from the double slit experiment. (Melissa Dancy et al. (2002))
