Clark and Malecki 10-item Academic Grit Scale (Turkish adaptation)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 10-item, single-factor, five-point Likert self-report scale adapted into Turkish by Kaya et al. to measure primary students' academic grit and school-related perseverance.

Components¶

  • Academic grit scale (Clark & Malecki 10-item, Turkish adaptation): A 10-item, single-factor, 5-point Likert academic grit scale developed by Clark and Malecki (2019), translated to Turkish and back-translated, pilot-tested with 33 4th graders. In this study CFA showed good fit (CFI=0.99, TLI=0.97, RMSEA=0.08, SRMR=0.04) and Cronbach's alpha was α=0.96. Items include "I push myself to do my personal best in school". It was used to measure school-related grit as mediator. (Kaya et al. (2022))

Claims¶

  • Academic grit has a significant positive effect on primary students' math achievement [+M]
