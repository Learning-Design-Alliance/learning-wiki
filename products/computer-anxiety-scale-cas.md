## Description
A 19-item self-report Likert-scale instrument developed by Heinssen, Glass, and Knight to measure individuals' computer anxiety and confidence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Computer Anxiety Scale (CAS), a 19-item self-reported rating scale**: The outcome measure of the trial was the Computer Anxiety Scale, "a 19-item self-reported computer anxiety rating scale developed by Heinssen, Glass, and Knight". It elicits data on participants' levels of confidence and anxiety about using computers, answered on a 4-point Likert scale from strongly agree = 1 to strongly disagree = 4. The article reports the instrument's internal consistency was established at 0.79, and the authors modified the title to read computer anxiety scale. (Onah BI et al. (2023))
