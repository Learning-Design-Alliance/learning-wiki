Description¶

Chenguang Pan et al.'s offline educational-intervention-effect explanation system, combining a LoRA-adapted Gemma E2B model with dashboard and conversational interfaces for parents, students, counselors, and other stakeholders.

Claims¶

  • LoRA fine-tuning hyperparameters are not model-agnostic: increasing rank above 64 destroyed LLaMA's feedback generation while Gemma remained robust [~W]
  • LLaMA's LoRA adaptation compresses its prediction range to roughly 2.0–4.0, while Gemma maintains a broader distribution closer to the ground truth [+W]
  • Adaptation gains grow monotonically with model capacity under the same LoRA recipe, though three data points cannot establish a scaling law [~W]
