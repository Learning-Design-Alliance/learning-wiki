## Description
An interactive HubbleSite multimedia website that uses animations, astronomical imagery, and user participation to explain black-hole physics and astronomy to general audiences and introductory astronomy students.

## Components

- **Black Holes: Gravity’s Relentless Pull — an interactive multimedia website for education and public outreach about black holes**: Black Holes: Gravity’s Relentless Pull is a multimedia website, part of HubbleSite, that ”explains the physics and astronomy of black holes for a gener al audience” through user participation, animations and astronomical imagery. It won the top prize of the 2005 Pirelli INTERNETional Awards and can be ”used as an aid in teaching introductory astronomy at the undergraduate leve l”. (Roeland P. van der Marel et al. (2006))
