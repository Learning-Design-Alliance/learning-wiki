## Description
A TAACCCT-funded Virginia community-college workforce-development initiative that used career coaching and related strategies to connect workers with health-sciences education and employment pathways.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Career coaching as a bridge strategy between workforce development and community college systems**: The report frames career coaching as a strategy to close systemic gaps: coaches were hired to "close gaps in training, capacity, and supportive activities that existed between the workforce development and community college system so that Virginia workers are better prepared for employment." It operated as one of seven strategies of the RETHINKS Health Sciences Education program, funded by a $24 million TAACCCT grant and implemented across all 23 Virginia community colleges. The framework positions coaching as supportive infrastructure linking dislocated and low-skilled workers to credential-bearing pathways. (Cecilia Speroni (2015))
