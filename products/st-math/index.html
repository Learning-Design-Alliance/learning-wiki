ST Math¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ST Math is game-based instructional software for K–12 mathematics developed and provided by the MIND Research Institute, using visual-spatial puzzles to support mathematics learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Grade-level clusters provided with ST Math scored higher on state math proficiency rates and scale scores than matched comparison clusters across grades 3-5 [+M]
  • Schools implementing ST Math with fidelity in at least one grade level showed larger math advantages (ES 0.31-0.35) than matched comparison clusters [+M]
