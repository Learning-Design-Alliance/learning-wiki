## Description
A USRA–NASA Ames QuAIL research-internship programme in which students work with mentors on quantum-computing projects and produce code or publications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **USRA Feynman Quantum Academy internship program at NASA Ames QuAIL**: The Feynman Quantum Academy is an internship program placing students in the USRA-NASA Quantum Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (QuAIL) at NASA Ames Research Center. Internships "usually last 12 to 24 weeks", with rolling year-round applications, remote participation since 2020, and stipend-covered living expenses. Interns work with mentors, join group meetings and seminars, and "produce a code and/or publication from the work performed during the internship". The program draws funding from NASA, NSF, AFRL, DARPA, DOE, DHS, Fermilab and DLR, and provides access to quantum processors from vendors such as Rigetti and IBM plus high-performance computing through NASA's NAS and the NSF NRP. (Gonzalez Izquierdo et al. (2025))
