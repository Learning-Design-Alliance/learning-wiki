# Equity, Agency and Transparency (EAT) framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A research-informed framework developed by Rutherford et al. for evaluating and reflecting on higher-education assessment through assessment design, assessment literacy, and assessment feedback dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Equity, Agency and Transparency (EAT) framework organises effective assessment into three dimensions of four subdimensions each**: The EAT framework is a research-informed model for evaluating how far an assessment is focused on student learning. The article states it was 'drawn from an extensive review of many thousand published studies on assessment, focuses the elements of effective assessment into three dimensions, each with four subdimensions'. The three dimensions are Assessment Design (robust, equitable, engaging, transparent assessments), Assessment Literacy (understanding assessment parameters and their impact) and Assessment Feedback (feedback practices supporting ongoing learner development). The review selects subdimensions most relevant to biosciences and student-centred impact, noting that not all 12 subdimensions are addressed within its scope. (Rutherford S et al. (2024))
