## Description
A web-based classroom observation protocol developed for transitional bilingual, structured English immersion, and dual-language settings to record pedagogical and ESL instructional strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Transitional Bilingual Observation Protocol (TBOP) for observing pedagogy and ESL strategies**: TBOP is a web-based classroom observation protocol based on a four-dimensional pedagogical theory developed by Lara-Alecio and Parker (1994). Originally designed for transitional bilingual settings, it has been used in structured English immersion and dual language environments and was modified to include an ESL strategies component. In this study it served "as a fidelity check to capture teachers' pedagogical delivery, and to compare such delivery between treatment and control classrooms", with trained observers recording instructional events every 20 seconds. (Irby et al. (2018))
