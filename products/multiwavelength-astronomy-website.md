# Multiwavelength Astronomy website

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based astronomy curriculum resource developed by the authors under NASA E/PO grants, providing middle- and high-school learners with reviewed lessons on astronomy across electromagnetic-wavelength regimes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multiwavelength Astronomy website with scientist-derived lesson content**: A web-based teaching resource with modules for different wavelength regimes of the electromagnetic spectrum. Per the article, "The content of each lesson was derived from interviews with scientists, archived oral histories, and/or memoirs," and lessons were reviewed by a science educator and external subject matter expert before web publication. Modules span infrared, optical, ultraviolet, X-ray and gamma-ray astronomy. (Vikram V Dwarkadas (2021))
