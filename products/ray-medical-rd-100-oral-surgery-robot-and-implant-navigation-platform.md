# Ray Medical RD-100 oral surgery robot and implant navigation platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Ray Medical robotic oral-surgery and implant-navigation platform combining robotic manipulation, optical tracking, imaging, and navigation software for clinical training and remote instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ray Medical RD-100 oral surgery robot and implant navigation platform used as the digital teaching infrastructure**: The digital infrastructure of the training program is Ray Medical's oral robot RD-100, described as the first oral surgery robot product certified by NMPA in China, comprising a Universal Robots manipulator, a Claron Technology optical tracking and positioning device, a North China Industrial Control mainframe, and RemeDent surgical navigation software. The article reports that "With the precise positioning assistance of the robot, doctors can accurately complete the surgery, and the precision error is controlled within 1° and 0.5 mm." Patient imaging came from original Dicom-format CBCT data, and model scanning used a Carestream CS 3600. The platform was already installed in the outpatient department and Clinical Skills Center, supports data reuse and remote instruction, and the pilot incurred no additional material or consumable costs. (Cai Z (2025))
