# Einstein@Home

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Einstein@Home is a volunteer distributed-computing citizen-science project run through BOINC that recruits schools and members of the public to process gravitational-wave data for research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Einstein@Home distributed computing volunteer science project**: Einstein@Home uses BOINC distributed computing software to process gravitational-wave detector data on computers donated by volunteers worldwide. The article states participants download the BOINC software and the project "processes gravitational-wave data when the computer is not being used for other applications," and that it "does not affect the performance of computers and greatly speeds up this exciting research and has already published results." It is presented as a research experience for schools and citizens. (M. Cavaglia et al. (2008))
