A supplementary RAISE guidance document providing side-by-side bad and good reporting examples and explanations to help authors produce clear, transparent, aligned, and reproducible AI-focused educational research reports.

- **RAISE 2025 Reporting Examples and Explanation document with Bad and Good examples**: A supplementary document supporting application of the checklist. The article explains: "This document presents side-by-side “Bad” and “Good” reporting examples for each checklist item, accompanied by explanations that illustrate how to achieve clarity, transparency, and alignment with educational objectives."
