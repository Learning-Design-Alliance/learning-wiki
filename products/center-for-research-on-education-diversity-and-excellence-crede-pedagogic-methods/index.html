Center for Research on Education, Diversity and Excellence (CREDE) pedagogic methods¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A set of five research-based pedagogic methods developed by CREDE for effective, culturally responsive teaching across grade levels, learner populations, and content areas.

Components¶

  • Joint Productive Activity as a CREDE pedagogic method in which teacher and students co-construct meaning from a text: Joint Productive Activity is one of five pedagogic methods of the Center for Research on Education, Diversity and Excellence (CREDE), which the article says have proved successful over several decades across grade levels, student populations and content areas. In JPA during language arts instruction, the teacher and a small group of students co-construct meaning from a text, with the teacher as a full collaborator modelling effective reading comprehension strategies while assessing and assisting students' comprehension efforts. The article applies this framework to second language reading instruction in Nigeria. (Hanna Onyi Yusuf (2013))

Claims¶

  • Six weeks of Joint Productive Activity instruction significantly improved Nigerian Junior Secondary II students' reading comprehension performance from pre-test to post-test [+M]
