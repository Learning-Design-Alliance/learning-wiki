## Description
Second Life is an online three-dimensional virtual-world platform made by Linden Lab that lets users create and script interactive environments, including educational physics simulations and microworlds.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Second Life as an immersive 3D virtual world platform for physics simulations and microworlds**: Second Life (SL) is an online 3D immersive virtual world that the article evaluates as a platform for physics simulations and microworlds. Each Region is rendered by a server process that "simulates a full rigid -body physical dynamics , including gravity, elasticity, and conservation of momentum", and users create objects and scripts with the Linden Scripting Language, whose structure is based on Java and C and which provides nearly four hundred functions. (dos Santos (2012))
