# N.U.R.S.E.S., AI-ABCs, and CARE frameworks

## Description
A set of nursing-specific AI-education frameworks developed by Hoelscher and Pugh, Kobeissi et al., and Bester and Zeigler to organize nurses’ AI collaboration, AI-literacy objectives, and prompt-engineering skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Named nursing AI frameworks: N.U.R.S.E.S., AI-ABCs, and CARE**: The review identifies existing nursing-specific AI frameworks: the N.U.R.S.E.S. framework by Hoelscher and Pugh, which "emphasizes nurses’ roles as AI collaborators"; the AI-ABCs framework by Kobeissi et al., which clarifies fundamental educational objectives; and the CARE framework by Bester and Zeigler, which "structures prompt engineering proficiency through context, action, role, and expectation elements." The review argues these frameworks face challenges translating into concrete curricula because they lack clear pedagogical implementation pathways or focus on isolated skills. (Xiang Yue et al. (2026))
