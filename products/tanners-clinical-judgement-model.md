## Description
A nursing clinical-judgement framework organized around noticing, interpreting, responding, and reflecting, developed by Christine Tanner and used to structure clinical reasoning and education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tanner's Clinical Judgement Model (four stages: noticing, interpreting, responding, reflecting)**: Tanner's (2006) clinical judgement model is a four-stage model of nurses' clinical reasoning used in this study as the theoretical basis for a pre-simulation briefing form. The article describes "a noticing stage when a given clinical situation is perceived", an interpreting stage, a responding stage, and "a reflecting stage to focus on the patient's condition and response". Students in the experimental group wrote a briefing form structured by these stages before neonatal resuscitation simulation. (Yang S‐Y (2021))
