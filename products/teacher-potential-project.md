## Components

- **Teacher Potential Project (TPP): CCSS-aligned EL Education Language Arts Curriculum combined with intensive professional development**: The Teacher Potential Project is a professional learning program developed by EL Education that pairs "a Common Core State Standards (CCSS)-aligned EL Education Language Arts Curriculum" with "intensive PD". It was designed "to address the need for high quality and lasting professional learning for teachers" and was evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research in an independent study funded by a U.S. Department of Education Investing in Innovation (i3) grant, examining teachers' practices after one and two years of engagement. ## Description
The Teacher Potential Project is a professional learning program developed by EL Education that pairs "a Common Core State Standards (CCSS)-aligned EL Education Language Arts Curriculum" with "intensive PD". It was designed "to address the need for high quality and lasting professional learning for teachers" and was evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research in an independent study funded by a U.S. Department of Education Investing in Innovation (i3) grant, examining teachers' practices after one and two years of engagement.
