# Gradational gender expression metric

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Likert-type self-report instrument adapted from Magliozzi et al. that measures self- and reflected appraisals of masculinity, femininity, and androgyny and derives discrepancy scores for research use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gradational gender expression metric (self- and reflected appraisals of masculinity, femininity, and androgyny)**: A Likert-type instrument introduced by Magliozzi et al. and adapted to physics education, on which respondents rate their level of masculinity, femininity, and androgyny twice: once for self-appraisal (how they view themselves) and once for reflected appraisal (how they believe others perceive them). Differences between corresponding responses constitute a self-reflected gender appraisal discrepancy, calculated as self-appraisal minus reflected appraisal. It was used in the authors’ prior quantitative studies and in both the survey and interview stages of the present study. (Noah Leibnitz et al. (2024))
