## Description
LiftUpp is a digital educational platform developed for quality-assured workplace-based assessment, feedback, curriculum design, and learning-outcome mapping in dentistry and other clinical education programmes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LiftUpp digital platform for workplace-based assessment in dentistry**: LiftUpp is a digital educational platform supporting quality-assured assessment, feedback, curriculum design and mapping in workplace-based (clinical) education. Its core maps the learning outcomes of the entire degree programme to exams, individual exam questions and workflow items, with modules for assessment building, iPad-based data collection and a web portal. It is deployed in 70% (10 out of 14) of UK dental schools and in veterinary medicine, physiotherapy and nursing. (Frans A. Oliehoek et al. (2017))
