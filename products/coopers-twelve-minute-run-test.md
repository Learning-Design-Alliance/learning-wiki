# Cooper's Twelve Minute Run test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A timed track-running test developed by Kenneth H. Cooper that estimates cardiovascular endurance from the distance a learner covers in twelve minutes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cooper's Twelve Minute Run test for measuring cardiovascular endurance**: Cardiovascular endurance was measured with Cooper's Twelve Minute Run, administered on a 400-meter track before and after the ten-week program. The article reports "a reliability of 0.95 and the validity coefficients of .65" and that the test "possesses a high correlation with VO2max of r = .90". Students ran in pairs, with a partner recording laps to the nearest 25 meters, aiming to cover maximum distance in twelve minutes. (Shabeshan Rengasamy et al. (2011))
