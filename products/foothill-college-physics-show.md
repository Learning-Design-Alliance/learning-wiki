# Foothill College Physics Show

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Foothill College physics-outreach programme that brings local Title 1 elementary and middle school classes to campus for a demonstration show and college visit.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Physics Show field trip program for Title 1 schools**: An outreach component that invites classes from local Title 1 schools to the community college for a demonstration show plus a campus visit. The program "supports the cost of the buses, performs the show, provides a tour of the college's campus (lead by a current Foothill College student), and gives each child a Foothill College Physics Show shirt." It has grown to hosting five thousand students annually and over forty thousand underserved youth in total, and teachers report it is often the school's only field trip of the year. (Frank Cascarano et al. (2016))
