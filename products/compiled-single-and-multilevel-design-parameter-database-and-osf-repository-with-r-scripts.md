# Compiled single- and multilevel design parameter database and OSF repository with R scripts and interactive Excel workbooks for RT study planning

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Stallasch et al. research dataset and repository compiling empirical, meta-analytic, and simulated design parameters for planning randomized achievement studies, with R scripts, Excel workbooks, and the multides package.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cross-state database of ICC and R2 estimates for science achievement (Tables 4–8)**: A compilation of empirical design parameter estimates for science achievement outcomes drawn from state assessment data in Texas, Wisconsin, and Michigan, covering two- and three-level hierarchical models and three covariate sets. The article states these findings "represent the largest cross-state compilation and comparison of ICCs and R2s specifically for science outcomes." The estimates are presented in Tables 4 through 8 and are intended to improve a priori power analyses for science education CRTs. (Jessaca Spybrook et al. (2016))
