# EFN Competency Framework

## Description
A European Federation of Nurses Associations framework translating the competencies in EU Directive 2013/55/EU into six competency areas to guide nursing curricula and competency assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mapping of perceived competency deficiencies onto the EFN Competency Framework's six competency areas**: The study frames its qualitative results within the EFN Competency Framework, which gathers the eight competencies of Directive 2013/55/EU and six competency areas into guidelines for creating nursing curricula in Europe. Table 5 maps participant-reported deficient competences — cultural ignorance, decision-making doubts, communication difficulties, leadership inability, and knowledge gaps in mental illness, critical care, wound care and pharmacology — onto these areas, finding Competence C (health promotion) non-deficient. This framework functions as the study's organizing taxonomy for interpreting graduate perceptions. (López-Entrambasaguas et al. (2019))
