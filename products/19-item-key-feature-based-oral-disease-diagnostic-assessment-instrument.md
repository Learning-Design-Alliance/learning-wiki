# 19-item Key Feature-based oral disease diagnostic assessment instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A Delphi-validated case-based assessment developed by Farzan Ava et al. for measuring medical interns' oral-disease diagnostic reasoning using key features and unsafe-option penalties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A 19-item Key Feature-based oral disease diagnostic assessment instrument with Delphi validation and killer options**: An assessment instrument of "19 case-based Key Feature questions to assess critical steps in diagnostic reasoning," most accompanied by visual aids, covering common oral diseases of varying diagnostic complexity. Items were derived from an expert disease list via a two-round Delphi process with ≥ 80% agreement predefined. Scoring awards credit only when all essential options are identified, and selecting predefined unsafe "killer" options loses credit for the item. Reliability and validity were quantified (Cronbach's alpha = 0.70; ICC = 0.861; CVR 0.53–1.00; CVI 0.84–1.00). (Farzan Ava et al. (2026))
