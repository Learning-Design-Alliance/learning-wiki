# Safer Anaesthesia From Education Obstetric Anaesthesia (SAFE-OB) course

## Description
A three-day interactive obstetric anaesthesia refresher programme designed by the AAGBI and administered by the WFSA for trained anaesthesia providers in low-income countries.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAFE-OB obstetric anaesthesia refresher course**: The Safer Anaesthesia From Education Obstetric Anaesthesia (SAFE-OB) course is "a 3-day refresher course for trained anesthesia providers" in low-income countries, designed by the AAGBI and administered by the WFSA. It is "highly interactive using various teaching modalities such as scenario teaching, skills sessions, and small-group discussion" with lectures kept to a minimum. It is designed for 32 participants and 6–8 faculty to keep the faculty/participant ratio low for interactive learning, and was locally adapted (materials translated into French, sequential translation used, medication and timetabling adjusted after needs assessment). (White Michelle C. et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Knowledge and skills test scores improved significantly from precourse to postcourse and remained improved at 4 and 12–18 months after SAFE-OB training](../claims/safe-ob-knowledge-skill-retention-sustained.md) [+M]
- [Self-reported personal practice change at 12–18 months centered on obstetric hemorrhage, eclampsia, a structured ABC assessment approach, and spinal anesthesia, while neonatal resuscitation and airway management were reported only at 4 months](../claims/safe-ob-practice-change-themes-over-time.md) [+M]
- [Improved teamwork, communication, and preparation were the organizational culture change themes present at both 4 and 12–18 months, with new clinical protocols developed in Madagascar](../claims/safe-ob-organizational-culture-change.md) [+M]
