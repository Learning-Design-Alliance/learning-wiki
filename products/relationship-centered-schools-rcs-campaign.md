## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three principles of the Relationship Centered Schools campaign**: The Relationship Centered Schools (RCS) campaign, launched in 2015 by Californians for Justice, is a youth-led school transformation initiative organized around three guiding principles. The report states that the campaign "centers three principles to guide action and school improvement: (1) create space for relationship-building, (2) value student voice, and (3) invest in staff." These principles direct how youth organizers, educators, and district leaders collaborate to restructure secondary schools so that students of color and students from low-income backgrounds are known, supported, and able to reach their potential. The campaign has sought to spur transformation in 11 comprehensive high schools across four California regions. (Hernández et al. (2023))
