## Description
CPLINTS is a 43-item observational assessment tool developed for rating circulating nurses' task management, teamwork, situational awareness, communication, and leadership in operating-room practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CPLINTS observational tool for circulating nurses' nontechnical skills**: The circulating practitioners' list of nontechnical skills (CPLINTS) is a valid and reliable observational tool containing 43 observable behaviors grouped into five dimensions: task management, teamwork, situational awareness, communication, and leadership. Each item is scored from zero (necessary but not observed) to four (excellent), with a not-applicable option, and mean scores can be calculated per dimension. The study used it to rate nurses before and eight weeks after training, with an observer with seven years of experience who remained silent during surgeries. (Kalantari Reza et al. (2021))
