## Description
The Sensory Profile is a parent-reported standardized questionnaire developed by Winnie Dunn for assessing children's sensory processing in everyday activities, including auditory, visual, tactile, movement, and social-emotional responses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Sensory Profile parent-reported questionnaire and related checklists for assessing sensory processing in children with ADHD**: The review describes the Sensory Profile as "a widely used parent reported standardized questionnaire" in which children's responses to sensory events in daily life are rated by their parents. It assesses auditory, visual, taste/smell, body position, movement, and touch processing, plus activity level and social/emotional responses. Related instruments include a parent-reported auditory processing problem checklist (hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity to sounds) and a tactile sensory dysfunction checklist covering hypersensitivity, hyposensitivity, and poor tactile perception and discrimination. (Ghanizadeh A (2011))
