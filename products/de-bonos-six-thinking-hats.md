## Description
A thinking and facilitation framework developed by Edward de Bono that organizes individual or group reasoning into six symbolic modes for parallel, structured consideration of an issue.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six thinking hats technique as a multi-directional thinking framework**: De Bono's six thinking hats technique separates thinking into six modes represented by colored hats: white (objective facts), red (emotions and intuition), black (logical negativity), yellow (constructive benefits), green (creative alternatives), and blue (control of the thinking process). The technique "provides individuals with specific thought patterns and allows them to observe events from different angles", aiming to separate emotions from logic and creativity from knowledge accumulation. (Ercan et al. (2008))
