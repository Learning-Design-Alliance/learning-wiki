# ANZCA nine-point entrustment scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A nine-point workplace-based assessment scale developed and used by ANZCA to rate the level of supervision required by anaesthesia trainees, with parallel supervisor and trainee descriptors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ANZCA nine-point entrustment scale with parallel supervisor and trainee level descriptors**: The entrustment scale used in ANZCA workplace-based assessments is "a nine-point scale with three broad subdivisions", spanning needing the assessor in the theatre suite, needing the assessor in the hospital, and independent management without direct supervision. Tables 1 and 2 present matched supervisor-view and trainee-view descriptors for each level, phrased from each party's perspective (for example level 2: supervisor "Comfortable to leave trainee to go on brief coffee break in theatre tearoom"). Both parties rate the question of what level of supervision the trainee requires for the case overall. (Castanelli Damian J et al. (2024))
