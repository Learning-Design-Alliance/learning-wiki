## Description
IELTS Writing Task 1 is the writing-task component of the International English Language Testing System, developed and administered by the British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia, and Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IELTS Writing Task 1 pre-test/post-test assessment with four band-descriptor criteria**: The study used two versions of IELTS Writing Task 1 as pre-test and post-test, scored by two examiners on the four IELTS writing marking criteria: "task achievement, cohesion and coherence, lexical resource (vocabulary) and grammatical range and accuracy", each worth 25% of the total marks. The same task type was used at both time points to measure improvement, and the structure mirrored a formal IELTS writing exam. This instrument served as the dependent-variable measure for both groups. (Hosseini et al. (2019))
