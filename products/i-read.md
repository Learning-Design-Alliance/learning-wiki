## Description
i-Read is an online reading environment developed by Omheni et al. in which learners upload, annotate, archive, and share texts while the system analyzes annotation traces for reader profiling.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **i-Read online reading environment with personality profiling from annotations**: i-Read is an online reading environment where learners upload reading materials, annotate them, and share annotated documents. Its architecture includes a user annotation interface, an annotation analyzer module, a profile constructor module, and three databases with two servers. The analyzer computes six annotation features (total, average, graphical, textual, reference, and compounding annotation counts), and the profile constructor applies multivariate linear regression to estimate Big Five trait scores. The system "offers, besides the traditional annotation functionalities of creation, archiving and sharing, the functionality of readers' personality modeling based on their digital annotation traces." (Omheni et al. (2017))
