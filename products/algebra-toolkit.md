# Algebra Toolkit: a year-long PLC-based professional development program with Introduction, Modules 1–4, and a School Leader Guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Algebra Toolkit is a professional development program for middle and high school algebra educators delivered through collaborative professional learning community (PLC) sessions. The document states that "This list of resources assumes implementation of the full toolkit—that is, the Introduction, Modules 1–4, and the School Leader Guide—over the course of one academic year." Each module involves facilitated PLC sessions plus pre- and between-session work and classroom implementation of recommended instructional strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One facilitator per toolkit PLC with experience, time, and ability to support, guide, and coordinate sessions
- Educator professional development, release/preparation, and instructional time as quantified in the resource table
- A meeting space, computer access, internet connection, and basic materials such as sticky notes and a board
#### Constraints
- Resource list assumes implementation of the full toolkit over the course of one academic year
- Coffee Break Colloquia are optional

### Target Learners
- middle and high school algebra educators

### Target Learning Goals
- evidence-based algebra instruction
