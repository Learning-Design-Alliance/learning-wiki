## Description
A WordPress-based astroparticle-physics education and outreach web resource run within the German-Russian Astroparticle Data Life Cycle Initiative and hosted by the Matrosov Institute for System Dynamics and Control Theory.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **astroparticle.online educational and outreach web resource**: An educational and outreach web resource for astroparticle physics deployed since 2018 within the German-Russian Astroparticle Data Life Cycle Initiative (GRADLCI), built on WordPress and hosted at the Matrosov Institute for System Dynamics and Control Theory. Its main target audience is "students, who are interested in astroparticle physics and would like to collaborate with the Baikal region astroparticle physics experiments". It offers News, Science, Experiments and Projects sections plus a large Online School section, and serves the Baikal Multimessenger Lab as an education and outreach instrument. (Y. Kazarina et al. (2020))
