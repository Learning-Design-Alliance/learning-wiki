## Description
A 25-item CPR simulation observation checklist developed by Ammar et al. from 2021 European Resuscitation Council recommendations for medical students learning basic life support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Observer tool: a 25-item checklist for observers of CPR simulation**: The observer tool (OT) is a checklist "summarizing CPR skills and step-by-step actions" that observers fill in (observed/incompletely/not observed) for each item and each observation sequence. It was "designed by expert investigators in accordance with the 2021 recommendations of the European Resuscitation Council", tested and validated by experts, and totalled 25 items, with 7, 13, 20 and 25 items active as successive teaching steps were added. (Goulamhoussen Ammar et al. (2024))
