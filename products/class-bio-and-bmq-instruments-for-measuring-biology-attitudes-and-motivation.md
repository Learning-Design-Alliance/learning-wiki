# CLASS-Bio and BMQ instruments for measuring biology attitudes and motivation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pre/post student self-report assessment battery combining the Colorado Learning Attitudes about Science Survey–Biology and Science Motivation Questionnaire II–Biology to measure undergraduate biology learners’ attitudes and motivation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CLASS-Bio and BMQ instruments for measuring attitudinal and motivational shifts in biology courses**: The study used the Colorado Learning Attitudes in Science Survey–Biology (CLASS-Bio), 31 Likert items across seven scales reporting shifts in percent favorable (expert-aligned) responses, and the Science Motivation Questionnaire II–Biology (BMQ), 25 Likert items covering career and grade motivation, self-efficacy, self-determination, and intrinsic motivation. Both were administered pre and post and were "demonstrated to be valid in populations similar to ours (Cronbach’s α = 0.855 and 0.844)." (Cleveland Lacy M. et al. (2017))

### Claims
- [The two active-learning environments differed on two of seven CLASS-Bio attitude scales, with graphic-organizer/worksheet students showing more expert-like Enjoyment and Real-World Connections shifts](../claims/attitude-shifts-differ-two-class-bio-scales.md) [+M]
