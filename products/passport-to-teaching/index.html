Description¶

The American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence's Passport to Teaching is an examination-based teacher-certification programme intended to identify competent teachers regardless of their preparation route.

Components¶

  • Passport to Teaching, an alternative certification program offered by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE): Passport to Teaching is described as "the more recent Passport to Teaching, offered by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE)". It is presented as one example among several alternative certification programs, alongside Troops to Teachers and Transition to Teaching. The report it introduces studies the career choices and experiences of teachers certified through this route. (Steven Glazerman et al. (2008))
  • Passport to Teaching program of the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence: Passport to Teaching is a teacher certification credential developed by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence, an alternative route into teaching. The article states that "Alternative routes to teaching have been promoted during the past decade because of concerns about teacher shortages and teacher quality." The credential is being evaluated for its effects on teacher performance as measured by student achievement. (Steven Glazerman (2006))
