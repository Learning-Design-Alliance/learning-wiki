# Interactive HTML5/JavaScript molecular dynamics simulation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open, browser-based molecular-dynamics simulation written by Daniel V. Schroeder for exploring atomic-scale forces, motion, phases, and thermal equilibrium in undergraduate physics instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interactive HTML5/JavaScript molecular dynamics simulation with animated graphics released as an online supplement**: A browser-based molecular dynamics simulation, written in HTML5 and JavaScript, that models and animates hundreds of Lennard-Jones particles such as noble gas atoms. The author states: "I have written an interactive molecular dynamics simulation with animated graphics in HTML5/JavaScript, which is provided as an online supplement to this article." The code is made easy for beginning programmers to read so instructors can modify and adapt it, and it supports the twenty student exercises in the article. (Daniel V. Schroeder (2015))
