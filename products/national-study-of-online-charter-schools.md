# National Study of Online Charter Schools

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A national research dataset assembled through a survey of online charter-school principals and U.S. Department of Education administrative data to document the sector's operations and instructional approaches.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Study of Online Charter Schools principal survey and administrative data resource**: The National Study of Online Charter Schools is a research effort whose Mathematica report "provides the first nationwide data and analysis of the operations and instructional approaches of online charter schools." It draws on "a survey completed by 127 principals of online charter schools across the country and public data from the U.S. Department of Education," covering a sector of about 200 schools serving about 200,000 students. (Brian Gill et al. (2015))
