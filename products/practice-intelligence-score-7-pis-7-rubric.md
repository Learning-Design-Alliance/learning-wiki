# Practice Intelligence Score-7 (PIS-7) rubric

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PIS-7 is a physician-reasoning assessment rubric developed by Wattanasirichaigoon for formative evaluation of observable reasoning behavior in AI-supported professional learning environments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PIS-7 rubric scoring 7 reasoning domains on a weighted 0-100 composite**: The Practice Intelligence Score-7 is "a multidomain rubric designed to assess physician reasoning behavior within a PLE." Each of the 7 domains (professional question intelligence, reasoning depth, contextual integration, iterative inquiry, critical appraisal, reflective synthesis, AI literacy and prompt skill) is scored 0 to 5 and converted into a weighted composite from 0 to 100, with reasoning depth weighted highest at 20%. The rubric assesses observable reasoning behavior rather than the AI's answer. (Wattanasirichaigoon S (2026))
