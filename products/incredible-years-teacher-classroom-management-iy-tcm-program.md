# Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management (IY TCM) program

## Description
A six-day universal teacher-training and coaching programme developed by The Incredible Years for elementary teachers, focused on teacher–student and teacher–parent relationships and effective classroom management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management Program (IYT)**: IYT is a teacher training program within a series of parent, child, and teacher interventions designed to prevent and treat aggressive behavior and conduct problems in young children aged 3-8 years. Its approach includes "video-modeling, behavioral rehearsal of key skills through numerous role plays, classroom practice assignments, and teacher goal setting and self-monitoring." In the study it was delivered in five monthly full-day workshops led by two trained co-leaders with approximately 12-15 teachers per group, plus two brief classroom consultation visits and regular support emails. (Murray et al. (2014))
