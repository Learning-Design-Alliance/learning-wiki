## Description
START-Play is a pediatric rehabilitation early-intervention programme for infants with neuromotor disorders, developed and delivered by its programme authors and therapists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **START-Play intervention-specific fidelity measure**: A coding instrument developed to quantify fidelity of the START-Play intervention, which the authors call "the first intervention-specific measure used in pediatric rehabilitation". It codes six START-Play behaviors and five non-START-Play behaviors each minute (coded 1 if observed, 0 if not), plus four 1–4 Likert gestalt quality scales scored per 10-minute segment. Variables cover adherence rates, dosage in minutes, quality and responsiveness averages, and program differentiation. It can be used for replicating efficacy studies and by therapists to examine their own delivery of key components. (Mihee An et al. (2020))
