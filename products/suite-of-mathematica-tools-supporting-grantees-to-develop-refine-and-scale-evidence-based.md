# Suite of Mathematica tools supporting grantees to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Mathematica-developed suite of tools, including a Prior Research Table and measurement, evaluation, and design-research templates, that helps education grantees develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Suite of Mathematica tools supporting grantees to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based educational solutions**: The Prior Research Table is part of a suite of tools designed to support grantees as they develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions that demonstrate success in improving educational outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty, and to promote adoption of such solutions more broadly. Companion publications listed include a Measurement and Evaluation Needs Inventory, a Measurement and Evaluation Plan Template, and a Design Research Plan Template. (Lindsay Fox et al. (2023))
