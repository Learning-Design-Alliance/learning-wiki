# Obstetric Life Support (OBLS) curriculum

## Description
A named interdisciplinary simulation-based curriculum developed for healthcare workers to recognize and manage maternal cardiac arrest and other maternal medical emergencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Obstetric Life Support (OBLS) simulation-based curricula for hospital and prehospital settings**: OBLS is a validated, interdisciplinary simulation-based curriculum training prehospital and hospital healthcare workers to prevent, recognize, and manage maternal medical emergencies including maternal cardiac arrest. It has two curricula, one for hospital/urgent care settings and one for prehospital settings. Its primary components are "four hours of self-guided prework", "six hours of instructor-led, simulation-based rapid cycle deliberate practice using a custom-designed, mobile, low-cost maternal simulator", and a post-course megacode assessment of team-leader performance. (Cunningham SD et al. (2025))
- **OBLS training toolkits and online participant/instructor hubs**: To support local course delivery, each implementation site receives a specialized toolkit with all necessary materials, and the OBLS website serves as a resource hub. "Hospital and birthing center toolkits will include a custom simulator, training defibrillator, cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) rhythm generator, training code carts with simulated medications" and a resuscitative cesarean delivery kit; prehospital toolkits include a training AED and a GoBag. Each toolkit has two tablets for quality CPR monitoring, and the site offers a Participant Hub and an Instructor Hub. (Cunningham SD et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [The original OBLS curriculum, validated in a randomized controlled trial, significantly improved healthcare workers' knowledge and clinical competencies in managing maternal medical emergencies](../claims/obls-rct-improved-knowledge-competencies.md) [+M]
- [Healthcare workers who completed the OBLS simulation curriculum showed significantly higher cognitive, skills, and self-efficacy scores than controls in the reported randomized trial](../claims/obls-rct-higher-scores-than-control.md) [+M]
- [In the reported randomized trial, 90% of OBLS completers passed the course versus fewer than 10% of controls](../claims/obls-rct-pass-rate-gap.md) [+M]
