# Joint Network for Informal Physics Education and Research (JNIPER)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An American Physical Society initiative that connects informal physics educators and researchers, supports research–practice partnerships, and promotes research-based, equitable, and culturally competent public-engagement practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **JNIPER community of practice for informal physics education**: JNIPER (Joint Network for Informal Physics Education and Research) is an international community of practice for facilitators, designers, and scholars of IPE, officially formed in Fall 2022 and supported by APS with Michigan State University, University of Colorado Boulder, and the JILA Physics Frontier Center. The paper reports that "Currently, JNIPER has 325 members across over 35 countries, with the majority from the United States". Its community-of-practice elements (Table 1) are domain, community, and practice, enacted through monthly coffee hours, workshops, conference sessions, a professional development program, and online collaboration. (Lau et al. (2025))
