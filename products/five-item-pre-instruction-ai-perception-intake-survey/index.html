Five-item pre-instruction AI perception intake survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A study-developed anonymous Qualtrics survey that measures bioscience trainees' baseline AI perceptions and related intake characteristics before an RCR course.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The cohort's baseline perception profile shows high endorsement of critical-thinking risk and training interest and substantially more cautious trust for complex-task use than for accuracy [+W]
  • Self-reported LLM usage frequency shows Holm-corrected associations with all five baseline AI perception outcomes, strongest for complex-task trust, accuracy trust, and critical-thinking risk [+W]
  • Self-rated LLM familiarity is a weaker secondary intake signal, surviving Holm correction for three of five outcomes with a bottom-anchored pattern [+W]
