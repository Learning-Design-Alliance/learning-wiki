# Intrinsic Satisfaction Classroom (ISC) scale adapted to Mexican high school subjects

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The ISC scale is an eight-item, five-point student questionnaire adapted by Baños et al. for Mexican high school physical education, health guidance, English, Spanish, and mathematics classes to measure satisfaction or fun and boredom with each subject.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Intrinsic Satisfaction Classroom scale adapted to Mexican high school subjects**: The Intrinsic Satisfaction Classroom (ISC) scale, originally by Baena-Extremera et al. and adapted by Baños et al. to PE, GH, ENG, SP, and MATH, was adapted to the Mexican context for this study. "The instrument is composed of eight items; five of them measure satisfaction/fun level and three items measure boredom with the academic activities of each subject", answered on a 1 (totally disagree) to 5 (totally agree) scale. Confirmatory factor analysis with WLS estimation showed good fit indices for all subject models, with MATH and GH showing the best fit. (Baños Raúl et al. (2019))
