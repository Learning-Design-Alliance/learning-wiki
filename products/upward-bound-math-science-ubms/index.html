Upward Bound Math-Science (UBMS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A U.S. Department of Education Upward Bound programme administered through college and university projects that provides disadvantaged high-school students with intensive mathematics and science instruction, laboratory and field experiences, and exposure to mathematicians and scientists.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Interim impact estimates suggest UBMS participation improves several student outcomes in high school and college [+W]
  • Preliminary estimates suggest UBMS participation increases the odds of majoring in math or science [+W]
  • UBMS provides intensive academic instruction in math and science [+W]
