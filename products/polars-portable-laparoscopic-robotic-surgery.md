## Description
PoLaRS is a Unity-based portable virtual-reality robotic-surgery training system developed with Delft University of Technology for affordable remote practice of fundamental robotic skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PoLaRS portable virtual reality robotic surgery training system**: PoLaRS (Portable Laparoscopic Robotic Surgery) is a prototype VR training system developed with Delft University of Technology to train fundamental robotic skills remotely. It consists of a console with two handles, each with seven degrees of movement, custom I/O circuitry, and VR software programmed in Unity run on a regular Windows laptop. The article states it "is designed to train fundamental robotic skills on a remote basis at an affordable cost of less than 25 k Euro per system", and it can be installed on a table top at any height for use from sitting or standing positions. (Hardon Sem F. et al. (2022))
