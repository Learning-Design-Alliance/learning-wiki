# California College Promise Guidebook

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A California College Promise planning guidebook and toolkit developed by the California College Promise Project with worksheets, cost-estimation tools, and example implementation artifacts for college leaders and partners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **California College Promise Guidebook toolkit of nine worksheets, cost estimator tabs, and example artifacts**: The Guidebook embeds nine example worksheets (internal and external partner identification; needs, goals, and assumptions; measurable goals; logic model; eligibility and participation requirements; continuation requirements; student support services; goals and measures) plus a California College Promise Cost Estimator Tool with Assumptions, Student Costs, Institutional Costs, Summer and Intersession Costs, and Cost Summary Sheet tabs. Appendices supply example MOUs, applications and student agreements, data-sharing agreements, and data dashboards. Blank worksheets are provided in the appendices for team use. (Rauner et al. (2019))
