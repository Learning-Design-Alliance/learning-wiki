## Description
InfoCritique is a self-directed online critical-appraisal course developed at Laval University and culturally translated and validated for Spanish-speaking nurses, with modules, clinical cases, appraisal grids, assessments, and tutor support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish InfoCritique online critical appraisal course for nurses**: The element is the culturally adapted Spanish InfoCritique e-learning course on critical appraisal, originally developed in French at Laval University and translated following cross-cultural validation stages. As printed, "The four self-directed learning modules available in Spanish cover 1) clinical trials; 2) systematic reviews; 3) diagnostic tests; and 4) qualitative studies." Each module requires about 6 hours of student work, combines basic information with clinical-case exercises using study-design-specific critical appraisal grids, provides interactive glossary links, and ends with an assessment test and satisfaction form used as tutor feedback, supported by a course website and three tutors who answered questions by email. (Reviriego E et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [Nurses' critical appraisal knowledge scores increased significantly after the online InfoCritique course, from a mean of 70.5 to 86.6 out of 100.](../claims/elearning-critical-appraisal-knowledge-gain-nurses.md) [+M]
- [Nurses' self-directed learning readiness increased significantly after the course, with the mean SDLRSNE score rising more than 5 points.](../claims/sdlrsne-readiness-increase-nurses.md) [+M]
- [Participants rated the course 7 out of 10 overall, with scores ranging from 2 to 10, and a quarter considered the diagnostic tests module inadequate.](../claims/course-satisfaction-7-of-10-nurses.md) [+M]
