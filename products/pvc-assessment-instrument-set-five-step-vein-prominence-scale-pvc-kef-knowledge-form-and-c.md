# PVC assessment instrument set: five-step vein prominence scale, PVC-KEF knowledge form, and C-PVC 20-step checklist

## Description
A researcher-developed assessment set for measuring peripheral venous catheterization knowledge, psychomotor performance, and vein prominence in nursing students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PVC assessment instrument set: five-step vein prominence scale, PVC-KEF knowledge form, and C-PVC 20-step checklist**: The study used three instruments: a five-step vein prominence scale ("1 = no veins visible or palpable... 5 = clearly visible and easily palpable veins"); the PVC Knowledge Evaluation Form (PVC-KEF) of 20 questions scored 0–100, with a content validity index of 0.98 after review by five nursing and five measurement-evaluation experts; and the Checklist for PVC (C-PVC) covering 20 procedure steps scored 0–2 each (0–40 total), with a content validity index of 1.0 and inter-rater ICC of 0.899 between two independent researchers. (Yılmaz Hülya et al. (2025))
- **21-point PVC insertion checklist with Hofstee-set pass mark of 20**: The measuring instrument is a checklist divided into four units — preparing materials, preparation of the puncture, execution of the puncture, and fixation of the venous catheter — with seven, four, five and five subunits respectively, each successfully performed sub-unit scoring one point for a total of 21 points. The structure follows the OSCE checklist of Juenger and Nikendei. "a Hofstee score of 20 points was used as the final minimum passing score", set by a multidisciplinary panel of eight clinical experts, so passing required correct skin disinfection. (Friederichs Hendrik et al. (2016))
