## Description
GISA is a 45-minute scenario-based Internet assessment developed by ETS researchers to measure middle-school students’ ability to integrate information across multiple text sources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GISA: a 45-minute internet-delivered scenario-based reading comprehension assessment**: The Global Integrated Scenario-Based Assessment (GISA), developed by ETS researchers, is a 45-minute, scenario-based assessment of reading comprehension delivered over the Internet. Students read a motivating scenario (e.g., building a class website on organic farming) and then read varied text types — informational passages, simulated web searches and discussions, cartoons, charts, and graphic organizers — to answer embedded questions, tapping integration of multiple text sources. (James Kim et al. (2016))
