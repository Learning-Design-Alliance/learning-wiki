# Hearing Screening Simulator (HSS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
The Hearing Screening Simulator is a web-based digital-twin training tool developed by Mavragani Amaryllis et al. that simulates audiology screening procedures, patient profiles, grading, feedback, logging, and reporting for health-professions learners.
