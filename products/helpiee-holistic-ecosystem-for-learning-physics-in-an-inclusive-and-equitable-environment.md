# HELPIEE: Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
HELPIEE is a Cwik and Singh framework that combines problem-solving instruction with belonging, self-efficacy, physics identity, and growth-mindset supports for inclusive undergraduate physics learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HELPIEE: Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment**: The chapter introduces the HELPIEE framework (Cwik & Singh, 2021), shown in Fig. 3, for motivating all students to engage in productive problem-solving. It uses a chess/tennis-style "defense and offense" metaphor: "offense" covers helping students learn effective problem-solving approaches, transfer, and robust knowledge structure, while "defense" covers issues instructors have generally not considered, particularly detrimental to underrepresented students. The chapter states that building defense "refers to increasing students' sense of belonging, self-efficacy and identity as someone who can excel in physics and inculcating a growth mindset," because without it many underrepresented students are unlikely to derive the full benefit of problem-solving pedagogies. (Singh et al. (2022))
