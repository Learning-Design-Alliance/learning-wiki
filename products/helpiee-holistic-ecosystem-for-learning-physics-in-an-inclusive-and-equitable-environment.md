# HELPIEE: Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment

## Description
HELPIEE is a Cwik and Singh framework that combines problem-solving instruction with belonging, self-efficacy, physics identity, and growth-mindset supports for inclusive undergraduate physics learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HELPIEE: Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment**: The chapter introduces the HELPIEE framework (Cwik & Singh, 2021), shown in Fig. 3, for motivating all students to engage in productive problem-solving. It uses a chess/tennis-style "defense and offense" metaphor: "offense" covers helping students learn effective problem-solving approaches, transfer, and robust knowledge structure, while "defense" covers issues instructors have generally not considered, particularly detrimental to underrepresented students. The chapter states that building defense "refers to increasing students' sense of belonging, self-efficacy and identity as someone who can excel in physics and inculcating a growth mindset," because without it many underrepresented students are unlikely to derive the full benefit of problem-solving pedagogies. (Singh et al. (2022))
- **HELPIEE framework: instructors account for student characteristics to build equitable, inclusive physics learning environments**: The Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment (HELPIEE) framework holds that instructors have the power to help all students feel supported by accounting for students' characteristics and implementing effective pedagogies. The article states that "The HELPIEE framework posits that those in the position of power, e.g., instructors in physics classrooms, have the power to help all students feel supported by carefully taking into account students' characteristics and implementing effective pedagogies in an equitable and inclusive learning environment." Internal characteristics include prior preparation, self-efficacy, and identity; external characteristics include other commitments and support systems such as advising and mentoring. The framework guides analysis of how instructors can strengthen students' "Offense" or "Defense" skills for learning physics. (Lisabeth Marie Santana et al. (2024))
- **HELPIEE framework: instructors in positions of power must equitably support all demographic groups for a learning environment to be inclusive**: The Holistic Ecosystem for Learning Physics in an Inclusive and Equitable Environment (HELPIEE) framework "posits that those in the position of power, e.g., instructors in physics classrooms, have the power to help all students feel supported by carefully taking into account students' characteristics and implementing effective pedagogies in an equitable and inclusive learning environment". Under this framework, if students from different demographic groups do not have similar positive experiences and feelings of being supported, the learning environment is not equitable because those in power did not provide adequate support to level the playing field. The article uses HELPIEE together with the Domains of Power as synergistic lenses for analyzing the three departments. (Lisabeth Marie Santana et al. (2024))
