## Description
A commercially published astronomy workbook by Prather and colleagues that provides Socratic-dialogue, collaborative classroom activities for introductory astronomy learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lecture-Tutorials for Introductory Astronomy workbook**: Lecture-Tutorials for Introductory Astronomy (Prather et al. 2008, Second edition) is a commercially published workbook of collaborative, Socratic-dialogue classroom activities covering topics commonly taught in Astro 101 courses. The article describes how "the textbook provides a detailed exposition on these topics, while the Lecture-Tutorial uses a Socratic-dialogue approach of asking questions, and requires explanations to engage the student actively in grappling with the material". In the studied course the workbook was required throughout, used in class after mini-lectures, with unfinished activities completed as homework. (Rudolph (2011))
