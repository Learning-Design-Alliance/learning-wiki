# CResME (Clinical-reasoning Mapping Exercise)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A multimedia clinical-reasoning exercise system developed by Cloude et al. that presents linked clinical case information and requires medical students to match findings, construct diagnoses, and receive rubric-based evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Computer-based cognitive-mapping approach extending concept mapping to guide attention to key elements of cognition in clinical reasoning**: The article describes a computer-based cognitive-mapping approach that extends traditional concept mapping by guiding learners' attention to a set of key elements of cognition when working with clinical cases. Learners visualize a cognitive map in which one part represents the reasoning process — data capture, hypotheses formulation, reasoning with justifications — and the other represents the knowledge underlying reasoning, using captured data, hypotheses, support and against reasoning links, underlying concepts, and causal, hierarchical, and cross-link relations between concepts. The tool was refined from a prior pilot study based on learner feedback. (Wu Bian et al. (2016))
- **CRM's educational basis in meaningful learning, cognitive theory of multimedia learning, and Kolb's experiential learning cycle**: The author argues CRM's theoretical basis broadly fits constructivism and explains its utility through three named theories. Meaningful learning explains why learners found CRM helpful, since "the ability of CRM to link a collection of clinical events adds meaning by relating new information to those already known to the learner". Its combination of words linked by diagrammatic conventions reflects multimedia learning principles, where "words and pictures together" help produce logical mental constructs promoting deeper learning than words alone. CRM also enables reflection and abstract conceptualization, components of Kolb's experiential learning cycle. (Jayasinghe S (2016))
