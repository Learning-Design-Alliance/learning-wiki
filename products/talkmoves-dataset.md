## Description
The TalkMoves Dataset, created by Kirk Vanacore et al., is an expert-annotated corpus of K–12 mathematics classroom transcripts labeled for teacher discursive moves to support research on accountable-talk instruction and automated dialogue classification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TalkMoves Dataset of expert-annotated K–12 mathematics classroom transcripts**: The TalkMoves Dataset consists of classroom transcripts previously annotated and validated by expert educators to create ground truth labels for teacher discursive moves. The subset used in this study comprises "63 transcripts spanning a diverse range of grade levels," drawn from authentic K–12 mathematics settings including whole-class discussions, small-group problem-solving, and online lessons. Utterances are segmented by speaker and annotated at the utterance level with six pedagogically grounded Talk Moves; expert annotators achieved inter-rater reliability of Cohen's κ > 0.90. The experiment drew a proportional stratified random sample of 800 target utterances, yielding 467 unique chunks containing 13,239 utterances. (Kirk Vanacore et al. (2025))
