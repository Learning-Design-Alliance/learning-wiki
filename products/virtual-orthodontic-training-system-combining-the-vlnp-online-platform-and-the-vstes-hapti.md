# Virtual orthodontic training system combining the VLNP online platform and the VSTES haptic VR simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Nanjing Medical University software system combining the Virtual Learning Network Platform for online orthodontic practice with the Virtual Simulation Training and Evaluation System for haptic VR bracket-bonding training, automated scoring, and feedback.
