Parent Engagement Programme (PEP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A low-cost, school-based programme developed by Miller et al. that uses scheduled text messages to engage parents with pupils' tests, homework, and lesson content.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Texting parents about tests, homework and lesson content produced a small positive effect on maths attainment (ES 0.067) in a cluster RCT [+M]
  • Texting parents reduced pupil absenteeism (ES -0.054, about half a day) even though no texts concerned attendance [+M]
  • The PEP texting intervention showed no evidence of an impact on science attainment [-M]
