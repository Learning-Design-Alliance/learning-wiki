## Description
A structured, family-led meeting approach in which families, children, professionals, and significant others develop a shared plan to address a child’s needs, including school participation and wellbeing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Family Group Conference (FGC) in schools**: FGC is "a world widespread participatory approach" used to help families find their own solutions to problems affecting their lives. In schools it is a structured meeting between family members, professionals and significant others that produces a common plan for a pupil's school life; the referrer is the school rather than child-protection services. It is a family-led decision-making process based on the empowerment of families and children, combining family knowledge with professional knowledge. (Gianluca Argentin et al. (2016))
