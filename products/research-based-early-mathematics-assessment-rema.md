## Description
The Research-Based Early Mathematics Assessment (REMA) (Clements & Sarama, 2008) is the one mathematics outcome measure used in all three studies meeting WWC standards, administered at the end of 26 or 30 weeks of implementation. The report notes "The developer chose to include the term “Research-Based” in the name of the measure. This term does not reflect the opinion of the WWC." Group means, standard deviations, effect sizes, and improvement indices are reported per study.
