## Description
ConMap is a set of computer-administered term-association tasks developed by Beatty and colleagues to probe context-dependent conceptual knowledge structures in physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ConMap term-association tasks as probes of conceptual knowledge structure**: ConMap is a set of brief computer-administered tasks that elicit spontaneous term associations by presenting a prompt term, problem, or topic area and having subjects type related terms, recording each response with its time of entry to "capture the flow of concepts triggered in each subject's mind." Three variants probe different contexts: Term Prompted Term Entry (immediate conceptual neighborhood of a term), Problem Prompted Term Entry (associations in the context of a specific problem), and Free Term Entry (broad survey of a topic area). The framework rests on the premise that the structure of interconnections between knowledge elements, not just their presence, distinguishes expertise. (Ian D. Beatty et al. (2000))
