## Description
ALEKS PPL is McGraw Hill's adaptive mathematics placement and preparation assessment for placing post-secondary students into appropriate college mathematics courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALEKS PPL adaptive placement assessment**: ALEKS Placement, Preparation and Learning (ALEKS PPL) is an adaptive assessment system based on Knowledge Space Theory that recommends placement in post-secondary mathematics courses. It contains 314 items covering elementary mathematics to precalculus, with a limit of 29 questions. The assessment employs an adaptive querying process where "the student's previous answers affect the choice of the next item to ask." Approximately 97% of assessments reach the maximum limit of 29 questions. Placement recommendations fall into six categories based on item and percentage scores. (Matayoshi et al. (2021))
