## Description
A Cambridge-designed English proficiency assessment measuring general language skills, including listening, reading, writing, and speaking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Piloted mock Key English Test (KET) used for participant selection and as listening posttest**: A piloted mock Key English Test designed by Cambridge served two roles in the study: as a proficiency test to select a homogeneous sample, and its listening paper as the posttest. The article reports the selection test had "an acceptable Cronbach's Alpha Index of 0.849" and the 25-item listening posttest "enjoyed a reliability of 0.71". Inter-rater reliability for writing and speaking scoring was significant at the 0.01 level. (Hamid Marashi (2013))
