## Description
Family and Friends 2 MultiRom is Cambridge University Press's accompanying multimedia software providing game-like, computer-based grammar activities for learners using the Family and Friends 2 coursebook.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Family and Friends 2 MultiRom computer-based grammar activities**: The MultiRom accompanying the 'Family and Friends 2' coursebook (Naomi Simmons, Cambridge University Press) provides computer-based, game-like grammar activities for each unit, combining "colorful images and sounds". The software checks answers, marking correct matches green and incorrect ones red, and gives remarks such as 'Congratulations' or 'Try Again', which the authors treat as immediate feedback. It was used by both experimental groups while the control group received paper-based activities of similar content. (Karim Sadeghi et al. (2013))
