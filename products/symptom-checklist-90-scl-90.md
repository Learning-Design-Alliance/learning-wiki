# Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SCL-90 is a 90-item multidimensional self-report instrument used to screen psychological symptoms across ten symptom dimensions, developed for clinical and research use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SCL-90 symptom checklist as a multidimensional mental health screening instrument**: The Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90) is a "self-reported multidimensional inventory designed to screen for a broad range of psychological problems and symptoms of psychopathology, which includes 90 questions, each scored on a 5-point Likert scale". It assesses ten dimensions: somatization, obsessive–compulsive symptoms, interpersonal sensitivity, depression, anxiety, hostility, phobic anxiety, paranoid ideation, psychoticism, and additional sleep/diet items. In this study it was used to screen 6,923 undergraduates and classify positivity against national norms. (Cen Shanduo et al. (2025))
