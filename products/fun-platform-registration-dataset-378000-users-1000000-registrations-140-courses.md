# FUN platform registration dataset: 378,000 users, 1,000,000 registrations, 140 courses

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset supplied by France Université Numérique containing anonymized registration events and certificate outcomes from 140 French MOOC courses collected between October 2013 and September 2015.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FUN platform registration dataset: 378,000 users, 1,000,000 registrations, 140 courses**: France Université Numérique (FUN), the French national MOOC platform, supplied anonymized registration and certificate data collected from October 2013 to September 2015. The article reports that "In a 23-month period, the platform logged 1,000,000 registrations from 378,000 unique users to 140 courses." Each registration event records a user ID, time stamp, and whether a certificate was obtained; on average 8.1% of registrations produced a certificate. (Wintermute et al. (2019))
