> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Vanderbilt University framework that guides recruitment and hiring teams through self-reflection and bias-interruption strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PAUSE framework for interrupting bias in recruitment and hiring decisions**: The PAUSE framework, developed by Vanderbilt University, is presented as a tool that "can encourage critical self-reflection and interrupt the effects of unchecked biases." The guide recommends applying it to recruitment and hiring decisions to "reveal the ways in which bias might be manifesting in the process." It sits alongside measures such as the Harvard Implicit Association Test, which the guide calls "a starting point" for individuals and teams working to interrupt bias. (Equitable et al. (2023))
