## Description
A framework developed by the Partnership for 21st Century Skills that organizes core subjects, 21st-century content, learning and thinking skills, ICT literacy, and life skills for K–12 education and promotes integrated assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **P21's framework for twenty-first century learning adapted for AI-era teacher competencies**: The article applies P21's framework for twenty-first century learning to identify four key teacher competencies for online teaching with AI: core subjects, 3Rs and twenty-first century themes; learning and innovation skills; information, media and technology skills; and life and career skills. It emphasizes that teacher competencies "should not only focus on acquisition of basic AI knowledge and skills, but also cultivation of the qualities necessary for the teacher's adaptation to, survival in and control over future society". Examples span AI writing assistants, book recommendation systems, and workplace AI capability sets. (Davy Tsz Kit Ng et al. (2023))
- **Partnership for 21st Century Skills (P21) framework of learning and innovation, information/media/technology, and life and career skills**: The article organizes 21st century skills using the Partnership for 21st Century Learning framework, in which skills fall into three categories: "learning and innovation skills", "information, media, and technology skills", and "life and career skills" (Farisi, 2016; Pearson, 2013). The framework rests on "a solid foundation of content knowledge" supported by specific skills, expertise and literacies, and holds that every student should be a critical thinker, problem solver, innovator, effective communicator, self-directed learner, and information- and media-literate. Figure 1 depicts "pools" of standards and assessments, curriculum and instruction, professional development, and learning environment beneath the framework. (Hamid Ashraf et al. (2017))
