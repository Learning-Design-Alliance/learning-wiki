## Description
Mentice VIST C is a physics-based virtual-reality vascular-interventional simulator made by Mentice for hands-on endovascular procedural training with simulated patient cases, device interaction, haptic feedback, and performance metrics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VIST virtual reality endovascular simulation trainer**: The vascular interventional simulation trainer (VIST) is a physics-based high-fidelity endovascular VR simulator that "enables hands on procedural training for clinicians using real patient cases". It allows use of real medical devices for simulated endovascular procedures, provides step-by-step guidance and metric-based feedback, and computes haptic feedback in real time from the interaction between virtual devices and virtual anatomy (vessel geometry and vessel properties). In this article it hosted the construct validity assessment of the MT metrics. (Crossley Robert et al. (2019))

### Claims
