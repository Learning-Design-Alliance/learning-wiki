# NurSusTOOLKIT

## Description
NurSusTOOLKIT is a bilingual digital sustainability-literacy and competency resource package for nursing education, including the J3_A1 Children's Health and Environment module, developed for use by nursing educators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **e-NurSus Children intervention and NurSusTOOLKIT digital materials**: The e-NurSus Children intervention is a blended-learning teaching session on children's environmental health built from the NurSusTOOLKIT digital materials on sustainability and nursing, which the authors describe as "the only ones available that offer sustainability literacy and competency". Sessions use an asthmatic-child case study with group discussion, guided by trained tutors. All materials are available in Spanish and English on the NurSusTOOLKIT platform in the module J3_A1 Children's Health and Environment. (Álvarez-García C. et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Nursing students' attitudes, knowledge, and skills regarding children's environmental health rose from pre-test to post-test after the e-NurSus Children intervention, in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/enursus-children-improves-attitudes-knowledge-skills.md) [+M]
