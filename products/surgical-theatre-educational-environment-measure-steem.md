# Surgical Theatre Educational Environment Measure (STEEM)

## Description
A 40-item Likert-scale questionnaire developed by Cassar at the University of Aberdeen to measure surgical theatre learning environments across teaching and training, learning opportunities, atmosphere, and supervision/workload/support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Surgical Theatre Educational Environment Measure (STEEM), 40-item questionnaire**: The STEEM is a 40-item questionnaire developed by Cassar at the University of Aberdeen, evolved from PHEEM through a literature review and interviews with surgical trainers and trainees, and validated with Scottish basic surgical trainees. It measures the surgical operating theatre learning environment across four subscales — Teaching and training, Learning Opportunities, Atmosphere, and Supervision/workload/support — each item on a 0–4 Likert scale with reverse coding of 19 negative items, giving a maximum of 160. The article applies it online to podiatric surgery trainees for the first time. (Austen Thomas (2019))

### Claims
