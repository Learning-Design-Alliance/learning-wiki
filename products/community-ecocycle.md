## Description
The Community Ecocycle is a tool published by Equal Measure and Mathematica intended to help funders who invest in place-based systems change efforts. The article states it is "intended to help funders who have elected to invest in place-based systems change efforts in two ways": considering developmental phases and co-designing investment approaches with communities.
