## Description
CosmoQuest is an online citizen-science platform operated by the CosmoQuest project that supports authentic astronomy data-reduction tasks, embedded learning content, and volunteer community interaction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CosmoQuest online citizen science platform with embedded educational content and community engagement**: CosmoQuest is a fully-online astronomy citizen science facility that, per the article, embeds "educational content, community engagement, and collaboration" within its citizen science tools. It offers mapping tasks for the Moon, Mars, and Mercury, hosts discussion boards where many persons with social-emotional disabilities find safe and supportive social interaction, and signs off on volunteer forms for people receiving conditional disability support. Its 2019 Bennu Mappers launch returned to a text-heavy design explaining data use and credit. (Reiheld et al. (2019))
