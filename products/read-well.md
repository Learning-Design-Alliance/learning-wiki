## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Read Well® kindergarten and first-grade reading curriculum**: Read Well® is a reading curriculum for kindergarten and first-grade students whose goal is to increase literacy abilities, providing "instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, and fluency". It combines daily whole class activities with small group lessons, explicit systematic instruction in decoding, sustained practice in decodable text, and frequent vocabulary discussion, with story maps, story retells, and guided reports. It is developed by Sopris West Educational Services and distributed by Cambium Learning, with whole class, small group, and student packages available for purchase. (Read Well® (2010))
