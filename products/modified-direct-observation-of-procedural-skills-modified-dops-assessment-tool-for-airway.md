# Modified Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (modified DOPS) assessment tool for airway management simulation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A ten-domain, 1–9-rated observational rubric developed by Tang Lijun et al. for blinded evaluation of undergraduate trainees’ airway-management performance in simulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS) assessment tool for airway management simulation**: The study's primary outcome measure is "a modified Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS) assessment tool, which evaluated ten distinct aspects of performance", each scored 1–9 with bands for below-, at- and above-expected performance. Domains range from indications and anatomy through pre-oxygenation and difficult airway management to help-seeking, crisis management, team cooperation and overall performance. The authors report that significant improvements across multiple DOPS domains provide additional support for its practical application in anesthesiology education. (Tang Lijun et al. (2025))
