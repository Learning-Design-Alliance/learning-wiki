## Description
A Gates Foundation programme that supports networks of schools in using continuous improvement to improve outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, or experiencing poverty.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NSI conceptual framework: intermediary-led school networks using equity-centered continuous improvement to improve on-track outcomes**: The evaluation team's conceptual framework describes how intermediaries create and support networks of schools using continuous improvement to improve practices related to their outcome area. Schools form CI teams of teachers, counselors, administrators, and other staff expected to engage in six core CI parameters, with the initiative centering equity for Black, Latino, and students experiencing poverty at intermediary, network, and school levels. The framework links network participation to improved educator practices and student supports, and ultimately to improved on-track outcomes, with effective strategies shared across schools and districts over time. (Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report (2024))

## Description
The Networks for School Improvement (NSI) initiative is a Gates Foundation program that "support[s] networks of schools in using continuous improvement (CI) to improve outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, or experiencing poverty." The foundation sponsored an evaluation covering network formation, schools' use of CI, and impacts on student outcomes, comparing NSI schools to similar non-participating schools. This report covers impacts after schools' second year of participation, for 8th-grade on-track, 9th-grade on-track, and well-matched postsecondary enrollment NSI models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools participate in networks that use continuous improvement (CI) to improve targeted student outcomes, sponsored by the foundation.
- intermediary organizations to lead each network and coordinate school participation
#### Constraints
- The foundation expected the NSI to have their full impact after three years; this report presents impacts after schools' second year, which was heavily affected by COVID.
- impacts varied by network type, with college-ready on-track NSI showing no impact on any prioritized outcome

### Target Learners
- Students who are Black, Latino, or experiencing poverty in NSI secondary schools
- middle school students
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved student outcomes including GPA, core course pass rates, credit completion, FAFSA completion, and college enrollment
- course pass rates
- grade point average
- math achievement
- FAFSA completion
- college enrollment
