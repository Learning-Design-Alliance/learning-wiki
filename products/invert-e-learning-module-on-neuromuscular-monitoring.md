# INVERT e-learning module on neuromuscular monitoring

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A roughly 30-minute interactive e-learning module developed with a regional learning-technology unit to teach anaesthetists neuromuscular monitoring theory, practical monitoring and troubleshooting, and related clinical attitudes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **INVERT e-learning module on neuromuscular monitoring for anaesthetists**: A roughly 30-minute online module developed with a regional learning-technology unit to teach anaesthetists neuromuscular monitoring. It covered "theory of NMBA, residual neuromuscular block and reversal, as well as practical tips on neuromuscular monitoring and troubleshooting equipment malfunction," with interactive synaptic-cleft animations split into smaller sections "to reduce cognitive load," optional clickable content adapting to learners' needs, real monitor-output examples, and patient cases about butyrylcholinesterase deficiency aimed at changing attitudes. It included a ten-question pre- and post-course test. (Thomsen JLD et al. (2022))

### Claims
- [Anaesthetists' scores on a ten-question multiple-choice test of neuromuscular monitoring knowledge rose from pre-course to post-course after completing the e-learning module, in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/elearning-improved-nmba-knowledge-test-scores.md) [+M]
- [Acceleromyography use did not change detectably from before to after an e-learning module on neuromuscular monitoring across six Danish anaesthesia departments, in one pre-post study with no comparison group](../claims/elearning-no-overall-monitoring-effect.md) [-M]
