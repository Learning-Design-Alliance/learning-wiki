# Scientific Degree Project

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-year Italian national programme run through the Ministry of Education and collaborating universities to orient secondary students toward science degrees and develop science teachers through school–university laboratory projects.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Scientific Degree Project national structure: Ministry-funded collaboration among ministry, deans' conference and industry with over 100 sub-projects across 33 universities**: The Scientific Degree Project, started at the end of 2005 and financed for four years, was designed in 2004 by the Ministry of Education and Scientific Research, the National Conference of Deans of Science and Technology, and Confindustria. It pursued three main objectives: improving knowledge and awareness of science degrees in secondary school through stimulating laboratory activities, starting professional development of in-service science teachers through joint school-university work, and promoting alignment of university training with the working world. Student action was integrated with teacher training through more than 100 sub-projects with local referents in 33 universities, later 38, organized into four disciplinary areas. (Vera MONTALBANO (2012))
