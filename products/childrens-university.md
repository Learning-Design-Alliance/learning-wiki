# Children's University

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Children's University programme that validates supervised out-of-school learning activities and awards pupils credits and certificates for participation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Children's University Passport to Learning credit and certification system**: The Passport to Learning is each pupil's logbook (booklet and online version) recording every CU-validated activity and hours spent on it. Hours spent on approved learning activities are rewarded by collecting credits that lead to certificates, from Bronze Undergraduate Award level at 30 hours to Gold Fellowship level at 1,000 hours. Children choose their own activities and receive a stamp upon completion, and teachers log participation after training in the reward credit system. Completion of 30 hours earns certification and participation in a graduation ceremony attended by parents. (Stephen Gorard et al. (2017))
- **Children's University model of experience-based learning beyond classroom boundaries**: The Children's University concept is to take pupils' learning beyond the boundaries of the classroom, giving pupils more control of their learning aims through experience and action, rather than being taught in a classroom. The programme works through a network of local CU centres that approve and validate activities with structured learning aims conducted out-of-school hours, with no targeted syllabus content or prescribed activity outline. Learning Destinations such as museums, wildlife centres, historic places, theatres, and libraries provide opportunities to learn through experience, observations, attending talks and public lectures, and science festivals. Schools arrange visits to complement the curriculum and class lessons. (Stephen Gorard et al. (2017))
