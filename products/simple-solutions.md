# Simple Solutions spiral-review program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Simple Solutions is a spiral review supplement designed to build long-term mastery of key math skills through daily distributed practice. Each lesson includes "ten to fifteen review problems, typically completed in about 10–15 minutes per day", and the spiral structure continually reintroduces previously covered content to strengthen recall and reduce learning loss. It can be implemented in grades K–8 across math, reading, science, or social studies alongside the core curriculum, with online resources and automated scoring options.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom teachers deliver the daily lessons as a supplement to the core math curriculum; computers are needed for weekly quizzes.
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in grades 3–5 in a single Kentucky district quasi-experiment.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K–8; the evaluation covered grades 3–5, including economically disadvantaged students, students with IEPs, and English Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term retention and procedural fluency in mathematics, including problem-solving and multi-step reasoning
