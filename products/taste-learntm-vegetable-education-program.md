## Description
A teacher-led, five-week classroom vegetable-education programme for Australian primary schools, delivered through structured lessons involving tasting, sensory verbalization, science activities, and a positive learning environment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Taste & Learn™ vegetable education program for Australian primary schools**: Taste & Learn™ is a teacher-led, classroom-based 5-week vegetable education program for Australian primary schools, consisting of "5 × 1-h lessons for the three different stages of primary school". Its key elements are building exposure and familiarity with vegetables through tasting, verbalization of sensations, science experiments and a positive and fun environment, aligned to the Australian primary school curriculum. (Poelman A. A. M. et al. (2021))
