## Description
TiHoVideos is an open YouTube channel run by the University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover that provides multilingual instructional videos supporting veterinary clinical-skills learning stations and preparation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TiHoVideos YouTube channel of instructional videos for veterinary clinical skills learning stations**: TiHoVideos is a YouTube channel created by the University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover to provide open public access to the university's instructional videos accompanying CSL learning stations. Students use it "to help students prepare in advance for practical exercises, check their progress and practice their skills through repetition". By June 2018 it held 109 videos with 3160 subscribers, many videos translated into English, Russian, Chinese, Polish, Estonian and Spanish. (Müller Lina R. et al. (2019))
