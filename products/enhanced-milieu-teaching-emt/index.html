Enhanced Milieu Teaching (EMT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Enhanced Milieu Teaching is a semistructured, naturalistic, play-based language intervention implemented by trained teachers, parents, or clinicians to develop functional language skills in young children with language delays.

Components¶

  • Enhanced Milieu Teaching (EMT) as a naturalistic, play-based language intervention framework: EMT is described as "a well-researched, semistructured, play-based individualized intervention" implemented by teachers, parents, and clinicians, embedded in everyday activities and routines including play. It teaches functional language skills including vocabulary, early semantic relations, and syntax, using strategies such as language modeling in response to child communication and prompting language production in play. In this study, EMT sessions provided the interactional context in which teacher vocabulary use and supports were examined with children with low language skills in Head Start classrooms. (Ragan H. McLeod et al. (2019))

Claims¶

  • Teacher vocabulary used in related turns (following the child's lead) is more strongly related to child within-session vocabulary use than vocabulary in any utterance [+M]
  • Teacher use of target vocabulary words during EMT play sessions is significantly related to child target vocabulary use within the same session [+M]
