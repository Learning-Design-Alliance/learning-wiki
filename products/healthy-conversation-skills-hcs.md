## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Healthy Conversation Skills: a Social Cognitive Theory-based approach building self-efficacy through empowering, person-centred behaviour change conversations**: Healthy Conversation Skills (HCS) is an approach to behaviour change that trains healthcare professionals to communicate in a person-centred way, exploring patients' context and supporting them to find their own solutions. It is "Underpinned by the Taxonomy of Behaviour Change Techniques (BCTs)" and "based on Social Cognitive Theory", with behaviour change techniques such as problem solving and goal setting used to build practitioner self-efficacy, which practitioners then model with patients. It comprises four skills: asking open discovery questions, listening instead of giving information/making suggestions, reflecting on practice, and supporting SMARTER (Specific, Measurable, Action-oriented, Realistic, Timed, Evaluated, Reviewed) goal-setting. In this article it is integrated into the Wessex 'Making Every Contact Count' model and delivered as 2 x 3-hour online sessions accredited by the Royal Society for Public Health. (Parchment Amelia et al. (2025))
