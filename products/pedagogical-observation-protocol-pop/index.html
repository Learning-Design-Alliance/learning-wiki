Pedagogical Observation Protocol (POP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A low-inference, trained-rater instrument developed to measure teachers' pedagogical practices across classroom activity structure, instructional language, content, communication mode, grouping, and instructional strategy.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In an RCT, VPD+VMC treatment teachers differed significantly from control teachers in observed time allocation across five of six POP observation domains [+M]
