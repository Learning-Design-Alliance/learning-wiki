# Six foundational leadership knowledge areas for NGSS implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Domain 1 of the framework identifies six distinct but related knowledge bases leaders need: NGSS and the Innovations in NGSS; Equity and Access for All Learners; Instructional Materials, Curriculum, and Assessment; Professional Learning; Systems Change; and Continuous Improvement. The article states "leaders need in-depth knowledge within six distinct, but related knowledge bases (Figure 1.1)." These areas were identified through a summit with national experts and interviews with leaders in two states, and they inform all critical actions in Domain 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders at all levels need to develop knowledge in each of the six areas and understand intersections among them; depth needed may vary by role and system level.
#### Constraints
- No prior guidance existed on what knowledge leaders need for NGSS implementation; the areas were derived from the project's own literature review, summit, and interviews.

### Target Learners
- State, district, regional, and school science education leaders

### Learning Goals
- Understanding NGSS innovations, equity, curriculum and assessment quality, professional learning, systems change, and continuous improvement
