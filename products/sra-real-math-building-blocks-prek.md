## Description
Building Blocks for Math is a supplemental mathematics curriculum for preschool children that "embeds mathematical learning in children's daily activities", using software, manipulatives, and print materials across whole- and small-group activities, games, free-choice learning centers, and home activities. The software includes 150 activities, 60 intended for preschoolers, and activities aim to "mathematize" everyday activities such as building blocks, art, songs, stories, and puzzles. It was developed by Drs. Douglas Clements and Julie Sarama and distributed by SRA/McGraw-Hill.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implemented in center-based settings with children aged three to five or in preschool, in English, with teacher guidance provided for both off- and on-computer activities
#### Constraints
- Evaluated by the WWC only for mathematics achievement; no qualifying studies addressed oral language, print knowledge, phonological processing, early reading/writing, or cognition

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged three to five from low- and mixed-income families

### Target Learning Goals
- early mathematical knowledge, including number, geometry, measurement, and patterning
