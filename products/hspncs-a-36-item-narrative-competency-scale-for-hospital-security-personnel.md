## Description
A 36-item five-point Likert self-report scale developed by Fang Binru et al. to assess narrative competency and identify training needs among hospital security personnel.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HSPNCS: a 36-item narrative competency scale for hospital security personnel**: The HSPNCS is a newly developed instrument measuring narrative competency in hospital security staff. It uses a Likert 5-point scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The article states: "The HSPNCS is a newly developed 36-item scale consisting of nine first-order subdomains nested within three higher-order dimensions: self-reflection, empathy, and professionalism." It is intended for assessing competency and identifying training needs. (Fang Binru et al. (2026))
