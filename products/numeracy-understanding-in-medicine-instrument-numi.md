# Numeracy Understanding in Medicine instrument (NUMi)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NUMi is a 20-item multiple-choice objective health-numeracy assessment covering number sense, probability, statistics, and tables and graphs, developed for measuring health numeracy in populations including medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Numeracy Understanding in Medicine instrument (NUMi) used with medical students**: The NUMi is an objective health-numeracy measure consisting of 20 multiple-choice numerical tasks placed in health context, covering four domains: Number sense, Probability, Statistics and Tables and Graphs; the score is the sum of correct answers (theoretical range 0–20). In this study it was translated to Croatian and back-translated, with reliability α = 0.75 (95% CI 0.70 to 0.79). The authors report this is the first use of NUMi with medical students and note it may capture small but significant differences. (Buljan Ivan et al. (2019))
