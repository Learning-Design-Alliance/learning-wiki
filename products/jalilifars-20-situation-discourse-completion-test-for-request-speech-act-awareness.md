# Jalilifar's 20-situation Discourse Completion Test for request speech act awareness

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 20-situation written discourse-completion assessment developed by Jalilifar for measuring learners' production and awareness of request speech acts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **20-situation Discourse Completion Test for request speech act awareness**: The study's measure was a written Discourse Completion Test questionnaire developed by Jalilifar (2009), consisting of "20 situations followed by a blank space in which the participants were asked to write a request accordingly". Items were chosen for relevance to a university student's life so learners could imagine the scenarios. Each correct item was awarded one mark, with a maximum score of 20, and the same questionnaire served as pretest and posttest. (Fatemeh Abdollahizadeh Masouleh et al. (2014))
