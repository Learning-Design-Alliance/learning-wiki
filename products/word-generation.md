## Description
Word Generation is a school curriculum developed by researchers and educators to build middle-school students’ academic vocabulary and civic discourse through weekly dilemma-based units.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Word Generation weekly unit pattern: debatable dilemma, embedded academic words, discussion activities, debate, and a taking-a-stand paragraph**: A reusable lesson/unit pattern in which each week's dilemma is treated for 15-20 minutes a day over five days. Brief accessible articles present a balanced picture of a civic, moral, or economic dilemma with target academic words embedded; activities model additional word uses; a weekly debate lets students formulate and defend perspectives; and students write a brief paragraph arguing their own perspective using the target words. The article lists these as the program's basic design principles. (Catherine E. Snow (2014))
