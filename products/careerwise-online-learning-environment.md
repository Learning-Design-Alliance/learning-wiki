## Description
CareerWISE is a research-based online learning environment developed and evaluated by Bekki and colleagues to build interpersonal problem-solving and resilience skills among women pursuing STEM doctorates.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CareerWISE online learning environment for women STEM doctoral students**: CareerWISE is an online learning environment grounded in research on personal resilience and coping, self-efficacy and social-cognitive career theories, and problem-solving processes and cognitive-behavioral interventions. It offers skill building around four areas of common concern: "handling difficult interactions with an advisor, juggling academic and personal commitments, navigating a climate that can be unfriendly to women and managing delays and setbacks". The program contains "more than 250 content pieces", including over 160 video clips of 3 to 8 minutes and over 50 informational and instructional modules, self-tests, practice exercises, and links to professional resources. (Jennifer M. Bekki et al. (2014))
