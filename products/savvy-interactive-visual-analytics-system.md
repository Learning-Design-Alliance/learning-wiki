## Description
SAVVY is a research-developed interactive visual analytics system that helps teachers analyze student attention patterns and revise instructional videos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAVVY interactive visual analytics system with Concept, Group, and Individual modules**: SAVVY is an interactive visual analytics system that integrates visual and auditory attention to support top-down exploration of student attention variations in video-based learning. It comprises three coordinated visualization modules linked by a shared timeline: a Concept Module presenting course content structure and concept-level attention distribution, a Group Module visualizing group attention trends alongside audiovisual information density, and an Individual Module providing multi-temporal-resolution attention trajectories. The modules incorporate "course content structure, audiovisual information density, and attentional resource allocation," enabling teachers to analyze causes of attention fluctuations and inform instructional video improvement through a top-down workflow from course overview to individual verification. (Shixian Zhou et al. (2026))
