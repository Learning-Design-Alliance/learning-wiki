## Description
A Creative Commons collection of five short astronomy animations produced by MICA students with scientist mentorship for use in university science and art classes and public outreach.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Animated 3D electronic version of the nuclide chart with labelled towers, rendered in Blender and distributed as an mp4 flyby movie**: The article describes a complementary electronic artifact: "we have furthermore created an animated 3D electronic version of the nuclide chart, by using the software Blender". Its stated purpose is "the possibility of showing the full chart to participants, particularly early in the construction at large-scale events, while the chart is not still incomplete", so ambassadors can explain physical properties across the whole chart before the physical build is finished. The movie keeps the physical version's colour coding and tower sizes but labels each tower with the isotope name, and is distributed in mp4 format and on the project's YouTube channel. (C. Aa. Diget et al. (2016))
