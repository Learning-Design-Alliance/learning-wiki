KIPP Fisher Fellowship¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A KIPP programme that prepares new principals to lead new KIPP schools.

Components¶

  • KIPP Fisher Fellowship program preparing new principals to lead new KIPP schools: One of three KIPP school leadership programs examined in the evaluation, described as "the Fisher Fellowship program, which prepares new principals to lead new KIPP schools". The evaluation also examined the reliability of the instrument KIPP uses to select participants for this fellowship program. (Elias Walsh et al. (2022))

Claims¶

  • The Fisher Fellowship selection instrument is reliable, though three of 34 items had relatively low levels of one type of reliability [~W]
  • Former participants desired more follow-up with their cohort and more support developing talent, managing resources, and cultivating organizational culture [~W]
  • Former KIPP school leadership program participants reported satisfaction and application of program lessons in their own settings [+W]
