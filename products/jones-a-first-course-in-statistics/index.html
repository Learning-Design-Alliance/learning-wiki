Jones' A First Course in Statistics¶

Description¶

A statistics textbook by Jones that provides psychology and education research students with mathematical foundations in data description, correlation, probability, sampling, and curve fitting.

Components¶

  • Jones' A First Course in Statistics (1921), a two-part statistics textbook for education and psychology research workers: The reviewed artifact is Jones' textbook 'A First Course in Statistics' (G. Bell & Sons, London, 1921, pp. IX + 286). Part I, using economic and biological illustrations, covers "variables and frequency distribution; classification and tabulation of materials; averages, dispersion, graphs and correlation." Part II gives students "access to a treatment of probability, sampling, curve fitting and type distribution curves." (H. O. R (1922))
