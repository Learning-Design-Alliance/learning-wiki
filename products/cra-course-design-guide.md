## Description
This guide is a Digital Promise resource produced by the CRA Collaborative to support instructors in developing a new graduate-level introductory CRA course or integrating its components into existing measurement courses. It is organized into seven sections covering working definitions, learning objectives, course activities, course assessment, readings and resources, practical implementation considerations, and appendices with sample lessons. The structure is "intentionally modular—users may engage with the entire guide in sequence or draw on specific sections to meet their immediate needs."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors adapt the frameworks and examples to their institutional context; the guide explicitly does not prescribe a course sequence or fixed syllabus
#### Constraints
- It is not a comprehensive literature review, not a prescriptive curriculum, and provides no institution-specific guidance on prerequisites or policy alignment

### Target Learners
- graduate students in educational measurement programs

### Target Learning Goals
- design and implement a graduate course on culturally responsive assessment
