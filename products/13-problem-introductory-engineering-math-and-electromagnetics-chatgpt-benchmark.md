## Description
A benchmark dataset of 13 introductory engineering-mathematics and electromagnetics problems used to evaluate ChatGPT performance, compiled by Kumar et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A 13-problem benchmark of introductory engineering-math and electromagnetics problems with per-trial ChatGPT scores**: A set of 13 college-level problems spanning matrix operations, integration of charge densities, divergence, electric fields, capacitance, and induced current, drawn from UW-Madison ECE introductory electromagnetics flipped-learning sessions and quizzes. The article reports per-problem trial scores for ChatGPT-3.5, ChatGPT-4, and ChatGPT-4/CI, noting the problems "were selected somewhat arbitrarily from typical problems that UW-Madison ECE students in introductory electromagnetics were expected to complete." (Tanuj Kumar et al. (2023))
