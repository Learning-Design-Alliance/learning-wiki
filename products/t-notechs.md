## Description
T-NOTECHS is a five-domain, anchored Likert-scale assessment tool for rating leadership, communication, situational awareness, decision making and teamwork during recorded resuscitations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Use T-NOTECHS as the tool of choice for assessing non-technical skills of ED resuscitation teams via video review**: The review recommends T-NOTECHS for video-based assessment of NTS in ED resuscitation teams because it is validated, most studied, and balances usability, reliability and validity. It states that "when assessing NTS more holistically, T-NOTECHS demonstrates usability, reliability and validity". T-NOTECHS splits NTS into 5 categories on a 5-point Likert scale and provides score anchors to guide users. (Alexander Emily G et al. (2023))
