## Description
T-NOTECHS is a five-domain, anchored Likert-scale assessment tool for rating leadership, communication, situational awareness, decision making and teamwork during recorded resuscitations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Use T-NOTECHS as the tool of choice for assessing non-technical skills of ED resuscitation teams via video review**: The review recommends T-NOTECHS for video-based assessment of NTS in ED resuscitation teams because it is validated, most studied, and balances usability, reliability and validity. It states that "when assessing NTS more holistically, T-NOTECHS demonstrates usability, reliability and validity". T-NOTECHS splits NTS into 5 categories on a 5-point Likert scale and provides score anchors to guide users. (Alexander Emily G et al. (2023))
- **T-NOTECHS assessment tool for trauma-team non-technical skills**: The T-NOTECHS (Trauma NOn-TECHnical Skills) is a behavioral observation instrument adapted from aviation NOTECHS to assess trauma-team non-technical skills during resuscitation. "The T-NOTECHS consists of five behavioral domains: leadership, cooperation and resource management, communication and interaction, assessment and decision making, and situation awareness/coping with stress." Each domain is scored on a five-point Likert scale (5 = perfect behavior, 1 = behavior not demonstrated), and domain scores are summed to a total from 5 to 25, where 25 indicates perfect teamwork. This study evaluated its reliability when applied to video-recorded resuscitations. (van Maarseveen O. E. C. et al. (2020))
