# AAPT six-area taxonomy of learning goals for the undergraduate physics lab curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A curriculum-planning framework published by the American Association of Physics Teachers that organizes undergraduate physics laboratory learning goals into six areas: modeling, experimental design, technical and practical skills, knowledge construction, data analysis and visualization, and physics communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AAPT framework of six themes of learning goals for the undergraduate physics lab curriculum**: The American Association of Physics Teachers released a set of recommendations for learning goals spanning the full undergraduate physics lab curriculum. The recommendations "span a broad array of goals that fall into six themes": Modeling ("defining models as abstract representations of physical and measurement systems with limitations and approximations"), Designing experiments, Developing technical and practical skills, Analyzing and visualizing data, Constructing knowledge, and Communicating physics. The authors use this framework as the starting point for their own lab redesign. (N.G. Holmes et al. (2014))
