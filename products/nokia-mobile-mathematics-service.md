# Nokia Mobile Mathematics service

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free mobile-delivered mathematics revision service developed by Nokia and partners for Grade 10 learners in South African public schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nokia Mobile Mathematics service for Grade 10 learners**: A free, mobile-delivered Mathematics revision service for Grade 10 learners in South Africa, organised by the national curriculum topics, with short theory and worked-example sections and closed questions (multiple choice, spot-the-error, type-in-the-answer, true/false) drawn from a database of approximately 10,000 questions at three difficulty levels. Learners received hints and immediate feedback with complete worked solutions, and could choose topics freely. The content "aimed to support active learning and 'learning by doing' mathematics", and data costs were paid by mobile operators. (Roberts N et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Non-users of the service declined 19 percentage points in Mathematics attainment from Grade 9 to Grade 10 versus 11.5 points for regular/extensive users (d = 0.45)](../claims/non-users-decline-more-than-regular-users-mobile-maths.md) [+M]
- [21% of targeted Grade 10 Mathematics learners voluntarily used the mobile mathematics service regularly, with little teacher involvement](../claims/21-percent-voluntary-regular-use-mobile-maths.md) [+M]
