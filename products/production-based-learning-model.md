Students move through nine ordered steps, from identifying and defining product problems, generating and selecting alternative solutions, and designing solving techniques, through ordering work contracts, scheduling, executing orders, and quality control, to assessment. The article states the syntax's novelty "contains the syntax of the learning model to st rengthen the aspects of metacognitive and critical thinking skills needed to solve troubleshooting problems", and the steps are supported by model books, modules, and manuals.
