# Clinical Simulation Learning Leadership and Teamwork Scale (CSL-LTS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CSL-LTS is an 18-item self-report scale developed by González-Moret et al. to assess nursing students' teamwork and leadership competencies and their transfer from clinical simulation to clinical practice.

### Claims

- [The CSL-LTS demonstrates adequate content validity with Aiken's V values exceeding 0.8 for all three assessed attributes](../claims/csl-lts-content-validity-aiken-v.md) [+W]
- [The CSL-LTS demonstrates adequate internal consistency with Cronbach's α of 0.832 and McDonald's ω of 0.841](../claims/csl-lts-internal-consistency.md) [+W]
- [The CSL-LTS demonstrates strong temporal stability with an intraclass correlation coefficient of 0.991 over a 15-day test–retest interval](../claims/csl-lts-temporal-stability-icc.md) [+W]
- [A pilot test with 20 nursing students found no difficulties in understanding or completing the questionnaire](../claims/csl-lts-pilot-comprehensibility.md) [+W]
