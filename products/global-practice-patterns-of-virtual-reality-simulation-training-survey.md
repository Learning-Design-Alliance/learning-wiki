# Global Practice Patterns of Virtual Reality Simulation Training survey

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-created anonymous survey assessing access, use, training practices, and opinions concerning virtual reality simulation in ophthalmic surgical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **39-question Global Practice Patterns of Virtual Reality Simulation Training survey**: An anonymous 39-question survey entitled "Global Practice Patterns of Virtual Reality Simulation Training", created on SurveyMonkey and reviewed for relevance by ophthalmologists with VRS expertise. It was emailed on November 20th, 2023 to first or last authors of ophthalmology VRS publications on Pubmed and to directors of centers using VRS, with two follow-up reminders, closing March 31st, 2024. Respondents reported gender, age, position, simulator access, training time, module completion, and opinions on a surgical license. (Global trends and practice patterns in virtual reality simulation training for ophthalmic )
