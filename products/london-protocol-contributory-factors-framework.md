## Description
A patient-safety framework used to categorize contributory factors and latent safety threats across patient, task and technology, staff, team, work-environment, organizational, and institutional levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **London Protocol contributory factors framework for categorizing latent safety threats**: The London Protocol contributory factors framework is a comprehensive taxonomy of system threats ranging from proximal to distal, used in this study to code latent safety threats identified during in situ simulation. The article explains that "The set of 'contributory factors' in the London Protocol range from proximal threats such as staff skillsets and patient characteristics to more distal threats such as management decisions and organizational processes." The framework spans seven factor types (patient, task and technology, individual staff, team, work environment, organizational and management, institutional context), and the authors added three subcategories (task distribution, knowing names of team members, medication storage) where existing subcategories did not describe observed threats. (Long Jennifer A. et al. (2022))
