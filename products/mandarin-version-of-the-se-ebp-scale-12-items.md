# Mandarin version of the SE-EBP scale (12 items)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 12-item Mandarin self-report scale adapted for Taiwanese nurses to measure self-efficacy across the five steps of evidence-based nursing practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mandarin version of the SE-EBP scale (12 items) for measuring self-efficacy in evidence-based practice**: A 12-item, 10-point Likert scale measuring nurses' self-efficacy across the 5A steps of EBN, adapted from Chang and Crowe's 2011 SE-EBP scale. The article reports that three TEBMA-qualified experts reduced the scale from 28 to 12 items, achieving "the CVI value of 0.97and the internal consistency (Cronbach's α) of 0.97." Scores range from 12 to 120 in 1-point increments. (Chu Tsung-Lan et al. (2019))
