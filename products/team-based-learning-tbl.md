# Team-Based Learning (TBL)

## Description
A structured collaborative-learning framework supported by the Team-Based Learning Collaborative that organizes preparation, readiness assurance, feedback, team application activities, and peer evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The standardized TBL process framework: a prescribed sequence of steps plus four key components**: The article describes Team-Based Learning as "a highly structured form of learning, involving the performance of a prescribed sequence of steps and the implementation of key components." The sequence runs pre-class preparation, readiness assurance (IRAT, TRAT, immediate feedback and clarification), problem-solving activities, and a closing summary. The framework's four key components are "carefully formed and managed teams, frequent and timely feedback, problem-solving activities, and student peer evaluation." Application activities follow the 4 S's: Significant problem, Same problem, Specific choice, Simultaneous reporting. The study implemented this full framework in a 6-week systems course and assessed its effect on student perception. (Andrews-Dickert Rebecca et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Faculty at one osteopathic medical school reported wide variation in how often they implemented the standard steps and components of the TBL process, with peer evaluation and appeals least frequently implemented](../claims/faculty-variation-tbl-implementation.md) [+M]
