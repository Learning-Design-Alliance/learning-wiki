# HUFOES (HUman Factors in intraoperative Ophthalmic Emergencies Scoring System)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A scoring system developed by Wood Thomas Charles et al. for trained consultant ophthalmic surgeons to assess non-technical skills in cataract-surgery posterior capsule rupture simulations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HUFOES: the HUman Factors in intraoperative Ophthalmic Emergencies Scoring System for non-technical skills in cataract surgery**: HUFOES is a content-validated scoring system for non-technical skills using PCR as a focus, outlining the NTS required for managing intraoperative complications in ophthalmic surgery. The article uses it as the primary outcome measure, with "two independent and blinded assessors" rating recorded simulations, and reports it detected a statistically significant NTS improvement. Its skills taxonomy also informed the design of the simulation scenario. (Wood Thomas Charles et al. (2022))

### Claims
- [Surgeons' mean HUFOES scores for non-technical skills rose significantly from 48.7 ± 16.6 to 59.2 ± 14.8 after simulation-based PCR training with an NTS-focused intervention, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (p < 0.001)](../claims/nts-simulation-training-improves-hufoes-scores.md) [+M]
