CBM-W Picture Word task¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A curriculum-based writing measurement task developed by Pyung-Gang Jung and colleagues in which Grades 1–3 students generate sentences from pictured words under timed, standardized conditions for screening and progress monitoring.

Components¶

  • CBM-W Picture Word task for screening, progress monitoring, and pre/post assessment of early writing: Curriculum-based measurement in writing using the Picture Word prompt, in which students generate as many sentences as possible from nine pictured words in 3 minutes, scored as total words written, words spelled correctly, correct word sequences, and correct minus incorrect word sequences. The article uses it for screening, pre- and posttesting, and twice-weekly progress monitoring, noting it has "shown sufficient technical adequacy in terms of reliability, validity, and sensitivity to growth." (Pyung-Gang Jung et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Treatment effects did not differ by CBM-W scoring procedure or WJ III subtest, with students appearing to improve both basic and more complex writing skills [+W]
