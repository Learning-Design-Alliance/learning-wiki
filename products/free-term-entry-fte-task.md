# Free Term Entry (FTE) task

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A computer-based timed spontaneous term-listing assessment developed by Beatty et al. to elicit students' active domain vocabulary and investigate conceptual knowledge structure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Use spontaneous term-entry tasks as an easier-to-administer probe of core conceptual structure than hand-drawn concept maps**: Because TPTE response lists approximate a subset of hand-drawn concept map nodes, especially the most central level-one nodes, the article suggests "the possibility of using a TPTE-based assessment as an easier-to-administer, easier-to-evaluate equivalent to the much-studied HDCM." Concept maps are tedious and time-consuming to administer and score, limiting mass adoption, while term entry is rapid and computer-scorable. The principle targets diagnostic assessment of conceptual knowledge structure rather than grading or problem-solving measurement. (Ian D. Beatty et al. (2000))

### Claims
