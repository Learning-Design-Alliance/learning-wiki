## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BEST bilingual peer-tutoring program pairing high school mentors with low-income elementary children**: Bilingual English/Spanish Tutors (BEST), developed by Aaron Dominguez in Aurora, Illinois, aims to improve the future and stability of the Hispanic community by supporting its young people's educational and social accomplishments. It "pairs high-achieving high school stu- dent men- tors with low-income elementary school children," with tutors helping younger peers with homework, reading, and math after school twice a week. The talk reports the program "has enrolled over 60 students and 35 bilingual high school mentors," and that the BEST model has been replicated in the neighboring community of Batavia. (Chris Quigg (2005))
