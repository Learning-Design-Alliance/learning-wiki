# The Attitude Scale in Physical Education and Sports (BESTÖ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 24-item self-report scale developed by Demirhan and Altay to measure students' attitudes toward physical education and sports.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Course of Physical Education and Sports Attitude Scale (BESTÖ)**: The study used "The Attitude Scale in Physical Education and Sports (BESTÖ) developed by Demirhan and Altay (2001)" to measure student attitudes. The authors re-analyzed its construct validity, reporting "the Cronbach alpha value of the Physical Education Attitude Scale was calculated as .901 and the KMO (Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin Measure of Sampling Adequacy) value was calculated as .814". The 24-item scale was applied before and after the four-week intervention. (Mustafa Karadağ et al. (2018))
