## Description
ClinicalKey Student is Elsevier's digital medical education platform providing access to medical textbooks and related study features for health-professions learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ClinicalKey Student e-learning platform (Elsevier) with Adobe Analytics tracking**: ClinicalKey Student is an interactive education platform evaluated in this study that "provides access to more than 140 accepted medical textbooks covering over 40 medical specialties". It supports online reads, downloads to a digital Bookshelf app for offline access, and export of created presentations into PowerPoint. Adobe Analytics tracked access dates, daily activity counts, items used, and whether items were viewed online or downloaded. (Kipp Markus (2021))
