Ethical AI and Workforce Readiness (EAWR) micro-credential framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A proposed micro-credential framework by İsmail Şan et al. that organizes AI literacy for Turkish undergraduates across functional use, algorithmic understanding, and ethical governance to support workforce readiness.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • AI governance vocabulary (bias, transparency, privacy, accountability, regulation, fairness) is a structural zero in students' AI associative networks [+M]
  • Algorithmic and technical-mechanism concepts are marginal in students' AI networks: machine learning was produced by a single participant across 436 respondents [+M]
  • Turkish undergraduates show a use-cognition gap: 77.7% use AI tools at least several times per week while formal AI education remains low [+M]
