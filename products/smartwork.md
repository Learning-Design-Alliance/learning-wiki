# Smartwork

## Description
Smartwork is W. W. Norton & Company's digital learning platform for delivering interactive, textbook-linked assignments and quizzes with feedback and performance tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Smartwork weekly formative multiple-choice quiz system with immediate textbook-linked feedback**: Smartwork (W. W. Norton & Company) is an electronic platform used to deliver a weekly formative interactive multiple-choice quiz in a first-year Medical Cell Biology module. Questions combine instructor-designed items and eBook test-bank items covering recall, understanding, application and analysis. "Quiz feedback was immediate and crucially included links directly to relevant subsections in the electronic textbook", allowing repeat attempts after targeted reading. Built-in statistics tracked engagement, categorised as no, partial or full engagement. (Lees-Murdock D. J. et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Full engagement with weekly Smartwork active-learning quizzes is associated with significantly higher marks on class test, exam, coursework and overall module performance](../claims/full-engagement-smartwork-higher-performance-all-assessments.md) [+M]
- [Engaged students report positive perceptions of Smartwork quizzes, with about 78% believing the quizzes helped them perform better in class tests](../claims/student-feedback-smartwork-quizzes-positive.md) [+W]
