# Academic eValuation Matrix (AVM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A customizable qualitative and quantitative evaluation matrix proposed by Samar Shailendra et al. for academic institutions to assess the effectiveness and success of GenAI adoption across curriculum, teaching, access, integrity, participation, and progression.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Academic eValuation Matrix (AVM) for measuring GenAI adoption effectiveness**: The AVM is an evaluation matrix proposed to "assess the impact and effectiveness" of GenAI adoption changes. The article states it "can incorporate both qualitative and quantitative measures, is not a fixed matrix" and should be customised by the Academic Board and Learning & Teaching Committee to course and unit requirements. Parameters include awareness, readiness, ethics and privacy, ICT adoption, equitable access, placements, integrity incidents, participation, retention and progression. An example matrix with actors and measurement approaches is provided in the Appendix. (Samar Shailendra et al. (2024))
