## Description
SEMLA is a group-based preschool intervention developed by Gerholm and colleagues in which trained staff guide children through creative, socioemotional construction projects using an inspirational materials space.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SEMLA: a group-based socioemotional and material learning intervention for preschool children**: SEMLA (Social and Emotional Material Learning) is a group-based preschool intervention in which children in groups of 6–8, guided by trained staff, work on a creative construction project ("How to live and get around 100 years from now") in a material space of inspirational materials. Its stated mechanisms are individual and group learning scaffolded by teachers, a socioemotionally supportive learning environment, and aesthetic, playful, creative exploration, with pedagogical documentation revisited during the project. It was implemented for 1 ½-hour sessions, 4 days a week over 6 weeks. (Gerholm Tove et al. (2018))
