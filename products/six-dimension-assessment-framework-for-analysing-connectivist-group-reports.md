# Six-dimension assessment framework for analysing connectivist group reports

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A six-dimension framework developed by Daniel Alejandro Valderrama et al. for assessing prospective physics teachers' group reports across conceptualisation, argumentation, questioning, critical thinking, connection-building, and digital-resource use.

### Claims

- [In a connectivist Contemporary Physics sequence, prospective physics teachers' group reports showed generally favourable developments across assessed dimensions, with argumentation and questioning improving over the sequence](../claims/connectivist-sequence-report-dimensions-improved.md) [+M]
- [Group-level assessment figures showed variation between reports within the connectivist sequence, including a decline in Group 3's seventh report](../claims/group-3-report-variation-decline-seventh.md) [+W]
- [Participants in the connectivist activity predominantly agreed that connectivism is suitable for collaborative digital learning and that WhatsApp discussions helped them understand physics concepts](../claims/student-perceptions-connectivist-activity-favourable.md) [+W]
- [Students valued combining digital and conventional resources, with 19 of 21 strongly agreeing the combination enriched their learning experience](../claims/digital-conventional-resource-combination-valued.md) [+W]
