## Description
A computer-based virtual manipulative developed by Md Amiruzzaman and colleagues for teaching measures of central tendency by allowing learners to manipulate data elements on an interactive number line.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interactive Statistical Number Line (ISNL), a computer-based virtual manipulative for measures of central tendency**: The ISNL is a computer-based virtual manipulative in the form of a number line used within tasks on measures of central tendency. It "allowed PSMTs to manipulate the location of data as discrete elements within a dataset," and for mode-based tasks it generally resembled a line plot. The authors hypothesized that, though visually similar to a paper-based line plot, its virtual manipulation of each element would engage participants in considering how all elements are represented aspects of the data set. (Md Amiruzzaman et al. (2017))
