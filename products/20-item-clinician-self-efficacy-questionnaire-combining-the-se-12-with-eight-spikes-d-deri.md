# 20-item clinician self-efficacy questionnaire combining the SE-12 with eight SPIKES-D-derived items

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 20-item self-report questionnaire developed by FitzGerald et al. by combining the 12-item SE-12 clinical communication self-efficacy scale with eight SPIKES-D-derived items to measure clinicians’ self-efficacy in communicating dementia diagnoses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SE-12 self-efficacy questionnaire for clinical communication skills**: The SE-12 is a 12-item self-report questionnaire measuring clinicians' self-efficacy in clinical communication, with items reflecting the Calgary-Cambridge Guide structure. Each item asks "How certain are you that you are able to successfully …" followed by a specific skill, answered on a 10-point scale from 1 (very uncertain) to 10 (very certain), plus a "not relevant" check box and 5 background items. It is user-friendly and can be administered electronically. (Axboe Mette K. et al. (2016))
