## Description
An annual symposium series run at NetSci conferences that convenes network-science researchers and educators through presentations, posters, and roundtable discussions to advance network science education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NetSciEd symposium series at NetSci conferences**: An annual symposium series held at NetSci conferences every year since 2012 that, per the article, aims "to bring network science researchers and educators together to discuss any issues that arise at the intersection between network science and education." Programs include oral presentations, posters, and roundtable discussions, with NetSci High student posters displayed. Table 2 reports growing unsolicited submissions (from 0 in 2012-2014 to 6 by NetSciEd6 2017), which the authors read as indicating growing interest in education among network science researchers. A tangible outcome is the forthcoming Springer book "Network Science in Education" (Cramer et al. 2017). (Hiroki Sayama et al. (2017))
