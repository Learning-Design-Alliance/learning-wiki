## Description
A branded 5- to 7-week teacher-preparation programme run by Teach For America in which corps members teach summer school under supervision while receiving coaching, feedback, and coursework.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TFA summer training institute combining supervised practice teaching, coaching, and coursework**: The TFA training institute is a 5- to 7-week residential program in which corps members practice teaching in a real classroom under supervision while receiving observation, feedback, and coursework. The report describes how the institute "includes teaching summer school in a real classroom under the supervision of a regular classroom teacher", observation and feedback from TFA instructional coaches, small-group practice and reflection sessions, lesson planning clinics, and coursework in instructional planning, classroom management, diversity, and literacy development. (Teach For America (2016))
