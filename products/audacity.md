## Description
Audacity is a free, open-source audio recording, editing, and sound-analysis application developed by the Audacity team for use on major desktop operating systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Audacity free open-source audio program as a classroom tool for recording, displaying, and spectrally analyzing sounds**: Audacity is a free, open source audio program for recording, editing, and analyzing sounds, supporting Mac OS X, Microsoft Windows, and Gnu/Linux. In this application it is used with the computer's built-in microphone to record sounds, display waveforms, and compute Fourier transforms via the Analyze -> Plot Spectrum menu item, and it also offers a time-dependent spectrogram display mode. The paper presents it as an intuitive tool that lets students see and hear the frequency components of everyday sounds. (Amy C. Courtney et al. (2011))
- **Audacity open-source software for recording and spectral analysis of sounds**: Audacity is the freeware open-source tool at the core of the path's measurement activities. The software allows to record sounds by a personal computer or a mobile device; recorded files contain a digital sampling of the analog electric signal and can be read, modified, superimposed to other ones or decomposed into spectral components. Students use it to characterize phonemes they produce and to analyse environmental sounds such as guitar notes, wind and car stereo (fig. 5). (Montalbano V (2013))
