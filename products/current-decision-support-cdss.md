# Current Decision Support (CDSS)

## Description
A searchable Japanese clinical decision-support system made by Precision Co. that generates differential diagnoses from entered symptoms and provides disease characteristics, diagnostic criteria, treatments, and drugs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Current Decision Support (CDSS) used with ten expert-developed case vignettes**: The intervention tool was the Current Decision SupportⓇ CDSS (Precision Co., Tokyo, Japan), searchable in Japanese: "when a symptom is entered, a list of differential diagnoses is displayed in categories such as high frequency and serious", and selecting a disease displays its characteristics, diagnostic criteria, treatment methods and drugs. It was provided free to students and faculty at the research hospital. Students answered SQs first, entered them into the CDSS, then submitted a diagnosis based on the output, across ten case vignettes presented via Microsoft Forms. (Yanagita Yasutaka et al. (2025))
