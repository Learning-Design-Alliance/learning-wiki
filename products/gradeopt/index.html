GradeOpt¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

GradeOpt is a multi-agent LLM coding framework developed by Copur-Gencturk et al. that automates rubric-based coding of open-ended responses through Grader, Reflector, and Refiner agents.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The multi-agent LLM GradeOpt achieved substantial agreement with human coders on overall PCK items (κ = .68, QWK = .79) and outperformed RoBERTa, SBERT, and naïve GPT-4o prompting [+M]
  • On CK items, GradeOpt showed near-perfect alignment with human codes (κ = .88 and .85; QWK = .89 and .94), comparable to human–human agreement [+M]
  • Gaps between Cohen's κ and QWK indicate GradeOpt often assigned codes adjacent to human codes rather than entirely discrepant ones [~W]
  • All automated models, including GradeOpt, were most challenged by knowledge of teaching mathematics items (GradeOpt κ = .59) and least challenged by knowledge of students' mathematical thinking (κ = .79) [~W]
  • Grading recipes without calibration examples perform substantially worse on MAE, though multi-step without examples ranks second on QWK [~W]
