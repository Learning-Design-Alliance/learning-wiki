# WikiToLearn

## Description
WikiToLearn is an open-source Wikimedia-backed web platform run by its contributor community for creating, organizing, sharing, reusing, and compiling multilingual training materials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WikiToLearn collaborative web platform for shared training material**: WikiToLearn is an Open Source project backed by Wikimedia, implemented by a group of more than 30 Italian students. "It hosts training material in several languages, for several disciplines, ranging from Economics to Physics, Math- ematics, and several others." Because it is based on wikimedia software, material is easy to add and organise under topics, and students can selectively choose chapters and receive a compiled PDF book with index and chapters. The GridKa School of Computing's 2017 material was published on it as an example. (Dario Berzano et al. (2018))
