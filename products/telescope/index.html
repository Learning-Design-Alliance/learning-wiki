TELESCOPE¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TELESCOPE is a telehealth-based lung-cancer-screening shared-decision-making coaching and patient-navigation programme developed and delivered by trained patient navigators for primary-care patients.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Cancer-related fear and fatalism shape lung cancer screening decisions, and Hispanic adults preferred balanced risk communication that minimizes fear [+W]
  • Hispanic adults valued culturally relevant visual design — bright colors, Hispanic representation, and realistic imagery — in lung cancer screening educational materials [+W]
  • Hispanic adults preferred clear, friendly, direct, and nonjudgmental communication for lung cancer screening shared decision-making [+W]
