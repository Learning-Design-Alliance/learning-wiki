# GEA (Gioco di Educazione Alimentare)

## Description
A food-education mobile web application by Francesco Vona et al. that provides four game-based nutrition activities for individuals with neurodevelopmental disorders on tablets or in Google Cardboard virtual reality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GEA: a dual-interface food-education web application with four games, usable in VR (Google Cardboard HMD) or on a tablet**: GEA ("Gioco di Educazione Alimentare" - Food Education Game) is described as "an immersive virtual reality mobile application running on smartphones and tablets and usable in virtual reality on Google Cardboard that aims to teach food education to individuals with NDD." It was codesigned with therapists and specialists and teaches simple nutrition concepts: when a food can be eaten, which foods are healthier, and which allergens a food contains. Its games are Learn with the pyramid, Healthy or not?, Let's eat!, and Find the Allergens, with three difficulty levels; the VR version uses gaze-based interaction while the tablet version uses tap gestures. (Francesco Vona et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Playing GEA significantly improved food-education knowledge in individuals with NDD, with an average post-questionnaire improvement of 46%](../claims/gea-46-percent-food-education-gain-ndd.md) [+M]
