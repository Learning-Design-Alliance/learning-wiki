Curriculum-Based Professional Learning (CBPL) Questionnaire¶

Description¶

The Curriculum-Based Professional Learning (CBPL) Questionnaire is a survey instrument for districts and schools to characterize professional-learning structures, CBPL features, and implementation of high-quality instructional materials; its maker is not identified in the page description.

Components¶

  • Professional learning questionnaire characterizing curriculum-based professional learning across districts and schools: A questionnaire gathering information on professional learning in each district and its schools, grounded in a definition of curriculum-based professional learning (CBPL) as learning that supports implementation and sustained use of specific curriculum with integrity to the design while adapting to student needs. It includes four sections: basic district facts including built-in professional learning hours; types of professional learning structures such as PLCs and summer institutes with average monthly time; alignment with "11 CBPL features, including whether it is individualized or student focused"; and ratings of CBPL and HQIM implementation for each school.
