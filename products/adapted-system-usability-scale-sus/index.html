Adapted System Usability Scale (SUS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 design, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A ten-item, five-point Likert self-report instrument adapted from Brooke's System Usability Scale to measure users' perceived usability of an interactive learning environment.

Components¶

  • Adapted System Usability Scale (SUS) administered after learning to assess the learning environment: The variable table lists an "Adapted System Usability Scale, SUS" based on Brooke (1996), consisting of "10 items, rated on a 5-point Likert scale" collected post learning. It was used to capture participants' usability ratings of the interactive learning environment, complementing the 9-point Likert ratings of mental effort, concentration, interest, comprehension and difficulties related to the learning material. (SIGNALING IN MULTIMEDIA LEARNING: THE INFLUENCE OF PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (2007))

Claims¶

  • Adults rated the LLM-powered iCub storytelling system as usable, with a total SUS score of 76.70 indicating good product usability [+W]
  • The FACTRIA-aware chatbot received good usability ratings (SUS 70.84) and high usefulness ratings from most of the 11 stakeholders [+W]
  • After design adjustments, the LAD achieved good–excellent usability (SUS M = 4.13, equal to 78.25%) [+W]
  • Experimental-group participants rated TibetCPR highly usable (SUS = 84.3, an Excellent rating), with the highest items on voluntary engagement and independent use [+W]
