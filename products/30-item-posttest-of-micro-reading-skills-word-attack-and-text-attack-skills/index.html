30-item posttest of micro reading skills (word attack and text attack skills)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A researcher-developed 30-item posttest measuring university students’ academic content-area reading comprehension through word-attack and text-attack skills, expert validated and tried out before administration.

Components¶

  • 30-item posttest of micro reading skills (word attack and text attack skills) with expert validation and tryout: The study measured academic content-area reading comprehension with a reading comprehension test developed by the authors, expert validated and tried out before administration. "There were 30 items in the posttest measuring students' reading comprehension in terms of word attack skills and text attack skills", operationalizing comprehension as decoding meaning through context clues, denotation and connotation, topic of a paragraph, main idea and supporting details, and factual information. English proficiency was measured separately via a TOEFL PBT Equivalent test administered by an English institute in Malang. (Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo (2018))

Claims¶

  • Students taught with the PRWR reading strategy achieve better academic content-area reading comprehension than students taught with Translation and Reading Aloud [+M]
