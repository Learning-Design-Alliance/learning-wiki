# KaziKidz school-based physical activity program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
KaziKidz is a structured physical-activity teaching-material programme for primary schools, developed for the KaziAfya context and delivered by trained physical-education instructors supporting classroom teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KaziKidz toolkit: curriculum-based physical education lesson materials for South African primary schools**: The KaziKidz toolkit provided the lesson content for the PA intervention in the trial. The article states the lessons "were based on the KaziKidz toolkit, which was developed according to the South African curriculum in 2018", comprising one "moving to music" and one "physical education" lesson per week, delivered by a physical education coach assisting the class teacher. (Beckmann Johanna et al. (2022))

### Claims
