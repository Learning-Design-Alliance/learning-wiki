# Gen-Equip online genetics e-learning modules

## Description
Gen-Equip is a multilingual set of interactive online genetics modules and supporting resources for primary care professionals, developed by European partner organisations and hosted through Plymouth University's Open Digital Learning Environment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gen-Equip online genetics e-learning modules for primary care**: Gen-Equip comprises nine free online modules in six languages, developed by partners in six European countries for primary care practitioners in medicine, nursing, midwifery, and allied health. The modules were "based on cases that would typically be seen in primary care and were highly interactive, including videos, quizzes, and web links to external resources." Supporting resources include recorded 20-30 minute webinars, referral guidelines, family history taking tools, and short videos on explaining genetic concepts. Modules were accredited by the RCGP and European Accreditation Network. (Jackson L et al. (2018))

### Claims
- [Gen-Equip e-learning modules significantly improve primary care professionals' pre/post knowledge and skills test scores (overall 66.7% to 89.9%, p < 0.001)](../claims/gen-equip-prepost-knowledge-skills-gain.md) [+M]
- [Primary care professionals report high satisfaction with and relevance of the Gen-Equip resources (73.8% highly/very relevant to own practice; 82.5% would recommend)](../claims/gen-equip-satisfaction-relevance.md) [+M]
