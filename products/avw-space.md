# AVW-Space

## Description
AVW-Space is a digital platform developed by Vania Dimitrova and colleagues for teacher-curated video learning with timestamped comments, micro-scaffolds, and moderated social reflection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AVW-Space active video watching platform**: AVW-Space is a controlled video watching learning environment designed for self-study, in which teachers select publicly available videos and define aspects that serve as micro-scaffolds for learning. It comprises a Private Space, where students individually watch videos and write comments linked to a video timestamp and an aspect, and a Social Space, where teacher-approved anonymised comments can be browsed and rated using reflection-promoting categories. The article states: "AVW-Space fosters active learning via interactive note taking and by providing micro-scaffolds for reflection", and students can also rate comments written by others, providing opportunities for social learning. The instantiation for pitch presentations included eight YouTube videos (four tutorials and four example pitch presentations). (Vania Dimitrova et al. (2021))
