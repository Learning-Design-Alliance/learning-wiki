# Parents and Children Making Connections: Highlighting Attention (PCMC-A)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A family-based attention and self-regulation intervention developed by Pakulak et al. for lower-SES Head Start preschool children and their parents, combining child small-group activities with parent training and home-practice support calls.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Parents and Children Making Connections: Highlighting Attention (PCMC-A)**: PCMC-A is an eight-week, family-based training program with a child-directed component and a family-based, parent-directed component. The child component used "a set of 20 small group activities (4-6 children: 2 adults)" targeting vigilance, selective attention, task switching, and self-regulation of attention and emotion. The parent component adapted the evidence-based LIFT curriculum into "a scaffolded set of 25 strategies delivered in small group format," supplemented by weekly support calls confirming home-practice implementation. (Pakulak et al. (2013))

### Claims
- [PCMC-A produces significantly greater increases in ERP early attentional modulation (100-200 ms) than both HS-alone and ABC comparison groups in lower SES preschoolers](../claims/pcmc-a-erp-attentional-modulation-gains.md) [+M]
- [PCMC-A favored children over both control groups on standardized cognition and parent-reported behavior, with effect sizes from one-quarter to one-half a standard deviation](../claims/pcmc-a-cognition-behavior-gains-quarter-half-sd.md) [+M]
