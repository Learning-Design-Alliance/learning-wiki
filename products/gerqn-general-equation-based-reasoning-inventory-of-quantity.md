## Description
GERQN is a 17-item algebra-based physics quantitative-literacy inventory developed by Charlotte Zimmerman and colleagues for students in introductory algebra-based physics courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GERQN: a validated algebra-based inventory of physics quantitative literacy**: The GERQN (General Equation-based Reasoning inventory of QuaNtity) is an assessment instrument for physics quantitative literacy in algebra-based courses. The article states it is "an algebra-based version of the PIQL and is appropriate for most physics students". It was developed through initial review, iterative review, and validation stages involving student and expert interviews plus test administrations, and has 17 items taking about 30 minutes. (Charlotte Zimmerman et al. (2025))
