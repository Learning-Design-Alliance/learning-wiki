## Description
The DCCS program uses a working-alliance and time, structure, and accountability framework to organize weekly collaboration between ESL and classroom teachers of English learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DCCS collaboration framework: working alliance plus time, structure, and accountability**: The collaboration framework introduced in the DCCS program consists of two critical components: a working alliance, which addresses process characteristics such as effective communication, positive regard, and reciprocal leadership, and time, structure, and accountability (TSA), which covers time management, group norms and roles, and accountability measures. Teachers use it in weekly meetings to align content and language objectives, plan strategy implementation across ESL and regular classrooms, and review the previous week's plan. The model allows flexibility in teachers' choice of strategies to promote sustained use after developer support ends. (Leslie M. Babinski et al. (2018))
