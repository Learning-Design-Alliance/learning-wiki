# Digital Promise AI Literacy Brief Series

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A series of Digital Promise implementation briefs that provides audience-specific AI literacy recommendations, strategies, examples, and equity considerations for PK-12, postsecondary, workforce, and informal learning settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Digital Promise brief series on implementing AI literacy for four audiences**: Digital Promise developed a series of briefs with AI literacy recommendations for PK-12 education, postsecondary education, the workforce, and informal learning environments (the last noted as coming soon). The briefs "offer concrete strategies, implementation examples, and contextual considerations designed to help leaders make informed decisions that advance both innovation and equity in their communities." The series was developed with a technical working group, educator and student listening sessions, and an AI usage disclosure describing limited, human-led AI assistance. (Ruiz et al. (2025))
- **AI literacy for educators as competency in how AI works, its data collection, and data use**: The brief defines educator AI literacy as "general competency around how AI works, the types of data it collects, and how that data can be used." This framing organizes its recommendations: understanding system effectiveness and biases, data privacy, and ethical selection and use of AI systems in classrooms. (Jackson et al. (2021))
