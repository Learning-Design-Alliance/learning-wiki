## Description
A behavior-analytic training package used by trainers to teach practitioners accurate performance through instruction, modeling, rehearsal, and feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Behavioral skills training as a four-component training package**: Behavioral skills training (BST) is a training package that "includes the four training methods of feedback, instruction, modeling, and rehearsal delivered together and/or sequentially." The article defines each component operationally: feedback is performance feedback from an observer, instruction is verbal, digital, or print information, modeling is a live or video demonstration, and rehearsal is structured role-play with a trainer in the presence of a trainer. The meta-analysis uses these dummy-coded components as moderators of DTT implementation fidelity gains. (Joelle Fingerhut et al. (2022))
