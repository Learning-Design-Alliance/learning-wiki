## Description
A validated self-report assessment developed to measure cognitive, affective, and state empathy, including responses to scenario-based targets.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cognitive and affective empathy as distinct components of state empathy**: The article organises empathy around two components and a trait-state distinction. "Cognitive empathy refers to the accurate perception of another person’s perspective or mental state (feeling into), whereas affective empathy involves an ability to experience actual feelings or emotions that are consistent with another person". It also distinguishes trait empathy, a psychological disposition, from state empathy, an empathetic response at a specific point in time, and argues trait scales are unsuitable for short pre-test post-test studies. The Comprehensive State Empathy Scale operationalises this framework, measuring cognitive, affective and state empathy, and is used as the study’s outcome measure. (Donnelly S et al. (2019))
