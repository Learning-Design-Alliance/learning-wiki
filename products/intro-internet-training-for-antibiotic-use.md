## Description
A self-directed multilingual web-based training programme developed within the European GRACE project to improve general practitioners’ communication about antibiotic prescribing and use of CRP testing for lower respiratory tract infections.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **INTRO (INternet TRaining for antibiOtic use) web-based intervention**: INTRO is a self-directed web-based training programme developed within the European GRACE project to reduce inappropriate antibiotic prescribing for lower respiratory tract infections in primary care. The article states "INTRO has three main sections; an introduction, communication skills training and CRP training": the introduction covers the need for reduced prescribing, the communication training uses videos and a patient booklet, and the CRP training introduces and trains GPs in the CRP near-patient test. It was produced in five language versions with identical content. (Anthierens Sibyl et al. (2012))
