# MWU! (Astrophotography of the Multi-Wavelength Universe!)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A DoD-funded curriculum developed by Daniel E. Reichart and collaborating educators that provides eight Skynet-based optical and radio astronomy observing experiences for undergraduate astronomy courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MWU!: eight multi-wavelength observing modules adapting ERIRA for scalable classroom use**: MWU! ("Astrophotography of the Multi-Wavelength Universe!") is a DoD-funded curriculum of "eight new observing experiences (three optical, three radio, and two capstone experiences involving both)" based on ERIRA's manual observing experiences but delivered through Skynet so they can serve orders of magnitude more students than a field experience. The modules reinforce next-level astronomy course content on stars, galaxies, and their multi-wavelength emission and absorption mechanisms, and are being co-produced and trialed by 26 participating educators in two weekly meeting groups, with a deep evaluation of effectiveness planned alongside national scaling. (Daniel E. Reichart (2021))
