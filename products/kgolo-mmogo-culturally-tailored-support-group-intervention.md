# Kgolo Mmogo culturally tailored support group intervention

## Description
A six-month, culturally tailored support-group programme developed by Eloff and colleagues for HIV-positive mothers and their 6–10-year-old children in South African communities, delivered through separate and joint weekly sessions with trained, supervised community care workers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kgolo Mmogo culturally tailored support group intervention and open-access manual**: The Kgolo Mmogo intervention is a six-month, theory-based support group programme in which HIV-positive mothers and their 6–10-year-old children attend weekly small-group sessions. "The mothers and children attended 14 separate sessions and 10 joint sessions." The child sessions followed "a manual that was culturally tailored and focused on coping, problem -solving, emotional intelligence, social skills , and a positive future orientation", while mother sessions focused on coping with the HIV diagnosis, parenting skills and child development. The manual is "an open access resource" available online, and groups were facilitated by trained community care workers receiving weekly supervision from a psychologist and social worker. (Irma Eloff et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [In a one-group pre-test/post-test study, CBCL scores for children’s withdrawn, social, attention, rule-breaking and aggressive behaviour problems decreased significantly from baseline to six months after a 24-week support group intervention](../claims/support-group-intervention-decreases-cbcl-behaviour-problems.md) [+M]
- [The majority of mothers (95%) reported that parenting became easier after the support group sessions, citing improved communication, patience, support and discipline](../claims/mothers-report-easier-parenting-after-support-groups.md) [+M]
