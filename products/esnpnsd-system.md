## Description
A nursing education support system that integrates patient-care documentation, experience sharing, and curated educational resources for clinical-internship learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The ESNPNSD is structured into three primary functional components**: After consolidating overlapping items identified by health informatics experts, the final ESNPNSD system was organized into three primary components representing its key functionalities. The first, patient-related processes, includes "modules for primary assessment, triage, order checking, charting drugs in Kardex, and nursing report generation"; the second documents and shares useful experiences gained during patient care; and the third provides curated educational files and content. The pattern packages practice-oriented documentation tasks with experiential and resource components in a single platform. (Abedi Ali et al. (2025))
