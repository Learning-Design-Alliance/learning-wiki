## Description
The Institute of Physics New Quantum Curriculum is a set of online texts, interactive simulations, and guided activities for introductory university quantum mechanics beginning with two-level systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Institute of Physics New Quantum Curriculum simulations and activities for introductory quantum mechanics starting from two-level systems**: The New Quantum Curriculum (quantumphysics.iop.org) is a set of "online texts and interactive simulations with accompanying activities for an introductory course in quantum mechanics starting from two-level systems". One author designed 17 interactive simulations and activities covering linear algebra, fundamental quantum mechanics concepts, single photon interference, the Bloch sphere representation, entanglement, local hidden variables and quantum information. The article describes trialing and refining these resources with students. (Antje Kohnle et al. (2013))
