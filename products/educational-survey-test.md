## Description
A 30-minute composite elementary-school achievement test assembled from eight standard educational tests by Pintner and colleagues to measure attainment across major curriculum subjects using age and grade norms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Pintner-Fitzgerald Educational Survey Test**: A 30-minute group test booklet covering eight subject areas: vocabulary (Thorndike), arithmetic (Woody-Stone), two reading tests (Kelly and Thorndike), completion/language (Trabue), punctuation-grammar (Starch), geography (Hahn-Lackey) and history (Van Wagenen). Items were drawn from the Standard Educational Tests and Measurements "at approximately equal intervals" of difficulty. Timed subtests total twenty-five and one-half minutes, with about thirty minutes for the full administration, and scoring uses stencils and keys with subject weights summing to a highest possible score of 141. (Rudolf Pintner et al. (1918))
