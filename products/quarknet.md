## Description
QuarkNet is a long-term teacher professional-development and particle-physics outreach network run through collaborations among educators, physicist mentors, research institutions, and partner programmes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QuarkNet, a teacher-centered professional development and outreach network for particle physics**: QuarkNet, begun in 1999, is described as "the premier education program for broader impacts in particle physics in the United States." Its focus is teacher professional development: teachers receive development opportunities from staff and mentors and then "provide learning opportunities for their students," giving the program long-term reach. It operates internationally through CERN teacher programs, International Masterclasses, cosmic ray studies, World Wide Data Day, and collaborations with programs such as Netzwerk Teilchenwelt. (Arce-Larreta et al. (2021))
