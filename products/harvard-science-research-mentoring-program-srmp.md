## Description
A Harvard Center for Astrophysics programme that pairs high-school students with graduate-student and postdoctoral scientist mentors for independent astrophysics research and concludes with a public symposium.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Year-long science research mentoring program pattern pairing high-school students with early-career scientists**: The Harvard SRMP is a program pattern in which high-school students work on independent astrophysics research projects with graduate-student and postdoc mentors from the first week of September to the last week of May. Students receive "a very general introduction to astrophysics" in September, are assigned to ranked projects, then meet mentors "twice a week at the CfA, each time for two hours," aiming for roughly 100 contact hours. The pattern serves both student research exposure and mentor professional development, and concludes with a poster-and-talk symposium. (Or Graur (2020))
