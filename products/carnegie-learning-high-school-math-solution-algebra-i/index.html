Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Carnegie Learning first-year Algebra I curriculum that combines textbook and workbook materials with self-paced individualized instruction through automated tutoring software.

Components¶

  • Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I curriculum: A first-year algebra course designed for students ranging in ability and grade levels. The curriculum "combines traditional textbook and workbook materials with self-paced individualized instruction via automated tutoring software." It was evaluated in three qualifying studies with 20,485 students and carries a strong evidence rating with a weighted effect size of +0.04. (Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I (2014))
  • MATHia tutoring software: MATHia is "the technology component of Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution", described as "a web technology that runs on desktop/laptop computers as well as Chromebooks and iPad, Android, and Windows tablets." It delivers the self-paced individualized instruction via automated tutoring software that the blended curriculum combines with textbook and workbook materials. (Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I (2014))

Claims¶

  • In a cluster randomized study across 73 high schools, Carnegie Learning students scored higher on the Algebra Proficiency Exam, with cohort effect sizes of +0.07 and +0.21 [+W]
  • Algebra ability level differences appear on both criteria with no treatment-by-ability interaction [~W]
