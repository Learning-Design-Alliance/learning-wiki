## Description
A modular undergraduate data-science curriculum and coordinated cocurricular programme developed by Dill-McFarland and colleagues for progressive competency development among life-science students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EDUCE: a progressive, extensible framework building data science competency across several years of integrated practice**: EDUCE (Experiential Data science for Undergraduate Cross-Disciplinary Education) is a teaching and learning framework that equips undergraduate life-science students with core data science competency through modules integrated into required and elective courses plus coordinated cocurricular activities. Its focal objectives ask students to "explore and manipulate data", "visualize data in tables and figures", and "apply and interpret statistical tests". The framework rests on three components: "a modular curriculum plugged into existing courses; coordinated cocurricular activities; and a cross-disciplinary community of practice", allowing progressive competency development over several years without additional course requirements. (Dill-McFarland KA et al. (2021))
