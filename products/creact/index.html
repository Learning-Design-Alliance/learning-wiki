CREACT¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

CREACT is a five-step discussion-based teaching framework for creative thinking, developed by Sak and usable across lessons and topics.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Twenty-three 5th graders' creativity scores rise after nine CREACT drawing-arts lessons in a one-group pretest-posttest study without a control group (effect size .71) [+M]
  • CREACT groups outperformed control groups on all four TTCT subcomponents (fluency, flexibility, originality, elaboration) [+M]
