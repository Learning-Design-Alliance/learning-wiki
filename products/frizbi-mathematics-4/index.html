Frizbi Mathematics 4¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Frizbi Mathematics 4 is educational mathematics software for fourth-grade primary students covering multiplication, division, and fractions.

Components¶

  • Frizbi Mathematics 4 educational software: Frizbi Mathematics 4 is an educational software package for fourth-grade mathematics that was used as the experimental treatment in this study, covering three units: Multiplication of Natural Numbers, Division of Natural Numbers, and Fractions. The article describes it as "an effective tool" for "learning and teaching mathematics at the primary school level in North Cyprus." It was compared against lecture-based traditional instruction in a two-group quasi-experimental design. (Pilli et al. (2013))

Claims¶

  • Computer-assisted instruction with Frizbi Mathematics 4 improved fourth-grade mathematics achievement more than traditional lecture-based instruction [+W]
  • Retention gains from computer-assisted instruction held for multiplication and division units but not for fractions [+W]
  • Computer-assisted instruction improved attitudes toward mathematics and toward computer-assisted learning [+W]
