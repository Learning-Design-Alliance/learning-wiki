# Generalized Intelligent Framework for Tutoring (GIFT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GIFT is a software framework for authoring, integrating, and adapting computer-based tutoring and training environments, developed and maintained by the GIFT community.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GIFT Gateway Module for real-time persistence assessment across game-based learning environments**: The Generalized Intelligent Framework for Tutoring (GIFT) supports open and dynamic game-based learning environments with distinct messaging protocols. To detect persistence regardless of the educational game used, "a 'Gateway Module ' is incorporated that associates an external educational/training system 's state data with a domain or competency model built within GIFT." This linkage lets disparate systems communicate, enabling real-time assessment of persistence in players, with progress communicated to the learner model for determining transitions in performance or competency and to the pedagogical model for selecting feedback and remediation tactics. (Ventura et al. (2013))
- **GIFT: a generalized framework for building intelligent tutoring systems with authoring, instructional management, and evaluation functions**: GIFT is a research prototype ITS framework sponsored by the US Army Research Laboratory, described as a 'generalized framework for building ITSs' in the shell-tutor tradition. It ties together models of learners, instruction, and knowledge/skill domains through three functions: authoring, instructional management, and evaluation. Its stated design goals are to lower authoring skills/time, deliver adaptive instruction tailored to individual learners or teams, and provide tools to evaluate ITS effectiveness. The framework is grounded in learning, tutoring, and motivational theory and is released as free software. (Robert A. Sottilare et al. (2017))
- **GIFT authoring tools with standard gateway for external environments and drag-and-drop course objects**: GIFT's authoring function provides policies, tools, and methods enabling authors (instructional designers, developers, instructors, course managers, and subject matter experts) to create ITSs without programming knowledge, curate content across cognitive, affective, psychomotor, and social domains, and sequence it by hierarchical or dependency relationships. A 'standard gateway specification for interacting with all types of external environments' lets course objects interface with games, simulations, applications, and sensors, and course objects 'require no programming and may be added to a GIFT-based tutor through a simple drag and drop.' GIFT is also LTI compliant for MOOC delivery platforms. (Robert A. Sottilare et al. (2017))
