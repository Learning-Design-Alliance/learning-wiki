# Personal Assistant for Learning (PAL)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computer-based tutoring program that coaches learners through reciprocal-teaching and metacognitive problem-solving strategies, developed for educational use by Alexandru Maries and colleagues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PAL computer program and University of Minnesota computer coaches for metacognitive problem-solving**: The Personal Assistant for Learning (PAL) is a computer program that "made use of the reciprocal teaching method" to coach students through problem-solving strategies, then reverses roles so students make decisions assessed by the computer. In a three-group homework study matched for prior performance, PAL and human tutor groups performed similarly and significantly better than the no-guidance control. The University of Minnesota group now maintains computer coaches based on PAL, designed to "help students develop metacognitive problem-solving skills", recommended as required homework components. (Alexandru Maries et al. (2024))
