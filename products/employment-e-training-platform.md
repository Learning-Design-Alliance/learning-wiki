## Description
An online vocational e-training platform proposed and maintained by Taiwan's Council of Labor Affairs, providing subject units with practice, tests, information resources, and feedback for workplace-oriented learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Employment e-Training Platform transformer learning unit**: The Employment e-Training Platform, proposed by the Council of Labor Affairs in Taiwan, hosts the learning unit transformer, which the study used as a complement to the electrical machinery course. The article states the platform "focuses on the subjects of electricity, electronics , and food and beverage service," and that students logged in to access the transformer unit covering "its principles, structures, characteristics, connections, tests, and maintenance." Online practice and tests were available for each unit, enabling repeated practice, information collection, and feedback. (Chang et al. (2014))
