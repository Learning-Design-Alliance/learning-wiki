# Hands-On Astrophysics (HOA) and Variable Star Astronomy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Educational resource programmes created by John R. Percy, Janet Mattei, and Donna Young and hosted and disseminated by the American Association of Variable Star Observers (AAVSO) for teaching variable stars, mathematics, science, computing, and data analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hands-On Astrophysics and its successor Variable Star Astronomy educational resources**: In the 1990s, with National Science Foundation support, the author and Janet Mattei created Hands-On Astrophysics (HOA), an educational program using variable stars in math, science, and computer education. With colleague Donna Young, HOA later "morphed into Variable Star Astronomy", hosted at www.aavso.org/vsa. Education remains one of the AAVSO's highest priorities, with additional resources and a list of suggested student observation projects on its website. (John R. Percy (2016))
