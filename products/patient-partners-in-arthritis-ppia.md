## Description
A centrally coordinated standardized programme that trains volunteer patient educators with arthritis to teach and evaluate musculoskeletal clinical-examination skills in medical education, operated nationally through participating medical schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Patient Partners in Arthritis (PP-IA) standardized patient educator program**: The article describes the intervention program: "The MSK specific patient educator program, Patient Partners® in Arthritis (PP®IA), is a centrally coordinated, standardized national program that trains volunteer patient educators with arthritis to teach and evaluate MSK clinical examination skills." Patient educators undergo 100 hours of training. Introduced in Canada in 1995, the program is active at thirteen of the seventeen Canadian medical schools at undergraduate, postgraduate and/or continuing medical education levels, and intra- and inter-rater reliability of its trained educators in OSCE checklist assessments has been demonstrated. (Oswald Anna E et al. (2011))
