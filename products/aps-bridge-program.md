## Description
A national programme run by the American Physical Society's Department of Education and Diversity that supports institutions in preparing underrepresented minority physics bachelor's degree holders for admission to and completion of physics PhD programmes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **APS Bridge Program and its national network of institutions**: The APS Bridge Program is a national effort pioneered by the APS Department of Education and Diversity to increase physics PhDs awarded to underrepresented minority students. It 'has established six bridge sites in Indiana, Ohio, Florida, and California' and counts '93 Member Institutions in 36 states and 19 Partnership Institutions in 14 different states.' (Brian Beckford (2017))

### Claims
- [The APS Bridge Program has placed over 150 students into Bridge or graduate physics programs, with placements comprising 93% URM and 23% female students](../claims/aps-bridge-program-placement-outcomes-93-urm.md) [+M]
