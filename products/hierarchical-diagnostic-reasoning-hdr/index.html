Hierarchical Diagnostic Reasoning (HDR)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A structured case-based assessment proposed by Takahashi et al. that embeds intentional knowledge misuses and requires learners to identify and explain the errors to diagnose applied cognitive skills.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • ChatGPT-generated HDR problems measure the same ability as human-created problems, with high internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.78) and no significant difficulty difference [+M]
  • HDR scores correlate only weakly with reading comprehension (α = 0.36 and 0.41), indicating the task measures practical application ability rather than reading skill [+M]
  • Automated HDR problem generation makes repeated and longitudinal assessment feasible by producing structurally equivalent cases with different contexts [+W]
