SoS classroom fidelity observation tool¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A researcher-created 13-item, four-point classroom observation instrument used to assess fidelity of implementation of the SoS integrated STEM curriculum from video recordings.

Components¶

  • Project-created classroom fidelity observation tool scored on a 4-point scale from video recordings: The researchers built an observation instrument to measure the extent to which teachers and students experienced the SoS curriculum activities as intended. "The tool included 13 items representing implementation indicators on 4 critical component areas created by the researchers and based on Century et al.'s [30] framework." Fidelity was scored 0 (no evidence) to 3 (high evidence) from classroom video recordings, with inter-rater reliability reported using Krippendorff's alpha. (Sibuma et al. (2018))
