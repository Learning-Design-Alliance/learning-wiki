## Description
X-Ray Pro VR Suite is an immersive virtual-reality platform made by Virtual Medical Coaching for practising diagnostic-radiography procedures and decision-making in interactive clinical scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **X-Ray Pro VR Suite by Virtual Medical Coaching for diagnostic radiography training**: The X-Ray Pro VR Suite is the immersive virtual reality training platform provided by Virtual Medical Coaching that the VR-trained cohort used in this study. It offers dynamic, interactive clinical environments in which learners practise radiographic tasks, observe how kVp and mAs affect image quality, and receive immediate feedback within scenario-based training. The study used it to deliver the same clinical scenarios and total simulation time as traditional physical simulation for 40 radiographers. (Karimi Hossein et al. (2025))
