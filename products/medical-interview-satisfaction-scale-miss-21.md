## Description
The MISS-21 is a 21-item visit-based questionnaire developed for measuring patient satisfaction with primary-care consultations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MISS-21 questionnaire as the written patient satisfaction feedback instrument and primary outcome measure**: The 21-item Medical Interview Satisfaction Scale (MISS-21) serves both as the educational intervention's feedback content and as the trial's primary outcome measure. The article describes it as "a widely available 21-item validated visit-based questionnaire for measuring patient satisfaction in the primary care context", chosen for ease of administration, freedom from cost- or facility-based questions, and reported validity and reliability. An independent researcher interviews each patient teaching associate after each consultation to obtain the scores that intervention-group students later receive in writing. (Lai Michelle Mei Yee et al. (2014))
