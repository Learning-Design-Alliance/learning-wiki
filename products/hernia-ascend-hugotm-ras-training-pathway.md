## Description
A modular proficiency-based training curriculum for surgeons learning robotic transabdominal preperitoneal hernia repair, developed by Brucchi et al. for Hugo™ robotic-assisted surgery.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The ASCEND framework: a Proficiency-Based Progression training pathway for robotic hernia surgery in which procedure-relevant errors are explicitly defined and systematically avoided before progression**: The Hernia ASCEND Hugo™ RAS training pathway is a structured robotic hernia curriculum grounded in the Proficiency-Based Progression model. As the authors describe it, it is "founded on the Proficiency-Based Progression (PBP) model", in which "errors relevant to robotic hernia surgery (e.g., mesh malposition, vascular injury, or nerve entrapment) are explicitly defined and systematically avoided before progression". Learners advance by meeting defined proficiency milestones rather than by time served, and the pathway is described as modular and scalable across healthcare settings. (Brucchi F et al. (2025))
