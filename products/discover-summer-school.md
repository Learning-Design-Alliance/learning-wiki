## Description
A four-week summer literacy transition programme using writing and poetry workshops, enrichment activities, and the book The Unforgotten Coat for pupils moving from Year 6 to Year 7, delivered by trained teachers, authors, poets, Story Builders, and volunteers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Discover Summer School four-week literacy transition programme**: A four-week summer programme for children transitioning from Year 6 to Year 7, targeted at pupils predicted to achieve below Level 4b in English at Key Stage 2. It included "morning workshops focused on writing and poetry delivered by trained teachers, professional authors and poets, and enrichment activities" such as drama, sports and trips around London. Delivery followed a usual school week (9am-3pm, Monday to Friday) by trained 'Story Builders', teachers and volunteers, with free transport provided to encourage attendance. Teaching was framed around the book The Unforgotten Coat. (Torgerson et al. (2014))

### Claims
