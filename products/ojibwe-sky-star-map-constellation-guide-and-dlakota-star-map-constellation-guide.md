# Ojibwe Sky Star Map Constellation Guide and D(L)akota Star Map Constellation Guide

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two Native Skywatchers educational guidebooks that pair Ojibwe or D(L)akota constellations, teachings, artwork, and corresponding Western celestial objects for educators and community learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ojibwe and D(L)akota constellation guidebooks pairing native constellations with Western celestial objects**: In June 2014 two constellation guidebooks were published to accompany the star maps: Ojibwe Sky Star Map Constellation Guide and D(L)akota Star Map Constellation Guide. Each booklet contains "additional information about individual native constellations, selected teachings, and artwork," and highlights celestial objects from western European astronomical traditions in corresponding areas of the night sky. For example, on a fall evening a person might see the Ojibwe Mooz (Moose), the Dakota Keya (Turtle), the Greek constellation Pegasus, and the Andromeda Galaxy, M31. (Lee (2016))
