## Description
VR USGIVA 1.0 is an immersive virtual-reality training simulator developed by VitaSim for practicing ultrasound-guided peripheral venous catheter placement and tip tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR USGIVA 1.0 immersive virtual reality simulator for ultrasound-guided PVC placement training**: The VR USGIVA 1.0 simulator (VitaSim, Odense, Denmark) is an immersive virtual reality training environment for US-guided PVC placement, run on an HTC Vive system with head-mounted display and 2 controllers. "The purpose of the simulation was to give the participant practice with tip tracking and thereby improve competency in this specific skill." It contains 10 short virtual scenarios of increasing difficulty in which students place a PVC in a deep vein visible only via US, using a cross-sectional scanning plane and tip tracking, with an "evaluate position" button providing feedback on placement correctness. (Andersen NL et al. (2021))
