## Description
Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading is a comprehensive K–6 speech-to-print literacy curriculum developed and provided by Spalding Education International, integrating explicit instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, spelling, writing, and reading comprehension.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading: comprehensive K-6 speech-to-print literacy program**: A comprehensive literacy program for K-6 students implemented as a whole-class program, typically daily during dedicated literacy blocks. It "integrates explicit, systematic, and multi-modal instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, spelling, writing, and reading comprehension" and follows "a speech-to-print approach, helping students connect spoken language with written text through structured, diagnostic teaching methods." Its structured format is intended to support differentiated instruction for diverse learners, including struggling readers and English learners. (Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading (2024))

### Claims
- [Students tracked from grades 2-4 into grades 3-5 in Spalding schools scored significantly higher on the AASA (effect size +0.39), reaching 48% versus 29% proficiency](../claims/spalding-aasa-upper-elementary-gains.md) [+W]
- [Kindergarteners followed into 1st grade in Spalding schools showed higher FastBridge earlyReading gains (effect size +0.21) than matched comparison peers](../claims/spalding-fastbridge-earlyreading-kindergarten-gains.md) [+W]
- [Below-grade-level students in Spalding schools sustained significantly higher AASA performance through Spring 2025 (effect size +0.33), with twice the proficiency rate of comparison peers](../claims/spalding-struggling-readers-sustained-gains.md) [+W]
- [Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading improves whole-class literacy achievement with an average effect size of +0.35 across one study of 1,915 students](../claims/spalding-writing-road-to-reading-whole-class-gains.md) [+W]
