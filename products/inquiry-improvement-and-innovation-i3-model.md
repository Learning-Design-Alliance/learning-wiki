## Description
A framework developed by Hagle and colleagues to help clinicians and graduate nursing students select and apply evidence-based practice or quality-improvement, research, and innovation methodologies to improve patient care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Inquiry, Improvement, and Innovation (I3) Model for Advancing Quality Patient-Centered Care**: A model developed by Hagle and colleagues that the article uses as a guiding framework to "empower clinicians—across settings—to decide which method will best incorporate evidence or generate new solutions in their practice." The article explains that the I3 Model "includes professional activities for EBP and quality improvement (QI), research studies, and innovation to enhance nursing care" and "establishes the need to prepare graduate nurses to apply the 3 methodologies." In this study it justifies adding innovation alongside the QI and research methods usually taught in graduate nursing curricula, framing the I&HCD workshop as filling the commonly missing innovation component. (Holt JM et al. (2025))
