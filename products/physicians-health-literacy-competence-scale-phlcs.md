# Physician’s Health Literacy Competence Scale (PHLCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 20-item self-report scale developed by Chang C-C et al. to assess physicians’ health-literacy competence in clinical interaction scenarios, including trust-building, supportive communication, shared decision-making, and medication-adherence verification.
