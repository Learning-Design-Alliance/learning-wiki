## Description
Laparo Analytics is a sensor-equipped laparoscopic simulation and automated instrument-movement analysis system used by surgical learners and professionals for minimally invasive skills training and proficiency assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Laparo Analytics sensor-equipped laparoscopic simulator with automatic instrument movement analysis**: The course used Laparo Analytics laparoscopic simulators whose trocars measure instrument movements at 10 Hz and whose sensors and camera view analysis derive parameters including time, economy of movements, total distance, smoothness, acceleration, instrument activity, jolt and visibility, with scoring models based on expert recordings presented as weighted averages of components. The article states these simulators "possess a defined working area with a volume of 2.09 L" and log deviations beyond it as errors. The system served as an objective proficiency measurement intended to aid skill transfer to the operating theater. (Borselle D. et al. (2025))
