## Description
Through a research-practice partnership, the study links "student demographic data and initial English proficiency assessment data across seven cohorts of test takers" and observes "EL and SPED programmatic participation for these students over seven years." This linked longitudinal dataset is the evidentiary basis for the study's regression discontinuity and regression comparisons of EL status effects on special education placement.
