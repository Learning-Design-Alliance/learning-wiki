# Adobe Connect

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Adobe Connect is Adobe's web-conferencing and virtual-classroom software for delivering live online instruction with presentation, polling, chat, participant management, and related interaction features.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adobe Connect webinar platform with hidden answer windows, answer distribution display, and chat**: The webinars were developed with "Adobe Connect® software". The platform allowed hiding the answer window so residents could respond individually, showed the distribution of answers based on quality and percentage for multiple-choice questions, and provided a class transmission screen, open-question and multiple-choice panels, presenter camera, attendant list, and chat window. The article reports its use "was simple for teachers and residents". (Mayorga Eduardo P. et al. (2014))
