# Helping Mothers Survive Bleeding After Birth (HMS BAB) simulation-based training package

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Jhpiego and Laerdal Global Health simulation-based training package that prepares healthcare workers to provide basic delivery care and prevent and manage postpartum haemorrhage using the MamaNatalie simulator.

### Claims
- [Clinical performance of basic delivery skills (oxytocin administration, controlled cord traction, uterine massage) increased significantly after simulation-based training](../claims/basic-delivery-skills-improved-after-simulation-training.md) [+M]
- [Incidence of postpartum haemorrhage (500–1000 ml) fell from 2.1% to 1.3% after half-day simulation-based PPH training in one uncontrolled pre-post study, with a small effect size](../claims/simulation-training-reduced-pph-incidence-500-1000ml.md) [+M]
