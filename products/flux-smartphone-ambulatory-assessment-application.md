## Description
A dedicated smartphone application developed by the Technology Based Assessment Group at DIPF for the FLUX project to administer working-memory, sleep, and tiredness assessments to elementary-school children in daily-life settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FLUX smartphone ambulatory assessment application for children's daily sleep and working memory measurement**: A dedicated smartphone application developed by the Technology Based Assessment Group at the DIPF for the FLUX project ('Assessment of Cognitive Performance FLUctuations in the School ConteXt'). It was the only available function of the distributed Dell Streak 5 phones, with internet, calls, games, and the regular system menu blocked, and delivered numerical and spatial working memory updating tasks plus sleep and tiredness items at three fixed daily occasions in school and at home for 31 consecutive days. (Könen et al. (2015))
