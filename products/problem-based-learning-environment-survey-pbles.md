# Problem-Based Learning Environment Survey (PBLES)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A matched preferred/perceived learning-environment questionnaire developed by Yeh Ting-Kuang et al. for assessing Taiwanese nursing students' preferences for and perceptions of problem-based versus lecture-based learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Problem-Based Learning Environment Survey (PBLES) with matched Pr-LEI and Pc-LEI preference and perception instruments**: PBLES is a questionnaire assessing nursing students' perceptions of and preferences for the classroom learning environment, modified from the Classroom Environment Instruments. "PBLES consisted of two aspects: the Preferred Learning Environment Instrument (Pr-LEI) and the Perceived Learning Environment Instrument (Pc-LEI)", each divided into PBL and LBL dimensions with matched items scored on a five-point Likert scale. A pilot with 253 students yielded 36 items, four factors accounting for 45% of variance and Cronbach's α of 0.89 for the whole instrument. (Yeh Ting-Kuang et al. (2016))
