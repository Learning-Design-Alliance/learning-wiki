# E-learning Educational Atmosphere Measure (EEAM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 40-item self-report assessment developed by Mousavi et al. to measure virtual students' perceptions across programme effectiveness, teaching quality, ethics and professionalism, learner support, safety and convenience, and awareness of rules.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **E-learning Educational Atmosphere Measure (EEAM), a 40-item instrument covering six factors of the e-learning educational environment**: EEAM is an instrument developed by Mousavi et al. (2020), one of the eight studies included in the review, for assessing e-students' perception of the educational environment. An expert committee drafted 43 items, reviewed by further experts for face validity, and administered to 185 students studying virtual programmes. The review reports that "The final instrument, named as 'e-learning Educational Atmosphere Measure' (EEAM), included 40 questions that covered six factors consisting of programme effectiveness, teaching quality, ethics and professionalism, learner support, safety and convenience, and awareness of the rules." (Mutua Moses M. (2023))
