# Mixed-methods usability testing instrument toolkit for educational simulations

## Description
A modular assessment toolkit assembled by Lesselroth et al. that combines published usability, workload, satisfaction, observation, demographic, debriefing, and competency instruments for pre-pilot evaluation of educational simulations.
