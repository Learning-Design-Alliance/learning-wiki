## Description
LRS is an interactive classroom-response and formative-assessment system developed by Edge Hill University that provides pupils with immediate feedback and teachers with aggregated progress data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LRS theory of change: instant two-way formative feedback improves pace and quality of feedback**: The Edge Hill developers' logic model holds that timely, focused feedback facilitates learning: pupils respond to questions and are instantly told whether they erred, with suggestions for rectifying mistakes, while teachers receive aggregated feedback on each learner's progress, timing, attempts, and errors so they can adjust within the lesson and target struggling pupils. The report states "The LRS approach is based on the premise that timely and focused feedback is a powerful tool to facilitate learning". The trial tested this mechanism and found no measurable KS2 attainment benefit. (Meg Wiggins et al. (2017))
