# Instructional Conversation (IC) model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A CREDE-informed instructional intervention in which teachers receive professional development and ongoing coaching to implement instructional conversations, particularly for English-language learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Two-year IC intervention with professional development, coaching, and CREDE standards for pedagogical delivery**: The intervention element is the IC model implemented over a two-year period per cohort in a cluster randomized efficacy trial across 12 Georgia school districts. Experimental teachers "were first instructed in organizing classroom practices and in using the Center for Research on Education, Diversity & Excellence (CREDE) standards for pedagogical delivery." The program included a year of professional development with two full-day fall renewals, an initial intensive week of PD, and coaching feedback throughout the practice years, with both IC and control teachers monitored, assessed, required to complete logs, and videotaped during small group teaching lessons. (Portes et al. (2016))
