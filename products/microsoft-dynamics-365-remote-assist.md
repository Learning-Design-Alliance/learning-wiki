## Description
Microsoft Dynamics 365 Remote Assist is Microsoft's mixed-reality collaboration application for remote experts to view a user's perspective and provide real-time visual guidance through supported devices such as HoloLens, often alongside Microsoft Teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HoloLens 2 remote telementoring setup for procedural tutorials (Dynamics 365 Remote Assist with Microsoft Teams)**: A remote one-to-one tutorial configuration in which the student wears a Microsoft HoloLens 2 and the tutor connects via Microsoft Teams and Dynamics 365 Remote Assist over secure institutional Wi-Fi. The article states the device "provides the tutor with a first-person perspective of the student’s field of vision" and lets the tutor insert holographic artefacts such as pointers, diagrams, models and images. Costs included a €3500 device and €275 annual licence per user (n = 4), plus roughly 20 hours of development and training time. (Connolly Murray et al. (2026))
