# Weighted 15-item humeral intraosseous access performance checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 15-item, clinically weighted observational checklist developed by Ott Thomas et al. to assess medical students' humeral intraosseous access performance from videotaped tests using blinded dual-rater scoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Weighted 15-item checklist score (0–20) for assessing humeral intraosseous access performance**: A performance checklist developed by the authors because "there are no validated checklists for assessing humeral IOA"; they adapted an already validated tibial IOA score through five rounds of focus group sessions. "The resulting checklist consisted of 15 weighted items quantifying the performance of humeral IOA", with item weights of 1 to 3 assigned by clinical relevance, yielding a score from 0 (worst) to 20 (best). Two blinded raters evaluated videotaped tests and agreed on one score per videotape. (Ott Thomas et al. (2024))
