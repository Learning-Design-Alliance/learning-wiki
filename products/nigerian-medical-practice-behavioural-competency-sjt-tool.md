# Nigerian medical-practice behavioural-competency SJT tool

## Description
A 50-scenario ranked-response situational judgement test developed by University of Nigeria Teaching Hospital faculty to assess ten doctor–client and doctor–colleague behavioural competencies in clinical learners and resident doctors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nigerian medical-practice behavioural-competency SJT tool (50 ranked-response scenarios across ten competencies)**: A situational judgement test instrument developed by an expert panel of ten faculty subject-matter experts from five clinical departments at the University of Nigeria Teaching Hospital. It presents hypothetical work-related clinical dilemmas with five options that respondents rank from most to least appropriate. The tool covers ten competencies, five doctor-client (ethical responsibility, empathy, patient-centeredness, diligence, good judgment) and five doctor-colleague (respectful, teamwork, team leadership/conflict management, ability to take correction, tolerance), with "A total of 35 dilemmas were adapted, and 15 new ones were developed". It was administered via KoBo Toolbox on electronic tablets. (Obi Uche Shalom et al. (2024))
