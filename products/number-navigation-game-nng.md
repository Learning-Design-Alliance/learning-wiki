# Number Navigation Game (NNG)

## Description
A mathematical game-based learning environment developed by Phuong Bui et al. for primary students to practise flexible and adaptive whole-number arithmetic.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Number Navigation Game (NNG), an intrinsically integrated mathematics game for primary students**: NNG is a mathematical game-based learning environment for primary school students focusing on flexibility and adaptivity with whole-number arithmetic. Players navigate a ship on a 100-square map by entering arithmetic operations to collect materials and return to harbor, with moves and energy scoring modes. The article describes it as having "an 'intrinsically integrated' design (Habgood and Ainsworth 2011a, b) in which the core gaming mechanism is integrated directly to the educational content of the game". It evolved through four versions from 2D simple graphics to a 3D Unity3D version with extrinsic motivational features. (Phuong Bui et al. (2020))
