# WHO framework for integrating perinatal mental health into maternal and child health services

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A WHO service-delivery framework that organizes screening, first-level intervention, treatment, and specialist referral for perinatal mental health across primary, community, and specialist care providers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Stepped-care integration of perinatal mental health into maternal and child health services (WHO framework)**: The program used the WHO framework for integrating perinatal mental health into maternal and child health services, organizing care in tiers. "An algorithm was developed to screen, identify, and refer women with perinatal mental health conditions using a stepped-care approach with clear referral pathways." Frontline workers (Auxiliary Nurse Midwives) provide screening and first-level interventions, medical officers perform case identification and treatment, and specialist care is reserved for severe mental illness or suicidal risk. Training PCPs is positioned as the first systems-strengthening step within this framework. (Akkineni Raveena et al. (2025))
