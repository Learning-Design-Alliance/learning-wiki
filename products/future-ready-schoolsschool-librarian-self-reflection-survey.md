# Future Ready Schools—School Librarian Self-Reflection survey

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 5-point self-reflection survey developed by All4Ed to assess school librarians’ alignment with the Future Ready Schools—Librarian Framework; the study used a modified version suitable for administrators, teachers, and librarians.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Future Ready Schools—Librarian Self-Reflection survey instrument**: The study used a modified version of the Future Ready Schools—School Librarian Self-Reflection survey (All4Ed, 2023), an instrument "designed to assess a librarians' professional practices alignment with the Future Ready Schools—Librarian Framework." Approximately 2–4 questions aligned with each of the nine constructs, answered on a 5-point Likert scale from "Never" (1) to "Very Frequently" (5). Wording was changed from the pronoun "I" to "library services/personnel" so the instrument could be administered to administrators, teachers, and librarians alike. (Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik (2026))

### Claims

- [Teachers rated Personalized Professional Learning significantly higher than school librarians (p = .031)](../claims/teachers-rate-personalized-professional-learning-higher.md) [+W]
- [School librarians rated Robust Infrastructure significantly lower than both teachers and administrators](../claims/librarians-rate-robust-infrastructure-lower.md) [+W]
- [Teachers rated Use of Space and Time significantly higher than school librarians (p < .001), with no significant administrator–teacher or administrator–librarian differences](../claims/teachers-rate-use-of-space-and-time-higher-than-librarians.md) [+W]
- [School librarians rated Budget and Resources significantly lower than teachers (p = .019)](../claims/librarians-rate-budget-and-resources-lower-than-teachers.md) [+W]
- [No statistically significant stakeholder differences on Literacy, Curriculum, Instruction and Assessment, Community Partnerships, Collaborative Leadership, or Data and Privacy](../claims/no-significant-differences-on-five-frs-lf-constructs.md) [+W]
