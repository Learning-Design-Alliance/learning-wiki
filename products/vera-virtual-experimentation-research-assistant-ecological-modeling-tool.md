## Description
A publicly available online ecological modeling tool hosted through the Smithsonian's Encyclopedia of Life that supports conceptual modeling, agent-based simulation, hypothesis-driven experimentation, and model–simulate–refine activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Component-Mechanism-Phenomenon (CMP) visual language for conceptual ecological models**: The Component-Mechanism-Phenomenon (CMP) language is a visual modeling framework used in VERA to represent conceptual models of ecological phenomena. A CMP model consists of components (Biotic populations, Abiotic substances, and Habitats) and directed relationships between them (e.g., consumes, produces, destroys, affects, becomes on death). The ontology of components and interactions derives from Encyclopedia of Life and its partner Global Biotic Interactions (GloBI). This visual language allows novice modelers to express hypotheses without programming or writing mathematical equations. (An et al. (2022))
- **VERA: Virtual Ecological Research Assistant**: VERA is a web-based interactive learning environment that supports inquiry-based modeling of ecological phenomena for novice scientists such as students and citizen scientists. It provides a visual language (CMP) for constructing conceptual models, an AI compiler that automatically translates these models into agent-based simulations in NetLogo, and integration with the Encyclopedia of Life (EOL) to populate simulation parameters. The system enables users to engage in a generate-evaluate-revise loop for model construction, evaluation, and revision. (An et al. (2022))
