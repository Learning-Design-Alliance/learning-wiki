# PRONTO simulation-based nurse mentoring program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A high-fidelity in-situ simulation and video-guided debriefing programme developed by PRONTO International and implemented with CARE India to train nurse mentees in emergency obstetric care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PRONTO simulation-based nurse mentoring program embedded in AMANAT, Bihar**: A high-fidelity in-situ simulation training program developed by PRONTO International and implemented with CARE India within the AMANAT nurse mentoring program at 320 primary health centers in Bihar between 2015 and 2017. Nurse mentors rotated in pairs between facilities, facilitating video-recorded emergency obstetric simulations, each "followed by a video-guided debrief, led by the nurse mentor." The curriculum included 31 simulation scenarios, including two PE/E scenarios, and 3422 mentees were trained statewide across four phases. (Raney Julia H. et al. (2019))
- **In-situ simulation-based mentorship training (PRONTO within AMANAT) as a model for building clinical skills among nurses with limited formal medical education**: The article describes a train-the-trainer model in which PRONTO mentors, themselves nurses, deliver repeated in-situ simulations of neonatal and obstetric emergencies supplemented by teamwork, communication activities, skills stations, and case-based learning. Mentors rotate monthly across primary health centers over an eight-month phase, and all simulations are video-recorded "to enable video-assisted debriefing as well as for programmatic evaluation." The evaluation used this model to improve neonatal resuscitation skills among ANM/GNM nurses in rural PHCs. (Vail Brennan et al. (2018))
