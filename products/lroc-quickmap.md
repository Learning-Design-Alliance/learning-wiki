# LROC QuickMap

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
LROC QuickMap is an Arizona State University-hosted web platform providing interactive access to Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter and Kaguya lunar imagery, terrain, temperature, and compositional data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LROC quickmap open-access lunar data platform as a school science-education resource**: The LROC quickmap website hosts data products from the LRO and Kaguya missions that the project used extensively, including WAC and NAC imagery, LOLA/Kaguya terrain elevation and slope data, Diviner regolith temperature data and Kaguya iron oxide abundance data. The article describes it as "a powerful tool, offering an unprecedented level of detail about the Moon's surface", from high resolution imagery to topographic maps, and guides students to analyze it individually to foster critical thinking and data interpretation skills. (Kevin J. Luke et al. (2024))
