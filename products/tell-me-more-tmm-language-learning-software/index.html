Tell Me More (TMM) language learning software¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Tell Me More is Auralog's self-paced, multimedia language-learning software offering CEFR-aligned content, exercises, activities, feedback, and assessment across multiple language skills.

Components¶

  • Tell Me More Performance English ASR program: The instructional software used by the experimental groups: an ASR-based pronunciation course whose home page offers "three levels (beginners, intermediate, and advanced) and twelve lesson workshops", each containing exercises at word, sentence, and real-life dialogue levels. The student listens to a model, records an attempt, and "the program provides the student with automatic feedback to determine the quality of pronunciation" by comparing attempts to a native speaker. Four workshops (The Menu, Desserts, Organizing a Meal, Setting the Table) were selected for the study after supervisor and teacher review. (Amal Khalil Elimat et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • Teaching vocabulary through TMM software produced significantly higher post-test vocabulary scores than conventional instruction in Iranian intermediate EFL learners [+M]
  • Third-grade EFL students taught pronunciation with ASR software significantly outperformed peers taught with regular instruction [+M]
