# Balance scale task

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A developmental reasoning assessment task, originating in the work of Inhelder and Piaget and later used by Siegler, in which children judge balance by reasoning about weights and distances.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Balance scale task with latent class modeling as a worked example of theory-driven validation**: The article presents the balance scale task (Inhelder & Piaget, 1958; Siegler, 1981) as an example of validation anchored in a theory of response behavior. A latent class model conceptualizes developmental stages as latent classes, and the theory specifies stage-specific strategies: "children in the first stage will simply count the number of weights on both sides of the center to reach a decision," later stages incorporate distances and products of weights and distances. Items are constructed so that strategies give conflicting responses, allowing earlier-stage children to outperform later-stage children on some items. (Denny Borsboom et al. (2004))
