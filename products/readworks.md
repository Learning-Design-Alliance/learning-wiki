## Description
ReadWorks is a free digital platform providing K-12 teachers a library of curated nonfiction and literary articles, comprehension and vocabulary supports, assessments, and teacher resources. Through the LVP partnership it added student-facing scaffolds—audio, split screen, text magnification, guided reading strip, paragraph numbering, and article annotation—intended as scaffolds bolstering strengths and mitigating challenges tied to key learner factors such as attention, working memory, vocabulary, and vision. The platform's reach is large: 88 percent of all K-8 public and charter schools in the U.S. had a teacher who used ReadWorks in 2018-2019, potentially reaching over 15 million students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers assign articles to students to be read on the digital platform; features appear as options on the student-facing article interface, with audio requiring teacher enablement (later changed to default-on).
#### Constraints
- With the exception of the audio support, the described features are always available for all students; audio access depends on teacher assignment settings.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, especially elementary readers across the full range of reading abilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary development
- Student engagement with text
