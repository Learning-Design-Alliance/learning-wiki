## Description
TinkerPlots is dynamic statistical software for educational data analysis, visualization, and probability modeling, produced by its developers for learners from secondary school through university.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TinkerPlots dynamic statistical software used for experimental probability simulation activities**: TinkerPlots is the dynamic statistical software used in the experimental group's activities. The article describes it as software "used by the students from secondary school to university, which provides a dynamic learning environment with data analysis and probability modeling". Its changeable number of trials lets learners observe small and large samples, and experiment results are transformed into a dynamic, visual working environment through tables and graphics, supporting verification or revision of intuitions about probability and randomness. Prospective teachers first experienced the software during modeling, using the sampler toolbar. (Timur Koparan (2015))
