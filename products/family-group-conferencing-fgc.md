# Family Group Conferencing (FGC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A family-led child-welfare decision-making programme used by social-service agencies to help families and professionals create safety and well-being plans for children and young people.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FGC as a family-led empowerment model**: The article describes FGC not simply as a social work intervention but as "a cultural approach based on the strong principle of 'empowerment' of families and children". The model assumes families are experts about their own problems and can take control and make safe decisions for young people with support from welfare services. The FGC builds an alliance between families and professionals to share power and responsibilities in decision making involving a child, with a co-ordinator acting as a relational guide for the family. (Gianluca Argentin et al. (2016))
