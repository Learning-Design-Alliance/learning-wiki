# SKT-SRL: a scenario-based conditional strategy knowledge test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A scenario-based offline assessment developed by Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich and colleagues that measures college students’ ability to distinguish useful from less useful cognitive, metacognitive, and motivational learning strategies in specific situations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SKT-SRL: a scenario-based conditional strategy knowledge test for college students**: The SKT-SRL is a scenario-based offline instrument assessing conditional SRL strategy knowledge, defined as knowledge about which strategy is most useful for a specific learning situation. It presents seven learning scenarios, each with three useful and three less useful strategies, and scores learners on how well they differentiate useful from less useful strategies. It is the first test combining cognitive, metacognitive, and motivational components within a process model viewpoint for college students. (Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich et al. (2024))
