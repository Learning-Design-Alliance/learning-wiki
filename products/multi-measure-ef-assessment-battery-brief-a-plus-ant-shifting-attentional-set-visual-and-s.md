# Multi-measure EF assessment battery: BRIEF-A plus ANT Shifting Attentional Set-Visual and Spatial-Temporal Span tasks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A combined assessment battery using the BRIEF-A self-report and ANT computerized shifting and working-memory tasks to profile executive-function strengths and difficulties, including those relevant to academic progress.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multi-measure EF assessment battery: BRIEF-A self-report plus ANT Shifting Attentional Set-Visual and Spatial-Temporal Span tasks**: The study assessed executive functioning with both an ecologically valid self-report and computerized performance tasks, arguing that "both performance-based and self-reported behavioral measures of executive function provide important information about an individual's efficiency and success in achieving goals". The BRIEF-A rates frequency of everyday EF problems over the last 4 weeks across 9 clinical scales; the ANT tasks operationalized inhibition, cognitive flexibility (SSV) and working memory (STS) from speed and accuracy differences. (Dijkhuis Renée et al. (2020))

### Claims
- [Poorer self-reported daily EF correlates with poorer academic progress, strongest for plan/organize (r = −.57)](../claims/brief-a-correlations-academic-progress-asd.md) [+M]
- [Poorer cognitive flexibility and working memory performance correlate moderately with poorer academic progress (r = −.33 to −.35)](../claims/ant-flexibility-working-memory-correlate-academic-progress.md) [+M]
