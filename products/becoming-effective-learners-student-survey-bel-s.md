## Description
The BEL-S is a Rasch-based self-report instrument designed to measure student noncognitive factors (academic mindsets, learning strategies, perseverance, academic behaviors) and perceptions of classroom environment within specific target classes. The article states: "We used Rasch measurement models to create a survey instrument that could more precisely measure both noncognitive constructs and students' perceptions of their classrooms." Students respond to the full item bank twice, once for each of two preselected target classes, enabling within-student comparisons across classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Items are answered in the context of a specified target class, with target courses preselected by an algorithm to maximize classrooms covered and students per classroom
#### Constraints
- The article notes researchers have urged caution in the use of individual-level noncognitive assessments, particularly for accountability purposes

### Target Learners
- Middle school and high school students (grades 6-12)

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring academic mindsets, learning strategies, perseverance, academic behaviors, and classroom environment perceptions in specific academic classes
