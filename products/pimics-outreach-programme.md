## Description
Howell et al.'s PiMICS outreach programme uses servomotor-operated robots and centralized PiMICS.org, GitHub, and YouTube resources to engage young people and scientists with physics of light, imaging, and spectroscopy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PiMICS outreach robots and centralized online resources (PiMICS.org, GitHub, YouTube)**: Beyond skills-building, the camera system anchors a STEM outreach program: the authors built robots (PiMICS 1, 2, and 3) with features similar to PiMICS Zero, operated with servomotors, of which PiMICS 3 "can talk, tell jokes, move, and interact with the audience". The intent is to teach young people about the physics of light, imaging, and spectroscopy. A YouTube video and the PiMICS.org website centralize printer files, codes, designs, and instructional videos. (Howell et al. (2024))

### Claims

- [A pilot program in Ecuador, South Africa, and the United States had students build low-cost multispectral cameras and gain a broad set of physics and engineering skills](../claims/pimics-pilot-skills-building.md) [+W]
