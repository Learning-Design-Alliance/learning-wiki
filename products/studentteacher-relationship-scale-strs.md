## Description
The STRS is a teacher-report questionnaire developed by Robert C. Pianta for assessing conflict, closeness, and dependency in teacher–student relationships, primarily from preschool through elementary school.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Student–Teacher Relationship Scale (STRS) measuring conflict, closeness, and dependency**: The review describes the Student–Teacher Relationship Scale as a widely used and well-validated teacher-report questionnaire measuring perceptions of conflict, closeness, and sometimes dependency in relationships with specific children from preschool to upper elementary school. Conflict items include "Dealing with this child drains my energy"; closeness items include "My interactions with this child make me feel effective and confident." The review notes teachers' reports of relationship quality are unique predictors of children's school functioning above direct reports of student behavior, supporting their validity, though some researchers have questioned the validity of the dependency subscale. (Jantine L. Spilt et al. (2011))
