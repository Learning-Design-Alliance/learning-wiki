# Just Right Reader Take-Everywhere Literacy Packs™

## Description
Take-home literacy packs containing decodable books aligned with the Science of Reading and personalized to each student's phonics skill level. Each pack includes lesson plans, QR codes linking to short instructional videos in English, Spanish, and 12 other languages, the student's name on the pack, books with relevant characters and meaningful storylines, and writing pages that strengthen the connection between reading and writing. The design aims to let students "independently practice decoding strategies and apply letter-sound knowledge in authentic contexts, supporting their phonics progression."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers use the packs and their corresponding lesson plans weekly during small-group instruction, then send them home for additional practice; no additional staffing is required.
