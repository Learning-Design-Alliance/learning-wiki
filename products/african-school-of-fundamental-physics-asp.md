## Description
A pan-African physics education and outreach programme that runs scientific learning activities for high-school pupils, university students, and teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASP2024 three-component scientific program: learners, university students, and high school teachers**: ASP2024's scientific program was arranged in "three distinct components for high school pupils (learners), university students, high school teachers." The learners program ran April 15–19, 2024, with outreach events for over a thousand learners. The university-student program ran the full two weeks, July 7–21, 2024, using plenary sessions of general-interest topics with parallel sessions where students selected topics supporting their academic majors. The teachers program ran in parallel during July 8–12, 2024. (K´et´evi A. Assamagan et al. (2024))
