## Description
STEP-VR is a virtual-reality simulation station developed by ThreeDee GmbH for practicing and assessing management of complex medical-emergency scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STEP-VR virtual reality simulation station for emergency-medicine OSCE assessment**: STEP-VR (version 0.13b; ThreeDee GmbH) is the VR-based simulation of complex medical emergencies used as the VRS in the curricular OSCE, run with head-mounted displays and controllers. Students read the case, then manage septic or anaphylactic shock scenarios with dynamic vital parameters; the first-person view is transmitted to a screen for assessors, who rate performance with standardized candidate assessment forms. The article reports that "53 of 57 students (93%) were able to use the VR technology without any issues." (Mühling T et al. (2025))
