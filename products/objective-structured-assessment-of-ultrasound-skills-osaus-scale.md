# Objective Structured Assessment of Ultrasound Skills (OSAUS) scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The OSAUS scale is a structured performance-assessment instrument for evaluating ultrasound skills, developed for use in medical education and clinical competency assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrate the OSAUS scale into clinical settings for training and assessment of airway ultrasound competence**: The article concludes that the OSAUS scale, having demonstrated validity and reliability evidence for hyomental distance competence, should move from simulated validation into real training and certification use. It states: "The use of OSAUS scale should be integrated into the clinical setting for training and assessment of airway ultrasound competence." This addresses the lack of evidence-based guidelines for education or assessment of airway ultrasound. (Gomes Sara Hora et al. (2023))
- **Revised Objective Structured Assessment of Ultrasound Skills (OSAUS) practical examination**: The study used "a revised version of the Objective Structured Assessment of Ultrasound Skills (OSAUS), which allows the independent and unbiased assessment of ten aspects of ultrasonographic skills and knowledge of the candidate." The ten aspects range from information check and indication to image optimization, systematic examination, image interpretation, documentation, management, privacy protection and humanistic solicitude. The maximum score was 100 and a passing score was defined as 80; it served as the proctored practical outcome measure. (Lin Zi-mei et al. (2022))
