MMA rubric¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A modified Wang (2007) rubric used by Mansyur and colleagues to score university physics students' mental-model generation, manipulation, metacognitive monitoring, and self-checking.

Components¶

  • MMA rubric (Wang 2007, modified by Mansyur 2010) for scoring mental-modeling ability: A rubric used to assess students' mental-modeling ability, with components scored to a maximum total of 12: generating a mental model with and without a 2D representation (1a/1b), manipulating the mental model based on propositions or possessing a rigid model (2a/2b), metacognitive monitoring, and self-checking with an alternative approach. The article states, "MMA rubric was predominantly used to assess Component 1a (or 1b) and 2a (or 2b) of Table 1 as the main focus of this study." Components 3 and 4 were not included in this article. (Mansyur et al. (2016))

Claims¶

  • EDI qualitatively outperformed DI on MMA normalized gain, though both groups' gains were in the low category [+M]
