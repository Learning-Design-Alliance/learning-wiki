# 6+1 Trait Writing model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A writing-assessment and instructional framework developed by Northwest Regional Educational Laboratory that uses seven trait rubrics to structure feedback, self-assessment, peer assessment, and revision.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The 6+1 trait writing model as a rubric-based framework integrating assessment with writing instruction**: The 6+1 trait writing model is a supplementary means for theorizing and evaluating adequate writing, built on rubrics assessing seven characteristics: ideas, organization, voice, word choice, sentence fluency, conventions, and presentation. It "integrates evaluation with the teaching process" and its "main focus is on using rubrics to provide students with feedback through self-assessment, peer assessment, and teacher feedback at different phases of the writing process". In this study it was operationalized as a method giving students the traits of adequate writing and the opportunity to develop critical thinking and writing achievement. ([Qoura & Zahran, 2018](https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n9p68))
