## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Early Learning in Mathematics (ELM) kindergarten core mathematics curriculum**: ELM is "a core mathematics program for kindergartners that provides 120 45-minute lessons, in addition to daily 15-minute calendar activities." It is designed to be delivered by the kindergarten teacher to a whole class, with calendar lessons schedulable separately or incorporated into morning circle/opening activities. The program qualified for the "Promising" evidence category based on one study of 2,116 students. (Early Learning in Mathematics (ELM): Evidence Rating (2021))
- **Focused coverage of a small number of key mathematical ideas with explicit, systematic instruction**: ELM's instructional design pattern emphasizes depth over breadth: "The curriculum emphasizes a small number of key ideas in math to provide more focused coverage, instead of trying to teach a comprehensive but more surface set of skills." It pairs this focused coverage with "Explicit and systematic instructional design." The pattern structures a full year of 120 45-minute lessons plus daily calendar activities. (Early Learning in Mathematics (ELM): Evidence Rating (2021))
- **ELM (Early Learning in Mathematics) core kindergarten mathematics program**: ELM is described as "an empirically validated, core kindergarten mathematics program intended for use in general education settings." It is a 120-lesson scripted program delivered in whole-class settings 45 minutes per day, 5 days per week, covering five mathematical domains: Counting and Cardinality, Operations and Algebraic Thinking, Number and Operations in Base Ten, Measurement and Data, and Geometry. Its instructional design framework gives teachers specific guidelines to demonstrate content, facilitate frequent practice, and offer timely informational feedback. Teachers received four 6-hour workshops distributed across the school year. (Doabler et al. (2018))
- **ELM kindergarten mathematics curriculum with four content strands**: The Early Learning in Mathematics (ELM) curriculum is a full-year kindergarten core (Tier I) mathematics program for whole classroom settings, including "120 core daily lessons that are approximately 45 minutes in duration" incorporating 4-5 activities across four content strands: whole number and operations, measurement, geometry, and precise mathematics vocabulary. Mathematics content is explicitly introduced, systematically reviewed, and extended across lessons. (Doabler et al. (2013))
