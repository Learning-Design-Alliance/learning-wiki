## Description
EduTorcs is a modified version of the open-source TORCS driving game created by B. D. Coller and colleagues for undergraduate mechanical-engineering students to practice dynamic-systems and feedback-control design through code-driven gameplay.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EduTorcs video game for learning dynamic systems and control**: EduTorcs is a driving game created by making substantial modifications to the open-source game TORCS, in which students normally drive the car or bike by devising algorithms that take information about the vehicle and its dynamic state and calculate how much to step on the gas, apply the brakes, and turn the steering wheel. Students code their algorithms in C and compile their program, and the game links to the students' programs at run time. Students get immediate feedback as to whether their ideas work. At the time of writing, EduTorcs was not available to the public at large. (B. D. Coller et al. (2011))
