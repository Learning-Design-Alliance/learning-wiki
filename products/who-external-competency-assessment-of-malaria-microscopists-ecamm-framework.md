## Description
A WHO-standardized assessment and training framework using verified malaria blood-smear panels to evaluate and develop microscopists’ diagnostic competency.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECAMM framework for external competency assessment of malaria microscopists**: The WHO External Competency Assessment of Malaria Microscopists (ECAMM) framework is a standardized assessment and training structure for malaria diagnostic competency. It "employs standardized test panels comprising 20 negative slides, 20 positive slides (single/mixed infections at defined parasite densities)" plus falciparum-positive thick/thin smears for quantification, with slides verified by Level 1-certified experts and molecular diagnostics. This article adapted ECAMM into a condensed 4-day training prioritizing diagnostic accuracy over parasite quantification for post-elimination settings. (Ning et al. (2025))
