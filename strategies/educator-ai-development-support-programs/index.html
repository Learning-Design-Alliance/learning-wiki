Implement sustained educator development programs including needs assessment, coaching, communities of practice, and experiential learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends educator support programs addressing immediate and long-term needs: conducting a needs assessment first, incorporating faculty input (which "increases acceptance and participation"), and supporting lifelong learning through structured training, ongoing coaching, and communities of practice where educators peer review AI-assisted course designs. It also suggests train-the-trainer programs, micro-credentialing, and experiential professional learning, since "AI literacy cannot be developed without hands-on experience with AI."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Needs assessment to identify context-relevant supports
  • Hands-on experience with AI tools as part of professional learning

Constraints¶

  • The brief notes AI detection tools are often inaccurate and biased, so evaluations should build on learners' previous work instead

Target Learners¶

  • Postsecondary educators
  • Faculty and instructional staff

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Integrating AI into instruction and assessment
  • Developing AI literacy for teaching and research

Related Strategies¶

  • CEO Forum recommendations: prepare and certify teachers for technology integration across pre-service, licensure, and in-service career stages
  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Hold a kick-off event aligning coaching with school and district goals, and leverage early volunteers' testimonials to build teacher buy-in
  • Support first-year adopters of a comprehensive curriculum with quick-start overviews, editable materials, and curated course plans
  • Invest in educator professional learning on experiential AI pedagogies using existing federal funding streams
  • Invest in sustained, role-specific, discipline-specific professional learning and structured student AI literacy pathways across PK16
  • Invest in program coordination, instructional coaching, and bilingual paraprofessional career pathways to improve English Learner program design
  • Allocate ongoing professional development so teachers learn to evaluate and integrate AI systems appropriately
  • Implementation recommendations for school RJ programs: funding, sustainability, policy integration, and professional development
