# Optimal Student Flow

## Description
Organizing the classroom layout to match the flow of the day, such as moving from reading to math centers, can enhance efficiency. Clearly organized centers for math, reading, and writing, with good line of sight, support effective learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined learning centers, organized materials, good line of sight.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and organization. May require adjustments to adapt to changing student needs.
