# Preformulated teacher comments with embedded evidence-based hyperlinks as VP feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
After each free-text student answer, the VP presents a short preformulated teacher comment conveying an experienced family doctor's clinical reasoning, plus hyperlinks to deeper evidence-based material. The article states: "The teachers’ preformulated comments reflected evidence-based medicine relevant for the case and the tacit knowledge of an experienced clinician. The comments were written as a short text (100-150 words) following each part of the encounter." Further in-depth knowledge is accessible through embedded hyperlinks pointing to continuously updated evidence-based pages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comments written by experienced clinicians reflecting evidence-based medicine and tacit reasoning
- Embedded hyperlinks to continuously updated evidence-based pages
#### Constraints
- Comments are general and not individualized to each student's answer, as students noted

### Target Learners
- medical students working independently with the VP case

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- evidence-based medicine knowledge
- communication skills
