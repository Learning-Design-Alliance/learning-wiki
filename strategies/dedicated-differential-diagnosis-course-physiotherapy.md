# Add a dedicated postgraduate course on differential diagnostic thinking and skills, with emphasis on red-flag recognition, to physiotherapy curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that Hungarian physiotherapy education add explicit differential-diagnosis training. It states that in Hungary "the training outcome requirements for physiotherapists do not include differential diagnostic knowledge" and that no postgraduate course specifically teaches the process of differential diagnosis, concluding that "an independent postgraduate course aimed at acquiring differential diagnostic thinking and skills should be added to the curriculum". The University of Szeged's faculty will introduce a 14-hour BSc course on the basics of differential diagnosis from 2024.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- differential diagnostic reasoning for cervical spine complaints
- recognition of red flags and life-threatening warning signs and symptoms
