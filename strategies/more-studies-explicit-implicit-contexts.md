# Call for combined quantitative and qualitative studies to identify which contexts favor explicit versus implicit grammar teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The reviewer recommends that future research combine quantitative and qualitative methods to reach, if possible, final conclusions about which grammar-teaching approach is more effective and in which context. As the review states, "I need more quantitative and qualitative studies to form , if possible, final conclusions concerning which is more effective". Tode's study additionally suggests enhancing noticing of new structures and providing adequate practice contrasting them with previously learned ones.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must target English structures for EFL/ESL learners, per the review's selection criteria
#### Constraints
- The review covered only studies published between 2004 and 2007 located via EBSCOhost, ERIC, and Google Scholar

### Target Learners
- EFL and ESL learners, including teenagers and tertiary-level students

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 grammatical proficiency, including simple and complex structures such as subject-verb agreement, relative clauses, and the copular be
