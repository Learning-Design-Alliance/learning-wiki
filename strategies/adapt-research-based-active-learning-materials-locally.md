# Adapt established research-based active-learning materials to the local student context rather than transferring them unchanged

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The commentary describes a transfer-and-adapt approach to course reform: rather than inventing techniques from scratch, instructors take materials developed by established education research groups and refine them for local conditions. The reformed Omani courses "started with materials developed by Physics Education Research groups in Minnesota and Colorado and then further refined to provide context-rich activities" that fit the Middle East, accomplished without expensive equipment or technology beyond students' cell phones.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Established, discipline-specific research-based course materials must be available to adapt
- Instructors must refine the borrowed materials into context-rich activities suited to the local context
#### Constraints
- The commentary notes education is highly contextual, so methods optimized in one setting should not be assumed best in another

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate STEM students at Sultan Qaboos University in Oman, taught in English though it is not their native language

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved course scores, reduced failure rates, and more positive attitudes toward physics, astronomy, and quantum mechanics
