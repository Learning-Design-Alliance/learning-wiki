# Let learners choose personally resonant topics to sustain motivation in long interdisciplinary projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In a months-long project, the authors chose to highlight elements relevant both to the show's themes and to the students' personal experiences growing up in Appalachia. "Grace and Trevor each chose topics that resonated with parts of their lives, adding an intangible personal significance to the program." The authors frame enjoyment as important for sustaining effort across eight months or longer of work.
