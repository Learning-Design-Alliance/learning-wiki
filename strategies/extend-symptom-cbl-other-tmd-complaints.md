# Extend symptom-anchored CBL to other TMD chief complaints such as joint pain, clicking, and mandibular deviation

## Description
In the conclusion the authors recommend: “The future research studies should examine topics like ‘joint pain,’ ‘joint clicking,’ and ‘mandibular deviation’ to further improve clinical competence.” This is an implementable next step: build additional symptom-centered case series following the same chief-complaint-first design, each tested against conventional CBL as this pilot was.
