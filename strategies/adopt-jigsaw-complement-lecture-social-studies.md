# Adopt jigsaw to complement conventional lecture in Social Studies to prepare students for communal living

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that jigsaw students showed more favourable attitudes, the authors recommend that "Teachers should adopt the jigsaw learning technique in the teaching of concepts in Social Studies and indeed across other subject areas" to prepare students for future communal living, understanding of other peoples' culture, and living peacefully outside their own culture or ethnic origins. They also recommend frequent ministry-organised in-service training for teachers on methods of teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should be exposed to teaching strategies beyond the conventional lecture method
- Ministry of Education should organize frequent in-service training for teachers
#### Constraints
- Recommended as a complement to, not a replacement for, the conventional lecture method

### Target Learners
- junior secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- ethnic integration
- tolerance and peaceful co-existence
- sustainable development values
