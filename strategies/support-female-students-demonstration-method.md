# Pay particular attention to supporting female students' performance when using the demonstration method in agricultural science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers of agricultural science effectively employ the demonstration method in a way that helps improve female students' performance, because the study found a significant difference in mean achievement scores in favour of males. This strategy directs attention to the female learners who scored lower under both teaching conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be willing to adapt the demonstration method specifically to improve female students' performance.
#### Constraints
- The gender gap finding comes from a single quasi-experimental study in one education zone and has not been tested as an intervention.

### Target Learners
- Female senior secondary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Agricultural science achievement
