# Extend the films-as-vehicle approach to many other disciplines, with suggested film lists per discipline

## Description
The authors argue "This new technique can be extended t o many other disciplines," and provide a list of ten suggested disciplines each with suggested films: Math, Biology, Engineering, Archeology, Computers, Philosophy, History, Law, Forensic Science, and Psychology in Films/Movies. The strategy treats popular film as a familiar cultural medium through which discipline-specific principles can be analyzed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to films whose scenes illustrate principles of the target discipline
#### Constraints
- The listed extensions are suggestions with suggested films; the article reports implementation only for physical science and planned astronomy

### Target Learners
- college and university general-education students across disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- discipline-specific principles (mathematics, biology, engineering, and others) presented in an enjoyable context
