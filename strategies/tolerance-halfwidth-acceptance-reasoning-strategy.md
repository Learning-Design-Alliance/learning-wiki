# Specification-table tolerance half-widths as acceptance criteria for photonic device verification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module teaches engineering-style verification reasoning by having students read tolerance half-widths from specification-style summaries and treat them as acceptance windows around a design point. The prompt states: "The simulator reports tolerance half-widths around a chosen operating point. Explain how a tolerance half-width can function as an acceptance criterion in a photonic device specification." Students then discuss how this reasoning extends when the two-mode beam splitter is embedded in a larger interferometric network, where local tolerances must propagate through multiple optical elements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students compare the tolerance half-width with the fabrication spread and identify at least one additional system-level requirement, such as cumulative tolerance propagation, network architecture, or multi-element calibration
#### Constraints
- Network-level tolerance propagation is explicitly excluded from the module scope and named as a natural future extension

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate quantum optics students
- early graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- interpret engineering-style specification summaries as compact verification tools
- connect two-mode HOM reasoning to larger photonic systems
