## Description
The article identifies several problems needing solution for effective television instruction: good TV instructors, writers, and directors; psycho-educational experts to plan TV lessons; a method of rapidly measuring program effectiveness; technical equipment and engineers; and adequate distribution facilities. Addressing these production and planning needs is presented as necessary for successful television-based training programs.
