# Record discipline-specific writing videos and scholar interviews online so students in unstreamed first-year courses can access writing instruction relevant to their field

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy moves discipline-specific writing instruction outside the classroom by recording instructors and scholars from arts and science research areas discussing writing practices and conventions, and posting samples of disciplinary writing online. The article argues that "blended learning offers exciting possibilities for creating a dialogue between disciplines, allowing instructors from other areas of the university to record reflections or insights on writing in their disciplines." Students view the interview for their intended discipline and write a reflection, then identify the broad discipline of their research topic to inform their final paper's style.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a learning management system for hosting videos, readings, and writing exercises; students must complete an online reflection on the interview for their intended discipline
#### Constraints
- The article notes disciplinary distinctions extend beyond the issues covered, and this is a first-year introduction rather than full discipline-specific instruction

### Target Learners
- first-year university students from all areas of the university in unstreamed composition courses

### Target Learning Goals
- discipline-specific academic writing conventions and research-genre awareness
