# Use just-in-time e-learning booster review immediately before neonatal procedures to support knowledge and especially performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends positioning e-learning booster review immediately before the procedure in which the skill is needed, consistent with the just-in-time practice-learning approach it describes as used "to strengthen teamwork and technical skills just before the time in which they may be required". It suggests such boosters may increase knowledge and especially performance of practitioners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Providers must already be certified in NRP; the booster targets review of specific procedures rather than the full multistep algorithm.
#### Constraints
- The review concludes there is not enough evidence that e-learning prevents long-term knowledge and performance decay; immediate performance gains were significant only in some studies and measures.

### Target Learners
- Already NRP-certified physicians, residents, nurses, respiratory therapists, and neonatal nurse practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- Neonatal resuscitation knowledge and procedural skills such as neonatal intubation and LMA placement
