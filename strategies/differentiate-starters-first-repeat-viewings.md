# Differentiate conversation starters for first versus repeat viewings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A strategy in which conversation starters are designed as two series: one for a first viewing to contextualize the science content through the episode narrative, and another for repeat viewings to focus more heavily on science ideas and invite science discourse. The team created "one series of prompts was created for the first viewing and another set for subsequent (repeat) viewings" so that prompts over time reinforced more complex core ideas and practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of which viewing scenario a family is in
- Prompts for before, during, and after viewing
#### Constraints
- Families did not always use the starters in the way they were initially designed
- Before prompts were hard to use because families lacked episode context, and were ultimately removed

### Target Learners
- Parents and caregivers co-viewing science media with young children

### Target Learning Goals
- Deepening science discourse across repeated viewings
