# Engineer pauses, multimodal anchors, and improved prosody into AI-generated audio revision tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' design recommendations for future AI-audio tools, drawn from the qualitative integration: (1) deliberate silence engineered after case scenarios and between questions and answers to force retrieval practice and reduce cognitive load; (2) a visual companion such as a one-page key-point PDF to address the friability of audio learning; and (3) continued refinement of AI prosody to reduce monotony, which was identified as a barrier to sustained attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Audio production tooling must support inserting deliberate silences at scripted points and pairing audio with downloadable visual companions.
#### Constraints
- These refinements are recommended on the basis of qualitative feedback and hypothesised cognitive-load mechanisms; the article notes experimental comparisons of continuous versus segmented audio remain future research.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students using mobile, self-directed revision resources

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement and long-term retention of revised content
