# Provide repeated professional education for physicians on early childhood obesity knowledge and growth-chart use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends ongoing, repeated professional education for healthcare providers in the MENA region to close the gaps the survey documented in growth-chart use, cut-off knowledge, and comorbidity awareness. The authors state the findings warrant "the need for repeated professional education, to leverage expertise and improve overall health outcomes within the MENA region". The education targets both knowledge of complications and routine growth monitoring practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targets healthcare providers (general physicians, pediatricians, pediatric gastroenterologists, neonatologists) in regions where early-onset obesity is prevalent
#### Constraints
- The survey found only 0.7% of physicians answered all knowledge questions correctly, indicating baseline knowledge gaps the education must address

### Target Learners
- Physicians and healthcare providers in primary and specialty pediatric care

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct use and interpretation of growth charts
- Knowledge of cut-off definitions for infant overweight and obesity
- Awareness of short- and long-term complications of early childhood obesity
