# Implement PERCS-ICU in other residency schools and tailor it to specific communicative challenges such as remote family communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending the PERCS-ICU format to other residency programs and adapting it to emerging communication challenges. They state: "Given the flexibility of the format and the limited resources employed, PERCS-ICU could be successfully implemented in other residency schools." They also propose tailoring the course to challenges such as communicating with families by video or phone calls, which became necessary during the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic, and planning follow-ups to consolidate learning.

## Design Implications

#### Constraints
- the article notes generalizability is limited because the course involved only one residency school in Milan and lacked a control group

### Target Learners

- postgraduate clinicians in other specialities

### Target Learning Goals
- communication and relational skills for difficult conversations
- empathy cultivation
