## Description
One of six proposed strategies and actions, this strategy directs all stakeholders to focus learning analytics first on its use for learning. The article states that "learning analytics should focus first on its use 'for learning,' which contrasts with other potential foci such as 'accountability,' 'testing,' 'organisational change' or 'financial efficiency.'" A second measure under this strategy is providing specific user-centred analytics tools for different stakeholders, with the expert group specifically advocating to prioritise the learner-as-audience.
