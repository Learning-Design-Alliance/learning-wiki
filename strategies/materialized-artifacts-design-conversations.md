# Materialize design knowledge as artifacts to sustain organization-level design conversations

## Description
The amplitudes (design being) stage is a recursive process in which implementing teams become internal catalysts carrying design thinking, with its attitudes, aptitudes and local examples, to the rest of the organization. The article states that 'the mere existence of these materials is critical to support design conversations at an organization level, thus harnessing group knowledge, in order to sustain the transformational process'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- For the rest of the organization to act, the new knowledge must be materialized as a unique representation of a distributed understanding and voiced by someone or some group.
