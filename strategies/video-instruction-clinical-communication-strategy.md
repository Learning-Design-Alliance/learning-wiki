# Use instructional videos and involve students in creating video content to teach clinical communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes two video-based teaching practices: senior surgeons screened instructional videos showing both good and bad examples of communicating terminal diagnoses, and senior nurses involved students in producing healthcare videos for the local community. Interviewee H confirmed that instructional videos showcasing Hong Kong clinical scenarios "could help surgical students prepare for interspersing medical talk with interpersonal chat with patients". The authors suggest involving students in content creation encourages creative uses of digital technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos should showcase scenarios from the local clinical setting to be relevant to students' practice context.
#### Constraints
- The authors note most supporting video-instruction studies were conducted in English-speaking countries and did not focus on the teaching of clinical communication.

### Target Learners
- medical students
- nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical communication skills
- empathy with patients
- health literacy communication with disabled patients
