# Have students write a complete scientific paper (Introduction, Method, Results, Conclusion)

## Description
Writing a complete scientific paper "forces them to articulate their thoughts and logic in an intelligible manner." The introduction forces background research distinguishing relevant prior work; the method section should be detailed enough that another scientist could repeat the experiment; the results section narrates through figures, tables, and charts with at least one paragraph each; and the discussion expands each presentation bullet into a paragraph, most important points first. A written paper can be circulated to solicit feedback before competitive presentation.
