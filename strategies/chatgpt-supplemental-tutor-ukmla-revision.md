# Use ChatGPT as a supplemental AI tutor for UKMLA revision, including AI-supported mastery quizzing, while retaining traditional study methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend students use ChatGPT to quiz themselves on practice items and receive immediate detailed explanations, reviewing topics "until the AI selects the correct answer, a form of mastery learning supported by AI". They argue AI tutors can scale individualized support without proportionally increasing faculty workload, but stress AI should complement, rather than replace, traditional study methods and expert-led teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Item banks enhanced by AI with explanations; student supervision of accuracy given specialty-specific inconsistencies
#### Constraints
- Specialty-specific weaknesses (e.g., general practice, GPT-4o at 71%) call for caution; performance on the UKMLA cannot be extrapolated to other examinations

### Target Learners
- UK medical students preparing for the UKMLA
- re-sitting candidates without access to tutors
- learners in resource-limited settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Applied clinical knowledge for licensing examination preparation and knowledge-gap identification
