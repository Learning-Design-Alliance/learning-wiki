# Offer eLearning to practices with detected cold chain breaches and add reinforcement strategies for sustained improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's workflow links continuous temperature monitoring to targeted education: practices with logged cold chain breaches were identified and offered the guideline-based eLearning, and refrigerators served as their own control to evaluate change. The authors state that eLearning is "much more cost-effective" than onsite interventions, "yet additional reinforcement strategies are needed" because only 13% of participating practices reached the target range at follow-up. They also recommend no long-term monitoring was performed but is recommended for future studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous temperature data logging (1-minute intervals) to detect breaches; access to the freely available eLearning for all practice personnel.
#### Constraints
- Improvement was partial; most practices with breaches did not continuously maintain the target range after a single eLearning exposure.

### Target Learners
- general practice personnel (physicians and practice assistants)

### Target Learning Goals
- vaccine cold chain maintenance
- refrigerator temperature management

## Claims

- [A GAMLSS prediction model over 2 million temperature measurements showed that Euclidean distances of 95% prediction intervals from the 5°C target fell from before to after the first participant finished the eLearning, in a single-group pre-post analysis](../claims/gamlss-model-temperature-improvement-after-elearning.md) [+W]
- [Baseline cold chain breaches were highly prevalent: 73% of practices and 68% of refrigerators showed breaches at baseline](../claims/high-prevalence-cold-chain-breaches-baseline.md) [+W]
- [Among general practices with cold chain breaches, refrigerator temperatures were measured from baseline to follow-up after KeepCoool participation; at follow-up, 17% of participating refrigerators continuously met the 2-8°C target range.](../claims/keepcoool-elearning-improved-vaccine-cold-chain.md) [+W]
- [Vaccine storage knowledge scores rose from 5.6 to 9.8 of 11 correct answers among physicians and practice assistants after KeepCoool participation, in one single-group pre-post study with no comparison group](../claims/keepcoool-knowledge-score-increase.md) [+W]
