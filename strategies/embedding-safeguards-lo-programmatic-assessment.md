# Embedding safeguards: LO workshops, feedback phrased in LO terms, weekly teacher meetings, and management briefings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For embedding the approach in a standing organisation, the article recommends advancing student understanding of abstract LOs directly via workshops and indirectly by ensuring all offered feedback is phrased in terms of the LOs; weekly teacher team meetings to identify students stuck or dissatisfied; narrowing the technical/professional-skills feedback gap; and regular management briefings, since formative feedback is often invisible outside the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Management must endorse the renewals; coaching students who do not act on feedback is 'an important success factor of the entire project'
#### Constraints
- Students, teachers and parents may complain and push for traditional methods or lower standards

### Target Learners
- second-year BSc students
- teaching teams
- program management

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding learning outcomes
- responding productively to feedback
