# Apply existing test metadata to understand students' social-emotional needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy is the application the article's associated journal article names: an "initial application of test metadata to understand students' social emotional needs." Rather than administering separate social-emotional surveys, educators and researchers analyze metadata already generated during testing — how long students spend on math test questions — to identify students whose response behavior may signal motivation or self-efficacy concerns. The article frames this as an initial application, indicating the approach is exploratory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to item-level response time metadata from administered math assessments.
#### Constraints
- The article characterizes this as an initial application and notes limitations of using response time metadata to measure SEL.

### Target Learners
- K-12 math students

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying students' social-emotional needs
- Understanding motivation and self-efficacy
