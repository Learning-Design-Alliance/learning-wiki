## Description
The article states that "investment in structured coach development—alongside curricular planning—will be critical to optimising both technical skills training and the overall quality of the learning environment." Because "clinical expertise alone does not ensure coaching effectiveness", faculty may require targeted train-the-trainer preparation in educational techniques, feedback delivery, and fostering psychologically safe learning environments.
