## Description
The article states that it "concludes with a discussion of the implications for writing research and the teaching and development of writing", positioning the dual-process model as a basis for writing instruction. Teaching and development of writing should, on this account, engage both the knowledge-constituting and knowledge-transforming processes, since "both processes are required for effective writing". The supplied text indicates the implications are discussed rather than specifying concrete classroom activities.
