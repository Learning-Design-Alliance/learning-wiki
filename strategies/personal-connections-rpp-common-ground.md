# Build personal connections and find common ground in research-practice partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Project partners Neil and Traci advise that "Personal connections are vital" in partnership work: finding common ground with colleagues, clients or students so relationships form, enabling empathy and progress. They describe taking time in every virtual meeting to "catch-up" on organizational and life events, connecting on more than agenda bullet points. This strategy operationalizes the project's culturally responsive partnership approach.
