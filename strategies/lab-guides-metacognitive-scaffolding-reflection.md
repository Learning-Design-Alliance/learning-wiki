# Lab guides with metacognitive scaffolding prompting paired reflection on experimental results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's lab guides, designed to align with how many instructors implement the single-photon experiments, embedded prompts for students to reflect with their lab partner on what the experiments showed. As the authors state, "The lab guides included metacognitive scaffolding asking the students to reflect with their lab partner on the experimental results." This strategy supports in-the-moment sense-making conversations during hands-on quantum optics work and was tested with colleagues outside AMO physics before use with students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lab guides developed from literature and instructor-shared materials, adjusted to logistical constraints, and piloted before student use.
#### Constraints
- In this implementation the guides were used outside a course setting, so students received no additional instruction beyond the lab sessions and did not consider grades or lab write-ups.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students working in pairs

### Target Learning Goals
- sense-making about single-photon interference
- reflection on experimental evidence of quantum behavior
