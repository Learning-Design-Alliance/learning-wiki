# Expressing Interest in Students' Ethnic Backgrounds

## Description
Teachers can actively demonstrate genuine care for students' cultural, emotional, and intellectual needs by encouraging students to research and share information about their ethnic backgrounds. This involves analyzing and celebrating differences in traditions, beliefs, and social behaviors. For European-American students, this helps them realize that their beliefs and traditions constitute a culture as well, which is a necessary breakthrough in the development of a truly culturally responsive classroom. Also, it is essential to take the time to learn the proper pronunciation of student names and express interest in the etymology of interesting and diverse names.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for research and sharing, a supportive and inclusive classroom environment, and a willingness to learn from students.
#### Constraints
- May require additional research and preparation for teachers. Students may feel uncomfortable sharing personal information if the classroom climate is not supportive.
