# Refocus education from reckoning skills towards judgement skills and problem formulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends education shift emphasis from teaching functional AI reckoning skills towards building individuals' reasoned judgement and decision-making capabilities. It endorses Dede and colleagues' call for this refocus and, citing Acar (2023), argues curriculum at all levels must teach students how to recognise and formulate 'problems' AI can assist with, alongside prompt skills and a critical mindset for evaluating AI-augmented outputs. The article contends problem (re)formulation is a core component of dialogic discourse aligned with the Judgement Zone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum and assessment models requiring greater levels of metacognition, and explicit teaching of how to work in partnerships with AI-as-collaborator
#### Constraints
- The article notes functional skills such as prompt engineering will continue to remain relevant for the foreseeable future

### Target Learners
- students at all levels of education
- workers in AI-augmented environments

### Target Learning Goals
- reasoned judgement and decision-making
- problem formulation
- critical evaluation of AI-augmented outputs
