# Frame physics as a science that benefits society to boost female students' engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors, researchers, and curriculum developers "boost females' engagement with physics courses by framing physics as a science that can benefit society throughout a physics course sequence". This is a Factor 3 (implementation) move in the SELF framework: framing instruction and learning tools to get student buy-in. It rests on cited evidence that presenting STEM careers as altruistic and communal increased females' interest in STEM fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
