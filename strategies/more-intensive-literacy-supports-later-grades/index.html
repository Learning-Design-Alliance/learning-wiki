Provide more intensive literacy interventions and supports for at-risk students in later grades¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends that supports for older early-grade students be scaled up relative to kindergarten supports, because risk status is more persistent in later grades. It states that "Interventions and supports for students in later grades may need to be more intensive than those at earlier grades," citing data showing more grade 3 students at risk stay at risk over the school year. This is an implementable targeting rule for schools and districts using screener data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Twice-yearly (or more frequent) universal literacy screening to identify students at risk of reading difficulty

Constraints¶

  • Based on initial, somewhat limited data from grantee districts only

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1–3 identified as at risk on literacy screeners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early literacy benchmark attainment

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide early, effective, targeted intensive intervention to disrupt persistent trajectories of reading difficulty
  • Focus support early in the school year on students identified as at risk at BOY to get them on track by midyear
  • Schools and districts should choose screener benchmarks that match their goals and resources for defining and serving at-risk students
