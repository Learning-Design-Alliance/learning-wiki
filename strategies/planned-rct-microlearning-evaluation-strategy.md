# Evaluate microlearning effectiveness with an RCT adding objective outcomes to self-report measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that future work evaluate educational effectiveness through a randomized controlled trial comparing app-based versus traditional OSCE preparation, supplementing subjective questionnaires with objective measures. They state "we also intend to include objective measures such as OSCE performance scores and app usage statistics to complement self-reported data", because subjective measures are prone to bias and their validity should be critically examined.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Allocation of students to app-use and traditional-preparation cohorts; structured end-of-semester questionnaires
#### Constraints
- Self-assessment inherently carries limitations and must be interpreted with caution

### Target Learners
- Medical students preparing for the obstetrics OSCE

### Target Learning Goals
- Perceived preparedness, satisfaction, learning behaviors, and OSCE performance
