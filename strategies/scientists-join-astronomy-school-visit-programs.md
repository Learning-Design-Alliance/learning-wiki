# Scientists should get involved in school visits through astronomy-based programs

## Description
The article recommends that scientists visit schools, discuss with teachers, interact with students, and design such educational projects, either on their own or by joining an existing program in their country. The authors emphasize that astronomy is a very efficient way to teach science and that this is less time-consuming than one might think, and they warmly recommend that their colleagues try to be involved.
