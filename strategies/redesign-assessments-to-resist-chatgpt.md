# Redesign assessments to require critical thinking, originality, interactive activities, and real-time or proctored formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that university staff design assessments that are harder to complete with ChatGPT: tasks demanding critical thinking, problem-solving and communication, open-ended work encouraging originality and creativity, group discussions and presentations, and real-time or proctored exams. The article states such designs "can make it more difficult for students to use ChatGPT to complete their assignments" and promote critical thinking and independent learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessments must require students to generate original ideas, e.g. their own research questions or defended arguments, rather than essays on set topics
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is by no means certain who will win the arms race between detection and generation technology

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- problem-solving
- communication skills
- academic integrity
