Devise language interventions using naturalistic teaching for acquisition with progression to contrived approaches for generalization¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's future-directions recommendation inverts the common clinical sequence. Many interventions begin with contrived teaching during acquisition and move to naturalistic approaches for generalization, but the meta-analysis results suggest this method is not optimal. The authors instead propose that "it would be more effective to devise language interventions utilizing naturalistic teaching approaches for acquisition with a progression to more contrived approaches to train for generalization."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Interventions should include continuous assessment, empirically validated teaching methods, and data collection, with heavy emphasis on generalization and maintenance of skills learned.

Constraints¶

  • The authors note outcomes can be idiosyncratic and each child benefits most from an individualized approach based on the principles of applied behavior analysis; further investigation is warranted.

Target Learners¶

  • Children aged 2-13 diagnosed with autism, PDD, or PDD-NOS

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Acquisition, generalization, and maintenance of spoken language skills

Claims¶

  • Contrived approaches show greater generalization of spoken language skills than naturalistic approaches in children with autism [-W]
  • Naturalistic approaches show higher mean PND than contrived approaches for maintenance of language skills [+W]
  • Naturalistic language interventions produce higher mean PND than contrived interventions during intervention conditions for children with autism [+W]
  • Conducting maintenance and generalization phases was significantly associated with higher PND scores than treatment-only cases [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Design comprehensive AAC intervention protocols that extend beyond requesting to maintenance, generalization, and naturalistic contexts
