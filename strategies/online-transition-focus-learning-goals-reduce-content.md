# Keep online course design focused on learning goals and reduce content coverage to central topics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that instructors transitioning online keep the focus on course learning goals, such as helping students "learn to think like a physicist" and develop problem solving, reasoning and metacognitive skills. It advises instructors to think carefully about strategies for accomplishing those goals online and states instructors should "Feel free to reduce the overall content coverage focusing on the central topics" while using effective approaches to engaging students and assessing their learning in the online environment.
