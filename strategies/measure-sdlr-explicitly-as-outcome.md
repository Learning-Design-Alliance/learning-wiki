# Measure self-directed learning readiness explicitly as an outcome rather than interpreting it from other assessment metrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because conventional evaluation techniques did not provide a definitive assessment of students' self-directed learning readiness, the author recommends that propensity for self-directed learning be measured explicitly rather than interpreted from other metrics. The literature indicates fostering students' self-directed learning skills should be a top priority for medical schools, "thereby suggesting a need for a reliable and pragmatic approach to assessing them as an outcome." Suggested future work includes administering the SDLRS in the first year and again before graduation to gauge change over the curriculum.
