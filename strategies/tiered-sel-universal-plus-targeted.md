# Pair universal SEL programs with targeted individual instruction for students with more intense needs

## Description
The article recommends examining "a tiered approach to SEL by exploring the differential effects of a universal program versus a universal program coupled with targeted interventions for individuals with more intense needs." Because low social and communication skills predict bullying involvement among youth with disabilities, some students may need more individualized or direct instruction beyond what universal SEL provides.

## Design Implications

### Context
