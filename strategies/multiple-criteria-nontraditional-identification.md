# Use multiple criteria and non-traditional measures to identify diverse gifted learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The essay advocates restructuring identification to recognize "cultural diversity as differences, not deficiencies," noting "The use of multiple criteria and non-traditional measures is widely advocated" (Passow & Frasier, 1996). Suggested practices include observing students in classrooms with checklists and working with students on activities requiring problem-solving or creativity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practitioners must acknowledge that standardized tests are often culturally biased and that mainstream culture is not representative of all culture
#### Constraints
- Nonverbal ability tests such as the Naglieri Nonverbal Ability Test and Universal Nonverbal Test must be modified to be dynamic or coupled with dynamic assessment measures

### Target Learners
- culturally and linguistically diverse students potentially gifted

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate identification of intellectual potential typically overlooked by biased traditional evaluation
