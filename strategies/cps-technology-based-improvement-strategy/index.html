Use technology-based CPS with prompted questions and conceptual feedback to reduce writing burden¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on implementation difficulties, the authors suggest implementing CPS via a web-based program that presents problems, queries students about applicable concepts with selections provided, and delivers conceptual feedback. They state this "would streamline the analysis and reduce student frustration during times where they were stuck with the paper-and-pencil version" and add flexibility for teachers.
