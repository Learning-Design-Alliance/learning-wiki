# Include opportunities for mini-breaks during lessons, such as drawing, music, games, stretching, or going outside

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends "incorporating short breaks like drawing, listening to music, playing games, stretching, or going outside during lessons," which it says makes it easier for students to learn and digest new information and allows space for creative ideas to develop. It notes breaks also reduce lesson length, which can increase focus and engagement once students return to learning, citing research on off-task behavior and short school breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Breaks embedded within lessons rather than added as separate time

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, elementary through high school

### Target Learning Goals
- Focus, engagement, and digestion of new information during lessons
