## Description
Based on the finding that inquiry-based instruction promoted argumentation skills, the authors recommend that teacher educators model inquiry-based instruction incorporating argumentation skills, provide in-service education on inquiry-based instruction and student argumentation development, and develop inquiry-based curricula and lesson plans. The article states "teacher educators should model inquiry –based instruction and incorporate argumentation skills." Practitioners are advised to attend NATAA professional development workshops and to include direct instruction on argumentation skills using agricultural contextual problems supported by research data.
