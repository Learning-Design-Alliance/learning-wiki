# Make the state of pending administrative processes visible to participants to remove status-enquiry volume

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A design recommendation drawn from the study's finding that at least a fifth of queries ask where a pending submission has reached rather than request information. The authors recommend that "Making the state of a pending process visible to participants would remove the largest single source of volume without anyone answering more questions", drawing on service-operations evidence that operational transparency changes how waiting is experienced even when duration is unchanged. A participant who can see a certificate is received and queued for checking already holds the information the query would have sought.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A process whose pending state can be surfaced to the individual participant (e.g. received, queued for checking)
#### Constraints
- Applies to status-enquiry demand, whose answers are specific to one participant and change from day to day; it does not address procedural or conceptual questions

### Target Learners
- Participants in online programmes with multi-step, staff-verified onboarding workflows

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing avoidable help-seeking about pending administrative steps during onboarding
