# Develop OSCE scenarios with communication as the core of the clinical encounter, built on validated communication models taught during training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Participants suggested "developing specific OSCE scenarios where communication would be the core of the clinical encounter", proposing specific communication situations such as breaking bad news or a motivational interview. To ensure content validity, scenarios should be built on validated communication models such as the SPIKES model, corresponding to those taught during medical training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selected communication models must correspond to those taught during medical training.
- Communication experts should be paired with clinical experts in the case-writing process, since not all clinicians would be familiar with these concepts.
