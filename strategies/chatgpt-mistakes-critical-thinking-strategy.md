# Use ChatGPT's mistakes as material for cultivating critical thinking rather than banning the chatbot

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends integrating ChatGPT into teaching and learning instead of forbidding it, arguing that its frequent errors create "an excellent opportunity for further discussion on cultivating critical thinking among learners". Students can critically examine chatbot output, identify where calculations go wrong, and compare the confusion between eigenvalues and singular values with mistakes commonly made by learners themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be prepared to discuss and diagnose the chatbot's errors with students
- Students need sufficient basic linear algebra skills to check the chatbot's steps
#### Constraints
- Academically weak students may simply accept incorrect chatbot output, which the article flags as a risk
- Responses from the early-stage March 2023 version may no longer be relevant as versions improve

### Target Learners
- undergraduate linear algebra students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- algorithmic thinking in matrix factorization
