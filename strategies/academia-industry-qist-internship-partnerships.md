# Partner with companies and national laboratories to provide students internships, networking, and research experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond in-house research experiences and capstone courses, the article recommends that postsecondary educators "consider partnering with companies and national laboratories to provide students internship and networking opportunities". Such partnerships can excite students about QIST careers, give them a well-rounded understanding of non-academic work, and help develop degree programs and curricula that balance academic rigor with workplace needs.
