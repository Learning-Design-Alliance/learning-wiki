# Use the first day of class to set tone, share enthusiasm, involve students in discussions and boost motivation through relatable contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends treating the first lecture as crucial because "it sets the tone for the subsequent sessions". The teacher should share interest and excitement about the subject, incite curiosity about upcoming topics, create a positive and relaxed atmosphere, involve students in discussions about module content, and boost motivation by placing physics topics in contexts students can relate to, such as medicine, engineering or everyday life.
