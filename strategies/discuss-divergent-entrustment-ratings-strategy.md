# Promote discussion of divergent trainee-supervisor entrustment ratings to improve the feedback conversation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the entrustment scale functions as a shared standard and self-assessment is a social learning strategy, the authors argue the discussion of the reasoning behind each party's rating is likely to be even more important than the ratings themselves. Divergent ratings signal either that one party was unaware of a vital aspect of performance or that the scale was interpreted differently, making them "a valuable opportunity for reflection and discussion". The article recommends that training bodies promote such discussion to enhance trainee learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainee and supervisor ratings and comments must be finalised separately prior to any discussion so divergence is genuine
#### Constraints
- The study did not capture the discussion itself; the recommendation rests on analysis of written comments only

### Target Learners
- postgraduate anaesthesia trainees and their supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- calibrating self-assessment and judgement
- improving feedback conversations
- shared understanding of performance standards

### Affordances
- Entrustment Scale Shared Standard Scaffold
