# Collaborative Creation of Exemplars

## Description
After completing collaborative problem-solving tasks, learners examine leveled exemplars of responses and identify the characteristics of each level. They then create posters or anchor charts using their own work to illustrate the differences between the levels. This activity promotes metacognition, self-assessment, and a shared understanding of quality work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exemplars of varying quality, materials for creating posters or anchor charts, student work samples, time for discussion and creation
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of exemplars to ensure clear differentiation between levels. Some learners may struggle with self-assessment and providing constructive feedback.

### Personalization
- Exemplars can be leveled to match the learners' current skill level. Learners can also choose which pieces of their work to use as examples, allowing for individual expression and reflection.
