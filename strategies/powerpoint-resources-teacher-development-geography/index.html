Provide PowerPoint resources and teacher development for Geography instruction in Nigerian secondary schools¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that PowerPoint-taught students achieved more, the authors recommend two implementable actions for Geography education in Nigeria: making integration resources available and accessible, and building teacher competence through professional development. They write that "the resources for the effe ctive integration of PowerPoint in the teaching and learning of Geography in Nigerian secondary schools should be made available and accessible for teachers" and that Geography teachers should be exposed to professional development programmes enhancing PowerPoint integration competence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Availability and accessibility of PowerPoint resources (laptop, LCD projector, presentation content)
  • Professional teacher development programmes on PowerPoint integration

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one quasi-experiment in two purposively selected schools; the study notes findings on PowerPoint effects were previously inconclusive

Target Learners¶

  • Senior secondary school Geography students in Nigeria

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved cognitive achievement in Geography concepts

Claims¶

  • Senior secondary Geography students taught with PowerPoint presentation scored significantly higher on post-test achievement than students taught with the lecture method [+W]
  • The experimental group's Geography achievement scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test after PowerPoint instruction [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Equip Nigerian schools with PowerPoint presentation facilities and train teachers to use them for technology-based subjects
