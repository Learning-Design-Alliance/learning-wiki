# Use Taylor series kinematics as an enrichment topic where local sequencing permits, rather than to introduce kinematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author explicitly advises against using Taylor series to introduce kinematics, recommending instead that it "make connections and expand context." Because most introductory physics students meet Taylor series in calculus only after the kinematics unit, he suggests engineering alternative connection opportunities contingent on local conditions, such as a recitation section or the post-AP-exam phase of AP Physics C. He also notes instructors teaching both AP Physics C and AP Calculus BC may have expanded opportunities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have sufficient calculus background for the chosen placement
#### Constraints
- Opportunities are necessarily contingent on local conditions because most students encounter Taylor series well after the kinematics unit

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students
- high school AP Physics C students

### Target Learning Goals
- connect Taylor series to kinematics
- deepen understanding of familiar kinematic equations
