# Incorporate perspective taking experiences and exercises into teacher education and professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that perspective taking was the stronger predictor of CRT, the authors recommend incorporating "perspective taking experiences and exercises into teacher education and professional development programmes". Following Warren (2018), they suggest teachers read texts by and about culturally and linguistically diverse populations, participate in the social worlds of cultural communities different from their own, and engage in regular critical dialogue with colleagues about their reactions towards students and families.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experiences should be accompanied by regular critical dialogue with colleagues, with introspection on emotional, behavioural, and cognitive reactions towards students and their families as its basis
#### Constraints
- The article notes these recommendations rest on self-report findings from one Dutch study; actual classroom CRT practices were not investigated

### Target Learners
- in-service and pre-service primary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- culturally and socially sensitive teaching; perspective taking ability; multicultural attitudes
