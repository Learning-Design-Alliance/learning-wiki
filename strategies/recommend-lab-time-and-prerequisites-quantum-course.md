# Recommend dedicated weekly programming lab time and explicit prerequisites in future quantum programming courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
From their lessons learned, the authors "highly recommend a required weekly 90 or 120 minute programming section" with in-person access to an instructor with quantum computing expertise, since the course lacked a dedicated lab and relied on self-learning. They also recommend listing linear algebra and programming as prerequisites to set expectations, and removing up to three topics to enable deeper understanding of core quantum algorithms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduled lab time with an instructor who has quantum computing expertise
- Prerequisites of linear algebra and programming listed for the course
#### Constraints
- The authors found students struggled with advanced topics such as quantum simulation and quantum chemistry, which require domain-specific knowledge and advanced mathematics

### Target Learners
- undergraduate computer science students

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering fundamentals of quantum programming
- deeper understanding of core quantum algorithms
