# Elicit and critically engage with media-derived claims as a myth-busting approach to media-inflated preconceptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors describe instructors eliciting and then critically engaging with claims students have absorbed from popular media, analogous to the "elicit, confront, resolve" approach from mechanics PER. Edwin dispels the myth that quantum computation involves magically following every path simultaneously; Carl aims to produce graduates who can recognize when someone "tries to sell them snake oil." The authors call this a powerful approach for disentangling truth from marketing in all physics contexts.
