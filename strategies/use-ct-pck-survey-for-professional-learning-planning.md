# Use the CT–PCK Survey to identify professional learning needs and evaluate CT-integration professional development, not for teacher evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends school and district leaders use the CT–PCK Survey "to identify teachers' strengths and opportunities for professional growth in CT integration" and researchers use it "as an outcome measure in intervention studies designed to improve CT integration." It is positioned as a brief (under 20 minutes in shortened form) diagnostic for guiding professional learning and continuous improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration to in-service middle school math teachers; interpretation should rely on the overall 16-item score rather than flagged individual items
#### Constraints
- The report states the survey "should be used only to help guide teachers' integration of CT practices into their instruction or to inform continuous improvement in professional development," not for evaluation of teachers

### Target Learners
- Middle school math teachers in professional learning programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Integrating core computational thinking practices into math instruction
