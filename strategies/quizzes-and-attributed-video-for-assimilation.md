# Use attributed borrowed videos and quizzes in outreach sections to attract younger students and support assimilation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A presentation strategy for the resource's News, Science, Experiments and Projects sections: short text materials, videos borrowed from other sources with attribution, and quizzes. The sections "aim to attract younger students and schoolchildren" and include "quizzes for better assimilation of the material", pairing outreach content with low-stakes self-checking so that schoolchildren can engage with material about the Baikal-region experiments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Text and video materials, with borrowed videos referring to their source, plus quizzes for the material
#### Constraints
- Video materials are borrowed from other sources, so their use depends on referring to the source

### Target Learners
- younger students
- schoolchildren

### Target Learning Goals
- assimilation of astronomy and astroparticle physics material
- attraction to astroparticle physics
