# Make Learning Relevant

## Description
Teachers can boost student engagement and knowledge retention by connecting learning to students' interests, talents, learning styles, and sense of ownership in the education process. Teachers can give students a say in how the classroom operates, thus increasing students' sense of ownership in the education process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding students' talents, interests, and learning styles. Willingness to adjust teaching methods and strategies. Classroom discussions to allow students to provide input.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to understand individual student needs. May be challenging to accommodate all interests and preferences.
