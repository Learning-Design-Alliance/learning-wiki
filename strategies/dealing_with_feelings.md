# Dealing with Feelings

## Description
Adults can learn to deal with the emotions that arise when they make mistakes, even if they were not sufficiently guided as children. This involves creating a safe environment to express feelings without judgment, allowing one's 'inner child' to be seen and heard. This includes feeling emotions like sadness, anger, and shame, expressing what happened and why these feelings arise, listening without judgment, and comforting oneself. Writing can help express these feelings. By releasing tension, one can look at the situation more peacefully and honestly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A safe, private environment. Time for self-reflection and expression. Writing materials, if needed.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for individuals with deep-seated emotional issues or trauma, potentially requiring professional support.
