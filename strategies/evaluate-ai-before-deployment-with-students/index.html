Evaluate AI systems before deploying them with students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that edtech teams evaluate their AI models and outputs before going live with students, because failures like the LAUSD "Ed" chatbot and BuzzFeed's AI-first pivot show the costs of deploying without robust evaluation. The authors write: "Evaluate before you deploy with students." Buyers are increasingly asking vendors to demonstrate how well their AI actually works.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teams must build in tests that can provide convincing answers to buyers about AI performance

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students using AI-enabled edtech tools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Ensuring AI-generated feedback, scoring, and personalized learning content is fit for purpose before student exposure

  • Use an LLM web agent to compare potential lesson designs and get fast, low-cost feedback before deployment
