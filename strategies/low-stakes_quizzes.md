# Low-Stakes Quizzes

## Description
Low-stakes quizzes are brief assessments designed to help learners gauge their understanding without the pressure of high grades. These quizzes reduce test anxiety, prompt honest reflection, and improve focus and performance. Timing is important, with quizzes given right after a lesson and revisited throughout the school year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to assessment tools and minimal grading effort. Should be low-impact on overall grades.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful design to avoid increasing anxiety. Needs to be integrated with other learning activities.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, effective for K–12 and adult learners.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Gauge understanding, identify strengths and weaknesses, and reduce test anxiety.

### Affordances
- Reduces reliance on superficial learning strategies like rereading, promotes honest self-reflection, and improves focus.

### Personalization
- Can be delivered on paper, in Google Forms, with clickers, or using apps like Kahoot! or Quizziz.
