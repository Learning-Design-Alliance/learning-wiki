# Use faculty development workshops to standardize TBL facilitation with diverse team composition and case integration

## Description
The article recommends faculty development workshops to standardize how TBL sessions are facilitated, with particular emphasis on diverse team composition and the integration of clinical cases across disciplines. It states that "faculty development workshops are fully recommended to standardize TBL facilitation, with an emphasis on diverse team composition and case integration." This addresses the novelty challenges the authors observed, such as student discomfort in team-based activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators familiar with the three-phase TBL structure
- Deliberately diverse teams drawing from multiple health programs
#### Constraints
- The article notes challenges may be partially attributed to the novelty of TBL sessions at the institution

### Target Learners
- Faculty and instructors facilitating TBL in health-related programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective TBL session facilitation
- Supporting students in interdisciplinary team work
