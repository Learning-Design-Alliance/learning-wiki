# Incorporate SNAPPS into existing pathology case discussions and slide review sessions rather than separate teaching sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding SNAPPS in routine academic activities: "SNAPPS can be incorporated into existing academic activities rather than requiring separate teaching sessions." Because pathology residents already take part in detailed case discussions and slide reviews, preceptors can apply the six steps there, and the authors argue familiarity will reduce the time burden over practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preceptors sensitised to the SNAPPS model and calibrated on checklist scoring
#### Constraints
- Residents initially perceived the method as relatively time-consuming

### Target Learners
- postgraduate pathology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- active participation in case discussions
