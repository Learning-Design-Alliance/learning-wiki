# Provide synthesized and customized evidence tailored to the state context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
R15CC grounded participants' learning and decision-making in research by providing a literature review, scans of other states' approaches to ESSA requirements, and explanations of the strength of the research base "customized to the workgroup's needs." Facilitators also reviewed NDE CSI school improvement data and ran activities to examine and draw meaning from research to inform intervention selection decisions.
