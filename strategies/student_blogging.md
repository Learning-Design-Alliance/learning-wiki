# Student Blogging

## Description
Student blogs allow time to reflect on their mathematical process. Students can blog about how they solved a particular problem and what steps were needed. Blog explanations are especially useful when explaining solutions to problem-based learning activities. It’s also a stellar documentation tool. Keep in mind that the blogs may be public and not all students want to wave their mistakes in the air.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to blogs, time for blogging, and a way for students to share their thinking.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to blogs and may not be appropriate for all students.
