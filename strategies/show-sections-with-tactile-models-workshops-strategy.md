# Use individual show sections combined with tactile models to create accessible interactive workshops, and design future astronomy soundtracks to aid accessible communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
As next steps, the authors plan to "use individual sections of the show, in combination with tactile models, to create a series of accessible interactive workshops." They also hope future astronomy shows will consider how their soundtracks can be used effectively to aid accessible communication and that multi-sensory techniques become the norm when communicating astronomy to the general public.
