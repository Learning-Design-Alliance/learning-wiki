Develop new feedback models that operationalize the intricacies of the feedback phenomenon¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy, the first of the article's five steps, directs feedback researchers to build new theoretical models that capture the phenomenon's complexity. The article argues "there is a need to develop new models that thoroughly explore and operationalize the intricacies of the feedback phenomenon". It functions as a research-agenda recommendation for the field rather than a classroom technique, and it is motivated by the special issue's finding that feedback is a key educational factor.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researchers must construct models that both explore and operationalize the intricacies of feedback, per the article's stated need

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • feedback researchers and educational psychologists

Target Learning Goals¶

  • advancing theory and research design on assessment feedback

Claims¶

Related Strategies¶

  • Place the feedback agent at the core of the feedback process, attending to individual differences
  • Conceptualize feedback as a dynamic process and collect data that directly investigates that process
  • Leverage self-regulated learning measurement methods in feedback research
  • Employ multimodal methods to capture the multifaceted feedback process
  • Provide personalized feedback at different points of the learning process using a range of feedback functions and forms
