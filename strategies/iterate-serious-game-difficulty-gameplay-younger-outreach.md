# Iterate serious games by increasing difficulty and gameplay and extend outreach to younger demographics based on player feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on survey feedback, the authors recommend two forward-looking actions: future versions should increase difficulty and gameplay, since some respondents "expressed a desire for enhanced gameplay features and increased entertainment value," and outreach to younger demographics could be beneficial because teenagers (15.6% of respondents) may develop early interest in health care careers. Future iterations aim to integrate more engaging mechanics while preserving educational depth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A postgame feedback survey to identify desired improvements
#### Constraints
- Changes must preserve educational depth while adding engaging mechanics

- the general public

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement with neonatal care content
- early interest in health care careers
