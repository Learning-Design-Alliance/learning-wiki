# Incorporate suboptimal viewing scenarios and team training into camera navigation training programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the discussion the authors recommend extending camera navigation training beyond basic skills: "It might be relevant to incorporate scenarios with suboptimal viewing conditions" so that navigators can identify and understand the consequences of poor visualization. They also suggest examining simulation-based camera navigation training within team training, where the camera navigator and surgeon practice simultaneously, since team training "appears to be beneficial and has been shown to both shorten the learning curve and improve outcome."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further research into the relevance of simulation-based camera navigation training is necessary
#### Constraints
- The article reports transfer to the clinical setting could not be demonstrated, so the value of such programs needs further study

### Target Learners
- medical students and junior residents acting as operating assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- camera navigation skills
- identification of suboptimal viewing conditions
- non-technical team skills
