# Keep CDA instruction focused on the reading-comprehension goal and simplify the ten-question model's difficult concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its limitations and suggestions, the article advises teachers that highlighting ideology, power, and political issues "may distract the students from the main educational goal of enhancing reading comprehension skills", so attention should stay on the educational goal rather than political and ideological discussion. It also recommends that experts simplify the ten-question model, providing easier classifications with simpler ideological terms to lower learners' cognitive workload, and that further studies test the model's effectiveness for critical reading development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simplified classification schemes with simpler ideological terms provided to learners
#### Constraints
- The article states the study was conducted at only one university, so more extensive surveys across several universities are suggested for reliability

### Target Learners
- undergraduate EFL English-major students

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension enhancement without distraction into ideological debate
