# Use linked cross-system data to target programs such as dropout prevention and postsecondary preparation for students in foster care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's authors recommend that child welfare professionals, educators, and policymakers use cross-system data to inform interventions for students in foster care. Berliner states: "Now that we have solid data, we have a chance to really understand the specific risks and challenges that students in foster care face, so we can target programs and support—like dropout prevention and postsecondary preparation—to help them succeed in school and in life."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The linked cross-system dataset produced through the CDSS-CDE data-sharing agreement
#### Constraints
- The article notes the job of mining the dataset is not yet done, and Part 2 of the report examining relationships between foster care experience characteristics and education outcomes was still in development

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in foster care

### Target Learning Goals
- High school completion, dropout prevention, and postsecondary preparation
