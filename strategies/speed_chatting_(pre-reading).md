# Speed Chatting (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Speed chatting is a pre-reading activity where learners form two rows facing each other and ask simple questions related to the reading topic. They have a limited time (e.g., 60 seconds) to discuss each question with their partner before one row rotates, creating new pairs. This process is repeated several times to encourage quick, engaging interactions and activate prior knowledge about the topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a clear space for learners to form rows and pre-prepared simple questions related to the reading topic.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for learners who are shy or have difficulty speaking under time pressure. The effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the questions.

### Personalization
- Questions can be tailored to different proficiency levels, making it accessible for diverse learners. The number of rotations can be adjusted based on time and engagement.
