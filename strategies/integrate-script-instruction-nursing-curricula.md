# Intentionally integrate script-based clinical reasoning instruction into nursing curricula, with explicit teaching of clinical data organisation into scripts

## Description
In the implications section the authors state: "These collective findings advocate for the intentional integration of script-based clinical reasoning instruction into nursing curricula." They argue that by explicitly teaching learners to organise clinical data into scripts, educators can accelerate the transition from novice to competent decision-makers, and they recommend future research on longitudinal skill retention and implementation challenges in clinical training environments.
