District lessons for launching teacher-of-color support initiatives: safe space, clear goals, buy-in, and relationship time¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In its Implications section, the report distills advice from initiative participants for other districts. Contributors emphasized "building and establishing a safe space where people can be their authentic selves" as integral; an Admiral Squad educator advised "Define your goals. What makes your district special?" and to "Try to get buy-in"; and a SPECTRA teacher recommended creating space and time to meet together and identifying stakeholders with connections who can bring in human capital.

Requirements¶

  • Leadership in place and properly scheduled professional development days, per interviewee advice

Constraints¶

  • Lessons are drawn from four case studies; the report does not test their generalizability

Target Learners¶

  • District leaders and educators replicating teacher-of-color initiatives

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Designing and sustaining recruitment and retention programs for teachers of color

Affordances¶

  • Inclusive Innovation Model Digital Promise

Related Strategies¶

  • Before a study, researchers should present the proposal to district administration, hold conversations with administrators and teachers, and host parent events to build buy-in
  • Design future cross-sector collaboration efforts with clear goals and sufficient time period and scope, and include teacher-level activities
  • Retention supports for teachers of color: networking platforms, safe spaces, mental health resources, race-conscious mentorship, and flexible career pathways
  • Build authentic stakeholder buy-in through relational strategies: show don't tell, slow down to speed up, engage resistors, and asset framing
