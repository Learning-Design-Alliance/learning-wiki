# Heterogeneous team grouping with peer tutoring within CBI lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends organizing CBI classes into small heterogeneous teams: thirty learners were grouped into five teams of six, seeded so that "the first five highest scorers formed the first members of the five teams" and remaining scorers were distributed to maintain within-team heterogeneity. The author reports that one CBI lesson plan "has built-in peer tutoring and support within the heterogeneous class groupings", eliminating the need for several different plans for diverse students, and that CBI "can be used successfully for students of diverse abilities".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Achievement-score data to distribute learners across teams
- Group research tasks and information sources for each team
- A culminating product such as a group report
#### Constraints
- Team composition was made on the basis of learners' achievement scores

- students of diverse abilities in language classes

### Target Learning Goals
