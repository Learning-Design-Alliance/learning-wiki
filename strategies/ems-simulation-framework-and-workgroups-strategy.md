# Develop an EMS-specific simulation best-practices framework and regional/national faculty collaboration groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that program directors ensure faculty receive 'adequate initial and ongoing simulation education, mentors to assist with adoption of new technologies, and sufficient personnel resources and equipment (representing patients of all ages)'. It further proposes regional and national simulation work groups for faculty collaboration, and states that because no specific framework exists for the prehospital environment, 'The EMS community should collaborate to provide that framework.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrator support for faculty education and personnel resources; collaboration among EMS educators at regional and national levels.
#### Constraints
- The article notes existing standardized resources train nursing faculty and generic healthcare competencies exist, but none is geared to the unique prehospital environment.

### Target Learners
- paramedic program faculty and program directors

### Target Learning Goals
- effective adoption and integration of simulation in initial paramedic education
