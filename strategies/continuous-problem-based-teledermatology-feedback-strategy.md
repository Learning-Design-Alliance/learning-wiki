# Pair a one-day structured training course with a year of continuous problem-based feedback on real practice cases to build diagnostic competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's design recommendation is a full day training course containing "structured lectures and interactive problem based teaching", followed for the intervention group by a year of continuous problem based feedback on photographs from daily consultations. The authors justify the combination by citing that continuing medical education has effects superior to single interventions, and expect the feedback loop to produce a long-term effect the single course alone would not sustain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dermatologist capacity to give continuous feedback on all submitted lesion pictures
- GP access to a digital camera and Lumio loupe and willingness to submit pictures
#### Constraints
- The strategy was not yet tested at publication; outcomes are stated expectations of an ongoing protocol
- GPs cannot be blinded to the intervention condition

### Target Learners
- general practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- competence in the diagnosis of skin cancer
