Architect teacher-in-the-loop agentic AI with escalation protocols, guardrail adjustability, and state-interruptibility¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends an agentic educational architecture that includes the teacher as an active coordinator of the agent's state machine rather than a passive observer of student-chatbot interactions. It specifies three mechanisms: escalation protocols where the agent pauses and yields control to the teacher on predefined issues or thresholds; purpose and guardrail adjustability so teachers can adjust each agent's goals, prompt boundaries, tool access, and autonomy levels in real time; and state-interruptibility giving educators power to manually override an agent's planned trajectory mid-session. Embedding these in the agent's execution cycle keeps the educator empowered as a pedagogical director.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Escalation triggers must be predefined, such as persistent student misconception loops, excessive frustration or misuse, or content requiring relational teaching or special sensitivity

Constraints¶

  • Applies to agentic educational architectures specifically; the article contrasts this with standard generative AI systems that relegate educators to passive observers

Target Learners¶

  • teachers coordinating AI-supported classrooms and their students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • preserving teacher professional judgment and pedagogical direction in AI-mediated learning
