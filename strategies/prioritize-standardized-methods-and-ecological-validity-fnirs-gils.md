# Prioritize standardized fNIRS methods, ecologically valid learning settings, and evaluation of real-time neural adaptation feasibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For future multimedia learning research and neuroadaptive systems, the review recommends a three-part direction. The article states: "Future research should prioritize standardized methods, ecologically valid learning settings, and evaluation of the feasibility and educational value of real-time neural adaptation." Standardization addresses the heterogeneous acquisition, preprocessing, and analysis pipelines found across the corpus; ecological validity addresses the laboratory-dominated settings; and the third direction targets the gap toward real-time adaptive multimedia learning systems identified in RQ2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bridging methodological and ecological gaps in existing fNIRS-GILS research to enable real-time adaptive multimedia learning systems
#### Constraints
- Current findings do not establish that specific multimedia platforms cause particular neural or learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- learners across multimedia and game-integrated learning contexts, including inclusive designs for learners with cognitive or developmental needs

### Target Learning Goals
- valid and generalizable measurement of neural and learning outcomes in game-integrated learning environments
