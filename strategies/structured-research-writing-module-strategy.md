# Embed a structured research protocol writing module with collaborative and reflective practices in the core medical curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured modules in the core medical curriculum focusing on research writing skills with reflective practices embedded in a collaborative learning format. Students work in small heterogeneous groups to develop research protocols through an evidence based approach, then write individual reflective summaries in academic portfolios. The authors state this can foster critical thinking and collaborative learning among undergraduate medical students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small heterogeneous groups formed by random allocation
- A standard protocol template with defined headings
- An orientation session on electronic literature search
- Individual reflective summaries in portfolios after the activity
#### Constraints
- Time constraints and intra-group conflicts were reported as hindering factors
- No standardised critical thinking scale was used, so effects rest on self-report

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in clinical postings

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- collaborative learning
- research writing skills
