# VR-enhanced ultrasonography workshop combining Peyton's 4-step approach with 3D-model screenshot assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 6-hour practical workshop design in which both groups received an introductory lecture, a 60-minute anatomy screenshot assignment against a structure checklist, and three hands-on ultrasonography stations. The design was "based on Peyton's 4-step approach method optimized for ultrasonographic skill training"; the VR group captured screenshots from an interactive 3D model using a plane transection tool, while the control group used a digital atlas. Assessment used expert-consensus performance checklists developed in line with the ACEP 2016 POCUS policy statement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to VR hardware and anatomy software for the intervention arm; an electronic anatomy atlas for the control arm; live subjects and ultrasound equipment for the practical stations
#### Constraints
- Evaluated with novice learners on normal structures in healthy individuals; the impact on identifying pathological findings has not yet been determined

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students without previous ultrasonography training

### Target Learning Goals
- Ultrasonographic task performance and identification of anatomical structures in ultrasound and anatomical images
