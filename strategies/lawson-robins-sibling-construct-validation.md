# Use statistical and conceptual procedures to distinguish sibling constructs and compare sibling theories' predictions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that researchers make their definitions explicit and argue for what distinguishes any newly introduced construct from existing ones, citing Lawson and Robins (2021) as providing "statistical procedures and conceptual criteria" for identifying identical constructs that are nominally distinct and vice versa. The author extends this to comparing predictions of theories operating in the same area, which he calls sibling theories, arguing this is achievable with existing methodologies. Such detailed construct validation would need greater institutional support to flourish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clarity in definitions and an explicit argument for construct distinctiveness
- Institutional valuation of detailed construct validation work
#### Constraints
- Field dynamics currently incentivize novelty and splash over slower validation efforts
