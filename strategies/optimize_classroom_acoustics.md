# Optimize Classroom Acoustics

## Description
Reducing noise levels in classrooms can greatly affect teacher and student performance. Schools should be designed to buffer external noise from classrooms. The use of noise-dampening materials can mitigate internal sounds. Building classrooms with less external noise is positively associated with greater student engagement and achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Noise-dampening materials, building design considerations, noise monitoring equipment
#### Constraints
- May be costly to implement noise reduction measures in existing buildings. Some level of background noise may be necessary for social interaction.
