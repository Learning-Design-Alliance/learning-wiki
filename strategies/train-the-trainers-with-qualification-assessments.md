# Train local prospective trainers with assessments to build local training capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To meet local demand for large numbers of EFL teachers, the program recruited 21 EFL teachers with an English diploma and trained them as prospective trainers in phonics teaching, basic English courses and teaching method courses, plus trainer qualities such as designing training plans and creating a positive training environment. "A series of assessments were organized after each course, including oral test, written test, trial teaching and descriptive assessment to judge whether they could be qualified trainers." At the end, 15 attendees were confirmed as qualified trainers, 5 at provincial level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruit EFL teachers with an English diploma
- Assess trainees after each course before confirming them as qualified trainers
#### Constraints
- Prospective trainers expected more from the new phonics teaching method and reported anxiety about English teaching method before training

### Target Learners
- In-service EFL teachers with an English diploma in county education bureaus, teacher training schools and universities

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonics teaching and spelling rules
- Designing training plans and mastering training skills
