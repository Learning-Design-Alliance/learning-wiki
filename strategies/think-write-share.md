# Think-Write-Share

## Description
Think-Write-Share is a strategy that involves learners thinking about a question, writing down their responses, and then sharing their thoughts with the class. This allows every learner to engage with the material and encourages active participation. The written component ensures accountability, while the sharing component promotes discussion and revision based on feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A specific question, time for thinking and writing, and a method for sharing responses (e.g., document camera, whiteboard).
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the question and learners' willingness to share their thoughts. May require strategic selection of students to share to address misconceptions.
