# Build robots from electronic scraps and Arduino boards as a low-cost alternative where schools cannot afford robotics kits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Where commercial kits are unaffordable, the article recommends building robots from electronic scraps and using Arduino boards. The authors write that "the use of Arduino plates also presents itself as a cheap alternative to build eﬃcient robots with simpliﬁed programming that is easy to teach, and that have also a practical purpose." In workshops, students built insect robots on site from phone vibration motors, toothbrushes, batteries and wires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to electronic scraps and, for programmable robots, an Arduino prototyping platform with C/C++ programming support
#### Constraints
- Because a Educational Robotics Kit may have a high acquisition price, many schools in Brazil do not have the means to provide the necessary resources

### Target Learners
- high school students in Brazilian public schools
- undergraduate teacher-education (PIBID) fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- building electrical circuits and programming logic
- hands-on prototyping skills
