# Train paraprofessional tutors with one initial day of training plus two two-hour booster sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Number Rockets prepares non-specialist tutors — retired teachers and substitute teachers — with a light-touch training model: "Tutors receive one day of training before beginning, and two two-hour booster sessions afterwards." The scripted curriculum makes this brief training sufficient, and booster sessions reinforce practices once tutoring is underway.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scripted curriculum so tutors do not need to design instruction
- Scheduled booster sessions after tutoring begins
#### Constraints
- Training is provided through AIR and must be requested by type, grade level, number of trainees, and location

### Target Learners
- first-grade students struggling in math

### Target Learning Goals
- faithful delivery of the scripted math tutoring curriculum
