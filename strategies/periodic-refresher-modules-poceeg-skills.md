# Schedule periodic refresher modules to maintain point-of-care EEG skills after initial e-learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because skill and confidence decayed between posttest and the 3-month retention test, the authors recommend ongoing refresher training. They state that "the resurgence in guess rates at the 3-month follow-up suggests that periodic refresher modules or ongoing training might be necessary to maintain confidence and competence levels," and the conclusions stress "the critical need for continuous education and ongoing training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial training intervention establishing the skills, plus scheduled follow-up sessions before retention decays
#### Constraints
- Retention challenges are described as particularly pronounced in emergency department settings where critical care skills are infrequently used

### Target Learners
- Nonexpert pediatric emergency department staff trained in point-of-care EEG interpretation

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining EEG interpretation skill and confidence over months after initial training
