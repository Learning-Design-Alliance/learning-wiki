# Teach the delta function with construction prompts, integration practice in multiple coordinate systems, and explicit emphasis on its units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 5 studies (3 qualitative, 1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that instruction not assume prior familiarity with the delta function, since students are "unlikely to encounter the δ-function in their required mathematics courses". They suggest questions asking students to generate an expression from a description as a baseline assessment of activation, additional practice integrating 1D and 3D delta functions in multiple coordinate systems, and explicit emphasis on the fact that the delta function can have units and how to determine them from its argument.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to add A2-type construction prompts and integration practice beyond canonical delta-function questions
#### Constraints
- The study was not designed to investigate the impacts of different instructional strategies on the prevalence or persistence of these difficulties

### Target Learners
- upper-division (junior-level) physics students in electricity and magnetism

### Target Learning Goals
- activating, constructing, integrating, and checking delta-function expressions for charge densities
