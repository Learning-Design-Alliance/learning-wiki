# Integrate concept tests on time dependence of expectation values into lectures with peer discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors are developing reflective problems that complement quantitative problems and concept tests on time dependence of expectation values, similar to those popularized by Mazur. They recommend that "The concept tests on this topic can be integrated with lectures and students can be encouraged to take advantage of their peers’ expertise and learn from each other." This makes peer discussion of conceptual clicker-style questions an implementable in-lecture activity for upper-level quantum mechanics.
