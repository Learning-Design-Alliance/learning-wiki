# Extend a research program into a complex mentoring program with community events, educational workshops, and collaborative projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on survey feedback, the authors recommend adding community programs and educational sessions covering research and career-development knowledge transfer, linking research topics into collaborative projects, and adding personalized mentoring on application and proposal writing. They write that "Community programs and educational sessions were identiﬁed by the students as a top priority and should be considered as soon as the pandemic situation allows," and suggest extending support to doctoral students given inadequate Hungarian doctoral stipends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student demand for community and educational elements, as identified by survey
- Willingness of experienced researchers to help with proposal writing
#### Constraints
- Pandemic conditions delayed in-person community programs; Hungarian doctoral stipend levels are outside the program's control

### Target Learners
- undergraduate research students
- early-stage doctoral students

### Target Learning Goals
- career-path knowledge
- grant and proposal writing
- professional community building
