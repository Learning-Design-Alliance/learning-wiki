# Workshop-based mini-research project walking students through the full research cycle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a workshop, given to all new students and interns joining the group, requiring only an introductory quantum mechanics course. After a roughly 30-minute onboarding presentation, students spend 20-40 hours working through self-guided exercises and then an open-ended challenge: exciting a Bose-Einstein condensate from the ground to the first excited state. The goal is having students quickly work through hypothesis formulation, simulation design, data generation and analysis, and communication of results, ending in a 2-4 page report with instructor feedback.
