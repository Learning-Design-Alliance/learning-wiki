# Use ChatGPT to generate original, SLO-aligned homework problems tailored to class pacing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
In the studied course, the instructor used AI to generate homework assignments instead of conventional problem sets from published textbooks or online databases. The instructor "leveraged ChatGPT to generate original, SLO-aligned physics problems tailored to the specific needs and pacing of the class." The article reports this approach reduced the likelihood of academic dishonesty through online solution-sharing, encouraged deeper engagement with newly presented material, and allowed rapid iteration and refinement of homework questions in response to emerging student difficulties. A detailed discussion of this generative assessment framework is presented in a separate companion paper.
