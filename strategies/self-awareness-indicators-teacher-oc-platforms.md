# Offer self-awareness indicators about actions and contribution quality in teacher online community platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teacher online community platforms provide features giving members visibility into their own activity and the quality of their contributions. It states the results have "practical implications for teacher OC platforms, for example about the importance of offering and using features that provide self-awareness indicators about their actions and the quality of their contributions." This strategy targets platform developers and facilitators seeking to support engagement and quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supporting platform that logs user actions and contribution quality indicators
#### Constraints
- Results come from an online community in early stages, so they must be carefully analyzed regarding more mature communities

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- professional development through sharing and improving learning designs
