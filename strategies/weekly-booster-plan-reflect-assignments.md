# Optional weekly booster assignments to plan and reflect on study strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Optional extra-credit booster assignments gave students "weekly opportunities to plan and reflect on their study strategies": at week's start students listed three or more strategies they committed to using; at week's end they listed strategies actually used and reflected on how well they worked. This provided accountability for applying workshop content while preserving student choice, which the authors deemed important for motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Extra credit as incentive; instructor reminders a few times during the semester
#### Constraints
- Assignments were optional; the authors note mandatory reflective exercises could be counterproductive

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate introductory biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying workshop study strategies
- Self-regulation of learning
