# Adapt and tailor the triage simulation across program stages, modalities, and cohort needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors offer the simulation as a blueprint other programs can adopt and modify. "This triage simulation experience can be modified and adapted to be used for students at different points in their education and across in-person, hybrid, and distance-based programs." Scenarios can be tailored to content cohorts struggle with, conditions common in specific geographic areas, or emerging illnesses, and future scenarios should incorporate health equity and social determinants of health.
