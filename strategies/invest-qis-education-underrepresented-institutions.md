## Description
To close the disparities its regression documents, the article recommends investment in "QIS education programs at primarily-undergraduate institutions" and investigation of barriers at institutions with high enrollment of low-income students and at public institutions in rural states. It also calls on universities and funders to "support such grassroots initiatives wherever they emerge".
