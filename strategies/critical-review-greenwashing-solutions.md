# Critically evaluate advertised green building solutions to develop students' critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the urbanization session, proposed sustainable solutions are examined critically rather than presented as ready answers. The authors state that "some of the proposed solutions critically discussed to highlight the fact that none is ever perfect, but also to debunk some unreasonable performance promises or green-washing advertising." Students assess claims about shipping-container housing, air-filtering technologies, and energy sources.
