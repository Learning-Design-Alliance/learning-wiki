# Practical steps for building a pedagogical model in sport

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article offers practical recommendations aimed at building a pedagogical model in sports, particularly Russian lapta. The steps include "determination of the actual environment (competitive activity), where the studied process takes place", identification of external and internal factors and conditions governing the educational model, specification of the subjects of competitive activity and their functional characteristics, and development of indicators of technical and tactical skills expressed in space-time, quantitative and qualitative parameters.
