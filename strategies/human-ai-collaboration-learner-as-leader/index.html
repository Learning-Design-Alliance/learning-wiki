Human–AI collaboration design positioning students as leaders who assess AI-generated options¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes a framework aiming at an "AI-empowered, learner-as-leader learning goal, while protecting human rights and privacy". AI solves prediction problems by rapidly generating more options, while students assess these options and make appropriate decisions. The authors stress that predictions should be transparent and explainable, that biases in training data must be monitored, and that students must learn to align decisions with ethical, legal, and social norms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Predictions transparent and explainable to students
  • Monitoring and avoiding biases in predictions
  • Teacher guidance on the use of ChatGPT for coding support

Constraints¶

  • The study did not itself utilize ChatGPT or equivalent platforms

Target Learners¶

  • high school geography students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • data-informed decision making
  • ethical alignment of AI-supported decisions

Related Strategies¶

  • Seven-question scaffold with AI problem solving, game-based learning, and systems thinking to support proactive decision making
  • Involve teachers as co-designers of AI-based intelligent systems, with agency to train, correct, interpret, and override system recommendations
  • Safeguard student privacy in learning analytics by involving students in tool creation, building contextualized explainable configurable analytics, and empowering student agency
