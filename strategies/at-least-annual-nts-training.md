# Provide at least annual NTS training, aligned with aviation crew resource management practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Drawing on evidence about retraining frequency, the article points toward a minimum training cadence: "This points in the direction of at least annual training, similar to common practice for crew resource management (CRM) training in aviation." It cites findings that significant improvement persists at 6 and 12 months post-training and that neonatal resuscitation simulation studies favour low dose, high frequency training. The recommendation addresses the article's observation that duration and frequency of training varies and evidence on needed retraining frequency is limited but emerging.
