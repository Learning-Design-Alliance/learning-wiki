# Follow eight consultation guidelines when bringing Indigenous astronomy into planetarium programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper sets out eight guidelines for planetaria that want to include Indigenous content or develop Indigenous-specific programs, rather than building ad-hoc programs without community consultation. They include training staff in cultural competence, identifying local communities, due diligence, following established protocols, ensuring "constant consultation with the relevant community/tribe who must give final approval for all content and delivery options", ensuring mutual benefits, centering Indigenous voices, and producing a signed MoU.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A signed Memorandum of Understanding setting out permissions, program content and delivery, and future alterations
- final approval of all content by the representative Indigenous body
#### Constraints
- Some Indigenous knowledge may be sensitive or cannot be shared with non-tribal members; protocols vary from tribe to tribe and are regionally specific

### Target Learners
- Planetarium staff and educators
- museum professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Delivering culturally and ethically appropriate Indigenous astronomy programming
