# SEL Discussions on Ethical Dilemmas

## Description
This strategy involves using age-appropriate, real-life ethical dilemmas as discussion starters to help students practice responsible decision-making. It encourages students to identify problems, analyze situations, solve problems, and evaluate solutions while considering the well-being of themselves and others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a collection of age-appropriate ethical dilemmas and a teacher to facilitate discussion. Can be integrated into social studies, language arts, or advisory periods.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the selected dilemmas and the teacher's ability to guide discussion. Some students may struggle with ethical reasoning or expressing their opinions.

### Personalization
- Dilemmas can be selected to match students' age, interests, and cultural backgrounds. Teachers can provide scaffolds to support students who struggle with ethical reasoning or problem-solving.
