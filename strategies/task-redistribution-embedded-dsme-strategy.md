# Redistribute standardizable chronic care tasks to pre-existing community health workers and embed self-management education in routine consultations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends classifying primary diabetes care and self-management education/support activities by standardizability and required proficiency, then redistributing them to existing staff by expertise level: self-management support to barangay health workers during home visits, and one-on-one diabetes self-management education "embedded in the clinical consultation", making encounters biopsychosocial rather than purely biomedical. This uses pre-existing human resources without adding much strain to the health service.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained, pre-existing cadre of community-based workers (the BHW)
- Decision support via a basic training workshop on knowledge and skills
#### Constraints
- The article states continuous capacity building through refresher courses or supportive supervision will likely be needed to ensure long-term benefits

### Target Learners
- Adults with type 2 diabetes in community-based primary care
- Local government health unit staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Diabetes self-management knowledge and behaviors
- Self-care development and glycemic control
