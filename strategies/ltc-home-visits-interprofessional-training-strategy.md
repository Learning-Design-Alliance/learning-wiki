# Incorporate clinical training in long-term care facilities, home visits, and interprofessional education into geriatric dental curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the reviewed evidence, the review highlights concrete instructional strategies for geriatric dental education. It reports that "five studies underscored the importance of incorporating clinical training program, particularly in long-term care facilities setting, into undergraduate curricula." Related strategies include aging simulation, role-play, interactive online activities, home visits, and interprofessional education workshops, which studies reported improving empathy, attitudes, and collaborative-care understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical placements, simulation resources, trained supervisors, and interprofessional partners
#### Constraints
- Experiential approaches are resource-intensive and logistically challenging to implement, per the review

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students
- Postgraduate dental trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Empathy toward older adults, attitudes toward dementia and disability, and interprofessional collaborative care competencies
