# Teacher-Student Reading Conferences

## Description
Schedule regular conferences with students to get a sense of their independent reading and provide instructional support. Develop a plan for meeting with all students regularly, using a combination of scheduled and spontaneous conferences. Use these conferences to understand students' reading habits, offer individual guidance, and make notes about how students solve problems while reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduled time for conferences, tools for documenting student progress, and a plan for meeting with all students regularly.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant teacher time and effort. May be challenging to schedule regular conferences with all students. Some students may be hesitant to share their reading habits.
