# Use SP and OSCE stations with weighted scoring for summative clinical practice assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical assessment combines standardized patients with objective structured clinical examinations across four timed stations, scored out of 100 each and weighted 40%, 30%, 10% and 20%. Stations cover "clinical skills, clinical reasoning, interpretation of auxiliary examinations, and neurological examination", with psychiatric case questions drawn in a 5:3:2 ratio across diagnostic categories.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clinical competence assessment protocol for standardized psychiatric residency training as reference
- Standardized patients and multi-station OSCE logistics
- Station weighting scheme and pre-established case question bank
#### Constraints
- Assessment duration totals 70 minutes across the four stations, suited to psychiatry rotations

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate psychiatry interns

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills
- clinical reasoning
- interpretation of auxiliary examinations
- neurological examination
