# Graphic Organizers for Pre-Reading

## Description
Students identify the text structure (compare/contrast, cause/effect, etc.) before reading and complete a graphic organizer that supports that structure as they read. KWL charts help activate prior knowledge and set a purpose for reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates and reading materials.
#### Constraints
- Requires training to identify text structure. May oversimplify complex texts.
