# Develop academic buoyancy to benefit achievement of students with mild anxiety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications statement suggests "developing academic buoyancy can benefit the achievement of students with mild forms of anxiety", since buoyancy amplified performance most at low anxiety levels. Because the performance benefit of buoyancy declined at higher anxiety, buoyancy development is positioned as helpful for students facing everyday setbacks rather than severe anxiety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires students to experience only mild anxiety for buoyancy benefits to accrue
#### Constraints
- With higher anxiety, the differential benefit to performance offered by higher academic buoyancy diminished

### Target Learning Goals
