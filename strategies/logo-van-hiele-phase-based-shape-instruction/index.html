Teach two-dimensional shape properties through Logo construction activities sequenced by van Hiele phases¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's instructional approach integrates Logo programming activities with van Hiele-based phases: pupils name and recognize shapes, write commands to move the turtle, observe properties during Guided Orientation, present and discuss ideas during Explication, construct models in Free Orientation, and summarize properties in Integration. The authors recommend teachers "could use Logo as a teaching tool to create a meaningful lesson for pupils" and design instructional activities with Logo to increase interest in mathematical concepts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers should master the Logo programming language to guide pupils
  • Pupils need time to experience learning mathematics with Logo

Constraints¶

  • The article states pupils should be given more time to experience learning mathematics with Logo, given the short treatment period

Target Learners¶

  • Primary school pupils learning geometry

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding properties of two-dimensional shapes
  • Creative and higher-order thinking about geometric concepts

Affordances¶

  • Van Hiele Geometric Thinking Levels Logo Study

Claims¶

  • Year 4 pupils taught two-dimensional shape properties with Logo outperformed a traditionally taught control group on the post-test [+W]
  • Pupils reported generally positive perceptions of learning two-dimensional shape properties with Logo [+W]
  • Before treatment, the Logo and control groups showed no statistically significant difference in achievement on two-dimensional shapes [~W]
