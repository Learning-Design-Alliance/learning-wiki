# Deploy VR as a complement alongside a lecture rather than as a standalone replacement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's survey results support using VR together with accompanying instruction: "Most participants also agreed that VR is useful for teaching complicated concepts and that it is useful alongside a lecture, some strongly disagreed that VR could be used in teaching without an accompanying lecture, especially with a professional background." The authors add that earlier studies stress the importance of face-to-face interaction during the VR experience and that future VRE developments will consider such scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An accompanying lecture or face-to-face interaction and discussion of concepts during the VR experience
#### Constraints
- Some participants, especially professionals, strongly disagreed that VR could be used in teaching without an accompanying lecture

### Target Learners
- AEC students
- AEC professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of complicated spatial construction concepts
