# Use visits to and from local quantum companies to introduce students to the breadth of the industry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that quantum-industry-focused courses incorporate visits to, and visits from, local quantum companies during the first semester so students see what types of companies and jobs exist. Students in Q-Forge reported that these interactions made their picture of the industry concrete; Owen said the tours "made it really concrete that there's a lot of really cool stuff going on." Virtual visits are suggested for locations without local quantum companies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to local quantum companies willing to host or send representatives, or virtual visit alternatives
#### Constraints
- Visits alone left most students without detailed knowledge of specific job roles; the authors suggest companies discuss potential jobs in greater detail

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students preparing for the quantum workforce

### Target Learning Goals
- familiarity with the variety of quantum companies and career options
