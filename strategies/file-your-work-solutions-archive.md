# File your worked solutions in an organized personal archive

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The author recommends keeping copies of all worked solutions in labeled folders, recording when each problem was solved and, for hard problems, the wrong approaches tried and the time spent. Because submitted solutions may not be returned, he advises making two copies. He links this habit to Feynman, who 'had an extensive filing syste' and preserved his high-school notebooks, giving him 'an enormous storehouse of previous work'.
