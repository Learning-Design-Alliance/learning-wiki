# Grow the MCD item bank during operation using online calibration embedded alongside scored items

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To expand from 14 to 35 planned learning objectives without pausing testing, the authors recommend online calibration: "the system embeds new, unscored items alongside the operational item bank, collects real student responses to them during normal use, and estimates their DINA parameters from those responses." Once parameters stabilize, the item joins the scored bank, letting the bank grow while the MCD stays in use. They also recommend adding existing RBAs for uncovered topics and using accumulating EMCS responses to compare DINA against G-DINA and hierarchical alternatives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A live delivery platform with sufficient response volume for parameter estimates to stabilize
- Embedding unscored items during normal assessment use rather than a separate pretesting phase
