# Use hybrid human–AI feedback models that combine AI's and instructors' complementary strengths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose hybrid feedback models enabling human–AI collaboration in feedback provision, described as "a promising avenue for a scalable yet effective feedback practice." Two approaches are outlined: AI provides an initial round of feedback that instructors then review and enrich, or AI handles specific aspects such as syntax errors and formatting while instructors provide higher-order feedback on program logic and code efficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor capacity to review or complement AI-generated feedback; clear division of feedback responsibilities between AI and instructor
#### Constraints
- Presented as a design recommendation; the article did not test a hybrid model empirically

### Target Learners
- higher education students in large programming courses

### Target Learning Goals
- scalable and effective feedback on programming tasks
