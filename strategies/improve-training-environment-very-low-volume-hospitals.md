# Strengthen the training environment at Very Low-Volume hospitals through journal access, administrative support, and inter-hospital collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because residents at Very Low-Volume hospitals reported weaker training guidance systems, support systems, and journal availability, the authors recommend targeted interventions: "improving access to online journals, strengthening administrative support for training programs, and promoting inter-hospital educational collaboration." They also highlight enhancing the competencies of clinical training administrative staff, who coordinate departments, support residents, and provide adult education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical training administrative staff require skill development and institutional support to act as mentors
#### Constraints
- Findings are specific to community hospitals in Japan and may not generalize to university hospitals or other countries

### Target Learners
- PGY-1 and PGY-2 residents at small community-based training hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- improved quality of the clinical training environment
- self-directed learning through academic resource access
