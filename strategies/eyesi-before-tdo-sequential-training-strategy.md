# Sequence direct ophthalmoscopy training to begin with the Eyesi simulator before transitioning to the traditional ophthalmoscope

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "undergraduates practice with the Eyesi before transitioning to the traditional method, which is consistent with the preference of most participants in this study." In the trial, 75.59% of respondents felt inclined to practice with the Eyesi first before using TDO, and beginning with TDO was associated with reduced willingness to use the tool in future practice. The sequential approach is offered to enhance the learning experience and better prepare students for future clinical settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an Eyesi Direct Ophthalmoscope Simulator alongside traditional direct ophthalmoscope training within ophthalmic preclinical training
#### Constraints
- Transition from Eyesi to TDO poses challenges: communicating with patients, precise positioning with unstable eyes, varying pupil sizes, and possible confidence and anxiety issues

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- direct ophthalmoscopy fundus examination skill
