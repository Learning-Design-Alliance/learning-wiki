# Align testing policy and provide continuous support and coherent messages for curricular change adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that policymakers align all parts of the policy system with the curricular change and provide continuous support. As the article states, "from the testing policy to the college admission or teacher supervision, all should be rearranged according to curricular change at the macro-level". Support includes sufficient resources, physical conditions, appropriate professional development, and communication at horizontal and vertical levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coherence across testing policy, teacher education, and resource allocation with the curricular change
- Continuous long-term support rather than a single formal training session
#### Constraints
- Short-term, one-off training efforts risk becoming a waste of time and resources when change is newly initiated

### Target Learners
- K-12 EFL teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective implementation and adoption of communicative language teaching curricular reforms
