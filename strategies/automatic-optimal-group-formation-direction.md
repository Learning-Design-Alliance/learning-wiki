# Automatic optimal group formation balancing pedagogical objectives and pragmatic constraints

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editors identify automatic optimal group formation — forming groups from a pool of learners — as a key open challenge, noting it "is a challenging problem because of the need to satisfy many pedagogical objectives and pragmatic constraints." Pedagogical objectives drive composition in terms of distribution of learner skills, expertise and roles; pragmatic constraints include scheduling and limits on group size. They call for "publicly available datasets that can help define metrics for optimal groups" to advance the state of the art.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Publicly available datasets that can help define metrics for optimal groups
#### Constraints
- Pragmatic constraints include scheduling and observing limits on group size

### Target Learners
- learners in online and classroom settings with group projects

### Target Learning Goals
- effective group composition for learning
