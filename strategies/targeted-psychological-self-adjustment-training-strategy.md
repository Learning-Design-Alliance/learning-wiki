# Targeted training programs focusing on psychological self-adjustment for lower-competency nurse profiles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future competency-development programs address the weak psychological self-adjustment domain, "including emotional support, stress-coping resources, and resilience-oriented training", and suggests "targeted programs focusing on psychological self-adjustment may be explored in future longitudinal or interventional studies". Because the low-competency profile scored only 16.2 points in ethical care, the authors also suggest support in ethical communication, patient preference clarification, and multidisciplinary decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal or interventional studies to establish whether such programs effectively enhance competency
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design precludes causal inferences; findings should inform rather than definitively establish targeted competency-development strategies

### Target Learners
- oncology nurses in low- and moderate-competency profiles

### Target Learning Goals
- psychological self-adjustment
- ethical communication and decision-making
- resilience and stress coping
