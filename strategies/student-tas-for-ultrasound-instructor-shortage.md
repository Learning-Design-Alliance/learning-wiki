# Use trained student teaching assistants, with a pre-course didactical workshop, to expand hands-on ultrasound training despite instructor shortages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends replacing or supplementing scarce expert instructors with previously trained student teaching assistants for basic cardiac ultrasound instruction, arguing this is "a feasible solution for the shortage of available expert CU instructors and may reduce teaching-associated costs". It also recommends that future courses include a pre-course didactical workshop to equalize teaching ability between instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- TAs must have previously completed the introductory ultrasound course plus additional clinical, technical and methodology preparation to assure a similar standard of teaching.
#### Constraints
- The article limits this to basic image acquisition of normal anatomy; advanced techniques and pathology interpretation may exceed TAs' abilities.

### Target Learners
- medical students in their first clinical year

### Target Learning Goals
- basic cardiac ultrasound image acquisition across six standard views

### Affordances
- Curse Of Knowledge Peer Teaching Advantage
