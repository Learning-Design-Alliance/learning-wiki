# Prioritise longitudinal research and cost analysis when evaluating digital clinical skills modalities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future work assess long-term impact and economics of digital and blended learning. It states "Future research should focus on longitudinal studies to assess the long-term impact of digital and blended learning on clinical skills and professional competencies", and notes the lack of detailed cost analyses in most studies limits economic assessment. It also recommends exploring the optimal balance between digital and traditional methods and factors influencing student preferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must measure academic performance, not satisfaction alone, to meet the review's inclusion standard
#### Constraints
- The review excluded virtual reality technologies because they typically require face-to-face delivery and specialised equipment

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- long-term acquisition of clinical skills and professional competencies
