# Organize full-distance learning in SageMathCloud through the Manage a Course course manager

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends organizing full-distance learning in SageMathCloud with the course manager (Manage a Course), in which the structured (folder-distributed) components of the cloud-oriented program-methodical complex serve as its defining content elements. This turns the complex into a distance learning course with mediated management and control of learning. The strategy follows from the article's enumeration of course-organization capabilities such as adding students and monitoring their progress.
