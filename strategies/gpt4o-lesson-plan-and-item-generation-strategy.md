# Use GPT-4o to assist instructors in drafting lesson plans and generating test questions around specific clinical knowledge points, with expert oversight

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that prompt engineering enables ChatGPT to support instructional planning: "The model can aid instructors in developing lesson plans that address specific clinical knowledge points and in generating relevant test questions". The authors frame this as streamlining item development from image-based course materials and structuring lessons around clinical concepts, while stressing that ensuring clinical validity and pedagogical alignment "remains a critical challenge, underscoring the need for expert oversight".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-led review and revision mechanisms to ensure safety, accuracy, and pedagogical value of AI-generated content
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- Medical educators designing image-based instruction and assessment

### Target Learning Goals
- Structured lesson plans focused on core clinical knowledge areas
- Generation of relevant test questions from image-based course materials
