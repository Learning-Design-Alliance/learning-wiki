# Offer three voluntary multiple-choice questions per lecture on an online platform with feedback, without changing lecture structure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to accompany each lecture of a semester-long course with a small set of online multiple-choice questions that students may answer voluntarily at home, with correct answers shown after each session to provide feedback. The authors report that "The implementation of multiple-choice questions is feasible and easily established for everyone" and that no change in lecture structure is needed. On the basis of their data, MCQs are now routinely offered for every lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online platform on which the questions can be distributed and answered
- Correct answers shown after each session to provide feedback
#### Constraints
- Completion was voluntary and did not affect the final examination result
- The authors note results from one institution cannot be generalized

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge retention and recall of lecture material in gynecology and obstetrics
