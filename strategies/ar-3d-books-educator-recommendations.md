# Use AR 3D books for engagement and retention while regulating session duration and instructor support for comprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends educators deploy 3D books where students are bored or disengaged, since the medium boosts interest and retention, while relying on instructor guidance for higher-order comprehension skills. It advises that "it is crucial to regulate the duration of student engagement to prevent overdependence on the digital gadget", and suggests 3D books suit retention-focused content like terminology, historical events, and formulae.
