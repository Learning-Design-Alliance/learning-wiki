# Train-the-trainer facilitator preparation via an intensive 3-day course plus a 2-day standardization workshop with TeamGAINS debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To build capacity for increased simulation, simulation experts from a regional simulation center train departmental staff as facilitators. An "intensive 3-day course effectively equipped these individuals, including doctors and nurses, with the skills necessary to proficiently lead simulation exercises within their departments"; ten additional facilitators were trained in October 2022 on top of 25 existing ones. A subsequent 2-day workshop for 27 facilitators covered equipment, "refining debriefing skills utilizing TeamGAINS", fostering a psychologically supportive learning atmosphere, and executing scenarios, plus access to an online scenario repository.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regional simulation center experts to deliver the course; departmental staff willing to serve as facilitators; an online scenario repository for facilitators
#### Constraints
- The protocol states the efficacy of the intervention hinges on the competence and proficiency of facilitators, requiring continuous support and communication throughout the study period

### Target Learners
- Doctors and nurses serving as simulation facilitators in pediatric departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Leading simulation exercises, debriefing, and creating psychologically supportive learning atmospheres
