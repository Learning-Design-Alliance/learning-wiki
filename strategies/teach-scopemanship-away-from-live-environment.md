## Description
The article argues that although scopemanship attributes vary with personality and stressors, "the attributes of scopemanship can be taught away from the live environment to improve competence in endoscopy." Proposed formats include lectures on scopemanship principles for trainees, Train-the-Trainer courses adapted to emphasize scopemanship, and courses for leadership training and communication skills development. It cites brief interventions as sufficient to make a difference.
