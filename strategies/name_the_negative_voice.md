# Name the Negative Voice

## Description
Have students name their negative inner voice (e.g., Adverse Antoine, Ruthless Ralph, Cranky Kirsten). This allows students to step outside themselves and understand that they are not defined by that voice and can treat it like a separate entity, thereby taking back power and control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creativity, understanding of negative self-talk patterns.
#### Constraints
- May seem silly or ineffective to some learners. Requires a playful and open-minded approach.
