# AI in Education Toolkit for Racial Equity

## Description
Utilizing an AI in Education Toolkit for Racial Equity involves taking a more in-depth look into the data used to build algorithms and the way products are used by and for Black and Brown students in schools. The toolkit provides actions that can be taken at every stage of product design and development to mitigate racial bias in the design and development of products.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the AI in Education Toolkit for Racial Equity, a commitment to racial equity, and the ability to implement the recommended actions.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing commitment and effort to address racial bias. May be challenging to implement all of the recommended actions. May not be effective if users are not committed to racial equity.
