# Future evaluative research protocols should include avoidance of involuntary hospital admissions as a key outcome and use both PROMs and PREMs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "future evaluative research protocols should include avoidance of involuntary hospital admissions as a key outcome." It further recommends that evaluation frameworks incorporate both patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs), such as mood or distress levels, and patient-reported experience measures (PREMs), such as feeling validated. Service design protocols should emphasise collaborative safety planning and consent-based family engagement.

## Design Implications
