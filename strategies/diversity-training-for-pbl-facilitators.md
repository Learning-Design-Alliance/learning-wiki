# Provide teachers with diversity training and conflict-resolution skills for facilitating heterogeneous PBL groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study recommends that facilitators be made aware of students' expectations in heterogeneous groups and be appropriately skilled to handle them, since "Diversity training and conflict resolution is critical to subdue the pride and prejudice that creeps in." Facilitators themselves expressed a desire to understand diverse students holistically and their lack of skills for diverse groups. This was the second of three recommendations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address power issues and inequalities prevalent in diverse groups
- Facilitators need prior knowledge of students' backgrounds
#### Constraints
- The article notes this recommendation needs further investigation regarding how to train PBL teachers effectively in diverse groups

### Target Learners
- PBL facilitators/tutors of diverse student groups

### Target Learning Goals
- facilitating multicultural small groups
- conflict resolution
