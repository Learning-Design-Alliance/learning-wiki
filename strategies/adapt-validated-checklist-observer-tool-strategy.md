# Use a previously validated procedural checklist as the basis for an observer tool in simulation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 against) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy adapted an observer tool from a checklist previously validated for assessing CVC insertion performance. The article reports the source checklist "was validated by Hartmann et al. to assess performance during CVC insertion" and was adapted by "removing 11 checklist items that were not achievable during our jugular CVC insertion training". The resulting tool described the main CVC insertion steps chronologically and was used by observers without stakes in evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated procedural checklist covering the key steps of the target task must be available as a starting point
- Items not achievable in the specific training context must be identified and removed with documented rationale
#### Constraints
- The authors state no guidance exists on how to build an effective OT, so a tool constructed this way may be suboptimal

### Target Learners
- incoming first-year anesthesia residents

### Target Learning Goals
- central venous catheter insertion procedural skills
