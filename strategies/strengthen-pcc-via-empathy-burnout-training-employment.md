# Strengthen surgical nurses' PCC competence by improving empathy, minimizing burnout, offering humanistic nursing training, and improving employment conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' recommended intervention set, derived from their regression findings. They state that "steps should be taken to strengthen the key factors, such as improving empathy, minimizing job burnout, offering more humanities training, and enhancing personalities, to reinforce PCC competency." The conclusions similarly list promoting empathy, decreasing burnout, strengthening human-centered nursing education, and improving staff treatment and work circumstances.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should target the four factors the regression identified: empathy, burnout, humanistic nursing training, and employment status.
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a cross-sectional survey, so causal effects of these interventions are not established by this study; the sample was surgical nurses in tertiary hospitals in Anhui, China, and mostly female.

### Target Learners
- in-service surgical nurses with more than 1 year of clinical experience

### Target Learning Goals
- person-centered perioperative care competence
- empathy
- humanistic nursing practice
