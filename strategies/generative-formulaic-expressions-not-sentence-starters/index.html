Offer generative formulaic expressions instead of rigid sentence starters¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends replacing typical sentence starters, which "limit language choice for students" and act as "questions in disguise," with formulaic expressions that flexibly connect multiple ideas. Expressions such as "Starting from the point …" are temporary supports; students are not required to use them verbatim and naturally drop pieces. Broader expressions like "As x increases by … y changes by …" connect multiple mathematical objects and build linguistic autonomy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Expressions must connect multiple ideas and have flexible rather than scripted uses

Constraints¶

  • The brief frames typical sentence starters as offering little room beyond the script
