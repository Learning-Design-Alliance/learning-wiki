# Train health promoters to deliver oral health education to patients in clinic waiting areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that providers viewed health promoters—lay educators who address patients in the waiting room—as a valuable channel for oral health education. All participants stressed that "health promoters should be trained to deliver oral health education as one of the medical topics", and one doctor noted that because promoters speak Xhosa, "what they say tends to hit home better than what I tell a patient". The strategy uses existing staff and waiting-room time to reach patients in their home language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Health promoters already present at the facility who speak the patients' language
- A waiting area where patients gather before appointments
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as a provider suggestion; its efficacy has not been evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- Patients at primary care facilities in resource-poor communities

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral hygiene knowledge and prevention behaviors
- Knowledge of where oral health services are available
