# Schedule time for intentional learning and recognize children's backgrounds and experiences in preschool classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy, drawn from the guide's key findings, directs preschool educators to deliberately set aside scheduled time for intentional learning and to recognize everyone's backgrounds and experiences. The guide names it among the "Important factors in providing instruction in preschool," positioning it alongside intentional instruction, interaction and conversation, and sequentially built lessons as a practice priority for preparing children for elementary school.
