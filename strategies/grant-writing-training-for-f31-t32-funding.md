# Increase attention to training students and mentoring faculty in grant writing to gain F31 and T32 funding support for medical physics PhD training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that the medical physics field invest in grant-writing preparation to capture individual (F31) and institutional (T32) NIH training awards, noting that "More attention to training students and mentor faculty in grant writing to gain potential funding in F31s and T32s would benefit the field and provide needed funding support." This follows the survey's finding that F31 awards have risen strongly in engineering but only modestly in radiation-related departments. It is directed at program faculty and directors in accredited medical physics graduate programs seeking to expand PhD student support.
