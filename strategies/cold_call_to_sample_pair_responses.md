# Cold Call to Sample Pair Responses

## Description
Using cold call to select students to share their pair's responses reinforces expectations around engagement and attention. It ensures that all students are prepared to participate and provides a sampling of responses for the class to consider. This also allows the teacher to create a shared understanding of high-quality responses and identify areas for re-teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires establishing a routine for cold calling and ensuring that students understand that they may be called on to share their thoughts.
#### Constraints
- Some students may feel anxious about being cold called. Teachers must be sensitive to students' needs and provide support as needed.

### Affordances
- Encourages students to stay engaged, promotes active listening, and provides opportunities for students to learn from each other's ideas. Reduces the reliance on volunteers.

### Personalization
- Teachers can support less confident students by letting them know in advance that their ideas are good and that they will be called on to share.
