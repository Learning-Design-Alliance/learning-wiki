# Design diagrammatic quantum instruction that preemptively addresses the conceptual difficulties characteristic of categories 1 and 2

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that 'educators can use our findings to design instruction that preemptively addresses the conceptual difficulties characteristic of categories 1 and 2', supporting students' progression toward more sophisticated ways of understanding quantum teleportation. This means building teaching sequences that anticipate literal math-physics conflations, unclear temporality, and undiscerned roles of entanglement before they arise in group work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need awareness of the roles of temporality, entanglement, and measurement when students try to make sense of quantum teleportation, as provided by the outcome space.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the outcome space might differ 'with a larger study population', qualifying the generality of the design guidance.

### Target Learners
- upper-secondary physics students
- pre-service physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- progression toward more sophisticated ways of understanding quantum teleportation
