# Validate educational videos with both expert judges and the target audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends a two-layer validation strategy: expert judges with specialized knowledge validate scripts, storyboards and videos, and the target audience evaluates the finished video. It emphasizes that "it is important to validate the instrument with experts who have specialized knowledge on the subject matter, in order to help reduce the possibility of inaccurate results or biased measurements". Ensuring understanding and appeal for audiences with special needs requires agreement between specialists and the target population on items such as concept, clarity, content and language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of judges and their criteria must be rigorous; agreement measured per item, e.g. with the Content Validity Index
#### Constraints
- The review found 11 of 19 studies lacked target-audience validation and some did not disclose evaluator selection criteria

### Target Learners
- Nursing professionals producing educational videos
- Patients, family members and caregivers as target audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- Ensuring educational video content is valid, understandable and appealing to its intended audience
