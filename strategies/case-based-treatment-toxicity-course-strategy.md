# Case-Based Treatment Toxicity Course run monthly over a year

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Medical faculty run a case-based course describing common treatment toxicities, with "classes occur once per month over the course of a year" covering breast, genitourinary, gynecological, brain, pediatrics, lymphoma, head and neck, gastrointestinal, and lung cases. Toxicity presentation, progression, and likelihood of resolution are discussed. Physicist trainees take the course to prepare for likely patient questions and to communicate toxicity concerns to the attending radiation oncologist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- answering patient questions about treatment effects
