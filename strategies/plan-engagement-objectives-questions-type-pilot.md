# Plan engagement by defining objectives, aligned questions, engagement type, and piloted materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Phase 1 prescribes four steps: 'Define engagement objectives', develop questions 'aligned with the purpose of community engagement sessions, answerable, actionable, and specific', determine the engagement type (e.g., interviews, focus groups, surveys, town halls, or a mix), and 'Create accessible, culturally responsive materials; pilot materials with a small group; and adjust as needed.' It also advises recruiting the right people and identifying trusted individuals or organizations to help recruit.
