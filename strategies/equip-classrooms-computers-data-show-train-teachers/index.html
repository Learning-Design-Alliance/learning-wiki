Strategies

Equip secondary classrooms with computers and data show devices and train teachers to implement e-learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's recommendations to education authorities are to supply classroom hardware and build teacher capability for e-learning. It recommends working "on the provision of computers and devices ( Data show) in the classroom with a sufficient numb er in secondary schools" so teachers and students have sufficient time to use them, and running training courses on using computers, the Internet, and e-learning implementation. This follows the study's finding that e-learning enrichment outperformed traditional teaching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sufficient number of computers and data show devices in classrooms, with sufficient time allocated for teachers and students to use them

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one quasi-experiment with 64 female second-grade intermediate students in Mosul

Target Learners¶

  • second-grade intermediate school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • scientific thinking skills in physics
  • positive attitude toward physics

Claims¶

  • E-learning-enriched physics teaching significantly improved students' attitude toward physics from pre-test to post-test in the experimental group [+W]
  • E-learning-enriched physics teaching significantly improved scientific thinking scores from pre-test to post-test in the experimental group of intermediate girls [+W]
  • At post-test, the e-learning experimental group significantly outperformed the control group in attitude toward physics [+W]
  • At post-test, the e-learning experimental group significantly outperformed the control group in scientific thinking [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate AR into classrooms by training teachers, providing ready-made content, and equipping schools with devices
  • Train teachers and tutors on instructional use rather than device mastery, and include school-level storage and maintenance training
  • Build teacher media skills and classroom media facilities through training, resource centres and equipment provision
  • Government furnishes secondary schools with computers for sculpture study and runs computer appreciation and programming seminars for sculpture teachers
