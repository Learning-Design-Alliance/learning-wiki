# Explicitly discuss physics-specific covariational reasoning skills, including identifying important points and connecting slopes, during instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that physics experts use skills that may not be taught in mathematics courses, the authors recommend that instructors make covariational reasoning skills explicit in teaching. They suggest "instructors may find it useful to discuss how to identify important points in a problem and when it may be productive to simply 'connect slopes' rather than consider the behavior of the function between two points." They argue explicit identification of such skills during instruction may help students build their own facility.
