# Platform Agnostic Design

## Description
Instructional designers create tools that work across any platform, avoiding bias towards specific operating systems or web browsers. This ensures adult learners are not limited by their preferred technology and can access learning resources regardless of their device or software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of different platforms and browsers, use of web standards, testing across multiple devices and systems
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging to implement due to technical constraints or cost considerations. Requires ongoing maintenance to ensure compatibility.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Adult learners with varying levels of technological familiarity, particularly those with limited access to specific platforms

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Ensure accessibility of learning materials across different technology platforms, reduce technological barriers for adult learners

### Affordances
- Reduces frustration and barriers to entry for adult learners, promotes equal access to educational resources, allows learners to use familiar technology

### Personalization
- Design courses to function on multiple platforms (Windows, Mac, Android, iOS) and browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari). Offer alternative formats or offline access for learners with limited internet access.
