# Incorporate a simulation-based ultrasound module into mandatory physician orientation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding ultrasound training into required orientation rather than leaving it optional or absent, since few internal medicine residency programs provide formal ultrasound training. The authors conclude that "An ultrasound training module can be incorporated as a mandatory component of physician orientation", pairing a short didactic with hands-on practice on volunteers and cadavers. They suggest effectiveness should be reassessed over frequent intervals throughout residency, with future plans including objective knowledge and observed skill assessment at four-month intervals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mandatory orientation slot, ultrasound equipment, simulation and cadaver laboratory resources, and subspecialty fellow instructors
#### Constraints
- Initial effectiveness shown only for self-reported comfort; the article states further study is needed to objectively assess knowledge retention and skill acquisition

### Target Learners
- incoming internal medicine interns

### Target Learning Goals
- ultrasound-guided invasive procedure skills
- anatomic structure identification
