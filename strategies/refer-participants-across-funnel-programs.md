# Refer participants among funnel programs so they can grow while programs focus on new participants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that programs in the astronomy-education ecosystem advertise and refer participants to other programs, either at the same funnel level or further down, so repeat customers can grow while the original program keeps focusing on new participants. The author states "Programs should support other programs by referring participants when appropriate", and suggests application-based programs refer rejected applicants to programs at appropriate levels. Programs should probably not try to populate all funnel levels themselves but should expand the funnel as a whole by reaching other communities such as under-represented minorities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must know where they and other projects fall in the funnel
#### Constraints
- It is unlikely one project alone can populate all funnel levels because different levels require vastly different approaches and time investments, and funding is limited

### Target Learners
- program applicants and alumni
- under-represented minority and differently-abled audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- progressing deeper into authentic research participation
