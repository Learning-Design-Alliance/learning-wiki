# Implement a systematic faculty development program combining interdisciplinary team formation, structured training, peer observation, and weekly collaborative lesson preparation.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a systematic faculty development program with four key elements: "formation of an interdisciplinary tutor team, structured off-site training in PBL methodology, regular peer observation of teaching sessions, and mandatory weekly three-hour collaborative lesson preparation sessions." During these sessions, case authors presented their work and the team engaged in simulated student discussions, resolving interdisciplinary content questions and co-constructing detailed tutor guides, transforming individual case knowledge into shared team expertise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
