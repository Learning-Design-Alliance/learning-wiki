# Integrate flipped classroom pedagogy into anatomy curricula to improve student performance and satisfaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends integrating flipped classroom pedagogy into anatomy curricula, stating that "these findings support integrating flipped pedagogy into anatomy curricula to improve student performance and satisfaction". The strategy involves shifting knowledge acquisition to pre-class activities via instructor-developed videos and annotated images, then dedicating face-to-face time to collaborative, clinically oriented problem-solving with immediate formative feedback. The authors describe this as "a valuable and scalable instructional strategy for contemporary anatomy education".
