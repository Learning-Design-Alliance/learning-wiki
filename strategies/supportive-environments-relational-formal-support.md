# Teacher education programs should foster supportive learning environments and strengthen the relational accessibility of formal support structures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on its synthesis, the review recommends that teacher education programs foster supportive learning environments and make formal support structures relationally accessible. The authors state that the findings have "implications for teacher education programs, suggesting the value of fostering supportive learning environments and strengthening the relational accessibility of formal support structures". This responds to evidence that contextual and social conditions play an important role in AHS and that preservice teachers rely heavily on informal sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
