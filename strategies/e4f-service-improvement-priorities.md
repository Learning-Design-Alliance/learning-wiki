# Improve an evidence brokerage service by increasing answer speed, promoting the brokerage role, and encouraging teacher-researcher dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on pilot feedback, the report recommends that "providers need to focus on increasing the speed of providing answers, enhancing awareness of the brokerage role, and encouraging greater dialogue between teachers and researchers". These three improvements address the main feasibility challenges identified: users wanted faster responses, the brokerage role was not widely understood, and users wanted more direct exchange with the researchers answering their questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient recruited research academics to respond to teacher questions
- A broker function whose role is actively promoted to users
#### Constraints
- The report notes brokers found it more difficult to summarise a straightforward set of actionable recommendations for practice where research publications were more detailed

### Target Learners
- Teachers and school leaders using an evidence brokerage service

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely, well-matched access to research evidence for classroom practice

### Affordances
- E4F Linkage And Dialogue Knowledge Transfer
