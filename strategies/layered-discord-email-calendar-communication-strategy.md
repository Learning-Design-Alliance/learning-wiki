# Use layered communication channels (Discord plus weekly emails plus a shared Google calendar) for distributed programme announcements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To communicate efficiently and cost-effectively, the eIAYC used a Discord server as its main platform, letting leaders post in topical channels and participants mute channels or hold private conversations. Because Discord "did create a barrier to people who did not want to sign up for a separate service or were unable to check it regularly", organisers supplemented it with weekly emails to relevant mailing lists and a Google calendar participants could subscribe to for notifications. The strategy layers a rich community platform over low-barrier broadcast channels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Discord server with topical channels
- Weekly emails to relevant mailing lists
- A Google calendar kept up to date throughout the summer
#### Constraints
- Discord created a barrier for people unwilling to sign up for a separate service or unable to check it regularly

### Target Learners
- distributed online programme participants across time zones

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and awareness of programme activities
