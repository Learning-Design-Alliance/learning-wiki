# Supplement tutoring with vetted online video lectures and modeled solutions to close analogues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To serve more cadets with limited manpower, the author recommends compiling a vetted, topic-organized list of on-line resources and producing videos modeling well-communicated solutions. Cadet feedback indicates cadets "benefit more from on-line video lectures than from on-line written material." The author recommends modeling close analogues rather than the assigned exercises, so cadets do the mental labor of bridging the gap, and warns that first-year students often rely too heavily on written solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful vetting of resource quality and correlation of the list with specific course material
- Videos that model well-considered, well-communicated problem solving
#### Constraints
- Published answers and solutions are of variable quality and often overemphasize equations; first-year students may not yet be disciplined enough to benefit fully from written solutions

### Target Learners
- first and second year cadets in calculus and differential equations courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sound quantitative problem-solving methodology rather than formula roulette
