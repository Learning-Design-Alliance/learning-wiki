# Timely detection of technology addiction plus therapeutic and educational strategies to mitigate excessive device use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions apply instruments for timely detection of technology addiction and apply strategies to mitigate excessive use of electronic equipment. It states: "Se recomienda la detección oportuna de este hábito con la aplicación continua de este cuestionario en las diversas carreras universitarias ya que ha demostrado validez y concordancia." It further suggests prospective follow-up studies of therapeutic educational, sports, positive-psychology and group-game strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continued application of the Labrador questionnaire across university degree programmes, which the article says has demonstrated validity and concordance
#### Constraints
- The recommended strategies have not themselves been tested in this study; the article suggests prospective research to evaluate them

### Target Learners
- University students in Latin America

### Target Learning Goals
- Mitigating excessive use of electronic equipment that is associated with poor academic performance
