# Co-operative learning group presentations on death and dying themes using student-chosen stimulus material

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the end-of-life care module, "students were divided into co-operative learning groups" and each group presented experiences on a death-and-dying theme based on a film, article, story, poem or case study of its choice. Groups used demonstrations, acts, posters, video clips and mind maps across four scheduled learning opportunities, each presentation followed by an open class discussion introducing theoretical models and practical interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Co-operative learning groups; student choice of applicable stimulus material; a variety of presentation methods; scheduled learning opportunities followed by open class discussion
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a second-year undergraduate nursing module on end-of-life care at one South African institution

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding of grief, death and dying and end-of-life care principles

### Affordances
- [Constructivist Experiential Learning Model Eol](../theories/experiential-learning-theory.md)
