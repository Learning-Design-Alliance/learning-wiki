# Building a Community on Student Voice

## Description
Building a community on student voice involves creating opportunities for students to share and discuss their thoughts, ensuring that they feel safe to do so. This approach allows teachers to understand students' needs and wants while building their confidence to speak up and advocate for their learning. Strategies include daily check-ins, reflection sheets, sentence stems, starters, and word banks, and student-led discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive classroom environment, structured opportunities for student voice, and strategies to help students articulate their thoughts.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to create a supportive classroom environment and facilitate meaningful discussions. Students may initially resist sharing their thoughts or feel uncomfortable speaking up in a group setting.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Teachers can provide sentence stems, starters, and word banks for students who struggle to articulate their thoughts. They can also provide one-on-one support to students who may be reluctant to share their thoughts in a group setting.

- Observe students' participation in discussions, their willingness to share their thoughts, and their ability to advocate for their learning. Measure students' sense of belonging and their perceptions of safety in the classroom.

## Impact
- Research indicates that student voice leads to improved academic outcomes, increased engagement, and a more supportive learning environment. Students become more confident, capable, and successful in their learning.
