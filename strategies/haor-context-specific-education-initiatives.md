# Context-specific school and community initiatives: awareness campaigns, stipends, counseling, gender-sensitive supports, and infrastructure improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's respondents judged current school initiatives insufficient and recommended a set of context-specific interventions for Haor communities. These include public awareness campaigns through "yard meetings and community gatherings" engaging mosques and community organizations, increased educational stipends for male students, regular counseling and motivational services, gender-sensitive safety committees and dedicated child-protection staff, and improved classroom infrastructure. Each recommendation responds to a barrier documented in the study's own quantitative and qualitative findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation requires engagement of local institutions such as mosques and community organizations, and consistent follow-up, which informants said current initiatives lack.
#### Constraints
- The article states current initiatives have limited effect without continuous effort and consistent follow-up; recommendations come from respondent interviews, not from tested interventions.

### Target Learners
- Secondary students in Haor (flood-prone wetland) regions of Bangladesh

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving academic performance, regular school attendance, and reduced chronic absenteeism
