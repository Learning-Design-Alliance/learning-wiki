# Dedicate one or two classes to the Stern-Gerlach experiment before teaching entanglement

## Description
The article recommends that "at least one or two classes in a quantum computing module be dedicated to the study of the SG experiment." The rationale is that the SGE demonstrates angular momentum quantization in a single particle, entanglement involves correlation of angular momentum between two or more particles, and measurement outcomes in the SGE illustrate the fourth postulate (only eigenvalues of the measurement operator are observed).
