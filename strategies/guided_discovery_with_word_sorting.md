# Guided Discovery with Word Sorting

## Description
Use guided discovery with word sorting and teacher questioning to help students understand spellings that depend on the position of a sound in a word or established conventions (like –ve). For example, the –ge and –dge pattern for the phoneme /j/ lends itself to word sorting and guided discovery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word lists, sorting materials, direct instruction
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit and systematic instruction, may not be effective for learners with severe cognitive deficits
