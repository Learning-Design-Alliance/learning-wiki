# Provide a sonified HUDF alternative for visually impaired students alongside the visualization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To accommodate visually impaired students in the AHaH labs, the team provided an auditory alternative: "for visually impaired students, we made available the Astronomy Sound of the Month webpage for January 2018, which has a full display of the HUDF image and plays a tone when one moves the mouse over a given galaxy, of varying pitch depending on the distance to that galaxy." This converts spatial distance information into pitch, allowing non-visual access to the redshift-distance structure the visualization conveys. It was offered alongside the standard accommodations made for students attending remotely via Zoom during the pandemic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the external Astronomy Sound of the Month webpage and a mouse-driven interface
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as an accommodation provided, without evaluating its effectiveness

### Target Learners
- visually impaired students in introductory astronomy labs

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding galaxy distances and the structure of the HUDF through auditory pitch mapping
