# Involve students and alumni directly in campus activities, curriculum development and dialogue to build experience value

## Description
The article's closing recommendation directs higher education institutions to build student experience value through joint creation rather than focusing only on academic reputation. It recommends to "Involve various university activities such as curriculum development with students and alumni" and to "Create the value of student experience by joint creation between lecturers and students," and to invite students into dialogue about planned activities. This operationalizes co-creation inside and outside the classroom.
