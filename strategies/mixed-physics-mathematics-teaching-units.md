# Merge Physics interaction blocks with Mathematics analysis and geometry blocks into mixed teaching units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining Physics blocks 2–3 (Gravitational and Electromagnetic Interaction) with Mathematics blocks 3–4 (Analysis and Geometry) into mixed teaching units and learning situations, since the mathematical tools for the physics blocks are taught in the mathematics blocks. It states that "Merging these blocks into mixed teaching units and learning situations could provide students with a more practical view of mathematics and, likely, a better understanding of physics."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- collaboration and communication between teachers of the different subjects
#### Constraints
- the integration must be approached with care to avoid superficial understanding; interdisciplinary integration can fail to meet pedagogical objectives without a clear methodological proposal

### Target Learners
- upper-secondary (2º de bachillerato) Physics and Mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- practical understanding of mathematics
- deeper understanding of physics
- interdisciplinary science view

### Affordances
- Concept Network Curriculum Methodology
