# Train prehospital providers on the portable ultrasound devices their agencies already use or plan to adopt

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend incorporating the specific portable POCUS devices that participating EMS agencies use or plan to adopt into training. They report: "In our courses, we used the portable POCUS devices that the EMS agencies were already using or considering using. This also helped the participants become familiar with and troubleshoot their devices." The article states this potentially addressed challenges of using the technology and its limitations, such as battery time, glare from the screen, connectivity, and device protection in the field.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the portable ultrasound devices the learners' agencies use or plan to adopt
- Hands-on practice time with those devices during the course
#### Constraints
- The article links this practice to addressing technology-use challenges such as battery time, screen glare, connectivity, and device protection; it does not report a separate measured effect for this component

### Target Learners
- EMS providers whose agencies are implementing or considering POCUS programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Device familiarity and troubleshooting (knobology) for field ultrasound use
