# Use ONA-derived network visualizations to target debriefing on communication bottlenecks and assessment-to-action transitions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that simulation educators use TuRBO or similar rubrics with ONA to gather and analyze team interaction data, enhancing feedback specificity during debriefing. They suggest educators "develop targeted exercises that help teams recognize these communication loops and practice transitioning more efficiently from assessment to action," including deliberate pauses during simulations and video playback with overlaid network analysis, plus exemplar cases from high-performing teams to demonstrate effective transitions and task delegation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires video-recorded simulations, a coding rubric such as TuRBO, and ONA analysis to produce network visualizations that can be shown to teams.
#### Constraints
- Identified patterns cannot be generalized to real-world clinical situations without extensive additional analysis of simulation scenario fidelity; the study scenario emphasized individual rather than collaborative performance.

### Target Learners
- practicing anesthesiologists and acute care team members in simulation training

### Target Learning Goals
- recognizing and breaking unproductive communication cycles
- transitioning efficiently from information gathering to implementation
