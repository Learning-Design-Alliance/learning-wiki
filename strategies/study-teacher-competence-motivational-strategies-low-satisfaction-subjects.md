# Future research should examine perceived teacher competence and motivational strategies in mathematics, Spanish, and English

## Description
The authors recommend that future work examine teacher-related variables in the subjects where student satisfaction was lowest. They state: "We believe it would be interesting in future research to study the level of competence that students perceive in their MATH, SP, and ENG teachers, whether or not teachers use different teaching and motivational strategies, and whether the teaching–learning process matches the intellectual needs of each student". This follows their finding that these three subjects were the strongest predictors of school satisfaction yet had the lowest satisfaction means.
