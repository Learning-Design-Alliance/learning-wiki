Strategies

Incorporate palliative care into nursing curricula and provide regular palliative care training and continuing education for nurses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's concluding recommendation is to build nurses' palliative care knowledge through formal education and recurring training. It states "there should be incorporation of palliative care in the nursing curriculum" and that "palliative care training and continuous education should be given regularly for nurses". This rests on the meta-analytic finding that trained and B.S.-degree nurses had significantly higher odds of good knowledge.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training must be updated and delivered regularly, as the authors state getting updated training raises nurses' knowledge

Constraints¶

  • Supporting evidence is cross-sectional, which the authors state may be limited to generate a cause-effect link

Target Learners¶

  • practicing hospital nurses in Ethiopia
  • nursing students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • knowledge of palliative care for patients with life-threatening and chronic illness

Claims¶

  • B.S.-degree nurses show significantly higher odds of palliative care knowledge than diploma nurses (AOR = 3.01) [+W]
  • Pooled good knowledge of palliative care among Ethiopian nurses is 45.57%, meaning more than half had poor knowledge [+W]
  • Only 36.02% of reviewed Ethiopian nurses had palliative care training, and Egger's test indicated no publication bias [+W]
  • Palliative care training is significantly associated with Ethiopian nurses' knowledge of palliative care (AOR = 4.64) [+W]
  • Municipal care managers commonly lack palliative care education and report inadequate education resources for staff [-W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Hospitals and nursing educators should provide spiritual care education courses and integrate them into undergraduate nursing curricula
  • Include core diabetes competencies in nursing curricula and expand continuing professional development across SSA
