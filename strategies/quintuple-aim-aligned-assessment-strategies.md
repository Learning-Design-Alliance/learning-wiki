## Description
The article highlights potential assessment strategies aligned with each Quintuple Aim competency area, emphasizing formative and summative evaluations. The abstract notes these "include simulation-based assessments, real-world technology integration projects, and reflective practice portfolios." Table 1 maps specific methods to competencies, such as role-play scenarios for virtual care communication, scenario-based assignments for interoperability, and case-based discussions for ethical practice.
