# Readerthon: combining extensive and intensive reading over a short weekend period

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Readerthon is a reading activity in which each student reads ten volumes of the Just English magazine for fluency (extensive reading) over two days on a weekend, then completes a worksheet with 20 short-answer questions about the texts (intensive reading). The article describes it as "a combination of intensive and extensive reading task, called the Readerthon" and identifies it as a special activity that added variety and fostered learner engagement in the program.
