# Use weekly tutor feedback to move positive leaders toward a facilitation role and negative leaders toward positive leadership

## Description
The article's authors propose extending the study to test whether weekly tutor feedback shifts leaders' behavior: "Ideally, that feedback would move positive leaders to a facilitation role" and "move negative leaders to a positive leader position". P3 tutors already give weekly written feedback on group and individual performance, and Natasha's Week 1-to-Week 4 progression toward facilitation is the pattern the authors hope feedback produces.
