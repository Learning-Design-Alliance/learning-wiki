# Hold training workshops on the risks of Internet addiction and refer affected students to psychological services

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's conclusion recommends that authorities "should take the necessary measures to increase students' knowledge about the academic and professional risks of Internet addiction," that "holding training workshops can be useful," and that referral of Internet-addicted students to a psychiatrist/psychologist should be considered. It also suggests future causal and qualitative studies and similar work on other health professions students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training workshops that increase students' knowledge of the academic and professional risks of Internet addiction
- Availability of psychiatrist or psychologist referral for Internet-addicted students
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a cross-sectional correlational study; the article states no causal explanations could be derived from it
