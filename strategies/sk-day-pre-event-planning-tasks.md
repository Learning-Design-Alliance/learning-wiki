# Complete five pre-event tasks in priority order: set date and book rooms, find volunteers, advertise, secure funding, and prepare materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates "five main tasks to take care of before the event, which can be done as little as a month in advance", in order of priority: (I) set the date and book rooms, checking school calendars with parents; (II) find volunteers from graduate and undergraduate communities; (III) advertise with a poster, website, sign-up form, and school district announcements; (IV) secure funding; (V) prepare materials such as theme notes, folders, surveys, background checks, and image release forms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Background checks for anyone working with minors
- Details of attendees given to department officers several weeks in advance
#### Constraints
- The most successful days are Saturdays that do not coincide with holidays or school activities

### Target Learners
- female students aged 7-17 in the local school district

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in mathematics outreach activities
