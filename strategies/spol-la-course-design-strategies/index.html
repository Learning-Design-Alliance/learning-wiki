Four course design strategies for including learning analytics in the SPOL learning design loop¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends a set of course design strategies for online educators who wish to use learning analytics in SPOL: (1) add a diagnostic test and a learner profile survey at the beginning of a course; (2) embed formative assessment throughout the course, using auto-graded questions with unlimited attempts and time; (3) record assessment data server-side for LA to use, with incentive strategies such as grade allocation or gamified learning; and (4) embed an adaptive mechanism in the assessment. The article states these strategies are 'recommended for including LA in the loop of learning design' based on the LA applications, the LDA model and the determined data sources.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Auto-graded questions (e.g., multi-choice, fill-in-blank) should be used in formative assessment so feedback is timely and instructor marking workload is reduced
  • Data generated from the client-side should be sent to the server-side of the VLE for recording

Constraints¶

  • The strategies are mainly suitable for STEM disciplines because formative assessment questions need to be auto-graded for the computerized adaptive testing mechanism to be utilized

Target Learners¶

  • online educators designing self-paced online courses
  • adult learners in SPOL

Target Learning Goals¶

  • increasing learning awareness, identifying struggling students, and providing academic intervention in SPOL

Affordances¶

  • Lda Model Learning Design Analytics Loop

Claims¶

  • The choice of adaptive assessment mechanism should match the assessment's purpose: IRT for time-limited diagnostic tests, MAB for learning-gain formative assessment [+W]
  • BKT is recommended as the knowledge estimation approach for SPOL learning awareness and intervention because it balances explanatory power against predicting accuracy [+W]
  • The COMP272 case study confirms the SPOL learning barriers and shows the design strategies are practical to adopt, in a work-in-progress DBR project [+W]
  • Current course learning design practices do not consider what and how data should be generated for learning analytics, limiting LA implementation [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Include learning analytics in the design loop of self-paced online course design, with diagnosis, learner profiling, continuous assessment, and adaptivity
  • Extend LA reports with suggested interventions and pre-course team agreements on how to act on report data
