# Develop AR-supported materials for other Turkish Language Teaching lessons that students find difficult

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend extending the approach piloted for Ottoman Turkish vocabulary — printed materials augmented with QR codes and animated 3D graphics viewed through an AR application — to other difficult lessons in Turkish Language Teaching. They write that "AR technologies can be developed not only for Ottoman Turkish lessons but also for other Turkish Language Teaching lessons which the students have difficulty in learning", and that AR technology can be further developed for the Ottoman Turkish lesson to make learning more enjoyable and easier.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of the words or content students have difficulty with, as done by field experts in this study
- AR infrastructure such as the Aurasma application and students' smartphones
#### Constraints
- The data obtained through this study are limited to the study group and the determined words

### Target Learners
- students of Turkish Language Teaching lessons who have difficulty learning them

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary learning and permanence of learning in difficult language lessons
