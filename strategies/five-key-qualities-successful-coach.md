# Five key qualities of a successful instructional technology coach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report identifies five qualities of an effective coach: relationship builder, insider, strong communicator, tech believer, and experienced teacher. Successful coaches "establish relationships with teachers (and students) that are built on trust and respect"; being a former teacher within the school builds existing trust and understanding of the student population; coaches need an easy, open communication channel; they need belief in technology's potential and a growth mindset rather than pre-existing tech expertise; and recent teaching experience establishes credibility and empathy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches should preferably be hired from within the school with prior teaching experience, since principals in the pilot tended to prioritize innovative teaching history and existing relationships over technological knowledge.
#### Constraints
- The report states it is not necessary for coaches to already be tech savvy; a growth mindset around technology integration can substitute.

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers receiving coaching
- districts and principals hiring instructional technology coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective coach-teacher collaboration for technology integration
