# Use social-form changes to define observation intervals with a five-minute minimum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
OPTIS structures observations using five social forms (individual work, partner work, group work, plenum, and frontal teaching), with a new observation interval beginning each time the social form changes. A minimum interval length of five minutes is applied; if social forms change within less than five minutes, the protocol remains unchanged. This approach targets the goal of gathering as much information as possible since different social forms promote different teaching principles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Observers must document social-form changes occurring within five minutes in a comment section along with teaching content and methods used
#### Constraints
- The minimum five-minute interval was confirmed during piloting as the least amount of time needed to observe and rate all 39 categories

### Target Learners
- School students across subjects and grade levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Capturing variation in teaching principles across different classroom social forms
