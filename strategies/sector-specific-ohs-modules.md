# Adopt sector-specific OHS modules instead of generic courses.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends moving away from generic OHS courses toward sector-specific modules: for construction, intensive focus on "working at heights" and "scaffolding safety" using VR or physical simulations; for healthcare, modules on "safe commuting/transportation" and "ergonomics for patient handling"; for mining/industry, machinery lockout/tagout procedures and risk assessment matrices.
