# Adaptively administer trialogue designs according to the student's knowledge and psychological attributes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that the seven trialogue designs should be adaptively administered depending on the student's knowledge and other psychological attributes. Vicarious learning designs (1 and 2) suit learners with limited knowledge, skills, and actions, whereas designs 5 and 7 suit more capable students attempting to achieve deeper knowledge, and design 4 is motivating by virtue of the game competition. This is a current hypothesis guiding which two-agent configuration to select for a given learner.
