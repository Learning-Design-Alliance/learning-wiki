# Train all literacy-block staff, including classroom assistants, in the Morphology+ program for effective implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's staffing guidance recommends that implementation succeed when every adult delivering literacy instruction is trained: "All teachers, including classroom assistants, who work with students during their literacy block should be trained in IMSE's Morphology+ program." Teachers should adhere to the instructional routines and lesson components. IMSE supports this with in-person and virtual training, asynchronous courses, individual coaching sessions, and district-level consulting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers complete the 30 hours of educator training and use the 148 sequenced lessons as designed.
#### Constraints
- No specific technology is required; IMSE LAB is an optional online portal for planning and assessment.

### Target Learners
- Third- through fifth-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective implementation of explicit morphology instruction
