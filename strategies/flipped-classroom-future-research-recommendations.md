# Future flipped classroom research should use larger multi-site samples, standardized facilitator training, and mixed-methods data collection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In their limitations and recommendations section, the authors propose concrete next steps: "future studies should include a larger and more diverse sample of students from multiple institutions", long-term studies across semesters, expansion to broader curriculum subjects, "standardized training for facilitators" to minimize teaching-quality variability, direct observations, interviews and focus groups, ensuring technology access, mixed-methods triangulation, and replication in different contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional capacity to run multi-site studies and train facilitators consistently
- Technological resources and internet access for all students
#### Constraints
- The recommendations respond to stated limitations of this single-institution, 7-month, single-topic study with self-reported measures; they are the authors' proposals, not tested interventions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- valid and generalizable evaluation of flipped classroom effects on performance, engagement, and satisfaction
