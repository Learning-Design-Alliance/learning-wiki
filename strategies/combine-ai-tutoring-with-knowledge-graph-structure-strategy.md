# Combine AI tutoring with structured knowledge representation rather than using AI solely for question answering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that rather than using AI solely for automated question answering or content generation, educators should integrate AI tutoring with structured knowledge systems such as knowledge graphs. The authors state that "integrating AI with structured knowledge systems may produce more meaningful educational benefits", and that the evaluated model may provide a scalable approach for improving teaching quality in large classes by offering individualized support to many students simultaneously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured knowledge representation of the course content (knowledge graph) must be available alongside the AI tutor
