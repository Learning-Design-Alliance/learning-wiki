# Establish certifying oversight for quantum education materials to combat misinformation and ensure quality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends a systematic content-verification mechanism for open quantum education materials, proposing "the establishment of an endorsing authority, such as a United Nations-backed body or an international consortium of experts, that certiﬁes quantum education materials for public dissemination." This responds to quantum computing's "well-documented hype problem" and unvetted online content that can disseminate misconceptions to high-school students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Review mechanisms and credible endorsing bodies with defined authority and funding
#### Constraints
- The paper flags open questions: who determines authority, how bodies are funded, whether gatekeeping could discourage grassroots creation, and whether bias could enter decisions

### Target Learners
- High-school students consuming open quantum education content

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate quantum concepts
- Trust in scientific communication
