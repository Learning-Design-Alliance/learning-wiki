# Magnetism Investigations

## Description
Magnetism investigations involve exploring how magnets interact with different objects and mapping the effects of magnets on compasses. Learners observe and predict which objects magnets attract and investigate how compasses are affected by nearby magnetic fields. These activities improve understanding of magnetic properties and their applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Magnets, compasses, various objects (e.g., metal, plastic, wood), controlled environments
#### Constraints
- Requires access to magnets and compasses. Safety precautions may be needed.
