# Deliver communication-skill training online where needed, integrate communication skills longitudinally across clinical subjects, and prepare medical students for telemedicine consultations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's Fazit für die Praxis recommends that communication trainings in otolaryngology be conducted online where circumstances require, that communication skills be integrated longitudinally across clinical disciplines rather than in a single course, and that even after the pandemic "im Fokus stehen, Medizinstudierende auf telemedizinische Angebote vorzubereiten". The strategy rests on the study's findings of comparable acceptance and larger self-assessed gains in the online format, and on students' free-text endorsement of practising for telemedicine.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videoconferencing platform with break-out rooms
- Simulation patients and tutors available for synchronous online sessions
- Preparation videos on the relevant clinical content hosted on a learning platform
#### Constraints
- The study was not randomized and results should be replicated in a randomized controlled trial before wider adoption, per the authors
- Online delivery made evaluation of nonverbal communication more difficult for students

### Target Learners
- Medical students in clinical rotations, including otolaryngology block practicum

### Target Learning Goals
- Communication skills for in-person and telemedicine consultations, integrated longitudinally across clinical subjects
