# Tailor university ChatGPT adoption policies to local context, including student age and academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutional policies for ChatGPT adoption in higher education be tailored to individual contexts rather than uniform, because student attitudes and usage varied by country, age, and GPA in their multinational sample. The authors state that "the university policies should be tailored in various settings and based on factors such as age and academic performance as reflected by GPA," arguing that accommodating generational disparities and diverse academic achievements can enhance acceptance and promote equitable academic outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions need information on their students' demographic characteristics and academic performance profiles to tailor policies
#### Constraints
- Recommendation rests on cross-sectional, self-reported survey data from a convenience sample, which the authors state limits causal inference and generalizability

### Target Learners
- university students of varying ages and GPA levels in Arab higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and equitable integration of ChatGPT into learning processes
