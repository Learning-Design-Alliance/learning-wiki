# Provide frequent continuing professional development refresher courses on ECG interpretation for generalist practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends ongoing professional development to address the documented knowledge deficiency in ECG interpretation among generalist practitioners. It concludes there is "a need for frequent continuing professional development refresher courses on the interpretation of ECG tracings of clinical conditions commonly encountered by generalist practitioners", grounded in the finding that overall competence was 14.7% and did not improve with years in practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Refresher content should target the three tracing categories the study assessed: primary ECG parameters, ECG emergencies and common ECG abnormalities
#### Constraints
- The study's quantitative methodology limited enquiry into the basis of practitioners' self-ratings; the recommendation rests on a single cross-sectional assessment at one refresher course

### Target Learners
- generalist medical practitioners (interns, community service doctors, medical officers, GPs) in South African primary healthcare

### Target Learning Goals
- ECG interpretation competence: primary ECG parameters, ECG emergencies, and common ECG abnormalities
