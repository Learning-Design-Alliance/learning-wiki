# Use case-based learning and performance-based assessments to strengthen undergraduate vital pulp therapy education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future studies and teaching use case-based vignettes and performance-based assessments rather than self-report alone. It states that "Case-based learning may help students integrate exposure size with these diagnostic and clinical variables when considering VPT options," and calls for multicenter studies using standardized case-based vignettes, objective knowledge tests, and OSCE-style assessments linked to curricular exposure and simulation training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multicenter cohorts and standardized case-based vignettes with objective knowledge or performance-based assessment instruments
#### Constraints
- Self-reported survey findings cannot determine guideline concordance or clinical competence

### Target Learners
- final-year dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- vital pulp therapy decision-making
- recognition of VPT procedures
- rubber dam isolation and hemostatic protocols
