# Engage stronger students as informal tutors of weaker peers within a mixed-ability cohort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy turns the mix of student abilities into a learning resource: stronger students help weaker peers with mathematical and software tasks, observed "in all interviews and in [9]". The Warwick program fostered it via informal tutorials with problem solving left mainly for students to guide as a group, and the paper frames it through social constructivism, with the teacher reframed as facilitator providing scaffolding so students become a "more capable peer". Advanced students also benefit via deeper understanding of the material.
