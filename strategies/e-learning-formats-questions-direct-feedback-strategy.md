# Combine varied teaching formats with regular knowledge-testing questions and direct feedback in flipped-classroom e-learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
When developing the e-learning used in the flipped classroom, "conscious attention was paid to combining different teaching formats such as texts, videos or case-presentations and regular questions to test knowledge with direct feedback for the learners". The authors attribute part of the flipped format's superiority to this design, contrasting it with classic classroom teaching where individual learning pace and varied learning activators were not taken into account. Feedback is described as enabling students to compare their current knowledge with the desired target state.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-class material must be of high quality and integrate sufficient feedback
#### Constraints
- In classic classroom teaching the individual learning pace and combination of different learning activators were not taken into account, which the authors name as a possible reason for the flipped format's superiority

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge acquisition and application in emergency medicine
