# Facilitator role of inviting visitors to hack the live code and pointing to easily altered parameters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In DigiPlay, an on-site facilitator who is a member of the exhibit development team gives the public direct, live access to expertise. "The facilitator’s primary role is encouraging the visitors to “hack” the simulations, by showing them how to access the underlying code, and pointing them to relevant areas in the code that can be easily altered to potentially powerful effect." In the reported case, the facilitator first explained the three boids rules verbally and with embodied gestures, then invited the boys to hack and showed them where the force weights could be changed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator with deep understanding of the underlying code, ideally a member of the team that developed the exhibit
#### Constraints
- The strategy presumes a staffed installation; the article does not report outcomes of visits without facilitation

### Target Learners
- walk-in public visitors, including school-aged children

### Target Learning Goals
- modifying live simulations
- understanding how local agent-level rules shape emergent patterns
