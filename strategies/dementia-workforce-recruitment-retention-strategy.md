# Recruit and retain a dementia-capable workforce by identifying motivations and meeting training needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that organizations "collaborate, identify, and meet workforce dementia education and training needs" to increase the number of people providing dementia care. It notes many caregivers are recruited through personal circumstances while others enter as a career, and that "Understanding the motivations of caregivers and meeting their training needs may improve their recruitment and retention rates". Suggested competency areas include navigating healthcare and services, implicit bias, health inequities, caregiver stress, health literacy, and sex/gender appropriateness.
