# Implement GenAI literacy in teacher education through a scaffolded four-step approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends urgent, scaffolded implementation of GenAI literacy in developing countries rather than unstructured adoption. The scaffold begins "by helping PSTs understand GenAI's supportive role in education, followed by training in its ethical use, equipping them with prompt engineering skills, and enabling them to critically assess GenAI-generated content for biases and validity," and finally transitions PSTs into practical applications of GenAI to support their future students. Each stage builds capacity before PSTs apply GenAI in classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staged progression from understanding GenAI's role, through ethical-use training and prompt engineering, to critical assessment of outputs, before practical application
#### Constraints
- The article states empirical studies are needed to explore the practical steps necessary for responsible integration

### Target Learners
- Pre-service teachers in developing countries

### Target Learning Goals
- GenAI literacy
- Ethical use of GenAI
- Prompt engineering
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated content
