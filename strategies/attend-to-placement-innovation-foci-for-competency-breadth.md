# Educators should attend to the foci of placement innovations to ensure breadth of competency development in learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because different innovation cases developed different competencies within the same domains, the authors recommend that educators consider what each placement's innovation focuses on when arranging fieldwork. The article states that "Occupational therapy educators may wish to attend to the foci of placement innovations to ensure breadth of competency development in learners," motivated by the observation that culture, equity, and justice development appeared only in virtual care cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of which competency domains a given innovative placement's focus is likely to develop
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single-program qualitative multi-case study during the pandemic; competency development is complex and contextually based

### Target Learners
- occupational therapy MScOT learners

### Target Learning Goals
- broad competency development across all COTC domains, including culture, equity, and justice

## Claims

- [Across all three innovation cases, learners developed competencies in OT expertise, excellence in practice, and communication and collaboration, but which specific competencies developed depended on the case context](../claims/competency-domains-common-across-innovation-cases.md) [+W]
- [The culture, equity, and justice competency domain was addressed only in the virtual care case and was not explicitly identified in administration or intrapreneurship cases](../claims/culture-equity-justice-domain-silent-across-cases.md) [+W]
- [Innovative fieldwork placements during the COVID-19 pandemic (administration, intrapreneurship, and virtual care cases) supported competency development in occupational therapy learners, though development was complex and contextually based](../claims/innovative-fieldwork-supported-competency-development-pandemic.md) [+W]
