ESP teachers themselves furnish materials with pictorial context when textbooks lack face validity¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article suggests that where ESP textbooks lack face validity, teachers can apply the technique themselves rather than waiting for revised materials. The author states that "ESP teachers themselves can furnish the materials with this educational technique", supplementing reading passages with visual aids and pictorial schemata to make materials more relevant and engaging for learners.
