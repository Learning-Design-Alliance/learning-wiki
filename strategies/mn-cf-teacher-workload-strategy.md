# Use metalinguistic naught CF (rule explanations without error marking) to ease teachers' corrective feedback workload while retaining long-term effectiveness.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends metalinguistic naught CF, in which nothing is marked on errors and rule explanations are presented to the whole group, as a practical alternative to error-marking CF types. It notes "The teacher does not need to prepare and give MN separately for each individual written text as it offers the same information to the whole group simultaneously", and that the teacher need not identify or correct errors since learners do so themselves against the explanations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Metalinguistic rule explanations for the target structures must be presented to the whole group, and learners must inspect their own intact writing to locate and correct errors.
#### Constraints
- MN was not differentiated from MS on the immediate posttest, but its short-term effectiveness was demonstrated only for rule-explanation-level information, not for sparser metalinguistic codes.

### Target Learners
- Low-intermediate university EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term development of explicit and implicit knowledge of targeted grammatical structures
