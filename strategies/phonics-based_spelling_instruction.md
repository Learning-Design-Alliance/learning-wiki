# Phonics-Based Spelling Instruction

## Description
Encourages learners to apply their phonics skills to spell new words by segmenting phonemes (sound units) and connecting them with corresponding letters. This method builds on the understanding of the relationship between letters and sounds, making educated guesses about spelling. Encourages segmentation to spell and builds skills that can be applied when writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of phonics, word lists that align with learners' phonics skills, and materials for segmenting and representing sounds with letters.
#### Constraints
- Not effective for all English words due to irregular spellings. Relies heavily on learners' existing phonics knowledge.
