Establish high-retention preparation pathways—residencies, Grow Your Own models, service scholarships, and mentoring/induction—to stem turnover in high-need subjects and schools¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends service scholarships and loan forgiveness that "pay the cost of teacher preparation in exchange for a commitment to teach in a subject or location of need for 3 to 5 years," teacher residency programs with a full training year under a master teacher plus two years of mentoring, Grow Your Own models recruiting local community members, and high-quality induction with mentoring, collaborative planning, and reduced teaching loads.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programs must provide comprehensive preparation with strong clinical training and supports for candidates to succeed

Constraints¶

  • Service scholarship commitments are for 3 to 5 years in a subject or location of need

Target Learners¶

  • Prospective and beginning teachers in high-need subjects and hard-to-staff schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building a well-prepared, stable teacher workforce for high-need subjects and schools

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop high-retention pathways — Grow Your Own programs and teacher residencies — to build and diversify local teacher supply
  • Build high-retention, supportive pathways into teaching for candidates of color
  • Offer targeted service scholarships, loan forgiveness, and 1-year postbaccalaureate residency programs to rapidly expand the supply of well-prepared teachers in shortage fields and locations
  • A federal Marshall Plan for Teaching focused on seven areas: compensation, debt-free preparation, high-retention pathways, mentoring, expertise-sharing, school redesign, and accountability
  • Deploy service scholarships, loan forgiveness, and teacher residencies to staff high-need fields and schools
  • Eight state and local strategies to address California teacher shortages
  • Design service scholarships that cover a substantial portion of preparation costs, with stable multiyear funding and linked data systems
