# Train teachers and add coursework on collaborative dynamic web technologies, and give timely feedback in DWT-supported environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that DWT-supported problem-based collaborative learning positively affected achievement, the authors recommend providing courses for teachers to use the technology, adding a course on new collaborative technologies for CEIT departments, and using DWT-supported collaborative environments in Ministry of Education in-service training. They also advise that "Giving accurate feedback on time is appropriate because students construct knowledge continuously as they are working together". Instructional design based on collaborative learning should be encouraged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need training in using technology in collaborative environments, which the article says is currently deficient
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are grounded in one 8-week semi-experimental study with pre-service ICT teachers

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers
- in-service teachers
- CEIT department students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- collaborative work culture
- technology integration
