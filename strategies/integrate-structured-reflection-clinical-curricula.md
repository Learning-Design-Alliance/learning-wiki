# Integrate structured reflective sessions into clinical curricula, tailored to students' developmental stage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "clinical education programmes incorporate reflective components as integral elements, and that the design of these reflection sessions be tailored to students' developmental stage". The implementable recipe tested here combines a 2-hour introductory workshop, weekly scenario-based small-group discussions with structured immediate feedback over six weeks, and supervised individual reflective journaling during clinical placements, totaling 9.5 hours of facilitated instruction per cohort.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator training (a standardized 3-hour workshop), reflection templates, scenario scripts and feedback rubrics; submission checks to ensure completion of written reflections
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single institution with short-term, immediate post-intervention assessment; long-term retention and workplace transfer remain unassessed, and a Hawthorne effect cannot be ruled out

### Target Learners
- undergraduate operating room technology and allied health students in clinical placements

### Target Learning Goals
- deep, transferable clinical learning
- procedural competence
- educational satisfaction

### Affordances
- [Gibbs Kolb Structured Reflection Framework](../theories/experiential-learning-theory.md)
