# Integrate Generative AI literacy into curricula, including query formulation workshops and critical evaluation exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that educational programs treat Generative AI literacy — defined as "the ability to effectively interact with AI tools and understand how to formulate queries and interpret responses" — as a core curriculum component. Suggested activities include workshops on effective query formulation, sessions on interpreting AI responses, and exercises challenging students to critically evaluate AI-provided solutions. The recommendation follows from their finding that 63% of prompts were poor quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must allocate curriculum time for query-formulation practice and critical evaluation of AI responses.
#### Constraints
- Grounded in one introductory Python course at a single 4-year university; the authors state findings may not translate to intermediate or advanced courses.

### Target Learners
- Entry-level undergraduate computer science students

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective prompting of AI tools
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated solutions
