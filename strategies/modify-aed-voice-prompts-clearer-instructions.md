# Modify AED voice prompts to be clearer and more descriptive for inexperienced operators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes redesigning AED verbal instructions into a clearer, more step-by-step format, as demonstrated by the modified instruction set lasting 80 seconds compared to the conventional 22 seconds. The article states this "may represent a promising, low-cost strategy to facilitate device use by inexperienced rescuers" and calls for regulatory bodies to modernize standard AED voice protocols based on human-factor testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modified instructions should follow the structure of the device's original voice prompts while adding descriptive detail about each step.
#### Constraints
- The duration of the modified instructions was relatively long, and a shorter version should be developed to avoid unnecessarily prolonging the overall time to defibrillation.

### Target Learners
- Laypersons and healthcare providers with little or no prior AED training

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved adherence to critical AED procedures including device activation, electrode placement, and shock delivery
