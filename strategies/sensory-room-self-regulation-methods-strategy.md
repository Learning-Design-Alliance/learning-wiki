# Use sensory-room programs to teach adolescents affirmation, visualization and psycho-physiological self-regulation methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends its approach for practitioners: it reports that adolescents learnt to use methods of self-regulation of their mental processes and states including "affirmation, visualization and psycho-physiological regulation", and states the research has practical value for developing and testing teaching programs aimed at adolescents' self-regulation development through the system of sensory room, with methodological recommendations for psychologists, social and supervising teachers working with adolescents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sensory room and a group program structured around affirmation, visualization, breathing and muscle-tonus exercises
#### Constraints
- The authors note results would be more distinguishing if the work is done systematically during a longer period of time

### Target Learners
- adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulation of mental processes and states
- communication skills and subjective control
