# Teach adults the 3Rs (Recognize, Reframe, Respond) and 2Ss (Scaffold, Stretch) for temperament-matched behavior management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The INSIGHTS adult-facing curriculum trains teachers and parents in the 3Rs: "Recognize differences in children's temperaments", "Reframe their perspectives so that each temperament has strengths", and differentiate caregiver responses that are optimal, adequate, or counter-productive. The 2Ss add scaffolding a child in temperament-challenging situations and, if manageable with support, gently stretching the child to better regulate emotional, attentional, and behavioral reactions. The 2Cs address compliance and competence, including contracting with children who have repetitive behavior problems.
