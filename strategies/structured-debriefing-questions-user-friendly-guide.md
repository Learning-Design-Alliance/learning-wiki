# Use structured debriefing questions to ensure learning objectives are met regardless of facilitator experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors created predefined debriefing questions focused on medical expertise, team functioning, equipment, and human resources so that "any health care provider could employ this user-friendly guide and ensure that its full potential is realized." This contrasts with open-ended debriefing and is paired with a 1-page CRM primer and a latent safety threats template.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined debriefing questions addressing specific discussion points and objectives
- A CRM primer to aid the debrief
