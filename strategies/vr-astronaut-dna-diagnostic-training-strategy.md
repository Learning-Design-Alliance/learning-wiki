# Use immersive standardized VR training modules to prepare astronauts for rapid molecular diagnostics in space

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For space missions with limited onboard medical expertise and a need for quick decisions, the authors propose VR training for a PCR-based diagnostic system built on an expired patent. They state: "VR offers a promising solution for this challenge by providing immersive and standardized training modules that can prepare astronauts to perform DNA analysis and utilize diagnostic tools efficiently." Training covers DNA extraction, amplification, and analysis before the mission, reducing error risk in real-time applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skilled operators and precisely regulated parameters for DNA extraction, amplification, and analysis
- Crew must acquire proficiency in handling and interpreting test-kit results even when pre-prepared kits are used
#### Constraints
- Designed for the constraints of space missions, including microgravity conditions and limited onboard medical expertise

### Target Learners
- trained astronauts on space missions

### Target Learning Goals
- performing DNA analysis and molecular diagnostics
- using diagnostic tools efficiently under time pressure
