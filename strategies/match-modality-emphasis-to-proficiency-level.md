# Match training modality emphasis to trainee proficiency level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that training for beginners be dominated by theoretical lectures and simulator exercises, while intermediate and advanced trainees should prioritize perfusion and ex vivo practices with intensified simulator suture training as a supplement. This strategy follows directly from the observed level-stratified preference differences and is intended to optimize resource allocation and training effectiveness across cohorts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Proficiency-based stratification of trainees
- Access to box-trainer simulators, theoretical instruction, perfused organ and ex vivo modules
#### Constraints
- Based on short-term, single-center, single-arm evidence without a control group

### Target Learners
- Gynecology trainees across junior, intermediate and senior proficiency levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic surgical skill development tailored to stage-specific competency
