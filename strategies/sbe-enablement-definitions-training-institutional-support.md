# Enable SBE integration through clearer definitions, targeted educator training, and institutional support including shared resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a coordinated set of enablers for SBE in dietetic education: "clearer definitions and shared language around SBE are needed to help educators recognise existing practice"; targeted educator training covering "educator preparation, learner‐centred facilitation, psychological safety, effective pre‐briefing and structured debriefing"; and institutional support including "access to shared resources, protected educator time and interprofessional collaboration". Survey respondents identified better knowledge/understanding of SBE (93.0%), informal training (80.5%) and access to simulation resources (78.1%) as the top facilitators.
