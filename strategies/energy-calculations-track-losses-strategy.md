# Require energy calculations at each relevant point of the prototype, including losses, and set a minimum track length

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article directs that "energy calculations in each relevant point of the prototype are needed", considering translational and rotational kinetic energies, potential energy, and energy losses even if minimal, such as during the latch-sphere collision (small deformation, sound and heat) and possible friction along the track. The track must have a minimum length, and the steeper the track the more energy the sphere needs to reach the top, requiring circuit adjustments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The track must have a minimum length
- Energy calculations considering translational and rotational kinetic energies, potential energy, and energy losses
#### Constraints
- The steeper the track, the more energy is needed for the sphere to reach the top, and more adjustments are needed in the circuit to get that energy

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in an introductory electromagnetism physics course

### Target Learning Goals
- energy conservation
- accounting for energy losses in collisions and friction
