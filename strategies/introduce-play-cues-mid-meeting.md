# Introduce play cues mid-meeting as an external consultant or facilitator would

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests introducing play cues to meeting participants during a natural break, something "an external consultant might do to inject play, for example, during a creativity workshop." In the study, cues were placed during a fifteen-minute midmeeting break after participants completed a first questionnaire. The authors also note that organizational leaders demonstrating their own playfulness could lead to more robust outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meetings should include a break during which cues can be introduced
#### Constraints
- The short duration of the intervention was a stated limitation; a longer duration conceivably would have led to a greater effect
