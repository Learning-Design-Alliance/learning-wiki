# Involve target end-users in co-design reference groups to review language, feedback logic and module presentation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Target end-users were "invited to co-design the program by participating in reference groups": fourteen parents of children aged 5–11, recruited via staff newsletters, took part in a 2-hour workshop reviewing the parenting scale text, a mock feedback report, and two modules. Parents focused on "the acceptability of the language, and the logic of the feedback messages" and reviewed modules "for language, tone, degree of interactivity and density of content". Parents agreeing to further contact were consulted on registration website design, feedback usefulness, and module content and presentation.
