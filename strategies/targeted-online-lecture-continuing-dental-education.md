# Integrate targeted one-hour interactive online lectures on common orofacial conditions into continuing dental education, with periodic reinforcement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends delivering concise, evidence-based, interactive online lectures covering common orofacial lesions as a continuing education activity for recent graduates. The lecture used "case-based slides, clinical photographs, and open question and answer discussions" delivered via videoconferencing by a certified oral medicine consultant. The authors state such targeted interventions "should be integrated into continuing dental education programs".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content based on the best available evidence and on lesions common in the local population
- An interactive component encouraging engagement between learners and the consultant
- Delivery by a qualified oral medicine specialist
#### Constraints
- Gains in management and prescribing competency decayed within two months, so the authors state periodic reinforcement, spaced repetition techniques, or follow-up sessions are needed; didactic lectures alone may not suffice for long-term retention

### Target Learners
- Newly graduated dental interns
- General dental practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnosis of common oral lesions
- Management and referral decisions for orofacial conditions
