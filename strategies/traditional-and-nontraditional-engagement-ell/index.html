Combine traditional and nontraditional approaches to family engagement for ELL families¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy pairs conventional engagement methods with nontraditional outreach so that schools can reach ELL parents effectively. The brief states that "for schools to increase parental involvement most effectively, both traditional and nontraditional approaches to family engagement must be implemented" within culturally and linguistically appropriate practices. It is offered as an implementable recipe for schools and districts serving fast-growing ELL populations.
