# Introduce an explicit Critical Design Review and mandatory documented test campaigns before launch

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on six years of lessons learned, the authors plan two changes: restructuring the deliverables so the detailed design report is retitled as a Critical Design Review moved closer to the project midpoint, preceded by a reduced 20-minute PDR presentation and 4-6 page report; and requiring students to document test campaigns with test plans and short test reports that contribute to the final grade, guided by two mandatory tests. The authors state "We will require students to document their test campaigns with test plans and short test reports, which will contribute to their final grade."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time to run the additional milestone and mandatory tests
- CAA launch permissions must be obtained at least one month in advance, with 72 hours notice for the NOTAM
#### Constraints
- Students rarely appreciate the importance of testing given limited time and financial resources; the current PDR is submitted too early for students to have done prototyping, iteration or R&D

### Target Learners
- postgraduate MSc students in space science and technology

### Target Learning Goals
- testing and verification practices
- adherence to real-world project lifecycles
- design iteration
