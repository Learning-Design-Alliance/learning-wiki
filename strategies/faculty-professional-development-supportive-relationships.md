# Professional development for faculty members to build supportive and responsive relationships with students

## Description
Among the implications the authors draw for educational practice is professional development that helps faculty cultivate supportive and responsive relationships with students. The article states: "We discuss implications for educational practice (such as professional development for faculty members) and future research (such as a more expansive set of outcomes or mediators)." The strategy targets the relational dimensions the review identifies, particularly care, given that measures including a dimension of care showed higher correlations with GPA.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- Faculty development should address relational qualities (care, approachability, respect) rather than only increasing interaction frequency
#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
