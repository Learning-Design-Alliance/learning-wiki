# Organize teachers into shared-content teams with at least 90 minutes weekly to plan instruction and embed social-emotional skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends structuring master schedules so teachers in shared-curriculum teams get protected collaboration time. It specifies that "Teachers in shared-content (i.e. shared curriculum) teams will need at least 90 minutes per week with instructional experts to analyze student work, plan instruction using high-quality curricular materials, and identify opportunities to reinforce social-emotional skills during lessons." Collaboration with social-emotional support staff about individual students typically requires at least one hour every three weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated scheduled time; facilitation by instructional coaches or teacher-leaders with dedicated time for planning and coaching
#### Constraints
- Sufficient time for collaboration with social-emotional support staff may vary by school

### Target Learners
- All K-12 students via their teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Embedding social-emotional competencies into academic instruction
