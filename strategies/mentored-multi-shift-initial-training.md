# Mentored multi-shift initial training with certification for solo operations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
New astronomers receive an initial training of four shifts of 6-10 days/nights, split into two daytime and two night-time shifts, with an experienced astronomer acting as mentor. At the end, the trainee is certified for solo operations on one system and can routinely operate three instruments and troubleshoot with the TIO; after a year they can train others.
