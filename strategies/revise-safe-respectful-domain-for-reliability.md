# Revise the safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary school student survey to improve its internal consistency reliability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study team recommended revisions to the safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary school student survey after reliability analysis showed it "fell short of the established threshold for reliability based on the correlations among the items within the domain". The recommendation targets improving internal consistency reliability of that domain specifically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reliability analysis based on correlations among items within the domain to identify which items need revision
#### Constraints
- The recommendation applies to the safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary school student survey, not the other domains

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in Pennsylvania schools using the voluntary survey

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate measurement of school climate domains for tracking and identifying areas needing support
