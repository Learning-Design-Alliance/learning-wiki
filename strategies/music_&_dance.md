# Music & Dance

## Description
Incorporate music and dance into the learning environment to engage verbal, visual, and kinesthetic senses. This strategy involves connecting content with movement and rhythm to enhance memorization and understanding. Examples include creating dances to memorize concepts, using songs to practice skills (e.g., alphabet knowledge), or allowing learners to create instrumental songs and choreograph dance moves. This approach increases learner engagement and retention by activating multiple parts of the brain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom space for movement, access to music or instruments (optional), and willingness to integrate physical activity into lessons. Digital tools like Toca Dance Free can be used for creating music and choreography.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners with physical limitations or those uncomfortable with movement. Requires careful planning to ensure activities align with learning objectives.
