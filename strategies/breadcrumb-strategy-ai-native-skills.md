# Breadcrumb strategy: embed small AI-native skill exercises, nudges, and prompts across multiple courses throughout the four-year curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Breadcrumb strategy is a lightweight curricular approach proposed by faculty workshop participants for fostering students' AI-native skills. It "integrates small, practical exercises, nudges, or prompts into multiple courses — through assignments, exams, or projects", targeting skills such as ethics, social and ecological impact, and responsible AI. Breadcrumbs can be sequenced to build on previous knowledge, reinforcing learning year by year, and designed to fit diverse courses from theory to software engineering.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Breadcrumbs must be sequenced to build on previous knowledge and designed to fit diverse courses across the four-year curriculum
#### Constraints
- Proposed by workshop participants; the whitepaper states the proposed solutions remain to be tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate computer science students across all four years

### Target Learning Goals
- AI-native competencies including ethics, responsible AI, evaluating impacts, accessibility and legal standards, and responsible design choices
