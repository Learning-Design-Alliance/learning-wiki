Extend the five-phase inquiry framework to guide future research on online lab guidance¶

Description¶

Beyond applying the existing framework, the article recommends extending it. The authors state they "provide insights for extending the 5-phase framework for future research," positioning the framework as a living structure that should be refined based on emerging practice in online and remote lab instruction rather than treated as fixed.
