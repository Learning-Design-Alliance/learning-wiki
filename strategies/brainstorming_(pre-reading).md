# Brainstorming (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Learners are given a limited time (e.g., five minutes) to brainstorm ideas related to the reading topic. They then have additional time to organize their ideas and form sentences. After this preparation, learners move around the room, sharing their ideas with other learners. This activity encourages rapid idea generation and collaborative knowledge sharing before diving into the reading material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a clear space for learners to move around, timer, and optional graphic organizers or prompts.
#### Constraints
- May require guidance to ensure focus and relevance. Some learners may struggle with generating ideas quickly.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Generate a wide range of ideas related to the reading topic, encourage collaborative knowledge sharing, and prepare learners for the reading activity.

### Affordances
- Promotes quick thinking, activates prior knowledge, and fosters a collaborative learning environment. It helps learners feel more prepared and engaged with the reading material.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted with graphic organizers or prompts to guide brainstorming. The time allotted can be adjusted based on the complexity of the topic.
