# Adjust Time Frames

## Description
Break content into smaller time intervals if learners cannot stay on task. Children can concentrate on one task for two to five minutes per year old. Using timers, have learners show their work after a short period. Consider calling the child to the desk for checks. This provides physical movement and monitors progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Timers, structured tasks, designated check-in points.
#### Constraints
- May disrupt workflow if intervals are too short. Frequent check-ins may increase dependency on the instructor.
