# Enact interdisciplinary relations across five organizational levels, with the teacher active on one

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper reports Usova's five organizational levels for fulfilling interdisciplinary relationships: curriculum commissioners, syllabus developers, textbook and guide authors, school administrators and interdisciplinary commissioners, and school teachers. Usova holds that "it is necessary to fulfill a certain set of regular interdisciplinary activity by all participants of the educational process (on all organizational levels)," and the paper adds that the teacher "can be active only on one of them." As a strategy, it tells implementers to locate their own level and coordinate with the others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- regular interdisciplinary activity by all participants of the educational process
#### Constraints
- each hierarchical level manifests a distinct role and the levels coexist in a dynamic unity; a teacher is active only on one level

### Target Learners
- school students

### Target Learning Goals
- systematic enactment of interdisciplinary relations in science teaching
