# Adopt combined teaching methods in future primary school teachers' training programs

## Description
The article recommends that teacher educators and curriculum designers incorporate the combined (traditional plus active learning) teaching method into primary teacher training. It states that "the curriculum designers may include combined teaching methods as part of future primary school teachers’ training programs in Ukraine", with teachers, educational leaders and policy makers arranging and facilitating implementation. The recommendation rests on the study's post-test advantage for the combined group.
