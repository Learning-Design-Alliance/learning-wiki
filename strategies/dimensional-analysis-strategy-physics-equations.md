# Dimensional analysis: checking units to evaluate plausibility and describe situations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Dimensional analysis interrogates an expression at the level of units, where "in a physically valid equation, the units on both sides must agree". Checking homogeneity realizes the evaluate function; read as indicating what kind of quantity a composite term represents, it also describes situations, as when the quotient of mass and drag coefficient "carries the unit s", identifying the time constant of a drag fall.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quantities must carry units, so that the units on both sides of an equation can be compared
#### Constraints
- Dimensional homogeneity is "a necessary but not a sufficient condition for physical plausibility", and dimensionless factors such as 4𝜋 escape the check entirely

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating the physical plausibility of equations
- describing physical situations from the units of composite terms
