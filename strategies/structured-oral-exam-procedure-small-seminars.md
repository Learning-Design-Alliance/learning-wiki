# Structured 20-minute oral exam procedure with scheduled timeslots, verbal-or-whiteboard answering, and immediate debrief

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students schedule timeslots over three or four days and complete "two full question categories (six total questions) over the course of 20 minutes (10 minutes per category)" in a private space. Students receive questions on a half sheet of paper and may answer verbally or on a whiteboard; the instructor debriefs performance and feelings before releasing the student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A suitable private meeting space and a schedule of individual timeslots
- A few minutes of extra built-in time to allow students to go over slightly and to transition between back-to-back meetings
#### Constraints
- The maximum class size in which the author has used this system is 15 students, corresponding to five hours of student meetings
- Non-native English speakers reported feeling additional time pressure

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics and astronomy majors in classes of 7-15 students

### Target Learning Goals
- assessment of conceptual understanding under oral questioning
