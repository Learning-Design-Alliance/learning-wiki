# Speculative recommendations for units adopting paired teaching to disseminate RBIS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on preliminary results, the article offers recommendations for units considering paired teaching as an RBIS dissemination framework: "Ask instructors to volunteer (or even apply) to pair-teach"; "Place teaching pairs in courses where interactive materials already exist"; "Carefully map out future teaching assignments for pair-instructors"; and hold an orientation for teaching pairs to clarify goals and expectations and support a positive professional relationship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A department with existing expertise in evidence-based teaching and courses already transformed to active learning
#### Constraints
- The authors label these recommendations speculative, based on preliminary case study results whose conclusions may not generalize

### Target Learners
- University faculty novices in research-based instructional strategies

### Target Learning Goals
- Adoption and transfer of research-based instructional strategies to future solo teaching

### Affordances
