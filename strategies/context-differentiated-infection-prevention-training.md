# Differentiate infection-prevention training for childcare providers by routine versus outbreak context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that "infection-prevention training and organizational guidelines in childcare facilities should be differentiated based on the demands of routine vs. outbreak contexts." Routine prevention calls for strengthening environmental management and educational guidance competencies, while outbreak preparedness calls for surveillance, personal management, rapid decision-making, and interstaff communication. They also suggest practical, need-based activities such as handwashing roleplay, ventilation checklists, and outbreak-response drills, and competency-based assessments in certification renewal.
