# Use comparative analysis of Chinese and international journal articles to develop multi-dimensional understanding and global research perspectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course requires each group to select one paper from a Chinese core nursing journal and one from a high-impact SCI journal as materials for class presentation. The authors state that "by comparing the two sources, students develop a multi-dimensional understanding of the research topic." This strategy also aims to broaden students' global perspectives and improve their ability to interpret evidence across cultural and disciplinary contexts, fostering what the authors call research literacy and global academic competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both Chinese core nursing journals and high-impact SCI journals for literature selection.
- Joint reading sessions to clarify each article's research background, design rationale, methodological approach, and key findings before presentation.
