# Use cooperative group work especially in areas requiring mutual trust, communication, and practice, and direct higher education students to cooperation often

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that cooperative learning effects are high in domains requiring social skills and at the higher education level, the author recommends that "teachers should make arrangements based on the cooperative learning model in their classroom practices" and that "group work should be included to ensure cooperation in areas where mutual trust, communication, and practice are necessary". At higher education levels, where collaboration showed a higher impact, students should be directed to cooperation and group work often.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom arrangements based on the cooperative learning model
- Group work in domains where mutual trust, communication, and practice are necessary
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on meta-analytic evidence in which K12 effects were lower (ES=.43) than higher education effects (ES=.91)

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- K12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning outcomes in cooperation-dependent domains
- social skills
