# Embed mastery-threshold questions in computerized health education, requiring at least 80% accuracy to advance through content domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Computer + Mastery intervention embedded questions in each knowledge domain (52 opioid, 46 opioid OD, 16 opioid OD response questions) and required participants to achieve at least 80% accuracy to advance; failure restarted the domain content, up to a maximum of three presentations. This operationalizes a mastery training approach the authors cite from behavioral health literature suggesting mastery requirements support knowledge gains relative to direct presentation. In this trial it produced no measurable advantage over computer presentation alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Embedded questions distinct from the primary outcome knowledge questions
- A mechanism to restart content on failure, with a cap on repetitions (here, three)
#### Constraints
- Corrective feedback for incorrect responses could not be provided in this implementation, which the authors suggest may explain the absence of superiority over computer-alone delivery
- Most participants needed repeated presentations in the opioid domain (only 36% successful on first presentation)

### Target Learners
- adults in treatment for opioid use disorder completing a brief computerized educational session

### Target Learning Goals
- demonstrated mastery of opioid effects, overdose symptoms, and overdose response content
