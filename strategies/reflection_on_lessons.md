# Reflection on Lessons

## Description
This strategy involves teachers reflecting on each lesson before and after teaching. Record what worked well and what could be improved. Keep these reflections organized alongside your lesson plans, whether digitally or in a journal, and revisit them to make necessary adjustments for future lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection, a way to record reflections, and a willingness to make adjustments for future lessons.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to reflect on each lesson.
