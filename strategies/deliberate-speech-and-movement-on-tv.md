# Instructors on television should speak at a deliberate rate and use deliberate movements

## Description
The abstract's conclusions on instructor presentation specify two on-camera behaviors: "A deliberate rate of speaking is desirable" and "Deliberate movements by the instructor are mandatory". These are implementable presenter techniques for anyone teaching over television, derived from the army training research summarized on the page.
