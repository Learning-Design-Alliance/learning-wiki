# Microlearning-based e-learning modules with short video lectures can address unmet training needs for distance learners with poor internet bandwidth

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes how Uppsala Monitoring Centre developed e-learning modules using "the microlearning approach based on small learning units (lessons) connected to specific learning objectives". Each module was divided into concise video lectures of 3–5 minutes. This format was chosen to address technological challenges such as unstable internet connections and to facilitate 'just-in-time' training and repetition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning management system platform
- Video lecture production capability
- Pilot testing with a selected group of testers
#### Constraints
- Requires internet connectivity, though modules are designed to work with poor bandwidth
- Course content is specific to pharmacovigilance topics

### Target Learners
- Pharmacovigilance professionals
- National PV centre staff
- Pharmaceutical industry staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Signal detection concepts
- Causality assessment
- Statistical reasoning and algorithms in pharmacovigilance
