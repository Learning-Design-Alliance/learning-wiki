# Use evaluation findings to guide replication of effective principal preparation tools and strategies across charter, traditional public, and preparation-field settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evaluation positions its findings as usable beyond KIPP: they "can guide the replication of effective tools and strategies that increase the number of highly effective principals among other charter and traditional public schools as well as across the principal preparation field". This includes replicating the selection instrument and leadership development approaches examined in the three programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must demonstrate effectiveness through evaluation before replication is attempted
#### Constraints
- Findings are specific to KIPP's three leadership programs and their contexts

### Target Learners
- aspiring and practicing school principals in charter and traditional public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- school leadership effectiveness
- principal preparation
