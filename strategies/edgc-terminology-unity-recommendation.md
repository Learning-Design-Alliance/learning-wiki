# Recommend future studies develop a common concept or unity of terminology for educational digital games

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the word analysis found the same meaning attached to different concepts (teaching game, educational game, edutainment game), the article recommends that "future studies on this topic focus on developing a common concept or unity of terminology, or seek to determine whether these terms are used accurately". This is a direct author recommendation grounded in the terminology-diversity finding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers must be aware of the multiple existing terms (video games, computer games, educational games, serious games) when searching or classifying studies.
#### Constraints
- The article notes definitions/classifications are made according to the platform in which games operate, and smartphones and tablets have taken the place of computers, yet video games and computer games remain popular.

### Target Learners
- researchers studying educational digital games for children

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent terminology and classification in educational digital game research
