# Future research should establish causality of the PA-academic achievement relationship with high-quality RCTs, standardised outcome measures, and attention to moderators

## Description
The review's future research section identifies priorities for strengthening this evidence base. It states "the most prominent being the need to establish the causality of the relationship between PA and AA", recommending high-quality RCTs with multiple PA intervention arms plus a non-active control, collaboration between exercise scientists and neuroscientists, standardised outcome measures, standardised PA interventions, and consideration of relevant moderators such as SES, family support, age and sex. Researchers are advised to publish practical articles with education specialists so findings can be implemented by school administrators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- RCTs with different PA intervention arms in addition to a non-active control group
- Standardised outcome measures and standardised PA interventions
#### Constraints
- Current evidence base is limited by heterogeneous interventions, populations, designs, outcome measures and non-standardised AA definitions, per the review's limitations section

### Target Learners
- school-age children and adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
