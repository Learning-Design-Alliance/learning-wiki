# Notify test proctors when students disengage so students give their best effort on testing day

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has test systems alert proctors in real time when a student shows disengagement, so the proctor can intervene and restore effort. The brief frames it as a way to "help make sure students give their best effort on testing day," positioning proctor notification as an actionable response to disengagement during testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A testing system or observation mechanism that can detect student disengagement during the test
- Proctors available to receive notifications and act on them during the session
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- K-12 students taking standardized tests

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate measurement of achievement through maximal student test effort
