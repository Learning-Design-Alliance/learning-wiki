# Soliciting Feedback on Instruction

## Description
Instructors should encourage learners to provide feedback on their teaching. By actively soliciting student feedback, instructors can gain insights into their teaching effectiveness, identify areas for improvement, and create a more engaging and supportive learning environment. If we are open to it, we will quickly learn a few things about ourselves as educators. Remember that feedback goes both ways and as teachers it is wise to never stop improving and honing our skills as teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive environment, clear channels for providing feedback, willingness to listen and act on feedback
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to receive negative feedback, requires a commitment to continuous improvement
