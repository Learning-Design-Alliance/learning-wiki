# Embed assessment in meaningful classroom activity such as debates and collaborative projects

## Description
This strategy places assessment inside the learning activity itself instead of pulling students out of learning to test them. Gordon gives concrete instances: "a classroom debate can serve as an assessment of argumentation skills while also providing students with cycles of preparation and feedback regarding how to formulate and defend ideas," and a collaborative applied research project can assess problem-solving and teamwork while building those skills through practice. Feedback is immediate and natural, coming from peers and the teacher during the activity, and students often find the experience more engaging and relevant.
