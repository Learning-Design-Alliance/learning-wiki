# Recommendations for creating and conducting game-based learning experiences with teacher-authored games

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's conclusions section lists concrete recipes for teachers. For game creation: employ simple mechanics, "Design the game by properly balancing the ludic and the didactic aspects" with small learning objects (e.g., 1-2 minutes), trigger learning objects periodically (e.g., every 30-60 seconds for 1-2 minute objects), and combine theoretical content with self-grading questions. For conducting experiences: test the game beforehand, limit GBL experiences to about 1 or 2 per semester course, design sessions of 30-90 minutes, and include additional activities such as an introductory lesson, a debriefing session, and pre/post tests.
