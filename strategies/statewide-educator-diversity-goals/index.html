Set clear numeric educator-diversity goals at the state, district, and teacher preparation levels with public progress reporting¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The second policy lever is to "Create statewide goals to strengthen the teacher workforce" that districts can align with and report progress against, while encouraging educator preparation programs to set recruitment and retention goals for teacher candidates of color. Arkansas is highlighted for a numeric goal to increase teachers of color in public schools by 25% by 2025 and a requirement that all districts post a recruitment and retention plan.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Statewide goals must be public so districts can align and report progress; districts must create and post recruitment and retention plans

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • state leaders
  • district administrators
  • educator preparation programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • increasing the number of teachers of color recruited and retained

Related Strategies¶

  • Make educator diversity data visible and actionable by reporting teacher diversity on state dashboards
  • Six state-level strategies for increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce
  • Set measurable, time-bound goals for school leader diversity and make diversity data publicly visible
  • Publish school-level educator race/ethnicity data and program-completer and retention data on state dashboards
  • Invest in retention efforts for teachers of color that improve working conditions and provide personal and professional growth opportunities
