# Use AI chatbots as supplementary, not primary, resources in entrance-exam preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that healthcare-science candidates use AI chatbots to complement traditional study. Chatbots "can complement the traditional learning methods such as textbooks or in-person courses", can facilitate self-directed learning, provide explanations, select and filter materials, and be personalized to individual students' needs. The authors explicitly encourage candidates "to cautiously incorporate this new technology to supplement their learning rather than a primary resource".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates must be protected from using these tools to answer questions during administered exams; encouraging honesty by avoiding devices in classrooms is important
#### Constraints
- Chatbots cannot replace the value of reflection and discussion with peers and teachers, which are crucial for developing meta-competencies

### Target Learners
- Candidates preparing for the Italian entrance university standardized examination test of healthcare science

### Target Learning Goals
- Exam preparation knowledge and self-directed learning skills
