# Recruits should enhance high-intensity running, change-of-direction speed and aerobic fitness prior to academy to improve their chances of graduation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that graduates were faster in the 75-yard pursuit run and completed more MSFT shuttles than some separation groups, the article advises that recruits "should enhance their high-intensity running and change-of-direction speed (including strength, power, and sprint ability) and aerobic fitness prior to academy". It adds that recruits should learn the physical training practices of their specific academy so they can prepare for that academy's rigors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article cautions these results could have been influenced by the LEA's own PT practices, which involved high volumes of running

### Target Learning Goals
- successful academy graduation
