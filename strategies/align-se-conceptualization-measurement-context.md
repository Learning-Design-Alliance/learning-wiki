# Align conceptualization, definition, measurement, and context when researching student engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future SE research in undergraduate mathematics align its conceptualizations, definitions, measurement, and context to foster a shared understanding and guide interventions. It argues researchers should be guided by their SE conceptualization and research questions and "accordingly specify the level of granularity, data types and methods", choosing the unit-of-analysis of time, task, and agent. It also proposes convergence of data channels and methods, since each channel captures only specific components of SE.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit statement of the learning activity on which SE focuses (the object of student engagement)
- Specification of granularity (time, task, agent) before selecting measurement methods
#### Constraints
- Each measurement channel captures only specific components of SE and each method has strengths and weaknesses

### Target Learners
- undergraduate mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate measurement and support of engagement in mathematics learning activities

### Affordances
- Se Multidimensional Activity Specific Framework

## Claims

- [Most instruction-focused SE research in undergraduate mathematics conceptualizes engagement as the outcome of student-centred learning environments and optimized instruction](../claims/instruction-focused-se-frameworks-math.md) [+W]
- [Fewer than half of undergraduate mathematics SE studies define engagement explicitly, and definitions vary in their use of multidimensionality, psychological investment, and observable behavior](../claims/se-definitions-heterogeneous-math.md) [+W]
- [Most undergraduate mathematics SE studies aim to evaluate instructional methods and tools, with fewer aiming to understand learning behavior or examine factors of academic success](../claims/se-math-three-research-aims.md) [+W]
- [SE measurement in undergraduate mathematics relies predominantly on log files for online behavioral engagement and self-reports for course participation, with behavioral engagement the most examined dimension](../claims/se-measurement-logfiles-selfreports-math.md) [+W]
- [Less than half of undergraduate mathematics SE studies examined the relationship between engagement and learning outcomes, using variable-oriented and person-oriented approaches](../claims/se-outcomes-link-understudied-math.md) [+W]
