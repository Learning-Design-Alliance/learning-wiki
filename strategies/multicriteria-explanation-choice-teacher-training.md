# Give the limits of mathematical efficiency an explicit place in teacher training and favour multi-criteria evaluation of explanations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, if one defends helping teachers choose explanations on the basis of prior multi-criteria evaluations, then "les limites de l’efficience mathématique justifient des recherches ultérieures et une place explicite dans les formations d’enseignants". Training should help teachers appreciate "les conditions qui font parfois de l’efficience mathématique – considérée seule - le mobile de décisions pédagogiques hautement contestables", and see that the very goals of physics education are at stake in this debate.
