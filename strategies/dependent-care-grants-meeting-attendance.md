# Offer dependent-care grants funded by a mini-grant to remove family-care barriers to meeting attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The section used an AAPT Section Mini-Grant to fund stipends for attendees with dependent-care obligations: "We offered a $75 stipend to attendees who needed dependent care for one person, and $150 for more than one". Originally aimed at members who stopped attending after having young children, it was expanded to any dependent care need, including elderly parents. Applications ran through a short Google Form, funds were granted at the meeting, and unclaimed allocations were returned to the account.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A funding source such as an AAPT Section Mini-Grant; a short application process administered before the meeting
#### Constraints
- Expect no-shows roughly one-third of the time because the program intentionally reaches people with regular attendance barriers; stipend amounts were tailored to Northern California cost of living

### Target Learners
- Physics teachers with family-care obligations attending section meetings

### Target Learning Goals
- Enabling attendance at professional meetings for members barred by caregiving responsibilities
