# Use causal inference-based research designs to explore observed group differences in selection assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that future research on differential attainment apply causal inference designs to observed subgroup differences, so that discussion of this sensitive issue becomes more sophisticated and informed. It argues identifying causal mechanisms is the fundamental issue, asking whether problems lie in test design, how students view the examination, or how they prepare for it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research must move beyond describing group differences to identifying causal mechanisms for differential attainment
#### Constraints
- The article notes the reasons underlying subgroup differences are complex and in need of far more sophisticated research

### Target Learning Goals
