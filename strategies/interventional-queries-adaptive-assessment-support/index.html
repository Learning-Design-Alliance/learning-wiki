Use interventional queries to inform support decisions in adaptive assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article suggests that the causal framework's properties, particularly in settings where students can influence the conditions under which they complete tasks, enable principled disentangling of behavioural factors such as help-seeking from underlying proficiency. As the authors state, "These properties suggest possible applications in adaptive assessment scenarios, where decisions about offering support could be informed by interventional queries." This is a forward-looking recommendation rather than a tested deployment.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A validated causal learner model with elicited structural equations and assessment data

Constraints¶

  • The authors do not claim evidence regarding the framework's impact on decision-making processes; real-time deployment within an adaptive testing system is identified as future work

Target Learners¶

  • compulsory school students in adaptive assessment settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • distinguishing help-seeking behaviour from proficiency
