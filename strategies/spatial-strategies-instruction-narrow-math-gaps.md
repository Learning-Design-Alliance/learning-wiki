# Incorporate explicit spatial strategies into mathematics instruction to help narrow sex differences in later-grade, spatially intensive content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is that the content- and grade-specific gaps they document might be narrowed through instruction that explicitly builds spatial strategies in relevant mathematics areas. They state that "the gap might be narrowed with the development of interventions that explicitly incorporate the use of spatial strategies into instruction in some mathematics areas." This targets the growing male advantage in geometry and spatially intensive content in later grades. The recommendation is grounded in the meta-analytic patterns rather than a tested intervention in this article.
