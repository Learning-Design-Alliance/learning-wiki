# Use trending video games such as Fortnite as a hook to involve students in the chemistry classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes leveraging a trending video game to draw students into general chemistry, arguing that the use of trending games is a good way to get students involved. Because Fortnite gained enough popularity to make students excited to play and to carry it into the classroom, the author builds chemistry activities around in-game items students recognize, such as consumables, weapons, and building materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students should be familiar with the game so that the in-game items are recognizable and motivating
#### Constraints
- The game is described as more oriented toward physics than chemistry, so chemistry content must be selected carefully from the game's items

### Target Learners
- general chemistry students
- secondary and undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement with chemistry content
- connecting everyday popular culture to chemical concepts
