# Select participants for an advanced enrichment project by Fermi estimation questions that test physical reasoning rather than standard preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy recruits students for an advanced enrichment project by having registered applicants answer a small set of Fermi estimation questions, such as estimating the speed of hair growth in km/h. The author reports the aim was to identify students with "appropriate ability in 'physical' reasoning" rather than merely good standard preparation, and ten pupils aged about 17 were selected for the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pool of registered volunteer applicants to test with the estimation questions
- A small set of Fermi questions suited to the project's domain
#### Constraints
- Selection was performed at the beginning of December in the source project, which shaped one of the questions

### Target Learners
- outstanding high-school or undergraduate students with strong physical reasoning

### Target Learning Goals
- physical reasoning and estimation skills
- historical experimental inquiry in optics
