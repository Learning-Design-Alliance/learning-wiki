# Notetaking practices: verbatim statements, contextual notes, and 24-hour review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The notetaker guidance instructs staff to 'document participants' statements in their own words' to ensure accuracy and provide direct quotes, to take 'contextual details such as participant demographics... group dynamics... or notable reactions', since 'Both spoken and unspoken observations are important for qualitative analysis.' Within 24 hours after the session, notetakers review and clarify notes, expand shorthand, and upload finalized notes to secure storage.
