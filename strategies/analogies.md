# Analogies

## Description
Analogies are comparisons of two things that are alike in some way, used as advance organizers to help students realize that a new topic relates to something familiar. By linking new information to prior knowledge, analogies facilitate understanding and retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear understanding of the target concept and a relevant, familiar analogy.
#### Constraints
- Analogies must be carefully chosen to ensure they accurately represent the target concept and do not introduce misconceptions.

### Target Learners

- All grade levels, particularly useful for introducing complex or abstract topics.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Connect new information to existing knowledge, facilitate understanding of complex concepts, and improve retention.

### Affordances
- Enhances understanding by relating new information to familiar concepts, making abstract ideas more concrete, and improving engagement.

### Personalization
- Analogies can be tailored to the specific prior knowledge of students and the complexity of the topic.
