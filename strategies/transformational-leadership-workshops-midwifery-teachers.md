# Run workshops to familiarize midwifery teachers with the transformational leadership style

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is that midwifery educators be trained in transformational leadership, which emphasizes supportive behavior, to improve students' self-efficacy. They state that "through bolding workshops, the teachers can be familiarized with this style and take steps toward improvement of students’ self-efficacy." The recommendation rests on the study's correlational findings, not on a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers in clinical midwifery education settings; the article assumes leadership style can be changed through training workshops.
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and correlational; no workshop intervention was tested in this study.

### Target Learners
- midwifery students in clinical education

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical self-efficacy in midwifery practice
