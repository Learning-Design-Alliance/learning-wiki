# Have students verbally share personal life and reading experiences with others in exploratory dialogues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
A second design principle the review derives concerns classroom dialogue: "verbally sharing these experiences with others in exploratory dialogues". Students talk about the personal life and reading experiences they have activated and written about, in an exploratory rather than evaluative mode. The review presents this as an implementable activity recipe drawn from the interventions with empirical support.
