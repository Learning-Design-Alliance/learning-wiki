# Test LVG in a realistic operating-like environment with an on/off laser control and bilateral instruments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future LVG research be "performed in a more realistic testing environment, similar to that of real laparoscopic operations", since the flat, static box trainer may have obscured the depth cues. They also suggest developing an on/off button so the novice can choose when to use the grid, and making the laser available on both instruments to avoid disadvantaging left-handed users.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future trials with an adjusted prototype and realistic test environment
#### Constraints
- Current evidence comes from a single-center simulated trial with a first prototype; utility during actual operations remains untested

### Target Learners
- surgical novices in laparoscopic training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic skills acquisition
