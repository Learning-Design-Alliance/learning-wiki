# Close a geometry lesson by having the learner apply the new formula back to the object that opened it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter ends by returning to the soccer ball that motivated the lesson, now reinterpreted: its pentagons and hexagons are Great Circle polygons whose interior angles exceed 540 and 720 degrees respectively, "and the diﬀerence is proportional to their area." This bookend structure lets the learner see how much explanatory power was gained, and then extends the idea to all polygons on the sphere and Euler's formula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
