# Formalize peer tutoring through clear program structures, tutor training in pedagogy and communication, and recognition or incentives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions deliberately formalize and support peer tutoring initiatives rather than leaving them informal. It states this "includes developing clear program structures, offering training sessions for peer tutors, and providing recognition or incentives that validate their contributions." Tutor training should cover not only academic content but also pedagogical methods, communication strategies and boundary setting, and programs should address linguistic diversity and tutor preparation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors must be trained in academic content, pedagogical methods, communication strategies and boundary setting for programs to succeed.
#### Constraints
- Sessions need a clear structure and adequate time; online sessions and exam-period timing limited relationship building in this study.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students, particularly tutees with GPA below 2.5 and tutors with GPA above 3.5

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic comprehension and exam performance
- communication skills
- emotional resilience and confidence
- professional competencies such as empathy and collaboration
