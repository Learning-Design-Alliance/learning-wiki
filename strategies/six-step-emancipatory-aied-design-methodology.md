# Six-step design methodology: elicit values, minimal intervention, multi-level multi-outcome evaluation, iterate, simulate scale, sunset

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper outlines a design process expanding design thinking, value-sensitive design, and participatory design. Steps are: elicit stakeholder values and perform contextual inquiry (using rapid methods like VALA/AID where possible); design a minimal viable prototype; deploy in authentic settings and evaluate effects at multiple levels and outcomes and timeframes; iteratively revise aiming for good enough results across levels; model and simulate diffusion at wider scales; and repeat until ripple effects can no longer be measured, then sunset the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- longitudinal, multi-level measurement of multiple outcomes during deployment
#### Constraints
- the definition of concrete design methods that align with this vision, such as resolving value tensions, remains an open challenge

### Target Learners
- learners in authentic educational settings, from single classrooms to school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- cognition and knowledge
- metacognitive ability
- well-being
