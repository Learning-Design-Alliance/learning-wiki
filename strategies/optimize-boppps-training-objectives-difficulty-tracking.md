# Continuously optimize BOPPPS-simulation training by refining objectives, personalizing scenario difficulty, and tracking long-term outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future implementations focus on "dynamically refining training focus and clarifying objectives using pre-assessment data", on strengthening the instructional team's capabilities in scenario facilitation and feedback delivery, and on cultivating a sustainable teaching-quality culture. In the conclusion it names three directions: goal precision, process personalization (dynamically adjusting scenario complexity based on pre-assessment results), and systematic assessment using frameworks such as Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Evaluation Model for long-term tracking. It also suggests applying the ADDIE model and exploring virtual reality to support continuous improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-assessment data available to drive difficulty adjustment; structured evaluation frameworks such as Kirkpatrick's model; investment of more time in the Objective phase
#### Constraints
- The authors state the integrated model demands exceptionally high execution fidelity and that mechanically applying the framework can undermine the immersive quality of the simulation

### Target Learners
- Junior operating room circulating nurses in in-service training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate objective setting, personalized simulation difficulty, and long-term skill transfer to clinical practice
