# Schools assessing and tracking student depression and delivering school-based depression prevention programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends schools as the institutional site for protecting youth well-being: because most young people attend school, "schools are well placed to assess and track depression and other well-being indicators", and schools can liaise between families and community services. It further notes that "school-based depression and anxiety prevention programs have been shown to be effective, particularly for students that had high levels of depression", and that a positive school social climate can buffer depression and promote positive emotions.
