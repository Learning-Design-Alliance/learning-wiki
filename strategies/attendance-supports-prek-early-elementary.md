## Description
The article recommends providing supports to improve attendance in pre-kindergarten and early elementary grades, arguing that improving attendance in these years "has the potential to reduce achievement gaps and redirect struggling students onto the pathway toward educational success." The recommendation follows directly from the study's findings on chronic absence and later outcomes.
