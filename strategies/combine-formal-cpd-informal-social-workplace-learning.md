# Restructure clinical environments to combine outcomes-oriented formal CPD with informal, social workplace learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend creating optimal environments for lifelong learning through contextual changes: "restructuring clinical work to create more time and space for social learning processes such as observation, feedback conversations, co-learning", alongside high-quality formal CPD such as case-based conferences, interactive workshops, and journal clubs, and improved performance data as feedback for self-regulated learning. This embraces informal learning's relevance and timeliness while addressing its unreliability when physicians must self-identify gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Investment in structures and systems reinforcing contextual and social learning processes
#### Constraints
- Demanding clinical environments and productivity pressures increasingly limit capacity for independent lifelong learning

### Target Learners
- academic physicians
- practicing clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehensive lifelong learning spanning all domains of medical practice
