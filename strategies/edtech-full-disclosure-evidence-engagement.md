# Require full disclosure of an EdTech product's development and evaluation maturity, and assess developers' willingness to engage with research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that, in evaluation processes, schools, procurement teams and funders assess both the quality of provided evidence (e.g. methodological quality) and how developers view and apply evidence in their work. They state "EdTech products should provide a full disclosure on the stage of development/level of maturity in their design, development, implementation, and evaluation process for the respective product".
