# Team-Building Through Problem-Based Learning

## Description
Using problem-based learning in multi-cultural groups helps learners get used to working in a multi-cultural environment. Though diversity in the group may hamper coming to a quick solution, one is eventually reached. In the process, the group will also learn to put aside their differences, understand the way other cultures work, and find a way to work together as a team and come up with a solution

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A diverse group of learners, a facilitator to guide the process, and a problem that requires collaborative problem-solving.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to manage conflicts and ensure equal participation from all members. Requires careful facilitation and attention to group dynamics.

- Teams can be formed based on diverse backgrounds and skill sets. Problems can be tailored to reflect real-world multicultural challenges. Ground rules can be tailored to accommodate different opinions.
