# Design e-learning platforms with collaboration modules, quality content, diverse assessment, and navigable structure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that e-learning platforms "provide technological features to enable a collaboration environment", that content "should be retrievable, useful, understandable, interesting, and reliable", that institutions "design various ways of self-assessment through quizzes, tests, and other ways of testing knowledge", and that systems be easy to navigate and well-structured to increase satisfaction and usage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration features enabling communication between students
- Content that is retrievable, useful, understandable, interesting, and reliable
- Multiple self-assessment methods
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a single survey study in Brazilian higher education

### Target Learners
- higher education students in blended and fully online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- e-learning satisfaction
- e-learning use
- individual performance
