# Circulate and Listen During Pair Talk

## Description
Actively circulating and listening to pair discussions allows teachers to monitor student engagement, reinforce expectations, offer support, and gauge the success of the thinking process. This provides valuable information for informing future instruction and addressing misconceptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to be actively present and engaged during pair talk activities, moving around the classroom and listening to conversations.
#### Constraints
- Requires balancing monitoring with allowing students to have private conversations. Teachers must avoid being intrusive or disruptive.

### Affordances
- Allows teachers to check for understanding, address misconceptions, provide encouragement, and highlight good ideas. Ensures that students stay on task and engage fully in the discussion.

### Personalization
- Teachers can focus their attention on students who need the most support or who have specific learning needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the level of student engagement and participation. Listen for evidence of understanding or misconceptions. Note the types of questions students are asking and the connections they are making.

## Impact
- Active circulation and listening can improve the effectiveness of pair talk activities, leading to better student understanding and engagement.
