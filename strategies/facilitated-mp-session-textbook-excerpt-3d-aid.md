# Facilitated 25-minute mental practice session with step-by-step textbook breakdown, optionally supported by an interactive 3D anatomical model, before simulated surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention protocol: a facilitator demonstrates verbalised mental preparation, participants receive a surgical textbook excerpt with "a step-by-step breakdown of a LC" and are asked to "visualise" and "feel" the operation during a single 25-minute session; the 3D group additionally inspects the interactive model at each step. Simulator familiarisation (tool selection, diathermy pedals) is provided beforehand. The authors suggest the combined approach could extend to patient-specific rehearsals and novice didactic sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator present during the MP session
- Participants already familiar with the procedure (advanced beginners), so no procedural teaching is needed
- Access to the 3D reconstruction workflow for the aided variant
#### Constraints
- Exploratory evidence from a small case-matched study (n = 20); the authors state generalizability may be limited and call for a larger powered RCT

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees preparing for laparoscopic procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- pre-procedural preparation and technical performance on laparoscopic cholecystectomy
