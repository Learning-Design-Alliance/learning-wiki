# Design modeling assessment items to elicit students' justifications for their modeling pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their interview findings, the authors argue that modeling assessments for upper-division labs should probe competence with most modeling subtasks and should ask students to justify why they chose particular modeling pathways. This follows from instructors reporting that all subtasks matter and that modeling involves recursive, context-dependent choices. Test objectives derived from instructor goals anchor the assessment to widely shared course aims.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Test objectives established from empirical evidence about instructors' course goals
- Test contexts familiar to students across many institutions, e.g., photodiodes and op-amps
#### Constraints
- Modeling is one of several nationally recommended outcomes, so subtask prioritization varies by course

### Target Learners
- upper-division optics and electronics lab students

### Target Learning Goals
- model-based reasoning in experimental physics
