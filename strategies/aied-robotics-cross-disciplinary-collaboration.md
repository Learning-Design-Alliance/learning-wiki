# Build cross-disciplinary collaboration between AIED, robotics and electrical engineering to develop educational cobots

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends that AIED researchers reach out to roboticists and electrical engineers, since 'doing cross-disciplinary work is really hard' and each team member needs at least a rudimentary understanding of the other partners' fields. Concrete steps include joint workshops or summits, visits to each other's research labs, sabbatical visits, exchanges of doctoral students or postdocs, and persuading funding agencies to sponsor proof-of-concept work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding-agency support for 'getting to know you' events and joint research identification
- Time to build shared knowledge across disciplines
#### Constraints
- The paper notes that until the fields understand one another, ways to work together cannot be fully identified

### Target Learners
- AIED and robotics researchers and doctoral students

### Target Learning Goals
