# Use OPS-English vocabulary-based activities across the school day to reinforce word meaning where direct instruction time is limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Facing students with a deficit in second-language vocabulary and teachers with limited time for direct instruction, the researcher advises developing creative methods to expose students to vocabulary in many ways that develop and reinforce word meaning throughout the school day, using OPS-English vocabulary-based activities in the classroom to build students' vocabulary knowledge.
