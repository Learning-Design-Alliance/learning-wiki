Strategies

Use extracurricular activities such as learners' conferences and seminars to overcome lesson-time limits for biographies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article identifies limited lesson time as a core problem for studying scientists' biographies and proposes a concrete implementation route: "Extracurricular activities (e.g., conducting of learners' conferences, seminars) can solve the first problem." In the reported implementation, biographical material was reported at lessons and during extracurricular activities, and learners prepared reports about scientists, which the author says formed interest in the school subject.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must go beyond the textbook and use other sources of information such as books and websites

Constraints¶

  • Demonstrated in Ukrainian secondary physics teaching; the author notes the method can extend to other school subjects

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school learners, ages 13-15

Target Learning Goals¶

  • interest in physics
  • reading additional literature on physics
  • learners' own research activity

Related Strategies¶

  • Build safety and belonging by detracking, offering low-barrier extracurriculars, and implementing restorative approaches in place of exclusionary discipline
  • Implement extensive reading via stand-alone courses, curricular components, or reading clubs, with self-report accountability and no comprehension testing
