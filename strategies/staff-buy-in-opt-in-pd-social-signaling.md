# Build staff buy-in and mastery through opt-in professional development, social signaling, and preparation for fallback moments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends developing staff mastery of restorative practices because programming often fails to shift school practices when staff hesitate to adopt them. It states that "Research suggests that professional development may be more effective if provided to teachers who opt in to restorative practices training." It further recommends achieving buy-in via proactive discussions and early trainings, using social signaling—publicly displaying prosocial behavior to encourage more of it—and preparing leaders for 'fallback' moments when temporary setbacks tempt staff to abandon the practices.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
