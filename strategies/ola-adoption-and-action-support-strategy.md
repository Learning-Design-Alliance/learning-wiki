# Conduct large-scale studies of OLA framework adoption and learning impact, and close the loop with learner support actions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's pedagogical future directions call for evaluating frameworks at scale and for action support: "if open learning analytics frameworks are to serve the intended objective of achieving learning analytics at scale, large- scale studies are to be conducted with a focus on evaluating these frameworks regarding adoption and learning impact." It further states that current technical frameworks focus on data and analysis rather than supporting learners and teachers, and that "open learning analytics research needs to widen its focus to pay attention to closing the loop by exploring the use of data to provide effective learner support actions." It also calls for more work on human-centered OLA with effective user involvement in design, analysis, and evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technical OLA frameworks that currently focus on data and analysis aspects would need concrete solutions for deploying support actions
#### Constraints
- The review notes research on the effects of open learning analytics frameworks is still in its early stages

### Target Learners
- Learners and teachers in learning scenarios supported by open learning analytics frameworks

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective learner support actions and demonstrated learning impact from analytics at scale
