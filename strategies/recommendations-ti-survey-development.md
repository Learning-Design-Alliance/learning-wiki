# Researchers developing technology integration surveys should explicitly define TI, ground instruments in theory, and report validity and reliability information.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes with "some more general recommendations to ensure terminological unambiguity in TI research." These include clearly defining TI, grounding instruments in explicit conceptual or theoretical frameworks, and documenting validity and reliability evidence. The review found that 10 instruments had vague or no validity indications and 11 had vague or no reliability indications, motivating these recommendations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit definition of TI and theoretical grounding for new instruments
#### Constraints
- Current instruments often lack documented validity and reliability information

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved measurement of technology integration in educational research
