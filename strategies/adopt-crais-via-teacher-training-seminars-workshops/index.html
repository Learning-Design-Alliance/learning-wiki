Adopt active-participation strategies such as CRAIS for mathematics teaching and train teachers through seminars and workshops¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that mathematics teaching move away from abstract-only instruction by adopting strategies involving students' active participation, specifically CRAIS. It calls for "Training and retraining programmes such as seminars and workshops" organized by government and professional associations such as STAN and MAN so teachers learn CRAIS. It further recommends that government purchase and distribute teaching materials to schools to make learning real and increase assimilation.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must be trained in CRAIS; concrete teaching materials must be available in schools

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one six-week quasi-experiment in four Nigerian public schools covering circles, volume of solids, and angles of elevation and depression

Target Learners¶

  • senior secondary school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

Affordances¶

Claims¶

  • Senior secondary students taught with CRAIS achieved significantly higher mathematics posttest scores than students taught with modified conventional teaching strategy [+M]
