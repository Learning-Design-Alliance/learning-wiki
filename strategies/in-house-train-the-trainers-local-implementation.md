# Implement annual local multi-professional intrapartum emergency training via in-house trainers trained at a central train-the-trainers programme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementation model: units send a multi-professional trainer team to a central two-day T3 programme, then run their own local one-day PROMPT courses in-house, aiming to train all maternity staff annually. The implementation package "provides tools and guidance to Maternity Units on how to implement annual, multi-professional, intrapartum emergencies training by 'in-house' trainers for all maternity workers in their own Units". Local training uses lectures, video demonstrations, and multi-professional rehearsals of simulated obstetric emergencies with simple tools and algorithms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A team of four multi-professional in-house trainers per unit attending the T3 programme
- A PROMPT Course-in-a-box with course materials
- Telephone/email assistance and practical support for local training teams
#### Constraints
- Training all staff within a year is requested but at the discretion of each individual unit; the design assesses pragmatic implementation

### Target Learners
- all maternity workers in a unit (midwives, obstetricians, anaesthetists)

### Target Learning Goals
- management of obstetric emergencies such as eclampsia, post-partum haemorrhage, maternal collapse, sepsis and shoulder dystocia
