# Assess and iterate the program through faculty review, end-of-semester project review, and senior exit interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend periodic whole-faculty assessment of progress and discussion of projects based on experience and student feedback, a review of the projects at the end of each semester to decide on necessary changes for future years, and attention to feedback gathered from students. At Loyola, significant feedback about FP came through Senior exit interviews in which students reflected on their formative first-year experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Whole-faculty meetings to periodically assess progress
- A mechanism for collecting student feedback such as exit interviews
#### Constraints
- The benefits of the program may trickle in slowly, so some level of patience is warranted

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- continuous improvement of the research course
- student voice in program development
