# Early identification and preventive school health approaches addressing trauma exposure to improve academic trajectories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review recommends early identification strategies and preventive school health approaches that address trauma exposure to improve academic trajectories and reduce downstream educational disengagement. It indicates that intervening on behavioral health and factors like school engagement, absenteeism, and academic achievement could result in improved school outcomes, naming these mediators as potential points of intervention. It concurrently emphasizes upstream, primary prevention of ACE exposures at community and policy levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional research on mediating pathways, potential protective factors, and identification of subpopulations most at risk as a critical first step in developing effective interventions
#### Constraints
- The review states this recommendation rests on preliminary findings and that additional research on the complex relationship is needed

### Target Learners
- US adolescents with cumulative ACE exposure, including youth with CPS contact and youth with autism spectrum disorder

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved academic trajectories, school engagement, attendance, achievement, and high school completion
