# Structure MDT-CBL as three modules: preparatory self-study, traditional CBL application, and consolidation by a specialist team

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors modified traditional CBL into three sequenced modules: (1) targeted preparatory self-study using textbooks and clinical cases, including literature searches; (2) direct patient interaction and collaborative case discussion with instructor guidance; and (3) small-group consolidation sessions delivered jointly with radiologists and ultrasonographers after students have encountered real cases. The article states these sessions "provide focused learning and supplementation, improving comprehensive mastery of the subject matter".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors collaborating with specialists like radiologists and ultrasonographers for the consolidation phase
- Students having already learned relevant basic medical sciences before the targeted pre-study
#### Constraints
- Described in the context of urology internship teaching for urogenital disease cases

### Target Learners
- medical interns with foundational medical knowledge

### Target Learning Goals
- independent thinking and problem posing
- clinical case analysis and management
- consolidation of multidisciplinary knowledge
