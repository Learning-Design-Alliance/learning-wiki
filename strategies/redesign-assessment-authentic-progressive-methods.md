# Redesign assessment toward progressive, authentic, and reflective methods rather than intensive paperwork

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To preserve academic integrity and assessment fairness in the age of GenAI, the article recommends moving away from traditional paper-based coursework. It states that "assessment methods need to be redesigned, focusing on progressive learning processes, authentic assessments, reflective debriefings, and real-life project work rather than intensive paperwork." The conclusion repeats that educators may need to redesign assessment techniques from traditional paper-based work to more progressive, reflective, and authentic methods, and previous studies are cited as suggesting promoting creativity, critical thinking, and higher-order thinking assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment designs that focus on learning processes and authentic performance rather than submittable paperwork

- Recommended as an author interpretation in the discussion, not a tested intervention in this study

### Target Learners
