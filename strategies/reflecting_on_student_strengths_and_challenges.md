# Reflecting on Student Strengths and Challenges

## Description
Educators identify specific student strengths and/or challenges related to learning, social-emotional learning or other domains, and uses aligned strategies to leverage student strengths to support student learning and address challenges. This involves a deep understanding of each student's individual profile and tailoring instruction to meet their unique needs. The educator uses student data and evidence to inform instruction and identify student strengths and challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student data (e.g., assessment results, observations), knowledge of student strengths and challenges, and a repertoire of instructional strategies.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant time and effort to understand each student's individual profile. May be challenging to implement in large classes or with limited resources.

### Affordances
- Addresses individual student needs, fosters a positive learning environment, promotes self-awareness and metacognition. Allows instructors to leverage existing strengths of learners to address areas of need.

### Personalization
- Strategies can be tailored to specific learning domains (e.g., literacy, numeracy, social-emotional learning). Interventions can be adapted based on student age, grade level, and specific learning needs.
