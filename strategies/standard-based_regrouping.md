# Standard-Based Regrouping

## Description
Form homogeneous groups based on learners' mastery of specific standards. Assess learners formatively through pre-assessments, warm-up questions, and exit tickets to identify standard deficits. Pool learners from different classes into groups focused on mastering specific standards. This method allows instructors to address gaps efficiently and ensures that learners who have already mastered a standard are not held back.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires formative assessments to identify standard deficits, flexible scheduling to pool learners from different classes, and resources aligned to specific standards.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure all learners are engaged and supported. Needs clear expectations to prevent off-task behavior. Requires ongoing data collection and analysis to ensure appropriate group placement.

### Affordances
- Allows instructors to focus on specific skills and standards. Provides targeted support for struggling learners. Ensures that advanced learners are not held back. Efficient use of instructional time.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying group sizes, providing differentiated tasks aligned to specific standards, and allowing learners to self-select standards to focus on.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners’ engagement, participation, and mastery of standards within groups. Collect data from formative assessments, exit tickets, and group projects. Track changes in performance following implementation of standard-based regrouping.

## Impact
- Mastery of multiple standards can be achieved in a single class period, improving learning outcomes.
