# Use cross-disciplinary thermodynamics education research and mark student-data studies to target gaps

## Description
The review recommends that instructors and researchers draw on thermodynamics education research from outside their own discipline, since the disciplinary literatures rarely cite one another. It states the hope that the Resource Letter "will spark more interdisciplinary interaction", and it created a code denoting articles that included student data, to point to the need for additional research on students' ideas in different disciplinary contexts. Instructors teaching life science students are named as a primary audience.
