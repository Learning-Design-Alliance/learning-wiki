# Use a frontier science facility's detector search as a platform for multi-audience outreach programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The LIGO Observatories run a layered outreach program for general public, K-12, undergraduates and educators, using the excitement of the gravitational-wave search. The article states the Observatories "use the excitement of the search for gravitational waves as a platform for increasing science interest and science literacy among all ages." Activities include tours, open houses, field trips, classroom visits, undergraduate internships and teacher professional development, often with partner organizations.
