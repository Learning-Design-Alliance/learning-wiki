## Description
World Wide Data Day (W2D2) is a one-day simplified masterclass event held in November or December each year, using measurements from the ATLAS and CMS detectors at the LHC. The article notes that "W2D2 measurements are from the ATLAS and CMS detectors in the LHC and can be completed in about two hours, from introduction to videoconference end". High school teachers organize the measurements at their own schools and connect their students to physicists in videoconferences held in a day-long shift.
