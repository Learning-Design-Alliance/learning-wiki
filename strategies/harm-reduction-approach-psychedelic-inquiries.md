# Adopt a harm reduction approach to unsanctioned psychedelic use inquiries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article asserts that a harm reduction approach would be helpful for chaplains faced with participant inquiries about the unsanctioned and unsafe use of psychedelics. This approach allows for a non-judgmental posture, respect for autonomy, and education. As popular media sources have increased cultural awareness of psychedelics, increasing numbers of individuals are expressing interest in participating.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Maintaining a non-judgmental posture
- Respect for participant autonomy
- Providing education about risks
#### Constraints
- The authors do not endorse the competencies for use in unsanctioned settings

### Target Learners
- Chaplains
- Spiritual care providers encountering questions about psychedelic use

### Target Learning Goals
- Responding safely and ethically to inquiries about unsanctioned psychedelic use
