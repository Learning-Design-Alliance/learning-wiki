## Description
Beyond training design, the discussion draws a technology implication: the study "indicates the need for more sensitive instruments that allow for accurate haptic feedback". The recommendation is to develop laparoscopic instruments that transmit tissue-interaction forces to the trainee, complementing the benefit the study found for pre-task haptic exploration of models.
