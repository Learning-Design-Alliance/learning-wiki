# Math Journals

## Description
Math journals are a way to gauge a student’s understanding of particular math concepts. Students use their math journals to communicate their conceived strengths and personal concerns and reflect on their learning experiences through various journal prompts. Teachers can check the journals periodically and provide feedback to individual students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Math journals, journal prompts, and a way for teachers to check the journals and provide feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to check the journals and provide feedback.
