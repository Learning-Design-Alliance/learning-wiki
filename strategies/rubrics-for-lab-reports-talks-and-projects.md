## Description
The article's short-term priority response to assessment feedback is to 'establish and use rubrics for the creation and evaluation of lab notebooks, oral talks, written papers, and final projects.' The need arose from the customized SALG survey, where 'the course grading guidelines and student feedback on written reports were not rated as very helpful in promoting learning,' prompting development of rubrics to clarify expectations and standardize and speed up grading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubrics must clarify expectations and standardize and speed up the grading process
#### Constraints
- Parts of the course lacked adequate assessment tools linked with the learning goals, mainly the oral and written reports and final projects

- upper-division undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
