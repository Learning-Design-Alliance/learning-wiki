# Exploration Station

## Description
Establishing a dedicated computer as a station for exploration allows learners to discover their preferred composition pathway. Learners may utilize modes linearly (linguistic to audio to visual), begin by browsing digital images and audio files before writing, or use a recursive process, moving back and forth between drafts in digital, audio, and linguistic forms. Providing space for exploration creates ownership and values innovation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a dedicated computer with access to digital tools and resources. Needs a supportive environment that values exploration and experimentation.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who prefer structured guidance. Learners may need support in managing their time and staying focused.
