# Gather simulation educator feedback to shape future module topics and distribution design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors used NMS suggestions from the interviews to plan future Super Divya modules and the roll-out to Nurse Mentors. About half of NMS wanted more facilitator skills content, including "videos of Super Divya facilitating simulation training, how to mentor nurses, communication techniques," and five NMS wanted videos on how to run debriefing. Thirteen NMS requested clinical skills and complications content such as breech delivery and postpartum hemorrhage. Feedback also prompted work on an online Learning Management System and consideration of an app for distribution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A channel for collecting educator feedback after module distribution, such as the semi-structured interviews used in this study.
#### Constraints
- Suggestions reflect the perceived needs of NMS in Bihar; the authors note the study's limited generalizability beyond this tailored context.

### Target Learners
- Nurse Mentor Supervisors
- Nurse Mentors in the nurse mentoring program

### Target Learning Goals
- Simulation facilitation and debriefing skills
- Management of obstetric complications such as postpartum hemorrhage and breech delivery
