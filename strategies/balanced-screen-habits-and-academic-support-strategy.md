# Support adolescents to develop balanced screen habits and provide additional academic support for adolescents from families with low educational attainment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendations are that teachers, parents and professionals help adolescents "develop more balanced screen habits to create more opportunities for activities that strengthen academic performance, such as homework, reading, as well as physical activity", and that schools provide additional academic support to adolescents from families with low educational attainment. The rationale is the observed link between high weekday screen time at age 13 and lower odds of A–D grades at age 16, and the lower grade-improvement probability among adolescents whose parents had short education.
