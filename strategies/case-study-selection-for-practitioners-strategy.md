# Select case studies aligned with practitioners' daily activities, rich in complexity and stakeholder diversity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A selection strategy for RAI case studies: begin from the practitioner audience's background, knowledge, interests, and goals; choose cases "most adherent to the participants' daily activities or organizational reality"; prefer depth and complexity spanning ethical dimensions; and prioritize scenarios with multiple stakeholders holding divergent perspectives. For breakout activities, structure heterogeneous groups mixing backgrounds, roles, and perspectives on AI and RAI. The article reports this from pilot feedback and its own selection process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the audience's knowledge and objectives before selection
- Sufficient publicly available information when cases must be shareable and cannot center the partner's strategic products
#### Constraints
- When organizational access is limited, relevance is approximated by choosing cases broadly relevant to the partner's application domain and industry rather than its own products

### Target Learners
- diverse industry practitioners with varying skills and professional goals

### Target Learning Goals
- grounding RAI principles in realistic scenarios
- navigating stakeholder tensions in AI decision-making
