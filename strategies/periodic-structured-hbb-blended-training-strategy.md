# Deliver periodic, structured, precise HBB trainings as short one-day sessions at two-month intervals with ongoing quality monitoring through blended learning modalities.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a recurring low-dose training structure: deliver all HBB content in a single day followed by refresher sessions at two-month intervals, monitor CMWs at their birthing centers between sessions with unannounced visits and skill scenarios, and use blended modalities for scale-up. The study concludes that "periodic, structured and precise HBB trainings, with ongoing quality monitoring activities through blended learning modalities would help sustain and scale-up the intervention."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained local midwifery tutors who supervise and monitor between sessions
- A regulatory program body to arrange logistics and collect monitoring checklists
#### Constraints
- Findings come from one district and a small sample, so the study states they cannot be generalized

### Target Learners
- Community midwives in low-resource community settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Newborn resuscitation knowledge and skills (Helping Babies Breathe)
