# Use the game and physical activities course with an interdisciplinary approach for experiential elementary science teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that elementary school teachers who want an experiential, entertaining and active science environment teach science through the GPA course using interdisciplinary lesson plans. Supporting measures they name include guide booklets with lesson plans and game rules, an inventory mapping game concepts to science concepts, and spare materials for setbacks such as a Frisbee stuck in a tree.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guide booklets including lesson plans and information for teachers on how to use game materials, game rules, and games
- An inventory showing the overlap between the concepts in the games and science
- Alternative materials available in case of a possible setback
#### Constraints
- Practitioners should be careful and take necessary measures because students can encounter minor accidents such as crashing into each other or falling down while running in the school garden

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- science achievement and retention of science knowledge through experiential, game-based instruction
