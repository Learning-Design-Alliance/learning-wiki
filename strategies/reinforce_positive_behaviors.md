# Reinforce Positive Behaviors

## Description
Recognizing and positively reinforcing positive actions when they are observed. This involves recognizing positive behavior, asking how it made the student feel, and connecting extrinsic rewards to positive feelings to enhance intrinsic motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recognition activities and items, such as tokens, stickers, and certificates. Teacher awareness of positive behaviors.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent implementation, some students may become overly reliant on extrinsic rewards.
