# Improve student well-being and adjustment by promoting social support seeking and reducing disengaged and avoidant coping

## Description
A practical implication the authors draw from the supported process model: interventions in higher education should foster students' use of support-seeking coping and discourage avoidant and disengaged responses to academic stressors. The article states its findings carry "practical implications for improving student well-being and adjustment through promoting social support and reducing disengaged and avoidant coping strategies."
