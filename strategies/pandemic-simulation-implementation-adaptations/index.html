Adapt simulation delivery during pandemics using e-learning platforms, small groups, in-situ delivery, and PPE-conserving substitutions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review identifies concrete adaptations for implementing simulation under pandemic constraints. For safety and social distancing, one included study "implemented... an e-learning platform to avoid close gatherings of participants" and another divided students into small groups; for PPE shortage, "participants used standard clinical gloves in order to conserve precious PPE supplies"; and for staff shortage, studies such as Begley JL et al. "were carried out in-situ". The review suggests creating a contingency plan for simulation programs with clinician buy-in.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A contingency plan for implementation of simulation programs with considerable buy-in from clinicians
  • cooperative means in the faculty to include simulation as a main education approach

Constraints¶

  • Shortage of PPE creates tension between conserving it as a limited resource and using it in scenarios
  • educators from critical care, anesthesiology or emergency medicine were often unavailable due to patient influx

Target Learners¶

  • redeployed health care providers
  • medical, nursing and midwifery students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • critical care skills during pandemics
  • safe protocol practice such as PPE donning and doffing

Claims¶

  • Simulation training in critical care during COVID-19 enhanced knowledge and confidence more than theoretical and clinical training alone [+W]
  • Simulation training reached Kirkpatrick level 3 (behavioral change) in five studies, but no study assessed patient outcomes [+W]
  • Simulation-based training improved PPE donning/doffing performance and reduced cognitive load from pre-test to post-test [+W]
  • PPE, including FFP2 masks with and without valves, did not impair CPR quality in a randomized controlled trial [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Deliver NTS training through simulation-based courses with structured debriefing focused on Crisis Resource Management principles, supplemented by in-situ refreshers and visual reminders.
