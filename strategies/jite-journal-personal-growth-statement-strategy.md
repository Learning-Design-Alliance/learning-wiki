# Daily activity journals plus an end-of-project statement of personal growth

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In JITE, each student keeps a daily journal of activities and ideas, and "The value of project tasks to the student must be assessed by him/her before and af ter undertaking each task." At project end every student submits a statement of personal growth recording expectations and learning, which may include nontraditional objectives such as follow-up project suggestions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student keeps a daily journal of activities as well as ideas
- Statement of personal growth submitted at project end by every student
#### Constraints
- The statement can incorporate nontraditional objectives (relevance to town, province, nation and the world; cultural and ecological diversity and sustainability; follow-up suggestions) rather than only technical content

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students in JITE projects

### Target Learning Goals
- Introspection and reflection on learning; anchoring scientific and mathematical disciplines with humanities and social sciences
