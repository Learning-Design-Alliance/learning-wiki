# Provide guidelines to prepare students for online open book assessments before they sit them

## Description
The article recommends supporting students' preparation for the online open book format, since unfamiliarity with the method may explain the lower and more dispersed 2020 mid-semester scores. It states: "guidelines for supporting the students in preparing for the online open book assessment should be provided in the future." Many students reportedly did not know how to appropriately prepare for the assessment, which was new to them.
