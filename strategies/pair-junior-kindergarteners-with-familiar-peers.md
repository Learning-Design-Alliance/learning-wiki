# Pair junior kindergarteners with familiar peers to stimulate interaction during on-computer literacy activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that senior kindergarteners engaged and collaborated more than juniors, the authors draw a practical implication for on-computer activities involving junior kindergarteners: "to combine children familiar with each other to stimulate peer interaction about computer literacy activity and subsequent symbolic play in off-computer activity". The strategy addresses juniors' lower engagement, which the authors link to less developed, less socially oriented language use and less familiarity with classmates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pairs of pupils work together on each on-computer activity; familiarity between paired children is needed to stimulate peer interaction.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is drawn from observed differences between senior and junior kindergarteners in one school; the authors state it as a potential implication, not a tested manipulation.

### Target Learners
- junior kindergarten pupils (ages 4-5)

### Target Learning Goals
- peer interaction about computer literacy activity and symbolic play in off-computer activity
