# Use MOOC-based courses to provide free, accessible research-based continuing education to health professionals globally

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that MOOC-based medical education is a promising way to deliver accessible, free, research-based education to health professionals in both developing and developed countries. Online resources "may serve as substantial supplements to current post-graduate education" and help professionals who cannot find time to locate and evaluate the thousands of terabytes of digital resources, keeping them updated on new treatment therapies and guidelines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses designed with an appropriate pedagogical foundation
- Internet connection for enrollment regardless of location
#### Constraints
- Changing a person's knowledge does not necessarily translate into their practice or behavior; the study provides self-reported data only, not objectively measured impact

### Target Learners
- medical doctors
- nurses
- other health care professionals in postgraduate education

### Target Learning Goals
- continuing postgraduate medical education
- staying updated on new treatment therapies and guidelines
