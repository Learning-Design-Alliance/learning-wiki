# Culturally Responsive Curriculum Scorecards

## Description
Tools developed collaboratively by educators, parents, students and researchers as a method of evaluating the degree to which learning goals, assignments, books, materials and other elements of curricula are responsive to diverse cultural backgrounds and inclusive of multiple perspectives. These scorecards can be customized to fit various contexts. Completing the scorecard guides teams through evaluation and scoring, revealing strengths and weaknesses in representation, social justice, and teacher materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the curriculum materials. Evaluation team with diverse identities and roles. Familiarity with culturally responsive education principles. Time and capacity to conduct the evaluation.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming and resource-intensive. Requires a team of individuals with diverse perspectives. Results are subjective and dependent on the evaluation team's knowledge and biases.
