# Use peers and colleagues as a primary source of evidence and competency support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that clinicians favour consulting colleagues over searching research evidence themselves, because answers come quickly, and that peers serve both as support and as a source of evidence. Strategies mentioned include "continuing professional development activities, supervision of students and the use of peers/colleagues by way of a formal or informal mentor or an OT Facebook page". The authors suggest integrating knowledge in practice may only be possible within a social learning context allowing networking, feedback and reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A workplace culture and team that support informal consultation and knowledge sharing
#### Constraints
- The article notes evidence searching may take longer to answer clinicians' questions than asking a colleague

### Target Learners
- Practising occupational therapists and other health professionals maintaining competency in practice

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining and developing competency, evidence-based practice and reflective practice
