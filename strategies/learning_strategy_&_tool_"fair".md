# Learning Strategy & Tool "Fair"

## Description
A 'Learning Strategy & Tool Fair' allows learners to explore a variety of learning strategies and tools in a hands-on, exploratory environment. Inspired by instrument petting zoos, this approach provides opportunities for learners to experiment with different strategies and tools, discovering their own interests and challenges. Product developers can design resources within their products that suggest potential strategies based on learners' needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A collection of various learning strategies and tools. Time allocated for exploration and experimentation. Adaptive product capabilities (for digital implementation).
#### Constraints
- May require significant preparation to gather a diverse collection of strategies and tools. Learners may feel overwhelmed by too many options.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Encourage exploration of different learning strategies and tools, support personalized learning, foster learner autonomy

### Affordances
- Provides a low-pressure environment for experimentation. Allows learners to discover new strategies and tools that align with their individual needs and preferences. Encourages self-reflection on learning processes.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose which strategies and tools to explore based on their interests. Adaptive products can suggest strategies based on learner needs and performance.
