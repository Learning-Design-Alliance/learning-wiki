# Use local ancient monuments as outdoor classrooms, chosen after surveying what pupils and teachers do not know

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends exploiting nearby, lesser-known ancient monuments rather than famous sites: initial research with a core group of four G&T students revealed that famous monuments like Stonehenge were well known while "local and equally impressive monuments were unknown". This led to selecting sites only an hour's drive from the school as outdoor classroom environments. Planning included pre-visits for risk assessment and landowner contact.
