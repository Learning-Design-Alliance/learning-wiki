# Engage adult learners in supervised practice, feedback, and guided reflection so factual knowledge evolves into practice competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on education research the article cites, it argues that curricula structured around acquisition of measurable knowledge disregard insights "showing that adult learners need to be engaged in supervised practice, feedback and guided reflection, for factual knowledge to evolve into practice competence." It further reports that experiential learning such as early patient experiences, by providing relevance and motivation, helps students understand basic science better and develop clinical skills quicker.
