# Leveraging Strengths for Attention

## Description
Identifying students' strengths related to attention and using those strengths to support and further develop their attention skills. This involves understanding both sustained and selective attention and tailoring strategies to individual student needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires knowledge of students' individual strengths and challenges, and the ability to implement personalized strategies.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and resources to identify and leverage individual strengths. Success depends on accurate assessment of student needs.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->

### Affordances
- Personalized learning experience that builds on individual strengths, increases motivation, and fosters a positive learning environment.

### Personalization
- Strategies are tailored to the specific strengths of each student, such as using artistic abilities to create visual aids or organizational skills to manage tasks.
