# Incorporate dedicated grant-writing and professional development guidance into interventions supporting PhD students toward academic careers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on finding that both coaches and peers were called upon for grant and fellowship support, the article recommends that "any intervention seeking to encourage and support PhDs in their pursuit of an academic career should incorporate significant and dedicated grant-writing guidance as well as guidance related more generally to professional development skill sets." The rationale is that grant writing is integral to academic science careers and a major reason students move away from academic careers in the first years of the PhD, yet guidance on it may still be lacking even at the postdoctoral stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches or peers with knowledge of funding mechanisms, including minority-focused fellowships
- A forum in which students can discuss applications with people outside their own institution
#### Constraints
- Guidance may need to extend beyond the PhD, since the article notes information on grant writing may still be lacking at the postdoctoral stage

### Target Learners
- Biomedical PhD students, particularly URMs, pursuing academic careers

### Target Learning Goals
- Grant and fellowship application competence
- Professional development skill building
