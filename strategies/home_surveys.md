# Home Surveys

## Description
Sending weekly, biweekly, or monthly surveys home can provide ongoing data and insight into students and their families. Short surveys with one or two questions at a time have been found to be more receptive and responsive to families. Surveys can inquire about art at home, music, visual art, movies, and television shows, family traditions, favorite recipes, and student supports needed.
