# Use norm violation and metaphorical models to surface taken-for-granted communication assumptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The essay recommends that communication and rhetoric scholars adopt ethnomethodological techniques to expose the assumptions bonding human communication. "By purposefully violating norms in everyday communication settings, we can begin to see how others expect us to behave." Scholars can also describe situations where norms were broken, create their own metaphorical models of communication phenomena, and ask what we take for granted when communicating and whether those assumptions cause poor communication.
