Stakeholders should use both ED's rulemaking comment period and accreditors' own standards-revision input processes to advocate for opportunity-expanding policies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends a two-track engagement strategy. First, stakeholders should prepare public comments for ED's rulemaking comment period, expected to last only about 30 days, using the consensus draft, negotiators' proposals, and analyses on ED's negotiated rulemaking page. Second, once regulations are finalized and accreditors revise their standards, stakeholders should use accreditors' public input processes — which the brief notes are separate from ED's comment period — to advocate for policies that expand educational opportunity and promote student success.

  • Access to the consensus draft, negotiators' proposals, and additional analyses available on ED's negotiated rulemaking page
