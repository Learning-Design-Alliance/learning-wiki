# Develop an XR-based NICU simulation handoff training program using SBAR, prioritizing high-risk newborn care topics with the largest need-experience gaps and safety/protection handoff content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building a systematic XR-based neonatal care simulation handoff training program as an alternative to restricted NICU clinical practicum. It states that "an XR-based neonatal care simulation handoff training program using SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, and Recommendation) should be developed, with a primary focus on fluid imbalance and respiratory problems observed in the NICU." Training content is prioritized by the Borich and IPA results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Grounded in a needs assessment identifying priority topics; the article recommends "additional identification of the needs of trainees and suitable modification of the training content."
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from an online survey in South Korea; the article cautions "the results may be biased" and clinical situations differ across schools.

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year prelicensure undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Neonatal and high-risk newborn nursing care skills
- Neonatal and family handoff communication
