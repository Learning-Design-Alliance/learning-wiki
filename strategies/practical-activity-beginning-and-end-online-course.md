# Provide practical activity both at the beginning and at the end of an online BLS course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future implementations of similar online life-support courses schedule hands-on simulated practice at both the start and the end of the course. The recommendation comes from their experience evaluating practical performance only after the theoretical phase: "In conducting similar studies, practical activity is suggest, both at the beginning and at the end of the online course." A pre-course practical phase would enable measurement of practical learning gain against a baseline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a skills laboratory with manikins, AED, and feedback devices
- Trained tutors or evaluators for the simulation and debriefing
#### Constraints
- This study was performed without a practice pre-course phase, so baseline practical performance was not measured

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic life support skills in simulated cardiopulmonary arrest care
