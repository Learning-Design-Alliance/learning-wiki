# Recommendations for implementing MI-based environmental education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the article recommends that "the teachers should use this strategy in the environmental education in a separate course or in science and technology courses", that seminars and courses train teachers in the theory and practice, that teachers direct the process effectively to avoid student frustration, that teachers use all eight intelligence types where possible, and that environmental protection clubs and integrated environmental units be developed in elementary schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training seminars and courses on the theory and practice of the strategy
- Careful organization of indoor and outdoor activities
#### Constraints
- The article warns that if teachers cannot direct the strategy effectively, students can be frustrated, demoralized, or bored and the strategy can be unsuccessful

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- environmental awareness
- positive environmental attitudes
