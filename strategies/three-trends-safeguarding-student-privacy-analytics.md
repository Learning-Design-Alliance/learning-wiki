# Safeguard student privacy in learning analytics by involving students in tool creation, building contextualized explainable configurable analytics, and empowering student agency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on Ochoa and Wise's practitioner response, the article identifies three general trends in learning analytics that may help safeguard privacy. These are "involving students in tool creation," developing "analytics that are contextualized, explainable and configurable," and "empowering student agency with analytics as part of a 'larger process of learning.'"
