# Prefer structured courseware with modularized videos, integrated assessments, and set schedules, and engage actively with discussion forums

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 5 studies (2 design, 1 review, 1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Rule 2 and Rule 8 recommend choosing structured courseware in which video presentations are modularized and interspersed with assessments and homework — the model of Coursera and edX, which "should always be preferred" all else being equal. Rule 8 adds that learners should take advantage of instructor-monitored discussion forums "instead of just lurking", or recruit like-minded students to learn in a group, countering the isolated and impersonal nature of online learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to structured platforms (e.g., Coursera, edX) or, lacking a built-in community, effort to locate or recruit other like-minded students
#### Constraints
- Structured courses are often offered on a set schedule, reducing the flexibility of on-demand viewing; without a built-in community, group learning takes more work

### Target Learners
- online learners at any level, including those in computational fields such as bioinformatics

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining engagement, momentum, and social support during self-directed online study
