# Use digital resource selection plus moderated group discussion as a recurring activity in physics teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe: weekly, students select digital resources (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, videos) relating to contemporary physics concepts, discuss interpretations in a WhatsApp group, and a rapporteur consolidates the group's conclusions into a report. Students rated weekly resource selection highly (17 of 21 strongly agreed it was useful) and associated rapporteur consolidation with clearer discussions (15 strongly agreed). The strategy links scientific ideas to applications and students' experiences rather than relying exclusively on exposition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to digital platforms and a group messaging space
- A designated rapporteur to consolidate conclusions
- Weekly cycle of selection, discussion and reporting
#### Constraints
- The article's evidence comes from one qualitative experience without a comparison group, limiting claims about causal effects or transferability

### Target Learners
- prospective physics teachers
- university science students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding contemporary physics concepts
- developing argumentation and critical engagement
- preparing to use connectivist strategies in their own teaching
