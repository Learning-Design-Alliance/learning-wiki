# Think-Time and Re-Reading

## Description
Sufficient time is allowed for learners to process information and re-read materials if needed. This provides opportunities for deeper processing, reflection, and clarification. Allowing extra think-time supports learners who need more time to internalize and formulate responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexible scheduling, willingness to provide extra time, and a supportive learning environment.
#### Constraints
- May require adjustments to lesson pacing. Some learners may become disengaged if given too much unstructured time.
