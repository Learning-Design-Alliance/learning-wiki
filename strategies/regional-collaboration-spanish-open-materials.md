# Expand access through Spanish-language open materials, regional collaboration, and train-the-trainer development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A set of access-oriented strategies: developing and translating open-access materials and 'creating Spanish-language learning resources' to enhance inclusivity, inter-institutional collaboration across the region, and a train-the-trainer program for educators. Because courses are online, they can 'reach students from various universities, cities, and even countries across Latin America', mitigating disparities in infrastructure and faculty training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Close collaboration between universities, research centers, and industry partners throughout Latin America
#### Constraints
- The course proposals are initially designed to be offered online

### Target Learners
- Students and educators across Latin American universities, including Spanish speakers excluded by English-only materials

### Target Learning Goals
- Inclusive access to quantum education and local capacity to teach quantum computing
