Five recommendations for higher education systems elevating student and faculty voice in edtech evaluation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The conclusion offers implementable steps for other systems: "Community college and other higher education systems that are interested in elevating student and faculty voice in their edtech evaluation processes can leverage the following recommendations". The five are: inventory existing evaluation processes and workflows; engage the broader community through interviews and focus groups to identify priorities and pain points; center learners, instructors, and administrators by codifying an ideal evaluation system; collaborate with vendors with a history of productive collaboration to test new processes; and provide transparency into the newly designed process.

Context¶

Requirements¶

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students
  • Higher education learners broadly

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable access to well-vetted education technology supporting learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Five-step iterative co-design process for developing edtech evaluation criteria with learners and vendors
  • Conduct ed-tech pilots by articulating the instructional need, setting clear product-review criteria, involving educators in planning, supporting educators throughout, gathering data from all users, deciding from data, negotiating purchasing options, and sharing results
