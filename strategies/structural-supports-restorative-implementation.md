# Structural supports for restorative practices: funding, people, time, discipline policy, and equity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief identifies structural supports that must be attended to for restorative practices to be implemented and sustained: rethinking success beyond fewer referrals, allocating or reallocating funding, building teams of early adopters, rethinking and reallocating time, restructuring exclusionary discipline policies, and centering racial equity. It recommends creating advisory periods devoted to relationship building, using staff meetings for community building, and embracing the slow pace of community conferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders and administrators must rethink and reallocate their use of time in schools
#### Constraints
- The brief notes 'even these results will take several years or more to achieve' for reduced disciplinary incidents

### Target Learners
- School communities, educators, and district leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustainable implementation of restorative practices
- closing disproportionate discipline gaps
