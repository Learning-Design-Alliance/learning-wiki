# Customizable Display of Information

## Description
Providing adjustable settings and accessible formats to accommodate individual needs and preferences. This involves using materials that learners can modify based on their requirements, such as font size, contrast, or text-to-speech options. Customization enhances accessibility and supports diverse learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessible digital materials, software with adjustable settings, and awareness of learner preferences.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful design of materials and ongoing feedback to ensure customization options meet learner needs. Some learners may require guidance in selecting appropriate settings.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Learners with sensory disabilities (e.g., blindness or deafness), learning disabilities (e.g., dyslexia), and those representing diverse or non-dominant cultures and/or languages

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance accessibility, support diverse learning styles, accommodate individual needs and preferences, improve comprehension

### Affordances
- Enhances engagement by allowing learners to tailor content to their specific needs. Supports self-regulation by giving learners control over their learning environment. Promotes inclusivity by accommodating diverse learning styles and abilities.

### Personalization
- Offer options for text size, font, color contrast, audio descriptions, captions, and alternative formats (e.g., braille, large print)
