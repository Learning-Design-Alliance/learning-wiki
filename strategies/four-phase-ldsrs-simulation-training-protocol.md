# Structure simulation training in four sequential phases: video instruction, expert demonstration, supervised practice, and formative feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a standardized simulation-based training protocol structured into four sequential phases: pre-training video instruction detailing procedural steps, expert-led real-time demonstration emphasizing key operative maneuvers, supervised repetitive practice under direct supervision, and formative feedback after each practice attempt focusing on technique refinement and error correction.
