# Districts should consider curricula and professional learning that intentionally highlight mathematical learning progressions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's forward-looking recommendation for districts is to "consider curricula and professional learning that intentionally highlight mathematical learning progressions." It follows from the finding that productive adapters were more likely to consider learning progressions and adapt lessons to below-grade-level content, while nonproductive adapters adapted on-grade-level content during delivery. The recommendation targets districts selecting middle school math curricula and designing teacher professional learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts selecting or adapting middle school math curricula and professional learning
#### Constraints
- Based on a mixed-methods study of teachers' content adaptations; the recommendation is the authors' suggestion, not a tested intervention effect

### Target Learners
- Middle school math teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Aligning teachers' lesson adaptations with mathematical learning progressions
