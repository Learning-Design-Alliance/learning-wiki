# Model comprehension with read-aloud/think-aloud demonstrations and strategy mini-lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teachers model the comprehension process using read-aloud/think-aloud demonstrations and provide mini-lessons on specific comprehension strategies. This makes expert comprehension processes visible to students within the Strategic Reading block. The strategy is embedded alongside cooperative group work and self-selected reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in the demonstration technique; two days of start-up and awareness training for all Strategic Reading teachers
