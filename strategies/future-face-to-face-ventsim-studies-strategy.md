# Evaluate face-to-face simulation-based mechanical ventilation training with VentSim, including optimal trainee group size, in future studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that further studies assess the efficacy of face-to-face simulation-based educational programs on mechanical ventilation using VentSim, also evaluating the best group size of trainees to optimize such learning experiences. This follows from their finding that remote simulation added to standard teaching did not improve knowledge scores, and their interpretation that face-to-face programs of adequate duration may need to be resumed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies should compare face-to-face delivery against the remote delivery tested here and vary trainee group size
#### Constraints
- The authors note these pandemic years made remote solutions inevitable, and their results come from a single center with limited external validity

### Target Learners
- trainees in anesthesia and intensive care
- physicians with little or no specific experience in mechanical ventilation

### Target Learning Goals
- competencies in mechanical ventilation, including waveform interpretation and management of mechanically ventilated patients
