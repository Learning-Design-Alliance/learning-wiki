# Provide post-graduation mentorship, publication support and alumni engagement to further graduates' competence and employment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Respondents recommended that support and mentorship continue post-graduation, with institutions keeping contact with alumni to enable publishing research findings and involvement in university-based research. They also highlighted inequities where "medical doctors with MPHs are employed preferentially compared to other graduates", recommending a registering body for MPH graduates recognised by employers, and that universities advocate for and partner with employers to make graduates' skill sets explicit.
