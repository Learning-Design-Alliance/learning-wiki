Five-component systemwide support infrastructure for capacity building under next generation accountability¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that states and districts build a support infrastructure with five systemwide components for learning to improve: a systemwide culture grounded in "learning to improve"; strong pedagogical data-literacy skills for using EQuIP; resources beyond funding, including time, access to expertise, and collaborative opportunities; a coherent structure of state-level support including a strong Longitudinal Data System (LDS) infrastructure; and adjusted educator labor market policy where needed. The report argues success depends on school professionals unlearning past ways of doing things and on a support infrastructure exceeding that historically in place.
