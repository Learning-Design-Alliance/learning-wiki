# Exercise Contests

## Description
Organize contests to incentivize students to be more physically active, such as a month-long or year-long challenge. Use BINGO cards with exercises for students to check off for prizes, or have students log their physical activity using fitness trackers. Award prizes to students who complete the most activities or log the most hours of exercise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prizes, tracking system (BINGO cards, fitness trackers), organized timeline
#### Constraints
- May not appeal to all students. Risk of overemphasis on competition. Requires careful planning and monitoring.
