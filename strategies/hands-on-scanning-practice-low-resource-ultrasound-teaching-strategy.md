# Pair a short didactic session with small-group hands-on scanning practice on patient volunteers when teaching basic ultrasound in low-resource settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention pairs roughly two hours of didactic education with a hands-on interactive portion in which "Skills were taught in a separate, hands-on interactive portion for two to three participants at a time with demonstration using patient volunteers". The authors conclude this combination is "an effective method to improve knowledge and skills" in a low-resource setting, and cite literature identifying hands-on scanning and observation as the most effective ultrasound educational activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single trainer with senior-level ultrasound experience
- Access to pregnant patient volunteers for demonstration
- A pre/post survey to evaluate learning
#### Constraints
- Evaluated without a control group, so efficacy relative to real-time field practice without formal teaching was not assessed

### Target Learners
- Medical students and house officers in low-resource teaching hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic obstetric ultrasound knowledge and practical scanning skills
