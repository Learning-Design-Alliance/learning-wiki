# Produce and maintain free, on-demand targeted MOOCs at the undergraduate level for AS&E education and recruiting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that the US community produce and maintain a set of targeted MOOCs as a strategy to fill persistent needs for augmented training, refresher courses, and recruiting. These courses "could be free, on-demand and start anytime with embedded quizzes, and should be at the undergraduate level." Production costs approximately $200K to $250K per course at production values of platforms like Coursera. Recommended initial topics are Introduction to Accelerator Physics, Basic Accelerator Technologies, and Applications of Accelerators, with potential later expansion to Project Management for lab managers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community workshop to establish framework for approach, maintenance, and updates
- Identification of the most relevant focus topics to start
#### Constraints
- MOOC production costs approximately $200K to $250K per course at Coursera/U2 production standards

### Target Learners
- BS and MS-level engineers and accelerator operators
- Undergraduate students unfamiliar with AS&E

### Target Learning Goals
- Gentle orientation to accelerator science and engineering fundamentals
- Augmented training and refresher courses for workforce engineers
