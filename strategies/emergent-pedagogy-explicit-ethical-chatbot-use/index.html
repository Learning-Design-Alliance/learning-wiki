Emergent pedagogy with explicit instruction on ethical and responsible chatbot use¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The instructor adopted a thoughtful and emergent pedagogy: the syllabus and 117 resources were prepared ahead of the semester, while in-class activities were adjusted in real time to students' needs. Inside class, "students were prompted to search for information using the chatbot, discuss its responses and share their thoughts with other groups", alongside scenario-based prompts and role play with Cipherbot. Reflection during the course led the instructor to conclude that "students required explicit 'prompting' (pun intended) on how Cipherbot could be used ethically and responsibly", a component maintained consistently through the semester. The instructor also refrained from imposing the chatbot as a requirement to promote independent inquiry.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor familiarity with the technical workings of the GenAI system and willingness to adjust course design during the semester

Constraints¶

  • Despite this pedagogy, most student teachers including F-1 and F-3 participants still relinquished agency to the chatbot

Target Learners¶

  • University student teachers in teacher education programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Critical and ethical GenAI use, classroom management knowledge, self-directed inquiry

Claims¶

  • Factor 1 student teachers were confident self-reliant GenAI users who showed limited ethical awareness and reflective practices [+W]
  • Factor 2 student teachers enacted balanced, intentional and ethical engagement with Cipherbot through self-directed strategies [+W]
  • Factor 3 student teachers were uncritical users whose agency was almost completely subverted to the GenAI system [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Train teachers in prompt engineering, evaluating AI-generated responses, ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making
