# Create a supportive assessment environment that emphasizes the concept and purpose of formative testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that purely formative testing without consequences leaves most students unmotivated to prepare and to use feedback, and that a "supportive assessment environment that emphasizes the understanding of the concept, and purpose of formative testing is key in motivating students and support learning". Introducing formative assessments additionally requires a shift in focus toward the learning process rather than the outcome, and enhancing students' feedback literacy, in an environment where students develop feedback literate skills.
