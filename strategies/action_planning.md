# Action Planning

## Description
Action planning involves developing explicit plans to consciously modify behaviors and improve future performance based on self-reflection. It includes comparing prior knowledge with new knowledge, selecting significant learnings, envisioning how to apply these learnings to future situations, and committing to specific, actionable steps to change behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection, tools for documenting insights (journals, logs), and a commitment to enacting change.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the learner's willingness to follow through with the action plan. May require support to develop realistic and achievable goals.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Action plans can be tailored to individual goals, learning styles, and areas for improvement. Specificity in plans allows for personal agency.
