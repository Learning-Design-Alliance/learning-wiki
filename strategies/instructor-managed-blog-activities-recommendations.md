# Instructor-managed blog activities recommended for maximizing blog learning and teaching potential

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Churchill (2011) developed a set of recommendations for the use of blogs in educational applications based on interviews, surveys, end-of-course evaluation and participant observation with 26 master students. The review reports that "The following blog-related a ctivities were recommended for maximizing the blog learning and teaching pote ntial: reading blogs of peers, receiving comments from classmates, and r eviewing completed tasks of other students along with the related existing f eedback". The study found blogs had the potential to foster and improve students' learning provided that blog usage is appropriately managed by the instructor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Blog usage must be appropriately managed by the instructor for blogs to foster and improve students' learning
#### Constraints
- Deed & Edwards attributed incomplete critical construction of knowledge to the devoid of guidance in the use of blogs for academic purposes, since it was wrongly assumed that students had enough expertise with web 2.0 technologies

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and master students in ICT courses

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective practice
- peer learning
- self-directed learning
