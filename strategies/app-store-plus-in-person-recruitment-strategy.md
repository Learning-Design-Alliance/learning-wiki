# Distribute the training app through app stores plus in-person device-equipped sessions for voluntary recruitment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's multi-faceted data collection strategy combined public distribution with facilitated access: "in-person training sessions were conducted at local hospitals, where participants were provided with mobile devices... preinstalled with the app", plus availability on the App Store and Google Play Store and a dedicated website with direct download links. This enabled both real-time feedback during facilitated sessions and remote voluntary participation across a diverse user base.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distribution on major app stores; mobile devices for in-person sessions; a dedicated website with direct links
#### Constraints
- Participants who did not complete the entire session could not be evaluated, yielding incomplete pre or postsurvey data

### Target Learners
- diverse health care professionals recruited voluntarily

### Target Learning Goals
- broaden participation in health care skills training across professional backgrounds and locations

## Claims

- [240 voluntary participants completed the full app session, predominantly female and mostly aged 21-30](../claims/240-participants-completed-app-session.md) [+W]
- [The participant pool spans many health care professions, led by nurses (31.3%) and including nonclinical personnel](../claims/professionally-diverse-completer-sample.md) [+W]
