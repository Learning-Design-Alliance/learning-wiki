# Schrödinger's cat and EPR paradox plush toy demonstrations for superposition and entanglement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Two physical demos use Schrödinger's cat plush toys, depicting an alive state on one side and a dead state on the other. In the first, the facilitator switches the toy between states unseen and stops on observation to show probabilistic collapse, repeating with random outcomes. In the second, two toys interact then are separated to opposite ends of a solar system, and repeated trials reveal correlated collapses. The authors report the plush toy demo "amplifies engagement as it is amuses students and makes the session more memorable overall".
