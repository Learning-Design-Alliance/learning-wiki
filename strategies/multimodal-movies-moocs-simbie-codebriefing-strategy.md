# Combine medical movies, MOOCs, and simulation with co-debriefing as a scalable multimodal approach to stress and burnout education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating cinemeducation (grounded in vicarious learning, where movies evoke emotions and model effective and ineffective practices), asynchronous MOOCs delivering professionalism and stress education at learners' own pace, and SIMBIE with co-debriefing into one sequenced IPE program. It reports that prior to this study "no study has integrated medical movies, MOOCs, and virtual SIMBIE with co-debriefing as a combined strategy," and positions the framework as scalable for high-pressure clinical environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sequencing of movie and MOOC sessions (phase 1) before SIMBIE and co-debriefing (phase 2)
- Debriefing-trained facilitators
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-center quasi-experimental study; the VR variant showed no significant advantages over the other arms

### Target Learners
- undergraduate health care professional students across disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- stress and burnout mitigation
- collaborative practice competencies
