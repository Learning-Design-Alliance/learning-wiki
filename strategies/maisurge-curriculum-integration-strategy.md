# Integrate the MaiSurge model into gynecology residency curricula for continuous TLH training and skill assessment outside the operating room

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to use a realistic, procedure-specific, electrosurgery-capable uterus model for dry-laboratory training of total laparoscopic hysterectomy, allowing residents' first experiences, skill assessment, and learning-curve tracking without operating-room time or patient risk. All participants "agreed that the new MaiSurge uterus model should be integrated into training curricula to improve the performance of residents on TLHs."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A benchmark for time and performance scores must be set before the model can serve as a test before operating on a patient; the article states this requires further research to evaluate a meaningful cutoff
#### Constraints
- The article notes skill transfer to the operating room was not explicitly assessed in this trial, and the small sample size poses a risk of bias

### Target Learners
- gynecology residents and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- technical skill in total laparoscopic hysterectomy and readiness for operating-room performance

## Claims

- [On a baseline vaginal cuff suturing task, experts perform significantly faster and score significantly better than novices](../claims/maisurge-baseline-suturing-experts-faster.md) [+W]

- [Novices take significantly longer than experts to complete a total laparoscopic hysterectomy on the MaiSurge model (an 85.3% increase in operating time)](../claims/maisurge-experts-novices-operating-time.md) [+W]
