# Adopt the cloze approach for Igbo language reading comprehension instruction, adjust curriculum materials, and retrain teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the cloze approach was superior to conventional instruction, the researcher recommends that the cloze approach be adopted in teaching Igbo language reading comprehension at the secondary school level. The recommendation further states that curriculum materials in Igbo language should be adjusted to suit the cloze instructional approach, and that the Ministries of Education should intensify efforts at retraining Igbo language teachers on the applications of the cloze procedures in the classroom. Implementation in the study relied on instructional guides for teachers and pre-experimental training to establish uniform instructional standards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers require pre-experimental training and instructional guides to adhere strictly to the tenets of the instructional packages
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one quasi-experiment in Abakaliki education zone using intact classes without random assignment

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary school students of Igbo language

### Target Learning Goals
- Igbo language reading comprehension achievement
