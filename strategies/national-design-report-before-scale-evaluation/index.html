Commission a dedicated national design report before evaluating supplemental literacy programs at scale¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article exemplifies a strategy of producing an explicit design report prior to conducting a large-scale program evaluation. The report "lays out the design for the Evaluation of Reading Comprehension Interventions" before results exist, and it was "Prepared for: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences," with the National Center for Education Evaluation also named. This makes the evaluation plan publicly documented and federally specified in advance of the study's findings.
