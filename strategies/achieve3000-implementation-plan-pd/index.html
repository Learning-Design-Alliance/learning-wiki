Develop a customer-specific implementation plan identifying targeted students and outlining professional development¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As part of implementation, "Achieve3000 works with each customer to develop a plan that identifies the targeted students and outlines professional development and implementation services." This makes professional development and implementation support a planned, site-specific component of adopting the program rather than a fixed package.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coordination between the provider and the customer site to identify targeted students and arrange professional development and implementation services.
  • A plan developed with each customer identifying targeted students and outlining professional development and implementation services

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • students in grades 2-12 targeted by the implementation plan
  • targeted students in grades 2-12 using Achieve3000

Target Learning Goals¶

  • literacy growth through consistent program implementation
  • literacy skill development through differentiated content

Related Strategies¶

  • Layered professional learning and support for teachers implementing individualized software
