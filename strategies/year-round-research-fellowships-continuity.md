# Year-round research fellowships to provide research continuity beyond summer-only REUs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Center aims to support year-round research fellowships for 30 undergraduate trainees. The article notes that research experiences for undergraduates are often large factors in student success, but that REUs "are limited to the summer months, which do not provide an opportunity to establish research continuity"; a year-round training program increases skills in the field and positions students for future opportunities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to research infrastructure, even if not at the student's institution
#### Constraints
- access to infrastructure has been a sticking point of physics programs at HBCUs since the 1940s

### Target Learners
- HBCU undergraduate students in physics and QISE-related disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- STEM persistence, scientific understanding, scientist identity, research self-efficacy, graduate school acceptance
