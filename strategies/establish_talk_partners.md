# Establish Talk Partners

## Description
Establishing pre-arranged talk partners helps to streamline pair talk activities. These partners should be carefully selected to ensure equitable exchange of ideas, avoiding situations where one student dominates the conversation. This minimizes set-up time and fosters a comfortable and routine environment for pair discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires initial planning to establish appropriate pairings and ongoing monitoring to ensure pairings remain effective. Adaptations may be needed if students are absent or if pairings are not working well.
#### Constraints
- Fixed pairings may limit students' exposure to diverse perspectives. Requires careful consideration to avoid creating imbalanced or ineffective partnerships.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All grade levels, particularly useful in classrooms where pair talk is a frequent instructional strategy.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Facilitate efficient and equitable pair discussions, promote a comfortable and routine environment for pair talk, ensure that all students have a designated partner for collaborative activities.

### Affordances
- Reduces classroom management issues during pair activities, ensures that all students have a partner, and allows for intentional pairing to support diverse learning needs. Fosters routine and comfort.

### Personalization
- Partners can be strategically assigned based on academic level, confidence, or communication style. Pairings can be changed periodically to allow students to work with different peers.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the dynamics of established pairs during discussions. Assess whether both partners are actively participating and contributing ideas. Gather feedback from students about their experiences with their talk partners.

## Impact
- Well-established talk partners can lead to more productive and equitable pair discussions, enhancing student engagement and learning outcomes.
