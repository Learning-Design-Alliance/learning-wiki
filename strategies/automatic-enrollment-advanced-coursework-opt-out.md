# Adopt automatic (opt-out) enrollment policies placing qualifying students in advanced coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends districts automatically enroll students with qualifying grades or test scores in advanced coursework, with students free to opt out. It argues an "opt-out" policy rather than "opt-in" process "reduces the risk of gatekeeping practices." Evidence cited includes Washington's Tacoma schools doubling advanced enrollment and Texas's 2023 statewide math automatic enrollment law.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students with qualifying grades and/or test scores are identified for automatic placement
- State technical support and pilot grant funding to equip administrators, counselors, and teachers
- Extensive parent communications about the policy
#### Constraints
- Students retain the choice to take the regular course instead
- Notification letters alone still place the self-advocacy burden on families

### Target Learners
- High school students, particularly students of color and students from low-income backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to and success in advanced coursework including AP, IB, and accelerated math pathways
