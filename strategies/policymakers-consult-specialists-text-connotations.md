# Policy makers should consult specialists on the indirect connotations of texts in instructional materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that policy makers, facing the political and cultural implications carried by instructional course-books, "need to get a good command of indirect connotations of the text they put in the instructional materials through consulting the due specialists". This extends the study's findings on intertextuality's complexity to materials selection at the policy level.
