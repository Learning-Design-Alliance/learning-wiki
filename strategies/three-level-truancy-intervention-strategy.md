# Multi-level interventions to reduce psychological truancy: build self-concept, boost motivation, and improve school climate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend three coordinated intervention levers arising from their mediation model. First, educators should help students "increase their positive experiences of academic success" and form more positive self-evaluations, for instance through group counseling programs. Second, they can lower truancy by enhancing academic motivation, for example by changing single and boring teaching styles, using reasonable competition and reward or punishment mechanisms, and encouraging students appropriately. Third, a supportive psychological environment should be established, strengthening teacher-student relationships and creating a good interactive teaching environment.
