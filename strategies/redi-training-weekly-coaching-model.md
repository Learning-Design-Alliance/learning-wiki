# Support REDI implementation with brief inservice training plus weekly coaching from local consultants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a professional development model for REDI: two days of in-person training and 8 hours of online training (or four days of in-person), plus local coaches who "observe classrooms and meet with teachers to foster high-fidelity program implementation." Coaching "typically requires about one hour per week per classroom during the first year."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches can be program supervisors or master teachers with time allotted for mentoring
- Coaching about one hour per week per classroom in the first year
#### Constraints
- Coaching intensity is specified for the first year of implementation

### Target Learners
- preschool classroom teachers implementing REDI

### Target Learning Goals
- high-fidelity implementation of REDI teaching strategies
