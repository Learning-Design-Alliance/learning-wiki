# Use non-compensatory, patient-safety standard setting when competence across index procedures is individually required

## Description
The article's discussion recommends considering a standard-setting approach in which incorrect performance in critical items or procedures leads to failure without recourse to compensation, because trainees at the end of phase 2 are expected to display competent performance across a series of core procedures. It notes the current compensatory scoring may be inappropriate given curricular requirements for competence across several key index procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Competency expectations for each procedure outlined in more detail, assessing to the minimum standard required for all trainees regardless of sub-specialty interest
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as an argument for future iterations, not a tested manipulation in this pilot

### Target Learners
- higher specialist trainees in general surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- procedure-specific operative competence across core and emergency procedures
