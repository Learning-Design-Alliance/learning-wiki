# Use PhET simulations coupled with supplementary instructional materials to support blended MSS engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 13 claims (13 for) · 5 studies (2 qualitative, 2 design, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 13 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article leverages PhET sims' design features—allowing students to change parameter values and observe effects on other parameters—to structure engagement in blended MSS at various levels of sophistication. PhET sims "can be enhanced by coupling them with relevant instructional materials that include additional information (such as data, reading materials etc.)", and data can be provided when the sim does not fully support quantitative exploration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A PhET simulation (or similar interactive sim) associated with the target topic
- Supplementary data or materials when the simulation does not fully support quantitative exploration
#### Constraints
- The degree of quantitative exploration support varies across simulations; the Energy Forms and Changes sim is more qualitative than the Coulomb's law sim

### Target Learners
- undergraduate STEM students
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying variables and quantitative patterns
- developing and justifying mathematical relationships for scientific phenomena
