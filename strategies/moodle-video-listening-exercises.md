# Deliver video-based listening exercises through a structured online platform with limited replay attempts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes using Moodle to organize video clips into categories with study pages of 20 clips each (10 short, 10 long), generating user accounts, and limiting students to 5 watch/listen attempts per clip. Students wrote down sentences they heard, which were scored against originals using a five-level gradual scoring scale (rubric).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Moodle instance or similar LMS with video hosting and access controls
- A gradual scoring rubric for transcription accuracy
#### Constraints
- In this study, participants could only access the application at laboratory courses; the authors suggest Internet and mobile access as an alternative worth researching

### Target Learners
- university EFL preparatory students

### Target Learning Goals
- listening comprehension and accurate transcription of English sentences
