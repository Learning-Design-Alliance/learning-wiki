# Build collaborative LO components for producing, testing and comparing arguments, and form heterogeneous groups of about three

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that LOs intended for pairs include facilities that keep records of collaboration and let students "produce arguments and counter-arguments", "conduct experiments to support arguments", and "compare different arguments and opinions", so both partners' ideas are entered and sequentially tested. It further recommends assigning three students per group rather than two, and forming groups heterogeneous in gender, culture, language background and ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Worksheets scripting each student's role, as planned for the study's extension under Common Cartridge standards
#### Constraints
- Random assignment in this study prevented controlling member heterogeneity; teacher intervention during LO work may impede a student's internal discourse

### Target Learners
- seventh and ninth grade students working in pairs or small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- negotiating and elaborating knowledge through argumentation during LO tasks
