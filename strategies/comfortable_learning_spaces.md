# Comfortable Learning Spaces

## Description
Creating classrooms as comfortable spaces where students feel at home. This includes providing cozy spaces where students can relax and read, such as allowing them to sit or lie down while reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comfortable seating options such as bean bag chairs, cushions, and rugs. A designated space for reading and relaxation. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional space and resources. Some learners may find it difficult to focus in a relaxed environment. Requires clear expectations and guidelines for appropriate behavior.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase comfort, reduce stress, promote relaxation

### Affordances
- Promotes relaxation and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of reading.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred seating arrangement. Provide a variety of comfortable seating options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more comfortable.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in reading activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward reading.

## Impact
- Research suggests that creating comfortable learning environments can reduce stress and improve academic performance. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
