# Ask ethics, bias, and equity questions before adopting an AI system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educators interrogate AI tools before use by asking "Who developed this tool?" and "What data was used to train it?" and how that data and the algorithm are biased. It also directs attention to bias in evaluating learning materials and students, maintaining privacy of data shared with an AI system, and equity in terms of ability to access AI tools.
