# Award extra credit and small rewards to sustain engagement in an optional review game

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because participation is optional and the game runs near the final-exam period, the article describes a layered incentive system: extra credit for the winning team ("an additional point on the final grade for the laboratory classes, which is scored on a scale from 0 to 10"), sweets for all participants, and small handmade gifts for the winners. The instructor also communicates the indirect academic benefits of participating, such as reviewing and consolidating concepts before the final exam in a relaxed environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear communication to students of the indirect academic benefits of participating
- A grading system that permits a small bonus (here, laboratory grades on a 0-10 scale)
#### Constraints
- Participation is entirely optional, so some students may prioritize studying over attending

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students approaching final examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- Motivation to review and consolidate course concepts before the final exam
