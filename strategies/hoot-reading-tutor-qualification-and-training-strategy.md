# Staff online tutoring with experienced, highly qualified classroom teachers and provide training in literacy, trauma-informed teaching, and remote instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Hoot Reading's staffing model requires that all tutors are experienced classroom teachers with Bachelor's, Master's, or a Ph.D. and are highly qualified in early literacy instruction. The program provides professional development that includes literacy instruction, trauma-informed teaching, and teaching reading remotely. This strategy pairs tutor credentialing with targeted professional development for the online delivery context.

## Design Implications
