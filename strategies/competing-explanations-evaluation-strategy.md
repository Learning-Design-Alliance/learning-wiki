## Description
A third task type presents pupils with multiple possible explanations for an observed optical phenomenon, such as a 'broken' straw in a glass of water, which they must critically evaluate, discuss and devise ways to verify. The article states: "This type of task encourages comparing alternative explanations, working with evidence, and the ability to justify their decisions based on logic and observation". Supplementary transfer questions (e.g. 'What happens if we pour oil on the gel balls?') extend this by requiring application of learned knowledge to new situations.
