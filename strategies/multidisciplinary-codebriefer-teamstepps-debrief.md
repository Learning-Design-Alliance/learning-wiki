# Use a multidisciplinary co-debriefer model with the TeamSTEPPS approach for simulation debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Debriefing sessions of 15 minutes immediately after each simulation are delivered by an interprofessional co-debriefer team — an attending neurointensivist, neurocritical care pharmacist, and neurocritical care APP — using the TeamSTEPPS approach. The debrief focuses on "providing a psychologically safe environment, encouraging the use of effective, closed-loop communication," establishes a shared mental model through a learner case summary, and addresses roles, asking for help, and keeping team members informed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multidisciplinary co-debriefer team spanning physician, pharmacy, and APP roles
- Minimum faculty to learner ratio of 1:3
- Debriefing held immediately after the simulation and before post-simulation self-assessments
#### Constraints
- The TeamSTEPPS approach was created by the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality and applied as published

### Target Learners
- multidisciplinary critical care learners

### Target Learning Goals
- teamwork and communication
- role clarity and responsibilities
- reflection on decisions and behaviors
