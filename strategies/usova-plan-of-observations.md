# An eight-step plan of observations guiding students through purpose, procedure, results and analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among Usova's generalized plans is a specific plan for conducting observations, part of the "rules for conducting observations, experiments, and apparatus learning". Its eight steps move from "Time of the observation and information about the observer" and a purpose "formulated by a teacher or chosen by the students", through description of object, equipment, conditions and procedure, to "Results of the observation" and "Analysis of results and conclusion from them".
