# Prioritize non-coursework, skill-based experiences such as summer internships in quantum workforce development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on a survey of quantum-industry companies, the article reports that there is "greater consensus about which skills–rather than degrees–are important", and that "non-coursework experiences and training based on those skills, such as summer internships, should be prioritized". The strategy positions internships and other skill-based training as the most industry-useful educational approach, complementing rather than replacing degrees. The article's own Feynman Quantum Academy is presented as an instance of this approach in practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- identification of the skills the target industry values, e.g. via employer surveys
- host organizations able to offer hands-on project work with mentorship
#### Constraints
- the recommendation is attributed to a survey of quantum-industry companies and is framed for quantum workforce development, not general education

### Target Learners
- students aiming to fill technical or business roles in the quantum industry
- early-career scientists pivoting into quantum from other STEM backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- industry-valued skills in quantum information science and technology
- career readiness for quantum industry roles
