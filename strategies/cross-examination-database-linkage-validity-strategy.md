# Link databases from separate examination boards to validate assessments and analyse differential performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates combining routinely collected data from two separate examining bodies to study candidates who sat both assessments. Linkage was "by means of the GMC number", producing a merged file of 2,284 candidates. This allows concurrent-validity comparison across examinations with different syllabuses and organisations, and provides an external measure for analysing differential performance by ethnicity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates must have taken assessments from both boards and be linkable via a shared identifier such as a GMC number
#### Constraints
- The linked cohort is not a random or typical subset of candidates taking either assessment alone

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical trainees sitting MRCGP or MRCP(UK)

### Target Learning Goals
- validity and fairness of high-stakes postgraduate medical assessment
