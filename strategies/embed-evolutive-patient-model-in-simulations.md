# Embed the evolutive patient model in computer-based simulations and serious games for major incident management training, then assess teaching impact through randomized trials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a next step for the validated model: "Embedding this model in computer-based simulations and serious games designed to teach major incident management should now be considered." They state the model should be included in computer-based simulations and its impact on the teaching of major incident management assessed through randomized trials. Possible formats include computer-based table-tops, serious games, practical exercises without casualties, and full-scale simulations with tablets displaying vital parameters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of the HUMAn model into a computer-based simulation or serious game platform
#### Constraints
- The impact on teaching has not yet been assessed; randomized trials are required
- The model currently simulates adult trauma only and lacks treatment-specific effects

### Target Learners
- paramedics
- emergency medical technicians
- prehospital physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- major incident management decision-making
- triage under resource constraints
- preparing health professionals for the stress of managing major incidents
