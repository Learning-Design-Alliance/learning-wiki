# Weekly Student Reflection

## Description
Students spend 10-15 minutes every week reflecting on their learning experiences by responding to prompts, enhancing dialogue, inspiring openness, and diversity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Allocated time for reflection, structured reflection prompts, and a teacher committed to reviewing and responding to student reflections.
#### Constraints
- May require careful planning and facilitation to ensure students engage meaningfully with the reflection prompts.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Middle school learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Provide students with regular opportunities to reflect on what they have learned, how they have learned, and their experiences in the classroom.

### Affordances
- Helps students recognize mastery, enhance self-awareness and character development, appreciate diversity and relationships, think about their learning process, and engage in dialogue with the teacher.

### Personalization
- The prompts can be tailored to the specific content and activities of the week, allowing for personalized reflection based on individual learning experiences.

- Assess student growth in content knowledge, self-awareness, metacognition, and their ability to strengthen relationships within the class.

## Impact
- Promotes student autonomy and self-efficacy, which contribute to enhanced learning and character development.
