# Short writing assignments expressing abstract quantum ideas via analogies and concrete models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports assigning 'writing assignments to the students' — 'short essays (one to two paragraphs)' that 'encourage the students to express abstract ideas via analogies, or via more concrete models, which break down a complex system into smaller concrete parts'. The authors observe that 'students often invest significant thought into how to organize and express these ideas in their writing', making this a low-cost scaffold for abstraction.
