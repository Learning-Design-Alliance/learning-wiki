# Low-Stakes Writing

## Description
Teachers at University Park Campus School used daily low-stakes writing activities to foster student voice, self-confidence, and critical thinking skills—a school-wide strategy used in every subject.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires short writing prompts or activities. Requires time for students to write.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to generate ideas for writing. Teachers need to provide guidance and support.
