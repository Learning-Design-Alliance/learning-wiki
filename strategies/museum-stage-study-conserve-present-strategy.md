# Run a museum-based stage where students study, conserve and then present historical scientific instruments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific, implementable activity recipe: selected secondary-school students attend a one-week stage at University scientific museums, where researchers show them instruments from the collections, how they are restored, how they work and how they were used, and the students then present the instruments to visitors. The article states students were "showed some Bunsen burners belonging to the collections, how they are restored, how they work and how they were used in the chemical laboratories."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a University scientific museum or historical instrument collection
- University researchers available as tutors
- Selected groups of secondary-school students
#### Constraints
- Described for students aged 16–18 from local secondary schools
- Activity centred on instruments of historical and educational interest

### Target Learners
- secondary-school students aged 16–18

### Target Learning Goals
- interest toward historical scientific instruments and scientific studies
- understanding of physics and chemistry concepts connected to the instruments
