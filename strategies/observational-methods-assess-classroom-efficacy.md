# Use observational methods (e.g., CLASS) alongside self-report to assess classroom efficacy and reduce shared method variance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To reduce bias due to shared method variance, the authors recommend that future studies utilize observational methods to assess efficacy in the classroom, such as the CLASS (Pianta, La Paro, & Hamre, 2006). This addresses the limitation of relying solely on self-report measures of mindfulness and efficacy.
