# Structure out-of-school astronomy like a sports league: easy entry, escalating challenge, and high-level club experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review presents Gelderman's argument, by comparison to a successful school sports league, that out-of-school astronomy programs should provide "open and easy entry level recreational opportunities", then "a series of challenging activities with steadily increasing challenge and reward", then high-level competitive experiences in an astronomy club to drive further participation in extracurricular astronomy. This progression can reuse the formal-education and undergraduate activity types at greater depth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A ladder of activities from recreational entry through escalating challenge to high-level club experiences
#### Constraints
- Aimed at out-of-school time astronomy students rather than classroom instruction

### Target Learners
- youth in clubs, after-school programs, museums and camps

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement in extracurricular astronomy
