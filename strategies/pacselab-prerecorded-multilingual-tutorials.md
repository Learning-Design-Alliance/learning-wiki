# Distribute prerecorded multilingual tutorials to mitigate internet cost and language barriers in online citizen science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address the high cost of internet and unstable power supply that make scheduling Zoom meetings difficult, PACS e-Lab and its volunteers made prerecorded tutorials available for all projects, mostly in English, and are recreating them in Arabic, French, and other local African languages. The article reports this approach "has mitigated the cost of internet subscriptions that participants would have otherwise exhausted while learning about the projects."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Volunteers to record and translate tutorials, and distribution channels to reach participants.
#### Constraints
- Tutorials are mostly in English so far, with Arabic, French, and other local-language versions still in progress.

### Target Learners
- African participants in regions with high internet costs and language barriers, including non-English-speaking teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Training in citizen science project procedures (asteroid search, photometry, astrophotography) without live connectivity
