# Integrate crossword puzzles, social media and online resources into EFL teaching to engage passive learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating edu-gaming and social media into English teaching: crossword puzzles are described as "an electronic tool which is sometimes known as interactive puzzles integrated with edu-gaming", with readymade online programs available to create them. Popular social media technologies such as Facebook, YouTube, Wikipedia, Twitter and LinkedIn can be used for learning, since learners already use them; the instructor's task is to integrate the learning scenario and shift learner focus toward specific language targets. Online resources are said to catch the attention of even a passive learner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must integrate the learning scenario and motivate learners to shift focus from general issues to specific language targets
- Local platforms can host the puzzles for learners to practice under instructor supervision
#### Constraints
- The article notes both teachers and learners require initial training for efficient use of learning resources

### Target Learners
- Saudi EFL preparatory-year students
- young learners already familiar with computers, internet and communication tools

### Target Learning Goals
- English vocabulary learning
- learner engagement and motivation in EFL classrooms
