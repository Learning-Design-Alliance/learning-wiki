# Embed short exercises after each instructional section so students immediately apply the constructs just introduced

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Nearly every section of the manual closes with an exercises subsection (e.g. 1.1.1, 1.2.1, 1.5.2, 3.5.5) in which students perform tasks directly building on the section's content, such as calculating values, writing programs with only local variables, or comparing immediate and delayed assignments. The manual instructs learners to "Do the following calculations in Mathematica, and try to understand their structure". Solutions to exercises are collected at the end of the book.
