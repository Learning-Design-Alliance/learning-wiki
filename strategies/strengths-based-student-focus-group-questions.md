# Run a strengths-based student focus group using open-ended, neutrally framed questions about learning from home

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief describes a concrete inquiry method: convene students across grade spans in a virtual focus group and ask open-ended questions about both the challenges and joys of learning from home. The opening question "is framed neutrally, opening the door for students to share both positive and negative experiences," setting a student-centered tone and creating a jumping-off point for deeper questions about safety, support, and connection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identify a time and space where students feel safe and supported sharing, obtain consent for recording, and be transparent about how responses will be used
#### Constraints
- Designed to elicit what was positive about learning from home rather than emphasizing what did not work

### Target Learners
- K-12 students across elementary, middle, and high school

### Target Learning Goals
- School climate improvement; understanding student experiences of learning environments
