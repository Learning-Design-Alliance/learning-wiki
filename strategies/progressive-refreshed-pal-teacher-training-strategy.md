# Progressive, refreshed PAL teacher training with tailored ongoing support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that PAL training programmes meet the needs of all teachers, not just early adopters, and progress as teachers build experience and capability. Content "should be 'refreshed' and become more challenging over time", paired with tailored ongoing support that reinforces learning. The strategy informs the ACTivate project's free online professional development package for PAL practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be accompanied by resources and support so teachers develop the capability to implement and adapt PAL
#### Constraints
- Adequate resources, strong leadership and governance, an engaged activated community and political war may not currently exist in most schools

### Target Learners
- primary (elementary) school teachers, including those not yet motivated to adopt PAL

### Target Learning Goals
- developing capability, confidence and skills to deliver physically active learning lessons
