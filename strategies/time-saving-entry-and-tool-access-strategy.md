# Open post-qualification courses by demonstrating time-saving AI uses and secure free access to AI tools for teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends leveraging teachers' most common motive for AI use in lesson preparation and follow-up — saving time (n = 63) — as an opening argument, and having post-qualification specifically address how AI can save time in everyday teaching practice. Because lack of free access to AI tools was the most common reason for not using AI for preparation and follow-up (n = 21), responsible departments should make tools available to ease teachers' first steps.
