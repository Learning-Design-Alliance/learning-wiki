# Import the competencies into existing specialist training programmes and use them to direct a new GPwER qualification

## Description
The working group recommends disseminating the competencies by importing them into existing UK training programmes and using them to guide a new extended-role qualification. The article states that "the working group suggests that the competencies presented here could be used to direct a new GPwER", referring to the Royal College of General Practitioners framework for General Practitioners with Extended Role. It also recommends targeting the competencies at medical staff training as specialists, with later modification for undergraduate programmes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Wider discussion and consultation with professional bodies such as the Royal College of Nursing and the British Dietetic Association, endorsement by medical schools, and development of instructional guides for programme directors
#### Constraints
- The article notes the competencies should be used alongside high-quality evidence-based clinical guidelines such as NICE, and with appropriate supervision from senior medical staff

### Target Learners
- Medical staff training as obesity specialists in the UK, including general practitioners pursuing an extended role

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardised, competency-based obesity-related medical education at postgraduate and continuing professional development levels
