# Build supportive classroom and home environments and use constructive feedback to strengthen self-efficacy and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical-implications section recommends that teachers "create an inclusive and cohesive classroom environment" through "peer mentorship programs and collaborative learning activities", and that parents provide a focused study space at home. It further advises encouraging students to "participate in problem-solving activities that relate to real-life situations" and providing "timely and constructive feedback" so students can monitor progress and adjust, which the authors state "can significantly enhance students’ self-efficacy and belief in their own abilities."
