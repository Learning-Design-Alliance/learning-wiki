# Expand TBL across diagnostic imaging modalities including CT, musculoskeletal radiographs, and ultrasound

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend integrating TBL across the diagnostic imaging curriculum beyond chest radiographs. They suggest that 'expanding TBL to computed tomography (CT) interpretation, musculoskeletal radiographs, or ultrasound curriculum alongside hands-on practice could provide structure for repeated practice and allow students to build self-efficacy.' Implementation should be approached stepwise across imaging modalities with iterative feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stepwise implementation across imaging modalities incorporating iterative feedback and evaluation processes
#### Constraints
- Utility of TBL across all diagnostic imaging modalities has not yet been evaluated; other modalities may offer different opportunities

### Target Learners
- physician associate students

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnostic imaging interpretation across modalities
- building self-efficacy through repeated practice
