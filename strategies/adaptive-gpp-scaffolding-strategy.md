# Adapt GPP scaffolding intensity to individual student proficiency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future iterations of GPPs incorporate adaptive hints so learners with different prior proficiency receive appropriately tailored support. This would let high prior knowledge students maintain autonomy and exploration while offering additional scaffolding only as needed, addressing the finding that fixed GPP structure helped novices' rule understanding but could disrupt advanced learners' own reasoning processes.
