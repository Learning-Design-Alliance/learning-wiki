# Reduce conference travel through remote and Nearly Carbon Neutral conference models

## Description
The article recommends adopting remote observing, remote panel reviews, and virtual or Nearly Carbon Neutral conference models, in which participants pre-record talks and contribute to interactive online Q&A. It reports that increasing online conference presence reduces carbon footprint while increasing participation, especially for astronomers with fewer financial resources, and lists added benefits of reduced costs, increased productivity, and greater inclusivity for underrepresented groups.
