# Use CBA reporting tools to monitor and timely detect students with performance problems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends leveraging the reporting functionalities of Computer Based Assessment software such as QMP to monitor student progress and timely detect students with performance problems. The reports offer relevant information such as students' performance for each exercise and formative evaluation, and items that proved to be easiest or most difficult. This strategy is grounded in the authors' conclusion that QMP allowed the professor to monitor students thanks to the different reports it provides.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A CBA software platform such as QMP that generates reports including the Test Analysis Report
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in a specific ESP kinesiology course at UACh and the authors state they cannot generalize the results at this point

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension in English as a foreign language
