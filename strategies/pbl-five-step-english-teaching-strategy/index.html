Structure PBL instruction through the five steps of question, analysis, problem solving, results reporting, and reflection and evaluation¶

Description¶

The article reports that the PBL teaching model "can be divided into five major steps: question-analysis-problem solving-results reporting-reflection and evaluation". Teachers of English courses can implement PBL by organising instruction through these five phases, in which students define problems, analyse materials, present results, and conduct self- and peer assessment.

  • PBL teaching significantly improves the critical thinking skills of analysis and interpretation in English-major sophomores [+W]

  • Traditional classroom teaching showed no significant improvement in students' critical thinking ability over the same period [+W]
