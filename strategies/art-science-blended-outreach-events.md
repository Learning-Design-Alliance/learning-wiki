# Blend art and science in public exhibits and events to reach unconventional audiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The LSC plans exhibits and performances pairing art with gravitational-wave science, including a creative lighting artwork at the 2009 World Science Festival and a multimedia show by composer Andrea Centazzo. The article states such events "help to increase the understanding of gravitational-wave astronomy in an unconventional way," and that pairing the show with public lectures by a LIGO scientist aims to bring together scientifically-oriented and artistically-oriented audiences.
