# Library model: donate tablets to libraries and let students purchase them with earned SmileyCoin

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Tablets are donated to a library rather than directly to students. Pre-registered student accounts are set up in the tutor-web and assigned by the librarian. Students earn SmileyCoin by studying, and can purchase a tablet using 1 M SMLY by scanning a QR-code on the back of the device. The Smiley Charity replenishes tablet stock when students make purchases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Libraries with local WiFi and Internet access
- Agreements with libraries and contact persons
- Modified reward settings in the tutor-web
#### Constraints
- The Smiley Charity has finite funds
- Rewards must be tuned so the reward makes the effort required worthwhile

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in low-income regions

### Target Learning Goals
- secondary school mathematics
- KCSE national exam preparation
