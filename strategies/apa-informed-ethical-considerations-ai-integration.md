# Six APA-informed ethical considerations for integrating AI into systemic practice

## Description
The article adapts six ethical considerations from the American Psychological Association (2025) for integrating AI into systemic practice: informed-consent disclosure of AI use and its risks, bias mitigation for underserved groups, HIPAA-compliant tool selection, use of validated tools, and the stance that AI tools should "only be used to augment, not replace therapy."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinicians must include an informed consent statement disclosing AI use, evaluate tools to mitigate bias, select HIPAA-compliant tools, ensure tools have been validated so information is accurate, and remain the final decision makers on AI recommendations.
#### Constraints
- The article states AAMFT currently lacks ethical guidance for the use of AI in practice, so the considerations are adapted from the American Psychological Association (2025).
