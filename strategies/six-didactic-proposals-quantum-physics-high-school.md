# Six didactic proposals for high-school Quantum Physics teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article distils its experience into six recommendations, stating that "the high school teaching of Quantum Physics should benefit from" a brief view of Philosophy and Literature on QP concepts, a study of probability coordinated with Mathematics and Chemistry, a gradual path of awareness about sci-fi from enthusiasm to correction, familiarisation with few fundamental QP formulas, a scheme and poster explaining the non-linear QP progress, and a popularising narration of the QP vicissitudes and protagonists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection among the proposals must follow "the needs and potential of the learners", which the author calls the true test of the pre-university teachers who try themselves.
#### Constraints
- The author warns it is "utopian to consider feasible the whole range of these professional tips".

### Target Learners
- secondary students (ages 14-19) in Italian scientific high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- learning motivation for quantum physics
- understanding of fundamental quantum physics concepts and formulas

## Claims

- [In a 2007-2008 pilot class taught with strategies targeting the probabilistic interpretation, mathematical formalism, and the Copenhageners' low notoriety, four of twenty leaving-certificate exam papers were centred on Quantum Mechanics.](../claims/four-of-twenty-exam-papers-quantum-mechanics-2007-2008.md) [+W]
- [In 2009-2018 classes, combinations addressing other triplets of the six critical aspects produced overall lower marks than the 2007-2008 and 2008-2009 combinations.](../claims/later-combinations-lower-results-2009-2017.md) [+W]
- [In a 2008-2009 class with a predisposition to philosophical reflection, seven of twenty-four exam essays were on QM-related topics and were judged works of cultural depth and interdisciplinary value.](../claims/seven-of-twenty-four-exam-essays-quantum-2008-2009.md) [+W]
