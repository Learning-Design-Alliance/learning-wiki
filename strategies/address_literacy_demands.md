# Address Literacy Demands

## Description
Recognize that students may not share the ability to automatically read, interpret, and reply to textual information at the same level as educators. Avoid assuming that all students can fluently read, process, and respond to written communications. Remote instruction may force students to read and process text at an unprecedented level, which can overwhelm them. Keep written communication as clear and concise as possible, provide video directions to accompany text, offer face-to-face meetings, limit email communication, decrease quantity and increase quality, and proofread all written information to remove teacher jargon and ensure student-friendly language.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- Awareness of student literacy levels, commitment to clear and accessible communication, access to multimedia tools.
