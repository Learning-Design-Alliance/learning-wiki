# Explore learners' perspectives qualitatively before integrating m-learning e-resources into an academic curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a formative, exploratory approach in which "Exploring learners' perspectives could inform the development of such e-resources, before such e-resources are integrated into an academic curriculum". It used a small purposive pilot with open-ended responses and thematic analysis to gauge acceptability before considering a larger randomized controlled trial, and suggests educators use the qualitative themes to inform teaching methodologies catering to a range of learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formative pilot with relatively small sample size to explore acceptability before an expensive randomized controlled trial is considered
#### Constraints
- The article states barriers to m-learning were insufficiently explored and could be examined with focus groups or semi-structured interviews in future research

### Target Learners
- postgraduate clinical psychology trainees
- clinical psychology educators in Singapore

### Target Learning Goals
- designing m-learning that fits learners' needs, expectations and learning preferences
