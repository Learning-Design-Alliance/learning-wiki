# Divide pre-recorded lecture videos into concise recordings of at most 15 minutes to increase viewing and retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy is to chunk pre-class lecture content into short recordings, since, per the article, "Students view shorter videos more often and are more likely to watch their entirety, resulting in more excellent material retention than longer videos." Videos must be downloadable from the learning management system with user controls to pause, play, or rewind. The article applies a 15-minute maximum per recording in its nutrition courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos downloadable from the virtual learning environment with user control functions to pause, play, or rewind
- Adequate pre-class time for students to complete the learning activities

-

- online graduate health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of foundational knowledge before class
