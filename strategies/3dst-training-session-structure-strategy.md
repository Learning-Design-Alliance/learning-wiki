# Structured simulation session combining an instructional video demonstration with hands-on endoscopic stapes surgery on a patient-specific 3D-printed simulator, scored with modified OSATS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training recipe pairs a short didactic introduction with self-paced video review and hands-on practice: participants attended "a 20‐min lesson on SS, including an instructional video on endoscopic SS on the 3DST performed by an experienced surgeon", and were allowed to watch the video as many times as needed before performing the procedure with a standard instrument set. All simulations were video-recorded and later scored anonymously by two raters using modified OSATS scales, with disagreements resolved by discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructional video of the full procedure on the simulator performed by an experienced surgeon
- A standard endoscopic stapes surgery instrument set
- Recording of all simulations for blinded dual-rater OSATS scoring
#### Constraints
- The item 'measure length incus to footplate' was ignored because that step is not performed in the standard technique at the authors' institution
- Assessment of ossicular chain motility was omitted by most participants and scored as not applicable

### Target Learners
- ENT residents
- ENT specialists with limited stapes surgery experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Endoscopic stapes surgery technique
- Adherence to the stepwise stapedotomy approach
