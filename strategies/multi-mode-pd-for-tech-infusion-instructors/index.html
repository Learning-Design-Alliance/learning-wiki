Multi-mode, ongoing professional development for instructors implementing technology-infused courses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

When methods-course instructors with limited technology capabilities must deliver technology-infused instruction, technology infusion specialists provide a wide range of professional development: "face‐to‐face workshops and planning sessions, just‐in‐time training through e‐mail exchanges and phone communications, in‐class modeling, co‐teaching, and on-going dialogue" via a social media group. The amount and type of PD is based on task complexity and participants' prior backgrounds, and all instructors use a standard syllabus with technology-infused objectives and assignments.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Technology infusion specialists responsible for training instructors and providing ongoing support
  • Standard syllabus with technology-infused objectives and assignments

Constraints¶

  • In the program, PD was available but instructors were not required to participate nor received stipends, which the authors identify as a cultural problem

Target Learners¶

  • university methods-course instructors and faculty

Target Learning Goals¶

  • delivering technology-integration instruction with fidelity to technology goals
