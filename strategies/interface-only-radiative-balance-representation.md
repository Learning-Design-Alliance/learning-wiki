# Represent the radiative balance only at the Earth-atmosphere–Space interface

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A representation choice for the final step of the sequence: draw only the radiative balance at the interface between the Terre-atmosphère system and Espace, deliberately not representing the radiation exchanged between the surface and the atmosphere. The article argues that a balance including the surface and atmosphere « ne permet pas d'aller au bout de raisonnement suite à l'ajout de CO2 », because the infrared radiation emitted by the atmosphere toward the Earth is also affected by the added CO2 in a counter-intuitive way.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Concentrate the diagram on the interface between the Terre-atmosphère system and Espace, omitting surface–atmosphere radiation flows.
#### Constraints
- The atmosphere-to-Earth radiation is also affected by CO2 addition in a way the article judges contre-intuitive and difficult to explain for a non-specialist audience; this level of detail is not necessary to reach the targeted conclusion.

### Target Learners
- Lycée students, non-specialists in physics

### Target Learning Goals
- Raisonner sur la conséquence d'un ajout de CO2 sur l'évolution de la température du système Terre-atmosphère
