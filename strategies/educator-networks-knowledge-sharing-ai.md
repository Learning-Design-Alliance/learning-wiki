# Facilitate educator networks and knowledge-sharing hubs for educational AI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review reports, citing Salas-Pilco (2020), a strategy of organising educator networks to overcome obstacles such as time constraints, workload, and lack of resources for teacher training in AI education. It states: 'She also suggests that teachers and other stakeholders interested in educational AI must understand the benefits and challenges of implementing AI and robotics in education to develop and share learning designs in these networks.' The review argues this cooperative approach mitigates common challenges and fosters supportive, transparent discourse about AI tools.

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- contextualising AI use in education
- developing and sharing AI learning designs
