# Use reviewing medical records, discussions with physicians, VR-based programs, and bedside practice to prepare healthcare professionals for pre-treatment services

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "reviewing medical records, holding discussions with physicians, participating in VR-based programs to strengthen treatment-decision knowledge, and engaging in bedside practice are feasible strategies to prepare healthcare professionals in the general ward setting." The strategy aims to enable staff to offer comprehensive pre-treatment services using web-based VR or written educational aids, thereby improving quality of care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to VR or written educational aids
- Opportunities for bedside practice
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a small-sample pilot study with potential timing-of-intervention limitations

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in general ward settings
- Primary nurses caring for oral cancer patients

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehensive pre-treatment patient education
- Treatment-decision knowledge
