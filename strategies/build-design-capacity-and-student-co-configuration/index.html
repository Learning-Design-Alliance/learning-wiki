Build university design capacity and help students become self-managing participants in enacting designs for learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's key practical recommendation is "to make universities more hospitable environments for design—to build design capacity among all staff (lecturers, professional staff and managers), and also to help students become more capable, self-managing participants in the processes that complete and enact designs for learning". This includes helping students sharpen the definition of learning tasks, enrich their networks and configure their learning environment.
