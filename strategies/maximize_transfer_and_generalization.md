# Maximize Transfer and Generalization

## Description
Applying learning to new contexts helps learners develop the ability to use what they have learned in real-world situations. This includes providing opportunities for learners to practice applying their knowledge in new and different settings. It also involves encouraging learners to reflect on their learning and identify connections between different concepts and contexts. The goal is to help learners become lifelong learners who can adapt to new challenges and opportunities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to design learning activities that provide opportunities for application and transfer of knowledge. Instructors need to be able to guide learners in reflecting on their learning and identifying connections between different concepts and contexts.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and resources. Some learners may struggle to apply what they have learned in new contexts. It's essential to provide additional support and scaffolding as needed.
