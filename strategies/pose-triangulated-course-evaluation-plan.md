# Evaluate interdisciplinary course success with triangulated quantitative and qualitative measures, including pre/post tests, rubric-tracked journals, and an in-class ethics conference

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors plan four measurement strands: knowledge gains via "pre- and posttest results, as well as a qualitative assessment of content acquisition through artifact and interview data"; critical thinking via journal entries, papers, and presentations scored with rubrics tracking skill development over time; interest change via pre and post course surveys and interviews; and ethics and astronomy understanding via an in-class conference where each student presents a paper and comments on another's, evaluated with a rubric used in Ethics Bowls. Findings will be triangulated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubrics for tracking skill development over time
- An in-class student conference format
- Triangulation across measures
#### Constraints
- This is a planned evaluation design; no results are reported in the article

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in the PoSE course

### Target Learning Goals
- Content mastery
- Critical thinking development
- Sustained interest in the subject matter
- Ethical reasoning
