Have learners bring their own thinking before using generative AI to enable productive struggle¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Under the Foster Metacognition practice, the framework recommends encouraging "learners to bring their own thinking and ideas to the table before using generative AI, allowing them to engage more deeply in their own thinking and learning." It also advises creating a safe space for risk-taking and mistakes with productive feedback and multiple attempts, and keeping content appropriately challenging with new strategies offered.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tools must provide a safe space for taking risks and making mistakes with productive feedback and opportunities to try multiple times

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Learners using generative AI-enabled learning tools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Metacognition, productive struggle, and self-regulated learning

Affordances¶

  • Learner Centered Emerging Tech Agency Metacognition Accessibility

Related Strategies¶

  • Discourage mental offloading to AI by balancing guidance depth and frequency with application opportunities
  • Use technology after learner effort to protect productive struggle
  • Build in Thinking Time
  • Use an access-timing gate as the only safe AI presence during protected struggle
