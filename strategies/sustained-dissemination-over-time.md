# Sustain research-dissemination efforts over time rather than one-time workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues dissemination approaches need sustained engagement to succeed: "successful dissemination approaches typically need to engage par-ticipants over time to be successful; one-time workshops or a collection of minimally related workshops have not been found to be effective". PERbites operationalizes this by posting between 1-2 article summaries each month over four years, emailing each post to subscribers and advertising on social media to maintain a long-standing community presence.
