# Ask students what launch angle gives the farthest projectile distance in the simulation to surface healthy skepticism about simulation output

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends asking introductory physics students what angle gives the farthest distance traveled for the projectile in the simulation. Many students answer 45 degrees from the equation-sheet expectation, but "students that carefully investigate will find that angles that are slightly off from 45 degrees allow the projectile to travel slightly farther (due to numerical inaccuracies)." The question identifies which students approach the simulation with healthy skepticism and can launch a discussion of numerical methods, paralleling the shift in physics labs toward revealing when equations fail.
