Involve families in the SDLMI through homework assistance, activity sheet conversations, and CICO cards¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes three concrete ways families were involved in the SDLMI: "the families helped and checked their child's homework assigned by the special education teachers," families "had conversations with their children about what they had learned in Korean language classes using the activity sheets," and families "marked on the CICO card if their children showed the target behavior at home and signed it." These strategies enabled frequent communication between families and special education teachers and helped students maintain appropriate behavior across home and school settings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Families receive training with descriptions of SDLMI procedures and their roles
  • Teachers and families agree upon 3-4 target behaviors for the CICO cards
  • Concrete materials and specific guidance are provided to families

Constraints¶

  • Family variables such as parenting styles and home environments could not be controlled entirely

  • middle school students with disabilities who exhibit problem behavior
