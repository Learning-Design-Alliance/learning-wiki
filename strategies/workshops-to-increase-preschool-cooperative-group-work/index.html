Organize workshops and in-service training for small groups of pre-school teachers to increase use of cooperative group work¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Because teachers' negative attitudes changed positively after experiencing the implementation, the article recommends "that workshops be organized for small groups of pre-school teachers to increase the implementation of cooperative group work activities". It further suggests in-service teacher training programs, seminars and conferences about the method, and studies on the design of materials appropriate for teaching patterns through cooperative group work.
