# Schedule repeated hands-on simulation training for obstetric procedures on a yearly basis, with refresher sessions after initial training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion aligns with professional societies and guidelines "recommending hands-on simulation training of obstetric complications on a yearly basis", citing a 2012 study in which residents maintained a training effect for a year and repeating the simulation after one year brought additional improvement. Training programs should plan refresher hands-on sessions after the initial course rather than relying on a single training event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation facilities and instructor time for repeat sessions
#### Constraints
- The article states that if and how a repeated VAD hands-on training proves beneficial one year after the initial training has to be evaluated in future studies; its own follow-up was only 4–8 weeks

### Target Learners
- obstetricians
- residents
- midwives

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of vacuum-assisted delivery skills
- retention of obstetric complication management skills
