# Games + Structured Activity

## Description
Students engage with educational games designed to introduce or reinforce specific concepts. These games are combined with a structured activity to ensure active learning and application of the concepts. Games can make learning fun and engaging, while the structured activity helps to consolidate understanding and promote application.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires selection or creation of educational games, design of structured activities, and access to technology (e.g., computers, tablets).
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of content or learners. Requires careful alignment of games and learning objectives.
