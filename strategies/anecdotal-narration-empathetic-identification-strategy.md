# Use anecdotal narration and personality-revealing images of quantum physicists to foster empathetic identification

## Description
To remedy the low charisma of the Copenhageners, the article proposes anecdotal storytelling and imagery. The author writes: "We can remedy the lack of histrionics of the Copenhageners, included Niels Bohr (Clegg, 2013), in various ways: either with a partially anecdotal narration of the 30 years that 'shook Physics'" and with images stimulating imagination. The poster's poses let each student recognize the expression closest to himself, creating empathetic identification that arouses curiosity about the physicists' lives and work.
