# Use a validated debriefing framework such as the 'Diamond' framework and extend debriefing to the operating room to support NTS skill retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that the NoTSUS curriculum's debriefing be conducted with a validated framework, naming the 'Diamond' framework as "specifically designed to help assessors focus on NTS", particularly when less experienced faculty are employed for teaching. They further recommend OR-based debriefing as "a useful transferable skill from this training to potentially improve retention of skills", noting it must be brief to fit the busy OR schedule and requires purpose-created resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources created or modified for OR-based debriefing, differing from simulation debriefing
- Debriefing must be brief to fit in with the busy OR schedule
#### Constraints
- The debriefing aspect of the evaluated course was poorly documented and there was no feedback on the process

### Target Learners
- Urology trainees and specialists
- Faculty teaching non-technical skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Non-technical skills retention and transfer to the operating room
