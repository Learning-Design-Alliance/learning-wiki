# Differentiate GenAI writing instruction using scale profiles: full scale at course start, short form mid-course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend using the full-length scale at course start to profile each learner's regulatory strengths and weaknesses, then the 12-item form for mid-course formative assessment because it "retains the psychometric properties of the full-length instrument while placing less demand on classroom time." Higher-scoring learners can be assigned multi-draft argumentative tasks with documented GenAI consultations, including annotated records of which GenAI suggestions were "accepted, rejected, or modified and why."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual-level identification of regulatory differences is "a prerequisite step before any instructional differentiation can take place"
#### Constraints
- The differentiated task recommendations rest on the authors' interpretation and cited prior findings, not on a manipulation tested in this study

### Target Learners
- EFL university writers using GenAI tools

### Target Learning Goals
- Productive rather than dependent GenAI use; development of metacognitive monitoring and critical evaluation of AI feedback
