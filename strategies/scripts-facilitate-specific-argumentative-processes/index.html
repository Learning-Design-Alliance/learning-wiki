Use computer-supported collaboration scripts to facilitate specific processes of argumentative knowledge construction¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article situates computer-supported collaboration scripts as a form of process-oriented instructional support in CSCL, noting that research "focuses on the facilitation of specific processes of argumentative knowledge construction, e.g., with computer-supported collaboration scripts". The recommended practice is to target scripts at specific discourse processes and to evaluate their influence with the proposed multi-dimensional analysis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Script effects on specific argumentative processes should be measured, using discourse sampling, segmentation, and the four process dimensions.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • collaborative learners in CSCL environments such as discussion boards

Target Learning Goals¶

  • argumentative knowledge construction

Claims¶

  • Measuring the influence of collaboration scripts on specific argumentative processes is needed to refine process-oriented instructional support [+W]
  • CSCL is often based on learners' written argumentative discourse about problem perspectives with the goal of acquiring knowledge [+W]
  • Macro scripts foster knowledge-productive interactions: argumentation, explanations and mutual regulation [+W]
