# Add ongoing professional development and increased support for EL students when implementing IXL Math

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on teacher feedback from the implementation survey, the page identifies two improvement areas for IXL Math implementations: "adding ongoing professional development and increased support for EL students." IXL's Professional Learning Group already offers virtual and on-site sessions, targeted coaching, and administrator guidance customized to each school or district's implementation goals, so the strategy is to extend this support continuously rather than only at launch, with particular attention to English learners.
