Generate multiple concise LLM perspectives for learners to select from, and explore relevance-driven diversity aligned with learner interests¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that rather than presenting a generic explanation repeatedly, LLMs could generate multiple concise perspectives so students can select the angle they find most helpful, supporting differentiated instruction at scale. It further proposes "relevance-driven diversity, where the variation among explanations aligns with learners' individual interests or prior knowledge domains," noting this alignment supports equity by helping students who feel alienated by traditional examples. The authors ground this in evidence that explanations are among the most common ways students use generative AI tools and that students enjoy controlling the topics of exercises.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • LLM-based tutoring systems or instructors able to generate multiple concise explanations per code snippet

Constraints¶

  • The authors caution that reducing extraneous load alone may be insufficient if students do not engage deeply with the explanations, and that optimal numbers or types of explanations may vary with student expertise levels

Target Learners¶

  • first-year computing students
  • novice programmers using automated tutoring systems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • code comprehension
  • deepening learning through varied instructional content
