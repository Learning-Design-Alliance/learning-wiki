Recommended uses of blogs for language learning: teacher's blog, other skills, and e-portfolio¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In its recommendations the article proposes three implementable uses of blogs for language learning: teachers can use one type of blog, such as a teacher's blog, to increase knowledge; blogs can be used to investigate listening and speaking skills, extending beyond the reading results shown here; and blogs can serve as a student's e-portfolio where students "attach their videos, home assignments, and their writings". These extend the study's demonstrated reading use to new skills and artifacts.
