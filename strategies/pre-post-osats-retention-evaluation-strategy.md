# Evaluate simulation training with pre- and post-simulation objective tests and delayed retention assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For future investigations, the article recommends moving beyond single-moment self-evaluation: "a pre- and postsimulation evaluation is suggested, either with a theoretical test or with an OSATS and evaluation sometime later, to evaluate the retention of knowledge." This addresses the limitation that only the apprentices themselves evaluated the knowledge and skills acquired, and that skill improvement was not measured objectively as in other studies using tests or objective structured assessment of technical skills.
