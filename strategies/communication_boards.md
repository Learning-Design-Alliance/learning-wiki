# Communication Boards

## Description
Communication boards are visual aids that display symbols, pictures, words, or phrases to help individuals with limited or no verbal communication express their thoughts, needs, and ideas. These boards can be physical or electronic and are designed to provide a means of alternative communication. They are often used in educational, therapeutic, and everyday settings to support communication for individuals with speech and language impairments, autism, or other conditions that affect verbal expression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communication board (physical or electronic), symbols/pictures/words/phrases, training for users and communication partners, and ongoing support for implementation and customization.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the user's ability to understand and use symbols/pictures/words/phrases, may require training for communication partners, and can be limited by the user's physical or cognitive abilities.

### Target Learners

- Individuals with speech and language impairments, autism, or other conditions affecting verbal expression, including children and adults.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enable non-verbal or limited verbal individuals to express thoughts, needs, and ideas; support communication in educational, therapeutic, and everyday settings; facilitate language development and comprehension.

### Affordances
- Provides a visual and tangible means of communication, reduces frustration for individuals who cannot express themselves verbally, supports language development and comprehension, and facilitates social interaction.

### Personalization
- Communication boards can be customized with personally relevant symbols, pictures, words, or phrases. They can be adapted to different skill levels and communication needs, offering a flexible communication solution.
