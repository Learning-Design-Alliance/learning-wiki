Integrate scenario based and reflective learning applications into teacher training programmes and other courses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on participant views and the study's findings, the authors recommend extending both approaches beyond the Teaching Practice course. Participants "arrived at a consensus that these approaches should be integrated into the teacher training programmes", and the Recommendations section states "it is recommended that researchers can have the applications of scenar io based learning and reflective learning in other pedagogic courses apart from the teaching practice course". Participants further suggested applying the approaches at other educational levels and disciplines, and conveying reflective applications to teachers through in-service training.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Well thought and well organized plan at the beginning of the applications
  • Directing the learners regarding the applications

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations rest on one 12-week study with 62 prospective teachers in a single course

Target Learners¶

  • prospective teachers
  • teachers at different levels of education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • professional teaching competence
  • theory-practice integration

Claims¶

  • Both scenario based and reflective learning applications were followed by a positive increase in prospective teachers' professional self-competence perceptions [+W]
  • Prospective teachers' views on both applications clustered into four themes, with a minority of the reflective group reporting initial difficulties [+W]
  • No significant between-group difference in prospective teachers' professional self-competence perceptions after scenario based or reflective learning applications [~W]
  • Scenario based learning produced higher academic achievement than reflective learning in a 12-week Teaching Practice course [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Two-column reflective writings with guiding questions in the reflective learning group
  • Use scenario-based learning in teacher-education professional knowledge courses, with curricular activities fostering positive attitudes
