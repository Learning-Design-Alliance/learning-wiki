# SEL Discussions through Literary Characters

## Description
Students read and discuss social and emotional learning (SEL) topics through the lens of characters in literature. This involves selecting texts with strong characters who solve problems and address significant issues, analyzing character relationships, outlining plots, and exploring character change as a result of conflict and resolution. Discussions are structured to teach talking and listening skills using verbal prompts, clarifying questions, and confirmations. This approach connects students' emotions and backgrounds to the material, fostering community building, empathy, and problem-solving skills. The goal is to provide students with strategies applicable to their own lives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to appropriate literature, structured discussion prompts, and a supportive classroom environment. Teachers need to facilitate discussions and provide guidance on analyzing character relationships and themes.
#### Constraints
- May require careful selection of texts to avoid triggering or alienating students. Discussions need to be managed to ensure respectful communication and avoid dominating voices. Some students may find it challenging to connect personal experiences to fictional characters.

### Affordances
- Enhances emotional intelligence, provides a safe space for discussing sensitive topics, builds community, promotes active listening, and strengthens comprehension skills through character analysis.

### Personalization
- Texts can be selected based on student interests and reading levels. Discussion prompts can be modified to suit different developmental stages and cultural contexts. Students can choose characters or themes that resonate with their own experiences.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student participation in discussions, analyze written reflections on character relationships and themes, and assess the application of SEL skills in real-life scenarios. Track changes in student attitudes and behaviors related to empathy, communication, and problem-solving.

## Impact
- Research shows that integrating SEL into literacy instruction improves academic performance, enhances emotional well-being, and reduces behavioral problems. Students develop greater empathy, improved communication skills, and enhanced problem-solving abilities.
