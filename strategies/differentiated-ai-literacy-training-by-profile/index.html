Differentiated AI literacy training matched to acceptance profiles, with interventions beyond operational skills for Resistant Skeptics¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes that teacher education institutions use the empirically identified user types as the basis for AI literacy training design. As stated in the conclusions, "the study proposes a differentiated AI literacy training framework, emphasizing that profiles such as Resistant Skeptics require targeted interventions that extend beyond operational skills training." This follows from the finding that Resistant Skeptics already perceive ChatGPT as easy to use, so skills training alone would not address their negative evaluative beliefs and perceived social norms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Identification of learner profiles via person-centered analysis before designing training, consistent with adult learning theory's emphasis on learner heterogeneity

Constraints¶

  • Current AI literacy programs are often designed as uniform interventions that rarely take disciplinary background or individual differences in motivation and needs into account, which the article identifies as limiting the breadth and depth of AI literacy development

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service teachers in teacher education programs, in both STEM and non-STEM disciplines

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI literacy development and acceptance of generative AI tools for learning and future teaching

Affordances¶
