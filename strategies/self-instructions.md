# Self-instructions

## Description
Teachers model articulating how they solve a problem and prompt students to explain their own thinking. This allows students to become aware of how they think while also providing teachers insight on how students are understanding the concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for teachers to model problem-solving and prompt students to explain their thinking. Requires careful listening and observation to understand student thought processes.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for students who struggle to articulate their thoughts or have difficulty with metacognition. Requires a supportive classroom environment where students feel comfortable sharing their thinking.

### Affordances
- Increases metacognitive awareness by prompting students to articulate their thought processes. Provides teachers with insights into student understanding and potential misconceptions. Fosters self-reflection and awareness of problem-solving strategies.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by providing structured prompts or scaffolding to guide students in explaining their thinking. Teachers can tailor their modeling to address specific learning needs or challenges.
