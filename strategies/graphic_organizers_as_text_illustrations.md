# Graphic Organizers as Text Illustrations

## Description
Students add a graphic organizer to a finished expository or argumentative writing piece to illustrate a key concept or process. The organizer is a diagram to aid in the reader's comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates and writing pieces.
#### Constraints
- May not be applicable to all writing topics or genres. Requires additional time and effort to create a meaningful diagram.
