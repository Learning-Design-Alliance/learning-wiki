# Audit and adjust the sensory environment of the research group, offering concrete accommodations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide instructs leaders to check background noise, lighting, temperature, smells, textures, and visual distractions, which can cause sensory overwhelm. It lists concrete accommodations: "allowing use of earplugs or adjustable task lighting, adjusting seating arrangements and social expectations, scheduling quiet breaks", explicit turn-taking or written contribution modes in meetings, and remote work options.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Encourage trainees to experiment with different work environments and identify the conditions under which they function best
#### Constraints
- Each person's sensory profile is unique; the toll of sensory overwhelm may appear only hours after the offending exposure

### Target Learners
- autistic trainees in research groups

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained focus and wellbeing in the research environment
