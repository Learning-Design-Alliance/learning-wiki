Develop open, reusable, generalizable CBL platforms for specific learning contexts instead of building closed systems from scratch¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review's platform-development recommendation: over 25 platforms were built specifically for medical education with significant overlap in goals and features, so "it would be worthwhile for researchers to try to develop open and generalizable systems addressing specific learning contexts that can be reused by researchers from other medical schools." It prefers pluggable modules for mainstream learning management systems and reusable learning objects over closed systems.
