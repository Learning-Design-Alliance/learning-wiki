Adapt textbook exercises into open-ended, realistic word problems with higher-level reasoning questions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article outlines a process for creating lesson problems from existing materials. "Typically information was added to the textbook tasks to make it realistic. Finally, additional questions that required higher-level reasoning skills, such as analysis and evaluation, were included." The instructor examined state standards, drew on textbook tasks, and consulted students about contexts they perceived as realistic. Problems were expected to be solvable in more than one way.
