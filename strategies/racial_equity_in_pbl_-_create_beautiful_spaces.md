# Racial Equity in PBL - Create Beautiful Spaces

## Description
For so many of our students, their communities and homes are not always beautiful places... Imagine the power then for these same students to be able to walk into a building made beautiful by displaying student work from projects... It’s transformative and brings back life and energy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space for display; resources for creating a beautiful environment; commitment to valuing student work.
#### Constraints
- Requires resources and effort to maintain; facilitator must be intentional about creating a welcoming space for all students.
