# Use MVW-CPR team training as pre-training before, or repetitive training after, conventional CPR courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend multiplayer virtual world CPR team training with avatars as a way of preparing students for conventional CPR courses, since virtual pre-training effects on guideline adherence and compression pacing were still visible months later at full-scale simulator assessment. They state the added training "may serve as cognitive support for future training and real-world tasks" and suggest it might also be used for rehearsal after other modes of CPR training, though that was not the focus of this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the MVW-CPR serious game with avatars and standard computers with broadband internet
#### Constraints
- The aged population, in which cardiac arrests most commonly occur, might benefit but no conclusions can be drawn from this study
- Full-scale simulator training used for assessment is costly and in very limited supply

### Target Learners
- Pre-clinical medical students
- Other digital-native learner groups

### Target Learning Goals
- CPR guideline adherence, compression pacing, and team resuscitation performance
