# Structured training programs with simulation, biotissue training, and video study to shorten the RDP learning curve

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 13 claims (13 for) · 5 studies (2 review, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 13 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors advocate structured training programs such as the University of Pittsburgh program and the Dutch LEARNBOT program for safe implementation of RDP. These programs "include an extensive preparatory program with virtual simulation, biotissue training, and video library studies" and have been shown to decrease the learning curve for subsequent generations of surgeons. The Miami and Brescia guidelines recommend a minimal center volume of at least 20 minimally invasive distal pancreatectomies per year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Endorsement by professional bodies (e.g., European–African Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary Association)
- Virtual simulation curriculum
- Biotissue training models
- Video library studies
#### Constraints
- Recommendation based on cited programs; this study did not directly evaluate training program effectiveness

### Target Learners
- surgeons in training
- surgical teams adopting robotic distal pancreatectomy

### Target Learning Goals
- safe implementation of robotic distal pancreatectomy
- reduction of learning curve duration
