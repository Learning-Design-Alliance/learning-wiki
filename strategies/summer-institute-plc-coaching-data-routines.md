# Train educators through a Summer Institute plus PLC and coaching cycles on data use and instructional routines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's implementable recipe is to "Conduct a 2-day Summer Institute to train educators and school leaders on data use and instructional routines," then sustain the training with 9 professional learning community and 4 coaching sessions. These use "facilitated discussions, educator observations, and guided materials" so educators reflect on and continuously improve their data use and routines.
