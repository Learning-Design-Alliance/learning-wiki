# Quiet Classroom/Pockets of Quiet

## Description
Creating a quiet classroom by reducing background noise to support increased Attention towards learning. Designing small areas as quiet spaces can also be beneficial for students who need to be away from excess noise, leading to greater Self-regulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated space and efforts to reduce background noise. May involve tools to monitor and control noise levels.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in noisy environments or crowded classrooms. Requires clear guidelines for when and how to use quiet spaces.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those sensitive to noise or with attention difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase student attention and self-regulation by reducing noise and providing quiet spaces

### Affordances
- Enhances focus and reduces stress by minimizing auditory distractions. Provides a safe space for learners who need to self-regulate.

### Personalization
- Can be personalized by allowing students to choose when and how to use quiet spaces. Different types of quiet spaces can be created to meet different needs (e.g., individual quiet zones, small group quiet areas).

- Research indicates that reducing noise and providing quiet spaces can improve attention and reduce stress, leading to better learning outcomes.
