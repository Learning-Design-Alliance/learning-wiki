# Ten preparation and delivery tips for virtual surgical skills sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Table 2 lists the authors' "10 top tips" for course preparation and delivery, including providing equipment and resources well in advance, multi-modal learning resources, camera set-up instructions, scheduling across timezones, whole-group demonstrations before breakout practice, limiting instructor-to-student ratio, using 'pin video' for individualised feedback, and using a dark suture on light fruit for contrast.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Good internet connectivity and speed; surgical equipment and pre-recorded videos sent to students and instructors in advance; a separate communication channel amongst instructors.
#### Constraints
- The authors state these steps "can also be utilised to teach generic surgical skills and are not exclusive to ophthalmic surgical skills training".

### Target Learners
- Educators delivering virtual surgical or procedural skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Successful online delivery of practical skills teaching to diverse student groups
