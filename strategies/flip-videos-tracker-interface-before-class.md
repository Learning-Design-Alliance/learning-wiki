# Flip videos introducing the Tracker interface before class so lesson time goes to richer discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors created a series of YouTube instructional videos demonstrating simpler tasks such as installing Tracker, generating s vs t, v vs t and a vs t graphs, and analysing graphs by finding gradient and area. Students watch these at their own free time before lessons; the authors report that because students were introduced to Tracker's interface in the flip video earlier, "the lessons stand a higher chance of allowing richer discussions in class".
