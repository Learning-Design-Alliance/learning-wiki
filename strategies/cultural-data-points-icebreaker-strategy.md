# Use a cultural icebreaker with quantitative self-descriptions at the start of a virtual global collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In Phase 1 of the VGC activity, "Students participated in a cultural icebreaker activity using 'Cultural Data Points,' where they shared short quantitative descriptions of their daily lives." This was complemented by short videos introducing cultural contexts, encouraging students to reflect on how culture influences communication and teamwork. The strategy opens the collaboration by making cultural backgrounds visible through a data-flavored format suited to computing courses.
