Remedial strategies using remedial SEMs to correct misconceptions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Remedial knowledge consists of remedial SEMs that mirror the structure of domain knowledge SEMs but contain remedial information rather than shots. Remedial sub-goals include ME (using remedial information with initially tutored information), SIMILAR (presenting similar objects or events for comparison) and BLANKOUT (presenting tutored information with parts blanked out for the student to fill in).
