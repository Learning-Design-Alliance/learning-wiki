# Add oral health education and simulated-clinic role play to regular school health education before a dental visit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, since children in Shenzhen and other Chinese cities receive regular school health education, practitioners can "simply add[ ] a special oral health education and short session of role playing in a simulated dental clinic in the class" to reduce children's dental anxiety. School-based delivery allows the intervention to be performed at any time before the dental visit and on a large scale, in contrast to clinic-based interventions initiated just before or during treatment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a class of children shortly before a planned dental prophylaxis visit, and dental personnel to deliver the presentation and role play
#### Constraints
- Different EL activities might need to be delivered in schools to cover other dental procedures than PFS

### Target Learners
- Primary school children awaiting routine preventive dental care

### Target Learning Goals
- Reduced dental anxiety before and during dental procedures
