# Embed AI tools in peer, campus, and counseling ecologies instead of isolated individual apps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
One of the framework's five pathways: "AI tools should be embedded in physical education, sport clubs, counseling services, peer-led activities, and referral pathways instead of remaining isolated individual apps". The rationale is that students' regulation demands are distributed across classrooms, peer networks, sport clubs, and counseling systems, so support must integrate the social ecology rather than target an isolated user–system dyad.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers, coaches, counselors, and sport psychologists retain responsibility for interpreting AI outputs and supporting referral decisions
#### Constraints
- No forced disclosure of emotional data; students participate in co-design voluntarily

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- coordinated campus support for emotion regulation
- peer belonging and social connection

### Affordances
- Human Technology Environment Framework Emotion Regulation
