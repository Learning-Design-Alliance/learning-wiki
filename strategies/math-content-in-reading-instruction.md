# Use mathematics content when teaching reading to build on math as a relative strength

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because mathematics was a relative strength for fourth graders across communication status, income, and gender, the author suggests that teachers and SLPs could build on this strength when working on reading and science. For example, using mathematics content when working on reading may benefit students for whom mathematics is a strength. The author notes "Studies are needed to empirically assess the effectiveness of this approach."
