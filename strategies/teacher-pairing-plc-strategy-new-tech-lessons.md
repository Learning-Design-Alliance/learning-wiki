# Pair-of-teachers co-teaching with professional learning community preparation for new technology lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Six teachers were introduced to Tracker about three months before implementation, and teacher TKK led training sessions as part of a professional learning community that met fortnightly to design the worksheet and a Google site for "maintaining consistent and high fidelity teaching practices". During lessons, teachers taught in pairs so the partner teacher could co-teach and support the lesson with Tracker, addressing discomfort with the new software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fortnightly regular meeting schedule for professional development
- Lead teacher training sessions roughly three months before implementation
#### Constraints
- Informal discussions with teachers indicated some students and teachers were not comfortable using computers and the new Tracker software and would require further support

### Target Learners
- secondary three physics students in mainstream school classes

### Target Learning Goals
- high-fidelity enactment of technology-based kinematics lessons
