# Create culture kits

## Description
Creating culture kits involves students creating their own collections of items that represent their culture to bring their culture alive as a way of sharing and discussing what can be learned from different multicultural traditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for students to create and share their kits, access to relevant materials, and teacher facilitation.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure respect and inclusivity. May require additional resources to support diverse cultural expressions.
