# Run brief small-group mini-vocabulary lessons with a fixed routine: say the word, student-friendly definition, example, synonym, sentence writing, and graphic organizer

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For students struggling with vocabulary during additional small-group reading instruction, the guide demonstrates Mr. Parker's mini-vocabulary lesson: brief groups of about 3-5 students lasting 10-15 minutes with short-duration activities. The routine has six moves — "Says the word" with syllables, a "Student-friendly definition", an "Example", a "Synonym", modeling three sentences before students write their own in a vocabulary journal, and a "Graphic Organizer" connecting taught terms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of about 3-5 students struggling with mastering new vocabulary words; sessions of 10-15 minutes
