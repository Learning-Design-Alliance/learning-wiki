# Use analogies to everyday phenomena when teaching advanced physics concepts to high school students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When introducing topics beyond the usual curriculum, the author recommends relating them to familiar earthly phenomena. The article states "giving analogies to everyday phenomena really helps": hydrodynamic instabilities such as Rayleigh-Taylor and Kelvin-Helmholtz, initially confusing, become intelligible when linked to stalactites, dripping water, cream over coffee and jet trails, so students see cosmic physics is not so different from earthly physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
