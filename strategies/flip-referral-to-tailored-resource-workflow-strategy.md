# Referral-to-resource workflow: weekly on-call navigator shifts, standardized 15-minute intake, and a tailored resource document with 2–3 resources delivered within 1–2 days

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
This is FLiP's implementable service workflow. Electronic referrals from affiliated community health centers (or family self-referrals) are captured in REDCap. Each week, student navigators sign up for an on-call shift to call or text newly referred families and complete "a standardized intake form (∼15 minutes)" capturing resource needs, location, barriers, and federal nutrition program enrollment. Navigators then curate a tailored document, received by families "within 1–2 days of the initial intake call," containing "2–3 specific, tailored resources aligned to each area of need," drawn from the community resource guide and food resources guide. Navigators also screen for and refer eligible families to the FLiPRx produce prescription program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dynamic web-based community resource guide and a food resources guide with federal program eligibility criteria
- HIPAA-compliant electronic data capture and texting platforms
- Indirect supervision with a staff associate for logistics and a faculty supervisor for acute concerns
#### Constraints
- The workflow operates under indirect supervision rather than direct faculty supervision

### Target Learners
- medical student patient navigators

### Target Learning Goals
- resource navigation practice
- SDoH screening
- trauma-informed family communication
