# Manage anaesthesia-related maternal deaths as Patient Safety Incidents through a nine-step notify, investigate, classify, analyse, act and learn cycle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A specific implementable procedure, presented as a model SAQ answer, for handling maternal deaths as Patient Safety Incidents in a district hospital. The sequence runs from making the situation safe and preserving records, through family disclosure and police notification, SAC 1 classification, reporting within 24 hours, completing the incident reporting form during the 24-hour review, to structured analysis and follow-through: 'Step 7: Analysis: structured method (Root Cause Analysis [RCA], fishbone, London Protocol) integrating 24-h maternal death review; include human factors and environment', followed by SMART actions with owners and deadlines, feedback to teams and family, SOP updates, re-audit or PDSA cycles, and sharing at governance and district fora. The article frames this as supporting clinical governance through the notification, investigation, actioning and learning cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A patient safety incident reporting form and provincial or district notification route
- A 24-hour maternal death review meeting in which investigation and analysis are embedded
- Autopsy and medico-legal processes for maternal death, since notification of the police forensic section is required
#### Constraints
- Written for the South African district hospital and provincial reporting context, where maternal deaths are classified as SAC 1 PSIs under the national patient safety incident reporting and learning guideline

### Target Learners
- Family physicians and registrars chairing mortality reviews in district hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Patient safety incident management
- Clinical governance and root cause analysis after maternal death
