# Enriched Learning Environment

## Description
Creating a brain-friendly and enriched classroom environment involves optimizing the learning space by considering layout, furniture choices, color selections, lighting, nature, environmental print, and organization of materials. It aims to reduce distractions, promote a sense of calm, and encourage learner agency. Specific strategies include using muted color schemes, incorporating natural light and plants, minimizing clutter, and organizing materials in accessible ways. When creating this environment, learners are given agency in their learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thoughtful consideration of layout, furniture, color, lighting, and materials. Willingness to declutter and organize the space. Collaboration with learners to solicit their input and ideas.
#### Constraints
- May require initial investment in furniture and materials. Requires ongoing effort to maintain organization and minimize clutter. Not all strategies may be feasible in every classroom setting.

### Affordances
- Supports attentional focus by minimizing distractions. Promotes a sense of calm and well-being, reducing stress. Encourages learner agency and self-regulation by providing choices and control over the learning environment. Addresses diversity by adapting to student specific needs.

### Personalization
- Learners can contribute to the design and organization of the space. Furniture and seating options can be varied to accommodate different preferences. Environmental print and visual aids can be tailored to specific learning needs.
