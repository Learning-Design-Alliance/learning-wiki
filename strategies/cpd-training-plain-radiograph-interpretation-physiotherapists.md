# Include basic training in plain radiograph interpretation in physiotherapists' continuing professional development programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that routine plain film radiograph viewing techniques be prioritised in physiotherapists' continuing professional education, concluding that the findings "underscore the urgent need to include basic training in the interpretation of plain radiographs into the continuing professional development programme of physiotherapists in Ghana". This responds to their finding that mean agreement fell far below the radiologist gold standard despite PFR viewing being a routine procedure, and that agreement was unrelated to CPD attendance, suggesting current CPD does not cover interpretation techniques.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CPD programmes that allocate priority to plain film radiograph viewing techniques
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single cross-sectional survey using only three radiographs, which the authors state limits external validity

### Target Learners
- Practising clinical physiotherapists in Ghana and similar settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate interpretation of plain film radiographs of lumbar spondylosis, including identifying contra-indications to physiotherapy techniques
