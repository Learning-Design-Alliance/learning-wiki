# Evaluate digital decision-support in novice providers and optimize interface design to minimize cognitive overhead

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 mixed, 4 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Future research should focus on identifying the contexts in which digital decision-support tools provide the greatest benefit," including evaluating impact in novice providers, interdisciplinary teams with variable experience levels, and real-world clinical settings. Additionally, "optimization of user interface design and integration into existing workflows may help minimize cognitive overhead and enhance usability during time-critical situations."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequately powered study designs to detect clinically relevant effects
#### Constraints
- Current evidence is limited to short-term effects of real-time app use without assessment of long-term retention or patient-centered endpoints

### Target Learners
- Novice providers
- Interdisciplinary teams with variable experience levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective use of digital decision-support during resuscitation
