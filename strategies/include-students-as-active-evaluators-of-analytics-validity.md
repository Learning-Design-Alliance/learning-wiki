# Include students as active evaluators in the design and validation of learning analytics tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends including students in the design and validation of learning analytics rubrics as active evaluators of trace data validity. This participatory approach can enhance the validity and usability of learning analytics rubrics while also promoting students' metacognitive awareness and self-regulatory skills. The article notes this is aligned with a similar approach in open learner models, where data is presented to students so they can actively evaluate its validity while reflecting on their learning approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be given access to their system-generated scores alongside opportunities to self-assess
#### Constraints
- There is a risk that if tools are too 'recipe-like', students may use the rubrics to excel on the rubric itself rather than improving their SRL skills

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive awareness and self-regulatory skills through reflection on learning analytics data
