# Convert traditional viva examinations to structured viva/oral exams to improve fairness, validity, and reliability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's authors recommend replacing traditional oral examinations with structured viva, in which questions are standardized and marking is structured, to avoid subjectivity and low validity and reliability. They state: "The researchers recommend converting all traditional viva to structured viva/oral exams to be fair and to avoid subjectivity, low validity, and reliability." The review frames this as preserving the traditional viva's value for testing higher cognitive levels while adding measured validity, reliability, and acceptability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structuring the viva with standardized questions and a structured rating procedure, as evaluated in the reviewed studies
#### Constraints
- Few studies systematically calculated validity and reliability statistical parameters, so pooled effect sizes for those outcomes could not be computed

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and postgraduate health professions students (medicine, dentistry)

### Target Learning Goals
- Assessment of knowledge, clinical reasoning, and self-confidence

- [Structured viva shows higher internal consistency reliability (Cronbach alpha 0.75–0.80) than traditional viva (alpha 0.50)](../claims/structured-viva-cronbach-alpha-higher-than-traditional.md) [+W]
- [Structured viva shows significant inter-rater reliability between examiner pairs (r = 0.78 to 0.91) and high inter-rater agreement (86.7%)](../claims/structured-viva-inter-rater-reliability-significant.md) [+W]
- [Structured viva shows positive significant correlations with standard assessment tools (MCQs, OSCE, structured theory exam) while traditional viva does not correlate significantly with the structured theory exam](../claims/structured-viva-validity-correlations-standard-tools.md) [+W]
- [Structured oral examinations show higher reliability than traditional formats, with median Cronbach's alpha of 0.75 across studies](../claims/soe-outperform-traditional-reliability.md) [+W]
- [Examiner training and standardization are the most recommended solutions, and structured/mock formats show improved inter-rater reliability (kappa > 0.70)](../claims/training-standardization-solutions.md) [+W]
- [Student and examiner satisfaction is high for structured and virtual oral formats (72–96% agreement), with preference for SOEs over traditional viva](../claims/high-satisfaction-structured-oral.md) [+W]
