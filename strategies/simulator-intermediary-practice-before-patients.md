# Use simulator practice with feedback as an intermediary platform before supervised clinical TAD insertion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports inserting a simulation stage between didactic training and direct supervised clinical practice on patients, since residency programs otherwise lack "an intermediary platform for practice before a procedure". Residents complete pre- and post-test confidence questionnaires, place TADs on the simulator at two inter-radicular sites with audio-recorded think-aloud verbalization, receive real-time sensor feedback on root contact plus postoperative radiographs for self-assessed feedback, and repeat the placements after 15 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Timely and meaningful feedback, since the article states feedback is essential for deliberate practice to be effective; calibration of participants to the think-aloud protocol with practice opportunities
#### Constraints
- A single round of practice with feedback may not be sufficient for achieving expert-level skill acquisition, and the feedback captures only the outcome of placement technique for a specific task

### Target Learners
- postgraduate orthodontic, periodontic and oral-maxillofacial surgery residents who have completed didactic TAD training

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural considerations, decision-making and confidence in TAD insertion
