# Use calibrated Rasch item banks to enable equated repeat testing and potential computer adaptive testing of therapist competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building a bank of items calibrated on a common Rasch logit scale, from which subsets of items can form equivalent tests. "Use of the Rasch model in test development allows the eventual development of a bank of items calibrated on a common scale, any subset of which could potentially form a test to estimate an individual's ability." Three equivalent versions linked by anchor items already permit repeat testing of trainees in outcome and follow-up studies, and the authors note a computer adaptive testing approach might be possible.

### Target Learning Goals
