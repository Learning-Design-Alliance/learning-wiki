# Repeating Key Points in Different Ways

## Description
Repeating the key points for students several times and in several different ways helps reinforce understanding and supports learners who need more time to process information. This can involve rephrasing, providing examples, or using visual aids.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Conscious effort to repeat key points in different ways during instruction.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time during instruction. Needs to be done thoughtfully to avoid being repetitive or boring.

### Personalization
- Vary the repetition and modalities based on individual learner needs and preferences.
