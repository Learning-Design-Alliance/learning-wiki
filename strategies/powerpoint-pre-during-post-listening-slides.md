# Deliver L2 listening lessons with PowerPoint slides containing pictures and photographs across pre-, during-, and post-listening stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's treatment used PowerPoint slides at all three stages of a listening lesson: "The PET slides in the pre-, during-, and post-listening stages contain pictures and photographs". Slides were mostly scanned from the English textbook and matched the listening activity the textbook proposed. Each listening activity came from the textbook, took 15 to 25 minutes of class time, and was treated as aural practice rather than graded work; the experimental group received this treatment across eight one-hour sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Listening activities drawn from the course textbook
- Slides with pictures and photographs matched to each listening activity
- Sessions of about one hour across the project's eight sessions
#### Constraints
