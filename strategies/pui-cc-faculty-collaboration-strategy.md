# Build collaboration models that let PUI and community college faculty lead front-line HEP research with their students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends engaging teaching faculty at undergraduate institutions in front-line particle physics research, arguing this "will strengthen our academic workforce, and in turn inspire their students". It lists concrete actions: assessing faculty needs under high teaching loads, surveying successful PUI/CC–R1/laboratory collaborations, studying new collaboration models in which faculty are "eﬀective leader rather than regarded as secondary", and inventorying past and present PUI/CC activities in HEP.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Conduct an analysis to assess the needs of faculty at undergraduate institutions in order for them to be successful in developing vibrant research programs, whilst maintaining a significant teaching load
- Conduct a survey of R1 institutions and research laboratory physicists who might share an interest in collaborating with PUI and CC faculty
#### Constraints
- The article notes PUI/CC faculty face higher teaching loads, lack of informed guidance, and lack of collaborative opportunities that diminish their competitiveness for research funding

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students at PUIs and community colleges
- students from underrepresented groups

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in front-line particle physics research
- acquisition of skills preparing students for STEM careers
