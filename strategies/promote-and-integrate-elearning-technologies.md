# Actively promote and curricularly integrate new e-learning technologies with support from teaching and administrative staff

## Description
The article identifies active promotion by teaching and administrative staff as a key success factor: "Awareness by and support from the teaching community is only one of several factors that will make a switch toward the use of e-learning strategies successful". It further recommends curricular integration and administrative participation in providing devices, internet, and electronic resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- active participation from university and school administration
- curricular integration of the technology
#### Constraints
- administrative staff often exhibit hesitations due to economic, practical and administrative constraints

### Target Learners
- medical and dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective use of e-learning technologies such as virtual microscopy
