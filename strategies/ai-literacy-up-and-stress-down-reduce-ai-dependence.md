# Enhance students' AI literacy while alleviating excessive academic stress to reduce over-dependence on generative AI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's discussion recommends that educational practice address both protective and risk factors for AI dependence: "efforts should bemade toenhance students'AIliteracy,whilealsopayingattentiontoalleviatingexcessiveacademic stress on students, in order to reduce the excessive dependence of college studentsongenerativeAI." This strategy pairs AI-literacy instruction with stress reduction, grounded in the study's finding that AI literacy negatively and academic stress positively predicted dependence. It also emphasizes promoting responsible use of AI when introducing it into teaching.
