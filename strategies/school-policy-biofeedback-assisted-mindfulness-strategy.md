# Incorporate biofeedback-assisted mindfulness into school educational policies and continue psychophysiological monitoring of at-risk students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their results, the authors recommend that "schools incorporate in their educational policies the practice of mindfulness assisted with biofeedback, targeting teachers, students, administrative, and managerial staff", with the goal of improving capacity to face psychosocial and academic challenges. They also recommend continuing the psychophysiological assessment protocol used in the research to monitor students' psychological adjustment throughout their academic life, detecting moments vulnerable to academic stress, and conducting specific interventions for groups vulnerable to anxiety or academic lag.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions willing to adopt mindfulness-assisted-biofeedback practice and to run the psychophysiological stress-profile assessment protocol for monitoring.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single-group quasi-experiment without a control group and on a non-representative sample of high-performing scholarship students, as the authors state.

### Target Learners
- University students (with suggested extension to teachers, administrative, and managerial staff)

### Target Learning Goals
- Stress resilience, coping with psychosocial and academic challenges
