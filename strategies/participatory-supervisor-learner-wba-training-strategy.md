# Participatory supervisor and learner training on WBA purpose and feedback, within a trainee-focused relationship-centred teaching style

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing WBAs through participatory training of both supervisors and learners: "Developing participatory supervisor and learner training covering their roles, responsibilities, the purpose of WBA and feedback, and how to give and receive effective feedback will also help harness the full learning potential of formative assessments." This pairs with a shift "from a more trainer-centred critical approach to a more trainee focused empowering relationship-centred teaching style", addressing the power dynamics and trust problems the survey documented.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must cover roles, responsibilities, the purpose of WBA and feedback, and how to give and receive effective feedback
#### Constraints
- Resource limitations, time constraints and overburdened clinical responsibilities of supervisors constrain implementation; efficient verbal bidirectional feedback and digital WBA tools are suggested to work within these constraints

### Target Learners
- medical specialist trainees
- supervising consultant trainers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective formative feedback on clinical competence
- trust and respectful trainee-trainer relationships
- competency-based training progression
