# Interdisciplinary Unit Built on a Student's Household Knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teacher uses knowledge of a student's household experience to create "a series of interdisciplinary lessons centered around the theme" drawn from that experience. In the digest's example, a student's experience selling candy from Mexico became a week-long candy production unit spanning math, science, health, consumer education, cross-cultural practices, marketing, and food production, with a parent teaching the class to make Mexican candy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher awareness of a student's household experience, gained through household research.
- Weave knowledge exchanged between teachers and families into academic content and lessons.
- Invite a parent with relevant knowledge into the class to explain a process and help students.

### Target Learners
- Language minority students in the Tucson project's classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Math concepts such as comparing average numbers of ingredients
- Science, health, consumer education, and marketing concepts connected to a household theme
