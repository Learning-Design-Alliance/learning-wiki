# Just-in-time evaluation: let students examine the process and parameters during the experiment and compare outcomes to theoretical predictions immediately

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable activity design in which digital augmentations (graphs, numerical values, false-color images) are continuously updated during the running experiment so students can evaluate physical processes as they happen. The article states that "the just-in-time evaluation of the data yields the possibility for the students to directly examine the process itself and the parameter involved, and immediately compare the outcome to theoretical predictions", which the authors believe enhances the links between theory and experiment.
