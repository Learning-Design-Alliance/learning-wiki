# Adopt self-debriefing as a cost-effective alternative when budgets are constrained and effectiveness parity is established

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that when the decision-maker's willingness-to-pay is low, self-debriefing can replace instructor debriefing without sacrificing learning. It states: "If the decision-maker’s WTP is ≤Can$200, then self-debriefing could be considered as an economically attractive option." Cost savings may allow reallocation to other curriculum areas or additional simulation sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior evidence that the self-debriefing format is as effective as instructor debriefing for the target skills
#### Constraints
- When effectiveness is priced higher than cost savings and WTP >Can$300, instructor debriefing is the preferred alternative

### Target Learners
- anesthesiology residents
- postgraduate medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- crisis resource management skills
