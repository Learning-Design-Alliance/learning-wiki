# Provide explicit evaluation criteria and a forced-discrimination scoring rule in TBL peer evaluation to support reliability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy supplies students with concrete evaluation criteria (preparation, participation, contribution, respect, sincerity) and requires them to rate at least one peer above a set high score and one below a set low score. The authors credit these two design features with the instrument's acceptable reliability, noting forced discrimination counteracts the tendency of medical students to give peers similar scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be given the evaluation criteria to guide the peer evaluation process
#### Constraints
- The study assessed a single instrument in a single institution, so results may not directly apply to other peer evaluation methods in other circumstances

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing peers' contributions and accountability in team-based learning
