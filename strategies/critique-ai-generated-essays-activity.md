## Description
The article proposes an imaginative use of generative AI in teaching: the teacher employs Transformer AI to generate a set of alternative essays on a topic, then asks students to critique these and write their own better versions. Alternatively, students generate AI responses to a complex question, evaluate them against marking criteria, and write an integrative essay drawing on the AI answers.
