Provide prior training with new instructional technologies before technology-supported class activities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the experiment's finding that test-group students lost about 4 minutes learning the AR application before starting the task, the authors recommend that students receive proper instructions and become more familiar with new technologies through prior training. They state that "If students receive proper instructions and become more familiar with new technologies through prior training, they are more likely to perform better in comparison with those attending regular classroom sessions", which they expect will also motivate participation, communication, and active roles in learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors must provide proper instructions and prior training opportunities with the technology before the graded or assessed activity

Constraints¶

  • Without prior training, part of the activity time is consumed by learning the technology itself, as observed in the test session

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate engineering students in large-scale classroom settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective use of technology-enhanced learning activities
  • improved performance in hands-on design and assembly tasks

Claims¶

  • In a building design and assembly experiment, students using an AR instruction tool performed better on primary measures (building volume, number of elements) while print-manual students performed better on secondary measures (cost, embodied carbon) [~W]
  • Under NASA TLX, AR-group students felt more frustrated but believed they performed relatively better, with nearly equal overall workload scores [+W]
  • Test-group students rated the mobile AR application very positively: 92% found it helpful for learning, 89% interactive, 86% positive about wider course use, and only 4% distracted [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use AR applications with natural hardware markers for applied assembly and practice topics in vocational courses
