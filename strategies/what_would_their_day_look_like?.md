# What Would Their Day Look Like?

## Description
Learners select a photo of a person or animal in an unfamiliar situation and imagine what their daily life might be like. They research the topic further and present their chosen character’s daily routine to the class. This activity encourages learners to step into someone else’s shoes and understand the challenges and experiences of others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Photos of people or animals in unfamiliar situations, access to research resources, and time for presentation.
#### Constraints
- Learners may make stereotypical assumptions if they lack sufficient knowledge. It requires careful selection of photos and guidance from the instructor to ensure accurate and respectful representations.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who are interested in exploring different cultures and lifestyles

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop empathy, promote perspective-taking, encourage research skills, improve communication skills

### Affordances
- Encourages perspective-taking by imagining the daily life of someone different. Develops research skills by exploring unfamiliar situations. Improves communication skills through presentations and diary entries.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose characters and situations that resonate with their interests and values. The level of research and presentation can be tailored to their abilities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess the depth of research, the accuracy of representations, and the level of empathy demonstrated in presentations or diary entries. Monitor changes in learners' attitudes towards different groups.

## Impact
- Research shows that perspective-taking exercises can increase empathy and reduce prejudice. Learning about different cultures and lifestyles also promotes cultural understanding and global awareness.
