# Use an affordable mobile box simulator with instrument tracking for accessible suturing practice and pre-operative warm-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the eoSim as a training tool because its mobile case setup requires only a laptop, allowing practice in almost any location, and its tracking software gives objective parametric feedback without an expert observer. The authors suggest it "could be an attractive tool to train laparoscopic psychomotor skills as a pre-operative warm up tool", especially before complex procedures such as suturing in small areas or complex angles. They also highlight the option of introducing adapted tasks for the training of a specific type of surgery, as done with the self-made upside-down task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validation of the training system in terms of face, content and construct validity before implementation in training programs
#### Constraints
- The tasks validated were difficult and complex, which led to participant frustration that could negatively influence face-validity opinions
- Only a small group of surgeons qualified as experts, limiting expert-group sample size

### Target Learners
- surgical, gynaecologic and urologic residents
- practising surgeons preparing for complex procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- advanced laparoscopic suturing skills
- psychomotor warm-up before surgery
