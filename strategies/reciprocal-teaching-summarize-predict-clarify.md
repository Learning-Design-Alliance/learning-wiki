# Use reciprocal teaching having students summarize, predict, and clarify learned content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Reciprocal teaching is the process of teaching cognitive strategies to increase learning abilities by having students "summarize, predict and clarify a concept that has been taught/learned." The article reports this strategy achieved a very high effect size of ES = 0.74 across two meta-analyses, with no effect difference by grade level, group size, or who taught the strategies. The authors suggest it can be successful under changing policy conditions if permitted with proper oversight and funding for professional development.
