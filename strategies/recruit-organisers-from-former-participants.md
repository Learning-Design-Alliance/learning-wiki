# Recruit competition organisers from former participants to renew the team and motivate current students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CzAO sustains its organising team by recruiting graduates of the competition itself. The article states that "Traditionally, most organisers are recruited from former participants when they graduate from high school and go to university", and that this "ensures continuous renewal of the team, diversity and professional quality of the competition problems." The organisers also serve as role models: the article says they are an inspiration to participants, motivating them to continue pursuing STEM fields and, in many cases, enter scientific careers.

## Design Implications

### Context
