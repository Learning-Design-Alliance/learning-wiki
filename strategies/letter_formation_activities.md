# Letter Formation Activities

## Description
Activities focused on teaching and practicing correct letter formation, including directionality, sequencing, and motor planning. These activities aim to establish a strong motor plan for letter formation, enabling automaticity and legibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Handwriting worksheets, letter formation guides, multisensory materials (e.g., sandpaper, playdough), and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not address underlying issues such as fine motor weakness or visual perception difficulties. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->

### Affordances
- Establishes a strong motor plan for letter formation, enabling automaticity and legibility. Addresses specific errors in letter formation through focused practice.

### Personalization
- Activities can be tailored to individual letter formation errors or learning styles. Multisensory approaches engage different sensory modalities.
