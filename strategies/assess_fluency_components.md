# Assess Fluency Components

## Description
Assessing must attend to fluency components and the learner. Assessments often assess accuracy, neglecting efficiency and flexibility. Timed tests do not assess fluency and can negatively affect learners, and thus should be avoided. Alternatives include interviews, observations, and written prompts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires skilled educators, diverse assessment tools, and a commitment to learner-centered evaluation. Demands time, resources, and thoughtful planning.
#### Constraints
- Can be resource-intensive, may introduce subjective bias, and may require reevaluation of assessment policies and grading structures.
