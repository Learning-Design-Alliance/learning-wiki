# Report research methods in full detail to enable replication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that method sections of research articles be written in full detail so that readers can evaluate and replicate the study. They state that "Articles must be reported in such a way that there should not be any question left in any reader's mind and in a way to provide all details to a person who wants to replicate the same study." This strategy responds to the deficiencies they found in model, population, sample, and analysis reporting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors must report population and its characteristics, reliability and validity of scales, and analysis techniques fully and precisely.
#### Constraints
- The study is limited to articles on preschool education published in the specified SSCI and ULAKBIM journals, and articles were not examined for detailed methodology such as suitability of statistics used.

### Target Learners
- education researchers
- journal referees and editors

### Target Learning Goals
- improving the quality and replicability of published educational research
