# Supplement lectures with hands-on group problem-solving sessions pairing undergraduates with graduate mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Outside regular classes, the SIAM student chapter holds seminars and lecture series introducing C programming in Linux (three 50-minute lectures on UNIX commands and text editors). The article identifies "a vital pedagogical need" for monitoring students to verify they follow every step, so hands-on sessions follow the lectures in which "students work in groups on the computer-based solution of mathematical problems that range from simple to complex", and "whenever possible, we pair an undergraduate student with a graduate student to provide mentorship and facilitate solving the problems".
