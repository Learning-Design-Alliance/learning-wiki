# Sustain technology integration through ongoing professional development, technical support, and sharing of best practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that one-time training is insufficient: institutions aiming to scale UDL-based digital pedagogy should provide "sustained professional development, dedicated technical support, and the systematic sharing of best practices". This follows from the finding that adoption of pedagogically valuable tools like Wooclap remained low (29.5%) and that time constraints, workload, and technostress were key barriers. The strategy targets institutional commitment rather than individual educator effort alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
