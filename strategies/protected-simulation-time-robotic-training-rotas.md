# Design training rotas with consistent, protected time for access to robotic simulators for skill progression

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that training rotas be designed with protected time for simulation, given the low median simulation exposure reported. It states that "Training rotas could be designed with consistent, protected time to access simulators for skill progression." This addresses the finding that respondents had a median of only 5 hours of simulation training and that restricted access to robotic consoles for simulation was a significant barrier.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to robotic simulation consoles within training units
#### Constraints
- The article notes theatre time pressures and increased scrutiny on efficiency in the current clinical climate

### Target Learners
- Cardiothoracic surgery trainees (ST1–ST8) in the UK and Republic of Ireland

### Target Learning Goals
- Develop technical skills for robotic-assisted thoracic surgery through simulation-based practice
