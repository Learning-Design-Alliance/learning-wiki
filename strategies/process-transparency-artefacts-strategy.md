# Require process transparency artefacts: process logs, prompt records, reflective commentaries and oral defences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that students submit "process logs, AI prompt records, or other “behind the scenes” artefacts alongside the final output", so educators can see how students engaged with tools and exercised judgement. Short reflective commentaries explain key decisions and justify tool use with explicit criteria for depth, criticality and ethical awareness. Oral defences, annotated portfolios, or recorded walkthroughs assess reasoning in real time, mirroring professional practices such as pitching and peer review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reflection must be integrated into assessment design with explicit criteria, not treated as an afterthought
#### Constraints
- In large cohorts, adding process logs, reflective commentaries or oral defences may appear impractical; the authors suggest embedding these into existing formats rather than creating wholly new tasks

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- making reasoning and decision-making visible
- metacognitive reflection
- justification of AI tool use
