# Develop and refine the programme further, including targeted oral language support, before subjecting it to a full trial

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The evaluators conclude that Talk for Writing is not yet ready for a full effectiveness trial. Asked whether the approach was ready for a full trial, they answered no, stating "Further work is required to develop the programme before it is subjected to a full trial." They also suggest the programme might benefit from targeted support of key oral language competencies for early writers and those with weaker oral language skills, drawing on evidence that oral language plays a stronger role in the early years than in Key Stage 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional development work to refine the approach before any full trial
#### Constraints
- The evaluation could not securely estimate impact on attainment due to the non-random comparison and small number of schools

### Target Learners
- Primary school pupils, particularly struggling writers and those with weaker oral language skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Written text production supported by oral language competencies
