# Rich Library: Complexity

## Description
Providing access to a diverse collection of reading materials that vary in academic vocabulary and syntax complexity supports literacy and reading fluency. This includes different types of materials such as nonfiction news articles and fictional stories to facilitate understanding of disciplinary literacy for reading and writing. Allowing learners to choose texts they want to read improves their motivation to read, in turn supporting their growth in background knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a collection of books and reading materials with varied complexity levels. Access to online resources can further enhance the richness of the library.
#### Constraints
- May require significant initial investment to build a diverse library. Requires ongoing maintenance to ensure the library remains relevant and engaging.
