# Accept students into a 1-2 year MS-to-PhD bridging experience providing advanced coursework, research experiences, and substantial mentoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy most generally adopted by Bridge Programs is "to accept students into MS to PhD bridging experience". During the 1-2 year period, programs support students in "enhancing their academic and research skills for acceptance into PhD programs". The six APS-funded bridge sites in Indiana, Ohio, Florida, and California provide advanced coursework, research experiences, and substantial mentoring for accepted students, giving students who lacked traditional routes an opportunity to be successful.
