# Highlight Math Vocabulary

## Description
Model how to highlight or circle mathematical words in word problems that prompt multiplication or division. Brainstorm potential words indicating multiplication or division with learners. Display words as class word walls or graphic organizers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Highlighters or colored pencils, word wall space, time for brainstorming
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if learners lack conceptual understanding of math operations

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners who struggle with math vocabulary or word problems

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop math communication, support working memory

### Affordances
- Helps learners identify key information in word problems, reduces cognitive load

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to different grade levels and math concepts. Word walls can be customized with learner-generated vocabulary.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe whether learners are accurately highlighting key words in word problems. Assess the completeness and accuracy of the word wall or graphic organizer.

## Impact
- Word walls and graphic organizers have been shown to improve vocabulary acquisition and retention. Highlighting helps learners focus on important information.
