# Use structured reflection tables, learning journals, prompt variation, adversarial queries, and peer debate to cultivate AI intuition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends instructional practices that make each phase of the experiential cycle visible and assessable. "Structured reflection tables and learning journals support the articulation of inductive heuristics, transforming tacit experience into shareable and evaluable understanding in the form of rules of thumb." Teachers guide students to vary prompts, challenge systems with atypical or contradictory queries, and engage in guided debates (e.g., whether generative AI should write an English essay) in which students articulate, justify, and revise positions from experiential and ethical evidence.
