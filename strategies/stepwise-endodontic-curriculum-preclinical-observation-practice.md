# Structured stepwise endodontic curriculum: preclinical training, clinical observation, then supervised clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes and endorses a three-stage progression in which students first complete "extensive hands-on laboratory practice" on extracted teeth in phantom simulators, then observe postgraduate residents managing real patients for six months, then perform supervised treatment on real patients for a year. The authors attribute the general rise in self-confidence to "the structured endodontic training approach at our faculty, which consists of preclinical, observation, and practical clinical stages."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preclinical laboratory training on extracted teeth and simulators before clinical exposure
- An observation internship familiarizing students with clinical workflows
- Supervised clinical practice with faculty oversight
#### Constraints
- Evaluated at a single institution with a single cohort; generalizability is limited

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- endodontic procedural competence
- transition from preclinical to clinical practice
