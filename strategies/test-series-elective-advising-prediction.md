# Develop a series of 10 or 12 tests of signal predictive value for elective advising and prevocational aptitude indexing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article closes by proposing that a refined battery be built for course-selection guidance. It judges the current Alpha "as random an agent as the traditional campus method of selecting courses" for elective advisory purposes, but finds "positive tendencies which encourage the hope that a series of 10 or 12 tests may yet be evolved" with signal predictive value for elective advising and as a partial index of prevocational aptitudes. The strategy is to select and refine subtests toward those ends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A battery of 10 or 12 tests whose subtests demonstrably correlate with the specific subjects relevant to elective advising decisions.
#### Constraints
- Individual Alpha subtests in their present form and degree of difficulty are of little diagnostic significance; specific instances invalidate definite deductions.

### Target Learners
- College freshmen choosing elective courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Predicting performance in specific subjects to advise elective course selection and index prevocational aptitudes
