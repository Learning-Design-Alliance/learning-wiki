# Cross-Grade-Level Tutoring

## Description
Form cross-grade-level alliances where older learners tutor those in lower grades. Pair older learners with younger learners to review previously mastered material and delve deeper into content. This provides individualized help to younger learners while further enhancing the older learners' content mastery, critical thinking, and communication skills. Spiraled curriculum standards provide unique opportunities for mutually beneficial partnerships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires identification of older learners with mastery, careful pairing of learners, clear expectations for tutoring sessions, and monitoring of tutoring dynamics.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure all learners are engaged and supported. Needs clear expectations to prevent misuse of power or off-task behavior. Requires ongoing feedback and guidance.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying content areas, tutoring activities, and pairing learners with different needs.
