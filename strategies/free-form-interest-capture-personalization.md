# Capture student interests free-form and at full granularity when personalizing content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends capturing students' interests with an unrestricted, free-form format rather than predefined categories, because interests vary widely in specificity — from broad topics like sports to individual athletes. The authors state that "simplistic approaches such as offering predeﬁned or limited choices are insufﬁcient to address the diverse needs of students". LLMs then align generated content precisely to the stated level of interest, preventing reduction of the personalization effect through weak interest-object connections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A generation pipeline (e.g., LLM-based) able to produce content for arbitrary, unrestricted interest statements in real time
#### Constraints
- Relies on collecting sensitive personal interest data, requiring transparency, data economy, and voluntary participation per GDPR

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing intrinsic motivation, situational interest, and learning performance by aligning content with individual interests
