# Select K-12 AI topics by whether their prerequisite mathematics is covered in the high school math curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's methodology connects AI concepts to the underlying math knowledge required, selecting topics "based on whether the relevant math knowledge is covered by the high school curriculum." Knowledge Representation and Planning was excluded because logic is not commonly part of the US K-12 math curriculum. This yielded "five AI concepts were suggested: Search, Bayesian Networks, Decision Trees, Clustering, and Linear Regression," each mapped to high school math (e.g., Search to exponential and linear functions, Decision Trees to logarithms). Classic example problems were then re-scenarized for high school students.
