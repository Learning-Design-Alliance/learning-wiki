# Future red flag trainings should keep asynchronous flexible learning, vary formats, and add complex vignettes with detailed background information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendations for improving the digital training: "future studies should keep the asynchronous learning possibility, flexibility to learn when time is available, and the different learning formats", while adding "more complex clinical examples, more detailed explanations of background information and consistently longer videos". They also recommend exploring flexible learning options, complex scenarios, and direct feedback mechanisms, and building an interface allowing more flexible learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning platform that supports flexible access to individual modules for review
#### Constraints
- Face-to-face discussion rounds had minimal uptake despite participant endorsement and reminders

### Target Learners
- qualified physiotherapists in continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- screening for serious pathologies and referral decision-making
