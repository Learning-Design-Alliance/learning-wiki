# Physics departments and student organizations should build mentorship, leadership, and career-conceptualization programs to support women's persistence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that national organizations like SPS and Sigma Pi Sigma and their institutional chapters use the interconnection between identity, belonging, and support structures to "build effective programs for women and for all students in physics." Departments, clubs, and physics groups developing programs to increase women's persistence should deepen understanding of how mentorship, leadership, and career activities relate to identity development.
