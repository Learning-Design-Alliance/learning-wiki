Encourage and support teachers to implement the FNT method with metacognitive techniques in environmental education at any grade level¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the study's findings, the article's recommendation section states that the FNT method with metacognitive techniques is an effective teaching and learning method for enhancing knowledge, awareness, global warming decreasing behavior and critical thinking. It grounds the method in learner-centered activities, learning by experience and thinking, the social constructivist view, and metacognitive intellectual procedures, and concludes that teachers "should be encouraged and supported to implement this method in teaching environmental education at any grade level".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher support and encouragement to implement the method; lesson plans structured on the four FNT stages with embedded metacognitive questions

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single quasi-experiment with 80 grade 11 students in one Thai school, so generalization to any grade level is the authors' recommendation rather than a tested result

Target Learners¶

  • secondary students across grade levels

Target Learning Goals¶

  • environmental knowledge
  • environmental awareness
  • pro-environmental behavior
  • critical thinking

Affordances¶

  • Four Noble Truths Teaching Method

Claims¶

  • Prior Biology learning outcome interacted significantly with learning model on global warming awareness, decreasing behavior and critical thinking [+W]
  • Students in both the experimental and control groups improved on all four outcomes from pretest to posttest [+W]
  • Grade 11 students taught environmental education with the Four Noble Truths method plus metacognitive techniques scored higher than traditionally taught students on pretest-adjusted posttests of global warming knowledge, awareness, decreasing behavior and critical thinking, except in the deduction aspect [+W]
  • High prior Biology achievers showed more global warming awareness, decreasing behavior and critical thinking than low achievers, but did not differ significantly in global warming knowledge [+W]
