Select a sample of schools, including replacement schools when needed, to support generalizable impact studies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends that researchers sample schools in a way that represents the target population, and plan for "replacement schools, when needed" when originally selected schools decline to participate. This sampling-and-replacement step is presented as one of the key actions researchers can take so that study findings are more generalizable to the study's target population.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researchers must plan a school selection procedure and a replacement-school process in advance

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • education researchers designing impact studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • selecting school samples that support generalizable findings

Related Strategies¶

  • Assess and adjust for differences between the study sample and the target population
  • Actively manage school recruitment to protect the generalizability of impact studies
  • Define the target population as the first step in designing a generalizable impact study
