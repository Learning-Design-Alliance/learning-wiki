# Home Living Center Role Play

## Description
Learners visit the home living center to role play different characters and relate these characters to different emotions. Help them to make their characters even more real by giving them emotions and acting those out.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a facilitator to create the context and provide guidance during the activity.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be willing to perform in front of others. May not be effective with learners who are naturally introverted.

- Provides authentic scenario to explore emotions through role playing.

### Personalization
- Learners can select specific emotions they wish to explore. Prompts and character suggestions can also be provided.

## Assessment Evidence
- The facilitator can observe how the learner uses vocabulary to create their story. Ask learners to do an individual reflection of how they used emotions and what they learned in the role playing exercise.

## Impact
- Research supports the use of role-playing and dramatic activities to enhance emotional understanding and social skills. (Source: Spontaneous Drama Improves Communication Skills - https://www.livestrong.com/article/198645-spontaneous-drama-improves-communication-skills/)
