# Deliver MHFA as a blended or timetabled part of the medical curriculum to support skills rehearsal and uptake

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding the MHFA course within medical students' timetables and considering blended delivery. They note that "A 'blended' approach involving both online learning and brief face-to-face group training may be beneficial for skills rehearsal", since some participants found it difficult to rehearse new skills through eLearning alone. They also propose timetabling: "There may be benefit in timetabling the MHFA course in the medical curriculum as this could improve uptake of the course". This strategy addresses the high dropout observed when completion is left entirely self-paced.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum time or scheduling slots allocated to the course
- Access to brief face-to-face group training sessions for the blended option
#### Constraints
- The article states this as a suggested benefit to be tested in future research, not as a tested result of this pilot

### Target Learners
- UK medical students with heavy academic workloads

### Target Learning Goals
- Rehearse mental health first aid skills
- Improve uptake and completion of mental health first aid training
