# Introduce particle physics concepts of scale and matter in elementary education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their pilot study findings of engagement, understanding, and teacher-perceived relevance, the authors advocate moving concepts of scale and the nature of matter earlier in schooling. They write: "Based on this pilot study, we advocate incorporating concepts related to particle physics earlier in education." The literature they cite suggests concepts of small scale and the particulate nature of matter are accessible to young learners, supporting earlier introduction rather than deferring physics to high school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials must be presented "in an engaging and thoughtful way"; support from enrichment experiences such as museum programs and web resources
#### Constraints
- Early reviews suggested particle physics is too abstract for elementary students; the authors' counterargument rests on a single pilot study

### Target Learners
- elementary and middle school students (7-12 years old)

### Target Learning Goals
- concepts of scale and the nature of matter
- foundations for later understanding of particle physics
