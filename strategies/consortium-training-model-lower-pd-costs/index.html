Use a consortium training model to lower professional development costs to individual districts¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report describes a consortium training model for delivering professional development across districts: "The program can be implemented using a consortium training model, in which school leaders from multiple districts attend the training in a central location, to lower program costs to individual districts." In the reviewed study, principals attended training together at one of two locations closest to their districts. Facilities for the consortium model are provided by a participating district or a regional education agency.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A centrally located facility provided by one of the districts or a regional education agency; schools and districts are responsible for transportation to and from the location

Constraints¶

  • Actual program costs are only available in consultation with the developer

Target Learners¶

  • school leaders from multiple participating districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • accessing shared professional learning sessions at reduced per-district cost

Related Strategies¶

  • Balanced Leadership Pd Program
  • One-day teacher workshop plus optional training-of-trainers for implementing CSC
  • Start PD with ready and willing teachers and scale up over time, using train-the-trainers models
