# Close the loop by re-aligning teaching units and assessment components using ITU and ISAC evidence within PDCA cycles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes concrete re-alignment actions triggered by indicator discrepancies: redistribute instructional time toward under-delivered CLOs, rebalance exam and SAC design so outcomes are assessed proportionally to intended weight, diversify assessment methods such as project-based evaluation for higher-order outcomes, and re-map CLOs to underrepresented PLOs. It states that "Re-alignment uses TU and SAC evidence (e.g., ITU-CLO and ISAC-PLO) to recalibrate teaching and assessment to intended CLOs/PLOs within OBE", following Plan–Do–Check–Act improvement cycles to satisfy accreditation expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- End-of-offering alignment reviews by faculty using ITU-CLO, ISAC-CLO, ISP-CLO, ITU-PLO, and ISAC-PLO indicators, integrated into curriculum committee reviews
#### Constraints
- The article frames institutionalization as requiring a course-level dashboard tracking TU, SAC, CLO, and PLO alignment in real time, which it lists as future work to integrate with e-assessment data

### Target Learners
- higher education students in STEM programs

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced outcome attainment and continuous curriculum improvement
