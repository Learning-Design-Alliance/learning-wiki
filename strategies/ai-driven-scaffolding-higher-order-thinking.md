# Incorporate AI-driven scaffolding strategies to support students' higher-order thinking skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among the article's implications for practice, "Course designers may need to incorporate AI-driven scaffolding strategies to support students' higher-order thinking skills." This responds directly to the finding that models struggle to distinguish facilitator and co-learner questions: where automated classification of higher-order inquiry is weak, designed scaffolding is needed so that AI-supported activities still push learners beyond factual questioning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course designs that pair AI question analysis with scaffolding moves targeting facilitator and co-learner question types.
