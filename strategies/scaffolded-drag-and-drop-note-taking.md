# Scaffold note-taking in learning apps with drag-and-drop information items rather than free-form input

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
When learners must collect and summarize information while reading, restrict note-taking to drag-and-drop selection of pre-defined information items instead of free keyboard input. This scaffolds the note-taking process — "a pedagogical process experts use to help learners perform tasks they cannot do by themselves" — so users can complete the task within their zone of proximal development. The article adopted this after focus groups showed frustration with free-form notes.
