Deploy prevention and intervention supports for underachieving gifted students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief enumerates practices found successful in preventing or reversing gifted underachievement: early screening and identification (as early as kindergarten), meaningful extracurricular activities, counseling, mentoring, targeted tutoring, home-school partnerships, school-based consultants, and dual-diagnosis screening for twice-exceptional students. Each addresses needs the literature links to underachievement, such as low motivation, poor teacher relationships, and unmet affective needs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Availability of services within a comprehensive continuum addressing the needs of underachieving gifted students

Constraints¶

  • Schools cannot undo the negative effects of children's situations and environments, but can develop strategies and interventions that support achievement

Target Learners¶

  • Underachieving gifted students, including twice-exceptional students and gifted English language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Prevent or reverse underachievement and support reading achievement and work habits
