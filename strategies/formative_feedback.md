# Formative Feedback

## Description
Teacher performance management processes can be viewed as part of a growth mindset by making it formative, rather than summative, and inviting participation of the teacher in the process. The feedback can be more meaningful and applicable to the teacher’s daily practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a clear and transparent performance management system. Teachers need to feel safe receiving feedback and have opportunities to implement changes in their practice.
#### Constraints
- Some teachers may resist feedback or find it challenging to implement changes in their practice. Requires a long-term commitment to fostering a culture of feedback and continuous improvement.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Teachers and educators at all levels, particularly those seeking to improve their teaching practices or develop new skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve teaching practices, promote professional growth, and create a culture of continuous improvement in schools by providing timely, actionable, and supportive feedback to teachers

### Affordances
- Provides teachers with specific and constructive feedback on their teaching practices, helps them identify their strengths and weaknesses, and supports them in developing strategies for improving their performance. Promotes a collaborative approach to professional development.

### Personalization
- Tailor feedback to individual teacher needs and goals. Provide opportunities for teachers to reflect on their own performance and identify areas for improvement. Offer personalized coaching and mentoring to support teacher growth.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track teacher participation in feedback activities. Collect teacher reflections on their teaching practices and professional growth. Evaluate the impact of feedback on student learning outcomes.

## Impact
- Research suggests that teachers who receive regular formative feedback are more effective and more satisfied with their jobs. Implementing formative feedback fosters a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
