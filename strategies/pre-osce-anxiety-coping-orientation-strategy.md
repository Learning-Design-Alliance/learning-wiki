# Implement anxiety-coping programs and orientation sessions before OSCE examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends preparing students for the anxiety of high-stakes OSCE assessment. Its recommendations include to "implement coping anxiety program on the nursing students before OSCE examination" and to provide an orientation session before the exam. It further recommends identifying more anxious students during mock OSCE tests and supporting them through motivational interviews, and identifying predictive anxiety factors during mock OSCE tests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mock OSCE tests to identify anxious students before the examination
#### Constraints
- The anxiety coping program was not executed in this study; no follow-up assessment of student anxiety levels was carried out

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing anxiety before high-stakes clinical assessment
- improving OSCE performance
