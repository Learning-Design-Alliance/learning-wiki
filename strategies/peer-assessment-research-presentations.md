# Peer assessment of student research presentations to teach assessment criteria

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the second half of the year students are marked for three presentations beyond the proposal (two progress reports and one final report) by lecturers, supervisors, and fellow students. The authors state that "the key advantage of peer assessment is that it helps the student markers to understand the assessment criteria for the activity" and that it is a strategy consistent with the social construction model of learning.
