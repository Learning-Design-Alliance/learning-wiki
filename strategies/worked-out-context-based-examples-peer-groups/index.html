Use worked-out context-based examples with peer group study as the core instructional device¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a strategy in which learners receive a worksheet with worked-out examples at the beginning of instruction, work with peers in groups to study the examples and solve problems, and receive assistance when needed. Worked-out examples present a model solution step-by-step so learners devote cognitive capacity to studying the solution procedure and constructing a schema rather than searching for solutions. The article cites Cooper and Sweller (1987) that many worked-out examples are more effective than a few followed by instruction.
