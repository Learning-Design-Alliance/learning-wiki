# Weekly need-based e-mentoring messages via LINE combined with weekly in-class self-evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An e-mentoring team of one teacher and a teaching assistant sent different need-based messages of advice and encouragement to students every week through the social networking system LINE, with messages varied according to how successfully students had completed the learning materials. This out-of-class mentoring was combined with weekly in-class self-evaluations as part of the course routine, in a system intended to develop self-regulated learning attitudes, skills and behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students grouped by TOEIC score (high, mid, low) so messages can be need-based; a teacher and teaching assistant as the mentoring team; access to the LINE social networking system
