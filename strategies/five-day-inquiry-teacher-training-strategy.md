# Run a five-day teacher training and development program with expert supervision before implementing inquiry-based interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's interventions were prepared by teachers attending a five-day training program covering prior positive results, the defining properties of inquiry-based interventions, and common teacher misconceptions, after which teachers developed their own interventions with support and supervision from guidance counsellors and university professors who checked consistency. Teachers confirmed the development "would not have possible if it had not been for all this time they benefited from."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must have time (five days, separated by about two weeks) to develop interventions with support and supervision from counsellors and university professors.
#### Constraints
- Developing the interventions was challenging; some teachers called the development stages a headache, and compromises such as skipped steps and hints were made.

### Target Learners
- Secondary students in Grades 5 to 10 science classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Interest in school science and technology through inquiry-based teaching
