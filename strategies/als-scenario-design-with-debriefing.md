# Incorporate algorithms, technical skills and structured team communication into every simulation scenario, followed by extended debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's scenarios deliberately embedded all knowledge categories: "During HFS, algorithms, technical skills and team working/early warning scores/communication strategies were incorporated into each scenario." Each student acted in 3 scenarios, serving as team leader at least once, and scenarios forced participants to transmit information using a structured communication strategy. Every scenario ended with a debriefing lasting 2 to 3 times the scenario's duration, guided by an instructor using advocacy with inquiry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor with crisis resource management certification to run scenarios and debriefing
- A checklist verifying that all syllabus topics are included in each scenario
- Debriefing sessions lasting 2 to 3 times the scenario duration
#### Constraints
- Exact reproducibility of each scenario is difficult because it depends on participants' competence and interactions

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- ALS algorithms
- team working and leadership
- structured communication strategies
