# Weekly formative assignments: application cards after the first session and short written reflections after the second

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each week students complete two graded assignments. "After the  rst course period of every week, students will connect presented concepts and theories to real-world situations in order to develop an “application card.”" After the second session they submit a short written reflection identifying remaining questions and summarizing key points in a single sentence; sixty percent of the final grade rests on satisfactory completion of these weekly assignments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments are tied to the two weekly sessions: application cards after the first session and written reflections after the second.
#### Constraints
- Weekly assignments are graded on satisfactory completion and together account for sixty percent of the final grade.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate STEM students

### Target Learning Goals
- applying course frameworks to additional real-world contexts
- interpreting high-level concepts and communicating them in students' own words
