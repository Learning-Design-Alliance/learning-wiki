Assess the process: grade design rationales, prompt logs, and oral defenses, avoiding reliance on AI detectors¶

Description¶

The review's actionable takeaways include: "Assess the process: Grade design rationales, prompt logs, and oral defenses; avoid reliance on AI detectors." The process-based assessment workflow family elaborates this: grading design rationales, test-first thinking, and revision quality, and using Viva or code reviews to assess authorship and understanding. The practice-innovations section further specifies process-based assessment portfolios that assess evolution of prompts from solution-seeking to strategy-seeking, test-first and revision practices logged in version control, and oral defenses or code walkthroughs demonstrating understanding, with rubrics for process quality and LMS integrations for log capture.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Rubrics for process quality
  • LMS integrations for log capture
  • Faculty training on portfolio grading

Constraints¶

  • The review frames process evidence as an alternative to detector policing, noting detectors are unreliable

Target Learners¶

  • Higher-education CS students whose authorship and understanding are assessed

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Demonstrating authorship and understanding of submitted work
  • Strategy-seeking prompt evolution over solution-seeking

Claims¶

  • AI-detection tools produce inequitable outcomes, disadvantaging non-native English speakers through false positives [+W]
  • Universities disabling AI detection cite false-positive risk at scale, and detector influence persists in individual grading, journal screening, and institutions without policy revision [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Replace detector-centred assessment with a four-stage process-oriented model: declared starting point, versioned drafting, process artefacts, and targeted oral verification
  • Use weekly oral code review interviews to incentivize students to understand AI-assisted code
  • Two-instrument assessment: AI-free coding quizzes plus verification-gated oral defenses of comment-stripped AI-assisted work
  • Assess process over product using staged, connected tasks with iterative evidence
  • Stage GAI use in assessment: permit exploration, restrict independent reasoning, require justification
