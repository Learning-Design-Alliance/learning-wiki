# Form equitable TBL teams via a voluntary equity-score questionnaire and Greedy Algorithm-based balanced partitioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy collects voluntary, anonymized (roll-number based) questionnaire responses on willingness to participate, place of upbringing, economic status, and self-perceived preparedness, each answer carrying an equity score from 40 to 90. Students are sorted by score in descending order and paired highest-with-lowest into teams, with variance minimized by reallocating students. The article frames this as "a behavioural scaffold that aligns group dynamics with the broader objectives of inclusion and fairness."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary, informed participation with a preface communicating the purpose; students opting out are assigned a default score of 45
- Anonymity via roll numbers and simple, unambiguous question wording for non-native English speakers
#### Constraints
- The study is limited in scale, within a single institution and discipline
- The strategy focuses exclusively on socio-economic background and self-perceived preparedness, excluding gender, race, and neurodiversity

### Target Learners
- sophomore engineering undergraduates in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable collaboration in team-based learning
- fair assessment outcomes across socio-economic backgrounds
