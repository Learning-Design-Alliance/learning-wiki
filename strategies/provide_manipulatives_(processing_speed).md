# Provide Manipulatives (Processing Speed)

## Description
Providing manipulatives to help make complex concepts concrete and support processing speed. This strategy involves using physical objects and hands-on materials to illustrate abstract ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of manipulatives, careful planning of activities, and instructor guidance on their effective use
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure alignment with learning objectives, may not be suitable for all types of content, and some students may find manipulatives distracting

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students who struggle with abstract concepts and benefit from tactile experiences

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Make abstract concepts concrete, improve comprehension, and support hands-on learning

### Affordances
- Engages multiple senses, reduces cognitive load, provides a physical representation of abstract ideas, and supports active learning

### Personalization
- Manipulatives can be selected based on subject matter and student needs, with options for different materials and interactive activities.
