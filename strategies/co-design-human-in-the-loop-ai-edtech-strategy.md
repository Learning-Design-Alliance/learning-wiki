# Center co-design with teachers and students throughout AI tool R&D, using human-in-the-loop workflows as a primary risk mitigation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that AI developers "embrace and center the expertise of teachers and students by prioritizing co-design throughout a tool's R&D processes." Across risk sections, respondents described human-in-the-loop testing where experts and users evaluate output quality, and co-design to center users' needs. The report notes human-in-the-loop workflows were "highlighted as one of the most meaningful risk mitigation strategies" and recommends developers work more closely with teachers and students to co-design solutions meeting real needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Involving experts and users in evaluating output quality during development
- Engaging established partners and maintaining transparency and communication channels with users
#### Constraints
- The report notes that despite nearly every submission mentioning equity, rarely were submissions intentional about meaningful co-design processes

### Target Learners
- PK-12 mathematics students in the United States, particularly students who are Black, Latino, and/or from low-income backgrounds
- Teachers and instructional coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable mathematics teaching and learning
- Safe, ethical, and reliable use of AI-enabled tools
