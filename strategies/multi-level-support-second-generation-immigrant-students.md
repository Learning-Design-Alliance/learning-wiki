# Combine socioeconomic opportunity improvements, immigrant family mental-health support, and school-based support for immigrant children to narrow the performance gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends acting at several levels: societal interventions so that "societal interventions to improve the socioeconomic opportunities of immigrants are needed to reduce the gap in school performance"; support for immigrant families to reduce parental mental-disorder risk; and school-level measures. For individual-level school support, the article states that "more support at school for immigrant children to overcome language barriers may be crucial", noting existing Swedish measures such as Swedish-language instruction on arrival, mother-tongue courses, multicultural teacher training, and extra schooling hours.
