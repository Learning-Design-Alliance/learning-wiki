# Provide staff and student induction and ongoing training for educational technologies in inquiry-based contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that staff and student induction and ongoing training in the use of educational technologies for learning in inquiry-based contexts such as PBL is needed. It warns against the often-false sink or swim approach in which faculty assume students' prior technological skills or knowledge. Where new affordances are introduced, additional hands-on tutorials and training are helpful to facilitate student adaptation to the technology and ensure optimal benefit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guidance and support should be tailored to meet the needs of each user including learner, facilitators, and curriculum developers
#### Constraints
- The ever-changing nature of both technical knowledge of teaching staff and the technological affordances themselves creates ongoing training demands

### Target Learners
- health sciences students and teaching staff in PBL curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- effective use of educational technologies for inquiry-based learning
