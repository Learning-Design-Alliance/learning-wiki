## Description
The editorial describes two forward-looking practitioner and researcher directions from the special issue responses. Nisiotis points to how practitioners, scholars and learning designers can use the review's findings to effectively utilise mobile game-based learning approaches to support and enhance learning, and discusses future directions for smartphone enabled virtual reality. Eutsler provides a comprehensive list of recommendations to guide future research in the areas of design, pedagogy, and impact on student learning, offering a roadmap for research and practice as educators navigate remote learning.
