# Guided worksheet transferring small-scale convection findings to the Earth-scale ITCZ and Hadley cell

## Description
Activity 3 is a question-and-answer worksheet that walks students from solar heating of the ground, through rising humid air and rain at the equator, to descending dry air near 30 degrees latitude and the Trade Winds closing the Hadley cell. The article explains that "This worksheet helps students to transfer the knowledge learnt in the first two activities to the situation on Earth, where the Sun is the main heat source". It ends by asking students to draw a schematic of the Hadley cell.
