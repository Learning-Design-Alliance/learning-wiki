# Role Play

## Description
Role playing involves acting out different scenarios in a safe environment, allowing learners to make mistakes and learn from them. In a corporate setting, this could involve simulating customer interactions or workplace conflicts. This approach builds confidence, develops listening skills and engages learners in creative problem-solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator to guide the role-play, relevant scenarios, and a safe space for participants to act out and reflect.
#### Constraints
- Some participants may feel uncomfortable or self-conscious during role-playing activities. Success depends on creating a supportive environment.
