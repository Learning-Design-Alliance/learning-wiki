Strategies

Provide summary guidelines or instructional checklists to counter post-training fidelity decline¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that implementation fidelity drops slightly with time after training ends, the authors state that "providing DTT implementers with summary guidelines or instructional checklists could help counter a potential drop in DTT ability with time." This is a forward-looking recommendation drawn from the maintenance analysis, offered as a low-cost support for maintainers of trained staff.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Trainees must first be trained to implement DTT, with checklists serving as a maintenance support afterward

Constraints¶

  • The underlying maintenance decline was significant in the full HLM model but not apparent once 14 outliers were excluded

Target Learners¶

  • special education teachers
  • paraprofessionals
  • parents

Target Learning Goals¶

  • maintaining DTT implementation fidelity over time

Claims¶

  • DTT implementation fidelity declines slightly after training ends, though the decline may reflect outliers [+W]
  • DTT implementation fidelity declines slightly after training ends, with a statistically significant difference between intervention and maintenance slopes [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Deliver complete behavioral skills training (feedback, instruction, modeling, and rehearsal together or sequentially) to train DTT implementers
  • Provide summary guidelines or instructional checklists to counter a potential drop in DTT implementation ability over time
