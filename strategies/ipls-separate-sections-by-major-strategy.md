# Address the Health Science major achievement gap by coordinating with biology departments and health science colleges, potentially via separate sections per major

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend addressing the apparent achievement gap between majors by coordinating across colleges. They state this "may require offering separate sections for each type of major that more specifically addresses each major's needs", while cautioning that difficulties may arise in terms of available faculty members to teach both sets of sections. The recommendation stems from Health Science majors' more novice-like entering attitudes and lower grades in the mixed-major IPLS-like course.
