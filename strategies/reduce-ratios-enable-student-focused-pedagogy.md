# Redesign nursing education programmes to reduce student-teacher ratios and class sizes so student-focused pedagogical strategies become feasible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that excessive student numbers and large lecture halls correlate with non-adoption of the architectures the authors consider most pedagogically effective, the article recommends that "Any future redefining of nursing education must consider these factors to successfully favor the adoption of student-focused pedagogical strategies." It calls for careful evaluation by the bodies responsible for planning and organising degree programmes, within a coherent policy framework aimed at optimising learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination of activities of every member and institution involved at every level of the education process within a coherent policy framework
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and correlational, relying on self-report data
