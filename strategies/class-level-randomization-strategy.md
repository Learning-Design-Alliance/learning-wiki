# Class-level randomization with blinded examiners for comparing instructional methods across intact student groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When instructional methods must be delivered to intact classes, the article randomized at the class level using "a computer-generated random sequence with a 1:1 allocation ratio" implemented by an independent researcher. Examiners were blinded to group allocation, and students were not informed of the trial's specific purpose to minimize performance bias. Generalized linear mixed-effects models accounted for age and class-level clustering.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An independent researcher not involved in recruitment, teaching, or assessment implemented the randomization procedure.
#### Constraints
- Because the intervention was delivered within intact student groups, clustering effects cannot be fully excluded and may have contributed to variability in performance.

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students in pre-clinical skills curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- history-taking skills
