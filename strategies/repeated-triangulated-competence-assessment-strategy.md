# Assess competence development with repeated online tests, questionnaires, and interviews across a course sequence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study assessed learning outcomes by measuring domain competences three times using online tests, surveying perceptions in parallel with online questionnaires, and conducting semi-structured interviews at the end. The BIM test drew "5 randomly selected single-choice questions ... from a pool of 15 questions in total", and the 19-item Likert questionnaire was collected at the beginning of the course, the beginning of the practice task, and its end, enabling progression analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online test item pool from which questions can be randomly sampled
- A questionnaire administered at matched time points across cohorts
- Post-task interviews transcribed and analyzed by two coders with joint debriefing
#### Constraints
- The first VirtuIng measurement preceded any BIM domain input, making BIM-knowledge measurements subject to inaccuracy

### Target Learners
- higher education students in elective online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- domain competence progression
- student perceptions of learning and self-organization
