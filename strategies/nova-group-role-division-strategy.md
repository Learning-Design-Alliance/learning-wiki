# Assign complementary roles within small groups (prompt specialist, data analyst, group representative), with three members as the recommended maximum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To make ChatGPT-based simulation work productive, the article recommends dividing tasks within each student group while maintaining teamwork. It writes that "if the group consists of three members, which is the maximum recommended number, there could be a prompt specialist, a data analyst, and a group representative who reports their results to the class." This role structure maps onto the activity's stages of prompting NOVA, analyzing data, and reporting in the plenary.

## Design Implications

### Context
