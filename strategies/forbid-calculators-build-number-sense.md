# Forbid calculators during estimation work and have students practice putting in numbers by hand to rebuild their numerical sense

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During arithmetic-heavy estimation steps the author tells students that "calculators rot their brain" and bans them. The stated rationale is that students "would be able to calculate to one digit without a calculator, except that calculator use has atroph ied their numerical sense," so students "need to practice – they need to put in numbers – to recover thei r feel for numbers." Mental-arithmetic tricks (writing 300 as 0.3·10³, identities like 4×4=20) are taught in place of keying in calculations.
