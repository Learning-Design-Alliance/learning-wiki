# Share the simulation scenario with learners in advance and repeat the scenario within sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that students received the postpartum hemorrhage scenario and learning objectives one day before simulation, enabling preparatory reading and active participation, and that each scenario was performed twice per session. The authors attribute the improved knowledge partly to this advance sharing, noting consistency with the INASCL guideline that students "should get the simulation scenario prior to the day of the simulation."
