# Air quality detection case study using an accelerometer and Edge Impulse studio across four air-quality conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Students combine a development board and a three-axis accelerometer with Edge Impulse studio to detect different types of air quality. The article states "The classification for this air quality detection project is tested through four different conditions: clean air, slightly polluted, highly polluted, and controlled". Training data can be collected simply with an air purifier in a laboratory setting, which the article calls a widely accessible way to generate a training set. The article identifies this as the most accessible case study, with no student limitations regarding access or security.
