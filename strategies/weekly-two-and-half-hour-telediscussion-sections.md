# Schedule telediscussion courses weekly in two-and-one-half-hour sections with discussion before and after each telelecture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on student agreement in the trial course, the author recommends that future telediscussion courses meet once a week in longer sections, "with discussion of the telelecture both preceding and following the presentations." This responds to the finding that one-hour telelectures felt too short for both listening and questioning, and that adequate post-lecture discussion can help students acquire organized information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduling capacity for two-and-one-half-hour weekly sections; telelecturers available for hour-long presentations within them.
#### Constraints
- The article notes time limitations in a quarter system make interspersing telelectures with classroom theory lectures difficult.

### Target Learners
- graduate students in library science
- practicing librarians in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding current information retrieval projects and systems
