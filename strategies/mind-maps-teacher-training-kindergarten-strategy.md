# Use Mind Maps in kindergarten Arabic teaching and train kindergarten teachers in the method

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's results, the researcher recommends using Mind Maps in teaching Arabic language for kindergarten children and training kindergarten teachers on the methods of using Mind Maps, for their contribution to modifying lack of attention. The researcher further suggests future studies on Mind Maps' effect on achievement and information retention among kindergarten children, and on other subject areas. This is the article's own forward-looking recommendation rather than a tested manipulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need training on the methods of using Mind Maps before implementation
#### Constraints
- The author states results could not be generalized beyond kindergarten children in Riyadh city in the 2014/2015 school year, the first semester, and the Ministry-specified Arabic textbook topics

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten children

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing lack of attention in Arabic language class
