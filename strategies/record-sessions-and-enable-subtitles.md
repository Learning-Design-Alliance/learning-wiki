# Record online sessions and add subtitles so participants can attend asynchronously and across languages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An accessibility strategy for virtual teaching: the authors report that "all our activities were recorded and made ava ilable on youtube so that participants could attend at their convenience", addressing connectivity and power problems and overlapping events. Because "A few of the participants were from non-English speaking countries", they "enabled subtitles on our videos for their beneﬁt".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recording infrastructure and a video platform such as YouTube
- Subtitle authoring for non-English-speaking participants
#### Constraints
- Internet connectivity problems, power cuts and overlapping events limit live attendance

### Target Learners
- school students
- undergraduate and post-graduate students
- school teachers
- participants from non-English speaking countries

### Target Learning Goals
- astronomy
- physics
