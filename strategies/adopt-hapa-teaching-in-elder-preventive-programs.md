# Adopt HAPA-based structured teaching in preventive and educational programs for older people with chronic conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that healthcare authorities incorporate HAPA-based teaching into preventive and self-care programs for older adults, arguing such programs could be cost-beneficial because improved self-care prevents hypertension complications. The recommendation rests on their finding that a seven-session HAPA program significantly raised self-care in older hypertensive adults while a control condition did not change. They state: "it is suggested that healthcare authorities should pay attention to HAPA in teaching and preventive programs for self-care behavior in older people with hypertension."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured multi-session delivery addressing both motivational and volitional HAPA constructs, with follow-up support
#### Constraints
- Generalizability beyond South Fars health centers in Iran requires further research, as the authors state

### Target Learners
- Older adults with hypertension or other chronic diseases

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-care behavior improvement and complication prevention

### Affordances
