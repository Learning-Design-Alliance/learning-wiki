# PD providers should help teachers envision the destination and path of instructional change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Spillane et al. (2002), the article suggests PD providers in early-stage standards reform should help teachers understand what aligned instruction looks like. Teachers' nascent understanding of CCSS-ELA writing instruction inhibited substantial shifts. PD should help teachers envision both the destination (a research-based writing environment) and the path from current practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PD must address teachers' level of understanding of what new-standards-aligned instruction looks like
#### Constraints
- Most relevant in early stages of standards-based reform when teacher understanding is nascent

### Target Learners
- elementary teachers implementing new writing standards

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding and enacting CCSS-ELA-aligned writing instruction
