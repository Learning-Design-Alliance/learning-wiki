# Conduct Level 1 (no-AI) assessments under supervision or as low-stakes formative tasks to protect equity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For assessments designated Level 1, the article recommends supervision or low-stakes formative use, because unsupervised out-of-class no-AI work raises equity concerns: students with stronger English, higher digital literacy, or paid GenAI access may use AI in potentially undetectable ways. The authors cite "potential equity concerns with permitting out-of-class work under" no-AI conditions.
