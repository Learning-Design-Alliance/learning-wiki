# Dedicated weekly Year 7 oracy lesson curriculum built around four units culminating in a five-minute speech

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
School 21's dedicated oracy curriculum devotes one lesson a week to teaching oracy throughout Year 7, aiming to provide students with a variety of contexts in which to develop all four skill areas of the Oracy Skills Framework. Its four units are: Finding our voice (strategies and protocols for listening, talking and group work); Performance poetry (physical and social-emotional strands); Persuasive techniques (linguistic strand, formal and informal talk scenarios); and Ignite, which prepares students for a five-minute individual talk delivered without notes to an audience. Outline plans, resources and exemplar video clips are available on the Voice 21 website.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly curriculum time must be allocated; the evaluation notes adopting schools may need to allocate dedicated curriculum time
#### Constraints
- The evaluation found the curriculum needs to emphasise oracy within a diverse range of informal as well as formal contexts and strengthen the cognitive strand including exploratory talk

### Target Learners
- Year 7 students in secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- Development of oracy skills across all four framework strands, particularly persuasive and presentational talk

### Affordances
- Oracy Skills Framework Four Strands
