# Extend online stroke training with practical content, deeper explanations and longer duration, and keep the programme accessible beyond the research period

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on participants' improvement suggestions from the qualitative interviews, future versions of online stroke training should add practical components and clinical scenarios, expand duration and depth, and consider in-person or blended elements. Participants stated "The programme was short and lacked practical aspects" and asked for deeper coverage of outcome measures and telerehabilitation. Several participants also suggested the programme remain available beyond the research period and be widely disseminated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional time and content development to expand modules into more lectures
- Institutional support for practical or workshop components
#### Constraints
- Participants noted effective application of the training content in practice requires adequate time, good management and collaboration across departments, which may be difficult under time pressure

### Target Learners
- Physiotherapists in Saudi Arabia working with stroke patients

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical application of stroke assessment, goal-setting and self-management knowledge
