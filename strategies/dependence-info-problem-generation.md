# Use equation-dependence information during problem generation to support correct-solution generation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that Andes first applies its equation-dependence capability during problem generation. As printed: "The ability to determine dependence of equations is first used by Andes during problem generation, by providing information to that component of the ITS which generates correct solutions to the problem". This makes the dependence analysis a pre-tutoring step that feeds the solution generator.
