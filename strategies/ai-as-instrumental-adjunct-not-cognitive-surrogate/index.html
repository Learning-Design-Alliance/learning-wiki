Position AI as an instrumental adjunct that amplifies, rather than attenuates, students' direct experiential engagement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on the Neidan framework, this strategy holds that algorithmic systems in education should "serve to amplify, rather than attenuate, the student's direct experiential engagement with knowledge." AI may function as a refined implement supporting interior cultivation, but never as a cognitive surrogate or thought controller. Delegating the work of thinking to machines is framed as severing the connection between consciousness and cosmos, violating the tenet that self-cultivation requires the whole person to remain cognitively present.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educational designs must preserve learning as an embodied, effortful, self-transformative practice; learners must resist cognitive offloading

Constraints¶

  • The article frames this as a Daoist-derived normative stance, not a tested intervention; it acknowledges reliance on AI risks degrading critical thinking

Target Learners¶

  • educators and students in AI-mediated educational settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • preserving the integrity of the learning process and learners' cognitive agency
