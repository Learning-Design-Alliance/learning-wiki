# Use guided notes rather than distributed slide copies to increase attention during lectures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q4` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends guided notes as a more effective handout technique than distributing presentation copies. "Guided notes are a hierarchical outline of the presentation with key information intentionally left blank," which learners fill in as the lecture progresses, increasing attention and helping them discover relationships in the material. Because the notes are mostly complete, learners can take notes effectively and direct attention at the presenter instead of the notebook.
