# Improve the teaching quality of online content delivery and use video strategies such as interactive elements and cognitive-load management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends addressing the overall performance decline by improving how content is delivered online, whether live or recorded, because recorded-lesson quality affects examination performance and low-achieving students benefit most from quality improvements. It specifically advises "strategies for the effective use of videos, such as using interactive elements, managing cognitive overload, and considering technical requirements." This is a forward-looking design recommendation grounded in the study's findings of widespread score decline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online curriculum delivery in which recorded lectures form a substantial part of the formal curriculum
#### Constraints
- The article notes it would not be appropriate to simply convert recorded lectures to a live format, given students' high preference for recorded lectures and low probability of active participation in live online lectures

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in pre-clerkship phases

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge acquisition in integrated system-based pre-clerkship medical courses
