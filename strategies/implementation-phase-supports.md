# Implementation-phase supports: staff engagement, communication and action plans, thought partnership, and self-assessment rubrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During implementation, R15CC co-designs all-staff meetings and helps leaders connect authentically with staff; provides communication and action plan templates capturing "the intended audience, key timelines, necessary steps, roles and responsibilities, and strategic messages"; acts as thought partner and critical friend to directors and managers through regular one-on-one and group meetings; establishes divisionwide implementation working agreements (strong culture of collaboration, robust open communication, trusting and effective teams); and monitors progress with a self-assessment rubric during regular check-ins and meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Planned and structured feedback mechanisms so constant feedback flows into the system as new ideas and implementation realities emerge
- A clear transition plan and strategic communications, since transitions begin with an ending that can create strong staff reactions
#### Constraints
- Change efforts can stumble due to lack of implementation planning with clear progress milestones, overfocus on objectives rather than process, or failure to monitor progress

### Target Learners
- SEA division directors, managers, and full staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing leaders' capacity to manage change and sustain continuous improvement
