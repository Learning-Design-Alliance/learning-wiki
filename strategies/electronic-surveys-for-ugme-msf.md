# Adopt electronic surveys for undergraduate MSF to reduce rater burden and ease data management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Among the lessons learned, the authors recommend moving UGME multisource feedback from paper to electronic surveys. Paper surveys were used in the pilot for logistical reasons and students criticized the paper format as unsuited to supervisors' reality. The article states that 'Adopting electronic surveys would enhance the accuracy and efficiency of the assessment process while reducing the burden on raters and facilitating data management,' and notes its pilot data supports investing resources into electronic survey design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources invested in designing electronic surveys for UGME MSF
#### Constraints
- The pilot itself used paper surveys for logistical reasons, so the recommendation rests on student comments rather than a tested comparison

### Target Learners
- clerkship medical students and their raters (supervisors, peers, hospital staff, patients)

### Target Learning Goals
- collecting multisource feedback efficiently to support learning
