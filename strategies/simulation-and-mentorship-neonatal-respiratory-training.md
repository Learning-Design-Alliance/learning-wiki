# Provide simulation-based training approaches and collaborative mentorship opportunities for complex neonatal respiratory disease management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future developments include "provision of simulation-based training approaches and collaborative mentorship opportunities in caring for complex respiratory diseases affecting newborns." Simulation training is suggested to have a role in NICUs with fewer cases per year of rare pathologies such as CDH and pulmonary malformations, and clinical exchange programmes may enable trainees to gain exposure to specialised services such as neonatal ECMO.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation-based training curricula incorporating current European consensus guidance
- Mentoring programmes or exchange programmes enabling exposure to specialised services
#### Constraints
- Centralisation of care may further reduce the number of level 3 NICUs caring for complex pathologies

### Target Learners
- Neonatal sub-specialty trainees
- Junior consultant staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Management of complex neonatal respiratory diseases
- Procedural skills such as ECMO referral
