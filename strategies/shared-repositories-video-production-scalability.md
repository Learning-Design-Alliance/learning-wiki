# Distribute real-patient video production costs through interschool partnerships and shared digital repositories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A scalability strategy for resource-intensive real-patient video production: the authors recommend collaborative efforts among institutions. They write: "We suggest collaborative efforts, such as interschool partnerships and the development of shared digital repositories, to distribute production costs and enhance scalability." This addresses the stated limitation that producing high-quality videos demands substantial time and collaboration among educators, clinicians, and audiovisual specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration among medical educators, health care professionals, and audiovisual production specialists
#### Constraints
- Scalability of real-patient videos poses a significant challenge for widespread adoption

### Target Learners
- medical students across multiple institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- authentic community health care learning experiences
