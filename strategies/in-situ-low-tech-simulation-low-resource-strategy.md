# Use transportable low-technology simulation kits and in-situ simulation with locally available supplies to keep training costs low and setup simple

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PRONTO team prioritized running simulations at the participating facilities using only normally available resources, with a transportable low-technology kit including PartoPants and NeoNatalie. The authors report that "Using our transportable low-technology simulation kit and prioritizing in situ simulation with locally available supplies keep costs low and set-up simple." This made simulation training feasible in resource-limited hospital settings where expensive commercial simulators are out of reach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers must be flexible on some aspects of the training while maintaining fidelity to the curriculum as a whole
#### Constraints
- Simulation setups sometimes had to be delayed or rapidly taken down for actual patient care

### Target Learners
- obstetric and neonatal care providers in resource-limited hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- emergency response and team training for childbirth complications
