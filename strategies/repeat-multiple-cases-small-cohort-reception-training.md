# Incorporate multiple representative clinical cases repeatedly and group students into small cohorts to scale reception training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address the null findings in clinical judgement and medical record writing, the article suggests that "multiple representative clinical cases should be repeatedly incorporated into the training curriculum to improve students' clinical thinking abilities." To overcome the heavy teaching load of the one-to-one SP-and-judge model, it further proposes "grouping students into small cohorts for clinical reception training" to promote large-scale delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to multiple representative clinical case scripts and trained SPs; sufficient instructor capacity or a small-cohort grouping structure
#### Constraints
- The article states the limited number of cases in the training program hinders enhancement of clinical diagnostic thinking due to insufficient frequency

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in prosthodontic clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical judgement and diagnostic thinking
- medical record writing proficiency
