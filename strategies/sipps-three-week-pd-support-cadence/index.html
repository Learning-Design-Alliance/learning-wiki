Provide implementation professional development and support approximately every three weeks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the qualifying research, "teachers met with program implementation staff for professional development and support approximately every three weeks." The program also offers varied professional learning formats — in-person, virtual, self-paced independent learning, asynchronous online learning, and small-group — plus resources supporting teacher growth as they teach the lessons.
