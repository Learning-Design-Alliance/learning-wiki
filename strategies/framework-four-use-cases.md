# Use the framework in four ways: develop new tasks, characterize existing tasks, gain an overview of design principles, and support school-level teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that beyond task development, "the framework can be used to characterize already existing experimental tasks," letting instructors "reflect on and evaluate the quality and diversity of the tasks in their own lab courses" and giving researchers a tool to systematically review lab concepts. It also serves as an overview of design principles and adjustment screws, and could benefit physics teachers in high schools and colleges.
