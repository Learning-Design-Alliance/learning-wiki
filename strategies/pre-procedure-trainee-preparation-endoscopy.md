# Pre-procedure trainee preparation: assess skill level, set objectives, and agree on scope takeover

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before an endoscopy involving a trainee, the article recommends that "additional time should be invested before the procedure to assess the trainee's level of endoscopic skills, determine current learning objectives, and agree when the trainer would take over the scope." This preparation makes hands-on opportunities count, especially when training time is disrupted, and pairs with in-procedure mentoring that should "optimize cognitive load and give appropriate high-quality feedback."
