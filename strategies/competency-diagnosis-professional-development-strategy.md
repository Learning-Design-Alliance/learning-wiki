# Use validated competency questionnaires to diagnose educator strengths and design targeted professional development

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions use validated competency assessment tools to identify strengths and areas for improvement among teaching staff. It states that such tools 'can enable these institutions to design customized training and professional development programs that can address educators' individual and collective needs.' The strategy also fosters a culture of self-management and reflection among educators.
