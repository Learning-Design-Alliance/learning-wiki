# Targeted curricular updates and hands-on workshops to address OPMD knowledge gaps during internship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends specific curricular updates for dental training: integrating current cohort data on malignant transformation rates, updating standard teaching on leukoplakia risk, and implementing "a brief half-day workshop incorporating didactic review, OSCE stations, and case-based discussions" during internship. Training on adjunctive diagnostic aids and biopsy skills is also advised to bridge the theory-practice gap.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricular integration of current epidemiological data on transformation rates
- Workshop format combining didactic review, OSCE stations, and case-based discussions
#### Constraints
- The workshop model is drawn from a cited intervention; the article does not report testing it in this study

### Target Learners
- Dental interns
- Dental undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- Early detection of OPMDs and oral cancer
- Accurate knowledge of malignant transformation rates
- Screening and referral competencies
