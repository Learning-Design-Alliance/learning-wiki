# Run in-situ simulation sessions in five stages from pre-briefing to immediate feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The validated scenario organizes each in-situ simulation session into an explicit stage sequence: "1) pre-briefing (which is this current phase), 2) briefing, 3) scenario development, 4) scenario closure and 5) immediate feedback (right after closing) by the local team and, late feedback." In the pre-briefing the facilitator explains the suspension of disbelief, checks scenario assembly and participant roles; the briefing presents the clinical case and tasks; facilitators then manage distractors and close with feedback, optionally delayed via video review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained facilitator to conduct the pre-briefing and briefing, manage printed monitor parameters, trigger complications, and define the closing time of the scenario
#### Constraints
- The published text describes this structure for one pediatric emergency sedation scenario with two planned complications

### Target Learners
- multidisciplinary emergency teams including nursing, physicians, nursing technicians and physiotherapists

### Target Learning Goals
- sedation management, complication response, communication with family members, and team coordination under time pressure
