# Empower specific soft-skill values at each phase of the mathematics lesson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes matching named soft-skill values to lesson phases: "When opening the lesson, was empowered values of soft skills such as: religious, confident, polite, and honest." During independent work the values are self-confidence, curiosity, responsibility, independence, and hard work; during group discussion, teamwork, caring, respect, and logic; during class presentation of group results, respect, confidence, responsibility, and politeness. The strategy aims to make students comfortable and diligent participants in problem solving.
