Research and design priority: align school organisational structures with desired dimensions of teacher social capital¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's forward-looking recommendation is that research should examine how the organisational structure of schools relates to the bonding, bridging and linking dimensions of teacher social capital, in order to promote the desired outcomes it identified. It states: "More research is needed on the relationship between these dimensions and the organisational structure of schools, to promote the desired outcomes of teacher social capital identified in this review."
