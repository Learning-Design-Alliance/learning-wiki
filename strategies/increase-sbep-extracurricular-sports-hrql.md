# Increase school-based and extracurricular sports activity participation to improve children's health-related quality of life

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend expanding school-based exercise practices and adding extracurricular sports activities as a route to better health-related quality of life and obesity prevention. They write that "Increasing participation in SBEP and extracurricular sports activities on schools can be a vehicle for hope in terms of HRQL" and that supporting children with SBEP while decreasing sedentary time has sustainable potential for exercise self-efficacy. They also suggest extracurricular possibilities be developed because physical education teachers carry many responsibilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools able to host sports activities beyond the compulsory physical education course
- Community and after-school program involvement for extracurricular sports opportunities
#### Constraints
- Barriers to efficient physical education courses were detected due to the many responsibilities given to physical education teachers

### Target Learners
- Primary school children
- School-age girls

### Target Learning Goals
- Health-related quality of life
- Physical activity habits
- Obesity prevention
