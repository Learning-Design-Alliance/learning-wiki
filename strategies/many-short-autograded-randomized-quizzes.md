# Replace few long homework assignments with many short auto-graded randomized quizzes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Once automatic grading removed the grading burden, the author switched from about 10 homework assignments per semester to about 40 quizzes, each doable in under 10 minutes by a student who understands the material, closing 3-4 days after the class discussion, with generally 3 attempts and the chance to ask questions first. The author reports "the students appear to have a better retention of the material" and that in his impression this helped learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Auto-graded randomized quizzes (e.g., via Moodle and moodlequizR) so grading effort does not scale with quiz count
#### Constraints
- The retention and learning benefits are the author's impression, not a tested result

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of recently taught course material
