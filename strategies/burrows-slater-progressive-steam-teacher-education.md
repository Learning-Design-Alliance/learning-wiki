# Progressive teacher education trajectory from single discipline to constant STEAM education

## Description
Burrows and Slater (2015) propose a progressive education in which teachers move from a Level Zero (single discipline) to a Level Four (Constant STEAM education). The trajectory involves first adding quantitative reasoning or mathematics to concepts taught in the target discipline, then integrating two or more distinct science disciplines, then adding engineering projects that materialize concepts from scientific disciplines, and finally moving to pure STEAM education.
