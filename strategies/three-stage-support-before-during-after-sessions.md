# Organize faculty design support into three stages: before, during, and after sessions

## Description
The article proposes that support for university teachers' design work be structured across three temporal stages. Based on the findings about teachers' design practices, it states that "three stages of support can be devised for before, during, and after sessions." This gives institutions a concrete way to time their support offerings around actual teaching sessions rather than a single upfront training event.
