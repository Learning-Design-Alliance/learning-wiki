# Combine school-based nutrition programs with maternal education initiatives to support academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a combined approach in which schools and communities improve children's diets while educational initiatives raise maternal education. It states that "School-based nutrition programs and community initiatives can play a crucial role in improving overall dietary habits" and that educational initiatives for mothers "can contribute to creating a supportive learning environment at home". It frames this as a holistic approach combining education, nutrition and community engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to nutritious meals for students through school-based programs and community initiatives
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is based on a cross-sectional study, so associations do not establish that the interventions cause improved academic performance

### Target Learners
- First-cycle primary school students (grades 1–4) in low-income settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving academic performance through improved nutrition and parental education
