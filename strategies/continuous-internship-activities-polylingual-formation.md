# Include students in diverse educational, creative, scientific-research activities and continuous pedagogical internship to form polylingual professional competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that "The decisive condition for the formation of the future teacher's professional competence on the basis of polylingual approach is the inclusion of students in a variety of educational, creative, scientific and research activities in university pedagogical process, in the course of continuous pedagogical internship." This follows from the finding that the existing system of psycho-pedagogical training leads to predominantly low and insufficient formation levels.
