# Enhance nursing master’s students’ academic achievement by strengthening teachers’ ethical training, teacher-student relationships, and growth mindset support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a multi-pronged approach for graduate nursing education: improve teachers’ ethical literacy and behavior, build supportive teacher-student relationships, and cultivate students’ growth mindset. The authors conclude that enhancing achievement "involves not only improving teachers’ ethical training but also strengthening teacher-student relationship and supporting students in developing a growth mindset". Concrete suggestions include training and reward/evaluation systems for teachers’ ethical behavior, tailored support and feedback mechanisms, appropriately controlled task complexity, and cognitive function training such as short courses on neuroplasticity and metacognitive intervention.

## Design Implications
