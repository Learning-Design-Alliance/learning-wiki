# Recommend a deaf story drama training model grounded in deaf ontological, axiological, and epistemological commitments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on observed differences between the non-deaf training model and the deaf-led team's delivery, the article recommends a deaf story drama training model for deaf preschoolers built on the commitments of deaf-led instructors and teams. The study "recommends a deaf story drama training model for deaf preschoolers according to deaf ontological, axiological, and epistemological commitments of deaf-led instructors and teams".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Deaf-led instructors and teams whose commitments inform pedagogy
- Training that reflects ocularcentric, multimodal, and interactive approaches
#### Constraints
- Derived from one exploratory study in a single preschool classroom

### Target Learning Goals
