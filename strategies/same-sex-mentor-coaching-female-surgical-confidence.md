# Support female residents' surgical confidence through same-sex mentoring with additional cases, regular coaching, and raising awareness of gender career bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends concrete residency-program measures to raise female residents' surgical self-confidence: additional operative experience with a same-sex mentor, regular coaching lessons, and awareness-raising about gender career bias in the local training environment. It argues these address a "confidence gap" in which female physicians underestimate their abilities. The recommendations follow from the study's finding that gender independently lowers confidence ratings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ways of increasing practical/surgical confidence of female OBGYN residents must be prioritized within residency programs, alongside removal of structural obstacles
