# Retest the inverted classroom case-study format with more senior PharmD students (years 4-6) rather than third-year students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Although third-year students were not satisfied with the inverted classroom case-study experience, the authors argue the approach is worth retesting with more advanced students. They reason that third-year students may have significant difficulty with an application-based activity when their skill level sits lower on Bloom's taxonomy, and that later years bring more knowledge, clinical experience, and communication skills through traineeships. The approach is also valued for fostering self-learning skills needed for lifelong learning in pharmacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students with sufficient prior knowledge and clinical experience to handle an application-based case-study activity
#### Constraints
- Findings cannot be generalized to other year cohorts or other specialties, per the authors

### Target Learners
- fourth- to sixth-year PharmD students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical-thinking, communication, and self-learning skills in pharmacotherapy
