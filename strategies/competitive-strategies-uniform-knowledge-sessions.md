# Design competitive learning strategies to distribute knowledge sessions uniformly over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that competition-based activities be arranged so that study effort is spread across the course rather than concentrated before exams. It states that "competitive strategies can be designed to get a more uniform distribution of knowledge sessions over time and, therefore, promoting learners' deep understanding and long-term retention." In the study, experimental students had to study on a daily basis either to propose challenges or to answer those proposed by classmates across four semester contests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recurring contests across the semester so students must engage repeatedly
- A tool that lets students both propose and answer challenges, with teacher supervision
#### Constraints
- The strategy was very time consuming for the teacher in this implementation

### Target Learners
- higher-education students, including medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- deep understanding and long-term retention of course content
