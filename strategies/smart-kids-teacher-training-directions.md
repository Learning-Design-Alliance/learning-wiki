# Teacher training directions for Smart Kids technology in pre-service elementary teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that introduction of Smart Kids technology requires training elementary school teachers in using e-textbooks or EEGR during lessons, organizing pupils' ICT-based after-hours work, and supervising independent work. It lists main directions for building competence of a future elementary school teacher, including establishment of the learning environment, use of a multimedia complex, the teacher's briefcase of EEGR, forms of implementation, lesson planning with E-textbooks, the teacher's virtual office, and monitoring and assessment of pupils' attainment and of e-textbooks. These directions are to be included in curriculums of higher educational establishments training future elementary school teachers.
