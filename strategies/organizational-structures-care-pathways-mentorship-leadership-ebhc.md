# Build supportive organizational structures — clear career pathways, mentorship programs, and leadership development — to enhance nurses' EBHC competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
In its conclusions, the review recommends that "healthcare organizations and educators may consider them when designing educational interventions and building supportive structures, such as clear career pathways, mentorship programs, and leadership development, to enhance nurses' EBHC competence in practice". This responds to findings that background factors such as educational level and clinical setting shape competence and that workplace‐related factors, including leadership and organizational culture, are likely influential.
