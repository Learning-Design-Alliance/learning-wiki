# Multi-level integrative tasks at partially search, productive and creative levels for consolidating knowledge and developing professional skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article offers subject-integrative methodological tasks of three difficulty levels for future history teachers. As printed, "we hav e been offered subject -integrative methodological tasks of three difficulty levels to consolidate the theoretical knowledge and to develop of professional and pedagogical skills of the future history teachers." The partially search level stimulates initial vocational skills by completing missing fragments of proposed e-lesson plans; the productive level is based on modeling e-lesson plans, e-lecture notes for teachers and e-lessons for students; the creative level stimulates students to set objectives and solve professional problems independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Determination of initial conditions (class, lesson topic, lesson type) and content selection "were carried out by students independently" at the creative level.
#### Constraints
- The tasks were developed for History and English disciplines in future teachers' training, drawing on developments by U.A. Yesnazarova (2014) and N. P. Yachina (2015).

### Target Learners
- full-time and part-time university students of History and the English language faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidation of theoretical knowledge
- development of professional and pedagogical skills
- independent solution of professional problems
