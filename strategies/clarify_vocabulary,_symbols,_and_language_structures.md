# Clarify Vocabulary, Symbols, and Language Structures

## Description
Constructing meaning from words, symbols, and numbers using different representations ensures that learners understand the language of the content. This includes providing definitions, examples, and visual aids to clarify vocabulary. It also involves breaking down complex sentences and mathematical notation into simpler, more accessible formats. This strategy helps make learning materials more accessible to a wider range of learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful analysis of the language used in learning materials. Instructors need to be prepared to provide additional support and clarification as needed.
#### Constraints
- May require more preparation time and resources. Some learners may find too much simplification patronizing. It's essential to strike a balance between clarity and challenge.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly English language learners, learners with learning disabilities, or those new to a subject; suitable for K–12, higher education, and adult learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance comprehension, reduce cognitive load, support vocabulary development, promote accessible learning

### Affordances
- Supports comprehension by ensuring that learners understand the language of the content, reduces cognitive load by breaking down complex information, supports vocabulary development by providing definitions and examples, and promotes accessible learning by catering to diverse linguistic needs.

### Personalization
- Provide vocabulary lists and glossaries tailored to learners' needs. Use visual aids and graphic organizers to clarify complex concepts. Offer translations and simplified explanations for English language learners.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learner engagement and participation in activities. Assess learner understanding of vocabulary and concepts through quizzes or discussions. Track improvements in comprehension and application of knowledge.

## Impact
- Research supports that clear and accessible language can enhance comprehension and reduce cognitive load for all learners. Studies show that vocabulary instruction can improve reading comprehension and academic achievement.
