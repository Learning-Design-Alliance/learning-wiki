# Pair algorithm work with historical vignettes on the origins of 'algorithm' and 'algebra' — a text, a video and debate recovering the work of Muḥammad Al-Jwarizmī

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the course treats Nature of Science content alongside vector classical mechanics, the authors run a vignette-based activity around the concept of algorithms. The article prints: "una actividad de trabajo alrededor del concepto de algoritmos es la lectura de un texto, junto con la visualización de un video (BBC ideas, 2019) y posterior debate acerca de los orígenes de los términos algoritmo y álgebra." The activity recovers the work of Muḥammad Al-Jwarizmī, and the authors state that this focus de-centers the vignettes from Eurocentrism, valuing the contributions of the Abbasid caliphate, Arab and Persian cultures and the golden age of Islam.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A reading text on the origins of the terms algoritmo and álgebra
- The BBC ideas (2019) video and a subsequent debate
#### Constraints
- The activity is framed by the article's caution, following Lemke, against students leaving school believing science is a perfect path to absolute truths discovered by superhuman people; teachers are asked to emphasize the human side of science

### Target Learners
- first-year physics teacher trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- conceiving physics as part of culture and the sciences
- understanding the human, historical character of scientific knowledge
