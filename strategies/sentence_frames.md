# Sentence Frames

## Description
Use sentence frames to help structure respectful responses in debate or disagreement situations online. Provides examples of how digital citizens can present new ideas, agree with viewpoints, respectfully disagree, seek more information, or acknowledge new learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of respectful communication, ability to use online platforms, willingness to engage in thoughtful dialogue
#### Constraints
- May feel artificial at first, requires practice, depends on community norms and moderation
