# States and districts should treat identifying and developing effective principals as a priority given the central importance of school leadership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The fact sheet directs state and district attention to school leadership as a lever for improvement. It argues that because of "the central importance of school leadership," it is important to ask "what makes for an effective principal," positioning leader development as a system-level strategy for raising student achievement. The fact sheet itself is framed as guidance on how states and districts can develop effective school leaders.
