Support CBL implementation with instructor training, gradual student introduction, and monitoring of student stress and feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the discussion of factors affecting CBL efficacy, the article recommends that "instructors receive training in CBL teaching before its implementation to ensure their professionalism and to increase efficacy of the case instruction". It further suggests instructors monitor students' feelings and obtain timely feedback during implementation, citing evidence that student stress escalated on switching to CBL and that gradually introducing students to CBL supports autonomous motivation and achievement. Case quality and facilitation of discussion are treated as critical levers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors trained in CBL teaching before implementation
  • Attention to case quality, since case quality is critical for eliciting student interest and promoting substantive case discussions

Constraints¶

  • Fictional cases must be believable and indistinguishable from real cases to be effective
  • Student stress may escalate when students change to a CBL environment from traditional teaching

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate psychology students
  • instructors implementing CBL

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective case-based discussion and knowledge application

Claims¶

  • Students reported positive evaluations of CBL, citing aroused learning interest, enhanced self-study, improved communication and cooperation, and improved problem-solving [+W]
  • Case-based learning yields significantly higher psychology exam scores than lecture-based teaching, with a pooled Hedges' g of 0.68 under a random effects model [+M]
  • After removing four studies identified as heterogeneity sources via Galbraith plot, the pooled CBL effect remains significant (g = 0.65) [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement CBL successfully by ensuring pre-class preparation, authentic progressive cases, dual-competence facilitators, adequate time, and process-plus-outcome assessment
  • Gradual introduction of board-game-based learning to support inexperienced teachers
  • Frustration and Stress Prevention/Reduction Techniques
