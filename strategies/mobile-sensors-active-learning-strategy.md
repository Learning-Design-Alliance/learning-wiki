## Description
The article recommends integrating mobile-device sensors not merely as new instruments but as a lever for pedagogical change. The authors state the transformations "apunta a diseñar y promover estrategias de enseñanza y de aprendizaje activo" and seek to change the role of teaching, giving the student a fundamental role instead of being a passive receiver of knowledge. They also frame sensor work as fostering teamwork in a context of inclusion and equality, while cautioning that technology alone cannot transform education.
