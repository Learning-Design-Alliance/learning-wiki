# Incorporate social media and emerging interactive technologies into online EBM modules to enhance connectedness and satisfaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend supplementing the online EBM module with interactive technologies — chat rooms, blogs, video and multimedia clips, screencasts, RSS feeds and social media such as LinkedIn, Facebook and Twitter — to improve connectedness between students and tutors. They argue that "Incorporating social media in this module thus has the potential to enhance learning and both learner and tutor satisfaction with learning", citing evidence that social media in formal settings promotes informal and formal learning communities.
