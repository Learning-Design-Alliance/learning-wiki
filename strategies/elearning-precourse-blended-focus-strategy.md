# Integrate e-learning as pre-course preparation within blended-learning FOCUS training curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends including e-learning formats in certified ultrasound training curricula as pre-course preparation within a blended-learning design split into pre-course, course, and post-course phases. It concludes that "such formats in ultrasound training can effectively complement face-to-face workshops and should be included in certified training curricula." The authors also propose developing clear certification and accreditation guidelines for e-learning platforms through specialist societies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning content aligned with course learning objectives and specialist-society recommendations (e.g., WINFOCUS reference planes)
#### Constraints
- E-learning cannot fully replace in-person practical training and cannot entirely compensate for clinical experience

### Target Learners
- medical students
- physicians in ultrasound training

### Target Learning Goals
- foundational theoretical FOCUS competencies before in-person practical training
