Strengthen flipped pharmacy classrooms with interactive retrieval activities and patient-based case scenarios¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that instructors using flipped classrooms employ more interactive activities that strengthen retrieval processes, and use patient-based scenarios to connect coursework to pharmacy practice. They state instructors "should employ more interactive activities to strengthen the retrieval learning process" and "need to find more suitable patient cases and guide students to engage in discussion and problem-solving." Examples cited include crossword puzzles, Kahoot games, and electrocardiogram-interpretation exercises from included studies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors prepare pre-class materials and design in-class interactive activities such as quizzes, case discussions, and games

Constraints¶

  • The article notes students report greater time commitments than traditional methods, especially pre-class preparation; some students spent more than 3 hours preparing

Target Learners¶

  • pharmacy students in higher education programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved examination performance and course grades
  • practical and problem-solving skills for pharmacy practice
  • long-term retention through retrieval practice

Claims¶

  • Flipped classroom significantly improves pharmacy students' performance compared with traditional lecture-based approaches [+M]
  • Flipped classroom design features — start-of-class quizzes, patient case discussion, and short pre-class videos — are each associated with advantages over traditional teaching [+M]
  • Flipped classroom advantage holds across research designs but is much smaller in quasi-experiments than historical-control studies [+M]
  • Flipped classroom advantages appear across outcome measures, with the largest for exam scores and smaller for course grades [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Scenario Based Training
  • Strategically employ AI tools, including chatbots, within active learning methodologies such as problem-based learning, flipped classrooms, and virtual labs
