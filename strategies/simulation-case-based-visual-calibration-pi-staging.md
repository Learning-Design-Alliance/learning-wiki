# Use simulation-based training, case-based learning and repeated visual calibration exercises to strengthen pressure injury staging competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends moving beyond traditional didactic education for pressure injury staging. It states that "Structured simulation, case‐based learning and repeated visual calibration exercises may strengthen clinical judgement and promote more consistent application of staging criteria", citing a systematic review finding simulation-based PI education more effective than traditional didactic education. The strategy targets diagnostic precision and consistent application of staging criteria rather than knowledge alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires interactive and practical training approaches rather than traditional educational approaches alone, as the article states these may not be sufficient
#### Constraints
- The article calls for evaluation of these approaches; it reports no direct test of them in this study

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses caring for patients at risk of pressure injuries

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate classification and staging of pressure injuries using the international staging system
