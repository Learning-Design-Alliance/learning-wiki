## Description
The article details how the IDR methodology is made applicable to the wider community 'through a workshop model, which has been run and iteratively refined at five major international conferences, involving over 200 participants'. The workshop model operationalises the participatory pattern-design process for practitioners beyond the original project team. It was refined iteratively across conference runs.
