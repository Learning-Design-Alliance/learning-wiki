# Normalizing Struggle

## Description
Normalizing struggle involves creating a classroom culture where challenges and failures are viewed as opportunities for growth. This approach helps students accept difficulties as a natural part of the learning process, encouraging them to persevere and develop resilience. Teachers share personal problem-solving experiences and highlight how students worked through challenges to promote independence and productivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive classroom environment, opportunities for students to encounter and work through challenges, and strategies to help students reflect on their problem-solving processes.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset for both students and teachers to view struggles positively. Students may initially resist embracing challenges or feel discouraged by failures.

### Personalization
- Teachers can adjust the complexity of problems and challenges based on students' skill levels and learning needs. They can also provide personalized coaching and support to help students navigate difficulties.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe students' responses to challenges, their willingness to try new strategies, and their ability to articulate their problem-solving processes. Measure students' resilience and perseverance in the face of difficulties.

## Impact
- Research suggests that normalizing struggle leads to increased resilience, perseverance, and a growth mindset among students. It also fosters a more supportive and collaborative classroom environment.
