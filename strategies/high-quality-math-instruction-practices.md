# High-quality math instruction: conceptual emphasis, productive struggle, collaboration, multiple solution paths, culturally relevant tasks, and timely intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that teachers, supported by well-designed curricular materials, promote mathematical growth by emphasizing conceptual understanding rather than algorithmic problem-solving; giving students opportunities to grapple with challenging content within reach; creating well-structured collaborative learning; offering tasks with multiple means of solving that mirror real-world problems; providing culturally relevant and empowering learning experiences leveraging student assets; and including timely evidence-based interventions for knowledge gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Positive classroom conditions of safety, support, and belonging are preconditions that must be coupled with high-quality instruction for students to achieve their potential
#### Constraints
- The report did not review research on effective math teaching in relation to curriculum, standards, or licensure

### Target Learners
- K–12 mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding in mathematics
- Mathematical problem-solving and engagement
- Closing racial and socioeconomic achievement gaps
