# Teachers and students create their own 360-degree VR content using free apps like Google Street View

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teachers with moderate technology skills create VR experiences for their students using basic apps available on mobile devices. Applications like Google Street View, "a free website/app designed for 3d and 360o viewing and picture taking," let teachers or their students create their own 360-degree images, which can then be viewed on a phone or computer or inserted into a virtual reality headset such as Google Cardboard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Most available websites and applications for 360-degree viewing have a built-in capability to connect with VR devices; the created image must be activated on the mobile device.
