# Combine ChatGPT with human teachers in dialogic teaching rather than replacing human teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a hybrid arrangement in which ChatGPT and human teachers are used together in dialogic teaching so that each covers the other's weakness. It reports that "ChatGPT could help students establish a knowledge foundation and stimulate cognitive activity" but that its strengths in "promoting students' knowledge application and creativity were insignificant." The synergistic use of both is presented as providing more comprehensive learning support and contributing to teaching quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to LLM-based dialogue support such as ChatGPT for question-answering during instruction
- Availability of human teachers to handle knowledge application, innovation and students' emotional states
#### Constraints
- The article states ChatGPT "cannot fully excel in fulfilling teaching tasks in the dialogue teaching in information related courses"
- Findings come from one specialized science/engineering course with college students, which the authors say "might threaten the generalization of the findings"
- The transfer test used only one question, and the authors call for more objective measurements of learning performance

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in science and engineering courses

### Target Learning Goals
- basic knowledge concept acquisition
- knowledge transfer and application in dialogic instruction
