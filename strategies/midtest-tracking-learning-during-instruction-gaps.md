# Administer an additional midtest version of a concept diagnostic to track long-term learning during gaps in instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors introduced a CUE midtest — a subset of 12 post-test questions chosen to conform to course goals — administered between the standard pretest and post-test. Because OSU's restructured curriculum leaves roughly 10 months without E&M-related courses, the two midtests reveal how much students retain or lose outside formal instruction, which pre/post testing alone cannot show: "We showed how a CUE midtest can be used to track long-term, inter-instructional student learning and to assess student learning beyond the duration of the course."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A course structure with a gap in instruction in the tested content, so that an intermediate administration is informative
- A midtest subset of questions selected to conform to the course goals at the intermediate point
#### Constraints
- The intense pace of the Paradigms courses makes collecting survey data throughout a course difficult
- Testing circumstances differed between midtests (mid-quarter vs right after summer break), which the authors say may combine with forgetting to lower second-midtest scores

### Target Learners
- junior-level (upper-division) physics undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of electrostatics and magnetostatics
- long-term retention of upper-division E&M problem-solving strategies

## Claims

- [Students retained over 80% of learned E&M content after ~10 months without instruction, losing 9.7±2.4% (17.3±4.2% relative) between CUE midtests](../claims/cue-midtest-knowledge-decay-ten-months.md) [+M]
- [First CUE midtest scores correlated strongly with final grades in PH422 (r = 0.53, p < 0.001, N = 85)](../claims/cue-midtest-grades-correlation-ph422.md) [+M]
- [CUE post-test scores showed no statistically significant relationship with final grades in PH431 (r = 0.18, p > 0.05, N = 36)](../claims/cue-posttest-grades-no-significant-relationship-ph431.md) [~W]
