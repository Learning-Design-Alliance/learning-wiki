# Include speed reading exercises in English textbooks and have teachers prepare their own exercises when unavailable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the Ministry of Education in Jordan include exercises of speed reading on reading comprehension in English language textbooks, echoing Rababa'h (1991). Teachers should also prepare in advance exercises including speed reading strategies if they are unavailable in any class. This embeds skimming and scanning practice into regular curriculum materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Textbook exercises on speed reading strategies, plus teacher-prepared exercises as a fallback where textbook exercises are unavailable.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is addressed to the Jordanian Ministry of Education and EFL context of the study.

### Target Learners
- secondary students in Jordanian schools learning English

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- skimming and scanning skills
