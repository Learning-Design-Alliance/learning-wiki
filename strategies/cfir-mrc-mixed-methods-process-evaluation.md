# Conduct a mixed-methods process evaluation alongside the cluster RCT using the CFIR and MRC frameworks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The trial is paired with a mixed-methods process evaluation guided by the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research, the UK Medical Research Council process evaluation framework, and the Expert Recommendations for Implementing Change. Its aims follow the MRC guidance to "explain discrepancies between expected and observed outcomes, to understand how context influences outcomes, and to provide insights to aid implementation." Data collection includes purposive-sampled semi-structured individual and group interviews, participant surveys, LMS participation data, and project coordinator documentation, organised by CFIR domain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three local Project Coordinators, one per city and experienced residential care nurses, to standardise implementation and data collection activities
#### Constraints
- Process evaluation findings are contextual and interpretive, not tests of effectiveness

### Target Learners
- RACF staff, project coordinators and senior staff in residential aged care

### Target Learning Goals
- Understand how the training intervention is implemented, experienced and effective across care homes
