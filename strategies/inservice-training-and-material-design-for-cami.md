# Provide teachers in-service training on CAS-based CAMI and have them design worksheets and dynamic materials before instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Teachers can be provided trainings about CAMI and CASs such as Mathematica so they can benefit from ICTs by achieving the technology integration at high levels in their lessons." It further stresses that worksheets and dynamic materials have great importance in achieving high-level CAMI, so teachers or researchers must design them finely before beginning CAMI activities. In the study itself, three mathematics teachers received researcher-led in-service training before carrying out the implementations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Worksheets and dynamic materials must be designed in advance; teachers need prior in-service training on CAS use
#### Constraints
- Technology integration is described as a complicated, dynamic, slow and long-termed process

### Target Learners
- secondary education mathematics teachers and their 10th-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement in quadratic function graphs through technology-integrated instruction
