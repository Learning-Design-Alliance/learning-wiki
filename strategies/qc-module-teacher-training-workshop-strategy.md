# Support teacher adoption through compensated training workshops and focusing on one course stream initially

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article identifies time and upskilling as the main obstacle to new course utilization for teachers and students, and recommends "attending a fully compensated teacher training workshop through the AAPT" and "to initially focus on one stream of the course rather than the entire module". It also suggests the interdisciplinary nature of quantum computing makes the module excellent for collaborative teaching, and notes senior students with prior QC knowledge proved useful in explaining key concepts and helping with class activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher upskilling to learn the course content, achievable via the AAPT workshop or other means
#### Constraints
- Time is the main obstacle for both teachers and students; students would have preferred a longer study time, and the article suggests a summer course as a possibility

### Target Learners
- high school teachers adopting the quantum computing module

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher readiness to teach quantum computing content
