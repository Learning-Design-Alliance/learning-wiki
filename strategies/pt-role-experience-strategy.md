# Use peer tutoring role experience in university classrooms to build pre-service teachers' awareness of CL implementation factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific implementable activity: pre-service teachers act as tutors and tutees in structured peer tutoring sessions within their university subjects. Tutors "prepared some materials for the subject to be learnt by teaching them to groups of 5 peers", asking questions, giving examples, and monitoring problem solving. Focus groups afterwards surface implementation considerations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured peer tutoring sessions embedded in university coursework
- Focus group reflection sessions after the experience
#### Constraints
- Requires coordination between subjects and availability of participating students

### Target Learners
- Pre-service primary education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding of cooperative learning implementation factors
- Awareness of tutor and tutee roles
