# Revise layperson e-learning material using user feedback: pre-use orientation, plain short text, balanced quiz answers, summary pages, wider-age characters, and varied health examples

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A revision strategy in which focus group and questionnaire feedback is translated into concrete material changes. "Feedback from the focus group interviews included adding information before using the e-learning material to explicitly demonstrate the learning objectives and the time required to complete them, providing additional expressions for easy understanding without technical terms and shortening text length." Further changes equalized true/false answer ratios, added summary pages, added an older character, and diversified health-issue examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Qualitative semi-structured focus group interviews and a usability questionnaire with intended users to generate the feedback
#### Constraints
- Feedback came from a small purposive sample of health-conscious laypersons rather than a distribution-averaged user population

### Target Learners
- Health-conscious laypersons across a wide age range

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved readability and orientation when learning EBM fundamentals online

## Claims

- [Focus group interviews found the e-learning material's effectiveness was influenced by its story and characters and by consistency with interviewees' daily interests](../claims/ebm-elearning-effectiveness-story-characters-daily-interests.md) [+W]
- [Participants found the true/false quizzes relatively easy but the detailed descriptions difficult because they contained many technical words, and sentences were too long](../claims/ebm-elearning-quizzes-easy-explanations-technical-words-difficult.md) [+W]
- [In usability testing, 76% of 25 laypersons were interested or very interested in the EBM e-learning material, 88% found it easy to follow, and 72% found it useful in daily life](../claims/ebm-elearning-usability-questionnaire-interest-easy-useful.md) [+W]
- [Content analysis of 12 existing EBM materials for laypersons yielded 217 descriptions integrated into 56 major elements, from which 18 elements were selected for the e-learning material](../claims/ebm-layperson-material-18-elements-from-56.md) [+W]
