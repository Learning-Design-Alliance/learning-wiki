Use culturally responsive practices to make preschool learning relevant for children from all backgrounds¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The panel directs teachers to reflect and value the cultural, racial, and linguistic backgrounds of children, teachers, and community. The guide advises: "To make learning more relevant, engaging, and interesting for all children, the panel suggests using culturally responsive practices." Concrete moves include learning about children's families and communities, ensuring materials reflect classroom diversity, seeking books with authentic positive characters, inviting families to share traditions, displaying real family and community pictures, and placing children in diverse heterogeneous groups.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Knowledge of the children, their families, and their communities used to adapt teaching, materials, and classroom setup
  • Incorporating practices into current materials may need collaboration with a curriculum or preschool director

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Children from a wide variety of cultural, racial, and linguistic backgrounds, including dual language learners and children from low-income families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Positive self-identity
  • meaningful learning about various cultures
  • engagement and relevance of instruction
