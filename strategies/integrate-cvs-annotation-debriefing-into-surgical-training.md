# Integrate structured feedback and video debriefing with CVS annotation into preclinical and clinical surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that postoperative structured feedback and video debriefing with CVS annotation be integrated into surgical training, concluding that it "could thus be integrated into preclinical and clinical training". The strategy pairs each trainee-performed laparoscopic cholecystectomy with a tutor-led joint video review annotating Go/No-Go zones and the critical view of safety. In the trial this followed each of four ex-vivo porcine LCs performed in teams of two trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained tutor experienced in performing and teaching laparoscopic cholecystectomy
- Video capture of each procedure and an annotation platform such as LabelMe
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from medical students without prior MIS experience in an ex-vivo preclinical setting; the authors state the concept needs testing in a clinical setting to verify the results

### Target Learners
- novice surgical trainees and medical students in their clinical years

### Target Learning Goals
- safe laparoscopic cholecystectomy performance
- critical view of safety achievement
- reduction of technical complications
