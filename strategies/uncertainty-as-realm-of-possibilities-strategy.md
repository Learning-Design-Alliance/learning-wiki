# Redefining uncertainty as a realm of possibilities, illustrated with two single-photon interference measurement styles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy redefines the negatively connoted word uncertainty as a "realm of possibilities" and grounds it in concrete experiments. Two measurement schemes are used: a "wait and sweep" style in which a telescope waits on one region then changes orientation to build a histogram whose spread determines Δx, and a "wait" style in which a wide-angle CCD camera accumulates single-photon blips across the whole screen. The author reports this "converted theoretical subtleties and syntactic nuances into concrete physical occurrences" and comforted perplexed students.
