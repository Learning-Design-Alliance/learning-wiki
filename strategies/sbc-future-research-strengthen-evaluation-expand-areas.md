# Strengthen educational evaluation of SBC proposals and expand research in AI, disabilities, robotics, and STEM areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that researchers strengthen the evaluation of SBC proposals from quantitative, qualitative, or mixed approaches, since most studies were technological proposals analyzed under a technical lens. It further recommends examining areas such as AI, the impact of programming on the skills of minorities, and STEM education, and investigating people with disabilities and robotics more extensively due to their small number of educational studies. It also calls for adequately evaluated educational methodologies from other engineering fields such as robotics, control systems, IoT, or image processing.

## Design Implications
