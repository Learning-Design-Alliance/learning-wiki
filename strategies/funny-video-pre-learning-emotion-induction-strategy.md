# Use short funny videos or brief activities before learning to prepare students' positive pre-learning emotions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers evoke students' positive emotions before learning with easy and economic means: "the appropriate use of short videos can promote students' positive emotions and thus improve their learning performance". It also advises that "it is wise to execute some activities such as singing and playing games to prepare students for positive emotions before learning", and that teachers should attend to students' pre-learning emotions because they affect learning results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Short (2–3 min) funny video clips played before the learning task begins
#### Constraints
- The funny video used was unrelated to the learning content; the authors note videos compatible with the course topic have boosted retention in prior work, and whether other positive-emotion video types produce the same results remains to be studied

### Target Learners
- primary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving transfer of learning material through positive pre-learning emotions

## Claims

- [Watching funny videos before study makes elementary students feel significantly more positive (happier, more content) and less frustrated and bored before learning than watching neutral videos.](../claims/funny-videos-induce-positive-pre-learning-emotions.md) [+W]
- [No significant differences were found between the funny-video and control groups in learning motivation or learning satisfaction.](../claims/no-motivation-satisfaction-difference-funny-videos.md) [~W]
- [Positive pre-learning emotions induced by funny videos improved knowledge transfer but not knowledge retention in elementary students learning from a video lecture.](../claims/positive-pre-learning-emotion-improves-transfer-not-retention.md) [+W]
- [After learning, the experimental group's happiness decreased and frustration and boredom increased significantly, while the control group's emotions did not change significantly.](../claims/post-learning-emotion-declines-after-funny-video-induction.md) [~W]
