# Practice Time

## Description
Give students time to practice what they just learned in a low-stakes environment, without grading or evaluation. Provide feedback from near neighbors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate writing prompts and exercises. Time for practice in class. A classroom environment that values creativity and self-expression.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle with open-ended exercises. Requires careful planning to ensure that exercises are relevant and engaging. May be time-consuming.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for developing writing skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote experimentation and risk-taking, enhance understanding of writing techniques, develop fluency in using new techniques, reduce anxiety and pressure

### Affordances
- Creates a safe space for students to experiment with new ideas. Encourages them to take risks and stretch their writing muscles. Promotes a playful and engaging learning environment.

### Personalization
- Practice activities can be tailored to student interests and writing goals. Students can be given the freedom to choose which techniques to experiment with. Scaffolding can be provided for struggling learners.
