# Augmented Reality (AR) for Military Training

## Description
AR creates training environments required for soldiers to train more often, save travel costs, and reduce the need for special equipment. Augmented reality technology is used to add virtual objects to the physical world, such as virtual vehicles and enemies. This method creates systems that eliminate the need to travel to remote locations and helps soldiers train without danger.
