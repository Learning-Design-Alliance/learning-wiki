# Organizing Information and Taking Notes

## Description
This strategy involves effectively structuring and recording information to enhance understanding and retention. It includes techniques such as using graphic organizers (Venn diagrams, flow charts, concept maps, matrices), sorting tasks, and structured note-taking systems like Cornell Notes. Explicit instruction and gradual skill-building are essential for independent use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction in note-taking and organizational strategies, provision of graphic organizers, opportunities for practice and feedback
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit instruction and practice, may be challenging for learners with limited prior experience in formal education
