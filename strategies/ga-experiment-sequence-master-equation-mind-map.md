# Sequence basic field experiments with each Maxwell equation, using the GA master equation as an overview mind map

## Description
For introducing new students to electric and magnetic fields, the article recommends that "a series of basic experiments on the ﬁeld properties are best undertaken and the relevant Maxwell equation introduced in sequence." At each step, GA provides an overview "showing how each equation can be extracted from the master equation, Eq. (4), thus providing students with an overview, or a mind map within which new results can be placed." The authors add this also benefits past students familiar with the experiments, who gain a unified mathematical framework.
