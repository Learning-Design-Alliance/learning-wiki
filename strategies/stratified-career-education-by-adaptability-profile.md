# Stratified career development education and psychological interventions tailored to career adapt-abilities profiles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because latent profile analysis revealed pronounced heterogeneity, with over 40% of students in the low adapt-abilities group and fewer than 10% in the high group, the article recommends stratified interventions rather than uniform support. The article states that "for students in the negative profile group, the focus should be on cultivating their career adapt-abilities and reconstructing self-cognition; for those in the positive profile group, efforts should concentrate on consolidating psychological strengths and enhancing their specific human–machine collaboration skills". University English programs are advised to place greater emphasis on cultivating career adapt-abilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
