Teach cognitive, metacognitive, and self-regulation reading strategies within a gradual responsibility transfer model¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that reading instruction convey strategy use through structured handover of responsibility: "To improve students' reading, the use of cognitive and meta -cognitive strategies, and self -regulation strat egies should be conveyed within the frame of gradual responsibility transfer model". In the study this meant teacher modeling of strategies, supervised student practice with teacher intervention when needed, and finally independent strategy use by students. The article also recommends supporting self-regulation based reading skills in the classroom and training teachers through in-service workshops.

Claims¶

  • Self-regulation based strategic reading education produces significantly higher reading comprehension post-test scores than the current program in 5th grade students [+W]
  • Self-regulation based reading education significantly raises 5th graders' reading motivation relative to the current program [+W]
  • Self-regulation based reading education significantly increases 5th graders' use of reading strategies relative to the current program [+W]
  • Self-regulation based reading education significantly improves 5th graders' motivation and learning strategies (self-regulation skills) relative to the current program [+W]
