## Description
The article's procedure requires the advertiser to set acceptable and unacceptable efficiency limits and the level of risk of incorrect decisions before sample evidence is examined. From these inputs, "it is possible to set up a series of figures" for the sequential analysis. This makes the method implementable for evaluating television programs economically.
