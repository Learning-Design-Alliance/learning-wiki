Systematize polytechnic material in physics using classroom examples from different areas of scientific and technical progress¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors developed guidelines showing how to systematize polytechnic material in physics through examples from different areas, considered within regular lessons. As printed: "We have developed guidelines, which show the way to systematize polytechnic material in physics in the form of examples from different areas and encouraged to consider these examples in the classroom, which does not require spending additional time." Examples include heat engines and thermal power plants, capillary phenomena and ore flotation, and electrolysis and welding during electrodynamics study.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Guidelines for systematizing polytechnic material and teacher discussion of related professions during lessons

Constraints¶

  • Designed to fit existing lessons without requiring additional time

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school physics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • connecting physics laws to modern production and technology

Claims¶

  • Polytechnic knowledge and skills in experimental classes increased on average by 16% relative to control classes [+M]
  • Experimental classes showed a markedly better distribution of polytechnic knowledge levels, with 85% at average or high level versus 52% in control classes [+M]
  • Students in experimental classes reached higher levels of measuring, computing, graphical and experimental polytechnic skills than control-class students [+M]
