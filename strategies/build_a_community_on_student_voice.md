# Build a Community on Student Voice

## Description
Building a community on student voice involves creating a classroom environment where students feel safe and empowered to share their thoughts, ideas, and needs. This includes daily check-ins, reflection sheets, sentence stems, and student-led discussions. By amplifying student voices, educators can better understand their needs and build their confidence to speak up and advocate for their learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to create a safe and supportive classroom culture, provide opportunities for student sharing and discussion, and offer support tools for students who struggle to articulate their thoughts.
#### Constraints
- Requires a significant time investment and careful facilitation to ensure that all voices are heard and respected.
