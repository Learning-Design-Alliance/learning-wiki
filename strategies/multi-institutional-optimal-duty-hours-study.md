# Fund a multi-institutional study to identify optimal duty hours balancing curriculum objectives, continuity of care, and team training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review endorses the ACS task force recommendation to fund a multi-institutional study identifying optimal duty hours. It states: "We support the recommendation of the ACS task force [1], in their response to the 2008 IOM report, to fund a multi-institutional study to identify optimal duty hours that achieve curriculum objectives, maintain continuity of care, and address team training efforts." The study should also examine long-term effects of duty hours on readiness to enter surgical practice.
