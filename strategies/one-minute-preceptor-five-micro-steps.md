# One-minute preceptor: five micro steps for short, focused bedside teaching encounters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A just-in-time clinical teaching recipe for limited time, based on "five micro steps: get a commitment, probe for supporting evidence, teach general rules, reinforce what was done right, and correct mistakes". The learner commits to a diagnosis or plan, verbalizes and justifies their reasoning, receives targeted teaching on identified gaps, and gets reinforcement and error correction, focusing attention on a specific objective and reducing overload risk.
