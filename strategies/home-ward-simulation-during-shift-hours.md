# Deliver simulation on nurses' home ward during regular shift hours rather than at a remote simulation centre

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend conducting simulation on the nurses' own ward and keeping sessions compact enough to fit regular shift hours. This brought the simulation to the nurses, created a comfortable environment near their actual patients, avoided accruing additional paid time, and was logistically convenient and seemingly cost-effective. They state that "conducting the simulation on the nurses' home ward should be considered rather than in a remote simulation centre."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two conference rooms or equivalent space on the ward and facilitators able to run sessions during shifts
#### Constraints
- Participating in the HFS during regular shift hours proved difficult for some nurses; eight who answered the pretest never progressed further

### Target Learners
- bedside registered nurses on hospital general wards

### Target Learning Goals
- early recognition of and response to sepsis
