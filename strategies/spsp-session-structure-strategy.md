# Structured SP simulation session recipe: pre-briefing, 15-minute interview with checklist, and three-part debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a repeatable session structure for SP-based MSE training: a pre-briefing on the scenario, distribution of an MSE category checklist to each student, a 15-minute simulation in which one student interviews the SP as the nurse while others observe, and a 10-minute structured debriefing. Debriefing followed "the three-part debrief paradigm, which entails defusing, identifying, and developing", with SP feedback to student observers and nurses about communication and evaluation methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized patients trained in scenario portrayal, verbal interaction techniques and debriefing strategies
- A trained facilitator available during simulation and debriefing
- MSE checklists mapping simulation content to assessment categories
#### Constraints
- Session dose in the study was three sessions per week over four weeks; the article does not evaluate shorter formats

### Target Learners
- Nursing students learning clinical interview and assessment skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Performing mental status evaluation, probing patient experiences accurately, and giving and receiving feedback
