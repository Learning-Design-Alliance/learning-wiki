# Add a trauma domain to the Domain Management Model in future virtual patient training and research with traumatized refugee patients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on the Domain Management Model, a systematic approach to organizing primary care clinical information consistent with the biopsychosocial approach, the authors recommend adding a trauma domain in their forthcoming RCT. The rationale is that PCPs "maybe will not pay attention to severe traumatic life events in the assessment and follow up of the patient with trauma", given participants' increased awareness of the continuing effect of past trauma after VP training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured domain framework (the DMM) for organizing clinical information in primary care encounters with complex patients
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is stated for the authors' forthcoming RCT with traumatized refugee patients, not as a tested result of this pilot

### Target Learners
- primary care practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- attention to severe traumatic life events in assessment and follow-up of traumatized patients
