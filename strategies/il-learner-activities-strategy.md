# Structured IL learner activities building self-regulated learning, exam preparation, and peer networks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The IL course pursues explicit objectives through concrete activities: students "journal about personal choices made regarding study strategies and effectiveness of them; debrief actions taken before exams and their potential impact on the final score," develop time management, predict exam questions, practice with quizzes and mock exams, organize small group discussions, and explore class and cultural transition issues. These activities target textbook use, critical thinking, self-regulated learning, peer networks, exam preparation, comprehension of target-course material, and cultural adjustment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An IL instructor skilled in pedagogy, peer cooperative learning, classroom management, curriculum development, and classroom assessment
- Preference for instructors with experience working with culturally diverse students
#### Constraints
- Activities are tied to the concurrently enrolled companion course's content and schedule

### Target Learners
- First-generation, low-income, and historically-underrepresented first-year tertiary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning and study-strategy selection
- Exam preparation and error-pattern analysis
- Peer collaboration and cultural transition
