# Use the Preview and Predict strategy: students preview title and pictures, predict from prior knowledge, then read and verify predictions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The procedure as printed: students begin by reading the title and looking at pictures, decide whether the text is story or informational, then use prior knowledge and preview information to predict what will happen or what they will learn. Students then read, verify their predictions, and monitor and change them as needed. The article reports it is useful for narrative and expository text as well as Mathematical concepts, and for second language learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher has to direct the activities of the students
- Can be combined with other methods like discussion
#### Constraints
- Most effective when students have knowledge of the text and the concepts

### Target Learners
- junior secondary school students
- second language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- constructing meaning
- reading comprehension
- predicting and monitoring understanding
