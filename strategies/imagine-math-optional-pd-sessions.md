# Use optional professional development sessions for administrators and educators to support Imagine Math implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Imagine Learning offers "various optional virtual or on-site professional development and training opportunities for administrators and educators." Administrator sessions focus on leading implementation tailored to each school's model and goals; educator sessions cover getting started, using the program's reports and features, and providing targeted support for individual students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Imagine Learning's Customer Success team and the optional virtual or on-site professional development sessions.
#### Constraints
- The professional development and training opportunities are optional rather than required.

### Target Learners
- Administrators and educators implementing Imagine Math

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective program implementation, use of reports, and targeted support for individual students
