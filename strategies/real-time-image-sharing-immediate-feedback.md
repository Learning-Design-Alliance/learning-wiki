# Use real-time networked image sharing to give instructors immediate visibility of student microscope operations and enable immediate feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy equips every student microscope with networked digital imaging so the instructor can simultaneously view all live student images and detect operational errors without waiting for students to ask. The article states that "instructors can simultaneously observe all students' microscope images on the teacher-terminal computer," enabling "immediate detection of operational errors, even in the absence of student feedback," which addresses delayed feedback and beginners' inability to self-identify technical flaws.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 5G LAN connecting microscopes, software terminals, and the instructor's computer; students' devices with the system app installed
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one small biology course; scalability of real-time transmission to larger user numbers is untested per the authors

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates learning microscopy

### Target Learning Goals
- timely correction of operational errors
- acquisition of microscopy technique
