# Visual overlays (QCD colour flow, electroweak flow, coupling-strength visualisation) as scaffolds for judging diagram importance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
FeynCraft offers overlays that add information to a drawn diagram: a possible QCD colour flow, an illustrative electroweak colour flow, and a visualisation of vertex coupling strengths. The authors state that the coupling-strength option "allows students to roughly gauge how important different diagrams are for a particular process by comparing the size of the coupling constants involved, which is a skill often taught in introductory particle physics courses".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Overlays are toggled on drawn diagrams; colour flow applies to processes with strongly interacting particles and electroweak flow to processes with electroweak particles
#### Constraints
- The coupling-strength display is illustrative: the total degree measure counts coupling constants and does not account for the different strengths of different interactions or other vertex-rule factors

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate students in QCD and electroweak course segments

### Target Learning Goals
- relating Feynman diagrams to colour flow
- estimating the relative importance of diagrams via coupling strengths
