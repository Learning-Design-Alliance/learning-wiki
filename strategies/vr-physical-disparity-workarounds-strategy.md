# Incorporate VR-physical disparity workarounds into future VR tools for surgical rehearsal and education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that lessons learned from designing the SurgiSim VR microscope be carried into future VR surgical tools: "We recommend incorporating these learnings into the development of future VR tools for surgical rehearsal and education." The workarounds address disparities between VR and physical environments, including vibration feedback on encountering microscope handles, thumbstick-click entry into microscopic view, weight-based locking of the microscope head, restricted zoom, and an OR layout map in the microscopic view. Each iterative modification was aimed at the communication, form-of-reality, and presence design principles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Iterative end-user feedback from surgeons during development to identify VR-physical disparities needing workarounds
#### Constraints
- The maneuverability problem was only partially solved; all participants said they would not use the platform in its current state for microscope training, and future versions will add physical constraints mirroring the OR
