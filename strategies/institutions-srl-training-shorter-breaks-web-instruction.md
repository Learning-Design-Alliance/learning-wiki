# Universities should provide SRL training and consider reducing the length of breaks between lectures, using web-based instruction to give flexible attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the institutional level, the article recommends "training interventions for students to foster SRL strategies", noting face-to-face trainings have been demonstrated effective but that "a web-based training approach ... was demonstrated to be more efficient" for reaching large numbers. It further suggests universities "should consider reducing the length of breaks between lectures so that there is less need for students to actively fill these gaps", potentially by increasing web-based instruction that lets students choose their optimal attendance time. This targets the finding that breaks use independently predicts satisfaction.
