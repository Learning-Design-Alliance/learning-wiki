Establish data-driven curriculum updates triggered by national accident statistics.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that educational boards establish a feedback loop where national accident statistics (e.g., a rise in specific injury types like falls or electrocution) directly trigger updates in VET OHS curricula. Policy interventions must also be developed to identify and support high-risk groups to prevent industrial accidents effectively.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

  • Educational boards establish a feedback loop between national accident statistics and VET OHS curricula

  • curriculum relevance to current sectoral risks

Claims¶

  • Up to 67% of injuries in vocational settings occur within the initial weeks of employment (first-month vulnerability). [+W]
  • Generic safety curricula fail to address specific lethal risks such as falls in construction or transport-related injuries in healthcare. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt sector-specific OHS modules instead of generic courses.
