# Embed new curricular content by replacing an existing clerkship activity rather than adding instructional time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To fit human trafficking education into an already full third-year curriculum, the authors replaced an existing pediatric clerkship simulation case that did not feature complex socio-economic topics with the M-SIGHT module. The article advises other institutions similarly: "It is advisable to consider how this content could complement or replace existing curriculum, since additional instructional time is rarely available." This kept faculty time, staff time, SP salary, and tools within the resources allocated to the previous activity, and no additional funding was needed for development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examination of the institution's curriculum to determine the most appropriate home for the content
- An existing activity whose replacement does not reduce essential learning
#### Constraints
- The article notes time is a highly-valued commodity and resources are important when proposing new curricular content

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- human trafficking recognition and intervention integrated into core clerkship training
