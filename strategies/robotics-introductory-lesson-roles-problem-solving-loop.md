# 120-minute collaborative robotics lesson with role assignment and a problem-solving loop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article details a 120-minute Sec 1 Design and Technology lesson introducing robotics. Students are seated in designated groups, assign a Leader, Manager, Engineer and Scribe, and work through skills of "identifying the problem, comparing & contrasting ideas, discussing solutions, deciding on the best solution", guided by a problem-solving loop and Bloom's Taxonomy, with experiential tool-handling practice and reflective closure.
