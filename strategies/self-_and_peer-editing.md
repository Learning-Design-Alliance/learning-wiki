# Self- and Peer-Editing

## Description
Self- and peer-editing are collaborative processes where learners evaluate their own writing and provide feedback to peers. The self-edit step encourages students to evaluate specific features of their writing, increasing self-awareness of writing conventions. The peer-edit step helps build a learning community in which peers work collaboratively and heightens the awareness of various print and grammatical conventions for the peer editor and the author.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a structured checklist or rubric and a supportive classroom environment for collaborative work. Effective modeling of the process is essential.
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit instruction and modeling to be effective. Some learners may be uncomfortable providing or receiving feedback.
