# Use validated nurse manager competency instruments as a basis for competency-based training programs, assessment tools, and recruitment strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that results on validated competency instruments shape the design of "competency-based academic and training programs for nurse managers as well as for the development of competency assessment tools, performance appraisal tools, and staff recruitment strategies." The GRADE A instruments are recommended for clinical practice use, and defining cut-off points and repeated-interval comparisons are suggested to track competence over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use instruments recommended by the review (GRADE A tools) and apply COSMIN guidelines when designing new instruments
#### Constraints
- The authors state the identified instruments should be used cautiously in clinical practice because of variations in evidence about some measurement properties

### Target Learners
- Nurse managers at first-, middle-, and top-level positions
- Nursing personnel

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing leadership and management skills
- Assessing and developing nurse manager competencies
