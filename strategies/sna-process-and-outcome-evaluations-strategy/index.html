Use SNA in both process and outcome evaluations of programs¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends applying social network analysis in two evaluation types. It states that the brief 'illustrates how to use this method in process and outcome evaluations,' meaning SNA can serve both to describe how a program operates (process evaluation) and to assess the results programs produce (outcome evaluation). Evaluators can therefore choose the evaluation type that fits their question and apply SNA concepts accordingly.
