## Description
The article states that "The structured format developed for this study could inform faculty marking proformas or assessor training to promote consistency, particularly in SAQ feedback." It further notes that even where AI tools are not adopted, structured rubric-aligned feedback with explicit justification of marks and clear guidance for improvement enhances feedback transparency. This strategy is implementable independently of AI adoption.

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty-designed rubric assessing defined domains
- Explicit scoring and explanatory bullet points in the feedback output

### Target Learning Goals
