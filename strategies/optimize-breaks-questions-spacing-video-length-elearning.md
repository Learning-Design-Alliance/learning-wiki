# Investigate optimal integration of periodic breaks, interspersed test questions, spaced learning intervals, and video length in video-based e-learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend continued investigation of how to augment learning and retention in video-based e-learning, specifically weighing "the need to integrate periodic breaks, interspersed test questions, and spaced learning intervals, in addition to determining optimal video length." They also call for e-learning platforms to support robust analytics collecting privacy-protected, deidentified data to inform future research on optimal learning strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning platforms capable of delivering videos with interspersed breaks or test questions
#### Constraints
- The study's own 23-minute video and single testing interval may have limited detection of between-group effects, per the authors' limitations

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical trainees (pediatric residents)

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge acquisition and long-term retention of clinical content such as mechanical ventilation concepts
