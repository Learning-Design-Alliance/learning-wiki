# Emphasize sleep hygiene as a regulating factor for academic performance in emergency remote teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend attention to sleep hygiene for students in remote, screen-mediated teaching. They state "the importance of sleep hygiene, which is a regulating factor for academic performance and psychological fulfillment considering the new forms of teaching implemented during the COVID-19 pandemic." The recommendation rests on their observational finding that prolonged screen use was associated with poor sleep quality and decreased academic performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applies in teaching modalities that require prolonged student screen exposure, such as emergency remote teaching
#### Constraints
- Based on a single-institution, self-report, cross-sectional survey; the association reported is not a tested causal effect

### Target Learners
- medical students
- undergraduate students in remote teaching

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining academic performance
- memory consolidation and attention during remote classes
