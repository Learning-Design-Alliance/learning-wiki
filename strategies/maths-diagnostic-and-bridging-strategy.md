# Use entry diagnostic assessment, online bridging resources and embedded contextual mathematical support to close the mathematics transition gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A coordinated set of interventions for the mathematical transition: "a mandatory diagnostic assessment at the entry to establish each student's mathematical background", accompanied by freely accessible online bridging resources covering the highest-priority topics, differentiated workshops and tutorials for varying preparedness, and embedding contextual mathematical support within first-year physics modules rather than treating mathematics as standalone remediation.
