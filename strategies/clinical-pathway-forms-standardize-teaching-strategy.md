# Use clinical pathway forms as a standardized teaching scaffold to avoid inconsistencies between teachers' varied teaching programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because teachers' teaching ability differs, the article recommends establishing "a standardized and programmed disease diagnosis and treatment program around which clinical teaching work can be carried out." The clinical pathway form for each teaching disease specifies the content and operational highlights interns must master from admission to discharge, standardizing the teaching process and helping students form a systematic clinical knowledge structure while shifting teachers from teaching medical knowledge to teaching learning methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical pathway forms must be prepared for each teaching disease and explained on the first day of the internship
- Teachers must integrate the method with existing medical resources and conditions
#### Constraints
- Diseases with complex courses, numerous variations and uncertainties cannot be treated with clinical pathway therapy

### Target Learners
- Medical interns in gynecological clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized clinical thinking
- Mastery of disease diagnosis and treatment
