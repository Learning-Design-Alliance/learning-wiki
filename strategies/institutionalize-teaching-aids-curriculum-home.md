# Institutionalize teaching aids across curriculum, classrooms and home learning for young children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the article recommends a multi-level adoption strategy: "The federal government, ministry of education and c urriculum planners should enforce the use of teaching aids in the elementary school level curriculum", teachers should use aids to motivate interest, school management should make their use compulsory, and parents should expose children to toys and teaching aids at home, including "use of computer, software to help recall what has been taught". Parents are also advised to feed children high-nutrient foods supporting brain development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment from government, curriculum planners, school management, teachers and parents
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single quasi-experimental study in Delta State, Nigeria, with one week of teaching per group

### Target Learners
- elementary school children aged 5-6 years

### Target Learning Goals
- interest in learning
- academic achievement
- brain development
- recall of taught content

## Claims
