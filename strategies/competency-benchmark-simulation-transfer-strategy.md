# Establish competency benchmarks and objective assessment tools to translate simulation skills into operating-room practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that future work should set competency benchmarks for surgical training and evaluation tools and find ways to transfer simulation-acquired skills to real operations. They state: "Future research endeavors will consider to establish a competency benchmark for these training and evaluation tools, exploring ways to seamlessly translate the skills acquired through simulation training into real-world applications within the operating room." This strategy responds to the current absence of standardized, universally applicable assessment tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized, objective, and universally applicable tools for measuring trainees' surgical competence progress, which the authors state are currently absent
#### Constraints
- The authors note the absence of high-quality tools to assess the effectiveness and reliability of existing simulators complicates comprehensive retrospective analyses

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- young surgeons in training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of technical surgical skills
- transfer of simulation-trained skills to real operating-room performance
