# Warning and Consequence

## Description
This technique involves giving high school students a clear warning that talking during the lesson will result in a consequence, such as taking time off their break. The teacher writes instructions in bold letters on the board, stating that students need to be sitting in silence by the time the title is written, and anyone still talking will be kept behind for five minutes. This approach provides adequate warning and sets clear expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear communication of expectations, consistent enforcement of consequences, willingness to follow through on warnings.
#### Constraints
- May be perceived as punitive, requires consistent follow-through, may not be effective for all students.
