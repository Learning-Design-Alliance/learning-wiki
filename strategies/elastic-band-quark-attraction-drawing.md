# Elastic-band resisted drawing to embody the increasing attractive force between separated quarks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students draw with a pencil while an elastic band wound around the pencil and their other hand provides increasing resistance, physically enacting the strong interaction. The article explains that "The increase in attractive force between two quarks as they are separated is explored by drawing against the restrictive properties of an elastic band wound around the pencil and other hand." Students use different colours and multiple pens to express the increasing attraction, and discussion introduces colour as the charge mediating the strong interaction.
