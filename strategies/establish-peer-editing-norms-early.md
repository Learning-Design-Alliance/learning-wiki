# Establish clear peer editing norms and structured guidelines early in online collaborative writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators proactively address discomfort around overriding peer contributions by "encouraging open communication about editing expectations, discussing how to handle conflicting edits, and ensuring that feedback norms are established early on". It suggests creating structured guidelines for peer editing, similar to other forms of peer feedback, to contribute to a more productive and collaborative writing environment. This strategy targets the emotional barriers to peer editing identified in the preliminary study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must dedicate time early in the collaboration to discuss editing expectations and feedback norms
#### Constraints
- The article states that without support from tools like SITU, students may face challenges related to overriding peer contributions; the strategy complements rather than replaces awareness tools

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in higher education courses using online collaborative writing

### Target Learning Goals
- willingness to edit peers' written products
- constructive peer feedback
