# Implement dual-focused nursing curricula combining communication skills training with alexithymia reduction and self-efficacy enhancement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends pairing nurse-patient communication skills instruction with psychological supports that reduce alexithymia and build self-efficacy. It states: "The study provides empirical evidence for implementing dual-focused educational strategies targeting alexithymia reduction and self-efficacy enhancement in nursing curricula." Concrete elements the authors name include role-playing-based communication courses, mindfulness techniques, mentorship models, and school-hospital collaboration such as bedside teaching before internships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between hospitals and schools in teaching
- Communication skills training using methods such as role-playing and simulation
- Psychological supports such as mindfulness training and mentorship to address alexithymia
#### Constraints
- Intervention efficacy is not yet tested; the article states further research is necessary to validate the associations and find appropriate interventions
- Evidence base is a cross-sectional mediation study

### Target Learners
- Nursing interns in clinical practicum
- Nursing students preparing for internship

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical communication competence
- Emotional awareness and expression
- Self-efficacy
