# Integrate artificial intelligence into digital teaching with careful planning, quality-assured data, and explicit teaching of critical handling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using artificial intelligence in digital pharmacy teaching for support tasks such as "die Strukturierung von Lehrveranstaltungen, die Erstellung von klinischen Patientenfällen, die Formulierung von Arbeitsaufträgen oder die Erarbeitung von Prüfungsfragen", provided quality and a critical approach are guaranteed. It further recommends teaching critical handling by having AI answer clinical questions which students then critically question and interpret. The strategy is framed by the article's warning that AI use in teaching risks hindering the development of critical thinking and independent decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Correct formulation of the question (prompting) and consideration of all relevant parameters; the data available to the algorithms must be transparent, of high quality and current, with curated datasets used to train the language models according to their purpose
#### Constraints
- Für den Einsatz künstlicher Intelligenz in der Lehre besteht die Gefahr, dass insbesondere die Entwicklung von kritischem Denken und die Fähigkeit, eigene Entscheidungen zu treffen, behindert werden

### Target Learners
- pharmacy students and university lecturers in pharmacy education

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking and independent decision-making when using AI-supported tools in pharmaceutical tasks
