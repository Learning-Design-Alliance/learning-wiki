# Phonological Awareness Development through Rhyming

## Description
Using rhyming as a literacy tool to develop phonological awareness. During a read-aloud, students identify rhyming words in the text to enhance their understanding of sound patterns in language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Age-appropriate texts with clear rhyming patterns, interactive read-aloud sessions.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for students with significant phonological processing difficulties or limited exposure to rhyming patterns.

### Target Learning Goals

- Develop phonological awareness, improve literacy skills, enhance understanding of sound patterns in language

### Affordances
- Enhances literacy skills through active engagement with rhyming words, supports phonological awareness, and improves reading comprehension.

### Personalization
- Adaptable for different literacy levels by selecting texts with varying degrees of complexity in rhyming patterns.

- Assess student's ability to identify rhyming words in texts, monitor their participation and engagement during read-aloud sessions, and observe their use of rhyming in writing and speaking.

- Research supports the use of phonological awareness activities, such as identifying rhyming words, to improve reading skills and literacy development in young learners. (Source: National Reading Panel)
