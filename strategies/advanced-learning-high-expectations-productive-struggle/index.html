High expectations with advanced learning opportunities and productive struggle to empower Black students' genius¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Under the Empowering Genius element, the brief recommends that the Center "Establish high expectations" by supporting "curricula and advanced learning opportunities (e.g., Gifted and Talented Education, Dual Enrollment) while embracing productive struggle." This strategy responds to community voices asking that children be free to pursue academics and build the stairs to success without prejudice. It pairs with interest surveys, career days, tutoring, mentoring, SAT/ACT prep, and multiple pathways including internships and job placement.
