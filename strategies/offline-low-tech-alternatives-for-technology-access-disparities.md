# Address technology access disparities with offline or low-tech alternatives when implementing gamified learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address technological access disparities, the review recommends that "Educators can provide alternative resources or training for students who may lack access to advanced technology", including offering "offline versions of gamified activities or utilizing low-tech solutions" for students without reliable internet access or modern devices. This responds to reviewed challenges including internet connectivity problems and device limitations such as small screens and restricted input.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of students' technology access and connectivity before selecting delivery modes
#### Constraints
- Internet connectivity and device limitations can restrict the type of content that can be effectively delivered

### Target Learners
- EFL learners with limited or unreliable technology access

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable access to gamified language learning activities
