# Deploy roving (itinerant) teachers who teach in other schools for a period without being transferred, to equalize quality and workload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The roving teacher policy periodically transfers teaching assignments: "Itinerant teachers are teachers who are given the task of teaching and/or being involved in developing the quality of education in other schools outside of the school where they serve for a certain period of time without being transferred from their original school". Stated objectives include equalizing learning quality, teacher professional quality, and workload, and lightening the load of teachers in remote areas. Benefits include helping teachers under 24 hours/week meet their load and giving students a changing learning atmosphere.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful consolidation regarding teacher readiness
#### Constraints
- Geographically difficult due to the spread of schools in remote/island areas
- Requires stronger energy and mind because teachers must move around in teaching

### Target Learners
- Teachers and students in schools with teacher shortages across South Bangka Regency

### Target Learning Goals
- Equalizing learning quality, teacher competence development, and workload balance across schools
