# Curate and coordinate DS/ML physics-education materials and share teaching experiences through community forums

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A community-level strategy recommended by the white paper: "We recommend careful curation of materials for data science and machine learning in physics education and the open sharing of these materials", together with a forum for openly sharing teaching experiences and discovering who has developed and taught such courses. The authors argue the information is available but diffuse, so coordination would make sharing of knowledge and of projects utilizing HEP data much more efficient. The DSECOP program is cited as an existing example, with a Slack community, workshops, and stipended Fellows developing and testing materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A coordination mechanism (forum, repository, or community of practice) must exist to collect and expose information about courses, open data, and existing training materials.
#### Constraints
- The authors note universities must support educators, who are most often not experts in these technologies, in maintaining a working environment of tools and infrastructure.

### Target Learners
- physics educators and early-career researchers developing DS/ML curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- effective integration of data science and machine learning into physics courses
