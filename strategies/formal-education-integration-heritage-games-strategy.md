# Tighten the connection of spatial heritage games with formal teaching and study their effects on learning performance, motivation of weak students, and attitudes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors conclude that "a tighter connection with teaching in formal educational settings is needed" for spatial games for culture. They propose researching the effect of cultural games on students' learning performance, their potential to motivate "weak" or uninterested students, their effectiveness relative to other teaching methods, effects on players' attitude towards cultural heritage, and the influence of the context of use (formal, non-formal, informal) on outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluation studies connecting game interventions with formal teaching practice
#### Constraints
- The review found no evaluations studying the impact of the setting or the coordinator on game outcomes

### Target Learners
- school students
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- cultural knowledge acquisition
- attitude change towards cultural heritage
- motivation of weak or uninterested students
