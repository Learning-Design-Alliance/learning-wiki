# Emotion Cue Cards

## Description
Emotion cue cards are portable visual or auditory prompts designed to remind students of strategies for managing emotions, such as using a fidget or taking a mindfulness break. Students can carry them on a keyring or access them digitally, selecting strategies that work best for them. Consistent use of these cards aims to integrate self-regulation techniques into long-term memory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cards (physical or digital), time to introduce and practice strategies, support from educators and caregivers
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness may vary depending on the severity of emotional distress. Requires consistent use and reinforcement.
