# Advise teachers to use Twitter learning activities in blended courses integrating face-to-face and online discussion

## Description
Based on the study's results, the author recommends that teachers and actors in online education adopt Twitter to pursue learning activities within a blended course combining face-to-face learning and Twitter learning activities. The article states: "the researchers advise teachers and actors in online education to use Twitter to pursue learning activities in a blended course integrating both face-to-face learning and Twitter learning activities." Listed benefits include facilitating online discussion and social interaction, ease of use, and knowledge sharing.
