Train teachers of students with autism on evidence-based strategies applied in the natural environment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The author recommends training teachers of students with autism on applying effective strategies, "especially strategies applied in the context of the natural environment of these students", developing special-education curricula that include such training, and providing workshops and courses. The recommendation follows from the study's finding that trained-teacher application of natural communication strategies and social stories improved students' social skills.
