# Use Smartboards regularly and systematically in EFL classrooms to facilitate, encourage and involve learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their results, the authors recommend that Smartboards, "if used effectively in the classrooms", could facilitate learning and instruction in EFL classrooms, and that their "regular and systematic use by the teachers" could facilitate, encourage and involve learners in the language classroom. This goes beyond occasional use, which the authors note limited earlier studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be prepared, collaborative, malleable, productive, enthusiastic, open-minded and prepared to design teaching and learning procedures, per the article
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that motivation is a long and tedious process and that studying Smartboard effectiveness and its implications might provide a more comprehensive understanding

### Target Learners
- Adult EFL learners in university preparatory year programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining and increasing learner motivation in EFL classrooms
