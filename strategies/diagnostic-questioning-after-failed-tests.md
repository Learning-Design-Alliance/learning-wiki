# Guide iteration with diagnostic questioning after failed tests

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During build-and-test blocks the teacher responds to failed trials with questions about causes rather than fixes: from which side the submarine tilted, why the imbalance occurred, and how the system could achieve balance. The author states the teacher's role is "to give them feedback by questioning them regarding the reasons why the submarine failed to perform a certain task". Similar prompts address propulsion efficiency and balloon-inflation timing.
