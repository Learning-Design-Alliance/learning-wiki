## Description
The article proposes a qualitative classroom strategy inspired by quantitative model-selection: construct tables whose rows are phenomena and whose columns are historical models, marking with exclamation points where a model was surprised by a phenomenon. It suggests "Similar tables for your own perspective on phenomena and models covered in any given class... may be worth putting together for sharing with your students and (e.g. in electronic collaboration spaces) with the larger teaching community as well". Sharing such tables may clarify why idea-sets change, and student input is invited since expert and beginner perspectives differ.
