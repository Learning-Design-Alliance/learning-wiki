# Content-Related Signal Word

## Description
Using a content "word of the week" to signal that it’s time for silence. Examples include: integer, renaissance, or circuit. This integrates vocabulary review with classroom management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to introduce and consistently use the signal word. Students must understand the meaning of the word and its association with silence.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if students do not understand the meaning of the signal word. The novelty may wear off over time.
