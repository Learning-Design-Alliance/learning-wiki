# Use metacognitive activities and process evidence to help students monitor the purpose of their AI use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes educational practices that shift attention from how often students use AI to why they use it, helping students recognize avoidance-driven engagement. Suggested tools include "reflective prompts, process-based assessment, and AI-use logs" and encouraging students to generate initial ideas independently before consulting AI. The authors frame these as proposals warranting future empirical evaluation rather than validated interventions.
