Use real-time teacher reports of skill-level strengths and weaknesses to guide whole-class and small-group follow-up¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Wayang's teacher tools reify the tutor's assessment through a graphical interface delivering real-time reports of learning per knowledge unit, per problem and per class. "Teacher tools provide a precise assessment of students' strengths and weaknesses at specific math skills, in a language with which teachers are familiar", mapped to Common Core standards. Teachers use them to identify the hardest problems a class is struggling with and then invite students to discuss those problems in front of the class or work them in small groups with teacher support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Practice activities internally mapped to standards-based math skills with mastery levels computed and updated instantly
  • Teacher access to the reporting interface or regular progress emails

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • middle and high school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • remediation of weak math skills
  • class discussion of challenging problems

Claims¶

  • A small randomized controlled study found adaptive problem selection improved learning compared with random problem selection [+W]
  • Students using Wayang Outpost earned higher state standardized test (MCAS) scores at year end than matched control students [+W]
  • In the 2012 study, classes using Wayang showed significantly larger MAP gains in patterns and algebra than control classes (d = 0.38) [+W]
