# Forty-hour intersemester training course introducing exact-science teachers to the smartphone-app method

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Alongside the second application of the method, the author structured a course for teachers of the exact sciences to show them that the era of new tools has arrived. The 40-hour course was taught during the intersemester period in January 2018. Teachers downloaded the same apps used with students and followed the same explanations as the first student group; some brought their calculators because they did not believe results were obtained with more approximation digits, and began to interest themselves in updating their use of these applications.
