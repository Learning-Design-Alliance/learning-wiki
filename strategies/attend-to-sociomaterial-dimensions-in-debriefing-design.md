# Design debriefing with awareness of sociomaterial dimensions so intended learning outcomes are not blocked out

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that simulation pedagogy attend to how debriefing unfolds in practice, since "Awareness of how debriefing unfolds and is enacted in practice directs the attention towards sociomaterial dimensions". Because material set-ups, protocols, room arrangements and time constraints prefigure what becomes discussable, designers should consider these arrangements — not only instructor style — when aiming for reflection on intended outcomes such as interprofessional collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attention to the interrelated cultural-discursive, material-economic and socio-political arrangements of the debriefing setting
#### Constraints
- The sociomaterial lens does not provide causal explanations of the effects of different teaching or venues for debriefing

### Target Learners
- simulation instructors and designers of interprofessional simulation education
