# Sequence 360-degree NRP video first as a refresher, then the VR simulation, and offer both as adjuncts to in-person NRP training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participant feedback and study results, the authors recommend an ordering and integration scheme for the two VR modalities: "360○ video should be presented first, as a refresher, followed by the VR simulation, to allow for a more hands-on experience." They also suggest the two technologies "could be offered as an adjunct to the in-person training as an additional resource for healthcare providers", complementing the in-person NRP Provider course required at minimum every 2 years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both 360-degree NRP training videos and the VR-NRP simulation with HMD equipment
#### Constraints
- Positioned as an adjunct to, not a replacement for, in-person NRP Provider training

### Target Learners
- NRP-trained healthcare providers
- providers in rural or remote facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- NRP skill refreshing and booster learning between Provider courses
