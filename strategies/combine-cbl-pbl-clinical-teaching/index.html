Strategies

Combine CBL and PBL approaches in clinical teaching settings to target both theory and practical outcomes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that in clinical teaching settings educators employ case-based learning combined with problem-based learning, carrying out targeted teaching on both theoretical and experimental outcomes. They base this on RCT evidence that the combination "shows an obvious learning improvement effect", helping students apply knowledge and enrich experience through solving problems, alongside the NMA finding that CBL leads for clinical-curriculum theory and PBL for practical skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators working in clinical teaching settings with clinical-curriculum content

Constraints¶

  • The NMA itself excluded studies combining two or more methods in the same course, so the recommendation rests on other RCT evidence rather than this NMA's pooled estimates

Target Learners¶

  • medical students in clinical curricula

Target Learning Goals¶

  • theoretical knowledge application
  • clinical practical skills
  • problem-solving

Claims¶

  • CBL is the highest-ranked strategy for enhancing medical students' theoretical test scores, with CBL, PBL, SBL and FC each significantly outperforming LBL [+W]
  • PBL is the highest-ranked strategy for improving medical students' experimental or practical test scores, significantly outperforming LBL [+W]
  • In TACE clinical teaching in China, combining PBL and CBL shows more effectiveness than other compared teaching methods across nine measured outcomes [+W]
  • For knowledge understanding degree and clinical practice capacity in TACE teaching, PBL combined with CBL ranked best, followed by PBL alone [+W]
  • For clinical thinking capacity in TACE teaching, PBL combined with CBL ranked best, followed by CBL alone [+W]
  • For method satisfaction degree in TACE teaching, PBL combined with CBL was the most satisfied among students, followed by PBL alone [+W]
  • For theoretical knowledge examination scores in TACE teaching, PBL combined with TBL ranked best, followed by PBL combined with CBL [-W]
