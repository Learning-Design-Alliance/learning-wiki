# Use non-organic silicone materials for accessible, reproducible tendon repair simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends non-organic materials such as DragonSkin silicone as practical alternatives to animal or cadaveric tissue in simulation training, since they are "relatively easily accessible, reproducible, cost-effective and do not require any special licences or facilities to use". This supports integration of tendon repair simulation without specialised licences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Material must possess face and content validity for the task
#### Constraints
- Non-organic materials generally possess lower physical and functional fidelity than organic materials

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- tendon repair technique practice outside clinical settings
