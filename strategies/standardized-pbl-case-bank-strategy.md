# Prepare standardized PBL cases ahead of time and store them in a PBL case bank

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address ambiguous case scenarios and inconsistent delivery, the article recommends building a reserve of standardized cases. Its conclusions state that "Standardized PBL cases should be prepared ahead and stored in the PBL case bank to ensure a continuous supply of varied cases for effective PBL facilitation." The discussion adds that standardized cases with clearly defined objectives and scopes ensure consistency and reduce student workload by eliminating confusion about preparation materials.
