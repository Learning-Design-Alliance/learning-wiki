# Reinforce growth mindsets through explicit messages plus implicit classroom practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teachers encouraged growth mindsets both explicitly and implicitly. Explicitly, they repeatedly communicated the conviction that each and every student can grow their mathematical abilities and reminded students that learning math is a process in which it is typical not to experience success right away; nearly half of the teachers emphasized that learning requires taking risks, trying new approaches, and exerting sustained effort. Implicitly, teachers reinforced growth mindset through their approach to collaborative work, praise of students' problem-solving processes, and use of mastery-oriented assessments.
