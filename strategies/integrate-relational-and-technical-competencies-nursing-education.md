# Integrate relational and technical competencies in nursing education frameworks to address the assessment gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that nursing education frameworks better integrate relational competencies alongside technical skills, so that assessment gives relational skills such as compassion and communication the same attention as task-focused skills. They argue this integration would enhance patient care and support nurses' mental health and wellbeing for holistic post-pandemic practice. The article states: "Bridging this gap will require educational frameworks that integrate relational and technical competencies, ultimately preparing nurses to meet the complex and evolving demands of healthcare."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment tools that operationalise relational competencies measurably, since the article notes technical skills are currently favoured because they are easier to measure
#### Constraints
- The article notes articulating and assessing relational skills such as compassion can be inherently challenging even within mental health nursing

### Target Learners
- student nurses pursuing adult nursing

### Target Learning Goals
- compassionate, person-centred care
- communication skills
- technical clinical competence
