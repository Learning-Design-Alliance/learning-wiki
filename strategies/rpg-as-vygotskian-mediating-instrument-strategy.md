# Use an RPG as a Vygotskian mediating instrument for investigative teaching of Modern Physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Grounded in Piaget's problem-situation constructivism and Vygotsky's socio-historical theory, the strategy casts the game as a mediating tool: "Podemos entender um Jogo, em especial um RPG, como um instrumento mediador entre a pessoa que joga e o próprio mundo do jogo." The game universe gives players methods of interacting with a Modern Physics phenomenon that is not part of everyday life, approximating investigative teaching. Gamification elements support this: explicit challenges per stage, rewards, progression through phases, immediate feedback on errors, and cooperation between students and teacher as primordial for completing the task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-studied and well-executed gamification process, in the authors' words "quando bem estudado e executado"
- Problem situations that move students out of their comfort position so they reason and build knowledge, then socialize and expose ideas to peers
#### Constraints
- The approach targets phenomena that do not typically belong to students' daily lives, such as Modern Physics content

### Target Learners
- secondary and higher-education students in science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- constructing Modern Physics knowledge participatively
- socialization of knowledge and group work skills
