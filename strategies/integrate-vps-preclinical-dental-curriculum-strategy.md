# Integrate virtual patients into preclinical dental curricula and evaluate with objective outcome measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends incorporating concept-based virtual patients permanently into the existing curriculum so students can apply and deepen knowledge and train for work with real patients, and calls for further development and evaluation using objective outcome measures in addition to self-assessment. It also suggests virtual patients could serve as objective instruments in examinations and that joint use among universities could expand the case pool across dental disciplines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The concept structure serves as a guideline for case creation by teachers or students, with teacher supervision of content accuracy
#### Constraints
- Self-assessed competence only in this study; the article notes the lack of objective assessments limits statements about actual performance gains

### Target Learners
- preclinical dental students transitioning to clinical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- preparation for real patient contact and comprehensive dental treatment competences

## Claims

- [Users of the virtual-patient e-learning tool showed statistically significant improvements in perceived competence in almost every item, with overall agreement rising from 8.3% to 75.0%](../claims/vp-elearning-users-perceived-competence-improved.md) [+W]
- [Students evaluated the e-learning tool positively, with 75.0% overall agreement on functionality and satisfaction and 87.5% on scope](../claims/vp-elearning-high-satisfaction-evaluation.md) [+W]
- [Non-users of the e-learning tool improved far less, with overall agreement reaching only 33.3% and significant gains on only two items](../claims/vp-elearning-nonusers-limited-improvement.md) [+W]
- [At baseline, students did not feel well prepared for clinical treatment, with a total mean competence score of 2.5 and 8.3% overall agreement](../claims/baseline-students-unprepared-clinical-course.md) [+W]
- [Students' free-text expectations at baseline centered on wanting better preparation, especially for treatment planning and first patient contact](../claims/baseline-comments-wish-better-preparation.md) [+W]
