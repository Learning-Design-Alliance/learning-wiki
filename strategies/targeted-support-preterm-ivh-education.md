# Targeted educational support for very preterm children after IVH, especially in numeracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends ongoing, domain-targeted educational support for very preterm children with IVH, since academic gaps were fixed with age and all groups showed parallel academic improvement. It names existing supports: "preterm-awareness education packages for schools, deferred school entry, and personalized interventions targeting weaknesses in learning domains may help this population." Because numeracy was consistently the weakest domain for high-grade IVH, targeted numeracy support is highlighted as important information for families and educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Support must be ongoing, since the article reports parallel trajectories with fixed between-group differences and no catch-up with age.
#### Constraints
- The article notes standardized assessments like NAPLAN may not fully capture the progress of children receiving specialized support, and it had no data on individualized educational supports.

### Target Learners
- Very preterm children (born at less than 32 weeks' gestation) with intraventricular hemorrhage, ages 8 to 13 years

### Target Learning Goals
- Numeracy attainment
- Meeting national minimum standards in literacy and numeracy assessments
