# Embed a simulation-based procedural workshop within the clinical clerkship to teach invasive procedures safely

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend incorporating simulation-based procedural workshops into clinical clerkships, since "simulation-based education provides a controlled setting in which learners can practice procedures without risk to patients." The workshop was integrated into the regular nephrology clerkship curriculum, linking hands-on experience to a clinically meaningful context and offering early procedural exposure where direct participation opportunities are limited.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A durable simulator suitable for repeated puncture
- Integration within the clinical clerkship with clearly focused procedural learning objectives
#### Constraints
- Single-institution delivery by a single instructor; generalizability may be limited

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- invasive procedure skills
- procedural confidence
- interest in nephrology
