# Deliver proximal interventions targeting the child's specific impaired reading component, delivered by a specialist or online programme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, once a poor reader's profile is assessed, the intervention should focus on the proximal impairment of the child's reading behaviour rather than a possible distal impairment. It cites controlled trials and a systematic review suggesting that different proximal impairments can be selectively trained to improve the corresponding reading behaviours. Proximal interventions can be executed by a reading specialist or a highly-sophisticated online reading training programme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of the child's reading behaviours and proximal impairments must precede intervention selection so the training targets the right component.
#### Constraints
- The article describes the supporting evidence as tentative, from two recent controlled trials and one systematic review.
