# Strategies to Develop Positive Classroom Pride

## Description
Use these strategies to develop positive classroom pride: display learner work, positively reinforce learners verbally on a regular basis, publicly ask other staff members to enter the classroom so that they can see a specific accomplishment of your class, recognize accomplishments in areas such as kindness, citizenship, test scores, or athletics, and extend the pride taken in the class by developing parental pride in learner accomplishments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be sincere in their proclamations of pride in learners.
#### Constraints
- Pride does not always have to involve only excellence. Pride in improvement is an important type of pride to nurture.
