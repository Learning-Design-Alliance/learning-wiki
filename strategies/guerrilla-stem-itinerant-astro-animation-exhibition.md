# Guerrilla-STEM: an itinerant astro-animation exhibition with hands-on activities in unexpected public locations to engage audiences not a priori attracted to science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A proposed future direction extending astro-animation beyond the classroom into informal learning. The authors state: "we intend to design an itinerant astro-animation exhibition with hands-on artistic and scientific activities, to be displayed in unexpected locations such as libraries, train stations or music festivals". The goals are to engage participants who are not a priori attracted to or aware of science, integrating animation with astronomy to generate curiosity and initiate easier learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Locations that reach audiences not already attracted to science, such as libraries, train stations, or music festivals
- Hands-on artistic and scientific activities accompanying the exhibition
#### Constraints
- This avenue is exploratory and proposed, not yet implemented or evaluated in the article

### Target Learners
- general public participants not a priori attracted to, or aware of, science

### Target Learning Goals
- increased interest in STE(A)M
- expanded participation in science by underrepresented groups
