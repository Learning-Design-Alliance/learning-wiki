# Establish equitable access and a national registry of trainee procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article highlights wide variability in individual trainee exposure — some performed up to three times as many ETIs and five times as many arterial lines as others — and calls for mechanisms to ensure fair distribution. It also notes the lack of exposure data limits national training-needs analysis, asking "Without a registry of trainee procedures, how do we adapt the EM training programme to guarantee exposure?" and recommending a centralised national approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A centralised national registry of trainee procedures to enable analysis of training needs
#### Constraints
- Current data collection is limited by individual engagement, heterogeneous electronic systems nationally, and ED time pressures

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable access to procedural training opportunities
