# Reading Documentation and Reflection

## Description
Have students record details about their reading such as book titles, number of pages read, and new vocabulary. This process encourages reflection on their reading and can increase accountability. However, this should not be overly burdensome and should align with the goals of fostering a love of reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools for recording reading details, such as journals, logs, or digital apps, and time for reflection.
#### Constraints
- May become a chore if not implemented thoughtfully. Some students may find it burdensome or demotivating. Requires clear expectations and guidelines.
