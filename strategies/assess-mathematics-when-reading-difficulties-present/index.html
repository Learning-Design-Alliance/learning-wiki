Specifically assess mathematics in educational evaluations of children with reading difficulties, especially reading comprehension difficulties¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors draw an assessment recommendation from their finding that mathematics is more closely linked to reading comprehension than to word decoding. They predict that "children with reading difficulties are at elevated risk for co-occurring difficulties in mathematics, and this risk is highest for children with deficits in reading comprehension." They recommend that mathematics be specifically considered in assessments of children presenting with reading difficulties in elementary and middle school, and within progress monitoring initiatives such as response to intervention.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educational assessment and progress-monitoring systems must include mathematics measures alongside reading measures when a child presents with reading difficulties.

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is grounded in a genetically-sensitive correlational study at age 12; the authors state conclusions may or may not apply to younger and older students.

Target Learners¶

  • elementary and middle school students with reading difficulties

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early identification of co-occurring mathematics difficulties in poor readers

Claims¶

  • A common genetic factor influences mathematics, word decoding, and reading comprehension, with substantial genetic overlap across all three domains [+W]
  • The genetic correlation between mathematics and reading comprehension (.76) is significantly higher than between mathematics and word decoding (.50) [+W]
  • At age 12, mathematics is more strongly correlated with reading comprehension than with word decoding at the phenotypic latent-factor level [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Identify specific comprehension deficits early and target intervention at comprehension rather than decoding
