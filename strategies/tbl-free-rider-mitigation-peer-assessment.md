# Mitigate the free-rider phenomenon in TBL through peer assessment and individual accountability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 4 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors identify the "free-rider" phenomenon in teams as a threat to TBL learning outcomes, sensitive to internal team dynamics and varying member abilities. They recommend implementing peer assessment and individual accountability measures: students rate each other's contributions to group work, ratings factor into individual grades, and specific roles and responsibilities are assigned to each group member. This promotes active participation and reduces the likelihood of free-riding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer assessment of each member's contribution to group work
- Individual accountability measures with ratings factored into individual grades
- Assignment of specific roles and responsibilities to each group member
#### Constraints
- The free-rider phenomenon is sensitive to the internal dynamics of the team as well as the varying abilities of its members

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
