# Use regression discontinuity designs to replicate or check experimental impact estimates for education interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report demonstrates using a regression discontinuity design as a replication approach for experimental impact estimates of education interventions. It "attempts to replicate the estimated impacts from experimental studies of two different education interventions using a regression discontinuity design", comparing the RD-based estimates against the experimental benchmarks. This offers evaluators a non-experimental method for assessing whether experimental findings hold when random assignment is not available.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A forcing variable and cutoff that permit regression discontinuity estimation for the intervention being evaluated
#### Constraints
- The report examines two education interventions, so the demonstrated replication scope is limited to those cases; differences between RD and experimental point estimates were sometimes nontrivial

### Target Learners
- Students in education interventions evaluated by IES and Mathematica studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Estimating the causal impact of education interventions on student outcomes
