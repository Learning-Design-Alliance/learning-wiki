Use real-life scenarios requiring learners to produce goal-directed language with target vocabulary¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends implementing authentic problem-based tasks in language classes: learners receive a real-life problem, decide how to respond (for example writing a letter or making a phone call), prepare the needed language, and produce and perform a solution. The author argues that because PBL is learner-centered and provides opportunities to socialize and practice the language, "authentic problem-based tasks can be suitable tasks for language classes".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Scenarios must connect to learners' real-life problems
  • Learners must work collaboratively to generate and evaluate solutions

Constraints¶

  • The author suggests future research should focus on other proficiency levels and learning styles, since this study covered only intermediate adult learners

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate adult EFL learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • vocabulary learning
  • communicative competence

Claims¶

  • Authentic problem-based vocabulary tasks improve immediate vocabulary recall of intermediate Iranian EFL learners compared with conventional input-based tasks [+W]
  • Authentic problem-based vocabulary tasks improve two-week retention of vocabulary compared with conventional input-based tasks [+W]
