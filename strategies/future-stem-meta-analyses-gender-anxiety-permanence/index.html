Future STEM research should meta-analyze additional outcomes such as gender, anxiety, and knowledge permanence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's SUGGESTIONS section directs future researchers to extend meta-analytic synthesis beyond achievement, attitude, and scientific process skills. It states that researchers "will be able to learn STEM education practices during the education period; gender and anxiety on factors such as the effect on different topics can perform meta-analysis studies", and recommends focusing more on the effect of learned knowledge on permanence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to primary studies reporting the additional outcome measures (gender, anxiety, permanence) with quantitative data suitable for meta-analysis

Constraints¶

  • The present meta-analysis excluded outcomes other than academic achievement, course attitude, and scientific process skills

Target Learners¶

  • primary and secondary school students
  • higher education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement in STEM subjects
  • attitude toward STEM courses
  • scientific process skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Extend meta-analytic synthesis of flipped learning to additional outcomes, samples, and regions
  • Future research should examine drama's effects on attitudes, values, skills, and motivation in social studies, and include more doctoral-level studies
  • Prioritize new digital technology-assisted STEM research at primary school level and in mathematics, where effects are currently weakest
