# Provide three categories of support to help primary school EFL teachers implement IFLT: administrative support, intercultural materials and resources, and practice-oriented teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on convergent questionnaire and interview findings, the article concludes teachers need support in three categories: "support from the external environment, for example, support from authorities and school administrations", materials and resources related to intercultural teaching, and teacher training on intercultural competence. The transformation to IFLT is described as a systematic project involving learning objectives, classroom activities, teaching methods and assessment, which individual teachers cannot accomplish alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training on intercultural competence should be practice-oriented and guide actual teaching, since current courses were rated unhelpful
#### Constraints
- Teachers lack time and energy for self-improvement given heavy teaching tasks and trivial affairs

### Target Learners
- primary school EFL teachers in China

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing intercultural foreign language teaching and cultivating pupils' intercultural competence

### Affordances
- Byram Intercultural Communicative Competence Model
