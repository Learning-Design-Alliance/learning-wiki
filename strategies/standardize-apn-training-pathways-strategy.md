# Promote standardized training pathways and ongoing professional development for APNs to support skill standardization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review's policy implications recommend that health systems and educators promote specific training pathways and continuing professional development for advanced practice nurses. As the review states, "promoting specific training pathways and ongoing professional development for APNs would facilitate skill standardization, fostering a more efficient healthcare system oriented toward improved patient outcomes". This strategy responds to the documented international variability in certifications and training requirements, which the review links to ambiguity in practice boundaries and confusion among policymakers and professionals.

## Design Implications
