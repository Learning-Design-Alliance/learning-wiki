# Align didactic communication coursework temporally closer to the simulation activity and add session-summarization and nonverbal-cues training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on observed trends, the authors recommend curricular adjustments: "A few suggestions would be to move the didactic portion of the curriculum closer to the simulation activity," emphasizing "the importance of nonverbal cues training for students" and adding "session summarization" training, since teach-back is taught but session summarization is missing from the didactic portion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing didactic communication courses in Years 1–3
- SP simulation activities in the third year
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from single-institution trend data and may not generalize to other dental schools

### Target Learners
- pre-doctoral dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- communication skills
- session closure skills
- nonverbal communication
