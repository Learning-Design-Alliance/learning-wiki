# Send targeted encouragement emails to discovered student clusters, using A/B testing to measure impact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enables instructors to "save discovered student groups and send targeted emails to members of a speciﬁc group", or to half of a cluster for A/B testing, comparing metric changes on the respective group pages to track effects. This makes instructor encouragement actions targeted and measurable rather than generic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must first cluster students using chosen metrics and assign meaningful labels to the found clusters.
#### Constraints
- The article reports that the actual impact of such encouragement actions was only tested once in a live course, and statistical significance has not yet been shown.

### Target Learners
- MOOC participants

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing student success through targeted, measurable instructor interventions
