# Provide highly contextualized and resource-intensive federal support for school improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that federally supported school improvement will require "highly contextualized and resource intensive support" given the finding that there is no single effective approach. The authors argue the Department of Education would need to provide "substantial amounts of direct and individually tailored technical assistance to state departments of education and possibly also to local school districts." This strategy responds to the finding that the standardized CSR program did not yield comprehensively reformed schools or widespread achievement gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Substantial federal resources for direct and individually tailored technical assistance
#### Constraints
- The article notes no one way to implement effective school improvement exists because different factors play out differently in different settings

### Target Learners
- Students in chronically low-performing K-12 public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- School-wide achievement improvement in mathematics and reading
