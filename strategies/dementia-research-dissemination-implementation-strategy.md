# Require dissemination and implementation plans so dementia education research reaches practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "Evidence‐based practices of effective care must be broadly disseminated to translate and implement effective care", noting that without a well-developed dissemination and implementation plan research results will not be used effectively. It proposes that grant makers require recipients to provide dissemination plans ensuring results go "beyond publications and presentations" and are integrated into healthcare practice, and that recipients report on dissemination activities. No single strategy is universally applicable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education as an essential part of any dissemination and implementation plan
- Educators communicating complex information in a culturally appropriate manner at the learner's educational level
#### Constraints
- Dissemination and implementation are complex processes involving many disciplines and partners; the who, what, when, where, why, and how vary with topic, content, and audience

### Target Learners
- researchers
- educators
- funding agencies

### Target Learning Goals
- translating research into dementia care practice
- evaluating educational inputs against patient and system outcomes
