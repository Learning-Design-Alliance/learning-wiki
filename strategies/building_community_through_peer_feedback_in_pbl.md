# Building Community Through Peer Feedback in PBL

## Description
Building community through peer feedback involves giving students opportunities to frequently share feedback and revise their work. This creates moments to witness growth and progress and allows students to contribute to a strong, supportive classroom culture, building the relationships and connection they need.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured opportunities for peer feedback, clear guidelines for providing constructive feedback, and a classroom environment that values collaboration and support.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure that feedback is constructive and respectful, and may necessitate additional training or support.
