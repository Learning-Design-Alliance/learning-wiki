# Replace standard numerical free-response homework with truly open-ended or carefully crafted multipart questions to discourage trial-and-error

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a forward-looking design recommendation, the article suggests that the observed lack of learning from failed attempts reflects trial-and-error rather than metacognitive thinking, and that question design could address this. It states that "Truly open-ended questions or carefully crafted multipart questions may yield more desirable results, and most online homework systems would be capable of administering them."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online homework system capable of administering open-ended or multipart questions, which the article says most systems already are.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that sufficient data is currently lacking for these question types, so the recommendation is not supported by its own data.

### Target Learners
- students in large enrollment introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- higher-order metacognitive problem solving rather than trial-and-error
