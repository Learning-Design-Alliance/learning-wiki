## Description
The article catalogs methods for improving peer-review quality, including "calibration, reputation, human ('manual') and machine ('automatic') meta-reviewing, rejoinders (feedback from the author to the reviewer)", and different scales for evaluating critiques. These map to the framework's user need of improving review value/quality and are implemented in systems such as CPR, CrowdGrader, Expertiza, Mobius SLIP, and Peerceptiv.
