# Pair the end-of-term DRT with twice-per-term one-to-one oral notebook assessments for in-flight corrections

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To avoid the DRT being purely retrospective, each student has a one-to-one oral assessment of their laboratory notebook twice during the term. These mini-orals give advice on recording practice, help students fine-tune their techniques, build confidence for the DRT, and let students ask questions of experienced practitioners they might not otherwise ask.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A DRT at the end of the module as the summative anchor
#### Constraints
- The author considered a DRT only at the end of term would be too retrospective on its own

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving laboratory notebook recording technique during the term
