# Use interpretations allied to a virtual experiment to enrich quantum mechanics teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends discussing interpretations of interference phenomena alongside a virtual Mach-Zehnder Interferometer available on the worldweb, so that interpretations allied to virtual simulations become a powerful tool in teaching effectiveness. Students can compare how the undulatory, complementarity, hidden-variables and many worlds readings each account for the same punctual detections and interference patterns, making epistemological plurality concrete in a single experimental setup.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the virtual MZI simulator with single-photon and detector-in-arm regimes
#### Constraints
- The authors state it is not their point to favor one particular interpretation over another

### Target Learners
- school teachers in initial and continued formation
- introductory quantum physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of quantum interference
- awareness of rival interpretations of quantum formalism
