# Structure positive interdependence through goal, role, and reward interdependence in cooperative base groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends cultivating positive interdependence deliberately rather than merely forming heterogeneous groups. Positive interdependence "can be fostered through a combination of goals, tasks, resources, roles and rewards". In practice the author used personal support tasks (group name, slogan, banner, logo) for goal interdependence, rotating complementary roles (group leader, secretariat, researcher, spokesperson) for role interdependence, and whole-group scoring with rewards for reward interdependence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Roles must rotate weekly so each member is accountable for due contribution
- Personal support tasks as icebreakers are helpful on the first day of class
#### Constraints
- First group assignments are challenging because there is little emotional bond or individual accountability among members initially

### Target Learners
- English major undergraduates in Chinese independent colleges

### Target Learning Goals
- English language achievement
- classroom engagement

### Affordances
- Social Interdependence Theory Cooperative Learning
