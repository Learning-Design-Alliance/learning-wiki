# Involve local domain-unit staff for the full workshop day rather than as an end-of-day panel

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When training reviewers of a specialist methodology, embed staff from the local operating unit for the entire workshop day so they can answer technical questions in context. Two pilot workshops compared formats: "The presence of data linkage staff throughout the whole workshop was better utilised and valued by participants than the panel discussion." The authors report this also gave unit staff insight into committee members' concerns and built a working relationship between the groups, benefits they judged unlikely to be achieved online.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of local unit staff willing to attend for the full day
- Pilot workshops to determine the optimal involvement format
#### Constraints
- The authors state these benefits of personal interaction are unlikely to be achieved had the training been delivered online

### Target Learners
- Human Research Ethics Committee members reviewing specialist-methodology applications

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding the technical mechanics of the methodology under review
- Building working relationships between reviewers and the operating unit
