# Structure integrated CBL-MDT interventions as five curated cases with pre-class self-directed learning, small-group analysis, simulated MDT discussion, and structured feedback.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental group received an integrated CBL-MDT intervention structured as case selection and preparation, pre-class self-directed learning, case analysis session, simulated MDT discussion, summary and feedback, and post-class extended learning. Selected cases encompassed diverse clinical scenarios with comprehensive documentation including medical history, imaging studies, and pathology reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Five representative cases spanning the disease spectrum
- Specialists from six disciplines participating in structured MDT discussions
- Learning management system for distributing case materials one week in advance
#### Constraints
- Rapid advances in HCC management require continuous case updating
- Engaging multiple specialists simultaneously requires advance planning

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnostic reasoning
- Interdisciplinary integration
- Evidence utilization
