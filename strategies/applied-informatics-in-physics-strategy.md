## Description
The article proposes applied informatics — "digital skills, programming and data science" — in physics as a new trend in didactics that could increase pupils' motivation and practical use of knowledge and skills. It argues physics with mathematics gives informatics its science and technical bases, and that applied informatics in theoretical and experimental physics is the engine of research and development.
