# Blind motivational-statement selection with diversity-aware optimisation to manage oversubscribed camps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To cope with oversubscription (179 applicants for 64 places in 2019) while maintaining cohort diversity, IWA evaluates applicants on a short motivation statement rather than academic performance, with multiple reviewers making a blind, subjective evaluation to limit unconscious bias. The organisers also "tested an algorithm similar to Entrofy which frames applicant selection as an optimisation problem", letting organisers specify diversity criteria such as gender or nationality, combined with the existing selection process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple blind reviewers evaluating motivational statements with limited supporting information
- Human oversight of any algorithmic selection
#### Constraints
- Blinding may lead to other selection biases, for example how fluent the applicant is in written English
- The algorithmic approach still requires significant human oversight and needs further iteration

### Target Learners
- Applicants to oversubscribed outreach programmes, including under-represented groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining diversity within the participant cohort
- Fair selection independent of academic performance
