## Description
The article recommends splitting pedagogical emphasis by topic: algebraic skills for orbital mechanics, where calculation questions dominate and mathematical errors cost most marks, and qualitative modelling plus precise definitions for field theory, energy and potential, where conceptual gaps dominate. The author states that "Teaching strategies for these topics must prioritize qualitative understanding", and suggests visualisation software and metaphor as supports for distinguishing abstract concepts.
