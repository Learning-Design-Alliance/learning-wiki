# Strengthen SEM OSCE research through larger samples, longitudinal data, and fuller assessment-quality measurement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is to improve research methods for evaluating OSCEs in SEM. It urges "increasing the sample size of studies" and using longitudinal exam data to study re-failure rates, candidate characteristics, and examiner change over time, and calls for studies to involve "appropriate measurements of assessment quality such as utility, acceptability, and validity". It also highlights exploring the relationship between candidate characteristics and OSCE scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
