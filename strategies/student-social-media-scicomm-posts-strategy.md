# Student-produced social-media science-communication posts as a mandatory concluding activity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a mandatory activity to conclude the project, students created posts for social platforms such as Facebook and Instagram, one post per month (4 posts in total on 4 different arguments) on topics from the seminars. Each post had a fixed structure: author names, school and class, one image, a caption, references, and hashtags. Working alone or in groups, students could "manage the learning process in such a way they act as science communicators", preparing materials for a specific platform and target. The posts were later transformed into qr-codes forming a collective art exhibition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A fixed post structure including author information, one image, a caption, references, and hashtags
- Topics drawn from seminar content the students attended
#### Constraints
- Only 127 of the 236 attending students completely ended the project by writing the posts

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 17-19

### Target Learning Goals
- science communication skills
- understanding of seminar topics in physics and philosophy
