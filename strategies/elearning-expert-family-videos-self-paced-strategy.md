# Embed expert and family videos and self-paced flexible delivery in online professional education to widen reach and consistency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using an eLearning format for professional education because, compared with face-to-face teaching, it "enhanced national reach, including those living in rural and remote areas, and ensured consistency of content" and provided flexible, self-paced learning. Embedding videos let participants learn from topic experts and from families impacted by cCMV. The format was also accessible and inexpensive during COVID-19 lockdowns.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning management system for distribution and promotion (the ACM LMS, website, social media and eNewsletters)
- Content developed with an expert reference group
#### Constraints
- The article notes a small proportion of respondents (24/296, 8%) had difficulty navigating the course and a minority wanted more information or found it too long

### Target Learners
- Midwives and other maternity health professionals
- Professionals in rural and remote areas

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent guideline-aligned clinical knowledge
- Confidence in providing prevention advice
