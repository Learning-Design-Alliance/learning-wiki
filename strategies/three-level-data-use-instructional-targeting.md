# Use achievement and contextual data at school, classroom, and student levels to target instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Turnaround schools considered data at three levels: "at the school level to focus on areas that needed schoolwide improvement to meet adequate yearly progress, at the classroom level to focus on teachers' instructional strengths and weaknesses, and at the student level to focus on instructional needs of individual students." Schools disaggregated data by demographics, objectives, and item responses to identify students needing help on specific skills.
