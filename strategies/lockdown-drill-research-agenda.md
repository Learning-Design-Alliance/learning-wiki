# Prioritize a research agenda on the long-term effects, optimal parameters, and trauma-skill tradeoffs of lockdown drills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief calls for "carefully designed studies" to fill the gaps in the sparse research base on lockdown drills. It enumerates four question areas: long-term effects of drills on students, faculty, and staff; identifying procedures that maximize skill mastery and minimize trauma; mechanisms to standardize drill parameters for districts and schools; and how drills fit within thorough emergency response plans. The brief argues that answering such questions through rigorous research will build a more holistic understanding of drill effects and best practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rigorous, carefully designed studies are needed because current research on the direct impact of drills on students is sparse.
#### Constraints
- The brief notes few state and local statutes are currently specific in defining the parameters of required drills.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- school faculty and staff

### Target Learning Goals
- emergency preparedness
- evidence-based school safety policy
