# Incorporate dynamic learning materials (videos, comics, simulations) to facilitate students' ideas of change and dynamics about stars

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend supporting ideas of change and dynamics about stars with dynamic materials: "incorporating learning materials that depict these dynamic properties", such as videos or comics, or materials that "enable learners to interact with the material", such as simulations. The recommendation responds to persistent static views of stars found in the study, especially in the lower high school classes, and to documented confusions of central ideas like stellar system and solar system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning materials must depict dynamic properties of stars, such as stars changing brightness or stars changing color, or enable learners to interact with the material
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is the authors' surmise from the study findings; no such intervention was tested in this study

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students, especially lower high school classes where static ideas were found

### Target Learning Goals
- Ideas of change and dynamics of stars, such as stars changing brightness or stars changing color
