# Train teachers in prompt engineering, evaluating AI-generated responses, ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For policymakers incorporating AI tools such as ChatGPT into the English language curriculum, the article states it "is essential to provide teachers with training and professional development on eﬀectively utilizing AI tools in the classroom". Training "should focus on prompt engineering skills, strategies for evaluating AI-generated responses, understanding ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making". Instructors also need practical skills in guiding learners' ethical use of ChatGPT and designing AI-supported learning tasks that balance cognitive, emotional, and relational aspects of language learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to teacher professional development on AI tool use
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a single-institution qualitative study whose transferability is limited to settings with similar learner characteristics and pedagogical practices

### Target Learners
- teachers and instructors of English in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- effective classroom integration of AI tools; guiding learners' ethical use of ChatGPT; designing AI-supported learning tasks
