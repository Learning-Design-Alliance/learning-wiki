# Select relatively simple cases and simplify preparation tasks to reduce the burden of SCL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article reports that the negative learning experience associated with the SCL model's preparation burden can be mitigated by case selection and task design. Specifically, it states that "this negative learning experience can be improved by selecting relatively simple cases and simplifying the preparation tasks." This strategy targets the preparation phase, where students retrieve literature and gather clinical guidelines before class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must select representative cases and prepare questions in advance of the seminar session.
#### Constraints
- May reduce the depth or complexity of case-based learning if cases are too simple.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students
- Postgraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical knowledge acquisition
- Clinical reasoning
