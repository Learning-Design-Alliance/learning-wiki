# Expand simulation-based teaching to additional physiology practicals and organ systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend leveraging clinical technologies in other parts of the curriculum and expanding simulation-based practicals to other complex organ systems to generate further empirical evidence and build a foundation for students' future clinical success. This follows the positive student reception of the cardiovascular pilot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Positive pilot reception as a precondition for curricular expansion
- Availability of appropriate simulators and simulation center capacity
#### Constraints
- Pilot findings limited to one study year at one institution

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in preclinical physiology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning, clinical exposure and physiological concept mastery across organ systems
