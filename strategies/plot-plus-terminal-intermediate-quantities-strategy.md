# Pairing the simulator's visual plot with terminal readouts of intermediate vector quantities to make electric-field computation visible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The article recommends using the simulator so that, while the plot is obtained, students can review in the terminal the magnitudes of the charges, the position vectors, the relative position vectors with their magnitudes and unit vectors, and the electric field vectors from each charge and from superposition. This connects the graphical representation with the intermediate quantities of the seven-step algorithm, addressing the authors' concern that abstract concepts lack a previous sense experience.
