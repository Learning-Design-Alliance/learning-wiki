# Include more asynchronous e-learning activities to enhance teaching and learning in resource-constrained settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's conclusion recommends that faculty designing e-learning in resource-constrained settings "include more asynchronous e-learning activities to enhance teaching and learning and improve student experiences", because students in this course struggled to complete synchronous activities on the platform. Asynchronous components used included a peer-graded written assignment and course evaluation, each with three days for completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An e-learning platform such as the Moodle LMS used in this course, with asynchronous activities students can complete within a set window
#### Constraints
- Students in this setting faced technological barriers accessing the platform during synchronous activities, so asynchronous design alone does not remove connectivity and device constraints

### Target Learners
- higher diploma health-professional students in resource-constrained institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- research competencies
- flexible access to course content and assessment
