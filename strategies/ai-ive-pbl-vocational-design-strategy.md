# Use AI-enabled immersive virtual environments to enrich project-based learning in vocational design education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends integrating the advantages of an AI-enabled immersive virtual environment with the features of project-based learning, "including inquiry, collaboration, and active participation," to advance design creativity among vocational students. The strategy pairs immersive, intelligent, feedback-rich technology with PBL's collaborative inquiry structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI-enabled immersive virtual environment with stereoscopic immersion, embodied engagement, intelligent interaction, and real-time feedback
#### Constraints
- Evidence to date comes from a single vocational college course (Public Space Interior Design) evaluated with a two-group quasi-experiment

### Target Learners
- vocational college students

### Target Learning Goals
- design creativity
