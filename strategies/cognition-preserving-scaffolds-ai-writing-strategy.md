# Embed structured planning and targeted-generation supports in AI writing tools so students engage with cognition-preserving scaffolds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends designing AI writing tools so that structured supports for planning and targeted generation are embedded in the writing process rather than offering task-agnostic content production. Its deployment shows that “when systems provide structured supports for planning and targeted generation, students naturally choose to engage with these cognition-preserving scaffolds.” Instructors positioned AI support during reflective stages after initial drafting, using prompts that scaffold reflection and critical evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI support should be embedded in process-oriented writing activities and introduced after students have produced an initial draft, with instructional framing from the instructor
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one-week deployments; long-term effects on habit formation and retention were not assessed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate writing students

### Target Learning Goals
- argumentative writing quality
- critical AI literacy
- metacognitive regulation
