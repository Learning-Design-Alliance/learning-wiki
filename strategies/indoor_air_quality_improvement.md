# Indoor Air Quality Improvement

## Description
Improving indoor air quality reduces absenteeism, particularly for students with asthma, and addresses “sick building syndrome.” Proper ventilation systems and pollutant reduction create a healthier learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Improved ventilation systems, air purifiers, pollutant reduction strategies.
#### Constraints
- Implementation can be costly and may require significant facility upgrades.
