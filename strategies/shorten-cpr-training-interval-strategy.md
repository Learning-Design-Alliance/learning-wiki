# Shorten CPR retraining intervals and add practice opportunities, plus promote first-aid law awareness, to improve student CPR awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends consolidating CPR learning by "reducing training intervals to consolidate training", since the adjusted analysis showed only intervals <6 months independently predicted qualification. It further recommends providing more practice opportunities beyond the largely theoretical training in China, and promoting the Shanghai Emergency Medical Services Regulations to reduce fear of legal responsibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training delivery must fit within students' schedules, which the authors note are constrained by college entrance examination pressure; practical as well as theoretical training is needed.
#### Constraints
- Evidence is cross-sectional from three Shanghai schools; the authors call for prospective, large-sample confirmation before firm conclusions.

### Target Learners
- senior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- cardiopulmonary resuscitation knowledge and first-aid awareness
