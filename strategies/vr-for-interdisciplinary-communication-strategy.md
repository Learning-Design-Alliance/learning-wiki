# Use VR to support interdisciplinary communication in research and education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying VR as an ICT tool to support interdisciplinary communication, which it calls vital to the success of interdisciplinary research and education. Case studies include a Vanderbilt course mixing future engineers with future non-engineers, an industrial heritage project where VR represented objects and scenes for monument preservation collaboration, and a factory automation case using immersive VR to build mutual understanding across mechanical, electrical and computer engineering teams at different locations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple viewpoints are critical in interdisciplinary matters, so the VR environment must support shared representation of objects and scenes across disciplines
#### Constraints
- In the factory automation case, the authors report the concept seemed very promising, yet many aspects needed to be investigated and developed in more detail

### Target Learners
- interdisciplinary researchers
- university students in cross-disciplinary courses

### Target Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary communication and collaboration
