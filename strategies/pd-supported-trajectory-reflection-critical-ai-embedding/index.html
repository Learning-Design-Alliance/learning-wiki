Structure AI-focused professional development as a supported trajectory of planning, reflection, design autonomy, and embedded critical AI literacy¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that professional development move beyond tool demonstrations and technology-acceptance outcomes toward a supported developmental trajectory. Programs should "build confidence and informed willingness to experiment, scaffold cycles of planning and reflection, provide autonomy to design and revise modules, and examine evidence from learner work." Critical AI literacy should be embedded in planning, differentiation, inquiry, and assessment rather than confined to a separate ethics session, and assessment should include artifacts and rationales rather than confidence surveys alone.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programs that scaffold cycles of planning and reflection and give teachers autonomy to design and revise modules

Constraints¶

  • The typology used as a formative design heuristic is not a validated measurement scale; claims rest on teacher reports without student outcome measures

Target Learners¶

  • in-service teachers in AI-focused professional development; teacher educators designing such programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • teacher professional learning for AI-enhanced heutagogical learning design and critical AI mediation

Affordances¶

  • Dual Layer Teacher Regulation Learner Design Ai

Related Strategies¶

  • Operationalize the faculty standards through structured professional learning pathways with modules, microcredentials, and peer review
  • Invest in sustained, role-specific, discipline-specific professional learning and structured student AI literacy pathways across PK16
  • Design AI-related teacher professional development as practice-oriented, case-based and connected to teachers' real pedagogical decisions
  • Embed AI training for pre-service science teachers within science pedagogy and inquiry-based teaching rather than general tool operation
