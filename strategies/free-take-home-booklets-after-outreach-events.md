# Distribute free take-home printed booklets after astronomy outreach events

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends giving participants something physical to keep after an outreach event, because "people often like to have something to keep after a conference, a planetarium show or a school activity, so that they can remember or share their experience with their families". TUIMP implements this with free downloadable, foldable booklets that can be printed cheaply and handed out.
