# Adapt feedback tone dynamically across re-attempts, starting supportive and transitioning to challenging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's discussion proposes that adaptive feedback systems adjust tone in real time: "starting with a supportive approach for an incorrect answer and, based on student performance on re-attempts at a particular question, transitioning only to a challenging tone to promote deeper understanding and reduce frustration". A system could also become more supportive based on a student's engagement. This strategy rests on the observed tone-by-difficulty interaction effects rather than on a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A feedback system capable of real-time tone adjustment and tracking student performance on re-attempts
#### Constraints
- Proposed in the discussion; the article did not test this adaptive strategy with learners

### Target Learners
- students answering MCQs in digital learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- deeper understanding and reduced frustration after incorrect answers
