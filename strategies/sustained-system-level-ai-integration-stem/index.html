Implement AI-supported STEM instruction through sustained, system-level integration of one to two months or more rather than isolated short interventions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that institutions prioritize long-term integration of AI tools rather than short-term or isolated interventions, stating that "effective AI integration requires system-level planning and sustained implementation, rather than short-term or isolated interventions." This aligns with its moderator findings: interventions of more than one and up to two months produced the largest effect (g = 0.833), while one-to-seven-day interventions were not statistically significant.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Alignment of AI tools with curricular and pedagogical goals, continuous evaluation, and instructional design that manages cognitive load

Constraints¶

  • The article notes more time does not automatically translate into greater effectiveness, since effects did not increase consistently beyond two months

Target Learners¶

  • STEM students across primary, secondary, and tertiary education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • STEM academic achievement

Related Strategies¶

  • Prioritize sustained, pedagogically grounded faculty TPACK development over one-off technology workshops and infrastructure investment
  • Invest in sustained, role-specific, discipline-specific professional learning and structured student AI literacy pathways across PK16
  • Pursue sustainable AI integration in PBL through attention to design, ethics, and policy
  • Adopt a systems approach to AI integration rather than spotlights or gaps
  • Adopt AI-PBL frameworks with institutional and policy-level support and teacher training investment
  • Sustain doctoral methodological competence through continued practice, feedback, and authentic application rather than isolated short courses
  • Align peer mindset interventions with teacher practices and broader classroom context rather than isolating them
