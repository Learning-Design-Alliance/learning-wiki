# Sustained funding and outreach recommendations for growing local participation in Hawai'i astronomy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors issue a set of recommendations: "Consistent and long-term support for the development and/or maintaining of these mentoring programs should be emphasized," since lack of consistent funding undermines STEM mentoring programs; more programs should help public-school students catch up and build confidence; more resources should go into financial support for local students, advertised widely; and "more resources be placed into outreach to share the amazing work being done on the mountain to the local communities," plus support for interdisciplinary work bridging Hawaiian culture and astronomy such as the Physics of Pō and ʻImiloa.
