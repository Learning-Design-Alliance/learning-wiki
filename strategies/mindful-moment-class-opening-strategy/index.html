Begin class with a mindful moment to settle students’ minds and bodies in preparation for learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A deliberate pause at the start of class helps students settle their minds and bodies before learning. The brief’s example is to “Begin class with a “mindful moment” where students are guided through either (1) sitting and noticing their bodily sensations” or slow, deliberate movement pairing breaths with arm raises. The stated benefit is that students learn to slow down, quiet their nervous systems, and check in with feelings or thoughts needing attention.
