# Three paired, modular simulation-based lab activities on measurement uncertainty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes three paired lab activities designed to help students develop and practice measurement-uncertainty skills, each grounded in a realistic short-story scenario with the student as main character. Lab 1 focuses on uncertainty of single and multiple measurements, Lab 2 on comparing single versus multiple measurements, and Lab 3 on advanced topics such as error propagation and curve fitting. Each activity starts with five minutes of open exploration and follows a predict–gather/analyze–conclude/reflect structure, and each is self-contained and modular so instructors can use one or two independently.
