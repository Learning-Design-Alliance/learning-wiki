# Integrate interprofessional simulation-based CRM workshops into longitudinal curricula early, frequently, and across rotations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants recommended embedding simulation-based CRM training into standard curricula from early medical school, repeating it across rotations and specialties, and scheduling it on days without other obligations. As one participant put it, "I think that we should conduct such workshops more frequently and have more practice." The article presents these as participants' recommendations for future sessions rather than tested manipulations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions should be scheduled on days without other obligations to provide an undistracted, focused environment, and a briefing of workshop objectives beforehand.
#### Constraints
- Participants reported physical exhaustion when the workshop followed a full day of other duties; the recommendation rests on participant suggestions, not on comparative outcome data.

### Target Learners
- medical students
- postgraduate residents
- critical care nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- crisis resource management skills
- interprofessional teamwork and communication
