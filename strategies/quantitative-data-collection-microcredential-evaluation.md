# Plan long-term quantitative data collection and interoperable systems to evaluate micro-credentials' social mobility impact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because most rural micro-credentialing programs remain in pilot phases, the report recommends that institutions "plan for long-term impact and evaluation" to determine whether micro-credentials promote social mobility, particularly for poverty-impacted BIPOC learners. Initiatives should collect and monitor data on student demographics, educational attainment outcomes, and wages; ensure data systems across organizations are interoperable; fund personnel to collect and analyze information; and improve messaging to earners, employers, and funders. The report includes an appendix template to help initiatives collect analyzable program-outcome data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding for personnel responsible for collecting and analyzing information
- Data interoperability across higher education, adult education, training providers, and employers
#### Constraints
- In this study the data obtained from sites were not in line with the research question assessing shifts in socioeconomic mobility, and the number of participants earning micro-credentials was not robust enough for thorough analyses

### Target Learners
- Poverty-impacted rural adult learners, particularly BIPOC learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Credential attainment
- Workforce entry and promotion
- Salary increase and educational attainment
