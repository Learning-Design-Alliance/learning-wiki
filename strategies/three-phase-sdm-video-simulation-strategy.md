# Three-phase simulation session: expectation-setting, observing an inappropriate-model video, then an appropriate-model video with discussion and communication practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable session recipe runs in three phases. Phase 1 spends five minutes refreshing participants' experience, managing expectations, explaining learning objectives, and introducing cases. Phase 2 involves "immersion, observation, and reflection": participants watch a 10-minute inappropriate-version video of nurses communicating about EOL care, followed by a 10-minute instructor-led discussion. Phase 3 involves "reflection, feedback, and conversation": a 20-minute appropriate-version video, then 10 minutes of discussion and 10 minutes of communication practice, with choice, option, and decision talk carried through the reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two faculty facilitators; paired appropriate/inappropriate video scenarios; standardized patients trained for eight hours for the accompanying OSCE
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated for EOL care SDM scenarios with licensed nurses; effect on attitudes was not statistically significant

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses in EOL care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- SDM communication skills
- attitudes toward shared decision-making
