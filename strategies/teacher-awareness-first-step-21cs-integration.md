# Build teacher awareness of 21st century skills as the first step toward integrating them into language classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, because using 21st century skills in EFL classes can improve learning by shaping appealing environments, "the first step should be increasing awareness of teachers of the 21st century skills, their importance, and their various exercises". Knowledgeable teachers are seen as best placed to transmit these skills, and teachers can gain proficiency in preparing and revising how to teach skills such as problem-solving, creativity, cooperation and critical thinking. This strategy follows directly from the study's finding that skills-integrated instruction improved listening and speaking outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- EFL teachers of secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher professional development in 21st century skills integration
