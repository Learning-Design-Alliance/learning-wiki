# System-oriented curricula using CBL, PBL and TBL around real ward cases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors initially established "system-oriented curricula" for 16 outstanding students from the class of 2010, integrating clinical information and basic medical science through case-based, problem-based and team-based learning. Respiratory, cardiovascular and digestive system cases (pleural effusion, acute chest pain, acute abdominal pain) guide group discussion, ward practice and simulator rehearsal of procedures such as thoracentesis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared cases with teaching objectives
- Group arrangements for PBL discussion
- Access to wards, endoscopy room and simulators
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness and implementation of this approach should be further analyzed and improved, per the authors

### Target Learners
- outstanding third-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical thinking
- diagnosis and treatment of system-specific disorders
- integration of basic science with clinical practice
