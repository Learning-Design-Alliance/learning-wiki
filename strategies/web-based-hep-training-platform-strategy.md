# Web-based training platform with incremental contribution and persistent container environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes a web-based platform as a solution to lack of funding and expert time, in which tutors add material slowly ("one slide a week or a chapter a day") and collaborators or students expand it. Combined with remote virtual machines or Docker containers, students could "run complex examples from home" in an exact replica of their experiment environment, with containers preserving modified environments across sessions to allow prolonged skill development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web platform hosting materials; Docker or remote virtual machines so students need no specialist local installation; moderation and validation policies for student contributions
#### Constraints
- Contributions from others to the training material "needs to be moderated and validated with appropriate policies"; the paper calls for evaluating how schools and web courses can efficiently cooperate

### Target Learners
- Distributed HEP researchers and students worldwide, learning at their own pace

### Target Learning Goals
- Machine learning, statistical analysis with ROOT, good practices in C++, and Python programming for scientific computing
