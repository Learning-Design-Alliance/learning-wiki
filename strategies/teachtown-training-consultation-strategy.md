# Two-day didactic training plus webinars and monthly consultation delivered by program developers to support CAI implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol specifies a training and support package for teachers receiving TeachTown: "teachers in the intervention group (and the control group in year 2) will receive 2 days of didactic training at the start of the school year, with experiential components, webinars throughout the school year, and monthly consultation." Training and monthly consultation are provided by the program developers, described by the authors as consistent with best practices. The same team also provides didactic training and in vivo classroom coaching in existing EBPs.
