# Break Time Consequence

## Description
Writing instructions in bold letters on the chalkboard stating that students who continue talking during the lesson will have time taken off their break. By the time the title is written on the board, students need to be sitting in silence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to consistently enforce the consequence. Clear communication and follow-through are essential.
#### Constraints
- May be perceived as punitive and could negatively impact student-teacher relationships. May not be effective for students who do not value break time.

- Provides a clear warning and consequence, giving students the opportunity to adjust their behavior. Offers a structured approach to managing classroom noise.

### Personalization
- The amount of time taken off break can be adjusted based on the severity of the disruption and the needs of the class.
