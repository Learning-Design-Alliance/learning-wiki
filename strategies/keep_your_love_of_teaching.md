# Keep Your Love of Teaching

## Description
Continue creating engaging projects and lessons and keep enjoying the students. Educators should be willing to laugh at their own faux pas to help students understand that it is okay to make mistakes and continue to grow.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A positive attitude, a sense of humor, and a commitment to creating an engaging classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to maintain a positive attitude in the face of difficult situations.

### Affordances
