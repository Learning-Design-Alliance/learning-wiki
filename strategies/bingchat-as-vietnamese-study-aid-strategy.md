# Adopt BingChat rather than ChatGPT as an LLM-based study aid for Vietnamese students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends BingChat as the preferred LLM-based educational assistant for Vietnamese students, on the grounds of availability, GPT-4 technology, hyperlinks, subject performance, online information access, and real-time data. It notes "BingChat is officially available in Vietnam, while ChatGPT is not," and that "BingChat also offers a hyperlink feature that facilitates students in conveniently accessing supplementary resources and exploring related information." Its Bing search connection lets it retrieve current online information, whereas ChatGPT's training data has a cutoff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Microsoft Bing Chat, which is officially available in Vietnam
#### Constraints
- The paper states BingChat currently lacks capabilities for subjects demanding sophisticated cognitive processes such as mathematics, physics, and chemistry; ChatGPT outperforms it in literature

### Target Learners
- Vietnamese high school students
- students seeking LLM-based assistant tools in education

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic support across nine VNHSGE subjects
- foreign language acquisition
- accessing and verifying supplementary learning resources
