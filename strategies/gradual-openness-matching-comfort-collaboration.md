# Facilitate online educational collaboration by matching participants on comfort and gradually increasing openness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 5 studies (2 review, 2 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that educational communities facilitate online collaborations more effectively by matching participants not only on shared interest but also on comfort, such as a trusted environment with known peers, and by enabling a gradual increase in openness through inclusion of further participants. The model shows the benefits of openness and collaboration are not reaped if too much openness is set at the start.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Platforms must allow closed collaborations based on strong existing ties and let individuals announce openness preferences without fear of losing face
#### Constraints
- Neither the benefits of openness nor collaborative efforts are reaped if too much openness and transparency are set at the start of collaboration

### Target Learners
- educators in online communities developing OER

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge exchange and collaborative development of educational resources
