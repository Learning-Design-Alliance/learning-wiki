# Use web-based learning as a flexible supplemental tool before practical training, pending further research on its optimal use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend web-based learning as a supplemental format for practical skills training because it "increases the flexibility of learning in terms of location and time, is available to participants both before and after practical training and can be cost effective". They argue further research is needed to investigate its optimal use, noting web-based learning often ignores educational foundations such as interaction and timely adapted feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attention to educational foundations such as interaction and timely adapted feedback, which the authors say web-based learning often ignores
#### Constraints
- In this trial the supplemental web course did not improve practical CPR skills; further research on optimal use is needed

### Target Learners
- School students preparing for practical CPR training

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for practical skills training and theoretical knowledge of cardiovascular disease
