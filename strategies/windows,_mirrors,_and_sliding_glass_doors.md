# Windows, Mirrors, and Sliding Glass Doors

## Description
This strategy involves using stories and materials that act as mirrors, windows, and sliding glass doors to help students identify their place in the world. A window offers a view into someone else’s experience, a sliding door allows the reader to enter the story and become a part of the world, and a mirror is a story that reflects one's own culture and helps build identity. It is critical to understand that students cannot truly learn about themselves unless they learn about others as well. Students can learn about how other people conduct themselves in the world in order to understand how they might fit in.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diverse resources, a culturally sensitive curriculum, and time for reflection.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to find diverse resources that accurately reflect the experiences of all students. Requires ongoing commitment and support from teachers and administrators.
