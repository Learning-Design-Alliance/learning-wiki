# Cultivate Understanding and Respect Across Languages and Dialects

## Description
Using translations, descriptions, movement, and images to support learning in unfamiliar or complex languages promotes understanding and respect for diverse linguistic backgrounds. This includes providing translations of key terms and concepts. It also involves using visual aids, gestures, and real-world examples to clarify meaning. The goal is to create a learning environment where all languages and dialects are valued and respected.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to translation resources and multilingual materials. Instructors need to be trained in culturally responsive teaching strategies.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and resources. Some learners may be resistant to using translations or visual aids. It's essential to be sensitive to learners' cultural and linguistic needs.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Promote linguistic diversity, enhance communication, foster cultural awareness, support inclusive learning

### Affordances
- Supports linguistic diversity by valuing and respecting all languages and dialects, enhances communication by providing strategies to overcome language barriers, fosters cultural awareness by exposing learners to different linguistic backgrounds, and supports inclusive learning by creating a welcoming environment for all learners.

### Personalization
- Provide translations of key terms and concepts in learners' native languages. Use visual aids, gestures, and real-world examples to clarify meaning. Encourage learners to share their own linguistic and cultural experiences.
