# Coaches and teachers should develop athletes' success experiences and motivation for success to support extra training and academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that motive for success was the salient mediator for both outcomes, the authors recommend that coaches and teachers "focus more on developing collegiate athletes' success experience, improving their motivation for success, and designing appropriate lectures and training to improve their interest in learning". The intent is that strengthened success motivation will translate into more extra training and more time devoted to academic work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must target motive for success, the only achievement-motivation attribute significantly associated with both outcomes in this study
#### Constraints
- The authors caution the causal relationship could not be confirmed in this prospective design and should be examined with stricter experimental designs such as randomized controlled trials

### Target Learners
- collegiate athletes

### Target Learning Goals
- increased extra training time
- improved academic performance
