# Teacher Racial Identity Work

## Description
Teacher racial identity work involves educators examining their own racial identity, biases, and privilege to create a more inclusive and equitable classroom environment. This includes taking responsibility for one's own education around race, creating space for challenging conversations, and learning about the racial identities of students. By modeling this self-awareness, teachers can empower students to advocate for themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to commit to ongoing self-reflection, engage in challenging conversations, and seek out resources to learn about race and cultural identity.
#### Constraints
- Can be emotionally challenging and time-consuming. Requires a willingness to confront personal biases and privileges.
