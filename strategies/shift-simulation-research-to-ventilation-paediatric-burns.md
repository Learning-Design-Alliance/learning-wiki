# Direct future prehospital simulation training and research toward ventilation, paediatric resuscitation and burns management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends redirecting simulation research and training toward the under-served areas its mapping revealed. It states that "a shift of simulation research focus towards also integrating ventilation, paediatric resuscitation and management of burns victims may be beneficial", because staff knowledge in these areas was described as inadequate across the studies. It further recommends that these techniques be taught by simulation and followed up and maintained to ensure quality of care and patient safety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff must be familiar with and trained on the identified topics: intubation, trauma care, CPR, ventilation and triage
#### Constraints
- The review notes research needs to examine whether staged scenarios are comparable to real events, something it says lacks in evidence today

### Target Learners
- paramedics
- EMTs
- medical doctors
- nurses
- fire fighters

### Target Learning Goals
- ventilation skills
- paediatric resuscitation
- burns assessment and management
- CPR quality
