# Assign a random pair member as reporter in think-pair-share to broaden verbal participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A specific think-pair-share implementation in which, after students pair up and compare ideas, "the student in each pair whose name came ﬁrst alphabetically" shares the pair's discussion. By assigning a reporter based on a random personal characteristic, the instructor created verbal participation opportunities for students who might not otherwise volunteer and encouraged a collaborative community. The instructor then typed responses into a lecture slide, making ideas visible and acknowledging each response.
