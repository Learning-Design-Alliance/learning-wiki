# Clarifying

## Description
Clarifying involves identifying and resolving areas of uncertainty in a text or problem. This includes unfamiliar vocabulary, complex sentence structures, and difficult concepts. In math, this entails identifying facts that are known and determining what information is still needed to solve the problem.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to resources for clarifying vocabulary and concepts, such as dictionaries or online tools.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources and support for learners with significant gaps in knowledge.
