# Embed non-topic filler questions in pre-tests to minimize priming and expectancy bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The investigators embedded the four adrenal-focused questions among ten multiple-choice questions on general pediatric emergency management. The article states the inclusion of additional non-AC questions "was intended to minimize priming and reduce the likelihood that participants would anticipate the simulation topic." This design choice strengthened methodological rigor by reducing expectancy bias in the pre-post assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pool of plausible non-target questions on related emergency topics
#### Constraints
- Completion of the pre-test immediately before the simulation may still have increased participant awareness of adrenal crisis topics

### Target Learners
- PGY1 pediatric and medicine-pediatric residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate measurement of baseline knowledge without priming
