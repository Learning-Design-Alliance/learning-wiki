# Integrate empirically sound communication concepts into health professions curricula instead of induction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors conclude that although interprofessional practice and communication are now curricular subjects and various competence frameworks exist, concrete imperatives for competence-oriented implementation in teaching and examination are lacking. They recommend integrating "empirically sound concepts" into health professions degree courses instead of induction, to provide a basis for further developing communicative competence.
