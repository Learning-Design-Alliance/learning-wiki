# Use PSOT at small institutions with one-on-one weekly content preparation sessions to improve or change LA-faculty working relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends PSOT as a tool for faculty at smaller institutions where weekly content preparation meetings are one-on-one, so new adoptees and experienced LA Program members can analyze their sessions and shift toward a desired partnership type. As printed: "Due to the nature of this study, PSOT is recommended to be used by faculty at smaller institutions which tend to have one-on-one weekly content preparation session meetings." Faculty can use PSOT trends to create opportunities for LAs to display content knowledge, contribute ideas, and take leadership roles.
