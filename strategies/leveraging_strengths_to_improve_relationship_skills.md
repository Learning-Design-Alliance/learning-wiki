# Leveraging Strengths to Improve Relationship Skills

## Description
This strategy focuses on identifying and supporting students' relationship skills by leveraging their existing strengths. Relationship skills encompass the ability to form and maintain healthy connections through effective communication, cooperation, and conflict management. By recognizing and harnessing a student's strengths in these areas, educators can more effectively address their challenges. This approach emphasizes that all students have varying degrees of strengths and challenges related to relationship skills, and that focusing on strengths can be particularly beneficial. Strategies include pairing students with strong role models, providing group assignments that highlight common interests, using visual aids to outline fair communication protocols, and incorporating literature or social stories to teach relationship skills explicitly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to have a deep understanding of their students' individual strengths and challenges, as well as a willingness to provide personalized support. Clear communication and consistent reinforcement are also essential.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort to identify and leverage students' strengths effectively. Some students may struggle to recognize their own strengths, requiring guidance and encouragement.
