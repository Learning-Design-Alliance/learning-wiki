# Integrate the PechaKucha technique into nursing education for concise, clear, less complex topics after evaluating content suitability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "the PechaKucha technique can be integrated into in-service training programs, diverse nursing courses, and activities aimed at summarizing comprehensive topics or raising awareness on specific issues." They pair this with a screening step: "it is crucial to carefully evaluate the suitability of instructional content for the PechaKucha format before implementation." The strategy is grounded in the trial's skill and satisfaction findings and bounded by the mixed prior literature on knowledge outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty review and approval of the presentation, visual materials aligned with the skill checklist, and pilot applications to test clarity and feasibility, as the article describes for its own presentation.
#### Constraints
- The article states the rapid pace may be insufficient for highly detailed and comprehensive content, and future work should ensure equal training durations between compared formats.

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- staff in in-service training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of concise procedural and psychomotor skills
- engagement with skill-based learning
