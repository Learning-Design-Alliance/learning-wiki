# Translate ready-made science outreach material into under-served languages using local professional astronomers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article notes that ready-made outreach material is plentiful in English but "likely scarce in the majority of languages", and that astronomers in countries where astronomy is little developed have little time to create original outreach. TUIMP addresses this by providing material written by professional astronomers "that can be translated and used for free in any region of the World", with translations done by professional astronomers or astronomy students to insure accuracy.
