# Adopt FSP-TCM as a feasible, practical, and cost-effective alternative to OSP-TCM simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using faculty members as standardized patients in TCM clinical skills training as an alternative to recruiting occupational standardized patients from the general public. The conclusion states FSP-TCM "possesses certain characteristics that render it feasible, practical, and cost-effective, and thus, it presents a viable alternative to OSP-TCM simulation", based on the trial's competence, feedback, and cost results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- faculty volunteers with 5+ years teaching experience, rigorous SP training with seasoned trainers, and certification through tests and performances before serving as FSPs
#### Constraints
- the study lacked follow-up on trainees' post-training clinical abilities, providing limited knowledge about post-training capabilities
- FSPs cannot fully represent the diversity of clinical patients

### Target Learners
- undergraduate TCM students in clinical skills training courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competence in TCM consultation, physical examination, syndrome differentiation, and doctor–patient communication
