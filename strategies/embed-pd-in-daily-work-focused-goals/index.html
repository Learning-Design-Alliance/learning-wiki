Embed professional development in daily school work through multiple mutually reinforcing conduits focused on a limited set of school-wide goals¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Several RSF schools stood out for intensive professional development addressing "a manageable set of key school-wide goals." Professional development was "embedded in the daily work of the school through multiple conduits, such as team planning led by instructional coaches, modeling and individual coaching, after-school professional development, and feedback following regular observations." One school scheduled afterschool professional development and collaborative time 4 days per week with a full-time literacy coach; another provided 4 weeks of pre-school professional development plus weekly workshops and intensive weekly coaching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructional leaders and coaches with time built into daily operations; the report notes most schools instead tried to address everything at once

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers in new K-8 schools serving low-income urban students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher instructional skill in literacy and mathematics, including teaching and assessing for mastery
