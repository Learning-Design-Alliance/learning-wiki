# Use a structured multi-tool assessment toolkit combining workplace-based observation and standardised examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 2025 ETR recommends a broader, structured set of assessment tools to evaluate trainees across knowledge, skills and professional behaviours. These include the Mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise (Mini-CEX) and Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS) for real-time clinical performance, Case-Based Discussions (CBD) for clinical reasoning, Multi-Source Feedback (MSF) or 360-degree assessment, and the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) with timed, objectively scored stations. Completion of accredited leadership and procedural courses is also encouraged.
