# Rank applicants with a coarse-grained admissions rubric combining GPA, recommendations, personal statement, and undergraduate institution

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author describes an admissions rubric his program is developing to replace overweighting of GRE scores. It ranks applicants on "a variety of factors we have determined to be useful in predicting research success: GPA, recommendations, personal statement, and undergraduate institution," noting liberal arts students have been very successful in his program. The strategy aims to reduce the unintentional weight GRE scores receive while maintaining qualifications, not to admit less qualified students for diversity's sake.
