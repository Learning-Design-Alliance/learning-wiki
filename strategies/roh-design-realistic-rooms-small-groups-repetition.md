# Design room of horror exercises with realistic room design, group sizes below four, and repetition with increasing complexity across the programme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback and the authors' conclusions, future room of horror designs should use realistic room sets, keep search groups small, and repeat the format as qualifications develop. Students "suggested incorporating RoH exercises throughout their studies to simulate increasingly complex situations" and recommended "smaller group sizes of fewer than four participants". The conclusion adds that a repetitive format aligned with evolving qualifications and potential interprofessional orientation would be desirable.
