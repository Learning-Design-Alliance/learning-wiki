# Align educational social media content with lecture timelines and schedule posts during high-engagement periods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Histo.Embryo account deliberately aligned posts with the timeline of histology and embryology lectures and practical sessions, and scheduled posts during high engagement times based on platform analytics. Posts were spaced out over the week to avoid overwhelming users. This strategy supports just-in-time learning by delivering targeted content when students need it for concurrent coursework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination between content creators and course schedules
- Access to platform analytics for timing optimization
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing faculty availability for regular content production

### Target Learners
- Preclinical medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely reinforcement of concurrent course content
- Spaced repetition
