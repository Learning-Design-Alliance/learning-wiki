# Use mined student factors to provide personalized teaching content and methods by group differences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools mine the main factors affecting achievement in a subject with a model like EGWO-MLP, then analyze learner characteristics to understand the learning environment, cognitive factors, and learning ability of individuals. On this basis, "teachers can provide personalized teaching content and method according to group differences and learners' characteristics", giving a basis for adjusting teaching strategies according to students' aptitudes. The method is said to be applicable to other subjects so students maintain a good learning state.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained achievement prediction model and analysis of learner characteristics (learning environment, cognitive factors, learning ability)
#### Constraints
- Prediction currently relies on structured data, which the article says makes it difficult to deeply and accurately mine learners' states and emotions, affecting prediction accuracy

### Target Learners
- secondary school students grouped by mined family and personal characteristics

### Target Learning Goals
- improving overall learning effect and maintaining a good learning state

### Affordances
- Egwo Mlp Student Achievement Prediction Model
