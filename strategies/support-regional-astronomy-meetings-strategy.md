# Develop and financially support regional astronomy associations and regularly held regional meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper recommends building regional astronomy groups and regular regional meetings, modeled on APS and AAPT sections, because low travel costs and timing make them accessible to PUI faculty and students who often cannot attend national meetings. Regional meetings "make these experiences more accessible for students at PUIs," offering student travel awards, research prizes, and small-meeting presentation opportunities, as exemplified by the 50-year-old Astronomical Society of New York with over 30 member institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regional meetings must satisfy a few basic AAS rules to receive official AAS endorsement; they are organized by regional organizations, not the AAS itself.
#### Constraints
- Financial support at many PUIs does not cover travel to national meetings, and the January AAS meeting timing conflicts with the start of the academic term for some faculty and undergraduates.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics and astronomy students at PUIs

### Target Learning Goals
- Professional development through attending and presenting original research at science meetings
