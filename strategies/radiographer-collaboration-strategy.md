# Collaborate with radiographers when junior doctors interpret plain trauma X-rays

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies enhanced collaboration with radiographers as a strategy to mitigate the risk of misinterpretations among junior doctors, noting that radiographers demonstrate high accuracy in interpreting plain trauma X-rays, sometimes outperforming junior doctors. Collaboration can take forms including the red dots system, open communication, preliminary clinical evaluation, and hot reporting by reporting radiographers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to experienced radiographers willing to collaborate on image interpretation
#### Constraints
- The review notes that the choice of collaboration form depends on the setting

### Target Learners
- Junior doctors in emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate interpretation of plain trauma X-rays
