# Develop OSCE preparatory curricula via Kern's six-step approach with a Delphi-defined goal and objectives stage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates implementing a preparatory curriculum by systematically working through Kern's six sequential steps: problem identification and general needs assessment, targeted needs assessment, goals and objectives, educational strategies, implementation, and evaluation and feedback. Goals were set by "a structured Delphi process ... to define the educational goals and objectives of the planned OSCE preparatory tool", anchoring design decisions in panel consensus rather than instructor preference alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A convened expert panel including educators and student representatives; alignment with national and institutional curricular standards
#### Constraints
- Development was tailored to the local curriculum at the University of Bonn, and existing e-learning platforms were rejected for lacking institutional specificity

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students preparing for an OSCE

### Target Learning Goals
- Uniform theoretical preparation and equitable exam readiness
