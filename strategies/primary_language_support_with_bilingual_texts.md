# Primary Language Support with Bilingual Texts

## Description
Using bilingual picture books and resources in a learner's primary language to support their comprehension and engagement with new content. This strategy leverages the learner's existing linguistic knowledge to scaffold understanding and facilitate exposure to English or other target languages. It can be implemented by incorporating bilingual texts in classroom libraries, using websites like the International Digital Children's Library, and providing access to digital books in multiple languages. This approach not only aids in language acquisition but also validates the learner's cultural and linguistic background.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to bilingual books, digital libraries, or online resources. Educators need to be aware of and value the linguistic diversity of their learners.
#### Constraints
- Availability of bilingual resources may be limited for certain languages. Educators need to ensure that the materials are high-quality and culturally appropriate.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Enhances comprehension by leveraging existing linguistic knowledge, increases engagement through culturally relevant materials, and supports language acquisition by providing exposure to the target language.

### Personalization
- Offer a variety of bilingual books and resources to cater to different languages and reading levels. Allow learners to choose texts that align with their interests and cultural backgrounds.
