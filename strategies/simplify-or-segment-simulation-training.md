# Simplify simulation models or divide training into smaller sections to maintain engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback that the multi-trauma, two-dentition session felt long, the authors recommend adjusting session structure. They write that "future simulation trainings may benefit from simplifying the models used or dividing the training into smaller, more manageable sections," so that motivation and engagement can be maintained throughout. This is a forward-looking design recommendation grounded in the satisfaction data, not a tested manipulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires satisfaction or feedback data to identify concentration or duration problems before restructuring
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single session covering four trauma types across two dentition periods; the adjustment itself was not tested in this trial

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in preclinical simulation training

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and concentration during hands-on trauma management practice
