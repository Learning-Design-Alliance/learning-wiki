# Involve faculty in student recruitment by discussing physics relevance to daily life and applications across fields

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that faculty take a direct, effective role in recruitment by talking with incoming students about physics. It specifies discussing the 'relevance of physics to daily life and its application to other fields such as computational physics, chemical physics, astrophysics, biophysics, and medical physics'. The authors state that if such steps are taken, 'enrollment will increase'.
