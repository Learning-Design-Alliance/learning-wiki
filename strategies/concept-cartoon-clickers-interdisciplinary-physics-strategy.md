## Description
The article recommends teaching interdisciplinary scaling concepts via concept cartoon clicker questions that prompt students to reason about scenarios where the surface area to volume ratio governs an outcome. The authors propose: "using concept cartoon clicker questions to enhance student understanding by encouraging student engagement", explicitly aligning with National Research Council recommendations that introductory physics emphasize active student participation. Each question follows the cartoon-clicker format: an illustrated scenario, multiple-choice options, and a correct answer explained in the caption.
