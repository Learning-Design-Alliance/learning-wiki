# Explore hybrid assessment models combining AI conversational agent-based and face-to-face formats, with long-term study of effects on performance and well-being

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that future work move beyond comparing purely AI-mediated and purely face-to-face assessment by developing and studying hybrid formats, and by examining longer-term consequences. It states: "Future research could explore the value of hybrid models that combine AI conversational agent-based and face-to-face assessment formats, and examine the long-term effects of AI-led assessments on student performance and well-being." This follows the authors' caution that benefits of AI-mediated assessment for attainment remain unestablished.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further investigation to ascertain whether AI-mediated assessment is ultimately beneficial for learning and attainment before large-scale adoption
#### Constraints
- The article notes AI-mediated interaction may discourage students from developing coping strategies for the discomfort authentic face-to-face assessments create

### Target Learners
- students undertaking performance-based assessments across educational levels

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing excessive assessment anxiety while maintaining valid assessment of competence and student well-being
