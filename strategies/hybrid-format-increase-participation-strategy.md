# Organize intensive physics schools in a hybrid format to increase participation when resources and logistics prevent full in-person attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report recommends organizing the ASP event in a hybrid format as a way to increase participation when resources and logistics do not allow for in-person participation of all selected candidates. The authors state that "Organizing ASP in hybrid format seems to be a way to increase participation" and commit to allocating more staff and equipment to make the hybrid format effective. ASP2022 itself was run as a hybrid event, with 62 of 157 survey respondents participating online.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- More resources, both in staff and equipment, are needed to make the hybrid format effective.
#### Constraints
- Online participants were less satisfied than in-person participants, and most expressed desire for in-person participation.

### Target Learners
- African university students
- High school teachers
- High school pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- Broadening access to training in fundamental and applied physics
