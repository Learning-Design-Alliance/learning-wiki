# Adopt Sim + LBL as a cost-effective, scalable approach for visually intensive, practice-oriented disciplines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend the Sim + LBL model for wider adoption in dermatology and venereology curricula and adaptation to other medical fields. They describe it as "a cost-effective, practical, and scalable approach for visually intensive and practice-oriented disciplines such as dermatology", noting that scenario-based simulation avoids the costly equipment and dedicated facilities of high-fidelity simulation, VR, and AR modalities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same teaching team, syllabus, instructional hours, and assessment instruments across compared groups
- Structured debriefing, Q&A sessions, and peer feedback after each simulation to enable timely correction of misconceptions
#### Constraints
- Supported by a single-site prospective cohort study with non-random assignment and short-term outcomes; the authors call for longitudinal follow-up with performance-based assessments such as OSCEs

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnostic skills
- clinical reasoning
- knowledge consolidation
- learning interest
