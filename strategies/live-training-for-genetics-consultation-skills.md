# Use live training to complement online genetics modules for performance-oriented consultation skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the online module's self-reported applicability of practice skills received neutral scores, the authors suggest that "A live training on oncogenetics may put more emphasis on these performance-oriented aspects reflected in increased consultation skills", such as recognizing patients with genetic disease, referring to a Clinical Genetics center, and explaining possibilities and limitations of genetic tests. This strategy pairs an accessible online knowledge module with face-to-face training targeting consultation behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online module to carry the knowledge content, freeing live sessions for performance-oriented skill practice
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as a suggestion; the performance-oriented aspects were not tested in this trial

### Target Learners
- General practitioners
- Primary-care physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognizing patients with genetic disease
- Referring appropriately to clinical genetics services
- Explaining possibilities and limitations of genetic tests
