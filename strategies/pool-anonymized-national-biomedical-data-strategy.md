# Pool anonymized institutional data nationally to build more robust and validatable predictive models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "biomedical schools with similar outcomes pool anonymized data together and test robust predictive models for admissions and curriculum review committees", envisioning a national de-identified database spanning medical, dental and pharmacy programs. Larger pooled datasets would be easier to validate due to large sample sizes, and demographic variables such as gender, age and biomedical background would be readily comparable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating schools must share de-identified data with similar outcome measures and comparable examination/milestone structures
#### Constraints
- The author acknowledges confounding variables could restrict use, proposing non-parametric analysis and other statistical tools to minimize differences in independent variables

### Target Learners
- medical, dental and pharmacy program applicants and matriculants

### Target Learning Goals
- admissions decisions, curriculum review, and remediation planning for at-risk students on national licensing exams
