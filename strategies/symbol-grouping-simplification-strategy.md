## Description
The article describes an instructional move in which the instructor grouped the constant factors in two drag-force equations into named constants A and B, letting the student see the solvable equation F=Av²=Bv. It concludes: "We need to demonstrate it and teach it explicitly. It is an important component skill for picking out functional dependencies."
