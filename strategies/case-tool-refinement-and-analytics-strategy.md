# Refine case-based formative assessment by enabling student-to-student feedback, increasing case complexity for high performers, and using completion analytics to flag underachieving students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations for improving the formative assessment tool. The authors state that "Other design of the resource that allowed obtaining student to student feedback would be probably preferable", acknowledge this "would be probably preferable... but also more time-consuming", suggest that "increasing the complexity of the proposed clinical cases, students with high performance might obtain benefit, too", and propose that analysing system use "may help to predict underachieving students" to plan corrective interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ability to record and analyse which students used the system and the answers they gave
#### Constraints
- Student-to-student feedback designs are described as more time-consuming than the immediate automated feedback used here

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students, including high previous performers and potentially underachieving students

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge acquisition in pathophysiology and early identification of students at risk of underachieving
