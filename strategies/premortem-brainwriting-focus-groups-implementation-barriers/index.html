Use premortem brainwriting focus groups early in intervention design to surface barriers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For designing implementable practices, the article recommends engaging community members in "premortem brainwriting focus groups (Gilmartin & Battaglia, 2023; VanGundy, 1984) to identify barriers and pre-implementation challenges that may arise within their specific context" so developers can anticipate collective barriers and facilitators early in planning. A cited illustration is Rabin et al. (2024), who asked participants to generate ideas for why a COVID-19 testing workflow would fail, producing over a dozen substantial changes.
