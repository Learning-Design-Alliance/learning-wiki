# Use weekly electronic reflection sheets to maintain dialogue and offer personalised support during school placements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module tutor collects eight weekly reflection sheets submitted electronically during the placement. These serve to keep in regular touch with students, engage them in dialogue about their experiences and learning, and allow personalised support; a small amount of academic credit encourages participation. The strategy pairs reflective dialogue with a mid-semester in-person workshop to promote community and reflect on practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A module tutor available to read and respond to submissions weekly
- A small amount of academic credit attached to submissions to encourage participation
#### Constraints
- Designed for a small-cohort placement module with limited places

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students on school placement

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on teaching experiences
- professional skill development
