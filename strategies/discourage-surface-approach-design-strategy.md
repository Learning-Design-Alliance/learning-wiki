# Advise students toward strategic study behaviors and design assessment that discourages surface approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (3 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that strategic approach scores were positively and surface approach scores negatively associated with grades, the authors recommend that "students with a desire for obtaining good grades ought to use strategic study behaviors and avoid using surface approach behaviors". For educators, they recommend organizing "curricula, assignments and exams in ways that discourage students from relying on a surface approach to studying", routinely assessing students' study behaviors, and targeting surface-inclined students for support programs on how to study successfully.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruments or routines to assess students' study behaviors so surface-inclined students can be identified
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on cross-sectional associations; the design does not allow causal interpretations

### Target Learners
- first-year higher education students, particularly occupational therapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving exam grades and academic performance
