# Expand the package with documents on helium atom microscopy, time-of-flight methods and general surface science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' stated future work is to broaden the package's topical coverage: they "hope that documents pertaining to helium atom microscopy and time-of-flight methods can be added to the package, along with documents aimed to serve more general surface science". They also aim to submit an updated publication during late 2021 after internal checking and updating.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A process for internally checking and updating the package before rerelease as a higher quality set of documents
#### Constraints
- The checking process is anticipated to take a significant length of time because the package is approximately 300 pages in length

### Target Learners
- new researchers entering helium atom scattering and surface science

### Target Learning Goals
- helium atom microscopy methods
- time-of-flight methods
- general surface science
