# Use simulated newborn resuscitation on an anatomical model as a practical way to assess provider skills and identify weaknesses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study assessed every selected provider's resuscitation skills by observing performance on an anatomical model with a 39-item checklist, concluding that "simulations with an anatomical model are a practical way to assess newborn resuscitation skills and identify providers' weaknesses." This approach avoids waiting for real cases to occur and can be used in facility assessments and continuing education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An anatomical model and a structured skills checklist; assessors trained with tested intra- and inter-assessor reliability
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that "a clinical simulation with anatomical models may not reflect providers' actual performance on the job"

### Target Learners
- Skilled birth attendants (doctors, midwives, other health professionals)

### Target Learning Goals
- Newborn resuscitation clinical skills and identification of performance weaknesses
