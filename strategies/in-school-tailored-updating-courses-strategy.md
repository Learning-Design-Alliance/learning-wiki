# Reach in-service teachers through updating courses held in their own school and tailored to the school context, with management support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that updating courses are almost the only way to reach in-service teachers, "better if held in their school", and that such courses "can be designed taking in account of the context" of the school where they are realized. Because updating is compulsory for Italian in-service teachers (6 hours every year), teachers have an interest in courses genuinely useful for their teaching practice. The authors state that strong school management support is crucial for obtaining wider teacher involvement, and their most satisfactory school-based course produced a detailed set of learning paths covering the first year of a reformed scientific high school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Compulsory annual updating (6 hours every year) gives in-service teachers a reason to attend genuinely useful courses
#### Constraints
- Strong school management support is needed for wider teacher involvement; courses with weak or absent support were rated unsatisfactory in the article's own cases

### Target Learners
- in-service secondary physics and mathematics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving teaching of physics and mathematics
- designing laboratory learning paths for reformed curricula
