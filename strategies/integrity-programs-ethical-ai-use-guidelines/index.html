Institutional integrity programs combining ethical AI-use guidelines, clear definitions of dishonesty, and risk-awareness education¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that universities "implement educational programs to establish ethical guidelines for its responsible use, heightening the awareness of the potential risks involved with the use of AI tools." Programs should define dishonest behaviors clearly and detail penalties, since an environment of unclear rules fosters unethical behavior. Educators are advised to add anti-plagiarism measures, adapt assessment, and follow four formative lines of action: conversations about plagiarism, teaching citing and referencing skills, feedback opportunities, and formative and summative assessment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Clear institutional definitions of dishonest behaviors and stated penalties
  • Educator engagement in formative integrity practices (conversations, citation instruction, feedback, assessment)

Constraints¶

  • The article notes universities and society are still formulating clear guidelines on appropriate and inappropriate uses of AI tools

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate university students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic integrity
  • responsible and ethical use of AI tools such as ChatGPT

Affordances¶

  • Rct Chatgpt Usage Decisions

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop clear institutional GenAI policies, communicate them, raise awareness, and promote digital literacy
  • Build ethical AI integration on four coordinated levels: faculty professional development, ethics curricula, clear institutional guidelines, and interdisciplinary updating
  • Prepare teachers to use AI expeditiously and provide AI-text detectors for evaluation, alongside updated academic-integrity rules
  • Position AI detection tools as one component within a broader institutional decision-making process, with regularly updated benchmarks
  • Combine psychologically informed, ethically grounded, and institutionally supported interventions to reduce AI-assisted academic dishonesty
