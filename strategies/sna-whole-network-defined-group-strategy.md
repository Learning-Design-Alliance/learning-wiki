# Use SNA at the whole-network level to study all ties within a defined group

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy applies SNA at the level of whole networks: the evaluator measures and maps 'all of the ties within a defined group,' such as a program's participants or partner organizations. Mapping the complete tie structure within a bounded group lets evaluators describe how relationships are organized across the group as a whole.
