# Small-group student-constructed reference products such as a Philosophy Dictionary

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Under objective A, the guide's first suggested activity has students build their own glossary of the ideas behind the cultural movements. As printed: "Workirri in smallgrows, make a Philosophy Dictionary of terns useful to understanding philosophical, scientific,andsocio-econcmic concepts related to 19th and 20thcentury cultural now:merits." Suggested entries range from free will and determinism to dialectic materialism and metaphysics, grounding later interpretive activities in shared vocabulary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small-group organization and reference sources for defining philosophical terms
#### Constraints
- The activity serves objective A, which presupposes philosophical, scientific, and socio-economic theories as background

### Target Learners
- secondary school students working in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- commanding philosophical and scientific vocabulary needed to interpret cultural movements
