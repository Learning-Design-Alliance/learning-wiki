# Build authentic school-family partnerships to reinforce students' social and emotional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The page recommends that schools form authentic partnerships with families and caregivers, who bring deep expertise about their children's development, culture, and learning needs. It states, "Research suggests that evidence-based SEL programs are more effective when they extend into the home." Recommended avenues include two-way communication, helping caregivers understand child development, volunteering opportunities, and extending learning activities into homes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Inclusive decision-making processes that ensure families, particularly those from historically marginalized groups, are part of planning, implementing, and continuously improving SEL
#### Constraints
- Partnerships are more likely when schools' norms, values, and cultural representations reflect families' own experiences

### Target Learners
- children and young people in school, and their families and caregivers

### Target Learning Goals
- social and emotional development reinforced across home and school
