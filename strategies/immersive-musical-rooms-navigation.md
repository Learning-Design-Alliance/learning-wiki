# Immersive navigation inside musical rooms for active genre listening

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A teaching strategy in which students are immersed in music performances of different genres while navigating inside several musical rooms, using mobile VR. The article states this can "improve the music learning experience in primary education, in terms of active listening, attention, and time." The strategy was used in synergy with traditional teaching methodologies rather than replacing them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mobile VR technology accessible to students
- Musical rooms with genre-specific performances
#### Constraints
- Used in synergy with traditional teaching methodologies, not as a replacement

### Target Learners
- primary education students

### Target Learning Goals
- active listening
- music genre learning
