# Acceptance, Responsibility, and Sharing

## Description
This strategy involves accepting what happened after processing emotions, taking responsibility for any wrong actions, and sharing feelings and experiences with others involved. If the relationship with the other person is important, it requires having the courage to talk to them without accusation or excuses, simply sharing how one feels and what one experienced. This includes being open to receiving the other person's sadness and anger, understanding that their feelings matter too. If resolution isn't possible, accepting that outcome while still learning from the situation is crucial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courage to be vulnerable, willingness to take responsibility, and the ability to communicate openly and respectfully.
#### Constraints
- Requires willingness from both parties to engage. Resolution may not always be possible.
