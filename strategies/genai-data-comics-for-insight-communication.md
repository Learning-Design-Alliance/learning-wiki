# Use GenAI-co-created data comics to communicate data insights to novice students, with human oversight to address misinformation and bias risks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends exploring data comics, co-created with GenAI, as a scalable way to communicate data insights in education, since GenAI addresses the production bottleneck that has limited systematic evaluation. The authors conclude their findings "highlight the potential of data comics as a potentially effective tool for data communication in education", while stressing that ethical concerns about AI-assisted creation must be addressed before deployment in authentic settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Human oversight of GenAI-generated content for credibility and validity
- Carefully designed prompts and diverse, well-curated datasets to reduce bias
- Transparency about the role AI plays in imagery production
#### Constraints
- Students raised concerns about misinformation, bias, ownership and reduced trustworthiness
- Comics may overload readers and suit complex multi-dimensional comparisons less well than conventional visualisations

### Target Learners
- novice university students
- students with limited visualisation literacy

### Target Learning Goals
- information retrieval from data visualisations
- comprehension of data insights
