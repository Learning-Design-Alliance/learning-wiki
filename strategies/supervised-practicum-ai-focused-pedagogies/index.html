Expand supervised practicum experiences in teacher preparation to include AI-focused pedagogies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that teacher education programs expand supervised practicum experiences so teacher candidates learn AI-focused pedagogies: scaffolding students' iterative interrogation of AI responses, examining AI-generated content for bias and hallucination before classroom introduction, and documenting pedagogical decision-making about when AI use is appropriate. This responds to the identified gap that current preparation programs lack frameworks and strategies for developing practical pedagogical repertoires for AI-mediated classrooms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher educators with the capability to model verification processes and make real-time decisions about when students should engage AI versus develop unassisted capability

Constraints¶

  • The article frames this as a recommendation derived from expert scenarios, not a tested intervention

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • scaffolding student interrogation of AI outputs
  • critical evaluation of AI-generated content
  • judging when to engage or bypass AI assistance

Related Strategies¶

  • Use the simulated class as low-stakes deliberate practice placed late in teacher preparation, with transcript-based debriefs
  • Design AI-related teacher professional development as practice-oriented, case-based and connected to teachers' real pedagogical decisions
