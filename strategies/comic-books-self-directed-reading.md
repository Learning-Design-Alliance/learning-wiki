# Include illustrated comic books in self-directed reading schemes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that avid language learners consider illustrated comic books in their self-directed and self-selected reading lists. As the author states, "Students, by reading comic materials will be able to get a sense of pleasure which will, in turn, increase their interest and reading ability." This strategy leverages pleasurable reading to build reading habits and comprehension outside the classroom.
