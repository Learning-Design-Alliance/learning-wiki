# Supplement preclinical operative dentistry live demonstrations with procedure-specific educational videos delivered through an E-learning platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy blends a procedure-specific educational video (produced by the same instructor as the live demonstration to ensure "uniformity in learning experiences for all students") with the routine live lab demonstration, delivered via an E-learning tool such as Blackboard. Students watch the video before the evaluation session, allowing them to "revise the procedural steps before, during, and after the skill lab session as per the students' convenience" and to replay the procedure repeatedly. In the trial, video access was restricted to the intervention group and Blackboard tracking ensured a minimum of five to six views.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The video should describe identical steps to the live demonstration and be produced by the same faculty member to avoid information differences
- Access control and statistical tracking through the E-learning platform to ensure students view the video multiple times
#### Constraints
- Educational videos can only be used as adjunct tools, not as alternative tools for the learning process
- The most significant limitation of E-learning tools is the lack of demonstrator interaction
- Since the study was conducted on a single cohort from one speciality of dentistry, results are not easily generalized to other branches of dentistry

### Target Learners
- undergraduate second-year dental students in preclinical operative dentistry courses

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor preclinical skill competency in class I cavity preparation and amalgam restoration
