# Offer participation credit and provide multiple reminders (in class and by email) when administering low-stakes assessments online out of class

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends four instructor practices for motivating students to complete online out-of-class assessments: "1. multiple email reminders, 2. multiple in-class announcements, 3. participation credit for the pretest, and 4. participation credit for the posttest". When faculty followed all four, CBT participation matched PPT rates. The study modeled these practices as a cumulative recommended-practices count (0 to 4) that predicted CBT participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must implement the recommended practices, modeled as a count from zero to four, to raise CBT participation
#### Constraints
- The practices were modeled for CBTs only because they focused on improving participation on the CBTs

### Target Learners
- college introductory physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- maximizing student participation in low-stakes online assessments
