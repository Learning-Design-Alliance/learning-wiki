Use log data from digital assessment to reconstruct solution processes and identify validity threats, disengagement, and technical obstacles¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends analyzing log data capturing how an assessment was completed, including navigation between items, help access, answer order, and time measures. Log data can reveal validity threats such as repeated construction and deletion of the same arc (hinting at technical problems), repeated selection and de-selection of checkboxes (showing distractor influence), and rapid-guessing or low-engagement behavior identified via answer times.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Relevant sequences have to be identified in order to make full use of the log data

Constraints¶

  • The reconstruction of the solution process does not give unambiguous insights into students' thinking

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 5–7 students who struggle in mathematics

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Deep conceptual understanding of abstract concepts
