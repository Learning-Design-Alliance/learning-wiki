# Write out abbreviations and specify technological context in survey questions to ensure consistent respondent interpretation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that survey designers replace ambiguous abbreviations and broad technological terms with explicit, user-friendly language. Students were uncertain whether "VP" referred to the virtual patient or the platform, and whether "navigate" meant platform movement or something else. The authors note that "refining technological terminology into more user-friendliness language and avoiding unexplained abbreviations can substantially enhance clarity and promote more consistent interpretation among respondents."
