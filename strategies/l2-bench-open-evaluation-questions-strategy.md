# Evaluation frameworks for AI feedback should address cross-cultural validity, uncertainty communication, and multi-turn interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors raise three open questions for evaluation developers: how to evaluate AI systems' ability to adapt feedback styles to diverse learner populations in global English teaching contexts; how AI systems should communicate uncertainty when multiple formulations are acceptable or diagnostic uncertainty is high; and how to account for multi-turn interaction, which they call an essential condition for evaluating pedagogical efficacy and socioaffective risk.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluation criteria are needed that distinguish pedagogically appropriate expressions of uncertainty from evasiveness or overconfidence
#### Constraints
- The authors note no AI for education development or evaluation team appears to have made much progress on multi-turn evaluation

### Target Learners
- diverse learner populations in global English language teaching contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- pedagogically appropriate AI feedback across cultures, uncertainty situations, and extended dialogue
