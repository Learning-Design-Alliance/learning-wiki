Design PjBL projects so students must evaluate, critique, and improve AI-generated outputs rather than prohibiting AI use¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on feedback that students increasingly use tools such as ChatGPT during project work, the authors recommend designing projects so that students must evaluate, critique, and improve AI-generated outputs rather than prohibiting such tools. Projects were revised to emphasize justification of decisions, comparison of alternative solutions, debugging, and assessments that reward reasoning and verification. The strategy treats AI assistance as an design opportunity to promote deep understanding rather than a threat to be banned.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Assessments that reward reasoning and verification
  • Project tasks requiring justification of decisions and comparison of alternative solutions

Constraints¶

  • AI tools may also support instructors in drafting materials, but these still require instructor review for technical correctness and alignment with learning objectives

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate computing students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • deep understanding, debugging and verification skills, higher-order evaluation of AI-generated solutions

Related Strategies¶

  • Engage students in active learning assignments using generative AI with structured reflection and critique of AI outputs
  • Embed AI as a learning partner and redesign assessments for AI-rich contexts
