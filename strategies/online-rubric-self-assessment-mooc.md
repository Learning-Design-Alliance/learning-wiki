# Use an online self-assessment rubric to evaluate MOOC learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article evaluates its MOOC with an online rubric of 11 items describing the attitudinal, conceptual and procedural competencies students should achieve, scored 0–3 points per competence for a maximum of 33 points. The authors state that "the use of the rubric that we suggested is a useful tool to assess the MOOC", aligning with systematic reviews recommending guided peer review with rubrics for MOOC quality assessment. Experimental-group participants scored a mean of 21 (SD = 6.88) on the rubric.

## Design Implications
