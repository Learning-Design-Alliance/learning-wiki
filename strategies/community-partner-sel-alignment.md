# Align school and community partner SEL efforts through common language and coordinated communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The page recommends integrating SEL across the school day and out-of-school time by aligning with community partners. It notes community partners provide safe, developmentally rich settings and are trusted by families, and states that "school staff and community partners should align on common language and coordinate strategies and communication around SEL-related efforts and initiatives."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School staff and community partners must align on common language and coordinate strategies and communication
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on partners' trust with families and understanding of community needs and assets

### Target Learners
- young people participating in school and out-of-school-time programs

### Target Learning Goals
- practice of social and emotional skills in personally relevant settings
