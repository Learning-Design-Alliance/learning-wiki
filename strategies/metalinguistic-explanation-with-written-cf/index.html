Provide clear, simple metalinguistic explanations with rules and examples alongside written corrective feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q1–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's pedagogical implications point to the correction methodologies of the Bitchener and Knoch studies, in which direct CF was paired with written and/or oral metalinguistic explanation. The article quotes their recommendation that "the provision of clear, simple meta-linguistic explanation, namely, explanation of rule(s) with example(s), is the best type of written CF for long-term accuracy".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Explanations should be clear and simple, stating the rule with examples

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation comes from studies limited to referential 'a' and 'the' and the meta-analysis included only ten studies with a potential confounding variable of four studies by the same principal researcher

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate and advanced L2 writing students in university settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • long-term accuracy in targeted grammatical forms

Claims¶

  • On the immediate posttest of explicit L2 knowledge, direct CF and metalinguistic CF with rule explanations outperformed the control group while metalinguistic code CF did not. [+W]
  • On the immediate posttest of implicit L2 knowledge, direct CF and metalinguistic CF with rule explanations outperformed the control group while metalinguistic code CF did not. [+W]
  • Metalinguistic written CF was equivalent, not superior, to non-metalinguistic direct CF, contrasting with previous studies whose metalinguistic CF also contained correct forms. [+W]
  • Most students judge written corrective feedback alone insufficient and want accompanying metalinguistic explanation [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use metalinguistic naught CF (rule explanations without error marking) to ease teachers' corrective feedback workload while retaining long-term effectiveness.
