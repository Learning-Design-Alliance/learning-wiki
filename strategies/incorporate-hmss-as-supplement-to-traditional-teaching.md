# Incorporate the HMSS into health management teaching curricula as a supplement to traditional teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "health management educators could incorporate the HMSS into their teaching curriculum," positioning the simulation as "a supplement to traditional teaching methods and activities." The course integrates all health management courses and implements the entire health management process, addressing gaps left by lectures and textbook reading and by short internships that cannot cover long-term chronic disease management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online simulation platform covering the full health management service process
- Small-group arrangements with teacher-organized debriefing
#### Constraints
- Transfer of knowledge from the simulator to the clinical setting with actual patients is always a challenge

### Target Learners
- undergraduate health management students before direct contact with patients

### Target Learning Goals
- practicing health management skills, self-directed learning, and preparation for professional careers
