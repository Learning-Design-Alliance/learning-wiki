# Strategy-detail-verify educator review of generated representation variants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends structuring instructor review of each generated card representation in three levels, adapted from SPHERE's strategy-detail-verify workflow. "For strategy, the system explains why a particular representation was generated"; at the detail level the educator inspects the specific generated content; at the verification level the educator approves, edits, or rejects the variant. The generated variant is surfaced alongside the original semantic fields so the educator can check whether it preserves the learning objective, introduces factual or conceptual errors, and remains appropriate for target learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must be able to preview and edit representations; the system remains an authoring assistant, not an autonomous teacher
#### Constraints
- Dedicated interface design for multi-representation authoring remains largely unexplored, per the authors

### Target Learners
- Non-technical educators reviewing AI-generated content

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining content accuracy and pedagogical alignment of AI-generated representations
