Present contextual mathematics problems in the local cultural context¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend grounding the model's contextual problems in students' local culture so mathematics is learned in a real context. The article states that "Contextual problems can be presented to the local cultural context that allows students to learn mathematics in a real context", building on the investigation phase's analysis of students' cultural background and on cited work that contextual problems engage and motivate students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Problems presented in varied forms (pictures, stories, videos) that enable critical, analytical and creative thinking

Constraints¶

  • Recommended for classroom mathematics learning; tested only in the Grade VIII geometry implementation reported here

Target Learners¶

  • Grade VIII junior high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • connecting mathematics to real and cultural contexts; higher-order thinking

Claims¶

  • Experts judged the contextual mathematical thinking learning model valid, with an average evaluation score of 4.23 [+W]
  • Students taught with the contextual mathematical thinking learning model showed significantly higher enhancement of higher-order thinking ability than the control group [+W]
