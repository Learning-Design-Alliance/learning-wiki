# Use a modular lesson structure to keep integrated AI curricula scalable under time constraints

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because AI is not typically in standard curricula, the article recommends that "it becomes crucial for educators to consider scalability in the design of such curricula". The study adopted a modular approach in which each lesson was a module placed after students learned the relevant biology knowledge. This structure let the curriculum adapt to varying educational contexts and time limitations while core objectives were still met, for example when Lesson 2 reflections were cut short yet its core objectives were met.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each lesson designed as a self-contained module sequenced after the relevant subject knowledge is learned
#### Constraints
- Educators must balance incorporating AI topics with state-mandated standards, which can challenge coverage of all intended integrated topics

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy integration within existing subject curricula
