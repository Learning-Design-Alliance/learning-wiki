# Think Time

## Description
Allow plenty of think-time and time for re-reading for students who might need more time to process. Thinking takes time. Avoid penalizing learners who need more time to process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Readily available think time and an instructor that reinforces it.
#### Constraints
- Students may not be self-aware enough to ask for more think time. It is on the instructor to encourage it.
