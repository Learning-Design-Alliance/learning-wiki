# Adopt FSN with implementation adherence fidelity of at least 75%

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The synthesis concludes that adopters can expect substantial impact on student problem behavior and social skills provided the program is implemented with sufficient fidelity. The review states "those who adopt FSN can be confident that FSN will have a substantial impact on student PBs and SS, so long as it is implemented with adherence fidelity of at least 75%." Fidelity was monitored in the trials using an implementation fidelity checklist assessing adherence and quality of implementation on three to four occasions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fidelity monitoring using an implementation fidelity checklist, with expert raters collecting data on three to four occasions covering the coach phase and the teacher phase.
#### Constraints
- The review notes impact is likely more robust in the school versus the home setting, and expectations for school-personnel delivery should align with the smaller effectiveness-trial effects.

### Target Learners
- pre-K through Grade 2 students at risk of school failure due to challenging behaviors

### Target Learning Goals
- reduce problem behaviors
- increase prosocial behavior and academic engaged time
