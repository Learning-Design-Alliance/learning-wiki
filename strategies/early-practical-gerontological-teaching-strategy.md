# Incorporate practical gerontological nursing teaching early in the curriculum, using situational simulation and case-based teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that gerontological nursing programs place practical teaching early so students attain sufficient clinical competence, and that educators and clinical instructors collaborate on integrating gerontological nursing content into curricula. They state students "may improve their competence in addressing real-world nursing problems through the combination of situational simulation training and case-based teaching", particularly for prevalent health issues among older people.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between educators and clinical instructors; curricula with room for gerontological nursing content and practice opportunities
#### Constraints
- The article's evidence is cross-sectional and self-reported; it calls for longitudinal and educational intervention research to verify different educational strategies

### Target Learners
- First- and second-year nursing students in higher vocational colleges

### Target Learning Goals
- Gerontological nursing practice ability and clinical competence with older people
