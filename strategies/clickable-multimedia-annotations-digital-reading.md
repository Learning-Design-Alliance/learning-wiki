# Embed clickable multimedia annotations in digital reading texts on a learning platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates embedding highlighted target words in a digital reading text hosted on a Moodle site, where clicking a word displays its annotation (definition, picture, or video) in a display area beside the text. This design keeps annotations proximate to the reading, minimises interruption of reading flow, and attracts learner attention. Teachers can replicate this by hosting annotated texts on a course platform and linking difficult words to multimedia explanations.
