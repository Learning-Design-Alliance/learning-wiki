# Design e-learning for chronic disease management with clinically-relevant scenarios that translate knowing into doing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that educators "need to be aware of the need for e-learning resources to explicitly translate content knowledge (i.e. ‘knowing’) into skills-based competencies using clinically-relevant scenarios (i.e. ‘doing’)". Students in the study wanted videos of physiotherapy consultations, interactive case studies where they could practice developing a treatment plan and review best-practice examples, and practical examples of interprofessional roles and referral. This bridges the know-do gap the authors identify for learners lacking experiential learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interactive and downloadable resources that are readily updatable and alignable with curriculum changes
#### Constraints
- Implementation of interactive strategies such as blogs needs pragmatic balancing against resourcing for moderation and maintenance, especially at scale

### Target Learners
- Pre-licensure health professional students

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical clinical skills for chronic disease management
- Interprofessional practice and referral decision-making
