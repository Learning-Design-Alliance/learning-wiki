# Provide ongoing, in-depth faculty professional development that targets peer discussion as an essential component of clicker use.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because faculty often modify research-based pedagogies, the article recommends moving beyond one-time basics sessions: "faculty need ongoing, in-depth professional development and support." Professional development should ask participants how they already use clickers, present research findings on peer discussion, and engage them in identifying question features that encourage productive discussion. At the University of Maine this takes the form of a yearlong program with rotating pods of three faculty who teach, observe with COPUS, and give feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple opportunities for faculty to learn about using peer discussion with clickers over the long term.
#### Constraints
- Onetime faculty professional development workshops have a limited capacity to create change.

### Target Learners
- university STEM faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- effective clicker implementation with peer discussion
