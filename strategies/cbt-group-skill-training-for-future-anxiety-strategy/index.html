Use CBT-based psycho-educational group skill training to help university students cope with future and trait anxiety¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends delivering CBT-based coping skills through structured psycho-educational groups for university students, since "FA and TA are common facts for competitive societies and cognitive-behavioral interventions can help people cope more effectively." The recipe combines cognitive restructuring, hypothesis testing with group support, acceptance skills for uncontrollable variables, relaxation methods, and homework across eight sessions, with the aim that students "can be more productive during their life and improve their psychological health."
