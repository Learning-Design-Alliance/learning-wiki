# Have staff familiar with the students and their classroom experiences approve rosters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that roster approval be carried out by staff who know the students and their classroom experiences, rather than by staff without such knowledge. This grounds verification of teacher-student links in firsthand classroom knowledge, supporting accurate attribution of students to teachers of record before evaluation results are computed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must have staff with direct knowledge of students and their classroom experiences available to review rosters.
