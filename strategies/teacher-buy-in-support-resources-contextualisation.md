# Secure teacher buy-in for large-scale pedagogy trials through management support, standardized resources and freedom to contextualise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes that teacher buy-in was enhanced when three elements were in place: teachers "were well supported by school management", were provided with and used clear standardized resources and pedagogies, and after training "had the freedom to contextualize resources for their classroom within the pedagogic guidelines". It suggests co-construction in standardized resources during an RCT gives teachers a sense of ownership required for systematic buy-in.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior leaders appointed as programme leads to oversee and support staff
- Standardized teacher manual and resources
- Training before delivery
#### Constraints
- The trial design sacrificed some standardization for realism, expected to result in natural variability in delivery

### Target Learners
- teachers delivering structured co-operative learning programmes in secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- implementation fidelity of co-operative learning programmes
