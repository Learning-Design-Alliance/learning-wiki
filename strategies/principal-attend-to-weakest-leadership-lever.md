# Principals should attend to all three contingency levers, especially the weakest, position power

## Description
Based on the measurement model, the article recommends that principals attend to the leadership aspect with the smallest contribution. It states the measurement model "can be used as an illustration that the principal needs to pay attention to the aspect of the position of the leader that has the smallest contribution", including the leader's strength in directing and evaluating followers and authority to reward and punish. It also recommends leaders influence followers through legitimate authority, role modeling, goal setting, rewarding and punishing, restructuring, and communicating a vision.
