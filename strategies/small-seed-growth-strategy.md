# Start network growth from small-scale seeds using tested inquiry materials rather than large-scale projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends initiating the network through small seeds: a group of interested teachers first earns funds and studies in the existing network, then drafts vision, mission and business plan statements before opening a small school. The author states "Life begins in small seeds, not big scale projects" and that the first step for interested teams is "studying a bit of basic physics by inquiry using specially designed and well tested materials."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to specially designed and well tested inquiry materials, and contact with a local network subsystem that delegates a specialist to help identify goals
#### Constraints
- The full financing scheme via tax forms is not yet implementable, so only a seed or initial business plan is offered for practice today

### Target Learners
- teams of teachers and researchers
- students in newly formed schools

### Target Learning Goals
- learning basic physics by inquiry
- building a sustainable school
