# Model and Example Feedback

## Description
This approach involves communicating with learners the purpose for an assessment and/or student feedback. Demonstrate to students what you are looking for by giving them an example of what an A+ paper looks like. Provide a contrast of what a C- paper looks like. This is especially important at the upper learning levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-quality models and examples, clear explanations
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of models and examples, may be challenging for learners to transfer knowledge to new situations
