# Practise prompt engineering through simulations, prompt-a-thons and scenario exercises to obtain accurate LLM outputs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that physicians develop LLM use expertise by deliberately practising prompt construction and iteration with colleagues. It states that 'physicians need to construct specific, contextualised instructions (or prompts) that enable the chatbot tool to generate the most nuanced, relevant and factually correct output, with reduced risk of 'hallucinations''. Suggested formats include simulations or 'prompt-a-thons' and scenario exercises in which physicians iterate prompts and learn which tactics produce the most precise and informative outputs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to sandbox or simulated LLM environments
- Collegial collaboration to compare prompt tactics
#### Constraints
- Physicians must also understand the limitations and errors of LLMs; prompts reduce but do not eliminate hallucination risk

### Target Learners
- practising physicians
- medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- effective prompt design and querying of LLMs
- reducing hallucination risk in LLM outputs
