Provide training and jargon-free resources so council members can make informed contributions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because many parents and practitioners are unfamiliar with educational policy, shared governance, or group decision-making, the brief recommends capacity-building supports. It advises that "Organizations and schools should offer training sessions on relevant topics and provide accessible, jargon-free resources to ensure parents and practitioners can make informed contributions." This strategy treats member knowledge as a precondition for meaningful participation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training sessions on educational policies, shared governance structures, and group decision-making processes
  • Accessible, jargon-free resources for parents and practitioners

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • parents and caregivers on parent councils
  • family engagement practitioners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • understanding educational policy
  • group decision-making skills
  • shared governance literacy

Related Strategies¶

  • Maintain transparent, ongoing communication through consistent schedules, agendas, and multiple channels
  • Remove participation barriers and use broad outreach to ensure fair representation on parent councils
  • Regularly evaluate parent councils through surveys and feedback, supported by a family engagement coordinator
  • Establish Group Norms
