# Racial Equity in PBL - Focus Projects on Issues of Justice

## Description
Authentic projects that address issues of justice lead to higher student engagement because students are doing work that matters, work that impacts their community, their lives, the lives of those they love and deeply care about.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum that allows for exploration of issues of justice; facilitator who is comfortable discussing these topics.
#### Constraints
- May require more preparation to address sensitive topics; facilitator must be prepared to handle differing perspectives.
