# Offer open-choice creative extra-credit projects in which students weave their interests and hobbies with physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable teaching activity: offer an extra-credit assignment inviting students to choose their own approach and medium for connecting physics with art, humanities, social sciences, and everyday life. The article encourages instructors to "incorporate these types of extra-credit projects in their courses to help bring out student creativity", and notes students chose their approach and produced stories, poems, drawings, sculptures and video shorts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An extra-credit grade incentive so all students take the task seriously.
#### Constraints
- The showcased example predates generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, and students could choose their approach only if it wasn't computer-generated.

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students in courses geared towards bioscience, health-related majors and non-science majors

### Target Learning Goals
- creativity and critical thinking
- connecting physics concepts learned to everyday life
- deepening physics knowledge
