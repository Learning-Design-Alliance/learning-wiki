# Scaffold method choice by student progression: CBL early, transitioning to PBL and TBL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a "scaffolded integration of these methods": utilizing "Case-Based Learning (CBL) in early undergraduate years to introduce clinical contexts in a structured, low-stakes manner." As students progress from novice to advanced beginner, curricula can "transition to PBL and TBL to challenge students with higher-level ambiguity and complex group dynamics", aligning pedagogical complexity with students' developmental trajectory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula must sequence methods to match students' developmental trajectory from novice to advanced beginner
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is based on the authors' synthesis of comparative effects rather than a direct test of sequenced curricula

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students progressing through program years

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical context acquisition
- problem-solving abilities
- communication skills
