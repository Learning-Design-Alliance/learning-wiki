# Embed recurrent, context-aligned ALS rehearsal with feedback supported by protected training time, accessible infrastructure, and ongoing performance monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review recommends treating ALS competence maintenance as an ongoing workplace capability rather than episodic recertification. Critical care programmes should embed recurrent rehearsal with feedback, prioritise high-consequence actions, and reinforce role clarity, leadership, and closed-loop communication, supported by organisational conditions. The review states: "Critical care programmes should embed recurrent rehearsal with feedback, prioritise high consequence actions and reinforce role clarity, leadership and closed loop communication."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organisational support including protected time, governance, standardised roles, audit with feedback, and accessible training infrastructure
#### Constraints
- Psychological readiness may be included within broader programmes but should not be presented as a proven standalone mechanism

### Target Learners
- physicians and nurses in hospital-based critical care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of ALS competence including knowledge, psychomotor skills, and non-technical team behaviours

### Affordances
- Three Domain Als Competence Maintenance Framework
