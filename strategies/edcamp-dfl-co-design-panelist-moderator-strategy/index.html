Co-design professional learning with educators as panelists and moderators¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The series invited educators during registration to volunteer as panelists or breakout moderators, then prepared selected volunteers in a pre-Edcamp background building session with a content expert, resources, guiding questions, and facilitation tips. The report states this "created the roles of panelist and moderator to support this goal" of centering practitioner voice in sharing learning sciences principles, and the post-survey showed at least 90 percent agreement that the roles increased expertise and confidence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Volunteer recruitment during registration
  • A background building session with a content expert before the event
  • Moderation techniques and time to explore topic resources

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated in a pandemic-era online format with experienced educators (more than 75 percent with over 10 years in the profession)

Target Learners¶

  • volunteer educators serving as panelists and moderators, and their peer participants

Target Learning Goals¶

  • teacher leadership development and practitioner-centered sharing of research-based practices
