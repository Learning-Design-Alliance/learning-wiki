## Description
The article recommends investing in faculty pedagogical enhancement rather than temporary student workshops, arguing that developing a core cohort of competent DT instructors yields a sustainable institutional multiplier effect. The authors write that "Directing development funds toward faculty pedagogical enhancement yields a more sustainable institutional 'multiplier effect' than funding temporary, standalone student workshops", ensuring long-term educational continuity and scalable curriculum reform.
