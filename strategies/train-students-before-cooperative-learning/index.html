Train students in cooperative learning before applying cooperative techniques¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the null result, the author recommends that "techniques bas ed on cooperative learning should not be applied unless the students are well trained about cooperative learning approach." Citing Panitz (2006) via Karakoyun (2010), the article states full conversion from conventional to cooperative learning cannot be realized unless students are trained, and that Jigsaw I will not solve grammar-teaching problems on its own unless students are equipped with various social skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must be equipped with social skills and trained in the cooperative learning approach before techniques like Jigsaw I are used

Constraints¶

  • The article also cautions that conventional approaches should not be ignored all the time and may be appropriate for some subjects in some schools

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students accustomed to teacher-centered teaching

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Turkish grammar and other subjects taught cooperatively

Claims¶

  • Jigsaw I produced no significant difference in attitudes toward the Turkish course compared with conventional teaching [+W]
  • Jigsaw I produced no significant difference in grammar success scores compared with conventional teacher-centered teaching in grade-8 Turkish grammar [+W]
  • Prior Turkish research overwhelmingly reports positive achievement effects of cooperative learning techniques, in contrast to this study's null result [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Extend Jigsaw research to other grammar topics, language skills, and cooperative techniques
  • Introduce prompt-engineering training before students use Gen AI for structured self-study techniques
  • Implement TGT game-based cooperative learning in after-school programs with adequately trained teachers and extended treatment duration
  • Adopt cooperative TGT as an alternative mathematics teaching approach, supported by teacher training and ministry guidelines
  • Implementation safeguards when applying jigsaw in laboratory courses
