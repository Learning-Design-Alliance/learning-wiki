# Identifying and Supporting Learners with Relationship Skills

## Description
Identifying and supporting learners who have learning strengths or challenges related to relationship skills helps them improve and succeed. Relationship skills involve the ability to form and maintain healthy connections with others by communicating, cooperating, and managing conflict effectively. Relationship skills also require the capability to resist inappropriate social pressure and offer or seek help when appropriate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor must identify each learners' strengths and support them effectively.
#### Constraints
- This can be difficult for learners who have serious relationship challenges.

### Affordances
- Focusing on learners' strengths helps them build relationship skills and succeed socially and academically.

### Personalization
- Strategies include pairing learners with others who model strong relationship skills, providing group assignments where learners can find common ground, display anchor charts outlining fair communication protocols for class discussions, and use literature or social stories to explicitly teach relationship skills.
