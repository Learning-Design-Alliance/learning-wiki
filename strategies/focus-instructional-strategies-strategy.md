# Research-based instructional strategies: jigsaw, discovery-based learning, CRA manipulatives, and technology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
FOCUS lesson plans deploy jigsaw grouping, discovery-based learning, the Concrete-Representational-Abstract (CRA) sequence with manipulatives, and technology. The article reports that "The instructional strategies used in teaching these three areas included jigsaw (Aronson & Patnoe, 2011), discovery-based learning and the use of manipulatives—Concrete to Representational to Abstract (CRA; The Access Center, 2004)—and technology". In jigsaw activities, students master a topic in expert groups then return to teach their home groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination of lesson plans across the credit-bearing, developmental, and learning support courses
#### Constraints
- Strategies were implemented within the FOCUS program's coordinated curriculum at one university

### Target Learners
- Developmental mathematics and College Algebra students

### Target Learning Goals
- Multiplying binomial expressions and other algebra skills with conceptual understanding
- Understanding continuous and discontinuous functions
