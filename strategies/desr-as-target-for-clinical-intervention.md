# Treat DESR symptoms as an important focus of clinical intervention in adults with ADHD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that impairing emotional symptoms in adults with ADHD be identified and addressed clinically, arguing that DESR's association with lower quality of life and worse social adjustment makes it "an important focus of clinical intervention." It notes preliminary pharmacotherapy evidence and calls for psychosocial treatment studies to determine whether such treatments reduce DESR symptoms. The recommendation rests on the study's own functional-impairment findings rather than on a tested intervention in this article.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- identification of DESR symptoms in adults with ADHD, for example via the eight-item self-report measure used here
#### Constraints
- evidence that DESR is treatment-responsive is preliminary and drawn from prior clinical trials, not tested in this study
