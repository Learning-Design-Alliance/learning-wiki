# Minimal feasible components for a simulation quality assurance program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
For programs that cannot support a full two-person QA team, the authors propose a reduced quality assurance package drawn from their trial experience. It consists of "a group feedback session after most actors have performed in at least 10 simulations and individualized feedback on a random sample of 10% of all simulation performances" evaluated by a dedicated reviewer throughout a study or project, with the package tailored to each program's situation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated reviewer to evaluate a random sample of simulation performances throughout the study or project
#### Constraints
- The authors frame these as minimal components based on their experience and recommend designing a feasible QA program tailored to each program's specific situation

### Target Learners
- Standardized patient actors in simulation programs
- Clinician learners participating in simulations

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining consistent, standardized actor performances across a simulation-based educational program or trial
