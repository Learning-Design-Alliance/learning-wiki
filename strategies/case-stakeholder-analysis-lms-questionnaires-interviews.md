## Description
As the next phase of the research, the authors plan to "employ stakeholder analysis using a case study approach to investigate stakeholders in higher education", conducting "Questionnaires and interviews" with key stakeholders to determine their roles, level of importance, and interactions in the LMS implementation process. This operationalizes the three-step stakeholder analysis method with concrete data-collection instruments at a single institution.
