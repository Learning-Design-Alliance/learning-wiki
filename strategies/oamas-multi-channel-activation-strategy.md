# Multi-channel activation of students before and during class

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends activating students' bodies, emotions, and cognition through engaging, content-related activities in which all students participate, rather than relying on pre-class previews and class tests that "may cause students anxiety and distraction." Concrete moves include role-play of clinical reality, character stories and news reports to build empathy, positive clinical cases to activate responsibility and confidence, and clinical micro-videos with thematic discussions and brainstorming on the learning platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activation tools closely related to the learning content
- Participation by all students
#### Constraints
- The article warns that in pure hybrid teaching students may quickly get answers by intelligent equipment, weakening interest

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in blended courses

### Target Learning Goals
- learning interest and enthusiasm
- classroom engagement
- cognitive activation
