## Description
INFN_KIDS is a line of intervention opened to reach "(mostly) 8-12 years old, a public difficult to reach and that cannot be addressed with usual means". The article says INFN is developing "a wide variety of activities, from podcast to comics, from hands-on activities (when they can be performed) to online games (like the Advent Calendar for Christmas 2021)", plus series. The line also took part in Lucca Comics, a four-day fair attracting about 300,000 people.
