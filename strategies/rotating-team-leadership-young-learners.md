# Rotate team leadership among young learners to build speaking and communication skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the camp's team-based activities, leadership roles were rotated among students. The article reports that "Students also liked the idea of rotating team leadership between them as it gives an opportunity to improve their speaking skills and communication." This is a concrete, implementable classroom activity for cultivating communication and leadership within collaborative hands-on projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Team-structured classroom and homework activities in which each student can take a leadership turn
#### Constraints
- Observed with five students aged 8-10; leadership skills received the weakest grades in the cohort

### Target Learners
- Early childhood learners aged 7-10 working in teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral communication and speaking skills
- Leadership skills
