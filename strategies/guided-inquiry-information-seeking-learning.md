# Guided Inquiry: immersing students in information seeking as a way of learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Guided Inquiry is a programme the author is developing for librarians and teachers of elementary and secondary school students. It "immerses students in information seeking as a way of learning and prepares them for the active engagement with information required in all aspects of living and working in the information society". It applies many concepts from library and information science research as basic strategies for lifelong learning.
