# Maintain attendance by holding workshops at retreats and framing each as both curriculum step and entry point

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their attendance analysis, the authors recommend two actions for future workshops: "we will continue to oﬀer a workshop at the retreat for CU-Prime leaders," and they will frame each workshop "both as a part of a broader curriculum and as an acceptable entry point for newcomers to the curriculum." This strategy follows directly from the observation that the retreat-based Workshop 4 and the catch-up-framed Workshop 6 drew the highest attendance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing multi-day organizational retreat and an ongoing workshop sequence to which newcomers can be introduced
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from attendance patterns in one student organization and the authors note workshop agendas may not be widely applicable beyond their local context

### Target Learners
- Volunteer student leaders with many demands on their time

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained repeated exposure to diversity-related curriculum
