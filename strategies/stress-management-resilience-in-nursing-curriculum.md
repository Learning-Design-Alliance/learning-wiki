# Integrate structured stress-management and resilience-building supports into undergraduate nursing education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that stressors reduce readiness for clinical practice, the authors recommend that educators and policymakers ensure a well-designed and organized educational process in which novice nursing students can manage stress and connect theory with clinical practice. They state that "Structured stress-management training and resilience-building strategies should be integrated into undergraduate nursing education," naming concrete components and placement options.
