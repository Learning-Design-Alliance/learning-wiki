# Integrate 3D-printed biomimetic phantoms into VR/MR environments to create a 'see–feel–do' training continuum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes integrating the 3D-printed simulator into virtual and mixed reality platforms to establish "a 'see–feel–do' continuum, reinforcing tactile practice on elastic, patient-derived tissues with immersive visual analytics and unlimited virtual rehearsal." This builds on evidence that VR simulators can serve as equal first-step platforms for psychomotor skill acquisition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR/MR training platform infrastructure
- Sensor-equipped printed phantom for automated feedback
#### Constraints
- The cited VR and MR evidence comes from other studies and procedures, not from direct testing of this simulator in immersive environments

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- postgraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor skill acquisition
- sensory function development
- surgical motor skills
