# Cross-match course goals with student activities and graded items to assess goal-activity alignment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors analyzed alignment by matching instructors' selected course goals against the activities students participate in and the items used for grading. "We then determined whether instructors are connecting their course goals with the activities students participate in and in the ways they are evaluated in the course." Matching was performed by two authors plus an outside researcher who coded independently and then reached agreement, before counting how many chosen activities and graded items matched each goal. This provides an implementable recipe for auditing constructive alignment in lab courses.
