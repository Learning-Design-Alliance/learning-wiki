# Have students explain module content to younger students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After completing the modules, students take on the task of 'telling younger students about the contents of this and other modules'. The authors report that this was quite a success and state 'We can recommend this approach', making peer explanation of module content a recommended feature of the Route.Net course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must first have followed the modules themselves
- An audience of younger students must be available
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on the authors' experience with one cohort of ten students

### Target Learners
- secondary school students who have completed Route.Net modules

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidate understanding of module content by explaining it to younger students

## Claims

- [A second cohort given a peer-teaching task appeared to understand the module better, and the authors recommend the approach](../claims/second-cohort-peer-teaching-better-understanding.md) [+W]
