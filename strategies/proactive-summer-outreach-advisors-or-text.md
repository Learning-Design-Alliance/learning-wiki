# Proactive summer outreach via advisors or personalized text messages to college-intending graduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools and access programs provide proactive summer outreach to college-intending students offering assistance in completing college financing and informational tasks, delivered by high school or access program counselors, by digital text message, or both. Evidence indicates being proactive with students is critical to connecting them to summer support. At minimum, schools can produce personalized summer pre-enrollment task worksheets; costlier options include intensive advising, which raised enrollment five percentage points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Active outreach rather than passive availability of help, since only 2% of informed control students sought support
#### Constraints
- Texting showed no impact in Boston where college planning supports were already concentrated

### Target Learners
- college-intending low-income high school graduates

### Target Learning Goals
- completing college financing and informational tasks and matriculating on time
