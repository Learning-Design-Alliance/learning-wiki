# Create a relaxed, worry-free discussion environment with student-chosen topics and rotating team leadership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe for oral-skill sessions: seat students facing one another so the teacher is not the focus, allow cultural comfort items, let students choose weekly topics and distribute roles, and rotate the team-leader position so every student leads. The article states "The topic for the each week was chosen by the students so there would be no compulsion or force on the students’ freedom."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors who encourage English use rather than Arabic and prepare guiding materials and keyword lists in advance
#### Constraints
- Without deliberate time allocation, some students may dominate the conversation and speaking time may not be distributed among group members

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate EFL students with low speaking confidence

### Target Learning Goals
- Willingness to communicate orally in English
- Self-confidence in speaking
