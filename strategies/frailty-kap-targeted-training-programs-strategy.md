# Equip clinical nurses with frailty knowledge and skills through practical continuing-education programs, operation manuals, and targeted training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (10 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications for clinical practice call for targeted training built on the KAP relationships it found. It recommends that clinical nurses "need to be equipped with appropriate knowledge and skills and their positive attitudes need to be enhanced", that the lack of knowledge and practice scores be addressed "in a practical continuing education program and a corresponding operation manual", and that researchers develop localized implementation programs based on evidence-based guidelines. Hospital administrators are asked to support this through training, optimized procedures, and resource allocation.
