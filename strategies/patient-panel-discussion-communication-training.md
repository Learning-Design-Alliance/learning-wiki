# Include a patient panel discussion to ground communication training in patient experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 1-hour panel with community members who experienced receiving bad news (e.g., pregnancy loss, cancer diagnoses) lets learners ask questions about the impact of physician communication. The panel "provided a concrete experience in which learners heard from the real-life experiences of patients", completing the experiential learning cycle.
