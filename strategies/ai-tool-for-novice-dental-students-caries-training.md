# Use AI-based caries detection applications as educational tools for dental students with limited clinical experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating AI-based web applications into dental curricula as one e-learning option for training bitewing caries detection, concluding that "the AI-based web application could serve as an educational tool for caries detection in bitewings, especially for dental students with limited clinical experience." The recommendation rests on the trial showing AI training produced improvements comparable to a prerecorded lecture and an annotated dataset, with gains across all confusion-matrix categories.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evidence-based integration into curricula should maintain educational quality and patient safety, per the article's framing
#### Constraints
- Improvement outcomes with the AI tool were more variable across individuals than with the other methods
- The study compared AI training only against these two e-learning methods, with no no-training control group

### Target Learners
- dental students, particularly preclinical students

### Target Learning Goals
- bitewing caries detection skill
