# Create instructional environments that trigger and stabilize the productive aspects of students' existing epistemological views rather than confronting and replacing problematic beliefs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because epistemological stances are nuanced and context-sensitive, the authors recommend that "instructors can create instructional environment that tend to trigger and stabilize the more productive aspects of the epistemological views that student already has." This avoids the deficit trap of substituting an epistemological deficit for a concepts deficit. It builds on seeds of expertise such as Jim's recognition that common sense connects to formalism in some circumstances.

## Design Implications
