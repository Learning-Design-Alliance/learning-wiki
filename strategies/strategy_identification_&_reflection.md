# Strategy Identification & Reflection

## Description
This strategy involves having students actively identify and reflect on the strategies they use to overcome challenges. The instructor checks in with learners to help them name their process, and then add to a list of strategies that students are using and, if they are struggling, help students name a specific process. By providing language to match what learners do, they have a strategy they could use in other times of struggle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor must provide prompts and check-ins to help students name their process. Requires a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require significant time and effort from the instructor. Some students may struggle to articulate their strategies.
