Use task-based activities as the core unit of planning and instruction for grammar teaching in EFL classes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that teachers replace traditional product-based grammar instruction with tasks as the core unit of planning, since its results "suggest that task-based language teaching can be used in English classes to develop grammar ability among Iranian EFL learners." The strategy involves designing activities and tasks similar to real contexts that foster motivation and conscious attention while covering the grammatical points of the course program.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tasks aligned with the ongoing school program and the grammatical points to be covered
  • Attention to the learners' proficiency level, which the article calls the main factor the teacher should not ignore

Constraints¶

  • Evidence comes from one quasi-experiment with 80 female pre-intermediate students in Ahvaz, Iran, over 12 sessions

Target Learners¶

  • EFL junior high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Grammar learning
  • Motivation toward English

Affordances¶

  • Tblt Communicative Task Framework

Claims¶

  • TBLT produced higher grammar posttest scores than traditional instruction among 80 female pre-intermediate EFL students at one Iranian junior high school [+W]
  • TBLT increased EFL junior high students' motivation significantly more than traditional instruction [+W]
  • The experimental group's grammar scores rose significantly from pretest to posttest under TBLT [+W]
  • The control group's grammar scores also rose significantly from pretest to posttest under traditional instruction [~W]
  • The control group's motivation also increased significantly from pretest to posttest of the questionnaire [~W]
  • The experimental and control groups did not differ in motivation or grammar at pretest [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Supplement task-based language teaching with grammar instruction and use communicative drills for automatization
  • In same-language EFL classes, use intervals of the students' native language with jocular comparisons to explicate difficult grammar points
  • Use group interaction, peer evaluation and shared productions in task-based grammar teaching
