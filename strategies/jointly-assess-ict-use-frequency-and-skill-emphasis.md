# Assess frequency of ICT use and emphasis on digital skills jointly to capture quantitative and qualitative aspects of ICT integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that research on ICT integration assess ICT-use frequency and TEDDICS together, because "the two constructs are empirically distinct" — one quantitative, one qualitative. Joint assessment yields detailed information on different aspects of teachers' ICT use that either measure alone would miss. This follows from the finding that correlations, though high, are not perfect.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use of validated measures of both ICT-use frequency and TEDDICS in the same study
#### Constraints
- Correlations between the two are high but not perfect, so neither substitutes for the other

### Target Learners
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- ICT integration in classroom practice
