# Culminate RAI training by having practitioners create and peer-review their own case studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A capstone strategy in which practitioners become creators and assessors of case studies drawn from personal and professional experience. They design real-world AI scenarios, describe systems with controversial effects on diverse stakeholders, build assessment matrices, identify benefits, harms, tensions, and mitigation strategies, and conduct peer reviews. The article states this "empowered practitioners to apply RAI principles in practice and simulate the assessment of real-world case studies" they may encounter in daily work, and builds confidence and communication skills.
