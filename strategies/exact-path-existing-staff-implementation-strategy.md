# Implement Exact Path with existing district staff supported by vendor professional development and standard technology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program is designed for implementation without new hires or additional technology: districts use their current teachers and leaders with guidance from Edmentum's Customer Success Managers, and the Professional Services Team offers "dedicated professional development focused on how to elevate instructional practices". The software "operates on all commonly used devices and browsers in school districts" and requires no additional applications or devices, lowering the implementation burden for adopting schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Edmentum's Customer Success Managers and Professional Services Team for guidance and professional development.
- Districts use current teachers and leaders rather than hiring additional staff.
- Professional development is delivered by Edmentum's Professional Services Team in alliance with subject matter coaches and professionals.
- Access to Edmentum's Customer Success Managers and the offered professional development opportunities for continuous learning.
#### Constraints
- The strategy is specific to the Exact Path program and its vendor-provided support structure; the summary does not report effects of the professional development itself.
- Runs on all commonly used district devices and browsers without additional technology applications or devices.

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in district implementations
- K-12 learners in implementing districts

### Target Learning Goals
- ELA and Mathematics achievement through personalized instruction and formative assessment
- Accelerating and reporting student achievement in Math and English Language Arts standards
- Mathematics and English Language Arts standards achievement
- Mathematics achievement
- English Language Arts achievement
