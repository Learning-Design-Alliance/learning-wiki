# Targeted national education strategy for GPs on evidence-based imaging indicators, WAD treatment and early psychological referral

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends continuing education for GPs on road traffic crash injury care, stating that "a national strategy may be required to raise awareness amongst Australian GPs of evidence-based indicators for imaging referral following neck injury". It further suggests clinical training on evidence-based WAD treatment and on the benefits of early psychological referral for patients presenting with PTSD and pain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education delivery must overcome low uptake: an online program that improved WAD guideline knowledge found "the uptake of the program was relatively low".
#### Constraints
- Self-reported training interest may reflect response bias, as "high proportions of respondents selected positive options (e.g., I am very likely to attend further training)".

### Target Learners
- General practitioners in Australia

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based diagnosis and management of whiplash associated disorders and PTSD following road traffic crashes
