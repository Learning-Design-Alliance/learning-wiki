# Provide an ongoing remote image-uploading gateway so experts can continue supporting trained non-cardiologists after formal training ends

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the formal training period, the study team established a pathway for continued expert oversight: "a gateway for image uploading was provided", allowing the cardiology fellow to keep observing echocardiograms obtained in Ghana and to help the imagers with questions or concerns, particularly images outside their scope of practice. This extends supervision beyond the structured curriculum.

#### Requirements
