# Link earned micro-credentials to permanent base salary increases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's second incentive strategy is a compensation structure connecting salary advancement to earned micro-credentials, piloted by Kettle Moraine School District with unanimous school board approval in spring 2014 and implemented in 2015-16. The model "awards a permanent base salary increase, ranging from $200 to $600, depending on the difficulty of each earned micro-credential." Educators first seek pre-approval with a written rationale, then collect evidence artifacts, then submit proof to Human Resources. KMSD deliberately chose permanent increases over one-time stipends and designed the model so teachers who do not earn micro-credentials are not penalized.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-approval process with written rationale tied to district mission and student impact, coordinated between Human Resources and Teaching and Learning offices
#### Constraints
- The model was designed to be flexible and not penalize teachers who did not earn micro-credentials

### Target Learners
- District teachers eligible for salary advancement

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuous and reflective educator learning and competency-based practice
