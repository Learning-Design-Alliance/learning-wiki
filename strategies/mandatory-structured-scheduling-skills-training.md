# Schedule practical skills training as mandatory, structured sessions rather than voluntary participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that practical skills training sessions be scheduled and made obligatory, because residents' busy schedules mean voluntary training does not take place. Residents and program directors "stressed structured scheduling of the training sessions as an important condition for successful implementation of the D-UPS program", and the discussion cites Chang and colleagues' finding that voluntary use of a surgical simulation laboratory resulted in minimal participation in the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Protected scheduled time within the working week
- Obligatory rather than voluntary attendance
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence on voluntary participation comes from a cited surgical simulation study (Chang et al.), not from data collected in this article

### Target Learners
- surgical and urology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- practical and procedural clinical skills
