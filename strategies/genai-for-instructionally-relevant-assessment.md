# Use generative AI to make assessment systems more instructionally relevant, not to reify problematic practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends harnessing generative AI for reimagining assessment systems rather than reinforcing harmful practices. It states: "GenAI should be used to make assessment systems more instructionally relevant, not to reinforce practices that have the potential to cause harm to students and their learning." Instead of using large-language models to generate superficial multiple-choice items, developers could use the technology to customize deeper learning tasks to local contexts and operationalize asset-based scoring and reporting of performance assessments at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional relevance and equity considerations as first-order principles when deciding whether and how to incorporate genAI
#### Constraints
- The report frames this as a near-future possibility contingent on current efforts, not an established result

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in state assessment systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Authentic and sophisticated assessment at scale
- Asset-based scoring and reporting of performance assessments
