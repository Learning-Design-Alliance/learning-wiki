# Use the interactive debriefing interface to valorize hunts: itineraries, safety discussion, alternatives and learner self-explanation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After each hunt, the trainer sees each team's total time on a single timeline, hunters' paths drawn on floor maps and screenshots taken during the session, all synchronized with a movable timeline cursor. The interface is deliberately a tool leaving liberty to the trainer, displayed in the classroom to focus on pedagogically relevant elements. The authors anticipate uses such as discussing itineraries and their safety implications, suggesting alternatives with additional constraints, asking learners to explain their own choices, comparing sessions with the same objective, and evaluating long-term progression across learners' careers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Screenshots and itineraries recorded during the hunt
- a trainer desktop client with the debriefing interface
#### Constraints
- Designed to be as little restrictive as possible so trainers define the pedagogical valorization themselves

### Target Learners
- professional trainees in industrial facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective review of navigation choices
- safety awareness in facility interventions
