# Milestones for implementing a longitudinal undergraduate US curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete milestone list the authors recommend for faculties moving toward longitudinal US education. It includes training peer-student-tutors or faculty "to supply hands-on scanning time for small groups (maximum of six medical students per group)", ensuring specialist supervision when examining human models, letting specialists rotate between study groups, supplying a learning objective catalogue, evaluating courses, and introducing standardized assessment such as OSCE or DOPS.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Networking with the university hospital, medical faculty, dean's office, skillslabs, student initiatives and national US societies.
#### Constraints
- The authors state the competency level in national learning objective catalogues would need adjusting from 'knows how' to 'shows how'.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- hands-on ultrasound scanning skills and standardized assessment of US competence
