# Teaching three kinds of comprehension questions with a question-chart during text discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During comprehension training, teachers read aloud parts of texts beyond students' decoding skill and discussed them, making explicit three question types: "(1) the answer is right there in the text; (2) you may ﬁnd the answer if you put different segments of the text together; and (3) the answer is not in the text so you have to attend to your prior knowledge." These three points were printed on a chart, whose reverse listed who, where, which, what and why questions to support story retelling. Teachers asked questions before, during and after reading to prompt reflection.
