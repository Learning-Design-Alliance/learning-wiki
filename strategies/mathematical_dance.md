# Mathematical Dance

## Description
Mathematical dance involves using movement to illustrate mathematical concepts. This approach can demonstrate links between different shapes and illustrate complex ideas. For instance, a dance can be created to show how two consecutive triangular numbers make a square number. It combines mathematics and performance to teach an audience complex concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mathematical concept, choreography, performers, and an audience
#### Constraints
- Requires expertise in both mathematics and dance, may be time-consuming to choreograph
