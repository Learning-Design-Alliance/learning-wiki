# Schedule rapid, repeated peer evaluation cycles so students receive feedback before the next project deliverable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To make peer evaluation formative rather than functionally summative, the evaluation process should be rescheduled so that cycles of work, peer evaluation, and feedback occur rapidly. In the treatment semester, this rapid succession gave students the ability to receive feedback prior to engaging in the next main deliverable, and two additional formative evaluations were spaced apart so students could review and discuss results before the next iteration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The evaluation process must be timed so feedback reaches students before the next project component begins.
#### Constraints
- When survey administration and feedback span at least two weeks and overlap the next project component, students cannot learn from results in time, making the evaluation functionally summative.

### Target Learners
- university students in team-based project courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improved team performance through timely feedback

## Claims

- [Formative peer evaluation improves students' perceptions of the learning climate and competence, but not doubt, compared to summative-only peer evaluation.](../claims/formative-peer-evaluation-improves-perceptions.md) [+W]
- [Formative peer evaluation cycles improve student performance on final project components and course grades compared to summative-only peer evaluation.](../claims/formative-peer-evaluation-improves-performance.md) [+W]
