# Divide collected measurement data into sets so different student groups get different statistical tasks in the computer lab

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After data are collected by the Geiger detector and downloaded into a computer, the teacher divides the data into sets in order to give different tasks to any group of students in the computer lab. This lets one shared measurement campaign feed multiple parallel analyses, such as comparing samples of different sizes against a very long measure of more than 1000 data.
