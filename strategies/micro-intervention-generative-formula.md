# Use the generative formula to craft new micro-interventions: observe a cue, connect it to a deeper need, and bridge with a reframe plus a tiny prescribed action

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Rather than memorizing scripts, instructors develop a generative capacity using the formula: "When you observe [STUDENT BEHAVIOR/EMOTIONAL CUES], connect it to the [DEEPER HUMAN OR PROFESSIONAL NEED], using a bridge that [REFrames & PRESCRIBES a tiny action]." Worked examples include reframing a sigh in a stats lab as "the sound of a hard-working brain" and prescribing writing "the worst possible first sentence" to defeat blank-page paralysis.
