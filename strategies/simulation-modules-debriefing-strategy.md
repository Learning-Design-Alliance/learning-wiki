# Embed simulation-based scenarios into the curriculum as structured learning modules with guided debriefings and reflective exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that advanced simulation-based scenarios be embedded into the curriculum as structured learning modules, allowing students to engage in realistic, high-pressure situations requiring effective communication, decision-making and role-based leadership. They further recommend that 'These simulations should be accompanied by guided debriefings and reflective exercises to reinforce learning outcomes.' This strategy is offered as a way to support the integration and development of leadership and teamwork skills across training levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation scenarios must require effective communication, decision-making and role-based leadership.
#### Constraints
- The authors propose a longitudinal validation plan to assess the sustained impact of these interventions over time, indicating that sustained effects have not yet been established.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- leadership development
- teamwork development
- communication and decision-making under pressure
