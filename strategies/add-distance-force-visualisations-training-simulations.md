# Add distance and force visualisations to accessible clipping simulations, adaptable to similar navigation simulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their evaluation, the authors recommend enhancing accessible surgical training simulations with distance and force visualisations: semi-transparent rays support clip navigation while a colour map supports assessing different clipping methods. They note that "the distance visualisation is not limited to cerebral aneurysms and can be easily adapted to similar simulations, including navigation", and propose refinements such as a NASA TLX scale in further expert evaluations, more clip models, and adding aneurysm exposure to the workflow.
