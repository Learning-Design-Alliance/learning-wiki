# Reinforce the algorithmic component for computer science students and deepen the technological component for electrical engineering students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The author's forward-looking recommendation distinguishes audiences: "for computer science students it would be good to reinforce the algorithmic component, while for electrical engineering students, it is important to deepen the technological component". For both groups, the article stresses understanding the restrictions imposed by NISQ technology, in which non-error-corrected qubits implement quantum algorithms, and awareness of the longer-term effort of building larger-scale error-corrected quantum computers.
