# Case study method combined with project method for developing independent cognitive activity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the practical stage the authors used "an innovative Case study method in teaching Informatics combining it with other methods". Students independently analysed a formulated problem situation, searched for solutions, then shared results in small groups, received feedback, and presented optimal solutions. The method of projects was "the leading method of performing independent work", with projects running in parallel with laboratory classes and incorporating interdisciplinary multimedia content.
