# Prioritize authentic engagement with experts and career exploration to develop informed career goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding implication is that educators "should prioritize providing opportunities for authentic engagement with experts and career exploration in QIS to help students develop clearer and more informed career goals." In the Pathways program this took the form of field trips to quantum labs, hearing scientists describe their research and career paths, and policy visits, which helped students adjust expectations and plan next steps. Incorporating QIS into high school STEM curricula could help bridge the knowledge gap and spark interest.
