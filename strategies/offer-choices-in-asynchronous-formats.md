# Offer learners more choices and interactive features within asynchronous online formats to raise motivation and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators "design learning environments that offer learners more choices, even in asynchronous formats, to increase motivation and engagement." Because the webinar's motivation advantage was fully mediated by perceived choice, asynchronous courses should add interactive and choice-rich features, such as discussion forums, Q&A sessions and gamification elements, while keeping a balance between autonomy and guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Asynchronous learning platforms should incorporate interactive features, such as discussion forums, Q&A sessions and gamification elements.
#### Constraints
- Maintain a balance between autonomy and guidance to avoid overwhelming learners with too many choices or a lack of direction.

### Target Learners
- paramedics completing mandatory further training
- online learners in continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- intrinsic motivation and engagement in online learning
