# Sustain the workshop by expanding to biannual sessions run through a medical student organization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is to institutionalize the workshop: "we are expanding the program to include biannual workshops for all undergraduate levels" in the BS/MD program, with medical students facilitating "through a student organization at the Medical College of Georgia, ensuring long-term sustainability and evolving case content as students progress." This makes student facilitation a standing, peer-run structure rather than a one-off event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guidance from the Medical Student Affairs Office and a student organization to host facilitation
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single small cohort; the authors note the small cohort size and subjective self-reported data as limitations

### Target Learners
- all undergraduate levels of a BS/MD program

### Target Learning Goals
- ongoing preparation for medical school expectations and CBL competencies
