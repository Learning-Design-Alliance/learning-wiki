# Use the GROW model (Goal, Reality, Options, Will) to structure coaching conversations at the start and end of clinical teaching sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The GROW model structures coaching conversations in four phases: Goal establishes the wanted outcome, Reality clarifies the current situation, Options shifts to a solution-focussed conversation, and Will determines commitment to action. The article sets out how to apply it at the start of an endodontic session (clarifying goals, self-assessment of prior similar successes, planning for complications and anxiety) and at the end (self-assessment against agreed criteria, commitment to next steps).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Endodontic clinical supervisors familiar with coaching techniques
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as the most straightforward way to introduce coaching, within a broader proposed model

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and postgraduate endodontic students in clinical or pre-clinical sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- Specific clinical skills such as access cavities
- Self-assessment, awareness and accountability for learning
