# Discuss mathematical concepts with students rather than telling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The synthesis emphasizes discussing rather than telling mathematical concepts, with teachers facilitating classroom discourse so students take on most academic thinking. Teachers used turn and talks, think-pair-shares, purposeful questioning, ample wait time, and "why" questions to facilitate dialogic discourse, which aids assessment by letting teachers learn from in-the-moment evidence of student thinking.
