# Background Knowledge Activation

## Description
Before engaging in text-based discussions, instructors activate learners' background knowledge related to the text's setting and themes. This involves asking open-ended questions to prompt learners to connect their prior experiences and knowledge to the new content. Activating prior knowledge helps learners relate to the material, improving comprehension and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires knowledge of learners' backgrounds and thoughtful development of open-ended questions.
#### Constraints
- Learners may struggle to connect if questions are not relevant. Misconceptions can be activated if background knowledge is inaccurate.
