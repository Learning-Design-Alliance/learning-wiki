# Develop targeted, competency-based interprofessional continuing education programs and European frameworks for geriatric care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends designing continuing professional development (CPD) around the empirically identified high-need topics and building on interprofessional education (IPE) standards. It states that "targeted competency-based cross-professional educational initiatives and frameworks are needed to strengthen geriatric care and support workforce mobility across Europe", and points to the US-IPEC Framework and the Irish HSE Framework as best-practice examples for future educational offers. A European interprofessional educational framework addressing regional training needs is a stated goal of the PROGRAMMING COST Action 21122.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content selection should follow empirically identified educational needs, here the Z-score analysis showing highest need in dementia management, depression, chronic pain, resilience and diversity, MCI/dementia screening and sarcopenia
#### Constraints
- Given the large proportion of medical doctors in the DACH subsample, interprofessional generalizations should be cautious

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals working with older people across professions in the DACH region and Europe

### Target Learning Goals
- Geriatric competencies including dementia management, depression, chronic pain, cognitive screening, sarcopenia, and interprofessional communication
