## Description
The authors' future-study recommendation: beyond PGY trainees and supervisors, "the DOPS is also used for the fieldwork education of senior OT students and their clinical supervisors", and "future studies may investigate the user experiences of the senior OT students and their clinical supervisors in fieldwork education". This extends the survey approach used in this study to a new population.
