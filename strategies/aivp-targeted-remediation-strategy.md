# Use AI virtual patients as targeted remediation adjuncts for students needing structured OSCE support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (5 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "AIVP appears most effective as an exam-aligned tool supporting cognitive and procedural skill development, particularly in remediation contexts." The strategy is to identify students requiring additional structured preparation (as the MEET cohort was identified by a senior emergency physician) and provide independent AIVP access with exam-aligned scenarios and immediate feedback mapped to OSCE criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students identified as requiring additional support to achieve the expected standard
#### Constraints
- Future work should prioritize technical refinement, improved conversational realism, and thoughtful curricular integration.

### Target Learners
- medical students requiring remediation or additional structured support

### Target Learning Goals
- OSCE performance
- history-taking
- diagnostic reasoning
- management planning
