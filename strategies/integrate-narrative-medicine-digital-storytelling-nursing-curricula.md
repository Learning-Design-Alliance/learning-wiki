# Integrate narrative medicine modules, simulation-based patient interaction, reflective writing, and digital storytelling into nursing curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that nursing education simultaneously develop digital literacy, self-directed learning, and nursing professional values to foster narrative competence. Specifically, they recommend "integrating curriculum modules on narrative medicine, simulation-based patient interaction training, and combined reflective writing with digital storytelling activities into nursing education". This strategy follows from the observed associations in their SEM model, though it rests on cross-sectional data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targeted curriculum integration in nursing programs, since the authors note a lack of sufficient narrative care training programs in current nursing curricula
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and cannot establish causal effects of these curricular changes

### Target Learners
- Chinese pre-clinical nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Narrative competence (listening, understanding, reflecting)
- Digital literacy
- Self-directed learning
- Nursing professional values
