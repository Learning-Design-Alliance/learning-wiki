# Regular Text Referencing

## Description
Regularly referring back to mentor texts in teacher conferences, whole-class lessons, or discussions reinforces learning and helps students internalize new techniques. Remind students that this can be applied in the future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to previously used mentor texts. Time for review and discussion in class. A classroom environment where students feel comfortable revisiting and reflecting on past learning.
#### Constraints
- May be repetitive if not implemented thoughtfully. Students may become bored or disengaged. Requires careful planning to ensure that referencing is relevant and meaningful.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for reinforcing previously taught concepts

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Reinforce learning of writing techniques, promote internalization of new concepts, enhance transfer of learning to new contexts, increase retention of information

### Affordances
- Provides ongoing support and reinforcement. Helps students connect new learning to prior knowledge. Promotes long-term retention of information.

### Personalization
- Frequency and type of referencing can be tailored to student needs. Students can be given opportunities to choose which mentor texts to revisit. Scaffolding can be provided for struggling learners.
