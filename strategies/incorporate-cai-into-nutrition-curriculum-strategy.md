# Incorporate the CAI into master's nutrition curricula for clinical reasoning practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding the learning-oriented CAI into academic curricula so students practice integrating theoretical and empirical issues in nutritional assessment. They state that with the aim of improving students' skills in clinical reasoning and information management, the CAI has been incorporated into the curricula of master's students of the University of Navarra, and that the application 'can be implemented in their academic curriculum'.
