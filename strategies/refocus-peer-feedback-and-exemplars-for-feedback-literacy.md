# Re-focus peer feedback and exemplar analysis as core curriculum activities with coaching and dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends making peer feedback and exemplar analysis core curriculum elements, re-focused explicitly towards developing feedback literacy. Peer review should include "purposeful coaching", and exemplars "should be seen not as models but as opportunities for dialogues that clarify the characteristics of quality work". Teachers communicate rationales, set expectations of pro-activity, and model uptake of feedback, including sharing their own experiences of academic peer review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers create curriculum environments for active learner participation and provide guidance, coaching and modelling
#### Constraints
- The article notes exemplar use raises teacher misgivings that students may treat them as models to imitate, concerns the authors argue are not fully justified

### Target Learners
- undergraduate higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- developing evaluative judgment and capacities to act on feedback
