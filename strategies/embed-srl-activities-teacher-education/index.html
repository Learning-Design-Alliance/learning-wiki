Embed SRL-promoting activities in post-secondary teacher education lessons¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that "Post-secondary instructors who have a role in educating elementary school teacher candidates should include learning activities that promote self -regulated learning skills for elementary teacher candidates in their lessons." It also recommends increasing field experience duration so candidates gain more practice applying SRL strategies, and conducting further qualitative research on SRL skill effectiveness in Life Science Teaching courses.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Course structures with field experience allowing goal setting, monitoring, strategy use, and reflection

Constraints¶

  • Supported by a single quasi-experimental study; sub-factor gains were limited to lack of self-directedness

Target Learners¶

  • elementary teacher candidates

Target Learning Goals¶

  • self-regulated learning skills

Affordances¶

Claims¶

  • SRL-based activities reduce lack of self-directedness among teacher candidates [+W]
  • Pintrich SRL-based activities improve elementary teacher candidates' overall self-regulated learning perceptions [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed adaptive SRL scaffolding and short learning-skills training into online courses, with targeted social-engagement support for students with limited socialization skills
  • Foster autonomy and curiosity-driven learning by embedding metacognitive processes and self-regulation elements into education
  • Design teacher PD on SRL to combine situated learning with cognitive instruction, and use short workshops as an economical entry point
