# Include additional learning materials — MCQ tests with accessible correct answers, clinical cases, and a reflective written assignment with anonymous peer review — to provide feedback and support motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete configuration strategy from the co-design workshops: supplement the core digital resources with multiple-choice tests, several clinical cases, and written assignments. Students wanted access to correct answers and case solutions because "this type of immediate feedback could help them understand their own level of performance and identify knowledge gaps," and suggested that correct answers could unlock further advanced learning material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly stated learning outcomes for the additional material, and visual presentation of results or feedback (audio animation or a pop-up effect) for motivation and self-efficacy
#### Constraints
- Students preferred anonymous rather than open peer review because of relationships between students and insecurities about giving peer feedback; peer review was excluded for first-year students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in clinical rotation

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying knowledge gaps
- reflection on B-PAS use
- giving constructive feedback
