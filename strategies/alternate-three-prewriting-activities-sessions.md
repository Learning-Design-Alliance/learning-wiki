# Run pre-writing strategy instruction as alternating treatments across sessions, with an essay after each strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete instructional recipe: introduce a topic, have learners brainstorm related concepts and draw a concept map; in other sessions practice reading skills (skimming, scanning, note taking) on relevant passages; in others, hold class discussion with the teacher recording ideas in two for-and-against columns. After each treatment students write an argumentative essay on the topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who models the strategies, e.g. telegraphic language forms for concept maps
- Controversial topics from an oral practice book such as 'For and against'
#### Constraints
- The article tested this sequence only with advanced adult learners in a 12-week course with 18 treatment sessions

### Target Learners
- advanced adult EFL students aged 19-35

### Target Learning Goals
- generating and organizing ideas before writing argumentative essays
