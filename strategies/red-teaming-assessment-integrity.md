# Apply red teaming (adversarial penetration testing) to expose vulnerabilities in assessment integrity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends translating an approach from cybersecurity: "'Red teaming' (PWC, 2024) involves applying an adversarial lens to an organisation whereby a group of people try to hack or break into a process or system, such as the integrity of assessment hence of qualifications". Such ethical hacking or penetration testing helps expose vulnerabilities and reveal areas in need of development, and by moving beyond entrenched thinking seeks to close the gap between prevailing practice and policy and emerging or frontier threats and opportunities. It is positioned as part of multifaceted approaches to cultivating integrity and technology literacy education.
