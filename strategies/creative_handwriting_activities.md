# Creative Handwriting Activities

## Description
Engaging and enjoyable writing activities that move away from traditional pencil and paper tasks. Instead, these activities incorporate elements like sensory materials, movement, and play to encourage handwriting skill development. They focus on skills like spacing, letter sizing, and paper orientation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creative materials (e.g., playdough, shaving cream, stickers), varied writing tools, and a supportive environment. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not directly translate to formal handwriting tasks without explicit connections. Relies on transferring skills from creative to traditional writing contexts.

### Target Learners

- All grades, particularly useful for students who are reluctant writers or struggle with traditional handwriting methods.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop various handwriting skills (e.g., spacing, letter size) through creative and engaging activities.

### Affordances
- Increases motivation and engagement by making handwriting practice fun. Addresses skills in a less formal, more relaxed environment.

### Personalization
- Activities can be tailored to individual interests and needs, focusing on specific skill deficits or preferred sensory experiences.
