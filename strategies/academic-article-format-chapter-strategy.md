# Present one cosmology chapter in academic-article format to give students contact with scientific writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
One chapter of the book, on the birth of cosmology and the discovery of the expansion of the universe, is deliberately written in the form of an academic article. The authors flag that teachers must support students through it and see the format itself as a learning opportunity: "Uma atenção especial é necessária para esse capítulo, pois ele está escrito na forma de um artigo acadêmico." They state the intention was for high school students to have contact with academic writing, and advise that if the chapter is used directly with students, the teacher should assist them during the process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher assistance while students work through the article-format chapter
#### Constraints
- The authors warn that the chapter requires special attention because of its academic-article form, implying it is harder reading than the other chapters

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- familiarity with academic scientific writing
- understanding the birth of cosmology and the discovery of the expansion of the universe
