Teach mathematical processes before task-analysis skills, using sensory stimuli and strengths/weaknesses analysis¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In its recommendations the article advises paying attention to teaching the processes to female students before using analysis skills in learning, using every sensory channel, and paying attention to using sensory stimuli and bright and familiar colours to allow a gradual transition to common conceptual skills. It further recommends analysing strengths and weaknesses and the skills where children fail, then consolidating between the two issues when making the teaching plan, and encouraging areas of strength.
