# Promote intrinsic motivation through interactive teaching methods that relate lessons to real-life applications and recognize student achievements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends "fostering a positive learning environment that requires promoting intrinsic motivation through interactive teaching methods that relate lessons to real-life applications, thereby recognizing student achievements to build confidence." The practical-implications section adds that intrinsic motivation, which makes learning relevant, should be emphasized along with student-set goals. This strategy stems from the study's finding that intrinsic motivation plays a central role in fostering academic engagement while external rewards serve as moderate, supplementary motivators.

## Design Implications
