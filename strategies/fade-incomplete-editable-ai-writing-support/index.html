Design AI writing support to reduce ownership loss: fade AI interactions over time, generate incomplete text requiring rewriting, and make the chatbox editable¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on their finding that AI support at different writing stages has different ownership costs, the authors present three design implications for AI writing tools: "temporally fading out AI interactions, generating incomplete text that requires rewriting, and making the chatbox editable." These aim to keep writers contributing text and ideas themselves, since greater AI contribution of both was associated with lower ownership, while AI contribution improved essay quality.
