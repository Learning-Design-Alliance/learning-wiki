# Use generative dialogue within DBIR to guide collaboratively developed differentiation practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Generative dialogue is a tool the article recommends for guiding collaboratively developed practices within the DBIR approach. The article states it "can contribute to a teacher's ability to develop and successfully implement differentiation strategies for a diverse mathematics classroom". It draws on the Socratic tradition and instantiates as a complex interpersonal relationship facilitating trust, loyalty and mutual respect between professional educators, aiming to move beyond discussion or debate to reflective, generative discourse that builds organisational learning and shared frames of thinking.
