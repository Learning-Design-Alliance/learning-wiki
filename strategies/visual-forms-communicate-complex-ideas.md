## Description
The sixth strategy is "Using visual forms to communicate complex ideas about exploring real-world problems." Cognitive tools like concept maps, reasoning maps, evidence maps, and causal models "have been used to communicate thinking and understanding in complex situations," and graphic formats can reduce cognitive load when used appropriately.
