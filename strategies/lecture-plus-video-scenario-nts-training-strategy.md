# Combined lecture plus simulated video-scenario sessions for teaching non-technical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention delivered non-technical skills training "using a combined technique of lectures and simulated video scenarios delivered in two two-hour training sessions". After a lecture on each skill, the corresponding video scenarios were shown; participants gave feedback on the scrub practitioner's performance, followed by detailed explanation and discussion. Sessions were held in the operating room during regular working hours, spread over three days with a one-day interval to accommodate work schedules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two two-hour sessions; scenario content reviewed by faculty; coordination with participants' varying work schedules
#### Constraints
- High workload and staffing shortages prevented longer or more frequent sessions, which the authors say could have enhanced effectiveness

### Target Learners
- Scrub practitioners (operating room nurses)

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-efficacy in intraoperative non-technical skills
