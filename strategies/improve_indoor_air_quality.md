# Improve Indoor Air Quality

## Description
Poor indoor air quality in schools is a major contributor to absenteeism, especially for students with asthma. Many schools suffer from “sick building syndrome,” which affects the absenteeism and performance of all students. Indoor pollutants from office equipment, flooring materials, paints, cleaning products, and poor ventilation systems negatively affect children. Improving ventilation systems and reducing indoor pollutants can improve attendance and performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Improved ventilation systems, air purifiers, low-emission materials, regular HVAC inspections and cleaning
#### Constraints
- Implementation can be costly. Effectiveness may vary based on the source and type of pollutants.
