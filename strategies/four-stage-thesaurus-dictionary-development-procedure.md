# Four-stage procedure for developing a learner's terminological thesaurus and dictionary

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a four-stage development procedure: 1) designing, where requirements are determined; 2) selecting sources and making a word-list via external, internal and terminological classification of the subject area; 3) logical and conceptual analysis of the material, developing classification schemes and thesaurus, forming the glossary and compiling the dictionary; 4) implementation of an electronic version and experimental validation. Sources must be reliable, generally accepted, chronologically adequate, and authentic; terms are included based on semantic value, frequency of use, belonging to the terminological system, synchronism, derivational value and collocability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reliable and generally accepted sources with chronological adequacy and authenticity of analyzed texts, such as terminological standards, encyclopedias, reference books, textbooks and explanatory dictionaries
#### Constraints
- The concise thesaurus covered only the most general topics plus topics studied in the first and second years of the course

### Target Learners
- students majoring in Software Engineering

### Target Learning Goals
- occupational foreign language lexical competence
