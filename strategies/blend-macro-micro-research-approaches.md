# Blend macroscopic and microscopic research approaches to study students' use of mathematics across topics and courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose that future research in upper-division physics education combine macroscopic difficulty-uncovering with microscopic, theory-grounded accounts of in-the-moment reasoning. They argue "a synthetic approach in which researchers blend both approaches and/or collaborate with colleagues across approaches will result in more complete solutions". The strategy targets research that explains how difficulties connect to reasoning across topics, concepts, and courses rather than single topics.
