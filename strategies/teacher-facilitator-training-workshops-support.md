# Train classroom teachers as facilitators with ongoing experiential workshops and monthly support meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The programme's implementation model trains regular classroom teachers as facilitators. Intervention teachers attended "an initial presentation of the programme", "three whole-day bi-monthly experiential workshops, during which they were trained mostly in modeling and transferring the programme’s skills in the hidden curriculum" and "monthly three-hour afternoon meetings with the coordinators". This structure supports teachers who have not studied social-emotional education to deliver a structured curriculum with fidelity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordinators available to run the initial presentation, workshops and monthly meetings
- A structured curriculum with detailed implementation instructions
#### Constraints
- Control group teachers in the study received no training until the following school year

### Target Learners
- first and second grade elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
- personal and social skills
- self-esteem
- empathy
