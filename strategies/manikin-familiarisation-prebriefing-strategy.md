# Prebriefing with hands‐on manikin familiarisation before the simulation scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before the simulation scenarios began, the researchers ran a prebriefing in which they explained the manikin's characteristics and let students handle it freely, then explained each phase of both scenes and gave a seated rest period. The article reports "explaining the characteristics of the high‐fidelity manikin and giving the participants time to freely touch the manikin (10 min)" before Scene 1.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A high‐fidelity manikin and a pre‐simulation briefing slot in which participants are "given time to freely touch the manikin (10 min)" before the first scenario.
