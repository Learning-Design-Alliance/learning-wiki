# Provide additional support to involve early-years students in simulation, especially high-fidelity designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because first-year students scored lowest on both scales, the article recommends, "it is also recommended to put in strategies to involve early years students to utilise simulation better and make them feel more comfortable in using simulation, especially the recommended high‐fidelity designs." This targets the gap between first-year students (SDS M = 2.81) and fourth-year students (SDS M = 3.7) reported in the survey.
