# Adapt existing coursebook tasks into culture-based activities across unit stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes modifying and adapting existing coursebook tasks into culture-based activities at every stage of each unit, from warm-ups to homework. Activities included games, role plays, dialogues, video clips, discussions, and comparisons of local and target cultures. One student noted: "if the teachers select the right activity for each task in the units of the book it will be more beneficial."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need flexibility to modify coursebook tasks while maintaining the structure, topic and order of the tasks in each unit
#### Constraints
- Control groups still followed ordinary pace of lessons; teachers were not asked to ignore or skip activities which provided certain target culture insights

### Target Learners
- University-level adult EFL students in preparatory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- English language skills
- Cultural awareness
