# Anchor voluntary case-based e-learning in exam relevance and repeatedly reference it in in-person teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends establishing case-based e-learning on a voluntary basis with thematic relevance to the performance assessment, and repeatedly referencing the cases and their content during in-person sessions, which "könnte die Nutzungshäufigkeit steigern". It cites a comparison of integration strategies showing very low use (8.8%) for voluntary offerings without exam relevance versus very high use (90.7%) with exam relevance, and notes its own lower uptake despite the same conditions, attributing this partly to the cases being promoted only once.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exam relevance of the case content and alignment with lecture topics
- Repeated referencing of the e-learning offer during in-person teaching
#### Constraints
- The article reports its own uptake (66.24%) remained below the 90.7% figure it cites for voluntary use with exam relevance

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement with voluntary digital case-based learning aligned to assessed content
