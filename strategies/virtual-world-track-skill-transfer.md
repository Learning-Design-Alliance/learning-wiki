# Use virtual-world tasks to track learners' application and transfer of skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SPICE develops the Center's back end to track learners' application of skills, which Hughes calls a critical step in developing learners' confidence to teach their children, and something she says SPICE is only able to do because of technology. One method is a "Do Not Enter" room: after learning to teleport, students encounter a room they were told not to enter, and their choices are "recorded as evidence of skill transfer or lack thereof" — teleporting out shows they transferred knowledge to a new situation.
