# Provide ready-made resources and schemes of work with an active learning component to support PAL delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants identified resource availability as a barrier and proposed that resources and ready-made schemes of work be made available so teachers can incorporate PA into lessons without writing lesson plans themselves. One participant suggested "incorporation with schemes of work. So every scheme of work or schemes of work has to have an active learning component in a scheme of work". Providing such resources may also reduce preparation time, which the article notes can act as an additional barrier for practitioners.
