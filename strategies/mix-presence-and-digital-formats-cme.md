# Develop a suitable mix of presence and digital formats for continuing medical education seminar programs.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that "it is important to develop a suitable mix of presence and digital formats with the aim to improve the attractiveness as well as sustainability of continuing medical education". It motivates implementing e-learning beyond the pandemic as a supplement at the KWBW, while preserving regional personal reference during seminar days.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Maintain the regional personal reference during seminar days, which face-to-face seminars had provided for networking and professional identity.
#### Constraints
- Important goals such as personal peer-group interaction could not be achieved by the digital format alone.

### Target Learners
- post-graduate GP-trainees in general practice

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge transfer
- networking among trainees
- professional identity in general practice

## Claims

- [Trainees named no travel, time and cost savings and increased flexibility as main advantages of e-learning, and less personal interaction and technical obstacles as main disadvantages.](../claims/e-learning-advantages-disadvantages-gp-trainees.md) [+W]
- [Preparatory tasks in the flipped-classroom webinar design were rated helpful by 82% of GP-trainees, citing positive effects on learning success and better understanding of content.](../claims/flipped-classroom-pre-tasks-helpful-gp-trainees.md) [+W]
- [GP-trainees showed high acceptance of the converted digital seminar format, with 99% willing to participate in an online seminar again and 58% satisfied or very satisfied with the implementation.](../claims/kwbw-webinar-high-acceptance-gp-trainees.md) [+W]
- [Lecturers rated trainee interaction as worse or significantly worse in the digital format, and a majority assessed trainee learning success as worse than classical teaching.](../claims/lecturers-worse-interaction-learning-online.md) [+W]
- [For 52% of participating GP-trainees the attitude towards e-learning changed positively through participation in the digital seminars.](../claims/positive-attitude-change-e-learning-trainees.md) [+W]
