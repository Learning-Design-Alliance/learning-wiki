# Follow-Up After Disciplining a Student

## Description
After disciplining a learner, the instructor should do the following: touch base with the learner, acknowledge post-disciplinary successes, and don't give up too quickly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors should be willing to put in the effort to follow up with learners.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may negatively respond to positive attention, but the instructor should keep trying and not give up.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Demonstrates that the instructor cares about the learners, even after they have made mistakes.

### Personalization
- Follow-up shows the learner that the instructor hasn't given up on them, and will support them going forward.

- When learners are used to getting into trouble and having negative attention, it takes a while to break this cycle.
