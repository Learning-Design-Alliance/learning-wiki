# Establish formal, compensated worker advisory councils and co-design teams in skills-first system design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends establishing "formal, compensated mechanisms (e.g., Worker Advisory Councils, co-design teams) to authentically integrate the lived experiences and insights of frontline workers, opportunity youth, and veterans into every stage of skills-first system design, implementation, evaluation, and model iteration." It argues this ensures solutions are relevant, accessible, and responsive to needs including trauma-informed support, and calls for specific outreach strategies for opportunity youth and veterans acknowledging compounding barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Compensation and positioning of workers as subject matter experts; specific outreach and engagement strategies for opportunity youth and veterans
#### Constraints
- The report notes existing engagement is often limited, inaccessible (e.g., scheduling conflicts, untrustworthy spaces), or one-offs, and feedback designs may be too jargony

### Target Learners
- frontline workers, opportunity youth (ages 16-24 disconnected from employment and education), and veterans

### Target Learning Goals
- relevant and accessible skills-first system design; authentic integration of worker lived experience
