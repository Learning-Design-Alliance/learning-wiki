# Combining podcast lectures and simulator training to deliver ultrasound education where local expertise or equipment is lacking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (5 for, 4 mixed) · 6 studies (2 review, 2 associational, 1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that combining modalities could extend ultrasound education to institutions lacking local expertise: "Podcast lectures are a solution to lack of ultrasound faculty, and simulator training could reduce the cost of ultrasound machines and live models." The strategy pairs web-based lectures with computerized simulation to substitute for scarce faculty, devices, and live models. In this trial the combination was tested by replacing one of two hands-on hours with self-instructed simulator modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- English-language proficiency for lecture and module materials, which was a participation requirement in this study
#### Constraints
- The hour-for-hour simulator substitution tested did not improve measured learning outcomes, so the strategy's effectiveness remains unproven

### Target Learners
- Medical residents in institutions lacking local ultrasound expertise

### Target Learning Goals
- Introductory point-of-care ultrasound knowledge and image acquisition skill
