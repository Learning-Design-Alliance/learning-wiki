# Hire peer family care navigators and build county-level coordinated care systems for English Learner families

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends investing in formal English Learner family care navigators who are peers who have lived in the community and speak its languages, to help families navigate education and community supports and advocate for their children. It further recommends designing coordinated care systems linking education, health, social, and neighborhood domains at the county level, and piloting a block grant combining state funding streams for community needs outside of school. The brief states such collaboration with families "is a necessary first step toward a successful transition to multilingual instructional programs."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal navigator roles, cross-agency coordination at the county level, and combined funding mechanisms
#### Constraints
- The brief positions these recommendations as building on existing initiatives such as community schools and the Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative

### Target Learners
- English Learner students and their families/caregivers in California

### Target Learning Goals
- Family access to program information and noneducational services supporting students' education
