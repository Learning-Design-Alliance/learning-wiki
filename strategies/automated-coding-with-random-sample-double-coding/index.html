Automate self-affirmation essay coding with a fine-tuned model while double-coding a random sample for reliability¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends a structured workflow for scaling coding of open-ended student reflections: train the model on coded essays, validate reliability with sample human coding, apply it to new samples with sample-based validation, and retrain if context shifts. It states: "we strongly recommend that researchers double -code a random sample of the data to ensure reliability when using the fine -tuned model in their work". Automated coding reduces the time and cost of manual coding for large-scale interventions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A labeled training dataset of coded essays
  • Periodic human double-coding of a random sample to verify reliability

Constraints¶

  • Researchers must remain vigilant about bias in training data and predictions; the article recommends regular audits of model performance and diverse training data

Target Learners¶

  • Middle school students (7th grade) in value-affirmation interventions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Scalable, reliable identification of self-affirming content in student writing to support intervention research and educator feedback
