## Description
Framing is a visual arrangement strategy that enables a substantial amount of information to be put in the form of a grid, framework, spatial or matrix, originally introduced by Minsky (1975) as a product of spatial learning strategy. Students identify and list main ideas, principles and concepts, point out relationships between them, and label rows and columns to form a grid, stimulating connections of main ideas and their relationships to make learning more meaningful.
