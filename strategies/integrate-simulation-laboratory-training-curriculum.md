# Permanently integrate simulation laboratory sessions into the curriculum of all students of health disciplines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
On the basis of strongly positive student evaluations, the authors recommend that “simulation laboratory sessions should become an integral part of the curriculum of all students of health disciplines”, moving laboratory training from a one-off project to a permanent curricular component. They also state an aim to extend the simulation experience to the entire medical and nursing student population of their university.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an Advanced Simulation Center with an interprofessional team of doctor and nurse trainers and simulation materials and instruments
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from one cohort in a single year of one medical school, where students were “only 3rd Year students of medicine”

### Target Learners
- students of health disciplines, including medical and nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- communication and gestural skills
- clinical skills acquired before direct contact with patients
