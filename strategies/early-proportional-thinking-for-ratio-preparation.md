# Build on children's informal proportional understandings to prepare for ratio and proportion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Beyond sharing and dividing, the presentation reports that researchers and mathematics educators suggest building on children's informal understandings of proportional relationships. This "will lay the foundation for learning more advanced concepts of ratio and proportion in the upper elementary grades." Suggested activities include pointing out size and pattern relationships in children's stories, preparing recipe mixtures involving relationships among quantities, and showing how objects vary together in size or length.
