# Keep guideline e-learning concise and integrate text with images and videos

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participant feedback and the physicians' preferences literature, the authors recommend streamlining guideline e-learning. "Several participants stated that the VLCAM program should be more concise, and include more images and videos of various clinical situations." They add that the program "should be required to design to improve the integration with text, narration, and visual objects (images, graphic novels, and videos), and revised to a format more suitable for physicians", since the full-text e-book format could reduce motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must balance completeness of guideline content against physicians' preference for concise, algorithm-supported formats
#### Constraints
- Physicians' limited time is the stated motivation for conciseness

### Target Learners
- Physicians and physicians-in-training

### Target Learning Goals
- Efficient learning of clinical guideline content
