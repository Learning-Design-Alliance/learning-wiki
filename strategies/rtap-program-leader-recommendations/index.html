Program-leader recommendations for strengthening teacher apprenticeship experiences¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based directly on apprentices' perspectives, the report recommends that program leaders invest in recruitment through advising, outreach events, and peer ambassadors; offer flexible timelines and robust supports for adult learners; ensure consistent placement with high-quality, trained, representative mentors; maximize time apprentices spend with mentor-teachers and limit assignments forcing them to teach alone; provide clear, regular, transparent communication about costs, salaries, and expectations; and invest in structured cohort models to foster community and retention.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District-EPP partnership capacity to host candidates and supply mentors

Constraints¶

  • The report states no two states will have the same education policy landscape, so applicability and feasibility of policies differ state-by-state

Target Learners¶

  • teacher apprentices, especially apprentices of color and adult learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • completion of high-quality teacher preparation leading to licensure and retention in the profession

Claims¶

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustain and expand residency pathways through maintained state grant funding, financial aid, apprenticeship structures, and technical assistance
  • Develop high-retention pathways — Grow Your Own programs and teacher residencies — to build and diversify local teacher supply
  • State actions to strengthen and sustain a funded residency pathway: clarify goals, upgrade data systems, adjust grant parameters, offer transition grants, and pair new LEAs with experienced IHEs
  • Treat pathway legitimacy as a design target: ensure alternative routes are perceived as legitimate, usable, and socially recognized
  • Partner with and train in-service local teachers to co-instruct AI workshops and become ambassadors for AI education
