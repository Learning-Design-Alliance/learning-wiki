# Multimedia-based tutoring strategies exploiting spatial and temporal relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Bigger Bits uses multimedia-based tutoring strategies that exploit the spatial relationships in SYMs and temporal relationships in TEMs to use media in interactive ways, such as referring to objects by their relative spatial locations and highlighting events as they occur within the media. Students can respond by clicking on objects in video, typing answers, or selecting from multiple-choice options.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- media segments must be available and modelled with SYMs and TEMs
#### Constraints
- non-multimedia-based strategies are needed when media cannot be used or its use is unnecessary

### Target Learners
- students learning VLSI

### Target Learning Goals
- tutoring goals for device knowledge
