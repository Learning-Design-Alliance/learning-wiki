# Two-session evaluator workshop with video scoring and consensus discussion to establish inter-rater reliability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (3 design, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before administering the Mini-CEX, evaluators attend a two-session workshop: first the tool's background, purpose, and procedure are presented; then evaluators independently score a video of a Mini-CEX encounter, explain their reasoning, discuss definitions of "excellent," "satisfactory," and "unsatisfactory," and reach consensus before re-scoring. This procedure produced an inter-rater reliability of 0.7.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluators must be qualified nurses with instructor certification, having attended faculty development programs and passed accreditation by the Joint Commission of Taiwan
