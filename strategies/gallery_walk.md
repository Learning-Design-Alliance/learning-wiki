# Gallery Walk

## Description
Stations or posters are set up around the classroom, on the walls, or on tables. Small groups of learners travel from station to station together, performing a task or responding to a prompt, either of which will result in a conversation. Variations include groups creating informative posters and acting as tour guides, or using video tutorials created by learners themselves. The teacher prepares discussion prompts or content-related tasks and sets them up around the room for learners to visit in small groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires physical space to set up stations or posters, preparation of tasks and prompts, and clear instructions for learners. Post-it notes and markers for learners to record ideas.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging in small or crowded classrooms. Requires careful planning and monitoring to ensure all learners are engaged and on task. Can become chaotic if not well-managed.
