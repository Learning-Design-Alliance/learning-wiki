# Future direction: integrate large language models into branching virtual simulations for realistic dialogue generation and adaptive feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 5 studies (4 design, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a future study perspective, the authors propose integrating Large Scale Language Models such as ChatGPT into virtual simulations. The models "could be used to automatically generate realistic dialogues between characters in simulation scenarios" and could serve as virtual assistants providing evidence-based clinical guidance and immediate feedback on students' decisions, adapting in real time to participants' responses and chosen paths, and personalizing learning to the professional's level of experience.
