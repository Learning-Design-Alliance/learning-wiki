# Pair direct observation of real clinical tasks with immediate structured ("sandwich") feedback and instructions for improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Mini-CEX encounter, an evaluator directly observes a trainee performing a real clinical task, then completes the form and provides "sandwich" feedback followed by direct instructions for improvement. The article reports mean observation and feedback times of 14.30 and 6.79 minutes, noting the feedback time was relatively long due to its structured nature, with evaluators giving detailed recommendations and trainees encouraged to respond. The article frames feedback after direct observation as "a critical component of deliberate practice", a strategy believed to maximize clinical competency improvement through repetition, guidance, and coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Time allocated for structured feedback after observation

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skills improvement through formative feedback on observed performance
