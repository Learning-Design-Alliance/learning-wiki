# Support curriculum-industry links with invited industry speakers and extracurricular technology activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To increase the curriculum's relationship with the sector, the article recommends taking sector support in relevant professional courses and supporting courses with invited speakers from various companies. It also recommends encouraging students toward extracurricular activities such as seminars, hackathons and competitions organized by research groups, computer clubs and developer groups, observing that companies' events include sessions on current technologies, especially mobile software development. Students' personal development should be supported with special sessions too detailed for the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to companies and developer groups willing to organize events or provide speakers
#### Constraints
- Topics too detailed for the curriculum or hard to find information about are handled outside regular courses

### Target Learners
- computer engineering undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of current technologies
- lifelong learning and self-renewal
