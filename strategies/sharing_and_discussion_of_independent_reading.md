# Sharing and Discussion of Independent Reading

## Description
After independent reading, gather students to talk about their books through various activities such as book clubs or creating commercials to interest others. These activities foster engagement and allow students to share their reading experiences. When possible, allow students to take books home to encourage reading outside of class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for sharing sessions, materials for creating commercials or other activities, and permission for students to take books home.
#### Constraints
- Requires structured activities to be effective. Some students may be hesitant to share their reading experiences. Requires careful planning and management.
