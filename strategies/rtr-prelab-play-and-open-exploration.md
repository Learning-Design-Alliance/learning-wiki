# Require pre-laboratory play with the simulation and provide more open-ended exploration time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their evaluation, the authors recommend two changes for future use: "we shall require students to 'play' with the simulation as part of the pre-laboratory preparation, so that they have some familiarity with the controls" and "We shall also provide more opportunity for open ended exploration, as this appears to be its strength." This addresses student complaints about difficult controls and a too-prescriptive manual.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the simulation before the assessed laboratory session
- Laboratory structure that permits open-ended investigation alongside required measurements
#### Constraints
- The article notes this is a planned change based on their experience, not yet tested

### Target Learners
- first year university physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- developing familiarity with simulation controls and discovering relativistic effects through exploration
