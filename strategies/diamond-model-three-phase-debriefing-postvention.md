# Debrief the postvention scenario with "The Diamond" model across descriptive, analytical and application phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The scenario's debriefing stage follows "The Diamond" model, developed "through three consecutive phases": a descriptive phase on what happened without judging performance, an analytical phase on non-technical skills and feelings, and an application phase on applying the learning in clinical practice. The article characterizes debriefing as "an important moment of communication with emphasis on reflection, feedback and self-analysis of the participant, mediated in a structured way by the facilitator".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A prepared and experienced facilitator to conduct the simulated scenario and mediate the structured reflection moments.

### Target Learners
- Scenario participants and observers (health-area students and health professionals)
