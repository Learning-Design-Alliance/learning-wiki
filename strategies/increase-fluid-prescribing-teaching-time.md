# Increase formal teaching time on fluid and electrolyte prescribing in medical school and during the Foundation Programme, augmented by other safety mechanisms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy is to dedicate more formal curricular time to fluid and electrolyte prescribing, both during medical school, where hours appear low, and after it, specifically using mandatory Foundation Programme education time. The authors caution that education "should be augmented by other mechanisms" to translate into safer prescribing practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Relies on the availability of mandatory education time during the Foundation Programme or curricular space in medical school
#### Constraints
- Education alone is described by the article, citing ISMP, as a “weak improvement strategy” that needs augmentation by other mechanisms

### Target Learners
- Foundation Year doctors
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and effective fluid and electrolyte prescribing
