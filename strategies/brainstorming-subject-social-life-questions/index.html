Use brainstorming questions that probe a subject's social life and prior discursive handling¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A dialogic approach to probing subjects during the writing process entails paying attention to the subject's social life. During brainstorming, teachers ask questions such as "How has this subject been previously handled in oral and written discourse?" and "How does this subject implicate us as authors and readers?" This implements Bakhtin's view that subjects carry within themselves the traces of preceding usage.

Context¶
