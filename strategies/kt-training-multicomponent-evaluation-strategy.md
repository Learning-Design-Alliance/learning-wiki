# Multi-component evaluation strategy combining trainee output metrics, summative surveys, and yearly formative semi-structured interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To determine whether the training initiative meets its objectives, the program uses core measures such as number of trainees per stream, publications, presentations, grants, honours, programs developed, research impact, stakeholder engagement, and researcher recruitment and retention. Summative evaluation surveys participants from all three streams about perceptions, experiences, and effects on employment and practice. Formatively, "each year a sample of trainees from each of the three streams will be invited to participate in a semi-structured interview" to propose revisions, with results used to continuously refine the initiative.
