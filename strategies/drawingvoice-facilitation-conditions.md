# Facilitation conditions for the drawing and Facebook activities: clear presentation, small rotating comparison groups, climate of trust, and active participation in online sharing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors list the conditions that supported the drawing activity and the following discussion on Facebook, including "presenting the methodology clearly and explicitly with times, methods, and activities", "creating a climate of trust and equality in promoting the sharing of ideas, knowledge, emotions", and supporting "the sharing and the discussions on the Facebook group by actively participating". These facilitation moves are the implementable recipe for running the method.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher or facilitator with knowledge of group interaction to manage the small comparison groups
#### Constraints
- The authors state that drawing, teamwork and web interaction as keys to the method require teachers supported by psychological and group interaction knowledge

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- participation and sharing of ideas, emotions and emerging representations
