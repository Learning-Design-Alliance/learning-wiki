# Develop a scholarly collegiality of teachers on a departmental level through three simultaneous directions: exploring student experiences, shared awareness of ways of understanding, and creative dialogue with empowered students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that whole departments or the college of teachers in a programme engage in a discourse in which students' learning can be discussed, moving in "three important directions ... simultaneously to develop a scholarly collegiality on a departmental level": exploring students' experiences using means such as study diaries; developing shared awareness of different ways of understanding physics concepts using educational research; and engaging in creative dialogue with students empowered to analyse and articulate their study situation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must participate collectively rather than as isolated individuals, since the examples discussed in the article lack embeddedness in a college of teachers.
#### Constraints
- The authors note that addressing the implications takes consideration, cooperation and effort and is not instantaneously done.

### Target Learners
- university physics students taught by a departmental teaching collective

### Target Learning Goals
- bringing the structure and internal relations of physics into students' awareness
- supporting students in taking control of their studies
