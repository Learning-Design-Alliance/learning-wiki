# Integrate clozapine training with clinical experience and extend it to all professionals, supported by dedicated clinics and specialist advice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends combining education, training and hands-on experience rather than treating them separately. It concludes that "Training can enhance competence in clozapine management if it is integrated with education and experience", that training "should be made available to all professionals (medical and non-medical), not just those in training", and that dedicated clozapine clinics plus access to specialist advice services can bolster confidence in clozapine use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programmes combining teaching with practical experience in settings where clozapine is used
- Availability of dedicated clozapine clinics and specialist advice services
#### Constraints
- The review warns special clozapine services might deskill professionals not operating within them; different professionals may have different training needs

### Target Learners
- psychiatrists and trainee doctors
- general practitioners
- nurses, pharmacists and other non-medical professionals involved in clozapine care

### Target Learning Goals
- competence in clozapine initiation, monitoring and side-effect management
- confidence in adhering to guidelines and managing complex cases
