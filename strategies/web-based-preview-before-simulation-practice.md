# Use web-based preview before simulation practice so students prepare autonomously

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable component of the article's SBME design: between their study and practice sessions, students use web-based learning (the "Muke." app) to preview the operations they will practice, consulting technical data by themselves. The article reports that "the students received web-based learning by using the \"Muke.\" app in the durations between their study and practice sessions," and that this previewing may help students learn the content of their practice autonomously. In the discussion the authors state the app was interspersed throughout the whole SBME process, contributing to mastery of cognitive and behavioral outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An internet interactive software app students can access between study and practice sessions
- Practice content matching the previewed operations
#### Constraints
- The article evaluates this within its own SBME course for junior surgery students at a single center

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate junior medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Autonomous preparation for clinical practice sessions
- Mastery of cognitive and behavioral outcomes of surgical practice
