# Assign pre-class draft answers with attempt credit to ensure preparation for in-class problem solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In this modified flipped classroom, all course content is presented as online module PDFs and specific questions are assigned in advance for a small amount of credit. Students must "make an initial attempt at these questions before each class period and then to submit their draft answers before class", receiving full attempt credit for good-faith efforts. The instructor reports that use of this attempt credit "greatly enhanced student preparation for the in-class problem solving activities" and calls it the most successful such strategy across 14 semesters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Module PDF files posted online before class, with specific questions assigned in advance for a small amount of credit
#### Constraints
- The article notes that a flipped classroom is probably not the optimal method

### Target Learners
- undergraduate STEM students

### Target Learning Goals
- preparation for in-class problem solving
- self-directed study skills
