# Small school reformers should intentionally build professional community, principal leadership, and teacher influence rather than relying on size reduction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that reformers treat small size as only a lever, not a guarantee. Because "reducing size does not automatically lead to such developments, however, schools will need to intentionally focus on creating these key organizational characteristics." The strategy directs school leaders to develop strong teacher professional communities engaged in collective work on instruction, deep principal leadership that initiates and sustains that work, and teacher voice in shaping it, since strength in just one or two areas was not enough in the sample.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intentional and concerted effort by school leaders and reformers to develop multiple organizational areas simultaneously
#### Constraints
- Findings come from ten CHSRI schools serving predominantly low-income African American and Latino freshmen with below-grade-level incoming achievement; generalization beyond this context is not established

### Target Learners
- first-time freshmen in small urban high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- raising freshman course performance and on-track rates through organizational improvement
