# Establish standardized, ethically sound guidelines and coordinated stakeholder efforts for integrating LLMs into dental education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that dental students, educators, curriculum administrators, and technical developers work collectively to establish comprehensive, evidence-based frameworks for ethical and effective LLM integration. It states "a collaborative approach is essential" and that future research should focus on "establishing standardized, ethically satisfying, and technically sound guidelines for integrating LLMs into dental education." Educational institutions and examination boards must develop robust policies and technological safeguards to prevent ethical misuse, including unauthorized use during high-stakes licensing examinations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordinated efforts from students, educators, curriculum administrators, and technical developers
- Development of robust policies and technological safeguards by educational institutions and examination boards
#### Constraints
- The review notes there is still a lack of studies addressing the ethical and legal issues related to LLM use in dental education, which hampers guideline development

### Target Learners
- Dental students
- Dental educators
- Curriculum administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Responsible and effective integration of LLMs into dental education
- Prevention of academic dishonesty and ethical misuse
