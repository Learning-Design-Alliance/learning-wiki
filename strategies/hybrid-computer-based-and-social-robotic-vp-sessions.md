# Use social robotic virtual patient sessions as a scheduled complement to broadly accessible computer-based virtual patients in a hybrid approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend combining traditional computer-based virtual patients, which offer broad access, with scheduled sessions using social robotic virtual patients to deepen skill development. They state the platform "may be most effective as part of a hybrid approach, combining traditional computer-based VPs for broader access with scheduled sessions using social robotic VPs to deepen skill development", a way to optimize educational impact while managing practical resource constraints across medical education settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a social robotic platform for scheduled in-person sessions
- Computer-based VP cases available on students' personal computers for broader access
#### Constraints
- Costs and logistics may constitute limitations for implementation of the social robotic technology
- Further evaluation and comparisons with other LLM-driven modalities are needed to assess the unique benefits of a physical robot versus an LLM alone

### Target Learners
- medical students in health professions education curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning training
- bridging theoretical learning and clinical practice
