# Encourage essential collaborative behaviours via feedback or student evaluations in small groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using their identified behaviours to promote learning in small groups: "The findings of the present study could be employed to encourage, via feedback or student evaluations, essential behaviours for learning by individual students in small groups." They note this aligns with data showing medical students perceive the need to promote collaborative capabilities through structured evaluation and feedback. The strategy targets individual student behaviours such as preparedness, active listening and constructive feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of the essential collaborative behaviours to be encouraged, as characterised in the study's themes
#### Constraints
- Studied in the context of junior medical students in scenario-based small group learning; generalisability to other contexts is not established by this study

### Target Learners
- junior medical students in small group learning

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative learning behaviours
- effective group interaction and learning
