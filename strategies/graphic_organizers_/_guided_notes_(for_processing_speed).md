# Graphic Organizers / Guided Notes (for Processing Speed)

## Description
During lecture, learners are provided with graphic organizers or guided notes to help pull out the important points. These tools structure the information being presented, making it easier to process and retain. Guided notes prompt learners to fill in key information, promoting active engagement during the lecture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared graphic organizers or guided notes, writing utensils or digital input tools, and instructor guidance.
#### Constraints
- Requires preparation time for the instructor. Some learners may find the structure too rigid if not tailored to their needs.

### Target Learners

- Learners who struggle with processing speed, those who have difficulty taking effective notes, and students who benefit from structured learning environments.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Support information processing, improve note-taking skills, enhance comprehension of key concepts, and increase engagement during lectures.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured framework, reduces cognitive load, promotes active engagement, and supports information retention.

### Personalization
- Customize graphic organizers and guided notes to match individual learning styles and content. Provide options for filling in information (e.g., writing, drawing, digital input).
