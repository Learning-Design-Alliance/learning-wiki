# Run online PBL by uploading micro clinical cases 3 days before class, having students present via video conferencing, and quizzing after each class

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe for WeChat-PBL: micro clinical cases with relevant questions are uploaded to the group 3 days before class; students divide into two subgroups to create PowerPoints by reading books, reviewing literature, or asking others; group representatives present via WeChat video conferencing; and every participant completes a tutor-graded quiz after each class so the tutor can monitor students who performed poorly. This combination was used across 8 cases with 48 postgraduates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A WeChat group containing a tutor and 6 or 7 postgraduate students
- Tutors who visually display students' progress and monitor students who performed poorly
#### Constraints
- Described and evaluated in the context of hematology postgraduate teaching in China

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- disease-specific theoretical knowledge
- active participation and preparation
- self-regulated learning
