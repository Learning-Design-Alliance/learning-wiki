# Clarify regulatory scope before performing diagnostic or interventional ultrasound

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because diagnostic and interventional US fall within physical therapy scope in only some jurisdictions, the article advises that 'Regardless of training or expertise, physical therapists should clarify their scope of practice for these US applications by contacting their regulatory body prior to performing diagnostic or interventional US.' It notes that legislation extending scope may be required first, and that training through courses built for other healthcare practitioners may lead therapists to operate outside their professional scope.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Contact the regulatory body before performing diagnostic or interventional US
#### Constraints
- In many instances a change in legislation to extend physical therapy scope may be required before therapists can use US in this manner

### Target Learners
- practising physical therapists
- physical therapy regulators and educators

### Target Learning Goals
- practising within jurisdictional scope of practice
