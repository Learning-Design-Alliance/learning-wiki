# Braille Texts for Literacy Development

## Description
Braille texts are used to build literacy skills such as decoding and syntax. Braille is a tactile writing system used by people who are blind or visually impaired. It uses raised dots to represent letters and other symbols, allowing individuals to read and write by touch.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to braille texts and materials. Braille reader or device.
#### Constraints
- Braille literacy requires specialized training and practice. Braille materials can be bulky and expensive to produce.
