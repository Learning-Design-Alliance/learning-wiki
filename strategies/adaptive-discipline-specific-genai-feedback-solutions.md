# Develop adaptive and discipline-specific GenAI feedback solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's forward-looking design recommendation is that future GenAI feedback tools should be adaptive and tailored to disciplines. It states that "Future developments should focus on creating adaptive and discipline-specific GenAI solutions", motivated by the observed mismatch between GenAI outputs and diverse student expectations and by students finding generic outputs redundant when feedback was already clear.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must account for diverse student needs and disciplinary differences in feedback expectations
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single study of 18 students across four disciplinary areas

### Target Learners
- higher education students across disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- effective engagement with AI-generated feedback
