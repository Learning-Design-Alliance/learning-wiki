# Reflective Assessment

## Description
Grades should reflect learning over time rather than be an end in and of itself. Learning through multiple modalities that leverage learners' languages and cultural insights.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexible assessment methods, emphasis on learning over evaluation
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset from traditional grading
