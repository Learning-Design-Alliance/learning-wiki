# Charter leaders and management organizations should assess teacher understanding and buy-in of their evaluation systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because matched charter teachers reported lower perceived fairness of teacher evaluation, the article recommends that charter school leaders and charter management organizations assess how well teachers understand and accept current or new evaluation systems. It suggests this could address perceptions of unfairness that, per cited literature, can undermine morale and increase turnover.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders must assess the level of teacher understanding and buy-in of the current evaluation system or any potentially new evaluation system.
#### Constraints
- The finding comes from teacher perceptions in one Texas charter district comparison; the article notes early implementation studies of new evaluation systems were still emerging.

### Target Learners
- teachers in charter schools

### Target Learning Goals
- perceived evaluation fairness
- teacher morale
- teacher retention
