# Can You Make It?

## Description
Learners draw a dot card and recreate the arrangement on a large, blank dot card using counters. This helps them count and recognize various arrangements of quantities. Start with small numbers like 1-3 for beginners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dot cards, large blank dot card, counters.
#### Constraints
- May require one-on-one instruction or facilitation for learners who struggle with counting. The game's effectiveness relies on having physical materials readily available.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Pre-K to 1st Grade learners who are beginning to learn subitizing.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop number sense, recognize quantity arrangements, improve counting skills

### Affordances
- Provides a hands-on approach to understanding number arrangements. Enhances number sense by associating physical representation with numerical value. Allows learners to work at their own pace and build confidence.

### Personalization
- Adaptable by adjusting the range of numbers used. The large, blank dot card can be modified to different sizes to suit individual learner needs.
