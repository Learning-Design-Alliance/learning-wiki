# Offer flexible part-time instructional roles to bring certified, nonemployed educators back into classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that states and districts offer flexible instructional roles with less stringent full-time commitment to attract certified teachers who have retired, taken breaks, or moved to education-adjacent fields. Specific measures include "job share" arrangements splitting the workweek between two part-time certified teachers, and "adjunct" instructor roles at the secondary level where a certified teacher teaches just a section or two of a course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State and district flexibility, such as enabling retired teachers to continue collecting pensions while working part-time or negotiating with teacher unions to permit job shares
#### Constraints
- The brief frames these as strategies with potential to reduce reliance on substitutes, not as evaluated interventions

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in districts with teacher and substitute shortages

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining access to certified, experienced classroom instruction during staffing shortages
