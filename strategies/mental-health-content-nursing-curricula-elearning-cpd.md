# Include mental health content and clinical learning experiences in undergraduate children's nursing curricula, and use co-designed e-learning for continuing professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study results, the authors recommend that mental health content and clinical learning experiences be included in undergraduate children's nursing curricula so students can provide first assistance for mental health problems, and that children's nurses' access to continuing professional development be made more attainable, realistic and relevant through flexible e-learning. E-learning allows nurses to learn at their own pace and permits access for rotating rosters and geographically-diverse regions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning co-designed with stakeholders and tailored to the needs of the nursing profession
#### Constraints
- The authors recommend further adequately powered evaluation before including the intervention in training

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate children's nursing students
- Registered children's nurses undertaking continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- Mental health knowledge and first assistance for mental health problems
- Care of children and young people who self-harm
