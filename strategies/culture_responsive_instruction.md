# Culture Responsive Instruction

## Description
Creating culture responsive instruction asks teachers to provide culturally responsive instruction for their students by making standards-based content and curricula accessible to students and teaching in a way that students can understand. This can be done by incorporating relatable aspects of students' daily lives into the curriculum, such as language, prior knowledge, and extracurricular interests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cultural sensitivity, an understanding of student backgrounds, and the ability to integrate cultural elements into lesson plans.
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging to implement authentically without oversimplifying or stereotyping cultures. Requires ongoing effort to stay informed and responsive.
