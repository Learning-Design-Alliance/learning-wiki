# CRITO: a five-step method for critically assessing one's own arguments (Conclusion, Reasons, Inference, Truth, Objections)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CRITO is an acronymic method formed from "the logical terms Conclusion, Reasons, Inference, Truth, and Objections." Students first identify a substantive, debatable conclusion, supply sufficient reasons, test the inference connecting reasons to conclusion, test the truth of the reasons, and finally construct the strongest imaginable objections. The paper claims it "addresses both the principled reason assessment and critical attitude components of critical thinking" and works as an outline for argumentative or evaluative essays.
