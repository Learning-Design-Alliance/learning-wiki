## Description
A pillar of the proposed Baikal Open Laboratory is the development of open training programs: all programs and their sources, such as scripts, slides and problems, are to be published online under a free license. The article states they "can be adopted by the third-party institutes and lecturers", and that lectures and seminars will be given at ISU and kept alive and updated.
