# Staged hybrid model: programmed instruction for foundational skills, then case-based scenarios for clinical reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the discussion the authors recommend sequencing behaviourist and cognitive approaches: "Future hybrid models could incorporate both: using programmed instruction for foundational skills acquisition, then transitioning to case-based scenarios for higher-order clinical reasoning." The rationale is that programmed instruction builds behavioural precision and procedural accuracy, while unstructured case-based approaches such as problem-based learning promote clinical reasoning and empathy, which the authors state their behaviourist programme did not fully assess.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Foundational skills must first be established through programmed instruction before transitioning to case-based scenarios
#### Constraints
- The authors note this staged model is a proposal addressing limitations identified in their study, particularly the limited assessment of critical thinking and empathy; it was not tested in this trial

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- foundational psychomotor skill automation
- higher-order clinical reasoning
