# Teach tense/aspect choice through keyhole pictorializations of boundedness and the present tense as a short window on the situation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the cognitive treatment, learners were shown timelines of situations (e.g. trust, build) with the present tense depicted as a keyhole of about 1-2 seconds, and given rules such as: "Stative verbs may be used in the simple present because their endpoints are not important/relevant (the verbs do not describe change), so we can view their situations through the (small/short) keyhole of the present tense." Parallel rules covered dynamic verbs with the present continuous and performative verbs with the simple present.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pictorial representations and pedagogically worded rules; the treatment required approximately 15 min more than the traditional 70-min treatment because of the more detailed semantic discussion
#### Constraints
- All metalinguistic comments were in Polish owing to participants' low level of advancement; intermediate verb types and atelic activities were excluded

### Target Learners
- senior high school foreign-language learners of English

### Target Learning Goals
- choice between present simple and present continuous with stative and dynamic verbs at speech time

- Cg Tense Aspect Perfective Imperfective Account
