# Target future BLS/AED training at recognition of cardiac arrest, minimizing hands-off time, adequate tidal volumes, and defibrillation safety checks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, because the assessed rural EMS providers showed specific skill deficits, "future training should be targeted to recognition of cardiac arrest, minimizing the hands-off time, and providing ventilation with adequate tidal volumes" and that defibrillation safety checks should be reinforced. Training should be at regular intervals in realistic settings with qualified instructors, and targeted at the specific difficulties identified by this manikin assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should occur at regular intervals in realistic settings with qualified instructors
- Training should be targeted at the specific difficulties identified by the assessment
#### Constraints
- A specific course with systematic retraining and re-assessment was not possible for logistics and economic reasons in this study

### Target Learners
- rural EMS providers trained in BLS and AED use

### Target Learning Goals
- cardiac arrest recognition
- high-quality chest compressions with minimal interruptions
- adequate tidal volume ventilation
- AED safety checks
