# Administer the LASSI with pre-matriculation data and provide targeted support in weak study-strategy areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the LASSI in medical school alongside pre-matriculation data, since it "consistently yields academic performance predictors". Students scoring low in Concentration, Time Management, Test Strategies, and Motivation should receive targeted support in those areas on entry, and a second LASSI at the start of the second year lets students continue monitoring their study strategies and focus on specific weaknesses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete the LASSI and receive a performance profile comparing their ten subscale scores to a norm group
#### Constraints
- The article notes the LASSI factor structure did not replicate previous research, so the scale may lack some stability and may be vulnerable to the testing environment

### Target Learners
- medical students entering the preclinical curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- study strategies including concentration, motivation, time management and test strategies
- self-directed learning skills and lifelong learning
