# Two-level difficulty scaling from labeled boards to charge-and-strangeness computation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The game scaffolds learning across two levels. At the beginner level, 'names of baryons and mesons can be written on the boards' and players may consult reference tables of particle names, symbols and quark content. At the intermediate level, 'they must place them on the board themselves based on their total electric charge Q and strangeness S,' requiring players to add per-card values from a provided table and to spot assembly opportunities. This lets players progress once they have mastered the simpler level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reference tables of baryons, mesons, and per-card strangeness and charge values for the intermediate level
#### Constraints
- The intermediate level assumes familiarity with individual particle strangeness and charge

### Target Learners
- beginners using labeled boards
- intermediate players computing Q and S sums

### Target Learning Goals
- adding electric charges and strangeness values
- identifying valid hadron combinations
