# Deliberately bridge student experience data work across tools, initiatives, and partner organizations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants recommended making deliberate connections between student experience data work and other district tools and initiatives—such as the Continuous Improvement Work Plan (CIWP), the RigorWalk observation tool, and ILT/distributed leadership work—so the effort does not feel like separate tasks. Coordination suggestions included establishing clarity of roles, optimizing existing meeting times, and developing a system strategy addressing what each stakeholder does and how efforts coordinate. One participant said, "we have to be a little bit more deliberate about the connections that we make and the collaborations."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intentional collaboration amongst entities engaged in the work
- Organizations understanding their own culture and structures before collaborating
#### Constraints
- Relationships are relationship-dependent and not structured for long-term collaboration, with staff transitions causing pauses in partnership

### Target Learners
- School leaders and educators in CPS schools
- District staff and external school support organizations

### Target Learning Goals
- Aligning student experience data use with schools' instructional focus and improvement priorities
