# Use interactive dynamic simulations to visualize relativistic phenomena when addressing student difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose, as the next step of their research program, an educational approach that uses interactive dynamic simulations so that learners can see relativistic phenomena that lie "outside our everyday experience". The strategy directly targets the difficulties their study documented, since acknowledging students' difficulties is said to lead to "the construction of an effective educational approach". It is a proposal, not yet an evaluated intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An educational approach built on the specific student difficulties the study identified
- Simulations capable of visualizing relativistic phenomena outside everyday experience
#### Constraints
- The article presents this only as a proposed next step; no evaluation of the simulation approach is reported

### Target Learners
- non-major physics undergraduate students
- upper secondary students (proposed extension)

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the basic principles and consequences of Special and General Relativity
