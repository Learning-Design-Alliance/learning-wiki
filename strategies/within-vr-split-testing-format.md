# Split-testing format: embodied interaction versus 3D video demonstration within the same VR environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a novel split-testing format with two versions of the laboratories: Version A, with a high level of embodiment and congruency where students interact with simulated objects and perform a learning assessment, and Version B, where students observe a demonstration of the laboratory in a 3D video within the same virtual reality environment, with a lower level of immersion. The authors state this structure "enables direct comparison between VR interaction, and non-interaction within the same VR environment," addressing the confounded educational contexts of earlier 2D-versus-3D comparisons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both versions must be hosted within the same virtual reality environment so that immersion level, not platform, is varied
#### Constraints
- The 3D video environment (Version B) was not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- engineering students in materials, mechanical, aerospace, civil, and biomedical disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- isolating the effect of VR interaction versus observation on learning
