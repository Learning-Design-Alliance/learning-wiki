# Mitigating Racial Bias in Edtech Products

## Description
The AI in Education Toolkit for Racial Equity provides guidance on how to mitigate racial bias in the design and development of edtech products. It encourages edtech companies to design for Black and Brown students, understand their needs, and champion their success. The toolkit details actions to take at every stage of product design and development, including examining data, assumptions, and algorithmic design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A commitment to racial equity, willingness to learn about the experiences of Black and Brown students, and a commitment to data privacy and accessibility.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and commitment. May require significant changes to product design and development processes.
