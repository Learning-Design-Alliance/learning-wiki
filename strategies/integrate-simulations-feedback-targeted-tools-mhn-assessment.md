# Integrate complementary methods — simulations, feedback, and targeted tools — into mental health nurse training and assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that competence assessment of Mental Health Nurses combine multiple scales with complementary methods rather than relying on knowledge questionnaires alone. It states it is "recommended that complementary methods, such as simulations, feedback, and targeted tools, be integrated into MHN training and assessment", and its conclusions likewise urge that "multiple scales and complementary methods, such as simulations and feedback evaluations, be integrated". This addresses the identified gap that skills are rarely assessed because they require more complex and costly methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools specifically designed for postgraduate mental health nursing and residency contexts, which the review identifies as a pressing need
#### Constraints
- The review notes cultural and contextual differences make global consistency ambitious, so national-level standardization is proposed as feasible

### Target Learners
- Registered Mental Health Nurses and Psychiatric Nurses in clinical practice
- Mental Health Nursing residents in Specialized Health Training Programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical competences of mental health nurses: knowledge, skills, and attitudes in areas such as physical healthcare, psychiatric emergencies, therapeutic restraint, and therapeutic communication
