# State education agencies can support rural turnaround staffing and professional development through recruitment supports, shared PD, and technology assistance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a set of SEA-level supports for rural schools drawn from the CST handbook chapter and survey. SEAs "provide support in the form of career fairs, access to alternatively certified teachers, and strategic partnerships," and some "gather groups of rural districts together for shared professional development and collaboration" or "offer technological support to share resources and knowledge across remote areas." Rural districts themselves may offer bonuses, additional pay, or travel expenses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State education agencies with capacity to organize recruitment events, partnerships, shared professional development, and technological support for remote districts
#### Constraints
- Most rural schools lack the dedicated resources Amargosa received through its SIG-funded School Improvement Director

### Target Learners
- Teachers and administrators in remote rural preK–8 schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Staffing turnaround schools with effective teachers and leaders; providing access to professional development and collaboration
