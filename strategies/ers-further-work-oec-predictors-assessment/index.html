Three further-work priorities: comprehensive OEC, identifying predictors of learning, and efficient outcome-based assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The review proposes three significant areas of focus for further work in ERS research. First, "Defining a comprehensive overall evaluation criteria (OEC) that incorporates outcome-based, user-centric, and product-oriented metrics". Second, identifying the key predictors of learning, i.e. metrics and user-context properties that strongly correlate with learning when recommending items. Third, experimenting with various efficient ways to implement outcome-based assessments, possibly through automation or correlated metrics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Correlation between proposed indirect metrics and learning must be demonstrated in a specific setting before indirect measures replace assessment

Constraints¶

  • Designing well-aligned learning assessment is described as a demanding and resource-intensive task requiring domain knowledge and understanding of learners

Target Learners¶

  • developers and researchers of educational recommender systems
  • educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring pedagogical effectiveness of recommendations
  • aligning evaluation with learning outcomes

Claims¶

  • Formal education is by far the most common deployment context for ERS, with learners as the main end-users [+W]
  • Rating-based relevance is the most popular target metric in educational recommender systems, with only 12 of 28 reviewed papers optimizing learning-centered metrics [+W]
  • Higher user satisfaction and self-evaluation do not necessarily correlate with academic performance in an ERS deployment [+W]
