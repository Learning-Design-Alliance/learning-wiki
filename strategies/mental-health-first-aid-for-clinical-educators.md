# Mental health first aid training and wellbeing curricula to support educators and students with mental health FTP issues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes mental health supports as a response to educators' low confidence and comfort with mental health FTP issues. It notes that "a mental health first aid program improved participant's knowledge about mental health, increased confidence in helping those with mental health issues and benefitted participants' own mental health" in randomised controlled trials, and suggests wellbeing curricula shown to support stress management might be extended to clinical educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessible strategies to identify student mental health issues and respond appropriately including referral to appropriate support services
#### Constraints
- The article notes it may also be important to recognise the inherent stress in supporting mental health issues, patient safety and other high stakes concerns

### Target Learners
- Physiotherapy clinical educators
- Health professional students

### Target Learning Goals
- Mental health support
- Fitness to practise
- Stress management
