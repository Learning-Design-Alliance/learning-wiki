# Education systems should develop policies and practices that improve substitute teachers' work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond professionalization, the review calls for concrete system-level action: "systems need to develop policies and practices that improve substitute teachers' work." This recommendation follows the review's mixed-methods synthesis finding that substitute teachers' conditions and experiences are less than satisfactory and that this is exacerbated by a lack of targeted support. It is aimed at the education systems that employ and deploy substitute teachers in primary, middle, and secondary schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- System-level policy-making capacity over substitute teacher employment, deployment, and support.
#### Constraints
- The review links the need for such policies to its finding of unsatisfactory conditions and a lack of targeted support for this heterogeneous occupational group.

### Target Learners
- Substitute (casual/relief) teachers in primary/elementary, middle, and secondary/high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving substitute teachers' work conditions and experiences through targeted system policies and practices
