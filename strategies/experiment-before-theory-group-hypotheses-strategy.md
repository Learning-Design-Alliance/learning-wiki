# Present the Arduino experiment before the theoretical content, with students generating hypotheses in small groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's classroom procedure runs the experiment before the correlated content is taught: students in 4 groups develop hypotheses about the phenomenon, observe relationships among the physical quantities, and only after presenting a written report is the content presented and difficulties corrected. The stated aim is that "o aluno faça uma análise sobre o experimento e crie hipóteses acerca das relações entre as grandezas físicas", developing "um olhar crítico e científico sobre os fenômenos observados". Before the activity the teacher introduces initial Arduino concepts and provides a written script (roteiro) to guide the practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A roteiro (activity script) guiding the practice, initial Arduino concepts presented beforehand, and students divided into small groups to produce hypothesis reports before theory is given.
