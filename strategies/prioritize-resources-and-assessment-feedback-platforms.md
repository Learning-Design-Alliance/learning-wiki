# Prioritize systematic integration of instructional resources and timely feedback of assessment information in online teaching platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the functional-demand survey, the authors recommend that online teaching platforms "prioritize systematic integration of instructional resources and timely feedback of assessment-related information", since learners value structured, reusable resources and transparent assessment requirements. Future optimization should include standardized skills demonstration videos, visualized skill assessment and progress reports, and intelligent recommendation systems based on learning behavior data.
