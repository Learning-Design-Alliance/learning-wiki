# Train writing teachers to provide corrective feedback through elicitation and metalinguistic clues in interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that teacher preparation programs familiarize teachers with different feedback types. It suggests "providing training courses for them, and familiarizing them with different types of feedback, especially elicitation and metalinguistic clues, through interaction". The aim is to make teachers conscious of the significance of proper corrective feedback so learners notice and correct their own errors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training courses covering feedback typology, especially elicitation and metalinguistic clues
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single quasi-experimental study with 25 participants per group

### Target Learners
- pre-service and in-service EFL writing teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- delivering interactive corrective feedback
- error treatment in L2 writing instruction
