# Adopt the flipped classroom when sufficient teaching resources are available, and design distance courses progressively with clearly instructed in-class group activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation for instructors choosing between teaching models. They write that teachers "should consider using the flipped classroom if there are sufficient teaching resources available" and suggest instructors "design the distance learning courses progressively and conduct the in-class group activities with clear instruction." The model gives learners opportunities to interact with TAs and peers while receiving immediate in-person guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient teaching resources, including TAs for group guidance
- Clear instruction for in-class group activities
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single quasi-experiment in one compulsory freshman physics course

### Target Learners
- university students in distance or blended courses

### Target Learning Goals
- applying knowledge through problem-solving activities
- deepening understanding of pre-class materials through in-class interaction
