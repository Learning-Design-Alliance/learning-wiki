# Use a structured 3-phase debriefing model as the core of in situ simulation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Debriefing is described as one of the most important features of in situ SET and forms the core of training. The tutor employs a structured 3-stage model divided into periods of response, understanding, and summary. During the response period, nurses express feelings about the training; during the understanding period, the tutor discusses the simulation process and whether communication and co-operation were reasonable; during the summary period, each nurse reviews and shares key points learned and applies them to clinical work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two tutors co-ordinating guided feedback for approximately 30 min each time
- A learning environment of respect, safety, confidentiality, and trust

### Target Learners
- Clinical nurses with less than 5 years of work experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflect on simulation behaviors and results
- Adjust incorrect thinking and psychological frameworks
- Apply key learning points to clinical work
