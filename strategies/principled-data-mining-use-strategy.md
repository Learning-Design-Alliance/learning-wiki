# Use data mining in education research in a principled, goal-driven way informed by domain knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's implications section recommends that researchers "be principled in their use of this approach": methods and interpretation should be informed by what is known in the field, the specific objective (prediction, theory development, or hypothesis generation) should determine the method, and special attention should go to sampling, over-fit avoidance, and predictor set completeness. It also urges researchers to maintain a balanced perspective and report null results and unintended downstream consequences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires domain knowledge: mining data "with hardly any knowledge of the domain" is likely to hinder the field

- The verdict is still out on data mining's usefulness in educational research; convincing applications remain rare even in learning analytics and EDM
