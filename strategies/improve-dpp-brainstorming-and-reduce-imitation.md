# Provide more opportunities for brainstorming and discourage imitation of teacher operations in distributed pair programming

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback and the null/negative quantitative results, the authors recommend that teachers provide more opportunities for students' brainstorming, deter students from imitating teachers' programming operations, encourage idea sharing and discussion between partners, and avoid conflicts between distributed pair programming and peer-facilitated learning implementations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must actively structure pair activities to promote independent idea generation rather than replication of demonstrations
#### Constraints
