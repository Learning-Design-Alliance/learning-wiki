# Listening to the Voices of Students in PBL

## Description
Listening to the voices of students involves giving them authorship in sharing and telling their own stories, creating agency and leadership. Student voice is essential in PBL, as it creates agency and leadership and helps build the culture for PBL. This approach contrasts with traditional teaching methodologies where students are not given importance in processing, challenging, and questioning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom environment that values student input and storytelling, and instructional strategies that support student voice and choice.
#### Constraints
- May require a shift in traditional teaching roles and practices, and necessitates creating a safe and inclusive space for students to share their stories.
