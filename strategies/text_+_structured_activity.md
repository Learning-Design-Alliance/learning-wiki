# Text + Structured Activity

## Description
Students engage with text resources (physical or digital) accompanied by a structured activity designed to ensure active learning and engagement with the material. This combination provides instruction even in the pre-class phase. The text serves to convey information, while the structured activity facilitates understanding and application.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful selection or creation of text resources and design of structured activities. Instructor must provide clear instructions and feedback.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of content or learners. Requires careful planning to ensure active engagement and understanding.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who prefer text-based learning and those with limited access to technology or bandwidth.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Provide accessible and flexible content, promote active learning, facilitate understanding and application of concepts.

### Affordances
- Offers a low-tech alternative to video, provides flexibility and accessibility, promotes active engagement with the material.

### Personalization
- Structured activities can be tailored to different learning styles and needs. Text resources can be selected based on learner interests and preferences.
