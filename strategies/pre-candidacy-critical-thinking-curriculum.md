# Implement pre-candidacy curriculum supporting critical thinking and analytical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because verbal GRE percentiles, which the authors describe as examining "the ability of an applicant to comprehend complex literature and form conclusions based on the information provided," predicted candidacy exam outcomes, the article recommends implementing programs and targeted curriculum pre-candidacy to develop PhD students' critical and analytical thinking skills. The progression from pre-candidacy to post-candidacy is treated as a key transition phase and common success measure in STEM PhD programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must identify the pre-candidacy period as the window for targeted skill development.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single-institution correlational finding; the article notes additional studies are needed for some related results.

### Target Learners
- pre-candidacy PhD students in biomedical sciences

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking and analytical skills needed for the PhD candidacy examination
