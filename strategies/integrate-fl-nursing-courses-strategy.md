# Integrate flipped learning into theoretical and clinical nursing courses to build lifelong-learning competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends integrating flipped learning into nursing curricula because it requires students to come prepared and to search for resources, preparing them to be lifelong learners who keep current with evidence, and because its active environment lets students practice interdisciplinary communication and collaboration. The article states: "This researcher recommends integrating this learning approach into theoretical and clinical nursing courses and studying its effect on the student’s learning outcomes and acquiring competencies."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need to understand implementation challenges, since integrating FL in nursing courses might be challenging for both students and the educator
- Technological infrastructure, user-friendly platforms, and clear expectations
#### Constraints
- Students reported stress managing assignments and unfamiliar digital tools; success depends on cultural context, technological capacity, and readiness for self-directed learning

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in Saudi Arabia

### Target Learning Goals
- lifelong learning habits
- evidence-searching competency
- interdisciplinary communication and collaboration
