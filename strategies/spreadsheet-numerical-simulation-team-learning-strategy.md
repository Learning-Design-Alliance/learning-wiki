# Use simple spreadsheet-based numerical simulation of orbital systems as an engaging team-based learning activity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends having students solve the multiple-body motion equations numerically themselves, using "a simple spreadsheet where a convenient, but accessible, manipulation of equations can be made to solve the problems numerically". The authors state this is "a very engaging strategy that involves actively students in team-based learning and results in an effective understanding of physics". Students compute trajectories with basic equations and no pre-established formulas, only considering gravitational interactions.
