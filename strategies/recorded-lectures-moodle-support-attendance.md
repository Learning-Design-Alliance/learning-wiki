# Provide recorded lectures and web-based course management systems to support attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the review's implications for practice, providing recorded lectures and introducing web-based course management systems "appeared to positively impact attendance", and blended learning models combining face-to-face and online synchronous teaching were associated with increased attendance. Educators can adopt these innovations without fearing attendance loss.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In-person or blended delivery format with supporting digital infrastructure
#### Constraints
- Associations are based on single studies, some without statistical testing; the review calls the evidence base nascent

### Target Learners
- medical students
- medical educators

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining attendance at in-person teaching sessions
