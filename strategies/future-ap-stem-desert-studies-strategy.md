## Description
The authors propose future investigations building on their physics findings: similar studies of STEM AP class availability along demographic lines, whether one AP STEM class makes others more likely at a school, whether there are "AP STEM deserts" reflecting certain demographics, and examination of access through a rural/suburban/urban lens. They note that in 2017 Hispanic students took about 39% of AP exams in California despite being 54% of the state's public school population.
