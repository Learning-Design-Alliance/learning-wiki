# Use advanced LLMs as supplementary oncology education and decision-support tools, with outputs verified by medical professionals and models regularly updated

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that LLMs such as Claude 3.5 Sonnet and ChatGPT 4o be treated as potential aids in oncology education and clinical decision support — assisting clinicians in staying updated, supporting patient education, and enhancing decision-making — while cautioning that "The models should not be used as a sole source of information, and their outputs should be verified by medical professionals." It further states regular updates and enhancements are necessary to maintain relevance and accuracy, especially to incorporate the latest medical advancements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Outputs verified by medical professionals before use
- Regular updates with the latest medical literature and guidelines
#### Constraints
- The article notes a survey of Turkish oncologists revealed lower-than-expected AI literacy and utilization in clinical practice, and that multiple-choice assessment may not fully capture the complexity of clinical reasoning required in oncology practice

### Target Learners
- medical oncologists
- oncology trainees
- patients receiving education support

### Target Learning Goals
- staying updated with oncology knowledge
- clinical decision support
- patient education

## Claims

- [ChatGPT 4o outperformed Llama-3 and Gemini 1.5 across all question categories, with an average score of 67.8 and its highest accuracy in basic oncology (86.4%)](../claims/chatgpt-4o-second-tier-oncology-exam-performance.md) [+W]
- [Claude 3.5 Sonnet outperformed ChatGPT 4o, Llama-3 and Gemini 1.5 on Turkish oncology board examination questions, passing all eight annual exams with an average score of 77.6%](../claims/claude-35-sonnet-outperforms-other-llms-oncology-board-exams.md) [+W]
- [LLM accuracy varied significantly across oncology topics, with the lowest performance in colorectal tumors (mean 48.3%) and genitourinary tumors (mean 50.8%)](../claims/llm-accuracy-varies-across-oncology-topics.md) [~W]
- [LLM performance declined on more recent exams, with ChatGPT 4o failing the 2024 exam and Llama-3 failing the last four consecutive years, suggesting outdated training data](../claims/llm-performance-declines-recent-oncology-exams.md) [~W]
- [Overall performance differed significantly among the four LLMs, with a post-hoc ordering of Claude 3.5 Sonnet > ChatGPT 4o > Llama-3 ≈ Gemini 1.5, while passed-exam counts did not differ significantly](../claims/significant-llm-performance-differences-oncology-exams-anova.md) [+W]
