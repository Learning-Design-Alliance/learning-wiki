# Emotional Regulation Strategies

## Description
Educators identify students' strengths and challenges in emotional regulation—the ability to control responses to emotional stimuli. Strategies aligned with these strengths are used to support student learning and overall well-being. Emotional regulation can manifest as resolving conflicts, adapting to change, or handling frustration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of student strengths and emotional regulation skills.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent implementation and may not address underlying emotional issues.
