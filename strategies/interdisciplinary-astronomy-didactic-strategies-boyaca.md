# Develop interdisciplinary didactic strategies linking astronomy concepts with physics, chemistry and biology, and revise the curricular distribution of astronomical concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is to strengthen scientific concepts through didactic strategies that connect astronomy with other disciplines, and to review and update how astronomical concepts are distributed across national curricular guidelines so they align with the progression of supporting physical and chemical concepts. The authors argue this could "fomentar una comprensión más profunda del conocimiento científico" and promote participation in scientific careers and technological development in the region.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Didactic strategies must reinforce scientific concepts by linking them with other disciplines such as physics, chemistry and biology, as the article's conclusions state.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from an exploratory study of one regional student network in Boyacá and responds to the specific weaknesses documented there.

### Target Learners
- Secondary education students (grades 6–11, ages 10–19)

### Target Learning Goals
- Astronomy concepts aligned with Colombia's Estándares Básicos de Competencias and Derechos Básicos de Aprendizaje
