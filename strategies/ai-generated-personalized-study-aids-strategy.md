# Replace time-consuming searching for ambient study aids with AI-generated personalized audio and visuals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy addresses the burden of learners searching online for ASMR videos, white noise playlists, or relaxing images before studying. Instead, the system generates matched visual themes and auditory elements (white noise, ambient music, layered sounds) from user prompts, synchronized with a study timer. The article states that "Whisper alleviates this burden by using AI to generate personalized music and images based on user preferences, helping learners achieve a calm and focused state without the time-consuming search."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the LLM-based generation backends for images and music
- User input specifying preferred visual style, audio type, and duration
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness for focus and emotional regulation has not yet been validated with user data in this article

### Target Learners
- independent learners and professionals lacking access to ideal study spaces
- neurodivergent or easily distracted learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing setup time and distraction so learners reach a calm, focused state for self-directed study
