Implement CoBl with prepared resources, multi-role instructors, and active collaborative learners¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's suggestions section recommends conditions for adopting the CoBl model: prepare and allocate resources adequately (software, computer laboratory, presentation equipment, internet network); ensure instructors and learners understand the process and participate in group activities and online discussion via chat and LMS; and have instructors take many roles. The article states "Instructors take many roles such as facilitator, manager, planner, consultant, and assessors." Learners should actively play a role and cooperate in group activities and knowledge sharing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Stakeholder cooperation from administrators and teachers
  • Instructor and learner understanding of the teaching and learning process

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are drawn from a single 14-week implementation in one physical education institute

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate students
  • instructors in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning achievement
  • thinking ability
  • knowledge sharing

Claims¶

  • Experimental-group students showed significant pre-to-post gains in learning achievement with the CoBl model [+W]
  • CoBl students showed greater learning achievement than traditional-instruction control students (p = .001) [+W]
  • Students rated their opinions of learning with the CoBl model at the high agreed level (overall mean 4.23) [+W]
  • Both groups gained in thinking ability pre-to-post, but CoBl and traditional instruction did not differ in thinking ability (p = .501) [+W]
