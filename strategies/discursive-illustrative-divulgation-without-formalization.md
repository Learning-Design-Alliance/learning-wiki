# Discursive, illustrative presentation of rigorous science content without technical formalization for lay audiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For its divulgative activities aimed at the general public, the project prescribes a presentation style that keeps scientific rigour while dropping professional-level formalism: "si privilegerà una presentazione discorsiva, illustrativa, volta alla descrizione della fenomenologia più che alla formalizzazione tecnica". Content is not simplified in difficulty; rather, phenomena are explained rigorously but without the technical and mathematical formalization relevant only to a professional approach, and without gratuitous spectacularization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content of varying and not necessarily small difficulty, presented as self-contained monographic initiatives not requiring attendance at one another
#### Constraints
- Intended for leisure-time socialization and cultural recreation rather than professional enrichment; formalization is set aside only for this lay audience

### Target Learners
- the general citizenry
- adults and families in their leisure time

### Target Learning Goals
- contact with natural sciences and with the world of those who work in them professionally
