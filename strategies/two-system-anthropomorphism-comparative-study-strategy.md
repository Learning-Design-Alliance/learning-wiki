# Comparative study design: two learning systems differing in anthropomorphic factors, a decision-making task, and emotional-state questionnaires

## Description
To evaluate whether anthropomorphized LLM chatbots can induce intense emotions of irritation and strangeness and whether these emotions influence learning outcomes, the authors plan a comparative study. "For this study, we will develop two different learning systems: one system integrating the relevant factors of anthropomorphism and one which does not." Performance and decision-making times are captured via a decision-making task, and "we will evaluate the emotional states of the participants during the task using questionnaires".
