# Six engagement-based incentive mechanisms that build evaluative moments into chatbot interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article transfers misinformation-intervention logic to conversational AI: "Engagement-based incentive mechanisms remove this dependence by building evaluative moments into the flow of interaction itself." Table 1 details six mechanisms — cooling-off periods, engagement gates, structured dialogue scaffolds, adaptive friction, social verification signals, and commitment prompts — each with a trigger, example implementation, primary outcome measure, and primary user profile served. The chatbot setting adds sustained, personalized, bidirectional interaction that static platforms lack.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mechanisms must activate under high-stakes conditions and remain skippable to protect autonomy
#### Constraints
- Social verification signals in particular must earn their keep empirically, since a direct tournament of message-based norm nudges found no significant effects

### Target Learners
- Low-motivation and low-ability chatbot users

### Target Learning Goals
- Making evaluation happen by building it into the interaction
