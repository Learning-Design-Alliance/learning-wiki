# Push for Deeper Analysis

## Description
Encourage students to go beyond surface-level analysis and consider how sentence structure and other elements create meaning. Give them time to reread and think.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material. Time for reflection and analysis. A classroom environment that values critical thinking.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle with deeper analysis. Requires careful scaffolding and support. May be time-consuming.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for promoting critical thinking and analytical skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance critical thinking skills, promote deeper understanding of text, develop the ability to analyze complex relationships between elements of writing, encourage thoughtful reflection

### Affordances
- Challenges students to think beyond the obvious. Encourages them to consider multiple layers of meaning. Creates a more engaging and intellectually stimulating learning experience.

### Personalization
- Students are given the freedom to explore their own interpretations. The teacher can provide prompts and questions to guide their analysis. Students can work independently or in groups.
