# Assess e-learning readiness and motivation before implementing the flipped classroom, and provide brief in-class explanations of out-of-class assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the study's problems and conclusions, the article recommends that "students' motivation and readiness level to learn outside the classroom be identified and necessary arrangements be done before applying the FC Model". It also suggests instructors provide a brief in-class explanation about the assignments students must complete outside the classroom, use Khan Academy videos as video materials, use Kahoot as an assessment tool, choose rich content videos produced specifically for out-of-class study, and reflect the classroom environment in video images while organizing activities around learners' needs and interests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors can identify students' readiness level for e-learning before implementation
- Videos appropriate to the students' level must be produced or sourced from open-access materials
#### Constraints
- The article states the study is limited to a small number of students in one Computer I course, so generalization requires larger samples in different courses and education levels

- higher education students
