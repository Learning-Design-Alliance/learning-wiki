# Recruit female instructors and explicitly request female nominations to improve gender balance in outreach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
ThaiPASS participation is by teacher nomination, and the organisers strongly encouraged each school to nominate at least one female student. Feedback showed that "Feedback from female participants clearly praised the positive impact of having female instructors, who were regarded as positive role models for women in STEM." The team plans to explicitly ask schools to nominate female students, recruit more female instructors (particularly PhD assistants) with the aim of a 50:50 balance, and run a separate five-day Python training for teachers with attention to gender balance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools willing to act on nomination requests; funding for a larger applicant pool
#### Constraints
- ThaiPASS'19 performed worse in overall F:M ratio (67:33 students) than 2018 (50:50), and teacher and instructor balance still uneven

### Target Learners
- female secondary school students in STEM outreach
- teachers attending CPD

### Target Learning Goals
- aspiration and participation of women in STEM
- access to STEM career role models
