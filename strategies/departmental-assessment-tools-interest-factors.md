# Use departmental assessment tools to identify factors that most positively and negatively shape students' interest in physics methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors' long-term goal is to "design departmental assessment tools that can identify which factors contribute most positively and negatively to students' interest in a particular method of doing physics", leading to evidence-based strategies implemented in physics departments to support students. This follows from their finding that learning experiences, self-efficacy, outcome expectations and belonging shape low interest.
