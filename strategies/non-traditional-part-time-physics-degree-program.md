# Offer a non-traditional part-time physics degree program for in-service working students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes UHCL's non-traditional physics degree program, whose majority of students are in-service part-time students who take one or two courses per semester while working. These students are described as more motivated, with employer support contingent on at least B grades, and graduate class sizes of "usually 8 to 10". The article recommends such programs be developed at other state colleges, especially favorably located campuses, to promote physics and increase enrollment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Favorable campus location for working students
- Small graduate class sizes comparable to research universities
- Employer financial support tied to grade performance
#### Constraints
- Students face time limitations due to work responsibilities and take only one or two courses per semester

### Target Learners
- In-service part-time students with jobs
- Working professionals seeking physics degrees

### Target Learning Goals
- Physics degree completion while employed
- Improved physics understanding and problem solving
