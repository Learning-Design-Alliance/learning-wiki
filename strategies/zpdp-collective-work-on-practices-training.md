# Teacher professional development scenarios built on collective work on actual practices, extending the ZPD to a ZPDP

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors extended their reflection to teacher training by enlarging the notion of ZPD to the development of practices (ZPDP, P for practices or professional). Training scenarios strongly involve collective work on actual practices, allowing the installation of questioning, awareness-raising, and discussion of palettes of possibles in terms of choices of content and classroom unfoldings. The work also included transposing the research tools for trainers and teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
