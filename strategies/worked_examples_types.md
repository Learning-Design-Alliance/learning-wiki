# Worked Examples Types

## Description
Modelling examples provide maximum guidance by paying explicit attention to the process needed to reach an acceptable solution, such as a librarian internship where the librarian explains what (s)he’s doing and why it’s done in that particular way. Case studies could be a next step in the learning process, giving learners descriptions of actual or hypothetical problem solutions situated in the real world.
