# Embed peer learning activities and recorded-performance review in RT remedial teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that RT instruction incorporate social learning strategies: engaging underachievers in cooperative learning, small group discussions, and evaluating learning through recorded sessions. These activities give students opportunities to practice English communication in a real context, encourage participation, and, per the article, increase willingness to participate and reverse negative emotions toward English learning. The experimental group's lessons paired oral reading, repetition, role assignments, performing, video recording, and group discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A comfortable and supportive learning environment with good teacher-student interaction and mutual peer support
#### Constraints
- Students' communicative abilities can be exposed to scrutiny in the classroom, which can heighten learning anxiety

### Target Learners
- underachieving college English learners

### Target Learning Goals
- oral reading ability
- willingness to participate in classroom activities
- English communication skills
