# Disease-centered case selection linking non-malignant disease datasets to related cancer datasets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Cases are chosen so each disease connects multiple datasets and algorithms without fragmented analysis. The article 'opted for a disease-centered approach, selecting gout, gastritis, liver cirrhosis, inflammatory bowel disease, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease as teaching practice cases,' each paired with a cancer it may progress to (e.g., gout to cholangiocarcinoma), so students trace one disease from gene expression data through machine learning modeling to survival analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Literature review establishing disease-to-cancer progression links; matching GSE, TCGA, and UK Biobank data items per case
#### Constraints
- Aims to illustrate disease progression in the absence of clinical intervention; depends on availability of suitable public datasets

### Target Learners
- medical students in interdisciplinary practice teaching

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering dataset operations and the standard disease-based simulation analysis process
