# Optimise the mix of classroom instruction with online learning and performance support tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "the blend itself will focus on optimizing the mix of classroom instruction with online learning, and performance support tools that can maximize the total impact on students learning experiences in Indian context". It recommends that blended learning design combine the classroom segment's role plays, case studies, and discovery learning procedures exploring higher order thinking about real-life issues with technology-enhanced online components, sustaining the shift from teacher-centred to student-centred learning culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consideration of students' profiles and backgrounds in design
- Making students part of the learning resource and material
- Sustaining the shift to a student-centred learning culture
#### Constraints
- Challenges include unrealistic student expectations, student-perceived isolation, technological problems, time commitment, and lack of support for course redesign

### Target Learners
- students in Indian higher education and teacher education contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- deeper understanding of basic concepts and procedures
- independence in learning
- student satisfaction
