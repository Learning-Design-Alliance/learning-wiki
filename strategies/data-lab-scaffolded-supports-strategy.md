# Layered supports easing students into open-ended laboratory work: workshops, pre-class homework, notebooks, and CPHWs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
DATA Lab scaffolds its open-ended experiments with a stack of supports: three DL1 workshops on measurement, uncertainty, and documentation; an introductory experiment all students complete; pre-class homework of "2-4 questions designed to have students prepare for the upcoming experiment"; frequent feedback through in-class participation grades and lab notebooks; and three communication-project homework assignments. A CP prep day in spring gives in-class time before the first formal CP.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors who can draw on pre-class homework answers to be more responsive during class
- Rubrics for participation, notebooks, and CPHWs aligned with CP rubrics
#### Constraints
- The CP prep day depends on the MSU academic calendar offering time in the spring semester

### Target Learners
- Introductory physics students new to open-ended experimental work

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for experiments, reflection on progress, documentation practice, and development of communication-project components
