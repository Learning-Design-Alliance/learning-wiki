# Embed disaster preparedness, crisis pedagogy, and CT-focused high-stakes instruction in teacher education programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that "teacher education programs should incorporate dedicated coursework on disaster preparedness and crisis pedagogy" and that "instructional approaches that develop critical thinking in high-stakes contexts — including problem-based learning, scenario analysis, and disaster-based case studies — should be systematically embedded in teacher training curricula." Universities should also develop institutional emergency distance education protocols specifying flexible assessment, psychosocial support pathways, and crisis pedagogy guidelines prior to crisis events.
