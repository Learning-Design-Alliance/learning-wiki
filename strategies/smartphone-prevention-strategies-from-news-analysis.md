# Use newspaper coverage patterns to plan psycho-socio-educational prevention strategies about teenage smartphone use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames its analysis as a starting point for prevention planning: "This analysis is a starting point for planning psycho-socio-educational prevention strategies to help raise awareness to society, educators and the family environment about the benefits and risks of smartphone use by adolescents." Schools should offer targeted protection to minors, involve families actively, and establish clear usage rules and times agreed with teenagers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness among administration, society, educators and families of the risks of teenage smartphone use
#### Constraints
- The study analysed only main Spanish digital newspapers, so findings may not generalise to other countries or media

### Target Learners
- teenagers
- families
- educators

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible smartphone use
- digital, emotional and psychosocial skills
- media literacy
