# Assign end-of-unit letter-writing tasks summarizing science concepts for a chosen audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete implementable activity: after completing a science unit, students summarize the major concepts in the form of a letter addressed to a specified audience (younger students, peers, or teacher), written at home and submitted within a set period. The study gave students a handout informing them how to write a letter and asked them to summarize major mixture concepts such as types of mixtures and separation of mixtures. Teachers can assess the letters with a structured writing scale covering language, content, and audience appropriateness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A handout informing students how to write a letter
- A specified audience for each letter
- Adequate submission time (the study allowed one week)
#### Constraints
- Students unfamiliar with letter writing may find the format boring, as 12% of interviewed students did; teacher-audience letters risk becoming evaluation-oriented reproductions of taught content

### Target Learners
- 9th grade chemistry students
- secondary science students

### Target Learning Goals
- reviewing and consolidating unit concepts
- audience-appropriate communication of science
- conceptual understanding of mixtures

### Affordances
- Prain Hand Five Element Writing Framework
