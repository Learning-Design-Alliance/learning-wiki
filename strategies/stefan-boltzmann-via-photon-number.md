# Present the Stefan-Boltzmann law in two steps: photon number flux first, then total power as photon number times average photon energy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends extending the photon-based presentation to the Stefan-Boltzmann law: first give the photon flux Nphot = (1.5205 × 10¹⁵ photons m⁻² s⁻¹ K⁻³)·T³, then combine it with the average photon energy to recover F = σT⁴. The authors state this approach "spells out the two main concepts behind the Stefan-Boltzmann law more explicitly," since students can see the two factors — average photon energy and photons emitted per second — in the product.
