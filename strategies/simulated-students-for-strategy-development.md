# Use simulated students with richer cognitive and motivational models to develop more effective teaching strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes that progress toward motivationally sophisticated teaching tactics may come from using simulated students, such as SimStudent, "but incorporating more cognitively and motivationally mature models of human learning" as a route to developing more effective teaching strategies. SimStudent is a machine-learning, knowledge-tracing simulated student embodying the same skill representation as cognitive tutors, and studies with it identified factors such as the quality of learners' explanations that helped both learner and simulated student learn.
