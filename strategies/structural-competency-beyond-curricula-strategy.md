# Operationalize structural competency at provider, organizational, and systems levels beyond curricular frameworks

## Description
The article recommends that "structural competency must be developed in nursing beyond curricular frameworks," with nursing research creating a framework to operationalize the concept "at provider, organizational, and systems-level contexts, in addition to the educational context." The health-related literature offers guidance on applying structural competency to program development for populations such as people who inject drugs in the forensic system and survivors of domestic violence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nursing must develop response and action capacities, which the nursing literature comparatively lacks
#### Constraints
- Most structural competency literature arises from the United States' predominantly for-profit and fragmented health system; no published Canadian literature on the concept exists to date
