# Train allied health professionals to deliver counseling, nutrition education and hygiene support, offsetting physician workload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
To address de-prioritization of health promotion under heavy patient load, the article proposes that "Training the allied health professionals like AYUSH practitioners, dieticians, physiotherapists etc. for counseling, nutrition education, hygiene, physical activities etc. could be a cost effective and efficient solution for the same." This distributes health promotion delivery beyond physicians, whose curative workload crowds out preventive care.
