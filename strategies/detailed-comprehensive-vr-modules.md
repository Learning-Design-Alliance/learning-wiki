# Develop detailed and comprehensive VR modules that include all steps of construction processes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that VR applications for construction learning be complete and detailed, because early versions missing steps reduced their benefit. Participants indicated early VR applications "were missing critical steps in construction processes, which were later improved", and adding steps significantly enhanced comprehension of the building process. The review concludes a detailed and comprehensive VR module is needed to maximize learning benefits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Iterative user feedback during module development to identify missing steps
#### Constraints
- Initial implementation may face challenges related to the completeness and detail of the simulations

### Target Learners
- Construction, architecture and engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehension of multi-step construction processes such as building assembly sequences
