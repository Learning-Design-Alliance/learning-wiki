# Promote a Growth Mindset

## Description
Promoting a growth mindset involves giving feedback that values effort and encourages persistence. In mathematics, students often think that intelligence is fixed. By valuing effort and encouraging persistence, teachers can show students that there is always room for them to improve their skills. This helps students develop a more positive attitude toward learning and encourages them to persevere through challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need to shift their focus from assessing innate ability to assessing effort and progress. They should be prepared to provide specific, actionable feedback that focuses on students’ strategies and approaches.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in classrooms where traditional assessment methods are heavily emphasized. Requires a significant shift in teacher mindset and instructional practices.

### Affordances
- Supports the development of a growth mindset and encourages students to persevere through challenges. It helps students see learning as a process of continuous improvement and encourages them to take risks and embrace new challenges. It can also lead to increased self-efficacy and a more positive attitude toward learning.

### Personalization
- Teachers can tailor feedback to individual students, focusing on their effort, strategies, and progress. They can also provide opportunities for students to reflect on their own learning and identify areas for improvement.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess students’ attitudes toward learning through observation and participation. Collect feedback from students on their perceptions of the classroom environment and their feelings about their own learning. Analyze student work to identify evidence of effort, persistence, and a growth mindset.

## Impact
- Research shows that promoting a growth mindset can lead to increased motivation, improved performance, and a more positive attitude toward learning. Students are more likely to persevere through challenges and achieve their goals when they believe that their abilities can be developed through effort and practice.
