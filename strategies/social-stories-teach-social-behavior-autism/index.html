Use social stories to teach appropriate social behavior through structured short stories¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Social stories are brief stories describing a social situation and presenting effective behavioral responses "through sentences that: reflect specific situations, written according to clear rules, supported by visual stimulus". They answer who, what, when, where, and why, and are supported by techniques such as prompting, modeling, role playing, and feedback. The article's program used them to develop social skills.
