# One-day nurse training in motivational interviewing, stage-of-change, medication side effects, SMI lifestyle risks and the TLM web tool

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before the trial starts, nurses delivering the 12-month intervention receive one day of training covering (a) basic components of motivational interviewing and the stage of change model, (b) side effects of psychotropic medication, (c) lifestyle of and risks for SMI patients, (d) working with TLM, and (e) environmental factors affecting effective use of TLM, such as staff health behaviour or availability of unhealthy products at home. An evaluation session after three months is planned to discuss obstacles with the tool and in motivating patients, and to recollect the study protocol.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A three-month evaluation session and regular research team visits to sustain implementation
#### Constraints
- Trained LION nurses are, due to the nature of the intervention, not blind for study allocation

### Target Learners
- Mental health nurses in Flexible Assertive Community Treatment and sheltered living teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Motivational interviewing skills
- Knowledge of SMI lifestyle risks and psychotropic medication side effects
- Practical skill in working with the TLM web tool
