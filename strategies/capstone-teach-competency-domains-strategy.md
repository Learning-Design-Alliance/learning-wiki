## Description
The article identifies how the global health capstone can be used as a tool to address each CUGH competency domain. For example, completing literature reviews based on capstone research questions can increase understanding of morbidity and mortality in the population/region of interest, and developing equitable relationships with local partners and key stakeholders builds collaboration skills.
