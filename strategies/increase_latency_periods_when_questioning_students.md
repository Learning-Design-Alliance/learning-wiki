# Increase Latency Periods When Questioning Students

## Description
Increasing latency (wait time) for low-achieving learners will help them improve. Give learners adequate time to answer questions to communicate that you have positive expectations for them. The instructor could ask a teaching peer to observe the instruction and chart the length of the latency periods given to each learner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors should ensure they are not cutting off low-achieving learners too quickly. Monitor which learners are getting more time than others.
#### Constraints
- This can be difficult to implement without practice and thought. Some learners may not need the time and may feel they are being singled out for not knowing the answer.
