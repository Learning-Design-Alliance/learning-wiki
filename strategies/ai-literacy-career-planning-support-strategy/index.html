Integrate AI literacy, digital skill development, and technology-focused career planning into coursework, with targeted support for higher-anxiety groups¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on its findings, the article recommends that universities offer programs that raise awareness of AI-driven change, foster positive AI attitudes, and reduce anxiety. It states that "Integrating AI literacy, digital skill development, and technology focused career planning into coursework would be helpful." Because anxiety was higher among female, social science, and second-year students, it recommends prioritizing career counseling, psychosocial assistance, and skills-based training for these groups, plus early career-orientation initiatives and AI-supported tools expanding internships, project-based learning, and structured mentoring.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional commitment to embedding AI literacy and career planning within existing coursework, and targeted counseling resources for higher-anxiety groups.

Constraints¶

  • The article notes policy-level strategies should also address structural inequalities to reduce interdisciplinary gaps in career readiness, implying coursework alone is insufficient.

Target Learners¶

  • university students, especially female students, social science majors, and second-year students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI literacy, digital skills, career orientation, and labor market preparedness

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate career adaptability cultivation and AI literacy with career planning in higher education curricula
  • Integrate AI literacy into health sciences curricula and tailor career counselling to programme type
  • Differentiated interventions integrating AI literacy training, psychological capital cultivation, and professional identity reinforcement
  • Combine accurate AI labor-market information, employability-building activities, and growth-oriented career support for students facing AI employment threat
  • Fund AI literacy initiatives integrating AI ethics into curricula and adapting assessments
