# Pair and group writing exercises: categorizing items, completing outline skeletons, and discussing paragraphs with a partner

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's supplementary exercises implement a concrete activity recipe: students work with partners to organize items into main groups, read a passage and complete an outline skeleton together, and individually write a paragraph on a topic and then discuss their ideas with a partner. Each exercise names its social arrangement, e.g. "Work with your partners. Organize the following food into three main groups."
