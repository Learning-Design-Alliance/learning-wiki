# Structured Reviewing: reviewing vocabulary at progressively increasing intervals until materials become automatic

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Structured Reviewing is a direct vocabulary learning strategy in which learners review materials at different intervals. The article describes it as acquiring a collection of vocabulary items, then practicing them 15 minutes later, an hour later, three hours later, the next day, two days later, four days later, the following week, and so on until the materials became more or less automatic. Learners are also suggested to put the vocabulary into a context or recombine them to make new sentences.

## Design Implications
