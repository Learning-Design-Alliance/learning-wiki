# Dolch Sight Word Lists

## Description
The Dolch Sight word list includes the most common 220 words and 95 nouns encountered in children’s books. Dolch words, or sight words, are critical in early reading development because they represent high-frequency words and are difficult to sound out or to illustrate. Resources include lists organized alphabetically by grade, by frequency by grade, and by frequency combined, with activities free to copy and distribute.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to printable worksheets and activities. Digital resources can be used for virtual learning environments. Teacher guidance in selecting appropriate lists and activities.
#### Constraints
- Focus on memorization may not suit all learners; contextual understanding of words is also needed. The lists may need to be supplemented with additional words for diverse reading materials.
