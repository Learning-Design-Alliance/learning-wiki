# Carefully select songs for EFL teaching, since only carefully selected songs evidently support vocabulary and grammar acquisition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers screen songs before classroom use. Problems it names include lyrics that "pay little or no attention to capitalization and punctuation", informal or dialogic wording that may confuse learners, and casually written lyrics that fall short of grammatical completeness. The conclusion states that "only carefully selected songs can be e vidently helpful with the acquisition of vocabulary and grammar", so teachers of song education need to be especially careful in this regard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must vet song lyrics for capitalization, punctuation, register, and grammatical completeness before assigning them
#### Constraints
- The article states there could be more discussion of the standard for song selection and proposes a detailed discussion of selection criteria as future work

### Target Learners
- Chinese EFL learners, especially English majors

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary and grammar acquisition
- lexico-grammatical accuracy
