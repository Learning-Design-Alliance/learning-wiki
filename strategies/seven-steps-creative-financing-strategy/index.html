7 Steps to Creative Financing: a stakeholder-team process for identifying financing for ed-tech¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy is "a new process and set of best practices designed to help districts identify the best means to financing ed-tech solutions." It assembles a diverse team of district stakeholders, end users, and local experts to identify underlying needs, gather inspirational finance solutions from outside industries, and brainstorm ideas. The seven steps are: Set Vision, Build Team, Build the Case, Brainstorm Solutions, Meet the Board, Build Based on Imput, and Apply Process Elsewhere. It responds to the theme that shifting, complex, and limited resources make sustainment a challenge.
