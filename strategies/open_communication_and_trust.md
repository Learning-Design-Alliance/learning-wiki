# Open Communication and Trust

## Description
Friendly message from teacher to foster open, comfortable communication systems and mutual respect, and when helping students succeed becomes a group effort, academics and attitudes improve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Easy and convenient means to communicate with students and families
#### Constraints
- Parent must respond to teacher and family to have open communication
