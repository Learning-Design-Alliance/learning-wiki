# Apply MPSA Cone across professions to interpret cognitive structure, stress, personnel selection, and robot design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes the MPSA Cone as an interpretive tool across diversified professions: in information technology, it "may provide alternative inquiry and interpretations regarding human's cognitive structure" when studying how people retrieve information presented by technology experts; in psychology, counseling, and health professions, for studying stress caused by differing problem-solving modes; in business, for identifying CEOs, fund managers, and analysts; and in engineering, for deciphering robot design styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practitioners need to determine which mode of problem-solving action characterizes the clients, personnel, or systems they are analyzing
#### Constraints
- All applications are speculative proposals; the article poses questions (e.g., whether technical-mode CEOs perform better in economic downturns) without answering them with data

### Target Learners
- professionals in information technology, psychology, counseling, family and consumer sciences, health professions, business, and engineering

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting clients' and colleagues' problem-solving dispositions
- professional development and personnel selection
