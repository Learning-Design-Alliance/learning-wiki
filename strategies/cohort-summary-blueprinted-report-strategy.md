# Provide a cohort summary blueprinted report so teachers can identify gaps in teaching and trends in learner performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A summary blueprinted report of average performance for the whole cohort gives the teacher the ability to generalise trends in the acquisition of objectives. Because each assessment item is mapped to a learning objective and the teaching session it was taught in, the report shows what content was well communicated, highlights gaps in teaching or missing resources, and can reveal trends in weaker or stronger learners to inform future teaching. Blueprinting also saves time compared with meeting students individually and allows mapping of objectives sampled across sessions.
