# Culturally informed research agenda: re-evaluate motivation constructs within cultural socialization, test cultural moderation, and develop alternative East Asian frameworks

## Description
The article proposes directions for future motivation research grounded in its cultural analysis. Researchers should 're-evaluate the nature of widely used motivation constructs within the context of particular cultural socialization, to explicitly test cultural moderation in motivational dynamics, and to develop alternative concepts and frameworks that more accurately represent East Asian students' motivation.' The goal is refining and expanding motivation theories so their explanatory power broadens across diverse contexts.
