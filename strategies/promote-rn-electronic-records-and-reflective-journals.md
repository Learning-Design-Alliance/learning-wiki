# Promote Reference Nurses' use of the electronic record system and analyze student reflective journals for improvement areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend two follow-up actions: encouraging the clinical nurses who supervise students (Reference Nurses) to use the electronic registration system as a learning and assessment tool, and analyzing the reflective journals in student portfolios. They state that software tools "can improve clinical skills of nursing students and encourage them in learning and implementing the nursing process during the clinical practice", and that journal analysis "could help us identify other possible areas for improvement in the teaching-learning process".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Involvement of all stakeholders, including Reference Nurses and professors, in continuous quality improvement
#### Constraints
- Innovative use of mobile electronic recording during clinical practice can cause resistance and hinder access to records

### Target Learners
- Second-year undergraduate Nursing students in supervised clinical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- Implementing the nursing process and clinical documentation skills
