# Cooperative adaptation-card sorting to compare two turtle species

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A pre-visit activity in which "Students will participate in a cooperative learning activity designed to illustrate the similarities and differences between the loggerhead sea turtle and the box turtle." Each student receives a card describing one adaptation, reads it aloud to classmates, and physically sorts into two groups by species without showing cards. Groups then prepare and present a natural history of their turtle and help complete a class comparison chart.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One set of 30 Turtle Adaptation Cards, cut and shuffled; time limits set by the teacher
#### Constraints
- Group size of maximum 30 students; 45-60 minutes

### Target Learners
- Grades 5-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Adaptations and anatomy
- Comparing marine and terrestrial species
- Inference about endangered status
