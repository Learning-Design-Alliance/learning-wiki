# Subscribe to arXiv daily email lists for a subfield to track the newest results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To keep up with the bleeding edge of a subfield, the guide recommends signing up on the arXiv help page for daily emails listing papers related to specific astronomy subfields, such as Astrophysics of Galaxies or Earth and Planetary Astrophysics. It also points to the arXiver webpage, an automated figure-focused view of the latest uploads, for readers who find the email walls of text unengaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An email subscription via the arXiv help page, or use of the arXiver webpage
#### Constraints
- arXiv has less advanced search features than ADS and accepts uploads from anyone, so pre-release papers may not yet be peer reviewed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring new research in a subfield
- browsing recent literature efficiently
