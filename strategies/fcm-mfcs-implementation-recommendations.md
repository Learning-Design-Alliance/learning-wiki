# Four-step implementation recommendations for the FCM-MFCS blended model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on their teaching experience with the model, the authors give educators four recommendations: "Begin with pre-class adaptability training to identify and support students who may struggle with the new approach"; pilot the flipped classroom with a small number of core skill units before expanding; assign a teaching assistant to manage the cameras using standardized angles (45-degree overhead, first-person, and close-up views); and establish a dynamic feedback mechanism through regular surveys or interviews after each module to enable ongoing adjustments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching assistant to operate the multi-functional camera system
- A learning platform hosting pre-class materials
- Regular surveys or interviews after each module
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from the authors' own teaching experience in one Chinese rehabilitation therapy program, not from a tested manipulation

### Target Learners
- health professional students in practical courses

### Target Learning Goals
- smooth adoption of flipped and camera-supported teaching
- reduced student burden and sustained engagement

## Claims
