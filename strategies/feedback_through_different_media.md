# Feedback Through Different Media

## Description
Delivering feedback through various media, such as video, allows for a more encouraging tone of voice and relaxed body language compared to impersonal email feedback. This approach ensures learners understand that feedback is constructive and well-meant, especially when in-person feedback is not possible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recording equipment and software. LMS or platform for sharing feedback. Clear guidelines on providing constructive feedback.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and resources. Potential for technical issues. Relies on the quality of the feedback and the ability of the instructor to communicate effectively through different media.

### Affordances
- Enhances clarity and reduces misinterpretation. Allows for a more personal and encouraging tone. Provides a richer and more engaging feedback experience.

### Personalization
- Customize feedback based on individual learner progress and needs. Offer options for video, audio, or written feedback. Encourage learners to provide self-reflection as part of the feedback process.
