Decompose aggregate models of group learning using fine-grained behavioural observations¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As a future direction, the editors recommend moving beyond treating group learning "as an aggregate function of the individual learning outcomes," which "limits us to a macroscopic view of learning in groups and prohibits the study of the underlying processes." Capturing fine-grained observations of individual and group behaviour in digital learning environments allows conventional models to be decomposed and studied at finer granularity, supporting group awareness widgets, tracking stages of group development, and prediction of group performance.
