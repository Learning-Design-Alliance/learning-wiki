# Use Kahoot! as a tool to study students' comprehension also during ordinary in-presence lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on quiz data that the authors read as "a clue as to the understanding of the treated topics in the short term", they recommend that "the usage of Kahoot! could be considered as a valuable tool to study students’ comprehensio n also during ordinary lessons in presence", to be deepened in future work with feedback from school teachers who adopt Kahoot! in their lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School teachers adopting Kahoot! in their lessons to provide feedback on its comprehension-checking use
#### Constraints
- The authors present this as a direction to deepen in the future, not a tested result

### Target Learners
- high school students in ordinary in-presence lessons

### Target Learning Goals
- studying students' comprehension of the treated topics
