# Route students with weaker grade 12 science grades to extended programmes or provide close monitoring and academic support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing education institutions use grade 12 science performance in admission and support decisions: "Students with less satisfactory grades in grade 12 science subjects may be considered for the extended nursing programme, or if admitted to the mainstream nursing programme, they should be monitored closely and be provided with the needed academic support". This follows from the finding that grade 12 mathematics and life science predict first-year science module performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to students' grade 12 mathematics and science grades at admission
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted with a limited sample from one university, which may limit generalisability to nursing schools at other universities

### Target Learners
- first-year Bachelor of Nursing students at risk of unsatisfactory academic performance

### Target Learning Goals
- success in first-year nursing science modules
