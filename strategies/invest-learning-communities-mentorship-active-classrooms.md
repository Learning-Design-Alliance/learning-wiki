# Invest in learning communities, mentorship programs, and active learning classrooms to boost belonging and engagement among engineering students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Drawing on the model's emphasis on peer support, faculty interaction, and hands-on engagement, the article recommends that engineering programs "invest in learning communities, mentorship programs, and active learning classrooms to boost belonging and engagement among students." The systems perspective explains why such investments pay off: community, competence, and motivation reinforce each other over time through feedback loops.
