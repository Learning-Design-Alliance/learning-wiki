# Use ESR eGUIDE in teaching radiology to train appropriate imaging test selection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors conclude that the clinical decision support tool is suitable for training medical students and can be recommended in teaching. They state: "The clinical decision support tool ESR eGUIDE is suitable for training medical students in choosing the best radiological imaging modality in typical scenarios, and its use in teaching radiology can thus be recommended." Implementation followed a voluntary radiology lecture on test selection and Bayes-based pre-test probability, followed by enrollment in the e-learning activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An introductory lecture covering general principles of the five diagnostic imaging tests and the Bay principle with pre-test probability estimation
- Access codes for the ESR eGUIDE platform
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-center study with a small number of participants; larger studies in more than one medical university are recommended

### Target Learners
- medical students in undergraduate radiology education

### Target Learning Goals
- appropriateness of imaging test selection
- understanding of clinical decision support and Bayesian test selection
