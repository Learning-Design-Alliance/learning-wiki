# Use advisories and looping to ensure every student is known and supported by adults

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends specific relationship-building structures for whole child cultures in schools serving students from deeply poor families. Advisories are "small groups that provide every student with a caring community and a school advocate", and looping is "the practice of keeping students with the same teacher or team for more than 1 year so that they know each other well and develop positive relationships". Schools can also train staff to recognize trauma and respond with caring, increase family outreach, and integrate stress-reduction practices such as mindfulness and physical movement breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structures that ensure every student is known and supported by adults
- Educator training to recognize and respond to trauma with caring instead of frustration or punishment
#### Constraints
- The report notes working in high-poverty schools is difficult and that teachers' mental health and wellness, positive working conditions, and reasonable pupil loads are needed to sustain these practices

### Target Learners
- Students living in deep poverty
- Students experiencing trauma

### Target Learning Goals
- Secure attachments and affirming relationships
- Physical and psychological safety
- Well-being and readiness to learn

### Affordances
- Deep Poverty Responsive Schools Framework
