# Use short (5 to 10 minute) educational videos with conversational narration and visual demonstrations to support learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Cognitive Load Theory, the article recommends educational videos as a content-delivery tool for flipped, blended and online classes. It states that "Research studies recommend short video lessons (about 5 to 10 minutes) that are proven most effective to support student learning [16, 17]." Videos should begin with a clear introduction, use conversational storytelling-style narration, and maintain eye contact with the camera; instructors should favor pictures, illustrations and animations over mathematical expressions for IPLS students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video must begin with clear introduction about what it will cover
- Narration should be in conversational style like storytelling
- Maintaining appropriate eye contact while speaking to the camera
#### Constraints
- Planning, recording, and editing a high-quality educational video is time consuming

### Target Learners
- undergraduate life sciences students in introductory physics, including flipped/blended/online formats

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of physics concepts such as electric fields
- student engagement
