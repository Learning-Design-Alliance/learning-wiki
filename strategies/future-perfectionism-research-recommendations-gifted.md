# Prioritise longitudinal designs, valid multidimensional measures, and common gifted-identification reporting in future perfectionism research with gifted students

## Description
The review's future-research section sets out implementable methodological recommendations. It advocates that "researchers adopt valid and reliable multidimensional scales that can be integrated into commonly adopted perfectionism frameworks", recommends longitudinal designs to disentangle antecedents and consequences, and suggests following Carman's (2013) reporting guidelines for gifted identification, plus informant reports from parents or teachers to supplement self-report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers must adopt multidimensional scales integrable into the higher-order perfectionism framework and follow common gifted-identification reporting guidelines
#### Constraints
- Existing evidence is dominated by cross-sectional designs, limiting causal inference about antecedents and consequences
