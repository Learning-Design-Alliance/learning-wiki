# Provide underrepresented students opportunities to engage in authentic disciplinary practices of science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors argue that promoting minority students' interest in science requires providing opportunities to engage in authentic disciplinary practices: "engaging in generating causal explanations for natural phenomenon, empirical reasoning, and design of experiments." They note many youth from low-income communities lack access to such resources and are often positioned as recipients rather than participants in expertise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom experiences that connect conceptually or epistemologically to students' identities
#### Constraints
- Minority students have limited access to schooling supportive of critical thinking and authentic science instruction

### Target Learners
- students from underrepresented populations in science
- middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- engaging in scientific argumentation and sense-making
- pursuing science careers
