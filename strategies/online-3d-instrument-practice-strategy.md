# Move instrument practice onto the Internet with 3D simulation and group discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend breaking through the traditional lab-operation practice mode by establishing the cooperative learning environment of instrument practice on the Internet, integrating virtual reality, embedded system and remote control parameter transmission technologies. They report that 3D virtual instrument simulation training and online grouped discussion "increase the interests of the learners in practice courses and train up their initiative and interaction", and decrease the damage rate of traditional instruments and school facility purchasing costs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- an embedded broker backend, 3D virtual instrument interfaces, and an embedded RS-232 module on real multimeters for authentic measurement data
#### Constraints
- the approach targets operation training of online-simulated instruments and circuit measurement, an area the authors say existing network cooperative learning systems rarely address

### Target Learners
- students in practical electronics courses working in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- instrument operation skills and circuit measurement through cooperative online practice
