# Design long videos' content carefully or shorten them, since completion of viewing declines with video length

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because both completion-rate measures negatively correlate with average video length, the article reasons that "A long video is hard to attract learner attentions from beginning to end", and concludes "a long video's content should be carefully designed if its length cannot be shortened". This gives MOOC designers an implementable rule for video production.
