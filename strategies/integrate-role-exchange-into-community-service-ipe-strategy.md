# Integrate profession-role exchange into cooperative learning-based community service learning as an extracurricular IPE design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends combining profession-role exchange with an existing IPE sequence: cooperative learning in interprofessional teams followed by team-based community service, with the exchange performed during the service step. The authors state that "The integration of profession-role exchange component in an interprofessional student team-based community health service-learning experience could be used as a comprehensive extracurricular IPE tool for improving the role awareness of health-care students". They add that the study "provided a referential model for the professional role assignment during the further simulated IPE programs".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interprofessional teams with members of multiple professions, prior cooperative learning to prepare knowledge and skills, and a real community service context with supervision by qualified practitioners
#### Constraints
- The article notes attitudes are only a proxy for behavior and there is still no quantitative method to measure interprofessional collaboration behavior

### Target Learners
- health-care students (medical, pharmacy, nursing)

### Target Learning Goals
- interprofessional role awareness and collaborative skill acquisition in a safe environment
