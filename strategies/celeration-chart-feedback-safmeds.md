# Use standard celeration charts with weekly written feedback to monitor and support learner progress during fluency-based practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In this study, participants' trial data were used with the Chartlytics platform to generate a standard celeration chart (SCC), "a semi-logarithmic chart that depicts the frequency of a target behavior and allows for the ascertainment of whether a growth in learning is occurring". Participants received updated charts weekly with written feedback, and those not progressing were directed to revise relevant content. The authors describe the SCC and feedback as a potentially 'hidden' component of the intervention whose separate contribution is not yet established.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular collection of trial data (corrects and incorrects per one-minute trial)
- A charting platform or tool to generate standard celeration charts
- Weekly feedback loop to participants
#### Constraints
- The article states it is "not apparent what degree of impact of the intervention is attributable to this component alone" and calls for research comparing SAFMEDS with and without this feedback

### Target Learners
- medical students undertaking self-directed fluency practice

### Target Learning Goals
- self-monitoring of learning progress toward fluency criteria
