# Adopt Blackboard-based blended teaching in universities with faculty training and equipped classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its Recommendations section the article calls for institutional adoption of blended teaching: "Utilizing blended teaching, based on e-learning management system “Blackboard”, in university teaching and creating supporting plans in universities". It further recommends providing faculty members with various training courses to activate blended teaching across courses, and providing classrooms equipped with the technologies essential for blended teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty training courses on activating blended teaching via Blackboard
- Classrooms equipped with the technologies essential for blended teaching
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in a single quasi-experiment at one university; the study itself found effects only on academic achievement, not study skills or self-confidence

### Target Learners
- University students

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement in university courses

## Claims

- [Blackboard-based blended teaching produced significantly higher academic achievement than traditional teaching, with η² = 0.75](../claims/blackboard-blended-teaching-achievement-eta-075.md) [+W]
