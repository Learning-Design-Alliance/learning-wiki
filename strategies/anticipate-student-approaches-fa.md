# Anticipate multiple ways students can approach mathematical tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When planning assessment tasks, teachers should anticipate different ways students may understand or approach tasks and consider possible errors on specific standards. Wallinga (2017) found teachers developed anticipation by planning multiple FA methods to collect diverse student thinking on a common topic, and An & Wu (2012) used analyzing student homework to help teachers understand diverse sense-making.
