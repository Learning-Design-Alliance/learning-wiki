# Calibrate GenAI feedback through calibrated prompts and pedagogical (re-)framing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete technique for integrating generative AI into writing instruction: the instructor designs calibrated prompts for the AI and applies pedagogical (re-)framing to its output. The article states that "Through calibrated prompts and pedagogical (re-)framing, GenAI can supplement human feedback effectively but will not replace it." This operationalizes the hybrid model in day-to-day feedback practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Calibrated prompts designed by the instructor
- Pedagogical (re-)framing of the AI-generated comments
#### Constraints
- Even with calibration, GenAI supplements rather than replaces human feedback

### Target Learners
- first-year university EAP students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving argumentative essays through formative feedback
