# Video-clip techniques: silent viewing, pause-based prediction, and rewind

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For video clips of recorded songs, the article prescribes three techniques: "use of video without sound" with tasks of relating video to song text or predicting content; "use of pauses, predicting subsequent text or display a description of the plot/character sequence"; and "use quick/rewind". Videotext is valued because it combines aspects of verbal interaction, lets the visual line strengthen factual and linguistic features in context, and supports comparison of cultural realities and behaviour in intercultural communication.
