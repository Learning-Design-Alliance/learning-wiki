# Use the validated SSE scale routinely to track satisfaction and support data-driven curriculum improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend integrating the validated Slovak SSE translation into routine educational assessment: "Using the scale regularly can help track the effects of any curriculum changes and support ongoing quality improvement." Data gathered through the scale can inform perceptions of simulation designs, debriefing methods and teacher approach, and universities can compare courses or student groups to decide where to focus resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated language version of the SSE for the student population being assessed
#### Constraints
- Cross-group comparisons of latent means should be cautious where scalar invariance is only partial (Items 9 and 12)

### Target Learners
- medical students in local-language and English study programs

### Target Learning Goals
- program evaluation and quality improvement of simulation-based education
