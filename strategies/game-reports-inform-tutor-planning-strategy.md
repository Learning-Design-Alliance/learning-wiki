# Use game-generated reports to inform tutors and assign targeted follow-up work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy interweaves gameplay with tutoring: when students play the game, "reports were generated for tutors on what concepts students might be struggling to understand", and tutors can assign follow-up work in the game and receive a report on that work. The authors credit this integration with letting tutors target the areas of fraction understanding most in need and with providing students extra practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shared game platform that generates per-student reports on concepts students struggle with
- Tutors able to assign follow-up work in the game and receive reports on it
#### Constraints
- In this design the contribution of the tutoring and gameplay components cannot be analytically separated

### Target Learners
- Elementary students receiving supplementary tutoring in mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- Targeted remediation of fraction misconceptions
