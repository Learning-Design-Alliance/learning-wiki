# Research-ethics certification and referencing software as required scaffolding for undergraduate original-research papers

## Description
To facilitate the final original-research paper, the course required two activities: passing the USA national ethics examination for doing research on people (the CITI Program) and learning referencing software to reduce plagiarizing, with Refworks available at the time and Zotero now more popular. The final paper, a group effort of two to four students, had to address an original question or hypothesis with at least three supporting arguments supporting their conclusions, plus an oral presentation.
