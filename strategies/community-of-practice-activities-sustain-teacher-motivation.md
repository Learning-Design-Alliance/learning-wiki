# Launch community-of-practice activities and interprofessional empowerment networks to sustain teacher motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends establishing diverse empowerment mechanisms, leveraging technologies, and creating interprofessional empowerment networks to sustain teacher motivation. Specifically, 'Launching 'community of practice' activities to empower, develop knowledge, and solve community problems may have a positive effect on creating motivation and developing a sense of self-worth', in which teachers develop personal management skills and specialized capabilities that facilitate their pathway of excellence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal and informal networks that connect teachers across disciplines and with the university-society relationship
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a qualitative study limited to Iranian medical sciences universities

### Target Learners
- Teachers and faculty in medical sciences universities

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining teacher motivation, professional growth, and sense of self-worth
