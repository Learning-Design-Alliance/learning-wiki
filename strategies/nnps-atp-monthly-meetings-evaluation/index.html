Monthly Action Team meetings with shared leadership and activity-quality evaluation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An implementable operating routine for school-family partnership teams: ATP members meet monthly, work together, and share leadership while conducting activities that engage all families in their children's education. The team evaluates the quality of each activity implemented to continually improve outreach to all families, and NNPS helps each team assess the quality and progress of its work on partnerships at the end of each school year. This cycle of monthly action plus evaluation is the day-to-day mechanism through which the NNPS model is sustained.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A standing Action Team for Partnerships composed of the principal, parents, teachers, others (e.g., counselors, community members, alumni) and students in high schools.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • School partnership team members: principals, parents, teachers, counselors, community members, alumni, and high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained, continually improving family and community engagement outreach to all families

Affordances¶

  • Nnps Six Types Involvement Framework

  • Families partner with schools on attendance and grades through belonging, collaborative support, and joint monitoring
