# Fist Tapping & Arm Tapping

## Description
Fist tapping and arm tapping are multisensory spelling strategies that involve tapping out the syllables and sounds of words. In fist tapping, learners tap each syllable of the word using the side of their fists, then tap each sound within the syllable as they spell it. In arm tapping, they use two fingers of one hand to tap on the forearm of the other hand, following the same procedure. These strategies enhance memory by engaging muscle memory and promoting systematic processing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space for physical movement, list of words to be spelled, and understanding of the connection between tapping and spelling
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for learners with physical limitations, requires coordination of hand movements and phonemic awareness, may be disruptive in some classroom settings
