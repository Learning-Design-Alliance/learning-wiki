# Interweave equity content throughout each intervention practice rather than siloing it as a separate module

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's design recommendation from its implications section is that equity-explicit components work best when interwoven with core practices rather than delivered separately. The authors state "the equity-explicit components were not siloed from other intervention components, but was interwoven throughout each EMR practice", giving teachers specific instructions for establishing, maintaining, and restoring relationships with equity front and center, and report believing these concrete recommendations were pivotal. The monthly PLC cycle of goal setting, action, and reflection was designed to build both equity-explicit behavioral habits and reflective dispositions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Concrete, specific instructions for applying an equity lens within each existing practice
- An equity lens embedded in professional learning communities, not treated as standalone training content
#### Constraints
- Evidence for this recommendation comes from a pilot trial with no significant main effects, so the authors' belief in its pivotal role is an interpretation rather than a tested finding

### Target Learners
- Ninth-grade high school teachers
- Racially minoritized high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Culturally responsive relationship-building
- Equitable student outcomes across racial/ethnic groups
