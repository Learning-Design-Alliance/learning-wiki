# Character Scrapbooking

## Description
Students select four key events from a novel and create scrapbook entries representing a character's inner thoughts, feelings, and experiences during each event. They use drawings, images, magazine cutouts, and quotes to share insights. Entries can be shared and discussed with peers, or transposed to social media platforms. This activity promotes relationship skills by encouraging students to unpack relationships within the text, examine how these relationships interact, and consider how people handle similar situations in different ways. This analysis celebrates creativity and visual literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completed reading of a novel. Materials for scrapbooking (e.g., paper, markers, images, quotes). Option to use social media platforms.
#### Constraints
- Requires creativity and visual literacy skills. Success depends on students' willingness to share and discuss their personal insights.
