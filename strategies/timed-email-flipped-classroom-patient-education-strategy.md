# Deliver tailored written education material by timed email before appointments and reserve appointment time for question-and-answer collaborative learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article adapts the flipped classroom to patient education: three written pieces (introduction to radiation oncology, treatment planning scan, treatment delivery) are emailed at three timed points before appointments, and appointments are used for "question‐and‐answer‐ and clarification‐based discussions" rather than didactic lecturing. A CRM system monitors the treatment schedule, adapts to schedule changes, and delivers the emails automatically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Written education material customized for disease site at an appropriate reading level, augmented with graphics and illustrations
- An automated delivery system that monitors the treatment schedule and adapts dynamically to schedule changes
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a small single-cohort pilot that was not a controlled trial; analyses did not control for additional external education accessed by patients

### Target Learners
- radiation oncology patients (breast and prostate cancer)

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding of radiation oncology treatment purpose, planning, and delivery
