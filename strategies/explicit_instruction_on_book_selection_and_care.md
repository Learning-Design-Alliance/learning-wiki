# Explicit Instruction on Book Selection and Care

## Description
Teach learners how to select appropriate independent reading books, care for them, understand expectations for their reading work, and follow library maintenance procedures. Model, teach, practice, and revisit all procedures related to independent reading, including selecting new books, managing browsing boxes, deciding to abandon a book, documenting reading, following bathroom procedures, accessing reading logs and printed materials, volunteering to share reading experiences, and preparing for and responding to sharing sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom library with labeled books, instructional time for mini-lessons, materials for modeling procedures, and tools for student practice.
#### Constraints
- May require significant instructional time initially. Requires ongoing reinforcement and revisiting of procedures. Some students may need individualized support.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Offer mini-lessons on various topics related to independent reading procedures and allow students to practice and revisit these procedures as needed.
