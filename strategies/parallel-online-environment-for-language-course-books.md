# Provide foreign language course books with a parallel online learning environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends that "the foreign language course books constitute in the mean time a parallel online learning environment for the course book". Building such a blended environment enhances face-to-face lectures and verifies distance learning in an active way, with flexible person and group based online aspects and without time and place concerns.
