# Avoid a harder, more optimised AI opponent because it is unlikely to improve educational outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article advises against adding a more sophisticated AI opponent for new learners. It notes a proposed "hard mode" using a minimax search algorithm, but concludes that "While such a system may increase engagement for some learners, it is unlikely to enhance the educational outcomes of the game and may even discourage new learners." The recommendation follows from the game's short, learning-focused play sessions.
