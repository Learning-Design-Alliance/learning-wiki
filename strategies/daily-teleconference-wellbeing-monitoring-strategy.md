# Daily teleconferences and interval wellness surveys to protect team psychosocial well-being during remote rotations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To minimize the psychosocial impact of social distancing, the remote team holds daily teleconferences, which the authors state "minimize the impact of social isolation while promoting social distancing and preventing disease spread" and provide a sense of community and team well-being. The department also plans to distribute surveys at regular intervals using short-form measures of burnout, social isolation, and well-being to monitor resident and faculty mental health.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A regular teleconference schedule for the remote team
- short-form burnout, social isolation, and well-being instruments for the planned surveys
#### Constraints
- Described as part of the pandemic response; the wellness surveys are planned rather than reported as completed

### Target Learners
- orthopedic surgery residents and faculty on remote rotation

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining team well-being and community during remote work
