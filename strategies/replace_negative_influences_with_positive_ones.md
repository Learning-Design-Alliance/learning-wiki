# Replace Negative Influences with Positive Ones

## Description
This strategy focuses on intentionally minimizing exposure to negative external factors and actively seeking out positive influences to support a more constructive mindset.  It involves identifying negative influences, such as toxic relationships or unsupportive environments, and limiting contact with them. Simultaneously, it emphasizes surrounding oneself with positive influences, such as supportive friends, uplifting content, and empowering communities, to foster a more optimistic and growth-oriented mindset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires self-awareness and a willingness to evaluate and manage social and environmental influences. May benefit from external support such as therapy or coaching to address underlying issues. Access to resources such as support groups, positive media outlets, or mentorship programs can be helpful.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in situations where negative influences are unavoidable (e.g., family relationships, work environments). Requires ongoing effort and vigilance to maintain positive influences and minimize negative ones. May not be effective for individuals with severe mental health conditions without professional support.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Minimize exposure to negative external factors, identify and limit contact with negative influences, surround oneself with positive and supportive people, foster a more optimistic and growth-oriented mindset.

### Affordances
- Promotes positive emotions by reducing exposure to negativity. Increases motivation by surrounding learners with supportive and empowering influences. Fosters a growth mindset by encouraging learners to seek out environments that support their goals. Enhances self-esteem by promoting a more positive self-perception.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by identifying and prioritizing individual sources of negativity or positivity. Flexible practice allows for integration into daily routines through social media curation, community involvement, or relationship management. Scaffolding can be provided for beginners, such as checklists of negative influences or templates for building positive relationships.

Studies show that exposure to positive media can enhance mood and promote prosocial behavior. Regularly engaging with supportive communities can foster a sense of belonging and purpose.
