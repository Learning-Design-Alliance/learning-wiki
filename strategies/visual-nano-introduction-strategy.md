# Use visual, computation-based introduction to atoms and molecules to introduce nanotechnology concepts to high-school students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends introducing nanotechnology through visual representation of its building blocks: "The visual introduction to atoms and molecules, which are the building blocks of this technology, is an effective way to introduce the key concepts in this area." Students computationally implement abstract mathematical and physical concepts and visualize them concurrently, which the authors say solidifies basic knowledge and opens critical thinking early in academic careers.
