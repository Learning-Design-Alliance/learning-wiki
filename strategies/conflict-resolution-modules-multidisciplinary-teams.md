# Build conflict-resolution and communication skills into multidisciplinary team projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the conflict and communication breakdowns observed, the authors recommend explicitly fostering interpersonal and conflict-resolution skills. They report that faculty in Communications specializing in Dialog expressed interest in "developing modules on conflict resolution and project management" for future renditions, and that guidelines for online communication etiquette were posted mid-semester in response to miscommunications.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor commitment to supervising and mentoring team dynamics

### Target Learning Goals

- interdisciplinary communication
