# Use the defined competency framework to restructure undergraduate teaching and build permanent education programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose concrete uses for the competency list. In undergraduate courses, the competencies "can contribute to the expansion of the discussions about the teaching process" and help "generate subsidies for the creation of instruments to evaluate its consolidation among nursing students". Among practicing professionals, "these competencies can be used to define permanent health education programs". The framework thus serves both initial training and continuing education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions adopt the competency list as a guiding axis when planning or evaluating teaching
#### Constraints
- Future studies are recommended to detail the competencies by knowledge, skills and attitudes dimensions to expand implementation pathways

### Target Learners
- nursing undergraduate students
- practicing nursing professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- prevention and control of healthcare-related infections
