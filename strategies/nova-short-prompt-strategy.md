# Keep chatbot prompts short, concise, and structured, and specify the simulated star's spectral type to improve response accuracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article advises that prompts to NOVA follow the structure presented in the worksheet and remain brief: "the prompts were always short and concise, as this is a basic requirement to get the most out of a chatbot." It further recommends always specifying the spectral type of the simulated star, since this technical detail increases the accuracy of the chatbot's responses even though students need not understand the term.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students interact with NOVA following the worksheet's prompt structure
#### Constraints
- Technical terms not strictly necessary for high school students, such as logarithmic plots, orbital eccentricity, and uncertainty calculations, are intentionally avoided

### Target Learners
- high school students and their teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective interaction with an AI assistant during data-generating inquiry
