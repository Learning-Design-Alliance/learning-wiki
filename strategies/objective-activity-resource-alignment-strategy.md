# Pair each performance objective with a demonstration-based suggested activity and coded resource references

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable planning recipe used throughout the guide: every topic outline row states a performance objective, then a suggested activity, then numbered resource references matching the unit bibliography. Activities typically follow the form "Explanation and demonstration" plus supervised student participation, e.g. students draw lines with various pencils and lead holders after the instructor demonstrates. This gives teachers a directly usable lesson-planning structure keyed to state-vetted materials.
