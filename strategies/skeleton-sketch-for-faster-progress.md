# Sketch the skeleton of a field for background-free learners to enable faster later progress

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An onboarding strategy: bring a flavor of a discipline and 'sketch its skeleton for those without any signiﬁcant background', so that learners progress faster by afterwards 'consciously collecting and organizing relevant information from other sources'. The article applies this to condensed matter physics for readers without significant background.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of representative topics that together sketch the field's skeleton.
#### Constraints
- The authors note the material 'will remain an illustration' rather than a complete curriculum.

### Target Learners
- learners without significant background in the discipline

### Target Learning Goals
- building initial intuition and a mental skeleton of condensed matter physics
- independent information gathering and organization
