# Structured individual research-project sequence with faculty mentorship and formative feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This is the concrete PrBL recipe the article implemented: students individually selected a topic from a curated shared pool aligned with curricular outcomes and healthcare challenges, then under faculty mentorship formulated a focused research question, conducted a structured literature review, designed a mini-study or hypothesis-driven investigation, and prepared a formal academic paper following institutional writing guidelines. As the article puts it, "Faculty mentors provided formative feedback throughout the 4-week development period," culminating in a graded written submission and oral presentation assessed with a rubric.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior foundational courses in Academic Writing, Critical Thinking, Introduction to Medical Research, and Biostatistics and Scientific Reporting
- Faculty mentorship with equitable distribution across students
- A shared pool of research topics aligned with curricular learning outcomes
#### Constraints
- Assignment to the project pathway was non-random, so selection of more motivated students is possible
- Topic heterogeneity may have introduced variation in task complexity and scope

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students (twelfth semester)

### Target Learning Goals
- Formulating research questions
- Structured literature review and study design
- Academic research writing and oral presentation
