# Consider evaluation issues up front when launching educational reform initiatives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy the guide embodies is that states launching new reform initiatives should anticipate evaluation issues at the launch stage rather than after implementation. The page frames the guide's purpose this way: it was developed "to help you consider evaluation issues likely to arise as you launch ARRA-funded initiatives and other educational reform activities." The page does not print the guide's specific evaluation methods, so the strategy is recorded at the level of orientation the landing page states.
