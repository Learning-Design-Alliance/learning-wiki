# Deploy short-duration podcasts as supplementary revision resources after didactic lectures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's operational recipe: record brief (≤3 min) high-yield video capsules that cover only highlights of a topic, release them on a learning portal immediately after the corresponding lecture, and state explicit learning objectives per episode so students can use them for "revision especially at the last-minute" before assessments. Students confirmed using them for topic review, overview, and quick revision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supplement, not replace, traditional teaching — the consensus the authors endorse
- Advance-scripted content fitting the time frame, with pilot feedback before full production
- Reconcile delivery medium with student devices (consider multiple streaming portals) to reduce download barriers
#### Constraints
- Benefits concentrated among heavier users and above-average performers in the exploratory analysis
- Short format limits the information conveyed and presumes some prior knowledge

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate medical students in lecture-based biochemistry courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Consolidation and last-minute revision of lecture content
- Assessment preparation
