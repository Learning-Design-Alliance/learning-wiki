# Liberalise internet trade markets through legal frameworks to de-monopolise internet service provision and reduce the costs of digital infrastructure for education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that governments of developing countries move beyond traditional donor and stakeholder quota funding for the digitalisation of higher education. Its recommendation is to "liberalise internet trade markets through legal frameworks to de-monopolise the provision of internet services", which it argues would reduce the costs of erecting digital infrastructures and provision of services. The strategy is addressed to governments rather than to individual institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
