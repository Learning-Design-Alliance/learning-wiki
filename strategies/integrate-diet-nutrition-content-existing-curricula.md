# Integrate diet- and nutrition-related oral health content into existing curricula via online instruction, coursework integration, or nutrition courses with clinic integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that diet- and nutrition-related oral health content can be integrated into existing oral health curricula via online instruction, integration into existing coursework, or through nutrition courses with clinic integration. Interprofessional instruction engaging both nutrition and oral health care faculty is encouraged to bridge disciplines and maximize student education and training. Early clinical experiences evaluating their own or peers' diets are appropriate prior to evaluating patient diets on the clinic floor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires engagement of nutrition faculty and oral health care faculty in interprofessional instruction
#### Constraints
- Addressing patient knowledge and culinary skillset limitations is beyond the scope of practice of the oral health care practitioner

### Target Learners
- Oral health care students

### Target Learning Goals
- Develop comfort screening and assessing diets and providing dietary counseling within scope of practice
