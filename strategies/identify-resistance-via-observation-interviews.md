# Identify resistant students through observations, teacher interviews, and follow-up forms before intervening

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's method combines qualitative and quantitative data collection: "data were collected from 95 students and 14 teachers using both qualitative (students’ follow-up forms, observation, and interview forms) and quantitative (achievement test and personal information survey) methods". This mixed-method identification strategy located 25 resistant students and enabled analysis of behavioral change. Content analysis with NVivo 9.0 identified major themes from observations and interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher participation in interviews and observations of students during teaching–learning processes
#### Constraints
- Purposeful sampling was used, based on socio-economical levels of families and student maturity

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
