Implementation recommendations from the process evaluation: behaviour management training, targeting pupils in Year 7 and below, and lengthening sessions to allow punctual starts¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The process evaluation's recommendations for improvements address three delivery problems observed in the trial. Deliverers had no behaviour management training, so external agencies would "need thorough training in behaviour management" or sessions should be led by school staff. Teaching assistant comments indicated the programme in its current format suits pupils in Year 7 and below, with primary-age pupils benefiting most, since Year 8 pupils found it 'babyish'. Sessions hindered by late arrivals could be made 15 rather than ten minutes long to secure ten minutes of concentrated delivery.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Deliverers need behaviour management capability and, per the teaching assistants, a musical background is beneficial

Constraints¶

  • The recommendations arise from a small qualitative process evaluation of one developer-delivered trial, not from a tested comparison

Target Learners¶

  • primary-age pupils and pupils in Year 7 and below

  • Process evaluation found that session timing, pupil resentment and poor behaviour undermined engagement in Rhythm for Reading sessions [+W]
