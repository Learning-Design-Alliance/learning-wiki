# Structure VDT practical sessions as paired 2-hour table sessions with general training followed by student-led clinical application

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course's virtual dissection teaching followed a fixed hands-on structure: "the practical session was divided in two lessons of 2 h each: 2 h were dedicated on Anatomage® table session, the last 2 h on SECTRA® table". Each lesson began with one hour of general training on the software and hardware, followed by an hour of clinical application directly conducted by the students, who explored anatomical structures using gross anatomy libraries and imported CBCT exams. Sessions were kept one per week for a total of six weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both an Anatomage and a SECTRA table
- An initial hour of general training on each table's software and hardware before the student-led clinical application hour
#### Constraints
- The authors state the number of hours spent during the table sessions is low (4 h)

### Target Learners
- postgraduate dental residents in oral surgery and orthodontics

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical oral anatomy
- hands-on exploration of anatomical structures and radiological exams
