# Leveraging Native Language in PBL

## Description
Leveraging the native language in project-based learning (PBL) involves using students' multilingual abilities as an asset. Educators can ask students to create products in both English and their native language to broaden the project's audience and impact. Instead of seeing language as a barrier, view it as an opportunity for students to interact with diverse audiences and enhance their learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recognition of multilingualism as an asset, opportunities for students to use their native languages, and inclusive assessment practices.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources or support to accommodate multiple languages, and needs careful planning to ensure equitable participation and evaluation.
