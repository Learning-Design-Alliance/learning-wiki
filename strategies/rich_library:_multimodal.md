# Rich Library: Multimodal

## Description
Providing learners with a variety of resources in multiple formats, such as blogs, lyrics, videos, web pages, online discussions, and fanfiction, to enhance disciplinary literacy and engagement. This approach gives learners choices that increase interest and motivation, builds background knowledge, and improves composition skills. It's especially important for learners lacking technology access outside of school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a variety of multimodal resources, either physically or digitally. Learning Management Systems like Canvas can aid in this.
#### Constraints
- May require careful selection of resources to ensure relevance and quality. Learners may need guidance in navigating and evaluating diverse sources.
