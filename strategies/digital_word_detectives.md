# Digital Word Detectives

## Description
Students read e-books and use digital tools (dictionaries and notes) to support their development of vocabulary. Students assume roles of “word detectives” as they look up words in digital dictionaries and use other strategies to identify the meaning of vocabulary words. The teacher identifies approximately 3-4 vocabulary words from each chapter of the e-book. Before reading each chapter, the teacher determines which words the students already know and which words are unfamiliar. As students read and encounter the vocabulary words, they use the digital dictionary and other strategies to figure out what each word means, inserting a digital note next to that word with an example and/or a definition in their own words. Students then meet in small groups to compare their digital notes and discuss the meaning of the vocabulary words, followed by a whole-class discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital readers for student use, e-books compatible with digital readers, visual presenter/document and LCD projector for demonstration, printed handouts.
#### Constraints
- Access to digital readers may be limited. Students may need instruction on how to use digital dictionaries and insert digital notes into an e-book.

### Affordances
- Electronic books extend connections between readers and text as engagement with and manipulation of text is made possible through electronic tools and features. Digital note-taking features engage students in the reading experience.

### Personalization
- Select multiple titles to allow for differentiated instruction, if appropriate. Number of selected vocabulary words may be adjusted depending on chapter length and student grade level.

Digital note-taking features engage students in the reading experience.
