# Hybrid curriculum model combining standardized core competencies with customizable case-based and modular delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a hybrid approach that pairs standardized informatics competency-based curricula with customization through case-based learning and modular content delivery. Core competencies are standardized across programs while elective modules or microcredentials address local legislative policies, electronic health records design, or virtual care workflows. The authors state that "a hybrid approach, combining core standardization with customization through CBL and modular content delivery, potentially offers a sustainable model for developing digital health curricula."
