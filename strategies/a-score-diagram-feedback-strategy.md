# Score full conceptual-index diagrams A and other students B so students benefit from each other's help in solving problems.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the fifth step of the Education Management Model, students are placed in examination conditions and asked to draw the diagram of links between main subjects and mention the conceptual and basic indexes of each topic and subtopic. The full work receives an A score and other students take B scores "to benefit from each other's help in solving their problems." Students who object to their score can take feedback through A score students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher asks students to be placed in examination conditions
- Students draw the diagram of links between main subjects and mention conceptual and basic indexes
- Each student who objects his score can inform the instructor through A score students
#### Constraints
- Only the full work receives an A score; all other students receive B scores

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students
- Second-grade boy students in public elementary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Domination over the insight or general image of the course subject
- Peer support in problem solving
