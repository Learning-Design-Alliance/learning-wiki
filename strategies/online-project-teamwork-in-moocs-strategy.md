# Incorporate online project teamwork into instructor-paced MOOCs, with an educational bot absorbing organisational load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends extending the collaborative blended design to MOOCs: "online project teamwork could become a good solution to the problems of instructor-paced MOOCs and require further research". Because a shift to collaborative programming assignments could create teacher management overload, the article proposes an educational bot that could answer common organisational questions, provide online tutorials on tools, and automate some project management tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teamwork tools (Trello, GitHub, Discord) to manage collaboration and capture activity data
- Further research, since teamwork inside a MOOC can raise new questions
#### Constraints
- Students who chose MOOCs to study independently might experience frustration or dropout when required to rely on peers; the study did not measure the teacher management needed to facilitate Trello and GitHub conversations

### Target Learners
- instructor-paced MOOC learners, especially blended MOOC students

### Target Learning Goals
- peer communication
- engagement and learning outcomes in online programming courses
