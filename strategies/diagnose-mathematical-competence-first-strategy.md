# Diagnose students' mathematical competence with dedicated instruments before developing more complex medication calculation competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that mathematical competence predicted 60.9% of general medication calculation ability, the authors recommend that "University professors must develop and implement instruments that allow them to diagnose their students' mathematical competence, before developing more complex ones". Such diagnostic tools would also allow assessment of the effectiveness of educational interventions and comparisons between populations or over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development and implementation of diagnostic instruments for mathematical competence
#### Constraints
- The authors note a solution is not foreseen from pre-university educational levels, given sustained low Spanish PISA mathematics results

### Target Learners
- Second-year undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematical competence underpinning safe paediatric medication calculation
