# Support interventionists with focused professional development workshops plus ongoing in-classroom coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a professional development model in which instructional assistants attended three 4-hour workshops organized around "(a) active participation, (b) content focused, and (c) coherence," split across the first and second halves of the curriculum, supplemented by at least three in-classroom coaching visits from two expert former educators. Coaching visits included direct observation and postobservation feedback on instructional delivery and implementation fidelity, with extra visits when warranted. The article cites implementation research that ongoing coaching enhances sustained use of new instructional practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches knowledgeable in early mathematics development and instruction; workshops timed to curriculum halves
#### Constraints
- Some IAs received more than three coaching visits when student behavior problems or implementation struggles warranted more support

### Target Learners
- instructional assistants delivering Tier 2 kindergarten mathematics intervention

### Target Learning Goals
- faithful implementation of the ROOTS curriculum and validated instructional practices such as teacher-provided academic feedback
