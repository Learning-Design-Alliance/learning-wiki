# Venn Diagrams

## Description
Venn diagrams are visual organizers that emphasize comparisons and contrasts between two or more items or concepts. They consist of overlapping circles, with each circle representing a different item. The overlapping sections represent the characteristics that are common to all items, while the non-overlapping sections represent the unique characteristics of each item.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paper, markers, or digital tools for creating Venn diagrams, learning material to be compared and contrasted, and understanding of the key characteristics of the items being analyzed
#### Constraints
- May require time and effort to create effective Venn diagrams, effectiveness depends on the accuracy and completeness of the information included, may not be suitable for learners who prefer linear note-taking
