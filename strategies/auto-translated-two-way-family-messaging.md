# Use universally designed auto-translated two-way messaging so families need only basic text messaging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy is to communicate with families through two-way messaging automatically translated into families' preferred languages, so that engagement does not depend on smartphones, shared languages, or extra staff time. The article states families "need only a simple cellular phone with basic text messaging to get started" and can also use online sign-in or a free mobile app.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers, administrators, and staff sign in online or use a mobile app; educators receive embedded guidance and in-app coaching
