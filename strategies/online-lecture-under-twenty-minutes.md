# Keep online lecture videos under twenty minutes and segment by topic with speed control

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article synthesizes FL literature recommendations that online lectures should be less than twenty minutes to retain learner attention, and even less than ten minutes for static screencasts without the instructor's face. It also recommends creating one clip per topic and providing speed control functionality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires video production capability including editing and audio quality control
#### Constraints
- The twenty-minute recommendation applies to typical attention spans; static screencasts without the instructor's face should be under ten minutes

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- attention retention during online lectures
