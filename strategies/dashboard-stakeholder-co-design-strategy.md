# Involve stakeholders in dashboard co-design and select visualizations that avoid ineffective practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that dashboard designers "carefully select visual representations and interactions to avoid promoting ineffective instructional practices", and states that "the success of dashboards depends on the extent to which stakeholders are involved in their co-design". It further advises displaying relevant information within a single screen in an easily interpretable format that supports quick decision-making, grounded in cognitive science principles such as situational awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stakeholders must participate in the design process
- Visual representations must fit users' goals and cognitive processes
#### Constraints
- A survey cited by the review found many dashboards have poor interface design and inadequate usability testing

### Target Learners
- course authors and instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting learning analytics to improve content
