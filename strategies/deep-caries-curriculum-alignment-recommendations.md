# Align dental curricula with national evidence-based guidelines and strengthen deep-caries teaching through faculty consistency, added training time, online courses, and conference participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose five curriculum-level actions to improve knowledge, confidence and standardisation among students treating deep caries and exposed pulps. They state that "The curricula of any dental school should be in accordance with the evidence-based guidelines that have been developed within the respective country", and additionally recommend faculty-consistency exercises, more didactic and clinical training time, new online courses and educational videos, and conference attendance integrated into the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dental school curriculum that can be updated to track evidence-based guidelines developed within its respective country
- Faculty coordination exercises to establish consistency within and across disciplines
- Curriculum time for didactic lectures, preclinical and clinical training in deep caries and exposed pulp
#### Constraints
- Confidentiality prevented disclosure of the participating institutions' curriculum details, so the survey could not compare schools' knowledge levels
- The recommendations arise from a survey with selection bias in school recruitment and variable response rates across schools

### Target Learners
- Final-year dental undergraduate students
- Postgraduate endodontic students

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based decision-making in managing deep carious lesions and exposed pulps
- Consistent treatment protocols for vital pulp therapy and caries removal
