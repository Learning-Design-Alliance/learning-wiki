# Tailor contextualized physics problems to students' learning stage instead of blanket implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that CPP benefits fade as students advance, the article recommends that "contextualization should be tailored to students' learning stages" and suggests "a mixed approach incorporating both" contextualized and decontextualized problems rather than blanket implementation. It also underscores addressing the teacher-student perception gap through targeted teacher training and professional development so educators understand how students actually experience contextualized problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need awareness of students' grade level and experience with physics before selecting problem formats
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness of CPP appears limited to students newly introduced to the subject (8th grade); older students under exam pressure prefer decontextualized formats

### Target Learners
- secondary school physics students in grades 8–11

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining interest and motivation in learning physics

## Claims

- [Student attitudes toward contextualized physics problems are slightly positive only in 8th grade and turn neutral-to-negative from 9th grade onward, plateauing through 11th grade](../claims/cpp-attitude-declines-with-physics-experience.md) [+W]
- [Among 8th graders, boys hold a significantly more positive attitude toward contextualized physics problems than girls, but this gender difference disappears in grades 9–11](../claims/cpp-gender-gap-younger-boys.md) [~W]

- [Teachers rate contextualized physics problems as far more motivating and interesting for students than students themselves do, with moderate-to-large perception gaps on motivation/interest items](../claims/teacher-student-perception-gap-cpp-motivation.md) [~W]
