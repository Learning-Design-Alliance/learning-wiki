# Provide different levels of controllability to meet diverse stakeholder needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's practice recommendation is that while stakeholder control and personalization of indicator implementation is beneficial, systems should offer graduated control rather than a single mode. As the authors state, "it is essential to provide different levels of controllability to meet the demands of stakeholders with various needs, goals, and experiences." This follows from involving students and teachers in the indicator implementation process through a human-centred LA approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stakeholders such as students and teachers must be involved in the indicator implementation process
#### Constraints
- The tool targets stakeholders with prior data analysis and visualization knowledge

### Target Learners
- students
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- personalizing LA indicators to individual needs and goals
