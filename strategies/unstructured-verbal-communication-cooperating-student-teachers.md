# Encourage unstructured verbal communication between cooperating and student teachers during student teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy directs teacher development programs to foster informal, unstructured verbal feedback from cooperating teachers to student teachers throughout the placement, rather than imposing structured communication instruments or peer pairings. The authors state that "unstructured verbal communication betw een cooperating and student teacher should be encouraged throughout the student teaching experience," because verbal feedback was positively correlated with efficacy while a structured communication tool was detrimental. Programs should also consider the number of courses a student teacher can successfully facilitate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A cooperating teacher who provides regular verbal feedback to the student teacher
#### Constraints
- Structured communication tools and pairing two student teachers in one placement showed no benefit and, in the structured case, harm

### Target Learners
- student teachers in agricultural education
- cooperating teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- developing teaching self-efficacy during student teaching
