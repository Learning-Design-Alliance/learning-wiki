# Provide up-to-date virtual environments that allow individual engagement alongside collaborative tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors conclude that "programme developers and institutions need to develop up-dated virtual environments which, even when equipped with collaborative and sophisticated online tools, allow participants to engage with learning individually." Interaction and collaborative views should be negotiated rather than imposed. This responds to findings that primitive LMS usage and poor tutoring skills may push trainees toward an individualist image of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual environments must support individual engagement even when equipped with collaborative tools
#### Constraints
- E-learning remains linked to internet access and may be restricted and elitist in low-connectivity contexts

### Target Learners
- practicing teachers in isolated regions

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining motivation in online teacher education
