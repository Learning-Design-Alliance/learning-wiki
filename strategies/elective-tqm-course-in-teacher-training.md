# Add an elective course on applying TQM principles in the classroom to teacher-training programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teacher-training institutions add an elective course on implementing TQM principles in the classroom, so that teacher candidates can help their students learn in a more qualified way. It states: "In the programs of institutions of teacher training should be added an elective course (lesson) comprising the implementation the principles of TQM in the classroom." It also recommends continuous in-service training and master's program work to improve awareness of TQM.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In-service training and master degree program work on TQM should be done to improve awareness and should be continuous, as the article states.
#### Constraints
- The supporting study had a small sample size, ran over three weeks, and evaluated only one course group of teachers, per the article's limitations section.

### Target Learners
- teacher candidates in teacher-training programs
- in-service teachers and school principals

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of total quality management principles and their application in classroom teaching
