# Seed discussions with simpler related questions when small groups are missing a critical idea

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
A facilitation strategy used within Modeling Discourse Management: when student groups lack a needed idea, the teacher poses simpler, related questions that act as stepping stones. The article writes that "teachers should attempt to \"seed\" discussions by asking simpler, related questions that could serve as stepping stones to the main result." Students worked in groups of four with small whiteboards, supported by Think-Pair-Share.
