Move beyond generic AI policies to method-based ethical policies and process-oriented assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that ethical policy for AI in online education be tailored to specific teaching methods rather than generic, method-agnostic guidance, because each method places distinct pressures on authenticity, authority, and collaboration. It also "emphasizes a process-oriented assessment framework to ensure transparency in education and mitigate the risk of unethical issues". The three-domain framework is offered as a roadmap for policymakers and educators.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Understanding of which pedagogical method is in use, since tensions are method-specific

Constraints¶

  • Findings are specific to language-acquisition classrooms in online higher education; the five methods are frequently combined in practice, so tensions may recur across method boundaries

Target Learners¶

  • university students in online language courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic integrity, authentic language learning, fair assessment in AI-mediated online instruction

Affordances¶

  • Three Domain Ai Ethical Tensions Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Use a mixed-methods assessment sequence: diagnostic inventory, structured verification tasks, and discipline-specific critique or workflow-design assignments
