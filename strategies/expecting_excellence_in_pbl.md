# Expecting Excellence in PBL

## Description
Expecting excellence in PBL involves setting high expectations for all students and actively dispelling the myth that PBL does not work for "those kids." By ensuring proper scaffolding and understanding students' current realities, educators can guide students toward the excellence they are capable of without lowering expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A belief in excellence, high expectations for all students, and effective scaffolding strategies to support student success.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset for some educators and may necessitate additional training or support to implement effective scaffolding strategies.
