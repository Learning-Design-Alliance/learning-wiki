# Integrate career adaptability cultivation and AI literacy with career planning in higher education curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that institutions "prioritise enhancing students' career adaptability rather than merely alleviating anxiety symptoms", integrating future planning, autonomous decision-making, curious exploration, and confidence building into curricula alongside internships and project-based learning. It further recommends that AI literacy education be "deeply integrated with career planning", using case studies and industry lectures to shift career positioning from competing with AI to collaborating with AI. These strategies follow from the finding that adaptability mediates AI anxiety's harm to career decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional investment in career development services and industry-education collaboration, including internships, project-based learning, and volunteer activities
#### Constraints
- The article notes current university education and vocational training resources may be limited, constraining self-efficacy development in the face of AI technology

### Target Learners
- college students
- undergraduate students in AI-exposed labor markets

### Target Learning Goals
- career adaptability
- AI literacy
- career planning and preparedness
