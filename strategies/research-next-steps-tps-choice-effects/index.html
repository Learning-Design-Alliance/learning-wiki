Description¶

The brief proposes a research agenda alongside its evidence summary. It reports that "The brief also addresses the implications of this evidence for policymakers and proposes next steps for research," positioning continued study of choice effects on traditional public schools as a field-level priority. This strategy directs researchers and funders toward evidence generation on the spillover side of school choice.
