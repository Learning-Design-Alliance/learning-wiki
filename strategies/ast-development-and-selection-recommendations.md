## Description
For assistive technologies as a component of adaptive cloud-based learning systems, the article recommends: "Development/selection of ASTs according to the needs of a specific user group", compatibility with tasks and emotional needs of users, support for policies providing vulnerable groups with access, ensuring ease of use with accessible instruction manuals, and "Involvement of users in the design of the ASTs - at different stages of development and testing".
