## Description
The article's concluding recommendation for management training programmes: "simultaneous and continuous supervision and support is highly beneficial for improving the effectiveness of any training course". This aligns with the discussion's point that improving the effectiveness of the management training programme and monitoring the specific system is the most useful way of providing continuous education services for health managers. The recommendation extends the fellowship beyond one-off courses into ongoing support in trainees' work settings.
