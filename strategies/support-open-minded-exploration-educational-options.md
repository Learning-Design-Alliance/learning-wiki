# Support all students' open-minded exploration of educational and vocational options from early on

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that motivational stability and changes ahead of the upper secondary transition are tightly connected with gender and prior achievement, the authors' Educational Impact and Implications Statement recommends that "supporting all students ' open-minded exploration of educational and vocational options from early on sets an important yet demanding task for educators." The strategy addresses the risk that early motivational and achievement differences channel students into tracks before they have explored options. It is aimed at educators working with adolescents approaching educational transitions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators begin supporting exploration of educational and vocational options early, before the final-year transition
#### Constraints
- The article notes the task is demanding given that motivational dynamics are tightly connected with gender and prior achievement

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade students approaching the general-versus-vocational upper secondary choice

### Target Learning Goals
- informed and open-minded educational and vocational decision-making
