# Reward top national-exam students with summer internships at research labs or tech companies, allocated per district to foster equal opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Among four proposed measures to modernize Portuguese secondary science education, the article recommends bridging the school-university disconnect by rewarding students with top results in national exams with summer internships at research labs or tech companies, with student matching done by the hosts. To foster equal opportunities, the article states internships "should not be merely awarded to the students with the numerically highest grades" but, for example, to the highest-grade student in each district, reaching about 20 students, or each municipality, reaching about 300.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- National exams whose results can be used for allocation
- Host research labs or tech companies willing to do the student matching
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a suggestion to contribute to debate on the Portuguese system; implementation bottlenecks named are political will, the teaching-to-the-test approach and teacher-union reluctance

### Target Learners
- secondary students with top national-exam results

### Target Learning Goals
- bridging the disconnect between secondary school and university/research environments
