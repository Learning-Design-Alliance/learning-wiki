# Use a Delphi panel with predefined inclusion and stopping criteria to build cross-institution consensus on procedure steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Assemble a panel of experienced physicians selected by reproducible criteria (e.g., at least five years of experience, high procedure volume, guideline or instructor roles), survey them anonymously online with a Likert scale on each proposed activity, allow new activity proposals, and iterate until responses stabilise. The article predefined inclusion as activities with "a percentage greater than or equal to 75%" of Include responses and a stopping criterion of average absolute percentage change below 15% between rounds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reproducible expert selection and a defined stopping criterion, maximum rounds, and per-item exclusion criterion, following Diamond et al.'s methodological quality criteria
- An online survey platform presenting the model and structured Likert questions
#### Constraints
- Restricting the panel to national experts may limit wider applicability

### Target Learners
- medical residents across specialties and institution types

### Target Learning Goals
- bias-free, cross-institutional breakdown of procedures for training
