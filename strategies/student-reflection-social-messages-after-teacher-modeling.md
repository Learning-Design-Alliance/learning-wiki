# Ask students to reflect on how they have been exposed to and impacted by society's messages about race, gender, and sexual identity, after the teacher has modeled vulnerability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a sequencing move: the instructor first demonstrates honest self-assessment, then invites students into reflective work on social messages. The authors note that asking students "to be vulnerable in talking about how they have been exposed to, and impacted by, society's messages about race, gender, and sexual identity" carries "a responsibility to first demonstrate that vulnerability ourselves." The strategy orders teacher disclosure before student reflection.
