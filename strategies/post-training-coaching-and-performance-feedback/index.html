Provide coaching and performance feedback to teachers after initial training to sustain knowledge and skills¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Because follow-up knowledge showed a small decrease that the authors attributed to not guiding teachers or giving feedback on their instruction, they recommend that future research examine whether providing performance feedback while teachers teach increases their knowledge and skills. They note literature stating that "coaching and providing feedback to the teachers improves their teaching skills and instructional practices as well as implementation integrity of their treatment".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Trained observers or coaches available to observe teachers during instruction and deliver performance feedback

Constraints¶

  • Recommended for future research; not tested in this study

Target Learners¶

  • Inclusive classroom teachers who have completed initial social skills training

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Maintenance of social skills teaching knowledge and classroom technique use over time

Claims¶

  • Knowledge gains from the SSTP were maintained at follow-up, with a small decrease attributed to absence of coaching and feedback [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Two-tier professional development: intensive teacher training with weekly coach follow-up, plus a five-day summer institute and weekly seminars for coaches
  • Pair new instructional practices with sustained in-class coaching after initial professional development
  • Pair Tier 2 intervention delivery with professional development workshops and in-class coaching visits
  • Design training opportunities to combine modeling with performance feedback, ideally as an initial workshop followed by one-to-one coaching
  • Train educators through a Summer Institute plus PLC and coaching cycles on data use and instructional routines
