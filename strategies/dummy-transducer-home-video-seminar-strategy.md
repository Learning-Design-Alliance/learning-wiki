# Home simulation of sonography with a dummy transducer under tutor guidance via video seminar

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe for teaching sonography remotely: students receive a dummy transducer (Schallkopfattrappe) and ultrasound gel and "sonographierten" their own neck section at home while the tutor displays the corresponding ultrasound image in B-mode via a videoconferencing system (Zoom). Peyton steps 1–3 (demonstration, deconstruction, comprehension) are completed at home in self-study; the actual performing step and the learning success check take place in person on a real device.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Previously issued dummy transducers and ultrasound gel for each student
- A commercial videoconferencing system to show the corresponding B-mode image in real time
- Tutor guidance during the home practice sessions
- Access to a real sonography machine for the final check
#### Constraints
- The moving, pulsating image of structures such as the A. carotis communis is better conveyed by the moving real-time image than by a dry exercise under verbal guidance in the virtual space
- The lack of real-time visual-tactile feedback is a limitation of a digital ultrasound course

### Target Learners
- medical students (sonographic novices)

### Target Learning Goals
- performing the six sonographic sweeps of the head and neck and sonographic fundamentals
