# Use generative AI to connect school subjects to students' immediate concerns and cross-domain applications rather than to core identity components

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the individualized identity-based AI dialogue failed while the collective one worked, the article recommends that "utilizing generative AI to establish relevance of the subject with students' immediate concerns or general relevance with different fields turned out to be more effective in promoting adolescents' relevance as identification with the subject". Concretely, AI dialogue should surface connections to current concerns such as time management and to math's applications across daily life and different fields, instead of directly linking content to ideal careers or life purpose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Semi-structured AI dialogue under guided prompts, as implemented in the study's intervention conditions
#### Constraints
- The article notes the finding comes from adolescents in a medical vocational high school, whose identities are still developing; replication in general high school settings is suggested

### Target Learners
- adolescent high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- relevance as identification with school subject content
- interest in the math class
