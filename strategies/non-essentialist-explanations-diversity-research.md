# Researchers on instructor diversity should define race carefully and posit non-essentialist explanations for identity-outcome associations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that studies of instructor racial/ethnic diversity avoid using racial/ethnic identity as an untheorized proxy for educator knowledge, practices, biases, or expectations. It argues that "research on instructor racial/ethnic diversity should carefully define and conceptualize the meaning and significance of race and should (at the very least) posit non-essentialist explanations for why particular attributes or outcomes may be associated with instructors' racial/ethnic identities," including theorizing higher-level mechanisms such as labor-market discrimination that could produce such associations.
