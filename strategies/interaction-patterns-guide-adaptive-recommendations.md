# Use interaction-pattern knowledge to guide adaptive recommendations in LMS environments.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that knowing how different interaction patterns relate to achievement can inform the design of Adaptive Educational Systems and recommendation systems. It notes that two clusters with similar achievement showed different underlying LMS interaction patterns, which "could be modeled by a recommendation system under very different terms". The article states that different types of adaptation at a high granularity level could be useful to ensure academic success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Log data sufficient to characterize students' effort, working time, and procrastination behavior.
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is necessary to have the type of knowledge obtained in this study before adaptation can be designed; the patterns come from one course context.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate university students in blended or online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning and study strategies
