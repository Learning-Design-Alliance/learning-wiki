Coach-the-coaches model for sustaining teacher professional learning implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The SWELL approach provides "coaching and technical assistance to coaches who will support teacher implementation" of Write to Succeed, rather than coaching teachers directly. A long-term outcome is that "NMPED and district coaches are positioned to sustain program implementation," building state and district capacity so the program continues beyond the partnership.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coaches who are effective instructional coaches

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • NMPED and district instructional coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supporting teacher implementation of professional learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Two-tier professional development: intensive teacher training with weekly coach follow-up, plus a five-day summer institute and weekly seminars for coaches
  • Designate a Spalding coordinator and provide 45-70 hours of formal teacher training with ongoing mentoring to support implementation fidelity
  • Implement Curiosity Corner with two initial training workshop days plus ongoing onsite coaching and support
  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Implement EWIMS through combined in-person and online training with ongoing technical assistance liaisons
  • Phased iterative testing: optimization testing before efficacy testing before statewide scale-up
