# Provide instructional support for motivational regulation and training in MRS use in online medical learning

## Description
The article recommends that educators and instructional designers support students’ motivational regulation and teach effective use of MRSs in online learning. It concludes that "instructional design and practice to support learning motivation is needed to promote cognitive learning and training programs to practice the effective methods of using MRSs in learning need to be provided." It further suggests integrating motivation theories such as ARCS-V, self-determination theory, expectancy-value theory, and control-value theory into instructional design.
