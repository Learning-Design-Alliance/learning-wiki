# Reaction Observations

## Description
Reaction observations involve observing and describing the properties of liquids and their mixtures. Learners examine the bleeding of colored inks in water and the properties of two liquids and their mixture. These activities improve observational skills and understanding of basic chemical reactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Colored inks, water, various liquids, containers
#### Constraints
- Requires careful observation. May need safety precautions when working with chemicals.
