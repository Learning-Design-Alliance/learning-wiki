# Adopt a measurable competency framework with OSATS-based assessment, supervised cases, re-certification and audit before implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For future implementation, the authors propose a governance-oriented credentialing pathway for emergency cranial trauma surgery by non-neurosurgeons. They "propose a measurable competency framework for future implementation studies, including objective skills assessment (for example OSATS-based), supervised initial clinical cases, periodic re-certification, and ongoing audit of indications and outcomes." This aligns the educational intent of NEUROSAVE with governance and patient-safety requirements and structures evaluation in a prospective multicentre setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Defined indications, minimum case volumes, objective assessment standards, and maintenance-of-competence requirements
#### Constraints
- The pilot assessed only self-reported confidence, not objective technical performance or patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- general and pediatric surgeons in regional emergency contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- credentialed, audited competence in temporising cranial trauma procedures
