# Improve announcements and cross-link related e-learning to increase voluntary uptake of an e-learning supplement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends two concrete measures to raise voluntary use of an e-learning offer: better promotion and integration with other faculty e-learning programs. The authors write that "acceptance of the program might be further increased by improving announcements about it" and that it "could be made even more appealing with links to e-learning programs of other subjects (e.g. anatomy)". Non-users themselves cited lack of time, not having been informed, and preference for books as reasons for non-use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing LMS or e-learning infrastructure whose familiarity with students predicts uptake
#### Constraints
- Addresses voluntary uptake; non-users' reasons (time, information, preference for books) may persist regardless

### Target Learners
- Medical students in clinically oriented courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Voluntary engagement with supplementary e-learning content
