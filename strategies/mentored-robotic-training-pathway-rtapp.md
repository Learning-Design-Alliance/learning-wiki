# Mentored robotic training pathway combining simulator, dry-lab and wet-lab sessions, hands-on courses, and first-assistant experience before independent rTAPP

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes how a surgeon new to the robotic platform prepared for robotic TAPP: simulator practice, dry-lab and wet-lab sessions, hands-on courses, and case observation, with roughly 20 supervised robotic procedures before the first study case. Throughout, "The experienced robotic surgeon mentored the trainee surgeon during the learning period", and the trainee functioned as first assistant surgeon for all study procedures while performing other robotic operations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attendance at standard robotic training programs plus specialized rTAPP training including dedicated courses and case observation
- Constant supervision, with "Approximately, segments of 20 robotic procedures were carried out, under constant supervision, before the first rTAPP in this study"
- Mentoring of the trainee by an experienced robotic surgeon during the learning period
