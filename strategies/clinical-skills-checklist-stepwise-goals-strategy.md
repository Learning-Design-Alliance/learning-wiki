# Use a clinical skills checklist to document achievements and set clear, achievable stepwise goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project documented students' clinical skill acquisition on a skills checklist, which students valued for recording their achievements. "The students valued the clinical skills checklist for documenting their achievements, which helped to identify clear, achievable goals in a stepwise fashion." Structured tasks reduced perceptions of uncertainty, corresponding to higher performance and satisfaction, and the article states the checklist is a tool that can further augment learning in a simulated setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration with the simulation-based course so documented skills align with instruction
#### Constraints
- Evaluated within a single university's Fundamentals of Nursing course

### Target Learners
- baccalaureate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skill acquisition and goal-setting
