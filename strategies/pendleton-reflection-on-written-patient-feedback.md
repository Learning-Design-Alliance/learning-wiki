# Deliver written patient feedback as an intervention pack with Pendleton-framework guided self-reflection before the final consultation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The trial's educational intervention is a written feedback pack given to intervention-group students no later than one week before their final consultation of the semester. "The intervention pack includes patient feedback questionnaires for all previous student-led consultations as well as written instructions about self-reflection on the feedback received based on the Pendleton feedback framework." Students are requested to complete the reflection exercise using the Pendleton model, and email adherence reminders emphasize reading the written feedback and contacting the coordinator about problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery of the pack no later than one week prior to the last consultation of the semester
- Written instructions structuring self-reflection on the feedback using the Pendleton feedback framework
- Email adherence reminders sent after participants obtain the written feedback
#### Constraints
- Control-group students obtain MISS-21 patient feedback only within one month after their final consultation session, after the intervention period
- Debriefing and referral for counselling are available to any student upon request, and emergency unblinding is considered only on student request and approval

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students in their first clinical year

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-reflection on patient satisfaction feedback
- Improvement of clinical consultation skills across a semester of consultations
