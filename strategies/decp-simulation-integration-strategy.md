# Integrate web-based decision support with simulation practice for medication safety training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "decision support systems such as DECP and simulation practices be transformed into a standard practice in nursing students' education on medication and dose calculations." The strategy combines a risk-free simulation scenario on a pediatric mannequin with a web-based double-check tool, letting students self-regulate their knowledge and calculations before comparing them with program output.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need an information meeting on using the web page and program before practice
#### Constraints
- The article notes double-eye control cannot be performed in every pediatric clinic due to working conditions and a lack of medical staff, motivating the web-based alternative

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- senior pediatric nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- pediatric medication administration
- dose calculation
- patient safety
