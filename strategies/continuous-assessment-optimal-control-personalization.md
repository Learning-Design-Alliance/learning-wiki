# Personalize treatments in place and time using continuous individual assessment with recursive optimal control

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that once continuous assessment and storage of each individual's life-history trajectory becomes feasible, combined with genotype and background knowledge, "Using recursive optimal control techniques, it then becomes possible to personalize treatments in place and time." The strategy treats the individual as a dynamic system whose trajectory is monitored online and steered by optimal control, extending an earlier application of on-line optimal control in an individual psychotherapeutic process (Molenaar, 1987).
