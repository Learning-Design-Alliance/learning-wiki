# Multimodal Assignments

## Description
Multimodal assignments involve a variety of assessment methods that challenge learners to create video or audio projects, write personal essays, or complete quizzes. These assignments encourage learners to use different parts of their brain, promoting active involvement in their own learning and improving retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to tools for creating video and audio projects. LMS or platform for uploading and sharing assignments. Clear instructions and assessment criteria.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources and technical support. Requires clear instructions and assessment criteria. Potential for inequitable access to resources.

### Affordances
- Engages different parts of the brain, promoting active learning and improving retention. Allows learners to demonstrate their understanding in creative ways. Encourages learners to use different parts of their brain and think outside the box.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose assignment types based on their strengths and interests. Provide options for individual or group projects. Offer flexible deadlines and personalized feedback.
