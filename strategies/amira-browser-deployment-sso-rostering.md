# Deploy Amira as a browser-based program integrated with district systems through Single Sign-On and automated rostering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Amira is a browser-based program requiring no special software, working on Chromebooks, PCs, Macs, iPads, and tablets. It "integrates with district systems through Single Sign-On (Clever, ClassLink, Google, SAML) and supports automated rostering via SIS/LMS." A standard school or home internet plan is usually sufficient, though stronger bandwidth improves performance when many students are online at once.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Devices should have at least 4 GB of RAM and a working microphone; headphones with a mic are encouraged in noisier classrooms.
- A standard school or home internet plan is usually sufficient.
#### Constraints
- Amira operates as a closed, secure system that never accesses the open internet; the platform is fully compliant with COPPA, FERPA, and CIPA.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten and first grade students
- elementary struggling readers

### Target Learning Goals
- early reading outcomes
- oral reading fluency
