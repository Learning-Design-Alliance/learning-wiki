# Explain the rationale for prediction activities and reframe failure to sustain student engagement with desirable difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students may not recognize the value of constructing their own knowledge, the article recommends that "when using prediction activities, extra care may be necessary to explain the rationale in order to convince students of their value". Instructors can frame prediction as preparation for independent learning about students' own interests to raise utility value, and use instructor talk to remind students that failure is part of innovation and learning, reinforcing a growth mindset.
