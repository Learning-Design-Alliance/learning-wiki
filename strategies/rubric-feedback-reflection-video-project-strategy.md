# Provide a detailed rubric, peer feedback cycles, and a final reflection when implementing student video projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' implementation strategy for adopting student-created video assessments: pair the video assignment with structured peer review, a quantitative grading rubric, and self-reflection. They state that "it is necessary to provide a detailed rubric, chances for students to receive and implement peer feedback, and a final reflection on the overall learning process", with reflection opportunities at the midpoint and end of the semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quantitative rubric developed for the project that can be used by students for grading.
- An example grading process demonstrated using the quantitative rubric.
#### Constraints
- Peer grades skew high when students know the grade is final, so the strategy suits midterm rather than final assessment use.

### Target Learners
- university students in large introductory science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate peer assessment
- reflection on understanding
- improvement of didactic video quality

## Claims

- [Video quality improves from the first to the second submission, suggesting an iterative process with peer review contributes to learning](../claims/iterative-peer-review-improves-video-quality.md) [+W]
- [Peer reviewers inflate grades when they recognize the grade they are giving as final, so rubric-based training is needed for peer review to serve as assessment](../claims/peer-grade-inflation-final-assessment-video-project.md) [+W]
- [Students reported majority-positive experiences with the peer review process and perceived experiential learning in the video project](../claims/positive-peer-review-experience-experiential-learning.md) [+W]
- [The video project's grade distribution closely resembles the first midterm's distribution, supporting its use in place of a midterm assessment](../claims/video-project-grade-distribution-resembles-midterm.md) [+W]
