# A dual-track training model based on continuous learning plus long-term practice is the fundamental pathway for specialist nurses to develop into clinical nursing experts.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Interviewees identified a dual-track training model based on "continuous learning + long-term practice" as the fundamental pathway for specialist nurses to develop into clinical nursing experts. Periodic retraining includes regularly participating in specialized peritoneal dialysis training, academic seminars, and workshops on new technologies. Long-term clinical practice involves managing a broader range of patients, participating in consultations for complex cases, and receiving clinical mentorship from senior nursing experts. Both components are indispensable.
