# Expect Excellence

## Description
Demonstrating a belief in excellence and setting high expectations is essential for creating a successful PBL culture. Dispelling the myth that PBL does not work for all students and providing proper scaffolding are key to helping students achieve their full potential.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a commitment to providing high-quality instruction and support. Demands a belief in the potential of all students.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and resources to provide individualized scaffolding. Can be challenging to balance high expectations with student support.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Foster a growth mindset, promote high expectations, provide proper scaffolding, dispel myths about student capabilities, create a culture of excellence.

### Affordances
- Promotes a belief in the potential of all students. Creates a culture of high achievement and excellence. Provides the necessary support for students to succeed.

### Personalization
- Scaffolding can be tailored to individual student needs and learning styles. High expectations can be communicated in a way that is supportive and encouraging.
