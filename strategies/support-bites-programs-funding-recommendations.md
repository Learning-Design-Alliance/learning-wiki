# Increase financial and structural support for volunteer-run Bites programs and for graduate-student science-communication training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its Recommendations section, the white paper urges the astronomy community to sponsor research on the efficacy of Bites programs, increase financial and structural support for volunteer-run sites, and support graduate-student education in scientific communication. It notes the programs "are run entirely by community volunteers and operate on a lean budget", and that external funding could cover author travel to conferences for live-blogging, community-building, and education workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community or society financial investment; structures like the AAS EPD Mini-Grant Program
#### Constraints
- Current operations depend on volunteer labor and AAS-provided server support; some sites are self-funded
