# Bounce Cards

## Description
Bounce cards provide a structured way for students to engage in cooperative learning by promoting active listening, turn-taking, and perspective sharing. Students choose topics for classroom conversation and plan how to discuss them in a variety of ways. In this strategy, teachers model conversation techniques and then explicitly teach the following three approaches that students can use to respond to one another’s comments during a conversation: Bounce: Bounce an idea off what a peer said or elaborate on it. Sum it up: Rephrase what a peer said and comment on certain parts. Inquire: Ask a question.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured conversation prompts, time for modeling and practice, a classroom environment conducive to dialogue, intentional grouping of students
#### Constraints
- May require significant upfront training to ensure students understand and apply the techniques effectively; some students may struggle with the open-ended nature of the discussions
