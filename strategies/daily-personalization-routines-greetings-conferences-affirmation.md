# Daily personalization routines: greeting students, short conferences, open-door policies, office hours, active listening, and authentic affirmation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy packages the brief's everyday classroom routines for deepening student-teacher relationships: greeting students as they arrive, holding short conferences during instructional or advisory periods, maintaining an open-door policy and weekly office hours, practicing active listening and question posing, and expressing authentic affirmation such as "I believe in you" or "Thanks for sharing." The brief says these practices make young people feel valued and reinforce that teachers care about their growth and success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent implementation; the brief states the tenor and authenticity of implementation matter, and practices are most effective when they lead to trustful, respectful, and reciprocal interactions
#### Constraints
- Secondary schedules with seven or eight teachers per day and teachers seeing more than 150 students daily make such relationship-building challenging without structural support

### Target Learners
- secondary school students, including middle and high schoolers

### Target Learning Goals
- student-teacher trust and connection
- student sense of being valued
- foundation for building knowledge from what students bring
