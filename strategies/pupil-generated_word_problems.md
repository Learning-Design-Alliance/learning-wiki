# Pupil-Generated Word Problems

## Description
Learners create their own word problems to enhance awareness and sharpen basic math skills, fostering comfort in solving a variety of math problems. This active creation process helps learners become more attuned to the structure and components of word problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to math resources, tools for writing and solving problems (e.g., paper, pencils), and structured time for learners to create, share, and solve problems.
#### Constraints
- Learners may struggle with creating problems that are appropriately challenging and aligned with learning objectives. Some learners may be hesitant to share their problems or may be less motivated to solve problems created by their peers.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Elementary and middle school learners, particularly those studying math and related subjects.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop problem-solving skills, enhance awareness of math concepts in real-world contexts, promote critical thinking, and foster creativity and confidence in solving math problems.

### Affordances
- Promotes deeper understanding of math concepts, enhances problem-solving skills, fosters creativity, and builds confidence in solving a variety of math problems. Learners become more aware of the structure and components of word problems, which aids in comprehension and application.

### Personalization
- Problems can be tailored to different skill levels, math operations, and real-world contexts. Learners can choose the type of problem they want to create, the complexity of the problem, and the settings in which the problem is situated.
