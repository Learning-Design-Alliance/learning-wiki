## Description
The partnership builds the toolkit through joint development with participating districts rather than delivering a fixed product. Activities listed include "Co-develop the TFT" and "Refine the TFT", and the document notes that "REL Midwest employs an iterative research and development model, across several cohorts, in the development and refinement work".
