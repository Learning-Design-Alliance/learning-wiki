# Teach the RAP paraphrasing strategy with reading strategy charts as the portfolio entry routine

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation recipe: train students to read a paragraph, ask themselves about its main idea and details, and put them in their own words in a reading strategy chart, so each lesson can be paraphrased via RAP. The article instructs that students "were guided to record their writings (through the RAP strategy) in a chart", with some students presenting their writings in class and receiving peer comments. The authors name this strategy-and-chart combination as an implication of the study for developing reading comprehension through portfolios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Initial strategy training with several models written on the board involving both teacher and students
- Two sessions of training concerning the development and evaluation of portfolios
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in the context of reading comprehension (III) for translation majors

### Target Learners
- EFL university students in reading comprehension courses

### Target Learning Goals
- paraphrasing and main-idea identification
- reading comprehension
- portfolio documentation of progress
