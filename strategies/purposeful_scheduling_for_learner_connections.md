# Purposeful Scheduling for Learner Connections

## Description
Instructors can allocate class time specifically for getting to know learners through various activities. Sitting with learners during group work facilitates learning more about them. One-on-one conferences allow learners to share information, fostering connections. Before and after class, instructors can engage with learners, uncovering their interests and building rapport. This strategy makes content personal and relevant by integrating learners' experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated class time, incorporation of individual or small-group activities, and willingness of the instructor to share personal anecdotes.
#### Constraints
- Time constraints, curriculum demands, and careful planning are needed to balance content delivery and relationship-building activities. Relevance of the activity to learners’ lives must be considered to foster authentic engagement.
