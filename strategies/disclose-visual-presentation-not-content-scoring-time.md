# Disclose to learners what changes (visual presentation) and what does not (question content, scoring, time allowed)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For behaviourally-adaptive security layers, the article recommends that institutional communication focus on the separation between presentation-level change and unchanged assessment substance. Because the mechanism never alters content and its footprint is demonstrably reduced for accommodated candidates, disclosure can be framed around exactly those two boundaries rather than around the mechanism's internals.
