# Integrate expert-narrated videos as pre-session material within a blended neuroangiography curriculum combining case-based learning, discussion, and simulation practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose a curriculum structure in which an expert-narrated instructional video is used before sessions, followed by interactive components: "we propose that an effective neuroangiography curriculum incorporates the combination of an instructional expert video (pre-session video), followed by case-based learning, interactive discussions, and simulation lab practice". They argue video alone is insufficient because it lacks real-time interaction and exposure to complications, while blending video with hands-on training can build both conceptual understanding and procedural execution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires hands-on simulation practice and live discussion components alongside the video, since video-based instruction alone lacks real-time interaction and error-management exposure.
#### Constraints
- The study assessed only immediate performance on aortic arch vessel selection in a silicone flow model; transfer to clinical angiography and long-term retention were not evaluated.

### Target Learners
- Third- and fourth-year medical students
- Neurointerventional residents, especially beginners

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic neuroangiographic catheterization skills
- Catheter selection and vessel navigation
