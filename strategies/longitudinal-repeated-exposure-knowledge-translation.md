# Use longitudinal approaches combining repeated exposure, clinical case discussions, and practical application to translate knowledge into practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "future educational strategies could include integrating environmental health considerations into clinical reasoning, for example, by systematically exploring occupational and environmental exposures during patient history taking or by documenting potential environmental determinants of health in medical records." It further states that "longitudinal approaches combining repeated exposure, clinical case discussions, and practical application may help facilitate the translation of knowledge into practice," aligning with spiral curricula and progressive integration of competencies over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for learners to apply knowledge in real clinical settings over time
#### Constraints
- Early-stage residents may have limited opportunities to apply newly acquired knowledge in clinical settings immediately

### Target Learners
- family medicine residents
- early-stage residents

### Target Learning Goals
- apply environmental health knowledge in clinical practice
- integrate environmental exposure assessment into patient history taking
