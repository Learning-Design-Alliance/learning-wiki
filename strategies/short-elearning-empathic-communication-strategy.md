# Use short e-learning courses to expand empathic communication training for busy perinatal clinicians

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that busy clinical midwives improve communication skills via well-designed short e-learning materials, because e-learning content is accessible anywhere and requires few staff and limited preparation time compared with OSCE-style assessment. The authors state that "Busy clinical midwives are recommended to improve their communication skills in a short duration via e-learning educational materials that are well designed", enabling large-scale, low-cost skills acquisition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-designed e-learning materials combining knowledge and skills content
#### Constraints
- Effects were demonstrated in enthusiastic volunteer participants in a 1-day seminar setting; long-term effects are unknown and the sample was smaller than planned

### Target Learners
- midwives
- perinatal healthcare workers

### Target Learning Goals
- empathic communication skills
- perinatal mental health knowledge
