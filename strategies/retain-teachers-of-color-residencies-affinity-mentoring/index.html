Invest in retention of teachers of color through residencies, affinity groups, and induction/mentoring¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief warns that recruitment efforts "are compromised when districts and schools have difficulty retaining teachers of color," and recommends investing in high-retention pathways, leadership growth, and early-career support. Illinois' actions include an Alternative Educator Licensure Program requiring a first year of residency, Teacher Residency Planning Grants partnering higher education with high-need districts, a $2 million investment in affinity groups for educators of color for community building and professional development, and a two-year induction and mentoring program for new teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programming must provide ongoing support and growth opportunities and improve working conditions that disproportionately affect teachers of color

Constraints¶

  • Illinois earned a red on cultural-competence professional learning for school and district leaders: an unpublished Advance Illinois survey found most districts had not done required implicit bias training or relied on unsatisfactory click-through training

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers of color early in their careers and seeking leadership growth in Illinois

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Retaining a diverse educator workforce and improving working conditions

Affordances¶

  • Six Strategy State Educator Diversity Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Invest in high-quality induction and mentoring programs, which are associated with higher retention particularly for teachers of color
  • Retain teachers of color through leadership pipelines, multi-year induction and mentoring, and culturally responsive school climates
  • Invest in retention efforts for teachers of color that improve working conditions and provide personal and professional growth opportunities
  • Build high-retention, supportive pathways into teaching for candidates of color
  • Diversify the educator workforce through Grow Your Own programs, scholarships, and retention supports
  • State and federal policy recommendations: recruit and prepare more candidates, retain new teachers through early-career mentoring, and improve working conditions
  • Underwrite comprehensive preparation, improve teaching conditions, raise compensation, and expand induction to retain teachers of color
  • Support teacher residency programs, which are associated with higher retention rates for teachers of color
