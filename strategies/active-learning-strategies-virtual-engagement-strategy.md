# Implement active learning strategies such as case studies, group discussions, and virtual collaborative projects to enhance engagement in virtual courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends replacing traditional lecture-based virtual teaching with active learning approaches to counter the boredom and disengagement students reported. It states that "active learning strategies such as, case studies, group discussions, and virtual collaborative projects can be implemented" to enhance student engagement and reduce boredom. It further suggests effective use of technology, including interactive online platforms, virtual reality simulations, and gamification elements, to create more dynamic learning experiences.
