# Extend a vaccine education knowledgebase with multimedia, storytelling, cancer-awareness content, and subject matter expert accuracy evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking development strategy for the knowledgebase: obtain "feedback from subject matter experts to improve the overall quality once the prototype SEMS tool can facilitate accuracy evaluation"; add patient-level education about HPV-caused cancers such as cervical and oropharyngeal cancer to raise vaccination awareness from a cancer-awareness perspective; link concepts with complementary multimedia content like video and images for dynamic delivery; and explore incorporating storytelling components, since "there is also evidence that storytelling may have an impact to educate and raise awareness of vaccination".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A prototype SEMS tool able to facilitate accuracy evaluation by subject matter experts
- A machine-readable ontological knowledgebase to which multimedia and story components can be linked
#### Constraints
- Additional HPV-related cancer information is stated as needed to improve the robustness of the knowledgebase

### Target Learners
- patients and parents considering HPV vaccination

### Target Learning Goals
- raise awareness of HPV vaccination need from a cancer-awareness perspective
- improve patient vaccine literacy
