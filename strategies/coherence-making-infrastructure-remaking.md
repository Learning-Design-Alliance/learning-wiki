# Rally for coherence-making and infrastructure remaking activities between innovation and practice

## Description
This fourth IMPROV action area increases the fit between an intended innovation and organizational practice. The article states that "Coherence-making activities are pivotal for increasing the ﬁt between the intended innovation and organizational practice." These activities require participation of both researchers and practitioners on a continual basis, involve negotiation among actors with different professional conventions, and constantly reshape power relations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coherence-making requires intention and care, negotiations by actors representing different professional conventions or technological capabilities, and continual participation of researchers and practitioners
#### Constraints
- Coherence is not always achievable, especially for entrenched beliefs; new practices might demand alteration or abolishment of existing infrastructures that carry systemic oppression and inequity

### Target Learners
- teachers
- district leaders
- researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- aligning innovations with organizational routines, resources, and policy contexts
