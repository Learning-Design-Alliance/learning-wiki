# Support college transitions by addressing college choice, institutional fit, and college support — not student preparation alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes that efforts to promote attainment must attend not just to skills making students "ready for college," but also to college choice and the role colleges play in supporting students. It recommends that high schools help students make informed choices and that post-secondary institutions be ready to support students in attaining a degree, since the transition to college removes familiar structures and supports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High schools help students make informed college choices; colleges provide supports matched to student needs
#### Constraints
- Structures and supports that guided academic behaviors in high school may not be available in college, so some students struggle

### Target Learners
- first-generation college students
- underrepresented minority students
- high school students transitioning to college

### Target Learning Goals
- college persistence and degree attainment
- sustained academic behaviors during transition
