# Check terrain safety and prefer outdoor play for the game

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors instruct organizers to check potential pitfalls and dangers on the terrain before play and recommend taking children outside, calling it "a wonderful way to spend the day". If no safe outdoor terrain can be secured, the game may be played indoors in a gymnasium or sports hall. They also note the game could be adapted as a computer game, but argue most of the fun comes from real-time interaction among real players, which trains endurance, creativity, sneaking skills and fortitude.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-play check of the play terrain for pitfalls and dangers
- Safe outdoor terrain, or alternatively a gymnasium or sports hall
#### Constraints
- Indoor play is a fallback only when no safe outdoor terrain can be secured

### Target Learners
- secondary and middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and engaging implementation of active outdoor science games
