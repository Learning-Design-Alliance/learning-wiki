Replacing think-write pedagogy with conversation-based writing activities¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper's first recommendation is "to consider alternative strategies to the think-write pedagogy", in which writers work in isolation. Teachers would elicit the talk about writing that literate writers engage in, making allowances for reading aloud, pair invention, co-authoring, small group work and a writing studio or workshop arrangement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Course plans make allowances for reading aloud, pair work to invent material for texts, co-authored texts, and small group work on important writing problems.
  • The classroom is arranged as a writing studio or writing workshop to elicit the vocal-to-scribal dialogue the paper links to effective writing.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • College composition students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Producing effective writing through dialogue about aims, ideas and methods
