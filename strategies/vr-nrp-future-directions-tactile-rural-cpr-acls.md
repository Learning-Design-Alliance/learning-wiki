# Future VR-NRP development: add tactile feedback, target rural access, and adapt the environment to CPR and ACLS training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors outline forward-looking development paths for the platform. They propose that "by adding tactile feedback to the VR simulation, in particular when practising the bag mask procedures, trainees will develop a stronger sense of realism", responding to open-ended feedback calling for a more hands-on approach. They also plan to explore use "in the context of healthcare professionals working in remote or rural areas where in-person training may be difficult to access", and note the environment "can be easily modified for CPR and ACLS training in the pediatric and adult patient contexts".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physical bag-and-mask hardware that inflates according to the user's actual operation for tactile feedback
- a field experiment or evaluation in a rural clinical setting to support the rural-access idea
#### Constraints
- These directions are proposed, not yet tested; the authors state a rural field evaluation might provide better support for the access claim

### Target Learners
- NRP providers
- healthcare professionals in remote or rural areas
- CPR and ACLS trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Resuscitation skill refreshing
- increasing access to standardized refresher learning
