# Student Feedback on the Class

## Description
This strategy involves asking for student feedback to inform your teaching practice and improve engagement. Regularly solicit feedback from students to understand what works and what doesn't, especially when starting a new grading period or introducing new content. Be mindful of how you ask questions to encourage honest and constructive criticism and consider providing sentence starters or multiple-choice options to make it easier for students to provide feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A way to solicit feedback from students, a willingness to listen to student feedback, and a willingness to make changes based on student feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires a willingness to be open to criticism.
