# Formulate behavioural objectives with action-verb descriptors instead of know or understand

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Acknowledging that re-framing knowledge behaviourally and steering clear of objectives such as know or understand can be a challenge initially, the annex provides a non-exhaustive list of descriptors for behavioural objectives, organised into information acquisition, communication, analysis, and leveraging. It is offered as a kick-start for readers to develop their own behavioural objectives for PI.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objectives must name observable learner actions rather than internal states such as knowing or understanding
#### Constraints
- The list is explicitly non-exhaustive; the authors encourage exploring the extensive possibilities of natural language and action verbs

### Target Learners
- course content creators
- educators

### Target Learning Goals
- writing measurable behavioural objectives for PI materials
