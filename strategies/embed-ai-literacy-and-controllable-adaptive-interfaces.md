# Embed AI literacy as a credit-bearing module and design adaptive systems with transparent, user-controllable recommendation interfaces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that AI literacy buffers negative emotional effects, the article recommends that universities embed AI literacy as a credit-bearing module and that designers build adaptive systems with "lucid, user-controllable recommended interfaces." For educators, the article advises tutoring students to use AI tools critically and actively rather than passively, and incorporating reflective actions that help students recognize when algorithmic recommendations may undermine their own planning and monitoring tactics.
