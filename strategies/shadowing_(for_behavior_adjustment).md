# Shadowing (for behavior adjustment)

## Description
An adult follows a child everywhere they go, staying within arm's reach to prevent unwanted behavior before it happens. The child should not feel singled out or punished, and the other children should not be aware of the shadowing. It is used to keep the class safe while teaching the child new strategies or waiting for them to outgrow the behavior. Shadowing continues until the child is two weeks incident-free.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated adult, close proximity to the child, awareness of the child's typical behavior patterns
#### Constraints
- Requires a dedicated adult resource, can be labor-intensive, may not address the underlying cause of the behavior
