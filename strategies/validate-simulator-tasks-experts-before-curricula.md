# Validate simulator training tasks with multiple experts and similar tasks across systems before implementing a simulator into training curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that before a training simulator such as PoLaRS is adopted into robotic training curricula, validation should use similar training tasks on both systems and involve multiple experts at different experience levels. The authors write that "it is advised to validate similar training tasks for both systems with multiple experts", and the conclusion states full construct validity needs to be established for different experience levels and training tasks before curricular implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Similar training tasks must be available on both systems being compared
- Multiple experts at different experience levels must participate in validation
#### Constraints
- Skills transfer between training tasks and systems was not demonstrated yet in this study
- Future studies should be conducted with comparable body postures across systems

### Target Learners
- novice robotic surgeons
- surgical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- robotic surgical technical skills
