# Provide accessible caregiver resources and QR-code video lessons to strengthen the school-home literacy connection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Literacy Packs include a Family Support Hub giving families instructional materials, comprehension prompts, and QR codes linking to short video lessons that model decoding strategies. The article states these tools "empower families to reinforce phonemic awareness, phonics, and fluency skills at home, strengthening the school-home connection and boosting students' motivation and confidence in reading."

### Context
#### Requirements
