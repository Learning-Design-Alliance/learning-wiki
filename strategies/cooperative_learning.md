# Cooperative Learning

## Description
Designing classroom activities that involve cooperative work encourages a growth mindset. Learners feel a sense of responsibility to the group to try their best, which creates a positive feedback loop of effort and success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-structured group activities, clear roles and responsibilities, facilitation skills to manage group dynamics.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure equal participation and avoid social loafing. May be challenging for learners who prefer individual work.

### Personalization
- Group assignments can be tailored to individual learner's strengths and interests, ensuring that everyone can contribute meaningfully.
