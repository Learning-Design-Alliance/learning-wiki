# Engage faculty in reviewing student perception data at a curriculum retreat as an ongoing student-faculty partnership for curricular assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors describe sharing their perception–performance analyses with faculty at an annual curriculum retreat so instructors could re-examine content selection, and frame this as recurring student-faculty partnership. They report "The information was perceived useful by faculty in re-examining the selection of content and topics to be delivered during their modules" and that they "continue to collect this data" to evaluate curricular changes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Annual collection of student perception data and a faculty venue (curriculum retreat) for reviewing and acting on the findings
#### Constraints
- Impact of resulting curricular changes on Step 1 performance was still being evaluated at the time of writing

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in preclinical (M1 and M2) curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- continuous improvement of preclinical curriculum coverage of licensing-examination content
