# Support students' sensemaking in physics by promoting modeling practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the observed intertwining of modeling components with sensemaking stages, the authors recommend that future research support students' sensemaking in physics by promoting modeling practices. The case studies suggest that when students construct mental models and engage external representations through the DFDIF components, they can navigate the Sensemaking Epistemic Game, while barriers to modeling can cut sensemaking short.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction should elicit engagement with mental models and external representations so that modeling components align with sensemaking stages.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation derives from two case studies of one task; the authors frame it as suggesting ways for future research rather than a validated instructional prescription.

### Target Learners
- introductory college physics students

### Target Learning Goals
