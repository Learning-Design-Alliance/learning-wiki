# Incorporate CPR skills training into Nigerian secondary school curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the Federal Ministry of Education give serious attention to incorporating CPR skills training programmes into the Nigerian school system, in line with the global trend and in preparation for better management of expected increases in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest cases due to growing modernization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained instructors (AHA Certified CPR Instructor), manikins, power point lectures, skills evaluation guides
#### Constraints
- Based on evidence from one Local Government Area in Rivers State; the article recommends more studies in other parts of Nigeria

### Target Learners
- Nigerian senior secondary school students (SS1 and SS2)

### Target Learning Goals
- CPR skills acquisition, retention, and willingness to perform as bystander CPR providers
