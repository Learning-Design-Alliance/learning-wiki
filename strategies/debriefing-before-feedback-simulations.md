# Include debriefing with reflective questions after simulation sessions before receiving feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend including reflective questions after the lesson and before feedback, to stimulate reflection and support retrieval practice. They propose questions such as "When did you notice that the pupils did not really understand the content of the lesson?" to deepen self-assessment before feedback is given.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reflective questions should follow the lesson and come before the feedback is given
#### Constraints
- The authors note that timely feedback differs depending on the task, and sometimes delayed feedback can promote transfer better than direct feedback

### Target Learners
- preservice teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective practice
- managing classroom dynamics
- fostering inclusive and engaging discussions
