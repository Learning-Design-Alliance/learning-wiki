# Screen lessons, texts, activities, and videos in advance to ensure they are not activating for students and families

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before using materials, the brief instructs educators to "ensure that they are not activating for students and their families." Teachers should check that all content is developmentally appropriate and free of aggression and violence, even in cartoons or fiction, and ensure all language is nonviolent, avoiding words such as trigger, shoot, and aim. Where possible, families should be told a video or activity topic in advance so they can opt out or choose an alternative if the topic is activating for them.
