# Gallery Quote Walk

## Description
Students pick a quote that expresses how they feel in the moment. Display quotes around the classroom to create stations. After reading all quotes, each student stands by the quote that reflects how they are feeling. Volunteers then share their quotes and how that reflects their current feelings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quotes (printed and displayed), time for individual reflection and whole-class sharing
#### Constraints
- Some students may not be drawn to written language or may not feel comfortable expressing their feelings through quotes. It is important to provide a variety of quotes to choose from and to create a safe and supportive environment where students feel comfortable sharing.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Connect feelings with written expressions, encourage self-awareness, promote sharing and empathy

### Affordances
- Provides a creative and engaging way for students to explore and express their feelings, especially for those who may be drawn to written language. Normalizes talking about feelings and empowers students to view their emotions as assets.

### Personalization
- Students can create or pick their own quotes if there is not one that resonates with them. Instructors can provide a variety of quotes to choose from, representing different emotions and perspectives.
