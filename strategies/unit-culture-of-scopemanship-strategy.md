## Description
The article recommends practical steps to establish a culture of scopemanship in the endoscopy unit: "these principles could be incorporated in the unit mission statement and propelled by the involvement of colleagues and a commitment from staff of all hierarchies." It also encourages trainees and trainers to "join the conversation on social media to share positive examples of scopemanship to promote good practices," enabling global dialogue and direct interaction with patients and patient advocates.
