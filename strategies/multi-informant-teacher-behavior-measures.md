# Use multi-informant or observational measures of teacher behavior in future rGE research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To clarify whether teachers respond differently to children depending on genetic propensities, the article recommends moving beyond teacher-reported questionnaires that may partly index child behavior. The authors write that "Future work should incorporate multi-informant or observational measures of teacher behavior to better clarify whether, and how strongly, teachers respond differently to children depending on their genetic propensities."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to independent observers or multiple informants of classroom interaction
#### Constraints
- Current findings rest on teacher-reported STRS items, some of which refer to the child's actions or affect

### Target Learners
- early primary school children

### Target Learning Goals
- student–teacher relationship quality
- educational performance
