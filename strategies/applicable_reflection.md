# Applicable Reflection

## Description
Applicable reflection involves guiding students to consider how their past performance will influence their future work. It reminds students that they’re going to have to use at least some of these skills again, so there’s no sense in making the same mistakes. Reflection has a real and immediate purpose: You know where this course is going, so how are you going to improve the quality of your own journey?

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Transparency about future tasks and assignments, documentation of reflections.

- Provides a framework for assessing how past performance will influence future work, encourages continuous improvement. Digital tools can make reflections easy to access for individuals.

### Personalization
- Teachers can be transparent about future tasks and assignments, and make reflections accessible. Students can store their reflections wherever that work is happening.
