# Developing workplace e-PBL programs grounded in practitioner needs interviews and case-based scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's development workflow pairs in-depth interviews with practitioners to identify education goals, suitable media, and training duration, with a literature review to justify the instructional method, then builds PBL scenarios "based on various infection control cases collected from the in-depth interviews to enhance the suitability of the problem scenario". Expert review by nursing and computer education specialists finalizes content before pilot testing and deployment. This yields an authentic, workload- and context-sensitive online program for time-poor staff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to experienced practitioners for interviews and case material
- Collaboration between domain experts and e-learning producers
#### Constraints
- The article notes the strategy was shaped by constraints of the COVID-19 pandemic and staff workloads, favoring short (2–3 h) web-based delivery

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses, care workers, and nursing assistants in long-term care

### Target Learning Goals
- Early detection and prevention of infection
- Critical thinking in real workplace problems
