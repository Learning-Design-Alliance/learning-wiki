Word-finding warm-up game with flash cards¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A classroom activity in which, at the start of a session, previously taught words are reviewed and students search flash cards to find the new word matching a definition or synonym given by the teacher. Per the article, "Then the students were to find the new words in the flash card according to the definition and synonyms about each word presented by the teacher. The first student who found the word raised her hand and introduced the new word to the other students." The article frames this as a low-risk warm-up that fosters interaction and participation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A set of vocabulary flash cards from prior sessions and teacher-provided definitions or synonyms as cues

Constraints¶

  • Described for pre-university EFL classroom settings in the study

Target Learners¶

  • pre-university EFL students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • vocabulary retrieval, pronunciation and interactive use of new words

Claims¶

  • Traditional vocabulary teaching produced no statistically significant pretest-to-posttest gain in the control group [+W]
  • The experimental group showed a statistically significant vocabulary gain from pretest to posttest under the flash card treatment [+W]
  • Iranian pre-university students taught vocabulary with flash cards significantly outperformed traditionally taught students on a vocabulary posttest [+W]
  • The study's findings are limited to female Iranian pre-university students at one public school, with psychological factors unexamined [~W]
