# Post-test follow-up protocol: flag failures to personal tutors and assign topic-specific handwritten exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A follow-up protocol attached to the preparatory tests: students must pass all tests at a 75% pass mark by the end of the first week, repeating tests as many times as they like; "After the deadline, results are shared with all lecturers, and failures are flagged up to each student's personal tutor." A student who fails a topic meets their tutor and completes handwritten exercises on that topic, submitted by an agreed date.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must pass all tests (the pass mark being 75%) by the end of the first week of the semester, with no time limit per attempt
#### Constraints
- For wrong answers, hints and explanations of common misconceptions are given, but the correct answers are not shown in this case

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate mathematics students who fail one or more diagnostic topics

### Target Learning Goals
- remediate topic-specific gaps in background mathematics identified by the diagnostic element
