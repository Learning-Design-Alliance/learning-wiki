# Use multisensory exercises (throats, mirrors, kazoos, rubber bands, sound associations, finger counts) to help learners comprehend pronunciation features

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The digest recommends that, in addition to careful planning, teachers "be responsive to learners' needs and explore a variety of methods to help learners comprehend pronunciation features." It lists concrete exercises: touching the throat to feel voicing vibration, using mirrors to see tongue and lip placement, using kazoos to reinforce intonation patterns, stretching rubber bands to illustrate vowel lengths, providing visual or auditory associations for a sound (a buzzing bee for /z/), and holding up fingers to indicate syllable counts.
