# Anonymous monitoring page displaying library-level drill progress to encourage competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A monitoring page was set up displaying the number of students in each library, their total attempts at KCSE drills, and overall progress in terms of acing individual drills and the entire KCSE drill collection. The page is refreshed several times per hour and serves to monitor progress and to demonstrate aggregate performance to all libraries, enticing staff to advance use of the system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet-connected libraries
- Anonymised tutor-web drill data
#### Constraints
- Data are only accessible for general anonymised statistical analysis since there are no instructors in the library setting

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in low-income regions

### Target Learning Goals
- secondary school mathematics
- KCSE national exam preparation
