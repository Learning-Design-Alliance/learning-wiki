# Use validated situations as starting points with 3–5 learning objectives per simulation scenario

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends building simulation scenarios from generic situations as starting points (SSPs) rather than fully detailed clinical cases, because SSPs align with the French educational reform and support scenario design by teachers. "The SSPs can be declined in an unlimited number of scenarios leading to various diagnoses, thus allowing the learning of the targeted TS or NTS to be adapted to the progression of the residents." Each scenario must meet specified learning objectives, following the SoFraSimS framework, "they recommended 3 to 5 main learning objectives (TS or NTS) per scenario."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each scenario must be designed to meet one or more specified learning objectives, in compliance with a formalized model such as the SoFraSimS scenario framework
#### Constraints
- Use of the selected SSPs is at the discretion of the teachers

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Contextualized learning of targeted technical and non-technical skills through varied clinical scenarios
