# Real-world Math Applications

## Description
Connecting math concepts to real-world applications is a strategy that can be used to enhance understanding, relevance, and engagement. Instructors provide context and connect math concepts to an adult learner's world, so math can be seen as relevant and applicable to their daily lives and work. Real-world connections are also great ways to integrate math with other disciplines to create cross-curricular connections, supporting Disciplinary Literacy, and fostering confidence and Motivation by helping adults see the relevance of all they are learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful planning and preparation, access to relevant resources and materials, and a supportive learning environment. Instructors need to be knowledgeable about real-world applications of math and able to make connections to learners' lives.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming and resource-intensive. May require significant upfront planning and preparation. Learners may struggle with self-direction and time management.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Adult learners in basic education programs, particularly those who struggle to see the relevance of math to their lives.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase learner engagement, improve understanding of math concepts, enhance confidence in math skills, and promote transfer of learning to real-world situations.

### Affordances
- Helps learners see the relevance of math to their daily lives, increasing motivation and engagement. Provides concrete examples and applications that make abstract concepts more understandable. Strengthens retention by connecting new information to existing knowledge and experiences.

### Personalization
- Tailor real-world examples to learners' individual interests, experiences, and cultural backgrounds. Allow learners to choose projects or activities that are relevant to their lives.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track learner progress on project milestones, assess the quality of their final product, and evaluate their ability to apply knowledge and skills to real-world problems. Measure gains in content knowledge, self-confidence, collaboration, and engagement through pre- and post-assessments.

## Impact
- Leads to increased learner engagement, improved understanding of math concepts, enhanced confidence in math skills, and promotion of transfer of learning to real-world situations. Increased learner outcomes and increased motivation. Increased ability to apply knowledge and skills to real-world problems.
