# Provide schools, administrators, and teachers with supports and resources when curbing suspension use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that districts planning to roll back suspensions consider what supports schools need to make the change work. It states that "administrators may need support to create school-wide culture changes and protocols, and teachers may need resources related to managing classroom behaviors or trauma-informed instructional practices." It adds that implementing restorative approaches requires training and staff resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must think through the implications of rolling back suspensions and allocate resources in terms of training and staff
#### Constraints
- The article states implementing more restorative approaches to addressing student misbehavior and conflict is hard work and requires resources

### Target Learners
- high school students in district public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- school safety, attendance, and academic achievement supported by improved discipline practice
