# Targeted educational initiatives to close the gap between evidence-based sepsis recommendations and clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends education as the route to better guideline adherence: "Our findings underscore the imperative for targeted educational initiatives aimed at closing the gap between evidence-based recommendations and clinical practice, particularly in resource-constrained settings." The survey results it rests on show deficient recognition of sepsis and low adherence to treatment guidelines among Brazilian pediatricians. Education must be paired with resources, since the authors state it is important "to have knowledge of the guidelines but also to have resources to implement them".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational initiatives must target the specific knowledge deficits the survey identified, particularly recognition of sepsis and severe sepsis and the ACCM and SSC treatment guidelines.
#### Constraints
- The authors note adherence difficulties are related to technical and/or human resource limitations, so education alone may not close the gap in resource-constrained settings; survey data are self-reported and the sample skewed toward the southeastern region.

### Target Learners
- Pediatricians of various specialties, including general pediatricians, emergency care clinicians, pediatric intensivists, neonatologists, and residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognition and classification of pediatric sepsis and septic shock
- Adherence to international sepsis treatment guidelines including timely antibiotics and fluid resuscitation
