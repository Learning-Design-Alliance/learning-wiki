# Two-stage sequence: manual box-counting first, computational tool second

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Sequence the fractal-dimension activity in two stages. Students first "learn how is calculated the fractal dimension of any ﬁgure and they do this manually", then repeat the same box-counting procedure with ImageJ and FracLac. The deliberate pre-binarization by students has "a didactic objective, namely, to allow students to understand the process of binarization that precedes the estimation of the fractal dimension". This ordering grounds the software in a manual procedure students already command.
