# Sail through Math App

## Description
A fun math fluency iOS app that uses storytelling to help young learners develop math skills over time. Offers three different levels for playing with numbers to help kids learning addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. Besides building basic math skills, the app also helps with addition equations, subtraction equations, multiplication equations, two-step equations and some other relatively advanced math facts.  Built with a pirate theme, even has fun background music and cannonballs firing!
