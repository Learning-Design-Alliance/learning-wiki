Policy recommendations for sustaining and expanding teacher residencies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on TCLAS implementation findings, the report recommends: continuing to require key features (full-year co-teaching placements, shared governance, specified educator roles); facilitating expansion by developing new EPP partners and allowing longer implementation timelines; building pipelines for mentor teachers and residency faculty; creating multidistrict residency consortia linked to Education Service Center technical assistance; offering matching grants and funding-braiding support for sustainability; and targeting support for rural and high-poverty districts where strategic staffing is insufficient.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • State guidance and coordination; district–EPP partnership capacity

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are focused on the Texas context, though the report states they can inform other states

Target Learners¶

  • Teacher residents, mentor teachers, and residency faculty in K–12 partnerships

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustainable, scaled teacher residency pipelines addressing shortages

Related Strategies¶

  • Form partnerships with community organizations, higher education, and CTE programs to expand emergency staffing and build the talent pipeline
  • Reallocate existing district roles and budget lines—substitute teaching, paraeducator positions, and extended-day staffing—to fund and pay residents
  • Sustain and expand residency pathways through maintained state grant funding, financial aid, apprenticeship structures, and technical assistance
  • State policy levers for sustaining yearlong, stipended clinical preparation: require or incentivize full-year stipended clinical experience, fund partnerships, and reduce testing fees
