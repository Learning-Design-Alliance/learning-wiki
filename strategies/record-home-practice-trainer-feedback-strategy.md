# Record home simulation practice on the smartphone and review it with a trainer for feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the smartphone records the simulation view, trainees can capture their practice sessions and review them with a trainer, adding a feedback loop to unsupervised home practice. The article states that "the smartphone use will enable the practice to be recorded and discussed with a trainer for feedback", addressing the isolation of take-home simulation from supervisory assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A smartphone with video recording capability and access to a trainer willing to review recorded practice
#### Constraints
- The article does not report any test of this feedback mechanism's effectiveness
