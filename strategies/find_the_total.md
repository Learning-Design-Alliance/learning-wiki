# Find the Total

## Description
Learners are shown two dot cards and asked to find the total number of dots. In a number talk, learners explain how they found the total, promoting understanding of addition strategies. This activity encourages learners to practice addition in a visual and concrete way. Engaging in number talks enhances mathematical language skills and promotes the development of flexible problem-solving strategies. Supports the development of mental math skills and promotes fluency in addition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dot cards.
#### Constraints
- Learners may need initial guidance on addition strategies. The activity's effectiveness relies on clear dot arrangements that are easy to count.
