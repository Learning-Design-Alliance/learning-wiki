# Mindfulness Techniques for Emotional Regulation

## Description
Incorporating mindfulness techniques helps students manage their initial emotional impulses and reactivity. This involves practices such as meditation, deep breathing, and body scans to promote calmness and self-awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires regular time for mindfulness practice and a quiet environment. Training in mindfulness techniques for both students and educators.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for students with attention difficulties, and requires consistent practice to achieve noticeable results.
