# Scenario-based simulation training before completing a standardized self-assessment instrument

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes having professionals work through realistic case scenarios that bundle facility information by assessment-framework component, so they practise assigning component and total scores before completing the real instrument. "Simulation in health care is widely used in medical education as an active learning method and it has been shown to have great potential." The scenarios were designed "for simulation training purposes to achieve standardization and interactive learning" prior to the 2019 WHO global survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case scenarios containing information appropriate for each component of the assessment framework
- A validated scoring framework with defined component and total score ranges
#### Constraints
- The article cites prior work [22–24] for simulation's potential rather than testing these scenarios' learning outcomes itself

### Target Learners
- IPC specialists
- professionals implementing hand hygiene improvement strategies

### Target Learning Goals
- standardized correct completion of the HHSAF
