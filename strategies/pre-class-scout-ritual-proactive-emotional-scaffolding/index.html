Run a pre-class Scout ritual: identify 2-3 cognitive-emotional bottlenecks, prepare 1-2 interventions each, and anticipate logistical failures¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A three-step lesson-planning ritual requiring "no more than 10-15 minutes": identify the cognitive-emotional bottlenecks where mental friction will be highest, select or develop targeted micro-interventions written into the lesson plan as speaking notes, and anticipate mundane non-content failures (a working URL shortener, a one-page troubleshooting FAQ, a starter template, and a "panic button" protocol for students stuck over seven minutes).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Conducted during lesson planning before class

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Higher education students in labs and workshops

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Pre-empting panic and sustaining momentum through anticipated difficulty

Related Strategies¶
