Reword learning outcomes with broadly applicable terms ('healthcare seeker', 'health professional') to support interprofessional application¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

During qualitative synthesis the research group replaced the term 'patient' with 'healthcare seeker' and replaced profession-specific terms identifying the target learner (eg, dentist) with 'health professional', because most learning outcomes had potential application to a variety of professions and communication partners. The article states the term healthcare seeker "was more broadly applicable across healthcare contexts and acknowledged the active role" of the individual. The authors suggest these common outcomes may provide a platform for interprofessional education and a shift from siloed to collaborative practice and education.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learning outcomes must be reviewed for patient-specific or profession-specific wording that could equally apply to other communication interactions such as colleague or student interactions

Constraints¶

  • The parallel between therapeutic and collegial or educational interactions is identified by others whom the review cites

Target Learners¶

  • health professionals across professions
  • educators designing communication skills programmes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • communication skills applicable across healthcare contexts and communication partners

Claims¶

  • A systematic review of 168 papers yielded 1669 communication skills learning outcomes, refined to a final set of 205 [+W]

Related Strategies¶
