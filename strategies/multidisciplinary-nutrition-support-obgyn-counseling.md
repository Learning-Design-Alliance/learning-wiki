# Pair ObGyn nutrition training with multidisciplinary dietitian support while building residents' own counseling capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states nutritional counseling was historically assigned to nutritionists and dietitians and that ObGyns still need their support, but teams face "a shortage of nutritionists and dietitians in many multidisciplinary teams". It therefore recommends both improving dietitian presence and implementing ObGyn nutrition knowledge during training so residents can provide "basic and evidence-based guidance".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multidisciplinary teams with access to nutritionists or dietitians where available
- Integration of nutrition content into residency training
#### Constraints
- Short duration of clinical examination poses a limitation for integrating nutritional counseling into routine care

### Target Learners
- ObGyn residents and practicing obstetricians and gynecologists

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based nutrition counseling for women with gynecologic problems or in specific life stages
