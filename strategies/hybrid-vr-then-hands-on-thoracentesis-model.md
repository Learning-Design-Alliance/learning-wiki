# Hybrid model: initial VR training for procedural familiarity followed by supervised hands-on practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose that if confirmed in larger studies, VR could serve as a useful adjunct to traditional thoracentesis teaching. Their recommended hybrid model uses "initial VR training for basic procedural familiarity, followed by supervised hands-on practice" to balance the strengths of both approaches. This addresses the finding that 20% of participants noted the lack of realistic tactile feedback, suggesting a need for supplementary hands-on practice. Institutional investment recommendations await cost-effectiveness and long-term outcome data.
