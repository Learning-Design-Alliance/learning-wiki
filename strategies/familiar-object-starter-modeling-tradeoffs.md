# Use a familiar-object starter activity to introduce modeling trade-offs before demanding content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before introducing CCD content, students inspect familiar airplane models (die cast, remote-controlled, computer simulator, balsa wood) and compile pros and cons of each, which facilitators synthesize on a poster. The starter was "designed to act as a scaffold to encourage the learners to consider the pros and cons of their design of a model". It also served to group students into teams and introduce engineering concepts in a comfortable setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objects familiar to all students regardless of cultural or technical background
- Facilitators keep a close watch on time spent on this component
#### Constraints
- Airplanes are not related to CCDs in terms of content, so time on this component must be monitored

### Target Learners
- community college students in engineering technology

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding trade-offs in engineering models
- team communication
