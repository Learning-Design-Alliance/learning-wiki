Educators should provide clear instructions and avoid limiting student discussion to promote SDL¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that educators provide clear instructions regarding the learning activity for the promotion of SDL, and that educators should not limit students' discussion by interrupting or providing teaching. This strategy is drawn from the educator influence theme, citing Leatemia et al. (2016) and Jouhari et al. (2015).
