# Start small and stage QIST program development from individual faculty expertise toward a future standalone major

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A staged growth strategy for PUIs: begin with one faculty member developing expertise, add course modules or a course, expand faculty interest and administration buy-in, then build a concentration, minor, or certificate, with a standalone major only in the future. The article says: "One possible approach for creating QIST programs at PUIs is to sta rt small and build from there." This builds demonstrated student demand for administrators and "allows for an exit before committing too much in case the programming does not work out."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty need to coordinate the message of student demand, industry need, and external funding to administrators
#### Constraints
- A standalone QIST major is positioned as a future step since the definition of quantum engineer remains unclear in the young industry

### Target Learners
- undergraduates at predominantly undergraduate institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- access to QIST training pathways
