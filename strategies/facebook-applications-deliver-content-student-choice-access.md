# Use Facebook applications to deliver Islamic Studies content with student choice of access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy where the teacher delivers lesson content through Facebook social network applications such as wall, private message, chat box, and note, supplemented with uploaded video content in YouTube format. Students are given the choice to follow the lesson anywhere and any time they wish, and may ask questions, give opinion and interact among friends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires Facebook social network applications and uploaded video content such as drama, film editing, TV documentary, and religious discussion programs.
#### Constraints
- The strategy was implemented in a specific study over 14 weeks with secondary school Islamic Studies students in Malaysia.

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- creativity enhancement
- Islamic Studies content learning
