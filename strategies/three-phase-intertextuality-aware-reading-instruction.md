# Three-phase intertextuality-aware reading instruction (pre-reading, during-reading, post-reading)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes intertextuality-aware instruction as a teaching method "composed of three phases of pre -reading, during -reading and post -reading". Pre-reading stimulates background knowledge; during-reading raises learners' consciousness of reporting strategies and intertextuality types while clarifying ambiguous vocabulary; post-reading asks students to relate understanding to real life and express opinions. The experimental group received this in ten two-hour sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor who can analyze passages for intertextuality types and reporting modes and raise learners' consciousness of them
#### Constraints
- The article tested it only with intermediate EFL learners aged 19-23 in a private institute over ten sessions

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners of both genders

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- awareness of intertextuality and reporting strategies
