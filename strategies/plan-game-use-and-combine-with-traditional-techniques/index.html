Plan game use deliberately and combine it with traditional vocabulary techniques¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the limitations it identifies, the review recommends that teachers not bring games into class without thoroughly planning how to use them, and not apply digital games for their own sake given students' different backgrounds, needs and expectations. It also reports de Haan et al.'s (2010) recommendation of "pre-teaching vocabulary using drills and dictionary work", and stresses that language teachers and institutions must know more about computer games to use them effectively.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers and institutions need knowledge about computer games to use the media effectively

Constraints¶

  • Not all video games are useful for language learning

Target Learners¶

  • foreign language learners across age groups, particularly children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • foreign language vocabulary learning and retention

Claims¶

  • Educational computer games are effective for vocabulary acquisition in foreign language learning, per a systematic review of six studies [+W]
  • Digital game-based vocabulary learning increases children's motivation compared with traditional methods [+W]
  • Game-based vocabulary interventions outperformed control conditions in several reviewed studies [+W]
  • High game interactivity hinders vocabulary acquisition: players recalled significantly less vocabulary than watchers [+W]
  • Inference-based computer games produce better vocabulary learning than traditional hardcopy word lists [+W]
  • Newly arrived migrant children taught Italian through games showed larger vocabulary gains than traditionally taught peers on a vocabulary checklist inventory, not a direct test. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement classroom videogame interventions with teacher training, curriculum alignment, extended schedules, and administrative support
