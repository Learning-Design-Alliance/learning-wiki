# Set part of the final exam from the student-question repository so exam results become the prize for good game preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Tell students up front that good questions they author may be used to set part of the final exam, have the instructor review and amend them, build a large repository with answer keys, use it for a class game, and select a balanced subset for the exam MCQ test. "a subset of repository questions was selected by the instructor to set the MCQ test for the ﬁnal exam."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed question-authoring instructions: question stem, one correct answer and three distractors, uniform topic coverage, varied difficulty and question types, and explicit warnings against trivial, plagiarized or beyond-course-level questions
- Instructor selection criteria for exam MCQs: broad difficulty range, homogeneous chapter coverage, and questions taken from all participating students (two MCQs per student, with no points awarded for a student's first two correct answers to ensure fairness)
#### Constraints
- The method requires comprehensive instructor revision of a large number of questions and is therefore suited to small groups where human resources are limited

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in higher education, e.g. 2nd-year engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- motivating systematic exam preparation
- developing cognitive and metacognitive skills such as rigorous use of scientific language

## Claims

- [Students who authored MCQs scored significantly higher on the exam's student-question-based MCQ test than a traditionally taught control group](../claims/mcq-authoring-higher-exam-mcq-score.md) [+W]
- [Authoring MCQs was not detrimental to problem-solving performance: problem-solving and global scores were higher but not significantly different](../claims/mcq-authoring-problem-solving-not-significant.md) [+W]
- [Instructor review of student-authored MCQs retained most of them in a repository whose questions spanned diverse taxonomic levels of knowledge](../claims/student-mcq-repository-quality.md) [+W]
