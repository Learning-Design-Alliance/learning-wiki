# Extend E-CLASS validation via a national dataset and correlate scores with final-project performance in BFY labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' future-work agenda: use the "growing, national data set of student responses from multiple courses and levels across many institutions" to establish the statistical validity and reliability of the instrument for a broad student population, and correlate E-CLASS scores with scores on the final project in BFY labs to investigate the link between epistemology and success on project-based assignments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A national, multi-institution dataset of student responses across courses and levels is needed to establish statistical validity and reliability for a broad student population.
#### Constraints
- The current evidence base is limited to two semesters of four lab courses at a single institution (CU); the final-project correlation has not yet been carried out.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics laboratory students across institutions and course levels

### Target Learning Goals
- validating measurement of students’ epistemologies and expectations about experimental physics
