# Offer locally developed, hands-on IPV/FV education combining practical skills training with local referral information for regional and rural GPs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends locally developed and run education for regional and rural GPs covering the GP's role in facilitating disclosure, validating patient concerns, patient-centred decision making and local referral information, plus practical skills sessions on identification, safety planning, brief interventions and decision assistance. It argues "Practitioner education alone does not significantly increase identification rates, but interventions that also incorporate practical strategies and skills are associated with increases in IPV/FV identification", citing the WEAVE study's training in active listening, motivational interviewing and problem-solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be tailored to the local regional context, including local community IPV/FV referral and service information
#### Constraints
- The authors note these proposed approaches warrant further investigation as to their effectiveness for improving regional and rural primary care IPV/FV recognition, assistance and outcomes

### Target Learners
- Regional and rural general practitioners and GP registrars

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying and responding to IPV/FV disclosures
- Creating safety plans and matching interventions to patient readiness to change
- Knowledge of local referral resources
