# Provide compulsory ongoing education and routine assessment for nurses involved in blood transfusion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that overall knowledge was moderate with deficits across all aspects of transfusion, the authors recommend that hospital authorities provide training courses and continuous medical education, plus routine assessments, to improve nurses' transfusion knowledge and skills. They state that "a compulsory ongoing educational program is required for a better outcome" for all Malaysian nurses involved in the transfusion process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires training courses and continuous medical education provision by the governing authority, with routine knowledge assessments
#### Constraints
- The authors note knowledge status does not reflect actual practice of the transfusion procedure, and further studies are warranted to determine the educational effect on knowledge and practice

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses involved in blood transfusion procedures in Malaysian and overseas hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe blood transfusion practice, including ABO grouping, blood warming, transfusion rate, and complication management
