# Teach the source of free electrons before describing electron motion in conductivity instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific instructional sequencing strategy from the New Model Course tutorials: students first build a model of band diagrams and describe the origin of free electrons using one-dimensional finite square and Coulombic wells, and only after describing the origin of free electrons do they return to describing the motion of free electrons as particles flowing through an atomic lattice. This order was adopted after interviews showed students could not account for the existence of free electrons, and after the original tutorial sequence (motion first) underperformed.
