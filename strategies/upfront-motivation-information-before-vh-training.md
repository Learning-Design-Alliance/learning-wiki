# Increase upfront information explaining the importance of nonverbal communication to motivate skeptical learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose addressing low motivation, which they found linked to poorer MPathic outcomes, by explaining before training that nonverbal expression transmits a large portion of information. They note ironically such skeptical students may benefit most from communication training, and that MPathic-VR could be available on demand to fit learners' schedules. This is an authors' recommendation, not a tested manipulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Upfront explanation of why nonverbal skills matter before learners begin the simulation
#### Constraints
- Motivating skeptical learners remains an open research question the authors assign to future work

### Target Learners
- Medical students skeptical about communication training

### Target Learning Goals
- Engagement and motivation in communication skills training
