# Strength-Based Strategies for Responsible Decision-Making

## Description
Educators identify students' strengths related to responsible decision-making and leverage these strengths to address challenges. Responsible decision-making involves making constructive and ethical choices, analyzing situations, evaluating consequences, and reflecting on outcomes. Strategies include brainstorming a decision-making process, practicing with problem scenarios, defining ethical responsibility using graphic organizers, reading current events, and developing the educator's understanding of responsible decision-making through resources like the Learner Variability Navigator. The focus is on helping students use their inherent abilities to improve their decision-making skills and overall well-being.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires understanding of students' strengths, access to problem scenarios, graphic organizers, current events, and resources like the Learner Variability Navigator. Educators need to facilitate discussions and provide guidance on ethical decision-making.
#### Constraints
- May require time to identify individual student strengths and tailor strategies accordingly. Some students may struggle to reflect on their decisions. Ethical dilemmas need to be carefully chosen to avoid triggering or alienating students.
