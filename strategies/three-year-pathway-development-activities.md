# Sequence pathway development activities across three years: buy-in, SCRIPT assessment and summer institutes led by model-district teachers, then piloting with microcredentials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation lays out development activities by year. Getting Started (SY19-20) includes "Develop innovation mindset; Get buy-in", "Visit South Fayette", book clubs, "SCRIPT assessment", and inventory taking. Getting Going (SY20-21) includes "Summer Institute #1 (SF teachers lead)", pilot lessons, microcredentials, and sketching the pathway. Getting Better (SY21-22) includes "Summer Institute #2 (SF & KY teachers lead)", pilot lessons, microcredentials, and refining the pathway. Professional development was pivoted online during the pandemic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the model district's teachers as PD leaders, and a structured assessment such as SCRIPT for inventorying current CT activity
#### Constraints
- The second summer institute shifts leadership to both SF and KY teachers, and the pandemic forced the summer institute online

### Target Learners
- K-8 teachers in rural districts

### Target Learning Goals
- teaching computational thinking and coding
- demonstrating teaching proficiency via microcredentials
