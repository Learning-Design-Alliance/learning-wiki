Model social imagination of fictional characters' perspectives using connection stems, character-focused questions, and letters to characters¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In Relationally Oriented Reading Instruction, the teacher first models the social imagination of fictional characters' perspectives by using predefined connection stems such as I wonder and this is making me feel. In a second reading, the teacher asks students questions focusing on the exploration of the fictional characters' inner worlds, and children's imaginings are further supported by writing letters to characters. Books are selected for literary quality, particularly language that invites social imagination.
