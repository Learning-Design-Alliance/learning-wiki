# Implement simulation-based team training as a nationwide programme open to all stroke centres, inviting whole logistics-pathway teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "Simulation training should be applied in all stroke centres nationally." The implementable recipe is an open call to every certified centre, requiring participation from all staff along the thrombolysis logistics pathway and strongly encouraging department heads whose decision-making power facilitates workflow change. The Czech experience shows one-fifth of centres participating produced measurable nationwide impact on door-to-needle time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open invitation to all stroke centres
- Multidisciplinary attendance including neurologists, radiologists, emergency physicians, nurses and technicians
- Leadership participation to enable workflow change
#### Constraints
- The observed association is not confirmed as causal; the authors call for other studies to confirm it

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in stroke-centre logistics pathways

### Target Learning Goals
- Faster initiation of intravenous thrombolysis
- Improved team logistics and communication
