Provide modeling media in multiple file formats and physical copy options to support student access¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article reports that offering OMS materials in several compressed file formats for download, plus CDs, DVDs and VHS tapes on request, supported access to the modeling media. In the discussion the author states that "Provisions such as options for downloading, a variety of file formats, and the availability of CDs and DVDs seemed to be an effective method for supporting the technique." No VHS requests occurred, and demand for CDs and DVDs became equal by semester end.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ability to produce and distribute media in multiple formats, including physical copies

Constraints¶

  • Even with compression, some students waited one or two hours for downloads

Target Learners¶

  • Distance education students in Web-based courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reliable access to modeling media supporting cognitive apprenticeship methods
