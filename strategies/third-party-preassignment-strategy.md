# Use third-party video resources as preassignment work for flipped classrooms to lessen faculty burden

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes assigning material from outside sources such as YouTube videos, massive open online courses like Khan Academy, or subscription services like Boards and Beyond as preclass assignments. This lessens the impact on faculty who may be unfamiliar with voiceover technology while still enabling "more active learning in the classroom, including review games." Students in the study supported faculty using "alternative preassignments, such as short reading assignments, YouTube videos, or third-party resources" (5.47 out of 7).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty must curate high-quality third-party resources appropriate to the topic
#### Constraints
- Faculty surveyed thought students spend too much time studying outside third-party resources and an insufficient amount of time studying materials provided in class

### Target Learners
- first-year and second-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- preclass preparation for active learning
- reducing faculty production burden
