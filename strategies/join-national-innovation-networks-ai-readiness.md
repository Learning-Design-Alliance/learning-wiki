# Join national innovation networks and build internal leadership academies to advance responsible district AI adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes how Orcutt Union School District joined Digital Promise's League of Innovative Schools and created an internal AI Leadership Academy of classroom teachers and Teachers on Special Assignment to operationalize Recommendation 1: collaborating with state, local, and district hubs that 'share tools, policies, and evaluation practices.' Network participation gave the district convenings, work groups, and peer-generated resources, while the academy 'evaluate[s] pedagogical "best uses" and ensure[s] responsible AI adoption at the student level.'
