# Integrate PhD and industry guest-speaker sessions to connect students with the global quantum ecosystem

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Planned sessions with 'national and international PhD researchers and industry professionals' give students insight into real-world challenges and how they are addressed. 'These sessions will also expose students to global career paths and collaboration opportunities', delivered through interactive discussions, Q&A sessions and mentorship for students with limited direct exposure to the quantum industry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to national and international PhD researchers and industry professionals willing to give sessions
#### Constraints
- The courses 'intend to integrate' these sessions; they are planned, not yet implemented

### Target Learners
- Latin American students with limited direct exposure to the quantum industry

### Target Learning Goals
- Awareness of real-world quantum challenges, required skills, global career paths, and mentorship connections
