# Implement flipped reading instruction by assigning pre-class videos and reserving class time for collaborative application

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that EFL teachers adopt and adapt the flipped model: teachers "can develop new and customized ways to improve the flipped model effectiveness in their teaching environments and they can modify it based on their students' needs and interests". The procedure is to make instructional videos available in advance, then devote class sessions to discussion, practice, and application of the pre-learned content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-created video lessons prepared in advance of each class
- Class time reallocated to activities where students apply what they watched
#### Constraints
- Students who do not complete the videos may come to class unprepared
- Teachers may need more cost and time to create instructional materials

### Target Learners
- EFL learners in language institutes or academic contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Collaborative learning and learner engagement
