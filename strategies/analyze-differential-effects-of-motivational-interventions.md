# Analyze differential effects of motivational interventions by testing interactions with multiple student characteristics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond estimating average treatment effects, the article recommends examining conditional effects — interactions of an intervention with each of several student characteristics — to determine for whom an intervention works. The authors state their findings "demonstrate the relevance of conducting a detailed analysis of the differential effects of motivational interventions", using the EffecLiteR approach to test each moderator separately across outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires measurement of candidate student characteristics (e.g., gender, prior grades, motivation) and an approach for testing intervention-by-characteristic interactions
#### Constraints
- In this study, conditional-effect analyses revealed moderation only for prior grades on 3 outcomes

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students in motivational intervention studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding for whom motivational interventions are effective
