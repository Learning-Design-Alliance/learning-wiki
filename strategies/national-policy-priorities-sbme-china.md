# National authorities should prioritize standardized faculty certification, structured funding mechanisms for simulation centers, and inter-institutional collaboration networks to promote equitable, scalable SBME

## Description
A policy-level strategy the article recommends for standardizing and expanding perioperative simulation-based medical education: national authorities should act on faculty certification, funding structures, and collaboration networks. The article states: "National authorities should prioritize standardized faculty certification, structured funding mechanisms for simulation centers, and inter-institutional collaboration networks to promote equity and scalability in SBME." It rests on the survey's findings of resource shortages and uneven implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- National-level authority over medical education funding and certification
- Survey evidence base identifying faculty, space, and simulator shortages
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a voluntary, non-probabilistic survey whose results the authors state may not be fully generalizable

### Target Learners
- Perioperative healthcare professionals and trainees across Chinese medical education institutes

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized, equitable access to simulation-based perioperative training
