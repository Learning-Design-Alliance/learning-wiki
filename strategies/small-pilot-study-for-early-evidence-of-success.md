# Conduct a small pilot study to assess early evidence of success before full validation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Phase 3 of the evidence-building process prescribes running a small pilot study as the mechanism for early evidence gathering. The document "describes how to conduct a small pilot study and demonstrate that a solution is associated with improved outcomes for students in a priority community." This sits between refining a solution (Phase 2) and validating effectiveness (Phase 4), providing an intermediate test before larger validation efforts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A solution that has already been designed and refined in Phases 1 and 2
#### Constraints
- A pilot demonstrates association with improved outcomes, not validated effectiveness, which is reserved for Phase 4

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community

### Target Learning Goals
- early evidence of improved student outcomes
