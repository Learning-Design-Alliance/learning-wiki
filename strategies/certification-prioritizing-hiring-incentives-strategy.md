# District hiring and incentive structures that prioritize certification: screening requirements, time limits for uncertified teachers, salary differentials, and designated certification staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes a bundle of district practices that prioritize certification across recruitment, hiring, and employment. Districts use "candidate screening and/or hiring requirements that prioritize certified teachers," limit uncertified hires to those with school experience or who have begun certification, set time limits requiring uncertified teachers to become certified, and assign staff to track certification progress. Two districts use significant salary differentials, and leaders highlighted certification specialists and mentors knowledgeable about certification.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District capacity to track and support certification, such as designated certification specialists or new teacher mentors knowledgeable about certification
- Partnership options with preparation programs so uncertified hires have routes to certification
#### Constraints
- Qualitative findings come from six suburban or urban districts with at least 5,000 students and likely do not reflect smaller, more rural districts

### Target Learners
- Uncertified teachers working toward certification
- Newly hired and prospective teachers in Texas districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Earning state teacher certification
- Retention of new teachers in the profession
