# Pretend Play

## Description
Pretend play, also known as dramatic play or make-believe, involves learners exploring imagined situations and roles. It fosters communication, social awareness, relationship skills, and can integrate math skills through activities like shopping lists, measuring in a play kitchen, or creating a cash register. It allows children to explore and develop their Social Awareness and Relationship Skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom or play area. Props such as shopping lists, plastic measuring cups, or paper money. Integration with math activities.
#### Constraints
- Success depends on the learners' engagement and willingness to participate. May not be effective if the learner does not understand the scenario or lacks interest.

### Target Learners

- Toddlers (1-3), Preschoolers (3-5), Kindergartners (5-6)

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance communication skills, develop social and emotional skills, integrate mathematical concepts, foster imagination and creativity

### Affordances
- Develops social skills by interacting with peers in various simulated scenarios. Supports the development of emotional skills by allowing learners to explore and express their feelings in a safe environment. Strengthens mathematical skills through integration with counting, measuring, and problem-solving activities within play scenarios.

### Personalization
- Adaptable based on learners' interests and cultural backgrounds. Different themes and scenarios can be introduced to cater to various preferences. Tools and props used can be selected based on the learner's developmental level.

- Observe learners' communication skills, interaction with peers, and integration of math concepts during play. Track the level of engagement and creativity displayed during the play scenarios.

## Impact
- Supports development of language, social-emotional, and math skills. Enhances creativity and problem-solving abilities through engagement in imagined scenarios. Builds Social Awareness and Relationship Skills.
