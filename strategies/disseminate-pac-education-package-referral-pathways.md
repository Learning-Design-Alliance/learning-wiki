# Rapidly disseminate an accessible online education package into existing community optometry referral pathways to reduce false positive PAC referrals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend deploying the relatively quick, low-cost online education package into routine community-based optometrist referral pathways, because it improved agreement and specificity at the joint RCO/CoO referral threshold. They state it "could be rapidly deployed into existing CBO referral pathways and routine practice" and "may reduce false positive PAC referrals". The recommendation rests on the vignette study's measured specificity gain, not on real-world referral data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An accessible online format with CPD accreditation as an enrolment incentive
#### Constraints
- Further research is needed to assess real-world PAC referrals after the education intervention; the drop in sensitivity at the referral threshold is a stated trade-off

### Target Learners
- UK community-based optometrists working in primary care

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved specificity in applying the LACD <25% referral threshold for suspected primary angle closure

## Claims

- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, community-based optometrists' interobserver agreement with ophthalmologists on 7-point LACD grading rose from moderate Kw 0.42 to 0.47 after an online education package](../claims/education-package-improves-lacd-agreement.md) [+W]
- [Agreement on the 4-point LACD grading scale (Kw 0.61 pre, 0.64 post) was higher than on the 7-point scale both before and after the education intervention](../claims/four-point-scale-higher-agreement-than-seven-point.md) [+W]
