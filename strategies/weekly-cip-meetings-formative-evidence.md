# Weekly grade-level communities of instructional practice built around formative assessment evidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the MFAS intervention, treatment teachers participated in weekly community of instructional practice (CIP) meetings with members of their grade-level team, facilitated by district lead teachers. The meetings focused on implementation of MFAS-CCSS and differentiation of mathematics instruction based on the results. The authors state the findings support the benefits of teachers engaging in communities of instructional practices to discuss student work and analyze formative assessment data for differentiated instruction.
