## Description
The review proposes four forward-looking directions: deepening existing theoretical accumulation, emphasising 'the interpretation of concepts from different national and cultural backgrounds', exploring influence mechanisms with multiple factors rather than single-factor tests, and developing comprehensive intervention strategies that are experimentally verified for evidence-based evaluation. These guide scholars conducting relevant research and practice.
