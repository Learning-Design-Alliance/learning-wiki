# Use the validated Korean HLC instrument for competency assessment, CPD gap identification, and pre–post training evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that health system administrators and educators embed the validated instrument into professional development. They write that "integrating the validated instrument into continuing professional development programs may help identify competency gaps and facilitate the delivery of tailored HLC training, with impact evaluated through pre–post assessments." They also suggest assessing HLCs before and after training, when new patient-education materials or workflows are introduced, or after communication-related incident reviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the validated 43-item Korean HLC instrument and organizational capacity to administer it to patient-facing professionals
#### Constraints
- The authors note that expected HLCs differ according to professionals' specific roles and responsibilities, so programs should be profession-specific

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in Korea, including nurses, physicians, pharmacists, and dietitians

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying and remediating health literacy competency gaps in patient communication and education
