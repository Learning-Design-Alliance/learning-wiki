# Sequence a project course as a theoretical-analysis phase, an experimental phase, a written report and an oral presentation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In Phy Ex, after project selection, students spend the first phase on theoretical work and reading: "Then, the students devote the ﬁrst 5 time slots (20 hours) to the theoretical analysis of their subject." They formalise the question in scientific terms, read a scientific paper, perform dimensional analysis and plan measurements before presenting their theory and a short written report; only then do they build and run the experiment for the remaining sessions, ending with a second report and an oral presentation before all students and teachers.
