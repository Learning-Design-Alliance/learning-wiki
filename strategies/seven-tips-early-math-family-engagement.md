# Seven practical tips for integrating early math into family engagement programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief distills the experiences of five Heising-Simons Foundation grantees into seven implementable tips for practitioners: "build on successful family engagement approaches and ensure staff buy-in; focus on developmentally appropriate math concepts; tailor early math projects to a caregiver's language and culture; offer practitioners opportunities for professional development; provide families with early math activities they can use in their everyday lives; help families overcome their anxiety about math; and use data for learning and improvement." These tips are offered as guidance for programs integrating early math into family engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs need staff buy-in and existing successful family engagement approaches to build on, per the first tip.
#### Constraints
- The tips emerged from the experiences of five foundation-funded grantees rather than from a controlled evaluation.

### Target Learners
- Young children in family engagement programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Early math concepts and mathematical thinking
