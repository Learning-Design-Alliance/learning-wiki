# Education researchers can refresh their understanding of frontline teaching by spending time in classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article models a simple practice: a researcher whose desk work concerns education spends time in an actual classroom. The author reports that after he "spent some time in a classroom at my daughter's school" he came away with renewed appreciation for the work that crosses his desk, suggesting direct observation reconnects researchers to practitioners' realities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a school or classroom setting for observation
#### Constraints
- Grounded only in the author's single personal visit; the article presents no systematic evidence that such visits improve research practice

### Target Learners
- Education researchers and policy analysts

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding the realities of teachers' and students' daily classroom work
