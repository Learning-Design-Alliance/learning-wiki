# Appoint a designated project lead in each school and use multiple methods to engage parents when delivering parent education interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A delivery strategy drawn from the staff reports in the process evaluation: each participating school should have a designated project lead, and parental engagement should use multiple methods rather than relying only on written communication. The report states that "Staff reported that having a designated project lead in each school and using multiple methods to engage parents (rather than just written communication) were necessary for successful delivery." It also highlights the full-time area programme manager as important to successful development and delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A designated project lead in each school
- Use of multiple methods to engage parents rather than just written communication
#### Constraints
- Schools need to consider the cost implications of these staffing requirements when deciding whether to adopt the intervention

### Target Learners
- Parents and carers of Key Stage 2 pupils (Years 3 to 6) in English primary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving pupils' English and mathematics attainment via the home learning environment
