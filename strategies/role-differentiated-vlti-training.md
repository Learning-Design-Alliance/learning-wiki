# Role-differentiated interferometry training: hands-on workshop for operators, night training for shift coordinators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
VLTI training distinguishes the needs of TIOs and astronomers. TIOs attend a dedicated workshop on the physical principles of stellar interferometry with lectures, hands-on backyard interferometry experiments using punched aperture masks, and problem-solving sessions. Shift coordinators must follow two nights of VLTI operations training as if they were night astronomers, covering hardware requirements and data quality control via transfer functions.

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The paper notes optical interferometry is often not part of the standard training of astronomers, and hopes universities will incorporate it systematically into astronomy courses

### Target Learners
- telescope and instrument operators and PSO shift coordinators

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient VLTI operations and generation of quality interferometric data
