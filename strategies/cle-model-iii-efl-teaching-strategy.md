# Use CLE Model III as an alternative method for solving English teaching and learning problems in EFL contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends CLE Model III as "an alternative method for solving the issues in learning and teaching English", particularly in contexts where students are passive receivers relying on rote memorization and teachers use grammar-translation methods. The strategy involves engaging students in student-centered, activity-based learning with both individual and group work, giving them opportunities to practice all language skills through reading, discussing, and writing tasks connected to meaningful content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need sufficient English communication skills to scaffold student language use and model appropriate language forms
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in Thai EFL contexts and replicated in similar developing-country contexts; generalization to other settings is not established by this study

### Target Learners
- Secondary EFL students in contexts with teacher-centered, grammar-translation instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- English reading and writing proficiency
- Student motivation and satisfaction
- Communicative language ability
