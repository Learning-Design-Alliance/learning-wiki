# Design outreach experiments to be easily repeatable so teachers can incorporate them into their own lessons, and keep teachers central to the activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In this strategy, visiting scientists run workshops in which classroom teachers are active participants rather than spectators, and every experiment is deliberately designed to be cheap and simple enough for the teacher to reproduce later. The authors state: "We tried to ensure teachers were central to the activities, and also designed the experiments to be easily repeatable so that teachers could incorporate them into their own lessons once the workshops were over." This turns a one-off outreach visit into lasting classroom capability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experiments must use cheap, easily obtainable materials
- Teachers must be involved centrally during the workshop
- Full assembly instructions should be made available for download

### Target Learning Goals
- basic science concepts that teachers can continue teaching after the outreach visit
