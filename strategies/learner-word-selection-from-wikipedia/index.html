Let learners select the words and texts they want to learn from Wikipedia¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that teachers give learners choice over vocabulary: "Teachers could and should let the learners select the words they would like to learn", helping them choose lexes relating to their lives and experiences across contexts, learning styles, and interests. This draws on the claim that learners who choose which vocabulary to learn retain words better, and that Wikipedia's variety of authentic texts fosters learner autonomy by letting learners select texts based on their own interest.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must help learners connect chosen words to their lives and experiences through varied contexts and learning styles

Constraints¶

  • Special attention should be paid to the meaning of selected words since it enhances memory retention, cognitive processing, and motivation

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate EFL learners in language institutes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • vocabulary retention
  • learner autonomy
  • motivation in vocabulary learning

Affordances¶

  • Wikipedia Authentic Vocabulary Resource

Claims¶

  • Intermediate EFL learners taught vocabulary through online Wikipedia texts outperformed learners taught with printed Wikipedia handouts on a researcher-made vocabulary posttest [+W]
  • Learners in the Wikipedia classroom reported higher motivation, sense of communal work, collaboration, and pleasure, and less confusion than learners in the traditional classroom [+W]
