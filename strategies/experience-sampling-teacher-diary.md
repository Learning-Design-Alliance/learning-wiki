# Use experience sampling with web-based teacher diaries to trace technology integration and motivation in real classroom contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates using an experience sampling approach with a web-based teacher-diary over six weeks, where teachers weekly document one technology-based lesson and rate their self-efficacy and utility-value. This method does not interfere with daily professional practices and enables tracing trajectories of professional behaviour with high ecological validity. The approach combines quantitative (variance component analysis, growth-curve models) and qualitative (criterion-based sampling) analyses of the same data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers provide one entry per week including lesson documentation and motivation ratings
- A web-based diary platform for data collection
#### Constraints
- Requires teachers to sustain participation on top of regular teaching tasks; the authors note many invited teachers provided too few entries to be included

### Target Learners
- in-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- technology integration
- teacher motivation
