# Extend training for nonexpert echocardiography practitioners with more supervised scans and targeted focus on image acquisition, mild MR, and AR

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend revising the training program because better accuracy was achieved by higher-volume practitioners. They suggest "a longer training course with a minimum of 100 supervised scans before final assessment could lead to improved performance", that "training to improve image acquisition should be a priority", and that major interpretation deficits in "the identification of mild MR and any AR" should receive additional focus. They also recommend incorporating language-based material for health workers with English as a second language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A longer training course with a minimum of 100 supervised scans before final assessment
- Additional focus on image acquisition and on identifying mild MR and any AR
#### Constraints
- The article states further research is needed to evaluate whether these changes improve diagnostic accuracy sufficiently to warrant scale-up

### Target Learners
- nonexpert practitioners (nonspecialist doctors, nurses, health workers)

### Target Learning Goals
- improve diagnostic accuracy of SPLASH echocardiography
- improve image acquisition quality
