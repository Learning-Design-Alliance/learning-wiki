Validate new strategy-use assessment methods against three validity criteria: internal consistency, construct validity, and external validity¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article prescribes that introduction of any new assessment method for strategy use be accompanied by validation on three criteria: "internal consistency or reliability of a measure, construct validity, and external or predictive validity." Convergent validity should be established in a multi-method design against other assessments of the same construct, and a strategy-use instrument should adequately predict reading comprehension, as most theories claim better strategy use leads to better comprehension.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • New methods should be validated at introduction (Veenman 2007); convergent validity only makes sense if both measurements represent the same construct of strategy use

Constraints¶

  • Within-method comparisons only establish stability of measures, not construct validity; external validity cannot be determined when the performance measure is incorporated in the calculated score

Target Learners¶

  • researchers and assessment designers studying learner strategy use

Target Learning Goals¶

  • valid measurement of strategy use in reading

Claims¶

  • Cromley and Azevedo's prompted strategy-use measure correlated .23–.74 with other measures and accounted for about 30% of comprehension variance on average [+W]
  • External validity of the special-issue strategy measures was mixed: low-to-moderate comprehension correlations for Bräten and Strømsø and Magliano et al., indeterminate for Schellings and Winne and Muis [~W]
  • Schellings' questionnaire-thinking-aloud convergent correlation of .51 rests on a small sample and is questionable against other studies [~W]
  • Within-method convergence (RSAT coding vs human coding; G vs d') indicates stability, not construct validity [+W]
