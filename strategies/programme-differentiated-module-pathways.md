# Differentiate module pathways by programme so future clinicians practise critical consumption rather than implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Modules are differentiated by programme destination. "Clinical and pharmacy students take a reduced M1 and a specially written subset of M3 and M6 focused on interrogating and interpreting model output rather than building it", reflecting that "the competency a future clinician needs is critical consumption rather than implementation". Biomedical engineering students take M1, M3 and M6 with emphasis on device adaptation and edge-terminal debugging.
