# Professional development on WCF should be practical, context-sensitive training rather than theoretical lectures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that teachers possess experientially developed but theoretically unsupported awareness, the article recommends that development programs move beyond lectures. It states "Professional development programs must move beyond theoretical lectures and provide teachers with practical, context-sensitive training on implementing various WCF strategies effectively, helping them make informed choices that align their practices with their beliefs and students' long-term needs". The recommendation addresses the systemic failure to give teachers evidence-based WCF frameworks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must address the systemic constraints (time, workload) within which teachers work
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is grounded in the Iranian EFL university context where no formal WCF instruction existed for participants

### Target Learners
- In-service EFL writing teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Aligning WCF practices with beliefs and students' long-term needs; informed choice among WCF strategies

### Affordances
