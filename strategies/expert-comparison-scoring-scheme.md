# Compare assessment responses with expert responses to build a scoring scheme distinguishing expert-like from novice-like reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As the next phase of PLIC development, the authors plan to compare student responses with expert responses to quantify differences between expert-like and novice-like reasoning. This comparison will provide the basis for a scoring scheme in which "answers most similar to expert-like score higher than those more dissimilar". Once complete, a version of the PLIC will be assembled for large-scale, operational use.
