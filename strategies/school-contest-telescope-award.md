# Run a school-group contest with a real telescope as the award

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For school groups the organizers prepared a contest in which each class presented a small project on astronomical observations to be carried out within the school program. The scientific commission examined all proposals and the best one was awarded a real telescope, linking the exhibition visit to hands-on observational activity back at school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scientific commission able to review class proposals and a telescope prize to award
#### Constraints
- The contest was offered only to school groups, not to the general public

### Target Learners
- school groups

### Target Learning Goals
- planning and carrying out astronomical observations within the school program
