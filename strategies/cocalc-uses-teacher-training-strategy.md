# Use CoCalc in science and mathematics teacher training for communication, real-time collaboration, and educational data processing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that cloud services may be purposely used in science and mathematics teachers training as tools for "group communication of listeners (synchronous and asynchronous)", collaboration in real time, and processing, systematization and storage of educational data. It enumerates areas of CoCalc use including supporting individual and group forms of the educational process, visualizing mathematical models and abstractions, providing open access through a common interface, increasing mobility in time and space, and compatibility with other mathematical software such as Octave.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers use the control and monitoring tools offered by the system to supply and control student performance
#### Constraints
- Group-work assessment is limited because there is no option to create or evaluate activities for a group of users as a whole

### Target Learners
- pre-service mathematics and science teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- professional competency formation in mathematics teaching, mathematical modelling and visualization
