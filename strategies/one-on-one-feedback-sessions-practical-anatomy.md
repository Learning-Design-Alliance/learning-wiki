# Extended one-on-one feedback sessions to support post-pandemic cohorts in overcoming practical anatomy challenges

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following the study's results, the department introduced personalized, in-person, in-depth one-on-one feedback sessions between students and teachers, largely extending a component that had existed pre-pandemic. The sessions let students "review their practical exam results directly with teachers and clarify any uncertainties" and demonstrate deeper conceptual understanding not fully captured by summative practical examinations, providing formative assessment and helping identify and bridge knowledge gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching staff available for personalized, in-person, in-depth discussion with individual students
- Direct review of practical examination results with teachers
#### Constraints
- Introduced specifically to support post-pandemic cohorts in overcoming practical anatomy challenges

### Target Learners
- First-year medical students in a gross anatomy course

### Target Learning Goals
- Overcoming practical anatomy challenges
- Clarifying uncertainties about practical examination results
- Deeper conceptual engagement with anatomy
