# Harmonize European HF nurse education, training and career pathways through an updated curriculum, accreditation and certification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends developing "a European HF nurse career pathway that aims to reduce the diversity across countries and standard practice", updating the HF nurse curriculum for professional development, and considering a certification programme for nurses within the ESC community similar to the certification for HF physicians, with accreditation for educational and clinical advancement. The new curriculum is stated to align with the ESC ACNAP Core Curriculum while providing a more comprehensive and higher level requirement of independence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Update of the HF nurse curriculum to reflect the increased clinical and educational activities undertaken by HF nurses
- Accreditation for educational and clinical advancement of HF nurses
#### Constraints
- The survey could not calculate a response rate because the dissemination strategy made the number of eligible nurses who received the invitation undeterminable; answers were self-reported with possible response bias

### Target Learners
- Heart failure nurses across Europe
- Cardiovascular nurses and allied professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge and optimization of guideline-directed medical therapy
- Comprehensive clinical assessment to recognize decompensation
- Patient education to promote self-care
