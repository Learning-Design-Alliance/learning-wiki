# Model assets and threats to availability by blending inter-individual self-reports with process-oriented intraindividual online measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article closes by proposing a research paradigm for handling complexity captured in real-life circumstances. "Assets and threats to one's availability to learn can be modeled across an ecological system that blends observation of typical self-reported inter-individual differences with the process-oriented intraindividual study of SRL afforded by online measures." These online measures include think aloud protocols, learning analytics, field observation, and self-reports, allowing researchers to observe learners typically omitted from laboratory studies.
