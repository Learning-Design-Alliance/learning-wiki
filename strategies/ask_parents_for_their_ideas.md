# Ask Parents for Their Ideas

## Description
Schools can include parents by regularly asking for feedback regarding issues of concern. Teachers can elicit questions from parents about potential discussion topics before Back-to-School Night, making them feel valued and increasing interest. This helps create a culture of common understanding and empowers parents to be directly involved with their child’s specific academic journey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communication channels to solicit feedback, willingness to listen and respond to parent concerns
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to analyze and respond to feedback, may be difficult to address all concerns
