# Use the UNI to screen students and target psychosocial support services

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend universities administer the UNI to identify which psychosocial supports individual students currently need, then direct appropriate services accordingly. "We recommend that universities use the UNI to help screen and identify the psychosocial needs of their students and in turn identify appropriate support which could help reduce their psychological distress, improve their psychosocial well‐being and increase academic performance." Because the stem asks what students currently need, screening should be timed to capture needs at the relevant point in the academic year, and practical needs in particular appear strongest early in semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration of the 30-item UNI with its 'I currently need…' stem and five-point response scale
#### Constraints
- The scale captures current needs, which vary by point in the academic year
- Validation is initial; invariance across year levels and cultures not yet established

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate university students, especially first-year students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing psychological distress and supporting academic performance through targeted psychosocial support

### Affordances
- Psychosocial Needs University Students Framework
