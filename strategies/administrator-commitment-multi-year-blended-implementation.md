# Secure administrator commitment and multi-year investment when implementing a blended learning program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
In its conclusions the article states that "a solid commitment from administrators at all levels in an organization (school district or charter school network) is essential so that all aspects of the program (e.g., use of digital technology, classroom resources, scheduling) are fully supported." It further recommends investing in the program over multiple years, since implementation takes time and experience to perfect, citing work suggesting benefits may then be quite striking. The article also describes structured implementation training modules (launch, data coaching, instructional materials) offered to treatment-school teachers.
