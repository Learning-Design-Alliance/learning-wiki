# Integrate chatbot interactions deeply into the gaming narrative to blend entertainment with learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future VR designs make game progression depend on chatbot dialogue, for example by requiring learners to "engage in dialogues with the chatbot to solve culturally relevant puzzles or obtain essential information". This aims to blend entertainment with learning, enhancing user engagement and achieving more profound educational impacts than the current design where chatbot interaction sits alongside gameplay.
