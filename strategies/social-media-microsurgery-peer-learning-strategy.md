## Description
The article recommends online communities to sustain peer learning while working remotely: "The use of social media, such as the International Microsurgery Club (Tang et al.) can also provide peer learning and communication with experts in the microsurgical community, whilst working remotely". This complements in-department supplementary training sessions run by senior trainees and consultants using microscopes and screens to allow social distancing.
