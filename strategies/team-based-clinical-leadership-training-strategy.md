# Use team-based training approaches so clinical leadership development transfers skills while building teams and reducing resistance to change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends a team training approach to clinical leadership development as a strategy to ensure sustainability of intervention gains. It serves a dual purpose: transfer of skills and teambuilding, since teambuilding is both an integral part of and an outcome of clinical leadership development. Training multiple professionals together reduces resistance to change and to frontline staff taking clinical leadership roles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple professionals trained together within a team-based delivery format
#### Constraints
- Recommended based on one reviewed intervention (MacPhail et al.); the review notes resistance from colleagues who did not take part in programmes was a reported limitation elsewhere

### Target Learners
- frontline healthcare providers including nurses, midwives, medical doctors and allied health professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical leadership competencies and multidisciplinary teamwork
