# Predictability: Environment & Structure

## Description
Creating consistent interfaces and repetitive structures or frameworks in learning environments to facilitate predictability. This helps learners know what to expect, allowing them to focus their attention on learning content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Establishing and maintaining consistent routines, procedures, and interfaces. Regular communication about expectations and schedules.
#### Constraints
- Over-reliance on predictability may reduce adaptability in novel situations. Must be balanced with flexibility to accommodate unexpected changes.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who benefit from structured environments and clear expectations

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Reduce uncertainty, enhance focus, create a sense of safety, improve learner attention

### Affordances
- Reduces cognitive load by providing a stable and predictable environment. Allows learners to focus on content rather than adapting to changing structures.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to various environments by establishing routines and procedures that are consistently followed. Structures can be adjusted based on learner feedback and needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student behavior (e.g., reduced anxiety, increased focus). Gather feedback on the clarity and effectiveness of routines and structures.

## Impact
- Classroom routines and procedures help make classrooms more predictable and, subsequently, help students feel safe. When students can confidently predict classroom structure and teacher behavior, they are free to immerse themselves in their curiosities, freely take risks and safely negotiate their social interactions.
