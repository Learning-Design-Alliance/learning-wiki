# Ensure supervisor quality through preparation, observation, feedback and post-event evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a multi-phase quality-assurance approach for GP supervisors: before the course, goals and criteria are defined and supervisors deployed where qualified or internally trained; during the course, ongoing observation and direct feedback by the responsible professor maintain support quality; afterwards, feedback sessions and an evaluation form allow reflection and continuous adaptation of the GP. Supervisors may be dentists, dental assistants, dental hygienists or higher-semester students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors must be trained for their tasks at the stations; the better their understanding of the learning objective, the better they can supervise
#### Constraints
- Most supervisors reported insufficient preparation time for their specific station in the 2021 survey

### Target Learners
- dental students participating in simulation stations

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining quality of supervised simulation-based learning at each station
