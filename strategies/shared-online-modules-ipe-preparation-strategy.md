# Use shared online EBP modules to prepare heterogeneous student cohorts for interprofessional learning, and expand MeSH and critical appraisal content in future iterations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future iterations of the modules "may contain more content about MeSH and critical appraisal of research articles", based on the observed weak quiz performance on those skills. It also concludes that using the same modules ensured students from different professions and program stages had consistent knowledge, enabling each student to fully engage in the interprofessional evidence-based activity, and that the project was feasible and could be scaled up.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules should be completed by all participants before the shared in-person activity to establish a common baseline
#### Constraints
- The pilot had no control group and a small sample, with possible self-selection bias from volunteers

### Target Learners
- health professions students preparing for interprofessional education activities

### Target Learning Goals
- EBP knowledge and skills
- PubMed searching
- critical appraisal
