# Design creative and challenging motor tasks during sensitive periods to support confidence, memory, and anticipation

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that educators design "creative and challenging motor tasks in the course of sensitive periods" that may develop self-confidence, memory, and anticipation in children. It frames such tasks as a way schools and families can facilitate stimulating physical exercise both in and out of school, given that two weekly physical education classes are insufficient for high motor competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Motor tasks must be creative and challenging and timed to children's sensitive periods, per the article's conclusion.
#### Constraints
- The article notes Serbian schools provide only two 45-minute physical education classes per week, which it calls insufficient for high motor competence and fitness.

### Target Learners
- young school-aged children in elementary school

### Target Learning Goals
- motor competence development
- physical fitness
- academic achievement support
