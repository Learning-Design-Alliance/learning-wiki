## Description
Since creativity scored lowest among the SEC skills (59.4 out of 100), the article recommends specific pedagogical approaches: "Diversity learning [39,40], freedom to learn [39], self-directed learning [18], learning with confidence [39], and learning through group work [32,39] help improve creativity." It adds that innovative pedagogy should support students' creativity and creative problem-solving, and that instructors should consider ways to foster creativity in their pedagogical strategies and study environment.
