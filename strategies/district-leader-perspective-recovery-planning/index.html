Include a district leader's perspective on supporting returning students in recovery planning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The webinar deliberately pairs research and policy with ground-level district practice. Among what panelists share is "the perspective of a district leader and what their district is doing to support students as they return to school this coming fall," grounding recovery recommendations in a specific district's actions (Middletown Public Schools).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A district leader able to describe concrete local supports for returning students

Constraints¶

  • Illustrated by a single district's context (Middletown Public Schools)

Target Learners¶

  • Students returning to classrooms after pandemic disruptions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supporting student transitions back to school
  • District recovery planning

Related Strategies¶

  • Deploy federal recovery funding to support recovery, accelerate learning, and transform schools
  • Use federal COVID-19 aid, including the American Recovery Plan influx, to transform education for students with disabilities
  • Educational leaders should weigh the demographic shift in tested students and other contextual data when planning student growth and recovery support
  • District leaders use a recommendations framework to plan and implement effective summer programming
  • Surface best practices for crisis-era learning support from district, school, and teacher leaders' perspectives
