# Study invisible student minorities and students with disabilities more, using case studies and focus groups when resources are constrained

## Description
The article closes with three points of opinion: invisible student minorities "are numerous, but they are not investigated as much as members of obvious minorities"; students with disabilities should be studied more; and research should advance beyond conceptual learning. Because resources are constrained, the author recommends increasing use of methods like case studies and focus groups, which do not require recruiting large numbers of minority students.
