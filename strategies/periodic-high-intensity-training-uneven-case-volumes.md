# Use periodic high-intensity training sessions to compensate for uneven clinical case exposure in paediatric surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that in paediatric surgery, "where case volumes may be limited and procedural exposure uneven", "periodic high-intensity training sessions could compensate for variability in clinical practice and help maintain high standards of individualized care". It also suggests system-level benefits including more standardized quality of care and reduced operator variability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured training programmes integrated into continuing professional development
#### Constraints
- Robotic simulator access may be constrained by costs and limited paediatric settings

### Target Learners
- paediatric surgeons in low-volume or variable-exposure settings

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of advanced laparoscopic proficiency
- standardization of care quality
