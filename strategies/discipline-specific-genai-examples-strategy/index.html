Share discipline-specific GenAI examples aligned with instructional needs to improve teacher adoption¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that instructional designers support GenAI adoption by providing concrete, discipline-relevant illustrations of GenAI use that match teachers' instructional needs. It states that "Instructional designers could improve adoption by sharing discipline-specific examples aligned with instructional needs." The strategy works by making GenAI's pedagogical value visible within teachers' own subject contexts rather than presenting generic capabilities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructional designers with access to discipline-specific GenAI use cases aligned to teachers' instructional needs

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers considering GenAI adoption

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Thoughtful integration of GenAI into teaching practice

Related Strategies¶

  • Fund AI literacy initiatives integrating AI ethics into curricula and adapting assessments
  • Tailor institutional GenAI training to teachers' prior GenAI use experience
  • Invest in dedicated instructional technology leadership roles and role-specific professional learning for AI literacy
  • Personalized, subject-specific professional support combining teacher characteristics with school-level collaboration and leadership support
  • Embed AI literacy across disciplines by identifying intersections with existing content and using case studies, interdisciplinary modules, and collaborative projects
  • Develop adaptive and discipline-specific GenAI feedback solutions
