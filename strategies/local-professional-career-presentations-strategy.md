# Career mentoring through local professional presentations on educational and vocational pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The My Future component addressed at-risk rural youth's lack of interest in higher education by having local professionals deliver presentations on "the benefits of educational training and the career opportunities in their specific fields." Presenters spanned college-degree fields (education, nursing, agriculture) and vocational trades (welding, appliance repair, paramedics), connecting career exploration to a regional university and vocational school.
