# Journal Reflections for Self-Awareness

## Description
This strategy involves having students think back on their experiences and outcomes and then record them in a journal.  Students can think and write about their experiences throughout a day or class period and look for patterns, as well as create and manage goals. It can provide them with a greater understanding of themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires journals or notebooks and dedicated time for writing and reflection. Can be integrated into any subject area or advisory period.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on students' willingness to engage in self-reflection. Some students may struggle with writing or expressing their thoughts and feelings.

### Personalization
- Journal prompts can be tailored to students' age, interests, and learning goals. Teachers can provide sentence starters or graphic organizers to support students who struggle with writing or self-reflection.
