# Silent Game Incentive

## Description
A silent game incentive uses a gamified approach to encourage students to quiet down after an activity. For instance, students are given a short time frame (e.g., "Silent 20") to return to their seats and be completely quiet. If they succeed within the time limit, they advance on a game board, such as a facsimile of "Game of Life," and earn a reward (e.g., a popcorn party) upon reaching the end. This technique leverages competition and reward to motivate quiet behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Game board or tracking system, clear explanation of rules and rewards, consistent implementation.
#### Constraints
- May not appeal to all students, can create competition or anxiety, requires consistent tracking and reward delivery.
