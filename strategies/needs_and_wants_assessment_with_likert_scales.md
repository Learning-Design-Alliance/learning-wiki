# Needs and Wants Assessment with Likert Scales

## Description
Using Likert scales and fill-in questions to gauge student interest and understanding allows educators to inform their planning based on student needs. This allows students to see that they are being listened to and that the educators care about their opinions, as well as tailoring the educator's lesson plan to things that will be useful for the student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful design of assessment questions. Educators must be willing to adapt instruction based on student feedback.
#### Constraints
- Likert scales may not provide in-depth information about student interests and understanding. Requires careful analysis of student responses to identify patterns and trends.
