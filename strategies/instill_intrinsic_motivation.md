# Instill Intrinsic Motivation

## Description
Helping students understand that positive actions lead to positive feelings about themselves. Involves teaching students a three-step process: thought, action, and feeling. Students learn to change negative thoughts into positive ones, leading to positive actions and feelings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction on the thought-action-feeling process, classroom activities to practice identifying and changing negative thoughts, opportunities for reflection and feedback
#### Constraints
- Requires patience and consistency, some students may need additional support to change negative thought patterns.
