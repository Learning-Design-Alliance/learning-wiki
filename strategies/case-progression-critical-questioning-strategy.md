# Use a progression of clinical cases to bridge prior knowledge and prompt critical questioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a case-sequence strategy in which students first revisit prior learning through several typical clinical cases, then encounter a new case that challenges an established assumption. For example, after four dentin hypersensitivity cases established a shared understanding, a fifth case involving a patient without obvious dental defects prompted students to question whether the condition could be an independent disease entity.
