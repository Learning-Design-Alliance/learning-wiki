Train English language teachers to create and integrate graphic novels in vocabulary teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend raising trainee English teachers' awareness of graphic novels by adding the topic to teaching departments' curricula and improving trainees' ability to write graphic novels through creative writing. They argue that teachers equipped with knowledge about graphic novels "can innovatively integrate them into their classrooms and curricula" to support language learning including vocabulary learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Addition of graphic-novel content to English language teaching department curricula
  • Focus on creative writing to develop trainees' ability to write graphic novels

Constraints¶

  • Teachers' unfamiliarity and lack of knowledge of graphic novels' potentialities are cited as reasons for non-use

Target Learners¶

  • trainee English language teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • ability to design and integrate graphic-novel materials for vocabulary and language teaching

Claims¶

  • Both the graphic-novel and traditional-activity groups improved significantly from pre-test to post-test, with a larger gain in the experimental group [+M]
  • The authors interpret the graphic-novel advantage as arising from presenting idioms through both verbal and visual modalities within a storyline [+W]
  • No significant difference existed between the graphic-novel and control groups on the pre-test [+M]
