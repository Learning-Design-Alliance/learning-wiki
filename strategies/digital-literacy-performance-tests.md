# Apply performance tests to determine digital literacy levels before online course implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes (P4) applying performance tests to determine the level of digital literacy, motivated by findings that self-evaluations of technology skill diverge from actual performance: "there are significant differences between students' self-evaluation and their effective knowledge and performance" (Hargittai, 2010, as cited). The strategy addresses the gap between perceived and actual digital skill before institutions implement online courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions should assess actual digital skills rather than rely on self-report
#### Constraints
- The proposal is a research proposition (P4) rather than a tested intervention in this review

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- digital literacy
- effective use of learning technologies
