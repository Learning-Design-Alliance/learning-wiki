# Decompose evidence-chain explanation into trainable parts with targeted feedback to improve investigation interpretation, the domain the pathway program did not move.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the non-significant difference in the investigation interpretation domain provided a clear direction for iteration, the authors propose that evidence-chain explanation can be further decomposed into trainable parts, such as urinalysis interpretation, image selection and key reading points, indications for cystoscopy, and explanation of findings, based on specific, targeted feedback to generate more consistent measurable differences in a short period of time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specific, targeted feedback on each decomposed part of the evidence-chain explanation
#### Constraints
- Investigation interpretation may be more dependent on longitudinal case exposure and multiple deliberate practices

### Target Learners
- Urology clerkship students

### Target Learning Goals
- Interpretation of investigations in hematuria workup, including urinalysis, imaging, and cystoscopy indications
