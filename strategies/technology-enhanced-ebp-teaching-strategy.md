# Incorporate internet and widely used smartphone applications into future EBP teaching to foster online interactive learning and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future studies take advantage of technology improvements and "incorporate the use of internet and widely used smartphone applications with the aim to foster online interactive learning and engagement". It observes that the use of technology to promote EBP through mobile devices, simulation, and the web is on the rise, and that web-based educational platforms have been demonstrated as an effective and desirable mechanism to deliver educational content to health professionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to internet-enabled devices or smartphone applications for students
- Online material and tools designed for EBP teaching
#### Constraints
- The review notes online EBP teaching is in its infancy, with only 2 of 20 included studies adopting an online approach

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate health students

### Target Learning Goals
- EBP research skills and online interactive learning engagement
