# Teach sign conventions by emphasizing consistency and that signs cannot be chosen arbitrarily for variables

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors suggest that instruction targeting the outer/inner-minus difficulty should build on students' own consistency-seeking: they suggest "effective instruction on this issue would address Max's use of outer and inner minus possibly by emphasizing the use of consistency and helping students develop the idea that one cannot choose signs arbitrarily when working with variables." The aim is to help students see that a variable may take negative values on its own rather than needing a hand-inserted minus sign.

## Design Implications

### Context
