# Deliver a multifaceted education package combining formal training sessions, knowledge assessment, and patient education pamphlets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To overcome identified barriers, the team developed an education package including "several formal 30–40 minute education sessions for the 14 nurses", a knowledge questionnaire assessing acquisition of screening, prevention, and management knowledge, and a patient education pamphlet covering definition, risk factors, common symptoms, screening, prevention, and treatment of PSD. Training occurred twice a month for a total of four 40-minute sessions. For patients unable to use pamphlets, nurses provided videos and health education lectures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurses willing to participate and training attendee lists for verifying participation
- Education materials developed from best practice recommendations, books, and guidelines
#### Constraints
- The article notes some patients were unable to understand the pamphlets or reluctant to learn independently, requiring alternative education means

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses in hospital neurology wards
- Stroke patients and their caregivers

### Target Learning Goals
- Increase knowledge of poststroke depression screening, prevention, and management among nurses, patients, and caregivers
