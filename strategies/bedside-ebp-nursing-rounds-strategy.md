# Conduct bedside evidence-based nursing rounds during Year 4 clinical internships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article describes an 8-hour evidence-based nursing round practicum during Year 4 clinical internships. Before the practicum, coordinators and clinical nursing educators delivered structured preparatory training covering literature retrieval, evidence appraisal, guideline application, and clinical presentation skills. During the practicum, students selected a clinical problem from their internship ward, retrieved and appraised relevant evidence, and presented their findings in a bedside evidence-based nursing round.
