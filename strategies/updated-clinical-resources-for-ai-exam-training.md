# Equip future AI exam-preparation models with consistently updated clinical resources such as UpToDate instead of static textbooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that further training phases incorporate continuously updated clinical resources to overcome the limitation of chronically out-of-date textbooks. They write that "Further training phases may seek to incorporate clinical resources that are consistently updated, such as UpToDate", which would expose the model to a larger, more accurate and more diverse dataset of medical concepts and terms not captured by the Regular version. This addresses the static knowledge base identified as a key limitation of the custom model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a subscription or source of continuously updated clinical reference material
#### Constraints
- Static textbook knowledge may not reflect rapid advancements in medical research and practice

### Target Learners
- medical students preparing for board examinations
- developers of medical education AI tools

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining current medical knowledge in AI study tools
- board examination preparation
