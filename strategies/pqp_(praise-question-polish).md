# PQP (Praise-Question-Polish)

## Description
The PQP (Praise-Question-Polish) technique guides learners to provide specific and constructive feedback on each other's work. In PQP, group members take turns reading their drafts aloud as the other students follow along with copies. The responders then react to the piece by writing specific comments guided by questions on the PQP form, which require specific examples of praiseworthy elements, questions the responders have about the draft, and specific suggestions for improvement. This form gives structure, objectivity, and positivity to peer feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PQP forms for each learner, copies of drafts for all group members, and structured group time
#### Constraints
- May require initial training to ensure learners provide specific and constructive feedback. Learners may need guidance to maintain a positive attitude toward the peer-review process.

### Affordances
- Provides structured feedback, fosters a positive attitude toward critique, and enhances learners' ability to self-edit and listen effectively. Helps learners identify specific areas for improvement and recognize praiseworthy elements.

### Personalization
- The PQP form can be adapted to include specific criteria relevant to the task or subject matter. Sentence stems may be used for learners who need additional support.
