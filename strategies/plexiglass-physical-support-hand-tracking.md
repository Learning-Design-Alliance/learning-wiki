# Provide BVI users a tangible physical reference, such as a transparent plexiglass sheet in front of the screen, to support hand tracking during interactive sonification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
When interactive sonification requires users to move a hand in the air in front of a webcam and screen, give blind and visually impaired users a touchable surface aligned with the screen. The authors suggest "using a physical support (e.g., transparent plexiglass sheet in front of the screen) that the users could touch and use to track the position of their hand with respect to the screen and camera", addressing the spatial-reference difficulty reported by the BVI student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A transparent, touchable sheet or equivalent support mounted in front of the screen and camera
#### Constraints
- Derived from feedback from a single BVI student during the first test phase

### Target Learners
- blind and visually impaired middle-school students

### Target Learning Goals
- locating and exploring sonified areas of images independently
