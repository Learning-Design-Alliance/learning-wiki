# Teacher professional development through customizing Open Source Physics simulation templates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article advocates teachers customizing existing Open Source Physics simulation templates as a form of professional learning, calling it "an innovative method for teacher professional development" achievable "at practically zero dollars". Customization lets teachers align student learning activities with the simulation tool, which the authors argue would not be possible with non-customizable existing simulations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to customizable OSP template models and the EJS toolkit
#### Constraints
- The approach depends on templates that can be customized; the authors note this would not be possible with existing simulations that cannot be customized

### Target Learners
- Physics teachers seeking professional development in computer model design

### Target Learning Goals
- Designing and aligning inquiry-based simulation activities with syllabus objectives
