# Use school credit, apprenticeship-hour recognition and material awards to incentivize student and teacher participation in a reading program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ASIMOV Prize encourages large participation through a set of incentives for both students and teachers. Students who read at least one book obtain school credits and/or recognition of young apprenticeship programs (PCTO) "for a total of 30 hours per book"; awarded students win educational trips to one of the organizing bodies, as well as gadgets or bonuses for buying books. Teachers actively taking part in the national/regional committees acquire recognition of successful completion of the official SOFIA teachers training course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An official framework for recognizing participation as school credits or apprenticeship hours (in Italy, PCTO)
- Organizing bodies able to offer educational trips, gadgets and book bonuses
- An official teacher training course (SOFIA) whose completion can be recognized
#### Constraints
- The incentive structures described are tied to the Italian school system's PCTO and SOFIA schemes

### Target Learners
- Italian high-school students
- High-school teachers on national/regional committees

### Target Learning Goals
- Motivating sustained engagement with reading and reviewing popular science books
