# Integrate BOPPPS, flipped classroom, and case-based learning into a single pedagogical model for residency training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "medical education policymakers consider the strategic scaling of this model across training sites, while also investing in faculty development programs to ensure effective implementation." The strategy combines BOPPPS structure with FC pre-class preparation and CBL authentic cases, creating what the authors call "a whole that is greater than the sum of its parts."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development programs for effective implementation
- Instructors trained in both BOPPPS and FC-CBL methods
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center RCT with 38 participants over 12 weeks

### Target Learners
- General practice residents
- Medical trainees in postgraduate programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical thinking
- Self-directed learning
- Knowledge integration
- Practical skills
