# Clinical scenario comprehensive training with high-fidelity models and case libraries to build integrated root canal treatment competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that Chinese dental specialty teaching adopt "clinical scenario comprehensive training" to train students' decision-making abilities through high-fidelity models and case libraries, narrowing the gap between "item-by-item proficiency" and "overall competence." It draws on Jiangxi University of Traditional Chinese Medicine's adoption of "full-process situational simulation," reported to have effectively improved clinical skills training effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to high-fidelity models and case libraries for scenario-based training, as the recommendation specifies.
#### Constraints
- The article notes most institutions lack the financial support to purchase VR equipment on a large scale, limiting technology-enhanced versions of such training.

### Target Learners
- Dental specialty and undergraduate dental students in China

### Target Learning Goals
- Integrated decision-making across root canal treatment stages
- Managing operational errors and clinical emergencies
