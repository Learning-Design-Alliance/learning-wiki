# Optimize teaching structure and expand case resources when integrating an academic HIS into case simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback from both groups, the authors recommend reallocating classroom time and enriching case materials when embedding an academic HIS in simulation. Both groups reported that "the teaching content was extensive and that insufficient time was allocated to each component of the course" and asked to extend skill practice and discussion time. The authors propose developing the NSTIS educational assistance module into an integrated resource platform with clinical case libraries and skill demonstration videos, with instructional videos prerecorded and uploaded as pre-class tasks so lectures focus on key content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the NSTIS educational assistance module for hosting prerecorded videos and learning materials
- Sufficient course time to redistribute across simulation, discussion, and skill practice
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from one lesson using a single COPD case at a single university; the authors call for further research and validation of long-term effectiveness and scalability

### Target Learners
- Senior undergraduate nursing students in comprehensive training courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Case simulation skills
- HIS operational competence
- Learning engagement and immersion
