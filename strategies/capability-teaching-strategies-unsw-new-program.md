# Bundle early clinical exposure, case-based teaching, mini-CEX assessment, tandem communication-skills teaching and teamwork activities into the curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article attributes the new program's stronger capabilities to a bundle of teaching strategies: "New program students have early clinical exposure with more emphasis on case-based teaching" and use innovative assessment tools such as the mini-CEX; communication skills and motivational interviewing are taught in tandem with clinical skills; and "Teamwork, group projects, peer-feedback and an emphasis on team dynamics are all integral to the learning and assessment process".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communication skills must be taught in tandem with clinical skills, and teamwork, group projects and peer-feedback must be integral to the learning and assessment process.
