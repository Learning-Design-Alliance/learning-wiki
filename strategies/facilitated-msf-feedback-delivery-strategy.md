# Deliver MSF results through facilitated feedback embedded in a supportive organizational framework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that MSF results be delivered to the assessed doctor with facilitation and embedded in a broader education framework. It states that "feedback collected by means of MSF is given to the assessed doctor in a facilitated fashion, either by an appraiser, mentor, facilitator, or coach", citing a review suggesting facilitation quality influences acceptance and behavioral change. It also stresses that success depends on the organization's attitude toward positive change.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A positive and supportive organizational climate, and a facilitator such as an appraiser, mentor or coach to deliver the feedback
#### Constraints
- The article notes MSF is just a tool whose effect on personal development depends on implementation within a larger postgraduate education framework

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents
- practising physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- professionalism
- reflective practice and behavioral change
