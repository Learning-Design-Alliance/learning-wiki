Implement PAIRR through scaffolded steps: AI readings, criteria-based peer review, role-assigned chatbot prompting, and comparative reflection¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article offers a seven-step implementation recipe for professional writing courses: incorporate a PAIRR overview and AI guidelines in syllabi; assign short instructor-chosen readings about AI (ethics, bias, linguistic justice, LLM design, privacy, acknowledging AI use); require a reflection and class discussion on those readings; require peer review of drafts using assignment-specific criteria such as a checklist or rubric; choose a chatbot and guide students in protecting data and privacy; guide students to prompt the chatbot for feedback with a supportive peer reviewer role and assignment-specific criteria; and require students to reflect on and compare peer and AI feedback, consider what advice to take, and develop a revision plan, with reminders that "chatbots can make mistakes."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Assignment-specific criteria (checklist or evaluation rubric) for both peer review and chatbot prompts
  • Adequate credit for peer review (e.g., 10% of course grade) and strong models of peer review
  • Guidance on account creation and data/privacy protection

Constraints¶

  • The article states it is outside its scope to offer thorough guidance on peer review, pointing instead to its materials repository

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate professional and business writing students
  • multilingual international students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI literacy
  • criteria-based feedback evaluation
  • revision planning and rhetorical awareness
