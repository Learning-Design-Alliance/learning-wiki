# Meet consistently with the coach throughout the school year in dedicated one-on-one time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's fourth action is consistent administrator-coach meetings across the year. Coaches need "frequent, dedicated time with their school administrator to share successes and challenges and to seek constructive feedback and support"; in the report's research, coaches reported that one-on-one formal meetings with principals were most valuable for their coaching roles. Recommended steps include pre-year structured time for relationship-building, collaborative goal-setting, and adapting the coaching model to the school context, followed by consistent meetings on bright spots, challenges, and trends.
