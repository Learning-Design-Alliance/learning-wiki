# Multimodal Learning Logs/Blogs

## Description
Learning logs or blogs are used for groups to reflect on their learning, document the content of each session, and share what supported or interfered with their learning. They serve as tools for inquiry and for sharing their learning with others. The logs/blogs include text, visuals, video clips, and links to other resources to describe their learning. Students can embed links within their text to video clips, additional websites, and photos that describe their learning for others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to computers with internet access, blogging platform or learning log software, class time for reflection and documentation.
#### Constraints
- May require technical support for students who are unfamiliar with blogging platforms. Requires careful monitoring to ensure appropriate content and respectful communication.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Provides a platform for students to express their learning in various formats. Supports collaboration and peer learning. Enhances digital literacy skills. Encourages students to reflect on their learning process.

### Personalization
- Students can choose the format and content of their learning logs/blogs. Flexible grouping allows for diverse perspectives. Can be adapted to fit various content areas and learning objectives.
