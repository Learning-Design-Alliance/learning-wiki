# Low SES schools improve teacher-student relationship quality to promote academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because school SES is a distal factor schools cannot easily change, the article recommends acting on the proximal factor of teacher-student relationships: "low SES schools could make efforts in improving teacher-student relationship’s quality to promote students’ academic performance". School administrators should create a supportive climate for positive relationships, teachers should attend to students’ real needs, and parents should be more involved in school activities. The recommendation rests on the finding that the math teacher-student relationship partially mediated the school SES–math achievement link.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires school administrators, teachers and parents to act jointly: administrators create a supportive relationship climate, teachers attend to students’ real needs, and parents participate in school activities.
#### Constraints
- The article states it is unable to change the effect of school SES, which it treats as a distal factor; the mediation evidence is specific to the math teacher-student relationship.

### Target Learners
- middle school students in low SES schools in mainland China

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement in math and Chinese
