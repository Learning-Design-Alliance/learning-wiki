Integrate pronunciation-focused technology such as language learning apps and mobile-assisted pronunciation training into the CLT curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy uses language learning apps, online platforms and mobile-assisted pronunciation training to give learners interactive, immediate feedback on pronunciation within the CLT framework. The review presents it as a practical answer to resource constraints: "The use of language learning apps and online platforms can significantly improve pronunciation by providing learners with interactive and immediate feedback." It also reports that mobile-assisted training showed positive effects on learners' pronunciation performance and attitudes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to technological tools such as apps, online platforms and mobile-assisted training

Constraints¶

  • Resource constraints include insufficient access to technological tools, per the review's challenge analysis

Target Learners¶

  • ESL learners in Bangladesh, secondary level

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English pronunciation accuracy and intelligibility
  • communicative competence in English

Related Strategies¶

  • Platform Agnostic Design
  • Investigate teachers' and learners' 'emic' perspectives on MALL to identify obstacles to integration
