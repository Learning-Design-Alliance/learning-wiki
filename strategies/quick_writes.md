# Quick Writes

## Description
Quick Writes are short, focused informational writing tasks completed in under ten minutes, aimed at helping students process, organize, and remember information and ideas. This strategy is designed to improve content writing skills by providing regular, brief opportunities for students to articulate their understanding of a topic. Quick Writes are intended to encourage students to think critically and express their thoughts concisely, enhancing both comprehension and retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires brief dedicated time for writing, clear and concise prompts, and potential access to writing tools (paper, digital devices).
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for in-depth exploration of complex topics, students may need scaffolding to write effectively within the time constraint.
