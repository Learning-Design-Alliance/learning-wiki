# Evaluate the program against objective exam outcomes and add hands-on dissection workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose two next steps: future work "could compare objective anatomy question grades in faculty medical exams between attendees and non-attendees", testing whether perceived gains translate into measurable learning, and "developing extracurricular dissection workshops" so students can "revisit concepts through hands-on experiences". These steps would extend the current perception survey toward objective outcomes and active learning formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to faculty medical exam anatomy question grades for attendee and non-attendee comparison
- Cadaveric or dissection resources for hands-on workshops
#### Constraints
- Current evidence is limited to self-reported perceptions from one survey

### Target Learners
- medical students in a problem-based learning undergraduate medical curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- objective anatomy knowledge assessment
- hands-on reinforcement of anatomical concepts
