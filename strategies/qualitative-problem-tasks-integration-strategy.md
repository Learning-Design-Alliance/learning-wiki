# Integrate qualitative problem tasks into lectures and seminars alongside quantitative tasks

## Description
The article recommends integrating qualitative (problem) tasks into Physics teaching because, while solving them, "students learn to analyse the phenomena, develop logical thinking, sense and creativity". Qualitative tasks support fixation of knowledge, enable testing of knowledge and practical skills, increase student interest, and reveal misconceptions when students realise they do not understand a phenomenon as well as they thought. In seminars, problem or combined tasks (partly calculation, partly problem tasks) are integrated beside traditional quantitative tasks.
