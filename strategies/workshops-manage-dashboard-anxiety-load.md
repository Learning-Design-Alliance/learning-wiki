# Provide educator-led workshops and support to help students manage dashboard-induced anxiety and cognitive load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For educators implementing learning analytics dashboards, the article recommends attending to diverse student needs, since some students are more sensitive to the anxiety and cognitive load dashboards may induce. It suggests that "educators could conduct workshops on effectively using the dashboard's feedback, strategies for managing test anxiety, and techniques for reducing cognitive load during the writing process." This personalized support is intended to ensure all students benefit in human-AI collaborative environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must implement the dashboard with supplementary training time and be prepared to offer individualized support
#### Constraints
- Not all students respond to the dashboard in the same manner; the strategy addresses the anxiety and load increases observed in the experimental group

### Target Learners
- higher education students using learning analytics dashboards
- educators implementing dashboards

### Target Learning Goals
- managing test anxiety
- reducing cognitive load
- effective use of dashboard feedback
