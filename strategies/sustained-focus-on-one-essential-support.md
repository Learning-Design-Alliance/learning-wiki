# Select one essential support area to improve—chosen for community interest, need, or buy-in—and continue working on it, since improvement in any essential positively impacts student success

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief advises: "The most important thing is to select an area to work on, and continue working to improve it." It states the best focus area is one "that the school community has interest in, need for, and/or buy-in from staff and the broader school community," because these drive the efforts that fuel improvement. It also states improvement in any essential has a positive impact on student success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff and broader school community interest, need, or buy-in to sustain improvement efforts
#### Constraints
- Continuous improvement work is described as truly continuous; the brief warns schools do not completely start over each year but must keep working

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in elementary and high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- School climate and culture improvement supporting attendance and learning
