Concentration tests on originals under deliberate distractions, with and without unusual distraction conditions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 7 studies (5 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), q1–q3 · 1 of 7 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

To test concentration, the article proposes timed work on originals requiring concentration under definite distractions such as the ticking of a metronome, the reading aloud of an interesting book, or a recitation conducted in the same room, repeated over time with originals of about the same difficulty, to see whether the time required diminishes as geometry study proceeds, with parallel non-geometrical exercises and no-distraction counterparts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶
