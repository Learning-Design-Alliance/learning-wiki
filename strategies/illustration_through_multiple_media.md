# Illustration Through Multiple Media

## Description
Making learning come alive with simulations, graphics, activities, and videos. This involves using a variety of media to illustrate concepts and engage learners. Multiple media formats can enhance understanding, retention, and engagement by catering to diverse learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diverse media resources, software for creating and delivering multimedia content, and awareness of learner preferences.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of media to ensure relevance and appropriateness. Some learners may be overwhelmed by too many media options.

### Target Learning Goals

- Enhance understanding, improve retention, increase engagement, cater to diverse learning styles

### Affordances
- Enhances understanding of concepts. Improves retention of information. Increases learner engagement. Caters to diverse learning styles.

### Personalization
- Use simulations, graphics, activities, and videos to illustrate concepts. Allow learners to choose the media formats that best suit their learning styles. Provide opportunities for learners to create their own media to demonstrate understanding.
