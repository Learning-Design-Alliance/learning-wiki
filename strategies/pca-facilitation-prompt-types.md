# Use PCA facilitation messages that prompt reflection on task goals, metacognition, and motivation during collaborative explanation tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes five types of PCA facilitation messages used during collaborative learning, including facilitation "reflecting on the purpose of the task and the significance of achieving the objective" and facilitation promoting metacognition. The PCA intervenes approximately once per minute when conversation pauses, with an animated face and upper body, delivering remarks encouraging reflection on goals, metacognition, and motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A PCA situated between collaborating learners with speech input (via Wizard-of-Oz) and voice output
- Intervention timing at conversation pauses, roughly one message per minute
#### Constraints
- In the reported experiment this facilitation did not yield significant performance gains over control

### Target Learners
- university students in dyads

### Target Learning Goals
- coordination in knowledge integration tasks
- metacognitive reflection
