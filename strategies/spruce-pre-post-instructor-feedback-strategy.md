# Use SPRUCE pre-post feedback to identify and address challenging measurement uncertainty concepts at the classroom level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
SPRUCE is intended "to help instructors and researchers identify and improve instruction around measurement uncertainty concepts and practices that are challenging for introductory physics lab students." The paper presents an example of instructor feedback after pre-post implementation and discusses how it could be interpreted and acted upon. Results are reported as averages and aggregated data to inform instruction at the classroom level, not to evaluate individual students for grades.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration both pre-instruction and post-instruction in the same course
- Interpretation at the classroom level rather than the individual level
#### Constraints
- Detailed discussion of instructor reports and the scoring paradigm underlying them is deferred to upcoming papers

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year undergraduate physics lab students
