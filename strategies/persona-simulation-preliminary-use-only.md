# Use LLM persona simulation only for preliminary experimentation, stress testing, and hypothesis generation, with separate validation for consequential applications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's responsible-use recommendation is to deploy LLM-based student simulation as a complement to, not a replacement for, research involving people with lived experience. It may reduce demands on participants with specific neurodevelopmental profiles and enable reproducible preliminary experiments, but the authors state they "view persona simulation as most appropriate for preliminary experimentation, methodological stress testing, and hypothesis generation". Consequential applications such as teacher training or educational decisions require separate validation and meaningful involvement of relevant communities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validation involving the populations represented before any consequential application
#### Constraints
- A stable simulation may still reproduce stereotypes or distortions encoded in its prompts or model, and consistency may lend those representations unwarranted credibility

### Target Learners
- Pre-service teachers practicing with simulated students; educational researchers prototyping adaptive-tutoring designs

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe preliminary exploration of learner-interaction designs before evaluation with real learners
