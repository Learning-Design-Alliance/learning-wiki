# Authors should provide clear, labeled details about their empirical work to support replication and meta-analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's central recommendation is that CER authors report their studies with clearly labeled details so the work can be reused. "We encourage authors to provide clear, labeled details about their work so that readers can use the study methodologies and results for replications and meta-analyses." Supporting practices the review attributes to prior work include providing checklists for consistent reporting of subject and context data, archiving course information such as syllabus, learning outcomes, infrastructure, teaching approaches, and population demographics, and adopting open science practices such as pre-registering studies and sharing data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors report research objectives, participant descriptions, study design, data collection, and threats to validity with clear labels
#### Constraints
- The review frames this as a community norm to mature over time; it does not claim current papers meet it

### Target Learners
- computing education researchers
- authors publishing in CER venues

- supporting replication, meta-analysis, and theory building in computing education research
