# Mindfulness Practices

## Description
Incorporating mindfulness practices such as yoga, breathing exercises, and meditation into the learning environment to calm the nervous system and promote self-regulation. These practices can be used throughout the school day to help learners manage stress and improve focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A designated space for mindfulness activities. Training for educators in mindfulness practices. Clear guidelines for participation in mindfulness activities.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be resistant to participating in mindfulness activities. Requires careful planning to integrate mindfulness practices into the learning schedule. May require ongoing support and training for educators.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Provides a way for learners to manage stress and improve focus. Can be integrated into various learning activities and settings. Is a low-cost and easily accessible strategy.

### Personalization
- Offer a variety of mindfulness practices to meet the diverse needs and preferences of learners. Allow learners to choose the type and duration of mindfulness activities that work best for them. Provide options for individual or group mindfulness activities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement and participation in mindfulness activities. Collect feedback from learners on the effectiveness of different mindfulness practices. Track changes in learners' behavior, attention, and emotional regulation.

## Impact
- Research indicates that mindfulness practices can reduce stress, improve focus, and promote self-regulation. Studies have shown that mindfulness can improve academic performance and emotional well-being.
