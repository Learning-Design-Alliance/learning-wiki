# Run the dam visit as a multidisciplinary activity in which Physics, History and Geography teachers contextualize the same site around operation, economy and impacts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The visit and follow-up model building were carried out "as a part of a multidisciplinary activity involving several areas of knowledge, as Physics, History, Geography and Portuguese". Back in the classroom, "it was accomplished with a interdisciplinarity among Physics, History and Geography", discussing siltation, transportation, economy, irrigation water use and electricity generation, alongside social, economic and environmental impacts of the dam. The article reports that "the students realized that the economic development is linked to the scientiﬁc development of a country".
