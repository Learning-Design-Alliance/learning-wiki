Instructors apply chatbots to support goal setting and social presence in fully online activities¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors offer instructors practical suggestions for applying chatbots in teaching and learning, derived from the two studies: “We also provided suggestions for instructors to apply chatbots in teaching and learning.” The strategy involves deploying chatbots to scaffold goal setting before online classes (SMART-based questions) and to provide socially present conversational support during online practice tasks such as EFL listening.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors need a chatbot designed around a relevant framework (e.g., SMART goal setting or the social presence framework) integrated into their fully online course activities.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students in fully online courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • goal setting, social presence, and engagement in fully online learning

Affordances¶

  • Social Presence Framework Three Communication Types

Claims¶

  • Chatbot support for goal setting and social presence in fully online activities was evaluated through behavioral engagement from conversation records plus perceptions and interviews [+W]
  • Learners in fully online courses reported positive perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use of both a goal-setting chatbot and a learning buddy chatbot [+W]
