# Adapt the two question-design strategies across disciplines: novel scenarios for logic puzzles, guided reasoning for proofs and critical analyses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In future work, the authors recommend extending the framework beyond computer science: "Strategy 1 is well-suited for novel thought experiments or logic puzzles, while Strategy 2 can also be applied to constructing proofs in STEM or even outlining critical analyses in the humanities." They identify the primary challenge for cross-disciplinary scaling as the creation of effective, domain-specific question sets by educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must create effective, domain-specific question sets for each new discipline
#### Constraints
- The primary challenge for cross-disciplinary scaling lies in creating effective, domain-specific question sets

### Target Learners
- students across STEM and humanities disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- constructing proofs, outlining critical analyses, and solving novel logic puzzles through teaching an LLM
