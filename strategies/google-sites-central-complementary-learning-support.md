# Integrate a Google Sites platform as a central learning support complementing tutorial sessions and portfolio monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using Google Sites as a central learning resource alongside existing face-to-face and portfolio-based methods: "We recommend the use of Google Sites as a central learning support tool, complementing tutorial sessions for direct supervision and the ECG portfolio for individualized progress monitoring." The platform provides regular practice, structured learning, and continuous assessment while tutorial sessions retain direct interaction and the portfolio tracks individual progress. The tool was time-consuming to develop but offered significant pedagogical return on investment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutorial sessions remain essential for in-person learning and direct interaction
- The ECG portfolio is retained for monitoring learners' progress throughout the clinical rotation
#### Constraints
- Development of the platform content was time-consuming
- Conventional approaches remain dependent on learner motivation and tutor availability without the platform

### Target Learners
- Pre-graduate medical students in clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition of ECG interpretation competency
- Adoption of rigorous, methodical working practices
