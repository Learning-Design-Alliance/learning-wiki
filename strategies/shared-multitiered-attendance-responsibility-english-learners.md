# Build shared districtwide and schoolwide responsibility for multilingual learners' attendance through a multitiered approach with a strong foundational tier

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that a collective commitment to English learners' attendance begin with "adopting a multitiered approach, with a strong focus on the foundational tier," which creates positive conditions for learning. Foundational-tier strategies include building trusting relationships with students, partnering with families in their home languages, holding multilingual orientation sessions, conducting home visits, using student surveys, maintaining two-way attendance messaging in home languages, ensuring home-language access to internet and school portals, and connecting students with enrichment opportunities such as after-school programs and summer learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two-way communication with families must be maintained in their home languages, including home-language access to reliable internet service and school portals
#### Constraints
- The brief presents these practices as promising approaches grounded in cited work, not as effects tested within the brief itself

### Target Learners
- English learners and their families across elementary and secondary grades

### Target Learning Goals
- Attendance, engagement, and social–emotional and academic success
