# Mini-Lessons on Features

## Description
Create mini-lessons for each writing feature you want students to emulate (organization, title, sentence variety, imagery, evidence, introduction).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation of focused mini-lessons. Time for instruction and practice in class. A classroom environment that supports focused learning.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming. Some students may prefer a more holistic approach to writing. Requires careful planning to ensure that mini-lessons are relevant and engaging.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for developing specific writing skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop specific writing skills, enhance understanding of writing features, provide focused instruction, improve overall writing quality

### Affordances
- Provides targeted instruction on key writing features. Allows students to focus on one skill at a time. Creates a more manageable and less overwhelming learning experience.

### Personalization
- Mini-lessons can be tailored to student needs and learning goals. Students can be given opportunities to choose which features to focus on. Scaffolding can be provided for struggling learners.
