# Metacognitive Strategies

## Description
Metacognitive strategies teach students to think about their thinking. When students become aware of the learning process, they gain control over their learning. Metacognition extends to self-regulation or managing one's own motivation toward learning. Metacognitive activities can include planning how to approach learning tasks, evaluating progress, and monitoring comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to facilitate activities that promote reflection and self-assessment.

- Requires students to be actively engaged in reflecting on their own thinking.
