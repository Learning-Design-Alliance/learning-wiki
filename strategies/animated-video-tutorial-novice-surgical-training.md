# Use an animated training video demonstrating the guided-surgery workflow to prepare inexperienced operators before hands-on performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article used a manufacturer-produced training video that demonstrated the steps of the static navigation workflow before operators performed the placement autonomously under supervision. The authors report that "the use of such an animated video tutorial, which demonstrates the complex process steps of the static navigation system, seems to be a proven training tool even for people outside the field or inexperienced operators." They recommend providing the instruction as an animated video tutorial to guide surgeons before and during intra-operative use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The video must demonstrate the individual steps of the procedure, as the article's video "showing the clinical application of the guided-implant systems" did, followed by autonomous supervised practice of the demonstrated steps.
#### Constraints
- The authors state further investigation is recommended, especially by manufacturers and providers of static navigation systems; the result comes from a highly controlled in vitro setup investigating only single procedures of a specific guided surgery workflow.

### Target Learners
- inexperienced operators and people outside the field, as studied with dental technicians and non-clinician office workers

### Target Learning Goals
- performing a fully guided implant placement protocol with a static navigation system
