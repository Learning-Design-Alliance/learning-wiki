# Introduce a standardised national undergraduate curriculum of rheumatology competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends a standardised undergraduate curriculum of competencies for rheumatology to reduce the significant variation in delivery between and within countries, and to better reflect the needs of an ageing, multimorbid population. The authors call for "a national undergraduate curriculum of competencies to better reflect the needs of our current population". Prior surveys similarly suggested developing a core European curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination at national level
- Alignment with population health needs, including multimorbidity
#### Constraints
- Curricula are heterogeneous even within a single country, which the review notes complicates multi-centre research

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- rheumatology competencies including inflammatory and mechanical joint disease
