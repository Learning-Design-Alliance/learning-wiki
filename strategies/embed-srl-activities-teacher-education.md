# Embed SRL-promoting activities in post-secondary teacher education lessons

## Description
The article recommends that "Post-secondary instructors who have a role in educating elementary school teacher candidates should include learning activities that promote self -regulated learning skills for elementary teacher candidates in their lessons." It also recommends increasing field experience duration so candidates gain more practice applying SRL strategies, and conducting further qualitative research on SRL skill effectiveness in Life Science Teaching courses.
