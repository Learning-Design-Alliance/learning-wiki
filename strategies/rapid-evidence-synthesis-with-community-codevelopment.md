# Pair rapid evidence synthesis with community co-development to speed usable implementation plans

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes using CAPERS "adjunctively with rapid synthesis, to ensure validity, reduce bias, increase relevance of findings, and speed the process of applying evidence synthesis data to real-world change efforts." In the cited Martinez et al. (2023) example, a rapid evidence synthesis tightly scoped to determinants of progress monitoring served "as the scaffold for subsequent collaborative implementation activities," with practitioners and leaders co-developing implementation blueprints in community mental health clinics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tightly scoped rapid review questions and procedures aligned to partners' priorities and timelines
#### Constraints
- The article notes community-partnered research has been critiqued for pace, motivating the rapid-synthesis pairing

### Target Learners
- Implementation teams and community partners in education and allied settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Produce timely, actionable, co-generated implementation plans
