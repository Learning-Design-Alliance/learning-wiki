# Use RIOT 2 to measure active-learning teacher moves, track teaching changes over time, and support instructor-LA reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend RIOT 2 for researchers of LA programs and for instructors: researchers can "measure the presence (or absence) of active learning teacher moves in classrooms, track changes in teaching over time, or compare faculty." Similar to prior RIOT work, it can support instructors and LAs in checking consistency between their behaviors and their active-learning instructional goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained observers and, for reliability, additional calibration of observers and triangulation with videorecordings as the team plans
#### Constraints
- Data from single sessions are not generalizable to a given curriculum, LA, or instructor

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in LA-supported and multi-instructor classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring and improving active-learning instruction and LA-faculty practice
