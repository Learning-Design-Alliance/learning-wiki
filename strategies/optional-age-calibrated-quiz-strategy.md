# Optional age-calibrated multiple-choice quizzes with explanations at the end of each game level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The game integrates learning checks as optional end-of-level quizzes that pause play without breaking flow. 'The questions are calibrated according to the age of the student and propose three possible answers of which only one is correct'; a correct answer earns extra points added to the total score, and 'After the response, either negative or positive, the game shows an explanation of the correct answer.' Questions relate to biology and protein docking and are drawn from the UK curriculum (A-Level or GCSE).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A question bank calibrated to the students' age and curriculum, offered at natural pause points so the player can keep the dynamic flow of the game
