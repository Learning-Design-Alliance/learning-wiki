# Program for generalization by coaching practices across different times of day and activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because teachers did not consistently use targeted practices in untrained activities, the authors recommend programming for generalization explicitly. They write that "it might be necessary to program for generalization by coaching during different times of the day and in different activities and explicitly coaching teachers on how to use the practices in a variety of activities." They also suggest translating maintenance reminders into periodic check-ins with teachers to ensure continued use of targeted practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaching must extend beyond the primary data-collection activities to other times of day and activity types
#### Constraints
- In this study, generalization activities often differed substantially from target activities due to classroom scheduling, and some checklist indicators could not be evaluated during them

### Target Learners
- preschool and early childhood special education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained use of social-emotional and behavior support practices across classroom routines
