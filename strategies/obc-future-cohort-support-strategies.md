# Support future edtech OBC cohorts with readiness assessments, early planning, needs-assessment framing, and cross-cohort collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report offers role-specific recommendations for future OBC engagement: develop readiness assessments to prepare districts and providers; frame contract development as a needs assessment leveraging existing processes; begin exploring the OBC process well before traditional contract timelines; create an OBC brief template for districts to share experiences; push for pricing transparency and shared pricing norms; and encourage more engagement across cohorts so districts can collaborate together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts need support shifting mindsets and developing new processes and infrastructure for outcomes selection, population targeting, and pricing
#### Constraints
- There will not be a one-size-fits-all approach to OBC

### Target Learners
- District leaders, school teams, edtech providers, and OBC coaches in K-12 districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective edtech procurement, implementation, and outcome measurement to improve learner outcomes
