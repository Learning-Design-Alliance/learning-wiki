# Collaborative workshop as a methodology for scaffolding simulation learning design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article uses a two-stage collaborative workshop as a research and design methodology: workshop 1 with a small group of clinical teachers and learning technologists identifies and selects curricular learning materials, and workshop 2 with a large group of clinical teachers gathers feedback via breakout discussions to redesign the learning design and develop CAF criteria. Researchers act as participant–observers, and the approach is argued to produce reliable and valid feedback through meaningful negotiation between researchers and participants.
