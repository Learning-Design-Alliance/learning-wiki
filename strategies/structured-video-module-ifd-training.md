# Deliver structured video training modules covering epidemiology, diagnostics, and therapeutics over a one-month period with reminder prompts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a video training course comprising "eight structured sessions" covering IFD epidemiology, fungal detection fundamentals, IC and IA diagnosis, other IFDs, and IFD treatment, distributed over one month with weekend email/SMS reminders. This strategy is implementable for continuing medical education on guideline-based clinical topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured curriculum segmented into discrete sessions aligned with guideline recommendations
- Email or SMS reminder system to prompt engagement
#### Constraints
- Physicians received reminders "via email or short message service on weekends," which may not generalise to all scheduling contexts

### Target Learners
- Practising physicians in specialty departments managing invasive fungal disease

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved knowledge of IFD diagnosis, diagnostic stratification, and therapeutic protocols
