# Conduct well-designed long-term randomized controlled trials of PBL in oral radiology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the included interventions were mainly short-term and the review found no long-term evidence on effectiveness, the authors recommend that future research use well-designed long-term randomized controlled trials to establish whether PBL methods are superior to traditional teaching in oral radiology among dental students. They also note subgroup and sensitivity analyses could not be performed due to the small number of studies under similar outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Long-term follow-up designs; larger samples, since small sample sizes decreased the power of the included studies
#### Constraints
- Current evidence base is limited by clinical heterogeneity, small samples, and students being unused to the new methods, which influenced satisfaction levels

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- radiographic interpretation skills
- knowledge scores
- satisfaction levels
