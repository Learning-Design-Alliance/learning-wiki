# Implement World of Words with developer training, ongoing coaching, and structured text-set materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes an implementation package for educators adopting World of Words: a minimum of a half-day of professional development prior to implementation, ongoing coaching (e.g., biweekly), and six sets of topic materials each including "five texts (including predictable, informational, and narrative texts), a scripted teacher's guide, 15 picture cards, and a topic poster." Teachers dedicate approximately 12-15 minutes each day to intervention activities plus planning time, and classrooms may need media-equipped devices for audio and video portions of lessons. As of December 2021, the program was available for $450.00 ($75.00 per text set).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need a minimum of a half-day of professional development prior to implementing World of Words
- Teachers need ongoing coaching (e.g., biweekly)
- Internet access and separate meeting spaces may be required for web-based consultations or on-site training
#### Constraints
- Training and coaching were provided by the developer at no cost as of December 2022; costs and availability may change
- Classrooms may need media-equipped devices for playing audio and video portions of World of Words lessons

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten children in Head Start and state-funded preschools

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary knowledge
- concept knowledge
- science content knowledge
