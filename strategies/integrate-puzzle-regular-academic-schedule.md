# Integrate peer-to-peer Puzzle activities into the regular academic schedule to maximize participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends scheduling Puzzle activities within standard teaching sessions rather than ad hoc supplementary sessions, as integration into the routine framework yielded substantially higher participation (54.17% vs. 26.67–40.68%). The authors state that "the integration of the activity within the regular academic schedule resulted, as expected, in higher participation rates compared to the groups where the activity was conducted during a complementary session". Future implementations should prioritize curricular embedding to enhance engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activity must be scheduled within existing academic session time slots
#### Constraints
- Ad hoc sessions outside regular timeframes significantly reduce voluntary participation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- maximize student participation in voluntary active learning activities
