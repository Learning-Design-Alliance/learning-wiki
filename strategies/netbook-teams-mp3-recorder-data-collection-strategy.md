# Have teams of 3-4 students share laptops (or mp3 recorders) for data taking and analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For running the measurement with relatively large groups, the authors used small laptops (netbooks), allowing teams of 3-4 students to share a computer for data taking and analysis. If computers are unavailable or insufficient, "one can do the sound recording with an mp3 recorder/player—a gadget that students frequently carry with them—and later transfer the audio ﬁles to a computer for analysis". This makes the activity feasible in a gymnasium or open space.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Portable computers if possible; otherwise mp3 recorder/players plus later access to a computer for analysis
#### Constraints
- The experiment is better executed in a gymnasium or in open space, favoring portable equipment

### Target Learners
- high school students working in teams of 3-4

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative data collection and analysis in experimental physics
