# Start from science-fiction enthusiasm and move gradually to correction through find-the-mistake exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To avoid emotional barriers with students passionate about science fiction, the article recommends not correcting quantum inconsistencies immediately. Instead, "it would be better to start from the zest that films and entertainment TV series arouse in young people to establish a common language and activate emotional intelligence." Only once pupils have sufficient knowledge and interest does the teacher return to detecting the science fiction's limits, through funny exercises like "find the mistake!".
