# Music and Emotions

## Description
Learners listen to different types of music and discuss how it makes them feel, fostering emotional awareness and providing a creative outlet for emotional expression. This activity can also highlight connections between music and emotions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a music player. A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be respectful of musical tastes. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance emotional expression, improve emotional awareness, promote positive associations with emotional expression

### Affordances
- Connects emotional expression with music. Promotes a auditory-based approach to learning about emotions.

### Personalization
- Music can be selected to reflect diverse cultural backgrounds and musical styles. Learners can also create their own music.
