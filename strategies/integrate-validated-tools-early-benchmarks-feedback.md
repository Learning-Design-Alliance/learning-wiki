# Integrate validated data-driven assessment tools early in surgical training and maintain use throughout residency with pre-course benchmarks and immediate feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends integrating "validated assessment tools early in surgical training and maintain their use throughout residency, fellowship, and advanced procedure training, supporting lifelong learning and development principles." Pre-course metric benchmarks or training goals can be set based on experience level, and immediate feedback enables trainees and trainers to identify improvement areas. This aligns with deliberate practice principles, optimizing learning curves by targeting specific technical skills for competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Direct feedback mechanisms for trainees and trainers
