# Body Scan Meditation

## Description
Incorporate a body scan meditation to bring calmness and awareness to the classroom, maximizing students’ potential. This practice helps students become more aware of their physical sensations and emotional states, which can lead to improved self-regulation and focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meditation script or recording, quiet space, time for meditation
#### Constraints
- Some students may find it difficult to sit still and focus during meditation. It is important to create a comfortable and supportive environment where students feel safe and relaxed. Meditation may not be suitable for all students, especially those with certain mental health conditions.

### Personalization
- Instructors can choose different meditation scripts and durations to suit the needs of their students. Students can also be encouraged to modify the meditation to fit their own preferences and needs.
