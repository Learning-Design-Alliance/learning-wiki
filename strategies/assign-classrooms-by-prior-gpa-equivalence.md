# Assign students to classrooms by prior GPA to establish equivalent experimental and control groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In this quasi-experiment, since random assignment of individuals was not used, the author assigned whole classrooms based on prior achievement: "Participants were assigned to A and B classrooms relying on their GPA's in the kindergarten so that to ensure relative equivalence between both classrooms." Baseline equivalence was then verified with a two-way ANOVA of kindergarten Arabic grades before the intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior achievement data (kindergarten GPA) must be available for assignment, and baseline equivalence should be checked statistically
#### Constraints
- The article states this yields relative equivalence between classrooms, not random assignment

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in intact classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Valid comparison of teaching methods on achievement outcomes
