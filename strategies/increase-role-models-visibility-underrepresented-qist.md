# Increase role models and the visibility of underrepresented QIST researchers, e.g., as invited speakers and on decision-making committees

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Educators recommended outreach to diverse students in formal and informal settings and raising the visibility of underrepresented researchers. One educator said "it's important to emphasize and underline the contributions of those role models and to show to people that this is a fie ld for everyone", noting many women and people from minority ethnic groups do internationally leading research in the area. He also urged examining recruiting practices and how meetings are organized.
