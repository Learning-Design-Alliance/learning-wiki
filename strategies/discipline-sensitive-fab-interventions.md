# Discipline‐sensitive interventions to foster growth‐oriented beliefs in preservice teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "interventions to foster growth‐oriented beliefs among preservice teachers should be tailored to different disciplines." For mathematics‐intensive subjects, targeted strategies should challenge deterministic views of ability and emphasize effort and learning strategies. In physical education, interventions could focus on instructional practices and effort‐oriented feedback, an approach shown to support student motivation and achievement across disciplines. The conclusion adds that interventions should consider the normative beliefs and professional identities tied to each subject.
