# Hybrid pedagogical framework: AI-generated cases refined through expert review and supplemented with curated imaging data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes using LLMs such as ChatGPT to rapidly produce first-draft clinical cases and MCQs, followed by mandatory expert quality control and supplementation with curated imaging materials. It states the model is "not one of replacement but of collaboration, where the AI accelerates the initial phase of content creation, and the educator provides the necessary quality control and pedagogical expertise." Teachers supplement text with imaging such as fundus images and OCT scans, since ChatGPT cannot generate imaging data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Precise, structured prompts, including a persona pattern ("an ophthalmology teacher well-versed in undergraduate medical education") and a predefined case framework; using the disease name directly as the keyword improved syllabus alignment.
- Expert review and refinement of LLM-generated content before use in teaching or assessment.
#### Constraints
- Raw AI output cannot be used directly for high-stakes assessment or teaching without professional validation due to hallucinations.
- ChatGPT cannot generate imaging data, limiting case realism for imaging-interpretation competencies.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in ophthalmology

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnostic and differential diagnostic reasoning
- Knowledge application and integration across syllabus chapters
