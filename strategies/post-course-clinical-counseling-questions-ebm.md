# Follow the EBM course with clinical laboratory counseling or real multidisciplinary patient-care questions to strengthen application skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To address students' low willingness to apply EBM clinically, the authors recommend that after the EBM course, learners tackle foregrounded questions from actual practice: "clinical laboratory counseling or questions in patient care from the multidisciplinary medical team should be discussed after the EBM course". Students can identify relevant literature and discuss issues face-to-face with physicians and cross-disciplinary teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real clinical questions from patient care or laboratory counseling and to physicians and multidisciplinary teams for discussion
#### Constraints
- The article states interns in this study lacked sufficient clinical experience, which the authors suspect caused low willingness to apply EBM

### Target Learners
- medical technology interns
- health professions trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- applying EBM methodology to solve real clinical problems
