# Segment recorded lectures into shorter videos with embedded interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback criticizing long recordings, the article recommends that "Lectures should be structured into shorter segments (10–15 min) with embedded quizzes or discussion prompts." It also suggests platforms like Edpuzzle to integrate interactive questions directly into video timelines, and dividing videos by slides with a comment feature for discussion.
