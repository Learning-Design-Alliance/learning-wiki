# Raise stakeholder awareness of drama-based instruction's utility and investigate teacher and student attitudes toward it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend championing drama-based instruction among school administrators and stakeholders, since research suggests administrations view drama as "an essentially “frivolous”" activity despite its documented benefits. They call for more short- and long-term research into drama's potentials and challenges, multi-sourced classroom-based investigations, and study of teacher and student attitudes, which may challenge wider implementation in Jordan and beyond.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness efforts directed at school administrators and other key stakeholders
#### Constraints
- Attitudes of teachers and students versed in traditional teaching may constitute a barrier to wider implementation

### Target Learning Goals
