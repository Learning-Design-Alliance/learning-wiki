# Target QS graduate training programs at management and personality development, combining management content with personal image and innovative insight

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the measured gaps, the article recommends educational initiatives emphasizing personality development and management. It states the findings "highlight the need for training programs focused on management and personality development for quantity surveying higher vocational college graduates" and emphasizes interdisciplinary skills through programs combining management with personal image and innovative insight. The authors also stress greater industry-academia collaboration and individualized learning strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs should address the specific gap areas identified: business administration, personal image, and innovative insight
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on self-reported data from a purposive sample of graduates with no more than three years of QS work experience

### Target Learners
- quantity surveying graduates of higher vocational colleges

### Target Learning Goals
- business administration knowledge
- professional image and self-presentation
- innovative insight and creative problem-solving
