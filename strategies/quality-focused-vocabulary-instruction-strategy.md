# Focus on the quality of vocabulary instruction tasks rather than relying on authentic input sources when program constraints limit efficiency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Drawing on Laufer (2009), the article recommends that when problems limit the efficiency of a vocabulary program, "teachers focus on the quality of their selected approach of vocabulary instruction rather than relying on authentic sources to provide new lexical items". This means prioritizing different tasks, word elaboration, and frequent exposures over the source of input itself. The article pairs this with engaging meaning-focused input and tasks with increasing numbers of repeated exposures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select instructional approaches and tasks with word elaboration and frequent exposures
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of Iranian EFL settings where time, facilities, and class size limit listening-based vocabulary instruction

### Target Learners
- EFL learners in foreign-language contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 vocabulary learning and productive vocabulary growth
