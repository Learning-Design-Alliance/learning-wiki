# Teach lexical chunks through a reading-into-speaking cycle combining underlining, sentence production, paragraph writing, discussion, and role play

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's treatment had learners study units of Collocations in Use plus ten chunks per session, underline chunks, and "use them in their own sentences". They then wrote unified paragraphs, presented and discussed them, and role-played provided conversations. The authors credit these varied contexts of chunk use with the fluency gains on the post-test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction delivered over multiple weekly 90-minute sessions (three per week for six weeks in the study)
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from lower and average intermediate Iranian teenage EFL learners at one institute; the article scopes its recommendations to that population

### Target Learners
- lower and average intermediate EFL learners
- teenage learners (ages 12–17 in the study)

### Target Learning Goals
- speaking fluency
- use of lexical chunks, collocations, and fixed expressions in oral production
