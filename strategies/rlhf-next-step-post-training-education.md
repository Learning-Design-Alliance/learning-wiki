# Next-step post-training strategy: reinforcement learning from human feedback on authentic learning dialogues and scaling student feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is to move beyond supervised fine-tuning toward RLHF using authentic learning dialogues, and to incorporate post-trained models into the student journey to quantify student feedback on model performance. The authors state they are "now focused on realizing the full benefit of post-training through reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)," calling it a natural next step given the availability of authentic learning dialogues involving humans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of authentic learning dialogues involving humans as the basis for RLHF
- More sophisticated evaluations capturing longitudinal interactions between students and tutors
#### Constraints
- The article notes current evaluations relied on automated processes and limited human feedback from non-learners rather than actual students

### Target Learners
- Students on the Polygence platform who would interact with post-trained tutor models

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving pedagogical performance of AI tutors beyond what supervised fine-tuning achieves
