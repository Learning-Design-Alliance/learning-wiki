# Incorporate SPS combined with online CBL into pediatric surgical training to enhance preparedness and well-being

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is that "Incorporating SPS combined with online CBL into pediatric surgical training may simultaneously enhance clinical preparedness and psychological well-being among junior surgeons in the short term." They call the blended digital teaching model "a promising and scalable strategy" and note future studies with multi-center designs, larger samples, randomization, and longer follow-up are needed to confirm durability and real-world clinical performance impact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured online case-based discussions alongside simulation of emergency scenarios
#### Constraints
- Short-term effects only as currently evidenced; durability not yet confirmed

- junior pediatric surgeons in residency training

### Target Learning Goals

- psychological well-being
