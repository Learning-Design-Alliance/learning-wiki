Complement experimental scale-up research with design and improvement methods such as RPPs, DBIR, and Networked Improvement Communities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The chapter describes a set of complementary methods focused on the design dimension of scaling, recognizing that scale involves designing an approach to be adaptable and robust across varied environments. "Researcher-Practitioner Partnerships ... emphasize the importance of identifying authentic problems of practice and having researchers and educators work together to address them." Design-based Implementation Research emphasizes layers of design supporting uptake, and Networked Improvement Communities combine improvement science with six core principles including focusing on a problem of practice, attuning to variation, and using measurement to drive improvement.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Variation across network entities' conditions must be treated as information about what is necessary and sufficient for the approach to work

Constraints¶

  • Improvement science emphasizes iterating toward a future state rather than a single definitive impact experiment

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students and the educators implementing inquiry approaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • High-quality implementation and improvement of inquiry approaches at scale
