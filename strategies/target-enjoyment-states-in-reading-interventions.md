# Target situational reading enjoyment in reading interventions as a route to costly reading behaviours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, because dynamic states of enjoyment are linked to willingness to take on costs and to comprehension, educators and researchers "could design targeted intervention strategies focusing on reading enjoyment to promote positive reading behaviours", noting that "Focusing on building states of high enjoyment could be an easier target during intervention than changing people's self-perception of themselves as readers". Future research should establish whether boosting enjoyment improves comprehension and generalises to poor readers.

## Design Implications
