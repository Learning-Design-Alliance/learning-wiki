# Bring physics–environment relations into teaching practice through interdisciplinarity, contextualization of concepts, complex thinking and critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Asked how physics–environment relations can be carried into teaching practice, trainee teachers produced a word cloud from which the article derives four emergent categories: "Enseñanza de la física desde la interdisciplinariedad", "Contextualización de los conceptos físicos", "Pensamiento Complejo" and "Pensamiento Crítico". The strategy recommends integrating environmental examples, experiments, excursions, measurements and laboratories into physics classes so that physical concepts are contextualized in real environmental problems, fostering critical and complex thinking about environmental balance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between knowledge areas, since interdisciplinarity "implica combinar conocimientos y enfoques de distintas disciplinas para abordar problemas complejos"
- Real environmental problems or cases to which physical concepts can be contextualized
#### Constraints
- The article notes interdisciplinarity "presenta desafíos" even though it can generate significant advances in problem resolution

### Target Learners
- pre-service natural science teachers
- secondary and primary science students

### Target Learning Goals
- contextualizing abstract physics concepts in real environmental issues
- developing critical and complex thinking about environmental balance
- making physics learning meaningful and enduring

### Affordances
- Physics Environment Three Category Framework
