# Integrate LLM-driven avatars and multimodal feedback to enable dynamic, context-dependent sales dialogue practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As future work, the authors propose replacing the premade script with large language model-driven avatars so trainees can engage in flexible dialogue. They state: "In the near future, the integration of LLM will be explored to foster the training effectiveness", hypothesizing this will allow participants to respond flexibly to customer inquiries and objections. They further suggest multimodal feedback mechanisms extending beyond visual and auditory feedback to include emotional response tracking.

## Design Implications
