# Apply CDM analyses at other institutions to gather construct validity information on their selection procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that other educational institutes use cognitive diagnostic modeling to understand their selection procedures more thoroughly. The authors write that "these analyses can be applied at other educational institutes as well", to gather validity information beyond predictive and incremental value, enabling critical reflection on individual items and competencies. They report that, on the basis of CDM, the local selection committee grew more critical about the competencies intended per item.
