# Future research recommendations for Facebook-based writing education studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors close with five recommendations for follow-up work: replicate with different experimental groups, extend the intervention across two semesters, examine reading comprehension levels and reading habits as an underlying variable behind the lack of impact on achievement, investigate whether education-faculty students see writing as significant in their lives given their initially low anxiety, and allow experimental-group students to write essays without providing a topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longer intervention duration (the authors suggest planning the study in two semesters)
#### Constraints
- The present intervention lasted only 14 weeks, which the authors cite as a possible reason for the lack of significant self-efficacy and anxiety differences between groups

### Target Learners
- education faculty undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- writing achievement
- writing anxiety
- writing self-efficacy
