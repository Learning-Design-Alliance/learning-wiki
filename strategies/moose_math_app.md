# Moose Math App

## Description
A game app made of funny characters, referred to as the Dust Funnies. These characters, at various stages, will help kids master the various types of math problems. When you come out victorious at one stage, another opens with a different set of problems. The app touches almost every basic mathematical problem in a fun way that quickly engages the learner.
