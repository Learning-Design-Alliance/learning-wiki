# Recruitment strategy: match candidate profiles to available training programs through thorough interviews before selection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program recruits Masters-level students with computer science skills and English, then matches each candidate to a specific lab group's offered training program. The organizers state "It is essential to find a match between the profiles of the candidates and the interest of the groups", and only students matching an available program are selected. Pre-selected candidates undergo thorough interviews with CAIF and sponsor representatives before supervisors make work offers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Available training programs must be identified early: CAIF members inquire about programs at the lab and get a clear description of each offered program before matching
#### Constraints
- Only students matching an available training program are ultimately selected; youngest undergraduates are excluded by requiring Laurea Magistrale or Masters enrollment

### Target Learners
- Masters-level physics and engineering students from Italian and European universities

### Target Learning Goals
- Research training in particle physics, astrophysics, detector and accelerator technology, and engineering
