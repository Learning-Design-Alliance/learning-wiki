# Use a three-phase branching narrative with converging checkpoints to place learners in diverse communicative scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Structure a task-based role-play as sequential phases with distinct communicative goals: preparation and information gathering (dialoguing with NPCs for guidance), exploration and problem-solving (puzzles granting abilities), and strategy and leadership (a tactical battle requiring use of acquired skills). "Within each phase, player responses drive branching interactions" and all paths converge at a checkpoint before advancing, keeping the narrative coherent while allowing learner-driven variation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined NPC characters with descriptions, a set of target phrases, and a chosen hero with abilities to ground the Game Master's narrative generation
#### Constraints
- Final outcomes are determined based on a combination of checkpoint values and the Master's assessment

### Target Learners
- adult second-language learners in immersive spoken practice

### Target Learning Goals
- active spoken language use across diverse communicative scenarios
