# Reduce student commuting time through school-location and transport policies to protect academic performance and mental health

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that policymakers optimize school location and layout to shorten student commutes, given the measured negative associations between commuting time and both mathematics performance and mental health. It states the findings "offer valuable insights for education policymakers in optimizing school location and layout strategies" and highlights "the importance of pursuing 'school district' reforms in urban areas". Future intervention strategies named include optimizing public transportation systems and providing school bus services, to be evaluated for effectiveness.

## Design Implications
