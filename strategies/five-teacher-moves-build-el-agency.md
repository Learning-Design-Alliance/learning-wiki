# Teachers enact agency-building instruction through five moves: valuing strengths, fostering autonomy, encouraging initiative, reinforcing growth mindset, and modeling metacognition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief enumerates five teacher actions for the agency feature of quality instruction: support students to value the strengths and experiences they bring, design instruction that equips students with strategies and language to regulate their learning, encourage initiative through collaborative goal setting, reinforce a growth mindset by highlighting students overcoming challenges through perseverance, and model learning approaches including one's own metacognition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must equip students with both the strategies necessary to regulate their learning and the language they will need to do so.
