# Use home-viewable videos (Home Links) to reinforce classroom language and vocabulary learning and connect families to instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ATC strategy sends home videos children have seen in class so families can rewatch them, reinforcing classroom language concepts and giving parents insight into instruction. The report states Home Links provide "the repeated experiences with language concepts and vocabulary that are necessary to language development," and parents reported the videos let them "see what my daughter is learning each day in school and engage her in more conversation about it." Most children watched daily, with an adult or sibling.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distribution of DVDs or home media to families
- Training or resources for parents on use of home media (a component whose implementation could not be verified in this study)
#### Constraints
- About 40% of parents noted the stories were too long or caused distraction; some teachers recommended shortening videos

### Target Learners
- preschool students
- kindergarten students
- families of treatment students

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary development
- language concepts
- home-to-school connection
