# Future enhancements: proactive daily engagement features, gamified retention, and CEFR-aligned proficiency levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the conclusion the authors recommend evolving LangLingual "from a reactive assistant to a proactive daily learning partner that encourages consistent engagement through regular check-ins and practice prompts". They further propose improving learner retention with streak tracking, daily notifications and gamified elements like badges and achievements, adding pedagogy-informed thresholds for advancing proficiency levels with rewards for consistent performance, and aligning proficiency levels with internationally recognised standards like the CEFR.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Proficiency tracking would need pedagogy-informed thresholds for learners to advance to the next proficiency level
#### Constraints
- These are planned future enhancements, not features evaluated in the current system

### Target Learners
- English language learners using LangLingual across proficiency levels

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and retention
- consistent practice
- internationally comparable proficiency tracking
