# Attach grade incentives to formative assessments within remediation to boost engagement and performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "attaching some form of credit to formative assessments (FAs) can increase student engagement and attendance". Many students do not take remedial measures seriously unless they are associated with some form of credit for their FAs, so the study incorporated grade incentives as a key component of remediation training: each student's rubric-scored remedial activity points were prorated to a scale of 6 and an equivalent grade incentive was added to their final score. The authors report this approach led to improved student participation in all remedial modules and better grades in both FAs and SAs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A rubric scoring method to assess students' levels of achievement and participation in each remedial activity
- Faculty training that includes offering timely rewards and recognizing student efforts

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Increased engagement, attendance, and formative and summative assessment performance
