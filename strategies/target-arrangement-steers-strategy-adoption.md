# Manipulate goal/environment features (target arrangement) to steer learners toward specific strategies or broader exploration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes manipulating goal features as a principled means of guiding learners toward more effective sensorimotor strategies in clinical and coaching contexts. A 'more constrained environment' rules out competing hypotheses and promotes adoption of a specific strategy, while 'more unconstrained contexts' support broader exploration of multiple candidate strategies. The two experiments operationalize this by varying the spatial arrangement of training targets, showing that geometry biases which action–outcome hypotheses learners adopt.
