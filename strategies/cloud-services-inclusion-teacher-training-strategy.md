# Include corporate and public cloud components, open-science cloud services and scientific-educational networks in future teachers' training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future teachers' training include components of the corporate and public clouds of the educational institution — databases and data collections, adaptive content management systems, cloud-based office software applications, specialized software training tools, language processing tools, educational robots — as well as services of publicly available information systems. It further determines it is "expedient to include cloud services of open science", including European research infrastructures and the European Open Science Cloud, in the facilities of adaptive cloud-oriented systems in a pedagogical university.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design of the system to fit the aims of teachers' sustainable education and professional development
- Selection of services for each component as a context-specific task
#### Constraints
- The separation between public and corporate cloud services is conditional; hybrid solutions mean no strict limitation on which services belong to either part

### Target Learners
- pre-service and in-service secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- ICT competence
- professional development through adaptive cloud-based learning
