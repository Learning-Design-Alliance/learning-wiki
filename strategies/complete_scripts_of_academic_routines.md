# Complete Scripts of Academic Routines

## Description
Provide scaffolding, like speech examples, for discourse routines that may not be obvious to learners. Examples: “The topic of my presentation is ______.” “In the first part, I give a few basic definitions. In the next section, I will explain ______. In part three, I am going to show ______.”

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepare scripts for common academic routines.
#### Constraints
- Learners may rely too heavily on the scripts.
