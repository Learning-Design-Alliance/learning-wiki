# Combine MLL and non-MLL early-grade test scores into a common latent scale to obtain an unbiased view of literacy growth

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Districts can use the latent-score linking approach to combine scores across the different assessment systems that multilingual learners and non-MLLs take, avoiding bias from students moving between systems. The article states that "districts could potentially combine the test scores of multilingual learners and non-MLLs in the early years to get a comprehensive view of literacy growth, rather than one that is biased by students moving from one assessment system to another." This supports studying districtwide trends and policy effects on early learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to administrative test records covering multiple assessments with sufficient student overlap across tests
#### Constraints
- Requires sufficient common support across assessments; not all districts have students taking multiple tests at the same time

### Target Learners
- K-2 students, including multilingual learners moving between assessment systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Monitoring early-grade literacy growth and evaluating districtwide program effectiveness
