Use activity based learning in language lessons and train teachers to implement it¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the study's results, the authors recommend that "teachers should use the activity based learning method in the classroom" to enhance students' speaking skills, and that "teachers should be provided training to implement the activity based learning method in language lessons". In the Pakistani context this is described as a new initiative, since most public-sector elementary language teachers use conventional methodologies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training in the activity based method before implementation

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on a single seven-week experiment with 50 male students in one school

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students learning English as a second language

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Speaking skill development

Claims¶

  • Activity based learning produced significantly higher speaking achievement than traditional language teaching in a 6th-grade experiment [+W]
  • High achievers taught through activity based learning significantly outscored high achievers taught traditionally on the speaking post-test [+W]
  • Low achievers taught through activity based learning significantly outscored low achievers taught traditionally on the speaking post-test [+W]
  • Experimental and control groups were equivalent in speaking achievement before the intervention [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement TBL with dedicated teaching teams, systematic teacher training, and diversified assessment
  • Provide professional development programs so teachers learn to implement self-regulated learning strategies in their lessons
  • Support PBL adoption in EFL with teacher training, careful class planning, and advance clarification of the method to students
