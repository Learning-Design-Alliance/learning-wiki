Develop screening measures and targeted interventions specific to the preterm population's learning difficulties¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that differences in the etiology and presentations of learning difficulties in preterm compared with term-born groups suggest "the need for measures that screen for problems specific to the preterm population". As the first meta-analysis to delineate academic subskills and their associations with preterm birth, its subskill-level findings are offered to inform screening measure development and to help teachers and education specialists develop targeted interventions or specialized teaching plans for preterm students experiencing academic problems.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Measures more sensitive to reading and mathematics deficits in preterm children than traditional psychoeducational batteries, which are normed to the general population

Constraints¶

  • The article notes psychoeducational batteries may not be sensitive to the patterns of learning deficits in children born preterm

Target Learners¶

  • school-aged children (5-18 years) born preterm

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading and mathematics achievement across higher- and lower-order subskills

Affordances¶

  • Preterm Academic Subskill Taxonomy

Claims¶

  • Children born preterm score significantly lower than term-born peers on aggregate standardized mathematics measures, with a larger pooled deficit than for reading [+W]
  • Preterm children show deficits in all mathematics subskills, with the higher-order skill of applied problems most associated with preterm birth [+W]
  • Children born preterm score significantly lower than term-born peers on aggregate standardized reading measures [+W]
  • Preterm reading deficits appear at all assessment ages but are less pronounced at 12 to 18 years, whereas mathematics deficits remain similar in magnitude across age groups [+W]
  • Preterm academic deficits persist across birth eras, with a substantial mathematics achievement gap in the most recent era (2001-2018) [+W]
  • Mathematics impairments are more pronounced than reading deficits for extremely preterm children, and deficits appear across all gestational age strata including late preterm [+W]
  • Preterm birth is associated with deficits in both higher-order reading comprehension and lower-order reading subskills of decoding and word identification, but not pseudoword decoding [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Response To Intervention
  • Use broader baseline cognitive predictors beyond conventional phonological measures to detect at-risk learners and personalize literacy intervention
  • Include attention difficulties and social skills in preschool academic screening, and consider prosocial components and feelings about school in early interventions
  • Use kindergarten-age screening information to identify children at potential risk of math difficulties and provide access to early support
