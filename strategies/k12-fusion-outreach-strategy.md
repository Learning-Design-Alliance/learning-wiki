# Run a comprehensive K-12 public engagement and outreach strategy for fusion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The report recommends a coordinated K-12 strategy to build fusion awareness and career interest, arguing that "A comprehensive K-12 strategy can raise the awareness and attractiveness of fusion as an education and career option while enhancing public acceptance". It specifies interactive workshops, camps and science fairs with hands-on experiments, professional visits and guest experts, student competitions and hackathons such as the ITER Robots competition, integrating fusion topics into K-12 textbooks and curricula, and measuring impact through conversion metrics from outreach into internships and enrollments.
