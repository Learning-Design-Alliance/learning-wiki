# Run cycles of experimentation, discussion, argumentation and re-experimentation to cultivate critical thinking

## Description
The article's closing recommendation is that teachers "Encourag[e] cycles of experimentation, discussion, argumentation, and re-experimentation", which it says cultivates critical thinking and decision-making skills, empowering students to analyze outcomes critically and refine their approaches based on evidence and reasoning. In this article's laboratory, that cycle is embodied by students proposing models, measuring with smartphone sensors, discussing the models with the whole group, and reconsidering validity ranges.
