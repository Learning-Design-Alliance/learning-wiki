# Brief video orientation plus one-on-one hands-on cadaver workshop followed by an independent skills test

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A compact training sequence in which learners first view a 15-minute instructional video on fundamental ultrasound technique, then complete a 20-minute one-on-one hands-on workshop on a formalin-embalmed cadaver, practicing needle guidance into the target zone with continuous tip visualization, and finally undergo a five-minute skills assessment with a different instructor. The article shows this sequence let novices "independently maneuver a needle into the target zone under ultrasound-guidance."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching rubric to standardize what is taught and topic ordering across instructors
- Ultrasound equipment with linear transducers and cadaveric specimens with clear target-region anatomy
#### Constraints
- One-on-one training is time consuming and labor-intensive with large groups, limiting participant numbers
- Sterile technique and fluid administration were not taught in this implementation

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students and novice medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- independent ultrasound-guided needle placement into a femoral nerve block target zone
