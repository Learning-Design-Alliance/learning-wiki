# Engage both data professionals and teaching professionals, and involve students, in LA data use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among the chapter's five recommendations for LA practice are that "it is important to engage both data professionals and teaching professionals" when making sense of data for improving teaching and learning, with decisions about learning processes resting with practitioners, and that designers should "Involve students and analyze their role in using the data". This addresses the general lack of participatory approaches engaging students and teachers in LA design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participatory approaches that take into account the needs and preferences of students and teachers in the LA design process
#### Constraints
- The chapter notes inspiration can come from both data and practice, but decisions in learning processes rest with practitioners

### Target Learners
- Students and teachers in online and face-to-face education

### Target Learning Goals
- Translating data awareness into action that improves learning behaviors and outcomes

### Affordances
- Human Centered Learning Analytics Approach
