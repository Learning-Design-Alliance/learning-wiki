# Exclude anatomically difficult patients from novice EVH practice during early training to reduce vessel damage risk

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review argues that patient selection for novice practitioners is an important consideration for avoiding exacerbated vein damage. Thin-legged patients with superficial veins have hair-line branches at high risk of tearing, and diseased or varicose veins predispose to graft failure, so "exclusion of these patients during the early stages of operator training could decrease risk of vessel damage, and consequently improve operator confidence". Pre-operative vein mapping is cited as a means to assess vein quality.
