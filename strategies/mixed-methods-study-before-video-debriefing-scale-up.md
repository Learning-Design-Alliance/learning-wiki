# Before scaling up video-debriefing for HBB, conduct a larger mixed-methods study with qualitative and cost-benefit analyses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' recommendation is that scale-up of video-debriefing for HBB in this context should be preceded by further effectiveness research. They state: "A mixed method study on a bigger population should be embarked upon to assess the effectiveness of adding video-debriefing to standard HBB neonatal resuscitation training on the competence of frontline SBAs. This research should also incorporate qualitative and cost-benefit analyses."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A larger study population, mixed methods, and qualitative and cost-benefit components to justify scale-up.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is specific to justifying video-debriefing within standard HBB training in this low-resource Ugandan context.

### Target Learners
- frontline skilled birth attendants in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of neonatal resuscitation competence
