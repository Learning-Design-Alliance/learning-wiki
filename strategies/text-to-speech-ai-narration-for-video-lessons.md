# Use a free text-to-speech AI model to generate narration and avoid repeated recording restarts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because manual narration recording is repetitive and a single misspoken word forces a restart, the article recommends generating narration with a text-to-speech AI model. The author used MiniMax AI's model, which "generates natural-sounding voices from the user's input text for free," and notes that many other models are available. This makes adding narration to rendered Manim videos practical for individual educators.
