# Provide two days of pre-implementation training plus ongoing technical support for school teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ATI-UP's professional development model has "school-based teams attend two days of training prior to program start and have access to ongoing technical support via email communication." During training, "teams learn about the core components of ATI-UP and build their school plan for tackling attendance challenges," preparing each school to implement its own attendance plan.
