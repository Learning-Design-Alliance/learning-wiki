# Use action research projects in which pre-service and in-service teachers jointly inquire into instructional decision-making

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Action research is defined in the digest as "a tool of curriculum development consisting of continuous feedback that targets specific problems in a particular school setting." The digest describes a University of Hartford project in which experienced teachers identified their knowledge and decision-making processes for language arts instruction, a pre-service course agenda was built from those discussions, and students developed portfolios while conducting their own action research in a critical learning community.
