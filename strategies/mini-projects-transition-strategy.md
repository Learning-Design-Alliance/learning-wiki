# Subdivide a large project into mini-projects that integrate school pupils with undergraduates and staff to support the school-to-FE/HE transition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given limited resources, the team subdivided the project into several mini-projects carried out over a longer period with fewer staff. This allowed integrating school students and undergraduates into the work, trialling activities, and collecting feedback on resources, some developed by the students themselves. The authors state the most important aspect of this approach was the support of students in the transitional period from school to FE/HE, achieved by introducing project based learning to work experience students in a realistic learning environment comparable to universities. Figure 1 shows how members of different educational institutes become inter-dependant within a mini-project, easing transition.
