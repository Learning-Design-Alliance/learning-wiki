# Recommendations for undergraduate particle physics education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The report's undergraduate recommendations urge departments to avoid calculus-entry cutoffs in admissions and orientation, offer engaging modern-physics-first general education courses for non-science majors, and prioritize teaching methods verified as effective, especially discovery learning, project-based coursework and active learning. They recommend departments "ask students to start research projects early" without overemphasizing publishability, encourage multiple simultaneous mentors for minoritized students, and provide funding and recognition for inclusivity and equity work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding for classroom materials and professional development to support verified teaching methods.
#### Constraints
- Undergraduate projects should not be judged by publishability; repeating something instructive or foundational should be acceptable.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors
- non-science majors

### Target Learning Goals
- early research experience
- persistence to a physics Bachelor's degree
- modern physics understanding for scientifically literate citizenship
