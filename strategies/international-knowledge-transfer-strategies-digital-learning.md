# Strategies for international knowledge transfer in digital learning development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the five challenge themes, the authors recommend four strategies to enhance future interprofessional and cross-national knowledge transfer among higher education institutions: apply systematic and continuous needs assessment with timely bidirectional feedback before and during transfer activities; create a conducive environment for both providers and receivers to acquire knowledge beyond their professions; develop pragmatic strategies for cascading new knowledge into receivers' organisations; and acknowledge and address cultural differences while optimising their positives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires early-phase, continuous needs assessment of both knowledge receivers and providers
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single qualitative case study (the ACoRD project) of one intercontinental capacity building project

### Target Learners
- Higher education staff and institutional leaders in provider and receiver universities

### Target Learning Goals
- Capacity building for digital learning development across organisations and countries
