# Increase radiology education emphasis in the medical curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors state that "More emphasis should be placed on radiology education in the medical curriculum." This responds to their finding of a significant deficiency in interpreting radiographic images, with only 33.8% correctly identifying an MRI hemorrhage type and 29% diagnosing an umbilical hernia on CT, and 41.4% admitting complete unfamiliarity with the image.
