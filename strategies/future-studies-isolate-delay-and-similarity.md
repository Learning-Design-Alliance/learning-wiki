# Future studies should isolate the time-delay effect and the question-similarity effect separately

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's own forward-looking recommendations: a future study should subdivide the population into groups taking the learning test at different times relative to the initial collaborative exam, "with times on the scale of a small number of days through the times used in this study", to isolate the time-delay effect; a second future study, alongside or after that one, should isolate the effect of question similarity. The authors also recommend "use of sets of questions that have undergone even more thorough validation and question-pair matching".
