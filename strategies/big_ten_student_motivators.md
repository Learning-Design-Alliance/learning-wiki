# Big Ten Student Motivators

## Description
These strategies encourage playful learning, boost students’ innovative thinking and problem-solving skills, and at the same time engage students in reading, writing, researching, discussing, and analyzing materials in all subjects. They include choice in independent reading, writing topics, and projects, collaboration, meaningful talk, problem solving, inquiry learning, encouraging risk taking, unlocking creativity, developing empathy, teacher-student negotiating, and going out for recess/enjoying time to play outside/five-minute brain breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of learning materials and resources. Collaborative learning environment. Instructor facilitation and support.
#### Constraints
- May require significant planning and preparation. Some learners may need more guidance and structure.
