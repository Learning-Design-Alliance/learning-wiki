# Jigsaw

## Description
In the Jigsaw method, learners are divided into 'expert groups' to master a specific portion of the material and then regroup to teach that material to their peers. This approach enhances collaboration, encourages engagement, and results in better learning as learners take on the role of both teacher and learner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful planning and preparation of materials. Learners need to be divided into groups, and each group needs access to the relevant content.
#### Constraints
- May require more time than traditional instruction. Some learners may feel uncomfortable teaching their peers.

### Affordances
- Increases math talk, strengthens math communication skills and mathematical flexibility. Supports learners' varying speeds of processing by allowing independent review before small group discussions.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to different subjects and content areas. The complexity of the material can be adjusted to suit different learner levels.
