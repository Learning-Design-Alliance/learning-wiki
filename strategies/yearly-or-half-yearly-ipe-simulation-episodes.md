# Organise interprofessional simulation training in recurring yearly or half-yearly episodes to retain learned abilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (3 review, 1 causal), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors argue that because long-term retention of simulation-learned abilities is often not measured and confidence can decline years after training, optimal interprofessional training may be best organised in recurring episodes rather than one-off sessions. They note that ideally IPE training would be done often, but that costs of IPE simulation training are relatively high, making frequency a trade-off.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recurring scheduling (yearly or half-yearly episodes)
- Simulation infrastructure and trained teachers
#### Constraints
- Costs of IPE simulation training are relatively high

### Target Learners
- medical students
- nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- long-term retention of interprofessional teamwork and communication skills
