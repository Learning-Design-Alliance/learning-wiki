# Structure science lessons in four constructivist stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-design strategy that sequences science instruction through engagement, exploration, explanation, and decision-making. In the exploration stage "the teacher divides the learners into heterogeneous groups" to collect data, classify, hypothesize, and reach solutions through social dialogue, with the teacher acting as facilitator. The strategy was implemented in ten 45-minute lessons on the light unit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Heterogeneous grouping during exploration
- Teacher acts as facilitator who raises additional questions
- Sufficient time for learners to propose suggestions and interpretations
#### Constraints
- Application success depends on cooperation among teachers, school principals, and the researcher

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade science students

### Target Learning Goals
- building scientific concepts
- scientific thinking skills
