# Strengthen research competence in PBL-based curricula to meet job requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that PBL-based curricula improve how research competence is taught, since PBL graduates reported a deficit of research competence relative to job requirements. The authors link this to a prior review suggesting PBL-based curricula have a disadvantage in ties between educational theory and research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL curricula must measure the gap between research competence required at work and taught in school using graduate self-assessment
#### Constraints
- Findings are limited to the single German medical school offering a complete PBL curriculum during 1996-2002, which may limit external validity

### Target Learners
- medical students in problem-based learning curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- research competence
