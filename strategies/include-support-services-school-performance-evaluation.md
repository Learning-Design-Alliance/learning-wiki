# Include information on support services when evaluating the school performance of very preterm children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that evaluations of very preterm children's school performance should capture the support services children receive, because age-appropriate teacher ratings can coexist with substantial service use. The Key notes state: "The future studies evaluating school performance of very preterm children should consider including the information about support services." The authors also recommend teachers receive information about the pupil's preterm status to facilitate use of additional resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher or study records of received support services such as special education, grade repetition and assistant services
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is essential to study which support services are effective in each context, since effectiveness is not yet established

### Target Learners
- very preterm children at school age

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate assessment of educational abilities and support needs
