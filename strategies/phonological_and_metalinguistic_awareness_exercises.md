# Phonological and Metalinguistic Awareness Exercises

## Description
Facilitate reading acquisition by asking students to say words that start with the same sound(s) in English or the L1. Ask how words are changed and formed in the home language (singular and plural forms, present tense and past tense forms of verbs) to enhance their word knowledge

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to have some knowledge of the phonology and morphology of students' home languages.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for monolingual teachers to implement effectively without additional training and resources.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance phonological and metalinguistic awareness, facilitate reading acquisition, and improve word knowledge.

### Affordances
- Builds on students' existing knowledge of their home language to support the development of English literacy skills.

### Personalization
- Exercises can be tailored to the specific needs of students with different language backgrounds and reading levels.
