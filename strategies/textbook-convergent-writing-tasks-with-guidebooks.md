# Include convergent writing tasks in textbooks with accompanying teacher guidebooks

## Description
The article recommends that syllabus designers and materials developers produce textbooks highlighting convergent writing tasks, accompanied by teachers' guidebooks to assist application. The authors state that "syllabus designers and material developers can produce textbooks which highlight convergent writing tasks; naturally, such materials must be accompanied by teachers' guidebooks thereby assisting teachers in the their application." This gives teachers flexibility to select tasks according to learners' interests while maintaining an emphasis on creativity.
