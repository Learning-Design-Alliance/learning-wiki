Use intensive training and residency programs for school leaders to share best practices across district-charter schools¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report describes intensive training and residency programs for school leaders as a mechanism that helped facilitate sharing of best practices among collaborating schools. Shared practice areas named in the report include "teaching English-language skills, working with students with disabilities, small-group instruction, teacher coaching, and strategic data use."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Intensive training or residency programming for school leaders
  • Willingness of participating schools to move from sharing practices to implementing them

Constraints¶

  • Some schools involved in the study often had not progressed to implementing these shared practices

Target Learners¶

  • School leaders (principals) in district and charter schools
  • Students in collaborating schools, including English-language learners and students with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sharing and implementation of effective practices in English-language instruction, special education, small-group instruction, teacher coaching, and strategic data use

Related Strategies¶

  • Establish purposeful partnerships among co-located schools with leaders invested in all students in the building
