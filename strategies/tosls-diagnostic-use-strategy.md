# Use the TOSLS as a pre/post or diagnostic assessment to reveal skill gaps and guide instructional planning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend instructors administer the TOSLS at the start of a course as an in-class pretest (paper-based or Web-based) and again as a posttest, or use it diagnostically to reveal the extent of students' literacy development. They state: "Instructors may use the TOSLS as a diagnostic tool in the beginning of the semester to reveal the extent of students' literacy development." Class results may guide instructional planning, and instructors could tailor study suggestions for individual students' skill development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The assessment should be administered in class to motivate students to take the test seriously and minimize variability in completion time
#### Constraints
- The instrument measures a single construct, so it indicates overall scientific literacy progress rather than separate skill profiles

### Target Learners
- Undergraduates in large-enrollment general education biology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Scientific literacy skills, including evaluating arguments, sources, and quantitative data
