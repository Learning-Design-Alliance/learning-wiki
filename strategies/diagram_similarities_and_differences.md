# Diagram Similarities and Differences

## Description
Learners generate a list of similarities and differences between words and complete a Venn diagram. This activity is an example of a high-yield instructional strategy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Provide Venn diagrams or other graphic organizers.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners with limited background knowledge.
