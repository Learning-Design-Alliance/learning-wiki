# Assignment design strategy using ULOs, qualification level and Bloom's Taxonomy action verbs, with expert examination and refinement rather than regeneration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A strategy for creating assessments with GenAI, shown in Figure 3. The fine-tuned model is prompted with assignment-specific ULOs, qualification level and assignment type, and "Action verbs from Bloom's Taxonomy [35] can be used to ensure the qualification level and learning outcomes." An expert examines the assignment; if requirements are unmet, refine prompts update the existing assignment rather than generating a new one.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- ULO(s) specific to the assignment, qualification level, Bloom's Taxonomy, type/sample of assignment as prompt inputs
- Expert examination of accuracy, learning outcomes and qualification level
#### Constraints
- Refinement loops continue "until the expert is satisfied that the assignment meets the requirements about learning outcomes and qualification level"

### Target Learners
- Higher education students at the specified qualification level

### Target Learning Goals
- Authentic assessments aligned with learning outcomes and qualification level
