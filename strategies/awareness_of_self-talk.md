# Awareness of Self-Talk

## Description
Creating awareness of self-talk involves understanding what self-talk is, how it impacts confidence and self-esteem, and identifying it in real-life situations. This includes recognizing the judgments individuals have about their abilities in various areas and discussing how confidence and past experiences impact their inner voice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for explicit instruction and discussion. Materials for creating KWL charts or self-talk logs (notebooks, journals, printables). A supportive classroom environment where students feel safe sharing their thoughts and feelings.
#### Constraints
- Students may initially find it difficult to identify their self-talk. Requires a trusting classroom environment for students to share honestly. The activity's success depends on students' willingness to engage in self-reflection.

### Target Learners

- K-12 students, especially those struggling with self-esteem and confidence.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- To help students understand the concept of self-talk and its impact on their confidence and self-esteem.

### Affordances
- Enhances self-awareness by prompting learners to identify and analyze their internal dialogue. Supports self-reflection by encouraging learners to examine their judgments about their abilities. Promotes a learner mindset by showing them the power of their thoughts.

### Personalization
- Adaptable to different age groups and learning styles. Can be integrated into various subjects, not just social-emotional learning. Educators can use various reflection prompts to tailor the activity to individual needs.
