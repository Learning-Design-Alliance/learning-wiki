# Graphical DM dashboard of FCI pre-test results for rapid formative feedback in large cohorts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
For large physics cohorts, deploy the FCI pre-test via a learning management system, export the item-level data, and plot % correct against %nDM with thresholds as a one-page dashboard. The article states this representation 'is well suited to large cohorts where various LMSs could be used to collect data that is then exported as a 'dashboard' info-graphic.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The FCI must be administered as a pre-test and deployed through a learning management system so item-level responses can be exported and processed into the infographic.
#### Constraints
- No paired pre/post data are needed, but the method addresses the pre-test snapshot rather than gains; the authors note there are currently no FCI versions in African languages, which may affect completion time and overall scores for multilingual cohorts.

### Target Learners
- first-year university physics students in large, linguistically diverse cohorts

### Target Learning Goals
- rapid formative assessment of conceptual understanding
- evidence-based adaptation of teaching to cohort misconceptions
