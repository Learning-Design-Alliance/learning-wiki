# Assign students the eraser variants and the delayed-choice analysis as problems and discussion topics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article lists variants of the eraser experiment for instructors to use as further discussion or student problems, including changing the analyzer-loop orientation to 45 degrees, placing the de-exciter before the final vertical analyzer, having students complete the delayed-choice analysis themselves, and having students consider whether the superpositioner could be placed after the vertical analyzer but before the de-exciter. "There are a number of variants one can include for further discussion or as problems for the students."
