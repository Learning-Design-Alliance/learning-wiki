# Incorporate virtual patient cases into continuous professional development of nurses at primary health centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health systems use web-based VP cases as CPD for in-service nurses who work independently in consultation rooms with limited colleague interaction. VP cases expose nurses to "real-life-like cases in a learning environment where making an error cannot harm a patient" with expert feedback, and online delivery lets nurses train "without leaving their workplace" and at any time. This is especially pertinent where nurses perform physician-like diagnostic processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases adapted to local context and validated by local experts; tablets and internet access provided; asynchronous scheduling compatible with work duties
#### Constraints
- Attrition from work responsibilities was substantial (76 enrolled, 56 completed); English proficiency was required, excluding French-trained nurses

### Target Learners
- in-service nurses at primary health care centers
- newly graduated nurses assigned to consultation rooms

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning for timely diagnosis, management, and appropriate referral of non-communicable diseases
