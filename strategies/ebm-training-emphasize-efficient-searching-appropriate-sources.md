# EBM training should emphasize efficient searching by focusing on appropriate clinical information sources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors observed that students reported fast searches but low satisfaction with results, possibly because they searched primary databases like PubMed or single journals, leading to overly broad or narrow yields. They suggest future EBM training should emphasize searching efficiently by focusing on appropriate clinical information sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programs should incorporate guidance on selecting appropriate clinical information sources
#### Constraints
- Based on authors' interpretation of search satisfaction data rather than a directly tested intervention

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient literature searching in EBM
