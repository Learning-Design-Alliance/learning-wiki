# Adopt a guided hybrid homework approach when seminar time is limited for cartooning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that when seminar time is insufficient for cartooning, instructors provide students with clear examples and prompts, optionally allow brief group discussions, and assign a structured out-of-class cartooning task to complete before the next session. They state that "some level of guided support, particularly during students' initial exposure to cartooning as a learning consolidation method, is essential to maximize engagement and optimize learning outcomes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear examples and prompts must be provided
- Structured out-of-class task assigned before the next session
#### Constraints
- Guided support is described as particularly essential during students' initial exposure to cartooning

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students new to cartooning as a learning method

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidation of complex topic knowledge through self-generated cartoons
