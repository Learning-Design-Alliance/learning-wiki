Use a green/red card cue system with group-dependent rewards to reinforce classroom behavior¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The PFS classroom component uses a green card displayed for positive behavior and a red card for negative behavior, paired with group-dependent contingencies. "When a reward criterion is met in the classroom, as determined by the teacher and coach, the participating child earns a brief activity reward (e.g., classroom game, extra recess) for peers." The authors note this cue-and-reward system appears unique among two-tier programs.
