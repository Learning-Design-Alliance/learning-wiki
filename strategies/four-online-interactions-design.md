# Design and facilitate four types of interaction: with resources, instructor, peers, and self

## Description
The review's third guideline enumerates the interactions online teachers should design: "students' interaction with resources, interaction with the instructor, interaction with the peer, and interaction with self." Interaction with resources is addressed through well-organized instructional materials; instructor and peer interactions create mutual cognitive and social presence; interaction with self supports metacognitive goal-setting and progress monitoring.
