# Use Positive Affirmations

## Description
Replace negative messages from the inner voice with positive affirmations. A negative message can be replaced with a positive affirmation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- List of positive affirmations, time for daily reflection and repetition.
#### Constraints
- May feel artificial or ineffective if not genuinely believed. Requires consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Affordances
- Boosts self-esteem, promotes a positive outlook, reduces stress and anxiety.

### Personalization
- Learners can select or create affirmations that resonate with their personal values and goals.
