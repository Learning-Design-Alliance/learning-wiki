# Design online health-professions curricula with efficient learning strategies that foster self-directed learning while valuing active learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "dental educators are encouraged to carefully design online curricula with efficient learning strategies that help students improve learning performance and foster self-directed learning skills while valuing active learning in an online environment." The strategy pairs pre-recorded self-directed content with interactive virtual sessions, since students preferred the flipped format even though learning gains were equivalent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful curriculum design; recorded pre-class material plus virtual discussion sessions; alignment of assessments with lecture learning objectives.
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a small single-institution pilot with only male students; the authors call for larger multi-center studies before generalizing.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- health professions students in online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- learning performance
- self-directed learning skills
- active engagement online
