# Revise inter-professional course curricula toward well-defined, mainly practical learning outcomes and a competency model shared across professions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the pilot findings, the authors recommend revising the target competencies of inter-professional dementia care training so they reflect students' learning needs and comply with recommended competency frameworks. They state that "a model of target competencies for dementia care should be developed, which details the competencies equally relevant to future physicians and nurses", to achieve better transparency of learning objectives and a stronger focus on competencies required in clinical practice, such as treatment of neuropsychiatric symptoms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A model of target competencies detailing competencies equally relevant to future physicians and nurses
- Systematic inquiries of process and outcome quality in subsequent course deliveries
#### Constraints
- The pilot data reflect only participants' subjective perceptions with a small sample, so the recommendation rests on descriptive findings

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based dementia care competencies
- Clarity of practical learning outcomes
