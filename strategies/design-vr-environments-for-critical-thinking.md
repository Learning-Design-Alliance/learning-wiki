# Meticulously design virtual learning environments to foster critical thinking while managing cognitive load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because VR showed no significant critical-thinking advantage, the authors recommend that future VR learning environments be deliberately designed to develop critical thinking. They note that "the utilization of virtual learning environments encounters challenges such as heightened cognitive load and information disorientation", so careful environment design is paramount. They also call for refining technical effectiveness and improving transfer of practical skills from virtual to clinical settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meticulous design of virtual learning environments to maximize critical thinking development
- Managing heightened cognitive load and information disorientation
#### Constraints
- Current evidence shows no significant critical-thinking benefit from VR as implemented in the included trials

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- college students in virtual learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- transfer of practical skills to clinical settings
