# Cascade compact training across the midwifery service and integrate it into pre-registration curricula and mandatory CPD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding the compact training model at scale: cascading it across the midwifery service, integrating the topics into pre-registration midwifery programmes and post-registration continuing professional development, and including it in annual mandatory training. They argue the compact format is feasible "with minimal disruption to, and loss of midwifing working hours" and that use of local NHS venues and expertise keeps delivery cost efficient. They also advocate engaging education providers to review curriculum needs given identified gaps in obesity and nutrition education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Registered dietitians to support midwives in providing nutrition education to ensure advice stays current
#### Constraints
- Workforce pressures and mandatory training commitments limit scope to release midwives for longer training

### Target Learners
- practising midwives
- pre-registration midwifery students

### Target Learning Goals
- giving nutrition, physical activity and weight management advice during pregnancy
- raising the issue of weight sensitively
