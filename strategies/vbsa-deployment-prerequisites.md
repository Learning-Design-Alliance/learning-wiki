# Prerequisites for viable classroom deployment of a voice-based training assistant: rapid development, verification and validation, and sustainable deployment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors state that implementing a voice-based synthetic assistant in real training is viable only if three conditions are met: "(1) rapid development for real-time application, (2) verification and validation of VBSA, and (3) sustainability of deployment." They also identify the scripted vocabulary limitation as needing further investigation, since scripted language lengthened training time, and call for rigorous development, more testing, and large-sample studies before deployment. This strategy guides institutions considering assistant-augmented skills training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rapid development for real-time application
- Verification and validation of the assistant
- Sustainability of deployment
- Resolution of scripted-vocabulary limitations through further investigation
#### Constraints
- In its current form deployment is not recommended due to limitations such as lack of large-sample studies on effects on trainees

### Target Learners
- emergency care provider trainees
- novice learners in procedural skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural accuracy and speed in emergency treatment tasks
