Pair JAS with student-centered models and strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study, q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends implementing JAS through student-centered learning models and strategies that make the approach seem more real, naming "a model of cooperative learning, think pair share, strategy role-playing, the investigation group, learning cycle 5e, hands-on activity". Reviewed studies combined JAS with role playing, Team Game Tournament, Group Investigation, Problem Based Learning, and Quantum Teaching, reporting increased activity and study results.

Requirements¶

  • The environment (physical, social, technological, cultural) must serve as the learning source

Constraints¶

  • Time and power allocation difficulties reported by 77% and 54% of users

Target Learners¶

  • elementary to university biology students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • study results
  • student activity
  • critical thinking and creativity

Affordances¶

Claims¶

  • Studies report experiment classes using JAS achieve higher average study results than control classes [+W]
  • A meta-analysis of 103 JAS studies reports the approach's effectiveness in 97% of researches [+W]
  • JAS most frequently optimizes study results (52.83%) and student activity (40.57%) [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement cooperative learning with real problems, slow growth, team building, a safe environment, teacher modeling, mixed academic levels, and a teacher shift from directing to facilitating
  • Use named cooperative learning structures adapted to adult classrooms, including Student Team Learning, Think-Pair-Share, Jigsaw, Teaming Together, and Group Investigation
  • Home Living Center Role Play
