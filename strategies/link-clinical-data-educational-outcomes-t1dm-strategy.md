# Future research should use contemporaneous data and link clinical measures such as HbA1c with educational outcomes to inform school support for children with T1DM

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation: because included cohorts were born 1972–1985 before major changes in T1DM management, "more contemporaneous studies are required to assess the impact of advances in treatment alongside current examination conditions and data records", ideally linking clinical data such as HbA1c with educational outcomes to aid future recommendations on school support, especially around high stakes testing. It also recommends studies from broader contexts and alternative methods where large-scale data linkage is not viable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must use contemporaneous data reflecting current T1DM treatment regimens and current examination conditions and data records
#### Constraints
- Large-scale data linkage is not viable in all countries, so alternative methods are needed for broader contexts

### Target Learners
- children and young people with type 1 diabetes in compulsory schooling

### Target Learning Goals
- educational attainment in high stakes standardised testing at the end of compulsory schooling
