# Include relevant textbook-based visuals alongside textual explanations in conversational AI for visually rich STEM domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that conversational AI systems for visually rich STEM domains pair textual explanations with relevant visuals drawn from the learning material, because text-only conversational systems were rated highly but produced the lowest learning outcomes. As the authors state, "the inclusion of relevant textbook-based visuals alongside textual explanations is both necessary and beneficial for learning, as evidenced by our findings."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to grounded visuals from the source learning material
- A conversational interface so that reduced extraneous load is complemented by multimodally induced germane load
#### Constraints
- For domains where visuals are less critical, the article suggests exploring alternative strategies such as metacognitive prompting or introducing friction in human-AI interactions

### Target Learners
- learners in visually rich STEM domains, including higher-education biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving learning outcomes, not just perceived learning experience, from AI-supported self-learning
