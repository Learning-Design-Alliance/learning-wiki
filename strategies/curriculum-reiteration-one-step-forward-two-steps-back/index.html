Build regular reiteration of previously covered vocabulary topics into the EFL curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that, because the curriculum allocates only about a month to 100-120 target lexemes, courses should systematically revisit previously covered topics. They suggest "including in the curriculum of the course 'English as a Foreign Language' reiteration of the previously covered topics on a regular basis under the motto 'one step forward – two steps back'". As a next step for their translator/interpreter trainees, they also propose listening to professional texts dense with target vocabulary to build recognition skills for professional communication.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Curriculum time must be reserved for regular revisiting of earlier topics
  • Audio or listening materials containing target vocabulary need to be available

Constraints¶

  • The article notes the current curriculum's month-long allocation is significantly too little for retention and speech mastery

Target Learners¶

  • University ESL students training as philologists, teachers, translators and interpreters

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Retention of target vocabulary and its recognition and use in professional oral communication

Related Strategies¶

  • Spaced Practice Scheduling
  • Distribute audio vocabulary listening across a spaced repetition schedule during routine physical activity
