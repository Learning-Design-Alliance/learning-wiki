# Assign intelligence-matched art activities to student groups identified by an MI inventory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementation recipe: after administering an MI inventory, students are "categorised into intelligence groups according to the results obtained from MI inventory" and each group receives a work plan tailored to its intelligence field — e.g. verbal students write a story about the miniature sample, logical-mathematical students analyse geometric forms and symmetry, visual-spatial students answer guided observation questions, bodily-kinaesthetic students perform role plays, musical students write song words, social students discuss in buzz groups, intrapersonal students keep diaries, and naturalist students name objects. Activities ran three weeks, two hours weekly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated MI inventory to classify students into intelligence groups
- A prepared work plan specifying an activity per intelligence field
#### Constraints
- Tested only for the miniature picture topic in 8th-grade visual arts over three weeks

### Target Learners
- 8th-grade secondary school visual arts students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding and producing miniature picture art
