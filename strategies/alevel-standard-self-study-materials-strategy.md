# Provide A-Level-standard self-study materials with self-assessment questions for topics unseen by a significant proportion of the cohort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that where the course structure is inflexible, "self-study materials at an A-Level standard with self-assessment questions and in accessible formats should be made available" for topics previously unseen by a significant proportion of the cohort (around 25-75% is suggested). It further suggests university courses could consider excising some of the most basic concepts in kinematics and electric circuits, and further increasing homogeneity in first-year content alignment with A-level specifications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Topics targeted must be ones identified as previously unseen by a significant proportion of students, with around 25-75% suggested
#### Constraints
- Applies where the course structure is inflexible

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students transitioning from A-Level

### Target Learning Goals
- closing cohort knowledge gaps in core first-year physics topics
