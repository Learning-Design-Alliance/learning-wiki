# Encourage pre-service teachers to approach science learning from the perspective of a teacher to leverage their higher agency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because pre-service teachers were more explicit and prescriptive when prompting AI as teachers, the authors recommend that "Pre-service teachers should be actively encouraged to approach their learning in science courses from the perspective of a teacher who is capable of comprehending the learning material beyond what they would need to deliver to their students." Teacher preparation programs can tap this role-based agency to support content learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Science courses for pre-service teachers that include opportunities to adopt the teacher perspective
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehending science content beyond what is needed to deliver to students
