# Analyzing Errors (with Taboo game)

## Description
Learners play Taboo to practice choosing keywords, and the instructor identifies the strategies of finding synonyms and giving precise descriptions. The instructor guides reflection on the keyword search strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Taboo game, sample keyword lists for commonly searched topics
#### Constraints
- The game may not transfer well to other contexts.
