# Ask clicker questions even on concepts students already perform well on, as warm-ups building to harder questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because CQS questions build on one another, the authors recommend that instructors still ask clicker questions related to concepts students master readily after lecture. They state: "we recommend that instructors still ask the clicker questions related to these concepts. The questions are written to build on each other, so these would serve as a good warm-up to get students ready for the later questions, without taking very much time."
