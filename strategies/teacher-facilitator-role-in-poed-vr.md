# Teacher as facilitator monitoring VR progress and debriefing after diagnostic VR sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a facilitative rather than directive teacher role in the POED-based VR design. Before sessions the teacher introduced learning objectives and the POED structure; during sessions the teacher "monitored real-time progress through the system dashboard, identifying students who might require support based on repeated errors or prolonged inactivity"; after sessions the teacher conducted brief debriefings to reinforce concepts and guide reflection on learning strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system dashboard providing real-time visibility of student progress and error patterns
#### Constraints
- The article notes the teacher remained available for technical difficulties or conceptual misunderstandings that could not be resolved independently, while the VR system handled most content delivery and feedback

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade junior high school students in VR-based science inquiry

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and conceptual clarity during inquiry-based VR learning
