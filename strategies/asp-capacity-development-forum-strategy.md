# Organize a policy forum during the school to engage African policymakers on capacity development and retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During each school the IOC organizes a one-day forum on capacity development and retention in Africa. It "provides the platform to engage African policymakers on physics education and research, the media, the industries and the international community in a discussion about the role of the ASP." The forum also promotes research collaboration and consortia, and encourages collaboration among African countries to share experience and find concerted solutions to common issues in education.
