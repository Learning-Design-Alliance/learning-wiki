# Build metacognitive reflection opportunities into AI-integrated instruction to prevent cognitive overload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that instructional designers integrating AI into instruction create structured opportunities for students to reflect on their learning and monitor their AI use. It states designers "should allow for some type of opportunity for students to engage in metacognition, reﬂect on their own learning, monitor their progress, and develop strategies to eﬀectively use AI tools without developing cognitive overload." This follows from the finding that AI's benefit depends on learners' self-regulatory ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
