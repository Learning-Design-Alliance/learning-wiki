# Use EQUIP data to take action: test new strategies, collect data again, and align professional development to surfaced needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that teachers use EQUIP data to motivate change: try a new classroom strategy and collect data again to check for improvement, and use data from a department, grade level, or school to design or identify professional development aligned to teachers' areas for growth. Cathery's team, noticing multilingual learners were not participating in whole-group discussions, focused professional development on strategies for that need.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple rounds of data collection so users can see change over time
- Teacher agency in choosing the problem to address
#### Constraints
- The experience should be used to motivate change given the vulnerability of surfacing inequities

### Target Learners
- elementary, secondary, and community college mathematics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable participation in mathematics classrooms
