# Replicate the near-peer anatomy supplement at low cost using free cadaveric images and student volunteer tutors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend the model for institutions seeking to supplement anatomy education in PBL environments: it "can be easily replicated without any specific preconditions or financial resources, utilizing free cadaveric images and making implementation accessible". Tutoring relies on volunteer medical students selected for a strong anatomy foundation rather than formally trained faculty, keeping the supplement scalable and inexpensive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Free cadaveric images
- Student volunteer tutors with a strong anatomy foundation
#### Constraints
- Intended for self-directed, PBL-based settings

### Target Learners
- medical students in a problem-based learning undergraduate medical curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- anatomical knowledge acquisition in settings with limited anatomy lab hours
