# Implement PCP with initial 3-hour training plus semester follow-up sessions and onsite coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The recommended professional development model for PCP is "Initial 3-hour training with follow up one-hour questions and answer sessions during each semester along with onsite coaching", with the minimum being the 3-hour initial training. No special staffing or technology requirements are needed; HTML digital presentation files support visual access to instructional steps and routines and can be displayed on any device.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Minimum 3-hour initial training; follow-up one-hour question and answer sessions during each semester and onsite coaching are recommended
#### Constraints
- No special staffing requirements and no special technology requirements are stated

### Target Learners
- Teachers implementing the 95 Phonics Core Program in grades K-5

### Target Learning Goals
- Fidelity of implementation of explicit, structured daily phonics lessons
