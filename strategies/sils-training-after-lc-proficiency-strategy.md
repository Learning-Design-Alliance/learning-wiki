# Train senior residents in SILS after demonstrated proficiency in traditional LC, with case-step review and pre-case verbal walkthrough.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes an institutional approach to incorporating single-incision laparoscopic surgery into resident training: residents "were expected to be proficient in traditional LC before training in advanced techniques", supported by instructional multimedia reviewing the technical steps and a routine pre-operative walkthrough of the dissection steps with the attending surgeon. The study's outcome data support that this pathway produced comparable patient outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents must first be proficient in traditional laparoscopic cholecystectomy before training in advanced techniques
- Attending surgeon supervision of all cases, with a single experienced surgeon supervising SILS procedures
#### Constraints
- The institution does not provide SILS simulators, and the authors caution outcomes may have been affected by the greater experience of the senior residents performing SILS

### Target Learners
- senior surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- advanced laparoscopic technique (single-incision cholecystectomy) performed safely
