> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The project's approach was to develop cases of practice, identify key formative processes within them that are affected by the use of technologies, and from this analysis extract patterns to inform future software design. This strategy links practitioner accounts of formative e-assessment directly to software design requirements.
