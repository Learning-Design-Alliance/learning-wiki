Encourage students to use AI-generated responses while observing ethical guidelines¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that students be encouraged to engage with AI-generated response tools responsibly and within ethical boundaries. It states that "students be encouraged to use AI-generated responses while observing ethical guidelines in their use". The strategy treats ethical use as a condition on AI-supported learning rather than a prohibition on the tools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ethical guidelines governing students' use of AI-generated responses must be in place

Constraints¶

  • Derived from findings on quadratic-equation learning with Nigerian secondary students; the article does not print the specific guidelines themselves

Target Learners¶

  • Secondary school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Responsible and ethical use of AI-generated responses in learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Institutions should respond to generative AI in education with guided critical engagement rather than prohibition
  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
