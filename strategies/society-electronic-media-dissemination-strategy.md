## Description
The paper recommends deploying education and scientific initiatives first through professional scientific societies, which promote quick advancement of nascent initiatives and worldwide adoption. It highlights that MGI findings and tutorials have already been disseminated through innovative electronic media delivery, and that these capabilities encourage participation from institutions with limited or incipient research efforts such as community colleges or newly founded universities.
