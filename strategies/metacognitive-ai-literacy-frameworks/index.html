Develop AI literacy frameworks that build metacognitive awareness distinguishing system-assisted performance from independent competence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends updating AI literacy frameworks so they "move beyond tool usage to address metacognitive awareness, enabling users and evaluators to distinguish between system-assisted performance and independently grounded competence." This includes shared norms around appropriate use, transparency, and interpretation of AI-mediated outputs, and clarifying when outputs can and cannot indicate skill.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Explicit attention to the distribution of human and machine contribution in evaluated work

Constraints¶

  • Proposed in the article's implications discussion; not tested empirically

Target Learners¶

  • LLM users, students, evaluators, and hiring professionals

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Calibrated self-assessment; accurate interpretation of AI-mediated outputs as skill indicators

Claims¶

  • The article proposes an empirical validation agenda: comparing perceived competence under LLM-assisted versus unaided conditions, with measurement frameworks and longitudinal designs [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Evaluate LLM-supported learning beyond binary access by measuring enacted interaction and preserving no-help assessments
  • Institutional governance should establish practical conditions supporting AI literacy, learner agency, and critical evaluation rather than relying on prohibition and detection
  • Transition from restrictive AI governance to frameworks that formally integrate operational and epistemic workflow competencies with subject-sensitive guidance
  • Formative friction: require goal statements, alternative comparison, and justification before high-stakes delegation, kept proportionate and accessible
  • Integrate emotion-regulation and metacognitive support into AI literacy education, with differentiated learning assistance
