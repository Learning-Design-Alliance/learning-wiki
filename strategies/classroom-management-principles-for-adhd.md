# Provide teachers with a concrete set of classroom management principles for stimulating lessons, clear instructions, routines, and strategic seating

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training manual included nine basic management principles: keep lessons stimulating, involve students actively, reduce complex information to key messages with short sentences and repetition, give regular immediate specific feedback, develop classwork routines, plan ahead for critical transitions, seat disruptive students near the teacher away from activity centres, help students stay organised with homework journals, and define clear rules with immediate consequences displayed on a posterboard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need training on how to apply the principles to individual students
- Rules should be discussed with the entire class and printed visibly in the classroom
