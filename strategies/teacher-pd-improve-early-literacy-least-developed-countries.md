# Use teacher PD targeting pedagogical content knowledge, research-based practices, and print environments to improve early literacy instruction quality in low-resource countries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article draws implications for improving the quality of early literacy instruction in the least developed countries from its Rwanda trial results. It states: "We discuss implications for improving the quality of early literacy instruction in the least developed countries." The strategy is to invest in teacher professional development that builds early literacy pedagogical content knowledge, promotes research-based pedagogical practices, and enriches classroom print environments, since these outcomes showed significant improvement in the trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A PD program addressing teachers' early literacy pedagogical content knowledge, classroom pedagogical practices, and classroom print environments
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a rural Rwanda context; observed classroom practice differences were not statistically significant

### Target Learners
- Early-grade students and teachers in least developed countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Early literacy instruction quality and reading skills
