# Research should evaluate cognitive and academic effects of very short (≤5 min) classroom PA breaks, the form staff consider feasible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research strategy: because a gap exists between the forms of PA staff consider feasible (classroom-based, ≤10 min) and the forms examined and proved effective in research (acute PA of at least 10 minutes; chronic interventions of 15 minutes or longer per session), future studies should investigate the acute and long-term effects of repeated very short classroom PA breaks, and include measures of teachers' experiences alongside standardized performance tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies should include a measure of teachers' experiences in addition to standardized performance tasks
#### Constraints
- The article notes the current evidence base on PA for academic performance contains many inconsistencies and obscurities

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in grades 5 and 6

### Target Learning Goals
- Cognitive and academic effects of short physical activity breaks
