# Build patient education skill development from role models, real-world practice opportunities, and performance feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on prior work it cites, the article recommends that "For skill development in patient education, recommended educational strategies are: exposing students to appropriate role models, providing explicit opportunities to practice skills in real-world settings, and providing opportunities for feedback on performance". Its own trial supports this by combining theoretical teaching, simulated practice with peers, and structured feedback in a single brief session. The authors conclude such interventions "could fit into an existing physical therapy curriculum".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for practice and patient education during training, along with observation and feedback, are described as necessary for physical therapy students' training
#### Constraints
- Further research is needed on long-term impact or maintenance of skills and their sustained impact on patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- final-year physiotherapy students
- health profession students

### Target Learning Goals
- patient education skills
- communication skill development
