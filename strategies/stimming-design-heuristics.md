# Three design heuristics for leveraging stimming pedagogically: offer stim affordances, legitimize stims as discursive resource, and leverage stims as epistemic actions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes three guiding heuristics for instructional design that embraces stimming rather than suppressing it. First, offering stim affordances means instrumenting stimming with media that facilitate stimming activity; second, legitimizing stims as a discursive resource means treating stims as multimodal utterances within classroom interaction; third, leveraging stims as epistemic actions means instrumentalizing stimming so it is functionally superimposed with thinking in meaningful activity. The authors state: "We draw examples from the BBM design to propose and elaborate upon three guiding heuristics for pedagogical design: offering stim affordances (“Instrumenting Stimming” section), legitimizing stims as a discursive resource (“Interactional Stimming” section), and leveraging stims as epistemic actions (“Instrumentalizing Stimming” section)." Depathologizing and normalizing stimming is positioned as freeing cognitive resources currently spent on suppression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning contexts must depathologize and normalize stimming, and provide media that facilitate stimming activity
#### Constraints
- The heuristics are proposed as conceptual guidance illustrated by one exploratory design (BBM), not yet validated at scale

### Target Learners
- all learners
- with particular attention to neurodivergent children marginalized for their stims

### Target Learning Goals
- integrating self-regulatory movement with curricular content learning
- inclusive participation in classroom discourse
