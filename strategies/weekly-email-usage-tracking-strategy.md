# Use weekly email reminders and self-reported minutes-per-week categories to track engagement with pre- and post-course materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the pilot, after "weekly email reminders to use the preparation materials", students reported how much they had used the app or notes, measured in categories of 0, approximately 10, 20, 30, 60, or ≥90 minutes per week. This longitudinal tracking enabled the correlation between app usage time and MC improvement. The authors themselves note the limitation that the reports were retrospective and suggest automatic recording of app usage as the better method.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly email reminders to students to use the preparation materials and report their usage
#### Constraints
- Retrospective self-reports are subject to the bias of personal memory, which the authors state made them of less importance than automatic recording would be

### Target Learners
- medical students using pre- and post-course preparation materials

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring engagement with preparation and review materials
