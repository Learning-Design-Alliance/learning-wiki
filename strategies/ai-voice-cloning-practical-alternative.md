# Use AI voice cloning as a practical alternative for prerecorded course audio production

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports using AI voice cloning to generate audio for prerecorded courses where it reduces production time without compromising learning outcomes or satisfaction. The authors conclude it is "a practical alternative for prerecorded medical courses." AI allows for rapid corrections and consistent results with minimal additional effort.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a commercial AI text-to-speech system with voice cloning capability
- Informed consent from the voice owner
#### Constraints
- Some students may perceive synthetic voices as less authentic
- Generative technologies are not yet mature enough to produce realistic video of the teacher speaking

### Target Learners
- medical students
- medical educators

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient production of prerecorded course content
