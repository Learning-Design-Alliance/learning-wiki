# Establish standardized evidence-based guidelines and adequate institutional resources to sustain co-teaching with individuals with Disorders of Intellectual Development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that the field needs "standardized, evidence-based guidelines and adequate institutional resources to support co-teaching involving individuals with Disorders of Intellectual Development in healthcare education." It reports that systemic barriers, exclusionary system structures, institutional practices, and professional attitudes can undermine the transformative potential of user involvement, and that inadequate investment in planning-phase time and resources can undermine effectiveness — not because of the concept itself, but because of insufficient support structures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment and overcoming persistent barriers
- Additional time and resources in the planning phase for curriculum development and implementation
#### Constraints
- Hesitancy toward these approaches has been observed among senior faculty members due to limited experience and implementation guidelines
