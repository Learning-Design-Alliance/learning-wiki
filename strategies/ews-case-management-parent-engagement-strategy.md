# Use case management and parent engagement to support at-risk students identified by an Early Warning System

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Once the EWS identifies at-risk students, school directors, homeroom teachers, PTA members, and community members "develop relationships with parents of at -risk students through phone calls, conferences, and home visits" to keep parents informed of their children's progress and to teach parents how to better monitor attendance. Schools and communities then carry out "first response" activities including classroom practices, case management, and community and parental awareness building and involvement.

## Design Implications
