# Change agents should support instructors to use RBISs beyond lecture supplements and raise active-learning time above two-thirds of class time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that "change agentsshould focus efforts on motivating and supporting instructors to use more RBISs that are not primarilysupplements to lecture (i.e. studio or SCALE-UP) and increase the time spent in active learning to be greater than ⅔ of class time." This shifts emphasis from promoting awareness and first trials toward deeper implementation, reflecting findings that knowledge is near saturation, discontinuation is low, but lecture time remains high and high-impact strategies are rarely used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continued support structures for instructors during implementation, since the article reports low discontinuation where support exists
#### Constraints
- The recommendation targets US introductory physics instruction and rests on the cited expectation that deeper-departure RBISs produce stronger outcomes

### Target Learners
- US postsecondary introductory physics instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing proportion of class time in active learning; implementing higher-impact instructional strategies
