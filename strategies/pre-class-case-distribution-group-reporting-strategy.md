# Pre-class case distribution plus in-class group reporting within a flipped classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe the authors describe for FC+CBL: send classic cases to students before class through an established online platform, have students pre-group, analyse the case, and prepare a PowerPoint or discussion position; then students report in class and the teacher explains case-related knowledge points, with students discussing and summarising. The authors also recommend "Sending students the latest cutting-edge developments related to course knowledge after class" to expand research thinking and knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-established platform (e.g., QQ) for distributing cases
- Sufficient class hours; the authors note using FC or CBL alone was insufficient for the course's many teaching contents
#### Constraints
- Developed for a graduate MPH course with small cohorts; the authors plan to keep refining the case library

### Target Learners
- Master of Public Health (MPH) graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying theory to solve practical public health problems
- Independent learning and research thinking
