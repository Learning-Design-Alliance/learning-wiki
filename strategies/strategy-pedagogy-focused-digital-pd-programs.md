# Implement structured professional development that pairs technical skills with pedagogical strategies, hands-on workshops, peer collaboration, and ongoing mentorship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practice recommendation is structured training programs that extend beyond basic IT skills to build the pedagogical strategies teachers need to integrate technology into instruction. It specifies that "Effective professional development should include hands -on workshops, peer collaboration, and real -world application scenarios", and calls for ongoing support and mentorship so educators can navigate technical challenges and continuously refine their digital teaching strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must pair technical skills with pedagogical strategies aligned to teachers' existing practices and beliefs.
- Ongoing support and mentorship must accompany initial training so teachers can refine digital teaching strategies.
#### Constraints
- The article notes many professional development programs focus primarily on technical skills rather than pedagogical strategies, leading to gaps in effective classroom technology use.

### Target Learners
- teachers and instructors across primary, secondary, and higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- translating digital knowledge into meaningful classroom experiences
- building digital pedagogical readiness
