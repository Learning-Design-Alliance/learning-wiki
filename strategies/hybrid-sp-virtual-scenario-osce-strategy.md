# Combine real SPs for doctor-patient communication with virtual scenarios for clinical examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Reflecting on limits of SP recruitment, the authors propose a hybrid assessment design: keep SPs for the doctor-patient communication component while using virtual patient scenarios for the clinical examination component. "Therefore, we are currently trying to continue using SP for doctor-patient communication while using virtual scenarios for clinical examination." They argue this can overcome the limitation of SPs' actual dental conditions, enable long-term SP cooperation, and reduce the impact of inadequate training and frequent SP replacement, while noting SP encounters emotionally prepare candidates in ways digital technology cannot replace.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual scenarios able to simulate clinical scenes and disease situations that SPs cannot replicate, such as maxillofacial fractures and tumors
#### Constraints
- The authors note SP encounters may cause examinee anxiety, which they view as realistic emotional preparation that digital technology cannot replace

### Target Learners
- dental residents in standardized training

### Target Learning Goals
- doctor-patient communication assessment
- clinical examination assessment
