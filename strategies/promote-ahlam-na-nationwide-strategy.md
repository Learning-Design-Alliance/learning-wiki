# Integrate and actively promote the AHlam Na 2.0 app across Philippine regions to widen adolescent health education reach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the observed knowledge gains and positive satisfaction ratings, the authors recommend deploying the app beyond the study site: "it is recommended that the app be actively promoted in various regions across the Philippines to reach a wider audience and enhance health awareness among young people." They further state that expanding availability could contribute to improving adolescent health knowledge on a national scale, and that the abstract recommends integrating the application into adolescent health curricula in schools across the Philippines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mobile phones available to adolescents
- Content aligned with Department of Health and POPCOM standards
#### Constraints
- The study suggests future research should examine long-term impacts on health behaviors and expand the sample to more diverse demographic groups before broader claims

### Target Learners
- Filipino adolescents in junior high school

### Target Learning Goals
- Raising adolescent health awareness at national scale
