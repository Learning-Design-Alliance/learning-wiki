# Partner with an Indigenous programs academic unit to develop local Indigenous astronomy content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because little has been published on Aboriginal astronomy in the Sydney region, the Observatory developed a partnership with the Nura Gili Indigenous Programs Unit at the University of New South Wales. Under the partnership, students in the undergraduate unit ATSI 3006: Astronomy of Indigenous Australians research Aboriginal astronomical traditions in the Sydney region and develop a planisphere based on these findings, with content to be incorporated into the program in mid-2014.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An academic partnership with an Indigenous programs unit
- A university course in which developing educational materials is a major assessment
#### Constraints
- Little has been published on Aboriginal astronomy in the Sydney region, so content development depends on new research

### Target Learners
- Primary school students
- Undergraduate students in ATSI 3006

### Target Learning Goals
- Incorporating local Sydney Aboriginal astronomical traditions (Dharug, Dharawal, Gundungurra, and Kuringai) into the education program
