# Orient PHC-based supervisors to the skills laboratory program before hosting preclinical students for early clinical experiences.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before the ECE attachment, supervising general practitioners were deliberately aligned with the school's skills training standards: GPs "were invited to the skills laboratory to be introduced to the skills training program for pre-clinical students, to attend demonstrations of some skills trainings". This ensures that supervised practice in PHC centers reinforces, rather than diverges from, the procedures students learned in the skills laboratory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skills laboratory staff must extensively inform supervisors-to-be about students' competency level, learning needs, and standards for executing clinical procedures.
#### Constraints
- Requires willing GP participation and available staff time in the PHC centers.

### Target Learners
- preclinical medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent clinical skills training across laboratory and clinical settings

- [Clerkship supervisors gave students with early PHC clinical experience more opportunities to perform clinical skills than their skills-laboratory-only peers.](../claims/supervisors-give-pt-more-performance-opportunities.md) [+W]
