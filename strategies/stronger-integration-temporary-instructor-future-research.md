# Future studies should integrate the temporary instructor more strongly with relevant learning materials and balance instructor-present and instructor-absent slides

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the tested implementation confined the instructor to introductory and transitional slides without relevant information, the authors recommend that future work vary how temporary presence is implemented. They write that "future experiments may look into strategies of temporary instructor presence in that the instructor is more strongly integrated with relevant learning materials", for example presenting instructors based on an assessment of the visual relevance of the on-screen information, and they suggest balancing slides with and without instructor presence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategies that rely on presenting the instructor on introductory and transitional slides that do not provide relevant information are the scope within which the null findings apply
#### Constraints
- The current implementation left the instructor invisible about 80% of the time, which may not have been sufficient to harvest the benefits of instructor presence
