# Strength-Based Approach to Responsible Decision-Making

## Description
This approach involves identifying and leveraging a student’s strengths to address challenges in responsible decision-making. Educators identify specific student strengths and/or challenges related to responsible decision-making and uses aligned strategies to leverage student strengths to support student learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator understanding of student strengths and challenges. Access to tailored strategies and activities. Supportive and positive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful assessment of student strengths and challenges. May be difficult to identify and leverage strengths for all students.
