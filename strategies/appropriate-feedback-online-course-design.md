## Description
The article identifies "appropriate feedback" as one of the major issues to be taken into consideration when designing online courses, based on Ke's (2016) systematic review. It discusses from a practical perspective how this theme can be brought to life when transitioning a course to an online teaching modality, aimed at assisting course instructors making a fast transition.
