# Repeated aneurysm clip occlusion practice with fluid-color success feedback until success

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable drill in which a trainee applies aneurysm clips to the pulsating blister-like dilation while dyed fluid circulates, reads the fluid color change as immediate success-or-failure feedback, removes the clip after a failure, and repeats. The article reports that 'the resident can simulate aneurysm clips to occlude the aneurysm repeatedly until success', with fluid color controlled by an on-off switch.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The cerebral aneurysm simulator with blood flow driver, aneurysm clips, and differently colored dye fluids whose flow the trainee controls with an on-off switch
#### Constraints
- Assessed only qualitatively in a pilot with 4 medical students and 6 neurosurgeons; the authors state the overall simulator still needs validation

### Target Learners
- neurosurgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- aneurysm clip occlusion technique
- recognizing failed occlusion during simulated surgery
