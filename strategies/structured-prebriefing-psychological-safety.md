# Structured prebriefing to establish psychological safety before the simulated scene

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The prebriefing prepares participants for the scenario: participants previously received scientific references, and at the Simulation Center the prebriefing covered "Agreement of the rules, the roles to be played, mutual respect and confidentiality", identification of prior simulation and childbirth experience, clarification of objectives, scene time, and presentation of environment and equipment, followed by familiarization. The article states "The purpose of the prebriefing is to establish a psychologically safe environment for the simulation participants, with the establishment of a work 'contract' for the activities". In this study the prebriefing lasted five minutes.
