Increase teacher training to include technology in classrooms and recommend computers and interactive teaching software for Latino-ethnic students¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's recommendations section identifies implementable actions: "Recommendations were made to increase trainings for teachers to include technology in the classrooms," and it states that "recommended technology for the Latino-ethnic group that leads to academic success in higher education include computers and interactive teaching software." These follow from the finding that most analyzed articles linked technology to increased academic success.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need training to include technology in classrooms

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Latino-ethnic students in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic success in higher education through technology use

Claims¶

  • In a four-article meta-analysis, seventy-five percent of articles found technology increases academic success in higher education among the Latino-ethnic population [+M]
  • One analyzed article found that lack of access to technological resources in higher education limits academic success among the Latino-ethnic population [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Future research should use qualitative field data and secondary analysis of online-technology classrooms in Hispanic Serving Institutions
  • Train teachers and tutors on instructional use rather than device mastery, and include school-level storage and maintenance training
  • Engage students in self-learning by providing an array of technology resources, with teacher training through workshops and seminars
