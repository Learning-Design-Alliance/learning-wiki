# Segment trainees by initial level and add consolidation modules in the final training phase

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend adjusting MI training design: "One key improvement would be to segment the participants according to their initial levels", so training matches specific needs, and including consolidation and backup modules in the final stage to sustain learning. They also recommend practical reinforcement and objective assessments to consolidate gains that fluctuated in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
