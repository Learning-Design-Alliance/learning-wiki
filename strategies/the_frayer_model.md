# The Frayer Model

## Description
A tool for teaching a concept, not isolated facts.  Includes four pieces: Definition, Essential Characteristics, Examples, and Non-Examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Definition, essential characteristics, examples and non-examples of the topic.
#### Constraints
- May require some teachers to clarify what the concept really means.
