# Structure intensive schools with plenary lectures for general topics and parallel sessions for advanced topics matched to participants' academic concentrations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report describes designing the ASP2022 program with plenary lectures for topics of general interest followed by more topical and advanced lectures in parallel sessions, based on participants' academic concentrations. Participants were expected to select the lectures that best supported their academic needs, with selections given to lecturers before the event. The authors state this organization "allowed coverage of a remarkable amount of materials in the short duration of a two-week engagement."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers must consider the backgrounds of their audience, as shown in participant academic-level and concentration data, in the design and delivery of their materials.
#### Constraints
- Parallel lectures drew a mixture of students with various levels of training in the subjects, and the broadness of subjects made it difficult for some participants to digest lecture materials.

### Target Learners
- University students with diverse academic majors and levels of training

### Target Learning Goals
- In-depth coverage of topics supporting students' academic majors
- Efficient coverage of material in a short-duration intensive school
