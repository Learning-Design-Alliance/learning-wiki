# Integrate family and school contexts in interventions and promote adolescent academic self-regulation to support the high school transition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend prevention and intervention programs that integrate family, school, and individual domains rather than targeting them in isolation, because the domains are interdependent and improvements in one bolster the others. They specifically advise that "efforts to promote adolescent ASR through school and family contexts are likely to be well-served in promoting a successful transition to high school". Suggested approaches include family-school collaboration and embedding family-centered interventions within schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must address family and school domains together, since the study found interdependencies among the three domains
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a primarily White, rural sample; the authors state replication with more diverse samples is needed

### Target Learners
- early adolescents in middle school transitioning to high school

### Target Learning Goals
- academic self-regulation
- school adjustment
- academic achievement
