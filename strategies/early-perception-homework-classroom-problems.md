# Early identification of homework-management and classroom-performance problems by parents and teachers to prevent long-term academic underachievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that parents and teachers watch for early signs of academic difficulty because symptoms of ADHD and ODD in adolescence have pervasive effects on adult attainment. They state that "The role of parents and teachers is important, and early perception of problems with homework management and classroom performance can have significant long-term implications." The strategy is to monitor and act on these problems early, informed by this cohort's finding that deficits persist into adulthood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adults in contact with the adolescent (parents and teachers) must be positioned to observe homework management and classroom performance
#### Constraints
- The article does not test an intervention; the recommendation rests on the observational cohort findings rather than a manipulated or tested programme

### Target Learners
- adolescents with symptoms of ADHD or ODD

### Target Learning Goals
- preventing academic underachievement and limited educational attainment
