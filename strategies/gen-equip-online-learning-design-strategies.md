# Design online health-professional e-learning with a high pass mark, active interactivity, continuous feedback, and optional extension material

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors attribute the modules' effectiveness and perceived helpfulness to specific online learning principles: "setting high expectations (by using a high pass mark), ensuring learning materials encouraged active participation (using interactive features), giving feedback throughout the modules regarding correct or incorrect choices, and addressing the diverse range of learners by including optional material." Participants confirmed these strategies were helpful and stimulating. The 80% pass mark was set to keep the education challenging, reflecting professional clinical exam pass marks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interactive features and in-module feedback on correct or incorrect choices
- Optional material to address a diverse range of learners
#### Constraints
- Live training may be more effective for improving consultation skills, per the review the authors cite

### Target Learners
- primary care health professionals undertaking continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- genetics knowledge, skills, and confidence applicable to daily practice
