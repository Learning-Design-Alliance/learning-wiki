# Facilitate knowledge sharing between organizations experienced with evidence-based strategies and organizations seeking to use them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The brief proposes knowledge sharing between education organizations as a mechanism for spreading evidence-based strategies: "One way to support the spread of evidence-based strategies is to facilitate knowledge sharing between education organizations" that have implementation experience and those seeking to use the strategies. The SEED Partnership Project is the article's working instance of this approach, pairing national non-profits with educational organizations. The strategy operates at the organizational level rather than the classroom level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An organization with experience developing and implementing the evidence-based strategy must be willing to share knowledge with a partner organization
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as one way among others to support the spread of evidence-based strategies, not as a tested or exclusive mechanism

### Target Learners
- educators and the educational organizations that prepare and develop them

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying and adapting evidence-based strategies for improving student outcomes
