# Use a gamified quiz platform (Kahoot!) as a playful learning check after the calculation tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond checking the correctness of students' individual answers, the handout proposes a playful form of learning control using Kahoot!, an interactive online platform. The teacher defines a quiz that students play on their smartphones; as game leader the teacher can see the number of correct answers per question, display them graphically for all, and inspect who gave right or wrong answers in order to follow up — for example by asking why a particular answer was chosen. A ready-made quiz on the way to the Moon is provided via a share link.
