# Minimum viable implementation: adopt QLP Tiers 1–2 in one course with free platforms and a pre/post concept inventory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes an incremental adoption path deployable "within a single existing course by one instructor, focusing on Tier 1 and Tier 2". It requires only freely available quantum software platforms (pennylane, Qiskit or Cirq), one project-based assignment, and a validated quantum concept inventory administered before and after instruction. Learning gains may be evaluated through pre/post assessments, student reflections, or comparison with a parallel offering when available.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Freely available quantum software platforms such as pennylane, Qiskit, or Cirq
- One project-based assignment
- A validated quantum concept inventory administered before and after instruction
#### Constraints
- Evaluation via comparison with a parallel offering is only possible when one is available

### Target Learners
- Students in a single existing quantum computing or adjacent course

### Target Learning Goals
- Tier 1 phenomenological understanding and Tier 2 computational abstraction of quantum concepts
