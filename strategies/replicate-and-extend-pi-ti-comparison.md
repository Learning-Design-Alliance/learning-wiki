# Pilot follow-up: replicate the PI-versus-TI comparison, extend it to other question pairs, and test combining TI after PI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In their closing paragraph the authors propose follow-up studies for their pilot: "It would be interesting to replicate our pilot study to see if the same conclusion holds", extending the comparison "to other pairs of similar questions of different topic and difficulty level (as measured by the PCA for Q1)", and studying whether adding TI after PI between the two similar questions beats either method alone. This treats the reported result as provisional and defines the conditions under which the protocol comparison should be generalized.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional semesters or courses in which the same two-instruction comparison is repeated with comparable question pairs
#### Constraints
- The present evidence comes from one course, one topic (Lenz's law) and one instructor pairing, so the authors themselves frame replication as needed

### Target Learners
- first-year engineering physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual application of physics laws such as Lenz's law
