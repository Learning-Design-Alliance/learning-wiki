# Poll participants' baseline tool familiarity at the start of a session and adjust demonstration time per tool accordingly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The workshop used Zoom polling at the outset to gauge which interactive tools participants had previously used, which "allowed us to tailor the demonstration of the tools to the audience." After Workshop 1 revealed most participants already knew basic chat, polling, and whiteboard functions, the facilitators shifted Workshop 2 time toward alternative tools. The authors recommend future facilitators do the same.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A videoconferencing platform with a polling feature, or an equivalent polling tool, to assess baseline familiarity
#### Constraints
- Requires facilitators to adapt timing on the fly based on poll results

### Target Learners
- Health professions educators
- Faculty development participants

### Target Learning Goals
- Selecting and using interactive virtual tools appropriate to audience needs
