# Use real-time feedback and repeated practice in preclinical surgical simulation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training sequence has instructors demonstrate procedures, then lets students operate independently while the system monitors actions and delivers immediate scores and feedback, with replay for review. The authors state the system "can provide real-time feedback on students' learning results, through repeated practice can enhance students' self-confidence in clinical practice", and they contrast this with manikin training where "immediate feedback on their operative outcomes is mostly absent". This makes automated feedback plus repeatable practice the operative recipe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An evaluation system capable of real-time scoring and feedback on each student's operation
- Instructor demonstration and theoretical explanation before independent practice
#### Constraints
- Requires specialized equipment and higher investment cost, per the authors' stated limitations

### Target Learners
- preclinical dental and medical students learning surgical procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical operative skill
- self-confidence in clinical practice
