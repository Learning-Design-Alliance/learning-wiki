Automating the Obstacle Course with trial-end and trial-start gates and automated reward delivery¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For future OC-based studies the authors propose a specific automation recipe: insert "a trial-end gate that closes behind the animal once it reaches the reward site" and "a trial-start gate that opens on the other side of the reward site", and automate reward delivery, both operating via a photo-sensitive beam for motion sensing. The trial-start gate can control the number and timing of laps where control of lap number rather than total time is desired. Such automation would mitigate the labor-intensive nature of the OC and CC and add an additional degree of control.
