# Use corner states to interpolate between the six Bloch Cube face states and general Bloch Sphere states

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To counter the impression that only six quantum states exist with only 0%, 50%, and 100% measurement probabilities, the article recommends considering "four pairs of “corner states”" on the Bloch Cube. Opposite corners are distinct from one another but not fully distinct from other pairs or the face states, and measurement outcomes are probabilistic for all three questions X, Y, Z, with probabilities different from 0%, 50%, and 100%. This is accompanied by a video in the same style.
