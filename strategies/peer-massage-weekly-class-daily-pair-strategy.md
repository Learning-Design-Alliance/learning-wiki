# Peer massage program: weekly 50-minute massage classes plus daily 10-minute peer massage before snack break

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom program in which a teacher trained for 4 hours demonstrates massage techniques to children seated in randomly assigned pairs, who perform maneuvers on each other's shoulders, arms, hands, and scalp while wearing cotton T-shirts. The article states children "were asked to practice the massage with randomly determined pairs, for 10 minutes, being 5 minutes to give and 5 to receive massage" daily before the snack break after the first class. Classes lasted 50 minutes weekly on the same weekday for ten weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom teacher trained by the researcher for 4 hours prior to the beginning of the massage classes
- Randomly assigned pairs of children and a fixed weekly class time
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one public school with children from low-income families aged 6.5–8.1 years

### Target Learners
- Elementary school children aged 6–8 years

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing aggressive behaviors
- Improving academic performance
- Social and emotional development
