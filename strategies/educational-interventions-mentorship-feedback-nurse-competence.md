# Implement organizational educational interventions — mentorship, structured feedback, and continuing professional development — to raise nurses' clinical competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that healthcare organizations implement educational interventions to enhance nurses' clinical competence, including "continuous professional development opportunities, mentorship programs, inclusive training initiatives, and structured feedback mechanisms". These measures are intended to keep nurses up-to-date with practices and technologies, create a supportive learning atmosphere, and address the differing needs of nurses across genders and specialties. The conclusion additionally suggests exploring role play, problem-based learning, e-learning, and simulation as training modalities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Healthcare organizations must provide conditions that help nurses advance their careers, including ongoing evaluations of clinical competence and practicing advanced nursing skills
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a cross-sectional self-report survey; the authors state future research should use longitudinal and intervention studies to test specific training interventions

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses in outpatient clinics and hospitals, particularly those with weaker advanced nursing skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical competence, especially advanced nursing skills, critical thinking, and continuous professional learning
