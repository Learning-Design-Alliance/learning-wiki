# When adding tests to a postgraduate rotation, gather learner preferences on number of questions, duration, and repetitions to inform test design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors surveyed participants about optimal test parameters after the trial and report the preferences directly: "The optimal parameters for the exam, as indicated by the participants, were 15 questions with a duration of 20 min, to be taken twice." A majority also favored performing the test twice. This strategy treats learner-reported parameters as input for designing test-enhanced learning activities in a residency curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A voluntary, anonymous questionnaire administered after testing to gather learner opinions about test-taking and learning habits
#### Constraints
- These are self-reported preferences, not empirically validated optima; the intervention tests in this study used ten questions rather than the preferred fifteen

### Target Learners
- first-year anesthesiology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- designing MCQ-based test-enhanced learning activities during clinical rotations
