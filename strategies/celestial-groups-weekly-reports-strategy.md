# Celestial-body student groups delivering weekly five-minute reports

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the described course, students divided themselves into celestial groups, each focusing on a celestial body or bodies such as the Sun, the Pleiades, Eclipses, or the Zodiac. Each group was required to give detailed scientific explanations of their celestial focus, give visibility updates, and present relevant myths and legends broken down into five-minute reports delivered each week. This social structure organized weekly discussion of astronomy content alongside cultural narratives.
