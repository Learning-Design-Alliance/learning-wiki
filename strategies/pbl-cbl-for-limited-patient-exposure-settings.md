# Use combined PBL-CBL teaching in clinical settings where direct patient exposure is limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the PBL-CBL model as "a feasible and scalable pedagogical innovation for residency programs, particularly in clinical settings where direct patient exposure is limited". It simulates authentic clinical scenarios within a structured and safe learning environment, addressing constraints such as patient privacy and gender-related restrictions on observation and practice in gynecology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor preparation of structured pre-class materials
- Small-group case discussion with instructor feedback
#### Constraints
- The authors note its application in China is still limited and in the stage of exploration and research

### Target Learners
- Gynecology residents
- Postgraduate medical trainees in specialties with restricted clinical practice opportunities

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Patient-centered care
- Engagement and satisfaction
