# Number Talks

## Description
Number Talks are short, daily classroom routines that provide students with opportunities to mentally solve and discuss numerical problems. The teacher poses a problem, and students share their different strategies, promoting mental math skills, number sense, and conceptual understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher preparation of appropriate problems, facilitation of discussions
#### Constraints
- May require some initial training for teachers to implement effectively
