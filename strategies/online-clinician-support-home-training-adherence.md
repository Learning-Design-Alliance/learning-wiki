# Deliver home-based computerized training with weekly online clinician support to reduce family burden and support adherence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The trial pairs its home-based online training programs with online clinician support: a trained coach conducts a Start-up session covering set-up, training structure, scheduling, motivation, and rewards, then holds weekly online meetings (via Skype Business/Google Hangouts/GoTo Meetings) for 5 weeks to provide support and answer queries, monitoring training and compliance through a secure server. The authors state this design choice explicitly: "The use of online training programs and online clinician support will ensure fewer burdens on families, higher participation, and adherence to training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained coach/facilitator, internet access or a provided internet-enabled iPad, and scheduled weekly online meetings for the duration of training
#### Constraints
- Technical issues must be addressed by the project co-ordinator in the weekly meetings; compliance depends on families supporting the child through completion

### Target Learners
- Children post-TBI and their families training from home

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement and adherence to a 5-week computerized cognitive training program
