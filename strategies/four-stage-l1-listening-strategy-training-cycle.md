# Four-stage L1 listening strategy training cycle per session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A repeatable session structure for listening strategy instruction drawn from Vandergrift (1997) and used in the intervention. Each 32-40 minute cycle comprises: i) introducing/explaining the listening strategy (8-10 minutes); ii) modelling the listening strategy through an example or two (8-10 minutes); iii) giving students a chance to discuss/practise the listening strategy with their peers (8-10 minutes); and iv) evaluating students' listening strategy through spot checking (8-10 minutes).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher or researcher directing the intervention; L1 oral input materials for practice; a quick recap of previously taught strategies at the outset of every session
#### Constraints
- Sessions in the study ran 32-40 minutes within regular 90-minute classes; strategies were sequenced across 14 sessions

### Target Learners
- Lower-intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 listening comprehension through explicit strategy use
