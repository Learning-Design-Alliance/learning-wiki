# Teach astronomy through dual-perspective storytelling (indigenous and Western science stories woven together)

## Description
This strategy teaches astronomical content by telling both the indigenous cultural story and the Western science story of the same sky object, weaving them into one dense tapestry of knowledge. The article grounds it in research that "our brains are wired to understand and retain stories," and illustrates it with the Wintermaker constellation, which a Western-science viewer sees as Orion: Ojibwe stories relate seasonal spirits, while Western science tells of star formation in the Orion Nebula. Since 2016 NSW resource development includes short storytelling videos such as Wintermaker and Ojiig.
