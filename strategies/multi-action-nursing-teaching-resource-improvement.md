# Multi-action improvement of nursing teaching resources: increase investment, allocate resources reasonably, optimize infrastructure, build clinical bases, train educators, promote exchanges

## Description
The article's implications section recommends a package of actions to improve teaching resources for nursing undergraduates: "increasing education investment, reasonably allocating teaching resource, optimizing nursing education infrastructure, building clinical teaching bases, training nursing educators and promoting academic exchanges of nursing education at home and abroad." These map directly onto the six domains of the developed criteria and are offered as the path to "further improve the education quality of nursing undergraduates."
