# Have students discover individual resistances rather than giving them from the outset

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A specific design move for circuit labs: instead of supplying the resistor values, the assignment asks students to infer them from equivalent-resistance measurements. The article records that "Kyle Rogers suggested that students should discover the individual resistances, instead of have them given from the outset." This turns a measurement exercise into an algebraic puzzle in which measured equivalent resistances become a system of equations to solve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- students must be able to measure equivalent resistances for at least three distinct configurations
#### Constraints
- the choice of resistor sets must satisfy the real-solution condition on the measured equivalent resistances

### Target Learners
- general physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- systems of equations applied to physical measurements
- series and parallel resistance concepts
