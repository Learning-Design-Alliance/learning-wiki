# Offer a for-credit seminar program where students attend seminars and either write a report or present one, building a majors community

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The department established a seminar program specifically to provide a meeting place for majors and faculty, with 1 credit of course work requiring attendance plus a written report or a presentation. Speakers included faculty from physics and other departments, physics majors presenting their research and internships, and outside speakers on research and careers. Students outside physics also signed up, and the group grew.
