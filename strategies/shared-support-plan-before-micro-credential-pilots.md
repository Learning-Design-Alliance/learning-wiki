# Share a full support plan—vision, choices, incentives, supports, and timeline—before micro-credential implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on dozens of pilots across six states, CTQ's Jennifer Barnett reports that "We find a great deal of success when a theory of action and a shared plan for support are present." The support plan should be shared with educators well ahead of a pilot and include the vision or 'why' for micro-credentials, the range of choices, the incentives, the supports available during submission, and the timeline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A theory of action and a shared plan for support must exist and be communicated ahead of implementation
#### Constraints
- Success is the organization's reported experience across pilots, not a measured comparison

### Target Learners
- teachers participating in micro-credential pilots

### Target Learning Goals
- successful earning and application of micro-credential competencies
