# Extend the friction laboratory to kinetic friction using the same set-up with video analysis software

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes a further quantitative step: measuring the coefficient of kinetic friction by recording the moving block with a camera and analysing the images with a video analysis tool such as the open-source Tracker software, thereby verifying Coulomb's law for friction. The same experimental set-up is reused in the dynamic case by hanging more mass. The article stresses that students need more solid laboratory training as a prerequisite.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- More solid training in lab as prerequisite
- Motion of the block recorded by a camera
- Video analysis tool (e.g. Tracker) for image elaboration
#### Constraints
- The same experimental set-up must be used with hanging more mass for the dynamic case

### Target Learners
- upper secondary school students, ages 15-19

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring the coefficient of kinetic friction
- verifying Coulomb's law for friction
