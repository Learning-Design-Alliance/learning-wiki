# Support youth with disabilities in transition through intensive case management, early work experiences, and a family focus

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents PROMISE's approach to supporting SSI youth during the transition to adulthood as a model for current initiatives: "intensive case management, facilitating early work experiences, and a family focus were the primary PROMISE innovations to usual services." Lessons from the evaluation are described as relevant to initiatives supporting youth with disabilities during transition to adulthood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal partnerships that are given time to form, service benchmarks, and regular communication
#### Constraints
- The intensive case management "might be difficult to sustain in the current system"; identifying SSI youth for outreach is challenging under the current system

### Target Learners
- Youth receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) during the transition to adulthood
- Youth with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for employment and successful transition to adulthood
