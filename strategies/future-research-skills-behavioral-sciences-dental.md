# Future research should evaluate active learning effects on skill development and behavioral sciences content using robust evaluation designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's conclusion directs future research toward gaps it identified. It states: "Future research should evaluate not only the impact of active learning strategies on satisfaction and knowledge acquisition, but also on skill development to support competency-based teaching and assessment in dental education." It further recommends that "active learning should be used and evaluated across all the main components of dental education, including behavioral and social sciences", and that journals encourage compliance with evaluation and reporting standards.
