# Use concept tests with peer instruction: poll challenging multiple-choice questions, then have students discuss and repoll

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The concept test/peer instruction model developed by Eric Mazur stops lectures every five to seven minutes to pose a challenging multiple-choice question with distracters replicating common student errors. Students answer individually, see the polling results, discuss with a classmate, and are repolled. The article reports evidence from Harvard and other institutions that, compared with traditional lectures, this improves student conceptual understanding and problem-solving skills.
