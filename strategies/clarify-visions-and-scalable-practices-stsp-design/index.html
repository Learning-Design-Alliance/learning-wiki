Bridge scientist-educator divides by clarifying visions of participation and identifying scalable practices¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In designing a Student-Teacher-Scientist Partnership focused on climate change, scientists and educators bridged the divide between scientific and learning goals by, first, clarifying each of their visions for student and teacher participation in the science, including roles, tasks and responsibilities, and second, identifying the specific practices of schooling and of climate change research that can be scaled to other classrooms while still partnering with local scientists. The commentary presents this as a research-evidence-based recommendation for navigating dual goals in CCS design.
