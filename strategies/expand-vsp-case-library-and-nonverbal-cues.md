# Expand the VSP case library, stratify difficulty, and add richer interactivity and non-verbal cue capture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendations for strengthening VSP-based psychiatric interview training. They state that "Future iterations will expand both the number and variety of psychiatric cases and will stratify difficulty levels to accommodate learners with differing competencies," and that impact "could be further strengthened by the integration of richer interactive functions, the expansion of case libraries, and refinements in the feedback mechanisms," including facial-expression recognition and eye-tracking technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- New cases require interview scripts refined through the modified Delphi expert consensus process used in this study
- Difficulty levels must be stratified to accommodate learners with differing competencies
#### Constraints
- The system currently comprises only three cases, which is insufficient for robust training
- It did not capture subtle emotional cues, such as gaze aversion or prosodic variation, nor fully evaluate non-verbal communication

### Target Learners
- postgraduate psychiatry students
- psychiatry standardized trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- psychiatric interview skills
- non-verbal communication assessment
- empathic capacity
