# Strengthen digital health systems to capture waist circumference and MASLD-specific fields for automated high-risk case detection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends modifying the National NCD Portal and NCD registers so that MASLD risk data are captured and acted upon automatically. It notes the portal "does not fully capture MASLD data or store actual WC values", and that the CBAC captures waist circumference for risk scoring but the numerical value is not retained or displayed on dashboards, preventing automated identification of individuals exceeding referral thresholds (> 80 cm for females; >90 cm for males). The recommended changes include MASLD-specific fields, automated alerts for high-risk cases, and monthly reporting integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Portal architecture modified to include MASLD-specific fields, automated alerts for high-risk cases, and monthly reporting integration
#### Constraints
- Community acceptance of WC measurement and worker training in culturally sensitive communication is essential, per the article

### Target Learners
- Health system administrators, NCD programme managers, and frontline health workers using the National NCD Portal and CBAC

### Target Learning Goals
- Proactive identification and referral of individuals at high MASLD risk using waist circumference thresholds
