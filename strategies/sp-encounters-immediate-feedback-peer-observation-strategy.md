# Use standardized patient encounters with immediate faculty feedback and peer observation for communication skills practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learners in small groups of 4–6 observe peers in SP encounters from outside OSCE rooms, with one resident participating per encounter. "Principles of deliberate practice were incorporated into the SP encounter, where the interaction was observed and learners received immediate, directed feedback from faculty." The SP provides 2 minutes of verbal feedback, followed by a formal faculty debrief.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- OSCE rooms with observation capability
- Faculty facilitators per case
- Trained SPs
- PEARLS Healthcare Debriefing Tool for debriefing
#### Constraints
- Observed patient encounters could be distressing to some learners; authors plan to provide evaluation checklists and peer feedback guidance

### Target Learners
- Medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Communication skills for delivering difficult news
