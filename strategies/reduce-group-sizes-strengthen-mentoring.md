# Reduce clinical group sizes and strengthen mentoring and feedback structures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends reducing the number of students in practice groups and strengthening mentoring systems and feedback mechanisms. Students recommended reducing student numbers (6 participants) and increasing instructor numbers (6 participants), citing that in large groups some students 'don't even get to touch a patient that day'. The article states crowded groups limited individual feedback and guidance quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
