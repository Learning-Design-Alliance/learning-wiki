# Report to Future Researchers: a cross-semester reflection assignment that feeds prior cohorts' advice into course onboarding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the final week, students write a 400–800 word report introducing incoming students to the course and project, addressing the research's purpose and significance, lessons learned, advice for future participants, and keys to success. The reports are deidentified and read by the next cohort in week one, with each student reading two reports individually before group discussion of themes and surprises. The article states that "The reports are incorporated into subsequent iterations of the course," and that the activity "is designed to continue across semesters, with each cohort engaging with reports produced by the previous class." In this study the reports also served as the qualitative dataset for evaluating student experience.
