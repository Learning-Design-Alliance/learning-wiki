# Ask students to justify each FCI answer in writing when deploying conceptual tests online

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend extending FCI deployment so that "students justify each of their answers", which they say "would allow for a more nuanced and less biased analysis that goes beyond understanding “misconceptions”". Combined with online platforms such as Blackboard, collecting written explanations would also let researchers study student engagement in the online learning environment through attendance, continuous-assessment marks and task completion times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online assessment platform able to collect written explanations alongside conceptual-test responses.
#### Constraints
- The authors frame this as future work motivated by the limitations of the current multiple-choice FCI format, not as a tested intervention.

### Target Learners
- First-year physics/engineering students in online or blended courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnosing conceptual understanding and misconceptions in Newtonian mechanics
