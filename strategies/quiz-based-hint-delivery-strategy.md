# Deliver escape room hints only after teams pass a self-assessed quiz on the subject matter

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends delivering hints through "an approach like the one described in Section II-F, which requires students to pass self-assessed quizzes in order to obtain hints." Teams request hints via the platform, must pass a five-question quiz (at least four correct) drawn from a question bank, and wait at least 5 minutes between hints. This approach covers concepts hard to address through puzzles and lets teachers adjust difficulty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A software platform that can administer quizzes and gate hint delivery automatically
#### Constraints
- Teams must wait a minimum of 5 minutes after receiving a hint before requesting another

### Target Learners
- higher-education students working in teams

### Target Learning Goals
- subject-matter concepts embedded in escape room puzzles
