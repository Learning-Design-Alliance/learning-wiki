Convene cross-functional generative AI task forces to guide ethical adoption decisions and procurement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's second use case shows Indian Prairie School District establishing a Generative Artificial Intelligence Task Force of teachers, administrators, principals, and district office representatives before deploying AI in 2024-25. Its working groups created a belief statement and guiding principles, designed professional learning, identified AI resources, and explored how to optimize student learning—implementing Recommendation 2 to 'strengthen the adoption and governance of AI-enabled edtech through cross-functional teams.' The task force presented progress to the Board of Education, producing endorsed policy updates.

Related Strategies¶

  • Set clear AI guardrails positioning AI as a complement to human instruction with age-appropriate progressions
  • Establish AI-specific accountability systems with iterative playbooks, family guides, and continuous evaluation cycles
  • Ask ethics, bias, and equity questions before adopting an AI system
  • Districts should build community-shared visions, evaluation infrastructure, and policies for procuring and using AI edtech
  • Structure OBC procurement around intentionality questions on learner goals, populations, outcomes, and stakeholders
  • Embed AI policy literacy, peer-led communities of practice, and shared AI resources into professional development
