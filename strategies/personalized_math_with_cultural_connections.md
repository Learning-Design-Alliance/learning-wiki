# Personalized Math with Cultural Connections

## Description
Effective math instruction connects to real-life and culturally relevant contexts, engaging students and making concepts more accessible. Incorporating hip-hop, sports, or other student interests makes learning more relevant and sincere.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of student interests, ability to create relatable examples, and willingness to adapt instructional methods.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful consideration to ensure sincerity and avoid appearing insincere. May require additional resources to support culturally relevant examples.
