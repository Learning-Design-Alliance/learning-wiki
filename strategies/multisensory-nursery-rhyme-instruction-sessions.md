# Deliver explicit nursery rhyme instruction through multisensory visual, auditory, tactile-kinesthetic and hands-on activities in short weekly sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention gave eleven pre-K children explicit instruction on ten nursery rhymes using diverse learning styles: posters and rebus books, reciting and singing, role play and readers' theatre with props and puppets, and prop boxes and felt boards. The author reports "The phonological awareness training was conducted for 15-20 minutes once per week over a period of ten weeks," and this training produced significant gains on both measures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions require materials such as rebus posters, prop boxes, felt boards, puppets and simple costumes to support recall and dramatization
#### Constraints
- The measure used only Euro-American nursery rhymes, so cultural and language differences were not taken into account

### Target Learners
- three-year-old pre-K children in an inclusive preschool

### Target Learning Goals
- nursery rhyme recall
- rhyme awareness
- beginning sound awareness
- phonological awareness
