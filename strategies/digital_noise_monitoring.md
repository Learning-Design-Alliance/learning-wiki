# Digital Noise Monitoring

## Description
Digital noise monitoring involves using apps or devices like Super Sound Box, Class Dojo, or Too Noisy to detect and signal excessive noise levels in the classroom. These tools provide auditory or visual cues when the noise exceeds a predetermined threshold, helping students self-regulate their behavior and maintain a quieter learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to digital devices or apps, setup and calibration of noise monitoring tools, clear explanation of noise level expectations.
#### Constraints
- May be distracting or disruptive if not implemented properly, requires technical setup and maintenance, some students may ignore the cues.
