# Use instructor-led reflective debriefing and role-play with standardized patients to prepare students for the transition to clinical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors argue that role-play with standardized patients and guided debriefing sessions equip students with strategies to manage stressful or negative clinical experiences: "role-play with standardized patients and guided debriefing sessions equip students with strategies to manage stressful or negative clinical experiences, thereby fostering resilience and maintaining confidence in their clinical skills". The module functioned as a structured, psychologically safe space that substituted for live supervision before clinical immersion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-led debriefing sessions at each simulation station
- Trained standardized patients and fully scripted scenarios
#### Constraints
- The hidden curriculum in high-pressure clinical environments can undermine formal professionalism training if taught values and observed practices mismatch

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate dental students transitioning to clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- professional behaviors such as empathy, ethics, and teamwork
- resilience and confidence before clinical immersion
