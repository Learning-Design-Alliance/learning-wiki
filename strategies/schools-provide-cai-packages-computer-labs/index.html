Strategies

Provide CAI packages across school subjects in school computer labs with dedicated time, staffing and funding¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that schools adopt CAI packages beyond the tested physics unit: they state that "CAI packages on various topics of science can be prepared and given to students" and that schools "should come forward to provide CAI packages on various school subjects in computer labs of schools". Supporting actions include allocating more time for computer periods, heads of institutions insisting teachers prepare CAI packages, and government funds for buying computers and maintaining proper computer labs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School computer labs
  • More time allocated for computer periods
  • Teachers prepared to prepare CAI packages
  • Government funding for buying computers

Constraints¶

  • Study evidence comes from a single urban-centre experiment on one physics unit with English-medium IX standard students of the Tamil Nadu State Board

Target Learners¶

  • IX standard students in Tamil Nadu State Board schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Learning school subject content, especially science, through computer assisted instruction

Claims¶

  • The CAI package on Work, Power and Energy significantly improved physics achievement for boys in the IX standard experimental group [+W]
  • The CAI package on Work, Power and Energy significantly improved physics achievement for girls in the IX standard experimental group, significant at the 0.05 level only [+W]
  • Learning the physics unit Work, Power and Energy through a CAI package produced significantly higher posttest achievement than traditional teaching among IX standard students [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Expose physics students to computer-assisted STAD cooperative instruction to improve performance
  • Establish an ICT Resource Centre under each district's School Management Board with production, research-assessment and technical sections
