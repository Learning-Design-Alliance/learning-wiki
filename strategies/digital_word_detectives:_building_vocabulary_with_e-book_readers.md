# Digital Word Detectives: Building Vocabulary With e-Book Readers

## Description
Students read e-books and use digital tools (dictionaries and notes) to support their vocabulary development. They assume the role of "word detectives" as they look up words in digital dictionaries and use other strategies to identify the meaning of vocabulary words. This requires students to have prior knowledge of e-book reading and familiarity with the specific tools and features of their digital readers, including how to access and use the built-in digital dictionary and how to insert digital notes into an e-book. Students work in small groups to compare digital notes and discuss word meanings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital readers, e-books compatible with digital readers, access to digital dictionaries and note-taking tools, vocabulary pre-assessments and post-assessments, vocabulary rubric, observation form, digital reading tools assessment.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to digital devices and reliable internet connectivity. Students need prior knowledge of e-book reading and digital tool usage.

### Personalization
- Differentiated instruction through selection of appropriate e-books, flexibility in selecting vocabulary words, and options for independent or partner reading.
