# Combine online modules with hands-on simulation training to teach POCUS-guided procedural skills to novices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article outlines a structured, competency-based training framework combining five online modules with in-person hands-on training. Students were given shared online access to five online modules covering the modified Seldinger technique, in-plane and out-of-plane ultrasound technique, principles of pleural space disease, and SBWGTT insertion for PE and PN. Completion of a corresponding self-assessment for each module was required before moving on. This was followed by in-person, hands-on training with simulation models and deliberate, guided feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online modules with self-assessment quizzes
- In-person hands-on training with simulation models
- Deliberate and guided feedback from experienced instructors
#### Constraints
- Students were given 1 month to complete online training
- The in-person training sessions lacked available cadavers for practicing landmark-guided placement

### Target Learners
- Novice veterinary students

### Target Learning Goals
- POCUS-guided SBWGTT placement
- Modified Seldinger technique
- Pleural space disease recognition
