# Practice Responsible Decision-Making

## Description
Give students opportunities to practice responsible decision-making by using pairs or small groups to analyze problem scenarios (e.g., cheating, bullying) that connect to their everyday lives at school, in order to make decisions about, including analyzing the situation and possible consequences. Have them share their decisions with the whole class and reflect on how they arrived at them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problem scenarios, time for students to analyze the scenarios, and a way for students to share their decisions and reflect on how they arrived at them.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to create and facilitate the activity.
