# Drawingvoice 2.0: a three-phase method combining small-group collaborative drawing, Facebook sharing, and face-to-face re-discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawingvoice 2.0 is an instructional method in which "group activities, collaborative pencil and paper drawing (Cigoli, 2018; Fuks, 2009) and asynchronous communication are combined". Phase 1 is classroom discussion and joint drawing in small groups; Phase 2 shares the produced materials on a course Facebook group for re-elaboration and comparison; Phase 3 is a face-to-face re-discussion of the online materials and of the whole process. The authors propose it as an awareness-raising method for professional decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher acting as facilitator conducts the group sessions
- A Facebook account is created specifically for the training to share materials produced in class
- Drawing materials (sheets, posters, colours, pencils, pens) are provided
#### Constraints
- Originally designed for face-to-face classroom work with online sharing, not fully online delivery

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- reflexivity
- self-awareness about training and professional pathways
