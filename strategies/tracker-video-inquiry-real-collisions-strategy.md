# Use Tracker video analysis of real collisions to connect simulation concepts to real-life applications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Responding to the FGD theme that the computer model alone did not relate to real life, the authors recommend that "Readers could explore use of Tracker ... to allow students to inquire into videos of real collisions for a stronger connection of scientific concepts to real life applications". Tracker is a free video analysis and modeling tool for physics education cited from Brown (2012) and the authors' own prior work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the free Tracker video analysis and modeling tool and video recordings of real collisions.
#### Constraints
- Stated as a suggestion to readers, not as an intervention tested in this study.

### Target Learners
- Secondary and junior college physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Connect Newtonian collision concepts to real-life applications
