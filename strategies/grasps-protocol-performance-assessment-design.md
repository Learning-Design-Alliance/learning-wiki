## Description
For performance or authentic assessment, the article offers the GRASPS protocol as a reliable process prompting teachers to consider epistemic aims in task construction. It states: "the GRASPS protocol (goal, role, audience, situation, product or performance, standards for success) prompts the teacher to consider the goal of the performance assessment and the evaluation criteria to determine performance." The protocol also guides development of a storyline including the role students assume and the product they create.
