# Structured mentorship system in which trained nurse mentors deliver EBP education, bedside teaching and ongoing monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Ten ICU nurses were recruited as mentors, trained for one week by the research team (lectures, simulation, a skill video), then tasked with delivering evidence-based education, bedside teaching and monitoring of study nurses throughout the intervention. The article states mentors "were responsible for delivering evidence-based education, conducting bedside teaching in the ICU, and monitoring the participating ICU nurses throughout the intervention." The authors credit mentoring with supporting behavioral change and sustaining engagement during implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentors with active licenses, ≥2 years clinical experience, clinical management capability, IT proficiency, communication skills and English fluency
- Organizational support and resources for mentors to continue supervision after training
#### Constraints
- Mentors also evaluated the nurses, which the authors note introduces observational bias

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses and their trained peer mentors in low-resource tertiary hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained application of evidence-based VAP prevention practices in daily clinical care
