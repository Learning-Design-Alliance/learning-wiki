Transform Mesnevî stories into structured classroom activities ending with homework¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The researchers converted each Mesnevî story into classroom events by asking students questions about the lesson to be taken from the story and structuring activities under fixed headings. The article states that "all the stories were transformed into events under the headings of brainstorming, sentence completion, drama, box of values, our teacher talking, values tree enthusiasts, and at the end of the events homework was given to the students." This recipe operationalised story-based values education across all five target values.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A story matched to each target value
  • Activity headings such as brainstorming, drama and sentence completion
  • Homework assigned at the end of events

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • 4th grade primary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • values acquisition
  • attitude change towards hospitality, philanthropy, patriotism, family unity and tolerance

Claims¶

  • Mesnevî Supported Values Education produced significantly higher posttest attitudes towards the five targeted values than normal curriculum instruction in 4th graders [+W]
  • Posttest attitude differences between experimental and control groups did not vary by gender [+W]
  • Posttest attitude differences did not vary by family income level [+W]
  • Posttest attitude differences did not vary by parents' educational level [+W]
  • The classroom teacher judged the application successful and positive, recommending its extension to all lessons [+W]
