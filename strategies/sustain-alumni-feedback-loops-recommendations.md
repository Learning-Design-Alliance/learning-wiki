# Sustain alumni feedback loops through strategic planning integration, cross-functional interpretation teams, transparent value-exchanges, and phased data collection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's recommendations for practice distill lessons from its multi-year study into four actions: integrate alumni surveys directly into district strategic planning; establish cross-functional teams to collaboratively interpret and act on alumni data; implement transparent feedback loops and value-exchanges to sustain alumni networks; and adopt a phased approach to data collection to maximize resources and prevent survey fatigue. These target leaders seeking to institutionalize alumni engagement beyond a one-off survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational alignment on motivations and a clear purpose, since building these feedback loops is rarely easy
#### Constraints
- Moving beyond logistics is needed so efforts translate into genuine institutional change

### Target Learners
- Educational leaders in districts and high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Building sustainable, mutually beneficial alumni networks and feedback loops
