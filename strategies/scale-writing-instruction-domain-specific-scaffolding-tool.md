# Use a domain-specific scaffolding environment to scale academic writing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends addressing high labor costs and increasing student numbers in writing instruction through a technological solution: "academic writing and its instruction can be scaled (i.e., designed more efficiently) using a technological solution." Thesis Writer embodies this by scaffolding planning and genre mastery rather than only giving feedback, allowing instructors to support more students in group assignment settings.
