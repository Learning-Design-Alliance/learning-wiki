# Provide dedicated nutrition counselling slots, extended consultations and postgraduate nutrition pathways including lifestyle medicine curricula and nutrition subspecialties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review recommends allocating more time and resources for nutrition within clinical settings, noting GP clinics "might need to be designed such as to include dedicated slots for nutrition counselling for patients", supported by government funding. It also advocates postgraduate pathways such as lifestyle medicine curricula teaching motivational interviewing and exercise prescription, and new nutrition and obesity subspecialties to produce doctor nutrition subspecialists.
