# Teacher acts as a non-knower co-participant who attributes boredom to the materials rather than the students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
During EDA mediation the teacher steps out of the authority position and acts as "a non-knower co-participant in the interactions who is seeking further information from her students". When learners show signs of boredom she blames the materials or herself rather than the students, saying things like "this question might be kind of boring. Let's get through it together", and encourages persistence on difficult tasks with reassurance that the class can get through it together. The strategy keeps affective rapport intact while mediating assessment tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher must be the same instructor familiar with the learners and willing to cede the authority position during mediation
