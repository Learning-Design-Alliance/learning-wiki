# Use augmented reality in existing buildings to increase realism and obtain more reliable fire-behavior data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a future development, the authors recommend "the use of augmented reality to particularize cases in specific buildings", arguing that "Capturing behavioral data through the use of augmented reality in existing buildings can increase the user’s sense of realism and therefore obtain more reliable data". They add that augmented reality would avoid the use of the platform for travel within the space and make the experience more realistic.
