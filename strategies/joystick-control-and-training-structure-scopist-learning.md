# Use joystick-controlled flexible laparoscopes and structured training together to shorten the scopist learning curve in advanced laparoscopy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that camera-system design is a lever for training efficiency: "joystick-controlled flexible laparoscopy may attenuate the scopist learning curve compared with conventional dual-lever control, suggesting that both training structure and camera system design are important levers for safer and more efficient dissemination of advanced laparoscopic liver surgery." The strategy pairs ergonomic controller choice with structured camera-navigation training for novice scopists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Novice scopists learning flexible-scope camera navigation in laparoscopic surgery
#### Constraints
- Evidence is retrospective, single-center, single-surgeon, with limited subgroup sample sizes and operation time as the only learning-curve surrogate

### Target Learners
- Newly learning scopists (less than 6 months of experience) in laparoscopic liver surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition of stable camera navigation and exposure skills in laparoscopic liver resection
