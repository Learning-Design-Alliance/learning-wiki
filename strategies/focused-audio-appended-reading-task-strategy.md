# Use focused audio-appended reading tasks with highlighted target forms to promote L2 grammatical accuracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers and task designers use implicit focus-on-form within focused audio-appended reading tasks: texts with the target structures highlighted, played with audio, re-read by the teacher with intonation changes and repetition, followed by exercises practicing the highlighted forms. The authors state teachers "can use implicit FOF instruction along with focused and unfocused audio -appended reading tasks to promote their learners' grammatical knowledge".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reading texts with target forms highlighted, accompanying audio files, teacher emphasis during re-reading, and post-reading exercises on the target forms
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one ten-session quasi-experiment with ninety intermediate female learners in an Iranian institute; generalization to other populations is not tested

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners in formal classroom settings

### Target Learning Goals
- grammatical accuracy in production (passive voice, prepositions)
