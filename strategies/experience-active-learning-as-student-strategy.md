# Provide instructors opportunities to experience active learning as students (e.g., co-teaching, instructional development teams) to build their use of it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because instructors with prior exposure to active learning as students or student instructors lecture less, the article recommends that "it is likely valuable to find ways for them to get such experience, such as co-teaching or participation in a local instructional development team." It frames such exposure as "the beginning of a cultural change in higher education." This strategy targets the individual factor the survey found most clearly linked to use of active learning among instructor characteristics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires institutional leaders and change agents to learn about instructors' past experiences with active learning and how to build on them
#### Constraints
- Most instructors in the survey sample (75%) had no prior exposure to active learning, so building experience requires creating new opportunities

### Target Learners
- Postsecondary introductory STEM instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- Adoption of active learning instructional strategies by instructors
