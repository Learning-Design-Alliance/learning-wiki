## Description
The article recommends that courses covering the proposed technology trends be integrated into the curriculum using project-based learning, citing Dym et al. (2005). This makes the trend topics implementable as design projects rather than lecture-only content, consistent with the article's emphasis on scenario-based, application-specific system design in IoT and wearable courses. The article states: "Önerilen teknoloji eğilimlerinin olduğu dersler proje tabanlı öğrenme (Dym vd. 2005) ile müfredata eklenmelidir."
