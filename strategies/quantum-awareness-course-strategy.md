# Offer a one- or two-semester quantum awareness course for engineers and computer scientists, in hardware and software tracks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Companies in the study requested that higher education provide "a one or two semester course in order to increase quantum awareness" for engineers and computer scientists, with two versions: a hardware track (device physics, qubits, error correction, control electronics) geared to electrical, mechanical, and optical engineering students, and a quantum software track (algorithms, applications, programming languages) focused on software engineering and computer science students. This widens the recruiting pool beyond the currently dominant physics Ph.D. route.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors or institutions able to offer a one or two semester course in quantum information basics
#### Constraints
- The request came from the 33% of interviewed companies asking for quantum awareness courses; content value depends on industry demand and institutional expertise as the industry evolves

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students
- undergraduate and graduate computer science and software engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic quantum awareness sufficient to enter the quantum industry workforce
