# Relevant Vocabulary Instruction

## Description
Instructors should present vocabulary that is useful and/or necessary for learners, based on their learning goals. One way to do this is to ask learners to identify vocabulary topics they want to learn in class, then compile the most common suggestions. For students who need to learn vocabulary topics not of general interest (e.g., writing a CV/resume), instructors can assign individual homework assignments and suggest online resources. After instructors teach a new word, learners should use it in their own words, such as in a conversation, role play, or original sentence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires pre-planning to determine most useful vocabulary topics, access to relevant resources, time for practice and conversation
#### Constraints
- Requires instructor to tailor instruction to meet diverse needs, may be time-consuming
