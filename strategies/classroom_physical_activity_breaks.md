# Classroom Physical Activity Breaks

## Description
Integrating movement-based activities into the classroom to energize learners, relax and calm a class, or redirect attention. These breaks are short (1-5 minutes) and should be implemented 2-3 times per day. Encourages teachers to get out of their seats and participate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires 1–5 minutes of class time, incorporation of school health team, school wellness policy knowledge and administrator approval. Minimal equipment.
#### Constraints
- Some activities may not be suitable for all learners. Requires teacher participation and enthusiasm.
