Six recommendations for school and district leaders to create equitable SEAD learning environments¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report outlines six recommendations: (1) provide meaningful professional development and supports, (2) engage parents, students, and communities as full partners, (3) diversify the workforce, (4) ensure equitable access to and supports for success in rigorous and culturally sustaining coursework, (5) develop inclusive discipline and dress code policies, and (6) provide access to integrated wraparound services and supports. These span changing adult beliefs and mindsets and changing systems and policies.

Context¶

Requirements¶
