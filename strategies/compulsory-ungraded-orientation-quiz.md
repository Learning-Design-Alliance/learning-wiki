# Use a compulsory but ungraded quiz to verify student familiarity with orientation material

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes that the TNE online module's quiz be compulsory but excluded from grade calculation. If "the corresponding quiz is compulsory (but has no influence on grades), the teaching staff can have much greater confidence that the students are really familiar with the corresponding TNE material". This addresses the problem that existing orientation events and webpages are passive experiences with no examination of whether students absorbed the material.
