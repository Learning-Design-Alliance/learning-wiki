# Maintain professional development and educator collaboration throughout an edtech pilot

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete implementation practice for district pilots: provide professional development before and during the pilot and build in educator collaboration. In Cajon Valley, 100 percent of responding educators participated in professional development averaging 2 to 3 hours and nearly 80 percent said it prepared them to pilot the tool; in Vista, over 40 percent reported training under an hour and nearly half said it only slightly prepared them. The report advises districts to "maintain professional development opportunities throughout the pilot and consider opportunities for educator collaboration when designing the pilot."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District commitment of time and resources for initial training and ongoing support from the product company throughout the pilot
#### Constraints
- Where training was brief (Vista), educators reported feeling unprepared, and coaches struggled to interpret the tool's data

### Target Learners
- Educators and coaches implementing edtech with English Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective classroom implementation of piloted edtech tools for English language development
