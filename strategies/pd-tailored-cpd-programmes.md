# Continuing professional development programmes tailored to personality disorder management, including short informal awareness sessions and simulation learning, should be evaluated for effectiveness.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy holds that continuing professional development programmes tailored to personality disorder management should be developed and evaluated for effectiveness in improving trainee ANPs' confidence and competence. Participants demonstrated high motivation for future professional development, suggesting that short, informal awareness sessions or videos would be useful, especially for busy schedules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CPD programmes tailored to personality disorder management
- Ongoing training and simulation learning opportunities
#### Constraints
- Further research is needed to evaluate the effectiveness of targeted educational interventions; this study did not evaluate the intervention

### Target Learners
- postgraduate nursing students in Advanced Nurse Practitioner pathways
