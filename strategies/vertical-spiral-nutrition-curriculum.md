# Integrate nutrition throughout medical training using a vertical spiral curriculum with practical teaching methods such as cooking lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends curricular innovations that embed nutrition across all years rather than as an isolated component. It highlights a University of Cambridge initiative where "a vertical spiral curriculum was introduced so that students could develop their nutrition competencies and build on them every year". Practical methods such as cooking lessons and use of the hidden curriculum across biochemistry and physiology are advocated to build confidence and personal health habits.
