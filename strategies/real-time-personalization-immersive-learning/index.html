Incorporate real-time personalization in immersive learning environments for future deployments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on formative evaluation of Kid Space, the authors identified "the need for real-time personalization" as an actionable insight for improving future deployments. They plan to "deploy Kid Space with real-time personalization features for longer periods with more students."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sensing and sensemaking infrastructure capable of adapting responses to individual children in real time

Constraints¶

  • Identified through a preliminary formative study with eight students

Target Learners¶

  • first-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustained engagement during play-based learning

Claims¶

  • First-graders using Kid Space demonstrated high levels of engagement during play-based learning [+W]
  • Kid Space use shifted children's attention away from the screen toward the physical environment [+W]
  • First-graders using Kid Space showed physical activity during play-based learning, in an exploratory case study without a comparison [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Blend digital and physical learning experiences to address screen-time concerns in early childhood technology
  • Future improvements: real-world user studies, advanced student modeling, and spaced repetition
