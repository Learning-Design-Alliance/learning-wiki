# Applied Science Testing

## Description
Applied science testing involves using scientific principles to evaluate real-world materials and products. Learners test the solubility of different packing materials and compare the quality and price of different kinds of plastic kitchen wrap. These activities improve evaluative skills and understanding of the application of science in daily life.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Packing materials, plastic wraps, water, measuring tools
#### Constraints
- Requires access to various materials and products. Evaluation criteria need to be well-defined.
