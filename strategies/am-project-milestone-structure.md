# Milestone structure for team innovation projects with early team formation and provisional patent filing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A staged milestone schedule that pulls students into the capstone early. The article states: "During the first week, we asked the students form project teams based on common interests, and to brainstorm project concepts as they complete the introductory lectures and laboratories during the first four weeks." The first milestone is a project proposal describing the concept, importance, literature review, rough semester goals, and needed skills and resources; the second is a midterm review with refined proposal, initial analysis, proof-of-concept experiments, and concrete final-prototype objectives. Teams also met the MIT Technology Licensing Office so provisional patents could be filed when appropriate prior to the final expo.
