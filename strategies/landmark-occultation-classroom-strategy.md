# Use landmark occultations of bright stars and planets, filmed with ordinary cameras, as an adaptable classroom introduction to occultation astronomy and astrometry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to stage an occultation-like event in which a landmark (a building dome) plays the role of the Moon and a bright star or planet is the target, filmed with an ordinary camcorder or mobile phone. The author states this "occultation-like phenomenon is useful for introducing to occultation astronomy a class of student up to university level" and that "the observation can be easily organized at convenience of the audience". Frame-by-frame inspection of the video lets students see parallax, scintillation and stellar-diameter effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A bright star or planet visible near a suitable landmark from the observation position
- An ordinary camcorder or mobile phone camera capable of video recording
#### Constraints
- Arcturus is not in the Zodiacal Catalogue so a lunar occultation is impossible for it; the landmark event substitutes for one
- The stellar diameter effect for Arcturus (1.3 ms) is below the 1/30 s video sampling time, so it is not resolvable with ordinary camcorders

### Target Learners
- astronomy students up to university level

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding stellar occultations
- positional astronomy and astrometry
- signal analysis of astronomical video
