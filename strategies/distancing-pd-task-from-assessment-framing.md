# PD facilitation design that distances collaborative content discussion from assessment contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The facilitators deliberately reduced socio-emotional threat in the CKT-E activity through several design choices: framing the task loosely, abstaining from the conversation, positioning themselves as peers rather than authorities on physics teaching, and asking teachers for feedback on the questions' value for future PD. As they state, "The collaborative activity, task framing of providing feedback on the value of these questions for future PD, and the teaching scenario context of the CKT-E questions were all employed to distance the activity from typical assessment contexts." They also intentionally selected a partner for the sole pre-service teacher with whom she got along well socially.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators willing to cede authority and abstain from the discussion
- Attention to pairing and group composition
#### Constraints
- Design was implemented in one PD partnership with a small group of four experienced teachers and one pre-service teacher

### Target Learners
- Secondary science teachers in mixed-experience PD programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Willingness to share thinking and (mis)understanding in collaborative content discussion

### Affordances
- Comfort Building Moves Construct
