# Use the Flow State Scale to evaluate the efficacy of training programs in multidisciplinary teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose administering the Flow State Scale before-and-after successive training models as an evaluation instrument for multidisciplinary team training. They write that "The data open the way to the usefulness of FSS as a trustworthy tool for the evaluation of the efficacy of training programs in a multidisciplinary team", based on the observed M1-to-M2 improvements in involvement-related subscales among 43 perinatal practitioners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration of the FSS after each training model or scenario
- Multidisciplinary team composition of trainees
#### Constraints
- The authors note the need to correlate psychological stress levels with video-computerized evaluation programs and to investigate a wider study-population

### Target Learners
- Multidisciplinary perinatal teams (physicians, midwives, nurses)

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating training program efficacy for delivery-room management and resuscitation

## Claims

- [In a cohort of 43 practitioners, three Flow State Scale subscales — merging of action and awareness, unambiguous feedback, and loss of self-consciousness — had significantly higher self-reported scores at the second than the first high-fidelity simulation course in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/hfs-improves-fss-subscales-delivery-room.md) [+W]
- [Most Flow State Scale measures showed no significant change between the two simulation courses: 29 of 36 items and 5 of 9 subscales did not differ significantly](../claims/most-fss-measures-unchanged.md) [~W]
- [Seven of 36 individual self-rated Flow State Scale item scores were significantly higher at the end of M2 than M1 in one uncontrolled pre-post study of practitioners, chiefly items concerning performance awareness and reduced self-consciousness](../claims/seven-fss-items-improved-m1-m2.md) [+W]
