# Use scale-visualization imagery to convey astronomical magnitudes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The book deploys visual scale devices so learners can grasp otherwise incomprehensible astronomical ranges: its cover is "Extracto de una ilustración a escala logarítmica del universo observable" (Pablo Carlos Budassi's logarithmic-scale illustration), and an entire inquiry activity is devoted to "API 2: Escalas en el sistema solar". Combining a logarithmic image with a hands-on scaling activity gives teachers a two-step strategy: first a compressed visual overview of cosmic scale, then an active lesson constructing solar-system distances at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a logarithmic-scale universe illustration and materials for a solar-system scaling activity
#### Constraints
- The printed solar-system figures in the book note 'Distancias no a escala', so static diagrams alone must not be relied on for scale understanding

### Target Learners
- school students developing size and distance intuition

### Target Learning Goals
- astronomical scales
- relative sizes and distances in the solar system
