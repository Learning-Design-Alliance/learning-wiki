# Implement a 2-week CDIO-PBL rotation cycle combining PBL case-based theoretical clinics with a conceive-design-implement-operate skills cycle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable teaching recipe for clinical rotations: teams of 8–10 students rotate every two weeks; theoretical learning uses weekly teaching clinics with real patient cases, group PowerPoint presentations, and class discussion; skills training follows the four CDIO phases, with procedures taught under instructor supervision using simulation training models that provide "a safe, controlled, and repeatable environment". At each cycle's end teachers assess effectiveness and refine the next cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Eight instructors acting as facilitators for case information, supervision, guidance, and support
- Simulation training models and access to suitable teaching cases
- Communication channels between students and instructors (WeChat or face-to-face meetings in this study)
#### Constraints
- The authors state the study "is limited to one cohort of students in an 8-year system from one educational institution, which makes it difficult to generalize the study results to students in other disciplines"

### Target Learners
- Medical students and trainees in clinical obstetrics rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- Obstetric theoretical knowledge
- Procedural and clinical skills such as fetal heart rate monitoring, Bishop score evaluation, and episiotomy and suturing
