# Use clinical pause points during simulation debriefing to promote timely knowledge construction and clinical judgement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review reports Kacalek et al.'s clinical pause approach in simulation debriefing, finding that "the clinical pause facilitated timely knowledge construction, contextual thinking, and clinical judgement of students". Teaching staff perceived the approach provided timely feedback and reflection-in-action, and the timing of the pause promoted "rich discussion and robust learning based on recent recall instead of remote recollection". The strategy embeds pause points within simulation for immediate engagement.
