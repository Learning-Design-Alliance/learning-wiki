# Frame sex education for college men around responsibility for younger boys and leadership in others' character development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review reports that Galloway's handbook rests on the idea that a college man is more likely to develop a sound personal standpoint regarding sex when he takes responsibility for younger boys and prepares for leadership in their character development. This is an implementable framing strategy: position the learner as a guide for others rather than only as an individual recipient of instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must be given a responsibility toward younger boys and prepared for leadership in the character development of others.
#### Constraints
- The book is intended primarily for the use of college men, though the reviewer notes it should find an audience in other circles.

### Target Learners
- college men

### Target Learning Goals
- developing a satisfying point of view in matters pertaining to sex
- character development
