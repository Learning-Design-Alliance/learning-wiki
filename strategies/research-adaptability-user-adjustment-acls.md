# Dedicate research effort to adaptability of ACLS systems so users can adjust the collaborative situation to their needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "More research effort should be dedicated to the adaptability of ACLS systems (i.e. the possibility for users to adjust the collaborative learning situation to their current needs or goals by making active choices)". This counters the risk that optimizing adaptivity overemphasizes the system's role rather than empowering students and teachers. The authors point to mirroring systems that let students adjust their collaboration from visualizations, script fading that shifts from system-regulation to co-regulation, and first attempts at adaptable collaboration scripts built from interoperable components.
