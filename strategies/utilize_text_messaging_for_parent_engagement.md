# Utilize Text Messaging for Parent Engagement

## Description
Text messaging is used as a primary means of communication for educators and community leaders to engage parents. Studies show high rates of parent engagement and positive outcomes for middle school students and preschoolers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a texting platform, contact information for parents, and willingness to send regular updates.
#### Constraints
- Some parents may not have access to or be comfortable with texting. Text messages can be easily missed or ignored.

### Affordances
- Easy, accessible, and familiar communication method for most parents. Provides quick updates and reminders. Can break down language barriers.

### Personalization
- Messages can be tailored to specific students or groups of parents. Translation features support multilingual families.
