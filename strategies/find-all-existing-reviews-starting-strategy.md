# Start a literature-review course by having students find all existing reviews on the topic to expose the gap and teach search tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The starting point of the course was to have students find all existing reviews on the subject. This introduced the research form of literature review and, as a by-product, "enables an interesting discussion on various search tools and the use of up-stream and down-stream citations". Finding that the more than 50 existing reviews were all very focused, with none comprehensive on the experimental properties of graphene, directly motivated the course's own review article. This addresses the observation that postgraduate students often underestimate the need for critique in existing literature.
