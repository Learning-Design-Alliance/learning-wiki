# Integrate nature (greenspace) into schools to enhance students' mental, physical and social wellbeing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that findings "support the integration of nature in schools to enhance the mental, physical and social wellbeing of children and adolescents". Implementable forms include outdoor learning in forests, school gardens, green schoolyard renovations, and nature walks or camps. Schools are "ideally placed to facilitate nature activities that students may not otherwise experience", and school greenspace "bridges the health equity gap" for students lacking greenspace access.
