# Avoid plagiarism by paraphrasing in your own words and citing generously

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The guide devotes a section to avoiding plagiarism, defined as copying, using, distributing or presenting someone else's intellectual property as your own or without proper acknowledgement. Recommended practices include expanding vocabulary with sources like the Manchester Academic Phrasebank while aiming for clarity and simplicity, using attribution phrases such as 'As argued/discussed/suggested by Author et al. (year)', never taking notes by copy-pasting statements including from past publications, and not saving on citations while checking that a cited publication indeed supports the statement.
