# Deliver PA Lessons in small groups of 4-5 students with one teacher, supported by 90-minute virtual training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article specifies the delivery conditions under which the program is intended to work: it is "Best used in small group settings with 1 teacher to 4-5 students", teachers receive a "90-minute virtual training", and no technology is required. These staffing, training and technology requirements describe how schools implemented the program in the studies that produced the reported reading gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small group settings with 1 teacher to 4-5 students
- 90-minute virtual training for teachers
#### Constraints
- No technology required, so delivery depends on teacher-led small-group time

### Target Learners
- Pre-K through 1st grade students in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- phonological awareness
