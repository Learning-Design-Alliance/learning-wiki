# Phased implementation strategy for adopting design-thinking flipped classrooms: pilot small, train teachers, provide demonstrations and feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's closing recommendations give educators an adoption pathway for the model. It advises that "educators need to ensure the support of online platforms and multimedia tools as well as specialized training for teachers in design thinking and flipped classrooms," and to "implement it first in a small scope of chapters or classes to reduce the difficulty for teachers and students to adapt." Teaching demonstrations and feedback mechanisms should then help teachers optimize strategies and enhance student participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support for online platforms and multimedia tools; teacher training in design thinking and flipped classrooms
#### Constraints
- Recommended as a staged adoption path; the article warns that content diversity, differing teaching needs, and environmental adaptability must be addressed when promoting the model

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate clinical medicine students in morphology-related courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustainable adoption of design-thinking flipped teaching
- Improved student participation
