# Multisensory Memory Strategies for Irregular Words

## Description
Multisensory memory strategies for irregular words involve using multiple senses (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and tactile) to enhance memory and retention of irregularly spelled words. These strategies include grouping words with memorable similarities, calling attention to the odd parts of words, repeated practice through copying and saying letters, discussing irregularities, and using mnemonics. This approach is beneficial because irregular words do not follow typical phoneme-grapheme correspondences, making them difficult to spell using standard phonics rules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- List of irregular words, multisensory materials (e.g., letter tiles, sand tray), direct instruction from a teacher or tutor, and consistent practice
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming, especially for learners with numerous irregular words to learn. Requires explicit and systematic instruction. May not be suitable for learners who prefer a more traditional, rule-based approach to spelling.

- Adaptable to various learning styles by emphasizing different sensory modalities (visual learners may focus on visualizing the word, auditory learners on saying the letters aloud, kinesthetic learners on writing the word)
