# Extend quantum chemistry coursework to thermodynamics by computing reaction S, H, and G with the Thermodynamics function and Hess's Law

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The package's utility 'extends beyond only explicit electronic structure applications': students use the Thermodynamics function to calculate S, H, and G for each species in methane combustion, retrieve 3D geometries from PubChem via the MolecularData function, and combine species values using Hess's Law to obtain the reaction entropy, enthalpy, and Gibbs free energy. Results can then be compared with tabulated standard formation values.
