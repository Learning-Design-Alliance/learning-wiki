# Journals should actively solicit replication studies, meta-analyses, and large-scale impact reports

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The editorial's core recommendation is that a journal explicitly welcome and publish replication studies, meta-analytic syntheses, and reports of large-scale, sustainable, systemic impact. ETR&D "hereby issues this call for papers reporting replication studies and studies reporting large-scale, sustainable, systemic impact on educational practice", accepting both success stories and reports of problems and shortfalls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Submission must follow journal guidelines and meet the publication standards of ETR&D
#### Constraints
- The editorial notes few success stories of large-scale reform exist, so submissions of problems and shortfalls are explicitly welcomed

### Target Learners
- education researchers
- graduate students
- junior faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- building a firmer empirical foundation for educational technology practice
