# Rule-based question selection for evaluation: non-repetition, full concept coverage, all difficulty levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The tutor extracts pre-test and post-test questions from a question base, each question given a specific score. Question selection must satisfy rules the article enumerates: "Firstly none of them should be repetitious even if a learner would be trained one concept several times. Secondly the question must be planned for all sections of a concept entirely. Thirdly expert tutor plans questions in all level." Sequence, number and level of questions are then determined according to learner level and learning type.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A question base covering all sections and all levels of a concept; scores assigned per question
#### Constraints
- Questions must not repeat even across repeated training of the same concept

### Target Learners
- learners at varying knowledge levels in web-based tutoring

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring conceptual and objective understanding of lesson content
