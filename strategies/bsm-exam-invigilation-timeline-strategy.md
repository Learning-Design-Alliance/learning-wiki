# Follow a timed invigilation protocol before, during, and after examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A step-by-step proctoring protocol taken from the BSM common examination proctor guide. Twenty minutes before the exam the TA checks in at the examination office and verifies the venue; ten minutes before, the TA sets the phone to vibration mode and writes the course name, course code, exam time, and instructor's name on the whiteboard; five minutes before, "The sealed envelope should be opened in front of the examinee" and papers distributed with a check for visibility and completeness. During the exam the TA identifies students and signs the test/answer sheets; afterwards the TA counts collected sheets and returns them to the Examination Office.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An examination office that provides attendance sheets, test/answer sheets, the proctor guide, and sealed envelopes.
- Student ID cards (physical or e-student ID on a mobile phone for identification purposes only).
#### Constraints
- Once the exam starts, examinees are not allowed to leave the classroom before it ends; illegitimate activities such as cheating result in failure and possible disciplinary action.

### Target Learners
- graduate teaching assistants serving as proctors
- undergraduate examinees

### Target Learning Goals
- secure and orderly examination administration
