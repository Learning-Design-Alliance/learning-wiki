# Offer theory-driven web-based interventions, optionally combined with face-to-face support, to reach students facing access barriers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article states that "Theory driven, web-based intervention programs offer an acceptable and effective method of providing psychological treatment within the university system". It argues online therapy may appeal to students who cannot access clinics due to barriers including stigma, and suggests combining web-based interventions with face-to-face support for best improvements in emotional wellbeing. This responds to the article's observation that accessing mental health services in the Middle East is challenging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Theory-driven design of the web-based intervention programs
#### Constraints
- Accessing help for mental health services in the Middle East area is challenging, with stigma and shortages of culturally competent clinicians cited as critical barriers to effective treatment

### Target Learners
- University students in the UAE who experience depression or anxiety

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving emotional wellbeing so that mental health difficulties do not undermine academic performance
