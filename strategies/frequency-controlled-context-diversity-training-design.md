# Design training studies that hold word exposure frequency constant while manipulating the number, timing or nature of diverse contexts

## Description
The article recommends a specific research and instructional design: children read novel words embedded in stories whose context varies from story to story, while frequency of exposure to each word is controlled. It states that "carefully designed training experiments have utility here, as do computational models", to determine whether diversity of contexts, their temporal spacing, or the nature and similarity of linguistic contexts drives learning. Sensitive measures are needed for knowledge that is partial and incremental.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Control for frequency of exposure to each new word while manipulating the number or nature of contexts or episodes; use sensitive measures of partial, incremental learning
#### Constraints
- The article notes the relevant notion of diversity is unresolved: number of contexts, temporal spacing, or nature/similarity of contexts may each matter
