# Adopt brainstorming in English teaching and embed creative-thinking skills in textbooks and teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its findings, the study recommends that "English teachers have to use brainstorming strategy in the teaching process", that English-language textbooks at all educational stages include creative-thinking skills such as fluency, flexibility and originality, and that training courses and workshops be organized for supervisors and teachers, with qualified trainers, on preparing and using brainstorming.
