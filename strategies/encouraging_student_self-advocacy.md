# Encouraging Student Self-Advocacy

## Description
Empowering learners to communicate their needs, understand their rights, and actively seek support. This involves fostering self-awareness, providing accessible resources, and creating a supportive learning environment where student voices are valued. Strategies include reflection on academic standing, utilizing tools effectively, and building a community where students' voices are valued.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection activities, access to educational tools, and a classroom environment that values student voice.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in classroom culture to prioritize student voice and self-advocacy. Students may need explicit instruction and support to develop self-awareness and communication skills.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Adapting tools and strategies to meet individual student needs, such as providing varied methods for self-assessment and making resources readily available.
