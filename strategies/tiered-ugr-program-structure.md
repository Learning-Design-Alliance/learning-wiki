# Introduce a tiered structure in UGR programs to separate well-prepared students from those needing foundational reinforcement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that agency-mediated UGR programs could adopt a "‘tiered structure’" in which the existing research-project track is kept for students properly equipped for research, while under-prepared students are inducted into "‘summer school’ like programs where they rein- force their UG learning itself". The REAP (Research Education Advancement Programme) run by the Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium (Bangalore) is cited as an example of such a program. This strategy addresses the selection problem of under-prepared entrants identified in the article's concerns section.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A selection process capable of distinguishing students properly equipped for research from under-prepared students
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a proposal to be considered, not an evaluated intervention

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in India, including under-prepared applicants to agency-mediated UGR programs

### Target Learning Goals
- reinforcing undergraduate learning
- preparing students for genuine research participation
