# Case Studies

## Description
Case studies involve the use of real-life situations that have occurred. Cases come in many forms, from a simple 'what would you do in this situation?' question to a detailed description of a situation with accompanying data to analyze. Most case-based assignments ask learners to answer open-ended questions or develop a solution to an open-ended problem.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-life cases that reflect actual situations, facilitator to guide the learning process
#### Constraints
- Cases may not be fully representative of all situations, and success depends on the facilitator's ability to guide the learning process.
