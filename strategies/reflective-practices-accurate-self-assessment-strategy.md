# Incorporate reflective practices, feedback mechanisms, and pre/post ADKS assessment into training to foster accurate self-assessment of dementia knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address the gap between perceived and actual knowledge, the authors urge educational strategies that "not only enhance knowledge but also foster accurate self-assessment among healthcare professionals." They propose incorporating reflective practices and feedback mechanisms into training programs, and using the ADKS before and after educational interventions to gauge training effectiveness and staff preparedness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment tools such as the ADKS administered before and after interventions to provide insight into training effectiveness.
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a cross-sectional snapshot; the article states it cannot indicate how knowledge changes over time.

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in acute care and primary care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate self-assessment of dementia knowledge; effective dementia care
