Encourage and improve the use of creative drama activities in Social Studies teaching, with extra attention at the 4th-grade level¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies, q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the meta-analytic finding of a wide and positive effect on course achievement, the article recommends that "activities related to the creative drama method should be encouraged to be used in the education and training process, and efforts should be made to improve their quality". It further suggests that at the 4th-grade level, where the effect was only medium, "the quality of the work to be done to improve the effectiveness of creative drama method can be increased or alternative teaching methods can be used".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Creative drama activities should be used with attention to their quality, since the article recommends efforts to improve their quality.

Constraints¶

  • At the 4th-grade level the effect was only medium and positive, and the article suggests the method can be perceived as a game by students rather than its teaching at this age.

Target Learners¶

  • Primary and middle school students in Social Studies courses in Turkey (grades 4-8)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Social Studies course academic achievement

Claims¶

  • Creative drama method has a very large positive effect on Social Studies course achievement in favor of experimental groups (random-effects d = 1.171) [+M]
  • Effect size of creative drama on Social Studies achievement varies by grade level, largest at 8th grade (d = 2.733) and lowest at 4th grade (d = 0.709) [~W]
  • The effect of drama on social studies achievement does not differ significantly by grade level (grades 4–7) [~W]
  • The drama approach has a strong positive effect on students' academic achievement in social studies courses compared with traditional teaching [+M]
  • The very large effect found here is consistent with prior meta-analyses of creative drama, which report medium-to-huge positive effects on achievement [+W]
  • Effect sizes across creative drama studies in Social Studies are highly heterogeneous (Q = 90.019, p < .05; I² = 78.893%) [~W]
