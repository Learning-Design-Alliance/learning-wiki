# Interventions that strengthen belonging, academic confidence, and social connection—particularly for women in male-dominated STEM fields

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 qualitative, 1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend interventions that "strengthen belonging, boost academic confidence, foster social connections, and address gender-specific experiences in male-dominated fields like physics and astronomy." The literature review also suggests raising awareness of stereotypes and subtle biases among those who interact with women in STEM as a potential strategy for improving women's persistence. Educators are encouraged to create inclusive environments that address challenges affecting academic outcomes for physics graduate students.
