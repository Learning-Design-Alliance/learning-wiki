# Alternate learners between participant and observer roles across two simulation cases with a teaching talk between them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Divide learners into 2 groups of 3-6; one group participates in the first case while the other observes via audio-video connection, then they switch for the second case. A brief teaching talk between cases introduces a framework for post-ROSC care. The article states this structure allowed observation of peers for vicarious reinforcement and enabled including a greater number of learners per session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A separate observation room with audio and video connection to the simulation lab
- Two simulation cases with a debrief and teaching talk between them
#### Constraints
- Learners who arrived late were not given preparticipation surveys to avoid disrupting the flow of the activity

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Post-ROSC clinical management
- Teamwork and communication during resuscitation
