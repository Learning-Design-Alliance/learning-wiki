Four-step assessment twin design process: identify vulnerabilities, align outcomes, select complementary tasks, develop interdependent marking schemes¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes an implementable design process for building assessment twins. As printed, "we propose an assessment twin design process: identifying vulnerabilities, aligning outcomes, selecting complementary tasks, and developing interdependent marking schemes". Each step moves an instructor from diagnosing GenAI vulnerability in an existing task to constructing a linked pair with shared outcomes and mutually dependent grading.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors must first identify the GenAI vulnerabilities of the existing assessment and align both twins to the same learning outcomes before selecting tasks and building interdependent marking schemes.

Constraints¶

  • The authors acknowledge the approach is resource intensive and raises equity concerns.

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students
