# Prioritize longer-term longitudinal studies and analysis of learners' virtual-world behaviors for SLA research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editorial recommends that future research on embodied cognition and language learning in virtual worlds move beyond short-term designs, stating that "longer-term longitudinal studies, rather than studies in short terms like several hours, days, or 1 or 2 months, are needed." It also calls for analysis of learners' learning behaviors in virtual worlds relating to SLA, such as their communication patterns and strategy usage, to build the knowledge base.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research designs must extend beyond short-term studies of several hours, days, or 1 or 2 months
#### Constraints
- The editorial identifies current evidence as largely short-term, limiting conclusions about long-term effects of virtual-world language learning

### Target Learners
- second language (L2) learners in virtual worlds

### Target Learning Goals
- second language acquisition, including communication patterns and strategy usage
