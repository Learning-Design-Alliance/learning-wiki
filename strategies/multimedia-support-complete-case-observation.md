## Description
The article recommends using multimedia materials alongside case review sessions. The article states "Multimedia materials were used to support students’ observation and understanding of complete cases and clinical decision-making processes." As an image-assisted learning approach, it may further facilitate understanding of treatment planning, tooth movement, and procedural details.
