# Supplement online competition programs with lectures from international professionals who serve as role models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
GeCAA's Estonian organizers "scheduled two lectures by an eminent astronomer and an astronaut for all the participants, which proved very popular among the students". The authors state that "Supplementing the program with lectures from international professionals, who will be seen as role models, is of immense value", reinforcing the educational character of the activity.
