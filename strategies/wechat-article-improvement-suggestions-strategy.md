# Improve WeChat public account learning articles by increasing knowledge points, providing Chinese-English comparisons, and unifying layout design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students' feedback identifies concrete adjustments to WeChat public account literature articles: "63.27% of the students think increasing the knowledge points is the top-one concern of the designer and writer of those Wechat articles." Providing Chinese and English comparisons (57.14%) supports comprehension of content that is hard to understand in English, and unifying layout design (34.69%) matters because first impressions determine whether students read short WeChat articles at all.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Article designers must gather student feedback on knowledge coverage, bilingual support, and layout before revising the weekly articles
#### Constraints
- Applies to WeChat public account based mobile learning modes for literature courses as evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- university students learning English and American literature

### Target Learning Goals
- literature content learning and learning efficiency with bilingual materials
