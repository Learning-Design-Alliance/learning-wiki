# Summer internships at national laboratories and industrial affiliates provide hands-on training and mentoring that enrich on-campus coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because relatively few universities operate accelerator research facilities, the paper recommends enriching on-campus coursework through summer internships at collaborating institutions such as national laboratories and industrial affiliates. Internship programs provide “hands-on training and mentoring from world-class accelerator scientists at active research centers.” The paper lists four career-development benefits: familiarizing students with evolving programs and career opportunities, establishing personal contacts with professionals, emphasizing the integration of accelerator technology with the needs of accelerator-based physics, chemistry and biology, and strengthening career opportunities after the graduate degree.
