# Provide counselling services and an academically supportive environment for LGBTQ+ college students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that Chinese colleges "implement adequate support and counselling services to enhance the proactive coping abilities of LGBTQ + students and create an academically supportive environment to alleviate the adverse effects of perceived stress". Administrators are advised to establish counselling services, provide support for resource recovery, and strengthen inclusive education to reduce homophobia. The recommendation rests directly on the two moderation findings.
