# Remotely delivered hands-on suturing workshop using multi-angle streaming and peer review via video conferencing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy delivers a hands-on psychomotor workshop fully remotely. During the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic, the author "used a streaming application (https://streamyard.com) and multiple mobile devices in order to provide various angles to stream the hands-on suturing lecture" to students at home who had picked up bananas and suturing instruments beforehand. Students practiced in their own sessions, then joined a Google Meet meeting where they presented their work in turn and evaluated and learned from each other's work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must obtain practice materials (bananas and suturing instruments) beforehand; multiple mobile devices are used to capture several camera angles; a video conferencing tool is needed for the closing peer-review session
#### Constraints
- The article reports this was implemented during the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic period when students could not attend school

### Target Learners
- dental students learning suturing skills

### Target Learning Goals
- hands-on suturing technique
- peer evaluation and learning from others' work
