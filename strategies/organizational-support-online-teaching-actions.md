# Support teaching quality monitoring, training, and technical assistance when promoting faculty online teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article translates its finding that teaching quality monitoring most influenced faculty acceptance into three concrete actions for administrators and policymakers: ensure learning management systems support monitoring activities, organize training and sharing sessions, and provide timely technical support. The authors state administrators should "ensure that the adopted online learning management system and related technological tools can support faculty to conduct teaching quality monitoring activities."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An adopted online learning management system and related technological tools that support faculty monitoring activities such as checking assignments and reviewing student performance data
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a mainland China COVID-19 context; the authors call for testing generalization to countries or regions with different culture or educational management philosophy

### Target Learners
- College faculty in higher education institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Faculty acceptance and sustainable implementation of online teaching
