# Administrators should plan teacher training in the situational leadership approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because applying four distinct styles is hard for individual teachers, the article recommends institutional support: "educational administrators are recommended to chalk out plans for the training of teachers under this model to get the improved performance of students, a major national education objective". The strategy treats situational-style teaching as a school-level capability built through planned professional development rather than an individual improvisation. It follows directly from the study's finding that readiness-matched styles improved achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
