# Use narrative story structure to ease student tension and increase relevance when teaching sensitive health topics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors suggest that structuring lessons using a narrative format with the four C's may help ease student tension around difficult subject matter, make material more relevant, and provide opportunities for discussion about personal application of health-related skills. They list sensitive topics including supporting family members with chronic diseases, managing mental illness, responding to a friend diagnosed with an STI, dealing with drug addiction, and helping a friend struggling with gender and identity issues.
