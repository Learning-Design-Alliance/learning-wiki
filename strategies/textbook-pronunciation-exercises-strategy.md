# Textbook designers should include more pronunciation exercises and rules in learners' materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that textbook designers "pay much more attention to the pronunciation part included in the students' textbooks" and provide more exercises and pronunciation rules. This addresses the finding that pronunciation receives little attention in Iranian English classes despite its importance for communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Textbook designers need to allocate space and time to pronunciation content within existing materials.
