# Teach physics concepts through a historical approach showing discovery and evolution over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a historical teaching method that "helps the students find out when a Phenomenon/hypothesis/concept was discovered and then understand its evolution over time", citing Mach and Duhem's advocacy of the historical method. It shows students an example of scientific progress and their potential active role in further development, can be linked to the philosophical ideas behind scientific theories, and introduces physics as a culture universal across gender, country and race, ideally supported by films, documentaries and videos.
