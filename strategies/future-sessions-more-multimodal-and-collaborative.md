# Design future sectional anatomy sessions to be more multimodal and collaborative

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants suggested that future sessions should include more multimodal learning practices (e.g., incorporating more Netter images and 3D physical or digital models) and more collaborative learning activities. The article notes this suggestion "is compatible with the multiple representations principle of instructional design described in CTML" and that "well-designed collaborative learning activities can potentially further improve the cognitive efficiency of learning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to complementary resources such as 3D models, Netter images, and collaborative spaces
#### Constraints
- Despite these suggestions, the current study shows instructional activities in sectional anatomy can be successfully completed by individual learners

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students learning sectional anatomy

### Target Learning Goals
- Build spatial sense of anatomical structures in CT images
- Improve engagement and cognitive efficiency in learning sectional anatomy
