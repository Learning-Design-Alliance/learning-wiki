# Integrate longitudinal support such as group mentorship into change-agent training to foster lasting skill development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose extending agency training beyond a short workshop by adding ongoing support over time. They write that "une autre avenue serait de poursuivre la formation auprès des ergothérapeutes en y intégrant un soutien longitudinal (p.ex., mentorat de groupe), reconnu comme étant plus efficace", and suggest also exploring and evaluating other pedagogical formats, including contextualized training, to support the transition of agency skills to real-life application.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained engagement of professional programs, orders and associations to offer such opportunities
#### Constraints
- The article reports that short-duration training may be insufficient for durable effects on skills and their exercise in real contexts; the proposed supports are future directions, not tested here

### Target Learners
- practising occupational therapists
- health professionals in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- durable development of change-agent competencies
- transfer of skills to real-life contexts
