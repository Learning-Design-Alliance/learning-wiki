# Address preservice teachers' EE teaching efficacy through infusion across methods courses or a separate EE course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding of combined negative self-efficacy and positive outcome expectancy, the author concludes preservice teachers are receptive and ready to receive training in EE teaching. The training "could be done through infusion in the methods courses or through hauing a separate course in EE." Given state-mandated curriculum streamlining, the author suggests the infusion approach might have the upper hand: integrating how to teach EE in all methods courses and environmental science concepts in science requirements for liberal education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher education programs must make room for EE content either within existing methods courses or as a standalone course
#### Constraints
- Institutions operate on state-mandated realities for a streamlined curriculum, which the author notes favors infusion over a separate course

### Target Learners
- preservice elementary teachers in teacher education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge and skills for teaching environmental education
