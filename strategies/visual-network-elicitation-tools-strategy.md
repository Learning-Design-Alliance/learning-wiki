# Use visual network-elicitation tools in qualitative data collection to empower participants and surface unique perspectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy recommends incorporating visual tools during data collection so that participants can draw or otherwise construct their networks. The article cites prior research reporting that "such techniques empower and give agency to users while also allowing for the articulation of unique perspectives." In the project described, interviews served as egocentric name generators while participants built a visual representation of their personal networks, which the authors connect to critical methodologies' emphasis on participant agency and voice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interviewers must be prepared to support participants in creating a visual artifact of their networks and to treat that artifact as data.
#### Constraints
- The article cites the empowerment finding from other studies (Gameiro et al.; Mannay), not from its own dataset, so the benefit is attributed rather than demonstrated here.

### Target Learners
- adult interview participants in education research studies

### Target Learning Goals
- eliciting richer, participant-centered accounts of social networks
