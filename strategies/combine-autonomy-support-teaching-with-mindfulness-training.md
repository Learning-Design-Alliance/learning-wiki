# Combine autonomy-supportive teaching environments with school-based mindfulness training to support students' need satisfaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends creating autonomy-supportive teaching environments ("listening to students' opinions, offering choices to students, and acknowledging student feelings") and, in addition, cultivating students' mindfulness, since mindfulness practices "may increase the receptivity to PE teachers' positive teaching behaviors as well as decrease negative responses to non-supportive or controlling teaching climates." It notes school-based mindfulness programs such as mindfulness-based yoga interventions and guided meditation are available for this training purpose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in autonomy-supportive teaching behaviors and availability of school-based mindfulness programs for students
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a cross-sectional survey in Hong Kong PE classes; the article notes results may not generalize to all secondary school students and other populations

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
