# Use short variable tests as a pre-lecture attentiveness and attendance check

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because variable questions can be created quickly and manipulated conveniently, a teacher can form a test immediately before a class for a specific learning situation. "by preparing a short test of 2-3 questions on the lecture subject, teachers can combine a short test of student attentiveness and knowledge, and the electronic registration of class attendance." Tests take just 5 to 10 minutes in practically any class, with students using their smartphones.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A test bank of variable questions generated in advance
- Browser access to the testing system via student smartphones
#### Constraints
- Intended for short 2-3 question tests on the lecture subject, not full examinations

### Target Learners
- engineering students attending lectures

### Target Learning Goals
- checking attentiveness and knowledge of the lecture topic; registering attendance
