Apply cognitive science design principles simultaneously and in combination when revising curriculum, with targeted rather than arbitrary development decisions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Math Center applies its four design principles simultaneously, in combination, when revising the math curriculum, an approach the article says differs from studies exploring the principles one at a time. Davenport urges curriculum writers to make conscious, targeted decisions, such as integrating visuals in strategic ways that complement a lesson's key concepts and maximizing the use of students' limited working memory.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Curriculum developers need knowledge of the research behind the design principles; teachers in the study completed a two-day online professional development workshop on the principles
