Give pop music examples a greater place in music teaching programs and lesson books¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that because using pop music examples in song learning positively affected students' attitudes, "a greater place should be given to pop music examples in teaching programs and lesson books." They argue greater inclusion "will create a link between the school and private lives of students more easily" and may contribute to increased success levels of music education through national education policy and application.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Music teachers must identify songs to be used in music education carefully, avoiding damaging or vulgar content

Constraints¶

  • The research was limited to Burdur Province because of time and budget limitations

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • positive attitudes toward music lessons
  • motivation for music lessons
  • desire to participate in music activities

Claims¶

  • Using Turkish pop music samples with karaoke recordings in song learning produced significant pretest-posttest increases in middle school students' attitudes to music lessons on most scale items [+W]
  • Traditional song teaching with educational music repertoire produced significant attitude increases only on performance-related items and no significant change on other items [+W]
  • The largest attitude increase in the experimental group was on the item about use of technological facilities in music lessons, attributed to karaoke recordings [+W]
  • In the experimental group, no significant pretest-posttest difference appeared on items 4, 7, 8 and 15, including the item about teachers' use of instruments [+W]
  • Before the application, students held moderately positive attitudes to music lessons, with lowest points on items about teacher instrument use and technology [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Give creativity greater importance in teaching environments, especially in the application dimension, and conduct more experimental studies
