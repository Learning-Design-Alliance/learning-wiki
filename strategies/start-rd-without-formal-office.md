# Start R&D without a formal office: a single school initiative paired with strategic research support can yield meaningful change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawn from the rural district use case's lessons learned, this strategy holds that formal R&D infrastructure is not a prerequisite. The report states: "You do not need a formal R&D office to start. A single school initiative, paired with strategic research support, can yield meaningful change." Companion lessons from the same case include studying current practice to reveal gaps between design and use, and treating student voice as a research asset that explains the why behind data trends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategic research support, such as a partnership with a Regional Educational Laboratory
#### Constraints
- The report notes this work "was localized to a single school and not embedded district-wide"

### Target Learners
- School-level leaders and teams beginning R&D in any K-12 context

### Target Learning Goals
- Building local research capacity and informing improvement decisions
