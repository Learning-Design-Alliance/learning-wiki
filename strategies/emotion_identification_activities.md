# Emotion Identification Activities

## Description
These activities focus on helping learners identify and express emotions through various means, enhancing their emotional literacy and self-awareness. Examples include making emotion faces and having children guess the emotion, labeling their own emotions, and guessing the emotions of characters in stories. Puppets, emotion walking games, emotion hello songs, and emotion guessing games are used to engage learners. Learners are asked to look at each other's faces and think about how they are feeling and asked to share their own emotions. Learners also play emotion guessing games by revealing faces showing an emotion and having others guess what the emotion being shown is. Other activities involve changing familiar songs by singing them with a different emotion and modelling the silliest face you can make and have the children participate as well.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires puppets, emotion cards, stories, music, and space for movement. The instructor must facilitate discussion and model emotional expressions.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may struggle to express emotions, activities must be culturally sensitive, and the instructor must be skilled in facilitating emotional discussions.
