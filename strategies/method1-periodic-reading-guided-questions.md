# Periodic Astrobites reading assignments with guided questions, electronic response collection, and in-class discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Method 1 "makes use of periodic reading assignments to promote student familiarity with the astrophysical research, and uses structured online questions to encourage and check student understanding." The instructor selects an article, formulates 2-4 guided questions, collects responses through a fillable online form, scores them with a rubric, and reviews the questions in the next class period. Optional tasks include commenting on the Astrobite and contrasting a press release with the source paper.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The instructor "formulates 2-4 guided questions" answerable by applying concepts from previous lessons to the reading
- Responses are gathered electronically, e.g. through a fillable Google Form
#### Constraints
- Questions typically either would not include calculations, or would focus only on "symbolic manipulation or scaling relations"

### Target Learners
- students at any course level, including introductory undergraduate through graduate

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- conceptual understanding of course curriculum
- familiarity with the astronomical literature
