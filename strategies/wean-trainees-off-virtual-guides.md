# Progressively wean trainees off virtual guidance aids during simulator training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article observes that students progressed in adopting the right drilling angle only while the angulation guide was in place, with stagnation or slight regression once it was removed. Since the operator will not have such indications during surgery, the authors argue it is necessary to determine how long trainees depend on the guides before progressively doing away with them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulator software that can toggle positioning and angulation assistance across trials
#### Constraints
- The article states the necessary duration of guide dependence still must be determined; it did not test a weaning schedule itself

### Target Learners
- dental students training on haptic simulators
- surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- independent, guide-free drilling accuracy
- transfer of simulator skills to real surgery
