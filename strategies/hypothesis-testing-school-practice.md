# Test the hypotheses of the science of teaching against concrete facts found in school practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that students of method complete the empirical half of the science of teaching by confronting existing hypotheses with classroom realities. As the author puts it, "Hypothesis forming has been only half the work, testing has been the other half." The strategy is to work with concrete facts as found in school practice, in the manner of the sawmill machinist's book that captured a complete, tested procedure, producing books on the teaching of writing, reading, spelling, arithmetic, history and other subjects.
