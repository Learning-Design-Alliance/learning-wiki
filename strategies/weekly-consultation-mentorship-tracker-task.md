# Weekly scheduled consultation time-table for close teacher mentorship during the performance task

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors found that a weekly schedule consultation time-table supported the task's success: students are given opportunities to discuss their analysis with teachers both in and outside class, and teachers make suggestions to refine videos, give direction for further readings, or suggest analysis approaches.
