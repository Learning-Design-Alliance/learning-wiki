# Three-phase debriefing (meeting, analysis, summary) after the simulated scene

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the scene, debriefing was structured "according to the emotional, behavioral and cognitive stages" and mediated by a facilitator. The article divides it into phases: "Phase 1 - Meeting", listening to the participant (5 minutes); "Phase 2 - Analysis", reflecting on actions taken and what would be done differently (15 minutes); and "Phase 3 - Summary", identifying situations in light of scientific evidence (5 minutes). In this scenario the debriefing lasted 20 minutes and focused on humanized care, newborn evaluation and Apgar score, skin-to-skin contact and breastfeeding in the first hour.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator to mediate the phases, seeking first the verbal expression of participants on the scene and then of other group members
#### Constraints
- The time used for the debriefing depends on the situations of the proposed simulation scenarios, the objectives, the facilitator and the students

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- health professionals in training

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on clinical decision-making
- consolidation of humanized care learning
