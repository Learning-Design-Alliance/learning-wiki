State supports for UPK expansion: fund facilities and ratio implementation, update reimbursement rates, offer curriculum and assessment technical assistance, and expand professional development and inclusive-classroom supports¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report closes with a set of state-level actions to sustain UPK quality as TK expansion finishes. It recommends that "the state can consider funding facilities upgrades and implementation of new adult-to-student ratio requirements, updating reimbursement rates to meet the cost of care for publicly funded preschool and child care programs, offering technical assistance on curriculum and assessments, supporting high-quality professional development for early educators and school leaders, and expanding supports for inclusive classrooms." These respond to survey-reported challenges in facilities, funding, staffing, and developmentally appropriate practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sustained state investment and targeted support, which the report calls "critical to ensuring high-quality early learning opportunities for all young learners in the state."

Constraints¶

  • Funding was a particular challenge for small LEAs with low TK enrollment and basic aid districts that do not receive additional funding for TK students.

Target Learners¶

  • 4-year-olds enrolled in transitional kindergarten
  • income-eligible 3-year-olds in state preschool programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • access to high-quality, developmentally appropriate prekindergarten learning environments

Related Strategies¶

  • Use local resources to serve children birth through age 3 to stabilize community providers as 4-year-olds move into TK
  • Expand transitional kindergarten and preschool by braiding UPK funds with hiring, culturally responsive curriculum, and community college partnerships
  • Target state UPK technical assistance on coaching and mentoring, curriculum and assessment guidance, facilities, multilingual learner supports, and paraprofessional workforce investment
