# Capacity building, experienced–inexperienced team pairing, and midwife recruitment to improve partograph knowledge and use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend interventions to strengthen partograph knowledge and systematic use, especially in Health Centers: "conducting interventions aimed at capacity building and the systematic use of partograph", pairing less experienced providers with more experienced ones, and recruiting more midwives. The recommendations follow directly from the survey's associated factors: qualification, institution type, location and years of service.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applies to health facilities employing obstetric care providers with varying qualifications and experience levels
#### Constraints
- Based on a cross-sectional self-reported survey in the West region of Cameroon; the authors note a tendency to social desirability bias

### Target Learners
- Obstetric care providers (nurses, nurse assistants, midwives, general practitioners) in health centers and district hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct knowledge of partograph use and interpretation for labour monitoring
