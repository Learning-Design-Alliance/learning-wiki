# Teach order-of-magnitude estimation through timescale calculations to build students' timescale intuition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends explicitly demonstrating how the duration of phenomena can be estimated through simple order-of-magnitude calculations and discussing the limitations of these estimates. Students are then "encouraged to make numerical estimates by themselves, which will eventually help them to develop their own 'timescale intuition'". Examples include dimensional analysis of the capillary time, ultrasound frequency limits, and electron-ion energy transfer counts.
