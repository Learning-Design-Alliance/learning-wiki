# Target under-represented groups in outreach to widen participation in physics from early years to pre-university

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (3 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Outreach and Public Engagement programme engages students of all ages up to university entry, focusing mainly on 11-14 year olds and starting with under-5s storytelling, with the message that physics is for everyone. It particularly addresses "under-served audiences in physics such as girls, students from disadvantaged backgrounds and under-represented ethnic minorities", through Connect Physics workshops, the Shattering Stereotypes project on gender stereotyping, teacher CPD, and a collaborative recruitment project using widening participation initiatives.
