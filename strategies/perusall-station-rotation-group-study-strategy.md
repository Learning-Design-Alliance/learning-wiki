# Perusall group-study station within a station-rotation blended learning session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Physics Practical I course, the authors use Perusall synchronously inside face-to-face blended sessions: "students are divided into groups (2-3 students per group), with each group working on one of the stations according to the station rotation model." Students rotate through learning stations in the classroom, and one station involves a Perusall group study of a video tutorial focusing on an important step of the measurement process. This implements collaborative annotation as one rotating activity among several.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom organized into learning stations and small groups of 2-3 students; a curated Perusall video tutorial resource for the annotation station
#### Constraints
- Described for a course with 10 students in 2021; the article does not report comparative evaluation of this specific arrangement

### Target Learners
- higher education physics students in laboratory-style practical courses

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding steps of the measurement process
- collaborative study of video tutorials
- active engagement during face-to-face sessions
