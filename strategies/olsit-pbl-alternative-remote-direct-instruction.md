# Use OLSit-based project-based learning as an alternative to replicating direct instruction remotely

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Edyburn recommends that instead of attempting to implement direct instruction at a distance, teachers flip the paradigm: "project-based learning offers a powerful alternative to promoting student engagement". The strategy restructures learning assignments so the shift in responsibility from teacher-focused to student-centered instruction can be readily accomplished, with teacher-student conversations providing guidance for a personalized learning plan. He cautions that "teachers, students, and parents should not expect immediate impact after trying the strategy a single time".
