# Use simulated student sections as a scalable supplement to teacher training and lesson preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend deploying simulated small-group sections as a supplement to traditional teacher training in large online courses such as Code in Place or Schoolhouse, where scaling live coaching is hard. Simulations "can serve as high-frequency training that leaves more time for feedback iteration between follow-up synchronous sessions with coaches", and teachers can also rehearse upcoming lessons to refresh understanding and engagement strategies before teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The simulation environment resembles online teaching contexts, which may be text-based with fewer physical cues
#### Constraints
- Learning from practice depends on experience and uptake of the generated feedback; some instructors may need more structured experiences such as in-session hints or specific scenarios

### Target Learners
- Volunteer and novice teachers in massive online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Practicing lesson delivery, anticipating student questions, and refining engagement strategies
