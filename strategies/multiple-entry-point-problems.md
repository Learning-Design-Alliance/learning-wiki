# Choose problems with multiple entry points to stimulate discussion of different solution approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To encourage varied approaches, "teachers should try to present problems with multiple entry points." Entry activities include drawing a picture of key elements, preparing a representation such as a strip diagram, listing what is known and needed, or acting out a transaction. The guide states that discovering more than one solution way "may spur their curiosity, deepen their engagement, and solidify their understanding" and facilitates lively class discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problems must be selected that admit several starting approaches; teachers allocate advance preparation time to find such problems.
