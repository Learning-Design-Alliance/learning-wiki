# Wooden-dowel physical model for making the shower front plane tangible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To make the inclined shower front tangible, students cut wooden dowels (e.g. supermarket shish kabob sticks) with lengths proportional to the arrival time of the air shower at each station. A print-out of the station positions and signals is attached to styrofoam, and the dowels are fixed to each station; "it becomes apparent that they form a plane which is the shower front". A stick through the impact point perpendicular to this plane gives the arrival direction, and students measure the elevation and azimuth angles directly.
