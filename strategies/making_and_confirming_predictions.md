# Making and confirming predictions

## Description
This strategy involves forming educated guesses or anticipations about the content or outcome of a text or audio material and then verifying these predictions as one engages with the material. It encourages active engagement, critical thinking, and monitoring of comprehension by comparing existing knowledge to new information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading and listening materials, explicit instruction, guided practice, opportunities for discussion
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners with limited vocabulary or background knowledge. Requires explicit instruction and practice.
