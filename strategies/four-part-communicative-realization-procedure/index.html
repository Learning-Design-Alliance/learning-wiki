Four-part realization procedure for communicatively developing technology in retraining courses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article prescribes a concrete procedure for realizing the communicatively developing technology in advanced-training courses. As printed, "The procedure of communicatively developing technology realization must include: 1. Case study of orthoepy and speech culture. 2. Case study – “communicative core” the analysis of texts and connected speech fragments. 3. Seminar-discussion (“rhetoric duel”) on psychological and pedagogic themes. 4. Speech control (correction of IAT listeners` speech activity)." It pairs case-based text analysis with discussion formats and ongoing speech correction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Trainer functions as facilitator, role model, group leader and partner during interactions

Constraints¶

  • Designed for teachers in the course-retraining (IAT) system, particularly Tatar-school teachers

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers of Tatar schools in advanced training courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Activation of oral (Russian) speech activity
  • Formation of professional literate speech

Claims¶

  • Tatar-school teachers in course groups taught with communicatively developing methods scored higher than control groups on eight oral-speech characteristics, with no sample size or statistical test reported [+W]
  • Baseline analysis of 600 teacher monologue utterances found most trainees made few mistakes, pointing to need for systematic work on oral text [+M]
  • Group form of orthoepic practicum work is reported as better than frontal form for trainees' individual recording and collective activity [+W]
  • Early realization of a shared object/motive bonds adult course participants into a quasi-community [+W]
