# Use short introductory vodcasts (10-15 minutes) as a preview tool to reduce the time burden on students in compressed curricula.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose reducing vodcast duration as a solution to the time burden that limited preview uptake in their study. They report currently investigating 'introductory vodcasts' averaging only 10-15 minutes as a preview tool for GEM 1 students, hoping the shorter format will be more palatable and acquire more views than the full-length vodcasts used in the current study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vodcasts must be substantially shorter than full lecture length to reduce study time burden
#### Constraints
- This strategy is proposed but not yet empirically tested; the article reports it as ongoing work

### Target Learners
- first-year graduate entry medicine students

### Target Learning Goals
- lecture preparation
- managing study time in compressed curricula
