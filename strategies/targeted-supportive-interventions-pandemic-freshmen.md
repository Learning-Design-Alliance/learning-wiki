# Develop targeted supportive interventions for female and Italian freshmen and interventions to mitigate the pandemic's emotional impact and help freshmen regain future perspective

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing interventions to mitigate pandemic consequences in freshman subgroups. It concludes that "Specific supportive interventions should therefore be developed for these target students" — females and Italian freshmen — and that interventions are needed "to mitigate the emotional impact of the pandemic to help freshmen regain perspective for the future". The recommendation rests on the study's own subgroup findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identify affected subgroups (females, Italian freshmen, highly stressed students) using surveys similar to those the study used
#### Constraints
