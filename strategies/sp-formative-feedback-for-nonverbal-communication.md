# Build nonverbal communication education into SP formative feedback during repeated communication practice rather than a dedicated NVC lecture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In this course, built on the Calgary-Cambridge guide, "There was no formal NVC lecture in this medical communication course. Instead, students were educated on NVC through experienced SPs' formative feedback." Each of five topics provided a 2-hour exercise with SPs in which each student practiced a 5-minute interview and received immediate feedback from the SP and peers. The authors conclude it is "necessary to activate the NVC education using the SP feedback" and that SP training programs should emphasize nonverbal behaviors and appropriate feedback methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SPs with substantial experience and training in feedback (in this study over 5 years of experience and training every 2 years); repeated practice opportunities across topics
#### Constraints
- SP feedback may vary qualitatively and quantitatively, a limitation the authors note, partly offset by repeated SP practice

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students without prior clinical education

### Target Learning Goals
- nonverbal communication in medical interviews, including facial expression, body position, silence, movements, and giggling
