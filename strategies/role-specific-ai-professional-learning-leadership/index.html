Invest in dedicated instructional technology leadership roles and role-specific professional learning for AI literacy¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's third use case illustrates Recommendation 3—investing 'in role-specific professional learning aligned to grade levels and disciplines to build educator capacity for teaching AI literacy.' In May 2025 Allentown School District approved instructional technology leaders, including a Director of Innovation and Instructional Technology and Supervisors who support teachers and coaches, 'creating a multi-tier professional learning ecosystem.' For example, Bridgeview Academy hosted a PD session where teachers used Playlab AI to design custom classroom apps from question generators to behavior tracking systems.

Related Strategies¶

  • Join national innovation networks and build internal leadership academies to advance responsible district AI adoption
  • Pair technology access with targeted investments in infrastructure, staffing, and support, and pilot initiatives with dedicated budgets
  • Invest in sustained, role-specific, discipline-specific professional learning and structured student AI literacy pathways across PK16
  • Invest in educator professional learning on experiential AI pedagogies using existing federal funding streams
  • Layered educator support: coaching, curated resources, low-lift entry points, and community engagement
  • Embed AI literacy across disciplines by identifying intersections with existing content and using case studies, interdisciplinary modules, and collaborative projects
  • Support digitally fluent career pathways through ecosystems, educator training, work-based learning, convenings, and showcases
  • Pair digital-infrastructure funding with blended-learning support and AI literacy training for students
  • Share discipline-specific GenAI examples aligned with instructional needs to improve teacher adoption
  • Provide teachers with adequate training and support for AI use, embedded in leadership-led professional development
