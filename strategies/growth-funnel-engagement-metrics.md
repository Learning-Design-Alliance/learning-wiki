# Measure partnership success with a growth funnel of reach, engagement, and dedicated users

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (4 design), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Center borrows funnel analysis from marketing and growth analytics, mapping key metrics to a growth funnel: 1) reach, "which includes social media engagement and interactions", 2) engagement, the number of individuals attending events, engaged in online communities, and opted into the community, and 3) dedicated, the number of users on IBM Quantum systems listing an HBCU as their institution affiliation. Future evaluation will add publications, joint collaborations and proposals, and student placements.
