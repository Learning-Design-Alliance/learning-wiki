# Future gamification research should target the four English language skills individually and collectively.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future work "focus on the effects of using the gamification method in English on four skills, individually or collectively (reading, listening, writing, and speaking)". The authors present this as a powerful strategy for helping teachers and learners teach and acquire the four primary English skills efficiently, and suggest delivering the skills through activities supporting active use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should examine gamification's effects on reading, listening, writing, and speaking, individually or collectively, as the article advises.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is based on a meta-evaluation limited to Turkish studies with quantitative and mixed methods published between 2012 and 2022.

### Target Learners
- English language learners in Turkey across age and class groups

### Target Learning Goals
- English reading, listening, writing, and speaking skills
