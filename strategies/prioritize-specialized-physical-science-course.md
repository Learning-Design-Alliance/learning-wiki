# Elementary education programs offering a specialized science course should prioritize a course in physical science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that elementary education programs seeking to replace general education science courses with specialized courses for future teachers focus their first specialized offering on physical science. This follows from ELED majors matching STEM majors in Biology but scoring lower than STEM majors in Physical Science, which "suggests elementary education majors would benefit more from a specialized course in physical science than in biology". The strategy targets the content area where preservice elementary teachers currently show the largest relative grade gap.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The program must offer a specialized science course as an alternative to general education science courses, which the article notes are challenging to implement and maintain.
#### Constraints
