# Motor Images

## Description
Motor images, also known as muscle memory, involve using physical movements and actions to enhance learning and recall. These strategies capitalize on the brain's ability to remember procedures and information that have been practiced repeatedly. Multisensory strategies that combine seeing, hearing, touching, and moving with the information are particularly effective.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space for physical movement, learning material to be practiced, and understanding of the connection between movement and recall
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for learners with physical limitations, requires careful supervision to ensure safety, may be disruptive in some classroom settings
