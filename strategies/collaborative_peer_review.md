# Collaborative Peer Review

## Description
Learners connect with others to provide and receive feedback for improvement on writing tasks. Students do their own thinking and writing first and then connect with others to provide and receive feedback for improvement. This type of collaborative learning contrasts with cooperative writing where students in a group complete individual parts of a writing task without peer feedback and then put the pieces together to present as a final product.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing tasks, peer review checklists or guides, digital platforms for collaboration and feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires structuring and modeling, especially for emergent writing. May need teacher supervision to ensure maximum benefit.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly beneficial for English language learners. Can be adapted for various grade levels.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop writing skills, enhance critical thinking, foster collaboration and communication, improve revision skills.

### Affordances
- Reduces anxiety and increases self-confidence and motivation, especially for English language learners. Enhances writing quality through peer feedback and collaboration.

### Personalization
- Use checklists or guides to have learners review each other's writing and make comments. Products can also have sentences or word banks that allow learners to collaboratively arrange them to create a story.
