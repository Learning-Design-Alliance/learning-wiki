# Two-phase classroom session: individual exploration followed by collective competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The pilot's implementable session recipe consists of a two-hour session in which students first play individually at their own pace and then compete on common levels. This design allowed the authors to observe both autonomous gameplay and emerging dynamics of competition, problem-solving strategies, and social motivation, and students reported that group challenges increased participation and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shared set of common levels for the collective phase; web-enabled devices for each participant
#### Constraints
- In the pilot, students interacted with the game using only a mouse to control the character

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade students in secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting motion graphs
- collaborative problem-solving
