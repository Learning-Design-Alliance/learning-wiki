# Optimize e-learning platform usability and provide technical support to maximize engagement across diverse nursing staff groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participants' free-text evaluation, the authors recommend that future implementations "aim to further optimise usability and provide additional technical support to maximise educational impact across diverse staff groups." Participants reported being overwhelmed by the learning platform and needing a more user-friendly introduction, and some suggested older staff members might require additional technical support. The strategy addresses these reported technical and structural barriers so that flexibility and accessibility advantages of asynchronous learning can be realized for all staff groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A more user-friendly introduction to the learning platform for participants who feel overwhelmed by it
#### Constraints
- The requirement for self-motivation and independent time management was identified by participants as a potential challenge that platform design alone does not remove

### Target Learners
- nursing home staff of varied ages and technical experience, including nursing assistants and volunteers

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement with and completion of asynchronous online continuing education on delirium
