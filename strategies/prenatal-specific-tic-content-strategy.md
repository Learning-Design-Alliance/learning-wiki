# Develop prenatal-specific, practice-oriented TIC training content with role-specific skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants recommended developing prenatal-specific content using practice-based scenarios and trauma-informed communication strategies, including concrete role-specific skills such as trauma-informed pelvic exams and responding to disclosures of trauma. Training should move beyond conveying generic knowledge to focus on trauma-informed practices and organizational culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practice-based scenarios relevant to the prenatal context
- Structured guidance on trauma-informed language during exams and procedures
#### Constraints
- Generic TIC knowledge alone is insufficient; content must be tailored to the prenatal clinical context

### Target Learners
- Prenatal clinicians
- Certified nurse-midwives
- Obstetrician-gynecologists

### Target Learning Goals
- Trauma-informed pelvic exam technique
- Trauma-informed communication
- Responding to trauma disclosures
