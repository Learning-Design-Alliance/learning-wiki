# Devote early course time to LLM capabilities, limitations, and best practices, then let students decide for themselves whether to use LLMs in their research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
Reflecting on the first implementation of semester-long LLM-assisted research in the course, the authors conclude that "students would have benefited from one class period and/or several early homework problems devoted to understanding LLMs, their limitations, and the best current practices for their use in research." Such early exposure would let students see benefits and pitfalls and decide to what extent they wish to use LLMs going forward. In future semesters students will be encouraged early in the term to review free online resources and will decide for themselves whether to use LLMs for their research projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires course time for an introductory class period or early homework problems on LLM use, plus access to free online resources on generative model applications.
#### Constraints
- The article notes some problems encountered, such as frequent coding and citation inaccuracies, may be resolved before the next academic cycle given the rapidly improving LLM landscape.

### Target Learners
- first-year graduate astronomy students
- graduate students in research-intensive courses

### Target Learning Goals
- informed, autonomous decisions about LLM use in research
- awareness of LLM benefits and pitfalls for scientific work

## Claims

- [LLMs struggled to generate complex functional code and could not autonomously retrieve astronomical datasets from online archives or query data tables](../claims/llm-code-and-archive-access-failures.md) [+W]
- [LLMs returned false citations, links, or paper summaries about 20% of the time during graduate literature research](../claims/llm-false-citations-twenty-percent.md) [+W]
- [LLMs made implicit, overly simplifying assumptions without justification and often doubled down after being corrected, including fabricating measurement uncertainties](../claims/llm-implicit-assumptions-doubling-down.md) [+W]
- [Students reported positive surprises including near-instant literature synthesis, astronomy knowledge exceeding their own starting background, and ChatGPT diagnosing a code error from a scientific plot alone](../claims/llm-positive-surprises-literature-synthesis-plot-debugging.md) [+W]
- [All seven first-year graduate students completed draft research papers on unsolved galaxy problems using LLMs for typically 5–10 hours, though only about half judged that the models saved them time](../claims/llm-graduate-class-original-research-semester-outcomes.md) [~W]
- [For one student's dynamical simulation, Claude Opus 4 was more successful at generating and debugging code than Gemini Pro 3 and the ChatGPT-5 thinking model, though its lower usage limit constrained its use](../claims/claude-opus-4-outperformed-peers-simulation-code.md) [+W]
- [LLM assistance can enhance employees' creativity, especially for those with skills to reflect on AI use](../claims/llm-assistance-enhances-employee-creativity.md) [+W]
- [Students predominantly recommended future students use office hours and formal course staff, with LLMs positioned as a supplement](../claims/students-recommend-office-hours-over-llms.md) [+W]
