# Incorporate Astrobites into astronomy coursework using the collaboration's lesson plans and grading rubrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Astrobites collaboration assembled lesson plans and, with instructor feedback, published pedagogical tools for teaching with Astrobites, "including three sample lesson plans and grading rubrics" spanning early undergraduate through graduate stages. The collaboration also ran education workshops at the 231st AAS Meeting (2018) and EWASS 2019 on using Astrobites as an objective-based, student-focused teaching tool, and is conducting an ongoing study of its classroom efficacy via educator focus groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor familiarity with the published lesson plans; educator participation in the ongoing efficacy study
#### Constraints
- Detailed recommendations are given in Sanders et al. 2017; a thorough efficacy study is still pending per the authors

### Target Learners
- early undergraduate through graduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- reading, parsing, and analyzing scientific literature
- practicing scientific communication
