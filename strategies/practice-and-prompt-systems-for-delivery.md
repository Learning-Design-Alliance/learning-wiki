# Practice extensively and use prompt systems other than slides for effective delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states that "The key factor for effective presentation delivery is practice" and that "The goal of practice is to find a point of preparedness that makes the presenter confident and fluent, but does not destroy his or her instinctive behavior." It also recommends using notecards with highlighted key words that are "glanced at – not read from", or flash cards, mind maps, or a written summary as alternatives to reading from slides.
