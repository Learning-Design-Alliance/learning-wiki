# Use the validated KAP questionnaire to guide targeted educational interventions and programs for EVD care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nurse administrators administer the Adult EVD Nursing KAP Assessment Scale to identify gaps in nurses' EVD-related competencies and then design education accordingly. It states the tool "can guide targeted interventions and educational programs", which the authors call "crucial for enhancing the quality of care, reducing complications, and improving patient prognosis". This makes the instrument a diagnostic step preceding staff education rather than a training method itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurse administrators must administer the questionnaire and interpret dimension scores before designing interventions
#### Constraints
- The article notes the questionnaire was developed within the Chinese cultural context and may exhibit limitations when generalized to other countries

### Target Learners
- neurosurgical nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- EVD care knowledge and practices
- reduction of EVD-related complications
