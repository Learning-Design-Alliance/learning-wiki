## Description
The article's practical implication is a dual approach: train teachers in supportive, engaging teaching practices while providing comprehensive support that strengthens students' academic self-efficacy. The authors write that "Practical implications encompass implementing teacher training to enhance supportive teaching practices and providing comprehensive support to strengthen students' academic self-efficacy."
