# Include physical activity in medical school academic requirements under college administration supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend institutional action to raise activity levels among medical students, given that nearly half did not meet WHO recommendations and study load was the leading barrier. They write: "We recommend that physical activity be included in the academic requirements to overcome the reasons for the lack of regular physical activity among medical students under the supervision of the college administration." This is framed as balancing academic and personal life, not as a means of raising GPA, since no academic association was found.
