# Use a phased unplugged-to-plugged progression to build girls' CT confidence before introducing digital tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a phased, mixed pedagogical approach for encouraging girls' participation in CT. Instruction begins with unplugged activities that introduce foundational concepts so girls "build confidence and familiarity with core ideas without the immediate pressure of technology use"; once competence and engagement are established, plugged activities such as programming are gradually incorporated in a supportive, scaffolded manner. This progression is grounded in the meta-analytic finding that unplugged approaches showed negative, non-significant gender effects while plugged and mixed approaches favored males.
