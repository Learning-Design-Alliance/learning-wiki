# Motor skill development through physical education as a leverage point for improving achievement in students with insufficient performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article suggests that because motor coordination ranked as the node with the highest overall centrality for the insufficient-achievement group, "physical education classes could offer means for children to enrich their motor repertoire". The authors propose motor skill development as "a potential leverage point within the complex system influencing school achievement in this vulnerable subgroup", noting it may also positively impact attention and engagement in school activities.
