# Periodically assess teaching strategies to derive the most adequate and effective teaching plan

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nurse educators continuously evaluate their clinical teaching approaches rather than retain them by default. It states: "The study recommends that teaching strategies must be assessed periodically to derive the most adequate and effective teaching plan." In this study that assessment took the form of comparing intern-nurse outcomes under interactive versus traditional learning activities with a validated clinical assessment tool. The recommendation is grounded in the finding that the interactive approach outperformed the traditional one on all measured factors.
