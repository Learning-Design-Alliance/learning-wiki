# Bridging Cultures

## Description
The Bridging Cultures Project helps teachers understand assumptions underlying individualism and collectivism, highlighting cultural differences that may diverge from school-based practices and values.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development programs, workshops, and resources on cultural values (e.g., individualism and collectivism)
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing commitment and self-reflection. May require significant adjustments to established teaching practices.

### Affordances
- Provides insights into cultural values, facilitates communication, creates inclusive classroom environments, enhances understanding of student behaviors.

### Personalization
- Allows teachers to reflect on their own cultural biases and assumptions. Encourages adaptable teaching practices to meet diverse student needs.
