# Extend uncertainty learning to everyday contexts with fluctuation-based challenges and magnetometer experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of out-of-lab extensions in which students apply fluctuation analysis with their own devices: a challenge to capture the picture or selfie with the lowest level of fluctuations, evaluation of the comfort of means of transport such as car, bus or plane, and magnetometer experiments like the screenshot the article reproduces "using, in this case, the magnetometer". The authors also invited students to observe what happens while traveling by car or bus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need their own smartphones with the relevant sensors (camera, accelerometer or magnetometer) to carry out the challenges outside the laboratory.
#### Constraints
- The authors explicitly do not recommend the experiment studying pedestrian gait fluctuations as a function of alcohol beverage intake, which they describe as "not recommended by the authors".

### Target Learners
- science and engineering students with personal smartphones

### Target Learning Goals
- applying uncertainty and fluctuation analysis in everyday situations outside the laboratory
