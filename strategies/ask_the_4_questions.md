# Ask the 4 Questions

## Description
This approach involves asking and answering four key questions to provide quality feedback: What can the student do? What can't the student do? How does the student's work compare with that of others? How can the student do better? Providing answers to these questions helps the learner understand where they stand in regards to their work and guides them towards improvement. These four questions are also helpful when providing feedback to parents.
