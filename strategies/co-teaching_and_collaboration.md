# Co-Teaching and Collaboration

## Description
ELL teachers and math teachers collaborate to identify target content, key vocabulary, academic language, and language-focused tasks for lessons. These tasks may include writing a proof, reading a math problem, explaining problem-solving, or writing numbers as words. The ELL specialist guides the math teacher through this process, which becomes integrated into the math teacher's planning. After the lesson, teachers identify areas needing clarification and share insights with colleagues. Content teachers can use language objectives to target language demands.
