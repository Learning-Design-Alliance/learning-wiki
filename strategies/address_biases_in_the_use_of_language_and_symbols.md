# Address Biases in the Use of Language and Symbols

## Description
Honoring a wide variety of forms of language ensures that learning materials and discussions are free from bias and stereotypes. This includes avoiding language that reinforces harmful stereotypes. It also involves using inclusive language that respects diverse identities and backgrounds. This strategy promotes equity and creates a welcoming learning environment for all learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful analysis of the language used in learning materials. Instructors need to be trained in culturally responsive teaching strategies.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and resources. Some learners may be resistant to challenging their own biases and stereotypes. It's essential to create a safe and supportive learning environment.
