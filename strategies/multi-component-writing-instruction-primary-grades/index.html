Target multiple writing components (as in SRSD) rather than transcription skills alone for primary grade writing instruction¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the meta-analytic findings, the authors recommend that writing instruction for primary grade children address multiple component skills rather than transcription alone. They state that "writing instruction that targets transcription skills alone without systematic teaching of other important skills (e.g., language, self-regulation, text structure) may not have a consistent impact on composition skills," and conclude that the results "indicate a need for greater efforts that target multiple components to improve primary graders' writing skills." SRSD is one such multi-component approach, but the authors note instructional approaches addressing multiple skills are not limited to SRSD.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Systematic teaching of other important skills such as language, self-regulation, and text structure alongside or instead of transcription

Constraints¶

  • Future work is needed on multi-component instruction aligned with theoretical models to investigate efficacy and scalability

Target Learners¶

  • students in kindergarten to Grade 3
  • students with weak writing skills

Target Learning Goals¶

  • writing quality
  • writing productivity
  • written composition skills

Affordances¶

  • Diew Direct Indirect Effects Model Writing

Claims¶

  • SRSD instruction had large and consistent positive effects across composition outcomes (0.59–1.04) for primary grade students [+W]
  • For writing quality, SRSD outperformed transcription instruction (β = 1.32) and effects were larger for weak writers (β = 0.92), both significant [+W]
  • Transcription-focused instruction did not yield statistically significant effects on any composition dimension due to large variation across studies [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Systematically and explicitly teach transcription skills, vocabulary and oral language to support beginning writers' writing quality
