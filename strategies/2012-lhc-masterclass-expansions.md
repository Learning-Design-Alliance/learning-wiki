## Description
For the 2012 masterclasses the article keeps LHC data as the main thrust and plans expansions: for CMS, "a new W/Z exercise is being developed" in which students measure the W+/W- and W/Z ratios and create Z mass plots from dilepton events with calculated invariant masses below 120 GeV; the ATLAS masterclass will add data on lepton directions to probe W bosons more deeply.
