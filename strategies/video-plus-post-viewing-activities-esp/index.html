Combine video viewing with post-viewing activities to test comprehension and encourage productive use of technical vocabulary¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Following Tarnopolsky's constructivist blended learning approach, the article proposes that post-viewing activities are as important as the viewing activity itself. The study implemented this by having students complete a five-minute pre-test, watch a ten-minute mini-lesson, complete a 25-minute test, and fill in a five-minute feedback questionnaire.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Internet resources must be implemented as instructional materials alongside structured post-viewing tasks

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • first-year university students in technical sciences

Target Learning Goals¶

  • comprehension of technical ESP content
  • vocabulary production in technical English

Claims¶

  • A single exposure to video materials produced a marginal advantage in comprehension and vocabulary test scores over text-based materials in an ESP setting [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Combine video clips with Small Group Activity techniques, using video-support techniques such as slowing playback, pre- and post-viewing comprehension sessions, and repeating or pausing scenes
  • Videos
  • Video-clip techniques: silent viewing, pause-based prediction, and rewind
  • Structured YouTube viewing sequence for vocabulary lessons: pre-questions, dual viewing with pausing, group discussion, and role-play follow-up
