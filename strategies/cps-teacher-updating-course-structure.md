# Three-phase CPS teacher-updating course: instruction, teacher-as-learner practice, and coached student testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's teacher-updating course on CPS had three phases: formal instruction on the approach, teachers experiencing the method themselves as group members, and supervised application with real student classes. "beside receiving the instruction of the approach (12 hours), the teachers discussed motivations and ways to apply CPS in a high school class and participated to few hours of exercitation where the teachers itself acted as members of the group (6 hours)". Finally "the approach was tested with two mixed large classes of high school students, the teachers being involved as active coaches or as passive scouts (4 hours)".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with physics disciplinary background (40 attendees, mainly from scientific high schools)
- Time allocated to instruction, practice and supervised student sessions
- Teachers produce and examine enriched problems, building a common shared database of problems
#### Constraints
- The student-testing phase was a simulated application with teachers as tutors or coaches, not a full classroom implementation

### Target Learners
- in-service Italian high-school physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- applying the CPS method in high-school physics classes
- designing text-enriched problems
