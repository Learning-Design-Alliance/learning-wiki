# Peer-led continuing-education workshops by skillful iPad users to reduce the computer-proficiency gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends mitigating variability in users' computer proficiency by having more advanced residents and preceptors lead workshops for peers. These workshops would "facilitate sharing their knowledge on how to select apps and resources and how to use them in clinical teaching/learning, thus reducing the gap between expert and novice iPad users". In the pilot, an early-adopter participant already delivered two additional workshops beyond the introductory one.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of participants with more advanced iPad skills willing to lead workshops
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a small single-institution pilot; the article calls for future research to establish a minimum competency standard for residents and preceptors

### Target Learners
- Family medicine residents
- Clinical preceptors

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective use of tablet computers for clinical teaching and learning
- Selection and use of medical apps and curriculum resources
