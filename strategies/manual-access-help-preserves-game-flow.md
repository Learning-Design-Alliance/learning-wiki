# Make in-game help manually accessible and general-purpose, with notification markers guiding students who need it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's student-assistance redesign directs students to a specific help area addressing their issues while keeping the assistance general-purpose, requires students to manually access help materials so prompts do not break game flow, and places a notification marker on the help menu when a student performs poorly. This gives students freedom to accept help, explore at their own pace, or solve problems independently.
