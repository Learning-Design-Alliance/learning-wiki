# Build a diverse teacher pipeline through Grow Your Own grants, teacher academies, scholarships, MSI investment, and residency pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's Goal 3 and Goal 4 criteria enumerate pipeline strategies states should fund: scholarships and loan forgiveness targeting students of color, rigorous program approval standards, investment in minority serving institutions, Grow Your Own (GYO) programs, teacher academies and dual enrollment, district-preparation-program partnerships, and diversifying leadership pipelines. Massachusetts "provides competitive grants to districts to build out Grow Your Own programming" and "invests in the InSPIRED Fellowship that focuses on the recruitment of high school students into the teaching profession," but lacks scholarships, MSI investment, and residency pathways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State-level funding, guidance, and public support for the pipeline programs
#### Constraints
- The brief states recruitment efforts are compromised when districts and schools have difficulty retaining teachers of color

### Target Learners
- High school students, paraprofessionals, and candidates of color entering teaching

### Target Learning Goals
- Recruiting and preparing teachers of color
