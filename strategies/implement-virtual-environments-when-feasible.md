# Implement virtual environments in grammar teaching whenever appropriate and feasible, and teach a grammar feature as a sequence of lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that in foreign language contexts with little real exposure to the target language, teachers provide opportunities to use it in settings resembling natural ones, and that they "should seek opportunities to implement these virtual environments in their teaching practices whenever deemed appropriate and feasible". It explicitly does not claim virtual worlds must be applied at all costs or that traditional grammar teaching should be abandoned. It also endorses teaching a grammar feature as a sequence of lessons rather than single classes, as the study's treatment did.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a browser-based virtual world available for free on the Internet, usable as part of regularly scheduled instruction
#### Constraints
- The article states virtual worlds should not be applied at all costs in grammar instruction and traditional attempts need not be abandoned altogether

### Target Learners
- senior high school foreign language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- grammar structures such as the second conditional

## Claims

- [Internet-based instruction with a browser-based virtual world produced a large, statistically significant advantage over the control group on the immediate posttest of the second conditional](../claims/virtual-world-instruction-immediate-posttest-advantage.md) [+W]
- [Treatment students evaluated the lessons positively, reporting that the classes helped them master the second conditional](../claims/positive-student-evaluations-virtual-world-lessons.md) [+W]
- [The treatment did not level out inter-subject differences and made the treatment group more diverse in second conditional performance](../claims/treatment-increased-inter-subject-variation.md) [~W]
- [The treatment's benefits on the second conditional were maintained on delayed posttests four and eight weeks after instruction](../claims/virtual-world-gains-maintained-delayed-posttests.md) [+W]
