# Relationship Skills Identification and Support

## Description
This strategy involves educators identifying students with strengths or challenges in relationship skills and implementing targeted supports. Relationship skills encompass effective communication, cooperation, conflict management, and resisting inappropriate social pressure. The strategy focuses on leveraging students’ existing strengths to address their challenges. Educators observe student behavior to identify relationship skills strengths and challenges. Strategies include pairing students with positive role models, providing group assignments that foster common interests, displaying communication protocols, and using literature to explicitly teach relationship skills. Student reflection on the strategies used is essential to determine effectiveness and guide adjustments.
