# Teach advanced science concepts explicitly along their underlying neurosemantic dimensions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's authors recommend that if the underlying dimensions of neural representation of concepts in a scientific discipline are known, "it may be advantageous to intentionally teach those concepts using the underlying neurosemantic dimensions to describe, explain, and de ﬁne the concepts". As an example, they note the oscillatory nature of light and sound energy concepts could be explicitly taught together, pointing to existing undergraduate courses with titles similar to "Waves and Vibrations".
