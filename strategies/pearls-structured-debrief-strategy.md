# Use a structured debrief based on PEARLS covering teamwork, communication, and clinical reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After each simulation case, a faculty facilitator leads a structured debrief based on PEARLS (Promoting Excellence and Reflective Learning in Simulation), divided into two parts: the first on teamwork and communication skills, the second on clinical reasoning, medical knowledge, and application, including review of the critical action checklist. Each part begins with discussion of what went well, followed by areas for improvement, eliciting perspectives of the team leader, active participants, and observers.

#### Requirements
