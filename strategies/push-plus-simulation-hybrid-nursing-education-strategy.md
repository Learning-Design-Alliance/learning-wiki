# Combine push-based mobile learning with simulation practice and tiered content in a push-practice-feedback loop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the observed knowledge attrition and the insufficiency of purely didactic delivery, the authors recommend exploring "a hybrid model of “push + simulation training”" that combines behaviourist and constructivist theories to strengthen skill internalisation, and conclude that a "“push-practice-feedback” closed-loop system, combined with tiered content design and blended teaching" is needed. They also advise embedding instructional videos and synchronous consultation modules to increase media richness and engagement, and incorporating push content into continuing-education credit systems or offline workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Career-stage-adapted content development, since junior and senior nurses needed different content depths
#### Constraints
- Push-only delivery showed limited long-term knowledge retention and lacked individualisation in this trial

### Target Learners
- Clinical nurses across career stages in resource-limited hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained PIVC knowledge retention
- clinical skill internalisation
