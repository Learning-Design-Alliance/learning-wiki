Interview the student one-on-one about strengths, interests, and growth areas before writing the IEP¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's mindset-shift section instructs teachers to put the student at the center: "Make sure the student's perspective is considered. Talk one-on-one with the student about their strengths, interests, and growth areas." This can be done formally, such as with a student interview, or informally by asking what is going well and what the student thinks could help. The template then carries student input fields for academic and SEL strengths and growth areas.
