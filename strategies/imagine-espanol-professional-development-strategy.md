# Offer optional virtual or on-site professional development tailored to administrators and educators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Imagine Learning offers optional virtual or on-site professional development and training for administrators and educators, in addition to Customer Success support. Administrator sessions focus on "how to lead with success, tailoring conversations to fit each school's implementation model and goals," while educator sessions cover getting started, using reports and features, and providing targeted support for individual students.
