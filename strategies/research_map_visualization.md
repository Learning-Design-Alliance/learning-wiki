# Research Map Visualization

## Description
This is a visual representation of education and learning sciences research published in peer-reviewed journals from 2009-2018. Data from over 110,000 articles published between 2009 and 2018, found in 191 academic journals from the Web of Science database, was used to analyze the bibliographic record (title, keywords, author, cited references, and abstract) for each article and create a bibliographic coupling network, to link articles sharing at least two common references. An algorithm was used to detect groups of articles with densely shared links and gathered into nodes/circles called topics. Circle size indicates article numbers and line thickness shows connection strength.  The analysis and visualization was completed using BiblioTools and D3.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Web access, visualization tool (BiblioTools and D3).
#### Constraints
- Does not subscribe to a particular point of view about teaching and learning. More like an interactive encyclopedia than a literature review.
