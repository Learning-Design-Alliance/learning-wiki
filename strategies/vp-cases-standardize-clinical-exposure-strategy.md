# Use interactive virtual patient cases to standardize clinical exposure and reinforce core internal medicine presentations for junior trainees

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (5 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors developed a pool of VP cases "to standardize clinical exposure and improve trainees’ knowledge and perceived preparedness to manage core internal medicine cases", given variability in rotation experiences from local practice, duty hour restrictions, and competing educational requirements. VP cases let learners make diagnostic and therapeutic decisions in a safe environment and can complement curricula to help trainees manage common presentations in a protected environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases focused on junior trainees as target audience
- Interactive features such as drop-down menus and guided questions
- Cases kept concise, as trainees found the evaluated case lengthy with extraneous detail
#### Constraints
- Simulations cannot replace real world experience of patient care
- Modest effects on knowledge acquisition in this evaluation

### Target Learners
- medical students transitioning to clerkship
- first-year internal medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnostic and management skills for common internal medicine presentations
- non-medical-expert CanMEDS competencies
