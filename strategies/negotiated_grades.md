# Negotiated Grades

## Description
Grades should be negotiated between students and teachers according to agreed upon learning goals and evidence of learning in one or more languages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student-led conferences, agreed-upon learning goals
#### Constraints
- Requires significant time and effort, may not be feasible in all contexts
