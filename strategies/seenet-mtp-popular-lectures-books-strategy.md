# Popular public lectures adjusted to high school students, disseminated as books with multimedia CD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Network promoted science to broad publics through attractive public lectures on modern topics in physics, cosmology and biology-genetics, adjusted to high school students, which gathered audiences of hundreds. The lectures are "very useful for teachers as well, and available through Internet or as (two already issued) books with the corresponding CD". A bilingual (English and Serbian) book on Modern Topics in Science collects popular-nontechnical articles by leading researchers, suitable for teachers, gifted pupils and young students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers able to adjust modern research topics to a high-school audience; cooperation with a ministry or publisher for the book and CD editions
#### Constraints
- Lectures and publications were produced with UNESCO and ministry support; the authors note that closer focused research needs different financing not possible through UNESCO and similar institutions

### Target Learners
- high school students
- gifted pupils
- teachers
- undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of modern physics and science topics
- promotion of interest in science careers
