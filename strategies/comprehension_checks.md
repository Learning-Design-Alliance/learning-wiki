# Comprehension Checks

## Description
During reading activities, instructors periodically check for understanding by asking questions. These questions encourage learners to revisit their initial thoughts and predict outcomes based on textual evidence. Learners ground their responses in the text, ensuring comprehension and reinforcing critical thinking skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful development of targeted questions and active monitoring of learner responses.
#### Constraints
- If not properly scaffolded, questions may confuse learners. Over-reliance on comprehension checks can interrupt flow.
