# Adopt SQ4R in language classes and add it to teacher guidebooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend that teachers use the SQ4R technique in Turkish language and other classes, that students be encouraged to read more, and that the technique be added to the Ministry of National Education teacher's guidebook, since SQ4R was ignored there and teachers may not be aware of it. They also suggest researchers compare SQ4R with other reading comprehension techniques and test it at different education levels and subject areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- teacher familiarity with the SQ4R technique, which the guidebook currently omits
#### Constraints
- the recommendation rests on one quasi-experiment at the fourth-grade level in Turkish language classes

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension

## Claims

- [Fourth-grade students taught with the SQ4R technique scored significantly higher on reading comprehension post-tests than control-group students](../claims/sq4r-improves-reading-comprehension-posttest.md) [+W]
