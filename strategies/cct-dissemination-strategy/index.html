Disseminate conceptual change texts through teacher training and curriculum materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on their findings, the authors recommend extending conceptual change texts beyond this unit through several channels. They suggest that "Various conceptual change texts can be prepared and used for different Social Studies units and topics", that teachers receive in-service training on preparing and implementing CCTs, that teacher candidates be trained about them, and that Social Studies textbooks include CCTs as alternative activities to help overcome misconceptions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers are the basic implementers of the conceptual change texts, so training is needed for preparation and implementation.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Primary school students in social studies courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Overcoming misconceptions in social studies concepts

Claims¶

  • Conceptual change texts produced a significant pre-to-post achievement gain in the experimental group [+W]
  • The pre-to-post achievement gain was significantly larger in the conceptual change text group than in the control group [+W]
  • Conceptual change texts were more effective than traditional teaching across the nine concepts, though the democracy concept remained difficult [+W]
  • Fourth graders taught with conceptual change texts scored significantly higher on the post-test than peers taught with traditional methods [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Include case studies, worksheets and conceptual change texts in textbooks, and vary fossil examples beyond animals
  • Implementation recommendations for adopting pictorial teaching at scale
