# Integrate the seven components of a math-enhanced lesson into agricultural teacher preparation as an instructional strategy for contextualized mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the MTIS treatment had no negative effect on the three efficacy constructs and based on prior research supporting the effectiveness of the seven components of a math-enhanced lesson (Stone et al., 2006), the authors recommend that teacher education programs consider integrating these components into the teaching methods course. The strategy would have future school-based agricultural educators use the seven-component model as "an instructional strategy for teaching contextualized mathematics." The authors also state that exposure to the NCTM sub-standards is important and should be included in the agricultural teacher preparation curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instruction on the seven components of a math-enhanced lesson, access to NCTM sub-standards cross-referenced to agricultural content standards, and opportunities for preservice teachers to deliver and observe math-enhanced micro-teachings.
#### Constraints
- The article notes knowledge of contextualized concepts may alternatively discourage preservice teachers from teaching mathematical concepts or from continuing in the profession because of a lack of expected success.

### Target Learners
- preservice agricultural education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teaching contextualized mathematics in secondary agricultural education
