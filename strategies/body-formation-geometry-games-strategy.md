# Use team body-formation games in which pupils build geometric shapes with their bodies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete activity recipe from the program's main and final lesson parts: pupils work in groups and follow the teacher's verbal instructions to form right angles, rectangles and squares using their bodies, racing between marked areas. "During the main part of the lessons (lasting 30 minutes each) pupils acquired geometrical knowledge of right angles, rectangles and squares through various games." The winner was the team that formed a proper rectangle or square first, and pupils noticed that a square has four equal sides through the game itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who explains and clearly demonstrates every exercise, places pupils in a straight line beforehand, and gives verbal task instructions
#### Constraints
- The article implemented this in a large classroom/court setting with 9×9 meter boundaries; transfer to other settings is not tested

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade elementary school pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- recognizing and constructing right angles, rectangles and squares; understanding that a square's four sides are equal in length
