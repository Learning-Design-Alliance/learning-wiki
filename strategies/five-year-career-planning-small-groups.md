# Guide stage-based small groups through a five-year career planning path with senior mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students track their career paths over the next five years across their next big career transition point, in small groups organized by academic stage (undergraduate through assistant professor), each advised by a senior mentor. Groups identify barriers, solicit peer and mentor advice to build a coherent strategy, and report plans to the whole workshop. The article reports this built "a portrait of career planning that spanned from undergraduate education all the way through tenure in the academic pathway and into the ﬁrst ﬁve years of the industry workforce."
