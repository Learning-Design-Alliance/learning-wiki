# Use reading strategies inventories to identify which strategies learners currently use before designing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers help learners identify their reading strategies by using reading strategies inventories. Such inventories inform teachers and learners which reading strategies learners currently use, and having that information assists teachers in designing reading strategies instruction that focuses on strategies that are new to the learners rather than re-teaching familiar ones.
