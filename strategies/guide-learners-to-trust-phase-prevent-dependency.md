# Guide learners toward the Trust Phase while actively preventing a slide into Dependency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's closing insight states that education's goal "is not to resist AI, but to guide learners toward the Trust Phase while actively preventing a slide into Dependency." In the Trust phase AI functions as a cognitive scaffold for brainstorming, clarification, and exploratory reasoning while responsibility for evaluation and understanding remains with the learner. Success means enabling students to use AI to reach higher levels of abstraction, metacognition, and creativity without relinquishing foundational cognitive skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI integrated into learning workflows as an assistive rather than substitutive technology
- Learners actively decide when and how to use AI and assess its outputs
