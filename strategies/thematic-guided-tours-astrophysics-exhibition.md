# Organize guided tours along thematic routes selected by the audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
During guided tours, the guide focused the audience's attention on only a few pictures, following an itinerary chosen by the audience among a selection of possible thematic routes, such as 'colors in astronomy', 'the Solar System', and 'high-energy astrophysics'. Tours crossed the three main exhibition sections and were offered to both school groups and the general public, with school groups also able to visit during morning lesson time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of curated thematic itineraries and a guide able to adapt the route to the audience's choice
#### Constraints
- Guided tours focus on only a few pictures per visit rather than covering every image

### Target Learners
- general public visitors
- school groups

### Target Learning Goals
- focused understanding of selected astrophysics topics
