# Use a student-run speed-of-sound experiment as an introduction to Bayesian data analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors used their speed-of-sound measurement to introduce students to Bayesian methods, having students compare competing atmospheric-composition models via likelihoods and odds ratios. They report: "We have used our speed of sound experiment to introduce students to Bayesian data analysis methods" and that "The students seem to be able to grasp the fundamental ideas of Bayesian probability naturally." They suggest similar experiments can serve as an introduction to this analysis tool.

## Design Implications
