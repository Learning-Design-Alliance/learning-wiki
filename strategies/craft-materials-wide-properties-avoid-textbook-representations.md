# Selecting making materials with wide property ranges that avoid textbook representations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An instructor design lever in the making project is the deliberate curation of materials. The article reports: 'In selecting the materials, mostly obtained from a craft store, we specifically chose items that had a wide range of physical properties in terms of rigidity, malleability, texture.' The team also 'avoided objects that explicitly resembled "masses" or "springs" that might obviously recall textbook representations of oscillators.' This strategy positions students as designers with agency over how to initiate construction and formulate the problem.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials should each be usable in more than one way and provide a wide variety of properties
#### Constraints
- Described specifically for the oscillator project in a computational physics course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- designing physical artifacts for quantitative modeling
