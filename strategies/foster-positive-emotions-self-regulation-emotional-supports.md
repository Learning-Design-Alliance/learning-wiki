# Foster positive emotions, support self-regulatory skills, and provide emotional supports for students shifting to digital learning

## Description
From the Ge perspective on practical and policy impact, the editorial notes that teachers and students shifting rapidly to digital learning may experience negative emotions. It suggests multiple extensions: "developing and testing strategies to foster positive emotions," "supporting students with self-regulatory skills that can reduce the negative emotions," and providing emotional supports for students, along with multiple data collection methods to measure emotions.
