# Special masterclasses for the International Day of Women and Girls in Science with female scientists as role models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
IMC holds special masterclasses for the International Day of Women and Girls in Science on 11th February each year. The article states these are "much like standard masterclasses but are designed to especially benefit young women with female scientists as role models and discussions of women in physics". The format adapts the standard masterclass to provide role models and targeted discussion rather than a different measurement structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Female scientists serving as role models; discussions of women in physics
#### Constraints
- Held on 11th February each year; otherwise much like standard masterclasses

### Target Learners
- Young women high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Engaging with particle physics data analysis
- Seeing female scientists as role models
