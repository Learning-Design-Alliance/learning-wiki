# Run the Nerf Gun Challenge with balloons of varying mass to visualise the origin of the uncertainty principle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the uncertainty principle arises from photon momentum, students fire Nerf bullets at suspended balloons containing different masses of water. The article explains that "the momentum of the bullets displaces the position of the balloon causing intrinsic fuzziness in the 'image' of the balloon," with the greatest oscillation for the lightest balloon. The activity's poster frames quantum uncertainty as arising because photons disturb the things they measure, and the article notes the balloon scaling matches the uncertainty of an atom observed by a photon.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Balloons filled with 2g-150g of water, a Nerf gun, bullets, string, and an accompanying poster outlining the physics
#### Constraints
- The article states the argument is a simplification of the uncertainty principle, extendable for senior students to laser interferometer quantum limits

### Target Learners
- school students, with extensions for senior students with basic statistical knowledge

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding that measurement disturbance from photon momentum underlies the Heisenberg uncertainty principle
