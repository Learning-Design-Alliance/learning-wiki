# Promote interaction between provider and receiver of peer feedback to stimulate elaboration on judgments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend increasing the effectiveness of modeling examples by stimulating elaboration on judgments, which they argue is a form of generative learning because it challenges students to explain their thoughts coherently to others. They propose "promoting interaction between the provider and receiver of the feedback", for instance by adding a discussion between provider and receiver after both have observed a modeling example and given each other peer feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for provider–receiver dialogue after feedback is given
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of the study's null effect on elaborations and cited literature, not directly tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students giving peer feedback

### Target Learning Goals
- elaborating on feedback judgments
- generative learning through explaining
