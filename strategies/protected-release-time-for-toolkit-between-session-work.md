# Schedule protected release and preparation time for educators completing pre- and between-session toolkit work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The resource plan treats educators' out-of-session work as a budgeted resource rather than an unfunded expectation. It allocates "Time for educators who are participating in the toolkit professional development to complete pre- and between-session work outside of their normal workload," quantified per module (e.g., Modules 1–3: 2.5 to 5 hours each), and notes this may include existing planning time if other expectations are shifted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District or school agreement to shift or supply planning and release time
- Quantified time allocations per module as listed in the resource table
#### Constraints
- The document notes the additional time "may include time the educators already use for planning, if other expectations are shifted"

### Target Learners
- middle and high school algebra educators

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing recommended evidence-based algebra instructional strategies
