# Use e-learning as structured preparation before a lecture and practical seminar (blended design for skills courses)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Assign the e-learning programme as pre-course preparation before the face-to-face lecture and practical seminar, so students arrive with the theoretical background already studied. The authors state the blended approach "provided a 'structured preparation' for the lecture and practical seminar; this may be preferable to leaving students to prepare on their own for the course." This design produced the best theoretical test results while keeping practical skills performance equal to other formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an e-learning programme whose content is identical to the lecture content
- Login details distributed several days before the lecture so use is voluntary and self-paced
#### Constraints
- The blended group had more total learning time than the comparison groups, a bias the authors acknowledge; only short-term retention was assessed

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students in preclinical skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge of local anaesthesia and preparation for supervised practical injection training
