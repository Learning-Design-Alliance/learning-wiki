# State policy levers for sustaining yearlong, stipended clinical preparation: require or incentivize full-year stipended clinical experience, fund partnerships, and reduce testing fees

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For state policymakers, the report recommends that states "Require or incentivize candidates to complete a full year of stipended clinical experience to earn a teaching credential", fund EPPs and district partners to develop and sustain yearlong clinical experiences including mentor compensation, provide guidance and technical assistance for practice-based coursework redesign and shared governance, require robust EPP–district partnerships in program approval, blend and braid funds for paid residencies, and reduce or eliminate testing fees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding streams (federal, state, philanthropic) to sustain paid residencies
- Technical assistance providers experienced in practice-based course redesign
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are drawn from three case-study programs in one state context; the report states its purpose was to elevate best practices, not to comprehensively evaluate programs

### Target Learners
- Prospective and current teacher candidates statewide, particularly in high-need certification fields

### Target Learning Goals
- A well-qualified, stable, and diverse teacher workforce prepared to meet diverse student needs from day one
