# Use LLMs as pedagogical co-facilitators in professor-led learning through jury simulation, jargon translation, and metacognitive assistants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For professor-led learning, the article proposes LLM actions across four pedagogical domains: aids in giving critique (ethical reasoning prompts, creativity-focused critique modules), jargon translation and ambiguous-feedback interpretation, guides for designing within constraints plus metacognitive assistants, and jury simulation platforms. It states that "jury simulation platforms enable professors to conduct mock critiques where students rehearse argumentation and response strategies using LLM-generated juror personas," developing confidence and rhetorical precision before formal reviews.
