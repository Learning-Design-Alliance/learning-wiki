# Adapt Higher Education professional learning models to centre disciplinary culture when developing teachers' digital and pedagogical capabilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that professional learning for Higher Education teachers be redesigned around disciplinary culture rather than generic schooling-sector models. It argues that previous frameworks were developed for schools and that "The professional learning models for developing teacher capability need to be adapted for Higher Education and to centre disciplinary culture", so that capability development aligns pedagogy with each discipline's traditions and technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional resources and time to enable capability development, which the authors cite as a key resource
#### Constraints
- Recommendation arises from five disciplines in one New Zealand university; the authors note generic digital capabilities were acquired in a haphazard way and not all teachers possess them

### Target Learners
- Higher Education undergraduate teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Pedagogical practices aligned with disciplinary culture and available technologies; equitable access to quality learning experiences

### Affordances
- Disciplinary Culture Digital Change Account
