# Tossing Activity

## Description
A hands-on activity that integrates movement with learning, often involving the physical act of tossing objects. This can stimulate brain/body connections, especially in areas like math, by engaging students through movement and active participation. The activity can be adapted to various subjects by customizing the object being tossed to include numbers, visuals, or other relevant content. It promotes engagement and can foster a love for the subject matter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physical space for tossing, objects for tossing (e.g., bean bags, balls), and materials for customization (e.g., markers, numbers, visuals).
#### Constraints
- May require additional space and preparation. Could be challenging for learners with physical limitations without appropriate modifications. Requires active engagement from learners.
