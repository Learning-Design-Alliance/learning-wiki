# Replace the pre-defined lab script with a student-proposed experimental project

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the proposal seminar stage, the student "must present the necessary materials, as well as procedures and methodology to be used", designing a project to verify theoretical concepts. The article frames this as a shift in which "The old 'script' takes on a more professional role with the concept of developing a 'project'", with the teacher acting as tutor, assessing feasibility and risks while leaving protagonism with the student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher reviews proposals for feasibility and possible risks before students carry out the experiments
- Laboratory is available to students and they can discuss proposals with the laboratory technician
#### Constraints
- The article notes the high degree of freedom can facilitate failures such as a lack of responsibility, which students themselves flagged in survey item 6

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics laboratory students, applied to engineering in the case studied

### Target Learning Goals
- creativity and critical sense in proposing materials and equipment
- connecting theory to experimental practice
