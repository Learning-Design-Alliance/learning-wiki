# Future research should use standardized validated outcome measures, longitudinal follow-up designs, and methods linking learner gains to patient-relevant outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research prioritize standardized and validated outcome measures, longitudinal follow-up designs, and methodologies capable of examining whether educational gains translate into patient-relevant outcomes such as satisfaction, trust, and adherence. It notes mixed-method approaches and multilevel study designs may be particularly valuable, aligning with Level 4 of Kirkpatrick's evaluation model, which emphasizes behavioral change and improvements in patient outcomes. Greater emphasis should also be placed on curricular integration, structured feedback mechanisms, and resource considerations.
