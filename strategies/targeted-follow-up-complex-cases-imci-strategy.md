# Target training and supervision resources at underperforming health workers and at complex, severe, and co-morbid cases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends directing quality-improvement resources toward individually identified weak performers and toward patient types the training underserved. It states supervisors "should focus more attention on underperforming health workers", and that managers "should take advantage of training modalities such as videos to expose health workers to more complex cases". Supervisors should review documented danger-sign and anemia cases during visits and refresher training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular evaluation of individual health worker performance to identify underperformers
- Supervision visits and refresher training in which documented complex cases are reviewed
#### Constraints
- Derived from an observational cohort in a small geographical area of Benin immediately after initial IMCI introduction; the authors call for confirming the associations through additional studies

### Target Learners
- Facility-based health workers caring for children under five in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Adherence to IMCI case-management guidelines, including assessment, classification, and treatment of potentially life-threatening childhood illnesses
