# University teachers should act as role models by explicitly communicating lecture goals and time plans and reminding students to start exam preparation early

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's teacher-level recommendation is that "University teachers can support the development of SRL strategies by acting as role models", achieved "by explicitly communicating lecture goals and time plan as well as by reflecting whether these goals have been achieved". Teachers are also advised to "remind students to start studying for examinations early". This operationalises the process model of SRL's preaction planning phase within the lecture context, supporting students' own planning and time management.
