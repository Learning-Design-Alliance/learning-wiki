# Build trusting relationships among workgroup participants through structured facilitation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The R15CC team deliberately cultivated meaningful and trusting relationships among participants as "an essential foundation for authentically sharing opinions and experiences." Practices included purposefully assigning participants to small-group breakout sessions, strategically using icebreakers, and using outside facilitators to mitigate positional power, so participants could challenge each other's ideas and ask hard questions about past and current practices.
