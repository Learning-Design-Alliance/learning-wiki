Project-based assessment with community exhibition for WRD learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study used project-based assessment for assessing students' projects relevant to WRD, consisting of 13 items measured on a four-point scale of very good, good, fair and needs improvement. Students in the experimental group created problem-solving projects that were presented and exhibited to the community, such as the "Drought in Our Home" Project and the "Smart Children on Nam Phong River Conservation" Project. The article reports these projects showed that students have knowledge and attitude concerns about WRD and have thinking skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Projects must focus on solving problems relevant to WRD
  • Community presentation and exhibition opportunities must be arranged

Constraints¶

  • Applied specifically to water resource and disaster management content in ninth-grade science

Target Learners¶

  • Ninth-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Problem-solving skills relevant to water resource management
  • Environmental knowledge and attitudes

Related Strategies¶

  • Exhibition Of Learning
  • Give learners control over how learning is measured via assessment choices and science-fair-type projects
  • Varying Levels of Choice in Assessments
