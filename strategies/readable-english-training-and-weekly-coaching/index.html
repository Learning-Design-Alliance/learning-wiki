Pair program adoption with two days of initial training and weekly coaching meetings¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The page specifies a professional development model for implementing Readable English: "Two days of training prior to implementation, in addition to weekly meetings with an assigned Readable English reading coach hired by the district". This combines upfront scripted-lesson training with ongoing, district-hired coaching support throughout implementation, alongside a small group reading instructor/teacher staffing requirement.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District hires an assigned Readable English reading coach
  • Two days of training prior to implementation

  • Fidelity of implementation of scripted phonics, decoding, fluency, and comprehension lessons
