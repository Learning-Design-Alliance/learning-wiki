# Run organized humanistic activities — forums, reflective writing, and small-group programs — to shape an organizational culture of humanism

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy implements the study's organized-activities theme: institutions schedule recurring programs that bring colleagues together around humanistic practice. Study physicians believed such programs helped shape the organizational culture because they "allowed for deeper discussions with colleagues" and "allowed the formation of a community of professionals that would reinforce the importance of treating others with respect." Named examples include Schwartz Rounds, The Healer's Art, Reflection Rounds, reflective writing exercises, and the faculty development program itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular scheduling that allows deeper discussions among colleagues and community formation
#### Constraints
- Described as downstream activities that may assist physicians in dealing with stress without altering practice
