# Use clickers as a supplementary tool to enhance ESP learners' performance, including embedding clicker activities in ESP course book iTools sets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that ESP teachers use clickers in their classes and that course designers allocate more clicker use in the iTools sets of ESP books instead of providing just videos of related topics. It argues clickers "may be used as a supplementary tool to enhance performance of ESP learners in the field of tourism and hospitality", turning students' smartphones from distractors into pedagogical tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors need a computer, a projector, and clickers, which can be replaced with mobile phones running classroom applications.
- Questions should be prepared in advance and supported with pictures or videos where relevant.
#### Constraints
- The article states results cannot be generalized to the entire population of tourism and hospitality students due to the diverse nature of these students.

### Target Learners
- ESP students at 2-year vocational schools, particularly in tourism and hospitality

### Target Learning Goals
- ESP language performance in tourism-specific topics
