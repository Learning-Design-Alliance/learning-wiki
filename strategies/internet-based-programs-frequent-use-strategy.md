# Use attention-attracting internet-based education programs more frequently, with infrastructure, mobile compatibility and teacher awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the study's results the authors recommend that "Internet-based education programs, which call and attract students' attention, must be used more frequently." They further suggest setting up sufficient infrastructure for internet-based programs, developing mobile-compatible applications so students can use them while spending time on mobiles, focusing research and development and curriculum development efforts on internet-based education, and raising teachers' awareness with practical demonstrations of the applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient substructure concerning internet-based programs; teacher awareness-raising with practical demonstrations
#### Constraints
- Recommendations follow from one small quasi-experiment in a single mathematics topic at one study centre

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement in mathematics topics taught with internet-based materials
