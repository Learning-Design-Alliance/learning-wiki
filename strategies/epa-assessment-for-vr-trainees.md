# Pair VR haptic training with Entrustable Professional Activities (EPA) assessment to gauge trainee readiness for independent surgical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As a forward-looking recommendation for patient safety, the authors propose: "it would be optimal to introduce Enstrustable professional activities (EPA) assessment to establish when trainees are ready for independent practice and less supervision." Under this approach the clinical activity context is adjusted to the trainee's progress by increasing patient complexity and environmental factors at the individual's rate of development, complementing simulator-derived performance data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EPA assessment frameworks and progression data from simulator sessions to inform supervision-level decisions
#### Constraints
- Presented as a future goal, not implemented in this case report

### Target Learners
- postgraduate dental trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- readiness for independent surgical practice
- individualized progression of supervision level
