# Integrate coping psychoeducation into academic support workshops and counselling for university teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "academic support systems could integrate psychoeducation on coping and well‐being directly in workshops and counselling". The proposed support includes three components: discussing different ways of coping and building flexible strategy repertoires that include task-oriented strategies; engaging faculty in reflective exercises on how they previously used such strategies and how effective they were; and offering practical training on situationally fitting, high-quality strategy implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further research based on the present findings is necessary to effectively guide such interventions, per the article
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a two-wave correlational study during the COVID-19 pandemic; the article calls for replication in less demanding situations

### Target Learners
- university teaching staff across ranks (doctoral students, postdocs, professors)

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge and flexible, high-quality use of coping strategies for teaching-related stress
