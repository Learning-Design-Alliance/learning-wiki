Involve teachers as co-designers of AI-based intelligent systems, with agency to train, correct, interpret, and override system recommendations¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The reflections panel recommends human-centered AI in learning: 'Empower and involve teachers (in addition to subject matter experts, learning scientists, and technologists) from the beginning as co-designers of AI-based intelligent systems'. Teachers should be enabled to train and correct systems that respond incorrectly, and 'Teachers and students should also be given agency to interpret and potentially override a system's recommendations'.

Related Strategies¶

  • Center co-design with teachers and students throughout AI tool R&D, using human-in-the-loop workflows as a primary risk mitigation
  • Seven research priorities for AI in learning, spanning expanded scenarios, teacher assistance, assessment, responsible AI, equity policy, stakeholder engagement, and ecosystem strengthening
  • Layered educator support: coaching, curated resources, low-lift entry points, and community engagement
  • Systemic, human-centred approach treating digital data use as a school-and-teacher system, with AI integrated into professional development
  • AI system developers should ensure human-centric design, institutional leaders should stay updated on global AI regulations, and LA experts should engage policymakers through dialogue
