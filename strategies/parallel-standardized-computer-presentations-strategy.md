# Use parallel, standardized interactive computer presentations with multimodal demonstrations for technique-skill training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article built PowerPoint-based trainings comprising "written text, oral explanations, pictures, and videos", with separate presentations for left and right handers, a mirror for self-checking, and prompts to exercise immediately what is seen. Participants can navigate and repeat every part as often as they want, supporting self-paced practice. The same structure was used for Fones, Bass, and control conditions, differing only in the technique shown.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Presentations must be parallel in major aspects (slides, videos, design, demonstrations) when comparing techniques
#### Constraints
- Computer-based training as provided took about 45 minutes, which exceeds what is commonly provided in dental practice

### Target Learners
- adult university students learning oral hygiene skills

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of a toothbrushing technique (motor skill)
