# Encourage young children to write with touch screen tablets at home to support emergent literacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's findings suggest that the frequency of writing with tablets, not just e-book reading, is the tablet activity associated with emergent literacy skills. Parents and educators can therefore prioritise tablet writing activities, since "a positive association was found between the frequency of writing with tablets and print awareness, print knowledge, and sound knowledge". The author recommends further research to test effects experimentally before strong causal claims are made.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Children need access to tablet apps that support writing activity in the home.
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is correlational; the article states further research is needed to investigate effects of tablet writing on emergent literacy development.

### Target Learners
- children aged 2–4 years

### Target Learning Goals

- print knowledge
- sound awareness (phonological awareness)
