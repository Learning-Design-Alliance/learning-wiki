# Deploy the VR simulator as a tiered training and remote/team-based education platform across experience levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using the simulator differentially by trainee stage: as an assessment tool for experienced surgeons, a focused training platform for fellows, and an introductory experience for MFM specialists exploring fetal therapy. The platform's "capacity for remote education opens new possibilities for collaborative surgical networks", particularly benefiting surgeons in lower-volume centers, and augmented reality allows whole-team practice of room setup, safety protocols, and emergency scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meta Quest 3 hardware and transport case for portability
- educator interpretation of performance data for competency decisions
#### Constraints
- further multi-centre studies are needed to fully establish the platform's educational efficacy

### Target Learners
- fetal therapy fellows
- maternal-fetal medicine specialists considering fetal therapy specialization
- surgical teams in lower-volume centers

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural competency in fetoscopic laser surgery
- team-based operating room preparation and safety protocols
- maintenance of skills through regular simulation practice
