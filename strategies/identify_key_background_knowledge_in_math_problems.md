# Identify Key Background Knowledge in Math Problems

## Description
Identify what kinds of background knowledge learners need to understand a math problem. Brainstorm potential questions learners might have, some relevant and some not. Examples: a math problem about a coupon and sales tax at a dog groomer requires knowing what a dog groomer, sales tax, coupon, deluxe, estimate, and discount are. This area is powerful for collaboration between math teachers and ELL teachers.
