## Description
The report recommends including professional skills in undergraduate QISE education because "Employers consistently identify communication and collaboration as critical skills." It states that these skills "should be embedded within QISE courses and programs to ensure students are prepared for industry roles," reflecting the finding that master's-level roles in particular stand out for their communication and collaboration demands.
