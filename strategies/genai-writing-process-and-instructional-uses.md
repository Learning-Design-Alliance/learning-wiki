# Use GenAI across the writing process stages of pre-writing, drafting language support, and revision feedback, and for discussion questions, differentiated explanations, and scaffolded practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The classroom-practices brief states "GenAI, when used responsibly, could be used to support students' writing processes" at pre-writing and planning, drafting language support, and revision and feedback stages, with example prompts for each. It also lists instructional uses: generating discussion questions, differentiated explanations, and practice problems with scaffolding. After outputs are generated, students learn to analyze for accuracy, evaluate bias, create improved versions, and reflect.
