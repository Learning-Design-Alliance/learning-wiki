Teach ESP technical vocabulary through sequenced pre-task, task-cycle and post-task activities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's Appendix A gives an implementable recipe for task-based technical vocabulary teaching: pre-task schema activation and context guessing, cooperative dictionary use, negotiation of meaning between A and B groups, information-gap word-definition pairing, guessing and matching tasks, then post-task re-reading, teacher-diagnosed problem areas, fill-in-the-blank and word-class sorting exercises. The authors state the three stages "can be used to teach vocabulary and other language skills" and that it "can be an alternative to the common PPP model".

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Class time divided into pre-task, task cycle and post-task phases
  • Pair and group work with dictionaries and word/definition materials

Constraints¶

  • Developed and tested for Persian literature ESP students in an Iranian university context

Target Learners¶

  • University ESP students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Technical vocabulary knowledge
  • Communicative language use

Affordances¶

  • Tblt Three Phase Framework

Claims¶

  • Task-based teaching of technical vocabulary produces significantly better post-test performance than traditional translation-based teaching in Iranian ESP courses [+W]
  • Within the task-based group, technical vocabulary scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test [+W]
  • The control group taught by the traditional method showed no significant pre-test to post-test improvement [+W]
