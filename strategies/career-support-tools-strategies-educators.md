# Educators and policymakers develop career support tools and strategies informed by a complete overview of career choice factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that knowledge of the factors influencing career choice be used for "the development of tools and strategies for career support by educators and policymakers", and that educational programs consider how the benefits of student agency can be communicated with those who start clinical training. An overview of all influencing factors is presented as useful for supporting students and for a better distribution of graduates across specialties.
