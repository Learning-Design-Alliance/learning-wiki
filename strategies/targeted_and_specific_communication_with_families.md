# Targeted and Specific Communication with Families

## Description
Tailoring message content for different populations, such as non-native English speakers, families with different cultural backgrounds, and those with students in special education. Messages should focus on the individual student’s progress or contain steps families can take to support their child. Schools should also offer modes of two-way communication between educators and families.
