# Practise PPE procedures in simulation without consuming scarce equipment: verbal walkthroughs, PPE stickers, and simulated props

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because PPE and certain medical equipment are in actual or anticipated short supply, the article proposes ways to practise during simulation without consuming them: stating how PPE will be applied and where it is located in the facility, using PPE stickers that participants apply at appropriate moments, or using props or simulated PPE such as cloths with elastic bands to represent masks. Reusable equipment poses its own transmission risks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of current or anticipated supply shortages for PPE and medical equipment
#### Constraints
- Using potentially scarce equipment for simulation may not be justifiable unless it is reusable, which poses its own transmission risks

### Target Learners
- obstetric health care teams

### Target Learning Goals
- correct PPE selection, donning, and doffing
