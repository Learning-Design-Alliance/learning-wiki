# Think-Write-Pair-Share

## Description
Think-Write-Pair-Share is a collaborative learning strategy where students first think individually about a topic or question, then write down their thoughts, pair with a partner to discuss their responses, and finally share their ideas with the larger group. This method encourages individual reflection and active participation before engaging in group discussion. It can be used as an introduction to a topic, a formative assessment, or a way to generate ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A topic or question, writing materials, and time for individual thinking, pair sharing, and whole-class sharing.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation to ensure all students participate actively. Some students may be hesitant to share their ideas with the larger group. Effectiveness depends on the quality of the topic or question.
