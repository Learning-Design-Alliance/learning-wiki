# Investigate bright spots to identify and disseminate effective practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In continuous improvement efforts, unusually large class-level gains in learning conditions (“bright spots”) are treated as leads on effective strategies. Improvement teams investigate what went right by “holding community of practice discussions and talking to students about the data,” then disseminate hypothesized effective approaches to other classes on the team and test whether their shifts improve. Over time, once-exceptional performance is expected to become commonplace.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple planned improvement cycles with class-level learning conditions data so that bright spots can be identified and investigated
#### Constraints
- Bright-spot investigation depends on having some classes with dramatic positive shifts, which the report's data show emerged mainly at sites with 4+ planned cycles

### Target Learners
- middle and high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving learning conditions and spreading effective classroom practices across improvement teams
