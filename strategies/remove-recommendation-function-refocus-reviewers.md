# Editors should remove the reviewer recommendation function and refocus reviewers on strengths, weaknesses and improvement suggestions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because editors give considerable weight to reviewer recommendations despite unclear category definitions, reviewer disagreement and bias, the article recommends that "journal editors should seriously consider removing the reviewers' 'recommendation function'". This would realign reviewers as advisors, place editors in the sole decision-maker position, and refocus reviewer effort on evaluating strengths, weaknesses, flaws and suggestions for improvement across multiple dimensions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Considerable efforts should be made to communicate to peer reviewers the new focus on evaluation and improvement suggestions
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in the study's interview findings and existing research on recommendations versus editorial outcomes, not in a test of removal itself

### Target Learners
- journal editors
- peer reviewers of biomedical journals

### Target Learning Goals
- aligning reviewer roles with editorial decision making
