# Use a concentrated medicine procedure service with expert faculty to standardize assessment and feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 6 studies (3 design, 2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states that "by using a concentrated group of expert faculty, the MPS model facilitates more consistent training, standardized evaluation with less interrater variability, and the delivery of robust feedback." The MPS is a 7-day/week consultation service staffed by hospitalist attendings and residents. It provides early simulation-based familiarization with assessment tools before clinical exposure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A small dedicated group of expert faculty involved in tool development
#### Constraints
- Maintaining standardized, unbiased assessments becomes increasingly complex as the supervisor cohort grows in size and diversity

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents rotating on a procedure service

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate procedural skill assessment and reliable feedback
