# Begin laboratory training with explicit laser safety protocols before any alignment work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Tutorial 1 opens with a laser safety section that students must absorb before touching the equipment, covering laser classifications, eye-level discipline, beam termination, and reflective-surface hazards. The first rule given is to "Always assume a laser is dangerous and treat with caution and respect," and students are told to "Never look into the output of a laser." Safety notes recur throughout later tutorials, such as the instruction to use wooden rather than plastic or metal rulers because reflective ruler surfaces are a safety hazard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Safety instruction must precede hands-on alignment work; beam blocks and safe beam termination must be in place before powering the laser
#### Constraints
- Written for laboratory lasers including Class 4 lasers that can cause permanent retinal damage in a fraction of a second

### Target Learners
- students with no prior optical laboratory training

### Target Learning Goals
- safe working practices in an experimental optical laboratory
