# Assign students new word problems requiring them to discover their own solution steps and algorithms.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "to efficiently enhance students' problem solving skills they should be assigned word problems which are new to them and to which they themselves have to find the steps to the solution, the algorithm." This strategy develops independent thinking and problem-solving skills rather than procedural recall.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problems must be genuinely new to students so they cannot simply apply a memorized procedure.
#### Constraints
- Course books and workbooks in Romania contain no open problems, limiting access to suitable material.

### Target Learners
- university students
- school students

### Target Learning Goals
- independent problem solving
- algorithm discovery
- mathematical competence
