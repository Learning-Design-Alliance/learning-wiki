# Use unmixed partial derivatives in quantum mechanics to probe students' separation of variables structure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that the "simpler, unmixed partial derivatives in quantum mechanics better illustrate effective use of pulling out and functions as constants", and "allow for better probes of the structure of students' separation of variables resource". Instructors can therefore use free-particle TDSE problems to observe how students productively perform partial derivatives and solve PDEs.
