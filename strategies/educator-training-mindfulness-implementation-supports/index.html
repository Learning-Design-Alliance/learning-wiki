Train and support educators to implement mindfulness through programs, coaching, and shared leadership¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends training teachers to directly facilitate mindfulness activities, using resources such as Mindful Schools courses for under-resourced public schools, the CARE program to increase prosocial behavior by improving educators' well-being and student relationships (Jennings & Greenberg, 2009), and the Mindfulness in Schools Project for educators serving ages 7–18. It also recommends integrating mindfulness into coaching, extending professional development schoolwide via a train-the-trainer model, sharing leadership with student ambassadors, and monitoring effectiveness against articulated goals.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School leaders make mindfulness a part of their approaches to leadership and instructional support

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • teachers and school educators
  • school leaders
  • student ambassadors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • educator stress reduction and self-regulation
  • classroom management strategies
  • prosocial student behavior

Related Strategies¶

  • Select evidence-based mindfulness programs matched to school needs assessment data
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Support first-year adopters of a comprehensive curriculum with quick-start overviews, editable materials, and curated course plans
  • Integrate mindfulness throughout the school day via mindful moments, pausing before reacting, and reflective norms
  • Use targeted mindfulness supports for at-risk students with trauma-informed adaptation and participation choice
