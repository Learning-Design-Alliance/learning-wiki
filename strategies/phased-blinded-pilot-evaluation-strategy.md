# Use blinded external evaluators and a phased refine-then-reevaluate process when piloting an educational simulation system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study ran three phases: iterative development, initial testing by two fellows whose feedback guided adjustments, and a final evaluation by six fellows 'blinded to and not involved in the development and initial evaluation phases' who rated the same 20 cases with a standardised Likert form. This allowed comparison of scores before and after refinement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardised evaluation form with Likert-scale items across defined domains
- Evaluators independent of the development process
#### Constraints
- The evaluation tool was not formally calibrated or independently validated
- All evaluators were co-authors, so positivity bias cannot be ruled out

### Target Learners
- gastroenterology fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluation of educational simulation tools
