# Districts should clearly articulate the EL leader with full contact details, use consistent terminology, and describe programs and policies on websites

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy, offered by the article's authors for communications directors, web developers, and EL program staff, directs districts to make their websites and communications outlets more navigable. Districts should "Clearly articulate the leader or director of English Learner programs and services, and always include a name, title, direct phone number, and email contact information," use consistent terms for students and programs, provide clear descriptions of programs and the federal and state policies they address, and offer accessible high-quality translation of site content that augments rather than replaces interpretation services.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A named leader or director of English Learner programs and services with name, title, direct phone number, and email contact information
- Consistent terms describing students and programs across district communications
#### Constraints
- Translated website information should augment rather than replace robust interpretation services for events, written communications, and telephone conversations

### Target Learners
- Secondary English Learners and their families

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable access to information about English Learner programs and services
