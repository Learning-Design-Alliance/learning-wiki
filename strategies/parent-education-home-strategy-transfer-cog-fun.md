# Involve parents through post-session education and environment customization so execution strategies transfer to home homework routines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After each intervention session, therapists consulted with parents and "provided parent education that enhances the understanding of children with ADHD and appropriately customizing environment for each child to perform tasks". The authors list parent-related factors for improvement, including "setting realistic goals" and learning execution strategies in home environments. Parents thus transfer the child's strategies into home homework routines.
