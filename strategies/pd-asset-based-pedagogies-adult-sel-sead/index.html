Professional development in asset-based pedagogies and adult SEL to build culturally affirming environments¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief argues educators need skills to create culturally affirming environments, build relationships with students, and support academic success, plus 'mindsets geared toward anti-racism.' It recommends professional learning in asset-based pedagogies such as culturally responsive practices, diversity, equity, and inclusion, adult social-emotional learning (SEL) or adult mindsets, and evidence-based, equity-focused systemic SEL for students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educator mastery of a combination of skills and knowledge needed to equitably integrate and support SEAD
  • Robust funding for districts to create evidence-based professional development structures
  • Educator competency requirements covering equitable practices such as cultural competency and positive discipline

Constraints¶

  • The brief notes Massachusetts should still require DEI or anti-bias/anti-racism training in preparation programs and SEAD mastery for licensure renewal
  • Tennessee's current PD policies lack clear equity-centered goals addressing adult mindsets and SEL, per the brief

Target Learners¶

  • Educators and school adults supporting P-12 students' SEAD
  • All adults in schools, and through them P-12 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Educator mindsets and skills for culturally affirming, safe, and supportive learning environments
  • Educator capacity to support students' well-being and academic success through culturally affirming environments
