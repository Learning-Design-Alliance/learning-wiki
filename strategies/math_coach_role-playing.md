# Math Coach Role-Playing

## Description
Organize students into small groups where they take turns acting as "math coaches." One student reads a word problem aloud, and the other students (the math coaches) advise the reader on which mathematical operation to use in solving the problem. Math coaches listen closely, underline key words in the problem, and use flash cards to "flash" the mathematical operation they decide to use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word problems, key word flash cards, and small groups of students.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation to ensure all students participate and benefit from the activity. Some students may be hesitant to take on the role of "math coach."

### Affordances
- Provides a collaborative and engaging approach to problem-solving. Encourages active listening, critical thinking, and communication skills. Reinforces math concepts through peer teaching and feedback.

### Personalization
- Allow students to choose roles within the group. Provide different levels of word problems based on student abilities.
