# Deliver enhanced ward nurse education combining a lecture, a wall-displayed leaflet, and weekly review sessions to improve inpatient colonoscopy bowel preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's enhanced education for ward nurses combined an initial lecture with a question-and-answer session, a leaflet posted on the ward wall, and "brief review training sessions for nurses were repeated every week for 1 month". The training covered the importance of bowel preparation, possible adverse effects, the precolonoscopy diet and its rationale, purgative completion instructions, and the importance of drinking additional water. The authors conclude clinicians should apply ward nurse education in each healthcare facility for better inpatient bowel preparation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert endoscopists deliver the initial education with an opportunity for nurses to pose questions, and the leaflet remains displayed on the ward for ongoing review.
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in a single institution with gastroenterology-department inpatients only, so care should be taken in generalization, and the duration of the education effect was not evaluated.

### Target Learners
- Ward nurses caring for hospitalized patients scheduled for colonoscopy

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding the importance and rationale of bowel preparation, diet and purgative instructions, and adverse effects, in order to deliver effective patient care and information
