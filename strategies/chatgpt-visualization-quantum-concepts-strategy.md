# Use ChatGPT-generated visualizations to make abstract quantum mechanics concepts accessible to undergraduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends having students request ChatGPT to solve a quantum problem, generate a visualization script, and iterate on it, so that abstract results such as orbital shapes, symmetry, and superposition become dynamically observable. The authors state that AI tools' "ability to dynamically illustrate complex ideas offers a promising avenue for enhancing students' comprehension of difficult concepts in quantum mechanics" and that this approach can support educators in making difficult concepts more accessible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to ChatGPT (the paper used ChatGPT-4o) and a Python environment in which to run the generated scripts
#### Constraints
- The paper demonstrates the strategy on the specific case of hydrogen 2p orbitals; it reports no empirical evaluation of learning gains

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students newly introduced to quantum mechanics

### Target Learning Goals
- understand symmetry, mixed states, and superposition in quantum mechanics
