# Do not use ChatGPT3.5 as an in-class question-answering tool in physics; ChatGPT4 may serve as a student copilot

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends against deploying ChatGPT3.5 as an in-class tool students consult for brief clarifying questions, because "it’s hit rate for correct answers and cor- rect explanations is simply too low" and the risk of confusing students is too great. It suggests ChatGPT4 "seems likely to have crossed the crucial threhsold" and may be usable as a copilot akin to calculators, while leaving desirability open.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A model whose correct-answer and correct-explanation rate is high enough not to mislead students, as the article states for ChatGPT4.
#### Constraints
- The article does not opine on whether copilot use would be desirable; ChatGPT4 was subscription-only with rate limitations at the time of writing.

### Target Learners
- introductory university physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of kinematics and Newtonian dynamics
