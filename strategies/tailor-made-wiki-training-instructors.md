# Provide EFL instructors with tailor-made training on integrating wikis in business writing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that EFL instructors receive tailor-made training on using wikis in business writing instruction so that students' business writing develops smoothly and writing anxiety is reduced. The training "should provide teachers with valid strategies to be implemented in the classroom to incorporae wikis successfully in writing classes", drawing on the reviewed guidelines for orienting students, structuring wikis and guiding collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must first develop their own skills with Web 2.0 technology and understand the wiki's potentialities
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single study at one university; the author calls for replication with larger samples

### Target Learners
- EFL university students in business writing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Business writing skills and reduced writing anxiety
