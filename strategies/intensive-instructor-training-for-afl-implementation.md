# Train instructors intensively before implementing assessment for learning in a new setting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the study's success, the researcher recommends generalizing assessment for learning in Sudanese settings but conditions this on preparation: "the instructors and teachers need to be given intensive training on how to conduct this form of assessment". In the study itself, the experimental-group instructor received a five-day intensive training course during the break between semesters before the experiment began.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be fully acquainted with the philosophy and merits of the approach before implementation
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is framed for a Sudanese university setting and generalization there
