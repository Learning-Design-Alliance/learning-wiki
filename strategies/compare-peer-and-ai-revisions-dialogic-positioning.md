# Have students compare their peer-feedback revisions with AI-generated revisions to examine dialogic positioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "peer feedback and AI-generated revisions can be used together as complementary resources in the revision process". Students first revise drafts based on peer feedback, then compare their revisions with AI-generated versions, guided by teacher questions about whether an expression strengthens or softens the claim and how it positions alternative viewpoints. Teachers then select representative examples for class discussion of which Engagement-resource choices are appropriate in context.
