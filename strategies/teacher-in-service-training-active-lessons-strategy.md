# Train classroom teachers via an initial in-service workshop with ongoing individual feedback and annual boosters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A implementable training recipe for classroom-delivered active lessons: "Teachers in intervention schools were trained to deliver A + PAAC during a 6-hour in-service session conducted at the school prior to implementation of the intervention," followed by individual meetings with study staff for one-on-one feedback and a 4-hour booster in-service at the start of intervention years 2 and 3. Teachers personally engage in the lessons to model active behavior, and receive a written teacher's guide with sample lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A research staff trainer experienced with elementary school physical activity; alignment with existing district in-service schedules
#### Constraints
- The article notes classroom staff observing physical activity and attention-to-task cannot be blinded to condition, a measurement limitation rather than a training one

### Target Learners
- Elementary school classroom teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Skills to deliver physically active academic lessons, reduce lesson planning time, make smooth transitions, and act as active role models
