# Incorporate learner-recommended external question banks and apps alongside instructor-curated OLRs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants recommended specific external websites and apps—such as PassMedicine, Quesmed, Anki Flashcards, and Quizzez—for testing knowledge across stages. The article notes that "learners' resource selection is highly dependent on peer recommendations, far more than on instructor recommendations," and provides a table of recommended resources per stage. Educators can use these learner-generated recommendations to supplement the formally curated resource list.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors should gather learner recommendations (e.g., via open-ended survey questions) and review them for alignment with course objectives
#### Constraints
- Recommended external resources may not align with session objectives or assessments, a content issue participants themselves raised

### Target Learners
- Early-year medical undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-testing and exam preparation using question banks
- Supplementing instructor-curated resources with peer-recommended tools
