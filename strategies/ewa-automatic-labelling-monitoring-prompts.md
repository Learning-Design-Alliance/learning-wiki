# Future EWA development: automatic labelling and monitoring of interactions to prompt students from questioning to writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a system-design implication, the authors propose that "Future EWA development could integrate automatic labeling and monitoring of interactions to prompt students to move from asking questions to writing essay sections". Such monitoring would detect passive questioning behaviour in real time and nudge students toward submitting drafts, so that they "benefit more fully from GenAI -supported learning environment". This builds directly on the study's qualitative finding that active draft submission distinguished higher from lower writing-quality groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interaction logs that can be automatically labelled with the study's student action codes (e.g., hec, hep, w) during writing sessions
#### Constraints
- Proposed as future work; not implemented or evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students using GenAI writing assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- moving from passive questioning to active writing and feedback-seeking in GenAI-supported essay writing
