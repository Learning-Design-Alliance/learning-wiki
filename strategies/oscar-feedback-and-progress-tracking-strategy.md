# Use the PK OSCAR for self-guided study, evaluator feedback, and tracking resident performance over time

## Description
The article recommends using the rubric in two ways: 'This type of evaluation can be used individually by residents to guide their study and by evaluators to provide feedback and track resident performance over time.' Because the numerical scoring allows a total score to be monitored over time, the tool supports progression toward surgical competency and benchmarking in training programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The rubric's numerical scoring (novice = 2 through competent = 5) to allow total scores to be calculated and monitored over time
#### Constraints
- The tool applies to learners performing routine PK only; combination procedures and complex therapeutic cases are not addressed

### Target Learners
- ophthalmology residents
- surgical evaluators and program faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical skill development in penetrating keratoplasty
- formative feedback and progress tracking
