# Align video recording decisions with residents' self-regulated learning goals rather than investigator-determined parameters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review found that in all included studies, investigators rather than residents determined video recording parameters, "revealing a gap in aligning video practices with personalised learning trajectories". The authors recommend that "More tailored educational practices could be achieved through fostering self-regulated learning, stimulating reflective practices and providing more helpful goal-based feedback". This strategy calls for letting residents initiate and direct video recording toward their own learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents able to articulate learning objectives
- Recording infrastructure flexible enough for resident-initiated capture
#### Constraints
- The review notes it remains unclear whether recordings in Wouda et al. were aligned with the content of residents' learning objectives

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulated learning
- reflective practice
