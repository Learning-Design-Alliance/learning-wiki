# Model the photoelectric effect by firing Nerf bullets at ping-pong balls in bowls of varying depth

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 theoretical, 1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In this interactive activity, Nerf bullets represent photons of a given frequency, and ping-pong balls resting in bowls represent electrons bound in a metal. As the article puts it, "The depth of the bowls represent the magnitude of the metal's work function." Balls in shallow bowls are ejected while those in deeper bowls are not, mirroring threshold frequency behaviour; an alternative arrangement of bowls in ascending depth lets students find the threshold depth for their bullet energy.
