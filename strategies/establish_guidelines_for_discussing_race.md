# Establish Guidelines for Discussing Race

## Description
Establish guidelines or agreements for group members to make when having discussions of race to ensure respectful and productive dialogue. These agreements may include staying engaged, experiencing discomfort, speaking your truth, accepting non-closure, being physically considerate, being verbally considerate, respecting classmates' feelings and space, and being willing to try new things. It also involves introducing a "pause of respect" after a student has spoken something especially honest or hard, and never asking students to speak for anyone else but themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator, a safe and comfortable space, guidelines or agreements, and a commitment to respectful dialogue.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for individuals who are uncomfortable discussing race or sharing their personal experiences. Requires ongoing commitment to creating a safe and inclusive environment.

### Affordances

- Guidelines can be adapted to fit the specific needs and context of the group. Facilitators can adjust the level of structure and guidance based on the group's experience and comfort level.
