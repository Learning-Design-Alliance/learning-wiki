# Develop and disseminate free, society-based early education models for disadvantaged children and families

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the combination of Turkish preschool access statistics and the study results, the authors conclude "it is necessary to develop and disseminate society based models which provide free educational opportunities for the disadvantaged children" who lack access to preschool education. They further recommend enriching rural preschool programs with materials based on children's actual needs, involving teacher candidates, and having academics and administrators develop local society-based models with supportive education programs for teachers and families.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organization of supportive education programs for both teachers and families in line with regional needs, and involvement of education academics and administrators
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is framed for contexts such as Turkey where a stated 38% of children may start elementary school without preschool education

### Target Learners
- disadvantaged preschool-age children, including the disabled, the poor, immigrants, Gypsies and children in remote areas

### Target Learning Goals
- removing early-age prejudice through topics such as conflict solutions and respecting differences, and supporting early development
