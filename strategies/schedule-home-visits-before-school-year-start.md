# Schedule teacher home visits before the start of the school year to build family relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has teachers visit students' homes before the school year begins, establishing a relationship-building contact with families early. The article reports that "a home visit before the start of the school year reduced the likelihood of a student having a disciplinary incident in that school year," supporting early timing as the implemented design in DC Public Schools' family engagement program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and a family engagement program infrastructure to arrange and conduct structured visits before the school year starts
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a matched comparison study in grades 1-5 in one district (DC Public Schools)

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades 1-5

### Target Learning Goals
- Reduced disciplinary incidents and improved attendance
