# Optimize Natural Lighting

## Description
Artificial lighting has negative impacts on those in schools, while natural lighting has positive impacts. Appropriate amounts of natural lighting boost morale, reduce off-task behavior, and improve test scores. Students with the most exposure to natural daylight progress faster in math and reading. Schools should maximize the use of natural lighting in classrooms to enhance the learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Larger windows, light shelves, strategic classroom orientation
#### Constraints
- May be limited by building design and location. Can be affected by weather conditions and time of day.
