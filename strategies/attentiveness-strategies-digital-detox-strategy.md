# Teach students a set of attentiveness strategies, including digital detox, to increase classroom attentiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents, via a mind map (Fig. 1), strategies students can follow to increase attentiveness in class: "taking a digital detox, minimizing digital distractions, taking effective notes, actively participating in class, learning autonomy, properly managing tasks, and following a healthy lifestyle." The notetaking branch further includes handwritten rather than digital notes, visual concepts, memory aids, structurally consistent notes, and mnemonics, symbols, and abbreviations. The article recommends students turn off notifications and avoid internet-connected devices during lectures unless the learning strategy demands otherwise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be taught the strategies and recommended to turn off notifications and manage daily communication during class.
#### Constraints
- The article notes digital technology use is unavoidable in the current era, and device avoidance applies unless learning strategy, instructional approach, and contextual factors demand it.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in lecture-based courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained attentiveness during lectures
- minimizing digital distraction and multitasking
