# Address the shortage of research mentors through national-level task-shifting and workload reforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes national-level reforms to address the shortage of research mentors in Japanese healthcare. The authors suggest that "Progress in task-shifting to nurses and pharmacists, along with digital transformation of healthcare administrative work, could reduce physicians' non-clinical workload" and create more capacity for experienced clinicians to serve as research mentors. They also propose clarifying institutional roles to prevent dispersion of the limited number of research mentors.
