# Develop an evidence-based framework of five policy recommendations, with implementation proposals, to inform a statutory primary-secondary transitions strategy in England

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's central output is a set of policy recommendations generated by integrating the systematic review and stakeholder consultation. As the abstract states, "Meta-inferences were then drawn to develop an evidence-based framework outlining five policy recommendations, with implementation proposals of how each might be enacted in practice." The authors argue a strategy underpinned by legislation and evidence-informed recommendations would make transitions a statutory responsibility, formalise accountability, and ensure provision is well-resourced and embedded in school routines rather than reliant on individual staff or informal arrangements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time and resourcing as part of a planned, supported process to ensure sustained commitment and systemic-level change
#### Constraints
- England requires a strategy designed for its distinctive policy context and systemic conditions rather than a direct adoption of any existing model from the devolved nations

### Target Learners
- policymakers, Local Authorities and schools in England responsible for primary-secondary transitions

### Target Learning Goals
- improving attainment, attendance, social adjustment and mental health outcomes across school transitions
