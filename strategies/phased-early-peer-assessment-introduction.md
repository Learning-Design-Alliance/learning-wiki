# Introduce peer assessment early and phasewise in the medical curriculum, starting with formative feedback plus skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a longitudinal implementation of peer assessment: schools should "introduce peer assessment early in the medical curriculum" so students develop critical assessment skills and acceptance of being evaluated by peers. It specifies a phased approach beginning with less threatening formative feedback before summative peer marking, accompanied by training in giving and receiving constructive feedback and conflict resolution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skills training in giving and receiving constructive feedback
- A phased sequence starting with formative feedback before summative peer marking
#### Constraints
- The trial's students had no prior peer assessment experience, which the article says may have contributed to more negative attitudes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- teamwork contribution
- critical peer assessment skills
