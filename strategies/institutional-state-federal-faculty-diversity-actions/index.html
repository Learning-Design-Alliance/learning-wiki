Institutional, state, and federal actions to increase faculty diversity¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends a multi-level action set: institutions should develop specific targets for increasing Black and Latino faculty, increase recruitment and retention funds, create opportunities for students to connect with faculty with shared identity, align policies with diversity goals, and improve campus racial climates. States should include faculty diversity in strategic planning, rescind affirmative action bans, and prioritize funding for minority-serving institutions. Federal actors should increase funding for research pipeline programs such as TRIO, Upward Bound/McNair Scholars, and Title III-B grants.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Alignment of institutional mission, goals, and policy implementation with faculty diversity initiatives; funding for recruitment, retention, and campus climate work

Constraints¶

  • The federal government "has no formal role in promoting faculty diversity in higher education", so federal leverage comes through executive orders and funding; affirmative action in admissions is banned in nine states

Target Learners¶

  • Black and Latino undergraduate and graduate students
  • aspiring faculty of color
  • current faculty of color

Target Learning Goals¶

  • increasing faculty diversity
  • improving persistence and completion for students of color
  • building inclusive campus climates
