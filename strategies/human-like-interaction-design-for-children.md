# Design interactive educational systems to exhibit human-like behavior patterns: conversation, questioning, answering, volunteering information, and responding to emotional changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on cited findings that children with ASD show preferences among communication modes and that successful communication involves active involvement, asking and answering questions, volunteering information, and responding sensitively to partners, the article concludes that "an interactive system appealing to children must first exhibit behavior patterns similar to those of humans". The recommended design includes voice conversations, asking questions, answering questions, providing additional information, and responding to children's emotional changes.
