# Act on response-log findings with unrestricted hint panels, misconception-targeted hints on bottleneck exercises, and revised task wording.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates that response-log analysis can drive concrete course changes between runs. "Between the 2024–2025 and 2025–2026 runs, an unrestricted hint panel was added below every circuit exercise; learners can open it at any point with no scoring penalty." In addition, the two bottleneck exercises identified by the answer-space analysis received hints targeting the candidate misconceptions surfaced by the wrong-construction profiles, and the wording of tehtava-virheenkorjaus was revised to clarify the intended scope of the correction task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A platform supporting built-in exercise components with hint panels and automatic assessment, plus answer-space analysis to identify bottleneck exercises and candidate misconceptions.
#### Constraints
- The redesign actions are based on one cohort of one course; cross-cohort replication is in progress.

### Target Learners
- novice learners in introductory quantum computing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing avoidable frustration on bottleneck exercises
- clarifying task interpretation
