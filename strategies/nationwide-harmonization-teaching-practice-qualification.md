# Harmonize teaching practice qualification nationwide to enable cross-site programs and mutually recognized qualifications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that a working group of GHA and DEGAM reach a nationwide consensus on a core curriculum for block practicum and practical year qualifications. Because qualification formats and duration currently vary across sites, harmonization would "enable cross-site programs and mutually recognized qualifications, thereby supporting sites with fewer human resources". The authors extend this to quality assurance aspects such as evaluations and complaints management that should also be agreed upon nationally.
