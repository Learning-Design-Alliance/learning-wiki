# Creative Analogies and Comparisons

## Description
Simplify complex ideas by linking them to familiar situations, making them easier to understand and remember.  Help highlight the importance of concepts. Learners can see how theoretical ideas apply in real-world settings, which can enhance their understanding and motivate them to apply these concepts to their own work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful selection of analogies that accurately represent the concept. Tools for creating visuals to support the analogies.
#### Constraints
- Analogies can be misleading if not carefully chosen. Overuse of analogies can become distracting.
