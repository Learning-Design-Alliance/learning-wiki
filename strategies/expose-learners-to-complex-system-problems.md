# Expose learners to real-world problems embedded in complex systems with multiple interactive components

## Description
The first coping strategy is "Exposing learners to real-world problems that are often involved in complex systems consisting of multiple interactive components." Problematizing encourages students "to challenge, to question, and to be curious rather than simply assimilating facts." Immersive technologies let learners access such complexities via interaction with simulated problems.
