# Recruit ICT-trained teachers and outline computer-based demonstration approaches for the agricultural science curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the authors recommend that recruiting, preparing and training teachers with ICT knowledge should be central strategies for improving schools, since teachers translate subject content into teachable lessons. They further recommend that "Ministries of education, curriculum planners and developers should outline appropriate Computer -base demonstration mode for use by teachers to teach any topic highlighted in the agricultural science curriculum", and that individual teachers apply appropriate instructional technologies to capture and sustain student interest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Qualified teachers with ICT knowledge to handle the teaching
- Good learning environment with adequate instructional materials in all secondary schools and technical colleges
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single quasi-experimental study of 80 students in two schools in one Local Government Area

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary school students studying vocational subjects

### Target Learning Goals
- Achievement in agricultural science and vocational subjects
