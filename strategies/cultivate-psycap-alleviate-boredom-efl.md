# Cultivate EFL learners' PsyCap and alleviate academic boredom through goal-setting training, teacher support and varied instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that EFL educators strengthen students' PsyCap and reduce boredom: foster "a collaborative classroom environment", align strategies with students' goal orientations, "create educational challenges that effectively strengthen students' PsyCap through the implementation of goal-setting training", provide teacher support behaviour training, exhibit enthusiasm, and employ "diverse teaching methods" to enhance interest and engagement. These are offered as practical implications following from the mediation findings in the CHC context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators able to monitor students' needs and provide timely academic and emotional support
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
