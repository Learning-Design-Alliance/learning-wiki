# Bright Spaces with Plenty of Light and Air

## Description
Creating spaces that are colorful, special vents controlled air flow, and having an accessible fan can replicate this if vents aren't available.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of lighting options such as lamps and colored lights. A designated space for reading and relaxation. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the changing lights.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Improve engagement, improve productivity, reduce stress

### Affordances
- Promotes relaxation and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred lighting arrangement. Provide a variety of lighting options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more comfortable.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that access to natural light and fresh air can increase engagement, improve productivity, and reduce stress. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
