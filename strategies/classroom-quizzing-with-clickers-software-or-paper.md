# Classroom Quizzing with Clickers, Software, or Paper

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Frequent classroom quizzing implements retrieval practice within courses. "Clicker systems allow instructors to pose questions (typically in multiple-choice format), record responses from all students regardless of class size" and give immediate feedback; custom software can deliver daily or spaced personalized quizzes; and daily or weekly paper quizzes also work. The chapter says quizzing has firmly established benefits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quizzes are given frequently, for example daily at the end of lectures or before and after lessons and before exams.
- Clickers or specialized software are optional; paper-and-pencil quizzes have also produced positive effects.
#### Constraints
- Students who experience test anxiety benefit from retrieval practice but may not benefit as much as students without anxiety.

### Target Learners
- Middle school, high school, and college students

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved classroom exam performance
- Long-lasting retention of course content
