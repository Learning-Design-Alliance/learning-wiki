# Leveraging Strengths to Address Attention Challenges

## Description
Identify specific student strengths and/or challenges related to attention then use strategies to leverage student strengths to support a student’s attention during learning. Key strategies include preferential seating, graphic organizers and visual aids, and chunking directions into smaller sections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of individual student strengths and challenges, implementation of strategic seating, provision of visual aids, and chunking of directions.
#### Constraints
- May not fully address severe attention deficits without additional support, such as special accommodations or interventions.
