# Target development of math self-efficacy, especially for elementary school girls, through teacher professional development on supportive practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that schools create professional development opportunities teaching teachers how to increase and sustain students' self-efficacy, with particular attention to elementary school girls' math self-efficacy. Suggested practices include "creating situations of academic success by assigning tasks within students’ zone of proximal development, providing supportive feedback on how to do tasks, and acknowledging students’ emotional reactions to successes and failures". The recommendation rests on the study's finding that self-efficacy relates to achievement and moderates well-being effects for girls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need professional development opportunities to learn practices that increase and sustain self-efficacy
#### Constraints
- The study was cross-sectional, in one region of Russia, with fourth graders only; effect sizes were small

### Target Learners
- elementary school students, especially girls in fourth grade

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
- reading achievement
- self-efficacy
- subjective well-being in school
