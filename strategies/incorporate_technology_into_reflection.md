# Incorporate Technology into Reflection

## Description
This strategy involves leveraging students' familiarity with technology to create more engaging and interactive reflection opportunities. Teachers can use microblogging, collaborative reflection tools, digital reflection prompts, and multimedia feedback tools to encourage students to reflect on their mathematical learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to technology, a variety of technology tools, and a willingness to allow students to choose the tools that they find most engaging.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to technology and may not be appropriate for all students.
