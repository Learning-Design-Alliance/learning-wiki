# Empathy for Educators

## Description
Engage in actions to build an empathic mindset. Read stories from the perspective of characters similar to your students. Follow a student schedule for a day. Survey students frequently using low-tech or high-tech tools. Ask, “What’s one thing teachers should know about students?” or “What’s the most important thing I should know about you?” Use survey data to drive instruction with an empathic mindset. Remember that students watch teachers constantly, and actions can unintentionally model unempathetic behavior. Offer solutions-based perspectives and model citizenship.
