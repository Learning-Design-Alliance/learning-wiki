Add a teacher page to WebQuests listing objectives and collecting student solutions for discussion¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Teachers recommended an informative page letting them study the WebQuest's learning objectives and solve the activity before implementation, and a space where students upload solutions so all groups' work can be compared during presentations. The article links this to the literature's 'teacher page'. One teacher suggested: "students would upload solutions as they completed them and all groups would see their solutions altogether." The authors read these suggestions as evidence teachers understood the WebQuest construct well.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need access to the learning objectives and attainments of each WebQuest before implementation

Constraints¶

  • The article notes a student-suggested prior-knowledge reminder page may restrict solution approaches because students may think listed concepts must be used

Target Learners¶

  • ninth-grade mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • data analysis and probability attainments in the ninth-grade mathematics curriculum
