## Description
The article recommends that parents be informed about gaming's potential academic consequences and that families adopt a structured media plan. It states practitioners "can encourage families to develop a personalized family media use plan, paying particular attention to gaming during middle childhood," with particular attention to boys given the sex-specific findings. This is a practice-oriented recommendation grounded in the study's longitudinal associations.
