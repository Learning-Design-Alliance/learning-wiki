# Multiprofessional in situ simulation of procedural complications to develop situational awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because complications are inherent to endoscopy and human factors are amplified under stress, the article recommends that "multiprofessional in situ simulations of procedural complications may be used to develop situational awareness." Simulation trains trainees and teams to maintain an accurate perception of how a procedure is going, anticipate potential complications, and avoid tunnel vision on the monitor while vital observations and staff warnings are missed.
