# Create AI professional development for K-12 teachers addressing both AI content knowledge and AI pedagogical knowledge, for in-service and pre-service teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues for developing "the most effective AI professional development (PD) for K-12 teachers," entailing "a research program in design principles for creating professional development around both AI content knowledge and AI pedagogical knowledge." PD research should take into account the broad range of competencies in AI that teachers and administrators begin with, and should address pre-service as well as in-service teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context

- AI pedagogical knowledge
