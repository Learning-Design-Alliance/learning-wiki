# Offer teacher continuing education remotely, with distributed dates and times and recorded sessions, to widen participation and scientist contact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's conclusion states that "o formato online e a distribuição dos horários e datas favoreceu e facilitou a participação dos professores e o contato com os cientistas/especialistas das mais diversas áreas". All meetings ran via Google Meet in evening slots, were recorded, and teachers residing in different states could follow the full course through the recordings, enabling contact with researchers from varied scientific fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recordings of all meetings made available to participants
- Scheduling that avoids congruence with teachers' working hours
#### Constraints
- Some participants criticised session start and end times because post-lecture discussions overran the scheduled 19:00–21:00 period

### Target Learners
- practising basic-education teachers across multiple states

### Target Learning Goals
- access to current science and researchers beyond the textbook
