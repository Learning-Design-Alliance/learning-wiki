# Training Handouts

## Description
Training handouts are supplementary materials distributed during training sessions to enhance learner engagement, structure interactions, and provide a reference for key content. They can take various forms, including outlines, worksheets, feedback forms, and action plans. Effective handouts should not merely duplicate presentation slides but should instead encourage active participation by incorporating note-taking sections, interactive exercises, and opportunities for peer collaboration. Well-designed handouts contribute to a more professional learning environment and improve learner retention by prompting learners to actively engage with the material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful instructional design to align with learning objectives. May require printing or digital distribution. Implementation involves guiding learners to actively use handouts during the session.
#### Constraints
- If poorly designed, handouts may distract from the presentation. If handouts contain all the information, learners may become passive. Effectiveness depends on active engagement by learners.
