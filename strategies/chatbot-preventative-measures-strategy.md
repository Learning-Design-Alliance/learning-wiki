# Implement preventative measures: student awareness programs and regular professional development training for educators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that educational institutions implement preventative measures when integrating AI chatbots. These include creating awareness among students about digital inequality, reliability and accuracy of AI chatbots, and ethical considerations, plus regular professional development training for educators covering integration of diverse in-class activities, nurturing critical thinking, and best practices for integrating chatbots into teaching methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to ongoing educator professional development training
#### Constraints
- The review states AI cannot completely replicate human instructors' emotional support and mentorship

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- educators

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible and effective use of AI chatbots in learning
- critical thinking and fair performance evaluation
