# Use standardized EPQ feedback to organize and optimize the quality of simulation education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators use the validated EPQ as a standardized feedback tool to raise the quality of simulation teaching. As printed, "Educators responsible for the organization of teaching will be able to use the results of the EPQ scale to ensure the optimal quality of teaching". Standardized instruments also make simulation assessment comparable nationally and internationally, providing scientific evidence for continued use and development of simulation programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized tool previously tested on similar populations, as required by the INACSL Standards of Best Practice for simulation participant evaluation
#### Constraints
- The tool can also be used in postgraduate education of medical staff, but the validation sample was undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- nurses and other health professions in postgraduate simulation centers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving quality of simulation-based education
- increasing learners' involvement and responsibility for results

## Claims
