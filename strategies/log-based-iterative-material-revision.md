# Revise e-learning material iteratively using LMS log files, mapped learning habits and evaluation-system results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the LMS logging capability and the evaluation system to understand learning habits and revise the material accordingly. The author examined Moodle logs, learning habits and students' answers, then made concrete changes: "Some practical exercises were put in the part of theory and the students had to to send the resulted images of some practical exercises to the teacher guaranteeing the feedback this way", and expressive examples and animations were added to make topics more understandable. The revised material produced higher averages in the next semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An LMS with logging capability and an evaluation system whose results can be analysed to map learning habits
#### Constraints
- The article notes the number of students was not large, so results must be treated with caution

### Target Learners
- daytime and correspondence university students in an optional e-learning course

### Target Learning Goals
- improved achievement in theoretical and practical parts of a digital image processing course
