Supplement in-class physics teaching with a teacher-developed website offering modelled solutions, online quizzes with feedback, and moderated chats¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends supplementing face-to-face physics teaching with a dedicated teacher-designed website, after more than 80% of students responded positively to supplementing in-class learning this way. The website combined concise modelled notes and worked examples, online multiple-choice quizzes giving instant computer-generated feedback, links to real-world examples, and after-school moderated chat sessions. Students cited learning at their own pace and reviewing work without distraction as benefits.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher time to produce materials and moderate chats; the article notes chat sessions were very labour intensive

Constraints¶

  • Some students may not engage with the website (one student disliked computers and did not access it)

Target Learners¶

  • senior secondary physics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • physics concept knowledge, revision, and complex reasoning

Claims¶

  • After-school moderated chat sessions enable scaffolded problem-solving discussion beyond classroom hours [+W]
  • Blended web-based learning with Getsmart significantly improves Year 12 physics Knowledge and Complex Reasoning post-test scores relative to a comparison class [+M]
  • Students report positive attitudes towards studying physics in the blended web-based environment [+W]
  • Students accessed the website extensively outside school hours, with use spanning up to 20 hours per day [+W]
