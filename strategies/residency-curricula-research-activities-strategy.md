# Include research activities in ENT residency curricula to strengthen physicians' scientific education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding research into residency training so that physicians develop reviewing skills, clinical rationale and ongoing learning. It states that "the curriculum of residency programs should contemplate research activities" and that science education in residency includes lectures, seminars, research projects and paper presentations. Success factors it cites include guidance, coaching, basic research-method training, time and infrastructure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of guidance and coaching, training on basic research method, time and infrastructure for scientific research support, as the article lists as success factors
#### Constraints
- The article notes the results would be better understood if objective data on the quality of research in Brazilian residency programs were available

### Target Learners
- ENT residents
- otorhinolaryngologists in specialized medical training

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding of scientific method and literature interpretation
- ability to plan and conduct research projects
