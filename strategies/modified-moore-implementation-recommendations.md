## Description
The article's recommendations for implementing the modified Moore learning approach: lecturers 'should be more patient in giving guidan ce and presenting excersices task so that  students were motivated to compose mathematical proof by the mselves', conduct the lesson in one semester, and continuously cultivate positive learning dispositions. Learning materials should be completed with more examples, illustrations, and exercises of varied difficulty with guiding questions, and difficult proof problems can be assigned as homework or group tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials completed with examples, illustrations, and exercises at various difficulty levels with relevant guidance and questions
- Discussion of student presentations and selected exercise tasks during lessons
#### Constraints
- Findings were lower than prior studies because this study ran half a semester on a more complex course with medium-low ability students

- undergraduate mathematics students, including those with low prior mathematics ability
