Build the STEM evidence base and expand capacity for monitoring and evaluation of programs like REU¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The fact sheet frames its contribution as "Building the STEM evidence base and expanding capacity for monitoring and evaluation" of the NSF REU program. It positions documentation of the program as a way to support evidence-based oversight of STEM workforce development efforts. The strategy is descriptive rather than evaluated in this source.
