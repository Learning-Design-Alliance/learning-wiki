# Debriefing Emotional Experiences

## Description
Debriefing emotional experiences involves a structured discussion at the end of the day where each child expresses an emotion they experienced and shares the context. This helps children process their feelings and develop emotional self-awareness. Sentence prompts can be used to guide the expression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a set time at the end of the day (e.g., 5-10 minutes). A facilitator to guide the discussion and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Some children may be reluctant to share their emotions in a group setting. Requires a supportive and inclusive classroom environment.

### Personalization
- Sentence prompts can be adapted to suit different age groups and emotional literacy levels. Children can choose to express emotions privately or share with the group.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the frequency and depth of emotional expression. Track the use of emotion words and the ability to connect emotions with experiences.

## Impact
- Studies show that regular emotional check-ins and discussions can improve children's emotional regulation and social competence. (Source: The Impact of Check-ins on Social Emotional Learning Outcomes - https://www.panoramaed.com/blog/check-ins-social-emotional-learning-outcomes)
