# Tailor e-learning by pre-scoring knowledge of red flags and spinal pathologies and offering additional content before continuing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Given physiotherapists' divergent views on the relevance of red-flag, spinal-pathology, and pain-mechanism content, the article recommends a tailored approach: "educational interventions should consider a tailored approach where participants are scored first on their knowledge of the importance on red flags and spinal pathologies with an opportunity to receive additional content on this topic before continuing with the educational intervention." This addresses both overconfident participants who skip such content and those who need it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-intervention knowledge score on red flags and spinal pathologies to route participants to additional content
#### Constraints
- A considerable proportion of physiotherapists lose interest when refresher content is overly theoretical

### Target Learners
- Licensed physiotherapists with varying baseline knowledge of red flags and spinal pathologies

### Target Learning Goals
- Differentiating specific from non-specific low back pain through red-flag screening
