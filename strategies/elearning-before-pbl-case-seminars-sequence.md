# Sequence self-directed e-learning before interactive PBL case seminars so seminars build on an established theoretical foundation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course pairs roughly ten hours of self-paced online study with two interactive two-hour seminars per sub-course. The e-learning component "provided a structured knowledge framework, effectively preparing students for the seminars", after which groups analyze assigned clinical cases, articulate decision rationales, engage in peer discussion, and receive faculty evaluation. The authors state this sequence let students consolidate knowledge through practical application and integrate multiple aspects of patient management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online e-learning platform delivering the theoretical content before seminars
- Pre-assigned clinical case topics per student group
- Faculty-led discussion with feedback aligned with clinical practice
#### Constraints
- Designed for a multidisciplinary clinical topic whose content is dispersed across several mandatory courses

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- integrating theoretical and clinical knowledge
- clinical reasoning
- collaborative problem-solving
