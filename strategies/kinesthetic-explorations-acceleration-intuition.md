# Use kinesthetic explorations and body-intuition prompts to help students develop intuition for acceleration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends giving students opportunities for kinesthetic explorations and asking them to imagine what they would feel during a motion, since the human body senses acceleration directly. As the article puts it, "If students are encouraged to imagine what they will feel if they were experiencing a particular motion, they may develop a better intuition for the conc ept of acceleration." This leverages inertial cues such as being thrown outward in a turn or lunging forward when a car stops.

## Design Implications
