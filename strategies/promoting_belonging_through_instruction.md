# Promoting Belonging Through Instruction

## Description
Teachers promote belonging in the math classroom through instructional choices and practices that address equity and opportunity. Developing healthy mindsets and using equity-based teaching practices help create a sense of belonging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training on promoting belonging, understanding of diverse learners, and commitment to creating an inclusive classroom.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and sensitivity. May require additional resources to support students who have experienced marginalization or discrimination.
