# Weekly TA/LA training meetings using situated apprenticeship: modelling authentic student difficulties and practicing Socratic-style questioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before each week's recitations, the first-time instructor trained the graduate teaching assistants and undergraduate learning assistants in weekly meetings. There they "reviewed the coming week's activities and engaged in a version of situated apprenticeship" in which they modelled authentic student difficulties and practiced using Socratic-style questioning techniques to help each other overcome those difficulties, preparing them to facilitate collaborative problem solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly training meetings attended by the course instructor, teaching assistants, and learning assistants before each recitation week
