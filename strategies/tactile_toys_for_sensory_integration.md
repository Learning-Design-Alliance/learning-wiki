# Tactile Toys for Sensory Integration

## Description
Providing tactile toys can help awaken sensory receptors, which may decrease fidgeting, attention issues, and behavior problems. Tactile stimulation is important for helping learners regulate their emotions, behavior, and focus. Tactile toys can be introduced into a learner's routine for calming and focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tactile toys and a routine for their use.  Understanding of individual sensory needs to select appropriate toys.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective for all learners, and some toys may be distracting if not used as intended. It's important to differentiate between using a tactile toy as a tool versus using it as a toy.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners with sensory processing challenges, including those who are hypersensitive to textures or sensory seekers

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve sensory integration, regulate emotions, behavior, attention, and focus

### Affordances
- Provides a means for learners to calm their bodies and brains for better learning. Helps learners who are hypersensitive or sensory seekers to regulate their needs and improve concentration.

### Personalization
- Tactile toys can be chosen based on individual sensory preferences, either providing calming input for hypersensitive learners or stimulating input for sensory seekers.
