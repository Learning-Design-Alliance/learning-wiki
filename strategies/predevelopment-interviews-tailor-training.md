# Conduct patient interviews and clinician focus groups before developing a clinician training to tailor content to identified knowledge gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before building the curriculum, the team ran semistructured interviews with patients and focus groups with clinicians, analyzed with the constant comparative method, and used the resulting themes to shape training content. The authors state that "Engaging clinicians beforehand allowed us to obtain baseline knowledge and assess deficits to tailor the training to the individual needs of our clinicians." Feedback on cardiac devices led to adding a device review, and motivation themes led to a behavioral change section.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to homebound patients and clinicians willing to participate in interviews and focus groups before curriculum design
#### Constraints
- The article notes the small samples from one home health agency limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- home care clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- alignment of professional development content with identified clinician and patient knowledge gaps
