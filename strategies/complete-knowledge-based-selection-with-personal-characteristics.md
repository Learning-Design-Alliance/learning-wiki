# Medical schools using knowledge-based selection should complete their procedure to also select caring doctors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that schools selecting students purely by knowledge exams supplement the procedure so that selection addresses both 'getting ahead' skills (conscientiousness) and 'getting along' skills (sociability, flexibility, empathy, openness, agreeability). As printed, "it could be important for medical schools adopting this approach to complete their procedure in order to ensure that they select students able to become not only diligent but also caring doctors." The recommendation rests on the finding that the exam privileged diligence while being neutral to the humane profile, and on the under-selection of women.
