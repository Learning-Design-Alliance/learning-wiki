Evaluate & Reflect then Sustain & Scale: analyze pilot data against targeted goals and share results with critical partners to plan expanded use¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Step 5 directs leaders to "Analyze pilot data to determine whether the tool sufficiently addressed your targeted teaching and learning goals." Step 6 directs leaders to "Share the pilot results with critical partners, such as the school board, community partners, funders, teachers, and students, to collaboratively develop a comprehensive plan for expanded and/or long-term use of successful tools." Evaluation feeds a collaborative scaling decision.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Pilot data analysis against targeted teaching and learning goals
  • Sharing results with critical partners including the school board, community partners, funders, teachers, and students

Constraints¶

  • Scaling applies to tools the pilot shows were successful

Target Learners¶

  • District and school leaders
  • School boards
  • Teachers
  • Students
  • Community partners and funders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Determining whether edtech addressed targeted teaching and learning goals
  • Planning expanded or long-term use of successful tools

Affordances¶

  • Edtech Procurement Framework Six Steps

Related Strategies¶

  • Analyze & Inventory: use cross-departmental data analysis and an inventory of existing interventions before buying new edtech
  • Conduct ed-tech pilots by articulating the instructional need, setting clear product-review criteria, involving educators in planning, supporting educators throughout, gathering data from all users, deciding from data, negotiating purchasing options, and sharing results
  • Design & Plan: build a professional learning and measurement plan to monitor implementation fidelity and evaluate impact
  • Mobilize & Implement: provide professional learning throughout the pilot and use real-time data dashboards to keep usage aligned with dosage requirements
  • Monitoring Progress
  • Discover & Decide: establish evidence-based selection criteria and instructional alignment in an RFP process to select edtech for priority learner populations
