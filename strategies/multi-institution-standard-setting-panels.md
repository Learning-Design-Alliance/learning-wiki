# Involve multiple institutions in standard-setting panels to broaden credibility of licensure examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend recruiting Angoff panellists from several universities rather than a single institution, so that pass-mark standards reflect a broader range of educational practices and are consistent at a national level. They state: "Involving external panellists, from all national universities with a dental programme, is therefore recommended to mitigate the potential influence of specific practices or biases from individual institutions." The study's own cross-institutional consistency results support feasibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruitment of clinical teachers from multiple dental programmes
- Written instructions sufficient for novice raters, as used in this study
#### Constraints
- Consensus meetings would require substantial resources, making them impractical for regular examinations

### Target Learners
- foreign-trained dentists undergoing re-certification licensure

### Target Learning Goals
- credible, nationally consistent licensure standard setting
