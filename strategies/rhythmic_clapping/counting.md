# Rhythmic Clapping/Counting

## Description
The teacher claps/counts out a rhythm. Students imitate the rhythm of the clap and the count.

- K-4, adaptable to other grades

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance auditory discrimination, improve coordination, reinforce rhythm, build pattern recognition

### Affordances
- Simple and engaging activity to reinforce rhythm and coordination. Movement refreshes students and provides another take on math concepts.

### Personalization
- Adjust the complexity of the rhythm based on students' abilities. Students can create their own rhythms.
