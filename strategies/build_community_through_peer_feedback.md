# Build Community Through Peer Feedback

## Description
Offering students frequent opportunities to share feedback and revise their work fosters a supportive classroom culture and builds relationships. Meaningful feedback contributes to growth, progress, and a sense of connection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires establishing clear guidelines for providing constructive feedback. Demands a classroom environment that values collaboration and mutual respect.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time to facilitate peer feedback sessions. Can be challenging to ensure that feedback is always constructive and supportive.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Create a supportive classroom culture, foster relationships among students, promote growth and progress, improve student work through meaningful feedback.

### Affordances
- Enhances collaboration and communication skills. Provides opportunities for students to learn from each other. Builds a sense of community and mutual support.

### Personalization
- Feedback can be tailored to individual student needs and learning styles. Allows students to provide feedback on topics that resonate with them.
