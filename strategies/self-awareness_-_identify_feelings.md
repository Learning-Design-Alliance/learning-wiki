# Self-Awareness - Identify Feelings

## Description
This strategy involves having learners identify, label, or match facial expressions and feelings. You can also ask learners to categorize feeling words by the type of feeling. Using literature to have learners analyze characters’ feelings is also helpful. Self-awareness is the ability to correctly identify your own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they impact behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a facilitator and a set of expressions and emotions.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be more extroverted and find categorizing emotions difficult.
