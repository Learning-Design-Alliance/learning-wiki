# Evaluate AI-assisted training with a post-training unaided assessment on a separate radiograph set

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To determine whether improvements are retained independently of AI support, the article recommends that "future research should include a broader range of participants, such as senior dental students and junior clinicians across multiple institutions, as well as a more diverse dataset of carious lesions." It further calls for "a post-training evaluation without AI assistance—ideally using a separate set of radiographs" to test retention and transfer to unaided clinical contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A post-training evaluation without AI assistance, ideally using a separate set of radiographs, to determine whether improvements are retained independently of AI support.
#### Constraints
- The study was limited to a single cohort from one institution, with the sample size constrained by time.

### Target Learners
- dental students
- junior clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- retention and transfer of diagnostic skills without AI support
