Teachers should prepare active learning environments and create 5E-based techniques across subjects¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

From the study's findings the authors recommend that "Classroom teachers should consider how to prepare learning environments in which students will be active in accordance with their characteristics and then present these environments to students." They further recommend creating techniques based on the 5E instructional model for various subjects, which they say will attach higher importance to the model, and note that the education of trainee teachers will benefit from these methods.

  • Fifth-grade students taught science through inquiry-based instruction supported by the 5E learning cycle achieved significantly higher post-test scores than students taught by traditional methods [+W]
