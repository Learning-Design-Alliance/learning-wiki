# Train EFL reading teachers to employ CDA instruction and select useful CDA tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations: "Teachers of EFL reading need to be trained to employ CDA instruction in their classrooms", and because not every CDA concept is equally useful, facilitators and students should select tools that help find the writer's purposes. The authors also recommend extending CDA to multimodal texts such as videos and social media, and using interviews and observation cards to capture how students resist texts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in CDA instruction before classroom implementation
#### Constraints
- Results at the explanation level remain general; further research is suggested on CDA's role in fostering critical intercultural awareness

### Target Learners
- EFL reading teachers
- EFL undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical reading of ideologically loaded materials
- critical intercultural awareness
