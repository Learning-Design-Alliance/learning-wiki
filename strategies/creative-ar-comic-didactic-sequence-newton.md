# Creative didactic sequence combining a comic magazine read in groups with app-triggered AR Gifs, drawn questions, and written reviews for teaching classical mechanics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy organizes physics content into a comic magazine with anime-like characters using simple, youth-oriented language, structured as a story of students who dislike physics and must take a Newton's-laws test. After reading each section aloud in groups of five, students observe app-triggered Gifs without inter-group communication, answer drawn questions, and produce written reviews or texts explaining the phenomena scientifically. The authors describe it as "uma atividade lúdica" that uses challenge and catharsis to bring everyday situations to be resolved scientifically, aiming at creative learning and scientific literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group formation with distributed characters for role reading
- A teacher-mediated app activation passed from group to group
- An introductory lesson and PHET simulator work on prior knowledge before starting
#### Constraints
- Application occurred late in the school year when students were tired; the authors note the material demands adjustments to questions and chosen Gifs for future applications

### Target Learners
- adolescent high school students, including EJA (Youth and Adult Education) classes

### Target Learning Goals
- learning and applying Newton's laws and classical mechanics concepts to everyday events; scientific literacy
