# Produce PBL scenario animations inexpensively with off-the-shelf web-based animation apps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends producing PBL problem scenarios as multimedia animations using readily available web-based animation tools rather than costly bespoke video production. It reports that "multimedia animations have been produced inexpensively with off-the-shelf web-based animation apps" and that "these technological advances may democratize the production of video-based cases in the future." The workflow combined Vyond animation, volunteer and simulated-patient voice recordings, and Audacity audio processing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to off-the-shelf web-based animation applications
- Visual processing software for disease-symptom imagery
- Volunteer voices or simulated patients, with written informed consent for recording
#### Constraints
- The article notes learners in one cited study preferred text-based presentations, suggesting video-based scenarios should be used more selectively in problem types when determining learning objectives

### Target Learners
- Medical undergraduates in PBL programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Realistic, multimedia presentation of clinical problem scenarios
