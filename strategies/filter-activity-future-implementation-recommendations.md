# Recommended additions for future implementations of the filter activity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend for future implementations hardware devices to reinforce practical understanding, design constraints such as rounding and cutoff, a more descriptive title with clearer objectives, and 'A clear description of the time required for each task', which 'relieves students from having to manage time themselves' and supports students with time blindness or dyscalculia.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recommendations apply to future implementations of the filter activity and similar assessment designs.
#### Constraints
- The recommendations are the authors' proposals; the article reports no test of them.

### Target Learners
- university science students, including those with learning differences such as dyslexia

### Target Learning Goals
- deeper analysis and evaluation in digital filter design and smoother progression through Bloom's levels
