# Have students compare calculated vibrational frequencies with literature values and explore higher-level model chemistries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the geometry optimization and normal modes activity, students calculate the optimum geometry and vibrational normal modes of hypochlorous acid, animate each vibration, and 'can compare calculated frequencies with literature values (3609 cm−1 (OH stretch), 1239 cm−1 (bend), and 724 cm−1 (Cl-O stretch)) and can explore higher level model chemistries to get better agreement with experiment.' This gives students a concrete loop of computation, comparison against reference data, and method refinement.
