# Use sunray diagrams in undergraduate thermodynamics to clear up beginners' misconceptions about partial derivatives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend deploying the sunray diagram's visual intuition and aesthetic brevity in undergraduate education. They state that 'The sunray diagram effectively clears up beginners' misconceptions about the partial derivatives, providing a transparent and unambiguous picture,' and that visualizing thermodynamic degrees of freedom as the diagram's dimension may help beginners distinguish the number of variables from the actual thermodynamic degrees of freedom. Users can also classify and generate identities and grasp proof blueprints.
