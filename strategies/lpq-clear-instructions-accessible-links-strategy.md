# Provide easily accessible web-based LPQs with clear instructions and manage frequency to sustain engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing more easily accessible web-based LPQs with clear instructions of use, and increasing the frequency of LPQs during lectures, which "could contribute to a more engaging and effective learning environment for some students". It draws on students' qualitative suggestions (more accessible links, more LPQs per lecture) and on the correlation between instruction clarity and LPQs helping identify focus areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web-based polling tool whose links are easily accessible to students
- Clear instructions for using the polling software
#### Constraints
- The benefit is stated only for some students; increased frequency risks overuse and diminished out-of-class motivation for others if not managed carefully

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Computer Science students in face-to-face lectures

### Target Learning Goals
- lecture engagement and attention
- comprehension of lecture material
- identifying areas needing further study
