# Alternating inter- and intra-lab peer-review critique groups with guided feedback rounds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable peer-review routine: the instructor forms critique groups of three to four students, alternating between inter- and intra-lab membership so students receive "a range and depth of perspective." Writers brief peers on their goals and desired feedback; reviewers get a week to give written comments against instructor-provided guidelines; groups meet over a 50-minute period to share comments with instructor or teaching-assistant support; writers then have a week to revise before the cycle repeats with a new group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-provided feedback guidelines
- Instructor or postbaccalaureate teaching assistant available to model the peer-review process and resolve confusion
- Manageable five-page draft units for submission, critique, and revision
#### Constraints
- Intra-lab groups are described as effective for science accuracy feedback and inter-lab groups for writing to broader audiences; the two group types serve complementary purposes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate science students writing senior theses

### Target Learning Goals
- Scientific writing quality
- Ability to give and use constructive peer feedback
- Science reasoning
