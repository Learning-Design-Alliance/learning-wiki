# Capture tacit expert knowledge by codifying it into learning materials and enabling direct expert transfer

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because fusion remains in an R&D phase where niche expertise and decades of experience exist as unwritten tacit knowledge in a few key experts, the report calls on program elements to capture that knowledge through codification into learning materials and direct transfer from experts. It states performers "should encourage activities involving various stakeholders to improve the capture and sharing of tacit knowledge between institutions", extending beyond physics research to scarce skill areas, with the goal of a comprehensive, broadly available knowledge hub complemented by AI-driven access tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Complement codification with (AI-driven) extensive tools enabling easy access to generated knowledge and identification of the right experts
#### Constraints
- Repeating mistakes in design, commissioning and operation of fusion devices can lead to months/years of delays, making capture urgent

### Target Learners
- early-career fusion researchers and technicians
- new entrants to the fusion workforce

### Target Learning Goals
- explicit knowledge of device design, commissioning, and operation
- scarce skill areas and lessons learned
