# Cooperative Learning Strategies

## Description
Using structured group activities to enhance learning and social interaction. These activities involve movement, collaboration, and discussion, such as gallery walks, two stray one stay, round robin, and simultaneous round table. These strategies provide opportunities for students to engage with content in a dynamic and interactive way.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared activities with clear instructions and roles. Time for group work and discussion.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful management to ensure that all students participate and contribute. Some students may dominate discussions or rely on others to do the work.
