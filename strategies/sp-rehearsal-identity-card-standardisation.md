# Rehearse SP roles with an identity card, an ACP coach as conversational partner, and joint reflection to improve standardisation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
SPs rehearse role by role. The group creates an identity card on a flipchart summarising the role's character traits and their translation into body language and expression, and practices the conversation with an ACP coach as partner. The authors report joint development and rehearsal of the roles 'improved the standardisation' because SP colleagues served as orientation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The role script must be sent well in advance and prepared independently by the SPs; all SPs, the SP coach and at least one ACP coach attend the rehearsal days
#### Constraints
- Half a rehearsal day per role proved useful, so rehearsal is a substantial time investment for each role

### Target Learners
- Simulated persons preparing roles for ACP facilitator courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardised, repeatable portrayal of ACP roles and giving feedback after conversations
