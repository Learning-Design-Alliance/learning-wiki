# Build a functional Yagi ground-station antenna from makeshift materials with an empirically tuned driven element

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To strengthen reception of the CanSat's 433.35 MHz signal, students built a rudimentary Yagi antenna from makeshift materials: an insulating plastic boom cut to 392 mm, drilled holes, aluminum elements, and a cable connected to the axial dipole with grounding on the opposite side. The antenna's directivity "provides good attenuation against signals from directions other than pointing".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A driven element nominally of length λ/2, empirically found best at 0.93 λ/2 (320 mm in this build)
#### Constraints
- The antenna design shown is rudimentary, built from makeshift materials
