# Teachers design learning that activates all senses rather than focusing on one sense

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's practical implication is that "learning should activate all senses, not only focusing on one of the senses". Teachers act as designers, actors, and evaluators: creating learning atmospheres that stimulate maximum use of all senses, selecting strategies and media that trigger various sensory organs, and reflecting on whether their teaching accommodates all learning-style characteristics. The article also recommends constructivist learning strategies appropriate to elementary students' development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select learning strategies or models that facilitate use of multiple senses equally in receiving instructional information
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of elementary education in Ambon-Indonesia, where the article says violence practices and conventional teacher-centered learning hinder emotional development

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- developing students' emotional intelligence alongside intellectual intelligence
