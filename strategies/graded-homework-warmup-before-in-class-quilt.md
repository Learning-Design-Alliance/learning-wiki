# Administer the QuILT warmup as graded homework before the in-class tutorial

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on posttest results, the authors recommend that the QuILT's warmup, which contains background on the classical beam of light through the MZI, be completed before class. They note the warmup was ungraded and propose: "the warmup should be administered as a graded homework to ensure that students complete it before working on the QuILT in class." This targets the lingering Q1 difficulty on classical interference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The warmup on the classical beam of light must be completed by students before the in-class QuILT on single photons
#### Constraints
- The difficulty arose when the warmup was administered at home as ungraded before class

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate students in quantum mechanics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- interference of a classical beam of light through the Mach-Zehnder Interferometer
