# Use shared frameworks, cross-district collaboration, and state-level policy alignment to scale AI-enabled edtech

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
For the scaling and sustaining phase, the article recommends developing standardized frameworks, strengthening state-level policy coordination, and coordinated funding structures. It states these would allow districts to "leverage shared resources and collaborative approaches, reducing the current need for each district to independently build complex procurement systems."
