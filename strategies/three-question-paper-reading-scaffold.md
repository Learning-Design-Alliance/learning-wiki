# Progressively answer three questions while reading: what problem, how addressed, what was found

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The guide structures deep reading as three cumulative comprehension checkpoints, each with a fill-in sentence the reader should be able to complete: first, what mystery or controversy the paper addresses (found near the end of the introduction); second, how the authors address it (sample, observations, physical measurement); third, what they found or did not find (from the discussion and conclusions). Readers are told to focus on physical meaning rather than arcane instrumental details and to ask advisors questions as needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the paper's introduction, sample/data, discussion, and conclusions sections; willingness to ask advisors or local experts questions
#### Constraints
- The guide advises focusing on basics in the data section and circling back to details only if needed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehending the purpose, methods, and findings of a research paper
- grounding abstract concepts in physical meaning
