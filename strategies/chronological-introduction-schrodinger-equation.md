# Introduce the Schrödinger equation chronologically, deriving it from the classical wave equation using the photon-energy and de Broglie-wavelength relations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is a chronological teaching sequence: the lecturer first reminds students of the classical wave equation, starting from an intuitive phenomenon (water ripples) before the mathematically simpler sine wave, then applies the quantum concepts of Planck and Einstein and de Broglie to transform the classical wave into the Schrödinger equation. The author states: "My preferred method is the chronological one." The derivation follows "the step to derive the classical wave equation," replacing the displacement A(x,t) with the wavefunction Ψ(x,t).
