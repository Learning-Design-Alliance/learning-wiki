# Short structured LUS training combining e-learning, half-day on-site session and focused proficiency assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PLUS-IS-LESS LUS training programme combined "an e-learning course and a half-day on-site theoretical and practical session", followed by a 32-question online quiz and submission of supervised LUS examinations, with certification requiring ≥80% on both components. Remote review of submitted scans demonstrated the feasibility of remote supervision in routine practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- completion of all four components: e-learning, on-site training, online quiz and required LUS examinations
- supervision by certified LUS experts
#### Constraints
- certification threshold based on expert consensus rather than empirically validated cut-off values
- long-term skill retention not assessed

### Target Learners
- emergency department medical supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- pneumonia diagnosis with lung ultrasound
- standardised LUS scanning protocol
