# Differentiate AI integration guidance by educational level (primary, secondary, undergraduate, graduate)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CAIAF tailors AI integration guidance and examples to each educational stage. The article states that "The AIAS differentiates between educational levels in order to account for the various stages of development, cognitive abilities, and academic requirements of students at each level." Primary education emphasizes basic AI ethics and safety through practice; secondary education introduces more complex tools; undergraduate work applies AI in STEM and social science with integrity emphasis; graduate work involves advanced applications with ethics training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distinct examples for all six levels across primary, secondary, undergraduate, and postgraduate settings
#### Constraints
- The original AIAS authors identified failure to separate K-12 from higher education as a notable limitation

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary (K-12) students
- undergraduate and graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- developmentally appropriate AI literacy
- ethical behavior that persists into adulthood
