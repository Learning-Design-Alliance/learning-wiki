# Student-Generated Questions in PBL

## Description
Sparking engagement with student-generated questions involves encouraging students to investigate topics they care about and identify questions they want to answer. This approach fosters investment in the inquiry process and empowers students to take ownership of their learning. In contrast to traditional instruction where information is spoon-fed, PBL allows students to explore what matters to them, making learning more meaningful and relevant.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom environment that values student input and curiosity, and instructional strategies that support student-generated questions.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and flexibility in the curriculum, and may necessitate additional support for students who are not used to generating their own questions.
