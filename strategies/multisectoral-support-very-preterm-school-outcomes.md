# Improving educational outcomes for very preterm children may require mitigating social disadvantage and addressing modifiable early-life medical risks

## Description
The article recommends that support for children born very preterm operate across multiple levels rather than through clinical care alone. It states that "Interventions may need to operate at structural (eg, reducing child poverty), family (eg, supporting parent-infant interaction), and area (eg, access to high-quality early-years education) levels, particularly for children born very preterm." It also identifies practical intervention targets including maternal nonsmoking, appropriate corticosteroid use, and breastfeeding, and suggests deferred school entry or targeted academic support depending on birth season.
