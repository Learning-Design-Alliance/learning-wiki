# Require departments to self-commit to teaching PBL groups as a condition of sending tutors to training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes that "For each PTT trainee, the department had to self-commit to teach at least two PBL groups in the following four semesters." This institutional strategy secured tutor recruitment, reduced the number of required training courses, and ensured that trained tutors would actually apply their training in practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional backing from a vice deanship or equivalent body to enforce and monitor the commitment
#### Constraints
- It increases the number of involuntary participants, some of whom found the training an annoying commitment

### Target Learners
- department heads in medical faculties
- new PBL tutors

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained application of PBL tutor training in practice
