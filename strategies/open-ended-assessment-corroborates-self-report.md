# Use open-ended scenario-based assessments to corroborate self-report evaluation results in online learning environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that developers of online learning environments use open-ended, skill-based assessments alongside self-report rating scales when measured learning objectives include skill-based concepts. The authors state that "The use of open-ended assessments as a method for corroborating evaluation results obtained from self-report scales also provides important evidence for other online learning environment developers who are looking to demonstrate the effectiveness of their instructional materials." Although resources required are higher than for a standard rating-scale assessment, confidence in the results should be higher for skill-based concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development of an effective scoring rubric and training of scorers who can apply the rubric reliably.
#### Constraints
- The resources required to administer such an assessment are clearly higher than would be necessary for a standard rating-scale-based (e.g., Likert) assessment.

### Target Learners
- Graduate students in online learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- Skill-based learning outcomes such as interpersonal problem solving
