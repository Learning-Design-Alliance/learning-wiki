# Optimize equipment and resource availability for sepsis detection and priority management, including continual core temperature monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's third recommendation is optimization of the resources or equipment required for sepsis detection and implementation of priority measures. Continual core temperature monitoring is a patient monitoring standard in the study ICUs given the accuracy limitations of peripheral thermometers and was a perceived facilitator, while delayed access to antibiotics and other equipment gaps were perceived barriers to priority sepsis management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires requisite follow-up at the local level, including root cause analysis of equipment delays, and engagement of interdisciplinary stakeholders including pharmacy to co-develop solutions
#### Constraints
- The article notes the reasons for perceived antibiotic delay at the local level are unknown and require further evaluation

### Target Learners
- ICU registered nurses
- Interdisciplinary critical care teams including pharmacy

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely sepsis recognition
- Implementation of priority sepsis interventions
