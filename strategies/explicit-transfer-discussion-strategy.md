# Include explicit transfer instructions discussing how trained strategies could apply to new tasks to promote transfer of metacognitive training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that training groups explicitly discuss how trained strategies could be used on different tasks: trainees "do not practice applying the strategy to the new task, but just discuss (with the trainer) how the approach could in principle be used on other tasks." These explicit transfer instructions produced reliable transfer to text learning in older adults trained in imagery and sentence generation. The authors suggest this works by prompting task evaluation to determine whether and how a self-regulatory strategy applies to new materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trainer-led discussion of how the trained strategy could in principle be used on other tasks
#### Constraints
- Transfer instructions alone involve discussion without practice applying the strategy to the new task

### Target Learners
- older adults in memory-training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- transferring trained learning strategies to untrained tasks such as text learning
