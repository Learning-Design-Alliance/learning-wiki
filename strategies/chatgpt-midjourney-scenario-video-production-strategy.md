# Use ChatGPT to break case scripts into scenes, prompt Midjourney for animation, and generate and place question-bank items in scenario simulation videos

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a concrete production workflow for AI-empowered scenario videos: the teaching team writes case scripts, then asks ChatGPT "How many scenes can this script be broken down into for animation video creation?"; prompts ChatGPT to act as the "Midjourney command optimization master" to generate scene descriptions in Chinese and English; draws animation with Midjourney; and has ChatGPT write questions from the script plot with answers and scoring criteria. Teachers review each output for accuracy and alignment with educational goals before the videos are integrated into the class app.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching research team trained in scenario simulation teaching who are proficient in using ChatGPT reviews AI outputs for accuracy and alignment with teaching objectives
#### Constraints
- Teachers found AI sometimes "cannot answer my questions well" and flagged accuracy of answers and academic honesty as concerns requiring caution

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates in clinical internships

### Target Learning Goals
- cardiovascular disease clinical reasoning and clinical operation skills
