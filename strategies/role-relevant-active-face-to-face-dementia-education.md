# Make dementia education relevant to participants' role and experience and involve active face-to-face participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy directs program designers to tailor dementia education to what participants actually do and have experienced, and to deliver it through active, in-person participation. The synthesis found efficacious programs are "relevant to participants' role and experience" and "involve active face-to-face participation," engaging care staff as active learners rather than passive recipients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program content must be matched to participants' role and experience, and delivery must involve active face-to-face participation.
#### Constraints
- Derived from a synthesis of studies of variable quality; the review states further robust research is required.

### Target Learners
- Health and social care workforce staff receiving dementia education and training

### Target Learning Goals
- Dementia knowledge and skills relevant to participants' roles, learned through active participation
