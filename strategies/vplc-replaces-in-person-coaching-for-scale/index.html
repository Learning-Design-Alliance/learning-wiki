Replace labor-intensive in-person coaching with a virtual professional learning community to scale professional development¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In prior ASSISTments implementations, teachers received in-person training plus three one-on-one coaching visits per year with between-session coach access. The report states: "While this model offered valuable support, it was labor-intensive, costly, and ultimately not scalable", and teachers lacked opportunities to build peer networks. The current intervention therefore introduced a vPLC as a scalable alternative that sustains instructional impact while fostering teacher collaboration, reflection, and professional growth across districts and states.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The vPLC requires facilitators experienced with the platform, an asynchronous community channel (Slack), and structured synchronous sessions to substitute for individual coaching visits.

Constraints¶

  • The strategy was developed to support implementation at scale, particularly in rural and underresourced settings where travel and substitute-teacher logistics bar in-person training.

Target Learners¶

  • Rural and underresourced middle school math teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Scalable professional learning supporting effective implementation of an evidence-based digital math program

Related Strategies¶

  • Deliver Getting Ready educator training and coaching through virtual formats
