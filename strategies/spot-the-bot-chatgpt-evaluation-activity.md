# Use masked ChatGPT responses in spot-the-bot activities to foster students' critical evaluation of scientific accuracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes presenting students with ChatGPT responses alongside masked expert solutions so they critically evaluate scientific accuracy and completeness. It reports that "false ChatGPT responses can be used to educate students, e.g., in an activity like ''spot the bot''", and that some surveyed students "wished for more exercises of this kind". The activity gives students a unique opportunity to promote metacognition and reflect on their understanding and reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors guide students and teach them how to conduct the evaluation process based on knowledge taught in the study course program
#### Constraints
- Research about learning with errors shows such activities are most beneficial for advanced students and only with an explicit intervention

### Target Learners
- introductory university physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of AI-generated explanations
- metacognition
- recognizing faulty physics argumentation
