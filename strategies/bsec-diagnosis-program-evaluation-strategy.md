# Use the BSEC to diagnose secondary students' entrepreneurial competency training needs and evaluate business education program effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article positions the BSEC as a practical assessment tool for entrepreneurship education in compulsory secondary schooling. Because it is a brief validated 13-item scale, practitioners can administer it to "assess and detect students' training needs and to find out the effect of business education programs on entrepreneurial competencies", enabling both diagnostic use and program evaluation in adolescent populations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to secondary students enrolled in or comparable to entrepreneurial education program settings, with informed parental consent and a supervised administration session
#### Constraints
- Validated in the Spanish compulsory secondary context with YEE program participants; the authors caution it is not a universal instrument and further validation in other school and age contexts is needed

### Target Learners
- Adolescent students in compulsory secondary education prior to entering the workforce or higher education tracks

### Target Learning Goals
- Development and assessment of entrepreneurial competencies
