# Use a fold-and-tear carbon duplicate form so judges sort name slips into rank order and then transcribe ranks onto the original list

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes an implementable rating procedure: fold the sheet on the center perforation, insert carbon paper, enter names on the left; the judge tears off the duplicate slips and arranges them "by first arranging them into groups, each group to be later classified within itself." The judge may "re-arrange the names as often as he wishes, without erasing," then writes rank numbers opposite each name on the original alphabetical list. The original list is mailed or taken to its destination while slips are rearranged for other traits or discarded.
