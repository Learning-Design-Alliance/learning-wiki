# Embed children's environmental health topics in nursing curricula using blended-learning interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that topics on children's environmental health be included in nurse education because students are aware of the issue but lack the knowledge and skills to manage environment-related problems. They state the intervention "can easily be added into most nursing curricula to enhance learning in this vital area" and urge future projects to develop it across universities internationally, ideally with control groups where the topic is not yet in the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- digital materials such as the NurSusTOOLKIT module, trained tutors, and session time within the curriculum
#### Constraints
- the evidence base is a single quasi-experimental pre-post study without a control group; the authors call for controlled future research

### Target Learners
- nursing students in undergraduate paediatric/child health courses

### Target Learning Goals
- children's environmental health attitudes, knowledge, and skills
