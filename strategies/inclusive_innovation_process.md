# Inclusive Innovation Process

## Description
The Inclusive Innovation Process is a framework that facilitates growth with equity through a student, community, and educator-led research and design process. It involves five phases: Connect and Commit, Inquire and Investigate, Design and Develop, Implement and Iterate, and Sustain and Scale. The process emphasizes intentional inclusion, collaboration, and reflection to create solutions for equity-related problems in the community.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A diverse team including students, educators, administrators, and community members, time for each phase of the process, and resources for data collection and analysis.
#### Constraints
- The process can be time-consuming and requires a strong commitment from all participants. Success depends on the ability to foster open communication and collaboration among diverse stakeholders.
