# Use detailed rubrics and additional TA guidance to increase feedback on student lab notebooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the transformed course, TAs grade submitted notebook PDFs online, making "comments directly on the PDFs using detailed rubrics to assure consistency in grading among the 22 TAs." Because about 40% of students reported receiving too little feedback, the authors state they "plan to modify the grading rubrics to allow TAs to more easily give students additional feedback" and that "additional guidance for the TAs is necessary to increase the amount of feedback they provide to students on their notebooks."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed grading rubrics used consistently across the TA team, and the rubrics must be designed so TAs can easily provide additional feedback
