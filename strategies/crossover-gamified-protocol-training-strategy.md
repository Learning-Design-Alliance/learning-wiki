# Crossover gamified childbirth-protocol training design: half the topics gamified, half lectured, then reversed

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating interactive learning and gamification into midwifery education programs, implemented here as a crossover design: one group learned half of the six protocol topics via gamification while the other used lectures, then the remaining topics were taught in reverse order. Gamification ran weekly for four weeks with sequenced content, points, and leaderboard feedback, and performance was assessed at the maternal bedside via a Ministry-of-Health-based checklist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment by lottery, informed consent, first-time registrants for the clinical course, and a blinded data analyst
#### Constraints
- Blinding was not feasible for participants or instructors due to the nature of the interventions; the study's short duration may not have allowed assessment of long-term impacts or knowledge retention

### Target Learners
- undergraduate midwifery students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge and practical performance of the childbirth protocol
