# Use Inductive Miner SRL behavior models to build early warning systems and SRL-based recommendation systems for eLearning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
As forward-looking applications, the authors propose using the discovered behavior models predictively and for personalization. They write that "Modeling behavior with IM algorithms could contribute to developing early warning systems to predict at-risk students while a course is in progress." They further suggest the results could contribute to personalizing e-learning environments in terms of self-regulation, building recommendation systems based on different SRL behaviors and evaluating the adequacy of different types of prompts for different SRL behavior models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Process mining models of student SRL behavior discovered from LMS event logs, and visual analytics of learning models to support real-time teaching decisions
#### Constraints
- The authors present these as promising areas of future study, not as tested applications in this study

### Target Learners
- at-risk university students in online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- early prediction of failure and difficulties, and personalized support of self-regulated learning
