# Provide teachers with time, resources and professional development to explore iPads before implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that schools support iPad implementation through innovative pedagogical design and by giving teachers time, energy and resources to explore the devices. Teachers adopting iPads "must have time to evaluate and mediate their own professional learning needs" and be provided "opportunities for critical reflection throughout the journey".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School support allowing teachers to gain confidence through time, energy and resources to explore iPads and new teaching and learning practices
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises in the context of a rapidly changing digital technology landscape and Ministry of Education encouragement to adopt digital tools

### Target Learners
- middle-school teachers implementing iPads

### Target Learning Goals
- effective integration of iPads into reading instruction; teacher professional learning
