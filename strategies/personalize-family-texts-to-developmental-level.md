# Personalize and differentiate family text messages to each child's developmental level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The kindergarten follow-up tested whether "personalized and differentiated messaging based on each child’s developmental level would enhance outcomes compared to general literacy messages." The personalized text arm showed the largest literacy gains (effect size = +0.18), supporting tailoring message content to the child's level rather than sending general literacy content to all families.
