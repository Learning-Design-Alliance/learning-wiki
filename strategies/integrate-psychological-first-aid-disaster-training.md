# Integrate psychological first aid and psychological preparedness content into disaster training programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies "integration of psychological preparedness" as a common element of effective programs and reports that psychological first aid training produced significant improvements in nurses' and students' psychological preparedness, self-efficacy, and related outcomes. This strategy recommends embedding PFA modules, based on international guidelines and adapted to local cultural and disaster contexts, within disaster preparedness curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers qualified to deliver psychosocial content (in the reviewed studies, a licensed psychologist delivered the modified RAPID-PFA program)
