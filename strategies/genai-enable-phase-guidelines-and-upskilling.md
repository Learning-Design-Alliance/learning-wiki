## Description
The article's Enable phase prescribes concrete institutional actions: course and unit coordinators redefine learning outcomes, evaluation methodologies and assessments to accommodate GenAI usage; academic staff require upskilling with tools and training; and evaluation methods for assignments and exams must become GenAI-friendly. As printed: "policies and guidelines regarding ethics, privacy, and academic integrity must be updated to address these issues", and faculty and students should be educated about related ethical and privacy issues.
