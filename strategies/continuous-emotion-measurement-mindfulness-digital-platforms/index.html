Measure and respond to student emotions continuously in digital platforms and promote mindfulness and emotional awareness¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

From the Moore perspective on K-12 students, the editorial highlights the shift from classrooms where teachers can see and react to student needs to digital platforms where such engagement may be limited. It recommends measuring and responding to student emotions in digital platforms continuously, and focuses on "interventions that may promote mindfulness and emotional awareness and longitudinal studies that can explore young learners' shift to digital learning."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Continuous measurement of student emotions in digital platforms

Constraints¶

  • The K-12 student audience should be studied before applying findings from medical students, who are likely high performing and motivated

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students learning on digital platforms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Emotional awareness and mindfulness in digital learning

Claims¶

  • Medical students' positive emotions correlate with better diagnostic performance in a computer-based learning environment [+W]
  • Cluster analysis of medical students' emotions after diagnostic tasks reveals three distinct emotion clusters [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Foster positive emotions, support self-regulatory skills, and provide emotional supports for students shifting to digital learning
  • Integrate mindfulness throughout the school day via mindful moments, pausing before reacting, and reflective norms
  • Emotional Check-ins
  • Use pausing, body-centered awareness, and mindful listening as tools in restorative justice and conflict resolution
  • Develop and test interventions and teacher reflective practices to harness emotions as malleable factors in K-12 settings
  • Use robust causal research designs (What Works Clearinghouse guidelines) for future emotion and digital learning studies
