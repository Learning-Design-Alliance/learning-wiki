# Hiring committees should weigh education-research training when PoTs are expected to engage in scholarly activity, and provide teaching relief and physical space

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions supporting teaching-focused faculty with scholarly expectations align hiring, training, and resources accordingly. It states that "a lack of education research training does not disqualify PoTs from pursuing such research, it does put them at a disadvantage both in terms of their abilities to publish peer-reviewed work as well as obtain extramural funding". It also recommends teaching relief and physical space, noting the misconception that education research can be conducted within the classroom.
