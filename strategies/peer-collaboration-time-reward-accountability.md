# Support unfacilitated peer collaboration with a time constraint, a small reward, and an incentive for individual accountability.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that because unfacilitated peer collaboration requires minimal instructor effort, students "should be given ample opportunity and incentive to c ollaborate with peers" both inside and outside the classroom. It identifies three optimizing factors: a time constraint to keep students focused, a reward system such as homework, quiz, or bonus points, and an incentive for individual accountability such as a future exam worked alone on similar concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A time frame within which collaborative work should be submitted, even for out-of-class collaboration
#### Constraints
- The collaboration studied was unguided, with no help or facilitation from the instructor beyond prior coverage of the relevant physics principles

### Target Learners
- calculus-based introductory physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of electricity and magnetism
