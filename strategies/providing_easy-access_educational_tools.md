# Providing Easy-Access Educational Tools

## Description
Making educational tools readily available to learners promotes independence and self-directed learning. By storing materials in accessible locations, learners can quickly and independently access the resources they need to support their learning. This approach is particularly helpful for learners who require accommodations or have specific learning preferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires organizing materials in accessible locations, providing clear instructions on how to use the tools, and encouraging learners to use them independently.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and organization. Some learners may need additional support in learning how to use the tools effectively.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who may struggle to access materials or require accommodations.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote learner independence, facilitate self-directed learning, and ensure that all learners have access to the tools they need to succeed.

### Affordances
- Increases learner autonomy, promotes self-reliance, and reduces reliance on the educator for support.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by providing different types of tools and resources based on individual learner needs.
