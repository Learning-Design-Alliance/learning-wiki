# Correlate-then-explain: students search freely for correlations, then seek scientific causes and present seminars

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After a class discussing phenomenon, observation, measurement, physical laws, theory and causality, students were asked 'to search freely for search terms correlations related to Physics Teaching in Google Correlate'. Once the best correlated terms were obtained, students deepened their research from several other sources, 'looking for possible scientific explanations (causation) for them', and present results as seminars; the best works may be converted into science-teaching articles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A prior class discussing the concepts of phenomenon, observation, measurement, physical laws, theory, and causality; access to Google Correlate.
#### Constraints
- The emphasis is on the search process of possible causes for the correlation, not on the identification of the correlation itself or its quantitative aspects.

### Target Learners
- pre-service Physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding of phenomenon, observation, measurement, physical laws, theory, and causality
- technical and ethical preparation for the scientific challenges posed by Big Data
