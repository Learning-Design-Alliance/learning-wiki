# Manipulatives: Sentence Combining

## Description
Using physical or digital manipulatives to combine simple sentences, thereby reducing working memory load and improving syntax knowledge. This involves providing a bank of movable sentences and clauses, potentially color-coded by parts of speech, to support learners of all different needs and serve as a bridge in understanding sentence structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to physical or digital manipulatives representing sentences and clauses. Color-coding materials can be useful but not required.
#### Constraints
- May be less effective if learners lack basic vocabulary or grammatical understanding. Reliance on manipulatives may hinder the development of abstract understanding if not faded appropriately.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, particularly beneficial for learners who struggle with sentence structure and syntax.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve syntax knowledge, reduce working memory load, support sentence construction skills

### Affordances
- Reduces cognitive load by providing manageable sentence components. Supports kinesthetic learning through physical manipulation. Color-coding provides visual cues to enhance understanding of sentence structure.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying the complexity of sentences and clauses. Digital versions can offer adjustable levels of scaffolding and automated feedback.

- Research indicates that manipulatives can improve reading comprehension and writing skills. Providing sentence components reduces cognitive load, which supports learners' working memory capacity. Color-coding parts of speech enhances syntax knowledge.
