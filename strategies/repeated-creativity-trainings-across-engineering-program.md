# Schedule repeated creativity trainings across the engineering program with creativity educators embedded in integrative projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student comments and the view that cognitive abilities must stay anchored through continuous practice, the authors recommend two creativity trainings (e.g., autumn of 1st and 3rd year) completed with the involvement of creativity educators in integrative projects each spring. They argue this would "allow great opportunities to master the cognitive abilities and apply them to engineering projects", extending the single elective course into a program-level design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous practise is important for the cognitive abilities to stay anchored and useable by the learners
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is offered as a more ambitious training program beyond the current elective course; it has not itself been tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students across program years

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering and applying creativity-related cognitive abilities in engineering projects
