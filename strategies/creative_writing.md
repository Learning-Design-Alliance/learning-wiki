# Creative Writing

## Description
Former teachers Ed Kang and Amy Schwartzbach-Kang incorporated storytelling and creative writing into their after-school program’s science lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires prompts or activities that encourage storytelling. Requires time for students to write.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle with creative writing. Teachers need to provide guidance and support.

## Examples
