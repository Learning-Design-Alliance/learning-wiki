Adopt sector-specific OHS modules instead of generic courses.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends moving away from generic OHS courses toward sector-specific modules: for construction, intensive focus on "working at heights" and "scaffolding safety" using VR or physical simulations; for healthcare, modules on "safe commuting/transportation" and "ergonomics for patient handling"; for mining/industry, machinery lockout/tagout procedures and risk assessment matrices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educational institutions shift from generic safety modules to sector-simulated environments

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • vocational students in construction, healthcare, and mining/industry programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sector-specific hazard recognition and response

Claims¶

  • Generic safety curricula fail to address specific lethal risks such as falls in construction or transport-related injuries in healthcare. [+W]
  • Active learning methods significantly improve safety motivation and internal locus of control compared to traditional passive instruction. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Establish data-driven curriculum updates triggered by national accident statistics.
