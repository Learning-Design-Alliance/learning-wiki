# Pencil-lead line-drawing procedure for estimating molecule size in class

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A classroom procedure in which students draw many equal lines of known length on millimeter paper with a pencil lead kept straight, measure the lead before and after, and compute the molecular size from the volume match. The authors found it "easier to draw short lines, about 10 cm long, in the central columns of the millimeter paper, starting from above".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pencil lead, millimeter paper and a measuring instrument; the HB lead used had H = 60 mm and 2 R = 0.5 mm according to the manufacturer's specifications.
#### Constraints
- Lines are drawn in the central columns of the paper starting from above, and the estimate assumes no graphite is lost off the paper.

### Target Learners
- students at the basic level of education

### Target Learning Goals
- estimating the size of molecules through measurement and calculation

### Affordances
- Pencil Lead Volume Matching Upper Bound Model
