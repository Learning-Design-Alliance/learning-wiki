# Use "I" Statements to Avoid Blame

## Description
Avoid using “you” statements, which can make students defensive. Instead, use “I” statements to talk about situations without placing blame and to acknowledge your own feelings. These questions also allow you to acknowledge your own feelings, and may encourage students to consider your emotions.
