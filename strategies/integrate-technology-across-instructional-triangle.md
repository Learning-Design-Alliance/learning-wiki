# Integrate technology across the entire instructional triangle of teachers, students, and instructional resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Cohen et al. (2003), the report states that "education only improves when changes occur to integrate technology across the entire instructional triangle—a triangle of connections among teachers, students, and instructional resources." This means technology should support students working alone or in groups, teachers' work with students, and teachers' access to useful information—not serve merely as a substitution for one narrow activity such as practice problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meaningful use must address the full range of curricular goals and standards, including conceptual understanding and long-term skills
#### Constraints
- The report cautions against accepting vendor claims of personalization at face value and notes machine learning algorithms can exacerbate inequities if trained on biased datasets

### Target Learners
- K-12 students and their teachers in virtual or blended settings

### Target Learning Goals
- deep engagement with curricular standards and skills through meaningful technology use
