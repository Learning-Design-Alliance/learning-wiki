# Provide open-ended, making-focused environments with space to experiment and invent as a route to powerful mathematical discoveries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable approach: facilitate wonderful and powerful mathematical ideas through open-ended, making-focused, analog technology-rich environments such as learning to weave. The article states: "It seems that wonderful and powerful ideas can be facilitated by open-ended, making-focused, (analog) technology-rich environments such as learning to weave." The author notes that mathematical discoveries about shape, number, and more may not have been possible in a traditional classroom setting without an approach that allows for experimentation as well as space and time to solve complex challenges in multiple ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space and time to solve complex challenges in multiple ways
- An approach that allows for experimentation
#### Constraints
- Space to experiment and invent may not always be possible in classrooms

### Target Learners
- middle-school youth

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematical discovery
- creative mathematical engagement
