# Extend competition benefits beyond participants by sharing excellent works, videos, and operational guidance through open online registration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Discussion the authors propose that future competitions increase overall quality and benefit more students by providing excellent works, videos, and operational guidance on a special website, and by adopting open registration to promote sharing of education resources. They argue that "even if only a few students are permitted to attend the final competition, other dental schools and students could benefit from the professional resources provided by the websites and the precompetition training or selections". They also propose adding clinically relevant items such as composite resin filling, root canal treatment, tooth extraction, 3D digital intraoral scanning, and case-based clinical simulation items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A special website hosting excellent works, videos, and operational guidance for standardized clinical operations
- Open registration to promote sharing of education resources
#### Constraints
- The article states future goals will focus on evaluating the outcomes of these new measures, i.e., they had not yet been tested

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students, including non-participating students at dental schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized clinical operations
- Clinical skills and theoretical knowledge beyond the competition participants
