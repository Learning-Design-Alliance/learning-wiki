# Integrate resilience-building strategies and psychological skills into communication training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that internal psychological factors—especially resilience—were the strongest predictors of perceived competency, the authors recommend that "communication training should shift from purely technical training to integrating resilience‑building strategies and psychological skills." They further recommend nurse leaders implement ongoing mentorship and feedback, and that administrators design separate training tracks for junior and senior nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- organizational commitment to longitudinal, simulation-based training rather than rote checklists
#### Constraints
- recommendation derives from a single-group quasi-experimental study without a control group

### Target Learners
- qualified nurses across experience levels

### Target Learning Goals
- resilience and volitional control in clinical handover
