Apply survey-sampling non-response analysis methods to site recruitment in experiments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes extending non-response analysis techniques from survey sampling to the problem of site recruitment in randomized experiments, treating districts that decline participation as survey non-respondents. It frames this as an unaddressed problem: "The concern with non-response is one that has yet to be addressed in current literature". The authors apply these methods in the full paper to compare recruitment-decision groups on covariates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A detailed list of eligible schools with covariate data, grouped by recruitment decision
  • Recruitment staged so that eligibility and participation decisions can be tracked

Constraints¶

  • Results of the non-response analysis were not available in the abstract due to space; only first-wave recruitment was assessed

Target Learners¶

  • Districts and schools recruited into large-scale education experiments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving generalizability of experimental treatment effects through recruitment design
