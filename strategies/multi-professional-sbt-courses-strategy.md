# Develop multi-professional simulation-based training courses so nurses and doctors train the same algorithms and non-technical skills together

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The doctors expressed that training with other health professionals such as nurses might be beneficial, since nurses are the team members doctors collaborate with in real emergencies. The article suggests that "transfer of skills would be enhanced if both professions were trained together" and recommends developing multi-professional SBT courses in handling critically ill patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurses should also train the same algorithms and NTS as the doctors
#### Constraints
- The article notes concern about staff willingness and preparedness in using these tools, which currently impedes transfer

### Target Learners
- first-year doctors
- nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- handling critically ill patients
- shared algorithms and non-technical skills for acute team situations
