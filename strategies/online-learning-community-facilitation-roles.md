# Facilitate a learning community in which the teacher-learner knowledge boundary is blurred and social presence is cultivated

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's fifth guideline states that "effective online learning can be sustainable and strengthened by a learning community," in which teachers are not the only source of knowledge and students learn from teaching and observing peers. Teachers take multiple roles: information provider, facilitator of participation and peer learning, curator connecting students to resources, and lifelong learner, not just an evaluator.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Social presence is crucial: members including teachers should feel comfortable being who they are, share struggles and emotions, and teachers should show a non-judgmental attitude and vulnerability about their own learning needs.
