# Explicitly teach a divide-and-conquer problem-solving approach and pair quantitative with conceptual problems on the same physics principle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "students should be explicitly taught the divide and conquer approach, i.e., how to solve a problem systematically by dividing it into sub-problems", for example drawing free body diagrams and considering the electric and magnetic forces separately before taking their vector sum. It further recommends that students "be given opportunity to solve paired quantitative and conceptual problems which use the same principle of physics together" so that they do not treat conceptual problems as a guessing task.
