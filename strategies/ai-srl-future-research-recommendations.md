# Future AI-SRL research should broaden search strategies, include educators and younger learners, address motivation, and apply longitudinal designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on identified gaps, the review recommends that future reviews use "broader search strategies, such as keyword expansion or full-text searches, to provide a more inclusive representation of the literature". It further recommends longitudinal research to assess long-term impacts of AI on SRL, more global participation to bring diverse perspectives, greater attention to the motivational aspect of SRL, and inclusion of educators, who are described as crucial in implementing SRL strategies. It also recommends a full quality appraisal of studies in any future full systematic review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future reviews should conduct a full quality appraisal of included studies
#### Constraints
- The review notes its own strategy's Global North geographical and linguistic bias and that restricting keywords to title or abstract may have excluded relevant work

- primary and secondary students
