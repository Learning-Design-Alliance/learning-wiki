Use the IPLP word-learning task within the first 3 years of life to identify children at high risk for poor vocabulary development after cochlear implantation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends using the IPLP word-learning task as an early clinical screening tool. The authors state that 'this word-learning task has the potential to serve as a relatively early measure of language performance that may be predictive of vocabulary outcomes' and that such a tool 'could help clinicians identify children who are at risk for poor vocabulary acquisition after cochlear implantation'. Because word-learning performance correlated with later vocabulary size, clinicians could use this measure to adjust intervention strategies before children fall behind.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires administration of the IPLP word-learning task within the first 3 years of life (10 to 20 months after CI switch-on)

Constraints¶

  • Correlational findings should be interpreted with caution because of small sample sizes; further work is needed before drawing firm conclusions

Target Learners¶

  • Deaf children with cochlear implants within the first 3 years of life

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early identification of children at risk for poor vocabulary acquisition
