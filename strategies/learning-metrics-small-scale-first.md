# Use small-scale learning metrics during iteration and reserve proof metrics for later stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article advises defining meaningful metrics: "Measuring gains in student learning can require large studies over long time periods." Instead, teams should use "learning" metrics that matter on a small scale to guide the iterative process, and "Only invest in establishing 'proof' metrics once you are confident you're on the right track." This sequences measurement investment to match the loop's decision stages.
