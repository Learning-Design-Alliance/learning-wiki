# Use a national e-portfolio logbook (JETS) to log all EUS training procedures and enable KPI audit and learning-curve research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Trainees should preferably log all training procedures onto the JETS e-portfolio, which is recognised by all UK endoscopy trainees and trainers and enables formulation of unassisted KPIs embedded into EUS certification criteria. The pathway is being launched in conjunction with the JETS EUS logbook, and the authors state that "Data from JETS will provide a national picture on trainees’ progress, which will inform future research on learning curves on EUS."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees must demonstrate the certification KPIs recorded through the e-portfolio before summative assessment
#### Constraints
- Evidence for a similar model exists using the Rotterdam self-assessment ERCP form; validity is supported from other training modalities rather than EUS-specific studies

### Target Learners
- UK and Ireland EUS trainees and trainers

### Target Learning Goals
- objective tracking of procedural skills acquisition and competency development
