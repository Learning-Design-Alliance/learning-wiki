# Employ educational games in primary science teaching and train teachers to design them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that games outperformed traditional teaching, the article recommends employing educational games in teaching first grade and other grades, running "workshops and training sessions for Science teachers to enable them to design and use educational games in Science", including game-design theory models in teacher preparation programs, and extending similar studies to other subjects and grades.

## Design Implications

#### Constraints

- primary education students

### Target Learning Goals
