# Blended learning package combining an animated e-learning video with structured face-to-face training and supervised real-life practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's blended package combined the animated e-learning video with "an initial workshop run in two successive days in each of the two maternity units", including lectures on OASIS rates, diagnosis, risk factors and prevention, illustration of bPST and accurate episiotomy technique, followed by instructor-guided practical training on birthing simulators. A 10-day period of supervised real-life training in the labor rooms by tutor midwives followed, and more than 90% (84 doctors and midwives) of birth attendants were trained.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires senior obstetricians and tutor midwives to deliver the workshop and supervised labor-room training, with local research fellows assisting.
#### Constraints
- The workshop was run over two successive days in each unit so attendants working one day could attend the other; tablets were kept on site during and after the face-to-face training.

### Target Learners
- Birth attendants (doctors and midwives) in maternity units

### Target Learning Goals
- Performing the bimanual perineal support technique and indicated-only episiotomy to reduce obstetric anal sphincter injuries
