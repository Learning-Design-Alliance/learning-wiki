# Reflection with Others

## Description
This strategy involves teachers reflecting on their teaching practice by engaging in peer collaboration and seeking feedback from colleagues who teach similar math concepts. Schedule informal meetings or visits to discuss what's working well in your classroom and identify areas for improvement. Invite colleagues to observe a lesson and provide non-evaluative feedback, focusing on specific strategies, techniques, and best practices that you can incorporate into your own teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for peer collaboration, a willingness to seek feedback, and a focus on specific strategies, techniques, and best practices.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to engage in peer collaboration.
