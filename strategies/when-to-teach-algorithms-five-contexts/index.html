Embed algorithmic thinking lessons across content, standalone, SEL, problem-based, and review contexts¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The deck advises teachers on placement of algorithmic thinking lessons, listing that "Teachers might use algorithmic thinking lessons when" teaching their content, as a stand alone lesson, as a social emotional lesson, with problem-based strategies, or as a review/synthesis. It cautions that timing "depends upon your students, the grade level, the content, and the context", so placement is a professional judgment rather than a fixed sequence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must judge placement based on their students, grade level, content, and context

Constraints¶

  • The deck states timing depends on students, grade level, content, and context rather than prescribing a fixed placement

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8 across subject areas

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing algorithmic thinking within content instruction, problem-based work, and review

Related Strategies¶

  • Social Emotional Learning Integration
  • Integrate computational thinking into PreK-8 core subject matter rather than relying on stand-alone clubs and classes
  • Embed computational thinking across existing K-12 subject areas using everyday and data-based activities
