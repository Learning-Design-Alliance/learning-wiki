# Rebuild problem types around modeling, authentic labs, multi-representation translation, and peer learning that AI cannot substitute for

## Description
Beyond assessment architecture, the article recommends foregrounding what AI cannot substitute for: "modeling the world, arguing from evidence, and making principled approximations." Concretely, it favors Fermi estimates, novel contexts with explicit assumptions, multi-representation translation between diagram, math, and prose, and diagnose-and-repair tasks where students improve a flawed solution; it also urges authentic mobile-lab experiments with uncertainty analysis and messy data, and having students learn with and from each other.
