Anticipate barriers to data use by building in strategies for future data use from initial conversations, and trust the process by going at the community's pace.¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the implementation phase, the project found that programs sometimes struggled to use alumni survey findings to reflect on current practices and find space for healing beyond further connecting with alumni. The stated solution is to build in strategies for future data use in initial conversations. A companion strategy, trust the process, holds that when community members drive a process, facilitators go at their pace, sometimes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Build in strategies for future data use during initial conversations with programs

Constraints¶

  • Programs sometimes found it difficult to use the findings collected to reflect on current practices and find space for healing beyond further connecting with alumni
  • Community-driven processes move at the community's pace

Target Learners¶

  • high school program staff and alumni in equity-focused school communities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • using alumni survey findings for program reflection and improvement

Related Strategies¶

  • In co-design meetings, position community members as experts and create space for challenging ideas, including adapting survey questions to community feedback.
  • Form design teams by building trust and relationships and prioritizing meaningful representation before project kick-off.
