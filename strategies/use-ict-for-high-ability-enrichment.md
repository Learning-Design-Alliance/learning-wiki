# Use ICT to provide enrichment and extension activities for high-ability students

## Description
Based on its synthesis, the review recommends that educators leverage ICT to enrich and extend learning for students with high abilities, for example by offering digital resources that go beyond the standard curriculum. The review presents this as a promising practice supported by the literature it surveyed, while acknowledging the limited strength of the underlying evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to appropriate ICT resources and infrastructure
- Teacher capacity to select and integrate digital tools for enrichment
#### Constraints
- The evidence base is limited and methodologically heterogeneous

### Target Learners
- Students with high abilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Curricular enrichment and extension
