# Inquiry-based primary-school physics lesson structure alternating guided discussion, hands-on experiments, and a closing synthesis game

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each Fisici Senza Frontiere lesson follows a repeatable structure: guided discussions alternate with practical experiments; "con l'inquiry based learning attraverso domande stimolo, gli allievi devono interpretare alcuni fenomeni"; examples and counterexamples connect content to everyday experience; students build artefacts such as a thermoscope; and the lesson closes with "un gioco sotto forma di mappa concettuale o cruciverba per ordinare e creare una sintesi della lezione".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stimulus questions for inquiry-based interpretation
- Easily available low-tech materials alongside laboratory equipment
- Recap moments in which pupils themselves recount what they learned
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Primary school pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- Distinguishing heat from temperature, conductors and insulators, heat propagation methods
- Interpreting everyday phenomena with the scientific method
