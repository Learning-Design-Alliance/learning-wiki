# Four classroom uses: visualization, exploration, student-driven discussion, and guided-inquiry assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates four ways instructors can use Composer in an educational setting: visualizing concepts during lectures, open-ended exploration of systems beyond the curriculum, student-driven discussions where students predict the effect of manipulating variables and discuss in groups, and stand-alone curriculum-based assignments with pre-made flowscenes. The exercises typically use a guided-inquiry approach where students explore dependencies between parameters and quantities.
