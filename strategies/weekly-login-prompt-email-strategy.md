# Send teachers weekly emails prompting them to log into their learning platform dashboard to check an updated, personalized report on their students' progress

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's winning intervention prompted teachers to log into the Zearn Math dashboard weekly to view an updated report on how their students were performing that week. Emails referencing personalized, classroom-specific statistics—directly in the body or by hyperlinking to the dashboard—outperformed those without such data. The authors suggest this approach "may be that capitalizing on teachers’ intrinsic motivation to help their students is a distinct and potentially cost-effective approach that can complement other interventions, such as offering performance bonuses and other extrinsic incentives".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A platform dashboard containing personalized, classroom-level progress data teachers can access, and a channel (email) for weekly delivery of prompts referencing that data
#### Constraints
- Effects were small (5.06%, or 3.30% after winner's-curse adjustment) and were demonstrated over a 4-week intervention with 8-week follow-up on one math platform; the personalized-data benefit needs confirmation given mixed evidence in other domains

### Target Learners
- elementary school teachers using an online math instruction platform

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing students' math lesson completion and progress
