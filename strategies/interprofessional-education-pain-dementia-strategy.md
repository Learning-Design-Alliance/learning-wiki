# Address GP pain-management knowledge gaps through inter-professional education, including ECHO-model telementoring, combined with reimbursement and organisational incentives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends educational interventions for GPs focused on pain assessment and management in dementia, noting that Project ECHO-model teleconferencing interventions "have been found to improve healthcare professionals' knowledge and self-efficacy". Because GPs see nurses as facilitators while nurses have identified GPs as barriers, the article underlines "the importance of inter-professional educational initiatives in this area". It also cautions that education alone has limited effect on behaviour and must be combined with adequate reimbursement and organisational incentives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education needs to be combined with adequate reimbursement and organisational incentives to effectively improve GPs' performance in dementia care.
#### Constraints
- The article states educational interventions alone have limited effect in changing GPs' behaviour in dementia care, and that telementoring formats are favoured because they eliminate travel and prolonged absence from practice.

### Target Learners
- General practitioners
- Nursing staff
- Palliative care professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Pain assessment in dementia
- Pain management in dementia
- Inter-professional communication about pain in dementia
