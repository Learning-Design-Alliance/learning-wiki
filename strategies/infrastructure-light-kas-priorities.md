# Prioritize infrastructure-light KAS sub-dimensions in resource-constrained nursing curricula

## Description
For low-resource settings, the review recommends teaching strategies that do not rely on continuous access to advanced AI systems, and prioritizing KAS sub-dimensions less dependent on advanced technical infrastructure: K1 and K3 rather than technical K2; A1, A2, and A3; and S4 and S3 rather than complex S1. The article states "nursing curriculum design should prioritize KAS sub-dimensions that are less dependent on advanced technical infrastructure." It also suggests offline approaches such as case-based discussions and screen-based simulations with pre-recorded cases.
