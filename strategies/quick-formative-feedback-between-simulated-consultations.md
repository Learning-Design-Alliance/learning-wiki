# Use a short post-consultation questionnaire to give fast formative feedback between simulated patient interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding a brief rating instrument inside simulation-based training so that simulated patients can rate each consultation immediately and students receive feedback between interviews. The authors state its "great advantage over existing instruments is its quick use during simulations after every conversation, which allows for fast formative feedback to simulation participants". This was implemented within a competence-based simulated first day of residency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short questionnaire that simulated patients can complete in the few minutes between consultations
- Simulated patients trained with the instrument beforehand to standardize answers
#### Constraints
- Items were not anchored by examples to keep reading time short
- The instrument lacked a preceding validation study

### Target Learners
- Final-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Communication skills
- Interpersonal skills
- Formative feedback
