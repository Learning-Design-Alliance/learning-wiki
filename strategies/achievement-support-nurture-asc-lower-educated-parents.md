# Provide academic achievement support during elementary school to nurture ASC of children of lower-educated parents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that, because child-level academic achievement mediated the link between lower household parental education and less positive ASC, "one way to nurture the ASC development of children of lower-educated parents is to provide them with academic achievement support during elementary school". They also point toward a need for stronger academic support in lower parental education schools, and argue effective ASC interventions while children are still in elementary school may help maintain high ASC levels before the transition to secondary education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should target children while they are still in elementary school, since ASC levels were high but risk relative decline for lower-SES children later
#### Constraints
- The authors state more research is needed at the school level before firm conclusions; results do not imply causal associations

- children of lower-educated parents
