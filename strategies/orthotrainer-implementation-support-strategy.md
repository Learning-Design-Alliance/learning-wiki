# Support web-based diagnostic trainers with a short tutorial video, an instructional manual, and reliable network access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From their implementation experience the authors recommend supplementing the ten-minute tutorial video, writing that "the creation of a comprehensive instructional manual could be considered, in addition to the video", so students can outsource preparation time. They also advise dedicated sessions with a subject matter expert and stress that facilities must ensure reliable network access for the web-based tool.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are introduced to the application via a ten-minute practical video that guides them step by step through the app and presents a sample case
#### Constraints
- The article states it is imperative that facilities ensure reliable network access
- The Orthotrainer was still a pilot version restricted to RWTH Aachen University students and staff at the time of the study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in clinical orthodontic courses

### Target Learning Goals
- orthodontic diagnosis and case planning, including model, OPG and LCR analysis
