Run preparatory pre-activities to adapt students and teachers before PBL treatment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Because implementing PBL proved difficult in this study, the authors recommend preparatory work before treatment: "pre -activities should be performed for the adaptation of students and teachers before treatment." They further suggest giving students simple project works throughout the semester, presenting projects in a science exhibition to raise motivation, preparing a well-prepared time schedule, and having teachers give timely feedback on project work.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers, not researchers, should conduct the study to eliminate bias and overcome the implementation threat

Constraints¶

  • Treatment lasted only four weeks, so PBL activities were not implemented sufficiently in terms of time limitation

Target Learners¶

  • Seventh grade students new to PBL
  • science teachers new to PBL

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement in science
  • meaningful learning
  • adaptation to project work

Claims¶

  • The authors attribute the null PBL effect partly to students' adaptation problems when moving from traditional instruction [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Recommendation: evaluate PBL effectiveness through multicenter primary studies comparing educational centers, cultures, and psychological adaptation factors
