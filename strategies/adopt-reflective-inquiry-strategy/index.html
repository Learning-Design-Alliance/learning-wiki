Strategies

Adopt reflective inquiry instruction and institutionalise it in technical college curricula and teacher development¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on its findings, the article recommends that technical college teachers "adopt the use of the reflective inquiry instructional technique to the teaching of Electronic work trade", that the NBTE review the trade curriculum to incorporate the technique, and that ministries and college administrators organise workshops, seminars and conferences to improve teachers' knowledge and skills in its use. This is a system-level implementation recipe following the demonstrated achievement advantage.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training: the study organised a one-week intensive training programme for the involved teachers before treatment

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single quasi-experiment in Lagos State technical colleges with intact classes and no random assignment of individual students

Target Learners¶

  • Technical college Electronic Works trade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved achievement and ability level in Electronic Works trade

Claims¶

  • Reflective inquiry instructional technique produces significantly higher achievement in Electronic Works trade than conventional teaching method [+W]
  • No significant achievement difference between high- and low-ability students taught with reflective inquiry [+W]
  • No significant interaction between gender and ability level on achievement under reflective inquiry [+W]
  • No significant interaction between instructional treatment and gender on Electronic Works achievement [+W]
