# Assess and Differentiate

## Description
It is important to assess not only the content but also the language as you scaffold and teach vocabulary, structure, and function of the language. Through assessment of these students' language skills, you can decide how you will differentiate, whether through products or process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment tools that measure both content and language and a flexible approach to teaching.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to assess and differentiate effectively.
