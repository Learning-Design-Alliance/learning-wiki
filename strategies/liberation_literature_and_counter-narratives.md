# Liberation Literature and Counter-Narratives

## Description
Liberation literature involves exposing learners to literature that places out-group identities at the center of the curriculum to counter common negative stereotypes or omissions. This ensures that texts used in the classroom are mirrors for all learners. The strategy involves selecting texts that embody a variety of voices and perspectives, exploring the texts' authors, authenticity, and characters, and filling out a group representation chart for each text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to liberation literature texts, graphic organizers, and manipulatives.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of texts to avoid perpetuating stereotypes or causing harm. Requires a safe and inclusive classroom environment.

### Personalization
- Provide sentence starters for English language learners to help them structure effective and critical questions. Use of graphic organizers and manipulatives aids overall language acquisition and reading comprehension.
