## Description
The article describes integrating high school students into producing the gallery exhibit: students were trained to photograph conference attendees whose portraits joined the show. The authors state this "will provide a real basis for interactions between scientists and students, giving the students a chance to see scientists as people" and hope it inspires "a sense of ownership" motivating visits by friends and family.
