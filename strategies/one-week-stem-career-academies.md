# Run one-week summer STEM career academies combining a project-based learning unit, an industry tour, and a family showcase

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A CAC member described a summer career-exploration model in which students "try out our different pathways" through a week-long project-based learning unit, an industry tour, and a closing showcase for families. The member reported adapting this model for students who are homeless and foster youth so they can experience a college campus, see it as attainable, meet industry people, and practice networking — combining committee members' ideas to spark career exploration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a college campus, industry partners willing to host tours, and family engagement for the end-of-week showcase
#### Constraints
- Described by one committee member as an existing local practice being extended, not an evaluated intervention

### Target Learners
- High school students, including students who are homeless and foster youth

### Target Learning Goals
- STEM career exploration, college attainability beliefs, and networking with industry professionals
