# Use single-subject designs in future studies of word problem solving interventions for students with ASD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researcher recommends that future studies focused on students with ASD use a single subject design, as it would be more sensitive to this population and allow repeated measurement via CBM two or three questions at a time each day. This methodology may better assess change over time and whether a causal relationship exists between the intervention and performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should examine only one or two types of word problems, or problems identical in nature with different stories and numbers
