# Pair college-transition messaging with more specific college-related information and adequate advisor capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the null results, the evaluation offers guidance that messaging strategies "may be more effective" when students receive more specific information. The authors suggest broad advisor caseloads limited college-specific guidance, so messaging supports should ensure advisors can provide detailed, college-specific help rather than generic reminders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advisors with caseloads small enough to provide college-specific guidance
#### Constraints
- Grounded in the authors' interpretation of a null result, not a tested comparison

### Target Learners
- Low-income and first-generation college-going students

### Target Learning Goals
- College enrollment and persistence
