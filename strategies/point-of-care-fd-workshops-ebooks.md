# Deliver convenient point-of-care faculty development workshops with companion eBooks on mobile devices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors designed and delivered workshops with companion eBooks to teach and evaluate the Professional, Health Advocate and Scholar roles at the point of care. Resources are "full of teaching and evaluation resources that can be accessed on mobile devices at the point of care", first presented at the annual FD day on May 14, 2014, with three more workshops (Leader, Communicator, Collaborator) under construction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Needs assessment with stakeholders to identify what faculty need before designing faculty development
#### Constraints
- Evaluation of whether the workshops improve prevalence and effectiveness of faculty evaluating and documenting competencies was still planned, not yet completed

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical faculty members

### Target Learning Goals
- Teaching and evaluating the intrinsic CanMEDS roles (Professional, Health Advocate, Scholar)
