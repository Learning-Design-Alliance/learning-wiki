## Description
The article recommends building strong foundations in prerequisite skills, stating "there must be a strong foundations on students pre-requisite skills, specifically on algebra, analytic geometry and differential calculus". It further recommends College Algebra carry five units like Analytic Geometry and Differential Calculus, and strict implementation of student admission policy, because students' performance in Integral Calculus will remain low without this foundation.
