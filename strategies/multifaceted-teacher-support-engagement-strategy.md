# Combine multifaceted teacher support and constructive feedback with future-oriented dialogue and deliberate class-environment building to promote engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers combine emotional, competence-oriented, and instructional support with deliberate class-environment building and explicit future-oriented dialogue. The authors state that Teachers should strive to offer multifaceted support, encompassing emotional, competence-oriented, and instructional dimensions, to promote student engagement, and that support for low future-orientation students may need to be complemented with explicit future-oriented dialogues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers in ongoing contact with a stable class whose environment they can shape through group activities
#### Constraints
- The article notes that merely providing emotional or instrumental support may be insufficient for students who seldom consider their academic futures

### Target Learners
- Junior high school students, particularly those with low future orientation

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning engagement and adaptive achievement goal adoption (mastery approach and performance approach goals)
