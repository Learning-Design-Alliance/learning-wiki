# Discussing Emotions

## Description
This strategy involves incorporating discussions about emotions into the learning environment through whole-class discussions and individual reflections. It encourages learners to consider, label, and express their emotions to optimize their learning experiences. Apps and ed tech products can support this by including features for tracking and managing emotions, setting goals, and accessing resources for emotional support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom time for discussions and reflection; materials such as emotion charts, puppets, or art supplies
#### Constraints
- Learners may be hesitant to share personal emotions; discussions may be superficial if not facilitated effectively

- Primary school students; adaptable for various age groups and learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Help learners identify, label, and express their emotions; promote emotional awareness; develop coping strategies for managing emotions

### Affordances
- Facilitates emotional literacy, supports self-regulation, and enhances social-emotional development. Learners gain self-awareness and learn to manage emotions constructively.

### Personalization
- Adapting the emotion discussions to individual needs and experiences; allowing learners to express emotions in various ways (verbal, written, artistic)
