# Use practice-with-feedback activities such as Microteaching, Midterm Evaluations, and Classroom Observations as core GTA professional development elements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's assessment data indicate GTAs value practical, interactive activities most: Microteaching, in which GTAs take turns practicing facilitating a problem-solving session with peers and receive feedback, was the top-rated activity in every surveyed year, and Midterm Evaluations and Individual Classroom Observations also ranked highly. Programs preparing GTAs should therefore build in peer practice with feedback from peers and the instructor, plus classroom observation with video used for later discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for GTAs to practice teaching with peers and receive feedback from peers and the instructor
- Classroom observations conducted during the teaching semester
#### Constraints
- Ratings come from GTAs at a single institution; the article notes GTAs have also shown interest in learning more details about research-based teaching methods

### Target Learners
- first-time graduate teaching assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- facilitating problem-solving sessions
- managing classroom dynamics
- improving teaching through feedback
