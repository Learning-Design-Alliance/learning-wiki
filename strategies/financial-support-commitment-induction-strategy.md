# Provide financial support and induction mentoring in exchange for a multi-year teaching commitment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Residencies offer living stipends, tuition remission, and loan forgiveness in exchange for a commitment to teach in the sponsoring district, typically 3-5 years, and provide 1-3 years of early career mentoring after candidates become teacher of record. The report states these incentives attract "high-quality candidates with diverse backgrounds and experiences while providing them intensive preparation".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District and philanthropic or federal funding to sustain stipends and induction coaching
#### Constraints
- Resident contributions for training and master's degrees ranged from $0 to $36,000 across programs reviewed; living stipends are often lower when tuition reimbursements are higher

### Target Learners
- Career changers and recent college graduates entering teaching

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of well-prepared teachers in high-need districts
