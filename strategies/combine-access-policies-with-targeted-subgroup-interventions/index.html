Identify cross-subgroup themes and combine broad access-expanding policies with targeted subgroup-specific interventions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's third recommendation is to look across subgroups for common themes, framing discussion around access and opportunity so districts can address root causes of achievement gaps. It advises that "some strategies may need to be specific to certain subgroups based on their unique needs and challenges" and that leaders adopt evidence-based practices and community understanding that numbers alone may not reveal.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires subgroup data analysis plus deep, nuanced understanding of the communities served

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students across all subgroups in a school or district

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Expanding access to advanced coursework, extracurricular programs, and other learning opportunities

Related Strategies¶

  • Examine data across subgroups and account for diversity within subgroup categories before interpreting score differences
  • Frame subgroup score discussions around subgroup overlap and access to opportunity
  • Use a targeted universalist approach to identify student groups with less positive survey reports and respond with empathy, curiosity, and support
