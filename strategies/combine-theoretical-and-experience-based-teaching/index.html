Combine theoretical background and experience-based teaching strategies to develop relational competence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In its discussion of various learning strategies, the review argues that theoretical knowledge about relational competence helps students see connections and put them into meaningful context, while experience-based strategies anchor that knowledge in practice. The authors state it is "important to combine theoretical background and experience-based teaching strategies to develop relational competence". Reflection is presented as the bridge that helps students develop their own experiences into professional knowledge about therapeutic relationships.

  • Individual active participation in learning interventions promotes the development of relational competence in mental health education of healthcare professionals [+W]
