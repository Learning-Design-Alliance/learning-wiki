# Refine hybrid BOPPPS structures by stressing course interconnections, clearer online instructions, and students-led summary while reducing self-learning burden

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend specific refinements to improve HBOPPPS efficiency while reducing students' burden of self-learning. These include "putting stress on the interconnections between course/lectures by adjusting the contents of micro-video lectures and quizzes at the beginning of lecture", "making the online instructions clearer (assigning 1–2 associated chapter or short essays from a specific text book or online resources to read while viewing the micro-lecture video)", and "strengthening on-site digestion of course contents by fully applying students-led summary in the classroom and avoiding an open end of the online discussion/mind mapping". They also suggest institutions coordinate teaching contents across disciplines through an integrative education strategy to relieve the heavy load.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching materials should consider the lack of focus on keywords and definitions of individual facts, since the improvement of students' ability to answer multi-choice and single answer questions is not significant compared to classical BOPPPS
#### Constraints
- The article notes the learning obstacles revealed in present and previous studies were not fully considered, such as course connections and limitations of self-studying time

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in Physiology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improved teaching efficiency
- reduced self-learning burden
- on-site digestion of course contents
