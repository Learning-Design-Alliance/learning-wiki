# Balance exam-oriented paid resources with support for broad-based clinical knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article cautions that heavy reliance on high-yield exam questions from paid e-learning resources "might limit students’ understanding of broader medical knowledge that is essential for high-quality patient care". It recommends that medical schools and educators "should be cautious not to encourage students to focus exclusively on exam preparation at the expense of developing well-rounded clinical skills and knowledge necessary for patient care", and that "a balance between providing resources for exam success and promoting comprehensive learning is crucial".
