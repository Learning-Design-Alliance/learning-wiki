# Frame-aided writing procedure for authors of historical explanations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete writing recipe in which authors construct a frame map of the to-be-explained content, sorting notes into the slots of a Problem/Solution frame map to reveal the depth, breadth, and detail of available information. Authors then evaluate the adequacy of the frame-mapped notes against the target audience's prior knowledge, write from the map — which the authors found more straightforward than writing from an outline or notecards — and use the map afterward as an editing aid by comparing map and text to check that all necessary content and relationships are included.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors begin by constructing a frame map and sorting notes into frame slot categories before writing
#### Constraints
- The procedure is illustrated only with the Problem/Solution frame

### Target Learners
- authors and publishers of history and social studies textbooks

### Target Learning Goals
- producing coherent, well-structured historical explanations
