# Incorporate flipped learning into the nursing curriculum for patient safety education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that nurse educators incorporate flipped learning into the nursing curriculum and inform educators of its potential for building patient safety competencies. The strategy pairs pre-class online materials with in-class active techniques — "discussions, question‐and‐answer sessions, brainstorming, and case studies" — and the authors suggest the approach may extend to other nursing subjects given high student satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online platform (e.g., Microsoft Moodle) for distributing pre-class materials
- In-class time for active techniques such as case studies and discussion
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is senior nursing students at a single university; self-reported outcomes; findings may not generalize to other populations

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- patient safety competency
- satisfaction with instruction
