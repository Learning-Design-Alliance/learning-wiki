# Teach word-learning strategies — context clues, word parts (morphology), and cognates — so students can independently figure out word meanings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As part of intensive vocabulary instruction, teach word-learning strategies to help students independently figure out the meaning of words. The guide illustrates three strategies with the zoo text: using "Context clues" from surrounding sentences, analyzing "Word parts (morphology)" such as natural versus unnatural environment, and leveraging "Cognates" like option/opción in Spanish, opção in Portuguese, and opsyon in Haitian Creole. It also shows rewriting a complex sentence to model reducing linguistic complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Target words with affixes, multiple meanings, or cross-language potential make the strategies teachable

### Target Learners
- English learners in elementary and middle school, including multilingual students whose home languages offer cognates

- Independent word-meaning inference; academic vocabulary growth
