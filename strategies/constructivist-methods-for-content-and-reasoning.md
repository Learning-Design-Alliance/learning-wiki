# Adopt constructivism-based education methods to develop both content understanding and reasoning ability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because traditional content training alone did not improve general reasoning, the article calls for new methods: "New education methods are needed in order to help students develop both content understanding and reasoning ability. In order to do this, we need to look more seriously into the constructivism based education methods." The strategy is to reform instruction toward the constructivism paradigm so that reasoning development is explicitly fostered rather than expected to follow from content drill.

## Design Implications

- physics content understanding
