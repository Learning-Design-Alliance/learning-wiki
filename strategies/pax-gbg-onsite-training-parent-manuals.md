# Implement PAX Good Behavior Game via one-day onsite teacher training with home-support manuals for parents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's implementation strategy centers on onsite training delivered by experienced therapists, priced at "$2900 for 40 people for 1-day, on-site training for implementing PAX Good Behavior Game®", supplemented by manuals, timers, and auxiliary materials. Parents also receive manuals so they can "use similar methods at home", extending the group contingency approach beyond the classroom. The technology requirement is minimal: any computer with internet access, or even offline use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 1-day onsite training for implementing the program
- Manuals, timers, and auxiliary materials
- Parent manuals for home use
#### Constraints
- Training cost is $2900 for 40 people for one day

### Target Learners
- elementary school students and their teachers and parents

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent behavior management across classroom and home settings
