# Translate HHSAF results into action plans and reassess at least annually

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that facilities use HHSAF results diagnostically and iteratively: "information provided by the use of the HHSAF can easily be translated into action plans to support the implementation and improvement related to specific indicators of hand hygiene promotion". It further states that facilities should implement continuous assessment by utilizing the HHSAF tool systematically, with the current WHO and expert recommendation being use at least annually, enabling facilities to track progress and set new targets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of the HHSAF to generate a score and identify indicators requiring attention
- Use of WHO hand hygiene promotion tools and template action plans matched to the score
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as WHO and expert recommendation rather than a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- healthcare facility IPC teams
- hospital leadership

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained hand hygiene improvement
- continuous quality assessment
