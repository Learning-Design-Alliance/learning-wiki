# Design report topics that require creative approaches and resist ready-made online solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the report activity within the coaching method, the article recommends teacher groundwork: preparing a topic that would allow students to take a creative approach and that would make it difficult to find a ready-made solution online. Example topics on modern artists include information on solo art shows, interviews with the artist, or interviews with their colleagues and contemporaries about the artist.
