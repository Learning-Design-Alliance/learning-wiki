# Future dissemination of START should secure management buy-in, ensure practitioners and supervisors are available, and consider alternative delivery modes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on their evaluation, the authors recommend that "Future dissemination requires management buy-in, availability of practitioners and supervisors and consideration of other ways of delivery". They conclude train-the-trainer workshops are only the beginning, and that "a range of top-down and bottom-up approaches are needed to implement more broadly".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Management buy-in and ring-fenced time for local set-up
- Availability of practitioners and supervisors, including junior colleagues to be trained
#### Constraints
- Train-the-trainer workshops by themselves were found not to ensure implementation into practice

### Target Learners
- clinical psychologists
- admiral nurses
- service managers and commissioners

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained implementation of an evidence-based psychosocial intervention in routine services
