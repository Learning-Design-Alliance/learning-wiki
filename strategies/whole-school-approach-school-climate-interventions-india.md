# Whole-school approach with school-based intervention packages to improve school climate in Indian secondary schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that school-based intervention packages are needed to improve school climate and that "The whole school approach could be a better strategy for intervention." Its recommendations include enhancing teacher-student supportive relationships, strengthening peer group support through extracurricular activities, organizing regular parent-teacher meetings, reducing class sizes in government schools, and training school counselors on the significance of school climate. The only identified Indian intervention program (SEHER) showed that an encouraging school climate significantly reduces symptoms of depression, bullying victimization, and perpetration of violence.
