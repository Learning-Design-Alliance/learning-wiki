# Develop a pilot simulation course combining Reason's and Round's taxonomies to validate the blended framework

## Description
As a forward-looking step, the authors propose developing a pilot simulation course that combines Reason's and Round's taxonomies in practice. They write: "we propose the development of a future pilot simulation course to explore the practicality of combining Reason's and Round's taxonomies." Such a course could generate additional data to test the hypothesis that an optimal taxonomy for simulation-based medical education involves a combination of the two classification systems.
