# Co-develop intervention materials with teachers and administrators to fit classroom routines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The research team worked with teachers, school administrators, and math administrators to design assignments that "would not be disruptive in classrooms, would not require extensive professional development or resources, and would not ask teachers to make large changes in their teaching practices". All decisions about creation and ordering of problems and examples were vetted and improved by practitioners. The authors recommend that future researcher-practitioner collaborations focus on such innovative implementation to increase buy-in when time and monetary resources are limited.
