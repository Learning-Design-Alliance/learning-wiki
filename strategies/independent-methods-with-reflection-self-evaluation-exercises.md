# Combine independent learning methods with reflection and self-evaluation exercises when teaching pedagogical subjects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's formative phase combined several concrete moves: students "learned to identify key words and phrases specific to the subject", learned independent learning methods/techniques and applied them in independent activities, developed applications using subject content, and completed reflection/self-evaluation exercises for strategic learning. Teachers were given methods and techniques for involving students in independent activities plus ready-made reflection/self-evaluation exercises.
