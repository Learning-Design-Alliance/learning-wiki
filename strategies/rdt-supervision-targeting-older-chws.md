# Target post-training supervision and support to older CHWs and emphasize faint-positive interpretation conditions and explaining results to patients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future programmes engaging CHWs to conduct RDTs direct supervision and support toward the groups and steps where errors concentrate. They state: "Future programmes that engage CHWs to conduct RDTs should focus additional supervision and support on older CHWs, should encourage that testing be performed in environments conducive to accurately interpreting faint-positive test results, and emphasize the importance of explaining test results to patients." Safety supervision should emphasize finger cleaning, lancet sterility, and sharps disposal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision structures such as monthly meetings with study or sub-county health staff that supply replenishment and spot checks
#### Constraints
- Monthly supervision meetings in this study did not include practicing or observing RDT procedures, so skill-specific supervision effects could not be distinguished from training

### Target Learners
- Community health workers conducting malaria RDTs in community case management programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintain safe, accurate RDT procedure and correct test interpretation over time
