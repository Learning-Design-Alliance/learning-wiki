# Provide real-time automated feedback on insertion depth and target-point accuracy during simulated needling practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific implementable feedback strategy within VR needling practice: "Based on predefined depth thresholds for the selected acupoint, the interface provides prompts indicating whether the insertion is too shallow, within the appropriate range, or too deep". Performance is evaluated automatically according to predefined rules based on target-point accuracy and insertion depth, combined with haptic feedback and a magnified close-up view of the insertion area.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined depth thresholds for each selected acupoint must be specified in advance.
#### Constraints
- The strategy was implemented within a VR simulation and evaluated only against a conventional-instruction control in a single two-week study.

### Target Learners
- novice acupuncture students
- healthcare trainees learning needle-based procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- needle insertion technique
- acupoint localization accuracy
