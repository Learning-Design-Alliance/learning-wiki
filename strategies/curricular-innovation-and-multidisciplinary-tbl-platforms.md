# Strengthen science communication competency through curricular innovation and multidisciplinary team-based practice platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address weaker outputs from medically homogeneous groups, the authors propose two targeted strategies: "first, curricular Innovation—introducing specialized courses such as medical visualization, narrative medicine, and communication psychology" and "second, practical Integration, establishing multidisciplinary practice platform based on Team-Based Learning (TBL) to foster collaborative problem-solving and cross-domain knowledge integration." They ground this in transdisciplinary education theory, positing that co-creation among diverse professionals elevates health communication quality.
