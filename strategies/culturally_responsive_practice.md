# Culturally Responsive Practice

## Description
Engaging learners cognitively and emotionally by understanding how their identity and perspective are shaped by culture and community experiences. This practice promotes equity and fosters a Sense of Belonging for each learner by understanding different cultural frameworks to identify and push back on implicit bias and dominant narratives that have historically excluded students. It is a learner-centered, whole-child approach that taps into students’ funds of knowledge and builds cognitive and emotional capacity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding different cultural frameworks and the ability to identify and push back on implicit bias and dominant narratives.
#### Constraints
- Focuses on aspects of racial identity, but should encompass many different aspects of a person’s identities.
