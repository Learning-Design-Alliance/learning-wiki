# Stagger training sessions and tailor content to lower academic levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two lessons-learnt recommendations from the implementation. First, phasing: the team ran two one-day trainings so half the trainees attended while the other half offered patient care, avoiding service interruption. Second, content tailoring: "the training content should be tailored to suit the lower academic levels and should include more on background knowledge in addition to the practical skills", given many providers are not formally trained.
