# Use easily adaptable e-learning with additional elective chapters to accommodate international variety in clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To handle differences in ICU practice across countries, the article recommends e-learning as a vehicle whose content is easily updated and extendable. It states that "e-learning modules prove to be efficient teaching tools, as the content is easily adaptable and additional, elective chapters could be added", and that modules can channel ongoing research-education-clinical communication to give students authentic content. Future work should add chapters facilitating complex clinical reasoning and clinical performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A hosting system flexible enough for easy content updates, as with the module's transition to G Suite
#### Constraints
- Local variety also requires local supervision, since some site expectations resist the evidence-based content taught

### Target Learners
- Internationally mobile undergraduate physiotherapy students preparing for ICU rotations worldwide

### Target Learning Goals
- Adaptation to varied local ICU roles, assessment tools and clinical expectations
