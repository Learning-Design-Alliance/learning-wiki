# Representation-specific validation of generated educational content across modalities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that hallucination and error risks be treated as non-uniform across representations: errors in text or visuals are easier to identify and correct, while errors in audio narration or interactive components may be less transparent to learners. It therefore calls for "representation-specific validation strategies, rather than treating all generated outputs uniformly", combining design-time instructor review with systematic AI evaluation for detecting errors across modalities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design-time instructor review as a partial safeguard
- Systematic AI evaluation for detecting errors across modalities
#### Constraints
- The authors state AI evaluation remains crucial even with instructor review in place

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
