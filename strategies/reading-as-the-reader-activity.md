# Use a reading-as-the-reader activity in which writers re-read text from the reader's perspective before revising

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable activity in which young writers take the reader's role — reading their own or similar descriptive text as a reader would — before revising or drafting. The article found that "Both the 9th and the 5th graders showed consistent significant improvement under the 'reading-as-the-reader' condition, both when revising their essays and when drafting anew." It can be applied both to revision of existing essays and to fresh drafting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Descriptive writing tasks with an identifiable reader whose informational needs the text must meet
#### Constraints
- Studied with US students in grades 5 and 9 on descriptive essays

### Target Learners
- Upper elementary students
- High school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Revision of descriptive writing
- Perspective-taking for audience awareness
