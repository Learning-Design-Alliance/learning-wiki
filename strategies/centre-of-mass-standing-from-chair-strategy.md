# Shift centre of mass forward over the feet to stand up from a chair, and feel centre-of-mass shifts for balance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes experiencing centre of mass bodily: shifting it left-to-right while standing, noticing support shifts at the feet when lifting arms, and finding hip position that eases one-leg balance. For chair standing it states 'we need to shift our centre of mass forward until it is over our feet in order to stand', and notes students can use this to help older relatives who have difficulty standing from sitting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attention to support sensations at the feet during postural shifts
#### Constraints
- The chair case requires shifting mass forward over the feet; keeping it over the chair prevents standing

### Target Learners
- high-school physics students
- first-year university physics students
- older adults assisting students' relatives

### Target Learning Goals
- centre of mass and balance
