# Select a sample of schools, including replacement schools when needed, to support generalizable impact studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends that researchers sample schools in a way that represents the target population, and plan for "replacement schools, when needed" when originally selected schools decline to participate. This sampling-and-replacement step is presented as one of the key actions researchers can take so that study findings are more generalizable to the study's target population.
