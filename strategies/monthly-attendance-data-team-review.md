# Use a monthly school attendance team to review data and problem-solve

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ATI-UP requires each school to "designate a team of at least three members (administrator, interventionist, and teacher) who meet monthly to review attendance data and problem-solving." This regular data-review cycle is the operational core of the school-wide framework, connecting monitored attendance data to coordinated school action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least three team members: an administrator, an interventionist, and a teacher
