# Connect Math to Student Strengths

## Description
Help learners understand how math is used in ways that relate to their personal strengths by relating math to student interests. Activating learner expertise in math contexts gives them power as math learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of learner interests and strengths, ability to find real-world math examples that relate to those interests.
#### Constraints
- Requires extra effort to find relevant examples for each learner, may be challenging to implement in large classes.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who struggle to see the relevance of math or lack confidence in their abilities.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase confidence, promote relevance, activate learner expertise

### Affordances
- Increases motivation, promotes relevance, builds confidence, and helps learners see themselves as capable math learners.

### Personalization
- Tailor math examples and applications to the individual learner's interests, hobbies, and strengths.
