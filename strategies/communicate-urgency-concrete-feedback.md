# Communicate the urgency of change using concrete negative feedback rather than big-picture vision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors found that discussion sessions framed in big-picture terms lost stakeholder interest, while reframing the message around concrete poor student feedback — "We are receiving extremely poor feedback on teaching from students in some of the courses in Phase-1" — captured sponsors' and stakeholders' attention and eased implementation. They conclude that dealing with immediate problems precedes presenting vision.
