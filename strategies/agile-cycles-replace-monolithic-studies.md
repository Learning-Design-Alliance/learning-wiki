# Replace monolithic comprehensive studies with smaller, agile research cycles that test one conjecture at a time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy directs researchers to break a single large empirical study into a sequence of smaller, rapid cycles, each testing a smaller conjecture. The report argues monolithic designs slow funding, IRB approval, and recruitment, and that "Conducting the exploratory research as agile cycles may lead to a more focused set of conjectures worth testing at scale." Smaller cycles also build trust with schools before requesting sensitive equity-relevant variables.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clear conjecture or theory worth testing before extensive data are collected
#### Constraints
- The report notes peer reviewers appear to expect a single comprehensive study and may score agile sequences of studies lower

### Target Learners
- K-12 and higher education students using digital learning platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving equity-relevant education research design and its impacts on practice
