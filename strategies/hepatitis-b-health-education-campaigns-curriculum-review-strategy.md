# Health education campaigns and curriculum review to raise hepatitis B awareness among trainee nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that training institutions mount sustained hepatitis B awareness activities and revise what is taught. Specifically, it states that "This then calls for a massive health education and a review of the teaching curriculum among these nurses" and that institutions "can use the World Hepatitis B Day, which is celebrated every year, to embark on campaigns with the aim of creating hepatitis B awareness among the nurses as well." The recommendation responds to observed gaps in transmission and curability knowledge among the surveyed students.
