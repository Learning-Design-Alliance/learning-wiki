# Task Management for Writing

## Description
Task management focuses on helping students plan and manage their time and distractions while working on writing assignments. This includes creating a clear plan of how to get the writing done, agreeing on a time to take a break, and talking about how to manage distractions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing assignment; facilitator; time management tools (optional)
#### Constraints
- May be difficult to implement consistently; requires self-discipline; effectiveness depends on student engagement
