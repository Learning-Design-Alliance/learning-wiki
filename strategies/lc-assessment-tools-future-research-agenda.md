# Staged future-research agenda for transitioning LC assessment tools from developmental validation to safe clinical integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's future research agenda lists five directions: prospective multicentre validation across heterogeneous pathology and surgeon experience levels; transparent, standardised annotation protocols to strengthen ground-truth reliability; evaluating "behavioural changes, learning curves, and patient outcomes, rather than relying solely on technical classification metrics"; shared benchmark datasets for reproducible external validation; and aligning tool design with intended use, distinguishing formative feedback, monitoring, and credentialing functions. For real-time AI deployment, the authors recommend alignment with the IDEAL and DECIDE-AI staged evaluation frameworks, preserving explicit human oversight.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Progress depends on multicentre heterogeneous datasets and evidence of clinical impact rather than further technical refinement alone, per the review's conclusion.
#### Constraints
- The review currently supports no tool for integration into surgical credentialing or performance monitoring systems.

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees
- practising surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- objective technical skill assessment
- safe integration of assessment tools into training and governance
