# Select feedback technology to match the learner population, setting and learning objective, and reinforce with spaced booster training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's concluding recommendation is that real-time feedback is valuable but its effectiveness is tempered by device type, learning group and training context, so technology must be carefully selected for the intended population and objective. It further recommends combining the chosen technology with reinforcement: "a carefully selected technology, in combination with some degree of reinforcement (spaced and booster training), will assist in the retention of skill acquisition." The review also notes monthly refresher sessions and AHA-recommended booster sessions every 1 to 6 months help maintain proficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection must consider the demographic population, educational or clinical setting, and the intended learning objective(s), as the review states
#### Constraints
- The review notes challenges including added mental load, possible distractions from patient care, and equipment cost that institutions should weigh when planning training programs

### Target Learners
- medical and nursing students
- healthcare professionals
- laypersons and secondary school students learning BLS

### Target Learning Goals
- high-quality chest compression depth, rate, recoil and hand positioning
- retention of CPR skills over time
