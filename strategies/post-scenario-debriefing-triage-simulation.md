# Structured post-scenario debriefing after each simulation scenario

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After each scenario, small groups reconvene for a facilitator-guided debrief reviewing the clinical situations, delegation, role experiences, and challenges. "After each 20-minute scenario, the small groups came together for a 20-minute debrief of the activity, and the clinical situations were reviewed." The debrief enables reflective analysis and helps prepare students for the next scenario, supporting both constructivist and experiential learning principles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A lead facilitator to guide the debrief, answer questions about scenarios and roles, and prepare students for the next scenario
- Time allocated equal to the scenario length (20 minutes each)

- 

### Target Learners
- graduate midwifery and WHNP students in simulation-based coursework

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective observation, abstract conceptualization, and integration of clinical triage knowledge
