Use rubric-scored feedback plus self-assessment and satisfaction checks to develop critical-appraisal skills¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete teaching strategy the article employed: instructors provide "essential feedback was provided through assessment scores using rubric scores, facilitating reflections on student work" alongside student self-assessment before and after instruction and a satisfaction questionnaire. The strategy positions instructors as coaches and ties feedback to re-evaluation of new clinical studies.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A rubric with explicit score criteria (Table 1) covering study validity, methodology, results, local applicability, and conclusion quality
  • Time for students to complete RCT evaluations, typically within 7-14 days per the article

Constraints¶

  • The article states that in larger classrooms, instructors would need to consider suitable feedback mechanisms tailored to the class size

  • fifth-year pharmacy students in a new-drugs course
