Teach conflict resolution experientially over an extended period with opportunities to practice skills¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the null results, the article recommends that conflict orientation training sessions "should cover an extended period of time with opportunities to practice the skills", and cites the view that "Positive conflict resolution is a skill learned only through practice and training need to be taught experientially (Drew, 1987)." It further recommends incorporating conflict handling training into the course of study in school settings and studying groups classified by gender, ethnicity, age and life experiences.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training must extend over time and include practice opportunities rather than a single short session

Constraints¶

  • The article notes adults may have acquired conflict management skills through diverse life experiences and might not be flexible to other forms of training

Target Learners¶

  • college students
  • school and college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conflict resolution skills
  • constructive conflict orientation

Claims¶

  • Conflict orientation scores decreased significantly in both trained and untrained groups, so the decrease cannot be ascribed to the training [~W]
  • Conflict resolution skills training produced no statistically significant change in college students' conflict handling styles on any of the five MODE subscales [~W]
  • The one-week, one-time training duration was too short to produce statistically significant change in conflict handling styles [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide opportunities for independent practice with repeated cycles of use and structured reflection to strengthen regulatory skills over time
  • Design RaT programs as longitudinal, integrated curricula with structured instruction, direct practice, formative feedback, and ongoing coaching
  • Design ICT-integration teacher training that is subject-oriented and workshop-based over a long period
  • Build an orientation activity into XR study designs and use multiple sessions to increase tool familiarity
