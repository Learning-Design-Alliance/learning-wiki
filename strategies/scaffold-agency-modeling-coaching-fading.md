# Balance structure and open-endedness by scaffolding agency with modeling, coaching, and fading support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends supporting students to make their own decisions in open-ended coursework rather than simply removing structure, since supporting agency is more than full autonomy. It draws on social-learning literature suggesting "teaching processes such as modeling, coaching, scaffolding, and then slowly fading support", so students transition toward more independent, higher-agency learners. In the studied labs this was enacted by gradually increasing agency across units toward the Project Lab.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have the motivation and self-efficacy to take up the agency afforded
#### Constraints
- Open-ended labs are rarely fully open; the goal is for students to learn to make decisions in line with scientific practice, not simply to make decisions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students transitioning from novice to more independent learners

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific decision making and agency in experimentation
