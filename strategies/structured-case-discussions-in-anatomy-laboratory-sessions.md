# Integrate structured clinical case discussions into laboratory sessions following completion of core anatomical units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental intervention consisted of "standardized clinical case scenarios co-developed by clinical physicians and anatomy faculty", with discussions "systematically implemented following the completion of core anatomical units, sequentially covering the locomotor system, splanchnology, vascular system, special sense organs, and nervous systems." This strategy embeds case-based learning within existing laboratory sessions using clinically co-developed materials sequenced to the anatomy curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between clinical physicians and anatomy faculty to co-develop standardized case scenarios
- Integration into laboratory sessions after core anatomical units are completed
#### Constraints
- The article notes the study design cannot isolate the specific contribution of implementation factors from other concurrent changes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate clinical medicine students in foundational anatomy courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical-anatomy integration and applied synthesis of foundational knowledge
