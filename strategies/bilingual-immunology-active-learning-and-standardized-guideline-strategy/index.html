Integrate bilingual immunology teaching with active learning strategies and establish standardized implementation guidelines¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends pairing bilingual immunology teaching with student-centered active learning approaches and developing a standardized implementation guideline to improve comparability across institutions. It reports that case-based learning, application-based flipped classroom, and patient-oriented problem-solving "are effective instructional strategies for enhancing students’ motivation to learn", and that at its own institution English must be used for more than 70% of instructional time with over 50% of assignments completed in English.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Use of original English-language textbooks and English-language instructional materials
  • English used for more than 70% of total instructional time
  • Students complete over 50% of out-of-class assignments in English
  • Assessments conducted in English with at least 50% of examination content requiring responses in English

Constraints¶

  • Implementation is currently confined to select universities and colleges across varied institutional contexts; challenges may arise from disparities among institutions, variations in instructor proficiency, and differences in student backgrounds

Target Learners¶

  • Chinese undergraduate students in medicine and the biological sciences

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Immunology content mastery
  • Professional English proficiency
  • English application competence
  • Self-directed learning skills

Claims¶

  • Bilingual teaching benefits on course performance may not extend to second-year students who already meet an English proficiency threshold [~W]
  • Evidence for bilingual immunology teaching rests on few, mostly moderate-quality non-randomized studies with substantial heterogeneity [~W]
  • Bilingual immunology teaching significantly increases the percentage of Chinese undergraduates achieving course scores above 80 [+W]
  • Bilingual immunology teaching significantly improves undergraduates' competence in English application [+W]
  • Bilingual immunology teaching significantly improves professional English proficiency among Chinese undergraduates [+W]
