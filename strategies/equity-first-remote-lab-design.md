# Treat equity and student access to technology and materials as a primary constraint in remote and hybrid lab course design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report recommends treating equity as a primary constraint in remote and hybrid lab course design: "we recommend recognizing and addressing students' challenges and access to technology in current and future remote/hybrid course design." It advises surveying students' home technology and materials before assigning hands-on work and holding regular check-ins, since tape and magnets proved an issue for some students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Survey students about the technology and materials they have at home before choosing household-equipment activities, and hold regular check-ins throughout the semester.
#### Constraints
- Because the student survey was administered online, the report notes its access numbers are "likely underestimating the more severe cases of lack of access to technology"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics lab students, including those with limited home access to technology and materials

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable access to hands-on lab learning opportunities
