# Train supervisors to select ICT tools purposefully and avoid 'digital laziness'

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the expert interviews, supervisors should choose "the appropriate ICT tool to interact with remote workers considering objectives, circumstances and employees' tech skills" rather than defaulting to digital channels. Interviewees described "digital laziness" as over-adopting digital tools and holding online meetings even when co-present. This strategy operationalizes the SIMI competency's second behavior and is intended for supervisor training and organizational intervention content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of task objectives, circumstances, and team members' technical skills when choosing interaction media
#### Constraints
- Grounded in qualitative expert interviews from Italian organizations with high digital-work maturity

### Target Learners
- supervisors and line managers of remote and hybrid teams

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and well-being-supportive ICT-mediated communication and work organization
