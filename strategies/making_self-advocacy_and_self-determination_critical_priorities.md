# Making Self-Advocacy and Self-Determination Critical Priorities

## Description
Integrating self-advocacy and self-determination into education systems to empower students with disabilities. Incorporate self-advocacy and self-determination throughout education by including them into teacher preparation, assessments of learning, and measures of school performance such as chronic absenteeism. Provide tangible opportunities for students to practice self-advocacy skills and self-determination in their education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time, resources, training, and intentional actions to foster self-advocacy and self-determination.
#### Constraints
- Require an investment of time, resources, and training; intentional actions; difficult conversations; and most of all, a mindset that all students can make active choices about their learning and lives.
