# Informal Games and Jam Sessions

## Description
Encourage participation in informal games and jam sessions to foster creativity, collaboration, and enjoyment without the pressure of competition. This approach promotes social interaction, teamwork, and self-expression, allowing learners to explore their interests and talents in a relaxed environment. Informal games and jam sessions can enhance motivation and create a sense of community among learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a space where learners can gather and engage in activities. Instructors need to create a relaxed and supportive environment and provide materials and resources as needed.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure inclusivity and prevent cliques from forming. Some learners may be hesitant to participate if they are not confident in their skills.
