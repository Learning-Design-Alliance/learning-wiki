# Introduce simplified game versions to younger learners with rules added gradually alongside the physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For players not yet familiar with particle interactions, the author recommends starting with the game rules and adding the physics background afterwards, and for younger children using simplified versions: "only cards representing particles are used, and rules of card placement are introduced gradually, paralleled with the introduction and learning of specific forces." Antiparticle rules are introduced later, once their properties and differences from particles are understood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator who can sequence the introduction of forces and antiparticle rules with the learners' growing understanding
#### Constraints
- The author suggests this for middle-school children learning about elementary particles and fundamental forces, "with some simplifications"

### Target Learners
- middle-school children
- novices unfamiliar with elementary particle interactions

### Target Learning Goals
- which particles interact through specific interactions
- properties of antiparticles versus particles
