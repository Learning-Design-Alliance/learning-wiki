# Annual concept formation conferences as a vehicle for teacher–researcher exchange on concept teaching methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Since 1971, Usova organized annual conferences in Chelyabinsk every May, whose participants "debated the problems of the established concepts formation, interpretation of new scientif ic concepts necessitated by the development of science". Discussion spanned philosophers, psychologists, educators, physicists and other specialists, and the proceedings "have been the basis for hundreds of master's, and doctoral dissertations" and thousands of methodological materials.
