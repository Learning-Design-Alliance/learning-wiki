## Description
The article's conclusions lay out a research agenda: "future research should aim to tease apart the impact of particular elements of effective life-wide learning interventions through a focus on collecting detailed implementation data, exploring reading skill development in low-resource settings qualitatively, and studying how home and community learning environment interventions differ in effectiveness across different low-resource settings." This reflects the current study's reliance on participation indices rather than element-level implementation data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed implementation data collection, qualitative methods, and multi-setting comparison designs
#### Constraints
- Current evidence cannot separate the impact of particular intervention elements

### Target Learners
- primary school-age children in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- reading skills development inside and outside school
