## Description
The article recommends utilizing this instructional approach — introducing a genAI agent that supplies controversial viewpoints into small-group processes of newly formed student teams — and calls for further research on it. The authors state the findings are "reported and discussed alongside recommendations for further research and utilization of this instructional approach". The strategy targets the groupthink risks of rapid team formation in virtual interprofessional problem-based learning.
