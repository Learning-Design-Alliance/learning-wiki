# Workshop sequence: elicit prior ideas, simple introductory talk, consolidation with tactile material, then questions and conclusions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's workshop recipe begins by recognizing participants: "at the beginning of each session, we recognize the participants", asking them for ideas about the topic to identify points to reinforce. An introductory talk follows "in the simplest and most didactic way", after which participants consolidate what they have learned using the tactile materials, with auditory material in some cases. Small groups enabled close interaction, evaluation by questions, feedback, and drawing conclusions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups that facilitate close interaction with participants
- Suitable tactile materials and adequate training on special education by the teachers
#### Constraints
- The strategy was designed considering that many participants have no previous knowledge of Astronomy, and each workshop's strategy depended on the target audience, infrastructure and institution coordinators

### Target Learners
- people with blindness and low vision
- participants without previous Astronomy knowledge

### Target Learning Goals
- concepts of galaxies, formation, electromagnetic spectrum
- the Moon's formation and surface
- the Solar System, planets, sizes and the Sun
