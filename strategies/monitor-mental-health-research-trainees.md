# Monitor and support mental health of medical undergraduates in research training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because longer weekly training duration was positively correlated with students' stress scores, the article recommends attention to trainee well-being. It states that "Mentors and medical school staff should focus on and regularly check the mental health of these medical undergraduates" and that institutions and mentors should provide support and resources. This is a workload-management and well-being support strategy accompanying research training programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research training program with identified mentors and institutional staff who can check on students' mental health
#### Constraints
- Based on a cross-sectional correlation that may be confounded by examination periods and clinical rotations; causality not established

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates participating in research training

### Target Learning Goals
- research skills development while maintaining student well-being
