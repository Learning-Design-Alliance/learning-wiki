# Hodoscope and Extensive Air Shower coincidence exercises with varying detector separation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two hands-on data-taking exercises use coincidence mode, in which detectors in stacks register a hit when modules trigger within a 1 microsecond time window. In the Extensive Air Shower exercise, students "collect five minutes of cosmic data from two detectors in coincidence mode, starting with a horizontal separation of 1 cm," increasing the separation by 4 cm after each measurement until reaching 30 cm, then repeating vertically. The horizontal exercise illustrates Extensive Air Shower events while the Hodoscope exercise solidifies the notion of particles passing through the detectors.
