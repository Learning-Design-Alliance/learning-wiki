# Restate The Question

## Description
Restating the question encourages critical reading skills and provides clarity around instructions. The culture of “right answer” is filled with shame and shaming, and students will try repeatedly to just give the “correct” answer to the original question. To avoid missed opportunities for developing understanding, instructors must insist students restate the question/problem in their own words.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skilled teachers who can ask effective questions and facilitate meaningful discussions.
#### Constraints
- Requires teachers to be skilled in questioning techniques. May be time-consuming, especially in large classes. This strategy is not useful for students with no prior knowledge of the topic.
