# Funding agencies should require graduate student mentoring plans in all grants that fund graduate students and weigh their evaluation significantly in review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that agencies "Require graduate student mentoring plans in all NSF AST grants (in addition to the already required postdoc mentoring plans)". Plans would have two parts: a boilerplate departmental section describing mentoring practices and a personal mentoring plan from the applicant. It further recommends weighing plan evaluation "significantly in the review process so that high-quality, inclusive mentoring becomes a high priority for grant applicants," with inadequate plans potentially denied funding at the program officer's discretion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Contract with the social science community to develop rubrics for evaluating mentoring plans, since current panels lack that expertise
- Professional development training for program officers in evaluating mentoring plans
#### Constraints
- The department section would not be required, and a poor department plan should not prevent a grant from being funded

### Target Learners
- Astronomy graduate students
- Astronomy postdoctoral researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- High-quality, inclusive mentoring of graduate students and postdocs
