Online college search tools should prominently display both four-year and six-year completion rates, disaggregated by race/ethnicity, gender, and Pell eligibility¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that college data tools show both completion timeframes because most tools emphasize six-year rates and omit the on-time completion information four-year rates provide. It states that "Online college search tools should prominently display both four-year and six-year completion rates" and that disaggregated rates let students judge how colleges serve students like them. Table 1 shows that as of October 2023, several widely used tools report only one timeframe.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to institutional data reporting both four-year and six-year completion rates, such as IPEDS graduation-rate components

Constraints¶

  • The report notes website information was as of October 10, 2023, and that not all tools provide disaggregation by gender, race/ethnicity, or Pell status

Target Learners¶

  • Prospective college students, particularly first-generation and low-income students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Informed college enrollment decisions and timely bachelor's degree completion

Related Strategies¶

  • Use disaggregated attainment data with an aspiration benchmark to identify where students face barriers and hold institutions responsible for change
  • Disaggregate all higher education data by race and income to reveal which student groups have which opportunities and outcomes
