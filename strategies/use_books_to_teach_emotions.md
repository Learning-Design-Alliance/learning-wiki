# Use Books to Teach Emotions

## Description
Stories are used as examples of handling emotions in a responsible way. Serve as safe and inviting ways to start the conversation about the range of feelings we have and how to handle each. By reading and talking through how the characters’ emotions connect with their actions, students can start to talk through how emotions drive behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of appropriate books, time for reading and discussion
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to connect with characters or themes, requiring thoughtful facilitation and adaptation.
