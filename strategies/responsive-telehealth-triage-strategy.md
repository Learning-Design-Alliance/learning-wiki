# Adapt clinic operations to emergent community needs, including telehealth triage during clinic closures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that clinic teams adapt services to community circumstances: "During the COVID-19 pandemic, the dental team led an IPE telehealth strategy for triaging oral health complaints so they could deliver dental care to these patients when all community dental clinics were closed." The clinic likewise responded to the 2021 Afghan evacuation by providing free, culturally sensitive health services when resettlement organizations lacked capacity.
