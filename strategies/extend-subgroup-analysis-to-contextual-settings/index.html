Extend program subgroup analysis from individual characteristics to contextual settings (schools and neighborhoods)¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study proposes moving beyond "what works for whom?" at the individual level to "what works best in which contexts?" by "extending subgroup definitions to the contextual level." It examines how school characteristics (size, resources, safety perceptions) and neighborhood characteristics (SES, racial composition) moderate program impacts, building on ecological theory.
