# Use item parcels (testlets), not items, as observable indicators when evaluating construct validity of CAT data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because CAT missingness is MNAR and item-level factor models fail to converge on CAT data, the article's results support evaluating internal structure with parcels: "by parceling items and using parcels as observable variables, the test internal structure can be recovered." Analysts should aggregate items into sub-content testlets and fit models at the parcel level, while checking for over-fitting since simpler models may fit complex-model data well.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Testlets defined as sums of items for a given sub-content, with fit evaluated against multiple goodness-of-fit indices under both strict and relaxed criteria
#### Constraints
- Parceling over-fits models, so simpler models may spuriously fit data from more complex generating models

### Target Learners
- K-12 students assessed with computerized adaptive tests

### Target Learning Goals
- Establishing internal-structure validity evidence for adaptive achievement test scores
