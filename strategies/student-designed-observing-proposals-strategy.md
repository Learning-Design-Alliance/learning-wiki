# Future research projects for secondary education may include student participation in designing professional observing proposals and leading novel research under postdoctoral supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a forward-looking recommendation, the authors state: "we plan to expand this recognised work (Prieto et al. 2022) with new research projects adapted to secondary education which may include an active participation in the design of professional observing proposals and/or leading novel research under the supervision of postdoctoral researchers". They note the initiative was successfully implemented in other projects, citing Dunn et al. 2018.

## Design Implications
