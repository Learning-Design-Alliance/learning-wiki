Train teachers to create educational games with iPad applications through school workshops and an application-specific teacher manual¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's recommendations section calls for training teachers on using iPad educational applications "by holding workshops in schools", benefiting from the author's teacher manual on TINY TAP, Puzzle Games and Quiz maker, and looking for other iPad applications that enhance motivation and satisfy learner needs and attitudes. This follows the study's conclusion that teachers must keep pace with technology to avoid a frustrating learning environment lacking motivation.

  • Achievement gains from educational games-based iPad applications were retained four weeks after the post-test, with significant delayed-test differences favoring the experimental group [+W]
