# Assess readiness at the end of each practice session and provide tailored remediation before advancing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the deliberate practice sessions, instructors assessed each student's readiness to proceed to the next topic at the end of every session. If any student required additional practice to meet competency standards, "tailored guidance and additional opportunities were provided to ensure all participants were prepared to advance to the next stage of training." Performance was evaluated with a structured checklist assessing accuracy, safety, and procedural steps, with immediate instructor feedback after each practice session.
