# Make general-education lab learning objectives explicit and assessable independent of lecture outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that science departments make labs' specific general-education goals explicit and provide evidence they are achieved, noting that "Without clear objectives, studies like ours will be cited to suggest that labs are ineffective—and that would be a gross overextrapolation of our data." They further suggest considering an experiential science lab for nonmajors, tied to a discipline rather than a specific lecture course, where goals are truly independent and the outcome is experiencing the process of science.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Departments must articulate and document lab goals and align curricula, then assess effectiveness through research
#### Constraints
- Data apply to a single open-enrollment institution's nonmajors course; results may not transfer to majors courses

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nonmajors in general-education science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Science literacy and experiencing the process of science, distinct from lecture content knowledge
