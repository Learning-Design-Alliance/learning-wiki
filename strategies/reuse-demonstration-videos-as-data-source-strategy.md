# Reuse pandemic-produced laboratory demonstration videos as a data source for deeper quantitative analysis in distance-learning physics labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends improving laboratory exercises by capturing data from footage of demonstration videos rather than only watching them: the department "decided at the beginning of the pandemic to prepare video demonstrations of the laboratory exercises", and the authors later found that "some of the videos contained detailed information concerning the measured data", which "gave rise to the idea to improve one of the exercises, using footage from its video". The strategy supports teaching of damped oscillations while students practise frame-by-frame measurement and regression-based parameter estimation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-definition video recordings of the exercise with visible scale and known frame rate
- a frame-stepping program and spreadsheet software for statistical processing
#### Constraints
- Developed and demonstrated in the context of COVID-19 distance learning at the Technical University of Sofia; demonstrated on one exercise only

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in distance or hybrid laboratories

### Target Learning Goals
- improved understanding of damped mechanical oscillations
- attention to physical processes involved in the exercise
- statistical data-processing skills
