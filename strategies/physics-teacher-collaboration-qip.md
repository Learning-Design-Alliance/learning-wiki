# Collaborate across subjects with physics teachers to cover the quantum information teacher-training gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the training of informatics teachers currently does not include quantum information processing, the article recommends interdisciplinary cooperation: "Hier kann es sich lohnen, fächerübergreifend mit Physiklehrern zusammenzuarbeiten, da deren Ausbildung die Grundlagen der Quanteninformation in einigen Fällen abdeckt." This addresses the open question of how teachers acquire the subject knowledge needed for such a unit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to physics teachers whose training covers the foundations of quantum information.
#### Constraints
- Only an interested and further-trained subset of the teaching staff can currently consider teaching the topic.

### Target Learners
- upper-secondary students in informatics or physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- enabling teachers to deliver quantum information content in school
