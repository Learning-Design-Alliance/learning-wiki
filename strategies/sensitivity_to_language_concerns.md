# Sensitivity to Language Concerns

## Description
Celebrating diversity of language and tailoring instructional materials to non-native English speakers are essential for a culturally responsive classroom. This involves providing materials in the student's primary language while encouraging mastery of English. Students who are not native English speakers feel less marginalized when their original language is valued and supported.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bilingual instructional materials, resources for language support, teacher awareness of students' language backgrounds.
#### Constraints
- Availability of resources in multiple languages, potential for logistical challenges in providing individualized support.
