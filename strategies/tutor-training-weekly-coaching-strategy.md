# Provide tutors 20 hours of pre-service training plus ongoing weekly coaching and professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
OnYourMark prepares its tutors with "20 hours of training prior to tutoring start" and sustains quality through "ongoing weekly coaching and professional development throughout the school year". This staffing and development model underpins delivery of the foundational literacy curriculum in virtual sessions.
