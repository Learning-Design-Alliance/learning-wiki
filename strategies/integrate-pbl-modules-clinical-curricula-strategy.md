# Integrate PBL modules into existing clinical nursing curricula to cultivate reflective thinking and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the discussion the authors recommend that clinical nursing education programs incorporate structured PBL modules: "Institutions may consider integrating PBL modules into existing curricula to cultivate reflective thinking and student engagement, especially in resource-constrained environments." They add that "targeted instructor training and the development of PBL case libraries may enhance the scalability and sustainability of this teaching model in diverse institutional settings." The recommendation rests on the study's observed improvements in assessment scores, competencies, and satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targeted instructor training
- Development of PBL case libraries
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center, two-month study; the authors call for multi-center longitudinal research before broader applicability is established

### Target Learning Goals
