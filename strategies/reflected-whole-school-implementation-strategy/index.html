Implement ReflectED as a whole-school programme and allow more than one year for it to become embedded¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Teachers in the trial suggested that ReflectED would work best as a whole-school programme: "This would enable the school to more easily embed the programme in its work; tackle issues with timetabling; and enable children to develop a reflective approach to learning earlier in school." They also indicated delivery becomes easier after the first year, so schools should plan for multi-year implementation and allow time for the approach to become embedded across year groups.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Whole-school adoption across year groups rather than delivery in a single class
  • Time for teachers to become familiar with the programme through a first year of delivery

Constraints¶

  • This recommendation comes from teacher suggestions in the process evaluation, not from a tested whole-school implementation in this trial

Target Learners¶

  • Primary pupils across year groups

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing a reflective approach to learning across the primary curriculum

Claims¶

  • Most teachers felt longer than two terms was needed to embed Dialogic Teaching, so observed effect sizes may underestimate potential impact [+W]
  • Most schools were already teaching metacognitive and reflective skills similar to those encouraged by ReflectED, which may have limited the programme's additional impact [~W]
  • Nearly a quarter of schools failed to deliver the intervention, and successful implementation required senior-leader support, dedicated space, staff time and a timetabled slot [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implementation recommendations: increase pupil motivation, clarify session structure in training, and trial an expanded dialogic reading component separately
