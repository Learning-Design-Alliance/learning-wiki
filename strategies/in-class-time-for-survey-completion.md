# Allocate in-class time for survey completion to raise participation rates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
Because data-privacy rules barred course credit for survey completion, the authors recommend giving students time during class to complete the GE-CLASS surveys. They observed that "courses in which we gave in-class time (indicated with a symbol **) show higher participation rates", making this a low-cost lever for instructors adopting the system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must allocate extra time during class to complete the surveys
#### Constraints
- Course credit cannot be given for completion under the applicable data-privacy rules

### Target Learners
- physics laboratory course instructors at German-speaking institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing students' views of experimental physics
