## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is to structure aide competencies in tiers, with advanced training beyond basic care tasks. It states: "We recommend a tiered system of caregiving competencies, incorporating advanced training to improve aides' understanding of swallowing, chewing, and oral care—crucial for preventing aspiration pneumonia." Meal preparation is framed as part of comprehensive care rather than an isolated task. A pilot implementation with long-term care institutions is planned to evaluate feasibility, effectiveness, and cultural relevance.
