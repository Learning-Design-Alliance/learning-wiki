# Develop dedicated augmented reality libraries for building learning systems in the mobile-oriented learning environment of higher education institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its future-research outlook, the article recommends "Розробка окремих бібліотек доповненої реальності для створення навчальних систем у мобільно орієнтованому середовищі навчання ВНЗ" — developing separate AR libraries for creating learning systems in the mobile-oriented learning environment of higher education institutions. This follows from the article's treatment of AR objects as manipulable markers that let users assemble electrical circuits, model situations, and observe concepts such as current direction, open/closed circuit, and parallel/series connection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AR software able to detect and track markers, and 3D models associated with those markers
#### Constraints
- Stated as a prospect for further research, not an evaluated intervention

### Target Learners
- higher education students in engineering and physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental and research work in physics laboratory courses
