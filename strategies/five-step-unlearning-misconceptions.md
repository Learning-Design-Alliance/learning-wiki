# Use a five-step un-learning plan within a contextual framework to correct astronomy misconceptions (attributed to Bennett)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For remediable misconceptions, the article reports that Bennett advocates "the use of a ﬁve step plan based upon the un-learning of these misconceptions", arguing that by establishing a contextual framework, misconceptions about astronomy can expediently be corrected. The article illustrates the approach with the historical case of an Aristotelian geocentric viewpoint, which observational arguments — retrograde motion, the Galilean satellites, the phases of Venus, sunspots and the supernova of 1604 — would readily challenge and correct.
