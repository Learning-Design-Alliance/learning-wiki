Research agenda: investigate affective dynamics with within-person, longitudinal, and multi-channel methods¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends three methodological advances: complement self-report with behavioral observation, implicit measures, physiological analysis, and neuroimaging; replace largely correlational cross-sectional designs with experimental and controlled longitudinal research; and use within-person designs combined with between-person analysis to capture moment-to-moment affective dynamics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Self-report must be complemented by other assessment channels because it is limited to conscious experience and subject to recall biases

Constraints¶

  • Self-report cannot provide moment-to-moment temporal resolution; between-person designs cannot investigate within-person processes

Target Learners¶

  • students in educational research studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • understanding and fostering curiosity and interest during learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Use multimodal data channels combining subjective and objective measures to overcome single-channel constraints in SSRL research
  • Integrate physiological, dialog, video, and self-report data to analyze collaboration at individual and group levels
  • Study effort regulation in real-world, personally relevant contexts over longer timeframes, including via ecological momentary assessment
  • Adopt an integrative methodological toolkit using machine learning and computational models to capture SR's multi-level, temporal, and social dynamics
  • Assess motivational development with multi-faceted, ongoing, longitudinal measures including scaffolding observation
  • Specify the knowledge-acquisition component when using curiosity and interest terms, and prefer experience-sampling over one-shot self-report
