# Deliver ultrasound-guided aspiration training as a 15-minute orientation video followed by a 20-minute one-on-one workshop and a 5-minute independent skills test

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete training sequence the article used: participants first watched an orientation video, then practiced one-on-one with an instructor at a simulation station, then took a timed independent skills assessment. The article states "participants watched a 15-min training video covering the basics of imaging synthetic cysts and the steps required to access and drain them using a static (in-plane) approach under US-guidance prior to attending a training workshop." During the workshop each participant had 20 minutes of unlimited attempts with three cysts, and instructors rotated stations so they did not test participants they had trained.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized teaching rubric used by all instructors during training sessions
- An adapted, previously validated global ratings scale rubric for scoring the skills test
#### Constraints
- The skills test required full drainage of 15–20 cc within a 5-minute limit, with large needle-angle redirects not permitted to preserve tissue integrity

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- independent performance of ultrasound-guided cyst needle aspiration
- needle-tip visualization and tracking
