# Use dictogloss tasks in listening lessons to raise motivation and integrate all four language skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that English language teachers use dictogloss tasks "to raise learners' motivation in their own learning and that of their classmates, while working in groups". Teachers can replace culturally or age-inappropriate listening content with suitable texts read to the class, and syllabus designers can insert dictogloss tasks into textbooks so learners listen, take notes, speak, write, and read cooperatively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers able to prepare or adapt listening texts matched to learners' needs
- Classroom time for group reconstruction and analysis stages
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on one comparative study with 60 learners at one language school

### Target Learners
- Teenage EFL learners
- Pre-intermediate language school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Listening comprehension
- Motivation for listening tasks
- Integration of listening, speaking, reading, and writing
