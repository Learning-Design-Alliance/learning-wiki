# Nursing managers should reduce workload, improve employment status, and provide quality in-service education to improve nurses' clinical competence

## Description
The article recommends that nursing managers act on organizational levers to raise clinical competence and care quality. It states that "Nursing managers should employ strategies such as reducing nurses' workload, improving their employment status, and providing them with quality in-service education in order to improve their CC and the quality of their services". This is an author recommendation offered alongside the regression findings rather than a tested intervention.
