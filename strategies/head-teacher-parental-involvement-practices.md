# Head teachers should involve parents through meetings, report card attendance, and joint improvement plans

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the third factor identified in the study, head teachers in Nepal's public schools can support students' academic development by strengthening parent-school communication. The three items call on the head teacher to "calls class class-wise parents' meeting and discusses students' progress", require parent presence at report card distribution, and meet parents of poor-performing students to share roles the school and parents can take together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Head teachers must organize structured opportunities for parent-school interaction
#### Constraints
- The strategy reflects Nepal's family-oriented cultural context and may not transfer where parental involvement shows no effect

### Target Learners
- head teachers of public secondary schools in Nepal

### Target Learning Goals
- supporting students' academic progress through parent-school collaboration
