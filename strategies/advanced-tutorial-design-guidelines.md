# Design advanced-physics tutorial activities that focus on concepts, scaffold problem difficulty, and allow generous completion time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article distills its focus-group experience into three general strategies for making activities tractable within a single class period: "Focus on concepts", "Scaffold the problems", and "Don't underestimate completion times". Tasks should progress in difficulty with initial problem statements as explicit as possible, and students should determine qualitative features such as the sign of a quantity or whether it is zero or non-zero rather than perform complicated calculations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must fit within a single 50-minute class period, with adequate space provided for written work
#### Constraints
- The authors observed students seemed to always require at least 10 minutes for every page of activities

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate physics majors in upper-division electrodynamics

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of electrodynamics topics such as Maxwell equations, boundary conditions, and plane waves
