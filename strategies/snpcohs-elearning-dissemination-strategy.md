# Disseminate SNPCOHS as a self-directed e-learning resource for NPs in primary care units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend disseminating and supporting the SNPCOHS program as an e-learning resource, stating it "is well-suited for self-directed learning for nurses in PCUs, supporting effective occupational health service delivery." E-learning is positioned as a flexible training method that avoids interrupting patient care or requiring time away from work, addressing barriers such as limited availability and scheduling conflicts. The recommendation extends to integrating the program into educational curricula in agriculture-reliant regions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An e-learning platform accessible to computer-literate NPs with internet access
#### Constraints
- Current Thai Ministry of Public Health policies do not support occupational health training for primary care nurses, restricting such opportunities to higher healthcare settings

### Target Learners
- Nurse practitioners in primary care units, especially in Southeast Asian agriculture-reliant regions

### Target Learning Goals
- Occupational health service competency for agricultural workers exposed to pesticides
