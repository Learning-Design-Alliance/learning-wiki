# Mindful Moment

## Description
A mindful moment involves pausing, settling the nervous system, and refocusing attention and energy on a new task. It entails inviting learners to close their eyes (if they’re comfortable) and take a few deep breaths, paying attention to the sensation of breathing or noticing their thoughts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a quiet space and brief periods of time (e.g., 1-5 minutes). May use guided audio, videos, or instructor-led exercises.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find it difficult to focus or may be skeptical of the practice. Requires consistent implementation to see benefits.

## Examples
- At the beginning of class, or after a transition, invite kids to close their eyes (if they’re comfortable doing so), and take a few deep breaths. They can pay attention to breathing — what does it feel like when they breathe in and out? Or they can notice their thoughts — is their mind really busy or quiet right now? Use a short video as a guide.
