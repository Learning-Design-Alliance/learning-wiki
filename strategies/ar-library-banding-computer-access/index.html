Provide a well-stocked banded library and easy computer access as the main implementation requirements for Accelerated Reader¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The evaluation identifies the material conditions schools need before implementing AR: a library with a wide book collection banded by the AR readability formula, and readily available internet-connected computers for STAR tests and quizzes. The report states these "are the main requirements for successful implementation". Schools in the trial bought additional books, banded stock by ATOS level, and in two cases purchased tablets for quiz-taking.

Claims¶

  • Accelerated Reader modestly improves reading scores for Year 7 pupils with weak KS2 English results, with an effect size of +0.24 after 22 weeks [+W]
  • Accelerated Reader shows a larger, though less secure, effect for FSM-eligible pupils (+0.38) and does not widen the poverty gap [+W]
