Deliver video podcasts via shared URLs for supervised self-paced learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study used a delivery approach in which video files were uploaded to a website and the web address was shared with students. "The investigator shared the web address to all the experimental group students and supervised the students during the time of students' learning through Video Podcast." Corrective feedback and re-screening were provided when content was not learned.
