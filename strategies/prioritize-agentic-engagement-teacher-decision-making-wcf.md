# Prioritize student agentic engagement and teachers' decision-making in natural classroom WCF research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that future WCF research emphasize students' agentic engagement — described as "students' reciprocal, constructive, and proactive actions to put forward their writing progress" — and study teachers' metacognition and decision-making to uncover why teachers provide feedback as they do and how it affects students' writing quality. It also calls for tripartite commitment from instructors, students and institutions, including developing both teachers' and students' feedback literacy and fostering positive emotional experiences during feedback processing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies conducted in natural classroom settings rather than laboratory-like contexts
- Attention to both teacher and student feedback literacy
#### Constraints
- The article states most existing WCF studies were carried out in a laboratory-like context, limiting research impact

### Target Learners
- L2 writing students
- L2 writing teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving written corrective feedback implementation and student writing development
