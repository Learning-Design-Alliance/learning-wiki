Establish discussion expectations and practice classroom discussion routines at the start of the year¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A strategy drawn from the literature the article cites for making group discussion productive: classroom discussion routines should be practiced from the beginning of the school year, with the teacher setting expectations, giving ownership of each learner's comment, and building an environment in which all are equal participants. The article reports that "classroom discussions must be practiced at the beginning of each school year so students will have a clear understanding of what is expected of them." It invokes this strategy to explain why its own discussion treatment may have failed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The teacher must set the discussion expectations and students must understand they are responsible for their own learning.

Constraints¶

  • The article does not test this strategy; it cites it as a possible reason its 16-session treatment showed no effect.

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate EFL learners in institute classes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • second/foreign language writing ability
  • participation in academic discussion

Claims¶
