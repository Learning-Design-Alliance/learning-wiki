# Train-the-trainer (TTT) programs to standardize endoscopy training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
TTT programs arose after a UK national colonoscopy audit exposed pitfalls of informal, highly subjective teaching under the see one, do one, teach one model. The model, premised on the finding that performing a procedure does not translate into being an effective trainer, promotes standardization by (1) educating the trainer on how to effectively and efficiently teach endoscopy and (2) stressing development of a structured curriculum with pre-established learning objectives. Since implementation in the United Kingdom, TTT programs have been applied in other countries with proven impact on endoscopic training, and the review argues building an effective trainer pool is paramount for disseminating novel endoscopic skills.
