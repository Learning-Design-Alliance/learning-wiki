# Integrate occupational adaptive learning with simulation training, case studies, and interdisciplinary collaboration to strengthen clinical pharmacist preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the discussion, the authors recommend combining pre-training occupational adaptive learning with complementary educational methods: "it is crucial to integrate occupational adaptive learning with other educational methods such as simulation training, case studies, and interdisciplinary collaboration". The strategy positions CPOAL as one component of a broader preparation pathway rather than a stand-alone replacement for standardized training, aimed at deepening role comprehension and clinical soft skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing occupational adaptive learning or pre-training component to integrate with
- Access to simulation facilities, case materials, and interdisciplinary partners
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of the Chinese training system and emerging clinical pharmacy disciplines; the authors also call for larger samples and extended follow-up to validate enduring effects

### Target Learners
- Newly recruited clinical pharmacists in pre-service training

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehension of the clinical pharmacist role
- Clinical soft skills such as communication and clinical reasoning
