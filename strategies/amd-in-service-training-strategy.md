# Expand in-service training for primary care physicians and integrate updated clinical guidelines into primary care to close AMD knowledge gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends expanding in-service training for primary healthcare professionals and integrating updated clinical guidelines into primary care, so that physicians can recognize AMD risk factors, detect early visual complaints, and refer appropriately. It states: "Expanding in-service training and integrating updated clinical guidelines into primary care are recommended for earlier diagnosis and appropriate referral." The strategy targets the specific gaps the survey documented, including treatment accessibility and reimbursement knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targets the documented gaps: disease classification, risk factors, and treatment access and reimbursement within the healthcare system
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in Türkiye, and the authors note results may not be directly applicable to countries with different healthcare systems and socioeconomic conditions

### Target Learners
- General practitioners
- Family medicine residents
- Family medicine specialists

### Target Learning Goals
- AMD recognition and risk-factor identification
- Timely referral to ophthalmology
- Knowledge of treatment options and healthcare-system accessibility
