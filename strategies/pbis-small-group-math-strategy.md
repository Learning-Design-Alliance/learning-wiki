# Implement problem-based learning in small groups of about four students for senior secondary mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that practicing mathematics teachers use problem-based learning instructional strategy in small groups (PBIS) irrespective of students' numerical ability or gender. The study's PBIS condition placed students in small groups of four subjects per group, with the teacher and students identifying problems after which students exercised control of how to carry out the research. This was the most effective condition in the study.
