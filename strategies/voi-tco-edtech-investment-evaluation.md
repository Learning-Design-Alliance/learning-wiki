# Evaluate edtech investments by Value of Investment alongside Total Cost of Ownership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends that districts move beyond cost analysis when justifying edtech purchases. While Total Cost of Ownership provides a clear picture of lifecycle expenses, Value of Investment evaluates the broader impact of an investment—its contribution to student outcomes, operational efficiency, risk reduction, and strategic goals. The guide states that together they "ensure decisions are both financially responsible and mission-aligned," and revisits the framing in Step 5 by asking what usage and performance data suggest about ROI.
