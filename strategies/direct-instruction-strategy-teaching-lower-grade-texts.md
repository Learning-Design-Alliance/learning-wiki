# Teach learning strategies through direct instruction with practice on texts two or three grades below the students' level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article used a direct teaching approach for strategy instruction, arguing it "is effective in the teaching of what the learning strategies are and how they should be used". Implementation directives and plans followed the steps of direct teaching, and students practiced strategies on written materials drawn from ministry-approved Turkish textbooks. Following Lenz (1992), texts were chosen from 4th and 5th grade levels so that examples come from subjects two or three grades below the students' own, to reinforce the feeling of success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation directives and plans prepared according to the steps of direct teaching
- Practice materials prepared in advance from approved textbooks
#### Constraints
- The article applies this to sixth grade Turkish course strategy instruction over 30 hours across 15 weeks

### Target Learners
- sixth grade primary education students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning strategies (repetition, interpretation, organization)
- independent strategy use
