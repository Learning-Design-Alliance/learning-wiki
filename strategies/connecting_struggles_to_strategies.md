# Connecting Struggles to Strategies

## Description
Connecting struggles to strategies involves explicitly teaching students various problem-solving strategies and helping them understand when and why to use each strategy effectively. Educators provide opportunities for students to practice getting 'stuck' on problems and then working through them, while being mindful of their process. This helps students become self-directed learners who can independently apply and adapt their thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to provide challenging problems, check-in with students during problem-solving, and help them name and reflect on their strategies. It also involves creating and maintaining a resource of strategies co-authored with students.
#### Constraints
- Requires a significant time investment and ongoing effort to develop and maintain the strategy resource. Some students may struggle to identify and articulate their problem-solving processes.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Improves self-awareness, enhances problem-solving skills, and promotes self-directed learning. It also helps students see the value in working smarter by trying new and different strategies.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by providing individualized strategy lists and language prompts based on student needs and learning styles.
