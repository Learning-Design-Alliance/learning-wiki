# Brain-Based Learning Through Movement

## Description
Brain-based learning strategies involve engaging multiple senses and encouraging movement to improve learning and retention. After content introduction, students teach each other in partnerships or small groups. This approach activates different parts of the brain, enhances understanding through verbal and visual explanations, and provides necessary breaks for brain refocusing. Tools like GoNoodle can be used to encourage movement in the classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom space, access to movement-based tools (e.g., GoNoodle), and structured activities for peer teaching and processing information
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to align with curriculum, may be challenging in restrictive classroom environments, and some students may be uncomfortable with movement activities
