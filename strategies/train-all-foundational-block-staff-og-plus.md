# Train all staff who deliver the foundational literacy block in IMSE OG+

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The summary recommends that implementation fidelity depends on training everyone who works with students during the foundational literacy block. It states that "all teachers, including trained classroom assistants, who work with students during the foundational literacy block should also be trained in IMSE OG+" and adhere to the scope and sequence, instructional routines, and lesson components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers participate in a 30-hour training and receive all the necessary materials for immediate implementation of the OG+ curriculum.

- 

### Target Learners
- General education, special education, and intervention teachers; trained classroom assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective delivery of structured literacy foundational skills instruction
