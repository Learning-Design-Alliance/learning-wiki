# Adopt a ≤50% AI-generated content boundary as a practical guideline for classifying work as human-authored

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose a threshold policy for assessment integrity in a future of ubiquitous AI assistance: "any work with ≤ 50% AI-generated content be classified as human-authored as a starting practical guideline". They argue this boundary is strictly weaker than requiring majority human-authored content, making it more inclusive of AI-assisted writing while still respecting human authorship. It is offered as a starting point for national standards on permissible AI contribution.

## Design Implications
