# Pre-questioning: teacher and students generate questions and inferences about a passage summary before reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Pre-questioning introduces "a summary that echoes the text content," after which teacher and students ask questions, guess answers, and draw inferences before reading begins. In the study, students received a one-sentence summary, wrote down ideas and questions on paper, and were urged to make inferences built on previous knowledge; questions aim to pique curiosity rather than test comprehension. The activity is intended to activate schemata and motivate purposeful reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefulness in preparing questions in advance of reading, on the part of the teacher
#### Constraints
- In the study, the activated background knowledge of the pre-questioning group was judged possibly insufficient to form a meaningful perception for building upcoming information

### Target Learners
- EFL college students in preparatory-year English classes

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- motivation to read
