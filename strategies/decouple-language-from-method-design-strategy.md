# Decouple instructional language from instructional method at the design stage

## Description
The article recommends that "future studies should incorporate these features and, where feasible, decouple instructional language from instructional method at the design stage." This means designing comparisons so that language track and teaching format vary independently, enabling isolated estimation of instructional effects rather than confounded cohort contrasts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Study designs that disentangle instructional language from instructional method
#### Constraints
- Feasibility may be limited in institutional settings where language tracks are structurally fixed

### Target Learners
- first-year dental medicine students
- students in dual-language curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- isolating instructional method effects
- anatomy learning
