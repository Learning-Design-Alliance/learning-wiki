# Use the SAFT where EBP is emerging and time is limited; reserve the full AFT for advanced groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the SAFT for contexts where EBP is an emerging approach and time is at a premium, because it is quick to complete and score while demonstrating excellent reliability and responsiveness. It states the SAFT "may be more applicable in contexts where there has been limited EBP exposure whereas the AFT is recommended for groups who have progressed beyond the early stages of EBP as it tests more advanced search and critical appraisal skills".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A grading rubric and profession-relevant scenarios; the article notes the instrument may be adapted for other rehabilitation professions by writing new scenarios and revising grading rubrics
#### Constraints
- Assesses only the first two steps of the EBP process, so it cannot measure critical appraisal or application competencies

### Target Learners
- Occupational therapists and other rehabilitation professionals in the early stages of EBP learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Detecting change in EBP knowledge and skills after educational interventions
