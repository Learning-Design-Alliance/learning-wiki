# Use personalized courses to prepare patients for specialist meetings and support treatment learning between visits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two future applications emerged from the evaluation. The article states: "From a physician's perspective, the framework can be used to create a course that will prepare patients for their meeting with specialists by educating them about their condition and helping them formulate questions to ask during the encounter." From the patient's perspective, courses can be tailored toward learning more about the prescribed treatment and its progression. Clinical specialists also suggested patients could access materials before contacting their provider or after visits to reinforce important points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses must be generated from the eLearning ontology for the patient's specific condition and treatment
#### Constraints
- The framework has so far been evaluated only with scenarios and mockups, not with actual patients

### Target Learners
- outpatients preparing for specialist encounters or managing treatment between visits

### Target Learning Goals
- formulating questions for clinical encounters; understanding prescribed treatment and its progression
