# Pair offline skills lessons targeting identified comprehension deficiencies with small-group and whole-group teacher-led instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program assigns students "appropriately leveled offline skills lessons that target identified deficiencies" based on comprehension-skill competency tracked during scaffolded silent reading. Teachers are provided guidelines for organizing small-group and whole-group comprehension skills instruction using these offline assignments, blending computer-based practice with direct instruction.

### Context

- Ongoing tracking of competency with 25 comprehension skills during scaffolded silent reading activities to identify deficiencies
