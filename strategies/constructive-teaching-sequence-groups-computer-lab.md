# Constructive teaching sequence: problem-solving in small groups with computer-laboratory support and guided discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's constructive method presents new knowledge as a problem to be solved with existing knowledge. Students work in small collaborating groups, with the instructor guiding inquiries with hints; groups then announce solutions, a guided discussion analyses failures and compares solutions, and the instructor forms the final conclusions. A great part of the process took place in a computer laboratory where students used mathematical software designed by experts, based on the APOS/ACE finding that computer tasks can build the mental constructions needed for learning.
