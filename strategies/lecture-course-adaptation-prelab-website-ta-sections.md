# Lecture-course adaptation using a pre-lab website and TA-supported discussion sections

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
For U.C. Santa Cruz's large (250 student) lecture course, the activity was adapted into a 70-minute required discussion section of about 40 students with two TAs. A website introduced the transit detection method with demonstration videos and assigned a pre-lab group question-generation task due at least 24 hours before section, so section time was used solely for investigation plus a brief sharing/synthesis segment. Formal presentations were cut and met later in the course through a different activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching Assistants to support students during discussion sections
- A website with demonstration videos and a pre-lab question-generation assignment submitted at least 24 hours before section
#### Constraints
- Formal student presentations were cut from this implementation

### Target Learners
- large-enrollment introductory astronomy students, non-majors

### Target Learning Goals
- practice with scientific process skills
- generating investigable questions
