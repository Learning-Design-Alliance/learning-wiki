# Use nominal group technique in expert workshops to reach consensus on training principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To develop the training content, discipline-specific and interdisciplinary expert workshops use the nominal group technique to generate consensus on the principles of injury prevention training and examples of context-specific exercises. The technique proceeds through introduction and explanation, silent individual generation of ideas, sharing ideas without debate, group discussion, and consensus, producing a first draft of the model at the interdisciplinary workshop.
