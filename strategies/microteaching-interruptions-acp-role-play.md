# Organise SP role-play as microteaching with frequent coach-initiated interruptions instead of a long simulation with wrap-up feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Microteaching, equated by the authors with Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice, organises the SP role-play around frequent coach-initiated interruptions rather than a longer simulation followed by a wrap-up discussion. During an interruption the SP preserves the emotions of the previous minutes so the simulation 'can be resumed seamlessly at a point determined by the coach and participants'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The ACP coach must recognise irritation caused by interruptions early and apply the microteaching techniques judiciously
#### Constraints
- In the authors' experience, participants can occasionally become so irritated by the repeated interruptions that they are no longer able to continue the role-play

### Target Learners
- Participants in the ACP facilitator qualification practising conversations in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Trying out alternative behaviour for specific situative details of ACP conversation conduct
