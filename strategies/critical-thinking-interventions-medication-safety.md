## Description
The article recommends that continuing education on medication safety emphasize critical thinking training, especially for nurses with lower competence. It states "Continuing education on medication safety should pay greater attention to improving critical thinking skills in nurses, especially those with low or moderate medication safety competencies." Critical thinking supports analyzing complex situations and safe medication decision-making.
