# Seven-step genre-based writing lesson sequence: brainstorm, free compose, compare with authentic genre models, and revise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's experimental instruction followed seven steps: teacher-suggested brainstorming on a topic, free composition, distribution of authentic texts differing in formality, structure and context, discussion of differences, comparison of learners' compositions with authentic versions to notice gaps, homework rewriting based on the models, and teacher-led encoding and discussion of the writings. As printed, "Brainstorming: teacher suggested a topic (for example, marriage and its challenges), the participants —each one — said something regarding the topic which the teacher wrote on the board." The sequence aims to consciously raise learners' genre awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to several authentic forms of writing that differ in terms of formality, structure and context
- Teacher encoding and discussion of learner texts to raise genre awareness
#### Constraints
- Implemented over sixteen hours with Iranian BA EFL learners; the article does not claim effectiveness for other populations

### Target Learners
- Iranian BA EFL university learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved writing performance through genre awareness
