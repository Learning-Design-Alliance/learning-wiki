# Use Quotations to Cite Textual Evidence

## Description
Using quotations to cite textual evidence involves teaching students to select and incorporate direct quotes from texts to support their claims. This strategy provides students with clear guidelines on how to identify and use evidence, enhancing their ability to construct well-supported arguments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to texts, explicit instruction on how to select and integrate quotations, and opportunities for practice and feedback.
#### Constraints
- May lead to overuse of quotations if not balanced with student commentary. Requires careful instruction to avoid formulaic writing.

- Can be adapted for different content areas and writing tasks. Scaffolds can be adjusted based on student needs, with more support provided initially and gradually reduced as students gain proficiency.
