# Educate students on the technical and ethical challenges of generative AI and the critical thinking needed to engage with such tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that, assuming students will use generative AI, "students should not only be educated on the technical and ethical challenges of generative AI systems, but also on the critical thinking required to successfully engage with such tools." Suggested measures include establishing committees for ethical oversight and adding classroom discussions on AI tool use, clarifying plagiarism and the boundary between legitimate assistance and misconduct.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom discussions and oversight structures to clarify what constitutes plagiarism and academic integrity boundaries
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a longer-term response alongside assessment redesign, not a substitute for it

### Target Learners
- university students using or likely to use generative AI tools

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking about AI tools
- academic integrity
- ethical use of generative AI
