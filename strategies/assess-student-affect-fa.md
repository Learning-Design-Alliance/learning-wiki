# Include assessment of student affect through planned check-ins

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teachers in the meta-aggregation studies planned frequent check-ins about students' experiences, beliefs, confidence, and attitudes, going beyond gathering technical information about mathematical thinking. Bonham (2018) teachers used exit tickets or thumbs-up/down questions to gather information about students' disposition toward mathematics, often planned early in a lesson when new material was presented.
