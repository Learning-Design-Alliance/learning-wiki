# Tailor case questions and session depth to learner seniority by emphasizing or skipping question subsets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The module's thirty questions are organized so facilitators can adapt the session across training levels: questions 1-7 focus on evaluating fever and altered mental status in infants, questions 8-25 on treatment, and questions 26-30 on complications and prognosis. The article advises that "Senior learners familiar with initial assessments may focus on questions 8-18 and 24 to review key management points and delve deeper into areas of controversy and emerging evidence." Session length and discussion depth can likewise be tailored.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions grouped by topic and difficulty within the case
- Facilitator guidance on adaptation across levels
#### Constraints
- Adaptation guidance was not formally evaluated in all delivery formats

### Target Learners
- pediatric residents across training levels

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluation of fever and altered mental status
- management of bacterial meningitis
- complications and prognosis
