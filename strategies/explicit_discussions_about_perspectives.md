# Explicit Discussions About Perspectives

## Description
Have explicit conversations about emotions and social issues, including issues of social justice. This helps learners understand diverse viewpoints and develop empathy. Using current events and fictional characters can support learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires discussion prompts and facilitator guidance. Learners need a supportive environment to explore and discuss social issues.
#### Constraints
- May require careful facilitation to avoid triggering negative emotions. Requires a safe and inclusive environment.
