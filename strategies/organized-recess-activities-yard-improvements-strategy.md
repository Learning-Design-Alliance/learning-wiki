# Guide students in organized activities during recess and improve the recess play yard to raise physical activity, especially for girls

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends school-based recess programs that structure student activity and improve the play yard as a route to increasing children's physical activity. It states that programs that "guide students in organized activities during recess and make improvements to the recess play yard might lead to significant increases in physical activity—especially for girls." The recommendation is grounded in the randomized Playworks evaluation, whose benefits appeared for girls but not for boys on accelerometer measures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A school-based program that guides students in organized activities during recess and makes improvements to the recess play yard
#### Constraints
- In the supporting trial, significant accelerometer-based increases were found for girls but not for boys

### Target Learners
- elementary school students at recess

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing physical activity during recess
