Teacher strategies for alleviating EFL speaking anxiety: warm atmosphere, encouragement, L1 use, and learner-centered activities¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends a set of implementable classroom strategies for reducing students' anxiety in speaking English inside or outside the classroom. Teachers should "create a warm atmosphere in classrooms," respect students, give them enough time to express themselves, build self-confidence through encouragement, provide sufficient input, and "make use of students' L1 in order to decrease their anxiety" by letting students speak in their native language when they cannot recall a target-language word. Reducing classroom noise and raising awareness of foreign cultures are also recommended.
