# Give pre-exam academic integrity reminders regardless of exam format

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before exams, instructors should present academic integrity reminders covering the university's integrity policies, the negative consequences of cheating, and a real case of academic dishonesty and its punishment. The mini-meta-analysis found reminders significantly reduced cheating rates with very strong Bayes evidence (BF10 = 37.94), and the effect did not differ between unproctored and proctored formats. The reminders were printed on the exam booklet cover page in Studies 3 and 4, modeled on content from prior studies.

## Design Implications
