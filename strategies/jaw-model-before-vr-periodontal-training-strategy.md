# Apply the jaw model prior to virtual reality in preclinical periodontal training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends sequencing hands-on jaw-model practice before virtual simulation in periodontal preclinical training. It argues that jaw training builds cleaning feeling and force control, after which students can "purposefully and selectively master skills in virtual stimulation systems". The J-V sequence produced the best supragingival scaling effect in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both a physical jaw model (NISSIN) and a virtual reality training system (UniDental), with about 4 hours of training on each within an 8-hour operation-training block
#### Constraints
- VR "should not be used as an alternative to traditional methods"; long-term effects and the optimal application period were not established in the 14-hour study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate stomatology students in preclinical periodontology

### Target Learning Goals
- supragingival scaling technique
- comprehensive periodontal examination
- theory-to-practice integration in periodontal procedures
