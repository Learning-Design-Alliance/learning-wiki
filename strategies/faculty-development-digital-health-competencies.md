# Structured faculty development to prepare educators for digital health competency teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends targeted faculty and preceptor development so educators can teach and assess digital health competencies. It specifies that "Examples include structured faculty development programs focused on telehealth workflows and virtual care delivery, foundational AI literacy workshops addressing model limitations and ethical risks, and hands-on training with sensor data and remote patient monitoring platforms," supplemented by partnerships with health systems, simulation centers, and digital health vendors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured programs, workshops, and hands-on training platforms must be available to faculty
- Partnerships with health systems, simulation centers, and vendors support integration
#### Constraints
- The review notes access to specialty education in nursing informatics and AI expertise is currently limited for many baccalaureate programs

### Target Learners
- nursing faculty
- preceptors

### Target Learning Goals
- telehealth workflow instruction
- AI literacy teaching
- sensor and remote monitoring training
