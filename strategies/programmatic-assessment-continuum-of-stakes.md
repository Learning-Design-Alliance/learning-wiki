# Embed formative assessment in a programmatic assessment continuum of stakes rather than a formative/summative divide

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
As an implication for practice, the article recommends the programmatic assessment approach, in which "assessments are no longer divided in formative and summative, but rather represent a continuum of stakes (from low to high)". It cites Heeneman et al.'s positive results on feedback use when a formative PT was embedded in such a system with reflection on the PT and mentor guidance built into the curriculum.
