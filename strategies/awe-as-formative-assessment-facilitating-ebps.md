# Position AWE as a formative assessment tool that facilitates evidence-based practices in writing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 3 against) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames AWE as an "AI-enhanced formative assessment tool" intended "for use as a formative assessment tool" that facilitates multiple evidence-based practices in writing instruction. Its claimed affordances include saving teachers grading time, providing a supportive writing environment, enabling timely, frequent and personalized feedback loops, aiding higher-level feedback, and encouraging revision. Statewide deployment as a state-contracted tool is the article's exemplar of this strategy at scale.

## Design Implications

### Target Learning Goals
