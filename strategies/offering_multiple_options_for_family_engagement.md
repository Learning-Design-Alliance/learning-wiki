# Offering Multiple Options for Family Engagement

## Description
Providing a variety of options will improve overall participation, particularly for families with significant barriers to engagement. This includes school-based involvement options, home visits, materials to guide families in supporting children’s learning at home, and technology. Connect family members for peer-to-peer support and partner with local organizations to offer family members ways to learn skills needed to become leaders in schools and the local community.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple engagement options, resources for home visits, technology, and partnerships with local organizations.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and coordination to manage multiple engagement options. Success depends on family participation.
