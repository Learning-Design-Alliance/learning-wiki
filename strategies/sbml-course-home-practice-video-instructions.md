# Simulation-based mastery learning course with at-home simulator practice supported by written and video instructions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article implemented the simulator within a simulation-based mastery learning (SBML) course for first-year surgery residents, who took simulators home for deliberate practice. Residents "were given written and video instructions of the procedure via an online platform before and during the course", including one instrument-review video, one full-length procedure video on a real patient, one full-length video on the simulator, and nine short step-by-step videos on the simulator. Residents completed the feedback survey after successfully passing the course post-test.
