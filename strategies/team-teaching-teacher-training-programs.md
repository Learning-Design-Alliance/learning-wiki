# Adopt teacher training programs on team teaching English language skills in secondary schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's own recommendation, drawn from its results: "the researcher s recommended to adopt good teacher training programs on team teaching English language skills for all secondary schools in Jordan." The training should prepare teachers to co-plan, co-instruct and co-assess reading lessons, since the study's team teachers were trained on how to implement team teaching before the treatment. The recommendation extends a single-school quasi-experiment to all Jordanian secondary schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in how to implement team teaching before deployment
- Joint advance planning and division of work between the two teachers
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one purposefully chosen school with two eleventh-grade sections (48 students)

### Target Learners
- EFL students in Jordanian secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- English language reading comprehension skills
