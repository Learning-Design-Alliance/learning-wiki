# Designate a senior district leader to oversee turnaround efforts and advocate urgently for low-performing schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the practice of prioritizing improvement and communicating its urgency, the guide recommends that districts "Designate a senior district leader to oversee turnaround efforts" and "Advocate relentlessly for the success of low-performing schools by sharing the urgency and importance of turnaround efforts with all stakeholders, including families, staff, and community members." Supporting actions include implementing systems for collecting, analyzing, and sharing school performance data and using data to guide resource allocation and celebrate incremental successes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A senior district leader with accountability for supporting turnaround efforts across all schools
