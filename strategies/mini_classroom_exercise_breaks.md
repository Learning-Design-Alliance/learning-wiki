# Mini Classroom Exercise Breaks

## Description
Implement short exercise sessions throughout the school day, such as encouraging jumping jacks in the morning or scheduling five-minute movement breaks between classes where students can do activities like push-ups, dancing, or running in place. These breaks revitalize students and provide a chance to move, improving focus and attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduled breaks, teacher implementation, classroom space
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent scheduling and teacher involvement. May disrupt class flow if not managed effectively.
