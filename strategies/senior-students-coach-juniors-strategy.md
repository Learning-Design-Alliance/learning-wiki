# Senior students coach junior students and take on school responsibilities to free teacher time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes senior students helping teach junior students as "the summit of productive learning and teaching methodology," requiring expertise in coaching familiar from scouting, and calls the help of students in educating students "probably the greatest, most promising and least effectively used resource of meaningful education." Students can also help run schools and free teachers from mundane functions, freeing resources and giving teachers more time for advanced learning and improving teaching.
