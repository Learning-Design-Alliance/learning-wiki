# Use autograded graph problems with None-of-these options and student-generated graphs, grading for consistency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends presenting blend-building graph problems as multiple choice or matching questions that are easily autograded, deliberately including items where the correct choice is "None of these graphs work" so students must reason through the graph-physics connection rather than focus on the answer. It also recommends having students generate their own graphs in hand-graded homework or in-class group work, and grading multi-graph consistency problems with partial credit when reasoning is correct from a wrong starting graph.
