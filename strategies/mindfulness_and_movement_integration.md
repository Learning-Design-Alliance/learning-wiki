# Mindfulness and Movement Integration

## Description
Integrating mindfulness with movement involves combining physical activity with practices that promote awareness, focus, and presence. This approach aims to enhance both physical and mental well-being by engaging mind and body simultaneously. It includes activities such as mindful walking, yoga, and Tai Chi.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Minimal equipment needed for most activities. Requires awareness and intention to integrate mindfulness practices with physical movement.
#### Constraints
- May require initial training in mindfulness techniques. Some individuals may need to adapt activities to accommodate physical limitations.
