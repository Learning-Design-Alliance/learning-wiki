# Assign Monte Carlo computer simulations to confirm analytical solutions empirically

## Description
The article suggests that computer simulations using Monte Carlo methods might be assigned alongside the elaborate analytical examples in order to confirm the solutions empirically. This provides a computational complement to the analytical derivations of probability distributions, expected values, variances and relative standard deviations in the stochastic physics exercises.
