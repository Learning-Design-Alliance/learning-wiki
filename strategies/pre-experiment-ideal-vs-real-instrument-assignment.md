# Assign a pre-experiment spreadsheet calculation comparing instrument readouts under ideal versus real internal impedance.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the laboratory exercise, students receive a calculation task on a circuit similar to the experimental one. They "calculate the deviation in measurement devices re adouts between two cases; when the instruments were ideal (no internal impedance) and when they were real". The assignment can be extended across a range of frequencies using a spreadsheet program, connecting to a separate Personal Computer Applications course.
