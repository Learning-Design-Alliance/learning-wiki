# Develop more realistic, clinically relevant VR software and naked eye 3D technology to improve simulated teaching efficiency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that anesthesia simulation programs invest in VR software development and display technology. It states "more realistic and clinically relevant software and naked eye 3D technology can be developed to further improve the efficiency of simulated teaching". This follows its conclusion that hardware and software development is an important condition for VR adoption, and that current VR crisis-management simulation should increase real effectiveness and integrate with traditional medical training methods.
