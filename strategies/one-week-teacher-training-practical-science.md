# Train research assistants/teachers with lesson packages and experimental guides before practical instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To control teacher quality, the study trained the science teachers as research assistants "for one week with lesson packages and experimental guide", followed by mock teaching sessions to demonstrate understanding of the procedure before treatment began. This preparation is offered as a way of ensuring competence to teach and carry out practical activities on solubility.
