# Conduct randomised and cost-effectiveness trials comparing peer simulation, SP-based SBE and informal role play

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking research recommendation is a formal trial with economic evaluation. They state that "a formal trial appears to be warranted that includes comparisons between the cost-effectiveness of peer simulation, SBE with SPs, and informal role play", and that a randomised controlled trial comparing different peer simulation training activities would identify the design features supporting portrayal skill development. Cost-effectiveness studies considering both attainment and costs would inform integration guidelines.
