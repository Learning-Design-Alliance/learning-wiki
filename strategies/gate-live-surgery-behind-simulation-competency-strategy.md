# Sequence supervised live surgical training only after adequate deliberate practice with feedback, reflective learning, and competency-benchmarked assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that trainee eye surgeons participate in focused, intense simulation training courses before operating on patients. "We believe that supervised live surgical training on patients should begin only after engaging in adequate deliberate practice with feedback, reflective learning, and a competency outcome assessment benchmarked to appropriate standards." The OLIMPICS trial's 5-day course modeled this sequence, with competency assessed against the Sim-OSSCAR before live surgery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated competency assessment rubric benchmarked to appropriate standards
- Opportunity for sustained deliberate practice with feedback and reflective learning before live surgery
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a belief about training sequencing; the trial tested the simulation course's effect, not the gate itself

### Target Learners
- Trainee ophthalmologists
- Novice surgical trainees with zero prior procedures as primary surgeon

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe acquisition of cataract surgical competence
- Reduced surgical complications on patients
