# Prioritize construct-to-data mappings, multimodal validation, ethical implementation, and cross-disciplinary collaboration for domain-specific adaptive systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the issue's discussion paper, the editorial sets priorities for future research on domain-specific adaptive support: "stronger construct-to-data mappings, rigorous validation of multimodal indicators, transparent and ethical implementations, and closer collaboration between domain experts, learning scientists, and data scientists". The stated aim is adaptive systems that are both technically sophisticated and instructionally meaningful, coupling advanced AI with domain-specific models of learning and instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- stronger construct-to-data mappings
- rigorous validation of multimodal indicators
- transparent and ethical implementations
- closer collaboration between domain experts, learning scientists, and data scientists
#### Constraints
- General-purpose AI systems are not inherently aligned with the conceptual and pedagogical structures of specific domains and individual differences between learners in these domains.

### Target Learners
- learners in domain-specific adaptive learning environments across subject domains

### Target Learning Goals
- enhancing learning outcomes through domain-specific adaptive support
