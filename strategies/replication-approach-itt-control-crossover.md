# A replication approach for testing nonexperimental methods against experimental ITT impact estimates when experiments include substantial control crossover

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper develops a methodological strategy for evaluating nonexperimental methods by comparing their estimates to results from rigorous randomized experiments. As the abstract states, "This working paper develops a new replication approach that facilitates testing nonexperimental methods against rigorous experimental ITT impact estimates when the experiment includes substantial control crossover." The approach is designed for evaluation contexts where control-group members receive the treatment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An available rigorous randomized experiment whose ITT impact estimate can serve as the benchmark for replication
#### Constraints
- Designed for the context in which the experiment includes substantial control crossover

### Target Learners
- education researchers and evaluation methodologists

### Target Learning Goals
- improving the validity of causal impact estimates in education research
