# Adopt a hybrid teaching strategy prioritizing face-to-face instruction for practical skills and remote education for theoretical content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends combining traditional teaching with computer-assisted learning rather than replacing instructor-led training. It states that "educators are encouraged to adopt a hybrid teaching strategy: face-to-face instruction should be prioritized for the development of practical skills, while theoretical content can be delivered through remote education." This leverages traditional teaching strengths while taking advantage of the convenience and flexibility of digital education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Balancing the proportion of online digital education with hands-on practical training
#### Constraints
- Studies cited report lower student progress and course completion rates in fully online dental courses

### Target Learners
- dental and orthodontic students

### Target Learning Goals
- practical clinical skills and theoretical knowledge in dental education
