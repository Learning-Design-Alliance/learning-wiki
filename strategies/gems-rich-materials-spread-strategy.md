# Emphasize rich materials and varied scientific activities, and spread GEMS-style education in preschool settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend spreading GEMS education nationally and internationally and foregrounding its main elements: "the use of rich materials and different scientific activities". They further recommend teacher awareness activities and offering teachers and families courses, workshops, and seminars introducing the GEMS program, and developing activities grounded on the GEMS approach for different subjects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in the program's approaches and materials
- Access to rich and varied activity materials
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on one semi-experimental study with 25 children in two private schools; the science process skills result showed no between-group difference

### Target Learners
- preschool and kindergarten children

### Target Learning Goals
- problem solving skills
- science process skills

## Claims

- [GEMS education produced higher post-test overall problem solving scores in science education than the control program (d = 1.41)](../claims/gems-improves-science-problem-solving-posttest.md) [+M]
- [GEMS education produced a large post-test advantage on the Material Usage Problems subscale (d = 1.77)](../claims/gems-material-usage-problems-advantage.md) [+M]
- [No significant post-test difference between groups on science process skills despite significant within-group gains in both groups](../claims/gems-no-between-group-science-process-difference.md) [~W]
- [No significant post-test difference between groups on the Science and Nature Problems subscale](../claims/gems-no-difference-science-nature-problems.md) [~W]
- [Parents reported that after GEMS education children's interest in science and mathematics increased and they asked more questions](../claims/gems-parents-report-increased-science-interest.md) [+W]
