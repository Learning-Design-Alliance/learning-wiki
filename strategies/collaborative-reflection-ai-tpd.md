# Structure AI-supported professional development around collaborative reflection rather than technical training alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that AI should not serve as a shortcut for training but rather as a supportive tool embedded within reflective and collaborative learning processes. Teachers engaged in structured reflection with trainers and peers, critically evaluating AI-generated resources and discussing pedagogical implications through weekly hybrid meetings that were not only technical but deeply reflective.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly collaborative meeting structures with time for reflection and peer discussion
#### Constraints
- Findings drawn from one rural school context with eight teachers

### Target Learners
- Rural language teachers participating in professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated teaching resources
