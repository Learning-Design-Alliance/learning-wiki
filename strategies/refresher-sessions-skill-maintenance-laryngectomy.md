# Schedule refresher sessions and repeated practice opportunities to maintain urgent laryngectomy skills over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future programmes address skill decay by building in ongoing practice. It states that retention of airway management skills has been shown to be optimised through regular practice and feedback, and that opportunities for further learning and training exposure are key factors for SLTs in maintaining confidence in voice prosthesis management; it concludes that future programmes should therefore consider refresher sessions for skill maintenance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing opportunities for practice and exposure beyond the initial training day
#### Constraints
- The article notes further research is needed to assess whether competence is maintained over time and whether Human Factors learning generalises to clinical practice

### Target Learners
- Multidisciplinary healthcare providers, particularly clinicians with reduced clinical exposure to laryngectomy

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintenance of urgent laryngectomy care competence, including airway management and voice prosthesis skills
