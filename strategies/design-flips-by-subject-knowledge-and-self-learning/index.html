Design flipped classroom instruction according to subject type, knowledge type, and students' independent learning ability¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's Implications section recommends designing flipped classroom teaching scientifically according to students' characteristics at different stages, subjects, and knowledge points. For theoretical knowledge it advises designing "more background knowledge of advanced organizers before class" and internalizing critical points in class; for practical knowledge, arranging operational knowledge before class and focusing class time on deeper skills. It also recommends cultivating students' independent learning ability and developing subject-specific flipping models.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must provide appropriate assistance to monitor and understand students' pre-class knowledge learning to provide more targeted classroom guidance
  • Teachers must build a relatively complete learning support system and construct a virtual learning environment suitable for independent learning

Constraints¶

  • The article states flipped classroom is not suitable for reasoning, conceptual and systematic courses, such as language, history, and other liberal arts courses, without adaptations like effective grouping, role assignment, and process evaluation

Target Learners¶

  • college students in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved academic performance across subject and knowledge types
  • independent learning and self-regulation skills

Affordances¶

  • Flipped Classroom Blended Model Theory

Claims¶

  • Flipped classroom effects differ by subject type, favoring science (SMD = 0.75) and liberal arts (SMD = 0.72) over engineering (SMD = 0.34) [+W]
  • Flipped classroom effects do not differ significantly between theoretical and practical knowledge, though practical knowledge shows a slightly larger effect [~W]
  • Study type, educational level, and year do not significantly moderate the effect of flipped learning on success [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt flipped classroom in preventive medicine courses according to institutional resources, with teacher information-technology training
  • Re-arrange and extend the web-assisted flipped-classroom activity materials to other science subjects and disciplines
