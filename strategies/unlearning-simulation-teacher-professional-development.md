# Use unlearning-based simulated students as scalable practice environments for teacher professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's implications section proposes that because the unlearning approach can generate simulated students at different knowledge levels, it can populate training environments where teachers or tutors rehearse pedagogical skills. The authors state "it could support training environments in which teachers or tutors practice responding to varied learner needs", offering a wider range of learner profiles than role-play or live coaching typically allows. This extends the teachable-agent pipeline from student simulation to educator practice infrastructure.
