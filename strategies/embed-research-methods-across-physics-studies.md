# Embed research methods and creative activity across physics studies to strengthen competency formation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the experiment's findings, the authors identify ways to improve the process of forming experimental research competencies of students studying physics at the undergraduate level: to continue introducing modern research methods into all academic studies including practical physics, to increase students' creative activity in learning general physics, and to ensure thematic unity of learning and research and scientific-research work of students at different courses. The strategy treats the laboratory course as one component of a broader integration of research activity into the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creative work should be introduced in all disciplines, including the labs of general practical physics in the general physics course
#### Constraints
- The article notes even a partial realization of the developed approaches can significantly improve formedness, implying full implementation was not achieved in the experiment

### Target Learners
