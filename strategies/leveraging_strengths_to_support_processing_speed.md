# Leveraging Strengths to Support Processing Speed

## Description
This strategy involves identifying a student’s strengths and using them to address challenges related to processing speed. Processing speed is the pace at which one takes in information, makes sense of it, and begins to respond. It is crucial to understand that processing speed varies among individuals and even within the same person depending on the context. Strategies include providing graphic organizers, repeating key points, using manipulatives, and allowing think-time and re-reading. Educators need to know their students well to leverage their strengths effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of processing speed and its impact on learning, knowledge of individual student strengths and challenges, access to appropriate support materials (e.g., graphic organizers, manipulatives).
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to identify student strengths and tailor strategies, may not be effective if strengths are not appropriately leveraged.
