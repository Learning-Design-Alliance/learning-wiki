# Use hybrid simulations combining real-world materials with low-cost simulators to enable realistic, interactive scenarios at reduced cost

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends hybrid simulation approaches that combine different low-cost methods, such as non-computerised mannequins with facilitators playing the patient, in-situ simulations with standardised patients, and moulage applied with real medical materials. The review states hybrid simulations "enhance training by integrating real-world materials with low-cost simulators, enabling realistic, interactive scenarios". This was the most common strategy in the included studies (19 of 39).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real clinical materials such as anaesthesia apparatus, or facilitators who can play the patient role
#### Constraints
- The review notes these methods were often adopted based on feasibility rather than deliberate alignment with curriculum-based competency priorities

### Target Learners
- Nursing students
- Practising nurses in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Obstetric and neonatal emergency management
- Teamwork and communication
- Technical clinical procedures
