# Pair consistent tutors with small groups and use bi-weekly assessment data to drive grouping and instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's delivery model assigns the same tutor to a small group throughout the semester and regroups students from diagnostic and progress-monitoring data. Comprehensive diagnostics "inform both group placement and bi-weekly assessments to track student progress," and tutors deliver explicit, skill-based instruction from a systematic reading curriculum grounded in the Science of Reading.
