# Student-Driven Classroom Transformation

## Description
Involving students in the design and transformation of their learning environment to promote ownership, engagement, and a sense of community. This approach leverages student input to create a brain-friendly enriched classroom environment by considering factors such as layout, furniture choices, color selections, lighting, nature, environmental print, and organization of materials. By actively participating in the transformation, students are empowered to shape their learning space in a way that best suits their needs and preferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher willing to share control over the classroom environment. Open communication and collaboration between teacher and students.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort. Some students may be hesitant to participate. Requires careful facilitation to ensure all voices are heard and valued.
