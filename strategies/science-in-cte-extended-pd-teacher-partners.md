# Extended professional development pairing CTE teachers with science teacher partners in a community of practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (5 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental treatment mirrored the Math-in-CTE model of extended professional development: experimental group CTE teachers received six days of extended PD, were paired with science teacher partners, and engaged with them in a community of practice to map curricula, develop science-enhanced lessons, and critique and refine lessons. Teachers received ongoing support from their science teacher partners at their schools and participated in two days of additional PD at a midpoint in the treatment period. The article recommends future studies apply the model to other CTE areas and increase the amount of inquiry in the pedagogical model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Science teacher partners at the same schools as the CTE teachers
- Extended PD sessions during which teacher teams create, present, and refine science-enhanced lessons with full-group feedback
#### Constraints
- Teachers who participated volunteered and were randomly assigned, so an element of self-selection existed; teachers also received monetary compensation, raising questions about whether results would differ for instructors representing a wider array of teaching abilities, motivation, and CTE contexts

### Target Learners
- secondary agricultural education students

### Target Learning Goals
- science knowledge and skills embedded in CTE curricula
