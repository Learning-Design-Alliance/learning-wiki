# Redesign the geriatric curriculum to comprehensively cover interdisciplinary teamwork and patient/caregiver-centered competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the identified underrepresentation of interdisciplinary teamwork and patient/caregiver needs, the authors recommend redesigning the geriatric curriculum: "there appears to be a pressing need to redesign the geriatric curriculum to cover these critical core competencies more comprehensively, especially with regard to interdisciplinary team engagement." They argue integration of these less-discussed competencies into training of medical students, physicians, and nurses will ensure a more holistic approach to geriatric care. This provides a foundation for curriculum redesign, faculty development, and competency-based assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The recommendation responds to the finding that the current subspecialty-milestone focus fails to prepare practitioners for the complexity of caring for older adults

### Target Learners
- medical students
- residents
- nurses
- other healthcare professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary teamwork
- patient-centered and family/caregiver-responsive geriatric care
