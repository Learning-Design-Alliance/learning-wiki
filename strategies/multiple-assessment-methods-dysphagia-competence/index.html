Use multiple assessment methods to comprehensively evaluate a healthcare provider's competence in all aspects of dysphagia management.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that "multiple assessment methods are needed to comprehensively evaluate a healthcare provider's competence in all aspects of dysphagia management," because "No single assessment type can fully capture and measure all competency areas." Standardized competency evaluation metrics with both self-report and expert observation of knowledge, specific skills, and global ratings would increase the accuracy and reliability when evaluating specific clinical competencies in dysphagia management across healthcare professions.

Target Learners¶

  • Healthcare providers and trainees being assessed for dysphagia management competence

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Comprehensive evaluation of dysphagia management competence across knowledge, skills, and performance domains

Claims¶

  • No specific standardized protocol currently exists for the assessment of dysphagia competence across healthcare disciplines. [+W]
  • The reviewed competency assessments can be classified into three categories: broad assessments, narrow assessments, and unclear or unspecified assessments. [+W]
  • A prominent trend was the reliance on competency metrics that involved expert observation of specific clinical skills, with a notable absence of comprehensive measures combining broad scope and operationally defined skill sets. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop a standardized metric to assess competence in dysphagia management among healthcare providers.
  • Integrate complementary methods — simulations, feedback, and targeted tools — into mental health nurse training and assessment
