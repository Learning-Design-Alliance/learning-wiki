# Brief students on the flipped classroom model's intention and value before class to foster self-active study

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that FC improved motivation but not perceived self-active study, the authors recommend that instructors "should convey the intention, value and implementation of the FC model in detail to all students before class" and inspire enthusiasm for self-active study, since pre-class study is hard to supervise. This is an authors' recommendation drawn from their questionnaire findings rather than a separately tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time to explain the FC model before class
- Preprovided course materials students can study on their own time
#### Constraints
- Pre-class study lacks supervision and management, so self-active study varies from person to person, per the authors

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students new to flipped classroom

### Target Learning Goals
- self-active pre-class study
- preparedness for in-class group discussion
