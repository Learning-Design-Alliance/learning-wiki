# Use multi-component interventions combining oral language instruction with word recognition and other foundational literacy skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends remediating SCD with interventions that combine components rather than targeting vocabulary alone. It states that knowledge that SCD children "also have oral language problems provides support for teaching oral language to children with SCD ... in addition to building word recognition skills," and that instructional approaches should also strengthen other foundational skills such as background knowledge and inference-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should incorporate multiple elements (oral language, word recognition, background knowledge) rather than a single focus
#### Constraints
- Because the vocabulary weakness is a developmental delay, oral language weakness is likely a correlate rather than a cause of reading comprehension difficulty

### Target Learners
- children with poor reading comprehension despite adequate decoding

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension remediation
