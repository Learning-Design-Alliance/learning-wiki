# National-level actions: create astrostatistics chapters and working groups, partner with CANSSI, set conference guidelines, and build a national Centre of Excellence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose organisational chapters or working groups in Astrostatistics within the Statistical Society of Canada (SSC) and CASCA, collaboration with CANSSI on curricula and workshops, guidelines for interdisciplinary conferences, enhanced statistical-analysis delivery on the CANFAR cyber-platform, and a long-term national Astrostatistics & Astroinformatics Centre of Excellence. Conference guidelines include ensuring "a mix of statisticians and astronomers on the LOC and in the program" and stressing that presenters define all terminology clearly rather than assuming basic knowledge.
