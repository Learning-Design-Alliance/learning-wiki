# Incorporate the two-minute bag and mask training into existing neonatal resuscitation training programs, with frequent repetition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding the short, simple key-point training into every NRP program and local training programs wherever a self-inflating bag is used, without major changes, and suggest that "frequent repetition of the two minute mask training should be taken into account when incorporating this into the simulation-based training programs", citing the low-dose high-frequency Helping Babies Breathe approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A self-inflating bag used in the setting
- An instructor able to deliver the standardized two-minute key-point instruction
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is possible that long-lasting effects shown for earlier mask training do not account for a 2 minute training
- The study was performed in a center where mask training with a leak-free manikin and RFM is incorporated, which the authors note could have biased results for experienced caregivers

### Target Learners
- Experienced and inexperienced neonatal caregivers in units using a self-inflating bag, including low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved immediate neonatal mask ventilation skills: reduced mask leak and reduced inadvertent excessive pressures
