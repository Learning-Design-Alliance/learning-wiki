Design plugged CT experiences to be accessible and appealing to girls, and address technology-related anxiety through supportive, culturally relevant designs¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q4 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that plugged pedagogical devices be designed for accessibility and appeal to girls: intuitive user interfaces with clear step-by-step guidance, welcoming aesthetics such as friendly icons and customizable avatars, scaffolding strategies like mind mapping before programming tasks, and personalization of projects. It also recommends tackling anxiety caused by AI and emerging technologies through collaborative tasks, pair programming, culturally relevant tasks connected to health or daily life, and female role models leading coding workshops.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Intuitive user interfaces with clear, step-by-step guidance
  • Scaffolding strategies such as mind mapping before programming tasks

Constraints¶

  • The article presents these as design recommendations informed by cited studies, not as effects tested within the meta-analysis itself

Target Learners¶

  • girls in K-12 and higher education CT courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • computational thinking engagement, motivation, and self-efficacy

Claims¶

  • Pedagogical tool significantly moderates the CT gender gap, with programming and robots associated with male-favoring gaps and other tools (e.g., web-based) showing a negative significant effect [+W]
  • Gender disparities in CT education show a very small but statistically significant overall effect favoring males (g = 0.106) [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use a phased unplugged-to-plugged progression to build girls' CT confidence before introducing digital tools
  • Universities combine responsible AI use guidelines, accessible technical support, AI literacy training, and assessment-embedded critical evaluation activities to address AI learning anxiety
  • Design MALL tasks to be user friendly, socially and culturally sensitive, engaging, and short
