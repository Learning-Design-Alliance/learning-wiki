# Use timed reading activity with level-appropriate passages to build reading speed, while providing separate vocabulary-focused activities for comprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends employing timed reading activity specifically for enhancing reading speed, using passages written within the 1000 most frequently used words of English, each followed by ten comprehension questions. It simultaneously advises that teachers or curriculum designers "need to employ other remedial techniques to improve the language learners' comprehension", since the timed treatment did not significantly raise comprehension scores.
