## Description
The article states that daily assessment can be applied in various environments with creative planning, describing two alternative approaches: a testing room managed by doctoral students at a large research university, and putting assessments online. The authors note that the online approach may limit the use of partial credit and that each program would have to determine the specifics of the application.
