# Prioritize multicenter, randomized designs with standardized reporting and longitudinal follow-up in future surgical education research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review identifies key methodological gaps in the current evidence base and states that "Future research should prioritize multicenter, randomized designs, standardized reporting, and longitudinal follow-up to better define the long-term effectiveness, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness of simulation and multimodal training in endocrine surgery." This strategy directs researchers toward stronger study designs to address the limitations of small, single-center, non-randomized studies that dominate the current literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multicenter collaboration
- Randomized study designs
- Standardized reporting instruments
- Longitudinal follow-up protocols
#### Constraints
- Current evidence base is limited by small samples, single-center designs, and heterogeneous outcome measures

### Target Learners
- Surgical education researchers
- Training program directors

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based curriculum development
- Long-term skill retention assessment
