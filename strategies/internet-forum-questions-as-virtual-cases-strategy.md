# Use real patients' Internet forum questions as virtual cases for teaching and assessing clinical decision-making in undergraduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article puts forward "a new method of teaching and assessing undergraduate students – the medical question asked by real patients in virtual settings without the possibility of further exploration of patients". Teachers select suitable forum questions, students solve them as if the patient attended their practice, and reports are scored with the validated 10-item tool. This addresses the scarcity of live family practice patients and the difficulty of obtaining verbatim consultation transcripts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A moderated Internet health forum as a source of patient questions; teacher screening of cases; the validated assessment tool for scoring reports
#### Constraints
- The article states the educational value of such virtual cases was not evaluated, and the tool was not compared to classical assessment in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in family medicine courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical decision-making
- primary care approach
- holistic and comprehensive patient management
