# Provide pre-implementation orientation, curated strategy lists, physical strategy cards, and a user forum to improve digital CPD tool uptake

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the evaluation, the authors recommend concrete improvements to the TARGET-tool: simplifying complex features such as scheduling student scans, providing a curated list of motivating strategies to assist selection, enhancing visibility of results presentations, adding QR codes, a FAQ feature with video tutorials, and theoretical background at different levels. They also propose delivering a tangible card set of strategies to keep them in teachers' attention and stimulate departmental discussion, and building a learning community by connecting a forum feature so users can collaborate and exchange ideas, especially when a teacher is the only tool user in the department.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation requires attention to teachers' varying knowledge, skills, and abilities
#### Constraints
- Digital-only delivery may let strategies move into the background; physical cards are proposed as a complement

### Target Learners
- Secondary school PE teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement with professional development tools
- Collaborative professional learning
