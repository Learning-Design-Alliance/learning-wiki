# Coordinate skills-focused PBL courses with traditional didactic instruction rather than running them stand-alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In Future Directions the authors recommend pairing PBL skills courses with didactic teaching: "Coordination between the two modalities would obviate the need for background research in the PBL component, reinforce the basic knowledge presented didactically through application, and prepare students for higher order thinking". Their own course was stand-alone, requiring students to research all background material themselves. They add that maintaining a balance between problem-based and traditional instruction may be key to faculty engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A traditional didactic component whose content is aligned with the PBL problems
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is the authors' forward-looking proposal, not a tested manipulation in this study

### Target Learners
- first-year biomedical doctoral students

### Target Learning Goals
- application of foundational knowledge to experimental design and higher order thinking
