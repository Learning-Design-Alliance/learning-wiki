# Run a simulated clinical practice ability assessment before residents leave the department and evaluate teaching from results plus student feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding assessment into the teaching cycle: "Before residents leave the department, their teacher will conduct a simulated assessment of the clinical practice abilities of standardized obstetricians and gynecologists. The teaching effect is evaluated based on the assessment results and student feedback." Teachers then guide residents to search for books and related materials and summarize theoretical content, skill operations, and examination points of each session to ensure all teaching content is mastered.
