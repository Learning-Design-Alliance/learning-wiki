# Reading and Writing for Real-World Math Applications

## Description
Learners select and summarize published articles that highlight the ways math is present in different facets of the real world. They post summaries on an online magazine and include a visual to aid comprehension and cite sources. The assignment connects math concepts to everyday experiences, fostering a quantitative understanding of the world around them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to articles highlighting math applications, online magazine platform, and assessment rubric.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to relevant articles and a platform for creating the magazine. May be time-consuming and require technical support.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Illustrate the relevance of mathematics, develop comprehension and summarization skills, enhance critical thinking, and provide a deeper appreciation for the usefulness of mathematics.

### Affordances
- Allows learners to choose articles according to their interests, and appreciate that the end product, the magazine, can be easily shared with family and friends and stored in their own e-portfolios.

### Personalization
- Learners can select articles that pique their interest, allowing for a personalized exploration of math applications.
