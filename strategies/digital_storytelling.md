# Digital Storytelling

## Description
Digital stories are multimedia narratives that combine various elements such as photographs, video, animation, sound, music, text, and a narrative voice. Students can produce digital stories individually or collaboratively. These stories can be uploaded to the internet and made available to an international audience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to computers, multimedia software, recording equipment, and internet connectivity. Also requires training in digital storytelling techniques.
#### Constraints
- Requires technical skills, can be time-consuming, and may require access to specialized equipment or software.
