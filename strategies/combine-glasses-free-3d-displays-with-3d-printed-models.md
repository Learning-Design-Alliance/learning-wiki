# Combine glasses-free 3D displays with 3D-printed physical models to compensate for the lack of tactile feedback in digital anatomy learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating glasses-free 3D digital visualization with physical models to address a stated limitation of all digital materials — absence of tactile input. It states: "The combination of glasses‐free 3D displays with 3D‐printed physical models could help mitigate the lack of tactile feedback while retaining the strengths of 3D digital visualization." Physical models advantage understanding of individual structures' shapes, while digital content floats structures in space and supports quantitative assessment such as the puzzle-like method.
