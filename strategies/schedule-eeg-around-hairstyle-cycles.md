# Schedule EEG visits around long-lasting, event-related, or special hairstyles and offer flexible timing before families arrive

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review describes “birthday hair” or event-related hairstyles — special, longer-lasting, professionally installed styles around birthdays, holidays, graduations, and school milestones — and finds that “Families are much more likely to refuse EEG recordings if they would ruin a child’s birthday or event-related hair.” It recommends procedures to communicate with families before the visit about current or intended hairstyles and flexibility about when families come into the lab, including scheduling around the next time a child’s hair is to be styled.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-visit communication about current/intended hairstyles must be in place before scheduling decisions are made.
#### Constraints
- Scheduling around styles can be difficult because plans and schedules change unexpectedly, and some styles such as locs are never removed.

### Target Learners
- Black and/or Latinx children and families in pediatric developmental research

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing refusal of EEG participation and preserving family trust and hairstyles during research visits
