# Integrate metacognitive reading strategy tasks into regular EFL reading courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the significant experimental-group gains and the null control-group result, the article recommends that teachers "assist EFL/ESL students to improve reading comprehension by integrating meta-cognitive reading strategy tasks in their regular English reading courses". It further suggests curriculum designers and instructors conceptualize, plan and implement reading strategies in reading classrooms, and notes the approach needs only teacher modeling, feedback and practice opportunities rather than sophisticated materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher modeling and feedback
- opportunities for students to practice each strategy until they can read independently
#### Constraints
- Effects were demonstrated under a specific term of training; the article states long-term follow-up treatment is needed to determine if effects persist
