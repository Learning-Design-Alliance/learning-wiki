# Student Shadowing (Qualitative Data Collection)

## Description
A school leader spends time shadowing a student to gather qualitative data about their lived experiences in the school. This involves objective observation and note-taking on facts (body language, interactions, behaviors) without interpretation or judgment. The goal is to uncover truths about the school environment from the student's perspective and identify areas needing improvement (e.g., instructional practices, biases).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Shadowing template, dedicated time to shadow the student, and willingness to objectively observe and reflect.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful note-taking to avoid bias, may not capture the full picture of a student's experience, and can be time-consuming.
