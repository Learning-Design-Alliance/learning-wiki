# Recruit real virtual-world interlocutors from target cultures via language-exchange platforms before SL tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To secure authentic intercultural interaction, students were asked at semester start to enrol in exchange platforms and contact SL users from target countries, especially Asia, Africa and the Middle East. The article states "A crucial step toward ensuring student engagement i n SL is task design. It is imperative that instructors provide clear guideline s regarding what students should do once logged into SL, tasks to develop and with whom they should interact". Recommended platforms were My language exchange, Language for Exchange and Polyglot club; contacting users proved easier than expected.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must specify tasks and interaction partners in advance
- Students need platform enrolment and weekly availability of interlocutors
#### Constraints
- Interlocutor availability depends on volunteer SL inhabitants; the article reports this worked well in this course but states no failure conditions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate ESP students in monocultural settings

### Target Learning Goals
- authentic intercultural communication in the target language
