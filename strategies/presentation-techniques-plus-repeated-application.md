# Train oral presentation techniques then provide repeated real application opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The discussion reports a two-step approach to building oral presentation skill: first providing information about effective techniques, then ensuring ample practice. Participants "were given information about the different techniques that a good presenter uses," including effective opening of speech, using visuals and commenting on them, and using sounds effectively. The author notes this information alone was insufficient and stresses that it "was followed by many chances for real application and experimentation" through the program's multiple and varied activities, providing drill practice in presentation skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction in presenter techniques (openings, visuals with commentary, effective use of sounds)
- Multiple and varied follow-up activities allowing real application and experimentation
#### Constraints
- Reported within a single one-group pre-post study of fourth-year EFL pre-service teachers; no control condition

### Target Learners
- Pre-service teachers of English (EFL)

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral presentation skills; transfer to giving advice, expressing requests and suggestions
