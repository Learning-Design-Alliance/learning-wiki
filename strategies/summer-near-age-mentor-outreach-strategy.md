# Extend college support services over the summer through simple, cost-effective near-age mentor outreach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "Extending support services to students over the summer through simple, cost-effective outreach might further increase the likelihood of students successfully fulfilling their postsecondary aspirations." The implementable recipe is near-age mentors (undergraduate and graduate students working on campus over the summer) contacting assigned graduating seniors at least twice by email and phone, then holding follow-up meetings in person or by phone or Skype for interested students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentors trained beforehand and provided with conversation templates and roadmaps
- Caseloads below forty students per mentor to keep outreach feasible
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness on fall enrollment was not yet established when the abstract was written; analysis of NSC enrollment data was forthcoming

### Target Learners
- graduating high school seniors with stated postsecondary plans

### Target Learning Goals
- fulfilling originally stated college goals
- completing enrollment steps over the summer
