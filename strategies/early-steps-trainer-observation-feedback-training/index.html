Intensive trainer-led observation and feedback model for tutoring professional development¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Early Steps training involves 9 full-day visits by the trainer across the school year. On each visit the trainer works with the same nine teachers-in-training spread across three sites, observing them tutoring children and providing guidance and feedback; teachers are released for two hours on training days and attend a 1-hour after-school seminar. This embeds coaching in live tutoring practice rather than standalone workshops.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A trainer able to visit sites across the school year
  • release time for teachers (two hours on each training day)
  • a 1-hour after-school seminar on training days

Constraints¶

  • Training capacity as described is nine teachers-in-training spread across three sites (three teachers at each of three schools)

Target Learners¶

  • classroom teachers, reading teachers, and paraprofessionals serving as tutors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • tutoring practice for beginning reading instruction
