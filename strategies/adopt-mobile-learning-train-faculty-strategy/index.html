Adopt mobile learning and train faculty to produce mobile-fitted teaching materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the positive findings, the article recommends that educationists use Mobile Learning in similar contexts, develop conversational skills of English majors with it, gradually extend it to other colleges to replace traditional methods, publicize its benefits for turning leisure into productive activity, and "train faculty members on how to produce teaching materials and prepare their courses that fit Mobile Learning through professional development training sessions."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development training sessions for faculty on mobile-fitted course production

Constraints¶

  • The article warns learning activities must be well designed and carefully implemented, or learners risk superficial learning

Target Learners¶

  • University students, especially English language majors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic achievement and conversational skills development

Claims¶

  • Mobile learning produced significantly higher conversational skills ratings than face-to-face learning in the same quasi-experiment [+W]
  • Mobile learning produced significantly higher academic achievement than face-to-face learning in a fourteen-week quasi-experiment with English-major students [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt mobile messaging applications, especially WhatsApp, across English-language teaching
  • Train students and teachers on academic mobile-phone use and introduce it gradually in small doses
