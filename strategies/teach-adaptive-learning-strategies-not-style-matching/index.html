Strategies

Shift from matching teaching to learning styles toward teaching students adaptable, effective learning strategies aligned to task complexity and goals¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's core recommendation is that "instead of tailoring instruction to student preferences, educators should focus on teaching students how to select and apply adaptive learning strategies, which have a demonstrable positive impact on learning". Strategies should align with task complexity and learning goals, and teaching should attend to facts, ideas, and concepts versus problem-solving and connecting ideas. The request is to move the debate to discussions about optimal learning strategies, how they can be taught in context, and their differential impact.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Strategies must be taught in context and matched to the nature of the task and learning goals

Constraints¶

  • The article notes too few think-aloud or experimental approaches exist even in learning strategies research, so strategy claims also need better evidence

Target Learners¶

  • all school and higher education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • selecting and applying effective learning strategies
  • academic achievement

Claims¶

  • Meta-analyses testing the matching hypothesis show a negligible overall effect of matching teaching to students' learning styles (d = 0.04) [+M]
  • Correlational meta-analyses of learning styles and achievement average r = 0.24 but do not test or support the matching hypothesis [+W]
  • Belief in learning styles remains near-universal among educators, with pre-service teachers believing more than qualified teachers [+W]
  • Labeling children as visual versus hands-on learners biases adults' and children's judgments of intelligence and subject expectations [+W]
  • Deep approaches to learning correlate positively and surface approaches negatively with academic achievement [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Align taught discussion strategies with learning tasks and provide timely individual feedback on strategy and label use
