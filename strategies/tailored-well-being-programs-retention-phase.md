# Provide tailored well-being support programs differentiated by gender and school type during grade retention periods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that well-being trajectories around grade retention differ by gender and school type, the article recommends that "tailored programs for both genders and students in different school types should be provided to foster well-being during this phase". The strategy targets the retention decision year, when school satisfaction drops significantly, and the subsequent repetition year, promoting students' well-being throughout the educational career particularly in these critical periods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must be differentiated by gender and by attended school type, since the article shows heterogeneous trajectories across these groups
#### Constraints
- The article notes it could not distinguish mandatory from voluntary grade repetition due to lack of respective items, and could not account for parental separation, divorce or death

### Target Learners
- secondary school students facing grade retention decisions
- students repeating a grade in German secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining and fostering general and school-related well-being during critical transition periods

### Affordances
- Critical Events Stress Development Perspectives
