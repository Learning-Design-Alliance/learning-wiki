# Adapt the summer research internship model down to high-school level with group research projects and mentoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors argue the LEAPS concept of training students through hands-on research is flexible and adaptable, and that "programmes such as LEAPS could be down-scaled and adapted to high-schools". The proposed adaptation would propose simple research projects, potentially for groups rather than single students, with the aim of mentoring students and teaching them how to conduct scientific research. International mobility may apply at national level depending on the country.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simple research projects suitable for high-school students
- Mentors to teach students how to conduct scientific research
#### Constraints
- The international mobility aspect may not apply directly at high-school level, though it may apply at national level depending on the country

### Target Learners
- High-school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning how to conduct scientific research
- Building long-term motivation for research careers
