# Implement TPSR through teacher co-design, professional development, and existing PE programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The protocol's implementation recipe has regular PE teachers facilitate the intervention after a professional development program and a co-design process with researchers. Teachers "will work with researchers to identify social issues at their school; they will then work with researchers to apply TPSR principles to address these issues". The co-design process aims to "share power" between researchers and teachers, limit disruption to practice, and promote sustainable in-school practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 3-hour face-to-face professional development block before the intervention plus ongoing Zoom check-ins; teachers embed TPSR in existing units to minimize additional workload
#### Constraints
- Delivery is confined to the PE lesson (one per week, 45-60 minutes typical in NSW schools), which the authors state may limit transfer to other learning contexts

### Target Learners
- School students in years 5, 6, and 8 in NSW PE classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Social outcomes including personal and social responsibility, leadership, and engagement in PE
