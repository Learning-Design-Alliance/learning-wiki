## Description
The article closes with a roadmap for achieving gender equity in Indian physics institutions. It calls for "continued efforts to address biases, mandatory ge nder sensitization programs, nationwide surveys and collection of statistics, and career development and mentoring programs for young students," plus involving social scientists and men as partners, and implementing proposed policies such as the STI Policy's "mandate of at least 30% women in leadership positions." The ultimate stated goal is to make gender a nonissue.
