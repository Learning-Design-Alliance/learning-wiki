# Action-Oriented Feedback

## Description
Action-oriented feedback focuses on providing students with specific steps they can take to improve their writing. Rather than just marking grammar mistakes, this approach aims to help students see how the actions they take can benefit their future writing. Conferring with students can facilitate this process, allowing teachers to suggest specific actions, such as varying sentence structure. Peer editing with specific suggestions can also move writers forward.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to engage in conferencing and provide actionable suggestions. Peer editing can supplement teacher feedback.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and effort compared to simply marking errors. Students must be receptive to feedback and willing to take action.

### Personalization
- Teachers can tailor feedback to the individual needs of the student, suggesting actions that are relevant to their specific writing challenges.
