# Weekly peer evaluation with a nurse-of-the-week recognition programme to increase nurse motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The research team designed a peer group evaluation in which nurses completed a five-item questionnaire naming the colleague doing the best nursing care, the most cooperative, the most disciplined, the most empathetic and the most knowledgeable and skilful during the past week. Every week a nurse was selected according to the peer evaluation and a small present along with acknowledgement was given by the head nurse. The article's impact statement says pedagogical strategies such as selecting the nurse of the week can increase motivations and job satisfaction in nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A peer group evaluation scale designed for the ward and head-nurse involvement in giving the acknowledgement.
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a single surgical department with 14 nurses; the article notes interventions were planned according to their feasibility and applicability in that setting.

### Target Learners
- staff nurses in hospital wards

### Target Learning Goals
- motivation
- job satisfaction
- professional dignity
