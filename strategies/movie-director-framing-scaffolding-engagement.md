# Frame simulation lessons as a movie-director game with scaffolding, varied texts, and reflective discussion to sustain engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To promote engagement and motivation, the training was presented as a Bmovie director training^ teaching children to create a Bmovie from the text^ in their head and experience events as the main character. The authors also applied scaffolding techniques including gradual fading of teacher support and transfer of responsibility to students, used varied level-appropriate text examples, and ended each lesson with a reflective discussion about what and how children had learned and why it matters.
