# Morphological Instruction

## Description
Instruction that makes students aware of inflections (–ed, –s, –es, –ing, –er, –est, which are also called grammatical suffixes), morphemes that change the number, person, or tense of the word to which they are added, but they do not change its part of speech.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word lists, morphological charts, direct instruction
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit and systematic instruction
