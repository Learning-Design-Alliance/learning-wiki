# Incorporate the cognitive representation approach into medical education programs after refining interfaces and adding early guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on interview and expert feedback, the authors recommend adopting the approach in medical education with specific refinements. Learners "suggested that the interfaces of the system could be improved and more learning guidance could be provided at the beginning of the learning program". Domain experts and the instructor "suggested the incorporation of the approach into medical education programs after refining some interfaces". This positions the approach as a supplement to existing clinical reasoning education once usability issues are addressed.
