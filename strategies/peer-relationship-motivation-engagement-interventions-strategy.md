# Foster positive peer relationships, motivation, and engagement through classroom interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that peer relationships relate to achievement both directly and through motivation and engagement, the authors recommend that "fostering positive peer relationships should be prioritized in educational settings", for example through peer mentoring programs or cooperative learning activities, alongside an inclusive classroom climate. They further recommend problem-solving activities connecting learning to students' lives, timely constructive feedback, flexible deadlines, meaningful classroom activities, and emotional support to prevent learning fatigue.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should target peer relationships, learning motivation, and learning engagement together, since all three were linked to achievement
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and from China's test-oriented learning environment, which the authors say may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- junior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving academic achievement
- sustaining learning motivation and engagement
