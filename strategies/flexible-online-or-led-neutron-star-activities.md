# Run neutron-star spreadsheet activities flexibly online or in demonstrator-led sessions with supporting multimedia

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends flexible delivery modes for the two activities: "They can, for instance, be used as online individual activities for independent students. A minimal instruction set and some online background material (such as the video here) may be helpful in this case. Alternatively, the activities could be part of a group discussion or a demonstrator-led outreach or educational session." The spreadsheets visually illustrate concepts of binding, nuclear physics, astrophysics and general relativity in either mode.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A minimal instruction set and online background material such as the linked video for independent use
#### Constraints
- The data used in the second activity depends on the results of the first, so the two activities are sequenced

### Target Learners
- secondary school A-level students
- outreach event audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- neutron-star properties including density, pressure and mass limits
- multidisciplinary applications of scientific models
