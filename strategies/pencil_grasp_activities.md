# Pencil Grasp Activities

## Description
Activities aimed at promoting a functional pencil grasp, which is essential for comfortable and efficient handwriting. These activities focus on improving fine motor skills, hand strength, and thumb opposition to support a proper pencil grasp.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Various writing tools (e.g., small pencils, golf pencils), fine motor materials (e.g., beads, clothespins), and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not address underlying issues such as sensory sensitivities or postural control. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Affordances
- Improves comfort and efficiency in handwriting by establishing a functional pencil grasp. Supports fine motor development and hand strength.

### Personalization
- Activities can be tailored to individual grasp patterns and hand strength. Varied tools and materials engage different motor skills.
