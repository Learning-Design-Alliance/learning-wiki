# Steer system use toward subject-appropriate activities: training quizzes for languages, reading texts for societal and science subjects, and school-hours use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the clustering results, the authors recommend optimizing learning by matching activity type to subject and context: for languages, focus on exercises at the read-and-understand Bloom level and on training quizzes; for societal subjects, focus on analyzing-and-evaluating exercises; and encourage use during school hours with longer sessions, since home quiz use showed no performance benefit. The article suggests teachers could recommend training quizzes for language students and allow more system time in school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of which subject a student is working with, and the ability to assign or recommend quizzes, texts or exercises of suitable Bloom level
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from observational clustering of one proprietary system's logs; the article notes better and more reliable results might be obtained by including more data

### Target Learners
- primary school students using an online educational system

### Target Learning Goals
- improving quiz performance and balanced use of Bloom's taxonomy levels
