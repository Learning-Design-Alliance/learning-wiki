# Pilot AI-generated items as unscored exam items, review their psychometrics, and bank those meeting quality standards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Tip 9's banking component instructs that questions generated through the preceding steps be piloted in actual exams as additional items, reviewed for difficulty and psychometric properties, and archived in a question bank for future use if they meet quality standards. This creates a continuous improvement loop in which real exam data validates AI-generated content before it enters operational use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions must first pass the earlier generation and review steps before piloting
#### Constraints
- Only questions meeting quality standards should be archived in the question bank

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate medical education trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- a growing bank of validated, psychometrically sound assessment items
