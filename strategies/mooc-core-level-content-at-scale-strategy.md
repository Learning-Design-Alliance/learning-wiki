# Keep MOOC content at core masters-course level rather than diluting it for a massive audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that even with thousands of students a MOOC should continue to draw on advanced tools and aim at deep insights, comparing the MOOC to a city marathon that remains 42.195 km long while appealing to a large public with varied goals and performance levels. The author writes: "even with a big crowd, we would engage in core-level (rather than introductory) academic teaching". The course thus kept all the advanced mathematical tools of the ENS classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large audience is needed for MOOC teaching to take off, and the massive nature is described as both an opportunity and a survival condition to sustain liveliness in teaching.
#### Constraints
- The sheer size carries its own dangers: catastrophic failure, such as the inability to go through with a course, presents a real threat and is widely publicized.

### Target Learners
- Massive, self-selected audiences with varied backgrounds, personal goals and performance levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Deep physical insight and advanced mathematical competence in statistical mechanics and computing
