Three-part strategy for responsible AI use in education: empower reasoning, foster emotional well-being, and provide institutional support¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper proposes that AI in education should follow three principles. First, empower reasoning: prioritize constructivist, active learning, and metacognitive strategies so that "rather than providing direct answers, AI tools should be used to support scaffolding, prompting deeper reflection, reasoning, and understanding". Second, foster emotional well-being through guidance on how and when to use AI, metacognitive training, and preserving meaningful human interaction. Third, institutional support: robust AI literacy programs, professional development for educators, and clear policies on transparency, data privacy, acceptable AI use, and equitable access. The paper notes only 25% of U.S. colleges currently offer formal AI training.
