# Use the coaching framework as a structured discussion guide with a note-taking grid and study group questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief operationalizes its framework as a discussion tool: Figure 2 provides a grid with space for recording "Current Work" and "Ideas to Move Forward" across the three levels, and five suggested discussion questions prompt groups to locate current coaching work on the trajectory and connect it to the recursive change process. This gives coaches a concrete protocol for reflecting on and planning their mentoring and advocacy efforts.
