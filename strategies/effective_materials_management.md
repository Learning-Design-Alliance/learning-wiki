# Effective Materials Management

## Description
Using an 'in' and 'out' basket system for papers, labeling cubbies, bookshelves, bins, and folders, and providing easily accessible supplies cuts down on student time locating materials and supports organization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In/out baskets, labels, storage containers, readily accessible supplies.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort to maintain organization. May require training students on how to use the system effectively.
