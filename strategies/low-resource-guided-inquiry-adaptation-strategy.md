# Adapt guided inquiry laboratory instruction to resource-limited settings using locally available materials, virtual labs, and teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that resource-limited schools implement guided inquiry laboratory instruction by using locally available materials for cost-effective experiments, incorporating virtual laboratory tools to simulate processes that limited equipment cannot support, and providing comprehensive teacher training in inquiry-based pedagogy. It also recommends gender-sensitive teaching practices so that both boys and girls participate actively in inquiry-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comprehensive teacher training programs focused on inquiry-based pedagogy and continuous professional development
#### Constraints
- Intended for resource-limited settings where well-equipped laboratories and extensive materials are unavailable

### Target Learners
- Secondary school science students in low-resource schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Science process skills and conceptual understanding in biology
