# Pre-Teaching Vocabulary

## Description
Pre-teaching vocabulary involves introducing new words before encountering them in a text or lesson. This strategy helps learners activate prior knowledge, increase motivation, and assess what they already know about a topic. By addressing any misconceptions or gaps in background knowledge, instructors can ensure learners are better prepared to understand the material. Pre-teaching vocabulary can also help learners understand the purpose for reading or engaging with a text, ultimately leading to increased comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of key vocabulary words from the text or lesson. Development of engaging activities to introduce and reinforce the new words.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and planning to prepare vocabulary activities. Can be challenging to select the most relevant and impactful words for pre-teaching.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those in upper elementary through high school, who need additional support in building vocabulary and improving comprehension.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance vocabulary acquisition, improve reading comprehension, and activate background knowledge by introducing new words before engaging with a text.

### Affordances
- Activates prior knowledge and reduces cognitive load during reading. Ensures learners understand key terms and concepts, promoting deeper engagement with the material.

### Personalization
- Instructors can select vocabulary words based on the specific needs of their learners and the content they are studying. Activities can be tailored to address individual learning styles and prior knowledge.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track learner performance on pre- and post-tests, participation in vocabulary activities, and demonstration of understanding during discussions. Monitor improvement in reading comprehension.

## Impact
- Learners demonstrate improved vocabulary, enhanced reading comprehension, and a greater ability to engage with complex texts. Pre-teaching vocabulary provides a solid foundation for learning and ensures that learners are prepared to succeed.
