# Structure vaginal myomectomy training as approximately 30–34 supervised cases with mentorship and stepwise responsibility for trainees with prior vaginal surgery experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that surgical training programs use its observed learning curve to structure trainee progression: "trainees with prior experience in basic and advanced vaginal surgery may require approximately 30–34 supervised vaginal myomectomy procedures to achieve a stable level of operative efficiency." It pairs this case threshold with "structured mentorship, stepwise progression of surgical responsibility, and careful patient selection during the early learning phase." The strategy derives from one surgeon's initial 60-case series, not a training experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees should already have prior experience in basic and advanced vaginal surgery before starting supervised vaginal myomectomy cases
#### Constraints
- The findings reflect a single surgeon with approximately 20 years of gynecologic surgical experience and should not be interpreted as a definitive training requirement

### Target Learners
- Gynecology trainees and fellows with prior vaginal surgical experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Achieving stable operative efficiency in vaginal myomectomy
