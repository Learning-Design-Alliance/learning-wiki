## Description
Based on the IMPART study, the authors outline generalizable recommendations in three groups: understanding context, maximising initial buy-in and continued use, and providing relevant feedback and managing expectations. Recommendations include clarifying how the DHI fits into care pathways, ensuring the DHI "is clearly identified as a clinical initiative and not identified as primarily research", appointing champions, providing ongoing multi-channel training that accommodates staff turnover, and making audit reports of DHI use easily available to stakeholders.
