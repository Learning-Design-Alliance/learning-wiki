# Combine CTF performance data with team discussion for role assignment in team projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adding a CTF exercise so that teams gain "an additional data point regarding their team members’ abilities" beyond discussion alone, then combining the CTF performance data with the team's discussion to determine each member's functional roles. Discussion prompts have teams reflect on the algorithm's pre-assigned role, share backgrounds and expertise on a worksheet, consider role changes, and reflect on how CTF and discussion aided expertise recognition. The two processes are presented as complementary for accurate expertise recognition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A CTF exercise that measures participant competency in the role/skill categories relevant to the team project, plus a structured discussion worksheet for sharing expertise beyond CTF performance
#### Constraints
- The article frames the algorithmic assignment as a starting point from which members may revise roles based on preferences and other inputs; the strategy was studied in a small newly formed university team setting.

### Target Learners
- engineering and cybersecurity students in newly formed teams

### Target Learning Goals
- expertise recognition
- role assignment aligned with member strengths
- team knowledge sharing

### Affordances
- Ctf Computer Human Team Construction Framework
