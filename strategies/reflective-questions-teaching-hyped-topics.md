# Reflective question set for instructors teaching topics garnering significant media attention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on common wisdom and the demonstrated importance of media hype to their interviewees, the authors propose five questions instructors may find valuable when teaching hyped topics. These ask about the instructor's goal in bringing the topic into the classroom, what media coverage students were likely exposed to and how accurate it is, what preconceptions students bring, where the technology sits in the hype cycle, and how to leverage hype discussions to build scientific literacy, critical thinking, ethical reasoning, and science communication skills.
