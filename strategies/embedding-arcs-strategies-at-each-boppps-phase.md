# Embedding ARCS motivational strategies at each BOPPPS phase in clinical course design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy maps each ARCS dimension to a specific BOPPPS phase: clinical cases and VR immersive experiences during Bridge-in capture Attention; articulating relevance of learning goals to professional development during the Objective phase enhances Relevance; scaffolded task design throughout Participatory Learning builds Confidence; and showcasing exemplary student work with positive feedback during Post-assessment and Summary fosters Satisfaction. This achieves "a deep integration of motivational strategies within the pedagogical structure."
