# Skimming

## Description
Skimming involves reviewing a text quickly, focusing on headings, captions, and highlighted information, to gain familiarity with the material before reading it thoroughly. Younger students might take a "picture walk" through the reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reading materials.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if the skimming is not focused or if the student does not have the background knowledge necessary to understand the material.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly students who struggle with reading comprehension.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- To make students familiar with the material before they read it more thoroughly, to improve reading comprehension.

### Affordances
- Provides a preview of the text and highlights key points, making it easier for students to understand the material when they read it more thoroughly.

### Personalization
- The teacher can select the headings, captions, and highlighted information that are most relevant to the lesson.
