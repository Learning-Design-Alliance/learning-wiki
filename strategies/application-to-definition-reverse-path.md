# Reverse 'application to definition' path: start from societal concepts and reveal the physics quantities behind them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teaching strategy that inverts the usual 'definition to application' sequence of school programs. The session starts from general social concepts like "sustainability", "ecology", "recycling", and then shows that behind them lie precise physics quantities such as "energy", "efficiency" and "entropy", related by precise physics laws. The authors used it to introduce thermodynamics through society and the economy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A moderator able to alternate brainstorming with slides showing plots and diagrams that reveal the underlying physical principles.
#### Constraints
- The article reports the resulting topic is not fully identified as a standard part of the school's program, differing from typical classroom language and methodology.

### Target Learners
- high school students
- public audiences at science events

### Target Learning Goals
- fundamental laws of thermodynamics
- reversibility, efficiency and entropy
- sustainability and energy issues
