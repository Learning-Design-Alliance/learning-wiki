# Use the Start, Stop, Continue process to surface and act on promising practices with educational partners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Start, Stop, Continue process lets district and school staff engage educational partners in reflecting on problems of practice or school climate goals. Partners reflect on what promising practices should be started, what current practices should be stopped because they may cause harm or yield fruitless results, and what should be continued because they lead to beneficial outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A regular routine of identifying and celebrating achievements within school and district communities to create opportunities for uncovering promising practices
#### Constraints
- Intended for reflecting on problems of practice or school climate goals with educational partners, not for individual leader decisions

### Target Learners
- School and district staff working with students, families, and community partners

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying promising practices
- allocating time and resources toward successful practices
- phasing out less effective approaches
