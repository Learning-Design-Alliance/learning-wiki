# Assign purpose-built voice AI debate practice before class to build students' participation in live discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors deploy purpose-built voice AI platforms for pre-class rehearsal: "Our findings point to voice AI platforms as a way for instructors to engage their students in new modalities of learning that can not only trigger greater critical thinking, but also motivate students to participate in other vital learning-based interactions, such as classroom discussion." The implementable recipe is to assign students to debate a case question with an AI that takes the opposing position, before the class in which the case is discussed, with the expectation that benefits appear in later sessions after repeated use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The AI tool must be purpose-built and tailored to the topics of the anticipated class discussion; benefits required two assigned uses in the study
#### Constraints
- The treatment effect applies to comparing students who have used the tool with those who have not yet used it; the authors could not compare against students who never used it, nor against human discussion partners

### Target Learners
- MBA / professional-degree students in discussion-based courses

### Target Learning Goals
- voluntary in-class participation
- comfort speaking up
- perceived learning of course material
