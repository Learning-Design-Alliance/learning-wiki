# Integrate virtual patient cases as an alternative to lecturer-led small-group teaching within a curricular clerkship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using virtual patient cases as an alternative, not merely an addition, to lecturer-led small-group teaching inside a curricular clerkship. The authors report that "integrating VP cases is feasible within a curricular Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Clerkship and leads to substantial growth of clinical competence in undergraduate dental students". Implementation matched the comparison format in time (240 min), room, learning objectives and multiple-choice questions, with students allowed to exchange ideas in both formats. The authors add that future work should examine cost effectiveness, compare case designs, and determine how to implement expert feedback into VP cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VP cases designed per published design principles with specific feedback, appropriate difficulty and interactivity
- Curricular time slot and seminar room equivalent to the replaced teaching format
#### Constraints
- Results are from a preliminary study with n = 57 and must be checked for transfer to other subjects and faculties

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge and clinical reasoning in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
