# Pair each take-home simulation kit with an online module and virtual debriefing with senior residents and attendings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a workflow in which each distributed kit "is linked to an online module with an associated case and description, and the procedure is discussed virtually with a senior resident and otolaryngology attending after completion." This pairs independent home practice with guided case-based framing and expert feedback at a distance, and pre and post surveys are provided to residents to evaluate the exercise's utility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a kit with model, supplies and instruments; an online module with an associated case and description; a senior resident and attending available for virtual discussion
#### Constraints
- developed in the context of COVID-19 restrictions on in-person surgical training

### Target Learners
- junior surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural skill maintenance during reduced operative volume
