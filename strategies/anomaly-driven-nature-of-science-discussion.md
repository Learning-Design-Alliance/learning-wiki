# Use anomalies in real data (KOI-55, K2-285, TRAPPIST-1) to teach the limitations and revision of physical laws

## Description
The article suggests exploiting systems that deviate from expectations to discuss the nature of science. KOI-55's deviation is linked to a host star that underwent a red giant phase, and the article notes "a subsequent study suggested that the signals associated with the detection of both planets were instead due to pulsations of the red subgiant star." The authors state that limitations of physical laws and their constant reconsideration allow teaching physics as fascinating and useful, far from a reductionist and decontextualized vision.
