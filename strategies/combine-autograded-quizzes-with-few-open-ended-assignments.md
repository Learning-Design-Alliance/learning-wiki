# Combine autograded frequent quizzes with few gradetools-graded open-ended assignments when teaching staff is limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For large classes with limited teaching staff, the article recommends a mixed assessment strategy: frequent quizzes autograded with another tool such as Gradescope online assignments, combined with few open-ended assignments graded with gradetools. This is offered because gradetools "speeds up the process by automating repetitive tasks, but still requires considerable time relative to assignments that could be autograded".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an autograding tool for the quiz component and gradetools set up for the open-ended component
#### Constraints
- The time gain from gradetools may still not be enough to allow grading many open-ended assignments throughout the quarter for large classes

### Target Learners
- students in large data science and statistics classes

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced formative assessment within limited grading capacity
