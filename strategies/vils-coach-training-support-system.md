# Multi-layer coach training and support system: coach camp, paired mentoring, ongoing webinars, and coach-the-coach sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Digital Promise supports program coaches through a layered professional learning system: new coaches attend a four-day in-person coach camp practicing coaching conversations and coaching cycles; first-year coaches are paired with experienced practicing coaches; all coaches receive webinars, in-person events, asynchronous courses, and cross-district problem-solving sessions; and Digital Promise staff coach the coaches through twice-per-month one-to-one sessions. Coaches then structure goal-setting coaching cycles with teachers, including co-teaching, feedback, and Teacher Leader Corps groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches hired specifically for the program and dedicated Digital Promise staff to provide the ongoing one-to-one support
#### Constraints
- The report describes this system as implemented within the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program's resources and structure

### Target Learners
- instructional technology coaches
- classroom teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective technology integration
- coaching skills for working with adult learners
- teacher leadership
