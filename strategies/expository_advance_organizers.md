# Expository Advance Organizers

## Description
Expository advance organizers give students a broad idea of the lesson’s purpose before it begins by clearly stating the goals of the lesson. Teachers often state goals and write them on the board.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined learning goals and a means to communicate them to students (e.g., verbally, in writing).
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if goals are too broad or not clearly connected to lesson activities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Student understanding of lesson goals, participation in activities aligned with those goals, and ability to articulate what they have learned.

## Impact
- Explicitly stating the purpose of the lesson prepares students to think critically and purposefully about the content to be learned.
