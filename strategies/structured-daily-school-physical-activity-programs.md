# Integrate structured physical activity programs into the daily school curriculum to sustain MVPA

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools embed structured physical activity into the daily curriculum so students reach recommended MVPA levels. It states schools "should integrate structured physical activity programs into the daily curriculum", including regular physical education classes, active breaks during lessons, and after-school sports programs. This follows from the finding that sustained high MVPA trajectories were associated with better academic achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools provide regular physical education classes, active breaks during lessons, and after-school sports programs as the article lists.
#### Constraints
- The article notes only 12% of children maintained high MVPA over three years, so sustaining levels over time, not one-off participation, is the target.

### Target Learners
- Primary school children

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement in reading, mathematics and language; physical health
