# Implement multilingual content moderation for open-ended user input in educational AI systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Multiple participants (P1, P2, P4, P6, P9, P10, P13) raised concerns about users submitting inappropriate content through the custom input feature; P6 noted "content moderation is very important". The authors derive the design principle of proactive safety through multilingual content moderation: educational AI systems, especially when portraying vulnerable or marginal groups, must implement robust content filtering while preserving the open-ended interaction that makes them educationally valuable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applies to educational AI systems that accept free-form user-generated input alongside structured choices
#### Constraints
- Moderation must not compromise the open-ended interaction that gives the system educational value

### Target Learners
- all users of open-input educational AI systems, including students in classroom settings

### Target Learning Goals
- safe engagement with open-ended AI-mediated learning interactions
