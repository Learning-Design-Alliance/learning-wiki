# Incorporate potential moderators into experimental designs for multimedia testing research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research on pictures in test items systematically incorporate potential moderators—including item complexity, subject domain, test-taker abilities, age, and affective-motivational factors—into experimental designs. The authors state that "Future research should therefore increasingly incorporate potential moderators into experimental designs" because conclusions on item-level and test-taker-level moderators were often not possible due to sparse data. They also recommend standardized assessments of cognitive abilities and prior knowledge to improve comparability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experimental designs that manipulate or measure moderator variables alongside picture function
- Standardized assessments of cognitive abilities and prior knowledge
#### Constraints
- Current database is heterogeneous with substantial differences in tasks and domains
- Some studies lack control groups, limiting causal interpretation

### Target Learners
- Test-takers across educational levels and domains

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding boundary conditions of picture effects in assessment design
