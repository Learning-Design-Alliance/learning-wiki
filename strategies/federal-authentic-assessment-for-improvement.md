# Federal policy should support authentic performance assessments used for improvement rather than punishment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends federal testing requirements focus on quality rather than quantity, supporting authentic measures of relevant knowledge and skills as used in the real world. It points to measures examining critical thinking, complex problem-solving, and inquiry through student performance on real-world tasks requiring research, experimentation, analysis, use of evidence, and construction of solutions, used for ongoing improvement and expanding opportunity.
