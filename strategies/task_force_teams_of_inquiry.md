# Task Force Teams of Inquiry

## Description
Forming task force teams of inquiry by letting teams form (or helping with this) and asking each team to select a topic from the brainstorm list. Their mission is to explore, research, and present on that topic. Connect the inquiry to educational standards that your school follows.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to provide clear guidelines and expectations for team projects. Requires learners to collaborate effectively and share responsibility for their work.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who struggle to work in teams or who prefer more individual work. Requires careful facilitation to ensure all team members contribute and benefit from the experience.

### Target Learning Goals

- Promote inquiry-based learning, develop research and presentation skills, and foster collaborative teamwork.

### Affordances
- Encourages teamwork and collaboration, developing social and communication skills. Supports inquiry-based learning and critical thinking. Allows learners to take ownership of their learning and explore topics of interest.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying the level of guidance and support provided to the teams. Roles and responsibilities within the teams can be differentiated to suit different skill sets and learning preferences.
