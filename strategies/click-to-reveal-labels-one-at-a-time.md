# Let learners reveal labels and color codes one at a time by clicking buttons in a computer-based lesson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In computer-based lessons, learners click buttons to make a single label or color code appear at a time, rather than seeing all cues simultaneously. The article argues this approach "affords the opportunity to show a single label or color code at a time", helping learners focus attention, and permits viewing cues "at their own pace", which may promote learning. In the study, all participants in cue conditions clicked every button while reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer-based lesson interface with buttons that trigger the display of labels or color codes
#### Constraints
- Only one cue area was visible at a time; previously shown cues disappeared when a new button was clicked

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- probabilistic reasoning
- posterior probability calculation
