# Move from delayed toward real-time LLM feedback to improve argumentation across the semester

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because feedback in this study was delayed, the authors state "we intend to provide feedback more promptly, starting with shortly after quizzes are completed to eventually real-time feedback," with the goal to improve student argumentation throughout the semester. They argue that students who previously would have received little to no feedback on their written essays will eventually be able to receive individualized and constructive real-time feedback in large-enrollment courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large-enrollment course context where individualized feedback on strategy essays is otherwise prohibitive, and an LLM feedback pipeline already in place.
#### Constraints
- The article states that as a result of the delayed feedback there was likely little benefit to the progression of student written argumentation in this study.

### Target Learners
- students in large-enrollment introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improving written scientific argumentation throughout the semester

## Claims

- [On a high-difficulty energy problem, GPT-4o scored the written arguments of students who answered the multiple-choice question correctly significantly higher than those who answered incorrectly (Cohen's d = 0.87)](../claims/llm-quiz08-scores-differ-by-mc-correctness.md) [+W]
- [On a low-difficulty energy problem, GPT-4o scored the written arguments of students who answered correctly significantly but only slightly higher than those who answered incorrectly (Cohen's d = 0.22)](../claims/llm-quiz09-scores-differ-by-mc-correctness-small-effect.md) [+W]
- [The authors conclude that LLM scoring of written arguments works for both high and low difficulty problems, with scores matching quiz averages](../claims/llm-scoring-works-high-and-low-difficulty.md) [+W]
- [Across both quizzes, the majority of students found the LLM feedback mostly useful and mostly accurate, with no significant difference between students who answered the MC question correctly versus incorrectly](../claims/students-rate-llm-feedback-mostly-useful-accurate.md) [+W]
