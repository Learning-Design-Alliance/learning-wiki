# Remove Visual Distractions

## Description
When learners are struggling with a difficult task, clutter in the classroom or on the desk can make it impossible to keep the brain focused. Remove unnecessary clutter and visual stimuli from the workspace to help the learner focus on the task at hand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clean workspace, minimal visual stimuli, designated quiet space.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in shared spaces. Some learners may find the lack of stimulation demotivating.

### Affordances
- Reduces sensory overload, minimizes distractions, promotes focus, enhances attention, creates a calm environment

### Personalization
- The level of reduction can be adjusted based on individual needs and sensitivities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the learner's ability to stay on task. Track the frequency of distractions. Collect feedback on the learner's sense of focus.

## Impact
- Removing visual distractions can significantly improve focus and attention, especially for learners who are sensitive to sensory overload. It creates a calm and supportive environment that enhances concentration.
