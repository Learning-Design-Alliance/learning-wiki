# Provide structured, level-tailored career preparation programs (resume workshops, mock interviews, employer research guidance) for CS students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that academic institutions offer structured career preparation programs tailored to students' academic levels before career fairs. As printed, "These should include resume workshops, mock interviews, and guidance on employer research," plus soft-skills workshops and mentorship or affinity-group support for underrepresented students. The recommendation follows from the finding that preparation varied widely and no participant sought advice from faculty or career offices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to career preparation programming tailored to academic level
#### Constraints
- Recommendation arises from a single-institution exploratory survey; the authors state results may not be directly generalizable to a broader population

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate Computer Science students attending career fairs

### Target Learning Goals
- career fair preparation
- job search strategies
- professional communication skills
