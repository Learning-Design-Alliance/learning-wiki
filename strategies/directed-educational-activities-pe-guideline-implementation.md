# Directed educational activities and continuing education to improve guideline implementation in post-preeclampsia care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends establishing continuing-education offers for outpatient obstetrician-gynecologists covering the association between preeclampsia and long-term cardiovascular risk and the current German follow-up guidelines. It states that counseling deficiencies "would be amendable to directed educational activities to improve the implementation of current guidelines", and that 96.2% of surveyed physicians wanted further training. The strategy targets practicing physicians in outpatient care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physicians must be reachable through continuing-education offers; the article reports 96.2% of participants indicated an interest in further training.
#### Constraints
- The article does not test an educational intervention; the recommendation rests on observed knowledge and counseling gaps in one regional survey.

### Target Learners
- practicing obstetrician-gynecologists in outpatient care

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of long-term cardiovascular and renal risks after preeclampsia
- knowledge of and adherence to German follow-up and counseling guidelines
