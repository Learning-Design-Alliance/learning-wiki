# Address MOOC personalization at scale by using learners as helpers plus personalization technology to find the right helper at the right time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
For massive courses where each learner has idiosyncratic needs that a small cadre of human instructors cannot tend to, the article draws two insights from the PHelpS/I-Help experience: "(i) use the learners themselves as helpers; and (ii) support these learners through person-alization technology that can find the right helper at the right time in clever and scalable ways." Peer help in large classes is both scalable and affordable, and learner modelling research supplies the personalization needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sufficient critical mass of participating learners
- Personalization technology (learner modelling and matchmaking) able to find suitable helpers at scale
#### Constraints
- The article notes the 1-on-1 recommender was underused due to the cost of building task models, lack of a critical mass for synchronous help, and unresolved motivational issues

### Target Learners
- Students in massive or large online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Getting timely help with course problems at scale
