# Implement school breakfast clubs and active-transport walking programs combining low-GI breakfast with light bilateral coordinative exercise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (9 for, 2 against) · 5 studies (2 causal, 2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q4` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports healthy lifestyle programs in which adolescents consume a low-GI breakfast and complete about 30 minutes of aerobic plus bilateral coordinative exercise in the morning. The bilateral coordinative ball exercises were rated as light (RPE of 11), and the authors conclude the findings "lend support to the implementation of healthy lifestyle programs, such as school breakfast clubs and walking programs for active transport to schools".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A low glycemic index breakfast (meal GI of 46 in this study) and a 30 min bout combining treadmill running with bilateral coordinative ball exercises
#### Constraints
- The bilateral coordinative exercises were rated as light (RPE of 11) by participants, and improvements were transient and limited in extent

### Target Learners
- adolescents aged 14-19

### Target Learning Goals
- short-term mathematics fluency and cognitive performance
