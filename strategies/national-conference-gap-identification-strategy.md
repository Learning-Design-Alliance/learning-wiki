# Identify information gaps through national conference review to set research and development strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project used a two-day national conference of experts, held after drafts were circulated, to elicit suggestions for revision and recommendations for future research. The report states that "Information gaps in the affective area are identified, and suggestions for research and development strategies are proposed." This strategy pairs manuscript reviews of theory, techniques, materials, and instrumentation with structured expert consultation to define a research agenda for affective development in handicapped preschoolers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Draft manuscripts must be prepared and distributed to participants before the conference so suggestions can be elicited
#### Constraints
- The report notes a paucity of research on affective development in handicapped children made outlining development a difficult task

### Target Learners
- preschool children, normal and handicapped

### Target Learning Goals
- affective development research planning and assessment
