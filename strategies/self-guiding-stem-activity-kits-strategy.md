# Use self-guiding STEM activity kits for parent-child play to boost some parents' STEM talk

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends self-guiding STEM activity kits designed for parent-child play as a support that helps parents incorporate STEM talk during guided play. It concludes that "Self-guiding STEM activity kits designed for parent-child play are effective for boosting some parents’ STEM talk that has been linked to better STEM outcomes in prior research." The strategy targets parents who may need support to incorporate STEM vocabulary and scientific inquiry talk.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Kits must include guidance features such as embedded stories, parent tips, and extension ideas rather than construction materials alone
#### Constraints
- The benefit was limited to some parents — specifically those with less formal education — and no main effect appeared on STEM talk or directive management overall

### Target Learners
- parents of preschool-aged children

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing parents' STEM talk (STEM vocabulary and scientific inquiry) during joint play
