# Prepare sessions with a topic choice, 20–30 minutes of short pre-reading, an institute-wide invitation email one week ahead, and minimal slides

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article gives a replicable recipe: facilitators select a topic (EDI challenges in STEM or local discrimination issues), identify short articles or videos meant "to ignite curiosity" rather than be comprehensive, send an institute-wide email one week in advance explaining purpose and sharing materials, book a circular-seating room, and prepare minimal slides.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- only an institution-wide email, a room booking, and a session plan; minimal-to-no institutional funding
- participants expect to spend 20 to 30 minutes on preparatory material
- engagement with pre-session material is not required for attendance
#### Constraints
- A busy institute calendar and scheduling conflicts can affect attendance; the authors suggest coordinating with those who set the tone and culture, whose presence signals the work is institutionally valued

### Target Learners
- students, researchers, instructors, and administrators in STEM disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- running structured EDI reflection sessions
