# Use a VR precedent-study environment with narration and abstract concept models as a low-cost substitute for visiting architectural precedents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions adopt the BIM+VR strategy to provide rich learning objectives for design-related and cross-disciplinary studies, since visiting architectural precedents "can become very costly for students and institutions" and local visits involve no layers of interactivity. The prototype pairs precedent exploration with voice/video narration and geometric abstract concept models before an assemblage exercise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A developed VR game environment with precedent model, narration and assemblage tasks
#### Constraints
- Especially important given ongoing pandemic travel restrictions; a larger scale experiment with human subjects is still being developed to test how well learning occurs

### Target Learners
- architecture and construction students
- institutions seeking accessible precedent study

### Target Learning Goals
- architectural precedent study
- understanding of building forms and tectonics
