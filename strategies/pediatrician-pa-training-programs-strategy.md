# Provide pediatricians with adequate information and training on pediatric physical activity recommendations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that programs providing adequate information are needed because pediatricians recommend physical activity but hold limited specific knowledge. It recommends public policies and campaigns to disseminate pediatric recommendations, and raises questions about whether medical curricula and residency prepare pediatricians to give these guidelines safely in clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must provide adequate information on physical activity appropriate for children and adolescents, including duration, strength training, screen time and activities for obese youth
#### Constraints
- The study's convenience sample and 62.9% sampling loss limit external validity, so the knowledge gaps documented may not generalize to all pediatricians

### Target Learners
- pediatricians and medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of pediatric physical activity recommendations for health promotion and obesity prevention
