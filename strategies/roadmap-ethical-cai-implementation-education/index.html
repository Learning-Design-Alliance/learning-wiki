Adopt a roadmap for ethical CAI implementation in education¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review concludes by proposing a roadmap for ethical CAI implementation in education, built on its six-theme framework of responsible-use directions and its identified framework gaps. The article states: "The article concludes by proposing a roadmap for ethical CAI implementation in education and identifying priority areas for future research". Institutions and policymakers can use the roadmap to sequence governance, privacy, integrity, AI-literacy, design, and equity measures when deploying conversational AI agents in educational settings.

Claims¶

  • CAI-in-education research shows a framework gap: no end-to-end design guidance, weak CAI-specific usability methods, unclear pedagogical guidance, and limited AI literacy support [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Establish comprehensive institutional AI governance frameworks including task forces, roadmaps, and transparent policies
  • Adopt ethical safeguards and pedagogically grounded frameworks when deploying GenAI assessment tools
  • Prioritise longitudinal validation, pedagogical grounding, governed adoption of agent infrastructures, and ethics with human oversight in educational agentic AI research
  • Deploy agentic AI in education with transparency about agentic decisions, human oversight and institutional responsibility
  • Justify GenAI integration through a when-how-why framework: proven contexts, specific implementation methods, and pedagogical reasoning

  • Address theoretical grounding, longitudinal study designs, and standardized design protocols before broad implementation of GenAI virtual patients
