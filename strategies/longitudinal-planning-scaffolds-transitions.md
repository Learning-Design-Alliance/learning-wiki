# Embed longitudinal planning scaffolds and time support at semester transitions in preclinical curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because planning and implementation was both the weakest-rated domain and the strongest GPA predictor, the authors recommend moving "beyond isolated SDL workshops toward longitudinal embedding of structured planning exercises, post-exam reflective activities, and iterative learning plans with faculty feedback". They further recommend targeted mid-year support timed to the M1 Spring vulnerable window — advisory sessions, peer-learning communities, or brief SDL reinforcement modules — and a progressive instructional model with closer faculty guidance in Year 1 transitioning to greater learner independence in Year 2, consistent with Grow's staged autonomy framework.
