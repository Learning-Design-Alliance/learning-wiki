# Use short reading passages from supplementary books as springboards for integrated skills practice (retelling, summarizing, role play)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends extending supplementary reading passages into other language skills. After reading, learners can be asked to narrate the story in their own words, give chronological sequences of happenings, paraphrase, or state a summary. Students can also "do the role play, act out some of the story parts, or dramatize the characters in the story," because language skills are interrelated in action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- The article presents this as a recommended practice grounded in the literature rather than as a separately tested manipulation

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension, listening, speaking, and vocabulary development in EFL
