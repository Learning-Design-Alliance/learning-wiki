# Use video-based case analysis of everyday situations to promote perception of mathematics in children's play

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
EmMa works with case studies presented in short video sequences of everyday situations. Based on these video sequences, the perception of mathematical content in specific situations and the interpretation of the situations facing the math skills of the children are promoted. Analysis is initially done in pairs and then in small groups (stimulating cooperation), followed by plenary collection of substantive aspects and discussion of ways of supporting the children's learning processes.
