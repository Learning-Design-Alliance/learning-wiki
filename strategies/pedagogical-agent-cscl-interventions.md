# Pedagogical agent interventions to improve collaborative learning: keep conversation coherent, encourage deliberative discussion, and monitor peer needs

## Description
The article lists example interventions a pedagogical agent can make during collaborative learning: "Attempt to keep the conversation coherent, focusing one topic at a time," help learners "reach a complete correct solution by encouraging further discussion of incomplete or incorrect solutions," and "Encourage deliberative discussion." Agents should also coach learners to interact with peers to increase knowledge and keep learners aware of other learners' needs. These are concrete agent behaviors for facilitating productive group dialogue.
