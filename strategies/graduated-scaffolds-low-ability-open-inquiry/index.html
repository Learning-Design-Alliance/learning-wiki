Support low-ability students in open inquiry with graduated scaffolds such as general procedures and worked examples¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends adapting and modifying open inquiry for students with lower abilities rather than applying it as a direct, fully student-centered method. It suggests "providing general procedures during the initial learning period or allowing students to watch examples and attempt activities before classroom instruction could be beneficial". This makes the highest inquiry level accessible by temporarily structuring the task and withdrawing support as students gain competence.
