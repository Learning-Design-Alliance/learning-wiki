# Deliver case-based learning inside a BOPPPS framework by sending real cases and questions to students before class, quizzing, discussing cases, testing, and having students build mind maps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy implements CBL within the BOPPPS phases for clinical postgraduate teaching. The teacher sends case content and related questions to a We Chat group three days before each course, runs quizzes to gauge understanding and adjust teaching, teaches through real cases to connect knowledge points, tests mastery, and has students summarize with mind maps or knowledge trees. The article states this mode "guides students to think and learn independently by introducing real cases, and cultivates students' clinical critical thinking and problem-solving abilities".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real clinical cases from the department
- A pre-class communication channel such as a We Chat group
- Quizzes and tests for pre- and post-assessment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single-center randomized study with 38 master's students over 12 months; the authors note the sample size is small and the research time is short

### Target Learners
- Clinical postgraduate students (Y2 and Y3 master's students) in oral and maxillofacial surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical speculative thinking ability
- Independent learning and problem-solving ability
- Integration of theoretical knowledge with clinical practice
