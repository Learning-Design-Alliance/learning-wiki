# Require multi-frame mosaics (rather than single exposures) to force planning and note-taking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because an unmodified webcam could image the whole Moon in a single exposure, 'making this lab trivial,' the lens is removed so each frame covers only a small region. To build the mosaic, students must plan their observations and take good notes in an observing log, steps the author says 'align well with the learning objectives of the course.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An imaging setup whose field of view covers only a small region of the target, and a two-session structure with analysis separated in time from observation.
#### Constraints
- Some students ignore the planning and logging steps and cannot complete the mosaic; mosaic-smoothing software assistance is considered beyond the lab's scope.

### Target Learners
- non-major undergraduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific planning and record-keeping practices
