# Colocate oral health services with medical services at the primary care facility to improve access, efficiency, and inter-provider learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Providers identified establishing oral health services at Gugulethu Community Health Centre as the most effective intervention for increasing access. They reasoned that "colocation of care would increase efficiency in care delivery and improve patient experience", that patients referred elsewhere "might get lost to follow-up", and that an on-site dentist would enable case discussion and shared knowledge between specialties, citing their existing benefit from a weekly on-site psychiatrist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space and staffing to host dental services within the medical facility
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as a provider suggestion; the study did not implement or evaluate colocation

### Target Learners
- Medical providers seeking interprofessional learning
- Patients with oral health complaints at primary care facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Appropriate triage and referral of oral health conditions
- Inter-provider knowledge exchange about oral health
