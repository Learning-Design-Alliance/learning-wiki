# Refine answer keys and content instruction, fix platform technical issues, and build an accessible feedback archive based on student feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback, the authors commit to three improvements: "refinement of content instruction and answer keys to enable more specific AI-generated feedback", enhancement of the EduAlly platform to address technical issues, and development of an accessible feedback archive for student future reference. These respond directly to qualitative themes on specificity, technical glitches, and inability to revisit feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teacher-maintained answer keys and rubrics specific enough for the AI to generate context-sensitive feedback
#### Constraints
- The authors note the pilot was at a single institution with a relatively small sample size, limiting generalizability

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students in writing-intensive teacher-education courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improving specificity and usability of AI-generated formative feedback for revision

## Claims

- [Students valued the balance, clarity, specificity, and immediacy of AI feedback, but reported mixed emotions, context-recognition failures, and technical problems](../claims/student-perceptions-of-ai-feedback-mixed.md) [+W]
- [Teachers assigned significantly higher grades than the AI on students' initial submissions, with the AI adhering rigidly to answer keys](../claims/teacher-grades-higher-than-ai-attempt-one.md) [+W]
