# Use podcasts on mobile phones for extensive out-of-class listening practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that learners practice listening with cell phones and extensive podcasts listened to at home or wherever students like, so that listening happens in informal situations without time-limit pressure. Learners download and save podcasts on portable devices and complete course listening exercises anywhere at any time, which the authors link to greater motivation to listen and more practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need access to portable devices capable of playing downloaded podcast audio files
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a twelve-week study with two intermediate EFL courses of female learners aged 12-30

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- listening comprehension
- listening anxiety reduction
- listening practice outside class time
