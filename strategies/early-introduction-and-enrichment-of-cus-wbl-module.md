# Introduce CUS interpretation training early in postgraduate training and enrich WBL modules with videos, Doppler tracings, case-based questions, and mobile access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on trainee feedback and the finding that senior trainees had better pre-test scores from informal exposure, the authors recommend introducing the module earlier in training, with one trainee suggesting it "should be made mandatory to PGY1 residents". Requested improvements included adding live scanning videos, more flow Doppler tracing, more information on long-term pathology outcomes, more case-based questions, and converting the module into a smartphone/tablet app. The experience also inspired planning similar modules for neonatal ventilation and hemodynamics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A needs assessment to align content with trainee-identified gaps; collaboration with an education technology specialist
#### Constraints
- Hands-on scanning skill acquisition was explicitly requested but not covered by this interpretation-focused module

### Target Learners
- junior postgraduate trainees (PGY1 residents)
- pediatrics residents
- neonatal-perinatal medicine fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- neonatal cranial ultrasound interpretation
- earlier acquisition of image interpretation skills
