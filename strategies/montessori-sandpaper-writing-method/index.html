Montessori method: teach writing as a game from ages three to four using sandpaper letters, crayon filling, and progressive tracing¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Madam Montessori teaches writing as a game between three and four years of age, beginning with children tracing with their fingers the forms of letters cut from coarse black sandpaper and pasted on white cards, while learning to read by forming words with the letters. Practice in holding the crayon comes from filling in geometrical forms, and after six or eight weeks children write simple words and sentences.
