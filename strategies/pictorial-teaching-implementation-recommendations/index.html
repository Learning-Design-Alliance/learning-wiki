Implementation recommendations for adopting pictorial teaching at scale¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the findings, the authors propose steps for implementing pictorial teaching beyond a single classroom: teachers should receive "intensive training on how to implement pictorial teaching" before it becomes part of the curriculum; initiatives should be "complemented with support networks and ready-made materials"; and engagement with management is needed because adoption decisions are often made by administrators. They also suggest introducing phrasal verbs at elementary levels so students get used to them in context.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Intensive teacher training before adopting pictorial teaching
  • Support networks and ready-made materials
  • Engagement with the management level

Constraints¶

  • The authors note one key challenge is adopting such approaches where decisions are made by administrators whose primary interest may not be teachers or students

Target Learners¶

  • EFL learners at elementary and pre-intermediate levels

  • learning and active use of phrasal verbs in English
