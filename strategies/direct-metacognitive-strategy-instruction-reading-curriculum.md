# Incorporate explicit direct instruction of metacognitive reading strategies into the reading curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that direct instruction of metacognitive strategies be built into EFL reading lessons: it "should be explicit and relevant with reading tasks and should be prescribed in textbooks which are currently used in secondary schools". Teachers should raise less successful learners' awareness that metacognition can increase academic success and help them construct explicit knowledge of when and where to use appropriate strategies, enabling planning, monitoring, and evaluation of reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction should be explicit and relevant to reading tasks, and embedded in the textbooks currently used in secondary schools
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a small case study in one rural Vietnamese upper secondary school

### Target Learners
- grade-11 EFL learners in Vietnamese upper secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- English reading comprehension through metacognitive strategy use
