# Reform national exams: hold all exams at the end of 12th grade, compress classes into two terms, and have university lecturers set the exams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends moving all national exams, certainly the Physics exam, to the end of 12th grade, using the lighter contact-hour third term as an exam-preparation and thesis-completion period, and having exams "set by university lecturers or researchers, not by school teachers". The rationale is that school teachers have decoupled from contemporary academia, contributing to mismatched student expectations and early drop-out in competitive courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Using the third term (Easter to June/July) free of classes so students can prepare for roughly 4-5 exams and finish theses
#### Constraints
- Entrance interviews for some degrees are acknowledged by the author to entail larger logistical difficulties

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in grades 10-12 aiming at university degrees

### Target Learning Goals
- preparation for university-level study
- accurate expectations of academic disciplines
