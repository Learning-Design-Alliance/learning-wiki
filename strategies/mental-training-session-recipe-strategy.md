# Mental Training session recipe: video instruction, brief practice, individual mental map, subvocal visualization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's Mental Training protocol fits in a 60-minute session: students first receive the same standardized video instruction as the control group, then practice the skill under tutor supervision for 30 minutes. They then "actively developed an individual detailed, stepwise sequence of the performance of a structured facial examination under the supervision and review of the tutor", noting sub-steps on index cards, and finally visualize and internalize every step subvocally for 30 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized video demonstration of the skill, a trained tutor, and index cards for the individual stepwise mental map
#### Constraints
- The authors state this was tested for a structured facial examination and note future studies must investigate for which skills and complexity levels Mental Training is superior

### Target Learners
- 4th-year dentistry students in small groups of five to six

### Target Learning Goals
- performing a structured facial examination correctly and retaining the skill over weeks

### Affordances
- Mental Simulation Theory Neural Pathways
