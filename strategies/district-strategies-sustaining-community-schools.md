# District strategies for sustaining and supporting community schools initiatives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report distills implementation implications for districts adopting community schools: "Districts adopting community school initiatives should consider implementing processes to enable broad-based support among school and community actors; diversifying funding sources; and formalizing plans and commitments through district policy and documentation." Additional recommendations include centralizing partnership processes, increasing cross-sector county collaboration, developing school-level roles such as community school managers and universal systems like COSTs, providing professional learning and coaching, and engaging families in decision-making through outreach, conferences, and inclusive decision-making structures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District capacity to coordinate funding braiding, policy documentation, partnership management, and professional learning
#### Constraints
- The report notes sustaining all supporting initiatives is difficult under budget cuts, as when RJ coordinator positions lost funding

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in community schools

### Target Learning Goals
- whole child outcomes including attendance, achievement, school climate, and family engagement
