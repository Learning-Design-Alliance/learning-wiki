# Three recommendations for improving Tracker-based kinematics instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their reflections, the authors recommend: a) "start using Tracker for easier horizontal kinematics task to lower cognitive loading for novice students", b) use the video modeling pedagogical approach to improve learning targeted at Questions 7 and 8, and c) "integrate tracker use in topics on kinematics, dynamics and work & energy for sustainable and seamless learning". They also recommend design-based research to continually improve the lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher direct instruction of video modeling activities
- Design-based research cycles to refine the lessons
#### Constraints
- The teachers were only aware of the video modeling approach later and were unable to enact it across all three classes in this study

### Target Learners
- novice secondary three physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- kinematics, dynamics, and work-energy concepts
- deepening understanding of velocity-time graphs for varied initial speeds and gravitational accelerations
