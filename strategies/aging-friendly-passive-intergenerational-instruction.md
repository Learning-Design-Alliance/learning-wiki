# Design aging-friendly instruction using structured, explanatory passive content delivered by younger instructors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on both studies' findings, the authors recommend that "digital platforms and group-based learning interventions could be improved by incorporating structured, explanatory content and intergenerational instructional formats", since these adaptations "could foster neural alignment and reciprocal coordination" and promote more effective lifelong learning for aging populations. The strategy pairs the passive instructional format that favored older learners in Study 1 with the younger-instructor pairing that favored them in Study 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors trained to deliver standardized, explanatory scripts at controlled duration and speech rate
#### Constraints
- The observed pattern may be culture-specific given the Confucian tradition of lecture-based learning in China
- Residual differences in teaching style could not be eliminated despite standardized training

### Target Learners
- Older adult learners in senior universities, community education, and digital learning platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual knowledge acquisition in interactive, instructor-led learning

### Affordances
- Ins Hyperscanning Interactive Learning Framework
