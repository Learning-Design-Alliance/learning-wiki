# Run a teacher-led daily voice-only WhatsApp group with compulsory English, daily student-posed questions and reformulation instead of explicit correction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article prescribes an activity design for developing oral skills outside class: "writing was forbidden in order to force learners to speak", English use was compulsory, a different student posed a daily question, each student answered at least once per question, image-sharing was allowed, and the teacher participated as a student. Errors were not corrected explicitly; reformulated parts were emphasized to help learners notice them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students must have smartphones with the WhatsApp application downloaded
- The teacher participates actively in the chat as a participant
#### Constraints
- Explicit correction was deliberately avoided; the design targets a synthetic interaction environment rather than accuracy instruction

### Target Learners
- University students at B1 English level

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 speaking proficiency
- negotiation of meaning
- pronunciation repair
