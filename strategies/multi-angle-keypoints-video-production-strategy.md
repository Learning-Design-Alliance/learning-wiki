# Use multiple camera angles, key points, enlargements, and replays to depict essential examination aspects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (3 causal, 1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The video production strategy highlights essential assessment aspects through specific filming and editing choices: "Various camera angles, key points, image enlargements, and replays were used to better depict essential aspects of the assessment." Examples include a second camera perspective for reading sagittal measurements, enlarged pictures for marking reference points, and on-screen display of muscle names and key terms such as pain and referred pain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Background noise reduced to a minimum
- Key terms displayed and repeated during the examination
#### Constraints
- A narrator was not included due to the extra length it would have added to the video

### Target Learners
- dental and medical students
- medical and dental professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding examination techniques and verbal commands
- reading measurement values correctly
