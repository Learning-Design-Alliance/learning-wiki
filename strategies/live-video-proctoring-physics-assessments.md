# Live-proctor virtual assessments via video conference to address academic integrity in physics problem-solving exams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A real-time proctoring procedure for virtual physics assessments: quizzes and exams are proctored live over a video conference platform, "Students were asked to frame the working area to the camera", audio is unmuted with no headphones so students cannot discuss problems, and only the provided equation sheet is allowed. The authors report this minimizes or eliminates academic integrity concerns that software-based proctoring cannot address for open-ended physics problem-solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video conference platform with camera and audio for each student (Webex was used)
- Live instructor monitoring throughout the assessment
#### Constraints
- Standard software-based proctoring methods do not work for virtual calculus-based physics because assessments are based on open-ended detail problem-solving

### Target Learners
- students in virtual-synchronous calculus-based physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- academic integrity in online assessment of open-ended problem-solving
