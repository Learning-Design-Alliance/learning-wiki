# Limit pre-class video length to 20 minutes to reduce student burden

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that pre-class videos "cannot be too long and should be restricted to 20 min, which could be acceptable for most students". This strategy addresses the preparation burden that may cause negative student attitudes toward flipped classrooms. It is drawn from the authors' discussion of influential factors for flipped classroom effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-class video materials must be prepared in advance and kept within the recommended duration
#### Constraints
- Excessive preparation time and resource requirements may negatively impact effectiveness and student attitudes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate clinical medicine students

### Target Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge
- Practical clinical skills
