# Use a synthesized set of research questions to define best practices for future VR development and implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors synthesized a set of research questions (Table 2) that may help define best practices for future VR development and implementation, organized by the four themes: focus on cognitive skills, access to education, resource investment, and balancing immersion. Each question has an example study idea to demonstrate how researchers might approach it with different methodologies and paradigms. The examples are explicitly not meant to be comprehensive or prescriptive.
