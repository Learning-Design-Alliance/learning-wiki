# Employing college students as paid on-stage presenters to make physics relatable to young audiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A staffing strategy in which community college students, paid from the show budget, perform on stage explaining physics. The authors state this "puts people on the stage who are more relatable to the young attendees" and "reinforces the idea that they can do physics when they get older." Over fifty students have served as performers, and many recent presenters first saw the show as children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Budget to pay students, plus faculty time for training and rehearsing presenters
#### Constraints
- The authors note this involves the additional tasks of training and rehearsing with student presenters

### Target Learners
- Community college students as presenters; elementary and middle school children as audience

### Target Learning Goals
- Physics identity and the sense that attendees can do physics when older
