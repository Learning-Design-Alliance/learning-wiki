# Emotional Walking Game

## Description
The Emotional Walking Game involves learners walking around while embodying different emotions upon hearing a signal. This activity combines physical activity with emotional expression, helping learners connect emotions with physical movements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an open space for walking. A facilitator to guide the game and provide signals.
#### Constraints
- Requires physical mobility. Some learners may be uncomfortable expressing emotions through movement.

- Combines physical activity with emotional learning. Encourages kinesthetic learners and promotes emotional awareness through movement.

### Personalization
- Emotions can be tailored to suit the group's needs and interests. Different signals (e.g., verbal cues, music) can be used.
