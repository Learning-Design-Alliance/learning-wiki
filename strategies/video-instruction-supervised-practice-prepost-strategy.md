# Pair a pre-test anastomosis attempt with standardized video instruction and supervised practice before a post-test on the same simulator

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training protocol had residents first perform an end-to-end anastomosis without prior instruction, then receive "a 10-minute instructional video demonstrating fundamental principles of vascular anastomosis, including needle handling, suture spacing, and knot security", followed by supervised practice on the same platform used for assessment, then repeat the procedure. This standardized sequence was associated with improved performance outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identical instructional content and practice format for all participants, with supervision by an experienced instructor
#### Constraints
- The single-group pre–post design cannot fully distinguish the effects of the educational intervention from immediate familiarization with the simulator

### Target Learners
- general surgery residents

### Target Learning Goals
- end-to-end vascular anastomosis technique
