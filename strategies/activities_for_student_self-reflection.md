# Activities for Student Self-Reflection

## Description
This strategy involves creating activities that allow students to reflect on their own learning. Encourage students to reflect on their learning by engaging in activities that promote metacognition. This could involve writing summaries using prompts, creating concept maps or videos, or writing a math autobiography.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities that promote metacognition, a way for students to record their reflections, and a willingness to allow students to choose the activities that they find most engaging.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to create and facilitate activities that promote metacognition.
