# Scoring-format variations to shift the assessment toward formative or competitive functions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes varying the scoring format to stress different aspects of the Quiz. Offering full credit in both the individual and collaborative phases "could make the assessment more formative", with development predicated on students reflecting on which phase determined the correct answer. Conversely, tracking which partners provide the greatest benefits to collaborators across many assessments would make the Quiz more competitive and summative. Quantitative questions are also suggested as possibly holding significant formative potential.
