Implement mathematics instruction with Model Eliciting Activities combined with Cabri 3D¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that mathematics teachers implement instruction with Model Eliciting Activities with Cabri 3D. In this approach students construct mathematical concepts through realistic problems while using Cabri 3D to manipulate and visualize figures; students are also given opportunities to discuss with classmates, create plans, and determine appropriate steps to solve mathematical problems. The recommendation follows directly from the study's finding that MEAC students showed better spatial reasoning improvement.
