# Survey students on preferences before designing instructional videos

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before developing the videos, the authors administered a short questionnaire to students about their perceptions and preferences regarding instructional videos, such as narrative and visual content, sequencing and length. Findings showed "dynamic online videos would be the most preferred instrument to complement regular lectures." Videos were then developed in accordance with students' preferences and empirical literature on video design characteristics.
