# Provide digestible treatments of recent QIS research developments for non-specialist instructors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Because many introductory QIS instructors are non-specialists, the article recommends accessible scientific communication of novel results aimed at a non-specialist but technical audience. Franz, a CS-department instructor, reports: "It's not easy for me (as a non-specialist) to read the primary literature, and it's really difﬁcult to make it accessible to my undergraduate CS audience." He suggests "digestible treatments of recent research developments" as a tool he would like, worrying his curriculum may be out of date without knowing it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Keeping introductory QIS curricula current with recent research developments
