# Implement REACT through hands-on and minds-on geography assignments linking content to real-world contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe is to teach geography through REACT by assigning hands-on and minds-on activities tied to real contexts: drawing maps of natural resource distribution, analyzing data on the potential of natural resources in Indonesia, and writing papers, so that learners "have the opportunity to learn abstract concepts to be more concrete". Teachers are advised, citing Davtyan (2014), to relate content to the real world, have students experience and apply new knowledge, communicate in problem solving, and transfer knowledge to future experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning resources such as globes, maps, and observation data tables and charts; authentic assessment; active learner roles as discussion participants, presenters and project implementers
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one grade XI class in a single school (SMAN 15 Surabaya) studying mineral distribution material; the article does not test the strategy across other topics or schools

### Target Learners
- high school geography students (grade XI)

### Target Learning Goals
- geography skills
- connecting abstract concepts to concrete experience
- communication and cooperation skills
