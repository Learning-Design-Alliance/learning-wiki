# Address resource constraints and design quality when implementing virtual laboratories in low- and middle-income contexts

## Description
The review notes that two-thirds of included studies were conducted in high-income countries, so findings may not cover challenges facing low- and middle-income contexts. It states that "limited internet access, lack of digital tools, lower digital literacy levels, and inadequate technical support can significantly affect the feasibility and effectiveness of virtual laboratories," and calls for "further research on adaptable and affordable virtual laboratory models matched to resource-constrained environments."
