# Dose simulation training at about 29 procedures with focused early practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training-dosage recommendation derived from the learning curve results: "This information is valuable for designing training programs, suggesting that around 29 procedures should be sufficient for most trainees to achieve competency." Because "The data also highlights the rapid improvement in needle insertion time with initial practice, suggesting that focused training early on is crucial," programs should concentrate practice early and verify competency individually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to a validity-supported simulator for the target procedure
- learning curve monitoring to confirm when individual competency is reached
#### Constraints
- prior experience with invasive prenatal procedures reduces the number of procedures needed
- repetitive task practice "may not always be feasible in clinical settings"

### Target Learners
- novice surgeons and maternal–fetal medicine trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- achieving technical competency in RFA for TRAPS with efficient training dosage
