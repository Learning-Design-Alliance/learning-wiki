# Use professional exchange programs and technology-supported CME to extend ID training beyond local provision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose two complementary routes to widen access to ID training: professional exchange programs such as those provided by EFPT and EPA, which "offer a means to complement the training curricula where opportunities are not available locally", and quality Continuous Medical Education delivered through new technologies after postgraduate training, which can increase the reach of ID training while overcoming location and economic barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to exchange program infrastructure (EFPT, EPA) and to technology platforms for CME delivery
#### Constraints
- Intended to complement, not replace, national curricula where local opportunities are unavailable

### Target Learners
- psychiatric trainees and practising psychiatrists in Europe

### Target Learning Goals
- intellectual disability psychiatry knowledge and skills
