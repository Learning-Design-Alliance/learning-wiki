# Use multi-college consortia funded through TAACCCT to build career pathways for dislocated and low-skilled workers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy is a federal grant approach in which community colleges and groups of institutions working as consortia improve education, employment, and training outcomes for economically dislocated and low-skilled adult workers. The report states the Department of Labor "is funding an ambitious, multi-pronged effort encouraging community colleges and groups of institutions working as consortia" under the TAACCCT grants program. Documenting baseline program models may inform potential replication.
