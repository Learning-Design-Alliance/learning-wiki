Combine individual adaptive reinforcement with collaborative approaches in a hybrid model for future development¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

As a future research direction, the article proposes linking individual adaptive reinforcement learning with CSCL scripts, where the collaborative learning environment is enhanced. It states that "A new hybrid model that combines both strengths could improve individual concept deficiencies while promoting collaborative skills and peer learning in user-friendly interfaces, such as Genially.com or Canva." This is a forward-looking recommendation rather than a tested design.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A collaborative learning environment enhanced by CSCL scripts combined with the individual adaptive reinforcement model
