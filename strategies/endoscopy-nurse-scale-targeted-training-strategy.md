# Use the validated competency scale to identify strengths and weaknesses and build targeted training and development plans for endoscopy nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends applying the validated 41-item scale as a management tool for competency evaluation in endoscopy nursing. It states that "By identifying strengths and weaknesses, this scale facilitates the development of targeted training and development plans, ultimately enhancing nursing practice and improving overall care quality." The scale also supports objective staff selection and human resource allocation, and it offers a more standardized alternative to subjective evaluations or generic competency checklists that may not capture specialty-specific skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated, standardized specialty-specific assessment tool (the 41-item, 5-dimension scale) administered to nursing staff in endoscopy centers
#### Constraints
- All respondents were drawn from tertiary hospitals in China, which the authors state may limit external validity across cultures and regions

### Target Learners
- Gastroenterology endoscopy nurses assessed for core competency development

### Target Learning Goals
- Targeted professional development in the five competency dimensions, including technical skills and soft skills such as communication and teamwork
