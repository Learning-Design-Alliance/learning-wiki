# Use escape room assessment results as a needs assessment to target follow-up research education workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using escape room evaluation results diagnostically: after the game served as a comprehensive needs assessment, the team ran targeted research education workshops for participants. These workshops "were designed to address specific gaps and build on the strengths identified during the escape room activity", providing a tailored educational experience intended to further enhance research competencies beyond the assessment itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An escape room assessment producing station-level or competency-level performance data; capacity to run follow-up workshops
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated in a single-institution context with voluntary participants, mostly medical students

### Target Learners
- health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- research competencies identified as gaps during gameplay
