Scaffolded AI integration with teacher training and reflective tasks to reduce over-reliance¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends that Arab universities integrate AI tools into EL2 instruction in a scaffolded way, pairing AI use with teacher training and reflective tasks so that learners critically engage with AI output rather than depend on it. It states the paper "recommends scaffolded integration, teacher training, reflective tasks to reduce over-reliance on AI tools." Across the reviewed studies, researchers advocated integrating AI while simultaneously developing learners' critical thinking, ethical awareness, and responsible AI-use practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training and AI literacy programs so educators can guide student interactions with AI systems

Constraints¶

  • The review notes most supporting studies are perception-based, with few controlled interventions measuring actual learning gains

Target Learners¶

  • EL2 learners in Arab university classrooms
  • ELT instructors in Arab higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English writing, vocabulary, grammar, and speaking skills
  • critical and ethical engagement with AI-generated content

Related Strategies¶

  • Balance human and automated feedback to optimize writing skill development
  • Design curricula giving students opportunities to critically engage with AI and AI-generated content through validation, interpretation, and revision tasks
  • Manage students' over-reliance on ChatGPT by having teachers and students double-check AI-provided information against other sources and structuring classroom interactions
  • Use ChatGPT as a supplementary tool for grammar, writing, and conversational practice so teachers can focus on higher-order thinking skills, while integrating human interaction to offset emotional detachment
  • Design AI-integration activities that set explicit expectations for AI-assisted work, integrate reflective assessments of AI outputs, and build tasks strengthening critical thinking and academic integrity
  • Train teachers in prompt engineering, evaluating AI-generated responses, ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making
