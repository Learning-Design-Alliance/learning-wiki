# Use behavior ratings to provide feedback to students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Recommendation 7 has teachers use behavior ratings, such as a Daily Behavior Report Card (DBRC) template, to give students feedback on their behavior. The introduction states that Recommendations 6 and 7 "use behavior ratings to provide feedback to their peers." Table I.1 assigns this recommendation a minimal level of evidence, meaning evidence may not meet WWC standards or may exhibit inconsistencies.
