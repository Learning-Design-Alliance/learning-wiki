# Prediction Relay

## Description
A PALS activity that emphasizes making predictions while reading. Students each read a paragraph and then predict what will happen next, which helps develop reading comprehension and expand their Vocabulary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material, trained students, timer
#### Constraints
- Requires clear instructions, effectiveness depends on active peer support.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop prediction skills, improve reading comprehension, expand vocabulary, practice summarizing

### Affordances
- Engaging activity that encourages active reading and critical thinking, peer support for generating predictions.

### Personalization
- Texts can be adjusted to reading levels; prompts can be varied to encourage different types of predictions.
