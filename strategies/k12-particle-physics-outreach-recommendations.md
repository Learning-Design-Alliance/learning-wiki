# Recommendations for K-12 particle physics outreach programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report's K-12 recommendations call for a community forum open to physicists, engineers, technicians, computer scientists and secondary school educators; local needs-assessment sessions with teachers; and programs that "prioritize exposing all students to age appropriate STEM topics" while designers "be mindful of bias in program designs that might filter student participation by race, gender, or socioeconomic background". It further urges engagement with sister STEM fields on science communication, funding including compensation for organizers, and acknowledgment and reward for participants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding agencies should provide funding for outreach activities including compensation for those who organize and run them.
#### Constraints
- Particle physicists alone cannot restructure or change the purpose of the high school physics curriculum set by textbook publishers and state adopters.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific literacy
- equitable access to physics
- understanding how scientists develop knowledge
