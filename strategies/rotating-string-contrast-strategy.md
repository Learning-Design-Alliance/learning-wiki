# Contrast the sliding bead with the familiar fixed-bead-on-rotating-string problem to connect the puzzling case to prior knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The instructor brings up the high-school problem of a fixed bead at the end of a uniformly rotating string, analyzed from the inertial frame without invoking centrifugal force. There, a radially inward force produces no radial motion — 'exactly the opposite scenario of the sliding bead on the rod problem where there is no radial force but there is non trivial radial motion.' This contrast helps students connect the puzzling situation to a familiar one and reiterates that the force–motion relation can be non-trivial.
