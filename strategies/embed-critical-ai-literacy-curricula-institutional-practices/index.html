Embed critical AI literacy into curricula and institutional practices in higher education¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The editorial's forward-looking recommendation is that intellectuals in higher education lead a transformative agenda by "embedding critical AI literacy into curricula and institutional practices". The stated aim is to ensure AI serves as a tool for enhancing human insight and social justice rather than perpetuating technological illusions. This is the article's own call to action, offered as an editorial recommendation rather than a tested intervention.

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed AI policy literacy, peer-led communities of practice, and shared AI resources into professional development
  • Fund AI literacy initiatives integrating AI ethics into curricula and adapting assessments
  • Build ethical AI integration on four coordinated levels: faculty professional development, ethics curricula, clear institutional guidelines, and interdisciplinary updating
  • Embed AI literacy across disciplines by identifying intersections with existing content and using case studies, interdisciplinary modules, and collaborative projects
  • Embed AIL across disciplines by adding modules to existing Digital Literacy courses and offering interdisciplinary AI ethics and legal seminars
