# Advise PER graduate students on career paths and increase the field's visibility to undergraduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendations target three needs: making PER visible to undergraduates, encouraging collaboration across departments and research settings, and actively advising graduate students about careers. The authors state "we should also be actively advising our graduate students as to what career paths exist for them and what they might find most fulfilling," and argue that informing undergraduates about PER could shift recruitment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PER faculty and programs must communicate the existence of PER and available career paths to undergraduates and graduate students
#### Constraints
- The authors note increasing tenured PER positions will likely also be needed, and that their data do not directly address undergraduate awareness or employment outcomes

### Target Learners
- physics undergraduate students
- graduate students in physics education research

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of PER as a field of inquiry
- informed career decision-making for PER graduate students
