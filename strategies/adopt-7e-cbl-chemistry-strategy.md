# Adopt constructivist/inquiry strategies (7E and CBL) in chemistry teaching with adequate teacher preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the article recommends that science teachers, especially chemistry teachers, "should employ modern, practical teaching strategies such as the 7E learning cycle model and case -based learning that allow students to construct their own knowledge and actively partic ipate in the learning process." It further recommends equipping teachers with the skills to create environments where all kinds of students learn meaningfully individually or in groups, reviewing the chemistry curriculum to accommodate learner-centered strategies, and providing laboratory facilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers adequately equipped with the skills needed for meaningful individual or group learning
- Teachers intending to use 7E thoroughly trained on the seven steps within a reasonable timeframe
#### Constraints
- 7E requires more class-time and teacher expertise; CBL is cheaper, requires lesser class time, and is easier to execute

### Target Learners
- senior secondary school chemistry students

### Target Learning Goals
- improved chemistry achievement
- enhanced attitudes toward chemistry
