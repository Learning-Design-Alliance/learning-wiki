# Valuing the Learning Process

## Description
Valuing the learning process involves emphasizing the importance of understanding, exploration, and growth over achieving high grades. Instructors can promote a growth mindset by focusing on the journey of learning, encouraging curiosity, and celebrating progress rather than solely focusing on outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a shift in emphasis from grades to learning. Requires instructors to create a supportive and encouraging learning environment.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging in systems that heavily emphasize grades. Requires a change in mindset for both instructors and learners.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote a growth mindset, encourage curiosity, foster a love of learning

### Affordances
- Encourages learners to focus on understanding rather than just memorization. Promotes intrinsic motivation and a love of learning. Helps learners see mistakes as opportunities for growth.

### Personalization
- Activities can be designed to cater to individual learners' interests and learning styles. Instructors can provide different types of support based on individual needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement and enthusiasm for learning. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward grades. Measure learners' ability to explain concepts and apply knowledge.

## Impact
- Research suggests that valuing the learning process can lead to increased motivation, improved understanding, and a greater love of learning. Learners who value the process are more likely to persist in the face of challenges and achieve long-term success.
