# Develop surgeon-specific surgical preference card libraries with regular updates and team collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals and educational managers implement preference cards as clinical and educational tools through surgeon-specific libraries. As the conclusion states, "One effective strategy could be the development of surgeon-specific SPC libraries, where preference cards are tailored to the unique needs and practices of individual surgeons." The authors add that regular updates and collaboration between surgeons, operating room staff and educators are essential to maintain effectiveness, and that this approach improves staff preparedness and coordination during surgeries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tailoring of cards to each surgeon's unique needs and practices
- Regular updates to keep card content accurate as techniques and preferences change
- Collaboration between surgeons, operating room staff and educators
#### Constraints
- The authors note implementation barriers including surgeons' reluctance or lack of awareness of the educational value of SPCs, and that cards are often not well-maintained or updated in practice

### Target Learners
- Operating room nursing students
- Operating room personnel

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparedness and coordination for surgical procedures
- Clinical competence in perioperative care
