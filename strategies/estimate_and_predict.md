# Estimate and Predict

## Description
Learners use estimation and prediction skills by asking how they might do on an upcoming project or how long it might take them to complete a task or assignment. They can record their estimates and then reflect on the accuracy of their predictions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks or projects for estimation, a method for recording estimates and actual outcomes, and time for reflection.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent effort and may not be effective if students are not motivated to reflect honestly.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students who need to improve their ability to estimate their performance and time management skills.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance self-awareness, improve accuracy in self-assessment, and develop realistic expectations about task completion.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured method for self-assessment and reflection. Helps students develop realistic expectations about their abilities and task completion.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to different tasks and subject areas. The level of detail in the estimates and reflections can be adjusted to suit the student’s needs.
