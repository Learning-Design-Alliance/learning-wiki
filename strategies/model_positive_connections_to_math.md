# Model Positive Connections to Math

## Description
Model positive connections to math is a strategy in which educators explicitly demonstrate curiosity, usefulness, and positive affect toward mathematical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent teacher language that frames math as meaningful and learnable.
- Examples that connect math to authentic situations and identities.
#### Constraints
- Surface positivity without support for success can feel inauthentic.

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve learner confidence, belonging, and willingness to engage with mathematics.

### Instructions
- Narrate positive mathematical habits, such as persistence and sensemaking.
- Connect tasks to real uses and learner interests.
- Respond to mistakes as part of learning rather than as failure.
