# Establish an inclusive AI task force before revising responsible use policies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before revising responsible/acceptable use policies, districts should establish an AI task force: "A group of people who purposefully meet to address a specific topic and accomplish a defined objective." The AI task force "Provides feedback on AI-related policies and practices during their development and ongoing evaluation as emerging technologies change." Task forces should be inclusive of community members, learners, educators, and guardians, and districts must secure resources to raise awareness and empower others to become learners about AI.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts need to secure resources necessary to raise awareness and empower others to become learners about AI and emerging technologies
- Task forces should aim to be inclusive at all levels including community members, learners, educators, and guardians
#### Constraints
- The deck recommends acknowledging that everyone is at different stages of experience and use, and focusing on adult use initially, then other audiences

### Target Learners
- school district leaders
- educators
- learners
- guardians
- community members

### Target Learning Goals
- participating in AI policy development and co-learning about emerging technologies
