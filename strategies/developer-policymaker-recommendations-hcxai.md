# Developers should prioritize user understanding and bias mitigation, while policymakers should mandate explainability, accountability, and educator AI training

## Description
The paper assigns developers the duties of user-understanding-first design, diverse explanation strategies, iterative testing with educators and students, and active bias identification and mitigation. It assigns policymakers the duty to "develop guidelines and regulations" emphasizing explainability and accountability, establish ethical standards, and fund educator training in AI so classroom AI use remains informed and responsible.
