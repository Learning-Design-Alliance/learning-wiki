# Adapt the POE (predict-observe-explain) method to smartphone-based physics experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy adapts the POE method so that students first predict an experiment's outcome, then collect data with smartphone sensors, and finally explain the phenomenon theoretically. The article states "teachers are encouraged to adapt the POE method (predict-observe-explain) for smartphone-based experiments" and argues that using this method with familiar smartphone technology yields improvements in conceptual understanding of the underlying phenomena.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need access to smartphone sensors and suitable data-collection apps to collect the observational data
#### Constraints
- The article grounds the effectiveness of smartphone experiments in a single cited investigation on acceleration concepts

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and high school introductory physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of physical phenomena
- critical deductive thinking in experiment design
