# Transform Mesnevî stories into structured classroom activities ending with homework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researchers converted each Mesnevî story into classroom events by asking students questions about the lesson to be taken from the story and structuring activities under fixed headings. The article states that "all the stories were transformed into events under the headings of brainstorming, sentence completion, drama, box of values, our teacher talking, values tree enthusiasts, and at the end of the events homework was given to the students." This recipe operationalised story-based values education across all five target values.
