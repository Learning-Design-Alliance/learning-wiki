# Examine longitudinal impact of phonics interventions on reading comprehension and study phonological awareness instruction at the prekindergarten level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 2 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The review recommends that "future studies of phonics interventions should examine not only the immediate eﬀects of instruction on word-level reading skills but also the longitudinal impact on reading comprehension outcomes." It further recommends that future intervention research examine the implementation of phonological and phonemic awareness instruction, particularly among deaf children at the prekindergarten level, using existing curricula or readily available interventions to permit study replications.
