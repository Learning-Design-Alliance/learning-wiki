# Highlighting and Exploration of Patterns, Critical Features, Big Ideas, and Relationships

## Description
Accentuation of important information and its relationship to the learning goal. This involves emphasizing key patterns, critical features, big ideas, and relationships within the content. Highlighting and exploring these elements helps learners focus on the most important information and understand how it relates to the overall learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Visual aids, graphic organizers, concept maps, and simplified explanations.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of information to highlight. Some learners may become overly reliant on highlighted information.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance understanding, improve retention, focus attention, clarify learning goals

### Affordances
- Focuses attention on the most important information. Enhances understanding of key concepts and relationships. Clarifies the learning goals.

### Personalization
- Use highlighting, underlining, and other visual cues to emphasize important information. Provide graphic organizers and concept maps to illustrate relationships. Break down complex concepts into simpler components.
