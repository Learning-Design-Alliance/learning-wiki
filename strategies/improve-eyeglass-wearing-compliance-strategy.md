# Future vision programs should focus on improving eyeglass-wearing compliance, which was low in both provinces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes that although partial compliance did not drive the between-province difference, compliance with wearing eyeglasses was low: only 38.4% in Gansu and 41.6% in Shaanxi treatment schools at endline. It states that "vision programs need to focus on improving compliance" and that more research is needed on why compliance is low and how to improve it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must address the misunderstanding among students, parents, and teachers about the importance and safeness of wearing eyeglasses that the article identifies as a driver of partial compliance.
#### Constraints
- The article notes more research is needed to understand why compliance is low and how to improve it.

### Target Learners
- Myopic primary-school students in rural China who receive free eyeglasses

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained eyeglass wearing to support seeing learning materials such as the blackboard
