# Package labs in an auto-graded online manual with a cost-return sustainability model to support scaling and long-term operations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
OPIS! partnered with Cengage/WebAssign to package the labs into an interactive, maximally auto-graded online manual/text with an ISBN that integrates into standard LMSs. Most participating institutions choose it for ease of use and simplified grading of large numbers of students. A portion of the typical $65/student charge returns to UNC-Chapel Hill to support telescope and software operations, giving the curriculum "a long -term sustainability model tha t has allowed it to survive grant funding cycles" while Skynet costs ≈$350K/year to operate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Short answers, and submitted images and graphs must still be graded manually; only numerical responses and calculations are evaluated automatically.
#### Constraints
- Cengage/WebAssign charges for the product, typically $65/student.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate introductory astronomy students at adopting institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Completing and submitting lab measurements and calculations at scale
