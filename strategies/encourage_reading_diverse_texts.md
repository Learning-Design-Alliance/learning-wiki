# Encourage Reading Diverse Texts

## Description
Learners read diverse texts and then think and talk about different genres to acquire academic language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a variety of texts.
#### Constraints
- Learners may need guidance in selecting appropriate texts.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->

### Affordances
- Builds vocabulary, increases comprehension, enhances understanding of genres

### Personalization
- Learners can choose texts based on their interests and needs.

- Reading and then thinking and talking about different genres is a robust sequence for learning academic language.
