# Language Experience Approach

## Description
The language experience approach is a whole language method that integrates the development of reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills through the use of learners' experiences. Learners share their experiences, and the instructor records them verbatim. The recorded experience is then used as the basis for reading and writing activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners' experiences. Instructor to record and transcribe the experiences. Materials for reading and writing activities (e.g., paper, pencils, books).
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming. Relies on the instructor's ability to record and transcribe accurately. The quality of the experience shared can impact the effectiveness of the approach.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly effective for young learners and English language learners.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills. Connect learning to learners' experiences. Build confidence and motivation.

### Affordances
- Connects learning to learners' experiences. Uses accessible vocabulary and syntax. Integrates multiple language skills. Builds confidence and motivation. Supports cultural and life experiences connections.

### Personalization
- Experiences shared can be personalized to the learners' backgrounds and interests. Activities can be adapted to learners' language proficiency levels.
