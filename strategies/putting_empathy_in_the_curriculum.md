# Putting Empathy in the Curriculum

## Description
Explicitly discuss empathy with students and integrate it into existing lessons. Define empathy as a class. Identify characters in stories, novels, or plays that demonstrate empathy. Use the design-thinking model for empathizing with the user by identifying their thoughts, feelings, values, and worries. When requiring a presentation, ask students to empathize with their audience. When introducing behavioral expectations, role-play different scenarios and brainstorm how empathy could change or shape those scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration into lesson plans, access to relevant resources (stories, design-thinking models), time for role-playing and discussion
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and integration, may be challenging in overly structured curricula, and depends on student engagement.
