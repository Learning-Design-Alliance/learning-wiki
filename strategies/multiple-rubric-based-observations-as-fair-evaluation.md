# Use multiple classroom observations with rubric-based ratings and evidence as the evaluation component teachers perceive as fair

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The REACH design observes teachers on multiple occasions rather than once, rates observations against a detailed rubric requiring evaluators to provide evidence for each rating, and pairs this with pre- and post-observation conferences. Teachers consistently perceived this observation component as fair and reported using the resulting feedback, in contrast to their negative perceptions of student growth measures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluators must be certified (in CPS, only principals and assistant principals) and must provide evidence for each rating assigned.
#### Constraints
- The observation process was also reported to increase teacher stress and to demand substantial administrator time.

### Target Learners
- K-12 classroom teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving teaching practice through observation feedback and reflection
