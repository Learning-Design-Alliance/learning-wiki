# Use drill skill model for teaching physics concepts requiring practice and skill development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the drill skill model produced better achievement, the article recommends it as "an appropriate model for teaching and improve learning achievement of science-physics especially for students of 8th grade" at the junior high school level. The model directs students to exercise their individual abilities and skills, which the authors suggest increases motivation and positively influences learning achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students carry out training activities on what they have learned
- Application of theory to practice with student feedback recognized and increased
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is specific to light concept material for 8th grade students in this study's context

### Target Learners
- 8th grade junior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Physics learning achievement
- Understanding subject matter
- Individual ability and skill development
