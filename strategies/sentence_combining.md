# Sentence Combining

## Description
Practice how to combine two simple sentences into a compound or complex sentence.  Use this technique in conjunction with other effective writing techniques, such as encouraging peer discussion as part of collaborative writing, to help reinforce the practice. Has a positive impact on overall writing quality and can boost learners’ reading comprehension skills as well.
