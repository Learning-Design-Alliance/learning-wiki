# Use repeated measurements by two students to surface disagreements about measurement rules

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends that repeated measurements by two students are usually more valuable than a long derivation because they surface exactly the disagreements that matter within this lab set: galaxy edge choice, Jupiter-center choice, planet boundary choice, limb definition, crater-count threshold, and whether a sunspot group is counted as one feature or several. This is an implementable classroom activity recipe grounded in the author's design experience.
