# Prioritize comprehensive, tailored AI training programs covering technical skills and ethical considerations such as data privacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions and ministries provide comprehensive training programs for teachers on AI in pronunciation instruction. It states it is essential to "prioritize comprehensive training programs that address both the technical aspects of AI tools and ethical considerations, such as data privacy." Programs should be tailored to teachers' varying qualifications and experience, and primary-school strategies should raise awareness of pronunciation and use AI tools designed for younger learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address both technical aspects of AI tools and ethical considerations such as data privacy, and be tailored to teachers' diverse needs and levels of qualification and experience.
#### Constraints
- The article notes many teachers currently receive limited training, and the study's evidence comes from the local context of Cyprus.

### Target Learners
- In-service EFL teachers in primary, secondary, and adult education

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective integration of AI tools in pronunciation instruction
