# Use AI as a tool for critique and refinement rather than a substitute for student work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The article recommends structuring assessment tasks so students critique, refine, validate, and contextualize AI-generated outputs instead of merely generating content with AI. As stated, "Using AI as a tool for critique and refinement, rather than a substitute for student work, may help educators prepare learners for responsible AI use". Concrete instances include students refining AI-generated clinical coding or SQL outputs against standards-based frameworks and critically evaluating AI-generated business proposals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator supervision and structured task framing so refinement targets discipline standards and conventions
#### Constraints
- The pilot examined only final AI outputs and did not evaluate AI as a formative learning partner in iterative critique workflows

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and master's-level digital health and health information management students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of AI outputs
- applied clinical coding proficiency
- responsible and ethical AI use
