## Description
The article states that faculty members play a crucial role as role models, and that "making their own research visible and accessible to students can strengthen the perceived relevance of research in education and foster a stronger sense of scholarly identity." This positions faculty as scholarly role models shaping students' academic identity within the teaching–research nexus.
