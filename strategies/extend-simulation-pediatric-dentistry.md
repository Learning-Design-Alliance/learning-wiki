# Extend high-fidelity clinical simulation to other Dentistry curriculum areas such as pediatric dentistry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending the approach beyond special care dentistry: "future research could explore the implementation of high-fidelity clinical simulation in other areas of the Dentistry curriculum, such as pediatric dentistry", where simulation "could help students practice managing pediatric patients with behavioral issues or learn how to appropriately interact with the children’s family members".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation scenarios adapted to the new clinical area, such as pediatric behavioral management and interaction with family members
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as future research, not as a tested result

### Target Learners
- Dentistry students

### Target Learning Goals
- managing pediatric patients with behavioral issues
- interacting with children’s family members
