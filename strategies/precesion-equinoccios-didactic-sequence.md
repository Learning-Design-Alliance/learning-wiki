# Progressive astronomy didactic sequence on precession of the equinoxes for upper secondary teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-activity sequence that introduces the precession of the equinoxes progressively, building on a prior sequence about day-night, seasons and Earth's axial tilt. The author states the theme central 'a ser presentado en forma progresiva, es el movimiento de precesión de los equinoccios' and warns the new activities should not be attempted without the earlier ones. It is intended for teachers to take into the classroom regardless of school year or socio-economic level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should first familiarize themselves with the first part of the sequence ('El movimiento de los cielos') before using this one
- Materials: the previously built mini solar system model, a 12cm foam sphere, a 16cm wooden rod, a spinning top and small water balloons
#### Constraints
- Aimed at students in the last years of secondary school with some disposition and training for abstract, reflective thinking and model construction
- Sky observation is largely absent because the topics addressed are imperceptible on human timescales

### Target Learners
- upper secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the precession of the equinoxes
- understanding Earth's real shape and axial wobble
- understanding the zodiac and seasonal constellations
