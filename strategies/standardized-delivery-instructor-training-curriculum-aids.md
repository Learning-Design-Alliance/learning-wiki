# Standardize delivery of a new teaching model via instructor training, standardized curriculum and shared teaching aids

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
To ensure consistent teaching delivery across groups, the authors implemented three measures: "all instructors underwent a comprehensive training program that covered the BOPPPS-CBL model, the standardized curriculum for chest pain management, and the use of teaching materials"; a standardized curriculum with lesson plans, case scenarios and assessment tools; and standardized teaching aids such as slide decks and handouts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor training program covering the teaching model, curriculum and materials
#### Constraints
- Blinding of participants and instructors was not feasible due to the nature of the educational intervention; outcome assessors were blinded instead

### Target Learners
- Resident physicians in standardized training

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent implementation of a structured case-based teaching model
