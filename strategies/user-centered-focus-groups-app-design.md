# Involve potential end users in focus groups at the design phase of a health app to align content and features with user-elicited preferences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a user-centered approach in which focus group discussions with intended end users are conducted during the design phase of a mobile health app. The article states that "Involvement of potential users in the early stages of app development is likely to optimize usage by aligning the app content and features with user-elicited preferences". In this study, two 75-90 minute focus groups with recent colonoscopy patients, analyzed thematically, produced the content areas and features later built into colonAPPscopy, including postcolonoscopy information and reminder features absent from prior apps designed without user input.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to eligible recent patients willing to participate in focus groups
- A trained facilitator and a structured focus group guide covering content (what) and features (how)
#### Constraints
- Participants had all undergone colonoscopy, so opinions may not reflect individuals who opted not to have the procedure
- Findings may not reflect the needs of patients not fluent in English or French

### Target Learners
- Adult patients (50-75 years) preparing for a preventive health procedure

### Target Learning Goals
- Designing app content and features that match end-user informational and support needs
