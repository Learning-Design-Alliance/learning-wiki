## Description
The article recommends analyzing homeschooling guided learning within the context of the sudden shift to digital education during the COVID-19 lockdown. It positions this analysis alongside Nacu, Martin, and Pinkard's ten focal topics and three elements as part of understanding how educator learning-support roles can be enabled online. The strategy treats home-based guided learning as a site requiring instructional-technological design attention.
