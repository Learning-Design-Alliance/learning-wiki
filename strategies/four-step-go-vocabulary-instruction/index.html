Implement GO-based vocabulary instruction through four steps: preparation, presentation, practice and evaluation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a four-step implementation sequence: "preparation, presentation, practice and evaluation". Preparation trains students on each word feature separately (including IPA symbols and dictionary use); presentation models GO creation and use; practice involves guided, gradually fading supervision of GO assignments inside and outside class; evaluation uses marked daily GOs as study material with announced testing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Initial orientation and training sessions before students practice independently
  • Researcher supervision of individual GO assignments that gradually fades
  • Tests counted toward class work marks to ensure seriousness
