# Teach students to recognize when they are applying heuristics and how to exert control over them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study found that heuristics "act in tandem or conjunction when students work towards the solution of a task given", the authors propose helping students "better recognize the use of their heuristics in the tasks that they are engaged, when they apply them and how to exert control over their application". The strategy targets the dual-process failure in which faulty System 1 intuitions go undetected by System 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need metacognitive skills to trigger System 2 intervention over System 1 responses, as the article states
#### Constraints
- The interconnection of heuristics makes it difficult to propose targeted strategies to help students avoid these cognitive biases

### Target Learners
- secondary physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring and controlling intuitive reasoning during physics problem solving

### Affordances
- Dual Process Framework Physics Reasoning
