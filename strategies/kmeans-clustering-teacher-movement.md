# Use K-means clustering of spatial point-pattern statistics to identify teacher movement patterns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An analytical strategy for identifying distinctive movement subgroups from time-series tracking data. The article computes eight summary statistics of movement as spatial point patterns (e.g., Clark-Evans index, dwell duration), reduces dimensionality with PCA, determines cluster number via WSS scree plot and gap statistic, and validates with Silhouette and S_Dbw indices. This strategy enabled the article to identify "three distinctive movement patterns" with high internal validation.
