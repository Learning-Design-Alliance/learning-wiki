# Train physicians and nurses together in mixed-professional simulation sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors deliberately trained physicians and nurses together because quality of rt-PA treatment depends on synergic action between medical and paramedical operators. They state, "For these reasons, we train physicians and nurses together," and report that the wide scope of professional qualifications did not hinder the training, with all learners expressing high satisfaction despite different learning goals. Learners were pleased to broach the procedures in a multidisciplinary way, and the article cites evidence that simulation training improves cohesion and communication inside healthcare teams.
