# Extend worksheets into later units with general templates and student-selected articles addressing a group-generated clinical question

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because developing a patient-centered question and identifying relevant evidence are crucial EBM skills, the authors plan later worksheets providing general templates for broad study types rather than tailored articles. A weekly student leader will select an article addressing the group's clinical question, share it, identify the appropriate CAW based on study type, and the PBL group will use the CAW as a rubric to appraise the chosen article. The authors state this "more advanced use of the worksheets will require not only critical appraisal skills but also additional steps in the EBM process such as asking a clear question and acquiring the evidence."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL groups identify a clinical question related to the established PBL curriculum content, and a weekly student leader selects the article and appropriate CAW.
#### Constraints
- The authors believe initially using preselected articles with tailored CAWs better ensures all students gain the same foundational skills before moving to student selection.

### Target Learners
- medical students in later PBL curriculum units

### Target Learning Goals
- asking a clear clinical question
- acquiring evidence
- critical appraisal of self-selected literature
