# Use individual development plans with milestone tracking to evaluate career guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the career guidance domain, the article recommends individual development plans (IDPs) as an evaluation instrument. It notes: "Most IDPs require that the mentee articulate concrete goals and expected accomplishments over a 3- to 5-year time horizon." Joint development and monitoring of an IDP facilitates career-planning discussions and supports self-reflection. The article further recommends external audits of the IDP process, tracking key career milestones against planned timelines, to enrich program evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentee articulation of concrete goals and expected accomplishments over a 3- to 5-year time horizon
#### Constraints
- The article notes assessment of mentee career planning may be limited at an institutional level and must be measured indirectly via satisfaction surveys or IDP implementation assessments

### Target Learners
- Mentees in academic and research career pathways in LMIC institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Career planning, professional development, and career advancement
