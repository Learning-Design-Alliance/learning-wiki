# Use weekly reflections aligned to ownership hallmarks as formative data alongside a validated survey

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researchers developed three alternating sets of reflection prompts—on weekly goals, technical problems, and successful moments—asking students to describe how they felt and summarize their own, peers', and instructor's roles. Prompts were "administered during the last six weeks of the seven-week project," each set administered twice, and coded for intentionality, self-efficacy, peer interaction, and affect with reported inter-rater reliability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between research team and instructor to develop prompts aligned with ownership hallmarks
- Two independent raters and a coding scheme with examples and counterexamples to establish reliability
#### Constraints
- Relevance was deliberately not probed to avoid calling attention to lack of relevance for uninterested students

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-monitoring of project progress
- reflection on agency, efficacy, peer support, and affect
