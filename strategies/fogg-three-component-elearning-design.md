# Design conflict-management e-learning by explicitly strengthening motivation, ability, and prompts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe builds each FBM component: motivation via "determining the educational needs, clarifying the goals, expressing the importance of the subject", attractive infographics, and evidence-based statistics; ability via simplifying skills step-by-step with completed worksheets and two-minute animated clips; prompts via sparks, facilitators and signals including immediate post-role-play feedback and educational SMS at specified intervals. Content was delivered through WhatsApp and an LMS between synchronous sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery via accessible messaging or e-learning channels so prompts reach learners at set intervals
- Materials reviewed for content validity (here, by a panel of eight experts)
#### Constraints
- Evidence for effectiveness is from one center with preclinical medical students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- conflict management attitudes and skills for healthcare teamwork
