# Recalibrate CPR training toward regular, simulation-based skills refreshers with real-time audiovisual feedback and instructor-led debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend rebalancing CPR education away from theory-dominant formats toward "a more balanced, competency- and simulation-based approach" with continuous psychomotor reinforcement. They propose regular structured refreshers using manikin-based simulation with real-time audiovisual feedback within continuing medical education, plus instructor-led debriefings and scenario-based assessments integrated with clinical quality-improvement data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to manikin-based simulation with real-time audiovisual feedback capability
- Certified BLS instructors for scoring and debriefing
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a cross-sectional study in one Chinese province; the authors note it precludes assessment of long-term skill retention and patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- Emergency physicians and nurses
- Healthcare professionals in resuscitation training programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Psychomotor CPR skill execution
- Translation of theoretical knowledge into practical performance
