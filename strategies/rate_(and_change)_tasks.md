# Rate (and Change) Tasks

## Description
Ask learners to rate the level of challenge in an activity on a scale of 1 to 10. If the activity is rated an eight or higher, ask what could be done to make the task a two or three. This provides insight into how to help the learner decrease frustration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A rating scale, structured tasks, and the ability to adjust tasks based on feedback.
#### Constraints
- Learners may not always provide accurate ratings. Some adjustments may not be feasible.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track the learner's ratings of task difficulty. Monitor changes in engagement and frustration levels. Collect feedback on the effectiveness of task adjustments.

## Impact
- Rating and changing tasks can significantly reduce frustration and increase engagement by ensuring tasks are appropriately challenging. This approach promotes a positive learning environment and supports individualized instruction.
