# Let students know what order they will be called on

## Description
This strategy allows students who are prone to challenges with inhibition to plan their response and wait until their turn. Knowing the order in advance reduces anxiety and impulsivity, providing a structured environment for participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of student needs and potential challenges with inhibition. Flexibility in classroom management to accommodate different needs.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective for all students with inhibition challenges. Requires ongoing assessment and adjustments based on student feedback and performance.

### Affordances
- Provides a predictable structure for participation. Reduces anxiety and allows students to prepare their responses thoughtfully.

### Personalization
- The order can be adjusted based on individual student needs. Students can be given advance notice of when they will be called on.
