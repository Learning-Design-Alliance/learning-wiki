# EQ Feeling Chart Check-In

## Description
Students use a provided EQ Feeling Chart to identify and communicate their current emotions. In pairs, they discuss their feelings, where those feelings are coming from, and any other emotions they may be experiencing. The class then reconvenes to share what they noticed during their discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EQ Feeling Chart, time for paired discussions (5-10 minutes), and a willingness to share in a whole-class setting.
#### Constraints
- Some students may be hesitant to share their feelings, even in pairs. The effectiveness of the activity depends on creating a safe and supportive classroom environment.

### Target Learning Goals

- Communicate feelings, practice emotional awareness, build relationships, normalize discussions about emotions

### Affordances
- Provides a structured framework for identifying and discussing emotions. Offers a visual aid (EQ Feeling Chart) to support emotional literacy. Encourages empathy and understanding through paired discussions.

### Personalization
- The EQ Feeling Chart can be adapted to include a wider range of emotions or to focus on specific emotional categories. The depth of discussion can be adjusted based on the age and maturity of the students.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student participation in paired discussions and whole-class sharing. Assess the depth and specificity of students' reflections on their emotions. Gather feedback from students on the usefulness of the EQ Feeling Chart.

## Impact
- Acknowledging emotions calms the amygdala and reduces the intensity of felt emotions, helping individuals feel more in control.  Promotes emotional literacy and awareness.
