Five-day teacher workshop for WRD curriculum framework training¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The study found that teachers most preferred a five days' workshop format for training on the WRD curriculum framework. The workshops gave teachers "enough knowledge and skills of how to design learning units and assessments on WRD, as well as information about community learning resources on WRD". After the workshop, the voluntary 28 teachers were able to write learning units and lesson plans on WRD, with five producing material of a high standard (mean 3.54) and 23 at a good level (mean 3.35).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must be willing to participate and attend fully
  • Training must cover learning unit design, assessment design, and community learning resources

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated specifically for the WRD curriculum framework in Khon Kaen, Thailand

Target Learners¶

  • In-service secondary science teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Designing learning units and assessments on water resource and disaster management

Claims¶

  • Most partner professional development relied on traditional short-term workshops with little classroom-level intensive assistance [~W]
