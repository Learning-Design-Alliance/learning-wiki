# Incorporate collaborative learning activities within AI literacy teaching, attending to activity modality and integration with other activities and assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that instruction for improving AI literacy incorporate collaborative learning activities so learners can co-create knowledge contextualized to their needs, with activities targeting literacy, specific use-case knowledge, or domain knowledge depending on instructional goals. It advises that "The modality of the activity is an essential consideration for educators developing AI literacy programs," and that special attention be given to how learners contextualize AI in their micro and macro decision-making. It may also be necessary to integrate CL with other activities and assessments to enhance comprehension and engagement.
