# Choose-your-own-adventure storytelling templates for training differential diagnosis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes adapting the choose-your-own-adventure (CYOA) story-telling technique, in which readers intervene at decision points and the ending varies with the decision, to case-based learning in medical education. Because differential diagnosis requires clinicians to keep questioning and ruling out options until one answer remains, students who "practice making their own CYOA while recording these thought processes in their own CYOA template" can naturally familiarize themselves with the issues involved in making an accurate diagnosis.
