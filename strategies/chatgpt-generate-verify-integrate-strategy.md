# Have students generate, verify, and integrate ChatGPT answers judged by a teacher to build critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom activity using ChatGPT to train information retrieval and critique: the instructor selects a teaching topic, students obtain a ChatGPT answer, verify it by other methods, integrate both into a final answer, and the teaching pharmacist judges the answer and the references used. The article states "Students first asked ChatGPT to generate an answer and then used other methods to verify the answer," framing seeking and critiquing information as practice in, and assessment of, critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching pharmacist who judges the integrated final answers and the appropriateness of the references students used
- Access to ChatGPT plus alternative verification sources such as literature retrieval and reading reports
#### Constraints
- The article warns that "this platform may also provide false medical information"

### Target Learners
- clinical pharmacy trainees in hospital-based programs

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- information retrieval and verification
- self-directed learning
