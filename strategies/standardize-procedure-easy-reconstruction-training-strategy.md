# Standardize operative procedures and use an easy reconstruction method when training trainee laparoscopic surgeons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation for surgical training programs: "Making laparoscopic procedures standard and using an easy reconstruction method are useful in the success of the training system." Standardization means a fixed dissection order performed like page-turning to maintain vision and avoid repeated clamping, while the augmented rectangle technique reduces suturing demands. The strategy is grounded in the study's finding that trainee reconstruction time matched trainer time and outcomes were safe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standard operative procedure taught to all trainees, and a reconstruction technique simple enough for trainees to complete in trainer-comparable time
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated for TLDG with Billroth I reconstruction using the augmented rectangle technique at one high-volume department

### Target Learners
- trainee gastrointestinal surgeons learning total laparoscopic distal gastrectomy

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering standardized laparoscopic procedure and intracorporeal reconstruction efficiently and safely
