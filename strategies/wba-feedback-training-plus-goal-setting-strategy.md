# Pair structured workplace-based assessment with supervisor training in timely, specific, actionable feedback and add goal-setting or reflective portfolios to support self-directed learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's actionable implementation steps for workplace-based assessment programs: invest in "faculty development for timely, specific, actionable feedback and trust-building", and incorporate "self-reflection, feedforward planning, and follow-up opportunities to strengthen self-directed learning". This responds to the finding that independent study behaviors were not significantly affected, which the authors attribute to the clerkship's structured, supervised nature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors trained in structured feedback delivery and trust-building
- Assessment embedded in ongoing supervision and educational alliance building
#### Constraints
- The study did not formally measure fidelity of feedback delivery or long-term maintenance of assessor calibration

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in clinical clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical competence and self-directed learning
- Motivation, communication, and professionalism
