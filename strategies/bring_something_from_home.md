# Bring Something From Home

## Description
Ask students to bring something from home that makes their classroom feel more like home, such as a photograph, a stuffed animal, or a musical instrument. This provides students with something familiar if they’re feeling down or homesick and represents all families within the classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom space to display items, clear guidelines for items brought from home
#### Constraints
- May be difficult for students without access to meaningful items, requires sensitivity to diverse family situations
