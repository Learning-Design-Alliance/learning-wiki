# Use established theoretical models to guide the deductive development of a construct-based selection SJT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A practical implication the article draws for medical schools using or planning construct-based SJTs is "the use of established theoretical models to guide the deductive development of an SJT". The article argues this facilitates understanding of why an SJT is related or unrelated to a criterion, supports meaningful comparisons with other selection predictors, and enables comparison of different SJT formats measuring the same construct. The models need not concern integrity; the article notes they may involve other constructs such as social competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Future research is required to identify which characteristics of the deductive development approach positively influence SJT validity
