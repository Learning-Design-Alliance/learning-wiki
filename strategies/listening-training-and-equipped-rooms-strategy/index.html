Strategies

Provide pre-service training courses and well-equipped listening rooms to support listening instruction¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that the Ministry of Education provide training courses to pre-service teachers and that schools have special rooms equipped and designed for listening classes, free of sound distractions. It also recommends designing textbooks that focus on all language skills including listening.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training courses for pre-service teachers on teaching listening comprehension

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are based on a single questionnaire and interview study in Karak schools

Target Learners¶

  • EFL teachers in primary and secondary schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English listening comprehension instruction

Claims¶

  • Problems related to availability of resources and teaching aids were rated medium, with an overall mean of 3.04 [+W]
  • Teachers report medium-level problems related to their own proficiency, with an overall mean of 2.81 [+W]
  • Teachers report medium-level problems related to teaching environment, with electricity supply unavailability the highest-ranked difficulty [+W]
  • Most interviewed teachers held negative attitudes towards teaching listening comprehension [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use visual and aural aids and varied, vivid lesson structures in listening lessons
  • Provide institutional support for educational technology in schools: ministry provision, expert training, and a utilization manual
  • Equip teachers for digital story use through in-service training and teacher education courses
  • Optimize Classroom Acoustics
  • Deliver in-service teacher professional development that is closely linked to the books and lesson plans in use and supported by frequent classroom observation, through existing ministry structures
