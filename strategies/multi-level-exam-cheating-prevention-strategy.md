# Multi-level prevention strategy for exam cheating: integrity workshops, formative assessment, curriculum revision, staff training, and standardized exam conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's conclusion recommends practical actions for policymakers and educational managers to prevent cheating in nursing education. The authors state that "organizing integrity workshops, using more formative assessment methods, and revising the curriculum to include ethical decision-making skills can help reduce cheating", alongside training for faculty and exam administrators and standardizing the test environment and questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policymakers and educational managers must attend to root causes and make better policies at all levels
#### Constraints
- The recommendations derive from a qualitative study in two Iranian nursing colleges and have not been tested as interventions in this article

### Target Learning Goals
