# Provide additional guidance tools such as intraoperative fluoroscopy during a trainee's first 20 CT-navigated THA cases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend supplementing CT-based navigation during a trainee's earliest cases, because the first 20 cases carried a markedly higher risk of alignment and position outliers. They write that "when inexperienced trainee surgeons perform THA using CT-based navigation in the first 20 cases, the use of additional tools, such as intraoperative fluoroscopy, might be beneficial." This is a supervision-and-support recommendation for early surgical training, not a claim that navigation alone is insufficient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applies to inexperienced trainee surgeons performing THA with CT-based navigation
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is bounded to the first 20 cases, the cutoff identified in this single-trainee retrospective cohort

### Target Learners
- inexperienced orthopedic trainee surgeons (<20 prior THA cases)

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate acetabular cup alignment and 3D positioning in total hip arthroplasty
