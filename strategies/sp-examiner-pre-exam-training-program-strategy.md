# Comprehensive pre-exam SP and examiner training program with scripted cases, rubric walkthrough, video review, and simulated examinees

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before each OSCE, the authors ran a structured training sequence: scripts and real clinical patient videos were distributed and explained, the chief examiner explained scoring rubrics and reviewed previous-year assessment videos, and simulated scoring was conducted with eight simulated examinees so SPs performed their task and scored alongside examiners. "In the present study, SPs were trained three times before the examination, and SPs and matched examiners were trained two times." A detailed checklist of communication assessment points was designed to increase scoring consistency. The authors state these measures could reduce differences between raters and increase examination reliability regardless of examiner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SPs and examiners recruited 3 days before the examination
- Each examiner paired with two SPs of the same script in four groups
- A checklist with detailed contents of communication assessment points
#### Constraints
- Training must be repeated for each examination because a new group of SPs is recruited each year

### Target Learners
- standard patients
- OSCE examiners

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate standardized scoring of examinee communication and examination performance
