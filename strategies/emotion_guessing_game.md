# Emotion Guessing Game

## Description
An emotion guessing game involves an instructor or student making an emotion face, and the others guess the emotion being expressed. After guessing, the group discusses situations that might evoke that emotion, enhancing emotional vocabulary and social awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires minimal materials (e.g., paper or blanket to cover face). A facilitator to guide the game and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may feel uncomfortable making facial expressions in front of others. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Affordances
- Engaging and interactive, encourages active participation, and promotes understanding of the connection between facial expressions and emotions.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by using pictures or written prompts instead of facial expressions. Learners can take turns being the 'emotion actor.'

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' ability to accurately identify emotions and connect them with appropriate situations. Assess the depth and complexity of the emotions discussed.

## Impact
- Research suggests that activities involving facial expression recognition can improve emotional intelligence and social skills. (Source: Facial Expression Recognition and Emotional Intelligence - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6225237/)
