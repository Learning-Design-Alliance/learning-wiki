# Use guiding questions in a Socratic style to break research tasks into smaller steps and steer discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Ciênsação offers 'guiding questions' that divide the student research task into smaller steps, which, when used in a Socratic style, can advance discussion in the intended direction. This helps teachers find the balance between restricting discussion and letting students develop their own ideas, and avoids frustration for both teachers and students. The guiding questions also help teachers make a smooth transition from an experiment to the respective theoretical content without losing the motivating momentum of the activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each experiment must be built around a specific student research task whose question or challenge provides clear orientation on what the student should discover
#### Constraints
- Teachers face the challenge of finding the thin line between containing the debate and allowing students to develop their own ideas

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school science students

### Target Learning Goals
- engaging in evidence-based discussion
- developing own ideas through inquiry
