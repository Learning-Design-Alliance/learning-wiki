Use structured reflection tables, learning journals, prompt variation, adversarial queries, and peer debate to cultivate AI intuition¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends instructional practices that make each phase of the experiential cycle visible and assessable. "Structured reflection tables and learning journals support the articulation of inductive heuristics, transforming tacit experience into shareable and evaluable understanding in the form of rules of thumb." Teachers guide students to vary prompts, challenge systems with atypical or contradictory queries, and engage in guided debates (e.g., whether generative AI should write an English essay) in which students articulate, justify, and revise positions from experiential and ethical evidence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must design activities and assessment practices that make each phase of the exploration-observation-reflection-adaptation cycle visible and assessable

Constraints¶

  • The article presents these as design recommendations from classroom practice, not as empirically validated interventions

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • articulating inductive heuristics about AI behavior
  • connecting personal experience with shared norms of responsible AI use

Affordances¶

  • Ai Intuition Experiential Inductive Construct

Related Strategies¶

  • Future research agenda: longitudinal studies, evidence-based strategies, teacher professional development, and domain-specific frameworks for AI intuition
  • Classroom exercise cycle: select a task, formulate a baseline prompt, submit it to an LLM, then critically analyse the output before revising instructions
  • Provide opportunities for independent practice with repeated cycles of use and structured reflection to strengthen regulatory skills over time
  • Design authentic, scaffolded activities that convert students' intuitive GenAI concerns into explicit ethical competencies
  • Scaffold AI use without undermining student autonomy through structured prompts, ethical checklists, and reflection journals
  • Staff development should make teaching principles explicit through articulation, reflection and evaluation, supported by institutional incentives and training
