# Administrator involvement to sustain school-wide maker programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report finds that making in schools often begins at the grassroots level with educators organizing clubs or in-class activities, but that efforts unsupported by school administration "often stall or end due to burn-out or employee transition". To grow activities into sustainable programs across entire schools and districts, administrators must get involved and ensure structures cultivating an institution-wide maker culture, create professional learning opportunities for educators new to making, and celebrate making with all school stakeholders to grow community buy-in.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrators who get involved and ensure structures are in place to cultivate an institution-wide maker culture
#### Constraints
- Grassroots educator efforts not supported by school administration often stall or end due to burn-out or employee transition

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in schools with emerging maker programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustainable, school-wide maker learning programs
