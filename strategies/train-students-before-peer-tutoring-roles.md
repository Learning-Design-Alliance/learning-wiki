# Train students in tutor and group roles before routine use of peer tutoring or response groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before peer tutoring or response groups are used routinely, students must be taught how to interact in their assigned roles. The report states: "Before implementing peer tutoring groups, students are trained to interact as tutor and tutee or to work in small groups." The reviewed studies illustrate this: Serrano's student tutors received a series of 20-minute training sessions including demonstrations, modeling, prompting practice, and role-playing, while Prater & Bermudez's teacher spent the first week modeling how groups would work and how to respond to peers' writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specific instruction on tutoring procedures or role assumption before routine implementation
- Teacher time to model group procedures or deliver tutor training sessions
