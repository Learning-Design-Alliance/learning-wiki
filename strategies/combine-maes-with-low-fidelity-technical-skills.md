# Combine MAES© with more restrictive or low-fidelity simulation methodologies for technical skills learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the areas-of-improvement dimension of the qualitative analysis, some students expressed the need to combine MAES© with other simulation methodologies more restrictive or based on low fidelity for learning technical skills. This student-generated recommendation addresses the limitation that self-designed, high-fidelity scenario work may not best serve deliberate technical skill practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to complementary low-fidelity or restrictive simulation formats alongside MAES© sessions
#### Constraints
- Other students found high-fidelity simulation slightly uncomfortable, reporting that working under pressure and being observed limits performance

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- postgraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- technical clinical skills
