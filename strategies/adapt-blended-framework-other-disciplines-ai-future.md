# Adapt the blended online-PBL-CBL framework to other surgical disciplines and incorporate AI-assisted learning

## Description
The article recommends that the blended framework is adaptable to other surgical disciplines "by updating discipline-specific online resources and PBL/CBL cases" and can be promoted to institutions with mature online platforms and trained faculty. Future iterations could incorporate AI-assisted learning, including "AI-driven case recommendation, automated formative assessment, real-time clinical reasoning feedback, and personalized learning path generation", to compensate for limitations of human-led PBL/CBL.
