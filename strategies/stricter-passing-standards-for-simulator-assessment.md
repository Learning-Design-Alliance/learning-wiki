# Set more stringent minimum passing standards for simulator-based assessments to bridge the gap with real-patient assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because simulator scores run higher than real-patient scores, the article recommends adjusting cut scores when simulators are used for competency decisions. It states: "we suggest that when applying simulator assessments, more stringent minimum passing standards should be established to effectively bridge the gap between assessments on simulator and assessments on pregnant volunteers".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness that simulator conditions involve lower psychological pressure and standardized, predictable scanning conditions
#### Constraints
- Applies to simulator-based assessment use, not to simulator-based training itself

### Target Learners
- Ultrasound residents assessed via simulation

### Target Learning Goals
- Ensuring trainees possess practical operational skills required in clinical settings
