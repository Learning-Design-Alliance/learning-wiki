# Adapt instructional systems to local contexts through systematic inquiry and collaboration among educators, developers, and researchers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy, drawn from a special-issue paper summarized in the editorial, holds that "instructional systems need to be adapted to their local contexts" and that doing so "requires promotion of systematic inquiry and collaboration among educators, developers, and researchers". It treats deployment of AIED in real-world contexts as an ongoing improvement process rather than a one-time build, with educators, developers and researchers jointly investigating how systems perform locally. The editorial reports no evaluation data for this approach.
