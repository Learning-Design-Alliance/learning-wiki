# For concept inventories, either require one common instrument or let instructors choose a research-validated inventory matched to course topics, addressing comparability analytically

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Requiring all courses to use the same concept inventory "facilitates direct comparisons of data across courses and institutions" but may reduce measurement validity when content is not covered. The alternative is to "allow instructors to select a research-validated concept inventory that best aligns with their course topics," improving validity while handling comparability through methods such as meta-analysis; teams should provide selection guidance or constrain choices to approved instruments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research teams should be prepared to provide guidance to instructors unfamiliar with DBER, e.g., via assessment repositories and PhysPort
#### Constraints
- Restricting participation to courses covering one inventory's topics may substantially limit the pool of eligible participants

### Target Learners
- Instructors of introductory courses across disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- Valid measurement of conceptual learning comparable across institutions
