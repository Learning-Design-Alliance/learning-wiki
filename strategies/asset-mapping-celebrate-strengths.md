# Use asset mapping to identify and celebrate existing strengths in the school and community

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Asset mapping is "an activity that focuses on the strengths that currently exist within a system or community," looking at the school, district, and surrounding community as a whole to identify assets worth celebrating and learning from. The resource lists benefits including building on current successes, generating shared awareness of assets, and identifying opportunities to increase capacity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders must consistently, intentionally, and deeply engage with educational partners so successes not apparent in data reports become visible
#### Constraints
- Aimed at strengths that are not easily quantified, such as personal growth, staff collaboration, relational trust, and positive culture shifts

### Target Learners
- School and district communities, including students, families, and community members

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognizing and celebrating community strengths
- shaping school community narratives
- building pride and collective responsibility
