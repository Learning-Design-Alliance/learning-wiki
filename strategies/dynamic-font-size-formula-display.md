# Dynamically resizing on-screen formulas to link game actions with analytical expressions

## Description
The article proposes displaying the analytical expressions for kinetic and potential energy during play with font sizes that grow and shrink according to changes in each energy type. The intent is to create a visual connection between intuitive gameplay and the formulas students would use outside the game, making the theoretical learning content visible.
