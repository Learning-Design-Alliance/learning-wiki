# Peer teaching and debrief of curriculum lessons within teacher professional development workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A professional development activity in which teacher participants deliver curriculum lessons to an audience of peers to highlight target teaching practices, then debrief the lesson together. As printed: "teachers deliver lessons from the curriculum to an audience of peers that highlight these teaching practi ces. Afterward teachers debrief the lesson, allowing them time to reflect with peers about how implementation should be tailored for their own classrooms." The design is modeled after the five requirements of transformative learning outlined by Louckes-Horsley and colleagues (2010).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops must devote time for the debrief so teachers can reflect with peers about tailoring implementation for their own classrooms
