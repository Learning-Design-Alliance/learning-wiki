Design JiTT web exercises as small clusters of open-ended questions tied to learning outcomes and misconceptions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends developing JiTT exercises as "small clusters of questions" addressing key course learning outcomes, typical student misconceptions, conceptual bottlenecks, and critical/analytical or metacognitive skills. Exercises may combine multiple choice, cloze tests, and free writing, but are most effective when students must comprehend the assigned material, and open-ended questions help make student thinking visible.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Questions requiring students to do something before class (read, complete a simulation, watch a video)
  • At least some open-ended questions to make thinking visible

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • vocational and higher-education students in introductory courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • higher-order thinking skills
  • comprehension of assigned material
  • preparation for interactive lectures
