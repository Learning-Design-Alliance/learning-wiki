# Staff Passport to Literacy with trained paraprofessional interventionists receiving 8 hours of training over 2 days

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's staffing model uses paraprofessionals as interventionists: "Reading instruction is provided by trained interventionists in small groups of 4-7 students. In the validating study, the interventionists were paraprofessionals with at least B.A. degrees." Training was brief but structured: "interventionists received 8 hours of training over 2 days." This makes the program implementable without certified reading specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventionists with at least B.A. degrees
- 8 hours of training over 2 days
#### Constraints
- Training and staffing details reflect the validating study's configuration

### Target Learners
- K-5 students reading below grade level

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational reading skills and comprehension
