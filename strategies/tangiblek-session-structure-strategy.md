# Run robotics sessions structured by the TangibleK curriculum with small collaborative groups of 4-5 children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention consisted of seven working sessions: an introductory session exploring the robot's functionalities, followed by six sessions organised by dimension — sequences, action-instruction correspondence and debugging. Sessions were designed using the TangibleK robotics curriculum as reference, took place during school days of approximately four hours, and children "worked in small groups (4-5 members) collaboratively", with teachers supervising and co-evaluating performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher collaboration for planning, supervision and joint evaluation of children's performance
#### Constraints
- The intervention required working with intact groups as allowed by the school

### Target Learners
- early childhood education children aged 3-6

### Target Learning Goals
- computational thinking and programming skills
