# Use in-service training to increase teacher knowledge, skills, and confidence, funded through BOS budget slots

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In-service training is described as "an effective method for positively increasing the knowledge, skills, and confidence of teachers", comprising workshops, lectures, teacher meetings, seminars, and school visits organized to improve the performance of assigned personnel. In South Bangka its implementation "in terms of quantity is still rarely implemented because in -service training activities require a large enough budget". The proposed alternative is allocating available School Operational Assistance (BOS) fund slots, with the Education Office providing adequate socialization and budget simulations so schools can implement them properly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate socialization and budget simulations provided by the local government through the Education and Culture Office
- Allocation of available BOS fund slots according to technical guidelines
#### Constraints
- Requires a relatively large budget
- Possible many in-class hour vacancies because teachers leave class to attend training
- Material received is not continuous and often boring
- Relatively small participation opportunities because training activities are few while interested teachers are many

### Target Learners
- Certified, in-service teachers in South Bangka Regency

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuing professional development in knowledge, skills, and experience for carrying out teaching duties
