Districts respond to shortages with teacher preparation and pathway strategies, especially partnerships coordinating student teaching and residency placements¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Almost all shortage districts (93%) report adopting one or more preparation/pathway strategies: 72% work with teacher preparation programs to coordinate student teacher or residency placements, 62% to communicate hiring needs, 56% develop teacher leadership roles, and smaller shares build paraprofessional, high school career, and volunteer pathways ("sometimes referred to as Grow Your Own teacher preparation models").

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires partnership with higher education preparation programs to coordinate placements and communicate hiring needs

Constraints¶

  • The report notes these policies have generally not been targeted to shortage fields

Target Learners¶

  • California K-12 students in districts with teacher shortages

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Access to fully credentialed teachers in all subjects and grades

Related Strategies¶

  • Districts use financial, personnel management, and working-conditions strategies to recruit and retain teachers, including leadership compensation, mentoring, and collaboration time
  • Develop high-retention pathways — Grow Your Own programs and teacher residencies — to build and diversify local teacher supply
