# Six defined roles and responsibilities for an early-career special interest group

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
NESIG's Terms of Reference define six roles: creating virtual and in-person spaces where early-career members can "confidentially ask questions, seek advice, express ideas, and strengthen their professional networks"; organising tailored networking and professional development events; encouraging participation in association committees; nurturing connections with international groups such as young-ESTRO and the AAPM Student and Trainees Subcommittee; advising the association board; and appointing working groups with approved terms of reference and specified duration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formally approved Terms of Reference document
- A leadership committee selected through an Expression of Interest process
#### Constraints
- Working groups are subject to approval of terms of reference, including a specified duration

### Target Learners
- Newly-joined and early career members of ACPSEM and similar professional societies

### Target Learning Goals
- Professional networking, confidence in seeking advice, participation in professional governance, and career development
