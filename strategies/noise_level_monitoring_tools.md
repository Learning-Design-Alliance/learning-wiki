# Noise Level Monitoring Tools

## Description
Using electronic tools to monitor classroom noise levels and provide auditory or visual signals when the noise becomes too loud. Examples include Super Sound Box, Class Dojo, and Too Noisy. These tools detect the noise level and produce a signal, such as a sound or visual indicator, when the threshold is exceeded. They provide real-time feedback on noise levels, helping students self-regulate their behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Electronic device (e.g., computer, tablet, smartphone), noise level monitoring software or app (e.g., Super Sound Box, Class Dojo, Too Noisy), and clear explanation of the tool and its purpose to students.
#### Constraints
- May become a distraction if not managed properly. Requires reliable technology and may not be accessible to all classrooms. Some students may become overly focused on the noise level, leading to anxiety or stress.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Monitor and manage classroom noise levels, provide real-time feedback to students, promote self-regulation of behavior, and create a quieter learning environment.

### Affordances
- Provides objective, real-time feedback on noise levels, helping students become more aware of their behavior. Promotes self-regulation and encourages students to take responsibility for maintaining a quiet classroom.

### Personalization
- Noise level thresholds can be adjusted to suit the specific needs and expectations of the classroom. Different types of auditory or visual signals can be used to provide feedback.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track the frequency and duration of noise level alerts. Monitor the overall noise level in the classroom. Gather student feedback on the effectiveness of the tool.

## Impact
- Improved self-regulation: Real-time feedback on noise levels helps students become more aware of their behavior and take responsibility for maintaining a quiet classroom. Reduced noise levels: Monitoring tools can effectively reduce classroom noise, creating a more focused learning environment.
