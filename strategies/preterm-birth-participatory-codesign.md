# Participatory co-design of e-learning content with multi-stakeholder workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The resource was developed using a participatory methodology in which 26 key stakeholders — teachers, teaching assistants, educational psychologists, parents of preterm-born children, and preterm-born young adults — attended a workshop and "developed storyboards to guide the content and design of the resource". Storyboards were distilled into written content specifications reviewed by field experts for accuracy, and prototype RLOs were reviewed by two teachers whose feedback was incorporated. This strategy grounds content in both expert evidence and practitioner and lived-experience perspectives.
