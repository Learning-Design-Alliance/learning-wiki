# Non-linear Point-and-Click interaction with optional hand hygiene, Quests, instructional videos, and an end-of-simulation feedback report

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The simulation uses Point and Click interactions and Quests that participants trigger at their own discretion, "regardless of whether the chosen action is correct or not", to replicate the non-linearity of real-world clinical practice without prerequisites or fixed sequences. Hand hygiene is intentionally optional, with visibly soiled avatar hands and a hygiene-status icon. Instructional videos demonstrate technical steps the technology cannot execute, and a chronological action report supports self-assessment and reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simple interaction mode learnable even by participants unfamiliar with computers or video games
#### Constraints
- The chosen technological alternatives prevent execution of certain technical procedures relevant to the case, requiring instructional videos

### Target Learners
- Nursing students
- Nursing professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Decision-making
- Self-assessment of performance
