## Description
The article recommends that future studies use its six scaffolding elements to produce controlled comparisons: "Future studies can make use of the elements of scaﬀolding to produce low and high scaﬀolding versions of the same question for use in experimental exams." This addresses the limitation that its own observational data came from actual exam responses, so low and high scaffolding questions did not match exactly as in the Cambridge study that designed questions specifically to measure scaffolding gains.
