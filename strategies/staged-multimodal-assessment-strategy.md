# Stage-sequenced assessment strategy: staged single-mode and multimodal examinations culminating in case analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable assessment recipe used by the article: five examinations across three stages — an image basic knowledge theory exam, single-mode and multimodal image analysis exams after the imaging-teaching stage, and a final theoretical exam plus a five-case analysis exam requiring interpretation, analysis and diagnosis from patient information and CFP, FFA, ICGA and OCT images. Each exam is scored out of 100 points, allowing stage-wise comparison of teaching effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A disease image database from which examination cases can be randomly selected
- The same instructor and same teaching content and images across compared groups
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated for postgraduate ophthalmology residents in a single center

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical students in residency training

### Target Learning Goals
- image interpretation skill
- clinical case analysis and diagnostic reasoning
