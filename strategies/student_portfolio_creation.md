# Student Portfolio Creation

## Description
Student portfolio creation involves students selecting and organizing their best work samples to demonstrate their progress, reflect on their learning, and set goals. Portfolios can include a variety of artifacts, such as written assignments, projects, artwork, and reflections. The process of creating a portfolio encourages students to take ownership of their learning, identify strengths and weaknesses, and communicate their growth to others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time for students to select work samples, reflect on their learning, and organize their portfolios. Teachers need to provide guidance and feedback to support the portfolio creation process. Various portfolio formats can be used (e.g., hanging files, file folders, digital platforms).
#### Constraints
- May require significant time and effort for both students and teachers. Some students may struggle with self-assessment or portfolio organization. Portfolios can become overwhelming if not managed effectively.
