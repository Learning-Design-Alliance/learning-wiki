# Use established routines such as course-map and unit-plan review cycles to monitor and sustain instructional work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The schools used established and repeated activities to guide and sustain instructional improvement, setting common expectations, providing guidelines, and creating feedback mechanisms. Two schools regularly reviewed course maps and unit plans; one assistant principal described unit plans "submitted on a rolling basis every five weeks," timed to progress-report grading cycles, with administrators reviewing and returning comments to teachers.
