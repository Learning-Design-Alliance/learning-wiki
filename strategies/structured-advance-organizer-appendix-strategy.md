# Offer a structured advance organizer before the full article for readers who benefit from one

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "Readers who benefit from a structured advance organizer should consider reading the appendix at the end of this article before reading the entire article." This is an implementable presentation strategy: supply a structured advance organizer (here, an appendix) that readers may consult before the main text to scaffold their comprehension of the complex material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured advance organizer must be prepared and placed where readers can access it before the main content
#### Constraints
- Benefits readers who profit from advance organizers; the article frames it as optional for such readers

### Target Learners
- readers of complex instructional material

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of complex expository text
