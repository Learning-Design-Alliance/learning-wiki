# Self-Talk Brainstorm

## Description
Provide learners with scenarios of challenging situations and have them brainstorm possible neutral or positive self-talk responses. Examples include: being excluded from a lunch table, a teacher not having time for your question, being blamed for something you didn't do, and failing a test. For each scenario, provide examples of negative self-talk and prompt learners to generate more positive or neutral alternatives.
