# Integrate societal partners and real-world problems into PBL programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To counter the second critique of isolation, the article suggests that "working with societal partners and real-world problems are both integral to PBL". More generally, reflection on how to deal with responsibilities students will face should be part of the programme. This strategy reconnects the tutorial to the world at large and widens PBL's original community orientation from the local community to the shared world as a whole.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programme-level reflection on responsibilities students will face should be built into the curriculum
#### Constraints
- Does not imply replicating an actual job situation in the PBL tutorial; a critical difference remains between working and studying

### Target Learners
- higher education students in PBL programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- responsibility for a shared world
- sustainable professional practice
