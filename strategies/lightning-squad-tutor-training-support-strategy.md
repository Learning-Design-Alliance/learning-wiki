# Implement Lightning Squad tutoring with non-certified tutors supported by structured onsite training, coaching, and data reviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's implementation model relies on tutors hired by schools or community organizations, most of whom have a college degree but are not certified teachers. Each school receives 1 onsite training day and 4 onsite support sessions through the first year of implementation; in years 2 and beyond, each school receives 3 days of onsite support. "Training and coaching support, including periodic data reviews to insure high-quality implementation," are part of the license and service contract.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each school receives 1 onsite training day and 4 onsite support sessions through the first year of implementation.
- Success for All Foundation supports tutors hired by schools or community organizations.
#### Constraints
- Computers and broadband are required.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 1-5 receiving small-group tutoring

### Target Learning Goals
- Early decoding skills
- Reading fluency
