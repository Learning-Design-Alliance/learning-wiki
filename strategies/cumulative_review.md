# Cumulative Review

## Description
Cumulative review involves systematically revisiting previously learned concepts and skills throughout a course or learning period. This strategy aims to reinforce understanding, improve retention, and ensure that learners maintain mastery of essential material over time. Effective cumulative review incorporates various methods, such as revisiting key concepts through discussions, quick quizzes, or activities that integrate past and present learning. The goal is to combat the forgetting curve and enhance long-term memory by consistently activating and applying prior knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires systematic planning to integrate review sessions into the learning schedule. Instructors need to track learner progress and tailor review activities to address specific needs.
#### Constraints
- If not structured effectively, cumulative review can become repetitive and disengaging. May require significant time allocation, potentially reducing time for new content.

### Affordances
- Strengthens long-term memory through repeated exposure. Helps learners connect new information to prior knowledge. Provides opportunities for instructors to identify and address gaps in understanding.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by focusing on areas where learners struggle, using varied review methods (quizzes, discussions, activities), and allowing learners to self-select review topics.
