School and district leaders' recommendations for implementing effective classroom coaching programs¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on DLP evidence, the report recommends that school and district leaders work with their coach to build better partnerships (promote teacher buy-in, voluntary participation, teacher-set goals, protected coaching time, and coach-teacher confidentiality kept separate from formal evaluation), promote teachers' active learning through frequent in-school-day sense-making opportunities, personalize support, ensure sustained follow-up time for coaches, and provide subject-specific support where the teacher remains the content expert while the coach advises on strategies and tools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coaches need time in their schedules to provide sustained follow-up and informal support to teachers

Constraints¶

  • The report states coaching should be kept separate from a teacher's formal evaluation and coach-teacher confidentiality respected

Target Learners¶

  • school leaders
  • district administrators
  • instructional coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • implementing and sustaining effective classroom technology coaching programs

Related Strategies¶

  • Hold a kick-off event aligning coaching with school and district goals, and leverage early volunteers' testimonials to build teacher buy-in
  • Engage district leaders as they implement a coaching program
  • Provide clear guidance to coaches when implementing a districtwide coaching program
  • Track coaching activities to support districtwide coaching implementation
  • Protect confidentiality and a non-evaluative stance so teachers trust the coach-teacher relationship
  • Protect coaches' time so the majority is spent directly with teachers
  • Recommendations for adopting and sustaining high-quality coaching
  • Design coaching programs as a systemic collective enterprise with shared vision, recruited and developed coaches, and continuous evaluation
  • Integrate bilingualism into all instructional settings through districtwide professional learning and in-service coaching
