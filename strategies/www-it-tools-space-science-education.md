# Use the World Wide Web and information technologies as interactive tools in space science education programmes at all levels

## Description
The article recommends, from its review of practice in developed nations, that space science and technology education be pursued interactively with information technology. It states that "Education in space science and technology in developed nations has become highly interactive; the World Wide Web and other information technologies have become useful tools in education programmes at all levels." This positions web-based resources as instruments for making phenomena experienceable where facilities are underdeveloped.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
