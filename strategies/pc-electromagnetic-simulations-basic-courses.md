# Use PC-based electromagnetic simulations as a pedagogical tool in basic electrodynamics courses

## Description
The article recommends having students simulate electromagnetic phenomena in PC labs, arguing that "Today the PC is a suﬃciently powerful tool for model- ing many electromagnetic phenomena within just seconds of computational time." Suggested student exercises include edge effects in parallel plate capacitors, distributed currents, the Hall effect, eddy currents, inductive heating, magnetic diffusion and magnetic shielding. The strategy presupposes supporting theory instruction.
