# Structured Music and Dance Activities

## Description
Structured music and dance activities involve incorporating movement and rhythm into educational content, particularly for preschoolers. Activities are designed to be done indoors in small spaces, and incorporate healthy messaging related to physical activity and healthy eating. These activities integrate math, science, music, creativity, and fundamental motor skills to promote brain development and social interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Music, dance resources, small indoor space, and facilitation by a teacher or caregiver
#### Constraints
- Requires planning to align with educational content, some children may have physical limitations
