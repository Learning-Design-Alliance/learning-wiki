# Use student and teacher voice as reliable, valid inputs in school improvement efforts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study recommends that schools treat what students and teachers report about school experiences, culture, and climate as valuable inputs to improvement. As districts ask how best to support students' mental health and involvement, "this study shows that student and teacher voices are reliable, valuable, and valid inputs for schools to consider in their school improvement efforts." The 5Essentials Survey measures of school climate completed by students and teachers supplied these data.
