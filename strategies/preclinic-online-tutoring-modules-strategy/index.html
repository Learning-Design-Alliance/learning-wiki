Use online tutoring modules before clinic entry to reinforce diagnostic skills through repetitive application on complex cases¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that pre-doctoral students practice radiographic diagnosis through online tutoring modules before entering clinic, so they can "reinforce their foundational knowledge through repetitive application of these skills on complex case exercises." The pilot module was positioned as an adjunctive resource, with the authors suggesting implementation in the overall curriculum and extension to other topics and health-science fields.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Faculty confirmation of module content; more test questions with answer keys and explanations, and improved navigation, per student feedback

Constraints¶

  • The pilot was observational with self-selected module use, so effectiveness was difficult to conclude per the authors

Target Learners¶

  • predoctoral dental students preparing for clinic

Target Learning Goals¶

  • radiographic caries detection and diagnosis

Claims¶

  • Module users showed a different response-frequency pattern: more correct responses but also more extreme over- and under-diagnoses [~W]
  • Most module users reviewed the module before entering clinic [~W]
  • Accessing the online caries diagnosis module did not significantly improve dental students' radiographic diagnostic accuracy [-W]
  • Students who used the module reported positive perceptions and supported its curricular implementation with modifications [+W]
