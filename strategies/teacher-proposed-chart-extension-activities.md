# Extend the workshop with teacher-proposed activities on the completed chart, such as isotope scavenger hunts, decay-chain identification and binding-energy calculations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Teachers at training events proposed follow-up activities that use the finished chart for further learning in physics, chemistry and mathematics. The article lists ideas that "include: ﬁnd an isotope (or chemical element) scavenger hunts; identiﬁcation of decay chains; calculations of binding energy; calculation of energy from fusion reactions; work on integrations and/or volumes (depending on the year group); as well as abundances of isotopes and/or chemical elements". These turn the completed artefact into a resource for subsequent quantitative and exploratory tasks.

### Target Learning Goals
