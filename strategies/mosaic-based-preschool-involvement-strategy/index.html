Use mosaic approach based education and teacher training to support preschool children's involvement¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on their findings, the authors recommend that preschool teachers plan and apply activities supporting children's involvement and different development fields based on mosaic approach based education, and that training, seminars, and workshops on the mosaic approach contribute to preschool teachers' occupational development. They further recommend experimental studies on different developmental skills, programs for younger children, and international comparative studies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need familiarity with the mosaic approach, supported via training, seminars, and workshops

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations follow from a single two-school study in Afyonkarahisar, Turkey

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children, including younger children proposed for future programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • children's involvement levels and different developmental skills

Affordances¶

  • Mosaic Approach Three Stage Framework

Claims¶

  • Mosaic approach based education produced significantly higher post-test involvement levels than the standard preschool program in five-year-old children [+W]
  • Within the experimental group, involvement rose from pre-test through post-test and was retained four weeks later [+W]
  • The control group's involvement also improved significantly under the standard program, but only from extremely low to low level [+W]
