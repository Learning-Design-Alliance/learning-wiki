Implement multi-tiered supports focused on acceleration, not remediation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The blueprint's equity dimension calls for a system of multi-tiered supports with interventions targeted to what individual students need, when needed, with student and family voice prioritized. Tier 1 is "provided to all students; is focused on acceleration, not remediation" and includes culturally and linguistically responsive instruction grounded in universal design for learning, social and emotional learning models, and positive behavioral support programs. Tier 2 serves students at elevated risk; Tier 3 serves students with particularly high levels of risk or whose needs are not met by Tier 2.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tier 2 supports include academic supports, family outreach, counseling, and behavioral supports, which can include small group strategies or partnerships with community-based organizations
  • Tier 3 supports include collaboration with community organizations, one-on-one health or mental health supports, effective special education services, and social workers

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • All students, with elevated tiers for students at elevated or high risk

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accelerated learning grounded in culturally and linguistically responsive instruction rather than remediation of learning gaps
