# Non-stigmatising interventions should focus on trainee–trainer relationships at work and organisational changes improving trainees' ability to seek social support outside work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation for reducing differential attainment is two-pronged: interventions on workplace trainee–trainer relationships and organisational changes to work–life balance and autonomy. The authors stress interventions "should focus on trainee–trainer relationships at work and organisational changes to improve trainees' ability to seek social support outside work", and that these need to be developed, trialled and rigorously evaluated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must be non-stigmatising; the authors state care should be taken to avoid stigmatising trainees with interventions.
#### Constraints
- The authors state interventions need to be developed, trialled and rigorously evaluated; the study provides a basis but not tested interventions.
