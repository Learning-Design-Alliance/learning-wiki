# Present a menu of help-request options to overcome students' question-generation difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To stimulate question formation during tutored problem solving, the article presented students a menu listing five request types: "(a) What's the answer? (b) Is this right? (c) Can I have a little hint? (d) Can I have a big hint? and (e) How do you do it? (Explain it to me)." Each option's meaning was explained with worked examples, and students remained free to ask any other questions, which were answered directly and completely. The two hint levels were included to probe how precisely students matched request content to perceived difficulty.
