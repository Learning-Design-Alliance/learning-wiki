# Planned Physical Activity

## Description
Planning for physical activity is essential for pre-teens and teenagers to ensure they meet daily requirements. The planning process involves considering available spaces, low-cost options, active friends, and activities that the family can do together. Planning helps balance physical activity with homework, casual work, and social activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of physical activity guidelines, a commitment to planning, support from family and friends.
#### Constraints
- Requires discipline and commitment. May be difficult to sustain without support.
