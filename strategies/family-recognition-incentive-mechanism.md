# Use family recognition and weekly ranked scoring to incentivize intern motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes an incentive mechanism in which "the 3-person guidance team comprehensively scored interns’ literature retrieval ability, systematicness of disease knowledge collation, and accuracy of answering parents’ questions, selected the top 10 interns with the best performance and publicized the ranking". Family members also give "targeted thanks and praise to interns in the group based on the actual gains from health consultation", which the authors state enhances "interns’ professional identity, medical curiosity, and sense of value".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scoring mini-program or equivalent to objectively record intern performance data
- Family members willing to provide feedback and recognition
#### Constraints
- The article notes this approach addresses insufficient learning motivation caused by low social recognition of nursing in the Chinese context

### Target Learners
- General surgical nursing interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning motivation
- Professional identity
- Medical curiosity
