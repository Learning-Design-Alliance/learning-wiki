## Description
Alongside the scale, the article urges institutions to keep developing GenAI policies and student-facing guidelines covering ethics of use, transparent citation, limitations and biases of GenAI tools, and security and privacy concerns. The authors note that "guidelines and supporting multimedia content can be an effective way to provide additional context to how GenAI tools might be used in a safe and ethical manner."
