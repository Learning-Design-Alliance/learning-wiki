# Embed self-directed learning in project-based learning carried out on Edmodo over 12 weeks with 5 hours per week.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment lasted for 12 weeks with 5 hours per week as required for training the participants on using Edmodo in conducting the project to develop SDL among them. The project included the stages countdown to project launch, project launching, throughout the project and project evaluation, and SDL can be embedded in PBL. Students collect resources, organize work, manage long-term activities, design, revise and share their ideas and experiences with authentic audience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The researcher must train the participants throughout all of the project stages on using the platform individually and collaboratively.
- Assignments with each individual and each small group must be checked on a regular basis to ensure movement toward applying the project objectives.
#### Constraints
- The intervention was delivered by the researcher herself to her own Research Methods course students without a control group, so generalization requires replication with more number of students.

### Target Learners
- university EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed learning
- research methods
- abstract writing
