# Use information technology to streamline documentation and protect feedback time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To overcome excessive time spent on documentation, the article recommends that "Information technology can streamline processes, improve efficiency and ensure sufficient time for feedback". It further advises arranging feedback time reasonably, establishing an effective feedback mechanism, and reducing educator workloads with incentives, since without sufficient time the tools lack their desired educational effect.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to reducing educator workload and resourcing feedback time
#### Constraints
- Time-pressure barrier is systemic in high-volume settings like Chinese tertiary hospitals

### Target Learners
- clinical nursing educators and novice nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- timely, structured formative feedback
