Facilitate a multi-session collaborative workgroup to co-develop state guidance¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

R15CC designed and facilitated a highly collaborative six-session workgroup in which NDE staff and education leaders from across the state used evidence and research to make collaborative decisions about the MRI menu content and a related guidance document. The team adapted the Collaborative Solution Discovery framework, "a problem-solving framework for finding sustainable solutions to local complex problems," into an inquiry-driven problem-solving cycle guiding the sessions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Involving interest holders from across the state and inviting education leaders from NDE and LEAs to build commitment

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • State education agency staff and local education leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building SEA staff capacity to lead complex statewide initiatives and design implementable resources

Related Strategies¶

  • Pilot guidance documents with a small set of schools and iterate before wide dissemination
  • Cross-division convenings and joint review sessions to strengthen a state education agency's internal collaboration on multilingual policy
  • Provide synthesized and customized evidence tailored to the state context
