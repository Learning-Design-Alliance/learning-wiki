# Use CTDR-derived teacher characteristics to develop faculty development programs and evaluation criteria for supervising dentists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the identified characteristics of good clinical teachers be used to build faculty development (FD) programs for supervising dentists and to formulate evaluation criteria for their performance as mentors. It states that "the insights gained from this study can be harnessed to develop FD programs for supervising dentists and formulate appropriate evaluation criteria for their performance as mentors", addressing a gap that previously hampered evidence-based FD guidelines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated set of teacher characteristics perceived by the target learner group (here, CTDRs)
#### Constraints
- The identified themes blend Japan-specific and CTDR-stage-specific attributes, and new themes may be dental-field-specific rather than CTDR-exclusive

### Target Learners
- supervising dentists
- clinical trainee dental residents

### Target Learning Goals
- improving clinical supervision quality and mentor performance evaluation
