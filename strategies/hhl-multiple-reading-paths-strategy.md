# Offer multiple reading paths through the walkthrough depending on reader background

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper's 'How to use this paper' section directs different readers to different entry points: readers with fresh memory of the basic concepts start at the analytical Section II; readers needing review go through the Appendix first; and readers who already studied HHL elsewhere start with the numerical example in Section III. As printed, "For readers who need reviews on the basic concepts first, they are encouraged to go over the Appendix first before reading the main text." Appendix equations are numbered with a 'V' prefix and reuse main-text equation numbers for cross-referencing.
