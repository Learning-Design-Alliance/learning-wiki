# When a sudden shift to digitally-based learning is required, take time to properly prepare instructors and develop support structures for instructors and learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends, implicitly through its critique, that institutions avoid making sudden shifts to digitally-based learning without preparation. It warns that efforts "are being made without spending the time to properly prepare instructors or develop appropriate support structures for them or for learners". The strategy is to invest that preparation time and build those support structures before or during a required shift to digital learning and instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time allocated to preparing instructors
- Appropriate support structures developed for both instructors and learners
#### Constraints
- Applies to situations where everyone is required to make a sudden shift to digitally-based learning and instruction

### Target Learners
- instructors making a sudden shift to digitally-based learning
- learners in digitally-based learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining effective learning and instruction during rapid digital transitions
