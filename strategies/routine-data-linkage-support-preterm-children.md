# Use routine data linkage across health and education services to identify and longitudinally support children at risk from prematurity and early school entry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends greater use of routine data linkage so that at-risk children can be "monitored and supported longitudinally, across health and education services". The study itself modelled this approach, linking Born in Bradford maternity records with local authority educational records to identify Early Academic Starts children and track their EYFSP outcomes. This strategy supports evidence-based allocation of resources at the school-entry juncture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Linked hospital maternity and local authority educational records including gestational age at birth and child development at 5 years old
#### Constraints
- The authors note they do not have permission to republish independently the data shared by the cohort, as per the conditions of their data sharing agreement

### Target Learners
- children born preterm entering school

### Target Learning Goals
- early developmental readiness for schooling
