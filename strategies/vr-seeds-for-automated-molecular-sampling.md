# Use interactive VR molecular manipulation to generate seeds for automated sampling searches and to let experts express 3d biomolecular intuition collaboratively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend exploiting human intuition inside interactive VR simulation for problems whose search spaces are too large for brute force approaches: "using VR technologies to generate seeds for subsequent automated searches could represent a powerful new strategy for obtaining molecular insight, which is likely to save on valuable computational clock cycles". They further suggest machines might learn efficient navigation strategies by accumulating observational data on how human experts navigate hyper-dimensional biomolecular spaces in VR, and aim to investigate VR's potential in research, communication, and educational contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An interactive molecular simulation framework responsive enough for experts to formulate and evaluate dynamical hypotheses in large molecular systems in real time
#### Constraints
- The authors frame this as a suggested future strategy and aim, not as a tested result of the reported studies

### Target Learners
- research experts such as structural biologists, molecular modelers, and medicinal chemists
- non-specialists learning about molecular science

### Target Learning Goals
- testing hypotheses about molecular mechanism and design
- understanding complex 3d molecular data
