# Shared Reflection

## Description
Shared reflection involves creating a classroom culture where each student is recognized as being on an individual path of improvement—and, no student has reached the end of the path, because there is no end. It involves students sharing their reflections on their academic work, and collectively supporting each other. By sharing their achievements, students help those who struggled with that particular task, and by sharing their weak spots they troubleshoot as they work through a problem set or have a peer edit a rough draft.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom culture of trust and support, structured opportunities for sharing reflections.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation to ensure that students feel comfortable sharing their weaknesses.

### Personalization
- Teachers can index, search, and organize material by theme. Anonymous posting allows them to receive guidance and support without embarrassment.
