# Combine online pre-learning with supervised one-hour small-group simulator practice for invasive procedural skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program pairs an online handout with slides and a 10-minute video covering indications, consent, equipment, procedure flow, and complications, followed by "a group of approximately four to five individuals at a time of one hour" of simulator practice supervised by one or two hematologists. Instruction follows a 26-item checklist and adds clinical tips such as limiting aspiration volume and working quickly to protect specimen quality and reduce patient pain. Students train until they can perform the full procedure series.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision by one or two hematologists to provide guidance and ensure safety
- A validated checklist to structure instruction and evaluation
- Pre-alignment of evaluators on scoring boundaries
#### Constraints
- Simulator limitations (no skin reproduction, no fluid collection, no patient communication) should be explained to instructors and students
- Items scored below 50% completion (complications, body position, puncture site, inspection items) should be addressed in future programs

### Target Learners
- fourth- and fifth-year medical students
- young internal medicine physicians (planned extension)

### Target Learning Goals
- BMAT procedural technique
- communication with patients including consent and explanation
- knowledge of BMAT indications and complications
