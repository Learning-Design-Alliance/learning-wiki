# Embed actual past licensing-examination questions into chapter review materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The teaching team compiled actual and practice questions from the past 25 years of physician licensing examinations for each chapter's knowledge points, and used the most recent examination question as an illustrative practice exercise with emphasis on the topic's high prevalence in the examination. The article reports that the knowledge point "Massive loss of gastric and intestinal fluid is the main cause of hypokalemia" appeared in the 2003, 2012, and 2013 examinations, and presents a 30-year-old patient vignette as the practice item to improve preparedness and adaptability for the test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a bank of past examination questions
- Faculty synthesis of essential knowledge domains relevant to the examination
#### Constraints
- Question selection must be aligned with identified examination knowledge domains, per the article's described process

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students preparing for staged licensing examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- Familiarity with licensing examination question formats and high-frequency topics
