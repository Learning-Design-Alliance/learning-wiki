# Incorporate persuasive system design features — tailoring, personalisation and self-monitoring — into web-based interventions to improve adherence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article grounds PaRK's design in persuasive systems design, defined as "computerised software or information systems designed to reinforce, change or shape attitudes or behaviours". Citing a systematic review by Kelders and colleagues, it states that "adherence to web-based interventions can be improved by incorporating persuasive technology elements into the design". PaRK's persuasive features include tailoring of feedback and program, personalisation via participants' own dashboard and diary pages, and self-monitoring of progress through goals and modules.
