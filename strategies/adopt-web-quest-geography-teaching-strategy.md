# Adopt Web Quest Strategy in geography teaching and train teachers in its use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the study's positive results, the author recommends "Using the Web Quest Strategy in teaching the Natural Resources Unit in Geography subject and not only depending on the Traditional Method" to improve eighth-grade students' concept acquisition and achievement. Further recommendations include Ministry of Education training for geography teachers in the strategy, attention from curriculum designers, a dedicated ministry unit for producing Web Quest-based geography units, and using technology that supports student activity and self-learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in employing the Web Quest strategy
- Prepared teaching plans and curated electronic resources for the target unit
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one quasi-experiment in one Jordanian directorate; the author calls for larger samples from different regions and other subjects

### Target Learners
- Eighth basic grade geography students in Jordanian public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition of geographic concepts and improved achievement
