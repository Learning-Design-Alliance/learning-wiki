Establish reciprocal communication with learners to understand their contexts and create equitable online experiences¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends establishing reciprocal communication with learners in ERT design, which allows teachers to understand the contexts learners go through and create equitable online experiences that consider learners' particular learning needs. This helps teachers identify digital pedagogy practices that support rather than impede learning, and to determine challenges learners face such as technology access and unreliable Internet.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ongoing two-way communication channels between instructor and students

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • university students in emergency remote teaching, including those with limited technology access

Target Learning Goals¶

  • equitable online learning experiences
  • inclusive digital pedagogy

Related Strategies¶

  • Design assignments with flexible deadlines during ERT to accommodate learners' increased obligations in crisis times
  • Ensure timely responses to student inquiries and prompt feedback on assignments during ERT
  • Place resources such as FAQs, introductory videos, and assignment rubrics in an easily accessible course location
