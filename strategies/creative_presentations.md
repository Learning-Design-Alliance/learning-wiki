# Creative Presentations

## Description
Creative presentations are visual aids designed to engage learners and keep their attention through the use of bright colors, creative fonts, and a combination of photos, charts, and icons. These presentations go beyond traditional slide formats to present information in a visually stimulating and memorable way. By mixing and matching styles, such as combining script fonts with modern fonts, and using image frames to seamlessly incorporate pictures, presenters can create presentations that capture and maintain learners' interest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to presentation software and design tools. Implementation involves careful planning and integration of visuals. May require training in presentation design.
#### Constraints
- Overly complex or distracting visuals can detract from the content. Ineffective use of design can confuse learners. Requires time and effort to create high-quality presentations.
