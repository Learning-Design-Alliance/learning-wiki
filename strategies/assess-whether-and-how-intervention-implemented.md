# Assess whether and how the planned intervention is implemented

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A core strategy the guide recommends is measuring fidelity and enactment: researchers should 'assess whether and how the planned intervention is implemented' as part of the impact study. This documents the actual delivery of the intervention against what was planned, enabling interpretation of impact findings in light of implementation quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data collection within the impact study must capture whether and how the planned intervention was delivered.
