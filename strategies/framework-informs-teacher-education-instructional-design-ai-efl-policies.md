# Use the integrated technical–pedagogical limitation framework to inform teacher education, instructional design, and institutional AI policies in undergraduate EFL programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends using its unified analytical perspective as an evidence-based foundation for responsible AI implementation in undergraduate EFL higher education. It states that "By integrating technical and pedagogical limitations into a higher-order analytical framework, this review provides an evidence-based foundation for teacher education, instructional design, institutional policies, and the responsible implementation of AI in undergraduate EFL higher education." The strategy treats AI challenges as a multidimensional implementation problem involving system performance, access, learner behavior, teacher mediation, instructional design, and institutional conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher mediation, instructional design, and students' AI literacy as conditions for meaningful AI-supported learning experiences
#### Constraints
- Findings reflect Scopus-indexed evidence from 2021–2026 concentrated in a limited number of national contexts and should not be assumed to generalize uniformly across undergraduate EFL settings

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate EFL students in higher education
- EFL teachers and teacher educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Responsible integration of AI in EFL instruction
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated output
