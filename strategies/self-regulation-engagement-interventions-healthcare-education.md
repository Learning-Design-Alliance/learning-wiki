# Combine self-regulation scaffolds, interactive engagement methods and clinical mentorship to support healthcare students' academic success

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured interventions in both academic and clinical settings: integrating planning techniques and metacognitive strategies into the curriculum, using interactive teaching methods such as gamification and peer learning, and strengthening clinical engagement through structured reflection, clear clinical objectives and one-on-one mentorship. It also suggests technology-based tools such as "virtual simulations and personalized feedback mechanisms" to support self-directed learning and confidence in clinical decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should be implemented in both academic and clinical settings, as the article states structured interventions should span both environments.
#### Constraints
