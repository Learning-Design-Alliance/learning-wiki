# Provide remote students with sample data, video lecture, and virtual walkthrough when in-person access is impossible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
When pandemic conditions halted in-person instruction, the authors provided remote students with a recorded prelab lecture, a virtual lab walkthrough slide deck, and the datasets they would have collected. The article states remote students "were sent sample data to analyze for their reports from home," and the report scores indicate these students still achieved the learning goals, though the article notes in-person students had the added in-lab experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A repository of authentic sample data (LabVIEW outputs and pump-probe traces) and instructional walkthrough materials must be prepared in advance.
#### Constraints
- Remote students missed out on the in-lab experience of performing the measurements themselves.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physical chemistry students unable to attend in person

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis of autocorrelation and pump-probe measurements
- understanding of solid-state chemistry and ultrafast spectroscopy concepts
