# Deliver targeted educational interventions combining e-learning, mentorship and simulation-based training, prioritising nurses with knowledge deficits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's recommendation for closing enteral nutrition knowledge gaps is structured, multi-modal training targeted at the nurses the regression identified as disadvantaged. It recommends "Targeted educational interventions—including modular e-learning for knowledge gaps, mentorship programs to reinforce attitudes and simulation-based training for skill development". Educational modules should integrate theoretical instruction with "practical demonstrations and case studies" engaging real-world scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- structured, evidence-based training that integrates theoretical instruction with practical demonstrations and case-based learning
- institutional support through standardised protocols, quality management and adequate resources
#### Constraints
- cross-sectional design limits causality inference; longitudinal studies are warranted to evaluate long-term effectiveness

### Target Learners
- ICU registered nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of enteral nutrition management including tube flushing, complication prevention and nutritional risk screening
