## Description
A strategy in which researchers use data visualization when communicating their work, so that 'complex concepts' become 'accessible' to audiences. The guide treats visualization as one of the graphic-design tools, alongside photographs, images, color, tables, figures, and type, for creating useful publications and digital products.
