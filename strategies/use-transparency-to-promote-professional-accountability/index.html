Use transparency to promote professional accountability¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Among its three concluding recommendations, the review advises policymakers to "use transparency to promote professional accountability." This strategy treats transparency as an instrument for strengthening the professional form of accountability, complementing the recommendation to expand professional accountability's role. It is offered as part of building a complete accountability system for education.
