# Children's Literature Integration

## Description
Integrating children's literature, especially multicultural literature, into math instruction to enhance engagement and comprehension. By using storybooks that cover math topics, instructors can supplement in-class learning, create connections to real-world contexts, and motivate learners. This approach can be extended through digital libraries or home lending systems to support reading with families.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a collection of children's literature that covers math topics. Digital versions can supplement a physical library. Requires time to integrate these books into lesson plans.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the literature. Requires careful selection to ensure alignment with learning objectives.
