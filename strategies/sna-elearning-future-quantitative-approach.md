# Future SNA e-learning studies should include a quantitative approach exploiting the statistical power of SNA to explain complex systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends a methodological shift toward quantitative analysis in future work: "Whenever possible, future studies should also include a quantitative approach that exploits the statistical power of SNA to explain complex systems." This follows the review's observation that existing e-learning SNA studies have applied various descriptive methods, and aims to strengthen explanatory power.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Study designs and data that support quantitative statistical analysis of network measures.
