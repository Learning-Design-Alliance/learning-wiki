# Offer structured swimming training programs to encourage stress reduction in students aged 11-13

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "swimming training program was effective to reduce stress level of the students ages 11 -13" and that "people can be encouraged to participating in swimming sports or any program including physical activity." The tested recipe was a program "planned as two sessions, each lasting two hours for 8 weeks", designed by sport-branch experts, with attendance recorded and sessions video-recorded.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured swimming sessions led under expert design, with attendance noted by an instructor
- Pre- and post-administration of a stress measure to evaluate the program
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one school cohort of 60 students aged 11-13; the authors suggest future programs "for all different age categories" to see differences between ages and programs

### Target Learners
- students aged 11-13

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing stress levels through physical activity
