Embed AI policy literacy, peer-led communities of practice, and shared AI resources into professional development¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's recommendations for professional development design are threefold: "embedding artificial intelligence policy literacy into professional development, fostering peer-led communities of practice, and creating shared resources on artificial intelligence." These respond directly to the qualitative finding that educators struggled to reconcile institutional policy gaps with personal ethical values, and they extend PD beyond individual technical skill toward collective, policy-informed practice.
