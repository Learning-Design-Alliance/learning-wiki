Embed AI policy literacy, peer-led communities of practice, and shared AI resources into professional development¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's recommendations for professional development design are threefold: "embedding artificial intelligence policy literacy into professional development, fostering peer-led communities of practice, and creating shared resources on artificial intelligence." These respond directly to the qualitative finding that educators struggled to reconcile institutional policy gaps with personal ethical values, and they extend PD beyond individual technical skill toward collective, policy-informed practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional and peer infrastructure to sustain communities of practice and shared resource creation

Constraints¶

  • Recommended in the context of Global South higher education EAP settings; the article notes the PD model's scalability needs further research

Target Learners¶

  • Higher education EAP educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI policy literacy
  • Collective ethical practice around generative AI
  • Shared resource development

Related Strategies¶

  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Pursue inclusive digital strategies, educator professional development, and ethical AI governance for AI in adult education
  • Support faculty AI literacy through needs assessment, targeted training, teaching resources, and communities of practice
  • Supports to Raise Teachers' Expectancy and Value and Lower Cost for AI Use
  • Convene cross-functional generative AI task forces to guide ethical adoption decisions and procurement
  • Fund AI literacy initiatives integrating AI ethics into curricula and adapting assessments
