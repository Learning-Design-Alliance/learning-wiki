Sustain school mental health programs through multi-payer fee schedules and alignment with existing initiatives¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief's sustainability approach is to move districts beyond one-time grant funds by aligning resources and partnerships. SCOE works "with its districts to help them achieve a stage where they can implement a multipayer fee schedule, ensuring the sustainability of their programs beyond the initial infusion of one-time funds," while integrating initiatives such as the Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative, the Student Behavioral Health Incentive Program, and the upcoming Cal-Well round.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Strategic alignment with existing county and state initiatives and diversified funding sources

Constraints¶

  • Reliance on one-time funds until a multi-payer fee schedule is achieved

Target Learners¶

  • students across Stanislaus County districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • continued availability of school mental health supports

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustain the school mental health system through braided funding, LCAP indicators, and ongoing program mapping
  • Sustain school mental health services by blending one-time grants with ongoing reimbursement streams
  • Sustain district mental health initiatives by braiding grant funding into annual plans, ongoing surveys, and reimbursement streams
  • Sustain cross-agency collaboration through a dedicated district leader and monthly partner meetings
