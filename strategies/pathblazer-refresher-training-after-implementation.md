# Provide refresher training after implementation begins, not only pre-adoption training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The implementation notes identify a suggested improvement from the Floyd County context: "refresher training to be available once implementation began." This augments the standard model of one day of in-person training prior to adoption plus a half-day off-site session after a short period of use. The recommendation is to plan for ongoing training touchpoints during live use rather than front-loading all training before adoption.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Initial training: 1 day in-person before adoption and ½ day off-site after initial use
#### Constraints
- Suggested improvement raised in the Floyd County implementation context

### Target Learners
- teachers implementing Pathblazer in elementary classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained, faithful implementation of the Pathblazer reading and mathematics program
