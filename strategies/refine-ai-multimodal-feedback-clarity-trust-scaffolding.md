# Refine AI multimodal feedback by enhancing explanation clarity, building trust mechanisms, adding adaptive scaffolding, and extending feedback channels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose four refinements for AI-facilitated multimodal feedback systems: "enhancing explanation clarity by providing explanations for specialized terms", "building trust mechanisms by ensuring AI feedback's explainability with clear rubrics" and stopping thresholds for phrases like "you could say more", "developing adaptive scaffolding strategies by implementing multi-level hints", and "extending feedback channels by incorporating audio options and color coding." These respond directly to observed clarity, trust, and cognitive-load problems in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to course materials for inline references linking AI feedback to specific slide information
#### Constraints
- The combined feedback risked cognitive overload, particularly within the time constraints of the Prolific platform

### Target Learners
- online higher-education learners
- beginner-level students who need simplified language

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehensible, trustworthy, and actionable feedback on open-ended responses
