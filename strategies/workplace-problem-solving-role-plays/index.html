Teach workplace problem solving through case studies and role plays with a structured procedure¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Module 5 applies cultural knowledge to everyday work situations. Participants analyse a case (Aurora being told to shut up during overtime) from worker and supervisor perspectives, follow a nine-step problem-solving procedure from defining the problem through review and adjustment, work through five worker-dilemma tasks, and perform paired supervisor-worker role plays based on realistic workplace conflicts such as misunderstanding silence, perceived slowness, and safety-rule conflicts with prior qualifications.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Role play cards pairing a supervisor and a worker perspective
  • A structured problem-solving sequence displayed to the group

Constraints¶

  • Assessment for this module is by role play

Target Learners¶

  • Workplace supervisors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Resolving workplace problems arising from cultural and language differences
