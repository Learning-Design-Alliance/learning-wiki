# Differentiate types of parental involvement in involvement-promoting strategies such as parent workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study found that involvement types differ in their associations with performance — only mother-child communication was positively related to all three subjects — the authors recommend that "parental involvement-promoting strategies, such as schools holding workshops for parents, should focus on how to encourage different types of parental involvement". Educators are advised to acknowledge disparities among involvement types rather than treat involvement as a single construct.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools or stakeholders able to run parent-facing programs such as workshops
#### Constraints
- Evidence base covers two non-academic involvement types only; academically related types were untested due to data limits

### Target Learners
- Chinese middle school students and their parents

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic performance in Chinese, mathematics, and English
