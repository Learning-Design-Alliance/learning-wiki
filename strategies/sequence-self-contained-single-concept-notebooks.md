# Split advanced topics into a sequence of self-contained, single-concept notebooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
OSSCAR designs each web application to be self-contained and focused on one core concept; when more complex concepts are needed, content is split into a sequence of propaedeutic smaller notebooks. This makes each application usable without teacher supervision and lets the series guide students in a progressive learning process. The article states: "we prefer and suggest splitting the content into a sequence of propaedeutic smaller notebooks, each focusing on a single (sub)topic".
