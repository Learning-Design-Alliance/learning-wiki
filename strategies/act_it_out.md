# Act It Out

## Description
Role-play how to handle different situations, especially those that students struggle with. Write each scenario on a note card. For example: You get invited to a birthday party. You slip and fall while walking home from school. You open a birthday present you don’t like. Then, have students talk them through or act them out. How would they feel? What would they do? How does their feeling connect with their behavior?

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Note cards with scenario descriptions, time for role-playing and discussion
#### Constraints
- Some students may feel uncomfortable acting or sharing personal experiences, requiring thoughtful facilitation and adaptation.
