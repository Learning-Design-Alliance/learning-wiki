# Use dynamic frames and end-of-unit tables with interpretive tasks to discriminate old and new properties of revised notions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using dynamic frames, a tool from philosophy of science, formatted to visualize continuity and change in both concepts and constructs. The frame structures "an end-of-unit table containing interpretive tasks to discriminate between the old and the new properties of the notion, identifying the correct context of application of each one". Tables also bridge the two theories in terms of categorical structure rather than limiting processes.
