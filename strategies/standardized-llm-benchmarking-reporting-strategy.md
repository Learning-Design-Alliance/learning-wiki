# Standardize reporting of LLM versions, processing dates, input selection criteria, and data quality protocols in future MCQ benchmarking research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its future directions, the article calls for standardized reporting in primary research, akin to the CONSORT‐AI guidelines for clinical trials, noting that "Standardized reporting of LLM versions, processing dates, input selection criteria, and data quality protocols could minimize study ambiguities/heterogeneity and enhance study replicability." Authors should explicitly state how MCQs were developed or obtained and whether they were accessible online, especially since newer LLMs possess web browsing capabilities. Comparative discipline‐specific assessments of non‐OpenAI models and of custom chatbots should also be broadened as data become available.
