# Families use positive family capital to foster children's creativity and geography performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that parents deploy family capital deliberately: "Parents should utilize positive FC to promote creativity and improve students' performance in geography by adjusting their consumption structures and educational philosophies." They also advise careful attention to family education, since "the cultivation of a stimulating familial setting can significantly enhance children's creative development."
