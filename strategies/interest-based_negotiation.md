# Interest-Based Negotiation

## Description
Interest-Based Negotiation involves establishing different perspectives, backgrounds, interests, and options for resolution, creating a single team out of the interested parties. It centers around people of different positions working together instead of against each other. It fosters a collaborative manner of argumentation and provides a healthier model for conflict resolution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training in Interest-Based Negotiation, facilitator to guide the process, willingness to engage in collaborative problem-solving.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming, requires a commitment to collaboration, may be challenging to overcome entrenched positions.
