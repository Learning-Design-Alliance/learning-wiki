# Select simulator fidelity according to trainee experience level and prefer low-fidelity simulators where equivalent, to use resources efficiently

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends choosing "the most appropriate simulator for the target audience" for efficient use of resources, noting that HF simulators are generally more expensive and that LF simulators may suffice for inexperienced trainees while HF may benefit experienced trainees. It also stresses reporting simulator costs in future studies, since cost was stated in only a few of the 17 studies (e.g. a CDN $20 LF urology model versus a CDN $3700 HF model).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cost information must be reported in simulator training studies to allow comparison of this parameter
#### Constraints
- Existing data are insufficient to draw firm conclusions about level of experience and simulator fidelity; further study is needed

### Target Learners
- novice medical trainees
- experienced surgical residents and faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural and surgical skill acquisition at lower cost
