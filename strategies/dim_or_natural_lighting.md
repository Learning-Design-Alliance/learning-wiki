# Dim or Natural Lighting

## Description
Using dim or natural lighting in classrooms or learning spaces to promote a calm and focused environment. This involves adjusting lighting to mimic natural light, which can have a positive impact on children’s moods and creativity. Harsh, flickering, or humming lights can be distracting or painful, especially for individuals with autism. Adjustable lighting allows for control over visual stimuli, which is beneficial for creating a calming effect.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adjustable lighting systems (e.g., LED lights with dimming and tuning capabilities), understanding of the impact of different light settings on sensory processing.
#### Constraints
- Initial investment in adjustable lighting systems may be required. Effectiveness may vary depending on individual sensitivities to light.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learner behavior and focus levels under different lighting conditions. Collect feedback from learners on their comfort and concentration levels. Monitor the frequency of distractions or sensory-related issues.

## Impact
- Research indicates positive associations between certain colors and mood, and adjustable lighting can help create a happy, creative environment. Calming sensory lights have been shown to benefit children with autism by meeting their sensory needs and promoting emotional well-being.
