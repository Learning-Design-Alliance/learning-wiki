# Begin teaching sequences with activities that draw out students' ideas and provide opportunities for expression

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As a practical implication of locating students within the ADD before teaching, the article recommends that "lecturers should begin teaching sequences with activiti es which draw out students’ ideas and provide opportunities for expression of those ideas". This diagnostic opening lets the lecturer organise students' answers using the ADD to find the disciplinary discernment level at which teaching should start, exemplified with students expressing their answers in writing after studying a simulation.
