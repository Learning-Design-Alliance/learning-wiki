# Provide special instruction for individual difficulties without full-day withdrawal from the regular classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The outline recommends special instruction to meet individual problems, including corrective gymnastics, special teaching for specialized mental disabilities in arithmetic, language, and reading, development of special talents, corrective work for sensory and motor defects such as speech difficulties, and counteractive aids for obsessive mental imagery. The special teacher gives daily such time as the individual case requires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Co-operation between teacher and diagnostician should be particularly close in these cases
#### Constraints
- None of these special measures necessitates withdrawal from the ordinary classroom during the whole day or the formation of special classes

### Target Learners
- young offenders with individual physical, mental, or sensory difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- correction of individual disabilities and development of special talents
