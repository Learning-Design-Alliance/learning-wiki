# Picture Writing

## Description
Picture writing unlocks the imagination, builds confidence, and develops foundational early literacy skills. Drawing pictures allows children to unleash their imagination on paper. A writer is born the first time a child puts crayon to paper. When pre-writers use picture writing, they are expressing ideas through illustrations. As their literacy skills develop over time, the picture will serve as a plan for their writing. A picture sparks ideas, provides details, and serves as a framework for a piece of writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires paper and crayons or other drawing materials. Requires time for children to draw, talk about their pictures, and write about their pictures.
#### Constraints
- Children may need guidance to stay focused on the picture and connect it to their writing. The picture is a writing tool, not an entry into an art contest.

- Assess children's ability to match their words to their pictures. Evaluate the development of their writing skills over time. Observe their engagement and enthusiasm during picture writing activities.

## Impact
- Picture writing helps children understand that illustrations represent ideas and that ideas can be represented in print. It is a huge concept in early writing and early reading.
