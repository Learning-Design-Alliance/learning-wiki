# Blend e-delivered theoretical content with face-to-face practice to develop student teachers' teaching skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy combines e-learning tools — computers, internet lessons, lectures, and training sessions in computer laboratories and intelligent classes, with assignments delivered electronically via a learning management system — with regular face-to-face meetings. The article reports that experimental groups used "the blended E- learning in Field Education course" while meeting students "face to face in the same time every weak along the study period," and students reported that blended e-learning helped them improve their teaching skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning management system (King Saud University used Blackboard) for delivering assignments electronically
- Access to computer laboratories or intelligent classes
- Students have previously studied teaching methods coursework
#### Constraints
- Applied to student teachers in field education; the one-group design limits causal attribution

### Target Learners
- student teachers
- pre-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teaching implementation skills
- planning skills
- classroom management skills
- evaluation skills
