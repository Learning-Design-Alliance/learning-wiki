# Design agents with coordinated media features to differentially target specific learning and motivational outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (3 review, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q4` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends designing an agent's appearance, voice, nonverbal communication, and messaging as a coordinated whole persona rather than isolating single media features. Carefully coordinated features may increase believability and naturalness, and by careful design "the agent can differentially impact specific learning and motivational outcomes." The review also endorses implementing motivational coaching as a distinct agent rather than rolling it into a general help agent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must coordinate appearance, voice, nonverbal communication, and messaging around an intended role and persona
- Agent role instantiations should be validated with learners to confirm perception as intended
#### Constraints
- Perceptions of realism or attractiveness differ among learner groups depending on their ages

- K-12 students
