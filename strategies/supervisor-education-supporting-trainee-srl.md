# Targeted supervisor education on supporting trainee SRL and improving feedback quality in formative workplace assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that supervisors co-regulate every phase of trainees' SRL during the mini-CEX, the authors recommend that "Supervisors may benefit from targeted education regarding their role in supporting a trainee's SRL and improving feedback quality". They also state that trainees will require a deeper understanding of their individual SRL to participate meaningfully in formative learning opportunities as competency-based medical education is further implemented.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors who understand their co-regulatory role across goal setting, task translation and self-reflection phases
#### Constraints
- Recommendation derives from a qualitative study of twelve trainees in one specialty in Melbourne; the authors note their experiences may not fully represent other specialist trainees

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical trainees and their clinical supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulated learning and high-quality formative feedback in workplace-based assessment

### Affordances
- [Zimmerman Srl Framework Lens](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md)
