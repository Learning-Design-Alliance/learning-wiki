# Embed AI training for pre-service science teachers within science pedagogy and inquiry-based teaching rather than general tool operation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teacher educators go beyond general AI tool operation, such as using ChatGPT or generating teaching materials, and instead embed AI training within science pedagogy and inquiry-based teaching. Suggested activities include guiding pre-service teachers in using AI to design inquiry questions, evaluate AI-generated scientific models, employ simulations for experimental reasoning, judge the scientific accuracy of AI-generated content, and identify ethical risks and biases in AI-supported instruction. This follows from the finding that AI literacy relates to integration intention through AI-TPACK and self-efficacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher educators with capacity to design science-specific AI activities spanning inquiry design, model evaluation, simulation use, accuracy judgment, and ethical reflection
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and self-report; the article states findings should be interpreted as associations rather than a demonstrated developmental sequence

### Target Learners
- pre-service science teachers in teacher education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- subject-specific AI-related pedagogical competence for inquiry-based science teaching
- critical evaluation of AI-generated scientific content and ethical risks
