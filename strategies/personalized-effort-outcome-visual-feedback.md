# Provide personalized visual feedback linking self-reported effort, judgments of learning, and actual test performance per study strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
After the test, students in the feedback condition received "a personalized visual overview" of their self-reported experiences (mental effort, concurrent and prospective judgments of learning) and actual learning outcomes (test performance), per study strategy. To encourage processing, participants were prompted to interpret the feedback by writing down what they could conclude from the visual overview. This intervention significantly improved students' considerations about interleaved studying, though not enough to make them prefer it over blocked studying.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback must display the student's own self-reported effort and judgments of learning alongside their actual test performance, per strategy
- Students should be prompted to interpret the feedback by writing down conclusions
#### Constraints
- Feedback improved considerations about interleaved studying but did not make students consider it more effective or desirable than blocked studying
- In Experiment 2, participants could not compare study experiences or outcomes because they studied with only one strategy

### Target Learners
- higher-education students learning with self-selected study strategies

### Target Learning Goals
- calibrating study-strategy perceptions with actual learning outcomes in category learning tasks
