Include both knowledge resources and cognitive supports in museum mobile learning materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's trails combined 'knowledge resources' (curator information plus tutors' and students' responses) with 'cognitive supports' (questions to consider and prompts to reflect on learning processes and differences between responses). This dual structure, using Hsi's characterisation, aimed to encourage students to look at objects in new ways and reflect on their own learning in the museum.
