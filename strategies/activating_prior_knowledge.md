# Activating Prior Knowledge

## Description
Activating students' prior knowledge involves prompting learners to recall and share what they already know about a topic before introducing new information. This process helps learners make connections between existing knowledge and new concepts, increasing engagement and comprehension. Strategies include brainstorming, listing everything they know about a topic, and creating concept maps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires structured prompts, such as open-ended questions or graphic organizers. Instructor must facilitate discussion and address any misconceptions.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time to uncover and address misconceptions. Effectiveness depends on learners' ability to connect new information to their prior knowledge.
