# Use Current Events & Fictional Characters

## Description
Using current events and fictional characters as tools to teach social awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to current events articles or fictional texts, class time for analysis and discussion, and a skilled facilitator.
#### Constraints
- Some current events or fictional characters may be sensitive or controversial. Learners may struggle to relate to certain characters or events. Discussions can be dominated by a few learners.

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, including K–12, higher education, and adult learners in professional development programs

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop social awareness, enhance empathy, improve understanding of different perspectives, promote critical thinking, and foster positive relationships.

### Affordances
- Provides real-world examples and relatable characters, encourages critical thinking, promotes empathy and understanding, supports positive relationships, and fosters social awareness.

### Personalization
- Current events and fictional characters can be chosen to align with different age groups, cultural backgrounds, or interests. Support can be provided for learners who struggle with social awareness or empathy.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners’ participation in discussions. Review learners’ written or verbal reflections on current events or fictional characters. Evaluate learners’ understanding of social awareness and empathy.

## Impact
- Using current events and fictional characters can enhance social awareness, promote empathy, and improve understanding of different perspectives. Real-world examples and relatable characters can make social awareness more engaging and meaningful.
