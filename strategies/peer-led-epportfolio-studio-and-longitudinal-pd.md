# Provide dedicated peer-led ePortfolio support focused on ePortfolio making skills, and scale professional development longitudinally with adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes two implementable supports for scaling ePortfolio. First, as adoption grew, IUPUI created "a peer-led ePortfolio Studio ... as a dedicated campus space where students could work with a peer mentor on creating and refining their ePortfolios and getting feedback," focusing on making skills such as design, structure, aesthetics, audience awareness, cohesiveness, and multi-modal communication. Second, professional development is approached "with a longitudinal lens," iterating as buy-in shifts from a single faculty member to program-wide commitment, using consultations, workshops, Communities of Practice, grants, and conference funding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- coordination and development of groups of faculty and staff working with intentionality over a period of time
- professional development needs change as ePortfolio activities expand across courses and programs
#### Constraints
- University College lacked resources to sustain intensive professional development at scale, and pilot-year student success gains were not consistently sustained

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- faculty and staff implementing ePortfolio

### Target Learning Goals
- ePortfolio making skills
- integrative learning
- reflection
