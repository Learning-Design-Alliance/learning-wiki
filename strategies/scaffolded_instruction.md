# Scaffolded Instruction

## Description
Scaffolded instruction involves supporting learners in various ways as they learn and gradually withdrawing supports as they become capable of independent performance of a task or skill. Supports include clues, clarifying questions, reminders, encouragement, breaking the problem down into steps, or anything else that allows the student to grow in independence. This interactive process bases instruction on learners' prior knowledge, provides needed support, and gradually removes the support as it becomes less necessary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to be attentive to learner needs and provide tailored support. Requires allocation of time for monitoring learner progress and adjusting support accordingly.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming to implement effectively. Some learners may become overly reliant on support if not gradually withdrawn.
