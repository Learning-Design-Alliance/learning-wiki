# Match decision rule to gender composition when convening deliberative groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This implementable recipe follows from the article's interaction finding: when few women participate, use unanimous rule; when many women participate, majority rule preserves their voice and authority. The article reports the gender gap "disappears under unanimous rule and few women, or under majority rule and many women", so conveners can select the rule that fits the group they expect.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Conveners must be able to choose the decision rule and anticipate the group's gender composition
#### Constraints
- Derived from experimental deliberation groups as reported in the article

### Target Learners
- adult citizens in deliberative forums

### Target Learning Goals
- equal voice and authority in group decision making
