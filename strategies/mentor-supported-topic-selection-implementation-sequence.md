# Mentor-supported implementation sequence: pre-course orientation workshops, one-on-one topic selection and individualized learning contracts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete implementation sequence for research topic–driven learning. Before the course, "orientation workshops were organized before the course started. These workshops trained students in basic literature searching, data extraction, and reference management software, which prepared them for subsequent assignments." Students then determined topics in one-on-one discussions with faculty mentors and, at the start of the semester, "students collaborated with their faculty mentors to design individualized learning contracts, setting personal goals and timelines" including milestones for literature review, presentation preparation and self-assessment criteria. Faculty also received development sessions to align supervision styles and expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty mentors available for one-on-one discussions and personalized guidance
- Faculty development sessions to align supervision styles across instructors
#### Constraints
- Some students struggled to assess the novelty and feasibility of their topics, and the article states more personalized and structured guidance is needed

### Target Learners
- Graduate students beginning a research-integrated course

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent topic selection and hypothesis formulation
- Self-regulated learning and accountability
- Literature search and synthesis skills
