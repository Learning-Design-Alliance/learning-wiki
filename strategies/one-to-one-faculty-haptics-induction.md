# Faculty induction through flexible one-to-one tailored drop-in training sessions over an extended timeframe

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To integrate haptics, all faculty received one-to-one training with complete uptake, delivered through flexible tailored drop-in sessions over an extended 6-month period. Faculty were inducted first, before learners, through dedicated sessions covering the haptic machine's capabilities, available models and cases, and pre-prepared cases highlighting haptic feedback differences between enamel, dentine, pulp and caries. A haptics teaching group supported evaluation and reflective practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clinically trained haptics manager role for troubleshooting and faculty support; an extended timeframe to accommodate labour-intensive sessions
#### Constraints
- The one-to-one sessions were labour intensive, enabled only by the extended 6-month timeframe

### Target Learners
- Dental faculty and specialist teachers (endodontics, cariology, fixed prosthetics)

### Target Learning Goals
- Faculty capability with haptic technology and case creation
- Collaborative case development matching the curriculum
