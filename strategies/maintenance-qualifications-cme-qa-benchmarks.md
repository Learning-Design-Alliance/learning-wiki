# Maintain qualifications through stroke-specific continuing education, quality assurance review, and minimum outcome benchmarks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy specifies how qualified physicians maintain competence over time. It combines ongoing stroke-specific continuing medical education ("A minimum of 16 hours of stroke specific education every 2 years is suggested"), participation in an ongoing quality assurance and improvement program reviewing all emergency interventional stroke therapy patients with outcomes monitored peri-procedurally and at 90 days, and participation in a national quality improvement registry when available. The document also proposes minimum performance benchmarks against which individual outcomes are judged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quality assurance program must review all emergency interventional stroke therapy patients
- Outcomes should be tracked and recorded
#### Constraints
- Threshold levels for recanalization and complication rates have yet to be established; the printed benchmarks are suggested minima

### Target Learners
- practicing neurointerventionalists performing acute stroke intervention

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of stroke intervention qualifications
- quality improvement in endovascular stroke therapy
