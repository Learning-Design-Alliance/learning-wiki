# Trauma-informed school support strategies for maltreated children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that schools support maltreated children through trauma-informed practices and targeted interventions. It states that "Incorporating maltreatment awareness into teacher education programs and implementing trauma-informed practices in schools could help create more supportive learning environments", and names teacher–child relationship building, structured classroom support, and parental involvement as effective strategies, alongside cross-sectoral collaboration between schools, health services, and welfare agencies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and school staff equipped with knowledge and tools to recognize and respond to the challenges maltreated children face
- Collaboration between schools, health services, and social welfare agencies
#### Constraints
- The article notes the effectiveness of intervention programs still requires evaluation in future research

### Target Learners
- Primary-school children (ages 6–12) with a history of maltreatment

### Target Learning Goals
- School attendance and engagement
- Academic performance
- Reduced need for educational crisis support
