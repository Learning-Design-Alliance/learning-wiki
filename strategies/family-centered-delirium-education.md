# Integrate family-centered and empathic care into delirium education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students emphasized that delirium management requires a holistic approach incorporating the patient's family, having observed that family presence played a significant role in calming patients. The authors recommend that delirium education encompass "effective communication strategies among patients, families, and healthcare teams" and address the psychological burden on families experiencing delirium for the first time.
