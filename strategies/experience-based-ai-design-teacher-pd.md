# Shift teacher professional development from information-based training to experience-based AI design activities in sustainability contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teacher training programs prioritise applied learning rather than introducing AI concepts or promoting positive attitudes alone. It states that "programs must also give teachers opportunities to design, experiment with, and reflect on AI tools within sustainability-oriented instructional contexts." This follows from the finding that practical AI use and ESD material development, not knowledge or attitudes, predicted integration ability. The recommendation is for collaborative, experience-based design activities involving real-world sustainability content and AI-supported instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for teachers to design, experiment with, and reflect on AI tools within sustainability-oriented instructional contexts
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is correlational and drawn from teachers already enrolled in a professional development program, which the authors note may introduce selection bias

### Target Learners
- in-service science and green energy teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- AI pedagogical readiness and technological competence in sustainability education
