# Review PAL training content, particularly the outdoor component and the amount of activity introduced, before further secondary-school PAL research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that future PAL interventions review the training content, particularly the outdoor component whose value teachers did not understand, and review the PAL strategies shared with teachers "with a focus on the amount of activity introduced", since the delivered dose produced no measurable activity change. They also suggest teacher concerns about student learning must be central to future PAL design, because implementation is likely contingent on teachers perceiving that PAL supports learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address teachers' perceptions that PAL supports student learning, since the article states implementation of PAL is likely contingent on this perception
#### Constraints
- The article notes a 3-hour after-school training session was considered too long by the feasibility school, limiting how much more training could be added
- The article concludes the current PAL training programme should not continue without review

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers of all subjects

### Target Learning Goals
- designing PAL training that supports student learning and increases activity intensity
