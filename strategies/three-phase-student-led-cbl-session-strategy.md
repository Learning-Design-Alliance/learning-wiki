# Before/during/after CBL session recipe: pre-class case distribution and student-led preparation, student-facilitated in-class discussion, and post-class self-assessment and homework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a concrete three-phase implementation recipe for each case. Before class, a week ahead, the teacher distributes cases and materials, assigns online self-study, and an elected group leader coordinates initial discussion. During class, "The group leader facilitated CBL discussions and communications, while the recorder documented the classroom content," with students searching literature and debating controversial issues while the teacher guides and oversees. After class, students complete self-assessment and CLEE forms and receive case-based homework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online educational course group must be established for communication; a team leader and recorder are designated for each case; the CBL process and evaluation methods are explained two weeks before class starts.
