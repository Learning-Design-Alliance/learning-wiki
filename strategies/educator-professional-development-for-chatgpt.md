# Provide educators with professional development and training to use ChatGPT in instructional practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's implications and recommendations sections state that successful integration of ChatGPT requires educator capability-building. It reports that lecturers "need to have the right training and assistance" and that "educators must undergo professional development an d training to acquire the necessary skills and knowledge." The strategy targets helping educators use ChatGPT in instructional practices and support students in overcoming the technology's limitations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must allocate professional development and training for educators before ChatGPT integration
#### Constraints
- The review frames this as necessary for successful and responsible usage, without specifying training content or format

### Target Learners
- higher education lecturers and educators

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and responsible use of ChatGPT in instructional practices
