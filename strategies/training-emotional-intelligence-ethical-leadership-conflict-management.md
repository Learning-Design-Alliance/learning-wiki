# Training programmes for nurse managers should target emotional intelligence, ethical leadership and adaptability to develop conflict management competency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that healthcare organisations foster integrative conflict management approaches through targeted training programmes. It states that 'training programmes focused on enhancing emotional intelligence and leadership skills can be developed to improve the ability of nurse managers to navigate conflicts in diverse and high‐pressure environments', and that programmes emphasising ethical leadership and emotional intelligence provide tools to foster teamwork and improve job satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organisations should invest in leadership development programmes that prioritise conflict management, emotional intelligence and adaptability to modern healthcare challenges
#### Constraints
- The review notes the predominance of moderate risk-of-bias studies and the reliance on self-reported data, which may affect generalisability

### Target Learners
- nurse managers
- healthcare organisations developing nurse managers

### Target Learning Goals
- navigate conflicts in high-pressure environments
- foster teamwork and reduce conflict
