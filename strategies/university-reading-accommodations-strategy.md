# Provide university accommodations and comprehension support for students with poor reading comprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that poor readers at university seek special considerations and, where possible, remediation, and that universities provide accommodations such as additional time in examinations and multimodal learning options. The authors write that "potential accommodations could include additional time in examinations and providing options for multimodal learning, including visual aids and face‐to‐face teaching", and note interventions have successfully increased post-secondary comprehension skills through reading comprehension strategies integrated in course assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of students with poor comprehension, e.g., screening university applicants for comprehension difficulties
#### Constraints
- Uptake of university accommodations is low even among students aware of their difficulties; remediation of reading difficulties is less common than accommodation at university

### Target Learners
- university students with poor reading comprehension or a history of reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- reading comprehension
