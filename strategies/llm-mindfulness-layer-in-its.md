# Deliver scalable socio-emotional support in ITSs via LLM-based mindful chat, breathing exercises, and mindful hint language

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends embedding mindfulness into structured ITSs through LLM-driven interactions rather than instructor-led programs: a context-sensitive mindful chat between math units, brief guided breathing exercises, and hint/feedback messages rewritten with mindful language based on Kabat-Zinn's seven principles. The authors state this offers "a scalable approach to socio-emotional support that does not require specialized instructor training" and positions LLMs as "an adaptive, socio-emotional layer within cognitive math tutoring".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LLM prompts translating mindfulness principles into communicative intentions suitable for an educational context
- Hard-coded, accuracy-checked mindful messages for hints and feedback to avoid hallucination during problem solving
#### Constraints
- Differential effects on anxiety and learning were obscured by the classroom implementation and language barrier; the authors note the need for further research on how mindfulness components can be optimally embedded

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade math students in classroom ITS settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing state math anxiety while maintaining algebra learning gains
