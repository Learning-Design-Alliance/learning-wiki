# Student Game Design

## Description
In this approach, learners build their own game around a specific course topic or principle. Learners identify learning objectives, design game mechanics, and create rules, fostering creativity and critical thinking while reinforcing content knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor guidance and resources. Time for game design and development. Opportunities for testing and feedback.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming. Requires instructor expertise in game design. May be challenging for learners with limited technical skills.

- All learner levels, particularly effective in technical and design-focused classes

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance understanding of course topics. Develop creativity and problem-solving skills. Promote learner engagement and ownership.

### Affordances
- Promotes deeper understanding through active design. Fosters creativity and critical thinking. Increases engagement and motivation through ownership.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose topics and design games that align with their interests. Instructors can provide guidance and support to meet individual needs.
