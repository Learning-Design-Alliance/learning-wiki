# Provide additional self-defense OHS training for students entering SME placements.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that schools identify students entering Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) for internships and provide them with additional "self-defense" OHS training, as they are unlikely to receive robust safety onboarding from their employers. This addresses the finding that smaller firms have higher accident rates and lower safety standards.
