# Provide individualized feedback on assessment design during professional development implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's cases suggest that professional development programs support uptake by having coordinators or peers review faculty's draft assessment items during implementation. Molly credits a Fellowship coordinator who "would look at my exam questions, and he would help me see that, while implicitly there was a practice there, I wasn't explicitly asking students to show me their understanding of the practice," after which her "questions just got much cleaner." This concrete feedback loop helped the one Fellow who successfully aligned assessment with instruction and planned to continue using 3DL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to coordinators or peers with assessment-design expertise who can review faculty-authored exam questions
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single Fellow's positive case within a three-case qualitative study

### Target Learners
- post-secondary STEM faculty participating in faculty learning communities

### Target Learning Goals
- writing three-dimensional assessment items aligned with instruction
