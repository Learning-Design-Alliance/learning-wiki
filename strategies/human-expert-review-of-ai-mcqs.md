# Subject AI-generated items to trained human expert review, including a technical review and difficulty refinement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Tip 7 makes human oversight a required stage: AI-generated items are rated by a small expert medical panel including content and process experts, then technically reviewed for stem, lead-in, options, flaws and unintended clues, and finally refined for difficulty. Difficulty can be adjusted using cognitive models such as Bloom's Taxonomy and Item Response Theory, or by adding comorbidities, withholding data, or making distractors more plausible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reviewers have to be trained; the panel should include content as well as process experts in item writing and review
#### Constraints
- Regular reviews and potentially pilot-testing are recommended to enhance psychometric characteristics, reliability, and validity

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate medical education trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate, valid, reliable assessment items congruent with learning goals
