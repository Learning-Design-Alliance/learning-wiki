# Ensure stable team learning conditions to counteract decreases in team learning behaviours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review draws the practical implication that organisations should sustainably foster work teams "by ensuring stable team learning conditions for work teams to counteract decreases in the engagement in team learning behaviours". Because team learning behaviours emerge bottom-up through repeated interactions and can decrease as well as increase over time, organisations should identify their desired outcomes and understand which behaviours are more likely to lead to the end goal before intervening.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organisations must identify their desired outcomes and understand which behaviours lead to the end goal
#### Constraints
- The review notes not all combinations of change types and characteristics have been examined yet, so whether all types occur in practice cannot be answered

### Target Learners
- members of natural work teams in organisations

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining engagement in team learning behaviours over time to reach team performance and innovation goals
