# Use psychology findings to select engagement metrics and intervention versions for a target course outcome such as retention

## Description
The paper outlines a use case: designers of an online course caring about a specific outcome such as increasing retention can use "Findings from the psychology literature" to point "to appropriate metrics for measuring engagement as well as potential interventions to adopt." Candidate interventions include advice videos on the landing page, messages above exercises, or email reminders; measures include self-reported mind-wandering, time on page, and number of attempts on a difficult problem.
