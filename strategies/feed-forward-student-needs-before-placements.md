# Feed-forward of student learning needs prior to clinical placements to support fitness to practise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Feed-forward involves providing clinical educators with pre-existing information about learner needs in a timely manner. The article defines it as "being provided with pre-existing information about learner needs in a timely manner to enable implementation of effective learning strategies to support students in the clinical environment". Educators nominated it as a valuable prevention strategy, and options include learning needs forms and student self-disclosure to university staff prior to placements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must feel safe to disclose FTP issues without fear of failing the placement
#### Constraints
- Debate exists regarding feed-forward due to potential for legal action, unfair treatment, bias and concerns for student privacy

### Target Learners
- Physiotherapy students on clinical placement

### Target Learning Goals
- Fitness to practise
- Clinical competence
- Professional behaviour
