# Shadow a Student

## Description
Shadowing involves following a student throughout their school day to gain insights into their experiences, challenges, and needs. By observing without assumptions, educators can question their preconceived ideas and learn how to better support their students. It encourages them to create learning goals, coordinate with students and staff, and prepare for a meaningful shadowing experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination with students and staff, commitment to observation without judgment, reflective practice
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming, may be disruptive to student routine, requires careful planning to avoid biases
