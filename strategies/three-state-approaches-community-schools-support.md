# Three state approaches to supporting community schools: ongoing school funding formula allocations, competitive grant funding, and capacity-building supports

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report highlights "three potential approaches to state support for community schools: (1) ongoing support through school funding formulas, (2) competitive grant funding, and (3) capacity-building supports (such as certification processes)." Maryland and New York exemplify the funding-formula approach (an entitlement grant and a set-aside respectively); California, Illinois, New Mexico, and Vermont use competitive grants funded by state general funds, federal ESSER funds, or both; Florida and Georgia use certification-based capacity building. Together these approaches create state infrastructure with continuous funding and technical assistance rather than isolated, expiring grants.

- Requires state legislative or budget action to establish the funding stream or certification mechanism, and identification of eligible high-need schools or districts.
