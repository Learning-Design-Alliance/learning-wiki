# Design AI-related teacher professional development as practice-oriented, case-based and connected to teachers' real pedagogical decisions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on recent AI-focused literature, the review recommends orienting AI professional development toward teachers' actual practice. It reports that studies by Ding et al. (2024) and Tan et al. (2025) "suggest that AI-related professional development needs to be practice-oriented, case-based and connected to teachers' real pedagogical decisions". This aligns with the AI subset's finding that superficial integration and insufficient AI-related preparation are the dominant barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Connection to teachers' real pedagogical decisions and case-based practice rather than tool-focused training
#### Constraints
- The review attributes this recommendation to the cited AI-focused literature and its own synthesis; the recommendation exceeds what any single reviewed study demonstrated

### Target Learners
- in-service teachers
- pre-service teachers
- teacher educators

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy and competence
- responsible and pedagogically justified AI integration
