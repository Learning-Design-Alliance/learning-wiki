# Require brief pre-session comprehension checks to improve pre-class preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Instructors reported that "Most students did not read the materials before the face-to-face session," diminishing the effectiveness of in-person sessions. The article recommends addressing this "by requiring students to complete brief comprehension checks, such as three-question quizzes, before attending live sessions," citing research that pre-class preparation significantly enhances engagement and learning outcomes.
