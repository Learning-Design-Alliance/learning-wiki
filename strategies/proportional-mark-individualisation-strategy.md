# Individualise team marks proportionally to the average peer assessment mark each student receives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (6 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 7 studies (4 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 7 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete marking recipe: after each team assessment task, every team member rates all members including themselves on contribution criteria; the average rating received by each student is computed, and "The mark the teams received from the academics is then individualized proportionally based on the average of the peer assessment mark each student received from the team members". The rule is simple for students to understand and directly translates peer judgements into final individual marks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer assessment conducted after each assessment task with a week for completion
- An online peer assessment tool to administer and aggregate ratings
- Blind assessor identity and publication of average ratings and written feedback to students
#### Constraints
- If a student receives zero average peer assessment mark, their individual mark for that assignment will be zero regardless of the mark given to the team by the academic staff

### Target Learners
- engineering students working in teams of 4–6 members

### Target Learning Goals
- fair allocation of team assessment marks accounting for individual contribution
