# Use cooperative or problem-based learning to convey the nursing methodology instead of traditional lectures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
After finding that traditional lectures were used to explain the nursing process and that methodology-related items fell below the quality threshold, the authors propose cooperative or problem-based learning as an alternative. They argue it "places the student at the center of the teaching-learning process, enables significant learning and provides good results", while acknowledging its difficult implementation. The conclusion calls for evaluating its implementation when conveying the nursing methodology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness to move from lecture-based explanation of the nursing process to student-centered formats
#### Constraints
- The authors note the approach has a difficult implementation

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying the nursing methodology (assessment, diagnosis, planning, evaluation)
