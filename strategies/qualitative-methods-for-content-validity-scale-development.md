# Use extensive qualitative research to establish construct understanding and content validity when developing psychometric scales

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that scale development be preceded and informed by qualitative study of the construct, because "scale development requires a rich understanding of the assessed construct." It states that the value of qualitative methods "is arguably most visible in establishing content validity, where expert opinions—including the lived experiences of the population of interest—help identify the most suitable items for a scale," and that critical inquiry can strengthen content validity by justifying who qualifies as an expert. Qualitative and quantitative methods are described as an iterative, synergistic cycle.
