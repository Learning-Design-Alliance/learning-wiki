Use a life-relevant theme (smart city) as the STEM practice curriculum with staged 18-week activities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends choosing learning content in technology as the course basis and using "the smart city was used as the theme for the STEM practice curriculum," so students incorporate electronic elements into life-related content. Implementation ran over 18 weeks in three stages: introduction to smart-city concepts (weeks 1-2), basic applications and programming (weeks 3-7), application and practice (weeks 8-12), intelligent streetlight and smart trash can design (weeks 13-17), and a learning-outcome check in week 18.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Content designed so students can apply what they learned in class to their lives
  • Continuous instruction time, such as two consecutive sessions or club time

Constraints¶

  • When teaching time is short, many students are interrupted when about to engage in practice; the authors say implementation complexity should be simplified in the future

Target Learners¶

  • university students in information technology courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • interdisciplinary STEM integration
  • programming and problem-solving practice

Claims¶

  • A substantial fraction of students reported increased interest in physics and ability to connect physics to their life science majors and careers [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Select theme-set resources that appeal cross-culturally and present content on students' terms
