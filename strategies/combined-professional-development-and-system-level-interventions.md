# Combine professional development with organizational and system-level interventions to strengthen medication safety competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that strengthening medication safety competence requires interventions addressing both individual and system-level conditions. Suggested measures include "Structured mentorship and feedback mechanisms", training with realistic scenarios reflecting interruptions and time pressure, optimizing workflow design, minimizing interruptions, ensuring timely pharmacological consultation, and promoting a non-punitive reporting environment.
