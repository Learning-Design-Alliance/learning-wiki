# Body-motion graph construction with a real-time position sensor as an introductory activity for position and velocity concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An introductory activity in which students build position-time graphs by walking in front of a position sensor (Go!Motion) connected to a computer, followed by quantitative analysis of the graphs produced. The authors chose it for its easy realization, even in a simple classroom equipped with an interactive whiteboard and not necessarily in a laboratory, and justify it through the embodied-cognition account: measuring physical quantities linked to one's own body facilitates knowledge construction. After the hands-on phase, the teacher moves to formalizing the concepts introduced.
