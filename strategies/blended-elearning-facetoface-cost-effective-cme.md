# Use a blended e-learning plus face-to-face approach as a cost-effective way to deliver continuing medical education to clinicians

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a blended format for continuing medical education: online learning offers flexibility and cost-effective delivery, while participant feedback favoured the face-to-face seminar as an important part of the program. The authors conclude that "This type of 'blended' approach (e-learning plus face-to-face) may provide a cost-effective way to deliver CME to clinicians."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of both an online learning platform and a facilitated face-to-face seminar
#### Constraints
- The authors note the evidence base for the effectiveness of e-learning or blended learning approaches is limited by the methodological quality of studies

### Target Learners
- general medical practitioners
- clinicians undertaking continuing medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- continuing medical education
