# Strengthen quality management systems components in medical laboratory pre-service education curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond in-service CPD, the article recommends that "Medical laboratory science pre-service education programs should also strengthen components of quality management systems in their curricula". This responds to the finding that the greatest self-perceived training gap lay in quality management systems, and to the article's observation that such training may not have been fully integrated into pre-service education, with a 2007 curriculum review of Botswana's diploma program having recommended strengthening it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum change should be informed by needs-assessment data such as this survey, which the authors say can also inform medical laboratory pre-service education curriculum.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in workforce self-reported needs in Botswana; the article notes a single quantitative method was used and suggests mixed-methods approaches to increase validity of findings.

### Target Learners
- Pre-service medical laboratory science students in Botswana

### Target Learning Goals
- Quality management systems knowledge and skills for laboratory practice
