# Let student groups devise their own delay-measurement method and estimate their own uncertainties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the echo activity, "the students were given freedom to devise the more appropriate one" method of measuring the echo delay, and "Each measurement has an associated uncertainty, that the students were asked to estimate as well". Groups compared methods (beginning, end, or maximum of the signal within a syllable) and pooled results by averaging the 10 measurements with the maximum shift from average as uncertainty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups must compare and agree on a common final result and uncertainty convention
#### Constraints
- The article notes the uncertainties of the maximum-of-signal method were substantially larger, and the students' pooled error estimate is larger than a correct statistical treatment would give

### Target Learners
- high-school physics students working in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- measurement uncertainty estimation
- experimental design decisions
