# A standardized retraining curriculum delivered through combined online and offline teaching methods helps reduce disparities in faculty resources and teaching capacity across different hospitals.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The standardized retraining curriculum, delivered through a combination of online and offline teaching methods, helps reduce disparities in faculty resources, teaching capacity, and instructional approaches across different hospitals. This unified approach enables nurses from different regions to receive consistent retraining, promoting both equity and accessibility. Before the program began, a DingTalk learning group was established to facilitate communication and distribute relevant notifications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online learning activities, assessments, and evaluations were conducted through an online platform.
#### Constraints
- The courses included in this retraining program were developed in accordance with China's cultural context and professional environment and have not yet been implemented in other countries.

### Target Learners
- nurses from different regions of Zhejiang Province

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent retraining promoting equity and accessibility
