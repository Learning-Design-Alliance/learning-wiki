# Map chronotype differences and implement targeted sleep hygiene interventions in schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "psychologists, educators and healthcare professionals could first collaborate to map individual differences in chronotype and sleep/wake patterns among youth" within the school context. This mapping would enable "the planning and implementation of targeted interventions enhancing sleep hygiene in at-risk adolescents, aiming to mitigate the potentially detrimental effect of circadian misalignment". They also recommend that families receive guidance on fostering healthy sleep routines such as "facilitating regular bedtimes and limiting electronic devices during the evening". The strategy is grounded in the finding that differences in educational identity processes between chronotypes appear at initial levels, making early identification crucial for prevention.
