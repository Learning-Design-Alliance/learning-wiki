# Disseminate updated clinical guidelines through mobile notifications and periodic quick examinations, and revise nursing curricula toward practical, motivational training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend specific measures to raise guideline awareness and empower nurses. For dissemination, "managers could be used of new methods of announcements such as mobile notification or tack periodic quick examinations from the last version of guideline". For education, they call for "use of practical/motivational training methods and some revision and possibly changes in nursing student curricula seem necessary", with measurable and reliable criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Manager support for new guideline-announcement methods such as mobile notification and periodic quick examinations
- Educational methods with measurable and reliable criteria
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one cross-sectional survey in orthopedic wards of one Iranian university's hospitals; the authors note participants may not represent all Iranian nurses

### Target Learners
- Practicing orthopedic-ward nurses
- Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Osteoporosis prevention and screening knowledge
- Awareness and use of the national osteoporosis clinical guideline
- Confidence in patient counseling
