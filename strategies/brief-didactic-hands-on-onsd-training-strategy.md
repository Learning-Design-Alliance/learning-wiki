# Brief ONSD ultrasound training: 5-minute didactic plus 20 minutes of guided hands-on practice on healthy peers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training recipe: "A 5-min lecture introduced the concept and technique of ONSD measurements" followed by small-group demonstration and "hands-on instruction from the instructors for a total of 20 min", with trainees practicing the axial-view technique on other healthy trainees using the linear probe and superficial setting. Measurements were then taken 3 mm posterior to the retina on the closed eyelid.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with extensive ultrasound training to demonstrate technique and give hands-on guidance
- Ultrasound machines with a linear probe and superficial setting
- Healthy volunteers for supervised practice
#### Constraints
- Scans were performed on healthy, uninjured volunteers in a classroom/simulation environment, which the authors state may limit external validity to unstable patients
- The Mindray machine used is not a device typically used in the military

### Target Learners
- Military medic trainees with minimal ultrasound experience
- Novice point-of-care ultrasound users

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurately measure optic nerve sheath diameter with ultrasound
- Support rapid triage decisions for suspected elevated intracranial pressure in remote or low-resource settings
