# Organise computational modelling classes as small-team exploratory workshops with PDF activities, embedded video guidance and an online platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the implementation described, students in the computational modelling classes were "organized in groups of two or three, one group for each computer in the classroom" and worked through activity sets built around a small number of problems about easily observed physical phenomena. "Each class activity consisted of a set of ﬁve modelling tasks in mechanics, presented in PDF documents, with text and embedded video support" for use in class or at home within a Moodle-centred collaborative online context. Instructors accompanied teams continuously and held global class discussions when needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activity documentation providing physical, mathematical and computational modelling guidelines
- continuous instructor accompaniment during exploration
#### Constraints
- The article notes students perceived the content load as too heavy and the available time as insufficient, and recommends less guided, more discovery-oriented guidelines.

### Target Learners
- first-year university general physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- exploratory and interactive learning of mechanics through computational modelling
