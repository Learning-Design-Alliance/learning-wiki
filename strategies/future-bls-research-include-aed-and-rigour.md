# Future BLS research should include AED use outcomes and high methodological quality

## Description
The review recommends that future studies of BLS training among health science students measure automatic external defibrillator use and adopt rigorous designs. Only one included study implemented an AED measure, and it obtained unfavourable results, so "a comparison could not be made". The authors also recommend high methodological quality so studies carry greater relevance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must include measurement results for AED use, since most selected studies did not report them
#### Constraints
- AHA and ERC recommendations evolve continuously, making it difficult to assess studies published over the last 10 years in the same way

### Target Learners
- health science university students
- nursing students
- medicine students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic life support skills
- cardiopulmonary resuscitation technique
- safe use of automatic external defibrillators
