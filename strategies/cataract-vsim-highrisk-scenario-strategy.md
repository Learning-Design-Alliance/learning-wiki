# Simulate high-risk scenarios such as posterior capsule rupture management to build risk awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The platform deliberately designs high-risk scenarios for pedagogical purposes. "High-risk scenarios specifically designed for this pedagogical purpose include phacoemulsification, energy control, and posterior capsule rupture management." The authors report that simulating such scenarios helps students develop awareness of risks and strategies for responding to unexpected situations, with 38.7% of students particularly impressed by posterior capsule rupture recovery procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time warning prompts flag critical errors such as posterior capsule rupture
#### Constraints
- Findings are preliminary without a control group

### Target Learning Goals
- Risk prediction awareness
- Management of surgical complications
