# Peergrade

## Description
Peergrade is a platform that allows learners to give and receive feedback effectively. Learners anonymously offer different types of feedback using a rubric set by the teacher, which allows for some objective and some open feedback questions. The feedback receivers can then go through the feedback and react to it, rating the feedback itself and offering tips for improvement. In the case of disagreement, learners can flag responses so that teachers can intervene and respond to the feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the Peergrade platform, a clear rubric or set of success criteria, and structured time for learners to provide and respond to feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to technology and internet connectivity. Some learners may be hesitant to provide honest feedback due to the fear of being identified.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured and anonymous platform for peer feedback, promoting honest and objective critique. The rubric-based feedback ensures that learners focus on specific aspects of the work and provide targeted suggestions for improvement.

### Personalization
- Teachers can customize the rubric to fit different content areas and learning objectives. Learners can provide both objective and open-ended feedback, allowing for personalized critique.
