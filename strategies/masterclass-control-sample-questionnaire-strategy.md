# Administer the scientific-career questionnaire to a control sample of students not attending the Masterclass

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
To interpret the Masterclass questionnaire findings, the authors asked physics teachers to propose the general scientific-career section of the questionnaire also to students who will not attend the Masterclass. This "will provide a control sample including students not as good as the ones coming to the Masterclass and not necessarily interested in science as a career", allowing the attendee sample to be compared against a broader student population.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physics teachers willing to administer the career questionnaire section to non-attending students
#### Constraints
- The attendee sample is selective: students chosen for interest and grades, so attendee results may not generalize to typical students

### Target Learners
- high-school students in their final year

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding gender differences in perception of scientific careers
