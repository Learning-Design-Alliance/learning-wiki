# Targeted educational efforts for prenatal providers on SMA carrier screening and result interpretation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends targeted education to close provider knowledge gaps in SMA carrier screening. It states that "The association of provider knowledge with comfort found in this study suggests that targeted educational efforts may improve the rates of provider comfort in discussing SMA CS and results", and concludes "Educational measures should be undertaken to improve provider, and by proxy, patient understanding of available CS options". Future work should identify optimal educational approaches, applied to other genetic conditions as well.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education must address SMA knowledge and the interpretation and management of carrier screening results, including linked variant results
#### Constraints
- The article states the optimal approaches to educate providers are not yet identified and require further work

### Target Learners
- Prenatal care providers (MFM attendings and fellows, OBGYN generalists and residents, certified nurse midwives, nurse practitioners)

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of SMA disease, inheritance, and carrier screening interpretation
- Comfort in discussing SMA screening and test results with patients
