# Integrate AI education throughout the K-12 curriculum via innovative approaches to integrative AI-science, AI-math, AI-language arts, and AI-social science education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article calls for "innovative approaches to integrative AI-science, AI-math, AI-language arts, and AI-social science education" so that students become adept at using AI across disciplines. It argues this integration will yield more effective approaches to, for example, "doing science," and will also address the practicalities of school curricula that are already exceedingly full.
