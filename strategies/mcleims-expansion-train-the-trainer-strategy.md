# Expand mCLEIMS through faculty development, standardized scenario templates, and a train-the-trainer model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "mCLEIMS may be expanded through faculty-development workshops, standardized scenario templates, shared facilitator briefing materials, simulation-patient training, and a train-the-trainer model". A multi-institutional version could preserve the core structure while allowing local adaptation of specialty scenarios, professional roles, and assessment tools. The most transportable elements are station timing, pre-brief and debrief structure, interprofessional role design, and domain mapping.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-development workshops and simulation-patient training to support implementation
#### Constraints
- Local adaptation of specialty scenarios, professional roles, and assessment tools is needed for multi-institutional versions

### Target Learners
- medical students in clinical clerkship

### Target Learning Goals
- resuscitation skills
- integrated clinical practice
- interprofessional collaboration
