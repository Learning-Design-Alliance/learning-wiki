# Implement the boot camp on an online platform with free materials and instructors prepared to teach social determinants of health, interprofessional competencies, and team-based care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Implementation guidance drawn from the program's next-steps section: run the boot camp on an online learning platform, use the freely available slides and videos, and schedule training so basic competency is reached early. Instructors "should be prepared to offer an overview of social determinants of health, interprofessional care competencies, and the importance of team-based care."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementing this program requires an online learning platform.
- Instructor(s) should be prepared to offer an overview of social determinants of health, interprofessional care competencies, and the importance of team-based care.
- The training schedule should support all students in achieving basic competency early in their graduate training.

## Claims

- [Clinical knowledge scores across all training modules rose from pre-test to post-test after the interprofessional clinical skills boot camp, in one uncontrolled pre-post study, with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d = .77)](../claims/boot-camp-across-module-knowledge-gain.md) [+W]
- [Holm-adjusted comparisons indicated students considerably improved following all training modules, with moderate to large effect sizes](../claims/boot-camp-all-module-holm-improvement.md) [+W]
- [More than 80 percent of boot camp participants gave favorable ratings to all aspects of the training](../claims/boot-camp-favorable-trainee-ratings.md) [+W]
