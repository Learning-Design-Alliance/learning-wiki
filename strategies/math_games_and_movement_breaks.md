# Math Games and Movement Breaks

## Description
Incorporating quick, fun, physical math games for when learners get antsy. Breaks refresh students while giving them another take on math concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- No materials are needed, just quick and fun physical math games.
#### Constraints
- Student-pair games need an even number of students. The teacher's enthusiasm is critical in these games.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Movement refreshes students while giving them another take on math concepts.

### Personalization
- There are teacher-led games and student-pair games. Some game examples are groups, stand up/sit down, and finger speed-sums. The games are flexible and can be adapted by the teacher or student.
