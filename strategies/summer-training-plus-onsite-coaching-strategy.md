# Deliver professional development through a 2-week summer institute followed by year-long on-site coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AMSTI's professional development model combines an intensive summer training session with sustained on-site coaching during the school year. The article states teachers and principals "attend a 2-week training session in the summer, followed by on-site coaching during the school year," with the professional development focused on hands-on, inquiry-based instruction in math and science. This pairing of initial intensive training with ongoing in-classroom support is the core delivery mechanism of the program.
