# Train and standardize instructors in the RCDP methodology before delivering learner sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before learner sessions, the program trained instructors in the RCDP methodology: each instructor led a round of simulations while others enacted the case, clarified doubts, defined expected standards, aligned learning objectives, and practiced the methodology. The authors state "This qualification was an important part of the project as it enabled standardized training for all instructors", noting the challenge of transitioning from established traditional simulation to RCDP. Survey items on instructors emphasized the relevance of this prior training and involvement in scenario construction.
