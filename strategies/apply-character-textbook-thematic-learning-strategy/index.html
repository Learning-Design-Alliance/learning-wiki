Teachers apply the character-education thematic textbook in thematic learning to sustain engagement and strengthen character¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors' closing recommendation is that "teachers can use and apply the textbook thematic based character education on thematic learning in order that students do not feel bored or tired in learning." Teachers are positioned as part of effective programming: they should improve learning results and train and strengthen students' character during learning activities. The strategy treats the textbook as a medium for fun learning and for supporting students as independent active learners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must be able to improve learning results and train students' character during learning activities

Constraints¶

  • The article notes students' academic abilities differ, so the approach requires patience and some students work slowly

Target Learners¶

  • fourth-grade primary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • thematic learning engagement
  • character strengthening

Claims¶

  • Learning with a textbook thematic based character education produces higher thematic-learning outcomes than a conventional textbook for fourth-grade primary students [+W]
  • The character-education thematic textbook also raises experimental-class posttest means well above pretest means [+W]
  • Authors report, without measuring character, that a character-education thematic textbook strengthens students' character [+W]
