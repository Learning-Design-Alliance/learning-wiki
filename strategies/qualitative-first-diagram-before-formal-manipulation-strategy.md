# Guide students to begin problem solving with qualitative reasoning and a diagram before formal mathematical manipulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The instructional strategy opens problem solving with visualizing the physical situation — "step 1: drawing a diagram to clarify the physical setting" — creating a bridge from conceptual account to later formalization, before students justify relevant principles, plan a solution, execute it, and evaluate coherence. In the GR course this was implemented through a template hosting all tasks, with students marking frame shifts and separating qualitative justification from algebraic execution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A template presenting the five-step procedure and hosting all tasks
- A tutor-modeled fully worked example introducing the methodology
#### Constraints
- The tutor did not co-solve tasks or steer students' framing during the week; feedback was provided afterward

### Target Learners
- master's-level physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- connecting mathematical formalism with physical conceptual understanding
- developing symbol sense and expert-like problem solving
