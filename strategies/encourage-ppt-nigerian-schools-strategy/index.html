Strategies

Equip Nigerian schools with PowerPoint presentation facilities and train teachers to use them for technology-based subjects¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that students taught with PowerPoint outperformed chalkboard-taught peers and that the medium benefited high, medium and low achievers while being gender friendly, the article recommends that Nigerian schools adopt PPT, especially for technological and practically oriented courses. It states this "could be achieved if government and other education stakeholders co uld provide IWB (PPT) to schools with adequate infrastructure and training of teachers on its usage".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Government and education stakeholders provision of ICT facilities and adequate infrastructure
  • Training of teachers on the usage of the technology

Constraints¶

  • The article calls for further empirical studies on different subjects and at different levels to provide a sound basis for integration

Target Learners¶

  • Junior secondary school students in Nigerian public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Basic Technology and other technological or practically oriented subjects

Claims¶

  • Achievement level significantly differentiated posttest performance among students taught with PowerPoint, with high achievers outperforming medium and low achievers [+W]
  • All achievement levels benefited from PowerPoint instruction, with mean gains for high, medium and low achievers alike [+W]
  • No significant difference in achievement between male and female students taught Technical Drawing with PowerPoint presentation [+W]
  • Junior secondary students taught Technical Drawing with PowerPoint presentation achieved significantly higher posttest scores than students taught with chalkboard instruction [+W]
  • Sidak post-hoc comparisons show high achievers significantly outperformed medium and low achievers, and medium achievers outperformed low achievers, under PowerPoint instruction [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Improve rural school teaching facilities and encourage wide reading irrespective of location and gender
  • Provide PowerPoint resources and teacher development for Geography instruction in Nigerian secondary schools
