# Provide structured training for clinical teachers and Mini-CEX assessors in specific, student-centred feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured training for clinical teachers and Mini-CEX assessors to improve their ability to provide specific and student-centred feedback, so as to optimize the Mini-CEX as a learning tool. It argues the concept of "student-centred feedback needs to be emphasized throughout the feedback provision process", while acknowledging that case complexity and assessor position influence feedback type. It also recommends assisting students to identify their own deficiencies and strengths.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessors need knowledge of the level of performance expected from students and the ability to produce valid and reliable scores
#### Constraints
- Training sessions at the clinical schools likely varied in approach taken by individual leaders, per the article

### Target Learners
- clinical teachers and Mini-CEX assessors in undergraduate medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- provision of specific, student-centred written feedback
