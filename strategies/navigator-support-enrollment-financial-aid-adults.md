# Provide navigator support through enrollment and financial aid, plus continued support after enrollment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete high-touch recipe drawn from Adult Promise states: assign navigators who guide adult learners step by step through the enrollment and financial aid process, and keep support in place after the learner engages with an institution. The brief states adult learners "often need more high-touch approaches such as the support of navigators to guide them through the enrollment and financial aid process."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff or navigators available to guide adults through enrollment and financial aid steps
- Continued support mechanisms once learners are engaged with an institution
