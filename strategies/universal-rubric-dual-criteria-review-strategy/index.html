Use a universal rubric with dual criteria — standards alignment and access/equity — for cross-content-area review of instructional materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The refined Nevada process uses "a materials checklist, roles and responsibilities guidance, a timeline, and a universal rubric" so reviewers across content areas rate materials consistently. "The rubric requires any approved materials to meet two sets of criteria" — alignment to the Nevada Academic Content Standards and the access and equity dimensions of the material — replacing content-area rubrics that previously varied in scope and complexity.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reviewers must be trained on the review process, NVACS, the universal rubric, and rubric calibrating procedures before rating materials

Constraints¶

  • Applies to statewide adoption of core instructional materials in Nevada; approved materials must pass both the alignment and access/equity criteria sets

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in districts of all sizes and types across Nevada

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Access to standards-aligned, culturally engaging instructional materials across content areas

Related Strategies¶

  • Represent small and rural districts in statewide materials review through regional professional development program coordinators
  • Checklists & Rubrics
  • Equity Audits
