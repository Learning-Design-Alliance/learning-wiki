# Support AI literacy, safe AI use, team-based learning, and innovation-supportive work environments, with mentoring for low-support settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nurse managers support AI literacy, safe AI use, team-based learning, and innovation-supportive environments, and that education programs include "AI literacy, ethical use, data privacy, algorithmic bias, and verification of AI‐generated information" in curricula. For nurses in environments with limited organizational support, the moderation findings suggest "additional mentoring and structured opportunities for problem‐solving could be considered." Initiatives should start as monitored pilots evaluated with objective outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
- The study did not evaluate an educational intervention, so these strategies are not proven to improve competence; findings come from Türkiye with convenience sampling and should be adapted to local healthcare systems

### Target Learners
- Hospital nurses

- AI literacy
