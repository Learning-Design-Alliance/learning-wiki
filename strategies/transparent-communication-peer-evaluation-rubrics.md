# Clearly explain the purpose, benefits, challenges, and evaluation criteria of group work, and use detailed rubrics, training, and practice to support equitable peer evaluation.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The essay recommends that instructors "clearly explain the types, goals, benefits, and challenges of group work, along with how contributions will be evaluated." For peer evaluation, it recommends detailed rubrics, evaluation training, and practice with the rubric to support high-quality peer evaluation and help avoid the impacts of implicit bias, and notes there are advantages and disadvantages to providing students with their peers' feedback anonymously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors should use individual, group, and peer evaluation in tandem with clear criteria
#### Constraints
- Peer evaluation can be compromised by student bias; there is no broad agreement on anonymity of peer feedback

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- fair evaluation of group contributions
- reducing student resistance
