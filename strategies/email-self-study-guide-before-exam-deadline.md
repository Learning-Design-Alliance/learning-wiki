# Email the self-study guide to targeted students 6-10 weeks before the exam deadline as a low-resource supplement to existing preparation resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a specific implementation recipe: "we recommend emailing it to all fourth-year medical students planning to apply to emergency medicine residencies 6-10 weeks before the deadline to complete the SVI." Distribution by email required minimal resources, needed no gathering of students in one location, and the guide is positioned as supplementing, not replacing, existing resources such as the AAMC's tips for applicants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Only requirement for use is access to a computer with PowerPoint software
#### Constraints
- The guide is not meant to replace existing resources, such as the AAMC's Preparing for the AAMC Standardized Video Interview: Tips for Applicants, but rather to supplement them

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students applying to emergency medicine residencies

### Target Learning Goals
- independent preparation for a standardized video interview assessing professionalism and communication skills
