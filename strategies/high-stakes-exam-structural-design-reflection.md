# Reflect on the structural design of high-stakes examinations to reduce perceived catastrophic consequences of a single assessment.

## Description
The article argues that reflection is needed on the design of high-stakes examinations whose outcomes decisively shape careers, noting that while national ranking procedures are objective and fair, they concentrate pressure within a narrow time window. The authors recommend ensuring transparent assessment criteria, providing formative nOSCE experiences, and offering multiple exam sessions or remediation pathways to reduce the perceived catastrophic consequences of a single examination.
