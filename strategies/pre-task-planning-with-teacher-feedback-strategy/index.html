Strategies

Use 10-minute pre-task planning, ideally collaborative and with teacher feedback, before L2 argumentative writing tasks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that L2 writing teachers adopt the techniques it tested: giving learners about 10 minutes of pre-writing planning (idea generation, organization, goal setting) before a 30-minute composing session, with collaborative peer planning and teacher feedback on outlines as the most effective variants. It states "the L2 writing teachers can apply the techniques utilized in this research in their classes, and hopefully obtain beneficial results."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A dedicated 10-minute planning phase before writing
  • Teacher availability to review outlines and give feedback during planning

Constraints¶

  • The article cautions that its groups comprised small sample sizes, which can affect generalizability of the findings

Target Learners¶

  • EFL learners in language institutes, upper-intermediate level

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving structural organization and clarity of argumentative essays

Affordances¶

  • Sociocultural Account L2 Writing Planning

Claims¶

  • Collaborative pre-task planning produces better essay structural organization and clarity than individual planning [+W]
  • Pre-task planning, whether individual or collaborative, with or without teacher feedback, improves structural organization and clarity of argumentative essays relative to no planning [+W]
  • Feedback-mediated planning groups (FMIP and FMCP) outperform non-feedback planning groups, and do not differ significantly from each other [+W]
  • Pretest scores strongly predict posttest essay performance in the planning experiment [+W]
