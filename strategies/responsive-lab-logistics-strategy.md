# Adjust lab logistics responsively to student feedback: provide a submission window after lab sessions and form gender-balanced groups with freedom of choice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two implementable adjustments drawn from the course's own student feedback. First, after students flagged time management, the teacher adjusted the report requirement, "providing a 24-hour submission window", which students reported greatly appreciated and which significantly improved their attitude towards the course. Second, to promote fairer group work in future editions, the authors will "allow students the freedom to form groups while requiring gender balance" and frequently ask students how their group work is progressing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mechanism for collecting student feedback, such as an anonymous end-of-course questionnaire.
- Frequent check-ins on how group work is progressing.
#### Constraints
- Student comments about imbalanced groups came remarkably all from female students, so the gender-balance requirement responds specifically to that pattern.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students working in lab groups

### Target Learning Goals
- managing lab-report workload
- equitable collaborative group work
