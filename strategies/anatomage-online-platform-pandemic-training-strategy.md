# Deploy the Anatomage Table through an online platform to sustain anatomy and surgical training during emergencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends using the Anatomage Table via an online platform so that anatomical and surgical training on virtual dissecting cadavers can continue when in-person dissection is suspended, as during COVID-19 lockdowns. It reports that "the Anatomage Table allowed the continuation of surgical training activities, since it can be combined with a software called Touch Surgery" on tablets or smartphones, and that the table was deployed at the Africa School to facilitate effective teaching of anatomy and related basic medical science.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional technological investment in the Anatomage Table and compatible online platforms or companion software such as Touch Surgery
#### Constraints
- The review cautions that virtues such as courage, empathy, and compassion can only be learned through attendance of an anatomy laboratory

### Target Learners
- medical students
- surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- gross anatomy dissection
- surgical training
- basic medical sciences
