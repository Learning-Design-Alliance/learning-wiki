# Text + Social Annotation + Structured Activity

## Description
Students engage with text resources, typically online versions, that are equipped with social annotation tools. These tools allow students to mark up the text, leave notes, and engage in discussions with each other, all visible to all the students. This is combined with a structured activity to ensure active learning and application of the concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires online text resources with social annotation tools (e.g., Perusall, hypothes.is), design of structured activities, and facilitation of online discussions.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to technology and online resources, may require moderation to ensure productive discussions.
