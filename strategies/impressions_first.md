# Impressions First

## Description
Before diving into specifics, encourage students to share their initial impressions of the mentor text. This helps them connect with the text on a personal level and develop their own critical thinking skills. Reading to learn is something that they ultimately will do on their own.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material. Time for individual reflection and group discussion. A classroom environment where students feel comfortable sharing their thoughts and feelings.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to articulate their impressions. Requires careful facilitation to ensure that all voices are heard. May be time-consuming.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance critical thinking skills, promote independent reading, encourage personal connections with text, develop the ability to analyze text based on initial reactions

### Affordances
- Encourages students to trust their own instincts. Validates diverse perspectives and interpretations. Creates a more engaging and interactive learning experience.

### Personalization
- Students are given the freedom to express their own unique impressions. The teacher can facilitate discussion without imposing their own interpretation. Students can reflect on how their impressions change over time.
