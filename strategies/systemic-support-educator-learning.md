# Sustain educator learning through schoolwide, network, and districtwide approaches that embed professional learning across the system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that professional learning be part of a sustained approach at school, district, and network levels creating opportunities for learning, dialogue, and shared practices. It highlights schoolwide approaches such as the School Development Program, EL Education, and the Institute for Student Achievement that "have evolved into networks of schools... developed around practices grounded in the science of learning and development," and notes districtwide approaches iterating on learning opportunities are important for sustained transformations in pedagogy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need opportunities to continue learning after initial preparation because children are not standardized and problems of practice are never routine
#### Constraints
- The report describes these approaches as holding promise, not as proven at scale

### Target Learners
- teachers, principals, and district leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained enactment of developmentally grounded pedagogy
