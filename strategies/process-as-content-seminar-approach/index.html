Use a process-as-content seminar approach in which learners read, analyze, synthesize, apply, and evaluate individually chosen problems¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In a group context such as a seminar, the report recommends a process approach in which the learner engages a number of intellectual processes: choosing information sources, analyzing acquired information, synthesizing conclusions in keeping with a personal value system, applying them to a real-life situation, and evaluating the work. The problem can differ for each class member, and the objective is simply to encourage individual growth.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learners must have access to multiple sources of information they may elect to read, visit, or interview

Constraints¶

  • The report states research is lacking on how many intellectual processes could or should be employed in a man-technology program

Target Learners¶

  • Teacher education students; youth in technological society programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Inquiry, problem-solving, creativity, and self-realization through intellectual processes

Related Strategies¶

  • Socratic Seminar
  • Book Clubs / Literature Circles
