# Recruiting geographically diverse guest lecturers via videoconferencing for a remote course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because NE290 was first offered during the COVID-19 pandemic, the teaching team leveraged the newfound ubiquity of videoconferencing technology to recruit geographically diverse guest lecturers. Guests included historians, engineers, and practitioners from institutions such as the University of Maryland, University of New Mexico, Stevens Institute of Technology, Virginia Tech, RAND, and the IAEA, covering topics from cyclotron history to nuclear colonialism and the Iran Deal. The authors highlight that people involved directly in recent events were lecturing, connecting the current generation of nuclear scientists to those who made the history they must live with.
