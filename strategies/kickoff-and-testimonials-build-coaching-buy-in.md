# Hold a kick-off event aligning coaching with school and district goals, and leverage early volunteers' testimonials to build teacher buy-in

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy addresses the first administrator action in the report: building teacher buy-in for coaching. Administrators should clarify how coaching aligns with district, school, and teaching goals, because the report finds that "when teachers volunteer to participate in a coaching program with a positive perception and a clear understanding of what it involves, they are more likely to be invested in the process and benefit from the program." Concrete steps include a beginning-of-year kick-off event and amplifying early volunteers' coaching success stories in faculty meetings, word-of-mouth, and social media.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrators communicate a common vision linking coaching to district, school, and teaching goals at the start of the year and reiterate it over time.
