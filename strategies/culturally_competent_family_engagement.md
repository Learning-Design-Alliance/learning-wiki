# Culturally Competent Family Engagement

## Description
Culturally competent family engagement involves training school staff in behaviors, attitudes, and policies that enable effective cross-cultural relationships. Staff shifts from deficit thinking to strengths thinking, viewing families as having significant expertise and resources that can inform and improve student learning. This includes getting to know families, local community resources, and partners, and creating a welcoming school culture that emphasizes equality, acceptance, and shared values and goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires professional development for school staff on cultural competency, time for staff to get to know families and community resources, and a commitment to creating a welcoming school culture.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and commitment from school staff. Success depends on genuine empathy and a willingness to challenge existing biases and assumptions.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Schools seeking to improve engagement with diverse families, particularly those serving low-income families and families of color.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve cross-cultural relationships, shift from deficit thinking to strengths thinking, and create a welcoming school culture that emphasizes equality, acceptance, and shared values and goals.

### Affordances
- Creating a welcoming school culture improves engagement, supports student success, and empowers families to actively participate in their child’s education.

### Personalization
- Training can be customized to address the specific cultural and linguistic diversity of the school community. Staff can engage in ongoing professional development to deepen their understanding of different cultural norms and practices.
