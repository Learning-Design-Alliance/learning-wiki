# Design classroom contexts that align activity goals and practices with student goals to promote belonging

## Description
The article recommends that teachers alter contexts to facilitate students' sense of belonging by creating activities aligned with students' goals and practices. "Our model suggests that contexts can be altered to facilitate a student’s sense of belonging by creating activities that are in better alignment with his/her goals and practices." This positions motivation as partly within teacher control rather than fixed.
