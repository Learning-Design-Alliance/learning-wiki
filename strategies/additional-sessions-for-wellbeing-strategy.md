# Extend patience training with additional sessions to target well-being as well as patience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the tested program raised patience but not well-being, the authors recommend designing follow-up studies with more sessions and similar training events. As they put it, "It may be advisable to design new studies with additional sesions and similar patience training events applied in this r esearch and prepared by the researchers in order to raise also the well-being leveles". This is a forward-looking design recommendation, not a tested manipulation.
