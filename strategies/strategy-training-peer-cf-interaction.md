# Strategy training to strengthen learner-provided corrective feedback during peer interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review highlights learner-provided corrective feedback and recommends strategy training as a way to strengthen the role of CF during peer interaction. As the abstract states, "CF provided by learners and the potential benefits of strategy training for strengthening the role of CF during peer interaction are highlighted". The strategy equips learners to notice and respond to each other's errors in pair and group work, where teacher CF is absent, thereby extending feedback opportunities beyond teacher-led exchanges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
