# Mini homework defenses: short oral one-on-one assessments of student solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A formative oral assessment structure shared by a faculty member in Electricity and Magnetism 2: "short booked appointments in which a Learning Assistant randomly selects one of the student's completed homework problems, the student prepares briefly and then presents and is questioned on their own solution, receiving an immediate pass/fail score." The structure makes student reasoning visible and was presented as an AI-resistant assessment option. Several faculty expressed interest in and implemented oral assessments afterward.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning Assistants or graders to run appointments, plus timing, rubrics, and student guidelines
#### Constraints
- Faculty saw oral defenses as valuable but labor-intensive

### Target Learners
- Junior-level undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Making student reasoning visible and assessing authentic problem-solving in an AI-resistant format
