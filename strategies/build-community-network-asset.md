# Proactively build a professional community as an asset during institutional disruption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A strategy directing early-career scientists to build community proactively through open-source software contributions, online workshops and forums, hackathons, and cross-sector mentorship, using society platforms such as AMS and AGU annual meetings. The authors write that "in a time of institutional disruption, your professional network is a great asset." They add that these contributions are a form of evidence about passion and competence that a transcript cannot provide.
