# Use videoconferencing meeting mode rather than webinar mode so students can activate microphone and webcam

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To facilitate students' intervention during the online meetings, the organizers "opted for the meeting mode and avoided the webinar one, to allow them to turn on the microphone and the webcam at any time". Students were often questioned about their impressions of a given activity, and simple exercises through special software or platforms were proposed to ensure the active involvement of introverted students as well.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A videoconferencing platform offering a meeting mode in which participants can activate their own microphone and webcam
#### Constraints
- Reported for online meetings; the article does not evaluate the mode choice against an alternative

### Target Learners
- high school students, including introverted students

### Target Learning Goals
- active involvement of all participants during online lessons
