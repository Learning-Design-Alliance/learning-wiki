# Check network capacity and space pupils in larger rooms before implementing online one-to-one tuition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report's implementation recommendations for schools adopting online one-to-one tuition: "Schools should consider whether their computer network can support the implementation of an online programme", and, because the tuition is a talking intervention, pupils should be spaced out in larger rooms so noise from other sessions is less distracting. These recommendations arise from the process evaluation's identification of elements of successful delivery, including short-lived technical issues at the beginning of the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A reliable internet connection and adequate computer provision, verified before implementation
- A designated tuition space large enough to separate concurrent sessions acoustically
#### Constraints
- The process evaluation's findings, from which these recommendations derive, are stated by the report to be not generalisable

### Target Learners
- Primary school pupils receiving online one-to-one tuition

### Target Learning Goals
- Maths skills delivered through remote one-to-one tutoring sessions

## Claims

- [The talking-based online tuition appeared to work better when pupils were spaced out in larger rooms with less noise from other sessions](../claims/tsl-room-spacing-condition.md) [+W]
