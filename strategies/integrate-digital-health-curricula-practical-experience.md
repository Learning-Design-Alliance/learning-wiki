# Integrate digital health topics and practical experience with digital technologies into medical curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that medical educational institutions and policy makers respond to gaps and more reserved student attitudes by strengthening digital health education. It states institutions "should consider integrating more digital health topics into curricula, offer practical experience with digital health technologies, and implement effective teaching strategies for digital health," and that integration should be carefully considered and evaluated to meet the needs of both students and health care systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula must include digital health topics and provide practical experience with digital health technologies, implemented through effective teaching strategies.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that introducing digital health topics and innovation activities into medical curricula has proven challenging in practice due to crowded curricula designs and competing interests.

### Target Learners
- medical students
- future health care professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- digital health competence
- keeping professionals up-to-date with the evolving health care environment
