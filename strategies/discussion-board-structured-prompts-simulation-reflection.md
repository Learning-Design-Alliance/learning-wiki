# Use required online discussion boards with specific prompts and peer commenting to facilitate reflection during SLT simulation placements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study used a required online reflective discussion post after day one of simulation, in which students considered information or feedback gained from their clinical educator, simulated patient and peers and commented on at least three peers' comments. The authors report that "Embedding the use of specific prompts in this study seemed to guide students to process their experiences and emotions with their peers, whether they were completing placements online or in person." They encourage educators to consider this when designing reflective practice tasks, aligning with prior SLT research supporting structured prompts or facilitation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must complete a required reflective discussion post following the simulation debrief, commenting on at least three peers' comments.
#### Constraints
- The study could not access some original posts but only comments, and the authors note member checking could have strengthened confidence in the themes.

### Target Learners
- Mid-program speech and language therapy students (third-year undergraduate or second-year graduate entry masters)

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflective practice
- processing experiences and emotions with peers
- developing capability and internal motivation to use reflective practice
