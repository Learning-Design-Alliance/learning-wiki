# Incorporate metacognitive interventions such as mindfulness, feedback, and problem-based programs into educational practice to support adaptive offloading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends introducing metacognitive interventions into educational practice to enhance students' self-monitoring and regulation, helping them accurately assess task difficulty and cognitive load before deciding to offload. "Several methods, including mindfulness and feedback, have been shown to positively enhance metacognitive abilities." Improved object-level cognitive abilities through intervention training enable more informed use of compensatory strategies such as cognitive offloading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must improve metacognitive abilities (e.g., via mindfulness, feedback, or problem-based learning programs) so learners can make informed offloading decisions
#### Constraints
- The review presents these interventions as promising directions for future research and practice rather than as directly tested offloading interventions

### Target Learners
- students in higher education and general educational settings, including learners with mathematical difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive awareness, self-monitoring, and adaptive use of external cognitive aids
