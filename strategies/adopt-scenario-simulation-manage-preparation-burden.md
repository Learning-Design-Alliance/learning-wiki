# Adopt practical training scenario simulation teaching in teaching-hospital internships while managing preparation burden via standardized case templates or digital platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends promoting the practical training scenario simulation method in teaching hospitals, concluding it "can effectively improve the clinical practice ability of medical interns, reduce potential medical risks, and avoid unnecessary doctor-patient conflicts". Because teachers reported heavier pre-course preparation, longer course time, and difficulty grasping course progress, the authors propose developing "standardized templates for case scenario simulations or use digital platforms" to simplify preparation and make the method more accessible and scalable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time for case selection, pre-class assignment of problems, supervision of role-play, and post-simulation summary discussion
#### Constraints
- Implementation difficulties reported: more pre-course preparation, longer course time, and difficulty in grasping the course progress

### Target Learners
- Medical interns in the clinical internship stage at teaching hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthening clinical skills, including doctor-patient communication and clinical thinking, during internship
