# Community mobilization through sensitization campaigns, monthly meetings, and VDC/SMC involvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol prescribes community sensitization "through mobilization campaigns and monthly meetings" to motivate children to attend the classes and engage parents on the importance of education. Communities identify para-teacher candidates and participate in final selection, and the team works with Village Development Committees and School Management Committees to involve parents, manage the classes, and monitor children's attendance and learning progress, with regular meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communities provide the space for classes and are responsible for identification of possible candidates for para-teachers
#### Constraints
- VDCs and/or SMCs are involved where available in each village

### Target Learners
- Children and families in rural Gambian intervention villages

### Target Learning Goals
- Attendance at academic support classes and shared family responsibility for children's learning outcomes
