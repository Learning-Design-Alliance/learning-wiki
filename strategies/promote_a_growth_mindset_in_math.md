# Promote a Growth Mindset in Math

## Description
Promote a growth mindset by giving feedback that values effort and encourages persistence, showing learners that there is always room to improve their skills. In mathematics in particular, learners often think that intelligence is fixed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent feedback, a focus on effort and strategies, and a classroom culture that values growth and improvement.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset for both learners and teachers, may be challenging to implement in traditional, performance-focused classrooms.

### Affordances
- Encourages persistence, promotes a growth mindset, and helps learners see themselves as capable of improvement.

### Personalization
- Provide specific and meaningful feedback on individual learner efforts and strategies.

- Observe changes in learner persistence, monitor increased effort and engagement, and assess the development of a growth mindset.

## Impact
- Research shows that promoting a growth mindset increases persistence, improves problem-solving skills, and leads to greater academic success in math.
