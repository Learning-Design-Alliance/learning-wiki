# Train-the-trainer SCOPE forum preparing location champions to run peer-led simulation and debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NSW Ambulance ran 6-hour SCOPE forums in which one champion per location was trained to operate the manikin and support colleagues. The forum "focused exclusively on instructing champions about how to use the manikins, care for and maintain the technology, how to conduct peer-led simulations, and debrief paramedics afterward using a modified SHARP debriefing tool" (SHARPR, adding a referral process). Champions then returned to their stations to encourage colleague engagement with the manikin and scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Champions attend a forum within 2 hours of their station
- Experienced paramedic educators delivering written, verbal, and practical instruction in a consistent format
#### Constraints
- The article notes implementation after the forum was not standardized across stations

### Target Learners
- paramedic champions who then teach location colleagues

### Target Learning Goals
- peer-led simulation facilitation and structured debriefing for resuscitation skills maintenance
