# Replace the weekly lecture with an inverted lecture plus interactive, active-learning formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because all necessary content cannot be covered in a one-hour lecture and data analysis is more effectively learned actively, the course uses an inverted lecture style: "Lecture content was recorded as brief (<15 minutes) slideshow videos and supplemented with reading assignments." The freed lecture hour is used to work through sample calculations, interpret data, demonstrate software, and answer troubleshooting questions. Interactive formats include students collaboratively drawing chromatograms and expected SDS-PAGE gels on the board, concept mapping in week two, and questioning students about projected data figures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practical execution of lab methods provided as videos, either found online or created in-house
- Instructional staff engage students about their findings in real time during computational sessions
#### Constraints
- The most suitable instructional styles vary with the exact concepts, students, and instructors involved, and should be researched by the instructor

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biochemistry lab students

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis and interpretation
- statistical data processing
- conceptual understanding of lab methods
