# Build eLearning-based CPD systems that are user-friendly, enjoyable, and habit-forming, supported by infrastructure and digital-literacy training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend that developers and managers foster intention to use eLearning-based CPD by making systems user-friendly and enjoyable, building habit through familiarization, and strengthening enabling conditions. They advise that "eLearning system developers should concentrate on the system's user-friendliness" and that managers "increase familiarization with the eLearning system" via ICT-based training platforms, role-specific eLearning workshops, and time-based incentives, while encouraging rather than forcing uptake.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of resources, technical infrastructure, and support such as Wi-Fi access points and internet bandwidth within facilities
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on a cross-sectional study of public referral hospitals in one Ethiopian region; the authors note a cause-effect relationship cannot be established and call for larger-scale studies including private, primary, and general hospitals

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in referral hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuing professional development via eLearning

### Affordances
- Modified Utaut2 Computer Literacy Elcpd
