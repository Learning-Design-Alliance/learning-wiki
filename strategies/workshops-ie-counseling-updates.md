# Provide updates and workshops on intradialytic exercise for practicing nephrologists, particularly those with more years since graduation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends continuing-education activities for practicing nephrologists because most reported no formal exposure to intradialytic exercise during training. The authors state that 'updates and workshops could positively impact the low rates of IE counseling, particularly among older nephrologists', positioning professional development as the lever for closing the knowledge gap that suppresses exercise counseling for hemodialysis patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuing-education offerings must reach nephrologists already in practice, particularly those with more years since graduation, who were least likely to have received information about IE
#### Constraints
- The authors note that 'simply acquiring this knowledge is not sufficient' to address sedentary behavior in dialysis centers; knowledge is one barrier among several, including funding and qualified personnel

### Target Learners
- practicing nephrologists registered with the Brazilian Nephrology Society
- nephrologists with 11 or more years since graduation

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of the importance and benefits of intradialytic exercise for patients with chronic kidney disease
- ability to provide exercise guidance and counseling to hemodialysis patients
