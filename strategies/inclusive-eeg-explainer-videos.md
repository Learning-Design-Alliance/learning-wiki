# Send explanatory videos showing successful EEG visits with families of similar racial or ethnic backgrounds and varied hair types

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends providing a brief video showing EEG capping and collection, balancing simple language with useful detail and made fun and appealing for child participants. It states that “including short clips of a variety of families, with different hair types and styles, will assure participants that the lab is well-equipped to approach their hair with comfort and care.” Labs may send the video before a call to allow questions, or after a call to illustrate concepts discussed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video should strike a balance between simple language and useful details; labs should show real examples of successful visits with participants from similar racial or ethnic backgrounds.
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Families of infants and young children considering EEG participation, particularly Black and Latinx families

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing family understanding of the EEG procedure and reducing disproportionate decline of participation
