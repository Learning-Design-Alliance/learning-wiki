# Numbers to 100

## Description
A game where learners find numbers in sequential order on road signs, license plates, and maps to reinforce number recognition and ordering skills. Adjusting the target range (e.g., 1 to 20 or 1 to 200) adapts the difficulty to suit different age groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Road signs, license plates, maps, or other real-world number displays.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on availability of numbers in the environment. May become repetitive if played for too long without variation.
