# Motion and Forces Experiments

## Description
Motion and forces experiments involve exploring how forces affect the motion of objects. Learners demonstrate the transfer of energy by measuring the distances different balls move a cup and investigate variables that affect the movement of a pendulum. These experiments enhance understanding of energy transfer, motion, and forces.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Balls, ramps, cups, pendulums, strings, weights
#### Constraints
- Requires careful control of variables. Precise measurements are essential for accuracy.
