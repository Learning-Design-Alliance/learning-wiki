Immediate possibilities for tutoring systems to engage learner values: help teachers frame the system, provoke discussion of purpose, and hold a conversation about purpose and outcomes¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The authors propose three implementable ways for systems to attend to learner values. First, helping teachers understand how a classroom-embedded system can develop learners' investment in learning so work with it is appropriately introduced and followed up. Second, "Getting the system to prompt discussion between the learner and others — learners or teachers, or indeed via internal reflection — on the purpose and likely consequences of the interaction with the system." Third, engaging the learner in a menu- and keyword-based conversation about purpose, outcomes and process at the start and end of an interaction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The system need only provoke the discussion, not observe and understand it; the value-conversation approach may later need to take learners' cultures into account

Constraints¶

  • Establishing a learner's actual values interactively would require greater developments in natural language understanding

Target Learners¶

  • classroom learners using tutoring systems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • investment in learning, awareness of purpose and consequences of the interaction
