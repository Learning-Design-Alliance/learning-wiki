Develop or update continuity of learning plans using evidence-based and promising practices to prepare for blended learning and possible school closures¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy directs local education agencies to develop or update continuity of learning plans that keep students progressing academically across in-person and home settings. The fact sheet states it "highlights evidence-based and promising practices that local education agencies can consider when developing or updating their continuity of learning plans." It was produced as schools transitioned instructional programs from in-person to blended learning environments to mitigate coronavirus spread and prepare for possible additional closures during the 2020-2021 school year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A continuity of learning plan developed or updated by the local education agency
  • Instructional programs able to operate across in-person and blended learning environments

Constraints¶

  • Practices are described as evidence-based and promising rather than experimentally verified in the fact sheet itself

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students learning at home or at school during COVID-19 disruptions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Continued academic progress regardless of learning location

Related Strategies¶

  • State education agencies can commission regional educational laboratory analytic support to develop COVID-19 mitigation guidance for school reopening
  • Initiate trauma-informed planning before the start of the school year
  • Review the evaluation plan and possibilities for further research when funding is restored
