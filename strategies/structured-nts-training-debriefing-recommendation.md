# Incorporate structured non-technical skills training and structured debriefing sessions within simulation-based education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding structured non-technical skills training in simulation-based education: "Incorporating structured training in teamwork, communication, leadership and decision-making within SBE would enhance student preparedness for the role of a paramedic", reinforced by structured debriefing sessions. It further advises programmes to dedicate more time to reflection and to offer clearer guidance and preparation for reflective activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured debriefing sessions should be employed to reinforce these competencies
- Educational programmes should consider dedicating more time to reflection, offering clearer guidance and preparation and incorporating structured models to support the reflective process
