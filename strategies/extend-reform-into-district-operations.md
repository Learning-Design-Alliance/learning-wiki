# Extend systemic reform deeply into central district operations such as budgeting, finance, human resources, and facilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that districts pursuing standards-based reform include central operations units in their core reform strategies, because in most districts reform has barely touched operations like budgeting and finance, human resources, or facilities. It argues that "weak and disconnected local central operations undermine progress in instruction and teacher capacity-building by failing to free up money to help improve instruction." The recommendation appears in the Executive Summary and the section on extending instructional reforms deeply into operations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
