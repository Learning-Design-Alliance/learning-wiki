# Future SMS microlearning evaluations should add validated knowledge assessment, randomized comparison, broader sampling, and follow-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a design agenda for strengthening SMS microlearning research: "Future studies should include a validated knowledge assessment, a randomized comparison, broader sampling, and follow-up to assess durability and impact on care." This responds to the pilot's limitations: no objective knowledge test, no comparison group, a small self-selected sample, and outcomes assessed only immediately after the series. They also suggest randomization to alternative content or wait-list control and strategies to reach less-engaged clinicians.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated knowledge assessment aligned to the message set
- Randomization to alternative content or wait-list control
- Larger and more diverse samples with strategies to reach less-engaged clinicians
- Follow-up to assess durability and clinical impact
