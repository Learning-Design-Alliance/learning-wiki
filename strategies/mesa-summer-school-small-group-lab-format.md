# Run intensive hands-on computing schools with small groups, one teaching assistant per table of three learners, and mostly lab time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MESA Summer Schools offer participants a week of extensive hands-on labs; the article reports about 20% of the time is spent in lecture format while participants spend most of their time setting up, running, and interpreting models in small groups. One teaching assistant at each table of three learners ensures hands-on participation and energetic interactions. The instructor, TA and participant cohort now numbers over 500 across roughly 100 institutions.
