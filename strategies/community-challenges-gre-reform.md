# Community-level challenges: reform program admissions practices, reward appropriate practices, and remove the GRE from program rankings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author closes by assigning each stakeholder group a concrete action to reduce the GRE's role in physics graduate admissions. PIs should get involved in reforming their graduate programs; programs should determine and share admissions factors that actually predict research success and reduce the unintentional weight GRE scores receive; administrators should encourage and reward appropriate admissions practices, practices rather than policies; ranking entities should abandon using the GRE in program evaluations because it puts downward pressure on diversity; professional societies should develop statements on GRE usage.
