# Extend hybrid learning to teachers at all levels and to adult education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on its findings, the article recommends that "Hybrid learning can be extended to the teachers at all levels", that it "can be encouraged to be implemented in adult education", and that it "may be more supportive to promote teacher Education". The strategy is to adopt the mixed-methods hybrid approach in place of conventional methods when revamping the communicative skills of in-service teachers and pre-service trainees.
