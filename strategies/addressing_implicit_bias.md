# Addressing Implicit Bias

## Description
Recognizing and addressing implicit biases involves moving away from the idea that being biased is inherently negative and acknowledging that everyone possesses biases. This process necessitates bringing biases into the open for examination in a non-judgmental manner. Key strategies include personal self-awareness through techniques like substitution (considering if feelings or responses would be the same if the person were different) and analyzing informal networks to identify potential homogeneity. Additional measures encompass leadership trials, building blended teams to increase awareness of diverse perspectives, and utilizing panel interviews to reduce bias in recruitment.
