# Workplace facilitation of learning emphasizes team competence, dialogue about knowledge quality, and collaborative relationships

## Description
The discussion section identifies workplace facilitation as a strategy for effective CPD: "Facilitation of learning in the workplace emphasizes team competence and holistically creates opportunities for dialogue about the quality of knowledge used in practice and active sharing and implementation of different types of knowledge." Workplace facilitation aims to grow collaborative relationships amongst frontline practitioners, which encourage positive workplace cultures and engagement with different types of knowledge. Skilled facilitators with expertise in relevant fields support knowledge translation for evidence-based healthcare.
