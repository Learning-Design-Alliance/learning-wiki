# Summative Assessment to Measure Learning Over Time

## Description
Interim and summative assessments both measure learning over time. Interim assessments show individual student progress toward a set of standards. Summative assessments compare a student or group of students against a set of standards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Summative assessments, performance assessments, clear standards, and consideration of technology access.
#### Constraints
- May be limited by what can be assessed in the available time, and may not account for varying access to technology.
