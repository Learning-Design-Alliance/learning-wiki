# Simulations + Structured Activity

## Description
Students engage with simulations designed to provide hands-on experience with real-world scenarios or abstract concepts. These simulations are combined with a structured activity to ensure active learning and application of the concepts. Simulations allow students to experiment and learn from their mistakes in a safe and controlled environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to simulations (physical or virtual), design of structured activities, and facilitation of simulation experiences.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of content or learners. Requires careful alignment of simulations and learning objectives.

### Affordances
- Offers a hands-on and interactive approach to learning, promotes critical thinking and problem-solving skills, enhances engagement and retention.

### Personalization
- Simulations can be tailored to match learner interests and skill levels. Structured activities can be designed to address specific learning objectives.
