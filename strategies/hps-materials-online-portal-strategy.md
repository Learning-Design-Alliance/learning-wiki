# Establish an online portal collecting and disseminating HPS-based science lesson plans with NGSS references, teacher notes, and expert access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Teacher Preparation Working Group observed that HPS lesson plans and modules exist but "are not often available to teachers, either because they are published in scholarly journals for which the teachers h ave no access, or because they are provided in a form that is not user friendly". It proposed an online portal whose materials include NGSS references, notes by teachers who used them, and access to a local HPS expert, with lesson plans created at summer institutes bringing together HPS experts and high school teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Summer institutes pairing HPS experts with high school teachers to create the lesson plans
#### Constraints
- Proposed remedy addresses accessibility and usability, not the existence of materials

### Target Learners
- high school science teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- integrating history and philosophy of science into science teaching
