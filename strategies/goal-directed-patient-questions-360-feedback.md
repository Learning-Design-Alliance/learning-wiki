# Replace broad Likert-scale patient ratings with goal-directed questions and multisource (360°) feedback structures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend rethinking how patient feedback is collected to make it actionable: rather than broad Likert evaluations, "perhaps we could provide more goal directed questions that require reporting details of care provided during a particular clinical encounter". They situate this within multisource (360°) feedback, which gathers questionnaire data from peers, co-workers, and patients for physician reflection, and note the NHS Choices model lets physicians respond to individual patient comments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback collection structured around specific care encounters rather than broad rating categories
#### Constraints
- More formal rating structures may remove the anonymity present on less regulated sites, per the article

### Target Learners
- practicing physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- obtaining actionable patient feedback for practice improvement
