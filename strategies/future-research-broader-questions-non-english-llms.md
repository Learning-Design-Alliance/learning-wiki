# Future research should use broader question formats, non-English training data, and LLMs beyond ChatGPT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendations call for expanding evidence beyond multiple-choice questions and ChatGPT. The authors state that "future research should explore the application of LLMs beyond ChatGPT to offer an up-to-date perspective on their efficacy in medical and health education", and recommend incorporating additional training data in languages other than English to improve performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must include open-ended or scenario-based question formats and specialized training data for different question types.
#### Constraints
- The current evidence base covers only multiple-choice questions and ChatGPT models; no grey literature was included and the protocol was not registered.

### Target Learners
- medical and health profession students and professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating AI chatbot utility in health education and clinical settings
