# Leverage hearing and everyday audio to build qualitative understanding of the Fourier transform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Activity 1 exploits the fact that the FT underlies human hearing: students generate musical chords in Python, listen to them, and observe that spectral peaks align with the component frequencies. The article grounds this in the principle that "Involving multiple senses lowers barriers when acquiring new information," and explains: "Because the FT lies at the core of our hearing and vision, we leverage this connection to design Activity 1." Interactive sliders let students control wave frequencies, signal length, and dampening, then press play to hear the result, connecting time-domain changes to spectrum changes.
