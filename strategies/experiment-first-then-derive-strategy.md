# Sequence inquiry before derivation: show students the experimental W-state data first, then ask them to derive the equation

## Description
For correlation functions that lack an intuitive conceptual explanation, such as the W-state function, the authors recommend an inquiry-first ordering: students see measured data and are then motivated to derive the analytic expression themselves. The article states, "Instructors may wish to have students obtain experimental results ﬁrst and then wonder, 'How do I derive an equation for this?'" This positions the remote quantum experiment as a puzzle generator rather than a verification exercise.
