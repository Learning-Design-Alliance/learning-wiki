Use an access-timing gate as the only safe AI presence during protected struggle¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

During Probe, the move of protected struggle before instruction, the article recommends keeping AI out except for one mechanism: "an access-timing gate that unlocks the tool only after an independent attempt, which mirrors productive failure." The gate turns a struggle-killer into a struggle-respecter by ensuring the student generates an attempt before any assistance appears. Teachers can implement this per task without banning AI course-wide.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The tool must remain locked until the student has made an independent attempt on the problem
