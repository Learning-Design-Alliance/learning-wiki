# Use blended learning with 'internet plus' and flipped classroom in evidence-based medicine teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing blended learning comprising 'internet plus' and flipped classroom in evidence-based medicine courses for medical postgraduates. The recipe is: send videos, handouts, knowledge maps and advance questions a week before class; have subgroup debriefs scored by the instructor; close with a summary of common problems and multimedia resources for review and consolidation. They also urge providing richer resources such as MOOCs and micro-courses and exploring diversified methods including concept map and case-based teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be able to constantly adjust the teaching plan according to student feedback
- Fully prepare before class with appropriate resources such as videos and handouts
#### Constraints
- Requires teachers' transition input and equipment such as projectors and screens

### Target Learners
- medical postgraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of evidence-based medicine knowledge and problem-solving abilities
