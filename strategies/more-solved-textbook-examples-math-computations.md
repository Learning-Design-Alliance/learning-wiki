# Textbooks should include more solved illustrative examples for rotational inertia, center of mass, and field computations

## Description
Because textbooks provide only one or two simple worked examples for these computational techniques, the article recommends that publishers add more solved examples on computing center of mass, gravitational and electric fields, and "constantly point out that the involved techniques are essentially th e same" across topics. This supports active-learning strategies that require students to acquire basic knowledge through reading the textbook.
