# Provide targeted academic and psychological support for female students and adolescents with mental health challenges during critical educational years

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that school support systems should focus on the groups showing the strongest achievement deficits: "targeted psychological support for females and adolescents". It grounds this in the stronger associations among females and for later-onset diagnoses, and its key-points section states that "School support systems for early identification and intervention might improve learning, especially for females and later‐onset cases".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School systems able to identify students with mental health difficulties, since the recommendation rests on diagnosis-based risk groups
#### Constraints
