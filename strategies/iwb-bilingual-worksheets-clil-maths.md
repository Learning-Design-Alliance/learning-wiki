# Use interactive whiteboards with bilingual materials and worksheets for CLIL mathematics lessons.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes preparing digital teaching materials in the SMART Notebook environment, processed in an interactive form and supplemented with sounds recorded by native speakers. Materials included a guide for teachers and worksheets for independent in-class student work, designed to use interactive whiteboard elements and mathematical programs such as GeoGebra.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must prepare worksheets and educational materials in advance, with careful thought given to all activities and situations.
#### Constraints
- The program language could be switched back to Czech if necessary.

### Target Learners
- Primary school pupils in grades 6-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning mathematics content through English
- Developing mathematical craftsmanship alongside language skills
