# Simulate the actual ED's resource constraints, including realistic delays for lab and radiology results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The curriculum mirrored the resources of the actual emergency department: workups were provided only if available in the real ED, and results were delayed to match real workflows. The authors write that "labs or radiology ordered in simulation had a delay prior to results being given as there was no point of care or portable radiology available in the actual department," with a 3- to 5-minute delay. Learners also had to verbalize specific medication dosing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of the actual ED's available workups, dispositions, and turnaround times
#### Constraints
- CT and MRI were less readily available at Patan Hospital and were not offered during simulation

### Target Learners
- Medical officers
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Resource-appropriate clinical decision making
- Medication dosing
- Patient disposition
