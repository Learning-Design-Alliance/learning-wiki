# Collaborate with campus wellness services to incorporate local sexual assault trends into the four C's of prevention lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that health educators designing sexual assault prevention curricula use narrative approaches and specifically collaborate with campus wellness services to determine current local sexual assault trends, then incorporate those into the four C's when creating lessons. They state this 'may prove to be effective in increasing college students' concern about sexual assault.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to campus wellness services data on local sexual assault trends
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is extrapolated from a small pilot without a comparison group

### Target Learners
- college undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- increase concern about sexual assault
- sexual assault prevention knowledge and skills
