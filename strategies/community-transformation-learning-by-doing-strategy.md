# Transform community climate attitudes through student fieldwork using learning by doing, demonstrations and workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends transforming community attitudes via students during real work lectures or fieldwork lectures, using learning by doing or experiential learning methods that involve the community in an activity together. Explanation is directed to pragmatic and contextual concepts using guidance and counselling methods through physical and visual demonstration accompanied by workshops, in which the community sees, participates in doing, and implements practices such as making bio pores, planting trees, and making infiltration wells in the family environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- students equipped with theories and applications ready to be implemented in society
- community involvement in shared activities
#### Constraints
- aimed at communities who have been exposed to climate disasters

### Target Learners
- university students in fieldwork programs
- community members in disaster-exposed areas

### Target Learning Goals
- behavior change toward environmental care
- adaptation and mitigation practices at household level
