# Read and Reflect

## Description
Read and reflect involves students engaging in discussions or summarizing activities after reading independently. Students can do a turn and talk about their favorite part of a book, if they would recommend the book to a friend, or whether or not they would have ended the book differently. For a challenge, students can try to summarize the book in fewer than 280 characters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated time for reflection and discussion after independent reading. Clear prompts or questions to guide reflection and summarization.
#### Constraints
- May require scaffolding for students who struggle with reflection or summarization. Success depends on students engaging thoughtfully in the reflection and discussion process.
