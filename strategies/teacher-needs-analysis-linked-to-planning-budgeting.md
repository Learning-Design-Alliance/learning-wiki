# Conduct teacher needs analysis linked to organizational planning and budgeting, with follow-up beyond administrative data entry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teacher needs analysis develops personnel requirement data elements that must be linked to strategic organizational planning, budgeting, and recruitment and training planning activities. In South Bangka the Education and Culture Office struggled with adjusting teacher data for the Dapodik system, so "the real problems that occur in the field are not really resolved, because the focus of resolution is only on the administrative level". The strategy requires resolving field problems such as excess or shortage of teaching hours and equitable teacher quality distribution in real terms, not only administratively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accurate teacher data accepted into the Basic Education Data (Dapodik) system
- Linkage to strategic organizational planning, budgeting processes, and recurring recruitment needs
#### Constraints
- Cannot be realized directly because it must wait for the budgeting process
- Requires accurate data to carry out the analysis process
- Implementation takes a long time because field surveys are needed first

### Target Learners
- Teachers in South Bangka Regency, Indonesia

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher recruitment, placement, and distribution aligned with actual school needs
