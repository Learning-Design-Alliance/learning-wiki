# Storyboarding as a method for bootstrapping the design of computer-based educational tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Storyboarding is a method for capturing and sharing initial design ideas and their evolution when developing a learning task. The article presents it as a way of systematising thought experiments, producing storyboards that act as "virtual data" providing "an audit trail of evolving design ideas and enabled peer feedback to be sought". It was used to design a diagnostic computer-based mathematics task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sharable, ubiquitous tool such as MS PowerPoint with copy-paste and date-stamped annotation facilities
- A peer willing to review storyboards and add feedback
#### Constraints
- Only one example of storyboarding was presented and no attempt at generalisation can be made
- The method was experimental in origin and developed as it was used

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- relational understanding of arithmetical equivalence
