# Three-part implementation guidance for applying CBL in Chinese dental education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (3 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion recommends how teachers should apply CBL: design a teaching programme that improves students' ability to "think critically and treat patients comprehensively"; "develop or select realistic intriguing cases which link theoretical knowledge to practical aspects through inquiry-based learning methods"; and treat CBL as a three-dimensional cooperative relationship among teachers, students and virtual-realistic patient–doctor scenarios, with students at the centre engaged in information-gathering, analysis, synthesis and evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- teachers should design an effective teaching programme based on teaching experiences
- cases should account for the patient's medical, dental, psychological, socioeconomic and cultural conditions
#### Constraints
- time limitations in the Chinese curriculum make schools employ less time-consuming teaching models

### Target Learners
- dental students in Chinese dental schools

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- comprehensive patient treatment
- active learning capacity
