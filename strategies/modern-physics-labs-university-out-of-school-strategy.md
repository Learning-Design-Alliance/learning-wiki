# University-based modern physics laboratories where secondary students conduct experiments alongside researchers and then report to peers back in class

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy places fifth-year Liceo students in a research environment to perform hands-on experiments on modern physics topics listed in the national Indicazioni. Students work in groups beside researchers: "conducono in prima persona l'esperimento" and, back at school, "devono relazionare ai pari l'esperimento condotto in tutte le fasi". The article positions this as out-of-school learning that also serves university orientation and research dissemination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- University or research-centre laboratories with equipment for modern physics experiments not available in schools
- Researchers willing to supervise student groups during the experimental sessions
#### Constraints
- Designed for the fifth year of Liceo Scientifico, addressing topics in the national Indicazioni Nazionali

### Target Learners
- Upper secondary school students (fifth year of Licei Scientifici)

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding modern physics concepts (quantum mechanics, relativity) through direct experimentation
- Team working and peer communication of scientific results
