# Run rupture-focused supervision as a standardised five-step procedure of mindfulness, case introduction, two role plays with doubling, and a third role play on the therapist's own contribution

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAFT-D supervision is strongly standardised: each approx. 45-minute patient discussion follows a fixed sequence. The article states: "Supervision follows the following standardised procedure (duration approx. 45 min per patient): (A) Mindfulness exercise (to increase awareness and openness to one’s own emotional experience), 3–4 min" — followed by case and relationship introduction with a video sequence and rupture 'screenplay', two role plays each with doubling, and a third role play practising meta-communication with supervisor prompting and focus on the therapist's own vulnerabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fixed supervision groups of about four participants, one supervision session per four therapy sessions, and supervisors trained in MAFT-D with monthly 2-hour online booster training
#### Constraints
- Changing the format from group to individual supervision is generally not permitted within the study; a group of only two participants on a long-term basis is considered a protocol violation

### Target Learners
- trainee therapists treating study patients with depressive disorders
- experienced group supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- meta-communication about alliance ruptures
- awareness of the therapist's own contribution and vulnerabilities in ruptures
