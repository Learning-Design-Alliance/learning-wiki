# Explicitly address educators' theories of intelligence during instructor professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy embeds education about fixed versus growth mindsets into instructor development programs before or alongside training in active-learning practices. The article recommends that "Interventions might benefit from providing educators with knowledge of the downstream effects of both growth and fixed mindsets, explicitly emphasizing the relationship between such mindsets and the implementation of active-learning innovations." Once informed, educators can make their own decisions about pedagogical practices; suggested techniques include relevance intervention, framing struggles as opportunities, case studies, and explicit examples of growth in ability over time.
