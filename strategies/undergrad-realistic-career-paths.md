# Present undergraduates a realistic view of particle physics career paths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Universities should provide undergraduate students with a more complete picture of what particle physics trained researchers do. The report states that a realistic view of common career paths post baccalaureate and post graduate school should be presented, including for theoretical and experimental positions as well as non-academic careers. It further recommends developing connections and networking opportunities to strengthen links between undergraduates and HEPA community activities.
