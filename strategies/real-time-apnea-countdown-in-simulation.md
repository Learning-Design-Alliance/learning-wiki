# Display non-hypoxic apnea duration as a real-time countdown in difficult-airway simulation scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention adds focused training on non-hypoxic apnea duration plus a visible countdown during the scenario. The duration is "defined as the duration until SpO2 reaches 90%", set at 247 s and "displayed as a real-time countdown during the simulation". Trainees see an objective time budget while managing an unexpected difficult airway, which in this trial accompanied higher skill and satisfaction scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 15-minute focused pre-simulation briefing on the concept, its clinical significance and time-frame management, delivered as a lecture with interactive question-and-answer.
#### Constraints
- The 247 s value was specific to the simulated scenario (sourced from Tang et al., 2022); the study was small, single-center, and short-term.

### Target Learners
- anesthesiology undergraduate trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- airway management clinical skills
- pre-anesthesia preparation
- help-seeking and crisis response
