# Investigate other non-verbal first-impression factors and contextual factors in ad hoc entrustment for EPAs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future research move beyond the Duchenne smile to other first-impression cues and contextual influences on ad hoc entrustment in medical education. They state: "Future research could focus on exploring the influence of these other factors of non-verbal first impressions on ad hoc entrustment decisions in relation to EPAs in medical education." They also note that far more observable indicators of real first impressions, such as confidence in appearance and tone of voice, exist beyond the smile manipulation tested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must operationalize first-impression indicators beyond the Duchenne smile, such as confidence in appearance and tone of voice.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation follows a null result in a highly simulated avatar setting; the authors note the self-selected sample as a limitation.

### Target Learners
- medical students
- clinical supervisors making entrustment decisions

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding determinants of ad hoc entrustment decisions in EPA-based assessment
