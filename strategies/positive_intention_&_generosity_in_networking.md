# Positive Intention & Generosity in Networking

## Description
The "Positive Intention & Generosity in Networking" strategy emphasizes focusing on how one's interests and goals align with those of the people they meet and offering valuable resources beyond the obvious. It encourages individuals to think broadly about what they can offer others, such as gratitude, recognition, inclusion, unique insights, or knowledge. Framing networking in terms of collective benefits instead of personal gains fosters authenticity and increases the likelihood of successful relationship-building.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shift in mindset to focus on providing value, active listening, empathetic engagement, recognition of others’ contributions, and willingness to offer support.
#### Constraints
- Requires a genuine commitment to helping others, may not yield immediate personal gains, challenges traditional self-promotional networking approaches.
