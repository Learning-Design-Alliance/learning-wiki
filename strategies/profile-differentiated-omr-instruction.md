# Differentiate OMR instruction by motivational-emotional profile: autonomy support, relevance and mindset training, or strategy instruction with emotional regulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that educators should tailor OMR instruction to students' motivational-emotional profiles. For positive-profile students, "Educators should provide autonomy-supportive practices" to maintain their profile and performance; for moderate-profile students, personally relevant texts and growth mindset training to enhance tolerance for trial-and-error and persistence; for ambivalent-profile students, specific multimodal strategy instruction with emotional regulation support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The recommendations derive from one study of 280 Hong Kong fourth graders and three public primary schools of medium socioeconomic status
