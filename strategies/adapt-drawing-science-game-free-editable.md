# Release the game free and editable so educators adapt categories, language and cultural context, with a mime mode extending inclusion to sign language

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The complete game "in Portuguese and English versions is available for free and can be edited", and the authors leave suggestions that educators implement improvements adjusted to their cultural and educational reality. They suggest replacing the six physics-weighted categories with the six National Curriculum Parameters themes, and note the mime modality could be used to teach sign language in undergraduate courses for teacher training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All players must accept the decision to play the mime modality before the game starts
#### Constraints
- The six card categories were chosen within the context of a physics teacher training course, which gave more emphasis to physics words

### Target Learners
- high school students
- undergraduate students
- teachers in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary Natural Sciences concepts
- sign language communication
- cultural and educational adaptation of science learning materials
