# Pair automated online problem-set quizzes with lectures, with rapid scored feedback and immediate posting of solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes replacing TA-led discussion sections with an on-line quiz system delivering mathematically intense problem sets: quizzes are "scored automatically, and the results (plus solutions) posted immediately after the deadline", providing students rapid evaluation. Deadlines fall several days after each lecture, allowing time to work solutions and seek assistance, while automatic tracking of scores eases analysis of achievement data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A network tool infrastructure able to automatically score online quizzes, post results and solutions immediately, and parse and extract large volumes of student record data.
#### Constraints
- The course as described had no teaching assistant support, which is what motivated the move to the on-line quiz system.

### Target Learners
- non-science-major undergraduates in large introductory survey courses

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematical reasoning in stellar astronomy
- practice solving quantitative problem sets with rapid feedback
