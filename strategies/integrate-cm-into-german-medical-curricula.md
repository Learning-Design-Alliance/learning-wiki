# Integrate culinary medicine into compulsory German medical curricula with prevention and interprofessional learning contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that German universities move CM beyond electives into the curriculum, following models implemented at some American universities. It argues that "future curricula at German universities should also incorporate CM within the contexts of prevention and interprofessional learning", and that future research should identify competencies, skills and appropriate assessment mechanisms, with longitudinal studies measuring long-term effects and endpoints based on counselling skills actually acquired in working life.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future research should identify competencies, knowledge and skills and appropriate assessment mechanisms to improve transparency regarding needs and limitations
#### Constraints
- Current evidence rests on elective, mostly self-assessed studies; the review calls for objective endpoints rather than self-assessed skills

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in Germany

### Target Learning Goals
- diet-related knowledge, skills and attitudes
- nutrition counselling competencies
