# Bibliometric mapping of top research groups in Physics and Mathematics in Southeast Europe

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a planned sub-project, the Network will map the top research groups and organizations in Physics and Mathematics following their publication records from 2000-2009, surveying ten SEE countries. The authors state that "almost all authors agreed on the fact that the 'bibliometric' observation of publications in scientific journals or of the quotations in the major bibliographical databases offers the best image on the level of scientific production and collaboration". The research is planned to last 18 months, from Nov.2009 to April 2011.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to international bibliographical databases and quantitative analysis techniques; an 18-month research period
#### Constraints
- The resulting report will be an intermediary one, with the final report asking for supplementary validation in a forthcoming project

### Target Learners
- graduate students and young researchers in the surveyed SEE countries

### Target Learning Goals
- identification of groups of excellence and strengthening of interest for physics and mathematics, especially among youth and women
