# Phased implementation strategy for robot-assisted training to distribute costs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions adopt a phased implementation strategy to improve feasibility of robot-assisted training, such as sharing the robot as a core facility across departments or collaborating with regional teaching centers to distribute costs, thereby maximizing its educational value. This addresses the significant barrier of initial purchase and maintenance expenses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to sharing resources across departments or regional teaching centers
#### Constraints
- Cost remains a significant barrier, as the system's initial purchase and maintenance expenses far exceed those of traditional simulation head models

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- patient interview skills
- clinical examination skills
- doctor-patient communication
