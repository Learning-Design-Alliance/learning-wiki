# Pose open ethical and epistemological questions about space exploration as inspirational anchors for student reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course anchors student engagement in open questions that go beyond scientific reasoning, such as "Who 'owns' the Moon? Or the planets?" and "Could planetary exploration activities pose a contamination risk; if so, what kind of precautions must we adopt?" The authors highlight that "the basis of our program is to use the inspirational nature of these questions," aiming to prepare students to be ethical and responsible researchers and citizens.
