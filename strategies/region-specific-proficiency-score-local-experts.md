# Set a region-specific proficiency score based on local expert performance when adapting a validated simulation for a new training context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
When deploying a simulation system validated elsewhere in a new training context, the article demonstrates deriving a locally calibrated proficiency benchmark rather than importing the original one. The authors recalculated proficiency "on the basis of the median overall score on the five tasks for urogynecology fellows at the study hospital, as has been done in other basic skills programs", yielding a cutoff of 266 instead of the original 400. This approach accounts for local context, protocol modifications, and the benchmark group's clinical experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A local expert benchmark group whose performance is an appropriate reference for trainee proficiency
#### Constraints
- The authors note future research should evaluate more validity evidence for the FVS scoring system within this clinical region

### Target Learners
- Ob/Gyn residents in LMIC training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Establishing and assessing baseline and proficiency-level surgical skills
