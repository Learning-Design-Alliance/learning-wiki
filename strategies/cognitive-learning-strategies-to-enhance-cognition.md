# Teachers employ cognitive learning strategies — spaced practice, interleaving, elaboration, retrieval practice, reflection and mindfulness — to deepen knowledge processing

## Description
The article recommends that, because students' basal cognition levels can be assessed, "Teachers are recommended to employ cognitive learning strategies" that enhance learners' capability to process knowledge deeply and transfer it to new circumstances. It enumerates spaced practice, interleaving, elaboration, retrieval practice, reflection training and mindfulness learning, and notes medical schools are moving to competency-based curricula where reflection, case-based discussions, journal clubs and self-directed learning are becoming essential.
