# Half-day repetition training sessions with new scenarios and raised performance expectations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the main training, SHOs take part in half-day repetition sessions in which one clinical scenario is executed and repeated. New scenarios are designed on the same emergency obstetric situations, but "expectations for the level of performance will be raised and some extra elements such as hand hygiene will be added to keep it challenging." Each repetition session starts with an introduction in which learning goals are defined and ends with evaluation and summarization of those goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local facilitators organize each session, with two local trainers per training session

### Target Learners
- Senior house officers who completed the main training day

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention and refinement of emergency obstetric technical and teamwork skills
