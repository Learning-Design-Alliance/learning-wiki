# Engage community stakeholders as equal partners to design, pilot, and deliver bias training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The trial used a participatory action research approach in which investigators collaboratively partnered with diverse community leaders and academic faculty to design case simulation scenarios, a trainee performance evaluation checklist, and standardized patient training protocols. Recruiting community members as SPs created, in the authors' words, an opportunity for empowerment and equity, as community SPs provided direct skills feedback and contributed their voice as equal partners in refining the simulation scenarios.
