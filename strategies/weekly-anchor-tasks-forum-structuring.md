# Use weekly instructor-created anchor threads to structure forum discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes an instructor practice of creating weekly optional discussion topics via the CN Tasks feature, which appear at the top of the forum page. The authors connect this to Guzdial and Turns' recommendation of "anchor" threads that prompt students with key discussion topics. Semesters using weekly tasks showed denser, more educationally useful networks than the semester without them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A forum platform supporting instructor-created, prominently displayed discussion prompts updated regularly
#### Constraints
- The authors cannot say the absence of weekly tasks was the sole cause of Semester 2's loss of network connectivity

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus-based physics undergraduates at a commuter urban public university

### Target Learning Goals
- building classroom community through asynchronous peer discussion
- supporting course success through peer interaction
