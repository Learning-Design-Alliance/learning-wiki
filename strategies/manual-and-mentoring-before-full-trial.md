# Provide a programme manual plus mentoring and support before scaling up a teacher-developed physical activity intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evaluation's development recommendation for PAL: address inconsistent delivery and unclear fidelity benchmarks by producing a manual and strengthening support structures. The report states that "Many of the issues that arose in the teacher and headteacher interviews can be addressed through the development of a programme manual alongside the provision of additional mentoring and support." The manual should give teachers a clear understanding of aims and outcomes and a comprehensive implementation guide, especially for integrating activities into literacy lessons. For a full trial, the report further recommends paper-based tests, additional explanatory variables, and successful year-group-level randomisation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A programme manual detailing aims, outcomes and implementation guidance
- A stronger mentoring and support structure throughout the delivery phase to ensure fidelity and quality of implementation
#### Constraints
- The programme required further development before it was ready to be tested using a full trial

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils (Years 4 and 5)

### Target Learning Goals
- literacy and numeracy attainment through physically active lessons
