# Develop Educator Understanding

## Description
Educators develop their own understanding of self-awareness and how it affects learning by reading up on the resources and research available. Use the Learner Variability Navigator to support your learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to research articles, books, and online resources, as well as time for reading, reflection, and discussion.
#### Constraints
- Requires a commitment to ongoing learning and professional development. Educators may need support in applying the research to their practice.

### Personalization
- Educators can focus on the resources and research that are most relevant to their students' needs and learning context.
