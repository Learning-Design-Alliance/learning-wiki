# Strengthen in-service training and resilience support to raise early childhood teachers' self-efficacy, pedagogical competence and professional well-being

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that kindergartens and society support early childhood teachers' well-being by building resilience, self-efficacy and pedagogical competence. It states that "having professional training, including resilience-related courses as well as practicum experiences, can increase teachers' levels of professional well-being", and that kindergartens should "promote the concept of resilience to teachers" and improve educational efficacy and competence in various ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires provision of resilience-related courses, education courses and practicum experiences for teachers
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a cross-sectional survey of early childhood teachers in one Chinese city and cannot be generalised to other school segments, per the authors

### Target Learners
- Early childhood (preschool) teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher resilience
- Teacher self-efficacy
- Pedagogical competence
- Professional well-being
