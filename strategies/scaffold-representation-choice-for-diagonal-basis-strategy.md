# Revise the CQS to scaffold choosing a representation in which the operators of interest are diagonal

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the in-class evaluation, the authors plan revisions so students learn to select a basis convenient for computing matrix elements. "More scaffolding will be provided to help students construct a set of basis states that is not only complete, but is also convenient for evaluating the matrix elements of operators corresponding to observables" — for example, choosing the coupled representation would have made both Hamiltonians diagonal. The second section will offer additional practice constructing a coupled-representation basis and the third section more practice identifying a representation in which a given operator is diagonal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional scaffolding questions integrated into the second and third sections of the CQS
#### Constraints
- Motivated by observed posttest difficulties on questions (b) and (d); effectiveness of the revision is not yet evaluated

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate quantum mechanics students

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive selection of a basis in which operators corresponding to observables are diagonal

## Claims

- [Think-aloud interviews indicate QM experts consider whether operators are diagonal in a representation before choosing a basis, while students generally do not perform this metacognitive check](../claims/experts-check-diagonality-before-choosing-basis-students-do-not.md) [+W]
- [After the CQS, students performed poorly on the spin-spin Hamiltonian matrix elements (question (b)) and on judging diagonality (question (d)) because most chose the uncoupled representation, in which the spin-spin Hamiltonian is not diagonal](../claims/uncoupled-basis-choice-hurts-non-diagonal-matrix-elements.md) [+M]
- [Compared with the CQS group, most QuILT students answered the diagonality question (d) correctly on the posttest but struggled to calculate every matrix element of the 6x6 matrix in question (b)](../claims/quilt-group-diagonality-correct-but-full-matrix-elements-difficult.md) [~M]
- [CQS students improved from pretest to posttest on all four pre/posttest questions, with normalized gains from 0.31 to 0.88 (N=16)](../claims/cqs-pre-post-normalized-gains-all-questions.md) [+M]
