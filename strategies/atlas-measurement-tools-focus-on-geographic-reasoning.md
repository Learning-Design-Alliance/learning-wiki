# Use electronic atlas measurement and coordinate tools to shift student attention from routine calculation to geographic reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using the atlas's built-in measurement functions (distance, area, coordinates) to relieve students of laborious manual measurement on paper maps. According to the authors, these tools "exempt him from routine measurements and calculations" and "allow one to focus on the geographic nature of the results," making practical tasks on measurements and calculations more feasible within limited instructional time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the electronic school atlas software with measurement toolbar functions
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of Kazakhstan school geography lessons where paper-map measurement is time-consuming

### Target Learners
- Secondary school geography students

### Target Learning Goals
- Distance and area measurement
- Geographic coordinate determination
- Interpretation of geographic results
