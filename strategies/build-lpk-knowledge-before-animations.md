# Build low-prior-knowledge learners' knowledge with other learning modes before introducing animation-based teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Since animations cannot be assumed effective for everyone and were ineffective for LPK students, the article recommends a sequencing move: "sufficient knowledge regarding concept must first be shared with LPK students before teaching them with animations". Other modes of learning should be used to enhance the prior knowledge of LPK learners, and only subsequently should the animation-based teaching methodology be introduced to them. The authors flag this sequencing as an area of research for the future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial instructional phase using other modes of learning to raise prior knowledge before animations are introduced
#### Constraints
- The article notes this recommendation is itself another area of research for the future, not yet empirically tested

### Target Learners
- low prior knowledge learners

### Target Learning Goals
- raising domain/topic prior knowledge so that animation-based teaching can be effective
