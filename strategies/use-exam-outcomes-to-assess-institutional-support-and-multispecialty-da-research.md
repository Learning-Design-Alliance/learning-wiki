# Use major examination outcomes to assess how schools support doctors from different backgrounds, and pursue multi-specialty, intersectional DA research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend against reducing judgements of doctors to simple examination performance, which they warn "may exacerbate differences and reinforce the status quo". Instead they propose using outcomes on major examinations "as a way of assessing how different medical schools support doctors from different educational and social backgrounds to reach their full potential", alongside future research that is multi-specialty and examines intersectionality across multiple variables rather than limited personal characteristics.
