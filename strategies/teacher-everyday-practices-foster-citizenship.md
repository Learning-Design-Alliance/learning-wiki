# Teachers foster citizenship through everyday practices: modeling, feedback, and social referencing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes that beyond formal curricula, teachers convey citizenship through daily interactions in three ways: "teachers can model how to act as citizens," they can provide feedback on how children act toward classmates, and they indirectly signal how much they appreciate a child via daily interactions (social referencing), thereby promoting or hindering positive peer relationships. These practices affect both individual children and the classroom as a miniature society.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness among teachers of the citizenship messages their everyday teaching practices convey; integration of interpersonal skills with curricular activities
#### Constraints
- Some cited findings on teacher practices and group-level norms rest on cross-sectional data, so directionality is not established

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Solidarity, peaceful (nonviolent) behavior, and social cohesion among classmates
