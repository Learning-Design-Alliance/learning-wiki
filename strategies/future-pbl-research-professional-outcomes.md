# Future pharmacy education research should prioritize experimental designs assessing professional effectiveness outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review states that future research should prioritize experimental designs that assess outcomes more directly associated with professional effectiveness and more valuable in the workplace. This addresses the limitation that no included study reported professional activity-related outcomes, so the review cannot infer that academic improvement results in better professionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experimental designs
- Outcomes directly associated with professional effectiveness
#### Constraints
- The current results are based on a small number of studies that may not reflect the diversity of pharmaceutical education in different contexts

### Target Learners
- pharmacy students and practicing pharmacists

### Target Learning Goals
- professional effectiveness and workplace performance
