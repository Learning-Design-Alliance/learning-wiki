# Building a Supportive Classroom Environment for Mistakes

## Description
This strategy focuses on specific actions to foster a learning environment where mistakes are seen as opportunities. Teachers should avoid simply marking errors without explaining why they're wrong, give students a chance to correct their mistakes and redo their work, and make improvement a significant factor in the evaluation process. In class discussions, respond to mistakes by asking students to explain their reasoning, starting with what is right, and allowing them to seek help from classmates. Additional approaches include showcasing mistakes and lessons learned, holding biweekly class meetings to share mistakes, and teachers sharing their own mistakes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher commitment to changing their approach to mistakes, a classroom culture that values learning over perfection, and structured activities that encourage reflection and sharing.
#### Constraints
- Requires a significant shift in mindset and may face resistance from those accustomed to traditional evaluation methods. May take time to establish trust and openness in the classroom.
