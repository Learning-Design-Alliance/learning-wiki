# Sequence lightly scaffolded hints before heavily scaffolded hints so scaffolding fades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the tutorial's guided questions, a first hint presents a hypothetical discussion between two students offering one correct and one incorrect viewpoint, and a second, more heavily scaffolded hint offers three candidate answers with minor differences. The article argues that 'Using a more lightly scaffolded hint first, followed by a more heavily scaffolded hint, allows the student to develop self-reliance as they become less dependent upon the hints,' enabling reliance on scaffolding to fade.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions that build on each other with hints embedded at each step
- Correct answers with explanations provided after students have attempted each question
