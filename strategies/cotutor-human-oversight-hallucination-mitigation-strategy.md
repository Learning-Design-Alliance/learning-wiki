# Design AI tutoring copilots with human oversight, hallucination mitigation, and privacy safeguards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educational AI copilots mitigate hallucination risk and preserve instructor authority by standardizing generative replies where feasible, logging all outputs for instructor review, and requiring explicit educator approval for consequential decisions. Oversight is implemented through mandatory override justifications, pre-deployment AI evaluation training, and continuous educator feedback channels, complemented by pseudonymized, encrypted data handling and role-based access controls.

## Design Implications

### Context
