# Integrate AI chatbots into dental education as supervised supplementary tools for exam preparation and formative self-assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using AI chatbots as supplementary tools in dental education for exam preparation, formative self-assessment, and clinical reasoning exercises, "particularly in preclinical and early clinical training phases." It frames their pedagogical value via retrieval practice, scaffolding through stepwise explanations, and cognitive load management through immediate feedback. Because accuracy and response characteristics vary across models, outputs must not be treated as authoritative and require faculty guidance and critical appraisal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI-generated outputs must be accompanied by faculty guidance, critical appraisal skills, and awareness of potential misinformation
#### Constraints
- The study did not directly assess learning outcomes or clinical performance; these should be addressed in future educational intervention studies
- Model performance may be influenced by prompt formulation, and alternative phrasing could yield different outcomes

### Target Learners
- dental students in preclinical and early clinical training phases

### Target Learning Goals
- restorative dentistry knowledge
- clinical reasoning
- exam preparation and formative self-assessment
