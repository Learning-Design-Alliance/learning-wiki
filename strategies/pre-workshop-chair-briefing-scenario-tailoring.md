# Tailor simulation scenarios to each board via a pre-workshop chair briefing selecting a starting point

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before each workshop, the facilitation team reviewed public materials about board members and spoke with the Board Chair for 20–30 minutes to learn the board's current function and identify specific issues. The chair could "specify which of the three possible 'starting points'—asking difficult questions, dealing with pushback or refocusing the conversation—would be the most suitable starting point for their board", which "was used to establish the best scenarios for the workshop". The CEO was also briefed beforehand.
