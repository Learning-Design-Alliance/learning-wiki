# Set measurable, time-bound goals for school leader diversity and make diversity data publicly visible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that state and district leaders begin diversity efforts by "setting measurable and time-bound goals" supported by widely accessible, high-quality data. States should publish annual school-, district-, and state-level leader workforce diversity data including turnover rates disaggregated by race, require preparation programs to publicly report candidate racial makeup, and require local education agencies to set and report educator diversity goals periodically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Widely accessible, high-quality data on leader workforce diversity, published via dashboards, school report cards, or state reports
- Clear numeric goals that are measurable, ambitious, achievable, and time bound

### Target Learners
- State and district education leaders
- School leaders and aspiring school leaders of color

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing the racial diversity of the school leader workforce
- Equitable student access to experienced, diverse leaders
