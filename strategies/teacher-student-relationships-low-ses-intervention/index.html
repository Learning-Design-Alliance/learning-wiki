Promoting teacher–student relationships for children from low SES backgrounds may be a promising intervention avenue¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review highlights that teacher–student closeness was found to buffer the association between SES and achievement in multiple studies, and that teacher–student relationships play a key role in student engagement and achievement. It suggests that promoting teacher–student relationships for children from low SES backgrounds may be a promising avenue for future research aimed at identifying intervention targets.
