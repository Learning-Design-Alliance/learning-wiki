# In training MBCT therapists, pay explicit attention to explaining the rationale and engaging patients in agreeing on therapeutic goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the finding that agreement on therapeutic goals predicts better outcomes be reflected in MBCT therapist training. It suggests that more attention could be paid, for example, on explaining the rationale of MBCT and engaging patients in the process. This is a forward-looking clinical implication drawn from the alliance goal-subscale finding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Therapists need opportunity in training and sessions to explain the MBCT rationale and negotiate treatment goals with patients
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on an exploratory, uncontrolled completer analysis in distressed cancer patients and should be interpreted with that scope

### Target Learners
- distressed adult cancer patients participating in group-based MBCT

### Target Learning Goals
- reduction of psychological distress (anxiety and depressive symptoms)
