# Foster positive teacher-student relationships through mindful practices, positive reinforcement, and out-of-class communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that TSRQ can amplify positive emotions and diminish negative ones, the article recommends that educators cultivate robust teacher-student relationships. It states that "adopting mindful practices, providing positive reinforcement, and incorporating out-of-class communication emerge as effective approaches for educators seeking to cultivate robust teacher-student relationship," which may in turn support students' emotional experiences in learning English as a foreign language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained, high-quality interpersonal relationships between English teachers and their students.
#### Constraints
- Recommendation derives from cross-sectional correlational evidence, which the authors state precludes causal inference.

### Target Learners
- Chinese college EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- enhancing enjoyment and reducing anxiety and boredom in EFL learning
