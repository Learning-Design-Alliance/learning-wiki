# Use instructional videos of standard physical examination procedures, including common mistakes, as resources for independent student learning and teaching

## Description
The article suggests that "It has proven useful to utilize instructional videos on standard procedure of physical examination", with students using them for independent learning and clinical teachers adopting them as teaching resources. It further recommends that videos also address common PE mistakes, not only the standard procedure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos depicting standard examination procedure; ideally also demonstrating common PE mistakes
#### Constraints
- Recommendation drawn from cited literature rather than tested in this study

### Target Learners
- medical students in preclinical and clinical diagnostics training

### Target Learning Goals
- correct physical examination technique
