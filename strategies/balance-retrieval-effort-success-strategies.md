# Balance retrieval effort and retrieval success by adding feedback, repeated spaced retrieval, and hybrid question formats to VSAQ practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To optimize retrieval practice, particularly with VSAQs whose initial retrieval success is lower, the authors recommend "integrating immediate or delayed feedback, and implementing repeated spaced practice retrieval." They also propose "Hybrid questions, such as combining VSAQs with MCQs or using stepwise MCQs," which "engage students in initial effortful retrieval (i.e., answering an open question without cues) followed by multiple-choice options for direct feedback." Self-assessment with immediate self-feedback VSAQs is offered as an alternative to teacher feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback provision or self-feedback mechanisms to raise initial retrieval success
- Teacher familiarity with VSAQ construction, supported by clear instructions or workshops
#### Constraints
- The article notes further research is needed to evaluate the effectiveness of hybrid formats in health professions education

### Target Learners
- health science students
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals

- recognize knowledge gaps and misconceptions
