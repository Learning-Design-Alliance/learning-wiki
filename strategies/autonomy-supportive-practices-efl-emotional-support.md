# Adopt autonomy-supportive practices to cultivate emotionally supportive EFL environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that EFL teachers use autonomy-supportive practices—"acknowledging students’ perspectives, providing meaningful rationales, and framing challenges constructively"—to generate positive academic emotions that enable agentic participation. It also suggests concrete activities: portfolio-based self-assessment to foster pride and shared leadership roles in collaborative tasks to cultivate hope. Teacher education programs are advised to develop teachers' capacity to support students' emotional experiences.
