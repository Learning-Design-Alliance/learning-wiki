# Use the SOE formatively to give students individual feedback on weak skill profiles, and combine it with SKT or KFE modules for broader level-2 evidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond summative grading, the article proposes two implementable uses: (1) formative feedback — "individuelle Leistungsprofile sichtbar werden, etwa bei der Befundinterpretation oder der Benennung von Differenzialdiagnosen", so the format can be used for targeted student feedback; and (2) triangulation — combining the SOE as an oral format with Script Concordance Test or Key-Features Examination modules to broaden evidence for competency level 2, since SKT and KFE are more standardized and less vulnerable to rater effects but require elaborate item/panel processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SOE score records broken down by category to reveal individual profiles; for triangulation, capacity to build SKT/KFE item and panel processes
#### Constraints
- SKT and KFE demand elaborate item/panel processes; the article states these formats capture less of communicative structuring and at-case reasoning than the SOE

### Target Learners
- medical students; also candidates in specialist (Facharzt) examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning under uncertainty, decision-relevant key steps, differential diagnosis
