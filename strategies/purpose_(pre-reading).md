# Purpose (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Learners are given a specific purpose for reading, typically in the form of discussion questions about the content prepared by the teacher. They review these questions before reading and prepare answers while reading the text. Afterward, they discuss the questions with a partner. This strategy focuses attention, activates prior knowledge, and enhances comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires pre-prepared discussion questions related to the reading content.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the questions. Some learners may focus too narrowly on finding answers rather than engaging deeply with the text.
