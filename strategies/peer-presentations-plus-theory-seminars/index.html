Structure teacher support events as a mixture of peer presentations by fellow teachers and theoretical ideas to prompt reflection and peer support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors structured their online seminars to offer "a mixture of peer support through presentations by fellow teachers and theoretical ideas which would assist the teachers in the audience to reflect on their own emerging practice". As weeks progressed, teachers shared practice stories and encouraged each other through the text chat feature of the conferencing tool. Because active and experiential learning was not feasible in their setting, the seminars offered another channel for reflection and peer-support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An online conferencing tool with text chat; invited teacher contributors willing to share practice stories

Constraints¶

  • Active and experiential learning was not feasible in this webinar setting, so the format substitutes reflection and peer-support for it

Target Learners¶

  • primary and secondary school teachers engaging with online teaching

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reflection on emerging online teaching practice
  • peer support and motivation among teachers

Affordances¶

  • Philipsen Tpd Obl Framework

Claims¶

  • During emergency remote teaching, teachers' professional learning was opportunistic rather than planned, with no clear duration [~W]
  • The pandemic made professional learning about online and blended learning immediately relevant and highly sought after by teachers who had previously been difficult to persuade to prioritise it [+W]
  • The Philipsen et al. framework of TPD components for online and blended learning is biased toward higher education settings [~W]
  • Schools that had previously invested in technology and professional development for digital learning responded more quickly to the shift to online teaching during COVID-19 closures [~W]
