# Use shared, continuously visible online laboratory report templates with real-time and threaded commenting to scaffold instructor guidance at a distance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each GEAS project provides a Google-Docs template laboratory report that students copy to their own account and share with instructors, so instructors can view reports across the entire two-week project interval rather than only at the end. Students are strongly encouraged to enter experimental data into tables as collected, a built-in chat function allows real-time comments in the report margin, and asynchronous threaded comments can be placed at specific locations. The article frames this as creating "an interactive process of exploration and advancement between students and instructors as the data are taken, analyzed, and reported for each project," particularly critical for distance cohorts lacking easy back-and-forth discussion.
