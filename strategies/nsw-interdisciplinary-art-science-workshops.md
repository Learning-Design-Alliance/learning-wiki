# Interdisciplinary hands-on art-science-culture workshops (carving, clay, art-making)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
NSW delivers hands-on community workshops where participants learn science content through culturally relevant art-making, then make the ideas their own. Examples include "Carving your Animal Clan," which layers the Ojibwe clan system with the geologic story of local rocks, and "Be Humble for you are made of Earth," which pairs the glacial story of Minnesota's lakes with indigenous pottery and clay teachings. The article reports that "Across the state over 40 art-focused NSW workshops have been delivered primarily in native communities," with participants from youth to elders in venues including community centers, museums, schools, and libraries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Native team-members and cultural experts leading workshops
- materials for participants to create their own artwork
- encouragement for participants to consult local elders when tradition has been lost
#### Constraints
- The article notes realistically not all native or mixed-race native participants are knowledgeable about their family clan, and much tradition has been lost

### Target Learners
- native community members from youth to elders
- K-12 educators
- mixed-race native participants

### Target Learning Goals
- Ojibwe clan system
- geology and glacial history
- cultural teachings such as the sweat lodge ceremony
