# Implementation strategies for flipped clinical reasoning courses: manage pre-learning load, simplify quizzes, orient thoroughly, and use reflective journals for assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion offers concrete implementation recommendations: upload pre-learning materials at least a week before class, keep opening quizzes to "two or three questions that all pre-learners can answer," provide detailed orientation on flipped learning, assessment, and worksheets, consider replacing the post-course test with reflective journal writing, and rotate question-asking responsibility among groups during presentations. These respond directly to challenges students and professors reported.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
