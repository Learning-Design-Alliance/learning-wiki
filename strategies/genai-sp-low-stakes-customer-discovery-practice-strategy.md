# Use speech-enabled GenAI standardized patients as a low-stakes practice venue for customer discovery interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends offering students a low-stakes simulated setting in which to practice customer discovery interviews with GenAI standardized patients, since such interviews "can be particularly intimidating for students since they usually have little experience with this skill". Implementation uses scenario-specific agents with structured role instructions, knowledge boundaries, timing rules, and the same rubric-based feedback logic as human SPs, delivered in a supervised simulation environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured scenario instructions, sample questions and answers, and an evaluation rubric shared across modalities; iterative agent review by instructors and SP training staff; supervised setting with technical support
#### Constraints
- Voice service minute limits, account setup friction, and agentic logic tuning (e.g., reducing overly cheerful responses) required iteration; generalizability beyond a single school and pathway is unknown

### Target Learners
- medical students
- I-Corps participants including company founders, students, and senior faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- customer discovery interview technique
- communication confidence and self-efficacy
