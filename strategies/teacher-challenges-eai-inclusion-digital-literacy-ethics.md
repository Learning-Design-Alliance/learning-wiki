# Teachers adopting EAI should address digital literacy and ethical design challenges identified for each AI dimension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies challenges teachers must take on when including AI in education, adapted from Flores-Vivar & García-Peñalvo (2023), across dimensions such as accessibility, intelligent tutoring, virtual facilitators, intelligent content, teacher-AI collaboration, content management and analytics, and learning management automation. It stresses that "the implementation of these technologies in instructional processes involves a great educational and ethical challenge for teachers in relation to their design and implementation". Table 3 maps each dimension to a challenge and educational stage, including digital literacy plans and ethical issues associated with data processing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need digital literacy: knowing how the modalities work, their viability and usefulness in their subject, and how to extrapolate them to their classroom reality.
#### Constraints
- The review states AI requires specific software and the design of materials and technological resources, and notes a high economic cost.

### Target Learners
- teachers at all educational stages implementing EAI

### Target Learning Goals
- ethical and effective design and implementation of AI-based instructional processes
