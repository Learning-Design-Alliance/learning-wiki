# SOLE sessions structure self-organized collaborative inquiry in fixed 60-minute blocks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SOLE (Self Organized Learning Environment) activity divides students into groups of two or three who work together on activities proposed by themselves or the teacher, then present results and draw conclusions with the docente. Each SOLE hour is programmed as: 5 minutes to pose the question or activity, 35 minutes for group development of the topic, 15 minutes for each group's conclusions presentation, and 5 minutes for final conclusions and summary led by the docente.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of two or three students working jointly on activities proposed by themselves or the docente, with each group presenting its results
