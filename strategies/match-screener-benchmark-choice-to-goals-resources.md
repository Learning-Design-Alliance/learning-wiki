# Schools and districts should choose screener benchmarks that match their goals and resources for defining and serving at-risk students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that schools and districts deliberately align benchmark selection with their local definition of risk. It states that 'schools and districts must carefully consider their goals and resources in choosing how to use them to identify and address student needs,' because benchmark scores link to a wide range of the MCAS scale. If the risk cut score is too low, needy students go unserved; if too high, resources are spent unnecessarily.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A local definition of what 'risk' means and what instructional supports and resources are available to respond
#### Constraints
- Data included in the analysis are described by the authors as somewhat limited

### Target Learners
- Students in kindergarten through grade 3 in Massachusetts grantee schools and districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Early literacy and reading proficiency; identification of students at risk of reading difficulty
