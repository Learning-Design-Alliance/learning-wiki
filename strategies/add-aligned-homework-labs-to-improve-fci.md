# Instructors facing low FCI scores in a reformed curriculum may add homework problems and lab activities more aligned with the assessment's scope.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article reports that the authors made small modifications to the M&I curriculum by adding homework problems and lab activities more aligned with the scope of the FCI, and observed small improvements to FCI scores. This strategy responds to assessment results by adjusting course materials rather than abandoning the reform curriculum. The authors note they have not made a systematic study of which modifications are most effective for improving student performance on the FCI.
