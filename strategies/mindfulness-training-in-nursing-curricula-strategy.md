# Integrate mindfulness training programs into nursing curricula to support focus, self-awareness, and emotional regulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends that educators and academic institutions implement mindfulness training programs for nursing students, aimed at improving focus, self-awareness, and emotional regulation to support academic success. The strategy rests on the observed cross-sectional association between mindfulness facets and GPA, with the author noting "mindfulness-based interventions could be integrated into nursing curricula". Tailored strategies addressing observed gender differences are also suggested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mindfulness-based training programs delivered within nursing or health education settings
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and cannot establish causality; the author recommends additional experimental research

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- healthcare students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance (GPA)
- attentional capacity and focus
- emotional regulation and well-being
