# Introduce model discussions and scrutiny of model limits already in beginner physics lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend, based on their research group's further studies, that discussions and reflections about models be addressed already in beginners' physics lessons, giving students the opportunity to question the limits of models as each model is introduced. This operationalises the meta-conceptual-awareness principle from the start of physics instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Models must be introduced with explicit opportunities for students to scrutinise their limits
#### Constraints
- Supported by the authors' own further studies, described only briefly in this article

### Target Learners
- beginner physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- recognising limits of physical models
- distinguishing between alternative models
