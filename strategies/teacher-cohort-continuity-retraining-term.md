# Keep the same subject teacher with a cohort for all three years and use freed third-term time for meaningful certified teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that each student cohort have the same physics (or other subject) teacher in all three years, rather than three different teachers, because teachers prefer teaching a single grade. With classes compressed into the first two terms, the third term gives teachers "a significantly reduced teaching load" for certified, meaningful training. The author argues this yields "a better prepared, more rested and more motivated teacher cohort".

## Design Implications

### Context
