Use broad and specific keywords and develop methodological search filters to improve retrieval of arts-based research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To facilitate information retrieval in a field with no shared vocabulary, the review recommends that ABR researchers "use broad as well as specific key-terms in the indexation of their articles" and combine methodological ABR terms (e.g. photovoice, theatre of the oppressed, participatory video) with types of arts-based research (art in research, art as research, research about art) and research phases. Review authors could also develop and test methodological search filters for sensitivity and specificity, since specific filters to retrieve ABR studies have not yet been developed or tested.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Indexation combining broad ABR terms with specific technique names and research-phase terms

Constraints¶

  • No specific search filters for ABR studies existed yet at the time of the review

Target Learners¶

  • educational and social science researchers using or reviewing arts-based methods

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving retrieval and communication about arts-based research across disciplines
