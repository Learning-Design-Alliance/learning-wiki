Position the language teacher as diagnostician, prescriber, motivator, and evaluator of student production¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article argues that interactive video cannot evaluate students' productions, so teachers should project how their roles can change and become more professional. The instructor is described as the only evaluator capable of judging student production in terms of accuracy, intonation, emphasis, meaning, and appropriateness in context, while the technology provides definitions, cultural information, and simulated conversations tirelessly and positively. Professional language instructors using interactive video in concert with other approaches can look forward to acting as professional diagnostician and prescriber, motivator, and evaluator, directing the total learning environment like a physician relying on technology and assistants.
