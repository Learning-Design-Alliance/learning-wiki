# Role-Playing Emotional Scenarios

## Description
Learners act out how to manage emotions in different scenarios. Writing scenarios on note cards and having learners discuss or act them out helps connect feelings with behaviors. Enhances empathy and problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires scenario prompts and facilitator guidance. Learners need a supportive environment to explore and practice different responses.
#### Constraints
- May not fully simulate real-life challenges. Requires careful facilitation to avoid triggering negative emotions.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Elementary to middle school students, learners with social-emotional skill deficits

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance emotional regulation, improve problem-solving skills, promote empathy

### Affordances
- Provides a safe and structured way to practice emotional responses, enhances problem-solving, and improves social interactions. Connects feelings with behaviors.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by allowing learners to create their own scenarios and solutions. Flexible formats can include group discussions, skits, or written responses.
