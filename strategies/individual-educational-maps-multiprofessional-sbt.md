# Define profession-independent overarching goals and individual educational maps for multi-professional simulation groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For multi-professional target groups with different clinical backgrounds, training levels and experience, the author finds it helpful to define one or two overarching, profession- and specialty-independent educational goals, such as identifying a deteriorating patient or effective and safe team communication. Educators then draw individual educational maps and vignettes for each trainee, detailing level of training and clinical expertise, presenting a step-by-step road map to track assessed learning outcomes and individual improvement.
