# Collect classroom observations at scale via mailed standardized recording equipment, and plan logistics around instructional milestones with intensive tracking and follow-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For video-based observation, the article recommends instructor participants "record their class sessions using standardized recording equipment provided by the research team (i.e., via mail)," ensuring consistent audio and video quality while allowing flexible camera placement. For logistics, it suggests planning multi-semester timelines, structuring timepoints relative to instructional milestones such as the first week of class, using email protocols and tracking spreadsheets, and monitoring response rates with follow-up emails.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video consent procedures must be navigated with participants and institutional IRBs prior to implementation, accounting for FERPA and varying state and institutional recording policies
#### Constraints
- Response rates for multi-institutional work may be lower than for single-site work; out-of-class survey administration may lower response rates or increase external resource use

### Target Learners
- Instructors and students in introductory courses across institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized behavioral characterization of classrooms without researcher travel
