Diagnose reading and writing disorders using a 1.5 SD discrepancy criterion with validated tests and exclusion criteria¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The German S3 guideline (AWMF, 2015), summarized by the review for an interdisciplinary audience of physicians, therapists and pedagogs, recommends diagnosing reading and/or writing impairment when there is "a discrepancy of at least 1.5 SD" between intelligence test outcome and reading and/or writing performance, with domain test performance 1 SD below the arithmetic mean. Impaired vision and peripheral hearing disorder are exclusion criteria. Tests should meet criteria of objectivity, retest-reliability, validity and a representative norming sample of n ≥ 250.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tests meeting objectivity, retest-reliability, validity, description of theoretical principles, and a representative norming sample of n ≥ 250

Constraints¶

  • The review reports that for the German language, no tests were found that fulfill these criteria, and no adequate materials for (young) adults or terminally appropriate tests for underlying disorders exist

Target Learners¶

  • children and adolescents with reading and writing disorders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • accurate diagnosis of reading and writing impairment and underlying deficits
