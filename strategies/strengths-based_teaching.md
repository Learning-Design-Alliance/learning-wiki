# Strengths-Based Teaching

## Description
A teaching approach that focuses on identifying and leveraging learners’ strengths to improve overall learning and engagement. By acknowledging and creating opportunities for learners to let their skills shine, instructors can redefine learners’ perspectives on their education and capabilities. Instead of focusing on deficits, this model harnesses strengths to create more engaged, confident learners. Key components include measuring strengths, noticing and acknowledging strengths daily, creating opportunities for success, providing options for demonstrating knowledge, and teaching collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strengths assessment tools, opportunities for learners to demonstrate their talents, a positive and supportive classroom environment
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset and a willingness to focus on learners’ strengths rather than deficits. Can be challenging to implement in a classroom with diverse abilities.

### Affordances
- Allows learners to redefine themselves and their education in terms of their strengths. Increases engagement and creates a more independent, self-advocating learner. Fosters a positive atmosphere and promotes collaboration.

### Personalization
- Tailor activities and assessments to allow learners to showcase their unique skills and talents. Provide options for demonstrating knowledge to accommodate different learning styles.

## Assessment Evidence
- Increased learner engagement, improved academic performance, greater self-confidence, positive feedback from learners

## Impact
- Learners taught in a strengths-based model earned higher GPAs and were absent from school less often. Helps learners develop the confidence to be learners and the courage to overcome weaknesses.
