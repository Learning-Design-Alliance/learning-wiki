# Schools should run time-management and study-skills workshops, provide academic counseling, and teach coping strategies to manage academic stress

## Description
The article's recommendation section proposes concrete school-level actions based directly on its findings: "Secondary schools should organize and implement workshops that focus on time management and study skills" and "programs that are organized to teach students effective coping strategies should be developed to help manage academic stress, pressure, and anxiety." Teachers should also provide constructive and timely feedback on academic performance to help students understand strengths and areas for improvement.
