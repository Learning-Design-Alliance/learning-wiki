# Future IDSE should deliberately focus on relationship building and communication openness alongside clinical content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on intern feedback, the authors recommend that interdisciplinary simulation-based education be made deliberate and explicit about its interpersonal curriculum rather than relying on hidden effects. Interns "emphasized the interdisciplinary simulation-based education needed to be deliberate and focused", and the conclusion urges future IDSE to integrate "experiences that promote the development of skills in relationship building and communication openness while imparting basic clinical and procedural content".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An interactive forum allowing discussion of multiple perspectives
- A safe learning environment with passionate educators who model interdisciplinary collaboration
#### Constraints
- The authors note other educational modalities may have contributed or yielded similar results, and future research should address staff training level and workforce-representative curricula

### Target Learners
- medical interns and residents across disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- relationship building, communication openness, and reduced interdisciplinary bias

## Claims

- [Qualitative analysis of IDSE intern focus groups identified four themes: relationship building, communication openness, attitudinal shifts, and enhanced learner experience](../claims/idse-qualitative-four-themes.md) [+W]
- [IDSE-exposed interns showed statistically significant pre-post improvements in five of seven interdisciplinary attitude areas on a 10-point Likert survey](../claims/idse-improves-five-of-seven-intern-attitude-areas.md) [+W]
- [Between-cohort comparisons showed IDSE interns improved significantly more than non-IDSE interns in views of other disciplines (p = 0.02) and comfort learning with other disciplines (p = 0.04)](../claims/idse-vs-non-idse-change-differences-two-items.md) [+W]
- [Non-IDSE interns exposed only to within-discipline simulation showed no statistically significant change in interdisciplinary attitude ratings](../claims/non-idse-cohort-no-attitude-change.md) [+W]
- [A one-way ANCOVA controlling for pre-test scores found a significant difference in composite post-intervention viewpoint scores between IDSE and non-IDSE cohorts (p = 0.0018)](../claims/ancova-composite-post-score-difference-idse.md) [+W]
