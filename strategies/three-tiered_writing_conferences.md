# Three-Tiered Writing Conferences

## Description
The Three-Tiered Writing Conference approach provides a structured format for brief, effective writing conferences between teachers and students. It involves three distinct layers of feedback: first, a personal response to the writing to connect emotionally; second, feedback as a member of a writing community offering suggestions; and third, a teacherly response providing direct instruction or strategies. The conference concludes with the student reflecting on the feedback and planning next steps, reinforcing their role as an author. This method ensures conferences are predictable, brief, and useful by disciplining the feedback to focus on one aspect in each tier.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the three-tier structure, preparation with prompts and questions, time management (5-7 minutes per student), active listening and documentation skills
#### Constraints
- Requires discipline to maintain structure, may feel unnatural initially, students need to be prepared for the conference format
