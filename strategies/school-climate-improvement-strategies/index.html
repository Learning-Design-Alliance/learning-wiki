School-level strategies for improving school climate¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report names commonly employed school-level strategies for improving climate: "schoolwide prevention approaches that involve all staff in the installation of a behavior management system," and "student social and emotional learning approaches that rely on classroom social-skills instruction." It gives two examples: restorative justice programs, which focus on relationships between misbehavior perpetrators and the school community rather than punitive discipline, and transformative classroom management that leverages student motivation and engagement to increase adherence to behavioral norms.
