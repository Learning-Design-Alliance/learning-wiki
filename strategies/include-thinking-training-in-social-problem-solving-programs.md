# Include Thinking Training activities in programs supporting young children's social problem-solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that training programs developed to support 5-6-year-old children's social problem-solving skills "should include Thinking Training activities within their contents." They further propose developing qualified Thinking Training-oriented education programs and a Thinking Training activity pool with easily reached documents such as booklets and web content for teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Before Thinking Training application, teachers should receive adequate training via a scheduled training process, which the authors say will significantly affect the quality of education given to children
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is grounded in a single experimental study with 70 children in one district of Ankara

### Target Learners
- 5-6-year-old preschool children

### Target Learning Goals
- social problem-solving skills
- thinking skills
