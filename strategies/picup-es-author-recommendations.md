# Five recommendations for authors submitting computational Exercise Sets to the PICUP Collection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article closes with five recommendations for Exercise Set authors: replicate current successes at the High School and Advanced Course Levels; expand the representation of programming platforms, since PICUP accepts additional code templates and completed codes after publication; develop Exercise Sets around more context-rich, analytically intractable problems such as molecular dynamics and time-dependent quantum mechanics; develop Exercise Sets integrating Course Contexts typically isolated from each other; and include a greater variety of computational methods and engagement elements, especially those students are likely to encounter in graduate education and industry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors submit through the PICUP peer-review process following the required Exercise Set template
#### Constraints
- The recommendations target gaps identified in the 62 Exercise Sets analyzed at the time of writing; some gaps (e.g., Condensed Matter Physics) were being closed as the article was finalized

### Target Learners
- high school through graduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- computational physics skills
- connecting physics subjects through computation
