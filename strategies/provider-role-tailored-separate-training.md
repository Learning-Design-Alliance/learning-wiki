# Provide separate training programmes tailored to different provider groups to improve refresher attendance and performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Observing substantial attrition in refresher attendance and differential results by provider role in other multidisciplinary programmes, the article recommends tailoring training to provider groups. It suggests that providing separate training programmes tailored to the needs of different provider groups may improve attendance by increasing relevance of content and impact performance on competency assessments. This addresses the interdisciplinary nature of the programme, which the authors note may have made it less desirable for some providers given variable learning expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content tailored to each provider group's learning expectations and role-specific needs
#### Constraints
- Offered as a suggestion informed by other programmes' findings; not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- mixed provider groups (nurses, physicians, technicians, therapists) in refresher training

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained attendance and competency in new clinical technology use
