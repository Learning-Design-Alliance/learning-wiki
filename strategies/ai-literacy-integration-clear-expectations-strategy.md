# Integrate AI literacy into curricula with clear expectations to guide ethical engagement across the usage continuum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A concrete instructional strategy the authors recommend: embed AI literacy instruction in the curriculum and set explicit expectations for generative AI use, so that students across the adoption continuum are served appropriately. The article states educational practices 'should promote responsible, structured use—raising awareness among non-users and guiding frequent users toward ethical engagement'. This pairs the literacy content with differentiated guidance for low-usage and high-usage students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula with room for AI literacy content
- Clearly articulated institutional or course-level expectations for AI use
#### Constraints
- The authors note socio-economic factors like parental pressure and study expenses point to broader contextual influences, underscoring the need for holistic, differentiated strategies

### Target Learners
- University students, from non-users to almost-always users of ChatGPT

### Target Learning Goals
- Ethical and structured use of generative AI
- Awareness of AI tools among non-users
- Long-term academic independence
