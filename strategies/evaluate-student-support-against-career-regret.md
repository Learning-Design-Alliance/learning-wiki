# Universities should evaluate psychological support, academic advising, time-management resources, mentoring and realistic information about medical training demands

## Description
The article's implication section recommends that medical schools respond to career regret by reviewing the support infrastructure available to students. It states: "Universities may wish to evaluate access to psychological support, academic advising, time-management resources, mentoring systems and realistic information about the demands and duration of medical training." This follows from the observed associations of regret with mental HRQoL, GPA, study habits and reported psychological pressure. The recommendation is framed as an evaluation priority, not a proven remedy.

## Design Implications

### Context
