# Prepare teachers with one full training day plus a mid-year follow-up day

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Teachers are implementation ready after 1 full day of training; in the study they received a second full follow-up day around mid-year, "mostly used for sharing ideas and to overcome challenges." This light-touch professional development model supports rapid, low-cost implementation of the program.
