# Report sensitivity-analysis results using three guideline options depending on whether MAR and MNAR models agree

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article synthesises guidelines for reporting results from missing-data sensitivity analysis into three options. When MAR and MNAR models disagree, present both and "Cautiously choose; Present findings cautiously"; when they agree, report MAR models in detail and add that MNAR models do not contradict them; when ML disagrees with MI, MLaux, MIaux and MNAR, opt for the latter results. Contradictory results decrease confidence in the MAR model, so both model families should be shown with the most plausible assumptions clarified.

#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article notes MNAR models may produce wrong results when their assumptions are violated, and data cannot indicate whether MAR or MNAR holds
