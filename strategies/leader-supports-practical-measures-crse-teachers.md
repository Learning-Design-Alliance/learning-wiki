# Leader supports for teachers using practical measures to advance CRSE

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief specifies supports school and district leaders should provide: advocating for and modeling "flexibility in policies related to assessment," targeted professional development and guidance on using practical measures aligned with CRSE principles, training on designing culturally relevant assessment tools and interpreting data through a CRSE lens, access to culturally diverse assessment materials, celebration of successful case studies, collaboration opportunities for refining measurement strategies, schedule adjustments for data collection, and time and structures for building family relationships and feedback systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrative support including schedule adjustments so teachers can integrate measurement into instructional practice
#### Constraints
- The brief frames these supports as necessary because teachers will need help connecting broader CRSE outcomes with achievement and content standards

### Target Learners
- mathematics teachers, supported by school and district leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- effective use of practical measurement for CRSE
- culturally responsive assessment design
- family and community partnership in mathematics education
