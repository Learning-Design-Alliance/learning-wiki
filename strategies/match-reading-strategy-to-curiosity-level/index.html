Match reading strategy to student curiosity level: INSERT for high-curiosity, SQ3R for low-curiosity learners¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the significant strategy × curiosity interaction and the Tukey test, the article supports tailoring reading-strategy instruction to students' curiosity level. The Tukey test "described and showed that sudents’ lower curiosity is matching to the use of SQ3R and students’ higher curiosity is also matching to the use of INSERT strategy in teaching reading comprehension." Teachers can therefore assign INSERT to highly curious students and SQ3R to less curious students when teaching reading comprehension.

Requirements¶

  • Knowledge of students' curiosity level, e.g. via a curiosity questionnaire as used in the study

Constraints¶

  • Recommendation derives from a single quasi-experiment in one Indonesian private senior high school

Target Learners¶

  • Senior high school EFL students with high or low curiosity

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension achievement

Claims¶

  • Teaching strategy and curiosity interact: INSERT suits high-curiosity students while SQ3R suits low-curiosity students [+W]
  • Grade XI EFL students taught reading comprehension with the INSERT strategy achieved higher scores than those taught with SQ3R [+W]
