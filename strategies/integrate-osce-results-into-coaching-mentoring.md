# Integrate OSCE results into coaching or mentoring programs to target coaching and enable early identification of struggling interns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose using OSCE results beyond the stand-alone intervention: "integrating OSCE results into a coaching or mentoring program will help target coaching on specific clinical skills and improve performance in the long term." They expect this process may identify interns struggling with core clinical skills earlier, allowing early coaching intervention, and plan to build in time for self-reflection and goal setting before coach discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A coaching or mentoring program into which OSCE results can be integrated
- Time built in for intern self-reflection and goal setting
#### Constraints
- The authors state this is a hope for future studies; long-term performance improvement has not yet been demonstrated

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine interns early in training

### Target Learning Goals
- Targeted improvement of core clinical skills through early coaching intervention
