# Cross-disciplinary communication among QIST instructors to decide appropriate content and terminology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy, drawn from educator responses to how to build a common language, directs QIST instructors to deliberate with colleagues in other disciplines about course content and terminology. The article reports that educators "stressed the importance of communicating with educators from other disciplines" such as physics educators talking with engineering, computer science, and chemistry educators, to decide what topics are appropriate and what can be left out of a particular interdisciplinary QIST course. It supports building courses that do not disadvantage non-physics students.
