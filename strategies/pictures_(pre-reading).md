# Pictures (Pre-Reading)

## Description
The instructor selects three or four pictures related to the reading topic and provides them to small groups of learners. The groups work together to connect the pictures and guess what the reading will be about. Each group then presents their ideas to the class. This visual-based activity stimulates curiosity and encourages predictive thinking before reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires carefully selected pictures related to the reading topic, copies for each group, and facilitation for group presentations.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the pictures. Some learners may struggle with visual interpretation.
