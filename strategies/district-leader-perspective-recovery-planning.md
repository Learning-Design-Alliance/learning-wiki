# Include a district leader's perspective on supporting returning students in recovery planning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The webinar deliberately pairs research and policy with ground-level district practice. Among what panelists share is "the perspective of a district leader and what their district is doing to support students as they return to school this coming fall," grounding recovery recommendations in a specific district's actions (Middletown Public Schools).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district leader able to describe concrete local supports for returning students
#### Constraints
- Illustrated by a single district's context (Middletown Public Schools)

### Target Learners
- Students returning to classrooms after pandemic disruptions

### Target Learning Goals
- Supporting student transitions back to school
- District recovery planning
