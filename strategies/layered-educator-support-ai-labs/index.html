Layered educator support: coaching, curated resources, low-lift entry points, and community engagement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends building layered systems of support for teachers at every comfort level when introducing powerful technology. It advises: 'Provide scaffolding for teachers at every level of comfort and experience. Offer "low-lift" entry points, like turnkey project ideas and templates, alongside deeper professional learning opportunities.' Concrete mechanisms include one-on-one coaching, resource curation, conference engagement, newsletters, open-lab hours, and co-teaching, modeled on Digital Promise's supports at YCHS and proposed practices at Anaheim.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sustained investment of human resources alongside technology; ongoing professional learning tied to curricular goals

Constraints¶

  • Teacher engagement may lag student use; the report notes many teachers are unsure how to begin and need more direction

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 educators and instructional coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Confidence and fluency with AI tools and lab technologies
  • classroom integration of AI

Related Strategies¶

  • Curate and connect open education resources for making, re-formatted and linked to standards, to meet champions' demand for student project guides
  • District actions for sustained, equitable impact with powerful technology
  • Accommodate Varying Technology Experience
  • Multi-layer coach training and support system: coach camp, paired mentoring, ongoing webinars, and coach-the-coach sessions
  • Scaffold adults from beginning users to confident digital citizens with hands-on, competency-based classes and layered teacher support
  • Involve teachers as co-designers of AI-based intelligent systems, with agency to train, correct, interpret, and override system recommendations
  • Invest in dedicated instructional technology leadership roles and role-specific professional learning for AI literacy
  • Provide sustained, powerful professional development with professional learning communities for QTEL implementation
