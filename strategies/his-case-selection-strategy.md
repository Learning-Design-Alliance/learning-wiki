# Selecting and preparing educational cases from the Hospital Information System for case-based teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a stepwise procedure for instructors to select teaching cases within the HIS: clarify teaching objectives from the syllabus, apply selection criteria (representativeness, completeness, pedagogical value, diversity), search and screen cases using HIS keywords and filters, evaluate content completeness and privacy protection, and regularly update cases with feedback. Instructors log into the HIS with authorized accounts and use "the filtering function of the HIS system can be used to screen by date, diagnostic results, examination types, etc." Students must comply with data protection and privacy regulations under instructor supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases must comply with privacy protection requirements, removing or anonymizing patients' personal information; students access patient data under strict instructor supervision
#### Constraints
- Applicable where a Hospital Information System with searchable medical records and imaging is available

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical imaging interns

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- image reading ability
- integrating clinical, pathological and imaging data
