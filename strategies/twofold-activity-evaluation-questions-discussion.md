# Evaluate learning twofold: check activity results and question students in discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An assessment strategy conducted "twofold: by checking the results of the activities and from the answers of the students and their discussions." The teacher asks targeted questions — to what direction the Sun attains its highest elevation, how the noon elevation changes with latitude, why shadow length indicates latitude — and develops with the class a sketch of a stick producing a shadow, then asks about the trigonometric functions involved, and asks students where the Vikings originated from using a map of Europe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher checks the results of the hands-on activities and leads question-and-discussion sessions with the students.

### Target Learners
- students aged 12-16 in middle or secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- explain how cardinal directions derive from the Sun's highest elevation
- explain why shadow length at noon indicates latitude
