# Publish an alternative note explaining why data or materials cannot be shared when confidentiality prevents it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Where open sharing is impossible, the disclosure form offers a documented alternative: authors may check a box to include an alternative note published in the article, explaining the circumstances and linking any data or materials made available even if not badge-eligible. The form notes that "there are cases in which sharing participants’ data could violate confidentiality".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors must check the alternative-note box on the form and provide the text of the note for publication.
#### Constraints
- Publicly posted data must not contain information that would allow individuals to be identified without consent.

### Target Learners
- Researchers working with human participant data

### Target Learning Goals
- Balancing research transparency with participant confidentiality
