# Evaluate CBCL implementations with objective outcomes, longitudinal follow-up, and controlled designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future CBCL studies move beyond subjective perceptions: "Future studies using objective educational outcomes, longitudinal follow-up, and controlled or multicenter designs are needed to further investigate the educational impact and reproducibility of this approach." Its own evaluation plan had three levels — knowledge acquisition, immediate learner feedback, and later behavioral change — but only the feedback level was completed locally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Planned pre- and post-session MCQ knowledge assessment
- A feedback survey immediately after the session
- A later audit of practice change (e.g., chart audit six months after)
#### Constraints
- In this experience, the post-session MCQ and the planned chart audit were not performed due to operational and feasibility constraints

### Target Learners
- Radiology residents
- Residency program educators and researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring educational effectiveness of CBCL
- Assessing behavioral change in clinical practice
