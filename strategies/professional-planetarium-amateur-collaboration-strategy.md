# Strengthen collaboration between professional astronomers and planetaria, science centres and amateur astronomers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "the bonds of professional astronomers with planetarium and amateur astronomy community need to be strengthened" through joint development of exhibits, planetarium shows, telescope-making workshops, topical lectures and lecture courses. Concrete mechanisms include involving planetarium professionals in annual ASI meetings, building frameworks for institutions to share recent research with the planetarium community, facilitating regular conferences, and supporting a society for planetaria. It also urges research institutes with large datasets to co-produce show content using VR, AR, sonification and haptic models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Planned avenues for planetaria and science centre professionals to engage with the pan-India professional astronomy community
- A structured framework by which institutions regularly share recent research with the planetarium community
#### Constraints
- India has about 67 planetaria and about 50 NCSM science museums; commercially available shows mostly lack Indian context, and mobile planetaria are not economically viable for most stakeholders

### Target Learners
- general public
- planetarium and science centre audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- public astronomy literacy
- updating planetarium staff knowledge of current research
