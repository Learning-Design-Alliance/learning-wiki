# Four pillars for educational transformation: industry-independent accreditation, integrated curricula, objective competency assessment, and competency-based certification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on PBP principles, the statement proposes four implementation pillars: "Industry-Independent Accreditation", integrated theoretical and practical curricula blending online and in-person delivery with progression through simulation modalities, objective competency assessment based on validated metrics rather than time or case numbers, and competency-based certification independent from industry. The pillars are intended to ensure reproducible, standardized learning experiences regardless of location and to align with the 2024 EHRA core curriculum and ESC Roadmap for cardiovascular education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certification of training centres and mentors based on objective criteria: procedural volumes, case diversity, simulation resources, supervision ratios, and faculty pedagogical training
- Protected educational time so digital tools become structured training components rather than optional add-ons
#### Constraints
- Device-specific simulators inherently require manufacturer involvement for development and maintenance, while generic procedural simulators can be developed with greater academic independence

### Target Learners
- EP and CIED trainees
- training centre faculty and mentors

### Target Learning Goals
- standardized competency-based procedural training
- objective assessment and certification of procedural skills
