# Use triangle visualisations of equality (orthocentre-barycentre distance k) to help teachers prioritise and shape CPS interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes drawing triangles whose vertex heights are the member students' mean activity scores, so that a more equilateral triangle indicates more equal participation. The distance k between orthocentre and barycentre quantifies inequality (k=0.13 for high CPS Univ. D vs k=0.3 for low CPS Univ. K). Provided in real time, such visualisations let teachers "prioritise their interventions to these groups that have the highest k values" and shape scaffolding, e.g. stimulating a disengaged student.
