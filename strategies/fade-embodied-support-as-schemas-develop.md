# Fade embodied support as schemas develop, starting with the least embodied task-relevant support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable instructional recipe derived from the article's reformulation: begin with the least embodied, task-relevant support a learner needs, then progressively withdraw it. The article instructs designers to "use the least embodied, task-relevant support needed for the learner's current knowledge and the task's element interactivity, then fade that support as schemas develop", so that support tracks growing schema development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assess the learner's current knowledge and the task's element interactivity before selecting the level of embodied support
#### Constraints
- Formulated for embodied instruction in STEM

### Target Learners
- STEM novices early in expertise development

### Target Learning Goals
- Schema construction and flexible use of formal representations
