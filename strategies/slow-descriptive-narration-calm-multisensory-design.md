# Design multi-sensory shows with slow-paced, highly descriptive narration, advance warning of what comes next, and a calm low sensory load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's design lessons for accessible shows: complete the soundtrack first so it is understandable without visuals; use "slow-paced and extremely descriptive narration (but avoiding too much reliance on visual metaphors)"; give advance warning of what is coming next; and keep the production calm and low in sensory load. The calm, multi-sensory, clearly narrated approach is credited with benefiting not only BVI audiences but also wider SEND audiences with sensory needs and learning difficulties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with focus groups including BVI community members, teachers of BVI pupils, and BVI pupils themselves during design
#### Constraints
- Some audiences reported difficulty audibly distinguishing different planet sounds heard together, and sound balance issues were venue-dependent

### Target Learners
- BVI audiences
- SEND audiences including those with sensory needs and learning difficulties
- general family audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- astronomy content comprehension through sound
- inclusive access to science communication

## Claims

- [Audience members rate the sonification-based planetarium show very highly overall, with 91% giving a score of 4 or 5 out of 5](../claims/audio-universe-high-overall-audience-ratings.md) [+W]
- [BVI and non-BVI audiences responded similarly on most questions, with BVI audiences marginally more likely to rate the sounds 'very useful' (73% vs 61%)](../claims/bvi-non-bvi-similar-with-marginal-usefulness-difference.md) [+W]
- [Children were more likely than adults to become more convinced that astronomy is accessible for BVI people after the show (81% vs 71%)](../claims/children-more-convinced-than-adults.md) [+W]
- [Despite positive feedback, the show was incorporated into regular planetarium programming by only two known planetariums, limiting fully inclusive use](../claims/limited-regular-programming-uptake.md) [-W]
- [Watching the show shifted audience perceptions of accessibility: 74% were more convinced astronomy is accessible to BVI people and 82% wanted to find out more about science](../claims/show-shifted-accessibility-perceptions.md) [+W]
- [Both BVI and non-BVI audiences found the show's sound representations useful and enjoyable, with roughly 90% scoring 4 or 5](../claims/sounds-useful-enjoyable-bvi-non-bvi.md) [+W]
