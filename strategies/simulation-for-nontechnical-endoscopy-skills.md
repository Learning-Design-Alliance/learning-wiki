# Use high-fidelity simulation with standardized patients to prime non-technical endoscopy skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review identifies communication and teamwork, judgement and decision making, leadership, and situational awareness as the non-technical components of endoscopy, which are difficult to teach with partially sedated or awake patients. It proposes that "using high fidelity simulation with standardized patients and actors playing the role of nursing staff" can prime trainees to these non-technical skills, extending simulation beyond routine technical procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized patients and actors to simulate integration scenarios
#### Constraints
- Current SBT is limited to polypectomies and other routine procedures

### Target Learners
- novice endoscopy trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- communication and teamwork
- judgement and decision making
- leadership
- situational awareness
