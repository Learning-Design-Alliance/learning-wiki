# Braid state, federal, and grant funds through a needs-based planning approach to address multiple educator workforce priority areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's core strategy is fiscal braiding: education leaders 'coordinate, or braid, state funds, federal funds, and specialized grants' so districts can 'invest in strategies that address multiple priority areas.' Braiding starts with a needs-based planning approach (Figure 1): identify student and staff needs to determine priority areas, select a combination of strategies addressing multiple areas, then coordinate fiscal resources to fund implementation. Leaders are told to check how local priority areas overlap and align with each funding source's intent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Start planning from identified local student and staff needs rather than from available funds
- Select strategies that address multiple priority areas simultaneously
- Align uses with the intent of each funding source and with local data on areas of need
#### Constraints
- The examples offered are explicitly 'not an exhaustive list but are offered as a guide'

### Target Learners
- Educators and district staff in California local education agencies

### Target Learning Goals
- Building an effective, diverse educator workforce that fosters an equitable, high-quality learning environment
