# Implementation tips for ICM instruction: preparation materials, incentives, assessment, structure, no repetition, learning groups, iterative feedback and easy-access technology

## Description
The article closes with practical tips for implementing ICM instruction, based on publications of the Vanderbilt Center for Teaching, Bram and the University of Southern California. Instructors should provide the opportunity to acquire factual knowledge beforehand, create "incentive systems ... to stimulate students to address the contents before the classroom phase" such as assessed quizzes or forum discussions, implement assessment instruments for feedback, structure both phases firmly, avoid repeating online content, support learning groups, give feedback iteratively, and use technology that is easy-access and ideally already familiar to users.
