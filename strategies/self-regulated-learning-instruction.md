# Self Regulated Learning Instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 13 studies (7 quant-synthesis, 3 causal, 2 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q4` · 6 of 13 report an effect size

## Description
Self Regulated Learning (SRL) instruction explicitly teaches learners the strategies and self-management processes — goal setting, strategy selection, self-monitoring, self-evaluation, and adaptive adjustment — that effective learners use spontaneously. Rather than assuming students arrive with these skills, the instructor models, scaffolds, and gradually transfers control of the learning process to the student. SRL instruction is typically organized around cyclical phases (forethought, performance, self-reflection) drawn from [Self-Regulated Learning](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md) theory.

## Design Implications

SRL instruction works best when strategy teaching is embedded in authentic subject-matter tasks rather than delivered as a separate study-skills course; decontextualized training shows weak and non-durable effects [SRL strategy training embedded in content improves achievement more than generic study-skills instruction.](../claims/assessment-for-learning-improves-achievement.md) [+S]. Meta-analyses consistently find that explicit, extended, theory-informed SRL training produces meaningful achievement gains, with the largest effects when learners also receive feedback on their strategy use [Self-regulation strategy training improves academic achievement across domains and grade levels.](../claims/assessment-for-learning-improves-achievement.md) [+S]. Because SRL instruction shifts control to learners, it also depends on and develops [Autonomy](../principles/autonomy.md) — learners need genuine decision space to practice regulation [Autonomy supports intrinsic motivation and engagement.](../claims/autonomy-supports-intrinsic-motivation.md) [+S].

### Instructions
1. **Set goals with learners:** have students translate task requirements into personal, specific goals ([Advance Organizers](../elements/advance-organizers.md) and criteria rubrics support this step).
2. **Model regulation in context:** use [Think-Aloud](../elements/think-aloud.md) to demonstrate planning ("What do I already know?"), monitoring ("Does this make sense?"), and revision decisions while performing a real task.
3. **Guide strategy practice:** provide structured prompts or templates ([Checklists](checklists.md), planning sheets) during [Practice](../elements/practice.md) on subject-matter tasks.
4. **Prompt self-monitoring at pauses:** insert brief check-ins ([Check-In](../elements/check-in.md)) at natural task boundaries rather than during execution.
5. **Support self-evaluation against standards:** have learners compare their work to criteria and expert examples, with [Feedback](../elements/feedback.md) calibrating their judgments.
6. **Fade external scaffolds:** progressively remove templates and prompts as learners demonstrate independent regulation, transferring control to the student.

## Description
Self Regulated Learning (SRL) is the process by which learners take active control of their own learning: setting goals, selecting and adapting strategies, monitoring comprehension, and adjusting effort and approach based on feedback. As an instructional strategy, it means explicitly teaching these planning, monitoring, and evaluation processes — not merely expecting them — so learners internalize a cycle of forethought, performance, and self-reflection [Zimmerman's cyclical model](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md).

## Design Implications

SRL instruction works because learners who monitor their understanding and adapt their strategies allocate effort more effectively than those who simply study harder; strategy training alone is insufficient without instruction in when and why to deploy strategies [+S]. Effective programs embed SRL prompts and modeling within subject-matter instruction rather than teaching study skills in isolation Dignath & Büttner meta-analysis of classroom interventions. Because SRL depends on learner autonomy, overly controlling instructional environments suppress the very behaviors the strategy aims to build [Autonomy supports intrinsic motivation.](../claims/autonomy-supports-intrinsic-motivation.md) [+S].

4. Give learners access to accurate performance data so self-evaluation can calibrate against reality ([Assessment](../elements/assessment.md), [Check-In](../elements/check-in.md)).
