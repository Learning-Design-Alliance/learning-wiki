# Run multi-cycle faculty development workshops on student assessment using active teaching–learning strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's FD project offered 11 face-to-face workshop cycles of about 20 hours each: an initial Centralized Workshop for 27 local leaders with foreign and national expert faculty, followed by ten Decentralized Workshops delivered locally. The programme covered key assessment topics — programmatic assessment systems, quality criteria, curriculum–assessment alignment, clinical skills assessment, formative assessment and feedback, portfolios and mentoring — using "selected text readings, interactive short lectures, discussions in small groups, individual and group exercises, practical exercises with role-playing and plenary discussions". Local leaders consulted on programme fit before delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty development centre or team to design the programme and facilitate
- Local leaders to recruit participants and adapt emphasis to institutional needs
- Funding and time allotted for faculty participation
#### Constraints
- Participants criticized the scarcity of time and the conceptual and practical complexity of programmatic assessment; perception was lower for infrastructure and the virtual learning environment

### Target Learners
- Health professions teachers and preceptors
- Course managers and coordinators

### Target Learning Goals
- Student assessment knowledge and skills
- Programmatic assessment implementation capability
