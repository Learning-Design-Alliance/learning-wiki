# Tailored osteoporosis education by degree track plus interdisciplinary exchange and paired public-health outreach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends customizing osteoporosis education to each postgraduate track: timely research updates for clinically busy professional students and increased clinical exposure for academic students. It states that "fostering interdisciplinary communication between professional- and academic-degree trainees may serve as an effective strategy for mutual knowledge enhancement" and proposes paired outreach programs that consolidate knowledge while serving community health.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to differentiate content by degree track and provide clinical practice opportunities, especially for academic-degree students
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of a single-institution survey with a limited sample size; the authors note the study did not stratify participants by graduate year or major

### Target Learners
- medical postgraduate students (professional-degree and academic-degree) in orthopedics

### Target Learning Goals
- osteoporosis knowledge, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention practices
