# Offer multidisciplinary degree programmes to emphasize the resources of conscientious and open students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that university institutions emphasize the resources of highly motivated, hardworking (conscientious) and intellectually curious, creative (open to experience) students "by offering them degree programmes that embrace different academic disciplines." It notes that top universities such as Harvard, MIT and Oxford have introduced multidisciplinary courses integrating Humanities with Science and Engineering.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
