# Provide summer reading and writing opportunities to maintain school-year gains for at-risk students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors note that "in order to help maintain the progress at-risk students made during the school year, opportunities need to be provided for them to read and write during the summer months". They cite research showing students in primary and elementary grades lose much of their reading ability when they do not read during summer, and that students from lower socio-economic backgrounds suffer greater summer reading loss.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured opportunities for students to read and write during summer months when school is not in session
#### Constraints
- Summer reading loss disproportionately affects students from lower socio-economic backgrounds and those with poorly developed language skills

### Target Learners
- At-risk early grade readers from lower socio-economic backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining reading proficiency gains made during the school year
