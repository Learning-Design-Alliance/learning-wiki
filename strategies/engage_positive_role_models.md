# Engage Positive Role Models

## Description
Involving families and community members in classroom and school activities. Integrating them into curriculum activities, assemblies, committees, after-school events, and homework support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruitment and coordination of family and community volunteers, clear communication of expectations and roles, opportunities for meaningful involvement.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort to coordinate, some families and community members may not be able to participate due to time constraints or other factors.
