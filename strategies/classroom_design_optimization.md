# Classroom Design Optimization

## Description
Optimizing classroom design (noise levels, temperature, seating arrangement) can minimize distractions and improve learning. Strategies include building schools that buffer external noise, maintaining a comfortable temperature range (68–74°F), and using flexible seating arrangements matched to the task at hand. Factors that can interfere with learning are noise, temperature, and seating arrangement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Noise-buffering materials, thermostat control, and flexible seating options. Requires awareness of noise levels, temperature, and seating arrangement effects on learning.
#### Constraints
- Difficulty in controlling noise levels in some environments. Requires investment in materials and equipment.
