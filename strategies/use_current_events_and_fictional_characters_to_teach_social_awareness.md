# Use current events and fictional characters to teach social awareness

## Description
Utilize real-world events and fictional narratives to explore social dynamics, ethical dilemmas, and diverse perspectives. Analyze characters' actions, motivations, and relationships to promote critical thinking and empathy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to current events and fictional narratives. Requires instructors to be skilled facilitators who can guide discussions and help learners analyze characters' actions and motivations.
#### Constraints
- Current events can be controversial and emotionally charged. Instructors must be prepared to manage discussions and provide support to learners who are struggling.

### Target Learners

- All learner levels, particularly those who need to develop social awareness and critical thinking skills, K-12, higher education, and adult learners in professional development

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance social awareness, develop critical thinking skills, promote empathy and perspective-taking, foster understanding of social dynamics

### Affordances
- Provides a relatable context for learning about social awareness. Encourages critical thinking, empathy, and perspective-taking. Helps learners develop problem-solving and decision-making skills.

### Personalization
- Select current events and fictional narratives that are relevant to learners' interests and experiences. Topics can include social justice issues, ethical dilemmas, and interpersonal conflicts.
