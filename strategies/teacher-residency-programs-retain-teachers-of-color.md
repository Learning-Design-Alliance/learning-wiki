# Support teacher residency programs, which are associated with higher retention rates for teachers of color

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief identifies teacher residency programs as a retention investment: "Colorado, Illinois, Louisiana, Mississippi, Missouri, and Texas used funds to support teacher residency programs, which are associated with higher rates of retention for teachers of color." Residencies also appear as an allowable use under the TQP program and were funded via SEED grants.
