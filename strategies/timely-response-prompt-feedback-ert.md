# Ensure timely responses to student inquiries and prompt feedback on assignments during ERT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article identifies ensuring a timely response to student inquiries and questions, as well as feedback to student assignments, as a critical consideration for implementing pedagogy of care in ERT. Design work should be informed by "continuous dialog, clarity of communication, and promptness" (Velasquez et al. 2013). This supports building a climate of care online during crisis teaching.
