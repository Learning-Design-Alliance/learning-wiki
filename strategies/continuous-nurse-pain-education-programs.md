# Provide continuous pain management education programs for nurses regardless of experience level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals emphasize ongoing educational programs on pain assessment and management for nursing practice, because surveyed nurses showed poor knowledge despite nearly universal availability of pain assessment tools and protocols. The conclusion states "it is imperative that regardless of the level, nurses’ continuous education program might assist in proving the skills and knowledge of pain assessment and management." The recommendation is grounded in the finding that half the nurses had attended no pain-related workshops in the past two years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational programs should serve nursing practice and be continuous rather than one-off, per the article's conclusion
#### Constraints
- The article notes its small sample size and single setting hinder generalizability and mandate further multicenter studies
