# Implement guided reflective diary writing with weekly entries and individualized teacher feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This is the article's own implementable intervention recipe: students write one guided reflective diary entry per week for 12 weeks during a semester, prompted to record new knowledge, doubts, self-evaluation of academic advancement, emotion-inducing events, subjective emotions, and insights. Adherence mechanisms include "Personalized Feedback" on each entry, regular online Q&A sessions, and a structured biweekly collection protocol run by course representatives. The article recommends educators "integrate reflective journal writing into regular teaching practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One diary entry per week sustained over a 12-week period
- A face-to-face training session explaining the purpose and procedure of reflective writing
- Individualized written teacher feedback on each diary entry, with a structured collection protocol run every two weeks
#### Constraints
- The article notes potential risks of secondary emotional distress arising during diary writing were not systematically monitored and recommends ethical safeguards such as real-time emotional monitoring and psychological support
- Supporting evidence comes from a single-institution quasi-experimental study without a control group

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning engagement
- academic emotion regulation
- reflective integration of learning experiences
