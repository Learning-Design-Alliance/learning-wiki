# Reflection After Writing

## Description
Reflection involves pausing after completing a writing assignment to consider how the assignment went. Students are encouraged to identify roadblocks, discuss which strategies worked, and brainstorm changes for the future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing assignment; facilitator; reflection questions or prompts
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for some students to reflect; requires consistent practice; effectiveness depends on student engagement
