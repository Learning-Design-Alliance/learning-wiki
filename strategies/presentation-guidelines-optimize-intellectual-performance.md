# Present information in a manner that encourages learner activities optimizing intellectual performance

## Description
Cognitive load theory is described as "designed to provide guidelines intended to assist in the presentation of information in a manner that encourages learner activities that optimize intellectual performance." As an implementable approach, presenters of instructional material use these guidelines to structure information so learner activity is channeled productively rather than wasted on unnecessary processing.
