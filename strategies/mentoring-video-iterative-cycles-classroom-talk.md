# Combine mentoring, video/audio self-evaluation, and iterative target-setting cycles to change classroom talk

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention's professional development model combined three core strategies: "mentoring; video and audio recording for self-evaluation and development; and an iterative process of target-setting, implementation, recording, and review". Mentors organised planning and review sessions for each of the eleven cycles, playing and discussing video and audio footage as an essential part of the process, with mentoring intended as a peer relationship encouraging open, non-judgemental discussion. The report notes future research could differentiate the effects of these different elements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- schools provided with all necessary video and audio recording equipment
- mentor meetings per cycle, combining end-of-cycle reviews with planning of the next cycle
#### Constraints
- the report notes future research could aim to differentiate the effects of these different elements

### Target Learners
- Year 5 class teachers and their school mentors

### Target Learning Goals
- changing teacher classroom talk across the curriculum
