# Three-phase debriefing (reaction, analysis, summary) with formative critical-actions feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Debriefing was "structured based on the three-phase approach, including reaction, analysis, and summary subsections". The facilitator first asks participants to reflect on what went well and what could improve, then highlights key learning points on eCPR indications and contraindications, individual roles, and closed-loop communication, and finally invites each participant to share a take-home message. Assessment uses a facilitator-observed critical actions checklist built from Extracorporeal Life Support Organization guidelines, with strictly formative verbal feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An attending physician facilitator, a critical actions checklist aligned with current ELSO eCPR guidelines, and a debrief guide
#### Constraints
- Feedback was strictly formative and verbally delivered during the debriefing session

### Target Learners
- Simulation participants across emergency medicine, critical care, nursing, pharmacy, and respiratory therapy

### Target Learning Goals
- Determine indications and patient candidacy for eCPR
- Review and apply current Extracorporeal Life Support Organization eCPR guidelines
