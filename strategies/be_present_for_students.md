# Be Present for Students

## Description
When students talk openly about race, they can feel some anxiety. It's essential that students know this is not what the conversations are for, and that you're there for them to work through these feelings. This means being available for short talks before and after school or in the hallway between classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for individual conversations, a commitment to providing emotional support, and the ability to listen empathetically.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to find time for individual conversations. Requires ongoing commitment and support from teachers and administrators.
