# Badge-based competency coaching in which teachers design customized mastery paths and earn badges for demonstrated evidence of practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has teachers work with a Data-Informed Growth Coach to design "a customized path toward mastery of core instructional Best Practices and district-selected standards". Teachers "earned badges as they demonstrated evidence of practice", making educator learning competency-based rather than seat-time-based. The approach is job-embedded and customized to each partner district's needs and priorities, with all interactions documented on a coaching platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches who work with teachers to design customized mastery paths
- A system for documenting evidence of practice against district-selected standards
