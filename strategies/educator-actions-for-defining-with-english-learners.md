# Concrete actions for mathematics educators: focus on ideas over words, engage students in defining early, connect multiple representations, extend to formal definitions, and nurture the genre of definition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief closes with concrete, specific actions mathematics educators can take when planning learning opportunities for English Learners: think about the mathematical ideas rather than the words, choose a working definition that maximizes access without giving away what students could discover; engage students early in generating and continually refining working criteria; create activities connecting multiple representations to uncover relationships; invite students to extend to more formal definitions; and nurture students' understanding of the genre of definition in mathematics as a deductive discipline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must plan opportunities where students generate and refine working definitions rather than receiving formal definitions first.

### Target Learning Goals
- deep exploration of mathematical ideas
- developing formal definitions and deductive reasoning
