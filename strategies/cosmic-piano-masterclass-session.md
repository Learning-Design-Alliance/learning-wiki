# Cosmic Piano Masterclass: a session walking students through the complete experimental journey from detector assembly to data analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Masterclass is a structured session for high school and university students that begins with roughly one hour explaining the physical processes behind cosmic rays, followed by hands-on assembly of detector modules and then guided data analysis. The article says it "brings the audience through the complete journey of the particle physicist, from designing, to building a particle detector, to analysing the data collected with said experiment." Participants can assemble the Cosmic Piano themselves or observe via remote broadcast, then use the detector interactively before moving to analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to scintillator and APD components presented in isolated, protected states for safe assembly
- a Jupyter Notebook analysis environment
- pre-collected Cosmic Piano data
#### Constraints
- Assembly requires precision in handling scintillators and APDs, so components are initially presented in protected states

### Target Learners
- high school students
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding cosmic ray physics
- learning data quality control
- experiencing the workflow of an experimental physicist
