# Tailor postgraduate educational programs to performance-trajectory subtypes and average internship scores across a year of monthly evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend postgraduate programs matched to the three identified trajectory subtypes, aiming "not only to promote excellence but also to address and rectify the specific shortcomings identified in training physicians." They also highlight "averaging medical internship performance scores across 1 year of monthly evaluations within each department" as valuable for understanding clinical performance across diverse working conditions, given low single-rater reliability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monthly performance evaluations by department training directors across a full internship year, as in the studied SNUH system
#### Constraints
- Generalizability is limited because the study involved interns affiliated to a single institution; specialty and residency-stage variability was not examined; and no data on trainee self-efficacy or subjective well-being were available

### Target Learners
- medical interns
- first-year residents

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical performance development across patient care, communication, clinical skills, self-directed learning, patient safety, and professionalism
