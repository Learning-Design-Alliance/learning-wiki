# Sequence simulation first, then repeat the same assignment with physical laboratory equipment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the combined environment, students used the analogy-based simulation and the laboratory kits for the same assignments in a fixed order: "Students were first asked to complete the assignment using the analogy-based simulation; and then they were asked to repeat the assignment with the laboratory equipment kits." This sequencing, within the same curriculum materials and inquiry-oriented worksheets, defined the experimental condition that produced the highest corrected post-test scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both a computerized classroom with the simulation and laboratory equipment kits must be available for the same unit
#### Constraints
- Tested only with Turkish 7th-grade students on the 'Electricity in Our Life' unit over three weeks and 12 periods

### Target Learners
- Turkish 7th-grade elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding basic electric circuit concepts and relationships
