# Adopt TBL over lecture-based learning to develop clinical reasoning and engagement in health-professions education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend changing pedagogical strategies in nursing and midwifery education toward interactive, student-centered methods, with TBL as the example, because it "overcomes any barrier between the course substance and the application from the classroom to the clinical setting." They also suggest applying TBL for at least one semester to realize its maximal outcome, and note online TBL variants can address pedagogical needs in virtual learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation of vignette-based application exercises, readiness assurance tests, and a facilitator to run the sequence
#### Constraints
- The authors state the short three-session exposure was not an optimal duration for the most effective delivery of TBL, and the teacher's role driven by researchers may have affected the intervention

### Target Learners
- midwifery students
- nursing students
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- classroom engagement
