# Use modular real-time visualizations as interventions to study and shape distributed group communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying real-time, data-driven visualizations next to group video chat as an intervention on online team communication, building on prior work showing shared displays can reduce dominant behavior and real-time visualizations can increase interaction balance. It states that "the modular nature of the system allows for quick development and deployment of prototype visualizations, which may quicken the pace of group dynamics and intervention research". Researchers craft their own visualizations from analytics engine statistics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time interaction statistics from an analytics engine; a visualization toolkit (Breakout uses D3.js) displayed alongside the video chat
#### Constraints
- Prior supporting evidence (e.g., Meeting Mediator) comes from physical and distributed collaboration studies, not from Breakout deployments in online courses

### Target Learners
- distributed online learning groups in small teams

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced participation
- engagement
- collaborative communication skills
