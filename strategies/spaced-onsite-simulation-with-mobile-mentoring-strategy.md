# Use spaced, onsite simulation practice with mobile mentoring and peer coaching to sustain clinical learning after training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a specific implementable recipe: deliver training in short onsite courses spaced about one month apart, reinforce learning with simulation practice two to three times weekly at the job site, have facility-based peer staff coach colleagues as they practice, and support providers remotely through weekly SMS reminders and quizzes plus monthly half-hour mentoring calls. This recipe is the intervention under test against traditional offsite group training in the trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer Practice Coordinators receive technical updates and monthly mentoring calls from a trainer/master mentor
#### Constraints
- The strategy's effectiveness in Nigeria and other LMICs is what the trial is designed to establish; prior evidence from LMICs is limited

### Target Learners
- Health facility teams providing maternal and newborn day of birth care

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of clinical competencies and transfer to clinical practice over 12 months
