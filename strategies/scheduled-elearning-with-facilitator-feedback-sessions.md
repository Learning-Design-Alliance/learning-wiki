# Structure self-directed e-learning with a delivery schedule over fixed time periods and recurrent facilitator-led feedback sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend moving beyond purely self-active e-learning by combining the tool with scheduled delivery periods and recurrent feedback sessions organized by a facilitator, noting that structuring e-learning "in such a format has been shown to hold promise in medical education". This responds to feasibility findings that lack of interactivity and insufficient self-discipline were barriers to completing the tool without supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator to organize recurrent feedback sessions
- A delivery schedule over fixed time periods
#### Constraints
- Developed for busy healthcare workers for whom unsupervised self-directed completion showed high attrition

### Target Learners
- Healthcare workers in hospital settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Completion of e-learning modules on delirium prevention, detection and management
