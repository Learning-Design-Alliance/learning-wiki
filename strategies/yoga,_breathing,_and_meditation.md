# Yoga, Breathing, and Meditation

## Description
Using yoga, breathing, and meditation techniques in educational settings involves incorporating these practices to provide calming stimulation and regulate the nervous system. Moving slowly through a yoga sequence can provide calming stimulation to the vestibular, proprioceptive, and tactile systems. Calming breathing techniques can help to calm the entire nervous system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Yoga mat, quiet space.
#### Constraints
- Some children may have physical limitations or difficulties with certain poses. Instruction should be adapted to meet individual needs.
