# Schedule voluntary online image-based self-assessment quizzes shortly before the practical exam

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article administered two voluntary online quizzes of cadaveric images with typed anatomical-term answers and immediate feedback: a baseline quiz (Q1) and a second quiz (Q2) "scheduled 1 week ahead of the practical exam to serve as a self-evaluation tool". Students received a grade and immediate feedback per question, promoting engagement with the image repository.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 20-minute completion window, single attempt, typed answers with correct spelling, immediate feedback
#### Constraints
- Only about 50% of students chose to participate; quiz engagement was self-selected

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-assessment of anatomical structure identification
- exam preparation
