# Implement CWPT through daily peer-tutoring sessions with points, teams, and weekly testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes a concrete implementation routine for CWPT: practice 30 minutes a day, including 20 minutes for tutoring and 10 minutes for material preparation, with individual end-of-week testing and pretesting of upcoming material. Each Monday students are paired and assigned to one of two teams; partners take turns tutoring and testing each other and award points for correct answers, and the team with the most points is announced daily and recognized each Friday.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers create age-appropriate peer teaching materials accounting for tutees' language skills and disabilities
- Teachers receive CWPT training; in the study, teachers were considered trained at 85% or above on consultants' observation checklists
#### Constraints
- The developer states the program suits content areas involving drill and memorization

### Target Learners
- elementary students, including regular and special education students and English language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reinforcing concepts and skills initially taught by the teacher through repeated practice and immediate feedback
