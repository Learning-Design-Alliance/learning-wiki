# Use full-text captions on graded films in EFL listening instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends teaching listening through captioned films, with full captions as the most effective mode. In the study, 176 minutes of two animated films were divided into 15 sessions of 10-15 minutes shown at the end of class, with preview information, note-taking, dictionary consultation, and content questions. The authors state that "providing captions for films, regardless of the type of captions, was effective in developing general listening comprehension of EFL students", with full captions producing the largest gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Films appropriate to learners' proficiency level, verified by piloting
- Regular viewing sessions with teacher preview and follow-up questions
#### Constraints
- Findings come from intermediate-level female learners aged 15-18 in one Iranian institute over 15 sessions

### Target Learners
- intermediate-level EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
