# Improve future ASP editions by expanding hands-on activities, pre-distributing course summaries, and adding practical sessions, group projects, and field trips

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on collective feedback presented by randomly arranged student groups, the report lists improvements for future ASP events. Students recommended: "Expand hands-on activities to more areas of physics and applications"; optimizing the range and depth of lectures while avoiding repetitive materials; preparing course-content summaries before the event; testing logistics in advance; "adding practical sessions and group projects to enhance understanding"; and extending the program with field trips and courses on scientific entrepreneurship.

## Design Implications
