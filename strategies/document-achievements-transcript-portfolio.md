# Document online learning achievements in a transcript-like record and a portfolio of coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Rule 9 advises learners to "take concrete steps from the outset to document this in any way you can": record the program of study in something resembling a transcript with course descriptions, institutions, and dates of completion; create a portfolio of reading lists, major exams, and substantial homework; and for computational courses, assemble programming assignments and projects with impeccable, presentable documentation. The article frames this as substitute proof for the missing diploma.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing effort from the outset of study, including documented code for computational courses
#### Constraints
- Certificates of completion are carefully distinguished from actual course credits; badges such as Mozilla Open Badges may or may not carry weight with employers or universities

### Target Learners
- online learners seeking employment, advancement, or admission to academic programs

### Target Learning Goals
- demonstrating competencies and seriousness of study to employers or admissions offices without a formal diploma
