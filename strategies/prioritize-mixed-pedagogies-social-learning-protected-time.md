# Prioritise mixed pedagogies, social learning and protected time in supervision training design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The synthesis advises educators designing supervision training to build in multiple pedagogical modes and dedicated time. The article states: "Educators are encouraged to prioritize mixed pedagogies, social learning and protected time to maximize the positive supervisor outcomes from training." Mixed pedagogies include active and/or experiential learning alongside didactic elements, and social learning refers to learning from peers and others during training. The recommendation rests on the synthesis's demi-regularities across short and extended-duration interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training design must include mixed pedagogies involving active and/or experiential learning plus social learning opportunities
#### Constraints
- The synthesis notes future realist evaluation should explore further any differences between short and extended-duration interventions

### Target Learners
- Health and human services supervisors (e.g. clinical teachers, preceptors, field instructors)

### Target Learning Goals
- Supervisory knowledge, skills, confidence and supervisory behaviour change
