## Description
The author recommends a specific curricular placement for the qualitative analysis: "This approach may be introduced to students right after Newton’s second law and Hooke’s law are taught." It serves as an example of how to graphically analyze motion under a varying force rather than a constant one, and the author regards it as the proper way of teaching SHM to students. The strategy requires only the four elementary facts students have already learned.
