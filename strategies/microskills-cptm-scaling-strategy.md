# Phased implementation with shared video library and peer-assisted feedback to scale micro-skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address scalability, the authors recommend that "future implementations in resource-limited or large-class settings could consider the following strategies: (1) establishing a shared video library of standardized skill demonstrations to reduce repeated real-time recordings; (2) incorporating peer-assisted learning, where senior students or trained teaching assistants facilitate video review and feedback sessions; (3) adopting asynchronous online platforms to offload faculty time and allow students to complete reflective exercises flexibly." They also advise phased rollout in high-volume core specialties before expanding to all rotations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Shared video library of standardized skill demonstrations
- Trained senior students or teaching assistants to facilitate feedback sessions
- Asynchronous online platforms for reflective exercises
#### Constraints
- An increase in the number of participating students would increase teaching workload
- Clinical pathways require regular updates and local adaptations to maintain teaching quality

### Target Learners
- surgical interns in large-class or resource-limited settings

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural skill acquisition
- clinical reasoning
