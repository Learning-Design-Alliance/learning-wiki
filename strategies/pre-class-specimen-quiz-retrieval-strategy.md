# Use closed-book pre-class specimen quizzes as retrieval practice to activate prior anatomical knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before each flipped anatomy session, students take a short closed-book specimen quiz (about 5 minutes) on the Superstar Learning platform. The article frames these as "retrieval practice activities" that "actively activated prior knowledge", and reports a strong correlation between pre-class quiz performance and memory-item scores supporting the mechanism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-class video materials must be posted about a week ahead and students must actually watch them; engagement data showed 2.8 of 3 videos viewed on average.
#### Constraints
- Success is contingent on conscientious pre-class preparation; the article states inadequate preparation can undermine in-class activity effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidation of foundational anatomical knowledge
- laboratory specimen identification
