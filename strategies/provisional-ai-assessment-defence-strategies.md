# Provisional assessment-defence strategies: question styles AI struggles with, pre-approval open-book exams, and routine AI-capability monitoring

## Description
The article recommends educators use its open-source code to run their own risk assessments and be transparent with students about trusting AI output. In the short term it suggests "question styles that AI models currently struggle with: multipart problems, sketch-based questions, and queries requiring the interpretation of graphics". Longer term it proposes open-book exams be set so a modern AI model would score at most 40% before approval, more invigilated in-person exams, and a shift toward holistic, skills-based assessment.
