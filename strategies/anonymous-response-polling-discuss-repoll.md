# Use anonymous electronic response polling with discuss-and-repoll cycles and before-and-after topic polls

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Instructors pose questions to the entire class and students respond with electronic keypads; response statistics are displayed immediately with or without the correct answer. Display without the answer allows use "as a polling mechanism," with results introducing small-group discussion before the class is polled again with the correct response indicated. Polls taken before and after a topic show "any change of opinion on the topic after it has been investigated scientifically," and anonymity keeps all respondents on equal ground.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Electronic student response systems with keypads and immediate display of aggregated responses
- Anonymity of responses so students do not follow the first opinion expressed
#### Constraints
- One student resented being interrupted and having to think about the material during the lecture

### Target Learners
- Large lecture classes of non-science majors

### Target Learning Goals
- Class engagement and discussion
- Real-time assessment of student understanding
- Documenting change in opinions after scientific investigation
