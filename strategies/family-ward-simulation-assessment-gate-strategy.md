# Simulated family ward training with a pre-discharge competency assessment gate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy sets up "a family ward and guiding the children and their parents to view the ward as a family space and the doctors and nurses as family members" for 3 to 5 days, during which staff observe, record and correct problems. Parents are then assessed before discharge, and those who fail continue training with reassessment and home-visit guidance. This lets caregivers rehearse home care under supervision before discharge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A ward space configurable as a simulated family environment
- Staff available to observe and correct the simulation over 3 to 5 days
- A theoretical examination and operational assessment with a defined passing score
#### Constraints
- In this study no family failed the second assessment, so the fallback pathway (delayed discharge, home visits) was not exercised

### Target Learners
- Parents of hospitalized children with chronic conditions

### Target Learning Goals
- Home care and condition-management skills before discharge
- Treatment adherence behaviors
