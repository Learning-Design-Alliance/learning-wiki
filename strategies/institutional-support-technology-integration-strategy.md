# Institutional support package: professional development, technical support, engagement strategies, and assessment reform

## Description
Based on its findings, the article recommends a set of institutional actions to help academics balance traditional teaching with technology integration. University leaders should "prioritise professional development opportunities for academics to enhance their digital literacy through in-person and online training sessions." It further recommends adequate technical support services, strategies promoting active student participation such as interactive activities and virtual labs, and institutional review of assessment practices toward online quizzes, multimedia projects, and e-portfolios.
