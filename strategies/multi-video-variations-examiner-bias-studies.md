# Use multiple actors, accent types, scripts and OSCE scenarios in future examiner-bias experiments to improve blinding and validity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend design improvements for future studies of accent bias in OSCE assessment: "Using more videos with variations in accents, student characteristics and scripts would improve the blinding", and "Using several actors with different characteristics and accents would improve the reliability of the study." They also recommend several scenarios for higher validity and post-marking questions about what participants thought the study investigated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources to film multiple videos and employ several actors, which the study lacked due to resource constraints
#### Constraints
- The study's single actor and single scenario limited generalisability; the scenario was written for clinical-year students and may not apply to non-clinical-year students or postgraduate applicants

### Target Learners
- OSCE examiners
- Medical education researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- Detecting and controlling examiner bias in clinical skills assessment
