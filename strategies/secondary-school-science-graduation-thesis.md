# Require a multi-year natural-science graduation thesis with real, noisy data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes each student develop a school graduation thesis in a natural science (physics, chemistry, geology or biology), defined in 10th grade, developed in 11th grade with experiments and data collection, and written and presented in 12th grade. It stresses that "the experience of dealing with real, noisy data is a crucial one" and builds scientific writing, autonomy and resilience, supervised by a teacher with an academic or industry co-supervisor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher supervision in the relevant subject area, ideally with a co-supervisor from academia, industry or another real-world context
#### Constraints
- Phased across grades 10-12, so it presupposes the three-year structure and depends on teacher capacity

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in grades 10-12

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis and modelling
- scientific writing
- autonomy
- resilience
