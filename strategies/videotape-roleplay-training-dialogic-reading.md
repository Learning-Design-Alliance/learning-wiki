# Train teachers in Dialogic Reading via videotape, role-play, and group discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes a training approach in which "Teachers can be trained on the principles of Dialogic Reading through videotape followed by role-playing and group discussion." Across the reviewed studies, trainees watched vignettes of adult-child shared book reading that followed or did not follow the guidelines, analyzed the vignettes, and did one-on-one role plays with the trainer, with phase one and phase two sessions lasting 30 and 20 minutes respectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to videotape training materials covering the two phases of Dialogic Reading and a trainer for one-on-one role plays
#### Constraints
- Information is not available about the costs of teacher training and implementation of Dialogic Reading

### Target Learners
- preschool and child care teachers, aides, and undergraduate volunteer readers

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing interactive shared book reading techniques with young children
