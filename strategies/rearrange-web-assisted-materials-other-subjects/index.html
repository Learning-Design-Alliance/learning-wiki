Re-arrange and extend the web-assisted flipped-classroom activity materials to other science subjects and disciplines¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The author recommends that the developed materials be adapted beyond the original unit: "The materials developed in the present study can also be re -arranged/modified for use in other subjects of science classes" and designed for other disciplines. Researchers studying the flipped classroom model are invited to utilize and improve the activities in new web-assisted environments.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Materials must be prepared based on systematic planning, such as the Assure model

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation extends a material validated in one 7th-grade science unit; effectiveness in other subjects or disciplines was not tested in this study

Target Learners¶

  • Science students across units and grade levels; potentially other disciplines

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Individual learning of theoretical information before class and achievement in the subject unit

Claims¶

  • Adding web-assisted alternative assessment activities to a flipped classroom produces significantly higher unit achievement than video lessons alone in 7th-grade science [+M]
  • Within the experimental group, achievement rose significantly from pre-test to post-test, while the control group's gain was not statistically significant [+M]
  • Experimental-group students needed less teacher support for theoretical information than control-group students during the flipped lessons [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Flipped Learning
  • Flipped Classroom
  • Design flipped classroom instruction according to subject type, knowledge type, and students' independent learning ability
  • Adapt the two question-design strategies across disciplines: novel scenarios for logic puzzles, guided reasoning for proofs and critical analyses
  • Apply multimedia-supported 7E learning model materials to other science units and to courses with abstract content
