# Run a physical QR quest in which student groups decode question codes at stations under a time limit with a point-and-penalty scoring system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a physical QR quest as "гра за типом лінійного квесту": the class is divided into groups, each receiving a tablet with a decoder and presentation app, a station map, and a worksheet. Groups decode codes at stations, answer questions, log answers, take photo markers, and score points: 1 point per completed station with a photo marker, 1–2 points per correct answer, 0 for incorrect answers, and penalties of 1 point for a missing answer or for finishing late at 1 point per each 5 minutes of delay. Example stations for a grade 8 recap lesson on thermal phenomena are «Історична», «Метрична», «Технічна», «Літературна».

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- groups each need a tablet with a decoder program and presentation app, a station map, and a worksheet
- station questions should be short tasks, since long tasks cause QR reading difficulties
#### Constraints
- "Час квесту обмежений" — the quest runs under a time limit; the article notes short tasks should be chosen or scanning difficulties may arise

### Target Learners
- pupils in grades 8 and 10 of general secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- generalization and systematization of physics knowledge (e.g., thermal phenomena)
- team collaboration within groups
- independent work skills
