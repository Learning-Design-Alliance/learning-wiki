# Scaffolded simulation-based hypothesis testing in virtual astronomy labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the Astronomy Laboratory Template course, assessments contain series of questions based on student interactions with embedded OER simulation applets such as PhET's Gravity and Orbits. For these questions, "hypothesis testing is scaffolded for the students who must choose a hypothesis before getting the parameters for a simulation to test their hypothesis," and credit is given for correctly identifying whether the initial hypothesis was correct or incorrect.
