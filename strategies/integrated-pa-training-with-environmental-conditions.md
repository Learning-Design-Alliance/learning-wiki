# Integrated training programmes that include conditions in the physical and social environment of direct support professionals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future training programmes in physical activity support for direct support professionals combine capability and motivation components with environmental conditions. They state future training programmes "are strongly recommended to include conditions in the physical and social environment of direct support professionals", because opportunity can influence motivation and existing training does not distinguish the opportunities afforded. They further recommend providing training in physical activity support for all direct support professionals and designing internal training programmes with strategies for changing the physical and social environment within the organisational context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should be accompanied by attention to physical activity plans for all people supported
#### Constraints
- Existing training programmes were found not to distinguish between the opportunities afforded

### Target Learners
- direct support professionals supporting people with intellectual disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- capability and motivation to support physical activity in people with intellectual disabilities
