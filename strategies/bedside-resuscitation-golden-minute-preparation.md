# Start neonatal resuscitation at the mother's bedside within the Golden Minute with prepared clean equipment and preparation for every delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The midwives' accounts support preparing the birth area and equipment for every delivery and beginning resuscitation immediately at the bedside: "Preparation of the birth area and resuscitation equipment allows for a timely response at delivery if resuscitation is required." Starting on the mother's chest is deemed to expedite resuscitation, assist infection prevention and thermoregulation, and avoid misusing the Golden Minute on delays such as cutting the cord or moving to the baby bench, where midwives reported cross-infection risk.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clean bag and mask equipment ready by the bedside before delivery.
- Vigilant monitoring of labor parameters such as fetal heart rate to anticipate resuscitation needs.
#### Constraints
- Unproductive time seeking a clean bag and mask thwarts the Golden Minute when decontamination workload outweighs resuscitation workload, as reported by the midwives.

### Target Learners
- Midwives managing deliveries in busy low-resource labor wards

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely initiation of ventilation within the first minute after birth
- Infection prevention and thermoregulation during resuscitation
