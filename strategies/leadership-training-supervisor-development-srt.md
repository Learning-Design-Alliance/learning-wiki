# Incorporate paternalistic leadership training into residency supervisor qualification and development systems

## Description
The article recommends that medical education management prioritize developing supervisors' leadership styles as a core teaching competency. It proposes establishing dedicated "Teaching Leadership" modules within pre-service training and annual refresher courses, using role-playing, case analysis, and reflective discussion to help supervisors understand the differential impacts of the three leadership dimensions. Supervisors should practice moral leadership behaviors (leading by example, fairness) and benevolent behaviors (individualized consideration, emotional support) while reducing authoritarian behaviors (dictatorial decision-making, excessive control).
