## Description
The article argues measurement instruments have an essential role in science education and teacher education, and highlights concept tests probing for "intuitive c onceptions" of "misconceptions", such as the ideas that the seasons are caused by different distances to the sun, that a moving object looses force, or that dolphins are fish. Teachers have to know whether a given approach really increases understanding or motivation, and to be able to test this in their own classrooms. The article states such ideas are widespread and hard to change, even among university students.
