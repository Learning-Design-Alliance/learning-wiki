# Provide on-campus one-stop benefits screening, application assistance, and case management to reduce nonacademic barriers to persistence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy connects enrolled students to federal, state, and local benefits (food stamps, WIC, Medicaid) plus wraparound services (tax preparation, financial counseling, legal services, child care, housing) through on-campus coordinators. Coordinators conduct preliminary screening "to determine whether they qualify for federal, state, or local benefits", then provide application assistance and follow up, advocating with agencies if a student is wrongly denied. This is the intervention model whose implementation the WWC reviewed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Site coordinators trained through an eight-week online launch curriculum, an on-campus office, screening software, and comparison-group access limited to other campus and public resources
#### Constraints
- Intensity of support depends on individual student needs; the program provides no direct financial assistance to students

### Target Learners
- Low-income community college students

### Target Learning Goals
- Persistence in college
- Academic achievement
