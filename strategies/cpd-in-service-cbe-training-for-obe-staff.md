# Provide competency-based in-service training through the CPD system for OBE-trained nurses and supervisors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends recurrent competency-based in-service training so that experienced OBE-trained nurses and supervisors learn the approach CBE-nurses bring. Both supervisors and CBE-nurses expressed this need: "Most supervisors received OBE, so, they don’t know well about the competency-based approach. Training should be conducted for such supervisors". The authors argue all health facility staff need thorough CBE knowledge through the CPD system so CBE-nurses can demonstrate their acquired competence.
