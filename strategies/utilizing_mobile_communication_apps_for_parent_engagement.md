# Utilizing Mobile Communication Apps for Parent Engagement

## Description
Mobile communication apps (e.g., ClassDojo, Spotlight, Remind, and Seesaw) offer simple ways for educators to send texts, video summaries, and alerts to parents about school activities or their child’s progress. These tools give parents a deeper look into their children’s performance and experience in the classroom, while strengthening relationships between schools and families. Advanced features include translation of report cards into multiple languages.
