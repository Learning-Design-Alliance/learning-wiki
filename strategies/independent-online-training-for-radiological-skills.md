# Use independent online training (written instructions or case-based self-study) as an accessible, time-efficient alternative to hands-on expert-led sessions for radiological skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "Independent online training offers practical advantages over more time-demanding hands-on sessions, supporting broader clinical implementation." Online platforms reach a broad global audience and allow participants to engage "at their own pace, which may enhance knowledge retention and accommodate diverse learning needs." This strategy recommends deploying web-based platforms combining DICOM viewing with electronic case report forms for scalable radiology training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web-based platform combining DICOM viewing with electronic case report forms (e.g., the OHIF-based platform used in this study).
- For case-based self-study, annotated key images, common pitfalls, practical tips, and reference measurements embedded in the DICOM series.
#### Constraints
- The study used a selected case database with only good-quality images, which may not fully reflect real-world variability in image quality.
- Long-term knowledge retention was not assessed and remains an open question.

### Target Learners
- radiologists
- radiology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate measurement of anatomical structures on MRI
- improved diagnostic performance and interreader agreement
