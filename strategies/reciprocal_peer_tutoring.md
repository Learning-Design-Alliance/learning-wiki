# Reciprocal Peer Tutoring

## Description
Reciprocal peer tutoring (RPT) involves pairing students of similar abilities to work together, alternating the roles of tutor and tutee. Students take turns teaching each other, offering praise and feedback as opportunities arise. This approach eliminates the need to designate fixed tutor and tutee roles, allowing for a more collaborative learning environment where both students engage in teaching and learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful pairing of students, clear learning objectives, and structured activities. Teachers need to provide guidance and monitor progress to ensure effective collaboration.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all students, particularly those who struggle with collaboration or prefer individual work. Requires careful planning and monitoring to ensure both students are actively engaged and benefiting from the activity.

### Personalization
