# Fair Communication Anchor Charts

## Description
Displaying anchor charts outlining fair communication protocols for class discussions and/or projects requiring teamwork involves creating visual aids that articulate the rules and expectations for respectful and inclusive communication. These charts serve as constant reminders of the agreed-upon protocols, promoting active listening, equitable participation, and constructive feedback. Fair communication protocols may include guidelines for taking turns speaking, respecting diverse perspectives, avoiding interruptions, and providing specific and actionable feedback. By making these protocols visible and accessible, educators can create a classroom environment where all learners feel safe, valued, and empowered to express their ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development of clear and concise communication protocols that are aligned with the classroom's values and learning objectives. Creation and display of anchor charts that visually represent the protocols. Regular reminders and reinforcement of the protocols throughout classroom discussions and activities.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness of this strategy depends on the learners' willingness to adhere to the communication protocols. There is a risk that some learners may not fully understand or internalize the protocols or that conflicts may arise despite the best efforts of the educator.
