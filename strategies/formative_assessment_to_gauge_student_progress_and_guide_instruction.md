# Formative Assessment to Gauge Student Progress and Guide Instruction

## Description
Assessing student work on a regular basis provides both the teacher and the student with a sense of the student’s progress, indicates strengths and areas for improvement, and helps the teacher plan appropriately to meet the student’s needs. This formative assessment is part of the process of a learning sequence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regularly scheduled formative assessments, tools for data collection and feedback (e.g., Flip, Padlet, Google Sites, weekly surveys).
#### Constraints
- Requires time for data collection and feedback, may require adaptation to different learning environments and student needs.
