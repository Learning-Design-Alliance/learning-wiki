# Implement SEM with two weekly lessons of at least 60 minutes, totaling no more than 18 lessons, for secondary students without prior SEM experience in small class sizes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distills its moderator findings into a practical implementation recipe for frontline physical education teachers. It recommends that "frontline physical education teachers implement SEM interventions with small class sizes for secondary students who lack prior SEM experience" and design courses with "two lessons per week, with each session lasting no less than 60 minutes and a total of no more than 18 lessons." This strategy directly reflects the subgroup meta-analytic results for each moderator variable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must follow the SEM structural framework (season, affiliation, formal competition, culminating event, record keeping, festivity) rather than traditional teaching
#### Constraints
- GRADE certainty of the underlying evidence is very low
- Subgroup analyses of cognitive and non-cognitive indicators within each moderator were not conducted due to limited studies
- Publication bias was detected via significant Egger test

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in physical education

### Target Learning Goals
- cognitive PE learning outcomes (sports ability, physical fitness, knowledge)
- non-cognitive outcomes (motivation, interest, attitudes, mental health)
