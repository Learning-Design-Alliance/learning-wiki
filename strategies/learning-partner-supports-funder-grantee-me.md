# Engage a learning partner to support funders and grantees in designing and carrying out a learning plan and building M&E capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief states that "a learning partner can support funders and their grantees to design and carry out a learning plan, build the capacity of grantees to conduct their own M&E activities, and interpret and share findings that drive decision making and program improvement." The learning partner thus serves both as technical support and as a capacity-builder for grantee-led evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning partner must work with both the funder and grantees across designing the learning plan, building grantee M&E capacity, and interpreting and sharing findings.

- Education funders and their grantees implementing learning solutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Building grantee capacity to conduct M&E and using findings to drive decision making and program improvement
