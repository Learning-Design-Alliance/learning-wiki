Use a mnemonic phrase and colour-coded planet tokens to help young students memorize planet names and order¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A concrete memorization strategy in which each planet is matched with a colour on a colouring token and the planets' initial letters form an easy-to-remember sentence. The article reports that after matching each planet with a colour, students better recollected names and characteristics, and the mnemonic exercise was followed by six of 13 experimental-group students obtaining the maximum score on Item 3 of Test 3.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tokens with the eight planets for colouring
  • A mnemonic phrase mapping initial letters to planet names in order from the Sun

Constraints¶

  • Reported for Romanian-speaking first-graders; the phrase is language-specific

Target Learners¶

  • first-grade primary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • memorizing the names and order of the planets from the Sun
  • recalling planet characteristics

Claims¶

  • Drawing the Solar System without visual support revealed that experimental-group students formed accurate representations of planet order, size, and characteristics [+W]
