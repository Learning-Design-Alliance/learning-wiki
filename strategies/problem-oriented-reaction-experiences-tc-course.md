## Description
In teaching the TC course the author "adopted a problem-oriented approach", in which students approach topics by posing questions or exploring issues addressed through activities such as model construction, reading textbooks or reference literature, and discussing with teacher or peers after lecture. Table 3 operationalizes this with experiences built on simple reactions (H + H2, H + CH4, H2 + O2) across the four modules, needing about eight academic hours. For example, students first do force analysis to see its failure for reaction, then find Lagrange and Hamilton equations, motivating quantum mechanics.
