# Engage parents through home-learning packets with videos, tips, and activity suggestions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
As part of REDI, parents receive take-home materials supporting the program's goals: "Parents receive packets including videos, parenting tips, and suggestions for home learning activities." This extends the preschool program's social-emotional and literacy focus into the home.
