Co-designed recommendations for education/training providers: partnerships, wrap-around supports, and equitable recruitment¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Worker and learner participants co-designed recommendations for education/training providers across four themes: increase awareness and exploration, improve access to educational and training opportunities, implement equitable recruitment and hiring strategies, and intentionally facilitate career entry, advancement, and retention. Recommendations include forming "strong relationships/partnerships with HBCUs to create a talent pipeline," providing "scholarships, financial support, and other wrap-around services," and helping with "optimizing resumes for AI screening."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Providers must maintain partnerships with employers, HBCUs, and workforce development offices to bridge program completion and career entry

Constraints¶

  • Derived from the lived experiences of a specific participant group of Black workers and learners recruited through non-four-year-degree credentialing programs

Target Learners¶

  • Black workers and learners seeking tech careers via non-four-year-degree credentials

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Tech career awareness, training access, recruitment, career entry, advancement, and retention

Affordances¶

  • Scct Chat Tech Pathway Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Co-designed recommendations for employers: equitable hiring practices, inclusive work culture, and continued upskilling support
  • Colleges can adopt evidence-based support practices—wrap-around advising, proactive advising, non-tuition financial support, co-requisite courses, early alert systems, and disaggregated data—to improve completion
  • Supportive practices for inclusive youth co-design: reach beyond usual voices, respect time, ensure accessibility, and compensate fairly
