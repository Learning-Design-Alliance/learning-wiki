# Reinforce central OSCE modules and build integrated teaching modules around strongly connected skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the network findings, the authors recommend curriculum reform that emphasizes central modules and pairs strongly connected skills in combined teaching. As stated in the conclusion: "medical institutions should emphasize the “General Physical Examination” as a foundational component in OSCE curricula, given its demonstrated centrality in skill interconnections." They further recommend integrated teaching modules capitalizing on the Pediatric–Nursing and cardiac–pulmonary auscultation synergies, plus targeted interventions such as clinical reasoning workshops, mentorship, and experiential learning through ward rounds and case discussions.
