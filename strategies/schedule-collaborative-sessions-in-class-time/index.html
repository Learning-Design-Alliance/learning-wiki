Schedule collaborative sessions during regular class time to reduce scheduling barriers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because students struggled to arrange meetings around course loads, work and family demands, the article states "it is highly recommended that students' collaborative sessions be scheduled during regular class time". This strategy addresses the logistical impediment students faced during the study's semester-long collaborative marketing plan project, in which teams met seven times to develop components of a marketing plan for a chosen Fortune 500 company.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor must allocate regular class time for the collaborative sessions

Constraints¶

  • Some students may be apprehensive during group learning activities, particularly if they prefer working by themselves

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate students with heavy course loads and work or family commitments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • completing collaborative project tasks on schedule
  • teamwork and collaboration skills

  • Semester Long Collaborative Project Design
