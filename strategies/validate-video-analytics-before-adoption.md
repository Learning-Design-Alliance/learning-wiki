# Validate automated video analytics and reach metric consensus before widespread adoption in surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that educators, curriculum developers and professional bodies support validation studies in training environments before widespread adoption of automated video analytics. It states that "Establishing robust validity of automated video analytics is essential if this innovation is to help shape future training" and that consensus must be reached on which operative performance metrics are most relevant for surgical trainees. Only after validation can evidence demonstrating impact on training be established.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robust validity evidence for analysis techniques and generated metrics; consensus on relevant performance metrics
#### Constraints
- Robust protocols and frameworks are needed to address patient concerns over data storage, access and potential breaches

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- operative performance assessment and feedback
