# Reclassify lecture sessions as tutorials to make attendance compulsory and increase engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 associational, 1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because lectures were not compulsory at the University of Aberdeen while all other classes were, the course renamed its sessions: "by changing the session description to a tutorial, students were compelled to attend." The article reports this increase in attendance increased involvement, engagement and learning, and that it maximised contact periods for a time-restricted lecturer. Tutorials were facilitated by the lecturer who adapted teaching to the direction the learning demanded, replacing the previous lectures in timetabled contact time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The facilitator must be able to respond and accommodate while students take ownership of their learning
#### Constraints
- If the main facilitator is absent from the face-to-face session, the tutorial cannot be conducted as intended

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement and participation in dental materials teaching
