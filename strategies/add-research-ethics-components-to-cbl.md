# Add dedicated research methodology, ethics, and mentoring components to case-based curricula to address competency gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the progressive blended case-based model left research skills and professionalism unchanged, the authors recommend supplementing it with targeted components. They state that "future iterations of this teaching model should integrate dedicated research methodology workshops, structured scientific writing exercises, and guided clinical research opportunities" and that future designs "could incorporate longitudinal mentoring programs, structured ethical reflection sessions, and interprofessional education components."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated curriculum time for research methodology, scientific writing, and ethics beyond the clinical case-based module
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of null findings in one 4-week module; the authors note professionalism develops gradually through long-term mentorship and real-world exposure

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in competency-based curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- research capabilities and scientific inquiry
- professionalism and ethical decision-making
- interprofessional collaboration
