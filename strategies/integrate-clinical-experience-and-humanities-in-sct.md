# Integrate more clinical decision-making experience and medical humanities, add virtual simulation and optimize time use in future preclinical SCT courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their results, the authors recommend that preclinical teaching "integrate more imparting of experience related to clinical decision-making and medical humanities", give more guidance on extracurricular reading, and consider that "some virtual simulation techniques may be applied into SCT courses to improve the scenario simulation". They also identify time optimization of the complex course as key to improving teaching efficiency, and recommend including more varied dental defect cases in future curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Better preparation of teachers and students before class
- More cases of dental defects included in the future teaching curriculum
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted at only one dental school with a self-controlled design and no sample size calculation, which the authors state diminished reliability

### Target Learners
- preclinical undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical critical thinking and evidence-based treatment decision making
- medical humanities and dentist-patient communication

## Claims

- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, preclinical dental students' diagnostic accuracy for a tooth defect case was measured before and after SCT, with selection of apical periodontitis falling from 24.49% to 8.16% (p = 0.002)](../claims/sct-improved-case-diagnosis-accuracy.md) [+W]
- [After SCT, more students specified fiber posts (10.20% to 33.67%) and all students preferred all-ceramic over PFM crowns for the case](../claims/sct-improved-restoration-material-selection.md) [+W]
- [Students' selection of occlusal pain, stimulation/spontaneous pain and internal tooth resorption as anticipated complications for the case fell from before to after SCT in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/sct-refined-complication-anticipation.md) [+W]
- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, students' optimal restoration choices for the case changed: full crown fell from 47.96% to 9.18% while post and core crown rose from 16.33% to 70.41%, both p < 0.001](../claims/sct-shifted-optimal-restoration-choice.md) [+W]
- [After SCT, students' feasible treatment plan selections shifted significantly, with post and core crown rising from 39.80% to 96.94% and resin filling falling from 78.57% to 57.14%](../claims/sct-shifted-treatment-plan-selection.md) [+W]
- [Students self-reported improvement in treatment decision making, aesthetic restoration and dentist-patient communication after SCT, while difficult-issue selections showed no significant change](../claims/sct-subjective-gains-and-null-difficulties.md) [+W]
