# Provide the AI scaffold after independent work as a second opinion, or on demand for less motivated learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that students be given more agency in hint-writing by providing the AI-scaffold after they have written a hint on their own to provide them with a 'second opinion'. For learners with a broader range of motivation levels, such as MOOC learners, the article suggests the AI-scaffold could be provided on demand. This design has been found useful for improving the quality of generated artifacts in prior research on learnersourcing.
