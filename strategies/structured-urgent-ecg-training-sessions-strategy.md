# Provide well-designed, structured ECG training sessions for pediatric residents to fill competency gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured ECG training for pediatric and child health residents after finding that three-fourths reported insufficient training and emergent conditions were missed. It states that "Structured ECG training sessions are required to improve the competency of these postgraduate students" and concludes that "A well-designed and structured ECG urgent training sessions are required to fill this gap among the residents." The recommendation rests on the study's own findings of low diagnostic accuracy and low confidence.
