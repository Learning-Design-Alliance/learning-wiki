# Incorporate research methodology, learning psychology, and philosophy of education subjects into post-graduate curricular directives to improve teachers' reflection and pedagogic practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes changes to the curricular structure of lato-sensu post-graduate courses to improve the scientific and pedagogic education of Brazilian academic teachers. It recommends that "subjects such as Methodology of Scientific Research, Psychology of the Learning Process and Philosophy of Education (with ethical and communicative content) should be incorporated with the aim of improving the reflection and the pedagogic practice of the teacher." It also values classroom dialogue and developing teachers' abilities to teach students to investigate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pedagogic abilities must be developed within the post-graduate course itself, since teachers currently acquire pedagogic attitudes from experience rather than acquired education.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a descriptive case study in the State of Goias (Brazil); the article does not report testing these curricular changes.

### Target Learners
- University teachers with lato-sensu post-graduate training

### Target Learning Goals
- Pedagogic competence and reflective teaching practice in higher education classroom work
