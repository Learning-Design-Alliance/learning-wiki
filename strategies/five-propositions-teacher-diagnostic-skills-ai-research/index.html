Use five conceptual propositions to guide research and professional development on teachers' diagnostic skills in AI-supported formative assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article derives five propositions for future research and teacher education: (1) AI-generated inferences change the diagnostic situation by adding an information source, raising questions about when teachers accept, reject, or override them; (2) instructional use depends on integrating AI information with contextual knowledge; (3) data literacy and AI literacy are expected to influence evaluation and use of AI inferences; (4) instructional decisions depend on translating integrated judgments into adaptive decisions; (5) teachers should extend diagnosing to students' interactions with AI-supported learning environments, particularly when AI processes are not fully transparent.
