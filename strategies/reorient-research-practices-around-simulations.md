# Reorient research priorities, workflows, data infrastructures, and validation practices around theory-constrained learner-development simulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's agenda is an implementable program for the research community: reorient priorities, workflows, data infrastructures, and validation practices around simulations of learner development that are "theory-constrained, dynamically updateable, and empirically examinable." The authors stress this is "not merely a list of open questions" but a coordinated invitation to change how educational science operates. It implies building simulation-based validation into the research pipeline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A coordinated research community willing to reorient priorities, workflows, data infrastructures, and validation practices
#### Constraints
- Simulations must remain empirically examinable and theory-constrained per the article

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- pre-implementation evaluation of educational innovations
