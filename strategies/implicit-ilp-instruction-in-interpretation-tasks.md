# Provide implicit instruction on ILP features within interpretation tasks during the course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The treatment applied in the study was implicit instruction on the application of interlanguage pragmatic features embedded in students' interpretation tasks, delivered over a two-month period in the semester, while the control group was taught through the traditional method of teaching interpretation. The article reports this implicit ILP instruction produced significant post-test gains in interpretation ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Audio interpretation materials that incorporate a wide range of pragmatic features, as used in the study's pre-tests and post-tests
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in an Iranian university context with availability-sampled undergraduate translation students, so scope is limited to that setting

### Target Learners
- undergraduate translation students taking an interpretation course

### Target Learning Goals
- simultaneous interpretation of spoken English files containing pragmatic features
