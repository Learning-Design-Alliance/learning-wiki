# Train RCT recruiters to reflect on personal biases and recognise common ways these arise in recruitment discussions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends building generic, broadly applicable communication-skills training for clinical recruiters around the recurring equipoise pitfalls it observed. Clinicians "should be encouraged to reflect on personal biases and be mindful of the common ways in which these can arise in their discussions with patients." Because the pitfalls — recommendations, imbalanced descriptions, disclosed predictions — recurred "irrespective of RCT context", the authors argue "opportunities for specific training in communication skills that would be broadly applicable to a wide clinical audience" exist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should address the three identified pitfalls: offering treatment recommendations, imbalanced treatment descriptions, and disclosing personal predictions about trial outcomes.
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from RCTs experiencing or anticipated to experience recruitment difficulties, and need consideration in RCTs that recruit well.

### Target Learners
- clinicians recruiting patients to randomised controlled trials

### Target Learning Goals
- conveying equipoise neutrally to trial-eligible patients
- recognising and setting aside personal treatment biases during recruitment discussions
