# Pre-Entry Socialization

## Description
Pre-entry socialization involves engaging students in brief, positive interactions before they enter the classroom to set a productive tone. Behavior management expert Rob Plevin recommends using "nonconfrontational statements" and "informal chitchat" at the door to socialize students into productive behaviors. This proactive approach helps mitigate potential disruptions and create a positive classroom atmosphere.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher presence at the door, conversational skills, consistent implementation.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort, may not be effective for all students, relies on teacher-student rapport.
