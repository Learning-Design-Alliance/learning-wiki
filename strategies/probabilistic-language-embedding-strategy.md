# Embed probabilistic, conditional language into day-to-day science teaching without changing curriculum content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A short-term classroom strategy in which teachers embed the fundamental lessons of modern physics into everyday teaching without changing what is taught. The authors propose to 'begin to embe d some of the most fundamental lessons of 'new physics'' by replacing language of absolute causality with the more probabilistic and conditional phrasings indicated by quantum mechanics and chaos theory, which they argue are more accurate and honest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers replace language of absolute causality with probabilistic and conditional phrasings in their own day-to-day classroom teaching
#### Constraints
- The authors present this as a short-term approach whose potential impact is anticipated rather than tested; they do not claim answers for fuller curriculum change

### Target Learners
- school science pupils in the pre-16 and post-16 age range

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the probabilistic and approximate nature of physical models and explanations
