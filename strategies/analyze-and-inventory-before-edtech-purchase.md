# Analyze & Inventory: use cross-departmental data analysis and an inventory of existing interventions before buying new edtech

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Step 1 of the framework directs leaders to begin "with cross-departmental analysis of formative and summative data to identify priority learner populations, instructional gaps, and the root causes driving them." Simultaneously, leaders take stock of interventions already available, including edtech, to determine whether a new tool is needed at all. This grounds any purchase decision in learner needs and existing capacity.
