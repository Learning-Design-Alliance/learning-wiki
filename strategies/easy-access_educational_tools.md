# Easy-Access Educational Tools

## Description
Making educational tools readily available to students supports self-advocacy by fostering independence and choice. Tools like manipulatives, graphic organizers, audio recordings, magnifying paper, highlighters, choice boards, learning menus, and timers should be easily accessible in the classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of tools, clear organization, instruction on tool usage
#### Constraints
- Requires resources and space for tools. May require additional training for students and instructors.
