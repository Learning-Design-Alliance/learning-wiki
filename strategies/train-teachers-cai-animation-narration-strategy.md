# Encourage mathematics teachers to use animation-with-narration CAI and train them in computer-based multimedia instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the AN package was effective for teaching solid geometry, the article recommends that "mathematics teachers should be encouraged to use CAI(AN) for meaningful and effective teaching and learning of mathematics". To achieve this, it recommends training mathematics teachers in computer-based multimedia instruction through seminars, workshops and conferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training on effective use of computer-based multimedia instruction via seminars, workshops and conferences
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in one study of SSII students in Minna, Niger State, Nigeria, on solid geometry

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary school mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Meaningful and effective teaching and learning of mathematics, specifically solid geometry
