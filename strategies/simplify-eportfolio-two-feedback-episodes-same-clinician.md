# Simplify the ePortfolio platform, introduce authentic tasks, and reduce feedback episodes to two from the same senior clinician for consistency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend "simplifying the ePortfolio platform, introducing authentic tasks such as discharge summaries and letters to GP and reducing the number of episodes of feedback to two", provided by the same senior clinician for consistency of feedback. This responds to findings that feedback quality mattered more than quantity and that technical barriers hindered engagement with the platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A senior clinician must deliver detailed, constructive and personalized feedback in a timely fashion
#### Constraints
- Technical problems with the ePortfolio platform interfered with student engagement and would need resolution

### Target Learners
- Year 3 graduate medical students on clinical medicine terms

### Target Learning Goals
- improving written long case documentation and clinical presentation
- reflective practice from feedback
