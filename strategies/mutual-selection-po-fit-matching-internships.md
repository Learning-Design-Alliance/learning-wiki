# Mutual-selection matching of interns to kindergartens using P-O fit assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that universities "should abandon random allocation in favor of a 'Mutual Selection' mechanism", using P-O fit scale assessments before internships to match students' educational values with the organizational culture of potential kindergartens. This operationalizes the finding that value congruence is a primary driver of efficacy and satisfaction.
