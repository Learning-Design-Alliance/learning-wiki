# Anchor spherical-geometry calculations in real-world cases such as the New York–Paris flight path and the Bermuda Triangle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each abstraction in the chapter is immediately instantiated with a concrete geodesic case: the great-circle route from New York to Paris computed from real latitudes and longitudes, and the Bermuda Triangle's area from its three cities' coordinates, where the spherical area exceeds the flat Hero's-formula area. The historical Foucault pendulum is used to connect parallel transport to a buildable apparatus: "he hung a 27 kilogram weight from a 67 meter long wire from the dome of the Pantheon in Paris."
