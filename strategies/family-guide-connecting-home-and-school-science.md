# Connect home and school learning through a family guide linking playful everyday science activities to classroom instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program connects home and school learning by giving families a digital guide of brief, playful activities grounded in everyday experiences, plus material to help parents and caregivers "feel prepared to engage in rich conversations with their children." Teachers and staff shared classroom experience to inform "the design of classroom activities that resonate with families’ everyday experiences," so that home and school activities reinforce the same science, math and engineering goals.
