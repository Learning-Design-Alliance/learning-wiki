# Six concrete activities link students to research environments using real online data and professional tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article presents six specific activities (detailed in Appendix C) to link students to discovery environments, designed to be sustainable over time, scalable to courses of different sizes, and replicable in other Latin American institutions. All share four methodological features: contemporary topics with recent bibliography, access to real data sources available online, computational tools for data analysis and simulation with contrast between experiments and simulations, and writing a report in article format using professional composition tools, which for physics the authors consider LaTeX.
