# Practical orchestration measures for running iVR sessions in classrooms: space, time, supervisors, a Let's Play introduction, and motion sickness breaks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
From their experiences running ten HMDs simultaneously in two time slots, the authors derive concrete orchestration practices. They recommend "organizing enough space, time and capacity with the help of additional supervisors", preparing a larger room with taped play areas and hygienic covers, allocating time to remove equipment, and introducing the environment by showing a Let's Play video. They also recommend informing students about motion sickness and providing a break after 15–20 min.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional supervisors during the iVR session
- a larger prepared room with taped play areas and hygienic HMD covers
#### Constraints
- Equipment requires a great deal of preparation and removal time before continuing the lesson

### Target Learners
- vocational apprentices using HMDs in class

### Target Learning Goals
- smooth integration of iVR sessions into classroom teaching
