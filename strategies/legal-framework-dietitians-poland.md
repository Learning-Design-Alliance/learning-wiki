# Establish a dedicated legal framework for the dietitian profession in Poland

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends developing a legal framework for the profession of dietitians and, in line with international standards, equalising the profession with other medical workers. This would strengthen patient safety and the quality of dietary services, and relieve other healthcare professionals from compensating for the lack of structured competencies among dietitians. The recommendation responds to the finding that dietitians were not included in the 2023 Act on Certain Medical Professions.
