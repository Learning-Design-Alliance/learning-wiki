# Improve mannequin design and increase practice opportunities to enhance SBL in oral radiology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students recommended more realistic, well-maintained, and accessible simulation resources. Specific suggestions included better mannequin design ("the teeth fall out, and it's hard to adjust fingers"), more mannequins, better access to practice time, and improved film stabilization through "better holders or softer material for the mannequin's mouth." The authors conclude that improving mannequin design and providing more consolidation opportunities may further improve undergraduate learning experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient mannequin numbers and simulation lab access time to enable repetition
#### Constraints
- Increasing fidelity may increase cognitive load and contribute to learner overconfidence, per prior work cited by the authors

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- intraoral radiographic technique proficiency
