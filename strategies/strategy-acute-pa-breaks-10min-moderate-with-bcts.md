# Implement acute physical activity breaks of at least 10 minutes at moderate intensity with behavior change techniques

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 2 mixed) · 4 studies (3 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's recommendation for educational practice is to embed single bouts of physical activity in the school day, starting "with doses of at least 10 min of moderate intensity (as a minimum) and the use of behavior change techniques". It is grounded in the meta-analytic finding that behavior change techniques were the only significant moderator, and in the observation that improvements occurred with as little as a 4-minute dose. The authors flag that this advice "should be carefully considered" pending research on the optimal dose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 10 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity as a minimum dose
- Use of behavior change techniques such as prompt rewards, feedback on performance and provided instruction
#### Constraints
- The authors state this advice should be carefully considered because further high-quality research is required to decipher the optimal dose
- Moderation analyses are limited by third-order causation and likely underpowered

### Target Learners
- School-aged children and adolescents (ages 6-16 in the included studies)

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic performance in mathematics and language
- Classroom on-task behavior
