# Lower the grade impact of laboratory activities in future MOOC offerings to reduce attenuation and broaden participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' own design recommendation, drawn from YWYL: because the laboratory "represents 65% of the credit towards certiﬁcation" and the heavy emphasis on labs likely drove students away, they suggest that "lowering the grade impact of the laboratories in future offerings might lead to lower attenuation and broader participation." This is a proposed adjustment, not a tested manipulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory activities must still be central enough to the course that their grade weight is a meaningful lever on completion
#### Constraints
- Stated only as a possibility (
- might lead to
- ); the effect was not tested in this course
- Only 17% of students initially expected to spend more than nine hours on the course, against the 11-14 hours a fully engaging week requires

### Target Learners
- MOOC students in introductory physics courses with required laboratory components

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained participation and course completion across all course components, including laboratories
