Investigate limited responders' response to increasingly intensive levels of mathematics intervention within tiered systems of support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study, q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article identifies a gap: research on intensive mathematics interventions lags behind reading intervention research. It calls for studies of "limited responders' response to increasingly intensive levels of mathematics intervention (i.e., reducing group size, increasing the duration, frequency, or length of each session, and providing more explicit, systematic instruction)" and recommends funding longitudinal intervention research within tiered systems of support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research funding should target investigating the implementation of longitudinal mathematics interventions for students with MD within tiered systems of support

Constraints¶

  • In this corpus, the authors did not detect research investigating students' response to increasingly intensive levels of interventions within a tiered system of support

Target Learners¶

  • students with mathematics difficulty in grades 4 through 12 who respond poorly to initial intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics proficiency in upper elementary and secondary grades

Claims¶

  • Mathematics interventions for students with mathematics difficulty in grades 4–12 show a statistically significant pooled treatment effect of 0.85 under a random-effects model [+W]
  • Remediating mathematics difficulty for older students appears more challenging than for younger students, with lower adjusted effects than prior elementary-focused reviews [+W]
  • After adjusting for small-study effects and excluding small-sample studies, the underlying pooled effect of mathematics interventions for grades 4–12 is a moderate 0.49 [+W]
  • Interventions providing more than 15 total hours significantly moderate mathematics outcomes for students with MD in grades 4–12 [+W]
