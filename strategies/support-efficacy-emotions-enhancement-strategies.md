# Strengthen teacher support, academic self-efficacy and positive academic emotions to raise university students' achievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the mediation findings, the article recommends that educators move beyond content delivery to provide autonomy, emotional and competence support, and that institutions "prioritize comprehensive teacher training and ongoing professional development". It further recommends boosting self-efficacy through positive reinforcement, realistic achievable goals and feedback celebrating incremental achievements, and stimulating positive academic emotions via emotional literacy, supportive environments and peer collaboration.
