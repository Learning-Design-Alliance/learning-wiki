# Use an AI feedback system as a standalone self-directed training and assessment resource, freeing expert tutors for higher-value skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that because one-on-one expert tutoring is "extremely difficult to consistently replicate in the real-world ICU environment because of competing work pressures", an AI system that matches or exceeds tutoring benefits can serve as a standalone resource for self-directed practice and remote competency assessment. The expert clinical tutor "might then be freed to complement learner training, for instance in improving handling, dexterity and other value based bronchoscopic skills".
