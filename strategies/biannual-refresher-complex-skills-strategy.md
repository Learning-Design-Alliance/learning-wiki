# Schedule refresher training at biannual intervals for complex procedural skills and annually for easier ones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the observed skill decay in both groups, the authors recommend that skills taught in skills labs be refreshed periodically, with the interval scaled to skill complexity. They state: “To optimise long-term outcome, these skills should be refreshed in at least biannual intervals for more complex skills, and annually for easier tasks.” The recommendation follows directly from the trial’s finding that performance declined in nearly all students within 3 to 6 months regardless of teaching method.
