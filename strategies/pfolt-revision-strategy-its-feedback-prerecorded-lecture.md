# Revise the online LT with step-based tutoring, richer feedback, standardized pre-lecture, and reduced plotting redundancy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on instructor feedback and feedback-effectiveness literature, the authors plan specific revisions to the PFOLT. "Adding a step-based ITS would provide students with feedback and hints along each step of the problem-solving process", moving beyond the current answer-based, task-level feedback toward process and self-regulation feedback, which a meta-analysis of more than 400 studies links to greater effectiveness. They also plan to have graphs fill in automatically earlier to address plotting redundancy, and "we will embed a pre-recorded lecture video that will precede the interactive component of the activity" to standardize prior instruction.
