# Support Decoding of Text, Mathematical Notation, and Symbols

## Description
Ensuring that text and symbols do not impede the learning goal involves providing strategies and tools to help learners decode and understand written and symbolic information. This includes explicit instruction in phonics, decoding strategies, and mathematical notation. It also involves providing assistive technologies, such as screen readers and text-to-speech software, to support learners with reading difficulties. The aim is to remove barriers to learning by making information more accessible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to appropriate instructional materials and assistive technologies. Instructors need to be trained in effective decoding strategies and assistive technology.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and resources. Some learners may be resistant to using assistive technology. It's essential to provide support and encouragement.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance reading fluency, improve comprehension, support mathematical literacy, promote accessible learning

### Affordances
- Supports reading fluency by providing explicit instruction in decoding strategies, improves comprehension by helping learners understand written information, supports mathematical literacy by clarifying mathematical notation, and promotes accessible learning by catering to diverse reading and learning needs.

### Personalization
- Provide individualized instruction in phonics and decoding strategies. Offer assistive technologies, such as screen readers and text-to-speech software. Use visual aids and graphic organizers to clarify complex concepts.
