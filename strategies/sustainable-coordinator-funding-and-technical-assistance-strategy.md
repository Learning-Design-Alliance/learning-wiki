# Provide sustainable, dedicated funding for community school coordinators plus technical assistance and capacity-building

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that states provide "a sustainable, long-term, dedicated funding stream for the coordinator position," noting some states put such staffing allocations into the formula for all high-poverty schools. It further recommends that policymakers "help develop and provide resources for a stronger system of technical assistance and capacity-building efforts that lead to fidelity of practice," while allowing local flexibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stable state investment to seed, expand, and sustain the strategy
#### Constraints
- Recent funding reductions destabilized local implementation, causing reduced services, eliminated positions, and constrained programming

### Target Learners
- Students in high-poverty community schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained implementation fidelity and continued improvement in climate, attendance, achievement, and graduation outcomes
