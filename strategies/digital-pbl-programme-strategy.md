# Offer a digital PBL programme to improve uptake among coronary heart disease patients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose delivering PBL digitally to overcome barriers to attending 13 in-person sessions over a year. "One way to improve barriers to the uptake of the PBL programme is to offer a digital PBL programme." Digital delivery would enable participation by patients in suburban areas without practical or economic resources to travel, and prerecorded modules could make the programme more flexible and accessible, for example to patients of employable age.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL delivered as a pedagogy closely offered in a digital way to patients
#### Constraints
- This is a future option proposed in the discussion, not an intervention tested in this trial

### Target Learners
- patients with coronary heart disease, including those of employable age and those living far from care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- patient empowerment and secondary-prevention self-management
