# Make the undergraduate physics advisor deeply knowledgeable and constantly available, as a key retention factor

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article identifies advising as essential to a successful program: the advisor should know employment prospects, salaries, scholarships, internships, graduate schools and related practical matters, and be constantly available and willing to spend lots of time with students. In the authors' experience it is 'one of the most important f actors in retaining students once they sign up for the major.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advisor knowledge of employment, scholarships, internships, graduate opportunities and related student needs
- Constant availability and willingness to spend significant time with students
#### Constraints
- Not all faculty find this busy work palatable, the article notes

### Target Learners
- Declared and prospective undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention and completion of the physics major
