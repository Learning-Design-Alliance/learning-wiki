# Rotate students through experimental roles and run a mid-session mini-conference to build collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To practice collaboration goals, "students rotate though group roles similar to those of cooperative groups but that emphasize roles in experimental physics (a principal investigator, a science communicator, and Reviewer #2)." By formally rotating students through roles within the lab, they take on different perspectives and responsibilities in the experimentation process. Partway through the session, the instructor pauses the class for a "mini-conference" where groups share their results and models, leading to additional collaboration between groups for the second half of the lab.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formally rotating students through roles within the lab
- A mid-session instructor-led pause where groups share results and models
#### Constraints
- The roles are described for the E&M electrostatics activity; the article presents the strategy as design practice, not as a tested comparison

### Target Learners
- students working in lab groups in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- conduct an experiment collaboratively and ethically
- communicate the process and outcomes of an experiment
