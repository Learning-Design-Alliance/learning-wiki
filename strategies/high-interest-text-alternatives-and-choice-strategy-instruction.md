# Offer high-interest text alternatives and choice alongside short stories to sustain engagement during strategy instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because some students found single-theme short stories boring, the article recommends that the curriculum "should also provide high-interest alternatives and choice." The same strategies can be taught with brief topical texts such as news articles, informational passages, and infographics, graded readers or micro-stories, with varied themes and lengths and optional audio or visual scaffolds. Offering two or three thematically diverse text options per unit, pre-teaching key vocabulary, and chunking longer texts can maintain engagement while keeping strategy instruction consistent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategy instruction itself remains consistent across the alternative text options
#### Constraints
- Recommended in response to approximately 20% of respondents in this study reporting they did not enjoy short stories

### Target Learners
- EFL learners who do not engage with single-theme literary texts

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement while learning reading comprehension strategies in L2
