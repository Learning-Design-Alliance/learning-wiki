# Integrate PETs at every stage of the research lifecycle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The short-term recommendations advise researchers conducting research with DLPs to look for ways to integrate PETs into all stages of the research lifecycle: secure one-way identifiers at data collection, synthetic data for testing code at data cleaning/prep, federated learning during modeling, differential privacy for output reporting, and privacy preserving record linkage or secure multiparty computation for data sharing. The article also suggests PETs can lower barriers to data use, for example by supporting a doctoral student with a public use file while waiting for a data-use-agreement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational PET literacy built through trainings, speakers, and pilot studies
#### Constraints
- DLP data can be difficult to de-identify and integration with multiple systems compounds re-identification risk

### Target Learners
- researchers using digital learning platforms
- doctoral students awaiting data-use agreements

### Target Learning Goals
- privacy-protective research practice across the data lifecycle
