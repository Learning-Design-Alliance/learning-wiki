# Narrative Advance Organizers

## Description
Narrative advance organizers involve storytelling at the beginning of a lesson to engage students and relate to important concepts. Teachers tell an interesting story that relates to important concepts in the lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stories that relate to the learning material.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if story does not connect clearly to the lesson or if it is distracting.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly effective for making connections to students' cultural & life experiences.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Engage students at the beginning of a lesson and relate to important concepts by tapping into prior knowledge.

### Affordances
- Provides a hook for students and makes subject relatable through narrative.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to be culturally relevant stories that are tailored to the specific learning goals.
