# Conduct more rigorous studies of instructional and support strategies for adult learners

## Description
The report's forward-looking recommendation is that the field conduct "more rigorous studies" of adult education strategies. It argues this is needed because "there is not yet much evidence to guide decision making around instructional and support strategies for adult learners." The strategy is aimed at researchers and funders building the evidence base that WIOA's evidence-based encouragement presupposes.
