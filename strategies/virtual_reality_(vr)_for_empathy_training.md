# Virtual Reality (VR) for Empathy Training

## Description
VR is used to guide new workers through a virtual phone call with a Fidelity “customer” going through a financial crisis, training them about empathy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR headsets, appropriate software, and a space to practice in.
#### Constraints
- Some workers may have technical difficulties with VR.
