# Send text-message educational content at optimal times with repetition and keep messages concise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's discussion recommends that educators using text messaging attend to delivery timing, repetition, and message concision. It cites evidence that "text messages were sent at optimal intervals with predetermined times and repetition" improves retention, and concludes that character limits mean "text messaging might be of better use in delivering concise and simple educational content."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Finding optimal timing of message delivery, since busy schedules could prevent messages from being viewed
#### Constraints
- Character restrictions may lead to de-contextualization and misinterpretation of material

### Target Learners
- Residents in graduate medical education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge retention of concise educational content via mobile delivery
