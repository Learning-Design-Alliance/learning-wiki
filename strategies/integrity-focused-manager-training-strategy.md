# Develop training programs focused on improving managers' integrity to enhance subordinate work engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that integrity gains predicted subordinate work engagement gains, the authors recommend targeting integrity in manager training. They state that "a training program focused on improving the integrity of managers could enhance the work engagement of employees", and that it might be possible to develop training targeting specifically the integrity of managers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should give managers feedback on their current management competency (e.g., via the SMCIT) and opportunities to set personal goals and action plans
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from a single-group study without a control group in one company; the authors call for randomized controls and larger samples to confirm the influence of integrity

### Target Learners
- Workplace managers supervising subordinate workers

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving manager integrity behaviors to facilitate subordinate work engagement
