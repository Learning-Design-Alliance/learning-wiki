# Meaningful Grading Through Feedback

## Description
Grades should represent multiple opportunities for students to demonstrate learning based on descriptive, concrete, and actionable feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear grading policies, descriptive feedback, opportunities for students to demonstrate learning
#### Constraints
- Requires significant effort from teachers to provide detailed feedback
