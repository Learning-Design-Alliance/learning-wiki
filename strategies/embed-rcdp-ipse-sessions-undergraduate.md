# Embed short, high-frequency RCDP-based interprofessional simulation sessions early in undergraduate health curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' practice recommendation is for curriculum designers and educators "to consider embedding short, high-frequency RCDP-based interprofessional simulation sessions within undergraduate curricula to strengthen teamwork, communication, and crisis management competencies early in training." They argue early exposure may prevent development of professional stereotypes impeding teamwork, and that IPE can be implemented earlier in healthcare education than has traditionally been the case. Faculty development in RCDP and TeamSTEPPS principles is recommended to enhance implementation fidelity.
