# Rotate heterogeneous cooperative learning clusters across distinct cooperative techniques and topics throughout a unit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In experiment group 1, students were "heterogeneously assigned into clusters" and given successive scenarios aligned to lesson objectives. Five cooperative learning techniques (combining, ask together learn together, student team achievement parts, learning together, and group inquiry) were each used to teach a distinct topic, with group membership changed for each topic and seats rearranged before each lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Techniques and relevant tasks must be introduced and explained to students before the treatment
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a 4th-grade social studies unit over five weeks and 15 lesson hours

### Target Learners
- 4th grade elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Social studies unit content mastery and retention
