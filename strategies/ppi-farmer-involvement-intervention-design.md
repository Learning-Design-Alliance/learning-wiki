# Involve the target learners (patients/public) in developing, validating, delivering, and interpreting the educational intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Following the GRIPP2-SF framework, the protocol engages farmers, agricultural officers, and community representatives through focus group discussions to shape study design, intervention content, and outcome measures. Farmers will "develop and validate the educational intervention based on their lived experiences with pesticide use", public representatives support recruitment and give feedback on practicality, and findings are disseminated through village community meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured PPI per GRIPP2-SF; focus group discussions; documentation of PPI benefits and challenges for future research
#### Constraints
- Applied in a rural farmer population in Karnataka; the protocol states reflections on PPI impact will be documented but not yet reports outcomes

### Target Learners
- Farmers and community members in rural agricultural districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Contextually relevant, acceptable pesticide-safety education
