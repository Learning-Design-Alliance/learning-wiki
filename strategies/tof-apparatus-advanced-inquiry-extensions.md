# Extend the sound-speed apparatus with phase analysis, temperature studies, and known gas mixtures for advanced inquiry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors outline follow-up investigations that build on the basic measurement. "Phase analysis between the two recorded waveforms can be implemented to determine the time delay with greater accuracy or to examine subtle waveform spreading"; adding a thermometer enables systematic studies of temperature dependence, and gas mixtures in known proportions let students explore how molecular composition affects acoustic propagation. These are pitched especially at college-level coursework as an introduction to signal processing.

## Design Implications
