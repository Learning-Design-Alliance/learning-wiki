# Four-component evidence-based selection framework for surgical trainee recruitment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 unmarked) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a minimum standard for selection to surgical training that can operate in centralized and decentralized systems and under either employment or educational logic. It specifies four components: "1) ensuring multiple candidates to select from per position through wide advertisement and recruitment efforts; 2) conducting multiple mini interviews (MMI) or structured interviews by at least two assessors; 3) applying situational judgment testing (SJT); 4) conduct structured validated assessments during job trial periods." The framework is offered as an improvement toward evidence-based selection across the EU.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple candidates per position achieved through wide advertisement and recruitment efforts
- At least two assessors for MMI or structured interviews
#### Constraints
- The optimal weighting between the different instruments is stated to be unresolved
- Evidence on technical skill and aptitude assessment at selection is described as still equivocal

### Target Learners
- applicants to specialist surgical training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- selecting candidates with the capabilities needed to complete surgical specialty training
