# Have students encounter varied situations in which magnetic flux, not just magnetic field, changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that instruction present students with a variety of situations in which the magnetic flux rather than only the magnetic field changes. For each situation students "make the same sort of predictions, test them in the lab, and then discuss the results." This targets the finding that students could say a changing magnetic field induces a current without realizing this is because the flux is changing, including rotating-coil and shrinking-loop cases.
