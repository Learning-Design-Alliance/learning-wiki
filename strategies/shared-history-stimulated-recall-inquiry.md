# Shared history feedback inquiry using stimulated recall of time-lapse replays

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each week before new drawings, the previous week's time-lapse negatives are projected larger than life size and the artist is asked questions about how the drawings were made. The report describes that "the time-lapse negatives are projected larger than life size on the wall.At this time the history of each drawing is shared through the stimulated recall provided by the replay of the drawing processes from the week before." Questions aim to encourage recollection and make indirect contact with the creating stream of consciousness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
