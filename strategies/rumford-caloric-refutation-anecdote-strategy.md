# Use historical refutation anecdotes (Count Rumford and the caloric theory) to teach the nature of heat

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The notes present the discarded caloric theory of heat as a fluid running from hot body to cooler body, then overturn it with a historical episode: the theory "fell apart with the observation of Count Rumford: that by grinding away at the cannon barrel he was able to produce unlimited amount of heat". This shows heat can be created by doing work, leading into the mutual convertibility of heat and work and the impossibility of 100% conversion to useful work.
