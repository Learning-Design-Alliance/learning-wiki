## Description
The article's training intervention combined theoretical and practical sessions in prompt engineering. Teachers "were trained in the GPEI me thodology (Goal Prompt Evaluation Iteration)", alongside "a set of prompting techniques such as The Persona Pattern, The Interview Pattern, and Chain-of-Thought". The training ran for three weeks with two two-hour sessions per week, and this intervention was associated with improved teacher perceptions of ChatGPT.
