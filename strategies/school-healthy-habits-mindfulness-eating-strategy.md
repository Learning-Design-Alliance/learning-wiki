# School-based recommendations: teach healthy habits, mindfulness practice and healthy eating

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article closes with four school-level suggestions arising from its findings: "teach healthy habits: schools should teach students how to eat better and stay focused", "practice mindfulness: schools could teach students simple ways to stay calm and focused", "encourage healthy eating: schools should encourage students to eat well", and continued research on how food and focus affect school performance. These follow from the study's associations between mindfulness, junk food consumption, BMI and academic performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools able to integrate nutrition education and mindfulness practice into their curriculum, as Bo Kluea School already integrated mindfulness practices into its curriculum
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on a single-school cross-sectional study; the authors state further research is needed to validate the findings

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in grades 8–11

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance
- healthy eating habits
- focus and calm through mindfulness practice
