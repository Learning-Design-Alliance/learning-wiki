# Teaching-learning laboratory in which pre-service teachers study high school students' conceptions while reflecting on their own

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors plan a teaching-learning laboratory for pre-service teachers built on this study's findings, in which participants "can actively study high school students' conceptions while reflecting on their own." In the course preparing pre-service teachers for the lab, high-school students' conceptions are the basis for the pre-service teachers' own recognition of these conceptions. The authors state this design encourages the lab to function in a way that is rooted in conceptual change and will contribute to improving the structure of university nuclear physics courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The accompanying course must make high-school students' conceptions the basis for the pre-service teachers' own recognition of these conceptions.
#### Constraints
- The design rests on findings from 13 pre-service teachers at two universities; the sample was not randomly selected and is not representative of the total population of pre-service teachers in Germany.

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers in physics teacher education

### Target Learning Goals
- recognizing and reflecting on learners' conceptions of radioactivity
