# Process for Constructive Decision-Making

## Description
Brainstorm with students a process for constructive decision-making and display it in the classroom as a visual reminder of how to make good choices. The process involves steps such as staying calm, observing and identifying the problem, stopping to think and analyze the situation, capturing thoughts as you consider choices and consequences, making a choice to solve the problem, and evaluating and reflecting on the outcome.

### Context
#### Requirements
- A process for constructive decision-making, a way to display the process in the classroom, and a willingness to help students develop constructive decision-making skills.
