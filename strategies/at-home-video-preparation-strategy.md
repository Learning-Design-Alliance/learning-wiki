# Offer instructional videos as advance preparation for skills-lab learning stations, with students watching at home and practising hands-on at the station

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports a blended preparation strategy in which students watch short instructional videos at home on tablets or laptops before attending a skills-lab learning station for hands-on practice. The authors state that "The approach of most skills labs to offer instructional videos as preparation for the learning stations [30] is well-founded" and recommend production "should be continued in order to make high-quality educational material available".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-quality video production by the university; easy public access via a dedicated channel; videos aligned with the specific learning stations.
#### Constraints
- The program could be promoted more heavily since students view the videos mostly sporadically; adding preclinical-topic videos is suggested to attract preclinical students.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate veterinary students

### Target Learning Goals
- practical clinical skills preparation and repetition before hands-on practice on models
