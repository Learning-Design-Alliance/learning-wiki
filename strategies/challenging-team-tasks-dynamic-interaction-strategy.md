# Set team task difficulty so students must solve tasks through dynamic interaction rather than simple distribution of shares

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that professors set the difficulty level of team tasks so that low-level topics cannot be completed by merely distributing each member's share. Low-level topic selection, it argues, "can stop at the stage of 'distribution' of each member's share rather than moving forward to the stage of 'cooperation and collaboration' through active interaction among students." This addresses the observed segmentation of work among freshmen.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor control over the difficulty and cooperation demands of team topics
#### Constraints
- Some topics may be completable with several discussions and simple decision-making depending on what teams select

### Target Learners
- Premedical and medical students in team-based classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Collaboration and active interaction among team members

### Affordances
- Freshman Team Relationship Formation Framework
