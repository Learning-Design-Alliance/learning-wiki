# Use structured formative supports such as guided self-assessment checklists, benchmark examples, and mid-course feedback to scaffold novice self-evaluation in ultrasound training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students' perceived goal attainment did not reliably reflect their objectively assessed OSCE performance, the article recommends explicit instructional supports. It suggests "guided self-assessment checklists [25], benchmark examples of performance [26], or mid-course feedback sessions [27]" that "scaffold students' ability to evaluate their own competence and regulate subsequent learning." This addresses novices' underdeveloped self-awareness and calibration in early ultrasound training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured formative support mechanisms embedded in the ultrasound curriculum
- Feedback opportunities during, not only after, training
#### Constraints
- The article states repeated practice alone should not be assumed to lead to accurate self-evaluation

### Target Learners
- Novice ultrasound learners
- Undergraduate health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate self-assessment and calibration of procedural competence
- Self-regulated learning in ultrasound skill acquisition
