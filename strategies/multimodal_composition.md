# Multimodal Composition

## Description
Multimodal composition involves creating texts using various modes of communication such as images, words, sound, and movement. Writers compose slide shows, videos, audio documentaries, brochures, Tweets, Web sites, and much more. Digital literacy is the ability to use digital technology, communication tools or networks to locate, evaluate, use and create information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to computers, multimedia software, and internet connectivity. Training in various composition techniques is also beneficial.
#### Constraints
- Requires technical proficiency, can be time-intensive, and may require access to specific platforms or software.
