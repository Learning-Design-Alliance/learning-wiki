Description¶

The article's stated approach is to "review the accommodations landscape— discover the past, highlight our present progress, and uncover new areas to explore." This three-part review strategy — historical development, current state, and future directions — is how the article organizes its examination of accommodation policy change. It is intended to help readers understand where accommodation policy has been and where it is heading.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reviewers need access to the history of accommodation policies, current practice, and emerging technology trends to carry out the three-part review.

Constraints¶

  • The article notes the landscape is everchanging, so any review reflects a moving target rather than a settled state.
