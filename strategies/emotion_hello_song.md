# Emotion Hello Song

## Description
The Emotion Hello Song involves singing a greeting song with different emotional expressions. This helps learners start the day by identifying and expressing their emotions, fostering emotional awareness and connection with peers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires basic musical skills or access to a pre-made Emotion Hello Song. A facilitator to lead the singing and discussion.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be shy about singing or expressing emotions publicly. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Personalization
- Emotions can be chosen by learners or based on current events. Lyrics and actions can be adapted to suit different cultures and experiences.

## Impact
