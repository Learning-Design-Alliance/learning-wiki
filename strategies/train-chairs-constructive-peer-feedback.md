# Train department Chairs and Directors on providing peer-evaluation feedback in a constructive and motivating manner

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that clinical settings embarking on peer evaluation train their leaders in feedback delivery skills. It is "highly recommended for clinical settings that would like to embark on peer evaluation exercises to train their Chairs and Directors on the appropriate skills and communication techniques that would empower them to skillfully provide their staff with transparent feedback." In this study, the ED Chairperson met each physician individually, reviewed anonymous nurse evaluations, listened to the physician's feedback, and developed an action plan for improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback providers need considerable skill and experience in human performance appraisal to avoid putting physicians on the defensive
- Engaging physicians in the implementation process supports subsequent willingness to receive feedback
#### Constraints
- Derogatory or strongly offensive language was removed from open-ended comments before sharing, and qualitative comments were kept outside physicians' permanent performance records

### Target Learners
- Department Chairs
- Clinical Directors
- ED physicians receiving feedback

### Target Learning Goals
- Delivering constructive performance feedback
- Maintaining a collaborative work environment
