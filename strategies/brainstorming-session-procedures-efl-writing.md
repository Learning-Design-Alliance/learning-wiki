# Implement brainstorming sessions in EFL writing lessons through small monitored groups with clarified problems, open expression, deferred judgment and fixed timing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete procedure for running brainstorming in writing instruction, drawn from El-Sobery (2001). Students are divided into small monitored groups, the problem is introduced and clarified, every student is encouraged to express opinions, and "Warning against pre-formed judgments of others onions" guards against premature evaluation. Sessions have a fixed beginning and end, and students answer follow-up questions after the lesson.
