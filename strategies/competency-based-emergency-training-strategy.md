# Adopt competency-based education and rebuilt training resources to strengthen technical emergency competences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students lack technical competences while rating attitudes highly, the authors recommend curriculum-level action: "competency-based education is suggested for the core curriculum, and a functioning training resource platforms and program should be rebuild to provide core curriculum settings and teaching material". Educational strategy should combine formal training with on-the-job experience. The 43-competence framework serves as the reference for targeting these learning and training activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use the Delphi-derived 43-competence framework as the basis for core curriculum settings and teaching material
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center, self-reported survey; the authors state more work is needed to recruit participants from other institutes and regions to generalize the findings

### Target Learners
- undergraduate public health students in medical colleges

### Target Learning Goals
- practical knowledge and skills in field investigation, crisis awareness, and emergency response competences
