# Use patient-specific CT-derived cardiac models for preoperative simulation, training, and patient education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As future work, the authors propose obtaining clearer CT imaging of the mitral valve's chordae and leaflets to build patient-specific cardiac models enabling optimal preoperative simulation and training and helping educate patients, co-medical staff, and less experienced operators planning repairs of complicated diseases such as Barlow's disease. They frame this as important "in the era when the learning curve no longer becomes acceptable to patients, facilities, or the public."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed CT data showing heart details; a method to obtain the clearest image of the mitral valve by CT scan, which the authors had only started to consider
#### Constraints
- A CT scan can clearly demonstrate the aortic valve but not the mitral valve and tricuspid valve, particularly the chordae and leaflets

### Target Learners
- less experienced operators
- patients
- co-medical staff

### Target Learning Goals
- preoperative planning and simulation of patient-specific repair procedures; patient understanding of their disease and treatment
