# Teach concept map construction through a graduated briefing sequence before independent use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a stepwise introduction to concept mapping for EFL reading classes: introduce the technique, analyze a passage with a parallel teacher-made map, fill in an incomplete teacher-constructed map, then construct maps independently by extracting main ideas into boxes, connecting them with arrows, and labeling the arrows with linking phrases. Learners then compare their maps with the teacher's and "Exchanging their constructed maps and discussing on controversies". Maps are built with Cmap tools version 4.09 and compared to identify premises, reasoning, and inferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two briefing sessions before independent map construction
- Reading passages with corresponding teacher-constructed maps for the scaffolded steps
#### Constraints
- The study implemented this over 22 two-hour sessions with upper-intermediate and advanced learners; effects in other proficiency levels or skills are untested

### Target Learners
- upper-intermediate and advanced EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- identifying main ideas and rhetorical relationships in texts
