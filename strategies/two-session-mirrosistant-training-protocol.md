# Two-session Mirrosistant protocol: 2D shape tracing then 3D raw-egg preparation under indirect vision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable training recipe consists of two 60-minute sessions with Mirrosistant. Session 1 asks students to trace the edge and fill in the blank of prescribed shapes (G. V. Black's classification of cavities) using indirect vision, where "the edge tracing may improve the sense of orientation using indirect vision". Session 2 asks students to prepare a specified figure on raw eggs with a handpiece under indirect vision, with depth feedback: too shallow leaves shell, too deep perforates the egg. This progresses from two-dimensional perceptual practice to three-dimensional operative practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mirrosistant device (or daily-life materials to simulate it), dental mirror, handpiece, test sheets of prescribed cavity shapes, raw eggs
- Sessions completed within 60 min each
#### Constraints
- The device supports only vertical and facing reflecting angles; the article states skills acquired should also be assessed through objective evaluation of dental procedures in the future

### Target Learners
- second-year dental students in a hand skill course

### Target Learning Goals
- mirror perceptual skills (direction and distance perception)
- indirect-vision operative skills and dental fulcrum sensation
