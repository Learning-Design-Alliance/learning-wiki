# Use the VP implementation model to plan encoded design features and constructed delivery activity for a given topic and audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article offers the model as a practical planning tool for authors, commissioners and researchers. "Developers can consider which design features and electronic presentation to adopt, the 'encoded activity', and how they should be delivered, the 'constructed activity'." Combined with attention to student preconditions such as electronic prejudice, the framework is intended to elucidate how a VP might be developed or repurposed for any given topic, and to guide planning and transparent reporting of new research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consideration of student preconditions, technical facilities, curricular integration and the delivery environment alongside case authoring
#### Constraints
- The article states design-variable decisions cannot be generalised from the model alone but must be contextualised to the students and constructed activity

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- allied health professional students on Masters-level courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- effective engagement with virtual patient cases
