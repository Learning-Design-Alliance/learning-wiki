# Extension exercises building on the sundial data: daytime length, solar-day duration, azimuth check, and the analemma

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The conclusion proposes further student activities in the form of exercises: using equation (B.4) to calculate daytime length versus latitude and compare with calendar ephemerides; showing that the duration of a solar day is (1 + dE/dt) x 86,400 s and finding its extremes; using a fixed-base sundial to check experimentally that the Sun's azimuth at maximal elevation is always South; and plotting the analemma traced by the shadow end at mean local noon. The author writes that "The above measurements can be the basis of further activities for students".
