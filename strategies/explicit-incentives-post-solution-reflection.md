# Give students explicit incentives to reflect after solving physics problems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the survey showed a general lack of reflection after problem solving in every group, the article recommends that "instructors should consider giving students explicit incentive to reflect after they solve physics problems". Reflection is framed as essential for learning from problem solving, including asking what was learned, why one principle worked and another did not, and how to recognize when the same principle applies to a new situation. The article also recommends including both quantitative and conceptual questions to motivate reflection and help students develop intuition about the equations underlying problems.
