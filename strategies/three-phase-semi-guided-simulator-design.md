# Three-phase semi-guided exploration design for teaching energy with a simulator

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a didactic design in three phases: a teacher-run demonstrative class with open questions and group hypothesis debate, an instructional video on instrumental use hosted in Moodle, and an individual inquisitive questionnaire promoting exploration. The design intends "promover el aprendizaje colaborativo y exploratorio" and to question prior misconceptions while fostering student–simulator interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the simulator, a Moodle platform for the instructional video, and group work in the demonstrative phase
#### Constraints
- Designed for energy concepts (gravitational potential, kinetic, thermal energy and conservation) in a general physics course

### Target Learners
- university technical-degree students in mechanics and related programs

### Target Learning Goals
- gravitational potential energy
- kinetic energy
- thermal energy
- conservation of energy
