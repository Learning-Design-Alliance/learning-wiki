# Apply Student-Centered Strategies to Support Learning Differences

## Description
This strategy involves using a data-driven, student-centered plan to implement specific instructional strategies that address a selected student's learning differences (including strengths and challenges).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data on student learning differences (strengths and challenges), a student-centered plan, and specific instructional strategies.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful data collection, analysis, and the development of a tailored instructional plan.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Support students with learning differences by leveraging their strengths and addressing their challenges through targeted instructional strategies.

### Affordances
- Helps educators personalize instruction to meet the specific needs of students, leveraging their strengths to support their learning.

### Personalization
- Strategies are tailored to the individual student's data, including strengths, challenges, and learning preferences.
