# Accompany blended learning activities with immediate formative feedback and mode-appropriate activity variety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the researchers recommend that when using blended learning, "emphasis should be focused on activities", that various activities should suit face-to-face and online modes respectively, and that "Activities should be accompanied by immediate formative feedback". They also recommend that higher education institutions consider activity-based blended learning and that teachers be equipped with implementation skills through pre-service and in-service programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in activity-based approaches via pre-service and in-service programs; training packages may be developed
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a single small quasi-experiment (N = 52) in one course at one university

### Target Learners
- prospective teachers in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- achievement in educational technology
- motivation to learn
