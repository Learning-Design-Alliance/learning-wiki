# Robust governance frameworks, faculty development, equitable access, and rigorous validation required for successful AI integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that despite advantages of AI-based technologies, "Successful integration will require robust governance frameworks, faculty development, equitable access, rigorous validation, and continuous evaluation" to ensure ethical, effective, and sustainable implementation. Faculty need AI literacy, data interpretation skills, and pedagogical adaptation through structured development programs. Institutions must address resistance to adoption, workflow redesign, and the digital divide between resource-rich and resource-poor settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development programs with structured training in AI tools, learning analytics, and digital pedagogy; institutional leadership support; clear policies
#### Constraints
- Limited digital infrastructure particularly in developing nations; resource disparities create a digital divide between tertiary hospitals and primary care settings

### Target Learners
- medical faculty
- institutional leaders
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective AI-enabled teaching
- sustainable digital transformation
