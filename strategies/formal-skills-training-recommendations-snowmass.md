# Graduate programs should provide formal training modes for skills where self-teaching is inadequate, including a standard undergraduate statistics course and industry partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that physics programs replace inadequate informal skill acquisition with formal mechanisms: "Graduate programs in particle physics should support more formal modes of training for those skills where self-teaching is inadequate, in favor of the preferred mode as indicated in Table III." It further recommends that "Physics departments should consider making a course in statistics a standard part of the undergraduate physics curriculum", and that programs normalize industry training via partnerships such as summer research internships. Advisors should actively encourage use of these resources as part of graduate training.
