# Technology Accessibility

## Description
Classrooms that have a variety of media allow for different learning activities and might include a projector, a white board, digital cameras, laptops, satellite TV, video production, and even Ipad accessibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of technology options such as computers, tablets, and projectors. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the technology.

### Affordances
- Promotes engagement and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred technology. Provide a variety of technology options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more fun.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that technology can increase engagement, improve learning outcomes, and enhance collaboration. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
