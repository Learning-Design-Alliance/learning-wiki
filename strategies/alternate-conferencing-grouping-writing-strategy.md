# Alternate conferencing with grouping and incorporate peer review and editing in collaborative writing tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that conferencing alone is not a productive writing strategy and that the experimental class succeeded by alternating it with grouping. Grouping splits participants into cells assigned specific tasks within a time frame, during which issues are explained, decisions summarised, paragraphs revised and sections edited. The article states "it is not productive to adopt conferencing alone as a writing strategy" and describes how the experimental class alternated conferencing with grouping while using explaining, summarising, peer review and editing at each stage.

#### Requirements
