# Set clear numeric educator-diversity goals at the state, district, and teacher preparation levels with public progress reporting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second policy lever is to "Create statewide goals to strengthen the teacher workforce" that districts can align with and report progress against, while encouraging educator preparation programs to set recruitment and retention goals for teacher candidates of color. Arkansas is highlighted for a numeric goal to increase teachers of color in public schools by 25% by 2025 and a requirement that all districts post a recruitment and retention plan.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Statewide goals must be public so districts can align and report progress; districts must create and post recruitment and retention plans
