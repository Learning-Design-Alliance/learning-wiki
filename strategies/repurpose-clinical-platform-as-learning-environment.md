# Repurpose a clinical chronic disease management platform as an interactive learning environment in residency training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating a digital chronic disease management platform into residency rotations so that trainees manage real-time digitized patient profiles, monitor dynamic blood pressure trajectories, use embedded clinical decision support, and construct management plans under structured supervision. The authors argue that "a clinically authentic hypertension management platform can be repurposed as an interactive learning environment, extending the role of digital health infrastructure beyond direct patient care into graduate medical education."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty orientation on platform functionality and debriefing rubrics before implementation
- Structured supervision with periodic review of resident digital management entries and weekly debriefs
- Platform usage log audits to ensure intervention fidelity
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a retrospective, non-randomized single-department cohort; the authors call for prospective multicenter randomized studies to establish causal effects and evaluate long-term competency retention

### Target Learners
- resident physicians in standardized residency training

### Target Learning Goals
- longitudinal data-driven chronic disease management
- digital health competencies
- self-directed learning
- clinical competence
