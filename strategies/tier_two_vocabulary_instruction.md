# Tier Two Vocabulary Instruction

## Description
Focuses on teaching high-frequency words used by mature language users that can add significantly to language ability. This involves identifying Tier Two words in texts, selecting a manageable number of words to teach, and ensuring that learners understand the concepts represented by these words. Instruction includes helping learners express ideas using more precise and mature vocabulary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Texts suitable for the target grade level, teacher knowledge of Tier Two vocabulary, and instructional time for vocabulary activities.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of words to avoid overwhelming learners, and instruction must be engaging and meaningful to ensure retention.
