# Integrate targeted psychosocial support to mitigate anxiety and loneliness within physiotherapy education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that physiotherapy programs embed psychological-health and social-support measures because anxiety and loneliness were the psychosocial factors most strongly linked to lower GPA. As the conclusion states, "Improving the academic success and well-being of physiotherapy students may require targeted interventions to mitigate anxiety and loneliness", and it adds that "integrating psychosocial support within physiotherapy education may play a crucial role not only in academic success". The recommendation is framed as a potential improvement pending further research.
