Use the EPIS model's four stages (Exploration, Preparation, Implementation, Sustainment) to bring an educational technology intervention to scale in elementary schools¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy organizes scale-up of a research-developed intervention through the stages of the Exploration, Preparation, Implementation, Sustainment framework. The authors "draw from the Exploration, Preparation, Implementation, Sustainment Model (EPIS...) to outline a set of practices and procedures for supporting the implementation of A2i within kindergarten through third grade classrooms with high percentages of ELs". Concretely, they met principals to build partnership, ran kick-off trainings and monthly grade-level communities of practice, observed classrooms and coached teachers, and fostered sustainment by ensuring continued access and support without extensive researcher involvement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partnership-building with school principals and district leaders at the exploration stage
  • Initial teacher training plus personalized, continuous professional development during implementation

Constraints¶

  • Communities of practice meetings were facilitated only at school sites local to the research team

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten through third grade students, including English learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early literacy outcomes including word reading, vocabulary, and reading comprehension

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustain and resource statewide PBIS scale-up with emphasis on advanced-tier implementation support
  • Combine bottom-up co-design with top-down executive sponsorship, cross-unit collaboration and dedicated R&D capacity to embed institutional GenAI
