## Description
The article recommends incorporating physical quantities students already know from prior instruction into calculus instruction, framed through the FTC as change, rates of change, and accumulations. Table 2 provides worked mappings: change in velocity as an infinite sum of acceleration × time interval, displacement as velocity × time interval, work as force × displacement, and impulse as force × time interval. The strategy also calls for learning environments where students spend meaningful time on amount, change, rate of change, interval, and accumulation in contexts that matter to them, and prioritize conceptual understanding over procedural speed.
