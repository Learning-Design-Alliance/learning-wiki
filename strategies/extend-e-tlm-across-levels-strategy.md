# Extend multimedia e-TLM packages across subjects, school levels and adult education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's educational implications recommend widening the use of multimedia e-TLM beyond the study setting: "Using Multimedia e-TLM package can be used for learning different subjects and it can be extended to primary level, secondary level and higher secondary level." Further recommendations include adult education, teacher education, alternative schools, and support for slow learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation of a multimedia e-TLM package as modelled in the study
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single small quasi-experiment in vocabulary learning at standard VI

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary pupils
- adult learners
- slow learners
- teacher trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary and subject learning across the curriculum
