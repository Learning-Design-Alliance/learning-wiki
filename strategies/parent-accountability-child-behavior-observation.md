# Parents hold experts and teachers accountable by observing changes in their children's behavior

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that parents judge whether their funds are properly used by observing their children: parents "may regularly inquire about the opinions of students concerning performance of teachers" and observe whether proposed activities materialize in improvements visible in explicit statements children make at home and outside school. Services to teachers "are ultimately assessed by parents on the basis of changes in behavior of children." The author also states informing parents about changes in school practice is very important locally, with monitored indicators including changes in children's behavior, parent-teacher communication, and quality of activities.
