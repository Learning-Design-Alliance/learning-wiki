# Alphabet Books

## Description
Alphabet books involve students creating entries for each letter of the alphabet related to a specific topic, such as mathematics vocabulary. This strategy introduces students to a delightful genre while helping them develop essential vocabulary for mathematical thinking. The format invites the use of words, graphic representations, and symbols to communicate mathematical ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials for creating pages (paper, art supplies), access to textbooks, thesauruses, and math dictionaries, and a way to compile the book.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to resources and time for students to research and create their entries. May be challenging for learners with limited vocabulary.
