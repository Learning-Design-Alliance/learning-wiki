Teach adults the 3Rs (Recognize, Reframe, Respond) and 2Ss (Scaffold, Stretch) for temperament-matched behavior management¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The INSIGHTS adult-facing curriculum trains teachers and parents in the 3Rs: "Recognize differences in children's temperaments", "Reframe their perspectives so that each temperament has strengths", and differentiate caregiver responses that are optimal, adequate, or counter-productive. The 2Ss add scaffolding a child in temperament-challenging situations and, if manageable with support, gently stretching the child to better regulate emotional, attentional, and behavioral reactions. The 2Cs address compliance and competence, including contracting with children who have repetitive behavior problems.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Adults (teachers and caregivers) participate in the INSIGHTS training alongside the children's classroom curriculum

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten and early elementary teachers and caregivers of low-income urban children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • temperament-aware behavior management
  • supporting children's self-regulation

Affordances¶

  • Goodness Of Fit Temperament Classroom Framework

Claims¶

  • INSIGHTS produced differential benefits for children with high-maintenance temperaments in behavior problems, engagement, and off-task behavior [+W]
  • INSIGHTS produced significant growth effects for high-maintenance children in reducing behavior problems and off-task behaviors over time [+W]
  • INSIGHTS showed no significant main effects on behavioral engagement or off-task behaviors for the full sample [~W]
  • INSIGHTS slowed growth in behavior problems for the full sample of low-income kindergarten and first-grade students [+W]
  • Improvements in student-teacher relationship quality partially mediated INSIGHTS effects on behavior problems and off-task behaviors, but not on engagement, for high-maintenance children [+W]
