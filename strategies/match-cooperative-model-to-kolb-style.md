# Match cooperative learning model to learning style: GI for accommodators, STAD for assimilators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that faculty accommodate learning-style differences when designing instruction, concluding that "the students with accommodator characteristi cs are recommended to be learned with cooperative learning model type of GI (Group of Investigation), whereas assimilator learning style students are advised to be learned by means of cooperative learning model type STAD". It also suggests seminars and training so lecturers can implement GI appropriately and identify student style characteristics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers need sufficient knowledge and skills, supported by seminars and training, to implement GI appropriately and effectively
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in one quasi-experimental study of one course at one university and the author advises results be interpreted with vigilance

### Target Learners
- University students with accommodator or assimilator learning styles

### Target Learning Goals
