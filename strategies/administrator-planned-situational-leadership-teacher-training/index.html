Administrators should plan teacher training in the situational leadership approach¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Because applying four distinct styles is hard for individual teachers, the article recommends institutional support: "educational administrators are recommended to chalk out plans for the training of teachers under this model to get the improved performance of students, a major national education objective". The strategy treats situational-style teaching as a school-level capability built through planned professional development rather than an individual improvisation. It follows directly from the study's finding that readiness-matched styles improved achievement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educational administrators must allocate plans and resources for training teachers in the four-style model

Constraints¶

  • Evidence comes from one grade-8 private girls' school; the article calls for extended and varied samples from public as well as private schools

Target Learners¶

  • school students taught by trained teachers, exemplified by grade-8 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved student performance through situational teaching

Affordances¶

  • Hersey Blanchard Situational Leadership Model

Claims¶

  • Matching teaching style to students' readiness level raised grade-8 science achievement relative to traditional teaching [+W]
  • One-way ANOVA showed significant differences in pretest and posttest performance across control and the four style categories [+W]
  • Grade-8 students could be classified into the four Hersey and Blanchard readiness levels using a percentage-based questionnaire [+W]
  • Pretest-posttest gains were largest in the telling and selling (low-readiness) style categories compared with participating and delegating [+W]
  • Posttest achievement of the experimental group exceeded the control group except for delegating and telling, and delegating, participating and selling exceeded telling [+W]
  • The selling (R2, unable but willing) category showed the highest performance impact among the intervention categories [+W]
