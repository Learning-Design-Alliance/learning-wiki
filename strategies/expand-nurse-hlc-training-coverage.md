# Prioritize expanding health literacy and competency training coverage, focusing on knowledge and inter-professional collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the survey finding that nearly half of nurses had no relevant training and that training experience independently predicts HLC, the authors recommend that hospital administrators expand training coverage first, then optimize content and methods. "Expanding training coverage is the primary task, followed by optimizing training content and methods." Targeted programs should focus on "health literacy knowledge and inter-professional collaboration," the two weakest dimensions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires hospital administrators to regularly assess nurses' HLC using a validated instrument such as the HLC-N
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design precludes causal inference; the recommendation rests on observational associations

### Target Learners
- in-service registered nurses in primary, secondary, and tertiary hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- health literacy knowledge
- inter-professional collaboration competency
