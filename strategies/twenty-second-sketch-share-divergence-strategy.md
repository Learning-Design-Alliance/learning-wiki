# Twenty-second sketch-and-share group divergence strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy encourages students to think beyond common solutions and explore different ideas from those of their fellow classmates by rewarding divergence with points. It leads into the Five Design-Sheet method, which refines many sketched solutions into a final proposed idea.
