# Nurture intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation and foster behavioural and emotional engagement to harness peer-achievement spillover

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because ACE in L2 classrooms is stronger among students high in stimulation-oriented intrinsic motivation and in behavioural and emotional engagement, the authors recommend cultivating these psychological resources alongside attention to classroom composition. They write that "educators can harness the motivational benefits of social contagion and optimize the impact of peer achievement on individual achievement" by nurturing these factors. Concrete means suggested include engaging language learning activities, choice and autonomy support in L2 tasks, and a supportive climate encouraging active participation and peer interaction.
