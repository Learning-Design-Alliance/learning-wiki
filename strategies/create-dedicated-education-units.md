# Create dedicated education units at clinical learning sites to enhance clinical teaching and learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies "Creating dedicated education units at clinical learning sites" as a key strategy, reported by three studies as "a key way of enhancing clinical teaching and learning in undergraduate nursing education". DEUs involve nurses at clinical sites being directly involved in student education, with teaching staff support, fostering academic–practice partnerships and critical thinking. The review also notes reported challenges including untrained DEU nurses and overcrowding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- DEU nurses willing and prepared to take responsibility for students; support from teaching staff on managing students
#### Constraints
- Challenges reported include DEU nurses not trained or committed to student responsibilities, mismatched schedules, overcrowding, and limited recognition of DEU nurses

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competence
- professional socialisation
