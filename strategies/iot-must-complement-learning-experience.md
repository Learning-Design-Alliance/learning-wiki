# Design IoT devices so they complement the learning experience rather than merely accomplish specified tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that in education the concern is ensuring the device complements the learning experience by communicating information in an effective and meaningful way, not just performing specified actions. It warns a shallow or impaired education system based on technology may cause severe harm, and gives examples of malfunctioning devices: automated mobile devices causing confusion, a classroom camera causing accidents, or a smart thermostat causing overheating.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- IoT must be taken into account in the design processes to help ensure success; guidelines and cases for successful and effective utilization are needed
#### Constraints
- The article notes certification, testing and validation, and legal aspects of IoT devices in educational contexts were not discussed

### Target Learners
- students in IoT-equipped learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and meaningful communication of learning content
