# Incorporate learning about semantic qualifiers into medical curricula alongside CDSS training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "medical curricula should emphasize the importance of SQs when using a CDSS" and that "integrating CDSS training with clinical reasoning education may ensure that students develop both the technical and cognitive skills necessary for effective diagnostic processes". Specifically, students could learn about SQs and disease concepts simultaneously, improving diagnostic accuracy among residents and novice physicians and motivating effective CDSS use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must be able to teach conversion of patient symptoms into medical terminology usable by information technology tools
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single-institution trial with paper case vignettes; real-world settings with patients' own words were not evaluated

### Target Learners
- medical students
- residents
- novice physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- setting appropriate semantic qualifiers
- effective CDSS use
- diagnostic accuracy
