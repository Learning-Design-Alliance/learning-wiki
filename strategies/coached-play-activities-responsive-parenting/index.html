Use coached, developmentally appropriate play activities with caregivers to promote responsive parenting in low-resource settings¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's intervention strategy has primary caregivers instructed to try an age-appropriate activity with the child while community health workers coach them and provide feedback to enhance interaction quality, reinforced through monthly group community meetings. The authors report this approach produced sustained impacts on social skills and behavior problems in the preschool period and suggest "parenting interventions can have lasting and meaningful impacts on children's school readiness, even in highly disadvantaged contexts where parents have low levels of education themselves".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery through trained local workers within existing health services, with coaching and feedback on caregiver behaviors during activities

Constraints¶

  • Studied in a rural, disadvantaged district of Sindh province with low maternal literacy (68% illiterate mothers) and high poverty; effects on behavior problems operated only indirectly through home environment quality

Target Learners¶

  • primary caregivers with low education and literacy in low- and middle-income rural contexts, and their children from birth to age four

Target Learning Goals¶

  • responsive parenting behaviors
  • children's social-emotional skills and school readiness

Claims¶

  • Home environment quality mediates the intervention's effects on children's social-emotional outcomes at age four [+W]
  • Home environment quality at age four predicts children's behavior problems and social skills [+W]
  • The responsive stimulation intervention is directly associated with children's social skills but not behavior problems at age four [+W]
  • A responsive stimulation intervention delivered from birth to age two is significantly associated with home environment quality at age two in rural Pakistan [+W]
  • The zero-to-two responsive stimulation intervention shows sustained impacts on social skills and behavior problems into the preschool period despite severe disadvantage [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Scaffold infant participation in everyday activities with positive affect, encouragement, and guidance adapted to the child's developmental level
  • Teach-Model-Coach-Review caregiver coaching with mastery-timed workshops
