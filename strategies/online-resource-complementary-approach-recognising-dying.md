# Use online training resources as a complementary education approach to clinical training in recognising dying

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends offering online decision-training resources to medical students to practise prognostic decisions where clinical placements are scarce. It states that hospice or palliative care placements "can be extremely difficult and challenging to find due to limited resources" and that "The online environment, while unable to replace direct clinical contact, could be used to facilitate learning in this complex area, and provide a complementary education approach to clinical training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A training resource grounded in expert decision-making, as the evaluated resource was developed from expert palliative care doctors' weightings
#### Constraints
- Unable to replace direct clinical contact; the article notes limitations to ecological and face validity of the online vignette format before applying results to the clinical setting

### Target Learners
- Medical students, particularly those without access to palliative care placements

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognising when a person is dying
- Making prognostic decisions aligned with expert practice
