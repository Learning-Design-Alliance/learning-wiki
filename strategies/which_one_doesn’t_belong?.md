# Which One Doesn’t Belong?

## Description
The instructor or learners place 3-4 dot cards on the table. Two or three cards represent the same quantity, while one represents a different quantity. The learner then identifies the card that doesn't belong, prompting them to analyze and compare different quantities. This activity enhances critical thinking skills as learners must analyze and compare quantities to determine which one is different. Supports the development of number sense by encouraging learners to recognize and differentiate between various numerical quantities. Allows for differentiation by adjusting the numerical range and complexity of the dot arrangements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dot cards.
#### Constraints
- May require clear explanation and examples to ensure learners understand the game's objective. The activity's effectiveness depends on selecting appropriate cards that clearly represent different quantities.
