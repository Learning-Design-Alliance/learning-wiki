# Use deliberate practice with repeated case-based engagement and immediate feedback for assessment skill training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Design training modules according to deliberate practice theory, which "posits that skill improvement depends on task‐relevant repetition coupled with feedback". PainScan implemented this with 104 standardized cases drawn from real ICU settings, balanced across pain severity, age groups, and assessment methods, presented randomly in weekly exercises with immediate feedback including explanations for incorrect responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ample opportunities for repeated engagement with the same or similar tasks to enable gradual refinement of performance
#### Constraints
- The large case volume is an operational necessity of deliberate practice theory, not an extraneous dose; comparisons against conventional curricula reflect the whole package

### Target Learners
- PICU nurses
- clinical nurses learning assessment skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized, accurate clinical pain assessment skill
