# Adopt a mixed learning mode combining virtual simulation with practical tactile experiences and teacher guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Among the authors' prospects for future development is a "Mixed Learning Mode" that would "Utilize the simulation advantages of virtual reality while combining practical tactile experiences and complex scenarios to comprehensively improve students’ psychomotor skills and clinical decision-making abilities", integrating teachers' guidance with virtual reality technology to maximize teaching effect. This responds directly to the platform's stated lack of haptic feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both virtual simulation technology and practical tactile training experiences
#### Constraints
- The current platform's lack of haptic feedback limits learners' precise control over force, pressure, and touch essential to psychomotor skills

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in preclinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor skills
- clinical decision-making
