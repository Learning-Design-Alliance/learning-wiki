# Embed the activity in a larger seafaring context using oral presentations and documentaries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A contextualisation strategy recommending that the lesson sit inside a larger seafaring context in geography, history or literature. The article suggests the activity "could be combined with other forms of acquiring knowledge like giving oral presentations in history, literature or geography highlighting navigation", which would prepare the field in a more interactive way than teacher summarising, and lists three Viking and sea-exploration documentaries with durations and links as optional introductory viewing.

## Claims

- [After reading a story about a Viking voyage, students aged 12-16 are expected to be able to explain how the Sun can be used to find the cardinal directions.](../claims/vikings-sun-cardinal-directions-objective.md) [+W]
- [Through a hands-on activity, students are expected to be able to explain the basic functionality of the shadow board, an old Viking navigational tool.](../claims/hands-on-shadow-board-functionality-objective.md) [+W]
- [More experienced students are expected to explain the geometry of the shadow board and how trigonometry of a right angle triangle helps to calculate shadow length.](../claims/shadow-board-trigonometry-extension-objective.md) [+W]
