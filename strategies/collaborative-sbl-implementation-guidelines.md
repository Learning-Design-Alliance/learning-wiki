# Six-step implementation guideline for integrating collaborative simulation-based learning into health sciences curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a sequence for curricular integration: "First, it is necessary to identify the course, learning outcomes, and competencies to be developed to determine how many collaborative SBEs will be integrated." Instructors then provide baseline reading material, inform students in advance of the modality and their responsibilities, facilitate the design phase while reviewing clinical coherence, leave scenario execution to the designated student team (roles of patient, companion, timekeeper, and performance evaluator), and adapt the debriefing methodology to the instructor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-provided reading material as baseline input for case creation
- Advance information to students about the simulation modality and their specific responsibilities
#### Constraints
- Guidelines are recommendations from one trial in physical therapy education, not tested as a package

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physical therapy students
- Health sciences students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical competence development through student-designed simulation scenarios
