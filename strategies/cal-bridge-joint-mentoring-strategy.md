# Assign each scholar two mentors — a home-campus faculty member and a faculty member at a PhD-granting institution — meeting twice monthly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 6 studies (4 design, 2 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following the Fisk–Vanderbilt model, each Cal-Bridge scholar is formally assigned two mentors: a CSU faculty member at their home institution and a UC faculty member. Mentoring takes place via twice-monthly meetings; the CSU mentor advises on courses, research opportunities, and graduate applications, while the UC mentor is "especially qualified to provide guidance toward readiness for graduate school." Mentors also obtain monthly feedback from each scholar's instructors to track progress and catch problems early, and mentoring experts provide training workshops for faculty mentors.
