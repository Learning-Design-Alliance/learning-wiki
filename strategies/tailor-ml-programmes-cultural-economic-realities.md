# Tailor m-learning programmes to the cultural and economic realities of the local context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that implementation of m-learning in developing countries be adapted to local conditions rather than transferred wholesale. Its abstract states: "These results emphasise the need to tailor m-learning programmes following the particular cultural and economic realities of developing countries". The recommendation rests on the finding that adoption factors such as social influence and compatibility vary across the three countries, and on barriers the authors identify including poor infrastructure, digital illiteracy, and limited Arabic-language content.
