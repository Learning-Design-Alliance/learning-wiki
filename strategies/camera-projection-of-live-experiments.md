# Project experiments via cameras onto a screen above the stage so the whole audience sees details

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
"Almost all experiments are projected via cameras onto a screen, which is best well above the stage. This enables everybody to see important details of the experiments." Two people work full-time on the cameras onstage, and camera positions and actor placement relative to experiment and camera must be rehearsed. The oil drop experiment is projected onto a possibly separate screen and the water wave experiment directly onto a wall.
