# Teach vocabulary through contextual-clue inference strategies rather than mechanical grammar questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study group was taught "strategies on how to make use of contextual clues to glean the meaning", in contrast to a prior approach where grammar questions were mechanically answered without comprehending the passage. This strategy is credited with the study group's post-test vocabulary outperformance and aligns with the article's argument that vocabulary building requires context and crucial strategy use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authentic or constructed reading passages in which new words occur in context
#### Constraints
- The author notes the new approach was a challenge for students who needed more time to apply it

### Target Learners
- Low-intermediate EFL university students

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary expansion through inferring meaning from context
