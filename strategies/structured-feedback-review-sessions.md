# Schedule structured feedback review sessions with academics to support dialogic engagement with computer-based assessment feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because computer-based assessment is often chosen for efficiency in large cohorts, opportunities for feedback dialogue are limited. The review proposes systematic introduction of feedback review sessions with academics during instruction or personal tutor sessions, so that "students must not only receive feedback but need to proactively seek and engage with it in dialogue with instructors." This addresses passive receipt of feedback and supports the 2 sigma problem of scalable one-to-one tutoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- systematic introduction of feedback review sessions during instruction or personal tutor sessions
- structured time to reflect and engage in dialogue on feedback
#### Constraints
- must remain scalable and sustainable in large cohorts

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacology and medical students in large cohorts

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulation
- metacognitive skill development
- feedback engagement
