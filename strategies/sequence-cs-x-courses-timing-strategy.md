# Sequence CS+X courses carefully in the curriculum: not too early, not too late

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For programs that do use CS+X courses, the article recommends attention to sequencing, since discipline X will have practical limitations on the number of such courses that can be integrated. If introduced too early, students may not form effective connections between future discipline concepts and computing; if introduced too late, students might not be interested in re-learning their X concepts using computing and will not use computing skills effectively for their work in X.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of when students will encounter the relevant discipline concepts relative to the CS+X course
#### Constraints
- Discipline X will have practical limitations on the number of CS+X courses that can be integrated into their curriculum

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in non-computing disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- connections between computing and discipline X concepts
