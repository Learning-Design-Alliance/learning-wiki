# Provide structured instruction and support for community managers and richer resources for courses with less creative exposure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on field-study findings that managers carried heavy administrative workload and that programming-course communities generated little discussion, the authors recommend improving the system by "creating more structured instruction for community managers, and creating more community resources for courses that are unlikely to lead to exposure of creative ideas, e.g. the programming courses in our study." This targets facilitation quality as the lever for community engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community managers already exist with assigned duties and responsibilities
#### Constraints
- Derived from one 6-week field deployment; the authors call their examples not comprehensive

### Target Learners
- MOOC community managers and their members

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained community engagement
- Peer feedback and shared learning activities

## Claims

- [Community managers contributed 23% of conversation on average but their share varied widely across communities, from nearly all posts in C++ to 4% in Java, and managers reported heavy administrative workload.](../claims/community-manager-contribution-varies-and-workload-heavy.md) [+M]
- [In communities with engagement and facilitation, members willingly shared artifacts and gave feedback, while programming-course communities produced little interaction beyond code-error questions and snippet sharing.](../claims/facilitation-enables-artifact-sharing-and-feedback.md) [+M]
- [Communities coded with Communities of Inquiry social presence indicators differed dramatically in expressive behavior, e.g., emotion expressions ranged from 39.7% (Creative problem solving) to 2.3% (C++).](../claims/social-presence-indicators-vary-across-communities.md) [+M]
