# Follow Up After Discussions About Race

## Description
This strategy involves taking actions that might change inequities revealed during a discussion and giving learners time to do something that will change the learning environment. This may include encouraging students to begin taking actions that might change inequities, giving kids time to do something, like write a letter to the editor of their city newspaper, or offering to go with a representative group of students to talk with the principal about the way students are being treated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection, opportunities for action, teacher support, and access to resources.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to find appropriate and meaningful opportunities for action. Requires ongoing commitment and support from teachers and administrators
