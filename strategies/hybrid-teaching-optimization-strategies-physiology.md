# Optimization strategies for hybrid teaching: varied online instruction styles, curriculum reorganization, an online teaching library, and emerging technologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on their findings that live-lecture preference aligned with offline choice and recorded-lecture preference with hybrid choice, the authors recommend offering "a varied and flexible approach to online instruction which are tailored to the content and needs of the course". Future directions include reorganizing curricula to integrate basic and clinical knowledge, constructing a comprehensive online teaching library with basic, advanced and evaluative resources, building an online platform showcasing exemplary student work to stimulate peer engagement, and investigating game-based learning, simulation tools, virtual reality and AI tools such as ChatGPT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School network resources such as renowned faculty lectures and virtual simulation classrooms
- Correlation analysis between student engagement with library resources and academic performance to evaluate effectiveness
#### Constraints
- Recommended for post-COVID-19 hybrid medical education contexts

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing teaching effectiveness, students' participation, learning initiatives, and academic self-efficacy
