# Use the smartphone microphone as an accurate stopwatch for time-interval measurements in mechanics and waves experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The letter recommends repurposing the smartphone microphone for timing: "in a wide range of situations the microphone can be used as a stopwatch to accurately measure time intervals between sounds." A cited paper adds that "The precise determination of time intervals by means of the smartphone microphone enables experiments" comparing free-fall times, obtaining coefficients of restitution, measuring Doppler shifts, and measuring human reaction time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The experiment must produce discrete sound events whose intervening interval the microphone can time, such as impacts, echoes or frequency shifts
#### Constraints
- "in some modern smartphones the audio port is replaced by Bluetooth alternatives"

### Target Learners
- secondary-school and undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate measurement of time intervals in mechanics and waves experiments
