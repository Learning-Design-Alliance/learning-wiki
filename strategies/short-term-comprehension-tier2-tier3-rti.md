# Use short-term comprehension-specific interventions as Tier 2 or Tier 3 supports within RTI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends short-term, targeted comprehension interventions for upper elementary students within RTI, rather than multi-component interventions. The intervention "holds promise for use as a Tier 2 or Tier 3 intervention for students with comprehension-specific reading problems," was provided for the recommended 10–12 week length, taught in small groups by a qualified instructor, and designed to be supplemental.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small-group format with a qualified instructor
- Supplemental scheduling within school-wide intervention times
- Targeted to students with comprehension-specific rather than decoding or fluency needs
#### Constraints
- Effects appeared only on proximal measures; distal norm-referenced gains were not significant

### Target Learners
- Upper elementary students with comprehension-specific reading problems

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension of informational text
