# Teach a detailed pre-exam Plan of Study with weekly intention setting and accountability check-ins

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Within the CoC program, students completed a "Plan of Study" — a detailed schedule adapted from Steiner (2016) for the 7 days before an exam outlining what, how, and on which day they would study. At the end of each weekly meeting students set intentions about which strategies they would use, and at the next meeting the group discussed whether they upheld them and what parameters helped or hindered success. The authors believe this accountability, with a comfortable atmosphere, fostered belonging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly meetings with a consistent facilitator and peer group
- Low-stakes atmosphere with snacks and reflection time
- Follow-up discussion of prior week's intentions
#### Constraints
- The authors state this accountability effect was "not directly tested"
- The program did not markedly change student use of office hours or supplemental instruction, possibly because students have limited time

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduates in STEM gateway courses, especially those with low and medium preparedness

### Target Learning Goals
- Time management and study planning
- Metacognitive reflection on study strategy effectiveness
- Sense of belonging in the discipline
