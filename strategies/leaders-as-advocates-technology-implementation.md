# Leaders should act as advocates and role models when implementing technology into practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The qualitative synthesis identified leaders' ability to act as advocates to implement technology into practice as a main category, comprising training and guidance skills and showing support and example. The review states "leaders' roles in telemedicine included supporting and encouraging employees, being positive, and acting as an example", and recommends proactive mentoring of professionals to ensure a digitally competent workforce.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders need skills in training and guidance and must show support and example to employees
#### Constraints
- Advocating health IT systems was hindered by leaders' lack of complete information on health IT systems

### Target Learners
- Healthcare leaders such as nurse managers and frontline leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- Supporting employees' technology adoption
- Guiding professionals and patients in eHealth use
