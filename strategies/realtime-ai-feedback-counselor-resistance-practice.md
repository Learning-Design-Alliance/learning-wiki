# Deliver real-time multi-dimensional AI scores with explanatory feedback during counselor practice on resistance moments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates a strategy in which counselors draft responses to resistance utterances, receive AI-generated evaluation scores and explanatory feedback on each of the four communication mechanisms, and then revise their responses. As printed, the experimental group "were provided with AI-generated evaluation scores and explanatory feedback on their pre-phase responses" before revising. The feedback targets specific high-stakes moments rather than holistic dialogue quality, and participants expressed interest in "integrating the system into their ongoing reflective practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A fine-tuned evaluation model producing both multi-dimensional scores and textual explanations; feedback should be framed as supplemental, since participants were instructed to use it as a supplemental resource
#### Constraints
- Validated in a proof-of-concept study with novice counselors on text-based resistance moments; the article states the model "is not designed as a replacement for professional clinical judgment"

### Target Learners
- novice counselors in training and supervision

### Target Learning Goals
- responding effectively to client resistance in text-based counseling

### Affordances
- Four Mechanism Resistance Response Framework
