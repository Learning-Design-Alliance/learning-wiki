# Mindfulness Breaks

## Description
Mindfulness breaks are short, structured activities designed to create internal balance and a sense of being present in the moment. They are used in educational settings to help adults develop their awareness and ability to focus attention, which can mitigate negative effects of heavy media-multitasking and general cognitive decline. Frequent mindfulness activities enhance academic performance, support adults' physical well-being and mental health, and improve sleep. Self-regulation and inhibition can be strengthened by practicing mindfulness techniques.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a quiet space and brief periods of time (e.g., 1-5 minutes). May use guided audio, videos, or instructor-led exercises.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find it difficult to focus or may be skeptical of the practice. Requires consistent implementation to see benefits.
