# Combine Mini-CEX and DOPS workplace assessments with feedback at repeated time points during community outpatient training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy pairs two workplace-based formative assessment tools: Mini-CEX for consultation ability and DOPS for procedural skills, administered at admission, 2 weeks and 4 weeks of training, with results fed back after each evaluation. The article reports that "Mini-CEX combined with DOPS was used to evaluate the general practitioners at the time of admission, 2 weeks of training and 4 weeks of training, and the results were fed back". Formative evaluation is described as having the characteristic of timely feedback, improving students' learning ability and teachers' teaching level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers enthusiastic about outpatient practice and teaching, trained in Mini-CEX and DOPS to ensure consistency
- A real community outpatient clinical environment with real patients
#### Constraints
- The authors note the study did not combine analysis with test scores or specific cases, and that the evaluation is subjective

### Target Learners
- general-practice resident physicians in standardized training at community health service centers

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reception and diagnosis-and-treatment thinking ability
- procedural operational skills
- independent routine diagnosis and treatment in community clinics
