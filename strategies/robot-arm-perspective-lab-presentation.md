# Ready-to-use laboratory presentation in which students view the robot arm from different perspectives to study balance, torque, spring force and electronic components

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Teachers can use the robot arm in a presentation for laboratory work with students", showing it viewed from different perspectives. These presentations address the balance of a lever, torque, force on a spring via Hooke's law, and common electronic devices such as transistors, relays and potentiometers, after which the student can learn the controlling of any mechanical system.
