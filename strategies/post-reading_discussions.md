# Post-Reading Discussions

## Description
After reading, instructors facilitate discussions to explore learners' favorite parts of a narrative text or to confirm information learned in an informational text. These discussions promote student-to-student interaction and deeper engagement with the material. Revisiting opening questions helps learners reflect on their understanding and connections made throughout the reading process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires facilitation skills and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be hesitant to participate. Discussions may veer off-topic without proper guidance.
