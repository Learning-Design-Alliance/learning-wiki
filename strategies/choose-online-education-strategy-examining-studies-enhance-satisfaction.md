# Health managers and policymakers should choose online education strategies for medical students by examining studies in this area to enhance e-learning satisfaction

## Description
The review's implication section recommends that, given the inevitable rise of online learning, health managers and policymakers select the best online education strategy for medical science students by examining the existing research base, and that they address the recognized flaws and restrictions of the e-learning process to increase student satisfaction. The article states it is recommended that policymakers "choose the best online education strategy for medical science students by examining various studies in this area to enhance their satisfaction with e-learning."
