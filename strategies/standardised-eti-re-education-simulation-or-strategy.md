# Standardise ETI re-education for prehospital rescuers incorporating regular simulation training and operating room exposure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that ETI re-education for prehospital rescuers be standardised and incorporate two training modalities whose availability was independently associated with confidence: "regular simulation training" using a mannequin and re-education in the operating room. They argue improved confidence will in turn improve performance, and that governmental and professional agencies should strengthen leadership and guidance to improve re-education quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of mannequin-based simulation training programmes and operating room training opportunities for ELSTs
#### Constraints
- The study is cross-sectional and did not measure patient outcomes or the ETI procedure itself; the population-based findings describe Northern Japan only

### Target Learners
- Advanced-level emergency life-saving technicians authorised to perform ETI

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining and building confidence and proficiency in out-of-hospital endotracheal intubation and other airway management skills
