# Design online thesis-development courses around four KB principles and six student-centered activities in Knowledge Forum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course used four KB principles (authentic problem real idea, idea diversity, constructive use of authoritative sources, idea improvement) as flexible pedagogical guidance, combined with six activities: "Posting and sharing problems", generating and diversifying ideas, sharing initial draft proposals, working innovatively with diverse ideas, reflection, and rising above. The article demonstrates this design as an effective intervention for cultivating sense of promisingness among graduate students.
