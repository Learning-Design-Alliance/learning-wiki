# Nurse managers adopt the three-step model with Kirkpatrick-based evaluation for intern CPR training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable management strategy drawn from the article's implications section: nurse managers should apply the “online–simulation–bedside” model in CPR teaching for nursing interns and use the Kirkpatrick model as the evaluation tool. The article recommends that “nurse managers use the Kirkpatrick model as an evaluation tool to evaluate the effectiveness of team CPR skills training among nursing interns” and develop a distinctive team CPR training model based on the study.

## Design Implications
