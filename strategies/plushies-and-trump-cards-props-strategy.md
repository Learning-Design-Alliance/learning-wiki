# Use physical props — subatomic plushies and particle trump cards — as visual anchors and ice-breakers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Two props support the workshop: subatomic plushies, a cloth model for each particle used to provide a visual anchor to the discussions, and a deck of particle trump cards, one per particle, showing the particle name and basic properties with interaction properties codified as likes and dislikes. Each student reads out their card as particles are presented, which the article reports was very effective in engaging students and acts as an ice-breaker allowing active participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One card per student drawn randomly from a deck; 16 cards representing the matter particles, interaction carriers, and the Higgs boson
- Plushies arranged in families as the particles are presented
