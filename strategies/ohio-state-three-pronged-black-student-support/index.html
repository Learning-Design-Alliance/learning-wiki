Ohio State's three-pronged approach: early college pipeline, continuous on-campus support, and national best-practice hub¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Ohio State attributes its gains to a "three-pronged approach to boosting retention and completion among black students." The first prong, the Young Scholars Program, connects first-generation, low-income middle-schoolers (about 75 percent black) to college early with college-prep guidance, a need-based scholarship averaging $15,605, a summer bridge program, monthly success coaches, and weekly peer mentors. The second prong extends on-campus support beyond freshman year, including the Bell Center's Early Arrival Program and the LIWOC retreat for women of color. The third prong positions the Bell Center as a national hub for research and sharing of best practices.
