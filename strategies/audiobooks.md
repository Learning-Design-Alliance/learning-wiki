# Audiobooks

## Description
Allowing learners to listen to audiobooks supports learners with auditory processing strengths. This format lets them absorb information through their preferred modality, enhancing comprehension and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to audiobooks
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for learners who prefer visual or kinesthetic learning
