# Revise the gifted-class curriculum to reduce the range of selected contents while keeping quality and performance level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the external evaluation's perception findings, the article's closing recommendation is that "the students and teachers perceptions strongly recommend the revision of curricula in order to reduce the range of selected contents, but keep the achieved level of quality, as well as the performance level". This strategy targets the agreed weakness that the curriculum, while relevant, is too large, and is intended for programmes serving gifted science pupils where breadth crowds out depth. It should preserve the measured achievement gains while trimming selected content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Perception data from students and teachers identifying overloaded content
- maintained quality and performance level during revision
#### Constraints
- Applies to the special physics class programme evaluated in 2009-2010; the article offers no test of the revised curriculum's effects

### Target Learners
- gifted high-school pupils in physics and sciences

### Target Learning Goals
- functional science knowledge
- intrinsic and instrumental motivation for physics
