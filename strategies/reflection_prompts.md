# Reflection Prompts

## Description
Reflection prompts are specific questions or statements used to guide learners in thinking deeply about their learning process, content understanding, and personal growth. They encourage metacognition, self-assessment, and the application of knowledge to new contexts. Effective reflection prompts facilitate deeper engagement with complex ideas and allow students to find broader meaning in their work. Teachers can use various closing and reflective activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection during or after learning. Prompts can be delivered orally or in writing. Recording medium (paper, digital, etc.)
#### Constraints
- May require explicit instruction on how to reflect effectively. Students may need support to move beyond superficial responses.
