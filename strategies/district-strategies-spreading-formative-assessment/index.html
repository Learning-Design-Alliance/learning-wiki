District strategies for spreading and deepening formative assessment practice systemwide¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Sunnyside School District supported, spread, and deepened formative assessment districtwide through a set of enumerated strategies: constant and consistent messaging around the definition of and expectations for formative assessment; professional learning at monthly Principals Meetings; principal cadre and coaches walk-throughs/instructional rounds with the district team; real-time feedback and coaching with leaders and teachers; ongoing WestEd-sponsored courses providing time for peer feedback and self-reflection, beginning with 245 participants and adding a new cohort of 100+ each year; and identification of Lead Teachers who lead the work at their sites.
