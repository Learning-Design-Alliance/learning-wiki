Design AI-integrated, ethically grounded, and adaptable business education models guided by bibliometric evidence¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study positions its findings as guidance for educational design: "This study offers actionable guidance for educators, curriculum developers, and policymakers to design AI-integrated, ethically grounded, and adaptable business education models that meet the demands of an AI-driven global economy." Educators and policymakers should use the mapped clusters, trends, and gaps—especially the identified gaps in curriculum coherence, educator readiness, and assessment validity—to shape AI-integrated curricula.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Attention to the persistent gaps the study identifies: curriculum coherence, educator readiness, and assessment validity

Constraints¶

  • Guidance derives from a bibliometric mapping of the literature rather than from intervention testing

Target Learners¶

  • business education students in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • preparing learners for an AI-driven global economy

Affordances¶

  • Bibliometric Mapping Ai Business Education Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
  • Ground ethical inquiry into AI in the realities of teaching, assessment, leadership, and professional judgement rather than treating it as external compliance
  • AI system developers should ensure human-centric design, institutional leaders should stay updated on global AI regulations, and LA experts should engage policymakers through dialogue
  • Use the framework as a global reference to guide national AI competency frameworks, teacher training, and assessment parameters
