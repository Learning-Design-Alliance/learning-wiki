# Spatial social distancing in simulation: mark a person-free zone and confine each participant to a zone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Spatial social distancing maintains a 2-metre separation between simulation participants. A person-free zone is marked out on the floor, for example with masking tape, and each participant is confined to a zone and not allowed to cross into it. The article notes the disadvantage that physical contact and equipment transfers are prevented, offset by the ability of team members to communicate freely and in real time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A room in which a person-free zone can be marked out, e.g. with masking tape
#### Constraints
- Prevents physical contact or equipment transfers between participants

### Target Learners
- health care teams in simulation training

### Target Learning Goals
- team-based emergency management under infection control constraints
