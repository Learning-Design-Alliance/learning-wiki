# Use natural language statements as data units to ease curation and review of learning data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend leveraging ongoing AIED work on discourse analytics, reflection analytics and natural-language-based assessment so that data collection consists of verbal statements learners make. Because "humans are simply very good in expressing themselves in natural language", speakers can easily curate what they say to respect others' privacy and confidentiality, and humans can easily review collected natural language data before it is re-purposed for learning. Self-tracking as part of reflective practice then also constitutes conscious acts of mini-reflections.
