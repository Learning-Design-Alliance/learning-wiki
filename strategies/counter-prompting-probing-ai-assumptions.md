# Counter-prompting: deliberately probing AI systems for their assumptions and using responses as data about the apparatus

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Counter-prompting is a Critical Interrogation practice in which users probe AI systems reflexively to illuminate their own limitations. The paper defines it as "the practice of deliberately probing AI systems for their assumptions, asking them to represent alternative perspectives, and using their responses as data about the structuring assumptions of the apparatus." This transforms the user from a recipient of AI knowledge into an analyst of AI knowledge production.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users must treat AI responses as data about the system's structuring assumptions rather than as transparent reports on the world
#### Constraints
- The paper presents this as a conceptual practice within its theoretical framework, not an empirically tested instructional method

### Target Learners
- Students and adult learners developing critical AI literacy

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognizing whose knowledge traditions are centered or marginal and seeking alternative framings of AI outputs

- Three Part Ai Literacy Epistemic Agency Framework
