Layered professional learning and support for teachers implementing individualized software¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The summary describes a multi-channel support model so teachers can implement the program successfully: "Virtual and in-person training sessions and workshops tailored to the needs of the customer," free 30-minute virtual live Connect sessions schedulable during planning periods, a dedicated Success Team reachable via in-product live chat, email, and phone, and a free on-demand Help Center with video tutorials and user guides. A dedicated account manager works with school or district stakeholders during onboarding.

Context¶

Requirements¶

Target Learners¶

  • Classroom teachers, interventionists, and special educators

  • Effective implementation of individualized learning software
