Use the case method, theory-of-inventive-problems methods, and organizational-activity games as the core interactive methods for forming social tolerance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends centering tolerance formation on three interactive method families: the case method, in which students solve pedagogical situations and "state their position with conviction" in discussion; theory-of-solving-inventive-problems tools such as brainstorming, morphological analysis and the method of focal objects to generate original responses to asocial situations; and organizational-activity games with distributed roles (researchers, opponents, reviewers) devoted to tolerance problems. The authors judge these the most effective and relevant to the goal.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Problem situations must be created in which future teachers show flexibility, tolerance, creativity and ability to resist negative phenomena

Constraints¶

  • The authors note most experts pay insufficient attention to such interactive methods and literature offers only fragmentary studies

Target Learners¶

  • university students in professional pedagogical education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • tolerance for differing views within norms and laws
  • active resistance to asocial phenomena
  • conflict competence

Affordances¶

  • Seven Component Social Tolerance Formation Scheme

Claims¶

  • The experimental group's conflict level increased by 6.4 points (20%), which the authors interpret as greater readiness to resist asocial actions rather than heightened interpersonal conflict [+W]
  • Experimental-group students improved on all four measured prosocial-tolerance qualities, with the largest gain in courage in upholding position (~20%) and the smallest in independence (~10%) [+W]
  • The proportion of experimental-group students at a high tolerance level increased by 165% during the formative experiment [+W]
  • An interactive-methods scheme for forming social tolerance raised the tolerance index of future teachers by 34% in the experimental group versus 5% in the control group [+W]
  • Ranks of ethical and individualistic values rose in the experimental group but changed little in the control group [+W]
