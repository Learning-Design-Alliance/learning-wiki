# Include an immediate, structured debriefing session with descriptive, analysis and application phases in every high-fidelity simulation training

## Description
The review recommends that debriefing be treated as a critical phase of high-fidelity simulation training, grounded in the adult learning cycle of experience, reflection, conceptualization and experimentation. It notes that only four included studies reported structured reflection or debriefing involving both students and trainers, and recommends that "an immediate and meaningful debriefing session should be introduced in each high-fidelity simulation training in the future" with three phases for both students and trainers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Debriefing should be conducted immediately after scenario training and include both students and trainers
#### Constraints
- Only four of the included studies designed debriefing involving both residents and instructors/trainers, so current evidence on its contribution is limited

### Target Learners
- medical students
- residents
- physicians
- nurses
- midwives

### Target Learning Goals
- neonatal resuscitation knowledge
- resuscitation skill performance
- teamwork performance
