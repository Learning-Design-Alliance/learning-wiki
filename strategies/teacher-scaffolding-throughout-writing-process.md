# Provide teacher scaffolding throughout all stages of the writing process, giving feedback on drafts and gradually transferring responsibility to learners.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The teacher provides step-by-step guidance from the beginning until the end of the writing process, giving feedback on students' drafts regarding "improper use of articles, pluralization, and syntactic forms" as well as on drafts of less good quality such as lack of paragraph unity. Students use the feedback to revise and rewrite before editing and submitting final writings. The teacher gradually gives up support in order to transfer responsibility to learners for completing the task.
