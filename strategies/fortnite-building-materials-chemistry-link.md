# Link Fortnite building materials to chemical structures and chemical engineering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes connecting the game's building mechanic, in which players craft fortifications from materials such as wood, stone, or metal granted by destroying objects, to chemistry content. This aspect of the game can be linked to the chemical structures of these materials and to the chemical engineering needed to produce them, extending the game-based approach from reactions to materials science.
