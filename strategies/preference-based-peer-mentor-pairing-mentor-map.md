# Preference-based peer mentor pairing with mentor maps to broaden scholar networks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In response to the low-rated peer network, Cal-Bridge pairs volunteer peer mentors (alumni scholars) with mentees according to individual preferences such as academic seniority, research area, race/ethnicity, and gender. Pairs are self-guided in discussion content with provided guidelines for communication, scheduling, follow-through, and conduct, plus optional interactive workshops whose curriculum is chosen by scholars. In one activity, scholars work with a peer mentor to create a "mentor map" identifying people or groups they seek guidance, support, or inspiration from across areas such as Accountability, Feedback, Health, and Aspirations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Volunteer alumni peer mentors
- Mentee preference information for pairing
- Provided expectations and code-of-conduct guidelines for pairs
#### Constraints
- In its first pilot year the 1-on-1 component was offered only to Northern California scholars and workshops only to Southern California scholars

### Target Learners
- Current Cal-Bridge undergraduate scholars
- Cal-Bridge alumni serving as peer mentors

### Target Learning Goals
- Sense of community among scholars
- Broadened mentor networks
- Peer psychosocial and career support
