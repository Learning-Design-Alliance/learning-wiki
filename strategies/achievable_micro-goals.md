# Achievable Micro-goals

## Description
Setting achievable micro-goals encourages consistent, incremental progress, leading to a growth mindset and increased confidence. These small wins, repeated over time, help learners see that progress is attainable and that their efforts are paying off. This approach breaks down larger, more daunting tasks into manageable steps, making the overall goal seem less overwhelming and more approachable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful planning to break down tasks into manageable steps. Instructors need to provide clear guidance and support to help learners achieve their micro-goals.
#### Constraints
- If micro-goals are too easy, they may not provide enough of a challenge to promote growth. If they are too difficult, they may lead to frustration and discouragement.

### Target Learners

### Affordances
- Helps learners see tangible progress, reduces feelings of being overwhelmed, and builds confidence through repeated success. Instructors can use micro-goals to scaffold learning and provide targeted support.

### Personalization
- Micro-goals can be tailored to individual learner needs and skill levels. Instructors can provide different sets of micro-goals for learners with varying levels of proficiency.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track learner progress towards micro-goals, monitor their confidence levels, and observe their persistence in the face of challenges. Compare performance between learners who are using micro-goals and those who are not.

## Impact
- Research suggests that small wins repeated over time can lead to a growth mindset and increased confidence. Setting achievable goals can support self-regulation and increase motivation as learners begin to successfully tackle difficult challenges in smaller steps.
