# Three educational use cases: guided multi-week journeys, instructor demonstrations, and self-directed exploration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors envision three deployment modes for the games. As printed: "We envision three major use cases: first, students may take a complete journey through the four beginner games or all eight games over one to two months under teacher guidance"; second, instructors use specific visualizations to illustrate points such as k-space undersampling; third, students access individual games on their own time for longer exploration or as research projects contributing new games.
