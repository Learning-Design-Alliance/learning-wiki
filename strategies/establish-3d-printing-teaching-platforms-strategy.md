# Establish 3D printing teaching platforms in hospitals and universities to enhance interns' professional value recognition and internship teaching quality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "hospitals and universities can establish 3D printing teaching platforms to enhance interns' professional value recognition, improving the quality of clinical internship teaching" and promoting primary healthcare services. It further suggests that with reduced material costs and remote technical support, "simplified 3DIPT teaching models are expected to be promoted in grassroots medical education", and that the teaching model can be optimized according to community health service needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 3D printing equipment and material supply
- Remote technical support for grassroots institutions
#### Constraints
- Application in rural and grassroots medical institutions is currently limited by equipment cost and professional technical personnel

### Target Learners
- Clinical medicine interns
- Community physicians in training

### Target Learning Goals
- Core clinical competencies for community practice
- Professional value recognition
