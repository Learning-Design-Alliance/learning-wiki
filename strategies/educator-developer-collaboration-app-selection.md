# Collaborate closely with software developers when selecting or building educational applications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors found that although a wealth of anatomy applications existed, few were suitable for educational purposes and even fewer matched primary school complexity. They conclude this "highlights the need for collaboration between software engineers and educators", since engineers lack educational background and educators are unaware of technology's affordances and limitations. Close collaboration between the two groups is said to benefit students as end users of ICT tools.
