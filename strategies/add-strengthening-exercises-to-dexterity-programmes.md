# Incorporate strengthening exercises into future dexterity training programmes for dental students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the dexterity-focused home programme produced significant grip-strength losses, the authors recommend that future versions add strengthening work. The article states that "the decrease in grip strength suggests the desirability of incorporating strengthening exercises in future programmes." The strategy pairs fine-motor and sensitivity training with sustained grip-strength work so that precision gains do not come at the cost of maximal grip force.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monitoring of grip strength (e.g., dynamometry) alongside dexterity and sensitivity outcomes
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single-group pre-post study where the strength decrease could not be causally attributed to the training

### Target Learners
- first-year dental students in preclinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining grip strength while training dexterity and sensitivity
