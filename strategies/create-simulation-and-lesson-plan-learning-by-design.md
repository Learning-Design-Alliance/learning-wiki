# Challenge PSTs to develop both a simulation and an accompanying lesson plan

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's sixth design principle recommends a create-stage where PSTs develop their own simulation together with a lesson plan, adapted from learning-by-design. The authors state "The create-stage was the only stage where we found evidence of the PSTs' TPACK and therefore appears to be essential!", and that PSTs shifted focus toward TCK and TPACK while discussing appropriate tasks for pupils.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A create-stage task combining simulation development with a lesson plan, with sufficient in-class time to complete both
#### Constraints
- In Prototype 2, time constraints gave less time for the create-stage in class, which seemed to result in more focus on TCK than TPACK during class

### Target Learners
- Pre-service science teachers at the master's level

### Target Learning Goals
- Technological pedagogical and content knowledge (TPACK) for teaching programming of simulations in science
