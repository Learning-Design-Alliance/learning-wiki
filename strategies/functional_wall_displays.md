# Functional Wall Displays

## Description
Using walls for bulletin boards displaying student work and curricular supports, such as a word wall or math posters, makes resources easily accessible during instruction. Position displayed items strategically where they can be readily seen when needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Wall space, bulletin boards, posters, student work, and an organizational system for ensuring content is current and relevant.
#### Constraints
- If not managed well, wall displays can become cluttered and overwhelming, leading to distraction rather than support. Displays need to be periodically updated to stay relevant.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly visual learners and those who benefit from constant reminders of key concepts

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- To make learning materials readily accessible, promote student work, enhance classroom aesthetics, and support visual learning

### Affordances
- Maximizes use of space, creates a visually stimulating environment, serves as a continuous learning aid, fosters a sense of student ownership and pride.

### Personalization
- Involve students in creating and updating displays. Allow students to select which pieces of their work to showcase. Make displays relevant to current learning topics and student interests.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe how frequently students use the wall displays as resources. Gather feedback on the usefulness and clarity of displays. Monitor student engagement and behavior near the displays.

## Impact
- Promotes active learning and visual reinforcement of concepts. Increases student engagement and a sense of ownership within the classroom.
