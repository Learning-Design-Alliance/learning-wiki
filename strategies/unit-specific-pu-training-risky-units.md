# Prepare special, unit-specific training programs for nurses working in units at high risk of pressure ulcers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that training for pressure ulcer management be organised at the level of the clinical unit, with more intensive and evidence-based provision for clinics where pressure ulcer risk is high. It states that "More intensive and evidence‐based unit‐specific training should be organised for nurses working in clinics with a high risk of PUs" and, in the conclusion, that special training programs be prepared for nurses in risky units. This rests on the finding that the clinic worked in significantly affected self-efficacy scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institution and unit managers must organise and support the unit-specific training programs and encourage nurse participation.
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a cross-sectional self-report study in a single hospital; causal effects of training programs were not tested.

### Target Learners
- practising nurses in high-risk units such as intensive care, surgical, internal medicine and emergency clinics

### Target Learning Goals
- pressure ulcer assessment, planning, supervision and decision-making self-efficacy
