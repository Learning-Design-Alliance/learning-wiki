# Use AI coach prompts for structured reflection and premortems to build metacognition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper supplies two metacognitive prompts: a reflection prompt in which the coach helps a student reflect on a team experience by naming challenges, extracting insights, and setting goals, and a premortem prompt in which teams imagine their project has failed and list reasons, then plan how to strengthen the project. The premortem is designed to "guard against future failures via mental time travel, simulating future states of failure and considering ways to route around those possible failures." Both prompts use step-by-step instructions with the AI waiting for student responses and asking one question at a time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors try the prompts themselves and across more than one LLM before class use
- The exercise is ideally completed in teams in a classroom with oversight
#### Constraints
- The AI may mirror student tone, give bad advice, refuse to work, or get stuck in a loop; students must continually assess the AI's advice and can redirect or restart at any time

### Target Learners
- students reflecting on team experiences or planning team projects

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognition
- self-distancing
- prospective planning
- reflection after action
