Engage district leaders as they implement a coaching program¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends engaging district leaders as they implement a coaching program, the third of three insights from the New Teacher Center. District-leader engagement is presented as a source of the clear expectations and support coaches need to devote sufficient time to the full range of coaching activities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District leaders must be actively engaged in the coaching program's implementation.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • district leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving teachers' instruction through coaching

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide clear guidance to coaches when implementing a districtwide coaching program
  • Track coaching activities to support districtwide coaching implementation
  • School and district leaders' recommendations for implementing effective classroom coaching programs
