# Deliver communication skills training in an interactive classroom setting with practice using simulated clients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 5-day professional skills and knowledge training programme spent 32 hours over 5 days (approximately 6 hours per day) and was "delivered in an interactive classroom setting, with participants having the opportunity to practice their communication skills with simulated clients". The programme emphasised leadership and enhancement of pre-existing skills to maximise the RVT role in practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A communication training organisation delivers the programme; participants spend 32 hours in training over 5 days
#### Constraints
- Wellness appointment conduct is permitted only to the extent that licensing bodies allow, which varies by province in Canada

### Target Learners
- registered veterinary technologists/technicians

### Target Learning Goals
- communication skills
- small-animal wellness knowledge
- business expertise
