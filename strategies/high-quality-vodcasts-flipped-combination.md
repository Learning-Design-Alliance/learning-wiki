# Invest in high-quality prescripted vodcasts and consider combining vodcasts with live teaching (e.g., flipped classroom)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that well-executed video recordings outperformed live-streamed teaching on the promoting factors, the authors recommend that "Teachers should invest in the design and implementation of high-quality learning videos (vodcasts) that engage viewers through prescripted storytelling, real-life cases, relevant visual elements (authentic video clips) and incorporating practical tasks." They further suggest "combining the strengths of vodcasts and live teaching, for example, through the flipped classroom approach."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vodcasts must be well-executed: prescripted storytelling, real-life cases, authentic video clips and practical tasks
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on one 45-minute lesson in a single medical/nursing context; podcast and live-stream groups did not perform as well on experiences or test results

### Target Learners
- medical students
- nursing students in health science education

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical diagnostic skills (pediatric respiratory problems)
