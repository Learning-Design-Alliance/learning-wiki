# Test anastomosis patency using a saline bag infused with blue food colouring running at 10 drops per minute

## Description
For practice models that cannot use live flow, the article suggests a perfusion setup adapted from Zeng et al.: "A bag of saline infused with blue food colouring, running at a rate of 10 drops per minute (Zeng et al.) may be considered as a method of anastomosis testing in other models". This lets trainees check patency and flow when working with chicken thigh, vessel or other non-live models away from the laboratory.
