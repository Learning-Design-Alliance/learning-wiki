# Accommodate Varying Technology Experience

## Description
Recognize that adults have diverse levels of technology exposure and experience. Do not assume familiarity with current tools or platforms. Incorporate introductory resources and guidance for learners with limited experience to ensure they can successfully engage with the course materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of diverse skill levels, flexibility in instruction, supportive learning resources
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and resources to accommodate diverse needs

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Adult learners with varying degrees of technological experience and exposure.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enable all learners to participate, regardless of previous experience. Provide foundational knowledge for those with limited exposure to technology.

### Affordances
- Reduces barriers to entry, provides a supportive learning environment, promotes confidence and engagement

### Personalization
- Offer optional introductory modules on technology basics, provide step-by-step instructions for using online tools, assign peer mentors to support learners with limited experience
