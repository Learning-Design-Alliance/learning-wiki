# Community Building on Student Voice

## Description
Creating a classroom community where student voices are valued involves providing time and opportunities for students to share and discuss their thoughts. Strategies such as daily check-ins, reflection sheets, sentence stems, and student-led discussions can amplify student voices and build their confidence to speak up and advocate for their learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for discussions, supportive classroom environment, sentence stems and word banks
#### Constraints
- May require significant time and effort. Some students may be hesitant to share their thoughts.
