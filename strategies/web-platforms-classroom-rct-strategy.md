# Use web-based educational platforms to run individual-level randomized crossover experiments in the classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that web platforms such as LMSs and MOOCs make individual-level randomization, "the core of high quality RCTs", much easier, and that this may be their transformational impact on education research. A matched-pairs randomized crossover design within a single course eliminates student-to-student variability, enabling detection of small effects previously not detectable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web-based platform capable of personalizing individual experiences and logging assignment data
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated in a statistics course; crossover design requires repeated comparable units within a course

### Target Learners
- education researchers
- instructors of large college courses

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring causal effects of instructional interventions
