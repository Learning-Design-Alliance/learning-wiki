# Provide online learning repositories with browsing or topic-mapping functions alongside keyword search, and keep access simple enough for low-technology-confidence users

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Focus-group participants recommended expanding the repository while improving how resources are found. They suggested that mapping or browsing functions could make searching more accurate and reveal resources students did not know existed, since the repository offered only keyword search. They also emphasized that ease of use, not technological skill, drove uptake: one student liked that using it was "just like Google".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A keyword-searchable online repository of academically reviewed learning resources accessible to students in workplace practice
#### Constraints
- The article reports these as student recommendations from focus groups, not tested design modifications; no major modifications had been made to the repository at the time of writing

### Target Learners
- health professional students on clinical placements
- undergraduate physiotherapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient access to clinical revision resources
- independent learning and revision of practical skills
- lifelong learning after graduation
