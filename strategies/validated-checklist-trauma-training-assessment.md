# Use a validated checklist with timed-item documentation to assess and compare team performance in pre-hospital trauma training scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends assessing emergency trainings with a validated, scenario-based scoring instrument rather than unvalidated examinations, since existing assessments "were not suitable for verifying changes other than mortality from training". The PERFECT checklist scores primary and secondary assessment items with 'executed' and 'timely' options, allows independent real-time measurement of intervention times, and permits assessing comparable trauma training standards and simulation. The authors state that "a validated tool is now available to evaluate trauma trainings with comparable standards".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-trained users of the checklist
- Scenarios based on a comparable trauma-care standard (e.g., PHTLS recommendations fitting the German Level 3 guideline)
#### Constraints
- Use for real patient care in the prehospital setting or trauma room has not been investigated yet.

### Target Learners
- paramedics
- emergency physicians
- instructors of pre-hospital trauma courses

### Target Learning Goals
- objective evaluation and comparability of team performance in trauma care training
