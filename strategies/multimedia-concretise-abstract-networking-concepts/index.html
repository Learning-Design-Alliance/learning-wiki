Use multimedia learning to concretise abstract networking concepts and raise student interest¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends multimedia-based learning for abstract subjects: "Multimedia based learning was used to increase students’ interest and attitude. While, DPBL was used to increase students’ creative thinking and teamwork skill." Students agreed the multimedia could "concrete the abstract concepts of the Computer Networks subject" (mean 3.94) and the authors propose further development with more contents to improve interest, attitude, knowledge and problem-solving skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Multimedia combining image, text, animation and audio
  • A subject whose concepts students find abstract

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is student feedback from one quasi-experiment, not tested achievement gains

Target Learners¶

  • University students studying abstract technical subjects

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Increased interest and attitude; better understanding of abstract concepts

Claims¶

  • Students reported that multimedia learning motivated them and was more effective and interactive than traditional teaching [+M]
  • Students gave positive feedback on the contents and navigation of the mDPBL multimedia learning [+M]
  • Students gave positive feedback on the usefulness of the mDPBL multimedia learning for Computer Networks [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Apply multimedia-supported 7E learning model materials to other science units and to courses with abstract content
  • Use multimedia and real models alongside 5E activities, and assign homework for the enter and evaluation phases to save time
