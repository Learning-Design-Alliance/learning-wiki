# Disseminate evidence on effective peer tutoring types to practitioners in further and higher education

## Description
The article recommends wider dissemination to practitioners of what is already known about effective peer tutoring. It states that "Much is already known about the effectiveness of some types of peer tutoring and this merits wider dissemination to practitioners", positioning dissemination of existing effectiveness findings as an actionable step alongside further research. The strategy targets staff designing and delivering teaching in colleges and universities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing research findings on the effectiveness of specific peer tutoring types must be identified and translated for practitioner audiences.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation applies to the types of peer tutoring whose effectiveness is already known, not to all formats.

### Target Learners
- students in further and higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- improved teaching and learning quality, outcomes and cost-effectiveness through adoption of effective peer tutoring formats
