# Shift physics teaching from lecture methods to activity-based problem-solving models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the Target-Task group significantly outperformed the lecture group, the authors recommend that "Physics teachers should endeavour to shift away from the use of lecture methods to using activity-based methods". They further recommend using the Target-Task Model more often in classes with more male students and male low scorers, exposing pre-service physics teachers to problem-solving models during training, organizing training and re-training programmes for practicing teachers, and having textbook authors incorporate the model in new editions.
