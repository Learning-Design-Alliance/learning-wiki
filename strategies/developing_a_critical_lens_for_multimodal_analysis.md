# Developing a Critical Lens for Multimodal Analysis

## Description
Increasing students' awareness of effective and ineffective multimodal combinations by analyzing a mode's affordances and constraints. This involves starting with simple media (e.g., a picture book) and discussing how images and words convey different meanings, work together, or contradict each other. Students expand their critical lens by analyzing images in various genres and media. The goal is to collect exemplar structures to guide their own composition decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples of multimodal texts (e.g., picture books, informational texts, websites), discussion prompts, time for analysis and reflection.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for students with limited prior knowledge of multimodal composition. Requires careful selection of examples to illustrate different effects. Relies on active engagement in critical analysis.
