# Improve Classroom Air Quality

## Description
Poor indoor air quality is a major contributor to absenteeism. This strategy involves ensuring adequate ventilation systems, regular maintenance to prevent 'sick building syndrome', and mitigating indoor pollutants. Implementing measures such as upgrading ventilation systems, using air purifiers, and choosing low-emission materials for flooring, paints, and adhesives can positively affect children and reduce student absenteeism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Air quality monitoring equipment, ventilation systems, physical structure of the building. Consider costs related to equipment and operation.
#### Constraints
- Achieving optimal air quality can be challenging due to environmental constraints and individual preferences. Resource constraints (e.g., lack of air purifiers) can limit the ability to adjust.
