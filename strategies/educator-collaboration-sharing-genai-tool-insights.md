# Educators share experiences, strategies, and insights about generative AI tools to keep the education community informed

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Given the dynamic landscape of AI technologies, the article recommends collaboration among educators: sharing experiences, strategies, and insights about generative AI tools can help the education community stay informed and make better decisions about tool selection and integration. The authors welcome feedback on the typology and encourage educators to share their experiences with these tools to build collective understanding of effective generative AI use in education.
