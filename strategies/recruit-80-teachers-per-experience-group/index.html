Future LSVC-type studies should recruit larger cohorts, with at least 80 teachers per experience group¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

To strengthen statistical power and precision in evaluating differentiated PD effects, the article recommends enlarging future samples. It states, "To achieve greater precision and statistical power, future studies should aim to recruit larger cohorts, with at least 80 teachers per experience group." This follows the novice group's null result, which the authors attributed possibly to the small novice sample (n = 15).
