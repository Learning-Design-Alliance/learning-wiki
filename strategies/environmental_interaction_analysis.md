# Environmental Interaction Analysis

## Description
These activities explore the relationships between people and their environments by examining environments, inhabitants, and the tracks they leave behind. Activities prompt learners to write stories about predatory events based on tracks or to consider the role of environmental components like rocks, soil, and water in terrariums.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Terrariums, animal tracks, writing materials
