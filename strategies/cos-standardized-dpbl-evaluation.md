# Develop standardized core outcome sets (COS) for evaluating DPBL in medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review proposes developing standardized core outcome sets specifically tailored to evaluating DPBL in medical education to address inconsistencies in how satisfaction is measured across studies. The authors note that COS have been successfully implemented in fields such as rheumatology, pediatrics, and oncology to improve outcome consistency. Establishing a COS for DPBL research could support more rigorous and consistent assessments and promote more evidence-informed decisions in curriculum design.
