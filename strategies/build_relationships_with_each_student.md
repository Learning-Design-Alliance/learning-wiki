# Build Relationships with Each Student

## Description
Create positive learning environments and engaging instruction to help all students achieve success. Implement caring and emotionally supportive classrooms for students with challenging life or school experiences. This involves showing interest in students’ welfare, respecting their perspectives, telling them they can succeed, knowing their academic and social needs, and recognizing their achievements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time to get to know each student, a commitment to creating a caring and respectful classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming, requires genuine effort and consistency, may not be effective if students are resistant or distrustful.
