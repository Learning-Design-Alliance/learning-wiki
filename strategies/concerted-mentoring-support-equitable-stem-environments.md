# Universities should make concerted efforts to mentor and support all students and improve STEM learning environments to counteract stereotypes disadvantaging URM students

## Description
Based on the quantitative findings of higher URM attrition and lower grades, the article recommends that "It is critical that universities make concerted efforts to mentor and support all of their students, and improve the learning environment of these STEM disciplines to counteract the historically-rooted culture and stereotypes surrounding STEM that unfairly disadvantage URM students". The strategy targets the four EVT factors by improving role models, mentoring and guidance, anxiety, and performance feedback. The article frames this as a call for equitable and inclusive learning environments so URM students who come to college with severe disadvantages are appropriately supported and can excel.
