# Fund and prioritize low-GNI participation through pay-it-forward fees, grant prioritization, and low-GNI sponsorship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To counter the exclusion of participants from low-GNI countries, the article proposes that IWA explore having participants "pay it forward" when paying their fee rather than relying solely on alumni donations, prioritizing low-GNI applicants for grants, and working with previous members to find sponsorship in low-GNI countries. These are proposed solutions to a stated equity problem, not yet evaluated interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- IWA is run entirely by volunteers, so members have minimal time beyond organizing the camp itself
#### Constraints
- The current grant program, funded by donations rather than camp fees, is described as insufficient to close the gap, and IWA lacks sponsorship to lower the camp fee; past efforts were short-term with little impact

### Target Learners
- young people aged 16-24 from low-GNI countries, including Africa, eastern Europe, Asia, and South America

### Target Learning Goals
- access to astronomy education outreach and independent scientific inquiry on astronomical topics
