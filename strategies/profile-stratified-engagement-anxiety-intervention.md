# Stratified intervention: tailor support by engagement profile, attending to the highest-engagement group's anxiety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors state the study aims to provide 'a basis and support for constructing a learning psychological theory model and precise stratified intervention in educational practice'. Because anxiety differs by profile and self-efficacy mediates the profile–anxiety link, intervention should be profile-specific: boosting self-efficacy for the three higher-engagement profiles while monitoring the 'High Vigor-High Dedication-High Absorption' type, which the article reports as exhibiting the highest anxiety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires prior latent profile identification of students' engagement types so that supports can be matched to profile-specific mechanisms.
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a cross-sectional, self-report study of 936 students in three high schools in eastern Hubei Province, China; causal use is limited as reported by the authors.

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing learning anxiety
- enhancing academic self-efficacy
