# Rapid-response essay model: situate recently published research in an urgent context via invited commentary papers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editors proposed a special issue in which essay-style response papers situate recently published research studies in the pandemic situation, drawing insights and providing recommendations for teaching and learning during rapid shifts. Because new research could not be solicited in time, they used existing publications as anchors: 'we proposed a unique special issue idea to our ETR&D editorial board members' and called for 'commentary or essay-style papers that would situate ETR&D recently published research studies in the current pandemic situation'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A body of recently published, relevant research in a flagship journal to serve as anchor papers
- Editorial board members willing to serve as guest editors to accelerate the process
#### Constraints
- Used when there is no time to solicit and peer-review new research studies, 'because it would take time for scholars to conduct such research'

### Target Learners
- educational professionals integrating digital technologies into teaching practices

### Target Learning Goals
- informing rapid development and deployment of digital teaching and learning
