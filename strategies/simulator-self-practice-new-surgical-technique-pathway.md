# Simulator-based self-practice pathway for laser-naive surgeons adopting a new enucleation technique

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a learning pathway in which surgeons with consolidated endoscopic experience adopted a new laser technique by first visiting centers where the technique was regularly performed, then practicing all surgical steps on a dedicated simulator before operating on humans, without formal tutoring. The authors state "The main novelty during the learning process was the use of a simulator that faithfully reproduced all of the surgical steps in prostates of various shapes and volumes." This pathway allowed both surgeons to reach a self-assessed learning curve of about 30 cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Surgeons should already have long-term experience in traditional urological endoscopy
- Access to a simulator reproducing adenomas of various shapes and volumes
#### Constraints
- The authors state the findings still require further validation in larger multicentric study cohorts with several surgeons

### Target Learners
- laser-naive urologists with endoscopic experience

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical skill acquisition for thulium laser enucleation of the prostate
