# Sensitize instructors to students' likely conceptual difficulties when teaching first-year graduate quantum mechanics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 7 studies (4 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 7 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors use knowledge of documented student difficulties in planning instruction: "Instructional strategies that focus on improving student understanding of these concepts should take into account these difﬁculties" and "The results from this study can sensitize instructors of ﬁrst-year graduate quantum physics to the conceptual difﬁculties students are likely to face."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors need access to documented difficulty data such as the survey results reported here
#### Constraints
- Difficulties documented are for beginning graduate instruction on wave functions, energy measurement, and expectation values

### Target Learners
- first-year graduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- improved conceptual understanding of fundamental quantum mechanics
