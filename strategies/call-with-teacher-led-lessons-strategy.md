# Pair a CALL oral-language program with scripted teacher-led lessons for pre-teaching and reteaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators use scripted teacher-led lesson plans alongside the online CALL program, either to pre-teach content and language skills or to reteach skills students need additional practice with. Lessons are accessible through the program's progress monitoring platform, where educators view student achievement, progress, and usage data to provide targeted oral language practice in the classroom setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need access to the program's progress monitoring platform showing student activity, achievement, progress, and usage
#### Constraints
- Survey data showed only 40% of fall 2020 and 19% of spring 2021 respondents used the teacher-led lessons, with the majority citing time limitations

### Target Learners
- K-6 English learners using the CALL program

### Target Learning Goals
- Targeted oral language practice on skills students need additional practice with

## Claims

- [K-5 English learners who used the Lexia English CALL program scored significantly higher on the overall ELPAC than non-users (0.197 SD) in a 2020-21 district implementation](../claims/call-program-use-higher-overall-elpac-scores.md) [+W]
- [Program use was associated with significant gains on both ELPAC subdomains, with a larger effect for the oral subdomain (0.224 SD) than the written subdomain (0.153 SD)](../claims/call-program-oral-subdomain-larger-effect-than-written.md) [+W]
- [Effects of CALL program use varied by grade, with significant effects for grades 2 and 5 on overall and written scores and for grades 2, 4, and 5 on oral scores](../claims/call-program-effects-vary-by-grade.md) [+W]
- [Proficiency-level analyses found no significant differences between program users and non-users in odds of attaining ELPAC proficiency](../claims/call-program-no-significant-proficiency-differences.md) [~W]
