# Use free commercial apps such as Duolingo and Kahoot as gamified learning supports in language courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The reviewed studies show a practical, low-cost approach to gamification in SLA: incorporating freely available commercial apps into language courses as learning supports. The review notes "Duolingo and Kahoot were the most popular commercial apps" across the studies, used alongside or within courses of English, German, Spanish, French and Italian. Reported benefits include improved listening skills, positive attitudes towards the app, and increased student confidence and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Apps must be freely accessible or downloadable and usable on a computer or mobile device
#### Constraints
- Munday (2016) found students showed a positive attitude towards Duolingo at A1 level but not at B2 level, since they found the app too limited

### Target Learners
- higher education students learning a second language

### Target Learning Goals
- second language vocabulary, grammar, listening skills and motivation
