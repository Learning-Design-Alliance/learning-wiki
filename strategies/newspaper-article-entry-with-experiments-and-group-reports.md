# Use newspaper science articles as the entry point, then pair them with hands-on experiments and cooperative group reporting to trigger interest

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's own recommendation for effective implementation: begin newspapers-in-education teaching with the reading of science articles, then support students with small group discussions and hands-on experiments, and have students complete and share group reports through independent cooperative groups. The authors state this method "can trigger the learning and interests of students and offer in -depth learning experiences."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a newspaper with suitable science articles
- Opportunities for students to conduct hands-on experiments and work in independent cooperative groups
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a single-group study of 33 sixth graders; the authors' interpretation rather than a tested comparison

### Target Learners
- sixth grade elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- science interest
- science learning performance
- cooperative sharing of group reports
