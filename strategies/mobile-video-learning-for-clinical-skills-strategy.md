# Deliver a portion of clinical nursing skills instruction through mobile-based video learning that students can replay anytime

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that clinical skills teaching be supplemented with a mobile application containing procedure videos that students can access "at their own time and could watch the videos any number of times without any restriction." They suggest a certain percentage of every nursing course be delivered through a mobile-based learning approach, and that similar applications be extended to other significant nursing procedures and to new nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need access to a compatible smartphone and the application content must be reachable outside class time.
#### Constraints
- The study did not monitor how many times students watched the videos, which the authors note might have affected scores; compatibility was limited to android phones.

### Target Learners
- Nursing students
- New nurses in clinical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skill acquisition
- Procedural knowledge for operating theatre and other nursing procedures
