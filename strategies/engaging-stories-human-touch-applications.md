# Communicate societal applications of fundamental research through short engaging stories with a human touch

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
IPPOG's working group on Outreach of Applications for Society identified "the need for engaging stories with a human touch since they can be the most impactful and effective way to communicate to the public successful applications for the benefit of society". The group collects materials (e.g. from the CERN knowledge transfer collection), builds stories around available facts with anecdotes, and plans short stories with clear messages connecting science to everyday lives, covering subjects like PET and IRM scanners, cancer therapy, touch screen and GPS, to be offered via the IPPOG web page and RDB.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Story-building requires collecting materials and working around available facts while finding a human touch and anecdotes in each one.
#### Constraints
- The stories respond to growing pressure from taxpayers for tangible examples of return to society from fundamental research funding.

### Target Learners
- non-scientifically educated citizens
- young people considering physics studies

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the societal impact and applications of fundamental research
- motivation to undertake physics studies
