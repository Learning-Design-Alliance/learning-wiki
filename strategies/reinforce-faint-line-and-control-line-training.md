# Reinforce test-versus-control line distinction and faint-line positivity in diagnostic reading training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A targeted training strategy addressing the specific interpretation errors observed in the study. The article states: "Training programs should reinforce the distinction between test and control lines, ensure that CHWs make that distinction accurately, and emphasize that even faint lines indicate a positive result." It pairs with practice recommendations for blood collection: trainees should "practice with whatever blood collection device will be packaged with their RDT(s)" until they can collect the required volume every time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must include practice with the actual blood collection device packaged with the RDTs CHWs will use
#### Constraints
- The faint-positive decline appeared at 12 months specifically; the article recommends semi-annual supervisory visits stressing this skill

### Target Learners
- community health workers using malaria rapid diagnostic tests in village practice

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate interpretation of RDT result lines including faint positives and invalid results
