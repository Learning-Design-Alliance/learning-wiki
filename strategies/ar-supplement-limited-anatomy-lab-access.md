# Use AR to supplement limited anatomy lab access by simulating visuospatial aspects of cadaveric dissection with multi-user collaborative functions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends AR as a supplement rather than a replacement for cadaveric study. It states that "AR has the potential to supplement limited anatomy lab access by simulating the visuospatial aspects of cadaveric dissection with multi-user collaborative functions." This strategy responds to declines in body donation, specialist educators, and teaching hours coupled with growing concerns around ethics and equal access.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AR cannot replace cadaveric study nor sufficiently replicate touch and feel interactions
#### Constraints
- Commercial software simulating dissection uses heavily stylised anatomical models at significant financial investment

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical and allied health students

### Target Learning Goals
- Visuospatial understanding of anatomical structures
