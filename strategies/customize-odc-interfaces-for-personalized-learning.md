# Customize online community interfaces with filtering, structured comments, highlighting, and interactive note-taking to satisfy users' learning needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on their results, the authors "conclude with design considerations for customizing the interface of online communities to satisfy users' learning needs", discussing "the implications of customizing the features of online communities to support personalized learning tasks". The favored features were filtering cases by knowledge points, structured comments, keyword highlighting, and interactive mindmap note-taking, and the authors note DesignLearner could be extended as a social annotation tool engaging groups of learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community content must be structured by a knowledge taxonomy so that filters and highlighting can target learners' interested knowledge points
#### Constraints
- The article states the note-taking focus is individual, with social annotation extension left to future discussion

### Target Learners
- novices using online communities for informal learning

### Target Learning Goals
- acquiring and managing domain knowledge from user-generated community content
