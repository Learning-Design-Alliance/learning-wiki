# Dual-sourcing measurement strategy: pair validated self-report surveys with trace-based proxies validated on labeled subsamples

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that trait-aware learning analytics adopt a dual-sourcing measurement strategy: use validated survey instruments where feasible and behavioral trace proxies (e.g., multi-term delay indices) where surveys are impractical. The article states that "TALA adopts adual-sourcingstrategy (i.e., surveys where feasible, trace-based proxies where needed) paired with validation based on survey measures in smaller samples." Proxies should establish convergent validity against gold-standard surveys and temporal stability on a labeled subsample, with uncertainty reported explicitly; lower-fidelity proxies remain usable for institutional trend analytics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Behavioral proxies require convergent validity against surveys and temporal stability established on a labeled subsample before operational use.
#### Constraints
- Ultra-brief scales prioritize efficiency but yield lower psychometric precision; single-item measures may be inappropriate for routing interventions unless locally validated.

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate trait measurement for personalization
- reduced survey burden
