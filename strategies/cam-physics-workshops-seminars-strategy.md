# Use concept attainment model in classroom physics teaching and orient teachers via workshops and seminars

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that because the concept attainment model was effective for physics concept understanding, it "should be used by the school teacher in class room teaching especially in teaching physics concepts". To build teacher interest, the authors propose that "workshops and seminars should be organized". This strategy applies the study's findings to classroom practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need orientation to the concept attainment model, which the article proposes to supply through workshops and seminars.
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is grounded in a single quasi-experimental study in two schools in Mohindergarh (Haryana) with ninth-grade students.

### Target Learners
- Ninth-grade students (14-15 years old)

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition and understanding of physics concepts (motion, inertia, acceleration, force, gravitation, work, power, sound, evaporation, change of states)
