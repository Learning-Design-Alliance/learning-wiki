# Online Data Retrieval Test plus Traffic Light Feedback activities for formative feedback on notebook keeping in pass-fail laboratories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A short online review activity combining two ideas from UCL: the 'Data Retrieval Test' (DRT), where students find information such as sample mass, dates and time intervals in their notebooks, and 'Traffic Light Feedback' (TLF), which rates quality aspects such as recording habits on a formative scale. "We created a short online review activity consisting of 'survey' questions where any answer was marked as correct", so students can identify what they do well and choose areas to improve. Feedback statements are always positive and encouraging, suggesting strategies such as using stars, boxes or colours to highlight key information. Completion is required but carries no penalty for poor habits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pass-fail or completion-only laboratory structure so admitting poor note taking carries no penalty
- Students also fill in a logbook section with two things done well and two aspects to improve
#### Constraints
- Developed and used in a pass-fail laboratory; the authors note its use in graded laboratories should be straightforward but have not tested it there

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- laboratory notebook keeping
- self-guided review and improvement of record-keeping habits
