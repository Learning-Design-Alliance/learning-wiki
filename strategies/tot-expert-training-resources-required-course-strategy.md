# Train health teachers with nutrition experts and provide resources and required lessons to promote school nutrition education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's teacher-informed strategy set for promoting nutrition education in K-12 schools centers on professional development and institutional support. Teachers identified "Being trained by nutrition experts (41.3%)" as the top strategy, followed by providing teaching resources and setting nutrition lessons as a required course. The authors recommend that governments and school administrators recognize the requirement to teach nutrition and provide sufficient resources for teacher training, citing the Korean Nutrition Teacher System as a model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to qualified nutrition experts or training providers for teacher professional development
#### Constraints
- The article notes training opportunities are scarce and training providers may not be available or qualified
- Teachers may lack time or interest in additional training

### Target Learners
- K-12 health teachers in Shanghai, China

### Target Learning Goals
- Nutrition knowledge and teaching capability for health teachers
- Delivery of nutrition education to K-12 students
