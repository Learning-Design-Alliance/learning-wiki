# Deploy AI-assisted pre-consultation nutrition questioning in clinic waiting rooms to prepare patients and focus dietitian time on specialized guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends introducing a system where patients consult AI before meeting a dietitian: installing tablet devices in hospital or clinic waiting rooms lets patients ask basic questions about diet and nutrition, gathering preliminary information in advance. This helps dietitians understand patients' needs beforehand and complements patients' basic knowledge, allowing dietitians to focus on deeper, more specialized content during the consultation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tablet devices installed in hospital or clinic waiting rooms
- An AI system capable of answering basic diet and nutrition questions

### Target Learners
