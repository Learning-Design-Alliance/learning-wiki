# Standardized instructor feedback sessions of 10-12 minutes, limited to three, with learner-chosen timing of optional sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a bounded feedback recipe: up to three instructor feedback sessions, each "10 to 12 min of standardized assessment of an operation on the VR simulator and assessment of the automated feedback produced by the VR simulator". The first follows the first operation; the participant decides when to take the second and third. One instructor uses the same template for every participant to ensure consistency, operationalizing self-directed choice of when to receive feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single instructor providing feedback with the same template for every participant, to ensure consistency
- Participants in the intervention group choose individually when they want the optional second and third feedback sessions
#### Constraints
- Feedback is capped at a maximum of three sessions of 10-12 minutes each
- The first feedback is fixed, given following the first operation on the VR simulator

### Target Learners
- Novice surgeons and medical students training complex operational tasks on a VR simulator

### Target Learning Goals
- Reaching a predefined proficiency level in complex laparoscopic operational tasks efficiently
