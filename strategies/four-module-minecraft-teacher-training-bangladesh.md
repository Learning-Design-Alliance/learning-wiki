# Four-module progressive teacher training curriculum totaling 72 hours with optional authoring module

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Institutional and Training Layer specifies that "Teachers need real training, not just a quick demo" and defines four modules: Module 1 covers "basic digital literacy over 12 hours," Module 2 focuses on teaching integration "over 20 hours," Module 3 covers advanced features "over 16 hours," and Module 4 is "an optional 24-hour deep dive into creating your own content." Each module has an assessment, from a skills check in M1 to publishing a reusable world in M4. Support includes Bangla video tutorial libraries, peer mentoring networks, online forums, and webinars.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnerships with education authorities, pilot programs, open licensing, and alignment with government digital initiatives
#### Constraints
- Training demands are named by the authors as a real-world challenge that remains to be tested

### Target Learners
- Teachers in under-resourced Bangladeshi schools with low ICT confidence and limited experience with interactive pedagogy

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher ability to deploy, orchestrate, and author localized Minecraft-based lessons aligned to the national curriculum
