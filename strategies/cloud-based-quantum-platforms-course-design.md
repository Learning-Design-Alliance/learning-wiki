# Design quantum courses around cloud-based platforms and simulators where hardware access is limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because of 'limited access to real quantum hardware in Latin America', the courses are designed around cloud-based quantum platforms such as IBM Quantum and Amazon Braket plus simulation tools like Qiskit and Cirq. Hands-on programming of quantum algorithms bridges theory and practice, lets students experiment with quantum circuits before using actual hardware, and familiarizes them with industry-standard tools and workflows.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to cloud-based quantum platforms (IBM Quantum, Amazon Braket) and simulation tools (Qiskit, Cirq)
#### Constraints
- Motivated by limited access to real quantum hardware in Latin America

### Target Learners
- Latin American university students in regions without local quantum hardware

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical experience programming quantum algorithms and preparedness for research or industry careers
