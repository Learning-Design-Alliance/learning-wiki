# Multiple Representations: Visual Representations

## Description
Using visual representations can lead to clearer delivery of content and greater retention of key concepts in long-term memory. Visuals also serve an important role in maintaining learner attention and are a touchpoint for learners to refer back to on their own as external memory aids and for individual practice. Visuals can include videos, images, graphics, and other forms of media.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to visual materials, such as videos, images, and graphics. Technology for displaying visuals, such as projectors or computers.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective for all learners, especially those with visual impairments. Visuals must be carefully chosen and aligned with content to avoid confusion or distraction.
