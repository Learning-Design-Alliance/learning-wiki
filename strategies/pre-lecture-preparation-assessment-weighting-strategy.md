# Weight pre-lecture preparation activities in assessment to encourage a preparation-oriented approach in flipped classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because roughly half of the students approached the flipped course by prioritising post-lecture assessment activities, the authors recommend teachers give preparatory online activities assessment weight so students value preparation. They suggest that "teachers can use 5% to 10% of course marks for students’ timely completion (before the lectures) of pre-lecture videos and pre-lecture quizzes", alongside an online discussion board for exchanging understandings of pre-lecture readings and videos, and tutorial time for group presentations summarising pre-lecture material.
