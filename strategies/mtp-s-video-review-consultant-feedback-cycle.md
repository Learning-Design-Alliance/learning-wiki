# Cycle of videotaped instruction, consultant-selected segment review, and strategy conferencing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MTP-S coaching follows a recurring cycle: teachers submit videotaped sessions about twice a month; a consultant identifies brief segments for review; the teacher reviews the segments for their own behaviors and student reactions; and the teacher confers with the consultant in a 20- to 30-minute phone discussion in which the consultant "recommends strategies to enhance teacher–student interactions". Consultants also direct teachers to annotated video exemplars on the MTP-S website.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained consultant available throughout the school year
- Teachers videotape their focal class and submit sessions biweekly
- An initial workshop defining MTP-S principles and CLASS-S dimensions
#### Constraints
- The intervention involved a total of about 20 hours of in-service training over 13 months

### Target Learners
- middle and high school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- enhancing teacher-student interactions
