# Adopt the Eurogame victory-point layer with optional arbitrary puzzles in other educational escape rooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending educational escape rooms with a Eurogame-style victory point system and optional, arbitrary Eurogame puzzles that need no strong topical connection to the subject, so players self-adjust their challenge level by choice. They state: "The Eurogame concept using arbitrary EPs to collect VP can be applied to every eER and our findings support its implementation." The score list of VP can be reset for each new run, with the decision left to the e-game master, and EPs can include commercial puzzles, offering an optional supplement to any escape room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A rulebook listing VP values for mandatory and optional puzzles
- An e-game master managing the per-run score list
#### Constraints
- The article states EPs are not mandatory and are fully optional; PPs remain mandatory to master the escape room
- The article notes EPs are not Eurogames themselves but arbitrary commercial or self-made puzzles earning points

### Target Learners
- Students in educational escape rooms of any taught subject or topic

### Target Learning Goals
- Motivation and engagement through game-like challenge collection
- Decision-making and setting priorities by gauging puzzles
