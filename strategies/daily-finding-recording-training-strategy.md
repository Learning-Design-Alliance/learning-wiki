# Two-year daily training regimen of recording the 11 endoscopic findings and predicting H. pylori status for every patient

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured on-the-job training recipe in which trainee endoscopists, during daily clinical practice, record the presence or absence of 11 defined findings and predict infection status for every patient endoscoped, then are reassessed after two years. The article states training "was conducted in a systematical manner over two years of daily clinical practice" with these two recording activities. It reports the regimen improved beginners' yield and agreement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees judge every patient's images and record the 11 findings and a status prediction into the electronic endoscopic database
- Consensus seminars on the finding-to-status relations are held before judging begins
#### Constraints
- The reported benefit was observed in two beginner endoscopists over two years in one center

### Target Learners
- beginner endoscopists in gastroenterology training

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate endoscopic diagnosis of H. pylori infection status
