# Provide regular early shifts and periodic rotations to high-volume areas for new HEMS physicians with low overall prehospital anaesthesia exposure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
For services where overall exposure to prehospital advanced airway management is low, the article suggests maintaining repetition frequency through scheduling. The authors write that "Regular shifts at the beginning of the HEMS career are likely important even for consultant anaesthesiologists" and that "periodic rotations in high-volume areas might be advantageous." This addresses their hypothesis that the interval between repetitions influences early skill development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sufficient baseline of in-hospital anaesthesia experience before beginning prehospital work, as the study population had at least two years of such experience
#### Constraints
- The article notes a larger study would be required to assess whether similar cumulative experience gained over varying timeframes achieves different outcomes

### Target Learners
- Newly recruited HEMS physicians in their onboarding phase

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining and developing proficiency in prehospital anaesthesia through frequent repetition
