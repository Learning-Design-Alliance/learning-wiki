# Enhance VR-based simulation debriefing with automated checklists, multiplayer platforms, and hybrid feedback approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because VR's head-mounted displays prevented tutors from observing intern performance in real time, the article recommends future VR designs incorporate automated monitoring and richer feedback channels. Tutors "suggested the future integration of automated checklists and multiplayer platforms to enhance feedback and encourage peer learning," and the discussion proposes hybrid approaches such as AI-assisted feedback and real-time instructor input via integrated communication tools. Culturally adapted debriefing frameworks are also recommended for high-power-distance contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system for real-time monitoring or automated checklist systems to track intern performance during VR scenarios
#### Constraints
- Further research is required to clarify the impact of debriefing methods in VR-based training, as current evidence remains inconclusive

### Target Learners
- Medical interns in simulation-based emergency training

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflective learning, clinical reasoning, and peer learning during simulation debriefing
