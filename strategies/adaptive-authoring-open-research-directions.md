# Open design directions for adaptive authoring: crowdsourced standards, individual differences, modular customization, path visualization, and instructor real-time control

## Description
The editorial's forward-looking recommendations for future authoring research. It asks "Could standards be created that would allow crowd-sourced creation of adaptive content by teachers or learners?" and "Could adaptive learning systems be created that take into account individual differences among learners, perhaps based on their previous experiences, disabilities, or cultural background?", and proposes "A modular system might allow for local customizations so that the learning experience is tailored for the local context and student interests."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standards enabling crowdsourced content creation; system designs accounting for prior experiences, disabilities, or cultural background; modularity for local customization
#### Constraints
- These are posed as open questions and possibilities, not as tested or implemented approaches

### Target Learners

- learners
- instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- creating, customizing, and assuring the quality of adaptive learning experiences
