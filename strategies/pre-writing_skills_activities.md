# Pre-Writing Skills Activities

## Description
Activities focused on building foundational skills necessary before formal handwriting instruction begins, such as line awareness, spatial awareness, and pencil grasp. These skills are crucial for developing legible and efficient handwriting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Basic art supplies (paper, crayons, markers), various pre-writing templates, and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not address specific letter formation issues. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- PreK-1st grade learners or those with underdeveloped pre-writing skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve line awareness, spatial awareness, and pencil grasp in preparation for handwriting.

### Affordances
- Develops essential fine motor and visual perceptual skills. Builds a strong foundation for legible handwriting.

### Personalization
- Activities can be modified based on skill level, focusing on specific areas such as line tracking or shape formation. Can also be adapted to sensory needs.
