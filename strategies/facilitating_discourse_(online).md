# Facilitating Discourse (Online)

## Description
Facilitating discourse in online learning involves more than simply requiring students to post in a discussion forum. It includes regularly reading and providing feedback on student postings, encouraging participation, moving the discussion forward when it stalls or gets off track, identifying and drawing out areas of agreement and disagreement, pointing out linkages, and helping students articulate shared understandings. Immediacy behaviors, such as referring to students by name and sharing personal examples, enhance both social and teaching presence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a discussion forum or platform, active instructor participation, and clear guidelines for participation and feedback.
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging to manage and moderate, requires active instructor involvement, and may be difficult to ensure equitable participation.

### Affordances
- Encourages active participation, enhances social interaction, promotes deeper understanding through peer learning, and fosters a sense of community. Allows instructors to guide discussions and ensure relevance.

### Personalization
- Can be personalized by allowing students to choose discussion topics, providing tailored feedback, and using diverse formats (e.g., text, audio, video).
