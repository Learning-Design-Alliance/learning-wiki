# Use questionnaire results to design targeted professional development for clinical educators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions use OCTQ item-level results to develop their clinical educators. For reflective practice, it states institutions "could use this information to design professional development activities for their clinical educators to help them work with students to develop their reflective practice and self-directed learning skills". Similarly, global-rating results combined with individual items may assist educators and supervisors to develop targeted professional development activities or assist promotion decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Item-level results interpreted alongside global ratings because of possible ceiling effects
#### Constraints
- In the study, results were used for feedback purposes only and not for employment decisions or reward

### Target Learners
- Clinical educators supervising students in student-led clinics

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving clinical teaching quality, feedback provision, and stimulation of student reflective practice
