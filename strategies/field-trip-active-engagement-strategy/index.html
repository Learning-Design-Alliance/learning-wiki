Use field trips to promote active engagement, self-motivation, discovery learning and learning by experience in practical subjects¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that "Teachers should expose students to practical works through field trip as a method of teaching which will promote and encourage social interaction, active engagement in learning, self‐motivation, discovery learning, learning by doing and learning by experience." The strategy takes learners outside the classroom to observe engineering materials and works firsthand, supporting the practical orientation of the subject.

Claims¶

  • Upper basic students taught Basic Technology practical skills via field trip scored higher on a post-test than students taught by expository method [+W]
  • Among students taught Basic Technology via field trip, male and female post-test performance did not differ in the authors' conclusion, though the article also reports a significant difference [~W]
  • Teaching Arabic oral skills with a Virtual Trip Model produces significantly higher post-test achievement than traditional teaching among third graders [+W]
