# Use the simulator as the first step toward a BG-PDT simulation-based training program with multimodal competency assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose building a comprehensive BG-PDT training protocol for residents on the simulator, using the multimodal evaluation (success rates, time, ICSAD parameters, blind GRS) to track competency acquisition and learning curves. They state the multimodal evaluation will be useful for the evaluation of competency acquisition through the training program, having determined the basal level of novices and the benchmark level from experts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulator that discriminates novices from experts
- Multimodal performance evaluation including motion tracking and blind video assessment
#### Constraints
- Competency transfer from the simulator to real patients has not yet been analyzed; the authors state this as future work

### Target Learners
- Senior-year anesthesiology or internal medicine residents in ICU training

### Target Learning Goals
- Procedural competence in bronchoscopy-guided percutaneous dilatational tracheostomy
