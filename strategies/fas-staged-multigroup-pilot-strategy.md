# Staged multi-group pilot and mixed-method evaluation with a benchmark control group

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FAS research plan is a mixed-method non-experimental approach with pre- and post-tests, questionnaires and tool testing, tracked with learning analytics for iterative design. Testing is staged across groups: Focus Group A reviews lesson content, Test Group B pilots the prototype, Group C pilots the Chinese translation, Group D addresses technical challenges, and Control Group E provides a benchmark. The article states "A comparison student group (Control Group E) provides a benchmark against results with those who completed the AI modules".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruitment of first to fifth year undergraduate participants
- Multiple rounds of tool testing to ensure quality and reliability
#### Constraints
- Non-experimental design; benefits are to be measured with pre-post outcome tests compared with control group results

### Target Learners
- First to fifth year undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic integrity knowledge and competency
