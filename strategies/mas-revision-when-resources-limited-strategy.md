# Offer mobile application simulator revision as a low-cost, portable adjunct where skills-centre resources or access are limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends MAS-based revision as "a desirable revision alternative when traditional training equipment or facilities are limited", for example at satellite hospitals without skills centres or during independent study. Because the app is freely downloadable, it may confer economic benefits for trainees and training providers. Students in the study found the interface useful and user-friendly and endorsed its addition to the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The procedure to be revised must exist in the MAS procedure library and be developed and reviewed by procedure-specific experts
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is an underpowered single-school pilot testing one procedure, so generalisation to other populations and skills awaits larger multi-school studies

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- junior practitioners lacking access to skills-centre resources

### Target Learning Goals
- rehearsal of step-by-step ward-based clinical skills procedures
