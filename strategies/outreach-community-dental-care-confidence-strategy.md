# Use outreach community dental care experiences to build student clinical confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends exposing students to outreach teaching experiences such as community dental care, "for it proved to be a useful adjunct for clinical experience, communication skills as well as self-confidence." This strategy is offered as one way to overcome students' anxiety about independent practice given reduced clinical exposure during the pandemic.
