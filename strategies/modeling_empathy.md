# Modeling Empathy

## Description
Teachers' behaviors and actions create the culture in the classroom. Treat students as respected co-learners to model how all people should be treated. Reaffirm a positive, empathic response for all students by remaining consistent in pro-actions and reactions. When a student seems upset, demonstrate empathy by making eye contact, speaking privately, and maintaining respect. In emotional situations, remember that students may lack coping mechanisms and experience. The empathic response does not seek to embarrass, belittle, or punish the student but protects the learning environment and addresses the disruption with attention to the context and emotions of individual students. Own mistakes publicly to show it’s OK for students to take risks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of emotional intelligence, commitment to respectful communication, time to engage with students individually.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent effort, can be challenging in situations with significant classroom disruptions, may be difficult to implement with very large class sizes.
