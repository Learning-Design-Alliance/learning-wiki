# Enhanced web-based apparatus diagrams with hover-linked photographs, part numbers, and additional information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To assist students with minimal direct instructor involvement, the authors developed enhanced web-based apparatus diagrams. As the cursor moves over a component in a diagram such as Fig. 8, "a photograph of the physical device appears, together with the manufacturer's part number and links to additional information." This lets students identify unfamiliar modular RF components on their own during the experiment.

#### Requirements
