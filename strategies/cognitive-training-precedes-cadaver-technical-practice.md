# Provide cognitive training (live demonstration plus reference DVD) before cadaver-based technical skills practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that cognitive preparation precede technical skills training on the cadaver model. Novices and experts watched a live demonstration of the 5 tasks by a national laparoscopic trainer followed by an interactive session, and a DVD of the demonstration was available for reference. Because no trainee or expert referred to the DVD or asked for help, the autonomy domain was removed from the GOALS scale, which the authors read as evidence that "adequate cognitive training is of paramount importance to learn technical skills more effectively and should precede technical skills training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A live expert demonstration of the tasks and an interactive question session
- A recorded demonstration DVD available during practice sessions
- An invigilator to record help-seeking and DVD references if autonomy is to be assessed
#### Constraints
- If assessments are done face-to-face rather than by blinded video, the authors recommend the autonomy domain be retained in the assessment scale

### Target Learners
- Junior surgical trainees who are novices in laparoscopic surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective acquisition of laparoscopic technical skills with retained trainee autonomy
