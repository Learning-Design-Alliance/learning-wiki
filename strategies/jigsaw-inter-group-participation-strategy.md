# Use inter-group Jigsaw participation so each group member teaches his topic to other groups and returns to teach his initial group.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The fourth step of the Education Management Model uses inter-group participation based on the Jigsaw participative model, an invention of Arvin Sharon. Each group member goes to other groups to teach his subject, learns the basic points of other groups' subjects, returns to his initial group, and teaches his learning to others. The article notes this model is used more than other participative teaching models in the empirical researches on the effects of learning management model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The class is first divided into two-member groups with each taking twice its share
- Each group member teaches his subject according to the Jigsaw participative model
- After learning and teaching conceptual indexes of topics and subtopics, he returns to his initial group
#### Constraints
- The article recommends using the Jigsaw participative model as one of the participative models rather than ordinary group procedures

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students
- Students of second grade of public elementary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Familiarity with concept indexes of all content topics
- Teaching and learning basic points of subjects of other groups
