# Use early assignments and repeated reminders to teach students the tiered assessment scheme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the scheme differs from what students have previously encountered, the authors deliberately use the fortnightly assignments — the first task completed and marked — to communicate the assessment scheme, alongside introductory materials and the course profile. They report that the current scheme "does not appear to result in excessive confusion" but that it "is necessary to clearly explain it, and to remind students when appropriate". This makes scheme communication an ongoing teaching activity, not a one-off document.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments must be structured so their instructions model the scheme (Required/Additional labels)
- Explicit description of the scheme in introductory materials and the course profile
#### Constraints
- The authors judge the system a work in progress that can be further improved, based on student feedback and perceived correlation between achievement and grades

### Target Learners
- Upper-level undergraduate students encountering tiered assessment for the first time

### Target Learning Goals
- Students understanding how their performance maps to final grades across both tiers
