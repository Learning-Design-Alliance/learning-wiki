# Discharge geography, advertisement and author prestige from test-selection decisions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies a triumvirate of inexact criteria shaping test adoption: geographical preference, commercial publicity, and the prestige of the test's author. It recommends these be discharged from the educational judiciary, since the mails allow transmission of tests across areas, advertisement should bear no weight, and original data and construction technique are mostly available for direct inspection.
