# Deploy LLM-driven virtual patients for scalable, standardized deliberate practice of psychiatric interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends LLM-driven multimodal simulation as a scalable solution to structural bottlenecks in psychiatric training, where standardized patients are resource-intensive and case exposure is geographically uneven. The system "provides a highly scalable, standardized, and safe environment" aligned with Competency-Based Medical Education, generating "continuous, objective data traces, enabling granular formative assessments."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LLM-driven virtual patient system with dialogue and assessment logging capabilities
#### Constraints
- Standardized patients remain the gold standard for relational training

### Target Learners
- undergraduate psychiatry students
- medical trainees in less-resourced regions

### Target Learning Goals
- psychiatric interviewing skills
- clinical competence
