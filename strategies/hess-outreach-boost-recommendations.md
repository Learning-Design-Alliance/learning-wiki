# Fund school visits, a local visit coordinator, teacher training and artist-in-residence to boost H.E.S.S. outreach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the April 2021 H.E.S.S. collaboration meeting, outreach discussions identified additional funds as opening several possibilities: "supporting local schools to visit the observatory, or hiring someone locally who could coordinate visits via tour operating companies". The collaboration could also implement a teacher training program or host an artist-in-residence to further boost social impact.
