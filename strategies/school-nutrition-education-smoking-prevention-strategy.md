# School-based nutrition education, healthy food provision and smoking-prevention sessions to support adolescent achievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend incorporating nutrition education into the school curriculum, teaching adolescents the benefits of the Mediterranean diet, minimizing the sale of low-nutrient high-energy foods in school shops, and providing healthy alternatives. They also recommend that "Health educators need to also tackle the subject of smoking, discuss the negative effect of tobacco use, the hazards of smoking, teach adolescents how to be aware of social influences and how to resist them." Sessions should also target parents as key players in home habits.
