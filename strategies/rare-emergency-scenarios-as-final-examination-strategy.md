# Repeat and test rare emergency scenarios (pump failure, gas supply failure) as final practical examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course repeatedly drilled the two most serious ECMO emergencies and then used them as practical tests: "we did not only repeat many times during workshops on trouble shooting pump failure but also chose the scenario for one of the practical tests at the end of our course", with gas supply failure as the second scenario, based on real-life experience. Pump failure management achieved the best post-course score.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity simulators capable of reproducing pump failure and gas supply failure scenarios
- ELSO-endorsed course structure with practical examination components
#### Constraints
- Reported for a three-day simulation course for physicians; the article notes only short-term results are available

### Target Learners
- physicians training in extracorporeal life support

### Target Learning Goals
- managing pump failure during ECMO
- recognizing gas supply failure on VV ECMO
