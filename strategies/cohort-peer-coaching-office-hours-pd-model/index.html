Combine live cohort sessions, self-guided application, peer coaching, and virtual office hours in literacy professional learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's delivery model combines several professional learning activities: "Program participants attend live professional learning sessions with a dedicated cohort, complete self-guided learning and application, engage in a peer coaching model, and attend virtual office hours with their Greenfield Learning coaches." Sessions are delivered virtually, in-person, or in a hybrid model depending on district preference, and no additional staff or technology are required.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A dedicated cohort for live sessions and access to Greenfield Learning coaches for virtual office hours.

Constraints¶

  • Delivery format depends on each district's preference; no additional staff or technology are required.

Target Learners¶

  • third-grade teachers and literacy educators in elementary schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improve literacy instruction practices
  • raise student reading achievement
