# Use the format-focus-tone framework as a starting point for teacher training on written feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes that "Our 3 dimensions format, focus and tone of feedback may provide useful starting points for teacher training." Early experiences in training sessions using these characteristics were positive, and the dimensions were seen by the authors' teachers as a feasible framework for providing written feedback on reflective writing.
