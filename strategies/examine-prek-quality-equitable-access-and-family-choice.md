# Research and policy attention should now examine PreK program quality and equitable access across racial, ethnic, and linguistic groups, and how families choose among PreK options

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's concluding recommendation is that, now that enrollment expansion is underway, attention must turn to whether programs are high quality and whether access is equitable. It states that "ensuring the quality of these programs is also crucial" and that it "will therefore be important to examine the quality of these programs and whether children from different racial, ethnic, and linguistic backgrounds have equitable access to high-quality PreK." It also recommends research on how families make PreK decisions, noting possible influences such as program accessibility, hours of care, awareness of options, and preference for private programs.
