Use ChatGPT 4 for drafting MCQs while avoiding ChatGPT 3.5 and Llama 2 for examination question generation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q2–q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the network meta-analysis findings, the article states that "ChatGPT 4 is potentially the best resource for educators to generate drafts of MCQs". Other LLMs such as ChatGPT 3.5 or Llama 2 "are less likely to produce accurate and unbiased content, given their worse performance on qualitative and quantitative measures of question quality". Educators should still review all generated content for accuracy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators must review generated content for accuracy and coherence
  • Understanding of LLM capabilities and limitations

Constraints¶

  • Performance of questions created by ChatGPT 4 on quantitative indices is unknown
  • Certainty of evidence is very low across all outcomes
  • Future or novel models may not align with the patterns observed

Target Learners¶

  • Medical educators
  • Health professions educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Generating examination questions
  • Assessment design in medical education

Claims¶

  • ChatGPT 3.5 performs worse than ChatGPT 4 and Llama 2 on question clarity and distractor quality [+W]
  • Llama 2 generates MCQs of worse quality than humans on question clarity and distractor quality [+W]
  • AI-generated assessment items frequently need faculty correction, supporting AI as a draft generator within quality assurance [~W]
  • Faculty-generated SBMCQs showed superior discrimination indices and distractor effectiveness compared with ChatGPT-generated SBMCQs [-W]
