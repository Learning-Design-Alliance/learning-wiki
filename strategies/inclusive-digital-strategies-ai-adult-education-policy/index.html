Pursue inclusive digital strategies, educator professional development, and ethical AI governance for AI in adult education¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper's policy recommendations center on inclusive digital strategies: "A key priority is the development of inclusive digital strategies that ensure equitable access to AI-supported learning." This includes infrastructure investment (broadband, devices), targeted initiatives for disadvantaged groups, digital literacy promotion, and educator training spanning technical, pedagogical, and ethical competencies. It further recommends ethical AI frameworks emphasizing transparency, fairness, accountability, and human rights, plus stakeholder collaboration among governments, institutions, educators, and learners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Investment in infrastructure such as broadband connectivity and digital devices, plus training and professional development so educators can integrate AI effectively.

Constraints¶

  • The paper notes international ethical principles exist but their implementation remains uneven, and achieving lifelong learning ecosystems requires coordinated policy efforts and long-term investment.

Target Learners¶

  • adult learners, particularly disadvantaged and underserved groups
  • educators in adult education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • equitable access to AI-supported learning
  • digital and AI literacy
  • responsible and critical engagement with AI technologies

Related Strategies¶

  • Use the integrated technical–pedagogical limitation framework to inform teacher education, instructional design, and institutional AI policies in undergraduate EFL programs
  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Four strategic recommendations for supporting AI literacy across settings
  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
  • Adopt a systems approach to AI integration rather than spotlights or gaps
  • Embed AI policy literacy, peer-led communities of practice, and shared AI resources into professional development
