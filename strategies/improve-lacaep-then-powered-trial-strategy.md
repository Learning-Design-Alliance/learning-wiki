# Improve the e-learning package's explanations and feedback, then re-test in an adequately powered trial of 90 participants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on pilot feedback and effect-size data, the authors recommend refining the LACAeP before formal effectiveness testing. They state that "Improving the LACAeP before testing it in an appropriately powered trial, informed by the differences observed, is required", and calculate that a trial with 90 participants (45 per group) is needed for 80% power to detect a clinically relevant difference of 2.0 correct classifications out of 20 at the 5% significance level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Addressing participant-identified weaknesses: more explanation of terms and routes, more practice cases, better feedback on errors, and resolution of technical problems
#### Constraints
- The recommended trial size is specific to detecting a minimum worthwhile difference of 2 out of 20 correct classifications

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in training, initially paediatric medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate and consistent causality assessment of adverse drug reactions
