# Use bi-weekly social media quizzes based on recent articles to stimulate learning and audience engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project posts article highlights, fun facts and reader-question answers daily on Facebook and Instagram, and "include a bi-weekly quiz on Instagram with questions based on our recent articles in order to stimulate learning and involve our audience." The team reports this engagement format appears to be among the audience's favorites and drives readers to ask further questions about the topics discussed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a social media presence with daily posts leading back to full articles on the project website
#### Constraints
- the quiz content is limited to questions based on the project's own recent articles

### Target Learners
- social media followers, especially young adults (audience age span peaks at 25-34)

### Target Learning Goals
- stimulating learning and discussion of recent astrophysics article topics
