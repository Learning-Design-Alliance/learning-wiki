# Deliver A2i professional development through a 90-minute launch session, grade-team Literacy Huddles, and individualized coaching sessions over a 3-year rollout

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This professional development strategy combines a 90-minute getting started with A2i session in the fall, "up to 6 virtual meetings known as A2i Literacy Huddles" conducted with grade-level teams and led by a Literacy Specialist, and 3 virtual individualized A2i Classroom Support Sessions providing one-on-one coaching and feedback. The program follows a standard 3-year rollout plan commencing with K-1, then progressing to K-2 and K-3, with optional In-Person Flex Days adding further training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Literacy Specialist to lead the grade-level A2i Literacy Huddles
- A standard 3-year rollout plan commencing with K-1, then progressing to K-2 and K-3
#### Constraints
- Described as the standard rollout structure; the review does not separately evaluate the effectiveness of this PD model

### Target Learners
- Pre-K through Grade 3 teachers adopting A2i

### Target Learning Goals
- Individualized literacy instruction tailored to students' prior performance and needs
