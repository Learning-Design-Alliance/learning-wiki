# Post-GOT professional development should differentiate learning paths, complement traditional formats with communities of practice and peer support, and manage work overload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that post-GOT professional development account for teachers' heterogeneous digital competencies by creating different learning paths depending on initial stage, and that institutions "complement traditional instances of development with emerging approaches (for example, the development of communities of practice or peer support)". It further recommends incorporating pandemic lessons, providing systematic guidance on curriculum development and learning design, pedagogical frameworks such as Laurillard's conversational framework, and attention to time management and work overload.

## Design Implications
