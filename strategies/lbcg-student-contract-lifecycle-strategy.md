# Student-designed extra assignment contracts: propose, draft a contract with deliverables and proficiencies, revise, complete, and review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Extra assignments implement the contract component of LBCG through a lifecycle: the student proposes a project idea, drafts "a contract which clearly outlines the project, deliverables, and proficiencies to be demonstrated", the instructor suggests revisions until both are satisfied, the student completes the work, and the instructor reviews and requests revisions needed to satisfy the contract's proficiencies. Completion increments the grade by one step, while incomplete extra assignments can never decrement it. LaTeX templates with fill-in-the-blank proficiency language help students understand what a proposal and a proficiency look like.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Contracts must clearly outline the project, deliverables, and proficiencies to be demonstrated
- Multi-student projects require a clear division of labor and deliverable complexity commensurate with multiple students' work
#### Constraints
- New proposals were not accepted after a set date late in the semester, and all extra assignments were due by the end of finals week

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics and STEM majors

### Target Learning Goals
- student agency in designing projects and demonstrating self-chosen proficiencies
- scientific communication
