# Extend the ACE tool with additional scenarios across harm, diagnosis and prognosis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future refinement add patient scenarios beyond therapy questions. They note clinicians encounter "a variety of patient scenarios exclusive to therapy including aetiology, harm, diagnosis and prognosis", and that adding such scenarios "would provide a complete suite of instruments to assess EBM competency, regardless of patient scenario". Future versions also require alternate scenarios to mitigate recall bias on repeated use with the same cohort.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alternate scenarios are needed to permit repeated use and reduce recall bias during testing
#### Constraints
- Current version validated only for 'therapy' questions and only in medical trainees

### Target Learners
- medical trainees; potentially users in other health disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- EBM competency assessment across varied clinical question types
