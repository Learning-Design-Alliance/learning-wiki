# Introduce just-in-time simulation training immediately prior to clinical procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "the effect of the training intervention could be maximised in future studies by introducing just-in-time simulation training where participants perform a perfect simulated bronchoscopy immediately prior to each clinical procedure." They expect simulation-based performance targets would then transfer better to the clinical setting than in the current study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a simulator adjacent in time to each clinical procedure
#### Constraints
- Recommended for future studies; the current between-procedures intervention showed no significant transfer

### Target Learners
- bronchoscopists in training and practice

### Target Learning Goals
- transfer of simulation skills to clinical bronchoscopy performance
