# Blueprint recommendations for institutions implementing centralized, data-driven holistic review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose recommendations for a centralized holistic review process: institutions should "leverage the institutional mission, values, and goals to clearly outline the process of holistic review across departments, programs, and disciplines," involve key stakeholders (institutional leaders, program directors, admissions committee members) for transparency and buy-in, use a multitiered internal review system to reduce reviewer workload while reviewing all applicants, and staff the internal committee with doctoral-degree-holding program/institutional leaders serving staggered, multi-term appointments to preserve practices and prevent any member from dominating discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Key stakeholder involvement (institutional leaders, program directors, committee members) to ensure transparency, consensus, and buy-in
- Internal committee members holding doctoral degrees in a STEM discipline and serving staggered terms of at least two terms
#### Constraints
- The authors caution the recommendations should be applied with care to larger graduate programs because their sample is small relative to the national doctoral population

### Target Learners
- Doctoral program applicants in STEM fields

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable, workload-feasible review of all applicants in graduate admissions
