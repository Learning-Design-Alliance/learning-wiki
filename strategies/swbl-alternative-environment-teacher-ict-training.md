# Use SWBL as an alternative learning environment to equip pre-service teachers with technology-application knowledge and skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends SWBL as an alternative to traditional teaching for pre-service teachers' ICT preparation, arguing that teachers need knowledge and skills before applying technology in classrooms. It states SWBL "can be used as an alternative learning environment to TTs for pre-service teachers to be equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills to apply technology in their classrooms effectively." The recommendation rests on the achievement and satisfaction findings of this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
