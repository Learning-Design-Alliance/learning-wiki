# Use the K technique as a basis for building VR training models at scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that the K technique "can serve as a basis for building VR training models that explore its concepts at an even more affordable cost for a large scale of training". This extends the mnemonic into virtual reality environments where dry-lab platforms are unavailable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The K technique must first be established as a reproducible mnemonic method
#### Constraints
- VR training lacks consensus on which tasks and metrics are most effective for the da Vinci Skills Simulators

### Target Learners
- surgeons transitioning to robotic technique

### Target Learning Goals
- depth perception
- bimanual dexterity
- robotic control
