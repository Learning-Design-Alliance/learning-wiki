# Incorporate management, leadership opportunities, and career mentoring into training programmes so consultant skills are learnt during training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Themes from trainees' free-text responses indicated that management/leadership opportunities and more career mentoring/clinical supervision were missing from current training programmes. The article states these "should be considered when designing future training programmes so that “consultant skills” are learnt during training, after clinical manual skills have been acquired, rather than after training."
