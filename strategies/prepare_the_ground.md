# Prepare the Ground

## Description
Building a trusting community is crucial for honest talks about race. Giving students time to reflect and share about their lives creates connections that lay the foundation for fruitful discussions. Highlighting student voices and bringing in diverse experts helps create a safe and inclusive environment. Additionally, recognizing and addressing inequities within the school environment, such as disparities in class placement or disciplinary actions, helps to foster a sense of justice and trust.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection and sharing. Access to diverse experts. A willingness to address inequities within the school environment.
#### Constraints
- Some students may be hesitant to share their stories. Requires ongoing effort and commitment. May require additional support to address systemic inequities.
