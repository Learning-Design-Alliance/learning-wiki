# Form a scientist-teacher-educator-industry 'flight team' partnership to enhance an elementary science unit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A synergistic working relationship in which a physicist and a science teacher educator joined 'two elementary teachers, a curriculum support teacher, a volunteer pilot from US Airways' as the flight team, extending an ongoing Penn State and State College Area School District partnership. The team met regularly during spring 2001 and engaged in extensive discussions aimed at clarifying flight concepts and crafting opportunities for children to participate in elements of scientific inquiry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular meetings and extensive discussions aimed at clarifying concepts and crafting inquiry opportunities for children.
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as an example of what is possible when synergistic working relationships are established, within an existing university-school district partnership.

### Target Learners
- Elementary teachers in grades 3 and 4
- Elementary students in the Air and Aviation unit

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing the Air and Aviation unit
- Opportunities for children to participate in scientific inquiry
