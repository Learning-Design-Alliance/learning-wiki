# Form random and new groups for each activity so everyone works with everyone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To build a sense of group belonging that reinforces intrinsic motivation and participation, the course forms randomly composed, changing groups for every activity. The article states: 'An important strategy to achieve this is to form random and new groups for each activity in such a way that everyone has worked with everyone else at least a few times'. Random grouping also lets students get to know each other without biases, supporting the Equity and Inclusion theme, and facilitation plus the plenary sharing phase help every member feel integrated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitation and a structured sharing phase to stimulate participation of every group member
#### Constraints
- In classrooms with very different backgrounds, close attention must be paid to group dynamics and plenary sharing

### Target Learners
- University MSc students with varied national and disciplinary backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- Collaboration and inclusion
- intrinsic motivation to participate
