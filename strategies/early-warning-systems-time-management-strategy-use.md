# Use findings on strategy–time-management links to develop early warning systems and interventions promoting effective time management and strategy use in flipped classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that their findings on how learning strategies and timing of enactment jointly relate to exam performance can inform the design of learning analytics supports for flipped classrooms. As they state, "These findings may inform future development of early warning systems and instructional interventions to promote effective time management and strategy use, toward more productive SRL and improved course performance in flipped classrooms." The strategy targets students' preparatory study behaviour, which the article identifies as critical for success in flipped courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trace data capture of students' online preparatory activity to detect strategy and time management patterns
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a future direction informed by the findings, not as an intervention it tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in flipped classroom courses, particularly those with limited SRL skills

### Target Learning Goals
- effective time management and learning strategy use during online preparatory activities
