# Train-the-trainer workshops to disseminate the program to residency programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To support adoption in medical physics residency programs, the authors "propose to offer a series of 'train‐the‐trainer' workshops where residency directors would acquire the experience and materials required to implement a similar training program in their own residency programs." They suggest this work could ultimately be taken over by relevant committees within the AAPM.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- residency directors willing to attend workshops and implement the program
#### Constraints
- the authors note open questions about educator preparation, how much clinical experience residents need beforehand, and whether a 24-month residency has sufficient time

### Target Learners
- medical physics residency directors
- medical physics residents

### Target Learning Goals
- patient communication training program implementation
