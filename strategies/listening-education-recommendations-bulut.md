# Recommendations: involve parents in listening education, balance all language skills, and add listening training courses to teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Suggestions section the authors recommend, first, that "educational work on the importance and development of this skill should be given to parents" since listening training starts in the family; second, that teachers give equal importance to all Turkish language learning areas because development in one skill may affect others; and further, research on students' listening obstacles and elective listening-training courses in classroom teaching departments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher awareness that language skills interact; teacher education programs able to add elective courses
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are offered in light of a single semi-experimental study with fourth-grade students

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade primary school students
- pre-service classroom teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- listening skill development and vocabulary growth across language skills
