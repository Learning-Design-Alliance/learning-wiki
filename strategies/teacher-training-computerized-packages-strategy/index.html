Train teachers to prepare and use computerized educational packages and establish supervised Arabic educational websites¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article's recommendations call on teachers to use computerized educational packages in teaching and to hold "training session for teachers to be trained on preparing the computerized educational packages". It further recommends training circles for math teachers, ministry-supervised internet educational sites for different materials, continued development of Arabic educational sites, and published guides to the main Arabic education sites.
