# Design pay-for-performance bonuses that are substantial, differentiated, and challenging per TIF guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's findings imply that compensation reform should set award criteria consistent with guidance for challenging pay-for-performance bonuses. Because "districts expected most educators would receive a bonus, suggesting that the award criteria were not consistent with TIF guidance for challenging pay-for-performance bonuses," districts designing performance pay should ensure bonuses are substantial, differentiated, and not so widely awarded that they lose incentive value.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Award criteria must align with TIF guidance for challenging pay-for-performance bonuses
#### Constraints
- The finding reflects district expectations in the first year, before the first pay-for-performance payouts

### Target Learners
- Teachers and principals in high-need schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Reform compensation systems to reward educators for improving students' achievement
