# Shift physics culture to value technical and software contributions, via cross-department courses, rigorous computing training, and reevaluated thesis requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes "a cultural shift around the perception of what it means to ‘be a physicist’" so that technical and software contributions are valued "in the same manner we value analysis work". Concretely it calls for cross-department courses, workshops and research collaborations, graduate programs approaching technical and computing skills with the same rigor as traditional physics courses, allowing students and post-docs to work across disciplines and pursue industrial partnerships or internships, and a reevaluation of requirements for physics graduate theses.
