# Implement simulation-based learning and AR/VR in LMIC postgraduate dental programs through stakeholder investment, equitable access, and comprehensive training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the survey findings, "the authors propose steps (Fig. 3) to implement these resources in LMICs, with an emphasis on integrating modern educational tools to enhance dental training programs in Pakistan". The conclusion urges that "policymakers, educators, and institutions" prioritize investments in these tools, "ensuring equitable access and comprehensive training that aligns with global educational standards". The strategy targets contexts where SBL and AR/VR availability is limited.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prioritized investment by policymakers, educators, and institutions
- Equitable access and comprehensive training aligned with global educational standards
#### Constraints
- High costs associated with procurement, maintenance, and resources required for training faculty
- Shortage of trained facilitators reflecting a deficiency in the dental workforce
- Infrastructural inadequacies, such as frequent power outages, hinder consistent implementation and utilization of SBL platforms
- The study did not explore specific barriers institutions face, such as detailed cost analysis, infrastructural requirements, or faculty training needs

### Target Learners
- postgraduate prosthodontics trainees in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical and academic training quality in prosthodontics
- integration of modern educational technologies into postgraduate curricula
