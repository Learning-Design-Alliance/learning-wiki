# Organise refresher courses or simulation team training with a focus on CRM skills after an initial ABCDE simulation course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend follow-up training that specifically targets Crew Resources Management skills, because those are the skills that decayed most in the 3–4 months after the initial course. As they conclude: "CRM skills tend to decrease over time, so we recommend organising refresher courses, simulation team training or another kind of simulation training with a focus on CRM-skills." The recommendation rests on their own finding that most declining skills were CRM skills while technical resuscitation skills were maintained.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial simulation-based ABCDE course must have been completed, after which CRM-skill maintenance is the identified gap
#### Constraints
- Based on a single observational study with 30 voluntary participants and a 3–4 month follow-up window that varied by one month

### Target Learners
- Non-residents and first-year residents working in the emergency department

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining CRM (non-technical) skills such as communication, confidence and leadership in primary assessment of acutely ill patients
