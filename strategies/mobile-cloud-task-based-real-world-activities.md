# Use mobile devices and cloud-based technologies to support extensive task-based activities in real-world contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that CALL implementations become more innovative in their exploitation of instructional technology. They suggest that "mobile devices and cloud-based technologies provide ubiquitous access that allow learning to take place in real-world or life-like contexts conducive of advanced-level language performance." These resources should support extensive task-based activities such as role-playing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementations must support extensive task-based activities that require students to actualize the full range of can-do linguistic performance.
