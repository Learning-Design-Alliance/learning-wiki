Use the Ten Times Two routine to build observational vocabulary before data analysis¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Ten Times Two is a quiet observation routine: students look at an image for at least 30 seconds, list ten words or phrases about any aspect of the picture, then repeat the look-and-list cycle to add ten more. The session uses it as a five-minute vocabulary warm-up for the Data & Analysis topic, asking students to "List 10 words or phrases about any aspect of the picture."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A blank piece of paper per student and a displayed image
  • At least 30 seconds of quiet looking per round

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Data and analysis vocabulary development
  • Careful observation of visual data
