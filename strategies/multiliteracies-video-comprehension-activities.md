# Teach L2 comprehension through a multiliteracies curriculum with video-based visualizing and verbalizing activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that comprehension be taught through a curriculum of multiliteracies in which learners interpret text meanings through different modalities, such as visualizing and verbalizing. It proposes concrete activities: learners connect text segments with images by drawing a picture or describing their images, view video clips and recall content from a stopped scene, compare recall with the original script, and describe and illustrate clip content with chosen scenes. The recommendation rests on the study's significant VNT results and the multiliteracies pedagogy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video-based materials integrated with on-screen text and narrations, as in the VNT presentation condition.
#### Constraints
- The article states results reflect group tendencies and do not apply with equal consistency to every participant.

### Target Learners
- Chinese-speaking EFL university students

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 text comprehension at macrostructure and microstructure levels
