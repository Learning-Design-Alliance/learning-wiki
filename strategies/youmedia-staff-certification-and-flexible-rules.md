# Staff certification processes and flexible rule reevaluation to protect equipment and maintain a safe, productive youth-driven space

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In a youth-driven voluntary space, staff established and enforced rules while remaining flexible, reevaluating them regularly in response to how teens used the space. When novice users repeatedly damaged audio production equipment such as turntable needles, staff developed a certification process for using the equipment to reduce damages. Rule enforcement also served as a filtering process determining which teens remained in the space over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff must attend to teen interests while maintaining safe conditions; rules require regular reevaluation as use of the space evolves.
#### Constraints
- The report notes enforcement of rules filtered which teens remained, and staff had to balance a youth-driven approach with an adult agenda for learning.

### Target Learners
- High school-aged teens in voluntary out-of-school settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe and productive learning environment
- Sustained access to production equipment
