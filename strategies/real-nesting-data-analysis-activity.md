# Analyzing real park nesting data to teach resource management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A post-visit activity in which "Students will analyze data from the Hammocks Beach 1992 sea turtle nesting season. They will calculate averages and percentages to experience how data is compiled for a resource management project." Students calculate incubation periods and hatching success for individual nests, compute class averages, identify factors affecting hatching success, and recommend management strategies for North Carolina nesting beaches.
