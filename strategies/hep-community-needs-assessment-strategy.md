# Conduct a community needs assessment projecting person-power and educational needs and targeting low transition points in the education pipeline

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a Snowmass2021-linked study with three facets: a needs assessment of the community projecting person-power and educational needs annually over two decades; a needs assessment of the education process reviewing teaching at all levels, "particularly transition points between education levels – where ‘transmission coeﬃcients’ of students are low"; and model development with AIP and APS building statistical models of the current education situation and how it might be improved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Work with the various research and technical frontiers to develop projections of their person-power and educational needs on an annual basis over the next two decades
- Engage with AIP and APS who follow the education process of younger students upward through schools to develop statistical models
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a proposed study among the facets of the Snowmass2021 Community Self-study, not as a completed assessment

### Target Learners
- students at all education levels
- underrepresented groups in STEM

### Target Learning Goals
- improving student transition through education levels
- projecting the field's workforce and educational needs
