# Assess nontechnical skills in emergency simulations using the NOTSS scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nontechnical skills objectives, "including communication, collaboration, and professionalism, be assessed using the Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons (NOTSS) scale." In this boot camp, raters watched video recordings of simulations (2012–2014) and later faculty rated residents onsite supplemented by video raters (2016–2017), yielding cohort-level measures that informed scenario revision. An example checklist is provided in Appendix C for formative use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Raters trained in the NOTSS behavior rating system; video recording of simulations or onsite rating capacity
#### Constraints
- The simulation itself was formative, with no formal assessment of participants' medical judgments or technical performance

### Target Learners
- Junior residents in otolaryngology–head and neck surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- Nontechnical skills: communication, collaboration, professionalism, situational awareness
