# Use expert-led Tours and student Tour creation with facilitation as a strategy for astronomy teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a teaching strategy in which trained Ambassadors facilitate students' use of WWT and the creation of their own Tours. In the Clarke Middle School pilot, "Treatment students all created their own Tours, in groups of three or four", with Ambassadors facilitating "mostly by pointing students to additional online astrophysical resources". This strategy is recommended for expansion across ages from pre-K to retirees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to WWT software and expert-created or vetted Tours
- Trained facilitators (Ambassadors) in the classroom
#### Constraints
- 11-year-olds typically did not need much help learning to use the program, so facilitation focused on pointing to additional resources

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade students
- learners of all ages from pre-K to retirees

### Target Learning Goals
- astronomy knowledge, understanding, and interest in science
