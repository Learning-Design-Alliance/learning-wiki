# Adopt culture-based RME and strengthen prerequisite mastery to improve critical thinking and character

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that realistic mathematics education culture-based "become a learning alternative to use in schools by teachers, especially at the medium school level and low levels" to improve critical mathematical thinking skills and character. It further recommends balancing attention to character and the affective domain with cognitive outcomes, and that teachers "strengthen students' mastery of the material prerequisite math and choose the relevant cultural context for the material that will be taught."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select cultural contexts relevant to the material to be taught
- Students master prerequisite mathematical material
#### Constraints
- Recommended especially for medium and low school levels

### Target Learners
- junior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical mathematical thinking skills
- character and affective development
