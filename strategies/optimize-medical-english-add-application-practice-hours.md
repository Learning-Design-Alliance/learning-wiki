# Optimize the medical English course by adding application class hours and rehabilitation-specific materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on open-ended student feedback, the authors recommend course reforms: students reported the course content was difficult, the start of study was too early (first year), and total class hours were short (18 class hours). The majority of students in both modules thought it was necessary to increase practice of medical English, and students suggested "adding an application class ... to improve their comprehensive listening, speaking, reading, and writing abilities" and more rehabilitation-specific learning materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum time and materials: additional practice/application class hours beyond the current 18 class hours and learning materials specific to rehabilitation medicine.
#### Constraints
- The suggestions come from voluntary open-ended responses by only 24 Smart Class and 13 Traditional Class students answering Question 22, so they represent a small self-selected subset.

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate rehabilitation and physiotherapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehensive listening, speaking, reading, and writing abilities in medical English
