## Description
The curriculum held two telephone video conferences after 6 and 12 months with fellows and tutors, moderated by the chairmen of SERGS's Educational Committee, to take stock of progress and discuss feasibility. The article reports that "Telephone video-conferencing proved useful for interim analysis of the curriculum, especially since structured training needs monitoring." The conferences addressed supervision quality and drew conclusions on the feasibility of the pilot educational program.
