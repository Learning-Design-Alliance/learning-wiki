CSCL environments should support learners' engagement with multiple forms of representations and help them meaningfully connect them in ways that foster knowledge construction.¶

Description¶

The article recommends that CSCL environments should support learners' engagement with multiple forms of representations and help them meaningfully connect them in ways that foster knowledge construction. For example, the astronomy simulation provides information boxes alongside each annotated constellation that help students verbalize their observations and connect digital representation to conceptual understanding. The article further suggests that aligning gestures with virtual representations could strengthen the connection between learners' embodied actions and the digital objects they are manipulating.
