# Use an infographic of key decisions to guide states considering teacher apprenticeship programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The publication offers an infographic laying out decisions that its Wyoming and South Dakota partners have made and that states interested in implementing teacher apprenticeship programs might consider. It directs readers to "Read the full infographic to learn about the decisions that our partners have made," positioning documented partner decisions as guidance for other states adopting this new approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A state or partnership actively considering implementation of a teacher apprenticeship program to mitigate teacher shortages.
#### Constraints
- The guidance reflects decisions made by REL Central's two partnerships; the publication notes minimal rigorous research exists on these programs' development and implementation.

### Target Learners
- State education systems and policymakers designing teacher apprenticeship programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Making informed implementation decisions about teacher apprenticeship programs to mitigate teacher shortages
