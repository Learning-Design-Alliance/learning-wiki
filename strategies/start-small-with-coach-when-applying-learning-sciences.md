# Work with a coach or peer group, start with one insight, and gather data when applying learning sciences research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The resource recommends a staged approach to applying learning sciences research in classrooms, warning that "applying learning sciences research in practice can involve trial and error". It advises educators to work with an instructional coach or peer group to create a plan, focus on one insight at a time, gather data along the way, and involve students in the process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Work with an instructional coach or a group of peers to create a plan before experimenting.
#### Constraints
- The cards are an introduction to the learning sciences, not a comprehensive review of the field.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Translating learning sciences research into everyday classroom practice
