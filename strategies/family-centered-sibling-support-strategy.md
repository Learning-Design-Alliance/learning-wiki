# Family-centered school support that includes healthy siblings of children in poor health

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that school support services, which primarily focus on children in poor health, adopt a family-centered approach inclusive of the healthy sibling. It states that "school psychologists, counselors and teachers should also consider the needs of healthy siblings during their work with children in poor health, because they too can be disadvantaged." Concrete actions listed include psychoeducational support to the sibling and whole school (individual counseling or theme days on health and well-being with parents involved), increased teacher involvement to identify signs of maladaptive coping, and flexible assessment deadlines or extra orientation days when the ill child's disease intensifies.

## Design Implications

### Context
