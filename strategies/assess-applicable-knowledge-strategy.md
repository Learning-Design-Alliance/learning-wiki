# Target the assessment of applicable knowledge and large-scale implementation as priority research and design moves

## Description
In its outlook the article recommends attacking two open problems. Traditional tests "do not appraise the applicability of knowledge for solving complex problems of out-of-school relevance", so tried and tested procedures for reliably and validly assessing applicable knowledge must be developed. Second, remedial instructional models have so far been implemented only as prototypes, and scaling up requires resolving organizational changes, sequencing of problem-oriented units with teacher presentations, and teacher training needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- New assessment procedures valid for applicable knowledge; decisions on organizational changes, sequencing, and teacher training before scaling problem-oriented learning
#### Constraints
- Existing instructional models are only prototypes with some promising results; large-scale implementation remains open

### Target Learners
- Students in schools, universities, and vocational education

### Target Learning Goals
- Reliable and valid assessment of knowledge applicable to out-of-school problems
