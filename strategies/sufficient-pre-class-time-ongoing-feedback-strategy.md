# Provide sufficient pre-class preparation time and adjust teaching based on ongoing trainee feedback

## Description
The article recommends that 'To optimize teaching outcomes, the study recommends providing trainees with sufficient preparation time before class to conduct case analyses and literature review in a relaxed manner, and promptly adjusting teaching methods and content based on ongoing trainee feedback.' This addresses the pre-class time burden and insufficient pre-class preparation challenges identified in the feedback data.
