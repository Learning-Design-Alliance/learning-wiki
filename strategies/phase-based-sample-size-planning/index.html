Plan sample sizes phase by phase across the four evidence-building phases¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy organizes sample size planning around the four evidence-building phases the guide names: "Design the Solution (Phase 1), Refine the Solution (Phase 2), Assess for Early Evidence of Success (Phase 3), and Validate Effectiveness (Phase 4)". Organizations consult the guide's phase-specific considerations as they begin recruitment and plan measurement and evaluation activities, so that sample size decisions match the evidentiary purpose of each phase.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Organizations must begin sample size planning during recruitment and before measurement and evaluation activities.

Constraints¶

  • In Phase 3 and Phase 4, the guidance serves only as a starting point; a research partner should determine the specific sample size needed.

Target Learners¶

  • students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

Target Learning Goals¶

  • building evidence of effectiveness for educational solutions

Related Strategies¶

  • Use a suite of measurement and evaluation tools to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based educational solutions
  • Refine a solution based on lessons learned during implementation
  • Conduct a small pilot study to assess early evidence of success before full validation
  • Measure usability, usefulness, and utilization during development and early implementation
