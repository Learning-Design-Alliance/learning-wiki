Design AI-integration activities that set explicit expectations for AI-assisted work, integrate reflective assessments of AI outputs, and build tasks strengthening critical thinking and academic integrity¶

Description¶

This strategy packages the article's three named activity types for integrating generative AI into an academic writing course. The article states: "These activities include setting explicit expectations for AI-assisted work, integrating reflective assessments to evaluate AI outputs, and designing tasks that strengthen students' critical thinking and academic integrity." The activities were designed to encourage reflective practices, promote critical analysis of AI-generated content, and establish clear guidelines for responsible use.
