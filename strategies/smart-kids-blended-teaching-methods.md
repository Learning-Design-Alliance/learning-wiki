# Two blended-teaching methods combining computer-based and classroom work within Smart Kids technology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines two methods of blended teaching with E-textbooks/EEGR: in class, one part of a lesson is independent work with computers or an e-textbook while the other is frontal or group work in copybooks; at home, pupils watch video fragments and perform independent computer tasks, while in the classroom the class summarizes, masters skills and acquires competences. Specific task-organization characteristics include computer tasks under teacher supervision "complying with health standards (up to 12 minutes)" and video-based tasks such as describing an event or writing a continuation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Independent computer tasks must correspond to the topic of a lesson and comply with health standards; home computer work is under parents' supervision
#### Constraints
- Use of E-textbooks/EEGR at a lesson should not exceed 10-12 minutes

### Target Learners
- elementary school pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge retention
- skill mastery in game form
- development of imaginative thinking
