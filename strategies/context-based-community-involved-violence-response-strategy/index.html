Integrate multiple strategies in a context-based and community-involved approach to reverse violence and promote peace¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that policymakers and practitioners integrate multiple strategies in a context-based and community-involved approach. The paper states that by presenting a coherent overview of current educational responses it "assists policymakers and practitioners in integrating multiple strategies in a context-based and community-involved approach to reverse violence and promote peace." The strategy operationalizes the proposed reorientation: responses should combine approaches across levels and involve the community rather than relying on a single individual- or group-focused intervention.
