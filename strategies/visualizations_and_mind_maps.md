# Visualizations and Mind Maps

## Description
Use visuals such as infographics and mind maps to help transform abstract concepts into clear and engaging formats. By visually breaking down complex ideas, these tools make it easier for learners to grasp how different elements of a concept connect and interact within a broader business context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools for creating visuals (e.g., software, whiteboards). A clear understanding of the concept to be visualized.
#### Constraints
- Visuals can be overwhelming if not designed effectively. Some concepts may be difficult to represent visually.
