# Peer and Self-Assessment

## Description
Peer and self-assessment give students the opportunity to review their work against a set of criteria. Students can engage in discussion about mastery of skills. These assessments are tools for student self-advocacy because they aren’t reliant on the instructor and allow students to evaluate themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubric, checklist, or success criteria; time for assessment and discussion.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for some students to provide constructive feedback. Requires clear criteria and guidelines.
