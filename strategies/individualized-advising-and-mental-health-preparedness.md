# Provide individualized academic advising and train faculty to accommodate students' emotional and mental health needs

## Description
The article frames advising as central to program success: 'Advising is an art' in which students' individual needs are addressed and appropriate courses chosen. It further recommends that 'Faculty needs to be educated about growing mental health problems and trained to accommodate their emotional needs without compromising on the standard of education', responding to pandemic-era stress documented elsewhere in the article.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advising that addresses individual students' needs and course choices, plus faculty education about growing mental health problems.
#### Constraints
- Faculty must accommodate emotional needs 'without compromising on the standard of education'.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students
- students facing pandemic-related stress

### Target Learning Goals
- effective course selection through advising and supported student mental health
