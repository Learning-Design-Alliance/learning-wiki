# Embed autonomy-supportive, mastery-oriented teaching, developmental advising, and problem-centred pedagogy to strengthen motivation, satisfaction, and successful intelligence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's policy recommendations call on higher education institutions to embed "autonomy-supportive, mastery-oriented teaching approaches that heighten intrinsic motivation and strengthen person–major fit". It further states that "Project-based learning, problem-centred pedagogy, and real-world simulations can concurrently enhance motivation, satisfaction, and successful intelligence." Academic advising should adopt a developmental model cultivating self-belief, resilience, and adaptive competencies, with satisfaction monitoring tools to detect misalignments early.
