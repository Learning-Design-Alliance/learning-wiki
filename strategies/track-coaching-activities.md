# Track coaching activities to support districtwide coaching implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that districts implementing instructional coaching track coaching activities as part of a system of supports. It presents this as one of three insights drawn from the New Teacher Center's experience, alongside providing clear guidance to coaches and engaging district leaders, to help ensure coaches devote time to the full range of coaching activities.
