# Use simulation debriefs to determine whether more extensive needs assessment data are required

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy positions simulation debriefs as an initial, efficient screening step in needs assessment. The article states that "Depending on the purpose and scope of a given NA, simulation debriefs may stand alone or may be used to make decisions surrounding whether more extensive data are required". Because debriefs capture most themes at lower cost, they can triage which needs warrant deeper investigation through interviews or focus groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predeveloped simulation scenarios relevant to the assessment context
- A skilled facilitator able to balance education and data collection
#### Constraints
- Not all themes emerge from debriefs; scenario selection determines what needs can be surfaced
- Depth of data is lower than traditional methods

### Target Learners
- Healthcare teams and educators planning needs assessments in clinical settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Prioritising needs for curriculum or system-level change
