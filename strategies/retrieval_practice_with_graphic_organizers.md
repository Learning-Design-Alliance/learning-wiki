# Retrieval Practice with Graphic Organizers

## Description
After students do a Brain Dump, where they try to recall as much as they can about a given topic, without the aid of any supporting texts, they sort their dumped information into some kind of graphic organizer, and possibly working in pairs to do so.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizers.
#### Constraints
- Students may need to check their texts to confirm, correct, or add to the information they retrieved.
