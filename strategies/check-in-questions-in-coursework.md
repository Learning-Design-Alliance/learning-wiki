# Incorporate check-in questions within coursework (homework, quizzes, discussions) to lower students' initial hesitancy to reach out

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding open check-in prompts into regular coursework as a mechanism for creating space for student communication. As one participant described, a semi-regular question on homework or quizzes asks "How are things going? Is there anything you'd like to tell me about?" — an open invitation that sits between faculty noticing a change and leaving communication entirely to the student. The authors suggest this could lower the initial hesitancy of students reaching out, thereby giving faculty access to the contextual information needed to begin building empathy.
