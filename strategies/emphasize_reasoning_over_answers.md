# Emphasize Reasoning over Answers

## Description
De-emphasizing correct answers and spending more time on reasoning involves shifting the focus from finding the right solution to understanding the problem-solving process. This can be achieved by presenting worked examples with wrong answers and having students identify the errors. Teachers can also change how they facilitate class participation by asking students to share their initial thoughts and approaches before revealing the answer, encouraging diverse problem-solving strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need to shift their focus from assessing answers to assessing reasoning. They should be prepared to facilitate discussions, provide feedback on students’ thinking processes, and create a classroom culture where reasoning is valued over correct answers.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in classrooms where traditional assessment methods are heavily emphasized. Requires a significant shift in teacher mindset and instructional practices.

### Affordances
- Supports the development of critical thinking skills and problem-solving abilities. By focusing on reasoning, students are encouraged to explore different approaches, understand the underlying concepts, and learn from their mistakes. It can also make math class more inclusive and less intimidating for students who struggle with finding the right answer quickly.

### Personalization
- Can be tailored to different grade levels by adjusting the complexity of the problems and the level of support provided. Teachers can also differentiate instruction by providing students with different types of worked examples and problem-solving tasks.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess students’ reasoning skills through written explanations, verbal discussions, and problem-solving tasks. Look for evidence of critical thinking, logical reasoning, and the ability to explain and justify their approaches. Provide feedback that focuses on the quality of their reasoning, not just the correctness of their answers.

## Impact
- Research shows that emphasizing reasoning over answers can lead to deeper understanding, improved problem-solving skills, and increased student engagement. Students are more likely to retain information and apply it to new situations when they understand the underlying concepts and reasoning behind the procedures.
