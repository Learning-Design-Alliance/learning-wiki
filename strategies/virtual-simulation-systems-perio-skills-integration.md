## Description
In the discussion, the authors propose that "the acquisition of technical skills on simulated teeth in Perio may benefit from the introduction of virtual simulation systems, which could assist students in integrating theoretical and practical skills". They suggest this could enable a smoother transition from pre‐clinical training to clinical practice and potentially enhance clinical performance, offered as an interpretation of the null simulation–clinic correlation found in Periodontics.
