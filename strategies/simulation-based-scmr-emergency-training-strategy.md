# Incorporate standardized, simulation-based emergency response training and annual competency assessments for CT radiographers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that institutions implement mandatory simulation-based workshops and hands-on emergency drills for managing severe contrast media reactions, supported by annual competency audits and a national competency framework. They note that "simulations, which offer greater interactivity compared to traditional lectures, have demonstrated superior efficacy in enhancing both knowledge and confidence in managing IVCM reactions". The strategy targets radiography curricula, departmental protocols, and accreditation benchmarks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured SCMR management courses integrated into radiography curricula
- Departmental protocols standardizing radiographers' emergency roles
- Competency benchmarks set by healthcare accreditation bodies
#### Constraints
- Feasibility of radiographers administering emergency medications depends on the local legal and regulatory framework governing drug dispensation by non-physician workers

### Target Learners
- Accredited CT radiographers
- Radiography students

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognition and management of severe contrast media reactions
- Emergency medication administration under supervision
