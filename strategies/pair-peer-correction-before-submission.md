# Have students correct each other's compositions in pairs before submission

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A recommended classroom procedure in which students work in pairs to correct one another's compositions before handing them in. The article states this "allows students to work in a non-threatening educational setting that helps build their self-confidence and enhances learning by discovering and sharing". It is one of nine recommendations drawn from the study's findings on constructive feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must instruct students to work in pairs and correct each other's compositions before submitting them.
#### Constraints
- The article reports that students initially avoided group and pair checking because they felt humiliated under teachers' traditional, nonconstructive attitudes.

### Target Learners
- secondary-level EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- written English accuracy and self-correction skills
