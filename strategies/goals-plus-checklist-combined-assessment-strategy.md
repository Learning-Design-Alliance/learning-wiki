# Combine a validated global rating scale (GOALS) with a specific suture checklist for scored video assessment and individualized feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Performances at each test were filmed and anonymously viewed by a surgical education expert, then scored by summing GOALS and a Specific Checklist for Suture to assess learning and allow individualized feedback. The authors note checklists reduce evaluator subjectivity but can lose information compared with global scales, so "the joint use of these two assessment tools contributed to results interpretation and verification". Each participant then received written and oral individual feedback from a single specialist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos filmed at standardized test points and rated anonymously by an expert assessor
- Both tools validated in the literature, per the authors
#### Constraints
- Checklists alone may result in information loss compared to global scales, per the article

### Target Learners
- surgical residents in simulation-based skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- laparoscopic suturing skill assessment and feedback
