Train teachers to develop computer-based science software with tools such as Macromedia Flash and Adobe Photoshop¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In the suggestions section the author recommends that, given the effectiveness of the teaching package, "teachers can be asked to take part in-service training and can be taught how to use the programs such as Macromedia Flash, Macromedia Authorware, Adobe Photoshop" and "can be encouraged to develop the computer-based science and technology software". This turns the study's software-development experience into a professional-development recommendation for scaling CBI.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • In-service training opportunities and access to the named authoring tools (Macromedia Flash, Macromedia Authorware, Adobe Photoshop).

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation derives from a three-week study limited to the Earth, Sun and Moon unit in one primary school.

Target Learners¶

  • in-service primary and secondary science teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • developing computer-based science and technology software

Claims¶

  • Computer-based science and technology instruction produces significantly higher corrected post-test achievement than traditional teaching in the Earth, Sun and Moon unit [+W]
  • Computer-based science and technology instruction produces significantly higher corrected post-test problem solving skill scores than traditional teaching [+W]
