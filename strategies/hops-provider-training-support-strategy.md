# Low-dose provider training with website resources and on-demand consultation to support school-based HOPS implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The protocol specifies a training and technical-support model adapted from prior HOPS studies and from actual practice: providers receive the HOPS manual and brief training covering the conceptual model, behavior-change principles, session procedures, parent/teacher consultation, and reinforcer selection, delivered virtually by the program developer or a trainee. The article states "School and research providers will receive a copy of the HOPS manual [9] and 1.5–2 hours of training before providing HOPS." Ongoing support includes a website with training videos, slides, and FAQs, plus on-demand consultation by phone, virtual communication, or email, with each consultation logged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the HOPS manual, the training website (hopsintervention.com), and a training team available for logged consultation calls
#### Constraints
- Training is delivered virtually by the program developer or a trainee working under his direction; the developer provides training but no evaluation activities

### Target Learners
- School providers (teachers, school counselors, school psychologists) and research-team providers implementing HOPS

### Target Learning Goals
- Fidelity of HOPS implementation
- provider competence in organizational skills training
