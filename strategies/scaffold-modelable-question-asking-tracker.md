# Scaffold student question-asking by guiding questions toward model-able or complex phenomena suited to Tracker

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because getting Grade 9 students to ask and research their own physics questions is difficult, the article recommends a scaffold of guiding students to ask questions that are model-able using Tracker's dynamics or kinematics models, or complex questions such as moving reference frames. The article states "a possible scaffold could be guiding them to ask questions that are model-able" and gives the example of a video filmed on a moving boat used as a moving reference frame to determine the kinematics graph of another boat.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Tracker and its dynamics or kinematics models
- videos of phenomena that can be modeled in Tracker
#### Constraints
- The article notes this enactment of using existing EJS computer models for Practice 2 was not well enacted because of the difficulties of finding models that can be used

- Grade 9 physics students

- scientific practice 1: ask questions
