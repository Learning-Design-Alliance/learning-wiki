Mixed-methods systematic review combining random-effects meta-analysis of experimental and quasi-experimental studies with narrative synthesis of qualitative and non-intervention evidence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's core method: a mixed-methods systematic review that pools quantitative effectiveness evidence and synthesizes qualitative and non-intervention evidence separately. Quantitative studies must be experimental or quasi-experimental with baseline/endline or treatment-comparison data and selection-bias-correcting analysis; effect sizes are standardized mean differences combined in random-effects meta-analysis with meta-regression for heterogeneity. Qualitative studies are appraised with the 9-item CASP checklist and synthesized narratively.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Included quantitative studies must collect baseline and/or endline treatment-comparison data and use statistical matching, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, or other multivariate selection-bias-correcting analysis; interrupted time series need at least three data points before and after the intervention

Constraints¶

  • Meta-analysis restricted to randomized designs and quasi-experimental designs with nonrandom assignment; eligible comparison conditions are no intervention, pipeline, or business as usual

Target Learners¶

  • Children from birth through grade 3 in the LAC region, including children up to age 11 still in grade 3 due to late entry and retention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early grade reading outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Computational search methods plus grey literature retrieval to identify early grade reading evidence and minimize publication bias
  • Follow guidelines distinguishing joint display development for analysis (process) versus reporting (product) purposes
  • Use qualitative interview data from teachers and students to account for quantitative results and derive productive AR implementation strategies
