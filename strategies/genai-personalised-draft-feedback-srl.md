# Use generative AI for immediate, personalised feedback on drafts to support self-regulation through assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review briefly suggests how generative AI can support student-centred assessment despite its misconduct risks. It argues that 'GenAI can deliver highly individualised feedback tailored to each student's specific needs at the precise point it is needed in their learning journey', aligning students with an employability skill used across the bioscience sector. GenAI tools can assess performance instantaneously, offer guidance and corrections students apply immediately, provide feedback on drafts so students revise before final submission, and help students set realistic goals—supporting feedback for learning and, with proper guidance, self-evaluation approaches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- The article notes GenAI has potential to be problematic in student assessment processes through academic misconduct, and flags continuous evaluation of GenAI tools as essential for maintaining effectiveness and relevance

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
