# Ladder of Feedback

## Description
The Ladder of Feedback is a structured protocol to guide peer feedback that includes a sequential framework with specific questions, sentence stems, and success criteria. This process provides clear expectations for what elements to focus feedback on and scaffolds the process of giving positive but constructive feedback. The steps in the ladder typically include clarifying questions, valuing positives, addressing concerns, and making suggestions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clear rubric or set of success criteria, structured group time, and guided prompts for each step of the ladder.
#### Constraints
- May require initial training to ensure learners understand and follow the protocol. Some learners may find the structure restrictive.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- The specific questions, sentence stems, and success criteria can be tailored to the subject matter and the learners' needs. The ladder can be adapted to include additional steps or focus on different areas.
