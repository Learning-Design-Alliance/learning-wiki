Health managers and policymakers should choose online education strategies for medical students by examining studies in this area to enhance e-learning satisfaction¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's implication section recommends that, given the inevitable rise of online learning, health managers and policymakers select the best online education strategy for medical science students by examining the existing research base, and that they address the recognized flaws and restrictions of the e-learning process to increase student satisfaction. The article states it is recommended that policymakers "choose the best online education strategy for medical science students by examining various studies in this area to enhance their satisfaction with e-learning."

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educational managers and policymakers must examine various studies in this field before selecting an online education method

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation follows from a review of cross-sectional studies with methodological and instrumental variation that precluded meta-analysis

Target Learners¶

  • medical science students in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • satisfaction with e-learning and online education

Claims¶

  • Convenience showed a significant positive relationship with medical students' satisfaction with e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic [+W]
  • About half (51.79%) of medical science students were satisfied with e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic across 24 cross-sectional studies [+W]
  • Age, clinical year, and stress perception showed negative and significant relationships with medical students' e-learning satisfaction during the COVID-19 pandemic [+W]
  • Gender, prior e-learning experience, level of study, adaptation, course content, interactivity, understanding of content, instructor participation in discussion, multimedia use, and adequate time showed significant relationships with medical students' e-learning satisfaction [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Future research should evaluate the impact of different variables on e-learning satisfaction, compare online teaching approaches, and investigate e-learning limitations and obstacles
