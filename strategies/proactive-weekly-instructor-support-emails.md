# Provide proactive weekly instructor-initiated support emails to all online students without requiring them to ask for help

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Amplified assistance in U-Pace is "tailored assistance provided weekly by email to all students during the process of learning without them having to ask for help". The article motivates this with evidence that students most in need of help are unlikely to seek assistance, and that instructor support is important for motivation in online courses. Instructors base messages on engagement and performance data from the learning management system, combining instructional support, behavior-based praise, and belief-modifying elements.

## Design Implications
