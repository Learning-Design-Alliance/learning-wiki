# End each tutorial worksheet with an open invitation to challenge, add, rephrase, or clarify the agreement

## Description
A specific implementable move: end each tutorial worksheet by inviting small groups to reflect on the physics agreement — "Is there anything you want to challenge, add, rephrase, or clarify about the agreement so far?" The article reports students responded definitively with multiple modifications that felt important to them.
