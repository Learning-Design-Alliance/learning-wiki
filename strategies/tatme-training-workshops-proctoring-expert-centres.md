# Expert centres deliver TaTME training workshops and proctoring, with a consensus minimum of 14 procedures per year to assure quality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
As an implementable training arrangement, the review describes expert centres providing training workshops and facilitating proctoring of TaTME, anchored to the TaTME consensus group's minimum annual case volume. It states that "several TaTME expert centres across Europe and the USA provide training workshops and facilitate proctoring of the technique", and that the consensus group set a minimum of 14 procedures a year for optimal quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 14 procedures a year performed, per the TaTME consensus group, to assure optimal quality of the procedure
#### Constraints
- At this early stage of implementation the evidence base is mainly from expert centres and long-term outcomes are not yet available

### Target Learners
- surgeons in training for TaTME
- surgical teams at adopting centres

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent surgical quality in TaTME
- competence in single-incision laparoscopic surgery and two-team operating
