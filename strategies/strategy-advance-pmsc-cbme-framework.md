# Advance PMSC training through explicit competency definitions, structured workplace-based learning, competency-oriented assessment, national training modules, and faculty development.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends strengthening PMSC training by explicit competency frameworks, structured workplace-based learning, and aligned assessment strategies. Moving forward, it proposes developing national training modules aligned with standardized PMSC, embedding psychosomatic content into licensure and board certification assessments, and expanding case-based learning and simulated encounters. Faculty development, especially interdisciplinary teaching teams, is described as "equally essential".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit competency definitions and aligned assessment strategies
- Faculty development and institutional commitment
#### Constraints
- Proposed initiatives should be accompanied by rigorous evaluation and not replace core residency training requirements

### Target Learners
- General practice residents in standardized residency training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Psychosomatic medicine service competency including recognition of psychosocial contributors, psychoeducation, and referral
