# Use Kano Analysis surveys with the customer plus a broader audience survey to contextualize tailored solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the initial customer meeting, teams create a survey of five features using the Kano Method, which assesses how one feels if a feature is included, if it is not included, and how important it is. The article reports that "we asked the students to use the Kano Analysis method to assess the requirements of the customer" and additionally asks teams to distribute the same survey through the customer's network to assess generalizability. In the S-K-A-T-E scoreboard case, the broader survey (N=10) revealed attractive group features, but the team focused on the two-player version the customer wanted.
