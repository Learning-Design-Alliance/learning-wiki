Appoint a district-level CS/CT leadership position to center and prioritize computing as a curricular offering¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In Year 2, ICCSD appointed a district-wide CS Specialist to support teacher professional development and integration, which the district's reflection identifies as its second key decision: to center leadership and prioritize CS and CT as a curricula offering. The position supported implementation of PLTW at five schools during the 2020-21 academic year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District-level funding and recruitment capacity for a dedicated CS Specialist role

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers and students district-wide

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Computer science and computational thinking integration

Related Strategies¶

  • Leverage school-based librarians to create and deliver K-6 CS/CT lessons when classroom integration slows
  • Use NGSS-aligned K-8 science coursework as the starting point for district CT integration
  • Build teacher capacity through iterative, equity-centered professional development for pathway implementation
