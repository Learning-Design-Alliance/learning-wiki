# Synthesize cross-disciplinary evidence on education to guide policy-making beyond the 2030 education agenda

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ISEE Assessment translates synthesized knowledge into policy-relevant recommendations: it pools expertise from a broad range of knowledge holders and stakeholders, identifies knowledge gaps and future research agendas, and addresses overarching key questions through deliberative discussion. The article states: "By synthesizing the state of existing knowledge on education and learning across disciplines and regions of the world, the ISEE Assessment will generate the information and recommendations needed taking into account what works, what does not and where more research is needed to guide and support policy-making beyond the 2030 education agenda." This strategy targets policymakers who increasingly want education policy guided by science and evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A team of independent experts from a broad range of disciplines and world regions, interacting with key stakeholders in ongoing deliberative discussions
#### Constraints
- Distinct in nature from international large-scale student assessments such as PISA; such syntheses had never before been performed for education

### Target Learners
- learners of all backgrounds and stages of life affected by education policy

### Target Learning Goals
- reforming education systems to support human flourishing and the 2030 education agenda
