Substitute sound filmstrips for more expensive films or television, and use reading assignments in place of classroom lectures¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article concludes that "sound filmstrips might be substituted for more expensive films or television and that other methods such as reading assignments might be substituted for the classroom lecture." This recommendation follows directly from the finding of no significant difference in learning between the filmstrip and lecture conditions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to sound filmstrip equipment and produced filmstrip materials

Constraints¶

  • The result may apply only to graduate engineering students

Target Learners¶

  • graduate engineering students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • science content learning, specifically atomic bonding

Claims¶

  • Elaborate production methods, including color, are not necessarily needed for effective learning [+W]
  • Sound filmstrips and classroom lectures produced no significant difference in learning among graduate engineering students [+W]
  • Most graduate engineering students were willing to accept sound filmstrips for instruction, but many wanted opportunities to ask questions [~W]
