# Include more than one informant in future school climate study designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because most school climate measures target student respondents and self-report formats, the review recommends broadening informant pools. The article states that "future studies should consider including more than one informant in their design", arguing that triangulation increases validity and reliability, helps understand discrepancies, and minimizes both analytical and ethical risks, providing a more comprehensive and insightful view of school climate phenomena.
