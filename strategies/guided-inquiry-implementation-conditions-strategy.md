# Attend to student characteristics, resource availability, and time allocation when implementing guided inquiry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy directs chemistry teachers to plan guided inquiry lessons around three implementation conditions the review identifies: student characteristics, availability of learning resources, and available time allocation. The review states that "Pada pelaksanaannya perlu memperhatikan karakteristik siswa, ketersediaan sumber belajar, dan alokasi waktu yang tersedia", framing these as prerequisites teachers must manage alongside the model's long implementation time and students' unfamiliarity with inquiry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must assess student characteristics, the availability of learning resources, and the available time allocation before implementing guided inquiry, as the review states in its weaknesses analysis (Table 5)
#### Constraints
- The review notes implementing the guided inquiry model "memerlukan waktu yang panjang sehingga guru sulit menyesuaikan dengan waktu yang telah ditentukan", so the strategy is limited by fixed schedules

### Target Learners
- high school (SMA) chemistry students

### Target Learning Goals
- chemistry conceptual understanding
- active problem solving through inquiry
