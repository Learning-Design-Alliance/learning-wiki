# Replace repeated revision of the same assignments with easy preparatory homework followed by newly presented, cognitively more complex assignments for buzz-group discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation for improving the seminar format: because students revise preparatory assignments twice during the seminar and lengthy revision particularly decreases motivation to prepare, future seminars should provide relatively easy preparatory homework followed by newly presented, cognitively more complex assignments discussed in buzz groups so students can demonstrate facility and mastery of content. The authors anticipate this adaptation might further improve preparation for and/or active participation in seminars.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparatory homework consisting of relatively easy assignments
- Newly presented, cognitively more complex assignments for buzz-group discussion
#### Constraints
- The article states lengthy revision particularly decreases students' motivation to prepare; the adaptation is anticipated, not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in peer-instructed seminar courses

### Target Learning Goals
- preparation for seminars
- active participation in seminars
- demonstrating facility and mastery of content
