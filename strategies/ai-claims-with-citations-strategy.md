# Require literature citations on every substantive AI claim to build trust-but-verify habits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that AI tutor responses embed scientific references so students learn to justify simulation choices with sources. The authors state that "The design decision to include citations for every substantive AI claim reinforces scholarly practice and can alleviate concerns about AI accuracy in academic settings", ensuring the AI is a verifiable source rather than a hallucination risk. In practice, candidate lists, parameter critiques, and output analyses all arrive with references (DOIs highlighted), modeling evidence-based scientific communication.
