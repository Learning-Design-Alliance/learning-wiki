# Reciprocal Teaching: Peer Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS)

## Description
PALS is a peer tutoring strategy designed to improve reading comprehension and fluency among students, primarily in grades 2–6. In PALS, students are paired, and each member of the pair takes turns being the 'Coach' and 'Player.' The PALS structure includes three main activities: Partner Reading with Retell, Paragraph Shrinking, and Prediction Relay. During each activity, the lower-performing reader serves as the Coach, while the higher-performing reader performs the role of Reader, modeling good reading skills. After five minutes, the students switch roles. The pairs proceed through each of the three activities in the same manner. The teacher selects appropriate text for each student pair based on the reading level of the weaker reader. It is essential that while the students are engaged in peer tutoring activities, the teacher moves about the room and monitors their work. Doing so also provides a great opportunity for the teacher to offer feedback to students about how well they are reading, implementing the PALS activities and working with their partners. Each PALS session lasts for approximately 35 minutes, with about 10 minutes devoted to each of the activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading materials at varying reading levels, training for students on the PALS activities, classroom time of approximately 35 minutes per session.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful pairing of students to ensure effective tutoring. Requires initial training and ongoing monitoring by the teacher to ensure proper implementation. Effectiveness may vary based on student dynamics and reading skill levels.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Primarily designed for students in grades 2–6, but adaptable for older students with reading difficulties.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance reading comprehension, improve reading fluency, practice summarizing, improve vocabulary, develop social awareness and relationship skills

### Affordances
- Peer support can increase motivation and engagement. The structure allows for targeted practice of specific reading skills. Peer interactions also provide opportunities to practice social awareness and relationship skills. Facilitating conversations between learners about texts increases retention of content in Long-term Memory and also develops Speed of Processing.

### Personalization
- Teachers can adapt the complexity of the texts and the roles within the pairs to suit individual student needs. Flexible pairing allows for students to work with different partners, providing diverse perspectives and learning opportunities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Monitor student progress in reading fluency and comprehension through observation and assessment. Collect feedback from students regarding their experience with the PALS activities. Assess student ability to summarize and identify main ideas effectively.

## Impact
- Research suggests that PALS is an evidence-based practice to promote reading achievement. It has been shown to improve reading skills for students with and without learning disabilities.
