# Implement a structured clinical supervision model with trained supervisors, scheduled observations, and three-way conferences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing the CSM to systematize teaching practice: train cooperating teachers and university supervisors in observation techniques, the language of feedback, organizing three-way conferences, data collection, reflective techniques, and action plans. The authors argue that "a model that internally (by the participants of the process through collaboration and three-way conferences) and externally (by the coordinators of the process with process assessment tools) controls the teaching practice can help providing a structured and organized practicum."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal training for USs and CTs in supervision roles and techniques
- Scheduled observations (three per semester in this implementation) with pre- and post-conferences
- Coordinator oversight and process assessment tools
#### Constraints
- The article's evidence comes from one teacher education program in Turkey; the authors call for broader implementation but base the recommendation on this study's satisfaction findings

### Target Learners
- preservice teacher trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- quality supervision, systematic feedback, and reflective development during teaching practice
