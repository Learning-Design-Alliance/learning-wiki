# One-to-one induction meeting with a questioning supervisory style to support PLP creation early in the rotation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An individual one-to-one induction education meeting between each student and an academic staff member during the first two weeks of the clinical rotation. The supervisor reviews the student's completed PLP, explores and clarifies each section, and suggests possible learning strategies. The supervisor "adopted a 'questioning' style (rather than a 'didactic' style)" so the student creates their own plan, and the same experienced medical educator conducted every meeting to ensure consistency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete the PLP in advance of the meeting so it can be reviewed and amended there
- Meetings conducted by an experienced medical educator familiar with the course programme
#### Constraints
- The total face-to-face meeting time was 37 hours with a mean duration of 31 minutes ± 9 minutes per meeting, indicating a substantial faculty time investment

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students beginning a clinical rotation

### Target Learning Goals
- creating a personal learning plan for the rotation
- developing adult learning approaches suited to the clinical learning environment
