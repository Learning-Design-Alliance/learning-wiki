# Involving nursing students in patients' education from admission to discharge in hospitals with nursing shortage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the interaction between nurses and patients was inadequate and patients were not receiving the necessary information, largely due to nursing shortage, the team involved nursing students in patient education. With the support of the nursing faculty, the nursing students were obliged to have an active role in patients' education from admission to discharge, and the head nurse and mediator prepared the students and held several educational classes for them. The impact statement says nursing students in educational hospitals that have nursing shortage can help nursing staff in patients' education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination with the nursing faculty, preparation of the content of patients' education, and preparatory educational classes for the students.
#### Constraints
- The article notes overcoming basic shortages such as nursing shortage was beyond the abilities of the research team; student involvement was a collaborative workaround.

### Target Learners
- nursing students in educational hospitals
- hospital patients

### Target Learning Goals
- patient education
- clinical interaction skills
