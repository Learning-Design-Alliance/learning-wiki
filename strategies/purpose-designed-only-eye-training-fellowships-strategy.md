# Develop purpose-designed training fellowships and formally recognised mentoring programmes to prepare surgeons for high-stakes only eye procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that outcomes of only eye surgery may be improved through "development of purpose-designed training fellowships", alongside formally recognised mentoring. Such programmes "may enable appropriate access to high-stakes patients and nurture the learning processes for ophthalmologists-in-training", addressing the compressed training participants described.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Focused training and mentoring by senior surgeons who perform only eye surgery, with formal recognition of the mentoring role
#### Constraints
- The article notes wet-lab simulations are unable to mimic the true reality of operating on a patient's only eye

### Target Learners
- ophthalmologists-in-training
- early-career ophthalmic surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical technical skill for high-stakes procedures
- surgical resilience
- clinical competency in only eye care
