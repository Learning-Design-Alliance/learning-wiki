## Description
The article proposes extending the exoplanet activities across subject areas. It reports that "integrating this activity across physics and mathematics classes enhances data analysis skills, including the use of logarithmic functions and graph construction." For students unfamiliar with logarithms, the sequence has them cube or square table values before graphing; a logarithmic alternative uses Eq. (2) for students who know logarithms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to computers with Excel spreadsheets; data pre-adapted from the Open Exoplanet Catalog and NASA Eyes on Exoplanets
#### Constraints
- Chilean students may not yet know logarithmic functions, requiring the cubing/squaring variant

### Target Learners
- high school students in physics and mathematics classes

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis skills
- use of logarithmic functions
- graph construction
