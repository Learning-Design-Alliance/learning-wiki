# Ten-step 4C/ID training blueprint for designing whole-task instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 theoretical, 1 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a ten-step procedure for producing a detailed training blueprint: decompose the complex skill, sequence task classes, design learning tasks, analyse mental models, analyse cognitive strategies, design supportive information, analyse rules and procedures, analyse prerequisite knowledge, design just-in-time information, and design part-task practice. It states "The 4C/ID model suggests 10 steps for a detailed training blue print" and notes that steps 3, 6, 9 and 10 pertain to the four components while the remaining six are analytical. The author followed these steps to build the electrical circuits environment.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
