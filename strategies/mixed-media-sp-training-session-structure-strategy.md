# Deliver standard precautions training as biweekly mixed media sessions combining lectures, videos, role-play, and individual online supervision with feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention consisted of "3 biweekly mixed media education sessions, consisting of lectures, videos, role-play, and feedback with 15-20 minutes of individual online supervision and feedback sessions following each class." Session 1 delivered lectures on infection control principles and bloodborne pathogens; session 2 used videos demonstrating correct procedures; session 3 used small-group role-play with scripts. A Tencent QQ online learning group hosted materials and discussion, and researchers made a 15–20 minute one-on-one telephone call after each session to review material and collect feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers prepared a standard precautions procedure script with hospital infection management office staff
- An online platform (Tencent QQ) was set up to share lecture slides, videos, and guideline documents and answer questions
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in 1 hospital in Hubei, China; the authors state a multicenter study would be needed to confirm the model is effective and scalable

### Target Learners
- nursing students at the beginning of clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of standard precautions
- positive attitudes toward standard precautions
- compliance with standard precautions
- hand hygiene effectiveness
