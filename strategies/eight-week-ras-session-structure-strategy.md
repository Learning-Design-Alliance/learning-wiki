# Deliver an 8-week music and movement program of twice-weekly 50-minute sessions with tempo-structured phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's program ran "8 weeks with frequency twice per week for 50 minutes per session". Each session opened and closed with singing to improve mood and motivation, used a warm-up of stretching to 70-bpm 4/4 music, a main phase of walking and balance movements to 90-bpm music, and a cool-down of relaxation and breathing to 70-bpm music. This tempo-structured recipe is offered as a suitable short-duration alternative form of intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An electronic metronome and CD tracks in 4/4 meter at controlled tempos of 70-90 beats per minute
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a single small sample and the authors call for extension to other groups and greater numbers of participants to generalize results

### Target Learners
- adults with ambulatory cerebral palsy

### Target Learning Goals
- motor skill (gait, balance) improvement
- mood and self-esteem improvement
