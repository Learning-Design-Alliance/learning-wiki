# Build the educational intervention from a needs assessment combining farmer baseline KAP data with qualitative interviews of farmers and stakeholders

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy sequences a cross-sectional KAP survey with semi-structured interviews of farmers and key stakeholders (pesticide dealers, KVK scientists, agriculture directors) to identify community-specific needs, then uses those findings to develop and validate the educational video. The protocol states the "need-based educational strategy ensures that the intervention targets specific knowledge gaps and practices, thereby improving the effectiveness and long-term adoption of safe pesticide practices". Experts from multiple domains then validate the module.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Phase 1 survey of 175 farmers and Phase 2 interviews with 20–30 farmers and 10–20 stakeholders must precede module development; expert panel validation via Likert scale and focus group discussions
#### Constraints
- Scoped to pesticide-safety education for farmers in Udupi, Mysuru, and Shivamogga districts of Karnataka

### Target Learners
- Farmers with at least 5 years of pesticide-spraying experience, aged 18–80

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe pesticide handling practices tailored to identified local knowledge gaps
