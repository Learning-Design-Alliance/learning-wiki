Agree on clear assessment criteria with students before using self-assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete implementation step the article draws from Liang (2006): before self-assessment is used, teachers decide on explicit criteria and secure student agreement on them, and provide well-defined scoring descriptors. The article quotes that "Both teachers and students need to agree on clear criteria to use while assessing the students' performance" and that "well-defined scoring criteria and good descriptors lead to provide a close guidance in the assessment". Extensive teacher feedback for modeling is given as an example of creating a supportive assessment environment.
