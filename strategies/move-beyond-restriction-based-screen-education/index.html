Supersede restriction-based education and advance toward education that makes new generations aware of the negative consequences of technology use.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review argues that a need exists "to supersede an education based on the restrictions of times and usages" and to advance toward a system of education making new generations aware that technology use may entail negative consequences of which users are unaware. It frames screens as a cultural tool in initial identity building, calling for pedagogical approaches implementing critical digital education to develop a responsible identity and a critical and civic online mindset.
