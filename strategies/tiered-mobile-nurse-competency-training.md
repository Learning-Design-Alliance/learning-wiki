# Tiered competency training for mobile nurses: simulation-based serious games for junior nurses and crisis-leadership modules for senior nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 design, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes age-stratified, tiered training for mobile nurses. "For junior nurses, simulation-based 'serious games' [28] should be prioritized to enhance technical proficiency in high-stakes scenarios (e.g., emergency response capacity, weight: 0.0194), while senior nurses require advanced modules in crisis leadership and ethical decision-making"—an approach the authors say is validated in infection control nursing. It sits alongside policy-level standardization of competency certification and flexible workforce models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation-based serious-game training resources for high-stakes scenarios
- Advanced modules in crisis leadership and ethical decision-making for senior nurses
#### Constraints
- Regional disparities in training quality persist absent standardized competency certification
- Training design should reflect the model's stated gap in emerging technological competencies such as AI-assisted diagnostics and telehealth navigation

### Target Learners
- junior mobile nurses
- senior mobile nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- technical proficiency in high-stakes emergency scenarios
- crisis leadership and ethical decision-making
