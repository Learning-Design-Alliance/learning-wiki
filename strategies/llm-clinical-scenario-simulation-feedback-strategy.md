# Use LLM interactive features for clinical scenario simulations with performance-based feedback in medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends leveraging LLMs in medical education so that "Medical students can leverage LLMs to synthesize knowledge across diverse medical disciplines, enabling them to manage complex, interdisciplinary cases more effectively", and states that LLMs' "interactive features allow for clinical scenario simulations, providing performance-based feedback that supports medical students in steadily developing their clinical competence".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LLM versions with the capability to process complex text, images, video and audio, which the article says recent iterations have progressively enhanced.
#### Constraints
- The article notes ethical and legal issues, bias risks, content accuracy and public acceptance "must still be addressed", and that LLMs struggle with complex interdisciplinary scenarios.

### Target Learners
- medical students
- residents

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical decision-making practice
- clinical competence development
- synthesis across medical disciplines
