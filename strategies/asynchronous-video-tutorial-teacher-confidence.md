# Offer the oriented-globe video as an asynchronous tutorial so pre-service teachers can practice until confident enough to run the activity with pupils

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors dedicated attention to guiding the audience in the autonomous reproduction of the activities, so that pre-service teachers "can view our video at any time in an asynchronous way in order to practice until feeling" confident enough to realize the hands-on activity with their future pupils. They plan to keep using the resource and to translate the video into English for teachers beyond Italy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video that guides viewers step by step in orienting a common globe and using gnomons, with tools that are easy to find
#### Constraints
- The reported confidence and replication intentions come from post-lesson evaluation questionnaires with pre-service primary teachers, not from observed classroom implementation

### Target Learners
- pre-service primary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- confidence in replicating hands-on astronomy activities
- understanding seasons and time zones well enough to teach them
