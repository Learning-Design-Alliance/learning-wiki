# District actions to improve electronic grading system use: clarify formative/summative guidance, build mastery-based best-practice communities, and update grading standards with professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on observed variability in Gradebook use, the report recommends that CPS clarify grading practices around formative and summative assessments, help mastery-based grading teachers form a best-practices community, explain weighting options and Total Points Logic, update the CPS/CTU Grading Standards, and extend analysis to other subjects and grades. It states "Teachers using mastery-based grading may want to create their own best-practices community."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District and school staff engagement with the grading system (now ASPEN) and its configuration choices
#### Constraints
- The report notes greater consistency in grading practices is not necessarily desirable; it recommends communication rather than uniformity

### Target Learners
- CPS ninth-grade math students
- CPS principals, teachers, and network and central office staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent, well-understood grading practices and student grade improvement opportunities
