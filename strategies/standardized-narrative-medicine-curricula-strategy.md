# Develop standardized, unified narrative medicine curricula to improve study comparability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future research develop "standardized, unified narrative medicine curricula" to improve study comparability and generalizability, because the included studies employed highly variable narrative medicine practices with different supplementary elements and intervention durations. This strategy addresses the heterogeneity that limited the meta-analysis's pooled estimates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula should specify core approaches such as literature reading and parallel medical record writing, and whether supplementary simulation elements are included
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from heterogeneity the authors could not fully resolve through sensitivity or subgroup analysis; it is a research-design recommendation, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents across specialties

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance
- humanistic communication skills
