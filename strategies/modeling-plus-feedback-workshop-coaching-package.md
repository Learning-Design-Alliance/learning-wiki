# Design training opportunities to combine modeling with performance feedback, ideally as an initial workshop followed by one-to-one coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends training that includes modeling (in-person or video demonstration of implementation steps) followed by performance feedback (sharing fidelity data collected while the educator implements with students), since this combination was most strongly associated with larger effects and aligns with single-case design reviews. A practical approach is "an initial training workshop with follow-up training in a one-to-one coaching format," and existing stand-alone workshops can be adapted to add modeling, role-play, and in-classroom coaching follow-up.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunity for a trainer to observe educators implementing the intervention with students and share fidelity data; for group formats, creative solutions such as audio-recording teaching samples for peer critique are needed.
#### Constraints
- The overlap between the modeling-plus-feedback combination and one-to-one coaching format in the reviewed studies means effectiveness of these strategies purely in group format remains an open research question.

### Target Learners
- In-service teachers
- Pre-service teachers
- Paraprofessionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate implementation of evidence-based interventions for students with disabilities
