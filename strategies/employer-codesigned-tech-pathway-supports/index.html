Co-designed recommendations for employers: equitable hiring practices, inclusive work culture, and continued upskilling support¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Worker and learner participants co-designed recommendations for employers across awareness, training access, equitable recruitment and hiring, and career entry, advancement, and retention. Recommendations include creating "a welcoming, inclusive, trusting, and multicultural environment," implementing "nondiscriminatory and fair recruitment and hiring practices (e.g., decrease nepotism)," having "entry-level job opportunities without years of experience requirements," recognizing micro-credentials, and providing "stipends for continuous upskilling."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Employers must build partnerships with education/training providers and maintain transparent, equitable hiring rubrics and promotion paths for the recommendations to function

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations reflect the specific experiences of Black workers and learners in U.S. non-four-year-degree tech pathways and address systemic barriers in the tech industry hiring ecosystem

Target Learners¶

  • Black workers and learners seeking to enter and advance in technology industry careers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable hiring access, career entry, advancement, retention, and continuous upskilling in tech careers
