# Assess and train residents' metacognition to mitigate the Dunning-Kruger effect in surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that further research assess different groups of surgeons, including senior residents and experienced surgeons, and evaluate the impact of training aimed at optimizing metacognition and mitigating the DK effect. They note studies have shown that "training and education can reduce the DK effect by equipping individuals with better metacognitive tools". The strategy is to build metacognitive self-assessment training into surgical education so that overconfident low performers recognize their inadequacy.
