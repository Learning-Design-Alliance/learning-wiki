# Recruit through announced grants, qualification thresholds, and thorough interviews that check stated skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program recruits by posting grant announcements in Italian universities, screening applicants against eligibility rules, and interviewing candidates before selection. Applicants must be in the final year of the triennial Laurea or in Laurea Magistrale courses, and "an average Italian grade higher than 26/30 is requested." Computer science skills and English knowledge must be stated in the application and are later checked in a thorough interview conducted by a board, after which supervisors indicate the best-suited candidates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A board that performs thorough interviews with candidates in person or by Skype
- Supervisors who review qualified student profiles and indicate the best fits
#### Constraints
- Announcements are distributed by volunteers posting flyers; distribution through the Italian Ministry has not yet been achieved, which the authors note would allow more efficient information transfer

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate and master's-level physics and engineering students in Italy

### Target Learning Goals
- selection of students prepared for research internships abroad
