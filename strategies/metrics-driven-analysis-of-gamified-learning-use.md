# Analyse learner utilisation and engagement data from gamified educational software to identify modifiable engagement and attrition factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors modelled their analyses on the free-to-play game industry, where developers analyse player data to increase engagement and then tweak software parameters accordingly. They write that "Analysis of Kaizen-IM data can be viewed in a similar light as it provides insight into how to initially and longitudinally engage residents to encourage repeated investments of time-answering questions". The strategy couples a gamified platform with ongoing analysis of use, badge, notification and attrition data to guide iterative improvement and to identify learners' educational needs for targeted supplementary education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Software that logs participant-level and question-level data automatically, including dates, times, badges earned and response accuracy
#### Constraints
- Analyses of attrition and retention "can only describe associations between variables and cannot denote causality"

### Target Learners
- Graduate medical education trainees in time-constrained, work-hour-regulated settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining engagement and medical knowledge retention in supplemental extracurricular learning
