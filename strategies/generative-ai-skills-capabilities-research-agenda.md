# Research agenda for generative AI in teaching and learning: identify needed skills, resources and capabilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Across the expert contributions the article identifies questions for further research in three thematic areas, including teaching, learning, and scholarly research. A concrete strand asks, from a skills view, "which skills, resources and capabilities (both technical and non-technical) are necessary within organisations to better understand the limitations of ChatGPT within different contexts of use, and promote ethical decision-making". It further asks what role government policies, training providers, higher education and technology developers should play in developing these skills among a workforce whose jobs may be made redundant by robotic process automation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The agenda is a set of proposed research questions, not tested findings

### Target Learners
- Higher education students, educators, researchers and the human workforce

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding limitations of generative AI in different contexts of use
- Ethical decision-making with generative AI
