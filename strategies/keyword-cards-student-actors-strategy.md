# Keyword cards chosen by the students to support student-actors during performances

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To give student-actors greater comfort and confidence and facilitate their acting during performances, the article recommends that the teacher consider conducting the play with cards containing keywords. As printed, "o professor pode avaliar a possibilidade de conduzir a peça com o uso de cartões que contenham palavras -chave, escolhidas pelos próprios estudantes." This addresses the stated risk that anxiety and stage fright combined with many or long lines can make student-actors forget lines, make mistakes or lose the rhythm of the presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cards prepared with keywords chosen by the students themselves
#### Constraints
- Recommended as an option for the teacher to evaluate, in response to anxiety, stage fright, forgotten lines and lost rhythm during performances

### Target Learners
- high-school students acting as student-actors

### Target Learning Goals
- confident live performance of science content
