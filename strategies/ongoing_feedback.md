# Ongoing Feedback

## Description
Ongoing feedback involves providing feedback at various stages of the writing process rather than only on final products. This can be achieved by focusing feedback on specific sections or stages of writing. Students can also proactively seek feedback using digital tools like Google Docs or Kaizena. Stations with self-guiding questions can help students progress at their own pace while teachers address small groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to allocate time for feedback at multiple points during the writing process. Digital tools and self-guiding stations can help manage time.
#### Constraints
- Time constraints can be a significant challenge. Requires careful planning to ensure feedback is manageable and effective.
