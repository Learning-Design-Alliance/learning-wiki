# Later or more flexible school start times to improve adolescent sleep habits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes adjusting school schedules to fit adolescent sleep patterns. It observes that the demands of school hold wake times near 07:00 regardless of age while bedtimes drift later, and concludes that "sleep habits may be improved by later, or more flexible, school start times." This is an authors' recommendation arising from the observed age-related widening of the school/non-school sleep gap.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School schedules currently oblige older adolescents to rise earlier than they otherwise would, per the article's interpretation
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is the authors' interpretation of observational data; the article did not manipulate start times

### Target Learners
- adolescents aged 9-18, especially older adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
- adequate sleep to support learning, school attendance and performance
