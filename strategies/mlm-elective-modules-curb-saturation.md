# Offer MLM training as elective modules or student-selected components to ease curriculum overcrowding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address curriculum saturation and limited protected curricular time, the review reports recommendations to offer MLM training as elective modules or student-selected components. It states: "Proposed strategies to address these constraints include offering MLM training as elective modules or student-selected components, allowing learners to engage with leadership content without displacing core clinical instruction." Additional recommendations include blended or online modalities and interprofessional collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
