Adopt constructivist/inquiry strategies (7E and CBL) in chemistry teaching with adequate teacher preparation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the findings, the article recommends that science teachers, especially chemistry teachers, "should employ modern, practical teaching strategies such as the 7E learning cycle model and case -based learning that allow students to construct their own knowledge and actively partic ipate in the learning process." It further recommends equipping teachers with the skills to create environments where all kinds of students learn meaningfully individually or in groups, reviewing the chemistry curriculum to accommodate learner-centered strategies, and providing laboratory facilities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers adequately equipped with the skills needed for meaningful individual or group learning
  • Teachers intending to use 7E thoroughly trained on the seven steps within a reasonable timeframe

Constraints¶

  • 7E requires more class-time and teacher expertise; CBL is cheaper, requires lesser class time, and is easier to execute

Target Learners¶

  • senior secondary school chemistry students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved chemistry achievement
  • enhanced attitudes toward chemistry

Claims¶

  • Case-based learning yielded the highest attitude mean score, significantly above conventional teaching, but the 7E-versus-CBL attitude difference was not significant [+W]
  • Case-based learning produced the highest chemistry achievement, followed by the 7E learning cycle model, with both exceeding conventional teaching (all pairwise differences significant) [+W]
  • Treatment (7E learning cycle, case-based learning, or conventional teaching) has a significant main effect on senior secondary students' chemistry achievement [+W]
  • Treatment has a significant main effect on students' attitude to chemistry [+W]
  • Class IX physical science students taught with the constructivist 7E model achieved significantly higher post-test and gain scores than students taught with traditional methods [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Chemistry teachers should employ varied effective pedagogies, especially inquiry-based and problem-based approaches, supplemented with visualization and real-world materials
  • Use the constructivist learning approach to improve academic achievement, especially at college and high school levels and across subjects other than religious instruction.
  • Adopt guided inquiry teaching method for light and sound waves with adequate teacher training
