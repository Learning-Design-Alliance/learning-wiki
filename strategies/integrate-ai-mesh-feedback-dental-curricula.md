# Integrate AI-supported personalized feedback systems with MeSH-based structuring into dental curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial results, the authors recommend that "academic institutions could consider integrating AI-supported personalized feedback systems, such as ChatGPT-4o with MeSH based structuring, into dental curricula to enhance diagnostic training and promote individualized learning pathways". They add that such integration may also facilitate scalable, resource-efficient teaching models in settings with limited faculty availability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MeSH-based structuring of feedback so learning gaps are mapped objectively and systematically
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single-institution study of fifth-year students with only short-term outcomes assessed

### Target Learners
- Dental students across educational levels (recommended for future testing)

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnostic training and individualized learning pathways in radiographic interpretation
