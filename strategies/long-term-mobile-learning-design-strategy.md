# Design m-learning experiences as long-term studies targeting high-level skills and exploiting device mobility and context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's concluding recommendation is that future instructional designs with mobile devices should involve "the development of long-term experiences", be "oriented to evaluate high level skills", and "take advantage of mobile features of mobile devices to develop learning activities that be made anytime at anyplace and taking into account context and realistic situations". It also flags studying educational mobile development frameworks that help teachers build apps. This responds to the finding that most reviewed experiences were short-term and targeted low Bloom levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longer research studies and evaluation of high-level skills are needed because current experiences are short-term and low-level
#### Constraints
- The review notes novelty effects of technology may influence results and that only 7 experiences reported long-term periods

### Target Learners
- students in formal and informal educational settings using mobile devices

### Target Learning Goals
- high-level cognitive skills
- context-aware and situated learning anytime and anywhere
