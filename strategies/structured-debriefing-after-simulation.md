# Use a structured debriefing session after simulation scenarios to consolidate learning points.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors used the PEARLS (Promoting Excellence And Reflective Learning in Simulation) framework to guide post-simulation debriefing. Debriefing prompts "encouraged students to critically analyze their experiences, identify perceived challenges, and articulate key learning points," and qualitative analysis of debriefing comments surfaced six learning themes, including vital sign interpretation and medication considerations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained facilitator to guide the debriefing
- A debriefing framework such as PEARLS with structured prompts
- A private setting that encourages learner willingness to discuss reactions
#### Constraints
- The authors chose not to audio-record debriefings to protect privacy, relying instead on in-person notetaking, which may affect data completeness

### Target Learners
- pre-health undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on clinical decision-making
- consolidation of scenario-specific science concepts
