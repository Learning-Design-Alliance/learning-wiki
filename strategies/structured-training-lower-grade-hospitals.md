# Support competence growth in lower-grade hospitals via structured training, study visits, and regional nursing alliances

## Description
Noting that nurses in higher-grade hospitals showed higher core competence (p = 0.019), the article recommends lower-grade hospitals "implement structured training systems, reinforce education in infectious disease prevention and emergency preparedness, facilitate opportunities for inter-institutional study visits in higher-grade hospitals, and develop regional nursing alliances."
