# Run a pilot study before the full experiment and be willing to restart with an improved design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A pilot study is "a first attempt at executing the experimental design with a limited number of trials and/or a small sample size." Its purpose is to identify design flaws, estimate uncertainties for a given sample size, determine whether uncertainties can be reduced by more trials or by better measurement accuracy, confirm equipment operation, and control confounding factors. The authors report seeing many competitive-fair projects that could have been great had the initial attempt been treated as a pilot study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- After a few trials, the student must analyze the early data, recognize flaws or blind alleys, and adjust the experimental design — possibly beginning the experiment anew with a blank slate.
