# Support children's sense of school connectedness and enjoyment as modifiable levers for emotional and behavioural difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In discussing how genetic effects on children's depressive and ADHD traits depend on the child's home and school environment, the article points to school-level factors as modifiable supports: "a greater sense of con-nectedness to school and enjoyment of school may be bene ﬁcial for chil-dren’s depressive and externalizing dif ﬁculties". It notes students' sense of belonging differs substantially across countries and according to perceived school cooperativeness, and calls for further research into how students of all abilities can best be supported.
