# Establish an agency to collect and standardize curriculum materials as a basis for scientific curriculum construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Judd's vice-presidential address applied the scientific method to curriculum construction. The report states "there is at present no agency whose business it is to collect curriculum materials or to subject them to adequate standardization," illustrated by an attempt to develop social studies course material. The strategy is to create such an agency so curriculum decisions rest on standardized, collected materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An institutional body charged with collecting curriculum materials and standardizing them.
#### Constraints
- Judd offered no definite solution of the problem but presented it as one worthy of serious study.

### Target Learners
- students in social studies courses

### Target Learning Goals
- scientifically constructed curricula
