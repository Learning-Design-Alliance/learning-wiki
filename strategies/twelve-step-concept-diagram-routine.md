# Twelve-step teacher-led concept-diagram vocabulary lesson routine

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article specifies a 12-step instructional sequence for each vocabulary word: present a blank concept diagram, state and write the word, state and write the definition, then elicit and list characteristics always, sometimes, and never present, followed by examples and non-examples linked to the characteristics, answering questions and calling on all students an equal number of times. Procedural reliability of this routine was calculated to be 100%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A paraprofessional or observer checklist can verify the 12 steps and the 7 completed diagram sections
- Verbal praise and grades used as reinforcement; equal turn-taking tracked with tic marks
#### Constraints
- The routine was implemented by the teacher-researcher in resource language arts classes with students with LD; the article notes results may not generalize to all resource secondary classrooms

### Target Learners
- middle school students with learning disabilities in resource classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- learning and discussing meanings of content-area vocabulary words
