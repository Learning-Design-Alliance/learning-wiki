# Learning analytics with rapid feedback loops at many levels to build lifelong-learning qualities

## Description
The paper positions learning analytics as exploiting educational big data to deliver near real-time feedback to stakeholders from individual learners to institutions, regions and nations. In complex systems terms, "the creation of rapid feedback loops at many levels could transform the system's capacity to sense and respond effectively to the environment". It notes much current work optimises conventional attainment measures, and flags designing analytics for 21st-century transferable qualities as a particularly difficult challenge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational big data streams and analytics infrastructure capable of delivering near real-time feedback to stakeholders at multiple levels
#### Constraints
- The field is described as still at a nascent stage; much current work optimises conventional attainment measures in conventional institutions rather than transferable lifelong-learning qualities

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
