# Anchor energy quantities in everyday units and household scenarios (kWh, Btu, Cal) with fixed conversion relations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The notes teach energy measurement through units learners already meet in bills and diets, giving fixed conversions: "1 kWh = 860 Cal, 1 kWh = 3, 412 Btu, 1 Cal = 3.967 Btu". Each unit is grounded in a concrete action (a Btu raises a pint of water 1°F; a kWh is an hour's use of a toaster), and comparisons such as the low price of a kWh versus food make the quantities meaningful for planning decisions like how much gas powers a city.
