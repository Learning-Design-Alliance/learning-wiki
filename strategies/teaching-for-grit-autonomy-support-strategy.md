# Teaching for grit: build autonomy-supportive classroom climates with collaboration, low-competition culture, constructive feedback, and challenging long-horizon learning experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors' instructional-practice recommendations follow from their finding that perseverance, not passion, mediated the autonomy-support path to grades. They advise that teachers "look at failure as a data point rather than a normative indicator of student motivation", and that "These actions could include enhancements of collaborations among students, less focus on inter-competitive culture, providing constructive comments and feedback, and also improvement of creativity and innovations". They also call for designing learning experiences that challenge students and taking a long-term, outer-school perspective when fostering grit.
