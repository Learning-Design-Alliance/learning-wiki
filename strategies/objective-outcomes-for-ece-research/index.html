Strategies

Use objective outcome measures (blinded OSCE, standardized tests, structured scales) in future ECE research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that "Future studies should prioritize using objective outcome measures, such as blinded OSCE scores, standardized written or computerized knowledge tests, and structured empathy or professionalism scales." These measures would reduce heterogeneity and enable robust meta-analyses of ECE efficacy. They also call for studies from high-income countries to ensure global generalizability.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • adoption of blinded OSCE scores, standardized knowledge tests, and structured empathy or professionalism scales

Constraints¶

  • current evidence base is dominated by low and middle-income country studies with heterogeneous designs and measures

Target Learners¶

  • medical students in pre-clinical years

Target Learning Goals¶

  • competency development across clinical skills, knowledge, empathy, and professionalism domains

Claims¶

  • Early clinical exposure is associated with higher empathy scores regardless of gender and academic year [~W]
  • Early clinical exposure improves the clinical skills of medical students compared with traditional teaching [~W]
  • Most medical students report that early clinical exposure improves their communication skills [~W]
  • Early clinical exposure increases medical knowledge test scores and motivates learning [~W]
  • Students perceive that early clinical exposure improves problem-solving and confidence, and many redefine their understanding of professionalism [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Future research should use standardized validated outcome measures, longitudinal follow-up designs, and methods linking learner gains to patient-relevant outcomes
  • Future CB-PA research and practice should use standardized, ecologically valid outcome measures, objective activity parameters, and longitudinal designs
  • Study empathy in clinical encounters using observational and qualitative methods rather than self-report alone
