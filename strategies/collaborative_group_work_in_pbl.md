# Collaborative Group Work in PBL

## Description
In project-based learning (PBL), collaborative group work involves intentionally structuring activities to ensure students work together to achieve common goals. This can be done through critique and revision processes, collaborative products, labs, and team-building activities. Collaborative work provides English Language Learners (ELLs) with opportunities to practice academic vocabulary and language structures orally, supported by their peers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful planning of collaborative activities, clear communication of expectations, and facilitation of positive group dynamics.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation to ensure equitable participation and avoid dominant group members, and may necessitate additional support for ELLs to fully participate.
