# Add dedicated disciplinary-content sessions to tinkering training and co-design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that training and co-design phases, which until the Officina della Luce focused on the methodological aspects of tinkering and documentation, should also include dedicated sessions addressing appropriate disciplinary content, so teachers feel more competent and better equipped to guide students' inquiries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to disciplinary training on the physics content underlying the tinkering workshops
- Integration of such sessions into existing training and co-design phases
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from teachers' self-reported sense of inadequacy in scientific subjects, particularly physics, in this specific project context

### Target Learners
- primary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- guiding students' scientific inquiries
- disciplinary preparedness in physics

## Claims

- [Teachers predominantly integrated tinkering into language, expression and storytelling rather than science, despite a science-centred resource library](../claims/teachers-prefer-humanities-integration-of-tinkering.md) [+W]
