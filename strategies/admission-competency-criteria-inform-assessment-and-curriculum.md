# Use validated admission competency criteria to design admission-evaluation methodologies and align curriculum with graduation competencies

## Description
The article states that through a well-defined framework of admission competencies, "it is feasible to develop suitable admission-evaluation methodologies encompassing standardized performance assessments, multiple mini-interviews, and various tests, such as written examinations, essays, and evaluations of manual dexterity". It further recommends that developed admission competencies be "intricately linked to graduation competencies" and integrated into the curriculum, so institutions can distinguish qualities students should possess at entry from those to be instilled during education.
