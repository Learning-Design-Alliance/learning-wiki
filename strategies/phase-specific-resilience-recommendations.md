# Phase-specific and culture-level recommendations to strengthen resilience teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following the curriculum analysis, multidisciplinary collaboration with senior staff and clinicians produced "a comprehensive list of short, medium and long-term recommendations, including both modification of existing course elements and the creation of bespoke learning experiences". Recommendations were presented for each of the three course phases plus a category of medical school culture, student support and wellbeing. Examples include enhancing the Phase I to Phase II transition programme, reflective essays on resilience and self-efficacy, Balint Groups, Schwartz rounds, frameworks for managing the emotional impact of mistakes, and wellbeing workshops such as mindfulness and meditation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with senior staff and senior clinicians from the local NHS trust
- Data from the curriculum evaluation to identify less well represented areas
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are specific to the evaluated five-year MBBS programme's phase structure

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students across all five years

### Target Learning Goals
- Managing workplace transitions
- Managing emotions
- Reflectiveness
- Connectedness and belonging
