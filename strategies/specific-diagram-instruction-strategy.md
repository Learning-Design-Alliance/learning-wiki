# Provide specific instruction on drawing diagrams to increase students' use of diagrams in problem solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article observes that despite the lack of a figure in the problem statement, not all students started by visualizing the problem, and notes that "drawing appropriate diagrams is a useful problem solving strategy [10,11], with the possibility that speciﬁc instruction on this topic may increase students’ use of diagrams." Instructors could therefore explicitly teach diagram use when students solve problems that benefit from visual representation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
