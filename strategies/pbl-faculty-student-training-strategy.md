# Train faculty and orient students on PBL processes before and during PBL implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention included structured training for both PBL stakeholders. "Faculty development workshops were arranged with the help of the faculty development unit, and medical education department, who trained the faculty/facilitators on conducting PBL", while students received orientation classes covering the whole PBL process, their roles, and simulated PBL discussions. Three 3-hour faculty workshops and 2-hour student orientation sessions were delivered, covering PBL history and theory, hands-on facilitation and problem development, and reflections on challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development unit and medical education department support
- Sessions covering PBL history, purpose, learning theories, facilitation, and roles
#### Constraints
- The article attributes prior PBL gaps mostly to lack of training and preparedness by teachers, students, curriculum, and problem designers

### Target Learners
- Medical faculty facilitators
- Undergraduate medical (MBBS) students

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct PBL session conduction
- Understanding of PBL roles and steps
