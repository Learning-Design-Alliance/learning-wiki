# School initiatives targeting vulnerable students' well-being alongside academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends school initiatives, undertaken for example by school psychologists and teachers, that emphasize not only students' academic performances but also their health and well-being. Suggested measures include strengthening students' self-confidence and academic competences by focusing on personal and social strengths, and establishing positive learning environments characterized by "a culture of acceptance" rather than a strong focus only on academic performance. Such initiatives help detect young people at risk given differential sensitivity to class-level performance.
