Teachers should implement the developed experiential learning-based Mathematics teaching material¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's conclusion recommends that teachers "should implement the experiential learning-based teaching material in Mathematics that has been previously developed", that students actively comprehend material with it, and that other researchers use the study as a reference for similar development research. This recommendation follows directly from the study's finding that the material improved fifth-graders' Mathematics cognitive ability relative to conventional material.

Requirements¶

  • The teaching material must have been previously developed following the four experiential learning stages

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single quasi-experiment in two Indonesian elementary schools on volume of cube and beam content

  • Fifth-grade elementary school students
