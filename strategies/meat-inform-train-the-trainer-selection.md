# Use the MEAT to select or train trainers in train-the-trainer initiatives once predictive validity is established

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a forward-looking application: if trainer characteristics are found to be associated with desired training outcomes, the MEAT "could then be used to select or train trainers to be more effective in their role." It specifically envisions use within "train-the-trainer" (TTT) initiatives, informing both characteristics fostered in clinicians being trained to train others and selection among professional trainers previously evaluated with the measure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strong evidence for predictive validity of the MEAT must first be established before it is used for selection purposes
#### Constraints
- The current study provides only preliminary psychometric evidence; associations with behavior change have not yet been demonstrated

### Target Learners
- Clinicians being trained to train others in their organization; professional trainers of empirically supported treatments

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving effectiveness of training in empirically supported treatments through trainer selection and development
