# Establish a professional development training center for multiple intelligences-based teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends establishing "a training center for professional development for the staff at the College of Languages and Translation at Al-Imam University using multiple intelligences to train teachers designing various instructional plans." The center would provide professional development programs, help novice teachers acquire teaching skills based on multiple intelligences, and exchange services among local and international centers. This addresses the authors' observation that multiple intelligences "has not yet been applied in the Middle East, specifically at higher education."
