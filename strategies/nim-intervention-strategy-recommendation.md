# Use the Neurological Impress Method to improve reading fluency of students with learning disabilities in regular public schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that educators and teachers pursue applying NIM for teaching reading fluency to students with learning disabilities, and that its applications be improved in public schools in Jordan and other Middle Eastern countries. The strategy involves simultaneous repeated aloud reading with a trained teacher, in a quiet environment, without stopping to correct errors, over regular sessions across a semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in the method: the study's four experienced teachers were trained for one week
- Permission and scheduling: sessions ran five days a week for 45 minutes for about sixteen weeks
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted with fourth-grade students in four public primary schools in Amman, Jordan

### Target Learners
- Students with learning disabilities in public primary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved reading fluency
