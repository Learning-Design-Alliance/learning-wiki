# Connect content to student lives

## Description
This strategy involves showing students how a topic connects to their lives, giving an example of a particular idea as they would experience it in their native country, or asking them to bring music or art from their culture and describe its significance and meaning to their classmates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for research, flexible curriculum, willingness to incorporate student input, sensitivity to cultural differences.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and sensitivity to avoid putting students on the spot or making assumptions. May require additional resources to support diverse cultural expressions.

- Encourages student contributions, promotes cultural pride, enhances learning relevance, and provides diverse perspectives.

### Personalization
- Allows students to share their personal experiences and cultural traditions. Offers flexible ways for students to contribute, such as through music, art, or interviews.
