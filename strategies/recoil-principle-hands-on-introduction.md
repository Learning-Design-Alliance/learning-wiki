# Introduce the recoil principle through familiar, hands-on examples such as balloons, string-guided balloon rockets and water rockets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before formal treatment, the handout recommends anchoring the recoil principle (Rückstoß) in experiences students know or find interesting: an air balloon from which air escapes and which flies through the room, balloon vehicles on a string or on wheels, a propeller boat, and optionally a self-built water rocket as a cross-subject or club group project. The teacher lets students experiment with balloons and discuss what makes the balloon fly, and can show Alexander Gerst demonstrating rocket recoil in an experiment on the ISS.
