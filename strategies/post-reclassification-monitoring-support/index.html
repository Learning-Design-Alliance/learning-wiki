Integrate post-reclassification monitoring and support so students who exit EL status continue receiving integrated support in the general curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends integrating post-reclassification monitoring more meaningfully, ensuring that students who exit EL status continue to receive integrated support that helps them thrive in the general curriculum. It pairs this with helping schools reduce opportunity differences between EL students and other students by improving instructional quality and access before students exit EL status, and with continued monitoring to ensure PL 4 students have equitable access to advanced coursework.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Monitoring systems for students after they exit EL status and integrated support structures within the general curriculum

Constraints¶

  • Stated as a recommendation in the brief; the brief does not report a test of this strategy's effectiveness

Target Learners¶

  • Students who have exited English Learner status, particularly those at ELPAC overall PL 4

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Continued academic thriving in the general curriculum and equitable access to advanced coursework

Related Strategies¶

  • Consider removing Criterion 4 from California's reclassification requirements while maintaining the ELPAC overall PL 4 threshold
  • Consider policy design carefully to ensure equitable reclassification opportunities for EL students
  • Build long-term systems to track English learner progress, including students who have exited EL status
