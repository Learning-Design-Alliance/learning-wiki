# Trait Identification and Construction

## Description
These tasks involve the study of traits, requiring learners to identify animal body parts from puzzle pieces and construct either real or mythical animals. They encourage learners to measure, observe, and justify the design of their constructions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Puzzle pieces, drawing materials, rulers, measuring tools
#### Constraints
- May require access to specialized materials.
