# Intensive theoretical and practical BSE training sessions using lectures, demonstration and redemonstration on breast modules

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a multi-component educational program: "each group of participant we re given intensive theoretical and practical sessio ns in breast cancer and breast self exam." The theoretical part consisted of two lecture/discussion sessions on epidemiology, risk factors, presentation, screening programs, and BSE steps, delivered via PowerPoint. The practical part involved "two subsequent r einforcement sessions for practice (demonstration a nd redemonstration) using breast modules" with students practicing on modules and themselves under researcher supervision, plus self-explanatory pictures illustrating BSE positions and procedures.
