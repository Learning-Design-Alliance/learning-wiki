# Offer lecture-based online skills teaching with written questions as a scalable first step for first-year medical students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that didactic online delivery, though rated lower than small-group teaching, retains value at scale: "Lecture-based online skills teaching with written questions provides some benefit in learning surgical skills and has the additional advantage of teaching a large number of delegates simultaneously. This may be an appropriate first step in surgical skills teaching in the first years of medical school." In the course, two demonstrators presented skills at a pace students could practise alongside, with a moderated online forum for written questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A moderated online discussion forum monitored continuously for written questions
#### Constraints
- Lecture-based online teaching "will also require validation beyond self-reported development"

### Target Learners
- First-years medical students
- Large delegate cohorts

### Target Learning Goals
- Introductory surgical skills exposure
