# Maintain a peer-reviewed register of certified Urology apps or issue an official scientific seal of approval until formal regulation exists.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that, in the absence of industry standards or independent medical app regulation, the urological community itself should regulate app development. They write that "maintaining a peer-reviewed register of certified Urology apps or issuing an official scientific seal of approval, could influence overall app design and, consequently, improve urological mHealth solutions." They also propose that urologists become stakeholders in mHealth, shaping app design and promoting peer-review app validation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engagement of the urological community and scientific societies, since the article notes some issues may not be within the competence of Urology associations alone
#### Constraints
- Applies until clear regulation is enforced by government health authorities or independent organizations

### Target Learners
- Urologists and healthcare professionals using mHealth apps
- General-public users of patient-information apps

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe, scientifically accurate selection and use of medical apps for professional learning and patient information
