# Remove language and participation barriers by communicating with families in multiple languages through varied channels with interpreters available

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide directs states, districts, and schools to allocate resources to "dismantle barriers to information, participation, and communication for linguistically diverse families." It names barriers including language access, literacy levels, work schedules, and lack of trust for public institutions, and recommends one-to-one calls, texts, hotlines, radio, home visits, and events with food, childcare, live translation, and transportation reimbursement, conducted in multiple languages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communication must be conducted in multiple languages as appropriate to the community; interpreters and live translation must be available for relevant meetings and events, including IEP meetings, teacher conferences, and mental health support meetings
#### Constraints
- Traditional mass emails, flyers, or mass phone calls are not enough, especially if only communicated in English

### Target Learners
- Linguistically diverse families of English learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Family access to information and participation in school decisions and supports
