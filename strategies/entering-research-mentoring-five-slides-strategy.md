# Structured mentoring and professional-development sessions including Entering Research, Entering Mentoring, and Five slides in five minutes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The internship opens with an "Entering Research" session on day two to set expectations, develop identity as a researcher, and build understanding of the research environment, using guided group discussion and a feedback survey. On day three, an "Entering Mentoring" session for mentors uses CIMER material on aligning expectations and maintaining effective communications, with surveys, case studies and mentor-mentee agreements. Early in the internship, interns present their project understanding in a "Five slides in five minutes" exercise, which helped mentors evaluate the student's initial level and gave students confidence presenting before experts.
