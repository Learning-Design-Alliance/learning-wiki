# Concept Mapping

## Description
Concept maps are visual tools that help learners organize and articulate what they know about a given concept or topic. Important concepts are enclosed in a circle or box and connected to other concepts by lines that include explanatory phrases to define the relationship (e.g., “begins with”, “includes”, or “aids”). This strategy promotes meaningful connections within a topic, facilitates deeper understanding, and enhances retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires structured prompts and guidelines for creating concept maps. Instructor must facilitate the activity and provide feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure concepts are well-defined. Effectiveness depends on learners' ability to make meaningful connections.
