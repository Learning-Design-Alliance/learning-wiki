# Last Lesson, Last Week, Last Month

## Description
This activity involves looking back to the most important information presented yesterday, last week, and last month. Choose three questions from each and ask students to answer (retrieval practice) the questions with no assistance. This helps students realize that the importance of studying a particular topic doesn’t end with the summative assessment and that knowledge builds knowledge.
