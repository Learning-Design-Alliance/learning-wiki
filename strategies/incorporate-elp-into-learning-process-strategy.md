# Incorporate experiential learning programs into the learning process so students strengthen attitudes, abilities, and skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's impact statement recommends that "schools at all levels incorporate ELP into the learning process so that students can strengthen their attitudes, abilities, and skills in their learning activities". The strategy pairs embodied, realistic care experiences with reflective journaling and group sharing, and the authors note it "can be performed without facing people, it is suitable for use during a pandemic", making it usable when social distancing restricts interpersonal contact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be followed by journal writing and group discussion to share experiences
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single one-group quasi-experiment with no control group; effects on thoughts-domain self-reflection were not significant

### Target Learners
- students at all levels, as evaluated in freshmen nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-reflection
- critical thinking disposition
- attitudes, abilities, and skills in learning activities
