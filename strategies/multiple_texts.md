# Multiple Texts

## Description
Reading multiple texts on the same topic expands learners' understanding, encourages critical thinking, and fosters synthesis skills. This strategy involves establishing a clear purpose for reading, selecting short texts from various sources (news articles, video clips, trade books, diagrams), modeling connections between texts using tools like Inquiry Charts, facilitating discussions to combine information from different sources, and integrating sketching and writing to deepen understanding. By engaging with multiple texts, learners develop a sense of agency and can more effectively answer their own questions, while also building more knowledge of the subject matter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of multiple short texts related to a topic, clear research questions or purpose for reading, Inquiry Charts or other tools for recording information, time for reading, discussion, and writing or sketching.
#### Constraints
- May be overwhelming for some learners if not properly scaffolded. Requires careful selection of texts to ensure relevance and accuracy.
