# Hold professional development book clubs early in training, before clinical rotations begin

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends scheduling a professional development book club at the start of residency, before clinical duties begin, to introduce the importance of professional development. The authors state that "the period of time prior to starting clinical rotations was the ideal time to hold this exercise" and suggest it may also be beneficial during the final months of medical school as a deliberate attempt to jump-start postgraduate development. Program leadership may need to deliberately incorporate professional development into the curriculum.
