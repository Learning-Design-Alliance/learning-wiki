# Integrate a teacher or instructor interface to review and correct AI-generated content alongside AI feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends hybrid feedback integration in which driving instructors can review flagged content and supplement AI output, drawing on Ifenthaler et al.'s suggestion that combining AI-generated feedback with human intervention creates an optimal learning environment. The article states "The driving instructors can review flagged content, correct LLM errors, or highlight critical topics to supplement AI output". Although the self-taught driving theory context made this out of scope, the authors propose it would work well in a teaching environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An educator interface through which instructors can view user profiles and adjust settings
#### Constraints
- Not suited to the self-taught UK Driving Theory Test context studied; proposed for teaching environments

### Target Learners
- Learners in instructor-supported settings
- Driving theory candidates

### Target Learning Goals
- Correcting AI errors and misconceptions
- Prioritising high-risk topics
