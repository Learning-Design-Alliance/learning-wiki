> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The brief directs its recommendations and strategies at three distinct audiences: "state officials, district-level staff, and system developers who seek to promote effective data use by decision makers and educators at any level of the education system." Guidance is therefore differentiated by role rather than offered as one generic prescription, with this brief covering data-system development and companion briefs covering use planning and user support.
