Integrate the 5Es instructional strategy into civic education curricula, lesson plans, and teacher education¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on its finding that the 5Es strategy improved literacy scores, the study issues four recommendations: curriculum planners should integrate the 5Es into the senior secondary civic education curriculum; teachers should expose students to the strategy "by incorporating the strategy into their lesson plans and executing it properly"; seminars and workshops on the 5Es should be run for secondary school teachers; and universities and colleges of education should integrate the 5Es into their methodology curriculum. The strategy is positioned as developing students' generic skills and civic competence.
