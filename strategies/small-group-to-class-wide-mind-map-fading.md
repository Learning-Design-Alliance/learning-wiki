# Progress from small-group domain-specific mind maps to class-wide integration to fade scaffolding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable two-step recipe: each group reads assigned articles and builds a domain-specific mind map, then presents and integrates it into a class-wide map. The article describes this as demonstrating "fading scaffolding as peer and instructor feedback during integration builds student independence," moving learners from supported inquiry toward independent synthesis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Domain-specific research articles per group
- Time for presentation and collaborative integration sessions
#### Constraints
- Reported in one postgraduate immunology module; scalability across other disciplines not yet tested

### Target Learners
- postgraduate students in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- synthesizing primary research
- peer learning
- independent knowledge construction
