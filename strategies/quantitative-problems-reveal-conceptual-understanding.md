## Description
The article recommends that instructors use "properly designed quantitative problems that require students to illustrate their conceptual learning and understanding", stating such problems reveal much about students' learning, provide invaluable feedback, and help students understand physics concepts. This strategy addresses the instructor-side difficulty of teaching conceptual and quantitative reasoning holistically, and is complemented by laboratory work where conceptual learning is reinforced by experience.
