# Incorporate culturally responsive strategies into adult immigrant instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends instruction that encompasses cultural backgrounds by using learning materials and activities that allow for different levels of understanding. It lists four strategies: recognize and respect each student's unique cultural backgrounds; incorporate a variety of teaching strategies to meet diverse learning needs; engage with materials and examples that reflect the students' culture and experiences; and facilitate discussions about cultural differences and social justice issues. Kamisli's findings underscore incorporating culturally relevant materials and teaching strategies for adult immigrant learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must possess culturally friendly awareness and tailor lesson plans and activities based on learners' cultural concepts
#### Constraints
- Educators cannot imply learners are unable to acquire understanding based on a collectivist background

### Target Learners
- Adult immigrant English learners in ESOL and community programs

### Target Learning Goals
- English language acquisition
- Culturally sensitive learning experiences
- Integration
