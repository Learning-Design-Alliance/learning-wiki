# Divide laboratory activities into parallel student-group strands merged in a joint final analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experiment's activities were organised into parallel strands assigned to separate student groups, then merged for a joint analysis session. The article reports "The students were divided into two groups, with the first one focusing on the characterization of the MKID's resonances and superconductor band gap, while the second group worked on the data acquisition system", with the final data analysis "carried out by both groups together" to confirm photon detection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities that "could be carried out separately", so each group has a self-contained experimental strand.
#### Constraints
- A final joint data-analysis phase is needed to confirm the detection result.

### Target Learners
- graduate students in quantum technologies

### Target Learning Goals
- resonator characterization
- data acquisition system development
- calibration and data analysis
