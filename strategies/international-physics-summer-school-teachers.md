## Description
The paper's concluding recommendation is to "Establish, with international partners, an International Particle Physics Summer School for Teachers." The proposed school would have "a strong but accessible content orientation, along with emphasis on physics education reform," and is offered as a unique U.S. contribution complementing other international efforts such as CERN teacher programs and the African School of Fundamental Physics and Applications.
