# Real-time fluctuation-index monitoring with a 1.4 cutoff for perioperative skill feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose displaying the fluctuation index β on a surgical monitor in real time so that skill evaluation can occur during surgery rather than in retrospective expert video review. Because β "is an index that can be calculated in real time so skill evaluation can be performed during surgery," and it evaluates movements over the past 3 s, deviations below the 1.4 cutoff could trigger perioperative modifications. The article frames this as a development direction, not a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI instrument-tip identification pipeline accurate enough to yield coordinate time series from live video
#### Constraints
- Results are preliminary; the authors state further research is necessary to identify the AI-based gold standard, with more precise power analyses and data from more institutions and practitioners needed

### Target Learners
- surgeons receiving intraoperative feedback

### Target Learning Goals
- real-time surgical skill self-evaluation and perioperative improvement
