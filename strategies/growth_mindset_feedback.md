# Growth Mindset Feedback

## Description
Growth mindset feedback focuses on praising effort, strategies, and persistence rather than innate abilities. This approach encourages learners to view intelligence as malleable and cultivates a belief that skills can be developed through dedication and hard work. Providing respectful and specific feedback, highlighting 'good mistakes' as learning opportunities, and valuing the learning process over grades are key elements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires intentional language and feedback practices from instructors and peers. Classroom activities should emphasize cooperative work and the value of learning.
#### Constraints
- Praise for intelligence can undermine motivation. Without a focus on the learning process, learners may base self-perceptions on grades alone. Competitive environments may hinder the development of a growth mindset.

- Track changes in learners' attitudes toward challenges and effort. Measure improvements in academic performance and persistence. Observe learners' responses to feedback and their willingness to take on difficult tasks.
