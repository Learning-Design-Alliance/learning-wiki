# Recruit course completers as teaching assistants for a summer school to consolidate and extend their learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To keep students engaged after the semester ends, the authors recruit undergraduates who successfully completed HQW! as teaching assistants for their annual QISE summer school for high-school students. This gives the TAs "the opportunity to revisit the material almost six months later and to grasp it in a robust way, which is required for them to be able to teach it", and the authors found it "a good way for students to feel empowered and continue to be engaged" until they may take on a research project.
