Offer instructor-familiar graphic novel options to reduce preparation burden when implementing the comic-book assignment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend two implementable variants for instructors unfamiliar with many graphic novels: "assigning one graphic novel (with which the instructor is fam iliar) to all students; or providing an all-inclu sive list of graphic novels (each with which the instructor is fam iliar) from which the students can choose". These options reduce the time-consuming requirement that the instructor read many graphic novels before implementing the assignment.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The instructor must be familiar with the graphic novel(s) assigned or listed

Constraints¶

  • The original study's open-selection version depended on the authors' prior familiarity with the medium

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate psychology majors in an abnormal psychology course

Target Learning Goals¶

  • diagnostic evaluation skills

Claims¶

  • Undergraduate abnormal psychology students who completed a comic-book character diagnostic assignment scored significantly higher on a pop quiz than lecture-only students [+W]
  • Prior constructivist-inspired abnormal psychology assignments (mock client interviews, famous-person diagnoses, novels, historical figures, song lyrics) were reported favourably by students [+W]
