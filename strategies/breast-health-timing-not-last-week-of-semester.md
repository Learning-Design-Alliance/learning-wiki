# Schedule breast health instruction early enough in the semester that it is not perceived as an unimportant topic

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends attention to when breast health is taught: the programs were delivered from January through the first week of May, completed before end-of-semester distractions. It states "the timing of the breast health content is very important in order for it to be a meaningful experience for adolescent females," warning that teaching it the last week of the semester may lead students to perceive it as unimportant while they focus on exams and breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Breast health should be given the same importance as other health topics and not taught in the final week of a semester
#### Constraints
- Timing guidance is an author recommendation, not a manipulated or tested variable in the study

### Target Learners
- adolescent females in high school health education courses

### Target Learning Goals
- breast health knowledge and beliefs
