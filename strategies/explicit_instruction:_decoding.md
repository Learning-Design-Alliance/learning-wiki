# Explicit Instruction: Decoding

## Description
Explicit instruction in decoding involves providing direct and systematic teaching of phonics and word recognition skills. This includes teaching learners to sound out words, recognize common letter patterns, and use morphological awareness to decode unfamiliar words. Explicit decoding instruction is particularly beneficial for early readers and learners with reading difficulties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires systematic phonics instruction, trained educators, and appropriate instructional materials (e.g., phonics workbooks, flashcards).
#### Constraints
- May not be sufficient for all learners with reading difficulties. Requires ongoing assessment and adjustment to meet individual needs.
