# Strength-Based Approach to Self-Awareness

## Description
This method involves identifying a student's strengths related to self-awareness and using those strengths to address their challenges. Self-awareness is the ability to correctly identify one's own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they impact behavior. Strategies include having students identify and label feelings, engaging in journal reflections to understand patterns and manage goals, and using estimation and prediction skills to reflect on accuracy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of student's strengths and challenges. Journal materials and prompts. Activities for estimation and prediction.
#### Constraints
- Requires accurate identification of student's strengths and challenges. May need to provide additional support for students with significant challenges.
