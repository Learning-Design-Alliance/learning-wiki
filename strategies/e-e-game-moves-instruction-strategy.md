# Structure estimation problem instruction around the six e-e-game moves of problematize through evaluation

## Description
The article's analysis of the estimation epistemic game implies instructors can scaffold estimation problem solving around its moves: problematize the system, propose a method, tabulate what to remember, check if parts are enough, perform pure calculations, and evaluate. The authors state that through e-e-games, "students are able to apply their intuition and accessible knowledge in taking the first crucial steps of solving problems as a physicist."
