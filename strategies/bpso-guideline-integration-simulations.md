# Integrate BPSO guidelines into core courses through evidence-based situational simulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes integrating six BPSO guidelines into eight core courses through evidence-based situational simulations under the guidance of clinical nursing educators and curriculum design specialists. Guidelines were embedded with 2-8 hours per guideline module across courses including Fundamentals of Nursing, Medical Nursing, Surgical Nursing, and others. Faculty development workshops with guideline interpretation sessions, teaching demonstrations, and peer feedback supported implementation fidelity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a multidisciplinary development team including evidence-based nursing experts, clinical nursing educators, curriculum design specialists, and BPSO coordinators
#### Constraints
- The absence of standardized textbooks for evidence-based nursing education required faculty to develop original teaching materials, a time-intensive process

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in Years 2-3

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical appraisal skills, evidence synthesis ability, and understanding of research methodology

### Affordances
- Three Tier Ecological Environment Ebp
