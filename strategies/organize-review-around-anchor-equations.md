# Have students organize review notes around a few core ideas or anchor equations to transform how they view course knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The author advises students frustrated with quiz preparation to stop making long lists and instead take their reviews or class notes and 'organize them around a few core ideas — often around an anchor equation'. He reports that students then feel 'there’s not actually that much to learn' and that their quiz and exam performance improves dramatically, though this is his own unsystematic observation.
