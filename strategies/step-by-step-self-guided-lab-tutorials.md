# Write laboratory tutorials as detailed step-by-step instructions students follow independently without continual instructor guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each tutorial in the package is written as a numbered, granular procedure with equipment lists, safety notes, visual-check figures, and explicit success criteria, so that students can complete alignment tasks on their own. The author states the tutorials "are written using detailed step-by-step instructions for the students to follow independently without the need for continual instructor guidance." This frees instructor attention while standardizing the skills all students acquire.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructions must be detailed enough for students with no prior optical laboratory experience to follow without continual instructor guidance
#### Constraints
- Assumes students begin with no prior optical laboratory training or experience

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduates
- new graduate students in an experimental optical program

### Target Learning Goals
- independent execution of optical alignment procedures
- common fundamental laboratory skill base across the student group
