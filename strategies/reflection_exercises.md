# Reflection Exercises

## Description
Reflection exercises are activities designed to promote emotional self-awareness and regulation by encouraging learners to think about their feelings and behaviors. Examples include: Journal Reflections: Students think back on their experiences and outcomes helps provide them with a greater understanding of themselves. Students can think and write about their experiences throughout a day or class period and look for patterns, as well as create and manage goals.
