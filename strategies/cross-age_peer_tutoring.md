# Cross-Age Peer Tutoring

## Description
Cross-age peer tutoring involves pairing students from different grade levels, with older students acting as tutors for younger students. The older students reinforce their understanding of the material by teaching it, while the younger students benefit from personalized attention and guidance. This approach fosters a sense of responsibility and leadership in the older students and provides a supportive learning environment for the younger students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful pairing of students, clear learning objectives, and structured activities. Teachers need to provide training and support for tutors to ensure effective instruction.
#### Constraints
- May require additional planning and coordination to schedule tutoring sessions. Tutors need to be adequately prepared and supported to provide effective instruction.

- Can be adapted to various subjects and topics, allowing tutors to focus on areas where tutees need the most support. Flexible structure accommodates different learning styles and paces.
