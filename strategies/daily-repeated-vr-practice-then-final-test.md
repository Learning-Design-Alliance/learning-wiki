# Schedule repeated daily VR simulation sessions with formative feedback before a delayed practical test

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training regimen: students complete a formative test with real-time feedback and guidance, then practice the VR program repeatedly over consecutive days within the curriculum, followed by a practical test after preclinical training ends. The article required that "all the students were required to complete the offline virtual training one time each day for 5 consecutive days as part of the oral and maxilofacial surgery curriculum". Scores improved across repetitions but plateaued after 2–4 practices, informing how many sessions to schedule.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the VR system for daily sessions; introductory and instructional videos so users learn system operation before autonomous training
#### Constraints
- Scores plateaued after 2 to 4 practices, so additional repetitions beyond that showed diminishing gains in this pilot

### Target Learners
- fourth-year stomatology undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical suture and debridement procedural skills
- history-taking and clinical reasoning
