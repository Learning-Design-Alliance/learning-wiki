# Build a research foundation for K-12 AI education modeled on mathematics and science education research

## Description
The editors recommend establishing an evidence-based research enterprise for K-12 AI education rather than producing curricula without a research base. They state the field should "develop a research foundation for K-12 AI education that follows the models of mathematics education research and science education research" and build on computer science education research from the past decade. The aim is to create evidenced-based principles for teaching AI in K-12 settings.
