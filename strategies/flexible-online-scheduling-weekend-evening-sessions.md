# Schedule flexible online learning with weekend and evening sessions to support participation alongside clinical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends flexible, online-based scheduling to enable students to participate while carrying usual clinical commitments. Asynchronous microlearning lets students learn "in their own time and space", and the authors report that scheduling practical sessions during weekends and evenings facilitated learner participation. Heutagogy (self-determined learning) is credited as crucial for students balancing workload.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online delivery platform (e.g., Moodle) plus a video conferencing platform for synchronous sessions
#### Constraints
- The article grounds this in a pilot cohort of senior students who still had clinical duties; broader applicability requires replication at other institutions

### Target Learners
- Clinical-phase medical students concurrently completing usual clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained participation and completion of an online supplementary skills module
