# Develop comprehensive, standardized e-learning programs for nurses in hospitals and clinics, with standardized content and assessment tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's study implications call for coordinated development of e-learning for pressure injury management before universal implementation. It states that "standardization of content is important before proceeding with the universal implementation of e-learning programs" and that the assessment tool for nurse knowledge also needs standardizing, pointing to a recently validated knowledge scale for future trials. The strategy targets hospital and clinic nursing staff education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must be finalized and standardized through coordinated effort before universal implementation
- The tool used to assess nurses' pressure injury knowledge must also be standardized
#### Constraints
- Current evidence base is limited: most included studies had high risk of bias and substantial heterogeneity, and no well-designed large-scale RCTs of short- and long-term impacts exist

### Target Learners
- nurses in hospitals and clinics caring for pressure injury patients
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of pressure injury prevention and management
- pressure injury classification skills
