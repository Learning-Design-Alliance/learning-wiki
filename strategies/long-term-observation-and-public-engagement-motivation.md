# Long-term continuous observations and public engagement activities as motivators for developing research skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From their pedagogical experience, the authors report that children and young people benefit from recognizing "the importance of long-term efforts through continuous observations, which stand as basis to develop their own research skills working with data". They also report that students get motivated mainly by visits to astronomical observatories, star parties, STEM fairs and conferences, and that letting scientists engage with the public and share personal stories has shown a unique driving force.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained institutional support over years, since the authors stress none of this was possible without engaging administrative and educational heads of schools and universities
#### Constraints
- These are practice-based lessons from the Colombian context, offered without an educational theoretical position or evaluation data

### Target Learners
- school and university students
- the general public

### Target Learning Goals
- research skills with data
- motivation toward scientific careers
