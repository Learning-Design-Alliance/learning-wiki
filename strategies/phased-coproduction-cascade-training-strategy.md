# Phased co-production and cascade training model for developing and implementing new nursing education content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The programme was built through five sequential phases: co-production of training materials by Tanzanian and European lecturers with clinical staff; training of twelve master trainers by experts in Groningen; a baseline survey; delivery of student training; and post-test evaluation. Master trainers then trained lecturers and clinical instructors as trainer of trainees across the four institutions. The collaborative process built capacity in the universities and health system, supporting further provision of respectful and compassionate care in Tanzanian health services.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-institutional partnership between universities and training hospitals
- Funding and coordination for co-production and trainer cascading
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of an Erasmus+ funded collaborative project in Tanzanian nursing education; the customer care module developed in Phase I is not reported on here

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students
- University lecturers and clinical instructors as trainers

### Target Learning Goals
- Health literacy
- Respectful and compassionate care
- Teaching capacity of lecturers and clinical instructors
