# Universities should provide vivid guidelines and education on how and where ChatGPT may be used for learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that institutions should clarify regulations and teach students how to use ChatGPT productively. The study found students "are skeptical of its positive impacts on learning and think that universities should provide more vivid guidelines and better education on how and where the tool can be used for learning activities". The authors argue unclear policies may deter students from using the tool, and that finding the optimal way of using it and setting regulations is the job of universities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Universities must clarify regulations and rules so students know which activities the tool is allowed for
#### Constraints
- The authors note the use of ChatGPT in education is a very subtle area because it can easily be used for academic misconduct

### Target Learners
- MSc computer science students in higher education
- international non-native English speaker students

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective and responsible use of AI tools in academic learning
- Awareness of ChatGPT capabilities and limitations
