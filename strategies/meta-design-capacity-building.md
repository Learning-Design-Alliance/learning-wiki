# Orchestrate meta-design and capacity building so practitioners can engage in design in-use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This fifth IMPROV action area builds capacity for end-user design. The article explains that "Meta-design means designing for design," providing users with "opportunities, tools, and social structures to extend the system to ﬁt their needs" rather than closed systems. It includes historicity support giving users access to earlier design decisions, exploration support for trying technology configurations, and capacity building that ensures users understand the theoretical work undergirding design decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Capacity building must go beyond knowledge of technological features to include understanding the theoretical work undergirding design decisions, activating user expertise, and empowering users to change the system
#### Constraints
- Support must be designed for infrastructuring after a project's official lifespan or funding period, recognizing the spatial and temporal scale of activities

### Target Learners
- educators
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- empowering practitioners to creatively design or redesign systems in their local practice
