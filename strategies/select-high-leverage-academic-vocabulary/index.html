Select high-leverage academic vocabulary for direct instruction because the number of words that can be taught is limited¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because a rich, extended approach can teach only a relatively small number of words, the article recommends that "Schools and teachers need to select high-leverage academic vocabulary to teach directly that provides the biggest bang for the buck." Word selection is described as key given the significant instructional investment required for direct vocabulary instruction. The article notes there is no validated vocabulary curriculum but points to guidance and academic vocabulary lists.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Careful selection of high-utility academic vocabulary that occurs across content areas

Constraints¶

  • No validated curriculum of vocabulary that all students should learn exists, per the article

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten students at risk for language and learning difficulties
