# Individualized SMART learning plans and skill acquisition plans for clinical ultrasound training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that students work with a personal learning plan and a skill acquisition plan, developed jointly with the tutor after identifying learning needs. Any learning plan must be SMART: "Specific goal / outcome, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Timed interval". The skill acquisition plan breaks tasks into logical steps, provides a task development timeline and goals, allocated scan time, image portfolio and supervisor comments, enabling progression from simple to complex skills with sign-off as each core skill is completed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated tutors who identify learning needs, list learning goals, and jointly develop the plan with the student
#### Constraints
- Time taken to become competent is highly dependent on how readily the student can complete their learning plan

### Target Learners
- medical students at beginner, intermediate or advanced levels

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical scanning competence, e.g. scanning and measuring the aorta
