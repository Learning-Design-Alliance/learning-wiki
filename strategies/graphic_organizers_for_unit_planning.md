# Graphic Organizers for Unit Planning

## Description
Present students with a graphic organizer to show how the parts of a unit fit together, providing a sense of where they are in the unit and why they are learning the individual parts.  This exercise helps instructors decide what concepts are most important.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates and unit content.
