# Drill every targeted teaching technique in training and have GTAs cold-call groups rather than individuals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two design recommendations drawn from the study's findings. First, because nondrilled techniques were not used with any appreciable frequency, the authors state: "we recommend incorporating drills for all targeted teaching practices into the training program." Second, given documented instructor resistance to cold calling and the finding that group cold call produced more unique responders yet was used less, the authors suggest "encouraging GTAs to cold-call groups rather than individuals may have led to more persistent implementation of this technique."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training time to drill each target technique; a task standard and observation-based feedback cycle to monitor persistence of technique use
#### Constraints
- The group cold-call recommendation is a suggested change the authors propose to ameliorate declining adoption, not a manipulation tested in this study

### Target Learners
- Graduate teaching assistants in introductory STEM courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained adoption of participation-enforcement and active-learning techniques
