# Use virtual simulation as a structured, repeatable, risk-free complement to traditional bracket placement training in early skill development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining virtual simulation with traditional training rather than replacing it: "The potential benefit of virtual simulation is not in replacing traditional training, but in providing a structured, repeatable, and risk-free environment that could potentially aid in the early development of skills." The authors also state that a combination of virtual simulation with traditional methods may be viewed as a balanced approach, and conclude that adoption of the technology is a great addition to modern dental educational curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to virtual simulation software providing multi-angle, rotatable digital simulation of bracket positioning
- Standardized didactic instruction on principles such as FA point location alongside the simulation practice
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a small, underpowered pilot RCT; the authors state results must be treated with caution and that more research with larger sample sizes is necessary
- Skill transfer to actual clinical environments and long-term retention were not assessed

### Target Learners
- dental interns in early orthodontic skill training
- undergraduate dental students in preclinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate orthodontic bracket placement
- learner confidence and engagement in preclinical skills training
