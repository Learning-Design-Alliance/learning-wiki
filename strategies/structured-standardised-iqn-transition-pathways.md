# Develop structured, standardised transition pathways for IQNs including individualised plans, mentorship, CPD and competency-based recognition of prior expertise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured and standardised pathways so IQNs' diverse skills are recognised and utilised, noting "future work should prioritise the development of structured and standardised pathways for IQNs". Recommended components include hospital-based transition programs, mentorship and residency models, competency frameworks validating prior expertise, and collaboration among organisations, regulators and government agencies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordinated efforts from healthcare organisations, nurse managers, regulators and government agencies to provide targeted support during transition
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a self-selected online sample of Australian recruiting managers and may not transfer beyond the Australian context

### Target Learners
- internationally qualified nurses transitioning to Australian healthcare workplaces

### Target Learning Goals
- effective transfer and utilisation of specialty nursing skills in a new healthcare system
