# Prioritize low-fidelity simulations with trained simulated participants over high-tech manikins for clinical reasoning training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program intentionally prioritized low-fidelity simulations with trained simulated participants over high-tech manikins, consistent with the SimZone framework (Zone 2) and simulation standards. This approach focuses on decision-making and reasoning during debriefings while maintaining scalability. Scenario design involved collaboration between at least one simulation methodologist and one subject-matter expert.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained simulated participants who can provide feedback on scenario fidelity
- Collaboration between a simulation methodologist and a subject-matter expert for scenario design
#### Constraints
- A minimum of 12 rounds per case were conducted to refine scenarios

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physiotherapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical decision-making and reasoning during debriefings
