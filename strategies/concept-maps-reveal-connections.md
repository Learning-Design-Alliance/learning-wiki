# Use concept maps to help students articulate how facts connect and to reveal and track their developing understanding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends concept maps as a way to help students see how facts connect rather than memorize definitions. An instructor asks students to "diagram, explain, or label relationships between these terms using a concept map," typically for molecular biology terms students struggle to relate. The maps "will reveal what students are understanding, or not," give students feedback on their thinking, and let instructors track how knowledge changes as students develop expertise.
