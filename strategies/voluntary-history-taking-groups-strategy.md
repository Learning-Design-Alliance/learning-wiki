# Voluntary peer-tutored history-taking groups with structured feedback as communication-skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes an optional format in which small groups of students meet weekly with student tutors to practise taking medical histories from inpatients and receive structured feedback on communication. Students "can voluntarily take part in history-taking groups where usually around six to eight students meet weekly with one or two student tutors to take medical histories of inpatients and receive structured feedback on communication skills as well as student-patient interaction." Participation was associated with better self-rated conversational structure in the OSCE trend study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to inpatients for history-taking, one or two student tutors per group, and a structured feedback format
#### Constraints
- Participation is voluntary, so self-selection may contribute to the observed structure advantage

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- history-taking and doctor-patient communication skills
