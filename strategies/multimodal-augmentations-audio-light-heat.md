# Augment 3D tactile prints with audio, colored light, and heat cues for features such as stars and galaxy centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Across structured and informal groups, volunteers repeatedly proposed augmenting the tactile prints with additional sensory channels: "make areas of the prints produce audio explanations, illuminate different textures with different colored lights, make textures also have some kind of heat depending on the features, for example stars and the centers of galaxies would be warm". The article records this wish list from students and from technically experienced VRATE attendees as input for future print production and programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 3D print production capability and resources; the article notes some proposed augmentations would be expensive
#### Constraints
- These augmentations were suggested by volunteers, not implemented or tested in this study

### Target Learners
- individuals with blindness/visual impairment
- students in STEM outreach programs

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying bright/energetic features such as stars and galaxy centers
- engaging with astronomical concepts through multiple senses
