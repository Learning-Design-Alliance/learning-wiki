# Build teacher AI readiness through structured institutional support: professional development, mentoring, technical assistance, and AI literacy in teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions strengthen teacher confidence through structured, ongoing support rather than treating resistance as individual reluctance. It recommends "targeted professional development programs emphasizing self-efficacy, adaptability, andpedagogicalskills," plus continuous access to resources, technical assistance, structured mentoring, and incorporating AI literacy in teacher education programs. Policymakers and school leaders are advised to recognize that resistance to AI often reflects systemic support gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must provide continuous access to resources, technical assistance, and structured mentoring, and incorporate AI literacy into teacher education programs
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a survey of 260 teachers in Pampanga, Philippines, where support for internet reliability and infrastructure was reported as only moderate

### Target Learners
- elementary, secondary, and college teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher readiness and confidence for AI integration
