# Exchange personal and biographical information between speaker and students before the telelecture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The author recommends building personal contact into telelectures: "The students expressed considerable interest in knowing more about the speaker as a person.A picture plus some biographical information might have helped in this regard." He also suggests self-identification by each student before speaking, backed by biographical data about students sent ahead for the telelecturer's use, to help the lecturer relate more closely to the class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advance correspondence with the telelecturer, including biographical information and any special problems the class would like discussed.
#### Constraints
- Several speakers missed eye-to-eye contact with students, though the article reports students did not miss it.

### Target Learners
- graduate students in library science

### Target Learning Goals
- engaging with field experts and current information retrieval work
