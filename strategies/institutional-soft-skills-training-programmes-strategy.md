# Health establishments should develop needs-based soft skills training programmes, incorporate them into staff appraisals, and exploit e-learning platforms for flexible access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health establishments develop skills training programmes matched to professionals' needs and incorporate this training into regular staff appraisals. To maintain relevance over time, it advises promoting "a culture of continuous improvement by suggesting regular revisions and updates to training programmes based on feedback and new research findings". It also recommends exploiting "the potential of e-learning platforms and mobile applications for flexible and accessible training" for continuous soft skills development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programmes must meet the needs of professionals and be revised regularly based on feedback and new research findings to remain effective over time.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from the authors' discussion of this single-region public-sector survey rather than from a tested intervention in the article.

### Target Learners
- Practising nurses and healthcare technicians in health establishments

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuous development of soft skills, enhanced motivation, and improved working conditions
