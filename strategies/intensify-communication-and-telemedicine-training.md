# Intensify communication-skills training, including telephone- or internet-based physician-patient interaction, and integrate telemedicine via dedicated modules

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend preparing graduates for growing telemedicine and e-health use by strengthening communication training and adding telemedicine content to undergraduate curricula. They write that "training for communication skills (e.g., via telephone) should be intensified" and that integrating the field "could take place by means of telemedicine modules", pointing to an existing e-health learning-goals catalogue.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated learning goals catalog for e-health and telemedicine is available to consult for future developments
#### Constraints
- Older patients in Germany see telemedicine methods more critically and miss personal contact with and direct feedback from their physician

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- communication skills for telephone- and internet-based physician-patient interaction
- use of telemedicine and e-health applications
