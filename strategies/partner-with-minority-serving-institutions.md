# Partner with minority-serving institutions as equal partners to learn expertise in creating equitable outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends collaborations with minority-serving institutions (HBCUs, HSIs, TCUs) as an avenue of transformation for STEM departments. It notes that "Institutions founded specifically to serve marginalized populations have extensive expertise in creating more equitable student outcomes." Engaging with instructors from these institutions as equal partners and compensating them is important.
