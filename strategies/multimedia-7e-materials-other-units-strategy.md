# Apply multimedia-supported 7E learning model materials to other science units and to courses with abstract content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that multimedia-supported 7E materials raised achievement and retention, the authors recommend extending this approach beyond the studied unit. They suggest the materials "can be applied to other units of the Science course" and to "other courses where the course is particularly abstract", and that enriched multimedia applications be designed for each 7E stage within the science teaching curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multimedia-supported teaching materials prepared according to the 7E learning model stages
#### Constraints
- Recommendation extrapolates from a single fifth-grade science unit study; the article tested only the Matter's Change Unit

### Target Learners
- fifth-grade secondary school students
- students in courses with abstract content

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- retention of learned information
