# Use LLM-based feedback on resident reports so trainees can identify weaknesses, track growth, and reduce mentor workload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing LLM-based evaluation of resident reports as a complement to mentor feedback. Residents would "receive feedback from radiologists but also identify and improve their weaknesses with the help of LLMs", compare themselves with peers objectively, and track growth over time, while busy radiologist mentors are relieved of evaluating thousands of reports annually. Institutions can adapt the six-criterion framework by adding customized criteria for more comprehensive feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an LLM with high agreement with expert evaluators (GPT-4o in this study) and a tuned prompt with explicit evaluation criteria
#### Constraints
- Reliability is unclear for criteria requiring nuanced interpretation (C3–C5); the framework was validated only in one department's reports and only six criteria were evaluated

### Target Learners
- radiology residents
- radiology trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- radiology reporting skills
- self-assessment and monitoring of skill development
