# Operationalise workplace-based learning within national obstetric emergency training programmes, embedding learning visibly into real clinical settings alongside simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's top policy recommendation in table 4 is to "Explore approaches to operationalising WPBL within national OE training programmes by formalising educational strategies, creating content to explicitly 'train the trainer' and embedding learning visibly into real clinical settings alongside simulation." Its stated rationale is that WPBL may support context-sensitive knowledge-building and team readiness but remains poorly defined and largely assumed to occur without explicit direction in UK policy. The recommendation is presented as an area warranting further consideration, not a proven intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- WPBL must be formalised with explicit educational strategies and train-the-trainer content; structured implementation is needed to enhance its reach and impact
#### Constraints
- The review undertook no formal quality appraisal, so recommendations reflect the breadth and consistency of available evidence rather than its strength or certainty; implementation should be tailored to local organisational and healthcare contexts

### Target Learners
- Obstetrics and gynaecology doctors across all grades in UK maternity services

### Target Learning Goals
- Building and applying knowledge for managing obstetric emergencies within everyday clinical work
