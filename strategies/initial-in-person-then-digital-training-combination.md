# Combine initial in-person training with subsequent digital at-home training to satisfy trainee preferences while retaining digital flexibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion recommends that "a combination of initial in-person and subsequent digital training might be a suitable adjustment", noting that students in the digital group would have liked to be trained in-person. This hybrid sequencing aims to satisfy both modalities' preferences and cites prior evidence that trainees prefer online training after initial in-person training. It retains the flexible, accessible at-home training shown feasible in this trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured supervision with tutor feedback is retained during at-home sessions
#### Constraints
- The best implementation of un- and supervised digital training still needs to be evaluated in future studies; work-life overlap from at-home training is a burnout risk the article says needs careful evaluation

### Target Learners
- surgical novices and residents

### Target Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic surgical skill acquisition and continued skill development
