# Assign facilitators to fixed teams with periodic check-ins rather than rotating between groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During model building, one facilitator was assigned to two or three groups and checked in every 10 to 15 minutes. The authors decided not to rotate facilitators between groups "because of the added time that would be needed for the learners to repeatedly explain their model design", instead holding a mid-activity checkpoint where facilitators exchanged experience and advice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mid-activity checkpoint where all facilitators exchange experience and advice
- Facilitators monitor progress toward the content goal and challenge ideas as needed
#### Constraints
- Fixed assignment means facilitators do not share observations across teams except at the checkpoint

### Target Learners
- community college students working in design teams

### Target Learning Goals
- building a model of the CCD readout process
