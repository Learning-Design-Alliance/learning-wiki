# Develop entrepreneurship education programs that target students' personality traits to enhance both entrepreneurial intentions and academic performance

## Description
Based on the finding that personality traits mediate the relationship between entrepreneurial intentions and academic performance, the article recommends that "Educational institutions should develop entrepreneurship education programs targeting students' personality traits to enhance their entrepreneurial intentions and academic performance". It further suggests policymakers support implementing such programs to foster the entrepreneurial ecosystem, and that entrepreneurs use knowledge of these traits to select and develop team members. The discussion also highlights focusing on perceived behavioral control, educational support, and subjective norm to cultivate entrepreneurial intention.
