# Switch social planes adaptively based on student characteristics such as repeated errors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that beyond time-based switch points, "it may also be beneficial for students to switch social planes adaptively based on switch points triggered by student characteristics, such as repeated errors on a skill when working individually." This strategy would allow the tutor to move a student to collaborative work when individual performance signals difficulty, rather than switching only on a fixed schedule.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system capable of detecting student performance indicators such as repeated errors on a skill
#### Constraints
- This strategy is proposed as future work and has not been tested in this study

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural and conceptual fractions knowledge
