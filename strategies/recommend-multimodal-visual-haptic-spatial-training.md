# Recommendation that educators include implicit spatial training combining multimodal visual and physical resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators seeking to enhance three-dimensional spatial understanding in their learners should include implicit spatial training activities in their teaching that combine multimodal visual and physical resources, and explicit visual and haptic spatial training such as the Haptico-visual observation and drawing method and Haptic surface painting. This follows findings that a combination of visual and haptic modalities in practical and remote learning environments is likely to provide greater benefits to anatomy learners beyond visual-only methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to practical and remote learning environments supporting both visual and haptic modalities
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in prior work showing haptic observation skills correlate with visual spatial ability and that haptic perception can supplement visual skills in individuals with low spatial ability

### Target Learners
- Anatomy learners
- Medical and healthcare students

### Target Learning Goals
- Three-dimensional spatial understanding
- Spatial awareness and spatial ability development
