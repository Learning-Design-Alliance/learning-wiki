# Cursive Handwriting Activities

## Description
Activities designed to teach and practice cursive handwriting, which can be beneficial for students who struggle with pencil control and letter formation. These activities focus on smooth letter connections, loops, and retracing to improve fluency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cursive handwriting guides, lined paper, smooth writing tools, and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all students, especially those with significant fine motor difficulties. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

- Can improve fluency and legibility by promoting smooth letter connections. Engages different motor skills than printed handwriting.
