# Use a seven-point checklist to broaden the discussion section back to wide context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide lists key aspects to include in the discussion, 'broadening up the 'hourglass' as you go down the list': repeat why the study was carried out, return to and answer the main question posed in the abstract, explain how the answer is supported by data and results, relate results to other work including disagreements, give a view on disagreement or alternative explanations, describe wider implications and recommendations, and add what future work could clarify. The discussion also describes the limitations of the results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use sub-sections with well-chosen titles, strategic paragraphs, and topic sentences
- Summarise the main point in final sentences or paragraphs of each sub-section
#### Constraints
- Discuss alternative explanations without distracting the reader from the main message

### Target Learners
- beginning writers of refereed papers in astronomy

### Target Learning Goals
- write a critical discussion that contextualises and limits the results
