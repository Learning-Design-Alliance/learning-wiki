# Use dialogic lesson formats when teaching value-loaded critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's comparison of lesson series supports designing lessons for value-loaded critical thinking in dialogic form rather than non-dialogic form. The dialogic lesson series produced more positive effects on both generative fluency of reasoning and quality of value orientation, making dialogic organization the implementable choice the study's results favor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lesson series addressing value-loaded critical thinking, organized so that a dialogic format can be contrasted with a non-dialogic one.
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from pre-vocational secondary education in Biology; the available text prints no effect sizes.

### Target Learners
- Pre-vocational secondary education students

### Target Learning Goals
- Generative fluency of reasoning
- Quality of value orientation
