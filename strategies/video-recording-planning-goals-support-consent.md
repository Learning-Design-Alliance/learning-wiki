# Set clear recording goals, budget for equipment, and plan technical support and consent before classroom video recording

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's recommendations section advises teams planning classroom video to decide purpose and audience first: "before video recording, we strongly encourage you to set clear goals for what you hope to capture on video and its intended audience." It recommends budgeting roughly $800-1100 per camera setup, using tablets with external wireless microphones to capture student voices, arranging IT or site-coordinator technical support with quick guides and setup videos, and clearly communicating video use to teachers and parents to build trust and protect student privacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technical support for setup and troubleshooting, quick guides and short online videos, and a clear consent and privacy communication process for teachers and parents
#### Constraints
- Equipment that is difficult to set up and use can limit teacher participation; some teachers find video recording intrusive or uncomfortable

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers and school administrators participating in video-based observation

### Target Learning Goals
- capture usable classroom video for professional learning, evaluation, and research
