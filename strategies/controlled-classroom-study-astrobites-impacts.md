# Partner with instructors to run controlled classroom studies of the methods' impacts on literature facility, perceptions, and reading habits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend future evaluation work: "we hope to work with instructors to study the impacts of using Astrobites in their classrooms." They prefer measures that "would be more straightforward" than decades-long career-outcome tracking — facility with primary sources, student perceptions of consulting the literature, and literature reading habits — performed "across a range of student levels, from introductory undergraduate- through graduate-level" and across a range of institutional settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership with instructors using the methods in their classrooms
- Outcome measures covering facility with primary sources, perceptions, and reading habits
#### Constraints
- A longitudinal study of career outcomes "would take decades to perform and be very diﬃcult to control"

### Target Learners
- students from introductory undergraduate through graduate level across a range of institutional settings

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension and critical analysis of papers from the modern literature
- comfort consulting the literature for information
- literature reading habits
