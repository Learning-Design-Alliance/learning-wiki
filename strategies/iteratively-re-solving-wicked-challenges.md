# Iteratively re-solving wicked challenges through scenario development and collaborative deliberation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends handling wicked challenges not with one-shot solutions but through iterative, collaborative re-solving. It writes that 'Rittel introduces the notion of solving again and again, of re-solving wicked challenges, as being the iterative and collaborative attempt to inch ever closer towards addressing the complexity intrinsic to these challenges.' The strategy uses sophisticated scenario development and engagement in simulated and real stories of living in utopia, within environments like the Holodeck, to inch toward addressing inherently complex, multi-generational challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-generational engagement, dedication, and persistent lifelong work coupled with deep play
#### Constraints
- Wicked challenges' inherent interconnectedness means much work is still needed to solidify, scale, penetrate, and adopt evolving re-solutions

### Target Learners

- teams and communities

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative decision-making
- understanding multiple perspectives
- societal problem solving
