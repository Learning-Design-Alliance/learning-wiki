# Future research should examine factors supporting long-term student success beyond measured impacts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report closes by looking forward: it "discusses study limitations and identifies opportunities for future research to better understand the factors that support long-term student success." As a strategy, this directs evaluators of charter and choice schools to pair impact estimates with research on the mechanisms behind long-term outcomes such as credential completion and workforce participation, where this study found no detectable effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Impact evaluations should be complemented by research into the factors supporting long-term student success, as the report identifies opportunities for future research
#### Constraints
- The report's postsecondary findings are bounded by its study period, during which no detectable credential-completion effects were observed

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in KIPP Atlanta middle and high schools
- KIPP alumni in postsecondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement on statewide assessments
- college enrollment and persistence
- credential completion and workforce participation
