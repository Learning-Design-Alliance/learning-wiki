# Integrate decision-making practice throughout course problems rather than as a supplemental piece

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend, based on their null supplemental result and other studies, integrating decision-making practice throughout all course elements. Modest changes include modifying course problems to "have a written response portion", adding problems requiring "a solution plan instead of detailed calculations", and lectures including "intentional explanations of certain decisions". Integration may also address student motivation because practice becomes crucial to course success.
