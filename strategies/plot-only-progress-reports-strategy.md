# Close student research workshops with plot-only progress reports to maximize research time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the end of each Accel Kitchen workshop, each group reported its results to the researchers and discussed them. The article states that "To allow maximum research time, participants were forbidden from preparing any slides for presentation and were only allowed to use plots for describing their results." This format substitutes graphical data presentation for slide preparation, keeping participant time on the experiment itself while still requiring groups to communicate their findings.
