# Design didactic tools for teacher training that explicitly take teachers' misconceptions and learning obstacles into account

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that teacher training address prospective elementary teachers' alternative conceptions in basic astronomy, since elementary school programmes include these topics but appropriate training is generally lacking. They state that "an appropriate design of didactic tools that take into account teac hers’ misconceptions and other learning obstacles is currently under way", building on their questionnaire evidence of robust non-scientific models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diagnostic instruments (like their questionnaire) to put frequently used alternative models in evidence before designing instruction
#### Constraints
- The article reports this design work as currently under way, not yet evaluated

### Target Learners
- preservice elementary teachers in normal schools

### Target Learning Goals
- correct scientific understanding of basic astronomical phenomena taught in elementary school
