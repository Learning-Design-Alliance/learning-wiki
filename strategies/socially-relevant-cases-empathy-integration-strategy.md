# Integrate humanistic care into professional teaching by having students investigate socially relevant injury-prevention cases and adopt patients' perspectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A strategy the authors recommend for embedding empathy education within knowledge teaching: select cases closely related to daily life and societal issues (e.g., helmet use when riding electric bicycles, fireworks-related eye injuries), guide students to collect materials and take part in classroom discussions, so they "naturally adopted the perspectives of patients, family members and the public". The authors state this shows professional knowledge transmission and humanistic literacy cultivation can advance simultaneously via careful instructional design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases with clear social relevance and connection to students' daily behaviors
- Structured opportunities for material collection, discussion and reflection rather than passive lecture exposure
#### Constraints
- The authors caution the empathy gain may partly reflect the process of active inquiry and collaborative reflection rather than case content alone or the teaching model as a special mechanism

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students with limited clinical experience

### Target Learning Goals
- empathy
- humanistic care
- patient-centered communication
