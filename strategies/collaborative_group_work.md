# Collaborative Group Work

## Description
Teachers should emphasize collaborative learning within a project. It allows ELL students to be supported by their peers. It gives them opportunities to orally use and practice the academic vocabulary and other language structures and functions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom culture that values collaboration and well-designed collaborative tasks.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to manage groups effectively and ensure equal participation.
