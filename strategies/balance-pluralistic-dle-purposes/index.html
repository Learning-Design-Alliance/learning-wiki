Recognize pluralistic educational purposes in DLE and cultivate balance among them for holistic education¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's concluding recommendation is that data literacy education should recognize its pluralistic approaches and cultivate balance among qualification, socialization, and subjectification purposes to holistically educate students to exist in and with the data-rich world. It further encourages "informed engagement in them in local educational decision-making", urging fundamental discussions about educational emphases in DLE research. The recommendation follows directly from the finding that purposes span all three domains with synergies and cross-pressures.
