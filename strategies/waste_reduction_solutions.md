# Waste Reduction Solutions

## Description
These tasks involve examining and sorting waste items in a garbage can, categorizing and charting the items, and proposing solutions to reduce the amount of solid waste. They enhance awareness of waste management and health issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Waste items, sorting materials, charting tools
#### Constraints
- May require access to waste items. Safety precautions are necessary.
