Future peer-grading systems should evaluate formative feedback at scale using LLMs and extend the taxonomy to grade-synthesis algorithms¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review's future-work section recommends that "Future peer-grading systems should focus on evaluating formative feedback at scale, using techniques like LLM, which currently receives less attention." It also calls for exploring long-term effects of peer evaluation on student performance and development, and for extending the taxonomy to include review-distribution techniques and grade synthesis algorithms such as linear equations, probabilistic models, graph theory, and game-theoretic approaches. This addresses the identified gap that formative feedback evaluation mostly relies on manual approaches.

  • Requires LLM-based or other automated techniques for assessing feedback quality at scale

  • The review notes evaluating formative feedback remains challenging and most systems rely on manual approaches such as back-evaluation or instructor intervention

  • students in large and online courses receiving and giving formative peer feedback
