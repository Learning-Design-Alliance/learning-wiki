# Provide professional learning opportunities and free tools to support educators in conducting edtech pilot studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that organizations support educators' data collection for adoption decisions by continuing to "provide professional learning opportunities along with free tools and resources to support educators in conducting pilot studies." This capacity-building strategy responds to the survey's finding that educators value piloting but that skill, not will, is the limiting factor, and it is the strategy Digital Promise pursues through the Edtech Pilot Framework, pilot studies, and webinars.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations able to offer professional learning and free piloting tools and resources
#### Constraints
- Self-reported survey evidence; the report notes no clear coach or expert currently exists to support districts in acquiring analysis and interpretation skills

### Target Learners
- K-12 educators
- education leaders and district administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Skills to conduct, analyze, and interpret pilot evidence for edtech adoption and procurement decisions
