# Checklist for virtual training adoption in low-resource settings

## Description
The review proposes a ten-item checklist guiding institutions through adopting virtual surgical training, from assessing infrastructure and identifying training needs to selecting cost-effective tools, engaging stakeholders, developing curriculum, training faculty, implementing assessment with validated tools, monitoring outcomes, securing funding, and evaluating and scaling. The authors state it exists "To guide institutions in adopting virtual training". It operationalizes the review's policy recommendations into concrete institutional steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of local internet connectivity, electricity reliability, and hardware availability before adoption
- Stakeholder engagement with NGOs and academic institutions for funding and expertise
#### Constraints
- Designed for low-resource settings; the review notes technological, financial, infrastructural, and cultural barriers must be addressed for success

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees and institutions in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Cardiothoracic surgical technical skills and competency development
