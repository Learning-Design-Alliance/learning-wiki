# Teach students a broad set of strategies including sourcing, scrutinizing evidence, and corroboration to enable judging the accuracy of diverse online claims

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that when the educational aim is to enable students to judge the accuracy of diverse types of accurate and inaccurate online claims, a broader set of strategies is needed beyond identifying recurring forms of misleading communication. The article states that "a broader set of strategies is needed, including strategies such as sourcing, scrutinizing evidence, and corroboration." This strategy follows from the Apt-AIR analysis of competent epistemic performance for online claim evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must be designed for the specific epistemic aim; identifying misleading communication forms alone is insufficient for judging diverse claim accuracy
#### Constraints
- The appropriate strategy set depends on the specific epistemic aim; narrower aims such as recognizing misinformation patterns require fewer strategies

### Target Learners
- secondary and higher education students engaged in online inquiry and fact-checking
