# Conduct lessons using activity-based teaching and train teachers in activity-based methods to develop higher-order thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that lessons be conducted using activity-based teaching to develop higher-order thinking skills, and that institutions run teacher training programs on activity-based teaching. It frames ABL as "a setup where students actively participate in the learning experience rather than sit as passive listeners", built on active student roles and collaboration. It also recommends further studies across disciplines and levels to validate the results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in activity-based methodologies; the article recommends in-service teacher training programs to equip teachers with current techniques
