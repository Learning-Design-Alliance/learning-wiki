# State, district, and school leaders can support positive math classroom conditions through guidance, teacher education, funding, shared vision, and data-informed reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's policy and practice considerations direct state policymakers to issue curriculum and instructional guidance articulating the importance of positive classroom conditions for math learning, ensure teacher education programs instruct future teachers about these conditions, and allocate state funds for professional learning for in-service math teachers. District and school leaders are advised to establish a shared vision for excellent math instruction that includes positive classroom conditions as an essential feature and to support data-informed reflection on classroom learning conditions.
