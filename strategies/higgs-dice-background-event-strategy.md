# Use a dice roll in the advanced game so a candidate Higgs combination may be only a background event

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the advanced version, after a player finds a four-card combination matching a Higgs decay line of Table 1, "the player has also to throw a dice and if the result is a “6” than the cards really represent a valid Higgs boson, and the player wins the game". Any other result is a "background" event: the cards are kept but the player cannot claim winning and play passes on. This implements the idea that experimental signals must be distinguished from background.
