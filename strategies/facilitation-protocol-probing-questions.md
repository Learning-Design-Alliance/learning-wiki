# Facilitate sessions with a welcoming environment, probing questions, and structured pauses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The facilitation protocol assigns the facilitator the 'primary responsibility... to create a welcoming and positive environment that encourages open and thoughtful discussion', inviting 'a diversity of perspectives and even disagreement or debate'. Concrete tips include allowing '10–15 seconds for responses' after questions, using probing questions such as 'Could you say more about ____?', setting ground rules, and minimizing the facilitator's own input.
