# Top up intelligence interventions with process feedback when students receive grades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
The article recommends reinforcing an incremental theory by using process feedback, which attributes success to effort and techniques, at moments when students are evaluating their own performance. It suggests the intervention "could be 'topped up' when student performance is being evaluated by using process feedback", delivered verbally in small group teaching and in written feedback on essays, because combining intervention and feedback may produce a stronger longer-term impact.
