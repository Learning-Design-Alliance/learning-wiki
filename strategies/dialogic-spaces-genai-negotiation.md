# Create dialogic spaces where students and educators negotiate GenAI dilemmas together

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends institutional responses built on "the deliberate creation of dialogic spaces in which students and educators explore dilemmas together, articulate values, and rehearse ethical judgement." A seamful approach makes educational tensions and contradictions visible by design, inviting educators and students to learn together rather than resolving disagreements. Such spaces hold diverse perspectives "in productive tension, allowing ideas to clash, recombine, and evolve without necessarily resolving them."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cultures of openness, honesty, and vulnerability, coupled with educational guidance
- Accepting the discomfort of not knowing where conversations will lead
#### Constraints
- Massification, intensified workloads and limited time are constitutive conditions that shape what dialogue can be and where it can occur

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- educators

### Target Learning Goals
- ethical and context-sensitive judgement about GenAI use
- capacity to make responsible decisions beyond the immediate presence of educators
