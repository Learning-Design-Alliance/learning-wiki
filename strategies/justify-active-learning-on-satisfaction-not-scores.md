# Justify active learning on grounds of student satisfaction and wellness rather than shelf score gains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Since active learning does not appear to decrease shelf scores but has not consistently improved them, the review suggests increasing such opportunities could still be justified on the grounds of student happiness, satisfaction, or wellness. As the review states, "increasing such opportunities could still be justified on the grounds of student happiness, satisfaction, or wellness at large". Students overall enjoy learning more when changes engage them in their education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Engagement in education
