# Rice Tables and Sandboxes: Science Through Inquiry

## Description
Engaging learners in hands-on exploration and inquiry-based science education allows them to discover and test new understandings. Students follow the same processes real scientists use, asking questions, making discoveries, and testing those discoveries in the search for new understanding. It's less about understanding the basics of science, and more about creativity and innovation to design an exciting experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials and tools for scientific exploration. Instructor training and support.
#### Constraints
- May require significant resources and planning. Some learners may struggle with open-ended inquiry.
