## Description
The report frames normative data as a practical tool for educators making instructional decisions. It states that knowing how students performed or grew "compared to an appr opriate reference peer group also is important for individualizing instruction, setting achie vement goals for students or entire schools, under- standing achievement patterns and evaluating student perf ormance." The applications chapter elaborates uses of achievement norms, growth norms, and considerations such as setting reasonable expectations and the standard error of measurement.
