# Build in stop-and-reflect moments asking students what thinking they are doing versus what thinking the AI is doing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends, in both its agency and metacognition sections, to "Build in stop-and-reflect moments for students: What thinking are you doing? What thinking is the AI doing?" These pauses prompt students to notice whether AI is supporting understanding or helping them avoid thinking, and to consider when they should and should not use AI and what decisions they should have the freedom to make.
