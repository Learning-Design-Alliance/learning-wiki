Individualized Student Plan co-signed by student specifying interventions, responsibilities, and timelines¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Each coached student worked with the coach to develop an individual Student Plan for academic success containing "a series of questions that assisted the coach in outlining specific interventions for the student, determining who was responsible for each intervention, when the intervention was to take place and its duration." Students "demonstrated their commitment to their plan with their signature," and the plan conveyed intended outcomes so students, parents, school personnel, and project staff could collaborate on behalf of the student.

Claims¶

  • Over 50% of coached treatment students, all predicted to fail, progressed to the next grade level [+W]
  • A planned contrast showed coached and uncoached at-risk GPA trends diverged in different directions beginning at the onset of coaching [+W]
  • Coached at-risk 9th-grade students earned almost a full letter grade higher in core-course GPA than comparable uncoached baseline students [+W]
