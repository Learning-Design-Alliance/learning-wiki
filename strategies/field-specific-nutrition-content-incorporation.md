# Incorporate nutrition-related content into health professional programmes according to field-specific needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, until a dedicated legal framework for dietitians is established, nutrition-related content should be incorporated into health professionals' programmes in accordance with the needs of the student's field of study. The goal is to ensure acquisition of the knowledge, skills, and attitudes required to successfully manage patient nutrition. This addresses the current inconsistency where some programmes have no nutrition content at all.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alignment of nutrition content with the specific clinical needs of each professional field
#### Constraints
- Positioned as an interim measure until dietitian professional regulation is established

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate health professional students in Poland

### Target Learning Goals
- Patient nutrition management appropriate to professional field
