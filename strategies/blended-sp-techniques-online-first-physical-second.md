# Blended-learning sequencing: learn concrete conversation techniques online first, then deepen non-verbal and emotional aspects in physical SP units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking design recommendation arising from the digital teaching experience. Because concrete conversation techniques and the observation of conversation structures "can be trained well online", future students should first learn to recognize and apply concrete conversation techniques and structures in a technically oriented online class; subsequent physical SP units then focus on non-verbal and atmospheric aspects of conversation and the emotional experience of the student group, aiming at a learning experience that is as authentic as possible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online class oriented to concrete conversation techniques and structures
- Subsequent physical teaching units with SPs for non-verbal, atmospheric and emotional aspects
#### Constraints
- Motivated by the deficits of digital teaching in non-verbal communication and atmosphere; the improvement of communicative skills by the blended format is itself only planned for evaluation, not yet demonstrated

### Target Learners
- Medical students learning doctor–patient communication

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognizing and applying concrete conversation techniques
- Non-verbal communication and emotional/group aspects of medical conversations
