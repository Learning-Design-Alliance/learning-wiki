# Require use of available pathology teaching tools or real-time central pathology review to achieve complete compliance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future studies consider mandating use of available tools such as the web-based teaching set, or adopting real-time central pathology review, since voluntary use led to underutilisation. They write: "Moving forward, consideration for requiring use of available tools or real-time central pathology review may be warranted to achieve complete compliance." They also call for strategies for increasing awareness and use of similar tools in future studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Central review infrastructure or a web-based teaching tool must be available to participating pathologists
#### Constraints
- In RTOG 98-04 the tool's direct benefit could not be measured because use was underdocumented

### Target Learners
- pathologists participating in multicentre clinical trials

### Target Learning Goals
- reproducible application of protocol-defined histological grading criteria
