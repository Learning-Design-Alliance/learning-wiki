## Description
The article prescribes that mentors "simultaneously help students learn effective cognitive and meta-cognitive strategies to scaffold their knowledge and skill development". Working smart is defined as using effective approaches, so mentor support should pair belief in the mentee with concrete strategy instruction that builds on the mentee's current knowledge meaningfully.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentor must help students build on their current knowledge meaningfully
- Struggles should be framed as opportunities to develop expertise using deliberate strategies
#### Constraints
- The article notes students arrive with different motivational beliefs and prior preparations depending on background and privilege, so strategy support must be adequate guidance for all

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate physics mentees in instructional and research contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- using effective approaches for growth in physics knowledge and skills
- developing self-regulated problem solving in physics research
