# Integrate performance-based assessment tasks into senior high school mathematics lessons and train teachers to use PA-driven instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that mathematics teachers at the senior high school level make performance-based assessment tasks part of their lessons, and that teachers receive training programmes equipping them with the knowledge and skills to use PA-driven instruction. It also recommends choosing activities and problems that engage students' creative capabilities. These recommendations are grounded in the study's findings of higher achievement and more positive attitudes under PA-driven instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
