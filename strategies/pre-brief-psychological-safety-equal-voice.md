# Use a pre-brief that explicitly establishes psychological safety and equal voice before interprofessional peer feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends an effective pre-brief and brief in which clear, explicit instructions commit learners to psychological safety and equal participation. The authors' example verbiage states "during the peer feedback process we all have an equal voice, we are all here to be heard". They also emphasize giving all learners the same pre-course materials, voice, and expectations of participation to level the ground.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All learners access the same pre-course materials and are given the same voice and expectations of participation
#### Constraints
- Strategies for fostering psychological safety in the virtual learning environment were drawn from Cheng et al and applied in Zoom-connected rooms during pandemic social distancing

### Target Learners
- interprofessional simulation learners in surgical and emergency care

### Target Learning Goals
- fostering psychological safety
- effective interprofessional peer feedback
