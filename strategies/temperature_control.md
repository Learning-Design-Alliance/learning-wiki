# Temperature Control

## Description
Maintaining an optimal temperature range in classrooms affects student engagement and productivity. The ideal range for effective learning in reading and mathematics is between 68°F and 74°F. Teachers need the ability to control the temperature to ensure a comfortable learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Temperature control systems, thermostats, proper insulation.
#### Constraints
- Implementation can be costly and may require significant facility upgrades.
