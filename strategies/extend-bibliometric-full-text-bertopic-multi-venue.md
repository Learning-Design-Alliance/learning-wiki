# Extend bibliometric analysis with multiple venues, full texts, advanced topic models, and usage-based indicators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend concrete extensions of their analysis for future research: "Future studies could expand the scope of analysis by incorporating multiple journals, conference proceedings, and additional databases such as Web of Science or Dimensions", and "Researchers may also employ more advanced topic-modeling techniques, including BERTopic, dynamic topic modeling, and large language model-based approaches, to better capture thematic changes over time." They also advise complementing citation metrics with article download statistics and other usage-based indicators, and using full-text documents instead of abstracts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to full-text collections or additional databases (Web of Science, Dimensions) and computational tooling for advanced topic models
#### Constraints
- The present analysis was limited by restricted access to complete published articles, which is why abstracts were used

### Target Learners
- physics education researchers
- science-metrics researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehensively mapping thematic evolution and emerging research directions in physics education
