# In online flipped classrooms, set appropriate task difficulty, organize team grouping, build incentive mechanisms, and use multi-dimensional digital evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For online flipped teaching during COVID-19, the article recommends four practices: "teachers should set appropriate course task difficulties," reasonably divide students into groups before each task, establish an incentive mechanism rewarding best-performing groups each semester, and conduct objective multi-dimensional evaluation through digital means. It argues these measures mobilize participation, communication, and interaction that online education otherwise lacks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning platforms such as MOOCs or Tencent Classroom to integrate online courses into the flipped mode
- Digital evaluation tools for multi-dimensional assessment
#### Constraints
- The article notes online education lacks adequate supervision, communication, interaction, and cooperation compared with face-to-face instruction

### Target Learners
- college and university students in online or emergency remote settings

### Target Learning Goals
- behavioral participation
- group interaction
- learning quality in online flipped courses
