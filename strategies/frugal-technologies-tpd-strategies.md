# Incorporate frugal technologies into TPD strategies for online and blended learning from an international perspective

## Description
The authors recommend extending TPD strategies for online and blended learning beyond computer-based contexts by drawing on "the use of frugal technologies" — the remote-education mix of digital platforms, television, radio, take-home packages, home visits, text messaging and phone calls observed internationally during the pandemic. The article suggests elucidating "if new components should be aggregated for TPD strategies from an international perspective."
