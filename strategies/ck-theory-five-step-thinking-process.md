# Five-step C-K thinking process for creative design

## Description
The article recommends a five-step thinking process for applying C-K theory to creative design: 1) determining problems, requirements and goals of a creation, 2) searching for information and keywords to add to the Knowledge Space, 3) ideating relevant ideas in the Concept Space using mind maps, 4) checking acceptance and feasibility of new ideas against the Knowledge Space, and 5) summarizing and stopping when ideas are proved feasible and accepted by society.
