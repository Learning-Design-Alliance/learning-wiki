# Design VR sales simulations with supportive context, greater realism, minimized technical disruptions, and balanced cognitive load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 qualitative, 1 causal, 1 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the study's descriptive results and qualitative feedback, the authors distilled lessons learned for developing similar scenarios. These include that "Friendly environments and interactions appear to enhance user experience", that designers should make unfriendly scenarios realistic yet manageable, that greater realism in environmental and avatar interactions is needed, that system performance should be optimized to avoid lag and freezes, and that cognitive demand should be balanced to maintain engagement without excessive stress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must consider how to make unfriendly scenarios realistic yet manageable
- System performance must be optimized to ensure a seamless experience
#### Constraints
- Lessons derive from a single 20-participant usability study with no significant condition differences

### Target Learners
- designers of VR sales and negotiation training scenarios

### Target Learning Goals
- improving engagement and realism of immersive interpersonal-skills training
