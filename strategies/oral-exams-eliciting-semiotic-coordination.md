# Use oral exams with instructor hints to assess conceptual understanding, problem-solving and scientific communication while eliciting rich semiotic coordination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's context shows oral exams as assessment events that elicit visible coordination of semiotic resources: students are "required to complete two 20 to 30 minute individual oral exams with the instructor", used to assess "conceptual understanding, problem-solving and scientiﬁc communication skills". The instructor's hints, aligned with classroom practices, prompted Larry to add representations (e.g. "Draw your loop") at stuck points, letting the examiner observe how resources are built up and replaced.
