# Creating Strategy Charts

## Description
Developing visual aids and lists of problem-solving strategies in collaboration with learners. These charts serve as resources for students when they encounter challenges, helping them to identify and articulate their problem-solving processes. Language prompts are included to help students talk about their thinking and reflection, promoting both self-directed learning and a connection between struggles and strategic solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for collaboration, chart-making materials, and a classroom environment that values reflection and problem-solving.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing maintenance and updates. Students may need explicit instruction in how to use the charts effectively.
