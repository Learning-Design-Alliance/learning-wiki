# Promote learner responsibility and supervised independence to optimise the concrete experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy promotes learner responsibility during bedside teaching to enhance the quality of the concrete experience. It includes allowing supervised independence during history-taking and examination with tutors intervening only when necessary for patient safety, assigning defined roles such as leading the history-taking, performing the focused examination, observing communication and summarising clinical findings, and prompting autonomous clinical decision-making. This transforms learners from "passive observers into active participants".
