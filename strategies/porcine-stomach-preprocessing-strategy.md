# Pre-processing porcine stomachs to remove grease and gastric acid before simulation use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A five-step preparation recipe that makes the raw organ usable for trainees: cleaning grease with running water, smearing baking soda and soaking, repeating cleaning, rubbing with "salt, corn flour, and Chinese spirits (70% ethanol)", and rinsing with warm water. The authors state that after preparation "we can entirely remove the grease and gastric acid of porcine stomach", and gloves are recommended to protect trainees from acid and smell.
