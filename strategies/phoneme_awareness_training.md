# Phoneme Awareness Training

## Description
Direct instruction in phoneme awareness helps children in the early stages of learning to spell and helps remediate the problems of poor spellers at any age. A typical activity for developing this skill is direct teaching of all consonant and vowel sounds. Strengthen phonemic awareness by identifying, segmenting, and manipulating speech sounds in words. Examples include a 'sound workout' where learners use manipulatives to represent each sound.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials for phoneme manipulation (e.g., chips, blocks), word lists, direct instruction
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit and systematic instruction, may not be effective for learners with severe phonological deficits
