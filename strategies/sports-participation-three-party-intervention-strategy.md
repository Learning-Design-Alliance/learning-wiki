# Government–school–family coordination with autonomy-supportive, feedback-rich school sports plans to promote participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that government, schools, and families jointly intervene to raise sports participation: government fixes facilities and education structures, schools "make reasonable sports plans and breaks to properly mobilize students' focus and enthusiasm", and parents guide children's interests. Schools should support autonomy by offering choices of exercises or work and rest intervals, provide positive feedback and encouragement, conduct activities with friends, and use music and natural environments.

### Context
#### Requirements
