# Graphic Organizers for Note-Taking

## Description
Learners use graphic organizers to structure notes from readings, research, films, or podcasts. The instructor may select a specific graphic organizer to promote learning efficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates, materials for note-taking (paper, digital tools), and instructor guidance.
#### Constraints
- May over-simplify complex material if not carefully designed. Ineffective if students are not properly trained on their use.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly beneficial for visual learners and those who benefit from structured learning

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Organize information, identify relationships between concepts, improve comprehension and retention

### Affordances
- Visual structure aids comprehension and retention. Pre-selected organizers save time and effort. Note-taking during information consumption aids engagement.

### Personalization
- Teachers can customize organizers based on content and learner needs. Learners can use different organizers based on their preferred style.

- Research shows that graphic organizers incorporated into instruction improve student learning. Author-created graphic organizers can lead to deeper learning more quickly.
