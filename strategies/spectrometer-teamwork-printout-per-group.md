# Hand out one spectrometer printout per group of two to three students and master the measurement in teamwork

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends distributing one printout per small group because "the measurement procedure is mastered best in teamwork". Groups of two to three students cut out the triangle, fold the black box upright, cover the cut-out with translucent tape, and jointly shift the grating along the scale to align spectral bands with the white line. Group results are then compared to discuss accuracy-limiting factors.
