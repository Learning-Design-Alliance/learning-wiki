# Conduct clarification studies on how and why peer support works, framed by faculty development models and design-based research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's future-research recommendation is that clarification studies asking the 'how' and 'why' questions should be a key research focus, since few included studies systematically collected or rigorously analysed process evaluation data. Suggested investigations include alternative design characteristics for access (e.g. synchronous and asynchronous digital communications) and peer availability, the roles of social, cultural, professional and interprofessional networks, mechanisms to sustain programmes, and evidence of changed teaching practice and improved student learning outcomes.
