# Use fluorescent-marked solution and ultra-violet light to give direct visual feedback on hand hygiene technique

## Description
Within the technical skills training on hand hygiene guidelines, "Using a fluorescent-marked solution and ultra-violet light, participants were offered direct visual feedback on the quality of their hand hygiene technique and, thus, could improve on their performance." The authors observed this structured instruction was an essential part of the program even though all participants had undergone formal emergency medical training.
