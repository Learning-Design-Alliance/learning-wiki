# Use AI-based assessment periodically for formative feedback while avoiding AI in summative assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that "the use of AI in summative assessment should be avoided" while "the periodic use of AI-based assessment for formative feedback may be beneficial in supporting the development of students' knowledge and skills towards the attainment of the expected competencies." This strategy positions AI as augmenting rather than replacing human assessment, combining AI with traditional methods to enhance student evaluation while maintaining human oversight for high-stakes decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI integration must be carefully managed to avoid unintended outcomes, with careful oversight and input from educators to ensure quality, accuracy, validity and appropriateness
#### Constraints
- Dental educators expressed concerns regarding the effectiveness of assessments generated by AI for assessing student competency
- Concerns persist regarding accuracy in assessing practical skills, potential biases, and ethical implications

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Formative assessment and competency development
