# Provide more intensive language supports than conversation-based teacher PD for children with substantial language lags

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the conversation-based LLLI approach yielded no long-term vocabulary benefit and benefited only initially high-skilled children, the authors argue that "improving young children's language skills, speciﬁcally vocabulary, may require more intensive efforts than are currently being trialed." They suggest it may be time to consider fresh alternatives for children with the most intractable language problems, and point to avenues beyond the classroom such as elaborative reminiscing at home and frequent multi-turn caregiver-child conversations.
