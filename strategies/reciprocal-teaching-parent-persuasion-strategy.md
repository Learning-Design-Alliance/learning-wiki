# Reciprocal peer teaching with parent-facing explanation to sustain collaborative learning in exam-oriented contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
When parents of top performers objected that peer assistance wasted their children's time, the teacher presented research that "reciprocal teaching benefits both tutors and tutees equally", provided step-by-step training in peer-teaching strategies, had struggling students such as Baobei tutor peers after mastering corrections, and invited sceptical parents to try reciprocal teaching at home. Even the strongest doubters changed their minds after trying it with their children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Step-by-step student training in effective peer-teaching strategies during practice lessons and after class
- Direct explanation of the reciprocal-teaching rationale to parents, including home practice with their own children
#### Constraints
- Other teachers at the school struggled to implement reciprocal teaching; some abandoned peer assistance to avoid conflict with parents

### Target Learners
- elementary students across performance levels, with struggling students acting as tutors after mastering content

### Target Learning Goals
- peer tutoring and communication skills
- confidence and mastery of taught content
- parental trust in inclusive practices

- [Parental attitudes transformed from scepticism to high trust over the three cycles, with parents of low performers developing high confidence by Cycle 3](../claims/tprs-mtss-parental-trust-transformation.md) [+W]
