# Provide ongoing, targeted professional learning on ESL instructional strategies for middle grades content-area teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors encourage school administrators to "consider ongoing, targeted professional learning opportunities on appropriate ESL instructional strategies for their middle grades science (and other content-area) teachers." In MSSELL this took the form of bi-weekly, 90-minute, face-to-face after-school sessions over one year, taught by research coordinators specializing in science and second language development, covering upcoming lessons and the ESL strategies embedded in the scripted curriculum. The authors link this professional learning to the observed differences in teachers' ESL strategy implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions taught by coordinators specializing in science and second language development, covering upcoming lessons and ESL strategies used in the curriculum
#### Constraints
- The article notes many content teachers lack ESL preparation; as of 2014 more than 30 states did not require such professional development for classroom teachers

### Target Learners
- Middle grades English language learners and economically-challenged students

### Target Learning Goals
- Teachers' implementation of ESL strategies in content-area instruction to promote academic achievement and English language proficiency
