# Select training delivery method to match nurses' development needs: hybrid for patient care competency, onsite for service provision support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that "Community nurses should select training methods that best target their development needs: hybrid training for enhancing patient care competency and onsite training for improving competencies in service provision support." Hybrid training suits multifaceted care topics such as complex wounds or diabetes; onsite training best builds foundational knowledge and skills such as organisational empowerment and intimate partner violence support; online training suits focused skills such as venous leg ulcer nursing care. Nurse educators should consider nurses' basic knowledge when selecting methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurse educators need to consider the basic knowledge of community nurses to determine the most suitable training methods
#### Constraints
- Onsite training is more suitable for nurses who have adequate financial support or time to attend training without the constraints of workload

### Target Learners
- community nurses
- nursing policymakers
- nursing schools

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competency development
- service provision support
- patient care competency
