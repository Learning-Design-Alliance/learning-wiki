# Use institutional peer benchmarking with incremental benchmarks toward the 75th percentile of peer-group metrics over a 10-year period

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report proposes that accountability systems identify peer groups of institutions with similar characteristics — enrollment, shares of low-income undergraduates, part-time shares, selectivity, funding, and instructional spending — and "Set incremental benchmarks with a goal of achieving the 75th percentile of each peer group's metrics over a 10-year period." It grounds this in its CollegeResults.org research showing institutions enrolling similar students can have vastly different outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer groups developed using the six listed institutional characteristics so comparisons are between institutions with similar student populations
#### Constraints
- The report states outcomes should use multiple indicators rather than a single metric such as completion alone

### Target Learners
- students from low-income families
- students of color

### Target Learning Goals
- institutional improvement in access and completion outcomes
