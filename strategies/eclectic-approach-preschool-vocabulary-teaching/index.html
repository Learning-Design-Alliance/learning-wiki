Adopt an eclectic approach combining diverse vocabulary instruction types in early foreign language teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Because both computer assisted and traditional vocabulary instruction positively affected preschool children's vocabulary gain, the article recommends that teachers adopt an eclectic approach to teaching vocabulary with a combination of diverse instruction types. This follows from the finding that learning occurred regardless of instruction type and that the two methods did not differ significantly in effectiveness.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers should combine diverse instruction types rather than relying on a single method

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is limited to 35 five-year-old children in one kindergarten
