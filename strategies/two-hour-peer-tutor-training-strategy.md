# Train peer tutors in short structured sessions covering technical content, didactics, culturally sensitive behaviour and motivating feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the course, the two peer tutors received preparation designed to ensure both subject-matter and interpersonal competence: "Tutors were trained in two 2-h sessions. The training was led by a consultant of internal medicine and a psychologist, thus ensuring appropriate technical and didactic training." Training reviewed each topic, discussed questions, provided teaching materials such as a detailed model presentation, and added an introduction to culturally sensitive behaviour and the provision of structured and motivating feedback. Tutors were encouraged to be aware of participants' needs and free to select topics based on forthcoming exams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training led by both a medical expert and a psychologist
- Provision of teaching materials such as a detailed presentation
#### Constraints
- The article reports this training as part of one course; no separate evaluation of the training itself is presented

### Target Learners
- Senior international medical students acting as peer tutors

### Target Learning Goals
- Tutor competence in exam-relevant content delivery
- Culturally sensitive behaviour and structured, motivating feedback
