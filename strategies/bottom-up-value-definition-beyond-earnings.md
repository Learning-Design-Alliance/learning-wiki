# Define institutional value from the bottom up, incorporating learning outcomes, community engagement, and career readiness beyond earnings thresholds

## Description
The article recommends that higher education leaders on campuses and in state governments define what value means for their students and communities. It urges "pointing policymakers to concrete evidence of impact such as learning outcomes, community engagement, and career readiness that extends beyond job placement," while acknowledging student learning remains difficult to measure consistently across institutions.
