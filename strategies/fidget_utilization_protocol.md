# Fidget Utilization Protocol

## Description
Fidgets are tools to aid focus and self-regulation, not toys. This strategy involves setting rules for using fidgets at home or in the classroom. Rules include being mindful of when a fidget is needed, using it only to focus or calm down, not distracting others, and returning it to a designated place after use. Experimentation helps find the right fidget.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fidgets, clear rules, designated storage place, open communication, and a signed contract.
#### Constraints
- Requires commitment from both the learner and instructor to follow the rules. May not be effective if fidgets are misused as toys.
