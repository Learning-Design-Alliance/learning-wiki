# Use newsflashes—succinct email blasts announcing new research findings—to inform decision makers, stakeholders, and practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends the newsflash as a dissemination vehicle: "A successful newsflash—an email blast announcing new research findings—incorporates all of these elements." It is aimed at helping researchers "quickly and effectively inform decision makers, stakeholders, and practitioners through the use of newsflashes," replacing reliance on posting reports or sending press releases alone.
