# Discussions

## Description
Engage learners in text-based discussions where they discuss what they read with each other, and teachers set expectations and routines for productive discussions. Activating background knowledge, checking for understanding, and fostering student-to-student conversations are key components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Text for discussion, teacher facilitation skills, and established routines and expectations for productive discussions.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation to ensure discussions are productive and respectful. Facilitation skills are necessary.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially English language learners, can benefit from structured discussions.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop reading and writing skills, especially vocabulary development, and promote comprehension through oral language and interaction.

### Affordances
- Supports vocabulary development, encourages critical thinking, promotes active engagement with the text, and builds communication skills.

### Personalization
- Discussions can be tailored to the specific text, learners' backgrounds, and learning goals. Sentence starters can be provided for English language learners.
