# Prioritize sustained, pedagogically grounded faculty TPACK development over one-off technology workshops and infrastructure investment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the identified importance-performance gap in faculty competence, the article recommends that institutions invest in sustained, pedagogically grounded TPACK development programs for instructors rather than one-off technology workshops, and that human capital development precede or parallel hardware investment in institutional AI strategies. It also recommends systematic mechanisms for updating AI content (e.g., industry-academic curriculum labs) and intelligent instructional support systems integrating adaptive learning and real-time feedback. The article explicitly cautions these are "strategic guidelines for program optimization, not as evidence-based interventions proven to enhance learning outcomes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to sustained faculty development rather than one-off workshops
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are derived from expert-perceived priority rankings, not proven to enhance learning outcomes

### Target Learners

- higher education institutions

### Target Learning Goals
