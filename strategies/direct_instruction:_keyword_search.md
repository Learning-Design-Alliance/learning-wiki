# Direct Instruction: Keyword Search

## Description
Explicit instruction in keyword search strategies involves teaching learners how to effectively select and combine keywords to refine search queries and retrieve relevant information from the internet. This includes understanding the use of synonyms, precise descriptions, search operators (quotation marks, plus/minus signs), and Boolean logic. Learners also evaluate the credibility of online sources by checking domain extensions (.edu, .org, .gov, .com), identifying the author and their perspective, and verifying information across multiple sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access, search engines (e.g., Google, Bing), examples of reliable and unreliable websites, and instructional materials on keyword search techniques and source evaluation criteria
#### Constraints
- May require ongoing practice and reinforcement to maintain proficiency. Learners may struggle with identifying and evaluating biased or misleading information. Requires continuous updates to reflect changes in search engine algorithms and online information landscape.
