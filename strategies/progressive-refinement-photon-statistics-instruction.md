# Teach photon statistics through methods of increasing refinement folding light and detector characteristics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An instructional sequence in which "Methods fold the characteristics of the emitted light and the detector response, with an increasing level of reﬁnement": students estimate the mean number of fired cells model-independently, test the pure Poisson hypothesis by χ2, and fit a compound Poisson model with cross-talk. The model development "allows to address advanced topics in statistics and data analysis techniques, targeted to master students in Physics or Engineering."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A SiPM set-up with spectra recorded at different light intensities, and basic skills in probability and statistics.
#### Constraints
- Targeted to master students in Physics or Engineering.

### Target Learners
- master students in Physics or Engineering

### Target Learning Goals
- statistical properties of light emission
- data analysis techniques
- detector response modeling
