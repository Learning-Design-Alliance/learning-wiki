# District-developed coaching system tailored to individual teachers' curriculum implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Coaching is "an ongoing professional development model in which an expert (the coach) models instruction, observes teachers' practice, and provides teachers with constructive feedback on their pedagogy". In Boston, "coaching sessions were tailored to address the individual needs of each teacher in implementing the curricula and managing the classroom", delivered by masters-degree coaches with early childhood teaching experience, intended to improve and maintain quality at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches held masters degrees and had prior early childhood classroom teaching experience; support was weekly to biweekly, on-site, and from a coach trained in both curricula.
#### Constraints
- A district fidelity study reported the curricula were implemented with moderately high fidelity, not uniformly at full fidelity.

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten teachers in an urban public school district

### Target Learning Goals
- curriculum implementation fidelity
- classroom management
- instructional quality
