# Integrate formal VR education with hands-on clinical experiences in physical therapy curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing structured academic education in vestibular rehabilitation and combining it with practical clinical exposure. It concludes that "Integrating formal education with hands-on experiences in VR curricula could enhance knowledge and competence among PT interns." The discussion specifies that formal instruction builds the theoretical foundation while internship experience and workshops develop applied competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A comprehensive academic course integrating both assessment techniques and training maneuvers in VR, per the article's stated need
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a cross-sectional, convenience-sampled Saudi intern cohort, limiting generalisability per the authors' stated limitations

### Target Learners
- Physical therapy students and interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge and clinical competence in vestibular rehabilitation assessment tests and treatment maneuvers
