# Teachers guide CSCL use, provide satisfying collaborative environments, and mediate group conflicts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the micro level the article recommends that "Teachers should make efforts to guide students about the potential of CSCL and provide satisfying collaborative working environments from pedagogical, cognitive, and emotional perspectives". Teachers guide students in the use of collaborative communication tools that permit flowing, effective interaction within work groups and act as mediators in any internal conflicts that arise within groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher availability throughout planning, implementation, and evaluation phases of CSCL
#### Constraints
- Recommended for higher education contexts where teachers supervise online collaborative work

### Target Learners
- university students in online collaborative courses

### Target Learning Goals
- effective group interaction
- satisfaction with CSCL
