# Extend the game with a state-measurement operation to introduce simple quantum algorithms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the limitations and future improvements section, the authors propose a concrete extension: "An additional operation that could be introduced to the game is a state measurement operation which measures the state of one or more qudits, individually, at any point in the quantum circuit." This mechanic would add an extra layer of complexity and enable introducing simple quantum algorithms such as superdense coding, quantum teleportation, or entanglement swapping in the gameplay.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- New card type and updated rules plus software support for measuring individual qudits at arbitrary points in the circuit
#### Constraints
- Proposed as a future improvement; not part of the current version of the game

### Target Learners
- players progressing beyond the current riddle set
- quantum course students ready for simple algorithms

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding measurement as an in-circuit operation
- introducing superdense coding, quantum teleportation and entanglement swapping
