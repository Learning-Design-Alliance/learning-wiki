# Community Involvement

## Description
Encouraging community involvement by using online communities to support learning and fostering a sense of collaboration through social media. The physical space of the school should also allow for other adults to participate in classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to technology and social media. Designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the presence of additional adults.

### Target Learners

- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase collaboration, improve social skills, promote community

### Affordances
- Promotes collaboration and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred activities. Provide a variety of activities that involve the community. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more fun.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that community involvement can increase engagement, improve social skills, and enhance academic achievement. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel connected to their community.
