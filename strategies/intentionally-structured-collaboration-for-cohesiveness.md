# Structure collaborative tasks intentionally to build social connections, not just content mastery, if cohesiveness is a goal

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article found no cohesiveness advantage and reasons that "it is plausible that for cohesiveness to develop, collaborative tasks must be intentionally structured to promote social connections, not just content mastery." Its own flipped activities were short, task-focused exercises rather than long-term projects, and the four-week duration may have been insufficient for peer bonds to form.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaborative activities designed to promote interpersonal bonds, not only content mastery
#### Constraints
- Intervention duration of four sessions may be insufficient for strong peer relationships to form

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- sense of community and peer support in the classroom
