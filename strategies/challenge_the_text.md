# Challenge the Text

## Description
Challenge the Text is a strategy that encourages students to critically examine texts by asking and answering their own text-dependent questions. This process involves taking multiple perspectives and uncovering assumptions and biases within the text. By challenging what is said, students develop agency and enter their voices into the textual discourse. The strategy can be used during or after reading, and it helps students understand what is not included in the text, make inferences, and support their analysis with textual evidence. Students should be given prompts and scaffolding to generate questions. The strategy places an anti-bias lens on the literacy experience by raising issues related to power within social contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Central text (literary or informational), prompts and scaffolding for generating critical questions, time for students to generate and respond to questions.
#### Constraints
- Students may need significant support generating critical questions. May require careful facilitation to ensure a safe space for discussing sensitive topics.
