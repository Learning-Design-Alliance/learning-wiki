# Web-based Dictionary & Thesaurus

## Description
Incorporating web dictionaries and thesauruses into vocabulary lessons allows learners to quickly search for and understand unfamiliar words. When learners look up challenging words while they read, they monitor their comprehension and activate cognitive processes that support increased recall and consolidation to long-term memory. Features such as voice search, audio pronunciation, and embedded quizzes are also helpful for vocabulary building and practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to web-based dictionary and thesaurus tools (e.g., Merriam-Webster Dictionary, Dictionary.com), electronic devices, and reading materials.
#### Constraints
- Reliance on technology and internet access. May not be suitable for learners with limited digital literacy skills.
