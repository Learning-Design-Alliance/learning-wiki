# Sustain school mental health services by blending one-time grants with ongoing reimbursement streams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SCOE's sustainability strategy combines one-time initiative funds with recurring reimbursement options. The brief states SCOE leaders are "combining one-time funds from various initiatives," including Project Cal-Well, the Mental Health Student Services Act Grant, the Student Behavioral Health Incentive Program, and partnership and capacity-building grants, with reimbursement options such as those available through their FQHC, School Medical Administrative Activities (SMAA), and the multi-payer fee schedule. SCOE also plans to designate schools as satellite centers of the County Health Center to enable enhanced billing and more on-site billable services.
