# Deliver classroom-based physical activity with an academic focus to potentially support both academic performance and overall physical activity levels

## Description
Classroom-based physical activity is an approach of providing physical activity opportunities to students during the school day, distinct from physical education classes and break times. The review notes it "may involve the integration of PA into academic lessons or may include incorporating PA into the regular classroom routine without an academic focus". The review reports preliminary evidence that classroom-based PA, particularly with an academic focus, may positively impact academic performance and overall physical activity levels, while noting research in this area is still in its infancy with inconsistent findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be delivered during the school day, distinct from PE classes and break times
- An academic focus on the integrated activity is indicated by the preliminary evidence
#### Constraints
- Research evaluating the impact of classroom-based PA on learning is still in its infancy, with inconsistent findings from a small number of studies of varying methodological quality

### Target Learners
- school-aged children in primary school settings

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics performance
- reading performance
- physical activity levels
