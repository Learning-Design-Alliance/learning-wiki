# Use the validated VR TTE test as the final checkpoint of a mastery learning training program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends directing simulation-based training with the validated test so that trainees practise until reaching a pre-defined standard: "This test could direct a mastery learning training program where trainees practise until they reach the pre-defined level and secure a higher level of competency to ensure quality and safety for patients." Mastery learning with a final test ensures every trainee reaches the same competency level regardless of learning pace, with no time restraint so different paces can reach the same skill level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A good test with gathered validity evidence is a prerequisite for any mastery learning program; the pass/fail standard must be established before integration
#### Constraints
- The evidence on transfer of VR ultrasound skills to clinical performance is limited; a clinical-setting study on competence development is still needed

### Target Learners
- physicians in training for transthoracic echocardiography

### Target Learning Goals
- reaching a pre-defined basic competency level in TTE before examining patients
