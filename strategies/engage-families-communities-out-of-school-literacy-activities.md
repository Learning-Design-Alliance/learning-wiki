# Engage students, families, and communities in literacy activities outside of school

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on findings that home and community environments relate to reading outcomes, the article recommends that reading programs focus on engaging students both inside and outside of school, and that interventions "pay greater attention to home and community learning environments." Concrete mechanisms described include Community Action programs that establish book banks and reading camps for children and run reading awareness workshops for parents and community leaders, plus specific activities such as Reading Buddies, Book Bank borrowing, Reading Camps, Make-and-Take activities, and Read-A-Thons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community structures such as book banks, reading camps, and workshops for parents and community leaders
#### Constraints
- Studied in low-resource settings; the article notes effectiveness may differ across settings

### Target Learners
- primary school-age children in grades 1-4 in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- reading skills development outside of school
