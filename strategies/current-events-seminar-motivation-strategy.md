# Use current-events interest (e.g., a nuclear accident) and general-interest seminars to motivate students to engage with nuclear physics topics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends exploiting heightened public interest after a current event to draw students into learning about science topics of general interest. The author used the survey as "an extra tool to motivate students to participate in a seminar about the nuclear energy and the nuclear accidents", aiming to make students aware of both the useful applications of nuclear physics and the harmful effects of radioactivity. This links educational material with topics of current interest so students explore concepts more seriously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Topics must connect to current events of genuine student interest, and non-formal formats such as seminars must be offered, since 98% of surveyed students endorsed such seminars.
