# Persuasive messaging to faculty that reframes virtual care as a complement and addresses attitude, control and norms

## Description
The commentary recommends communicating persuasively with individual faculty about the advantages of VC training, which it says have not been well explicated. Messaging should present VC "not as a substitute for in-person training but as a valuable complement for a richer educational experience", convey that learner training is easy to control and responsive to faculty choices, and enhance perceived social approval by emphasising faculty responsibility as educators.
