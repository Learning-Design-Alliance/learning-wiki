# Offer recurring evidence-based suturing workshops, including through ACNM state affiliates, to support confidence and skill retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation: "Because confidence improves with experience and regular practice supports skill retention, ACNM state affiliates may consider offering recurring suturing workshops using these modalities." They also state any educators teaching suturing skills can implement the evidence-based educational workshops described, and that the program has begun inviting family practice residents and fellows with hopes to expand to medical and midwifery students learning together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to supplies, space, faculty time, and funding for repeated sessions
#### Constraints
- Students may present with a wide range of background experience or exposure to perineal laceration repair, requiring creative solutions

### Target Learners
- student nurse-midwives
- practicing midwives
- residents and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained suturing confidence and skill retention
