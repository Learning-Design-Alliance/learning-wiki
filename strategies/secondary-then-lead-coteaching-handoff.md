# Apprenticeship-style co-teaching sequence for sustaining reforms: secondary role first, then lead, then refinement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a faculty-development pathway modeled on co-teaching: an instructor new to reformed practices first serves in the secondary role (running Tutorial preparation sessions) under a well-versed lead faculty member, then assumes the lead role, then refines the approach in subsequent implementations. The authors describe this as "a modification of the co-teaching approach, advocated and studied by Henderson" and suggest it "may well be effective and sustainable" based on I4's trajectory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-versed (PER) faculty member leads the course during the apprentice's secondary-role phase
- Course materials, lecture notes, concept tests, exams and preparation materials are provided to the incoming instructor
#### Constraints
- The article notes the caveat that the apprentice's second implementation included a PER-versed secondary instructor, so gains may partly reflect added resources

### Target Learners
- university physics faculty new to research-based teaching practices

### Target Learning Goals
- faculty development in implementing research-based curricular reforms
