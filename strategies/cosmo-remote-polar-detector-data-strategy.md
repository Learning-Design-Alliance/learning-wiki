# Extending hands-on student projects with remotely operated detectors at polar research sites

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The hands-on experiments are extended by scintillation counters installed at the German Antarctic research station Neumayer III and on the German research icebreaker Polarstern. These detectors take data continuously, enabling analyses over long time ranges with sufficient statistics. All data will be made available on the internet so interested students and teachers can analyse them together with data from the CosMO detectors, including studies of geomagnetic cutoff and atmospheric pressure and temperature influences.
