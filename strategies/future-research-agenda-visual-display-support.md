# Pursue three lines of future research on supporting learning from visual displays: explanations, promising procedures, and neglected issues

## Description
The review closes by outlining "fruitful lines of further research" with three specific foci: (a) testing the tentative explanations the authors provide "on the basis of the best available evidence," (b) examining "promising but not yet fully approved support procedures," and (c) addressing "important issues that have largely not been researched up to now." This agenda directs researchers toward explanatory validation, maturation of emerging procedures, and coverage of neglected topics in research on learning from visual displays.
