# Raise Awareness of Self-Talk

## Description
Raise awareness by helping students understand the concept of self-talk: what it is, how it affects self-esteem and confidence. Then, ask students to identify the judgements they have about their abilities in different areas (sports, the arts, school subjects). Identify areas of struggle and strength. Discuss what their inner voice says when faced with a manageable task vs. a difficult one, and how confidence and past experiences impact their inner voice. A KWL chart can serve as a pre- and post-activity reflection tool.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Journal, notebook, or KWL chart.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners to initially identify their self-talk. Requires a safe and supportive classroom environment.
