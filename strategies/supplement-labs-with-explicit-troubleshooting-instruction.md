# Supplement existing lab activities with explicit instruction about troubleshooting strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that instructors and education researchers add explicit teaching about troubleshooting strategies on top of regular circuit-building lab activities: "instructors and education re- searchers can supplement existing lab activities with ex- plicit instruction about troubleshooting strategies." This follows from their finding that most instructors relied on the natural occurrence of troubleshooting in labs rather than teaching it deliberately.
