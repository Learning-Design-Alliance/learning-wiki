# Sequence AI-writing instruction by competence: build low-achieving students' independent process writing capacity first, then teach finer-grain AI integration skills to high achievers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implication section recommends differentiating AI writing instruction by students' independent writing competence. As printed, "educators should first aim to develop these students' process writing capacity, evidenced by students' drafting accurate and coherent compositions in their own words." For high-banding students who can already draft independently, educators could focus on finer-grain skills such as transitioning from long to short AI chunks and developing chatbot selection and prompting strategies. The goal is developing all EFL students as independent writers who use AI to enhance, not replace, human effort.
