# Specific Goal Questioning

## Description
Design questions with a specific goal in mind, such as identifying grabbers or understanding essay structure. This helps students pinpoint why a passage made them feel a certain way. Ask "why?"

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material. Preparation of targeted questions. Time for analysis and discussion in class.
#### Constraints
- May be overly directive. Students may miss other important elements of the text. Requires careful planning to ensure that questions are relevant and engaging.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for developing specific writing skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve understanding of writing techniques, enhance ability to analyze and interpret text, develop targeted questioning skills, focus attention on specific elements of the text

### Affordances
- Provides a structured approach to analyzing mentor texts. Helps students connect specific techniques to their overall effect. Increases efficiency by focusing attention on key elements.

### Personalization
- Questions can be tailored to specific student needs and learning goals. Students can be given opportunities to generate their own questions. Scaffolding can be provided for struggling learners.
