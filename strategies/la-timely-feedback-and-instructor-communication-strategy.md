# Use Learning Assistants to give student groups timely feedback and to surface where students are stuck

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying LAs in inquiry-based group work so that groups receive guidance when they need it rather than waiting for a single instructor. Because students will "open up more to the LA and ask the 'dumb questions'", LAs can guide students with leading questions and then feed that information back to the instructor about what is going well and what needs improvement. The article also notes LAs serve as peer mentors whom students ask for help outside class, such as exam-preparation study sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
