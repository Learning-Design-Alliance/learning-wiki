# Monitor and reduce adolescents' time spent on technology as a behavioural strategy to prevent problem technology use and support school outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends monitoring and reducing time spent on technology as behavioural strategies for secondary school students. It states that "Monitoring and reducing time spent on technology could be appropriate behavioural strategies to prevent problem technology use and possible means to improve school connectedness and academic performance among secondary school students." The recommendation rests on the study's observed associations between heavy and problem use and poorer school outcomes, and aligns with the Canadian 24 h movement guideline of no more than 2 h of recreational screen time per day.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
