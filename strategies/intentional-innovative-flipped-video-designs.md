# Explore more intentional and innovative designs for videos in flipped courses, since videos may not serve the designer's intended purpose

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' curriculum-level recommendation following their null result: in flipped courses, tutorial videos 'might not serve the precise purpose that the designer intended', so 'more intentional and innovative designs might be needed to be explored'. The recommendation concerns how the purpose of videos is framed relative to complex laboratory activities, and is offered as a direction for exploration rather than a validated design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A flipped course in which students watch instructional videos outside class in preparation for complex in-class or laboratory activities
#### Constraints
- Derived from a null result in a single calculus-based introductory mechanics course; the authors frame the need for new designs as something that might be needed to be explored

### Target Learners
- undergraduates in a calculus-based introductory mechanics course

### Target Learning Goals
- engaging in scientific practices such as developing models, constructing explanations, and communicating scientific information
