# Embed spelling (encoding) practice of taught words in decoding lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During word-reading intervention, have students spell the monosyllabic and multisyllabic words they are learning to read. "Spelling words will help reinforce the vowel and consonant letter-sounds and combinations students are learning." Students read the word aloud, think about its parts and syllables before writing it, spell additional words with the same letter-sounds and combinations, and finally write words from memory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Begin by asking students to read the word aloud and spell it
- Give students additional words to spell that include the same vowel and consonant letter-sounds and combinations
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4–9 with reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- Solidifying vowel and consonant letter-sound knowledge for reading multisyllabic words
