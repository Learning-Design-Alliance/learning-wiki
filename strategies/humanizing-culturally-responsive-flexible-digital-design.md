# Support rapid digital pivots with non-systematic design cycles, humanizing pedagogy, culturally responsive designs and flexible pathways

## Description
The editorial lists further response recommendations for designing under emergency conditions: "use non-systematic design cycles" ([Lee 2020](https://doi.org/10.1080/08957347.2020.1732383)), "include humanizing pedagogical and/or pedagogy of care" (Karakaya 2020), "consider culturally responsive designs" (Engerman and Otto 2020) and "create flexible pathways for teachers to engage in the practice of design" (Galyen et al. 2020). Together these position care, cultural responsiveness and flexibility as design considerations when teachers face urgent transitions.
