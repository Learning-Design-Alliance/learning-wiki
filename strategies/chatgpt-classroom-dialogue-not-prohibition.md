# Rather than prohibiting ChatGPT, educators can allow classroom use and facilitate dialogue about its benefits and drawbacks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An implementable classroom approach drawn from the finding that students themselves identified ChatGPT's limitations in their reflections. The authors recommend that "Rather than prohibiting the use of ChatGPT, educators can initiate a dialogue with students about the challenges associated with its use," allowing classroom use while facilitating discussion of potential benefits and drawbacks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators willing to permit ChatGPT use in class and to facilitate discussion of its challenges
#### Constraints
- Only a small number of students reflected on ethical considerations, which the authors say should still be addressed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of AI-generated content
- academic integrity awareness
