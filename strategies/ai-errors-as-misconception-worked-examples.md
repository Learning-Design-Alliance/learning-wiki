# Use AI-generated responses on visual and spatial reasoning questions as worked examples of common misconceptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that physics instructors integrate AI tools critically: models can support algebraic problem solving and conceptual review, but instructors should caution students against over-reliance on AI for diagram interpretation, graph construction, or three-dimensional reasoning. It states "instructors may find it pedagogically valuable to use AI-generated responses on such questions as worked examples of common misconceptions, turning model failures into teaching opportunities". This converts documented model errors into instructional material for targeting precisely the reasoning types central to AP Physics FRQs.
