# Prioritise adequately powered multicentre comparative studies linked to objective metrics and clinical correlation, and develop standardised competency assessment frameworks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that evaluation of technology-enhanced basic robotic skills learning requires "Adequately powered, multicentre, comparative studies, ideally linked to objective performance metrics and clinical correlation". It also states that standardised competency assessment frameworks are required to evaluate rapidly progressing tools, and identifies gaps in augmented reality, artificial intelligence, and docking and setup training. The recommendation follows directly from the review's findings of small single-centre studies and low-to-very-low certainty of evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardised competency assessment frameworks to evaluate rapidly progressing tools
- Objective performance metrics linked to clinical correlation
#### Constraints
- Current evidence base is limited by small, mostly single-centre studies and low to very low certainty

### Target Learners
- novice robotic surgeons
- surgical trainees
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic robotic skills training
- competency assessment
- docking and setup skills
