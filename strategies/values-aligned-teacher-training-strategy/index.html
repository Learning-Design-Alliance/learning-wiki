Values-aligned teacher training that connects growth mindset-supportive practices to teachers' core professional values¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Values alignment reframes a desired behavior in terms of a core value a person already holds, imbuing the behavior with motivational priority. TxMI researchers identified teachers' shared value of inspiring students' enthusiasm for learning through focus groups and a national survey, then built a ~45-minute self-administered online module whose first half used a values-aligned argument and whose second half provided concrete guidance on growth mindset-supportive practices such as revision and resubmission for credit. In a multi-arm RCT it outperformed non-values-aligned arms in promoting teachers' motivation to adopt the practices and improved student pass rates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Identify a genuine shared core value of the target teacher group through qualitative and confirmatory survey research before designing the argument

Constraints¶

  • The article notes conventional professional development workshops and evaluation-and-accountability schemes have failed to produce meaningful effects on student outcomes, and teachers face limited time, competing priorities, and a hype cycle

Target Learners¶

  • high school teachers, particularly in dual-enrollment courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • teachers' adoption and sustained use of growth mindset-supportive classroom practices

Affordances¶

  • Mindset Classroom Culture Framework

Claims¶

  • Inspiring students' enthusiasm for learning is a widely shared core value among U.S. high school math teachers [+M]
  • A teacher fellowship pilot during the pandemic improved growth-mindset classroom climate ratings and reduced teachers' intent to leave [+M]
  • Professional development from CHSRI and CPS is valued by some small-school staff but viewed by others as irrelevant, hard to schedule, and poorly connected to school needs [~M]
  • A values-aligned growth mindset-supportive teaching intervention in a multi-arm RCT raised course pass rates by four percentage points overall and six to eleven points in lower-SES classrooms [+M]
