# Daily brief presentations by a rotating student group representative to train communication and soft skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Once group project work begins, the organizers reserve the last ~30 mins of each day for a project representative to briefly present progress. The representative "has to change every day", the instances are informal, and they are used to train effective communication and general soft skills. For Chilean students these reports are often their first opportunities to present in English, and feedback from students indicates the instances are extremely beneficial for their future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A rotating presenter role so every group member presents
- Informal daily reporting sessions at the end of project work days
#### Constraints
- The authors note the strategy does not address soft skills formally: we do not do formally, but via the daily report and enforcing the rotation of the role of presenter

### Target Learners
- students in mixed US and Chilean cohorts, including non-native English speakers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective communication and soft skills in a research-team context
