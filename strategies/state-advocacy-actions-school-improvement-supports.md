# State-level advocacy actions to strengthen school improvement supports for identified schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends a set of state-level advocacy actions for advancing equity through school improvement: urging leaders to "Dedicate state funding to augment federal improvement dollars," revise funding formulas and oppose voucher programs, integrate improvement efforts into broader equity and accountability systems, promote a culture of continuous learning, and provide tiered support for all schools. These are grounded in the interview findings on limited resources, compliance-oriented dynamics, and nascent data use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State leaders and policymakers willing to act on advocate engagement
- Alignment of improvement efforts with existing statewide priorities and goals
#### Constraints
- Federal support may be unlikely under the current federal administration, so advocates should focus on state-level efforts while continuing to push for federal solutions

### Target Learners
- Students in schools identified for comprehensive school improvement
- Students from low-income backgrounds and students of color in underperforming schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved academic outcomes in identified schools
- More equitable educational opportunities and conditions systemwide
