# Extend reusable VR simulations with progress bars, awards, and social interaction across repeated runs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For VR simulations that can be used repeatedly, the authors recommend adding gaming elements that only become meaningful across multiple runs: "a progress bar and awards should be included to improve feedback and therefore the learning outcome of the participants". Comparing the time needed in each run could serve as a progress bar of sorts and could result in an award for the best time achieved, and comparison between participants in the form of social interaction could improve competitiveness and the learning experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulation that can be used repeatedly, since progress and awards presuppose multiple runs
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that an overload of gaming elements could distract from the learning outcome, and notes the importance of each element to learning outcome is not yet shown

### Target Learners
- medical students using VR skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural skill improvement through repeated practice and feedback
