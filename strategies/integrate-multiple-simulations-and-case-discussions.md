# Integrate multiple simulations and case-based discussions into the clerkship curriculum and prospectively evaluate randomized groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a next step, the authors recommend moving beyond single-session interventions: integrating repeated simulations and case-based discussions into the pediatric emergency clerkship and evaluating them prospectively. They write that "an appropriate next step would be to integrate multiple simulations and case-based discussions into our pediatric emergency clerkship curriculum and to prospectively evaluate groups randomized to each method regarding satisfaction, knowledge gains and both technical and non-technical skills and behaviors."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Randomized group allocation for future evaluation
- Measurement of satisfaction, knowledge gains, and technical and non-technical skills and behaviors
#### Constraints
- The current evidence base is a single-session, non-randomized study at one medical school, so the authors call the results not safely validated externally

### Target Learners
- medical students on pediatric emergency clerkship rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge acquisition and retention
- technical and non-technical emergency skills and behaviors
- satisfaction
