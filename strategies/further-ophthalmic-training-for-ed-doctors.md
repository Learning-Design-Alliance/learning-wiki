# Develop further ophthalmic training for doctors working in EDs, including funduscopy training for medical students and foundation doctors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends building targeted ophthalmic training for ED doctors because confidence in eye emergencies, funduscopy and slit-lamp use is low and formal ED training is widely absent. It states "we believe further funduscopy training for medical students and foundation doctors is vital" and suggests anterior segment examination is an achievable aim for foundation doctors. It also calls for assessing why foundation doctors feel ill-prepared.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must fit the ED context, addressing the lack of formal ophthalmology training the review documents in the ED
#### Constraints
- The review notes slit-lamp proficiency takes time and is not a compulsory part of the medical school curriculum

### Target Learners
- medical students
- foundation doctors
- Emergency Medicine physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- managing ophthalmic emergencies
- performing funduscopy
- using the slit lamp
- performing lateral canthotomy and cantholysis for retrobulbar haemorrhage
