# Two-video-tutorial training session followed by hands-on practice and blinded scored assessment for basic daVinci Xi skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (1 for, 5 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training program consists of two video tutorials — one explaining the platform's components (patient-side cart and console) and one demonstrating the exercises — followed by individual, videorecorded practice sessions at the cart and console. Exercises included trocar attachment, instrument insertion and replacement, optics repositioning, clutch and switch pedal use, and a timed 2/0 silk stitch with three knots on a silicone model. A blinded, robotic-experienced surgeon scored each exercise using predefined quantitative and qualitative criteria, and execution times were recorded. The authors state the platform "can be properly operated in its basic functions by young surgeons after a short training program even in the absence of previous exposure to robotic clinical procedure."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A robotic-experienced surgeon blinded to group affiliation to evaluate videorecorded exercises against the printed scoring criteria
- Individual practice sessions with only the trainee admitted to the operating room
#### Constraints
- Video tutorials alone proved insufficient for patient-cart operation, requiring targeted and more intensive training according to the authors

### Target Learners
- Surgical residents, including those with no prior robotic exposure

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic operation of the daVinci Xi robotic platform at the patient-side cart and surgeon's console
