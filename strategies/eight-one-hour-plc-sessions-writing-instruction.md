# Run eight one-hour professional learning community sessions spread across the school year

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The toolkit organizes professional development over the course of eight one-hour professional learning community (PLC) sessions, with activities to be completed during and between sessions and practices teachers can implement throughout the school year. This distributes writing-instruction learning across the year in short, collaborative meetings rather than a single training event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator, the Facilitator's Guide and slides, and Teacher's Guide activities for between-session work
#### Constraints
- Session structure was designed with busy educators in mind and piloted in charter school contexts

### Target Learners
- Elementary teachers in grades 2 through 4

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based writing instruction practices
