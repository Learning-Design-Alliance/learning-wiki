# Use ultrasound as a bridging technology across sequential training stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article uses ultrasound as a bridging technology that connects training stages: L1 biopsy practice builds fundamental skills in real-time lesion localization, probe handling, and image optimization; these skills directly transfer to L2 port implantation, which similarly depends on ultrasound for evaluating vascular anatomy, implantation depth, and tissue layers; and they underpin L3 breast-conserving surgery. This sequencing establishes a stable and precise operational foundation essential for subsequent procedures.
