Prepare-participate-reflect sequence with a mediation log for AI use in education abroad¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends structuring AI use as "a prepare-participate-reflect sequence": use AI to identify vocabulary, possible questions and areas of uncertainty; engage directly with the relevant person or setting wherever reasonably possible; then compare expectations, the actual encounter and the system's earlier assumptions. A brief mediation log records the situation, why AI was used, what role it performed, whether a human interaction followed, and what was accepted or questioned.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educator capacity depends on their own confidence with the systems, which reviews find uneven and shaped by institutional support, making this a staff-development question as well

Constraints¶

  • The mediation log is metacognitive, making invisible mediation visible, and should not become surveillance or a tally of prohibited uses

Target Learners¶

  • education-abroad students, especially those who would otherwise not participate due to anxiety

Target Learning Goals¶

  • host-community interaction
  • metacognitive awareness of AI mediation
  • communicative risk-taking

Related Strategies¶

  • Teach mediation literacy and redesign assessment and evaluation to measure interaction, not only satisfaction
  • Use a triadic guiding-question checklist to design and reflect on adult learning activities across preparation, performance and reflection
