# Reform TDI residency training with blended learning, simulation-based training, and competency-based assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a package of curricular reforms for TDI education: blended learning with knowledge graph-based MOOCs and flipped classrooms, PBL/CBL/TBL case discussions, virtual simulation and high-fidelity manikins or 3D-printed models for hands-on practice, and competency-based assessment via DOPS and OSCE. The authors state "it is recommended to integrate active learning strategies into teaching reforms enhancing clinical decision-making for acute dental trauma."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multidisciplinary collaborative training and cross-specialty rotations so every resident attains baseline proficiency in acute dental injury management
#### Constraints
- Study conducted at a single institution; regional training differences limit generalizability of the motivating findings

### Target Learners
- Dental residents across specialties (General Dentistry, Oral Medicine, Oral Surgery, and others)

### Target Learning Goals
- Management of traumatic dental injuries in immature permanent teeth, including emergency treatment, splinting, and patient education
