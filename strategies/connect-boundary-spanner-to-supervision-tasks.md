# Connect boundary-spanner role descriptions to supervisory tasks of PDSs

## Description
The authors recommend that future descriptions of boundary-spanning roles connect the work to the tasks of supervision in PDSs (Teaching, Collaboration and Community, Research for Innovation, Targeted Assistance, Curriculum Development and Support, Individual Support, and Equity). This strategy stems from the finding that "boundary-spanning roles are supervisory by nature" even when the role title does not include the word supervisor.
