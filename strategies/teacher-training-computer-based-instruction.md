# Train teachers on technology management, report interpretation, and minimal redirection before computer-based instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the intervention, the study's principal investigator trained teachers and assistant teachers on two separate days using "oral explanations, modeling, and guided teacher practice". Teachers were trained "to respond to technology issues (e.g., volume adjustments), to access and decipher reports, and to intervene and redirect" with a minimum amount of gesturing or gentle physical guidance, and received implementation checklists to monitor implementation integrity.
