# Institutional-level actions to improve astronomers' statistical training: add applied statistics courses, create cross-disciplinary venues, make joint hires, and reward interdisciplinary work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of implementable departmental actions proposed by the authors: adding or changing at least one undergraduate course, creating venues where graduate students and postdocs can meet methods-focused researchers, joint hires between statistics and astronomy/physics departments, cross-disciplinary research groups, and rewarding interdisciplinary faculty work. The article states such joint hires "will catalyze collaborative efforts, will raise awareness in both fields, and will transform the graduate education experience for multiple students at a time". These recommendations follow directly from the survey findings on self-taught training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intellectual buy-in at the institutional level, which the article identifies as necessary for interdisciplinary training and research to be successful
#### Constraints
- Interdisciplinary collaboration takes time to develop and changing curriculum requirements requires working through bureaucratic processes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and graduate astronomy and physics students
- Postdoctoral researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- Applied statistical and exploratory data analysis skills for astronomical research
