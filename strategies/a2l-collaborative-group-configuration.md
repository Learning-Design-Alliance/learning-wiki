# Configure collaborative groups of two to four students, assign roles, and still collect answers individually

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For collaborative work, the report recommends groups "as small as two students" that "in most cases should not be larger than three or four students", while answers are generally still collected individually so every student is invested. Teachers help students build interaction and conversational skills through special familiar-context activities at the beginning of the year and roles such as Manager, Encourager, and Clarifier.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need time to develop interaction and conversational skills through special activities with familiar contexts before learning new content in groups.
#### Constraints
- If students cannot rearrange themselves so that they are facing each other, groups should have only 2 or 3 students, otherwise one or two students will inevitably become disconnected from the conversation.

### Target Learners
- high school physics students
- college physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative learning
- communication skills
- conceptual understanding
