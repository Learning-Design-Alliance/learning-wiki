# State education agencies can commission regional educational laboratory analytic support to develop COVID-19 mitigation guidance for school reopening

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy pairs a state education agency with a regional educational laboratory to supply analytic support for policy guidance. In May 2020 the Pennsylvania Department of Education approached its partners at the Mid-Atlantic Regional Educational Laboratory "to provide analytic support for their guidance" as schools planned Fall 2020 reopening policies and procedures for mitigating COVID-19 spread and ensuring student and staff safety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing partnership between the state education agency and the regional laboratory, as PDE had with REL Mid-Atlantic
#### Constraints
- Applied to planning for re-opening in Fall 2020 during COVID-19 building closures statewide since March 2020

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Pennsylvania public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe continuation of instruction during pandemic conditions, including remote instruction and mitigation of COVID-19 spread
