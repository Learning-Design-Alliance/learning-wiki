# Use iVR refresher training before infrequently performed procedures to refresh skill and knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends immersive virtual reality simulation as an adjunctive refresher before operations a trainee or surgeon rarely performs, addressing skill decay in conceptualizing distorted anatomy. It states "iVR simulators can provide effective and efficient adjunctive, supplemental training to traditional learning formats and operative experiences," and suggests remote multiplayer consultation in iVR before surgery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an iVR simulator system with a procedure-specific training module (head-mounted display, haptic controllers, performance metrics)
#### Constraints
- Recognition and understanding of availability of this simulation technology by the surgical community likely remains limited, per the authors

### Target Learners
- Orthopaedic surgical trainees (PGY-5 resident in the case)
- Practicing surgeons refreshing infrequently performed procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- Surgical technical skill for percutaneous SCFE pinning
- Visuospatial understanding of distorted three-dimensional anatomy
- Fluoroscopic localization and screw placement
