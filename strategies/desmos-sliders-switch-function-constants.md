# Use interactive Desmos sliders so students modify switch-function constants and visualize their effects in real time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
While introducing the generalized switch function s(t), the author encourages students to open a Desmos graphing calculator link and change the constants tc, c, and α one at a time, observing how each shifts, reflects, or tightens the graph. This lets students "modify constants and visualize their effects in real time", grounding the meaning of the switch-flip time, switch direction, and transition rate before the optimal α is determined numerically.
