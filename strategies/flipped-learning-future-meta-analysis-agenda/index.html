Extend meta-analytic synthesis of flipped learning to additional outcomes, samples, and regions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's concluding suggestions call for future research beyond academic achievement: analyzing flipped learning's effects on "attitude, self-efficacy, and motivation" through meta-analysis, repeating the synthesis after the diversity of sample groups increases, and meta-analyzing studies outside Turkey for comparison. It also recommends research on flipped learning activities and learning outcomes variables to understand the rising effect and the strategic approaches contributing to student performance.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Further studies increasing the diversity of the sample group before repeating the meta-analysis

Constraints¶

  • The present synthesis covers only academic achievement, only studies performed in Turkey, published 2014-2018, with pretest/posttest experimental control group designs

Target Learners¶

  • students across educational levels in Turkey and internationally

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement, attitude, self-efficacy, motivation

Claims¶

  • Publication-bias checks indicate the pooled effect is unlikely to result from publication bias [+W]
  • Publication bias is present in the Turkish flipped learning literature, and correcting it lowers the pooled effect from 0.895 to 0.713 [+W]
  • Study type, educational level, and year do not significantly moderate the effect of flipped learning on success [+W]
