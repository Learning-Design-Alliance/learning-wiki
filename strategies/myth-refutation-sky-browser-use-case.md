# Use myth-refutation sky-browser activities (e.g. the 2012 doomsday claim) for learners not doing calculations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For younger students or the general public who are not able to or not interested in astronomical calculations, the team developed use cases using the Stellarium sky browser. One addresses the myth of a world-ending planetary alignment on December 21st 2012: the article states "This claim can easily be refuted by using Stellarium." The use case begins with a general introduction on how planets move and the phenomenon of conjunctions, then shows how to depict planetary positions for any given time and place, examines past conjunctions, and confirms there will be no special alignment in 2012.
