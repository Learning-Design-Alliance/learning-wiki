# Integrate low-cost BCI neurofeedback with online learning platforms for scalable, personalized math support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend deploying accessible single-channel EEG neurofeedback together with online learning platforms and machine-learning prediction to deliver targeted training at scale. They write that "By integrating BCI technology with online learning platforms and using machi ne learning techniques to predict learning outcomes, our approach can facilitate the delivery of targeted and effective neurofeedback training to large numbers of students". The strategy leverages the system's low cost, portability, and ecological validity for classrooms and remote learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A low-cost, easy-to-use single-channel dry-electrode EEG headset
- An online learning platform with which the neurofeedback software can be integrated
- Machine-learning models using pre-test performance and self-efficacy to target interventions
#### Constraints
- Long-term sustainability of effects is untested; the authors call for follow-up studies with larger samples before generalization

### Target Learners
- university students
- students in online and remote learning formats

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematical performance
- self-efficacy
- academic success and well-being in STEM fields
