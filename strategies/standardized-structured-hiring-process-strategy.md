# Standardize the hiring process with structured interviews, competency-aligned protocols, and performance-based tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide urges hiring teams to standardize hiring by "Auditing, updating, and standardizing the hiring process," "Developing a structured interview process that aligns ideal candidate competencies with interview questions and screening rubrics," and "Calibrating hiring tools with all hiring team members." Performance-based assessments are said to "minimize opportunities for bias to impact hiring decisions as they focus more on demonstrated competencies."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Annual audits of hiring processes and materials; collaboratively developed and annually updated protocols; coordination with school committees and unions for contract changes
#### Constraints
- Changes to hiring materials and processes may require working with school committees and unions to follow district contract policies

### Target Learners
- school-based hiring teams
- HR professionals
- building administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- fair, competency-based selection of learner-ready teachers and reduced bias in hiring decisions
