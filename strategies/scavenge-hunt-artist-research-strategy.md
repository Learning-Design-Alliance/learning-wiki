# Scavenge-hunt in-class artist research activity strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy follows lectures on general art, specific data-art examples, and algorithms with an in-class scavenger hunt where students locate different artists' work and art styles. Students compile their research into a dedicated 'mood board' section of their report referencing original works.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students locate artists and works that can be cited, focusing on artists with publications, blogs, installations, performances and exhibitions

### Target Learning Goals
