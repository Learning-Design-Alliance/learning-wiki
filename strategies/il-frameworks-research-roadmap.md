# Research roadmap for future iL frameworks covering immersion level, devices, development tools, user experience, simulator sickness, accessibility, reuse, platforms, infrastructure, outcomes and learning analytics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 5 studies (3 review, 2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on identified gaps, the review proposes aspects fundamental to support development for iL and to support teachers and instructors. Roadmap items include defining which human senses and interaction forms will be involved, choosing immersive devices balancing immersion and affordability, selecting development tools and SDKs, maintaining a stable frame rate preferably 60 fps to avoid discomfort, implementing teleportation to reduce motion sickness symptoms, adopting software reuse via Domain Engineering and Application Engineering, using immersive platforms that support educators, and capturing learning data. The review notes "no framework considered the monitoring of student learning in virtual environments" and suggests Learning Analytics, including biofeedback sensors, could improve the teaching-learning process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Frameworks should address technical development details and support use of immersive virtual environments by teachers and instructors who lack development skills
#### Constraints
- The review states increasing the degree of immersion of devices is not a guarantee of high academic performance; sense of presence and pedagogical aspects must also be considered

### Target Learners
- teachers and instructors adopting immersive teaching experiences
- developers of immersive educational applications

### Target Learning Goals
- improved learning outcomes through immersive experiences
- effective development of immersive educational environments
