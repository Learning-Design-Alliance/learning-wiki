# Replace oral nomenclature questioning with timed interactive online map quizzes submitted for marking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends replacing the traditional method of oral nomenclature questioning with wall maps, whose disadvantages include large time cost, obsolete wall maps and subjective assessment. Students use the Seterra online resource (second-year) and Click-that-hood (third-year) to "demonstrate the knowledge of the nomenclature within the time limit, save the version and send it to the teacher for marking". Stated advantages are individual pace, objectivity of assessment, mapping skills and rational in-class time management.
