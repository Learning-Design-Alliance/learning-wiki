# Use the DI checklist as a scaffold for reflective professional development rather than a to-do list

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that pre-service and in-service teacher education use the checklist to support evidence-informed decision-making when responding to student heterogeneity. Items are guidelines that stimulate reflective practitioners to assess their work; administration "may not be reduced to a 'to-do-list'". Teachers may use the checklist as a point of orientation when discussing strategies they use or intend to practice, in personal or collective team reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intense professional development is needed, since the introduction of differentiated instruction is often a long and difficult process that requires a lot of time
#### Constraints
