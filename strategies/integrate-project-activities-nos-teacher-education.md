# Integrate project activities and applied NOS courses into science teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's recommendations call for practical, project-oriented treatment of nature of science in teacher preparation: pre-service teachers should "gain experiences on the effective ways of teaching their knowledge of history and nature of science" via in-class practices, applied courses enabling concrete understanding of science and scientific research should join the theoretical HNOS course, and direct-reflective, indirect and historical methods for teaching NOS should be introduced with designed activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A History and Nature of Science course in the teacher-preparation curriculum around which applied courses and practical NOS activities can be organized
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single quasi-experimental study with pre-service science teachers in one Turkish faculty; the article presents these as implications for researchers in the field

### Target Learners
- pre-service science teachers
- primary school students taught by these teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- nature of science and nature of scientific knowledge understanding
- scientific literacy

## Claims

- [Most experimental-group pre-service teachers viewed the project-based HNOS course positively and recommended carrying it out through project activities](../claims/participants-endorse-pbhnos.md) [+W]
- [Post-test SUSSI scores of pre-service science teachers taught through project-based HNOS training are significantly higher than those of the control group taught by conventional method](../claims/pbl-hnos-higher-posttest-sussi.md) [+W]
