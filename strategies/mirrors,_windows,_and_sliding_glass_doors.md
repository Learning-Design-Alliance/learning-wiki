# Mirrors, Windows, and Sliding Glass Doors

## Description
Using the concept of mirrors, windows, and sliding glass doors helps students engage with diverse stories and experiences. Mirrors reflect a student's own culture and identity, fostering belonging. Windows offer a view into someone else's experience, promoting understanding of the wider world. Sliding glass doors allow readers to enter a story and become part of the world, encouraging reflection and action. By diversifying classroom materials and promoting reflection, educators can help students develop empathy, understanding, and a sense of global citizenship.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- Access to diverse resources. Time for reflection and discussion. A willingness to promote empathy and understanding.
#### Constraints
- Some students may be resistant to engaging with diverse perspectives. Requires ongoing effort and commitment. May require additional support to address biases and stereotypes.
