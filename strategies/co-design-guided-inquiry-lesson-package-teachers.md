# Co-design the guided inquiry lesson package with the classroom teacher and collect multiple data sources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the classroom deployment phase, the authors plan to "work closely with the teacher to co-design the guided inquiry lesson package" and to gather evidence from multiple sources. The stated instruments are teachers' feedback on the simulation, field notes from classroom observations, student-produced artifacts, students' feedback on the simulation, students' results, and the teacher's reflection on the lesson. This strategy pairs collaborative design with triangulated data collection to understand how the package affects students' understanding of the physics concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A willing classroom teacher participating in co-design
- A deployed lesson package built around the customized energy simulation
#### Constraints
- The strategy describes the planned next phase of an ongoing case study; classroom results were not yet available in this article

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in the deploying classroom

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved understanding of energy concepts through guided inquiry with the customized simulation
