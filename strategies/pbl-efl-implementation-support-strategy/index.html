Support PBL adoption in EFL with teacher training, careful class planning, and advance clarification of the method to students¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review derives implementation recommendations from the challenges reported across the 27 studies. It recommends comprehensive teacher training and support to overcome educator resistance to change, and advises that teachers "plan their classes carefully and clarify the process and purpose of the method to their students in advance to avoid any confusion about the method". It also lists practical challenges including equipment, technology, and difficulty formulating appropriate questions or scenarios.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must be familiar with the PBL methodology; the review states that if English teachers are not familiar with it, interventions will not be effective and students will be confused.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes limited resources including time, materials, and support, and possible disconnect between PBL objectives and broader curriculum requirements.

Target Learners¶

  • EFL students at all levels of education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English language proficiency (listening, speaking, reading, writing, vocabulary)
  • critical thinking
  • learner engagement and motivation

Claims¶

  • PBL approaches consistently enhance EFL student engagement, motivation, and active involvement in learning [+W]
  • PBL improves EFL students' critical thinking disposition, creativity, and language scores, with noted gender differences [+W]
  • PBL improves EFL reading proficiency and metacognitive reading strategies, though some studies lack control groups [+W]
  • PBL and hybrid PBL improve EFL speaking proficiency, including fluency, grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation [+W]
  • PBL, including PBL combined with VR, improves EFL vocabulary acquisition [+W]
  • PBL improves EFL writing proficiency across nine reviewed studies, including essay, paragraph, and descriptive writing [+W]
