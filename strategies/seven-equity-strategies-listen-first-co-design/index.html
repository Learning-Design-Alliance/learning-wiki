Seven equity strategies for local leaders, including listening first, strengths-based approaches, and co-design¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends seven strategies for local leaders to disrupt power imbalances and foster greater equity. These include: "Engage and listen to teachers, students, and families first, before taking action"; adopting strengths-based approaches to planning, decision-making, and communicating; embracing existing community values, histories, and relationships; co-designing, co-implementing, co-leading, and co-governing initiatives with students, families, and communities; using inquiry and reflection to disrupt implicit and explicit biases; using data collection and analysis to disrupt organizational and structural inequities; and helping children claim their power and lift up their communities.
