# Political Education

## Description
Political education involves developing a comprehensive understanding of the historical, social, and political contexts that shape a problem. It is crucial for understanding root causes and systemic issues before engaging in empirical research or action. This process includes examining power dynamics and positionality within dominant structures to foster critical consciousness and informed action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to relevant resources, skilled facilitators to guide discussions, and a safe space for open and honest dialogue.
#### Constraints
- Can be emotionally challenging and may require addressing sensitive or controversial topics. Requires a significant time commitment to develop a comprehensive understanding.
