# Noise exploration assignments using a noise simulator to teach quantum noise concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Noise exploration assignments introduce students to quantum noise and the different types of noise occurring in quantum systems, using the Microsoft QDK noise simulator with noise models configured via the QuTiP library. Assignments can go in either direction: students "implement an algorithm and explore its behavior assuming a specific noise model, or, conversely, identify the noise present in the system based on the histogram of the program outputs."
