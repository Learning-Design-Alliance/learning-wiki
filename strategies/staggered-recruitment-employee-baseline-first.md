# Stagger recruitment so employee baseline data is collected before managers receive the intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the trial's recruitment process, direct report employees receive their invitation first, and managers are invited only after the employee recruitment period closes. The article states: "This staggering of recruitment is designed to ensure that baseline employee data is obtained prior to the managers receiving any intervention." This sequencing protects the integrity of employee baseline measures in a design where managers and their direct reports are both assessed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruitment emails must be sequenced: employees first, then managers, with online opt-in links for each group.
