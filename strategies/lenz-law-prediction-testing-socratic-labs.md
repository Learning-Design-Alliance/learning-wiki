# Guide students through applying Lenz's law with prediction, lab testing, and Socratic dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that lab activities on induced EMF explicitly guide students in applying Lenz's law, including proper use of the right-hand rule. Students first predict the direction of induced current, then test their predictions in the lab, and are then "guided through the use of Socratic dialogue[ 10] to understand any deviations from their predictions." This addresses the observed difficulty that students knew a current would flow but could not determine its direction.
