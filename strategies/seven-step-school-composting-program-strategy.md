# Seven-step recipe for starting and sustaining a school composting program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distils its PIBID experience into an ordered implementation recipe: "1) Formar um grupo de compostagem; 2) Escolher um coordenador...; 3) Promover os benefícios da compostagem e a importância da separação correta do lixo; 4) Escolher um método de compostagem conforme a viabilidade da escola; 5) Planejar as atividades e construir as composteiras; 6) Estabelecer um sistema de coleta do lixo orgânico; 7) Executar o programa de compostagem". Each step is illustrated with what the PIBID students actually did, such as forming the school group, installing a kit of four colored 60-liter bins for selective collection, and awareness work with pamphlets and lectures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Planning, awareness and collaboration of the whole school community, since the process takes about three months and should continue beyond the engaged teachers' and students' tenure
#### Constraints
- The school must be able to separate waste correctly at the source; where no municipal selective collection exists, an alternative such as a cooperative with scheduled pickup is needed

### Target Learners
- entire school community including students, teachers, staff and families

### Target Learning Goals
- environmental education and sustainability practices
- scientific literacy and communication through student-led promotion
