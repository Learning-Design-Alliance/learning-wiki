# Provide more training, ongoing support and shared interactive resources to enable effective IWB use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's recommendations target the barriers its survey identified: "More training and ongoing support for educators in order for them to make maximum use of the interactive boards", designating a responsible staff member, teacher teams curating and adapting materials, peer help sessions at lunch, collaborative exchange of practices, sharing lesson plans to save time, reliable internet, a troubleshooting/maintenance team, budgetary support, and assessing students using technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Support from heads of school and colleagues; budget for internet connection, lesson purchases and maintenance
#### Constraints
- Recommended for the Mauritian primary school context where IWB use is in its teething stage

### Target Learners
- Primary school teachers in Mauritius

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective integration of IWB technology into teaching and learning
