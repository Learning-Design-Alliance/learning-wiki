# Provide data-centric research experiences in STEM courses and teach critical data science skills as graduation requirements

## Description
The paper's first recommendation to high schools, colleges, and universities is to "Provide data-centric research experiences in STEM courses" and "Promote the use of real scientific data in the classroom through mini-grants, curriculum mandates, and/or a faculty reward system." It further urges institutions to teach critical data science skills to all students as part of science graduation requirements, grow curricula in data science and astroinformatics, promote computational literacy across the curriculum, hire faculty with data science training, and establish mentorship programs for minority and underrepresented groups. The strategy treats astronomical databases as an effective mechanism for formal STEM education and for teaching data science skills relevant to all disciplines.
