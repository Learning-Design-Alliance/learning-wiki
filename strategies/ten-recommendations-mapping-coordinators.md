# Ten recommendations for curriculum-mapping coordinators derived from the four-faculty change-process analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article derives, from its Grounded-Theory analysis of the change process at four medical faculties, ten practical recommendations for mapping coordinators (printed in Table 1 as "Useful tips to facilitate the NKLM mapping in the process"). These bundle the identified motivating factors: clear "clear top-down and bottom-up management", continuous faculty information, adjusted support such as workshops and one-on-one counselling, portioning and student blueprint pre-mapping, and visibility of growing approval. They are intended to give other faculties practical assistance when implementing curriculum mapping.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty implementing the recommendations needs the resources and structures from Part I, including trained coordinators, systematic project management and a web-based mapping instrument (MERLIN database).
#### Constraints
- The findings arise from four German medical faculties with different framework conditions; the authors state bias cannot be excluded and factors were not quantified.

### Target Learners
- Faculty coordinators, deans of study and teaching, administrative staff and lecturers in undergraduate medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- Competence-oriented curriculum development and transparent alignment of existing curricula with the NKLM learning-objective catalogue
