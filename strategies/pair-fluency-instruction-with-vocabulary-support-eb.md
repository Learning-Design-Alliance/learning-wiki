# Pair fluency instruction with oral language and vocabulary support before first reading of a new text

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy, drawn from the review's first recommendation, directs teachers to pair fluency instruction with oral language and vocabulary support, "especially before first reading of a new text". It works by giving emergent bilinguals scaffolded access to vocabulary and meaning before fluency practice, so oral reading practice builds on understood language rather than decoding alone. The reviewed studies implemented this through vocabulary preview, explicit word work, discussion, and comprehension checks alongside fluency routines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vocabulary and oral language support must precede fluency instruction and/or practice on a new text
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on studies that embedded fluency in broader interventions, not on fluency-only designs

### Target Learners
- Emergent bilinguals / English learners in the primary grades

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading fluency (accuracy, rate, prosody)
- Vocabulary and oral language development
- Reading comprehension
