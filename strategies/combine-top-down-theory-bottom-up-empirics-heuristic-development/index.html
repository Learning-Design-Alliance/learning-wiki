Combine top-down theoretical and bottom-up empirical approaches when developing domain-specific heuristics¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that developers of new domain-tailored heuristics "combine top-down (theoretical) and bottom-up (empirical) approaches to address both pedagogical and domain-specific requirements". In this study, the top-down approach drew on Jahnke et al.'s LXD dimensions while the bottom-up approach synthesized findings from a design ethnography, instructor focus groups, and a student participatory design workshop, followed by iterative expert evaluation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to prior empirical studies of the target learning context
  • Involvement of expert evaluators with some domain familiarity

Constraints¶

  • The authors recommend heuristics be applied by experts external to the development process to avoid the inadvertent biases of those close to it

Target Learners¶

  • learning experience designers and learning analytics developers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • developing valid evaluation heuristics tailored to a specific professional learning domain
