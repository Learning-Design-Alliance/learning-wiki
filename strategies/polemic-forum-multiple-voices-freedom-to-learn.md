# Design polemic web forum tasks with multiple voices and student-centred decisions to prompt freedom to learn

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends designing polemic web forums based on real-world problems that engage students ontologically, creating spaces of negotiation involving ethical positions, values and moral judgments on open-ended problems. The authors state that "Adding obstacles, i.e. difficulties to the activity—such as the different voices and other positions in the statements or putting the students in the centre of a decision—prompts freedom to learn in collaboration". Perspective-taking tasks envisioning the future school are recommended as an alternative to memorisation or pre-defined questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks based on short texts from national online newspapers close to dilemmas students live at school, with contrasting voices and positions
#### Constraints
- The article's support comes from a single-dyad qualitative case study

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- perspective-taking, ethical reasoning, and collaborative meaning-making
