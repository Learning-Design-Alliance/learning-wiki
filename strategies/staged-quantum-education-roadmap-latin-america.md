# Four-phase regional roadmap for staged quantum-education integration (2026–2035)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a country-sensitive, regionally coordinated roadmap beginning with teacher development and pilot centers, leveraging open-source platforms and local-language resources, and scaling toward gradual curricular integration. Phase 1 (2026–2028) focuses on diagnosis and a Latin American Teacher Development Program; Phase 2 (2028–2030) runs curricular pilots in 3–5 schools per country with school-based quantum clubs and Q-Challenges; Phase 3 (2030–2033) builds a regional ecosystem with a Spanish/Portuguese repository and Q-LATAM teacher certification; Phase 4 (2033–2035) makes moderate curriculum adjustments including complex numbers, elementary linear algebra, and notions of superposition and qubits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between ministries, universities, NGOs, and technology hubs for sustainability
#### Constraints
- The roadmap is not prescriptive but an adaptable guiding framework; formal curricular integration is only feasible after consolidation of prior skills

### Target Learners
- High school students and teachers in Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina, Brazil, and Colombia

### Target Learning Goals
- Introductory quantum computing concepts and foundational mathematics and physics
