# Deliberately incomplete prior instruction: projects require gathering most information from outside coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In JITE project design, only part of the needed content is covered in regular coursework beforehand; the remainder "must be gathered from diﬀerent source s — schoolbooks, extracurricular books, the web, site visits, and interviews with practition ers." Students are encouraged to see projects as not requiring standard, back-of-the- book answers, so different teams could arrive at different conclusions and deliverables.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The project must be solvable with information obtainable from schoolbooks, extracurricular books, the web, site visits, and interviews with practitioners
- Students must be encouraged to see projects as not requiring standard, back-of-the-book answers
#### Constraints
- The design presumes students can gather information from sources outside regular coursework, including site visits and interviews with practitioners

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students, individually or in teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent information acquisition and synthesis across disciplines; tolerating open-ended problems with multiple defensible conclusions
