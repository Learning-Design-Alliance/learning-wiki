# Schwa Instruction

## Description
Teach children about schwa (/ ə/), the unaccented vowel sound that has been emptied of its identity and can be described as a lazy vowel. Explain that some vowel sounds have the stuffing taken out of them when they are unaccented. After students spell a word such as prob-lem, a-dept, or com-mit, they can say the word naturally and mark the syllable that has a schwa.
