# Address identified barriers to enhance adherence to online technology use in health professional education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research and practice focus on identifying and addressing barriers to online technology use, since doing so may optimize adherence and enhance learning outcomes. As the authors state, "Addressing such barriers may optimize adherence to the use of online technologies, enhancing learning outcomes." Barriers identified include internet connection difficulties, insufficiently interactive material, and preference for paper-based materials.
