Use the exploration strategy: have students discuss, criticize and review ideas in the material before seeking knowledge¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The exploration strategy has the teacher treat information as a formation trial to reach new knowledge, letting students guess, shape assumptions and go beyond the presented material. In this study the researcher "gave the students the opportunity to discuss opinions, ideas and beliefs that were mentioned in the material" and to criticize and review them, which the author credits for the significant gains in analytical reading and linguistic intelligence.

Requirements¶

  • Requires directed exploration procedures that require pre-thinking to get knowledge instead of getting lost in parts, as the researcher describes

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated only with second middle grade female students in one Najran school; the author recommends further studies on different school stages, other subjects and on both genders
