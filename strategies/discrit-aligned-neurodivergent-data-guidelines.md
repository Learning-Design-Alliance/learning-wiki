# DisCrit-aligned guidelines for neurodivergent demographic data collection and representation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors translate tenets of DisCrit into recommendations for neurodivergent demographic data collection, to be used alongside the descriptivist approach. Core suggestions include that "scholars create open-ended demographic questions to encourage participants to use their own words in their description of identity", include disability or neurodivergent identity items in all kinds of studies, involve neurodivergent scholars on research teams, take an intracategorical approach, and prioritize harm reduction.
