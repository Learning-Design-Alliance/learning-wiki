# Research recommendations: broaden program descriptions, account for prior experience and context, define outcomes, and use longitudinal mixed-methods designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that future research broaden scope to include detailed descriptions of program content and pedagogical approaches; account for principals' prior experiences, recruitment and selection criteria, and district context; better define outcome measures and include a broader spectrum of outcomes; take a longitudinal view; attend to implementation; and use mixed methods skillfully. It urges that "experimental designs can be strengthened by qualitative data about the program, its implementation, and the comparison group's experiences."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must provide detailed information about candidates, program content, and comparison groups to sort out which features are associated with which outcomes
#### Constraints
- The report notes that how formal learning opportunities interact with principals' prior knowledge and experiences is rarely addressed in the research, and no single study can address all interpretive challenges

### Target Learners
- principals
- aspiring principals

### Target Learning Goals
- leadership knowledge and skills
- effective leadership practices
