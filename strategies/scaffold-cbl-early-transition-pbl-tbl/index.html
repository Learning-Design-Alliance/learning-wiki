Scaffold method choice by student progression: CBL early, transitioning to PBL and TBL¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q3 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend a "scaffolded integration of these methods": utilizing "Case-Based Learning (CBL) in early undergraduate years to introduce clinical contexts in a structured, low-stakes manner." As students progress from novice to advanced beginner, curricula can "transition to PBL and TBL to challenge students with higher-level ambiguity and complex group dynamics", aligning pedagogical complexity with students' developmental trajectory.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Curricula must sequence methods to match students' developmental trajectory from novice to advanced beginner

Constraints¶

  • Recommendation is based on the authors' synthesis of comparative effects rather than a direct test of sequenced curricula

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate nursing students progressing through program years

Target Learning Goals¶

  • clinical context acquisition
  • problem-solving abilities
  • communication skills

Claims¶

  • CBL, PBL, and TBL each significantly improve nursing students' problem-solving abilities [+W]
  • Modern teaching methods significantly improve nursing students' problem-solving abilities (SMD = 1.05) [+W]
  • PBL and TBL significantly improve nursing students' self-directed learning [+W]
  • Of 22 included studies on active learning in public health education, 11 used PBL only, 6 used TBL, 3 integrated PBL and CBL, and 2 used CBL only [+W]
  • TBL alone and TBL combined with other methods both outperform LBL, with no significant difference between the two TBL formats [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt PBL in OB/GYN undergraduate curricula to enhance clinical competence and satisfaction
  • Align teaching method with competency gap and prioritize TBL for communication-focused courses
  • Weekly trainee-led CBL sessions with case presentation, self-directed research between sessions, and attendance and utility tracking
  • Integrate TBL with real-world clinical scenarios and clear discussion guidelines
  • Integrate TBL with other teaching methods and modern educational technologies
  • Combine CBL and PBL approaches in clinical teaching settings to target both theory and practical outcomes
  • Introduce and promote PBL pedagogy in undergraduate pharmacology education
