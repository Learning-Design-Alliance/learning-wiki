# Explore unexplored taxonomy quadrants by combining artificial characters with digital media and augmented reality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The author recommends that video producers and educators move beyond replicating the traditional classroom by exploring the third and fourth quadrants of the taxonomy. As stated: "it is worth exploring the combination of artiﬁcial characters with digital media, which might be facilitated by video-game development toolkits", and augmented-reality technologies could let digital characters manipulate physical objects. A screencast of slides might also be enhanced with a drawing board or talking-head feed to add personality.
