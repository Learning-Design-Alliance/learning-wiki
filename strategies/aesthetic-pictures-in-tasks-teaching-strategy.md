# Integrate aesthetic pictures into physics tasks at the start of a lesson and for application and transfer

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (1 for, 6 mixed) · 8 studies (7 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 3 of 8 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends overcoming the dichotomy between decorative and instructional pictures by using aesthetic pictures inside picture-related tasks, so they serve an instructional purpose beyond decoration. The authors see two stages: tasks with aesthetic pictures "can be used at the beginning of a lesson or teaching sequence to provide immersion in a topic and spark interest", and later "APs can be applied in tasks for application and transfer".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pictures must be selected according to the article's aesthetic, affective and practical criteria and embedded in tasks where learners identify picture elements, link them to physics objects, recognize the phenomenon in a second picture, and summarize it.
#### Constraints
- The study examined photographs of natural scenes in geometrical optics only; the authors note learning outcomes were not measured and call for studies on cognitive variables and learning.

### Target Learners
- junior high school students in physics (grades 7 and 8)

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement with and understanding of geometrical optics phenomena

## Claims

- [Aesthetic pictures selected by research-based criteria are rated significantly better than classroom experiment pictures in physics tasks on the same content, with large effect sizes for three of four topics](../claims/aesthetic-pictures-tasks-rated-better-than-classroom-experiment-pictures.md) [+M]
- [Female students rated aesthetic pictures higher than male students (d around 0.4), while motivation and interest in physics showed only weak correlations with aesthetic ratings](../claims/gender-motivation-interest-predictors-picture-ratings.md) [~W]
- [The difference in aesthetic and affective ratings between aesthetic and classroom experiment pictures interacted significantly with picture topic](../claims/picture-category-topic-interaction-perception-ratings.md) [~W]
- [No significant difference in aesthetic or affective ratings between the aesthetic and the classroom experiment picture for the spectrum topic](../claims/spectrum-topic-no-significant-ap-cep-difference.md) [~W]
- [Related decorative pictures do not enhance transfer performance in either experiment (null results for correctness and metacognitive monitoring)](../claims/related-decorative-pictures-no-transfer-effect.md) [~W]
- [Interesting but irrelevant details can impair comprehension and transfer, with effects that vary with the material and the learner](../claims/seductive-details-effect.md) [~S]
