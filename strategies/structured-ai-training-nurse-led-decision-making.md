# Implement structured AI training programmes and nurse-led AI decision-making to enhance AI competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends hospitals "implement structured training programmes to enhance nurses' AIC" and that "Nursing administrators can implement an AI‐assisted but nurse‐led decision‐making model to enhance AIC and decrease AIU." Training should include enhanced technical training and opportunities for clinical practice with AI in real-world environments. Nurses must also participate in AI design and implementation to ensure effective integration in clinical care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Hospitals must provide systematic support including technical training and clinical practice opportunities
#### Constraints
- Excluded nurses with prior AI training longer than 3 days, so findings may not generalise to highly trained nurses

### Target Learners
- registered nurses in clinical practice
- nursing administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- AI competence
- technological adaptability
- confidence in AI use
