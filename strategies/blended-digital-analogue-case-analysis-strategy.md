# Integrate web-based case analysis tools into blended learning combining digital case-based practice with face-to-face teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial findings of equivalent diagnostic outcomes, higher digital usability ratings, and domain-specific strengths of each modality, the authors conclude that "these findings support the integration of web-based tools in blended learning concepts combining digital case-based practice with traditional face-to-face teaching in orthodontic education." Students similarly "expressed a clear preference for further digital integration, including remote access to digital case analyses and hybrid teaching formats combining digital practice cases with face-to-face discussion."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Personal guidance and feedback from lecturers, which the authors state cannot be replaced by digitisation
#### Constraints
- The evidence base is a single-centre trial with a small sample over one semester; only Kirkpatrick Levels 1 and 2 were assessed

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Orthodontic diagnostic competence and treatment planning

## Claims

- [Web-based Orthotrainer training produced diagnostic performance equivalent to analogue case analysis training across training sessions and the final module exam](../claims/orthotrainer-equivalent-diagnostic-performance.md) [+W]
