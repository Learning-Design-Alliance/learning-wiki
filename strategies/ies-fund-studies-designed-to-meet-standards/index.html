Continue conducting and funding studies designed to meet evidence standards and tie decisions directly to evidence¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's forward-looking recommendation is that rigorous education research may continue to grow as the Institute of Education Sciences keeps conducting and funding studies designed to meet WWC standards and as more decisions are directly tied to evidence. The article states: "As IES continues to conduct and fund studies designed to meet standards, and more decisions are directly tied to evidence, the body of rigorous education research may continue to grow."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Funding bodies able to commission studies designed to meet systematic review standards; decision processes linked to evidence

Constraints¶

  • Growth in rigorous research is stated as a possibility, not a guaranteed outcome

Target Learners¶

  • education researchers, funders, and policymakers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • expanding the body of rigorous causal evidence for education decisions

Related Strategies¶

  • Weigh explicit considerations before deciding to fund or conduct a systematic review
