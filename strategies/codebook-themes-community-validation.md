# Codebook development, theme generation, and community validation of findings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Phase 3 analysis guidance has teams familiarize themselves with transcripts, create an initial codebook documenting 'each code's meaning and application', and code with an Excel template or qualitative software, revising codes as new ones emerge. Themes are generated by grouping codes, refining and interrogating them, defining each theme, and writing up findings with illustrative quotes. Step 5 states: 'Always share findings with the community representatives who contributed' so partners 'feel their contributions are truly represented.'
