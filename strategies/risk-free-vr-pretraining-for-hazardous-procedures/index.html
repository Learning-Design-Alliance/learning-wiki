Use risk-free high-fidelity VR simulation to complement physical training for hazardous clinical procedures¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that virtual simulation complement physical training for procedures involving radiation exposure and privacy-sensitive examinations. It argues the platform provides "unlimited, repeatable, and safe training environments" so trainees can master complex procedures without expensive specialized facilities, addressing bottlenecks from limited lab access, onsite trainer availability, and safety risks of live radioactive sources.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • High procedural asset fidelity (anatomically precise models and instrumentation) so spatial orientation relative to organs at risk can be learned

Constraints¶

  • Stated as a complement to, not a replacement for, the current physical HDR training system

Target Learners¶

  • medical trainees in radiation oncology

Target Learning Goals¶

  • complex procedural skill mastery before live clinical practice
