# Hoax Website Analysis

## Description
Learners examine famous website hoaxes to identify clues that suggest the site is not genuine. They look for inconsistencies, lack of corroboration, and other red flags.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to examples of famous website hoaxes and a framework for guiding the analysis (e.g., a checklist or rubric).
#### Constraints
- The activity may be less effective if learners are not familiar with the context or background of the hoax.

### Target Learning Goals

- Sharpens critical thinking and digital literacy by engaging learners in detecting deceptive tactics and misinformation.

### Personalization
- The complexity of the hoax websites and the evaluation criteria can be adjusted based on the learners' skill level.
