# Adopt the Ethics Quarter in healthcare organizations and continue ethics work with regular continuing approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that healthcare organizations adopt the EQ systematically to support nurse managers' ethical activities, since organizations "currently provide suboptimal levels of support for ethics for NMs". After the EQ, organizations could continue ethics work monthly with different continuing approaches such as multi-professional committees for consultation and discussion of ethical issues, ethics reflections, or short daily reflections among the work team. Education should start with those at higher levels of management, followed by unit-level managers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational commitment to systematic ethics support structures
#### Constraints
- Managers work in teams but are often trained as individuals; training the whole management team simultaneously may be more effective

### Target Learners
- Nurse managers at all levels of healthcare organizations

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained ethical activity profile and ethical culture development
