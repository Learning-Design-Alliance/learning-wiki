# Multistage consensus process combining nominal-group scope definition, two-stage blinded screening, a modified Delphi process with anonymous voting plus structured online discussion, and blinded categorisation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a five-stage process (2019–2024) for building a shared core assessment set across programmes: scope definition, Delphi preparation with initial screening, three modified Delphi rounds, categorisation by intended student engagement, and creation of educational material. Anonymous Excel voting was followed by ZOOM and Padlet discussions, and the authors report that "the anonymous voting process and subsequent open, trust-driven discussions seemed to support loyalty among lecturers and might have contributed to maintaining a high response rate". Each programme appointed one representative, with all lecturers invited to discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One designated representative per institution holding a faculty position and at least one year of neurological physiotherapy teaching experience
#### Constraints
- Maximum of nine votes per step, corresponding to the nine Austrian programmes; one representative missed the first vote due to sick leave

### Target Learners
- physiotherapy lecturers and programme representatives in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- harmonising entry-level curricula on standardised assessment use
