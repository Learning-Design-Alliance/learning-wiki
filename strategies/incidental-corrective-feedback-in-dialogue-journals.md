# Provide incidental corrective feedback through clarification requests, recasts, and a P.S.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The author recommends gentle, incidental corrective feedback that preserves the journal's communicative nature: clarification and confirmation requests such as "What do you mean by….?", recasting errors in the teacher's response (responding to "I go the mall last weekend" with "You went to the mall last weekend. What did you buy?"), and writing corrections as a "P.S." to the student, which underscores the primary importance of meaningful dialogue and the secondary importance of correct grammar usage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The journal must already be established as a venue for meaningful communication before corrections are added
#### Constraints
- Corrections are less likely to hinder communication if grammar correction is withheld during the initial get-to-know-you phase

### Target Learners
- adult ESL students

### Target Learning Goals
- noticing and correcting language errors while maintaining authentic written dialogue
