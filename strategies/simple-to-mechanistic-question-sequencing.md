# Sequence case questions from simple to complex, emphasizing molecular-mechanism questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends ordering case questions so simpler ones lead groups toward complex explanations, with mechanistic questions central so students can "clinically explain as a physician" the immunological basis of answers. Questions are designed not to give away answers, and bonus questions not on the student handout keep post-group questioning fresh and push on-the-spot thinking. In the sample case, questions move from TLR activation to cytokines, leukocyte migration, fever, leukocytosis, and septic shock.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions must not easily give away the answer
- Faculty facilitators hold bonus questions not included in the student preview
