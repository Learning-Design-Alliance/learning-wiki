# Sequence efficient training so cadets observe expert action videos before instruction and simulator practice, then self-record practice videos for comparison

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-phase training recipe implemented with the accelerated training system. In Phase 1 cadets "initially viewing expert operation videos and replicating the expert's actions on the training simulator" before the instructor teaches key movement points. In Phase 2 cadets replicate expert actions while performing their own tasks with real-time instructor corrections. In Phase 3 they practice independently, "recording their own practice videos for comparison and error analysis against the expert's videos, and receiving timely feedback".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to recorded expert-pilot operation videos and simulator equipment for each training phase
- A consistent instructor providing real-time corrections and standardized scoring
#### Constraints
- Plateau effects emerged for complex tasks such as landing and carrier landing, where improvements were slower for both groups

### Target Learners
- flight cadets learning perceptual-motor skills

### Target Learning Goals
- faster acquisition of takeoff, flight control, landing and carrier landing maneuvers
