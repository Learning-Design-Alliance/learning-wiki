# Higher education institutions should introduce training programmes for managing large virtual classes and designing virtual course content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that teachers received no prior training yet were required to move large classes online within a short period, the article recommends that higher education institutions in Ghana introduce training programmes aimed at aiding teachers to manage large virtual classes and appropriately design and deliver the content of virtual course materials. Internationally, it supports the call for university teachers to personally seek continual updating of skills and competency in virtual delivery because the approach is hinged on evolving technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address both class management and the design and delivery of virtual course materials
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a study limited to a Ghanaian sample during the COVID period

### Target Learners
- university teachers of large virtual classes

### Target Learning Goals
- virtual class management
- virtual course design and delivery
