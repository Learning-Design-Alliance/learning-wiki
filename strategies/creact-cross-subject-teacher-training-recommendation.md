# Extend CREACT practices across subjects and provide teacher training in the technique

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using CREACT and related programs beyond science, and building teacher capacity to deliver it. They state it is presumed that utilization of CREACT "in Science and Technology, Social Sciences, Turkish, Mathematics, Art, Music, etc. classrooms will increase student creativity and other high-order thinking skills and academic success." They recommend in-service training seminars for practising teachers on how to use the technique, and sample practices plus theoretical information for pre-service teachers. They also recommend further studies in different courses and samples, and qualitative or mixed-design studies to understand whether the technique is practical.
