# Incorporate focused discussion of adoption and explicit review of counseling components into reproductive health curriculum

## Description
Based on their results and those of Lupi et al., the authors recommend that as research defines the ideal curriculum, future interventions should incorporate focused discussion of adoption and more explicit review of the individual components of pregnancy options counseling. They argue meeting APGO objectives for non-directive counseling requires comprehensive curricular emphasis on all three options—parenting, abortion, and adoption—and that didactics, workshops, small-group sessions, and simulations will be necessary where clinical experience cannot be required.
