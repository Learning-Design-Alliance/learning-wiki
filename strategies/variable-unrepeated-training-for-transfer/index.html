Use training regimens with unrepeated, variable situations (like action video games) to foster transferable improvements in probabilistic inference¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends, on the basis of its training study and mechanism account, that training regimens likely to produce broad transfer are those in which "situations are rarely, if ever, repeated", forcing learners to acquire "how to rapidly and accurately learn the statistics on the fly and how to accumulate this evidence more efficiently". Action video games exemplify this regimen, and 50 hours of such training produced measurable gains in evidence use in non-gamers.
