# Attend to epistemic stances toward group work as one way to support productive and equitable group work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that instructors and researchers attend to students' epistemic stances toward group work—"views about how knowledge will be generated and applied in a group"—because misalignment of these stances can undermine equitable participation even when the group shares a cohesive epistemological frame. Identifying stances allows investigation of "the interactions between epistemology and social dynamics" and the social positioning of group members, offering a lever for fostering equitable group discourse beyond standard role assignment or group composition strategies.
