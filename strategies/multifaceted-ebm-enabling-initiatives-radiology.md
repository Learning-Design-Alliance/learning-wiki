# Multifaceted EBM-enabling initiatives for radiology technologists: training, resources, dedicated time and institutional leadership support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a bundle of organisational and educational strategies to raise EBM utilisation among radiology technologists, since knowledge, time, support and resources were each independently associated with better practice. It states: "Key approaches may include integrating EBM training into curricula and lifetime education, providing resources and internet access, allotting dedicated time for searching and appraisal, fostering institutional leadership support and promoting a culture valuing current best evidence."

## Design Implications
