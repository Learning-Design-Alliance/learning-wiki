# Use a Socratic-type prompting script that asks the AI to provoke reflection rather than deliver ready-made answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's third session opened with a detailed prompt instructing the chatbot to adopt a Socratic approach: "in every interaction, ask questions that encourage the student to reflect on their existing knowledge, rather than offering purely academic ready-made answers". The prompt also asked the chatbot to provoke critical thinking, help refine understanding, and include valid, close-to-life examples and analogies. This strategy operationalises the agents-to-think-with role and produced dialogue in which ChatGPT probed the student's answers step by step.
