# Add an interim or exit meeting with ongoing supervisory support to consolidate the personal learning plan

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that PLP support should not end with the induction meeting: an interim or exit meeting later in the rotation, and ongoing supervisory support more generally, may help students apply their plans and consolidate learning. It reports that "Students reported that an interim or exit meeting might have helped in the application of the learning plan" and concludes that "ongoing support may be an approach to produce an enhanced academic performance", while noting further research is required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty availability for additional individualised meetings beyond the initial induction meeting
#### Constraints
- Educators need to consider the significant time investment by faculty members to provide this level of individualised educational support

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students on clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- applying and adjusting a personal learning plan during the rotation
- consolidating learning approaches before rotation completion
