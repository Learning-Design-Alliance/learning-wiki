Follow key steps to conduct RCTs and address common questions and concerns¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide provides procedural support for leaders undertaking an RCT: it "outlines the key steps to conduct RCTs and responds to common questions and concerns about RCTs." This anticipates stakeholder resistance and gives leaders a ready sequence of actions for designing and running a trial within a district context.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Leaders need a planned program or policy to evaluate and answers ready for common stakeholder concerns about RCTs

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • district and school leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conducting randomized controlled trials
  • responding to stakeholder questions and concerns about RCTs

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed opportunistic RCTs into planned policies or programs
  • Use a checklist of key questions when conducting opportunistic experiments
