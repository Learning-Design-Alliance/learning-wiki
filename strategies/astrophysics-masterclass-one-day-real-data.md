# Run a one-day astrophysics masterclass in which high school students analyze real research data in a university research setting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Astrophysics Masterclass is a one-day outreach event during which high school students experience research methods in astrophysics by working in a university computer lab on real data from professional observing facilities. The article states it 'consists in a one-day outreach event during which high school students experience the research methods in the astrophysics field', allowing participants to work 'on real research data in a real research center'. Students characterize galaxies by measuring brightness, distance, luminosity and mass, culminating in identifying a type 1 AGN.

## Design Implications

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 16-18 with basic physics and math background, selected by their teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- experiencing scientific research methods in astrophysics
- understanding how science works and encouraging STEM careers
