# Disseminate principles knowledge alongside curriculum details so faculty can reinvent effectively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dissemination strategy in which the research community shares and studies the underlying ideas behind a curriculum, not just the finished materials. The authors argue that if "faculty are going to modify curriculum effectively, they need to understand both what works (details) as well as why it works (principles)." Without principles knowledge, faculty reinvent innovations in ways that drop essential features; with it, they are more likely to incorporate those features into their own reinventions or inventions.
