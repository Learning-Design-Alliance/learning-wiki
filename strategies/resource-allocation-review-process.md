# Conduct a resource allocation review to connect identified needs, evidence-based strategies, and resource allocation methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The resource allocation review (RAR) is a systematized process for SEA leaders to support LEAs in reviewing resource planning and alignment to address inequities. The document states the RAR "involves establishing connections between identified needs, evidence-based strategies, and resource allocation methods used to support the design and delivery of programs." It was developed through a four-year design process, field research, and a pilot audit by the U.S. Department of Education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SEA capacity to examine per-pupil expenditure reports and revenue allocations required by ESSA
#### Constraints
- Users are encouraged to refine and customize this guidance to fit their context

### Target Learners
- All students in LEAs and schools participating in the RAR

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable distribution of resources to improve student outcomes and the educational experience
