# Enlarge lab groups and add structured communication tasks to foster peer communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Prompted by E-CLASS findings of a lack of perceived importance of peer communication, the course added tasks focused on letting students brainstorm, discuss, and present ideas to peers, and enlarged groups: experiments use three students and projects up to four, with tutorial sessions ranging from 3 to 12 students. "Emphasizing the importance of structured group dynamics, we implemented mechanisms to ensure equitable participation, such as rotating roles during sessions or assigning a student as a communication facilitator to streamline intra-group exchanges or we reserved time for students' presentations to peers."
