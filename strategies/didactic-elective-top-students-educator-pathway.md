# Offer a didactic elective on education to top anatomy students as an early step on a medical-educator pathway

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practice points recommend that "Providing didactic series on education to the top students of the anatomy class pay dividends both to the school and the students", and that teaching peers at an undergraduate level "may be an exciting method for fostering a medical educator pathway". The elective exposes interested students to advanced anatomy beyond preclinical classes while preparing them to assist faculty teaching, addressing a local shortage of qualified anatomy instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Invitation limited to students who achieved a grade of 85 (out of 100) or above in the anatomy class
- A series of eight sessions with hands-on dissection, ultrasound, and guest lectures

- 

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students with strong anatomy performance

### Target Learning Goals
- teaching and didactic skills
- advanced anatomy knowledge
- professional growth toward educator roles
