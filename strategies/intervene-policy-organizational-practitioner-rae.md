## Description
The article recommends direct intervention at policy, organizational and practitioner levels to reduce and eliminate RAE-driven social inequality, especially during athletes' developmental periods. It states that "To reduce and eliminate this social inequality from influencing athletes' experiences, especially within developmental periods, direct policy, organizational and practitioner intervention is required." The recommendation follows from the meta-analytic evidence that RAEs are pervasive and context-dependent.
