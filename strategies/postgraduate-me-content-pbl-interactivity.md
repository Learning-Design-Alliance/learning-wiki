# Content domain: favour problem-based learning and interactivity, with modules built on cognitive load principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 6 studies (3 causal, 2 quant-synthesis, 1 theoretical), `q3`–`q4` · 2 of 6 report an effect size

## Description
The content domain is the aggregate material used to build lessons. "Problem-based learning was favoured by 13 articles as the best way to incorporate motivation and better understanding", and "Introducing interactivity to encourage higher-order thinking was also important (n = 16 articles)". Learning modules should end with summaries, consist of short paragraphs, state a timeline, and use milestones, with cognitive load principles guiding design.
