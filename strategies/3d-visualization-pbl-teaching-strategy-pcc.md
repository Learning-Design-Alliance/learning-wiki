# Combine 3D visualization reconstruction software with PBL case discussion for teaching rare complex tumors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a five-hour instructional sequence: residents receive case materials two days before class, install the Vitaworks 3D anatomy software on mobile phones, and use "measurement and segmentation" to explore tumor-adjacency relationships. The teaching physician then poses case-based questions (diagnosis clarity, fluid expansion, surgical approach, conversion to open surgery, complication management) that residents discuss before guided explanation with 3D images.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to 3D reconstruction software (Vitaworks) and pre-class orientation to its operation
- Specific PCC case materials distributed before the session
- A teaching physician to pose questions and guide discussion with 3D reconstructed images
#### Constraints
- Stated as still "at an early developmental phase, highlighting the necessity for further refinement and optimization"

### Target Learners
- resident physicians specializing in urology

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding pheochromocytoma diagnosis, surgical planning, and complication management
