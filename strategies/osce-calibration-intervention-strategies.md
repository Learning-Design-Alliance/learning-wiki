# Pilot calibration interventions targeting social/communication self-assessment: facilitated video review with checklists, vignette calibration with expert anchors, and pre- to post-feedback self-ratings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends feasible, theory-aligned steps to improve social-domain calibration, to be "piloted and evaluated longitudinally before broader adoption". Facilitated sessions have "students review their own OSCE communication-station recordings alongside examiner checklists and global ratings, debriefing discrepancies with structured prompts". In parallel, standardized vignette calibration has students rate patient-interaction videos and compare judgments with expert-consensus anchors, and a pre-OSCE self-prediction followed by post-feedback reassessment tracks within-student calibration shifts.
