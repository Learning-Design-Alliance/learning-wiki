# Appointment-based one-to-one tuition open to all level 1 physics and astronomy students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A proactive extra academic support measure in which "All students would be able to make use an appointment-based one-to-one tuition system, run by a few members of staff". It replaces traditional revision classes and large-class tutorials, which the article describes as notoriously badly attended and typically attended by the academically committed, and is presented as part of an integrated whole-class approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A few members of staff to run the appointment-based one-to-one tuition system.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the approach appears to contradict advice that "effective learning support is best offered within the curriculum", and mitigates this by presenting the support as part of an integrated whole-class approach.

### Target Learners
- first-year physics and astronomy undergraduates, particularly marginal-pass students

### Target Learning Goals
- improving the grades of marginal-pass students
- successful completion of level 1 physics
