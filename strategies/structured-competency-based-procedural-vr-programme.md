# Structured, competency-based procedural VR training programme for robotic surgery curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends developing robotic surgical training programmes around structured procedural VR simulation. Training "followed a competency-based approach whereby participants were encouraged to complete tasks in sequential manner", combining familiarisation on generic robotic tasks with randomised procedural or basic VR training over five weeks. The authors state these results offer important considerations for the development of robotic surgical training programmes and that procedural training may motivate participants through clinical relevance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured programme of training based on simulator training modules with sequential task completion
- Proctoring or ad hoc guidance from an expert surgeon during training sessions
#### Constraints
- Development of objective competency criteria will be required to support the integration of procedural VR into robotic curricula
- Further work is required to evaluate the role of procedural-based VR for more advanced surgical skills training

### Target Learners
- Novice surgical trainees without prior robotic surgery experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Technical skills transfer to clinical robotic surgical tasks
