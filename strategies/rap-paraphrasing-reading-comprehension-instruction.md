# RAP paraphrasing instruction for reading comprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes the RAP (Read, Ask myself, Put into one's own words) paraphrasing procedure, in which the teacher models paraphrasing by reading sentences aloud, paraphrasing them, and asking students to say sentences in their own words without using the original wording. Students practice with sentences and paragraphs, use dictionaries for synonyms, and share paraphrases with classmates. They also paraphrase short stories sentence by sentence at home using a table format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who models paraphrasing and cues student activity
- Sentences and paragraphs for practice
- A dictionary for finding synonyms
#### Constraints
