# Develop a validated learning-orientations survey with deliberate hypotheses, multiple statistical validity tests, and additional achievement-goal dimensions for future IPLS studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future studies generate a more robust feedback survey designed a priori with hypotheses in mind, following Hazari et al.'s approach of "explicitly designed a ... learning-orientations survey from hypotheses generated from in-depth interviews, using multiple statistical tests to confirm the validity of the survey's results." They also suggest adding achievement-goal dimensions not covered in this study, such as approach vs. avoidance motivations, to deepen future analyses of students' learning orientations in introductory physics course populations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A survey deliberately designed from hypotheses generated through in-depth interviews, with multiple statistical tests confirming validity, replacing reliance on single-item short-answer responses.
#### Constraints
- The strategy addresses the current study's limitation that its categories were derived from a feedback survey not designed a priori as a validated instrument; applicability is framed for IPLS-esque course populations.

### Target Learners
- life science majors in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring students' achievement goals and learning orientations to inform IPLS course transformation
