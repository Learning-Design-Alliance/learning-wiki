# Story Telling (Pre-Reading)

## Description
The instructor prepares a short, personalized narrative related to the reading topic. The story should be creative, engaging, and told enthusiastically, ideally including elements of suspense and humor. Sharing this personal narrative helps create interest, activate prior knowledge, and set the stage for the reading material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a well-crafted, engaging personal narrative and effective storytelling skills from the instructor.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the story and the instructor's storytelling ability. Some learners may not connect with the personal narrative.
