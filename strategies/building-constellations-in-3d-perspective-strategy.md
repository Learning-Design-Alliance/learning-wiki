# Building Constellations in 3D: an astronomy activity introducing perspective-taking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The second main bi-communal activity introduced "the concept of viewing objects (in this case, a constellation), or indeed situations, from different angles". By physically viewing a constellation from different viewpoints, children experience how perspective changes with viewpoint; the authors extend this metaphor to situations between the two communities, using astronomy content as a vehicle for perspective-taking.
