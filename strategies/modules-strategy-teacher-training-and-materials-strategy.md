# Recommendations: train teachers on the modules strategy and supply supporting textbooks and references

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's results, the authors recommend institutional actions to spread the modules strategy: holding "a number of the workshops to train the teachers on the modern strategy of the modules", reviewing and modifying scientific texts to include module-compatible aspects and reduce content load, supplying references and books that enrich the science subject, building modules for other units and subjects, and conducting similar studies in domains such as physics and mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- ministry of education involvement in courses and workshops; availability of textbooks and references that facilitate module-based teaching
#### Constraints
- the recommendations follow from a single two-school study in Mafraq, Jordan, as the article itself notes the lack of closely related prior research

### Target Learners
- seventh-grade primary students; science teachers in training

### Target Learning Goals
- improved teaching of the science subject through modern, self-learning-oriented methods
