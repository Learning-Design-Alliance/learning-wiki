# Future AI literacy scale research should test cross-cultural validity and measurement error, open raw data, and develop more performance-based scales

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's general recommendations for future research: "Cross-cultural validity, measurement error, and floor and ceiling effects of the existing scales should be checked." It further recommends that authors make raw data open (enabling multiple group factor analyses), design performance-based scales for populations other than higher education students, cross-validate performance-based against self-report scales, and periodically review and update the quality assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Raw data availability is needed for multiple group factor analyses and cross-cultural comparisons
#### Constraints
- The review notes its own limitations: a single author, and a search limited to Scopus and arXiv, possibly missing grey-literature scales

### Target Learners
- general population
- higher education students
- secondary education students
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- improved measurement quality of AI literacy assessment
