Direct future research and development toward under-represented genres, languages, and macro-level strategy support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review maps the state of the art and specifies directions for further research and development, addressing researchers, policymakers, tool developers, and practitioners of writing instruction. Because tools supporting "the development of writing strategies and encourage self-monitoring" are infrequent, and non-English genres and languages are under-represented, development effort should target these gaps rather than replicate the dominant English argumentative-essay, micro-revision profile.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tool developers and researchers should consider the reviewed feature framework when designing or evaluating new writing-support technologies

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • secondary and higher education students writing in under-represented genres and languages

Target Learning Goals¶

  • macro-level text quality
  • writing strategy development
  • self-monitoring

Claims¶

  • Available tools are imbalanced with regard to supported languages and genres, with argumentative essay writing in English over-represented [+M]
  • Automated support for micro-level revision targeting factual knowledge is well represented, whereas tools supporting writing strategies and self-monitoring for macro-level text quality are infrequent [+M]
