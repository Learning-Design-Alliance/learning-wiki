# Use narrative guidance features to guide learners to the internal structure and content of a multimedia narrative

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
One output from the Galapagos analysis was a set of guidelines for multimedia designers recommending the use of narrative guidance features to guide learners to the internal structure and content of the given narrative, its supporting arguments and evidence. The guideline specifies including a Goal to provide a coherent framework, Reminders of the goal to keep the structure in focus, a Guide to sub-goals or essential components, and a Model Answer providing formative feedback on learners' own accounts.
