# Response Devices

## Description
Response devices allow learners to anonymously share answers with the whole class. They provide a way to encourage whole-group participation and collect formative data. Instructors can present a problem, have learners work on it individually (e.g., on whiteboards), and then share their answers using a response system. The instructor and class then view the responses to assess overall comprehension and inform next steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Response system (e.g., clickers, mobile app, whiteboards), structured prompts, time for individual work and sharing
#### Constraints
- Requires a reliable response system, potential for technical issues, may be perceived as superficial if not integrated into meaningful discussion
