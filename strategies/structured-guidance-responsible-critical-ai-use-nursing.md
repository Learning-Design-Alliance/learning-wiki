# Integrate structured guidance on responsible, critical AI use alongside self-directed learning approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing programs combine learner-autonomy pedagogies with structured guidance on AI use. It states that "educational programs may benefit from integrating structured guidance on the responsible, critical, and contextually appropriate use of AI tools, alongside efforts to strengthen self-directed learning." This includes operationalizing AI use within clinical rotations with explicit justification and appraisal of AI-generated recommendations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development and curricular structures that support AI integration
- Access to appropriate digital tools
#### Constraints
- Overreliance on AI without critical evaluation may contribute to superficial learning or reduced independent thinking

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical AI literacy
- clinical reasoning
- responsible AI use in clinical practice
