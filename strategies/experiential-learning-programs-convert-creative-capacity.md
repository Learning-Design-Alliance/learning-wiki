# Invest in experiential learning programs to convert creative capacity into organizational effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that experiential learning partially mediates the creativity–maturity relationship, the article recommends that organizations "invest in experiential learning programs that include interactive training, simulations, real-world problem-solving, and reflective practices" to transform creative capacity into tangible organizational effectiveness. It also encourages fostering a culture of creativity through structured initiatives such as innovation labs and collaborative projects, and strengthening feedback systems and long-term employee development.

## Design Implications

### Context
