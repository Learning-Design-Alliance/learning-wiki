# Collect anonymous user questionnaires at exhibitions and schools to evaluate the games' educational effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The games are "accompanied by short questionnaires, handed-out at exhibitions and during visits to schools as well as online", collecting anonymous feedback particularly from teachers and students. The stated aim is "to evaluate the success of the games among the users and in particular about the effectiveness of their educational aspects." As a future step the authors plan a proper analysis of the feedback to link the games to specific elements of formal education and improve their integration within the syllabus.
