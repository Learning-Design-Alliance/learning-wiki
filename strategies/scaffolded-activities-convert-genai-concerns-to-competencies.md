# Design authentic, scaffolded activities that convert students' intuitive GenAI concerns into explicit ethical competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends designing authentic, scaffolded assessment activities informed by students' existing GenAI use and concerns. These activities "aim to convert students' intuitive concerns into explicit competencies in ethical decision-making, prompt engineering and verification," moving learners through staged tasks from human-only work to AI-integrated work with critical reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be informed by qualitative evidence of students' actual GenAI use and concerns
#### Constraints
- Grounded in journalism education contexts where GenAI raises integrity, authorship and professional-value concerns

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate journalism students

### Target Learning Goals
- Ethical decision-making
- Prompt engineering
- Verification
