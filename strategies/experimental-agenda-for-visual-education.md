# Direct experimental research toward the materials, organization, and attention problems of visual methods

## Description
Freeman recommends a program of experimentation in place of propaganda: determine which subject matter is best adapted to visual presentation, work out the span of attention of pupils systematically, find the best methods of securing and directing attention, study the rapidity of presentation and amount of detail, and determine how much repetition and review secures permanence of learning. He argues films must furnish intellectual stimulation and satisfaction in addition to sensory appeal, adapted to pupils' intellectual capacities, interests, and activities.
