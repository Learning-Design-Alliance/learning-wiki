# Enhance lacrimal irrigation simulation training with force-sensitive gloves, longitudinal validation, and motion analytics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendations for simulation-based procedural training: develop specialized training gloves with force-sensitive membranes to mimic mucosal resistance gradients; run longitudinal validation studies with larger samples to assess skill retention and clinical error rates over a 6-month period; and apply competency mapping using motion analytics, such as cannula path deviation metrics, to complement subjective assessments. They also suggest virtual reality and artificial intelligence could provide immersive simulations and real-time feedback for standardization.
