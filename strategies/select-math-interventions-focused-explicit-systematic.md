# Schools selecting Tier 2 mathematics programs should prioritize focused whole-number content and explicit, systematic instructional design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators adopting intervention programs examine whether candidates provide focused content coverage and an explicit and systematic instructional design approach, since these two components form the theoretical foundation of Fusion and mirror expert-panel recommendations. Schools can also support educators implementing programs lacking these features by collecting observation data on key instructional behaviors and linking findings to coaching, including support for "providing students opportunities to verbalize their mathematical thinking".
