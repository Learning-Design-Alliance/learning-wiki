# Strategic recommendations for policy makers: digital access plus pedagogical content, metacognition in the curriculum, constructive competition in maths, cooperative learning in reading and science, and family-involving support for disadvantaged students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distils its Random Forest findings into a set of policy recommendations for education authorities. It argues that "improving digital accessibility should not be limited to the provision of hardware, but should also be supported by the development of digital pedagogical content". It recommends integrating metacognitive skills into the curriculum through self-directed learning strategies, encouraging constructive competition in objectively assessed subjects such as mathematics while supporting cooperative learning in science and reading, and strengthening counselling and family-involving support systems for economically disadvantaged students.
