# Use progressive, structured EVH training with gradual introduction of case complexity and vein length to reduce learner stress and vein damage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends a procedure-based progressive surgical skills curriculum that begins by slowly introducing basic endoscopic skills, so learners improve hand-eye coordination and become versed in the fulcrum effect and depth perception. It advises that "the gradual introduction of two length and three length harvesting can allow operators to increase confidence and preserve vein integrity". Progressive learning under supervision is presented as reducing trainee stress and complications during the learning curve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured curriculum beginning with basic endoscopic skills before independent harvesting
- Training under proper supervision, especially for branch isolation
#### Constraints
- Learning for prolonged periods and training under direct supervision is not always practical in the clinical setting

### Target Learners
- Novice EVH practitioners in cardiothoracic surgery

### Target Learning Goals
- Psychomotor endoscopic surgical skills for vein harvesting
