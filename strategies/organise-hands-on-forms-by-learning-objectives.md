# Organise Embodied Hands-on and Cognitive Hands-on deliberately according to different learning objectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that design education organise the relationship between Embodied Hands-on and Cognitive Hands-on deliberately according to different learning objectives, rather than treating the forms as interchangeable or rejecting AI. Embodied Hands-on may more directly support real-world testing, while Cognitive Hands-on may strengthen comparison and judgement. The most urgent challenge is how process-oriented learning can be deliberately supported within human–AI collaborative workflows.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must remain involved in action, judgement, reflection, and refinement for the educational value of either form to be preserved
#### Constraints
- The framework underlying this recommendation is conceptual and not yet empirically tested

### Target Learners
- design students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- design judgement
- creative agency
- metacognitive awareness in human–AI collaboration
