# Teachers manage children's test-taking behavior by applying the CBT-Music program with defined supportive roles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that primary school teachers be enlightened on using CBT-Music to manage children's test-taking behavior, listing teacher roles: "always work with children and groups to improve mental health, encourage children to discuss emotions and experiences, help children define goals, plan action and gain insight, develop therapeutic processes, refer children to psychologists and other services, and take a holistic (mind and body) approach to mental health care." Community leaders are asked to assist by providing resources for implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training on the CBT-Music program; community leaders making available the resources needed for effective implementation.
#### Constraints
- The study did not factor in possible moderating effects of gender, age, and location, so generalizability should be done with caution.

### Target Learners
- Primary school children in basic science

### Target Learning Goals
- Managing test-taking behavior and test anxiety to support academic growth

## Claims

- [Music-based CBT significantly reduces test anxiety of primary school children in basic science at posttest compared with a control group](../claims/cbt-music-reduces-test-anxiety-posttest.md) [+M]
