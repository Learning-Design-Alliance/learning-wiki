# Use confidence-weighted FCI pretests to identify strongly held misconceptions for targeted instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes compiling confidence-weighted FCI data across questions before instruction to diagnose group misconceptions. If a class-average wFCI for an item is "highly negative (closer to -4)", the instructor is alerted to a misconception strongly held by many students and can devote more instructional time to it; items confidently correct can be briefly overviewed to reallocate time.
