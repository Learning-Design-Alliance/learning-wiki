# Adopt Qubit Touchdown as an accessible outreach and classroom resource for introducing quantum computing and boosting confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the game as a learning and outreach resource, stating "Qubit Touchdown could be a valuable resource for learning and outreach, giving high students students confidence to pursue technical fields they may have initially considered out of reach." This aligns with the national QIST workforce plan's call for learners to be "provided exposure to QIST via accessible outreach and educational opportunities," including games, beginning with K-12 age groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Free printables of the board and cards from the creator's website, or hand-drawn versions on index cards or card stock, plus any movable object as the football
#### Constraints
- The study population was 107 public high school students, mostly juniors and seniors, in one school day at one school

### Target Learners
- K-12 and high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- building interest and self-confidence in learning quantum computing

## Claims

- [High school students widely found Qubit Touchdown enjoyable, easy to learn, and worth recommending as a quantum computing learning tool](../claims/qubit-touchdown-enjoyable-easy-to-learn.md) [+W]
