Four recommended practices for implementing an admissions portfolio system¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report relays the 2022 Reimagining College Access tool's recommended practices for institutions implementing an admissions portfolio: know your "why" for requesting different or additional information; determine which artifacts to request in the most usable format; craft and refine a clear "ask" about expectations and intended use with supports for students; and conduct outreach to counselors, students, and families. The Ross case is presented as demonstrating how admission officers can put these practices into action.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Clarity about the purpose of requesting additional information
  • Supports so students understand the goals and expectations of the portfolio
  • Outreach to guidance and college counselors, students, and families

Constraints¶

  • Practices are recommendations from the RCA tool, illustrated by one institution's case rather than tested across sites

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate applicants, particularly those from historically minoritized communities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Communicating portfolio expectations so applicants can demonstrate their strengths effectively

Claims¶

  • The portfolio adds context to holistic review that readers say helps level the playing field for applicants from under-resourced high schools [+W]
  • Requiring an admissions portfolio coincided with a large drop in BBA applications, consistent with filtering for intentional applicants, with volumes recovering after outreach [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Calibration training and moderation to ensure reliable rubric-based portfolio review
