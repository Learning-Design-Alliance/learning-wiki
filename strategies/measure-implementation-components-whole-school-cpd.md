# Evaluate whole-school CPD with implementation measures tied to specific programme components

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evaluators recommend that future studies of whole-school CPD programmes systematically investigate implementation using measures that relate to specific programme components, and investigate changes in school culture and teacher behaviour. This trial could not relate implementation fidelity to attainment because "there were no consistent objective assessments of implementation fidelity conducted", and neither the evaluation team nor SSAT had structured monitoring of implementation fidelity. The process evaluation instead relied on interviews, focus groups, surveys, and lesson observations of perceptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- implementation measures that relate to specific components of the programme
- structured monitoring of implementation fidelity across all schools
#### Constraints
- In this trial, Year 9 attainment and attitudes data and implementation quality data were not collected due to circumstances beyond the control of the project teams, including the death of the principal investigator

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers
- school leadership teams

### Target Learning Goals
- improving classroom practice
- embedding whole-school pedagogical change
