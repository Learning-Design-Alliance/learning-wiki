# What’s Below the Surface? (Iceberg Analysis)

## Description
This strategy involves using the metaphor of an iceberg to encourage students to extend their knowledge and further develop their ideas beyond the surface level. Students start with a question related to a key event featuring an adolescent protagonist, then illustrate an iceberg with a clear waterline. Above the surface, they cite obvious scenes from the text that caused the event. Below the surface, they explore deeper nuances of the story, character traits, and facets of identity that led the characters to the event. Students then discuss how these underlying ideas help them understand more about the event and characters. This promotes self-management and responsible decision-making skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selected text featuring an adolescent protagonist and key events. Illustration of an iceberg. Facilitation of discussions and reflective activities.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of texts and questions to ensure relevance. Success depends on the instructor's ability to guide students in exploring complex character traits and motivations.
