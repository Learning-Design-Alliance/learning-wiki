# Targeted interventions and adaptive feedback to move learners from Observation toward full-cycle Exploration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that modeling environments use the identified behavioral patterns to personalize support. It states that "Targeted interventions to move learners from Observation to more integrative behaviors may bridge the gap between surface-level engagement and advanced modeling practices", and that personalized environments should tailor the experience through adaptive feedback—for example, recommending similar models to learners tackling specific problems, or feedback deepening conceptual comprehension for observation-oriented learners.
