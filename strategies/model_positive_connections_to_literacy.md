# Model Positive Connections to Literacy

## Description
Teachers share their own positive experiences with reading and writing to foster a positive literacy environment. This can involve sharing favorite books and encouraging students to add their own favorites to a classroom list. Developers can incorporate ways for teachers to share what they read or write with students in both physical and virtual classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A list of favorite books or examples of the teacher's writing to share. A space (physical or virtual) to display the list and encourage student contributions.
#### Constraints
- The teacher's personal preferences may not align with all students' interests. It is important to create an inclusive environment where all types of literacy are valued.

### Affordances
- Encourages positive attitudes toward literacy by demonstrating the teacher's enthusiasm and personal connection to reading and writing. Creates a shared space where students can explore and express their own interests.

### Personalization
- Students can contribute their own favorite books or writings to the classroom list, personalizing the experience and making it more relevant to their interests.
