# Use standards-based learner profiles to deliver targeted support and curriculum-level interventions in professional degree programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that program coordinators use the identified standards-based profiles to tailor support rather than merely audit learners, and to trigger curriculum-level responses. It states, for example, that "learners in the Knowledgeable and strategic group can be provided with more opportunities to develop and demonstrate their capabilities in creating and maintaining safer learning environments (Standard 4)". Profiles can also be computed at any program point (e.g., Semester 3) to enable timely, targeted interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment mappings to professional standards and grades available before the point of profile calculation
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated for a single cohort of one ITE program; generalizability requires analysis of future offerings and other programs

### Target Learners
- Pre-service teachers, particularly those in intermediate attainment profiles needing targeted support

### Target Learning Goals
- Development of professional teaching standards, especially standards where a learner's profile shows low attainment
