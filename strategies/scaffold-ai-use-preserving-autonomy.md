# Scaffold AI use without undermining student autonomy through structured prompts, ethical checklists, and reflection journals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that educators and instructional designers "scaffold AI use without undermining student autonomy" by providing clear structures such as step-by-step prompts, a checklist of ethical concepts, or reflection journals while still allowing students to decide how deeply to engage at each AI-ARC stage. This scaffolding is intended to build confidence and critical skills without slipping into overreliance. Integrating the framework with academic integrity policies normalizes responsible AI use as part of integrity rather than a separate compliance burden.
