# Team Assisted Individualised (TAI) instruction combining cooperative learning with individualised programmed instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
TAI combines cooperative learning in small mixed-ability groups with individualised programmed instruction where materials are arranged in small sequenced units called 'frames' that lead learners from known to unknown concepts. Students work at their own pace, make frequent responses, and receive immediate feedback. Teams of four to six heterogeneous members earn points and certificates for high performance, combining group goals with individual accountability.
