# Simulations

## Description
Simulations use electronic or software-based activities to simulate a real-world situation to which a learner must react. Learners have the opportunity to practice certain skills and make mistakes in a safe environment. Simulation-based training is popular in the healthcare and aviation industries, however, with the advancement of technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Virtual Reality (VR), simulation-based training is being adopted across all types of industries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer or other device to run the simulation, software to create the simulation, facilitator to guide the learning process
#### Constraints
- Simulations can be expensive to create and run, and success depends on the realism of the simulation.
