Use survey and assessment disengagement metadata as a new source of SEL data for practice, policy, and research¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends treating metadata from existing tests and surveys — particularly indicators of disengagement — as a source of SEL data that educators, policymakers, and researchers can use without administering new instruments. The abstract notes that "Implications of this new source of SEL data for practice, policy, and research are discussed," positioning disengagement metadata as a complement to conventional SEL measures. Because the metadata arise from assessments students already take, the strategy avoids the reactivity and burden of additional SEL surveying.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Existing assessment or survey administration must generate behavioral metadata that can be captured and analyzed.

Constraints¶

  • The article's title flags limitations of survey and assessment disengagement metadata, which the discussion section addresses.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students taking interim assessments and surveys
