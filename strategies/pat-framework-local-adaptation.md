# Local adaptation of the framework across diverse contexts

## Description
The article recommends that programmes using the framework review it alongside local regulations, professional standards, cultural guidance and service needs. Regulatory requirements, scopes of practice, cultural responsibilities, supervision models and service settings vary considerably across jurisdictions and organizations. Commitments related to safety, consent, boundaries, accountability and ethical practice may be broadly relevant, but their application, assessment and governance will vary across settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Review alongside local regulations, professional standards, cultural guidance and service needs
#### Constraints
- Differences in supervision requirements, documentation practices, informed consent processes, referral pathways and scope of practice are likely to influence implementation
