## Description
The survey outlines an emerging pathway toward equitable AI-driven education, recommending that the field pursue "balancing personalization with data privacy, addressing the digital divide in AI access and infrastructure, and improving teacher and student adoption through targeted training and support". These directions are presented as open challenges requiring inclusive, transparent, and context-aware strategies for integrating AI in education.
