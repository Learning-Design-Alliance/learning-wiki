# Use virtual simulation resources to prepare students before high-risk or complex experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends having students learn experimental principles and virtual operations through virtual simulation teaching before conducting experiments. The authors report this "could ensure the normal operation of some complex and high-risk experimental projects," using Western blot as an example, and that pre-learning "improved the success rate of experiments" and enhanced student confidence. They built pharmacology MOOCs and virtual simulation teaching resources integrated into experimental teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Constructed virtual simulation teaching resources and pharmacology MOOCs
- Online pharmacology course resources available to students before class
#### Constraints
- Described for complex, multi-step, or high-risk projects such as Western blot; the article does not report a controlled test of this component in isolation

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduate students in pharmacology experimental courses

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental principles and operation methods
- experiment success rate
- confidence
