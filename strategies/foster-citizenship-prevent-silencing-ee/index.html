Foster citizenship and prevent silencing of environmental conflicts and risks in school EE¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that EE in schools and teacher training move beyond conservation toward a holistic, systemic, and interdisciplinary perspective, as recommended by Brazilian legislation. Its recommendations aim "to foster the development of citizenship at schools and to prevent the silencing of issues such as the conflicts and risks associated with environmental problems." This entails incorporating social, economic, ethical, and cultural issues into EE rather than restricting it to preservation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Initial teacher training that includes the socio-environmental dimension, as Law 9795/1999 requires for initial and continuing teacher education curricula

Constraints¶

  • The article notes legislation alone does not guarantee EE activities, given the distance between normative discourse and school practice

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service biology and science teachers
  • basic education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • environmental citizenship
  • critical awareness of environmental and social problems
