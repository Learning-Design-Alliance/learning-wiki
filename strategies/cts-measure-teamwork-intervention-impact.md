# Use the Clinical Teamwork Scale to measure the immediate impact of teamwork interventions before and after implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the CTS as a measurement tool for evaluating teamwork interventions: "These data suggest that Clinical Teamwork Scale may be a useful tool to measure the immediate impact of interventions on teamwork that matter to the reduction of errors." Because the scale is free, quick to apply during live emergencies, and demonstrated reliable scoring, teams can assess teamwork before and after training or policy changes. The authors frame improving teamwork as a relatively low-cost intervention to improve patient outcomes and call for interventions, training and policies where findings replicate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluators trained in CTS use, with pilot consensus scoring to establish consistency
#### Constraints
- The article states it remains uncertain whether poorer teamwork causes errors, so the scale measures teamwork itself rather than proven error reduction

### Target Learners
- EMS professionals
- hospital emergency care teams
- clinical training programme designers

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring teamwork improvement
- reducing medical errors through teamwork training
