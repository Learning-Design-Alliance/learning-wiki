# Combine psychoeducation on ADHD/ODD with individualised behaviour modification plans delivered through weekly teacher sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The programme consisted of 12 weekly 120-minute small-group sessions covering ADHD/ODD aetiology and management principles, behaviour modification procedures (contingent reinforcement, token economy, response cost, time-out, peer tutoring), and a discussion segment where "individual behavioural problems with students occurring during school lessons or breaks were discussed" and concrete interventions were defined. Written handouts after each session supported independent classroom implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by a facilitator experienced in ADHD/ODD treatment (here a child and adolescent psychiatrist)
- A manualised structure with weekly sessions and written handouts
- Time for teachers to prepare and implement individualised classroom measures
#### Constraints
- Teachers found three months of weekly sessions plus preparation time too time-consuming
- Designed as a preventive classroom approach, not a substitute for clinical or social-work intervention with severe cases
- Teachers felt helpless when confronted with students' desperate social situations outside the classroom

### Target Learners
- elementary school teachers of students with ADHD or ODD symptoms

### Target Learning Goals
- improve teachers' skills in managing attentional and disruptive behavioural problems in the classroom
