# Use keyword method with L1 translations and pictures for deliberate vocabulary study

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy directs learners to associate a new L2 word with a similar-sounding L1 keyword and a picture representing its meaning. The article states that "The use of the L1 and pictures to provide the meaning for words is generally more effective than the use of L2 definitions" and that mnemonic techniques help vocabulary be retained for a long period.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need training in applying mnemonic techniques
- Access to bilingual dictionaries or picture resources
#### Constraints
- The article notes criticisms of the keyword method (Hall et al., 1981; Pressley, 1985)

### Target Learners
- University EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Deliberate learning and retention of target vocabulary
