# Recess

## Description
Offer daily recess to allow students to be active, which can improve memory, conflict resolution skills, and creativity. Structure recess with activity zones to provide options for different games and sports. A 20-30 minute recess can help students minimize disruptive classroom behavior and provide a much-needed daily break.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designated time, space, and supervision. Structured activity zones
#### Constraints
- May require significant time allocation. Needs effective supervision to ensure safety and fair play.
