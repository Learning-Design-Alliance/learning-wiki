Coordinate services across programs and agencies to reinforce parent and child outcomes¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief directs administrators to strategies for "effectively delivering and coordinating services across programs and agencies" when serving parents and children through combined approaches. It frames cross-program and cross-agency coordination as part of making intentionally combined services work, so that services relate to "mutually reinforcing outcomes for parents and children."

Context¶

Requirements¶
