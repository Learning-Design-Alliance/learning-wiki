# Provide teachers in-service training on enriched time-concept activities and preparing concrete materials

## Description
The article recommends that teacher preparation systems introduce enriched time-concept programs and train teachers to make concrete materials. It states that "Through in service training the activities enhanced regarding time concept or various educational programs can be introduced and the training of preparing concrete materials can be given to teachers", because the introduction notes teachers have inadequate levels of knowledge concerning concept teaching. This is a forward-looking recommendation, not a tested manipulation.
