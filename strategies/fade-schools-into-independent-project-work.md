# Fade structured teaching sessions into independent project work over the semester

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article schedules instruction so that guided teaching dominates early and independent project work dominates late. "The first two weeks are for onboarding the students and focusing on the methodological foundation, while the projects start in parallel. From weeks three to six, the time spent in schools decreases steadily as independent project work gains importance. From week seven onward, students focus primarily on their project." Milestones in week two (understanding ROS code, publishing topics, controlling data flows) gate the transition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School sessions concentrated in the first two weeks with defined early milestones
- Project tasks that progressively require applying school-taught knowledge to the running project
#### Constraints
- The authors state that optimizing the balance between school sessions and project-goal sessions is an open question requiring constant assessment

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in semester-spanning project courses

### Target Learning Goals
- transferring taught robotics concepts into independent application on a real system
