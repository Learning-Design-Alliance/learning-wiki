# Use a biographical structured interview scoring multiple dimensions including communication skills and coping with uncertainty as a selection component

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The UNSW interview is 'designed around a biographical assessment of the applicant’s life context and experiences which had led to a desire to pursue a medical career'. Interviewers score applicants on six dimensions: communication skills, motivation, empathy of others, self-awareness, responding to diversity, and ability to cope with uncertainty. Communication skills scores can be used alongside academic ranks in weighted selection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained interviewers scoring against a defined dimension set
- Preliminary application and curriculum vitae material explored in greater depth at interview
#### Constraints
- The 'cope with uncertainty' dimension showed negative predictive value for WAM outcomes in this study
- Interview resource costs and mixed validity results make the method controversial in the wider literature

### Target Learners
- applicants to undergraduate medical programs

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing non-academic capabilities and communication skills of medical program applicants
