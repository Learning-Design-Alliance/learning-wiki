# Whole-class facilitated play of the virtual environments with multi-purpose content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During deployment, the teachers changed the intended individual or small-group play so that "the whole class worked together as a large group": one or two students played while the teacher led the rest of the class in discussing solutions and reciting vocabulary. The teachers also made the sessions multi-purpose, using Environment 1 to teach math alongside Chinese and Environment 2's Chinese object labels to teach Chinese reading and English spelling. The teachers found large-group play a more effective way of engaging the class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher to select players, moderate discussion, and lead recitation
- At least one computer; the Kinect version was used for Environment 1 and mouse-and-keyboard for Environment 2
#### Constraints
- Designed for the resource-limited setting of one classroom and two teachers; whether it works without a facilitating teacher is untested

### Target Learners
- young children (6–8) in afterschool Chinese classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Chinese vocabulary and sentence practice
- integrated math and literacy practice
