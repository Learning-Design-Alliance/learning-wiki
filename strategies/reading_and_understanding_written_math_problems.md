# Reading and Understanding Written Math Problems

## Description
Word problems can be challenging. Use background knowledge of math as a stepping stone for other language learning by accelerating math instruction at the beginning of the school year and building on what students have learned. Read math word problems and demonstrate the logical thinking process while translating words into pictures and number sentences. Have students explain their thinking and correct each other. Capitalize on enthusiasm to teach them how to extract information from texts and move them toward oral and reading fluency. Provide explicit instruction of key vocabulary, daily practice of problem-solving, repeated readings, and hands-on activities.
