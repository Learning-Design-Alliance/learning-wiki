# Support STEM students' motivation by accounting for the lasting impact of prior experiences with math

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that motivational support for beginning STEM students look beyond the immediate achievement situation: "efforts to support STEM students' motivation should consider the lasting impact of previous experiences with math, as these experiences shape students' perceptions and engagement with new achievement situations within STEM." Because students with comparable test performance differ in motivation according to prior achievement and learning opportunities, support should be tailored to students' mathematical backgrounds. The Educational Impact statement adds that recognizing these individual differences can guide more tailored support strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Information about students' prior math achievement and high-school learning opportunities
#### Constraints
- The article states these prior-experience effects persisted even in the absence of situation-specific performance differences, so performance-based support alone is insufficient

### Target Learners
- beginning STEM students in gateway math-related courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining motivation and preventing early motivational declines in STEM study programs
