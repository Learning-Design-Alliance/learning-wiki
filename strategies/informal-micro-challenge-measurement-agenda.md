# Strengthen informal micro-challenge evaluation with embedded micro-assessments, process tracing, delayed transfer tasks, mixed-methods probes and controlled sequencing comparisons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a next-step measurement agenda for informal data/AI literacy interventions, because platform traces alone cannot distinguish persistence from conceptual uptake. Recommended additions include "Embedded micro-assessments: short comprehension checks after each door", "Process tracing: logging interaction sequences", "Delayed transfer tasks" applying learned logic one week later, "Mixed-methods probes" such as exit surveys, and "Controlled comparisons" randomizing users to blocked versus interleaved sequences. These additions would support stronger claims about what informal micro-challenges can and cannot teach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tighter event logging and instrumentation embedded in the platform before deployment
#### Constraints
- Current metrics are observational proxies, not validated literacy measures; the agenda is stated for future iterations, not yet tested

### Target Learners
- Secondary (high school) students participating in extracurricular informal learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Validating durable data/AI literacy learning beyond task completion and engagement
