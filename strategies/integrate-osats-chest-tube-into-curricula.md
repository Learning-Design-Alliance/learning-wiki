# Integrate the modified OSATS for chest tube insertion into modern medical curricula as standardized assessment and feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose deploying the modified OSATS in surgical training courses to provide "clear defined learning goals combined with structured and objective assessment and feedback", so that trainees' skill improvement can be objectified and feedback standardized without straining staff and resources. They suggest the tool could support competency-based teaching programs, certification processes, and entrustable professional activities, and plan to use it in courses for general surgery, trauma surgery and intensive care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further investigation beyond the pilot; trained raters and video-recording infrastructure for blinded rating
#### Constraints
- Pilot evidence only: single-center, small subgroups, self-estimated experience stratification

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- trauma surgeons
- anaesthesiologists
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- proficiency in chest tube insertion and standardized feedback on procedural skill
