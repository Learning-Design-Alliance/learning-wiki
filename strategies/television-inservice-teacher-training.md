# Use television for inservice training of teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The National Program's first-year report concluded that television proved valuable for the inservice training of teachers, alongside its use for large-class instruction. The abstract states plainly that "Television proved to be valu- able for inservice training of teachers." This is an implementable use of televised instruction reported from the nationwide experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the television instruction infrastructure described in the program, including facilities and trained television teachers
#### Constraints
- The program reported major problems with inadequate facilities, finding good television teachers, training teachers in handling large classes, integration of the telecast, and scheduling

### Target Learners
- Inservice teachers in large city school systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher professional development through televised instruction
