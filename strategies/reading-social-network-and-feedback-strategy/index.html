Establish a reading teaching social network, strengthen group discussions and feedback, and report platform errors quickly¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's suggestions section recommends three actions for instructors using cloud platforms in reading instruction: establishing a reading teaching social network to reduce the instructor's teaching load, strengthening group discussions and feedback so students obtain suggestions to increase reading comprehension, and reporting platform errors quickly to Microsoft in Taiwan so network administrators can provide immediate assistance. The article states these measures would "improve the function of the platform and thus the students can obtain better learning outcomes".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An instructor using a cloud platform for reading instruction, with access to platform administrators for error reporting

Constraints¶

  • Platform errors require external vendor assistance to resolve

Target Learners¶

  • junior high school (eighth grade) students learning Chinese reading

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved reading comprehension and reading attitude

Claims¶

  • Cloud-platform-assisted reading instruction produced significantly higher reading effectiveness than traditional instruction [+W]
  • Cloud-platform-assisted instruction significantly improved students' reading attitudes across cognitive, affective, and behavioral domains [+W]
  • Reading attitude, learning satisfaction, and reading effectiveness are significantly positively correlated, and reading attitude positively predicts reading effectiveness [+W]
