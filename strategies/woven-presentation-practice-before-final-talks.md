# Weave repeated low-stakes presentation practice into the course before the culminating research presentation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 5 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 2 review, 1 causal), `q3`–`q4` · 4 of 5 report an effect size

## Description
Because students' final success depended on giving an outstanding research presentation, the authors embedded multiple practice opportunities throughout the course: short talks on an Astronomy Picture of the Day with peer compliments and critiques, presentations within inquiry activities, and practice in the transferable-skills and partner courses. As they state, "we wove into the course opportun ities for the students to practice giving talks." Project advisors then devoted significant time to developing the final group presentations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple course components (inquiries, transferable-skills sessions, partner course) into which short presentation tasks can be inserted; peer feedback norms established early.
#### Constraints
- Designed within a four-week residential program culminating in a Presentation Day shared with partner clusters.

### Target Learners
- high-school students preparing research presentations

### Target Learning Goals
- oral presentation and communication skills
