# Five reconciliation strategies academics use to manage AI-related guilt: tool framing, critical thinking emphasis, boundary setting, transparency, and denying reconciliation is needed

## Description
In the qualitative narratives, academics described five approaches to reconciling AI use with academic values. "Tool framing was most common (30%)", comparing AI to calculators or spell-check; critical thinking emphasis (25%) stressed that human evaluation remains essential; boundary setting (20%) involved establishing clear usage rules; transparency (15%) meant openly disclosing AI use; and 10% asserted no reconciliation was needed. These strategies map onto the quantitative profiles, with boundary-setting mirroring the Cautious Users profile.
