# Adopt and adapt parts of several cooperative learning models to fit your teaching style and situation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its conclusion, the chapter recommends that teachers not limit themselves to one model: "Teachers can choose one of the models of CL described above, but may be better off adopting and adapting parts of several models to create their own flavor of CL that best fits their teaching style and situation." The chapter points teachers to the other articles in its volume for examples of how to do this, positioning model-mixing as the practical path for language teachers implementing cooperative learning.
