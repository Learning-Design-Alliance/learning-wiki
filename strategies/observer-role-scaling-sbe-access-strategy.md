# Use the observer role alongside active participation as a resource-efficient strategy to expand access to simulation-based education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that, "integrated alongside active participation, the observer role may allow learners to benefit from observational learning, offering a cost-effective and resource-efficient response to the logistical and staffing constraints involved in engaging large cohorts in hands-on simulation." Policymakers are encouraged to develop best-practice standards for learner role assignment with professional simulation societies, and educators are advised to add refresher sessions and spaced repetition in both roles to sustain learning given follow-up decline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Investment in faculty development and audiovisual infrastructure
- Best-practice standards for learner role assignment developed with professional simulation societies
#### Constraints
- Applicable within Kirkpatrick Levels 1 and 2 evidence; generalizability limited to settings resembling undergraduate nursing in high-income countries

### Target Learners
- Large cohorts of health professions students facing limited simulation site and faculty capacity

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining learning outcomes while expanding simulation access
