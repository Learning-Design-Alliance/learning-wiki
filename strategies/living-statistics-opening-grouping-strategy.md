# Open a workshop with 'living statistics' grouping to activate participants and build a positive learning environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the start of the workshop, participants physically divide themselves into groups according to their current professional position, with further grouping by areas of interest, prior knowledge, or company size. Because participants come together several times under different criteria, interaction between people with similar interests and backgrounds is created early. The authors report this was effective for making participants feel welcome and taken seriously, and the resulting profile of interests is then used to adapt the keynote presentations.
