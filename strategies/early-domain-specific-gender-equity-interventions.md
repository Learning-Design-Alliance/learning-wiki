# Provide early, proactive, domain-specific interventions to support equitable access to advanced mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends interventions that begin in the earliest school years and target specific mathematical domains, since disparities emerge by kindergarten and intensify across elementary grades. It states the results "highlight the importance of early, proactive, and domain-specific interventions that support sustained growth and equitable access to advanced mathematics learning opportunities", particularly when beliefs and trajectories are most malleable.
