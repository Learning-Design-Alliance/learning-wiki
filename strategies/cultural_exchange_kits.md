# Cultural Exchange Kits

## Description
Students create 'Culture Kits' with special items that bring their culture alive. This sharing and discussion of multicultural traditions enhances learning and fosters cultural understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for students to create and share their kits, access to relevant materials, and teacher facilitation.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure respect and inclusivity. May require additional resources to support diverse cultural expressions.

<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All students, particularly in multicultural classrooms.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote cultural sharing, enhance understanding of traditions, create engaging learning experiences, connect cultures in the classroom.

### Affordances
- Fosters cultural exchange, engages students, and promotes understanding of diverse backgrounds and traditions.

### Personalization
- Students create their own kits with items that are meaningful to them, allowing for diverse cultural expressions.
