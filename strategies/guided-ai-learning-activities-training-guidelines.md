# Incorporate guided AI-based learning activities, structured training on responsible AI use, and institutional guidelines for AI integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing educators "incorporate guided AI-based learning activities that promote active engagement while preserving independent critical thinking". It further recommends structured training sessions on responsible and ethical AI use, and at the policy level that institutions develop clear guidelines for AI integration, invest in faculty development programs, and ensure equitable access to AI technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development programs
- Clear institutional guidelines for AI integration
- Equitable access to AI technologies
#### Constraints
- Disparities in access to AI technologies may exacerbate existing educational inequalities, particularly in low-resource settings

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Active engagement with AI tools
- Responsible and ethical AI use
- Independent critical thinking
