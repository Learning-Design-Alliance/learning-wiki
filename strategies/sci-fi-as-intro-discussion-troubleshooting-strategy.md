## Description
The article recommends deploying literary works flexibly across levels: "as introductory points, as basis for class discussion, or as cases to be troubleshooted by the students." It targets audiences "from high school to amateur astronomers [21] and even graduate level students who can exercise debugging some of the false concepts and conclusions that were present in these works."
