# Future research should compare different game types and learner characteristics such as proficiency, age, and background

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors close by recommending that research move beyond the single mixed game package: "Further studies can be conducted to examine and compare the advantages associated with different types of games." They also suggest investigating whether performance differs with individual characteristics such as level of proficiency, age, and educational background.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comparative studies isolating the effects of individual game types
#### Constraints
- The present study bundled four games, so the contribution of any single game type remains untested

### Target Learners
- young foreign language learners across proficiency levels

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary learning effectiveness across game types and learner profiles
