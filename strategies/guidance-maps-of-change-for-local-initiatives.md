# Provide well-researched maps of change to guide local initiative, alongside pluralism and self-determinism

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's first lesson: "while it may be important to encourage local pluralism and self-determinism in developing, adopting, and implementing initiatives to improve schools, it may be equally important to provide guidance for local initiatives in the form of well-researched and well-thought out maps of change." The Challenge eschewed common goals and designated programs, and the report links its broad goals and vague strategies to shortcomings in implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reform funders willing to supply researched guidance without eliminating local pluralism
#### Constraints
- Stated as a lesson from the Challenge's experience, not a tested recommendation

### Target Learners
- elementary school students in urban public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- school improvement supporting student learning
