# Multi-component math interventions targeting achievement, numerosity, and anxiety together

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that struggling profiles differ in which components are affected, the authors recommend interventions that 'target multiple areas of math performance, for instance training on numerosity while also working on math achievement skills (e.g., calculation) with a component meant to reduce math anxiety.' They hypothesize multi-component interventions will outperform single-component ones such as intervening on non-symbolic number approximation only.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must be tailored to the child's specific profile of achievement, numerosity, and anxiety rather than one-for-all
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is a hypothesis from a cross-sectional profile analysis; the intervention itself was not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- children showing math difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- math achievement
- numerosity
- reducing math anxiety
