# Report control-condition context fully and adapt comparative methods to information completeness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that future empirical studies of games in education describe the control condition's teaching material, teaching method, and evaluation method so results can be compared. It further recommends that "comparative studies should take into account the diversity in the completeness of information, developing a methodology that can either adapt to it or compare different perspectives", and suggests applying the same clarity-scoring method to humanities and vocational education studies separately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Authors of controlled experiments must describe control-group materials, methods, and evaluation for replication
#### Constraints
- The authors advise applying the method to humanities separately rather than incorporating those studies into the current selection, since the groups present very different teaching goals and methods

### Target Learners
- secondary and higher education students in scientific disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of theoretical or practical scientific knowledge
