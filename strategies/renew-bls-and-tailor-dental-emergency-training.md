# Renew BLS certification more frequently and tailor emergency training to dental settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends shortening the BLS renewal interval below the usual 24 months and adapting emergency-management training to dental contexts, because self-efficacy declined within months of certification and recent BLS certification alone did not raise behavioral capability. It states: "we recommend conducting practical training for managing medical emergencies that is tailored to dental settings and renew the BLS certification more frequently."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practical training that is tailored to dental settings
- More frequent BLS recertification intervals (the article suggests e.g., within 6 months rather than 24 months)
#### Constraints
- The BLS-time effect on behavioral capability may be confounded by other training, per the authors
- Cross-sectional design limits causal inference

### Target Learners
- Dental students
- Dental interns
- Dentists

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-efficacy and behavioral capability for managing medical emergencies in dental clinics
