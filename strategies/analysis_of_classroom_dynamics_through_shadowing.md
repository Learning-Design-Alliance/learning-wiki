# Analysis of Classroom Dynamics Through Shadowing

## Description
This strategy involves a teacher or coach shadowing students to analyze classroom dynamics and student experiences. Key aspects include observing how long students sit passively, noting opportunities for active participation, and assessing the balance between lecture and collaborative activities. The goal is to identify areas where instruction can be improved to increase student engagement and learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for shadowing, a structured observation protocol, and a willingness to reflect on and adapt teaching practices.
#### Constraints
- Potential for observer bias, may not capture the full complexity of the classroom environment, and requires a commitment to change.
