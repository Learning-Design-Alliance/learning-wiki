# Provide dedicated well-being supports such as Wobble rooms and junior doctor forums for redeployed staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This strategy recommends simple measures to safeguard the mental well-being of doctors redeployed to demanding pandemic critical care: providing access to 'Wobble rooms', described as places for staff to unwind after dealing with difficult situations, and establishing junior doctor forums similar to Schwartz rounds where trainees can reflect on stressful experiences with peers. The authors recommend these from their own ICU experience, where redeployed doctors faced anxiety, helplessness, shock and grief.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior staff must actively establish the spaces and forums; signposting alone is insufficient.
#### Constraints
- The article states that signposting well-being resources will not necessarily lead junior staff to access appropriate help.

### Target Learners
- Redeployed junior doctors in critical care

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining mental well-being during demanding clinical rotations
