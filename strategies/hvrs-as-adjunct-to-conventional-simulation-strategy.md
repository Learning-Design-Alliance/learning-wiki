# Use HVRS as an adjunct to, not a replacement for, conventional mannequin-based pre-clinical paediatric dentistry simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (3 for, 5 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "HVRS should augment rather than replace conventional pre-clinical paediatric dentistry training", positioning haptic virtual reality simulation as a supplementary environment offering unlimited repetitions, standardized computer-generated feedback, and self-reflective practice alongside conventional mannequin training. Students most preferred experiencing HVRS after conventional simulation training. This strategy suits programmes integrating haptic simulators into pre-clinical curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students should retain conventional mannequin-based simulation training as the core pre-clinical experience
- HVRS sessions should be scheduled as supplementary practice, preferably after conventional training
#### Constraints
- Pilot evidence from a single institution with 14 participants and one operative procedure; the authors state results should not be over generalized

### Target Learners
- Year 4 undergraduate dental students in pre-clinical paediatric dentistry

### Target Learning Goals
- primary molar pulpotomy operative skills and confidence

### Affordances
- Deliberate Practice Hvrs Framework
