# Watch-Read-Think (WRT) pre-class strategy for online self-learning of sports action video content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The WRT strategy structures pre-class online self-learning as "action video watching, theoretical materials reading, and warm-up thinking" of the chapter content, encouraging students to preview learning content before the offline class. In the 'Sports Action Video Material Shooting' chapter, teachers guided students to think about problems such as traditional sports action teaching difficulties and advantages of Sports Multimedia Courseware through interactive discussion, with diverse materials (text, video, animation) and a Q&A module.
