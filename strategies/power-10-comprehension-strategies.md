# Use the Power 10 strategies to help students comprehend, organize, and remember information in any context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Power 10 are a set of teacher strategies used in both ECED curricula (Reading Matters and Writing Matters). According to the article, "teachers use strategies called the Power 10, to help students comprehend, organize, and remember information in any context". They are embedded in daily lessons across the two-year program and are modeled during professional development institutes and site visits.
