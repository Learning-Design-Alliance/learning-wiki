# Make the computational thinking pathway visible to the wider community through a public website

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report advises making the pathway "visible not just to teachers but to the wider community." Talladega was the first of the three Core districts to develop a community-facing website that defined CT for parents and families, offered resources for learning more, and published the district's grade-level integration pathway and course offerings. The site is populated directly from its original Google docs so updates remain timely.
