# Graphic Organizers and Guided Notes (Processing Speed)

## Description
Providing students with graphic organizers or guided notes during lectures to help them pull out the important points. This strategy involves offering structured note-taking templates that highlight key information and relationships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Graphic organizer templates, guided notes, and instructor guidance on their effective use
#### Constraints
- Requires careful design to ensure alignment with lecture content, may not be suitable for all types of content, and some students may prefer unstructured note-taking

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students who struggle with processing speed and need support in extracting key information from lectures

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Support information processing, reduce cognitive load, and improve note-taking skills

### Affordances
- Reduces the amount of information needed to be processed in real-time, highlights essential content, supports active listening, and enhances note-taking efficiency

### Personalization
- Templates can be customized based on subject matter and student needs, with options for visual cues, color-coding, and pre-filled sections.

## Assessment Evidence
- Review student's completed graphic organizers and guided notes for accuracy and completeness. Assess their ability to recall and apply information from the lecture.

## Impact
- Studies show that guided notes improve information retention and enhance note-taking skills, particularly for students with processing speed challenges.
