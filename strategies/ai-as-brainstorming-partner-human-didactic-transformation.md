# Use generative AI as a brainstorming partner for divergent ideation while treating didactic transformation as a human responsibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends positioning LLMs as tools to overcome the blank page and generate diverse activity ideas, while the educator acts as a critical filter who adapts algorithmic proposals to kindergarten realities. The findings show "the use of AI as a brainstorming partner is effective but struggles with didactic transformation", so preservice teachers should treat AI output as raw material for divergent ideation rather than as an expert guide, retaining control over translating abstract physics into play-based inquiry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need sufficient pedagogical content knowledge to assess AI proposals for scientific integrity and preschool appropriateness
#### Constraints
- The authors warn that reliance on AI as a pedagogical agent could lead to a superficial approach to teaching

### Target Learning Goals
