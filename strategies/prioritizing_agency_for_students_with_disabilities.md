# Prioritizing Agency for Students With Disabilities

## Description
Prioritizing agency for students with disabilities involves fostering self-advocacy and self-determination skills to enable them to engage more effectively in their education. This includes understanding their rights and needs, communicating them effectively, solving problems, setting goals, and reflecting actively. Engaging students in their IEP meetings and providing hands-on learning opportunities are key components of this approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to prioritize self-advocacy and self-determination, provide tangible opportunities for practice, and fully engage students in decision-making processes.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset and a significant investment of time, resources, and training.
