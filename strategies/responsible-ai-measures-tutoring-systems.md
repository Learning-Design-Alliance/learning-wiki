# Responsible AI measures for tutoring systems: encryption, security audits, access controls, transparency, bias mitigation, and governance oversight

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review prescribes concrete safeguards for ethical tutoring-system deployment: "implementing data encryption protocols, conducting regular security audits, and establishing stringent access controls to safeguard student data." It further calls for transparency with students and guardians about data practices, mitigating AI biases through diverse training datasets and fairness-aware algorithms, regular audits with human oversight, and governance frameworks with oversight committees to ensure compliance with data protection standards and build trust.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational institutions and developers must clearly communicate data collection and usage practices to students and guardians
#### Constraints
- The review frames these as needed measures because current literature lacks thorough examination of data management

### Target Learners
- Students using AI-powered tutoring systems and their guardians

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable, secure, and trustworthy AI-supported learning
