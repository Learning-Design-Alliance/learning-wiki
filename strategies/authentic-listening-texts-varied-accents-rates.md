# Use authentic listening texts with different accents and speech rates

## Description
The article recommends exposing students to varied authentic listening input because classroom listening relied on course-book samples of Received Pronunciation. The author writes that "Using authentic listening texts with different accents and rates might help students overcome the difficulties they have," and suggests further study of long texts with different accents and lower speech rate. This recommendation follows from reports linking difficulty to fast speech and native pronunciation/intonation.
