Engage parents through home-learning packets with videos, tips, and activity suggestions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As part of REDI, parents receive take-home materials supporting the program's goals: "Parents receive packets including videos, parenting tips, and suggestions for home learning activities." This extends the preschool program's social-emotional and literacy focus into the home.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Packets must be distributed to families as part of REDI implementation

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • parents of disadvantaged preschool students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • home support of school readiness and social-emotional learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Support REDI implementation with brief inservice training plus weekly coaching from local consultants
  • Home-Based Literacy Support
  • Non-intensive Home Numeracy Environment (HNE) Intervention Program
  • Parents support elementary writing skills at home through everyday writing activities like writing stories, reviewing movies, and describing things around them
