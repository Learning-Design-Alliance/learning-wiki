Plan for device distribution delays and extend engagement duration in practitioner advisory partnerships¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on the advisory board's first six months, the authors recommend that RPIP-style partnerships plan for procurement and contracting delays, since device distribution depends on factors outside the industry partner's control. In the absence of devices, the board could consider and discuss use cases, examples of specific situations where the device could be used. They also suggest longer engagement, since one practitioner said six months might be too short, to enable device engagement and deeper discussions.
