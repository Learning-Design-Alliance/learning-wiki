# Blend flipped and traditional models, and supplement with online materials like videos and podcasts to address individual needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that some students value live in-person lectures, the authors argue "this would argue for a mixture of the traditional and flipped classroom models". They further suggest extending the in-class experience by encouraging engagement with supplementary online materials such as videos and podcasts related to lecture topics, which serve as additional learning supports without replacing face-to-face instruction, and using multiple tools and strategies accommodating a variety of learning styles.
