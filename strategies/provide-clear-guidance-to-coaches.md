# Provide clear guidance to coaches when implementing a districtwide coaching program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The brief recommends that districts provide clear guidance to coaches as they implement a coaching program. This is one of three insights from the New Teacher Center, intended to ensure coaches understand expectations and devote sufficient time to the full range of coaching activities needed to improve teachers' instruction.
