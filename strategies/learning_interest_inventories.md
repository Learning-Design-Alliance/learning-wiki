# Learning Interest Inventories

## Description
Learning interest inventories help instructors and learners identify areas of interest and ways of finding joy in learning and demonstrating that learning. These inventories support increased participation. The instructor can incorporate those interests into teaching methods, encouraging enthusiastic participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning interest inventory tool, time for students to complete the inventory, willingness to incorporate student interests into teaching
#### Constraints
- May not cover all possible interests. Requires effort to align curriculum with student interests.
