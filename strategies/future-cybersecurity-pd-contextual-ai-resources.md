# Design future cybersecurity PD with contextual guidance, AI-related topics, and up-to-date resource alternatives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on interview and survey findings about challenges and needs, the authors recommend that future cybersecurity PD incorporate case studies and interactive activities tailored to participants' specific roles, add AI-related content, and keep resources current. Participants "emphasized the need for contextual guidance, including real-world cybersecurity examples, AI-related topics addressing risks like data breaches, and regular updates to resources with alternatives for tools blocked by their school districts". They also stressed clear communication and transparency when commonly used resources are blocked for security reasons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PD designers able to gather participant role-specific needs and maintain updated resource lists
#### Constraints
- Recommendations come from one PD evaluation with 50 participants in one southeastern state; the authors note generalizability may be limited

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers
- technology facilitators
- school administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- applying cybersecurity practices to participants' specific roles and contexts, including AI-related data privacy risks
