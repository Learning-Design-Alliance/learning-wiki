# Teach historical cosmologies as an interdisciplinary opportunity with History and Geography teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The book devotes a chapter to historical cosmologies — the origin stories and myths of ancient cultures such as the Caiapó, Egyptian and Mesopotamian peoples — and frames teaching them as a chance to work across school subjects. The introduction states: "O terceiro capítulo é dedicado a falar sobre as cosmologias históricas e se cosntitui uma ótima oportunidade para trabalhar junto aos professores de História, Geografia e demais disciplinas relacionadas." The chapter also aims to enrich knowledge and stimulate appreciation and respect for the customs and cultures of ancient peoples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with History, Geography and related subject teachers
- presenting the cultural cosmologies within their historical context to avoid superficial or prejudiced analyses, as the chapter itself warns
#### Constraints
- The chapter presents only three example cosmologies and notes that many other ancient cosmologies could also be worked in the classroom

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- defining and exemplifying what cosmology is
- valuing and respecting the cultures of ancient peoples
- understanding how cosmology emerged and evolved historically
