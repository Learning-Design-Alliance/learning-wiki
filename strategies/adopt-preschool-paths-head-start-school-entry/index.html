Adopt Preschool PATHS in Head Start and school programs to support social-emotional readiness for school entry¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The editors' strategic implication recommends that "Head Start and school programs will find these multi-informant data to be of interest as they consider a curriculum to help prepare children for school entry." The recommendation rests on randomized trial evidence that the curriculum improved emotion knowledge and social competence and reduced teacher-rated withdrawal in preschool-age children.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher capacity to deliver weekly lessons and extension activities across a 9-month period with coordinator support

Constraints¶

  • Evidence characterized as from a promising practice confirmed by randomized clinical trials, not as a definitive established practice

Target Learners¶

  • preschool-age children in Head Start and school programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • social and emotional competence
  • school readiness

Claims¶

  • Teachers implemented Preschool PATHS with high fidelity and positive engagement in the trial [+W]
  • Preschool PATHS increases emotion knowledge and parent- and teacher-rated social competence in Head Start children [+W]
  • Teachers rated PATHS intervention children as less socially withdrawn than controls at year end [+W]
  • A replication trial with 267 preschoolers found greater gains in social skills and stronger teacher-child and parent-teacher relationships than controls [+W]
