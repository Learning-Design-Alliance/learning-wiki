# Interventions to make physics welcoming to female students: boost the value of science capital, highlight utility value, provide female role models, and act before university

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend interventions to make physics a viable option for female students. These include using "interventions to boost the value that science capital holds in all areas of society", highlighting "the utility value of physics in culture, scientiﬁc literacy, and employment", increasing "exposure to female role models in physics, and examples of how physics can help people", and ensuring physics maintains "a culture that places value in the science cap- ital oﬀered by all groups of students". They also suggest an initiative similar to Athena SWAN at the institutional level.
