# Build and enhance protective factors in at-risk populations through experimental and intervention studies to support resilient developmental trajectories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The editors' forward-looking recommendation is that the field move from mapping strengths and weaknesses to testing how protective factors can be built or enhanced in at-risk learners. They write it is "crucial to start using this new knowledge to learn how protective factors can be built or enhanced in at-risk populations to create and support optimal learning environments". Supplementing risk-factor knowledge with protective-factor knowledge could, they argue, add new intervention and treatment options for educational professionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sensitive designs and thorough application of statistical techniques that allow discerning between promotive, protective, and skill-enhancing effects
#### Constraints
- The editors state that experimental or intervention studies manipulating protective factors currently seem too advanced for a field still building a sufficient knowledge base

### Target Learners
- At-risk learners in reading, writing, and mathematics, including learners with specific learning disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Fostering resilient developmental trajectories in foundational academic skills
