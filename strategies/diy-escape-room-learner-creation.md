# Invite learners to create their own games with the same free tools the designers used

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At the end of the experience, D-UDA publishes the hyperlinks and descriptions of the free online tools used to design and program the unit, encouraging children to build their own riddles and escape rooms and send them to the authors. The article states: "At the end of all the activities, we listed the free online tools used to design and program D-UDA." This DIY section aims to foster creativity and technological development, and the choice of free tools was made to include children with economic difficulties.
