# Augment complex diagnostic test result presentations with intuitive, easy-to-understand visualizations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 2 mixed) · 5 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 causal, 1 review, 1 theoretical), `q3`–`q4` · 3 of 5 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that designers of complex diagnostic displays preprocess numerical results into intuitive visualizations that reduce users' unnecessary cognitive load. They suggest that "relevant viscoelastic test manufacturers consider augmenting their complex result presentation with intuitive, easy-to-understand visualization to ease users' burden from unnecessary cognitive load and enhance patient care." The recommendation rests on the simulation findings that the Visual Clot display improved correctness, speed, and confidence of targeted coagulation decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The visualization must present results in a preprocessed form with distinct differentiable states while leaving the final decision to the user.
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from a single-center high-fidelity simulation study in a tertiary care hospital; the authors state results may vary under different circumstances.

### Target Learners
- clinicians interpreting point-of-care diagnostic test results, such as anesthesia teams

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate and fast interpretation of complex diagnostic test results
- informed treatment decision-making under time pressure
