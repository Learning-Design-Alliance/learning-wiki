# Optimize Classroom Acoustics and Noise Levels

## Description
Classroom noise levels significantly impact teacher and student performance. Excessive noise leads to dissatisfaction and stress. Schools with classrooms that have less external noise are positively associated with greater student engagement and achievement. Building schools or modifying classrooms to buffer external noise can improve student outcomes. Minimizing noise creates a more conducive learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Acoustic materials, soundproofing, strategic classroom layout, noise-canceling headphones (optional)
#### Constraints
- Implementation can be costly. Effectiveness may vary based on the type and source of noise.
