# Identify below-grade-level readers each fall and provide tutoring across the full school year

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's implementation strategy is to screen and select participants at the start of the year and sustain tutoring throughout it: the program "identifies students reading below grade level each fall and provides tutoring across the school year." This pairs fall identification with yearlong daily service rather than short-term pull-out support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fall literacy assessments to identify students reading below grade level
- Capacity to provide tutoring across the school year
#### Constraints
- Targets students scoring at the Tier 2 level on literacy assessments

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through grade 2 students reading below grade level

### Target Learning Goals
- Bringing below-grade-level readers up in literacy
