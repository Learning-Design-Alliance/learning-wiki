# Design future DD consultations with more preparation time, varied activities and broader project context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants' recommendations for future deliberative democracy experiments in higher education. Students and educators "identified a range of possibilities to improve the process for future DD experiments, such as more time and space to prepare and enhance contributions within the group, alongside ways to surface broader project linkages and test the final output". Specific suggestions included more homework between sessions, more varied activities and peer/expert collaboration, advance preparation and process overviews, continually updated information packs, more diverse facilitation, and involving experts in earlier workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional willingness to allocate more time and preparation space than the seven-week pilot allowed
- Communication platforms, while recognising not everyone likes using them
#### Constraints
- The limited timeframe means the final output will not be perfect, as educators noted

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- educators and tutors

### Target Learning Goals
- effective participation in deliberative co-production of ethical principles
