# Planned enhancements: multi-turn conversational follow-up, RAG-based personalization, and feedback analytics for AI-assisted instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors outline future iterations of the instructional assistant: interactive or multi-turn LLM feedback so students can ask follow-up questions simulating a conversational tutoring experience; Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines to tailor explanations to each student's feedback history and the course's exact terminology; sentiment analysis of feedback; and personalization of the output's tone, depth, or format based on individual learner preferences. These are stated plans, not evaluated features.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A deployed LLM-assisted instruction system already collecting student feedback
#### Constraints
- None of these enhancements has been evaluated in the present study; they are described as plans

### Target Learners
- university students in cybersecurity and, planned, courses beyond cybersecurity

### Target Learning Goals
- personalized, conversational clarification of instructional content aligned with course terminology
