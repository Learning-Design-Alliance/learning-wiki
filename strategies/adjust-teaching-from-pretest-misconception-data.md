# Adjust teaching and learning to reinforce concepts flagged by pre-test misconception data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the FCI is administered before instruction, its question-level results can inform teaching. The authors write that "the course instructors could attempt to adjust the teaching and learning to try to reinforce such a concept", using pre-test identification of dominant misconceptions to target instruction where the cohort's understanding is weakest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-test data must be collected and analysed at question level before teaching begins
#### Constraints
- Preliminary single-institution study; the authors note transient biases may exist in the current cohort and hope to extend data taking across future years and other South African universities with similar demographics

### Target Learners
- first-year university physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- reinforcing poorly understood mechanics concepts such as circular motion
