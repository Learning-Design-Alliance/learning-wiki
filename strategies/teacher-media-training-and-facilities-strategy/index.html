Build teacher media skills and classroom media facilities through training, resource centres and equipment provision¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

From its recommendations, the article proposes system-level actions to support consistent use of instructional media: introducing "variegated media sys tem to address the different course contents and teaching methods", having Educational Resource Centres acquire modern instructional materials and organise workshops and seminars to upgrade teachers' skills, and making classrooms conducive with "Facilities for both projected and non-projected media of instruction" installed. It argues educational policies should ensure teachers utilise instructional media consistently in teaching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Media skills can be impacted to teachers through in-house or out of house training, induction seminars, workshops and conferences.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Secondary school students in under-resourced regions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved learning outcomes through consistent use of instructional media

Claims¶

  • Secondary students taught the human digestive system with an animated instructional DVD significantly outperformed orally taught controls at all three Bauchi locations [+W]
  • The article reports that students taught with animated instructional material showed retention ability beyond those taught without it [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Layered educator support: coaching, curated resources, low-lift entry points, and community engagement
  • Prepare and test technology before launch, scaffold the learning curve, and leverage tech-savvy students as peer resources
  • Equip secondary classrooms with computers and data show devices and train teachers to implement e-learning
