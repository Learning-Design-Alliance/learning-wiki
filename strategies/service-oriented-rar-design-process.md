# Design a service-oriented resource allocation review using continuous improvement and design thinking, engaging LEAs and schools in design, pilot, and test phases before rollout.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that states draw from "principles of continuous improvement and design thinking" to create a process supporting educational leaders from an end-user perspective at each design stage. Engaging LEAs and schools in design, pilot, and test phases before rollout is said to mitigate frustration and distinguish immediate challenges addressable short term from systemic challenges addressable long term.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The process should minimize burden and be meaningful to educational leaders, for example by asking participating LEAs and schools to signal the best time for the review aligned with local planning and budget development.
