# Target remedial interventions specifically at vulnerable students after pandemic-related school closures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's forward-looking policy recommendation is that responses to pandemic learning loss should concentrate resources on disadvantaged students rather than the full population. The authors state that "the results call for a national focus on interventions specifically targeting vulnerable students", because learning losses were concentrated among students from lower-educated and poorer families who had less access to additional resources at home. This follows from the finding that losses were unequally distributed across parental education and income groups.
