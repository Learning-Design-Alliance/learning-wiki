# Align CPD activities, programs, and systems so they function in collaboration to support physician SDL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that, for the best results in physician engagement in SDL in CPD and measurable changes to clinical practice, "the different activities, programs, and systems within CPD need to be aligned with each other and function in collaboration." It further recommends that future studies address knowledge gaps about both physician engagement and SDL developer implementation, since implementation depends on factors affecting the entire spectrum from development and delivery through receipt and enactment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attention to barriers and facilitators at both individual and external levels, for physicians and SDL developers alike
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a scoping review of 17 Canadian articles published 2005–2015, limited to English-language scholarly literature

### Target Learners
- physicians in continuing professional development
- CPD program developers and deliverers

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement in self-directed learning and measurable changes to clinical practice
