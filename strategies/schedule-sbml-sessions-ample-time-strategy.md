# Schedule SBML sessions with ample, non-limiting practice time and offer additional sessions for learners who need them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because mastery learning requires unlimited deliberate practice until the minimum passing standard is met, the authors recommend that schools "recommend scheduling SBML sessions with ample time to accommodate all learners and offering additional training sessions for some learners as needed." In this study sessions lasted two hours with two faculty facilitators and a maximum of 16 students, and each student averaged 15 minutes to complete baseline testing, deliberate practice, and post-testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty time and simulation supplies for deliberate practice and proficiency testing.
#### Constraints
- Two subjects unable to reach mastery were attributed to the two-hour institutional time limit on sessions.

### Target Learners
- Medical students in pre-clinical and clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- Reaching a pre-defined procedural proficiency standard (e.g., PPE donning and doffing)

## Claims

- [Two pre-clinical students with very limited clinical exposure did not reach mastery after more than four post-testing attempts within a two-hour session, in one single-group pre-post study.](../claims/sbml-requires-time-unlimited-deliberate-practice.md) [+W]
- [Fellows required an average of 4.5 training sessions (about 90 minutes of individualized bedside instruction) to reach the mastery learning standard, and 7 of 11 recruited fellows met it](../claims/mastery-learning-sessions-to-mps-echocardiography.md) [+W]
- [Novices reach the minimum passing LISA-AT score of 39/40 in a median of 6 attempts (range 4–9) within one 90-minute simulation session with per-attempt feedback](../claims/lisa-at-mastery-six-attempts.md) [~W]
