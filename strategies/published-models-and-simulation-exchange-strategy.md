# Use previously published, validated, easy-to-build simulation models and support inter-institutional simulation exchange

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Rather than designing every model from scratch, the authors note "there are numerous previously published, validated and easy-to-build options in the literature we intend to take advantage of," citing a 3D-printed myringotomy simulator and a gelatin facial skin simulator. They also "see opportunities to support simulation exchange, whereby institutions or departments share models following use," reducing duplication of design effort across programs.
