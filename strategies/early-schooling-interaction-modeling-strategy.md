# Conduct interaction-based modeling in early schooling to reduce transmission of alternative conceptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends applying the interaction-centered model early: "the study of synthetic learning models based on interactions should be conducted in early schooling to create the possibility of very early action and therefore a better chance of success". Acting upstream, before alternative conceptions are entrenched, means fewer are transmitted to university and adulthood. The model can nevertheless be re-established and readjusted at any point of schooling as interactions evolve through time.
