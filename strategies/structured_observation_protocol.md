# Structured Observation Protocol

## Description
A structured observation protocol involves using a template or guide to record factual details about a student's experiences without interpretation, opinion, or judgment. For instance, note the student's body language, facial expressions, and interactions with teachers and peers. The protocol also encourages gathering perceptions of the school from teachers, staff, students, parents, and community members. After shadowing, summarize and organize notes to pinpoint areas needing attention and reflect on the insights gained.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student Shadowing Template from racialquityleadership.com, a willing student, time to shadow the student for a significant portion of the school day, time for reflection and analysis of observations.
#### Constraints
- Potential for observer bias, may not capture the full complexity of a student's experience, and requires a high degree of self-awareness for the observer. Requires a trusting environment.
