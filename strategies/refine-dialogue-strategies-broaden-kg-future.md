## Description
The authors recommend future work on refining the framework's dialogue strategies to accommodate various learning styles and broadening the scope of knowledge graphs to cover a wider spectrum of disciplines. They also flag the potential integration of emergent technologies to create more immersive and interactive learning environments, underscoring the framework's transformative potential in educational methodologies.
