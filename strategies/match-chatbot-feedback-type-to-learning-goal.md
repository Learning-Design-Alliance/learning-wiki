# Integrate diverse feedback types into educational chatbots, matched to instructional goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that chatbot systems integrate distinct feedback types selected for the target outcome: metacognitive feedback (targeted reflective questions after self-assessment) to promote transfer and metacognitive sensitivity, and affective feedback (praise and encouragement) to support retention and emotional processing. The authors state that "educators can tailor feedback to achieve various instructional goals" and that "educators should consider integrating metacognitive feedback into human-chatbot interaction to guide students in continuously mon- itoring and re ﬂecting on their learning processes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Chatbot must deliver feedback consistently after self-assessment phases; the study standardized delivery at the same frequency (15 times) with a uniform number of Chinese characters.
#### Constraints
- Feedback was not personalized, which the authors note could influence effectiveness; generalizability is limited by sample size, gender composition, and fNIRS coverage of only frontal and right temporoparietal regions.

### Target Learners
- college students learning biology concepts via chatbot

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge retention
- knowledge transfer
- metacognitive sensitivity
- self-regulated learning
