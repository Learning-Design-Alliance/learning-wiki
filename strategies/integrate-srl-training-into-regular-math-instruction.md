# Integrate brief classroom-based SRL strategy training into regular math instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical implication is that SRL interventions can be delivered in regular classroom settings to whole classes rather than individuals. The authors state that "the intervention was delivered in a classroom setting, which underscores its adaptability and ecological validity for groups of students rather than individuals", and that "even a brief SRL programme can lead to improvements" in both math performance and math-related motivational factors maintained over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions targeting the planning, monitoring and evaluation phases, with strategy checklists and math practice aligned to regular curricular content
#### Constraints
- The authors note the importance of "ongoing support and curricular alignment of SRL interventions to maintain improvements" in constructs where no persistent enhancement was observed, and that effects may partly reflect researcher versus teacher delivery

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in regular math classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Math performance, math perseverance, theory of intelligence and math utility value

### Affordances
- [Srl Cyclical Model Phases Theory](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md)
