# Extend e-PBL programs to mobile devices and other care topics, and evaluate long-term effects

## Description
The article's stated future directions are to adapt the program "to a mobile device environment" to enhance accessibility, to apply the approach to nursing problems other than infection control, and to conduct longer-duration studies. The authors propose "the development of a study on the long-term effects of the program and the development of mobile device education content" as next steps based on the present findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Redesign of the Chrome-optimized program for mobile environments
- Longer training or follow-up periods to measure durable effects
#### Constraints
- The current study could not measure long-term change in critical thinking disposition or infection incidence

### Target Learners
- Nursing home staff and practitioners in residential care

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical thinking disposition for infection management and other resident-care competencies
