# Extend supply-side 1:1 help to student teaching roles and use sessions as repeatable teaching practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on TeachNow's teacher-initiated design, the authors recommend opening the helping role to students and treating sessions as a training ground for teaching practice. They argue that allowing "students to take on teaching roles through TeachNow" would "increase the number of people who can be helped, while also reinforcing the understanding of the student who is teaching", citing evidence that near-peers are highly effective tutors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A functioning TeachNow-style matching system with a supply of vetted helpers and a mechanism to identify promising students to take on teaching roles
#### Constraints
- The article presents these as future directions and near-peer effectiveness from cited literature, not as results tested with TeachNow in this deployment

### Target Learners
- MOOC students, including promising students who could take on teaching roles

### Target Learning Goals
- Receiving 1:1 help at greater scale and deepening student understanding through teaching
