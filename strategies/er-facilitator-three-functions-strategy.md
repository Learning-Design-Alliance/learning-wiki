# Facilitator roles in educational escape rooms: prebriefing, gamemastering, and debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review identifies three integral facilitator functions in EERs: "conducting prebriefing sessions to introduce the EER, taking on the gamemaster role during the exercise, and conducting debriefing sessions afterward." In prebriefing, facilitators explained objectives and rules; in 17 EERs facilitators monitored progress and offered guidance or puzzle clues; some also acted as assessors. Debriefing, in-person or virtual, was present in 35 of the 52 studies, though some studies included no debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies suggest one facilitator for each group of three to six participants is optimal for group-based tasks
#### Constraints
- Some reviewed studies did not include any form of post-EER debriefing

### Target Learners
- healthcare students working in teams of two to twelve

### Target Learning Goals
- internalization of skills through structured debriefing
- reflection on accomplishments
