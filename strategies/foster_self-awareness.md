# Foster Self-Awareness

## Description
Self-awareness is the ability to correctly identify your own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they impact behavior. This strategy involves helping students develop a greater understanding of themselves by leveraging their strengths to address challenges. Strategies include: Identify Feelings, Journal Reflections, Use Estimation and Prediction, and Develop Your Understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an understanding of self-awareness principles, knowledge of specific techniques (e.g., journaling, literature analysis), and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing practice and reflection. Effectiveness depends on student engagement and willingness to explore their emotions and thoughts.
