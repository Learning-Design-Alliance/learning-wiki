# Educators should engage with explainable AI tools, teach critical evaluation of AI outputs, and feed usability insights back to developers

## Description
The paper recommends that teachers actively use AI tools that prioritize explainability, advocate for transparency in AI-based decision-making, and teach students to "question and evaluate AI outputs" critically. Educators should also collaborate with developers, providing usability and effectiveness feedback from real classrooms so HCXAI systems better serve both teachers and students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need to understand how the AI systems they use function and the rationale behind their outputs before they can guide students in interpreting AI-generated insights.
