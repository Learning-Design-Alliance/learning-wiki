## Description
As its forward-looking agenda, the article argues for educational psychology research examining "how learners may be consciously, reflectively, and critically engaged in using texts to understand their world". This strategy directs instruction and research toward deliberate, reflective, critical engagement with texts as instruments for understanding learners' own worlds.
