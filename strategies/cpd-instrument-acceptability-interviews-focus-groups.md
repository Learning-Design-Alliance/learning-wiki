# Phase 4 qualitative assessment of instrument acceptability through interviews and focus groups with four stakeholder groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To gather anticipated barriers and enablers for implementing the new instrument in CPD practice, the protocol plans individual interviews and focus groups with participants purposefully selected from four groups: Québec's key stakeholder CPD organizations, primary care physicians, specialists, and representatives of Québec's Ministry of Health and Social Services. Interviews and focus groups will be tape-recorded, transcribed verbatim, and analysed with a constant comparative method using NVivo software. Internal validity includes "member checking", whereby summaries of interpretations are returned to participants for comments and corrections.
