# Free Choice Time: Student-Directed Learning

## Description
Providing learners with choice in activities promotes engagement, responsibility, and independence. Allowing learners to choose their reading material, writing topics, and project formats increases motivation and investment in learning tasks. When learners perceive tasks as meaningful and content as valuable to their future lives, they are more likely to engage deeply and learn effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom or learning environment where learners can make choices. A variety of reading materials, writing prompts, and project ideas. Instructor willingness to allow learner autonomy.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find it challenging to make choices. Instructors need to manage the variety of activities to ensure alignment with learning objectives.
