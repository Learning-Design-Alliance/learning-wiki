# Classical Music for Ambiance

## Description
Playing classical music (e.g., Bach) at a low volume when students enter the room sets a professional and calming tone. The music helps create a focused atmosphere and can reduce disruptive behavior. However, it's essential to monitor student reactions to ensure they are comfortable with the music.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to music and playback equipment, selection of appropriate music, monitoring of student reactions.
#### Constraints
- May not appeal to all students, some students may find it distracting, requires careful monitoring and adjustment.
