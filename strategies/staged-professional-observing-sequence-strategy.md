# Staged sequence for student participation in professional observing: bibliography study, remote attendance at a training observing run, then conducting observations themselves under supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article used a three-stage preparation before students observed independently. First, students read the literature on Venus clouds, the SpeX instrument, and the control interfaces. Second, they received training by remotely attending a run: "the students beneﬁted from a training session by remotely attending an observing run during 17 of February 2022 (2022A058)". Third, they conducted observations themselves on 11 March 2022 under supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- students first use the bibliography to become introduced to the target, the instrument, and the graphical user interfaces before observing
#### Constraints
- the independent run was supervised: the student controlling the interfaces was supervised by Javier Peralta

### Target Learners
- secondary school students aged 15-16

### Target Learning Goals
- observational astronomy methods
- operation of professional telescope and instrument control software
- knowledge of Venus and its atmosphere
