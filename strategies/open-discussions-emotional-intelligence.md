# Follow experiential learning opportunities with open discussions focusing on emotional intelligence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "the learning opportunities need to be followed by open discussions focusing on emotional intelligence: ways to express and manage the complicated emotions nurses may experience during the provision of end-of-life care once in practice". This strategy addresses the emotional labour of end-of-life care, building on findings that students experienced sadness and guilt and that identifying emotional labour is a first step towards emotional intelligence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning opportunities that first elicit emotional responses, followed by facilitated open discussion in a safe and supportive environment
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of one qualitative study of students' reflections; effects on later clinical practice were not assessed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- emotional intelligence and management of emotional labour in end-of-life care
