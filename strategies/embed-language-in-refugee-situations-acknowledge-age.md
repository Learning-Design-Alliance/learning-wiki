# Embed grammar and vocabulary in situations refugees will encounter, and design instruction that acknowledges age

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The digest recommends basing teaching on needs analyses and learner consultation, since when "grammar and vocabulary areembedded in the situations refugees will encounter" learners come to class and risk using the language outside it. Environmentally, it advises listening and viewing components, good lighting, minimal noise, collaborative comprehension-focused activities, and avoiding fast-paced drills, extensive pronunciation correction and competition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Needs analyses of the target populations in a given community should be carried out, and adult learners should be consulted about what they want to learn.
#### Constraints
- Class activities which include large amountsof oral repetition or fast-paced drills, extensivepronunciation correction, or competitive exerciseswill inhibit the older refugee's active participation.

### Target Learners
- elderly refugees in adult ESL classes

### Target Learning Goals
- practical English for specific purposes learners deem important
