Use computer-based instruction with observation, collaboration, and semi-structured guidance, plus online metacognitive guidance, to enhance SRL in mathematics¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review identifies computer-based and online instructional methods as viable routes to enhancing self-regulated learning in mathematics. It reports a "Significant increase in metacognition and problem-solving of primary class students after Computer-based instructional method within an authentic context consisting of three main phases: observation, collaboration and semi-structured guidance", and that meta-cognitive guidance in online or face-to-face discussion attained a higher level of mathematical literacy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instruction should be embedded in an authentic context with the three phases of observation, collaboration and semi-structured guidance, as the reviewed studies implemented.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes middle-school students exhibited a more maladaptive self-regulation and motivation profile than sixth graders, so support needs differ by grade level.

Target Learners¶

  • primary and middle school mathematics students
  • secondary education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • metacognition and problem-solving in mathematics
  • mathematical literacy
  • self-regulated problem-solving skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide explicit teaching of SRL methods as structured guidance for regulating learning in online and blended settings
