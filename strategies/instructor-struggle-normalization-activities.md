# Use beginning-of-course instructor activities that normalize struggle in learning physics to eliminate the gender gap in performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 against) · 6 studies (3 associational, 1 causal, 1 review, 1 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports, citing prior research, that short instructor-led activities at the start of a course can close performance gaps. When instructors emphasize "that most students struggle in learning to solve challenging physics problems, that struggle is the stepping stone to learning physics," while discussing their own past struggles, the article states the gender gap in performance is eliminated. Instructors are also encouraged to reflect on whether all students can excel in their courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities occur at the beginning of the course and instructors discuss their own struggles when they were in similar courses
#### Constraints
- The article attributes this recommendation to prior research rather than testing it in this study

### Target Learners
- women and traditionally marginalized students in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- eliminate gender gaps in performance
- promote equity in college courses
