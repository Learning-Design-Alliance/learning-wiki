# Rhyming Games

## Description
Rhyming games involve activities that focus on identifying and creating rhyming words to enhance phonological awareness. These games often include matching rhyming words, drawing rhyming pictures, and creating word family charts. Direct instruction on rhyme recognition, paired with engaging activities, helps children develop essential reading and spelling skills. Games can be adapted for individual, small group, or whole-class settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials vary by game but may include rhyming books, picture cards, markers, chart paper, and printable activities. Preparation involves gathering materials and setting up game stations.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness relies on clear instructions and targeted activities. Some games may require significant preparation time. Ensure diversity in activities to maintain learner engagement.

### Personalization
- Picture-based rhyming for younger and struggling learners. Writing and creating activities for advanced learners. Diagrams and templates for scaffolding.
