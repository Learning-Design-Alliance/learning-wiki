# Allow cooperative teams to take midterm exams collaboratively while keeping final exams individual

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In CTBL, "team members take final exams individually as it is in CIRC, STAD, and TGT, they take midterm exams, tests or quizzes – but not finals - cooperatively." The author's stated philosophy is "to subordinate testing to teaching," and he argues this strategy creates opportunities for metacognitive transference of skills and strategies as students listen to teammates thinking aloud.
