Pursue open AIED-in-MOOCs research directions: sensing-based affective modeling for embodied adaptive learning and MOOC data visualization for stakeholder feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The editors name open issues the AIED community should address next. First, "how to take advantage of current sensing capabilities to model users' interactions in mobile-based settings, with the aim to model affective aspects of the learners' interactions, so as to enhance adaptivity in MOOCs, towards an embodied learning experience". Second, visualization of data collected in MOOCs to allow further insights about course pedagogy and outcomes and support meaningful feedback for all involved stakeholders. These are forward-looking recommendations, not tested results.
