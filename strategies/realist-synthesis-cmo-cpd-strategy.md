# Develop CPD strategies using realist synthesis CMO analysis (context-mechanism-outcome) with multi-stakeholder input

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This study demonstrates a methodological strategy for developing CPD strategies: an embedded case study using realist synthesis to identify "what mechanisms in what contexts would generate what desirable CPD outcomes." The approach bridges assumed ease of identifying change factors with practical challenges of recognizing change in frontline practice. It involved iterative phases of conceptual framework development, online survey testing, and consensus workshop refinement, with ERG validation at each stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to multiple data sources (literature, survey, workshop)
- Stakeholder engagement across CPD roles
- Expert reference group for validation of outputs.
#### Constraints
- Conducted in one NHS Trust in South East England, which may limit generalizability of the specific CMO relationships identified.

### Target Learners
- CPD stakeholders
- Professional development facilitators
- Organizational leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing strategies for effective CPD
- Understanding context-mechanism-outcome relationships in professional learning
