# Co-design simulated patient scenarios with clinicians, students, simulated patients, and patients using participatory design and patient/public involvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that physical therapist educators experienced in SBE use participatory design to collaborate with interested parties—including clinicians, students, SPs, and patients—to design and develop SP scenarios tailored to learners and context. Incorporating patient and public involvement (PPI) in SBE curriculum design is presented as crucial for ensuring learning outcomes are relevant and beneficial, helping educators create meaningful and realistic simulated scenarios that reflect the complexity and diversity of actual patient care.

## Design Implications
