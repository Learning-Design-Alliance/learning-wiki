# Use video guides and workbooks as supplementary resources to address resource intensity of hands-on simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The focus group consensus gravitated towards integrating video-based guides as preferred supplemental resources alongside workbooks, to address the time and resource intensity of comprehensive simulations especially for larger cohorts. Videos allow students to "replay video clips repeatedly until they have mastered a concept, method, or step within the planning software", supporting self-evaluation at their own pace; the article cites evidence this leads to enhanced assessment results and increased confidence. Direct engagement remained preferable for skill development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workbook-based platform as an interim solution where video development is deferred due to module launch constraints and limited teaching preparation resources
#### Constraints
- Video aids were deferred to the subsequent academic year due to imminent constraints related to the module launch and limited resource availability

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate radiotherapy planning students in large cohorts

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-directed mastery of planning software steps
- Catch-up on missed sessions via remote workbook learning
