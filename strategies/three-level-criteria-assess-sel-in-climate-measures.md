# Apply the review's three-level criteria to critically assess SEL integration in a school climate measure before adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review offers a three-level coding scheme for judging how a school climate measure embeds SEL support: (1) whether it includes an explicitly SEL-oriented subscale, (2) whether it includes items operationalizing social and emotional skills in students, and (3) whether it includes subscales the literature shows to be conceptually related to SEL. The authors state: "We encourage researchers and practitioners to apply our three-level criteria, to critically assess if a given school climate measure satisfies the integration of SEL support to be implemented in their settings."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the measure's full subscale and item content, since nine measures lacked publicly available item lists
#### Constraints
- Level 3 provides evidence of conceptual linkage, not direct assessment of SEL support

### Target Learners
- Upper middle and secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Selecting school climate measures that adequately integrate support for social and emotional learning
