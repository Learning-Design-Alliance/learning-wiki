# Select long-duration, preparation-heavy, condition-changing and special-condition projects for networked laboratory study

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author recommends which projects are better served by the WWSL than by traditional labs: "projects that require a long time to complete", projects requiring certain preliminary theoretical and/or practical preparation, projects requiring repeated change of experimental conditions, and projects requiring special conditions for their fulfilment. Traditional forms remain preferable for other laboratory studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects must be suited to remote control and networked data collection
#### Constraints
- The traditional form of conducting some experiments is still preferable for laboratory studies
