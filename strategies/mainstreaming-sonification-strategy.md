# Mainstream astronomy sonification through curriculum integration, community standards, and rigorous quantitative evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes concrete steps to make sonification mainstream in astronomy. It recommends "Introducing attentive listening and soni- ﬁcation tools into the mainstream edu- cation curriculum, in tandem with data visualisation" as a first step toward training the next generation, alongside adopting universally accepted standards and systematic quantitative evaluation of sonification efficacy. It further urges the community to define goals from the beginning of the design phase and carry out a rigorous evaluation plan, and to publish peer-reviewed findings, sonify conference graphs and journal figures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully defined goals from the beginning of the design phase and a rigorous evaluation plan, possibly following guidelines highlighted by Lenzi et al., Giordano et al. and Misdariis et al.
#### Constraints
- Cultural differences, such as the use of different musical scales and harmonies, are one challenge to the creation of universally accepted standards

### Target Learners
- astronomy researchers
- educators
- school pupils
- blind and visually impaired learners

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis and interpretation skills
- attentive listening for complex information
- accessible astronomy education
