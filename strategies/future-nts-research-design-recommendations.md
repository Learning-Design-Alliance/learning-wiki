# Adopt cluster-randomised or stepped-wedge trial designs with blinded, video-based assessment and pre-defined process and patient endpoints in future NTS training research.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research "adopt cluster-randomised or stepped-wedge trials that balance internal validity with feasibility in dynamic ED environments" and ensure allocation concealment, fidelity assessment, and transparent attrition reporting. For measurement, it recommends "blinded, video-based assessment with predefined competency anchors, rater training, and reporting of inter-rater reliability". For outcomes, it recommends predefining mechanism-linked process endpoints and powering for patient-centred outcomes with longitudinal follow-up to examine decay and booster effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-site pragmatic designs
- Blinded video-based assessment with predefined competency anchors and rater training
- Pre-specified process and patient endpoints
#### Constraints
- Cluster-randomised or stepped-wedge designs must balance internal validity with feasibility in dynamic ED environments.

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals working in emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Rigorous evaluation of NTS training effects on team performance and patient-centred outcomes
