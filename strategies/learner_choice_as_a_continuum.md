# Learner Choice as a Continuum

## Description
Learner choice involves transitioning from teacher-led content delivery to learner-driven purpose discovery. It includes a spectrum of roles from providing a menu of options to empowering learners as designers, advocates, and entrepreneurs who self-regulate learning based on their passions and purposes. The teacher moves from providing options to inviting input from learners, while the learner identifies challenges and strategies, expanding their purpose through business creation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to be open to learner input and willing to relinquish some control over content and process. Requires a flexible curriculum that allows for adaptation based on learner interests.
#### Constraints
- Learners may struggle to make choices if they lack experience or confidence. Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure choices align with learning objectives. Some learners may choose options that are not challenging enough or that reinforce existing knowledge.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Increases motivation by allowing learners to pursue areas of interest. Encourages self-direction and responsibility for learning. Fosters a sense of ownership and agency.

### Personalization
- Learner choice can be adapted to different levels by starting with a menu of options and gradually increasing the level of autonomy. Options can be structured based on learner readiness or interests. Can be tailored to different subjects and learning goals.

## Assessment Evidence
- Learner engagement and motivation can be measured through observation and self-report surveys. The quality of learner work can be assessed using criteria aligned with learning objectives. Reflective writing can provide insights into learner experiences and perceptions of ownership.

## Impact
- Research indicates that learner choice increases motivation and engagement, leading to improved learning outcomes. Studies show that self-directed learning fosters a learner mindset and prepares students for lifelong learning.
