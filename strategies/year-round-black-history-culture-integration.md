# Year-round integration of Black history, culture, and contributions across curriculum, environment, and programming

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Under the Embracing Black Identity element, the brief recommends the Center "Integrate Black history" by incorporating "Black history, culture, and contributions throughout the year while highlighting modern accomplishments and scholars." Supporting strategies include cultural programming and academic competitions focused on Black inventors and STEM contributors, lessons in art, music, dance, and ancestral traditions, and incorporating Black identities and achievements into murals, materials, décor, and media. The aim is that students feel grounded in their ancestry and express pride freely and confidently.
