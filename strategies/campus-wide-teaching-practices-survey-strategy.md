# Institutions should implement campus-wide faculty teaching practices surveys to establish baselines, measure initiative impact, and inform strategic planning.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that other institutions consider implementing surveys similar to UBCV's 2014 Teaching Practices Survey, which gathered baseline information on teaching practices and attitudes, measured the impact of teaching initiatives, and informed strategic decisions at institutional and Faculty levels. The survey instrument combined modified LEAD survey components with BVA and AAU additions, validated through faculty and stakeholder feedback. Feedback received from stakeholders across the institution indicates value in having data of this nature, and comparative data from future surveys will be even more useful.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional ethics review and approval
- Collaboration between teaching and learning support centres
- Faculty-specific survey instances to accommodate variations in context and timing
- Anonymization of data with no analysis on subcategories with fewer than 10 responses
#### Constraints
- Response rates varied widely across faculties (14% to 68%) depending on inclusion criteria
- No incentive for participation was offered

### Target Learners
- Faculty with teaching responsibilities
- Institutional leadership and administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Establishing baseline teaching practices and attitudes
- Measuring impact of teaching and learning initiatives
- Identifying conditions leading to change in teaching practices
