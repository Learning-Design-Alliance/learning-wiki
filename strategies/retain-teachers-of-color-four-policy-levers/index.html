Underwrite comprehensive preparation, improve teaching conditions, raise compensation, and expand induction to retain teachers of color¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's policy implications section recommends four levers for policymakers: "Provide Greater Access to Comprehensive Preparation", "Improve Teaching Conditions", "Increase Teacher Compensation", and improve access to mentoring and induction. Concrete mechanisms include underwriting preparation cost "through service scholarship and loan forgiveness programs as well as supporting high-quality teacher residencies and apprenticeships", equitable school funding formulas, working conditions surveys, salary increases, refundable tax credits, housing subsidies, and strong induction programs for early-career teachers of color.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Federal, state, and local agency action to fund preparation, conditions, compensation, and induction supports

Constraints¶

  • The report notes 2020–21 NTPS teaching conditions data may be idiosyncratic at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers of color in the U.S. public school workforce
  • Early-career teachers in their first 5 years

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher retention
  • Access to comprehensive preservice preparation
  • Supportive teaching conditions and mentoring

Related Strategies¶

  • Seven-policy comprehensive approach to equalizing student access to certified and experienced teachers
  • Build high-retention, supportive pathways into teaching for candidates of color
  • Sustain and expand residency pathways through maintained state grant funding, financial aid, apprenticeship structures, and technical assistance
  • A federal Marshall Plan for Teaching focused on seven areas: compensation, debt-free preparation, high-retention pathways, mentoring, expertise-sharing, school redesign, and accountability
  • State and federal policy recommendations: recruit and prepare more candidates, retain new teachers through early-career mentoring, and improve working conditions
  • State policy actions across the career continuum: attract, prepare, and develop/support/retain educators
