# Use identified programme weaknesses to inform redesign so that teachers can be further supported

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that identifying weaknesses in the EGRS programme — such as undifferentiated independent tasks and teachers' gaps in content knowledge for group guided reading — should feed programme redesign. It states that "Identifying these weaknesses in the EGRS programme is useful for programme redesign so that teachers can be further supported." It also names four further issues: designing a transition from formalism to comprehension-focused instruction, scaling from 50 to 20,000 schools, reforming upper levels of schooling, and addressing binding policy and organisational constraints.
