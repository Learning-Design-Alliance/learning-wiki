# Return graded three-tier tests, guide student reflection on learning-risk topics, and re-evaluate to monitor progress

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's didactic dimension: after the enriched three-tier test, papers are returned to students so they can identify, using the teacher's instructions, the topics where they exhibit learning risks. The teacher must "stimulate and guide the students in a reflection on what it was that led them into the zone of learning risk", and after adequate time give a new evaluation, accumulative or formative, to monitor progress of the identified risks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Test papers must be returned to the students, either temporarily or permanently.
- The teacher must stimulate and guide student reflection and guide them toward correct conceptual change.
#### Constraints
- Students do not need to be given the statistical indices or the bar charts; they can freely compare with classmates and consult the teacher as they deem necessary.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive awareness of one's own learning risks and conceptual change in physics topics

## Claims

- [In an electromagnetism item, 26% chose the correct answer, about 6% gave the ideal response, 32% omitted, and all wrong answers (42% of the class) fell in the learning-risk zone.](../claims/electromagnetism-item-learning-risk-zone-42-percent.md) [+W]
- [False positives (categories 1, 2 and 3) were no more than 1% of total responses, which the authors attribute to the required justification tier discouraging guessing.](../claims/false-positives-below-one-percent-with-justification-tier.md) [+W]
- [In a mechanics item, traditional multiple-choice scoring showed 75% correct answers while the enriched three-tier spectrum showed only 39% ideal responses (category 9).](../claims/three-tier-spectrum-reveals-fewer-ideal-responses-than-mcq.md) [+W]
