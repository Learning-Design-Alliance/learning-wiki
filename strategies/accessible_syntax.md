# Accessible Syntax

## Description
This strategy involves adjusting the complexity of sentence structures to suit the learner's cognitive capacity. In practice, instructors can provide sentence starters for learners to build more complex sentences, or they can simplify their syntax to reduce cognitive load on Working Memory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of learners' language abilities, ability to provide clear sentence structures and modifications.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful assessment of learners' needs, and instructors must be mindful of not oversimplifying to avoid undermining learners.
