# Incorporate simple oral health questions and a visual mouth check into the first antenatal booking visit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that midwives include oral health promotion in the first antenatal booking visit, since this visit reaches all clients and is an early opportunity to raise awareness of oral health risks. Midwives reported that "it doesn't take long to ask the questions, and look in the mouth", and that two simple questions could be included despite busy clinics. The strategy is supported by the revised Australian antenatal care guidelines, which incorporated oral health promotion into the first antenatal booking visit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- midwives trained in oral health screening and referral (e.g. via the MIOH program)
- referral pathways to public and private dental services
#### Constraints
- time constraints and competing health issues during booking visits were the main barriers reported by some midwives

### Target Learners
- pregnant women attending first antenatal booking visits

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of oral health risks in pregnancy
- good oral hygiene practices
- timely referral for dental care
