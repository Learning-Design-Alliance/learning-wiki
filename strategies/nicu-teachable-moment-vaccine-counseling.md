# Leverage the NICU stay as a teachable moment for structured vaccine counseling by nurses and midwives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that healthcare systems "systematically leverage the NICU as a critical 'teachable moment'" by integrating standardized vaccine counseling protocols such as NETM into routine neonatal care. The NICU is described as a unique clinical environment where parents experience heightened emotional vulnerability and increased receptivity to health-related information, making trust-based communication from nurses and midwives especially impactful. The article further recommends institutional protocols incorporating mandatory, evidence-based vaccine education sessions during the NICU stay.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional protocols supporting vaccine education sessions during NICU stay
- Healthcare professionals systematically trained in structured, interactive counseling approaches
#### Constraints
- Studied only among parents self-reporting vaccine hesitancy or refusal in a single Level III NICU

### Target Learners
- Parents of infants hospitalized in the NICU

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve vaccination uptake
- Strengthen informed vaccination decision-making
