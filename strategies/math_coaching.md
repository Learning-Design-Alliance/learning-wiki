# Math Coaching

## Description
Small groups of learners work together to solve word problems. One learner reads a word problem aloud while the other learners act as 'math coaches' and advise them on which mathematical operation to use in solving the problem. They underline key words in the problem, and follow the flash card mathematical operation that they decide to 'flash.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word problems, flashcards.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful grouping of learners to ensure that all members of the group participate and benefit from the activity.
