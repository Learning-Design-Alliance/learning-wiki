# Recording Devices

## Description
Allowing students to record lessons so they can play them back and go over anything that might be confusing. Encourage them to write down timestamps of when they got lost in their notes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to recording devices or software
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who struggle with self-monitoring or note-taking

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners who face auditory processing challenges

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance comprehension, improve retention, support learners who face auditory processing challenges

### Affordances
- Allows for revisiting content, provides auditory feedback on writing quality, enhances comprehension

### Personalization
- Encourage students to play back the recordings as many times as needed. Allow them to adjust playback speed for a comfortable listening experience.
