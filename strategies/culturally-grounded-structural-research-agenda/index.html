Conduct longitudinal, culturally grounded, structurally informed research and develop Ubuntu-informed instruments in African and Global South higher education¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's concluding recommendation is that "Longitudinal, culturally grounded, and structurally informed research is urgently needed in African and Global South higher education, alongside Ubuntu-informed instrument development." This entails treating structural determinants such as financial stress and food insecurity as primary explanatory factors rather than background covariates, and recognising validation studies as rigorous scholarly contributions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Investment in culturally grounded instrument development and validation resources.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes structural determinants remain insufficiently theorised in the current literature, treated as background variables rather than primary explanatory factors.

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate university students in African and Global South contexts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving student mental health support and academic retention and completion

Affordances¶

  • Ubuntu Informed Relational Resilience Framework

Claims¶

  • Western-developed instruments dominated measurement, with cultural adaptation reported in only five of 22 Global South studies of resilience [+W]
  • The evidence base is geographically uneven: no study simultaneously examined all three constructs within a sub-Saharan African context [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Recommendations for culturally responsive school belonging research: broader cross-cultural sampling, stronger methodologies, multilevel theorizing, and qualitative/mixed methods
  • Advance value-creating-modeled curriculum projects in Africana communities to foster ubuntu, happiness, and contributive lifestyles
  • Prioritise equity-centred approaches, methodological diversity, contextual specificity, implementation science and interdisciplinary collaboration in GenAI research
  • Researchers developing technology integration surveys should explicitly define TI, ground instruments in theory, and report validity and reliability information.
  • Research agenda: develop an existential hope scale, then test the framework with SEM and network analysis in longitudinal designs
