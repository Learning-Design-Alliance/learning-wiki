# Use NACADA's structuring factors to situate advising as a core institutional function

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends situating advising as a core function aligned with the institution's mission and goals. "Tools and resources, such as the National Academic Advising Association (NACADA)'s Factors to Consider When (Re)Structuring Academic Advising (Figure 1.1), can help institutions take stock of their current advising practices" as they integrate advising into the broader system of supports. The factors cover status and need, mission and philosophy, equity, delivery models, advisor caseload, assessment, funding, and implementation.
