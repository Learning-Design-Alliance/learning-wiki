# Reactive Cultural Response

## Description
Reactive Cultural Response is the ability to change the structure, activities, and focus of a class within the school year as you see the personality of a given group of kids emerge. This dynamic process is dependent on a classroom's day-to-day operations and the personalities and challenges that crop up throughout the year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Observation skills, flexibility, ability to adapt activities quickly, willingness to adjust classroom norms.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming, requires strong classroom management skills, may be challenging to balance diverse needs within a single class.
