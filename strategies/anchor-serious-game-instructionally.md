# Anchor the serious game formally and instructionally in follow-up examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From the first distribution experience, the authors draw a deployment recommendation: "In follow-up examinations it is advisable to anchor the game formally and instructionally." They also note that to lower the usage threshold for students and enable testing on a larger scale, the porting and publication of the game to mobile platforms (Android and iOS) was carried out. This strategy applies to introductory physics courses using the Vector Field Game alongside traditional instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal and instructional anchoring within the course rather than informal distribution of a download link
#### Constraints
- Uptake must be sufficient to carry out pre-/post-test evaluations

### Target Learners
- first-term university physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- representational fluency with vector field representations

## Claims

- [Informal distribution of the game without instructions yielded low uptake, preventing a post-test of learning gains](../claims/informal-distribution-low-uptake-no-posttest.md) [+W]
