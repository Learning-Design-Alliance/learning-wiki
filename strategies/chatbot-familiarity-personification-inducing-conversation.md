# Induce conversation through familiarity and personification to make the chatbot approachable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy addresses the interface element: designing learners' feelings, attitudes, and behaviors toward the chatbot. Familiarity guidelines include introducing itself and exchanging greetings, opening with conversations reflecting the team's latest interests, using learners' names and familiar conversational tones, avoiding negative feedback, presenting support in navigational format, and mimicking commonly used messaging apps. Personification guidelines include realistic reactions with interjections and context-appropriate emojis, initiating conversation after prolonged silence, and providing consistent context-based messages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The chatbot must access learners' names and current interests and monitor conversation for prolonged silence
#### Constraints
- The article notes avoiding negative feedback is intended to enhance the chatbot's likability

### Target Learners
- collaborative learning teams in classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- reduced resistance to chatbot use
- social presence
- open communication
