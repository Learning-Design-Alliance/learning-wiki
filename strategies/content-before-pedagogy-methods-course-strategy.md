# Integrate mathematics content with the context of teaching in methods courses, sequencing content before pedagogy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on participants' reports that they could not relate pedagogical activities without content discussion, the article recommends that methods courses address mathematics content "in the context of teaching," present content before pedagogical discussions, and sequence topics from easier to more advanced to accommodate diverse backgrounds. The authors state teacher educators should examine participants' needs before developing learning tools, and that activities should prepare participants for higher thinking levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examine the setting, participants and their needs before developing a learning tool for them
#### Constraints
- Participants still need content preparation before the methods course; the course alone cannot supply in-depth elementary geometry understanding

### Target Learners
- elementary school preservice teachers with limited and varied mathematics preparation

### Target Learning Goals
- geometry content knowledge and its integration with pedagogical content knowledge
