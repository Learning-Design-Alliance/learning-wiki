# Six policy areas state leaders can implement to create equitable SEAD-supportive learning environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief identifies six key policy issues for state and school leaders to create equitable and safe learning environments that promote positive SEAD: inclusive discipline and dress code policies, meaningful professional development for all adults, equitable access to rigorous and culturally sustaining curricula, engaging students and families as full partners, access to integrated wraparound services, and diversifying the educator workforce. The brief states these policies are 'integral to ensuring positive outcomes in school and beyond.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State and district leaders coordinate across all six policy areas rather than treating SEAD as a single program
#### Constraints
- Many of these policy areas are led at the local level, with state leaders playing a supporting role

### Target Learners
- P-12 students, particularly students of color and students from low-income backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- Social, emotional, and academic development; equitable access to rigorous learning
