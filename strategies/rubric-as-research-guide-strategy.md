# Provide students the research skill rubric as a guide before they begin their research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students felt perplexed at the start of the course about what to do, the authors recommend providing a rubric with explicit descriptions of research skills as a research guide. They state that "Providing students with a rubric with explicit descriptions of research skills as a research guide may help them become more comfortable with the notion of research, including conducting and directing it," noting further research is necessary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The rubric must include explicit descriptions of the research skills students might acquire
#### Constraints
- The article states further research to address this issue is necessary; the recommendation is not itself tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students new to research

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the research process
- identifying research skills to acquire
