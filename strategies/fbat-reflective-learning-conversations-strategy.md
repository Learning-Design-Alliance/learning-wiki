# Use the tool predominantly for reflective learning conversations within an intentional programme of peer coaching for simulation faculty development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a specific application mode for the assessment tool in faculty development: "We recommend that the Facilitation Behavioural Assessment Tool be used predominantly for reflective learning conversations on facilitation performance in a structured, theoretically informed and psychologically safe manner". They add that such conversations should ideally be integrated into an intentional programme of peer coaching to enhance debriefing skills, rather than being used solely for summative scoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured, theoretically informed and psychologically safe conversation format; integration into peer coaching
#### Constraints
- Recommended predominantly for reflective learning conversations rather than other uses; further work required to explore extrapolation and implications of its use

### Target Learners
- Simulation facilitators, particularly in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflection on facilitation performance and enhancement of debriefing and facilitation skills

## Claims

- [The Facilitation Behavioural Assessment Tool achieves suitable inter-rater reliability after short online rater training, with ICC2 of 0.73 and ICC2k of 0.89](../claims/fbat-inter-rater-reliability-icc.md) [+W]
- [Internal consistency of the tool's three category scores is satisfactory, with Cronbach's α of 0.84 for positive and mixed videos and 0.91 for the negative video](../claims/fbat-internal-consistency-cronbach.md) [+W]
- [Facilitator experience moderates scoring: novices rated mixed and negative videos higher than intermediate and experienced raters, qualified by a two-way interaction (F(4200) = 2.43, p = 0.049)](../claims/fbat-experience-interaction-scoring.md) [~W]
- [Raters using the tool discriminate between facilitation videos depicting positive, mixed and negative influence on learning, with all pairwise mean differences significant at p < 0.001](../claims/fbat-discriminates-video-levels.md) [+W]
