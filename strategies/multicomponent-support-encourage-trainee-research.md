# Multi-component institutional support to encourage specialty trainees' research engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its discussion of how to raise research assistants' scientific activity, the article recommends a bundle of supports: "providing mentor support from the beginning of medical school, holding meetings to discuss the importance of research, offering financial support for participation in research activities, and rewarding achievements". It also notes the example of India, where delivering oral/poster presentations and publishing articles has been made mandatory alongside conference attendance. The strategy targets the low publication rates the survey documented.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to mentoring, meetings, funding, and recognition mechanisms for research participation
#### Constraints
- The article presents these approaches as drawn from other countries' practices, not as interventions tested in this study

### Target Learners
- medical faculty research assistants and specialty trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement in scientific research, publication, and conference presentation
