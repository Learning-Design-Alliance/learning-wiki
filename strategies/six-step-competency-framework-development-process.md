# Follow a systematic, theory-informed six-step process when developing competency frameworks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Citing Batt et al., the review recommends that competency framework development "should follow a systematic, theory-informed process". The proposed six-step method includes defining the purposes and user audience (e.g., single or multidisciplinary), considering real-world contexts, exploring current practices, identifying relevant competencies, reporting the development process and establishing a plan for future framework updates. The review also notes frameworks must navigate disparate scientific perspectives, such as the emerging diagnostic criteria debate, to advance rather than impede educational progress.
