# Contextualise the Cavendish experiment with the evolution of unit systems and absolute measurements to reinstate accurate history

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that textbooks correct the false history by exploring why Cavendish could not determine G accurately, discussing the development of unit systems and homogeneous dimensional equations. As stated: "By connecting the Cavendish experiment with the evolution of unit systems and absolute measurements, the proper history can be reinstated in physics textbooks." This serves a dual purpose of rectifying the inaccuracy while conveying the importance of standardised units.
