# Use quizzes or assignments due at the beginning of class to ensure students review materials and come prepared.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors found students were initially slow to adapt to flipped course norms, so instructors used beginning-of-class quizzes or assignments to ensure preparation. This strategy addresses the adaptation challenge they identified in their study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular low-stakes accountability checks at the start of class sessions
#### Constraints
- Addresses initial student adaptation to flipped format norms; the authors note students "were initially slow in adapting to the different norms and expectations of the flipped course format"

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in flipped courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Pre-class preparation and completion of out-of-class materials
