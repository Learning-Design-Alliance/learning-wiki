# Pair deaf mentees with deaf mentors or mentors familiar with the Deaf community, and provide cultural competency training to mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because Deaf Community Capital, Asking for Accommodations, and Communication Access scores were highest when mentors were deaf or familiar with the Deaf community, the article concludes that "cultural competency training should improve these aspects of mentoring for deaf mentees." The strategy is to route deaf mentees toward Deaf or Deaf-know mentors and to build mentors' familiarity with Deaf culture and communication access practices. The authors note the framework and survey can supply programs with feedback on mentoring relationships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must be able to identify or recruit Deaf/Deaf-know mentors, or train hearing mentors in Deaf cultural awareness and communication access
#### Constraints
- Being a Scientist scores did not vary significantly with mentor hearing status or familiarity, so the benefit is specific to the three community-cultural-wealth factors; designated slots for deaf students are far fewer at graduate and postdoctoral levels than in undergraduate REU programs

### Target Learners
- Deaf STEM mentees, particularly undergraduates in mentored research programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving communication access, accommodation-seeking skills, and Deaf community support within mentored research experiences
