# Use the P2P-teacher competency-profile as a starting point for developing P2P-teacher training curricula and cross-institutional research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes that skills labs, clinics and institutes engaged in P2P-teaching use the draft competency-profile when developing, implementing and standardizing curricula for P2P-teachers: they "can focus competencies which represent the roles of P2P-teachers in their respective medical education programs and start with training measures", or leave competencies aside if judged unimportant, and may add missing ones. Researchers are invited to align P2P-research with the profile, set up theoretical foundations, and develop and validate tests and survey items for sharing across institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-institutional discussion, negotiation and empirical investigation, since the theoretical elaboration cannot be accomplished by any one medical university
#### Constraints
- The competency-profile in itself does not represent a curriculum; it is far from complete and represents a snapshot of the peer-reviewed discourse only

### Target Learners
- student P2P-teachers in human medicine

### Target Learning Goals
- P2P-teaching competencies across medical subjects

### Affordances
- P2P Teacher Competency Profile Draft
