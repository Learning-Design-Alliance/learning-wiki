# Use vignette results to identify poorly performing cadres and target training to them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes using vignette-based competence assessment to identify which health worker cadres perform poorly and to direct training accordingly, or to reconsider task-shifting. The conclusion states vignettes provide "an opportunity ... to identify poorly performing health worker cadres and target training to them". In this study, cadres other than midwives and doctors scored lowest, suggesting they may not be able to diagnose and manage delivery complications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vignette scores disaggregated by cadre
- Training capacity to respond to identified gaps
#### Constraints
- Results are specific to the two vignette cases and the Brong Ahafo study area

### Target Learners
- Health worker cadres other than midwives and doctors (e.g. medical assistants, nurses, health assistants, health extension workers)

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnosis and first-line management of obstetric emergencies
