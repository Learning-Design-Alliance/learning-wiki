# Crowdsourced study pipeline with quality control for investigating pedagogies at scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An end-to-end crowdsourcing pipeline for investigating pedagogies on a massive scale: collect course materials, recruit paid workers from Amazon Mechanical Turk, design workflows in which workers understand learning objects and label relations among them, and design learning tasks meaningful to workers whose performance is measured. The thesis adopts "a wide range of quality control approaches to improve reliability of online workers, including majority consensus, expert review, as well as clear task description, workflow, and interface", and motivates participation with the opportunity to learn from MOOC materials in addition to monetary incentives.
