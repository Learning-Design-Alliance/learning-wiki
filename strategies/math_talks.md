# Math Talks

## Description
Math talks are structured conversations focused on mathematical reasoning and problem-solving strategies. These discussions promote a deeper understanding of concepts, encourage learners to share their thinking, and provide opportunities for instructors to assess comprehension. Math talks support the development of communication skills and mathematical precision by being asked to make sure everyone understands a viewpoint. It comes from the use of specific mathematical terms, from labeling answers, and from using representations as tools to support communication about mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured mathematical problems or scenarios. A facilitator to guide the conversation. A classroom culture that supports open discussion and sharing of ideas.
#### Constraints
- May require careful planning and preparation by the instructor to ensure discussions are productive. Requires a classroom culture that values discussion and the exchange of ideas.
