# Identifying Obstacles

## Description
Identifying obstacles involves recognizing potential challenges that could impede progress toward a goal. This includes assessing gaps in knowledge, skills, resources, or other constraints. Recognizing these obstacles allows for proactive planning and problem-solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires self-awareness, critical thinking, and the ability to anticipate potential roadblocks.
#### Constraints
- Overemphasis on potential obstacles can lead to paralysis by analysis. Some challenges may be unforeseen and require adaptive responses.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Learners can identify obstacles specific to their circumstances and goals. Tailoring strategies to address these specific challenges enhances the likelihood of success.
