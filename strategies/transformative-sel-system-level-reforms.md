# Adopt transformative SEL and system-level policy reforms—funding restructure, disciplinary equity, healthcare access—to address poverty-driven inequity

## Description
The article endorses "transformative SEL" that would foster critical citizenship, promote positive cultural identity and agency, and equip students to challenge unjust norms, contrasting it with personally responsible forms that risk assimilation pressures on minority children. At the structural level it argues "a restructuring of funding policies is necessary", that property-tax funding produces "inherently unequal schools", and that policies must reduce poverty and structural racism, disciplinary disparities, lunch-cost burdens, and healthcare access barriers.
