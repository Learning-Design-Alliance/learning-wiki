# Use ASD-friendly visual and interactive methods when delivering oral health education to autistic children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes adapting teaching to the communication needs of children with ASD: education sessions used "a structured PowerPoint presentation, storytelling, and interactive games", individualized instruction with tooth models supported hands-on practice, and children took home structured brushing sequence charts as visual prompts for daily routines. When verbal commands such as "open your mouth" were not understood, students demonstrated actions visually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured visual aids such as sequencing charts and step-by-step guides, and awareness of visual processing strengths in many individuals with ASD.
#### Constraints
- Developed and reported for children with ASD aged 8–14 in a specialized school setting.

### Target Learners
- Children with autism spectrum disorder aged 8–14 years

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral hygiene knowledge and toothbrushing skills
