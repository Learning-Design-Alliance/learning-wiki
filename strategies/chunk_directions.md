# Chunk Directions

## Description
Giving learners more manageable chunks of information can help ensure they’re able to continue to engage in a meaningful way when dealing with sustained attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of formats to present directions and instructions. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources and prep. Some learners may not have a problem with long strings of directions and instructions.

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners who experience anxiety, sensory overload, or difficulty transitioning between activities

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve sustained attention

### Affordances
- Breaks down tasks in more manageable steps.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred direction format. Provide a variety of presentation options. Allow learners to ask questions along the way.
