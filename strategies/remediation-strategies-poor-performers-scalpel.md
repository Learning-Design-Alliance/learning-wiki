# Manage poor performers by increasing practice and exposure, providing positive rather than negative feedback, and moving students back a step on the teaching continuum to reduce complexity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports a range of remedial strategies nominated by educators for students struggling with manual clinical skills. These include increased teaching or practical exposure, increased positive feedback while avoiding negative feedback, and moving students back a step on the teaching continuum to decrease complexity, such as returning to simple tasks with soap or wax impregnated materials before progressing forward again. Each university program reported employing one or a combination of these strategies.
