# Provide pre-essay in-class activities, practice quizzes and worked examples before essay deadlines to prepare students for conceptual essay questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students found the conceptual portions of the essays hardest, the author plans to give relevant activities before deadlines: "in the future we will provide relevant activities prior to essay deadlines to help students create successful essays." Proposed supports include in-class activities covering Essay 1 and Essay 3 concepts, worked examples in the essay handout, and online quizzes closing at least a week before each deadline so students can practice and build confidence before submitting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Class time must be available for pre-essay activities and draft review, which the author notes competes with other course material that needs to be covered during the semester.
#### Constraints
- The author notes it may not be possible to devote class time to all concepts included in Essays 1 and 3, and the essays would be adjusted accordingly, e.g. removing the stellar-dimming question.

### Target Learners
- Community-college non-science majors in an introductory astronomy course

### Target Learning Goals
- Explaining transits, occultations and eclipses
- Predicting how orbital and object properties affect transit occurrence, duration and stellar dimming
- Writing about astronomy concepts
