# Build credentials that respond to four trends: competency- and market-oriented programs, lifelong learning structures, unbundled degrees, and quality assurance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper sets out an agenda for community colleges seeking a central role in the new credentialing market: they "must build credentials that respond to four growing trends and imperatives". These are building competency and market-oriented programs, structuring credentials to facilitate lifelong learning, unbundling learning in traditional degrees, and recognizing the need for quality assurance. The paper presents this as a design and policy agenda rather than a tested intervention.
