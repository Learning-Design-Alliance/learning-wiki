# Establish referral protocols and interdisciplinary collaboration structures between dermatology and oncology for CIA and pCIA care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address the low referral rates and communication gap the survey documented, the authors propose structural collaboration mechanisms: "establishing protocols for timely dermatology referrals, especially for patients at high-risk of pCIA or those with delayed hair regrowth"; integrating dermatologists into oncology teams; interdisciplinary meetings and joint educational sessions; shared electronic medical records to track patients at risk of pCIA; and cross-training during residency to promote mutual understanding between the specialties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Referral pathways must be standardized and timely, prioritizing high-risk pCIA patients and patients with delayed hair regrowth
- Shared electronic medical records are proposed as the coordination mechanism for tracking at-risk patients
#### Constraints
- Integrating dermatologists into oncology teams is proposed only "where feasible"
- The motivating referral findings come from a self-reported, exploratory survey at tertiary centers

### Target Learners
- dermatologists
- oncologists
- physicians in residency cross-training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary collaboration on CIA and pCIA management
- appropriate and timely dermatological referral of patients with incomplete hair regrowth
