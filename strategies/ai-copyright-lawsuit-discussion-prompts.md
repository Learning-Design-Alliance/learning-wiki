# Use current AI copyright lawsuits and generative-AI research tasks as case discussion prompts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The case provides thirteen discussion questions covering the problems experienced by artists, programmers, and content publishers; the nature and purpose of copyright versus patent and trademark; the logic offered by OpenAI and Microsoft; image metadata and data mining properties; and risks of training AI by scraping the internet. Several questions direct students to use a generative AI tool such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Google Gemini to research current court cases, methods for avoiding infringement, and the status of Kelly McKernan's lawsuit. The section "can be used for case and concept discussion".
