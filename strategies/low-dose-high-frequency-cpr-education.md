# Low dose – high frequency continuing education for CPR skills in Primary Health Care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends "ações de educação permanente teórico-prática com maior frequência e menor intensidade de novos conhecimentos", naming this the "low dose - high frequency model". It rests on the rationale that the less frequent the contact, the lower the retention of knowledge and skills, and that sporadic training may not be enough for effective arrest care. Participants also called for periodic training extended to all unit staff, from access controllers to cleaning and administrative personnel.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must occur regularly rather than widely spaced, given high staff turnover in Primary Health Care.
#### Constraints
- Daily training is often impossible because of high work demand, as one participant noted.

### Target Learners
- nurses and nursing technicians in Family Health Strategy teams

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of CPR knowledge and skills
- effective response to non-routine emergencies
