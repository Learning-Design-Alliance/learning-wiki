# Before a study, researchers should present the proposal to district administration, hold conversations with administrators and teachers, and host parent events to build buy-in

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has researchers invest in relationships before data collection: present the proposal to district administration, hold many conversations with school administrators and teachers, and host parent events to share goals, field questions, and gather feedback. Uncapher "presented her research proposal to district administration, and then held many conversations with school administrators and teachers" and hosted back-to-school night programs. Framing the study as producing data relevant to schools' work with students helped principals see value in participating.
