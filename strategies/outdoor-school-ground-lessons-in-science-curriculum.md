# Incorporate outdoor school ground lessons into primary science teaching and curriculum as an additional instructional method

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that science teachers use interesting and meaningful outdoor activities as an additional teaching method, and that curriculum developers consider putting outdoor school lessons into the science curriculum, based on the measured improvements in science process skills and scientific curiosity. Teacher training institutes may also use the study as a guideline for further research in outdoor education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School grounds with animals and plants accessible for structured lessons
- Teacher facilitation of observation, questioning and group discussion during outdoor lessons
#### Constraints
- Generalising to other elementary school students should be done in caution because convenient sampling was used; the article recommends randomization in future studies

### Target Learners
- Primary school science students

### Target Learning Goals
- Science process skills
- Scientific curiosity toward science
