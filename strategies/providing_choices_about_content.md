# Providing Choices About Content

## Description
Mature learners decide what to learn when they discover there’s something they need to know or something about which they need to know more. Choosing what to learn involves skill. The content must be identified, questions about it framed, and content boundaries set.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires learners to have sufficient background knowledge and self-regulation skills. Requires teachers to provide a range of content options and resources.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who lack experience in making decisions about their learning. Requires careful planning to ensure learners have access to high-quality content and resources.

### Target Learners

- Applicable to higher education and adult learners, particularly in self-directed learning environments.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote independent learning, develop decision-making skills, and increase motivation by allowing learners to pursue areas of interest.

### Affordances
- Develops independent learning skills and self-directedness. Increases motivation by allowing learners to pursue areas of interest. Fosters a sense of ownership over the learning process.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying the level of structure and guidance provided. Scaffolds can be used to support learners in identifying content, framing questions, and setting boundaries.
