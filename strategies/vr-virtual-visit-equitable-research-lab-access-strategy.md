# Offer VR-based virtual research-laboratory visits as an equitable, low-cost substitute for on-site visits in school curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose introducing virtual visits to research laboratories on a permanent basis in physics school curricula, "regardless of their availability of financial resources and their geographical location", since on-site visits are expensive in time and money. They believe the proposal is "worth being exported to other research centres or facilities, even in fields other than particle physics", and recommend adding more parallel VR stations to involve more students at once.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR application of the target facility, VR helmets, and researchers able to guide the visit and answer questions
#### Constraints
- Travel-expense savings depend on the remote delivery; the limited number of simultaneous VR participants must be addressed, e.g. by adding parallel stations

### Target Learners
- High school students of all backgrounds, in any location

### Target Learning Goals
- Raising awareness of research frontiers, giving a realistic view of scientists' work, and bringing students closer to STEM careers
