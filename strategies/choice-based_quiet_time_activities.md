# Choice-Based Quiet Time Activities

## Description
Choice-based activities during quiet time give students the power to select their preferred independent tasks, enhancing engagement and promoting a sense of control. Options can range from academic tasks like reading, writing, or unfinished classwork to relaxing activities such as doodling, playing solitary games, or meditating. The teacher’s role is to provide the options, model the routines, and ensure a quiet environment, while refraining from direct interaction with students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of quiet activities, clear explanation of rules and expectations, consistent implementation.
#### Constraints
- Requires planning and preparation, may be challenging to manage student choices, requires consistent reinforcement.
