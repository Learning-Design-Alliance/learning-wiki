# Instructors at all chemistry levels should use and share embedded lessons to integrate quantum calculations throughout the curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors close by urging that 'instructors from all levels of chemistry use the imbedded lessons as well as create their own to share with the chemistry and Maple communities in order to help integrate quantum calculations throughout the chemistry curriculum.' The strategy is community-driven lesson reuse and authoring: adopt existing importable Workbook lessons, adapt them, and contribute new ones. Each activity can be imported as a worksheet and modified as necessary by students or instructor.
