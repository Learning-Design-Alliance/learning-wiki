# Audiobooks for Literacy Reinforcement

## Description
Audiobooks can reinforce and inspire literacy skills and enjoyment for all students, including emerging readers and ESL/ELL learners. They offer a way to experience literature and other reading selections in audio form. Some selections are audio only, while others include text to follow or interactive features. They can improve English skills or study literature in new ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to audiobooks (either physical or digital), devices for listening (e.g., computers, tablets, smartphones), and a quiet environment for focused listening.
#### Constraints
- Audiobooks may not fully replace the benefits of traditional reading. Learners need to be actively engaged and not just passively listening to benefit from the strategy. Digital access may be a barrier for some learners.

### Personalization
- Offers multiple categories of books and different download or listening formats. Some selections are audio only, others include text to follow on the site as you listen, while others include options to interact with the book by clicking, responding, and watching animations.

- Observe student engagement and participation in discussions about the audiobooks. Assess comprehension through quizzes, summaries, or creative projects.
