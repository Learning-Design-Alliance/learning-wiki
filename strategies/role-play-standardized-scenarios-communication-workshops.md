# Use role-play of standardized clinical scenarios as the shared active-learning core of communication-skills workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Both interventions in the trial were built around role-play, which the authors describe as allowing participants "to simulate real-life clinical scenarios in a safe and structured environment". Role-playing enabled interns to practice communication skills such as delivering complex medical information and navigating difficult patient conversations, combined with structured feedback from peers, facilitators, or video review. The authors advocate broader integration of role-play within medical curricula to complement other active learning strategies.
