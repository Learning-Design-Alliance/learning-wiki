# Tutor facilitation through four dynamic roles during group inquiry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During group inquiry the tutor shifts from lecturer to guide and catalyst, patrolling among groups in four named roles. The article states: "Tutors patrol among groups and play key roles.Observer: Close monitoring of the discussion dynamics, participation, obstacles encountered, and thinking trends of each group." The roles are observer, questioner (using Socratic questioning), resource guide, and process manager, plus acting as a catalyst for explicit thinking by having trainees voice their reasoning.
