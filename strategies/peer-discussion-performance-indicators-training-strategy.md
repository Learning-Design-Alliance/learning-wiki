# Devote a large proportion of managerial training to peer discussion of multidimensional performance indicators to build capacity for using performance information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article links regional differences in the mediational path through performance information use to how much regional training curricula engage participants in discussing multidimensional performance indicators. Regions whose training devoted "a very large proportion of their training to peer discussion of multidimensional performance indicators" showed the significant UPI mediational circuit. The strategy is to embed discussion of indicators from national and regional performance evaluation systems in training, including benchmarking exercises recreating "reality-close decision-making dilemmas".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to multidimensional performance indicators from national and regional performance evaluation systems for use as learning material
#### Constraints
- The evidence for this strategy comes from post-hoc comparison of only two regional systems' curricula, and the authors call for further investigation of institutional isomorphism effects

### Target Learners
- Clinical directors and other hybrid professionals in healthcare management roles

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of performance information and its use in decision-making
- Involvement in top management decision-making
