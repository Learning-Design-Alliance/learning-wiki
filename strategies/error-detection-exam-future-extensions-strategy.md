# Extend error-detection examinations with photograph-based scenarios, more feedback time, reflection, and team-based learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendations for improving the educational effect of the error-detection examination. They state "we plan to use photograph‐based scenarios for the cohort group of students", hoping images will more accurately simulate clinical situations and help prepare for the Japan National Examination. They also plan to allocate more time for feedback, are considering reflection as another teaching strategy, and are considering applying the test to team-based learning with small groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Photograph-based scenarios require images of dental treatment; additional feedback time requires schedule allowance beyond the current 1-hr slot
#### Constraints
- The article presents these as plans and considerations, not as tested modifications; statistical comparison with the current format is planned but not yet conducted

### Target Learners
- fifth-year dental students in clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- simulating clinical situations, preparing for the Japan National Examination, and deepening understanding of conservative dentistry procedures
