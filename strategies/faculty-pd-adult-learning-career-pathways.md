# Provide faculty professional development in adult learning theory and pedagogy for career pathways instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because many CTE programs hire industry experts who lack teaching experience, the panel recommends pedagogical training covering practical tips for generating syllabi, delivering instruction, and creating assessments, plus peer mentors and communities of practice. The panel believes "all faculty can benefit from pedagogical training that specifically addresses adult learning theory and pedagogies for effectively teaching adult learners, thereby enabling them to meet students' diverse learning needs." Faculty without advanced degrees can pursue workforce educator degree programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional funding and training to support faculty development, especially for faculty transitioning out of industry into teaching
#### Constraints
- Most studies supporting the guide targeted and served older students

### Target Learners
- Adult community college students in career pathways

### Target Learning Goals
- High-quality occupational and basic skills instruction
