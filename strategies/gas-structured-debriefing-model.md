# Use the GAS (Gather, Analyze, Summarize) structured debriefing model after simulated emergency scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GAS is a three-phase structured debriefing method applied after each simulated scenario in this study. The article states the instructors "used the same structured model for the debriefing – called GAS (Gather, Analyze, and Summarize) – which is composed of three phases": Gather brings together the events experienced, Analyze promotes participant reflection, and Summarize reviews key points. It is a validated method designed to standardize debriefing for ACLS and PALS courses and is recommended by INACSL Standards of Best Practice for Simulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with pediatric board certification and previous training in simulation
- A simulated clinical scenario followed immediately by the three-phase debriefing
#### Constraints
- In this study it was applied to a high-complexity, low-frequency pediatric cardiopulmonary arrest scenario with fixed team roles across scenarios

### Target Learners
- Pediatric medicine and nursing residents
- Interprofessional healthcare trainee teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition and retention of non-technical skills (leadership, teamwork, task management) through post-scenario reflection
