# Develop and adapt intelligent-agent tutoring tools for Latin-American countries, where implementation is incipient

## Description
The article argues that tutoring tools are well implemented in the USA and Europe while "Countries in Latin-America are in an incipient development, this is an opportunity to researches in education and the implementation of Information Technologies." It recommends interdisciplinary collaboration among psychologists, pedagogues, institutions, computer scientists, teachers, and students, and calls for continued AI research to create an inclusive education system, adapting agents to Latin-American particularities.

## Design Implications

#### Constraints
