# Transplant ITS insights such as hint timing and solution review into regulative CSCL design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because regulative CSCL systems and ITS both implement regulative loops, insights recorded in Behavior of Tutoring Systems can be applied to CSCL. The article notes that "the whole discussion of when to give hints along with the related concepts of gaming the system, help abuse and help refusal, can be easily applied to CSCL systems that give advice to students on how to collaborate." Options for reviewing solutions could inform CSCL assessment or mirroring of collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A CSCL system taking a regulative approach that gives advice to students on collaboration
#### Constraints
- Applies to regulative CSCL systems, not structural ones such as scripts without monitoring of script-following

### Target Learners
- students collaborating with computer support

### Target Learning Goals
- effective collaboration skills supported by well-timed advice and review
