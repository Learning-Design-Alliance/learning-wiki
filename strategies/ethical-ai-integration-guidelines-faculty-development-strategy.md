# Establish transparent ethical guidelines and faculty development for AI use in postgraduate coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educators adopting AI-supported feedback establish clear rules governing student AI use and prepare faculty to steward it. It states that "educators must establish clear guidelines regarding appropriate use, academic integrity, data privacy, and the boundaries between human and machine-generated work." Transparency toward students about when and how AI is used, its limitations, and the necessity of human judgment is described as essential, alongside faculty development to detect overreliance and model reflective digital literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development so instructors can guide responsible use and detect overreliance or misuse
- Ethical governance and integration into existing pedagogical frameworks
#### Constraints
- The authors warn AI tools should enhance rather than replace critical thinking

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate students in research methods and similar coursework

### Target Learning Goals
- Responsible AI use
- Critical appraisal and research critique
