# Road shows along the project timeline with URM students as guides and speakers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among the emerging opportunities in Table 1, the article proposes developing road shows that provide information along the project timeline about the ngVLA, its progress, scientific outcomes, careers, and its incorporation into the landscape. The design deliberately uses "URM students as guides and speakers to provide opportunities for the students to gain experience speaking about their work, raise their visibility, and allow them to serve as role models for members of the audiences." Road show material can also be distributed through social media and traditional EPO routes, with lesson plans for local and state schools aligned to state standards.
