# Use conditional growth percentiles to judge whether a student grew like their academic peers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy replaces binary met/not-met growth targets with a normative comparison of a student's growth to similar students nationwide. The report states: "With accompanying conditional growth percentiles, norms t ell educators if students made growth con- sistent with their academic peers." Educators can thereby "understand the extent and magnitude by which a student's growth exceede d or lagged the growth observed for other similar students," supporting goal setting and instructional individualization.
