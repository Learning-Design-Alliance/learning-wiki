# Match presence types to task objectives when designing VR learning environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that designers tailor VR environments by selecting the presence types a task demands. It states that "By understanding these associations, designers and researchers can tailor virtual reality environments to meet the nuanced needs of various educational, training, and collaborative tasks." Spatial presence serves navigation and simulation, cognitive presence serves knowledge construction and problem-solving, and social/co-presence serve collaboration and communication. This targeted approach is presented as improving task performance efficiency, learning outcomes, and overall satisfaction.
