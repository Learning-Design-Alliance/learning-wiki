# Combine e-learning with face-to-face sessions or increase e-learning frequency and duration to achieve lasting attitude change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the evaluated e-learning course produced lasting knowledge gains but no lasting attitude change, the authors propose two developments for future research: "e-learning might be used in combination with face-to-face sessions, or more intensive (in terms of frequency and duration) e-learning sessions may be needed to achieve lasting changes in attitude". They are extending ELPAS with short e-learning modules embedded longitudinally in the curriculum and enhanced with simulated patient encounters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attitude change requires stimuli the authors describe as strong; permanent changes in attitudes require strong stimuli
#### Constraints
- The mainly theoretical education in year 3 of the curriculum may lack the situational context of clinical experience needed for attitude change

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- patient safety attitudes
- error disclosure confidence
- situation monitoring
- patient empowerment
