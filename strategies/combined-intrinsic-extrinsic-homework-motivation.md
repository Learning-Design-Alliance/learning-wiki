# Combine modest homework credit, in-lesson value discussions, and a promise that at least half of quiz questions come from homework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article sketches a blended motivational scheme for teachers who use both intrinsic and extrinsic motivations: "might award 5% of the course points for completing homework while devoting several minutes at the end of each lesson to a discussion about how the homework problems will build on what students learned in the lesson and, finally, promising students that at least half of the quiz questions will be taken from the homework." It combines the three incentives the study quantified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quizzes must include a substantial share of questions taken from homework, and lesson time must include homework discussion.
#### Constraints
- The article presents this combination as an illustrative design, not as a tested package; each component was studied separately.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- completing assigned homework problems
