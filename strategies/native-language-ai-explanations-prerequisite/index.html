Deliver AI tutor explanations in learners' native language as a prerequisite for meaningful educational access¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because foreign-language delivery of technical content significantly reduces content and language learning outcomes, the article recommends AI tutoring systems explain programming errors in the learner's native language. It states that for a rural Bengali-speaking student, "native-language instruction is a prerequisite for meaningful educational access rather than an enhancement layered on top of it." The language of the tutor's output is therefore a core design decision, not a localization convenience.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Native-language explanations require the underlying model and training data infrastructure to support the language adequately

Constraints¶

  • General-purpose models deliver explanations in English given current training data distributions, so the strategy depends on the structural remediation the article describes

Target Learners¶

  • Bengali-speaking learners with limited English proficiency
  • Non-English-dominant programming students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Programming concepts, error diagnosis, and debugging comprehension
