# Use goal-setting, reflection, gamification, and video review to enhance feedback in simulation curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review's future-directions section recommends concrete ways to enhance feedback delivery in simulation-based training. Trainees should "document and share their objectives with the expert endoscopist" before each session, reflect on what they will change next session, and curricula can incorporate feedback through gamification with incremental difficulty and goalposts. Video-recording procedures also supports improved trainee self-assessment over time.
