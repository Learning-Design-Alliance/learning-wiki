Establish an ICT Resource Centre under each district's School Management Board with production, research-assessment and technical sections¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

To put ICT tools infrastructure in place, the authors recommend that School Management Boards and government agencies procure funds and establish a unit called an ICT RESOURCE CENTRE. Its communication unit should consist of three sections: an ICT tools production section that constructs instructional packages and edits DVD/CD clips for children, a research assessment and liaison section that studies effects and gathers responses from teachers, pupils, parents and the production team, and a technical section that repairs and services ICT apparatus in viewing centres.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School Management Boards and government agencies procure necessary funds; selected teachers and pupils are trained to operate ICT equipment and handle minor repairs and routine maintenance

Constraints¶

  • Recommended specifically for schools in Chhattisgarh and stated as adoptable with modification

Target Learners¶

  • Primary and secondary school pupils in Chhattisgarh districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective delivery of subject teaching through ICT-based instructional media

Claims¶

  • Pupils taught with DLP achieved slightly higher average gain scores than pupils taught with Computer/laptop, and both ICT groups outperformed Chart and Chalk Talk groups [+M]
  • The study concludes that ICT tools/models help teachers teach effectively compared with traditional/conventional teaching methods [+M]
  • Teachers' lack of ICT knowledge and skills is reported as a major obstacle to ICT implementation, pointing to the need for further teacher training [+W]
