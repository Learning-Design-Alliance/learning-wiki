# Use the MEES questionnaire to evaluate postgraduate medical e-learning at Kirkpatrick levels 1 and 2

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend the MEES as "a valuable and practical tool for evaluating e-learning in medical education", assessing Kirkpatrick level 1 (reaction) and part of level 2 (learning) through motivators, barriers, learning enhancers, learning discouragers, and real-world translators domains on a 10-point Likert scale. They encourage its use for further evaluation of and comparison among instructional designs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each construct measured with 1 item, so internal consistency statistics such as Cronbach α are not applicable
#### Constraints
- Assesses only Kirkpatrick levels 1 and part of 2; the next step would be to measure the impact on clinical practice

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating reaction and perceived learning value of e-learning
