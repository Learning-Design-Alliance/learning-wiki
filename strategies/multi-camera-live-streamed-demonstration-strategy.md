# Use multi-camera close-ups and point-of-view shots during live-streamed clinical demonstrations to enhance visibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends using remotely controlled cameras with adjustable settings so teachers can select appropriate perspectives based on demonstration needs. Wide and medium shots showcased the comprehensive patient reception process, while "close-ups and point-of-view shots allowed students to observe the intricacies of the procedure". This addresses the limited intra-oral visibility of conventional face-to-face demonstrations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Remotely controlled cameras with adjustable settings
- An interactive recording and broadcasting system
- Teachers monitoring live streaming effects through multiple monitors
#### Constraints
- Requires robust network infrastructure to ensure seamless connectivity

### Target Learners
- preclinical dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- observing clinical procedural details
- understanding ultrasonic scaling technique
