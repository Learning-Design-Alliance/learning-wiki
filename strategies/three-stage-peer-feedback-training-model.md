# Integrated three-stage training model (preparation, modelling, reflection) for effective self- and peer feedback practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the focus group findings, the authors recommend "an integrated three stage training model" for effective self- and peer feedback practice in undergraduate medical education. Table 3 details a preparation stage (educating students on feedback's significance, agency and emotional demands), a modelling stage (standardized feedback forms, rubrics, checklists and technology), and a reflection stage (peer-review reflection sessions and small peer feedback groups).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
