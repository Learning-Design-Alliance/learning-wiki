# Estimation and Prediction for Self-Awareness

## Description
Learners use estimation and prediction skills to reflect on their abilities. Activities include estimating project outcomes or task completion times, recording estimates, and reflecting on their accuracy to enhance self-awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires tasks or projects with clear outcomes and timelines. Facilitator guidance is needed to help learners reflect on their estimates.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be comfortable with self-assessment. May be challenging for learners with cognitive or learning difficulties.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Middle and high school students, adult learners, learners with self-assessment skill deficits

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance self-awareness, improve self-assessment, develop realistic expectations

### Affordances
- Enhances self-assessment and self-awareness. Provides a structured method for reflecting on one's abilities and performance. Develops realistic expectations.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by allowing learners to choose tasks or projects of interest. Flexible formats can include written estimates, discussions, or visual aids.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track the accuracy of learner estimates over time. Monitor the depth and consistency of learner reflections. Assess the clarity and realism of learner expectations.

## Impact
- Improved self-awareness: Research indicates that self-assessment activities enhance understanding of one's abilities and limitations. Enhanced self-regulation: Accurate self-assessment supports better goal setting and planning.
