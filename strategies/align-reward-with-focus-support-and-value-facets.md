# Align reward with focus support and with SEVT value facets when designing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The FoRe-Squares model offers two guiding orientations for implementing reward: aligning reward with focus support, and aligning reward techniques with the value facets of Situated Expectancy-Value Theory—intrinsic value, attainment value, utility value, and costs. Techniques such as emotional design or relevant humor may increase intrinsic value; meaningful competence feedback may enhance attainment value; credits or exam relevance may increase utility value; and choice may reduce expected costs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must assess what learners experience as rewarding, since this varies by person, timing, task, and context
#### Constraints
- The model prescribes providing rewarding experiences through instructional design rather than exactly how reward should be implemented in any specific case

### Target Learners
- learners in technology-augmented instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- increased task value and motivation to process instruction
