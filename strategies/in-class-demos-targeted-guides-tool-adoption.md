# Use in-class demonstrations and targeted guides to support adoption of new tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors report that W&B adoption initially met with student reluctance but became central to the course workflow once supported by demonstrations and guides. They recommend "supportingtooladoptionthroughin-classdemonstrationsandtargetedguidestoincreasestudentacceptanceandreduce resistance to new technologies". This strategy addresses the onboarding challenges reflected in the second-year survey's mixed tool-usefulness ratings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time to prepare demonstrations and guides
- Alignment of tool features with team workflows
#### Constraints
- Varying levels of tool familiarity across students can still affect perceived usefulness

### Target Learners
- Higher education STEM students using professional ML toolchains

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective use of experiment-tracking and collaboration tools
