# Exit Tickets

## Description
Exit tickets are short prompts given at the end of a lesson to encourage self-assessment, reflection, and growth mindset. Students become more aware of their performance and can persist through challenging tasks. Responses also serve as formative assessments to help teachers shape lessons and differentiate instruction. Eighth grade teacher example provided.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Brief period of class time at the end of a lesson. Digital or paper tools to record responses. Teacher must review responses to inform future instruction.
#### Constraints
- May be viewed as a compliance task rather than a meaningful opportunity for reflection if not implemented thoughtfully.

## Assessment Evidence
- Thoughtfulness and honesty of student responses, alignment between student self-assessment and actual performance, changes in mindset over time

## Impact
- Studies indicate that reflecting on learning promotes student agency and improves self-assessment skills. Formative feedback from exit tickets helps instructors make timely instructional adjustments, and enhance student self-regulation skills.
