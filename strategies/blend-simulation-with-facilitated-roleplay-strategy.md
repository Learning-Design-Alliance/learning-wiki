# Deliver sensitive-conversation training as a blend of simulation practice with facilitator-led and role-play elements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants and the authors suggest combining simulator-based training with interpersonal learning strategies: some felt "the addition of a facilitator for group discussions would add to the realism and ability to reflect," and the discussion recommends "hybrid learning models that integrate digital and interpersonal learning strategies" to support engagement across learner profiles and professional groups with varying baseline expertise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a virtual simulation tool plus facilitation or peer/patient role-play opportunities
#### Constraints
- Responds to the minority who disliked the modality; the study did not test blended delivery empirically

### Target Learners
- Health care professionals and students training for weight-related consultations

### Target Learning Goals
- Build communication skills and confidence for sensitive weight-related conversations
