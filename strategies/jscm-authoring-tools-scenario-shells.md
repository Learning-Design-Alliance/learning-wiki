# Provide teachers with authoring tools containing scenario shells for the three JSCM game patterns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As a response to the absence of any method to facilitate the design of mobile collaborative serious games, the authors report ongoing work on authoring tools that embed scenario shells corresponding to the three patterns. The goal is "que les enseignants puissent, eux-mêmes concevoir des jeux adaptés à leurs besoins" and run them on the platforms at their disposal, turning the patterns into practical design scaffolds for teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenario shells matching the three JSCM patterns
- compatibility with the execution platforms available to teachers
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as work in progress, not as an evaluated tool

### Target Learners
- teachers designing mobile collaborative serious games for their students

### Target Learning Goals
- designing coherent game scenarios combining collaboration, game mechanics and mobility
