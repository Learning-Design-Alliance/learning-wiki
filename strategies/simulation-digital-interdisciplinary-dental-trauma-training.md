# Use simulation-based training, digital tools, and interdisciplinary workshops to teach dental trauma management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends simulation-based training, digital tools including the IADT ToothSOS app, and interdisciplinary programs involving medical and dental students. The authors state that "Simulation-based training or digital tools, including the IADT ToothSOS app, may be particularly effective in bridging existing gaps" and that such interventions "should be assessed for their ability to improve both knowledge and real-world decision-making."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should be evaluated for effects on both knowledge and real-world decision-making
#### Constraints
- Knowledge retention is a challenge; periodic refresher training may be needed

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical management of dental trauma including replantation timing, storage media selection, and contaminated tooth handling
