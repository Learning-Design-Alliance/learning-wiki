# Innovate learning methods as grade level increases: gamification for male interest and cooperative learning in senior grades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that adaptive motivation declines with grade level and the female advantage narrows, the authors recommend that "there is a need to innovate learning methods for primary school students when they move to higher grades". Specifically, they suggest "teachers can design gamification learning activities to boost male student learning interest and adopt cooperative learning in senior grades to encourage female students to tackle maladaptive motivation".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to adapt instructional methods as students progress to higher grades
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is grounded in a cross-sectional survey of two suburban Beijing primary schools; the authors note little can be concluded about causal relationships

### Target Learners
- Chinese primary school students in grades 4-6

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustain adaptive motivation and engagement in mathematics learning across grade levels
