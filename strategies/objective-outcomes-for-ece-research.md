# Use objective outcome measures (blinded OSCE, standardized tests, structured scales) in future ECE research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "Future studies should prioritize using objective outcome measures, such as blinded OSCE scores, standardized written or computerized knowledge tests, and structured empathy or professionalism scales." These measures would reduce heterogeneity and enable robust meta-analyses of ECE efficacy. They also call for studies from high-income countries to ensure global generalizability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- adoption of blinded OSCE scores, standardized knowledge tests, and structured empathy or professionalism scales
#### Constraints
- current evidence base is dominated by low and middle-income country studies with heterogeneous designs and measures

### Target Learners
- medical students in pre-clinical years

### Target Learning Goals
- competency development across clinical skills, knowledge, empathy, and professionalism domains
