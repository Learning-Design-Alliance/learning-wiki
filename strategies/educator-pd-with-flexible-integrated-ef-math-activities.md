# Deliver embedded EF-mathematics activities through educator professional development plus flexible weekly activity selection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ONE programme delivered four weekly 30-minute face-to-face workshop-style professional development sessions for early years practitioners, followed by eight weeks of implementation support, together with induction into 25 co-developed activities integrating EF and early mathematics. Activities were explicitly designed to be "chosen ﬂexibly each week by teachers, rather than in a ﬁxed order, to suit each setting's context", maximising acceptability and feasibility across diverse preschool settings. All activity cards described their mathematical content and executive demands explicitly, and complexity was scaled as children became familiar with the activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must be trained in how early mathematics and EF co-develop
- Activities must use low-cost, readily available materials and be embedded in everyday practice
- A minimum of three activities per week covering breadth in mathematical content (numbers and counting, patterns and ordering, space and shapes)
#### Constraints
- The trial compared against practice-as-usual, not against an isolated EF intervention, so the specific contribution of integration per se is not yet isolated

### Target Learners
- early years practitioners working with 3- to 4-year-old children in preschool settings

### Target Learning Goals
- early mathematics (counting, cardinality, ordering, spatial skills) developed alongside executive function skills

### Affordances
- Mutualistic Ef Math Codevelopment Framework
