# Cyclic iterative development and validation process for research-based tutorials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a four-stage cyclic process for developing and validating the QuILT and its pre-/post-test: (1) developing a preliminary version based on cognitive task analysis of the underlying knowledge from expert and student perspectives; (2) implementing and evaluating versions in one-on-one interviews with feedback from expert faculty and physics-education researchers; (3) determining impact on student learning in individual interviews and assessing inadequately addressed difficulties; (4) making refinements based on each round of feedback before in-class implementation.
