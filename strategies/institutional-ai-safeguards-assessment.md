# Institutional safeguards for safe, transparent AI use in assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper assigns institutions minimum responsibilities for enabling AI-mediated assessment: completing a privacy/data-protection impact assessment (PIA/DPIA) for any AI tool used in assessment with clear data-flow maps and student-facing summaries; vetting and approving tools against privacy, bias, accessibility and auditability criteria; and standardising prompt/process transparency conventions such as prompt-log formats and version control. These conditions let students evidence how they worked without disclosing personal data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional settings must enable safe and transparent use so students and staff can be explicit about AI-mediated work without privacy or equity penalties
#### Constraints
- Free tiers and institutional AI offerings vary in availability, capability and accessibility

### Target Learners
- higher education students and staff

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible participation in digital professional environments
- data privacy awareness
