# Circulate CoRT BREADTH training to most university students to develop communication skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the author recommends holding the first part of the CoRT training program (BREADTH) and circulating it to include most university students, to develop their effective communication skills. The recommendation follows from the observed pre-to-post gains and the finding that the program worked equally for male and female trainees. The article also recommends further studies on why students lack communication, on other variables such as motivation, self-control and self-regulation, and training courses covering the other five CoRT parts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training delivered through sessions and workshops as in the Excellence and Innovation Center model at Al al-Bayt University
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from a single one-group pretest-posttest study without a control group

### Target Learners
- all university students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective communication skills
