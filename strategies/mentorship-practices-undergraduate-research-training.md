# Structure mentorship with clear expectations, regular feedback, and a supportive environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To increase the effectiveness of mentorship, the article recommends that mentors "establish clear expectations, goals, and timelines for research projects", hold regular communication and feedback sessions, and create a supportive and inclusive learning environment encouraging open communication, collaboration, and knowledge sharing. It also cites a randomized controlled trial reporting that mentor training based on self-determination theory and peer group mentoring improved mentoring interaction quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified mentors; in this study all mentors had completed standardized training programs and passed institutional qualification assessments, with mentor–trainee matching by mutual selection
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on the authors' discussion and cited literature, not on a mentorship intervention tested in this survey

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates in research training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- development of research competencies through effective mentorship
