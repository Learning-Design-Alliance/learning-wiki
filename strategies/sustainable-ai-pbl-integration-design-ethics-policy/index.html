Pursue sustainable AI integration in PBL through attention to design, ethics, and policy¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on its synthesis, the review recommends that sustainable AI integration in education be pursued through design, ethical, and policy measures. The article states the review contributes "practically by highlighting design, ethical, and policy implications for sustainable AI integration in education." This strategy directs implementers to address the ethical concerns (data privacy, algorithmic bias, educator autonomy) alongside technical design when adopting AI-enhanced PBL.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Attention to design, ethical, and policy dimensions when integrating AI into PBL

Constraints¶

  • Derived from the review's synthesis rather than from an intervention tested in the review

Target Learners¶

  • educators and institutions adopting AI-enhanced PBL

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustainable, ethically sound integration of AI in education

Related Strategies¶

  • Pursue inclusive digital strategies, educator professional development, and ethical AI governance for AI in adult education
  • Prioritize real-world testing of AI tools in university settings, environmental impact measurement, and integrated ethical-sustainability policies
  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Adopt a systems approach to AI integration rather than spotlights or gaps
  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
  • Pursue ethical AI feedback use as a collective effort across multiple stakeholders in and beyond higher education
  • Implement AI-supported STEM instruction through sustained, system-level integration of one to two months or more rather than isolated short interventions
