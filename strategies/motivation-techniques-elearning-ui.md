# Increase learner motivation in e-learning interfaces using speech interface, informal style, animated pedagogical agents, color variety, learner control, and background music

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends a set of UI techniques to grow the feeling of requirement and motivation and reduce coercion: speech interface, informal communication style, animated pedagogical agents, color variety, learner control, and background music. It states that "Color variety, causes motivation and attention in students and makes educational materials seem more realistic" and that matching voice gender, personality, or accent fosters trust and interest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pedagogic stimulus should involve willingness, the deep desire to learn something, not fear of punishment or desire for reward
- Background music should have simple musical structure and be light with average volume; it should not be used if the designer lacks musical expertise
#### Constraints
- More than 10 colors distorts the relation between information
- Music and sounds are not suitable when learners are under increased mental pressure, such as unfamiliar content or high presentation speed

### Target Learners
- e-learning software designers
- self-learning students using educational multimedia

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained motivation and attention during e-learning
