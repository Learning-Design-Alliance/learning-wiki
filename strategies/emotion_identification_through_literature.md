# Emotion Identification through Literature

## Description
This strategy involves using emotion posters as a tool during read-alouds to help students infer and name a character's emotions through language and pictures. The teacher asks students to analyze the text and visuals to understand the character's feelings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a book with strong emotional content, emotion posters, and a teacher to facilitate discussion. Can be integrated into regular reading sessions.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the selected literature and the teacher's ability to guide discussion. Some students may struggle with emotional inference or expressing their feelings.

### Affordances
- Develops social-emotional skills, encourages critical thinking, connects literature to emotional understanding, promotes empathy by understanding characters' feelings.

### Personalization
- Adaptable to different age groups and reading levels by selecting appropriate texts and adjusting the complexity of emotion posters. Can be modified to focus on specific emotions or social situations.
