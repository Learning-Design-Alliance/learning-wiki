# Develop POCUS education programs by combining formal training, a formal competency assessment tool, and integration into real clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For settings where POCUS education faces limitations, the article's future-work section recommends a three-part avenue: "implementing the formal training of POCUS, creating a formal assessment tool of POCUS competency, and integrating POCUS education into real clinical practice". It further directs future work toward "long-term measures of student performance and the utility of POCUS used in specific disease cases", since the present study could not attribute competence to specific diseases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to a formal POCUS curriculum and a validated competency assessment instrument
#### Constraints
- Recommended from one retrospective study in a single Thai training hospital; per-disease competence was not assessed

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- POCUS competency development and assessment
