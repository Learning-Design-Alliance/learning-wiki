# Recommendations for future CCE-A accreditation standards: define chiropractor, review impact, add descriptive detail, revisit omissions, and address radiology and vitalism/subluxation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes with five recommendations, summarised in Table 5, for improving future graduate competency standards. These are to "Create a clear definition of chiropractor", review the impact of the changed standards, provide greater descriptive material for competencies such as peer review and self-awareness of personal biases, review competencies omitted from 2017, and add targeted descriptive information on known difficulty areas such as radiology and vitalism/subluxation. The stated aim is that programs understand expectations and educators can amend curriculum and demonstrate compliance.
