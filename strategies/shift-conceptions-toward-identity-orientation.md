# Shift teachers' conceptions of writing instruction toward identity orientation to develop transformative TPACK

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that identity-oriented conceptions align with Transformation-level TPACK, the article recommends that "teachers may need to shift the conceptions of writing instruction toward identity orientation to develop transformative TPACK". It further suggests teacher educators can develop teacher education programs regarding the use of SVVR in writing instruction and design assessment criteria for teachers' VRWI design and practices based on the identified hierarchical levels of SVVR integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher education programs targeting conceptions of writing instruction, not only technology skills
#### Constraints
- Findings come from experienced Chinese teachers at one school led by a highly dedicated Chinese teacher who is also an established writer, and may not be generalizable

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers of writing

### Target Learning Goals
- transformative technology integration in writing instruction
