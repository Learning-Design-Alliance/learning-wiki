# Train parents to use paired reading supplemented with comprehension, fluency, and vocabulary strategies during summer book reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy operationalizes the intervention: parents engage in daily paired reading of 5–15 minutes, ask wh-questions, retell, summarize, encourage predictions and connections, model oral reading, reread favourite passages, and support vocabulary through context inference and direct word explanations. Parents received book-specific information sheets with summaries, comprehension questions, and vocabulary definitions. The schedule and ordering of events remained constant over the eight-week period.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly book packages matched to child interest and reading level
- Parent training booklet and periodic telephone support
#### Constraints
- Least-implemented strategies were making connections to other relevant information and looking up words in a dictionary

### Target Learners
- Grade 3 and Grade 5 children reading below or at average levels

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension strategies
- oral reading fluency
- vocabulary acquisition
