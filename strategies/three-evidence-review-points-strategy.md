# Review evidence of learning at three points: before, during, and after the lesson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief identifies three critical opportunities for the teacher to review and act on evidence of learning: during planning, when the teacher reviews evidence from past lessons to select texts students can comprehend with support; during the lesson, when the teacher collects evidence by listening to discussions, reviewing annotations, and conferencing, enabling actionable feedback and in-flight adjustments; and after the lesson, to support planning and refining the next lesson.
