# Complement distributed peripheral information with aggregated display or multimodal notification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that designers complement distributed peripheral information with aggregated displays or higher-level multimodal notifications for moments when teachers need more accurate or more urgent information. Teachers could not quickly count lamps per colour once more than two colours were available, and often did not notice during instruction that many students could not keep up; a smartphone vibration cue helped teachers perceive this while focusing on their story. The article concludes that "at certain moments, teachers need to access aggregated information in addition to the distributed display".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An additional or alternative modality, such as vibration from a smartphone, must be available to raise the notification level above the peripheral display when signals are time-relevant.
#### Constraints
- The article states the peripheral display alone is not enough for teachers to shift their focus from their story to the students when many students can no longer keep up with instruction.

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- timely on-the-spot decisions about when to differentiate or adapt whole-class instruction
