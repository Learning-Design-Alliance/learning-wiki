# Use a five-part guideline (relevance, wordplay, clarity, grammar, fairness) to label acceptable crossword clue-answer pairs

## Description
The article's appendix defines a guideline for human and automatic evaluation of clue-answer pairs, built on five components: Relevance and Cohesion, Wordplay and Inventiveness, Clarity and Precision, Grammar and Language, and General Knowledge and Fairness. The authors state that "With strict adherence to this guideline, we can guarantee the accuracy of our classifier in assessing the quality of clue-answer pairs." This guideline grounded the 6,000 human labels used to train the quality classifiers.
