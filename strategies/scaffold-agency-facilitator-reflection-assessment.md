# Scaffold learner agency in PBL through facilitator coaching, structured reflection opportunities, and assessment and workload adjustments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend multi-level strategies to enhance learner agency in dental education. For students, they suggest "reflective tools, goal-setting workshops, and feedback literacy training" to empower proactive ownership of learning. For educators, professional development should emphasize facilitators as coaches who scaffold self-regulated learning and identity formation without diminishing autonomy, adapting support to variable student readiness. At the institutional level, they recommend adopting alternative assessments and facilitating guided reflection to create an environment where agency is nurtured.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator professional development to prepare facilitators as scaffolding coaches
- Institutional willingness to adjust assessment practices and workload distribution
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a single-institution qualitative study of preclinical students; the article notes generalizability is limited and longitudinal research is needed
