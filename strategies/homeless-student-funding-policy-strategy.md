# Four-part policy strategy: increase funding, revise allocation formulas, expand allowable uses, and improve spending data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that policymakers "consider increasing funding, making changes to funding distribution, expanding allowable uses of federal funding, and improving the quality of information about the use of funds." Concretely: raise McKinney-Vento toward the ARPA baseline of roughly $625 per student; shift allocation toward an entitlement model based partly on homelessness enrollment; align HUD's definition with McKinney-Vento's and allow emergency housing expenses; and make Title I set-aside reporting permanent so states can identify promising approaches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Federal and state legislative action on appropriation levels, allocation formulas, and allowable uses of funds
#### Constraints
- The report notes additional research is needed to determine the exact funding amount necessary, and ARPA funds were one-time rather than ongoing

### Target Learners
- Students experiencing homelessness in U.S. public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Educational access and stability
- Full participation in school
- College and career readiness
