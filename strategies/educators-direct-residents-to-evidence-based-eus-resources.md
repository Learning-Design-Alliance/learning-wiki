# Educators should direct residents to accurate, effective, evidence-based EUS resources and maintain faculty skills in advanced applications for supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that residency educators actively manage the supplementary EUS learning residents already undertake. Because "The majority of residents (73% [27/37]) used other educational resources outside of residency training", the authors argue "It is up to educators to direct residents to EUS resources that are accurate, effective, and evidence-based." They also argue educators need to ensure faculty continue honing skills in advanced applications to provide appropriate supervision.
