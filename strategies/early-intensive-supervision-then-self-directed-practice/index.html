Provide intensive early supervision to build mental representation, then transition learners to self-directed practice with automated feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

To address the human-resource cost of DP-informed SBST, the review proposes "providing intensive supervision early in the training to develop a mental representation adequate for subsequent self-directed practice" as a cost-effective option. This empowers learners to use automated VR feedback effectively. An alternative is asynchronous video-based feedback, though it conflicts with DP's immediate-feedback principle.
