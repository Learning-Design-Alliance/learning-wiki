# Teacher practices for implementing The Expert Mathematician: lesson walkthroughs, alternating direct and generative learning days, and the 30-second probe

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum prescribes specific teacher moves: teachers work through each lesson ahead of students following developer-provided instructions, may "alternate direct instruction days with generative learning days," reinforce successes, gently correct mathematical interpretations, and suggest extending investigations. To promote critical thinking, teachers prod students to explain a concept, "called the 30-second probe." The developer states the tools do not require extensive teacher training and reduce lesson planning time.
