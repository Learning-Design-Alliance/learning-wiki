# Use identified student intelligence levels to classify students as fast, medium, or slow and treat each category differently

## Description
The article describes how teachers use information on students' intelligence levels to "classify students into categories of fast, medium, and slow," with each category "treated differently so that students feel comfortable in understanding the material presented." Treatments include material enrichment or added tasks for fast students and appropriately organized material for slow students, with fast students sometimes serving as peers whose explanations are easier to understand, which the article says fosters good learning motivation.
