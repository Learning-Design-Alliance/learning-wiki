Integrate model-based supports across pretraining, in-simulation, and post-simulation stages of VR teacher practice¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends an integrative, model-based approach to supporting preservice teachers in VR teaching simulations, rather than isolated individual strategies. The authors state that "An integrative, model-based approach, incorporating pre-training, in-simulation, and post-simulation support, appeared beneficial for teaching knowledge development and showed potential in enhancing teaching self-efficacy." Supports include orientation videos, lesson plan templates, reflection prompts, modeling videos, questioning supports, and facilitated debriefing.

Claims¶

  • Model-based support provided numerical advantages over using the AI-powered virtual student agent alone for teaching knowledge and skills [+W]
  • Model-based support in an AI-enhanced VR teaching simulation improved teaching knowledge and skills more than a no-treatment control [+W]
  • Only the model-based support group showed a significant within-group improvement in teaching self-efficacy from pre- to post-test [+W]
