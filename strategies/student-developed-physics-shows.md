# Students develop and present the outreach show themselves, with senior-student mentoring and a yearly new show

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
"A distinguishing feature of the Bonn Physikshow is that the students develop and present the shows largely on their own", with technical assistance from staff and guidance mainly from more senior students. A new show is developed "almost every year", giving each new class of physics students the opportunity to create their own show; development takes roughly 6 months and between 15 and 25 students participate each year.
