# Combine VR task-training for operators with simulation team-training for the whole acute stroke team

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's quality improvement program pairs individual VR simulator task-training for interventional radiologists with regular simulation team-training and debriefing for the entire acute stroke team, including ambulance staff, emergency department staff, stroke neurologists, and nurses. The authors state that "a combination of VR simulation training to improve technical skills and simulation team training" may improve workflow times, reperfusion quality, and clinical outcomes. Debriefing feedback is used to optimize local EVT protocols.

## Design Implications

### Target Learning Goals
