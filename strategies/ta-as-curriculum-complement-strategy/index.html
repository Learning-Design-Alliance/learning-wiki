Integrate teachable agents as a complement to, not a replacement for, the adopted classroom curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends deploying TAs as an augmentation of district-adopted curricula rather than standalone instruction: "we use TAs as a complement to regular classroom instruction." Kit+TA teachers used the system at least once per concept map within the existing eight-week FOSS Environments unit, with no added instructional time, and were otherwise free to choose how and when to use the TAs. The study found the TA complement improved learning on the kit's own measures rather than displacing the curriculum's value.

Requirements¶

  • Relevant hidden expert maps for the curriculum's subunits must be produced before deployment

  • The demonstration study let teachers choose implementation, so dosage and integration varied across teachers
