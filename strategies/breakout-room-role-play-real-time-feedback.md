# Use breakout-room role-play and simulation with instructor and peer observation and immediate real-time feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During synchronous sessions, students rotate through roles (e.g., counselor, patient, observer) in small web-conferencing breakout rooms while faculty observe and intervene. The article states instructors "visit each breakout room to provide immediate feedback and redirection if necessary," and that role-playing patient-practitioner scenarios "increase critical thinking skills" citing prior work. Debriefing in the main room helps students analyze and synthesize the experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web conferencing platform supporting breakout rooms
- Faculty available to visit breakout rooms and give feedback in real time
- Structured debriefing back in the main room
