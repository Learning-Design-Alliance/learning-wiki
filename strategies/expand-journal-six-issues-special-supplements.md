# Expand a professional journal to six issues per year with special supplements to cover a rapidly changing field

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The foreword announces a journal redesign: beginning with the present volume the journal will be expanded to six issues per year, four regular issues with increased coverage of the AV communication field and two special supplements each devoted to an intensive consideration of some area of critical concern in AV communication. This is a publication strategy for keeping a professional field informed during dynamic change.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Editorial awareness of changes occurring in the field and imaginative interpretation of them as they pertain to educational applications
#### Constraints
- Supplements are expected to find audiences in other disciplines than our own

### Target Learners
- education researchers and practitioners in the audiovisual communication field

### Target Learning Goals
- staying informed about innovations in instructional technology and their educational applications
