# Use sentence construction activities to teach compound, complex, and compound-complex sentence types

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends introducing a sentence construction activity so students learn three sentence types: compound sentences (at least two independent clauses that are joined), complex sentences (an independent clause and at least one dependent clause), and compound-complex sentences (at least two independent clauses and at least one dependent clause). Teachers draw material from classroom books, students' lives, school events, articles, or students' own writing, model constructing each type, provide independent practice, and have students discuss their sentences in pairs or small groups. The tip is adapted from Saddler, Behforooz, and Asaro (2008); Saddler (2005); and Saddler and Graham (2005).
