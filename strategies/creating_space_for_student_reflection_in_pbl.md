# Creating Space for Student Reflection in PBL

## Description
Creating space for student reflection involves integrating moments for students to pause and think about themselves, their ideas, and how they feel about the project. This practice flips the script on traditional classrooms where the emphasis is on urgency, making schools safer places that can mitigate trauma and energize students. Reflection can be facilitated through mindfulness practices like journaling, meditating, yoga, or connecting to nature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time dedicated to reflection, a classroom environment that values mindfulness and self-awareness, and access to reflection tools such as journals or meditation guides.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in traditional teaching practices and may necessitate additional training or support to implement mindfulness practices effectively.
