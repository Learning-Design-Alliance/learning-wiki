Alleviate writer's block through planning and outlining, drafting without perfectionism, and editing beyond spell-check¶

Description¶

The article states that "ending writer's blocks can be achieved through three main steps: planning and outfling, just write it, and editing beyond spell-check". Writers are encouraged to plan the writing task using graphic organizers, visuals and charts that list facts, holdings and reasoning and support comparing and contrasting cases; the actual writing process should occupy about one third of the whole process; and proofreading and editing form the final phase, subdividable by writers' levels. The article attributes block partly to lack of planning and to perfectionism that crushes each sentence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Writers need generated ideas as raw material, since fruitful generated ideas minimize opportunities for blocks
  • An effective plan to edit is needed, since without it each sentence is crushed under the weight of perfection

Constraints¶

  • The article notes writer's block may exaggerate when writing business English, where the twin difficulty is the lack of technicalities and the shortage of mechanics
