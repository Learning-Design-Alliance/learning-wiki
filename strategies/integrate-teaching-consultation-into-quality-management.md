# Integrate teaching consultation into the quality management of medical universities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, because SETs alone do not considerably improve teaching quality, "a teaching consultation should be integrated into the quality management of medical universities". Combined with feedback interventions, the effectiveness of SETs can be improved, and consultation complements existing faculty development programs. The authors state the concept may also be applied to other faculties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A long-term support of lecturers and disciplines must be possible
- Close connection between faculty consultant, faculty development, and the SETs
#### Constraints
- Future research should clarify what changes can be expected and which factor of consultation is notably relevant

### Target Learners
- university teachers in medical faculties

### Target Learning Goals

- sustainable improvement of teaching quality
