# Use an in-game spear as a controllable velocity-vector representation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In Guardian of light, a character equipped with a spear moves at constant walking speed, so the spear offers a ready visual representation of a velocity vector of constant magnitude. Students walk the character on prescribed paths, such as a circle, while keeping the spear tangential to the path. The authors write: "By putting the student in control of a vector, the ph ysical nature of an abstract mathematical concept is elucidated."
