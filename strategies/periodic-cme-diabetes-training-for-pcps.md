# Build periodic diabetes care training for primary care physicians into mandatory annual CME

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends periodic training of primary care physicians on diabetes care as the primary response to the knowledge and practice gaps it documented. It states this "could be built into the mandatory annual CME required for renewal of practicing license", tying refresher training to an existing regulatory requirement. The recommendation follows directly from the finding that training non-participation predicted poor knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sponsorship or infrastructure to deliver periodic diabetes training workshops accessible to PCPs across both public and private primary care centers
#### Constraints
- In the studied population, 78.1% had not participated in any diabetes training since graduation, so delivery mechanisms must reach physicians who have never attended updates

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians (general practitioners) managing diabetes in low- and middle-income settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of diabetes diagnostic criteria, pharmacotherapy, glycemic monitoring, and complication screening
