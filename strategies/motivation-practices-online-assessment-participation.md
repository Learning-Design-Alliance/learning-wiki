# Use announcements, email reminders, and completion credit to achieve online RBA participation rates similar to in-class testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because online, out-of-class assessments suffer lower completion, the authors recommend that instructors motivate students by "making announcements in class, sending out email reminders, and giv- ing credit to students who complete the RBAs." They report these practices allowed instructors to achieve CBT participation rates similar to PPT assessments, while courses not using them can see quite low participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor effort: in-class announcements, email reminders, and giving credit for completion
#### Constraints
- Without these practices, participation rates on online assessments can be quite low

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students completing assessments outside of class

### Target Learning Goals
- maximizing completion of low-stakes online assessments so course outcome data are usable
