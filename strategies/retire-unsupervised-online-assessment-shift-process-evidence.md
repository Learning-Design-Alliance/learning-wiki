# Retire unsupervised closed-response online assessment and shift credit toward process evidence, supervised settings, and AI-transparent work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a decisive, discontinuous response to AI capability growth: closed- or short-answer online homework, quizzes, and exams conducted outside controlled settings are "no longer viable sources of credit or effective gatekeepers." Instead, instructors should require accountable work such as modeling assumptions, unit analysis, limiting cases, and a plan-solve-check trace, grade the reasoning rather than the result, move major certification to supervised settings, and require disclosure of AI use with representative prompts and outputs critiqued by students.
