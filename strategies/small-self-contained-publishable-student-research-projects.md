# Assign each student a small, self-contained, original research project completable in the available time and publishable with the student as co-author

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The author's core supervision strategy is to give each student (or pair) a bounded original project using real archival data, completed and published in a refereed journal with the student as co-author. He writes that his "strategy is to give each student (or two, or three) a small, self-contained, original research project". Students first read and meet weekly, then learn the data and software, and finally draft a report with supervisor feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects must be scoped to be completable in the time available and significant enough for publication in a refereed journal
#### Constraints
- The author notes the key tension of "how to guide the research, while allowing the students some initiative, creativity, and flexibility"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- outstanding senior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific research skills
- time-series data analysis
- scientific writing and communication
