# Set a High Bar for Each Student

## Description
Hold high expectations for all students. Convince students that they can learn and succeed through social-psychological interventions that focus on changing students’ thoughts, feelings, and beliefs in and about school. Focus on fostering a Learner Mindset, and building self-efficacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training or guidance on effective communication and motivation techniques, a supportive and encouraging classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent effort, may be challenging with students who have deeply ingrained negative beliefs about their abilities
