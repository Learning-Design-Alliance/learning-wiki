# Describe AI instructional role and learner–AI interaction design in detail (prompts, scaffolds, modality, dialogue structures)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the AI Role and Interaction domain, the article recommends a concrete reporting practice: "authors should describe not only the AI system’s instructional role but also the design of learner–AI interaction in sufficient detail, including prompts, scaffolds, modality, and dialogue structures." This lets the field distinguish pedagogical effects from the contingencies of specific interaction designs, and supports comparison and translation of findings across contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies document the AI's pedagogical role (e.g., tutor, feedback agent, scaffolded conversational partner) and the interaction design
