Appoint a dedicated coordinator to monitor the accuracy and frequency of parent texts¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report recommends that schools considering parent-texting programmes provide a dedicated coordinator to oversee text content, accuracy and frequency. Respondents felt "the presence of a dedicate d coordinator would be valuable to monitor the accuracy and frequency of texts", and teachers acknowledged someone must be responsible for coordinating texts within the school. Schools should consider whether they can provide this additional resource.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A person (or persons) within the school responsible for coordinating texts and monitoring text content, accuracy, and frequency

Constraints¶

  • Some schools struggled with the more complex and time-consuming texts, such as conversational prompts, and sent these less frequently

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school pupils in Years 7, 9 and 11

Target Learning Goals¶

  • parental engagement in pupils' learning
  • pupil attainment and attendance

Claims¶

  • Texting parents about tests, homework and lesson content produced a small positive effect on maths attainment (ES 0.067) in a cluster RCT [+W]
  • Texting parents reduced pupil absenteeism (ES -0.054, about half a day) even though no texts concerned attendance [+W]
  • The small positive English attainment effect (ES 0.033) is at risk of bias from missing baseline data and cannot be reliably concluded [+W]
  • The PEP texting intervention showed no evidence of an impact on science attainment [+W]
  • Intervention parents were more likely than control parents to ask their child about revising for upcoming tests [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Mobile Communication for Family Engagement
  • Utilize Text Messaging for Parent Engagement
  • Designate a Spalding coordinator and provide 45-70 hours of formal teacher training with ongoing mentoring to support implementation fidelity
  • Appoint a designated project lead in each school and use multiple methods to engage parents when delivering parent education interventions
