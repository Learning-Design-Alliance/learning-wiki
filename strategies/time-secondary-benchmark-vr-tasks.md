# Include time to completion as a secondary benchmark alongside simulator-generated total scores

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends incorporating task completion time into proficiency-based curricula because the time component of the efficiency subtotal has been found nondiscriminating, and score overlap between novices and experts on some tasks limits score-only benchmarks. "The overlap in total scores between novices and experts for Fourth Arm Cutting, Puzzle Piece Dissection, and Vessel Energy Dissection underscores the importance of including secondary performance metrics, such as time to completion, as an additional benchmark."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recording of simulator-generated time to completion in addition to total score
#### Constraints
- Attempts ending in critical error should be excluded from time calculations as the task was not performed to completion

### Target Learners
- novice robotic surgery trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- discriminating skill levels in VR surgical simulation
