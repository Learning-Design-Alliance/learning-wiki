# Cultivate innovation and problem-solving abilities through research projects and competitions

## Description
The authors recommend that teaching teams "place sustained emphasis on cultivating students' innovation and problem-solving abilities" by leveraging institutional and departmental platforms to promote participation in research projects and academic competitions. This recommendation follows from the decision tree finding that innovation ability improvement was the strongest differentiating factor for strong cognitive performance.
