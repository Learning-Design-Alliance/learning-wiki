# Replace teacher-centred delivery with Socratic questioning: student-led discussion built on topic questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Derived from the pedagogical approach of Socrates, this active-engagement method is described as "a powerful teaching tool to switch students from passive viewers to active participants" while developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills. In lectures designed this way, "teacher-centred delivery is replaced with a student-led discussion based on a set of questions related to a specific topic", with students directly asking and answering questions to develop deep learning. Electronic resources such as websites, video and audio material can support the method.
