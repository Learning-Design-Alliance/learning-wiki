# Combine a short SALT lecture with 30 minutes of patient-scenario group work within a one-hour session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training model combined a 30-min PowerPoint lecture on the SALT algorithm with a 30-min exercise in which participants triaged 10 trauma victims, either with trauma cards in groups of 3–5 or via presenter-led direct instruction. Both formats used the same starting scenario, a bus crash with three ambulances on site, and a SALT algorithm poster was available during the scenarios. Total training time was 60 min for both groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A presenter held by the same person across sessions with a passive observer to ensure conformity
- A test of novel casualties to measure individual triage skills before and after
#### Constraints
- The study used paper-based patient scenarios and the findings may not be the same as those for a real MCI

### Target Learners
- firemen
- prehospital care providers

### Target Learning Goals
- applying the SALT triage algorithm to casualty scenarios
