# Breathing Exercises

## Description
Breathing exercises can help learners to focus and settle their feelings and thoughts. Regular practice can support emotional regulation and improve attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated time for practice, instructor guidance
#### Constraints
- May require consistent practice for sustained benefits
