# Pair student-collected video datasets from physical home-buildable apparatus with PhET and/or oPhysics simulations in online labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For fully online first-year EM courses, record videos of physical apparatus so that each student analyzes a unique dataset, then supplement these measurements with circuit simulations. The author states that "industrious students ought to be able to construct equivalent apparatuses using only items that are relatively easy to acquire" and that "the measurements described in this paper will be supplemented with electric circuit simulations from PhET and/or oPhysics."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded videos uploaded and freely accessible so students can extract their own volume-versus-time data; a ruler beside the capacitor so heights can be read to about 0.5 mm
#### Constraints
- The author expects the visual demonstrations to remain useful even once in-person labs resume, framing them as motivated by the online-delivery need rather than a replacement for it

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students in large online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- collecting and analyzing unique experimental datasets
- visual demonstration of abstract circuit concepts
