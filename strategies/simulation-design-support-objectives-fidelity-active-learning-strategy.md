# Design simulation laboratories with strong student support, clear objectives and information, high fidelity, and active learning strategies

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that simulation-based education "needs to be incorporated at the different levels of nursing programs while focusing on increasing the support to students in simulation laboratories, helping students to comprehend and work toward achieving the objectives of the simulation activities and expanding the availability of high‐fidelity simulations." It further states that "active learning strategies should also be implemented to enhance students' opportunities to participate in simulation‐based educational activities." This follows from the study's finding that support, objectives and information, and fidelity were the highest-rated design dimensions and active learning the highest-rated practice.
