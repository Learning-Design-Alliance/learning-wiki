# Emotion Emojis Check-In

## Description
Students express their current feelings by drawing or sharing an emoji that represents their emotional state. Volunteers then share their emoji and explain how it reflects their current feelings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to emojis (either drawn or digital), time for individual reflection (2-3 minutes), and a willingness to share in a whole-class setting.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to find an emoji that accurately represents their emotions. The effectiveness of the activity depends on creating a safe and supportive classroom environment.

### Target Learners

- All ages, adaptable for pre-K through college students

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Express feelings creatively, increase emotional awareness, build comfort in sharing emotions, foster a positive classroom environment

### Affordances
- Provides a non-verbal way for students to express their emotions. Can be a fun and engaging way to check-in. Allows for individual expression and creativity.

### Personalization
- Students can choose from a range of emojis or create their own. The level of sharing can be adjusted based on individual comfort levels.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student participation in creating and sharing emojis. Assess the thoughtfulness and specificity of students' explanations of their emojis.

## Impact
- Acknowledging emotions calms the amygdala and reduces the intensity of felt emotions, helping individuals feel more in control.  Promotes emotional literacy and awareness.
