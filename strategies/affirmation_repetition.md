# Affirmation Repetition

## Description
This strategy involves repeating empowering statements to oneself during times of stress. Examples include 'I can only do my best' or 'I know I can do this.' Repeating affirmations helps build self-confidence and resilience, promoting a positive mindset during challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of personal challenges; ability to articulate positive statements; regular practice
#### Constraints
- May not be effective for all learners; requires consistent practice; may seem insincere without genuine belief
