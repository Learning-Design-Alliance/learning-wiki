# Volunteer-first faculty recruitment with modeling of good practice rather than top-down mandates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's tentative conclusions recommend recruiting faculty already committed to instructional quality and spreading adoption through their example. Project leads "believe that convincing less-interested and reluctant faculty through modeling of good practice by these volunteers and finding positive impacts on their students is more likely to succeed than top-down mandates." Clear contractual requirements also created urgency and drew in staff who do not usually collaborate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- External funding for faculty stipends or course releases
- Face-to-face interaction opportunities such as on-campus workshops and summits
#### Constraints
- Changes initially involved only a minority of each institution's faculty

### Target Learners
- higher education faculty teaching gateway courses

### Target Learning Goals
- faculty adoption of adaptive and active learning practices
