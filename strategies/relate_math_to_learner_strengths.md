# Relate Math to Learner Strengths

## Description
Help learners understand how math gets used in the world in ways that relate to their personal strengths. By connecting math concepts to learners' interests and expertise, teachers can empower students as math learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors need to get to know their learners and their interests and be creative in finding ways to connect math to those interests.
#### Constraints
- Requires instructors to be flexible and adaptable. May be challenging to implement in large classes with diverse interests.
