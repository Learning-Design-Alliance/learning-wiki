# Apply RAISE flexibly across methodologies and settings where AI is central or peripheral

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RAISE is intended for all researchers conducting empirical studies involving AI as part of the educational experience, directly or indirectly — from personalised tutoring and automated grading to curriculum generation, learning analytics, conversational tools, co-writing, and simulated peer collaboration. The article states: "It applies across methodologies; qualitative, quantitative, mixed-methods, design-based, and across settings, from formal K–12 to higher education and informal learning." The framework is designed to be flexible, scalable, and responsive to varying centrality of AI use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applicable where AI is a key contributing factor to the pedagogical intervention or approach
#### Constraints
- AI use may be central in some studies and peripheral in others; the framework flexes accordingly

### Target Learners
- Researchers, reviewers, and editors across K-12, higher education, and informal learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent transparency expectations regardless of methodology or setting
