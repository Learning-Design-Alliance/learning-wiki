# Link every STEM engagement activity to the wider learning ecology and deliver coherent reinforcing messages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
For providers unable to run deeper programmes, the article urges organisations where possible to involve and influence the wider ecology in an audience-focused way with every activity, to increase the likelihood of impacts lasting. One-offs retain inherent value and can collectively contribute to building and maintaining aspirations so long as they present coherent messages that reinforce one another. Repeated-intervention programmes additionally let providers control longer-term messaging and better capture impacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coherent messages across interventions that reinforce one another
- Audience-focused consideration of the young person's wider learning ecology
#### Constraints
- Wider collaboration raises practical challenges over management, coordination, resourcing, ownership, and data sharing and protection issues

### Target Learners
- young people in formal and informal STEM settings

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of STEM aspirations over time
