# Evaluate unaccompanied recorded singing with expert panels across five dimensions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After accompaniment-based teaching, "without accompaniment, each subject sang the song and their voices were recorded. An expert group of three experts evaluated and scored these recordings." Scoring used three stages (Totally, Partly, None) across five dimensions: melody, rhythm, lay-rhythm, intonation, and whole-song singing. Data were processed in SPSS with frequency, means and Independent-Samples T Test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Audio recording equipment; a panel of three experts; a defined scoring rubric (Totally, Partly, None) across five singing dimensions
#### Constraints
- Designed for evaluating individual unaccompanied song performance after instruction

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in grades 5-7

### Target Learning Goals
- Assessment of singing behavior in music education
