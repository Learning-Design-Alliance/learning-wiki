# Provide early-university guidance through encouragement, stimulation and welcoming

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes that participants, "especially the young women among them", benefit from the programme's guidance through three facets it provides: "encouragement (for studies in physics and mathematics, so far)", "stimulation (the courses and their content, the supplementary talks, the immersion)", and welcoming. Future modifications to the programme currently in preparation are intended to further enhance these facets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses, supplementary talks and immersion experiences that stimulate interest
- A welcoming atmosphere, such as a certificate ceremony held in the company of families and teachers
#### Constraints
- So far the guidance concerns studies in physics and mathematics only

### Target Learners
- pupils in the final or penultimate year of Secondary II (high school)
- young women considering studies in physics and mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- guidance towards university studies and careers in physics and mathematics
- a positive taste for student life and the university experience
