Institutional implementation recommendations for peer evaluation using the POET-O¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The research team proposes recommendations for implementing peer evaluation with the POET-O, including establishing "A committee that oversees policy and procedures for peer review" responsible for developing the review process and procedures. They further recommend that faculty reviewers receive release time or service credit, since "Completion of the total POET-O process takes approximately 3.5 h," and that all faculty receive training on accessibility per ADA section 508 guidelines with clear reviewer instruction on rating ADA compliance.
