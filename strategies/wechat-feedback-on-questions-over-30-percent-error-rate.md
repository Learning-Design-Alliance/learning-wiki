# Post-examination feedback on high-error questions: explain questions with error rates above 30% via the department's communication platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After each assessment, the teaching secretary reviews per-question correct and error rates on the QuestionStar platform, identifies items frequently answered incorrectly, and explains their image characteristics to residents through the WeChat communication platform. The article states the secretary explained the questions "focusing on the image characteristics of these cases" and that "The explanation for the questions that frequently led to incorrect answers was beneficial in enhancing the level of image interpretation skills." Both groups received this feedback, and both showed significant admission-to-exit gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An assessment platform that reports per-question correct and error rates, and a teaching secretary with time to analyze and explain high-error items
#### Constraints
- Applied to single-choice image-interpretation examinations of 10 questions per administration within one-month rotations

### Target Learners
- non-radiology residents in departmental rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- correcting recurring misinterpretations of imaging characteristics
