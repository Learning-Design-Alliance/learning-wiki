# Sound-blocking Devices

## Description
Sound-blocking devices, such as noise-reducing headphones or earmuffs, can help children with auditory sensitivities manage sensory input by reducing background noise. These devices lower the overall decibel rate of background noise, allowing children to focus and process information more effectively. They can be used in various settings, including school cafeterias, malls, concerts, and noisy streets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Noise-reducing headphones or earmuffs.
#### Constraints
- Some children may find headphones uncomfortable or may resist wearing them. The effectiveness of noise reduction may vary depending on the device and the environment.

### Affordances
- Reduces background noise, allowing children to better process information and focus on tasks. Provides a sense of control over the auditory environment, reducing anxiety and promoting self-regulation.

### Personalization
- Different types of headphones and earmuffs are available to suit individual preferences and needs. Some offer adjustable levels of noise reduction.
