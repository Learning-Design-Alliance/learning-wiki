# Focus CPD programs for laboratory personnel on quality management systems, competence assessment, customer care, and case studies on locally common disease profiles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that CPD programs developed from this needs assessment concentrate on the topics respondents rated most important: "quality management systems, competence assessment, customer care, and case studies on disease profiles common in Botswana". Case-study content should address the country's disease profiles, and providers should determine the most effective and sustainable delivery method while considering future trends in laboratory medicine and technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs should be based on an empirical assessment of needs, addressing shortfalls between existing knowledge or skill and needed competencies, as the article states for CPD development.
#### Constraints
- Needs will vary over time depending on prevailing challenges of practice, so the article calls for continuous evaluation of practice needs to remain relevant.

### Target Learners
- Medical laboratory scientists and technicians in Botswana and the region

### Target Learning Goals
- Quality management systems
- Competence assessment
- Customer care
- Case-based diagnostic reasoning on locally common disease profiles
