# Prefer PECS or speech-generating devices when selecting aided AAC approaches for individuals with ASD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the meta-analytic comparison of AAC intervention types, this strategy favors selecting the Picture Exchange Communication System or speech-generating devices over other picture-based systems for individuals with ASD. The article reports that "Effects of the Picture Exchange Communication System and speech-generating devices were larger than those for other picture-based systems," though other picture-based systems "did have small effects," so they remain an option rather than being contraindicated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires aided AAC options under consideration, including PECS, speech-generating devices, and other picture-based systems.
#### Constraints
- The evidence base is single-case research with small numbers of participants; other picture-based systems still showed small positive effects.

### Target Learners
- individuals with autism spectrum disorders who cannot speak or cannot speak intelligibly

### Target Learning Goals
- communication and other targeted behavioral outcomes
