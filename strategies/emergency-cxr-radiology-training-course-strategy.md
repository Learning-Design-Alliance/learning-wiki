# Implement radiology training on emergency conditions in family medicine residency, or use tele-radiology in primary care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that family medicine residency programs add radiology training focused on acute and life-threatening conditions, noting courses "could be delivered in a wide variety of formats, including an online platform", and that participants requested further online quizzes. They also suggest a diagnostic radiology rotation and tele-radiology support for primary healthcare centers as alternatives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Family medicine residents are expected to make clinical decisions based on chest X-ray interpretations
#### Constraints
- Further research is needed to re-assess interpretation skills after implementation of improvement measures

### Target Learners
- family medicine residents
- primary care physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate interpretation of chest X-rays for emergency conditions
