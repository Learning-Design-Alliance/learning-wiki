# Include formal instruction on CTML in university physics teacher education courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that Brazilian universities add CTML to physics teacher training so future educators can consciously choose, use and develop multimedia materials. Their justification is that even without formal instruction most participants chose aligned materials, yet "a significant proportion did not", and no undergraduates validated a Coherence-aligned choice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- curriculum space in physics degree courses for explicit CTML instruction
#### Constraints
- evidence base is a 13-participant case study; the authors call for further research before generalizing

### Target Learners
- physics teacher trainees
- physics teacher educators

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness and application of CTML principles when selecting and developing multimedia teaching materials

### Affordances
