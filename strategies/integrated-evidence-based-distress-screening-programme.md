# Implement distress screening as an integrated, evidence-based programme covering implementation, referral, and follow-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that "an integrated programme for implementing a distress screening is called for grounded on an evidence‐based strategy in which implementation, referral, and follow‐up are accounted for and continuously evaluated." This strategy responds to their finding that physicians generally accept standardized screening (M = 4.24) yet actual psycho‐oncology support remains low, and that perceived barriers correlate negatively with screening acceptance. Continuous evaluation of implementation, referral, and follow-up is central to the proposal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An evidence-based implementation strategy that accounts for implementation, referral, and follow-up, with continuous evaluation.
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as a call for future work, not an evaluated intervention; acceptance of screening was measured only by self-report in one center.

### Target Learners
- physicians at comprehensive cancer centers

### Target Learning Goals
- routine identification and referral of cancer patients' psychosocial distress
