# Integrate server-log network analysis with other methodological approaches to capture student learning strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend combining their log-based network approach with complementary methods. As they state, "we suggest that further research integrates this approach with other methodological approaches to get a fuller picture of how learning strategies are employed by students in settings with online features." This follows from their argument that server-log structures alone cannot capture intent or context, so behavioral structures must be triangulated with other data sources to approach learning behaviors and strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Non-anonymous data or additional modalities (verbal, physical) would be needed to ascribe sessions to students and build a combined map of learning behaviors
#### Constraints
- With anonymous server-log data alone, behavioral structures cannot be labeled learning strategies or learning behaviors

### Target Learners
- university students in blended online settings

### Target Learning Goals
- characterizing student problem-solving strategies
