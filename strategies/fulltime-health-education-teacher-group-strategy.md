# Build full-time health education teacher groups and provide regular health education training to reduce teaching difficulty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that education authorities "build a full-time health education teacher group in primary and secondary schools, and simultaneously enhance the health education training for teachers". It further suggests organizing provincial expert teams to compile unified health education teaching materials for children at different stages, and regularly organizing diverse training activities such as lectures, training camps, and online courses. These actions target the two regression-identified factors: occupation type and training experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policy support from education authorities to guarantee full-time health education teaching positions
#### Constraints
- At present, only 13.79% of health education teachers can work full-time, and more than 80% of them are required to take on other duties

### Target Learners
- Primary and secondary school students in China receiving school-based health education

### Target Learning Goals
- Health literacy development through school-based health education
