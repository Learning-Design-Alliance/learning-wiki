Establish student-led peer-based suicide prevention clubs using Sources of Strength¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The district partnered with Two Feathers Native American Family Services to train staff and students in Sources of Strength, described as "a peer-based mental health and suicide prevention resiliency program." Student-led SOS clubs were then established on secondary campuses as the district's suicide-prevention mechanism within Tier 1 universal supports.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partnership with a training provider (Two Feathers Native American Family Services) and staff and student training in SOS

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • suicide prevention and mental health resiliency

Related Strategies¶

  • Use visible adult identifiers and peer-focused media to signal safe adults and open conversations about suicide prevention
  • Conduct universal mental health screening with the Student Risk Screening Scale to inform intervention
