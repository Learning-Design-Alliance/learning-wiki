# Small Group Instruction

## Description
Small group instruction involves a teacher working with a small number of students on a specific skill or concept. It allows for targeted support and individualized attention, helping to address the unique needs of each learner. This approach enables teachers to provide instructional scaffolds, engage learners more effectively, and offer strategic coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small group of students, focused instructional materials, and a teacher or facilitator with expertise in the target skill or concept.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure alignment with overall learning objectives. May be challenging to manage if students have significantly different skill levels.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Allows for personalized attention, enables targeted intervention, provides opportunities for immediate feedback, and facilitates deeper engagement with the material.

### Personalization
- Content, pace, and instructional strategies can be tailored to meet the specific needs of the students in the group. Scaffolding can be adjusted based on individual progress.
