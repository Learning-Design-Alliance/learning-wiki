# Preferential Seating

## Description
Place students who have trouble hearing, seeing, focusing on, or paying attention to instruction happening in seats where they will be best able to focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexibility in classroom seating arrangements.
#### Constraints
- May not be feasible in all classroom settings.
