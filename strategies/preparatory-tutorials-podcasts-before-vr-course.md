# Provide preparatory VR tutorials and video podcasts before the VR course session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before the VR simulation, students complete a hands-on VR tutorial practising all necessary interactions in a modified form, and watch video podcasts covering the medical content and the hardware. The authors write that "video tutorials or hands-on VR-tutorials that are presented prior to the VR course can enhance the learning process, reduce cognitive load, and improve the user experience", likening this to the flipped classroom concept. Watching the hardware podcast became a prerequisite for the course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors available for technical support during the tutorial and course; podcasts produced in the learners' language
#### Constraints
- The potential added value of tutorials prior to VR interventions remains largely unexplored, per the authors

### Target Learners
- medical students with varying levels of VR experience

### Target Learning Goals
- VR navigation and interaction skills
- brain death examination procedure
