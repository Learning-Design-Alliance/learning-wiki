# Devote class time for EFL students to interact, express understanding, and discuss comprehension with peers

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the author recommends that EFL teachers give students sufficient time to engage with texts and with each other. The article states that "EFL teachers are encouraged to devote sufficient time for students to interact, manipulate ideas, and express their understanding of the text" and that students should discuss their understanding with peers, which raises awareness of comprehension, helps determine comprehension level, and supports self-correction of mistakes.
