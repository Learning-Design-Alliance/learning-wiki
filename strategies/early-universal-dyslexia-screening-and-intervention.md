# Screen all children for dyslexia early with an evidence-based screener and follow up with evidence-based interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "these children must be screened for dyslexia early and provided with effective, evidence-based interventions," noting educators now know "how to effectively and efficiently screen for dyslexia using evidence-based screeners." Interventions "might include placement in specialized schools such as the Louisiana Key Academy." The strategy targets children who struggle in reading but remain undiagnosed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evidence-based screeners following the criteria for dyslexia screening in federal law; teachers trained by experienced language therapists; parental consent before detailed individual testing
#### Constraints
- Testing depended on obtaining parental consent, which was obtained for 76.0% of at-risk children in this study

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through second-grade children, especially African-American children with reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- Early identification of dyslexia and provision of evidence-based reading interventions
