# Adapt and reuse existing outreach material, tailoring session length and topic selection to the audience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes adapting another group's published outreach material for a new audience and context, then iterating: after the first session the authors "prepared a somewhat shorter version of the talk", and they advise deciding beforehand whether to be more selective with topics. They explicitly encourage others to "adapt, and improve any material in this article".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination with the school's teachers beforehand to check the level at which the exposition could proceed and the type of activity
#### Constraints
- The authors had no time to repeat the activity in different places, so the experience rests on a single school context

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 15-18

### Target Learning Goals
- basic concepts of network science
- appreciating the importance of mathematics to understand networks
