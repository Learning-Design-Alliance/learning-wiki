# Validate ChatGPT-generated learning content through instructor review before giving it to residents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 8 studies (3 qualitative, 2 associational, 2 theoretical, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 8 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because ChatGPT can generate misleading or incorrect information and lacks real-time clinical knowledge, the article's procedure routes all AI-assisted learning content through instructor validation: dialogue and discussion topics are "formulated by the instructors based on the teaching syllabus and, after undergoing instructor validation, are provided to the resident doctors". Residents may then make adjustments based on the actual clinical situation. This strategy operationalizes AI assistance while keeping instructor oversight over content accuracy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must review and validate AI-derived dialogue topics against the teaching syllabus before distribution
#### Constraints
- The article notes ChatGPT's limitations include lack of real-time clinical knowledge, potential to generate misleading or incorrect information, and difficulties understanding complex medical contexts without precise prompts

### Target Learners
- clinical resident doctors in specialty training

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate acquisition of specialty clinical knowledge
- safe use of AI tools for self-directed learning
