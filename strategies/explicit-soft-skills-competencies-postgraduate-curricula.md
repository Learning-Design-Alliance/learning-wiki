# Explicitly incorporate soft skills as defined competencies within postgraduate curricula through periodic programme review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that regulatory bodies and National Specialty Commissions "may wish to consider explicitly incorporating soft skills as defined competencies within postgraduate curricula, alongside technical and clinical expertise". Accreditation and quality assurance frameworks could encourage this by requiring regular programme updates. Specific learning objectives are suggested for each of the five soft skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Periodic review and updating of nationally approved residency training programmes
#### Constraints
- The process might be lengthy due to the administrative procedures involved in approval and implementation

### Target Learners
- Healthcare residents in nationally regulated postgraduate training

### Target Learning Goals
- Situational awareness
- Decision-making
- Teamwork
- Communication
- Leadership
