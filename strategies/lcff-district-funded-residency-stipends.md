# District investment of LCFF funds in residency stipends written into Local Control and Accountability Plans as a workforce development strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A financial sustainability strategy in which partner districts write the residency into their LCAPs and allocate Local Control Funding Formula funds toward resident stipends and program support staff, recouping costs through reduced recruitment, hiring, and onboarding expenses given residents' multiyear service commitments. As printed, "residency partner LEAs wrote the residency into their Local Control and Accountability Plans (LCAPs)—a document that California LEAs use to set goals, plan actions, and detail how state formula funding will be used to achieve district goals—as a workforce development and hiring strategy." Kern Urban was 100% district funded during 2022–23.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district willing to treat residency as a hiring strategy and commit multiyear funding
- Residents' multiyear service commitments so anticipated savings can offset costs
#### Constraints
- The report notes grant reliance is a sustainability risk; university in-kind contributions and staffing costs could become an issue for long-term sustainability, per the former dean

### Target Learners
- Teacher residency candidates needing financial support during preparation

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining high-quality clinical teacher preparation pathways
