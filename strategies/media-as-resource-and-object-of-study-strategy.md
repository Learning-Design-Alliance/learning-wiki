# Use ICT and media simultaneously as didactic resources and as objects of critical study, including controlled social-network use before open consumption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining within teaching-learning processes the presence of media as didactic resources with their parallel treatment as objects of study, so that students analyze their characteristics, their own use of them, and their advantages and possible dangers. The author proposes that social networks could first be employed "under educational control as preparation for the knowledge and management of the most socially extended" networks that children will later encounter as consumers. The strategy is presented as an integral education contemplating ICT/media in their authentic social and educational dimension within techno-media convergence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate pedagogical design of the activities, so that results with tools such as social networks used for interactivity, group work, creativity and motivation are positive
#### Constraints
- The author warns that the margin of impact of social networks on society, especially their influence on childhood and youth, cannot be stopped and must be addressed in parallel

### Target Learners
- children and young adults in school contexts
- all citizens as users and consumers of media

### Target Learning Goals
- critical analysis of media and social networks
- strengthening interactivity, group work, creativity and motivation

### Affordances
- Media Education Paradigm
