Expose students to active class activities such as questioning, discussing and practical work to boost learning and skills acquisition¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete teaching activity recipe drawn from the article's recommendations: teachers should ensure students are well exposed to class activities. The article recommends that "Teachers should ensure that students are well exposed to class activities such as questioning, discussing, practical work among others to boost their pace of learning and skills acquisition." This operationalises the finding that active strategies outperformed lecture.
