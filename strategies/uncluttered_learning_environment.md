# Uncluttered Learning Environment

## Description
A clean, organized, and uncluttered learning environment is free from distractions and allows the focus to be on learning. Clear workspaces, limited decorations, and organized materials can positively impact student focus and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Storage solutions (shelves, cubbies), organization tools (bins, labels), and a commitment to maintaining a clutter-free space.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort to maintain organization. May require adjusting personal preferences for decoration.

- Reduces visual distractions, promotes a calm and organized atmosphere, supports concentration and focus.

### Personalization
- Provide individual storage spaces to keep personal items organized. Allow for flexibility in arranging workspaces based on activity needs.
