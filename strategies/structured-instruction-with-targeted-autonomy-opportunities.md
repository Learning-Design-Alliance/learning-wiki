# Leverage structured teacher-led instruction while strategically creating autonomy opportunities for responsible students

## Description
The article recommends against wholesale adoption of Western student-centered models in Chinese universities and instead proposes pairing structured, teacher-led instruction with targeted autonomy opportunities. It concludes that "enhancing engagement in China requires a nuanced approach that leverages the strengths of structured, teacher-led instruction while strategically creating opportunities—particularly for responsible students—to thrive in more autonomous learning environments". Practically, it advises cultivating students' academic responsibility and tailoring instruction to local conditions when implementing student-centered formats.
