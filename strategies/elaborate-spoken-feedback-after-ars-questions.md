# Deliver elaborate immediate spoken feedback after ARS questions, covering why the correct answer is right and why alternatives fail

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 1 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
After announcing the correct answer, instructors give detailed spoken feedback regardless of whether the majority answered correctly: "a brief summary of the reasons why a certain answer was correct and, if needed, why the other possible answers were not applicable". Instructors attended a seminar introducing feedback use and rules before the course. The control condition displayed only the correct answer and resumed the lecture without discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor training: participating instructors attended an introduction to the use of feedback and feedback rules by the principal investigator
- Feedback given directly in spoken form immediately after the answer is announced
#### Constraints
- In this randomized study the strategy produced no significant exam improvement over displaying the correct answer alone

### Target Learners
- dental students in their first clinical semester

### Target Learning Goals
- conservative dentistry content knowledge
