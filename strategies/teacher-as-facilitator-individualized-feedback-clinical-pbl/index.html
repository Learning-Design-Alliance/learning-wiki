Position the teacher as facilitator of discussion who lets students struggle with problems and gives individualized feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that in clinical PBL the teacher works as facilitators of discussion rather than an instructor. The facilitator's primary function is allowing students to struggle with a problem, providing guidance, reinforcing what is right, correcting errors and giving individualized feedback on performance; students so challenged are likely to progress their learning more rapidly.
