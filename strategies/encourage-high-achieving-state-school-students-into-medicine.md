# Encourage high-achieving students from state schools into medicine and consider accepting lower-scoring state-school applicants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that state-school entrants outperform independent-school entrants at medical school, the authors recommend that admissions may accept lower UCAS-tariff applicants from state schools on the expectation they make up the discrepancy in performance, and argue "there is an argument for encouraging high-achieving students from state schools into medicine". This strategy targets widening participation in medical education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Admissions teams must be able to identify school type as a contextual marker during selection.
#### Constraints
- The authors note that how to attract applicants from non-traditional backgrounds into medicine remains a challenge beyond the reach of this study.

### Target Learners
- prospective medical school applicants from state-funded schools

### Target Learning Goals
- widening access to medical education
