# Use Edpuzzle's embedded questions, analytics, and LMS integration to support differentiation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using Edpuzzle's customization and data features to personalize instruction: teachers place multiple-choice or open-ended questions and audio notes at precise moments, track viewing habits and struggle points, and grade through LMS integrations. Its "data-driven approach supports differentiation and personalized instruction", and teachers can integrate assignments with Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Schoology, Canvas, and more.
