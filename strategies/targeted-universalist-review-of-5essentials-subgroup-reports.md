# Use a targeted universalist approach to identify student groups with less positive survey reports and respond with empathy, curiosity, and support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that school leaders "apply a targeted universalist approach to look for areas where particular student groups share less positive reports—and respond with empathy, curiosity, and support." This is an implementable data-use routine: disaggregate survey reports by student group, locate groups with less positive experiences, and respond supportively. It complements school-wide 5Essentials summaries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to 5Essentials survey results disaggregated by student group
#### Constraints
- The brief notes that individuals within student groups can have widely differing experiences, so group-level reports do not capture every student's experience

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in elementary and high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving school climate, attendance, and student experiences school-wide
