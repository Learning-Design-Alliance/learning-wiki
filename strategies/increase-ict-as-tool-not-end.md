# Increase ICT in educational research as a tool serving educational objectives, not as an end

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends increasing the use of ICT in educational research, especially for data management, because it offers opportunities to enter the complexity of education and generate more accurate and reliable data. At the same time it warns that "ICT should be a useful tool for the process but not become the end of it," since new data-management possibilities can condition researchers to focus on method above real educational utility. It urges researchers to critically study the digital reality and its true impact on education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of ICT in the planning of research processes in the field of education, serving the objectives pursued
#### Constraints
- Several cited studies analyze possible drawbacks of the real effectiveness of ICT in educational research; ICT-driven rigor can accompany dubious contribution to educational knowledge

### Target Learners
- educational researchers
- teacher educators

### Target Learning Goals
- generating more accurate and reliable data about educational complexity and critically studying technology's impact on education
