# What's My Emotion? Game

## Description
Using an EQ Feeling Chart, students identify how they are feeling individually. Then, a student demonstrates how they are feeling through body language and facial expressions only, and the whole class tries to guess what that student is feeling.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EQ Feeling Chart, time for individual reflection and whole-class sharing
#### Constraints
- Some students may not feel comfortable demonstrating their feelings in front of the class. It is important to create a safe and supportive environment where students feel comfortable sharing. Some students may not be able to accurately identify or demonstrate their feelings.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Identify and demonstrate feelings non-verbally, encourage self-awareness, promote sharing and empathy

### Affordances
- Provides a fun and engaging way for students to explore and express their feelings, especially for those who may have difficulty articulating their emotions verbally. Normalizes talking about feelings and empowers students to view their emotions as assets.

### Personalization
- The EQ Feeling Chart can be adapted to different age groups and developmental levels. Students can choose to demonstrate different emotions, and the class can provide feedback on their accuracy.
