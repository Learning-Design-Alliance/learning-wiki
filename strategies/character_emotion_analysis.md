# Character Emotion Analysis

## Description
This strategy involves having students analyze characters' feelings in literature to improve their self-awareness and emotional recognition. Students identify, label, or match facial expressions and feelings of characters to connect literary analysis with emotional intelligence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a book with strong emotional content, visual aids (optional), and a teacher to facilitate discussion. Can be integrated into regular reading sessions.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the selected literature and the teacher's ability to guide discussion. Some students may struggle with emotional inference or expressing their feelings.

- Develops social-emotional skills, encourages critical thinking, connects literature to emotional understanding, promotes empathy by understanding characters' perspectives.

### Personalization
- Adaptable to different age groups and reading levels by selecting appropriate texts and adjusting the complexity of questions. Can be modified to focus on specific emotions or social situations.
