# Design instructional interventions around virtual care's unique affordances such as communication topics, non-intrusive observation and assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The commentary urges instructional interventions that help learners realise VC's unique affordances and acquire competencies for future practice. Interviews cited in the study showed VC is a good set-up for teaching "informed consent, privacy issues and VC etiquette, and communication", "observing learners non-intrusively", and efficient assessment. VC curriculum design should embrace these affordances and be geared toward VC fluency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must be well designed and geared toward VC fluency equipped with VC competencies.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Virtual care competencies including communication, consent, privacy and etiquette
