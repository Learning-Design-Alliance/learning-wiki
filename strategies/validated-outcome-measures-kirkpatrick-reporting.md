# Evaluate teaching skills programs with valid, reliable, objective outcome measures and report results using the Kirkpatrick model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future trials use pre and post assessment, valid randomisation with concealment, assessor blinding, and validated, reliable and objective outcome measures. It names instruments including "the Personal Teacher Identity Scale, OSTE, Clinical Teaching Assessment Form, Stanford Faculty Development Program Form-26, Clinical Teaching Effectiveness Instrument or the Resident Leadership Scale", and suggests the Kirkpatrick model as a standardised reporting process so results can be compared across studies. Trials should focus on higher Kirkpatrick levels (2b, 3, 4a, 4b) and whether effects are long lasting.
