# Universities should implement goal-oriented programs promoting the six dimensions of healthy lifestyle behavior

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that university management plan and implement programs motivating students to take responsibility for their own health, targeting the six measured dimensions of healthy lifestyle behavior. It concludes that "developing and implementing goal-oriented programs to promote dimensions of healthy lifestyle behavior such as stress management, health responsibility, interpersonal relations, physical activity, spiritual growth, and nutrition may promote healthy lifestyles among university students." It also suggests information initiatives about religious practice as a means to reduce unhealthy lifestyle behavior.
