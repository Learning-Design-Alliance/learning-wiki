# Support department chairs through orientation, mentoring, leadership development and clear evaluation indicators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes practical ways medical schools can improve department chair functioning: hold orientation programs, implement mentoring formally or informally, offer leadership development workshops, define performance indicators in advance, and clarify expectations and job descriptions. Participants said "Holding orientation programs for DCs is necessary, and people should be empowered from the beginning." The strategy pairs individual development supports with an evaluation and audit mechanism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Defined performance indicators and clarified expectations before evaluation
- Institutional commitment to mentoring and orientation programs
#### Constraints
- Derived from a qualitative Iranian medical education context; the authors note generalizability across contexts is difficult

### Target Learners
- New and incumbent medical school department chairs
- Medical school deans and senior academic managers

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthen chair leadership and management capability
- Improve chair performance in leading departments
