# Discussion-Oriented Flipped Classroom

## Description
Homework is assigned in the form of video lectures and external video resources. Discussions happen in the classroom time where topics are explored further. Having studied the basics, students can add value to discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires curation of high-quality video lectures and external resources, facilitation skills to guide discussions.
#### Constraints
- Dependent on learners' willingness to participate in discussions, may require careful moderation.
