# Shift toward resilience-sensitive differentiated support rather than blanket universal support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that post-crisis educational interventions move beyond uniform support toward designs that account for students' psychological resilience. The authors state that "Educational interventions could shift toward resilience-sensitive differentiated support." This follows from their finding that resilience selectively amplifies the motivation-to-support pathway, suggesting students with lower resilience may need more direct scaffolding to perceive and utilize available school support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
