# Propose a liberal arts course on mathematical modeling for sustainability for students from diverse academic backgrounds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a liberal arts course on mathematical modeling for sustainability, suitable for students from diverse academic backgrounds, to bridge learning about sustainability and sustainable learning. The course 'not only introduces students to sustainable development and the mathematics of sustainability but also enhances their quantitative thinking skills.' Learners grasp concepts 'through simple yet mathematically diverse examples' developed for teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simple yet mathematically diverse examples that can be further developed for teaching the course
- A suitable textbook, such as the reviewed quantitative critical thinking text
#### Constraints
- The candidate textbook does not cover calculus and differential equations, so instructors wishing to include them must add this content

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students from diverse academic backgrounds, including non-STEM majors

### Target Learning Goals
- quantitative understanding of sustainability
- sustainable learning in mathematics education beyond formal coursework
