# Ongoing math-specialist coaching and curriculum-based professional development for implementing teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each school is assigned "a designated on-site instructional coach who is a math specialist" supporting implementation monitoring, professional development, and coaching. Teachers receive curriculum-based training throughout the year plus "ongoing coaching in the form of modellings, observations and feedback sessions," including one-day in-person onboarding covering the CPA model, learning trajectories, and formative assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A designated on-site math-specialist coach per school; site visits scaled to district size; remote virtual check-ins for teachers and admins
#### Constraints
- PD/training varies depending on school availability

### Target Learners
- Elementary teachers implementing the mathematics curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- Fidelity of curriculum implementation
- Teacher knowledge of math education principles
