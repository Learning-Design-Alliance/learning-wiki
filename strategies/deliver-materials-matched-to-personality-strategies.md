# Deliver learning materials matched to students' personality-based learning strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A teaching strategy in which students' assessed personality traits determine which learning materials they receive. Following Duff et al. (2004), students tend to choose deep, surface, or strategic learning strategies based on personality traits; the authors prepared 4 labs completed in 6 weeks with materials designed around these three strategies and delivered materials matched to the experiment group's traits. Students "who were given learning materials based on their personality traits outperformed other students in their learning results significantly."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated personality assessment for each student; learning materials pre-designed for the three learning strategies.
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a small randomized experiment (10 experiment, 6 control students after withdrawals); the authors caution results may not be widely generalizable.

### Target Learners
- university students in elective programming courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improved programming-language learning performance (JavaScript)

## Claims

- [Students receiving personality-tailored learning materials scored significantly higher on JavaScript learning gains than controls in a small randomized experiment](../claims/personality-tailored-materials-improve-programming-gains.md) [+W]
