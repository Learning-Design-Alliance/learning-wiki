# Incorporate artificial intelligence and high-technology simulation methods into future oncology simulation research and practice

## Description
The authors identify the broad incorporation of high technology and innovative simulation methods as the main future research prospect for SBME in oncology. They describe AI as offering "tools for simulation, diagnostics, and personalized learning, thereby redefining the training approach for the challenges of modern medical education". Specific mechanisms cited include AI enhancing VR fidelity and optimizing 3D printing of precise organ models for surgical skills training.
