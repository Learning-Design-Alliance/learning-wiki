Design the review process to track and monitor resource allocation methods over time, adapt to changing needs, and share promising practices with other states.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's fifth lesson directs states to ensure the RAR process shows "how resource allocation methods will be tracked and monitored to demonstrate impact on student outcomes." It recommends an internal review to adapt the process to changing needs of educational leaders and students, and communicating new allocation methods and their measured impact to other leaders and the broader educational field.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Include an internal review to adapt the RAR process to the changing needs of educational leaders and students.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • state education agencies
  • LEA and school leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustaining and improving resource allocation review practice over time

Related Strategies¶

  • Conduct a resource allocation review to connect identified needs, evidence-based strategies, and resource allocation methods
  • Design a service-oriented resource allocation review using continuous improvement and design thinking, engaging LEAs and schools in design, pilot, and test phases before rollout.
