Teach signaling words to help students identify text structures¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends teaching students to recognize signaling words that mark the four main TLS organizational patterns. "Thoughtful student readers can take note of these signaling words to facilitate understanding or identification of the written text." Each structure type has characteristic words: listing uses "first, as well as, then"; problem-solution uses "a problem is… a solution is…"; cause-effect uses "because, consequently, therefore"; comparison-contrast uses "however, in contrast, although".
