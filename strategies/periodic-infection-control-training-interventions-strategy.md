# Implement periodic educational interventions and training programs on infection control practices for COVID-19 across all healthcare professions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that because knowledge gaps persist across all professional groups, institutions should "implement periodic educational interventions and training programs on infection control practices for COVID-19 across all healthcare professions." The recommendation rests on the survey's finding that more than a fifth of respondents had poor knowledge and specific gaps appeared in social distancing, respiratory etiquette, and management content. Health care professionals are advised to follow CDC and WHO guidelines in their health care settings.
