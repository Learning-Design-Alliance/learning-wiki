# Use AI-generated simulations where ready-made tools are unavailable, for edge cases, or as comparative learning tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends deploying custom LLM-generated simulations alongside, not instead of, established tools: the approach "may be beneficial in cases where ready-made simulations are unavailable, when exploring edge cases not supported by existing tools, or as a comparative learning tool". Combining professional and custom simulations gives educators and students a broader range of possibilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to LLMs and the prompt-based workflow; awareness of existing simulation libraries to identify gaps
#### Constraints
- The authors explicitly state the approach is not intended to replace well-established, robust simulations like PhET

### Target Learners
- physics educators
- students

### Target Learning Goals
- extending access to tailored interactive learning tools
- comparing custom and professional simulations
