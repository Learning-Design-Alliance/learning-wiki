Develop language intervention programs with longer time frames, transferable strategies, and follow-up assessments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's forward-looking recommendation is that future programs must extend beyond short vocabulary-training cycles. It states that "Programs with longer time frames and follow‐up assessments than what was included in this review must be developed in the future," and that interventions must teach transferable strategies usable when children encounter new words and unfamiliar sentences, not merely the specific words taught. It also calls for more RCT evidence to rule out systematic group differences.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instruction must focus on strategies usable in general language learning, not only trained word meanings

Constraints¶

  • The review notes few included studies reported follow-up assessments, limiting knowledge of lasting effects

Target Learners¶

  • preschool and school-aged children at risk for language and reading difficulties

Target Learning Goals¶

  • generalized linguistic comprehension
  • reading comprehension

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide pedagogical interventions to enhance ethnolinguistic minority students' metacognitive knowledge and English vocabulary knowledge
