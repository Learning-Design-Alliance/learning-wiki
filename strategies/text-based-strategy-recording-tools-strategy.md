# Provide text-based recording and annotation tools matched to how players actually share strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When forum discussion showed players' favoured methods of dissemination were primarily text-based, with gameplay videos rare and devoid of commentary, the game was updated (v4.0) to make it easier to provide good text-based explanations. Save files record all moves and support annotations, giving players a medium for reflecting on and explaining their strategies so scientists can build on them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The scientific problem requires players to reflect upon and explain their strategies, which cannot be replaced with any in-game automated process
#### Constraints
- Any attempt at gathering such information in-game should avoid leaving casual gamers with the impression that the science is demanding and technical

### Target Learners
- citizen scientist players seeking to compare and share methods

### Target Learning Goals
- articulating and disseminating problem-solving strategies
