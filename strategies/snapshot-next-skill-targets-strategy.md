# Use snapshot mastery patterns to identify plausible next learning targets when longitudinal logs are unavailable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that in settings where only snapshot assessments are available, estimating the next likely skill from CDM-derived mastery patterns can guide instruction. It states that estimating the next likely skill "can help identify plausible next learning targets and support assessment-driven instructional decisions even in the absence of longitudinal logs," enabling forward-looking decisions in data-constrained environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Snapshot response data scored with cognitive diagnostic models to yield binary skill mastery patterns
#### Constraints
- Current formulation relies on a non-forgetting assumption and was evaluated on a restricted real-world subset; future work should examine more heterogeneous learning settings

### Target Learners
- Students in large-scale periodic or school-based group assessments without learner-wise temporal logs

### Target Learning Goals
- Selecting next learning targets and planning instruction from assessment data
