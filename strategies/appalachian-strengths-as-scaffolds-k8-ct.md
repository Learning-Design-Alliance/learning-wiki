# Use named community strengths — kinship, hopes and dreams for the future, grit and resistance to injustice — as scaffolds in K-8 CS/CT instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The panel poses a focal discussion question asking how Appalachian strengths like "kinship," "ability to maintain hopes and dreams f or the future," "grit and ability to resist injustice" support in K-8 CS/CT education in KY Appalachia, and conversely how K-8 CS/CT education might further amplify these strengths. This bidirectional question offers a concrete discussion protocol for educators designing culturally grounded computing instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need knowledge of their own community's named strengths and cultural resources to pose and pursue this question

### Target Learners
