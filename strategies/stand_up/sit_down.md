# Stand Up/Sit Down

## Description
The rules are simple: if the teacher gives the number 10, students stand up. Any other number, they sit down. The trick is, the teacher will say things like '7+3' and '14 -5' (pick appropriate sums and differences for your students to solve mentally). This is a great game to try to 'trick' the students by standing up or sitting down on when they should be doing the opposite. There are endless variations, for example: stand when the number is larger than 5; sit if it is 5 or below, stand when the number is even; sit when it is odd, stand if the digit 1 appears on the number; sit otherwise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An open space where students can move freely. Teacher needs to be able to call out math problems.
#### Constraints
- Students may get confused by the 'trick' component. Not suitable for students with mobility issues or difficulty following instructions.
