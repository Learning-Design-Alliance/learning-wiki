# Providing Accessible Educational Tools

## Description
Providing easy access to a variety of educational tools empowers students to take control of their learning. This approach involves making resources readily available in the classroom and teaching students how and when to use each tool. Tools include manipulatives, graphic organizers, audio recordings, magnifying paper, highlighters, choice boards, learning menus, and timers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A variety of educational tools, a well-organized classroom environment, and explicit instruction in how and when to use each tool.
#### Constraints
- Requires an investment in educational tools and a well-organized classroom environment. Students may initially struggle to use the tools effectively.
