# Chladni-figures activity for making resonance visible with a speaker, function generator and metal plate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A laboratory activity in which a speaker connected to a function generator drives a metal plate holding salt; when the sound matches a normal mode frequency of the plate, salt jumps from vibrating zones and accumulates in fixed nodal positions, forming Chladni figures that depend on plate shape, material, thickness and boundary conditions. The article presents this as a very effective activity for understanding resonance and linking physics to biological systems related to sound, with applets helping figure out energy transport.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a speaker connected to a function generator and a metal plate placed over it
#### Constraints
- figures depend on the shape of plates, materials, thickness and boundary conditions

### Target Learners
- secondary scientific high-school students

### Target Learning Goals
- recognizing resonance and normal modes
- connecting wave energy transport to vibrating systems
