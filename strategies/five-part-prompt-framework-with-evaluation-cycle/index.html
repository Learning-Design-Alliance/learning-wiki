Teach prompting as a five-part skill (role, task, audience, constraints, justification) with a structured ask-generate-evaluate cycle¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The briefs prescribe a classroom strategy in which "A strong prompt usually includes:" a role, a task, an audience, constraints, and justification, noting teachers already ask specific and detailed questions in daily practice and AI makes this explicit. Students then follow a simple cycle: ask a question and develop a detailed prompt, AI generates a response, and students evaluate the output by checking accuracy, identifying missing perspectives, comparing sources with lateral reading checks, and revising.
