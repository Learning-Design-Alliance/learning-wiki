Implement continuous virtual professional development programs for faculty¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review's research team proposes "the implementation of a continuous professional development program for faculty, designed to be accessible virtually and on an ongoing basis." The initiative aims to keep faculty members in a constant state of development and skill updating, which the authors argue translates into a high-quality learning experience for students. This recommendation emerges from the review's conclusion about the importance of pedagogical best practices.
