## Description
The authors recommend extending the unconventional-context approach beyond teleportation: teachers should adopt new ways of enthusing students, and a similar lesson plan and worksheet could be formulated for other quantum physics concepts. The article notes students find quantum topics difficult and struggle to relate them to the macro-world, so popular-culture icons can garner attention. The authors envisage "that a similar lesson plan and worksheet could be formulated for other concepts from quantum physics".
