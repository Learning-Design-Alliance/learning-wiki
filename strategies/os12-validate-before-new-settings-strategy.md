# Validate the OS-12 before applying it in new countries or settings, with attention to which micro-skills the local training prioritized

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the C-CG is a generic communication skills teaching strategy, the article suggests the OS-12 may be used in other countries and settings whose training is C-CG-based, but it explicitly recommends re-validation first: "studies are required to investigate whether similar results can be obtained in other countries and when the OS-12 is applied in other settings and countries validation is recommended". Validation should include "careful consideration of which micro-skills have been given priority in the specific training course."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validation study including content validation by a panel familiar with the local communication skills training programme before use in a new setting.
#### Constraints
- The codebook reflects the micro-skills given highest priority at the authors' training course; different courses may prioritize different micro-skills.

### Target Learners
- Health care providers in communication skills training programmes based on the Calgary-Cambridge Guide

### Target Learning Goals
- Valid, reliable assessment of clinical communication skills across settings
