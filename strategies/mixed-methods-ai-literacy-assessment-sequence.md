# Use a mixed-methods assessment sequence: diagnostic inventory, structured verification tasks, and discipline-specific critique or workflow-design assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends combining validated self-report instruments with performance-based and process-focused assessment. "Effective AI literacy assessment requires a mixed-methods approach that combines existing validated instruments with performance-based assessments aligned to the continuum." A practical sequence begins with a low-stakes diagnostic inventory, moves into structured verification tasks, and culminates in discipline-specific critique or workflow-design assignments, with process evidence such as prompt histories and revision notes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Triangulation of self-report, artifact analysis, and process evidence rather than reliance on any single instrument
#### Constraints
- Standard instruments may assume a baseline of engagement, so reflective journaling and pre-course surveys are needed to identify Stage 0–1 behaviors

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing AI literacy stage
- distinguishing genuine understanding from surface performance

### Affordances
- Ai Literacy Continuum Five Stages
