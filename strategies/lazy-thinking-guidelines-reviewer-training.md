# Use enhanced lazy thinking guidelines with positive examples to train junior reviewers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors release enhanced annotation guidelines and positive examples intended for reviewer education: "We will release our dataset and the enhanced guidelines that can be used to train junior reviewers in the community." The guidelines were developed iteratively over three rounds, adding classes and extended descriptions from the EMNLP 2020 guidelines and incorporating positive examples for each lazy thinking class to promote quick learning through worked examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Annotated positive examples for each lazy thinking class; iterative refinement guided by inter-annotator agreement
#### Constraints
- Guidelines are specific to NLP conference reviewing and ARR-style review formats

### Target Learners
- junior reviewers
- PhD students new to peer reviewing

### Target Learning Goals
- writing constructive, justified, guideline-adherent reviews
- avoiding lazy thinking heuristics

### Affordances
- Lazy Thinking Heuristics Taxonomy
