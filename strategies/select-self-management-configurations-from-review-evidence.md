# Select self-management configurations based on review evidence for specific intervention approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's stated purpose includes understanding "the effectiveness of specific, commonly utilized intervention approaches", and the authors state that "Implications for selecting self-management configurations, as well as conducting future research, are discussed." Practitioners should therefore choose among configurations such as prompted self-monitoring, self-monitoring plus reinforcement, or prompted self-monitoring plus graphing using the configuration-specific evidence the review assembles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practitioners need access to configuration-specific effect size evidence, since mean effects vary in heterogeneity across configurations.
#### Constraints
- Selection guidance is grounded in single-case design studies with school-aged students; heterogeneity limits generalization across grade levels, disability status, and settings.

### Target Learners
- School-aged students, including those with disabilities and those in general or special education settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved student classroom behavior (e.g., on-task behavior, reduced disruptive behavior)
