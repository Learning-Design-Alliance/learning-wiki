# Assign trained teaching assistants to large virtual classes to support course management, grading, discussion monitoring and extra help for learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Participants in one case reported that their university assigned them large virtual classes without providing teaching assistants. A participant noted a TA "could have been organising virtual tutorials to assist students needing extra attention." The discussion, drawing on the literature, states TAs could have helped divide large virtual classes into smaller sessions, and assist with course management, grading, monitoring discussions, and identifying learners who need additional help; the literature adds that TAs need training to perform virtual duties diligently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- TAs need to be trained to perform all virtual duties diligently
#### Constraints
- In the studied institutions, no TAs were provided for large virtual classes during COVID

### Target Learners
- university students in large virtual classes

### Target Learning Goals
- timely feedback and additional support for learners in large virtual classes
