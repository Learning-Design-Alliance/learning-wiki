# Emphasize the Value of Learning

## Description
Help learners focus on and value the process of learning rather than solely on grades. Without this emphasis, learners may base their self-perceptions of intelligence and worth on grades received, promoting a fixed mindset. While grades are important, the value of learning should be prioritized to foster a growth mindset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to design learning activities that are engaging and meaningful. Instructors need to communicate the value of learning and provide feedback that focuses on growth and improvement.
#### Constraints
- Learners may still be motivated by grades, even when the value of learning is emphasized. Instructors need to consistently reinforce the importance of learning and provide opportunities for learners to reflect on their growth.

### Target Learners

- All learner levels, particularly those who are grade-oriented or tend to equate grades with intelligence

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Promote a growth mindset, shift focus from grades to learning, increase intrinsic motivation, and improve self-perception of intelligence and worth

### Affordances
- Encourages learners to see learning as valuable in itself, rather than just a means to an end (i.e., a good grade). Helps learners develop a growth mindset by focusing on the process of learning and improvement.

### Personalization
- Instructors can provide different types of learning activities and assessments that cater to individual learner interests and needs. Learners can be given choices in how they demonstrate their learning.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learner engagement in learning activities, monitor their self-perception of intelligence and worth, and track their grades. Compare the effectiveness of emphasizing learning versus emphasizing grades.

## Impact
- Research suggests that emphasizing the value of learning promotes a growth mindset and increases intrinsic motivation, leading to improved self-perception and academic performance. Without this emphasis on learning, students will often base self perceptions of intelligence and worth to grades received, promoting a fixed mindset.
