# Embed creativity development in ID courses through experimentation, open-ended projects, critique, and drills for variable answer development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Experts in the study described concrete teaching practices for fostering creativity in ID education. Hokanson recommends "Breaking the habits of the 'one right answer' and through increasing experimentation", with assignments developing creative answers, drills for rapid and variable answer development, extended critique focused on creativity, and pre- and post-testing with individual results explained. Mishra describes creating experiences allowing learners to engage with ideas through open-ended group projects and performances of understanding.
