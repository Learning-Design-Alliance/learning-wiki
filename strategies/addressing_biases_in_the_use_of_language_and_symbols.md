# Addressing Biases in the Use of Language and Symbols

## Description
Actively working to identify and eliminate biases in language and symbols to promote inclusivity. This involves using neutral language, avoiding stereotypes, and representing diverse perspectives. This approach creates a welcoming and respectful learning environment for all learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Neutral language, diverse perspectives, resources for addressing bias, and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing vigilance and reflection to identify and address biases. Some learners may be resistant to challenging their own biases.

### Affordances

### Personalization
