# Integrate tutoring into the regular school day rather than scheduling it outside instructional time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This recommendation advises districts to embed tutoring within the regular school day when structuring a selected program. The brief instructs districts to "Integrate tutoring into your regular school day," presenting this as a scheduling and structural decision districts control when supporting implementation. It is grounded in the Middle Years Math evaluations of eight tutoring programs funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.
