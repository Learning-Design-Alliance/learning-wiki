# Integrate KSX into MOOC platforms and run randomized trials varying collaboration and competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' design recommendations for MOOC providers are to implement a fully integrated system of MOOC and KSX on a single web platform, since the field study's separate registration and repeated platform switching may have weakened KSX effectiveness. They further recommend future research use a randomized controlled trial varying the degrees of collaboration and competition to measure causal impacts of different gamification mechanisms, and estimate heterogeneous treatment effects using participants' personality traits for more personalized gamification.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single web platform integrating MOOC and KSX registration and activities
#### Constraints
- The current evidence comes from a non-randomized field study with self-selection, so causal impact of specific mechanisms is not yet established

### Target Learners
- Adult MOOC participants in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Student engagement and interaction
- Course completion and exam performance
