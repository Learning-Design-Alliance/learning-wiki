# Use the misconceptions found in graph pre-tests to create multiple choice assessment questions for position, velocity and acceleration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author proposes a concrete reuse of the diagnostic findings: "The misconceptions found in this work may be used t o create multiple choice questions for assessments regarding position, velocity and acceleration for a free fall case." The wrong graph models documented in the pre-test serve as distractor material for such items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A graph-based pre-test documenting the local students' wrong models
#### Constraints
- Findings come from one small class of 14 students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing understanding of position, velocity and acceleration in free fall
