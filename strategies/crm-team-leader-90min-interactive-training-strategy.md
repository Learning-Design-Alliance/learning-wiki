# Deliver a 90-min interactive CRM team leader training combining CRM theory presentation, video exemplars, and continuous reflection with instructor feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CRM-TL training was a slightly modified version of a previously evaluated CPR CRM training, delivered to small groups of eight students. It "consisted of a presentation on the theory of CRM, the integration of CRM into CPR, and three video presentations showing teams performing ALS combined with an oral exercise to assure knowledge transfer"; students were "explicitly invited to continuously reflect, discuss, and challenge topics in order to facilitate active participation and enable concurrent feedback from the teaching staff".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A designated team leader per team
- Teaching staff available for concurrent feedback
- Video materials of teams performing ALS
#### Constraints
- Focus in this modified version was specifically on the team leader's coordination, time management and explicit verbalization methods

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students acting as designated team leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- leadership verbalizations and coordination
- time management during resuscitation
