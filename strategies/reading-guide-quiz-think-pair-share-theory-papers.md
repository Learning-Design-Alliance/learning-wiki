# Reading guides plus pre-class quizzes and think-pair-share discussion of theory papers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each assigned theory paper comes with a customized reading guide whose questions scaffold reading by priming background knowledge and directing attention to the paper's biological insights and the model's purpose. Preparation is enforced with a graded quiz per paper, and class time is spent discussing answers: "We use primarily a think-pair-share format, i.e. students write down their answer attempts, then pair up with a neighbor to compare and discuss their answers, before we finally ask them to share answers with the whole group". Instructors listen in to pairs to detect misconceptions early.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must grapple with the paper and questions before coming to class; quizzes and reading guides are used to ensure this
#### Constraints
- Switching to different papers requires generating a comparable set of reading-guide questions, which the authors describe as relatively low workload

### Target Learners
- graduate students reading theory papers without extensive math skills

### Target Learning Goals
- extracting biological insights from papers
- classifying the purpose of a model
