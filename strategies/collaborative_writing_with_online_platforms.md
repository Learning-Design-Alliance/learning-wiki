# Collaborative Writing with Online Platforms

## Description
Collaborative writing using online platforms involves students jointly composing texts through digital tools like Google Docs. These platforms enable features such as commenting on each other's writing, tracking changes in suggestion mode, and simultaneous co-authoring. Students can work together to write segments answering similar questions and then post the link to their creation in a class blog. This approach leverages technology to enhance collaboration and provides opportunities for peer feedback and revision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to online platforms such as Google Docs, internet connectivity, and familiarity with collaborative writing tools.
#### Constraints
- Requires reliable internet access and digital literacy skills. May be challenging for students with limited technology experience. Relies on constructive peer feedback, which requires training and facilitation.

### Affordances
- Facilitates real-time collaboration, provides clear records of changes and suggestions, allows for asynchronous contributions, and enhances peer interaction. Improves writing quality through constructive feedback and iterative revision.

### Personalization
- Students can choose their partners, select topics of interest, and use the platform's features to adapt the writing process to their needs.
