# Connor et al.'s guidelines for integrating arts-based pedagogies in undergraduate engineering courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on their experience integrating arts-based pedagogics to promote inquiry-guided learning in engineering undergraduate courses, Connor et al. (2015) propose a set of guidelines: embrace different disciplines, design flexible projects for students, tease out creativity, allow and encourage failure, realize students are different, consider vertical orientation (mixing students from different age groups), and explore horizontal blurring (projects and assessments stretching over multiple courses).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Different pedagogical methods such as problem-based, project-based, inquiry-based and discovery learning can coexist in the same instructional model
#### Constraints
- Little adoption of student-centric practices in undergraduate engineering education; chalk-and-talk remains the most utilized methodology

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- creativity
- inquiry skills
- interdisciplinary problem solving
