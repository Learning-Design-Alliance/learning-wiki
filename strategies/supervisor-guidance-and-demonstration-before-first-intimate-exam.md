# Give clinical supervisors more detailed guidance on their facilitation role and provide a demonstration before students' first intimate examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the evaluation findings, the authors recommend strengthening the tutor role and adding demonstration. They state that "Suggestions for improvement included giving more detailed instruction to clinical supervisors about their role in the examination process and having a demonstration of the exam on male and female patients prior to students undertaking their first examination". Clinical tutors have since been given more directed guidance on their role in the session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
