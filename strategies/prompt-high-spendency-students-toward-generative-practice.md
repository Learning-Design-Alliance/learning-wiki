# Recognize high-spendency behavior and prompt students toward less spending and more generative practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
As future work, the authors propose using spendency as an actionable signal in the student model: "if a student is engaging in a high spendency behaviors, it may be beneficial for iSTART-ME to have the capability to recognize this trend and prompt the student toward less spending and more generative practice." This strategy turns logged currency behavior into a trigger for adaptive steering toward more effective learning behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The system must log currency spending within its student model and be able to recognize high-spendency behavior patterns as they emerge.
