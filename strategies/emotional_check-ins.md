# Emotional Check-ins

## Description
Beginning the day by asking students how they are feeling helps to identify potential emotional triggers. Writing the question on the board and having students answer on sticky notes allows educators to understand which students may be having a difficult day and why, creating a supportive classroom environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sticky notes; whiteboard; facilitator to guide discussions
#### Constraints
- Learners may be shy or uncomfortable expressing emotions; discussions may be superficial if not facilitated effectively
