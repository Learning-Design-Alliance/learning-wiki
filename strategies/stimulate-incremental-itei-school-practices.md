# Stimulate flexible (incremental) perspectives on emotional abilities through school interventions and daily practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools address and stimulate more flexible perspectives among students regarding school-related attributes. It states there is "growing evidence for the need to address and stimulate more flexible perspectives among students" through interventions, curricula, informal education, and daily school culture. Concrete practices listed include valuing effort and improvement instead of academic marks, reinforcing that difficulties are necessary for improvement, giving feedback on improvements, proposing challenging tasks adapted to student profiles, teaching how the brain changes through practice, and introducing information about incremental and entity beliefs.
