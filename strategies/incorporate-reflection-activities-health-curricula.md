# Incorporate reflection-promoting activities into health professions curricula at all educational levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review documents that educators incorporate activities to promote reflection into undergraduate, postgraduate and continuing medical education and across a variety of health professions, as part of a change acknowledging the need for students to act and think professionally throughout their courses. The strategy positions reflection as integral to learning rather than an add-on. The review cautions that evidence for such interventions remains largely theoretical and that it is unclear which approaches may have efficacy or impact.
