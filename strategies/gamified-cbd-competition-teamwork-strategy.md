# Gamified case-based discussion with peer-to-peer competition and teamwork as an active-methodology comparator

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CBD condition used small-group interactive discussion of the same clinical scenarios as the simulation arm, in which students "were challenged to answer on a theoretical base how to conduct anamnesis, diagnostic, and therapeutic procedures in emergencies". Each topic had outlined and structured objectives, and "A gamified strategy (pedagogical methodology based on games), with elements of peer-to-peer competition and teamwork was used to motivate the students through healthy competition". Adequate and inadequate responses were discussed; each topic session lasted about 60 minutes, seven topics per student (7 h total), matching the simulation arm's contact time.
