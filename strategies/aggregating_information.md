# Aggregating Information

## Description
Aggregating involves finding and displaying patterns in discussions and other communications. This can help students see connections between ideas, identify common themes, and develop a shared understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a platform for communication (e.g., discussion forum) and strategies for identifying and displaying patterns.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming and require active instructor moderation. Can be challenging to ensure equitable participation.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those who benefit from collaborative learning and synthesis of information

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Identify patterns, connect ideas, develop shared understanding

### Affordances
- Promotes collaborative learning, enhances understanding of complex topics, and helps students see connections between ideas.

### Personalization
- Can be personalized by allowing students to contribute to the aggregation process, providing varied formats, and offering different perspectives.
