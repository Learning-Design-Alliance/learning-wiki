# Analyze pre-existing network structures and points of leverage at the outset of a cross-sector reform initiative

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among the report's considerations for the future, it recommends that "Future efforts like this might benefit from deliberate analysis of the pre-existing networks, the missing links, and the best strategies for disseminating information, gathering feedback, and collaborating with stakeholders to work toward transformation." It further recommends sharing states' experiences by establishing an online resources library including definitions of college and career readiness, examples of course alignment, and lessons learned from collaborative work groups, and stresses that validity studies of CCSS-aligned assessments may be the most pressing issue for ADs in the final grant year.
