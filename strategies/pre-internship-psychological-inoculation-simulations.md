# Pre-internship psychological inoculation through situational simulations and case studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Because self-efficacy was the dominant mediator, the article recommends that curriculum design "should incorporate situational simulations and case studies before the internship begins", strengthening students' "psychological immunity" (efficacy) so they can better interpret and manage the inevitable "reality shock" of kindergarten work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum time before internship for simulations and case-based preparation
#### Constraints
- Recommendation based on the finding that self-efficacy mediates the fit–satisfaction relationship; not experimentally tested in this study
