# Pre-recording Instructional Videos

## Description
Pre-recording instructional videos in advance is a versatile teaching strategy. Instructors can deliver course content in video format, which can be accessed and rewatched freely by students. It frees up synchronous class time for more interactive learning activities, and instructors can reuse and re-edit recorded videos. Types of videos may include a welcome video, weekly video messages, supplemental instruction, and lecture recordings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires video recording equipment (camera, microphone), editing software, and a platform for hosting and sharing videos (e.g., Canvas, YouTube).
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming to produce high-quality videos. Requires technical skills for recording and editing. May not be suitable for all types of content or instructors.

### Affordances
- Provides learners with the ability to review content at their own pace. Increases accessibility for learners with different learning styles and needs. Allows for efficient use of synchronous class time for more engaging activities.

### Personalization
- Instructors can create videos addressing specific learner needs or challenging concepts. Videos can be tailored to different levels of understanding and learning preferences.
