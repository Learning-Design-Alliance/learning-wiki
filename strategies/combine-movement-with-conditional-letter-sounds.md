# Combine movement specifically with conditional letter-sounds for children who already master standard sounds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the intervention effect was concentrated on conditional letter-sounds, the authors recommend that "teachers specifically work in the combination of movement and conditional sounds, as we observe the greatest effect on that specific parameter." The strategy targets children who already master standard sounds and pairs each conditional pronunciation with a whole-body movement-phoneme during systematic phonics instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Children should already have mastered standard letter-sounds before focusing on conditional sounds with movement
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a 4-week intervention with a 1:2 teacher-student fraction; effects on whole word reading were not significant

### Target Learners
- First-grade children in early literacy instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- Conditional letter-sound knowledge and spelling performance
