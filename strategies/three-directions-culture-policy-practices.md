## Description
The article recommends that teachers ensure partial organization of inclusion at school in three directions: "inclusive culture; inclusive policy; inclusive practices". Inclusive culture means forming a trusting environment and establishing values of inclusion; inclusive policy means organizing support for diversity through numerous teaching methods; inclusive practices mean organizing training and mobilizing resources to create a favorable place for study and recreation, including technical equipment and acceptable class space.
