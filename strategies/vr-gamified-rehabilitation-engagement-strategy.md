# Gamify prescribed rehabilitation movements in VR to sustain patient engagement and adherence

## Description
For rehabilitation, the article recommends embedding repetitive physical-therapy exercises in gamified VR experiences to counter monotony and demotivation. The authors write: "By integrating gamified experiences — such as using a virtual sword to fight a dragon while performing prescribed movements — patients can maintain high engagement levels, improving adherence and therapeutic outcomes."
