# Assign optical correlation mini projects using Vander Lugt and joint-transform correlation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends giving students mini projects on optical correlation, in which they record a joint Fourier transform of object and target transparencies on a photographic plate or digital camera, then replay it to obtain cross-correlation peaks at matched-target locations. The author states "The students can be given mini projects on optical correlation" and that Vander Lugt and joint-transform correlation "can be explained using both simulation and experiments". Projects can be carried out in GNU Octave or with the described optical setups.
