## Description
The article describes design fiction as a pedagogical method in Human-Computer Interaction courses: students create fictional designs and scenarios that make them reflect on the implications of technology. The author argues this supports critical thinking about technology's role in society. The article reports on the use of this method in an HCI course setting.
