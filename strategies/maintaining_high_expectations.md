# Maintaining High Expectations

## Description
In culturally responsive instruction, achievement expectations are determined individually. Students receive praise in proportion to their accomplishments, not lavish praise for simple tasks. When a student struggles, the instructor helps guide the student positively toward explaining how to complete the initial steps, fostering self-efficacy and problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individualized assessment, clear communication, supportive guidance.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to understand individual student needs, may be challenging to manage in large classes.
