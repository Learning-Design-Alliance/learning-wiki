# Rich Library: Multimodal Resources

## Description
Providing learners with access to multiple resources, including blogs, videos, audiobooks, and digital books, exposes them to diverse genres and can increase interest and motivation. Choice within a rich library can deepen background knowledge, which supports learning in other domains. Digital libraries can facilitate exploration of various topics through multimodal resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a digital library or collection of multimodal resources. Curated selection of resources aligned with learning objectives.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful curation to ensure resources are high-quality and aligned with learning goals. Learners may need guidance to navigate the library effectively.

- Track resource selections to gauge learner interests. Assess learner comprehension and knowledge gains related to the chosen resources. Monitor engagement levels.

- Increased motivation and engagement through learner choice. Deeper background knowledge improves learning outcomes and transfer of knowledge. Exposure to diverse genres and formats prepares learners for 21st-century literacy.
