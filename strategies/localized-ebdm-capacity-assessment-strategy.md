# Use localized EBDM capacity survey findings to target workforce training and incentive policies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that health departments and NGOs administer the survey tool to assess the unique EBDM capacity of their own workforce and use the localized findings to choose action points. It states localized findings "can provide direction for focusing workforce training programs and can indicate the types of incentives and policies that could affect the culture of EBDM in the workplace". Action points can include training programs focused on specific EBDM skills or incentives and policies affecting organizational culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A survey tailored to the agency's workforce and community partners, delivered to state- and local-level practitioners and community partners
#### Constraints
- The article notes agencies with limited funds will not be able to offer gift card incentives to increase response rates

### Target Learners
- public health agency workforces
- non-governmental organization staff

### Target Learning Goals
- strengthening EBDM competencies such as communicating with policymakers, economic evaluation, and translating research to practice
