# Develop, expand and test e-learning quality standards that integrate teaching and learning of clinical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future work create and validate evaluation standards covering the clinical component of undergraduate e-learning programmes in the health professions. It states: "Further research in this field should focus specifically on developing, expanding, and testing standards for evaluating the quality of e-learning that integrate teaching and learning of clinical skills." Such standards should let evaluators access clinical teaching sites, the educators within them, and the nature and quality of clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standards must allow evaluators to access clinical teaching sites and assess the educators and clinical practice within them
#### Constraints
- Motivated by the demonstrated absence of clinical-skills standards in all eight included LMIC articles

### Target Learners
- undergraduate health professions students in LMICs

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competence development and quality assurance of e-learning programmes
