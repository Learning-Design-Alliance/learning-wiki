# Acoustics and Noise Management

## Description
Managing classroom acoustics and noise levels greatly impacts teacher and student performance. Excessive noise can cause dissatisfaction and stress, reducing engagement and achievement. Building schools or modifying classrooms to buffer external noise improves student outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Acoustic materials, quiet spaces, noise-canceling headphones, or facility modifications.
#### Constraints
- Implementation can be costly and may require significant changes to the learning environment.
