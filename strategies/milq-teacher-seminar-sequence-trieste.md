# Teacher in-service seminars built around the four reasoning tools, sequenced by participant requests

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To spread the milq approach beyond the German-speaking world, the materials were used for physics teacher in-service training at the University of Trieste amid Italian curricular reform adding modern physics. "The topics of the seminars were chosen based on the requests of the participants after they were introduced to the four quantum reasoning rules", yielding a sequence from the reasoning tools through the photoelectric effect, single photons in a Mach-Zehnder interferometer, and Planck's constant measurement with LEDs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers first introduced to the four quantum reasoning rules before choosing further seminar topics
#### Constraints
- There was no systematic evaluation; the article reports only that teacher feedback was positive and that the activity will be continued and published in the near future

### Target Learners
- In-service secondary physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparing teachers to teach modern physics / quantum physics with the reasoning tools
