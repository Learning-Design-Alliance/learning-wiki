# Provide teachers a full day of training before starting ELM and a second full day before the second half of the program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because ELM is an explicit scripted core math program, the review recommends staged professional development: "teachers receive a minimum of a full day of training prior to starting the program with an additional full day of training prior to implementing the second half of the program." Experienced trainers can be found on the CTL Marketplace website. This two-day structure matches the program's two halves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to experienced trainers, which the review says can be found on the CTL Marketplace website.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is tied to the nature of an explicit scripted core math program; the review reports no study testing the training itself.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten teachers implementing ELM

### Target Learning Goals
- faithful implementation of the scripted ELM mathematics program
