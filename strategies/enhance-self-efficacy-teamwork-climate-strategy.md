# Enhance teachers' creative teaching self-efficacy and build a teamwork-oriented creative climate to improve creative teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health care schools seeking to improve teachers' creative teaching behaviors should target creative teaching self-efficacy and a teamwork-oriented school creative climate. The authors state that "health care schools should encourage a culture of teamwork and mutual support as part of the school's creative climate in order to enhance creative teaching behaviors" and conclude that enhancing creative teaching self-efficacy and teamwork-encouraging climates should be key considerations for implementing creative education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must be able to measure and support teacher self-efficacy and teamwork climate, e.g. using the scales in this study, which "could be used to assess the success of such changes"
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a cross-sectional 53-teacher Taiwanese sample; the authors state this limits generalizability outside Taiwan

### Target Learners
- Health care school teachers in Taiwan vocational and technical schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing teachers' creative teaching behaviors to foster student creativity in health care education
