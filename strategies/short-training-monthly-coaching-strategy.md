# Pair short group trainings with monthly onsite coaching visits to change instructional practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Across the country programs, the authors report that teacher support ranged from central group trainings to school-based coaching, and conclude that "short (1‐ or 2‐day) group trainings followed by onsite monthly coaching and reinforcement have proven most effective in producing improved instructional practice and gains in learning outcomes". Scaffolded lesson plans and student books accompany the coaching so teachers can enact the approach in daily lessons. The mix of supports should be locally contextualized to each country.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scaffolded lesson plans and student books must accompany training and coaching
- Coaches must be trained to observe, give feedback, and model lessons for teachers
#### Constraints
- The article states the best mix of ongoing professional development should be locally contextualized to each country

### Target Learners
- Early grade primary teachers in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved early grade reading instruction and student reading outcomes
