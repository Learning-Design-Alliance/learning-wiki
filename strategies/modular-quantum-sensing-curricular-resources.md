# Develop modular quantum sensing curricular resources that integrate into existing undergraduate quantum-related courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Rather than creating standalone quantum sensing courses or textbooks, the authors propose building modular resources that slot into existing quantum mechanics, modern physics, and quantum computing courses. The strategy starts with characterizing current textbook and course content to find natural entry points and students' likely prior knowledge. As stated, "The long-term goal of our project is to develop modular curricular resources that can be incorporated into existing undergraduate level quantum-related courses." Identifying which disciplines already offer sensing-related courses informs where modules could be meaningfully integrated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of existing curricular content and students' prior knowledge to identify entry points
#### Constraints
- No established conceptual frameworks or standard textbooks exist for quantum sensing, and its applications span multiple fields requiring interdisciplinary background

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students
- QISE students in adjacent disciplines

### Target Learning Goals
- quantum sensing concepts and applications
- interdisciplinary quantum technology background
