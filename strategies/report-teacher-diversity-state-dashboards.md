# Make educator diversity data visible and actionable by reporting teacher diversity on state dashboards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The first policy lever identified by more than 100 educators of color at the convening is to "Report teacher diversity on state dashboards" and urge state departments of education to collect and report such data, improving visibility of the lack of educator diversity. Delaware's educator mobility data dashboard, disaggregating retention, transfer, and turnover rates for full-time staff by race and ethnicity, is offered as a promising practice.
