# Design MALL tasks to be user friendly, socially and culturally sensitive, engaging, and short

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article endorses design guidelines for mobile-assisted language learning tasks drawn from the mobile-learning literature: "The tasks should be user friendly, sensitive to the social and cultural setting and engaging and short." Tasks built around these guidelines are said to have a lot of potential for learning. The strategy directs designers of mobile language-learning activities to attend to usability, sociocultural fit, engagement, and brevity.
