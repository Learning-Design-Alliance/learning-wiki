# Use Entrustable Professional Activities (EPAs) to standardise assessment of trainee independence in clinical skills

## Description
The article notes that assessing confidence and competence of trainees is challenging, giving the example that a 1st-year trainee may feel confident in intubation knowing a consultant is available while a 3rd-year trainee may feel less confident despite being more competent. It states "This is where the development and concept of EPAs can provide standardisation of assessing trainee level of independence in such skills."
