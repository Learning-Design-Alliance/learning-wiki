# Improve answer quality in questioning-while-reading activities through practice training with example answers and feedback on answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose two methods to raise the quality of students' answers to embedded questions and elaborative interrogations, since poor answer quality plausibly undermined questioning's benefits. The first is "practice training in which students receive examples of what types of answers are expected of them", providing a model; the second is feedback on answers, whether in the experiment, from peers working collaboratively after training, or through computer-based tutoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
