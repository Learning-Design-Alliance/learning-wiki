# Adopt the guided-exploration WebOPAC self-training tool in library orientation programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that librarians replace or supplement conventional orientation methods (tours, briefings, lectures) with the WSTTG guided-exploration self-training tool for teaching WebOPAC. The authors state that "the findings should alert librarians on the need to adopt this effective learning strategy for library orientation program and to be used by both the librarians and library users for WebOPAC teaching and learning purposes." The recommendation rests on the study's finding that WSTTG outperformed both the non-guided tool and traditional instruction at both logical thinking levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A WebOPAC web-based self-training tool with an accompanying guided-exploration manual
- Facilitators trained on the instructional methods; the study used librarians with more than seven years of orientation experience after one day of training
#### Constraints
- The study covered only three UiTM campuses, so "the results obtained are narrowed in terms of generalizability."

### Target Learners
- first year degree students attending library orientation

### Target Learning Goals
- information literacy skills for WebOPAC use
