# Adopt flexible, competency-based attendance policies and flipped classrooms for senior medical students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Colleges should also consider more flexible, competency-based attendance policies for senior students who balance heavy clinical responsibilities." It further suggests that "Approaches like flipped classrooms—where students learn theory online and use class time for case discussions—can better integrate digital learning while maintaining the value of in-person teaching." This strategy responds to findings that attendance perceptions decline sharply in senior years and that mandatory attendance is viewed negatively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online theory materials must be available so class time can be used for case discussions
#### Constraints
- The article notes strict lecture attendance can feel like a poor use of limited study time for senior students

### Target Learners
- senior undergraduate medical students in clinical years

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- integration of digital and in-person learning
