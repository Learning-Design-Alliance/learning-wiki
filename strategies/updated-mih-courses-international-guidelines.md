# Develop updated MIH courses for dentists aligned with international guidelines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that continuing education close identified knowledge gaps in MIH care. It states that "There is a need to develop updated courses that address the aetiology, diagnosis, and management of MIH lesions, based on international guidelines for the clinical management of patients with MIH." The recommendation rests on the survey findings that 77.8% of dentists wanted further training and that age-related biases and limited awareness of newer techniques persist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses must address aetiology, diagnosis and management of MIH lesions and be based on international guidelines, per the article's recommendation.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the predominant use of composites and extractions may reflect age-related bias and unfamiliarity with minimally invasive techniques, which updated courses must overcome.

### Target Learners
- practising dentists
- general dentists
- dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnose MIH
- select appropriate restorative treatments for MIH
- recognise when to refer MIH patients to paediatric specialists
