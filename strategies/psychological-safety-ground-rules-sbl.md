# Establish psychological safety through shared ground rules, confidentiality, respect, and a mistakes-as-learning philosophy during simulation-based learning

## Description
The article describes how facilitators established shared ground rules during pre-briefing including "confidentiality, respect, and a 'mistakes-as-learning' philosophy" to promote psychological safety. Facilitators monitored students' emotional responses and adjusted guidance accordingly, and provided emotional support when students exhibited distress or discomfort.
