# Guided reflection sessions built on video-recorded teaching with smart-ring arousal episodes (critical incident technique and stimulated recall)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In each guided reflection session, the teacher reflects on their previously video-recorded teaching situation together with a researcher. During the recorded session the teacher wears a Moodmetric smart ring measuring arousal level, and "episodes with different arousal levels (high, low, and changing) are presented to the participants" in the sessions, following the critical incident technique and stimulated recall interview method. After watching each episode, teachers reflect on what happened, what could have influenced their arousal level, and their associated feelings. The sessions are conducted via Zoom at the start and end of the intervention.
