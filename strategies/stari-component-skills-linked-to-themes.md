# Teach decoding and morphological analysis with words connected to unit themes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
STARI connects basic-skills instruction to cognitively challenging content rather than teaching skills in isolation. The program "directly links work on component skills—decoding, fluency, and morphological analysis—with cognitively challenging unit themes," and to promote interest and engagement, decoding and morphological analysis strategies are taught with words connected to STARI unit themes. Units are chosen to be interesting and of relevance and importance in young adolescents' lives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thematic units selected for relevance and importance in young adolescents' lives
#### Constraints
- Part of a supplemental intervention for struggling middle school readers

### Target Learners
- middle school students who struggle with reading

### Target Learning Goals
- decoding
- morphological analysis
- engagement with challenging texts
