# Recast and animate concept-inventory-style assessment questions to match the look and feel of the programming exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors are developing assessments for each exercise, delivered through a learning management system with animated questions shortly before and after each exercise. They argue "it is important to recast these questions to have a similar look and feel as the exercises in order to avoid situations where students may be confused by the change in the style of the axes", citing work finding an animated Force Concept Inventory more accurately gauged conceptual knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessments are incorporated into a learning management system and presented as animated questions administered shortly before and after each exercise
#### Constraints
- The BEMA contains static questions, so recasting and animating them is presented as planned work, not a completed evaluation

### Target Learners
- high school or early undergraduate introductory electromagnetism students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of particle repulsion, deflection of particles in electric and magnetic fields, and circuits
