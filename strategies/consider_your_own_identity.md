# Consider Your Own Identity

## Description
Educators need to reflect on their own biases and privileges to effectively lead discussions about race. Tools like Glen Singleton and Curtis Linton's 'Courageous Conversations About Race' and Peggy McIntosh's work on white privilege can help. The Intercultural Development Inventory can assess cultural acceptance and understanding. Constructing and sharing a racial autobiography can provide insights into one's own identity. This self-reflection ensures educators are aware of their own assumptions, privileges, and biases, which is essential for fostering an inclusive and equitable learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A commitment to self-reflection. Access to tools and resources. Time for personal reflection and discussion.
#### Constraints
- Some educators may find it challenging to confront their own biases. Requires ongoing effort and self-assessment. May require additional support or training.
