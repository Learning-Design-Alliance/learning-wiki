# Provide in-service physicians compulsory hands-on training in health promotion through continuing medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For physicians already in service, the article recommends that "In-service physicians should be provided with compulsory hands-on training through specialized health promotion as part of their Continuing medical education." This targets the documented gap between ideal and current practice among practising physicians, who in the study rated their current practice far below ideal on all sixteen elements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuing medical education structures that can host specialized, hands-on health promotion training
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from a small purposive sample and is offered as a pilot exercise

### Target Learners
- in-service primary care physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- delivery of health promotion services such as counseling and health education
