# Administer a pre-test perception survey immediately after the tutorial exercise so instructors can identify and correct exercise problems before assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that "the student’s pre-test feedback allowed the instructor to identify and make the necessary corrections in the exercises to improve the quality exercises". The concrete sequence is: instructor demonstration in week one, tutorial exercise in week two, pre-test survey immediately after, then formative assessment with post-test survey in week three.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Surveys conducted before the summative assessment period
#### Constraints
- The University policy prohibited surveys immediately before or after the summative assessment

### Target Learners
- Pharm.D. students in IPPE2 and APPE courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving quality of virtual simulation exercises

## Claims

- [In APPE, all exercise- and technical-domain items and three items each in the instructor and communication domains had significantly higher perception in the pre-test than post-test](../claims/appe-pretest-higher-perception-broad-domains.md) [+W]
- [In IPPE2, pre-test satisfaction was significantly higher for all five technical-domain items, while exercise, instructor and communication domains showed no significant pre-post differences](../claims/ippe2-pretest-higher-technical-satisfaction.md) [+W]
