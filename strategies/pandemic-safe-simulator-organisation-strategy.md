# Organise physical simulators for pandemic-safe use by spacing them apart, allowing only sequential use, and cleaning between users

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable arrangement for continuing simulator training under social-distancing requirements: place simulators further apart, allow only sequential use, and clean simulators between uses. The article also notes online and remote training allows training to continue off site. Manufacturers are said to need to provide adequate disinfection instructions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- COVID-19-proof disinfection before and after use, with adequate instructions given by the manufacturers
#### Constraints
- Trainees might need to travel to institutes where they can train, and such displacements might be difficult in COVID-19 times

### Target Learners
- obstetrics and gynaecology postgraduate trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- continued clinical skills training under social distancing restrictions
