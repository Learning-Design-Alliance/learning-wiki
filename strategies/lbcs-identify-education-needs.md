# Use the LBCS to identify needs for education concerning breastfeeding beyond 12 months

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying the LBCS as a diagnostic tool: by measuring knowledge, skills, and attitudes regarding breastfeeding beyond 12 months, it can reveal where public health nurses' competence is lacking so education can be targeted. The authors state that "The LBCS can be used to identify the need for education concerning breastfeeding beyond 12 months" and that identifying knowledge gaps makes it possible to target breastfeeding education according to needs. They also suggest the instrument may be used with other professional groups and breastfeeding supporters, such as volunteer workers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validity must be reassessed if the LBCS is used outside the Finnish healthcare context
#### Constraints
- Results regarding competence are preliminary pilot findings and reliability was assessed only from the point of view of internal consistency

### Target Learners
- Public health nurses
- Other health professional groups
- Volunteer breastfeeding supporters

### Target Learning Goals
- Competence in counseling and supporting breastfeeding beyond 12 months
