# Family literacy education and parallel school-home NIE programs to foster children's newspaper reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that families and schools jointly support children's newspaper reading: parents provide access to newspapers and interactional opportunities, while schools run Newspaper in Education programs in parallel with home reading. It states that "NIE programs in schools should also be utilized in parallel to newspaper reading in homes to maximize educational benefits" and that "family literacy education is the most ideal learning intervention for developing children," citing a meta-analysis of parental involvement interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parents need physical access to newspapers and opportunities for interactional activities such as discussion, play, online mediation, and scrap activity.
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a convenience sample of South Korean parents, and academic achievement was measured by parental self-report, so caution is required when interpreting.

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades 1–6

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading motivation and literacy development
- Academic achievement across school subjects
