# Future lab studies should analyze student positioning and body language to reveal how roles are assigned or assumed

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, in addition to collecting more data, "future work should also explore how students navigate into these roles", noting that previous work found most task allocations were not overt. They suggest that "analysis of stu- dent positioning and body language in the labs may shed light on how roles are being assigned or assumed", making observational methods that attend to positioning and body language a concrete next step for studying role emergence in lab groups.
