# Strengthen BEES training with integrated STEM courses, workshops, and technology-pedagogy integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion section sets out training recommendations for Moroccan pre-service elementary teacher education. It calls for "theoretical courses on the content and pedagogical strategies of integrated STEM education, as well as practical workshops", more STEM courses for literary-baccalaureate students, preparation for challenges elementary students may face with mobile devices, and training on the interdependence of content, pedagogical, and technological knowledge. It also recommends that mobile learning training need not be differentiated by gender or pre-university background.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programs must take into account recipients' beliefs and attitudes toward integrated STEM education and mobile device use, as the article states.
#### Constraints
- The study is based on a convenience sample limited to two Moroccan universities with self-reported online questionnaire data, which the authors state limits generalizability.

### Target Learners
- Pre-service elementary teachers (BEES students) in Morocco

### Target Learning Goals
- Implementing integrated STEM education in elementary classrooms
- Using mobile devices effectively in integrated STEM teaching
