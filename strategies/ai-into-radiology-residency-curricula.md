# Integrate AI assistance into structured radiology residency curricula to enhance diagnostic proficiency and accelerate skill acquisition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend incorporating AI into structured residency curricula, noting it "offers an opportunity to enhance diagnostic proficiency, accelerate skill acquisition, and foster confidence in early-career clinicians." In their reader study, residents' sensitivity and accuracy improved significantly with AI assistance, and AI features such as heatmaps and triage capabilities are described as potentially providing training support and continuous mentorship when consultant expertise is unavailable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents must be trained to recognize and address automation bias and to interpret unusual AI outputs, per the introduction
- Rigorous testing of thresholds and continuous evaluation with feedback loops, per the discussion
#### Constraints
- The article states careful attention to threshold calibration, feedback loops, and real-world validation remains essential, and further studies in routine screening settings are needed to confirm generalizability

### Target Learners
- radiology residents (junior Years 3-4 and senior final-year residents)

### Target Learning Goals
- mammographic breast cancer detection and interpretation
- working with AI as a non-interpretive clinical skill
