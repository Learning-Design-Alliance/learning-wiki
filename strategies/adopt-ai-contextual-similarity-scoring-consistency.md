# Adopt AI-based contextual similarity scoring for mathematical essay items to ensure consistency and save cost and time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teachers, evaluators and examination bodies use AI software such as AEEGS to score mathematical computation essay items, because human scoring of high volumes is subjective, time-consuming and costly. It states that "teachers, evaluators, and various examination bodies can use this instrument to provide consistent scoring for mathematics essays and tests", eliminating inconsistencies and biases and enabling prompt release of results, with saved time redirected to other teaching and learning tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examination bodies or institutions supply items and marking schemes; students answer extended essay items on computers
#### Constraints
- The article notes students' scores were low partly because they were unaccustomed to answering extended responses on computers

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary school students sitting certificate examinations with mathematical essay components

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent, timely scoring of extended mathematical essay responses in Economics
