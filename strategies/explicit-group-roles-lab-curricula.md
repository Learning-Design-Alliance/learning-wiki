# Assign and make deliberate explicit group roles such as group PI, reviewer, or science communicator in lab curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes building explicit group roles into lab curricula, similar to cooperative grouping, with roles such as "group PI", "reviewer" or "science communicator" that have students actively think about how roles are assigned and make deliberate choices regarding role division. This strategy is proposed to alleviate unintended consequences of subconsciously acting on implicit biases, and the article states it is the focus of further research rather than a tested intervention.
