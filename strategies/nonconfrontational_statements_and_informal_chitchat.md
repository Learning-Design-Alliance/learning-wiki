# Nonconfrontational Statements and Informal Chitchat

## Description
Catching students at the door before they enter the classroom and using “nonconfrontational statements” and “informal chitchat” to socialize them into productive behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to be present at the door and actively engage with students. Training on effective communication and behavior management techniques is beneficial.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming and require a significant commitment from the teacher. Some students may resist or be uncomfortable with this approach.

### Personalization
- Teachers can tailor their conversations to individual student needs and interests. Focus on building relationships and addressing specific concerns.
