# Implement the "Among" model through agreed rules, exemplary behavior, guidance, personal visits, and appreciation rather than punishment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy is the article's own five-step recipe for enforcing teacher work discipline with the "Among" leadership model: "a) formulating rules; b) discussion of joint regulations; c) agreement on collective rules; d) exemplary, guidance and motivation, personal visits; e) appreciation and reinforcement." It works by involving staff in drafting rules so they feel ownership, having the principal model the desired behavior, and replacing punishment with dialogue. The article states enforcement "is not based on punishment and threats, because it encourages more negativity, rejection, and rebellion."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A principal willing to arrive early, model the behavior, and conduct home visits and personal dialogue with non-compliant teachers
- Joint staff discussion and collective agreement on the discipline rules before enforcement
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single-school action research study at SDN 11 Simpang Rimba; the authors call for further research to enrich practical steps in other schools

### Target Learners
- school principals
- school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher work discipline in punctuality, working hours, and lesson plan preparation

### Affordances
- Among Leadership Model
