# Comprehensive standardized training with scheduled refresher courses and attitude education for post-rape care providers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends expanding the standardized curriculum into a national training programme with scheduled refresher courses, ensuring training is comprehensive, and educating providers on rape and its seriousness because knowledge "seems to be critically dependant on attitude". It also calls for continuous training and development during the in-service period, since providers who worked more than 15 years in the same facility had low knowledge and risk becoming complacent without updates from current literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be comprehensive and providers who are trained must remain knowledgeable and skilled in current best practices
#### Constraints
- The article notes the knowledge and confidence measures were used for the first time in this study and still need further assessment for reliability

### Target Learners
- Doctors and nurses providing post-rape care in South African public primary health care
- Experienced in-service providers at current facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Current best-practice knowledge in post-rape care
- More appropriate attitudes towards rape
