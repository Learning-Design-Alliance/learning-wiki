# Emotion Word Inclusion in Instruction

## Description
Educators frequently use emotion words when talking to learners, helping the learners understand how the educator is responding to their emotions and supporting the development of their emotional vocabulary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator who uses accurate emotional vocabulary.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be respectful of musical tastes. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance emotional vocabulary, improve emotional awareness, promote positive associations with emotional expression

### Affordances
- Connects language with emotions. Helps to develop vocabulary around emotions and demonstrate how to label them.

### Personalization
- The facilitator can use more complex vocabulary as the learners vocabulary increases.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' increased use of emotion words and more accurate association of words with the related emotions. Assess their participation and engagement in the activity.

## Impact
- Studies show that consistent emotion labeling can improve emotional regulation and social competence. (Source: The Benefits of Emotional Labeling - https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/article/item/the_benefits_of_emotional_labeling)
