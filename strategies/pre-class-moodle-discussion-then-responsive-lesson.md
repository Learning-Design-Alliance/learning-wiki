# Use pre-class online discussion on Moodle followed by in-class instruction responsive to the discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The implementation had students discuss the upcoming lesson topic on Moodle for about a week before class, then the instructor taught the lesson "according to the mistakes and missing information obtained after argumentation", finishing when the class summarised the discussion question. This recipe ran four hours a week for 12 weeks in a material development course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need continuous internet connection to follow the discussion
- Moodle registrations and orientation on using the platform before the discussion weeks
#### Constraints
- Students without internet access cannot participate and are left out of the process; the school setting must be supportive and answer students' needs

### Target Learners
- third-year science teacher candidates

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking disposition
- argumentation
- material development
