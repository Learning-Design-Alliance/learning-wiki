# Novelty Attention-Getters

## Description
Novelty attention-getters use unique and unexpected stimuli to capture young students' attention and quiet the classroom. Examples include using wind chimes, rain sticks, imaginary actions like "popping a marshmallow," or creating a "quiet spray" with a Windex bottle filled with lavender mineral oil. The use of unusual or imaginative tools can interrupt disruptive behavior and regain focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of novelty items, clear explanation of expectations, consistent implementation.
#### Constraints
- May lose effectiveness if overused, some students may be overstimulated by novelty items, requires creativity and adaptability.
