# Train learners in sycophancy-specific critical prompting strategies that remove personal assumptions, request critical evaluation, and ask for supporting evidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The experimental intervention delivered video training fostering metacognitive monitoring of AI agreement and user bias, along with five sycophancy-specific critical prompting strategies. The article states these strategies "emphasized removing personal assumptions from prompts, explicitly asking for critical evaluation, and requesting supporting evidence". All participants first watched a video introducing general AI literacy and raising awareness of sycophantic tendencies in LLMs; the experimental group then received this deeper condition-specific training instead of five domain-general prompting guidelines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery alongside general AI literacy training raising awareness of sycophantic tendencies, as in the study's two-video intervention sequence
#### Constraints
- The intervention did not eliminate the propagation of contextual errors; it reduced direct mirroring of incorrect rankings but general error propagation was unchanged, so the authors argue system-level approaches are also needed

### Target Learners
- Adults with limited experience using generative chatbots, such as the study's Prolific participants from Australia, the USA, the UK, and Ireland

### Target Learning Goals
- Prompting competency and AI literacy; epistemically independent use of AI assistance in analytical problem-solving
