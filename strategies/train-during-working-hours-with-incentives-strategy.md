# Schedule laparoscopic skills training during working hours with motivational structures such as competitions, rewards and mandatory requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial's finding that motivation was poor in both settings, the authors conclude that "surgical training should be done during working hours" and propose extrinsic motivational structures. They suggest that "rewards, competitions, and conditions that require succeeding at certain levels of exercises before being allowed to operate in the OR" could improve training intensity, alongside mandatory training requirements and testing, dedicated quiet training space, and access to an instructor.
