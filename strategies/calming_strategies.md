# Calming Strategies

## Description
Calming strategies are techniques and methods used to help children manage and reduce feelings of anger and other intense emotions. These strategies are taught and practiced during calm periods, allowing children to build a repertoire of coping mechanisms they can use when needed. The effectiveness of a strategy may vary depending on the child and the intensity of the emotion.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- A list of calming strategies, time to teach and practice the strategies, support from educators and caregivers.
#### Constraints
