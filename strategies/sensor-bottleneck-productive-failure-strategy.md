# Use an unplanned sensor-bandwidth failure as a productive-failure lesson in which students research semiconductor physics and propose replacement sensors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When the LDR's slow recovery blurred the pulse train beyond t≈4.0 s, the instructor treated the hardware failure as an instance of productive failure: the bottleneck "forced an unscripted engagement with datasheet-level semiconductor physics that no planned lesson had delivered". Students researched CdS photoconductive properties, computed the pulse period at 10 000 RPM, and proposed photodiode or Hall-effect replacements compatible with the existing sound-card pipeline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An authentic apparatus limitation that surfaces during use, which students can investigate with datasheet-level physics and propose concrete replacements for
#### Constraints
- The author frames this as an unplanned episode within a single-year retrospective case, not a designed instructional sequence; the project was carried out in 2010 and a microcontroller-based DAQ was beyond its scope

### Target Learners
- adult vocational students with practical trade experience and histories of disengagement from lecture-driven instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- semiconductor sensor physics, sampling and bandwidth concepts, and engineering troubleshooting
