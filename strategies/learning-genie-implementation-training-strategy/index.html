Implement Learning Genie with initial training on observation-based assessment and ongoing technical assistance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article describes an implementation strategy in which initial training covers "the use of observation-based assessment tools, DRDP documentation, lesson-planning workflows, and AI-supported instructional recommendations." Training can be delivered on-site, through live virtual workshops, or recorded webinars, with self-paced support materials, and ongoing implementation and technical assistance are available throughout the school year. A site- or district-level implementation lead coordinates rollout.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A site- or district-level implementation lead (administrator, instructional coach, or data manager) to coordinate account setup, SIS integration, and technical support.

Constraints¶

  • Schools require reliable internet access; technical specifications are provided in the platform's support documentation.

Target Learners¶

  • classroom teachers implementing the platform

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective use of observation-based assessment, DRDP documentation, and AI-supported lesson planning

Related Strategies¶

  • Learning Genie Platform
  • Implement Learning Genie with classroom teachers in regular workflow plus a site- or district-level implementation lead
  • Implement EWIMS through combined in-person and online training with ongoing technical assistance liaisons
  • Plan mutual accountability for implementation, including rollout plans, educator supports, and early indicators for pivoting
  • Deploy AI-assisted classroom observation as a complementary first-pass screening and reflection tool, with raters retaining interpretive judgment
