## Description
The review identifies specific tools for increasing interactivity in remote teaching: "tools such as breakout rooms and audience-response platforms (e.g., Mentimeter™) can facilitate interaction, provide formative assessment, and mimic elements of in-person education." Students participate in polls and quizzes, which the review says increases engagement and interactivity of RT.
