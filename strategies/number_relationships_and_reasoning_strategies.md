# Number Relationships and Reasoning Strategies

## Description
Basic facts should be taught using number relationships and reasoning strategies, not memorization. Learners who learn fact strategies outperform learners who learn through other approaches. Basic fact strategies use number relationships and benchmarks and thus support learners, emerging conceptual understanding and flexibility. Strategies such as Making 10 build a foundation for strategies beyond basic facts, such as Make-a-Whole with fractions and decimals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Demands thorough preparation, creative instructional strategies, and access to resources that effectively illustrate number relationships and reasoning strategies.
#### Constraints
- Instruction can be time-consuming and resource-intensive. May not be suitable for topics with limited real-world applications.
