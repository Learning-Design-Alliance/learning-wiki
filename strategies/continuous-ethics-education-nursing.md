# Provide continuous ethics education from undergraduate studies through professional development to support nurses' moral competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that support for nurses' moral competence forms a continuum from the beginning of nursing studies throughout careers, requiring education at every stage. The implications state: "designing and offering consistent educational strategies at the undergraduate and postgraduate university levels, as well as continuous education and professional development, is needed to support nurses' moral competence." Educational approaches named include interprofessional education, simulation-based and case-based teaching, and internship education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational strategies must be consistent across undergraduate, postgraduate and continuous education, and include examples applicable to practice.
#### Constraints
- The article calls for these strategies based on perceived needs; it did not test their effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- postgraduate nursing students
- registered nurses in continuous education

### Target Learning Goals
- moral competence
- preparedness for ethical dilemmas

## Claims

- [Focus groups with nurses and nursing students in six countries identify seven themes of support for nurses' moral competence spanning individual-relational, organisational and societal levels](../claims/seven-themes-support-nurses-moral-competence.md) [+W]
- [At the individual-relational level, nurses' moral competence is supported by valuing professional ethics, valuing others (including creating listening spaces) and working on oneself](../claims/individual-relational-support-moral-competence.md) [+W]
- [At the organisational level, nurses' moral competence is supported by creating an ethically supportive culture, a solid multi-professional team, qualitative care indicators and supportive working conditions](../claims/organisational-support-moral-competence.md) [+W]
- [At the societal level, nurses' moral competence is supported by recognition of the nursing profession's value and by influencing society through public awareness and reporting pathways](../claims/societal-support-moral-competence.md) [+W]
