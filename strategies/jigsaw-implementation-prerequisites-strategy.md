# Implementation prerequisites for jigsaw: teacher preparation, student briefing, target setting, and active observation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's suggestions section sets out steps teachers should take before and during jigsaw implementation. Teachers should research the method, prepare activities in advance, ensure the subject content suits the method, inform students of the technique beforehand, set the right targets, and "observe students' studies and intervene whenever necessary, in the right place at the right time".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should have adequate information on the cooperative jigsaw method before use
- Students should be informed of the method and activities beforehand
#### Constraints
- The article recommends using the method in secondary Social Sciences lessons only "if there are enough resources and time"

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement in social sciences
