# Develop modular ethics case-study resources with facilitation guidance and curated readings for QIS faculty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that education experts develop modular, QIS-specific ethics resources to lower the barriers faculty report. It states: "we echo Ref. [61] in recommending the development of modular resources cent ered on ethics case-studies, coupled with guidance for leading discussions and tie-ins w ith ethical theory." Curated readings address perceived lack of personal expertise; facilitation guidelines address facilitation skill gaps and political polarization concerns; and a modular format adaptable to the time available, whether 15 minutes or several lecture periods, addresses limited class time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources should be modular and adaptable to the time available, and paired with guidance for leading discussions.
#### Constraints
- The authors frame this as necessary but insufficient by itself to transform the cultures of QIS education; it must translate into actual classroom interventions.

### Target Learners
- faculty teaching introductory QIS courses
- undergraduate and hybrid undergraduate/graduate QIS students

### Target Learning Goals
- incorporating ethics and social responsibility into technical QIS coursework
