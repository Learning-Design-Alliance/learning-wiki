# Use online PBL formats as a safe harbor for students with high communication apprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article advocates "a tailored pedagogical approach, where digital formats serve as a “safe harbor” for students with high communication apprehension". Online synchronous delivery via video conferencing with breakout rooms and shared digital documents allowed students more time to process English-language content and participate at their own pace. This strategy is recommended particularly for complex topics requiring active participation and reflection in non-native-language settings.
