# Recommended reading path into the teaching-machine field: an introductory article first, then the basic Pressey (1950) and Skinner (1958) articles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For readers unfamiliar with the field, the authors recommend starting with at least one introductory article, naming articles by F. Carpenter, Fry, or Lumsdaine as examples. They then advise that "Most serious students will also want to read in the original at least the two basic articles in the field by Pressey (1950) and Skinner (1958)." These basic articles, they state, "are the point of departure for most of the writing in the field." The bibliography itself is positioned as a time-saving aid for locating such sources.
