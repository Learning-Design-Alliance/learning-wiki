# Culturally tailored chatbot design: position chatbots as reflective companions for Chinese users and as thinking partners for Western users, while deploying cognitive support broadly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend designing AI chatbot applications differently across cultural contexts based on the observed divergence. For Chinese users, chatbots "can be positioned as non-judgmental and empathic companions that facilitate self-reflection and adaptive coping", with features like journaling prompts; for Western users, designers should frame chatbots as "thinking partners" rather than empathic listeners. Because instrumental help-seeking showed stable positive associations, they suggest cognitive support applications "can be deployed broadly without extensive cultural adaptation".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers need to know the cultural background of the target user population
#### Constraints
- The recommendations rest on cross-sectional preference data, not on tested design interventions
