# Incorporate automated skills testing as formative assessment in an adaptive learning cycle with repetitive testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A proposed implementation strategy using automated skills testing as the formative assessment step of an adaptive learning cycle. The article recommends future studies "could incorporate automated skills testing as a formative assessment procedure in an adaptive learning cycle with repetitive testing", informing participants about performance and guiding them towards further skills improvement and mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Automated testing must inform participants about performance and guide them towards further skills improvement and mastery
#### Constraints
- Students did not receive feedback about their performance at the end of the test in the current prototype
- Further research is needed to confirm these results in terms of non-inferiority compared to IL testing and usability in other student populations

### Target Learners
- large groups of trainees requiring regular BLS skill assessment

### Target Learning Goals
- formative assessment and refresher training decisions for BLS skills
