# Hold evaluator workshops with video-based consensus scoring before administering the Mini-CEX

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before both the pilot and main assessments, evaluators attended a two-session workshop: the first reviewed the Mini-CEX's background, concept, purpose, and procedure; the second used "a sample video clip of a pharmacist-patient Mini-CEX encounter" which evaluators scored, discussed, and used to "establish performance standards and reach a consensus" before rescoring. Inter-rater reliability was then computed from the second-round scores. The article cites literature indicating faculty Mini-CEX workshops strengthen evaluator consistency and that rater-training workshops are associated with score accuracy improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluators willing to attend both workshop sessions and rescore after discussion
- A sample video encounter depicting a pharmacist-patient assessment
#### Constraints
- Workshop reliability evidence in this study rests on eight evaluators scoring one video scenario

### Target Learners
- Senior pharmacist evaluators and preceptors assessing trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent, reliable rating of trainee clinical performance across evaluators
