# Institutional supports for adopting collaborative teaching: curriculum integration, teacher motivation, and continuous evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's recommendations section sets out implementable steps for adopting collaborative teaching of PVE. It advises, for the short term, "To approve an approach to support collaborative teaching that entails slight changes in the school system and curriculum"; to promote the methodology through the Ministry of Education and teacher education; to plan collaborative teaching within future curriculum development; to create a motivating framework for participating instructors; to continuously evaluate the execution of collaborative teaching; and to secure better social communication among instructors, since compatibility between instructors is a necessity for success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A motivating framework for participating instructors; compatibility between instructors; commitment of school administration
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of Jordanian pre-vocational education and schools that may suffer from shortage of teachers

### Target Learners
- basic-education students in Jordanian public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- improved delivery of interdisciplinary pre-vocational education
- integrated curriculum implementation
