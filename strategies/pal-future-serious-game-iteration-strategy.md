# Iterate PAL programs toward interactive, teacher-configurable serious games with home and cross-grade variants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on teacher and student feedback, the article recommends developing the next PAL iteration as a serious game allowing "increased interactivity", letting students "interact with the character and solve quests", and giving teachers the ability to "select the difficulty level and activities". Teachers also suggested an "individual at home component" so students can "review the material at their own pace", and "adapting it for other grade levels" with different characters per cycle. These recommendations come directly from the program's future-use assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital platform or game development capacity
- Teacher ability to configure difficulty and activities
- Curriculum alignment for each targeted grade cycle
#### Constraints
- The article notes these are suggestions for future iterations, not tested features; teachers also advised avoiding use in September, May and June due to Quebec weather conditions

### Target Learners
- Elementary students across cycles 1-3 (grades 1-6)

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining engagement in physically active learning while reviewing grade-appropriate academic content
