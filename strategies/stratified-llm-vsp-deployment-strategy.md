# Stratified deployment of LLM-VSP practice: self-directed use for high-baseline learners, guided use for medium and low baseline learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the observed trend of differential benefits, the authors recommend that teaching practices consider baseline competency differences when deploying LLM-VSPs. High-baseline students might attempt self-directed use, whereas for medium and low baseline students the authors suggest exploring "a guided teaching model combining instructor guidance and LLM-VSPs" to help them consolidate theoretical groundwork and interpret AI feedback. The authors note further empirical research is required to verify whether this hybrid model improves proficiency.
