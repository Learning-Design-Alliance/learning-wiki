# Establish an early, preventive support system for low-achieving students, using high inter-course score correlations to predict at-risk students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends establishing a support system for the academic development of low-achieving students, arguing that the high correlation between tests means at-risk students can be predicted early with high probability. It advocates "preventive and proactive developmental approaches, focusing on developing students' personal and professional growth and lifelong learning skills" in early stages, citing strong evidence for remediation focused on assessing and improving self-regulation. The authors argue this is more educationally desirable than a deficit-reactive approach, which risks stigma or repeated failure even after remediation.

## Design Implications

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- prevention of summative failure and development of lifelong learning skills
