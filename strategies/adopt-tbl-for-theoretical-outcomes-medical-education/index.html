Strategies

Adopt TBL preferentially for theoretical knowledge outcomes in medical education, applied flexibly by context¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the meta-analytic advantage of TBL for theoretical test scores and teamwork skills, the authors recommend TBL implementation in medical education while cautioning that results "need to be applied flexibly according to the actual situation." They advise future studies to clearly describe the specific operational details of TBL and PBL and to use more objective and standardized evaluation indicators. The recommendation rests on the pooled SMD of 0.37 for theoretical scores and 1.18 for teamwork skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Clear description of specific operational details of TBL and PBL (course content, duration, frequency, group size, teacher role, learning resources)
  • More objective and standardized evaluation indicators

Constraints¶

  • No advantage was found for practical skills, learning interest, or understanding skills, so the recommendation is limited to theoretical and teamwork outcomes

Target Learners¶

  • medical students
  • residents in clinical specialty training

Target Learning Goals¶

  • theoretical knowledge mastery
  • teamwork skills

Affordances¶

  • Tbl Pbl Structural Differences Framework

Claims¶

  • TBL produces significantly higher theoretical test scores than PBL in medical education (SMD = 0.37) [+M]
  • TBL significantly improves teamwork skills compared with PBL (SMD = 1.18) [+M]
  • TBL and PBL show no significant difference in improving learning interest [~M]
  • TBL and PBL show no significant difference in practical skill scores [~M]
  • TBL and PBL show no significant difference in understanding skills, with a point estimate favoring TBL [~M]
  • Meta-regression finds discipline, country, year, and learner identity do not significantly explain heterogeneity in TBL-versus-PBL effects [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement TBL with dedicated teaching teams, systematic teacher training, and diversified assessment
  • Align teaching method with competency gap and prioritize TBL for communication-focused courses
  • Integrate TBL with other teaching methods and modern educational technologies
