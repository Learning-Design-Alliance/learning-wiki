# Integrate more hands-on labs, collaboration, and expanded project time in future camp iterations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on student feedback, the authors plan concrete changes for future QCaMP iterations: "More hands-on labs and activities will be integrated with each lesson module, including more opportunities for students to work collaboratively throughout the camp", plus an additional quantum chemistry and atomic qubits module and expanded group research project time. Student open-ended feedback also asked for more hands-on lessons and an expanded research portion. The authors further recommend additional programs such as summer camps, workshops, and technician training to fill pathway gaps at the high school, community college, and undergraduate levels.
