# Adaptive instructional planning allocates scarce patient-treatment opportunities to students who benefit most

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Patients requiring a particular treatment are a limited resource in a dental school, so LiftUpp uses its insight into student performance to decide which students will benefit most from additional practice opportunities, while other students are placed in 'holding patterns' where the frequency of their workplace-based assessment is reduced or shifted towards less resource-limited treatments.
