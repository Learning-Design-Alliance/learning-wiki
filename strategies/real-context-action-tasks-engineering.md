# Culminate engineering learning tasks in action within real contexts through industry, government, and NGO partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The practice-integrated pillar recommends that learning tasks end in real-world action rather than laboratory simulations or hypothetical projects, which the article says limit exposure to uncertainty, stakeholder diversity, and implementation constraints. The article states that "AI-SEE therefore emphasizes that learning tasks should culminate in action within real contexts," with implementation pathways including energy-efficiency diagnostics with enterprises, photovoltaic site selection with township governments, and social innovation projects with environmental NGOs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- partnerships with industrial parks, manufacturing enterprises, township or village governments, and environmental NGOs
- capstone and graduation projects sourced from industrial challenges and pressing societal issues
#### Constraints
- the article presents this as one pillar of a framework designed for resource-constrained, practice-oriented higher education contexts

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- resilience, systems thinking, and integrative coordination capabilities
- public service competence and civic engagement
