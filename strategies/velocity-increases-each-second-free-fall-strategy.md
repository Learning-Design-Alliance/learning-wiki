# Introduce constant acceleration by saying velocity increases by a fixed amount each second (e.g. 10 m/s per second in free fall) instead of stating a constant acceleration of 10 m/s²

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes starting the academic discussion of free fall by telling students that "la velocidad aumenta cada segundo en 10 m/s", rather than that the acceleration is constant at 10 m/s². Students then analyze the proportionality this statement implies, together with possible average velocities and their respective displacements, as tabulated for free fall in Table 1 (t from 0 to 1.0 s, v up to 10.0 m/s, vp up to 5.0 m/s, Δx up to 5.0 m). Concrete referents such as car or motorcycle speedometers help visualize the change of velocity over time.
