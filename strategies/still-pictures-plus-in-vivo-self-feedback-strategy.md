# Combine validated still pictures with an in vivo phase of real colonoscopies using self-administered pathology feedback when designing a self-learning program for optical diagnosis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program reviewed the NICE classification, used "a published set of still pictures" to identify optical characteristics of hyperplastic and adenomatous polyps under NBI, then practiced the classification on 50 consecutive real screening-colonoscopy lesions. The endoscopist reviewed pathological records and "diagnostic disagreements were reviewed with the pathologist", easing the transition from still pictures to real practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real colonoscopy lesions with available pathological records for self-administered feedback
#### Constraints
- Tested in a single experienced endoscopist; the article notes the results may be dependent on the trainee's characteristics

### Target Learners
- endoscopists training in narrow band imaging optical diagnosis

### Target Learning Goals
- differentiation of adenomatous from hyperplastic colorectal lesions
- meeting PIVI competency thresholds
