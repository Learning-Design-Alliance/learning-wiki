# Emphasise practical-type education and blended e-learning-plus-practice in pre-hospital CPC programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that CPC programmes for pre-hospital practitioners centre on practical, hands-on activities and use e-learning only when supplemented by related practical modules. The authors state that "prudent purveyors of education for pre-hospital practitioners should emphasise inclusion of practical-type education" and that less emphasis should be placed on e-learning alone. This strategy follows directly from the survey's relevance ratings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning components must be paired with related practical modules rather than delivered standalone
#### Constraints
- Based on a survey of Irish EMTs with a 43% response rate that may not be representative of EMTs in general; respondents skewed toward younger, voluntary-organisation-affiliated practitioners

### Target Learners
- Emergency medical technicians and other pre-hospital practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining professional competence through continuous professional competence activities
