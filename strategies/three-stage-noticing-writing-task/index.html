Three-stage writing task: write with problem notes, compare to a model paragraph, then rewrite¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study's implementable protocol: in Stage 1 the learner writes a paragraph in response to an illustrated question while noting problems on a second sheet (e.g., "I do not know how to write X"); in Stage 2 he compares his text to a model paragraph and notes what he notices; in Stage 3 he rewrites the original text. Each stage took roughly 10 to 15 minutes and was repeated for three questions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Model paragraphs on the same topics
  • Note-taking sheets and oral instructions for recording problems

Constraints¶

  • The article states notetaking as a feedback tool "only facilitates resolution of lexical problems"

Target Learners¶

  • Adult EFL learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Paragraph writing; noticing lexical and grammatical gaps
