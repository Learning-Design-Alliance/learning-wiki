# Intercultural programming to enhance socio-emotional engagement for probation students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends culturally relevant programming, peer role-model opportunities, cultural celebration events, health and wellness support, and dedicated support programs for personal issues. Institutions should ease the "deficit-orientation" of students and adapt flexible advising models centering student learning experiences. Programming for intercultural engagement should occur "inside and outside of the classroom through partnerships and collaborations with academic and non-academic units on campus."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnerships between academic and non-academic campus units
- Advising offices that feel safe and welcoming
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are drawn from literature and one institution's context, not tested experimentally

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students on academic probation
- Underrepresented and historically marginalized students

### Target Learning Goals
- Sense of belonging
- Socio-emotional engagement
- Intercultural competence
