# Hospitals and nursing educators should provide spiritual care education courses and integrate them into undergraduate nursing curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals conduct spiritual care education courses and targeted training, and that nursing educators embed such courses in undergraduate programmes to build spiritual care capacity in nursing students. It also suggests flexible delivery, such as online guidance courses nurses can choose by convenience, and regular seminars to raise awareness. This follows from the meta-analytic finding that spiritual education courses significantly influence competency.
