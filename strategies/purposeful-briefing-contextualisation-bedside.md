# Purposeful prebriefing and contextual richness to contextualise the bedside encounter

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the encounter, tutors conduct a purposeful briefing to activate learners' existing knowledge, gauge baseline knowledge and tailor the encounter, and attend to emotional readiness and orientation to the ward environment, including normalising anxieties to establish psychological safety. During and after the encounter, tutors discuss patient-related, sociocultural and workplace factors to provide "contextual richness" and situate learning within the broader clinical environment.
