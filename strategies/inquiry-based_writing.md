# Inquiry-Based Writing

## Description
Engage in focused investigation with “immediate and concrete data” that learners gather and analyze, as a springboard to higher quality writing. Assign authentic activities and materials as inquiry writing, either inquiry in the community or online as a web quest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research materials, inquiry techniques, analytical skills

- Requires research skills, may be time-consuming
