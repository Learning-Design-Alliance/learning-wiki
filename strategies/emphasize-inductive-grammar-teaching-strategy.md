# Emphasize the inductive way of teaching grammar while varying techniques to students' interests and achievement levels

## Description
The article recommends that EFL teachers vary their teaching techniques according to students' interests and achievement levels, with more emphasis on the inductive way of teaching grammar. It also encourages teachers and instructors to attend intensive training courses held by the Ministry of Education to become aware of modern ways of teaching English and to apply them in class.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Teachers attend intensive training courses held by the Ministry of Education to become familiar with modern teaching methods
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is drawn from a study conducted in Jordan; the author calls for further studies in other regions and on other syntax areas

### Target Learners
- EFL teachers and instructors at school and university levels in Jordan
