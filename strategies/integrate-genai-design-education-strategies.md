# Design educators should strengthen primary research, foster critical verification habits, and move students from consumers to makers of GenAI tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's conclusions propose educational responses to emerging GenAI practices in design education. The author asks whether educators should strengthen primary research so students collect their own data, how to prevent complacency in verifying outputs, and argues "There is a case to be made for ensuring that students become less of 'consumers' of GenAI tools and more 'makers' of their own tools" via custom GPTs, low/no-code apps and open source models. A yearly longitudinal survey is also recommended to monitor evolving practices.

### Target Learning Goals

- customising GenAI tools for project needs
