# Combine published assessment tools with government data and/or teacher reports to more effectively identify students in need of additional support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A future-research recommendation from the review's discussion: combining data sources may increase the sensitivity and specificity of assessments to identify students in need of support (citing Nelson et al., 2016). The review suggests "the combination of published assessment tools with government data and/or teacher reports, which may lead to more effective identification of students in need of additional support at school". It also recommends examining school attendance alongside disciplinary and academic outcomes when planning research and designing supports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both published standardised assessments and government or teacher-reported data
#### Constraints
- Government datasets may lack detailed information on factors associated with outcomes

### Target Learners
- autistic school students

### Target Learning Goals
- identification of students needing academic support
- understanding reasons for school non-attendance
