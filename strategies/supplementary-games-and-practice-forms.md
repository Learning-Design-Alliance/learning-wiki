# Supplementary games and practice forms to extend vocabulary practice after the core game

## Description
The article recommends reinforcing the vocabulary practiced in the marble game with supplementary games and written exercises. It lists supplementary games "namely 1) Musical chairs, 2) Telephone game, 3) Card game, 4) Clothesline game" that "can be added and modified by the children", plus a Word Salad alphabetical sorting exercise and five puzzle types (word puzzles in three directions, a picture puzzle, and a 400-box trilingual word search). The article states that using various games and supplementary material "helps the students focus on the subject matter for an extended period of time".
