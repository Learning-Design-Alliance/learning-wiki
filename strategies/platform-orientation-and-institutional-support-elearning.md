# Train students on the e-learning platform before teaching and strengthen institutional readiness and support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students spent the first week of the course learning to navigate the platform, and some suggested faculty should train students on how to use the Moodle platform before teaching. The discussion concludes "there is need to provide such training to enhance student learning experience" and recommends improving institutional readiness and support, including ICT support, developing lecturers' skills, access to computers, increased bandwidth and a videoconferencing solution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty time to deliver platform orientation before teaching begins
- Institutional investment in ICT support, computing devices, bandwidth and videoconferencing
#### Constraints
- The article notes e-learning effectiveness "requires sustained learner motivation, digital literacy and significant investment by the host institution"

### Target Learners
- higher diploma students new to e-learning platforms
- faculty coordinating online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- effective use of the learning management system
- reduced technological barriers to participation
