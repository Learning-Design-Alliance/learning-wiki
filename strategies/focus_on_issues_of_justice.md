# Focus on Issues of Justice

## Description
Authentic projects that focus on issues of justice engage students by having them work on something that matters, impacting their lives, their community, and the lives of those they care about. These projects go beyond teaching the standards by providing opportunities for students to become better people and act as change agents to make the world a better place.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitation by instructors to guide ethical inquiry and respectful dialogue, access to resources for research and action, a classroom culture that values social justice and equity
#### Constraints
- Requires careful consideration of potentially sensitive or controversial topics, demands thoughtful facilitation to ensure respectful and inclusive discussions, may require collaboration with community partners

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Middle school, high school, and higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase student engagement, promote social justice awareness, develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, foster empathy and compassion, empower students to be change agents

### Affordances
- Aligns learning with students' values and concerns, fosters a sense of purpose and agency, promotes critical thinking about social issues, encourages students to take action and make a difference

### Personalization
- Allows students to choose issues of personal significance, provides opportunities for students to connect with their community, can be adapted to different cultural contexts and perspectives

## Assessment Evidence
- Depth of research and understanding of social justice issues, effectiveness of proposed solutions, engagement and participation in project activities, demonstration of empathy and compassion

## Impact
- Projects focused on issues of justice lead to higher student engagement and a deeper understanding of social issues. This approach empowers students to become change agents and fosters a commitment to making the world a better place. (Chaffee, 2017)
