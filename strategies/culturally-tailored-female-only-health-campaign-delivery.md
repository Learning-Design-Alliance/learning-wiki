# Adapt health education campaigns culturally for sensitive topics using female-only settings and tailored language.

## Description
The article describes specific cultural adaptations for delivering breast health education in the UAE context. Sessions were "delivered exclusively by female pharmacy students in female-only settings within the schools", with language and materials adapted to respect local modesty norms. Visual content used stylised silhouettes rather than anatomical photography, and culturally acceptable terminology facilitated respectful communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Female facilitators in female-only settings for this topic and context
- Careful adaptation of language and visual materials to local norms
#### Constraints
- Specific to cultural contexts where breast health is considered taboo

### Target Learners
- Female secondary school students aged 16-18

### Target Learning Goals
- Breast cancer awareness and early detection knowledge
