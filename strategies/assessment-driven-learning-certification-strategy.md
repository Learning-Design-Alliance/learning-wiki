# Assessment-driven learning: use certification preparation to prompt preceptors' self-directed review, reflection, and pedagogical skill improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes the certification system as a faculty development mechanism in which preparing for structured assessment itself drives learning: the process "prompts me to proactively review and learn teaching theories" and to "reflect on and improve my own clinical operational skills" (questionnaire items Q8–Q9 scored 4.90 and 4.93). Certification is framed not as mere selection but as "an assessment-driven learning process" that embeds formative assessment, pedagogical reflection, and competency enhancement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured, standardized assessment with clear competency criteria and rubrics, plus pre-assessment training (mandatory modules on the hospital's training APP and specialized skill demonstration trainings) so candidates can prepare meaningfully.
#### Constraints
- 62.3% of participants called for enhanced pre-assessment training, particularly demonstrations for comprehensive assessments like teaching ward rounds; the authors note the heavy workload of clinical nurses necessitates finding ways to enhance training effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- Experienced clinical nurses preparing for preceptor certification

### Target Learning Goals
- Teaching theory knowledge, clinical operational skill refinement, theory–practice integration, and pedagogical reflection

## Claims

- [94.5% of surveyed nursing interns were relatively satisfied with teaching quality and 95.5% with their preceptors after certification implementation](../claims/intern-satisfaction-94-5-teaching-quality.md) [+W]
- [78.6% of 193 candidates for lead clinical nursing instructor certification passed the three-module assessment, with component pass rates from 83.9% to 93.3%](../claims/license-to-teach-pass-rate-78-point-6-percent.md) [+W]
- [Certified preceptors' open-ended feedback most frequently cited standardized teaching processes (49.0%), knowledge consolidation (46.2%), and professional recognition (37.2%), with 62.8% calling for stronger pre-assessment training](../claims/open-ended-feedback-standardization-and-training-needs.md) [+W]
- [Certified preceptors reported high satisfaction with the certification system, with every questionnaire item scoring above 4.7 on a 5-point scale (Cronbach's α = 0.936)](../claims/preceptor-certification-satisfaction-above-4-7.md) [+W]
- [The teaching ward rounds component had a lower pass rate (83.9%) than the theoretical teaching component (93.3%), indicating interactive teaching skills as a weakness among preceptors](../claims/ward-rounds-pass-rate-lower-than-theoretical.md) [+W]
