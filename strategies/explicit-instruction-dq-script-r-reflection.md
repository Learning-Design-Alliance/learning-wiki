# Explicitly teach construction of the differential charge element and difference vector, and prompt reflection on solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on documented student difficulties, the authors "advocate an increased focus on teaching students to construct the differential charge element and difference vector, as well as explicit discussion of how and when to reflect on their solutions." They also "encourage instructors to be aware of the resources their prompts cue students to access," since prompt wording (e.g., asking for total charge first) appears to cue unproductive Gauss' Law use.

## Design Implications
