# Feedback Format

## Description
Feedback can be delivered in various formats, including written, verbal, or video. Instructors can provide written feedback directly in learning management systems (LMS) or on assignment submissions. Verbal feedback can be provided through audio podcasts or screen recordings, while video feedback can include virtual sessions or video recordings combining screen capture and verbal explanations. The format should suit the content and learner needs to enhance engagement and understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to tools and platforms for providing written, verbal, or video feedback (e.g., LMS, Vocaroo, Jing, Zoom), and clear guidelines for effective feedback.
#### Constraints
- Some formats may require more time or resources. Accessibility concerns may arise for learners with disabilities, such as hearing or visual impairments.
