Provide female students with opportunities to learn mathematics in group learning environments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on their finding that female students outperformed male students after the group learning intervention, the authors recommend that "it is important that female students be provided with opportunities to learn mathematics in the classroom in the group learning environment." They further recommend education authorities encourage secondary teachers to use group learning and that teacher education institutions include it in training curriculum content.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers trained in the Learning Together model (the study gave teachers seven-day training)

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single quasi-experiment in rural Bangladeshi secondary schools, delimited to mathematics learning only

Target Learners¶

  • female secondary school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics achievement

Affordances¶

  • Learning Together Model Five Elements

Claims¶

  • Female students in the group learning group significantly outperformed female students in the conventional group on the mathematics post-test [+W]
  • Within the group learning treatment, female students achieved significantly higher mathematics post-test scores than male students [+W]
  • Group learning produced significantly higher mathematics achievement than conventional teaching among rural grade-nine students after a 15-week intervention [+W]
  • Male students in the group learning group significantly outperformed male students in the conventional group on the mathematics post-test [+W]
  • MANOVA with repeated measures showed significant main effects of test-time and a significant group-learning-by-test-time interaction on mathematics achievement [+W]
