# Use three parental mediation strategies—active mediation, restrictive mediation, and non-intrusive inspection—to reduce cyberbullying exposure among teenagers

## Description
The review documents three parental mediation strategies that impact the prevalence of cyberbullying among teenagers. As printed, "Active mediation, where parents engage directly with their children to discuss and explain media content, has been shown to effectively reduce privacy violations and cyber risks". Restrictive mediation involves setting rules and limits on social media use, and non-intrusive inspection means parents monitor online activity without direct interference, which "also correlates negatively with the incidence of cyberbullying and other online risks". The article positions parental mediation, with educators and policymakers, as part of eradicating cyberbullying.
