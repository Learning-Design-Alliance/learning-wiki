# Use solo computerized tutorials to supplement or replace prelecture quizzes rather than to replace in-person group tutorials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's participants suggested using the solo AcePhysics tutorial to supplement or replace the course's prelecture "preflight" quizzes, a use the authors flag as testable in future work. This positions the platform as useful practice alongside lecture rather than as a replacement for in-person group tutorials, consistent with the finding that students valued the tutorial yet engaged with it differently solo.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A course with existing prelecture quiz infrastructure or homework slots where the online tutorial can be assigned.
#### Constraints
- The article reports students may interact from an answer-matching mentality solo, so the strategy should not be treated as equivalent to group tutorials.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics and quantum computing students

### Target Learning Goals
- formative practice with QIS skills such as tensor products outside class time
