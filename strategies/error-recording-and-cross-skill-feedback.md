# Record the types of errors made so as not to keep making the same types of errors, and use writing feedback to support other English skills.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the editing stage, the teacher recommends and reminds students to record their errors so they do not keep making the same types of errors. Students should also use the feedback they receive on their writing to help them with other English language skills including reading, speaking, and listening. The author reports that the percentage of students who recorded their errors increased from 52% in the pre-test to 68% in the post-test, and those who used feedback for other skills increased from 44% to 70%.

## Design Implications

- Students apply writing feedback to other English skills
