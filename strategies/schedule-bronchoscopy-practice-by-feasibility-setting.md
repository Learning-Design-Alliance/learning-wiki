# Choose bronchoscopy practice scheduling by clinical feasibility: one-day distributed practice where instructor feedback is included, weekly where practice is unsupervised

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study found no additional effect of distributing bronchoscopy practice beyond a single day, the authors recommend letting feasibility guide scheduling. They state that "a less distributed practice schedule like the one-day distributed practice schedule may be favoured in a practice setting that includes feedback from an instructor, and a weekly distributed practice schedule may be favoured in an unsupervised practice setting." In supervised settings, coordinating trainee and instructor schedules is difficult amid busy clinical life, whereas unsupervised weekly scheduling involves only the trainee.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practice sessions on a high-fidelity bronchoscopy simulator with the same three-session programme; supervised settings require coordinating trainee and instructor availability
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is limited to bronchoscopy skills acquisition; the authors note results may not generalise to less complex procedural skills

### Target Learners
- PGY 1–PGY 3 respiratory medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic bronchoscopy skills: scope navigation, systematic airway inspection, avoiding wall collisions and red-out
