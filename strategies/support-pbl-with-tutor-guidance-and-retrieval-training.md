# Support PBL implementation in residency training with tutor guidance, retrieval-skills instruction, and improved teaching facilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the questionnaire and heterogeneity findings, the authors recommend practical supports for PBL in Chinese standardized residency training. They note the PBL group "requires time and effort for preparation before the class" and that students' literature retrieval level was limited, concluding that "improving the level of teaching hardware, and giving guidance on the retrieval methods is needed." They also state tutor guidance and encouragement are necessary for developing residents' communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors should provide guidance and encouragement for communication between resident and tutor and among training residents
#### Constraints
- Existing teaching facilities could not fully meet residents' preparation needs, as discussed by one included study

### Target Learners
- residents in Chinese standardized residency training

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed learning preparation skills
- communication ability
- effective use of PBL preparation time
