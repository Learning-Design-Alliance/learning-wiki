# Measure nurses' practical PICC maintenance performance directly and indirectly in future studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because theoretical knowledge scores do not capture actual care quality, the authors recommend that future work measure the quality of nurses' practical performance of PICC maintenance. They propose it "can be measured directly by observation at bedside according to the standardised PICC maintenance order sets and checklist", and indirectly through PICC indwelling time, incidence of PICC-related complications and patient satisfaction, with additional measurement methods to be explored.
