# Differentiate safety education content and teaching methods for novice versus experienced nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that when organizing programs to enhance nurses' safety culture competency, "contents, and teaching methods are specified for novice and experienced nurses". For novice nurses, programs should focus on recognizing the importance of a safety culture; for experienced nurses, they should address various cases occurring in real-life nursing situations. This career-specific approach responds to the finding that safety control is influenced by clinical career.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education content should be composed of what nurses need and help them efficiently cope with patient safety accidents
#### Constraints
- The study could not take into account qualitative differences in education types (workshops, seminars, online lectures)

### Target Learners
- novice nurses
- experienced clinical nurses

### Target Learning Goals

- patient safety culture awareness
