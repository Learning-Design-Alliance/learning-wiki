# Use the situated learning model as an intervention for alleviating mathematics anxiety in elementary mathematics classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, alongside other strategies, teachers use McLellan's situated learning model to reduce students' mathematics anxiety: "situated learning model of McLellan (1991, 1996) can be also be used effectively for the alleviation of mathematics anxiety in elementary school stude nts." The strategy embeds mathematics in social activity and authentic contexts instead of traditional, context-divorced presentation, which the author argues can make mathematics a source of pleasure and change community predispositions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must be context based and socially situated rather than traditional formula-then-practice teaching
#### Constraints
- The author cautions the finding needs replication with a longer treatment period and qualitative data, and notes the volunteer-delivered treatment raises an experimenter-effect concern

### Target Learners
- elementary and upper-elementary school students; the author suggests broadening to other age groups

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing mathematics anxiety and improving mathematics participation
