# Use motivation and study-strategy instruments specific to clinical practice when investigating clerkship learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A measurement strategy for medical education research: general motivation and study-strategy questionnaires cannot capture learning in clinical settings, so studies of clerkship performance should use instruments targeting clinical practice. The article concludes: "We recommend using a questionnaire measuring motivation specifically for clinical practice" and that the SPQ "can be used only for investigation of approach toward studying medical content/cognitive knowledge".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to or development of a validated questionnaire measuring motivation for clinical practice specifically.
#### Constraints
- The SPQ and SRQ-A as used measure approaches to studying medical knowledge, not clinical skills learning.

### Target Learners
- medical students in clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical performance
