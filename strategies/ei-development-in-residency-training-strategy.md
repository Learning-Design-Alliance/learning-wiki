# Integrate emotional intelligence development into residency training through humanistic care courses and social activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that residency programs embed emotional intelligence cultivation in standardized training. It states "Resident training institutions can develop and improve residents' humanistic caring skills by offering humanistic caring-related elective courses", and encourages residents to organize or participate in school and social activities, since residents with school community work experience scored higher in emotional intelligence. Medical educators are also urged to create a humanistic atmosphere and model caring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
