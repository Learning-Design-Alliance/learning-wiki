# Evaluate & Reflect then Sustain & Scale: analyze pilot data against targeted goals and share results with critical partners to plan expanded use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Step 5 directs leaders to "Analyze pilot data to determine whether the tool sufficiently addressed your targeted teaching and learning goals." Step 6 directs leaders to "Share the pilot results with critical partners, such as the school board, community partners, funders, teachers, and students, to collaboratively develop a comprehensive plan for expanded and/or long-term use of successful tools." Evaluation feeds a collaborative scaling decision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pilot data analysis against targeted teaching and learning goals
- Sharing results with critical partners including the school board, community partners, funders, teachers, and students
#### Constraints
- Scaling applies to tools the pilot shows were successful

### Target Learners
- District and school leaders
- School boards
- Teachers
- Students
- Community partners and funders

### Target Learning Goals
- Determining whether edtech addressed targeted teaching and learning goals
- Planning expanded or long-term use of successful tools
