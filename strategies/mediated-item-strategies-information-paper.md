# Mediate each grammar-test item interactively using clue-seeking, elimination and comparison strategies, with a take-home information paper

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's dynamic-assessment mediation procedure had teachers mediate each test item interactively using named strategies: "looking for clues, eliminating the answers that do not fit and comparison strategies." Mediation was divided into two stages: manipulation of the grammatical and structural point through these strategies, and an information paper given to students at the end of each session to work on at home.
