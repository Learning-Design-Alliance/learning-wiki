# Integrate AI literacy and critical appraisal skills into dental curricula alongside discipline-specific AI validation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building AI literacy and critical appraisal into dental education so students can use chatbots responsibly, and calls for discipline-specific validation, continuous performance monitoring, and tailored training datasets. It states that "discipline-specific weaknesses emphasize the importance of integrating AI literacy and critical appraisal skills into dental education." It also warns that excessive reliance on chatbots can hinder independent problem-solving and that unequal access may deepen educational disparities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully designed guidelines that safeguard equity and academic integrity
- Robust exam protocols and integrity safeguards alongside pedagogical integration of AI
#### Constraints
- Excessive reliance on chatbots can hinder development of independent problem-solving skills
- Unequal access to advanced AI systems may deepen educational disparities across institutions

### Target Learners
- dental students
- dental educators

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible AI use
- critical appraisal of AI-generated content
- independent problem-solving
