# Form journal clubs specifically designed for undergraduates and early graduate students, using Bites summaries as entry points

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that departments form journal clubs specifically designed for undergraduates and early graduate students, countering the expectation that juniors "learn by osmosis". Accessible summaries provide entry points for students to actively join discussions of the literature in "a non-intimidating environment" where they learn to read papers and provide constructive criticism. This recommendation rests on the authors' experience rather than a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Departmental sponsorship and a regular meeting structure; accessible summary resources such as Astrobites posts
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a recommendation based on experience, not an evaluated program

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- early graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning how to read papers
- becoming familiar with the literature
- critiquing research
