# SEL-Focused Digital Libraries

## Description
This strategy involves curating digital libraries to include a section of books dedicated to Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) topics. This allows teachers and students to easily search for and find titles that are relevant to them or focused on specific SEL skills, facilitating integration of SEL into literacy and reading activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a digital library platform, curated collection of SEL-focused books, and awareness among teachers and students about the availability and purpose of the collection.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and diversity of the book collection and the extent to which teachers integrate the resources into their instruction. Digital access may be a barrier for some students.

### Personalization
- Libraries can be customized to include books relevant to the specific needs and interests of the student population, cultural backgrounds, and specific SEL goals of the school or district.
