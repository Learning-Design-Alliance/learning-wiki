# Deliver school-based oral health education with parent–teacher collaboration and long-term follow-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing structured, school-based oral health education programs and states that "school-based education supported by collaboration between teachers and parents has proven to be more effective for children." It also recommends future programs with longer follow-up periods and enhanced parent–teacher collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Written informed consent from parents/guardians, as obtained in the trial.
#### Constraints
- The study lacked long-term follow-up beyond six months, which the authors list as a limitation.

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged 3–5

### Target Learning Goals
- oral and dental health knowledge and hygiene behaviours
