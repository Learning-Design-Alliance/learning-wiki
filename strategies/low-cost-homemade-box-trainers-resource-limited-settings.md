# Use low-cost homemade box trainers to expand access to laparoscopic training in resource-limited settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that where commercial surgical trainers are unavailable, particularly in developing countries with limited resources, low-cost homemade trainers offer an accessible alternative for acquiring basic laparoscopic skills. The authors state that "in this setting, the availability of low-cost homemade trainers may represent a noticable advantage" and that "thanks to the low cost and accessibility of materials, LABOT could represent a suitable solution in these countries".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to basic building materials such as plywood, rubber, a neon lamp, and a bullet mini camera, following the published construction instructions
#### Constraints
- The cost of the LABOT box is £68 (around 80 euros), which, while low compared to commercial alternatives, is not negligible

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees in resource-limited settings
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic technical skills acquisition
