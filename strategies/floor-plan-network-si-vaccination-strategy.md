# Building a floor-plan network of the school building and simulating SI epidemic dynamics with vaccination strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Pupils constructed a network of the n = 161 rooms of the hosting monastery school, self-organizing into per-floor groups with unique room labels and duplicate independent creation for error checking. They then simulated susceptible-infected dynamics on it and compared random vaccination with targeted betweenness-based vaccination. The article reports: "the pupils were able to identify that the betweenness-vaccination is slowing the spread of the disease."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A printable floor plan of a familiar building
- Pupils' prior knowledge of graph isomorphism conditions, centrality measures and SI dynamics
#### Constraints
- The final steady state showed the same number of infected rooms under both vaccination strategies, so only the spread speed differed

### Target Learners
- high-school pupils in an outreach summer course

### Target Learning Goals
- network construction from real-world spaces
- centrality measures
- epidemic dynamics and herd immunity
