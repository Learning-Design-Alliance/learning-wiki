# In resource-limited settings, begin simulation training with standardized patients and part-task simulators, progressing to high-fidelity simulation centers as funding becomes available

## Description
A staged implementation strategy for simulation-based medical education in financially constrained settings: organizations start with lower-cost modalities and scale up. The article endorses this approach, citing Riaz's suggestion that "organizations in resource-limited environments should initiate simulation training with standardized patients and part-task simulators, progressing to more advanced high-fidelity simulation centers as funding becomes available", calling it practical guidance for developing countries in early stages of SBME.

## Design Implications

### Context
