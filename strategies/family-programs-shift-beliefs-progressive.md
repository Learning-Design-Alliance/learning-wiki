# Family education programs should help parents reflect on and shift childrearing beliefs toward progressive orientations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that family education programs help parents reflect on their childrearing beliefs and shift them toward more progressive and less traditional orientations, because beliefs, though resistant to change, matter for authoritative parenting and children's math achievement. This recommendation is grounded in the study's finding that parenting beliefs predicted practices and math outcomes, and is framed against China's 2022 Family Education Promotion Law creating demand for evidence-based parenting programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must address beliefs themselves, since adjusting parenting beliefs may be critical to promoting and sustaining changes in parenting practices
#### Constraints
- Beliefs are often implicit ideas about childrearing and tend to be resistant to change

### Target Learners
- Chinese parents of young children

### Target Learning Goals
- improving parenting quality and children's academic development
