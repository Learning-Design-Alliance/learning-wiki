## Description
Vigyan Vidushi is a summer school for female students pursuing master's degrees in physics, organized by TIFR. The article describes its aim as mentoring women "at a stage when they are making a career choice," positioning targeted mentoring at the transition between master's study and a research career as an intervention point for women's retention in physics.
