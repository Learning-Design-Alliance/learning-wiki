# Positive Affirmations

## Description
Using positive affirmations involves creating and repeating positive statements about oneself to build inroads into the subconscious mind, opening up the possibility of a new state of thoughts.  These statements should be short, believable, focused, and delivered with feeling to influence the subconscious mind. Regular practice of repeating affirmations helps internalize these positive beliefs, leading to enhanced self-esteem, confidence, and a more optimistic outlook.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires consistent practice and willingness to believe in the positive statements. May benefit from external support such as therapy or coaching to identify and address underlying beliefs. Access to resources such as affirmation lists, guided meditations, or visualization exercises can be helpful.
#### Constraints
- May feel unnatural or insincere at first, particularly for individuals with deeply ingrained negative self-perceptions. May not be effective if not accompanied by genuine belief and emotional engagement. Can be challenging to maintain consistency in the face of significant stress or adversity.

### Affordances
- Enhances self-esteem by promoting positive self-perception. Influences subconscious mind by repeatedly reinforcing positive beliefs. Fosters a growth mindset by encouraging learners to focus on their potential. Increases motivation by instilling a sense of self-efficacy.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted with customized affirmations tailored to individual needs and goals. Flexible practice allows for integration into daily routines through verbal repetition, journaling, or visualization. Scaffolding can be provided for beginners, such as lists of common affirmations or guided scripts.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track changes in self-reported self-esteem and confidence using questionnaires or journals. Observe improvements in mood, motivation, and goal achievement. Analyze ability to articulate and embody positive beliefs in real-life situations.

## Impact
- Research suggests that positive affirmations can improve self-esteem, reduce stress, and promote goal achievement (e.g., PositivePsychology.com). Studies show that repeating affirmations can activate brain regions associated with self-related processing and reward. Regular practice has been linked to improved psychological well-being and resilience.
