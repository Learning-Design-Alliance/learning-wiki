# Validate LLM-based PFA training through larger-scale studies, trainee feedback, multimodal interfaces, and validated assessment frameworks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking research agenda calls for empirical validation before LLM-based PFA training is adopted at scale. "Future research should address our study's limitations by incorporating larger-scale evaluations with multiple participants, diverse scenarios, and more advanced LLMs", with direct trainee feedback to validate instructional value and multimodal interfaces such as voice, avatars, or virtual reality to capture nonverbal cues. Because PFA outcome measurement lacks standardisation, "future research should focus on developing validated assessment frameworks that ensure consistent evaluation of PFA training outcomes across diverse contexts".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Randomised controlled trials to determine whether LLM-based PFA training reliably builds helping skills without fostering overconfidence
#### Constraints
- Current variability in how PFA core competencies are assessed hinders comparison across programmes, including AI-enhanced ones

### Target Learners
- PFA training researchers and programme developers

### Target Learning Goals
- Rigorous evaluation of whether AI-enhanced PFA training adds pedagogical value
