# Provide remote automated certification of basic laparoscopic skills without on-site expert evaluators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that if AI assessment succeeds on the digital platform, "the certification of basic laparoscopic skills could be provided from anywhere in the world, without the need for expert evaluators available to perform the assessment". This would let trainees achieve proficiency certification without traveling to simulation centers or requiring synchronous video-conference assessment, addressing the scarcity of expert evaluators that limits expanding laparoscopic simulation training programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI algorithms achieving high agreement with expert evaluators across the training program's exercises, and platform modifications to automatically incorporate the algorithm outputs
#### Constraints
- The videos used for calibration and testing were all part of a standardized training program, so it is unknown if the algorithms can be applied to other training programs with similar results

### Target Learners
- surgery trainees in remote locations without immediate access to expert evaluators

### Target Learning Goals
- achieving proficiency certification in basic laparoscopic skills
