# Use visual and aural aids and varied, vivid lesson structures in listening lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends teachers update teaching methods and use visual and aural aids such as charts, pictures, flash cards, television, radio, slides and filmstrips for a more effective listening comprehension teaching process. It also recommends a non-punitive approach and varied, vivid, interesting lessons, and selecting a wide range of materials beyond textbooks.
