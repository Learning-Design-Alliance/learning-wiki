# Use standardized validated test data to argue for implementing BLS and AED courses in school curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that arguments presented to national governments and offices for mandating BLS and AED education in schools be backed by standardized data gathered with a validated instrument. They state that argumentation "need to be supported by reliable and validated data in order to gain their support", and that the validated test ensures that proposals to implement these courses in nationwide school curricula are credible and can be presented at legislative levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessments of schoolchildren's BLS and AED knowledge must use the validated standardized instrument rather than ad hoc questionnaires
#### Constraints
- Most countries still lack national policies on schoolchildren CPR education, and education in Europe is not centralized, so implementation depends on individual countries

### Target Learners
- Schoolchildren aged 12 years and older (K-12, elementary school)

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic life support and automated external defibrillator knowledge as part of compulsory school activities

## Claims

- [A 2-hour BLS and AED course with an interactive lecture and practical workshop significantly improved schoolchildren's knowledge, with the largest gain on how basic life support is correctly performed (r = 0,71 in Study 1)](../claims/bls-aed-course-improves-schoolchildren-knowledge.md) [+W]
- [The knowledge test showed measurement invariance across gender and school grade, with girls and ninth-graders scoring better at small effect sizes](../claims/bls-test-measurement-invariance-gender-grade.md) [+W]
- [Educometric item analysis identified and eliminated one inadequate question, and the revised test showed satisfactory difficulty and discrimination indices](../claims/educometric-item-analysis-revised-bls-test.md) [+W]
- [Previous attendance at a BLS or AED course had no significant effect on knowledge test scores, and the test is applicable regardless of baseline knowledge](../claims/prior-bls-course-no-effect-on-test-score.md) [+W]
