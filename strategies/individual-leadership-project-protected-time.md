# Require participants to complete an individual leadership project with a planning template and protected work time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Academy requires participants to complete individual projects and gives them dedicated time for this work, such as designing a year-long professional development program for paraprofessionals or expanding a co-teaching model districtwide. Organizers "provide them with a project-planning template, a process to use, and time to talk with us and each other about what their project is going to look like."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program organizers must supply a project-planning template, a process, and structured time for participants to plan and discuss their projects
#### Constraints
- Such projects are described as difficult or impossible for directors to tackle amid day-to-day job demands without the program's dedicated time

### Target Learners
- Experienced district-level special education administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying leadership learning to a concrete district improvement project
