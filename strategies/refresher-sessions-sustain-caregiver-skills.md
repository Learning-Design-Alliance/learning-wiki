# Provide regular refresher sessions and periodic reassessment to sustain caregiver emergency-response skills over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that to sustain and extend the benefits of simulation-based caregiver training, regular refresher sessions and follow-up programs should be offered. Periodic reassessment is proposed as a mechanism to ensure retention of critical skills and maintain caregiver readiness over time. This recommendation responds to the study's limitation that only short-term outcomes were assessed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing access to simulation facilities or equivalent training resources
- A schedule for periodic reassessment of caregiver skills
#### Constraints
- The study did not evaluate skill retention; longitudinal follow-up is needed to confirm the value of refresher sessions

### Target Learners
- Primary caregivers of children with Rett syndrome
- Caregivers of children with other high-risk conditions associated with sudden deterioration

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term retention of PBLS skills
- Sustained caregiver readiness for emergency response
