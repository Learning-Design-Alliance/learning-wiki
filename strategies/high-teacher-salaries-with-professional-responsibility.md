# Reward and develop high-quality teachers through substantially higher salaries plus increased professional responsibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy the report documents at TEP is to concentrate resources on the teaching force rather than on other inputs: teachers receive salaries of $125,000 along with substantial professional responsibility, funded within a standard public charter school budget. The report presents this personnel approach as the school's distinctive lever and associates it with consistently positive achievement impacts over the school's first four years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A school operating under standard public funding that reallocates its budget toward teacher compensation and responsibility
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one charter school; the report does not print causal decomposition of which personnel practices drove the gains

### Target Learners
- students in high-poverty urban public charter schools

### Target Learning Goals
- raising student achievement in math, English language arts, and science
