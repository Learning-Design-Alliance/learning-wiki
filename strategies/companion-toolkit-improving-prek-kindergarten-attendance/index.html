Pair attendance-improvement efforts with a companion toolkit for districts, schools, and early childhood providers¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Alongside the parent infographic, the publisher released a companion resource: "Strategies for Improving Attendance in Pre-Kindergarten and Kindergarten: Toolkit for Districts, Schools, and Early Childhood Providers," published November 21, 2019 by Martha Bleeker, Jaimie Grazi, and Felicia Hurwitz. The pairing suggests communicating attendance importance to parents while giving institutions practical strategies.
