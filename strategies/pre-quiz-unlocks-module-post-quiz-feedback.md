# Use ungraded pre-class quizzes to unlock module content and focus learning, paired with graded post-quizzes with feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before class, students complete a short ungraded quiz that gates access to the weekly module; the article notes "The quiz serves as a baseline knowledge assessment of the weekly content to assist students in focusing their learning." After class, students take a graded post-quiz in which they "obtain immediate feedback and the rationale for the answers," supporting formative assessment in the flipped cycle.
