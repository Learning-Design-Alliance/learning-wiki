# Extend discovery learning across subjects, school levels, adult education and teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
In its educational implications the article recommends broadening the use of discovery learning beyond the studied setting: 'Discovery learning can be used for learning different subjects and it can be extended to secondary level and higher secondary level.' It further suggests implementation in adult education, teacher education and alternative schools, and that late bloomers can be improved by using it. The recommendation rests on the study's finding of grammar achievement gains for standard V learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
