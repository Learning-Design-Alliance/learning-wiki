# Three-activity somatic pedagogy: watch, identify, and bodily replicate a digitized performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-step somatic pedagogy recipe for using a digitized performance in class: the experiment "included three primary activities" — watching the video, identifying actions and gestures relevant to physics concepts, and physically engaging (or replicating) and translating those actions and gestures with one's own body.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a digital performance video whose actions and gestures are relevant to physics concepts
- a room where students can stand up, move around, and provide explanations using their bodies
#### Constraints
- the article reports the process only for a teacher (A4) and three students in a seminar-type forum

### Target Learners
- education students
- small seminar groups

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding physics concepts through bodily replication
- assigning verbal explanations to embodied actions
