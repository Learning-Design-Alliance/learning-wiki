# Use structured online peer evaluation to reduce free-riding in cooperative group work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 5 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 5 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the MBCL platform, every student scores each teammate's individual contribution through a short Google Forms survey embedded in Blackboard, with results saved automatically to the instructor's Google Drive. The article states that "The adoption of peer evaluation was based on previous studies to reduce the free‐riding behavior, which takes place when an individual contributes so little to the teamwork, leaving the task for others to complete." Students can explain their reasoning for any unusually high or low scores given to teammates.
