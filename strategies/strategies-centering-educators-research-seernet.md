# Center educators in research by shifting power dynamics, including diverse educators, and structuring feedback loops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation enumerates three strategies for centering educators in research: to "Empower educators and intentionally shift power dynamics so that they have a voice, especially in research questions", to include educators with diverse backgrounds and expertise, and to structure feedback loops that transform knowledge, actions, or goals of all involved as studies move from abstract design to detailed plans. It presents these as its own recommendations from the Office Hours initiative.
