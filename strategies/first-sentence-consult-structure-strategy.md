# Teach the consultation call as a structured first sentence covering identity, diagnosis, stability, and purpose

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A communication recipe taught in debriefing for the simulated consultation call: students learned "introducing themselves, providing a diagnosis, conveying the stability of the patient, and stating the purpose of the call, all in the first sentence of the consult." Debriefing also emphasized keeping patients informed using easily understood language in a supportive, empathetic manner and asking whether they had additional questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
