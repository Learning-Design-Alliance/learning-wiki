# Structure teachable-agent dialogue around a five-stage help-seeking process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's design strategy is to guide students through structured help-seeking interactions by following "the five-stage help-seeking process": (1) recognizing the need for help, (2) deciding to seek assistance, (3) identifying a source, (4) employing strategies, and (5) responding to guidance. In Chat Melody this stages the dialogue so that learners first attempt analysis, then seek help from the agent, and finally respond to its reflective prompts, encouraging internalization of concepts through deliberate cognitive stages rather than absorbing AI-generated responses.
