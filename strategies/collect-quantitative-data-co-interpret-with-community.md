# As district-community teams continue implementing solutions, collect quantitative data aligned with team-defined outcomes and co-interpret the data with those closest to the challenge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's forward-looking recommendation is that ongoing solution implementation be paired with measurement and shared interpretation. The authors state: "we recommend that as district-community teams continue implementing their solutions, they collect quantitative data aligned with the outcomes and engage with those closest to the challenge to co-interpret the data." This extends the participant-perception evidence base with impact data while keeping community members central to evaluation, consistent with the report's view that using participant feedback fosters ownership and sustains initiatives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District-community teams continuing to implement their co-designed solutions
- Quantitative measures aligned with the team-defined outcomes
#### Constraints
- The report notes short-term and long-term impacts of the solutions, particularly for historically and systematically excluded students, are not yet understood

### Target Learners
- students furthest from opportunity
- historically and systematically excluded students in pilot districts

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating and refining equity-centered solutions to create powerful and inclusive learning environments
