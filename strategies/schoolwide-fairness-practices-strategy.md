# Foreground fairness and justice in schoolwide policy and practice to support adolescents with ADHD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends, from an applied perspective, that schools foreground fairness and justice in educational policy and practice, citing schoolwide interventions that can shift school climate including fairness perceptions. It highlights reducing exclusionary discipline in favor of proactive management strategies such as "establishing clear rules and expectations, using global and specific praise". The authors caution that the present study does not test these practices directly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions operate at the schoolwide climate level rather than as individualized fairness targets for single students
#### Constraints
- The present study does not test these practices directly; fairness is positioned within broader school-climate models rather than as a direct intervention target

### Target Learners
- early adolescents ages 10–12, particularly students with ADHD

### Target Learning Goals
- improved academic functioning, including fewer learning problems and more positive attitudes to school
