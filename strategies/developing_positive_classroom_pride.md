# Developing Positive Classroom Pride

## Description
Instilling pride is a powerful way to develop positive teacher-learner relationships. Classroom pride shapes identities that in turn drive behavior. Recognizing learner successes decreases the likelihood of negative pride and increases the likelihood of positive pride. As a classroom instructor, you should help learners take pride in their accomplishments and positive behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be sincere in their proclamations of pride in learners.
#### Constraints
- Must be modified for different age ranges. Children are intuitive regarding sincerity, and insincere comments will quickly backfire.
