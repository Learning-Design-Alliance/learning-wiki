# Extend hands-on and computer-application session durations and provide replication guidelines when organizing similar frontier-physics schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on observations during the school, the authors recommend extending experiment sessions: "The duration of each session was set to 90 minutes, but based on our observations, we would recommend extending this period to 2 hours in the future programs." They also recommend increasing time for the track-search task, and to support replication they made lecture videos and assessment data available and "prepared guidelines that can allow secondary education institutions to implement similar experimental setups for their own students".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the authors' released video recordings, assessment data, and setup guidelines
- Instructor and guide availability, including lodging with students to sustain a disciplined yet friendly atmosphere
#### Constraints
- Session-length recommendations are based on the authors' observations during this single school, not on a controlled comparison

### Target Learners
- High school students (11th and 12th grade) in outreach programs
- Secondary education institutions implementing similar setups

### Target Learning Goals
- Hands-on engagement with frontier particle-physics techniques
- Inquiry and experimental skills
