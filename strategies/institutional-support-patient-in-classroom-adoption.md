## Description
The article's implementation guidance: successful integration of real patients into classroom teaching depends on organizational infrastructure, not only pedagogy. The authors state that "Successful integration of patient-in-classroom teaching requires substantial institutional support, including sustainable pipelines for recruiting and compensating patient-instructors, faculty development for facilitating sessions, and logistical coordination." They recommend future implementation research on cost-effective protocols and scalability, and note these requirements may pose barriers in resource-constrained settings.
