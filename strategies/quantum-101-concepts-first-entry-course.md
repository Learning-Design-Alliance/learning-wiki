# Offer a concepts-focused, prerequisite-free Quantum 101 course as an entry point and recruiting tool

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends an introductory "Quantum 101" course with minimal math content, accessible to non-STEM students, to stimulate interest through concepts and applications and lower the barrier to entry. It states such courses "also function as recruiting tools due to their wide accessibility" and can be taught in community colleges, military schools, and institutions lacking advanced QISE curricula. Learning concepts first may build intuition connecting concepts and applications without advanced mathematical formalism.
