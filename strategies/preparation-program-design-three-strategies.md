# Design educator preparation around pedagogical alignment, tightly linked clinical experiences, and a developmental approach to educator learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that program design center on a coherent vision of whole child development, learning, and teaching, including "pedagogical alignment around a coherent vision of whole child development, learning, and teaching" so new teachers experience the strategies they are expected to use; "well-designed clinical experiences tightly linked to coursework" integrating theory and practice; and "a developmental approach to the development of educators" occurring experientially in supportive contexts. These strategies respond to the challenges of learning to teach and to adult learning needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program design must contend with the apprenticeship of observation, the problem of enactment, and the problem of complexity

- prospective teachers and school leaders in preparation programs

- enacting science of learning and development practices with students
