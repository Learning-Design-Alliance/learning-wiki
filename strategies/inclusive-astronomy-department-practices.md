# Departmental practices to broaden astronomy access: inclusive community, faculty and graduate student interaction, undergraduate research, and career expectancy communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend that departments and institutions facilitate the accessibility of astronomy at the collegiate level. Specifically, they suggest "promoting a more inclusive astronomy community, fostering interactions with astronomy faculty and graduate students, providing opportunities for undergraduate research, and communicating expectancy for astronomy-related future careers." These practices map onto the framework's constructs of belonging, socialization, recognition, competence, and career expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Departments with astronomy majors or active astrophysics research groups able to host undergraduate researchers and mentoring structures
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a qualitative study of ten undergraduates at two New York institutions, all from traditionally marginalized groups in astronomy

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students, particularly those from groups underrepresented in astronomy

### Target Learning Goals
- recruitment into the astronomy major
- persistence in astronomy research and careers

### Affordances
- Astronomy Identity Framework Undergraduates
