# Include specific training for physics teachers in education programs on designing prompts for effective use of large language models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that physics teacher education programs add explicit training in designing prompts for large language models: "It would be beneﬁcial to include speciﬁc training for physics teachers in education programs to design prompts for eﬀective use of large language models." In the study, ChatGPT output embedded tasks in a meaningful context only when users requested it, and participants rated output quality below neutral, making prompt skill the lever the authors identify for improving LLM-supported task development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prompt training should cover task-quality aspects that "needed to be explicitly prompted by the user of ChatGPT, such as the context of the task".
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on one randomized controlled trial with the January 30 version of ChatGPT 3.5, in a field the article describes as "quickly evolving" with models "likely to become more proﬁcient in such discipline-speciﬁc exercises as task development".

### Target Learners
- prospective physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- designing effective prompts for large language models in task development
- creating high-quality physics assessment tasks

## Claims
