# Use two-way clicker displays to pair students holding different conceptions and strengthen peer discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two-way clickers with LCD displays let students both send and receive data, such as acknowledgment of vote reception. The author proposes programming the displayed response "so that it pairs students of differi ng conceptions", relocating a student to sit beside a classmate holding a different conception. This would, in the article's words, have "the potential to change the quality of peer-to-peer di scussion and ultimately the effectiveness of PI in ways that are impossible with flashcards".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two-way clickers with LCD displays and instructor-side software that can compute and display pairing or relocation information
#### Constraints
- This is a proposed, untested use; the study did not implement or evaluate pairing

### Target Learners
- first-semester community college students in algebra-based physics

### Target Learning Goals
- higher-quality peer discussion
- resolving alternative conceptions
