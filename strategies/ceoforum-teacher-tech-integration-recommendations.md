# CEO Forum recommendations: prepare and certify teachers for technology integration across pre-service, licensure, and in-service career stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report issues a set of dated recommendations directed at educators, administrators, and business and community leaders. Schools of education should prepare new teachers to integrate technology into the curriculum, national accreditation standards should require this by 2000, faculty should receive tools and ongoing professional development by 2001, and licensure and certification should require technology integration proficiency by 2003. For current staff, every state should develop continuing-education standards by 2000, schools should establish long-term technology-related professional development plans and proficiency standards, and every professional development program should integrate technology into all training components. Policymakers should reward integration through hiring and performance evaluation standards, and corporations should collaborate with schools and colleges of education.
