# Creating a Common Language for Multimodal Composition

## Description
Empowering students with a shared vocabulary to analyze and discuss the multimodal composition process. This involves selecting one mode (linguistic, visual, audio, gestural, or spatial), analyzing examples, identifying its uses across different subjects and outside of school, and creating a class definition. The goal is to enrich discussions with common definitions and concrete examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Anchor chart, examples of different modes, time for discussion and analysis.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming if not managed effectively. Requires careful selection of examples to illustrate each mode. Relies on active participation and collaboration from all students.
