# Non-intensive Home Numeracy Environment (HNE) Intervention Program

## Description
Teachers can provide families information and suggestions for providing students more math exploration and play in their homes. They can also facilitate access to math games and manipulatives by allowing students to take home classroom materials. The goal is to encourage more frequent, personalized practice of math skills at home.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to math games and manipulatives, information about math exploration and play, communication between teachers and families
#### Constraints
- Requires family participation and support. May not be effective if families are not able to provide a supportive home numeracy environment.
