School-based counselling that targets irrational beliefs, attachment patterns and anxiety to support students' cognitive flexibility¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The author recommends that determination of variables predicting cognitive flexibility support professionals' applications in school-based psychological counselling services. Descriptive and practical studies on cognitive schemas focusing on flexibility and attachment of parents and children are proposed, in which individuals "can be provided with counselling services" and helped to develop corrective relationships, and variables increasing anxiety can be studied and intervened in to strengthen adjustment processes.
