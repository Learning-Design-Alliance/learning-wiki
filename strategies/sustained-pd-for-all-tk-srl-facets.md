# Strengthen all facets of teachers' SRL knowledge through sustained, theory-informed professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that teacher education and professional development explicitly target all three TK-SRL dimensions, since AK-SRL and PCK-SRL are under-examined and teachers' knowledge is unevenly developed. It emphasizes "the importance of sustained professional development to strengthen all facets of teachers' knowledge of SRL" and notes that developing robust CK-SRL requires "deliberate, theory-informed professional development and opportunities to apply SRL concepts in authentic teaching contexts". Clearer shared conceptualizations of SRL should underpin such programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development must be sustained rather than one-off
- Programs should provide opportunities to apply SRL concepts in authentic teaching contexts
#### Constraints
- The review notes empirical evidence that greater knowledge of SRL translates into more effective instructional support remains mixed and inconsistent

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers
- in-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teachers' content knowledge of SRL
- teachers' pedagogical content knowledge for promoting SRL
- teachers' assessment knowledge for diagnosing SRL
