# Pair a science culture venue with an active research programme so mediators are trained directly on live research data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends generalizing a sponsorship situation in which a working scientist continuously contributes to a planetarium's educational programme. At Vaulx-en-Velin, Hélène Courtois's contribution to the 'Un ciel de galaxies' project consists in 'former directement les médiateurs à l'analyse de ces catalogues et à l'actualité de sa recherche', so that the mediator can, with the public, reconstruct the evolution of the local Universe from galaxy positions and proper motions. This lets the venue constantly renew its databases of astronomical objects and present emerging knowledge in its production context, moving beyond one-directional deficit-model communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scientist actively involved in the researched domain, available to train mediators constantly as results evolve
#### Constraints
- The knowledge conveyed is unpublished and not yet stabilized, requiring ongoing researcher involvement

### Target Learners
- planetarium mediators and, through them, the visiting public

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding research in progress, here the mapping of dark and black energy densities within 100 Mpc
