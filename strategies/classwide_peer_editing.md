# Classwide Peer Editing

## Description
Classwide peer editing involves having students review and critique examples of student writing. The focus is on reviewing and critiquing examples of student writing, which can be real or mock examples displayed or distributed to the entire class. Students compliment the piece, from word choice to sentence structure and tone to organization, make specific suggestions about the piece, and point out objective grammar, spelling and continuity errors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires clear expectations for feedback, structured activities, and teacher guidance. Students need to be trained on how to give effective feedback and provide constructive criticism.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and monitoring to ensure both students are actively engaged and benefiting from the activity. Also, some students may struggle with giving or receiving feedback, requiring additional support.
