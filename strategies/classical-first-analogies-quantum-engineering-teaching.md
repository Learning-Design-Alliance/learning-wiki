# Introduce quantum topics through analogies with familiar classical engineering content, from classical control theory to Fourier filter design.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Across the Quantum Control and Quantum Devices & Computers courses, new quantum content follows familiar classical content: "Starting with the classical control theory has the benefit of first introducing key concepts in a familiar classical (non-quantum) setting". The filter function approach to noise, and linking hardware platforms back to the quantum harmonic oscillator, serve the same familiarity-building purpose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must already have the corresponding classical engineering background, such as control systems, circuits and signals, and digital signal processing.

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate quantum engineering students with electrical engineering backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- quantum feedback and stochastic control
- noise and dynamical decoupling in quantum systems
- hardware platforms for quantum computing
