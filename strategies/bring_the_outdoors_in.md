# Bring the Outdoors In

## Description
Allowing an indoor/outdoor space that becomes an extension of classroom learning. Spaces might include a door that rolls back or time to learn outside. This strategy focuses on having access to natural lighting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an outdoor space. Designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the outdoor environment.

### Target Learners

- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve engagement, improve test scores, reduce stress

### Affordances
- Promotes relaxation and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred activities. Provide a variety of activities that involve the outdoors. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more fun.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that access to nature can increase engagement, reduce stress, and improve academic performance. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
