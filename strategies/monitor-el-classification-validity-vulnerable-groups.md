# Regularly examine the validity of classification instruments and procedures, monitoring accuracy for student groups prone to classification errors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report directs states and districts to regularly examine the frequency, types, and causes of misclassification errors, with special attention to migrant students, students with interrupted formal education, Native American and Alaska Native students, and students with disabilities, who may be more prone to classification errors. Careful review of misclassifications may lead to changes in how the classification instrument is interpreted and used and in classification procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systematic collection and review of misclassification frequency, types, and causes
#### Constraints
- A larger proportion of students appearing to be misclassified should trigger a system-level review of classification criteria, procedures, or tools

### Target Learners
- Migrant students, students with interrupted formal education, Native American and Alaska Native students, and students with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate EL classification and appropriate service delivery
