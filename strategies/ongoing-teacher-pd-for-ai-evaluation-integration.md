# Allocate ongoing professional development so teachers learn to evaluate and integrate AI systems appropriately

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The brief recommends continuous training covering equitable practice evaluation, effective integration, and data awareness. It states districts should "Allocate training dollars to incorporate continuous professional development for teachers to better understand and inform them about AI systems, ethical practices, risks, and benefits," supported by administrators.
