# Planned validation pathway: IRT, factor analysis, student and faculty interviews, and growth-valuing scoring

## Description
The authors outline next steps for developing the PIQL: item response theory with dichotomous and nominal response scoring, exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis to ensure the desired balance of PQL components, additional interviews with undergraduates and faculty, and novel scoring techniques "to value growth in students' reasoning skills, not just mastery of the topic." The target is good psychometric parameters with breadth and depth of content, completable in about 30 minutes.

## Design Implications
