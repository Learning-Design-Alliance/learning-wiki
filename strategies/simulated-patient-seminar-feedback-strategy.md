# Supervised small-group seminars with simulated patients for communicative-skill training with patient-view feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the online e-case, cases are presented in supervised seminars where students train communicative skills, and psychotherapeutic consequences are addressed in supervised small-group seminars with simulated patients. The stated advantage is an "affectively wholesome and secure learning environment" in which students can try the demands of working with severely disturbed patients and "get feedback on their performance especially from the patient's view given by a sufficiently trained simulated patient". Feedback reflection is written into the student portfolio.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficiently trained simulated patients
- supervised small-group seminar settings
- portfolio documentation of feedback reflection
#### Constraints
- Described for psychiatric and clinical case topics in the Vienna medical curriculum

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- communicative skills
- clinical reasoning
- metacognition through feedback
