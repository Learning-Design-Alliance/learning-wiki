# Use structured VR simulator exercises progressing from simple shapes to complete tooth preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a sequence of VR exercises beginning with "Preparation of simple geometrical shapes" designed "to prepare the students for more complex preparations in the next steps", followed by simple patterns, outline form, guided preparation, and complete preparation. This progression breaks down complex skills into manageable components, consistent with micro-learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR simulator supporting multiple exercise types with increasing complexity
- Mentor review of saved student performance files for feedback
#### Constraints
- Faculty members unfamiliar with VR technology may influence the mentoring process and effectiveness of feedback provided during training

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- cavity preparation technique
- spatial cognition in tooth preparation
