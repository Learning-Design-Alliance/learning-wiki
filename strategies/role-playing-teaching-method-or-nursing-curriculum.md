# Use role-playing as a teaching method for clinical-skills training of operating room nursing students and revise the curriculum to include empathy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that "the role-playing technique should be used as a teaching method for training operating room nursing students in clinical skills, since it is thought to be a useful, engaging, and cost-effective technique." They also suggest changes to the curriculum of operating room nursing students to familiarize them with the concept of empathy and encourage attention to the human dimension of patient care in the operating room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum change to make room for empathy education
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is based on a single quasi-experimental study in one Iranian school; the cost-effectiveness judgment is the authors' stated view

### Target Learners
- Operating room nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Empathy with patients
- Clinical communication skills
