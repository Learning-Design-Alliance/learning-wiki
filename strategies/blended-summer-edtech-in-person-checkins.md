# Blend virtual ed-tech practice with regular in-person meetings and check-ins in summer learning programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that districts deliver summer ed-tech programs as structured, blended models combining virtual practice with live interaction. Teachers found it nearly impossible to monitor engagement and motivate young learners without regular in-person meetings, and one teacher suggested weekly physical check-ins to hold students and parents accountable and distribute incentives. The authors write that a "blended approach that combines virtual practice with live interaction and collaboration may limit summer learning loss."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular in-person meetings or weekly physical check-ins with an educator
- Educators and tech staff available to help students set up accounts before the school year ends
#### Constraints
- Entirely virtual and asynchronous programs made it difficult to resolve technical challenges and motivate students

### Target Learners
- K-8 students at risk of summer learning loss

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining math and reading skills over summer break
