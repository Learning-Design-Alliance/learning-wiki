## Description
For the supportive-information component, the chapter recommends hypermedia systems as the delivery environment. The abstract states: "They may study supportive information in hypermedia systems, where principles facilitate a process of elaboration and mindful abstraction." Hypermedia design for supportive information should thus be chosen to foster elaboration and mindful abstraction by the learner.
