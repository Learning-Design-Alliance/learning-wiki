# Optimizing Classroom Space

## Description
Overcrowded classrooms are linked to increased aggression and decreased engagement. Classrooms with ample space support effective teaching and 21st-century learning skills such as teamwork, problem-solving, and communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate classroom space, flexible seating arrangements, private study areas.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant facility upgrades, may not be feasible in all environments.
