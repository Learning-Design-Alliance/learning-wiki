# Attention Breaks

## Description
Practice attentive behavior during non-crucial times during the school day. At periodic intervals, have practice attention breaks using a timer or an app on the phone. Signal when the work period begins, and have the child mark whether he/she was paying attention. This can help train a student's brain to understand what attention looks like, and how often he/she is tempted to disengage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Timer or attention tracking app, designated work periods, a method for marking attention (e.g., checklist, digital tool).
#### Constraints
- May be disruptive if not managed effectively. Some learners may not accurately self-assess their attention.

- Track the frequency of self-marked attention lapses. Monitor the improvement of attentive behavior over time. Collect feedback from learners on their self-awareness.

## Impact
- Attention breaks can significantly improve self-awareness and attentive behavior, especially for learners who need structured practice. They provide a method for understanding and reducing attention lapses.
