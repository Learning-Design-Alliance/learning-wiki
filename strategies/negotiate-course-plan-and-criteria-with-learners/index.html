Involve learners in negotiating course plans and evaluation criteria¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that learners be involved in decision making about the course plan and evaluation criteria, sharing the initiative with the instructor so learners take an active, participatory position in language discovery and production. It advises that "instructors allocate time to have sessions of negotiation with the participants before tailoring a course design and assessment type for that particular group."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors must allocate time for negotiation sessions with participants before tailoring course design and assessment.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Tertiary-level language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Learner autonomy and engagement in language discovery and production
