> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that construction of the installation itself be a shared student activity rather than a pre-built display. It states it is 'highly recommended to involve the students in all steps of the building of this installation,' making assembly part of the learning experience and enabling collaborative work within the model.
