# Implement targeted training strategies plus standardized protocols and institutional support to improve diabetic foot management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining continuing professional education with organizational change: targeted training strategies, standardized clinical guidelines, unified working protocols, and specific units with qualified personnel across health areas. The authors conclude that "implementing targeted training strategies and addressing organizational barriers in order to improve diabetic foot management in Primary Care" is essential, alongside integrating evidence-based care models and institutional support to foster nursing autonomy and confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support through training, standardized clinical guidelines, and unified working protocols
#### Constraints
- Barriers are not only individual but also systemic; organizational issues such as absence of standardized protocols and poor interprofessional communication limit effectiveness

### Target Learners
- practising Primary Care nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- updated diabetic foot knowledge
- practical advanced wound care skills
- clinical decision-making confidence
