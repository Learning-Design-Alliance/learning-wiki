# Adopt validated clinical simulation scenarios as a perioperative nursing training resource

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adopting clinical simulation with published, validated scenarios as an educational strategy for surgical wound care training, arguing that validation "confere rigor científico ao cenário" (confers scientific rigor on the scenario). It proposes simulation for active learning, technical skill development and critical decision-making in realistic scenarios, and suggests its potential for continuing education in different care settings provided new validation is carried out.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scenario previously developed and validated by experts
- Simulation laboratory or center infrastructure and materials
- Professionals trained to mediate the scenario stages
#### Constraints
- The authors state its use in continuing education in different care settings requires new validation for that purpose
- Implementation requires investment in material resources and training of professionals

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- nursing professionals in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical wound management and complication prevention
- technical skills for wound assessment and dressing
- clinical decision-making
