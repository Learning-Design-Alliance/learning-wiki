# Scrutinize student-generated learning issues and self-study topics to monitor what content PBL students actually cover

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends comparing student-generated learning issues with the topics students actually cover during self-study as a monitoring practice. It concludes that "scrutinizing student-generated learning issues and topics covered during self-study may provide information about what content is covered by students in tutorial groups", giving tutors and course designers a window into self-directed learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Procedures for eliciting learning issues and for measuring self-study topics (such as the Topic Checklist) must be in place
#### Constraints
- The discrepancy between the measurements shows learning issues are not the sole source of self-study decisions, so the information is partial

### Target Learners
- Students in problem-based learning tutorial groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Monitoring and guiding self-directed learning content coverage
