# Improve blended in-situ retraining by clarifying certification criteria, pre-training non-technical skills, refining the checklist, and addressing in-situ logistics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the observed certification drop and focus group confusion, the authors recommend concrete program adjustments: "clearly communicating the certification criteria and distinguishing between training feedback and formal assessment, providing more focused training on non-technical skills (NTS) before certification, developing a more effective checklist to evaluate both technical and non-technical skills, and addressing logistical challenges during in-situ simulations by adjusting the layout or offering better guidance on managing real-world obstacles." These steps target the mechanisms the authors link to the lower certification rate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A distinction between formative feedback and certifying assessment must be communicated before simulation sessions
- Facilitators and an ad hoc checklist covering technical and non-technical items are needed
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a retrospective, single-province study; the authors call for RCT and longitudinal validation

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in blended in-situ CPR retraining programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Technical CPR/AED skills
- Non-technical skills including teamwork and communication
