# Finalize secondary natural-science study with a short generalizing integrative course, treating integration as a special case of relation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper reports Usova's recommendation that a "short generalizing integrative course should finalize the study of natural sciences at secondary school" and that integrative lessons are one way of actuating interdisciplinarity. Conceptually, the paper stresses that in Usova's version of interdisciplinary didactics integration is a special (particular) case of relation, and that Usova always clearly distinguished the concepts 'Integrative' and 'Integrated' in disputes on integration issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- the integrative course comes after, not instead of, the study of the separate natural-science disciplines
#### Constraints
- the paper presents this as Usova's own opinion about educational integration, not as a tested result

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- generalizing and unifying natural-science knowledge at the end of secondary school
