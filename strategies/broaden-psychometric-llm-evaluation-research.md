# Broaden instruments, methods, and expert judgment in psychometric LLM evaluation research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation for the emerging field of applying psychometrics to LLM evaluation is to extend the approach beyond their two case studies. They write: "Future research should broaden the scope of assessment instruments, expand the range of analytical methods employed, and incorporate human expert judgment in the interpretation of results." This positions their EFA-based workflow as a starting point rather than a complete evaluation methodology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to assessment instruments and human response data, plus domain experts able to interpret factor structures in context.
#### Constraints
- The article notes proprietary LLM use with non-public materials risks data leakage or future contamination, which constrains such evaluations.

- educational measurement researchers
