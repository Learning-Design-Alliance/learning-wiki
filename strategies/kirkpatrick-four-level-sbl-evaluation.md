## Description
The evaluation phase employs the Kirkpatrick model "across its four levels—reaction, learning, behavior, and results," each with specified methods: post-simulation satisfaction surveys, OSCE with rubric scored by two independent raters, longitudinal clinical follow-up for behavior, and post-course audits with error rates and complication rates for results.
