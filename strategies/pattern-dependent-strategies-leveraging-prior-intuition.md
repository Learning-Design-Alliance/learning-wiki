# Pattern-dependent strategies that revise classical concepts and leverage prior intuition according to the regularity pattern involved

## Description
The article's forward-looking design recommendation is that the dynamic-frame representation of conceptual trajectories be used to devise teaching strategies matched to the specific regularity pattern by which a concept changes. As the authors state, "the identification of the three patterns allow us to suggest pattern- dependent strategies to revise classical concepts and build a consistent knowledge of ir quantum version, leveraging prior intuition according to the pattern involved". The strategies aim at a productive use of prior intuition rather than only at remediating difficulties, and the frame representation may work as a guide to curricular design.
