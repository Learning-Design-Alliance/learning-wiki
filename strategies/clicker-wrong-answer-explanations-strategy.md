# Use clicker questions with instructor-elicted explanations of popular wrong answers to build coherence-seeking epistemologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the mathematical sensemaking large lecture, the instructors use peer-instruction-style clicker questions and peer discussion to generate discussion of ideas. For problems where responses do not converge, "the instructor will elicit explanations for the two or three most popular choices." The classroom motto is to figure out "not only why the right reasoning is right, but also why the wrong explanations are wrong," giving students experience resolving inconsistencies in the service of more coherent understanding.
