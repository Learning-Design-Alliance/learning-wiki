# Extend self-practice time and delay testing to strengthen AI-guided ultrasound training research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that subsequent research give participants a more comprehensive introduction to the AI tool and use larger samples with longer gaps between training and testing, since "a longer time gap between the initial training and the proficiency test may find more significant value to the AI tool". They note comparable studies granted participants multiple tests over an extended duration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Larger sample sizes and a longer interval between initial training and proficiency testing
#### Constraints
- The short self-training session might have undermined participants' grasp of the guidance tool's practical use; long-term effects and substitution for in-person guidance remain untested

### Target Learners
- novice cardiac ultrasound operators in medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of challenging cardiac ultrasound views
