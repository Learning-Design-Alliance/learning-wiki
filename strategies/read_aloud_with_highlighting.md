# Read Aloud with Highlighting

## Description
Read aloud features, often combined with word and sentence highlighting, provide auditory support while reading digital text. This can help with decoding, visual impairments, and second language acquisition. Highlighting helps learners focus their attention on the text as it is being read.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A digital device (computer, smartphone, tablet) with read aloud software or apps installed. Digital text content in a compatible format. Headphones or speakers for audio output.
#### Constraints
- The quality of speech synthesis can vary. Effectiveness depends on the clarity and accuracy of the highlighting. May not be suitable for all types of content.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Individuals with decoding difficulties, visual impairments, English language learners, or those who benefit from multisensory learning. Useful across all age groups and educational levels.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve decoding skills, enhance focus, support visual impairments, aid second language acquisition, promote multisensory learning

### Affordances
- Provides auditory reinforcement of written text. Improves reading fluency and comprehension. Enhances focus and reduces distractions. Supports phonological awareness and vocabulary development.

### Personalization
- Users can select from different voices, adjust the reading speed, and customize the highlighting style. Some tools offer options for sentence and word highlighting.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe improvements in reading fluency, accuracy, and comprehension. Collect user feedback on the usefulness of highlighting. Measure the time it takes to complete reading tasks with and without read aloud and highlighting.

## Impact
- Research indicates that read aloud with highlighting can significantly improve reading outcomes for students with decoding difficulties, visual impairments, and English language learners.
