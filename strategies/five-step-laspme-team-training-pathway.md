# Five-step structured training pathway for a surgical team adopting LASPME

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before performing LASPME on patients, the article describes a five-step team preparation sequence: extensive experience in open and combined thoracoscopic-laparoscopic minimally invasive esophagectomy, study of the published single-port mediastinoscopic dissection technique, a site visit to the originating team, repeated video study, and cadaver-lab simulation. The article states "The fifth step is to learn the cadaver dissection performed in the cadaver lab and simulate the surgical procedure." The whole process was supervised by the hospital ethics committee with an external expert evaluating and giving feedback before the first human case.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All staff must undergo rigorous training beforehand, and the team must already have extensive experience in open esophagectomy and thoracoscopic combined laparoscopic MIE
#### Constraints
- The training was supervised by the hospital's Ethics Committee with external expert evaluation, as described for this single center

### Target Learners
- surgical teams adopting LASPME, starting from experienced minimally invasive esophagectomy surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- acquiring the technical proficiency to perform laparoscopic-assisted single-port mediastinoscopic esophagectomy safely
