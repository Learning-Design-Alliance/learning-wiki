# Teach functions with the geometric functions approach and provide teachers in-service training in it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the geometric functions approach increased achievement goal motivation, the article recommends that "mathematics teachers can teach function s using the geometric functions approach since the students motivated for achievement goal tend to become more successful." It further recommends that "the Ministry of Education may organize in -service trainings for mathematics teachers to disseminate geometric functions approach." The strategy pairs the approach with dynamic mathematics software such as GeoGebra used as a presentation tool on interactive whiteboards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dynamic mathematics software (GeoGebra) and interactive whiteboards, since the school had no computer lab and the software was used as a visual presentation tool
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single quasi-experimental study in one Turkish high school; the authors recommend repeating the study at different schools and cultures to improve generalizability

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade high school mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing achievement goal motivation toward mathematics in the functions unit
