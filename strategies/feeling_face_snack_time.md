# Feeling Face Snack Time

## Description
Learners pull a 'feeling face' card and eat their snack while showing the matching emotion. This playful activity connects emotions with physical actions and can facilitate conversations about how different emotions look and feel.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to snacks and 'feeling face' cards. A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be comfortable expressing emotions publicly. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Target Learning Goals

- Enhance emotional expression, improve emotional awareness, promote positive associations with emotional expression

### Affordances
- Connects emotional expression with a familiar routine (snack time). Promotes a positive and playful approach to learning about emotions.

### Personalization
- Emotions can be tailored to suit the group's needs and interests. Different snacks can be used.
