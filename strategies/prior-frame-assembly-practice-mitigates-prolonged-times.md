# Practice frame assembly outside the operating room to mitigate prolonged procedure times for trainees

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the conclusion, the authors recommend that "Prior frame assembly practice outside the operating room could mitigate prolonged procedure times" for frame-based biopsies performed by trainees. This addresses the finding that residents required significantly longer operation and anesthesia times for frame-based cases. The recommendation is a forward-looking suggestion rather than a tested manipulation in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to stereotactic frame systems for rehearsal outside the operating room
#### Constraints
- Proposed in the conclusion without direct testing in this study

### Target Learners
- Neurosurgery residents in postgraduate year 1–7

### Target Learning Goals
- Efficient frame-based stereotactic biopsy performance
