Teachers should adjust writing assignments to students' learning styles, with the VAK model as one suitable option¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 against) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

From the findings and theory, the article's implication is that "language teacher or class teachers should concern on learning models that can improve students' skills in writing and adjust writing assignments to be given by paying attention to the learning styles of students". The VAK model is named as suitable for language learning about writing because it offers flexibility across visual, auditory, and kinesthetic styles, and teachers are urged to innovate in models, methods, and media suited to student characteristics.
