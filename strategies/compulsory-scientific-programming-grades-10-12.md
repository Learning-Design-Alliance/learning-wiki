# Introduce compulsory scientific programming in Grades 10-12 connected to other science subjects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends urgently introducing "a compulsory scientiﬁc programming subject in Grades 10 to 12 grades", "using languages such as Python, Octave or Julia", and connected to other subjects, especially Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics. This follows the observation that Portuguese students are, on average, far less familiar with scientific programming than their foreign peers, and that the current 'Computational Applications' subject is dismissed by the author as a waste of time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers able to teach coding and scientific programming; the article notes most secondary science teachers are in their 50s or 60s with little computing literacy and none in coding
#### Constraints
- The article names as bottlenecks the 'teaching to the test approach' and the reluctance of teacher unions to any kind of change

### Target Learners
- students in Grades 10 to 12 of secondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific programming skills connected to physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics
