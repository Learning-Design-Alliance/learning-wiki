Provide a pattern and purpose for listening so students identify needed information beforehand¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's concluding pedagogical recommendation is that "teachers should provide a pattern and purpose for listening so that students will become aware of the specific information they need before listening". This is implemented in the planning stage through pre-questioning forms of advance organizers and having students write their ideas about the topic before listening. The article also recommends that curriculum designers incorporate metacognitive strategy instruction into course books.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers design pre-listening activities that make the topic and purpose explicit before the oral text is played

  • Recommended on the basis of a single-term, single-class study; the article notes attitude, motivation and learning styles were not considered
