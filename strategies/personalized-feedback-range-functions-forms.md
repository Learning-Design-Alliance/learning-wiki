# Provide personalized feedback at different points of the learning process using a range of feedback functions and forms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that "personalized learning requiring feedback at different points of the learning process that utilizes a range of feedback functions and forms and, most of all, employs contextualization and a situated approach". This strategy responds to the COVID-19 shift to remote teaching, where feedback with a supportive function is essential. It includes providing diagnostic, formative, and summative assessments and one-to-one feedback using online technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must perform all teaching roles online and provide feedback remotely
- Online technologies offering ways for teachers to provide one-to-one feedback
#### Constraints
- In large classes, teachers' heavy workload gives priority to learning outcome-related feedback rather than feedback for social support

### Target Learners
- higher education students learning remotely

### Target Learning Goals
- learning improvement
- social-emotional support
