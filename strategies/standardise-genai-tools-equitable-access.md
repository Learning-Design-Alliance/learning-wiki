# Standardise the GenAI tools permitted in assessments, or limit advanced tools, to maintain equitable access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (3 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because access to GenAI tools varies, with some students using more advanced or paid models, the article recommends institutions consider standardising the tools permitted in assessments or limiting certain advanced tools to maintain a level playing field, noting particular equity concerns in the Global South. The article warns that "Access to GenAI tools can vary significantly among students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must address access disparity when integrating GenAI into assessments, for example by standardising permitted tools.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that as new GenAI tools continue to emerge, addressing access disparity in a practical manner has become a significant challenge.

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- students in the Global South with limited access to paid GenAI tools

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable participation in GenAI-inclusive assessment
