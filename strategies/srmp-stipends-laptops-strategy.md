# Fund stipends and laptops to remove financial barriers to participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The author recommends paying student stipends of $1,000–1,500, based on roughly 100 hours at a minimum wage of $10–15 an hour, and procuring laptops for students. This removes "two major barriers to participation," since some students cannot afford a computer and some must choose between the program and after-school work. Funds came from the City of Cambridge ($15,000 a year), Cambridge Rotary, foundations, and professional associations.
