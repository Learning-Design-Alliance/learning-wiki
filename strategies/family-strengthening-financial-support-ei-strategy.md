# Strengthen family systems and provide financial support to foster student emotional intelligence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that "Programs strengthening family structures and enhancing parental support are recommended, as students with married parents were more likely to exhibit higher EI." They further recommend that "Financial stability or support should be provided to students, as increased allowances were associated with better EI outcomes," and that intervention programs focus on improving EI, especially for individuals with psychiatric morbidity. They also suggest mixed-method designs for deeper insight into the interplay between EI, family dynamics and mental well-being.
