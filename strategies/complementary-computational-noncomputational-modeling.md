# Design complementary computational and non-computational modeling activities to support teacher adoption of computational modeling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend pairing computational modeling platforms with physical and embodied modeling so students both generate the phenomenon in the real world and design measures of change, such as using video analysis to design step-size measures. They state that "designing complementary forms of computational and non-computational modeling is critical for enabling teacher-adoption and appropriation of computational modeling in the K12 science curricula". In the study, the teacher initiated making things concrete with material forms and co-designed these activities with the research team.
