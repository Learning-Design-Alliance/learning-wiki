Use complex storytelling in spatial heritage games to keep players concentrated on narrative content and discourage mechanical task completion¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that complex storytelling in spatial games for culture be further exploited and evaluated, since this "inherent in cultural information, element can keep players concentrated on the narrative content of the game and discourage the mechanical carrying out of learning tasks". They suggest studying games based on enjoyment of a complex narrative while learning emerges as a side effect. The review found complex storytelling is rarer (8 games, 23.52%) than simple back-stories (15 games, 44.11%).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Narrative tightly interconnected with missions, presenting cultural elements together with their causal and temporal relationships

Constraints¶

  • According to the authors, citing Bopp (2008), designing educational games with complex narratives and multidimensional characters is still a challenge

Target Learners¶

  • cultural tourists
  • secondary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • deep understanding of facts and concepts
  • contextualization of cultural elements

Related Strategies¶

  • Tighten the connection of spatial heritage games with formal teaching and study their effects on learning performance, motivation of weak students, and attitudes
