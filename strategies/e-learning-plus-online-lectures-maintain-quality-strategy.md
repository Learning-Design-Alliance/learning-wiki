# Combine an e-learning application with online lectures and artificial-teeth practice to maintain clinical course quality when face-to-face teaching is disrupted

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
During the COVID-19 pandemic, the department moved lectures and seminars entirely online via video communication software, kept patient treatments in person under a hygiene protocol, and supplemented the traditional curriculum with the e-learning application and artificial-teeth practice. The authors conclude that "the quality of education may be maintained by implementing e-learning to compensate for face-to-face teaching", easing concerns that endodontic education suffers during disruptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-coordinated e-learning application combined with online teaching and practice on artificial teeth; continued in-person patient treatments
#### Constraints
- Performing root canal treatments on real patients remains crucial; professional staff and offline lessons cannot be completely replaced; students noted disadvantages of fully online teaching such as lack of communication

### Target Learners
- dental students in clinical endodontic courses during teaching disruptions

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining quality of first real-patient root canal treatment performance
