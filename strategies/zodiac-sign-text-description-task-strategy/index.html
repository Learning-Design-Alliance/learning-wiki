Strategies

Use personality-description writing tasks based on authentic texts to teach adjectives¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A concrete implementable recipe from the study: each session, learners receive a short authentic text (here, about one of the 12 zodiac signs), look unknown adjectives up in their dictionaries, and then "write a short description using the learned adjectives" of a classmate or family member born under the same sign. This links new adjectives to personal, meaningful content and requires productive use of the target vocabulary.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Printed authentic texts containing the target adjectives, one per session
  • Learners have access to dictionaries to look up unknown words

Constraints¶

  • Was implemented with Iranian intermediate EFL learners in intact classes over 12 sessions on zodiac-sign texts

Target Learners¶

  • Intermediate EFL learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Learning and productive use of adjectives

Claims¶

  • Iranian intermediate EFL learners who learned adjectives through authentic tasks outperformed learners who studied dictionary extracts on a vocabulary post-test [+W]
