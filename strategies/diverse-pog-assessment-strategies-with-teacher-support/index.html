Support the shift from standardized testing toward diverse assessment strategies—performance-based assessments, portfolios, and experiential learning—with teacher training, resource allocation, and accountability flexibility¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief calls for policies supporting "a more diverse set of assessment strategies— such as performance-based assessments, portfolios, and experiential learning" so students are evaluated on the full range of POG competencies. It states this transition "requires significant shifts in teacher training, resource allocation, and flexibility in state accountability measures," and that states and districts must collaboratively build systems and give teachers tools and professional development to administer and score these assessments.
