Combine GAI tools with traditional methods in a hybrid approach to balance efficient collaboration and critical evaluation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends a hybrid approach in which GAI tools streamline collaborative production while traditional methods preserve deeper critical engagement. The authors observe that "traditional tools could foster deeper critical thinking and more independent decision-making by requiring students to manually engage with each stage of problem-solving process," and conclude that "a hybrid approach, combining both GAI tools and traditional methods, may offer the best balance between efficient collaboration and critical evaluation." This strategy assigns GAI tools to content generation and feedback tasks while reserving evaluation and decision stages for unaided collaborative deliberation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to both GAI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Midjourney, Runway) and traditional digital tools (e.g., Photoshop, After Effects, Premiere Pro)
  • Instructional design that deliberately reserves evaluation and decision stages for manual, unaided collaborative deliberation

Constraints¶

  • The article presents this as an interpretation of the finding that the control group also improved significantly; the hybrid approach itself was not directly tested in the study

Claims¶

  • Interviews revealed six themes: enhanced efficiency, improved quality, facilitated reflection, increased interaction, improved collaboration, and concerns about over-reliance [+W]
  • University students held positive attitudes toward GAI in DST creation but expressed moderate concern about over-reliance and low concern about reduced social interaction [+W]
  • Excessive dependence on AI may reduce human interaction and dehumanize adult education [+W]
  • Students' main AI challenges are content reliability and depth, and most expect AI to serve as tutor, creative partner, and critical thinking partner rather than mere content generator [+W]
