# Optimize Classroom Size and Space

## Description
Overcrowded classrooms are linked to increased aggression and decreased engagement and learning in students. Classrooms with ample space are more conducive to providing appropriate learning environments. Adequate space allows for reconfiguring seating arrangements to facilitate teamwork, problem-solving, and communication. Creating private study areas reduces visual and auditory interruptions. Optimizing classroom size and space promotes student engagement and learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Larger classrooms, flexible seating, small learning centers, private study areas
#### Constraints
- May be limited by building design and budget constraints.
