# Increase situational interest in interprofessionalism through novel elements, interprofessional PBL, training wards and institutional culture change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address low engagement among some students, the authors propose increasing situational interest, hypothesizing that repeated experiences in an environment arousing situational interest can develop into individual interest. Proposed measures include integrating interprofessional modules with other healthcare students, interprofessional problem-based learning on patient cases, establishing interprofessional training wards, and promoting a culture of interprofessional collaboration through reducing hierarchies, shared learning spaces and interprofessional networks.
