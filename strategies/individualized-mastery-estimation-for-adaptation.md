# Enhance intelligent tutoring systems by individualized mastery estimation to guide instructional adaptation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends exploiting individualized knowledge estimates to enhance intelligent tutoring systems' mastery decisions. Its stated implication is "the ability to enhance existing intelligent tutoring systems to more accurately estimate when a student has reached mastery of a skill." The authors also discuss "Adaptation of instruction based on individualized knowledge and learning speed" as well as open research questions for exploiting student and skill information in user models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An intelligent tutoring system using an individualizable knowledge tracing model whose parameter estimates can feed mastery decisions
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as implication and discussion rather than as an intervention tested with learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- Students using intelligent tutoring systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Skill mastery estimation and adaptive instruction based on individualized knowledge and learning speed
