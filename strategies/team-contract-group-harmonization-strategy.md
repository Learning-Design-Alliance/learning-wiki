# Require a team contract at semester start to safeguard against group-work failures in ILB projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because ILB groups stay together across all block courses until semester end, the article recommends requiring each group to submit a team contract provided to them at the start of the semester. The authors call this "an effective and proven strategy for group harmonization" applied in other Sheridan engineering courses where teamwork is central, such as the Technical Reports and Presentations course. Groups of three or four students elect a designated team leader to facilitate interaction with instructors, and dysfunctional groups intervene with the ILB coordinator.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same group of students registered in similar sections of all ILB courses
- Groups of 3 or 4 students with an elected team leader
- ILB coordinator available to intervene when cohesion or coordination fails
#### Constraints
- The article notes groups remain prone to challenges such as members not actively participating and fragmented efforts despite this safeguard

### Target Learners
- first-year engineering students working in design teams

### Target Learning Goals
- group harmonization
- conflict resolution
- effective collaboration on engineering design projects
