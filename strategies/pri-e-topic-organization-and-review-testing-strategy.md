# Organize PRI-E presentation topics thematically and add basic-knowledge review and testing to offset exam weaknesses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address PRI-E's initial flaws, the authors reorganized presentations so that all participants delivered presentations related to one shared topic, with PBL showing clinical manifestations and latest classification, CBL sharing real cases, and EBL summarizing treatment-selective criteria. They also state: "We plan to include a certain proportion of basic knowledge review and testing in future PRI-E activities to compensate for this deficiency." These adjustments target the mode's low single-class efficiency and lack of examination guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- staff time to accumulate experience with the combined modes before efficiency rises
- systematic topic setting so presentation content correlates with the topic
#### Constraints
- the review-and-testing addition is a stated future plan, not an implemented or tested component

### Target Learners
- postgraduate stomatology students in oral and maxillofacial oncology rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering basic knowledge for examinations
- organized progression through PBL, CBL and EBL content
