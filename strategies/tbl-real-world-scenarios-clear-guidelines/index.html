Integrate TBL with real-world clinical scenarios and clear discussion guidelines¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that educators integrate TBL with real-world clinical scenarios and provide clear discussion guidelines to optimize outcomes. This addresses reported ambiguities in multiple-choice questions and the need for clearer guidelines during group discussions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Real-world clinical scenarios for application exercises
  • Clear discussion guidelines for group work

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Health professions students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Ethical decision-making
  • Application of ethical principles

Claims¶

  • The closer training content is to authentic clinical scenarios, the smoother its translation into practice [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Scaffold method choice by student progression: CBL early, transitioning to PBL and TBL
  • Use hybrid simulations combining real-world materials with low-cost simulators to enable realistic, interactive scenarios at reduced cost
