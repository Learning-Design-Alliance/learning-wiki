Teachers should introduce learning strategies, find out what learners already use, and help them monitor and evaluate strategy use across tasks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In the conclusion the article recommends, citing Chamot and O'Malley (1987), that language teachers introduce the various learning strategies to students, find out what strategies the learners are already using, help them monitor the use of strategies based on different tasks and instructions, and help them evaluate the effectiveness of using these strategies. The article presents this as a way for teachers to counter the difficulties students are likely to face and to avoid failing to monitor students' comprehension when proper strategies are not employed.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The article frames this for foreign language reading instruction, where teachers who do not employ proper strategies fail to monitor comprehension
