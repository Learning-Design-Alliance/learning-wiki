# Design emotion awareness tools to support both emotion-oriented and appraisal-oriented regulation strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article draws design implications for emotion awareness tools from its analysis of students' emotions and appraisals. As the abstract states, the authors "draw implications for the design of emotion awareness tools to support emotion regulation processes," discussing "the types of emotion-oriented and appraisal-oriented regulation strategies used in learning contexts." The strategy is therefore to build tools that surface not only emotions but also their antecedents and appraisals, enabling both emotion-focused and appraisal-focused regulation.
