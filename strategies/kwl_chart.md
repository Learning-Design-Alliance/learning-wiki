# KWL Chart

## Description
A KWL chart is a graphic organizer used to structure a lesson by tapping into what students already Know (K), what they Want to know (W), and what they have Learned (L) about a topic. Before the lesson, students fill out the 'K' and 'W' columns; after the lesson, they complete the 'L' column. This makes students think about what they already know, increasing interest and focus, and helps organize new information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-designed KWL chart (paper or digital); pens or pencils; access to learning materials (e.g., readings, videos, lectures)
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of students' initial knowledge and questions; can be superficial if not followed up with deeper exploration
