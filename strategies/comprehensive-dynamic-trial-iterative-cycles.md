# Two consecutive implementation-and-evaluation cycles informed by Comprehensive Dynamic Trials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To promote rapid learning, the study conducts two consecutive OBLS implementation and evaluation cycles, refining the second using feedback from the first. "Drawing on the literature on Comprehensive Dynamic Trials, which involve iterative cycles of implementation and adaptation, two cycles of OBLS implementation and evaluation will be conducted (8 sites per cycle)". The approach feeds information gained during the intervention back into the process to dynamically refine and enhance performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback and lessons learned from the first cycle must be incorporated before the second cycle
#### Constraints
- Limited to the 16 selected implementation sites, 8 per cycle

### Target Learners
- healthcare workers at implementation sites serving areas with high maternal vulnerability indexes

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained, equitable statewide implementation of simulation-based maternal emergency training
