# Faculty development combining technology-adaptation support, contextualized TPACK capacity building, and growth mindset interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the model's findings, the article recommends that universities establish "systematic technology adaptation support systems, such as embedded technical helpdesks, real-time online coaching, and hands-on digital tool training" to reduce technostress; strengthen contextualized TPACK capacity through interdisciplinary teaching laboratories, cross-domain design projects, and collaborative workshops; and incorporate growth mindset courses, reflective teaching salons, and peer coaching into faculty development. Micro-credentials, teaching showcases, and peer review are suggested to sustain participation.
