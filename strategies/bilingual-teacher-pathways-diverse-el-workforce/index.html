Invest in recruiting and retaining a diverse, EL-qualified educator workforce through bilingual teaching pathways¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide urges districts to use ARP and state funds for "recruiting and retaining a well prepared, diverse workforce, including creating pathways for teaching and bilingual teaching that provide incentives and support for immigrant communities." It notes that "teachers of color make a difference for all students, especially students of color," and that most states face a shortage of educators qualified to work with English learners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Engaging caregivers, teacher candidates, paraprofessionals, and afterschool providers in accelerated tutoring requires appropriate training, supervision, and support

Constraints¶

  • Most states face a significant shortage of educators qualified to work with English learners

Target Learners¶

  • English learners taught by a better-prepared, more diverse educator workforce

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Access to qualified educators and sustained language and content support

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide professional learning and a bilingual workforce so preK and early elementary educators can sustain home language and oral language development
  • Grow-your-own and recruitment initiatives: alternative programs, Minority Serving Institutions, high-school pipeline programs, and district human-capital efforts to diversify the teacher workforce.
  • State and federal policy recommendations: recruit and prepare more candidates, retain new teachers through early-career mentoring, and improve working conditions
  • Six state-level strategies for increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce
  • A federal Marshall Plan for Teaching focused on seven areas: compensation, debt-free preparation, high-retention pathways, mentoring, expertise-sharing, school redesign, and accountability
  • Use Teacher Residency Grant Program funds, braided with other sources, to recruit and retain a diverse teaching workforce
  • Build a diverse teacher pipeline through Grow Your Own grants, teacher academies, scholarships, MSI investment, and residency pathways
  • Multi-pronged state investments to diversify and retain the educator workforce
  • Provide English learner-specific summer learning and staff accelerated learning programs with EL specialists
