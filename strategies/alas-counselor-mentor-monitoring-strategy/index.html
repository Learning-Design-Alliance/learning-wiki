Assign a counselor/mentor who continuously monitors students and coordinates multi-context support¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

ALAS assigns each student a counselor/mentor who monitors attendance, behavior, and academic achievement, provides feedback to students and families, serves as a student advocate, and intervenes when problems are identified. Teachers provide weekly and, if needed, daily feedback through the counselor/mentor, and staff connect families to community services such as mental health and substance-abuse counseling.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ongoing staff salaries are the bulk of costs; supervisors provide ongoing training to counselors
  • Support and follow-up across multiple years of program participation

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • high-risk middle and junior high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school persistence
  • classroom behavior and homework completion

Related Strategies¶

  • Trainee-selected, program-screened and trained mentors sustained through monthly staff contact after residential programming
