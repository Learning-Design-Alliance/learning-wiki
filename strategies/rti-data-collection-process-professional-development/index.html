Provide professional development in the procedural steps of the RTI data collection process to all teachers, paraprofessionals, and administrators¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that professional development for everyone involved in the RTI process begin with the data side: "facilitating the understanding of the data collection processes, the data sources, and the data analysis that are necessary to meet the needs of struggling students". Based on the study results, the authors state all teachers and administrators involved in the RTI model need professional development in the procedural steps of the data collection process from start to finish, because staff do not always know the appropriate steps or whom to contact with questions about the decision-making framework.

  • Professional development must cover data collection processes, data sources, and data analysis for meeting the needs of struggling students
