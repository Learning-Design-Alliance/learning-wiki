# Teach students to monitor and reflect on their own behavior

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Recommendation 6 teaches students to self-monitor and reflect on their own behavior using recording sheets and procedural checklists. The introduction states that "Recommendations 6 and 7 focus on ways to teach students to self-monitor and reflect on their own behavior and to use behavior ratings to provide feedback to their peers." Table I.1 assigns minimal evidence, and the guide notes an app may be used "to solicit a behavior rating."
