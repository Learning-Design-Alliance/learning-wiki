# Use reflective practice as assessment strategy in courses promoting creative problem-solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that in courses promoting creative problem-solving, such as leadership, entrepreneurship and project-based courses, "it is realistic to teach team creative skills and use reflective practice as assessment strategies, provided a supportive classroom environment is created to inspire creativity". Reflective retrospective ratings revealed overconfidence that single post-workshop measures would miss. Educators can cultivate novel and adaptive thinking and show graduates how to transfer skills to career, organizational and societal challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive classroom environment that inspires creativity
#### Constraints
- Team work courses are time-consuming and face student resistance

### Target Learning Goals

- novel and adaptive thinking
