# Three modes of teacher professional development centered on the e-Lab portal

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project runs three modes of teacher professional development, all focused on the web portal. In one-week workshops with 25 participants, facilitators 'model the practices and habits of scientists' while teachers join peer research groups and solve problems using the portal. One-day workshops offer more guided exploration of portal features, and staff visit teachers and students for one-to-few coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experience is currently limited to teachers that understand the content embedded in the QuarkNet portal
#### Constraints
- Shorter one-day sessions leave less room for exploration, so important portal features are highlighted instead

### Target Learners
- high school teachers participating in QuarkNet

### Target Learning Goals
- using the e-Lab portal to guide student research
- cosmic ray content and student research techniques
