# Songs and Rhymes

## Description
Using songs and rhymes to memorize information is a mnemonic technique that leverages rhythm and melody to enhance recall. Information is set to a familiar tune or rhyme, making it easier to remember and recall. This method is effective for all ages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ability to create or adapt songs and rhymes. A clear understanding of the information to be remembered.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of information. The song or rhyme must be easily remembered itself.

### Target Learners

- All learners, particularly effective for auditory learners and young children.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve memory recall by associating information with music and rhythm.

### Affordances
- Makes learning fun and engaging. Leverages the brain's natural affinity for music and rhythm to enhance memory.

### Personalization
- Learners can create their own songs or rhymes based on their favorite tunes or rhythms.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess the number of items recalled correctly after learning a song or rhyme. Monitor the ease with which learners can generate and remember their own songs and rhymes.

## Impact
- Research shows that songs and rhymes can improve memory and well-being, especially in people with dementia (Thompson et al., 2021).
