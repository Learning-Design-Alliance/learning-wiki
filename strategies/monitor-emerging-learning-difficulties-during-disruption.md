# Monitor learning difficulties as they emerge during educational disruption rather than relying solely on preexisting vulnerability indicators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that school- and community-based programs "prioritize targeted and adaptive support for children experiencing learning difficulties as they emerge, rather than relying solely on preexisting risk profiles." They suggest embedding regular assessment and feedback mechanisms within school systems so educators can respond dynamically to students' evolving needs during periods of disruption. Interventions could include tailored academic support, guidance for parents to reinforce learning at home, and efforts to maintain routines that support well-being and engagement.
