Reduce institutional barriers through cross-department coordination, low-barrier review models, and faculty-based outreach¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Practitioners recommended cross-department coordination and administrative streamlining, citing "low-barrier review models, clearer internal protocols, and shared ownership across financial aid, basic needs, and student support offices as ways to reduce delays and confusion." On the access side, faculty are crucial partners because they are often among the first to notice struggling students, so faculty training and awareness-building can connect students to resources before crises escalate.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Investment in staffing capacity and internal clarity; rejection of fear-based decision-making in favor of speed and student well-being

Constraints¶

  • Compliance and audit risks of direct-to-student or vendor payments can be especially daunting for smaller institutions with limited administrative capacity

Target Learners¶

  • College students at risk of stopping out due to short-term financial crises

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Timely connection to emergency funds and basic needs supports to sustain enrollment

Related Strategies¶

  • Build a College Promise team spanning internal campus units and external cross-segment and cross-sector partners
  • Cross-division convenings and joint review sessions to strengthen a state education agency's internal collaboration on multilingual policy
  • Reduce barriers for diverse early childhood credential candidates through cost subsidies, flexible course logistics, cohort community, outreach, and specialized advising
  • Define emergencies with flexibility and trust rather than rigid proof-based criteria
