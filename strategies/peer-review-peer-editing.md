# Peer Review/Peer Editing

## Description
Peer review involves students providing feedback on each other's work to foster a collaborative learning environment, increase motivation, and develop a growth mindset. It allows students to give and receive constructive criticism, enhancing their understanding of writing concepts and improving their ability to evaluate and revise their own work. Specific action-oriented writing suggestions can help move writers forward.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear guidelines for providing constructive feedback, structured activities, and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure feedback is constructive and not discouraging.
