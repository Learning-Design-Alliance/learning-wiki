# Use history and philosophy of science cases, such as the falsifiability of the geocentric model, to teach what makes a theory scientific

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course teaches demarcation criteria through historical astronomy cases. Students examine Popper's view that "what makes a theory scientific is its falsifiability," using the geocentric model of Ptolemy as a genuinely scientific though false theory that was falsified by Copernicus's and Galileo's observational evidence for heliocentrism. The authors state these philosophical underpinnings "can help to improve the thinking of scientists through an exploration of the very basis of critical thinking and scientific reasoning."
