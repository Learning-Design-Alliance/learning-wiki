# Multi-level preparation strategy for converting SP cases to digital delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article lays out a stepwise preparation program for moving simulated-person teaching online: reviewing and modifying existing SP cases and learning objectives for digital feasibility, telephoning SPs about readiness and technical equipment, preparing SPs on “topic role protection, privacy, discarding the role”, a technical trial run, and rehearsal with student assistants, followed by continuous improvement through an iterative process. The authors add that “the situation should be trained with the SPs before starting”, emphasizing both communicative and technical aspects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SP readiness and technical equipment checked in advance; technical trial run; rehearsal with simulated participants; iterative improvement
#### Constraints
- Specific aspects of digital implementation such as role and data protection must be taken into account

### Target Learners
- medical students in curricular communication courses

### Target Learning Goals
- communication and social competencies trained with simulated persons
