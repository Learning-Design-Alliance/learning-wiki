# Emotion Password

## Description
Learners are required to make a particular face to enter the classroom. This simple act requires the learners to engage in self-assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Emotion prompts and a facilitator for instruction.
#### Constraints
- Learners must be comfortable performing or may feel embarrassed.

### Target Learning Goals

- Enhance emotional expression, improve self-assessment, promote empathetic understanding

### Affordances
- Engages learners to recognize the faces and emotions.

### Personalization
- Requires learners to be willing to perform in front of others. May not be effective with learners who are naturally introverted.

## Assessment Evidence
- The facilitator can observe how the learners perform. Ask learners to do an individual reflection of how they used emotions and what they learned from the activity.

## Impact
- Research supports the use of role-playing and dramatic activities to enhance emotional understanding and social skills. (Source: Spontaneous Drama Improves Communication Skills - https://www.livestrong.com/article/198645-spontaneous-drama-improves-communication-skills/)
