# Embed art appreciation as compulsory subject content in art and design programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that art appreciation be made a compulsory subject content in schooling for any art and design programs, and that the Ministry of Education introduce it not only in art history but in other art and design related subjects or programs. It argues that incorporating art appreciation lets students develop cognitive knowledge better and make thoughtful decisions in studio art production, and that courses emphasizing art appreciation should be structured in relation to other disciplines of art and to pedagogy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ministry-level curriculum rules and regulations tightened for a better art program
- Courses structured in relation to other art disciplines and pedagogy
#### Constraints
- The article notes program standards exist as guidelines but are not followed or implemented within the art education curriculum

### Target Learners
- art and design students from preschool to higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- cognitive knowledge development
- thoughtful decision-making in studio production
- creative problem solving
