Use explanation essays as a sensitive measure of student understanding¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article argues that "prompting students to articulate an explanation can provide a sensitive measure of student understanding" and that "explanation essays are a valuable way of assessing student learning." This strategy involves asking students to write explanations of how and why phenomena occur as part of inquiry activities, then evaluating those explanations for quality.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must be given source documents to read and integrate

Constraints¶

  • Explanations still need to be evaluated for their quality

Target Learners¶

  • middle school and high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • causal understanding of scientific phenomena

Claims¶

  • Argument-structure measures (propositions, links, easy chain) predict explanation quality and comprehension [+W]
  • Coh-Metrix causality and cohesion predict comprehension; lexical diversity negatively predicts explanation quality [+W]
  • Combined LSA and Coh-Metrix measures predict comprehension with all but COPY as unique predictors [+W]
  • Hand-coded causal-model measures (target concepts, connections) predict comprehension test scores [+W]
  • LSA similarity to idealized peer explanation predicts explanation quality and comprehension [+W]
  • LSA plagiarism score negatively predicts explanation quality but not comprehension [+W]
