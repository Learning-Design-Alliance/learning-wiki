# Use varied delivery mechanisms and embed ultrasound training across settings and existing coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends delivering ultrasound training through multiple settings and mechanisms, listing options such as 'pre-reading and exams, online resources, practical courses, virtual mentoring and supervised scanning or review of stored images or real-time clips for quality assurance'. It further suggests embedding training within existing entry-to-practice coursework (eg, electrophysical agents, anatomy, orthopaedics, neurology, professional issues courses) and supplementing modules with formal or informal mentorship, supervision and case-based examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction should be by physical therapists expert in the field and, where possible, involve other imaging disciplines
#### Constraints
- Access to established diagnostic and interventional US training pathways may be limited in some countries

### Target Learners
- physical therapy students
- practising physical therapists

### Target Learning Goals
- US image generation, interpretation and quality assurance skills
