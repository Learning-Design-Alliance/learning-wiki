# CERN Masterclasses: high-school students analyze real CERN data and rediscover e.g. the Higgs boson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As part of community-wide efforts to bring particle physics to high-school students, the group participates in CERN Masterclasses organized in Germany by the Netzwerk Teilchenwelt. The article reports that 'studentsexperienceanalyzingrealdatafromCERNexperimentsandrediscovere.g.the Higgsboson'. This places learners in the role of working with authentic experimental data rather than only hearing lectures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real data from CERN experiments and the Netzwerk Teilchenwelt organizational network in Germany
#### Constraints
- Targets high-school students as part of a community-wide effort, run locally by the Siegen group

### Target Learners
- high-school students

### Target Learning Goals
- experience analyzing real particle-physics data
- rediscovering results such as the Higgs boson
