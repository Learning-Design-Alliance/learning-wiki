Use sight translation exercises as a classroom reading-instruction technique in EFL reading courses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that ELT instructors use sight translation exercises alongside common reading instruction: students read textbook English passages and "simultaneously render them into spe ech into Persian", with about one fourth of the class hour spent on sight translation across eleven sessions of a semester. The author presents this as an effective technique for improving learners' reading comprehension ability in an Iranian context.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Bilingual classroom context in which learners can orally render English passages into Persian during class time
  • Sustained treatment across multiple sessions (eleven sessions in the study) alongside the common reading instruction method

Constraints¶

  • The study was conducted in an Iranian university and the author states results "can only be generalized with caution"
  • The author states usefulness of sight translation tasks in private non-academic ELT contexts should be determined through separate studies

Target Learners¶

  • Iranian undergraduate EFL students in reading comprehension courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension ability in English

Affordances¶

  • Sight Translation Mixed Mode Definition

Claims¶

  • The article reports a significant mediating effect of gender on the role of sight translation in reading ability acquisition, while its own printed posttest means for females and males are nearly identical [+W]
  • Iranian undergraduate EFL students who performed sight translation exercises during reading instruction scored significantly differently on a TOLIMO reading posttest than control students, which the author interprets as the experimental group outperforming the control group [+W]
