# Peer Coaching

## Description
Peer coaching is a reciprocal helping relationship between equals who support each other's learning through mutual trust and open discussion. Participants alternate between coach and client roles, sharing experiences, offering feedback, and holding each other accountable. Peer coaching fosters a strong support system, strengthens cross-functional networks, enhances leadership skills, and helps broaden understanding of organizational issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
