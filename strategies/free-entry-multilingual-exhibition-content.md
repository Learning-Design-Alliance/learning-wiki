# Lower barriers to access through free entry and multilingual content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To widen the reachable audience, Science Gateway removes two practical barriers. Socio-economic accessibility is addressed because "The entrance toScience Gatewayand the participation in any of its activities is free of charge, making it easier for individuals and families with a lower income to explore and enjoy the research and outreach activities at CERN." Linguistic accessibility is addressed by offering content in five languages (French, English, German, Italian, Spanish), which the authors describe as key to expanding the target audience to international visitors and sharing content with CERN member states.
