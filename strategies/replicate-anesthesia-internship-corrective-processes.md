# Replicate the internship model in Type 1 and 2 universities and apply corrective processes to eliminate identified weaknesses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending evaluation of the internship model beyond their Type 3 university setting and improving it by eliminating its weaknesses through corrective processes. They state the model's weaknesses "can be improved by eliminating them and carrying out corrective processes", and that the model "be replicated in Type 1 and 2 universities and universities with postgraduate education in anesthesia" with results compared to the present study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions addressing the identified weaknesses and challenges, such as insurance coverage and monitoring methods
#### Constraints
- Generalizability limited because the model was implemented in a university without anesthesia residents or graduate students

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate anesthesiology and operating room students

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving professional qualifications and clinical competence
- Reducing human-resource shortages in the clinical field
