# Extend session duration and reduce group size in simulator practicals

## Description
Based on student feedback, the authors plan to extend the practical session from 90 to 120 minutes to give students significantly more time for repeated individual simulations, and to enable work in smaller groups. This responds directly to the most common student criticism of the pilot session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate simulator capacity and simulation center space and time
#### Constraints
- Pilot duration was capped at 90 minutes because of space and time limitations in the simulation center

### Target Learners
- Second-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Repeated practice of auscultation and blood pressure measurement skills
