## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that CBL be deployed flexibly across contexts rather than as a fixed recipe. Its conclusion states that "CBL holds immense potential for implementation across diverse disciplines, community practices, and special projects within medical education" and that its "practical application of CBL is continuously evolving in response to changing times and can be seamlessly integrated into different contexts influenced by environmental factors and policies." Implementers should therefore match CBL formats to disciplinary, community, and policy conditions.
