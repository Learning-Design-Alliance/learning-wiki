# Exploration Station for Multimodal Pathways

## Description
Creating a designated computer station where students can explore their preferred composition pathway. Students can utilize modes linearly (linguistic to audio to visual), start with digital images and audio files, or use a recursive process (moving back and forth between drafts in digital, audio, and linguistic forms). Providing space for exploration aims to create ownership and value innovation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designated computer station, access to digital images and audio files, time for exploration.
#### Constraints
- May require significant resources to set up and maintain the station. Relies on student self-direction and exploration. Some students may need additional guidance to navigate the station effectively.
