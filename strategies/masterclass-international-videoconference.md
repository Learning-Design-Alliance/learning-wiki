# Capstone international videoconference combining results from 3-5 institutes measuring the same experiment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (4 design, 1 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The masterclass ends with an international videoconference in which 3-5 institutes that made the same measurement on different data combine and discuss their results. The article states that "The capstone of the masterclass is an international videoconference with 3-5 other masterclass institutes that have made measurements from the same experiment". Moderated by 2-3 physicists or graduate students from centers such as CERN, Fermilab, KEK, GSI and TRIUMF, the session includes combining and discussing results, general Q&A and a quiz.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 2-3 moderators (physicists, graduate students); moderation centers such as CERN, Fermilab, KEK, GSI, TRIUMF; an agenda of welcome, combination and discussion of results, general Q&A, and quiz
#### Constraints
- 3-5 institutes reflecting international collaboration where possible; 45-60 minute duration

### Target Learners
- High school students aged 15 to 19 years

### Target Learning Goals
- Combining and interpreting measurement results
- Experiencing international scientific collaboration
