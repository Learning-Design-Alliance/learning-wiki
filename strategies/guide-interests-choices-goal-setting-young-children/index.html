Strategies

Guide young students through investigating interests, making choices, and setting and attaining learning goals¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete teaching activity in which teachers use the Self-Determined Learning Model of Instruction to walk young children through investigating their own interests, facilitating their choices, and supporting goal setting and attainment. The article reports teachers "used the model effectively to support the investigation of student interests, the facilitation of choices, and the goal setting and attainment of young children."
