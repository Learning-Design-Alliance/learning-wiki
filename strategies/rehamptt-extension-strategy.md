# Extend the amputation rehabilitation e-learning module to other countries, translations, and health-professions education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend three forward-looking extensions: making RehAmpTT available to other countries — especially those with few or no specialised rehabilitation facilities for people after limb amputation — which would require translations into the respective national languages; and using the module in medical undergraduate and continuing education, explicitly including health-profession and nursing staff. They also invite users to apply the module in daily practice and give feedback for further development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Translations into the respective national languages for use in other countries
#### Constraints
- Evaluation in the German rehabilitation system would need to be examined for feasibility and meaningfulness

### Target Learners
- health-profession and nursing staff
- medical students
- learners in countries with limited specialised rehabilitation facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- rehabilitation knowledge after limb amputation
- continuing education in amputation care
