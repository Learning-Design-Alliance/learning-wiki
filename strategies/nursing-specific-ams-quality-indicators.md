# Develop nursing-specific AMS quality indicators to identify gaps and guide improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors identify a need to develop nursing-specific AMS quality indicators to identify gaps and guide future improvement, aligning with protocols of hospitals that adopt nurse-sensitive indicators. They state that "the ANA/CDC workgroup strongly recommends identifying nursing quality measures related to AMS." Suggested examples include contamination and rejection rates of microbiological culture specimens, number of patients switched from intravenous to oral antimicrobials based on a nurse's prompt, and promoting daily review of antimicrobial necessity during clinical rounds.
