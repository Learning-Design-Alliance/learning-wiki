# Convene a researcher professional learning community to address collaborative-research challenges

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article documents a strategy in which researchers facing common collaborative-research challenges convene a professional learning community: they identify challenges, seek resources and solutions, and then apply those solutions in their research-alliance work. The paper shares "the strategies they identified to address the challenges, and the tools they used to overcome the challenges" so other researchers can benefit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular meetings over an extended period
- A set of shared challenges experienced across research alliances
#### Constraints
- The article presents preliminary, self-reported reflections rather than evaluated outcomes

### Target Learners
- Education researchers new to the research alliance model

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying and addressing challenges in conducting collaborative research with practitioners
