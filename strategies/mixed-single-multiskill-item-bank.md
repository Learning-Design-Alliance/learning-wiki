# Design cognitive diagnostic item banks with a mix of single- and multiskill items and at least ten items per content-area and skill combination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that cognitive diagnostic item banks combine single-skill and multiskill items: "item banks should include a mix of single- and multiskill items to pick from to maximize the information generated by each item a student answers." Multiskill items are typically more difficult and provide different information than single-skill items. The authors also plan to "ensur[e] sufficient classification accuracy with a minimum of ten items for each content area and skill combination," which supports weekly tests on a single content area and skill combination, and to add items covering multiple content areas to inform how physics content interacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Enough items per skill and content-area combination to reach sufficient classification accuracy
#### Constraints
- The current combined bank lacks algebra items and lacks apply vectors and visualizations items for energy and momentum

### Target Learners
- introductory college mechanics students

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate skill classification in adaptive formative assessment
