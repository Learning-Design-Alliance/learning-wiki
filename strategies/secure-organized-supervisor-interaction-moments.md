# Organize and secure regular scheduled moments of supervisor-trainee interaction (debriefing and educational meetings) to support trainees' self-regulated learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that practices deliberately organize moments for interaction between trainee and supervisor, because scheduled debriefing sessions and educational meetings secure interactive reflection and meaning making, and a supervisor nearby facilitates instant consultation. As the article states, "Securing organized moments of interaction with supervisors is beneficial to trainees' self-regulated learning". Access to information sources such as computer facilities, books or other persons is also described as essential.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors with training in educational and coaching skills; enough contact time over time to develop a trusting working relationship.
#### Constraints
- Depends on the autonomy supervisors grant trainees; the article notes results may not transfer to contexts where trainee-supervisor relationships differ.

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical trainees in workplace-based training

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning, reflection, and growth of clinical competence
