# Support basic science knowledge renewal through CPD workshops, webinars and clinically relevant, easily accessible online resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Registrars proposed measures to support continuous updating of basic science knowledge, including "structured Continuous Professional Development (CPD) workshops on basic sciences, webinars, and easily accessible online resources", with CPD workshops the most favoured strategy. The discussion advises training programs to integrate clinically relevant basic science courses into required CPDs given busy schedules, and to develop reliable information systems aligned with point-of-care needs, ideally co-designed with GPs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources must be accessible, relevant and time-efficient to fit registrars' busy schedules, as the article states
#### Constraints
- The study sample came from one training program, so results may not generalise to other clinical settings

### Target Learners
- GP registrars
- postgraduate medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- retention and renewal of basic science knowledge
- integration of basic sciences with clinical practice
