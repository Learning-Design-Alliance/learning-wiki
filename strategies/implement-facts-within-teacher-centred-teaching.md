# Implement FACTs within prevailing teacher-centred teaching to develop inquiry skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's central implementable recommendation is that formative assessment classroom techniques can be introduced into ordinary teacher-centred science lessons without restructuring teaching toward inquiry-based science education. The authors report that "using FACTs – even without special intervention concerning systematic implementation of IBSE – is effective in the development of inquiry skills." Practically, teachers used FACTs at their own discretion in different phases of teaching and learning, at least once a week in at least one subject, choosing from a ready-made database.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a ready-made FACTs database for individual subjects
- Teacher training in FA and ongoing methodological guidance
- Coordination so FACTs are used regularly across at least three subjects
#### Constraints
- If teachers lack pre-existing FACTs or hold negative attitudes toward them, the statistical difference could be less significant; effects become visible only in the long run

### Target Learners
- upper secondary school students in science subjects

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry skills
- scientific literacy
