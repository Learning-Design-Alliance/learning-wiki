# Adapted 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction practice with 15 minutes of daily practice, adjustable to teachers' life situations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention adapts elements of the 8-week MBSR program: participants are instructed to do mindfulness practices 15 minutes per day during the 8-week intervention. Because reviews show teacher mindfulness interventions commonly run 8 weeks and often encourage shorter self-practices of 10-30 minutes instead of 45, teachers who cannot commit to intensive practice may adjust the effort to their own life situation, keep a record of practices completed, and follow practices on the Oiva web page—a research-based resource offering over 40 brief Finnish-language mindfulness exercises. A mindfulness expert guides participants throughout the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Daily practice time (15 minutes, adjustable), weekly Webropol reports of practice frequency and minutes, and guidance by a mindfulness expert
#### Constraints
- Teachers who cannot commit to intensive practice are instructed they may adjust the effort to their own life situation

### Target Learners
- higher education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- managing stress and emotions in teaching
- supporting well-being through mindfulness
