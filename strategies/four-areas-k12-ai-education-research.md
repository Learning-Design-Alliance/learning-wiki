## Description
The article argues that "research programs should be developed for four areas of K-12 AI education": K-12 AI Learning & Teaching; K-12 AI Education Integration into STEM, Language Arts, and Social Science Education; K-12 AI Professional Development for Teachers; and K-12 AI Assessment. Following the lead of science and mathematics education research communities, these programs would build an evidence base for AI literacy curriculum development.
