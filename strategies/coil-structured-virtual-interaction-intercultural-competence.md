# Use COIL methodologies and structured virtual interaction to foster intercultural competence in higher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies COIL (Collaborative Online International Learning), blended learning, and LMS-based virtual exchange as the predominant structured methodologies for developing intercultural competences, reported in 10 studies (45.45%) for pedagogical approaches and 15 studies (68.18%) for digital platforms. These tools "enable the creation of structured and intentional learning experiences for intercultural interaction". The authors recommend designing training programs that integrate digital and intercultural competences and promoting development of multicultural pedagogical content knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional partnership establishment, committed team members, sufficient time for module implementation, and teacher training in digital tools for intercultural purposes
#### Constraints
- Technological barriers including platform access difficulties and digital divide limit equity in access and participation; language barriers and lack of initial trust hinder smooth interaction

### Target Learners
- higher education students in multicultural and international learning contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- intercultural communication, cultural adaptability, intercultural awareness, empathy, and global teamwork
