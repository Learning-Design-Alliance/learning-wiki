# L2 teachers should consider if and how to incorporate visual input enhancement into the classroom given its unclear benefits over input flood.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that the difference between input flood and input enhancement may not be statistically or practically significant, the authors recommend that researchers, and especially L2 teachers, may wish to consider if, and how, they would like to incorporate this focus on form technique into the L2 classroom. This strategy involves deliberately deciding whether typographical manipulation of texts adds value beyond simply providing texts rich in target forms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should be aware that visual input enhancement may not draw more attention than unenhanced input flood for short, simplified texts.
#### Constraints
- The study found no differential improvement between enhanced and unenhanced groups on attention, awareness, cloze, or oral production measures.

### Target Learners
- second-semester college L2 Spanish learners

### Target Learning Goals
- noticing and knowledge of Spanish preterit and imperfect past tense forms

## Claims

- [Both enhanced and unenhanced groups improved on cloze tests of Spanish past tense with no differential improvement.](../claims/input-enhancement-no-differential-cloze-improvement.md) [+W]
- [Visual input enhancement did not improve learners' accurate use of past tense in oral production beyond input flood alone.](../claims/input-enhancement-no-differential-oral-production-gains.md) [+W]
- [Input flood may be just as salient and beneficial as input enhancement for short, simplified texts.](../claims/input-flood-as-salient-as-input-enhancement.md) [+W]
- [More participants in the unenhanced condition correctly identified the target structure than in the enhanced condition.](../claims/unenhanced-group-greater-awareness-target-structure.md) [-W]
- [Visual input enhancement of Spanish preterit and imperfect forms did not increase L2 learners' attention as measured by eye tracking.](../claims/visual-input-enhancement-no-increased-attention-eye-tracking.md) [+W]
