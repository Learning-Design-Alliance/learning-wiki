# Teach bridge analysis by transforming the π network of r, R2 and Xc into an equivalent T network so the circuit reduces to a familiar Wheatstone topology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article replaces the π network consisting of r, R2 and Xc with an equivalent T network whose three impedances are given by equations (2)-(4), reducing the Anderson bridge to an apparently simple circuit. The authors state: "The circuit now looks more like the fundamental Wheatstone bridge, which purely due to its topology looks trivial and overcomes a mental barrier a student might have due to a complex looking circuit." They offer this treatment as a pedagogical tool for circuit analysis alongside the standard Kirchhoff-law derivations of textbooks.
