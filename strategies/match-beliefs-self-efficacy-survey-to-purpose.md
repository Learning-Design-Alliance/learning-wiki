# Match beliefs and self-efficacy survey choice to purpose: CLASS for quick broad measurement, SOSEC-P for detailed self-efficacy, PSEQ for short combined use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article gives purpose-matched recommendations among belief and attitude instruments: the CLASS for a quick, widely compared, statistically validated survey; MPEX for a resources-perspective assessment; E-CLASS for experimental-physics beliefs; APSS for problem-solving attitudes; PGOS for goal orientations; SALG for perceived learning gains; and VOSE or VNOS for nature-of-science views depending on desired scoring format. For self-efficacy it states: "If you want to measure detailed changes in your students' physics course specific self-efficacy, use the SOSEC-P, as it probes several dimensions of self-efficacy and uses several questions to probe each." The five-question PSEQ serves when brevity is needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Choice depends on the instructor's assessment goal, population level, time available, and whether prior comparison data will be used.
#### Constraints
- CLASS and MPEX statements refer to traditional introductory course activities and may not make sense in very non-traditional courses.

### Target Learners
- high school through graduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring beliefs, attitudes, self-efficacy, goal orientations, and nature-of-science views
