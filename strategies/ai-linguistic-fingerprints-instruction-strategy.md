# Teach the linguistic fingerprints of AI-generated text to build algorithmic skepticism

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article describes training students to recognize "algorithmic fingerprints" by examining pairs of human-written and AI-generated texts on identical topics and collaboratively cataloging distinctive patterns. Students learn that metaphorical terms like delve, tapestry, symphony, intricate, nuanced, and resonates "tend to appear when AI is attempting to perform intellectual depth without substantive content," plus markers such as excessive hedging and unnaturally balanced viewpoints, developing what the author terms algorithmic skepticism.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
- AI-generated text often appears indistinguishable from human writing at a casual glance, so detection rests on subtle patterns

### Target Learning Goals
- detecting likely machine-generated text
- adopting a critical stance toward substantive claims in synthetic prose
