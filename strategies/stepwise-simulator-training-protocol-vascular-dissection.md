# Structured stepwise simulator training protocol: instructional video, console familiarization, then repeated dissection practice with and without force feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article’s training recipe consists of a standardized instructional video introducing robotic vascular dissection principles, a brief hands-on familiarization with the console, and five complete repetitions of the vascular dissection task — three with red-LED force feedback enabled and two without to assess skill retention. The authors state this "step-by-step procedural model" internalizes a reproducible approach including vessel isolation, safe vessel-loop passage, and accurate stapler positioning, supporting systematic progression along the learning curve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sensorized simulator with feedback capability
- Standardized instructional video
- Individual performance without peer observation to limit observational learning
#### Constraints
- Validated only in a preclinical porcine model; clinical transfer not assessed

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- safe stapler positioning
