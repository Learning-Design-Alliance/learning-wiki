# Storytelling in Explainer Animations and Videos

## Description
Combining visual elements with engaging narratives to capture the learners' attention and facilitate long-term information retention. The video or animation should start by presenting a common problem or a realistic situation that your audience might face in their daily lives. This introduction creates a familiar context and piques the persons' interest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools for creating animations or videos. A compelling story that illustrates the concept.
#### Constraints
- Animations and videos can be time-consuming to produce. Poorly designed stories can be confusing or ineffective.
