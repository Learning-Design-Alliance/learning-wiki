# Engage medical students as peer assessors in high-fidelity-simulation-based clinical performance evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend involving students actively in the evaluation process: "Efforts should be made to enable medical students to actively participate in the evaluation process as fellow assessors in high-fidelity-simulation-based assessment of clinical performance". Peers observe a fellow group's full simulation via a dedicated audio/video system and rate it on the checklist, which the article describes as a learning activity in which students review their own knowledge and skills through evaluating others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulation lab with real-time audio/video transmission of the practice process, and a checklist simple enough for student use
#### Constraints
- The article notes concerns that proper assessment of peers' competence may be challenging due to personal or competitive peer relationships, and that training to develop appropriate assessment skills should be considered

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning clinical skills through observing and evaluating peer performance
- developing assessment skills
