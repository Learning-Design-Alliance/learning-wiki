# Two-day adaptive optics workshop to raise AO knowledge among all scientific and technical staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
PSO ran a two-day AO workshop at ESO headquarters in Santiago, repeated twice so staff on duty at Paranal could attend, with a total of 80 participants. Day 1 covered principles of AO systems (image formation, turbulence, wavefront sensing and correction); Day 2 covered Paranal AO instruments and flavours. Talks and exercises were given by PSO AO astronomers and engineers.
