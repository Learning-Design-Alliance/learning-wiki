# Use a graded in-VR onboarding tutorial starting from a single basic action before task instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For self-practice by VR novices, onboard players inside the application starting at a very basic level and moving to more complex tasks. Players first touch a hovering red ball with no other instruction, then grip and move it to a circle outside their field of vision to prompt head turning, before the instruction on equipment and documentation begins, guided by voice and film.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks must start extremely simple and escalate gradually
- Instruction videos and voice guidance for the equipment phase
#### Constraints
- Even this two-feature onboarding was not enough for some players until the tutorial was added

### Target Learners
- Persons with no or little experience with head-mounted VR equipment and hand controllers

### Target Learning Goals
- Becoming able to interact with the VR environment and carry out the clinical observations
