# Deepen personalization for low-motivation learners with narrative elements, everyday-life relevance, and fewer tasks to prevent overload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For students with low motivation, the teacher experts recommended engagement-oriented design moves. The article reports that "experts recommended deepening personalization by embedding narrative elements and everyday-life relevance to foster engagement and problem-solving motivation" and that teachers "appreciated the reduced number of tasks for students with low motivation, noting that it prevented cognitive overload." This strategy pairs motivational framing with a lighter task load for low-motivation learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Recommendation stems from exploratory teacher feedback, not a controlled comparison

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement, problem-solving motivation, avoidance of cognitive overload
