# Scaffold from physical-system measurements to physics measured directly on living systems to foster interdisciplinary thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A sequencing strategy for interdisciplinary labs: first build measurement and analysis skills on physical systems, then apply the same methods to living systems, and finally ask what biology can be learned from the physical measurement. The article states it reinforces interdisciplinary thinking "by connecting labs where the focus is on physical systems and measurements (e.g. for random and directed motion of beads in lab 3... ) with labs where physics is measured directly on living systems (e.g. measurements of organelle motion in cells in lab 5...)". Students are also asked to seek biological authenticity in each scenario and to reconsider science content learned in previous courses.
