# Assign or encourage completion of online CBL modules before or early in a clinical elective rotation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors directed residents by email "to complete five self-directed online CBL modules prior to their first day" of the elective, aiming to bolster knowledge as trainees progressed through the rotation. They state this study "aims to increase this self-direction by specifically encouraging or assigning learners material to review rather than relying on the traditional module of outpatient residency rotations" that assume learners find material themselves. The strategy positions flexible online modules as an adjunct to, not a replacement for, clinical exposure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules must exist and be accessible online before the rotation begins; an email or assignment mechanism directs learners to them
#### Constraints
- The survey data could not differentiate whether residents completed all videos before the survey or when modules were completed relative to survey completion

### Target Learners
- pediatric residents beginning an optional subspecialty elective

### Target Learning Goals
- foundational outpatient subspecialty knowledge and preparation for clinic cases
