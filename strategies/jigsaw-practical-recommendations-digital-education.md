# Use jigsaw with attention to its practical challenges, drawing on the review's recommendations in the context of digital education

## Description
The review's authors close with implementable guidance for practitioners: they "discuss the challenges of jigsaw activities and the limitations of studies reviewed and conclude with practical recommendations in the context of digital education." This positions jigsaw implementation — including digitally mediated forms — as requiring attention to the challenges the review identifies, and directs educators and designers to the review's recommendations when deploying jigsaw activities in digital education settings.
