# Pre-Reading Graphic Organizers

## Description
Students identify the text structure prior to reading (e.g., compare and contrast, description, problem-solution, cause and effect, sequence of events) and complete a supporting graphic organizer while reading to fill in the components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supporting graphic organizer and texts.
#### Constraints
- Identification of text structure and students may need teacher modeling and guidance.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Older students, particularly those facing more challenging texts in content areas outside of English language arts.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance comprehension by identifying text structure before reading and actively filling in components in a graphic organizer.

### Affordances
- Boosts comprehension by preparing students for the structure of the text and actively engaging them during reading.

### Personalization
- Teachers can model how to use the organizers and provide a KWL chart.
