# Run an ask-an-expert Q&A section where public questions are answered by researchers in the relevant field

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Modeled on websites like "Ask an Astronomer", the project maintains a section in which "questions from the public are answered by experts in the relevant ﬁeld." Experts include members of the Hellenic Astronomical Society and invited scientists from around the globe, and many answers "provide extra educational references for further reading." The strategy creates two-way interaction between scientists and the audience and also builds the contributors' own science-writing and editing skills.
