# Anticipate and explicitly address the force-versus-acceleration confusion and the meaning of relative gravitational acceleration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article identifies specific misunderstandings students may have and recommends instructor moves. It is "common to confuse the force of gravity with the acceleration due to gravity", and instructors should point out, preferably with examples, that the acceleration does not depend on the baseball's mass but on the body's size and mass. For relative gravitational acceleration, instructors should have students use units carefully, noting the value is a dimensionless ratio, which can also introduce the utility of dimensionless quantities in physics.
