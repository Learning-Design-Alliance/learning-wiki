# Implement sepsis care bundles and formal sepsis training modules during healthcare student training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that sepsis education be formalized in medical and nursing curricula and that Surviving Sepsis Campaign care bundles be used as educational tools during training. Students themselves overwhelmingly support this: "Jamaican healthcare students agree that more training on sepsis is needed (98.3%) and that sepsis care bundles should be implemented during their training courses (94.2%)". The authors also point to a consensus document on core sepsis-related competencies to guide curricula for low or lower-middle income countries like Jamaica.
