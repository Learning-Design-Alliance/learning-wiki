# Structure AI virtual patient sessions with pre-briefing, a structured diagnostic guide, and case-specific debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends following Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice design: each session opens with a pre-briefing that includes "an introduction to the setting, instructions on how to use the programme and a guide for a structured approach to endodontic diagnostics". After the virtual patient consents to the suggested treatment, a case-specific debriefing provides AI-generated feedback on the student's diagnosis, treatment and patient communication, plus background information and a transcript of an experienced clinician's conversation with the same patient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-briefing introducing the setting and usage, and a structured diagnostic guide
- A debriefing with AI feedback evaluating the proposed diagnosis and treatment against the correct options
#### Constraints
- The AI-generated feedback was reported to recognize the correct diagnosis only if articulated in a specific manner to the patient

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Structured endodontic diagnostic reasoning
- Reflective comparison of one's own approach with an expert clinician's
