# Deliver AI-generated visual explanations adaptively based on learner engagement profiles rather than uniformly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends moving beyond a single representation for all learners: "A single rep- resentation is unlikely to benefit all learners equally, and adaptive delivery based on, for example, engagement profiles may be crucial to realize the full potential of automated visual explanations." Because engagement profiles moderated immediate performance effects, delivery of GATs could be tailored to low-, mid-, and high-engagement learners instead of applying one modality to every student. The authors frame this as a direction for future adaptive, learner-aware strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Measurement of learner engagement profiles, e.g., via CAP self-report instruments
#### Constraints
- The moderation analysis was exploratory and relied on self-report engagement measures, so results should be treated as suggestive rather than confirmatory

### Target Learners
- Novice programmers in introductory CS1 courses, particularly in large, heterogeneous courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding program execution and immediate post-intervention programming task performance
