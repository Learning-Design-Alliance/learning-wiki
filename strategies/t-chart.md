# T-Chart

## Description
A T-chart is a graphic organizer shaped like the letter T, with a horizontal line at the top to signify a broad category and a vertical line that allows learners to compare attributes of a concept, organism, or phenomenon. With a topic as the title on the horizontal line, learners compare two aspects of the topic on each side of the vertical line. These can be mutually exclusive, such as comparing mammals and non-mammals or insects and spiders, or they can compare two types of spiders with different characteristics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- T-chart template (either pre-made or self-drawn). Materials for recording information (e.g., paper, pencils, markers). Topic for comparison.
#### Constraints
- May be too simplistic for complex topics with multiple variables. Requires learners to be able to identify relevant attributes for comparison.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those needing support in comparing and contrasting information or ideas.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Compare and contrast concepts, organisms, or phenomena. Identify similarities and differences. Organize and analyze information.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured way to compare two aspects of a topic. Helps learners organize and analyze information. Facilitates identification of similarities and differences. Supports visual learners.

### Personalization
- Can be used with a wide range of topics and subject areas. Can be adapted for individual, paired, or group work. The complexity of the information can be adjusted based on the learner's needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Accuracy and completeness of the information recorded on the T-chart. Ability to identify key similarities and differences between the two aspects being compared. Clarity and organization of the information presented.

## Impact
- Charts and other graphic organizers have been found to be effective with students who are struggling with learning content at their grade level or who have difficulty learning.
