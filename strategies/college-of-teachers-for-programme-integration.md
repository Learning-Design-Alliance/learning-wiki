# Create a cross-departmental College of teachers focused on programme integration from the students' perspective

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' first proposed step for faculty is to establish a forum that crosses department boundaries and takes the whole programme, and integration from the students' perspective, as its theme. They propose that "the whole programme and integration from the students' perspective is the theme", that teachers learn about one another's subjects and above all about their students' learning within a theoretical framework, so that teachers build a knowledge object to offer their students.
