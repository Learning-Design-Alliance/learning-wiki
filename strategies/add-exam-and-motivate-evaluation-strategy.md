# Refine assignment-only assessment by adding a written or oral exam and motivating course evaluation participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on lessons learned from the first iteration, the authors recommend complementing assignment-only assessment with a written or oral exam, because evaluating students only through assignments, while fitting the hands-on approach, made it difficult to cover the topics of all lectures, especially the guest lectures. They also aim to further motivate students to participate in the course evaluation, since evaluation is not mandatory at their institution and only a small number of students completed it, limiting the feedback available for improving the course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A course structure with lectures whose topics need uniform coverage, including guest lectures
#### Constraints
- Course evaluation is not mandatory at the authors' institution

### Target Learners
- master's students in computer science and related subjects

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehensive coverage of all lecture topics
- collecting actionable course feedback
