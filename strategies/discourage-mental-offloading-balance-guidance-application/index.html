Discourage mental offloading to AI by balancing guidance depth and frequency with application opportunities¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Under Scaffold Creativity, the framework recommends encouraging creativity through open-ended exploration and to "discourage mental offloading to an AI system by balancing the depth and frequency of guidance with opportunities to apply their learning." It also advises integrating AI-enhanced features into platforms learners already use for creativity and providing scaffolds and starting points rather than designing the tool to be creative on the learner's behalf.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Scaffolds and starting points must support the learner's own creative process rather than replace it

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Learners engaged in creative learning and problem-solving with AI tools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Creativity and sustained creative problem-solving

Affordances¶

  • Skill Building Emerging Tech Durable Skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Have learners bring their own thinking before using generative AI to enable productive struggle
  • Integrate AI-driven scaffolding with teacher-led offloading and refine feedback granularity to prevent over-scaffolding
  • Design AI educational systems that fade support and require learner justification to preserve independent problem solving
