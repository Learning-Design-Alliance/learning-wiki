# Incorporate focus groups into formal education programs to foster learning about outpatients' narratives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study's focus group discussions gave students a role in reflecting on patients' narratives after the formal program, the authors suggest it "may be helpful to incorporate focus groups as a part of formal education programs to foster learning about the characteristics of outpatients' narratives." Sessions used open-ended questions and lasted 40–60 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator posing open-ended questions such as how students feel about the program
- Sessions of 40–60 minutes with homogeneous student groups
#### Constraints
- Students' comments were based on recall after the educational program

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students in clinical clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- reflecting on and learning the characteristics of outpatients' narratives

## Claims

- [Medical students recognize outpatients' narratives as irreproducible and moving across narrative quadrants, unlike simulated patients' and inpatients' narratives](../claims/outpatient-narrative-irreproducibility.md) [+W]
