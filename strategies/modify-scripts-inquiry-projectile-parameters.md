# Inquiry-based exploration: students modify θ and v0 in the provided scripts and immediately observe trajectory changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends having learners adjust launch parameters in the provided Python scripts and observe the effects on trajectory, range, and height in real time. It states that "these visualizations support inquiry-based learning strategies by allowing students to formulate and test hypotheses about the dependence of projectile motion on its initial conditions". This strengthens understanding of symmetry, energy conservation, and vector decomposition while fostering computational literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to the open-source Python scripts and a standard computing environment, since no specialized software is needed
#### Constraints
- the framework as presented models idealized motion without air resistance; extensions to dissipative forces are left as future work

### Target Learners
- undergraduate mechanics students

### Target Learning Goals
- formulating and testing physical hypotheses
- understanding parameter dependence of projectile motion
- connecting analytical derivations with numerical modeling
