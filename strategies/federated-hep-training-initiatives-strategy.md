# Federate training initiatives across experiments to share materials and reduce duplication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends deploying training efforts holistically and cross-experimentally to avoid duplicated effort, proposing "the creat ion of a federa- tion of training initiatives, aimed at improving the eﬃciency and cost-eﬀec tivness of this important activity." A common training programme would help share experience amongst experiments and grow a shared training culture. To handle the wide range of student competence, material should be organised as a large number of independent topics, with the IN2P3 authors restricting tutorials to 25 minutes inspired by the Pomodoro technique.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination so different software teams agree on common standards; incentives to reward those who train the community; a centralized catalog with authors free to host material in their location of choice
#### Constraints
- Trainers usually want to refactor reused material rather than use it as-is; it is tricky to keep small tutorials independent and meaningful when selected at will

### Target Learners
- Members of different HEP experiment collaborations at all experience levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Common software and computing competencies shared across experiments
