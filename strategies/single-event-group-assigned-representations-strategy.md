# Anchor a revision lesson on one physical event examined at fixed time instants through group-assigned representations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation recipe drawn from the lesson: open by physically tossing a ball and asking how physical quantities change "on its way up and down"; instruct students to ignore air resistance; specify fixed instants (t = 0 s to t = 6 s, including the highest point at t = 3 s); assign each group one representation and its guiding tasks via a handout; then have each group present while the teacher helps synthesize and consolidate learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A handout that gives "a broad overview of the lesson while defining clearly the specific task that each group had to undertake"
#### Constraints
- The strategy was described for a mechanics ball-toss context in an A-level revision lesson

### Target Learners
- Junior college (A-level) physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Describing vertical projectile motion with kinematics, dynamics and energy representations

### Affordances
- Ainsworth Three Functions Of Multiple Representations
