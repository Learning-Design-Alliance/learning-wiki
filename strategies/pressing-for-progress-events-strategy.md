# Pressing for Progress national events on gender in physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Pressing for Progress (PFP) is a recurring event format organized by GIPWG to address the gender gap in physics. The 2018 meeting at ICTS Bengaluru included "an open discussion on sexual harassment in academia, a first for India." The 2019 event was the first interdisciplinary conference in physics, where "About 250 physicists, social scientist s, educators, and diversity practitioners attended the conference to deliberate on the issue of gender diversity in physics," with keynote talks, posters, a panel, and three interactive workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organization by a gender working group within a national professional society, with interdisciplinary participation from social scientists and diversity practitioners
#### Constraints
- Events described were held in India in 2018 and 2019

### Target Learners
- physicists, educators, and diversity practitioners in India

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of gender inequity in physics and avenues for promoting gender equity
