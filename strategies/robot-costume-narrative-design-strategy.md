# Invite students to design costumes and narratives for their robots as legitimate epistemic engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors made an intentional design choice to have students customize robot exteriors and craft meaningful stories, which "positioned aesthetic, narrative, and expressive forms of engagement as legitimate alongside technical programming work." Designing costumes prompted students to reason about robot structure, weight, placement, and mobility as functional constraints, and public presentation let them personify their robots through narrative.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A design challenge with room for aesthetic customization without blocking sensors or wheels
- an authentic audience such as a Celebration of Learning Event
#### Constraints
- Costume weight and placement can hinder robot motion, so iteration is needed

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- robot structure
- motion planning
- connecting computing to personal interests
