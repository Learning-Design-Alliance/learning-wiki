# Make student media-production projects a more normal part of undergraduate teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends embedding original media-production projects in undergraduate teaching, because students learn research communication as well as content: one student states "I would encourage any effort to make such projects a more normal part of undergraduate teaching". The strategy lets students explore a domain, produce videos for a wide audience, and package them into a teaching product.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A working graphics or production codebase or equivalent artifact students can use to create original content
- an audience to communicate to, to motivate quality and communication practice
#### Constraints
- Creative work is time-consuming: the student reports the effort to add finishing touches seemed to increase exponentially, and the project began as an enrichment activity within an advanced study programme rather than a required course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- research experience
- science communication
- relativity
