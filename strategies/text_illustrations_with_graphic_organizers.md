# Text Illustrations with Graphic Organizers

## Description
Learners add graphic organizers to finished expository or argumentative writing to illustrate a concept, providing a supportive diagram that aids reader comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Finished writing piece and relevant graphic organizer templates.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all topics. Students should pay attention to diagrams in the texts they read.

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly useful for explaining complex or abstract topics.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance reader comprehension by visually illustrating concepts within expository or argumentative writing.

### Affordances
- Clarifies concepts through visual representation, aids reader comprehension, and encourages deeper engagement with the text.

### Personalization
- Organizers can be tailored to the specific content of the writing piece. Students can create diagrams to support their own texts.
