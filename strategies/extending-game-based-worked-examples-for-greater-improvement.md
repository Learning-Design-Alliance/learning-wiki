# Extending game-based worked examples with practice problems, fading, self-explanation, verbal instruction and subgoals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation, prompted by a gain they call small. "In order to obtain greater improvement, the worked example instruction could add: (a) practice problems", followed by (b) fading procedure, (c) self-explanations, (d) verbal instruction, and (e) subgoals, each tied to earlier worked example research. These are proposals for future study; the present experiment did not test them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing worked example intervention for a game-based problem-solving task, to which the five additions are made
#### Constraints
- Untested in this study: the authors propose the additions for future study
- Motivated by the observation that the improvement was small compared to expert performance

### Target Learners
- Adults: undergraduate or graduate students playing a computer puzzle game (SafeCracker)

### Target Learning Goals
- Greater improvement in content understanding and problem-solving strategies in game-based tasks
