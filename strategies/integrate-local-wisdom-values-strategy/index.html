Integrate local wisdom values into social studies materials to build knowledge and social attitudes from an early age¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete strategy for elementary social studies: gather local wisdom material and values from the surrounding community, embed them in textbooks and lesson materials, and use them as the basis for knowledge learning and attitude formation. The article's implications state this fulfils learning needs contextually, facilitates teachers "because it can be directly given examples close to the daily life of students", and can serve "as a source of supporting references in local social studies learning both KTSP and K-2013 curriculum". Teachers should choose text material carefully so students gain knowledge and build character based on local values.

  • elementary school students

  • social studies knowledge

Claims¶

  • Experimental classes taught with local-wisdom-based social studies materials scored higher on knowledge than control classes after one semester [+W]
  • Experimental classes taught with local-wisdom-based social studies materials scored higher on social attitudes than control classes after one semester [+W]
  • A local-wisdom-based social studies learning model is judged feasible by expert panels, with CVR > 0 on every assessed item [+W]
  • Teachers and students judge the local-wisdom-based model feasible in one-on-one and small-group trials, with CVR > 0 on all items [+W]
  • Baseline social studies learning in the two Muna elementary schools was non-contextual, with low student interest and very low social attitudes [+W]
