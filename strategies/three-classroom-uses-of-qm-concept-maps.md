# Three classroom uses of the quantum concept maps: lecturer-led walkthroughs, recitation-section map building, and student homework support

## Description
The article suggests "three different ways to use the maps in teaching". First, after explaining a new Hamiltonian, "the lecturer could show the maps to the class and walk the students through them", connecting each structure and procedure with the general knowledge needed to predict the processes. Second, during recitation sections the instructor could build the maps while solving problems, tracing individual pathways back to a general knowledge structure. Finally, students could use the maps on their own as support while solving homework problems.
