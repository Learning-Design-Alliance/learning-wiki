# Phased training sequence: prepare and peer-evaluate a standard tooth, then practice with a colored tooth and iterative digital feedback before the exam

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A stepwise lesson sequence for transitioning students from the observational "see" stage to the operational "practice" stage. After a faculty demonstration, "each student was provided with a colored tooth model as a guidance for Class II cavity preparation training, with the red area of the tooth indicated tissue need to be removed." Students first prepare and peer-evaluate a standard tooth, submit it for digital evaluation, then practice with colored-tooth guidance plus digital and instructor feedback before a graded exam. The authors report this enhanced training effectiveness and confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Colored tooth models, an intraoral scanner with evaluation software, trained students in scanner use, and instructor feedback availability
#### Constraints
- Designed as a teaching auxiliary alongside teacher feedback rather than generating scores or replacing teachers

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students new to Class II cavity preparation

### Target Learning Goals
- cavity preparation psychomotor skill, evaluative judgment, and clinical confidence
