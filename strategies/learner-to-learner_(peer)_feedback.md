# Learner-to-Learner (Peer) Feedback

## Description
Learner-to-learner (peer) feedback involves having learners provide constructive feedback on each other's work. This approach encourages learners to reflect on their own understanding, learn to give relevant suggestions, receive specific ways to improve, and engage in metacognition. It requires modeling appropriate feedback techniques and providing guidelines for constructive criticism. Train students to give each other constructive feedback in a way that is positive and helpful. Encourage students to use post-it notes to record the given feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear guidelines for providing feedback, modeling appropriate techniques, a supportive classroom environment
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners to provide constructive criticism, requires time and effort
