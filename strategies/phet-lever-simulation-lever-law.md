# Introduce the law of the lever with PhET mass-objects simulations before the dynamic law

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the bernoullian sequence, the law of the lever (m1/m2 = l2/l1) is introduced to students through the PhET simulation platform, in which objects of the same kind are differentiated only by their mass values. This gives students an operational entry to the first law of rest without requiring the dynamic law. The article notes masses are given rather than weights, conflating weight and mass, but argues the conflation is harmless because the acceleration of gravity cancels in the equilibrium equations.
