# Match assessment measures to course content and outcomes, establish validation criteria, and address all four stages in RI teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review derives three recommendations for RI teaching stakeholders. First, the knowledge deficit on validation should be addressed: "there is no clear criteria for how an assessment measure is judged to be validated for RI teaching". Second, developers should "determine what validated assessment measures are the most appropriate to use for a specific outcome (e.g. a specific stage of the ethical decision-making process)". Third, all stages should be adequately addressed, since the authors see insufficient empirical evidence to neglect any stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recommendations 1 and 2 must come to fruition before all stages can be adequately assessed, per the authors
#### Constraints
- In-house measures may be more appropriate for some teaching but are likely not transferable or scalable; global alignment of RI teaching goals is presently lacking

### Target Learners
- authors and developers of RI teaching approaches
- research institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- appropriate assessment of research integrity teaching effectiveness across all four decision-making stages
