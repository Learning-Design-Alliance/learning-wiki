Incorporate 20+ minutes of daily curricular physical activity breaks tied to academic content in elementary classrooms¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that elementary teachers embed daily physical activity breaks of 20+ minutes into academic lessons, stating "Elementary teachers should be encouraged to incorporate PA during their lessons in the classroom setting". In the pilot, breaks related to the math and reading content being taught, and the teacher received a 30-minute training plus activity break cards and web resources. The authors conclude this allotment of time does not detract from performance outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A classroom teacher trained in classroom-based PA (the intervention teacher received a 30-minute training and prior graduate coursework)
  • PA breaks integrated with the math and reading content currently being taught

Constraints¶

  • Evidence comes from a small quasi-experimental pilot (two classrooms, 29 students) and the benefit appeared mainly in mathematics

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students (third grade)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading fluency
  • mathematics fluency
  • physical activity levels

Claims¶

  • Classroom PA intervention students scored significantly higher on CBM mathematics fluency than control students at the final time point [+W]
  • The PA intervention's benefit appeared in mathematics but not significantly in reading outcomes [+W]
