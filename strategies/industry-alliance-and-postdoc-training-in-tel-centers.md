# Engage industry and train postdoctoral researchers as part of a learning-technology research center

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As part of its knowledge-network model, CILT pairs its research agenda with two capacity-building mechanisms: business engagement and workforce development. The article states that CILT "engages business through an Industry Alliance Program and is also training postdoctoral students." The strategy treats a learning-technologies center not only as a funder of studies but as a broker connecting commercial actors and early-career researchers to the shared research agenda on technology-enabled learning.
