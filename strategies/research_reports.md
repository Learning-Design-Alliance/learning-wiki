# Research Reports

## Description
Research reports are visual aids designed to present data and insights in an engaging and accessible format. These reports use visuals to emphasize important information and inspire readers to understand and act on research findings. Effective research reports employ elements such as circle icons to highlight key findings and bar graphs to present achievements. By making research visually appealing, it becomes easier to share findings with students, staff, and the broader community.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to design software and data. Implementation involves careful integration of visuals and text. May require training in research communication.
#### Constraints
- Poorly designed reports can be confusing or misleading. Inaccurate data can undermine credibility. Requires attention to detail and accuracy.
