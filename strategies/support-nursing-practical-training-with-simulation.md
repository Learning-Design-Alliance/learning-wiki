# Use computer-aided simulations in nursing skills laboratories and support practical training with simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that "it is recommended to use computer-aided simulations in nursing skills laboratories and to support practical training with simulation". The strategy pairs clinical practice with researcher-designed simulation scenarios on a patient simulator, structured with prebriefing, application and debriefing phases, to raise satisfaction and self-confidence in learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulation laboratory and simulation model; scenarios aligned with course content; delivery consistent with INACSL best-practice standards
#### Constraints
- In the reported trial the simulation did not significantly change anxiety levels; generalisability is limited to one nursing department and one specific course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills preparation, learning satisfaction, self-confidence
