Involve teachers in action research and collaborative inquiry with higher-education academics to improve science teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that teachers become active researchers to find solutions and interventions for effective teaching, and that teachers and academics work together on student motivation and achievement. It states "there is a need to involve teachers to become active researchers to find the solutions and interventions for effective teaching". Further collaborative inquiry between schools and higher learning institutions is urged to address motivation and achievement in learning science.
