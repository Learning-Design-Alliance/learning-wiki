# Pair chess tutors with actively engaged class teachers and strengthen tutor classroom-management training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the process evaluation, the report identifies two key areas for improving the intervention: improving tutors' teaching skills so they can keep all children engaged, particularly in managing difficult behaviour and mixed-ability classes, and increasing the amount of liaison between tutors and class teachers. CSC ran one-day training courses and weekend seminars where tutors exchanged teaching methods and received classroom management tips from schoolteachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Class teachers attend a CSC one-day training workshop and actively contribute during lessons
- Tutors receive training in class management for mixed-ability classes
#### Constraints
- The on-treatment analysis of tutor quality and teacher training attendance was not pre-specified in the study protocol

### Target Learners
- Year 5 primary school pupils in disadvantaged schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement in chess lessons and faithful delivery of the chess curriculum
