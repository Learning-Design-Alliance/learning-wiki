# Address summer learning loss for students with disabilities to narrow achievement disparities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the research finds that "steeper summer learning losses for students with disabilities contribute to widening disparities," educators and policymakers should focus intervention on the summer period for this group, since school-year growth alone does not produce widening gaps. The brief's associated policy work extends this finding to COVID-19 recovery planning for students with disabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must operate during summer breaks when loss occurs, complementing school-year support
#### Constraints
- The available page text reports the pattern but no tested summer intervention; the recommendation follows from the differential-growth analysis of grades K-4

### Target Learners
- Students with disabilities in grades K-4

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of academic skills across summer breaks
