# Use micro-modules pairing short audio lessons with reflection texts and interactive quizzes for adult ELL mobile learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief describes Cell-Ed's implementable recipe for adult English language learning on mobile phones: micro-modules consisting of a short audio lesson, a follow-up text reinforcing the lesson for learner reflection, and an interactive quiz for application. Feedback and encouragement are built in, so learners find out immediately whether they passed a quiz, and pre-recorded encouraging audio messages are sent at regular intervals to sustain engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules must pair audio lessons with a reinforcing text and an interactive quiz
- Immediate quiz feedback and regularly scheduled encouraging audio messages should be built in
