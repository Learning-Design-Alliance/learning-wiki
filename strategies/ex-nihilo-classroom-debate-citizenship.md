## Description
The article advocates "the use of a full classroom discussion to debate the issues surrounding the ex-nihilo obstacle", since dichotic opinions potentially lead to conflict. Such a discussion provides cross-curricular opportunities in which research skills, information technology skills, literacy and drama can play a significant role, and — with citizenship coming online in England from September 2002 — an astronomical origins debate can accommodate citizenship programme-of-study items on diverse identities and on developing skills of enquiry and communication.
