# Begin spectral assignment by scanning the whole spectrum for repeating patterns, then apply empirical relations and iterate in a bootstrap fashion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes an implementable assignment procedure: "assignment should begin by examining the e ntire rotational spectrum and looking for repeating patterns". Located patterns are compared with the template portions of spectra in Figures 3 through 8; once patterns are identified, the Table 1 empirical relations give initial approximations of A, B, and C. With these initial estimates further patterns may be searched for and "the proce ss repeated in a bootstrap fashion".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a broadband rotational spectrum and the template patterns of Figures 3–8 and Table 1
#### Constraints
- Caution is advised to identify the molecule's non-zero dipole components before analysis, since b-type and c-type transitions can occur in similar patterns

### Target Learners
- beginning students in rotational spectroscopy
- undergraduate physical chemistry lab students

### Target Learning Goals
- quantum number assignment
- approximate rotational constant determination
