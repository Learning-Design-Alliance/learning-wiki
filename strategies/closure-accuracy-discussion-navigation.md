# Close the lesson with a guided discussion of the method's accuracy and its limits compared with modern GPS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The closure section invites students to evaluate the method they just used rather than simply ending the activity. "The students are invited to discuss how accurate this method is", with guided questions on the steps needed to plot a ship's position, how weather interferes because the Sun must be visible at local noon, and what knowledge and skills the exercise required. A final contrast question asks what skills GPS navigation needs, with the suggested answer being very little, sharpening awareness of what the historical method demands.

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating the accuracy and limitations of celestial-clock navigation
- Contrasting historical navigation with GPS-based navigation
