# Align simulation topics with students' clinical interests and enhance feedback processes such as video feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend improving the module by aligning simulation topics with students' clinical interests and strengthening feedback processes. "future iterations should align simulation topics with students’ clinical interests and improve the feedback process." They report adjustments have already been made to make simulations more representative of clinical practice and that video feedback will be introduced for more detailed, reflective learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of students' clinical interests to select relevant scenario topics
- Structured, timely, and constructive feedback processes
#### Constraints
- The article notes simulation and role-play may not fully capture the complexity of real-world team dynamics

### Target Learners
- Medical students in interprofessional education modules

### Target Learning Goals
- Team competencies
- Reflective learning through feedback
