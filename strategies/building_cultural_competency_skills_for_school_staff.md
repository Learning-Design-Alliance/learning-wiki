# Building Cultural Competency Skills for School Staff

## Description
Training staff in behaviors, attitudes, and policies that enable effective cross-cultural relationships. This includes shifting from deficit to strengths thinking, which views families as having significant expertise and resources. Staff should get to know families, local community resources, and create a welcoming school culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training resources, cultural competency experts, and a supportive school administration.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and commitment from school staff. May be challenging to change ingrained attitudes and biases.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Training can be tailored to the specific demographics and cultural contexts of the school community.
