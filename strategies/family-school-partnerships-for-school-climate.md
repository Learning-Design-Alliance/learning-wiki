# Invest in family-school partnerships, including access to families during the school day and student-family-educator co-creation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that educators "incorporate access to families during the school day and create opportunities for student-family-educator partnerships to improve school climate." It cites external sources that meaningful family engagement positively affects children's health, academic, and wellbeing outcomes, and that students supported by parents and caregivers are less likely to disengage from school and learning.
