# Increase Classroom Space and Reduce Overcrowding

## Description
Provide ample classroom space to support student engagement and learning. Overcrowded classrooms are linked to increased levels of aggression in students and decreased levels of student engagement. Ensure classrooms are large enough to accommodate flexible seating arrangements, teamwork, problem-solving, and effective communication. Create private study areas as well as smaller learning centers to reduce visual and auditory interruptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient classroom space, flexible seating arrangements, private study areas
#### Constraints
- May be costly to expand classroom sizes or build new schools. Requires careful planning to maximize the use of available space.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track levels of aggression and student engagement in classrooms with different sizes. Monitor academic achievement and collaboration skills in classrooms with ample space.

## Impact
- Overcrowded classrooms are also associated with decreased levels of student engagement and, therefore, decreased levels of learning. Classrooms with adequate space to reconfigure seating arrangements facilitate the use of different teaching methods that are aligned to 21st century skills.
