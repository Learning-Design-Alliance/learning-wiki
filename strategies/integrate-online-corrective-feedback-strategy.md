# Integrate online corrective feedback into the teaching and learning of academic writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adopting email-based corrective feedback as an alternative to conventional pen-and-paper correction in academic writing classrooms, because it was viable, effective and time saving in their study. The abstract states that "online corrective feedback shou ld be potentially useful when  integrated into the teachin g and learning of academic writing". Instructors should also find out students' feedback preferences before the writing lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor and students have email and word processor access for submitting and returning drafts
#### Constraints
- The conclusion is only preliminary as the sample size for this study is rather small with the comparison of the two groups

### Target Learners
- tertiary ESL/L1 Malay academic writing students

### Target Learning Goals
- improve academic writing skills while saving instructor and student time

## Claims

- [Students were more motivated to correct errors when feedback was delivered electronically, a factor the authors attribute to the appeal of electronic devices](../claims/electronic-devices-motivate-error-correction.md) [+W]
- [The control and experimental groups showed no significant difference in writing performance on the first draft](../claims/ocf-ccf-no-draft1-difference.md) [+M]
- [Online corrective feedback produced larger reductions in sentence structure, grammar and vocabulary errors than conventional corrective feedback, which showed no grammar improvement](../claims/ocf-larger-error-reductions.md) [+M]
- [Tertiary L1 Malay learners given online corrective feedback via email outperformed the conventional corrective feedback control group on the final draft](../claims/ocf-outperforms-ccf-final-draft.md) [+M]
- [Most students preferred receiving corrective feedback online via email, with ease of submission and time saving the leading reasons](../claims/students-prefer-online-corrective-feedback.md) [+M]
