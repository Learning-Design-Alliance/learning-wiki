# Establish communities of practice, including an in-school PAL champion and connections to wider PAL networks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Participants identified the need for practitioners to engage in a community of practice where teachers share passion and enthusiasm for PAL and learn from each other in a supportive environment. The article reports that "participants recognized the need for an in-school 'PAL champion' at the micro-level to actively lead PAL provision", and that connecting to the wider PAL community at the macro-level was also deemed important. Private, tailored virtual networks are suggested as one solution to the challenge of widening the community beyond trusted networks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive environment in which teachers can share best practice and learn from each other
#### Constraints
- Widening the community of practice beyond trusted networks has previously proved challenging due to a lack of trust and familiarity; there is limited understanding of the essential characteristics required to create successful virtual PAL multi-stakeholder networks

### Target Learners
- school teachers and teaching assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained implementation of physically active learning across the school
