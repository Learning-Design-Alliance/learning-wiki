# Story Map

## Description
Story maps are graphic organizers that help learners visually represent and understand the key elements of a narrative, such as characters, setting, plot, conflict, and resolution. They can be used before, during, or after reading to improve comprehension, provide a framework for identifying story elements, and help learners organize information and ideas. Story maps can range from simple formats focusing on the beginning, middle, and end of a story to more complex organizers focusing on plot, character development, or theme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Blank story map organizers or digital tools for creating story maps, familiar stories or texts to use as examples, chart paper for whole-class story map construction
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who struggle with graphic organizers or visual representations. Effectiveness depends on the quality of the story map template and the instructor's guidance.
