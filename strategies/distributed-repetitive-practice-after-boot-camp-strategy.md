# Use distributed repetitive practice after intensive skills training to retain and strengthen acquired surgical skills and confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends continuing skills training after an intensive boot camp, citing studies that "have recommended distributed practice and demonstrated the benefits of distributed practice over massed practice for learning surgical skills." Repetitive practice provides "more opportunities for consolidation" and allows cognitive preparation and mental rehearsal, which help strengthen memory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured practice sessions scheduled throughout the first year after the intensive course, focused on boot camp content
#### Constraints
- Boot camp effects diminish over time without reinforcement; the delay between learning skills and needing them at work must be bridged

### Target Learners
- new surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- retention and strengthening of surgical skills and confidence

## Claims

- [Residents who received a two-week surgical boot camp plus a year of repetitive practice passed department rotation assessments at a significantly higher rate than residents who received routine residency training alone.](../claims/boot-camp-repetitive-practice-higher-rotation-pass-rate.md) [+W]
- [After a year of repetitive practice, the boot camp cohort showed significantly higher confidence than the routine-training cohort on six surgical tasks, including acute abdomen management, trauma management, and laparoscopic camera navigation.](../claims/repetitive-practice-confidence-exceeds-controls-six-tasks.md) [+W]
- [Residents' self-rated confidence scores rose from post-boot-camp levels to the one-year follow-up after repetitive practice, in a within-group pre-post comparison, with significantly higher scores on all surveyed items.](../claims/repetitive-practice-further-raises-confidence.md) [+W]
