# Launch reform through small recorded trials and scale up only what works

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends starting parental support for teacher education through small projects that keep records of actions and results, since verification of the idea "can only be gradual, starting from small projects." Failed attempts contribute information about what does not work if cases are recorded, allowing participants to filter out elements that work. The author also warns against spending resources on large projects not tried at small scale, and notes that scaling up successful projects brings new problems needing identification and solution.
