# Deliberate practice with immediate feedback and repeated simulation lab practice before competency evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy has students repetitively practice specific clinical skills in a simulation lab with immediate faculty feedback and augmented electronic feedback, followed by reflection, before a formal competency evaluation. "Deliberate practice is a focused approach aimed at a well-defined goal, not the mindless repetition of a task." The article reports that faculty demonstration, close supervision, guidance, and the ability to practice skills numerous times through deliberate practice before demonstrating competency was highly effective.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate time practicing in the simulation lab, immediate feedback on technique and performance, and reflection on the experience
#### Constraints
- Requires faculty guidance and supervision; more research is needed to show that simulation can be incorporated into nursing education

### Target Learners
- prelicensure baccalaureate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical psychomotor skills, competence, and confidence
