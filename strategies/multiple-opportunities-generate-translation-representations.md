# Give students multiple opportunities to recognize, recall, and generate translations between representations using research-based learning tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students proficient at recall still fail at generation, the authors recommend in the summary that "Students must be given multiple opportunities to not only recognize and recall but also generate answers to these types of questions related to translation between the representations" using research-based learning tools. The goal is developing a functional understanding of the underlying concepts rather than reproduction of expressions in the context in which they were discussed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must go beyond problem solving that requires recalling expressions from a particular context and reproducing them on exams.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is stated for translation between Dirac notation and position and momentum representations of quantum states.

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate quantum mechanics students
- graduate quantum mechanics students

### Target Learning Goals
- functional understanding of quantum state representations and Dirac notation
