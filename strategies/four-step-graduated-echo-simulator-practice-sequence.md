# Four-step graduated practical training sequence from trainer demonstration to learner performance with real-time feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
During the three-hour practical training, the trainer used a graduated four-step sequence: the trainer "demonstrated echocardiographic views in real time without comment", then repeated and interpreted each sequence, then the learners explained the techniques while the trainer performed them, and finally each candidate performed the sequence while receiving feedback in real time. A post-test identical to the pre-test followed this phase to evaluate knowledge and practice acquisition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A qualified person (a cardiologist) was available at all times to help the residents in case of technical difficulties, at their request
- Groups of one to three residents per three-hour session with the simulation mannequin

- 

### Target Learners
- Medical residents of specialties confronted with situations of cardio-respiratory emergencies

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquiring practical skill in obtaining and interpreting echocardiographic views
