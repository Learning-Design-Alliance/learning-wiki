# Targeted continuing professional development and specialist shadowing to address cognitive errors in oral cancer recognition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that diagnostic knowledge and confidence be enhanced through targeted continuing professional development (CPD) and shadowing specialists in maxillofacial surgery and oral medicine, so that dental professionals remain able to recognize subtle clinical features while minimizing unnecessary referrals. The study's clinical relevance statement says identifying confidence gaps "can inform targeted educational interventions for reliable oral cancer screening in general dental practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should be targeted at the specific lesion types and cognitive errors identified through confidence-accuracy assessment
#### Constraints
- The authors state AI-based tools should complement, not replace, clinical expertise, alongside ongoing professional education

### Target Learners
- Qualified dentists
- Dental students in clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate and confident recognition of suspicious oral lesions
- Timely and appropriate referral of oral cancer and potentially malignant disorders
