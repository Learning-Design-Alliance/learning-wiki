# Use peer-led, sectioned class discussion of test items as a structured remediation procedure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a nine-step remediation procedure in which test items are divided into sections, students with the highest score in each section lead class discussion to identify correct answers, students ask questions on difficult areas, and the teacher provides a guide and/or assists where the need arises. This structured, peer-led discussion follows feedback and precedes the next unit of instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formative test must be administered and scored before the remediation discussion, and feedback must be presented before the next unit begins
#### Constraints
- No form of remediation was given on the posttest in this study

### Target Learners
- junior secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
