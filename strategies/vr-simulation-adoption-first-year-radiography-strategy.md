# Introduce VR simulation in first-year radiography education, with further research before replacing physical simulation in later years

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their results, the authors recommend that "widespread adoption of VR simulation in first‐year radiography education is timely and desirable" and that the findings "provide evidence that it may be possible to replace the use of physical simulation across other years of the degree". They note such a decision could not be taken with certainty without extending the study to later-year students learning more complex procedures, and that further research is warranted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Investigation of VR simulation versus physical simulation for students in later years of the degree programme before full replacement
#### Constraints
- Findings may not arise in later year groups or in student groups in other universities with different previous experience

### Target Learners
- first-year radiography students
- radiography students in later years of a degree programme

### Target Learning Goals
- radiographic positioning
- preparation for clinical placement
