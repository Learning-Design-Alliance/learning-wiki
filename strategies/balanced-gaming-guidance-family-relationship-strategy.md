# Provide balanced guidance on video game use while fostering parent-child relationships, educational expectations, and learning attitudes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that parents, teachers, and policymakers jointly address excessive gaming: provide balanced guidance on video game use, foster supportive parent-child relationships with reasonable supervision, and cultivate adolescents' self-educational expectations and learning attitudes. The authors state stakeholders "should provide balanced guidance on video game use, while fostering positive family relationships to support adolescents' educational expectation and learning attitude". They also suggest educators "could offer courses on time management and self-regulation training" to help students balance study and recreation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation among parents, teachers, and policymakers
- Attention to both supervision and supportive family relationships
#### Constraints
- Grounded in cross-sectional, correlational findings; causal effects not established
- Moderation findings suggest close parent-child relationships may accompany freer gaming, so supervision must be combined with warmth

### Target Learners
- Adolescents in junior high school

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining academic achievement alongside recreational gaming
- Self-regulation and time management
- Positive learning attitudes and educational expectations
