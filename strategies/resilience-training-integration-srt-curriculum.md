# Integrate resilience training (CBT, mindfulness) into the standardized residency training curriculum for radiology residents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding resilience training into China's standardized residency training (SRT) for radiology residents, arguing it is "essential to integrate resilience training into the curriculum of SRT programs" and that this may be "helpful and cost-effective to help improve competency of residents". Candidate formats named are cognitive behavioral therapy, mindfulness-based training, and a combination of the two.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residency programs must be able to involve residents in resilience training programs, such as CBT (cognitive behavioral therapy), mindfulness-based training, and a combination of CBT and mindfulness training
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is a cross-sectional survey, and the authors caution that relying solely on professional resilience training might not fully address the underlying work stress issue

### Target Learners
- radiology residents in standardized residency training programs in China

### Target Learning Goals
- improving and maintaining resident competency
- enhancing adaptability to intense work stress
