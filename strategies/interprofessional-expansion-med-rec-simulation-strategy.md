# Expand the simulation to an interprofessional audience by adding profession-specific content, faculty, and adjusted assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For interprofessional use, the authors recommend "adding content and faculty representative of each profession involved" and adjusting knowledge questions to learner type and training level. The standardized patient case could be expanded to include functional deficits for rehabilitation therapy learners, socioeconomic factors for social work learners, and cognitive deficits for neuropsychology learners, reflecting that each profession contributes to medication management for older patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content and faculty representative of each profession involved, with knowledge questions adjusted based on learner type and level of training
#### Constraints
- The authors state that expanding content and evaluation to meet interprofessional education standards is a future direction, not something the current iteration did

### Target Learners
- medicine, pharmacy, and nursing trainees
- rehabilitation therapy, social work, and psychology learners

### Target Learning Goals
- interprofessional collaboration in medication management
- profession-specific contributions to medication safety for older adults
