# Integrate high-quality, high-fidelity simulation-based learning into endocrinology curricula alongside traditional methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions incorporate high-quality SBL courses into the endocrinology curriculum, integrated thoughtfully with traditional methods. It states that "investing in high-quality simulation resources could enhance learning outcomes," citing evidence that high-fidelity simulations with realistic scenarios, accurate anatomical models, personalized learning, and immediate feedback are more effective. Integration should consider simulation fidelity, compatibility with conventional instruction, and learner needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High simulation fidelity and ongoing adjustments based on educational research and technological advancements
- Consideration of specific contexts and individual learner needs when combining SBL with traditional methods
#### Constraints
- The article notes substantial resources are required for high-fidelity simulations and calls for cost-effectiveness research across different settings

### Target Learners
- endocrinology trainees: medical students, interns, residents, and primary care physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- endocrine knowledge acquisition
- clinical skills
- learner satisfaction and interest
