# Use cover, copy, and compare with graphic organizers as a study strategy for learning stimulus relations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students first review the content to be learned displayed on screen, cover the content by advancing to a blank screen, attempt to copy it from memory onto paper, and then compare their answers to the original content, making corrections with a different colored pen. The graphic organizer serves as "an analog to the study guide, and CCC functions as the study strategy." This procedure grows closer to the workflow a student might follow when preparing for a multiple-choice exam.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video training illustrating and describing the procedure
- Performance feedback on graphic organizer fidelity during initial training
- Blank paper and a red pen for the compare aspect
#### Constraints
- Outcomes can only be cautiously compared with basic preparations using less explicit instructions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- students of different abilities, academic risk level, and age

### Target Learning Goals
- learning stimulus relations
- note taking
- preparing for multiple-choice exams
