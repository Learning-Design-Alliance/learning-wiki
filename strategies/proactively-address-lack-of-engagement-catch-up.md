# Proactively monitor engagement trajectories and address students who fail to improve or catch up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators use longitudinal engagement monitoring rather than single snapshots, treating disengagement as a warning sign and lack of improvement as especially ominous. It states that "Lack of improvement or 'catching up' may be a more ominous sign that should be proactively addressed", while improving to higher engagement was associated with acceptable achievement and rare dropouts. This supports person-centered, trajectory-based early-alert practice rather than one-off engagement flags.
