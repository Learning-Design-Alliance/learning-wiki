# Assign mind-map notetaking homework connecting concepts across all chapters of the course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends having students create mind maps linking the concepts of every chapter covered, as a notetaking homework assignment. The authors state: "Asking students to make their mind map connecting different concepts in all the modules (chapters) covered in a course as notetaking homework, which we encour- aged students to prepare, is an excellent homework any instructor can assign." The instructor found this very helpful for students' overall understanding of the course, and mind mapping is described as helping find linkages between ideas and concepts.
