# Modify the Vietnamese physical therapy curriculum and use journal clubs to build EBP capacity and reduce the English language barrier

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends concrete changes to education and workplace policy to enable EBP implementation in Viet Nam. It states the PT curriculum should add English technical items, reading and appraising research articles, and EBP, and suggests to "implement journal clubs as an effective solution to create a positive environment to discuss and apply evidence, and to decrease the English language barrier". These recommendations follow directly from the survey's barrier and knowledge findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EBP must be required as compulsory credits in theory and practical courses
- Continuing education programming must be developed to support applying EBP
#### Constraints
- The article notes workload must be addressed first, recommending job analysis to address the number of patients per therapist

### Target Learners
- Vietnamese physical therapy students
- practicing physical therapists in Ho Chi Minh City

### Target Learning Goals
- reading and appraising research articles in English
- understanding and applying the five steps of EBP
