# Say it Once

## Description
Repeating instructions can lead to lazy listening habits in learners. By establishing a practice of providing instructions only once, instructors can encourage learners to actively listen the first time. For learners who may have missed the instruction, instructors can suggest they, "ask three, then ask me."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor consistency in providing instructions only once, establishment of 'ask three, then ask me' protocol
#### Constraints
- Some learners may feel left behind if they miss the initial instruction
