# Open the lesson with a guided question-and-answer sequence connecting life, liquid water and stellar heat

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The introduction scripts a short question-and-answer sequence: the teacher asks whether there is life on Mars, whether liquid water is present, how the two are related, what supports life on other planets and what keeps water liquid, with model answers supplied — "Ask the students if there is life on Mars and if there is water on Mars. Ask the students if there is any relation between them." — directing the discussion towards the presence of water before the analogue is introduced.
