# Revise professional development policies to build a structured framework for peer learning, including learning communities and succession planning programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that health education systems revise professional development policies to build a structured framework for peer learning, since in Iranian hospitals it lacks a formal role due to absent policies, limited resources, and low awareness among senior managers. Recommended strategies include "programs that foster horizontal interactions, create learning communities, support experience exchange, and strengthen peer-to-peer initiatives." The authors argue an integrated approach combining individual empowerment with structural change can maximize peer learning's impact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational structure reform, enhancement of managers' soft skills, promotion of a participatory culture, and reduction of systemic barriers
#### Constraints
- Simply providing educational opportunities or facilitating meetings is insufficient, per the article

### Target Learners
- Hospital managers and health system policymakers

### Target Learning Goals
- Managerial professional development and experience transfer
