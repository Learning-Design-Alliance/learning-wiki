# Quality-assurance measures for dual stain training: monitor agreement rates, expert-led workshops, external exams and certification, and an expert-technician network

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose concrete quality-assurance activities for laboratories implementing dual staining. They suggest "assessing dual stain agreement rates between experts and novice evaluators using slides from different age groups", monitoring improvement by "measuring the time needed to evaluate one slide", holding "face‐to‐face or digital work‐shops with experts" to discuss difficult cases, recommending "external exams and possibly certification" comparable to the QUATE exam, external staining quality control via NordicQC or UK-NEQAS, and establishing "a network of experts and cytotechnicians from routine screening laboratories" to build consensus on proper training programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to expert evaluators for agreement monitoring and workshops
- External examination and quality-control bodies for certification and staining QC
#### Constraints
- The study itself was not designed to assess the value of continued expert-led training of novice evaluators

### Target Learners
- Novice cytotechnicians and laboratory staff in routine screening laboratories

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining accurate and consistent dual stain interpretation skills over time
