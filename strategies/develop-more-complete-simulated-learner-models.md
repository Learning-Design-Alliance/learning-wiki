## Description
Based on their review findings, the authors recommend that future research focus on developing more complete simulated learner models rather than models capturing only narrow aspects of student learning. They state: "we propose that future research should focus on developing more complete simulated learner models". The recommendation follows directly from the finding of narrow model coverage and the validity-evidence gap.
