# Direct future AR-in-education research toward attitudinal, motivational, and demographic outcomes and larger samples including theses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends extending AR effectiveness research beyond academic achievement. It states that "Different research could be conducted to study the effect of AR applications in the learning process as it affects variables such as attitude, anxiety, motivation, etc." and that additional independent variables such as age or gender could be investigated, along with master's and PhD theses to examine larger sampling sizes. Researchers are also advised to test AR prototypes for benefits and user-friendliness.
