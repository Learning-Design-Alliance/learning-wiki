# Provide at least 25 hours of targeted volunteer training, with at least half pre-service and the remainder throughout the year

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
New volunteers "receive at least 25 hours of targeted training and coaching in literacy, behavior management, child development and other relevant areas". At least half of the training occurs pre-service, prior to entering the classroom, and the remainder happens throughout the year. Most training is conducted locally in a face-to-face format, with e-learning courses in development for online training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training topics: literacy, behavior management, child development and other relevant areas
- Local face-to-face delivery for most training
#### Constraints
- E-learning courses for online training are still in development

### Target Learners
- Volunteer tutors ages 50 or older

### Target Learning Goals
- Tutoring skills for struggling K-3 readers
