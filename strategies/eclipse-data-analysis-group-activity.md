# Playful group activity: students analyse the historical eclipse dataset to choose between two anonymous theories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a group activity for high-school students built on the released dataset. The lesson opens with a playful framing: "Two famous scientists A and B have proposed two alternative theories for an important astronomical phenomenon", with predicted values of 1.75 and 0.87 arcsec, and students told the scientific community is split. Each group receives a portion of the data from one expedition, freely chooses its analysis methods (graphical interpolation, spreadsheet, ad-hoc computer programs), and presents conclusions to the classroom after a few days.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Present the task as generally and anonymously as possible to keep students unbiased toward the data
