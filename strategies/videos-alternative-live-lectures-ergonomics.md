# Use videos as an easy-to-organize alternative to live lectures when teaching dental ergonomics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 5 studies (5 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 5 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that because live lectures and videos produced similar learning outcomes, videos are a practical substitute for live lecturing in dental ergonomics teaching. It states that "Videos provide an easy‐to‐organize alternative to live lectures in teaching dental ergonomics." The video session used in the study combined a Finnish Dental Association ergonomics video with YouTube clips, presented without verbal teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video content must cover the same topics as the lecture and give basic knowledge on the subject, as the article notes both sessions covered same topics
#### Constraints
- The study's video session gave no verbal teaching and contents were not fully identical to the live lecture, so outcomes may not generalize to fully identical recorded lectures

### Target Learners
- third-year dental students beginning clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of physical ergonomics in dentistry
