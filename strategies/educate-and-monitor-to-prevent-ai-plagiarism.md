# Prevent AI-assisted plagiarism through student education, signed declarations, draft submissions, clear guidelines, and close monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article lists staff strategies for preventing plagiarism with ChatGPT: educating students on what plagiarism is and why it is wrong, requiring written declarations that work is the student's own, requiring drafts for review before final submission, setting clear guidelines on when and how GPT-3 may be used and how AI-generated text must be cited, and monitoring student work closely, including asking students to present work in class.
