# Reinforce low-recognition topics through explicit teaching, clinical case illustrations, or repeated exposure in future module versions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that persistent knowledge gaps (behavioral disorders, alkylphenols, phenoxyethanol) be addressed by reinforcing "these specific topics through more explicit teaching, clinical case illustrations, or repeated exposure," noting "a single exposure to the content may not be sufficient for more complex or less salient topics." Additional recommendations include incorporating a standardized EDC assessment tool and adding content on substances with persistently low recognition rates through simplified explanations or interactive case scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of specific low-recognition items through pre-post assessment data
#### Constraints
- Applies to complex or less salient topics where single e-learning exposure proved insufficient

### Target Learners
- family medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- recognize less familiar EDCs such as alkylphenols and phenoxyethanol
- associate EDCs with behavioral disorders
