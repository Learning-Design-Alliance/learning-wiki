# Structure apprenticeship sessions as observation or co-treatment followed by an unstructured 15–20 minute reflective conversation and a written reflective log

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The protocol specifies a concrete session recipe for apprenticeship learning in clinical settings. "Following each treatment session students will have a 15–20 minutes reflective conversation with the therapist. This discussion will be unstructured so as to allow for any questions or topics that the student might find important or interesting." Learning is further facilitated by written reflective log entries on each session's experiences, which serve both to encourage reflection and to give researchers session-by-session insight into the learning process. Research team members meet students before and after each session to administer measures and monitor adherence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A therapist available for a post-session reflective conversation after each treatment session
- Time in the student's schedule for log writing and pre/post-session research-team meetings
#### Constraints
- The reflective conversation is deliberately unstructured; the protocol does not dictate the degree of student in-session participation, leaving it to therapist, student, and patient

### Target Learners
- Novice psychotherapy students observing or co-treating in real clinics

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflection on salient aspects of therapy sessions
- Understanding the learning process in apprenticeships
