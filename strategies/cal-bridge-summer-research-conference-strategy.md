# Place undergraduates in supervised summer research at PhD-granting institutions and require conference presentations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Cal-Bridge scholars participate in supervised research in the summer and during the academic year. The CAMPARE summer research program, running since 2009, places students from 21 CSU campuses and more than 30 community college partners at one of 18 research sites, including 7 UC campuses. Working with UC faculty gives scholars a window into research at a potential PhD campus and gives UC faculty an in-depth look they can describe in recommendation letters. All scholars are expected to present results at regional and national conferences such as the AAS meeting or the APS March Meeting.
