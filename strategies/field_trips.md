# Field Trips

## Description
Field trips are educational excursions that take learners outside of the classroom to experience real-world environments and engage with content in a multisensory, interactive manner. Field trips activate learners' background knowledge, extend classroom learning, and provide access to resources and experiences not available in the classroom. Whether physical or virtual, field trips create lasting memories and enhance understanding through direct engagement with art, history, science, and culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Planning, transportation, permission slips, funding (can be offset with fundraising). For virtual trips: devices and internet access.
#### Constraints
- Physical field trips can be costly and logistically challenging, potentially excluding disadvantaged learners. Overly structured trips may limit exploration and discovery. Virtual trips may lack the sensory richness and social interaction of physical trips.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Increase learner engagement, connect classroom learning to real-world experiences, promote socio-emotional growth, enhance critical thinking skills

### Affordances
- Provides real-world context for learning, increases motivation and engagement, supports multisensory learning, builds social-emotional skills, enhances long-term memory through memorable experiences, levels the playing field for disadvantaged learners by providing equal opportunities for enriching experiences.

### Personalization
- Field trips can be personalized by allowing learners to choose destinations, involving them in planning, and providing options for reflection and follow-up activities. Virtual field trips can further personalize the experience by allowing learners to explore at their own pace and focus on areas of interest. Pre- and post-trip activities can also be tailored to individual learning needs and styles.

## Assessment Evidence
- Increased test scores, improved engagement in follow-up activities, enhanced critical thinking skills, positive changes in empathy and tolerance, learner-generated artifacts (e.g., reflections, presentations) that demonstrate learning and connection to real-world experiences.

## Impact
- Research indicates that learners who participate in field trips have better grades, higher graduation rates, and greater income. Field trips enhance engagement, intellectual curiosity, and interest in learning both in and out of school. Additionally, field trips promote empathy, tolerance, and critical thinking skills (Whitesell, 2016). Virtual field trips, such as Google Expeditions, can increase learner motivation and provide access to inaccessible locations.
