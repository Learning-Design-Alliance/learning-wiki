# Guide students in defining a brick grammar connecting shapes and colors to types of information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the play phase, the educator assists students in defining the purpose of each group's task and the grammar of their bricks by making a connection between the various shapes and colors and the sorts of information that may be discovered. To facilitate the process, the grammar can be noted with sticky notes attached to the kit box, so that children always have a reference in front of them.

#### Requirements
- The teacher demonstrates how to stack and combine the data bricks and helps each group articulate the mapping between brick attributes and information types.
