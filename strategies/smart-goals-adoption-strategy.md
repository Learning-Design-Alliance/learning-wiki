# Students adopt SMART goals pursued vigorously to improve academic performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the authors recommend that students adopt a positive attitude to their studies by setting SMART goals — specific, measurable, attainable, reliable and timely — and pursuing these goals vigorously irrespective of family background and gender. Breaking learning objectives into small short-term goals is assumed to motivate students as each small goal is achieved, ultimately leading to better performance.
