# Engage passive observers in structured peer assessment with a checklist during observational phases of simulation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
When only some trainees can hold active roles in a simulation, give the observers a checklist of key skill aspects and ask them to assess the performing trainee's performance during each scenario. The article reports that "peer observers of the intervention group were asked to assess the team leader's performance during each scenario using the same checklist scoring form as the facilitator." This combines observation with assessment to engage observers actively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A checklist containing key aspects of the skill, provided to observers while they watch; scoring forms collected after each scenario
#### Constraints
- The immediate in-training benefit did not persist to a two-week retention test; the design could not isolate a testing effect because both groups were assessed during training

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students in simulation-based skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- acute patient assessment skills (ABCDE approach) and team-leader performance in emergency scenarios
