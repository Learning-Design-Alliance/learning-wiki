# Analyze transitions into and out of Just Math to understand students' collective math-readiness negotiation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose studying the scaffolding and negotiation that bring a group to a Just Math-ready state, and the individual transitions into and out of the frame. They write that "Transitions into and out of Just Math are a fruitful area of future research," noting that different group members may need different amounts of scaffolding to reach a common negotiated state. This strategy treats the frame boundary as the analytical unit for studying math use in groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A library of before, during, and after indicators of Just Math, which the authors plan to build by characterizing pre and post behaviors, discourse, and expectations in greater detail
#### Constraints
- Analysis is confined to contexts where students work together in groups and talk about their problem solving; individual Just Math while working alone has not been uncovered in a naturalistic setting

### Target Learners
- upper-division physics students in cooperative homework groups

### Target Learning Goals
- transitioning between conceptual sense-making and mathematical execution in physics problem solving
