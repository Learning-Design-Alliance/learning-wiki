# Trainer-of-trainees scale-up strategy combining Moyo device rollout with HBB simulation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The project used a trainer-of-trainees (TOT) cascade: master trainers at Haydom Lutheran Hospital trained four participants from each district hospital over 2 days (1 day HBB, 1 day Moyo) using hands-on simulation with a NeoNatalie simulator and upright bag-mask ventilator. TOTs then co-trained nurses, midwives and doctors at their own hospitals and supervised ongoing practice. The article states "local Trainers of Trainees (TOTs) were trained to support the use of Moyo for FHR monitoring and ongoing HBB practices at the facilities to sustain knowledge and skills."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experienced master trainers in HBB and Moyo FHR monitoring
- Supply of training and clinical care materials (Moyo devices, NeoNatalie simulators, upright bag-mask ventilators, Penguin suction) to the facilities
#### Constraints
- Apart from TOT supervision there were no other quality improvement activities related to maternal and newborn health at the district hospitals

### Target Learners
- nurses and midwives
- doctors at district hospitals in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- newborn resuscitation knowledge and skills
- fetal heart rate monitoring with Moyo
