# Implement blended simulation and e-learning through faculty development, infrastructure investment, and CBME alignment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The editorial recommends that institutions integrating simulation and e-learning provide faculty development so educators are "digitally native and skilled in facilitating both virtual and physical learning environments", invest in simulation spaces, digital platforms, and technical support for sustainability, and explicitly align both modalities with competency-based medical education (CBME) frameworks so they contribute to curricular outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategic planning and institutional support
- Faculty development for educators
- Infrastructure investment in simulation spaces, digital platforms, and technical support
#### Constraints
- Success of e-learning strategies depends on thoughtful design of study plans, equitable access to sustainable technology, and seamless integration into broader curricular frameworks
