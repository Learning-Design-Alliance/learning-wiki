# Family Engagement: Supporting Children with Disabilities

## Description
Family engagement is a practice in which families and school personnel collaborate to support and improve the learning and development of children, including those with disabilities. It involves behaviors and practices in the school environment, such as parent/guardian-child and parent/guardian-teacher communication, roles, and responsibilities at home and school. Effective engagement requires thoughtful planning, open communication, and sensitivity to the unique challenges faced by families of children with disabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment from school personnel, active participation from families, effective communication channels, and sensitivity to the unique needs of families and students with disabilities
#### Constraints
- Requires time, effort, and resources to develop and maintain. May be challenging to engage all families, especially those facing significant stressors.
