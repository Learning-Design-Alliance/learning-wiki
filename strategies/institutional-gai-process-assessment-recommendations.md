# Institutional recommendations: shift from detection to process-oriented assessment, transparent AI-use protocols, and revised integrity policies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article issues three strategic recommendations for institutions integrating GAI into assessment. First, 'institutions should transition from a reliance on detection-based approaches to a process-oriented philosophy', structuring assessments around learning processes and iterative development. Second, institutions should implement transparent AI-use protocols requiring students to document, disclose, and reflect on GAI use. Third, integrity policies should be revised so that responsible, well-documented AI integration is recognised as part of emerging digital and learning literacies rather than framed as misconduct.
