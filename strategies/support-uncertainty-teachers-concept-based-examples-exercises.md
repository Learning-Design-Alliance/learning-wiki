# Support teachers of measurement uncertainty with concept-based teaching, examples, exercises, software and exchange of good practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that uncertainty training emphasize "a concept-based approach and an emphasis on the underlying principles rather than statistical and calculational procedures alone". Teachers should use worked examples (e.g. an annotated template of a simple uncertainty budget), interactive exercises, quizzes and practical work to transfer higher levels of cognition, plus software, e-learning and video material, and exchange good practices through activities such as the MU Training consortium.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need content knowledge of the GUM suite, general pedagogical skills, uncertainty-specific pedagogical content knowledge, and awareness of audience needs and prior training
#### Constraints
- The article notes a caveat of exercises and examples: the principles underlying them must be understood to adapt them to other measurement models or conditions

### Target Learners
- teachers of measurement uncertainty
- trainees from NMIs, laboratories, legal metrology and universities

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the concepts and principles of measurement uncertainty, not only statistical and calculational procedures

### Affordances
- Eight Communities Uncertainty Understanding
