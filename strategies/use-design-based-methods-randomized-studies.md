# Use design-based methods for causal inference in randomized studies of individuals or groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends broadening knowledge of design-based methods by describing "their key concepts and how they compare to model-based methods," presenting the approach's rudiments "using simple mathematical notation" and discussing the underlying intuition "for designs where individuals or groups are randomized." Researchers conducting randomized evaluations can use this brief to understand and apply design-based causal inference.
