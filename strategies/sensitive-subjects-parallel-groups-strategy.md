# Require sensitive current-event subjects addressed in parallel by different groups to engage all three pedagogical dimensions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their empirical observations, the authors suggest that to offer learners in a class the opportunity to incorporate all three identified academic dimensions, "perhaps it would be wise to require current event subjects to be sensitive and have diMerent groups address the same subject in parallel." They judge such an approach could help develop learners' reasoning skills while questioning its educational scope.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one Newsgame in the class must elicit discomfort among some learners to promote debate on the communication potential of video games.
#### Constraints
- The authors question the potential educational scope, asking whether learners designing learning games or edugames instead would assimilate knowledge from an academic program.

### Target Learners
- engineering students in game design courses

### Target Learning Goals
- reasoning skills
- media education
- debate on current events

## Claims

- [Designing Newsgames in an engineering course stimulates debate on issues beyond video game design techniques and reinforces knowledge of the current events incorporated into the games](../claims/newsgame-design-stimulates-debate-and-knowledge.md) [+M]
