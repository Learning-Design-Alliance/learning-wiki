# Educational Research Map Visualization

## Description
This strategy involves using a research map visualization tool to explore education and learning sciences research. The visualization is built using data from academic journals, analyzing titles, keywords, authors, and abstracts to create a bibliographic coupling network. Articles sharing common references are linked, forming topics and subtopics. This tool allows users to explore common themes, connections between topics, and save articles of interest. It serves as an interactive encyclopedia, providing a wide breadth of information arranged by subject. The tool is designed to help educators stay informed about current research, explore topics in depth, and identify resources relevant to their practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to the research map visualization tool and internet connectivity. Users need to be familiar with basic research concepts and terminology.
#### Constraints
- The visualization is based on data from a specific time period (2009-2018) and may not include the most recent research. It is more of an encyclopedia than a structured literature review and does not offer a specific point of view.
