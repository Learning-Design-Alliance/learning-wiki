# Build state AI evaluation on the ESSA evidence framework and integrate results into the existing education evidence base

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In their discussion, the authors call on state leaders to co-design guidance with communities and to build collaborative approaches on the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA) of 2015, which established transparency expectations for educational outcomes. Integrating AI evaluation into the existing education evidence base is framed as creating an empirical foundation establishing what works and what does not for AI in learning environments, equipping leaders for informed decisions.
