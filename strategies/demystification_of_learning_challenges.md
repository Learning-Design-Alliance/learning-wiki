# Demystification of Learning Challenges

## Description
Demystification involves explaining to learners how memory and learning work, especially when they are struggling. By validating their experiences and providing insights into the cognitive processes behind learning challenges, learners can develop a better understanding of their strengths and weaknesses. This approach fosters a growth mindset and encourages learners to seek effective strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the learner's specific learning challenges, knowledge of effective learning strategies, and ability to communicate complex concepts in a clear and accessible way
#### Constraints
- May require time and effort to understand the learner's specific needs, effectiveness depends on the learner's willingness to engage with the explanation, may not be sufficient on its own to overcome significant learning challenges
