# Implement Measures for Student Self-Knowledge

## Description
Implementing measures for student self-knowledge involves using various tools and strategies to help students understand their strengths, interests, and areas for growth. This includes learning interest inventories, exit tickets, progress monitoring, and peer/self-assessments. By understanding themselves as learners, students can better advocate for their needs and take ownership of their learning journey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to incorporate regular reflection activities, provide feedback on student performance, and create a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires time for implementation and may require additional resources for certain assessment tools.
