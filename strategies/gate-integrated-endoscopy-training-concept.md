# Combine background theory, video demonstrations, and simulator training in an integrated endoscopy curriculum (GATE model)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 3 against) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review describes a training concept called "GATE- gastroenterological education- training endoscopy", established in 2001, which "provides a combination of background theory, video demonstrations, and simulator training". The review reports that "The integrated GATE training improved both theoretical knowledge and manual skill of physicians". This strategy pairs didactic background knowledge with observational video learning and hands-on simulator practice rather than relying on any single method alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a virtual endoscopy simulator for hands-on practice
- Video demonstration materials and theoretical background content
#### Constraints
- The review cites this as a single reported training concept; further studies comparing teaching methods for virtual simulator training are called for

### Target Learners
- Physicians learning gastrointestinal endoscopy
- Novice endoscopists

### Target Learning Goals
- Theoretical endoscopy knowledge
- Manual endoscopic skills
