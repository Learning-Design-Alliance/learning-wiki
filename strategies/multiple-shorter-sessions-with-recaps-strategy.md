# Deliver online course content in multiple shorter sessions with end-of-session recaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes spaced learning as an online-deliverable method in which content is split across sessions, each ending with a recap that fortifies retention, and notes breaks between sessions reduce distraction. It reports that "Shorter sessions of 10 to 20 min appear to be more effective, aligning with students' preferences for shorter, frequent classes over lengthy sessions", and suggests further research on shorter class durations and different intervals between sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three or more sessions offering information over time at intervals; a recap of covered material at the end of each session
#### Constraints
- The 10-to-20-minute effectiveness statement is the authors' suggestion for further research, not a tested result of this study, which used one-hour sessions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- online learners in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension and retention of complex content
