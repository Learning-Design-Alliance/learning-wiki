# Combine CST with case study, deductive-inductive lecturing, and frame teaching to raise engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend applying reasoning and case study methods along with inquiry, heuristic, discussion and situational teaching synergistically with CST "to enhance students' interests and to encourage them to engage with learning materials". Concrete moves include discussing negative drug news (e.g., GSP failures in transportation and storage causing impurities), having teachers act as organizer, instructor, listener or student, and drawing frame diagrams of identification, impurity test and assay keyed to drug structures.
