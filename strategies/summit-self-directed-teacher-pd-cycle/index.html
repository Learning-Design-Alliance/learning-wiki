Self-directed professional learning cycle in recurring two-week PD chunks with public goals and study groups¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper profiles Summit Public Schools, where every teacher participates in at least 40 days of professional development per year mirroring the student self-directed learning model. Teachers use a five-phase cycle: "set a goal, make plans to work towards that goal, learn towards the goal, eventually show what you've learned, and finally reflect." During four two-week PD chunks each year, teachers set their own public goals, form study groups with colleagues sharing similar goals, and access in-house and external resources and experts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Protected two-week PD chunks at four times throughout the year
  • Public goals visible to faculty and structures for peer study groups
  • Access to in-house and out-of-house professional development and outside experts

Constraints¶

  • Most of the two-week chunks are self-directed rather than meeting-driven, per the school's own description

Target Learners¶

  • Practicing K-12 teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-directed continuous professional growth
  • Development of cognitive-skills rubrics and other model-specific teaching practices

Related Strategies¶

  • Collaborative Autoethnography for Teacher PD
  • Provide implementation professional development and support approximately every three weeks
  • Self-Organized Learning Groups
