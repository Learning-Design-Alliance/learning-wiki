# Combine timed reading with repeated oral reading using chunked text and prosody modeling, holding comprehension at a 70% criterion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The TROR treatment had students read each passage silently under time pressure, then reread it aloud twice: once chorally after the instructor modeled a passage marked with forward slashes separating 3–5 word phrases, and once to a partner mimicking the instructor's prosody, pace, and rhythm. Students aimed for at least six of eight comprehension questions correct and were told to read more carefully if below 70% or push faster if perfect. This combination produced significant rate and comprehension gains.
