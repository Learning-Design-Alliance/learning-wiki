Description¶

The literature suggests teachers adopt different teaching strategies: completing reflection sheets, writing a reflections diary, storytelling, or debating openly. The current study asks students to write reflection diaries on blogs, with the freedom to add representative images and pictures to supplement their reflections, as an additional assignment for lower achieving students during the design processes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must have weblogs available for recording personal reflections of learning

Constraints¶

  • Students in the experimental group wrote reflective diaries, and knew that the activity entailed no extra-credit points

Target Learners¶

  • lower achieving college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reflective thinking
  • software design

Claims¶

  • Lower achieving students who wrote weekly reflective diaries during software design achieved overall course scores statistically comparable to higher achieving students who did not. [+W]
