# When implementing experiential school nutrition programs, secure sufficient duration, curriculum integration, teacher involvement, and supporting resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review identifies contextual conditions under which experiential nutrition education succeeds: "Program outcomes appear to be influenced by several contextual factors, including intervention duration, curriculum integration, teacher involvement, and the availability of resources supporting implementation". It recommends that schools and designers attend to these implementation factors when adopting cooking, gardening, game-based, or digital formats, since quality of implementation and integration within the school curriculum appear to determine effectiveness. Implementation barriers such as limited classroom time and insufficient resources recur across educational contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient intervention duration and intensity
- Integration within the school curriculum
- Teacher involvement
- Availability of resources supporting implementation
#### Constraints
- Limited classroom time, insufficient educational resources, reduced intervention intensity, and disruptions to program fidelity are recurrent barriers the review identifies

### Target Learners
- children and adolescents in school settings

### Target Learning Goals
- nutrition knowledge, food literacy, and sustainable healthy eating behaviors
