# Instruct novices to avoid a forward-leaning posture and keep the head tilted backward during intubation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that novices' forward-leaning posture reduces gaze distance, the article states "for novices to perform the procedure like experts, it is important to avoid a forward-leaning posture and to keep the head tilted backward". Instructors can coach this head positioning directly during simulation training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires observation or measurement of trainee head posture during practice
#### Constraints
- Derived from mannequin-based motion-capture observations rather than tested as a training intervention

### Target Learners
- novice physicians and anesthesia trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining expert-like posture and visual perspective during laryngoscope manipulation
