# Turn-and-Talk

## Description
Turn-and-talk involves having learners engage in short, structured discussions with a partner to review and reinforce what they have learned. This strategy promotes active recall, peer interaction, and immediate feedback. Learners take turns sharing their understanding, comparing notes, and helping each other identify gaps in their knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires structured prompts and guidelines for discussion. Instructor must facilitate the activity and provide feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful management to ensure all learners participate actively. Effectiveness depends on learners' ability to articulate their understanding.
