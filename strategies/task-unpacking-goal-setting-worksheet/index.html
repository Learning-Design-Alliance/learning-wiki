Have students unpack goals into tasks and subtasks to counter the planning fallacy¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The intervention's Week 1 homework asks students to write three goals (school, extracurricular, friends-and-family) and decompose each into tasks, subtasks, and sub-subtasks, then schedule the first task. The article explains that "One way to counter the planning fallacy and get people to more accurately estimate task completion times is to have them unpack tasks into smaller units". It is an implementable classroom activity requiring only worksheets and counselor-led discussion.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students generate goals across school, extracurricular, and social domains before decomposing them
