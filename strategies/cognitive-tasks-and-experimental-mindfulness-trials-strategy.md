# Use cognitive-task indicators and experimental mindfulness-training trials instead of GPA-based performance measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend moving beyond GPA when studying mindfulness and academic performance, and testing mindfulness experimentally. They write: "Instead of GPA, we recommend utilizing other indicators such as cognitive tasks, which could provide a better view of how mindfulness may relate to academic performance." They further encourage local researchers to run experimental studies of mindfulness training to establish causality and assess its effectiveness on mental wellbeing, and to assess both positive and negative psychological outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers able to administer cognitive-task measures and run experimental designs with mindfulness training
#### Constraints
- The observational design of the present study could not establish cause and effect, motivating the experimental recommendation
