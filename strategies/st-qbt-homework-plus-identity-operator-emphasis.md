# Assign the online basis tutorial as homework together with instruction emphasizing the identity-operator derivation of the projection method

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the three studied courses, the authors identify "the winning combination for teaching change of basis" as assigning the ACE Physics QBT as homework "and also emphasizing the insertion of the identity operator as a tool for understanding the projection method for basis change". The Spring 2022 professor emphasized this derivation, and this form of the projection method appeared in over a quarter of that semester's exam responses versus twice in Fall 2021.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assign the ACE Physics QBT as a homework problem and pair it with instruction that emphasizes deriving the projection method via insertion of the identity operator.
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from three semesters at a single R1 university; the authors state it is impossible to fully characterize the effects of different instructors.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students in a spins-first quantum mechanics course

### Target Learning Goals
- use projection inner products to change basis (LG2)
