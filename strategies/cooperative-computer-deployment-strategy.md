# Deploy school computers for cooperative rather than one-per-student use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students working cooperatively on shared computers outperformed those working individually, the authors recommend encouraging social interaction in computer use and procuring fewer machines: "A class of 40 would not need more than eight computers systems for instructional needs." This makes CAI feasible for resource-constrained schools. They also recommend developing relevant CAI packages and equipping Nigerian public schools with ICT facilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CAI packages relevant to the local curriculum must be developed and computer literacy addressed in secondary schools
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in one quasi-experimental study in three private Nigerian schools; the authors caution findings may not generalise to public or other private institutions

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students in Nigeria

### Target Learning Goals
- Biology and other science subject achievement through CAI
