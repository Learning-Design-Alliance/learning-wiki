## Description
The article's discussion identifies a condition for building and intervening with U-learning environments in formative processes: "the incorporation of experienced teachers in the research groups with pedagogical, technological, and research skills". It also recommends developing technology so integrated and natural that students use it "without even thinking about it", and suggests intervention initiatives in different educational contexts to assess generalization.
