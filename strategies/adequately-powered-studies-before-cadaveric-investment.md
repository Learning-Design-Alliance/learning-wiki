# Require adequately powered studies of skill retention and transfer before recommending major investment in cadaveric simulation

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that the field should conduct adequately powered studies demonstrating whether cadaveric-trained skills are retained and transfer to the operating theatre before major investment is recommended. It notes cadaveric simulation "is expensive, and therefore needs to have demonstrable superiority over less expensive alternatives", and that cost and infrastructure demands make rigorous evaluation particularly challenging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequately powered studies measuring skill retention longitudinally and transfer into the live operating theatre
#### Constraints
- Cost and infrastructure demands of cadaveric simulation make such studies particularly challenging

### Target Learners
- postgraduate surgical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- retention and transfer of surgical skills learned in cadaveric simulation
