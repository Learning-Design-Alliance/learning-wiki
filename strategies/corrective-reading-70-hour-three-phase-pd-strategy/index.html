Provide 70 hours of professional development in three phases: intensive training, practice, and implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the qualifying study, "teachers in the study received 70 hours of professional development which included three phases: intensive training, practice, and implementation". This specific, phased PD recipe is presented as the implementation support used in the evaluation. Ongoing support options include onsite coaching, monthly webinars, and training institutes.

Context¶

Requirements¶
