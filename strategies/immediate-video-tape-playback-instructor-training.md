# Use immediate video tape playback to give new instructors insight into their teaching errors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
A proposed instructor-training approach in which novice teachers review recordings of their own instruction immediately after teaching. The article states that "the immediate playback feature of video tape provides an oppor- tunity for insight into errors and defi- ciencies by new instructors not possible with other existing methods." An evaluation study was planned at Fort Monmouth and Fort Gordon.

### Context

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- New instructors at Army Signal Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving instructor teaching performance by identifying errors and deficiencies
