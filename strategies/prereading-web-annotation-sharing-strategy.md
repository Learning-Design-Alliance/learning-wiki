# Combine pre-reading with a Web-based annotation sharing mechanism to stimulate pre-reading engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating a Web-based annotation tool with a sharing mechanism into pre-reading activities: students annotate before class, view classmates' annotations, and repair or update their own in a repeating cycle. The authors "strongly emphasize that an integrated technology (a Web-based annotation tool with a sharing mechanism) in pre-reading was an effective way to help students prepare to learn", and report it helped form a pre-reading community that overcame the demanding, solitary nature of pre-reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Web-based annotation system with sharing capability (VPEN in this study)
- Teacher-provided guiding questions and annotation labels such as "uncomprehending idea" and "main idea"
- Training activities before intervention covering tool use and sharing
#### Constraints
- How to conduct efficient pre-reading was not evaluated in this study
- Some students lacked home computer access or parental support for pre-reading

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning readiness before class
- learning achievement in social course content
