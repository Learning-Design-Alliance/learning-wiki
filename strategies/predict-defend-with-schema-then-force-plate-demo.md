# Prediction defended with a system schema followed by a force-plate demonstration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
A classroom activity sequence for teaching Newton's Third Law: student groups first predict and defend a force comparison using a system schema, then observe a force-plate measurement of both forces. "Shown the physical situation of Fig. 1a, students groups were asked to predict how the contact force by a 10kg (lead) brick on a 3kg book compared to the force by the book on the brick. They defended their answer with a system schema."
