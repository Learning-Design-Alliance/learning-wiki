# Lesson-sharing with structured reflection prompts as a professional development activity for refining conceptions of inquiry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
In Streamline to Mastery, teachers and researchers each chose a lesson they deemed effective and inquiry-oriented, taught it to the group, and debriefed together. After each of five lesson-sharing events, teachers completed an online reflection responding to two prompts: "In what ways was this an inquiry lesson?" and "How might you modify this lesson for your classroom?" Posts were visible to other participants on an online message board, supporting shared discourse about inquiry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Semi-weekly group meetings
- online message board for reflections
- willingness of participants to share and critique lessons publicly
#### Constraints
- The five lesson-sharing reflections occurred in the first five months of the study

### Target Learners
- practicing secondary science teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- articulating and refining conceptions of inquiry-oriented instruction
