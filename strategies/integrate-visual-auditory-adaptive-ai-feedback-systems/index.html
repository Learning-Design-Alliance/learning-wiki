Develop and adopt AI feedback systems that integrate visual and auditory feedback and automatically adapt students' practice plans¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on the findings, the article recommends that future systems go beyond text-based analysis: "Gelecek çalışmalarda, görsel ve işitsel geri bildirim unsurlarını entegre eden ve öğrencinin çalışma planını otomatik olarak uyarlayabilen sistemlerin geliştirilmesi önerilmektedir." It further recommends that students regularly use AI-supported applications offering intonation and posture analysis, do out-of-class self-evaluation via mobile and free platforms, and treat feedback as a planning and motivation tool as well as a technical-development aid.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Cooperation with software developers; ethical regulations; mobile-compatible systems combining intonation and posture analysis with visualization and sound features

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation arises from a four-week, single-institution study limited to entonation measurement with two specific AI models

Target Learners¶

  • violin and instrument students in higher education
  • music teachers integrating AI into lessons

Target Learning Goals¶

  • intonation and posture improvement
  • out-of-class self-assessment and practice planning

Related Strategies¶

  • Deliver AI-generated visual explanations adaptively based on learner engagement profiles rather than uniformly
  • Multisensory Phonics Instruction
  • Integrate AI-driven scaffolding with teacher-led offloading and refine feedback granularity to prevent over-scaffolding
