## Description
The chapter recommends that designers of the next generation of distance and online learning draw on "principles and applications stemming from the theories of self-regulation and multimedia learning, research-based motivational principles, and collaborative learning principles". These evidence-based approaches are offered as ways to foster instructional improvement grounded in the interaction findings.
