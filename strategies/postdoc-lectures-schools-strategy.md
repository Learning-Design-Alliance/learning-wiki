# Have postdoctoral fellows deliver lectures in elementary and secondary schools and universities to convey cutting-edge science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that postdoctoral fellows, of whom Japan has many in elementary-particle and nuclear theory, be given chances to deliver lectures in schools and universities to convey the enchantment of cutting-edge science, since many students lose interest in natural science in elementary school. They arranged a lecture by one author at the College of Education, Ibaraki University in June 2006 and propose a long-term part-time lecturer system, with teaching-skill workshops for postdocs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system for short-term and long-term lectures by part-time lecturers, and workshops of teaching skills for postdoctoral fellows, most of whom lack a teaching certificate
#### Constraints
- Postdoctoral fellows in Japan mostly do not hold teaching certificates

### Target Learners
- elementary school students
- junior-high and high-school students
- undergraduate teacher-education students

### Target Learning Goals
- interest in natural science and physics
