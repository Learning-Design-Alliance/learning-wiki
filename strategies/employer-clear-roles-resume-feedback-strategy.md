# Employers should clearly communicate job roles and hiring expectations and offer on-site resume feedback at career fairs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
For employers, the article recommends maximizing career fair impact by clearly communicating job roles and expectations for the hiring process. As printed, "Offering on-site resume feedback and brief skill demonstrations can also be beneficial," and employers could collaborate with the university to align openings with student interests. This responds to participants' requests for resume feedback and clearer employer expectations.
