# Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS)

## Description
PBIS is a proactive approach schools use to improve school safety and promote positive behavior. It focuses on prevention, not punishment. With PBIS, schools teach students positive behavior strategies, just as they would teach about any other subject. All students learn about positive behavior, including kids with IEPs and 504 plans. PBIS recognizes that students can only meet behavior expectations if they know what the expectations are. Everyone learns what’s considered appropriate behavior, using a common language to talk about it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear behavior expectations, consistent implementation, and ongoing evaluation.
#### Constraints
- Requires school-wide commitment and may not be effective if not implemented consistently.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track student behavior, detentions, suspensions, grades, and bullying incidents.

## Impact
- Research shows that PBIS leads to better student behavior. In many schools that use PBIS, students get fewer detentions and suspensions. They also earn better grades. And there’s some evidence that PBIS may lead to less bullying.
