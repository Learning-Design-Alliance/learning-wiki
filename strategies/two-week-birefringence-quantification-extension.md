# Extend the lab to two weeks so students connect intensity measurements to quantitative birefringence via the phase-shift relation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends extending the lab to two weeks so students connect intensity measurements at several angles with the phase-shift of light through the sample to draw quantitative conclusions about birefringence. The article states: "If the lab is extended to two weeks, the students can further their understanding of anisotropic optically active materials by connecting the measurements of the intensities at several different angles with the phase-shift." Students measure sample thickness with a micrometer and use R=2d(Δn)/λ to estimate birefringence, linking it to internal order parameter and molecular polarizability.
