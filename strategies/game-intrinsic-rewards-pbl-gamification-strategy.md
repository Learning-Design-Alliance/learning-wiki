# Configure gamification rewards and punishments as intrinsic to the game and combine cooperation with competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' practical implications: gamified implementations from an MDA (Mechanic–Dynamic–Aesthetic) perspective should make "the configuration of rewards and punishments... intrinsic and connected to the game itself", avoiding approaches that encourage less autonomous motivation. The resource should be in a mature phase with a familiar interface, and, citing meta-analytic evidence, combinations of cooperation and competition with points, badges and leaderboards (PBL) are likely effective.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A technological resource in a mature phase whose interface is familiar to students
#### Constraints
- Public leaderboards for XP, HP and AP could negatively affect social comparisons; rewards must not be extrapolatable to tangible interests

### Target Learners
- university students
