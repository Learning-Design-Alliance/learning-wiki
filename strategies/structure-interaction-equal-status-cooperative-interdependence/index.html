Structure disability awareness programs to include extensive personal contact, joint participation, equal status, and cooperative interdependence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that programs like the DCP should structure interactions, encourage extensive personal contact, promote joint participation, facilitate equal status, foster cooperative interdependence, focus on similarities, and develop effective communication. These contact-based design features are presented as mechanisms for moving attitudes from negative to accepting.

Claims¶

  • A three-week Disability Camp Program significantly improved general attitudes of campers without disabilities toward including a peer with a physical disability in a summer sport and leisure activity camp [+W]
  • The DCP significantly improved sport-specific attitudes toward rule and game modifications for basketball, contrasting with prior studies showing decreased sport-specific attitudes [+W]
