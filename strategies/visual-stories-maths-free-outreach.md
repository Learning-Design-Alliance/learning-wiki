# Use 'maths-free' interactive Visual Stories with guiding narratives to engage general public audiences with complex mathematical models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Visual Stories are a collection on the VisualPDE site that explore phenomena "purely through interactive simulation with VisualPDE and a guiding narrative aimed at the general public", with no mathematical formalism. Interactive elements are embedded directly into narrative articles. The flagship example adapts Lau et al.'s airborne virus transmission model to show spatial infection probabilities from a rotating source, with and without advection from an air conditioner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preset simulations with parameters constrained by sliders and uneditable equations, which the authors suggest are more beneficial to novice users
#### Constraints
- The authors note important challenges regarding accessibility of such visual tools themselves remain an ongoing area of work

### Target Learners
- general public
- novice users unfamiliar with numerical methods

### Target Learning Goals
- developing intuition for complex models such as airborne virus transmission
- public engagement with contemporary research mathematics
