# Develop policies and pedagogical practices that create supportive, accessible, and welcoming digital spaces for adolescents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that policy and pedagogy should harness digital technologies for positive development rather than focusing solely on risk reduction. "The findings highlight the diverse opportunities digital technologies afford for adolescent flourishing and underscore the need for policies and pedagogical practices that create supportive, accessible, and welcoming digital spaces." This responds to interventionist policies emphasising harm prevention and offers educators guidance for fostering positive digital learning environments grounded in adolescents' own accounts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recognition of the potential benefits as well as harms of digital technologies in policy, research and practice
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on qualitative synthesis of adolescents' positive experiences, not on intervention outcome data

### Target Learners
- secondary-school adolescents aged 12–19, including underrepresented groups

### Target Learning Goals
- digital wellbeing, identity expression, supportive peer connection, and positive engagement with online learning
