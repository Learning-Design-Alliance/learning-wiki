# Raise awareness of smoking's adverse educational outcomes through public service announcements, curriculum-based education, and campus smoking restrictions

## Description
The article's conclusion recommends institutional actions targeting student smoking given its observed association with poor academic performance. It suggests that "higher university officials need to raise awareness regarding the adverse educational outcomes of smoking through public service announcements and curriculum-based education" and that policies restricting smoking in community spaces and university facilities may reduce smoking onset.
