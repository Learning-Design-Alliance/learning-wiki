# Integrate SWAL training into L2 writing instruction to foster motivation, confidence, and lower anxiety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that L2 writing instructors build students' writing assessment literacy into regular instruction. The conclusion states that "ByintegratingSWALtrainingintothecurriculum,L2teacherscanhelpstudentsdevelopabetter understandingofwritingassessments," which the authors say fosters motivation, confidence and less anxiety. Recommended classroom moves include explaining assessment criteria clearly, fostering positive beliefs about assessment as a growth tool, and encouraging constructive reflection on and use of feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers explain assessment criteria and the purpose of writing evaluations, engage students in discussion of assessments, and encourage constructive use of peer and teacher feedback
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of correlational questionnaire findings from Chinese undergraduate EFL freshmen; the study did not test an SWAL-training intervention

### Target Learners
- Chinese undergraduate EFL freshmen
- L2 writing learners in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Confidence and motivation in L2 writing
- Reduced writing anxiety
- Understanding and constructive use of writing assessment feedback
