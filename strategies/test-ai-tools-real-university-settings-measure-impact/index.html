Prioritize real-world testing of AI tools in university settings, environmental impact measurement, and integrated ethical-sustainability policies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's forward-looking recommendation is that future research move beyond conceptual and pilot work by testing AI tools in real university settings, measuring their environmental impact, and developing policies that combine ethical and sustainability goals. It argues greater involvement from students, staff, and faculty is essential to ensure AI supports a fair and sustainable future for higher education.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sustained investment, policy alignment, and interdisciplinary collaboration to achieve AI's transformative potential in higher education.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes governance frameworks in higher education often overlook environmental aspects, raising the risk of unintended ecological effects.

Target Learners¶

  • higher education institutions
  • students, staff, and faculty
  • policymakers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • responsible and sustainable AI adoption
  • institutional sustainability strategy

Related Strategies¶

  • Pursue sustainable AI integration in PBL through attention to design, ethics, and policy
  • Higher education leaders should develop more inclusive and future-oriented GenAI policies integrating social equity, interdisciplinary experimentation, and sustainability considerations
  • Future improvements: real-world user studies, advanced student modeling, and spaced repetition
