# Require and evaluate DA tasks throughout the course to reset epistemological expectations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (3 qualitative, 1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students see DA as unhelpful for getting numerical answers and resist learning it, the article recommends making DA a required, evaluated part of the course. The author presents DA in the first few classes, includes a DA problem in most weekly quizzes, and assigns DA problems whenever a concept with a new dimensionality is introduced, plus homework and exam problems in supplementary materials.
