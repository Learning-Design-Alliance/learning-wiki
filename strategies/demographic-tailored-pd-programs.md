# Tailor professional development to demographic subgroups of teachers (gender, education level, school level)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes designing teacher professional development that accounts for demographic differences rather than treating teachers as a uniform group. It states that "gender-specific professional development programs could be developed to address the unique needs of male and female teachers" and that "strategies tailored to different educational levels and school levels can improve the effectiveness of sustainability education in different contexts." The strategy follows from the study's focus on demographic moderators of the mindfulness–sustainability relationship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Information on participants' gender, education level and school level when designing programs
#### Constraints
- The article notes demographic differences may be influenced by cultural and social contexts and that further research is needed across settings

### Target Learners
- Physical education teachers of both genders, at middle and high school levels, with undergraduate or graduate education

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve effectiveness of sustainability education and mindfulness-based approaches across teacher subgroups
