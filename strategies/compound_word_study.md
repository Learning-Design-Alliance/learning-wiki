# Compound Word Study

## Description
Begin teaching how to spell multisyllable words by beginning with compounds. These offer two big advantages: Children more easily detect their syllables, and the spelling of each base word stays the same.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word lists, direct instruction

### Affordances
- Provides a concrete way to understand multisyllabic words, reinforces recognition of base words, builds vocabulary

### Personalization
- Word lists can be adapted to target specific compounds that the learner is familiar with. Instruction can be adapted for one-on-one or group instruction.
