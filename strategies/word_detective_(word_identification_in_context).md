# Word Detective (Word Identification in Context)

## Description
Learners identify target vocabulary within varied contexts. Learners search for and highlight specific words in diverse reading materials like song lyrics or sentences from a familiar text. This method reinforces word recognition and contextual understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to various reading materials (e.g., song lyrics, sentences from texts), highlighters or markers, and target word lists. Preparation involves highlighting words in the song lyrics for educators, and distributing song lyrics without the highlighting to the learners.
#### Constraints
- Material must be appropriate for learners. Implementation requires sufficient reading resources and targeted words. Success relies on consistent reinforcement and practice.

### Target Learners

- Early elementary grades (1-3), learners who need vocabulary reinforcement, and English language learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance vocabulary recognition, improve contextual understanding, reinforce reading skills, develop pattern recognition

### Affordances
- Actively engages learners in identifying vocabulary. Reinforces reading skills through contextual application. Provides repetition and pattern recognition for improved memory. Adaptable to diverse materials and skill levels.

### Personalization
- Scaffolded support is achieved by assigning a manageable number of words. Can modify word complexity based on proficiency. Adaptable to fit various reading materials and topics.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track the words identified within the context. Observe the ability to locate, highlight, and verbally share the vocabulary.

## Impact
- Direct instruction in vocabulary and context improves overall reading comprehension. Repetition supports memory and retention. Active participation increases engagement and learning outcomes.
