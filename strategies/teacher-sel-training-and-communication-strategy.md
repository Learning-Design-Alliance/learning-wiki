# Strengthen teachers' capacity to evaluate and foster SEL and increase communication among students, parents and teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given teachers' unique predictive power, the article recommends "more comprehensive professional training programmes that enhance teachers' capability to observe and evaluate, as well as foster SEL skills in classrooms alongside academic performance". It further recommends "increased communication among students, parents, teachers regarding SEL development, importance, expectations" to align perceptions across contexts, particularly at advanced grade levels where student and parent correlations with achievement diminish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional training programmes for teachers; communication structures linking students, parents and teachers about SEL expectations
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on this study's correlational and SEM findings in one population; the article does not report testing the training itself

### Target Learners
- Teachers of Arabic-speaking students in grades 3–9, and the students and parents in those classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate classroom evaluation of SEL skills and alignment of SEL perceptions across home and school

## Claims

- [In SEM, only teachers' SEL reports significantly predicted students' academic scores, with students' and parents' contributions non-significant](../claims/sem-only-teachers-sel-predicts-academic-scores.md) [+W]
