# Direct future attribution research at which causal elements apply most often to which sport situations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
In its conclusions, the review recommends that although attribution research has become more complex since Heider and Weiner, their original hypotheses should not be ignored. Instead, "research should look at their elements more closely to discover which causal elements apply most often to what sport situations." The review argues athletes will increasingly accept psychological help, sustaining demand for new findings in sport.
