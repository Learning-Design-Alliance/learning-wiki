Research and development should address the knowledge gap on how nurse managers perceive and influence palliative care practice¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's key recommendation is that future work target the identified knowledge gap. It states: "Future research is needed to explore nurse managers' perspectives on how they understand and perform their leadership role in relation to palliative care." The review also reports "a knowledge gap regarding how nurse managers perceive and influence palliative care practices in municipal care settings", so studies and development initiatives should foreground nurse managers' own perspectives on leadership in palliative care.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • empirical studies targeting nurse managers themselves in municipal care settings, since prior included studies often sampled staff broadly or did not specify manager numbers

Constraints¶

  • the review excluded intervention studies and non-empirical work, so no intervention evidence on building manager competence was synthesized

Target Learners¶

  • nurse managers in municipal care settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • palliative care leadership
  • awareness and competence in palliative care

Claims¶

  • Municipal care managers commonly lack palliative care education and report inadequate education resources for staff [+W]
  • Nursing home managers report significantly higher palliative care self-efficacy than residential care home managers [+W]
  • Care home managers show moderate palliative care knowledge, strong on psychosocial aspects but weak on biological and physical components [+W]
  • A narrative synthesis of eight studies identifies three themes describing nurse managers' palliative care awareness and competence in municipal care [+W]
