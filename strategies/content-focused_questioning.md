# Content-Focused Questioning

## Description
Asking students questions that focus on the main points of the text and how the author supports those points with evidence and examples. Ensure understanding before discussing techniques. Can start with what they know.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material. Preparation of thoughtful and engaging questions. Time for discussion in class.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming. Students may struggle to answer questions if they lack background knowledge or reading skills. Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure meaningful discussion.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for struggling readers and those with limited background knowledge

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Improve reading comprehension, ensure understanding of main ideas, develop critical thinking skills, enhance ability to identify evidence and examples in text

### Affordances
- Provides a structured approach to reading comprehension. Supports critical thinking by requiring students to analyze and evaluate evidence. Increases engagement by making reading an active and interactive process.

### Personalization
- Questions can be tailored to specific texts and student needs. Students can be given opportunities to generate their own questions. Scaffolding can be provided for struggling learners.
