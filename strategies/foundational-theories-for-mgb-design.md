# Provide foundational instructional theories, models, and methods as a design foundation for mobile game-based learning solutions

## Description
Drawing on Honebein's theory-based critique in the special issue, the editorial identifies a limitation of current mobile game-based learning research: the field lacks foundational instructional theories, models, and methods as a design foundation for mobile game-based learning solutions. The editorial frames this as a constraint that future research should aim to address, positioning theory provision as a concrete direction for research on mobile game-based learning design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future research should supply foundational instructional theories, models, and methods on which mobile game-based learning solutions can be designed
#### Constraints
- Current mobile game-based learning research is critiqued as lacking such a design foundation

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
