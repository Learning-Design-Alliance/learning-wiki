Explicit reading strategy instruction following the WWW&H rule: explain, model, and support strategy implementation with guided practice¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article describes explicit instruction as teaching where "the teacher clearly teaches students about a strategy by explaining and demonstrating how to implement a particular strategy, explaining the benefits of strategy use, and supporting students in strategy implementation". It follows the WWW&H rule (What to Do, When, Why, and How) and rests on explicit explanations, modeling, and guided practice. In this study the steps were: explaining and exemplifying strategy definitions, researcher modeling via read-aloud and think-aloud on force-motion and electricity texts, reading a photosynthesis-respiration text with a strategy checklist, and reading a phases-of-the-Moon text with an open-ended evaluation form.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher explains strategy definitions and benefits, models strategy use, and supports students during implementation

Constraints¶

  • The instruction process lasted 5 weeks in this study; effectiveness evidence here is limited to preservice science teachers reading science texts

Target Learners¶

  • preservice science teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • use of cognitive and metacognitive reading strategies
  • improving conceptual understanding of science texts
