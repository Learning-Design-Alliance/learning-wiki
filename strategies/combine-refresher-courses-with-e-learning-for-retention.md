# Combine refresher courses and/or tests with e-learning to prolong retention of hands-on skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A forward-looking recommendation from the Discussion: future studies should pair hands-on training with e-learning-based refreshers to maintain skills. The authors note that e-learning alone as a booster after an ALS course found no significant effect, with lack of social interaction identified as a major cause, and suggest "Future studies that combine refresher courses and/or tests with e-learning may demonstrate prolonged retention of skills obtained during hands-on training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning designed to complement, not replace, hands-on social interaction with instructors
#### Constraints
- E-learning alone as a maintenance booster after an ALS course showed no significant effect in the cited study by Lind et al.

### Target Learners
- Prehospital physicians and other clinicians trained in point-of-care procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term retention of psychomotor clinical skills such as ultrasound examination
