# Use STEEM as a quality-assessment and monitoring tool for theatre-based training environments, with possible adaptation for podiatric surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the STEEM questionnaire, administered online, to identify "discrete areas to be targeted for improvement as well as being used for monitoring of the environment". It proposes results could demonstrate part of the HCPC standards for podiatric surgery training, and suggests removing emergency-procedure items for future podiatric studies, which would require re-validation. Eraut's recommendation that each organisation self-evaluate its environment motivates this use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered to trainees and registrars working in or training within a podiatric surgery unit; respondents must not already hold the Certificate of Completion of Podiatric Surgery Training
#### Constraints
- The tool would require re-validation if emergency-procedure questions are removed or adapted for podiatric surgery
- Potentially limited by small sample size; podiatric surgery units may be acute, community or mixed settings, unlike the medical specialities in prior studies

### Target Learners
- Podiatric surgical trainees and registrars working towards the Certificate of Completion of Podiatric Surgery Training

### Target Learning Goals
- Quality of the workplace (theatre) learning environment for surgical training
