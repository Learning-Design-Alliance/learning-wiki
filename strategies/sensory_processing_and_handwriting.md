# Sensory Processing and Handwriting

## Description
Activities that incorporate sensory experiences to enhance handwriting skills. These activities engage different senses (e.g., tactile, proprioceptive, visual) to improve letter formation, pencil pressure, and motor planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multisensory materials (e.g., playdough, shaving cream, sandpaper), writing tools, and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for students with significant sensory aversions. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Personalization
- Activities can be tailored to individual sensory preferences and needs. Varied materials and techniques engage different sensory modalities.
