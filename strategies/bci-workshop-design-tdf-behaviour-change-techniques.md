# Behaviour change workshops for clinicians built on an implementation of change model, the Theoretical Domains Framework, and selected behaviour change techniques

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The workshops implemented GP delivery of an enhanced OA consultation through a theory-driven design: they "were developed using an implementation of change model", presented a "concrete proposal for change", addressed determinants of change identified with the Theoretical Domains Framework, and incorporated systematically selected behaviour change techniques. They consisted of four workshops mixing didactic and interactive sessions, were learner-centred, facilitated by local opinion leaders, mainly delivered at the GP practices, and reported against the GREET checklist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by facilitators identified as local opinion leaders; content targeting identified knowledge and skills gaps relevant to the target consultation behaviours
#### Constraints
- The article notes the workshops were labour-intensive to deliver and required GPs to commit considerable attendance time, which may limit general use across the health service

### Target Learners
- General practitioners in UK primary care

### Target Learning Goals
- Conducting enhanced initial osteoarthritis consultations, including explaining the diagnosis, addressing patient expectations, and promoting self-management
