Build coherent programmatic research efforts through collaborative theme identification¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that family medicine education researchers work collaboratively to identify current and relevant research themes and integrate work into coherent programmatic efforts rather than conducting single studies. The authors state: "a community of researchers should also aim for an integration of work into coherent programmatic efforts that can advance the field." Collaboration with researchers in other fields such as continuing education, interprofessional education and patient safety is proposed to support intersecting interests.
