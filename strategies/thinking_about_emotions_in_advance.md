# Thinking About Emotions in Advance

## Description
Begin the school day by asking your students a question to find out how they are feeling or what might be bothering them. Writing the question on the whiteboard and having students write their answers on sticky notes is an easy way to implement it into your routine. Discuss any event that likely elicited a feeling and have everyone share. This sets the tone for the day, giving you the heads up on who might have a tough day and why.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Whiteboard, sticky notes, and time for discussion
#### Constraints
- Some students may be hesitant to share. Requires sensitive and supportive facilitation by the teacher.
