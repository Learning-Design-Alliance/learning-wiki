# Two-phase train-the-trainer model building local OBLS instructor capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol uses a train-the-trainer model in which master trainers prepare locally embedded staff who then lead subsequent courses, expanding training capacity and reach. "In the first phase, 2–3 HCWs from each selected implementation site will be invited to participate in the OBLS course at one of four regional hubs"; trainees who pass and are recommended proceed to "a comprehensive 4-hour virtual program" preparing them to deliver OBLS. Each site designates a Course Director and receives an experienced sponsor with check-ins and in-person oversight during initial courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course completion plus a recommendation from an experienced OBLS instructor to enter the train-the-trainer course
- a designated Course Director per site and an assigned experienced sponsor
- minimum of 3 but no more than 6 mentored courses over 2–3 days with instructor evaluation
#### Constraints
- Instructors rated "needs improvement" or "unsatisfactory" must repeat a mentored course until receiving a "satisfactory" evaluation

### Target Learners
- local healthcare workers becoming instructors at hospitals, birthing centers, and prehospital agencies

### Target Learning Goals
- delivering the OBLS curriculum, facilitating rapid cycle deliberate practice, reflective checklist debriefs, and difficult-news role play
