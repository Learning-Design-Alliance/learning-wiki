# Address Stereotypical Beliefs About Parents and Poverty

## Description
Teachers and educators should challenge and avoid stereotypical beliefs about parents and families living in poverty. Recognizing that all parents want to be involved in their children's education, regardless of socioeconomic status, is critical. Empathy and kindness should be at the forefront of communication to connect with parents at their level and engage them in supporting their children’s education.
