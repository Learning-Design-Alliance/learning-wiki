# Partner with immigrant-serving community-based organizations to engage linguistically diverse families and sustain feedback loops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends that district leaders partnering on ARP-funded plans work with immigrant-serving community-based organizations to "draw on their expertise, share resources, and inform families, communities, and advocates about new resources available." Because these organizations already hold trusted relationships with communities, they can run focus groups, forums, surveys, and working groups that feed district policy, provided they are compensated for this work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations must be appropriately compensated for gathering input, ensuring access, and distributing resources
#### Constraints
- Especially important for districts that have struggled to engage immigrant students and families

### Target Learners
- English learners and their linguistically diverse families

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable representation of English learner needs in district spending plans
