# Integrate validated virtual simulation scenarios into nursing curricula and continuing education for Standard Precautions training

## Description
The article recommends incorporating validated virtual simulation scenarios into nursing education and health professional continuing education to develop knowledge, clinical skills, and self-confidence for settings with high exposure risk to infectious agents. The authors state that "validated scenarios such as this should be integrated into nursing curricula and health professional continuing education programs." The scenario is described as flexible for undergraduate education, periodic refresher training, and graduate-level courses in areas such as Infection Control and Intensive Care.
