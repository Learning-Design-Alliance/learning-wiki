# Remote Support with Augmented Reality (AR)

## Description
AR apps allow technicians and users to communicate with experts in real time. The tech support highlights objects in the user's field of view so they can assist in repair without ever having to leave their seats. AR headsets connect to Internet of Things (IoT) sensors and other diagnostic information to help technicians assess repair needs more quickly and reduce downtime.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AR hardware (smartphones or tablets), high-speed and reliable internet connection.
#### Constraints
- Requires expertise to operate properly and the risk of poor signal hampering communication.
