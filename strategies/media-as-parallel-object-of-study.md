# Use media themselves as parallel objects of study in the classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the conclusions the article recommends combining the presence of media with didactic resources "as an object of study", using technologies as didactic resources while also turning them "into parallel objects of study, of analysis of their characteristics". It gives the example of accessing permanently updated Internet editions of four or five newspapers to "do a critical analysis of the news", comparing coverage, sources and contrast across papers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technologies must first be adequately "designed based on pedagogy" for results to be positive.
#### Constraints
- The article warns of the influence of media on childhood and youth, including "possible dangers that they contain".

### Target Learners
- all citizens regardless of age, as users and consumers of media
- school students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical analysis of news and media
- critical reflection on technology use

### Affordances
- Techno Media Convergence
