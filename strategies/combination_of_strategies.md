# Combination of Strategies

## Description
Combining two or more calming sensory strategies to create a more comprehensive and effective approach to self-regulation. Examples include digging in a tactile bin while listening to white noise, sitting with a weighted blanket while looking at sensory bottles, or chewing gum while working on a fine motor task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A collection of sensory tools and activities. A designated space for implementing these strategies. Clear guidelines for participation.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and coordination to implement effectively. May require additional resources and support. Some learners may require individualized guidance to find the right combination of strategies.
