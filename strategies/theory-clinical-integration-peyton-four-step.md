# Integrate theory with clinical practice through coordinated planning and structured procedural teaching methods such as Peyton's four-step approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Educators emphasized linking theoretical instruction with clinical experiences and coordinating between theoretical instructors and clinical trainers. Peyton's four-step approach was used to support progressive skill acquisition, allowing students to "observe procedures, guide the instructor verbally, and eventually perform the skills independently." Regular preparatory meetings reviewed course learning outcomes before clinical training periods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination meetings between theoretical instructors and clinical trainers before training periods
- Structured progression from observation to independent performance
