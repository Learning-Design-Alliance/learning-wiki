# Direct future AR-in-education research toward social sciences and special education, and classify studies by outcome type

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that future studies be classified according to the outcomes of AR applications in different classes and that results be compared, and they identify under-researched fields. The article states that "the AR applications in social sciences classes were limited" and that "there are only a few studies on the use of AR applications in the education of the handicapped". It recommends the fields of Social Sciences and the Education of the Handicapped for future authors examining AR applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should report the statistical data (n, x, t, ss etc.) needed for effect size calculation, which was missing in most of the 171 scanned studies.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is based on the coverage of the 171 studies scanned in SCI and SSCI indices between 2005 and 2015.

### Target Learners
- primary, secondary, high school and university students
- learners with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- academic success
- attitude
- problem solving skills
- motivation
