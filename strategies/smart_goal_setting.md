# SMART Goal Setting

## Description
SMART goal setting ensures goals are Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, and Time-Specific. This involves setting clear, well-defined goals with measurable elements like dates and amounts, ensuring they align with resources and skills, and setting realistic deadlines to create urgency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires understanding of the SMART criteria and the ability to critically evaluate goals against these criteria.
#### Constraints
- Overemphasis on structure can sometimes stifle creativity. Ensuring goals are truly relevant and aligned with broader objectives requires careful consideration.
