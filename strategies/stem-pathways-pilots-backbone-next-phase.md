# Next-phase agenda: pilot and refine STEM pathways, align STEM education with local economic demands, and plan a sustaining backbone organization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the next phase, the report states the Community Advisors will "Develop and implement pilot programs to test and refine STEM pathways", begin strategic planning that aligns STEM education initiatives with local economic demands to prepare learners for high-impact careers, and design a long-term plan for a local backbone organization to sustain the regional coalition. Members expressed readiness, commitment to sustained engagement, and understanding that development will be iterative.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A local backbone organization to ensure the sustainability of the regional coalition
- Sustained member engagement and iterative development across phases
#### Constraints
- Members acknowledged potential barriers including access to inter-institutional data, an evolving political landscape, and a lack of financial and logistical resources

### Target Learners
- Learners in San Diego regional STEM pathways, Pre-K through postsecondary

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for high-impact STEM careers aligned with local economic demands
