# Run conferences, scholarships and returner programs to support women and non-binary physicists at key transition points

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review catalogues implementable support activities: the annual Conference for Undergraduate Women and Non-binary Physicists brings attendees together "to encourage and support them with their personal and professional development"; the Bell Burnell fund aids doctoral students from under-represented groups; the Daphne Jackson Trust runs returner programs after career breaks; and workplace programs such as WISE Ten Steps support cultural change.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding sources such as the gift that established the Bell Burnell Graduate Scholarship Fund in 2018
#### Constraints
- The review notes women take on more unpaid work and on average do around 60% more cooking, cleaning and childcare than men, challenging participation and career pathways

### Target Learners
- women and non-binary undergraduate physics students
- students from under-represented groups in physics
- physicists returning after a career break

### Target Learning Goals
- personal and professional development
- progression to postgraduate study
- career retention in physics
