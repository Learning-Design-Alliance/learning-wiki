# Disseminate PICC maintenance knowledge through multiple channels and target education by dimension-specific performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend improving nurses' PICC maintenance knowledge by disseminating content through varied channels and by directing education to the populations and knowledge dimensions that performed poorly. They write that "targeted education for nurses should be distributed based on their performance on specific dimensions, such as the replacement of dressing and needle free connectors", and that knowledge should be spread via "courses, lectures, seminars and new media". Particular attention should go to county-hospital nurses and other groups who responded poorly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment data identifying which dimensions and populations perform poorly, such as the questionnaire used in this survey
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on cross-sectional, self-reported data from hospitals that had already introduced PICC maintenance technology, so may not generalise to all nurses

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses, especially those in county hospitals, those with junior titles, those under 25, and those without prior PICC maintenance training

### Target Learning Goals
- PICC maintenance knowledge, particularly replacement of dressings and needle-free connectors
