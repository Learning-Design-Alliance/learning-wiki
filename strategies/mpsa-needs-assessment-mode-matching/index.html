Use MPSA mode analysis in needs assessment to match teaching methods with students' problem-solving modes¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In education, the article proposes using the MPSA Cone as an alternative method for needs assessment: studying "the common and different needs of different modes of problem solvers in the classroom" so educators can prepare appropriate teaching methods and materials. The strategy rests on diagnosing whether students and teachers lean technical, interpretive, or emancipating, and anticipating mismatches between them.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators need to assess the problem-solving modes of both students and teachers before selecting teaching methods and materials

Constraints¶

  • The article reports no empirical evaluation of mode-matched teaching; the classroom mismatch examples are illustrative assertions citing the author's prior work
