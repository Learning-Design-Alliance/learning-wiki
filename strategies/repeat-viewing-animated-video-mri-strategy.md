# Offer preimaging educational animated videos as a cost-effective, view-anywhere preparation that children can watch repeatedly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using preimaging interactive educational animated videos because they are "a cost-effective, non-pharmacological intervention as the video can be viewed repeatedly at anytime and anywhere", in contrast to a life-sized MRI simulator which requires space, manpower and expense. The authors further propose that future studies evaluate viewing the video several times before the scan, since information reinforcement could produce a larger effect than the one-time viewing used in the trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The video must be easily accessible and distributable online so patients can view it anywhere and reinforce knowledge by watching it multiple times.
#### Constraints
- The trial itself used one-time viewing before the scan; the effect of repeated viewing was not evaluated in this study.

### Target Learners
- Children aged 3–20 years scheduled for elective MRI

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for the MRI procedure without sedation or general anaesthesia
- Reduced anxiety and improved cooperation during scanning
