# Question-based eLearning Design

## Description
Question-based eLearning design is an instructional approach that replaces the traditional expository method with a more inquiry-based one. Instead of presenting information as statements, learners are posed with questions and invited to gather information to answer them, promoting active exploration and critical thinking. This can be achieved by presenting realistic scenarios, examples for concept identification, or complex problems that guide learners through a solution using questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-designed questions, relevant content, and guidance to support learners in finding answers. Requires careful consideration of the audience's abilities and knowledge.
#### Constraints
- May require more development time and resources. Needs careful design to ensure questions are relevant and engaging. May not be suitable for all learners or topics.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Arouses curiosity through relevant questions, makes learning active and engaging, aligns with natural learning processes, promotes independent thinking and knowledge construction, works in self-paced and collaborative settings, motivates adults through varied approaches, encourages creative learning design.

### Personalization
- Adjust the level of assistance and structure based on the audience's abilities and knowledge. Provide more guidance for novices and decrease it for experts to avoid the expertise reversal effect.
