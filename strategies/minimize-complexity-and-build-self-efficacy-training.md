# Minimize platform complexity and build instructor self-efficacy through training to support e-learning adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that e-learning designers and providers reduce the effort instructors expend on platform operations, and that institutions raise instructors' competence and confidence. It states "Nigerian instructors will benefit if designers and providers consider ways to minimize the complexity of content, collaboration, and communication processes within platforms", and that institutions "should design various ways of improving instructor self-efficacy through seminars, training, and other ways of improving their competence levels".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to seminars and training for instructors
- Platform design that simplifies content, collaboration, and communication processes
#### Constraints
- The study cannot accurately state if e-learning platforms are fully utilized or underutilized in developing countries

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
