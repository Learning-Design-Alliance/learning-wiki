## Description
The article describes five support streams the Center for Learner Pathway Innovations uses to embed digital fluency in career pathways: building local, regional, and state education ecosystems around credentialed pathways; training and professional development for teachers and instructors; innovative internship, mentorship, and work-based learning models with industry; in-person convenings, Action Summits, and design studios; and showcases of integrating districts and communities.
