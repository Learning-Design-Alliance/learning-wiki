# Extend the test by adding further electrics items and items of very high and/or very low difficulty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend two concrete extensions of their developed test: adding more electrics items, since electrics is a key topic in the German middle-school physics curriculum but only 2 of 20 items cover it, and adding items of very high and/or very low difficulty to close the Wright Map gaps at the proficiency extremes. They state it "might be reasonable to extend our developed test by adding further items on electrics" and that extreme-difficulty items "could further improve the psychometric quality of our developed tests".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the released TIMSS item pools and the adaptation procedure used in the study
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is specific to this test and its identified gaps; the authors note it is presumably, not demonstrated, an improvement

### Target Learners
- middle school students, including those with very high and/or low physics proficiency

### Target Learning Goals
- physics proficiency assessment across content areas including electrics
