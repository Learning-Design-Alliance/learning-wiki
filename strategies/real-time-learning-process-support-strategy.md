## Description
The strategy is to give distant learners real-time support on the learning process itself, not only communication tools, while they develop a case study solution. The article reports that LeCS "accomplishes functions regarding the learning process that together give real-time support to the distant learners during the development of the case study solution." These functions complement the set of collaboration tools provided to dispersed students.
