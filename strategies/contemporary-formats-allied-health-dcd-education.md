# Provide allied health professional education on DCD through contemporary, practice-oriented formats such as clinical guidelines, factsheets and online presentations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that continuing education for podiatrists about DCD be delivered using contemporary education practices in the formats practitioners themselves prefer. It reports that "A strong majority of participants (n = 304 (83%)) indicated willingness to receive further education on DCD", with clinical guidelines, factsheets and online presentations the most popular responses, and that podiatrists "appear to preference education strategies similar to other allied health professional". Education providers can use these format preferences when designing professional learning about DCD.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education content should target practitioners unfamiliar with DCD, since the majority were unfamiliar with the diagnosis
#### Constraints
- The article states its results should be only considered as preliminary, and not illustrative of any nuance within current podiatric management of children with DCD

### Target Learners
- Australian podiatrists
- allied health professionals in continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- recognising presentation of developmental coordination disorder
- appropriate assessment and referral practices
- evidence-based management strategies for children with DCD
