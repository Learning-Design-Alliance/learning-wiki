# Engage with Empathy

## Description
Engage in effective online communication with empathy by taking the time to read and understand the original post, exchanging ideas with one or more users, tagging and mentioning community members, carefully crafting your message, presenting evidence and personal experiences, upholding the digital community’s agreed-upon norms, and consuming as much as you contribute.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Basic understanding of online communication, access to online platforms, a commitment to respectful and thoughtful interactions
#### Constraints
- Requires conscious effort, may be challenging for those unfamiliar with digital norms, depends on community norms and moderation
