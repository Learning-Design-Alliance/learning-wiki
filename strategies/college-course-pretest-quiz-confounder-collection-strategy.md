# Implement causal evaluation in a standard college course via pretest, weekly quizzes, and confounder inquiry at student meetings

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation for putting its model into practice: administer a pretest measuring prerequisite knowledge plus questions on family, stress, emotional problems, and available weekly time; use pretest scores to set the initial treatment and study plan; quiz weekly to select subsequent interventions such as talking to the TA, instructor, or counselor; and have staff "ask questions during the meeting with students to collect information about the potential confounders" for later analysis of joint intervention effects on the exam score.
