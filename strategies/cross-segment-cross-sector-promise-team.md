# Build a College Promise team spanning internal campus units and external cross-segment and cross-sector partners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Guidebook directs teams to identify internal partners (leadership, faculty, trustees, foundation, students, financial aid, counseling, research, and special programs) and external partners (K-12 districts, four-year universities, local government, businesses, nonprofits, and foundations). It provides an Internal Partner Identification Worksheet and an External Partner Identification Worksheet, and recommends personally contacting each prospective partner and including more than one representative per institution to mitigate turnover. AB19 requires funded colleges to partner with at least one local educational agency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Personally contact each prospective partner before program meetings
- Include more than one representative from each institution or stakeholder group
#### Constraints
- External partners may evolve over time as program features expand

### Target Learners
- first-time full-time community college students, including first-generation and low-income students

### Target Learning Goals
- college readiness, enrollment, persistence, and completion or transfer
