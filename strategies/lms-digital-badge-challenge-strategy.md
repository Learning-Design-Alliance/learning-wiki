# Award digital badges through an LMS for graded challenges in a gamified unit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe is to sequence unit contents into levels (basic, intermediate and advanced), missions and adventures on an LMS, assign exercises and activities as challenges, and award badges whose tier reflects the qualification obtained. Badges symbolise satisfactory task completion or positive behaviour and are assigned to parameters of final evaluation. "Obtaining three badges meant that the teaching unit had been passed."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An LMS platform (here Moodle) able to issue digital badges
- Challenges corresponding to exercises and activities based on unit contents
#### Constraints
- Badge tiers (bronze, silver, gold) tied to qualification obtained; tested with sixth-grade primary students

### Target Learners
- primary education students

### Target Learning Goals
- healthy habits and nutrition content
- motivation and engagement through recognition

## Claims

- [Flipped Classroom and badge-based gamification in a nutrition didactic unit were followed by higher post-test scores in motivation, autonomy and self-regulation among sixth-grade primary students](../claims/flipped-gamification-nutrition-unit-post-test-gains.md) [+W]
