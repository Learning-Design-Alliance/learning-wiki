# Stepwise process for creating learning outcomes using SOLO taxonomy verbs and rubric scales

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article offers a stepwise recipe: write high-level future-proof lemmas describing the end-state student, refine aspects, build rubric scales of four or five columns ordered per the SOLO taxonomy from 'Failing' to 'Excellent', place target descriptions in the 'good' column, use 'SOLO verbs' to characterise other cognitive levels, iterate the rubric so each row suggests a path of growth, and validate with employers and workers in the professional field.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ideally wrought in a small team combining subject expertise, educational experience and sensitivity to language; several iterations are needed
#### Constraints
- The authors are 'unaware of scientific evidence which order is better' for column ordering

### Target Learners
- curriculum designers in higher professional education

### Target Learning Goals
- creating learning outcomes for integral programmatic assessment
