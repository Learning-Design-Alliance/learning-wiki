# Use sliced assessment data to calibrate observers and give staff feedback on their rating deviations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because LiftUpp data can be sliced by member of staff, the system can explore staff calibration: well-trained staff use the full range of the six developmental indicators, but some staff use only 2 or 3 of the 6 points, so one staff member's '3' may equal another's '2'. LiftUpp shows staff members the indicators for which their assessments deviate from other staff, and this information is used to train observers.
