# Municipalized after-school science tutoring with individual attention plus peer discussion groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A socially priced alternative to the unregulated private-tutoring market: the Cdcs offers a doposcuola in which students are followed individually "con la stessa attenzione che riceverebbe nella lezione privata", while using the centre's premises and materials. Teachers then encourage peer exchange: "gli insegnanti del Centro incoraggeranno gli stessi a discutere fra loro delle materie esaminate, formando gruppi in cui allo studio individuale e rigoroso seguano confronti e dibattiti".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Municipal promotion of the Cdcs with tariffs designed to make the service accessible to the maximum number of users; teachers designated by the Management Committee and working as freelancers
#### Constraints
- Conceived as dynamic and non-assistenziale; addresses students in difficulty within scientific subjects, not general childcare

### Target Learners
- students of public and private schools needing support or deepening in scientific subjects

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidating and deepening school science curriculum knowledge
