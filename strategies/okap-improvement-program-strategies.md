# Programs seeking higher OKAP performance should offer incentives, provide effective access to learning content such as online question banks, schedule OKAP-dedicated didactics, and allocate more resident self-study time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendations translate its correlational findings into program actions: "Programs wishing to improve resident OKAP scores might consider offering incentives, providing effective access to learning content e.g. online question banks, and adjusting the curriculum to highlight OKAP material." The conclusion adds scheduling didactics dedicated to OKAP material, making question banks available, and considering more time for resident self-study. These map onto the observed associations with incentives, question bank use, didactic emphasis, and study hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program control over curriculum scheduling, incentive structures, and resource access
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on observational survey associations, not manipulated interventions; repercussions showed no association with performance

### Target Learners
- Ophthalmology residents
- Graduate medical education trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Medical knowledge acquisition as measured by in-training examinations
