# Present physics test problems bilingually, with each Russian problem followed by an English rendering

## Description
Every problem in the collection is printed twice: once in Russian and once in English, back to back. This parallel bilingual presentation makes a single set of physics problems usable by readers of both languages and preserves the original wording the author posed to students while exposing readers to English physics phrasing. The abstract states the problems were used "as weekly control works" in 2005, and the Solutions section likewise alternates Russian and English worked answers for each problem.
