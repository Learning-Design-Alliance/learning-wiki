# Provide subgoal hints alongside step hints in proof construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
LOGAX keeps a list of subgoals while constructing a proof and uses it to give hints about a subgoal rather than only the direction of the next step. As the article states, "In LOGAX we keep track of a list of subgoals while constructing a proof. We use this list to provide hints about a subgoal." Subgoal hints are withheld when deduction can still be applied backwards, when the subgoal coincides with an unmotivated line, or when it equals the next step. This follows evidence that informative feedback combined with subgoal information helps students correct mistakes more effectively.
