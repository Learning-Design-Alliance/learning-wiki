# Targeted interventions using structured intergroup contact, diverse patient care experiences and education on structural determinants of health to foster critical consciousness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q4` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on the finding that perspectives evolved from merit-based to systemic thinking, the authors recommend targeted interventions including "structured intergroup contact, patient care experiences with diverse populations, and education about structural determinants of health" to facilitate movement toward more systemically informed viewpoints. They note professional observations of privilege can be particularly transformative, and suggest faculty development extend beyond fact-checking to targeted discussions and reflections on physicians' own interactions with diverse patient populations.

## Design Implications

### Context
