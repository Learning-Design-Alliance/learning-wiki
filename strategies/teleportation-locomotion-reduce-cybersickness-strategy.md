# Use teleportation locomotion in VR training to minimise cybersickness and keep frames per second steady

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The protocol adopts teleportation as the preferred locomotion method for user navigation within the VR environment to minimise the risk of cybersickness. The authors explain that "Traditional VR movement options such as joystick-based locomotion can cause discomfort and disorientation due to mismatched sensory feedback", whereas teleportation lets the user snap from one point to another, reducing nausea and dizziness. They add that teleportation also kept frames per second at a steady 72, supporting smooth rendering and immersion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
