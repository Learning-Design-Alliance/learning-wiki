# Strategic Desk Arrangement

## Description
Arranging desks strategically can support different learning activities. Small group work benefits from tables, while rows can focus attention on the board. Consider the needs of students with vision impairments when arranging seating.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexible furniture arrangements, awareness of student learning needs.
#### Constraints
- May require time to adjust seating arrangements. Requires careful consideration of student needs and learning activities.
