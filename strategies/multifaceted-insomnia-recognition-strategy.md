## Description
The article recommends a multifaceted approach addressing both clinician- and patient-factors, on the grounds that this "is likely to be more effective than a single intervention." Suggested targets include chart reminders for physicians, infographic resources for patients, professional development resources and journal articles, increased education and training, and brief interventions suited to the time constraints of family practice consultations.
