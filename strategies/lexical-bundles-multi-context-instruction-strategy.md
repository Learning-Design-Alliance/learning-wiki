# Teach lexical bundles through multiple use contexts: conversation, role play, paragraph writing, and discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's instructional recipe embeds target bundles in several communicative contexts drawn from the course textbook rather than teaching them in isolation. As printed, "the experimental group received instruction on lexical bundles in different cont exts of conversation, role  play, paragraph writing and discussion extracted from True To Life text- book". Learners then used the bundles to write unified paragraphs, presented and discussed them, wrote conversations, and performed role plays, which the authors credit for the fluency gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shared course textbook (here True to Life) supplying the bundles and contexts for both groups
- A treatment period long enough for repeated use; the study lasted a whole academic semester
#### Constraints
- The control group in this study received no bundle instruction, so the strategy was tested against placebo rather than against an alternative pedagogy

### Target Learners
- Iranian EFL university students majoring in TEFL

### Target Learning Goals
- Paragraph writing production fluency

### Affordances
- Lexical Bundles Functional Taxonomy
