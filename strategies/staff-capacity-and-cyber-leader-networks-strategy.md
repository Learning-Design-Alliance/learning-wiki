# Build school staff capacity to support student leaders and sustain cyber leader networks across schools

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations are to enhance staff capacity to enable student change agents and to sustain leaders' efforts by building cross-school networks. The authors write that "Further research is needed to determine how to enha nce the capacity of school staff to enable young people to adopt change agent roles", and suggest building "netw orks of cyber leaders across schools to communicate and share ideas via online social networks". Leaders themselves wanted to mentor new leaders and network with other schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School staff willing and able to engage with and support student leaders; an online platform or mechanism for cross-school leader contact.
#### Constraints
- The article frames both as directions for further research rather than tested strategies.

### Target Learners
- secondary school student leaders (grade 10 cyber leaders)

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained student-led cyberbullying prevention and peer support
