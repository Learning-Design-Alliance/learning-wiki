The article advocates extending the Physics Pavilion programme to the United States, noting 32 million people attended at least one US music festival in 2014 and that festivals sell experiences. It states 'we advocate for partnering with a wide range of festivals' because a folk festival and an EDM festival reach different slices of the public.
