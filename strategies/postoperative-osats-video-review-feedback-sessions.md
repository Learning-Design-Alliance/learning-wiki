# Use post-operative OSATS video review and feedback sessions in surgical training programs while automated video analysis is not yet available

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an implementable training activity: reviewing recorded surgical videos and grading them with OSATS after the operation, then using the scores for structured feedback. The authors state that "OSATS can still be effectively used in training programs through post-operative video review and feedback sessions", and that future "automated video analysis and machine learning may offer solutions" to the time cost of real-time scoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to full-length surgical video recordings and trained graders able to apply the OSATS rubric.
#### Constraints
- The time-consuming nature of OSATS evaluation poses challenges for scalability in training.

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees and surgeons in robotic pancreatoduodenectomy training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving technical surgical performance through structured feedback on operative video
