# Teachers develop their own curriculum-aligned multimedia vocabulary programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that Thai English teachers develop and implement multimedia programs specifically designed for the vocabulary materials of a given semester, since commercial programs often fail to match curriculum objectives. The author argues the program "should align with English learning objectives set out in the curriculum, as well as that the prog ram was constructed based on pedagogical principles", and demonstrates that a simple computer program suffices for teacher-built multimedia.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials selected from English books or reading sources established in the school curriculum
#### Constraints
- Not all multimedia programs are appropriate for classroom vocabulary learning

### Target Learners
- Thai primary pupils learning English

### Target Learning Goals
- English vocabulary achievement
