# Phased iterative testing: optimization testing before efficacy testing before statewide scale-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The partnership sequences program development into distinct phases: "Conduct optimization testing of Write to Succeed (2023/24)", then "Conduct initial efficacy testing of Write to Succeed (2024/25)", then "Statewide scale-up of Write to Succeed (2025/27)". Refinement and adaptation occur alongside early phases, so evidence of feasibility and efficacy is gathered before broad deployment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership with state and local partners to adapt and refine the program
#### Constraints
- Phasing is bounded by the REL Southwest contract period

### Target Learners
- New Mexico teachers statewide

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving literacy instruction for English learner students
