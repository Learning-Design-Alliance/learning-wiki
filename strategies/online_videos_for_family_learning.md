# Online Videos for Family Learning

## Description
Using online videos to make it easier for families with different language needs to explore and learn together. Hispanic-Latino families have reported using YouTube to learn everything from recipes and hairstyles to English lessons, and some used it to follow up on academic topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to internet and devices to watch videos. Selection of relevant, high-quality videos.
#### Constraints
- May require digital literacy skills. Not all families have access to reliable internet or devices.
