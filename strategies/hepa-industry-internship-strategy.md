# Create HEPA-run industry internships and mentor training so early career scientists can experience industry settings without diluting core research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that HEPA create its own focused internship opportunities at all levels to train people for industry jobs, since industry-funded internships at PhD or postdoc level may be harder to fund. They note industry internships let participants experience industry culture and apply HEPA skills in a goal- and profit-oriented setting with time-bound deliverables, while training "needs careful planning and should not dilute the focus on core research goals." They also call for workshops training supervisors and mechanisms to integrate internships into the research curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Changes to funding rules and regulations and to supervisors' mindset to allow time off from core research; a source of funding separate from core HEPA research support
#### Constraints
- Internships for senior researchers at PhD or postdoc level may not be desired or hard to fund by industry; the duration of detector work experience may be short because the end goal is physics results

### Target Learners
- HEPA undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- Application of research skills in industry settings
- Understanding of industry culture and environment
