# Train students extensively on the use of speed reading strategies (skimming and scanning)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's central recommendation, drawn from its experimental finding that training on skimming and scanning improved reading comprehension. Skimming is defined as reading a text quickly to get a general idea, and scanning as reading to find specific information. The authors state it is recommended that teachers should train students extensively on the use of speed reading strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need training on the use of speed reading strategies, mainly the newly appointed, per the article's recommendation to the Ministry of Education.
#### Constraints
- The study's results can be generalized only to second secondary literary male students in Al-Korah Directorate of Education and similar directorates.

### Target Learners
- second secondary literary stream students learning English in Jordan

### Target Learning Goals
- English reading comprehension
- reading speed via skimming and scanning
