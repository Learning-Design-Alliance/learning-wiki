# Run a two-phase term-guessing game: timed drawing rounds followed by charades-style acting of curriculum terms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to organize a small competition in which one student draws a curriculum term while the group guesses within a time limit, then accelerate the pace by shortening the drawing time, and finally switch to a second phase where students "act out" the term as in charades. Terms are selected by dice roll from grade-sorted card sets. The authors used this format across three summer school editions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Age-based groups, cards of curriculum terms, a dice, a timer (about one minute per turn), and scoring a point for a correct guess within the allotment
#### Constraints
- Developed for a summer school for competition-winning students; the authors note curricula differ between countries and recommend building one's own card sets

### Target Learners
- elementary school students
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding physical concepts
- applying physics terms in everyday situations
