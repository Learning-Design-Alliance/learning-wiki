# Treat ELVO patients only at centers with 24/7 multidisciplinary team access including neurocritical care and neuroimaging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
This institutional strategy holds that successful treatment of the ELVO patient occurs within a multidisciplinary team framework, so patients should be treated at a center with round-the-clock access to equipped angiography suites, dedicated stroke and intensive care units, vascular neurology and neurocritical care expertise, neurosurgical expertise, and all relevant neuroimaging modalities. The document states "we feel it is critical that the patients be treated in a center, which has 24/7 access to the following" resources. It grounds this in the Background observation that on-site expertise in vascular neurology and neurocritical care is paramount to good outcomes.
