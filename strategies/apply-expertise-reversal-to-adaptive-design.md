# Design learner-tailored instructional systems by applying expertise reversal findings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends using expertise reversal findings as the basis for learner-tailored design: identify the learner's prior knowledge level and select instructional techniques and procedures whose effectiveness is appropriate to that level, since techniques effective for novices reverse in value for more knowledgeable learners. The strategy is grounded in the review's synthesis of empirical findings, their cognitive load interpretation, and the experimental adaptive implementations it surveys.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of learner prior knowledge or expertise, and a repertoire of instructional techniques whose effectiveness varies with expertise
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from studies reviewed within the cognitive load framework; the review presents implementation as recent experimental attempts

### Target Learners
- Learners varying in prior knowledge, from novices to more advanced learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Optimizing instructional effectiveness and efficiency across expertise levels
