# Lessons-learned planning recommendations for institutions creating a cineVR medical curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's Textbox 1 summarizes recommendations for each production step. For finding a partner: "Prioritize production partners with experience in health care" and "Ensure that they have the necessary technical infrastructure to maintain data security and Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) compliance." For filming: "Iteratively test different camera configurations in the room with providers and staff to ensure that the equipment does not get in the way of patient care" and "Avoid technical complications by having 2 sets of all filming equipment." For privacy: consult data security, compliance, IT, and legal teams early, develop a patient consent plan, co-develop a surrogate consent protocol with the IRB, and station research staff outside each trauma bay door to consent unconsented individuals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with production partners, institutional facilities teams, IRB, legal, and IT/data security offices
- A standard file management protocol followed by both research and filming teams
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a single institution's experience filming in one trauma bay and are offered as guidance, not tested interventions

### Target Learners
- medical trainees at academic teaching hospitals and trauma centers

### Target Learning Goals
- trauma care training
- teamwork and communication skills
