# Guided Inquiry

## Description
Guided inquiry is an educational approach that blends inquiry activities with direct instruction. Inquiry activities let students explore and search for answers, guided to maintain a 'just right' level of difficulty similar to a mystery story. Direct instruction provides clear explanations and structures. The goal is to combine both to effectively achieve different learning objectives, such as learning ideas and skills in varied contexts like labs, lectures, and discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate lab setting, well-designed activities, skilled teachers, and tools for guidance and assessment.
#### Constraints
- If a lab is too difficult, it can cause frustration. Requires careful design to ensure appropriate challenge. Differences in 'amount of help' raise questions of fairness if labwork is graded.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Teach thinking skills, promote conceptual understanding, improve motivation, develop problem-solving abilities, enhance analytical skills

### Affordances
- Promotes thinking and motivation when labs are well-designed with appropriate difficulty. Short-term frustration can motivate students to improve skills. Skillful teaching can minimize permanent frustration and build confidence.

### Personalization
- Difficulty can be adjusted by modifying what students do in the lab (intrinsic difficulty) and providing guidance to support their ability to cope. Teachers can offer personally customized assistance during lab by observing students and adjusting difficulty level for individuals or groups.

- Observe student engagement, problem-solving skills, analytical abilities, motivation, and confidence. Assess learning outcomes based on conceptual understanding and skill development.

## Impact
- Inquiry experiences promote thinking and motivation when labs are well designed. It combines active reception of knowledge with discovery learning, promoting effective and efficient learning experiences.
