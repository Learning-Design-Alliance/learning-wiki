Sustain cross-agency collaboration through a dedicated district leader and monthly partner meetings¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

PUSD created or renewed community partnerships to improve delivery of school-based mental health services, using the Administrator on Special Assignment position to foster collaboration among state and county agencies and community organizations. "Ongoing monthly collaboration meetings have served as a platform for open dialogue, strategic planning, and implementation among these educational partners," resulting in student referrals to a web of community supports. The brief presents this as Component 3 practice worth sustaining.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • a dedicated district leadership position (Administrator on Special Assignment) and committed partner agencies

Constraints¶

  • developed in a rural district context following the 2018 Camp Fire, with funding tied to grants

Target Learners¶

  • PUSD students referred to community and school-based supports

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved access to mental health services and community supports that promote youth well-being

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustain district mental health initiatives by braiding grant funding into annual plans, ongoing surveys, and reimbursement streams
  • Sustain school mental health programs through multi-payer fee schedules and alignment with existing initiatives
  • Implement a web of supports for students in foster care: one-stop resource centers, school-based liaisons, tiered services, and cross-system collaboration
