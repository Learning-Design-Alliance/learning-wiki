# Restructure the communication course so that history taking and breaking bad news are taught from the first and second sessions onward

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Following the 2009/10 evaluation, the authors report conceptual changes to the course's session order so that core topics are addressed from the outset: 'These changes aim for the consistent teaching of the basics of medical communication using the topics of taking medical histories (1st course session) and breaking bad news (2nd session) so as to focus on the topics right from the outset.' This responds to free-text calls for more practice and less theory (43 of 73 improvement proposals).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multi-session course structure whose session order and topic allocation can be rearranged as part of continuous quality development
#### Constraints
- The change was introduced after the 2009/10 academic year as part of quality assurance; its effects were not evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- taking medical histories
- breaking bad news
- balancing theory and practice time
