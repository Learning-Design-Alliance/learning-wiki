# Extended Case Study approach: teacher-directed analysis of an environmental issue using primary and secondary sources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Extended Case Study is one of two school-level approaches the newsletter recommends for global change instruction. It is "a teacher-directed analysis of a particular issue, using both primary and secondary sources to deliver issue-focused information and skills to learners". Learners are oriented to the issue, investigate it in groups, reach a group decision, and then embark on remediation. The article notes the method gives the teacher flexibility and control but demands considerable time, energy and skill to assemble the case study.
