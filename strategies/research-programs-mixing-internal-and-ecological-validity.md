# Build research programs combining studies optimized for internal validity with studies optimized for ecological validity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The commentary concludes that the dual goal of use-inspired basic research probably cannot be fully accomplished within single studies, because integrating learning into the school curriculum costs experimental control. It therefore recommends research programs with differently structured studies: "Some studies should focus more on internal validity and other studies more on ecological validity, with both types of studies well aligned with each other". It cites work on reflective journal writing as an example of a program largely fitting this plea, and hopes future collections include thematically aligned ecologically optimized studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of differently structured studies aligned with each other thematically
#### Constraints
- Fully curriculum-integrated studies lose far-reaching experimental control, encountering the usual internal-versus-ecological validity dilemma

### Target Learners
- school students in authentic classroom settings

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining outcomes of meaningful learning over time
