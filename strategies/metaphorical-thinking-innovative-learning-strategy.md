# Apply innovative learning such as the metaphorical thinking method from elementary to secondary education so teachers and students think more comprehensively

## Description
The article recommends innovative learning to raise questioning ability: "it needs an effort in the form of innovative learning such as metaphorical Thinking method that can be applied from elementary to secondary education, so that both teachers and students will be accustomed to think more comprehensively from all directions of thought in solving problems of mathematics education." It rests on the experiment's finding that MT learning outperformed conventional learning.
