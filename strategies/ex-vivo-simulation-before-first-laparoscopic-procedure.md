# Integrate ex vivo laparoscopic basic-skills simulation training at the beginning of surgical residency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend broad, nationwide introduction of ex vivo laparoscopic basic-skills training into residency curricula, right at the start of training and before the first laparoscopic procedure. They argue the NOVICE trial "provides the highest level of evidence for integrating ex vivo laparoscopic basic skill training into surgery residency curricula right in the beginning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to validated simulation curricula with demonstrated operating-room transferability
#### Constraints
- In Germany and other countries, ex vivo laparoscopic training is not yet mandatory in formal residency training

### Target Learners
- first-year surgical residents (novices)

### Target Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic skills transferable to operating-room performance
