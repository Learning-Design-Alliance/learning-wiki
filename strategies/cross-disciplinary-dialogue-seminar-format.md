# Two-researcher cross-disciplinary dialogue format with moderator and live audience interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The seminars used an innovative format in which "two researchers coming from different fields of research (physics vs philosophy, history of science, scientific communication) meet a moderator and informally discuss about gravity and related phenomena". The public can chat and indirectly interact during the YouTube live using Mentimeter. Videos remain on the INFN Cagliari YouTube channel. Thirty-two scientists from European universities and institutions contributed across topics from quantum mechanics and general relativity to dark matter and dark energy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers from different fields paired in dialogue
- A moderator
- A live channel with an interaction tool such as Mentimeter
#### Constraints
- Delivered online due to the pandemic

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 17-19

### Target Learning Goals
- contemporary physics concepts
- philosophy and history of science
- science communication
