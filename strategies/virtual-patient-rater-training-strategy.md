# Use LLM-based virtual patient simulations to provide on-demand, repeated standardized interview practice for rater training and calibration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends leveraging virtual patient simulations to give trainees repeated practice opportunities and to calibrate raters throughout a clinical trial. By providing on demand access to a wide range of patient personae, the simulations could offer the repeated practice needed to build expertise, and their consistent patient stimuli could be leveraged to calibrate raters and mitigate rater drift, contributing to enhanced data quality in clinical trials.
