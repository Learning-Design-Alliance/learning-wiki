# Use LLMs as neutral mediators in peer learning through bias-mitigated feedback, structured-feedback scaffolding, and rubric-driven peer assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For peer learning, the article proposes LLM actions including virtual peer simulation of debate scenarios, adaptive tutoring prompts, bias-mitigated feedback, feedback-integration systems using semantic clustering, structured-feedback scaffolding, and rubric-driven assessment calibrated against instructor rubrics such as CLEAR or PMC. It states that "LLMs can mitigate this through bias -mitigated feedback, standardizing critique language and detecting emotional polarity to ensure balanced exchanges," addressing friendship-network and hierarchy bias in studio critique.
