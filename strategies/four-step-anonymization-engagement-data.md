# Four-step anonymization balancing disclosure risk and data utility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The anonymization tip sheet defines anonymization as 'removing information that could be used to identify someone' and prescribes four steps: redact direct identifiers such as name, address, or telephone numbers; highlight and consider indirect identifiers that could reveal an individual through cross-referencing; create and consistently use an anonymization log stored separately from anonymized files; and 'Avoid over- or under-anonymization to mitigate disclosure while maintaining data utility and value.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Store the anonymization log of original names, pseudonyms, and demographics separately from the anonymized data files
