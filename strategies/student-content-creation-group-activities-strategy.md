## Description
As an extension activity, the article recommends that students collaboratively produce new educational content on Mathematica applications in group theory and release it publicly. It gives one concrete example topic: "Generalized Euler angles for different groups", with a cited reference for further reading. This positions learners as contributors to open educational resources rather than only consumers.
