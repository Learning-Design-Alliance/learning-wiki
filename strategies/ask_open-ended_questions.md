# Ask Open-Ended Questions

## Description
Don’t assume you know how a student is feeling. Be attentive to nonverbal cues and ask them directly. When you ask open-ended questions, students can share what’s on their minds without feeling like you’ve already sized up the situation. Offer options by saying, “I noticed you had your head down in class today. Are you frustrated, angry, or nervous about something?”
