# Instructor-assigned groups and frequent check-ins to prevent student isolation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that instructors deliberately assign students to groups when needed and check in frequently with students, especially those who may be marginalized. The article states that instructors should "be careful to avoid marooning underrepresented students" and should "check in with students frequently." These strategies aim to ensure no student is left unsupported in pair-based lab work.
