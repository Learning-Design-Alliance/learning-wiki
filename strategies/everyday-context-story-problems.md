# Anchor physics teaching in everyday-life contexts, using newspaper and advertisement story problems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following context-based science education, teachers base instruction in everyday life contexts to bring the "dry" theories of physics closer to reality. The article gives the example of Kuhn & Müller (2014) using "newspaper story problems: a trip around the planet in an electric plane" as input for questions on electrical power production and consumption. Other examples include sales promotions for home appliances and claims about a man jumping from the stratosphere, which can be verified or rejected using scientific theory.
