# Begin explicit strategy instruction once learners reach a reading age of GE 5 with a critical vocabulary mass in place

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends timing reading and learning strategies instruction to the learner's vocabulary development: strategies become acquirable once fluent reading is possible. The author writes that "strategies are so important to ESL learners: they can begin to acquire them once they have an estimated reading age of grade equivalent (GE) 5 and the critical mass of vocabulary for fluent reading is in place." She pairs this with direct vocabulary instruction in context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A critical mass of vocabulary for fluent reading, estimated by the author at roughly 10,000-12,000 words
- An estimated reading age of grade equivalent 5
