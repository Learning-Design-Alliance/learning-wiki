Embed product rollout into existing routines with structured cadences, teacher champions, and stipends to accelerate adoption¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends supporting implementation with structured cadences such as grade-level meetings, teacher champions, and stipends. It advises that "When new products enhance existing structures rather than add new structural needs, their value is recognized more quickly." At CICS, the rollout paired the product launch with a stipend role for teacher champions, structured meeting cadences, and iterative prototyping, and deployment was timed to end-of-quarter data review cycles for natural integration.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires identifying teacher champions and providing structured meeting cadences and stipends to support implementation.

Constraints¶

  • The initial rollout missed school-level buy-in because the July co-design workshop included only a district leader, requiring a separate buy-in session for school staff.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 district and school staff adopting new student success tools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained adoption of student success technology integrated into existing workflows
