Structure AI-supported professional development around collaborative reflection rather than technical training alone¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that AI should not serve as a shortcut for training but rather as a supportive tool embedded within reflective and collaborative learning processes. Teachers engaged in structured reflection with trainers and peers, critically evaluating AI-generated resources and discussing pedagogical implications through weekly hybrid meetings that were not only technical but deeply reflective.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Weekly collaborative meeting structures with time for reflection and peer discussion

Constraints¶

  • Findings drawn from one rural school context with eight teachers

Target Learners¶

  • Rural language teachers participating in professional development

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Critical evaluation of AI-generated teaching resources

Related Strategies¶

  • Structure AI-focused professional development as a supported trajectory of planning, reflection, design autonomy, and embedded critical AI literacy
  • Integrate structured opportunities for critical engagement with AI into teacher education programs
  • Institutional AI initiatives should incorporate discussion, reflection, and distributed discipline-specific support networks rather than centralized information provision
  • Teacher AI professional development should pair readiness-building with structured engagement with attribution and pedagogy
  • Shift teacher professional development from information-based training to experience-based AI design activities in sustainability contexts
  • Embed AI training for pre-service science teachers within science pedagogy and inquiry-based teaching rather than general tool operation
  • Adapt Higher Education professional learning models to centre disciplinary culture when developing teachers' digital and pedagogical capabilities
