# Three-week staged SBAR plus mini-CEX clinical teaching sequence with one-on-one mentoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable clinical teaching recipe: establish a teaching group led by the head nurse, train teachers in SBAR concepts and mini-CEX scoring, then run three stages — day-1 theoretical teaching of the SBAR assessment form (S, B, A, R contents), week-2 daily one-on-one bedside case teaching with weekly focused guidance sessions, and a third-weekend teaching assessment in which each intern independently cares for one patient and is mini-CEX-evaluated across nursing assessment, examination, humanistic care, communication, problems and health education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching team of 3–5 nurses with bachelor's degree or above, 'competent nurse' or above and > 3 years of nursing teaching experience
- A fixed one-on-one teacher per intern and an independent blinded evaluator for outcome assessment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as a 3-week short-term intervention in tertiary teaching hospitals; the authors caution effects may be overestimated relative to resource-poor settings

### Target Learners
- nursing interns with at least 8 months of internship remaining

### Target Learning Goals
- structured clinical communication
- clinical thinking
- comprehensive clinical nursing ability
