Integrate collaborative online comic creation into Mandarin vocabulary learning activities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that collaborative learning, especially CSCL, should be applied in daily activity because it is significantly beneficial for students' learning activity, learning behavior, and learning products. Students work in small groups in a computer lab, with one computer per group, to create online comics on www.toondoo.com. Students communicate and negotiate before making group decisions about the story.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to a computer lab with internet-connected computers (18 sets in this study) with Simplified Chinese language input enabled on keyboards

Constraints¶

  • The article notes that internet connection stability and sufficient time duration are necessary for online activity

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students learning Mandarin as a foreign language

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mandarin vocabulary learning
  • Chinese character writing
  • collaborative story creation

Claims¶

  • Collaborative online comic creation produced significantly higher comic-creation scores than both individual online comic creation and paper-based comic creation [+W]
  • Students in the collaborative online comic group showed more positive attitudes toward comics learning than students in the individual online comic and paper-based comic groups [+W]
  • Students in the collaborative online comic group spent more time on learning-related behaviors and less time on learning-unrelated behaviors than students in the other two groups [+W]
  • Collaborative online comic creation improved Mandarin vocabulary performance significantly more than individual online comic creation or paper-based comic creation [+W]
  • Students reported that internet connection instability and limited time were barriers to creating online comics [~W]
  • Students had no significant improvement in Mandarin vocabulary performance in the control group or the individual comics group [+W]
  • A mobile multimedia learning system improved EFL students' posttest performance, and cooperative tasks outperformed individual tasks [+W]
