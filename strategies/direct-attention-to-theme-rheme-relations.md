# Direct students' attention to relating Themes and Rhemes to preceding and succeeding text when writing in English

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "pedagogical efforts are needed to direct students' attention to properly relating Theme or Rheme to preceding and succeeding Themes and Rhemes to hold onto important information when writing in English." This strategy shifts focus from sentence-level grammar to discourse-level information flow, implemented in the study through ten 50-minute sessions of explicit TP instruction over 10 weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction in TP patterns delivered over multiple sessions; the article notes TP instruction "required time for instruction results to emerge"
#### Constraints
- Effects were demonstrated with Chinese college English majors on TOEFL-style essay topics over a 10-week period; the design was quasi-experimental with intact classes

### Target Learners
- Chinese college students learning English academic writing

### Target Learning Goals
- Using thematic progression patterns to develop coherent English essays
