# Subject-matter faculty teach short lecture courses devoted entirely to teaching their subject in the secondary school

## Description
The department offers twelve short lecture courses in secondary methods taught by members of the faculty who teach the subjects themselves. As the article states, "A student must be prepared in subject matter before he enters one of these courses, and the lectures are devoted entirely to the teaching of the subject in the secondary school." Courses are offered in Latin, Greek, French, German, English Language, English Literature, Chemistry, Physics, Botany, Zoology, Mathematics and History.
