# Individual assessment during pair work: rotating roles, separate predictions and explanations, and independently-demonstrated ratings per outcome

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A proposed assessment procedure for shared-robot pair work: students alternate operating and checking roles and record contributions; at each checkpoint each submits a separate prediction and explanation; the instructor observes each student's practical task and asks a follow-up question without partner assistance. "The instructor records each outcome as independently demonstrated, demonstrated with prompting, or not yet demonstrated, with supporting evidence." Pair robot performance is recorded separately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Table IV evidence and success criteria for each learning outcome
- Role logs documenting individual contributions
#### Constraints
- The article states this assessment procedure is proposed and remains to be evaluated
- Role logs inform collaboration assessment but do not establish understanding

### Target Learners
- High-school students working in pairs on a shared robot

### Target Learning Goals
- Distinguishing individual conceptual understanding from team robot performance
