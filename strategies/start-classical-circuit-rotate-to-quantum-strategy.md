# Teach BV by starting from the classical parity circuit and rotating it into the quantum form

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends beginning instruction with the classical GF(2) write circuit and deriving the standard BV circuit from it, citing Mermin's comment that this is a much easier way to understand what is going on. The authors state their choice "for the most striking pedagogical effect and possibly least difficult intuition building, is to start with the classical circuit", while leaving ordering to educator discretion. The approach pairs visualized transformations with prompts about why verification is needed and where intuition may mislead.
