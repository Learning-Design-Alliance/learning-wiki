# Future research programme for digital movement-based learning in early childhood

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors set out a research agenda: validate multiple age-appropriate approaches for measuring cognitive load in young children (pictorial rating scales, simplified self-report, behavioural indicators, observer-based measures, physiological proxies); run longer programs (e.g., 4-6 weeks) during regular routines in early childhood education and care centres with longitudinal designs; replicate in low socioeconomic settings with a possible non-digital comparison group; and use mixed-methods designs, expert stakeholder input and randomised controlled trials to moderate cognitive demands of physical and learning tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research designs capable of measuring cognitive load and longer-term retention; access to early childhood settings with consistent attendance
#### Constraints
- The current museum context made multiple sessions impractical, since children irregularly or casually visit children's museums

### Target Learners
- Young children in early childhood education and care, including low-SES populations

### Target Learning Goals
- Establishing mechanisms and longer-term effects of digitally supported movement-based learning
