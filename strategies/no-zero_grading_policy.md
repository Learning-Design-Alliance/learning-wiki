# No-Zero Grading Policy

## Description
Setting the lowest possible grade for any assignment or test at 50 percent, even when students turn in no work at all, to give all students a chance to succeed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policy implementation
#### Constraints
- Can be seen as undermining instruction, may not teach responsibility
