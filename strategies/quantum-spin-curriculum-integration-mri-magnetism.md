# Incorporate quantum spin into existing curriculum through magnetism and real-world applications like MRI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends embedding quantum spin under existing Australian Curriculum elaborations rather than as separate content, using it to answer students' 'why' questions about magnetism. It states "Quantum spin is not explicitly mentioned in the curriculum, but it underpins many learning statements and can be easily incorporated into the classroom under the ACARA science curriculum elaborations." MRI is used as the anchor application, since it uses spin precession, spin flips and spin addition to image the body.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Linking activities to specific ACARA elaborations by year level (e.g. Year 6 Earth and Space sciences for precession)
#### Constraints
- Quantum spin itself is not explicitly named in the Version 9.0 Australian curriculum

### Target Learners
- Year 5-10 students in Australian schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Explaining why magnetism exists
- Connecting quantum spin to medical imaging technologies
