Collaborative Human-AI Revision Design embedding prompt engineering across drafting, feedback, and rewriting phases¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes a "Collaborative Human-AI Revision Design" that systematically embeds prompt engineering into three writing phases. In drafting, teachers guide learners to construct "constraint-based prompts" (e.g., outlines, vocabulary matrices, counter-arguments) while prohibiting continuous prose generation. In feedback, students use "criteria-specific diagnostic prompts" treating the AI as a formative peer reviewer. In rewriting, assessment becomes process-oriented, such as "Prompt Reflection Portfolios" rewarding metacognitive quality of interaction and productive failure.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Prompting is taught explicitly as a cognitive support strategy, not a shortcut for text generation.

Constraints¶

  • The strategy is a proposal aligned with the article's correlational empirical model, not an intervention the article tested.

Target Learners¶

  • University EFL learners writing with generative AI

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Deep revision, critical synthesis of AI feedback, and human-AI collaboration as academic competency

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement AI-peer integrated feedback as a standardized two-phase revision cycle with rubric-aligned prompting and instructor monitoring
  • Integrate AI feedback as a supportive resource within process-oriented drafting cycles, with explicit guidance on critical evaluation
  • Use GenAI across the writing process stages of pre-writing, drafting language support, and revision feedback, and for discussion questions, differentiated explanations, and scaffolded practice
  • Train teachers in prompt engineering, evaluating AI-generated responses, ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making
  • Process Writing Workshop
  • Peer Revision
  • Calibrate GenAI feedback through calibrated prompts and pedagogical (re-)framing
  • Implement an AI-Peer-Teacher hybrid integrated feedback model staged across drafting, revision, and polishing phases
  • Provide explicit instructional boundary cues, process-oriented assessment, and peer modeling of verification to frame responsible GenAI use
  • Design AI writing support to reduce ownership loss: fade AI interactions over time, generate incomplete text requiring rewriting, and make the chatbox editable
  • Provide timely automated feedback through an AI chatbot during EFL writing practice
  • Teach genAI operations, prompt engineering, failure modes, and supervised overreliance before graded genAI-assisted writing
  • Use AI tools to support assessment, feedback and monitoring of learner progress
