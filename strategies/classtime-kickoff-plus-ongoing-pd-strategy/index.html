Implement Classtime with a kick-off training day plus ongoing grade-specific follow-up sessions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The summary describes a professional development model for successful Classtime implementation: a foundational kick-off training session delivered in person or online (typically 6 hours) as early as possible in the school year, followed by grade-specific sessions in both fall and spring (typically 1–2 hours each). PD teaches educators the platform's features, the strategic thinking needed in math, science, and ELA, and how to analyze questions to determine appropriate instructional strategies. Successful implementations have included full kick-off days plus four on-site focus blocks with teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom teachers plus support from instructional coaches, math coaches, curriculum/assessment coordinators, principals, and district leadership
  • Classtime educational consultants and regional sales managers working directly with districts on implementation, training, and data analysis

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Classroom teachers and their students in grades 3–6

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective use of the Classtime platform
  • Strategic thinking in mathematics, science, and ELA
  • Analyzing assessment questions to select instructional strategies

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Provide virtual and on-site professional learning on the digital platform and grade-specific curriculum training
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Deliver RGR instruction via incremental self-paced online professional development completed before and after instruction begins
  • Implement World of Words with developer training, ongoing coaching, and structured text-set materials
  • Use optional professional development sessions for administrators and educators to support Imagine Math implementation
  • Brief initial training plus follow-up courses and coaching for program implementation
  • Combine summer professional development with school-year on-site coaching and provision of complete instructional materials
  • Implement EWIMS through combined in-person and online training with ongoing technical assistance liaisons
  • Support implementation with asynchronous onboarding plus ongoing data coaching and scripted lessons
  • Support implementation with initial Science of Reading training plus PLC-based lesson study
  • Provide on-site first-year training of 4-5 hours plus building-level ongoing support each year of ST Math implementation
  • Prepare teachers with one full training day plus a mid-year follow-up day
  • Provide various forms of follow-up to sustain new practices learned in PD
  • Build coordinator capacity through a structured weekly professional learning community with personalized coaching and targeted assets-and-needs-assessment training
