# Give life sciences students frequent practice negotiating among multiple model representations, such as translating equations into graphs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that instructors align models with their representations and give students practice moving among them, since models with multiple objectives may employ multiple representations. It states that "Life sciences students may need frequent practice in negotiating among the representations – for example, translating equations into graphs." Predictive models tend to use equations and graphs because predictions are often quantitative, so physics teachers are advised to emphasize mathematical representations when students engage with predictive models.
