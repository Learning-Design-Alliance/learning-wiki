# Archive theories and best practices as ways of goal-decomposition in an ontology-engineered database

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors envision archiving all learning/instructional theories in an advanced database in two complementary ways: semantic technology for intelligent search of related papers with well-organized metadata, and OMNIBUS-style ways of goal-decomposition to store theoretical knowledge operationally. They argue best practices extracted from practitioners should be stored on the same platform as theory-extracted decompositions, since the ontology of states lets many theories and best practices be captured in a common framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An ontology of states that adequately characterizes situations of learning/instructional processes and learner understanding states
#### Constraints
- Integration of top-down and bottom-up ontology engineering approaches is left as future work

### Target Learners
- Instructional practitioners and AIED system developers

### Target Learning Goals
- Making learning/instructional theories truly accessible to practitioners
