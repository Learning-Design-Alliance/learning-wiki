# Include all five consensus-defined fluoroscopic views in a simulation-based curriculum, even those that fail to discriminate experience levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that a simulation-based curriculum for fluoroscopy after volar plating of distal radius fractures should train all five views defined by the global Delphi consensus study, not only the three that discriminated between experience levels in this study. The authors state that "an ensuing simulation-based curriculum should include them", because several views are necessary to assess the surgical result, including a tangential view shown to be superior to the lateral view in detecting dorsal screw penetration and the AP view valuable for serial postoperative assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulator scenario supporting all five pre-defined fluoroscopic views: PA, AP, straight lateral, lateral facet, and dorsal tangential
#### Constraints
- The tangential and AP views did not discriminate between novices and experienced surgeons in this sample, as those views were not routinely used by the experienced surgeons

### Target Learners
- Orthopedic trainees learning intraoperative fluoroscopy

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquiring and interpreting all five consensus-defined fluoroscopic views for assessing distal radius fracture fixation
