# Extend errorless memory training dosage: increase sessions from 12 to 15, lengthen sessions gradually from 30 to 45 minutes, and add revision sessions at sessions 5, 10, and 15

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend strengthening the training program's intensity and consolidation. They suggest to "increase the number of training sessions from 12 to 15, with some training sessions lengthened gradually from around 30 minutes to 45 minutes when subjects have increased their attention span," and to add "three revision training sessions ... at training sessions 5, 10, and 15" to reinforce and consolidate memory based on errorless learning and spaced retrieval strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Session lengthening should follow increases in the subject's attention span
#### Constraints
- Some intervention outcomes did not achieve statistical significance, probably as a result of limitations in the intensity and duration of the training program and the small sample size

### Target Learners
- Patients with early Alzheimer's disease receiving individualized memory training

### Target Learning Goals
- Consolidation and retention of memory strategies and trained content over time
