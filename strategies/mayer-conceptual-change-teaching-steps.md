# Use the three steps of conceptual change as teaching starting points: confront misconceptions, provide concrete analogies, and practice testing hypotheses

## Description
The article distills Mayer's account of conceptual change into an implementable teaching recipe. Learning science involves changing one's mental model or schema, and Mayer names three steps: recognizing an anomaly, constructing a new model, and using the new model to make and test predictions. The article states that 'each step can be used as a starting point for teaching and learning: (a) Confronting misconceptions, (b) Providing a concrete analogy, and (c) How to test hypotheses, respectively'. Teachers are advised to be more aware of what and how students learn.
