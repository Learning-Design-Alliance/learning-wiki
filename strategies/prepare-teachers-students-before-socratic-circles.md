# Prepare teachers and students with awareness, vocabulary, and facilitation knowledge before implementing Socratic Circles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Post-implementation debrief sessions in the RCS pilot yielded iteration learnings about preparation needs. The report states that "Teachers needed guided preparation for expanding their awareness of the historical and modern underpinnings of the racialized landscape of education," alongside practical knowledge of Socratic Circles, monitoring tensions, and effective discourse facilitation moves. It further finds that "Students needed preparation with topic area vocabulary and concepts, and practical knowledge about Socratic Circles," and that students needed variation in access points for participation and leadership. These learnings improved subsequent implementations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development addressing both theoretical and technical knowledge of racial equity and Socratic Circle facilitation
- Student-facing scaffolding materials building topic vocabulary and concepts
