# Use flipped classroom with pre-reading multimedia materials and in-class team-based article appraisal practice for EBN training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy assigns learners to pre-read multimedia training materials before class, then uses classroom time for practice, discussion, and clarification of EBN steps, followed by team-based appraisal of research articles. The article reports that "The experimental group were asked to pre-read two multimedia training materials about the EBN 5As and spent the three-hour content-based section on practice, discussion and clarification on the EBN 5As, followed by a two-hour practice on appraising research articles." Both groups used team-based discussion during the appraisal practice segment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multimedia pre-reading materials covering the EBN 5As steps
- A qualified EBP instructor to coordinate the course
- Subgroup structure for team-based article appraisal practice
#### Constraints
- The article notes the course was "still focused on information delivery rather than providing nurses with virtual simulations to practice and apply the learned concepts in a stimulating context"

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses in hospital in-service training

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based nursing knowledge of the 5A steps
- Critical appraisal of research articles
- Self-efficacy in evidence-based practice
