# Fund large-scale, long-duration PUI-focused research collaborations that train faculty alongside students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because undergraduate research projects are short (a semester or summer) and students have little prior training, the paper recommends building long-running collaborative projects and programs where faculty are trained alongside students. The Undergraduate ALFALFA Team, founded for faculty development and undergraduate research in the extragalactic ALFALFA HI survey, provides "a support structure for PUI faculty that maximizes the time that they can devote to the project and develops their research and education skills."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained multi-year funding is needed; the authors suggest awards up to 5 years and note legacy research programs may not easily fit into current funding program structures, requiring new funding solicitations.
#### Constraints
- Undergraduate research projects are typically short-duration, started and finished within a semester or summer, and students often have little course-work or training in the project's research field.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students at PUIs
- Faculty at primarily undergraduate institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement in authentic astronomical research and development of faculty research and education skills
