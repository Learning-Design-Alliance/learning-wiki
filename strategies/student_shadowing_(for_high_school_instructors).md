# Student Shadowing (for high school instructors)

## Description
A veteran teacher or coach shadows one or two students for a couple of days to gain insights into their experiences. Key takeaways often include recognizing that students sit all day, listen passively for 90% of the time, and may not be held accountable for participating in class. The goal is to understand the students' perspective and adjust teaching practices accordingly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time to shadow students, awareness of key areas to observe (sitting time, passive listening, participation)
#### Constraints
- May not be representative of all students' experiences, requires willingness to adjust teaching practices
