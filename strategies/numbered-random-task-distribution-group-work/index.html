Distribute group tasks as numbered activities assigned randomly so each child participates fairly¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To secure the participation of every child, the article recommends the teacher "separate the tasks into as many activities as there are group members and to give numbers to these tasks". The numbered tasks are then distributed randomly, for example by having group members select numbered cards and fulfil the activities defined for that card. This creates a feeling of fair distribution and prevents members from drifting to other things instead of the task.
