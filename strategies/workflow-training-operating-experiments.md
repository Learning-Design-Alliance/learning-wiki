## Description
Students of ISU and visiting students work with real hardware, software and data of the TAIGA and Baikal-GVD experiments. The article describes an established workflow for young scientists that "includes interview, training, data analysis and simulation, field works, etc.", and reports that this workflow has shown its efficiency, resulting in significant results obtained by young members of the collaborations.
