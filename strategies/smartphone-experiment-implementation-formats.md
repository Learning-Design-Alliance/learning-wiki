# Three classroom implementation formats for smartphone experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper reports three observed ways teachers implemented the smartphone experiments: full experiment with worksheet-guided data collection and analysis; experiment stations where students move in groups between shorter experiments sharing similar concepts; and lecture demonstration where teachers show experiments to illustrate taught theory. Teachers can choose a format matching lesson time and goals.
