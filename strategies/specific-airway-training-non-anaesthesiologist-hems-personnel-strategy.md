# Provide specific airway management training for non-anaesthesiologist HEMS personnel, supported by simulation access and HEMS-tailored national guidelines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "it would be desirable to ensure specific airway management training when specialists other than anesthesiologists serve in HEMS operations", echoing Italian National Institute of Health guidance. They further recommend developing "a standardised HEMS equipment checklist", targeted training for non-specialist personnel, and "dedicated airway management guidelines tailored to the HEMS context" to harmonise care across Italian HEMS bases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation training facilities and funding to cover course costs, which the authors note range between €300 and €800 for a 16-hour difficult airway course
#### Constraints
- The article notes large areas of Italy lack access to simulation facilities, and the SIAARTI guidelines most often referenced have not been updated since 2010 and do not specifically address the HEMS setting

### Target Learners
- Non-anaesthesiologist physicians and crew serving in helicopter emergency medical services

### Target Learning Goals
- Airway management competence, including difficult airway and CICO scenario management in the prehospital setting
