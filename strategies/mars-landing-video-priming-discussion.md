# Open the lesson with Mars landing videos and phenomenological discussion questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The lesson opens with two Mars landing animations, "Curiosity has landed" and "InSight: Landing on Mars", which students watch "Zur Einstimmung"; teachers may instead have students research basic Mars characteristics themselves. Four opening questions then prompt discussion, among them "Warum funktioniert ein Fallschirm auf dem Mond nicht?" and what must be considered for a parachute to function well on Mars.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Playback of the two linked videos (2:30 and 3:18) or, alternatively, reference material such as Wikipedia articles for student research on Mars characteristics.
