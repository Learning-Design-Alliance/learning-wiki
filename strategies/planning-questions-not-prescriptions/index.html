Use planning questions rather than fixed prescriptions when designing staff development program features¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because staff development programs differ in type and purpose, the authors replace the earlier edition's list of research-based recommendations with pertinent questions and comments attached to each of the nineteen program-design features grouped under objectives, delivery system, and administration. They state: "instead of recommendations, this edition presents questions that should be askednot prescriptions that must be followedwhen designing the various features of a staff development program." Planners use these questions to tailor design features to their chosen objective and model.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The planner must first determine the program's objectives and select a model before applying the design-feature questions

Constraints¶

  • The authors argue prior recommendations seem less valid because they do not acknowledge that different types of staff development programs have different purposes

Target Learners¶

  • educators and policymakers designing staff development programs at building, district, or state level

Target Learning Goals¶

  • designing effective staff development programs
