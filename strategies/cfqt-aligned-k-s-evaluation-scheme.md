# Map course evaluation tasks to CFQT proficiency areas, pairing knowledge (K) and skills (S) assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An evaluation scheme mapping assessment tasks to the framework's three proficiency areas at the course's target level, pairing knowledge and skills measures: multiple-choice exam, concept identification, workshop, guided practice, short essay and discussion forum, weighted 25%, 15%, 20%, 10%, 20% and 10%. 'Slightly more weight is given to the first area' to ensure students have a strong theoretical foundation in quantum concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluation tasks must be developed from the proficiency level descriptions for the three areas, 'which detail the expected knowledge and skills'
#### Constraints
- In each of the three areas, a greater emphasis is placed on knowledge assessment rather than skills assessment

### Target Learners
- Students in the basic introductory quantum course assessed at the A1 awareness level

### Target Learning Goals
- Balanced assessment of theoretical understanding and practical application aligned with framework competencies
