# Guided derivation plus comparative Mars-Earth calculation and discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Core task sequence of the lesson: students first derive the terminal-velocity equation from F = m·a, then "berechnen die Endgeschwindigkeiten für eine Landung auf dem Mars bzw. auf der Erde", deriving Martian density from the ideal-gas equation for pure CO2. A closing discussion asks why only the last parachute matters, whether the rover would survive, and how sink speed can be reduced.
