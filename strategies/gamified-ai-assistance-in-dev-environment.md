# Integrate gamified AI assistance into the development environment to reduce the learning curve

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that artificial intelligence be embedded in the game-engine development environment to assist new users: "the use of AI needs to be gamified in a way that assists the user throughout the learning process", tied to a story-based narrative exposing skills needed for future multiplayer modifications. They sketch hosting such AI on an audited, secure network such as blockchain, modeling a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron network interfacing with modules like computer vision, speech recognition and translation, and experimenting with cost functions. The aim is to reduce the significant intimidating factor and learning curve of engine development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI integrated into the UX/UI and gamified with a story-based narrative; possibly a secure hosted network and interfacing modules (computer vision, speech recognition, translation).
#### Constraints
- This is a proposal only; the article reports no implementation or evaluation of the AI assistance.

### Target Learners
- novice developers
- students
- researchers new to game engines

### Target Learning Goals
- engine and programming skills
- rapid prototyping of educational and research tools
