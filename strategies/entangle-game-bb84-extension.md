# Play the BB84 quantum cryptography protocol using only part 1 of the game

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The game extends to quantum cryptography: one student plays Alice, another Bob, and the rest play quantum particles. Alice randomly sends the four states as body positions (legs left, legs right, arms front, arms back), Bob measures in a randomly chosen leg or arm basis, and bases are then announced publicly to build the key. An eavesdropper, Eve, can be added.
