# Exercise for Cognitive Enhancement

## Description
Regular physical exercise boosts cognitive function by increasing neurotransmitter levels, promoting neurogenesis in the hippocampus, and improving focus and attention. It has immediate effects on mood and concentration, and long-lasting protective effects against cognitive decline and neurodegenerative diseases. Exercise changes the brain's anatomy, physiology, and function, making it a powerful tool for cognitive enhancement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular commitment to physical activity, aiming for 3-4 times a week with minimum 30-minute sessions of aerobic exercise.
#### Constraints
- Requires sustained effort and commitment. Some individuals may need to adapt exercises to accommodate physical limitations or health conditions.
