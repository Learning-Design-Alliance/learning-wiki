# Accompany GAI-supported collaborative problem-solving with explicit scaffolds prompting students to examine, compare, and justify AI-generated evidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students in the study rarely questioned or critically evaluated AI-generated outputs, the authors recommend that GAI-supported CPS "be accompanied by explicit pedagogical scaffolds that prompt students to examine, compare, and justify AI-generated evidence rather than accept it uncritically." This strategy addresses the cognitive-reliance risk observed in the interaction logs while preserving GAI's benefits for participation and conflict resolution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a GAI tool during collaborative work, plus teacher-designed prompts that elicit examination and justification of AI outputs
#### Constraints
- Without such scaffolds, the article reports reduced opportunities for critical and reflective thinking as planning and reasoning tasks may be offloaded to the AI

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of AI-generated evidence and collaborative reasoning
