# Minimize platform-specific friction in workshop exercise materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The WAC team maintains a checklist of content-design guidelines developed through experience to keep a loosely-prerequisited audience focused on HPC content: "minimize makefiles, eliminate all possible platform-specific code, avoid language specific idioms, minimize libraries." Some are contrary to default practice but prove very effective. The guidelines continually change with experience, and the team intends to publish them as an open documented resource.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tight authoring timelines discipline and close attention to evaluations to discern where the pace has been too fast
#### Constraints
- Guidelines are continually changing as experience grows and the content base diversifies

### Target Learners
- Workshop students with very loose prerequisites in HPC topics

### Target Learning Goals
- Keeping learner focus on HPC content rather than build and configuration issues
