Apply mind mapping in mathematics teaching through overview, preview, inview, and review stages¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes four application stages for learning with mind mapping: "Overview is a comprehensive review of a topic durin g the learning process started", followed by "Preview, early reviews are a continuation of the ov erview", then "Inview, in-depth review sutau which is the core of the learning process", and finally "Review is a review carried out before the end of ja mpelajaran". Teachers can use these stages to structure topic study with mind maps, including a Master Mind Map summarizing a semester's topics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher delivers content and learning objectives before students construct mind maps
  • Students work in groups to create mind maps of studied material

Constraints¶

  • For very simple materials, the preview step can be skipped so it goes directly to the Inview step

Target Learners¶

  • junior high school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mastering mathematical concepts
  • mathematical reasoning

Claims¶

  • Improvement (N-gain) in mathematical reasoning ability is greater for students learning with mind mapping strategy than for students receiving conventional learning [+W]
  • Students learning with mind mapping strategy (SMM) achieve higher mathematical reasoning ability (KPM) posttest scores than students receiving conventional learning (PK) [+W]
  • Descriptively, SMM students scored higher on pretest, posttest, and N-gain of mathematical reasoning ability than PK students overall [+W]
  • There is an interaction between learning (SMM vs PK) and school rank in enhancing mathematical reasoning ability, with high-rank schools benefiting most from SMM [+W]
  • There is no interaction between learning (SMM vs PK) and early mathematical ability (KAM) in increasing mathematical reasoning ability [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Introduce concept mapping through direct training, attend to computer ability, and start with familiar topics
  • Train English teachers through workshops to design and apply electronic mind maps
