# Establish institutional supports — standardized MDT protocols, guidelines access, and regular wound care training — to translate knowledge into practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions establish standardized MDT protocols, ensure access to evidence-based guidelines, and support regular wound care training. It states "establishing standardized MDT protocols, ensuring access to evidence-based guidelines, and supporting regular wound care training initiatives would facilitate knowledge translation into clinical practice." It also recommends incorporating MDT competencies into performance appraisals and accreditation frameworks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to protocol development and regular training
#### Constraints
- Adoption is constrained by cost/availability, supply chain and regulatory issues for sterile larvae

### Target Learners
- hospital administrators and nurse educators
- registered nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent, evidence-based MDT wound care practice
