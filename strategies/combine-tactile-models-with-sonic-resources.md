# Combine tactile sky models with sonic resources (sonifications and audio descriptions) in inclusive astronomy activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends pairing tactile models with sound-based adaptations of astronomical data, such as "audification, sonification, or musification", and audio description. It states that "the simultaneous use of sounds in the activities focused on models ensures a much more efficiency in the teaching process when this material is used", because many objects in the model can also be represented by sonifications or audio descriptions, deepening the multisensorial aspect for BVI and sighted participants alike.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Available sound adaptations of the astronomical objects represented in the tactile model
