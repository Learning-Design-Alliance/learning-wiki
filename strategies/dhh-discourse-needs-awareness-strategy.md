# Raise instructors' and non-DHH students' awareness of DHH students' discourse needs and promote student motivation in lab settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To mitigate engagement barriers for DHH students, the article suggests raising "instructors’ and non-DHH students’ awareness of DHH students’ discourse needs in lab setting," since simultaneous group conversations make interpretation difficult. It further recommends that instructors "increase effort in promoting students’ motivation, such as showing support for students and expressing interest in students’ ideas and experiences," to build belonging and participation. The article also proposes evaluating group-level interventions such as modified partner agreement and reflection forms focused on discourse moves.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Modified partner agreement and reflection forms that address discourse moves rather than only equipment handling

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable small-group discourse participation and sense of belonging
