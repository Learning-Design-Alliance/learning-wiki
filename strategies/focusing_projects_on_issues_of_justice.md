# Focusing Projects on Issues of Justice

## Description
Focusing project-based learning (PBL) projects on issues of justice involves designing projects that allow students to work on topics that matter to their community, their lives, and the lives of those they care about. By addressing social justice in the classroom, educators can provide students with opportunities to become better people and act as change agents to make the world a better place.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects that are authentic and relevant to students' lives, and instructional strategies that support students in addressing complex social issues.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and sensitivity to ensure that projects are respectful and inclusive, and may necessitate additional resources or support.
