# Teach-Model-Coach-Review caregiver coaching with mastery-timed workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention used the Teach-Model-Coach-Review (TMCR) framework in every session: the therapist reviews two JASP+EMT+SGD strategies, models them with the child while verbally highlighting her use, coaches the caregiver's practice with feedback, and ends by reviewing and linking caregiver strategy use to the child's play and communication. Three didactic workshops (responsive interaction, language strategies, prompting) were timed to each caregiver's mastery of strategies, and caregiver practice time increased from 5 to 20 minutes across 36 sessions as therapist-delivered intervention decreased from 25 to 10 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops include a rationale, video examples, role-playing, discussion of adaptations, and question opportunities
- Strategy goal criteria are specified (e.g. matched turns at 80% correct, expansions at 40% of opportunities)
#### Constraints
- The article notes milieu prompting and matched turns declined at follow-up, suggesting booster training sessions may be required to maintain responsiveness

### Target Learners
- caregivers (mothers, fathers, grandmothers) of preverbal preschoolers with autism

### Target Learning Goals
- caregiver use of language facilitation strategies (matched turns, target-level language, expansions, time delays, milieu prompting)
