# Establish standard definitions and mission-aligned competencies for admissions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research establish a standard definition for both CBA and HA, and that desired competencies be established based on the program's mission and vision statements for what type of students they want to admit. It also recommends future research focus on long-term outcomes of implementing these practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must define mission and vision statements to anchor competency selection
#### Constraints
- Current literature lacks standardized definitions, limiting systematic study

### Target Learners
- Applicants to health professions education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Selecting well-prepared students aligned with program missions
