# Provide instruction clarifying the concepts of area and measuring areas and the area model of multiplication to children with incomplete understanding.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends providing instruction that clarifies the concepts of area and measuring areas and the area model of multiplication for children with incomplete understanding. It states that "the need for providing instruction clarifying the concepts of area and measuring areas and area model of multiplication to children who have this incomplete understanding." This targets children in the aF group who showed unstable ability to apply area formulas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must address both the distinction between area and measuring area, and the area model of multiplication.
#### Constraints
- Children with incomplete understanding needed instructional assistance for correcting mistakes, unlike children with good understanding who could self-correct.

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade students with partial understanding of area measurement

### Target Learning Goals
- stable application of area formulas
- justifying perimeter and area measures

## Claims

- [Children with a good understanding of the concept of area and the area formula exhibit competency in identifying geometric shapes, using formulas for determining areas, and self-correcting mistakes.](../claims/good-area-understanding-competency-claim.md) [+W]
- [Children with a good understanding of multiplication underlying the area formula, but who misunderstood the concept of area, showed some ability to use area formulas.](../claims/multiplication-understanding-partial-formula-ability-claim.md) [+W]
- [Children unable to interpret the property of multiplication underlying the area formula exhibited common weaknesses in identifying geometric shapes and differentiating between area and perimeter.](../claims/no-multiplication-interpretation-weaknesses-claim.md) [+W]
- [Approximately two-fifths of the children had difficulty differentiating the general concept of area from measuring area, despite good memorization of the area formula.](../claims/two-fifths-difficulty-differentiating-area-claim.md) [+W]
