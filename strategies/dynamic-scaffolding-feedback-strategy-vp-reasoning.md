# Add dynamic scaffolding and feedback to the concept mapping tool for learners struggling with summary statements and final diagnoses, and integrate VP courses into longitudinal clinical reasoning curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors plan further tool development "including more dynamic scaffolding and feedback elements to specifically support learners who have problems composing a summary statement and struggle to submit the correct final diagnosis". They also intend to expand the free VP courses and integrate them into healthcare curricula, especially longitudinal clinical reasoning courses using a mixed practice of topics and specialties, to fill the current gap where no structured clinical reasoning curriculum exists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the VP system and concept mapping tool; a curriculum context where clinical reasoning training can be embedded longitudinally
#### Constraints
- The article notes this may be challenging since often there is no structured clinical reasoning curriculum, and to date the VP courses have not been formally integrated into a curriculum

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students working with virtual patients

### Target Learning Goals
- composing summary statements with semantic qualifiers
- reaching correct final diagnoses
- clinical reasoning training
