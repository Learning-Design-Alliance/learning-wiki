# Use less active control groups in future SEL studies of grade retention to increase treatment contrast

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that "further work examining the effects of INSIGHTS—and other SEL studies—on grade retention may consider a less active control group in order to increase the treatment contrast and see how that change does or does not affect impacts on grade retention". The current study's attention-control reading program made the test conservative.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- randomized designs comparing SEL programs to less academically active comparison conditions
#### Constraints
- the active control was originally chosen to ensure effects were attributable to the SEL program rather than extra attention

### Target Learners
- children in early elementary school

### Target Learning Goals
- grade progression and behavioral development
