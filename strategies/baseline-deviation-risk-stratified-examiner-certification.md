# Use baseline deviation scores to identify high-variability examiners for targeted remediation and risk-stratified certification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Among the practical implications the authors draw for building a national, scalable examiner calibration system, "baseline deviation scores can be used to identify "high-variability" examiners, providing a basis for targeted remediation and supporting a risk-stratified approach to assessor certification." Because specialty background influenced both baseline deviation and dimension-specific improvement, calibration efforts can be targeted to the examiners and dimensions where deviation is greatest. The online format requires only internet access, lowering implementation barriers for remote settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-assessment that computes each examiner's scoring deviation against expert reference scores.
#### Constraints
- The authors recommend AI integration for personalized pathways as future work; the current study did not test it.

### Target Learners
- surgical examiners in national residency training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- standardized, accurate scoring across geographically dispersed examiner panels
