# Interrogate parent involvement constructs through a genealogical lens to advance equity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's approach implies that educators and researchers should critically examine taken-for-granted ideas about parent involvement and the home/school relation before designing involvement policies or practices. By examining "a century of rhetoric on the home/school relation and associated parent involvement," the review models a strategy of historical critique aimed at whether current ideas "might inhibit the realization of justice, equity, and excellence in education."
