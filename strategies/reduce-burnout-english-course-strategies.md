# Four-part strategy set for reducing learning burnout in English courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes four strategies to improve student outcomes and well-being: "making English courses more engaging to boost performance and confidence, reducing learning burnout"; encouraging and supporting students to enhance self-efficacy and motivation; assigning tasks seen as useful and interesting to lessen perceived workload and emotional exhaustion; and considering English anxiety in admissions. Each strategy maps onto a tested path in the SEM (engagement and self-efficacy against burnout, workload and anxiety as burnout predictors).

## Design Implications

### Context
