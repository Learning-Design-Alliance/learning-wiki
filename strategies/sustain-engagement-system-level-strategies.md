# Sustain engagement across the programme with system-level reforms, collaborative and experiential activities, and stimulating environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because engagement declined steeply after initially high levels, the article recommends strategies to sustain motivation throughout the programme. It cites "system-level reforms such as a transition to pass/fail grading, mindfulness training, and better communication between administration, faculty, and students", plus collaborative and experiential activities, creative spaces, online discussion forums and virtual learning environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to reforms such as pass/fail grading, mindfulness training and improved administration–faculty–student communication.
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of a medical Bachelor programme where engagement started high and declined; the authors note the decline may partly be a natural progression.

### Target Learners
- Medical Bachelor students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining academic engagement and motivation over a multi-year programme
