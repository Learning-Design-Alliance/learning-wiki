# Embed peer assessment throughout the program with enhanced feedback literacy training and process-focused criteria

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's limitations and conclusions, the authors recommend that "the assessment process should be applied to complex, challenging and productive tasks; more effort should be put into training by making examples and materials available to students to help them develop their feedback literacy; and assessment criteria should focus on both content and process." They further call for a curricular architecture enabling longitudinal development of participatory assessment processes beyond a single subject, coordinated with formal and informal feedback sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional decision-makers must provide the opportunities for participatory assessment to happen
#### Constraints
- The intervention was an isolated proposal in the whole syllabus with lack of specific training for students and even teachers

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- pharmacy faculty and curriculum designers

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluative judgement
- feedback literacy
- lifelong learning
