Federal policy should invest in high-quality professional learning for educators, prioritizing preparation and professional development designs including teachers teaching teachers¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief calls for increasing funding under ESSA and the Higher Education Act to establish, expand, study, and spread effective learning models for school leaders, teachers, and other staff. It prioritizes designs including models of teachers teaching teachers that build skills for teaching challenging content deeply, teaching diverse learners, supporting social-emotional and academic development in culturally and linguistically responsive ways, and using technology to enhance learning.
