# Adopt a 13-item checklist for RAMSing-bias-resistant meta-analyses with principled moderator selection and complete reporting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes a concrete roadmap for improving moderator analysis rigor: "we present a 13-item practical checklist for conducting RAMSing-bias–resistant meta-analyses while constraining researcher degrees of freedom and strengthening analytic accountability." The accompanying guidance includes comprehensive variable-level structural mapping before analysis, principled moderator selection rather than convention- or habit-driven probing, magnitude-based and uncertainty-sensitive interpretation against field-specific benchmarks, a priori power considerations, and complete reporting of all tested moderators including null results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comprehensive variable-level coding of the literature as a first step
- Bias-adjusted benchmarking and prospective power analysis to set interpretation and design expectations
#### Constraints
- The checklist was developed in STEM meta-research; the authors present it as a worked example intended for adaptation across domains rather than a field-specific exception

### Target Learners
- Meta-analysts and review authors
- journal editors and peer reviewers evaluating moderator evidence

### Target Learning Goals
- Transparent, reproducible moderator analysis
- Reducing selective reporting of null moderator findings
