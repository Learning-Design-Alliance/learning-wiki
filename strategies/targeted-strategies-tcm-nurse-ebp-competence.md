# Strengthen EBP competence through hands-on practicums, practice-ready evidence resources, clearer role expectations, and supportive organizational conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend targeted strategies addressing capability, opportunity, and motivation barriers. These include incorporating EBP practicums into hospital training, translating heterogeneous evidence into locally agreed, practice-ready resources that incorporate syndrome differentiation, clarifying specialist nurse role descriptions, and providing protected time and equitable resource access. Participants themselves stated a preference for courses "where we can practice, receive feedback, and solve real problems."
