# Implement workplace and curricular EBP training programs targeting question formulation, literature search, retrieval and critical appraisal

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospital management, training institutions and the nursing governing body in Ghana implement EBP training programs both at workplaces and within training curricula. It specifies that "These training programs must target the nurse's ability to formulate the research question, literature search, retrieval, and critical analysis of scientific literature," directly addressing the skill gaps the survey identified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must target formulating research questions, literature search and retrieval, and critical analysis of scientific literature
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from self-reported data in a survey limited to 3 teaching hospitals, 5 universities and 8 nursing training institutions, so generalisation should be done with caution

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses in Ghana
- nurse educators in Ghana
- nursing students in training curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- formulating answerable research questions
- literature search and retrieval
- critical appraisal of scientific literature
- integrating evidence into nursing practice
