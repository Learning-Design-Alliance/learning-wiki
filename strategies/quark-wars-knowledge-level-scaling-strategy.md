# Run the outreach game at multiple knowledge levels, starting at the layperson level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Quark Wars scales across knowledge levels. On a beginner level players read out only quark or lepton cards (e.g. red u, green u, blue d); on an intermediate level they learn that the Omega baryon is a red s, green s, blue s combination and that the Higgs boson is identified through leptonic decays; on an advanced level they must name hadrons such as proton (uud) and neutron (udd). The authors recommend starting "on a layperson's level, with as many ordinary students as possible" because students prefer to play and have fun first.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An intermediate or advanced level requires a supervisor or referee with basic knowledge of hadrons who can judge whether an identification was correct
- The hadron tables in Appendix A of the Quark Matter Card Game reference for advanced play
#### Constraints
- Advanced-level combinations like red u, green u, blue d are not sufficient or acceptable for identifying particles

### Target Learners
- laypersons and beginners
- secondary and middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- particle terminology
- hadron quark content and Higgs boson decay modes
