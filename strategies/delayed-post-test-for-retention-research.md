# Extend study duration and add a delayed post-test to measure vocabulary retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In its suggestions for further research, the article recommends longer studies and delayed measurement of retention. It states that "the duration of the study could be extended to administer a delayed post-test to gain more insights into learners' vocabulary retention." It also recommends widening the scope beyond tenth-form students in one secondary school and adding instrumentation such as classroom observation field notes, learner journals and videotaping.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies with extended duration to allow delayed post-testing
#### Constraints
- The original study's duration was rather short to allow learners to improve vocabulary without assistance

### Target Learners
- Grade-10 EFL students in Vietnamese secondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term English vocabulary retention
