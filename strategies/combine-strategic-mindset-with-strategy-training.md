# Combine strategic mindset intervention with explicit strategy training for less-prepared learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend pairing strategic mindset interventions with explicit instruction in specific learning strategies, since the mindset prompts strategy search but does not supply strategies. They suggest that "equipping students with a combination of a strategic mindset and explicit training in how and when to use speci c, effective strategies may strengthen the impact and durability of our intervention." This is aimed especially at students who lack a basic repertoire with which to answer strategy-eliciting questions.
