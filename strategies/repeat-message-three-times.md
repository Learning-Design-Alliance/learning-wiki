# Repeat the key message three times: spoiler in the Introduction, message claim in the Results, moral in Conclusions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To make the message sink in and avoid misunderstanding, the author recommends stating the key result explicitly at three points: "(i) a spoiler in the Introduction, (ii) a message claim in the Results and (iii) the moral in Conclusions". He grounds this in the claim that for a message to sink into a human brain it has to be repeated three times. This is an implementable recipe for the Discussion and Conclusions section.
