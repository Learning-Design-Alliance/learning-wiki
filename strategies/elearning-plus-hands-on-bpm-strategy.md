# Supplement e-learning modules with in-person practice and formative OSCEs with feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining e-learning with hands-on training rather than treating e-learning as standalone. Citing Hayer, it notes "an eLearning module may not be a standalone solution" and that "Certain skills will still require additional in‐person training with peers and support from healthcare educators." It further recommends "Further incorporation of formative OSCEs ... to continuously identify learning gaps" and simulation-based training with standardized BP arms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an interactive e-learning module (AMA BPM Essentials), supervised practice settings, and standardized patients or simulation arms for OSCEs
#### Constraints
- Authors note certain skills still require additional in-person training with peers and educator support

### Target Learners
- health professional trainees (medical, PA, nursing students)

### Target Learning Goals
- blood pressure measurement technique, accuracy, and patient communication
