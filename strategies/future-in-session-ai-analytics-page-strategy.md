# Extend AI-driven interface design to in-session analytics pages and iterate on further AI-expressive designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is to continue designing interfaces that make use of AI features beyond the diagnostic page. They state: "Avenues of future work include 1) updating a page summarizing the AI's analysis on the user in the learning session after the diagnostic test. 2) finding more interface designs that can further enhance student engagement by making good use of, expressing and utilizing AI features." This extends the diagnostic-page approach to ongoing learning sessions and continued design iteration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI models capable of summarizing user analysis during the learning session, not only at diagnosis
#### Constraints
- Proposed as future work; not evaluated in the reported experiments

### Target Learners
- Active users of mobile intelligent tutoring systems such as Santa

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement during learning sessions after the diagnostic test
