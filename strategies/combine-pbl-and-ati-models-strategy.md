# Combine the PBL and ATI models with different approaches for optimal results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Recommendation section the authors suggest that "the use of learning models can collaborate an approach PBL model for optimal results" and that further research follow "by combining PBL models and ATI models with different approaches." The strategy treats the two models' stages — PBL's problem analysis and open-ended discussion, and ATI's initial treatment, grouping, and intensive treatment — as complementary rather than competing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Further research to test combinations with different approaches
