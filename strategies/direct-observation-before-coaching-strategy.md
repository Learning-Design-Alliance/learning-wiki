# Add direct observation of the learner's simulated encounter before the coaching session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future iterations of the coaching intervention incorporate direct observation of the learner's recorded encounter prior to coaching. They note a large discrepancy between self-reported and observed skills, and argue that "adding direct observation prior to the coaching intervention may enhance the quality of the coaching intervention and avoid biasing the goal-setting period of the VCI." This is a forward-looking refinement, not a tested manipulation in this pilot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to video-recorded simulated encounters and a coach willing to review them before the session
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that a pre-coaching review may change the coach's approach to the session flow; untested in this study

### Target Learners
- health professions students receiving telemedicine coaching

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate coach calibration and goal-setting in telemedicine clinical skills coaching
