# Incorporate instant score feedback and richer communication interactivity into VR simulations to motivate repeated, self-directed use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that VR simulations aiming at self-regulated learning add simple instant feedback — showing scores or whether a user exceeded a set standard — because participants requested detailed explanations of why their answers were right or wrong. They caution that detailed feedback could reduce repeat rounds and that too much text could tire users, so "providing simple feedback, such as individual scores, could efficiently motivate students' self-directed learning." They also suggest recording students' communication with virtual patients so learners can identify flaws in their therapeutic communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A scoring or feedback mechanism built into the VR program; the current version could not incorporate one.
#### Constraints
- Detailed feedback may make students less likely to conduct another round, and too much text could cause tiredness in users.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students using VR simulation for mental health training

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-directed and repeated practice
- Therapeutic communication skill improvement
