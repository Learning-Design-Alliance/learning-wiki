Strategies

Design future music-and-surgical-skill studies with larger samples, participant-preferred music and a limited task range¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's forward-looking recommendation for research on music and surgical skill: "Future studies should be conducted using greater numbers of participants, participant preferred music, and focusing on a more limited range of tasks." It further recommends assessing effects on surgical team performance and patient outcome, and validating in the live operating environment, since all included studies were simulation-only.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Studies must compare music against another auditory condition or silence on surgical performance, per the review's inclusion criteria

Constraints¶

  • Current evidence base is limited to simulated settings; effects in the live operating environment remain unclear

Target Learners¶

  • surgical residents
  • surgeons in simulation-based training

Target Learning Goals¶

  • surgical psychomotor skill performance
  • efficiency of surgical task completion

Claims¶

  • Music exposure shows mixed but partly beneficial effects on time to task completion in simulated surgical tasks [~W]
  • Evidence is insufficient to definitively conclude music benefits simulated surgical performance, and no meta-analysis was possible [+W]
  • Music shows mixed effects on general surgical task performance scores in simulation [~W]
  • Classical music exposure during task repetition statistically significantly improves path length compared to other control conditions in one included study [+W]
  • Music reduces electromyographic activation of the extensor digitorum during robotic surgical simulation, an indicator of muscle fatigue [+W]
  • Participant-preferred music shows more consistent beneficial effects on simulated surgical performance than researcher-selected music [+W]
