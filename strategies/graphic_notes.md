# Graphic Notes

## Description
Use visual cues in note-taking to help learners organize the information you are sharing with them orally. This allows them to keep track of the lesson as you present, and the notes can be revisited for studying.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Visual aids, such as graphics, diagrams, and gestures
#### Constraints
- May not be accessible to all learners due to visual impairments. Note-taking can be a challenging skill for some.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance comprehension, improve retention, support learners who face auditory processing challenges

### Affordances
- Provides reinforcement of concepts through an additional sense, graphic notes are permanent so allow the students to revisit the information whenever necessary, students can take notes that best support their learning needs.

### Personalization
- Allow different types of graphic organizers. Some learners might prefer a mind-map while others need a timeline.
