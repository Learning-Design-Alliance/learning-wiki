# During initial evaluation, investigate legal compliance, equity, accessibility, and context-specific barriers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the initial evaluation stage, the framework directs decision makers to check compliance with FERPA, PPRA, CIPA, HIPAA, and COPPA; to "ensure emerging technologies are equitable for all students by cultivating a culturally responsive learning environment with specific considerations for student neurodiversity and accessibility"; and to investigate barriers for students and teachers in their context plus supports or modifications to mitigate them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions should be asked of procurement decision makers, district leaders, researchers/developers, educators, and families, caregivers, and students

### Target Learners
- PK-12 students, including students with IEPs or 504 plans and Emergent Multilingual students

### Target Learning Goals
- selecting technologies that are equitable, accessible, and appropriate for the local context
