When applying empathic design, consider delivery mode (synchronous and asynchronous), four dimensions of learner engagement, and three features of learning context¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Respondent Xie extends empathic design with considerations for digital delivery. The editorial reports that Xie "highlights both synchronous and asynchronous delivery modes while also introducing considerations for applying empathic design to learner engagement (i.e. behavioral, cognitive, affective, and social) and learning context (i.e. physical environment, technological, and social features)". Designers should therefore analyze engagement and context dimensions when empathically designing for digital learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Designers must consider both synchronous and asynchronous delivery modes when applying the framework

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • learners in synchronous and asynchronous digital courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • behavioral, cognitive, affective, and social learner engagement

Claims¶

  • Several students voluntarily used Zoom's recording and transcription features asynchronously after the live debate to support cognitive engagement [+W]
  • Most remote courses used both synchronous and asynchronous delivery mechanisms [+W]
  • The time students had to prepare responses before interacting was connected with all three dimensions of online learner engagement across asynchronous, synchronous, and quasi-synchronous debates [+W]
  • Time zone differences affected international students' timely participation in asynchronous forums, and a balance of synchronous and asynchronous communication was regarded as beneficial [+W]
  • Logistical flexibility of the asynchronous and quasi-synchronous modalities supported behavioral engagement, while the synchronous modality's scheduling demands disrupted participation [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Apply the empathic design framework in teacher training to increase design efficiency and teacher self-efficacy, especially in K-12
