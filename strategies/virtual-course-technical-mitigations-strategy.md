# Mitigations for delivering virtual surgical skills courses: facilitators, re-playable demonstrations, and connectivity safeguards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
After a pilot course identified challenges impeding communication and learning, the final course added specific mitigations: in-room facilitators for timekeeping and assistance, a pausable and re-playable video demonstration, Bluetooth headphones with microphones instead of speakers alone, power chargers for low laptop battery, and wired ethernet cables for internet drops. These low-cost adjustments made the virtual format deliverable at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators present in the teaching room
- A video demonstration that can be paused and re-played by trainers
- Bluetooth headphones with microphones
#### Constraints
- Mitigations were developed specifically to address problems identified during the pilot virtual course (Fig. 1)

### Target Learners
- Novice surgical trainees attending virtual skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Remote acquisition of minimally invasive surgery skills
