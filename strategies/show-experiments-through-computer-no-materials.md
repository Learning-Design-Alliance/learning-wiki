# Show science experiments through the computer when experiment materials cannot be supplied

## Description
A concrete fallback activity for preschool science teaching: when materials for hands-on experiments are unavailable, the teacher demonstrates the experiments on the computer instead of skipping them. The article states "the teachers can show the experiments through the computers without giving up doing experiments when they cannot supply materials", so that teaching and learning are still accomplished.
