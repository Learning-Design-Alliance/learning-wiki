Replace passive lectures with active learning models such as ZeroSicks or Attitude to Work.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 review), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends replacing passive lectures with participatory models like "ZeroSicks" or "Attitude to Work" that have "statistically proven efficacy in improving safety motivation and locus of control." Regular evaluation of the learning outcomes of these models is also deemed essential.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Replace passive lectures with participatory models like ZeroSicks or Attitude to Work

Constraints¶

  • Evaluating the learning outcomes of these models regularly is essential

Target Learning Goals¶

  • internal locus of control

Claims¶

  • Active learning methods significantly improve safety motivation and internal locus of control compared to traditional passive instruction. [+W]
  • Active methodologies show no definitive superiority over traditional teaching in undergraduate dentistry [-W]
