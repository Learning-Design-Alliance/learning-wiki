# Iteratively add complex clinical content and refine difficulty level before a full trial

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "The next iteration of the e-learning intervention will focus on the addition of complex clinical content, possibly multiple activities and testing focused on the appropriateness of the clinical content." Intervention development, testing and evaluation should be "an iterative process" based on "a user-centred approach". Suggestions also included adding learning outcomes, self-reflection questions, subtitles, diverse characters and scenarios with different challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- User-centred iterative development, testing and evaluation
#### Constraints
- Content level was judged too low for experienced prescribers; difficulty of clinical content requires further refinement

### Target Learners
- nurse and pharmacist independent prescribers, and suggested additional groups such as GPs, GP trainees, new prescribers and undergraduate healthcare students

### Target Learning Goals
- appropriate antibiotic prescribing for self-limiting respiratory tract infections
