# Integrate repeated, scheduled video viewing into preclinical curricula and add supervised hands-on practice for interdental brush sizing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that repeated mandatory video viewing be integrated before and during preclinical courses, that students voluntarily re-watch videos in post-practical sessions to reinforce learning and prepare for patient treatment, and that IDB-size training be expanded through "hands-on clinical teaching opportunities in which students work directly with patients under supervision". They also suggest tactile simulation models replicating varying interdental anatomies as a preparatory tool.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Viewing schedule must be verifiable (the study controlled access because private viewing could not be verified)
#### Constraints
- Timing and frequency of viewing may influence outcomes; irregular or infrequent viewing with extended gaps may reduce retention

### Target Learners
- preclinical and advanced undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of practical technique skills
- accurate interdental brush size selection
- confidence before patient treatment
