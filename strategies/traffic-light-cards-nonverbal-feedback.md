# Use green-yellow-red cards for quick non-verbal comprehension feedback during lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each participant keeps a green, yellow, and red card in front of them and raises the matching card to signal their current state of understanding. The authors report the method "proved extremely successful" and "enabled the participants to easily show their current state of understanding without having to verbalize it", and they plan to repeat it in a subsequent course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each learner needs a set of three colored cards at their seat during instruction.
