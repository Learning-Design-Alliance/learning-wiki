# Support basic psychological needs and mindfulness through family education sessions and school conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that families and schools create conditions that enhance independence, increase interactions with learners, and instill a sense of competence, and that schools run family education sessions addressing these factors. As the article puts it, "family education sessions held within schools are suggested to address this matter and elucidate the underlying factors contributing to successful academic identity". It also recommends psychologist and school counselor interventions to increase mindfulness, and that educators teach students how to learn, act and make choices alongside academic content.
