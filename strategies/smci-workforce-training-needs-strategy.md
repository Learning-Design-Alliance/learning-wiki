# Use the SMCI to identify workforce social media competency gaps and inform training development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends applying the SMCI to assess the readiness of health education specialists to use social media for health promotion. It states the SMCI 'can be applied to identify gaps in confidence and experience, as well as professional development needs within health education organizations,' with the resulting data used to inform the development of specific guidelines, training, and policies. This positions the instrument as a needs-assessment tool preceding training design rather than a high-stakes evaluation instrument.
