# Teacher professional development and school-leader support for enacting game-based learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a professional development pathway for teachers adopting game-based learning: pre-intervention training covering theoretical and pedagogical underpinnings, teachers playing the game themselves, and continuing in-class guidance with structured post-lesson reflection interviews. The article states: "Our research with the Statecraft X curriculum suggests that, on average, teachers need two rounds on intervention experience to effectively learn the ropes of the new practice." Teachers must also master dialogic facilitation and learn to let go of control to encourage productive student talk.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full support of the school principal and humanities head of department
- Time and space for teachers to reflect, such as reduced teaching contact time
#### Constraints
- The training program afforded teachers no opportunity to teach the curriculum with real students before the intervention

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers and school leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- enacting performance-oriented game-based learning pedagogy
- dialogic facilitation skills
