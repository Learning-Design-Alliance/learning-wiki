# Systemic, human-centred approach treating digital data use as a school-and-teacher system, with AI integrated into professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Following research on human-centred learning analytics, the authors suggest "the use of digital data in schools should be understood as a systemic issue that involves both school and teacher characteristics", calling for a broader framework considering school context, leadership support, and teacher collaboration alongside technical skills and beliefs. They further propose integrating AI-powered tools such as personalized learning platforms into professional development to help teachers analyze and interpret data more efficiently, while ensuring adequate training and careful consideration of ethical implications such as data privacy and bias.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Involvement of various stakeholders including school leaders, teachers, and local policy makers in decision-making
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that AI integration should ensure teachers are adequately trained and that ethical implications such as data privacy and bias are carefully considered

### Target Learners
- School teachers and school leaders in K–12 settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher data literacy and effective, ethically responsible use of digital data in instruction
