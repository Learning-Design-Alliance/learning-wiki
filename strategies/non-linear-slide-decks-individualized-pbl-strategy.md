# Use non-linear slide decks to deliver individualized PBL assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Replace group-based PBL with individualized non-linear slide deck assessments that retain PBL goals while enabling individual grading. Students navigate branching scenarios via hyperlinks, making decisions and seeing consequences. The article states this 'offers an engaging, flexible and pedagogically robust strategy that promotes active learning' and addresses challenges of group-based PBL formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PowerPoint hyperlink functionality
- VLE embedding capability
- Educator digital competencies to embed slide decks
#### Constraints
- More complex to develop and administer than conventional assessments
- Risk of students downloading and uploading to AI tools; mitigated by 'can view but not download' settings

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate pharmacology students

### Target Learning Goals
- Individual assessment of problem-solving skills
- Application of pharmacology knowledge to clinical scenarios
