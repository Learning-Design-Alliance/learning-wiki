# Use recasting during jigsaw retelling tasks to deliver incidental focus on form

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's treatment involved organizing learners into jigsaw task teams where each group retold part of a reading passage. The instructor provided recast feedback only on errors related to articles, tenses, pronouns, and prepositions, leaving communication unobstructed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must be organized into small teams with a jigsaw task structure, and the instructor must selectively correct only targeted grammatical categories.
#### Constraints
- The strategy was tested with female learners aged 16-18 in an Iranian language institute over eight sessions.

### Target Learners
- EFL learners aged 16-18

### Target Learning Goals
- grammatical accuracy

## Claims

- [Incidental focus on form has a greater effect on the accuracy of articles than on pronouns and tenses](../claims/incidental-fof-greatest-effect-on-articles.md) [+W]
