# Use a combined PBL-LBL dual-track teaching model in surgical education under resource constraints

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a dual-track model in which LBL handles theory and basic surgical skills while PBL handles surgical operation and case analysis. They state: "we believe that the PBL and LBL dual track teaching model can be used for surgical education under conditions of limited medical colleges and teachers". This assigns each method to the outcomes it performed best on in their meta-analysis. They also cite evidence that combining PBL with CBL improved performance in thyroid surgery teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires both LBL sessions for theory and basic skills and PBL sessions for operations and case analysis
- Requires sufficient teachers and course structure to run both tracks
#### Constraints
- Recommended specifically under conditions of limited medical colleges and teachers
- The authors note the feasibility and necessity of popularizing joint teaching models deserve further exploration

### Target Learners
- medical students in surgical education
- surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical theoretical knowledge
- basic surgical skills
- clinical competence
- case analysis ability
