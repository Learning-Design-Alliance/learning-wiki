# Apply the instrument from the beginning of SCI as continuous diagnostic assessment rather than only for final grading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that IAFEE-ECS® "not be used only at the end of the assessment process, with the sole purpose of assigning a grade to students", but instead "accompany the assessment process from the beginning of SCI, in order to serve as a tool for the continuous diagnostic assessment of learning". This enables identification of problems and difficulties and monitoring of the training process through feedback, allowing interventions to improve learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use by professors, supervising nurses and students throughout the internship period
#### Constraints
- Assessment often occurs only at the end of a study cycle, leaving short periods to assist students effectively

### Target Learners
- Nursing students in supervised curricular internship

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuous diagnostic assessment of learning
- Timely intervention to improve learning
