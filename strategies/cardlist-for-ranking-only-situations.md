# Deploy the card list form in rating situations where order-of-merit ranking is the only applicable method

## Description
The article identifies application settings for the form: "There are still a great many situations in which the order of merit method is the only one applicable," notably "experiments in mutual rating for personal traits by employees, supervisors, foremen, inspectors, teachers, etc." In the absence of objective measures of many vocational and moral traits, the form supports the best possible ranking practice in those settings.
