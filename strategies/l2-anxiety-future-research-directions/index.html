Description¶

The authors close by pointing the field toward further work: "We discuss the findings in the context of theoretical and practical concerns, and we provide direction for future research." Researchers in L2 anxiety should follow the directions the article specifies in its discussion of theoretical and practical concerns.

Target Learners¶

Target Learning Goals¶

Claims¶

  • L2 anxiety is negatively correlated with L2 achievement, with a mean correlation of r = −.36 across 216 effect sizes [+M]
  • The anxiety–achievement correlation varies across educational levels, target languages, and anxiety types [+W]
  • The anxiety–achievement correlation varies across types of language achievement measures [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Pursue SCT-L2 research on L2 as a psychological tool, the teaching-learning dialectic, Dynamic Assessment, and comparative theory work
  • Future AI-WTC research should use longitudinal designs, triangulated WTC measures, diverse samples, comparative tool research, pedagogically guided AI integration, and theory-driven models
  • Direct future RALL research toward the directions the review identifies for social robots in language education
