# Predicting

## Description
Predicting involves anticipating what will happen next in a text or problem. This engages learners' interest and helps them assess what they already know about a given topic. In math, it involves asking what type of question is being asked, what type of operations might be used, and what the answer might look like.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clear understanding of the topic and the ability to formulate relevant questions.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to have some prior knowledge of the topic.
