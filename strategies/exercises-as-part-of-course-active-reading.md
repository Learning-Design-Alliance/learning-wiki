# Treat embedded exercises as an integral part of a lecture course to stimulate active reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In these lecture notes the author instructs that the numerous exercises distributed through the text should not be treated as optional add-ons: "The numerous exercises must be considered part of the course itself; they are intended to stimulate active reading." The strategy is to embed exercises directly in the reading material so that students work through derivations while studying, rather than passively reading. It was used throughout a master-level gravitation course at AIMS-Cameroon.

## Design Implications
