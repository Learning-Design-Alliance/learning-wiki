# Integrate holistic health theory, health-risk knowledge, therapeutic skills and equal-health attitudes into master's level mental health nursing curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper recommends that master's level mental health nursing curricula be developed so that "Master's level mental health nursing education curricula need to integrate knowledge of nursing theories with knowledge of specific health risks among mental health service users". Curricula should prepare students in therapeutic and motivating skills and in teamwork with other professional caregivers, and equip students with an attitude of accountability regarding equal health and awareness of the impact of mental health stigma at different societal levels. The recommendation responds to identified gaps between holistic nursing theory and current praxis.
