# Invest state discretionary dollars in early intervention systems such as MTSS to reduce later intensive supports

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
State leaders recommended using state discretionary dollars to fund initiatives strengthening connections between general and special education, such as multi-tiered system of supports (MTSS), in which LEAs develop intervention systems supporting all struggling students, not just students with disabilities. The report states that "the preventative effects of intervention systems can yield a significant return on investment by improving student outcomes and reducing more intensive (and expensive) supports a student might otherwise need later on."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State discretionary dollars available for investment in early intervention and prevention-oriented support systems
#### Constraints
- The report also advises states to ensure MFS calculations include only funds made available for special education and related services, so schoolwide interventions are not counted toward MFS

### Target Learners
- All struggling students, including students with disabilities and students without disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving student outcomes and reducing need for later intensive and expensive supports
