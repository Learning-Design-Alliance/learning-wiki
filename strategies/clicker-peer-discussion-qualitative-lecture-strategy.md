# Use clicker questions with student-student discussion during lecture to build qualitative understanding of basic concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During lecture the instructor poses multiple-choice and True-False questions every 10-20 minutes of lecturing; students answer with personal-response devices, sometimes polling, discussing, and re-polling following Mazur's Peer Instruction prescription, sometimes discussing before polling. The article states these questions "test the students understanding of the basic concepts and/or help the students to build this basic understanding." Qualitative homework questions requiring considerable thought were also assigned during the qualitative weeks.
