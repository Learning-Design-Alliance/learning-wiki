# Schedule deliberate practice with repetitive reinforcement sessions to sustain NTS knowledge and behaviors over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that training programs include reinforcement schedules because short interventions without follow-up may not produce lasting results. The authors state that "Short training programs without a schedule of reinforcement may not produce the desired results, considering that knowledge and behaviors decline over time" and that "Deliberate practice with repetitive sessions are fundamental to produce better learning outcomes on long-term level."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schedule reinforcement sessions beyond the initial short training intervention
#### Constraints
- Evidence based on cited literature rather than directly tested within the included studies

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of non-technical skills knowledge and behaviors over time
