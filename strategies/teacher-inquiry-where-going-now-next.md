# Establish site-based professional learning structured around the three inquiry questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that leaders establish professional learning in which teachers engage in "ongoing and iterative learning driven by three questions: Where am I going? Where am I now? What should I learn next?" Teachers clarify goals, collect and interpret evidence of how students use formative assessment practices, and plan next steps. Suggested structures include "in-person or video-based peer observations using formative assessment protocols," review of model lessons, video study, and integration into existing PLCs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders must schedule ongoing structured opportunities and use current evidence of teacher and student learning to select next steps
#### Constraints
- Teachers need new resources and tools to notice and use evidence, just as students do

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers developing formative assessment expertise

### Target Learning Goals
- integrating daily formative assessment routines grounded in evidence of student learning
