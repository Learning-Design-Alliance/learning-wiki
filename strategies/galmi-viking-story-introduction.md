# Open the lesson with the story of Galmi the Viking to generate interest and contextualise navigation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A narrative engagement strategy: students read the story of Galmi, a Norse farmer who joins a raiding voyage, before the hands-on work. The article states that "A short story is included that tells the students something about what the life of a Viking may have looked like and generates interest to study this important and inﬂuential era of European history." In the story, the navigator Floki demonstrates the shadow board at noon, and a set of questions with answers checks comprehension of the plot and of the navigation method.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The story is provided as a separate document and is read by the students before the hands-on activity.
- Comprehension questions about the story accompany the reading.
