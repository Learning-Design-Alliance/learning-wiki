# Design warm-up problems with a capped attempt policy and office-hour overlap to keep students engaged without penalty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors assigned 4 to 6 LON-CAPA questions per warm-up, ranging from simple single-concept computational questions to conceptual and meshed questions. Students got at least 5 tries with no penalty for wrong answers, unlimited tries if they came to see their teacher, and at least 48 hours to complete the warm-up provided those hours overlapped the instructors' office hours. This made the structure easier for teachers to use and harder for students to skip.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online question-delivery system that logs attempts
- Office hours scheduled to overlap the 48-hour completion window
#### Constraints
- The authors capped attempts at 5 after previously observing students trying a problem 30 or 40 times before giving up

### Target Learners
- college physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- pre-class problem solving
- gathering enough information to solve warm-up problems whether or not they read the textbook

### Affordances
- Flip Jitt Six Step Warmup Pattern
