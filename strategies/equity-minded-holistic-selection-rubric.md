# Equity-minded holistic selection rubric with minimal eligibility requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Intern selection used an equity-minded rubric built on holistic components including non-cognitive competencies such as teamwork ability, commitment to long-term goals, and perseverance in the face of adversity. Eligibility requirements were designed to be minimal so a broad range of applicants felt qualified to apply, requiring only undergraduate enrollment, short responses on research interests, a transcript, and one recommendation letter. Special consideration was given to students at HBCUs, HSIs, MSIs, community colleges, and institutions that do not traditionally participate in HEP research.
