# Seven-step music lesson planning model incorporating a 10-minute DI component

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researchers present a seven-step set of lesson planning principles for class music lessons in lower secondary school, based upon a generic teaching model created by Hattie (2009). The model includes a lesson 'hook', clear learning intentions, the 10-minute scripted DI program, review and clarification, contextualisation activities, and student reflection. The 10-minute DI allotment at the start of each lesson is described as 'central to the effectiveness of the lesson.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning intentions written up on the board for students
- A 60-minute lesson as the basis for suggested timings
#### Constraints
- Suggested timings would vary between schools depending upon the length of the lesson in each school

### Target Learners
- Year 7 lower secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Music literacy skills developed through Direct Instruction and applied through student-centred activities
