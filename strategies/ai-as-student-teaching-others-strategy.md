# Use AI as a student that students teach, leveraging learning-by-teaching to check understanding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the AI-as-student approach, the AI takes the learner role and the student explains material to it, exploiting the finding that teaching others is a powerful learning technique and serving to check for understanding. The paper warns that AI "confabulation and argumentation may derail the benefits of teaching," so students must correct the AI's errors rather than accept them. Teachers provide guidelines so students push the AI to ask questions and request explanations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students know the material well enough to detect the AI student's mistakes
- Clear student guidelines distinguishing correct from confabulated explanations
#### Constraints
- The paper identifies this use case as having the lowest confabulation concern, but argumentation by the AI can still derail the benefits

### Target Learners
- students checking their own understanding after instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- explanation and elaboration
- self-assessment of understanding
