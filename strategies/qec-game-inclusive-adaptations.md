# Adapt the game for inclusivity and accessibility across mobility, sensory sensitivity, and perception differences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article provides non-exhaustive guidelines for accommodating a wide range of players, emphasising that "each player being unique, modiﬁcations must be tailored to their speciﬁc need s." Mobility can be accommodated by role choice or time bonuses and penalties; children sensitive to loud noises can speak words instead of shouting; abrupt movements can be averted by restricting players to walking; and sensory perception differences can be met with more visual communication, verbalisation, different marble textures, or accessible colour choices.
