# Consult practitioner community archives and plan timeline, staffing and pilot classrooms before building a DIY planetarium

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before buying equipment, the guide urges replicators to join the full_dome and small_planetarium Yahoo groups, "diving into their archives"; the team made heavy use of the email archive and asked the group specific questions to shortlist dome vendors and resolve dome size, entrance, light-tightness and mirror-cost concerns. It also advises planning nine months of part-time work, a 1-credit undergraduate training seminar, and pilot classroom meetings.
