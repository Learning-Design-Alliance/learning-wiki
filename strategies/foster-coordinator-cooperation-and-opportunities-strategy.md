# Foster inter-professional cooperation and expand activity opportunities to support coordinator role development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends building an environment that facilitates cooperation between coordinator groups and relationship-building, since each group's activities reflect its members' original occupations. It proposes workshops in which various occupations share information and engage in mutual consultation, expanding opportunities for Group B, and preparing reference materials or tools for coordinators. As the authors state, "there is a need to foster an environment that facilitates cooperation between coordinators, as well as relationship-building."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordinators from different professional groups must be able to meet and share information, e.g., through workshops and meetings.
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a questionnaire survey of coordinators trained by one prefectural government; the authors call for updated surveys as hepatitis treatment shifts toward oral medications.

### Target Learners
- health-care professionals certified as hepatitis medical care coordinators

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing awareness of hepatitis among health-care professionals and guiding patients toward suitable medical care
