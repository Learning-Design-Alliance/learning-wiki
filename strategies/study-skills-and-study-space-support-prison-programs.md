# Add explicit study-skills instruction and dedicated quiet study spaces to prison college programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants in the pilot study entered the prison college program without study skills and struggled to find suitable places to study in the prison environment. The article recommends that "Prison education programs should work to consciously create spaces that foster learning and effective studying" and be aware of context. This strategy addresses an identified gap rather than a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must acknowledge the unique challenges of learning in a prison setting, including noise, shared housing, and failed prior arrangements such as study halls
#### Constraints
- Prior attempts such as study halls did not work for various reasons, per one participant's account; the strategy is recommended, not evaluated, in this study

### Target Learners
- Incarcerated college students in women's prison facilities entering postsecondary coursework without prior study skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic study skills, course persistence, and effective learning within a secure facility
