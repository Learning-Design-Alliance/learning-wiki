# Iteratively refine hybrid genomics courses using student feedback: add synchronous video sessions, moderate discussion boards, and add a genomics policy advocacy assignment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on evaluation findings, the authors recommend specific future modifications to the hybrid courses. They propose "synchronous video sessions and greater moderation of discussion boards by the instructor and/or teaching assistant in order to increase student engagement within an online learning forum", responding to the recurrent student critique. They also suggest creating an assignment to draft a letter to an elected representative advocating for or against genomic policy legislation, targeting the low-agreement health-policy competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor and/or teaching assistant capacity to moderate discussion boards; course structure permitting synchronous sessions and policy assignments
#### Constraints
- Recommended in response to a single-year offering with 10 students; generalizability limited per the article's stated limitations

### Target Learners
- Advanced Practice Nursing students in graduate genomics coursework

### Target Learning Goals
- Genomic competency attainment including health-policy engagement and online engagement skills
