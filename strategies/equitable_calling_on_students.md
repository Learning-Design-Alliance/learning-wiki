# Equitable Calling on Students

## Description
Ensure all learners get response opportunities, not just high achievers. Monitor who you call on during discussions to avoid favoring certain learners. Individual learners may 'tune out' when they are not being called on, so it's important to call on all the learners in the classroom so that they know that their participation is important.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monitor interactions to call on all learners equitably. Keep a clipboard to record who you call on during class discussions.
#### Constraints
- This practice can be time-consuming in a class setting with lots of learners. Some learners may not wish to be called on at all.

### Personalization
- Call on all learners, especially those who don't perform well. Monitor which learners get more chances to speak than others.
