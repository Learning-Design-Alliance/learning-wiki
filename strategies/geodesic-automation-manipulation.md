# Automation and geodesic manipulation functions to shift learner focus from construction to physical significance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its 'Manipulate Geodesics' mode, V-SeMo provides automated features — 'Set Sectors', 'Complete', 'Direction' and joystick control — that speed up geodesic construction. The article states: "These automation and manipulation features signiﬁcantly simplify the construction of geodesics." They let learners quickly study long geodesics or many geodesics, make large models manageable, and easily study the impact of start parameters, so proficient users can focus on the properties of geodesics and their physical significance rather than on constructing them.
