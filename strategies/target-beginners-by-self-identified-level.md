# Target quantum event participants by self-identified beginner level rather than by level of education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on their survey finding that self-identified quantum level varied little across education levels, the authors recommend that organizers "target beginners in quantum computing regardless of level of education, instead categorized by self-identiﬁed level of experience in quantum computing". They argue this lets individuals whose first exposure came later in life, who are disproportionately underrepresented, have the opportunity to learn.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collect self-reported experience level at registration so participants can be categorized by experience in quantum computing rather than by education level

- 

### Target Learners
- beginners in quantum computing regardless of level of education
- learners underrepresented in quantum computing whose first exposure came at a later stage in life

### Target Learning Goals
- increase awareness of and first exposure to quantum computing
- support equitable and inclusive participation in quantum education
