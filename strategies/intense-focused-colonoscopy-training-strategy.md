# Sequence flexible sigmoidoscopy before colonoscopy training and schedule periods of intense focused colonoscopy training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that training intensity and prior flexible sigmoidoscopy experience were independently associated with colonoscopy completion, the authors recommend that trainees "should aim to have periods of intense focused colonoscopy training" and "may wish to start learning flexible sigmoidoscopy before colonoscopy training." They add that trainees need not be overly concerned about gaps in training, since breaks of six months or more showed no significant effect on completion. Training programmes are encouraged to exploit the intensity and prior-experience associations in programme design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to sufficient procedure volume to achieve high training intensity, measured as procedures per month of continuous training
#### Constraints
- The article states these associations could be exploited in training programmes but does not report a trial testing such restructuring

### Target Learners
- Early-stage colonoscopy trainees in endoscopy training programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing the rate of competency acquisition as measured by colonoscopy completion and caecal intubation rate
