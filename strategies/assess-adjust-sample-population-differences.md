# Assess and adjust for differences between the study sample and the target population

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide directs researchers to compare the recruited sample against the target population and to make statistical adjustments where the two differ. It names "assessing, and adjusting for differences between the sample and target population" as a key step researchers can take so that impact findings are more generalizable to the population of interest.
