# Integrate simulation training into surgical residency via structured curricula, rotation plans, mentor instruction and feedback, and regional simulation centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a structured curricular embedding of simulation training in surgical further education: existing simulators should be integrated "durch strukturierte Kurrikula in die Weiterbildungskonzepte der Kliniken", with simulation training added to training regulations. Supporting measures include free device access, initial instruction by a knowledgeable mentor, structured mentor feedback, rotation plans, documented feedback discussions, and centralized regional simulation centers to raise utilization and ease financing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Free access to the simulator for trainees
- Initial instruction by a knowledgeable mentor to avoid device damage
- Regular and extensive training to achieve the desired training effect
#### Constraints
- Financing of costly systems is an essential discussion point and not covered by the DRG system
- Regional simulation centers are proposed as one option to ease financing, not yet implemented

### Target Learners
- Physicians in surgical further education (ÄiW)
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical surgical competences and eye-hand coordination
- Teamwork, communication and management of complex treatment processes
