# Sign the Research-Based Product Promise to signal organizational commitment to research-based learning tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Research-Based Product Promise is a public pledge campaign through which individuals and organizations signal commitment to developing, purchasing, and/or using learning tools that incorporate research in their design and development. The report invites readers to "Sign the Research-Based Product Promise to join our campaign to promote high-quality edtech products that support the full spectrum of learners."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Signatories must be committed to developing, purchasing, and/or using learning tools that incorporate research in design and development
#### Constraints
- The report presents the pledge as a signal of commitment; it reports no evidence of effects on product quality or purchasing

### Target Learners
- education leaders and organizations purchasing or using edtech
- edtech developers

### Target Learning Goals
- promoting high-quality, research-based edtech for the full spectrum of learners
