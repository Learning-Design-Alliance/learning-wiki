# Instructor and Peer Feedback

## Description
Combining instructor feedback with peer feedback enhances the learning process. While instructor feedback is essential for progress, peer feedback allows learners to synthesize and re-teach content to each other, sparking innovative thoughts and increasing content mastery. Tools like VoiceThread can be used to provide written, verbal, and/or video feedback in a presentation format. Peer feedback also heightens social presence in online environments by creating an inclusive atmosphere where learners openly share and support one another.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to tools and platforms for providing feedback (e.g., VoiceThread), clear guidelines for effective peer feedback, and a structured process.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on learners' ability to provide constructive feedback and engage in supportive interactions. Requires initial training and modeling by the instructor.
