# Engage physics students through embodied movement and multisensory representations

## Description
Under the neuroqueer embodied invention theme, the article recommends that educators "Allow/encourage students use their bodies when demonstrating new ideas" and engage students with multisensory representations of phenomena. It further recommends multisensory and hands-on learning opportunities and demonstrations that encourage students to think in words and in images, treating embodied problem-solving as a valid mode of doing physics.
