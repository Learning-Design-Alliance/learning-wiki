# Replicate SWPBIS studies and conduct experimental research when applying SWPBIS in other countries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that SWPBIS be implemented in other countries to minimize problem behaviors and raise academic achievement, and that practitioners in other countries replicate the identified studies with different student participants in search of new models. It further recommends that academicians conduct future studies focusing on experimental designs in cooperation with schools willing to adopt the method, addressing the field's shortage of experimental control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation with schools willing to adopt the method
- Replication of the identified studies with different student participants
#### Constraints
- Maintained and extended uses of SWPBIS have not been regular or extensive in countries other than the USA

### Target Learners
- students in schools outside the USA

### Target Learning Goals
- minimize problem behaviors
- raise academic achievement levels
