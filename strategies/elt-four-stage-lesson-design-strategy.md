# Design lessons around all four ELT stages and ask application-oriented reflection questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that agriscience teachers "design lessons that consider each of the four factors in ELT" so students engage, reflect, conceptualize, and experiment, supported by high-quality professional development for preservice and in-service teachers. They also recommend replacing recall questions with application questions: instead of asking what students learned, ask "how can you use or apply what you learned today" to drive deeper thinking and transfer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher professional development to develop curricular activities that utilize and reinforce the tenets of ELT
#### Constraints
- The 50-minute class period limited experimental trials and reflection time; block scheduling is recommended for replication

### Target Learners
- secondary agriscience students
- preservice and in-service agriscience teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- student engagement
- discussion skills
- transfer of new knowledge to application situations
