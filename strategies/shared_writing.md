# Shared Writing

## Description
Shared writing involves a teacher and students collaboratively composing a text, with the teacher acting as a scribe. The teacher records student-generated ideas and then guides them to revise and refine the writing. This process helps learners internalize concepts and improve their communication skills through group interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Chart paper, pens (different colors for revisions), a student-generated word bank, and a teacher to guide and scribe the writing.
#### Constraints
- Requires strong facilitation skills to manage diverse contributions and guide revisions effectively. May be time-consuming and require multiple sessions.
