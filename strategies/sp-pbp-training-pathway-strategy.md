# Integrate the validated SP chicken anastomosis task into SP-specific Proficiency Based Progression training pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building structured, proficiency-based robotic training curricula for the Single-Port platform, starting with basic surgical skills on the validated chicken anastomosis task before practice on real patients. It argues that "development of precise PBP training pathways is fundamental to minimize the impact of the learning curve on OR efficiency" and patient outcomes, and that "future efforts should focus on translating these validated training frameworks from the MP to the SP platform".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objective performance metrics and validated proficiency benchmarks for the SP platform, as developed in this study
#### Constraints
- The impact of proficiency achievement for the SP chicken anastomosis task on clinical practice has not been evaluated

### Target Learners
- younger surgeons seeking access to SP robotic surgery and trainees in structured robotic curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- safe acquisition of basic robotic surgical skills (suturing, knot-tying) prior to procedural training and operating room practice

- [A modified Delphi process with 14 panelists achieved 100% agreement on a metrics set of 5 steps, 20 errors, 1 failure-to-progress definition and 4 critical errors for the SP anastomosis task](../claims/sp-anastomosis-delphi-metrics-consensus.md) [+M]
- [The SP chicken anastomosis metrics achieved high inter-rater reliability (IRR 0.96) between two blinded trained reviewers](../claims/sp-anastomosis-metrics-inter-rater-reliability.md) [+W]
