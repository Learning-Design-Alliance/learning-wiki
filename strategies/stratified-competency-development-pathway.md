# Profile-based stratified competency development: an assessment-intervention-reassessment pathway tailored to low-, moderate- and high-level nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nurse managers use the identified competency profiles to organise differentiated development support. It proposes an "assessment-intervention-reassessment pathway: strengthening foundational competencies in the low-level profile, supporting capability expansion in the moderate-level profile and preparing nurses in the high-level profile for clinical teaching, quality improvement and specialist development." Self-directed learning ability and self-efficacy are treated as modifiable targets embedded across all stages of competency development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Latent profile assessment of core competency across the seven CIRN dimensions to assign nurses to profiles before selecting support
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design precludes causal inference about the relationships between self-directed learning ability, self-efficacy and profile membership
- Findings come from two general hospitals in one city and may limit generalisability to other regions, hospital types and nursing populations

### Target Learners
- hospital clinical nurses in low-, moderate- and high-level competency profiles

### Target Learning Goals
- core nursing competency development, including clinical care, critical thinking, leadership, and professional development
