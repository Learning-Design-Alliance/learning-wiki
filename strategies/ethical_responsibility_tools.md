# Ethical Responsibility Tools

## Description
Defining responsibility and ethical responsibility, providing specific tools such as graphic organizers or visual representations to help students consider the ethical and moral responsibilities of their decisions. Reading current events and discussing if the decisions made are ethical, and evaluating those decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools such as graphic organizers or sticky notes, current events articles, class time for discussion and analysis.
#### Constraints
- Ethical dilemmas may be complex and lack clear-cut solutions. Some learners may struggle to apply ethical principles to real-life situations. Discussions can be sensitive and require skilled facilitation.

- All learner levels, including K–12, higher education, and adult learners in professional development programs

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote ethical reasoning, develop moral awareness, enhance decision-making skills, improve critical thinking, and foster self-reflection.

### Affordances
- Provides concrete tools for ethical analysis, encourages consideration of moral responsibilities, promotes collaborative discussion of ethical dilemmas, and fosters reflection on personal values.

### Personalization
- Graphic organizers and visual representations can be adapted to suit different learning styles. Current events can be chosen based on relevance to student interests or real-world concerns.
