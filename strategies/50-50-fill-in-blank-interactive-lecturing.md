# Use 50/50 and Fill-in-the-Blank interactive lecturing questions with whole-class choral responses for real-time formative feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The instructor poses a sentence with a missing word or a two-choice question, then pauses while the entire class says the answer aloud, e.g. "When the star is moving toward Earth, its light will be" followed by students shouting "blueshifted." The authors report this "sequenced questions offers a quick and effective way for the instructor and students to gain real time feedback" on whether students are building a coherent model of foundational knowledge. They recommend establishing it as a class norm at the start of the semester and using it multiple times per lecture, including during debriefs.

## Design Implications
