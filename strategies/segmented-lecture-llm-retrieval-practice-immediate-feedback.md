# Divide lectures into 10-15-minute segments ending with low-stakes LLM-generated MCQ retrieval practice and immediate feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
During intervention weeks, each 120-minute lecture was divided into 10-15-minute segments with LLM-generated MCQs at each segment end: 'students were asked to answer multiple-choice questions (MCQs) generated by an LLM as a form of retrieval practice. Immediate feedback on the correctness of responses was provided to students after each attempt.' Activities were explicitly designed as 'low-stakes learning opportunities' that students could attempt as many times as they wanted.
