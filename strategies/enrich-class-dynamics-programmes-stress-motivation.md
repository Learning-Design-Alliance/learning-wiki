# Implement educational programmes that enrich university class dynamics so students feel more motivated and can channel academic stress positively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that, given the observed paths from teacher behaviour through stress to motivation, strategies and performance, "it is necessary to implement educational programmes that enrich the class dynamics that take place at university, so that students feel more motivated and can channel academic stress in a more positive way". The strategy targets the teacher-controlled classroom environment as the lever for reducing stress and supporting motivation, metacognitive strategy use and critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programmes must enrich class dynamics so that students feel more motivated
- Teachers must adopt autonomy-supportive rather than psychologically controlling behaviour
#### Constraints
- The article states future studies should use mixed methodology and incorporate factors such as resilience, emotions and coping strategies, indicating the evidence base for programme design is still incomplete
