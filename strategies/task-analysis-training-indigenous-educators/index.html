Train indigenous adult educators in task analysis to build state needs-assessment capability¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

After judging the contracted region-wide needs assessment inadequate, the project shifted to building local capability: during the first year, with the Associate Director's assistance, it ran task analysis training sessions for indigenous adult educators. The report states that "task analysis training sessions were conducted to provide indigenous adult educators with one technique recognized as an appropriate procedure for identifying training needs." New Jersey then established four task forces (ABE, ESL, GED, counseling) and the Virgin Islands held a task analysis institute to identify initial competencies.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A capable staff member (the project used its Associate Director) to conduct the training sessions

Constraints¶

  • The strategy arose because a universal region-wide assessment was judged infeasible given modest funds and regional diversity

Target Learners¶

  • indigenous adult educators in state departments and local programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conducting task analysis to identify staff development and training needs locally

Related Strategies¶

  • Establish continuing one-to-one specialist relationships so expert skills transfer to the tribe
