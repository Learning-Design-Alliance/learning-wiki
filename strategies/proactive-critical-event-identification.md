# Proactively identify upcoming critical events via stress tests, risk analysis, and learning analytics on program operation data

## Description
The authors recommend that designers and coordinators of online study programs move beyond retrospective analysis: "We also recommend that future studies propose and develop novel approaches and solutions that will help the designers and coordinators of online study programs to map out upcoming CEs and plan how to proactively react to them." They suggest the four-pillar model equipped with critical event and pivotal factor analysis can be used for risk management, including stress tests in the form of simulations or scenarios supported by formal risk analysis.
