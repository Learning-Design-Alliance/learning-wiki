Structure AI-powered adult learning curricula around flexible goals, mastery-based content, varied assessments, and interaction supports¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The article's concluding recommendation is that "Institutions aiming to implement AI-powered learning for adult leaders should consider incorporating flexible learning goals and a variety of assessment methods to cater to different learning styles and needs." It further recommends that AI system design "should prioritize cognitive and socio-emotional support mechanisms," and that immersive game-based platforms and interactive interfaces may serve as effective mediums for enhancing learner-AI interactions. The strategy translates the study's five themes into implementable curriculum and system design choices for adult education.

Context¶

Claims¶

  • Adult learners prefer scenario-based, unobtrusive gamified, and competency-based assessment in AI-powered learning [+W]
  • Working adult learners perceive content mastery and developing a lifelong habit of learning as the optimal learning goals for AI-powered learning [+W]
  • Adult learners view building an ethical learner-AI relationship, with baked-in AI ethics norms, as important for socio-emotional interaction [+W]
  • Adult learners perceive immersive game-based platforms and interactive AI interfaces as effective mediums for enhancing learner-AI artifact-mediated interaction [+W]
  • Adult learners identify metacognition and crafting diverse, high-quality questions at different cognitive levels as crucial supports for learner-AI cognitive interaction [+W]
  • Adult learners value personalized mastery-based learning in AI-powered curricula, while some caution it can reduce exposure to new or unexpected content [+W]
