# Provide continuous tutor training in PBL case facilitation, assessment, and evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that tutor training be continuous and comprehensive so tutors can internalize and implement PBL methods effectively. In its conclusions it states that "These findings imply that continuous tutor training in PBL case facilitation, assessment, and evaluation is crucial." This responds to findings that newly engaged staff facilitate PBL without introductory training and that most tutors have served less than a year in the role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to providing recurring training opportunities for tutors
#### Constraints
- The article notes the current two to three days of tutor training is not provided consistently

### Target Learners
- medical school tutors and academic staff facilitating PBL

### Target Learning Goals
- effective PBL facilitation, assessment, and evaluation skills
