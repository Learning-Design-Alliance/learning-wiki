# Use BlindAid in three potential applications: O&M training simulator, diagnostic tool, and internet-based virtual exploration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
After further research, the authors propose that "BlindAid could play a central role in three potential applications": first, an O&M training simulator allowing practice of O&M skills with extra time in a safe environment; second, an O&M diagnostic tool allowing O&M specialists to track and observe participants' spatial behavior, spatial strategy, and O&M problem solving; and third, making BlindAid available on the Internet to support visually impaired people in virtual exploration of spaces.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further research is needed before these applications can be realized, including research on virtual echolocation feedback and a device for exploring spaces in advance of and during navigation
#### Constraints
- The authors state these are potential applications contingent on further research; the study did not test them as deployed applications

### Target Learners
- people who are blind or have low vision in O&M rehabilitation programs
- O&M specialists

### Target Learning Goals
- orientation and mobility skills
- spatial problem solving
- spatial awareness of unknown spaces

## Claims

- [Experimental-group participants who practiced with BlindAid were more successful in 73% of real-space orientation tasks, especially perspective-taking and reverse tasks](../claims/blindaid-experimental-more-successful-orientation-tasks.md) [+W]
- [Experimental participants used orientation problem-solving strategies more, including three times more landmarks and four times more going-back-to-starting-point](../claims/blindaid-more-problem-solving-strategies-landmarks.md) [+W]
- [The experimental group pointed more accurately at object locations in all indoor spaces, but the control group pointed more accurately in the outdoor campus task](../claims/pointing-accuracy-indoor-experimental-outdoor-control.md) [+W]
