# Explicit Conversations about Emotions and Social Issues

## Description
Engaging learners in explicit conversations about emotions and social issues, including issues of social justice, helps them develop a deeper understanding of social awareness and empathy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitation to guide empathetic discussions and time for reflection.
#### Constraints
- Requires sensitivity to learners' emotional states and cultural backgrounds. Discussions must be facilitated to ensure inclusivity and respect for diverse perspectives.

### Target Learners

- K-12 learners and adult learners in social skills and diversity programs.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance understanding of emotions, foster awareness of social issues, and promote empathy and social justice.

### Affordances
- Provides a platform for discussing complex emotions and social issues, promotes empathy and social justice, and fosters social awareness.

### Personalization
- Discussion topics can be tailored to match learners' interests and cultural backgrounds. Support can be provided to learners who struggle with empathy or social awareness.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' ability to articulate emotions, demonstrate understanding of social issues, and engage in respectful discussions.

## Impact
- Explicit conversations about emotions and social issues enhance social awareness and emotional intelligence. Social awareness has been connected to higher academic achievement and better coping skills (Jones, Greenberg, & Crowley, 2015).
