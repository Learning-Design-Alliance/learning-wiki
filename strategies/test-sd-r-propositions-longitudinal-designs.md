# Rigorously test SD–R propositions using longitudinal and shortitudinal designs, including interaction and spiral tests

## Description
The article sets a research agenda for validating SD–R theory: studies should test whether health impairment and motivational processes are unique and predict different outcomes, test statistical interactions between study demands and resources, and test loss and gain spirals. It recommends "longitudinal research designs with months or years between the assessments, or “shortitudinal” designs" with daily or weekly assessments so causal and reversed causal effects can be modeled, plus rigorous qualitative research on students' demands and resources.
