# Use a stepwise implementation approach for routine PRO assessment with motivated colleagues, onsite coaching and attention to barriers and facilitators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Interview participants recommended a stepwise approach to implementing routine PRO assessments in clinical settings: planning across organisational levels, identifying and collaborating with motivated colleagues, and onsite coaching. The strategy includes raising "Awareness of individual and organisational barriers and facilitators (e.g. individual barriers for staff, patient ability and motivation)" and highlighting "the supporting role of motivated colleagues and senior peers acting as leaders of integration". It addresses general planning including costs, resources, stakeholders and technical issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with motivated colleagues and senior peers acting as leaders of integration; engagement of staff in the initial phases of implementation.
#### Constraints
- Implementation planning must account for costs, resources, stakeholders, technical issues and keeping administrative burden as low as possible.

### Target Learners
- Oncology health care professionals, including those with decision-making capacity or in management positions

### Target Learning Goals
- Planning and organising the implementation of routine PRO assessments in clinical care
