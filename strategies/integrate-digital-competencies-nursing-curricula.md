# Integrate digital-competency development into nursing curricula through pedagogically guided real-world technology activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend prioritizing digital-competency development in nursing curricula as a key transferable skill, noting a significant gap caused by lack of systematic curriculum integration. They argue that "Providing access to technology is not enough; it is essential to design pedagogical activities that guide its use in real-world contexts", with educational video creation offered as a viable and effective strategy for preparing students for modern clinical practice and health communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systematic curricular integration rather than ad hoc technology access
- Pedagogical activities guiding technology use in real-world contexts
#### Constraints
- A significant competency gap persists where integration is not systematic

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- digital competencies
- patient education and empowerment through digital media
- information literacy
