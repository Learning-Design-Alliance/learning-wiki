# Use Student Smartphones to Reduce Cost of AI Education at Schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The curriculum recommends using students' own smartphones as the central technological element for all practical AI work. The article states this 'significantly reduces the cost of introducing this subject at regions with low economic capacity.' This strategy makes embedded intelligence education feasible without requiring schools to purchase expensive hardware.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large majority of high school students must have their own smartphone
#### Constraints
- If students cannot use their own devices, schools may face budget limitations to buy them; the article suggests donation campaigns for used phones as a mitigation

### Target Learners
- High school students aged 15-18 in scientific programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical implementation of embedded intelligence solutions using accessible technology
