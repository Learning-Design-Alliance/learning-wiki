# Actionable writing practices for researchers to produce policy-ready implications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review offers a set of writing suggestions for creating impactful implications, including: signal policy relevance early in the abstract; adopt the lexicon of governance; apply the WH-framework to implication narratives; calibrate rhetorical strength to the underlying evidence; use dedicated, clearly labeled subsections such as "Policy Implications"; prioritize implementation pathways; practice stakeholder co-production; include a "Policy Summary" table; explicitly address implementation trade-offs; delineate actor responsibility; and address "living", iterative policy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers adopt the WH-framework during their writing process so conclusions reach beyond the technical domain
#### Constraints
- Rhetorical certainty should be aligned with the strength of the underlying evidence to maintain scholarly credibility

### Target Learners
- education researchers, particularly those studying AI × SEL

### Target Learning Goals
- writing clear, actionable, reader-oriented policy implications
