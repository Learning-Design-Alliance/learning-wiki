# Support socio-emotional aspects of collaboration especially when group members experience divergent affective states

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that socially-related factors trigger mixed-valence activation, the article suggests that groups experiencing divergent affective states could benefit from support targeted at the socio-emotional aspects of collaboration. It notes that "advanced technologies can be used to track students’ socio-emotional processes" and could also be used to make these processes visible to students, helping prompt and support socio-emotional aspects of collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology or pedagogical means to detect divergent group affective states
#### Constraints
- Expressions and interpretations of socially triggered affect can be more covert and complex to interpret, especially in online contexts with constricted emotional cues

### Target Learners
- collaborative learning groups

### Target Learning Goals
- socio-emotional regulation during collaboration
