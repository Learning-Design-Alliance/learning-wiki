# School districts adopting AI should follow a five-step roadmap: engage communities, apply and develop policies, provide professional development, create supportive learning environments, and monitor and evaluate AI use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report synthesizes the state documents' advice into a common roadmap for local education leaders. The steps are to engage with communities, apply existing policies and develop additional AI policies aligned with regulations such as FERPA, COPPA, IDEA, and CIPA, provide professional development for educators, create a supportive learning environment with equitable device and internet access, and monitor and evaluate AI use. As the report states, "The roadmap below reflects common steps that appear across many of the documents."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policies must be aligned with regulations such as FERPA, COPPA, IDEA, and CIPA to ensure compliance with data privacy and security laws
- Acceptable Use and other policies should be regularly reviewed and updated as needed
#### Constraints
- The roadmap reflects steps common across many, not all, of the seven state documents

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Responsible and effective integration of AI into teaching and learning
