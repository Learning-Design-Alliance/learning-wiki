# In-service updating course for the teacher team to co-design the interdisciplinary path

## Description
To promote effective design and a real interdisciplinary approach, the article recommends organizing an updating course for in-service physics, mathematics and science teachers in the school, with the purpose of discussing and determining nature, degree of integration, scope and sequence of activities. The teacher's team should be empowered to shape and edit the curriculum according to the students' needs, and activities can be adapted and enlarged following students' interests.
