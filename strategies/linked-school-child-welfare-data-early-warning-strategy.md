# Use linked school and child welfare data to flag students at academic risk in the coming quarter or semester

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy combines administrative school records with child welfare data to predict which students will encounter academic problems in the near term. The fact sheet explains that it "describes predictors related to near-term academic problems, meaning academic problems in the next quarter or semester," drawing on a report that developed and assessed a predictive model for this purpose. Education agencies can adopt this approach to build early-warning indicators that operate on a quarter or semester timescale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A companion report's documented approach for developing the predictive model
- Linked school and child welfare data
#### Constraints
- Predictions address near-term academic problems in the next quarter or semester rather than long-term outcomes

### Target Learners
- Students in schools served by agencies with access to both school and child welfare administrative data

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely identification of at-risk students for near-term academic support
