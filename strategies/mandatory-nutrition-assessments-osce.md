# Include nutrition-related questions in mandatory written and practical assessments, including OSCEs, to motivate engagement with nutrition education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review reports that multiple cited authors recommend "including nutrition‐related questions in mandatory written and practical assessments". Written exams could use case studies on nutritional management of chronic disease, and OSCEs can assess dietary assessment, nutrition counselling, personalised nutrition planning and communication skills. Mandatory assessments are described as powerful motivators for student engagement.
