# Provide teacher-centered implementation support: training, clear student instructions, and inclusive materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that tool selection should be guided by classroom context, infrastructure limits, and learner diversity, and that "Even well-designed tools require support structures such as clear instructions, technical troubleshooting, and targeted training to be fully effective." Educators in the workshops requested further self-paced and instructor-led training and more inclusive materials for learners with special needs. Technology alone does not improve education; it must be aligned with pedagogical goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technical infrastructure including stable internet and available hardware
- Adequate training and support structures for teachers
#### Constraints
- Feedback was largely qualitative and self-reported; effectiveness was influenced by connectivity, hardware availability, and teachers' prior experience

### Target Learners
- secondary-level physics students
- physics educators

### Target Learning Goals
- effective integration of digital tools into physics instruction
- inclusive access for learners with special needs
