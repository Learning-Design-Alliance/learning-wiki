# Learning Clubs

## Description
Learning clubs are a grouping system used to organize active learning events based on student-selected areas of interest. Guided by the teacher, students select content area topics and draw on multiple texts—including websites, printed material, video, and music—to investigate their topics. Students then have the opportunity to share their learning using similar media, such as learning blogs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a variety of multimedia resources. Class time for research and discussion. Facilitation by the instructor to guide research and provide support.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure meaningful discussions and prevent off-topic conversations. Some students may be unprepared or dominate discussions, requiring instructor intervention. Assessment can be challenging due to varied learning materials and group dynamics.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve research skills, develop digital literacy, foster collaborative discussions, increase student engagement

### Affordances
- Encourages student ownership and motivation through choice. Provides a social context for learning and discussing texts. Promotes deeper engagement with reading material and critical thinking skills. Enhances vocabulary and communication skills.

### Personalization
- Offer diverse topic choices catering to different interests. Allow learners to set their research pace and discussion topics. Provide options for final projects to accommodate different learning styles.

## Assessment Evidence
- Monitor participation and engagement in discussions. Review learning logs, blog posts, and project submissions for evidence of research skills and comprehension. Assess group dynamics and collaboration through observation.

## Impact
- Research supports the use of literature circles and book clubs to support learning across topics. Students are motivated to read when given the opportunity to choose materials.
