# Collect pre- and post-surveys plus educator post-mortem sessions to evaluate and improve each training event

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Every HSF training event is accompanied by a structured feedback loop. Participants complete a pre- and post-survey covering demographics and questions assessing the quality and method of training, adaptable to each event's topic. After the survey results are in, educators hold a post-mortem session to internally discuss successes and failures, guided by the survey results, and a short presentation about the training experience is given at the HSF Training weekly or all-working-groups planning meeting. This makes evaluation a routine, community-visible part of every event rather than an occasional study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback is required to evaluate whether learning is being effectively facilitated and to ensure the success of future training.
#### Constraints
- The pre/post learning evaluation only probes perceived and self-reported learning of a skill, not later application in research.

### Target Learners
- HEP scientists participating in training events

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating and continuously improving software training events
