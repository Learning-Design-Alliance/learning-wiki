# Prioritize the specifics of selection interviews — question content, phrasing, interviewer characteristics, and how reports are used — over the panel-versus-MMI format choice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the reviewed papers did not report interview content or specifications, the authors argue the format distinction may matter less than assumed: "the specifics of the interviews—such as what is asked, how questions are phrased, the characteristics of the interviewers, how the data are computed and used, and similar considerations—should be prioritized while selection interviews are employed." They recommend research focusing on these specifics.
