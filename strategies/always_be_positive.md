# Always Be Positive

## Description
Maintaining a positive attitude in all interactions, from classrooms to playgrounds, during school and after. Responding to situations in a positive way, serving as a change agent for creating positive classrooms and schools that produce happy and successful students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment to maintaining a positive attitude, willingness to respond to situations in a positive way, ongoing self-reflection and self-care.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to maintain a positive attitude in difficult situations, requires ongoing effort and self-awareness.
