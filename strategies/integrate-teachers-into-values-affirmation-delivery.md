# Integrate classroom teachers more fully into values-affirmation delivery to resolve the stealth-blindness contradiction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future implementations integrate teachers more fully into the delivery process in order to resolve the conflict between keeping teachers blind to the experimental hypotheses on one hand and maintaining stealth and normalcy for students on the other. They argue this internal contradiction was implicated in the variation in teacher delivery observed despite detailed training, and they strongly caution that integrating these written interventions into regular curriculum must be handled carefully.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be trained before first implementation and complete classroom debrief forms after each exercise
#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
