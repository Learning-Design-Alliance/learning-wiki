# Train teachers in direct instruction and in differentiated and scaffolded instruction for tiered RTI interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends professional development in direct instruction, noting the district replaced DI with scripted mini-lesson format lessons, and that direct instruction "reinforces repetition necessary for the struggling student or the potential specific learning -disabled student", who needs to hear and repeat a concept many times. It further recommends professional development in differentiated and scaffold instruction for teachers applying the three tiers of RTI interventions, since remediation requires specialized and intensive teaching methods to pinpoint deficient skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers applying interventions related to the three tiers of the RTI process receive training in differentiated and scaffold instruction
- Teachers receive professional development in direct instruction
#### Constraints
- The recommendation responds to the district's replacement of direct instruction with scripted mini-lesson format lessons

### Target Learners
- struggling students
- potential specific learning-disabled students
- general education elementary teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- closing achievement gaps through remedial instruction
- mastering skills needing repeated practice
