# Tailored training strategies for PICC care knowledge by hospital level: standardized in-service training in secondary/primary hospitals and guideline optimization with self-directed learning support in tertiary hospitals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing managers focus on nurses with lower professional titles, lower educational level, insufficient PICC care experience, and no prior training, "particularly in lower-level hospitals". In secondary/primary hospitals it proposes "establishing mandatory, competency-based PICC training modules delivered through regional hub hospitals or online platforms"; in tertiary hospitals it proposes optimizing guideline implementation, audits, micro-credentialing programs (e.g., "PICC Care Champions" certifications), and protected time for professional development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nursing managers able to target training by professional title, education, experience, and training history
- Regional hub hospitals or online platforms to deliver training in lower-resource settings
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a cross-sectional study; causal relationships cannot be established

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses involved in intravenous therapy-related clinical work

### Target Learning Goals
- PICC care knowledge including function evaluation, disinfection, flushing and sealing, complication management, and health education
