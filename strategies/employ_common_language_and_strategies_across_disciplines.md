# Employ Common Language and Strategies Across Disciplines

## Description
Employing common language and strategies across disciplines involves aligning writing expectations and terminology across different subject areas. This approach reduces cognitive load for students by creating consistency in how writing is taught and assessed, allowing them to focus on skill development rather than adapting to different teacher expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires collaboration among teachers from different disciplines to align writing expectations and terminology.
#### Constraints
- May require significant coordination and communication among teachers. Requires flexibility to adapt common strategies to different subject areas.
