# Longitudinal integration of AI literacy competencies across all phases of medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that AI competencies be integrated longitudinally across all phases of medical education: foundational preclinical exposure to AI concepts and data literacy, intermediate clinical application in diagnostics and decision support, advanced critical evaluation of AI outputs, ethical and regulatory training, research-level innovation, and continuing medical education. Integration requires "alignment with existing competency-based frameworks and continuous evaluation" and interdisciplinary collaboration across medicine, data science, engineering, and informatics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alignment with competency-based frameworks; interdisciplinary collaboration; continuous evaluation of effectiveness
#### Constraints
- Requires institutional financial and digital readiness

### Target Learners
- medical students
- healthcare providers
- physicians in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy
- critical evaluation of AI outputs
- ethical AI use
