# Extend NOVA to five further low-computation astronomy activities beyond Kepler's third law

## Description
The article proposes that teachers explore other applications of NOVA beyond Kepler's third law, listing five simulations that "only require simple calculations and graph interpretation": the inverse square law of brightness, orbits of artificial satellites, light curves of transiting exoplanets, Kepler's first and second laws with eccentric orbits, and cosmology with the Hubble-Lemaître law. The choice of scenario is left to each school's curriculum, teacher preferences, and student interests.
