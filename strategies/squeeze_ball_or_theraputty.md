# Squeeze Ball or Theraputty

## Description
Providing a squeeze ball or Theraputty offers a tactile outlet for students to manage frustration and agitation. These tools can help students redirect their physical energy, reducing emotional intensity and promoting self-regulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessibility to squeeze balls or Theraputty, explicit instruction on their purpose and use.
#### Constraints
- May be distracting if not used properly. Requires clear expectations and guidance to prevent misuse.

### Personalization
- Offer different types of sensory tools (e.g., squeeze balls with different textures or resistance) to meet individual student preferences.
