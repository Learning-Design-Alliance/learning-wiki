# Recommendations for future PBL and concept cartoon research and materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article closes with three recommendations: future studies should "allow sufficient time and have a longer experimental practice" to determine the effects of PBL and concept cartoons; effects on different variables and with different workgroups should be examined; and textbooks and internet sites can include examples of problem based learning and concept cartoons to increase their use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- longer experimental practice than the 16 hours used here
#### Constraints
- The null knowledge-daily-life result may reflect the short duration, cognitive overload, and concept cartoons' limited number of views, per the authors

### Target Learners
- middle school science students

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry learning skills
- relating knowledge with daily life
