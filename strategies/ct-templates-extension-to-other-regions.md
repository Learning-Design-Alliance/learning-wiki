# Use CT-based reconstructions to create standardized proportional templates for other anatomical regions, with counterbalanced region and view across study phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending the template approach beyond the skull: "CT-based reconstructions could be used to create standardized proportional templates for regions such as the spine, pelvis, thorax, limbs, and major neurovascular pathways". They also advise that future studies "should counterbalance anatomical region and view across phases so that T1 and T2 drawings involve comparable task difficulty", and suggest examining whether TBDM can be combined with digital or AR-based anatomy tools.
