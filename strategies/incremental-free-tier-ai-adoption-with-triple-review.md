# Adopt AI incrementally in waves from simple to complex tasks, on free-tier tools, with mandatory multi-step human review before any document is issued

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy packages the article's operational recommendations for public units adopting generative AI under budget constraints. Adoption "did not happen in a single block but in waves: it began with the simplest and most stable tasks, advanced to activities of greater complexity as the team gained confidence and mastery". Applications are curated for high impact and low risk (drafting assistance, document synthesis, grammatical review), tools are free-tier commercial platforms, and every AI-assisted draft passes a three-step Triple Review (drafting professional, direct supervisor, signing authority) before issuance. The article also recommends documenting best practices so effective prompts and custom AIs become team-wide multipliers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formally documented governance layer (anonymization protocols, rules on what may be submitted, and mandatory human-review points) before routine use.
#### Constraints
- The method does not replace technical knowledge: critical review by a qualified professional remained indispensable; well-structured prompts produce significantly better results than generic ones and require training and practice.

### Target Learners
- Public-sector teams and managers in agencies with budget constraints adopting generative AI

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing document-production productivity while maintaining legal compliance, data protection, and document quality
