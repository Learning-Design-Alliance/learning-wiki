# Train gatekeeper staff who answer calls, emails, and visits so they understand EL program structures and points of contact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies employees who respond to phone calls, emails, and in-person visits as "important gatekeepers to valuable information about English Learner programs and services." It recommends that individuals in charge of EL programs work with these staff so they understand the structure and nuances of current programs and who the leaders or points of contact are, a depth of understanding the authors call especially important given the variety of labels used across districts and schools.

### Context
#### Requirements
