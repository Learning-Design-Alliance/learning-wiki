# Use an integrated laparoscopic surgical training program combining different training modalities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educators should develop an integrated laparoscopic surgical training program using different training modalities, because VR simulators and live animal surgical training have different benefits than cadaveric organ training. The authors state 'educators should develop an integrated laparoscopic surgical training program using different training modalities.' This strategy leverages the complementary strengths of each modality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to multiple training modalities including VR simulators, live animal surgery, and cadaveric organ wet labs
#### Constraints
- VR simulators have a high initial cost
- Live animal surgical training is expensive and raises animal welfare concerns

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees
- medical students
- urologists

### Target Learning Goals
- essential laparoscopic surgical skills
- management of unexpected bleeding
- bipolar vessel sealer use
