# Prepare novice teachers of the gifted in the sociocultural context of the gifted classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The essay argues that novice teachers of the gifted must be educated to recognize the needs and nature of the social and cultural setting they enter, including the gifted classroom's cultural rules for dialogue, demonstrating intelligence, and gaining acceptance. It recommends that "preparation of teachers of the gifted centers on both observations of self-contained gifted classroom environments, as well as focused social interactions with gifted children and their teachers."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Higher education institutions must alter their notion of gifted curriculum to meet shifting cultural needs of educators
#### Constraints
- The review notes little research has explored the nature of culture in a self-contained gifted classroom and suggests grounded theory study

### Target Learners
- novice teachers of the gifted

### Target Learning Goals
- commanding and communicating cultural standards and supporting gifted pupils' complete development
