# Deliver low-cost ongoing lay health worker training via onsite educational outreach with peer trainers plus a point-of-care reference tool, during regular work hours and without stipends

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This implementable recipe pairs onsite, case-based educational outreach sessions run by trained peer lay health workers with a two-sided point-of-care tool carrying an adherence inquiry guide and patient-education pictorials. Sessions used "a combination of didactic and interactive techniques, and emphasized case-based role playing and discussion of challenging cases". Because training general LHWs is part of TB focus LHWs' regular duties, sessions occur during regular work hours with no stipends, keeping costs low and supporting sustainability and scalability in resource-constrained settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer trainers who are themselves trained in the intervention; onsite scheduling during regular work hours; a point-of-care tool referenced during patient interactions.
#### Constraints
- The authors note that paying training stipends "would limit the sustainability and scalability" of the approach; some participants reported onsite sessions being disturbed by routine duties.

### Target Learners
- Lay health workers / health surveillance assistants in LMIC primary care

### Target Learning Goals
- TB treatment knowledge
- adherence support skills
- patient education and follow-up skills
