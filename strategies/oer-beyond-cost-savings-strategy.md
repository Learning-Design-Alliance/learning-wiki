The editorial reports that Tang's response explains how current OER work and research "needs to move beyond mere cost savings to better address pedagogical and social problems in four key ways." This strategy reframes OER adoption efforts around pedagogical and social value rather than price alone, complementing the editorial's takeaway that cost-savings framings of relative advantage are too narrow.
