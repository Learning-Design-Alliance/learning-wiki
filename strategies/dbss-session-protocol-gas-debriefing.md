# DBSS session protocol: defect-identification exercise followed by GAS-framework debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A repeatable weekly session structure for implementing DBSS: after a unified lecture, participants watch a 15-minute video with embedded defects, then in "a 30-minute group discussion where trainees identified “defect points” and documented them via an online survey platform (Wenjuanxing)". This is followed by "a 40-minute instructor-led debriefing structured around the Gather-Analyze-Summarize (GAS) framework", using submitted group records as the Gather element, then guided hands-on practice on manikins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors experienced in simulation facilitation; an online survey platform for collecting group defect records; instructional defect videos
#### Constraints
- The protocol as printed was delivered to practicing NAs in a single hospital; the article cautions outcomes might differ if applied to anesthesia nursing students

### Target Learners
- practicing nurse anesthetists

### Target Learning Goals
- identification and analysis of procedural errors
- adherence to standard operating procedures
- clinical judgment
