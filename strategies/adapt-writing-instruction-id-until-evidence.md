# Adapt writing instruction from effective interventions for typically developing students and students with dyslexia or dysgraphia until dedicated RCT/QED evidence for students with disorders of ID exists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because no RCT or QED writing-intervention evidence meets inclusion criteria for students with disorders of ID, the authors recommend interim options for teachers: "it may be useful to adapt writing instruction for these students to effective interventions for typically developing students and/or students with dyslexia and/or dysgraphia". The article frames writing skills as an important learning and communication strategy for this group and urges development and investigation of writing interventions using RCTs or QEDs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must learn writing strategies for this group of students even without dedicated trial evidence
#### Constraints
- This is an interim recommendation that applies until RCT or QED studies of writing interventions become available

### Target Learners
- Students with disorders of intellectual development

### Target Learning Goals
- Writing skills (encoding and linguistic production)
