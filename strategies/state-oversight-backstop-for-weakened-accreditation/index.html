State policymakers should strengthen their own oversight capacity as accreditors' watchdog role weakens¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because the forthcoming regulations are set to weaken accreditors' ability to function as independent watchdogs for educational quality and consumer protection, the brief recommends that states fill the gap. As printed: "State policymakers should strengthen their own oversight capacity and invest in the agencies and systems that support accountability across higher education." The brief pairs this with a caution that states must resist efforts by political actors to use state power to encroach on academic freedom and institutional autonomy.
