# Design flipped-classroom videos with sound-image synchronization, short durations, full-body instructor visibility, and opportunities for immediate questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article distills participants' recommendations for making flipped-classroom videos and learning more effective. Students asked that "the voice-footage synchronization should be noted in videos" and that durations be shortened, and suggested showing the instructor's full body, adding small jokes, enabling immediate questions, and using a social platform such as EasyClass for discussing unclear points while watching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos must be watchable before class, so synchronization and duration problems should be addressed before distribution
#### Constraints
- Some students judged the method unsuitable for field courses such as algebra or analysis because immediate feedback is unavailable

### Target Learners
- pre-service elementary mathematics teachers
- university students in instructional technology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding course content via pre-class videos
- developing instructional materials with technology
