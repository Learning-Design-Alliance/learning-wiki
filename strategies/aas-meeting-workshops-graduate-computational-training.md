The article notes that summer schools and workshops are a popular way to train graduate students in specialized codes and techniques, and encourages the AAS to extend and promote such training at its meetings. It highlights "the Software Carpentry 7 sessions at recent AAS meetings" as "an excellent example of this training" with significant potential to expand, and suggests instrument-specific sessions and instructor training.
