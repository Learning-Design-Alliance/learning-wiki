# Teacher pages with an e-Logbook for tracking, assessing and guiding student research teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teacher pages function like a teacher's edition of a textbook, listing prior knowledge, learner outcomes, standards alignment and teaching strategies. The e-Logbook, linked to the study guide, is 'a primary tool to track, assess and guide student work': teachers grade team descriptions, inspect notes for missed resources, and write comments. Online pre- and post-tests and a printable rubric support assessment.
