# Constructing dipole field lines by adding twice the parallel projection and the negative perpendicular component

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A step-by-step graphical recipe for drawing field lines of a single electric or magnetic dipole, based on the similarly rewritten field formula B(r) = C/r³ (2m‖ − m⊥). The prescription is to draw the dipole vector, decompose it along and perpendicular to the position vector, then "Add together the twice the projection along the position vector and add the reverse (negative) of the perpendicular part. This gives the tangential line." The paper notes this makes fast constructions in e.g. matlab possible.
