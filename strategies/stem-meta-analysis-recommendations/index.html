Recommendations for STEM research and implementation practice¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article closes with recommendations for both researchers and practitioners: researchers should share study data, state which effect size is calculated, and include only large-sample studies in meta-analyses; educators can start STEM practices from preschool by providing a suitable learning atmosphere, use STEM in class programs, revise based on the program, and add STEM classes to bachelor's programs. The article states "it is advised that the sample in experimental studies be no less than 20 people."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Experimental study samples of no less than 20 people to prevent bias, per the article's advice

Constraints¶

  • Current dissertations are limited to effect sizes for standardized mean differences, which the article notes do not cover all possible research designs

Target Learners¶

  • students from preschool through higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement in science, technology, engineering and mathematics

Related Strategies¶

  • Best practices for RL in education: evaluate live with actual users at scale, look beyond STEM, and target under-served learner groups including children
  • Advance AI-motivation research through theory-driven, pedagogically explicit, longitudinal designs covering broader motivational constructs
