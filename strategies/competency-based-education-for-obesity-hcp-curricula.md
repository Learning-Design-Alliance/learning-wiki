# Use competency-based education frameworks to make obesity care proficiencies explicit and measurable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review advocates competency-based education (CBE) as the framework for designing obesity curricula for healthcare professionals. CBE "makes this explicit by establishing observable and measurable performance metrics that learners must attain to be deemed competent", beginning with consideration of the competencies desired in the HCP workforce. The review argues this establishes shared understanding across education, assessment and regulation, fostering coherent multidisciplinary care, and recommends embedding obesity-related CBE into professional accreditation and evaluation frameworks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of comprehensive, up-to-date obesity education at entry, graduate and continuing professional development levels
- Clear definitions of competencies and what constitutes their achievement
#### Constraints
- Curriculum capacity is contested; the review notes multidisciplinary or chronic disease frameworks with disease-specific obesity inclusion may be more efficient for educators

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professional students
- Practicing healthcare professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Obesity prevention, diagnosis, treatment and management competencies

### Affordances
- Obesity Competency Domain Taxonomy
