## Description
The article recommends that institutions create dashboards allowing instructors and administrators to visualize student outcomes along many dimensions, including gender and racial inequities. It notes that "tools create equity reports that can fuel departmental conversations and instructional transformation." Free online resources such as EQUIP and the LASSO platform can automate data collection and equity analyses.
