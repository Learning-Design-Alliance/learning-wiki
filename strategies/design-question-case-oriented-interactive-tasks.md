# Design interactive online tasks oriented towards questions or cases aligned with deep-learning goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that course development teams "carefully design interactive tasks oriented towards questions or cases" that align with deep-learning goals and stimulate nursing students' interest in exploration. Task difficulty should be rationally allocated to avoid tasks that are too single or too complex, so students maintain a positive learning attitude and gradually improve their level of deep learning. Supporting measures include optimizing platform functions (group discussion rooms, real-time feedback tools, learning analysis dashboards) and establishing a regular feedback and iteration mechanism.
