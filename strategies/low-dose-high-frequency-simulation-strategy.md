# Use low-dose high-frequency simulation-based training with structured ongoing practice sessions to promote retention of clinical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article describes low-dose high-frequency training as a competence-building approach using "targeted simulation-based learning activities that are spaced over time and reinforced with structured, ongoing practice sessions" to promote maximum retention of clinical skills. It is offered as a way to counter the skills decay observed after a single intensive teaching block, with the open question of how to operationalise it within large undergraduate programmes.
