Design AI learning systems for inspectable, contestable use: provenance, represented uncertainty, user control and epistemic plurality¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's design implications prescribe that systems support "claim-level provenance where feasible, distinguish retrieved evidence from model-generated synthesis, and make source limitations visible". Uncertainty should be represented through alternatives, ranges and explicit unknowns rather than unsupported confidence scores; interfaces should preserve user control to revise prompts, compare outputs and reject recommendations; and systems should surface disagreement and diverse sources. These requirements demand sufficient traceability for educational judgement, not complete model transparency.
