# Move factual and preparatory learning to presession work to maximize active, hands-on in-session learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Residents received all handouts by email one week before sessions and were required to preview them, so limited in-person time could go to case-based discussion, calculations practice, and simulation. Respondents explicitly suggested to "Move factual and preparatory learning to completion prior to sessions (e.g., review handouts, complete problems and calculations) to increase active, hands-on learning during the 1-hour, in-person sessions." The authors also condensed lecture time in later iterations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Handouts distributed and reviewed before each session
- Faculty notes and guides prepared for each session
#### Constraints
- If presession review is not feasible, the authors suggest expanding to four sessions with an additional didactic session

### Target Learners
- residents in small-group preparatory curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- active application of factual content during limited in-person session time
