# Customize interventions to students' unique motivational and task-behavioral patterns

## Description
The article's Educational Impact and Implications Statement recommends that educators and policy makers use knowledge of distinct motivational profiles to design support. It states that "By customizing interventions to the unique motivational and task-behavioral pat- terns, it is possible to create more effective educational strategies that better address students 'speci ﬁc needs and promote academic success." The strategy rests on the finding that SEN students are distributed across all four profiles, so support should be tailored to profile membership rather than SEN status alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of students' motivational profiles (e.g., via person-centered assessment of action-control beliefs) and task-behavior data such as log-file time on task
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as an implication of an observational profile study; it did not test any intervention experimentally

### Target Learners
- students with diverse motivational profiles, including students with special educational needs in secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- academic success through support matched to motivational and task-behavioral patterns
