# Structure placements long enough for trainees to reach the taking-control stage, and minimise adaptation and box-checking phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends designing placement duration and EPA implementation so trainees can gain and maintain control of their learning. It states that "placements need to be at least long enough to enable trainees to gain and maintain control of learning" and that "Strategies should aim to minimize the duration of the ‘adaptation’ and ‘checking the boxes’ phases", since EPAs prove most beneficial once trainees transition out of adaptation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Placements must allow sufficient time to transition from the adaptation phase to the taking-control phase
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single-case qualitative study of GP-training placements
