# Deliver Indigenous astronomy integration through educator training, shared assets, and co-presented live programming

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article enumerates the collaboration's concrete deliverables as a replicable recipe: weekly training meetings serving as "conversational trainings" and a project forum; adding selected Indigenous astronomy content to the bi-monthly Star Map Booklet in two 2020 issues; a virtual thirty-minute family-audience live show co-presented with the Indigenous consultant; and a written framework document. Assets such as "recorded stories and constellation visuals" are built so educators can meaningfully integrate Indigenous star knowledge into planetarium shows, star maps, digital programs, star parties, and blog posts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An Indigenous consultant or collaborator to guide asset development, co-present programming, and supervise educator practice
#### Constraints
- The project timeline was three summer months and the COVID-19 pandemic required meetings and work to be done remotely

### Target Learners
- museum astronomy educators
- family audiences at planetarium shows

### Target Learning Goals
- educator fluency in Indigenous star knowledge
- public engagement with Dakota and Ojibwe astronomy
