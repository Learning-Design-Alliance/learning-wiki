# Arrange popular-science articles into three difficulty levels matched to readers' background

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project tags each article with a difficulty marker so readers can select material matched to their background: "The articles are arranged into three levels of diﬃculty (+: easy, ++: moderate, +++: advanced) which correspond to the background required." This lets the same corpus serve a broad audience range, from young students to experienced adults, without separate publications for each group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- authors willing to edit and rate each article's difficulty consistently
#### Constraints
- difficulty ratings correspond to the background knowledge required and were not validated against reader testing

### Target Learners
- young students
- experienced adults
- general public with varied physics background

### Target Learning Goals
- accessing accurate astrophysics content at an appropriate difficulty level
