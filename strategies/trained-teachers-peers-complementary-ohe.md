# Train teachers and peer leaders as complementary educators who can periodically repeat and reinforce health education messages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that trained teachers and peers serve as complementary deliverers of repeated and reinforced oral health education, acting as "all-time available experts in the school system to periodically repeat and reinforce OHE messages." This strategy leverages the sustained presence of teachers and peers in schools to provide ongoing reinforcement without requiring dentist involvement for every session, enhancing cost-effectiveness, sustainability, and availability of school-based health education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and peer leaders must be trained before delivering OHE
#### Constraints
- One-time teacher-led OHE was ineffective; the strategy works only when combined with repetition and reinforcement over time

### Target Learners
- adolescents aged 10-11 years in school settings

### Target Learning Goals
- oral health knowledge
- oral health behavior
