# Use a training video adapted from military source material before simulated WFWB procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An educational video was created based on the checklist, adapted from a previous training video by the Army's Institute of Surgical Research, and shown to participants before the simulated procedure. Participants then answered questions related to indications and contraindications for blood transfusion as discussed within the video, providing standardized preparation before hands-on assessment.
