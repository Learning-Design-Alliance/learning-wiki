# Provide organizational structures and opportunities for developing mentors' interprofessional mentoring competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's implication states that to ensure high-quality interprofessional clinical practice, "special attention should be given to mentors' interprofessional mentoring competence, and a range of opportunities and organizational structures should be provided for competence development." This directs healthcare organizations and educators to build mentor education and support structures. The strategy targets the preparation and development competency area of the framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational structures and opportunities for mentors' competence development
- Trained and resourced coordinators and manager support
#### Constraints
- Some mentors find interprofessional mentoring challenging

### Target Learners
- undergraduate healthcare, social care and medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- interprofessional mentoring competence
- interprofessional collaborative practice
