# Embed climate science lessons and advocacy practice in Astro 101 courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because roughly a quarter million students enroll in introductory astronomy each year and for many it is their last formal science education, the article recommends that faculty devote more lessons to topics like the greenhouse effect, assign readings, invite climate scientists and activists as guest speakers, and have students "practice advocacy skills by writing letters to 'someone in a position of power.'" This moves away from doom-and-gloom lessons and can empower students to take action.
