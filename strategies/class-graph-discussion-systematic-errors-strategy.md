# Conclude the lab with a whole-class graph comparison to launch discussion of systematic errors and experimental design

## Description
During the lab the instructor introduces the standard deviation and standard deviation of the mean as measures of statistical uncertainty; at the conclusion the class is shown a graph like Figure 1, which "serves as the basis for a discussion of systematic errors and experimental design". The juxtaposition of the three methods' data points makes the random-versus-systematic distinction visible.
