# Use lines of simultaneity in worldline plots when teaching the twin paradox

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend supplementing standard twin-paradox lessons with lines of simultaneity drawn on worldline plots: "we encourage the usage of lines of simultaneity in worldline plots". These lines, obtained by declaring t′ in the standard Lorentz transformation, show students that the time difference between the twins accumulates continuously rather than occurring entirely at the turnaround. The strategy directly targets the frame-swapping argument that can mislead students.
