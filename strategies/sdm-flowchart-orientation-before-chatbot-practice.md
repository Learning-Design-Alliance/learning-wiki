# Introduce SDM training with an explanatory flowchart of SDM steps before chatbot practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before the chatbot interaction, the study introduced participants to an explanatory flowchart illustrating 6 steps of SDM "to ensure a basic level of SDM knowledge across all participants." This orientation step standardizes prior knowledge so that differences in chatbot performance reflect practice rather than unequal familiarity with SDM models. A majority of participants (66.67%, 38/57) later agreed the flowchart would help them apply SDM more often in the future.
