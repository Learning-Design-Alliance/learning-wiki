# Provide tailored teacher training on communication-course learning methods and outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because some students perceived that certain clinical teachers prioritise biomedical knowledge over communication skills, the authors recommend: "Providing tailored training for teachers regarding the learning methods and the desired outcomes of communication courses is essential." This addresses criticism that simulations focused too much on biomedicine rather than communication, and supports teachers in emphasising communication skills during simulations and debriefings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical teachers willing to emphasise communication skills rather than clinical aspects during simulations
#### Constraints
- The recommendation responds to a minority critical viewpoint within one qualitative dataset

### Target Learners
- clinical teachers facilitating communication skills simulations

### Target Learning Goals
- facilitating communication skills debriefings
- valuing communication skills alongside biomedical knowledge
