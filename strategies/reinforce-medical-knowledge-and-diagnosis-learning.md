# Reinforce medical and psychological knowledge and occupational diagnosis learning in OT curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students identify performance problems well but perform poorly on symptoms/signs and diagnosis synthesis, the authors recommend curricular changes: "the modification of the curricula contents to promote the knowledge related to diagnostic reasoning," consolidation of skills to analyse and synthesize assessment information, and promotion of learning an occupational therapy diagnosis scheme. They specifically "advocate for reinforcing the acquisition of knowledge related to medical and psychological conditions" while strengthening practice models, arguing that medical and psychological knowledge assists in elaborating an occupational therapy diagnosis.
