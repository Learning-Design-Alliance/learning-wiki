# Stage ECEC physical activity policy implementation beginning with strategies rated easy and highly influential

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends sequencing implementation so services start with strategies that are "easy and more influential", building momentum before tackling harder longer-term strategies such as environmental upgrades. Experts identified seven such best-bet strategies, which the authors gave priority in the policy template.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of which candidate strategies experts rate as both easy to implement and highly influential
#### Constraints
- Services face existing workloads, competing priorities, administrative and reporting requirements and crowded curricula when implementing new policies

### Target Learners
- ECEC service managers, supervisors and educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Successful implementation of physical activity policy practices in ECEC settings
