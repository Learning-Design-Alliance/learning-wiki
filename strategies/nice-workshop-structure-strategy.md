# Structure teacher professional development workshops as short, off-site events with grouped collaboration and iterative feedback rounds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The workshop design that the article reports as successful is specific and implementable: hold a four-day event that is "away from school, short, and in early summer" so it is non-intrusive on school or holiday time; seat teachers in focus-area groups each partnered with a network scientist; blend lectures, discussion groups and hands-on sessions; and run repeated group presentations with feedback from scientists and peers so ideas are fully developed before resource completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Provide pre- and post-workshop support from organisers
- Provide a small stipend to remunerate teachers for their significant efforts
- Include pre-workshop materials, such as short online videos introducing network science
#### Constraints
- Locations were chosen to ensure the organisers' ability to provide adequate pre- and post-workshop support

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers of all grade levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning network science concepts and designing network-science lesson plans
