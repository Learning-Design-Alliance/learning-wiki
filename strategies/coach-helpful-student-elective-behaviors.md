# Coach students toward host-valued behaviors: humility, persistence, independence, and community immersion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on Table 1, the article recommends preparing students so they are, in the hosts' summary words, "humble, thoughtful students whose attitude is one of wanting to learn." Helpful students are "Politely persistent" in correspondence, "Prepared to identify needs and help out with anything that is needed – from entering data to washing coffee cups," work independently while knowing when to ask for help, and immerse in local community and culture. The article states the commitment "to learning before, during, and after the experience abroad is crucial."
