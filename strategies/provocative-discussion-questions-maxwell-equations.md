# Pose provocative open discussion questions about Maxwell's equations to stimulate the search for correct answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a series of invitations to discuss issues of interest through questions tied to specific Maxwell equations, such as whether in Gauss's Law the field E is attributable to the charge q, and whether the phrase "the field associated to a charge" is better than "the field produced by a charge". The authors state that the search for a correct answer can lead students toward Quantum Electrodynamics, and that class discussion of the proposed questions will probably promote research and deeper learning. The questions are designed for use within an electromagnetism course alongside historical quotes from Einstein and Bohr.

## Design Implications

### Target Learning Goals

- understanding of causality and field structure
