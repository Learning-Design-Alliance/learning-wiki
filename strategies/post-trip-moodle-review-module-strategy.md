# Add a post-trip Moodle review module (atlas, photo sharing, and drag-and-drop self-test exercises) after an experiential field trip

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to integrate an online review module before and after an experiential field trip. The Moodle course site was divided into five parts: "course review", "warm-up test (baseline test)", "atlas of herbal plants", "field trip herbal photo sharing", and "herbal plant identification exercise" with drag-and-drop exercises. Students review photos and self-test at any time over a 6-day period, on computer or mobile device, while teachers monitor activity logs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning management system such as Moodle accessible via computer or mobile device
- documented photos from previous field trips as content
- teacher and technical-staff review of the course site for feasibility and accessibility
#### Constraints
- Picture-based e-learning "allows visual recognition only" and cannot replace on-site multi-sensory identification

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Chinese medicine students

### Target Learning Goals
- recognition and identification of medicinal herbs
