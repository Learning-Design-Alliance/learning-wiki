# Use scaffolding techniques to help students complete tasks within their zones of proximal development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents scaffolding as the primary classroom application of Vygotsky's theory, citing Glasser's recommendation to "use scaffolding to assist students with completing tasks within their Zones of Proximal Development." Scaffolding is defined as a variety of techniques used to help students accomplish challenging tasks in instructional contexts, including asking productive questions, keeping students motivated, giving frequent feedback about progress, and helping students develop a plan for a new task.
