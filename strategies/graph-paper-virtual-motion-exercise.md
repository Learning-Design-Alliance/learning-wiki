# Graph-paper small-angle exercise to build the idea of virtual motion and virtual speed

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-step classroom strategy for the principle of virtual velocities. First, students draw the lever arm's position for progressively smaller angles on graph paper and observe, possibly using a smartphone photo enlarged, that the arc becomes a straight line, introducing "small angles". Second, the virtual speed is defined as the mean speed on the straightened arc, and similar right triangles yield the law m1∆v1 = m2∆v2 by elementary algebra.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
