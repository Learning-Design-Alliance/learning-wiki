# Adopt DPBL as an alternative teaching approach in higher education courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends DPBL as an alternative to pure direct instruction in higher education: "Therefore, DPBL approach was suggested as an alternative approach for teaching process." Implementation follows the three-phase sequence, blending explicit lecturer explanation with group problem identification, real-world problem solving, and a closing summary. The article states "this model was simple and easy to be implemented in the classroom."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers trained to run the three phases including problem presentation, group monitoring, and summarising
- Class organisation that supports small groups of five to six students
#### Constraints
- Results cannot be generalised to other settings because the study used third-year Computer Networks students from two departments in one Indonesian university

### Target Learners
- third-year university students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge
- creative thinking skills
- teamwork skills
