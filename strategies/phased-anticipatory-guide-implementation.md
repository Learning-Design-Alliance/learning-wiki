# Implement anticipatory reading guides through a phased release: teacher modeling, collaborative creation, then independent guide construction.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a gradual release sequence for introducing anticipatory reading guides. Students were "first taught the strategy of using anticipatory study guides through a teacher modelling phase," then co-constructed guide elements with the teacher, and finally created their own guides from passage questions independently. By the study's end, students could preview questions to build their own guides without teacher support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Six weeks of daily instruction with passages at students' instructional reading level
#### Constraints
- Limited practice time may prevent students reaching full mastery of independent guide creation

### Target Learners
- Grade three struggling readers in inclusion classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent comprehension strategy use and transfer across content areas
