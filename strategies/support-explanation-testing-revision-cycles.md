# Support interactional cycles that integrate explanation, testing, and revision during collaborative AI system design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's design recommendation from its conclusion: instructors and environment designers should structure collaborative AI development so that explaining reasoning, testing the system, and revising designs recur as integrated cycles rather than isolated or linear stages. The authors write that "supporting interactional cycles that integrate explanation, testing, and revision may foster more coordinated collaborative AI system design and development". This follows from the finding that higher-quality chatbots were associated with more integrated sequences linking these activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A collaborative, design-oriented AI development activity in which learners can explain ideas, test system output, and revise designs, such as pair-programmed chatbot building
#### Constraints
- The underlying associations are correlational and come from middle school students in a single chatbot design activity, so generalizability to other ages, contexts, or AI tools is limited

### Target Learners
- middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative AI system design
- AI knowledge
- chatbot development quality
