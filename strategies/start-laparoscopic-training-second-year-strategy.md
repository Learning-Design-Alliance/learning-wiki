# Start laparoscopic simulator training during the second year of medical school

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that second-year undergraduates were the only variable significantly associated with skill-acquisition improvement, the authors recommend that laparoscopic simulator training begin "during the second year of Medical school". The strategy positions systematic simulator training as beneficial for all students regardless of innate factors, with second year as the more advantageous starting point.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a validated box-simulator curriculum with FLS-style scored tasks and expert reference scores.
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single institution's 68-student cohort with a short training period; the authors note the small sample as a limitation.

### Target Learners
- Medical students in the early preclinical years

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition of basic laparoscopic surgical skills
