# Use a two-round modified Delphi process with specialty Scientific Committees and a predefined consensus threshold to build national competency catalogs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's method pairs preliminary catalogs developed by specialty Scientific Committees with a centrally coordinated, anonymous two-round modified Delphi process using a five-point Likert scale and a predefined consensus threshold of ≥75% combined agreement. This strategy harmonizes terminology across specialties while preserving specialty-specific content and incorporates participant-suggested competencies in the second round. It produced a national catalog spanning anesthesiology and 14 surgical specialties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination by a national body with a database of board-certified specialists for recruitment and electronic questionnaire distribution
#### Constraints
- The modified Delphi method "provides evidence of content validity through structured consensus rather than psychometric validation or direct evidence of educational effectiveness"

### Target Learners
- graduating medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- definition of minimum expected psychomotor competence levels at graduation
