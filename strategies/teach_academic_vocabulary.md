# Teach Academic Vocabulary

## Description
Provide explicit instruction on academic vocabulary that will be encountered during the project. Knowing these words ahead of time can help you plan instruction for teaching them to students inside the project. While all students will need this scaffolding, ELL students may require more targeted instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of key vocabulary and explicit instructional strategies.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming and require careful selection of relevant terms.
