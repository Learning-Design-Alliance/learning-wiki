Use AICO_edu in teacher training to identify professional learning needs¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that school leaders and educators use the instrument in teacher training contexts. "School leaders and educators should use it in teacher training to collect data about the perceived AI competence of in-service and pre-service teachers." Assessment results can inform organizational decisions about further teacher training possibilities and shape educational programs at universities and policy levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The instrument should be used where its purpose is to assess perceived, not actual, AI competence

Constraints¶

  • The authors note that at the time of data collection, no AI tool was approved for teaching and learning at German schools, limiting practical measurement of AI usage

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-service and in-service vocational school teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Identifying teachers' readiness for AI-supported teaching and informing professional development programs

Claims¶

  • AI competence dimensions are unequally developed among vocational school teachers [+W]
  • PF and AT scales carry a bidirectional interpretation problem for AI competence [-W]
