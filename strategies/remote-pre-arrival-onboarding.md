# Remote pre-arrival onboarding: complete visa paperwork, onboarding and computing-domain access from Italy before arrival

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before travel, students access the FermiWorks website from Italy to complete an Onboarding process and fill a New Hire document, and follow the rules for accessing the FERMI computing domain remotely so they can "gain access to the domain immediately upon arrival at the lab". This reduces administrative friction during the short two-month stay. CAIF members also support housing on arrival and an International Orientation session on the first two days, with a twin concluding session before departure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must accept the job offer and be responsible for obtaining their J1 Visa; the Fermilab Personnel Office and Visa Office process the paperwork
#### Constraints
- The program does not cover the round trip journey to the US or health insurance; the weekly salary is expected to cover these costs

### Target Learners
- International summer interns at a US national laboratory

### Target Learning Goals
- Rapid integration into lab research groups within a two-month training stay
