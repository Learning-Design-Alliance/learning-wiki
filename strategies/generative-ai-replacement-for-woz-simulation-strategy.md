# Replace Wizard of Oz facilitation with generative AI chatbots and automated AI-driven assessment in future simulator iterations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose that the next VITAL-COMS iteration be driven by generative AI: "generative AI chatbots, which now offer real-time natural language interaction and feedback capabilities" present "a promising alternative to the WoZ approach, potentially enabling broader and more scalable training delivery," and the feasibility of automated AI-driven assessment should be explored to address self-report limitations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Generative AI capable of real-time natural language interaction and feedback with virtual patient characters
#### Constraints
- The study tested only the WoZ prototype; AI-driven delivery and assessment remain untested proposals

### Target Learners
- Health care professionals across different settings and professional groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Scalable training in sensitive weight-related communication with automated assessment
