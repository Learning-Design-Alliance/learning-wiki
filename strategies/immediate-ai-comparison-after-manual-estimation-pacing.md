# Manage pacing within a 40-minute period so AI comparison immediately follows manual estimation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For replication of the lesson design, the article recommends that "Within a standard 40-minute instructional period, the temporal allocation should be carefully managed to maintain the cognitive coherence of each phase": 15 minutes of Mathematical Inquiry, 8 minutes of AI Prediction Comparison, and 5 minutes of Reflection on Prediction Failures. The AI prediction process should be introduced immediately upon completion of manual estimations to maximize cognitive dissonance and highlight the structural correspondence, while the final segment provides space for spontaneous deliberation on the boundary conditions of predictive models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must first internalize visual trend operations during the Mathematical Inquiry segment
#### Constraints
- Temporal allocations are specified for a standard 40-minute instructional period

### Target Learners
- Grade 4-5 elementary mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- activating awareness of structural correspondence between human estimation and AI prediction
