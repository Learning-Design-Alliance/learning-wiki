# Provide three kinds of feedback during simulation-based skill acquisition: automatic simulator feedback, instructor feedback during training, and post-session debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To foster motivation during skill acquisition in simulation training, the article recommends feedback from three sources: "automatically and immediately by the simulator itself", verbal technical and pedagogical support from the instructor during training, and debriefing by the instructor and team members after sessions. All kinds of feedback, the authors write, "can offer opportunities to tailor the trainee's abilities and feelings during the process of skill acquisition".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
