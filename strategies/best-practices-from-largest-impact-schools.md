# Identify potential best practices by comparing practices of the highest-impact schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's method for surfacing candidate best practices is comparative: it examines leadership practices across KIPP schools and regions and singles out the practices used where impacts were largest. As the article states, it examines practices "to identify potential best practices—the practices employed by the KIPP schools and regions with the largest impacts."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Impact estimates for the schools or regions being compared, so that high-impact sites can be identified
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as identifying potential best practices, not validated causal practices

### Target Learners
- Charter school network leaders and evaluators studying school leadership

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying leadership practices associated with the largest school impacts
