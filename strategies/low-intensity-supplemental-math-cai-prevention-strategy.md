# Use adaptive math CAI as a low-intensity, teacher-free supplemental prevention program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3`–`q4` · 3 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends supplemental use of Building Blocks Software as a prevention program for children at risk of school failure. It notes that "supplemental use of Building Blocks Software does not require teachers, does not interrupt children's regular mathematics classroom instruction, and does not interfere with other academic programming", making it implementable alongside existing programming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual computer time (90 minutes weekly in the study) scheduled in an ancillary block with adult supervision for behavior and technical support
#### Constraints
- The design does not permit statements concerning the merit of Building Blocks Software relative to other supplemental mathematics programs

### Target Learners
- monolingual English-speaking children from low-income and ethnic minority backgrounds in kindergarten

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing mathematical competencies and decreasing risk for school failure
