# Supplement large active-learning modules with breakout/tutorial sessions, interactive online activities and peer assessment

## Description
The authors propose concrete remedies for the improvement areas students identified: more contact and discussion opportunities, technology-mediated interaction, supplementary online material, and peer assessment. They write "we propose more breakout or tutorial sessions for students to discuss their problems" and plan "to trial the use of technology, such as Piazza to enable greater interaction with students".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated virtual learning environment (Moodle) on which supplementary online and interactive learning activities can be shared
- Instructor capacity to run additional breakout or tutorial sessions
#### Constraints
- The article frames these as future trials and proposals, not as tested interventions

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate engineering students in large-cohort modules

### Target Learning Goals
