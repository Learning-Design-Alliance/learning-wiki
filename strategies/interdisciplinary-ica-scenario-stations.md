# Interdisciplinary scenario stations combining 3D models, CT-A interpretation, and expert-led clinical discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each 30-minute station presented one ICA pattern case with textual information, CT-A imaging, and a 1:1 scale solid anatomical simulator for hands-on examination. Experts in anatomy, cardiology, and cardiovascular surgery guided discussion and answered student questions. The article describes "multiple integrated educational components, including interdisciplinary expert interaction, active learning activities, peer discussion, CT-A image interpretation, hands-on examination of tactile 3D vascular models, and clinically oriented reasoning exercises".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to clinicians from multiple specialties, patient-specific 3D printed models, CT-A images, and structured case scenarios developed collaboratively by experts
#### Constraints
- The article states the observed outcomes should be interpreted as the combined effect of a multifaceted instructional framework rather than the isolated contribution of the 3D simulator alone

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning, diagnostic interpretation of vascular patterns, and connecting anatomical knowledge to clinical application
