# Two-stage evidence-building: document scan followed by targeted state interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study builds evidence in stages: first scanning available data, literature, and recent studies to produce this overview, then conducting interviews with staff and partners from a subset of states chosen using the report. The authors state, "To inform this report, we scanned available apprenticeship data, as well as literature and insights from recent studies (conducted within the past four years)." Interviews in 2023 will "deepen understanding of state apprenticeship system capacity".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires available apprenticeship data and recent studies (within the past four years) for the initial scan
- Requires selection of a subset of states for follow-up interviews
#### Constraints
- Interview data collection was planned for 2023, after this report

### Target Learners
- state apprenticeship agency staff and partners

### Target Learning Goals
- building evidence on state apprenticeship system capacity
