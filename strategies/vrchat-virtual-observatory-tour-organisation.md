# Organising effective virtual observatory tours in VR social platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors distil practical recommendations from the tour: the quality of the virtual model is essential because recreating every detail increases felt reality and impression; collaborate with skilled modellers and domain experts; share the same virtual space to enhance communication; and provide technical support members familiar with VR. Streaming the event to YouTube alongside the VRChat tour extends reach to audiences without HMDs, at some cost in immersion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation with those who have high modelling skills and who can explain them properly
- A guide who is an expert, since 43 of 44 respondents selected 'because an expert will give a talk' as a participation reason
- Support members familiar with the VR environment to handle glitches
#### Constraints
- Limitation on the number of participants in one event due to server load; some people could not enter the world due to heavy access
- Some participants suffered technical glitches with VR instruments
- HMD equipment costs more than 2000 US dollars, limiting wide participation

### Target Learners
- Non-specialist public
- Astronomy outreach audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- Experiencing the atmosphere of cutting-edge astronomy facilities
- Gaining a deeper understanding of inaccessible astronomical facilities
