# Graphic Organizers and Visual Aids (Attention)

## Description
Using graphic organizers and visual aids to help students identify the most important information and focus on it. This involves tools like mind maps, charts, and diagrams that present information in a structured, visual format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Graphic organizer templates, visual aids, and instructor guidance on how to use them effectively
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of content, requires careful design to avoid overwhelming students, and may not address underlying attentional challenges

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students who need help focusing on key information and filtering out distractions

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Highlight essential information, improve focus, and support selective attention

### Affordances
- Presents information in a clear, structured format, reduces cognitive load, supports visual learners, and aids in comprehension

### Personalization
- Can be customized based on student needs and subject matter, with options for color-coding, labeling, and adding visual cues.
