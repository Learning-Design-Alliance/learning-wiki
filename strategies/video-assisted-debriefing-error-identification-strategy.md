# Use video recordings during debriefing to help students identify their own errors and support reflective learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy involves recording the learner's simulation performance and reviewing the video during debriefing so the learner can pinpoint specific mistakes. The article states that "video recordings were used as reflective tools to evaluate students' performance" and that "The video review enabled students to identify individual errors in blood loss estimation and adherence to the emergency bleeding protocol, thereby enhancing self‐awareness and facilitating the development of clinical skills." It positions video review as moving students from passive observation to active cognitive engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Camera system for recording scenarios and consent from participants for video recording
- Instructors expert in obstetric emergencies to conduct the debriefing
#### Constraints
- Requires written informed consent specifically agreeing to video recording for educational and feedback purposes

### Target Learners
- midwifery students
- nursing students in critical care preparation

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate blood loss estimation
- adherence to emergency bleeding protocols
- self-assessment and self-regulation
