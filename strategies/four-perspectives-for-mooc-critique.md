## Description
The editorial organizes the special section's responses to Loizzo and Ertmer (2016) into four perspectives on MOOC improvement. Paek elaborates the value of social learning democracy and proposes that "shifting to digital" is more about students controlling their learning environment; Akinkuolie & Shortt offer design suggestions based on six online learning culture themes; Hu & Spiro apply Cognitive Flexibility Theory; and Whitworth examines teacher and student practices.
