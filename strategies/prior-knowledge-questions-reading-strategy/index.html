Strategies

Use prior knowledge questions before, during and after reading comprehension passages¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete teaching routine in which the teacher opens reading activities with what pupils already know from home, community and school, then introduces questions such as "What does the title of the passage suggest?" and "Does it remind you of a similar experience?" The teacher designs activities meaningful in terms of local community norms, learns those norms by talking to parents and community members, and helps pupils connect and apply learning to home and community. The experimental group received this treatment for eight weeks and outperformed the control group.

Requirements¶

  • Teachers should construct questions that assist pupils in using their relevant schemata
  • Teachers should encourage pupils to participate actively by responding positively to the questions

Constraints¶
