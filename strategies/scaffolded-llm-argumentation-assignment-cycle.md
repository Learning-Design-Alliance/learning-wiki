# Scaffolded LLM-collaboration assignment cycle: draft, LLM critique, then rebut or incorporate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A repeatable classroom assignment cycle in which students first write an argumentative essay defending a position, then ask an LLM chatbot of their choice to critique the argument as thoroughly as possible, then respond by either rebutting the LLM's arguments or updating their original argument. The article reports this cycle was assigned five times in one semester, with prompt-engineering quality graded and a Guidelines for Prompt Engineering document provided at the start.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need guidance on effective prompting, such as the two-page Guidelines for Prompt Engineering document the teaching team provided; grading must reward quality of response to the LLM's critique
#### Constraints
- The article reports the study was exploratory and could not isolate the causal effects of the intervention; assignments were graded by a teaching assistant who was not blind to the timing of the assignment

### Target Learners
- college students in introductory social science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- argumentative writing
- responding to critical feedback
- prompt engineering
