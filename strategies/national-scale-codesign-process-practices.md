# Co-design process practices for national-scale projects: shared goals, panel-set timelines, sub-group co-leadership, and adequate resourcing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From their reflections, the authors recommend concrete practices for large-scale co-design: co-develop an agreed shared goal and values and restate them regularly; ask panel members what involvement is feasible rather than having the research team set the timeline, which generated self-selected sub-groups working online between in-person workshops; use strong PPI involvement and co-leadership of sub-groups to support relationship building and shared decision-making. They also caution that researchers should ensure adequate staffing resources for managing large panels and the volume of qualitative data produced, and that adhering to typical research time scales is challenging.
