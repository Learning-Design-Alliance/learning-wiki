# Commit 3% of budget and 5% of employee time to education (the 3+5 proposal)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The chapter proposes a concrete resource commitment: organisations and their supporting companies "could commit to 3% of their budget to education and 5% of any employee's time to work on the organisation's educational programs". The 3% of core budget funds education professionals and their direct resources; the 5% of time lets motivated employees spend "one full day a month preparing and actually working with schools or teachers" under the supervision of those professionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- If these 3 + 5 programs are implemented, they need to run long-term, and not be subject to budget cuts or attempts to subvert it through merging of budgets
#### Constraints
- Not included in this 3% would be money people or time for organising, creating, and supporting booths, meetings and conferences; town halls; press releases; social media production; graphics production; web site improvements; working with film crews; arranging interviews with media; rotary club talks; or for answering communications inquiries.

### Target Learners
- students in high-need elementary public schools and their teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- organised, supervised science education service to local schools
