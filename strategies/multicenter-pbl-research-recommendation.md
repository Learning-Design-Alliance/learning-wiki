# Recommendation: evaluate PBL effectiveness through multicenter primary studies comparing educational centers, cultures, and psychological adaptation factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that future primary research on PBL be conducted "at several different educational centers" so researchers can compare between centers and evaluate geographic, cultural, and technological factors that may support or hinder PBL implementation. They also recommend discussing psychological aspects, because first-year students may still carry learning habits from high school that affect PBL implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Primary studies should span multiple educational centers to identify cumulative effects and differences between geographic areas or races
#### Constraints
- The current evidence base is limited: no multiyear studies were found, instruments could not be equated, and socio-demography could not be assessed

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking/knowledge
- problem-solving
- self-directed learning
