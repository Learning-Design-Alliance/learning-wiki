# Combine sleep hygiene education and physical activity promotion in campus student support programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that universities integrate sleep education and PA promotion into student support services and campus health programs. It states that interventions combining "sleep hygiene counseling, time management training, and accessible opportunities for moderate-to-vigorous PA" could be tested for impact on well-being and academic outcomes. This follows from the finding that both behaviors independently predict GPA.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should address both sleep and physical activity, since the study found each independently associated with GPA
#### Constraints
- Cross-sectional design precludes causal inference; longitudinal and intervention studies are needed to confirm effects

### Target Learners
- male university students in Saudi Arabia and similar settings

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance (GPA) improvement through modifiable health behaviors
