# Multi-pronged cost-mitigation strategies for deploying live-video teaching equipment in low-resource settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because "the high cost of LTM equipment limits scalability in low-income areas", the authors propose a set of implementable measures: institutions can jointly purchase or borrow hardware or choose low-resolution streaming cameras; hospitals can pursue public–private collaboration with device companies for subsidized equipment or donations; and programs can adopt free browser-based streaming tools and shared platforms such as multicenter live-streaming. These are the authors' forward-looking recommendations for scaling the model beyond their well-equipped single center.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness of multiple institutions or industry partners to share or subsidize hardware and streaming infrastructure
#### Constraints
- Recommended, not tested in this study; the trial itself used a permanently installed 4K system in a single preclinical center

### Target Learners
- Dental and medical students in institutions with limited equipment budgets

### Target Learning Goals
- Procedural skills training via live video demonstration where equipment cost is a barrier
