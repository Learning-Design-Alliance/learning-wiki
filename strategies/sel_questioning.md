# SEL Questioning

## Description
Teachers can implement simple SEL questions focused on kindess, metaphors, challenges, and support to grow students’ emotional intelligence skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- List of SEL questions, time for individual reflection and class discussion
#### Constraints
- Some students may not feel comfortable sharing their feelings in a group setting. It is important to create a safe and supportive environment where students feel comfortable sharing. Some students may not be able to answer the questions fully or accurately.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, especially K–12

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Cultivate feelings of empathy and support, facilitate self-reflection, promote emotional awareness

### Affordances
- Provides students with opportunities to reflect on their own experiences and feelings, as well as those of others. Promotes a sense of community and connection in the classroom. Normalizes talking about feelings and empowers students to view their emotions as assets.

### Personalization
- Instructors can adapt the questions to suit the needs of their students and the topics being studied in class. Students can also be encouraged to create their own questions to explore their feelings and experiences.
