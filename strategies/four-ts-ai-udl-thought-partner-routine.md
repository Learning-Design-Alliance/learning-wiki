# The Four T's: Tell, Tinker, Tweak, Transfer for using an AI UDL thought partner

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Four T's is the authors' published four-step routine for engaging with LUDIA, first released in 2023 and free to access. The educator begins by telling LUDIA about "the learners in their care, the learning plans, and the challenges", then continues the chat asking for clarification, examples, and materials, adapts the provisional outcomes to fit context, and finally reflects on the impact of identifying and reducing barriers using Visible Thinking Routines such as "I Used to Think, but Now I Think", "Connect, Extend, Challenge", or "3-2-1 Bridge". The authors stress that the first response is not where the value lies; an exchange that stops at the second T is not yet full use of LUDIA as a thought partner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The Transfer step requires deliberate reflection routines; stopping after Tinker leaves the professional learning unrealised
