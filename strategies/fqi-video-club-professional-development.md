# Use the FQI to structure educator professional development on feedback, including colleague observation and video clubs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose professional-development uses of the FQI: educators prepare for feedback encounters with it as a sensitising technique, analyse encounters afterwards to trigger self-reflection, and use its items rather than gut feels to stimulate critique. The article recommends that "Clinicians could ask a colleague to observe their feedback practice, with learner consent, or instigate a ‘video club’ in which clinicians regularly discuss their own feedback practice videos". Planned exemplar videos and a possible mini-FQI would support adoption.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

#### Constraints
