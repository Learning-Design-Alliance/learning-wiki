# Use AI automated feedback tools to foster students' dispositions for partnering with teachers around feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends positioning AI automated feedback as part of developing the student side of feedback partnerships: students should build the dispositions needed to engage meaningfully with their teachers in relation to feedback. The authors frame this as part of what AI for teaching and learning should promote, so that automated feedback experiences are connected to, rather than substituted for, the teacher-student feedback relationship.
