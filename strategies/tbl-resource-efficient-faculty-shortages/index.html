Choose TBL over PBL when faculty resources are constrained¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In discussing resource allocation, the authors note PBL "often requires a high faculty-to-student ratio to facilitate small group sessions effectively", whereas "TBL allows a single expert to manage multiple teams within a large classroom setting, making it a more resource-efficient strategy for programs facing faculty shortages." This positions TBL as the resource-efficient choice where facilitation capacity is limited.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • TBL's single-expert, multi-team classroom format must be feasible for the cohort size

Constraints¶

  • Resource-efficiency argument is stated in the discussion as an implementation consideration, not tested as an outcome in the meta-analysis

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate nursing students in large-enrollment programs

  • problem-solving abilities

  • PBL and TBL significantly improve nursing students' self-directed learning [+W]
