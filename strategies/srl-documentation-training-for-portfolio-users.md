# Train trainees, teachers and supervisors in documenting SRL, not only in the technical and assessment aspects of an e-portfolio

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the studied e-portfolio was implemented with instructions on technical and assessment functions but none on documenting SRL, the authors recommend explicit SRL training for all portfolio users. "trainees, teachers and supervisors not only need to be supported in the technical aspects of a portfolio, but also need training concerning (documentation of) SRL, e.g. on effective reflection, feedback and goal-setting". This rests on research showing teacher/supervisor support is important for SRL development and that teachers often do not know how to support SRL effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must cover effective reflection, feedback and goal-setting, since documentation of SRL asks for a high level of introspection and writing skills and requires time
#### Constraints
- The clinical setting's time pressure and unpredictability may leave trainees without the time required to appropriately document learning processes

### Target Learners
- GP trainees, their teachers and supervisors in workplace-based medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- Documentation of self-regulated learning processes in e-portfolios
