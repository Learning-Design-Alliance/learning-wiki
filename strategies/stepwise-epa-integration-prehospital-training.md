# Stepwise integration of EPAs into prehospital emergency medicine training incorporating pediatric-specific EPAs, simulation-based refresher courses, and EPA-based assessment tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address barriers including limited training resources, time constraints, and the absence of standardized national curricula, the authors propose a stepwise integration of EPAs into prehospital emergency medicine training in Germany. This includes the incorporation of pediatric-specific EPAs into existing certification programs, development of mandatory simulation-based refresher courses focusing on high-acuity low-frequency scenarios, and use of EPA-based assessment tools to guide formative feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combination of clinical exposure, structured training, and simulation-based education to develop and assess EPA-related competencies
#### Constraints
- Several barriers may limit implementation in routine practice, including limited training resources, time constraints within existing EMS training programs, and the absence of standardized national curricula

### Target Learners
- EMS physicians in training and certification programs in Germany

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustain EPA-related competencies despite inherently low case volume of critical pediatric emergencies
