# Use evaluation to drive a cycle of improvement on incentive design and implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's third key question asks "How can evaluation promote a cycle of improvement on the incentive design and implementation?" It recommends that schools treat evaluation as an ongoing tool that feeds findings back into revising attendance incentives, rather than evaluating only once after implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must conduct evaluations of their incentive programs and feed results back into design decisions
#### Constraints
- Applied in contexts where schools are implementing incentive-based attendance interventions under accountability pressure

### Target Learners
- students from pre-K to grade 12

### Target Learning Goals
- improving student attendance
