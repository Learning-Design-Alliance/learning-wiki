# Targeted CME modules, standardized electronic follow-up templates, and stronger pediatric-endocrinology referral links to close the GDM knowledge-practice gap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a set of system-level interventions to translate neonatologists' high GDM knowledge into consistent clinical practice. They propose "Targeted continuing-medical-education modules standardized electronic follow-up templates, and stronger referral links with pediatric endocrinology" to bridge the knowledge-practice divide and realize national policy intent. The recommendation targets the specific deficits observed in the survey: suboptimal periodic evaluations and specialist referrals, and knowledge gaps on lipid and epigenetic sequelae.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires continuing-medical-education infrastructure and electronic follow-up systems within neonatal departments
#### Constraints
- The authors note barriers including limited subspecialist availability, absent national follow-up templates, and high patient-to-clinician ratios

### Target Learners
- Practicing neonatologists in Chinese hospitals, especially community and private settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of less-recognized long-term GDM complications (lipid abnormalities, epigenetic mechanisms) and guideline-concordant follow-up and referral practices
