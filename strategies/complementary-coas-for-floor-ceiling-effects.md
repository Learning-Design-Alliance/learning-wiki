# Use complementary alternative assessments when a standard SMA COA shows a suspected floor or ceiling effect

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends switching to or adding alternative assessments when standard SMA outcome measures no longer reflect functional ability. When a CHOP-INTEND ceiling effect is suspected, complementary COAs such as AIMS, PDMS-2, and BSID-III are appropriate; for RULM floor effects, ATEND 3.0 is recommended, and for RULM ceiling effects the Box and Block and Nine-Hole Peg tests; for HFMSE floor effects, ATEND 3.0, and for HFMSE ceiling effects, the 6MWT, 10MWT, TUG, TTR, PDMS-2 and BSID-III if age-appropriate. Transitioning from CHOP-INTEND to HFMSE was identified as appropriate once children sit independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluator familiarity with the alternative instruments; age-appropriateness checks for tools such as PDMS-2 and BSID-III
#### Constraints
- AIMS and PDMS-2 have robust psychometric support in general pediatric motor assessment, but validation in treated SMA populations remains limited

### Target Learners
- clinical evaluators assessing individuals with SMA across the lifespan

### Target Learning Goals
- selecting responsive outcome measures that reflect functional ability in treated SMA populations
