# Develop dedicated online competency modules combined with blended learning and telehealth interactions for clinical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend moving beyond online lectures toward dedicated online modules tied to defined competencies, planned blended learning activities, and telehealth interactions to fill the clinical skills gap. They state that "Accessing telehealth interactions in compliment with e-learning modules can address the perceived gap in clinical skill teaching" and that a good mix of online and face-to-face learning is suggested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development of dedicated online modules specific to defined areas of competency along with detailed planning of blended learning activities
#### Constraints
- Experiential learning with direct patient exposure in already strained resources is significantly challenged in optometry skill training

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and postgraduate optometry students in India
- Practicing optometrists in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skills training in optometry
- Competency attainment across the optometry curriculum
