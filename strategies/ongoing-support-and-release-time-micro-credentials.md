# Provide ongoing support and school-day time for teachers completing micro-credentials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends sustained, coherent support during the school year rather than episodic professional learning: leaders met cohorts at least three or four times in person, some held monthly virtual meetings, and districts supported micro-credential work during the school day. Repurposing agreed-upon staff meeting or PD time "makes it feasible for districts to integrate micro-credentials" and signals respect for teachers' time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduled time during the work day so full cohorts can meet completion expectations
#### Constraints
- Collective bargaining agreements may not have provisions for teachers to spend time on micro-credentials beyond defined staff meetings, PD requirements, and prep times

### Target Learners
- K-12 classroom teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained professional learning and micro-credential completion
