# Use online viva voce as an alternative when assessment duration cannot be kept as short as the invigilated original

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Where the invigilated-equivalent time limit is not feasible, the article recommends replacing the online open book assessment with an oral examination. It states: "alternatives such as online viva voce through the Blackboard Collaborate Ultra should be considered for replacing the online open book assessment." This targets situations where longer durations are unavoidable, such as institutional minimum-duration policies like the 4-h/24-h rule applied after May 1, 2020 at the author's institution.

## Design Implications

### Context
