# A three-part goal-setting roadmap: collaborate on goals, establish autonomous activities, and check in regularly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends a framework for turning a chosen growth goal into action, stating that "Setting a roadmap or action plan to achieve the chosen goal helps keep students on track." It has three steps: collaborate with students to set goals they are motivated to achieve; establish concrete activities students understand and can pursue autonomously, using Student Profile or Class Profile reports to identify focus areas; and check in regularly to monitor motivation and progress. The guide notes there is no single approach appropriate for all students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to MAP Growth Student Profile or Class Profile reports to identify specific focus areas and areas of mastery
#### Constraints
- The guide states there is no set approach that is appropriate for all students

### Target Learners
- K–12 students setting MAP Growth goals

### Target Learning Goals
- Goal setting, motivation, and progress monitoring toward academic growth targets
