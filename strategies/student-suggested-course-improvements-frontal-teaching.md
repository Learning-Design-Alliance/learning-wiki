# Improvements suggested by students: add frontal teaching practice, K-12 observation, and reduce reading load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the focus group, students proposed concrete changes to increase the course's appeal. A common discouraging response was that students felt they did not really get an opportunity to teach where the word teaching referred to frontal teaching; moving around the classroom helping students work on tutorials was not perceived as teaching. Common suggestions included observing, critiquing and delivering frontal teaching, observing and critiquing K-12 teaching, and reducing the amount of reading per week, although students appreciated the readings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A follow-up class offering opportunities to observe, critique and deliver frontal teaching
- Opportunities to observe and critique K-12 teaching
#### Constraints
- Students equated teaching with frontal teaching, so scaffolded tutorial facilitation was not perceived as teaching experience despite the course's intent

### Target Learners
- science and engineering undergraduates considering K-12 teaching careers

### Target Learning Goals
- developing classroom teaching skills and interest in teaching careers
