# Run a preclass 30-minute AI literacy training covering query formulation and critical evaluation of AI output

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before the course, the PBL-DeepSeek group completed a 30-minute training session on using DeepSeek. The training covered "how to formulate effective queries" and, importantly, "how to critically evaluate AI-generated information by cross-checking it against standard ophthalmology textbooks, clinical guidelines, and instructor feedback." This is a concrete, implementable preparation step the article recommends for future AI-assisted teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short structured training session before AI-assisted coursework begins
#### Constraints
- Training was specific to the DeepSeek-R1 model and glaucoma content in this study

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective AI query formulation
- critical appraisal of AI-generated content
