# Support implementation with asynchronous onboarding plus ongoing data coaching and scripted lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program pairs digital lessons with teacher-facing supports: "a 3-hour asynchronous teacher onboarding and professional development session at no cost, monthly data coaching, bi-weekly health reports, a dedicated customer success team, and 24-hour support." Teachers also receive "comprehensive lesson plans and scripted lessons" to ensure effective instruction, with optional half-day, full-day, or two-day sessions available.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Current ELA classroom teachers, school coaches, and para-professionals as implementers
#### Constraints
- Additional professional learning sessions beyond the initial onboarding are described as available opportunities, not requirements

### Target Learners
- PK-3 classroom teachers, coaches, and para-professionals implementing the program

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective delivery of explicit, systematic phonics instruction and use of actionable student data
