# Analyze learning curves as a subtler means of measuring learning gains than pre-post comparisons

## Description
Learning curves, originating in machine learning, plot error rates in learner performance against an estimate of student knowledge level, offering "a more subtle means of measuring learning gains" than standard pre-post measures, which are "rarely significant". The curves can be used to determine whether new modifications enhance or degrade performance. Martin et al. (2005, 2011) illustrate the methodology and discuss performance curves in both formative and summative studies.
