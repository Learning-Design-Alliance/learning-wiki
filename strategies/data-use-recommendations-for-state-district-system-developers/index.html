Offer data-use recommendations and strategies targeted to state officials, district-level staff, and system developers¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief directs its guidance at specific role groups across the education system. It states that the series "offers recommendations and strategies for state officials, district-level staff, and system developers who seek to promote effective data use by decision makers and educators at any level of the education system", so implementers should tailor data-use supports to these distinct audiences rather than a single generic audience.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Recommendations must reach state officials, district-level staff, and system developers who can promote effective data use
  • Recommendations must be tailored to the roles of state officials, district-level staff, and system developers.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • State officials, district-level staff, and system developers
  • district-level staff
  • system developers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • promoting effective data use by decision makers and educators

Related Strategies¶

  • Target data-system guidance separately to state officials, district-level staff, and system developers
  • Provide strategies and supports for improving automated writing feedback tools to meet teacher and student needs
  • Offer full-group or small-group follow-up sessions for staff needing aid in implementing the learning system model
  • Increase the supply of high quality systematic reviews so decision makers have more useful evidence summaries
