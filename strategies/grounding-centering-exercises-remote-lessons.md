# Begin remote lessons with grounding and centering exercises to help students focus

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that educators "Begin interactions and lessons with grounding and centering exercises that help students focus on what is happening in their mind, body, and/or surroundings," stating this can help students engage and stay focused. Suggested exercises include deep breathing, naming something in the environment that makes the student happy or calm, and body scans in which students silently tell tense parts to relax. Exercises can be recorded, spoken, written in instructional packets, or read aloud by an adult.
