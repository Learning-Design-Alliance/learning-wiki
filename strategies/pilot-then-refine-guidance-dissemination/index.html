Pilot guidance documents with a small set of schools and iterate before wide dissemination¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

R15CC supported NDE with an iterative process of creating implementation guidance for the MRI menu and a site visit protocol. Approximately 20 schools will pilot the MRI menu and provide feedback on their experiences to help NDE revise and refine the guidance document before distributing it more widely and expanding site visits to additional schools.
