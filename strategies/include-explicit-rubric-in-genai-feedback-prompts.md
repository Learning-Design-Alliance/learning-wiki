# Include an explicit rubric in GenAI feedback prompts, even in Zero-shot conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends supplying a detailed, task-relevant rubric when prompting GenAI for feedback. Unlike prior studies that withheld rubrics, "the present study included a detailed and relevant rubric for providing feedback, even in the Zero-shot condition", which the authors credit for Zero-shot feedback matching teacher feedback quality, since referencing rubric criteria "is a key characteristic of effective feedback". The discussion frames prompt design as "a fundamental pedagogical strategy" rather than a technical adjustment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a rubric aligned with the assessment criteria for the writing task
#### Constraints
- the recommendation is grounded in this study's objective, Toulmin-based quality assessment and GPT-4o; the authors note earlier studies with other models and subjective criteria favoured teacher feedback

### Target Learners
- higher education instructors and researchers designing GenAI feedback workflows

### Target Learning Goals
- generating high-quality, criterion-referenced feedback on argumentative writing
