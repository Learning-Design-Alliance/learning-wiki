# Train teachers in computerized educational software and equip computer labs for Arabic grammar teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In light of the study's results, the researchers recommend that education stakeholders "work on the training of teachers on new methods of teaching , such as the use of computerized educational software" to help increase student motivation, and that teachers be provided with a computer lab with special applications and devices for teaching and learning Arabic grammar. This follows from the experimental group's significantly higher adjusted achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in new methods including computerized educational software
- Computer labs with special applications and devices for Arabic grammar teaching and learning
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in one intentionally selected sample from schools that already had computers and instructional means

### Target Learners
- intermediate first-grade students in Iraq

### Target Learning Goals
- Arabic language grammar achievement
- student motivation toward education

## Claims
