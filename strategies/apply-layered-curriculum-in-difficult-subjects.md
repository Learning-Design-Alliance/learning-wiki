# Apply Layered Curriculum in subjects students find difficult to learn

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
When asked in which other courses Layered Curriculum could be used, interviewed students most often named mathematics and science, language arts, and social studies, and justified this with the reason "because these lessons are hard to learn". The strategy is to target layered, choice-based activity structures at topics where students struggle, offering easy-to-difficult activity sequences such as making poems or newspaper items about difficult content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A task list of C, B and A layer activities adapted to the target subject
#### Constraints
- Students preferred applying it not to all subjects but to the ones they have difficulty learning

### Target Learners
- 6th grade elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
- learning difficult topics in mathematics, science, and language arts
