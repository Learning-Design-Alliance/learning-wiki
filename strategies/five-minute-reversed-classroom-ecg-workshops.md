# Run brief, frequent reversed-classroom ECG workshops during cardiology internships, with students selecting, presenting and discussing real patient ECGs under cardiologist supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy consists of 5-minute workshops held 4 times a week for 5 weeks, using a "reversed classroom" method in which "students took turns to selecting and presenting ECGs from patients encountered during their cardiology internship". Each workshop covers a single ECG; students get one minute to reflect, the presenter corrects, and an arrhythmia cardiologist emphasizes key teaching points. The authors recommend extending such methods to other centers and medical topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A cardiologist specialized in arrhythmia management present to emphasize key teaching points
- Students have access to ECG tracings from patients encountered during their cardiology internship
#### Constraints
- The supporting comparison assigned teaching method nonrandomly by center, so center-level confounding cannot be excluded

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students during cardiology internships

### Target Learning Goals
- ECG interpretation, including recognition of life-threatening conditions
