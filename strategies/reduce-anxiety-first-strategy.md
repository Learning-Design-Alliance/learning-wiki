# Reduce anxiety first when multiple achievement emotions co-occur

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that when multiple emotions are considered simultaneously in achievement settings, "it may be more important to focus on reducing anxiety in the first instance than to attempt to reduce boredom and foster enjoyment", because anxiety showed the strongest unique relations with performance. The Educational Impact statement suggests reducing anxiety through "fostering their adaptive responses to failure and increasing perceptions of control".
