# Shared-vision curriculum change process using index cards, individual faculty meetings, and staged faculty votes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Paradigms 2.0 revision used a shared-vision change process in which course structures emerged from departmental discussions. Faculty interviewed recent instructors of each course, documenting roughly 700 index cards of topics sorted into course stacks, then met individually with every faculty member before two staged full-faculty meetings. The article reports that after this process the faculty unanimously voted to adopt the proposed changes, and that the process drew on a retrospective study of the original Paradigms 1.0 development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Department head support and prior faculty agreement obtained before the process began
- Individual meetings with every faculty member to explain and refine the proposal before voting
#### Constraints
- Developed in a single department with a tradition of meeting every three weeks to discuss upper-division teaching

### Target Learners
- physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- coherent upper-division physics curriculum
