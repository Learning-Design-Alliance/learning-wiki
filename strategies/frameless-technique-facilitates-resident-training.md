# Use frameless navigation-guided biopsy technique to facilitate early resident involvement in surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors suggest that modern frameless techniques may ease early resident participation because they appear easier to learn without compromising safety. Residents completed frameless biopsies faster than frame-based ones in anesthesia time, while staff were faster with frame-based procedures. The authors write that "modern frameless techniques may facilitate early resident involvement in surgical training, as they appear to be easier to learn without compromising patient safety."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of neuronavigation software and frameless biopsy needle systems
#### Constraints
- The authors note their software-familiarity explanation "remains speculative and requires further study"; technique choice should consider local availability, team experience, and individual patient factors

### Target Learners
- Neurosurgery residents in postgraduate year 1–7

### Target Learning Goals
- Acquisition of stereotactic biopsy technique
- Efficient operative performance during training
