# Use controlled designs with larger samples to confirm preclinical psychomotor training effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that follow-up studies of home-based psychomotor training add control groups and larger samples. The article states: "It is recommended to include control groups and expand the sample size to confirm these findings and optimise preclinical interventions." It also suggests comparisons with a control group not receiving the intervention to observe long-term effects at different educational stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Larger samples and control-group comparisons to attribute changes to the intervention
#### Constraints
- The present evidence base is a single-university pilot cohort with unbalanced sex representation and no control group

### Target Learners
- dental students in preclinical phases

### Target Learning Goals
- validating effects of home-based dexterity, sensitivity and strength training
