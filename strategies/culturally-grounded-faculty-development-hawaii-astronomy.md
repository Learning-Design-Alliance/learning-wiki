# Culturally based professional development that grounds curriculum in Hawaiian place and culture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper recommends supporting culturally based professional development that transforms classrooms into culturally contexted places of learning, exemplified by Uluakea, "a faculty development program that provides UH Hilo educators with the knowledge and experiences to design their curriculum from a place-based and culturally grounded approach." It further recommends supporting organizations fostering educational opportunities through cultural immersion within a scientific framework, citing A Hua He Inoa, which enables students to participate in modern astronomical research through a cultural lens.
