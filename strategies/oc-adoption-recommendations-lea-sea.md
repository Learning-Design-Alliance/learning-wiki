# Phased LEA and SEA recommendations for adopting Opportunity Culture staffing redesign

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief closes with recommendations for local and state education agencies interested in exploring Opportunity Culture. LEAs are advised to build cross-school commitment, determine essential design decisions, conduct school-level needs assessments, allow schools design authority within district parameters, communicate with stakeholders, and monitor and modify new staffing and roles. SEAs are advised to provide transition planning funding, help districts make strategic decisions about teacher-leader selection, compensation levels, sustainable funding, and phasing, provide training for new roles, remove policy barriers such as funding constraints, required staffing patterns, rigid class-size requirements, and inability to identify high-growth teachers, and gather data and foster networks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- district design processes that include stakeholder groups such as teachers
- state funding for planning and executing the transition
#### Constraints
- policy barriers the article names: funding constraints, required staffing patterns, rigid class-size requirements limiting flexible staffing, and inability to identify high-growth teachers

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in turnaround and high-needs schools

### Target Learning Goals
- implement tiered teacher-leadership staffing to improve instruction and student outcomes
