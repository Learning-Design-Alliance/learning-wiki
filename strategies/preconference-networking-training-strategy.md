# Run pre-conference networking and training activities (buddy pairing, introductory videos, talk/poster guidance) before an international meeting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before the conference, the organisers created a Slack space, asked participants to record two-minute introductory videos shared on a private playlist, paired African-affiliated and non-African-affiliated early-career researchers in a buddy system, and ran an online workshop plus guidelines and templates for talks, posters and 90-second 'sparkler' talks. The authors report these activities "help ensure an effective meeting where participants immediately feel confident and welcome, and deliver high-quality scientific contributions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A long lead time before the conference so pre-conference activities can be planned and completed, and clear guidelines for participants on what to prepare.
#### Constraints
- Only 35/107 participants created the introductory videos, with mostly more junior participants taking part, so uptake of individual activities can be limited.

### Target Learners
- early-career researchers
- participants with little-to-no experience of international conferences

### Target Learning Goals
- preparing high-quality scientific contributions
- building familiarity and confidence before the event
- networking across communities
