Strategies

Use picture-free audio listening tasks so students focus only on conversational content¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

During the listening sessions the researcher played compact-disk material whose "content's characteristics ... are without pictures, without expression, without the speaker's gesture so that students just listen and focus on the content of the conversation", using a laptop, loud speakers, and worksheets. Assessment took the form of multiple choice, writing alphabet responses, and selecting the right picture. This is the concrete task format the intervention used.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Equipment: a laptop, loud speakers, listening material CD, and worksheets

Constraints¶

  • Material had to be divided by the researcher because Curriculum 2013 does not divide material by semester
