# Leverage artificial intelligence and software technologies to further improve rhinoplasty education.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review proposes integrating artificial intelligence and software technologies to advance rhinoplasty education. Specific recommendations include leveraging technologies such as Google Glass and Microsoft HoloLens intra-operatively to allow residents to capture and record important procedural steps, building software to correlate techniques and anatomic configurations with three-dimensional contours for trainees, and using technologies such as Vectra to facilitate teaching of aesthetic goals. The authors state there is "an understated potential and opportunity to integrate artificial intelligence and other technologies to demystify and advance education in rhinoplasty." This strategy extends beyond current static simulation models toward dynamic, technology-enhanced learning tools.
