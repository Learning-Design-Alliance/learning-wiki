# Provide answers to probing questions one week after the session so students can quiz themselves for spaced repetition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the lab, students receive the answers to the probing questions one week later. The article states the questions "were written in a format that allowed the students to quiz themselves for spaced repetition to optimize long-term retention and recall of the material." The authors also suggest adding after-hours review sessions or revisiting the session later in the academic year to encourage spaced repetition and reinforcement before third-year clinical coursework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Probing questions distributed during the session with answers removed
- A mechanism for distributing answer keys one week after the session
