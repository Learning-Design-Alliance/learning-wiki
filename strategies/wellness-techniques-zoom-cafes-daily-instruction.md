# Set up learning environments encouraging mind and body balance by implementing wellness techniques such as meditation, yoga, and exercise in daily instruction, and use Zoom Cafés for social presence

## Description
As a future direction, the article proposes approaches to address mental needs during social isolation. These include "creating social presence and providing emotional feedback using videos or Zoom Cafés" and "setting up a learning environment that encourages mind and body balance, such as by the implementation of wellness techniques (e.g., meditation, yoga, exercise) in daily instruction". Such approaches could benefit students, instructors, and others in home-office settings.
