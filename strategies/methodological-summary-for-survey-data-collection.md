# Document survey data collection procedures and results in a methodological summary

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article models the practice of publishing a methodological summary alongside a survey: "This report summarizes the College Experiences Survey data collection procedures and results." Such documentation makes the data collection transparent to users of the evaluation. The report was published by Mathematica Policy Research as part of its evidence publications.
