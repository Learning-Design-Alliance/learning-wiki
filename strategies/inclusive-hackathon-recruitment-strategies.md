# Recruit for inclusive hackathons through personal invitations in pairs, women-only preparatory events, reduced competition emphasis, explicit valuation of non-coding skills, and role-based coordination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The diversity and inclusion breakout group compiled strategies to make hackathons more inclusive. For recruitment it proposed "personal invitations and recruitment in pairs, enabling participants to feel psychologically safe by being able to come to the event with a person they trust", and pointed to the women-only "She Innovates" hackathon as preparation for other events. For event design it recommended "having less emphasis on competition", "making explicit valuation of other skill sets in addition to coding", and "assigning roles (i.e., role-based coordination) to facilitate greater team participation". The group also discussed critical mass theory, suggesting a certain percentage of minority representation is needed for influence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system in place to support broader recruitment and support for people of diverse identities; representative leadership valued for diverse experiences and skills
#### Constraints
- The group noted diversity is context specific depending on the purpose and expected participants of the hackathons; whether event duration and funding are barriers to inclusivity remained open questions

### Target Learners
- women and underrepresented minorities in code-centric hackathons
- hackathon organizers seeking diverse participation

### Target Learning Goals
- inclusive participation in collaborative learning events
- community building across diverse participants
