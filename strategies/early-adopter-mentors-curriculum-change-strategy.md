# Support curriculum change through faculty preparation courses, regular meetings and early-adopter mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Following Licari's three steps for successful curriculum change, the faculty first established that a change was needed, then prepared staff through a 3-day course at a collaborating pedagogical unit, followed by ongoing support and regular all-teacher meetings. The article states that "Early adopters, who can demonstrate to other faculty members how a successful course change can be implemented, are invaluable when successfully adopting a curriculum change." Two early adopters served as mentors for colleagues struggling to adapt, and they continuously evaluated and revised the implementation. Staff preparation continues through education and workshops.
