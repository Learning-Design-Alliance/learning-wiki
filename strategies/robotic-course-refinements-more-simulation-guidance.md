# Refine robotic student training by shortening the IS-led overview, adding console practice time, and clarifying simulation guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports students' own recommendations for refining the pilot course: "First, they identified a preference for shortening the IS-led robot overview in favor of more simulation-based learning." Students also asked for more console time for skills such as knot tying and for clearer steps and real-time guidance during the simulation. The authors state they do not expect these adjustments to compromise feasibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to a robot trainer and console tasks for hands-on components
#### Constraints
- Preferences for simulation versus lecture-based learning are reported in the literature as mixed

### Target Learners
- senior medical students who completed core surgical clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- introduction to robotic features, functionalities, and roles, especially the bedside assist role
