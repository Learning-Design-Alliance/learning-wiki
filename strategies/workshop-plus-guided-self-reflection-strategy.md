# Combine a brief Teach-Back skills workshop with ongoing guided self-reflection surveys for telehealth communication training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention had two key components: guided self-reflection and a Teach-Back skills workshop. For 7 weeks nurses completed brief online surveys after each call, reflecting on communication effectiveness and perceived caller understanding, plus end-of-shift reflections; a 2-h workshop addressed knowledge, skills and beliefs using videos, discussion and role play. The article concludes "Guided self-reflection is a low-resource method aligned with nurse professional identity that can facilitate Teach-Back skills learning".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online survey infrastructure and email reminders; linking self-reflection to continuing professional development hours supported participation
#### Constraints
- Surveys became boring and repetitive after a while; future implementation must balance usefulness against annoyance; self-reflection data went to the research team, not managers, to avoid fear of judgment

### Target Learners
- telenurses and other telehealth professionals learning advanced communication skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Teach-Back and other advanced telehealth communication skills
