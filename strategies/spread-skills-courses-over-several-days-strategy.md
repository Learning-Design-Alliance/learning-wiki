# Spread time-limited skills courses over several days in shorter sessions rather than massing practice on one day

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For time-limited skills courses that typically use massed practice, the authors recommend distributing practice across several days with shorter sessions. They write that "training should be spread out over several days in shorter sessions, instead of doing it all on the same day," citing efficiency and reduced overload risk. Sessions in their own trial were capped at two hours, which they note reduces fatigue and boredom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course scheduling must allow multiple days of practice with non-training days between sessions
#### Constraints
- Recommendation rests on earlier studies plus this trial; transfer to clinical performance was not examined

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees and other novice psychomotor-skill learners

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient acquisition of surgical technical skills without overload
