# Use the PCK-Q questions for LA assessment or training, administered in a single set or 1-2 per week

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that instructors "use the questions to assess their LAs" or "use the questions for training LAs during class preparation sessions", administering them "in a single set or 1-2 per week". For widespread distribution they suggest developing easy-to-score multiple-choice or Likert-style formats that take less time to administer, score faster, and would not require trained coders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor oversight and, for free-response scoring, trained coders
#### Constraints
- Prediction of classroom PCK-Q is limited for LAs who answer fewer than three questions

### Target Learners
- learning assistants in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing and training LAs' questioning pedagogy (PCK-Q)
