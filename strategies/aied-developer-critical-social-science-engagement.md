# Pursue a productive future for AIED through deeper engagement between application developers and critical voices from the social sciences and history

## Description
The article recommends that AIED research and development be supported by social and historical sensitivity: developers bring pedagogic, design and technical expertise, while critical social scientists and historians bring expertise in understanding the complex and often unintended social effects of technologies. It states "a productive future for AIED would involve deeper engagement between application developers and more critical voices from the social sciences and history". Education sector professionals are urged to think much harder about how AI is being put to work in different contexts and with what social effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Acknowledging potential risks AI could bring, taking seriously its potential to worsen existing problems, and refusing the technochauvinist assumption that AI is the ideal solution
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is hard to anticipate downstream risks of any new technology, though professionals can consider the longer history of AI and related technologies
