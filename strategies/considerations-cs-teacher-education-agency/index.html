Address teacher agency development in computer science teacher education programmes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The paper "provides considerations for computer science teacher education", recommending that teacher education attend to how teachers achieve agency when enacting a new subject. Because teachers' agentic decisions shape learners' agency, preparing teachers for the realities of teaching LCCS should include supporting their agentic processes within school figurations.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher education providers must engage with the realities of teaching the new subject as experienced by teachers

Constraints¶

  • Considerations arise from a small Irish case study and may need adaptation to other curricula and systems

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-service and practising computer science teachers in Ireland

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing teacher agency in enacting a new curriculum subject

Affordances¶

  • Figurational Sociology Teacher Learner Agency

Claims¶

  • Responsibility for achieving agency should not rest solely on teachers and students, since contextual factors and educational social processes determine its fluctuation [+W]
  • Teachers' agentic decisions influence students' processes of achieving learner agency, both positively and negatively [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Cultivate agency among all categories of participants—students, educational staff, and societal partners—in transdisciplinary sustainability course design
  • Administrators and teacher educators should enable relational agency through consultation structures and honor teachers' emotions in responsive teaching
