# Measure perceived understanding before a lecture, after the lecture, and again after RLO use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The evaluation used a questionnaire adapted from a previous study, administered "prior to the standard lecture on meta-analysis, again after the lecture and then after accessing the RLOs". A 10-point numerical scale was used rather than a 4 or 5 point scale to reduce the possibility that students would remember prior responses, students could not refer to previous ratings, and questionnaires were analysed by an independent researcher.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the RLOs is only given after the lead lecture
- An independent researcher who is not the students' lecturer analyses the questionnaires
#### Constraints
- The time between administering the first and last questionnaire was several hours

### Target Learners
- postgraduate healthcare students in a blended learning session

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing change in perceived understanding of meta-analysis concepts
