# Compassionate Curiosity

## Description
Act as a non-judgmental investigator to better understand students and respond with empathy. Acknowledge there’s a lot you don’t know, ask caring questions, listen and observe, imagine the student’s experience, believe that every student wants to be known, and be patient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training in compassionate communication, willingness to be vulnerable and patient, a commitment to active listening
#### Constraints
- May feel uncomfortable or unnatural at first, can be time-consuming, requires a willingness to question assumptions
