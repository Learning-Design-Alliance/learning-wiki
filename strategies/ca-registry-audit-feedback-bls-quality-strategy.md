# Use cardiac arrest registries and audit-and-feedback as quality improvement to improve BLS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that hospitals participate in cardiac arrest (CA) registries and use audit and feedback on resuscitation quality as quality-improvement mechanisms. They state that "audit and feedback on the quality of resuscitation in CA is a requisite for quality improvement and strategies such as a CA registry can improve BLS", and note no active resuscitation-focused CA registries exist in Africa. In South Africa this could be embedded in the Ideal Hospital initiative.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Hospital participation in national or regional CA registries
- Quality assurance structures such as the Ideal Hospital initiative
#### Constraints
- There are currently no active resuscitation-focussed CA registries in Africa

### Target Learners
- Nurses and health care providers in district hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Quality of basic life support performance
- Continuous improvement of resuscitation care
