## Description
The article recommends that "educators and policymakers should embed real-life problems into national curricula, enabling students to explore intricate links between local and global issues". This strategy positions open schooling as a bridge between schools and community stakeholders, using authentic collaborative learning to develop transversal skills. It is grounded in the study's finding that students valued hands-on, inquiry-based learning connected to real-world challenges.
