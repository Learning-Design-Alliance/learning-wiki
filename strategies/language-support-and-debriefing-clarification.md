# Provide targeted language support and debriefing clarification opportunities for CALD students in SBL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends concrete supports for the linguistic demands of SBL: pre-teaching common clinical terminology before simulation, offering peer practice opportunities, scaffolding communication tasks, and structuring debriefing so students can clarify their reasoning when language limited their performance. These measures respond to findings that communication difficulties often reflect second-language articulation rather than gaps in clinical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators should be mindful that communication difficulties may not reflect actual deficiencies in clinical reasoning skills
#### Constraints
- Educators must balance accommodations against maintaining the fidelity of the simulation experience

### Target Learners
- Occupational therapy students from CALD backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- Therapeutic communication skills
- Demonstrating clinical reasoning in simulation
