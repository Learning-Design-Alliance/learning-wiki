# Respond to anaesthesia-related maternal deaths with three facility-level interventions: standardised neuraxial anaesthesia, difficult-airway readiness, and a haemorrhage-resuscitation bundle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 2 against) · 6 studies (3 causal, 2 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A model SAQ answer proposes three facility-level interventions after two anaesthesia-related maternal deaths, each paired with a feasible district-hospital implementation step. First, standardise safe neuraxial anaesthesia: 'Intervention: protocol for dose and positioning, fluid co-load, prophylactic vasopressor, target block height, monitoring schedule, management of high or total spinal', implemented via a one-page poster, WHO checklist integration, and monthly mini-audits of caesarean charts. Second, difficult airway readiness with an obstetric difficult airway cart, failed intubation algorithm, mandatory capnography for general anaesthesia, skills sign-off and quarterly simulation. Third, a postpartum haemorrhage and resuscitation bundle with quantified blood loss, uterotonics, tranexamic acid within 3 h, massive transfusion protocol triggers, monthly obstetric emergency drills and PDSA cycles to close gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stock availability of vasopressors (phenylephrine or metaraminol), tranexamic acid, and airway rescue equipment
- Skilled staff with sign-off for spinal anaesthesia, general anaesthesia and caesarean section
- Safety system infrastructure such as the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist, escalation pathways and a massive transfusion protocol
#### Constraints
- Scoped to a resource-limited district hospital: implementations assume basic rather than tertiary resources (for example video laryngoscope only 'if available') and rely on local audit and drill capacity

### Target Learners
- Newly appointed family physicians leading quality improvement in district hospitals
- Medical officers providing obstetric anaesthesia

### Target Learning Goals
- Prevention of recurrent anaesthesia-related maternal deaths
- Safe neuraxial anaesthesia, airway readiness and postpartum haemorrhage response at facility level
