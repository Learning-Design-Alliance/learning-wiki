# Adapt Dice versions and basis orientations across educational levels, and develop teaching guides and expanded protocols

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article outlines differentiation and future directions: a "simplified colourless version in which the outcomes always sum to seven" provides accessible entry points for different age groups; upper-level secondary students can implement all described quantum processes; university students can use "conventional state notation and mathematical representations". Because the system is fully programmable, measurement-basis orientations can be modified so different basis choices produce distinct statistics. Future work includes teleportation with a third die, a simplified CHSH experiment, qubit representations, teaching guides, and a follow-up study on learner mapping and teacher integration.
