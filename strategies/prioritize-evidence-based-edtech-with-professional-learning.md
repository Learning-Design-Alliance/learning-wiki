# Prioritize evidence-based technology programs and implement them with professional learning and integrated practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that schools and districts adopting educational technology "prioritize evidence-based practices" and track the impact of adopted technologies. They argue technology-based programs "along with accompanying professional learning and integrated with practices, can support teachers in creating classroom conditions that better prepare students for future math learning." They caution that without equitable implementation—unequal access, lack of data literacy and teacher training—technology programs can widen achievement gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation requires specialized training and professional development, which the authors note can be expensive and time-consuming.
#### Constraints
- Unequal access, lack of data literacy and teacher training, inequitable implementation, and lack of support for low-performing students can widen achievement gaps.

### Target Learners
- K–12 students, particularly middle school math students

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics achievement and equitable access to high-quality math education
