# Formalize clinical peer-mentorship programs pairing senior staff with junior nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals "Formalize clinical peer‐mentorship programs matching experienced senior staff with junior nurses to standardize clinical practice." This responds to the finding that only 2.8% of participants had over 10 years of experience, depriving the environment of informal peer-led guidance and tacit knowledge transfer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experienced senior staff available to serve as mentors
#### Constraints
- The setting's scarcity of senior personnel (only 2.8% with >10 years experience) limits mentor availability

### Target Learners
- Junior nurses and early-career clinicians in tertiary inpatient wards

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized clinical wound-care practice and tacit knowledge transfer
