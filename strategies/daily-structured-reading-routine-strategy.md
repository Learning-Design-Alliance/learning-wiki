# Implement a daily structured reading routine combining fixed free-reading and variable enrichment activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention ran five days a week for 45 minutes daily across a full school year. Each session had fixed parts (15 minutes free reading followed by writing-after-reading in a personal booklet) and variable parts (discovery reading, storytelling and retelling, functional reading, serial stories, dialogue reading, post-reading activities). This predictable structure addressed attention and processing difficulties while building a reading habit through daily repetition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Daily time allocation of 45 minutes within the school schedule
- Level-appropriate books with minimal text and large illustrations
- Personal response booklets renewed monthly
#### Constraints
- Designed and tested specifically for second-grade pupils with learning disabilities in special education classes

### Target Learners
- Second-grade pupils with learning disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading motivation
- Reading habit formation
- Reading comprehension
