# Make disciplinary norms of thinking explicit for effective reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The fifth strategy is "Making disciplinary norms of thinking explicit to learners for effective reflection on problem exploration experience." Such guidance helps learners "reflect on and communicate their experience and regulate their problem-solving activities by following disciplinary norms of thinking." These norms can also foster knowledge transfer as abstractions of problem-solving methods.
