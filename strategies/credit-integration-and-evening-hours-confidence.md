# Build researcher confidence by integrating autonomous projects into degree credit and extending opening hours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because university culture is oriented around progress in assessed activities, the programme integrated with intramural degree-credit courses and undergraduate/graduate degree courses so members could gain credit for autonomous projects, and shifted some spaces to operate 12-8pm so those with daytime courses or employment could pursue projects. The authors report this eased "the challenge to confidence presented by our interdisciplinary framework".
