# Calming Tactile Input

## Description
Using tactile input to provide calming sensory experiences involves engaging the sense of touch to reduce anxiety and promote relaxation. Activities include running hands through a tactile bin filled with materials like sand, rice, or beans, and applying deep pressure through hugs, weighted blankets, or weighted stuffed animals. Squishing with a pillow or beanbag chair can also provide full-body calming sensory input.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tactile materials (e.g., sand, rice, beans), weighted blankets or stuffed animals, pillows or beanbag chairs.
#### Constraints
- Some children may have sensitivities or aversions to certain textures. The effectiveness may vary based on individual preferences and needs.
