# Lessons for establishing inter-university programmes: shared vision, research-institute involvement, and flexible cooperation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distils implementation lessons: "the existence of a shared scientific vision among the participating institutions proved essential for building consensus and maintaining long-term commitment"; involvement of national research institutes enhanced educational value by providing infrastructure, ongoing projects and international collaborations; and flexible institutional cooperation enabled practical solutions where regulations gave no explicit guidance. The authors suggest these lessons may transfer to other disciplines and countries.
