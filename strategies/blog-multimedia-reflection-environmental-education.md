# Use blogs to share multimedia and reflective questions for environmental education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes an implementable approach: an instructor creates a blog to share environmental movies, animations, and weekly reflective questions, with students free to comment. Over eight weeks, eleven multimedia documents and weekly reflective questions were published, and the instructor acted as a facilitator. This strategy targets environmental literacy dimensions of knowledge, attitude, and usage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor creates and maintains the blog, publishes multimedia documents and reflective questions, and facilitates student engagement
#### Constraints
- The article concludes blogs are not sufficient enough to make students more interested in environmental problems

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students (seventh grade)

### Target Learning Goals
- Environmental literacy: knowledge, attitude, and usage of environment
