# Properly administered speed drills in arithmetic to improve both amount done correctly and accuracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends speed drill in addition as an effective classroom practice: distributed practice in adding columns under school conditions raised the number of examples done correctly while maintaining or improving the percentage correct. The authors state that "Properly administered speed drills will in fact improve accuracy as well." The strategy is having pupils add columns of ten one-place numbers rapidly against their own past record in regular school periods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Drills must be properly administered, with pupils competing against their own past record rather than one another
