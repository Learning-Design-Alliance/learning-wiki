# Expand feedback, critically review assessment strategies and assure exam quality as next steps for communication curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The survey's concluding recommendation for further curricular development of communicative competencies in German-speaking medical education. The authors identify three directions: "the expansion of feedback, the critical evaluation of appropriate assessment strategies, and in the quality assurance of exams". They specifically call for wider use of formative formats such as WBA and portfolios, and for context-rich written question types beyond factual-knowledge multiple choice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing communication instruction and assessment infrastructure at the faculty, as surveyed across the 40 degree programmes
#### Constraints
- The authors note formative assessment is generally less frequent in German-speaking countries, limiting uptake of formative formats

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in German-speaking Europe

### Target Learning Goals
- communicative competencies in medical studies
