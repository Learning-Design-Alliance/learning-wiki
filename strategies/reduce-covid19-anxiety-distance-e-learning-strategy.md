# Reduce students' COVID-19-related anxiety to enhance distance e-learning outcomes in nursing education

## Description
The article recommends that educators and staff in nursing schools actively address students' pandemic-related anxiety as part of distance e-learning delivery, because anxiety weakened the satisfaction-mediated pathway from learning flow to outcomes. It states "it is necessary for educators and staff in nursing schools to exert effort to reduce students’ COVID-19-related anxiety". This includes monitoring mental health alongside formal education, with special consideration for female students, who the article says are more vulnerable to negative mental health effects.
