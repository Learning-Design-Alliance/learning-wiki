Operationalize abstract ethical frameworks as concrete LLM agent personas with demographic cues and value prioritizations¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors found that instructing LLMs with naive ethical frameworks (e.g., "adopt a deontological perspective") often produced underspecified responses with vague language. To make each perspective actionable, they operationalized it as a persona with concrete behavioral guidance: demographic cues (age, gender, major), writing style, a brief ethical background, and a set of value prioritizations following VSD guidelines. This helped the agents express their positions in distinctive and specific ways.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Each persona prompt composed of demographics, ideology, and opinions, following Hwang et al.
  • Pilot validation that each persona is perceived as distinctive and representative of the designated perspective

Constraints¶

  • Personas were specified as undergraduate students so participants would regard them as peers rather than experts

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate STEM students discussing ethics with LLM agents

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Exposure to distinct yet interconnected moral approaches (care, deontological, pragmatic ethics)
