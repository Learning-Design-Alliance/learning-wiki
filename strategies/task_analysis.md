# Task Analysis

## Description
Task analysis involves breaking down a writing assignment into smaller, manageable tasks. It focuses on ensuring that students understand what they're supposed to do by having them read or listen to the instructions aloud and explain the assignment in their own words. Highlighting or underlining key parts of the writing task together can help.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing assignment; facilitator; highlighting or underlining materials
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming; requires patience and clear communication; effectiveness depends on student engagement
