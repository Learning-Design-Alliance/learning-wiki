# Embed dyslexia accessibility guidelines in AI-generated worksheet generation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends building research-based accessibility guidelines directly into the material-generation pipeline. The generator agent "incorporates accessibility guidelines identified in research as essential for neurodivergent learners, such as clear sans-serif fonts, sufficient line spacing, strong text–background contrast, single-column layouts, and explicit visual separation of tasks." Structured LaTeX output enables precise layout control for accessibility compliance while allowing export to editable formats for teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured, machine-readable output format (LaTeX) to ensure precise layout control; teacher review of outputs before classroom use.
#### Constraints
- Teacher survey ratings indicate font readability (M= 3.30) and suitability for reading difficulties (M= 3.46) fell near the midpoint, so typographical adaptations require further refinement.

### Target Learners
- Students with dyslexia and other reading-related learning differences in K–12 classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Accessible, readable instructional materials that reduce extraneous cognitive load for neurodivergent learners
