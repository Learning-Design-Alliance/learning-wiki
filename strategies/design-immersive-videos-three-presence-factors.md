# Design immersive videos for teacher education around the three presence factors to support viewer attention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that designers and teacher educators deliberately address all three XRPS presence factors when building immersive videos: emotional connectivity toward observed actors (even virtual characters), co-presence via feelings of agency such as placing the field of view near the learning activity, and awareness of self through a transparent, easy-to-navigate user experience. This follows from the finding that all three factors correlated with attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All three presence components should be supported and planned carefully to assist PSTs' focus, per the article
#### Constraints
- Additional analyses are required to shed light on the presence-attention correlation and related facets

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- attention and focus during immersive video viewing
- noticing of pedagogical events
