# Accommodate every MBTI preference in a course so students are exposed to non-preferred styles and diversify their learning strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (3 review, 1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond matching styles, the article recommends intervention by assisting students to diversify their learning strategies and encouraging optional choices outside a preferred style. Because "almost every class has some students of each type, so by accommodating every preference each student can be exposed to other preferences", helping students develop skills in their non-preferred areas and the balance adults need to function effectively.
