# Advise graduates to schedule the certification examination when fully prepared and no acute life events are occurring, using mock board scores as objective data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that faculty advise graduates to take boards only when fully prepared and free of acute life events, because resilient students use biased interpretation to schedule exams during major life stressors. Faculty should meet individually about postgraduation plans and review mock board examination scores to provide objective data on readiness. Students may notify the testing vendor without penalty within 2 business days to reschedule.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty access to students' postgraduation plans and mock board examination scores to give objective readiness data

- The advice is grounded in the author's one-on-one review observations and cited cognitive science, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- graduating midwifery students preparing for the AMCB certification examination

### Target Learning Goals
- sound decision-making about examination timing and readiness
