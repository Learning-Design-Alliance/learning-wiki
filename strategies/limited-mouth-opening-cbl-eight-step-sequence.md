# Eight-step symptom-focused CBL teaching sequence from trismus etiologies to precision treatment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enumerates an implementable sequence its CBL module followed: “Systematic exploration of trismus etiologies stimulates differential diagnosis review,” followed by anatomical-physiological correlations, mechanistic categorization of trismus severity into myogenic, arthrogenic, and structural etiologies, syndrome-pathology mapping, evidence-based diagnostic reasoning through history-taking and literature synthesis, exclusion criteria development via critical appraisal, multimodal examination planning, and “precision treatment strategies employing mind-mapping techniques.” Each step is a concrete activity a clinical instructor can replicate in a symptom-anchored case module.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured weekly case sessions with faculty debriefing
- Cases spanning the eight limited-mouth-opening disease categories
- Integration with clinical chair-side observation
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a single-hospital pilot with 15 residents per group

### Target Learners
- dental residents rotating through a TMJ specialty

### Target Learning Goals
- differential diagnosis of restricted mouth opening
- evidence-based treatment planning
