# Provide stress and depression counseling services and support structures in higher education institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings that academic and family stress raise depression and depress academic performance, the authors recommend that institutions support students' psychological wellbeing. They state that "Students should have access to stress and depression counseling services in their schools", alongside proper counseling, guidance from teachers, family support, and a positive and safe learning environment created by administrators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must currently lack support services for students' emotional wellbeing, which the article says accelerates stress and depression levels among students.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a single convenience-sampled SEM study conducted during COVID-19 in Pakistan.

### Target Learners
- university students experiencing pandemic-related academic and family stress

### Target Learning Goals
- alleviating stress and depression so students can learn and perform better
