# Integrated campus health promotion: physical activity support plus routine screening for disordered eating and depressive symptoms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that university health and academic support services promote regular physical activity and implement screening for disordered eating and depression. It states that "promoting regular physical activity through accessible gym facilities, peer-led exercise groups, or wellness breaks integrated into the curriculum may be considered as supportive strategies" and that "implementing routine screening programs for disordered eating and depressive symptoms—alongside low-barrier counselling and nutrition services" could help identify at-risk students. The authors frame these as exploratory given the cross-sectional design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- University health and academic support services able to offer facilities, screening, and low-barrier counselling and nutrition services
#### Constraints
- The authors state these implications "should be interpreted cautiously and viewed as exploratory, given the study design and sample characteristics"; causal or preventive conclusions cannot be drawn from the cross-sectional study

### Target Learners
- Saudi university students
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance (GPA)
- student well-being
