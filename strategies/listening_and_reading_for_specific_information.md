# Listening and reading for specific information

## Description
This strategy involves actively seeking particular details or information within a text or audio material to understand the overall message. It requires learners to listen selectively for specific words, phrases, or key ideas, monitor their comprehension, and infer meaning using linguistic, metalinguistic, and contextual clues. It is valuable in academic and workplace preparation, where adults may need to listen to lectures or prepare for exams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading and listening materials, explicit instruction, guided practice, modeling of strategies
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners with limited vocabulary or background knowledge. Requires explicit instruction and practice.
