# Use SNA visual analytics to monitor online discussions and guide teacher intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors use social network analysis visualization at course, discussion-thread and individual levels to spot gaps in collaborative learning, such as a dominating teacher, non-participatory threads or isolated students, and to intervene. It states that "monitoring individual discussions by means of SNA can help inform instructors about when to intervene", and that isolated students at risk of poor performance can be identified and supported through inclusive environments and collaboration scripts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to forum interaction data and an SNA tool such as Gephi for visualization and metric calculation.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the study is limited to a single course, which narrows the generalizability of the results, and that SNA has not found wide-scale adoption on the commercial level.

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates in online case discussions
- at-risk or isolated students

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement in collaborative case discussions
- early identification and support of underachieving students
