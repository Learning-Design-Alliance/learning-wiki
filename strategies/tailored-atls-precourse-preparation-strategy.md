# Tailor ATLS pre-course preparation and extend course duration for less experienced and at-risk participants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adapting the ATLS course to its diverse participants rather than changing the assessment tool. It states that "tailored pre-course preparation and an extension of the course duration for less experienced participants could provide the additional time needed to thoroughly assimilate and apply the material," and recommends intensifying course components for rural practitioners. The authors flag these as proposed solutions without solid evidence yet.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targets less experienced participants and at-risk groups such as general practitioners, OMFS specialists, and interns identified in the study
#### Constraints
- The authors state these are proposed solutions without solid evidence yet and that they do not advocate adapting the assessment tool itself

### Target Learners
- physicians taking ATLS courses, especially interns, general practitioners, and non-trauma specialties

### Target Learning Goals
- trauma management knowledge and practical skills sufficient to pass ATLS assessment
