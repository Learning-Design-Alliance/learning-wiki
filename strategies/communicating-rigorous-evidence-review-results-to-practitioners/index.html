Communicate rigorous evidence review results to practitioners through effect-size comparisons, benchmarks, improvement indexes, and accessible report design¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors present challenge/solution pairs for making rigorous evidence review results meaningful to practitioners and policy makers. Solutions include drawing "comparisons among effect size estimates for different classroom interventions", relating effect sizes to benchmark effect sizes from policy relevant research, translating effect sizes into an improvement index, discussing the consequences of basing decisions on studies that do not meet standards, and keeping reports short and accessible with report sections, subheadings and white space and a good topic sentence stating the single point of each paragraph.

Requirements¶

  • Reports must balance transparency with brevity, using multiple review cycles and appendices to describe methods

  • Findings from the study were still under review at the time of the proposal, so the communication solutions are proposed rather than evaluated
