# Employ trained teachers with adequate infrastructure to integrate e-labs into science lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's prospective field-work steps operationalize e-lab employment: contract science teachers across different educational regions, install the required technological infrastructure (e-lab, software, equipment, licenses), train the teachers on the use of research tools and software, and ensure equivalency of experimental and control groups before implementation. An external experienced consultant is contracted to ensure the application procedures of the e-lab field work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specialized team of experts in computers, curriculum and psychology, and science specialists, for design and production
#### Constraints
- E-labs rely on languages (specifically, Arabic) are still rare; equipment with special specifications is needed for clear representation of complex phenomena

### Target Learners
- basic education science students in Oman

### Target Learning Goals
- science achievement
- practical skills acquisition
