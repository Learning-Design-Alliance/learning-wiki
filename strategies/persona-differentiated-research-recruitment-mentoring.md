# Differentiate research recruitment and mentoring by student persona

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Recruit and support prospective research students differently depending on which persona best matches their motivations. Louis-type students 'may just need information such as faculty project details and contacts'; for Maria-type students, it is important to 'advertise the research in such a way that highlights its interesting aspects, promotes tips for self and time management'; Ashley needs close contact and collaboration with research peers and seniors and a faculty mentor who provides supportive collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty must be able to judge which persona best matches a given student's motivations before choosing a recruitment or mentoring approach.
#### Constraints
- The differentiation is grounded in one institution's interview data and may not transfer to other contexts.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students considering research

### Target Learning Goals
- recruitment into undergraduate research
- retention in research
