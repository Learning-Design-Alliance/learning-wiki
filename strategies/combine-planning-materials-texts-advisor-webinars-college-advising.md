# Combine personalized planning materials, student text messaging, and advisor training webinars to enhance college advising

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy pairs student-facing supports (personalized planning materials and text messaging) with advisor-facing training webinars to improve college advising. The article reports that this combination "increased the number and selectivity of colleges to which students apply", making it an implementable recipe for college-access programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training webinars for advisors so advising practice changes alongside student materials
#### Constraints
- Positive impacts were on application steps; no impact was found on the importance students placed on academic quality or on early FAFSA completion

### Target Learners
- High school students in college-access programs such as Upward Bound

### Target Learning Goals
- College application behavior
- Applying to a broader and more selective set of colleges
