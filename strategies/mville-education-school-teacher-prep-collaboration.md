# Collaborate with the School of Education to build a teacher-track physics major and a MAT program in physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Responding to New York State's raised standards for future K-12 teachers, the department developed, 'in collaboration with the School of Education, both an undergraduate educational track physics major and an Mater of Arts in Teaching (MAT) program in physics'. The state standard requires disciplinary content equivalent to an undergraduate major — for physics, 32 credits including 8 in mathematics — and the authors expect these programs may bring significant student numbers.
