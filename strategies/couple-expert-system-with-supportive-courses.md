# Couple an RBT-based expert system with school supportive-education courses and place students by beginning-of-semester testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that education ministries or schools couple the WBES system, or an expert system based on it, with supportive-education courses in middle or high schools, so that administrators can prepare supportive education based on reported levels and parents can see learning status. They further suggest that "all the students which will particip ate in the supportive education programs" be given "a comprehensive test in the beginning of the semes ter" to determine levels and form different classes via the WBES system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An expert system compatible with the RBT cognitive process dimension
- Teachers and administrators able to act on the system's level reports
#### Constraints
- The recommendation extends a system evaluated only in one eighth-grade mathematics supportive-education setting

### Target Learners
- middle and high school students in supportive-education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing and remediating gains across RBT cognitive process levels

## Claims

- [After traditional supportive education, completely learned students decreased in the remembering and analysis levels of the control group](../claims/traditional-supportive-education-level-declines.md) [+W]
