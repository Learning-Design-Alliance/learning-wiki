# Organise seminars, conferences and workshops on modern instructional strategies such as Round-robin for chemistry teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the Ministry of Education organise seminars, conferences and workshops on modern instructional strategies like Round-robin at local, state and federal levels for chemistry teachers, curriculum planners, school administrators, authors of chemistry textbooks and the various external examination bodies. This capacity-building strategy aims to spread adoption of activity-oriented strategies that the study found raised interest.
