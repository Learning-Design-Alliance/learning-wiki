# Use supplementary workshops, learning portfolios and formative testing to promote engagement with clinical skills SDL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors propose possibilities such as supplementary workshops, allied to encouragement of learning portfolio use by students such that they reflect on their progress, diagnose learning needs and create learning plans, and introducing more informal/formative testing throughout the academic year to increase SDL engagement among students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SDL laboratories and equipment must be accessible to students, since reduced access emerged as a barrier in student comments
#### Constraints
- The authors frame these as possibilities that could potentially increase SDL use, not as tested interventions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate-entry medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed learning of clinical skills
- life-long learning
