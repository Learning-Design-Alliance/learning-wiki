Enable regular, meaningful family engagement through diverse home-language communication, proactive meetings, student-led conferences, and shared decision-making forums¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief states there is 'no single strategy for cultivating effective family engagement' but recommends four approaches: diverse communication tools so families can consistently learn how their child is doing; proactively scheduled virtual or in-person meetings rather than only reactive ones; reimagined student–teacher–family conferences including student-led conferences; and family inclusion in decision-making forums that distribute power, such as school site councils.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Communication should be conveyed in families' home language wherever possible
  • Meetings should be conducted proactively to build rapport, with thoughtful scheduling and supports such as translation services and babysitting to increase family participation
  • Shared decision-making forums must allow families' knowledge and expertise to substantively inform a shared school vision, not merely collect opinions

Constraints¶

  • These approaches hold particular relevance in communities where interpersonal and institutional trust has been violated

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school students' families
  • students in communities with violated interpersonal and institutional trust

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school–family trust and alignment between home and school values, norms, and practices
  • family participation in school improvement and strategic planning

Affordances¶

  • Relationship Centered Schooling Framework
