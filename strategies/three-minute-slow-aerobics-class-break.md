# Incorporate a 3-minute low-intensity exercise break about 20 minutes into university lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable activity: near the start of a 90-minute lecture, after the review segment, students perform three minutes of slow aerobics with music following an expert-created DVD, framed as a natural transition rather than an interruption. The article argues the intervention "can be incorporated into various educational settings in a straightforward manner (i.e., brief and low-intensity exercise)" and offers it to practitioners as a simple strategy to motivate students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor to lead or hand out materials during the break
- A brief expert-created exercise routine with music
#### Constraints
- Effects demonstrated only for vigour and fatigue, in one psychology class; low intensity may not affect cognitive engagement

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- classroom motivation
- emotional engagement (vigour and fatigue)
