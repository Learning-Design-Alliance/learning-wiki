# Use integrated-skills project work on environmental topics to raise awareness and language skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends process- and product-oriented projects that connect classroom learning with pupils' lives: researching topics and writing academic papers, writing and illustrating children's books or comic strips, creating posters and brochures, writing for the school newspaper, debating, persuasive presentations, and role plays. It states that "Environmental awareness can be raised and language skills improved when students engage in process-and product-oriented projects". Field trips to recycling centres and guest speakers are suggested extensions with pre-taught vocabulary and post-activity responses.
