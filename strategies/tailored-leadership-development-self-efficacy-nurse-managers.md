# Tailor leadership development to managers' self-efficacy levels, combining context-specific education with self-efficacy building

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "leadership development initiatives should combine context‐specific education with opportunities to strengthen self‐efficacy through practical learning experiences". Managers with lower self-efficacy may benefit from structured education, mentoring, supervised leadership experiences, and constructive feedback, whereas those with higher self-efficacy may benefit from advanced leadership challenges and reflective feedback. Organizations could implement structured leadership transition programs, periodic coaching, and multisource (360-degree) feedback programs.

## Design Implications
