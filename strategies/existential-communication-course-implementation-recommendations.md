# Implementation-phase recommendations: more improvisation time, systematic integration of the question tool, and training in enduring without solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For a future implementation phase the authors propose improvements to the course format. "Several of the participants commented that the course could with advantage be longer by incorporating more time to communication training through theatre improvisation." Participants also "demanded a more systematic integration of the supportive communication tool developed for the course" and a broader vocabulary with suggestions for key questions. The authors additionally suggest a more specific focus on promoting less solution-oriented approaches, since endurance self-efficacy did not increase considerably.
