# Metacognitive Accountable Talk Stems

## Description
Accountable talk stems are phrases and sentence starters that facilitate structured, text-based discussions and promote higher-order thinking and metacognition. They guide students to clearly and accurately examine and convey complex ideas, effectively select and organize content, and analyze information while referring back to textual evidence and building on each other’s contributions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a set of accountable talk stems, a text or topic for discussion, and a facilitator to guide the discussion.
#### Constraints
- May feel restrictive if not used flexibly. Requires careful facilitation to ensure students engage meaningfully.
