# Develop training content systematically from a literature review plus semistructured interviews with patients, healthcare professionals and counselling experts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Both training interventions were built through a systematic development process rather than ad hoc design. The article states that 'the interventions were developed based on a review of the recent literature as well as semistructured interviews with patients with cancer having minor children, HCPs (eg, physicians, nurses, psychosocial staff) and experts in the field of counselling families affected by parental cancer'. This grounds module content in the documented needs and experiences of the target group.
