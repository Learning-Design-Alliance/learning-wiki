# Expand demographic, longitudinal, and funding data collection on physics and astronomy graduate students through the AIP Statistical Research Center

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper recommends departments provide more data about their programs and students, collected by an organization — a natural fit being increased support to the AIP's Statistical Research Center. It calls for more complete demographic data (particularly socioeconomic), educational histories, longitudinal studies of completion and attrition, loan-burden data for students leaving programs, and transparent funding data with cost-of-living estimates so students can make informed decisions, while maintaining student privacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Departments must provide much of the data about their own programs and students
- Data collection must maintain the privacy of individual students given small program sizes
#### Constraints
- Existing AIP surveys cover only first-year students for some measures and lack socioeconomic data such as parent education or family income

### Target Learners
- physics and astronomy graduate students
- prospective graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- informed decisions about graduate education pathways
- understanding attrition and completion rates
