# Identify Self-Talk

## Description
Have students record messages from their inner voice over a week in a journal or notebook. Remind them that self-talk is incessant and ongoing, and that they should aim to record at least 5 inner voice messages per day to capture this.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Journal or notebook, writing utensil, consistent daily practice.
#### Constraints
- May be difficult for some learners to consistently record their self-talk. Requires time and effort to observe internal thoughts.
