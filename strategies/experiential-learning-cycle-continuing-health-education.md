# Use experiential learning cycles to structure continuing health education for chronic disease management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends structuring continuing health education around Kolb's four-stage experiential learning cycle. The authors state that interventions "not only align with the principles of the theory based on a continuous four-stage cycle (concrete experience (CE) – Act; reflective observation (RO) – Reflect; abstract conceptualization (AC) – Conceptualize; active experimentation (EA) - Apply), but also meet specific needs of users." This strategy positions real professional experiences as the basis for reflection and practice transformation in chronic disease care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article notes that implementing changes in health services is often hindered by organizational micropolicies

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Transforming care and management practices for chronic conditions
- Developing practical skills for chronic disease management
