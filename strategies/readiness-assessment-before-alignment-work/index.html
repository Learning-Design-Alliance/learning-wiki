Assess team readiness across leadership, facilitation, communication, shared understanding, and capacity before starting¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Before beginning the guide's chapters, teams complete a readiness assessment evaluating "your district's collective skills, knowledge, and support for alignment and coherence of whole-person initiatives." The assessment poses questions in five areas: leadership (whether LEA leaders have prioritized whole-person initiatives and their alignment), facilitation, coordination and communication (including differences in how the agency defines whole-person work), shared understanding (shared goals and why alignment matters), and team capacity (skills, openness to adapting mindsets, data-informed decisions, and action-planning processes).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teams should use readiness responses to decide which preparatory actions to take before implementing the guide; a yes answer to every question is not required to start

Constraints¶

  • The guide states that team readiness will vary and that capacity will increase as the district engages in the work

Target Learners¶

  • district and school leaders
  • implementation teams

Target Learning Goals¶

  • organizational readiness for aligning whole-person initiatives
