Open the adaptation process with a briefing session introducing criteria for adapting listening materials, then keep the teacher available as consultant¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a two-part implementation recipe for learner adaptation of listening materials. First, a briefing session introduces the major learning issues and the criteria learners should consider when adapting materials; second, the teacher shifts from being the sole source of information to a consultant available at different stages of the adaptation process. The author reports this combination produced "high level of participation, interaction, collaboration and ultimately a sense of achievement".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A briefing session at the start of the experiment introducing the criteria needed for adapting listening materials
  • Teacher availability for consultation at the different stages of the adaptation process

Constraints¶

  • Material adaptation by learners does not mean learners alone undertake it; grading of listening materials and tasks should be carried out by professionals such as material developers and teachers

Target Learners¶

  • Iranian EFL university students (senior undergraduates, ages 20-26)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • listening comprehension of genre-based materials (news and movies)
  • positive attitude toward listening

Claims¶

  • Peer cooperative self-access listening materials adaptation improves EFL learners' listening comprehension performance more than teacher-selected materials [+W]
  • Both the movie-adaptation and news-adaptation groups significantly outperformed the control group on listening posttest, and did not differ from each other [+W]
  • Students' attitudes toward listening shifted from negative before the treatment to positive after cooperative materials adaptation [+W]
