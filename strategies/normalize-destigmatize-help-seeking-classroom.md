# Normalize and de-stigmatize help-seeking behavior in the classroom so academic help-seeking habits carry over to health issues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy asks educators to build a classroom culture that treats asking for help as normal and shame-free. The brief states that "The effective strategies educators use to encourage academic help-seeking behavior can also be used to encourage health help-seeking behavior," so the same practices transfer when a young person faces a health issue. It is an implementable classroom routine rather than a tested intervention in this brief.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators suggest students get extra help in a subject in which they are struggling
- Educators encourage students when they do seek out help and reinforce that there is no shame in asking for help
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, especially youth from marginalized groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Help-seeking behavior for academic and health needs
