# Develop learners' personal sets of effective vocabulary strategies rather than teaching arbitrary strategy lists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that strategy mastery predicted incidental vocabulary acquisition, the authors recommend that "teachers may encourage L2 learners to become strategic learners by developing a personal set of effective strategies, instead of teaching them a group of arbitrary strategies." They also suggest teachers may encourage incidental vocabulary learning anxiety because it guides attention to unknown words, while stressing that these instructional implications require future examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction should address cognitive (strategy mastery) and affective (anxiety) skills in addition to linguistic skills
#### Constraints
- The authors state these instructional implications require future examination; the study is correlational and cannot support causal conclusions

### Target Learners
- L2 English learners, particularly Chinese-speaking English majors

### Target Learning Goals
- Incidental vocabulary acquisition through reading
