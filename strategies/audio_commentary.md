# Audio Commentary

## Description
Audio commentary involves instructors and learners using audio recordings to provide personalized feedback on work. This approach can be more personal and nuanced compared to written feedback, allowing for tone and inflection to enhance communication. It typically involves recording verbal feedback and sharing it with the learner for review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to audio recording tools (e.g., smartphone, computer with microphone), a platform for sharing audio files, and clear guidelines for providing effective audio feedback.
#### Constraints
- May require more time to provide feedback compared to written comments. Accessibility concerns may arise for learners with hearing impairments unless transcripts are provided.

- Allows for more nuanced and personal communication compared to written feedback. Can enhance engagement by adding a human element to online feedback. Facilitates detailed explanations and guidance.

### Personalization
- Instructors can tailor their feedback to the individual learner, addressing specific strengths and weaknesses in their work.
