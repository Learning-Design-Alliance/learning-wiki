# Adapt a practical model of a coherent instructional system for reading and science improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on a case study from a U.S. school district, NWEA offers a practical model that school leaders can adapt to strengthen instruction across classrooms. The research brief “examines the practices and conditions of a coherent instructional system, and where and how it had the most impact,” focusing on reading and science improvement. The strategy is for leaders seeking system-level coherence rather than classroom-level changes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders need knowledge of the practices and conditions of a coherent instructional system as documented in the case study
- District-level coordination of instructional practices and conditions across classrooms
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single U.S. school district case study, as the page itself states
- Evidence base described is a single U.S. school district case study
