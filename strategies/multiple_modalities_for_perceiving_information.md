# Multiple Modalities for Perceiving Information

## Description
Sharing information through various modalities beyond text and images, such as audio, video, simulations, or interactive models. This approach ensures that learners with different sensory preferences and abilities can access the content effectively. It involves presenting information in multiple formats to cater to diverse learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diverse multimedia resources, software for creating and delivering content in multiple formats, and awareness of learner preferences.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful design to ensure coherence across modalities. Some learners may be overwhelmed by too many options.

### Target Learners

- All learners, especially those with sensory disabilities (e.g., blindness or deafness), learning disabilities, and diverse cultural backgrounds.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance comprehension, support diverse learning styles, improve engagement, accommodate sensory preferences and abilities

### Affordances
- Caters to various learning styles. Improves accessibility for learners with sensory impairments. Enhances understanding through multiple representations.

### Personalization
- Offer content in text, audio, video, and interactive formats. Allow learners to choose the modalities that best suit their needs and preferences.
