# Drive the demonstration coil with a car wiper motor for more efficient rotation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe for running this induction demonstration: after trying both options, the authors recommend powering the coil rotation with a car's wiper motor mounted via the belt system. They write that "A car's wiper motor also can serve even better, to rotate the coil", reporting that the motorized drive gives "more eﬃcient results" than manual rotation. This makes the demonstration practical for repeated classroom use.
