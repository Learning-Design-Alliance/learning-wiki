# Consult Fact-Checking Sites

## Description
Get used to regularly consulting fact-checking sites. These three nonpartisan sites below investigate the truth of various claims: FactCheck.org, Snopes.com, and Politifact.com. And to check the authenticity of images, teach your learners to reverse-Google them to find their origins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to fact-checking sites and reverse image search tools.
