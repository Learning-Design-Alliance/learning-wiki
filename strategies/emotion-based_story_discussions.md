# Emotion-Based Story Discussions

## Description
Emotion-based story discussions involve reading stories and having learners identify and discuss the characters' feelings. This activity helps learners connect emotions to specific events and understand different perspectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to age-appropriate stories. A facilitator to guide the discussion and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the chosen stories. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All age groups, including early childhood, elementary, middle school, and adult learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop emotional literacy, improve perspective-taking skills, promote empathetic understanding

### Affordances
- Uses stories as a relatable medium to explore emotions. Encourages critical thinking and empathetic responses.

### Personalization
- Stories can be selected to reflect diverse cultural backgrounds and experiences. Discussion prompts can be adapted to suit different age groups and learning levels.
