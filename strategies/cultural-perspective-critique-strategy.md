## Description
The editorial describes a cultural-perspective approach to reviewing a CALL study: "Shortt approaches the paper from a cultural perspective by initially inserting himself in the study and then analyzing the cultural context that could have enhanced the paper." This strategy combines researcher self-positioning with cultural-context analysis to strengthen critique of technology-design research.
