# Provide teacher training workshops and mentor support for virtual exchange practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The EVALUATE project organised two face-to-face teacher training workshops (Padova and León) and assigned "each virtual exchange partnership a mentor from the project consortium who provided support and expert advice to the teacher trainers as they ran their virtual exchanges". This combination of preparatory training and ongoing mentoring supported teacher trainers in implementing virtual exchanges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops and mentoring require project consortium infrastructure and funding.
#### Constraints
- Attendance at the second workshop was lower (15 vs 40), reflecting the smaller number of exchanges planned.

### Target Learners
- Teacher trainers in initial teacher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Capacity to design and facilitate virtual exchange
