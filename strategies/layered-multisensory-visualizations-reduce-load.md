# Design intuitive, multisensory, layered visualizations with evolving assessment to reduce cognitive load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's third design guideline calls for clear visual aids such as familiar graphs, heatmaps, and step-by-step animations to improve comprehension of complex mechanics concepts and reduce cognitive effort, together with layered visualizations that progressively introduce complex relations. It also recommends that evaluation extend beyond traditional testing: "evaluation approaches should extend beyond traditional testing to include engagement and conceptual understanding, supported by adaptive feedback and instructional scaffolding."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Visualizations must be clear, familiar, and layered so complex relations are introduced progressively
#### Constraints
- Poorly aligned or complex visualizations can hinder understanding, per the article's conclusion

### Target Learners
- engineering students with varied learning preferences

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of complex mechanics concepts with reduced extraneous cognitive load
