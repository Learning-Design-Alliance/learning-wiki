# Enrich secondary physics textbooks beyond official programs with historical notes, modern-physics perspectives, rich illustrations, and dialogued formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 theoretical, 1 review), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes Loedel Palumbo's 1940s textbook design: exploiting Kapelusz's modern illustration resources, he "trató de ofrecer una perspectiva más amplia que la que requerían los programas oficiales, agregando notas históricas y perspectivas de las ideas más modernas de la física", an approach the article says was absent from most Argentine textbooks of the time. He also experimented with dialogued formats — the didactic comedy Masa y Peso introducing mass and weight — and, in Enseñanza de la física, endorsed history's auxiliary role and everyday teaching resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a publisher with modern technical resources: "la Editorial Kapelusz adquirió recursos técnicos muy modernos que le permitían hacer un uso amplio de ilustraciones, diagramas y fotografías".
#### Constraints
- The article notes the broader modernizing transition "estuvo condicionada por los dictados de los programas oficiales de física, que decidían sobre el posible éxito de comercialización de esas obras".

### Target Learners
- Secondary-school students of mathematics, physics and astronomy; their teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning physics concepts with historical and modern-physics perspective beyond the minimum official program
