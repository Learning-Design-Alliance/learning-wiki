# Use validated self-assessment questionnaires to identify core aspects that help or hinder youth participation in digital citizenship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes developing and using a validated self-assessment instrument as a way to identify which aspects help or hinder youth participation and to inform education for digital citizenship. It states the study "intends to identify core aspects that help or hinder youth participation and contribute on how to educate youth and improve on digital citizenship through the development of digital and socio-civic skills." The DIGISOC questionnaire operationalizes this approach.
