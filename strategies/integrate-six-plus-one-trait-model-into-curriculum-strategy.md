# Integrate the 6+1 trait writing model into the language arts curriculum with rubric-based self-assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend that "training programs should integrate the 6+1 trait writing model into the language arts curriculum". The strategy trains students to apply the seven traits of good writing to assigned topics, use the model's rubrics to assess their own writing, and treat writing as a process of brainstorming, gathering data, outlining, drafting, editing, and writing final drafts. The authors state this leads to enhancement of writing skills, increasing of scores, and developing critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be trained on the traits of good writing and on assessing their writing using rubrics
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single quasi-experimental study with first-year pharmacy students at one university; the authors note upcoming studies are needed to explore the model's influence on students' multiple intelligences in different language areas
