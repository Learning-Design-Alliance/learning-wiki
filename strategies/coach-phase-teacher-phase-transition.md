# Coach-led initial implementation followed by teacher phase transition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
FSN uses a phased implementation in which "a trained coach delivers the first 5-7 days of the program (e.g., coach phase)" including behavioral lessons, the green-card game, and daily parent contact; between days 8-10 the coach transitions control to the teacher, who fully implements from day 11 onward. This sequencing pairs expert modeling with teacher handover and was supported by high fidelity and quality ratings in the RCT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained behavioral coach available for initial delivery
- Teacher training in FSN and classroom management principles before implementation
#### Constraints
- Implementation quality was lower during the teacher phase (M = .84) than the coach phase (M = .93)

### Target Learners
- preschool through grade 2 focus children and their classmates

### Target Learning Goals
- behavioral self-regulation and compliance with classroom expectations
