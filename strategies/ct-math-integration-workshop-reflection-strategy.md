# Integrate CT activities into weekly mathematics lessons with teacher workshops and reflective cycles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention model had teachers attend two workshops on CT and technology geared to grade-level mathematical content, then integrate CT work into five weekly mathematics lessons over two school years. Researchers alternated classroom observations with reflection meetings described as "a formative intervention, where the teachers and researcher (author number one) discuss the previous observation", helping teachers develop teaching and make didactical choices for integrating CT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers willing to spend time developing their practice and collaborate closely with researchers
- Teacher workshops on CT and grade-specific mathematical content
#### Constraints
- Teacher selection was voluntary, which impacts the ability to generalize data

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in grades 2–3

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics: number knowledge, algebra, geometry
