# Deploy senior postgraduates as one-on-one real-time skills mentors during operation practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
During in-class operation practice, postgraduates provide immediate, individualized correction of detailed problems such as "convergence control" and "suture stitch spacing grasp", while the teacher focuses on common difficulties. The article reports this reduced the student-teacher ratio from 1:40 to 1:8 and extended average operational guidance time per student, addressing the delayed-feedback bottleneck of large-class teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Postgraduates selected with at least two years of clinical and academic experience and trained in a standardized mentoring protocol
#### Constraints
- Additional contact time from mentoring may independently contribute to outcomes, per the article's own confounding limitation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate stomatology students in large-class skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- operational precision in clinical skills
- immediate error correction
