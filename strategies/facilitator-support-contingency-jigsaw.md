# Use trained facilitator support and contingency planning to mitigate jigsaw's coverage and participation risks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students reported that "some group members are hesitant and not effective in explaining" and that a failed explanation causes peers to lose that part of the topic, the article recommends a trained facilitator helping the affected group, plus structured guidance and contingency plans to address incomplete topic coverage. It also suggests longer sessions or improved time management to allow more in-depth discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained facilitator available to support groups where explanation fails
- Contingency plans and structured guidance to prevent incomplete topic coverage
#### Constraints
- Sessions as implemented left insufficient discussion time; mitigation requires longer sessions or better time management

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- complete topic coverage in peer learning
- effective peer explanation and knowledge transfer
