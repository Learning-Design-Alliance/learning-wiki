# Classroom Games

## Description
Classroom games transform review time into engaging, collaborative activities that motivate learners and reinforce learning. These games can take many forms, from board game adaptations to interactive question-and-answer formats. Key elements include clear rules, defined objectives, and opportunities for learners to actively participate and compete in a supportive environment. The goal is to make review fun, reduce anxiety, and promote deeper understanding through active recall and peer interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires planning and preparation to design or select appropriate games. Materials may include board games, cards, whiteboards, or digital platforms. Clear rules and objectives must be communicated.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to manage large classes or diverse skill levels. Some learners may be more focused on winning than on learning. Requires careful monitoring to ensure fairness and inclusivity.

### Personalization
- Games can be adapted to different learning styles and content areas. Rules can be modified to accommodate diverse learner needs and promote inclusivity.
