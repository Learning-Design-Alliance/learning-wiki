# SOAR Feedback Method

## Description
The SOAR method helps maximize the impact of feedback on student writing by ensuring it is Specific, Ongoing, Action-Oriented, and Reasonable. Specific feedback ties comments to particular elements of the writing (words, phrases, sentences, paragraphs) and addresses both the content (what) and the writing itself (how). Ongoing feedback is provided throughout the writing process, not just on the final product. Action-oriented feedback helps students take specific steps to improve their writing and build a growth mindset. Reasonable feedback is focused and avoids overwhelming students by selecting a specific area for feedback with each assignment and using 'Glow and Grow' comments to highlight strengths and areas for improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to be trained in the SOAR method, allocate time for ongoing feedback, and have access to tools for providing feedback (e.g., Google Docs, Kaizena).
#### Constraints
- Requires significant time and effort from the teacher to provide specific and ongoing feedback. Students may become overwhelmed if feedback is not reasonable or focused.

### Affordances
- Provides clear, actionable steps for students to improve their writing, encourages self-reflection, and fosters a growth mindset by focusing on progress and improvement rather than just error correction. It allows instructors to provide targeted feedback that is more likely to be understood and acted upon by students.

### Personalization
- Teachers can adapt the focus of the feedback to meet the specific needs of students or the learning objectives of the assignment. Students can choose which area of their writing they want feedback on.

## Assessment Evidence
- Improvement in student writing quality over time, increased student engagement with the writing process, and positive changes in student attitudes towards writing and feedback.

## Impact
- High-quality feedback enhances student learning and engagement. Specificity ensures comments connect directly to the work. Ongoing feedback supports iterative development. Action-oriented suggestions empower students to make improvements. Reasonableness prevents overwhelming students with too much information.
