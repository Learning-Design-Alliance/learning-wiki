# Express Confidence in Learners

## Description
Another way to communicate positive expectations is by directly telling the learners that they have the ability to do well. Express your belief in their capabilities to handle difficult assignments or improve their behavior. This can empower the learners to work hard and behave appropriately to prove that the instructor's confidence in them is justified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be sincere when expressing confidence in their learners' abilities.
#### Constraints
- Learners may not believe the instructor, especially if they are struggling.
