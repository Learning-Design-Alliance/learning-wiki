# AI system developers should ensure human-centric design, institutional leaders should stay updated on global AI regulations, and LA experts should engage policymakers through dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy distills the article's Notes for Practice into implementable practices for the LA community. The article states that "AI system developers should ensure that AI systems are human-centric and prioritize user needs," that researchers and institutional leaders should stay updated and comply with global AI regulations, and that through dialogue, LA and AI experts should work with policymakers and implementers to understand the trade-offs of specific regulations as they are proposed. These practices aim to ensure legality, minimize risks, and keep AI-driven LA systems ethical and compliant.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developers must prioritize user needs in AI systems; institutional leaders must monitor and comply with evolving global AI regulations
#### Constraints
- Regulations differ across jurisdictions and are constantly evolving, so compliance practices must be revisited as policies change

### Target Learners
- AI system developers
- Learning analytics researchers
- Institutional leaders and policymakers

### Target Learning Goals
- Ensuring AI-driven learning analytics systems are human-centric, legally compliant, and ethically deployed
