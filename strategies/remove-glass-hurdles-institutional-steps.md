# Institutional steps to remove the glass hurdles facing women physics Ph.D. students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend steps academic institutes should take to remove the barriers they call glass hurdles. The article states that institutes "are not taking care of leveling the playing ground" and lists concrete actions: harassment prevention programs, an inclusive discrimination-free environment, family-adapted policy, higher fellowships for student parents, and expanded psychological care, especially for female students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Academic institutes must act, since the gender-specific hurdles are hidden to the academic system
#### Constraints
- The additional difficulties women experience provide only a partial explanation for women's low representation in physics

### Target Learners
- physics Ph.D. students
- female doctoral students in STEM

### Target Learning Goals
- equal opportunity to succeed in doctoral physics studies and academic careers
