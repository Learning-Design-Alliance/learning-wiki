# Use a low-threshold online training plus a SMART template to prepare students for self-created learning outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends combining a short online module introducing learning theory, the SMART framework, and Bloom's taxonomy with a digital SMART template including examples, so students can formulate meaningful learning outcomes before electives. The authors state that "even minimal structural support – such as a short online module and a simple template for designing learning outcomes – can make a meaningful difference" and that the low-threshold online format and minimal faculty workload make the approach transferable to other faculties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A digital SMART template with examples guiding students through defining each learning outcome and assigning it to a Bloom's domain
#### Constraints
- Manual transfer of learning objectives into a digital platform created unsustainable workload at larger scale; future iterations should use a university-based online platform

### Target Learners
- medical students preparing for clinical electives

### Target Learning Goals
- formulating measurable learning outcomes
- self-oriented learning
