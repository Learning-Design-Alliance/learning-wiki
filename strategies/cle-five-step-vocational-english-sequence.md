# Five-step CLE instructional sequence for vocational English classes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article breaks the CLE process with reciprocal peer teaching into five implementable steps: (1) the teacher introduces the material and demonstrates the activity with a clear, slow, step-by-step oral explanation; (2) students orally review the materials before doing the activity on their own or in small groups; (3) the teacher demonstrates negotiating a group text and students negotiate text in groups of four; (4) each group makes and decorates its group book, with students taking turns as teacher to check mistakes; (5) groups use their book to play language games such as spelling, reading, and sentence writing games.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One week of orientation and training for teachers in the CLE process and reciprocal peer teaching strategies
- Trial-out teaching units of lesson plans approved by the researcher or program designer
#### Constraints
- Steps a and b differ for text-based units, where the teacher starts by reading the text with students and asking them to recall, discuss, and role-play the content

### Target Learners
- second-year vocational English students

### Target Learning Goals
- vocational English listening, speaking, reading and writing
