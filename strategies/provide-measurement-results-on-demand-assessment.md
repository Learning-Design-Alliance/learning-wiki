# Provide measurement results on demand in written modeling assessments rather than virtual instruments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the Phase 3 free-response assessment, the authors recommend letting students decide when and what to measure while the assessment supplies the result, rather than simulating an instrument. One way "to allow students to make measurements is to include an image of an oscilloscope screen that the students can use to measure the output and input signals," along with the ability to see a DMM measurement result. This design follows from findings that measurements dominate and connect the modeling pathway.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The assessment must give students the opportunity to see the results of measurements to guide their modeling process.
#### Constraints
- The authors contrast this with allowing students to interact with a virtual measurement instrument like a PhET simulation.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics laboratory students

### Target Learning Goals
- model-based reasoning
- interpreting measurement results to guide model revision
