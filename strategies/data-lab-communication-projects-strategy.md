# Communication projects as individual summative assessments replacing the lab practical

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Communication projects (CPs) are the sole summative assessment, completed individually in place of a traditional lab practical. In DL1 students write a proposal summarizing a prior experiment and proposing further investigation; in DL2 they create and present a research poster. Each project is "shared with and reviewed by their primary instructor and their peers in the class," and a first lower-stakes CP with feedback precedes the second to reduce pressure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubrics with prior- and future-work sections (DL1) and a presentation component (DL2)
- Formative supports such as CPHW assignments and notebook feedback aligned to the CP rubrics
#### Constraints
- CPs are not inherently collaborative even though experiments are done in groups

### Target Learners
- Introductory algebra-based physics laboratory students

### Target Learning Goals
- Communicating experimental process, results, and analysis in authentic forms
