# Incorporate key factors of integral listening-speaking instruction into EFL program development and curriculum reviews

## Description
The study recommends that EFL program development and curriculum reviews incorporate the key factors the study identified as influencing integral instruction of listening and speaking, namely using real-life listening materials, appropriate application of listening phases, and appropriate use of language features. The article states "EFL program development and curriculum reviews incorporate key factors influencing integral instruction of listening and speaking in the present study" and says it provided recommendations for EFL teachers and contexts beyond the studied settings.
