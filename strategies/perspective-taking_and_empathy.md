# Perspective-taking and Empathy

## Description
Take the time to consider each student’s perspective and the challenges they may be facing. Show genuine interest in your students, ask about their opinions, interests, and background, and listen as if you might be wrong. Use nonjudgmental, solution-focused problem-solving. Use and model perspective-taking and empathy to recognize each student’s strengths and understand their daily challenges. Avoid misunderstandings that harm relationships.
