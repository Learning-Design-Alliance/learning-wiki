Design ICT-integration teacher training that is subject-oriented and workshop-based over a long period¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because integrating ICT effectively into teaching is not straightforward, the article recommends that teacher training for ICT integration be tied to the subject being taught and delivered as sustained workshops rather than one-off sessions. The article states that 'teachers training programs of ICT integration has to subject oriented and carried out in a workshop manner over a long period of time.' This follows from the finding that the ICT treatment produced a small achievement gain requiring deliberate adoption of new methods and materials.
