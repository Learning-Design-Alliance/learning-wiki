Build a repository of cross-platform, customizable, pedagogically evaluated game-based learning applications to support educators¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The editorial recommends that, to support educators in the era of shifting to digital, the field "must quickly move toward building a repository of applications for game-based learning". These applications should run on a variety of devices and platforms (Android, Windows, iOS) or as web-based applications, accommodate all users, and be customizable to educator needs. Because few available applications have been pedagogically evaluated, the editorial names pedagogical evaluation as the focus of future research, bridging commercial and research-driven games.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Applications must run on a variety of devices and platforms (Android, Windows, iOS) or as web-based applications to accommodate all users
  • Applications should be customizable to meet the specific needs of the educator
  • Applications should be pedagogically evaluated

Constraints¶

  • As it stands, few of the available free or low-cost applications have been pedagogically evaluated

Target Learners¶

  • educators navigating the transition to remote and online learning

Target Learning Goals¶

  • supporting educators in selecting and using game-based learning applications for specific learning tasks

Claims¶

  • Commercial games are attractive and accessible to game-based learning advocates but their pedagogical value is often not documented [+W]
  • The effect of mobile game-based learning on learners' engagement and achievement is still to be documented in research [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use mobile game-based learning findings to guide practice and pursue smartphone-enabled virtual reality and design-pedagogy-impact research directions
  • Use review-derived criteria to evaluate commercial learning games when educators must select quickly
