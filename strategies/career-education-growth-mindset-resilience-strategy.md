# Incorporate career education and growth mindset programs, cultivate positive academic emotions, and improve learning conditions to foster resilience in disadvantaged students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's educational recommendations target the protective factors its regression identified. Teachers and psychology teachers are advised to "incorporate career education and develop growth mindset development programs to stimulate students' own intrinsic motivation". Parents are urged to create flexible communication and positive feedback, schools to improve access to knowledge and information, and teachers to cultivate positive academic emotions so students engage more actively in teaching activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers and psychology teachers in schools to deliver career education and mindset programs
- Requires parental communication and school improvement of learning conditions
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement across Chinese, math, and English or science
- Academic resilience against low family socioeconomic status
