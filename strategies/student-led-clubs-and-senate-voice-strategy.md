# Student-led after-school club creation and student senate structures to expand student voice and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Roswell community schools shifted decision-making to students: at Sierra, all after-school clubs are determined by students, who brainstorm ideas, gather peer interest, recruit a paid teacher host, and seek approval from the coordinator and principal; renaming tutoring as Homework Help removed stigma and improved voluntary attendance. At University High, the elected student senate runs announcements, operates monthly food distributions, created Worry Walls for hygiene products, and planned the school's first prom in over a decade. A staff member said students "feel like they're valued."
