# Structure health education sessions as homework search, small-group discussion, group reporting, and behavioral-change planning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A repeatable session recipe drawn from the intervention: assign a homework in which learners "search the assigned topic through books, on the internet, or using any other media"; in the next class participants raise and discuss ideas; an overview of group work results is shared with the class; participants then plan and implement healthy behaviors and review progress in group work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly sessions, small groups of 4 to 5, homework search tasks, and regular review of healthy-lifestyle progress within group work
#### Constraints
- Tested with older adults with low health literacy in a community setting over 24 weeks

### Target Learners
- older adults with low health literacy

### Target Learning Goals
- health literacy, self-management, and daily-life health behavior change
