# Optimizing Natural Lighting

## Description
Incorporating natural lighting in classrooms boosts morale, reduces off-task behavior, and improves test scores. Maximizing exposure to natural daylight enhances student learning and overall well-being.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate windows and skylights, proper window placement, light control measures.
#### Constraints
- May not be feasible in all environments, requires careful planning and design.
