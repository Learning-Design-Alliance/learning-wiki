Match candidate skills to school needs using multiple data sources, prioritizing highest-need schools¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the Talent Development domain, the guide's practice description directs districts to "Use multiple sources of data to match candidate skills and competencies to a school's needs, prioritizing the highest need schools." The LEA example describes a model for selecting and placing teachers and leaders with turnaround competencies, using "extended candidate observations in various settings" to assess readiness and potential, and creating in-house district preparation programs to develop future turnaround leaders and teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Human resources systems able to gather and use multiple measures and data sources in selection

Constraints¶

  • Applies to turnaround contexts where vacancies are expected during the improvement process

Target Learners¶

  • District leaders, human resources staff, principals

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Recruiting and developing staff with turnaround competencies
