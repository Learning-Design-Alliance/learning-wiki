# Gather extrapolation and implication evidence, and formally measure content validity, in subsequent SJT evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors set out next steps for the SJT programme: re-evaluate items with poor difficulty or discrimination indices for ambiguous language or deletion, and move beyond scoring and generalization evidence to the remaining Kane inferences. They state that "at this point of evidence gathering, it is yet inappropriate to assume that the supporting findings justify the proposed use and interpretation of the scores", and that making the final judgment depends on extrapolation and implication inferences, which are often neglected in the literature. They also recommend formal quantitative content-validity assessment in future research.
