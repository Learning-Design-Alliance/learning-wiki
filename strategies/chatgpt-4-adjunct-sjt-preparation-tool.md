# Use ChatGPT-4.0 as an adjunct practice tool for medical applicants preparing for the AAMC SJT, with human judgment retained

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, given ChatGPT-4.0's demonstrated proficiency on the AAMC SJT, "it could serve as an adjunct tool for students to practice and re ne their responses for complex scenarios" on the AAMC PREview. By engaging with AI-driven simulations, students may gain insight into effective professional behaviors. The authors stress LLMs are not a substitute for human situational understanding and ethical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the model's demonstrated proficiency; the article grounds this recommendation in ChatGPT-4.0's adjusted score of 71.2% on the 2021 practice exam
#### Constraints
- The authors state LLMs are "not a substitute for the depth of situational understanding and ethical reasoning of human judgment"
- All three LLMs made similar kinds of mistakes on complex social situations

### Target Learners
- prospective medical students preparing for the AAMC SJT/PREview

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating effectiveness of behavioral responses to professional dilemmas
- pre-professional social intelligence competencies
