Dormitory-based independent learning (muraja'ah) with teacher, mudabbir, peer, and independent review¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The model includes a dormitory phase of self-regulated learning intended to increase student responsibility and motivation. The article cites Bell and Akroyd and Chamot on self-regulated learning, in which students "have control over the learning process through the knowledge and application of appropriate strategies". Figure 1 lists muraja'ah (review) learning from four sources: teacher, mudabbir, peers, and independent study. Students develop confidence in decision-making, reflection on learning, and judgment about whether to try another approach.
