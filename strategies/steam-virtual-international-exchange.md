# Use STEAM and virtual international exchange workshops to connect underserved students through art and particle physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper describes STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) as a way to bring the arts into STEM motivation and open doors for students on different paths. It reports Agnes Chavez's STEMarts Lab, in which during lockdown "Students in underserved rural communities of New Mexico collaborated with students in Portugal through a 7-week interdisciplinary workshop" exploring the universe through art, particle physics, astrophysics, native science, and philosophy, culminating in a mixed-reality installation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual and augmented reality platforms; expert conversations; interdisciplinary facilitation
#### Constraints
- The reported case arose during COVID-19 lockdown, when virtual exchange addressed isolation in schools
