# Provide academic support and enrichment to improve academic performance of at-risk students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's third recommendation targets students' academic performance through individual or small-group support in test-taking skills, study skills, or targeted subjects such as reading, writing, or math. It also advises extra study time and credit recovery 'through after school, Saturday school, or summer enrichment programs.' The panel specifies it should be implemented in conjunction with the other recommendations.
