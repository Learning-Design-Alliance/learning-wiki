Use AI tools to support assessment, feedback and monitoring of learner progress¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article outlines concrete AI-supported assessment practices teachers can adopt: AI-driven writing assistants that evaluate and grade written work and identify features such as word usage and grammar, chatbots as virtual teacher assistants asking students questions, and learning analytics dashboards visualizing learning patterns such as utilization, satisfaction and achievement. Teachers should "learn how to utilize various types of AI technologies to direct monitoring learner progress, facilitate provision of feedback and allow themselves to assess and adapt their teaching strategies".
