# Focus Projects on Issues of Justice

## Description
Projects center on authentic issues that matter to students and their communities, impacting their lives and the lives of those they care about.  This promotes engagement and emphasizes the role of education in addressing social justice issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creating a classroom environment that supports open and honest discussions about social justice. Demands resources and materials that reflect diverse perspectives and experiences.
#### Constraints
- May raise sensitive topics that require careful facilitation. Can be challenging to balance curriculum goals with student-driven social justice issues.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- K-12 learners, particularly effective for engaging students in discussions about social justice and equity.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase student engagement, promote awareness of social justice issues, empower students to act as change agents, connect learning to real-world impact.

### Affordances
- Connects learning to real-world issues, making it more relevant and meaningful. Empowers students to see themselves as agents of change in their communities. Fosters a sense of purpose and responsibility.

### Personalization
- Projects can be tailored to address specific issues relevant to students' local communities. Allows students to explore topics related to their cultural backgrounds and experiences.
