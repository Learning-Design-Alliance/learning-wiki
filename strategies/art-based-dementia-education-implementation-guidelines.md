# Deliver artistic experiential dementia education weekly over 4–8 weeks with direct interaction, guided creative expression, and facilitated reflective discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on its findings that artistic interventions were most effective, the review recommends standardized implementation guidelines: "art-based programs may be delivered once weekly over 4 to 8 weeks, with each session lasting approximately 90 min", incorporating direct interaction with people living with dementia, guided creative expression such as storytelling or visual arts, and facilitated reflective discussion. For virtual formats, it recommends immersive VR platforms with spatial audio, haptic feedback, and dynamic narrative content, plus structured debriefing and emotion-focused reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Direct interaction with people living with dementia
- Guided creative expression and facilitated reflective discussion as key components
#### Constraints
- Virtual experiential learning showed inconsistent outcomes in current studies and needs continued refinement of virtual tools

### Target Learners
- healthcare undergraduate students in time- or resource-constrained institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- empathy and reduced stigma towards people with dementia
- person-centered dementia care competencies
