Use low-stakes, multiple-entry warm-ups like "Which One Doesn't Belong?" to reduce fear of wrong answers¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Teachers opened lessons with warm-up questions projected on the board that "offers multiple points of entry for differently skilled students and does not have a single correct answer." Students first shared with an elbow neighbor and then discussed as a whole class, with no grade attached. Across classrooms, students' eagerness to participate in "Which One Doesn't Belong?" suggested it may reduce anxiety about peers judging a wrong answer, since there is not just one correct answer.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • students with math anxiety
