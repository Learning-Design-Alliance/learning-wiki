# Spreadsheet homework sequence: reproduce small-system tables, then scale up and add entropy columns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors present the interacting-solids material in class with handouts of the figures, then assign homework in which students reproduce Fig. 1, modify parameters to larger systems (e.g., 100 and 200 oscillators with 100 energy units), identify most and least probable macrostates, and finally add entropy columns and compute temperatures via T = ΔU/ΔS at equilibrium and at qA=60. This graduated sequence moves students from brute-force counting to large-system irreversible behavior.
