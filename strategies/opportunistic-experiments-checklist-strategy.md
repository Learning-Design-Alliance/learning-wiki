# Use a checklist of key questions when conducting opportunistic experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide supplies "a checklist of key questions to consider when conducting opportunistic experiments," giving policymakers and researchers a concrete procedural tool for deciding whether and how to pursue an opportunistic experiment. The checklist complements the guide's discussion of issues to consider when identifying candidate experiments and its outline of the critical steps needed to complete them.
