Implement the layered curriculum with teacher-as-guide, choice-based activity lists and point-based progression¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a concrete implementation recipe: prepare an activities list across the C, B and A layers using tools such as presentations, computer programs, book abstracts, posters, poetry-story writing and video recordings; assign points by difficulty (Layer C activities toward 65-70 points, Layer B and A activities 15 points each); let students choose activities and collect them in a progress file while repeatedly asking why they chose them. The teacher's role is mostly to guide: "The teacher informs the students about the concepts o f the units to be taught by making short presentations, monitors each student, when necessary directs and encourages, and grades the activities and tasks they have completed".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Activities list prepared in accordance with the C, B and A layers with point values
  • Evaluation criteria prepared in advance for each type of activity
  • Teacher time to monitor each student and grade completed tasks

Constraints¶

  • Activities should attract students' attention, lead to new learning, and take students beyond the Layer C level

Target Learners¶

  • 6th grade secondary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning science topics in ways matched to learning styles
  • developing responsibility and research skills

Affordances¶

  • Layered Curriculum Cba Model

Claims¶

  • Students exposed to the layered curriculum chose activities they believed would reinforce learning and reported increased motivation and responsibility [+W]
  • When choosing Layered Curriculum activities, students prioritized enjoyable activities and activities they believed they could do [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Apply Layered Curriculum in subjects students find difficult to learn
