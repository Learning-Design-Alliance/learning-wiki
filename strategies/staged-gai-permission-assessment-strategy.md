# Stage GAI use in assessment: permit exploration, restrict independent reasoning, require justification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes concrete ethical boundaries for GAI use within LEARN-informed assessment. GAI 'may be used for exploratory purposes such as brainstorming, generating alternative perspectives, receiving formative feedback, or testing assumptions', but 'should be restricted when tasks require independent interpretation of evidence, final causal reasoning, personal reflection, or demonstration of disciplinary judgement'. Ethical use is operationalised through transparent documentation: AI-use logs, prompt records, reflective justification, and disclosure of AI assistance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Transparent documentation including AI-use logs, prompt records, and reflective justification of how AI outputs were evaluated and transformed
#### Constraints
- Submitting unmodified AI-generated work, fabricating sources, concealing AI assistance, or relying on AI to replace required reasoning are considered inappropriate uses

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- ethical AI use
- independent reasoning
- academic integrity
