# Deliver patient communication training virtually and build an institution-independent virtual training network

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy of delivering patient communication training online and building a shared virtual training network so institutions lacking local expertise, funding, or bandwidth can still offer it. The authors reason that "since the training proved to be effective even when given online, this allows us to consider creating a virtual training network for patient communication education in medical physics that is not institution dependent".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual delivery infrastructure for the lecture and the simulated patient encounters
#### Constraints
- The article notes certain aspects, such as eye contact, "may be different in the two settings" and were not explicitly addressed in the lecture given in this training

### Target Learners
- medical physics graduate students
- institutions lacking faculty comfortable leading communication training

### Target Learning Goals
- patient communication skills practiced in a telemedicine setting
