# Design a blockchain-based trusted achievement record with a friendly user interface to hide blockchain complexity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends exploring the design of a blockchain-based system providing a trusted achievement record service, with user needs in view. Because most stakeholders lack blockchain knowledge, it states "it is recommended to invest effort in the design of a friendly user interface to facilitate dealing with the complexity and usability of this promising technology". The strategy targets validation efficiency and usability for students, employers and academic staff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The system must support diverse document storage, including educational certificates and other achievement documents identified by respondents
#### Constraints
- End-users' poor comprehension of blockchain technology is a major hurdle that the interface design must address

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- employers
- academic and administrative staff

### Target Learning Goals
- trusted recording, sharing and validation of learning achievements and qualifications
