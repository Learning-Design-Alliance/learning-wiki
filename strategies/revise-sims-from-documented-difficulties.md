# Revise simulations in response to documented student difficulties by adding visualizations that show when errors do and do not occur

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors responded to two common difficulties from the 2014 trials by replacing the error-probability graph with a most-recent-key-bits panel that lets Alice and Bob compare 20 bits for errors, and by introducing turquoise highlighting of all measurements where Eve chose the wrong basis, so students can see that not all of these measurements lead to an error. They also modified the activity with two additional questions asking students to explain using a specific example how an error occurs and whether an error occurs each time Eve chooses the wrong orientation. The 2016 trial showed both targeted questions answered significantly better.
