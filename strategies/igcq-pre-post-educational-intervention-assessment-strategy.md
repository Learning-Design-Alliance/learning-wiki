# Use the IGCQ in pre- and post-intervention assessments of educational interventions on resident glycemic control knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend deploying the IGCQ to measure the effect of resident education on inpatient glycemic control. They state the tool "could be used to evaluate the effect of educational interventions on resident physician knowledge of IGC" and that future studies could "utilize the survey tool in pre- and post-intervention assessments". It might also be modified to focus on outpatient glycemic control by reframing Likert and knowledge questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- External validation of the IGCQ before wider use, as the authors indicate future studies should seek
#### Constraints
- Primarily appropriate for assessing resident physician perspectives on glycemic control of the hospitalized patient; outpatient use requires reframing items

### Target Learners
- Resident physicians managing inpatient glycemic control

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving resident physician knowledge of inpatient glycemic control and addressing barriers
