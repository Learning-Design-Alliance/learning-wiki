# Educator Ambassador model: master teachers train other educators in mission-linked professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Educator Ambassadors are "master teachers who engage other educators in professional development that is directly connected to teaching practices". The model recruits a small national cadre (currently 16 educators) who annually train thousands of other educators at local, state, regional and national teachers' meetings using SSU-approved workshops, and who receive biennial week-long training at SSU including mini-courses on gravitation, the dark universe and particle physics. This train-the-trainer structure multiplies the reach of mission-linked classroom materials.
