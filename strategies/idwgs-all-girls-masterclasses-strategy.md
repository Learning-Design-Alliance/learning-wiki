# International Day of Women and Girls in Science masterclasses with all-girl cohorts and women physicists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
IMC piloted special masterclasses for the International Day of Women and Girls in Science on February 10–11, 2017. "These were masterclasses from all four main LHC experiments for approximately 320 girls in 10 masterclasses." All students were girls and, to the extent possible, the physicists involved were women, and videoconference discussions included topics relating to studying science and being a scientist as a woman. The article reports these were received with great enthusiasm and the program was set to continue in February 2018.
