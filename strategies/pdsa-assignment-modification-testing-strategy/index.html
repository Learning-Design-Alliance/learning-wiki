Run PDSA cycles in which researcher-practitioner teams test modified assignments against as-is versions across multiple classrooms¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper recommends organizing improvement work as Plan-Do-Study-Act cycles in which teachers and researchers create "two or more versions of some assignments: the 'as-is' version and a modified version" and use a platform like Terracotta to test whether the modified version works better across at least three different teachers and classrooms. Iterative cycles test one idea at a time, keeping what works and discarding what does not, and improvements are checked when implemented by different teachers and settings.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A networked improvement community of researchers and practitioners
  • A digital platform capable of randomizing assignment versions and collecting detailed data

Constraints¶

  • Other research methods beyond what the platform supports would likely be needed for summative evaluations of combined improvements across the district

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students, including English learners and students with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving reading comprehension in STEM assignments and learning in STEM courses
