# Align simulation-based BLS training with INACSL guidelines including interactive lectures, skill demonstrations, high-fidelity manikin practice, scenarios, and GAS-model structured debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a structured simulation-based BLS training module "aligned with INACSL guidelines" that included "interactive lectures, skill demonstrations, hands-on practice using high-fidelity manikins, and simulation scenarios." Each simulation session was followed by structured debriefing based on the GAS Model (Gather, Analyse and Summarise), facilitated by trained nursing faculty. Students in groups of eight repeated and observed training on days 15, 30, 45 and 60 from day 1.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained nursing faculty facilitators, with researcher training at an authorized simulation center based on INACSL standards
#### Constraints
- Training was delivered in groups of eight members with scheduled repeat observations on days 15, 30, 45 and 60

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate Bachelor of Science in Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic Life Support skills and confidence for responding to real-life emergencies
