# Differentiate AI-integration support by learner profile rather than applying a uniform approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends tailoring AI-integration support to the four latent learner profiles instead of one uniform policy. For the majority Passive Followers, "structured AI literacy training and gradual exposure to AI tools may help transform passive technology users into active self-directed learners." For Cognitively Advanced but Constrained students, institutional normalization efforts such as explicit faculty endorsement, peer mentoring, and curriculum-embedded AI applications may relieve normative barriers; Active Adopters can serve as peer mentors or AI champions. The authors conclude that "a uniform approach to AI integration in medical education is unlikely to be optimal."
