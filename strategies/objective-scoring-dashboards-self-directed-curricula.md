## Description
The review recommends that "Incorporating objective, structured scoring systems into self-directed curricula can enhance learner motivation and allow for progress monitoring." It notes self-assessment, though subject to bias, remains valuable for reinforcing self-awareness and skill tracking, and suggests progressive dashboards such as infographics for dependent learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objective structured scoring systems (e.g., OSATS, GRS) integrated into the self-directed curriculum
#### Constraints
- Self-assessment is subject to potential bias, as the review states

### Target Learners
- medical students transitioning from dependent to autonomous learning
- surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
