# Implement DICE with 20-40 students in heterogeneous groups of 3-5, over a full day or several days, with most time on design steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend a classroom size of approximately 20-40 students, with small groups of 3-5 students from heterogeneous scientific backgrounds, so each group consists of students with different strengths and learning styles. DICE can be implemented within a full day of teaching or over several days: the morning for steps 1-3 and the afternoon for steps 4-6 ending with abstract presentations. The article advises that "Students should not be rushed through these steps and should be provided with sufficient guidance and support to find an adequate research question, study design, and set up the simulations."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of students with different strengths and learning styles
- Going through an example of a simulated study with Stata or R code in front of the class can help students who require more support with the computer code
#### Constraints
- Some students require more support to understand and use the provided computer code, particularly in settings with fewer students experienced in coding

### Target Learners
- Graduate students in medical sciences, including public health and epidemiology

### Target Learning Goals
- Statistical reasoning, study design, coding, and scientific communication
