# Have trainees choose critical respondents and train respondents in giving MSF feedback to strengthen narrative feedback quality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Future directions section the authors recommend that, in a supportive learning environment with formative MSF purposes, trainees be guided to choose respondents wisely — "specifically go for respondents that may be critical and are willing to give honest feedback" — rather than only positive ones. They further suggest respondents receive some amount of training in MSF, as part of more general training in feedback giving and receiving for both trainees and supervisors, to promote a good learning environment and feedback culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MSF framed as having only formative purposes within a supportive learning environment
#### Constraints
- The authors note trainee choice of respondents has advantages of feasibility and credibility, and that later studies have challenged earlier findings of no score differences by assessor choice

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical trainees and their clinical supervisors and respondents

### Target Learning Goals
- giving and receiving honest, constructive narrative feedback
