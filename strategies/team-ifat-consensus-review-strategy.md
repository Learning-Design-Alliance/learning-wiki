# Run a team-based answer-until-correct scratch-off review before exams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe: students first complete a multiple-choice test independently, then are randomly assigned to teams of 4 or 5 to reach consensus answers, and work through an IF-AT scratch-off card to check each consensus answer. There is no grade attached; team scores based on extra boxes scratched may be posted to "boost team rivalry". The exercise took about 30 minutes of class time with 100 students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- IF-AT scratch-off forms
- pre-exam objective review questions
- teams of 4 or 5 students formed after individual completion
#### Constraints
- No grade was associated with the exercise in this implementation
- about 30 minutes of class time needed

### Target Learners
- introductory accounting undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- reviewing objective course content
- peer discussion and consensus building before exams
