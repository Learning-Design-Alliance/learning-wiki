# Catalyse member projects with small-scale seed grants and skilled cross-disciplinary fellows

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (3 design), `q1` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Twice-yearly competitions award project-support prizes of £500-1000, with a permissive success criterion of whether the idea is exciting and feasible; the authors discovered promising ideas also arose in pure-research projects after initially preferencing commercial outcomes. Highly skilled cross-disciplinary fellows give technical and project advice, support spaces, deliver courses and events, and test new protocols and technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding for small prizes; clear contracts for Hackers-in-Residence to avoid free-riding; a clear onboarding process for fellows
#### Constraints
- Hackers-in-Residence free-riding by a minority required clear contracts; fellows blending research, teaching and equipment mastery present challenges to new starters from any one discipline

### Target Learners
- university members developing research or invention projects

### Target Learning Goals
- project development, technical skills, cross-disciplinary research practice
