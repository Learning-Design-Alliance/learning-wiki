# Four ecosystem recommendations: experiment with currency forms, protect time for inquiry, include everyone in team learning, and invest in adaptive platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on recent developments, early progress, and current challenges, the report recommends that the ecosystem focus on policies and practices that: create incentives for local educators to experiment with different forms of currency for earning micro-credentials; establish professional development processes and work schedules providing teachers more time and support for the long-term inquiry required by most micro-credentials; encourage everyone in the system — teachers, paraprofessionals, and administrators — to participate in ways that encourage team learning and leadership development; and invest in innovative and adaptive platforms offering educators voice and choice in how they use micro-credentials that fit their particular needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policy leaders must treat educator professional learning as personalized and competency-based if students are to experience such a system
#### Constraints
- The report cautions that the lack of perfection in the current micro-credential ecosystem should not stand in the way of progress, while acknowledging many questions remain

### Target Learners
- Education policy leaders, states, districts, unions, and all educators including paraprofessionals and administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Advancing personalized, competency-based professional learning and educator leadership
