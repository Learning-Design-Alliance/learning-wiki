# Involve instructors of each country in training the next country's group to build a multinational cooperation network

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An implementable cooperation recipe: instructors trained in one country join the educational team and help develop the program in the next country. The article states this created "an increasingly broad multinational cooperation group in which they share very different experiences", diminishing the coordinating group's role until it acted only as a general coordinating bond. The final activity was a Latin-American instructor course with instructors from the six participating countries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sequential country-by-country rollout so trained instructors can join the next deployment
#### Constraints
- The program was developed in several nearby countries of Central America, which allowed communication between members; each program must analyze regional features to exploit cooperation possibilities

### Target Learners
- Resuscitation instructors and health professionals across participating countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Paediatric CPR training capability and sustainable multinational cooperation
