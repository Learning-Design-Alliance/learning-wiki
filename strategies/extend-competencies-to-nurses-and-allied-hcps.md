# Extend the competencies to nurses, allied health professionals and other patient-facing staff via existing qualification routes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because UK obesity care uses a multidisciplinary team approach, the framework's scope was expanded beyond the US prescribing-focused competencies to any patient-facing HCP in obesity. The article states: "The competencies should also be extended to nurses via the Specialist Practice Qualification, and incorporated into specialist training for allied health professionals, and other patient-facing staff, including community pharmacists who offer weight management services". Each profession selects the competencies relevant to its members.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professions select the competencies considered relevant and appropriate to their members; training aligned to the core set is intended to increase patient confidence in staff advising and treating them
#### Constraints
- The US competencies were developed for HCPs with prescribing ability, so adaptation was needed for non-prescribing UK roles

### Target Learners
- Nurses, allied health professionals, community pharmacists and other patient-facing staff in UK obesity services

### Target Learning Goals
- Obesity care competencies appropriate to each professional role within multidisciplinary specialist services
