# Use virtual patients for formative practice rather than as an independent summative assessment tool until validity evidence strengthens

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the mixed validation results, the authors recommend deploying VPs in formative roles while summative use awaits stronger psychometrics: "the current version may not yet be suitable for use as an independent summative assessment tool, VPs can play a valuable role in formative learning". Formative use lets students practice clinical reasoning, communication and history-taking in interactive, feedback-rich simulations, integrated alongside traditional SPs and real patient sessions to support progressive competence development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback-rich interactive simulations
- Integration alongside traditional SPs and real patient sessions
#### Constraints
- Current reliability, construct and concurrent validity evidence is limited
- Small sample size, limited scenario complexity, and speech-recognition and system-responsiveness issues affected the findings

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- History-taking skills
- Communication skills
- Clinical reasoning
