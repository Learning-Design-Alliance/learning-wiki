# Co-construct definitions of active learning with students and train staff in practices that reduce anxiety and disengagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's learning points recommend that teaching staff "collaborate with students to define active learning during study support sessions at the course outset", co-producing active learning teaching sessions. Staff should support students to broaden their range of techniques beyond recall, embed critical evaluation of study skills, and receive training on why students disengage, including managing group relationships and reducing direct questioning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must first understand how students define active learning and how they interact with active learning taught sessions
#### Constraints
- The article notes UK medical schools' preparation for the UK Medical Licensing Assessment constrains moves away from fact-based exams

### Target Learners
- pre-clinical medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective use of a broad range of active learning strategies
- deeper knowledge construction beyond fact recall
