# GoNoodle: Physical Activity & Movement Breaks

## Description
GoNoodle offers videos and activities to encourage physical activity and movement breaks through gestures and movement. Incorporating movement can support attention, deepen understanding of key concepts, and enhance mathematical learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to the GoNoodle platform and appropriate technology.
#### Constraints
- Reliance on digital resources may pose barriers for learners with limited access to technology or the internet.
