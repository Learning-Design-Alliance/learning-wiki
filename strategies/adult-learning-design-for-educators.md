# Design educator learning experiences using adult learning theory: connect to prior experience, ensure real-world application, and enable perspective transformation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report argues that designing effective learning opportunities for educators requires understanding adult learning theory, since adults bring substantial prior knowledge and may hold fixed preconceptions affecting receptivity. It states that theoretical frameworks in adult learning emphasize that learning "should be related to adult learners' prior experiences and professional purposes, ensure real-world applications, and allow learners to understand and experience the perspectives of others," so they can transform preconceptions into more responsive practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An understanding of adult learning theory is essential for designing effective and useful learning opportunities for educators
#### Constraints
- The report presents this as a framework emphasis, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- in-service and preservice educators as adult learners

### Target Learning Goals
- transforming preconceptions into responsive teaching practices
