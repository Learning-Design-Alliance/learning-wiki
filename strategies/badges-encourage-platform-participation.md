# Reward students with badges on the platform for participation and completion to encourage more participation in all project stages.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The researcher rewarded the participants with badges on Edmodo when they completed their initial log on, when they inserted pictures on their profiles, and for the completion of the project. The badges encouraged them for more participation in all project stages, and this encouragement helped them become more able to demonstrate a greater responsibility to actively do the tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher must have an account on the platform and the ability to award badges tied to specific milestones such as initial log on, profile completion, and project completion.
#### Constraints
- Badge effects were reported by the researcher who taught the participants in a one-group study without a control group, so the encouragement which the participants received cannot be separated from other intervention components.

### Target Learners
- university EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- student motivation
- participation in project stages
