# Enrichment Through Art and Music

## Description
Teachers integrate art and music from diverse cultures into their lessons to enrich learning, engage students, and promote cultural understanding. Students are encouraged to share their own cultural art and music, fostering a sense of inclusion and connection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to diverse art and music resources, willingness to incorporate student input, and time for preparation and presentation.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to avoid cultural appropriation or misrepresentation. May need additional resources to support diverse artistic expressions.

### Personalization
- Allows students to share their own cultural art, music, and traditions. Offers flexible ways to engage with the material, such as discussions, comparisons, and presentations.
