# Progress Monitoring

## Description
Progress monitoring involves identifying skills that students have mastered and concepts that need additional attention. It can be used less frequently than exit tickets. Instructors can utilize existing tools within the curriculum or current structure. They provide learners with an ongoing view of their progress that they can use to adjust their strategies.
