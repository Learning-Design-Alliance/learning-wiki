# Selecting Tiered Vocabulary Words

## Description
Categorize vocabulary words into three tiers to prioritize instruction: Tier 1 (basic words, rarely require instruction), Tier 2 (high-frequency academic words, crucial for mature language), and Tier 3 (low-frequency, content-specific words). Focus primarily on Tier 2 words for broader academic benefit, but also include relevant Tier 3 words specific to the current topic or text. Let students select words, rate their knowledge, and guess meanings to inform instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of tiered vocabulary system, ability to analyze text for relevant words, and time for learners to select and rate words.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection to avoid overwhelming learners. Tier criteria can be subjective.

- Learners select their own words for review. Instructors choose words based on formative assessment data.
