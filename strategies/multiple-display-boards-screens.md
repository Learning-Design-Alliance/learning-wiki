# Multiple Display Boards/Screens

## Description
Using multiple surfaces or screens for instruction and collaboration. This strategy involves using multiple display boards or screens to enhance learning and collaboration in educational and work settings. It includes physical surfaces like smart wall paint for collaborative writing and digital setups with conjoined screens for seamless sharing and visualization of work. The approach aims to increase engagement and make information more accessible to learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires multiple screens or surfaces (e.g., smart wall paint, projectors, iPads). May require software for syncing screens (e.g., Reflector, AirServer).
#### Constraints
- May be costly to implement, especially if requiring new technology. Requires setup and technical knowledge to sync screens effectively. Attention can be a factor.

- Can be adapted to different classroom sizes and technological resources. Allows for flexibility in how students present and interact with information.
