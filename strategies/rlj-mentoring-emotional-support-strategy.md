# Use reflective journals with mentoring and emotional-support interventions to support students in complex clinical placements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends enhancing reflective tools to understand students' placement experiences and encourage personal and professional growth, encouraging male students to share feelings, designing interventions to reduce emotional impact, and having teachers and preceptors accompany students through shared reflective narrative. It states that "creating and sharing a reflective narrative (student–preceptor–teacher) can be very helpful" in the process of becoming a nurse.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher and nurse preceptor involvement throughout the mentoring process
- A relationship of trust between teacher and student
#### Constraints
- High care loads and staff shortages in these units can make it difficult for nurses to teach

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate nursing students in complex clinical placements

### Target Learning Goals
- coping with intense emotions during clinical practice
- professional identity development
- humanization of care attitudes
