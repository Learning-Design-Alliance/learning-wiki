# Use a briefing–simulation–debriefing structure around a clinical virtual simulator scenario in a case-based lesson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete lesson recipe: a 45-minute laboratory class in which "there was a simulation pedagogical strategy of briefing (5 min), simulation (20 min), and debriefing (20 min), with the same structure and contents" for both groups. The briefing sets up the case, students work through the virtual patient scenario, and the debriefing uses the simulator's report, timeline, and performance data to review decisions against evidence-based best practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator (the regular subject teacher)
- A simulator scenario with a debriefing tool presenting simulation report, timeline, and performance report
#### Constraints
- Tested on respiratory-process content (ineffective airway clearance and hypoxia) with second-year nursing students

### Target Learners
- nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- knowledge of respiratory process
- clinical decision making

### Affordances
- Clinical Virtual Simulation Concept
