# Analyze ratings within instructor-rating categories to rule out systemic bias from nonuniform rating distributions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address potential systemic bias, the authors compared student rating distributions between labs but within expert ratings, excluding the single "excellent" among all 100 instructor ratings due to statistical power concerns, and used a Kolmogorov-Smirnov two-sample test, described as "a nonparametric test suitable for comparing distributions of ordinal data". This within-rating analysis precluded the possibility that the overall gain in agreement was due solely to a nonuniform distribution of instructor ratings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Enough instructor ratings per category to support cross-lab comparisons
#### Constraints
- A single instructor rating in one category does not allow cross-lab comparisons within that rating; the authors excluded "excellent" for this reason

### Target Learners
- course instructors and researchers analyzing peer evaluation data

### Target Learning Goals
- valid assessment of peer evaluation behavior
