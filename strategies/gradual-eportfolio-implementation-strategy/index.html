Implement e-portfolios gradually in English programmes with limited aims, ease of use and agreed goals¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For institutions, the article recommends that faculties and administrators "must have agreed upon goals, intentions, and implementation strategies to enrich learning opportunities"; that e-portfolios focus on a limited set of aims and skills appropriate to each programme level; that they be easy to use so faculties can adapt features, train students and develop reflection methods; and that they be implemented gradually from the first level so learners gain experience and confidence with the technology.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Agreed goals, intentions and implementation strategies among faculties and administrators
  • E-portfolios easy to use and adaptable to curricula

Constraints¶

  • E-portfolio use will probably increase the time required to cover the curriculum
  • E-portfolios involve long-term processes necessitating gradual implementation

Target Learners¶

  • English language programme students and administrators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective integration of e-portfolios into language programmes
  • Deeper learning through appropriately selected e-portfolio assignments
