# Have CURE students disseminate their findings through conference poster presentations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article treats dissemination as an integral CURE component: students submit conference abstracts based on their hypothesis and results and present posters. It reports that this "led to three poster presentations at IEEE International Conference on Plasma Science (ICOPS) 2026 and two poster presentations at APS Division of Plasma Physics (DPP) 2026". Students who presented remained active in plasma physics and STEM after the course and are seeking future opportunities in the field.
