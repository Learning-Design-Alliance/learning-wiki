# Use quarterly IPP indicator data and twice-yearly data reviews with LEAs for continuous quality improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Project Cal-Well partners "provided quarterly Infrastructure Development, Prevention, and Mental Health Promotion (IPP) Indicator data to demonstrate progress toward annual goals." Twice a year, UCSF and the CDE met with the LEAs "to review data and progress to inform continuous quality improvement." This routine indicator reporting plus scheduled joint data-review meetings is the brief's stated mechanism for monitoring progress toward annual goals and adjusting implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partners must submit quarterly IPP Indicator data, and evaluation and state leadership must convene LEAs twice yearly to review the data
#### Constraints
- Implemented within the Project Cal-Well grant evaluation structure led by UCSF and the CDE

### Target Learners
- Local educational agency staff and program leaders implementing school-based mental health systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuous quality improvement of school-based mental health infrastructure and services
