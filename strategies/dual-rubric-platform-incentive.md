# Use two assessment rubrics so engagement with the adaptive platform can improve grades without penalizing non-users

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To cater for students' self-selection while still incentivizing engagement, the course computed final grades as the maximum of two rubrics: one contributing 40% final exam and 10% RiPPLE, the other 50% final exam and 0% RiPPLE. The RiPPLE marks rewarded answering and creating questions across four rounds (two marks per round, contingent on correctly answering 10 questions and creating at least one correct and effective question) plus a 2% overall-rating component. Instructors adopting a crowdsourced adaptive platform can use this design to reward engagement without making it mandatory for credit.
