# Three-stage implementation plan: phased field testing, national cross-sectional survey, and competency-based training optimization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose advancing the index system from theory to practice in three stages: (1) phased testing in standardized inpatient settings, specialized institutions, and primary/long-term care, including cross-cultural adaptation and measurement equivalence tests; (2) a national cross-sectional survey to identify competency weaknesses as a training basis; (3) optimizing training courses using the indicators via a "'theory + workshop + clinical shadowing' mixed model, with a total of 40–60 h of study, completed in 4–6 weeks".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Medical institutions provide time and resource support; educators receive specialized training on the indicator system; nursing managers coordinate training plans and establish regular review and feedback mechanisms.
#### Constraints
- The article states the index system is mainly constructed based on the Chinese mainland, Hong Kong and Macao background, requiring cross-cultural adaptation before use in other medical systems.

### Target Learners
- incontinence specialist nurses
- nursing students and junior nurses in clinical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- core competency development aligned with evaluation indicators
- evidence-based incontinence nursing practice
