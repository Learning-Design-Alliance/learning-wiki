## Description
The article closes with policy recommendations grounded in its finding that socio-economic conditions, not ethnicity, drive performance differences. It recommends "the design of tailored policies targeting the improvement of well-being, health, work opportunities, income and family education to vulnerable students in low-income settings irrespectively of their ethnic background", and separately recommends empowerment policies for stigmatized groups to homogenize performance at higher levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policies must target vulnerable students in low-income settings irrespective of ethnic background, per the article's recommendation.
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

- reducing ethnic achievement gaps
