# Run a nation-wide online Masterclass with recorded lectures, volunteer troubleshooting help, and consultant videoconference rooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
When pandemic restrictions cancelled most local IMC events in 2020 and 2021, Slovak institutions joined forces in one nation-wide online event. Lectures were replaced by recorded or live online sessions posted on youtube, participants installed analysis software at home with volunteer graduate students offering online help, screen-recorded instructions were posted, and consultants answered questions in videoconference rooms before a common closing session. "In order to uphold the tradition, we organised in Slovakia both years a nation-wide online event."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded or live online introductory lectures
- Online help with graduate student volunteers for software installation problems
- Consultants available in videoconference rooms during the analysis time slot
#### Constraints
- Only about twenty participants connected to the online events, versus a few tens per institution in traditional events
- IPPOG initially had no recommendations for such a format

### Target Learners
- upper-secondary level pupils studying from home

### Target Learning Goals
- hands-on HEP data analysis during remote learning
