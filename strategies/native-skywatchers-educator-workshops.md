# Dual-site Native Skywatchers educator workshops embedding star knowledge in authentic cultural context

## Description
Since 2012 the initiative has offered two consecutive days of workshop activities at two distinct sites, Fond du Lac Tribal & Community College and St. Cloud State University, co-funded by NASA-MN Space Grant, the university, and the tribal college. The workshop foci are to give participants information about Ojibwe and D(L)akota star knowledge, embed it in an authentic cultural context including history, language and art, and create awareness and dialog about respectful protocols for sharing cultural knowledge. Participants include K-12 educators, informal science educators, college educators, planetarians, administrators, and community astronomy enthusiasts.
