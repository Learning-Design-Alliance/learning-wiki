# Racial Equity in PBL - Spark Engagement with Student-Generated Questions

## Description
Projects driven by student-generated questions result in increased engagement and investment. Instead of spoon-feeding information, ask students what they care about, want to learn more about, or need to know the answer to. Black and Latinx students, in particular, can experience traditional instruction as disempowering and another example of systemic oppression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator who is open to student-driven inquiry, classroom environment that supports questioning.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift away from traditional instruction; may require more planning to align with curriculum standards.
