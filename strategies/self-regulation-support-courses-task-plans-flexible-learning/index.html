Support self-regulated learning in flexible formats with dedicated courses, coaching, and guided task plans¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Because students in the FLEX format reported problems engaging in self-regulated learning, the school introduced structural supports. The article states that "the SML introduced special courses, support and coaching to enhance students' capability for se lf- regulated learning in the FLEX format" and that students received "a task plan which can be used to guide them through each self-study phase". This responds to the finding that planning, organising and reflecting on individual learning is a major challenge in flexible study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Task plans are provided especially at the first-year level of the programme
  • Courses, support and coaching are offered alongside the flexible format itself

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • First-year Bachelor's students in blended or flexible learning programmes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-regulated learning skills
  • Independent navigation of online self-study phases

Claims¶

  • The most frequent student criticisms of FLEX concerned self-regulated learning, missing interaction, and difficulty estimating learning time [+M]
  • Students in the FLEX blended learning format rated the learning environment positively across all surveyed dimensions [~M]
