# Form parent groups at school to cooperate with teachers in supporting children's creativity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the study found children of BA and MA parents scored higher on creative thinking, the article recommends extending that support more broadly: "parent groups could be formed at school in which to exchange ideas and cooperate with teachers, with a view to the utilisation of the available facilities, in order to improve their children’s creativity-levels." This is a discussion-stage recommendation grounded in the parents'-education finding, not a tested intervention.

## Design Implications
