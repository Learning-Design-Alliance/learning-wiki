# Provide motivational interventions in the very early stages of students' college careers in math-intensive domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's Educational Impact and Implications Statement recommends that "educational interventions that support students' success in math-intensive study domains are needed in the very early stages of their college careers." Because the motivational shock occurred between Weeks 2 and 3 and the largest declines occurred in the first half of the semester, interventions should target this early window. The authors note that more intensive short-term analyses can help identify time points at which motivational interventions might be most fruitful and needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of sensitive time points at which motivational changes are most likely to occur
#### Constraints
- Interventions should account for the fact that motivational changes from midpoint to end of semester were substantially smaller than the initial decline

### Target Learners
- First-semester university students in math-intensive study programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Preventing motivational decline and course dropout in gateway math courses
