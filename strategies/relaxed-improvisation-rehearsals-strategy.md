# Relaxed, improvisation-rich rehearsals with teacher checking conceptual consistency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To motivate students to learn the physics concepts, the teacher conducted rehearsals in a relaxed way, with games, always stimulating students to improvise their lines and acting. The article reports that "o professor conduziu os ensaios de maneira descontraída, com brincadeiras, sempre estimulando os mesmos a fazerem improvisações em suas falas e atuações." This favored a more independent dynamic in which students privileged understanding and exposing the concepts in their own words rather than memorizing the original text, while the teacher analyzed the consistency of the improvised lines so that the proposed concepts were not addressed with errors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher availability to analyze the consistency of improvised lines for conceptual errors
- Rehearsal time outside regular class hours at the school
#### Constraints
- Improvisation requires that the characters understand the concepts for the scene movements to be correct

### Target Learners
- high-school students acting as student-actors

### Target Learning Goals
- deep, contextualized study of scientific concepts
- communicating science in a fun way
