# Combine PBL with LBL as a dual-track teaching model in Chinese medical education

## Description
The article recommends administering problem-based learning together with lecture-based learning rather than either alone: the PBL+LBL teaching model is "student-centered and question-oriented, combining the advantages of both", inspiring students to learn autonomously while under teacher guidance. The meta-analysis of 23 RCTs supports improved scores and satisfaction, and the authors conclude its use "may be optimal for improving medical education in China".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must include the PBL+LBL teaching model in the experimental group and the LBL teaching model in the control group, as in the included RCTs
#### Constraints
- The conclusion may be more suitable for medical education in China and Asia, not Western medical education
- None of the included studies reported allocation concealment and blinding, so methodological quality is not generally high

### Target Learners
- medical students in Chinese medical education, from Freshman to Masters level

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge scores
- skill scores
- medical writing scores
- comprehensive ability scores
- teaching satisfaction
