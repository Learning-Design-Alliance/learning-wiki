# Adopt flexible compulsory research education in dental schools with similar conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that dental schools with similar backgrounds implement a hybrid compulsory-elective research course: require all students to complete theoretical and experimental courses for systematic basic research learning, while encouraging top-rated students to join research tutor teams for advanced scientific research. They state the "flexible" CDBRC "is beneficial for UDBRE, which should be considered in some schools with similar conditions", and conclude by encouraging more dental schools to conduct analogous projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A basic research environment with instructors, funds, and laboratory equipment; the course should be reformed according to the college's conditions
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a single-institution retrospective comparison at one dental school; the authors note lack of instructors, funds, and laboratory equipment is the major barrier elsewhere

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- fundamental dental research skills and growth of clinical-scientific talent
