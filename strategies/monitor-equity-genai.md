## Description
The review's actionable takeaways include: "Monitor equity: Provision access for all students; audit outputs for bias; study heterogeneous impacts." This strategy responds to the review's finding that few studies stratify by student demographics or prior preparation, limiting equity claims. The equity-focused research section specifies stratified analyses and participatory design studies examining differential effects by prior preparation, language background, disability accommodations, and socioeconomic factors, with purposive oversampling of underrepresented groups, mixed methods combining analytics with interviews, and co-design of tools and policies with students.
