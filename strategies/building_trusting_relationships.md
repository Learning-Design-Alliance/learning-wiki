# Building Trusting Relationships

## Description
Prioritizing building relationships with learners can create a supportive and safe learning environment. Instructors greet each learner, and show vulnerability and care. When learners feel valued and supported, they may experience positive emotions that lead to more learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated time for interaction and check-ins. Intention to build genuine connections with each learner.
#### Constraints
- Time constraints, large class sizes, and reluctance to share personal information can be challenges. May come across as insincere if not genuinely executed.

### Affordances
- Creates a positive emotional climate, increases motivation, reduces behavioral issues, and improves learner achievement. Fosters feelings of safety, support, and value.

### Personalization
- Personalized greetings, individual check-ins, and sharing personal stories can be tailored to the specific needs and interests of learners.
