# Use the Marburger questionnaire for structures/processes and the German SFDP-26 for teachers as an interim evaluation toolkit, while developing and validating a new German-specific instrument

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a preliminary, resource-efficient solution for German medical schools, the review recommends combining instruments already available in German with good psychometric characteristics: the Marburger questionnaire for structural and procedural aspects and the SFDP-26 German version for teacher evaluation. As a medium-term goal, it proposes designing and validating a new questionnaire across several German medical schools to achieve greater comparability of results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The choice of data collection tool should be guided by the goal of evaluation, i.e. the teaching-quality dimension targeted
#### Constraints
- The review states its results do not justify general recommendations for specific questionnaires; English-validated instruments cannot easily be transferred to the German medical education context

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- valid and reliable evaluation of teaching quality at German medical schools
