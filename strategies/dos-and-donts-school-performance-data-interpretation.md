# Apply a set of dos and don'ts when examining school performance data to decide school support options

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The publication offers decisionmakers a practical guidance strategy: as they examine school performance data and consider options for supporting schools, they should keep in mind a set of dos and don'ts. Its purpose is to help states judge "what data they can trust and what inferences they can draw to best understand schools' needs" and to support learning acceleration and recovery decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State and local decisionmakers must be examining school performance data in the context of accountability and support decisions under resumed ESSA provisions.
#### Constraints
- The guidance is oriented to the post-pandemic 2022 accountability context, when accountability provisions returned after two years of pandemic-related disruptions.

### Target Learners
- State education agency decisionmakers
- Local education agency decisionmakers

### Target Learning Goals
- Appropriate interpretation and use of school performance data for identifying schools for targeted or comprehensive support and improvement
