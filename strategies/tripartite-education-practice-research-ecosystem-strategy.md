# Tripartite collaboration framework for implementing hospital management pedagogy: administrative data provision, university algorithm adaptation, and hospital co-construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address implementation challenges of pedagogical tools such as DRG groupers and performance algorithms, the authors propose "a tripartite collaboration framework": health administrative departments provide de-identified medical data, universities develop pedagogy-adapted algorithms, and hospitals co-construct a closed-loop "education-practice-research" ecosystem. They also caution that technological dependency risks must be mitigated, since overemphasis on quantitative metrics may overlook individualized patient needs, necessitating strengthened medical humanities modules in future curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of pedagogical tools with hospital management information systems
- De-identified medical data from health administrative departments
#### Constraints
- Technological dependency risks: overemphasizing quantitative metrics (e.g., CMI values) may lead individualized patient needs to be overlooked

### Target Learners
- undergraduate hospital management students

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced data-driven and humanistic management decision-making
