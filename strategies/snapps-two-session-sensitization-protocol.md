# Sensitize both preceptors and residents to SNAPPS through two 30-minute sessions using instructional video, role-play and a six-step handout before inpatient implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before SNAPPS case presentations begin, teachers receive a 30-minute introduction with a validated instructional video and a reinforcement session a week later, while residents receive two half-hour sessions in which "The first session included –discussion of conventional method of case presentations, introduction to the concept of SNAPPS technique, role-play demonstrating SNAPPS technique, instructional video demonstrating SNAPPS technique and an opportunity to ask questions." Residents also receive a card or handout highlighting the six steps.
