# Spread recess throughout the day and pair increased recess with positive behavior or conflict resolution curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that principals schedule recess periods distributed across the school day rather than concentrated in one block, because discipline referrals rise as time since the last recess elapses. It states "It may be best to reduce discipline referrals by spreading recess throughout the day to minimize long durations of school time without recess." Schools considering more recess are advised to add supportive curricula.
