# Encourage Cooperation

## Description
Learning is enhanced when it is more like a team effort than a solo race. Good learning, like good work, is collaborative and social, not competitive and isolated. Working with others significantly expands the potential of learning and the ownership of their learning responsibilities. Articulating and sharing ideas and responding to others’ reactions improves thinking and deepens understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for group work, tutor facilitation skills.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful structuring of group activities, potential for unequal participation.

### Affordances
- Expands learning potential, promotes ownership of learning, enhances critical thinking and understanding.

### Personalization
- Create study groups within the Learning Centre. Encourage tutees to work together and use small group discussions, collaborative tutoring assignments, and case study analysis. Encourage tutees to discuss key concepts with other students whose backgrounds and viewpoints are different from their own.
