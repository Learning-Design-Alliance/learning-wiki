# Deploy solar-powered edge devices with community review and local data governance for sustainable AI-driven education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's sustainability section describes a deployment approach combining solar power, community review, and local data governance. The authors "tested 8 Raspberry Pi 4B and 4 NVIDIA Jetson Nano platforms with solar power", all content "underwent community review for cultural appropriateness" by community leaders, and "All data remains on local devices with community-developed governance protocols". Test content also underwent human speaker validation against cultural misrepresentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnerships with active community organizations for cultural review; solar power infrastructure; local-device data storage with community governance protocols
#### Constraints
- Deployment is in an early phase, connecting with two USA-based communities serving rural Africa (AYCO and Florida Africa Foundation); widespread off-grid deployment models remain future work

### Target Learners
- learners in rural, resource-constrained Sub-Saharan African communities

### Target Learning Goals
- sustainable, culturally appropriate access to localized AI-driven education
