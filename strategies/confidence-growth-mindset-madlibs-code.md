# Boost learner confidence with growth mindset framing, early wins, and partially completed code

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends countering learners' belief that they are "just not good at it" by encouraging a growth mindset: "success is not about where you started, but the idea that you can continue to improve your skills if you continue practicing." Concrete tactics include getting to the "magic" early with plots and apps, celebrating small wins, avoiding comparisons between students, validating that material is tricky, and using "madlibs code" where students fill in blanks in mostly written code.
