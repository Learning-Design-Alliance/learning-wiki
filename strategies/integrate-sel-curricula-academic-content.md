# Integrate social-emotional learning into school programs alongside academic content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (5 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 4 of 5 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the moderate observed relationship between positive emotions and personality characteristics and academic achievement, the authors recommend that educational policymakers consider implementing structured SEL curricula in conjunction with traditional academic content. They suggest supporting students' motivation and emotional capacities through safe and supportive learning environments and both parental and teacher support, and note in-service training programs to enhance teachers' knowledge of students' motivation may prove fruitful.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher support and safe, supportive learning environments to maintain students' positive emotions
#### Constraints
- The relative influence of these traits slightly diminishes in later school stages, so support should be sustained even as emphasis shifts

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, with particular emphasis on preschool and kindergarten children

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement; self-regulation and positive learning attitudes; motivation and emotional capacities
