# Team training with variable leadership roles and simulated communication delays

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For crews facing Earth-independent operation, the review recommends team training that exercises coordination under realistic social and temporal conditions. It states that "team training should include practice sessions with variable leadership roles, conflict resolution, and communication delays or failures so that coordination skills are exercised under realistic social and temporal conditions". It also draws on aviation crew resource management methods such as briefing interviews, cross-referencing, appropriate challenge of authority, and recovery from communication failures, integrated into pre-flight selection and team preparation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practice must occur among the team, not among sole individuals; leadership roles should be shared and shift based on expertise and operational demands
#### Constraints
- Motivated by Mars-mission communication delays approaching 20 minutes each way that eliminate rapid consultation with mission control

### Target Learners
- astronaut crews and multidisciplinary mission-control, medical, and engineering teams

### Target Learning Goals
- shared mental models, communication protocols, backup behavior, adaptive leadership, conflict resolution
