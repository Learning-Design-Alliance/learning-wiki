# RentaProf: university professors take over a school lesson on demand, with discussion sessions that are particularly successful in primary schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RentaProf is a program organized centrally by Faculty IV of the University of Siegen in which teachers from schools near Siegen contact the university to arrange a special lesson. The article reports that 'University professors then visit the schoolandtakeoveralessonwithtopicslikeWhatisthesmallestthingintheUniverse?orSpecialRelativityorStatusQuoofParticlePhysics' and that the included discussion sessions 'are particularly successful in primary schools'. It is intended for teachers in the vicinity of Siegen.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers at nearby schools must initiate contact to arrange the special lesson
#### Constraints
- Organized centrally for one faculty at the University of Siegen and aimed at schools in the vicinity of Siegen

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school pupils in schools near Siegen

### Target Learning Goals
- sparking pupils' interest in physics topics such as particle physics and special relativity
