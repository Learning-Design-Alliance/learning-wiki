# Orient learners to the expectations and active nature of PBL to prevent lower satisfaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review attributes the single study reporting lower satisfaction with PBL to learner unfamiliarity with active formats, stating that this "highlights the importance of orienting learners to the expectations and active nature of PBL." Programs adopting PBL should therefore include an explicit orientation to the active, self-directed expectations of the format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must be oriented to the expectations and active nature of PBL before or during implementation
#### Constraints
- Learners familiar with passive formats may report lower satisfaction if not oriented to active formats

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- learner satisfaction with active learning formats
