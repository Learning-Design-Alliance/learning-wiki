Change-effort development tools: process maps, impact effort matrices, core competencies identification, and structure revision¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the Change Effort Development Phase, R15CC facilitates a high-level process map or block diagram—a "graphical representation of the steps needed to produce a particular outcome"—to build shared understanding and surface challenge points; an impact effort matrix plotting change ideas on effort and impact axes across four quadrants (quick wins, major projects, fill-ins, time-wasters); core competencies identification that deindividualizes tasks to reveal gaps and leverage points; and organizational structure revision built from the purpose statement and project inventory data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Consensus on process steps before the map can uncover inconsistencies and elicit change ideas
  • Grounding structure revision in Discovery Phase data to check whether the new structure addresses identified challenges

Constraints¶

  • Some teams require a full redesign while others change only a portion of their overall structure

Target Learners¶

  • SEA division leaders and managers designing change efforts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Designing and prioritizing change efforts that address root causes of system problems

Related Strategies¶

  • Implementation-phase supports: staff engagement, communication and action plans, thought partnership, and self-assessment rubrics
