# Emphasis on the Learning Process

## Description
Helping learners focus on and value the process of learning, rather than just the grades they receive, promotes a growth mindset. Without this emphasis, learners may base their self-perceptions of intelligence and worth on grades alone, which promotes a fixed mindset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular opportunities for reflection, ability to facilitate discussions about the learning process.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who are highly grade-driven. Requires a shift in mindset from evaluation to development.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Value learning over grades, foster a growth mindset, promote self-reflection

### Affordances
- Encourages learners to value the journey of learning, promotes intrinsic motivation, and fosters a growth mindset by focusing on improvement rather than innate ability.

### Personalization
- Reflective prompts can be tailored to individual learner's experiences and challenges, encouraging them to focus on their personal growth.

## Impact
- Research shows that emphasizing the learning process increases intrinsic motivation, fosters a growth mindset, and leads to greater academic success.
