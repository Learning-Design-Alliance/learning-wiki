# Ask students to find everyday-life applications of the course and run atypical experiments to verify and validate theoretical results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete activity recipe used with the P17 cohort: students are asked to "draw inspiration from everyday life situations to find applications of course and to do atypical experiments to verify and validate some theoretical results". Students then design, run and report their own experiments (goal, material and method, experiments and results, conclusions), as in the jerry-can emptying study.
