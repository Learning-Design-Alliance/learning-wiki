# Derive evidence-based selection criteria and explore misconceptions in quantum explanatory videos

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation for future educational research on quantum explanatory videos. Because "YouTube's surface features, however, are not likely to provide reliable quality indicators", they recommend that future research should (a) explore widespread misconceptions in explanatory videos on quantum concepts, and (b) make further efforts towards the derivation of evidence-based selection criteria that support both students and teachers/lecturers in detecting high quality content. This strategy addresses the fact that the internet is crowded with scientifically misleading or mystifying explanatory videos on quantum concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research effort to categorize misconceptions and to derive selection criteria, since surface metrics alone do not identify high-quality videos.
#### Constraints
- The study only analyzed scientifically sound videos on quantum entanglement and tunnelling, so the correlations found are not generalizable to different topics.

### Target Learners
- students watching quantum physics videos online
- teachers and university lecturers selecting videos

### Target Learning Goals
- selecting high-quality explanatory videos on quantum entanglement and tunnelling

## Claims

- [Unlike the earlier mechanics study, likes, dislikes and views each correlate significantly with explaining quality of quantum-topic videos in the total sample.](../claims/likes-dislikes-views-correlate-with-explaining-quality-quantum-videos.md) [+W]
- [The number of relevant content-related comments is the surface metric most strongly correlated with the explaining quality of YouTube explanatory videos on quantum entanglement and tunnelling.](../claims/relevant-comments-correlate-with-explaining-quality-quantum-videos.md) [+W]
