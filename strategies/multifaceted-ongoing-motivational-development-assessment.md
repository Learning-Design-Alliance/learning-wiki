# Assess motivational development with multi-faceted, ongoing, longitudinal measures including scaffolding observation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a "multi-faceted, ongoing assessment strategy" combining quantitative and qualitative measures: self-report questionnaires, interviews, reflective journals, and observations. It notes self-report limitations such as social desirability bias, and proposes tracking learners' reliance on motivational scaffolding during challenging tasks as evidence of progress toward motivational autonomy. Longitudinal assessment is emphasized for tracking development over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combination of quantitative and qualitative measures
- Regular check-ins over time (longitudinal assessment)
#### Constraints
- Self-report measures carry social desirability bias and potential lack of self-awareness, per the article

### Target Learners
- Elementary through postsecondary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Tracking progress toward motivational autonomy
