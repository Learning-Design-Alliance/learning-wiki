# Keep lessons simple and be flexibly prescriptive: narrow objectives, chunked code, one way first

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends lessons built on "well-structured learning objectives with relatively narrow scope", since "It is often better for students to walk away understanding a few things well, rather than many things shallowly or not at all." Being flexibly prescriptive means giving specific instructions now while noting that alternatives exist. Tactics include chunking code into separate steps, making materials skimmable, linking out extra detail, and deliberately using oversimplification (e.g., describing Docker as a computer run on your computer) until students are ready for nuance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must know their audience's stage and curb impulses to add nuance prematurely
#### Constraints
- Simplicity is relative to student stage; oversimplifications are later replaced with more nuanced concepts

### Target Learners
- beginning data science students
- busy learners with competing obligations

### Target Learning Goals
- foundational conceptual understanding
- readable working code
