# Implementation mitigations that convert school-based R&D frictions into standard work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper pairs predictable frictions that slow valid school-based R&D with mitigations turned into standard work: pre-approved data-sharing playbooks using FERPA school-official or research exceptions, published data dictionaries, minimum-viable telemetry aligned to use cases, plug-and-play onboarding supports, funded instrumentation sprints with vendors, and pre-committed de-identification with independent IRB review and opt-outs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires vendor cooperation on exposing event logs and feature flags, and sharing SDKs and open schemas where feasible.
#### Constraints
- The paper frames these as addressing data governance, school capacity and turnover, platform readiness, and privacy and trust frictions specifically.

### Target Learners
- Schools, districts, and education vendors participating in R&D

### Target Learning Goals
- Shortening time-to-evidence and enabling rapid, ethical learning cycles in schools
