# Administer concept tests at the beginning of the semester to identify students weak in mathematics and provide early intervention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because many IPLS students lack appropriate mathematical background, and quantitative skill deficiency is associated with higher failure rates, the article recommends systematically using concept tests at the start of the semester to gauge quantitative skills. It states that "Concept tests that are administered during beginning of the semester are proven to be effective to assess students' prior knowledge." Early identification lets instructors provide extra assistance such as office-hour exercise resolution, tutoring services, and repeated problem practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
