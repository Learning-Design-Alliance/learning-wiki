# Check context familiarity by asking the class to share what they know about a problem's context before working on it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before students work on a problem, the teacher can surface prior knowledge of its setting as a quick diagnostic of familiarity. The guide states that "teachers might ask the class to share what they know about that context before working on the problem with them." This strategy is flagged as "especially important for English language learners" and for students currently struggling with mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher must identify the problem's context and its required background knowledge during advance preparation.

- 

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4 through 8
- English language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Familiarity with problem contexts so students can focus on mathematics and reasoning
