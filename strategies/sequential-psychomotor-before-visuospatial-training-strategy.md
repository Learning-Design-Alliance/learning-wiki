# Teach the psychomotor movements on a transparent shoebox before adding the laparoscopic view

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention isolates the psychomotor component of intracorporeal suturing by having trainees first practice on a transparent shoebox where the tissue is directly visible, then repeat the same C-loop procedure on a box trainer with standard laparoscopic view. The protocol states the shoebox lets trainees learn "the psychomotor skills" without "the visuospatial orientation inherent to the laparoscopic view". Proficiency on the shoebox stage is required before advancing to the box trainer stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A transparent shoebox trainer and a standard box trainer with laparoscopic view, plus predefined proficiency criteria to gate progression between stages
#### Constraints
- The protocol notes it remains to be determined whether skills learned on the shoebox transfer to the box trainer with laparoscopic view

### Target Learners
- laparoscopic novices (medical students)

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor control and visuospatial orientation in laparoscopic suturing and knot tying
