# Use rater training, diversity recruitment, and equitable resource access to mitigate assessment disparities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend multifaceted interventions to mitigate rating disparities in standardized clinical assessment. They argue that because "the tool itself does not contribute to disparities, but real-world use of the tool by raters contributes to these differences", the scoring differences "support the use of rater training, which has been shown to improve the accuracy of workplace-based entrustment ratings". They also call for diversity efforts in recruitment, equitable access to resources, and URM-specific student support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rater training programs targeting accuracy of workplace-based entrustment ratings; ongoing equity and diversity initiatives across faculty and residents
#### Constraints
- The authors note testing by itself is not the solution for overcoming observed differences not explained by student performance

### Target Learners
- medical students on emergency medicine clerkships
- under-represented in medicine medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- fair and accurate clinical assessment of learner performance
- equity in clerkship grading and residency application materials
