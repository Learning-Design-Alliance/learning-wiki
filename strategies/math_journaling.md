# Math Journaling

## Description
In their math journals, students can write about things they are having trouble with or things that they've figured out. Putting these thoughts into words can help students get a more concrete handle on the logic of their ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Math journals or notebooks, prompts for reflection (optional).
#### Constraints
- Requires regular time for journaling and may require encouragement for learners to express their thoughts openly.
