# Tailor lecture content to the background of the local student cohort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the visited institutions varied widely in prior exposure to the course topics, the traveling lecturer adjusted the lecture level at each site. The article reports that "the visited institutions had a large diversity of experience and expertise in the mentioned topics" and that, accordingly, "the lectures were tailored to accommodate the background of the students according to their curriculum." Lectures were also open to a larger audience of interested students beyond the hands-on participants.
