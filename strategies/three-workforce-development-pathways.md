# Organize flat-optics workforce development as three recurring pathways grouped by handoffs and deliverables rather than device label

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes workforce development as a small number of recurring models rather than one catch-all curriculum: "a technician and manufacturing-test pathway, a design and foundry pathway, and a systems and productization pathway". Each pathway groups training by the kinds of handoffs and deliverables a learner must master — process travelers, calibration records, and pass/fail reports; traceability matrices and verified model packages; or qualification reports and release records. The pathways are explicitly not rigid tracks.
