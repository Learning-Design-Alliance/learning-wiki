# Pathway programs specifically designed for underrepresented minoritized students carry the strongest evidence for improving health workforce diversity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Pathway programs support high school, college, or postbaccalaureate students in acquiring the experiences and skills needed for entry into health professional training. The article states that "pathway programs specifically designed to recruit and support students who are underrepresented in the health professions have the strongest evidence of effectiveness for improving workforce diversity of the four highlighted program types." Examples include partnerships between health professional schools and high schools in underserved zip codes and entrance exam preparation at minority-serving institutions.
