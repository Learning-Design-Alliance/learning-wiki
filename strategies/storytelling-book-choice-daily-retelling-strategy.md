# Storytelling program: weekly 50-minute story classes with student book choice plus daily 10-minute peer retelling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom program in which a teacher trained for 4 hours tells a story to children seated in a circle on cushions, with books selected from a list of fifteen addressing themes such as friendship, empathy, and expressing feelings. The article states "Every week, students chose one book from three offered by the teacher" and that children could ask questions and make comments during the class. Daily before the snack break, children retold the week's story to a randomly determined pair for 10 minutes, 5 to listen and 5 to tell.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom teacher trained by the researcher for 4 hours before giving the storytelling classes
- A preselected list of fifteen age-appropriate books covering social and emotional themes
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one public school with children aged 6.5–8.1 years from low-income families

### Target Learners
- Elementary school children aged 6–8 years

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing aggressive behaviors
- Improving academic performance
- Verbal proficiency, empathy, and cooperation
