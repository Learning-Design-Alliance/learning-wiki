Strategies

Devise more engaging and compelling content structures in language-learning MOOCs to capture user interest and commitment throughout the course.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the observed drop in participation during the second week, the authors recommend that it is essential to "devise content structures that are more engaging and compelling, capturing users' interest and commitment to successfully completing the course." This strategy addresses the finding that content diversity alone is insufficient to ensure user engagement throughout the course.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Understanding of which course components and structures best retain users, derived from event log analysis

Constraints¶

  • The study notes that content diversity, while valuable, is insufficient on its own to ensure engagement.

Target Learners¶

  • MOOC users at risk of disengaging early in a course

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustaining user engagement and course completion

Claims¶

  • There is a considerable drop in course participation during the second week of the language-learning MOOC, indicating content diversity alone is insufficient to ensure user engagement. [+W]
  • Users who stay and finish the MOOC utilize the course material better than those who do not, suggesting interest in learning from the material is a contributing factor for completion. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Deploy analytical tools on learning platforms to detect learners exhibiting behaviours that may lead to failure or dropout and propose actions to recover them.
