# Evaluate AI tutoring with consistent support assignments over months and external standardized assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a longitudinal evaluation design to establish durable effects of AI tutoring: "Future research can overcome these limitations by assigning students to receive one consistent type of support for an entire study, ideally following their progress over several months and tracking their performance on external, standardized assessments." This addresses the session-level randomization used in the RCT, which measured immediate outcomes but could not isolate cumulative impact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assign each student one consistent type of support for an entire study
- Follow student progress over several months
- Track performance on external, standardized assessments
#### Constraints
- The exploratory RCT's session-by-session randomization prevented isolating the cumulative impact of working with LearnLM over time

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students (Years 9–10, ages 13–15) in mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- Durable mathematics learning and knowledge transfer beyond immediate mistake correction
