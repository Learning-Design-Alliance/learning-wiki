# Combine behavioural observation with explicit evaluation of formulation and reasoning processes in simulation-based assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future simulation-based assessments pair observation of interviewing behaviour with explicit evaluation of integrative reasoning. Following simulated encounters, "learners could be asked to construct brief biopsychosocial formulations, justify diagnostic hypotheses, identify contextual contributors to distress and explain the reasoning underlying their clinical decisions". This addresses the low visibility of integrative competence while maintaining validity, reliability and educational feasibility.
