# Separate Product, Process, and Progress Criteria

## Description
Instead of eliminating grades for behavior and participation, differentiate between grades for product, process, and progress criteria. This ensures grades for mastery are clearly delineated from more subjective factors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed rubrics, clear grading guidelines
#### Constraints
- Requires significant effort to differentiate grading criteria
