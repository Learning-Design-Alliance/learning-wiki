# Micro-Goal Setting

## Description
Setting achievable micro-goals encourages consistent, incremental learner progress. Repeated small wins can lead to a growth mindset and increased confidence. Micro-goals break down large tasks into smaller, manageable steps, making the overall goal less daunting and more attainable. This approach enables learners to experience frequent success, reinforcing positive beliefs about their ability to improve and master new skills. By focusing on incremental progress, learners can track their development, maintain motivation, and build self-efficacy. Instructors can support this strategy by helping learners define specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) micro-goals that align with their overall learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined overall learning objectives. Ability to break down large tasks into smaller, manageable steps. Regular monitoring and feedback to track progress.
#### Constraints
- If micro-goals are too easy, learners may not experience a sense of accomplishment. If micro-goals are too difficult, learners may become discouraged. Requires careful planning and monitoring to ensure that micro-goals align with overall learning objectives.

### Target Learners

- All learner levels, particularly those who struggle with large, complex tasks or who have low confidence in their abilities

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance motivation, promote a growth mindset, increase confidence, track progress, and develop self-efficacy

### Affordances
- Provides a sense of accomplishment and motivates continued effort. Allows learners to track their progress and see tangible results of their efforts. Reduces anxiety associated with large tasks by breaking them into manageable steps.

### Personalization
- Goals can be tailored to individual learner needs and abilities. Learners can choose the pace at which they progress through the micro-goals. Instructors can provide individualized support and feedback to help learners achieve their micro-goals.
