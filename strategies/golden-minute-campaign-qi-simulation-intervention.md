# The Golden Minute Campaign: a multifaceted data-guided QI/simulation intervention combining trained local-champion facilitators, monthly scenario team training, clinical debriefings, and clinical data feedback loops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Golden Minute Campaign was a year-long QI/simulation intervention at a rural Tanzanian hospital aiming to ventilate 70% of nonbreathing newborns within the first minute. Local champions received a 4-day EuSim Level 1 train-the-trainer course, then ran monthly in situ scenario team trainings targeting gaps identified in clinical performance data, daily shift-meeting and post-resuscitation debriefings, monthly QI meetings with action points displayed on a progress board, and low-dose high-frequency individual skill training on the NeoNatalie Live simulator. The article states the intervention "was multifaceted including systematic clinical debriefing, own data feedback loops, and regular scenario simulation training targeting identified gaps in clinical care."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local champions appointed and formally trained as simulation facilitators
- An existing infrastructure for collecting clinical performance data to identify training gaps
- Hospital management support and midwife participation in regular team simulations
#### Constraints
- Monthly meetings required human resources outside working hours, representing a challenge for sustainability of QI efforts
- The resource-demanding data collection system using trained research assistants observing all births is likely not an option for most places

### Target Learners
- Midwives and labor ward healthcare workers in low-resource hospital settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely initiation of newborn bag-mask ventilation within 60 seconds of birth
- Teamwork, communication, decision making, and leadership in newborn resuscitation

## Claims

- [A data-guided HBB QI/simulation intervention increased the proportion of nonbreathing newborns ventilated within 60 seconds from 15.8% to 68.5%, with partial reversal after the intervention ended](../claims/golden-minute-campaign-increased-ventilation-within-60-seconds.md) [+W]
- [Among ventilated nonbreathing newborns, fresh stillbirths decreased significantly from 3.2% to 0.7% during the intervention, remaining significant after risk-factor adjustment](../claims/fresh-stillbirths-reduced-among-ventilated-newborns.md) [+W]
- [The proportion of resuscitations provided by midwives increased throughout the study, while recent NeoNatalie Live training among providers declined sharply after the campaign](../claims/midwife-led-resuscitation-increased-recent-training-declined.md) [+W]
- [Perinatal mortality showed no statistically significant change for all births, and the mortality reduction among ventilated newborns lost significance after adjustment](../claims/perinatal-mortality-no-significant-change-all-births.md) [-W]
