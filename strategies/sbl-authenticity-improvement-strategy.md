# Enhance simulation placement authenticity by recruiting more service users, adding intervention-based elements and increasing educator-student contact time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation set for improving simulation-based placements: "our recommendations include recruiting more service users, incorporating and encouraging more intervention-based elements, and increasing the contact time between the practice educator and students." The discussion adds further changes: changing groups throughout the placement, reducing group sizes, shortening assessment and treatment time as the placement progresses, collaborating with other healthcare students, completing the CPAF one-to-one with the practice educator, and ensuring equipment availability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practice educators may require training and practice to provide constructive feedback and enable self-reflection effectively
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that "caution should be exercised in implementing any changes to ensure that the educational benefits are maximised"

### Target Learners
- Pre-registration physiotherapy students undertaking simulation-based placements before external practice placements

### Target Learning Goals
- Authenticity of simulated clinical practice
- Communication and clinical reasoning with real service users
- Treatment intervention skills
