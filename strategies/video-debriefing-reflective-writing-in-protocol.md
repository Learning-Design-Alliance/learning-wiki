# Include video debriefing and reflective writing in the description and analysis stages of simulation debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The protocol incorporates two concrete techniques drawn from prior studies: reviewing simulation videos during the description stage, and reflective writing in which learners analyze objective and subjective patient data. The article reports that "The video debriefing method had a positive effect of increasing the learners' clinical reasoning, clinical judgment, and debriefing satisfaction via reflecting the simulation experience", and that reflective writing occurs in a self-directed manner and can elicit learning motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recordings of the simulation implementation must be available for review
- Learners complete reflective writing on collected patient data
