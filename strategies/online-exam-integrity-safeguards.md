# Layer multiple safeguards to protect academic integrity in online examinations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To reduce cheating as a confounder in online examinations, the study layered several safeguards: verifying student identity through continuous authentication, security questions based on background student information, live online video monitoring of exam-takers, and "randomizing examination questions and supplemental oral tests using online video calls". The authors present these measures as ways to protect the validity of remote assessment in virtual learning environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- identity verification through continuous authentication
- security questions based on background student information extracted from the educational system
- live online video supervision of exam-takers
- randomized examination questions and supplemental oral tests using online video calls
#### Constraints
- The authors state that cheating methodologies, types, and facilitators are widespread, making this a critical limitation despite the safeguards
- online assessment faces infrastructural barriers, especially in developing countries

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students taking online examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- valid assessment of knowledge test scores in remote learning environments
