# Ball Toss Game

## Description
A simple game involving tossing a ball, often used with preschoolers to develop counting skills. It can be played indoors or outdoors and involves lightweight, easily accessible materials. The game can be adapted with themes (e.g., patriotic twist) and integrated with related learning resources to enhance the educational experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ball, targets (e.g., buckets), and materials for thematic customization (optional).
#### Constraints
- Limited in scope, primarily focuses on counting and basic motor skills. May require modifications for learners with physical limitations. Success depends on active participation.
