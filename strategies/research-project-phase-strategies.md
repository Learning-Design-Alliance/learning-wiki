# Phase-specific research-project activities: selecting situations and simulators, iterative design with expert workshops and pilot studies, and standardized study conduct

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's project-level checklist (Table 4) recommends, in the foundation phase, posing a focused research question, selecting "a practically relevant professional situation", operationalizing knowledge and competences, and choosing a simulator with sufficient authenticity, interactivity, and detailed log data. Construction follows iterative design-and-construction and evaluation-and-reflection cycles using expert workshops and pilot studies. Study-conduct recommendations differ by data collection type (lab-based, web-based) and simulation modality, and coding schemes for behavior videos should be tested on a small sample with two trained raters reporting inter-coder agreement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient statistical power and an appropriate number of simulated cases in pretest, intervention, and posttest to reach sufficient reliability
#### Constraints
- Complex investigations involving multiple simulators, instruments, or many participants have to be organized efficiently, saving costs and minimizing facility use

### Target Learners
- research project teams conducting simulation-based experiments

### Target Learning Goals
- facilitation and assessment of knowledge and competences through instructional support in simulations
