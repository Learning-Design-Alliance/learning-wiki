Prioritize larger multi-center RCTs with standardized AR systems, uniform metrics, and long-term follow-up when evaluating AR surgical training¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors' forward-looking research and design recommendation for AR in surgical education. They argue current evidence is limited by heterogeneous designs and systems, so "Conducting larger, multi-center RCTs with standardized AR systems and training protocols will enhance the robustness and generalizability of findings", alongside uniform evaluation metrics, transparent reporting of AR functionalities, long-term skill-retention follow-up, improved haptic feedback, and cost-effectiveness analysis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Standardized AR systems and training protocols
  • Uniform evaluation metrics to reduce heterogeneity
  • Transparent reporting, particularly regarding AR functionalities

Constraints¶

  • Current evidence base: only four of twelve studies were eligible for quantitative analysis due to differing outcomes and training content

Target Learners¶

  • medical students
  • junior physicians
  • surgical trainees

Target Learning Goals¶

  • basic laparoscopic surgical skills
  • long-term retention and transfer of surgical skills to real-world settings

Claims¶

  • Effect of AR on OSATS Specific scores is uncertain due to high heterogeneity and publication bias [+W]
  • Qualitative synthesis indicates AR shortens learning curves, reduces errors, and differentiates proficiency levels in laparoscopic training [+W]
  • AR reduces reliance on instructor supervision and enables autonomous laparoscopic practice [+W]
  • AR-assisted training improves GOALS Global scores in novice laparoscopic learners [+W]
  • AR-assisted training improves OSATS Global scores in novice laparoscopic learners [+W]
  • AR-assisted training reduces subjective workload (NASA-TLX) in novice learners [+W]
