Front-load Tier 1 vocabulary before the science lesson and teach embedded scientific vocabulary in the lesson's context¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Montebello teachers learned to distinguish Tier 1 words (Beck, McKeown, & Kucan, 2002)—basic, everyday words not typically emphasized in instruction—from embedded vocabulary that emerges from the lesson. Tier 1 words, such as names of objects to be studied and some descriptors, are front-loaded before the lesson to give English learners a foundation to engage and participate actively in the Explore phase. Embedded terms conveying scientific meaning, such as density, opaque, and mass, are taught in the lesson's context.
