# Account for stability of SEL competencies when using them in practice, policy, and evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's title and description direct teachers and schools to consider the stability of social-emotional learning competencies when using them: "Are social-emotional learning competencies stable over time? Implications for practice, policy, and evaluation". If SEL scores vary over time rather than behaving as stable traits, their use in evaluation and policy decisions requires caution; the brief states it draws out "the implications for teachers and schools".

### Context
