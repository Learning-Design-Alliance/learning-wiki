# Train teaching staff in SEL tools and abilities as a preventive route to student mental health

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "the future of SEL initiatives should focus on the need to develop in teaching staff the abilities to effectively co-regulate emotions in educational environments, contributing to the prevention of mental health problems in students from childhood." This implements RULER's theory of change, in which adult training precedes student skill development, via structured professional development plus year-long implementation support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development training for teachers (15 hours), follow-up sessions (3 hours), and SEL implementation guidelines throughout the school year
#### Constraints
- Evidence in this study comes from one experimental and one control school; the authors note participation of only one school per group makes generalization difficult

### Target Learners
- Primary school teachers
- Primary school students ages 6-12

### Target Learning Goals
- Emotional co-regulation
- Prevention of psychological maladjustment
