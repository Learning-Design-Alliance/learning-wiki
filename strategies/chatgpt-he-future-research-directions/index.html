Pursue future research on ChatGPT in higher education to fill the gaps the scoping review identifies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review sets out directions for future research in higher education as part of its proposed response to ChatGPT. The authors state they propose "a comprehensive framework for universities along with directions for future research in higher education as an optimal response." The strategy follows from the review's mapping, which found most studies come from high-income countries and few are empirical, implying research should extend to under-studied contexts and empirical designs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Future studies should address the gaps in the existing literature mapped by the review, including its concentration in high-income countries and its scarcity of empirical data.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students
  • higher education teachers and researchers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • building an evidence base on ChatGPT use and impact in higher education

Related Strategies¶

  • Prioritise longitudinal validation, pedagogical grounding, governed adoption of agent infrastructures, and ethics with human oversight in educational agentic AI research
  • Prioritize real-world testing of AI tools in university settings, environmental impact measurement, and integrated ethical-sustainability policies
  • Evaluate GenAI declaration frameworks empirically and pair them with institutional guidance on which tools are in scope
  • Future shared story reading research should extend to nonfiction texts, inclusive settings, and include practicality statements
