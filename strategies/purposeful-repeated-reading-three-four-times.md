# Reread the same passage 3–4 times, each time with a different purpose

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For fluency building, structure repeated reading so each reread has a distinct purpose rather than mere speed practice. "The panel recommends having students reread the same passage a total of 3–4 times, each time with a different purpose." Purposes include reading with expression, answering questions, identifying unknown words, or reflecting on what was learned; after each reading the teacher briefly discusses responses to hold students accountable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Choose short, content-rich passages at students' instructional level including previously taught words and vocabulary
- After each reading, briefly discuss student responses to the proposed questions
#### Constraints
- Reading the same passage several times, if not structured well, can be perceived as dull and discouraging for upper-elementary and middle school students

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4–9 with reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading effortlessly and with increased understanding of connected text
