# Pretest the assessment and evaluate fitness for purpose before live administration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The framework prescribes pretesting the assessment with a relatively small sample of students from the target population before administration, then evaluating fitness for purpose. The article states: "If the assessment does not function correctly, the eleventh step loops back to either the ﬁrst step of the ﬁrst stage or the ﬁrst step of the second stage, and the assessment developer should repeat all steps from that point on." Only when pretesting delivers the desired interpretation of assessment scores may the assessment be administered live, and quality should be constantly monitored thereafter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pretest sample large enough to draw meaningful inferences about the functioning of the tasks in the assessment
#### Constraints
- Administration does not mean the assessment is complete; fitness for purpose should be constantly monitored by educational and assessment experts

### Target Learners
- students from the target population of the assessment

### Target Learning Goals
- reliable interpretation of assessment scores for certification or placement decisions
