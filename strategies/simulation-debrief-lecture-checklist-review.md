# Pair the simulation with a structured 30–40 minute debrief including a PowerPoint lecture, reflection, and checklist review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the 20–25 minute simulation, the authors ran a 30–40 minute debrief combining a PowerPoint lecture on inpatient and outpatient management of EV and PUD, central venous access, massive transfusion protocol, and hemorrhagic shock, with group reflection and faculty review of a critical-action checklist. They chose a "lecture-based PowerPoint format to present visual aids and display information clearly on the screen, making it easier for learners to engage in discussions and formulate questions."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case authors and board-certified physicians plus simulation faculty to lead the debrief; a critical action checklist and debrief slides
#### Constraints
- When the simulation exceeded the allotted time, follow-up discussion was moved into the debrief

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents (PGY1–PGY3)

### Target Learning Goals
- Consolidating knowledge of GI bleed management topics encountered during the simulation and reflecting on team performance
