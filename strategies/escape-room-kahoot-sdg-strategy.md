# University students design and monitor gamified activities (escape room and Kahoot! quiz) to teach an SDG to secondary students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the A-BASE-DE-PROS project, university students acted as monitors of gamified activities for secondary students: an escape room on SDG 6 in which ESO students completed "6 pruebas" under university-student supervision, earning crossword clues whose yellow, green and blue letters yielded the final enigma (solution: ODS + AGUA + SANEAMIENTO), and a Kahoot! quiz competition prepared by university students and run with secondary students at Colegio El Porvenir after presentations of their university coursework.

#### Requirements

## Claims

- [Participation in the A-BASE-DE-PROS service-learning project increased students' knowledge of the SDGs, which before participation was around 50%](../claims/aps-sdg-knowledge-increase-abase-de-pros.md) [+W]
- [The final questionnaire indicated changes in student habits and behaviours producing small savings of water and electricity, and high motivation with the activities](../claims/habit-change-and-motivation-abase-de-pros.md) [+W]
- [Most secondary-school participants valued the service-learning collaboration positively: 60% liked collaborating with university students, 65% liked the activities, and 72% considered they had learned interesting things](../claims/secondary-students-positive-evaluation-abase-de-pros.md) [+W]
- [Students attributed competencies in teamwork, time management, quick thinking, critical thinking, communication, and confidence to team-based gamified quizzing](../claims/student-reported-competencies-gamified-quizzing.md) [+W]
- [A K-means clustering of students based on pre- and post-project SDG knowledge identifies three distinct learning profiles, with the largest cluster showing the greatest knowledge gain.](../claims/k-means-three-sgc-learning-clusters.md) [+W]
