# Move AIED beyond its modeling comfort zone into ill-defined domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends extending effectiveness research beyond well-modeled domains. It observes that "a great portion of today's AIED research is in something we may call its Bcomfort zone ^, where student and domain modeling is feasible with known and established techniques such as Constraint Based Modeling." It argues students could benefit from personalized automated feedback in domains like ethics essays or virtual reality surgery training, though this may require advances in natural language processing and knowledge representation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further developments in other scientific fields such as natural language processing technology or knowledge representation
#### Constraints
- Established modeling techniques cannot be applied easily in many ill-defined domains (Pinkwart et al., 2009)

### Target Learners
- students in ill-defined domains such as ethics, art, and surgery training

### Target Learning Goals
- personalized automated feedback beyond well-structured domains like geometry
