# Assess EBP learning with a performance-based instrument plus analysis of authentic written work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following the CREATE recommendation, the study assessed EBP knowledge and skills with the Adapted Fresno Test, a cognitive and performance instrument using clinical-uncertainty scenarios, and added a qualitative analysis of students' final monographs. The authors state it is "the first study to use a cognitive and performance assessment instrument (Adapted Fresno Test) with undergraduate nursing students, as suggested by CREATE".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scoring raters blinded to treatment assignment; scenarios presenting clinical uncertainty to prevent guessing
#### Constraints
- The Adapted Fresno Test may be less sensitive when small changes occur or students have low levels of EBP knowledge and skills

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- EBP knowledge retention and application skills in clinical scenarios
