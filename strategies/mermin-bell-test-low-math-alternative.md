# Mermin-style Bell-test alternative requiring less mathematical background but more measurements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
As an alternative to the CHSH approach, the article proposes a Bell-test treatment based on Mermin's 'Is the moon there when nobody looks?' exposition, which relies less on students' mathematical background and can be applied directly without previous explanation. The trade-off is statistical: "more measurements are needed to get good statistics, which translates into larger groups of students needed". Details and a comparison to the CHSH approach are given in the article's Appendix B.
