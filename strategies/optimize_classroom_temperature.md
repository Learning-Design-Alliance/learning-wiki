# Optimize Classroom Temperature

## Description
Maintaining an ideal temperature range (68-74°F) in classrooms can significantly impact student engagement and achievement. Implement strategies such as providing teachers with control over classroom temperature, ensuring proper ventilation, and using air conditioning systems to offset the negative impacts of heat. Consider factors like sunlight exposure and outdoor temperatures when adjusting classroom climates. Research indicates that extreme temperatures can lead to decreased concentration and productivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Temperature control systems, thermometers, and proper ventilation. Teacher training on optimizing classroom temperature for learning.
#### Constraints
- May be costly to implement temperature control systems in older buildings. Individual temperature preferences may vary, requiring compromise.

## Examples
- A study found that student achievement fell by the equivalent of 1% of a year’s worth of learning for each additional degree Fahrenheit in temperature during the year preceding the exam. School air conditioning penetration reported in 2016 mitigates the adverse effect of hot temperatures substantially, such that moving from a school with no air-conditioned classrooms to a school with all air-conditioned classrooms reduces the impact by approximately 78 percent.
