# Structure an advanced lab sequence as a professional scientific collaboration with inter-group model testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the three-week Manifold Lab, the class operates as a research collaboration whose design "seeks to emulate the experience of working within a professional scientific collaboration": groups share model reports, write grant proposals, and design experiments to test and revise each other's models. One group creates a model through a model-generating experiment, another tests it, and the results reject, revise, or further substantiate the model, completing a full ISLE cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A class-wide repository of model-generating reports; a single-page grant proposal summarizing another group's findings and proposing a testing experiment; and instructor review of proposals for feasibility, with equipment upgrade credits granted to each group.
#### Constraints
- Implemented as a three-week lab (VR3-VR5) for advanced students in a sophomore-level experimental physics course; the instructor works with each group to revise proposed experiments so each is likely to produce a clear outcome building on the prior group's findings.

### Target Learners
- sophomore-level applied physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- testing and revising other groups' models
- completing a full cycle of model generation, testing, and revision
