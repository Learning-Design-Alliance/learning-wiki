Equip teachers for digital story use through in-service training and teacher education courses¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study, q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the meta-analysis findings that digital story applications have a high positive effect on achievement, the authors recommend that teachers be sufficiently equipped regarding the digital story method. Existing teachers should gain skills through training programs such as in-service training or courses, and prospective teachers should receive training through courses on technology-based methods in teacher training institutions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The teacher's guidance is critical to achieve the desired results from the digital story method

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • existing teachers
  • prospective teachers in teacher training institutions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective implementation of digital story method in lessons

Claims¶

  • Digital story applications have a high positive average effect on students' academic achievement compared to other teaching methods (ES = 0.750) [+W]
  • Course type significantly moderates the digital story effect: mathematical/statistical courses show a larger effect (1.085) than non-mathematical courses (0.585) [~W]
  • Teaching level significantly moderates the digital story effect, with kindergarten showing the highest joint effect size [~W]
