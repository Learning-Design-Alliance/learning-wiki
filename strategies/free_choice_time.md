# Free Choice Time

## Description
Provide learners with the autonomy to select their learning activities, mirroring the self-directed learning seen in preschool environments. This strategy fosters intrinsic motivation, deep engagement, and a sense of ownership over the learning process. By connecting tasks to personal interests and future goals, learners are more likely to invest in their education and retain information effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires flexibility in curriculum and assessment design. Instructors must be willing to relinquish some control over the learning process and provide support for learners' self-directed activities.
#### Constraints
- May require more planning and resources to support diverse learning choices. Learners may struggle with decision-making if they are not accustomed to having autonomy in their learning.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase learner engagement, develop independence and responsibility, enhance retention of learning, connect tasks to personal interests and future goals

### Affordances
- Promotes intrinsic motivation, allows for deep engagement with content, and fosters a sense of ownership over learning. Learners are more likely to see the value in their work when they have a choice in what and how they learn.

### Personalization
- Offer a variety of choices that cater to different interests and learning styles. Provide support and resources to help learners make informed decisions about their learning paths.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learner engagement and motivation during free choice activities. Assess the depth of understanding and quality of work produced by learners. Gather feedback from learners about their experiences with free choice time.

## Impact
- Research indicates that choice-based learning increases learner motivation, engagement, and retention of information. Giving learners autonomy over their learning fosters a growth mindset and prepares them for lifelong learning.
