## Description
In its conclusion the article proposes extending mobile learning research beyond SMS to richer mobile media. It states that further studies "can test the multimedia features of especially mobile phones such as Podcast, Vodcast etc., and investigate the effects of these media on different course contents". This is a forward-looking research and design recommendation rather than a tested finding of the study.
