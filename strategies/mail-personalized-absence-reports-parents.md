# Mail parents personalized, actionable absence reports about their own student's attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's core implementable activity is delivering Absence Reports to parents and guardians containing "actionable, personalized information about their student's attendance." Districts first securely share attendance data, the program identifies students most at risk of chronic absenteeism and most likely to benefit, and reports with content tailored for each student are mailed directly home. This low-cost communication strategy was tested in two randomized evaluations and produced statistically significant reductions in absences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Secure receipt of attendance data from the district
- Identification of students at risk of chronic absenteeism to target the mailings
#### Constraints
- Evidence reported is for students determined to be at risk for absenteeism; effects are on absences, not on other outcomes

### Target Learners
- Parents and guardians of K-12 students at risk of chronic absenteeism

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing student absences
