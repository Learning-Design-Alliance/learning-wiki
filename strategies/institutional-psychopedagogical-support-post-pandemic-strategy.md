# Universities should provide psychopedagogical support and coordinate student mental-health follow-up with the public health network, and faculty should organize teaching tasks in a balanced way to prevent overload

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, with the resumption of in-person activities, "as instituições de ensino prestem apoio psicopedagógico aos seus discentes com alguma demanda de saúde mental e articulem o seu acompanhamento na rede de atenção à saúde do SUS". It also urges faculty and administrators to attend to students' feeling of overload by "organizando as tarefas de ensino de maneira equilibrada e fornecendo orientação e ajuda imediata". These actions are justified by the high prevalence of negative affectivity found and its association with academic decline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational institutions able to engage with SUS health-system management bodies regarding academic-community mental health demands
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a recommendation grounded in a correlational, single-institution cross-sectional study; it did not test the interventions' effectiveness

### Target Learners
- undergraduate university students in the post-COVID-19 pandemic context

### Target Learning Goals
- protecting academic performance and professional future outlook by promoting student mental health
