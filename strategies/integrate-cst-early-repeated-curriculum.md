# Integrate communication skills training early and repeatedly within the regular pharmacy curriculum rather than as a single course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that pharmacy educators and curriculum planners include communication courses in the undergraduate curriculum and integrate communication skills within the basic structure of the pharmacy curriculum rather than offering it as a single course. They cite evidence that early and repeated learning allows time to develop and refine skills, and that exposing students to training at an earlier stage would help build confidence to a greater extent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Suitable training programs with ample opportunities to learn and practice communication skills
#### Constraints
- The authors note Aspegren asserts training for more than one day is more effective and should be repeated to maintain skills; the present study tested only a single session

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students

### Target Learning Goals
- effective patient communication
- patient-centered care
- interprofessional communication
