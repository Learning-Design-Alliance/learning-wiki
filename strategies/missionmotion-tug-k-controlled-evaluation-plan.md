# Evaluate gamified kinematics interventions with a standardized conceptual test such as the modified TUG-K in a controlled design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For the second stage, the authors plan implementation in 4–5 schools with a sample of 150–200 students "under an experimental design with control and experimental groups", applying "standardized tests such as the modified TUG-K (Zavala et al., 2017)" to measure understanding of kinematics graphs and evaluate the conceptual impact of the intervention. They also plan to release resources in open-access format so the proposal can be replicated and adapted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized assessment instruments such as the modified TUG-K
- Control and experimental groups across multiple schools
#### Constraints
- This evaluation is planned but not yet conducted; no conceptual learning results are reported in this article

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
