# Use arrows as visuospatial cues to direct the learner's attention to relevant regions of an animation.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q4` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article identifies cueing as "emphasizing relevant regions of an animation in order to direct the learner's attention to them", with one approach focusing on manipulation of the visuospatial features including use of arrows. The researchers incorporated arrows as visuospatial cues in the animation they developed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cues should direct the learner to specific locations within the animation to help select relevant information.
#### Constraints
- Cues that direct the learner to specific locations within the animation are effective and help the learner select relevant information, but they do not necessarily assist the learner to identify causal relations.

### Target Learners
- practical engineering college students

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient processing of complex animations
