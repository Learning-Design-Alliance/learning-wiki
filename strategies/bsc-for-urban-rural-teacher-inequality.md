# Use BSC to bring good teachers from urban schools to rural schools to address the urban-rural education divide

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes BSC as a way to counter rural teacher shortages: a promising solution is "to bring good teachers from urban schools to rural schools by using BSC approach". Urban and rural classes are integrated so remote rural students interact in real time with urban teachers. The authors discuss large-scale application of BSC to deal with the urban-rural divide based on their results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient internet and synchronous technology infrastructure
- Teachers willing and able to give equal attention to local and remote classrooms
#### Constraints
- Addresses contexts where rural schools cannot attract enough good teachers

### Target Learners
- Rural primary school students lacking access to qualified teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing education inequality by improving rural students' learning performance
