Use mindful listening turn-taking to build communication and constructive feedback between students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief describes integrating mindful listening into the curriculum for elementary and secondary students. “Students learn to take turns as the speaker and as the listener; the listener does not interrupt the speaker while they are talking and instead practices focusing their attention entirely on what the other person is sharing.” After the speaker shares, listeners may offer feedback or ideas based on what they heard, with the stated benefit that students become better listeners and provide constructive, responsive feedback.
