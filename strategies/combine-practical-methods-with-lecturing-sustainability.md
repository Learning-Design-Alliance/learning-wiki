# Combine practical, experiential methods with traditional lecturing to enhance sustainability learning in dietetics education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that "Combining practical, experiential methods with traditional lecturing may enhance sustainability learning," noting that less effective methods like traditional lectures were combined with practical approaches such as project/problem-based learning and place-based environmental education, enhancing overall impact. It offers this as a valuable strategy for revising existing training concepts by supplementing currently used methods with more effective approaches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need sufficient time, institutional support and motivation to implement practical, community-oriented approaches alongside lecturing
#### Constraints
- The review cannot draw conclusions about the actual effect of the interventions or whether their duration was sufficient to foster sustainability competencies

### Target Learners
- Dietetics and nutrition students in tertiary and non-formal education

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustainability competencies, including systems-thinking and values-thinking

### Affordances
