# Embed mentoring into experiential learning training opportunities for teachers to build foundational teaching skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend pairing experiential training components with mentoring: "We suggest that providing experiential learning training opportunities with mentoring for teachers, regardless of the population they teach, may be beneficial in supporting and building the foundational skills needed for long term effective teaching". In the study, mentoring meant a mentor present during self-scoring who provided immediate corrective feedback following a predetermined formal script and procedural checklist. The recommendation extends the findings to teacher education generally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mentor who provides frequent and immediate corrective feedback following a predetermined formal script and procedural checklist
#### Constraints
- The evidence base is a small exploratory study of six teachers; the authors state the evidence must be considered preliminary
- No empirical studies support a specific duration for receiving mentorship

### Target Learners
- teachers and educators
- health science professionals working with children with autism

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained procedural integrity of instruction
- self-reflective critical thinking and error prevention capacity
