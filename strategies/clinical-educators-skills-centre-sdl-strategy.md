# Implement SDL through clinical educators supported by a clinical skills centre

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Appoint clinical educators to organize, follow up, and provide opportunities for reflection and feedback, supported by educational coordinators at a clinical skills centre. Clinical educators borrowed training materials to their own department, provided support and safety during skills training, and rotated between workplaces with flexible hours. The clinical skills centre allowed planning, support, and follow-up of educational activities based on workload and learning needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical educators with dedicated responsibility for learning
- Clinical skills centre support for planning and follow-up
- Managerial commitment to prioritizing learning time
#### Constraints
- Clinical educators expressed apprehensions that SDL may lead managers to deprioritize allocation of time for organizing practical training sessions

### Target Learners
- nurses
- assistant nurses
- clinical educators

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills training
- continuing professional development
