# Human-in-the-loop instructor control as a design direction for adaptive learning systems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A specific future-direction recommendation: because a fully automated system may not personalize adequately, the editorial proposes that "there be adaptive learning systems that allow a human-in-the-loop (the instructor) to adjust the experience with real-time control", so that "instructors who know their students could aid in that personalization process". It also asks for tools that ensure learners on different paths meet a given set of learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mechanisms letting the instructor adjust the learning experience with real-time control over the running adaptive system
#### Constraints
- Framed as an open possibility premised on the assumption that the system may not be smart enough to fully personalize every learner's experience

### Target Learners
- instructors
- learners

### Target Learning Goals
- personalizing adaptive learning experiences with instructor oversight
