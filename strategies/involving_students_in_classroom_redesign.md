# Involving Students in Classroom Redesign

## Description
Involving learners in the process of designing and transforming their learning environment can create a space that better meets their needs and preferences. This includes soliciting their input on layout, furniture choices, color selections, and organization of materials. By presenting research on how the brain works and the impact of the physical environment on learning, educators can empower learners to make informed decisions about their learning landscape. This approach recognizes that learners are active participants in their education and that their voices should be heard when creating a supportive and engaging classroom environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness to solicit student input, knowledge of brain-based learning principles, flexible approach to classroom design, resources for implementing student ideas
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort, potential for conflicting opinions among students, requires careful facilitation to ensure all voices are heard, may not be feasible in all classroom settings
