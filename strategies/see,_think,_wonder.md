# See, Think, Wonder

## Description
A thinking routine developed by Project Zero to stimulate curiosity, normalize error, increase critical thinking skills, and ensure that every learner starts from a place of success. It involves students observing a stimulating image, then recording what they see, what they think about it, and what it makes them wonder.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A three-column organizer (See, Think, Wonder), stimulating images, time for individual brainstorming and sharing
#### Constraints
- May require careful selection of images to ensure they are engaging and appropriate; some students may need prompting to move beyond surface-level observations

- Promotes critical thinking, encourages curiosity, makes thinking visible, lowers the stakes for participation, allows for diverse interpretations

### Personalization
- Image complexity can be tailored to student skill levels; response formats can be varied; students choose which question to share with a peer
