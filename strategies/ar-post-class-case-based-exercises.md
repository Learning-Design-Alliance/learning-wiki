# Integrate AR into post-class case-based exercises to reinforce knowledge retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using AR after class rather than before, since students preferred post-class AR use (73.68%). They state that "integrating AR into post-class case-based exercises may further reinforce knowledge retention, aligning with students' preference for post-class AR use (73.68%)." The strategy positions AR as a supplementary consolidation activity following traditional teaching and preliminary theoretical instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students should first acquire preliminary theoretical knowledge through traditional teaching before post-class AR use
#### Constraints
- Recommendation rests on subjective preference data from a single-group questionnaire study; the authors call for randomized controlled trials with quantitative outcomes

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates in obstetrics and gynecology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge retention of anatomical and delivery-process content
- clinical thinking through case-based exercises
