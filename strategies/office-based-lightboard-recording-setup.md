# Office-based lightboard recording setup: blackout blinds, black backdrop, mobile-device recording with manual-control camera apps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete recipe for recording lightboard videos in a faculty office. The authors "installed blackout blinds in their offices" to block external light causing reflections, temporarily hang black fabric backdrops, and record with college-issued iPads or mobile phones rather than dedicated cameras. For mobile devices with fixed apertures, they recommend third-party video apps allowing manual control of ISO, shutter speed, white balance, and frame rate, with iterative adjustment of settings and lighting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Third-party video apps allowing manual control of ISO, shutter speed, white balance, and frame rate for mobile-device recording
- Iterative adjustment of camera settings and lighting levels, since many combinations give an attractive result and many more do not
#### Constraints
- Settings are camera specific and impacted by pane and presenter lighting details

### Target Learners
- higher education faculty producing instructional videos

### Target Learning Goals
- production of high-quality instructional lightboard videos
