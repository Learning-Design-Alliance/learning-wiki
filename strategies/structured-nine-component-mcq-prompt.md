# Use detailed, structured prompts specifying question type, case details, distractors, explanation, source, and difficulty level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Tip 6 recommends composing prompts from defined components: topic in blueprint, question type, case structure, patient details, stem, four distractors with one correct answer, explanation, information source, and difficulty level. The authors combine components from published prompts (Zuckerman's NBME-style prompt and Kiyak's nine-component prompt) into a prototype prompt and show a ChatGPT-generated community-acquired pneumonia item that complies with the required structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient processing time; resources fully uploaded before prompting; avoid excessive batches which may lead to biases and errors such as AI hallucinations
#### Constraints
- Generated items may need more iterations and prompt refinement to achieve the desired level of clarity and difficulty

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate medical education trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- high-quality, flaw-free MCQ items matching specified format and difficulty
