Strategies

Use short 2-hour to 3-hour workshops with didactic and practical elements for perineal anatomy education.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review reports that the majority of effective perineal anatomy studies had an intervention duration of 2 days or less, and 2-hour to 3-hour workshops were just as effective as longer options. This suggests multiple days of education are likely unnecessary for learning perineal anatomy. These workshops typically involved didactic education followed by a practical hands-on workshop or dissection lab.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Workshops should pair didactic education on pelvic floor anatomy with a practical hands-on component.

Constraints¶

  • Limited research exists on the optimal duration of short educational interventions in healthcare.

Target Learners¶

  • Medical students
  • Residents
  • Midwives

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Perineal anatomy knowledge

Claims¶

  • Education and training programmes increase knowledge and skills of skilled birth attendants in perineal anatomy, assessment and classification of perineal tears. [+W]
  • Education programmes including perineal tear prevention training are associated with reduced severe perineal tear rates. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed routine evaluation into all training and education programmes to determine effectiveness.
