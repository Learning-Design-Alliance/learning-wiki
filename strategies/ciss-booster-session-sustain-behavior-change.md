# Add a reinforcing booster counseling session after a personalized risk reduction plan to help sustain safer sex behavior change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the CISS's 6-month effects became nonsignificant, the authors recommend a reinforcing booster session: "A reinforcing booster session to evaluate and adapt the personalized risk reduction plan if needed, could potentially contribute to sustaining behavior change." The strategy would give participants a structured opportunity, after the third session's individualized plan, to review and adjust the plan in real life with their counselor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A counselor available for follow-up contact after the three core sessions; a previously developed personalized risk reduction plan to review
#### Constraints
- The article states the added value of such a booster has been documented elsewhere, but future research should determine if it improves CISS effectiveness; the trial itself did not test it

### Target Learners
- HIV-positive MSM who completed a computer-assisted safer sex counseling program

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining safer sex behavior change beyond the short term
