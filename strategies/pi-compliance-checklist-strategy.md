# Use a five-question PI compliance checklist when designing course material

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To facilitate adoption by educators, the authors propose a checklist of the core elements of PI, usable "to evaluate how in-line one's program is with PI" when building educational sequences or designing course material. It asks whether behavioural objectives are finite and frame-separated, whether response requirements are detailed, whether direct immediate feedback is available per frame, and whether progression is individualised and mastery-based.
