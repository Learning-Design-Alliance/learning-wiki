# Association

## Description
Association is a mnemonic device that links new information to something already known to make it easier to remember. This can involve creating mental images, stories, or connections that tie the new information to familiar concepts or people. The more unique or silly the association, the better.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ability to create mental images and connections. A clear understanding of the new information to be remembered.
#### Constraints
- May be less effective for abstract or complex information without a strong personal connection.
