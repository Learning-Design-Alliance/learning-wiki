# Capstone in which teams connect galaxy case studies to reveal galaxy formation and evolution pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Module 7 closes the semester with a capstone in which each team studies one galaxy type across wavelengths, then the class synthesizes. Students "carry out LRGB + Hα narrowband imaging, and supplement these with archival MIR images," map their target for synchrotron emission from a central supermassive black hole, and collect an on-off HI spectrum. Finally, "in an inter-team / class-wide activity, students organize their galaxies by their observed star-formation and synchrotron-emitting properties." Because no one lives long enough to see galaxies evolve, "they instead 'connect the dots' of their case studies, revealing the two primary ways in which galaxies form and evolve."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple teams each studying a different galaxy type so the class-wide chart can be populated with diverse case studies
#### Constraints
- Galaxy evolution itself cannot be observed directly; the evolutionary story is inferred by connecting case studies, not watched

### Target Learners
- students completing the full MWU! module sequence

### Target Learning Goals
- multi-wavelength tracers of star formation
- galaxy classification and evolution
- synthesizing class-wide observations into a scientific narrative
