# Tutor carousel rotation to reduce tutor variation in multi-tutor online courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To mitigate the effect of tutor variation on training outcomes in the pilot, the article reports that "the carousel technique was implemented: tutors rotated weekly, with a dedicated tutor being responsible for the week's material, enabling them to interact with each group at least once during the training period." This is complemented by identical recorded lectures for all participants and consultation sessions among tutors in weeks 1, 3 and 6 to share experiences and align implementation. The strategy is an operational design choice for multi-tutor trials rather than a tested manipulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tutor pool of qualified instructors (here doctoral students, Oxford lecturers and Quantinuum researchers) willing to rotate weekly, plus coordination sessions to keep material consistent.
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a variance-reduction measure within one trial; it reports no comparative evidence on its effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- high-school students in tutor-led online tutorials of up to 15 students

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent delivery of weekly course material across tutorial groups
