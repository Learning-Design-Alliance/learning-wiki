# Implement mandatory structured in-service ECG training with simulation-based practice and rhythm-strip workshops for ICU nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured, mandatory in-service ECG education tailored to ICU nursing roles, combining "Simulation‐based learning, hands‐on rhythm‐strip workshops and assessment of ECG interpretation skills" integrated into routine clinical training. It grounds this in the finding that prior training predicted knowledge and attitudes, and that complex-pattern performance was low. Institutions are also advised to prioritise protected time and accessible training pathways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must provide protected time and accessible training pathways for ICU nurses, as the article states.
#### Constraints
- The article notes standardised ECG training in Greek undergraduate and continuing education pathways remains fragmented, and that its own knowledge measure used true/false items rather than actual rhythm strips.

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses employed in adult ICUs

### Target Learning Goals
- ECG rhythm recognition and arrhythmia detection
- Timely recognition of cardiac deterioration
