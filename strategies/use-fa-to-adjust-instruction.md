# Use FA to adjust instruction: alter pacing, tasks, and modes of engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teachers across the 11 primary studies adjusted instruction based on FA evidence, with common adjustments including "altering the (a) lesson pacing, (b) mathematics tasks and exercises, and (c) modes of student engagement" such as transitioning from whole group to independent or group work. Formative feedback discussed timely with individuals or small groups was highlighted as a tool for moving student thinking forward.
