Implement Learning Genie with classroom teachers in regular workflow plus a site- or district-level implementation lead¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's implementation model assigns the platform to "classroom teachers" as "part of their regular instructional and assessment workflow," with "no additional instructional staff are required." A site- or district-level implementation lead — an administrator, instructional coach, or data manager — coordinates account setup, SIS integration, and technical support during initial rollout. Initial training covers observation-based assessment tools, DRDP documentation, lesson-planning workflows, and AI-supported instructional recommendations, delivered on-site, via live virtual workshops, or recorded webinars with self-paced support materials and ongoing assistance through the school year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A site- or district-level implementation lead to coordinate account setup, SIS integration, and technical support during initial rollout
  • Initial training on observation-based assessment tools, DRDP documentation, lesson-planning workflows, and AI-supported instructional recommendations

Constraints¶

  • Ongoing implementation and technical assistance are needed throughout the school year

Target Learners¶

  • Prekindergarten through grade 12 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher capacity for standards-aligned, UDL-based instructional planning and observation-based assessment

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement Learning Genie with initial training on observation-based assessment and ongoing technical assistance
  • Implement the spiral-review supplement with existing classroom staff, teacher onboarding training, and weekly computer-based quizzes
  • Plan mutual accountability for implementation, including rollout plans, educator supports, and early indicators for pivoting
