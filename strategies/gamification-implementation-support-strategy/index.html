Support gamified learning implementation with teacher training, technical preparedness, and regular tool evaluation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's challenges section recommends that institutions implementing gamified learning provide comprehensive training and support for students and teachers, ensure adequate technological infrastructure, and regularly evaluate gamification tools. It states that educators should be "well-versed in gamification strategies and prepared to troubleshoot potential technical issues" and that "regular evaluation of gamification tools and methods is also essential" to maintain relevance and effectiveness.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators trained in gamification strategies and technical troubleshooting
  • Adequate technological infrastructure and platform access

Constraints¶

  • Technical challenges such as platform access issues and connectivity problems hindered effective interaction during the COVID-19 shift to online learning
  • Student difficulty integrating game-based formats with traditional learning methods

Target Learners¶

  • Second-language learners at tertiary and school levels using gamified vocabulary tools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Second-language vocabulary acquisition and sustained engagement

Related Strategies¶

  • Devote adequate formal training time to all school staff — especially non-team classroom teachers — when implementing a decentralized service model
  • CEO Forum recommendations: prepare and certify teachers for technology integration across pre-service, licensure, and in-service career stages
  • Prepare educational staff early for digital transformation competency and train teachers to select and use appropriate communication technology tools
  • Pursue continuous collaboration between digital learning platform providers and school districts, with teacher training, to interconnect platform use with instruction
