# Increase hands-on training and early patient contact to build clinical examination confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors suggest that "More opportunities for hands-on training and early patient contact may help students build confidence and improve their clinical examination skills." This strategy addresses the finding that students performed poorly on history-taking and examination items requiring practical exposure, such as breast cancer history (26.6% correct) and cardiovascular examination questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to patients for early clinical exposure
- Structured opportunities for supervised hands-on practice
#### Constraints
- Recommended based on observed knowledge gaps and comparison with international studies; not directly tested as an intervention

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in preclinical and clinical years

### Target Learning Goals

- Physical examination skills
- Clinical confidence
