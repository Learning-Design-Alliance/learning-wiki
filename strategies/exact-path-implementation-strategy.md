# Implement Exact Path with existing district staff supported by vendor professional development and Customer Success Managers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The summary states that Exact Path Growth "is implemented by a district's current teachers, school leaders, and district leaders with guidance provided by Edmentum's dedicated Customer Success Managers" and that "No additional staffing is required." Edmentum's Professional Services Team, with subject matter coaches, offers dedicated professional development focused on elevating instructional practices to foster continuous learning for districts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts use current teachers and leaders rather than hiring additional staff.
- Professional development is delivered by Edmentum's Professional Services Team in alliance with subject matter coaches and professionals.
#### Constraints
- The strategy is specific to the Exact Path program and its vendor-provided support structure; the summary does not report effects of the professional development itself.

### Target Learners
- K-12 learners in implementing districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Accelerating and reporting student achievement in Math and English Language Arts standards
