# Integrate AISP platforms into medical curricula as complements to traditional teaching with expanded case variety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student recommendations and the study's feasibility findings, the article suggests integrating AISP platforms into formal curricula, particularly for OSCE preparation and introductory pre-clerkship modules, while expanding case diversity. Students suggested a random case mode varying contextual details such as name, age, and background to maintain challenge, and described using the platform alongside lectures and workshops for individualized practice outside class time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty capacity to expand and maintain the case library
- Integration points in existing curricula and assessment frameworks
#### Constraints
- AISPs are best suited to structured, repeatable practice of history-taking and clinical reasoning rather than interpersonal and relational skills
- Speech recognition accuracy and conversational prosody in non-English contexts need refinement

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students
- Pre-clerkship students

### Target Learning Goals
- OSCE preparation
- Structured history-taking practice
- Clinical reasoning
