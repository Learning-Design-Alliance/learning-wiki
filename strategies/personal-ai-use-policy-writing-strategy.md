# Personal AI use policy writing with end-of-course revisiting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An optional extension in which students draft a brief AI use policy articulating how they plan to use, or intentionally avoid, AI tools in ways that support their learning and align with course goals. Near the end of the term, students return to the policy and reflect on how their practices evolved, emphasizing "ethical and constructive AI use as a developmental process rather than a one-time decision." The exercise is framed as non-evaluative to support openness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Framing as non-evaluative, with no penalty for acknowledging past or current AI use
- Individual reflection prompts addressing insights, shifts in thinking, and planned use
#### Constraints
- In smaller classes the activity may be adapted for small-group policy drafting; in larger courses individual policy writing may be more effective

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in courses of varying size

### Target Learning Goals
- ethical self-regulation of AI use
- alignment of study practices with learning goals
