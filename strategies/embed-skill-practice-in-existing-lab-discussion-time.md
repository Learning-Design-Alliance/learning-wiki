# Embed new skill practice in existing course time with in-class group discussion rather than adding class time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors designed their assignments "to require little additional class time or instructor resources" by replacing existing homework discussion questions and completing exercises as homework, while preserving an important component of class time: group discussion of the assignments. Students received each assignment 1 week in advance, brought printed answers, met in small groups for 10–15 minutes, then joined a GTA-led class-wide discussion emphasizing student presentations, with handwritten revisions allowed before submission.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-existing discussion-question time in the course schedule that can be repurposed
- Teaching assistants able to lead class-wide discussion emphasizing student presentations
#### Constraints
- The authors state an important component of class time was group discussion, which they credit with grounding students in terminology and contributing to tree-construction success

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in lab-based science courses with tightly booked schedules

### Target Learning Goals
- Skill-based knowledge such as constructing and interpreting phylogenetic trees

### Affordances
- Deliberate Practice Instructional Approach
