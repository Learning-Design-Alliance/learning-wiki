Integrate TBL with other teaching methods and modern educational technologies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend integrating TBL with other teaching methods such as Problem-Based Learning and Case-Based Learning to "fully leverage the advantages of different teaching methods to achieve better teaching results". TBL can be integrated with LBL, using LBL's teaching resources and advantages to improve students' application and understanding of knowledge through TBL's teamwork and discussion. TBL can also be combined with modern educational technologies such as the flipped classroom and mobile learning applications; the authors report that flipped classroom combined with TBL enhances academic achievements and garners higher satisfaction rates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers formulate questions, construct situations, provide task-driven guidance for autonomous learning, and analyze and discuss case materials

Constraints¶

  • Adapting TBL for online and hybrid teaching environments is an emerging trend still under development

Target Learners¶

  • medical students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • knowledge application
  • independent thinking
  • team cooperation

Claims¶

  • Combining teaching methods (PBL + WeChat, CBL + WeChat, PBL + CBL) achieves better academic performance than single methods in Chinese resident training [+W]
  • PBL and TBL significantly improve nursing students' self-directed learning [+W]
  • CBL, PBL, and TBL each significantly improve nursing students' problem-solving abilities [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement TBL with dedicated teaching teams, systematic teacher training, and diversified assessment
  • Scaffold method choice by student progression: CBL early, transitioning to PBL and TBL
  • Align teaching method with competency gap and prioritize TBL for communication-focused courses
  • Integrate innovative instructional approaches (gamification, VR/AR, robotic coding, STEM) and out-of-school settings into PBL
  • Teachers can combine educational technologies, or use non-technology-based exploratory learning activities before an ITS, to leverage the combination effect
  • Adopt TBL preferentially for theoretical knowledge outcomes in medical education, applied flexibly by context
  • Combine CBL and PBL approaches in clinical teaching settings to target both theory and practical outcomes
  • Three-part implementation guidance for applying CBL in Chinese dental education
  • Combine the PBL and ATI models with different approaches for optimal results
