# Preserve linkable participant identifiers so dormant trials can be reactivated through linkage to administrative data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A data governance strategy: retain trial participant identifiers rather than destroying them after a set time, so dormant trials can later be reactivated through linkage to routinely collected administrative data. The article argues that "Data retention policies should be re-examined to allow linkable identifiers to be collected and preserved", because identifier destruction limits the value and public benefit of research data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- retention of the participants’ identifiers
- legal, governance, and ethics approvals that enabled linkage between trial data and routinely collected schools data with exemption from the need for participant consent
#### Constraints
- linkage without renewed participant consent relied on section 251 (NHS Act 2006) support in England

### Target Learners
- infants and children enrolled in longitudinal nutrition and developmental trials
- adolescents sitting national examinations in England

### Target Learning Goals
- long term cognitive development as measured by academic performance
- policy-relevant evidence on whether early interventions affect later learning
