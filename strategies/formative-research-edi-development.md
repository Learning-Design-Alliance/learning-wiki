# Formative research procedure for enhancing an instructional design model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article adapts Reigeluth and Frick's formative research, as implemented by Chen and Teh, to enhance the DI model: selecting a design theory, designing an instance with an expert of the model to avoid omission and commission weaknesses, conducting a pilot study, collecting and analyzing formative data, repeating cycles to confirm findings, and offering tentative revision of the model. The article states that "formative research is a kind of developmental research intended to improve design theory for designing instructional practices or processes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Involving an expert of the design model to ensure the instance is as pure an instance as possible
- Repeating data collection and analysis cycles to confirm earlier findings
#### Constraints
- The article applies the procedure to one design model (DI) in introductory physics at Tadulako University

### Target Learners
- Introductory physics students in teacher-education programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing a research-based instructional design that supports mental-modeling ability
