# Try the PBL approach in more Chinese courses to verify the meta-analytic results

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 5 studies (5 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 4 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending PBL beyond medical psychology as a way of testing their pooled finding under the new conditions information technology creates for traditional teaching. They write that they "suggested trying PBL approach in more courses, so as to verify the results", and their conclusion calls for large and well-designed studies to further confirm whether PBL is superior to different teaching methods in China.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation in Chinese college or university courses where effects on examination outcomes can be compared against traditional teaching
#### Constraints
- The authors note many medical students and teachers could not accept this teaching mode well, which may relate to traditional Chinese culture, and most courses were still taught in the traditional way

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in Chinese colleges and universities

### Target Learning Goals
- medical psychology knowledge acquisition
- integration of learned knowledge
