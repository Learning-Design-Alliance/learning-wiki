# Co-involve students in producing podcasted lessons of their own course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to have students take an active creative role in the podcasting of their own course's lessons, rather than only consuming recorded lectures. The article reports that students who were co-involved in lessons' podcasting "outperformed colleagues of the previous years" and developed higher competitive agency, making co-creation the recommended implementation of the podcasting service.
