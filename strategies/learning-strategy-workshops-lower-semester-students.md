# Offer learning-strategy workshops to help lower-semester students bridge self-directed learning skill gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend designing workshops that focus on learning strategies to help lower-semester students bridge the skills gap and mitigate potential challenges they may face. This follows from their finding that students in the first-to-fourth semester scored lower on self-directed learning than those in later semesters, which they attribute to greater familiarity with metacognitive strategies such as planning and self-assessment among senior students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshop content should target metacognitive strategies including planning and self-assessment
#### Constraints
- Based on cross-sectional group differences rather than longitudinal evidence of skill development

### Target Learners
- first- to fourth-semester nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive strategy use
- self-directed learning readiness
