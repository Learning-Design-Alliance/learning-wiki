# Reading instruction and cognitive flexibility training as mutually reinforcing intervention targets in elementary education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article states its findings "provide evidence for future reading or cognitive interventions", indicating that systematic reading instruction supports cognitive flexibility development and that well-developed cognitive flexibility supports reading achievement. Because the reciprocal prediction held across demographic variables, interventions targeting either ability in school-aged children may benefit the other through shared neural mechanisms involving the left MFG and salience network.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal school-based instruction in reading for elementary school children
#### Constraints
- The mediation is correlational and based on two time points; the flexibility-to-reading path held only for children with higher baseline cognitive flexibility
