# Use interactive training formats — role plays, skill rehearsal, video examples and practical worksheets — when teaching brief psychological strategies to health professionals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training design was influenced by the evidence-based approach to mental health training for health professionals, "incorporating a focus on interactive training involving role plays, rehearsal of skills, and the provision of relevant worksheet materials". Sessions combined lectures, role plays, video and group discussion, with a purpose-built DVD of expert interviews and role play examples and step-by-step worksheets usable with clients as homework tools. In the trial this design produced large immediate gains in objective and self-perceived competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers experienced in researching and facilitating mental health training; purpose-developed video and workbook materials
#### Constraints
- The trial measured competence on simulated problem-solving consultations only, so performance in other trained skills was not directly assessed

### Target Learners
- social workers and allied health professionals in primary mental health care

### Target Learning Goals
- practical delivery of focused psychological strategies such as structured problem solving and activity planning
