# Make dementia workforce education and training cost-effective and evaluate its costs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "The emerging education and training needs of the dementia care workforce must be cost‐effective". It reports substantial dementia care costs ($259 billion total payments in 2017, plus $56 billion out-of-pocket borne by untrained caregivers) and states "The cost of educating a dementia‐capable workforce is unknown". Research focus areas include evaluating cost savings of dementia-capable training and comparing costs of dementia certification versus a learning certificate in dementia care.

## Design Implications
