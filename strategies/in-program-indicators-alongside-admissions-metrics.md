# Incorporate early in-program performance indicators alongside admissions metrics for a fuller picture of student outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the weak predictive value of GPA and BCPM, the article suggests that admissions committees and educators consider in-program indicators. It states that "incorporating early in-program performance indicators alongside admissions metrics may help provide a more comprehensive understanding of student outcomes", and that limited predictive value of GPA and BCPM could prompt reevaluation of the weight assigned to these metrics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both pre-admission metrics and in-program assessment data
#### Constraints
- Admission implications based on predictive values of GPA and BCPM are contextual, not prescriptive

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance prediction and student support
