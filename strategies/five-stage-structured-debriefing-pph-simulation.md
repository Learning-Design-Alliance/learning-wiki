# Structured five-stage debriefing after simulated PPH care, moving through emotional, descriptive, evaluative, analytical and conclusive stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the simulated scenario, the facilitator conducted a debriefing focused on the care provided to the postpartum woman with hemorrhage. It "was structured into emotional, descriptive, evaluative, analytical, and conclusive stages" and was mediated by the facilitator, who first sought verbal expression from participants who acted in the scene and then from other group members. The debriefing prompted reflection on risk factors, clinical judgment, visual blood-loss estimation, shock index calculation, early diagnosis, calling for help, and starting the medication protocol.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator to mediate the stages and a checklist of expected actions to guide the debriefing process
#### Constraints
- Debriefing was allotted 30 minutes within a 45-minute individual session

### Target Learners
- Nurses providing direct obstetric care

### Target Learning Goals
- Reflection on PPH management care, clinical judgment, and identification of positive points and areas for improvement
