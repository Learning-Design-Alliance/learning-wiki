## Description
The thesis's first strategic line is to strengthen cohesion within the learning ecosystem related to quantum physics — the network connecting researchers, teachers and students. The thesis states: "Researchers, teachers and students need to be enabled to carry out activities that allow them to develop knowledge and skills through dialogue, sharing and collaboration." Concretely this was implemented through professional development programmes (Quantum Skills 2019, Quantum Jumps 2020), regular working groups between physics and education researchers, and educational co-design groups producing teaching-learning sequences for classrooms.
