# Extend EFL learning beyond the classroom with daily Web 2.0 interaction alongside traditional teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that EFL teachers "do more to utilize useful Web 2.0 tools in order to extend their students' learning outside the classroom", changing teaching habits to offer innovative opportunities for self-confidence, self-monitoring and language practice in and outside class. In the study this took the form of six weeks of daily Edmodo interaction between students and their teacher at home, which was followed by significantly more positive EFL attitudes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher and student training (two Edmodo workshops were held in the study) and access to Internet-connected devices
#### Constraints
- Technical difficulties (72% agreement) and financial barriers remain impediments to integrating Internet technology in education

### Target Learners
- secondary school EFL students

### Target Learning Goals

- language skills practice
- autonomous learning
