# Develop personalized e-learning strategies tailored to students' performance level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that e-learning course design account for learners' performance level when addressing motivation, methods, and burnout. It advocates that "motivation can be enhanced by considering the value components, expectancy components, and affective components of students across different academic levels" and hopes a future personalized system "could use questionnaires to automatically provide tailored instructional content". The recommendation rests on the tier-specific correlation findings of this small survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questionnaire-based assessment of learners' motivation, approaches, and burnout to tailor content
#### Constraints
- Based on correlational evidence from 37 students; the authors state the study "only explored the correlations between learning motivation/ learning approaches/ burnout and academic performance levels"

### Target Learners
- medical technology students in e-learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance in e-learning coursework
- self-regulated study skills
