# Use affective backchannels (congratulating, encouraging, sympathizing, reassuring) timed to conversation states to support L2 learners emotionally

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy prescribes deploying four categories of affective backchannels at specific conversation states: Congratulatory AB when the conversation goes well, Encouraging AB when the learner hesitates or remains silent, Sympathetic AB when the learner's utterance does not match expectations, and Reassuring AB when the learner faces much difficulty. The intent is that conveying interest or sympathy 'might reduce anxiety and encourage learners to take risks in using the target language'. ABs are chosen stochastically within the triggered category.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detection of the learner's conversation state (silent, asking for help, NUNA, UNA, or successful turn) to trigger the appropriate AB category
#### Constraints
- The system only approximates learner states rather than determining them accurately, following the argument that an approximation can still make a system more empathetic

### Target Learners
- Anxious or reticent L2 learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing L2 communication anxiety and encouraging risk-taking in target-language use
