# Graphic Organizers and Visual Aids

## Description
Tools that help students identify and focus on the most important information by presenting it in a structured and visual format. These aids support the organization of thoughts, the prioritization of key concepts, and the reduction of cognitive load, making it easier for students to filter out irrelevant stimuli and concentrate on pertinent details.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the content and key concepts, creation or selection of appropriate graphic organizers or visual aids, and explicit instruction on how to use them effectively.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if the graphic organizer or visual aid is too complex or poorly designed. Some students may become overly reliant on these tools and struggle to process information without them.

### Affordances
- Provides a clear and structured framework for organizing information. Reduces cognitive load by externalizing key concepts and relationships. Helps students visualize connections and prioritize information.

### Personalization
- Graphic organizers and visual aids can be customized to match individual student needs and learning preferences. Examples include concept maps, Venn diagrams, timelines, and flowcharts.
