# Offer modular and streamlined versions of the lab to fit different course formats and time budgets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors design the activities to be adaptable: instructors can drop Activity 2.1 or 2.2 (saving about 50 minutes), omit Activity 3 for a single three-hour period (saving about 120 minutes), or use a streamlined notebook for lecture courses. They write: "we have provided a streamlined version of the activities combining Activities 1 and 2 that is appropriate for a ∼1-hour class period." In this version students work through the same conceptual material with more coding guidance and less emphasis on matplotlib plotting, maintaining the learning objectives in a condensed format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modular activity structure with separable notebooks (Activity 1, 2.1, 2.2, 3) and a provided Streamlined.ipynb in the Supporting Information.
#### Constraints
- As designed, the three activities take approximately two 3-hour periods; the authors recognize this is a significant time commitment and encourage instructors to adapt the modules as needed.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate chemistry students in laboratory or lecture courses

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding how the Fourier transform converts temporal signals into frequency-domain spectra within constrained class time
