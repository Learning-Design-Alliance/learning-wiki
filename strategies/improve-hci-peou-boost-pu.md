# Improve the human-computer interface (PEOU) to raise perceived usefulness and motivate e-learning intention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 6 studies (5 associational, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that technology developers enhance perceived ease of use, for example through the human-computer interface, because PEOU is significantly and positively related to PU in both groups with effect sizes the authors describe as large by Cohen's guidelines. An enhanced PEOU contributes to an enhanced PU, which in turn motivates students' e-learning intention. This strategy targets the design of the e-learning platform rather than the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology developers with control over the human-computer interface of the e-learning system
#### Constraints
- PEOU-related effects are attenuated in the anxiety group, so ease-of-use improvements work mainly through their effect on PU

### Target Learners
- college students in higher education using e-learning platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- e-learning continuance intention

### Affordances
- Tam Synthesized Anxiety Elearning
