# Adapt training scenarios to professional profiles using obstacle placement and difficulty configuration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Different professionals have different needs in terms of spatial knowledge of the facilities, such as emergency intervention teams, logistics supervisors, operators performing non-destructive testing, and safety engineers. Trainers adapt scenarios by varying the objective, the information given before the hunt, and by placing obstacles in the building; the obstacle functionality in particular plays a central role in adapting scenarios and offers a way to evaluate learners' global understanding of the building structure as they adapt their strategies in real time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainer access to the obstacle-placement interface and scenario configuration options
#### Constraints
- Scenario difficulty must be matched to the class by the trainer

### Target Learners
- beginners, experienced professionals, engineers or operators in industrial facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- adaptive route planning around obstacles
- global understanding of building structure
