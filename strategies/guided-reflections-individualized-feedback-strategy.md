# Weekly guided reflections on lifelong learning skills with individualized instructive and affective feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students write weekly reflections on their growth as learners, guided by a rubric of 10 lifelong learning skills that defines "beginning, developing, or succeedin g at the practice of each skill" plus prompt questions. Instructors respond to each reflection with individualized feedback combining instructive support (suggesting strategies and resources, e.g., metacognitive planning before and after problems) and affective support (affirming, empathizing, or normalizing responses). Reflections ran for the first 11 weeks so students could focus on final projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time to provide individualized feedback weekly; a rubric defining skills at multiple proficiency levels
#### Constraints
- Final summative reflections did not receive feedback; reflections were assigned only during the first 11 weeks

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulated learning
- monitoring progress, setting goals, and making plans
