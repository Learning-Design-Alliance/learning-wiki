# Formation of local collaborative STEM fora linking researchers and K-12 educators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report recommends forming local collaborative communities ('fora') of STEM experts of all backgrounds — physicists, engineers, technicians, students, and K-12 teachers — to create direct linkages among researchers and educators for dialog, collaboration, mutual enrichment and mentoring. These fora should be responsive to local community need and supported by a nationally or internationally organised online repository for sharing resources.
