# Implement Renzulli Learning as 2-3 hours of weekly use over 16 weeks in treatment classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evaluated implementation assigned treatment classrooms to use Renzulli Learning for "2-3 hours each week for a 16-week period", a dosage the cluster-randomized study linked to significantly higher oral reading fluency growth. This schedule is the implementable recipe the evidence supports, alongside the program's lack of additional staffing requirements and optional paid or free professional development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom access to the cloud-based platform for the scheduled weekly hours; optional online professional development at $1,200 per day or free 1-1 Quick Start sessions
#### Constraints
- Evidence covers elementary and middle school students in two schools over 16 weeks; effects beyond that scope are not reported

### Target Learners
- Elementary and middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral reading fluency growth
- student engagement through differentiated instruction
