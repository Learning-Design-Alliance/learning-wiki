# Scaffold early community-based experiences through longitudinal, progressively structured activities with supervision and reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the reduction in perceived costs was minimal among junior students, the authors conclude that "a brief, isolated exposure may be insufficient to reduce perceived barriers at early stages". Their curricular implication is that early community-based experiences "should not be assumed to be self-sufficient, but rather scaffolded through longitudinal, progressively structured activities with adequate supervision and reflection". Empathy and norms gains, by contrast, did not vary by academic level, supporting early inclusion of community-based activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal, progressively structured activities
- Adequate supervision and reflection
#### Constraints
- Brief, isolated exposure at early stages may make the personal demands of community work more salient rather than reducing perceived barriers

### Target Learners
- junior undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing perceived barriers to community engagement
- cultivating empathy and prosocial norms
