Certify tutors before tutoring begins and provide professional development to supervising school staff¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article states that "BookNook Tutors complete a certification prior to tutoring start date" and that "BookNook also provides on-site or virtual professional development for school staff." This preparation strategy pairs tutor certification with training for the on-site staff who supervise sessions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Time for tutors to complete certification before the start date
  • Delivery of on-site or virtual professional development to school staff

Constraints¶

  • The page does not report effects of the certification or professional development separately from the program as a whole

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students receiving tutoring (indirect beneficiaries)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective delivery of reading tutoring sessions

Related Strategies¶

  • Deliver high-dosage tutoring as recurring 30-minute sessions two to three times per week over 12 weeks
  • Provide tutors 20 hours of pre-service training plus ongoing weekly coaching and professional development
  • Operate high-dosage virtual tutoring with program-managed tutors and monthly literacy-specialist data meetings so school staff need no additional professional development
  • CEO Forum recommendations: prepare and certify teachers for technology integration across pre-service, licensure, and in-service career stages
  • Transportable tutoring protocol with structured tutor training and supervision
