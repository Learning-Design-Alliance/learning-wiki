Use the framework as a global reference to guide national AI competency frameworks, teacher training, and assessment parameters¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The publication positions itself as "a global reference" that "guides the development of national AI competency frameworks, informs teacher training programmes, and helps in designing assessment parameters." It also "provides strategies for teachers to build AI knowledge, apply ethical principles, and support their professional growth." Policymakers and teacher educators can adopt it for these three design uses.
