# Integrate counseling, stress-management, resilience, and emotional-intelligence development supports into teacher preparation programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Universities or teacher preparation institutions should offer a comprehensive support mechanism consisting of professional counseling services," including stress management workshops and resilience training, together with EI development activities "such as emotional awareness training and communication skill‐building." These supports are grounded in the finding that EI significantly moderated and strengthened the PWB–CGPA relationship, buffering academic stress for trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must provide professional counseling services and structured EI development activities accessible to pre-service teachers
#### Constraints
- The article notes EI's moderating role was not significant for the PWB–ASE relationship and frames EI support as playing "a supportive role in handling stress" rather than directly driving achievement

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers in a four-year bachelor of education programme

### Target Learning Goals
- emotional intelligence and stress coping
- psychological well-being
- academic achievement (CGPA)
