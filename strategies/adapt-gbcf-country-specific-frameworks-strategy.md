# Adapt the GbCF v1 to country context to design skill development and learning activities for early career pharmacy practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (4 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using the validated core competency set as a starting point that "can be further adapted to country context and used to design skill development and learning activities for pre- and in-service early career pharmacy practitioners". They argue none of the surveyed countries has a validated competency framework for early career practice, and that adapting the GbCF v1 is feasible particularly in the four countries with high response numbers, to facilitate performance improvement and identify learning needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further qualitative exploration of expert opinion and stakeholder input from policy-makers and pharmacy leaders
- A larger scale validation study including non-patient-facing practitioners
#### Constraints
- Findings are from an exploratory, self-selected non-probabilistic sample, limiting generalisability

### Target Learners
- Pre- and in-service early career pharmacy practitioners in African countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Professional competency development, identification of knowledge gaps and learning needs, performance improvement
