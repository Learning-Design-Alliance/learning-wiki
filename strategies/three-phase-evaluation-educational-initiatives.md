# Evaluate educational initiatives with front-end, formative and summative assessments, including pre/post testing and long-term follow-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes a three-phase evaluation recipe for educational initiatives such as teacher training and curricular materials: "front-end evaluation (done before the program, often to know the expectation of participants), formative evaluation (done during the program), and summa-tive evaluation (done at the end)". Assessments can test awareness, knowledge, interest, attitude change, and skill gains, using questionnaires, interviews, or a mix. Curricular materials can be tested by comparing pre and post-test results using statistical methods, and follow-up assessments gauge long-term retention. Guidelines should be prepared and each program assessed periodically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each initiative should be assessed after implementation through questionnaires, interviews, or a mix of them
- Follow-up assessment is needed where long-term retention of knowledge is of interest
