# Flexible Seating Patterns and Configurations

## Description
Providing a variety of different furniture that allows students to work in different areas, depending on the project or time of day. This adapts learning space for varied learning needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of seating options such as tables and chairs, couches, and bean bag chairs. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the changing seating arrangements.

### Target Learning Goals

- Increase collaboration, reduce stress, promote flexible thinking

### Affordances
- Promotes collaboration and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred seating arrangement. Provide a variety of seating options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more comfortable.

- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that flexible seating can increase engagement, reduce stress, and improve academic performance. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
