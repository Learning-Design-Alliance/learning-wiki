# Districts considering school closures should provide substantial planning time, transition support for merging communities, and integration supports

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the study's mixed-methods findings, the report's interpretive considerations point districts toward adequate planning and community-merging support. Staff said the "planning process for merging closed schools into welcoming schools was not sufficient", the moving process was described as chaotic, and staff "wished for more support in merging closed and welcoming school communities". Investments in Safe Passage, technology, and resources were appreciated by students and staff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient planning time before students arrive in welcoming schools
- Support for merging closed and welcoming school communities
#### Constraints
- The study did not address financial questions about the closings

### Target Learners
- Students in closed and welcoming schools
- Staff in closed and welcoming schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuity of student outcomes (attendance, mobility, GPA, test scores) during school transitions
