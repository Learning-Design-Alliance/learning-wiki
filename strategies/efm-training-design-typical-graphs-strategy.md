# Design fetal monitoring training around typical graph analysis with practical manipulation and a multi-function mini program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the questionnaire results, the authors recommend that fetal monitoring training focus on practical manipulation skills combined with analysis of typical graphs, and that a training mini program offer varied functions. Respondents gave high expectations to interpretation of fetal monitoring graphs (86.36%) and a question bank of case analysis (86.36%), and 87.88% considered typical graph analysis the content to be taught emphatically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training content should include typical graph analysis and exercises
- A small program for training should support multiple functions such as guideline interpretation, lectures, theoretical assessment, graph interpretation, and case question banks
#### Constraints
- The article advises strengthening basic knowledge training because 4 post-training questions scored below 50%
- Cutting-edge information such as the fetal reserve index might be difficult for study participants to understand

### Target Learners
- Midwives
- Obstetric care professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Interpretation and analysis of fetal monitoring graphs
- Practical manipulation skills in electronic fetal monitoring
