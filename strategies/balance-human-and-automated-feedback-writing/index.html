Balance human and automated feedback to optimize writing skill development¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's forward-looking pedagogical recommendation is to integrate AI-powered feedback while balancing it with human feedback, so that immediate, customized automated feedback is combined with teacher input to optimize writing skill. The article "highlights the pedagogical implications of Integrating AI-powered feedback" and "suggests directions for future research, particularly in balancing human and automated feedback to optimize writing skill."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Integration of AI-powered feedback alongside human feedback rather than as a replacement

Constraints¶

  • The review frames this as a direction for future research, not as a tested optimum

Target Learners¶

  • L2/EFL writing learners in the reviewed 2019–2024 studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Writing precision
  • fluency
  • grammatical proficiency
  • overall writing quality

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate AI-driven scaffolding with teacher-led offloading and refine feedback granularity to prevent over-scaffolding
  • Design AI educational systems that fade support and require learner justification to preserve independent problem solving
  • Discourage mental offloading to AI by balancing guidance depth and frequency with application opportunities
  • Scaffolded AI integration with teacher training and reflective tasks to reduce over-reliance
