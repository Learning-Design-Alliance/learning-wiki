Role-play activity training with doctor, standardized-patient and evaluator roles plus peer and teacher debriefing before real-patient interviews¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article implements a role-play activity in which "3-4 students formed a group", with one student acting as a doctor, one as a standardized patient according to a scenario, and the other 1-2 students serving as evaluators after teachers introduced the activities. Debriefing was given verbally by peer classmates and teachers, and teachers summarized history-taking methods and key points at the end of class. Six standardized clinical cases were used as scenarios, trained one hour per week over four weeks before students interviewed real patients.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Six typical and significant clinical cases standardized in accordance with the objectives and requirements of the teaching syllabus
  • Teachers introduce the role-play activities and summarize key points at the end of class

Constraints¶

  • Debriefing was given verbally only, mainly depending on direct observation; the authors note some researchers indicate feedback based on audiotaped and/or videotaped reviews is superior to verbal feedback alone

Target Learners¶

  • third-year medical students in a clinical medicine major

Target Learning Goals¶

  • history-taking skills
  • doctor-patient communication
  • medical record writing

Claims¶

  • Students trained with role-play activity score higher than control students on a practical history-taking test with a teacher standardized patient [+W]
  • Role-play-trained medical students score significantly higher than control students on medical case records from the third real-patient interview onward, but not in the first two interviews [+W]
  • Most role-play-group students report the activity improved their history-taking skills and confidence communicating with real patients [+W]
