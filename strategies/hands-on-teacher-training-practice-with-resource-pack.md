# Train teachers through hands-on practice of the tutoring procedure with a supporting resource pack

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Paired Reading delivery team trained teachers over two sessions totalling one and a half days, in which teachers were put in pairs and "practiced the programme as if they were pupils receiving the intervention". Teachers appreciated this hands-on format because it let them experience the approach before delivering it, and the support pack of slides, question cards, question mats and log books complemented the training. The strategy also included a follow-up half-day session four weeks into the programme to consolidate practice and enhance communication skills between tutors and tutees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A support pack containing presentation slides and programme materials
- Access to online resources including training films and PowerPoints for sessions 1-16
#### Constraints
- Some teachers suggested the training could have been reduced to a day or half a day due to the time it took
- Some teachers experienced difficulties with electronic materials and downloading resources from the dedicated website

### Target Learners
- secondary school teachers delivering peer tutoring programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- fidelity of programme delivery
- teacher understanding of tutoring procedures
