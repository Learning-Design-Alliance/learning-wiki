# Scale training sustainably through geographic spread, online delivery, community growth and a supported core team

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For long-term sustainability, the article recommends spreading training events and expertise geographically to keep costs low, moving to an online training model to reduce the financial burdens of in-person training, and structuring training so a minimal set of people is needed for maintenance and per-event costs are minimized. It further recommends growing the community, providing recognition and possible financial incentives, building a core team focused on the mission, and scaling mentorship and leadership at local and regional levels, including engaging more labs and institutes and promoting equity, diversity, inclusion and accessibility.
