# Evaluate multifaceted training packages combining manikins, computer simulation, expert patients and clinical supervision for pelvic examination competency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that achieving competency in gynaecological pelvic examination likely requires a multifaceted approach: "This may use low-fidelity simulation with manikins, computer simulations, expert patients and supervision in the clinical setting." They further state that "Packages of training should be evaluated for cost-effectiveness", noting that technological advances may allow incorporation of high-fidelity simulation manikins offering greater realism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluation of combined training packages for cost-effectiveness, not just single components
#### Constraints
- The article did not itself evaluate such packages; it recommends future evaluation and replication in other educational settings

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students on obstetrics and gynaecology placements

### Target Learning Goals
- competency in gynaecological pelvic examination
