# Include explicit content on under-recognised risk factors such as hormone replacement therapy and smoking in future workshops.

## Description
Based on the finding that awareness of hormonal therapy and smoking did not improve significantly, the article recommends that "Future workshops should therefore incorporate explicit, culturally tailored content on these under-recognised yet clinically important risk factors." This addresses the limited improvement likely reflecting restricted content coverage, as these topics were only briefly discussed.
