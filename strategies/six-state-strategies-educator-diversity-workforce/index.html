Six state strategies for increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce, from data visibility through COVID-relief investment¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief's policy scan organizes state action around six strategies: making educator diversity data visible and actionable, setting clear state and district goals, investing in educator preparation programs, targeting resources to districts and schools for recruiting and hiring a diverse workforce, investing in retention through improved working conditions and growth opportunities, and using federal COVID-related relief funds. Each strategy is rated against printed criteria on a three-level scale from "Meets all or most of the requirements within the goal" down to "Meets one or no requirements within the goal."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • State education agencies collect and share useful and timely educator workforce data that is publicly available and easily accessible so stakeholders can make targeted, strategic workforce decisions

Constraints¶

  • Recruitment efforts are compromised when districts and schools have difficulty retaining teachers of color, per the brief

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 public and charter school students in Pennsylvania

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable student access to a racially diverse, high-quality educator workforce

Related Strategies¶

  • Make school-level educator diversity data publicly available and easy to interpret so stakeholders can act on it
