# Train chemistry teachers to use computer simulations through seminars, workshops and conferences organised by professional bodies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that government utilise bodies such as the Science Teachers Association of Nigeria, Nigeria Union of Teachers and Chemical Society of Nigeria to "organise seminars, workshop and conferences to inform and train chemistry teachers and other science teachers on the use of computer simulation in teaching and learning". In the study, two research assistants were trained to carry out the teaching using computer simulations.
