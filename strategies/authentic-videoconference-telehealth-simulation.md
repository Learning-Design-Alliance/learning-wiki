# Simulate telehealth encounters over an authentic videoconference platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy directs simulation designers to deliver telehealth scenarios over a real videoconference platform rather than simulated face-to-face, because authenticity of the modality shapes rapport-building practice. The article reports that in this study the telehealth scenario was done face-to-face, and that educators and graduates believed doing it over Zoom "would create a more authentic learning experience". It cites supporting evidence that participants in targeted telehealth education over video platforms reported improved rapport with clients.
