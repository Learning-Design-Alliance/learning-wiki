# Deliver problem solving training as six two-hour weekly sessions using the Dezorilla and Goldfried model with varied active methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable training recipe: a course "based on Dezorilla and Goldfried model" whose "six stages ... were held in six two-hour sessions within six weeks." Each session's trainings were "offered separately using lecture method, group discussion, question and answer, brain-storming, scenario, and role play based on predetermined goals." Students learned problem types, finding possible solutions, applying solutions, and decision-making, and role-played problems practicing the model's stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Six two-hour sessions within six weeks, following the model's six stages
- Predetermined goals for each session
#### Constraints
- Participants in the trial were nursing students with self-handicapping grades higher than 50

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- problem solving skills
- decision-making
- effective confrontation with difficult situations
