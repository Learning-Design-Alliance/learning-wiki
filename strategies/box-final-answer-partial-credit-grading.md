## Description
A grading recipe for the quantitative section: "The student presents their steps, then box their final answer in the answer space provided." The instructor checks the boxed answer and, if it is wrong, reviews the steps, because "Partial credits are awarded accordingly based on the gravity of the mistake" — distinguishing lack of understanding of fundamental principles from an algebraic problem.
