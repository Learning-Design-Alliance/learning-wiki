# Two-day teacher professional learning workshop before simulation-supported inquiry implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
Before the intervention, the two participating teachers attended a two-day workshop covering PhET simulation introduction and practice, conceptual change and students' alternative conceptions, the Force Concept Inventory, the inquiry skills survey, inquiry-based instruction, guidelines for the conceptual change approach, lesson-plan and worksheet details, and the research schedule. This prepared teachers to enact the ISIA faithfully, which classroom observations then verified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must know how to conduct inquiry-based instruction; the article identifies lack of knowledge and experience with inquiry-based instruction as a barrier to teaching science this way
#### Constraints
- The study involved only two teachers in Beijing; the authors call for further research with larger student groups and in other areas of China and international contexts

### Target Learners
- secondary physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- enacting inquiry-based instruction with interactive simulations
- implementing the ISIA five-step sequence
