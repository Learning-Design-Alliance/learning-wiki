# Targeted working memory training for students with weaker English learning abilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that working memory updating and span had the strongest bridging influence on English academic performance, the authors recommend that "Targeted working memory training may benefit students with weaker Englishlearningabilities,effectivelyimprovingtheirlanguagecapabilities". The strategy directs intervention toward the executive function components the network identified as most influential, rather than treating executive function as a unitary predictor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of students with weaker English learning abilities; training targeting working memory updating and span specifically
#### Constraints
- The study is cross-sectional; the authors state causality remains unestablished and future intervention studies are needed, so the training benefit is a suggested direction, not a demonstrated effect

### Target Learners
- Chinese primary school students (grades 3–5) with weaker English learning abilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving English language capabilities across listening, reading, and writing
