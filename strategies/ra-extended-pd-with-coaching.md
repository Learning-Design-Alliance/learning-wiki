# Sustained, coached professional development over up to two years for RA implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The RA models require long-duration professional development rather than one-off training: in whole-grade and whole-school variations, "Teachers receive ten day-long professional development sessions over a period of up to two years." RAAL adds a 5-day pre-course institute, a 2-day fall institute, on-site coaching 4 times per year and online coaching 8 times per year, with annual tune-up meetings and options for second-year development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staffing and release time, e.g. RAISE summer release time of 5 days the first summer, 2 days winter, and 3 days the second summer; at least one teacher for a year-long dedicated RAAL class
#### Constraints
- iRAISE differs from RA only in that professional development is provided online; the review does not report differential outcomes by PD format

### Target Learners
- secondary teachers implementing RA in science, social studies, and dedicated literacy courses

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher capacity to model reasoning and teach metacognitive reading strategies within content areas
