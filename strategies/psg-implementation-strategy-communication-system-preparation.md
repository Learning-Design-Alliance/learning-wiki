# Implement shared goal setting by combining a structured patient-specific method with client-activating communication, system integration, and patient preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications for practice outline how shared goal setting can be operationalized: 'Physiotherapists can already use some elements of the PSG by informing patients, stimulating them to participate and focus on having a dialogue.' It further recommends methods prior to the first consultation that prepare patients for participation, integration of the PSG into Dutch electronic physiotherapy patient systems, and integration of its essential elements into Bachelor's and post-graduate physiotherapy education addressing communication skills and a client-centred attitude.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of the PSG into electronic patient systems
- Physiotherapist training in client-activating communication skills
#### Constraints
- The article notes extended follow-up and in-service coaching may be needed because the limited follow-up period may not have allowed sufficient improvement

### Target Learners
- Practising physiotherapists
- Physiotherapy students in Bachelor's and post-graduate programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- Adopting a client-centred attitude
- Shared goal setting and patient participation
