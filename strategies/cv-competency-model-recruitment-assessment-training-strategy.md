## Description
The article recommends its model as a practical tool for workforce decisions: it is "designed to provide effective guidance for the recruitment, evaluation, and residency training of cardiovascular physicians in China". The weighted two-level indicators and the five-point familiarity scale support assessing physicians against context-specific competencies aligned with the "Healthy China 2030" plan.
