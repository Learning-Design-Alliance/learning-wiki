# Use difficulty-scored manikins to sequence airway management training from standard to advanced modules

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the scoring system as an objective yardstick to select and sequence simulators: standard models for early practice and high-scoring models such as Model C for advanced difficult-airway training. The authors state the formula could serve "as a guiding benchmark for the innovation and refinement of novel training manikins" and support differentiating higher-order modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Manikins scored with the difficult intubation formula before curriculum assignment
- Structured briefing on standard intubation procedure before practice
#### Constraints
- Validated only in a pilot study with 19 physicians at a single tertiary care centre

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine residents
- post-graduate year doctors

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural skill in endotracheal intubation
- management of difficult airways
