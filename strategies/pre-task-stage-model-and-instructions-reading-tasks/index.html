Strategies

Use a pre-task stage with vocabulary review, clear instructions, a model task, and useful-language recall before reading tasks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In the experimental sessions, before learners performed each reading task the teacher reviewed the previous session's new words, introduced the topic, gave clear instructions on what to do, helped students recall language useful for the task, and often performed a similar task to give students a clear model of what was expected. Learners then completed the task individually or in pairs while the researcher monitored and offered encouragement, and afterwards prepared an oral or written report.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A pre-task stage including vocabulary review and a model of a similar task
  • Learners produce a short oral or written report after the task, practised before class delivery

Constraints¶

  • Described as implemented in a 20-session reading course for intermediate EFL learners

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate Iranian EFL learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • L2 reading comprehension
  • communicative language use

Claims¶

  • Task-based reading instruction produces significantly higher reading post-test scores than traditional reading instruction for intermediate Iranian EFL learners [+W]
