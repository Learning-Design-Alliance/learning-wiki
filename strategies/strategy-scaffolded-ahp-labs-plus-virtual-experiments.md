# Adopt a scaffolded series of At Home Physics labs and incorporate virtual experiments into the class portion of the course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on this study, the authors plan fall labs as "a series of At Home Physics experiments", "scaﬀolded to slowly introduce the various aspects of designing and executing an experiment", and, given virtual experiments' efficacy at teaching concepts, to "incorporate virtual experiments into the class portion of our course". A full AHP semester will also allow direct comparison with in-person labs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- scaffolding that gradually introduces the various aspects of designing and executing an experiment
- an assessment plan similar to this study's, plus an independent critical-thinking inventory
#### Constraints
- the efficacy of a full semester of At Home Physics labs is not yet assessed; the article states a dedicated semester "will provide us a better environment to assess their eﬃcacy"

### Target Learners
- introductory algebra-based physics students (non-majors)

### Target Learning Goals
- designing and executing experiments
- critical thinking about experiments
- data analysis skills
