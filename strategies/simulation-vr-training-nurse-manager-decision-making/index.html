Use simulation and virtual reality to train nurse managers' decision-making competency¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review recommends designing targeted training programs using simulation and virtual reality to strengthen decision-making competency. The article states that "Simulation provides a safe space for nurse managers to engage with high-stakes' scenarios, apply critical thinking, and receive structured feedback." The 15 identified characteristics can inform context-specific simulation challenges reflecting the multifaceted demands of nursing leadership.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Simulation scenarios should reflect the multifaceted demands of nursing leadership using the 15 identified characteristics

Constraints¶

  • The literature cautions against overreliance on automation, as it may undermine reflective and context-sensitive elements of decision-making

Target Learners¶

  • Nurse managers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Decision-making competency
  • Critical thinking

Claims¶

  • Simulation type–outcome alignment: high-fidelity formats favour judgment and decision-making, VR and games favour empathy and reflection [+W]
