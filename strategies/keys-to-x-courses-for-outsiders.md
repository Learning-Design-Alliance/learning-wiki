# Offer 'Keys to X' courses targeting outsiders to a discipline to build confidence and autonomous learning

## Description
As a future direction, the authors propose new 'Keys to X' courses with a different objective from introductory courses for those inside discipline X: they "instead target those outside the discipline concerned, with the principal objective of building confidence and autonomous learning in X". This responds to their finding that confidence, not just resource access, limits autonomous research.
