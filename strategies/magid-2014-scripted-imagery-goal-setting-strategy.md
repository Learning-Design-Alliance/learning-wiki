# Use scripted imagery of the Ideal L2 self combined with goal-setting and action plans to motivate language learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a program design in which learners listen to scripted imagery situations describing themselves using the target language successfully, then write goals and action plans with steps, start dates, and progress reviews. The author states that "the enhancement of language learners' vision of their Ideal L2 self through the use of imagery is an effective motivational strategy that may be employed by language teachers, writers of language textbooks, and language learners themselves."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Imagination training activities to prepare learners to visualise future scenarios
- Scripted imagery situations read to relaxed learners with eyes closed
#### Constraints
- The program was implemented with grade-five learners in Singapore; the author notes participants who wrote their own imagery situations in a previous study found them more motivating

### Target Learners
- Elementary school language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing motivation and time and effort devoted to language learning
