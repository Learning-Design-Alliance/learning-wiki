# Adapt quantum activity time allocations and materials by grade level, and provide extra resources for harder questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From teacher feedback, the article recommends adjusting activity timing and materials for different age groups: "This suggests that time allocations should be adjusted when planning activities for different age groups," because younger students need more structured instructions and support. Teachers also suggested grade-level differentiation of presentation slides and providing extra resources so students can look up their own questions about complex topics such as quantum computing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of which grade band (pre-high school vs high school) an activity targets
- Supplementary resource lists for topics teachers may not be able to answer confidently
#### Constraints
- The article's evidence for these suggestions is open-ended teacher feedback from a self-selected sample, not a test of the adaptations

### Target Learners
- Pre-high school students (grades 4-8)
- High school students (grades 9-12)

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational quantum concepts and sustained student interest
