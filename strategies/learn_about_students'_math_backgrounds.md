# Learn About Students' Math Backgrounds

## Description
Understanding learners' math backgrounds is important. Some may have strong skills from consistent schooling, while others may have limited formal education but practical skills. Connect instruction to their interests and responsibilities, such as sports or family finances. Be aware of different numeric and measurement systems learners may use. Look for leadership opportunities for all learners in math class, allowing them to teach others and demonstrate what they know in multiple ways. Group learners intentionally and with sensitivity. Veteran educators stress the importance of getting to know learners as a key part of supporting their success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assess learner's prior math instruction and skills, integrate learner interests into instruction
#### Constraints
- Requires time to gather information about each learner's background, educators must be sensitive to learners' cultural and educational differences
