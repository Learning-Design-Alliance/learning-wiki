Strategies

Evaluate the frequency of EBP intervention delivery, considering continuous or repeated interventions to sustain student comfort with EBP¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review identifies the frequency of intervention delivery as "an important aspect that should be considered in future research", noting that "continuous or repeated interventions may help more so that not only do students become more comfortable with EBP, but also they remain comfortable for a longer period of time". This strategy directs researchers to manipulate and measure delivery frequency, not just total duration.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Designs that vary or measure frequency of intervention delivery over time
  • Follow-up assessments beyond immediate post-test to capture sustained comfort with EBP

Constraints¶

  • The review found intervention duration was not related to EBP competence, so frequency rather than duration is the proposed lever

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate health students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained EBP competence and comfort with EBP processes over time

Claims¶

  • A 13-week multifaceted course for therapy and physiotherapy students produced statistically significant mean increases in perceived knowledge (14.15) and actual knowledge (3.56) [+W]
  • A multifaceted EBM course for medical students produced a mean pre-post Fresno test difference of 92.8 points (P<0.001) [+W]
  • An EBP curriculum culture intervention at a dental college increased students' journal reading, research-report evaluation confidence, and evidence-use experience [+W]
  • Exposure to a 14-session EBM intervention was associated with higher confidence in critical appraisal skills among medical students (M5 EBM 17.1 vs. M5 non-EBM 8.4, P<0.001) [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Future ISS research and evaluation should include detailed demographic and contextual data, develop valid data collection tools, and identify optimal frequency and length of simulations
