## Description
The article recommends structured professional development, hands-on training in standardized assessment tools, and integration of evidence-based fall-prevention frameworks such as STEADI into routine physiotherapy practice. It states findings "highlight the importance of structured professional development, hands-on training in standardized assessment tools, and integration of evidence-based fall-prevention frameworks into routine physiotherapy practice." System-level supports include workflow integration, quality metrics tracking tool usage, and objective chart audits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuing professional education and workplace-based mentorship opportunities
- Access to validated fall-risk tools such as STEADI within academic curricula and post-licensure training
#### Constraints
- The article notes self-reported tool usage may be inflated by social desirability bias, recommending objective chart audits to verify actual use

### Target Learners
- Licensed physical therapists working with older adults

### Target Learning Goals
- Apply evidence-based fall-prevention strategies and standardized fall-risk assessment tools in clinical practice with older adults
