# Provide researchers with education and training on study design classification and reporting guideline selection, and implement guideline policies matched to guideline availability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends educating and training researchers in both study design and reporting guidelines, and implementing journal reporting guideline policies that reflect the current development status of guidelines. This responds to its findings that many declared designs and guidelines needed reclassification and that some designs lack any corresponding guideline. As printed: "Education and training on study design and reporting guidelines for researchers are needed."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Journals should implement reporting guideline policies that reflect the actual development status of reporting guidelines for each study design
#### Constraints
- The editor notes "there are no suitable reporting guidelines for descriptive studies" and will continue using the STROBE statement for these studies while awaiting expert-developed guidelines

### Target Learners
- Health professions education researchers
- Authors submitting to health professions education journals

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurately classifying study designs
- Selecting and applying appropriate reporting guidelines
