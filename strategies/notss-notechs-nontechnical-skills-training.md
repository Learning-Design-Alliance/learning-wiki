# Use NOTSS for individual surgeon non-technical skills training and Oxford NOTECHS II for team-based training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy addresses development of non-technical skills — teamwork, leadership, situational awareness and decision-making — which the review says can be developed easily in a simulated environment and have a significant impact on surgical success. A recent systematic review by Wood et al. "concluded that NOTSS (Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons) was the gold standard training tool for the individual surgeon training whereas the Oxford NOTECHS (Nontechnical Skills Training Tools for the Surgical Team) II was the most favorable for team-based training".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulated environment in which individual and team reactions to system errors can be simulated, repeated and assessed
#### Constraints
- The review notes the robot's presence poses a unique challenge to team communication and risk management in the operating room

### Target Learners
- individual surgeons in training
- surgical teams

### Target Learning Goals
- teamwork
- leadership
- situational awareness
- decision-making
