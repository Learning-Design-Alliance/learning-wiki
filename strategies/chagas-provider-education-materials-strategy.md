# Create and disseminate educational material on Chagas-related heart disease, especially in higher-prevalence areas, targeting recognition, testing and early treatment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' recommended next step is provider education: "Future steps for our study would be to create and disseminate educational material especially in areas of higher prevalence to help ensure quicker diagnosis and referral to appropriate care for patients." The strategy addresses the knowledge gaps, low testing rates and discomfort documented in their survey of 119 U.S. pediatric cardiologists. It is directed at clinicians rather than students, aiming to increase diagnostic consideration and earlier treatment of Chagas disease in children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational material should target recognition, testing and early treatment of Chagas disease in pediatric patients
#### Constraints
- Material is most needed in areas of higher Chagas prevalence; the survey evidence base has a low response rate of about 10%

### Target Learners
- Pediatric cardiologists in the United States

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognize cardiac presentations of Chagas disease in children
- Include Chagas disease in the differential diagnosis and order appropriate testing
- Refer diagnosed patients to appropriate care earlier
