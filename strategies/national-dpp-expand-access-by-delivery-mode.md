# Expand access to National DPP delivery modes for underserved groups: make virtual modes accessible to older and minority participants and expand in-person programs in rural counties and urban centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend matching delivery-mode strengths to access barriers. They state "Making virtual programs more accessible and attractive to older participants may help those who face transportation challenges," that modes without in-person components "need to be made more accessible and appealing to minority racial and ethnic groups," and that expansion to underserved rural counties and urban centers must be a future priority.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery of the CDC-approved lifestyle change program through CDC-recognized organizations submitting biannual DPRP data
#### Constraints
- Most online and distance-learning enrollment has been through organizations without publicly available offerings, available only through employers or insurance plans

### Target Learners
- Adults 18+ at high risk of developing type 2 diabetes

### Target Learning Goals
- Lifestyle change to prevent or delay type 2 diabetes
