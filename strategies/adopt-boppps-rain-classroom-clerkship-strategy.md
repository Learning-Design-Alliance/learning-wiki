# Adopt BOPPPS integrated with Rain Classroom as a blended approach for clinical clerkship teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors conclude that the BOPPPS model integrated with Rain Classroom "may be a valuable pedagogical strategy worthy of further investigation" for clinical teaching. The implementable recipe is the six-stage BOPPPS session structure delivered through Rain Classroom's interactive features, replacing passive lecture formats in rotation-based medical education. The authors recommend future work exploring long-term effects, instructor perceptions, replication across cohorts, and applicability to other medical disciplines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor training in the BOPPPS stages and access to the Rain Classroom platform; standardized assessment aligned to shared learning objectives
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center study with a modest sample, non-random allocation, no blinding, and unvalidated satisfaction surveys; generalizability to other institutions and disciplines warrants further validation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge acquisition
- classroom engagement
- learner satisfaction
