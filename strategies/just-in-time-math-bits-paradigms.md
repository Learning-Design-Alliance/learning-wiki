# Just-in-time Math Bits integrated into each Paradigm course in place of a standalone Math Methods course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The revision eliminates the Math Methods course in favor of Math Bits: a single week of just-in-time math content incorporated in each Paradigm course. The article reports that earlier math-intensive weeks preceding Paradigm courses were found effective and popular with students, while the standalone course was unwieldy and hard to place helpfully in the curriculum. The Math Bits for the entire year is taught by a single professor, providing continuity and coherence and preventing instructors from short-changing math content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single professor teaches the Math Bits for the entire year to provide continuity and coherence
#### Constraints
- Advanced students may need the graduate-level Math Methods course for content no longer required undergraduates

### Target Learners
- upper-division physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- just-in-time mathematical preparation for physics courses
