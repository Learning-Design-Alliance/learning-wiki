Future directions: fine-tuning on human-refined frameworks and hybrid human–LLM or multi-agent annotation systems¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend two directions to improve scalability and annotation support: first, examine whether LLMs can be fine-tuned using the human-refined framework and annotated data to assess whether models acquire implicit knowledge not fully captured by explicit criteria; second, explore "hybrid systems in which human coders and LLM agents collaborate, and multi-agent systems in which multiple agents co-ordinate without human involvement". Both may refine frameworks, identify challenging cases, and support quality control while reducing human annotator workload.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Human-refined framework and annotated data for fine-tuning; coordination mechanisms for multi-agent systems

Constraints¶

  • A key open question is whether LLMs can make reliable autonomous decisions and align with human judgments

Target Learners¶

  • researchers annotating collaborative learning discourse

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reliable, scalable annotation of cognitive engagement
