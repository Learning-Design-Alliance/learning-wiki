# Online and hybrid communities of physics educators supporting EBAE adoption

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends building online communities of physics educators across institutions to sustain instructors whose EBAE implementations disappoint. It specifies a concrete structure: "this mutual support community of physics educators can communicate via an asynchronous platform such as Discord, a monthly  synchronous meeting of physics instructors with similar interests  on a platform like Zoom", for example instructors teaching quantum mechanics, to encourage persistence and comfort with multiple EBAE pedagogies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An asynchronous communication platform plus a recurring synchronous meeting of instructors with similar teaching interests
#### Constraints
- Proposed because local support may not be available to physics educators at all institutions

### Target Learners
- physics instructors across colleges and universities, e.g., those teaching quantum mechanics

### Target Learning Goals
- helping instructors persist with and refine evidence-based pedagogies so their students develop a functional understanding of physics
