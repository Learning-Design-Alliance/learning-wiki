# Extend classroom observations of nonsense-syllable retention to obtain statistical evidence on long-term retention and recall

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The author recommends that instructors in educational psychology extend observations of the kind reported here across more students, in order to obtain statistical evidence about the possibilities and nature of long-term retention and recall of nonsense syllables. The strategy treats the classroom demonstration as a seed for larger, aggregable data collection rather than a one-off anecdote.
