Video-clip techniques: silent viewing, pause-based prediction, and rewind¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For video clips of recorded songs, the article prescribes three techniques: "use of video without sound" with tasks of relating video to song text or predicting content; "use of pauses, predicting subsequent text or display a description of the plot/character sequence"; and "use quick/rewind". Videotext is valued because it combines aspects of verbal interaction, lets the visual line strengthen factual and linguistic features in context, and supports comparison of cultural realities and behaviour in intercultural communication.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Video use should compensate for the lack of a foreign-language environment, which the article says video helps to fulfill

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • university students learning English

Target Learning Goals¶

  • listening comprehension, prediction, intercultural comparison, aesthetic education

Related Strategies¶

  • Two-stage song-work technique: pre-listening word work, then work during and after listening
  • Videos
  • Combine video clips with Small Group Activity techniques, using video-support techniques such as slowing playback, pre- and post-viewing comprehension sessions, and repeating or pausing scenes
  • Combine video viewing with post-viewing activities to test comprehension and encourage productive use of technical vocabulary
