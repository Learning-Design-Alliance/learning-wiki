# Use a hybrid operating strategy with masks from the start rather than relying on reactive temporary closures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A decision strategy for school leaders reopening during a pandemic: adopt hybrid operation with smaller masked groups attending part-time from the beginning, rather than opening fully in person and closing temporarily when cases appear. The models suggest this both reduces infections and avoids the added schedule disruption that reactive closures cause.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Capacity to operate hybrid schedules with smaller masked student groups attending in person part-time
#### Constraints
- Applies to schools weighing reopening strategies during a pandemic; based on model predictions rather than measured outcomes

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Maximizing in-person attendance days while limiting infection spread
