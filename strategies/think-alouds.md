# Think-Alouds

## Description
Model learning by reading aloud an article on a current event or topic of study, pausing to ask questions and make comments and connections to prior knowledge. Have students do the same with a partner, then in groups of three or four.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to select articles that are relevant and engaging. Facilitation to ensure all students have an opportunity to participate and contribute.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who struggle with reading or who prefer more individual work. Requires careful planning to ensure the activity stays focused and productive.

### Target Learning Goals

- Model effective reading strategies, promote critical thinking, build prior knowledge, and develop collaborative learning skills.

### Affordances
- Provides a model for effective reading and learning strategies. Encourages active engagement with the material. Builds prior knowledge and makes connections to real-world events.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying the complexity of the articles used. Students can be allowed to choose articles that are of interest to them. Group sizes can be adjusted to suit different learning preferences.
