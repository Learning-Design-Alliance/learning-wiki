# Institutional adoption strategy for ABCD assessments: infrastructure, policy, faculty development, audits, and hybrid human-AI grading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's recommendation section lays out an implementable adoption recipe for higher education institutions. It states that "higher education institutions must invest in quality digital infrastructure as well as clearly defined policies on ethics, security, and data privacy", paired with faculty training programs using pilot projects and faculty-student teams, and student awareness programs. It further recommends "ongoing audits of the AI -based systems of grading for avoiding prejudice" and hybrid assessment models combining human checking with AI-based grading to safeguard fairness and reliability.
