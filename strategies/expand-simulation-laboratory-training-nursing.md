# Expand simulation-based and laboratory training before and alongside clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends increasing laboratory practice hours and expanding simulation-based training opportunities to address psychomotor skill deficiencies and reduce fear of making mistakes. Students recommended 'increasing laboratory hours' (8 participants), and the article states insufficient laboratory experience before practice is at the root of psychomotor skill deficiencies. Cited studies indicate simulation environments may reduce fear and increase confidence in decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation laboratories and sufficient laboratory hours before clinical placement
#### Constraints
- Some students reported never having the opportunity to use the simulation lab

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Psychomotor clinical skills
- Confidence and fear management in practice
