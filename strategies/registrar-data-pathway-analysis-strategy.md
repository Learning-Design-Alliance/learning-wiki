# Analyze institutional registrar data to contextualize student retention and recruitment in the major

## Description
The article recommends working "to understand what might be learned using data from institutions themselves": departments can collect and analyze their own timestamped registration data to unpack pathways into and out of the major and provide context for retention and recruitment work. The authors position these methods as complements to, not replacements for, rich qualitative studies of student experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Requires institutional registrar data with timestamped course and degree major choices, grades, and demographics
#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
