# Use survey-based validation to test the appropriateness and importance of the HST competency domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a follow-up survey study to validate the competency framework beyond their interview and job description samples. They write: "We suggest that a useful next step would be to take a survey-based approach to obtain a broader feedback on the appropriateness and importance of the competency domains identified in our study." This would also allow consideration of how much diversity exists between the competencies required by HSTs at different centers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A broader respondent sample than the original 30 interviewees to obtain feedback on the nine competency domains
#### Constraints
- The current framework rests on convenience and snowball sampling that may have biased findings, with an over-representation of simulationists based in Ireland

### Target Learners
- Health care simulation technicians

### Target Learning Goals
- Validation and refinement of the HST competency framework for training needs analysis
