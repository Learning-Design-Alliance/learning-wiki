# Four-step process: identify behaviors, explore context, integrate support, surface just-in-time recommendations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article outlines a four-step process to address learner needs at scale: "1) identify learner behavioral patterns; 2) identify learner contexts when exhibiting the behaviors; 3) integrate a system of holistic learning support; 4) combine behavioral data and ongoing context data to surface just-in-time support tool recommendations for a learner." Step 1 uses platform telemetry augmented by generative AI; Step 2 introduces frequent in-platform checkpoints with psychometrically-validated GEM-aligned questions; Step 3 combines technical tools with trauma-informed faculty mindset reframing; Step 4 merges behavioral and checkpoint data to recommend tailored supports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning platform telemetry and instrumentation
- Frequent in-platform checkpoints from orientation through the program
- Learner opt-in to data tracking
#### Constraints
- Human quality checks of AI recommendations and learner data security must be carefully considered and defined in implementation
- The format of the frequent checkpoints must not distract or impose on learners

### Target Learners
- online learners exhibiting neurodivergent traits, diagnosed or undisclosed

### Target Learning Goals
- timely accommodations and increased momentum toward academic goals

### Affordances
- Gem Guiding Empowerment Model
