# Optimize screen-reading environments with enriched presentations and physical adjustments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For contexts where screen reading is used, the authors recommend arranging physical factors and enriching content. They suggest that "Screen readers should arrange surrounding environment’s physical factors like light and colour in a way which would affect the reading process positively" and that "enriched presentations with the use of animation and figures instead of plain text could be efficient for visual memory and reduce the limitations of screen reading."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to adjustable lighting and colour conditions in the reading environment
- Ability to produce enriched presentations with animation and figures rather than plain text
#### Constraints
- The article frames these as suggestions thought to help, not as experimentally manipulated conditions in this study

### Target Learners
- students reading instructional texts on screens

### Target Learning Goals
- improved comprehension and retention of on-screen text
