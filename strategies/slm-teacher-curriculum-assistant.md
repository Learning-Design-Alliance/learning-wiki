## Description
The article proposes SLMs as flexible assistants for teachers in remote or under-resourced schools who often lack peer collaboration or up-to-date materials. "These models can assist in curriculum design by generating lesson plans, creating problem sets, and translating dense academic texts into simpler language." It further suggests that culturally and ethically adaptive outputs can align educational content with local values and social contexts, which the article calls essential for meaningful and sustainable learning.
