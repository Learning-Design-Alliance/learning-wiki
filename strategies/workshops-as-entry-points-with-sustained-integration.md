# Use one-off AI literacy workshops as entry points supported by sustained, systemic curriculum integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends treating scalable one-day AI literacy workshops that pair foundational AI concepts with an explicit emphasis on synthetic media safety as effective entry points, while recognising that influencing career aspirations likely requires a more integrated, sustained approach. Policy-makers are told workshops must be supported by systemic curriculum integrations to drive workforce readiness, and that education policy must capture both immediate literacy gains and long-term outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops should pair foundational AI concepts with an explicit emphasis on synthetic media safety
#### Constraints
- A single-day intervention may be insufficient to meaningfully alter long-term career trajectories; no claims can be made about sustained impact or durability of gains

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students (Years 7–10) in government school systems

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy and conceptual understanding
- Synthetic media safety awareness
- STEM career readiness
