# Assess clinical thinking with both a thinking-skills scale and a professional knowledge examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article contrasts its approach with prior work that used "a singular self-evaluation scale approach", evaluating trainees instead with both the Clinical Thinking Skills Evaluation Scale and a professional knowledge examination built on Bloom's Taxonomy. The CTSES covers critical, systems, and evidence-based thinking across 24 Likert items (Cronbach's alpha 0.962, test-retest reliability 0.861). Using two measures strengthened confidence that observed gains reflected the teaching methods rather than disparate pre-course familiarity.
