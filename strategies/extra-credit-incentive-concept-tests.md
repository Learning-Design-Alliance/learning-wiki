# Offer extra-credit incentives to encourage full effort on ungraded pre- and post-tests

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because some students quickly mark down answers on concept-inventory pre- and post-tests, the article recommends an incentive for all to try their best that is not directly grade-related, since grading pre-test material students have not seen would be unfair. Graduate teaching assistants were asked to offer extra credit, using methods such as rewarding top scorers and highest normalized gain, or counting the post-test as a replacement quiz grade based on effort.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Graduate teaching assistants willing to administer and judge the incentive schemes
#### Constraints
- The incentive cannot be directly tied to the grade for the pre-test, because it would be unfair to grade students on material they have not seen

### Target Learners
- Students in large introductory physics courses taking concept-inventory pre- and post-tests

### Target Learning Goals
- Reliable measurement of conceptual learning gains from pre/post testing
