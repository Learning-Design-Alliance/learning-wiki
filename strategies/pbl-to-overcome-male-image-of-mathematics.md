# Use problem-based learning as an additional mathematics teaching strategy to overcome the male image of mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "PBL should therefore be used as an additional teaching strategy to other traditional methods of teaching mathematics," so that male and female students compete, collaborate and gain from one another. Because the study found achievement and retention under PBL not dependent on gender, the authors argue PBL can help overcome the male image of mathematics and enhance both male and female students' achievement and retention, fostering what they call greater healthy rivalry in mathematics instruction.
