# Keep in continual contact with distance students to reduce disappearing, isolation, and anxiety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors of online mathematics courses maintain ongoing contact with students throughout the semester. It suggests that when a student is identified only as a name on a screen, instructors may forget the person behind it — likely an adult with family and career responsibilities. Checking in on participation "may also reduce feelings of isolation and anxiety" that the article, citing Sadik and Reisman (2004), notes as common in distance mathematics classes.
