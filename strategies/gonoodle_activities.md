# GoNoodle Activities

## Description
GoNoodle provides interactive videos that encourage learners to engage in movement and play, supporting attention and learning. It offers diverse activities that combine movement with educational content, including dance parties, breathing exercises, and ASL coloring sheets. It can be used in the classroom or designed into products using videos, prompts, and augmented reality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to GoNoodle videos and digital platforms. Classroom space or integration into product design.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on regular implementation and alignment with curriculum goals.
