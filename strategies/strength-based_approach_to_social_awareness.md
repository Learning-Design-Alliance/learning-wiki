# Strength-Based Approach to Social Awareness

## Description
This method involves identifying a student's strengths related to social awareness and using those strengths to address their challenges. Social awareness is the understanding of social norms for behavior and the ability to understand the perspectives of others. Strategies include modeling empathy, discussing different perspectives explicitly, having conversations about emotions and social issues, and using current events and fictional characters to teach social awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of student's strengths and challenges. Current events and fictional stories. Facilitation of discussions.
#### Constraints
- Requires accurate identification of student's strengths and challenges. May need to provide additional support for students with significant challenges.
