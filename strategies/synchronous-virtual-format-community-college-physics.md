# Offer calculus-based physics in a virtual synchronous format for community college students with time commitments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Deliver introductory calculus-based physics as a virtual synchronous class so students attend regularly at the same time as their instructor and classmates from a distance. The authors argue community college students' time commitments "can be easily addressed by offering the classes in synchronous format at the same time providing them the best quality education which can simulate the same learning experience as on-campus classes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-structured schedule throughout the semester
- A learning management system for materials, assignments, grades, and feedback
- Video conference technology for synchronous class meetings
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a single instructor's classes at one community college

### Target Learners
- community college students working full or part-time with family commitments

### Target Learning Goals
- access to introductory calculus-based physics without sacrificing learning outcomes
