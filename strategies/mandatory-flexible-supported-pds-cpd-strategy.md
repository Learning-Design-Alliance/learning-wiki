# Implement mandatory, flexible, and financially supported CPD programs in public dental services with peer learning and monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the conclusion the article recommends implementing mandatory CPD programs in the Public Dental Service for all dental professionals. It suggests "Combining learning approaches, promoting professional interactions, engaging digital tools, and providing financial support for CPD," and states regulators can encourage implementation "by offering flexible learning options, providing financial support to participate in CPD, offering free training programs, encouraging peer learning, and establishing monitoring and evaluation to track the impact of CPD programs." The recommendation rests on the observed trend toward improvement and prior evidence that knowledge declines without regular application.
