# Incorporate both deductive and inductive logic strategies in teaching to enhance conceptual understanding and problem-solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends combining deductive logic learning, which explains theoretical principles and demonstrates their application, with inductive logic learning, which develops deeper conceptual understanding beyond memorization. It states that "incorporating deductive and inductive logic strategies in teaching can enhance learners' conceptual understanding and problem-solving abilities". Concept mapping is given as an example of the deductive approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous improvement in students' inductive and deductive logical reasoning skills is necessary to cultivate higher-order cognitive skills such as critical thinking
#### Constraints
- Memorization-based knowledge that is merely retained can weaken nursing graduates' critical thinking and problem-solving abilities

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding
- problem-solving abilities
- critical thinking
