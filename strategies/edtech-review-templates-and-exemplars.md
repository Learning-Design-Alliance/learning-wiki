# Offer templates and exemplars to streamline edtech review requests for vendors and staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends providing completed example forms and a vendor-facing intake form so that technical compatibility, accessibility, and legal compliance information is gathered in one interaction. The report states that "Examples of completed forms for a variety of requests can help streamline filling out the form for the first time or for a new type of request." This eases the burden on district staff who previously needed multiple conversations with providers, and onboards providers to what to expect from evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires development and maintenance of intake forms, exemplars, and a vendor-facing form hosting an overview of the evaluation process.
#### Constraints
- The report describes this as addressing a burden identified in DPS's own rollout; earlier versions of the process required multiple provider contacts per tool.

### Target Learners
- School and district leaders submitting tool requests, and edtech vendors

### Target Learning Goals
- Efficient review of new edtech tools so instructional needs are met without excessive administrative burden
