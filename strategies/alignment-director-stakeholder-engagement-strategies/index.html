Alignment Director strategies for engaging stakeholders: leveraging existing networks, field-based pods, and cross-sector convenings¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report documents implementable strategies ADs used to build statewide networks: leveraging previously established networks and individual connections; leveraging PARCC/Smarter Balanced consortia activities; fostering "field-based pods of activity, like regional councils and faculty work groups"; statewide committees with feedback loops (Hawaii's "road show" outreach); campus-based leadership teams (Louisiana); regional curriculum councils (Tennessee); and cross-sector steering committees, faculty work groups, and funded regional partnerships (Washington). Getting faculty engaged in hands-on work such as reviewing assessment items and course design was described as "the primary driver of Washington's grassroots efforts."
