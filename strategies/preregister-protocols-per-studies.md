# Preregister study protocols so methods are refereed before data are gathered

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 review), `q3`–`q4` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As a procedural change to reduce causal-inference errors in physics education research, the author proposes preregistering protocols for studies. This "allows some refereeing of methods before data are gathered and before particular methods are closely tied to particular conclusions", helping limit cherry-picking of methods and data and reducing p-hacking and reverse p-hacking. The author cautions that pre-registration still requires a community of reviewers and readers who can distinguish valid from invalid methods, which may require help from outside PER.
