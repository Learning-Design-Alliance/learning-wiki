# Configure AI teammates to preserve opportunities for human-human exchange

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "AI teammates should be configured not only to contribute useful task information but also to preserve opportunities for human-human exchange." They suggest "Calibrated participation, reduced airtime dominance, and prompts that encourage human teammates to respond to and build on one another's contributions may help sustain the team's relational channel." This is a forward-looking design recommendation grounded in the observed social cost, not a tested manipulation in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams using conversational AI teammates where designers can tune the agent's participation rate, airtime, and prompts.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is stated prospectively in the discussion; the study did not experimentally manipulate AI participation strategies.

### Target Learners

- human-AI team members

### Target Learning Goals
