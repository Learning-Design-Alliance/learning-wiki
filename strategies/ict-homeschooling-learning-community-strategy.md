# ICT can strengthen homeschooling through learning communities and resource access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review concludes that information and communication technology has potential to develop strong learning communities and improve access to relevant materials for homeschooling families. It states "informational and communication technology has a brighter potential to further develop a strong learning community and access to relevant materials to homeschooling learning". Hanna's (2012) findings suggested ICT use might assist learning toward 21st century skills.
