# Explicitly teach and intensively practice self-regulated strategies in speaking classes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's first recommendation is that "Self-regulated strategies must be explicitly taught and intensively practiced by the students in speaking classes." This follows from the intervention, in which the instructor explicitly taught and modeled preparation, monitoring, and reflection strategies before students applied them in group speaking tasks. The recommendation targets teachers still using traditional reading-and-recitation approaches to oral skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors model the strategies and guide small-group application
#### Constraints
- Based on a one-group quasi-experiment without a control group

### Target Learners
- EFL university students
- pre-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- speaking proficiency
- reducing speaking anxiety
