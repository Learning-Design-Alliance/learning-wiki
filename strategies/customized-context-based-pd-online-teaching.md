# Provide relevant, customized professional development grounded in teachers' local contexts, with ongoing just-in-time support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's discussion recommends that professional development for online teaching be tailored rather than uniform. Because districts and teachers differed in needs and existing resources, the authors argue that "it is important to understand teachers’ local contexts and provide relevant and customized PD training." They further recommend ongoing support outside formal PD, citing supportive environments with regular and just-in-time feedback, and suggest communities of practice and peer support as efficient ways to sustain teacher learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding teachers' local contexts before designing PD, since a one-size-fits-all approach will not work across districts with different needs
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a survey of 107 US teachers in the early stage of the COVID-19 pandemic, not from a test of a PD program

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Online teaching skills and preparedness for emergency remote teaching
