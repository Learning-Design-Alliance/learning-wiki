# Effectively incorporate self-regulation tools and prolong exposure in Web-based computer-tailored interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future Web-based computer-tailored interventions ensure self-regulation tools are incorporated so participants actually use and integrate them, and that exposure is extended over time. They report that "exposure to these two follow-up sessions was very low", so participants "may have not sufficiently integrated self-regulation skills in daily life", and state that "more research is needed on how self-regulation tools and techniques can be effectively incorporated in Web-based CT interventions and how prolonged exposure and use of such interventions can be increased".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained participant exposure across follow-up sessions, prompted by e-mail reminders, so self-regulation steps are practiced in daily life
#### Constraints
- In this trial the self-regulation progression and maintenance sessions were placed in the second and third sessions, which few participants revisited

### Target Learners
- Adults using Web-based computer-tailored health behavior interventions

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained self-regulation of dietary behavior (goal setting, monitoring, coping planning, maintenance after relapse)
