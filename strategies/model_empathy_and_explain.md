# Model Empathy and Explain

## Description
Explicitly model empathy by demonstrating understanding and sharing in the feelings of others, then explain the thought process behind showing empathy. It involves expressing genuine concern, actively listening to others, and reflecting on their experiences to create a supportive and understanding learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to be empathetic and demonstrate genuine concern for learners' feelings.  Requires creating a safe and supportive learning environment where learners feel comfortable sharing their experiences.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for some learners to understand or internalize empathy.  Requires ongoing effort and reinforcement to become a natural behavior.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learner interactions and participation in group activities.  Assess learner responses to scenarios requiring empathy.  Collect learner reflections on their understanding and demonstration of empathy.

## Impact
- Research shows that modeling empathy can improve interpersonal relationships, reduce conflict, and enhance social-emotional development. Modeling empathy in a classroom is an extremely powerful tool because learners learn by watching and taking the lead of their instructors.
