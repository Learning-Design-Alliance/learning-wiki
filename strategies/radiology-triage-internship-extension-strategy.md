# Extend experiential radiology education with clinical participation programmes such as radiology triage and department internships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student recommendations and the Dartmouth-Hitchcock example, the authors suggest bringing clinical participation into experiential radiology education. They note that at Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center, 'students are required to attend a radiology triage programme to work with on-call radiology residents', which students reported as a valuable clinical learning experience, and that some of their own students requested radiology department internships. Such programmes are proposed as an important aspect of experiential education, alongside additional forms such as integrative teaching.
