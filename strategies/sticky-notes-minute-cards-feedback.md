# Sticky notes and minute cards for real-time feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each learner receives two sticky notes of different colors. Instructors use them for quick true/false questions and real-time status during hands-on work: "learners can put a green sticky on their laptop when they have something done, or a red sticky when they need help". Before each break, learners write one thing they learned on the green sticky and one confusing thing on the red sticky, giving instructors a quick way to adjust to learners' interests and speed.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two differently colored sticky notes per learner and a couple of minutes at each break to collate responses
