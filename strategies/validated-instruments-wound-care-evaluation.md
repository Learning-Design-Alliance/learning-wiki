# Use validated assessment instruments rather than self-created surveys when evaluating wound care education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that educators "use validated instruments for the evaluation, for example, established questionnaires rather than self-created surveys and predominantly subjective assessments by participants." Only one of the nine included studies used a validated tool (the Jefferson Scale of Attitudes towards Physician–Nurse Collaboration). This strategy aims to obtain clearer results on teaching effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to established, validated questionnaires or scales appropriate to the learning goals
#### Constraints
- In the reviewed literature, all other studies used simple rating scales, short pre- and post-quizzes, or subjective competency assessments

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- wound care knowledge and clinical skills
