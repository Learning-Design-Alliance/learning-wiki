# Use e-learning platforms to deliver in-service Scratch training, especially for teachers far from training centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends delivering in-service computer science teacher training through electronic learning platforms rather than requiring attendance at physical training centers. It states "There is a need to employ e -learning platforms in the training of Scratch programming language in the in -service training of computer teachers, especially those teachers who live in areas far from the training centers." The study's experimental group was trained this way remotely across Tarif, Rafha and Al Awayleh.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An electronic learning platform account and suitable instructional videos vetted for syllabus fit
#### Constraints
- The study was limited to one educational unit of the middle school third-grade computer science course in the Northern Border area of Saudi Arabia during the second semester of 2016

### Target Learners
- in-service middle school computer science teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Scratch programming skills
- attitudes toward teaching programming
