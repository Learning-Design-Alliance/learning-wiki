# Virtual Reality (VR) Training

## Description
Virtual Reality (VR) training is a method of using VR technology to create simulated environments where learners can engage in interactive training scenarios. VR training has proven effective in enhancing training efficiency and improving skill retention, especially in hands-on jobs. VR simulates real-world situations, allowing learners to practice and make mistakes in a safe, controlled environment, promoting experiential learning and mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR headsets and equipment, appropriate software, realistic and well-designed virtual environments, trained facilitators.
#### Constraints
- Initial setup costs can be high, requires technical expertise, may cause motion sickness in some users, needs ongoing updates and maintenance.
