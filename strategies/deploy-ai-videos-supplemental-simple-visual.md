# Deploy AI-generated videos for simple, supplemental, and visual content without displacing instructor presence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on student perceptions in this study, the authors conclude that "AI videos can play a meaningful role in computing education without displacing instructors' presence that students value." Students favored AI videos for foundational or easily visualized topics, high-level summaries, supplemental clarification, and personalized support, while opposing their use for complex, subjective, or interactive contexts and for replacing lectures. Instructors should therefore adopt AI videos as supplements to, not replacements for, instructor-led instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content suited to short-form video (simple, well-documented topics); instructor verification of accuracy before deployment.
#### Constraints
- Students expressed distrust when AI videos replace rather than supplement instructor-led content; concerns include hallucinated information, reduced interaction with educators, and diminished educational value.

### Target Learners
- computing students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- supplemental instruction on simple, visualizable programming topics
