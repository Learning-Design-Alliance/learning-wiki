# Implement a national licensing examination in Peru and redistribute residency spots beyond the capital

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend two policy actions for Peruvian medical education. First, given that applicants who fail the ENARM return to the healthcare system with no licensing examination in Peru, they state this "represents evidence of the need to implement one urgently." Second, given the overconcentration of applicants and residency spots in Lima, they argue the need "to redistribute residency spots across the Peruvian country," warning that if regulatory bodies do not consider the centralization of medical training, a collapse in the clinical fields is imminent.
