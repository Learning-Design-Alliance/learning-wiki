# Use AI-generated resources as a supplement or alternative to learnersourced content, with varied exemplars to stimulate diversity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because blind peer ratings showed AI-generated resources rated on par with student-generated ones, the authors recommend AI-generated resources "could be a viable supplement or alternative to student-generated resources in certain learning contexts", especially where student engagement is low. They further recommend "Incorporating a wider variety in terms of length and difficulty in the exemplar resources that are provided as prompts to the AI model could potentially stimulate the creation of more diverse learning materials."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Few-shot exemplars provided to the model; a more varied exemplar set is recommended to increase diversity of outputs
- Blind peer evaluation to monitor quality of generated resources
#### Constraints
- Perceived quality was measured, not learning outcomes; long-term impact is unexamined
- In low-engagement settings the value proposition may differ from the highly incentivised cohort studied

### Target Learners
- students in introductory programming courses
- learners in online platforms using personalised practice

### Target Learning Goals
- access to supplementary practice resources such as code examples with explanations
