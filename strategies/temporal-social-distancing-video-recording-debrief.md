# Temporal social distancing with video recording: run participants sequentially, record scenarios, and combine into a shared debrief to build a shared mental model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Temporal social distancing has team members participate one by one in sequential training sessions, with handwashing or sanitizing before and after participation. Without teammates present, participants must anticipate and state what other team members would do. Team leaders review all recorded individual scenarios, create an overview of potential problems and a combined debrief, thereby helping to create a shared mental model. Exemplar videos of rehearsed teams can also be disseminated to the department.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recording of individual scenarios and team-leader review of all scenarios
#### Constraints
- Participants are forced to anticipate and state what they expect other team members would do, rather than interacting live

### Target Learners
- obstetric health care teams

### Target Learning Goals
- shared mental model formation
- anticipating teammate roles in emergencies
