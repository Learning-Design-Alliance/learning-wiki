## Description
The article states that "the maintenance of manageable instructor-to-student ratios through multi-session small-class models, coupled with calibrated time controls and uniform scoring rubrics, may have helped preserve active learning integrity" during near-doubling of cohort size. This strategy addresses scaling case-based pedagogy to large enrollments while maintaining instructional quality.
