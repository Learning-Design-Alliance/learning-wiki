# Website Evaluation Checklist

## Description
Learners examine websites (both reliable and unreliable) using a checklist to assess various elements such as the corroboration of information, writer reliability, information currency, documentation, advocacy, and conflict of interest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A checklist of evaluation criteria and access to a variety of websites, including both reliable and unreliable sources.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness depends on the quality and comprehensiveness of the checklist, and learners may require guidance in interpreting and applying the criteria.
