# Engage in FA development opportunities: peer observation, PLCs, and coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teachers benefit from developing shared definitions and understanding of FA purposes with peers through collaborative discussions, professional learning communities, instructional coaching, and differentiated professional development. In Davis (2017), colleague observation with post-observation feedback built teachers' self-efficacy; Kim (2019) teachers were supported to set goals by analyzing curriculum materials in collaborative meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Critical self-reflection and willingness to seek collaborative discussions about FA practices
#### Constraints
- A lack of confidence as mathematics teachers and fear of not understanding students' thinking can hinder effective FA use

### Target Learners
- in-service secondary mathematics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher self-efficacy with FA
- shared FA practices and norms
