# Text-to-Text, Text-to-Self, Text-to-World

## Description
Text-to-Text, Text-to-Self, Text-to-World is a strategy that helps learners connect ideas in a text to their own experiences and beliefs (text-to-self), events happening in the world (text-to-world), and other texts they have encountered (text-to-text). This approach encourages deeper engagement with the material and promotes meaningful connections that enhance understanding. This strategy can be used at the beginning, middle, or end of the reading process to get learners engaged with a text, to help learners understand the text more deeply, or to evaluate learners’ understanding of the text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Text to be read, guiding questions for each type of connection (text-to-self, text-to-text, text-to-world), materials for recording connections (e.g., handout, journal).
#### Constraints
- Superficial connections may not enhance understanding. Learners may need explicit instruction and modeling to make meaningful connections.
