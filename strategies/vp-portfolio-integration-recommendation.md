# Connect virtual patient activities with learners' personal e-portfolios to support learner-centeredness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends collecting and connecting content, learning experiences, feedback, and other VP-related activity in learners' personal e-portfolios as a way of reflecting learner-centeredness in curricular embedding. Portfolios have become widespread in healthcare education in recent years, but "to our knowledge little development and research has been done on how to effectively combine them with VP activities". This is proposed as an open area for development and research rather than a demonstrated practice.
