# Train resuscitation team leaders in all four non-technical skill domains, with additional selective leader-focused training beyond whole-team training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that CPR training should not merely focus on general teamwork but address all ANTS domains, and that selective non-technical skills training for team leaders in addition to whole-team training could yield incremental benefit. They write that "additional selective non-technical skills training for team leaders—in addition to training resuscitation teams as a whole—could have an incremental benefit on team performance", especially under stressful conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should cover task management, team working, situation awareness and decision-making, not only teamwork
#### Constraints
- Benefit is expected especially under stressful conditions; under routine, straightforward CPR the role of non-technical skills seemed less crucial in this study

### Target Learners
- resuscitation team leaders
- CPR teams

### Target Learning Goals
- non-technical skills under stress
- technical CPR performance
