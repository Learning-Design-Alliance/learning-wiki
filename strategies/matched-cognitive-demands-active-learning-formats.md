# Balance active-learning formats by matching cognitive demands across comparison conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article designed CBL scenarios and GBL question levels to impose comparable cognitive demands, ensuring a fair comparison: "the CBL scenarios were designed to ensure that all these levels were covered in a systematic manner so that students from both groups underwent similar cognitive demands." Both groups received identical pre-class educational material on hypertension treatment, and all questions and scenarios assessed the same learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same educational material and learning objectives across formats; standardized question/scenario design with expert input
#### Constraints
- Exact questions presented during sessions were not always identical between groups

### Target Learners
- fourth-year PharmD students

### Target Learning Goals
- hypertension pharmacotherapy: factual retention and clinical reasoning
