# Stations: Interacting with Multiple Texts

## Description
The Stations strategy involves small groups of learners rotating through different stations to interact with a variety of resources (informational texts, poetry, art, photography, maps, videos, audio clips) related to a specific event, theme, or question. At each station, learners engage with the material and either answer questions or complete a reflective activity. This approach allows learners to engage with the topic using multiple modalities, promoting deeper understanding and multiple perspectives. The stations activity works well to launch a new unit or to explore in more depth something learners have already studied.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of diverse resources (texts, videos, images, etc.), materials for each station (folders, copies of resources, instructions), space for multiple stations, time for learners to rotate through stations.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant preparation to gather and organize resources. May be challenging to manage time and ensure all learners are engaged at each station.
