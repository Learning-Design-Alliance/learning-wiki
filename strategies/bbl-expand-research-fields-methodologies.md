# Expand brain-based learning research to chemistry, physics, biology and primary education using diverse methodologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that brain-based learning research and application be extended beyond its current concentration in science and technology education. It states that "brain-based learning should be more widespread in the fields of chem istry, physics and biology and primary education" and that "qualitative and mixed research as well as quantitat ive research methodology should be done for obtaining reliable, valid and in-depth results in the future."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research designs beyond quasi-experimental quantitative methods, including qualitative and mixed methods, to obtain reliable, valid and in-depth results
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is based on the Turkish dissertation corpus analysed (21 dissertations up to 2016), where chemistry and primary education had no studies at all

### Target Learners
- Primary and secondary science students in Turkey

### Target Learning Goals
- Science achievement and attitudes toward science
