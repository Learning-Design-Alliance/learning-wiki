# Creating Collaborative Spaces

## Description
Creating collaborative spaces involves designing the learning environment to facilitate group work and interaction among learners. This can include arranging desks or tables in clusters, providing comfortable seating areas for small group discussions, and creating writable surfaces for brainstorming and problem-solving. The goal is to encourage learners to work together, share ideas, and learn from one another. Collaborative spaces can support the development of communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a willingness to rearrange the learning environment to facilitate group work. May require adjustments to classroom management strategies to address learner interaction and collaboration. Instructors must be mindful of the impact of the learning environment on learner interaction and collaboration.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may prefer to work independently. It may be challenging to manage group dynamics and ensure that all learners are actively participating. Creating collaborative spaces may not be sufficient to address learner communication and teamwork problems.

### Affordances
- Increases opportunities for learners to interact and collaborate. Supports the development of communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills. Promotes peer learning and a sense of community. Can improve learner engagement and motivation.

### Personalization
- Instructors can create collaborative spaces that are tailored to the needs of different learners. Learners can choose to work in groups that suit their learning style and preferences. Creating collaborative spaces can be combined with other personalization strategies, such as flexible grouping and flexible pacing.
