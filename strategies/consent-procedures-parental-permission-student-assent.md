# Develop appropriate procedures for gathering parental permission and student assent

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Consent gathering is one of the six key decisions the review identifies, and the article's conclusions single it out as a practical step. It recommends "developing appropriate procedures for gathering parental permission and student assent" as part of ensuring high-quality research evidence in school-based trials. Because cluster randomized trials in schools involve both parents and students, researchers should plan consent procedures deliberately during trial design rather than addressing them ad hoc during implementation.
