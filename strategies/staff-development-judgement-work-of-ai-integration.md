# Focus staff development on the judgement work of AI integration rather than tool training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the shift from teacher-centric delivery to AI-augmented facilitation, the article recommends that educator development move beyond tool training toward integration judgement: deciding when AI should be invited into learning tasks, when it should be withheld, and making those decisions visible to students. The article states "staff development must focus less on tool training and more on the judgement work of integration". This is grounded in constructivist readings of AI as scaffolding and in TPACK's educator-capability requirement.
