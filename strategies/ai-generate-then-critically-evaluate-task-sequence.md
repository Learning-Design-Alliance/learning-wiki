# AI-generated-essay-then-critical-evaluation task sequence for teaching critical evaluation skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy embeds ChatGPT into pedagogy as a catalyst for critical thinking rather than a task-completion shortcut. Learners first receive an orientation on ChatGPT's capabilities and limitations, then generate an essay with the tool and critically evaluate that output, anchoring their critique in peer-reviewed literature. The article frames this as "critically evaluating the output using peer-reviewed sources", making each AI statement something that "warrants verification" and driving deeper engagement with the literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners are first introduced to ChatGPT's capabilities and limitations, including prompt formulation and editorial adjustment, in an orientation session
#### Constraints
- The article states AI should be used as a supplement, not a dependency, and that over-reliance risks curtailing learners' capacity for original thought

### Target Learners
- postgraduate master's students in biomedical science programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of scientific content
- reading and assessing academic literature
- research and analytical skills
