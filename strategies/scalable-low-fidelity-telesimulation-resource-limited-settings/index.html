Prioritise cost-effective and low-fidelity or remote modalities in resource-limited settings and pursue longitudinal, context-specific SBE research¶

Description¶

The review's forward-looking implementation and research agenda. It argues that "emerging strategies like tele-simulation and low-fidelity models present scalable, cost-effective solutions to mitigate access and equity challenges in resource-limited settings", and calls for research on optimal timing of SBE integration, longitudinal follow-up of retention and skill transfer, and expansion of research in underrepresented regions. Implementation requires balancing modality selection against institutional infrastructure, faculty development and cost barriers.

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Recognition and management of acutely ill children

Claims¶

  • The review reports that high-fidelity simulation outperformed case-based discussion for clinical reasoning and leadership in paediatric emergencies [+W]
  • Resource limitations, technical hurdles and time constraints were the primary reported barriers to implementing paediatric SBE [+W]
  • Telesimulation and remote-facilitated paediatric simulation achieved outcomes comparable to in-person training [+W]
