# Private-study method: read one text through for general survey, then proceed topically with outlines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The committee prescribes a sequence for candidates studying privately for the psychology examinations: first a whole-text reading for breadth, then topic-by-topic work across the selected books with a brief outline of facts gathered under each topic. The article states the outline "is an aid to thinking and is valuable in reviewing" and should be submitted with examination papers. Candidates are told to take time on questions and experiments, review by topics, set a definite study time, and proceed systematically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Select the required number of recommended texts (three for Elementary, six for Advanced) before proceeding topically
#### Constraints
- The outline is not obligatory; the article calls it an advantage

### Target Learners
- Teachers preparing for Iowa psychology certificate examinations by private study

### Target Learning Goals
- Mastery of psychology content for certification examinations
