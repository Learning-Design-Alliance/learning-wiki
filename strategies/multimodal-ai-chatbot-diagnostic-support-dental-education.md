# Use multimodal AI chatbots such as ChatGPT-4o as diagnostic support tools in dental education, particularly endodontics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying multimodal AI chatbots as diagnostic support tools in clinical training and decision-making, concluding that "ChatGPT-4o demonstrates significant potential as a diagnostic support tool in dental education, particularly in endodontics". Its high accuracy and consistency are presented as grounds for use as an innovative application in clinical training, with AI tools also framed as providing rapid access to information, personalized feedback, and individual support for learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multimodal model capable of processing images alongside text
- High-quality radiographs, as the model was not developed to directly analyze artifacts such as motion blur or metallic artifacts
#### Constraints
- Performance was tested only on multiple-choice questions, not open-ended formats
- Findings are constrained by the specific student population and a narrow range of clinical cases
- Broader testing with more complex and diverse clinical scenarios is needed

### Target Learners
- dental students in clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnostic accuracy and clinical decision-making in endodontics
