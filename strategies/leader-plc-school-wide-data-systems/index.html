Build school leaders' instructional leadership through five PLC sessions on school-wide data systems¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A parallel leadership recipe strengthens principals and other leaders: "Strengthen school leaders' skills in developing school-wide systems to support data use and providing coaching and instructional leadership on instructional routines through 5 PLC sessions." This treats leadership capacity as a distinct lever from classroom practice, developed in its own session series rather than in the educator PLCs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Five facilitated PLC sessions dedicated to school leaders

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • School leaders in partner districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing school-wide systems to support data use
  • Coaching and instructional leadership on instructional routines

Related Strategies¶

  • Train educators through a Summer Institute plus PLC and coaching cycles on data use and instructional routines
  • Use a diagnostic and implementation monitoring tool to give educators formative feedback on their instructional practices
