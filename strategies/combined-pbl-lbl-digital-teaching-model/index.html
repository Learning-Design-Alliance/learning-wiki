Use a combined teaching model mixing PBL with lecture-based and digital teaching, adapted to the local setting¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 11 claims (7 for, 2 mixed, 2 against) · 6 studies (6 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 3 of 6 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review observes that PBL "is often combined with other teaching methods, such as LBL and digital teaching", and that digital PBL may outperform traditional learning or traditional PBL for improving skills. It concludes a combined teaching model can be used in education, particularly in limited-resource medical colleges, and that non-Western adopters should modify PBL rather than transfer it unchanged.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators must adapt adoption methods to their particular setting rather than lifting PBL unchanged from its Western environment of origin

Constraints¶

  • The current application of PBL in surgical education has not been widely or thoroughly studied

Target Learners¶

  • medical students in limited-resource medical colleges
  • nursing and dental students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • clinical skills
  • critical thinking
  • learning efficiency

Claims¶

  • PBL significantly improves clinical skill scores compared with lecture-based learning in medical education [+M]
  • PBL shows significant advantages in critical thinking compared with traditional education or team-based learning in nursing and clinical student populations [+W]
  • PBL shows no benefits for hands-on, communication, or self-directed learning skills and no advantage in operational skills for diagnostic imaging [-W]
  • In first-year medical students, PBL showed no significant difference from non-PBL teaching in critical thinking, problem-solving, or self-directed learning [-M]
  • In Chinese neurology education, TBL alone or combined with other methods is associated with improved self-study ability, communication skills, and teamwork ability versus LBL [+W]
  • TBL alone and TBL combined with other methods both outperform LBL, with no significant difference between the two TBL formats [+W]
  • In Chinese neurology training, CBL, PBL, TBL, FCM and EBM each significantly improve theoretical exam scores compared with lecture-based learning [+W]
  • Eight new teaching methods (PBL + CBL, PBL + CP, CBL + WeChat, PBL + WeChat, TBL, PBL, PBL + EBM, CBL) each significantly outperform LBL on residents' theoretical scores [+W]
  • PBL-CBL outperforms LBL on subjective comprehensive competencies including self-learning, interest, efficiency, clinical thinking, teamwork, and communication [+W]
  • Insufficient evidence exists to compare TBL with other active learning methods [~W]
  • Evidence for PBL effectiveness is rated low quality with considerable heterogeneity, attributed to instructor supervision, participant characteristics, and unstandardized assessment [~W]
