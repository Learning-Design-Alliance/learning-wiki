# In future ESM studies, additionally assess students' subjectively perceived instructional clarity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because objective observer-coded instructional clarity explained almost none of the shared situational motivation variance, the authors recommend that future experience sampling studies also measure students' perceived clarity of a lecture. Perceived IC could be added at Level 1 (ESM-survey) to link a specific situation's perceived clarity to momentary motivation, or at Level 2a (students) as a stable average perception to examine how overall perceived clarity relates to dispositional motivation. The article states "it might be useful in future ESM studies to also assess students' perceived IC of a lecture".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- ESM surveys extended with items on students' perceived instructional clarity, alongside objective measures to compare the two.
#### Constraints
- The study itself could not provide insights on whether subjective perceptions align with objective indicators.

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding how perceived teaching quality relates to momentary and dispositional motivation
