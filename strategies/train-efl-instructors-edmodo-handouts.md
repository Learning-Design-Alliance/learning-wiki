# Train EFL university instructors on how to use Edmodo in developing SDL among students and provide published handouts on its use.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the present study's results, the researcher recommends that EFL university instructors should be trained on how to use Edmodo in developing SDL among students, that curriculum developers and textbook writers should include activities based on using Edmodo to develop SDL among students, and that published handouts on how teachers can use Edmodo to develop SDL among students should be provided to instructors of EFL at the university level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be provided to instructors of EFL at the university level on how to use Edmodo to develop SDL among students.
- Curriculum developers and textbook writers must include activities based on using Edmodo to develop SDL among students.
#### Constraints
- The recommendations are based on a single quasi-experimental one-group study with 45 students at one Saudi faculty, so the researcher suggests carrying out a replication with more number of students to generalize the results.

### Target Learners
- university EFL instructors
- curriculum developers and textbook writers

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed learning
- incorporating Edmodo in course teaching
