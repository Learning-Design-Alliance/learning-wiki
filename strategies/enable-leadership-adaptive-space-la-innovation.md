## Description
The article recommends enabling leadership that can navigate and negotiate existing resources to encourage change and nurture innovations during the learning phase of LA adoption. The authors illustrate this with case U42 (UK), which "successfully implemented a university-wide project within one year under prominent leadership of a coalition of the Head of Learning Technology, Chief Information Officer, and Pro Vice-Chancellor in Academic and Student Experience."
