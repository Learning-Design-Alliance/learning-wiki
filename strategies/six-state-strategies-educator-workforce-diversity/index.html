Six state policy strategies for increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief organizes its policy scan around six strategies states should pursue to diversify the educator workforce: making educator diversity data visible and actionable, setting clear state and district goals, investing in educator preparation programs, targeting resources to districts and schools for recruiting and hiring a diverse workforce, investing in retention efforts that improve working conditions and growth opportunities for teachers of color, and using federal COVID-related relief funds for these purposes. Each strategy is rated against printed criteria on a three-level scale from "meets one or no requirements" to "meets all or most of the requirements."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • States must create the right policy conditions to support educator preparation programs, districts, and schools in preparing, recruiting, and retaining teachers of color

Constraints¶

  • Ratings reflect one state's (California's) status in 2022 against the brief's own criteria, not measured effects of the strategies on workforce diversity

Target Learners¶

  • state policymakers and advocates
  • K-12 students of color

Target Learning Goals¶

  • increasing student access to diverse educators
  • recruiting, preparing, and retaining teachers of color
