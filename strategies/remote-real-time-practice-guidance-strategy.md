# Provide remote real-time guidance during student practice, allowing the teacher to observe operations and correct technique from a distance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
After the demonstration, students practiced on phantom heads under assistant teachers locally and remote guidance from the main teacher via real-time connection. "The main teacher could closely observe the students' operations and offer remote guidance accordingly", pointing out issues at each step. The monitor camera's angle and range could be adjusted to present students' posture clearly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time video connection between main teacher and practice sites
- Adjustable monitor camera angle and range
- Assistant teachers present at each participating college
#### Constraints
- High demands on network connectivity; disruptions could compromise the quality of the course

### Target Learners
- second-year dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- correcting ultrasonic scaling technique during practice
- proper instrument usage
