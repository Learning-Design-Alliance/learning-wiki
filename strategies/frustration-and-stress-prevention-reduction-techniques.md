# Frustration and Stress Prevention/Reduction Techniques

## Description
Educators should practice techniques to prevent/reduce their stress and frustration. Techniques include playing soft, relaxing music, displaying posters throughout the classroom of attractive destinations, modifying lesson plans and teaching a lesson that you enjoy, taking a short walk around the school, and asking a neighboring teacher to take a difficult learner for a short time. Some may find help by assigning independent reading time, or taking time to organize your work area.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must have a pre-planned list of stress/frustration reduction techniques to use.
#### Constraints
- Each instructor has unique needs, and it is important that the right strategies are employed.
