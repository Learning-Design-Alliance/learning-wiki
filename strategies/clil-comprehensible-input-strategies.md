# Use comprehensible-input strategies in CLIL: visuals, synonyms and paraphrasing in L2, and code-switching to L1

## Description
The article recommends developing CLIL methodology beyond task-based learning alone by making input comprehensible. It suggests "the use of particular strategies by the teacher such as visuals may enhance the communication of content", alongside L2-medium strategies such as synonyms or paraphrasing and L1-medium strategies such as code-switching, which may enhance L2 benefits.
