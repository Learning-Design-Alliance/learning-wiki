# Multimodal Composition Projects

## Description
Multimodal composition involves learners combining different modes of communication (linguistic, visual, audio, gestural, spatial) to enhance the message they want to convey. Learners use digital tools to combine images, audio, and video to represent their thoughts on different topics and share their perspectives. This can include creating web-based interactive stories with multiple endings and various genres to enhance comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to digital tools for creating and editing multimedia content. Needs instructor guidance on effective multimodal combinations and design principles.
#### Constraints
- Requires technical skills and access to technology, which may create barriers for some learners. Ineffective mode combinations can distract from the intended meaning if not carefully analyzed.
