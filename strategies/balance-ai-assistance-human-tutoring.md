# Balance AI assistance with human tutoring to optimize learning outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that AI-based coding platforms balance AI assistance with human guidance rather than replacing human support entirely. Interview participants "emphasized the importance of balancing AI assistance with human guidance for deeper learning," and the paper's third research question asks how the platform can balance AI assistance and human tutoring to optimize student learning outcomes. The strategy treats AI Socratic hints as one component within a broader support ecosystem including professors, peers, and structured courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI tools should provide Socratic hints rather than direct answers so students retain independent problem-solving
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as participant perception and an open research question, not a tested comparison of AI-only versus blended support

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students
- independent learners without expert guidance

### Target Learning Goals
- deeper conceptual learning
- independent problem-solving
- coding proficiency
