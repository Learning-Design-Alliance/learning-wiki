# Connect with Parents Early in the School Year

## Description
Connecting with parents as soon as possible in the school year through personalized phone calls, emails, in-person conversations, or letters home. This helps foster open, comfortable communication systems and mutual respect. Contacting parents early, especially with positive feedback, helps build a strong foundation for collaboration and support throughout the year.
