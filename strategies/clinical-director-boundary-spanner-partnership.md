# Clinical director as LEA–IHE boundary-spanner managing each partnership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each Alder–LEA partnership is managed by a clinical director, an LEA employee who is also a clinical faculty member at Alder. The report states the clinical director "ensured alignment between both sides of the partnership and oversaw most day-to-day residency operations." Clinical directors teach Resident Seminar, design activities around skills valued by the LEA employer, and connect coursework to clinical practice. Alder leadership also meets at least annually with each partner superintendent to present LEA-specific residency impact data, grounding the partnership in shared goals.
