# Predator/prey simulation game on the beach for hatchling survival

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An on-site activity in which "Students will play a predator/prey game on the beach to simulate what can happen to hatchling sea turtles as they crawl to the ocean." After the game, students discuss why sea turtles are endangered and what can be done to protect them. Objectives include listing natural predators, describing the nesting cycle, explaining the low hatchling survival rate, and describing human impacts on sea turtle reproduction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Beach location at the park; discussion time after the game
#### Constraints
- Conducted on-site during a park visit

### Target Learners
- Grades 5-8 students on a park field trip

### Target Learning Goals
- Predator/prey relationships
- Endangered wildlife
- Human impact on wildlife
