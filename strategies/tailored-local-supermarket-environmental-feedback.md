# Providing tailored objective feedback on local supermarket availability and price of healthy foods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The plus version delivers environmental feedback based on locally audited supermarket data: "The environmental information, which consists of the availability and price of selected fruit, vegetables, lower-energy snacks and lower-fat products, is collected via observations in the local supermarkets (n = 58)". A research assistant audited cooperating supermarkets and the observed information was incorporated into the tailored feedback, which is provided in response to participants' specific attitude and self-efficacy beliefs and in a separate section.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation of local supermarkets for audits and monthly price updates, or use of supermarket website information as a fallback
#### Constraints
- The article states this approach "appeared to be feasible, but very time-consuming", and 27 of 58 supermarkets did not cooperate, so those participants received general information

### Target Learners
- Dutch adults, especially low-educated groups for whom price is suggested to be more important

### Target Learning Goals
- correct perceived availability and price barriers to healthy food choices
