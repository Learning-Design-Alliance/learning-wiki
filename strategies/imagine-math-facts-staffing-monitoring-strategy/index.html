Implement Imagine Math Facts with existing math staff monitoring individualized learning paths and progress reports¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program requires no additional staffing: it is typically implemented by students' mathematics teachers, mathematics specialists, or lab instructors. These educators are responsible for "monitoring student engagement with individualized learning paths" and tracking the program's reports on student progress and growth, making implementation feasible within regular staffing.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators must monitor student engagement with individualized learning paths and track reports within the program
