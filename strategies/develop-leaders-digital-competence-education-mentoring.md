# Develop healthcare leaders' digital competence through continuous education, mentoring, and organizational structures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that developing and supporting healthcare leaders' digital competencies should be considered in healthcare organizations, research, and education. It states "we recommend that the development of healthcare leaders' competence in technology use via various educational activities is necessary" and that organizations should implement mentoring activities and expert advice, create structures enabling identification and development of leaders' digital competence, and weave AI concepts into leaders' education curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations and work units should create structures and possibilities enabling identification, evaluation, and development of leaders' digital competence
#### Constraints
- Leaders' attendance in training is challenged by lack of resources such as time, expenses, or broader organizational support

### Target Learners
- Healthcare leaders and managers at all levels of healthcare organizations

### Target Learning Goals
- Digital competence for health IT implementation
- Technology use in nursing management and leadership
