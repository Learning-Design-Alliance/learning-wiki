# Use the PIQL pre/post in introductory physics courses to raise instructor awareness of whether quantitative-literacy goals are being met

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend administering the PIQL before and after instruction so instructors can see the extent to which PQL goals are met, potentially driving curriculum development. The article states: "We intend use of the PIQL in introductory courses to raise awareness among instructors about the extent to which instructional goals surrounding PQL are being met." This mirrors how early concept inventories such as the FCI raised awareness that basic concepts were not understood at the level instructors assumed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration before significant instruction (as pretest) and after instruction, online or in-person
#### Constraints
- The measured flat PQL development (d = 0.3 and d = 0.1) suggests awareness alone reveals a gap; the article does not claim the PIQL itself closes it

### Target Learners
- students in introductory physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- developing physics quantitative literacy as a course outcome
