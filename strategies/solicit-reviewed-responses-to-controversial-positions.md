# Solicit and formally review unsolicited scholarly responses when publishing a controversial position

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When a journal publishes a controversial position, it can invite both solicited and unsolicited short responses from scholars, subject them to formal review and likely revision, and publish them in a subsequent issue. The editorial states that "any submission will not be automatically accepted for publication but will be subjected to a formal review process and likely revision requests."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A guest editor to coordinate responses and prospective authors contacting the editor with intent to write
#### Constraints
- Page limit for responses is brief, 10-15 pages, and submissions are not automatically accepted

### Target Learners
- Education researchers and scholars writing responses to position papers

### Target Learning Goals
- Scholarly debate and critical evaluation of research-based recommendations
