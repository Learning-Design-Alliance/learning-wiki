# When selecting mathematics assessments, choose one of the overlapping tests matched to your population and never mix tests across pre- and post-testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article advises physics faculty using mathematics RBAIs (QLCE, PCA, CCR, CCI) for readiness screening or pre/post measurement of instruction to select a single instrument whose content matches their population and needs. Because these four tests cover overlapping concepts, the article warns: "Don't mix-and-match these tests for pre- and post-test because you will have difficulty comparing pre-scores to post-scores." Tests used only before instruction should cover more elementary content fully taught in prerequisite courses, while pre/post tests may include co-requisite content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Match the chosen test to the student population and the assessment purpose (readiness screening versus pre/post measurement).
#### Constraints
- The CCR is recommended for placement packages but must be paid for; the CCI may be inappropriate for conceptual or algebra-based physics students.

### Target Learners
- introductory college physics students and high school physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing students' math readiness and calculus and vector skills for physics courses
