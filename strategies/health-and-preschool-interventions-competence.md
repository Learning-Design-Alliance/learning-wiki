# Raise future competence levels through health-focused and early-environmental interventions including pre-school education, reading to children, and cognitive training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its discussion of possible improvements to the projected trend, the article recommends interventions outside schooling: "Less in school education but more in the field of health: Prenatal care, neuroenhancement and therapies against cognitive decline in later adulthood can have substantial positive effects on individuals' and societies' competence levels." It further names pre-school education, reading to the child by parents and kindergarten teachers, and cognitive training as environmental interventions with known effects, and suggests combining known and future health-focused practices.
