# Wall Calendar

## Description
A large calendar for family appointments and events can help kids see that organization is a lifelong skill. Everyone — even young kids — can see how they fit into the bigger picture of the household. Try using a different color marker for each family member to highlight different schedules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Large wall calendar, colored markers
#### Constraints
- Requires a central location for the calendar. Dependent on consistent updating and use. May not be effective for learners with visual impairments.

- Promote awareness of schedules and events, enhance time management skills, foster a sense of belonging within the household

### Affordances
- Provides a visual representation of time and events, reduces reliance on verbal reminders, promotes communication and coordination.

### Personalization
- Calendar can be customized with different colors, symbols, and images. Family members can add their own events and appointments.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the learner's ability to recall and anticipate events. Track the frequency of on-time arrival at appointments. Gather feedback from the learner on the usefulness of the calendar.

## Impact
- Visual calendars have been shown to improve time management and reduce stress. Family calendars promote communication and a sense of shared responsibility.
