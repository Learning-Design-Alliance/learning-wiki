# Maintain a high demonstrator-to-student ratio at surgical skills stations to maximise feedback and supervision.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article states that "There is a high tutor to student ratio in each station to allow maximal feedback during the course", with ratios ranging from 1:1 at the EyeSI and phacoemulsification stations to 1:4 at suturing and cadaver stations. Students appreciated the low demonstrator-to-student ratio and reported receiving useful feedback and direction from demonstrators throughout the course, though they would have liked some extra supervision in the cadaver stations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
