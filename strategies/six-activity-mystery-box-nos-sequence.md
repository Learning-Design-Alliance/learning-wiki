# Six-activity mystery box teaching sequence mirroring the Higgs discovery milestones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete classroom recipe of six activities: demonstrating competing models (theory-laden NoS), systematic testing and inference (empirical and inferential NoS), devising a better-fitting model (creative NoS), retesting with a magnet (tentative NoS), poster presentation with anonymous peer review and consensus (social dimensions), and explicit reflection. The peer-review step "resembles the scientific peer -review process in which authors received anonymous feedback from independent reviewers."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared materials per team (printed box parts, steel ball, magnet), poster templates, and time for presentations and whole-class consensus discussion
#### Constraints
- The article recommends students first read and reflect on the story of the Higgs boson discovery before starting the activities

### Target Learners
- secondary school science students

### Target Learning Goals
- experiencing elements of scientific discovery
- explicit reflection on Nature of Science
