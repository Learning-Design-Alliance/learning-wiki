# Lecture Support with Graphic Organizers

## Description
Instructors present lecture content using graphic organizers rather than standard PowerPoints or outlines, visualizing how concepts are related to each other.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-designed graphic organizers and lecture content adapted for visual presentation.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure all key information is included in the organizer. May not be suitable for all types of content.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who benefit from visual aids and structured presentations.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Help students visualize how concepts are related, improving comprehension and retention during lectures.

### Affordances
- Provides a clear visual structure, enhances comprehension by showing relationships, and makes it easier to follow and retain information.

### Personalization
- Organizers can be adapted to different content areas and levels of complexity. Students can also use organizers to present their own information.
