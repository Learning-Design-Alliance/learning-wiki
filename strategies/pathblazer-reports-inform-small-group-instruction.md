# Use program-generated intervention reports to inform small-group instruction and one-on-one planning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Pathblazer produces intervention reports that "track student progress toward standards mastery, inform small-group instruction, and help teachers plan for one-on-one time with students." The strategy is to treat the program's progress data as a teacher-facing planning tool: teachers monitor student data as work is completed and adapt their classroom instruction accordingly. This pairs the online individualized paths with teacher-led grouping decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need access to the progress data Pathblazer collects as students complete their work
