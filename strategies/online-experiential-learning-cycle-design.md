# Design online course structure and flow around the experiential learning cycle rather than one-off lecturing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's first pedagogical guideline directs online teachers to structure courses as continuous experiential learning cycles: concrete experiences, reflective observation, identification of general principles, and application to further experiences. It states that "online teaching is not one single event of lecturing but continuous facilitation of the experiential learning cycle," with lectures serving as stimuli learners respond to and connect to prior experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need opportunities to connect their experiences with observations, actively engage to test what they learned, and receive expert scaffolding and guidance.

### Target Learners
- medical students, including preclinical and clinical learners

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge creation through cycling through experience, reflection, principle identification, and application
