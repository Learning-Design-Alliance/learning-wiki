# Promote mobile phone etiquette (mobiquette) through health education, workshops, and sleep-limiting apps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that universities promote mobiquette — responsible bedtime mobile device use — among students. They state that "Health education or workshops are beneficial for raising awareness about mobiquette" and that "Sleep applications could help students to set limits on mobile phone use during bedtime", citing Sleep Ninja, a CBT smartphone app for young people's sleep, as an example.
