# Use Bayesian estimation with a weak prior on the between slope to increase maximum power or reduce the required budget when resources are very limited

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that Bayesian estimation can complement optimal design in classroom climate studies. The prior distribution "can be specified in such a way that the variability of the results for the between slope will be small": with a weak prior the variability becomes a factor times the variability in Eq. 5, so "the maximum level of power will be increased given a fixed budget, and the minimum budget required to achieve a prespecified level of power will be reduced." The authors flag this as particularly attractive when a study's budget is very limited and call for further research on its benefits.
