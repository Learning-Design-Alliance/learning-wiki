# Tailor in-class flipped classroom design to students' prior knowledge and the learning context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructional design align with the learning context: bridge new and prior knowledge with cases where students have foundations; explain key points first where students lack prior knowledge; provide preparatory materials for multi-area courses; form groups with "a mix of strong and weak members" so stronger students act as peer instructors; refine videos and cases to be "appropriately challenging but not overly complex"; and use comprehensive evaluation incorporating a variety of assessment methods, targets, and subjects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors assess students' prior knowledge for each skills lab course and prepare cases, videos, and preparatory materials accordingly.
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted at a nursing school in China, which may limit its generalizability to other contexts.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students, including those with poor foundational knowledge

### Target Learning Goals
- active engagement and learning enthusiasm
- knowledge integration and retention
