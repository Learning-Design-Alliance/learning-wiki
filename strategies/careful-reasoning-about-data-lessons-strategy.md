# Teach students careful reasoning about data, using the flawed causal claims in physics-recruitment materials as an object lesson

## Description
The comment argues that the recruitment materials' claim that high MCAT and LSAT scores show physics gives a "competitive edge for Medical and Law School" is "a poor example of reasoning about causation". It concludes that students entering a wide range of fields would benefit most from lessons on careful reasoning about data and from researchers modeling such reasoning.
