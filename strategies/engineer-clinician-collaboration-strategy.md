# Structure sustained engineer-clinician collaboration to bridge the IT-medical gap in hospital learning technology projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors attribute failures of similar projects to "a lack of structured collaboration and mutual understanding between these 2 domains." They adopted an iterative design framework working closely with ED medical staff every month over a multiyear development, and detailed the technical architecture deliberately so hospital IT departments can deploy, maintain, and adapt similar solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional policies at management and IT levels must be considered; dedicated external servers address hospital security concerns
#### Constraints
- Development took around 3000 engineer hours spread over 8 years, partly due to starting from scratch and a COVID-19 interruption

### Target Learners
- Hospital onboarding program developers
- IT and medical staff consortia

### Target Learning Goals
- Successful implementation and sustained adoption of hospital learning technologies
