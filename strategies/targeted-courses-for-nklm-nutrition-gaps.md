# Teach the missing nutrition-counselling topics in communication courses or culinary medicine programs, and deepen breastfeeding counselling in mandatory paediatric specialist training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To close the identified NKLM 2.0 gaps, the authors propose that topics such as nudging, sensitive communication about body weight and systemic counselling "can be taught in a targeted manner in communication courses or as part of culinary medicine programs". They further state that "Mandatory advanced training in paediatrics and adolescent medicine, as well as clear guidelines regarding teaching formats and time requirements, appear to be sensible measures to ensure lasting learning success and effective prevention".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing communication courses or culinary medicine offerings into which the missing topics can be embedded
- specialist training structures in paediatrics and adolescent medicine
#### Constraints
- It is not clear which of the included competencies will remain in the final version of NKLM 3.0

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- paediatricians in specialist training

### Target Learning Goals
- nudging and systemic counselling techniques
- sensitive communication about body weight
- breastfeeding counselling competencies
