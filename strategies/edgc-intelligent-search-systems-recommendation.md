# Develop more detailed and intelligent search systems to support researchers facing excessive publication data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that, because the increase in publications makes datasets harder to handle, "more detailed and intelligent search systems are needed to support researchers". It proposes that software using word analysis/data analysis could generate inferences about samples, methods and technology and share the results with researchers, discoverable through the development of higher-order software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development of higher-order software capable of word analysis/data analysis to generate inferences about samples, method, and technology.
#### Constraints
- The article notes it is not yet evident what kind of findings can be obtained by performing investigations through data analysis.

### Target Learners
- researchers studying educational digital games for children

### Target Learning Goals
