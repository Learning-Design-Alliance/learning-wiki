# Use practice VP cases with feedback for independent self-learning, leveraging that assessment drives learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends making exercise VP cases with feedback available to students for independent practice before the exam. Students receive lists of asked/ordered and missing/not-asked items per case section and a grade rating current achievement, and "students know that they have to practice a large number of tutorial VP cases in order to be well familiar with the VP system". The authors state that learning by exercising the practice cases is the main purpose of the VP exam, and that the web-based practice modality enabled distant learning of patient management during COVID-19 social distancing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A bank of tutorial VP cases open to students for exercise and self-assessment
- Feedback displays of required versus missing items and a per-case grade
#### Constraints
- Novice students tend to ask irrelevant questions and use odd formulations, so familiarity with the system must be built through practice

### Target Learners
- medical students in clinical clerkships
- students studying remotely when access to wards or clinics is restricted

### Target Learning Goals
- patient management skills
- familiarity with the VP exam format
- self-assessment of clinical knowledge
