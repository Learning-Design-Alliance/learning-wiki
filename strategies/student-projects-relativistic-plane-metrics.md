# Assign extension projects on the relativistic plane solutions: transverse geodesics, horizons, wave-equation field trapping, and massless particles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The concluding section proposes assignable investigations that deepen understanding of the two plane metrics. It states: "There are additional projects or exercises that one could assign to students that would help to give a deeper u nderstanding of the physical meaning of the solutions." The enumerated projects are: (i) solving geodesic equations in the x or y directions and reconciling them with tidal-force expectations; (ii) analyzing horizons, including finite proper time to reach z = infinity; (iii) studying wave equations for spin-0, 1/2, 1, and 2 fields to test trapping near the plane; and (iv) redoing the geodesic analysis for massless particles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students should already have worked the base calculations (Einstein tensor and geodesic equations for the two metrics) before attempting the extension projects.
#### Constraints
- The article notes domain wall solutions are not phenomenologically viable, though they can introduce exotic cosmological solutions such as cosmic strings.

### Target Learners
- beginning general relativity students

### Target Learning Goals
- deeper understanding of the physical meaning of exact solutions
- geodesic computation skills
- introduction to horizons and field localization
