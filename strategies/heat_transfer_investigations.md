# Heat Transfer Investigations

## Description
Heat transfer investigations involve exploring how different materials and environments affect the transfer of heat. Learners conduct experiments to determine which materials are best for keeping drinks cool or observing how temperature changes in different environments. These tasks improve understanding of heat transfer principles and their real-world applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Containers made of different materials, thermometers, cold/hot drinks, controlled environments
#### Constraints
- Requires precise measurement and control of variables. May need access to specialized equipment.
