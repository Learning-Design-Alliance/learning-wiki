# Set the minimum adaptive-course workload at one-third of total assignments based on background overlap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because ALICE creates unique learning paths, students complete different assignments and some complete more lessons than others, so a minimum workload must be set. For the Systems Biology course, lessons were roughly one-third each mathematics, computer science, and biology, and students strong in one or two disciplines skip that content. The authors state: "Thus, we require all students to complete at least one-third of the total assignments for the "homework" portion of their grade." Instructors must also decide how to grade assignments and how to encourage or enforce lexia progression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A thorough examination of the design of content in the course should be conducted to decide the minimum number of lessons
#### Constraints
- The baseline assumes students strong in two of three disciplines would take only the third subject's content; the article notes policies should be adjusted if a course requires more substantial extrinsic motivating factors

### Target Learners
- graduate students from mathematics/statistics, computer science, or biology backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- competency attainment across required interdisciplinary content
