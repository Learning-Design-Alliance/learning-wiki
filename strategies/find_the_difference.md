# Find the Difference

## Description
Similar to 'Find the Total', but learners subtract to find the difference between two dot cards. They begin by identifying the card with the greater number of dots and then determine how many more dots are on that card than the other, reinforcing subtraction skills. This activity reinforces subtraction skills in a visual and concrete way. Encourages learners to compare quantities and understand the concept of difference. Supports the development of mental math skills and promotes fluency in subtraction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dot cards.
#### Constraints
- Learners may need initial guidance on subtraction strategies. The activity's effectiveness relies on clear dot arrangements that are easy to count and compare.
