Strategies

Use construct maps in teacher education to locate student teachers' development and identify next steps¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that teacher educators use the TPACK construct maps to understand where teacher education students are in their development of TPACK Confidence and Usefulness, establish goals and next steps for learning, and inform teacher education curriculum development. The authors illustrate this with a student teacher whose TPACK Usefulness is in Band 3 needing to develop "a stronger understanding of the value to students of using technology to develop deep understandings of the topic" to reach Band 4.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to a validated TPACK survey tool and Rasch analysis capability to locate students on the construct maps

Constraints¶

  • The construct maps are a preliminary effort based on self-report data from one survey instrument and one population of Australian practicing teachers

Target Learners¶

  • Teacher educators
  • Pre-service teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing TPACK Confidence and TPACK Usefulness in teacher education programs

Affordances¶

  • Tpack Confidence Usefulness Construct Maps

Claims¶

  • Five bands of proficiency were delineated for TPACK Confidence and five for TPACK Usefulness [+W]
  • Distinguishing feature between middle and higher proficiency bands is confidence/belief in technology for deep understanding and multidisciplinary knowledge [+W]
  • Teachers at higher levels of TPACK proficiency more strongly believe in the value of technology for facilitating deep thinking and learning [+W]
  • Most teachers agreed they were moderately to more than moderately confident/useful but few were extremely confident/useful [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use construct maps to develop and test hypotheses about TPACK development and refine measurement tools
