# Future research and program design: online collaboration activities, in-service ICT training needs follow-up, and investigating characteristics of best blends

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations: developers of teacher preparation programs must carefully identify appropriate ways to apply ICT tools throughout coursework for in-service teachers, and teachers should continue improving their ICT knowledge. The authors recommend research on online collaboration activities to raise awareness of factors related to online group work, determination of in-service training needs with follow-up support for successful use of new technologies, and comparative research into strengths and weaknesses of different ICTs integrated with face-to-face environments to investigate the characteristics of best blends for learning.
