# Phase-specific research-committee activities: setting requirements, monitoring fulfillment and standardization, and conveying a common knowledge base

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article provides checklists (Table 3) of research-committee activities and recommendations per phase. In constructing materials, the committee should "Set specific requirements for the learning and testing environments, scenarios, case vignettes, and (de)briefings" and provide common instruments. During investigations it specifies final targets such as required sample sizes, monitors standardization across projects, and conducts site visits. In the analysis phase it provides reporting guidelines, merges data, applies meta-analytic methods, and creates publications independent of and dependent on project data.
