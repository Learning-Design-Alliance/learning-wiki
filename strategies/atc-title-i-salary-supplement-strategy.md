# Reward service in high-poverty Title I schools with a salary supplement and portable credential for recognized effective teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposed ATC strategy ties tangible rewards to service in high-poverty schools. Members would "receive visible recognition for teaching excellence" and, conditional on serving in high-poverty Title I schools, a salary supplement and a portable credential. The article offers this as an incentive design for retaining effective teachers in disadvantaged schools, not as a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District or state evaluation systems that comply with federal standards to document teacher effectiveness
- Federal legislation to create the ATC
#### Constraints
- Salary supplement and portable credential are conditional on service in high-poverty Title I schools

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in high-poverty Title I schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to highly effective teachers in high-poverty schools
