Train educators through a Summer Institute plus PLC and coaching cycles on data use and instructional routines¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's implementable recipe is to "Conduct a 2-day Summer Institute to train educators and school leaders on data use and instructional routines," then sustain the training with 9 professional learning community and 4 coaching sessions. These use "facilitated discussions, educator observations, and guided materials" so educators reflect on and continuously improve their data use and routines.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Facilitators for PLC and coaching sessions, guided materials, and educator observations

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten and grade 1 educators and school leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Using decision-driven literacy data collection, analysis, and action
  • Effective phonological awareness and phonics instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Build school leaders' instructional leadership through five PLC sessions on school-wide data systems
  • Use a diagnostic and implementation monitoring tool to give educators formative feedback on their instructional practices
  • Two-tier professional development: intensive teacher training with weekly coach follow-up, plus a five-day summer institute and weekly seminars for coaches
  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Support implementation with initial Science of Reading training plus PLC-based lesson study
  • Support implementation with initial Science of Reading training plus PLC-based lesson study and model lessons
  • Deliver professional development through a 2-week summer institute followed by year-long on-site coaching
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Sustained, coached professional development over up to two years for RA implementation
