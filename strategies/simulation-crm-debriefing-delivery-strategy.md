# Deliver NTS training through simulation-based courses with structured debriefing focused on Crisis Resource Management principles, supplemented by in-situ refreshers and visual reminders.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The predominant instructional strategy across included studies was "simulation-based learning, commonly combined with didactic lectures, interactive discussions, and structured debriefing sessions focusing on Crisis Resource Management (CRM) principles". Specific approaches included script-based rehearsal, situation display systems for enhancing situational awareness, structured debriefing using the STOP5 format, and role play or procedural practice. To reinforce learning, certain interventions employed "in-situ refreshers, educational newsletters, and visual reminders".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation-trained faculty and clinical educators from emergency medicine, trauma, or critical care departments to lead delivery
#### Constraints
- Training courses were predominantly short term and delivered within a single working day, with sessions lasting from 1.5 to 7 h.

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals working in emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Teamwork, communication, situational awareness, and leadership skills within emergency resuscitation teams
