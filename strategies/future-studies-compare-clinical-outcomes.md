# Future studies should compare clinical outcomes of students trained with natural versus artificial teeth

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend, similarly to Tchorz et al., that "future studies should compare the clinical outcomes of the students in their clinical activities." This addresses the limitation that the present study assessed only preclinical errors and perceptions without tracking downstream clinical performance. Such studies would help determine whether the differences observed in preclinical error patterns translate to differences in clinical competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tracking students through their clinical endodontic activities after preclinical training
#### Constraints
- The present study used two different classes, so differences in interest and ability between groups could impact results

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical endodontic competence
