# Operationalise the PAOR cycle across Plan, Act, Observe, and Reflect stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PAOR cycle was operationalised across four sequential stages: Plan, Act, Observe, and Reflect. In the Plan stage, baseline findings were presented to local stakeholders and a competency development plan was jointly designed. In the Act stage, five training programmes were implemented in accordance with the model components. In the Observe stage, data were collected before and after each training session alongside qualitative observations and reflective feedback. In the Reflect stage, participatory review meetings were conducted to consolidate lessons learnt and develop recommendations for model refinement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data must be collected before and after each training session alongside qualitative observations and reflective feedback
#### Constraints
- Iterative improvement of the model for application in other settings depends on participatory review meetings among VHVs, key informants, and relevant stakeholders
