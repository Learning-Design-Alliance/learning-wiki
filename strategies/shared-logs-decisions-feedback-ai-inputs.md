# Maintain shared logs of decisions, feedback, and AI inputs to make design decisions visible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The team introduced a decision log recording what changed and why, a feedback log recording what users said over time, and clear rules for AI inputs recording what sources informed outputs. These shared logs created continuity, supported collaboration, explained changes, and let the team refine designs based on evidence rather than memory or opinion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A decision log, a feedback log, and clear rules for AI inputs, maintained as ideas change rapidly
