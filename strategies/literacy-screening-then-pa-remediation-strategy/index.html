Screen foundation-year EFL students for literacy and route low scorers into phonological awareness programs¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that Saudi universities administer EFL literacy tests to foundation-year learners, especially at beginning levels, and admit those who fail into phonological awareness literacy programs before joining EFL courses. It further recommends remedial reading literacy classes for EFL-illiterate learners and teacher training in PA methods, since "intensive exposure to language is barely enough to acquire a language skill".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A brief oral read-aloud screening test (the study used a 100-word A1 CEFR passage scored one mark per correctly read word)
  • Trained teachers and program directors aware of learners' literacy weaknesses

Constraints¶

  • The article states findings can hardly be generalized to non-Arab EFL learners because PA effectiveness depends greatly on the L1 phonological system

Target Learners¶

  • Adult EFL foundation-year university students with very low English literacy

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Basic English word identification and oral reading fluency

Claims¶

  • Intensive exposure to EFL classes alone produced no statistically significant literacy gains in adult learners lacking basic literacy [-W]
  • Explicit phonological awareness instruction significantly improved adult Saudi EFL learners' oral reading literacy from pretest to posttest [+W]
  • Posttest scores of the phonological awareness treatment group were significantly higher than the control group's, with a large effect size [+W]
  • Adult Saudi learners held positive attitudes toward phonological awareness instruction, preferring it to their prior schooling [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Combine translation-based contrastive analysis with phonological-awareness instruction when teaching formulaic sequences
