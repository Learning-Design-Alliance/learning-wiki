# Shift exams from almost 100% multiple-choice to a 50% MC / 50% open-ended mix to diversify reasoning structure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends changing how exams are held so that reasoning structures benefit from the advantages of both learning systems. Its first recommendation is to shift "from almost 100% MC questions to 50% MC questions and 50% OE questions (essays, etc)". Companion recommendations include emphasizing theory-type questions, giving fewer home-works with harder final exams, and increasing high-school Physics and Mathematics course levels.
