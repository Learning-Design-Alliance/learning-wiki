# School counselors set up peer-led small groups where trained students teach STAC strategies to their peers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on observed carry-over effects, the authors recommend that school counselors "set up a formal structure where students trained in the program can teach their peers how to stop bullying by utilizing the STAC strategies." Student leaders from different social groups facilitate small group meetings during the academic year in which peers discuss situations where they used the strategies, brainstorm more effective defending, and build accountability for continued strategy use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student leaders from different social groups to facilitate the small group meetings
- School counselor oversight to structure meetings and sustain an environment of accountability
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of possible carry-over effects, which the authors note may have dampened the study's observed intervention effects

### Target Learners
- junior high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- use the STAC defender strategies to intervene on behalf of bullying victims
