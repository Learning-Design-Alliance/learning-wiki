# Blended Learning

## Description
Blended learning combines face-to-face instruction with online learning through an LMS, offering the advantages of direct interaction and the convenience of eLearning. It involves live training sessions, additional online resources, and assignments to ensure complete understanding. This approach allows for documenting and reporting on training effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LMS that supports a wide range of content formats (Word, PowerPoint, PDF, video, audio). Face-to-face training facilities. Instructors skilled in both in-person and online instruction.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and coordination. May be challenging to implement in resource-constrained environments. Relies on the quality of both in-person and online components.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Suitable for corporate training, higher education, and adult education settings where both in-person and online resources are available.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Combine the benefits of face-to-face and online learning, enhance learner engagement, provide flexibility, document and report on training effectiveness.

### Affordances
- Combines the advantages of direct interaction with the convenience of eLearning. Allows for documenting and reporting on who has taken the training session and how effective it was. Provides flexibility for learners to access resources at their own pace.

### Personalization
- Customize online resources to match individual learner needs. Offer a variety of in-person and online activities to cater to diverse learning preferences. Provide personalized feedback through the LMS.
