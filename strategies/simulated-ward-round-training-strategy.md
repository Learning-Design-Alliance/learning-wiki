# Use simulated ward environments to train and assess surgical ward round processes through repeated safe practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends simulation to improve non-technical skills for undertaking efficient SWRs, via high-fidelity virtual world simulation portals or real-time highly immersive simulated ward environments that have been created and validated to assess SWRs. The key benefit is that it "allows repeated practice in a safe and controlled environment free from compromising the well-being of real patients". A four-bedded simulation ward costs a moderate £5000 initially, with running costs of hiring actors at £25 per hour, comparable to a simulated operating suite.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Initial creation costs (a moderate amount of £5000 for a four-bedded simulation ward) and ongoing actor hire (£25 per hour) must be resourced.
#### Constraints
- The review states checklists and simulation have value for the inexperienced clinician, but the verdict on their universal applicability is still pending.

### Target Learners
- junior surgical residents
- surgical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- leading surgical ward rounds
- non-technical skills for ward-based surgical care
- assessment of ward round performance
