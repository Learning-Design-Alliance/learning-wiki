# Use structured reflection questions in check-ins and board materials to surface leadership distress indicators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The toolkit recommends specific discussion prompts authorizers can use in formal or informal check-ins with board members and school leaders, such as "Can the leader articulate goals and strategies for improvement?" and "Can the leader and the board articulate a leadership succession plan?" It also lists questions for reviewing board meeting minutes about decision-making processes, communication, and role clarity between board members and the school leader.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing check-in and board-report review mechanisms that can be adapted to include the reflection questions
#### Constraints
- How the information is integrated or collected will depend on the authorizer's specific processes and relationships with schools

### Target Learners
- Charter school authorizers

### Target Learning Goals
- Collecting data on leadership indicators of distress through routine interactions
