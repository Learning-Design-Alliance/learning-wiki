# Structured inquiry session sequence: introduction, questioning, investigation, presentations, and synthesis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The activity runs as timed segments: a 25-minute introduction with slideshow, a 30-minute questioning phase in which students play with the models and refine impressions into investigable questions, a 60-minute group investigation with light meter and model, 45-minute oral poster presentations with required audience questions, and a 10-minute synthesis slideshow. Facilitators classified questions as "Investigable Today" and "Not Equipped", and demonstrated that all proposed questions were valuable regardless of eligibility for investigation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator support ("facilitation") to guide students toward desired conclusions
- Table-top model planetary systems and light meters
#### Constraints
- Time constraints limited presentation preparation to a quick skeleton of guidelines and 10-15 minutes

### Target Learners
- introductory college astronomy students in groups of two to four

### Target Learning Goals
- devising investigable science questions
- taking and plotting measurements
- presenting findings clearly and coherently
