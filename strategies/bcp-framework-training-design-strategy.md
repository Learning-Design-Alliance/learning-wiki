# Use the framework to design competence-based education, training programs, and assessment tools for BCP

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the 8 competence domains as clear targets for competence development. Educators can design learning activities, assessments, and supervision practices that systematically build readiness to work in BCP. At an organizational level, the framework may guide training programs, support quality standards for digital tools, and inform service protocols regarding data protection and ethical considerations. Therapists could use self-evaluation scales to assess their strengths and developmental needs.
