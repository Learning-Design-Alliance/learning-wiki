# Teacher-as-facilitator discussions driven by stimulating questions, with explicit promotion of proper scientific language

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The guide's overarching pedagogy is discussion-based: embedded (Q) questions stimulate curiosity and discussion, while "The teacher acts as a facilitator, promoting the good ideas, giving a direction to the discussions and encouraging the students to dare making hypothesis." The guide stresses that during discussions the teacher "should promote the usage of a proper scientific language, with correct terms and verbs throughout the activities."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher willing to act as facilitator rather than lecturer, steering discussions and encouraging hypotheses
#### Constraints
- Stated as essential to achieve the overarching goal of the activities: having students discuss and understand nuclear physics concepts through logics and experiment

### Target Learners
- GCSE students
- A-level students

### Target Learning Goals
- Discuss concepts of nuclear physics
- Develop proper scientific language
