# Use TORMES methodology to elicit from educators the personalized support to be provided in motor skills training

## Description
TORMES is a holistic design methodology the author recommends for eliciting educators' requirements about the personalized support learners training motor skills need. The paper states "educators need to be involved in what is known as an elicitation process" and that TORMES "can facilitate this elicitation process". In the proposed framework, TORMES addresses the support-provision direction: modelling the intervention, deciding the strategy and timing, and delivering personalized support.
