# Extend short-course duration to five or six sessions to spread material and allow more interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on pilot feedback that students had a harder time concentrating by the final hour of each three-hour lecture day, the authors recommend lengthening the course. "in future implementations of the course, it would be advised to extend the duration to five or six sessions in order to spread out the material and give more opportunities for questions and interaction with the Pluto notebooks." They also note students asked for more activity sessions, which had been restricted to two reactive exercises.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course material should be modular so it can be spread across more sessions without restructuring
#### Constraints
- The pilot's compressed three-day format was chosen to develop an accelerated introduction to computational thermodynamics

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate students
- graduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- computational thermodynamics
