# Use existing class combinations as springboards for creative self-listening studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends devoting a small but consistent period of technique class to the creative voice by using locomotor or culminating center-floor combinations as springboards. Students choose a featured quality or skill from the combination, create a short movement essentializing it, and use it as the primary vehicle for an improvisatory study. Alternatively, students infuse a given combination with their own phrasing, quality, timing, and pathway changes, optionally building pair duets that trade movements and include unison, followed by group discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A consistent period of class time; the purpose is tuning in to what the body's voice may wish to express, not making choreographers.
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as combining technical work with the individual voice rather than replacing technique content.

### Target Learners
- dance technique students

### Target Learning Goals
- develop individual creative voice and self-listening rather than other-imitating
