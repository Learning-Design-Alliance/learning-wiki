# Schedule within-group and between-group discussion sessions on epistemological beliefs in science lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend making epistemological discussion sessions a regular feature of science teaching, since group discussion and student-teacher interaction appeared to drive belief change. Their fourth recommendation states that "within and between groups discussions conducted as part of TAM and EETAM are effective in development of epistemological beliefs. Therefore, this type of discussions sessions should find more place in science lessons." Students defended written arguments (claims, warrants, qualifiers, rebuttals) against other groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Heterogeneously grouped students working in groups during lessons
- Activity sheets on which students write claims, warrants, qualifiers or rebuttals
#### Constraints
- Attributed effect rests on the study's comparison and on cited literature, not on an isolated manipulation of discussion format

### Target Learners
- 7th grade science students working in small groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Epistemological beliefs; argumentation skills
