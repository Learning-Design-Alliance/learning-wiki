# Online mid-term check on day 8 of practice to enable flipped learning throughout training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
From April 2021 the department introduced a mid-term check on the 8th day of the 28-day practice, conducted online by one of the two instructors. Students presented the tasks they had been training, the instructor answered questions and instructed them to revise their practical skills, and instruction time was shortened to approximately 1 h. The authors considered it "an essential educational communication tool" even though it did not significantly raise examination scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online system on which 14 students can simultaneously learn content in the time it would take to instruct one student
#### Constraints
- Did not produce a statistically significant increase in examination scores in this retrospective study

### Target Learners
- fourth- and fifth-year medical students in surgical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- revision of practical ligation skills and clarification of ligation concepts
