# Implement an ACS case teaching session combining pre-class self-study, small-group PBL, and simulated MDT role discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable session recipe drawn from the article's experimental group procedure. Before the clerkship, students study lecture videos and supplementary materials, search databases such as PubMed and the university library, and write study notes. In groups of 5 to 6 with a group leader, each member adopts a specialty role and "The group simulated an MDT team discussion, focusing on key issues related to acute coronary syndrome from the perspective of their respective disciplines". During the clerkship, groups integrate patient history, physical examination, and auxiliary test results to discuss diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and treatment, then the group leader presents findings and the instructor evaluates and guides next steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepare lecture videos and supplementary ACS materials in advance and assign pre-class study
- Assign specialty roles (e.g., cardiology, critical care, pharmacy) within groups of 5 to 6
- Provide a supervising teacher during the clerkship and instructor feedback after group presentations
#### Constraints
- The article reports this recipe only for an ACS case in a two-week clerkship; the authors state feasibility in other institutional contexts and the added faculty and student workload require further consideration

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate clinical medicine students on cardiovascular clinical clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnostic and treatment reasoning for ACS
- Interdisciplinary collaboration
- Literature retrieval and self-directed preparation
