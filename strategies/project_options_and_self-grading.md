# Project Options and Self-Grading

## Description
Offer up plenty of options in types of projects and also collaborate with students in creating a criteria chart for each project. Student uses the criteria to grade themselves in the end. Include an opportunity for students to do some reflective writing on why they’ve assigned themselves that grade.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to provide clear guidelines and expectations for projects. Requires learners to collaborate effectively and self-assess their work.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who struggle with self-assessment or who prefer more structured assignments. Requires careful planning to ensure the activity stays focused and productive.
