# Use the ComOn check for time-efficient assessment and feedback of student communication skills in teaching and OSCE contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends applying the ComOn check where quick, structured feedback on communication skills is needed: it permits "on-the-spot ratings during doctor-patient interactions" within 5-10 minutes, provides quantitative five-point Likert ratings sensitive enough to detect small changes after training, and can evaluate students' performance in objective structured clinical examinations. The authors invite readers to freely use the checklist, whose manual with behavior-based anchor points is available on request.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ratings undertaken only by trained expert raters following the instruction manual
#### Constraints
- Applicability in teaching projects, OSCEs, real-life encounters, change-sensitivity over time, and cross-cultural applicability still require evaluation, per the authors' future-research statements

### Target Learners
- medical students in the first clinical year

### Target Learning Goals
- general physician-patient communication skills
