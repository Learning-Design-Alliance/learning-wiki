# Classroom Seating Arrangements

## Description
This strategy involves strategically organizing the physical space of a classroom to maximize student engagement, motivation, participation, and positive relationships. Different arrangements, such as traditional rows, roundtables, horseshoes, or pods, can impact communication patterns, pedagogy, and on-task behavior. In virtual spaces, features like gallery view, breakout rooms, and screen sharing can enhance interaction. The goal is to align the seating arrangement with the instructional goals and activities to support student learning effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adaptable furniture (desks, tables, chairs), planning for space allocation, clear communication with students, and familiarity with virtual platform features
#### Constraints
- Requires physical or virtual space adjustments, may need modifications based on specific classroom constraints, and success depends on clear expectations and routines
