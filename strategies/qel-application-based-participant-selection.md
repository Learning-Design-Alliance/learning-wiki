# Select participants through a rated application process that prioritizes motivation and experience but excludes grades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The QEL limits participation to "5 teams of 5, i.e., 15 technical and 10 business students, who are selected through an application process" in which students submit a CV and ratings are assigned to several attributes to maximize motivation, reliability, and technical skill. Technical roles are rated on quantum focus of their major, time remaining in their degree, and industry or entrepreneurship experience; business roles are rated on extracurricular fit, quantum industry work experience, and startup or founding experience. Notably, the organizers "purposely do not look at grades as these are not useful for forecasting entrepreneurial success."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An application process with CV submission and attribute ratings
- Sufficiently high applicant numbers to support a waiting list
#### Constraints
- The selection process does not prevent technically trained students from taking business roles and vice versa

### Target Learners
- Master's students applying for technical or business roles

### Target Learning Goals
- Ensuring motivated, reliable, skilled team composition for project-based learning
