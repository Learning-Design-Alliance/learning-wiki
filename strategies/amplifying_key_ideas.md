# Amplifying Key Ideas

## Description
Amplifying involves drawing attention to important ideas and concepts, both in the course materials and in student comments or other work. This can be done through highlighting key points, providing summaries, or offering additional examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires clear identification of key concepts and effective communication strategies.
#### Constraints
- May oversimplify complex topics if not done carefully. Requires instructors to prioritize content effectively.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- Highlight important concepts, reinforce key ideas, ensure students focus on essential information

### Affordances
- Helps students prioritize information, reinforces key concepts, and improves comprehension and retention.

### Personalization
- Can be personalized by allowing students to choose which ideas to amplify, providing varied formats (e.g., text, audio, video), and offering additional resources.

- Improved student performance on assessments, increased focus on key concepts in student work, and positive feedback on the clarity of instruction.

## Impact
- Drawing attention to important ideas helps students move from surface learning to a deeper level of engaged learning (Cormier & Siemans, 2010).
