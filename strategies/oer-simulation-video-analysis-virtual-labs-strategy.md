# Use OER-based simulations and video analysis to simulate in-person physics labs remotely

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The manual's core strategy is to replace physical lab equipment with open educational resources: web-browser simulations (PhET vector addition and projectile motion, amrita.olabs Vernier caliper, micrometer and density simulations) and tracker video analysis of student-recorded slow-motion phone videos. The authors state "Virtual experiments were designed to simulate in person physical lab experiments," so students perform the same measurement, uncertainty, and graphing workflow they would in a physical lab. Detailed video tutorials accompany each experiment showing data collection from the simulator and analysis with excel.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a web browser for simulations, tracker software installed on a laptop/PC, a mobile phone capable of slow-motion video recording, and Microsoft Excel (free student copy available)
#### Constraints
- Home-based measurement substitutes are specified when standard instruments are unavailable, e.g. a standard printing paper roughly 27.9 cm long as a measuring reference

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students in remote or web-enhanced courses

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental measurement, uncertainty analysis, graphical data analysis in mechanics topics
