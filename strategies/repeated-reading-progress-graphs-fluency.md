# Repeated reading with individualized text sets and progress graphs for fluency training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Fluency was trained by repeated reading: "During the ﬁrst 6 weeks, one set of six texts with the same number of words and difﬁculty was used." Each week one text was read six times, taking about 5 minutes on first reading, with text length adjusted to the student's reading speed; students' progress was recorded by making graphs of rate and accuracy. A more advanced set of six texts was chosen if improvements were made.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sets of six texts of matched word count and difficulty, with more advanced sets available for students who improve
- Individual adjustment of text length to the student's reading speed
#### Constraints
- Reading speed is described as hard to improve

### Target Learners
- students with poor word decoding and slow reading speed

### Target Learning Goals
- reading fluency
- reading speed
