# Rich Resources: Diversity

## Description
Providing diverse materials in the classroom helps learners deepen their self-understanding and understanding of others, positively impacting their perceptions of themselves and each other. Diverse resources also allow different voices to be heard and validated that may not already exist in the classroom, contributing to a sense of belonging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to diverse materials such as books, articles, videos, and multimedia resources. Requires instructors to intentionally select and integrate these resources into instruction.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort to curate appropriate resources.  Instructors must be mindful of representing diversity authentically and avoiding tokenism or stereotypes.

- Promotes self-esteem, validates diverse experiences, exposes learners to different perspectives, and builds empathy.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by selecting resources relevant to learners' specific cultural backgrounds, interests, and learning levels.  Materials can include books, articles, videos, and multimedia resources.

## Assessment Evidence
- Measure learner engagement with diverse materials, assess learner understanding of diverse perspectives through discussions or written assignments, and track changes in learner attitudes and beliefs related to diversity and inclusion.

## Impact
- Research suggests that diverse representation in educational materials promotes self-esteem, builds empathy, and improves academic outcomes for all learners.
