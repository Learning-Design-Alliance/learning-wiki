# Involve undergraduate and summer students in building small outreach programs as induction projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a strategy in which new students, "during the initial 'induction' phase", are "encouraged to generate a small computer program on a GW subject of their interest", developed as a conventional student project under supervision. The sketches are written in the open-source Processing environment, which "allows students with very different computing-backgrounds to collaborate" and produce graphically impressive results quickly. Successful sketches are published on the gwoptics.org outreach website with instructions and a physics description by the student-author, and are used as interactive tools in seminars and school and university lectures.
