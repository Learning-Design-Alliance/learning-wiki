# Creating Visuals

## Description
When students express information visually, they are activating more cognitive processes while problem solving and increasing their experience with alternate texts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to art supplies or digital tools, supportive environment for creative expression.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle with visual expression, may require additional support.
