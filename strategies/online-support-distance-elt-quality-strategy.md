# Use online support in distance education to improve English language teacher education quality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that "The effective use of online support in distance education can improve the qu ality of English language teaching programmes in Pakistan". The strategy involves providing online study material, tutorials, assignment submission and tutor feedback through a blended computer-mediated communication model. The authors found this enhanced students' performance in terms of access, interaction and cost, and suggest it could minimise cultural and social barriers for females seeking higher education.
