Use video feedback alongside augmented feedback to support self-efficacy when learning a new psychomotor competency skill¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that video feedback, combined with augmented or direct feedback, be used to enhance psychomotor skill learning and self-efficacy. They state "The practical implication from the results of this study is that video feedback can positively influence the self-efficacy of an ATS when learning a new PCS", and argue self-efficacy should be considered in all facets of athletic training education. They also note VFB fits van de Ridder et al.'s elements of strong feedback, including highly specific information and a plan to re-observe.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Video feedback paired with augmented feedback from an assessor
  • An explicit scoring rubric so feedback is specific and criterion-based

Constraints¶

  • Support comes from a small pilot without a control group; the authors report weak support of the research hypothesis

Target Learners¶

  • entry-level master athletic training students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • psychomotor competency skill performance
  • self-efficacy for clinical skills

Claims¶

  • Group self-efficacy means increased across all five questionnaire trials, from a baseline mean of 6.14 to 9.51 post-intervention [+W]
  • Individual students' self-efficacy values did not all increase across trials, with some dropping after the first real-time assessment [~W]
  • No significant self-efficacy differences were found between trial pairs not spanning the video feedback intervention [+W]
  • Prior student experience and possessing a professional credential did not influence students' higher self-efficacy values [+W]
  • Video feedback intervention is associated with increased self-efficacy in athletic training students learning a psychomotor competency skill, with significant pairwise differences only between trials spanning the intervention [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt hierarchical hybrid teaching in vocational education: prioritize hands-on training with step-by-step instructional videos, and use industry-standard operational videos with virtual simulation for complicated tasks
  • Embed recurrent, context-aligned ALS rehearsal with feedback supported by protected training time, accessible infrastructure, and ongoing performance monitoring
