# Use Estimation and Prediction

## Description
Students use estimation and prediction skills. Ask them how they might do on an upcoming project or how long it might take them to complete a task or assignment. They can record their estimates and then reflect on the accuracy of their predictions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks or assignments with clear expectations, recording tools, time for reflection
#### Constraints
- Students may be inaccurate when starting, especially if lacking background knowledge. Requires support, guidance, and ongoing feedback.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who struggle with estimating their performance or time needed for tasks

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve self-awareness, develop realistic expectations, improve time management skills

### Affordances
- Promotes self-awareness by encouraging students to reflect on their past performance and use that information to make predictions about future performance. Improves time management skills and helps students develop realistic expectations.

### Personalization
- Tasks can be adjusted based on student abilities. Focus can be on academic projects or general tasks.

## Assessment Evidence
- Compare student estimates with their actual performance to assess the accuracy of their predictions. Track their ability to improve their estimation skills over time.

## Impact
- Improved self-awareness, realistic expectations, better time management, and increased self-efficacy. Aids development of a Learner Mindset.
