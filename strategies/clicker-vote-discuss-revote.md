# Use a vote–discuss–revote sequence with classroom response systems so students reach more accurate understanding through peer discussion alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A simple, effective talking strategy for even very large classes: ask students to answer a conceptual question via a classroom response system such as clickers or Top Hat, have them discuss responses with neighbors, then revote. The article states this is "effective for helping students come to a more accurate understanding without any instructor intervention," citing Smith et al. (2009). The instructor's job shifts to designing effective questions and structuring the discussion rather than delivering information.
