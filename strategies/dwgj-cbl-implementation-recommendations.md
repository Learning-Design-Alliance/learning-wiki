# When implementing DwGJ-based simulation with CBL, structure teaching hours and expand trained instructor capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations for implementing the combined approach: ensure well-structured allocation of teaching hours and optimized review-session design, enhance faculty development, and assign multiple trained instructors to facilitate simultaneous sessions, "thereby ensuring that all trainees have sufficient opportunities for hands-on practice". The authors also suggest extending the approach to bedside clinical instruction and discussion of complex or challenging cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-structured allocation of teaching hours between practice and debriefing
- Multiple trained instructors available to facilitate simultaneous sessions
#### Constraints
- The study was single-center with a relatively small sample, and most evaluations were self-reported within a simulated instructional context, limiting reproducibility and generalizability

### Target Learners
- resident physicians in standardized residency training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- hands-on clinical skills practice
