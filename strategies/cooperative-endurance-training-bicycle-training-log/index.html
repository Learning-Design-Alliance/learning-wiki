Cooperative group endurance training with rotating bicycle relief and training-log self-reflection¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete implementable activity recipe from the IdT project: within six weeks, "the students were to plan and implement twelve training units in run- ning groups of four both during and after school", creating individual training plans and goals in a training log and reflecting after each unit. Groups coordinated to run five and a half kilometres as quickly as possible, with "every group had one bicycle (and one helmet for safety reasons)" so an exhausted student could ride while others continued running, requiring communication and preventing over- or under-challenge in heterogeneous groups.
