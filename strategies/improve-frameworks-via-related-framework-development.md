# Improve a concepts framework by taking account of how other related frameworks have been developed, evaluated, and made useful

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy directs framework developers to systematically compare their framework with related frameworks and draw on those frameworks' development, evaluation, and use. The article states: "Our review has shown how it and other frameworks can be improved by taking account of the ways in which other related frameworks have been developed, evaluated, and made useful." The review operationalised this by compiling publications describing each framework's development, evaluation, and use as the basis for curricula, teaching and learning, and assessment tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- For each framework, compile publications describing the framework, its development and evaluation, and its use as the basis for curricula, learning resources, and assessment tools
#### Constraints
- The comparison reported here concerns frameworks for teaching or learning to think critically about claims, comparisons, and choices

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing treatment claims
- making informed health choices
