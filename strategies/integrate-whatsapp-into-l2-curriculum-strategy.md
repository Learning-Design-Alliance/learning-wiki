# Integrate mobile messaging applications such as WhatsApp into the L2 curriculum to promote regular-participation learning communities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, to support L2 learners who cannot meet their language acquisition needs within limited class hours, "social networks and mobile messaging applications like WhatsApp can be integrated into the L2 curriculum purposefully and appropriately, in order to promote learning communities where learners can participate on a regular basis." Teachers can use WhatsApp to provide authentic communicative situations and to detect learners' areas of weakness and strength.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Purposeful and appropriate integration into the L2 curriculum rather than unstructured use
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from a small sample of elementary-level learners in non-profit schools in Iran

### Target Learners
- junior high school EFL students
- L2 learners with limited class hours

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary knowledge
- communicative competence
