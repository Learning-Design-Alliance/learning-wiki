# Sequence novice educators' challenges in increasing complexity: individual patient education first, then group education, then individual counseling and follow-ups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Participants described a developmental sequence in which the novice begins with one-to-one education, where it is easier to observe and reflect on one's actions, then proceeds to group patient education requiring moderation of discussions, and finally to individual counseling and follow-ups, which participants said require "longer experience and more extensive education and training". This graduated structure lets competence build stepwise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities to conduct one-to-one education before group settings
- Supervision and feedback at each stage
#### Constraints
- Group education was described as more demanding because the educator must moderate discussions and involve more patients

### Target Learners
- Novice health professional patient educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Progression from individual to group patient education competence
