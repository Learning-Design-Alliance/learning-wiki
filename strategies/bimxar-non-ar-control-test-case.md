## Description
The authors' planned follow-up addresses the pilot's limits: "a test case will be conducted to measure a more detailed performance of BIMxAR and the effects of our approach on the student's knowledge gain using a larger sample size". The non-AR control group will use another BIMxAR version "with similar visualization functions, but without the AR registration feature", isolating the contribution of AR registration.
