The article states that its methods are broadly applicable beyond Head Start. The authors write that "Methods of examining the moderation of preschool impacts by later school experiences are relevant to a wide variety of experimental trials focused on early intervention (e.g., home visiting programs) and early schooling (e.g., reading curriculum in kindergarten)."
