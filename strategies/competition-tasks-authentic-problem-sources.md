# Use physics competition tasks as authentic problem sources spanning beginner to highly complex levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course draws its basic problem sources from primary and secondary school physics competitions, specifically the Physics Olympiad and the Young Physicists' Tournament. "The competitions offer tasks of varying difficulty levels, from interesting tasks suitable for beginner-level students (cat. F of PhO – 7th grade) to highly complex, challenging physics problems in the YPT." The two worked examples show the same Sage capabilities transferable across STEM disciplines.
