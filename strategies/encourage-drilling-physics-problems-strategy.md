# Faculty should encourage students in drilling physics problems and deliver more efficient teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that most students found it unlikely to enjoy learning physics because of their professor, the article recommends that faculty deliver more and efficient teaching, since students seek more than a satisfactory passing grade. It further recommends that "Faculty should encourage students in drilling physics problems", noting students like answering difficult physics questions in examinations and express having a mathematical mind. The department should continue motivating students to learn and study more.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty willing to deliver more efficient teaching and to structure problem-drilling opportunities in physics courses
#### Constraints
- Recommendation arises from a single correlational survey at one university; the article did not test the drilling strategy experimentally

### Target Learners
- engineering and technology students in physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- problem-solving skill in physics
- motivation and enjoyment in learning physics
