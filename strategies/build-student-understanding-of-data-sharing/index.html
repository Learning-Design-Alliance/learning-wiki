Build students' understanding of the purposes and benefits of sharing data before implementing learning analytics¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The commentary recommends "further consideration is needed to build students' understanding of the purposes and benefits of sharing data". Because students are not entirely comfortable sharing the full range of data learning analytics systems need, this strategy addresses the gap between desired system functionality and willingness to share data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Consultative processes that acknowledge difficulties in building a shared understanding and vocabulary with students about learning analytics

Constraints¶

  • Difficulties in building a shared understanding and vocabulary when speaking to students about the possibilities and ethical implications of learning analytics

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • data literacy
  • understanding of learning analytics purposes

Claims¶

  • Students want access to learning analytics systems offering broad information for metacognitive awareness and personalised feedback but are not entirely comfortable sharing the full range of data such systems need [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide in-depth privacy training and transparent communication about learning analytics processes to build user understanding and student trust
  • Address ethics and privacy considerations — transparency, security, student control, accountability, and informed consent — at the initial stages of MMLA design in simulation-based training
  • Involve key stakeholders directly in defining privacy and designing LA systems
