# Integrate structured DBS training, neurosurgery observation, and mentorship into pediatric neurology residency curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a set of curricular strategies to close the DBS knowledge and exposure gaps it documented among Saudi pediatric neurology residents. It proposes "Incorporating structured DBS training into residency curricula, including case-based learning and simulation sessions", alongside neurosurgery collaborations offering observation opportunities and mentorship programs pairing experienced neurologists with junior residents for case discussions and referrals. The recommendation follows directly from the survey's findings of minimal exposure and year-dependent knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires collaboration with neurosurgery departments to provide residents with observation opportunities for DBS procedures, as the article states.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the cross-sectional design, small sample size, and self-reported data prone to recall bias limit the evidence base for these recommendations.

### Target Learners
- pediatric neurology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of DBS indications, procedural knowledge, and clinical decision-making about DBS referral
