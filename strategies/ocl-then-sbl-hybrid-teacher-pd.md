# Sequence OCL before SBL in a hybrid professional development framework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that professional development programmes integrate online collaborative learning and simulation-based learning within a hybrid framework: "an OCL module can be initially used to cultivate theoretical and reflective engagement through peer dialogue, following which SBL sessions can be conducted to allow participants to apply their knowledge in simulated contexts." This sequencing is offered as a way to enhance knowledge retention and transfer and bridge digital learning and classroom implementation. It rests on the study's finding that the two modalities address complementary aspects of teacher training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both modalities must address the same professional development content, as in this study where both groups used identical instructional materials
#### Constraints
- The article examined only short-term effects and states longitudinal designs are required to evaluate sustained effects

### Target Learners
- In-service primary and secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Pedagogical knowledge of health policy content
- Teaching efficacy for medical-resource education
