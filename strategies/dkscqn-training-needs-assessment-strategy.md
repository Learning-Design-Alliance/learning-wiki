# Use the DKSCQ-N to identify training needs and evaluate dysphagia training outcomes in hospital settings.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the DKSCQ-N as a pre- and post-test measure in dysphagia training programs. It states the tool "can help identify training needs across specific domains and support targeted educational interventions" and "can also be used as a pre- and post-test measure in training programs". This supports targeted educational interventions and evaluation of change in knowledge and self-reported competence before and after training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration before and after dysphagia-related training programs to measure change
#### Constraints
- The self-reported competence domain was not externally validated against a competence-specific dysphagia tool

### Target Learners
- Nurses in hospital settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Dysphagia knowledge
- Self-reported dysphagia care competence
