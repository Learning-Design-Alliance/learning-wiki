# Improve the distance education learning environment and raise student satisfaction to enhance academic outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational managers improve the distance education learning environment and increase students' satisfaction with their field of study, because both were related to academic success in their survey. It suggests "holding group discussion meetings to understand educational problems, increase student satisfaction, and create constructive interaction to create an ideal and satisfactory learning environment." This rests on cross-sectional associations, not manipulated interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an operating distance education program whose learning environment can be assessed and improved, and institutional support from educational managers and relevant officials.
#### Constraints
- Evidence is a cross-sectional study of 208 students at a single medical sciences university, so causal effects and generalization to nonmedical universities are not established; the authors recommend longitudinal studies with larger samples.

### Target Learners
- Medical sciences university students (nursing and midwifery, medicine, allied medical sciences, public health) in distance education

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic success, including academic achievement, satisfaction, skills acquisition, persistence, attainment of learning objectives, and career success
