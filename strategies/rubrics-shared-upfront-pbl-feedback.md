# Share evaluation rubrics with students at course start and use them for feedback sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a systematic evaluation approach in which rubrics covering PBL strategies (group work, information searching and quality, hypothesis generation, critical thinking) were given to students at the outset so they knew the performance items to be observed. Tutors completed the rubrics from both face-to-face and online participation, and "the rubrics were also used as guidelines for the feedback the students received in one-to-one and group feedback sessions." Students additionally completed an online survey evaluating tutors and self-evaluating their performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubrics designed around the specific PBL strategies being assessed
- Tutors completing rubrics from observations of both face-to-face meetings and online interactions
