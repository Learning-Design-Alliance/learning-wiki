# Use size-matched randomized designs with baseline testing to isolate immersion effects in VR anatomy studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "future studies should use size‐matched randomized designs with baseline testing and richer qualitative methods to isolate immersion effects". This addresses the limitation that its VR condition was delivered in small groups with greater instructor interaction, while the control was a single large synchronous session, so differences may partly reflect instructor–learner ratio and interactivity rather than immersion alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comparative studies of immersive versus conventional instruction
#### Constraints
- The source study's nonrandomized intact-cohort design precludes causal inference

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- anatomy comprehension and spatial reasoning
