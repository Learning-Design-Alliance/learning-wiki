# On-the-Job Training (OJT)

## Description
On-the-job training (OJT) involves designing experiential activities for groups of trainees, based at the actual location where they will be working. OJT exposes staff to 'business as usual' situations - real customers, peers, supervisors, real products, and services - that they will continue to deal with once training is completed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A real work environment, real customers, peers, supervisors, real products, and services, facilitator to guide the learning process
#### Constraints
- Success depends on the availability of real-world opportunities and the quality of the facilitator.
