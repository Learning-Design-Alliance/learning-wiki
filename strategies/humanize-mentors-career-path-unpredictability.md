# Humanize mentors by having them narrate the stochastic, unplanned character of their career paths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy asks mentors to open their career stories by acknowledging that career destinations often result from a series of opportunities that can appear stochastic rather than from careful deliberation. The article used it to establish mentor credibility while humanizing them, so trainees "realize that there are others who came through the same rocky path, who faced these obstacles, and were able to reach the goal." It targets imposter syndrome and lack of belonging among underrepresented trainees.
