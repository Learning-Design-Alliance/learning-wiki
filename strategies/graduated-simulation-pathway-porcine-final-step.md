# Sequence porcine-model training as the final step of a graduated simulation pathway

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states that "porcine models should represent the final step of a graduated training pathway, following box trainers and virtual reality simulators", and similarly that animal models "should be used as the end of a training program involving box trainers and virtual reality tools". This sequencing concentrates high-cost, high-fidelity animal experience at advanced stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior completion of box-trainer and virtual-reality training stages
#### Constraints
- Animal-based training limited to advanced stages of skill refinement within comprehensive curricula

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- consultant surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- advanced laparoscopic psychomotor skills
- tissue handling
