Staff development should make teaching principles explicit through articulation, reflection and evaluation, supported by institutional incentives and training¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that university teachers articulate their theories and beliefs about teaching, since "making teaching principles explicit is a necessary step before they can be critically evaluated by the teachers". Staff development programmes incorporating "articulation, reflection, and evaluation activities" are said to have the potential to transform teachers' conceptions and practices. At the institutional level, the article stresses policy, technical readiness, infrastructure, and including innovation efforts in performance assessment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional incentives, sound infrastructure, and relevant technical training are considered important conditions for e-learning adoption

Constraints¶

  • Innovations not included in the teacher evaluation system face unwillingness to adopt, as reported by Chinese teachers

Target Learners¶

  • university teachers in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • adoption of e-learning, online collaborative learning, and social-constructivist teaching practices

Related Strategies¶

  • Support faculty AI adoption by engaging beliefs about disciplinary thinking, not only access and technical training
  • Use structured reflection tables, learning journals, prompt variation, adversarial queries, and peer debate to cultivate AI intuition
  • Support the evolving instructor role with professional development beyond product training, articulated role expectations, and communities of practice
  • Know Your Own Perspectives
  • I Used To Think Now I Think
  • Design teacher training on inclusive education to combine belief reflection with practical experience in inclusive classrooms
