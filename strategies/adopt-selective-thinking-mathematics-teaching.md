# Adopt the selective thinking strategy in mathematics teaching and train teachers in its steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author's post-study recommendation set: use selective thinking in mathematics teaching, extend interest in the strategy to teaching generally, include it in mathematics teaching-methods courses, and have mathematics teachers and university professors teach on the basis of the strategy's steps. The article recommends "The use of selective thinking in the teaching of mathematics because of its great benefits in achievement and the development of thinking and organizing scientific material strategy". Implementation includes practical, scientifically grounded training for students and teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The strategy be included within the course in methods of teaching mathematics and trained by students and teachers through practical training
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single quasi-experiment with 74 classroom-teacher students at one university, conducted in the second semester of academic year 2013/2014

### Target Learners
- classroom-teacher students in university mathematics courses
- mathematics teachers and university professors

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of basic mathematical concepts
- development of thinking
