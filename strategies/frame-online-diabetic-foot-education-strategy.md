# Adopt interactive online diabetic foot education such as the FRAME program to build and maintain stratification skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend an Australian diabetic foot education programme, such as "the interactive online diabetic foot training offered by the Scottish Diabetes Group and University of Edinburgh’s Foot Risk Awareness and Management Education (FRAME) program", to support healthcare professionals to develop and maintain examination skills and competencies. Telehealth education and the Indigenous Diabetic Foot Program are named as further alternatives requiring evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to "interactive online diabetic foot training" or telehealth education delivery
#### Constraints
- The article states these alternative approaches need to be trialled and assessed; their effectiveness in this population is not yet evaluated

### Target Learners
- Generalist healthcare professionals in rural and remote practice

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing and maintaining diabetic foot examination skills and competencies
