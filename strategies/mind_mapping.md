# Mind Mapping

## Description
Mind mapping is a visual thinking tool used to capture information and ideas. It helps learners capture and organize ideas and thoughts in a structured form. By connecting ideas via structures and branches, learners uncover new connections and discover new ideas and lines of reasoning. They can also help students learn visually and creatively by visualizing information in formats that are compatible with their favorite learning styles.
