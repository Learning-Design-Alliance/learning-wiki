# Daily Review

## Description
Daily review is a strategy where instructors begin lessons with a short (5-10 minute) review of previously studied material. This regular practice strengthens neural connections and helps learners retain information. Reviews focus on key concepts, important vocabulary, math or science formulas, homework, and problem areas identified by learners. Creative approaches like flashcards, pop quizzes, games, and mnemonics can make daily review engaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 5–10 minutes of class time at the beginning of each lesson. Requires preparation of review materials and creative activities.
#### Constraints
- If too much time is spent on review or if every point is reiterated, it can become ineffective. The key is understanding what to review and using time efficiently.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Reinforce learning, improve retention, strengthen neural connections, prepare learners for new content by reviewing previously learned topics

### Affordances
- Strengthens memory through repetition, reinforces key concepts, and creates a concrete base for further learning. When combined with creative techniques, it can enhance learner engagement.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to focus on specific problem areas identified by learners. Activities can be modified to suit different learning styles.

- Improved performance on quizzes and tests, increased retention of key concepts, and better understanding of current material due to a stronger foundation.
