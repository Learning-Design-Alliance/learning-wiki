# Teachers use the ADD to locate students' discernment level and craft teaching that supports crossing category boundaries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that after using the ADD to assess students' disciplinary knowledge, teachers attain new insights into creating more effective learning environments by explicitly crafting teaching to support crossing boundaries in the ADD model. "teachers need to find out where their students are in the ADD and then teach them to discern relevant disciplinary affordances of representations". The teacher's role is framed via Hattie's visible learning: teachers see learning through the eyes of students and help them become their own teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The lecturer must first determine what students discern, e.g. by organising students' written answers using the ADD
#### Constraints
- The article notes guidance is often required, since constructivist discovery alone may not be sufficient

### Target Learners
- university science students, particularly astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- progressing through the ADD levels of disciplinary discernment of representations
