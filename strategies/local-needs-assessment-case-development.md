# Develop simulation cases from an informal needs assessment of local ED census and staff input, keeping cases uncomplicated for simulation-naive learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors developed cases by reviewing the monthly patient census and discussing with local ED staff to cover the most common scenarios seen in the department, plus one airway procedure lab requested by staff. Cases were "purposefully kept uncomplicated as learners did not have simulation or role-playing experience." This grounds case selection in local epidemiology and learner readiness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Review of local patient census data and discussion with local ED staff to select case topics
#### Constraints
- Cases were kept uncomplicated because learners lacked prior simulation or role-playing experience

### Target Learners
- Medical officers
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Managing common local emergency presentations
