# Interleave a narrated video with live commentary and Kahoot! quizzes to engage online audiences and surface misconceptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In synchronous online events the researchers showed the video in parts, commenting between segments, and ran Kahoot! multiple-choice quizzes that participants answered from their mobile phones. They report the quizzes let people answer "without the fear of being judged", kept attention high, and brought out common misconceptions about seasons and the astronomical noon.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A synchronous online venue with a chat or microphone channel for interaction, and a game-based quiz platform participants can reach from their own devices
#### Constraints
- The authors' engagement reports are observational impressions from their own events, not comparative measurements of learning gains

### Target Learners
- general public
- high school students
- pre-service primary teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement and attention during online lessons
- surfacing misconceptions about seasons and the astronomical noon
