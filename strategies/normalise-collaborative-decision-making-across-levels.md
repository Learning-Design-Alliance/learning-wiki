# Normalise collaborative decision making and meaning making across upper secondary, undergraduate and postgraduate learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes with a forward-looking recommendation: "Measures and processes that normalise collaborative decision making and collaborative meaning making should become part of all upper secondary, undergraduate and postgraduate learning across the globe." This reorients knowledge discussion from adversarial critical thinking toward inclusive collaboration and emergent creativity, preparing students as creative agents for complex, intractable problems.
