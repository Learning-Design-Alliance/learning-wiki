# Make scientific programming a second core subject across all three years of the science-and-technology branch

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that the science and technology branch of Portuguese secondary education add "a second core subject, in addition to mathematics, spanning all three years: scientific programming, using languages such as Python, Octave or Julia". It argues this replaces a weak optional 12th-grade subject and builds the entry skills the author's supervision experience associates with better undergraduate outcomes. It acknowledges it requires a major retraining effort for science teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large training effort, since most Portuguese physics and other science teachers currently cannot code; teachers must retrain or be replaced
#### Constraints
- The author's stated evidence is personal supervision experience and international comparison; Portugal is described as more than a decade behind other countries in programming

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in the science and technology branch (grades 10-12)

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific programming skills
- applied computation in science
