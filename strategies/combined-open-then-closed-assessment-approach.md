# Use a combined assessment approach: allow AI and internet access for an initial information-gathering phase, then shift to closed-book for independent synthesis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes a staged assessment design as a way of advancing assessment in the LLM era. Students could initially be allowed access to AI and the internet for a defined period to gather factual information, after which "the examination could shift to a closed‐book format", requiring students to independently synthesise and articulate their understanding. The review frames this as a candidate combined approach warranting future research rather than a tested design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A defined initial period of AI and internet access, followed by a closed-book synthesis phase.
- Institutional capability to support secure testing platforms and quality assurance processes.
#### Constraints
- The review presents this as a proposal for future research, not an evaluated intervention.

### Target Learners
- University STEM students
- Medical school programme students

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent synthesis of information
- Articulating understanding without AI assistance
