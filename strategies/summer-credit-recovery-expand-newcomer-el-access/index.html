Offer summer credit recovery to expand newcomer English learners' access to academic subjects¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article investigates a summer credit recovery program aimed at expanding high school newcomer ELs' access to academic subjects, in which students recover or earn credits outside the regular school year. The study reports that "Credit recovery increased the number of math, English Language Arts, science, and social science classes taken by newcomer EL students," making summer credit recovery an implementable lever for increasing newcomer ELs' academic content exposure.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A district credit-bearing summer program that newcomer EL students can attend to recover or accrue course credit
  • Student-level course-taking data to monitor which subjects students take

Constraints¶

  • Graduation-rate benefits were imprecisely estimated in the study
  • English proficiency effects were described only as suggestive

Target Learners¶

  • High school newcomer English learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Increased access to and completion of academic content courses in math, English Language Arts, science, and social science
