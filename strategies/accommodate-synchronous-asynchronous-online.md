# Accommodate both synchronous and asynchronous online learning, matched to purpose

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review's second guideline holds that online learning has two technologically different processes based on timeliness of interactivity, and that "it is important to accommodate these two online learning activities for appropriate purposes." Asynchronous modes give learners pace control and reflection time; synchronous modes build social presence and support real-time feedback and discussion of prepared topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must weigh the pros and cons of each mode: synchronous activities have a limited timeframe leaving little time to reflect, so complex topics may need recorded lectures plus asynchronous discussion forums.
