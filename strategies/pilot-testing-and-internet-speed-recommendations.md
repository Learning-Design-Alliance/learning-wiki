# Include internet speed as a technology item and pilot-test to detect ceiling effects in future online-learning environment studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend two design improvements for future studies of online learning environments. First, pilot testing is recommended because "The ceiling effect occurs when a variable's scores approach their maximum", and their single-campus cohort may have produced one. Second, internet speed should be added as a technology-related item, since it was omitted from the technology scale in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should conduct pilot testing before data collection and include internet speed as a technology-related item
#### Constraints
- Collapsing categories to reduce ceiling effects may result in loss of information and power, per the authors

### Target Learners
- undergraduate university students in online learning

### Target Learning Goals
- accurately measuring environmental and technology factors affecting online learning outcomes
