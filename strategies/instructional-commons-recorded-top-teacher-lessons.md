# Instructional Commons: record and disseminate top teachers' full-year instruction for peer-to-peer professional use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief proposes identifying the best teacher for a given subject and grade, paying them to have "their entire year of teaching for that subject recorded," then digitizing the videos and supporting materials — lesson plans, worksheets, quizzes — for other teachers to use. It builds on earlier lesson-plan repositories and offers "a peer-to-peer training model, the opportunity for teachers to observe high quality instruction in depth," plus a common standard for professional development at relatively modest cost.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reliable estimates of teacher performance, drawing on student-level standardized test data over multiple cohorts plus assessments from principals, peers and other experts
#### Constraints
- Where supply of high-need students outstrips the supply of high-impact teachers, this second option is positioned as an alternative to incentive-based class expansion

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers seeking to improve instructional quality, and their students

### Target Learning Goals
- Raising the pace of learning by improving quality of instruction
