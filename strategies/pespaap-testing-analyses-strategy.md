# Test PESPAAP with mediation models, SEM, and compositional data analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends specific analytic approaches for testing the proposed model. It states that "The link between parent physical activity engagement, child and adolescent physical activity, and academic performance can be tested using a mediation model", with direct, indirect, and total effects quantified including the percentage of total effect mediated. It further recommends structural equation modeling with structural and measurement components, reversing direction arrows to test feedback mechanisms, and compositional data analysis within a constrained time-use framework for sedentary behavior and physical activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires measurement of parent physical activity engagement, child physical activity (and motivational correlates), parent academic support, and academic performance outcomes; longitudinal data are recommended for stronger evidence of directionality
#### Constraints
- The article notes no research has yet used CoDa to examine associations of lifestyle compositions with academic performance facets or within a mediated mechanism linking parent engagement and academic performance
