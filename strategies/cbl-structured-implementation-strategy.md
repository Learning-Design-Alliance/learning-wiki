# Implement faculty development programs, systematic curriculum integration, and student orientation to maximize case-based learning benefits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that medical schools implement faculty development programs for skilled facilitation, integrate CBL systematically throughout the curriculum, and provide student orientation sessions on collaborative learning expectations. They also recommend enhancing access to technological tools and library resources, maintaining manageable group sizes, and establishing regular feedback and evaluation mechanisms to continuously refine CBL activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development programs to ensure consistent and skilled facilitation
- Adequate technological tools, virtual cases, and library resources for case preparation
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a single institution and may not generalize to other medical schools

### Target Learners
- Clinical-year medical students (third to fifth year)

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Critical thinking
- Problem-solving
- Communication skills
- Teamwork
