Apply moderation-of-later-school-quality methods to other early intervention and early schooling trials, such as home visiting programs and kindergarten reading curricula¶

Description¶

The article states that its methods are broadly applicable beyond Head Start. The authors write that "Methods of examining the moderation of preschool impacts by later school experiences are relevant to a wide variety of experimental trials focused on early intervention (e.g., home visiting programs) and early schooling (e.g., reading curriculum in kindergarten)." The three-step process is intended to be made relevant to other applications.
