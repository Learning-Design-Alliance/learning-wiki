# Learning Logs

## Description
Learning logs are short, reflective writing activities completed at the beginning or end of a class to review previously learned material or focus on new concepts. Learners respond to a prompt in writing for a few minutes, focusing on mathematics. Prompts can include definitions, examples, personal connections, or summaries of key concepts. The activity encourages active engagement with the material and the construction of meaning from facts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a few minutes at the beginning or end of a lesson, a specific prompt, and a way for students to record their thoughts (e.g., notebooks, digital documents).
#### Constraints
- Quality depends on the clarity of the prompt and learners' willingness to engage thoughtfully. Requires time to review and respond to entries to be most effective.
