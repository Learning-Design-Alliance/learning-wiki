# Design AI-assisted activities that require students to explain reasoning and evaluate AI outputs against disciplinary criteria

## Description
The article recommends that teachers mitigate the risks of student overreliance on generative AI by designing activities that "require students to explain their reasoning, compare AI-generated responses to disciplinary criteria, identify errors or unsupported claims, revise outputs based on evidence, and reflect on when AI help was useful or erroneous". This positions AI as a scaffold for inquiry and revision rather than a shortcut around thinking.
