# Provide early rehabilitative activities and special training modules adapted to the child from early school life for children with bilateral cataract

## Description
The authors recommend that children with bilateral cataract, who more often have systemic diseases and neurodevelopmental disorders, receive rehabilitative activities and special training modules adapted to the child from early school life, alongside early intervention and comprehensive support systems. They state that "rehabilitative activities and special training modules adapted to the child from early school life may play a crucial role in how well they perform in school". The recommendation rests on the observed mathematics deficit among bilaterally operated children and on cited comorbidity findings.
