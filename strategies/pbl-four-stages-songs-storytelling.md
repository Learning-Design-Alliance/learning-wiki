# Implement problem-based learning through four stages, adapted for primary children with songs and storytelling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing PBL through "reviewing and presenting problems, developing strategies, implementing the strategy, and discussing and evaluating the results" (Eggen and Kauchak, 2012), and adds that for primary-age children it can be delivered through fun methods such as singing and storytelling, citing Levenberg (2015) on integrating songs and stories in mathematics to cultivate literacy and overcome learning difficulties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problems must be presented and reviewed before strategy development begins
#### Constraints
- Adaptation via songs and storytelling is offered as an expectation for engaging primary students, not as a tested manipulation in this study
