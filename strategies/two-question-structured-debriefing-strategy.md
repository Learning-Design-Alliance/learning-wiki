# Two-question structured debriefing after each simulation scenario

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After each scenario's closure, the researcher-facilitator asks participants two questions: what positive actions were taken, and what would you do differently on another occasion. The article reports that "debriefing was a key point for the development of the capacity to rescue critical-reflective reasoning about PIs", contributing to a positive self-image and improved skills during care practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher or facilitator to conduct the debriefing with the group after each scenario
- Participants who observed a colleague act as the volunteer performer in the simulation
#### Constraints
- The article describes this debriefing within realistic clinical simulation for nursing teams; it does not report testing the debriefing separately from the full five-phase program

### Target Learners
- nursing professionals in in-service training
- nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical-reflective reasoning about pressure injury care
- identifying successes and opportunities for improvement in clinical performance

### Affordances
- Five Phase Realistic Simulation Pi Program
