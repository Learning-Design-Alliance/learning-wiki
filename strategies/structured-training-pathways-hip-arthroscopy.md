# Use structured training pathways with supervised early practice and experience-informed patient counselling in hip arthroscopy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's clinical implications translate its learning-curve findings into a training approach: hip arthroscopy "can be performed safely during the early learning phase under appropriate supervision", while continued experience yields efficiency gains. The authors recommend "structured training pathways, careful case selection during early practice and more informed patient counselling regarding expected outcomes and surgeon experience", and point to simulation-based training and standardized surgical pathways as future directions.

## Design Implications

### Context
