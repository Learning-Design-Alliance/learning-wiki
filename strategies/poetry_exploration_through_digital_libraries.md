# Poetry Exploration through Digital Libraries

## Description
Using digital libraries with audio and visual components to engage learners with a variety of poems. Learners can browse poems based on category or age, enhancing their appreciation and understanding of poetry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to digital libraries with a diverse collection of poems, devices for learners to access the library (e.g., tablets, computers).
#### Constraints
- Requires access to technology and digital resources, may be less effective for learners who prefer traditional print materials.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All ages, adaptable for elementary students through adult learners.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance appreciation for poetry, develop literacy skills, improve understanding of poetic devices and themes.

### Affordances
- Provides access to a wide range of poems, supports differentiated learning through varied categories and age levels, and enhances engagement through audio and visual elements.

### Personalization
- Allows learners to choose poems based on their interests and reading levels, providing a tailored and engaging learning experience.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess student's engagement with the digital library, their ability to select and analyze poems, and their understanding of poetic devices and themes. Monitor participation in discussions and written reflections on the poems.

## Impact
- Research indicates that integrating digital resources and multimedia can enhance learner engagement and understanding in literacy education. (Source: International Literacy Association)
