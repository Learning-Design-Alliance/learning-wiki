# Procedural simulation instructional design strategy: mastery learning, process feedback, and competency decay prevention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of instructional design recommendations for procedural simulation: clearly define objectives per procedure, choose the modality (part-task trainer or hybrid with a simulated participant), vary task characteristics, provide a short clinical vignette for context, give process feedback on the entire procedure rather than solely on individual movements or overall outcome, use validated assessment scales, and prevent competency decay. The article recommends to "schedule skill maintenance sessions every 6 to 12 months, and use mental imagery practice before or after sessions to mentally rehearse the procedure."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Free access to the simulator once learning is achieved, with specific feedback and targeted objectives for deliberate practice
- Assessment after each step in a mastery learning approach
