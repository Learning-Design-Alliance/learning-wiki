Toolbox assignments applying taught reading strategies directly to the paired content course material¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

After in-class demonstration of strategies such as vocabulary development systems, information processing, textbook reading, exam preparation, metacognition, and critical thinking, students complete a toolbox assignment applying the learned strategies to the environmental science textbook for the next class. The article reports that "Students enjoyed these assignments and reported a positive impact on their environmental science scores." Appendix examples include SEE-I vocabulary cards, a self-scored exam-readiness activity, and Socratic questioning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A paired content course whose material the strategies can be applied to
  • Direct instruction of each strategy with an in-class demonstration before application

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • under-prepared first-year college students in a reading support course

Target Learning Goals¶

  • information processing and study strategies
  • metacognition
  • critical thinking about course texts

Affordances¶

  • Paired Course Dual Doing Becoming Model

Claims¶

  • Under-prepared students in a paired reading-plus-content course model made statistically significant pre-to-post gains on six of seven ELLI lifelong learning dimensions, with Learning Relationships the exception [+W]
  • Paired-course students achieved an 84% success rate in environmental science and an 84% fall-to-spring retention rate versus 73% for a similar-SAT comparison group [+W]
  • Students were highly satisfied with the paired course model and its components, with 100% willing to advise a new student to take the paired courses [+W]
