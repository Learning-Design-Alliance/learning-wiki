# Embed a brief purpose-reflection prompt and worked example into an existing course-search platform

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (1 for, 4 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates a low-cost intervention that reframes a course catalog's search prompt and search example so students articulate an interest with a purpose ('I want to learn about… because…'). Worked examples served as scaffolding: "Many students (40.2%) wrote their search queries according to the example format." The strategy integrates reflection into an authentic learning activity rather than a separate program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing digital platform with a search prompt that students actually use during course selection
- An example search query as a worked example to scaffold query writing
#### Constraints
- The intervention only engaged students once they started writing a query but may cause students to opt out of searching in the first place
- Primarily influenced new users rather than existing users
- May be undesirable in contexts prioritizing linear academic progress for students at risk of dropping out

### Target Learners
- college students, especially new users of a course catalog

### Target Learning Goals
- deliberate interest exploration during course selection
- connecting purpose to academic choices
