# Reallocate course hours based on evaluation results: reduce classical dynamics, expand BOA, add state-to-state reaction content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the evaluation results, the author proposes three revisions. First, "the module of classical dynamics will be reduced to about five academic hours" by combining points II and III and shortening point IV, to "largely reduce the learning curve for students in chemistry". Second, two academic hours will be added to the BOA module with more details and examples, while the familiar photochemistry point is cut to 0.5 hours and a geometry-phase example added. Third, points XXV and XXVI are combined as "reduction of the mechanism" using a combustion case study, and a new state-to-state reaction point is added.
