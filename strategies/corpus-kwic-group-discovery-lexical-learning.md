# Corpus-based group discovery of word collocations using KWIC concordance lines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
This is a learner-centered activity sequence in which students receive target lexical items, type a word into the corpus, display concordance lines in KWIC format, observe the lines to identify collocations, meaning, and part of speech, then discuss and report findings to the class. The article states students "become active 'digger' of vocabulary" rather than passive receivers of lexical information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short orientation on using the Wikipedia Corpus before beginning lexical teaching and learning.
#### Constraints
- The strategy was less effective for low-level learners, who found the new method too challenging.

### Target Learners
- First-year university English majors in mainland China

### Target Learning Goals
- Lexical acquisition: word meaning, part of speech, collocations, and commonly used expressions
