# Adopt the spiritual care training protocol as a reference for clinical nursing managers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that hospital nursing leadership implement this training protocol to develop nurses' spiritual care knowledge and skills: "Clinical nursing managers should use this protocol as a reference to help clinical nurses improve their spiritual health." They add that the protocol "should also be used to improve nurses' spiritual care knowledge and skills to enable them to better satisfy patients' spiritual needs and improve their nursing quality," and that it might be worth additional promotion among nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert lecturers, an intervention team, protected workday training time approved by nursing management, and monthly case-sharing and book-discussion structures
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness was verified in senior nurses (mostly over five to seven years of experience) at a single cancer hospital; the authors state uncertainty remains about less experienced nurses

### Target Learners
- Clinical nurses in oncology and palliative care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Spiritual care competency
- Spiritual health of nurses
