# Operationalise the topologies through a multi-paradigm, mixed-methods enquiry strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes how to put the topological approach into action: it fits "into the interpretive (holistic) and socio-critical paradigms", starts from "paradigmatic pluralism as the most suitable option", and, following the assumption of incommensurability, "the proposed methodology should combine both qualitative and quantitative methods". Research questions must align with understanding pedagogy in practice and teaching practice impact through concrete learning activities, grasping post-digital experiences across multiple layers of sociomateriality.
