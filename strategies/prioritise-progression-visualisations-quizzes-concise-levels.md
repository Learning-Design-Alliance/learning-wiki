# Prioritise progression, interactive visualisations, optional formative quizzes, and concise level design in gamified QC resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' design recommendation from their lessons learned: "Gamified QC learning resources should prioritise progression, interactive visualisations, optional formative quizzes, and concise level design." Interactive visualisations with immediate feedback helped participants understand complex concepts such as quantum gates and motivated continued play; optional in-game quizzes with immediate feedback supported learning and motivation; matching difficulty to prior knowledge can prevent frustration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mini-games should match students' prior knowledge to avoid frustration
#### Constraints
- Derived from a small, no-control study with 11 participants; the authors note these are lessons learned rather than tested comparisons

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students new to quantum computing

### Target Learning Goals
- improved motivation, engagement, and learning outcomes for introductory QC concepts
