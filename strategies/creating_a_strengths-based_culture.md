# Creating a Strengths-Based Culture

## Description
A method for empowering learners by focusing on their strengths rather than their weaknesses, creating authentic learning experiences that enable them to develop skills and talents. This approach maximizes motivation, contribution, and impact by building on assets rather than focusing on deficits. To create a strengths-based culture, instructors can have learners identify and discuss strengths with peers, celebrate strengths in action, identify goals and determine how strengths will be used to achieve them, allow learners to work on passion projects, have experts of the week share talents, create a wall celebrating strengths, model strengths, vary assessment, and reflect on how strengths are used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for learners to share and discuss their strengths, materials for passion projects, varied assessment options
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset and a willingness to focus on learners’ strengths rather than deficits. May be challenging to implement in a classroom with diverse abilities and needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Increased learner confidence, engagement, and motivation. Positive feedback from learners and peers. Improved collaboration and communication.

## Impact
- Learners are more confident, passionate, and do better work when you focus on what’s right with them instead of what’s wrong with them. Creating authentic learning experiences that empower learners to develop the skills and talents to manage themselves and build on their assets, rather than focus on their deficits, maximizes the motivation, contribution, and impact of all learners.
