# Professional societies can sustain education-outreach partnerships nationally by gathering data on existing partnerships, providing a knowledge repository, and convening meetings

## Description
The article assigns professional societies such as the AAS three supporting roles: gathering national-scale data on what education/outreach/community partnerships already exist; providing "arepositoryofknowledgeand initiativesinorderforthevarious collaborations tolearnfromeachother'sexperiences" to reduce likely effort duplication; and convening or sponsoring professional meetings with emphasis on the education/outreach interface.
