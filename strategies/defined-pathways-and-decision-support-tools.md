# Clearly defined clinical pathways, accessible decision-support tools and localised protocols for risk assessment and escalation should be introduced for personality disorder management.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy holds that rather than speculative solutions such as flexible scheduling, the study's findings support the introduction of clearly defined clinical pathways and accessible decision-support tools for personality disorder management. The development of localised protocols for risk assessment and escalation may help to reduce practitioner anxiety when managing patients in distress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined clinical pathways for personality disorder management
- Accessible decision-support tools and localised protocols for risk assessment and escalation
#### Constraints
- Participants described unclear referral processes and the absence of formal support structures; regional variation exists

### Target Learners
- qualified advanced nurse practitioners
- postgraduate nursing students in Advanced Nurse Practitioner pathways
