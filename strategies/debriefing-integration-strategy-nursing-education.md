# Integrate structured debriefing into simulation curricula with systematic teacher training and multi-method evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "nursing education institutions should commit to developing standardized debriefing operational guidelines and implementing systematic teacher training" focusing on "creating a safe psychological environment, applying heuristic questioning techniques, and connecting theory with practice." They further suggest integrating structured debriefing principles into clinical post-conference discussions, encouraging reflective journals, and adopting a multi-method evaluation approach including OSCE and debriefing logs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized debriefing operational guidelines
- Systematic teacher training incorporating PEARLS-based teaching exercises
- Sufficient time allocated for debriefing within the simulation curriculum
#### Constraints
- Students with weaker foundational knowledge may have more limited outcomes
- Introverted students or those less accustomed to discussion-based learning may need additional support

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students
- Nursing educators and facilitators

### Target Learning Goals
- Higher-order thinking development
- Transfer of reflective competencies from simulation to clinical practice
- Critical thinking across disciplines
