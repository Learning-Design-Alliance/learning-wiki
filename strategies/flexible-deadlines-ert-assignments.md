# Design assignments with flexible deadlines during ERT to accommodate learners' increased obligations in crisis times

## Description
The article recommends designing assignments with more flexible deadlines in ERT because learners' obligations with family, jobs, and other engagements are likely to increase during crisis times such as COVID-19. Flexible deadlines, along with accommodating learners' lived experiences and context, reduce unnecessary stress and support learners who may face mental and physical health issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor willingness to relax fixed assignment deadlines
#### Constraints
- Intended for crisis or emergency contexts where learner obligations increase

### Target Learners
- university students in emergency remote teaching

### Target Learning Goals
- reduce stress
- support learners managing family and work obligations
