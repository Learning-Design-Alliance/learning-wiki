## Description
The authors recommend concrete curricular placements for the module given typical time pressures in high school courses. The text states: "Given the usual constraints on teaching time, these materials could be used after the AP exams, in an extracurricular club, or as an independent project resource to give students a taste of what quantum computing is really about." This positions the module as enrichment content rather than a replacement for the standard physics curriculum.
