# Complement existing writing instruction with a pre-class textual simulation plus curated reading materials and mentor forums

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a workflow in which administrators complement existing scientific writing courses by preparing users with study material (peer-reviewed articles on scientific writing, slides and videos, structure-content templates, recommended books), delivering the simulation test via a link and login, and then scoring results and emailing them back for self-evaluation. Blogs and forums let users post questions and experiences and let administrators give individual-level guidance, addressing the fact that users may not be co-located. The authors recommend tailoring training to the needs of various specialties where possible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrators with the senior-researcher/mentor profile to design material and tests; users to review the provided guidance material before attempting the simulation
#### Constraints
- Users must be computer literate; the simulation addresses deterministic (structural) aspects of writing rather than topic-dependent content

### Target Learners
- Novice researchers and medical students in scientific writing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Manuscript structure and content placement before formal writing instruction
