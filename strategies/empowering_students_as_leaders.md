# Empowering Students as Leaders

## Description
Identify students with challenging behaviors who exhibit leadership qualities. Assign these students leadership roles within learning groups, such as group facilitator, timekeeper, or outcome checker. Pair these student leaders with others who struggle academically. This strategy leverages the leadership potential of disruptive students while providing academic support to those who need it, fostering on-task behavior and improving test scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires identification of student leaders, careful pairing of students, clear role definitions, and monitoring of group dynamics.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure all learners are engaged and supported. Needs clear expectations to prevent misuse of power or off-task behavior.

### Affordances
- Engages students with challenging behaviors in a positive way. Provides academic support to struggling learners. Improves classroom dynamics. Develops leadership and communication skills.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying leadership roles, pairing students with different needs, and providing specific tasks aligned to individual goals.
