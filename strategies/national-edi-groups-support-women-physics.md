## Description
The article describes a set of Canadian national groups working for equity in STEM, including the CAP Division for Gender Equity in Physics, the Canadian Conference for Undergraduate Women in Physics, the CAP EDI Committee, and the Laurier Center for Women in Science. These groups "work to support women and other equity-seeking groups to pursue the study of physics via opportunities to speak about their research, attend informative workshops and panel discussions, and network". Some experimental collaborations, including nEXO and EIC Canada, have incorporated EDI into their governance.
