# Use RTOP evaluation dissemination sessions with modeled exemplar teaching to develop reformed practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
After the first RTOP observation, the project held an RTOP Evaluation Dissemination session in which teachers shared experience and asked follow-up questions about each RTOP category and item. The team "felt that the process of identifying each item, addressing strengths and weaknesses in the overall group and modeling exemplar teacher performance" would help teachers develop pedagogical content knowledge and more effective mathematics instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained RTOP observers and completed pre-observations so that strengths and weaknesses can be addressed in the group session
#### Constraints
- RTOP observations and teacher comments continued to demonstrate the need to model and explicitly identify reformed teaching practices

### Target Learners
- High school mathematics teachers in professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- Reformed, inquiry-based classroom practice
- Pedagogical content knowledge in mathematics

## Claims

- [Participating teachers' classroom practice became significantly more reformed and inquiry-based over the PD year, as measured by RTOP](../claims/modeling-pd-improves-reformed-practice-rtop.md) [+M]
- [Teachers' RTOP scores correlated moderately-to-highly with their post-test content knowledge (Spearman rho = .50, p = .007)](../claims/rtop-content-knowledge-correlation.md) [+M]
