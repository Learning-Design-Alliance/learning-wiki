# Have paraprofessional home visitors trained on MB deliver the intervention to clients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the fidelity findings, the authors recommend that home visiting programs feel comfortable having paraprofessional home visitors trained on the Mothers and Babies curriculum deliver the intervention to clients. This positions paraprofessionals as a feasible, cost-effective alternative for delivering CBT-based preventive interventions in underserved communities, since most HV models already employ paraprofessionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paraprofessional facilitators receive 8–12 hours of training on the MB group intervention plus supervision during their first cohort
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that the null arm comparison was not a formal equivalence test, and subgroup findings on education and training modality cannot be deemed confirmatory

### Target Learners
- Pregnant and postpartum clients of home visiting programs in underserved communities

### Target Learning Goals
- Delivery of a perinatal depression preventive intervention with adequate fidelity
