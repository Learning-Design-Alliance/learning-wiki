Strategies

Combine summer professional development with school-year on-site coaching and provision of complete instructional materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's implementation model pairs a 2-week summer training session with on-site coaching during the school year and provision of all necessary materials in rotating kits. This combination supports teachers in shifting to hands-on, inquiry-based instruction, which the teacher survey linked to reported student engagement and increased active learning use.

  • on-site coaching during the school year
  • provision of all necessary materials including guides, assessments, software, and manipulatives
