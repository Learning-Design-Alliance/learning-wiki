# Future direct ophthalmoscopy simulation development should improve realism and address affordability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future developments in direct ophthalmoscopy simulation models address the challenges it identified. It states "it is important to ensure that simulation models enact a high level of realism", involving mannequin heads or spherical eyeball models that allow practising the manoeuvring involved in clinical examination. It also recommends that "newer simulation devices ... attempt to limit their cost to consumers with measures that do not compromise the effectiveness of simulation, such as by providing lower fidelity mannequins", noting 3D printing as a cost-effective approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation models should enact a high level of realism, including physical resemblance and how the exercise unfolds
- Newer devices should limit cost to consumers without compromising simulation effectiveness
#### Constraints
- Some existing models may still not be affordable in lower income societies where direct ophthalmoscopy is a required clinical skill

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Junior doctors

### Target Learning Goals
- Direct ophthalmoscopy technique
- Fundus pathology identification
