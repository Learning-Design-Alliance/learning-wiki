# Adopt a mixed mode combining classroom and e-learning for post-graduate health-professions education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that students proposed maintaining e-learning in the future by blending it with face-to-face teaching. A student suggested "a mixed mode", doing "something in the classroom and a part can handle it in e-learning", finding it "very interesting as a tool, also for the future". The authors add that e-learning could be used as a tool to enhance face-to-face teaching (T1 code 3.12).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology platforms and recorded content that students can access and review repeatedly
#### Constraints
- Students noted there had not yet been enough time to approach the mixed mode fully

### Target Learners
- Working post-graduate health-professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reconciling work and study while maintaining quality of educational life
