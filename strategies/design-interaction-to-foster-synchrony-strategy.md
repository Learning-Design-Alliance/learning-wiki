# Teachers should design interaction processes carefully to foster synchronization in learning behaviors and neural activities

## Description
The article closes with a concrete recommendation for future teaching activities: teachers should plan their interaction processes deliberately and work to engage students' attention. It states that "teachers should design their interaction processes with students carefully and scientiﬁcally and strive to catch students' attention and engage them, thereby fostering synchronization in learning behaviors and neural activities between teachers and students." This translates the study's IBS findings into practice guidance.
