# Teaching Academic Vocabulary

## Description
Explicitly teach mathematical academic vocabulary, including math-specific terms and the difference between mathematical and common definitions of words. Introduce vocabulary within the context of the content. Use scaffolds such as visuals, real objects, graphic organizers, and anchor charts. Offer opportunities to work with objects and images. Ask learners which strategies support their learning. Demonstrate that vocabulary can have multiple meanings and help learners understand how to use them correctly in a mathematical context. Draw on learners' home languages and use cognates, translated words, and encourage the use of their languages in group work.
