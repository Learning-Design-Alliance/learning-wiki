# Incorporating non-Western cosmologies into ngVLA education through indigenous talks, STEAM projects, and Star Parties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table 1 lists opportunities "to incorporate non-Western cosmologies into the education about the ngVLA." Proposed activities include inviting indigenous community members, both experts and local, to organized talks about indigenous cosmology; STEAM projects such as murals, stories, and videos to capture non-Western cosmologies in local schools and community centers; collaboration with SACNAS to engage experts and students, with a plan to publish comparisons of western and indigenous cosmologies in the context of the ngVLA locations; and Star Parties that incorporate different cosmologies.
