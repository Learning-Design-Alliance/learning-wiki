# Collect LGBT+ demographic and climate data only with anonymization and misuse safeguards, keeping it separate from personnel decisions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Guide treats data collection as "a vital component of diversity efforts" and urges departments to make demographic forms inclusive (sexual orientation, gender identity, non-binary write-in options, domestic partnership status) and to distribute research surveys widely given "a dearth of data on the numbers of LGBT+ physicists and astronomers." It pairs this with strict safeguards: "Survey responses should be anonymized; forms with these questions should clearly indicate what will happen to the data so that respondents can feel conﬁdent about how their answers will be used," and all such data should be separated from hiring, awards, and promotion decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear communication to respondents about how answers will be used
- Ability to prevent misuse and assure respondents of their privacy
#### Constraints
- The Guide states: do not collect this information if you cannot both prevent its misuse and assure respondents of their privacy

### Target Learners
- prospective and current students
- job applicants and department members

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing departmental and classroom climate for LGBT+ members to guide policy and improvement
