# Peer support and teacher scaffolds (planetarium software, planetarium visit, laser pointer) to secure the Polaris-finding prerequisite

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the field measurement, the article recommends organising the activity as a group experience in which students support each other, because 'Finding Polaris is a prerequisite for the success of this activity'. The teacher then scaffolds the sky-finding step by presenting planetarium software for practice, visiting a planetarium, and using a laser pointer during the field experiment, so that weak prior astronomy knowledge does not block the latitude measurement.
