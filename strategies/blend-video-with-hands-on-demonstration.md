# Blend video-based instruction with hands-on skill demonstration in nursing skills education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on findings that video teaching matched demonstration on knowledge and most skills while demonstration yielded higher satisfaction, the authors recommend combining the methods. They write that "Supplementing traditional skills demonstration with video is a promising strategy to provide a quality experience in nursing specialties such as operating theatre nursing", and recommend adopting a combination of teaching methods to increase students' skill development and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to prepared skill-focused videos alongside laboratory demonstration sessions
#### Constraints
- Satisfaction was higher for demonstration alone in this study, so the blend is recommended partly to address engagement, not because the hybrid was tested

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate nursing students
- perioperative nursing trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical aseptic skills
- psychomotor skill acquisition
- student engagement and satisfaction
