# Leaderboards

## Description
Leaderboards showcase the distribution of point totals that learners have accumulated through various learning activities. Competition can motivate learners and can be leveraged by leaderboards. To avoid discouraging students who are performing poorly, instructors can consider using a system in which learners see only the two students who are directly above them and below them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use of digital tools to track and display scores. Clear criteria for earning points. Careful monitoring to ensure fair competition.
#### Constraints
- Can be demotivating for learners at the bottom. May lead to a focus on competition rather than learning. Requires careful management to avoid creating a negative classroom environment.

### Affordances
- Leverages competitive spirit to drive engagement. Provides a clear visual representation of learner progress. Encourages learners to strive for higher achievement.

### Personalization
- Instructors can choose to display only a limited range of scores. Learners can focus on improving their own performance rather than comparing themselves to others.

## Impact
- Displaying all learners in order of point totals can be a disincentive for learners at the bottom.
