# Guest speaker career narratives to broaden students' sense of physics career paths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the IceCube AfterSchool program, eight speakers gave guest lectures on their IceCube work, with topics ranging from neutrino oscillations to cosmic ray detection to science communication. The talks included descriptions of presenters' careers so students could appreciate the multiple paths to success and the variety of careers involving physics. Several speakers "emphasized how their career journeys shifted as they matured", encouraging interns to imagine themselves as scientists even when unsure how to get there.
