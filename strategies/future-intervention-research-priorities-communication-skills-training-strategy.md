# Target future intervention research at teacher characteristics, cost-effectiveness, curriculum timing, information transfer, and skill generalisation across time and settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review identifies several areas where new intervention research should focus attention. It suggests "Understanding the characteristics/qualities of teachers that are the most successful in skills training may help to enhance transferability of skills to students", noting students may respond better if taught by a practicing clinician. It also recommends examining cost-effectiveness, understanding when skills should be administered throughout the program, ensuring a focus on information transfer as well as gathering, and examining whether skills generalise from undergraduate to resident to practicing clinician and across clinical settings.
