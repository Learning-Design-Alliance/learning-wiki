# Establish an Austrian-wide Competency Catalog for clinical training with supervisor accountability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that each student "must successfully complete a defined number of case-based competencies by the end of their training, specific for each medical field and department", trained in patient contact using evidence-based clinical methods. A physician supervisor takes responsibility for each student's completion of the catalog, and an Austrian-wide catalog would standardize training practices and ameliorate skill deficits. It is offered as a systematic framework to ameliorate the low-rated subcategories of supervision and team integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A physician supervisor responsible for verifying each student's completion of the catalog
- Competencies trained in patient contact using evidence-based clinical methods
#### Constraints
- The article notes a cost–benefit analysis should be performed before implementing the suggested changes

### Target Learners
- Clinical medical students (clerkship, rotation, internship year) at the Medical University of Vienna

### Target Learning Goals
- Case-based clinical competencies specific to each medical field and department
- Integration of declarative and procedural clinical knowledge
