# Increase assessor awareness of stereotype bias and provide bias training for simulated patients and examiners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that assessment systems act on demonstrated race-related scoring differences by raising awareness and training assessors: "It is essential to increase awareness of such bias and provide appropriate training for SPs and examiners." The recommendation rests on the observed lower scores for the black fail candidate and the authors' concern that a lower failing score could alter pass/fail decisions across clinical assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of the specific pattern of bias found (negative at clear fail, positive at borderline)
- Training provision for both simulated patients and examiner staff
#### Constraints
- The article does not evaluate any training intervention; effectiveness is untested

### Target Learners
- Simulated patients and clinical examiners in undergraduate medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- Fair, unbiased scoring of candidates' communication and professional skills
