# Use cultural informants from the target community to buffer against stereotyping and bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes cultural informants—members of the target audience who assist and inform design decisions—as a culturally pluralistic design specification. The informant 'acts as a buffer to inadvertently stereotyping or presenting biased perspectives' and helps ensure accurate portrayal of traditions, language and community interests. With the informant's assistance, designers can foster communication and understanding between the design team and the target audience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The cultural informant is usually a member of the community being designed for
#### Constraints
- Designers must still consider multiple interpretations and perspectives that can influence the design process

### Target Learners
- Instructional designers working with culturally diverse learner communities

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate, non-stereotyped portrayal of the target community in instructional materials
