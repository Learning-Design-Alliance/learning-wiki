## Description
The article recommends that AI Anxiety be tackled through coordinated interventions across four domains. It states: "To address these challenges, we propose multidisciplinary solutions, offering insights into educational, technological, regulatory, and ethical guidelines." For educational settings this implies pairing instruction about AI with technological safeguards, regulation, and ethical norms rather than treating anxiety as a purely pedagogical problem.
