# Foster supportive classroom environments and monitor academic emotions to improve English learning outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that "teachers should foster a supportive classroom environment, as caring behavior enhances academic performance by promoting positive emotions (e.g., EJ) and reducing negative ones (e.g., AX and BR)." Specific strategies include using inclusive language, providing personalized feedback, designing engaging activities like group discussions or role-playing, and regularly assessing students' emotional states to guide teaching adjustments.
