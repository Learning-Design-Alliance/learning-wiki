Virtual Argumentation strategy integrating Google Classroom, videos, sentence openers, prompt questions and instructor feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The VA strategy is a six-week online argumentation process in which teacher candidates draft and revise arguments about socio-scientific issues on Google Classroom, supported by "sentence openers, prompt questions, and videos" plus instructor feedback. Videos introduce both sides of each issue and are also used as feedback. The four tools had not previously been used together in an online environment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor feedback and expertise to remedy deficiencies of draft arguments
  • Videos selected from scientific channels covering both sides of each SSI

Constraints¶

  • The proposed VA strategy is not capable of enhancing the science teacher candidates' SHOM levels
  • GC platform was purposefully used; future studies with different platforms are warranted

Target Learners¶

  • science teacher candidates

Target Learning Goals¶

  • argumentation skills
  • scientific habits of mind

Claims¶

  • VA strategy does not enhance teacher candidates' SHOM; scores slightly and non-significantly decrease [-W]
  • VA strategy significantly improves teacher candidates' argumentation skills with large effect sizes [+W]
