# Provide immediate formative feedback from faculty and embedded participants after each OSCE station

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each station reserves 5 of its 20 minutes for feedback delivered by the embedded participant and faculty observer, informed by station-specific checklists. The authors argue this immediacy was essential: "incorporating immediate feedback from faculty was essential, as feedback timing is a commonly included element in feedback best practices." Faculty evaluators were instructed to incorporate verbal feedback from the embedded participant into their written assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Station-specific checklists for faculty observers to ensure structured feedback on educational objectives
- Scheduling faculty, fellows, and senior residents as observers and embedded participants
#### Constraints
- A recorded session would require fewer synchronous resources, but the authors believe immediate feedback was essential

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Core clinical communication skills: consults, informed consent, sign-out, and responding to nursing pages
