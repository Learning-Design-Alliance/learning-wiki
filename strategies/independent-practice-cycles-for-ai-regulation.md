# Provide opportunities for independent practice with repeated cycles of use and structured reflection to strengthen regulatory skills over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because regulation of cognition developed more slowly than knowledge under experiential learning, the article recommends that practitioners "should rather offer spaces for independent use in which students can refine their regulatory behaviour through repeated cycles of use and structured reflection." Teachers are told not to be discouraged when experiential exercises do not produce immediate regulated practice, since these activities often need time to take effect. The strategy extends a single intervention into ongoing opportunities for self-regulatory practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for independent practice must allow students to gradually strengthen self-regulatory skills through repeated engagement and guided reflection.
#### Constraints
- Simply encouraging more GenAI use is unlikely to close the gap between understanding of strategies and their translation into practice.

### Target Learners
- first-year university engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive regulation of cognition in human-AI collaboration
