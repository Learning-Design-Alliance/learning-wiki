# Route AI reasoning through interpreted cognition reports rather than raw behavioural logs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that AI agents should not reason directly from learner interaction traces; instead, "Learner interactions should first undergo cognition-level interpretation through AI Tutor analysis and multi-layer aggregation mechanisms," after which AI Twins reason through expert-readable dashboards. This design strategy positions cognition reports as the interface between behavioural data and adaptive intervention in AI-native education systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI Tutor interpretation layer and multi-level dashboard aggregation must exist before AI Twin reasoning
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a proposal for future AI-native systems, not an evaluated implementation

### Target Learners
- learners in AI-driven educational ecosystems

### Target Learning Goals
- adaptive intervention and expert-like pedagogical reasoning about learner development
