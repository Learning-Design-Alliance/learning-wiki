Assign guided or scaffolded note-taking to be completed while watching lecture videos to keep video viewing active rather than passive¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Instructors in the case study courses assigned guided lecture outlines or note-taking activities that students completed while watching recorded lectures and submitted for credit. A biology instructor explained that students "never extract the information" from videos alone, so questions about the script made the assignment "go from passive to active." The geology instructor used the submitted outlines to track engagement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor time to prepare guided outlines or note-taking prompts and to review submitted assignments

Constraints¶

  • Creating materials in smaller chunks placed a burden on instructors already impacted by the pandemic

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate students in remote or online STEM courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Extracting information from lecture videos and maintaining steady engagement with course content
