# Use blockchain ID plus official identification to enable borderless assessment participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that students can take an assessment at any university in the world without any further formalities: it is sufficient to show the blockchain ID and an official identification document to ensure identity. This would significantly reduce the bureaucratic obstacles to a stay abroad, changing the university or switching the course of study.
