# Embed ED problems in parallel with scaffolded lab experiments using physics principles

## Description
The article describes how its course integrated ED into the laboratory component in 2019, structuring "the problem to facilitate the application of physics concepts" across three units on momentum, energy, and angular momentum. Student teams identify the problem context, generate ideas using relevant physics knowledge, complete scaffolded laboratory activities in parallel, develop plans, apply scientific knowledge, exchange ideas, receive graduate teaching assistant feedback, iterate, and present final solutions in lab reports. The ED problem is client-driven and includes constraints such as cost, time, materials, and tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying fundamental physics principles (momentum, energy, angular momentum) to authentic design challenges
