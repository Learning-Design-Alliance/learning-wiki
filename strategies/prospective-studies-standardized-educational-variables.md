# Call for prospective studies with standardized clinical parameters, case-complexity assessment, and structured educational variables

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that future research on trainee performance should move beyond retrospective descriptive designs. It states that "Future prospective studies incorporating standardized clinical parameters, case complexity assessment, and structured educational variables are needed" to clarify how operator training level relates to clinical outcomes. This implies collecting variables unavailable retrospectively, such as ferrule height, endodontic quality, restoration type, and supervision intensity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prospective design capturing standardized clinical parameters and case complexity at treatment time
- Structured recording of educational variables such as supervision and case allocation
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a need arising because retrospective records could not consistently capture these factors, not as a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- postgraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating clinical competence development in restorative dentistry training
