# Use a pupil-plays-teacher error-correction game format for language testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each test presents a naturalistic composition seeded with typical language errors, and the pupil corrects it as if grading another pupil's work: "Correct by drawing a single line through words or expressions used incorrectly, and placing the correct words above them." This game framing yields a scorable count of rights while engaging pupils in editing authentic-sounding writing rather than answering isolated grammar items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Directions read aloud by the teacher with pupils following; a worked example ('He has went home' corrected to 'gone') is provided in the directions
#### Constraints
- Scoring counts rights only; the author notes directions were to be revised and simplified later

### Target Learners
- School pupils in grades III to XII

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognition and correction of incorrect words and expressions in written English
