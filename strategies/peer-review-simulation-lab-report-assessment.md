# Simulate the peer-review process for lab reports as part of assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After writing a detailed group report (one per pair or trinomial, several weeks allowed, inter-group exchange encouraged), students exchanged reports between teams and each assessed the strengths and weaknesses of classmates' work; teams could then respond to comments and revise. The teaching team evaluated reports before and after review, and evaluated the relevancy of each team's review, focusing final evaluation on how accurately comments were answered and translated into manuscript improvements. Students were also strongly encouraged to reproduce observations numerically in Matlab or clones, with no ready-to-use code provided.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reports exchanged between teams with time to respond and revise
- Teaching-team evaluation at both stages plus evaluation of review relevancy
- Students' prior experience in other modules sufficient for autonomous numerical work
#### Constraints
- No ready-to-use code was provided for the numerical validation task

### Target Learners
- Master's-level physics students working in pairs or trinomials

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific writing and revision
- critical evaluation of peers' experimental work
- numerical reproduction and validation of experimental observations
