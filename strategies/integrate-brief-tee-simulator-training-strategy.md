# Integrate brief simulator-based TEE training into undergraduate medical curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing TEE simulator training into medical curricula, noting the protocol teaches 4 standard views and is "on average, less than 30 min long" to address the barrier of insufficient curriculum time. The strategy involves pre-training video instruction, a live simulator demonstration by ultrasound-trained faculty, brief hands-on practice, and a skills assessment. The authors suggest this could assist educators in implementing TEE training effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ultrasound-trained faculty availability
- Access to a TEE simulator
#### Constraints
- Simulator availability and cost limit generalizability at schools without resources

### Target Learners
- Medical students without prior TEE instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- Develop TEE knowledge, confidence, and basic skills
