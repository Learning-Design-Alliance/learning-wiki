# Gradually withdraw teacher authority and provide detailed step-by-step guidelines in cooperative writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the teacher's participation in cooperative writing be withdrawn gradually, because students in China and other Asian countries see the teacher as authority and need detailed guidelines to accept cooperative writing as formal instruction. The teacher should "set sufficiently detailed guidelines for each step of the writing process" and even check with group members to make discussion effective and efficient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed guidelines for each step of the writing process
- Teacher checks with group members to make group discussion effective and efficient
#### Constraints
- In Chinese and other Asian contexts, students may not feel safe or satisfied until the teacher reads through their writings sentence by sentence, so withdrawal must be gradual

### Target Learners
- EFL vocational/polytechnic students
- students in teacher-authority-centered cultures

### Target Learning Goals
- career English writing competence
- effective group discussion in cooperative writing
