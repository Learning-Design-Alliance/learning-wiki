# Brain-Based Learning

## Description
Activating the different parts of the brain involved with the various senses during the learning process to engage learners with content, help them learn and retain more. Students get opportunities in class to get involved and connect with the content by using different songs, actions, and/or activities. Giving students time to process the information and rest their brain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Minimal preparation. Need to ensure content is taught in small chunks of time.
#### Constraints
- It can be intimidating to bring the ideas immediately into the classroom.
