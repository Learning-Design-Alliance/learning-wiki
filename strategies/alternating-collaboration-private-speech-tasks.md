# Alternate collaborative and private speech tasks across paragraphs in a reading lesson

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific instructional procedure: after the teacher reads a passage, students are asked to perform tasks such as paraphrasing, summarizing, and discussing ideas. For every other paragraph, tasks are done collaboratively; on alternating paragraphs, students work through private speech. The article states "if the students were asked to collaboratively paraphrase the first paragraph, they were asked to paraphrase the second one by themselves, i.e. practice private speech."
