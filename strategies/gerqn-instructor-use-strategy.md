# Use the GERQN to identify learning objectives, track cohort performance, and analyze items for areas of difficulty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends instructors use the GERQN to inform PQL instruction, since "[t]he GERQN can help identify meaningful learning objectives, track shifts in student performance over time, and highlight changes in specific skills through item-by-item analysis". Suggested practices include monitoring average scores across cohorts and analyzing individual items to identify areas of difficulty. Aligning early PQL development with students' recent math coursework may boost confidence and agency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration data must be interpreted with the 10-minute engagement cutoff and item-by-item analysis of scored responses
#### Constraints
- The article states no single assessment can fully address the longstanding challenges students face in developing PQL

### Target Learners
- students in algebra-based introductory physics, especially students from lower SES districts with limited access to physics and calculus in high school

### Target Learning Goals
- physics quantitative literacy development across sign reasoning, covariation, and proportionality
