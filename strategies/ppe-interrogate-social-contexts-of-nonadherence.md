# Use PPE tutoring to have students interrogate social contexts of non-adherence alongside biomedical implications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This implementable activity recipe comes from the paper's worked example: in a case featuring a diabetic patient who is 'non-adherent' to treatment, PPE tutors would help students interrogate social contexts shaping follow-through with prescribed treatment regimens, in addition to biomedical implications of poor glycaemic control. Students would also be encouraged to forge habits of reflexive practice that last throughout their careers.
