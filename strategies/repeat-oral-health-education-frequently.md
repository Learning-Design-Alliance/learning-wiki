# Repeat oral health education frequently and reinforce it with parental involvement and preventive measures to sustain effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because improved behavior, attitude and hygiene were temporary, the article recommends that oral health education "should be repeated with either method in order to maintain its positive results longitudinally". It further suggests the program "could be reinforced with parents' involvement in the educational process and other preventive measures such as tooth brushing and fluoride treatment", and could be used in earlier age groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs delivered by trained teachers; repetition over time to maintain effects
#### Constraints
- The article states the improved oral health attitude and behavior were not sustained in the long-term by either program

### Target Learners
- Primary school children

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained oral health behavior, attitude and hygiene
