# Employ a comprehensive local assessment model instead of relying solely on standardized test scores

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that schools employ a more comprehensive assessment model at the local level, listing five steps: "identifying and defining local issues and establishing strategic priorities"; piloting new interventions or programs; taking systematic measurements of successes and failures; analyzing data and committing to evidence-based improvement and action; and selecting an indicator that signals whether the intervention or strategy has been effective. They argue this makes it easier for administrators, teachers, community members, and parents to fight for what is essentially important for students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local identification of issues and strategic priorities
- Systematic measurement of successes and failures
- A selected indicator signaling whether the intervention was effective
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a local-level alternative in response to a case where a narrow test-score focus devastated school climate; it does not report testing this model

### Target Learners
- Junior high school students
- School administrators and teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Student motivation and school climate
- Meaningful learning beyond test scores
