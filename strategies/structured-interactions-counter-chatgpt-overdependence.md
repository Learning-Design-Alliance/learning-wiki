# Structure classroom interactions and diversify information sources to counter students' over-dependence on ChatGPT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Pre-service teachers proposed two interrelated remedies for over-dependence on ChatGPT: diversifying information sources beyond ChatGPT (teachers, peers, textbooks, and ChatGPT itself for diverse viewpoints), and structuring learning processes around classroom interactions. Examples include educating students about "characteristics of ChatGPT" and critical thinking before use, structured group discussions during use, and requiring experiments to verify ChatGPT-aided predictions after use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher guidance so students maintain critical thinking and compare ChatGPT's answers to other information sources
#### Constraints
- Remedies were proposed in lesson plans and surveys, not tested in executed classrooms; the article calls for empirical examination in real-world classrooms

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
