# Randomly assign students to treatment and control sections when course-level interventions can enroll large numbers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that claims about course innovations be tested with randomized experiments rather than observational comparisons. With large enrollments, the author argues, random assignment is feasible: "Pennebaker et al. ( 2013) could have easily designed a randomized experiment to test the effectiveness of the SMOCs." He notes almost one thousand students enrolled in the introductory psychology course each semester, making it easy to randomly assign half to a SMOC or control face-to-face section.
