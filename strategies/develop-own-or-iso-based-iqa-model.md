# Nursing educational institutions should develop their own instructional quality assurance models or adopt an ISO-based model like CU-QA84

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the HLM finding that own-developed models and the CU-QA84 model positively affected instructional quality, the article recommends: "Nursing educational institutions should develop their own models of instructional quality assurance, in accordance with own application, or develop models of instructional quality assurance from ISO basis as CU-QA84 model." Own-developed models suit institutional context and aims, while CU-QA84 is based on the ISO system with indicators, measurement and a distinctive assurance process. Institutions should also evaluate needs of stakeholders alongside external evaluation results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Needs evaluation concept applied to the individual context of each institution, as flexible as needed
#### Constraints
- Adapting general quality assurance models without regard to the specific professional context of nursing education was judged inadequate in the Thai context

### Target Learners
- nursing instructors and academic administrators in nursing educational institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- instructional quality assurance planning and institutional quality development
