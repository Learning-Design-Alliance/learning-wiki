# Tailor feedback strategies by question type: discourage MCQ trial-and-error and scaffold open-ended revision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the divergent engagement patterns by question type, the authors recommend differentiating feedback design: "For MCQs, systems should discourage superficial trial-and-error behaviors and focus on guiding conceptual understanding. For OEQs, structured, targeted scaffolding can foster deeper engagement and higher-quality revisions." They also suggest learning analytics consider multimodal indicators such as revision quality to capture meaningful engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback generation that can condition on question type and on the learner's specific answer
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-session crowdsourced study with one topic; the OEQ submission trend was not statistically significant

### Target Learners
- online learners completing practice activities with MCQs and open-ended questions

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding in multiple-choice practice and higher-quality revision of open-ended responses
