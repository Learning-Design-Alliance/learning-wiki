# Implement Photovoice with team-based learning as a two-stage activity: individual photo documentation with SHOWeD reflection, then group discussion and class presentation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete two-stage classroom recipe. In stage one, students photograph three community-level factors affecting child health and reflect using the SHOWeD questions ("What do you see here?" through "What can be done about it?") in a structured template. In stage two, students form groups of four to six, share and classify photos, and group leaders present 7–10 minute summaries to the class while the instructor provides feedback and reinforces key concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need mobile phones, cameras, or tablets for photography and a structured Word template for documenting photos and SHOWeD reflections
- Groups of four to six members with a selected group leader responsible for completing the form, managing time, and facilitating discussion
#### Constraints
- Photographing in public spaces and of children caused discomfort and ethical hesitation; unequal participation and time management difficulties arose in group work

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in a pediatric nursing course

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding social determinants of child health
- teamwork and collaboration
- critical thinking
- engagement and motivation
