# Establish ethical safeguards and debrief participants when piloting LLM tools with learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that any experimental pilot of LLM agents with learners establish ethical safeguards and "debrief participants on the tool's actual capabilities to mitigate such dangers" of bias or misinformation among impressionable learners. Responsible informed consent and moderated testing limit vulnerabilities and produce better risk-benefit assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Informed consent procedures and moderation of testing sessions
#### Constraints
- Aimed at pilots with vulnerable or impressionable learner populations

### Target Learners
- adult students participating in educational AI pilots

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and informed use of AI reading assistants
