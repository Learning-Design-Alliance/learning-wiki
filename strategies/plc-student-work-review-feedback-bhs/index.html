Use professional learning communities to review student work and give teachers feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A strategy in which teachers meet regularly in small interdisciplinary groups to analyze the quality of student work, compare expectations, and share lessons about which strategies are effective. The report states "Utilizing professional learning communities in this way proved to be the most effective means to provide teachers with feedback," creating a nonthreatening setting where teachers' impact on student learning could be assessed. Administrators supplemented this with regular non-evaluative classroom observations and a common rubric for assessing student writing.
