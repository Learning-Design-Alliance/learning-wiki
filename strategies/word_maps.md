# Word Maps

## Description
A word map is a visual organizer that promotes vocabulary development. Most word map organizers engage students in developing a definition, synonyms, antonyms, and a picture for a given vocabulary word or concept. Word maps can be used effectively with classroom read-alouds and word walls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vocabulary word, word map template, guidance on how to use the map, synonyms, antonyms, a picture to help illustrate the new target word or concept
#### Constraints
- May require additional time to teach and implement effectively.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All learners, particularly effective for second language learners and students with varying reading skills

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Develop understanding of vocabulary, think about new terms and concepts in multiple ways, build upon prior knowledge, support oral and reading comprehension

### Affordances
- Helps students develop their understanding of a word, encourages students to think about new terms or concepts in several ways by asking questions such as: What is it? What is it like? What are some examples? It helps students build upon prior knowledge and visually represent new information.

### Personalization
- Instructors can give students who need extra help the chance to work with a partner, allow students to use pictures to illustrate when appropriate, adjust the number of words students need to map, or provide students with sentences each containing the target word.
