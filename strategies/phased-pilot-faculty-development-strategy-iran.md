# Implement clinical education reform through phased pilot programs, targeted faculty development, and stakeholder engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, given Iran's economic and healthcare constraints, reforms proceed through context-sensitive pathways rather than sweeping nationwide changes. It states that "phased pilot programs, targeted investments in faculty development, and active stakeholder engagement represent viable, context-sensitive pathways for implementing competency-based clinical education in Iran within existing economic constraints." Examples cited include structured faculty development at Isfahan University and empowerment workshops at Shiraz University.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engagement with local stakeholders including budgeting and oversight bodies to align initiatives with institutional feasibility and policy readiness
#### Constraints
- Must account for Iran's economic crises, international sanctions, and resource-poor clinical training environments

### Target Learners
- clinical faculty members
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- competency-based clinical education
- faculty supervisory and teaching performance
