# Safeguard visiting research students' well-being through dual supervision, welfare checks, and cohort social support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because carrying out a research project in a foreign country may be stressful, LEAPS embeds welfare structures into the program. "During LEAPS, each project must have two supervisors to i) safeguard the welfare of the student, ii) make sure that at least one supervisor is present for the duration of the program for regular updates on the student's progress, and iii) allow the student to benefit from wider scientific expertise." Students also access welfare channels such as Observatory Allies and Confidentiality Counsellors, receive a mid-program welfare check, and share housing and office space to build cohort camaraderie.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two supervisors per project with at least one present for the full program duration
- Welfare channels (Observatory Allies, Confidentiality Counsellors) and a mid-program welfare check by the organising committee
#### Constraints
- Motivated by the context of academic internationalisation, where research abroad may be stressful and could negatively influence satisfaction with the experience

### Target Learners
- International visiting summer research students

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining student well-being during an international research experience
