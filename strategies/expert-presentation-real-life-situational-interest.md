# Invite a domain expert and use real-life context to trigger and maintain situational interest

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The activity linked a thermodynamics design task to organ donation: students watched a documentary on donor families, and a doctor-researcher specializing in organ conservation gave a presentation, with some students presenting their boxes and preliminary results. The author writes that "The presence of an expert can allow students to make connections between the activity materials and real life." Teachers can reproduce this by anchoring design tasks in authentic societal problems and arranging expert visits.
