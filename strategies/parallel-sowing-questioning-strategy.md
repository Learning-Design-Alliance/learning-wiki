# Parallel Sowing: teacher-guided elicitation of partial intuitions to seed multiple solution branches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Parallel Sowing is a parallel-thinking–oriented questioning strategy that initializes the student reasoning tree through teacher-guided multi-path exploration rather than requiring students to independently propose complete solutions. The article stresses that "Parallel Sowing does not assume that a novice learner can fully enumerate complete solutions"; instead, the teacher may elicit partial intuitions, observable constraints, decomposition ideas, or candidate methods, each serving as an entry point into a different branch of the Parallel Reasoning Tree.
