# Recommend PBL for high school chemistry teaching with daily-life scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend adopting problem-based learning in formal education institutions and high school chemistry teaching because the method is thought to raise skills such as "bringing the problem or problems which students may encounter in daily life into the class environment as scenarios, defining the problem in the scenario, structuring in their minds information regarding the problem and its solution, developing ways out for the problem and coming out with a solution."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problem scenarios drawn from students' daily life; teacher guidance; small heterogeneous groups
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in one 48-student study on the mixtures topic plus cited literature

### Target Learners
- High school chemistry students

### Target Learning Goals
- Problem defining and solving; structuring knowledge of chemistry topics
