# Teach physics educators a rudimentary understanding of LLM functioning so they can leverage its strengths and limitations in practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 3 studies (3 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that physics educators acquire a rudimentary understanding of LLMs' functioning to inform their practice, since anthropomorphising and treating the chatbot like a human interlocutor reduces output quality and can mislead learners. Understanding differences between LLMs and humans is presented as "an important resource for using LLMs as tools" and for setting realistic expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness to be selective and critical about which interpersonal-communication intuitions are applied when interacting with LLMs.
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that interacting with chatbots raises affective considerations about how interaction makes users feel and impacts self-perception as learners or experts, especially over long intensive periods.

### Target Learners
- physics educators
- physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- realistic expectations of LLM capabilities
- critical and productive use of LLMs in physics education
