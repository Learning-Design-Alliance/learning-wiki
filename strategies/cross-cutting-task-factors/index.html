Support competence formation through activity in cognition, self-reliant design work, convergence with real knowledge processes, and continuity between levels¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the experiment's results, the authors identify the factors that most noticeably influenced cross-professional competence formation when supported by the cross-cutting system of tasks. As printed, "The most noticeable influence on the formation of cross-professional competencies (based on the results of the experiment) is provided by the following factors supported by the cross -cutting system of tasks: activity in cognition; self -reliance in creativity, design of robotic systems; convergence of the learning process with the process of real knowledge, career guidance and socialization; continuity between the levels of training." These factors manifested in students' choice of optimal ways and means of activity and in developing the ability to manage their own and the team's work, including self-control and decision-making skills.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The factors must be supported by the cross-cutting system of tasks

Constraints¶

  • The influence is reported from this experiment's results, working with construction kits and software

Target Learners¶

  • students across preschool, school and university robotics education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • planning and carrying out cognitive work with machines, cyber-physical systems and artificial intelligence
  • self-control and decision-making in team problem solving

Claims¶

  • Students taught with the task-based robotics methodology formed cross-professional competences at a statistically higher level than students taught traditionally [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Extend the task-based robotics methodology toward nonlinear individual educational paths
  • Embed research methods and creative activity across physics studies to strengthen competency formation
