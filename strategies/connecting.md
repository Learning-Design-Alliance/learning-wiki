# Connecting

## Description
Connecting involves relating a current problem or text to prior knowledge and experiences. It asks learners if the current problem reminds them of any other math problem and how they have solved similar problems in the past.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient prior knowledge and experiences to draw connections from.
#### Constraints
- May require additional support for learners who lack sufficient prior knowledge or experiences.
