# Standardize EA wording and align activities with competency-based curricula through stakeholder consultation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Experts recommended clarifying and standardizing EA terminology so stakeholders share a common vocabulary, then revising existing EAs to align with competency-based curricula. One expert stated: "If we standardize these wordings, it would be better and easier for everyone to understand, especially the Academic Activities." Suggested steps include reviewing postgraduate health professional education literature on CBMEs and consulting key stakeholders, including trainees, for feedback during framework review.

## Design Implications

### Context
