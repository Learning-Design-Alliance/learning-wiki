# Regularly evaluate mentoring practices with a dual-purpose questionnaire and feed results into mentor training and organisational revision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, given the individualized, context-specific and dynamic nature of mentoring, programmes should regularly evaluate mentoring practices, for example with questionnaires, to facilitate organisational revisions and development of mentors' competencies. Results can be used at three levels: programme-level evaluation, anonymised feedback to individual mentors about gaps, and year-by-year comparison to attune mentoring to mentees' changing needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated dual-purpose measurement tool capturing both mentor and mentee perspectives
- A longitudinal mentoring programme whose goals include self-direction of learning
#### Constraints
- The authors note there are limited studies on the efficacy and usefulness of a formal evaluation process of the mentoring system
- Further development is needed for use in an international, non-western context to address cultural diversity

### Target Learners
- mentors and mentees in health profession education programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- alignment of mentoring expectations
- mentoring competency development
- self-directed learning

## Claims

- [Mentors and mentees show both alignment and gaps in perceived critical incidents, with cultural sensitivity a top gap for both groups](../claims/mentoring-alignment-gaps-cultural-sensitivity.md) [+W]
- [Mentees experienced progressively less mentor guiding role over the years, suggesting growing self-directed learning](../claims/mentee-self-direction-increases-guiding-role-declines.md) [+W]
- [Mentees in successive years of the programme experienced more mentor emphasis on reflection, personal issues and professional identity development, with mentor presence roughly constant](../claims/mentoring-emphasis-increases-over-programme-years.md) [+W]
