# Support students admitted by non-grades selection via assimilation or accommodation approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that medical schools organize additional support for, or adjust the learning environment of, students who enter medical school other than with high grades. It describes two discourses: assimilation, helping students fit the current environment, for example by building self-regulated learning skills, citing the Study Smart program shown to positively affect learning behaviour and academic performance; and accommodation, adjusting the environment, for example by offering holistic assessments and valuing students for the resources they bring. It also recommends critically considering the alignment between selection criteria and the medical school program.
