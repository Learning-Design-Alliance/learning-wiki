# Use GenAI as a tutor to generate practice MCQs and embed retrieval practice into learning and training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 5 studies (2 review, 2 design, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy in which learners use GenAI as an academic tutor that generates accurate, well-designed multiple-choice practice questions, asks them questions on topics needing support, and gives accurate and constructive explanations alongside the correct answer. The article presents this as a simple and efficient way to harness GenAI for personalised learning, exploiting the testing effect of formative practice tests as part of the learning process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Generated MCQs must be accurate and well-designed; the article cites studies showing ChatGPT is effective at generating such questions
#### Constraints
- Profound differences in performance between free and subscription versions risk exacerbating a digital divide between learners who can and cannot afford the subscription

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- biomedical science trainees and apprentices in laboratory training

### Target Learning Goals
- retrieval practice and formative self-testing across the curriculum
