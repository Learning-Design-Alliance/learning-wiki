# Communicating Positive Expectations

## Description
Teacher expectations have a dramatic impact on learner performance and behavior. Educators should monitor interactions to communicate high expectations to all learners. Techniques include calling on all learners equitably, increasing wait time, giving hints, and expressing belief in their abilities. This strategy bolsters student self-confidence and encourages them to remain on task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monitor interactions to call on all learners equitably, increase wait time, give hints, and express belief in their abilities.
#### Constraints
- Requires conscious effort to ensure equitable participation and appropriate support for all learners. It can be challenging to maintain high expectations for all learners, especially those with a history of underperformance.
