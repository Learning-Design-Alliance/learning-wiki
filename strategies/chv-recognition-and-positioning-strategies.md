# Signal value and recognition to volunteers through community positioning, ceremonies, role clarity, training, adapted incentives and supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of implementable approaches the review distils for policymakers and programmers: clear positioning of the CHV within the community, for example by establishing a local health committee involving CHVs and creating community feedback processes, and explicitly signalling that volunteers are valued, for example by "organising felicitation and/or graduation ceremonies". The review adds approaches entailing clarity of roles, provision of skill-based and ongoing training, incentives adapted to local context, good supervision and logistical support, which together can trigger the motivation mechanisms underlying sustained volunteer engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A stable and supportive cultural, political and social context, with the intervention closely linked to local health services
#### Constraints
- The review states these approaches "may or may not trigger the said mechanisms in contexts which are unstable"

### Target Learners
- Community health volunteers
- Volunteer programme managers and designers

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained volunteer engagement and motivation
- Role clarity and competence development
