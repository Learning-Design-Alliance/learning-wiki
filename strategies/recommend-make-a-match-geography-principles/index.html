Recommend make a match for geography learning activities, especially geographic principles material¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend adopting the make a match learning model for geography instruction on principles material, arguing it creates an active, fun atmosphere and fosters cooperation. They state the model "can be recommended for geography learning activities, especially on geographic principles", and note learning activities can be optimized by arranging them with models that help achieve learning objectives.
