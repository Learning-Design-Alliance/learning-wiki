# Collaborate with local knowers and socially validate interpretations through community-based discourse

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that, within progressive ideologies such as standpoint epistemology and critical pragmatism, inquiry into valued thinking should involve scientists collaborating with "local knowers"—people with firsthand, often minoritized, experience. Together they "investigate potential solutions for concrete problems and socially validate their interpretations through community- based discourse where all voices are heard." This is the article's forward-looking implication for theory and practice surrounding valued forms of thinking.
