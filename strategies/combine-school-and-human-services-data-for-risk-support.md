# Combine in-school and out-of-school data sources to understand and support students flagged as at risk

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends an approach in which local education agencies use both in-school data and out-of-school data—human services involvement and public benefit receipt—to understand why students are flagged as at risk and to decide how best to support them. It frames predictor analysis as a way to interpret model flags, not just produce them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to both school records and child welfare or human services administrative data
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated in two Allegheny County, Pennsylvania education agencies; generalization beyond that setting is not established in the provided text

### Target Learners
- K-12 students identified as at risk of near-term academic problems

### Target Learning Goals
- Timely identification and support of students at risk of absenteeism, suspension, poor grades, or low test performance
