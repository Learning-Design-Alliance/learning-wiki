# Specifically increase the use of professional accountability in schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a specific implementable recommendation within its multi-pronged approach, the paper advises that accountability design "should specifically increase the use of professional accountability, which has historically been underutilized in schools." This directs policymakers to add accountability mechanisms rooted in professional norms and expertise alongside existing test-based public reporting and sanctions.
