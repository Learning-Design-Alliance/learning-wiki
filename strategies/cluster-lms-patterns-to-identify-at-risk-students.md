# Use clustering of LMS interaction patterns to identify at-risk students early.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that instructors can use the identified interaction patterns to spot students whose behavior resembles the Non Task Oriented (procrastinators) cluster, which achieved the lowest marks. Because achievement is not used to build the clusters, the method can be applied before final marks are known. The article states these results "may help an instructor better understand students’ learning characteristics and interactions with LMS, thus helping them identify at -risk students".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Moodle log data covering effort, time spent working, and procrastination variables.
#### Constraints
- The patterns were derived from a single Moodle course in one psychology degree program; generalization to other courses or platforms is not established in this study.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate university students in blended or online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning and study strategies
