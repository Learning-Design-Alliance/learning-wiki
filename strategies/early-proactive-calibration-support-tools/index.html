Provide early, proactive metacognitive support that guides feedback interpretation and strategy adjustment, beyond merely presenting performance information¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend calibration support tools that "go beyond merely presenting information, offering guidance on interpreting feedback, and implementing strategies to better align students’ judgments with their actual performance". This stems from their finding that early calibration discrepancies shape subsequent strategy engagement, and that post-assessment feedback on performance discrepancies alone often fails to encourage reflection on the strategies that contributed to discrepancies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Detection of learners' early calibration discrepancies (e.g., estimated minus actual pretest performance)
  • Guidance that connects discrepancy awareness to concrete learning-strategy adjustments

Constraints¶

  • Prior feedback-oriented interventions have shown inconsistent effectiveness, e.g., Foster et al. (2017) found calibration accuracy did not improve with feedback on estimated and actual performance

Target Learners¶

  • College students in online or computer-based learning environments, including community college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate metacognitive calibration and effective engagement in metacognitive learning strategies

Claims¶

  • Pretest retrospective judgments, rather than actual pretest scores, predict college students' engagement in preparatory metacognitive strategies [+W]
  • College students who overestimate their pretest retrospective judgments engage less in preparatory metacognitive strategies before quizzes in computer-based learning environments [+W]
  • Overconfidence in self-assessment is especially concerning in online learning environments where limited external feedback widens gaps between perceived and actual performance [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Learning Dashboards
  • Develop AI literacy frameworks that build metacognitive awareness distinguishing system-assisted performance from independent competence
