# Stakeholder-recommended improvements: better SP compensation and breaks, backup rosters, hybrid training, and clinical faculty involvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants proposed concrete programme improvements: increasing SP pay to raise commitment, scheduling breaks between exam encounters to prevent burnout, advance scheduling with centralised communication (e.g. a common WhatsApp group), maintaining a backup SP roster, offering hybrid in-person/online training of at least two days, recruiting SPs with acting or healthcare backgrounds, and involving clinical faculty in SP training and script writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support for SP compensation and scheduling
- Structured case guidelines and briefings for SPs
#### Constraints
- Participants acknowledged break scheduling during exams may be logistically difficult

### Target Learners
- simulated patients
- medical students in clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- sustainable SP programme operation
- consistent and authentic role portrayal
