# Use web-based learning as an on-demand reinforcing agent to maintain newly acquired ECG skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because newly acquired ECG interpretation skills are rapidly lost without repetition, the article recommends that educators treat maintenance of knowledge as requiring effort from teacher and student. It states that "Retention of knowledge can be increased through reinforcement", and that the on-demand nature and unlimited repetitions of WBL make it an effective reinforcing agent, for example made available to students after a traditional lecture-based intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of the WBL intervention on demand after the initial teaching intervention
#### Constraints
- The article states that exactly how WBL should be implemented into the curriculum to reach maximum potential is not covered by the study, and further studies are needed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- senior students and residents during clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance and retention of ECG interpretation skills
