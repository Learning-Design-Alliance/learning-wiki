# Pair ed-tech homework tool adoption with multi-day professional development and coaching visits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its evaluations, ASSISTments implementation included "professional development and coaching designed to increase teachers' readiness and efficacy in using ASSISTments." The specified model is 5 days of professional development plus three in-person or remote coaching visits, delivered alongside the online homework tool rather than as software-only deployment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- trained facilitators and time for 5 days of professional development
- capacity for three in-person or remote coaching visits
#### Constraints
- the coaching model was evaluated with middle school math teachers using ASSISTments; effectiveness of the PD component in isolation is not separately reported

### Target Learners
- middle school mathematics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher readiness and efficacy in using the ASSISTments platform for math homework
