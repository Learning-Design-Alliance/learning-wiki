# Focus collaborative diagnosis on initially unshared information through a cycle of eliciting and sharing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because group members tend to share too little relevant information and shared information is discussed more often and earlier than unshared information, the article recommends that collaborators "focus more heavily on initially unshared information" in collaborative diagnosis, since this strategy also leads to more accurate diagnoses. The article concludes that "A cycle of eliciting and sharing information may consequently be beneficial"; simulation settings can scaffold such strategies during training.
