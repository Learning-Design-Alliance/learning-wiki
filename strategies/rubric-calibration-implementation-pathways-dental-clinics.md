# Calibration and implementation pathways for analytic rubrics in clinical dental courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes three actionable pathways for dental educators: clinical integration (formative rubric use of 1–2 cases per student per week with priority feedback, paired with digital tools such as PrepAssistant), curriculum adjustments (time-based exercises for low-scoring parameters and alignment of lectures with rubric criteria), and a calibration protocol (pre-semester calibration with five benchmark cases targeting κ > 0.6, with monthly recalibration using two new cases to prevent rater drift). The authors state that institutions may reweight parameters after pilot testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-semester calibration with benchmark cases targeting κ > 0.6
- Monthly recalibration with new cases to prevent rater drift
#### Constraints
- Institutions may reweight parameters after pilot testing; the rubric was developed for one institutional context and requires local adaptation

### Target Learners
- fifth-year clinical-phase dental students in prosthodontic courses

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent, calibrated clinical competency assessment
