# Provide additional instructional support such as cues and discussion of common errors for misconceptions that persist through demonstration and instruction

## Description
Because the inversion error survived both repeated observation of the demonstration and full augmented-video instruction, the authors recommend extra instructional support. They write that "some errors require special attention which potentially could be realized via the implementa- tion of cues [42] or via highlighting and discussing com- mon errors of students in advance," and conclude that the topic needs "additional instructional support in this topic for instance via cues which highlight essential information."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must go beyond demonstration and standard augmented-video instruction for error types that resist correction, such as the inversion error.
#### Constraints
- The article states a briefer instruction could fail to transfer the link between the mathematical equations and their application to the trajectory in both frames.
