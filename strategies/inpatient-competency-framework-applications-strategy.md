# Use the framework to build curricula, supervision, commissioning and audit for inpatient staff development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends several applications of the framework: developing curricula for multidisciplinary inpatient professionals, guiding supervision and self-reflection on practice, supporting commissioners to check services meet the competences and allocate skill mix, and benchmarking service compliance in audits and research. It notes "the framework lends itself to the development of curricula for multidisciplinary inpatient professionals, due to its logical structure covering basic, specific, and specialist competences". A combined training and supervision package is advised because one-off training alone does not sustain improvements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combined with long-term supervision, since sustained behavioural change cannot be achieved by training alone
#### Constraints
- Inpatient settings have high staff turnover, making comprehensive ongoing training provision necessary

### Target Learners
- Multidisciplinary inpatient professionals
- Supervisors
- Service commissioners

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained improvement in psychosocial inpatient care quality
