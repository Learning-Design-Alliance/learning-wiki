# Provide short, intensive, structured training in plain trauma X-ray interpretation for junior doctors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review identifies plain trauma X-ray interpretation training as a strategy to improve junior doctors' accuracy, highlighting that substantial improvement was achieved in just two weeks under the guidance of a consultant radiologist. It concludes that implementing such deliberate strategies in clinical practice could enhance diagnostic performance and trauma care in emergency departments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-structured training programme, ideally under the guidance of a consultant radiologist
#### Constraints
- The review notes future studies should verify these findings, as some were from single studies with few studies identified overall

### Target Learners
- Junior doctors in emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate interpretation of plain trauma X-rays
