# IKEA VR Kitchen Visualizer

## Description
The IKEA VR kitchen visualizer uses VR technology to give customers a better understanding of what to think about when planning and designing their kitchens and reassure them in their decisions by allowing them to walk around in a virtual version of their planned kitchen. It aims to transfer knowledge to customers, teaching them about useful issues such as the best-practice work triangle, waste sorting and corner storage. It also lets them make batter and fry pancakes in this virtual kitchen, with the option of multi-tasking by frying pancakes in four separate pans simultaneously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- HTC Vive or Oculus Rift VR headsets, a VR-ready computer, and access to the IKEA VR Kitchen Visualizer application.
#### Constraints
- Limited immersion due to the lack of haptic feedback, users who are not tech-literate may have difficulty setting it up, requires time and effort to plan the space.
