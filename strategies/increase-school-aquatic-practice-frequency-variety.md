# Increase frequency, intensity, duration and variety of school aquatic practice and adopt comprehensive pedagogical approaches per stage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends strengthening school swimming implementation by increasing practice dosage and breadth: "it would be necessary to increase the frequency, intensity, duration, and variety of aquatic practice, not only to improve specific motor skills but also to consolidate general competencies". It further highlights "the need for greater inclusion of swimming in school curricula, with more comprehensive pedagogical approaches adapted to each stage", alongside institutional support and teacher training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Greater institutional support, teacher training and methodological coherence for systematic implementation
#### Constraints
- Real school contexts usually offer weekly, short-duration classes, raising doubts about long-term effectiveness

### Target Learners
- school-aged children from early childhood to secondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- aquatic competence
- water safety
- long-term retention of aquatic skills

### Affordances
- Aquatic Competence Literacy Framework
