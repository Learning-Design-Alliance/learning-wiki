# Pre-semester training sessions plus continuous on-demand technical support throughout the semester

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the semester, the department organized special training sessions in which professors were trained to use the necessary hardware and software; demonstrations showed the three lecture modalities (blackboard, graphics tablet, third-party software), and students were invited to join remotely via ZOOM. In the second part, each professor personally tested the equipment and verified laptop compatibility. During the first week a top-down supporting service placed staff at the start of each lecture; afterwards the service changed to a bottom-up, on-demand type as professors reached autonomy. The article states that "a continuous technical support was organized throughout the entire semester", operated by technical staff and a specially hired group of trained students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- special training sessions before the beginning of the semester
- a group of specially trained students hired for adjoint help during the semester
#### Constraints
- the highly heterogeneous professor laptops made training and technical support particularly challenging and required a wide set of adapters

### Target Learners
- professors and technical staff delivering blended lectures
- undergraduate and MSc engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- smooth start and operation of blended lectures
- familiarity of professors with the blended equipment
