# Provide captions or transcripts alongside videos to support accessibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states it is "crucial to consider accessibility and provide captions or transcripts" so students who might struggle to access videos are supported. It notes this is a legal requirement for UK higher education institutions under the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018, and recommends also supplying PowerPoint slides or notes presenting the material in a range of formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Captions or transcripts must be provided; slides or notes replicating the video information should accompany the screencast
#### Constraints
- The legal requirement stated applies to higher education institutions in the United Kingdom under the 2018 Accessibility Regulations

### Target Learners
- higher education students, including those who might struggle with accessing videos, such as deaf or dyslexic learners

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable access to recorded video content
