# Critical Lens Development

## Description
Developing a critical lens involves analyzing the affordances and constraints of different modes of communication to understand how they enhance or inhibit meaning. This can be done by analyzing a single mode in various media, such as picture books, informational texts, poetry, magazines, blogs, and websites, to identify how images and words work together or against each other. By talking about how modes can enhance or inhibit meaning, learners develop a critical lens and collect exemplar structures to guide their own composition decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to various media for analysis. Needs structured discussion prompts to guide learners' thinking.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners to identify subtle affordances and constraints. Learners may need support in connecting their analyses to their own compositions.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by allowing learners to choose the media they want to analyze. Scaffolding can be provided by pre-selecting examples and guiding discussions.
