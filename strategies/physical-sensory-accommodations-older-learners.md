# Accommodate age-related physical and sensory changes through high-contrast large-print materials, clear face-to-face speech, easy-grip manipulatives, and short morning sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of concrete classroom accommodations for older learners' physical challenges: worksheets in "14-point black font" with plenty of white space, high-contrast enlarged visuals, easy-to-grasp manipulatives, facing students while speaking and enunciating clearly, reinforcing aural messages with visuals and gestures, teaching phrases for requesting repetition, and an accessible, well-lit classroom near bathrooms and an elevator. Scheduling uses several short sessions rather than one long one, with morning sessions favored for higher energy levels, and classes cancelled in inclement weather.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large white board is described as a necessity for students with vision or manual dexterity issues
#### Constraints
- The author cancels class rather than expose students to icy sidewalks or severe thunderstorms, and notes stamina and prolonged concentration may be problems

### Target Learners
- older adult English learners with vision, hearing, dexterity, or stamina limitations

### Target Learning Goals
- access to beginning ESL instruction despite sensory and physical limitations
