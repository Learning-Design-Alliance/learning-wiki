# AI voice co-instructor co-teaching methodology with peer observation and instructor modeling

## Description
The article describes a co-teaching methodology in which an AI voice agent serves as an active co-instructor during lectures, built on a conversational AI platform. Students interact with AI in real time in a social context where "peer observation creates additional calibration opportunities", and "the instructor models calibrated AI interaction"—disagreeing with AI, correcting errors, accepting genuine contributions—drawing on cognitive apprenticeship principles to make expert epistemic practice visible.
