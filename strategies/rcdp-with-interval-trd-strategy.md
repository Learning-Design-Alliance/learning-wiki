# Trial a combined debriefing strategy alternating RCDP practice cycles with interval reflective debriefing sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future simulation training trial a combined strategy in which RCDP is "conducted with interval or a final cumulative reflective debriefing", so learners gain both the behavioral correction of deliberate practice and the perspective-reframing of reflection. This responds to learner feedback in both arms: TRD learners wanted deliberate practice of discussed skills, and RCDP learners wanted opportunities to reflect and discuss rationale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators experienced in both RCDP and PEARLS-style reflective debriefing; pre-identified learning objectives anchored to scenario phases
#### Constraints
- The article states further research is needed before adoption; retention and clinical application of skills were not assessed in this study

### Target Learners
- Experienced interprofessional clinical teams in high-acuity settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Teamwork skill application and knowledge acquisition during simulation-based team training
