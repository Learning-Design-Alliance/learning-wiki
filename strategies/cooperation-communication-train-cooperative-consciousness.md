# Emphasize cooperation and communication to train students' cooperative consciousness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that, because knowledge is the social construction of individuals and others by negotiation, "students must cooperate and communicate with others" in the process of constructing knowledge. In a cooperation and communication environment, students can enlarge their views instead of receiving knowledge passively, build up their own knowledge system, cultivate innovative spirit and problem-solving ability, and learn to respect others' opinions and ideas. Traditional teaching neglected cooperation between teachers and students and among students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom environment that supports cooperation and communication between teachers and students and among students
