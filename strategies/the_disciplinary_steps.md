# The Disciplinary Steps

## Description
Follow these steps when disciplining learners: review what happened, identify and accept the learner's feelings, review alternative actions, explain the building policy as it applies to the situation, let the learner know that all learners are treated the same, invoke an immediate and meaningful consequence, let the learner know that you are disappointed that you have to invoke a consequence to their action, communicate an expectation that the learner will do better in the future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The instructor should be trained and familiar with the disciplinary process.
#### Constraints
- Following this process can be time-consuming, and may not be possible in all situations.
