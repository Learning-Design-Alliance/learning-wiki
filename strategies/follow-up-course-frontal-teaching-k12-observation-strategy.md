# Follow-up course with frontal teaching practice and K-12 teaching observation to further enthuse students about teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy drawn from the course's end-of-course improvement question, in which students suggested a follow-up class featuring observing, critiquing and delivering frontal teaching, and observing and critiquing K-12 teaching. Students also suggested the amount of reading per week can be reduced. The strategy addresses the students' reported feeling that helping students while they worked on tutorials was not teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A follow-up course structure in which students can observe, critique, and deliver frontal teaching
- Opportunities to observe and critique K-12 teaching
#### Constraints
- Students felt they did not really get an opportunity to teach where the word teaching referred to frontal teaching, despite the course attempting to bridge the gap between teaching and learning

### Target Learners
- science and engineering undergraduates considering teaching careers

### Target Learning Goals
- enthusiasm for teaching careers
- classroom delivery and frontal teaching skills
