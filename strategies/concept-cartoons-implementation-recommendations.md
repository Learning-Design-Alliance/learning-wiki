# Implement concept cartoons© by following the inquiry working procedure, praising all views, supporting discussion, and letting students practically verify their own assumptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the observation findings the article formulates recommendations for teaching practice: teachers should respect the working procedure based on the IBSE algorithm, praise each presented view even when not scientifically correct to remove fear, support class and group discussion in a friendly atmosphere, ask for substantiation of arguments, and ensure practical verification of assumptions. The article stresses "he/she should not only theoretically discuss it with students but he/she always should ensure the practical realization of practical verification of their assumptions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher should respect the working procedure of application, based on the algorithm of scientific investigation found in inquiry based science education
#### Constraints
- The teacher should avoid giving his own drafts of procedure for verification of students´ assumptions and let each group realize their own proposals when it is possible

### Target Learners
- primary school teachers
- fourth-grade primary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- development and modification of students' scientific ideas
- scientific reasoning and argumentation
