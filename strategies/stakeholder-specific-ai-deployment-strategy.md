# Tailor AI deployment to specific applications and stakeholder groups rather than applying a one-size-fits-all integration approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions prioritise transparency in AI operational processes, proactively incorporate explainable AI methodologies to clarify decision-making criteria, and establish clear privacy protocols that balance confidentiality and transparency. It concludes that "applying a one-size-ﬁts-all approach to AI integration in education is infeasible" and that careful consideration of specific AI applications and their impact on perceptions of global utility, justice, and confidence is needed. Educators must also be trained not only in how to use AI tools but in understanding their implications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear institutional policies addressing the problem of agency, building confidence and trust, and insisting on explainable AI systems
#### Constraints
- Transparency and explainability mean different things to different stakeholders, and their effectiveness is context-dependent

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- teachers
- parents

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing stakeholder acceptance and trust in AI education solutions

## Claims

- [Higher AI agency is associated with lower intention to use AI in education, while transparency, explainability, and privacy do not directly predict intention to use](../claims/ai-agency-negative-direct-predictor-itu.md) [+W]
- [For parents, higher AI agency lowered confidence, which indirectly reduced intention to allow AI to correct student work (Correct-AI)](../claims/parents-correct-ai-agency-confidence-mediated.md) [+W]
- [For students, AI agency reduced intention to use Correct-AI indirectly through lower perceived global utility and justice, with no direct effect](../claims/students-correct-ai-agency-mediated-justice-utility.md) [+W]
- [For students, privacy (Answer-AI) and explainability (Emotion-AI) each positively influenced intention to use indirectly through perceived global utility](../claims/students-privacy-explainability-indirect-global-utility.md) [+W]
- [For teachers, higher privacy levels lowered confidence regarding an AI supporting student emotional health, indirectly reducing intention to use (Emotion-AI)](../claims/teachers-emotion-ai-privacy-confidence-mediated.md) [+W]
- [For teachers, explainability (Correct-AI) and both agency and explainability (Answer-AI) influenced intention to use indirectly through global utility and perceived usefulness](../claims/teachers-explainability-agency-mediated-global-utility.md) [+W]
