# Use structured brainstorming rules (avoid judgment, prioritize quantity, embrace unconventional ideas, build on others' contributions) in ED problem scoping

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After observing that week-06 classroom instructions merely asked students to 'brainstorm' without guidance, the authors resolved to adopt Kaufman and McCuish's four rules for effective brainstorming in future iterations: "avoid judgment; prioritize quantity over quality; embrace unconventional ideas; and build upon each other's contributions." This strategy structures the idea-generation stage of ED problems to broaden the solution space student groups explore.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group brainstorming sessions during problem scoping, as in week 06 of the ED task
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a planned change for future iterations, not as a tested intervention in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate student teams solving engineering design problems

### Target Learning Goals
- generating diverse solution approaches in design problem scoping
