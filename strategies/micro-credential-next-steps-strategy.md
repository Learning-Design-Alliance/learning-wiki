# Advance micro-credentials through a piloting coalition, a rigorous research agenda, and dissemination of best practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report proposes three mutually reinforcing efforts for state and local policy leaders. First, "Invest in a coalition of educators, states, and districts to pilot this work," involving practicing teachers, district and union partnerships, providers, education agencies, researchers, and philanthropies. Second, "Assemble and pursue a powerful research agenda," because "the credibility of micro-credentials will depend on the evidence of impact of competency-based professional learning." Third, "Develop and disseminate best practices" through toolkits and publications, since professional learning is mostly embedded in state rules rather than law and state departments are understaffed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rigorous research requires support from institutions of higher education, research organizations, and philanthropies, with long timelines and substantial resources
#### Constraints
- Human capital data are often the subject of political controversy and should be handled neither punitively nor dismissively

### Target Learners
- state and district policy leaders
- practicing teachers
- professional development providers

### Target Learning Goals
- building competency-based professional learning systems
- evidence of impact on teaching practice
