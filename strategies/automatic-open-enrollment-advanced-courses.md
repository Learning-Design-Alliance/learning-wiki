# Adopt automatic enrollment and open enrollment policies for advanced courses to expand equitable access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that states and districts adopt policies whereby students identified for advanced coursework through multiple measures are automatically enrolled unless their families opt them out, and adopt open enrollment so any interested student can take an advanced class. It cites North Carolina's automatic enrollment policy, under which "92% of North Carolina students in grades 6 and above who scored at the highest level on their end-of-grade math test were placed in an advanced math course" in 2022-23.
