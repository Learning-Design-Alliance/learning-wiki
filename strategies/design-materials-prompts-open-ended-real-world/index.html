Design physics modules and worksheets with explicit critical thinking prompts, open-ended problems, and real-world decision-making scenarios¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's discussion identifies specific design features that its cited literature links to thinking-skill gains: structured materials with explicit critical thinking prompts, open-ended problems with multiple solution pathways, and real-world problems with decision-making scenarios. It states "Teaching materials designed with explicit critical thinking prompts significantly enhance students' analytical and evaluative capabilities (Erniwati et al., 2022)" and that modules with open-ended problems enhanced creative thinking (Asrial et al., 2021).

Claims¶

  • Modules and student worksheets show the highest average effect sizes among four physics teaching material types (3.93 and 3.97), both in the high category [+M]
  • Physics teaching materials show high average effect sizes on high school students' critical thinking skills (ES = 3.97) and creative thinking skills (ES = 2.97) in a meta-analysis of 20 national-journal articles [+M]
  • LLMs help bridge the gap between theoretical curricula and real-world skills by creating simulated scenarios, cases and problem contexts that are hard to reproduce in traditional classrooms [+W]
