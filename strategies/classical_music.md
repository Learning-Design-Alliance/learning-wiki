# Classical Music

## Description
Playing classical music (Bach, not Mahler) at a low volume when learners enter the room to set a professional tone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to a music player and classical music selections. Teachers should monitor student reactions and adjust the volume as needed.
#### Constraints
- Some students may dislike classical music or find it distracting. May not be effective for all classroom environments.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Sets a positive tone, reduces anxiety, and promotes a focused environment. Can be easily implemented with minimal effort.

### Personalization
- The specific music genre can be adjusted based on student preferences, but classical music is recommended for its calming effects.
