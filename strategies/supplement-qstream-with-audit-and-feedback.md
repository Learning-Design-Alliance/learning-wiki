# Supplement QStream programs with other implementation techniques such as audit and feedback to sustain behaviour change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that because multifaceted interventions are required for timely translation of research findings, a QStream program alone may not be sufficient for persistent behaviour change. 'It may be necessary to supplement QStream programs with other implementation techniques, such as audit and feedback to ensure behavioural change is present and persists beyond the implementation phase.' This pairs the brief spaced-education dissemination tool with complementary implementation strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multifaceted approach combining guideline dissemination with complementary techniques such as audit and feedback
#### Constraints
- Behaviour change may not persist beyond the implementation phase when the spaced education program is used alone

### Target Learners
- senior clinicians in high work volume clinical environments

### Target Learning Goals
- translation of updated clinical guidelines into sustained referral practice change
