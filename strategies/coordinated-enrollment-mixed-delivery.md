# Support coordinated enrollment across the mixed delivery system to ensure family choice and provider stability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that states organize recruitment, outreach, and enrollment — "coordinated enrollment" — so families can find settings that meet their needs. It states that "West Virginia requires that each county develop a unified enrollment system that ensures all eligible children are offered a placement," Alabama runs a statewide online enrollment system, and New York City uses a single application for all universal preschool providers. Coordinated enrollment also helps direct families to undersubscribed slots and stabilizes provider funding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A state or regional entity must operate a shared enrollment or application process across providers
#### Constraints
- Without coordination, providers run individual waiting lists and may suffer financially from enrollment fluctuations, per the report

### Target Learners
- Families choosing among public preschool settings
- Children eligible for state-funded preschool

### Target Learning Goals
