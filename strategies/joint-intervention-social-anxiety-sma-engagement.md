# Joint psychological, behavioral and academic support interventions targeting social anxiety and social media addiction to improve academic engagement

## Description
The article recommends combining mental-health and academic supports: "a joint psychological, behavioral and academic support intervention targeting social anxiety and social media addiction to improve academic engagement and outcomes among college students". It further recommends personalized academic support and mentoring programs to enhance engagement, plus cognitive-behavioral techniques, exposure-based interventions, and a supportive campus environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must address social anxiety and social media addiction together with academic support, since the serial pathway runs through reduced academic engagement.
#### Constraints
- The article states no causal inferences can be made from its cross-sectional, non-experimental design, and findings come from one college in Nantong, China, requiring cross-regional and cross-cultural validation.

### Target Learners
- college students with social anxiety

### Target Learning Goals
- improving academic engagement
- improving academic performance (GPA)
