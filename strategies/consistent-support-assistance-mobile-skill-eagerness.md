# Provide consistent support and assistance to maintain learner eagerness with mobile skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond initial skill-building, the authors recommend ongoing help so that learners keep experimenting with mobile technology. They state that "consistent support and assistance are perceived as concurrently important to maintain the simplicity of learning and retain the eagerness of learners to experiment and acquiring mobile skills," positioning support as a complement to imparting and encouraging IT skills so m-learning appears less discouraging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Support must be consistent and concurrent with IT skill encouragement to retain learner eagerness.
#### Constraints
- Stated in the conclusion of a cross-sectional survey study; the support mechanism itself was not manipulated or tested.

### Target Learners
- tertiary students adopting mobile learning

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained willingness to experiment with and acquire mobile skills
