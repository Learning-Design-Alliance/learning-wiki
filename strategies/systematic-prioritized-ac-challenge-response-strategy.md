# Policymakers should systematically analyze Assessment Center challenges, prioritize them by significance and feasibility, and draw on international experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is a policy sequence for addressing Assessment Center implementation challenges: policymakers should "systematically analyze these challenges, prioritize them based on significance and feasibility, and then collaborate with other national centers and draw from international experiences." This follows from the study's finding that implementation faces both external and internal challenges despite the approach being relatively recent in the health system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policymakers with authority over Assessment Center operation and the capacity to prioritize challenges and coordinate with national and international centers
#### Constraints
- The article notes the Assessment Center approach is relatively recent in the health system and that addressing challenges requires comprehensive attention from policymaking to implementation

### Target Learners
- Health system policymakers and senior managers
- Managers being assessed and developed through Assessment Centers

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving selection and development of competent health system managers
