# Incorporate artificial intelligence and platform-based course recommendations into future blended teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is to integrate artificial intelligence into pathophysiology education practice, building on intelligent medical engineering to advance reform and achieve improved pedagogical outcomes. As a concrete near-term step, because a satisfaction survey revealed that some students favor the Bilibili platform for learning, the teaching and research team will analyze the online courses those students access and recommend more appropriate courses to enhance educational satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analysis of the online courses students actually access on platforms they favor
#### Constraints
- Stated as future work, not as an implemented or tested intervention in this study

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in blended pathophysiology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved educational satisfaction and pedagogical outcomes through personalized course recommendation
