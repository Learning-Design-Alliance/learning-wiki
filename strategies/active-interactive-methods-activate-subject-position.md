# Active and interactive training methods to activate the subject position

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends training future teachers through problem-based learning, project method, thematic discussions, modeling, role plays, presentations, practical work contests with discussion, competitions of pedagogical skills, and case techniques. Implementation of the competence-based approach "should include the widespread use in the educational process active and interactive forms of employment" combined with extracurricular activities to form professional skills and activate the student's subject position.
