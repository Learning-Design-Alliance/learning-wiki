# Combine flipped classroom pre-class self-study with Peyton's four-step in-class skill teaching and CBL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe: replace early lectures with self-study using course videos and PPT on an online platform, with students posing questions about challenging content; devote subsequent weeks to Peyton's four-step skill teaching; close with CBL sessions addressing questions from both self-study and skill acquisition. The authors state this combination "effectively enhanced ultrasound residents’ skill scores and performance in case analysis" and holds promise for future ultrasound residency training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers provide slides and videos through online platforms prior to class
- A teaching team with experienced instructors and a supervising teaching director
#### Constraints
- Whether the combination shows advantages for other ultrasound subspecialties requires further investigation; the study covered only two teaching hospitals in China

### Target Learners
- ultrasound resident trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- thyroid and cervical lymph node ultrasound skills
- clinical case analysis
- autonomous learning interest
