# Compare conceptual learning gains across latent profiles of instructional practices in a broader set of active learning courses to identify causal mechanisms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that future research move beyond comparing named methods and instead collect concept inventory, network survey, and video data from a broader set of active learning physics courses, then compare gains across latent profiles of instructional practices. The authors state: "We recommend that future research experiments collect similar data from a broader set of active learning physics courses (i.e., including courses using any form of active learning, not only a named active learning method)". The goal is to identify specific causal mechanisms of conceptual learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article notes its own profile-level comparison was preliminary and confounded by small sample sizes for some profiles

### Target Learning Goals
