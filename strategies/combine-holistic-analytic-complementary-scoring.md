# Combine holistic with analytic scores as a complementary approach to enhance rating validity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article relays a recommendation, attributed by its authors to prior work, that rating validity can be improved by pairing grading modes rather than choosing one: "a way to enhance rating validity could be through the use of a complementary approach, which combines holistic with analytic scores". The article's own findings — slight analytic gains in expert consensus and low agreement under both approaches — motivate combination. The authors also note evidence that holistic marking practices could be improved by introducing analytic rubrics for feedback as an ancillary during marking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Raters must have access to both a holistic scale and an analytic rubric for the same student work, as participants in this study's grading task did.
#### Constraints
- The article reports the analytic-approach improvement in consistency over holistic as minimal in its own data.

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers
- in-service teachers rating student writing

### Target Learning Goals
- reliable and valid grading of student writing
- assessment consistency in teacher preparation
