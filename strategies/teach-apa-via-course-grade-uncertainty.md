# Teach a priori analysis by estimating the uncertainty of students' own course grades

## Description
The article presents data treatment through an application close to home: estimating the uncertainty of the grades students receive in the same course where the material is taught. The author reports the example "inﬀallibly attracts students' attention" and arouses curiosity, since a grade not given with perfect certainty surprises many students, making them more apt to remember the APA.
