# Use plug-and-play virtual simulation for tip-control training targeted at novice and beginner endoscopists before patient-based endoscopy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports integrating low-barrier virtual simulators into structured endoscopy training, particularly before hands-on endoscopy on patients. It recommends a step-by-step approach with "initial observation, followed by simulator-based endoscopy focused on scope manipulation and tip control training prior to hands-on endoscopy on patients," and identifies novices and beginners as the most suitable target learners for the tested device. The plug-and-play design lowers equipment barriers since only 16.1% of participants had institutional simulator access.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a virtual simulator with tip-control training modules and standardized instruction before use
#### Constraints
- The study was exploratory and drew no definitive conclusions about the simulator's impact on the endoscopy learning curve, which the authors say warrants longitudinal examination preferably in a randomized controlled trial

### Target Learners
- novice endoscopists
- beginner endoscopists

### Target Learning Goals
- endoscope tip control
- safe preparation for patient-based endoscopy
