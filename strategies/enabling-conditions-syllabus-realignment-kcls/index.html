Create enabling classroom conditions and realign the syllabus before implementing KCLS at higher secondary level¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on the identified barriers, the article recommends that schools "create enabling classroom condition for effective practice of KCLS as transformative teaching pedagogy" and that "the realignment of the existing syllabus is crucial to overcome this challenge". Curriculum specialists, planners and implementers are urged to become aware of the practical requirements of KCLS and make recommendations to policy makers and administrators, so that structures are not imposed on conditions (large classes, rigid syllabi) that impede them.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reduced or realigned grade 11 and 12 syllabi
  • Classroom space and class sizes conducive to team structures
