# Provide Writing Models

## Description
Providing writing models involves sharing exemplary pieces of writing with learners to illustrate effective writing techniques and structures. Learners analyze these models to identify strengths and areas for improvement, applying these insights to their own writing. This strategy can include teacher-created models, peer writing, or published works. Effective implementation involves guiding learners to observe and critique specific elements, such as structure, transitions, terminology, and use of sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to well-written models, structured analysis activities, feedback mechanisms. Requires instructor guidance to ensure learners focus on relevant aspects of the models.
#### Constraints
- Learners may blindly imitate models without critical thinking. Models might not align with all writing contexts or learner needs. Over-reliance on models can stifle creativity and originality.
