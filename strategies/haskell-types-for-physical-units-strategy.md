# Explore Haskell libraries for expressing physical dimensions and units in physics programming

## Description
A forward-looking design recommendation: 'Allowing the expression of units is very desirable from a pedagogical perspective.' The authors identify expressing physical dimensions and units as an unexplored use of types in physics and intend to 'explore their suitability to complement the ideas in this paper' using Haskell libraries available and being developed for this purpose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Haskell libraries that make multiplication 'multiply the units' as well as the numbers.
#### Constraints
- The article states this is 'not trivial to do with Haskell's type system.'

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- dimensional correctness in physics computation
