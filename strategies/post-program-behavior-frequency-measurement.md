# Measure manifested leader behavior via post-program frequency reports in participants' natural habitat

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
To measure manifested leadership behavior rather than proxies, the article recommends a concrete post-program behavioral-frequency strategy: weeks after an initiative ends, participants report how often they enacted desired leader behaviors within a short timeline. It suggests items such as "How many times within the past week have you… (desired behavior): never, once, a few times…," moving assessment beyond end-of-program self-report.
