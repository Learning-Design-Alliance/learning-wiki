# Compare the restructuring-strategy approach against other word problem training approaches in future research

## Description
The article's stated implication is that "The approach should be compared to other approaches to foster word problem solving." Because the feasibility study used only a neutral control group in which students participated in regular non-mathematics instruction, the authors state they cannot draw conclusions on effectiveness compared to other training approaches such as schema-based instruction. This strategy directs future research and practice evaluation toward comparative effectiveness studies before adopting the restructuring training over established alternatives.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
- The current evidence base is a single small feasibility study with a neutral control, so no comparative effectiveness conclusion is currently warranted
