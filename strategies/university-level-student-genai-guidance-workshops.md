# Provide university-level GenAI guidance for students, such as a GenAI policy and workshops, since students explicitly request structured guidance on AI use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that universities supply structured, student-facing guidance on generative AI rather than leaving all AI policy to individual instructors. Students themselves requested this unprompted: one suggested "mandatory workshops like the ones we have for phishing and cyber attacks" and another proposed "a course that teaches you how to use ai and how not to use it." The authors conclude that "a GenAI policy and/or workshop for students is recommended at the university level."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- University-level policy or workshop infrastructure addressing student AI use from the student perspective, not only faculty-facing guidance pages
#### Constraints
- At the studied university, the student code of conduct did not mention LLMs, AI, or artificial intelligence, and existing guidance web pages addressed faculty rather than students

### Target Learners
- University students, primarily in computing-related and other undergraduate programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Appropriate, honest, and learning-supportive use of generative AI tools in coursework
