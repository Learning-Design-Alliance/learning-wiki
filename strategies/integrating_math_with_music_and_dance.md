# Integrating Math with Music and Dance

## Description
Integrating math with music and dance is a strategy in which rhythm, pattern, movement, and structure are used to explore mathematical relationships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A genuine connection between the artistic activity and the math idea.
- Reflection that names the pattern, ratio, timing, or spatial relationship involved.
#### Constraints
- The artistic activity can become decorative unless the mathematical connection is made explicit.

### Target Learning Goals
- Support engagement, pattern recognition, and embodied understanding of mathematical structure.

### Instructions
- Introduce a math idea through rhythm, sequencing, repetition, symmetry, or coordinated movement.
- Ask learners to perform or compose a pattern.
- Debrief the mathematical structure they enacted.
