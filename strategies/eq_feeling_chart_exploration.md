# EQ Feeling Chart Exploration

## Description
Learners use the EQ Feeling Chart to identify and communicate their feelings with a partner. They discuss their feelings and their origins, then share observations with the whole class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EQ Feeling Chart, discussion questions, time for partner and whole-class sharing
#### Constraints
- Requires access to the EQ Feeling Chart or a similar resource. Some learners may be hesitant to share feelings openly.
