# Train local tour guides in astronomical knowledge for sustainable astrotourism

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes sustainable astrotourism as a human-capacity-building effort in Namibia in which "tour guides can be trained in astronomical knowledge" and indigenous star lore can be preserved. Such training empowers local guides to give tours of the night sky, increasing and diversifying their income and supporting rural communities.
