# Issue students the topics and instructional objectives a day or two before instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that Literature-in-English teachers give students the topics to be learnt together with their instructional objectives before the lesson, so students can study ahead and work toward the stated objectives. In the study, "the treatment group was issued instructional objectives on each topic that was taught a day prior to the lesson". The author concludes this strategy enhanced achievement and that teachers can adopt it.
