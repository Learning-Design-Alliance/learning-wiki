# Design & Plan: build a professional learning and measurement plan to monitor implementation fidelity and evaluate impact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Step 3 directs leaders to "Collaborate with your educators and school leaders to build a professional learning and measurement plan to monitor implementation fidelity and to evaluate impact for your targeted teaching and learning goals." Planning professional learning and measurement together, before implementation, ties the purchase to fidelity monitoring and goal-based evaluation.
