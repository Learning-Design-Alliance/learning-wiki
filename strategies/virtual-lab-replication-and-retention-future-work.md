# Future work: replicate with larger populations and assess retention time of knowledge gained under the two preparation methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors outline the key directions of their future work: replication of the comparison with more students, more institutions and other modules, and measurement of how long the gained knowledge lasts under each method. They write that "Future research should replicate the results of this study with a larger student population, in a wider selection of educational institutes and in different modules" and that they plan "to assess the retention time of the gained knowledge in the two different educational methods".
