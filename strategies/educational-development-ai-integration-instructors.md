# Provide educational development for instructors on purposefully integrating AI into online learning design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that educational development support instructors to design and facilitate learning that purposefully integrates AI technologies. Kumar et al. (2024) themes include "the use of GenAI for instructional design; (b) collaborative guidance for faculty integration of GenAI; and (c) training, resources, and guidelines on the integration of GenAI." Specific areas include introductions to AI, AI for course development or assessment, AI in teaching, image generation, and student-focused training, with emphasis on sharing, collaboration, and active participation among trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to educational development provision and collaborative guidance structures
#### Constraints
- The review cautions that intentional selection and integration of AI technology is critical and the human dynamic cannot be lost

### Target Learners
- higher education instructors
- instructional designers

### Target Learning Goals
- designing and facilitating online learning that purposefully integrates AI technologies
