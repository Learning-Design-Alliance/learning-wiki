# Organize rational and emotive-oriented conferences and orientations for schoolteachers before introducing them to online teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial's treatment outcomes, the authors recommend that computer educators, policymakers, and rational emotive behavior educators "should organize rational and emotive-oriented conferences for schoolteachers". They further suggest that national computer associations in Nigeria, as policy-making bodies, should organize conferences and symposiums for schoolteachers to change erroneous perceptions about teaching and computer skills, and that therapists liaise with computer educators and policymakers to give orientations to teachers before introducing them to online teaching and learning applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Involvement of computer educators, policymakers, and rational emotive behavior educators or therapists in organizing the conferences and orientations
#### Constraints
- The authors state the underlying finding is limited to primary schoolteachers and cannot be generalized to every teacher in Nigeria

### Target Learners
- Primary school teachers with computer anxiety about e-teaching and e-learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Changing erroneous perceptions about teaching and computer skills
- Reducing computer anxiety before exposure to online teaching and learning
