# Develop verifiable and trustworthy model explanations that let humans judge correctness

## Description
Based on the finding that participants were fooled by fabricated explanations, the authors propose future work should explore verifiable and trustworthy model explanations where humans can judge whether they are correct or incorrect. Their enumerated proposals include providing decision support using natural language from explanations, combining multiple models and explaining them together, visualizing and using explanations for debugging models, and providing explanations for the fairness of models.
