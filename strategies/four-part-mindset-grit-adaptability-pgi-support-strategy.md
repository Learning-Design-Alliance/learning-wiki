# Four-part support strategy: mindset interventions, grit-building tasks, adaptability support, and PGI-promoting growth opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical implications recommend that institutions integrate mindset training into curricula and campus culture, create long-term challenging projects that build perseverance of effort, provide orientation and guidance on time management and self-regulated learning to ease adaptation, and promote PGI through personal goal-setting, clubs, exhibitions, internships, self-reflection and feedback-seeking. Each recommendation is tied to the corresponding mediating or moderating role found in the study.

## Design Implications
