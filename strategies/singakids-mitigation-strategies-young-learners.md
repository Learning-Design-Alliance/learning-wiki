# Use visual highlighting, parent guidance, and modeling scaffolding to mitigate young learners' difficulties in AI tutoring sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From a preliminary study, the authors recommend concrete mitigations for young learners using AI tutors: noise-robust speech recognition for noisy environments, triggering the "Modeling" scaffolding type to prevent frustration, parent guidance for lower elementary grades, and visual highlighting such as bounding boxes. They observe that "adding visual high- lighting (e.g., bounding boxes) helps improve focus and comprehension" when pictures contain many objects and activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parent guidance and assistance for lower elementary grades
- Noise-robust speech recognition and speaker diarization to mitigate ASR errors
#### Constraints
- Findings are from a preliminary user study with 35 grade 1-2 students in real-world educational settings

### Target Learners
- Grade 1-2 elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement in oral language practice
- Focus and comprehension during picture description
