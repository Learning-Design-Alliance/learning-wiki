# Emotional Vocabulary Building

## Description
Learners are provided direct access to emotion words, using a word wall or chart to expand their emotional vocabulary. Connecting behaviors with feelings encourages learners to consider appropriate coping strategies. Emotion vocabularly is enhanced through the use of books and character analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a word wall or chart of emotion words. Facilitator guidance is needed to help learners connect words with feelings and behaviors.
#### Constraints
- May require ongoing reinforcement to ensure learners internalize and use new vocabulary. May not address deeper emotional issues.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Expand emotional vocabulary, improve emotional expression, enhance communication skills

### Affordances
- Enhances emotional literacy, supports understanding of nuances in feelings, and improves communication. Helps learners connect actions and emotions.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted using diverse methods, such as charts, word walls, and real-life examples. Word choices can be tailored to learner level.

- Improved emotional expression: Vocabulary-building activities enhance the ability to articulate feelings. Enhanced social interactions: Improved emotional literacy supports better communication and empathy.
