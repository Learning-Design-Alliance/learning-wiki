# Ask students to find an object representing the size of the entire solar system at the installation's scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To develop understanding of the real depth of space represented, students return to the solar-system pole and search the outdoor space for something that could represent the size of our entire solar system in the model. The teacher reinforces the scale by pointing out that the distance of the closest star system in the installation is 5 cm.
