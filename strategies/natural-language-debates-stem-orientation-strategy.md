# Use natural language, debates and controversy-oriented historical framing to teach contemporary physics and orient students toward STEM

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using natural language, debates and semiotic reflection to engage students in constructing understanding of gravitational physics. During the wording phase students reflected on "metaphors, analogies, and the limits of describing physical phenomena", and understanding word choices helps them articulate physics concepts. The authors suggest the TLS and its interdisciplinary approach "could be valuable tools for engaging students in STEM and guiding them toward academic careers", and report students recommended integrating such approaches into the curriculum.
