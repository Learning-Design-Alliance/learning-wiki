Select AI literacy scales by target population: AILS for general population, AI literacy test or ChatGPT literacy scale for higher education, AI-CI or AILQ for secondary students, Intelligent TPACK for teachers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review formulates recommendations drawing on COSMIN priorities, revalidation potential, and target populations. For general population it names AILS as the scale with the most robust quality evidence; for higher education, AI literacy test and ChatGPT literacy scale; for secondary students, AI-CI, AILQ, and Kim & Lee's instrument, with language availability guiding the choice; and for teachers' perceived readiness, Intelligent TPACK is the only instrument available. For medical students it names GSE-6AI, MAIRS-MC, and SNAIL as instruments of choice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Choices depend on the population and on language availability, e.g. Kim & Lee's instrument is only in Korean while AI-CI and AILQ are only in English

Constraints¶

  • The review cautions that none of the scales showed positive evidence for all COSMIN measurement properties and most studies suffered from poor methodological rigour

Target Learners¶

  • general population
  • higher education students
  • secondary education students
  • teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • valid assessment of AI literacy levels and development

Claims¶

  • Interpretability and feasibility of AI literacy scales remain largely unknown, and open raw data is scarce [+W]
  • AI literacy scales show good structural validity and internal consistency but are rarely tested for other measurement properties [+W]
  • A 2024 systematic review identified 22 studies validating 16 AI literacy scales, most of them self-report rather than performance-based [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Future AI literacy scale research should test cross-cultural validity and measurement error, open raw data, and develop more performance-based scales
