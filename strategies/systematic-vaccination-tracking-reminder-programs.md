# Establish structural and systematic vaccination supports: electronic immunization tracking, reminders, and extra social support for high-risk patients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends moving beyond one-off physician awareness training toward structural supports for adult immunization in diabetes care. It states that "More structural/systematic vaccination programs are needed to increase the vaccination rates in patients with diabetes" and that "Policy makers should consider establishing an electronic system that allows physicians to monitor the immunization history of their patients". It also suggests reminding calls and home visits for patients with poor health.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physicians need a way to monitor the immunization history of their patients, via electronically retrieved health records at visits
#### Constraints
- The article notes free vaccine coverage does not always result in increased vaccination rates, particularly in elderly diabetic individuals with multiple co-morbid conditions

### Target Learners
- adult patients with diabetes
- physicians caring for patients with diabetes

### Target Learning Goals
- increase influenza and pneumococcal vaccination uptake in high-risk adults with diabetes
