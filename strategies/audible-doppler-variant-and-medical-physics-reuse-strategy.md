# Extend the fan Doppler experiment to an audible sound source, or reuse it to teach ultrasound fundamentals in medical physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article offers two forward-looking uses of the apparatus. It states the experiment "could be performed with a sound source with a frequency in the acoustic range in which case the Doppler shift would be audible as well as graphical", noting 40 kHz was chosen only because of transmitter/receiver availability. It also suggests the experiment "could also be useful for teaching the fundamentals of ultrasound in a medical physics course", since it is analogous to medical ultrasound Doppler, where MHz sound reflected off blood cells yields shifts in the audio range.
