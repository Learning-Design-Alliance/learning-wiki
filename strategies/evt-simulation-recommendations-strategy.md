# Modified EVT simulation and clinical procedure: independent focused training, realistic task timing, anaesthesia challenges, revised checklist and protocol changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concrete recommendations (Table 2): providing independent EVT training with sufficient time for focused learning goals; letting anaesthesia professionals contribute to design and implementation; a wet mannequin arm for fluid infusion; ensuring "essential technical task performance within a realistic time"; implementing clinically experienced challenges such as haemodynamic and respiratory instability; and emphasising team leaders' role in facilitating shared understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient time allocated by management; presence of responsible clinical team members across disciplines
#### Constraints
- Mannequin limitations made some aspects (e.g. restless patient behaviour) difficult to simulate; the tailored training's clinical effects require the planned follow-up study

### Target Learners
- EVT/NORA multidisciplinary team members in the angio suite

### Target Learning Goals
- protocol compliance
- efficient task distribution
- team effectiveness
- shared understanding of anaesthesia care
