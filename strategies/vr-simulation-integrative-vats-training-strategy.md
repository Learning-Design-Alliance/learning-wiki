# Incorporate VR simulation into VATS training programs as an integrative tool alongside clinical experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends using VR simulation within surgical training programs while preserving real operative experience. Bedetti and collaborators "used to create a VR curriculum representing an evidence-based approach for VATS training programs", and the review concludes the simulator "could be considered a sort of integrative tool to train novices in everyday surgical activities and to accelerate their VATS learning curve". It stresses real-life clinical experience cannot be completely replaced by any simulator.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulator training must complement, not replace, real-life clinical experience in the operating theatre
#### Constraints
- Further data are needed to identify the ideal simulator model and to propose an efficient simulation training program with validated performance measures

### Target Learners
- thoracic surgery trainees and residents
- novices in everyday surgical activities

### Target Learning Goals
- VATS lobectomy technical proficiency before operating on patients
