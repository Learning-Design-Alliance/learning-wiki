# Use the three-polarizer sequence as a step-by-step demonstration in undergraduate quantum physics lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete classroom recipe: place two perpendicular polarizers (0° and 90°) between laser and photometer and show no light passes; then insert a third polarizer at 45° between them and show light appears; finally reorder the outer polarizers and compare readings to exhibit commutation. The article states the experiment "can be done with students or used as a demonstrative experiment in laboratory based or theory based courses requiring quantum physics content".
