# Embed gamified formative assessment with gated progression in VR anatomy training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The IAVSE course paired real-time 3D model manipulation and anatomical jigsaw puzzles with haptic feedback against formative assessment using "a composite scoring algorithm, including spatial reasoning tasks with automated feedback". Game elements gated progression: advancing to subsequent levels required correctly answering previous questions, which the authors say increased engagement and fostered a sense of accomplishment during knowledge acquisition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR interaction programming in Unity with controller-based manipulation, layer toggling, and interactive 3D labeling
- Scheduling VR sessions within one day of the corresponding theoretical course to maintain learning continuity
#### Constraints
- Headset availability limits simultaneous training; the authors used a staggered group training protocol to accommodate only 10 Meta Quest 2 units

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students taking anatomy electives

### Target Learning Goals
- anatomical spatial reasoning and sectional (tomographic) anatomy comprehension

## Claims
