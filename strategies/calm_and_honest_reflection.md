# Calm and Honest Reflection

## Description
After feeling and expressing emotions related to a mistake, engaging in calm and honest reflection involves asking oneself what happened and what triggered those feelings. This includes examining the facts, identifying potential information gaps, considering impulsive decisions, and exploring whether unresolved emotions from the past were triggered. Evaluating fairness, examining expectations, and attempting to view the situation from another's perspective are crucial. Honesty, however difficult, is essential for growth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for reflection, a willingness to be honest with oneself, and the ability to consider multiple perspectives.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for individuals who struggle with self-awareness or have difficulty seeing beyond their own perspective. Could be emotionally taxing.
