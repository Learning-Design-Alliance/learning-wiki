# Run the distance wet lab as a five-week course with weekly interactive lectures and two recorded, graded assignments per week sequenced across phacoemulsification steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course structured learning as five weekly live video-conference lectures and demonstrations, each addressing specific sequential steps of phacoemulsification, each paired with two wet lab assignments that residents practised, recorded and uploaded for grading. "The course was designed to run for 5 weeks, with each week addressing specific, sequential steps in phacoemulsification surgery." Practice was self-paced, and assignments were conducted on animal eyes or artificial/model eyes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a wet lab with animal eyes or artificial/model eyes for recording video assignments
- A remote mentor to deliver interactive weekly lectures and grade uploaded assignments against the OSSCAR rubric
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- second- and final-year ophthalmology residents new to phacoemulsification

### Target Learning Goals
- phacoemulsification surgical skills across all OSSCAR steps
