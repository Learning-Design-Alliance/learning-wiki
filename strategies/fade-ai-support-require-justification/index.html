Design AI educational systems that fade support and require learner justification to preserve independent problem solving¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends designing AI-supported instruction that balances external assistance with the establishment of self-control, so that systems are supportive enough while still allowing independent problem solving. Concrete design moves include adaptive hint reduction and pre-response justification prompts: "smart tutors can provide less and less hints as the performance increases, or ask the learners to justify their answers before giving them."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • AI tutoring systems capable of adjusting support levels in response to learner performance

Constraints¶

  • Systems offering ongoing support can yield immediate performance improvements while reducing learners' chances of self-regulation; efficacy should not be judged on short-term outcomes only

Target Learners¶

  • school-age learners
  • adolescents
  • adult learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • independent problem solving
  • self-regulation development

Affordances¶

  • Human Ai Co Regulation Framework
