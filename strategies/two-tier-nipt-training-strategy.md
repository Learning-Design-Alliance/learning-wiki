# Strengthen NIPT training by focusing on weak knowledge areas and providing two tiers of information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that training on NIPT be strengthened by "providing greater focus on the areas where knowledge remained low, such as timing of results and false positive rates, and by providing two different levels of information". The two-tier approach would offer basic information to midwives only providing information about NIPT and more detailed information for those offering the test and discussing results, catering for differing information needs reflected in their data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training resources that can be adapted to different roles, with updates to maintain knowledge as NIPT is implemented
#### Constraints
- Some midwives found the written information sheet too detailed, so a single level of information does not suit all users

### Target Learners
- midwives offering NIPT information or discussing results
- antenatal Screening Coordinators delivering mandatory training

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate understanding of NIPT accuracy, false positive rates and turnaround time
- role-appropriate counselling about NIPT
