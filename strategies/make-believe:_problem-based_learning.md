# Make-Believe: Problem-Based Learning

## Description
Providing learners with real-world experiences and problems, and allowing them to work alongside experts, fosters engagement and prepares them for real-world challenges. Educators can give learners complex, open-ended problems that they can address collaboratively, mirroring real-world tasks and engaging learners in roles from the real world. When real-world play is incorporated into the classroom, learners are more motivated when projects are expansive and ideas are big. This approach involves thinking critically about the big ideas and finding where they live in the real world, creating situations and problems for learners to grapple with.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-world problems and scenarios. Expert guidance and support. Collaboration opportunities.
#### Constraints
- May require significant resources to develop and implement. Some learners may struggle with open-ended problems.
