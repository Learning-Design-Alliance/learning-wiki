# Use AI-generated vignette item sets for post-lecture review and preclinical preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants suggested integrating VEGA at the class level: "it can be integrated at the class level, where students are asked to solve a set of items related to the assigned ward before entering clinical practice." The tool can serve as a review after completing a body system in theory class and support transitioning nurses, such as new graduates or nurses changing departments, in exposure to unfamiliar clinical areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the VEGA tool and alignment of generated vignettes with the ward or body system being studied
#### Constraints
- The article notes the current version requires users to provide their own GPT API key, which may pose a practical barrier to accessibility

### Target Learners
- nursing students entering clinical practicum
- new and transferring nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- applying theoretical knowledge to practice-oriented clinical scenarios
