# Gallery Quote Walk Check-In

## Description
Students read displayed quotes and choose one that reflects their current feelings. Students gather by the quote they chose, and volunteers share why that quote resonates with them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A selection of quotes, space to display the quotes, time for individual reflection (3-5 minutes), and a willingness to share in a whole-class setting.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to find a quote that accurately represents their emotions. The effectiveness of the activity depends on creating a safe and supportive classroom environment.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All ages, adaptable for elementary through college students

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Express feelings through quotes, increase emotional awareness, build comfort in sharing emotions, foster a sense of connection

### Affordances
- Provides a structured way for students to connect with their emotions through literature. Encourages students to share their feelings in a safe and supportive environment. Promotes a sense of community through shared reflection.

### Personalization
- Students can choose from a range of quotes or create their own. The level of sharing can be adjusted based on individual comfort levels.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student participation in selecting and sharing quotes. Assess the thoughtfulness and specificity of students' explanations of their quotes.

## Impact
- Acknowledging emotions calms the amygdala and reduces the intensity of felt emotions, helping individuals feel more in control.  Promotes emotional literacy and awareness.
