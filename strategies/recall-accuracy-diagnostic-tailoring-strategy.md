# Use diagnostic quizzing and real-time recall accuracy data to classify learning-object difficulty and tailor instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators exploit recall accuracy data collected by the platform for evaluation and tailoring. "By providing a diagnostic test on the beginning a course in the form of the quiz task, educators can get a detailed snapshot of the material difficulty for the class." Educators can access real-time recall accuracy to tailor class structure, classify learning-object difficulty, estimate course effort, and identify content needing redesign, adaptation, or introductory information. Deviations from an expected session-dominated variance pattern could suggest flaws in content design or excessive course difficulty.
