# Train raters systematically and re-establish reliability and validity when applying ANTS in a new population or scenario

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the review's findings, the authors recommend that raters receive systematic training before using the ANTS system, and that its psychometric properties be re-established for new uses. The training "should consist of the practice of observing and rating, receiving feedback on the scores and discussing the appropriate use of the system". The review also recommends that "the reliability and validity should be re-established when the ANTS system is used for assessment in a new population or scenario", and that cultural differences be accounted for when adapting the tool to other countries and specialties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rater training including introduction of the ANTS system and simulation practice before assessment
- Formative assessment should provide feedback to trainees, including post-assessment debriefing and feedback sessions
#### Constraints
- An 8-hour programme could not train anaesthetists to reliably use the ANTS as a summative assessment tool
- How much training is needed to be competent in using the ANTS system remains undetermined

### Target Learners
- anaesthetists
- clinical assessors and raters of non-technical skills

### Target Learning Goals
- standardised, reliable assessment of non-technical skills
