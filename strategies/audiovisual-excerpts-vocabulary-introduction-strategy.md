# Use online audiovisual excerpts to introduce new vocabulary in an authentic target-language context, followed by transcript-based identification and communicative practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend extending audiovisual materials beyond listening comprehension to vocabulary development: instructors provide a partial or full transcript of a dialogue, students identify unfamiliar vocabulary items and decipher meaning from conversational context, and a follow-up activity requires students to use the new items in a communicative exercise. The article states that "audiovisual excerpts can be used to introduce new vocabulary items in an authentic target-language context," leveraging the accessibility and diversity of online media.
