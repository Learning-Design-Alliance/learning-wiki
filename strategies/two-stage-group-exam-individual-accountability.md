# Two-stage group exams with individual follow-up responses to provide collaboration with individual accountability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
One physics instructor implemented a group portion to introductory exams: "students worked in groups of four on typical physics problems. Then, the next class, the students individually wrote responses to questions that asked about the strategies and physics concepts they used to solve the problems." The goal was collaboration during assessment while maintaining individual accountability and reducing pressure toward academic misconduct. The order of this group exam is opposite that often described in the literature.
