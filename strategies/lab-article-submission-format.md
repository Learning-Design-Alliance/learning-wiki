# Submit lab work in a 'lab article' format modeled on a research paper

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Instead of a traditional lab report, students submit completed work in a "lab article" format, "constructed to be as close as possible to a typical research paper". This aligns the assessment artifact with the research-oriented, open-inquiry character of the modules, in which students make and justify decisions about models, equipment, and error evaluation. The author states details of the format will be presented and discussed elsewhere.
