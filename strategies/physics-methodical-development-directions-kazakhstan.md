## Description
The article enumerates directions for developing physics methodical education in Kazakhstan, stating that methodical development "has the following areas" including "theoretical modeling of methodical system of teaching physics", a technological approach in the educational environment, student involvement in search and self-research using new technologies, "Developing training methods solving physics problems with a computer and the use of computer simulation training methodology", and use of the computer in students' physics teaching and research activities. These serve as an implementable agenda for curriculum developers.
