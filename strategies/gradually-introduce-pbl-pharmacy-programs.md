# Pharmacy course directors should consider gradually introducing PBL methods into their programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 3 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that directors and teachers of pharmacy graduate and undergraduate courses should consider gradually introducing PBL methods into their programs. This recommendation follows the meta-analytic finding that PBL students performed better on objective examinations, while subjective evaluations did not differ. The authors also state that pharmaceutical education courses should consider implementing PBL.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Teacher and staff training
- A necessary reduction in class size
#### Constraints
