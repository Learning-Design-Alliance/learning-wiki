# Combine adaptive online practice with scripted offline small-group lessons tied to the digital content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that a blended learning approach pair the adaptive online component with "scripted offline lessons tied to the digital content", so students who struggle online receive individual or small-group instruction on specific skills from a trained staff member. Paper–pencil activities (Lexia Lessons and Lexia Skill Builders) reinforce and extend skills online activities alone cannot address, especially expressive-language standards. Real-time online reports let teachers target these offline sessions to each student's skill gaps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must use online reports to track student usage and progress and apply offline instruction to address specific student needs
#### Constraints
- The study could only monitor implementation through online use and progress data; the extent to which rotation models and offline materials were used was not measured

### Target Learners
- elementary students in grades K-5, particularly at-risk readers and English Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reading skills including phonics, vocabulary, and comprehension, with targeted remediation of individual skill gaps

### Affordances
- Lexia Reading Core5 Program
