# Scenario A: designing and deconstructing AI prompts as an epistemic practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-part activity treating prompt design 'not as a technical skill to be optimised, but as an epistemic practice'. In Part I students individually design 2-3 prompts to elicit depth or multiple perspectives without AI access; in Part II they analyse AI responses for epistemic breadth, assumptions and omissions, then revise at least one prompt and reflect on how question framing shapes knowledge construction. The LLM becomes 'an object of inquiry rather than a resource of authority'.
