# Provide need-based, just-in-time support and resources for faculty design work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because university teachers follow a non-systematic, cyclic design process, the article recommends that support and resources be delivered on demand rather than on a fixed schedule. It states: "Given the non-systematic cyclic design process of the teachers, need-based, just-in-time support and resources may be helpful." This strategy aligns institutional support with when teachers actually need it during their iterative design cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Support must match the non-systematic, cyclic nature of teachers' design process
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is framed as potentially helpful, not as a tested intervention

### Target Learning Goals
