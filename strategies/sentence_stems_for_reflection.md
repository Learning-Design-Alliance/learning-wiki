# Sentence Stems for Reflection

## Description
Teachers can use sentence stems to stimulate reflection and make it more accessible to learners. Use sentence stems in conferences (where reflection can be modeled), or put them on a sheet for students who choose writing to jump-start their reflections. Examples of possible sentence stems: 'I selected this piece of writing because...', 'What really surprised me about this piece of writing was...', 'When I look at my other pieces of writing, this piece is different because...', 'What makes this piece of writing strong is my use of...', 'Here is one example from my writing to show you what I mean', and 'What I want to really work on to make my writing better for a reader is...'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sentence stems and a way for students to record their reflections.
#### Constraints
- Sentence stems may limit students' creativity and ability to reflect in their own way.
