## Description
The article describes training students in high-quality prompt writing, stepwise questioning (Chain-of-Thought prompting), role-playing, and scenario-based questioning, with differentiated instruction for specific teaching tasks such as slide optimization and interactive design. Students were guided to use structured prompts and encouraged to iteratively refine prompts to obtain more pedagogically relevant outputs.
