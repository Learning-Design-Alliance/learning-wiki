# Departments should implement evidence-based, systematic, holistic approaches to graduate admissions and partner with programs producing large numbers of underrepresented graduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Among the paper's recommendations to departments on admissions are three concrete practices: "Partner with and recruit from undergraduate programs that produce large numbers of graduates from underrepresented groups (e.g., MSIs, HSIs, and Tribal Colleges)"; implement "evidence-based, systematic, holistic approaches to graduate admissions"; and coordinate with graduate schools so that program-level diversity policies are supported institutionally. The stated goals are that admitted-cohort demographics reflect the undergraduate availability pool and that admissions broaden definitions of excellence and merit.
