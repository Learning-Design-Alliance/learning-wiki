# Increase dedicated women's nutrition training hours and interventions in ObGyn specialty programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that Italian ObGyn specialty programs systematically include women's nutrition education. It reports that "there is an urgent need to develop specific training and interventions to educate our ObGyn residents" and that data "could incentivize Italian Specialty Programs in Obstetrics and Gynecology to increase the hours devoted to women's nutrition", because most residents currently report insufficient knowledge and curriculum time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specialty programs must choose to include nutrition content, since the Italian residency curriculum does not mandate specific hours of nutrition lectures
#### Constraints
- Only 5% of residents currently recommend supplementation across all surveyed conditions, so training should cover when supplementation is appropriate as well as dietary adequacy

### Target Learners
- ObGyn residents in university specialty programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of women's nutritional needs across life stages
- Appropriate nutritional and supplementation counseling in patient care
