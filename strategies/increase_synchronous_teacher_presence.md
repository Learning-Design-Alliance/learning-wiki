# Increase Synchronous Teacher Presence

## Description
Teacher presence in synchronous sessions helps to reduce feelings of isolation in students enrolled in online courses and increase student engagement and agency, and ways to reduce distractions and cognitive overload.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should research, study, and experiment with synchronous sessions.
#### Constraints
- May be difficult to manage distractions.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Students are given a chance to interact with a teacher live in the synchronous session and can reduce feelings of isolation. Research shows synchronous sessions affect how satisfied students are with online learning and how useful the classes are.

### Personalization
- Instructors can use video chat to reveal body language and tone of voice.
