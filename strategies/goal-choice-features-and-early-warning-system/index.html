Build APLE features that let students choose learning goals and receive indicator-based guidance, plus an early warning system for students at risk of failing¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors propose two follow-on developments grounded in this study's indicators. First, APLE features should "allow students to choose their learning goals and receive guidance towards achieving them, based on the indicators revealed in the present study". Second, they plan "designing an early warning system and prediction algorithm that utilizes the indicators and values introduced in this study to support students who are at risk of failing the course". They also suggest logistic regression to identify predictive behaviors and goal surveys administered during the course.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Indicator values and thresholds identified in this study as a basis for guidance and prediction

Constraints¶

  • The study was quasi-experimental, single-course, and distance-only, which the authors state may limit generalizability to other educational levels or face-to-face settings

Target Learners¶

  • distance higher education students, including those at risk of failing

Target Learning Goals¶

  • achieving chosen learning goals (passing or mastering)
  • sustained engagement across course units

Claims¶

  • Student activity declines consistently from the first to the last learning unit across all three outcome groups [+W]
  • Mastering students outperform Passing and Non-passing students across all four dimensions of quiz task activity (views, submissions, repetitions, scores) [+W]
  • Self-assessment task activity shows no differences among outcome groups except for the number of tasks viewed [~W]
