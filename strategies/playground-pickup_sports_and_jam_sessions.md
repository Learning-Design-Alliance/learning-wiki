# Playground: Pickup Sports and Jam Sessions

## Description
Encouraging informal games and activities without emphasizing competition provides opportunities for play and creativity. Teens can engage in pickup sports or jam sessions to foster collaboration and enjoyment. This approach contrasts with competitive extracurricular activities and allows learners to play without the pressure of winning or performing professionally. Informal play promotes social interaction, creativity, and intrinsic motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Space and equipment for informal games and activities. Instructor encouragement and support.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may prefer structured activities. May require careful management to ensure inclusivity.
