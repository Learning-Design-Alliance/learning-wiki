# Integrate structured opportunities for literature analysis, evidence-based discussions, and small-scale research tasks early in the curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that faculty integrate "structured opportunities for literature analysis, evidence-based discussions, and small-scale research tasks early in the curriculum" to help students progress toward more complex and reflective forms of inquiry. This responds to the finding that research-related competences often become meaningful only in later stages of education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty capacity to design and supervise research-related activities
#### Constraints
- The article notes students frequently perceive research competences as meaningful only in later stages of education

### Target Learners
- first-cycle health professions students
- second-cycle master's students

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry skills
- evidence evaluation
- research engagement
