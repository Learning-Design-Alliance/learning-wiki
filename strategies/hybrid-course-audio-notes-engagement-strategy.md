# Require audio use and note taking and add engagement mechanisms when running a hybrid lecture course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendations for future versions of the hybrid class: "ensure that all students listen to the audio component of the online lecture and take handwritten or typed lecture notes" and "identify more ways (either online or otherwise) in which to engage students in the material". They also suggest possibly limiting freshman admission to the hybrid version to students above a threshold GPA, given that prior GPA and class standing predicted final scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must actually listen to the audio component and take handwritten or typed lecture notes for the online lectures to support performance
#### Constraints
- The authors note that inclusion of more online media is not necessarily more effective in increasing student learning than other approaches, citing the Means et al. (2010) report

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in large introductory science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- introductory microbiology course learning outcomes
