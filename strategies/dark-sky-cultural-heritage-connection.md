# Connect dark-sky observation to local cultural heritage and history to deepen engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Link night-sky observation to local history, stories, and cultural heritage to give students and the public a personal connection to dark-sky preservation. In the Cyber-Cosmos camp, students learned that "night navigation was essential in a region where transhumance was in a not so distant past an important seasonal activity, and where old trade routes between mountainous villages were established using sky reference points." This grounds astronomy in place-based meaning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A location with dark skies and documented local history or traditions connected to the night sky
- Willingness to collect and preserve local stories and legends
#### Constraints
- Most relevant to regions with tangible cultural connections to the night sky

### Target Learners
- young students
- local community members

### Target Learning Goals
- cultural heritage awareness
- dark-sky appreciation
- astronomy knowledge
