# Offer adult learners flexible, continuously available tutoring outside traditional work hours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because adult learners balance work and study, the article recommends technological flexibility in when tutoring is accessible. Usage data showed engagement peaking in evenings and late nights and on Thursdays, and prior work found learners "expressed a desire to access tutors at any time, highlighting the need for continuous availability to support diverse learning schedules". The strategy is to keep tutors available on demand so learners can engage around their unique schedules and before assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutoring system must be continuously available and integrated into students' existing learning management systems
#### Constraints
- Evidence is observational; the Thursday peak is the authors' interpretation linked to Friday examinations

### Target Learners
- adult learners balancing full-time employment with education

### Target Learning Goals
- flexible supplementary practice and skill reinforcement aligned to course schedules
