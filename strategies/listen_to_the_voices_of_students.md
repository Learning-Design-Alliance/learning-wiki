# Listen to the Voices of Students

## Description
Providing students authorship and allowing them to share their stories empowers them, fostering agency and leadership. This approach seeks to disrupt traditional power dynamics in the classroom by emphasizing youth voice and promoting equitable inclusion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creating a classroom environment that is safe and inclusive. Demands a shift from teacher-centered to student-centered instruction.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time to facilitate student-led discussions and activities. Can be challenging to manage if students are not accustomed to taking initiative.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- K-12 learners, particularly beneficial for students of color who may have their stories pre-written in traditional settings.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Empower students, foster agency and leadership, create an equitable learning environment, value student perspectives, disrupt traditional power dynamics.

### Affordances
- Creates a classroom environment where student voices are valued and amplified. Promotes agency by allowing students to shape their own learning experiences. Develops leadership skills by encouraging students to take ownership of their education.

### Personalization
- Allows students to share their unique stories and perspectives. Adapts to various communication styles and learning preferences.
