# Add tutor interaction channels (virtual forum and optional in-person sessions) and strengthen statistical explanations in online critical appraisal courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participant feedback and the authors' discussion of this course evaluation, they propose improving communication and support in online critical appraisal courses. Participants "suggested the creation of a forum to improve communication between participants as well as between participants and tutors, and more explanations of the statistical concepts." The authors add that "in-person training sessions to discuss doubts may be necessary for some learners" and note that email-only contact may have limited interaction. The strategy is to build tutor-student interaction mechanisms and reinforce statistical background content into e-learning course design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors available to answer questions and monitor student progress through the course
#### Constraints
- In this study, students only had contact with tutors and the coordinating team by email, which the authors say may have limited interaction

### Target Learners
- Nurses in continuing professional education
- Health professionals in online self-directed courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical appraisal skills
- Understanding of statistical and epidemiological concepts
- Communication with tutors and peers
