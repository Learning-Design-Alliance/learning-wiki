# Incorporate obstetric emergency simulation modules with structured personalised feedback into critical care nursing programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that critical care nursing programmes add obstetric emergency simulation with personalised feedback, because intensive care nurses may be first responders to deteriorating obstetric patients. It states that "critical care nursing programmes should consider incorporating obstetric emergency simulation modules with structured personalised feedback" and that the quality and personalisation of feedback, rather than equipment sophistication alone, may be the primary driver of skill acquisition in resource-limited settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation scenarios aligned with emergency obstetric care guidelines
- Structured, learner-centred feedback processes
#### Constraints
- The article notes this remains an understudied area for critical care nurses' readiness specifically, and recommends multicentric RCTs with larger samples

### Target Learners
- critical care nursing students
- nurses in intensive care or emergency settings

### Target Learning Goals
- recognition and management of obstetric haemorrhage
- readiness for high-acuity obstetric emergencies
