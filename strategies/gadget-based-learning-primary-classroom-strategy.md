# Adopt gadget-based learning as a classroom strategy for primary-level English grammar teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the author recommends gadget-based learning as an implementable classroom strategy: "Gadget Based Learning can become an effective strategy in the classroom teaching at primary level". He further states it is effective for grammar and related subjects, suits slow and average learners, fosters self-reliance, confidence and cooperation, and, if well planned and executed, can extend to upper primary levels and all categories of primary students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Gadgets prepared with low-cost materials and used with teacher facilitation
- Well planned and executed for extension to upper primary level
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is the author's own two-school study at standard II; further studies in different dimensions are called essential

### Target Learners
- primary-level students, including slow and average learners

### Target Learning Goals
- English grammar achievement and reasoning skills
