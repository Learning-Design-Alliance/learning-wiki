# Sentence Starters

## Description
When you ask a question, rather than just having students raise hands and you picking one volunteer, have every student in the class write a sentence. This way, students can be active and can get the feel of writing about math.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a question or prompt that can be answered in a sentence. Requires time for students to write and share their sentences.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of questions. Some students may struggle to write a sentence if they do not understand the concept.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Increase student participation, encourage active learning, and provide opportunities for students to practice writing about math.

### Affordances
- Promotes active participation from all students, not just those who volunteer. Provides a low-stakes way for students to practice writing about math. Allows the teacher to quickly assess student understanding.

### Personalization
- Teachers can provide different sentence stems or prompts based on student needs and abilities. Students can also be given the option to write more than one sentence.
