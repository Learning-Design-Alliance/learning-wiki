# Build a collaborative online discourse community for peer review in learning cycles to develop integrated reading/writing skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that reading and writing teachers attend to systemic use of online course tools and build a collaborative online discourse community for encouraging peer review in learning cycles. This fosters both accuracy and fluency in reading and writing through meaningful and useful feedback. The strategy was implemented through intra-team and inter-team work on discussion boards, blogs and wikis over a 16-week course.
