# Run a 3-2 dual degree program with engineering schools, guaranteeing acceptance on completion of required courses with a specified GPA

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Students spend three years studying physics at La Crosse, then transfer to a partner engineering school for two years, receiving a physics degree after the first engineering year and an engineering degree after the second. The essential feature is that 'a student is guar- anteed acceptance into the engineering school upon completion of a set of physics and other required courses with a speciﬁed grade point ave rage.' The authors call it one of the most important programs introduced and strongly recommend it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaborative agreements with engineering schools
- A set of required physics courses and a specified grade point average that trigger guaranteed acceptance
#### Constraints
- Requires sustained partnership agreements with named partner institutions (University of Wisconsin - Madison and University of Wisconsin - Milwaukee)

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics students interested in engineering careers

### Target Learning Goals
- Completion of both a physics and an engineering degree
