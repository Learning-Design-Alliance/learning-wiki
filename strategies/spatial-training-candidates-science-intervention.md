# Train mental folding, spatial scaling, and disembedding as candidates for curriculum-embedded spatial interventions supporting science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because spatial skills are malleable and folding, scaling, and disembedding predict children's science achievement, the article recommends them as good candidates for spatial training. It suggests long-term interventions training multiple spatial skills embedded within the curriculum, following a mathematics example, and notes such interventions could yield long-term benefits for science achievement and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- evidence that spatial skills are malleable; long-term, curriculum-embedded delivery training multiple skills
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is based on correlational prediction, not a tested intervention in this study

### Target Learners
- primary-school children aged 7–11

### Target Learning Goals
