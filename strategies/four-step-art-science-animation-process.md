# Four-step art–science collaboration process: scientist briefing, facility visit with concept pitches, mentored production with online feedback, and final screening critique

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a four-step process for pairing art students with scientists. Step one: a scientist visit explaining basic astrophysical concepts, followed by group storyboarding. Step two: students visit the lab and pitch concepts, after which mentors are identified. Step three: mentored production with a Tumblr page for mentor feedback. Step four: a screening whose discussion comments "were incorporated into the final versions."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to scientist mentors willing to give feedback
- A venue or online platform for mentor feedback during production
#### Constraints
- The process was carried out with 17 students in one junior-level 2D animation class over one semester

### Target Learners
- undergraduate art students

### Target Learning Goals
- communicating scientific concepts through animation
- understanding scientific practice
