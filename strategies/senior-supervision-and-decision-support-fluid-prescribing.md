# Increase senior supervision of fluid and electrolyte prescribing and develop clinical decision support tools to guide it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends more frequent senior input into fluid and electrolyte management, which may generate opportunities to change prescribing behaviours, and further development and implementation of clinical decision support tools to guide prescribing. It notes decision support has been associated with improved prescribing safety and has successfully increased prescribing of balanced crystalloids over 0.9% sodium chloride.
