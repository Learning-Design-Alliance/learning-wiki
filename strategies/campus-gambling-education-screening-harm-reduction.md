# Integrate gambling education, screening, and harm reduction strategies into student wellness initiatives on college campuses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that campuses respond to the potential academic harms of gambling among undergraduates by building gambling-related content into existing student wellness programming. It states that "Findings suggest a need to integrate gambling education, screening, and harm reduction strategies into student wellness initiatives," particularly given the rapid expansion of legalized gambling and targeted advertising aimed at youth. This is a programming recommendation grounded in the review's synthesis rather than a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires institutional attention to gambling as a public health and student wellbeing issue
#### Constraints
- The review notes additional research is needed to better understand the gambling-academic relationship before interventions can be fully informed

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate college students

### Target Learning Goals
- Protecting academic performance (grades/GPA) and supporting student success and wellbeing
