# Design fire alarm voice messages under 30 s in three phases: call to attention, reason for the call, and information on what to do

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the fire-scenario design step, the protocol specifies how alarm audio should be authored: "the alarm message must be set up to be effective, with a total duration of less than 30 s, divided into three phases: call to attention, reason for the call and information on what to do". This gives evacuation designers a concrete, implementable recipe for alarm communication, alongside defining the type, frequency and volume of the fire alarm as scenario parameters.
