# Expose students to learning through exploration, questioning, and writing down questions and answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes that when students are exposed to "learning through exploration, asking question, answering questions and writing down questions and answers on what they have explored, concepts are more appropriately internalized with the learner taking responsibility of their own learning". This describes the learner-centred activity structure of the SRL instructional package used in the treatment group, in contrast to expository chalk-talk teaching with minimal student participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers adhere strictly to a lesson procedure developed from the instructional package during pre-experimental training
#### Constraints
- Implemented over eight weeks on a single Basic Science unit with intact classes; initial equivalence of groups could not be achieved

### Target Learners
- Junior secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Internalising basic science concepts and taking responsibility for one's own learning
