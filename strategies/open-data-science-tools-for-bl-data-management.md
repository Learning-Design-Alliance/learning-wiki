# Use modern open data science tools, increasingly accessible via generative AI, to simplify handling of educational data in blended learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that blended learning teachers and researchers use modern open data science tools to simplify the handling of educational data. From the authors' ongoing projects, "these tools greatly streamline the process with just a few clicks, eliminating the need for extensive setups or substantial software investments". With the rise of generative AI such as ChatGPT, these tools have become accessible to BL teachers and researchers without programming skills, allowing a broader range of educators to enhance their teaching, while teachers can supply measurement data for new statistical analyses in physics and STEM education.
