# Develop screening measures and targeted interventions specific to the preterm population's learning difficulties

## Description
The article recommends that differences in the etiology and presentations of learning difficulties in preterm compared with term-born groups suggest "the need for measures that screen for problems specific to the preterm population". As the first meta-analysis to delineate academic subskills and their associations with preterm birth, its subskill-level findings are offered to inform screening measure development and to help teachers and education specialists develop targeted interventions or specialized teaching plans for preterm students experiencing academic problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Measures more sensitive to reading and mathematics deficits in preterm children than traditional psychoeducational batteries, which are normed to the general population
#### Constraints
- The article notes psychoeducational batteries may not be sensitive to the patterns of learning deficits in children born preterm

### Target Learners
- school-aged children (5-18 years) born preterm

### Target Learning Goals
- reading and mathematics achievement across higher- and lower-order subskills
