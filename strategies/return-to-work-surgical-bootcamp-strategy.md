# Structured return-to-work simulation bootcamp for surgeons returning after time out of training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's authors recommend a structured return-to-work program focused on reacquisition of operating skills before resuming independent practice. Multiple included studies "have called for the requirement of a surgical 'bootcamp' prior to return to work", combining simulation sessions such as cadaveric and laparoscopic workshops with refresher training. The precise curriculum remains to be determined, and each moderating factor (task, operator, retention interval) should be addressed within the program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized method of quantifying pre-time-out and post-time-out performance to identify surgeons requiring retraining, which the review states does not yet exist
#### Constraints
- The precise curriculum for the bootcamp requires further research
- The factors' individual contributions to skill decay are unclear

### Target Learners
- surgical residents and surgeons returning to clinical practice after time out of training

### Target Learning Goals
- regain technical operative skill (speed and accuracy) and confidence before resuming independent operating
