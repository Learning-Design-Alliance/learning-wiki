# Use technology after learner effort to protect productive struggle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends giving learners space to generate their own thinking and ideas before introducing technology supports. When learners use LLMs to automate writing before initial sense-making, this 'can reduce both cognitive effort and ability to reflect upon and modify the automated output.' When learners work through sense-making first, they are better able to consider where the technology improves their initial thinking, using it as a partner and enabling deeper reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning designs must provide space and sequencing for learners' own initial efforts before technology support is introduced
