# Use mutual learning structures as an instructional modification requiring no extra teacher time or effort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the researcher recommends mutual learning structures as an easily adopted modification to ordinary instruction. The article states "Mutual learning structures can be easily used as a modification to instruction with no extra time or effort required of the teacher" and that one lesson plan using such structures "has built in peer tutoring and support within the heterogeneous class groupings," eliminating the need for several different plans to meet all students' needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Heterogeneous class groupings with built-in peer tutoring and support within the lesson plan.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation derives from a single quasi-experimental study of B.Ed students in one college.

### Target Learners
- B.Ed students of diverse abilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement in Optional II English
