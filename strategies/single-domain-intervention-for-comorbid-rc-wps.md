# Use single-domain text-structure intervention to address comorbid reading and word-problem difficulty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because cross-domain transfer was demonstrated in both directions, the authors suggest text-structure intervention in one domain may help address comorbid RC and WPS difficulty, easing scheduling and resource burdens. The article states: "Findings suggest that text-structure intervention may help address the complex needs of children with comorbid RC and WPS difficulty." Because reading intervention usually takes priority, many such children are underserved in math; a single-domain approach offers proof of concept for serving both needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- intervention must include text-structure vocabulary spanning both domains and explicit transfer instruction on domain parallels
#### Constraints
- Proof-of-concept from one RCT at second grade; not preregistered; outcomes were researcher-developed measures

### Target Learners
- children with comorbid reading comprehension and word-problem solving difficulty

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- word-problem solving
