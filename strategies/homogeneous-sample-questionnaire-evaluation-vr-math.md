# Evaluate VR math environments by collecting homogeneous-sample student questionnaire responses before drawing conclusions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose evaluating their system by having high school students rate the work via questionnaire, selecting a sample "homogeneous by social and cultural level, in order to obtain a set of coherent and indicative answers", so that a sufficiently high number of students allows objective conclusions about how VR affects perception of mathematics. They also plan to gather interest, empathic response, sensations, disturbances and emotions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a number of students high enough to draw objective conclusions
- a significant sample homogeneous by social and cultural level
#### Constraints
- At the time of writing the questionnaire had not yet been administered, so no conclusions are reported in this article

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- perception of and attitude toward mathematics
- quality and livability of the immersive learning environment
