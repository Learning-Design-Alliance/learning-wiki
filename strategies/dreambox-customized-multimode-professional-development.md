# Provide customized, multi-mode professional development for teachers implementing DreamBox Math

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
DreamBox Learning offers "comprehensive professional development that is customized to the unique needs of each district, school, and teacher". Delivery is flexible — "in person, online via webinar, and through job-embedded learning opportunities" — with PD courses letting teachers "brush up on skills or learn new strategies for teaching a concept". This makes teacher support an implementable part of program rollout.
