# Design in-class interventions and school-based programs that facilitate, promote, and foster personal best

## Description
The article recommends that educators deliberately design learning environments that help students reach their personal best. It states: "As educators, we need to consider different types of in-class interventions and/or school-based programs that could facilitate, promote, and foster personal best." The study's findings suggest such interventions should target motivation towards learning, personal resolve, and social relationships, since these operated as sources of, or agencies for, optimal best practice in the SEM model.
