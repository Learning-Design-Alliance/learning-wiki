# Standards-Based Grading

## Description
Shift from arbitrary points and percentages to evaluating students on mastery of specific learning standards, making expectations clear and grades meaningful.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined standards
#### Constraints
- Requires significant training
