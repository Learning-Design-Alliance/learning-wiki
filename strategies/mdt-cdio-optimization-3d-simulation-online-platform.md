# Increase 3D simulation training frequency and establish an online MDT discussion platform to optimize the MDT-CDIO model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on student optimization recommendations from the qualitative analysis, the article endorses two improvement directions: increasing the frequency of 3D simulation training to enhance understanding of orthopedic conditions, and establishing an online MDT discussion platform to foster teamwork and knowledge sharing. The authors state these suggestions "should not be viewed as 'additional topics' separate from the research findings, but rather understood as improvement directions proposed by students based on their actual learning experiences." The 3D suggestion aligns with orthopedic learning's need for spatial anatomical understanding and visualization of surgical pathways; the platform suggestion extends interdisciplinary communication beyond fixed classroom sessions to pre-class preparation, post-class debriefing, and continuous collaborative learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology-enhanced teaching tools such as 3D printing and multimodal visualization tools
- An online platform supporting pre-class preparation, post-class debriefing, and continuous collaborative learning
#### Constraints
- Suggestions derive from student recommendations in a single-center study and their educational value is expected to be further validated in larger-scale implementation

### Target Learners
- orthopedic clinical internship students

### Target Learning Goals
- spatial anatomical understanding
- visualization of surgical pathways
- teamwork and knowledge sharing
- continuous collaborative learning
