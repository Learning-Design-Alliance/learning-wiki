# Use extended matching questions with long shared option lists to assess clinical reasoning without cueing

## Description
The article describes EMQs as developed so that "without cueing from a short list of response options, clinical reasoning and knowledge may be assessed". An option list of 5 to more than 25 options is shared across questions on a theme, and changing the patient scenario can change the correct answer, reflecting clinical practice.
