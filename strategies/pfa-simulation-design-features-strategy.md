# Deliver pre-foundation simulation with hands-on scenarios, structured debriefing and facilitator continuity to optimise the learning environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that simulation-based learning for pre-foundation doctors emphasise hands-on learning, effective debriefs and facilitator continuity. The authors state these insights "suggest that integrating simulation-based learning for pre-foundation doctors may help with their preparedness for practice, with particular emphasis on ensuring there is hands-on learning, effective debriefs and facilitator continuity." Facilitator continuity fosters personalised feedback and a trusting, psychologically safe learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured debriefing sessions after each scenario
- The same facilitator across scenarios to enable personalised, cohesive feedback
- Authentic, immersive scenarios that mimic real clinical practice
#### Constraints
- Evidence for these features comes from student self-report; the authors note unclear evidence regarding which aspects of simulation create an optimal learning environment in this setting

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students
- pre-foundation doctors

### Target Learning Goals
- preparedness for practice
- confidence in managing acutely unwell patients
