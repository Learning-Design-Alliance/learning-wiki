# Embed technical skills practice in a full clinical encounter simulation with preoperative and postoperative tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training protocol wraps the technical procedure in a simulated complete clinical encounter. Trainees first complete "preoperative preparations, including evaluation of the superficial mass, determination of the suitable surgical procedure, informed consent, and patient identity check", then perform the operation, then handle postoperative tasks such as sharps disposal, instrument recycling, and postoperative communications. This strategy assesses both technical procedural skills and essential clinical competencies like communication and preparation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers who quantitatively rate performance using a procedure-specific rating scale and surgical DOPS
- A simulation scenario with defined preoperative, operative, and postoperative steps
#### Constraints
- Assessment in this study was short-term, with no follow-up on skill retention or transfer to real clinical procedures

### Target Learners
- surgery residents (PGY-1 to PGY-3)

### Target Learning Goals
- informed consent and patient communication
- aseptic technique
- superficial mass resection
- postoperative care and documentation
