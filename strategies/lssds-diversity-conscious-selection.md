## Description
A selection strategy in which application sorting deliberately balances multiple diversity dimensions before admission. The article states: "When sorting the students applications, we try to keep a balance between the diﬀerent minors and majors, we look for diversity of instit utions (keeping a balance between students from big / well-known schools and those le ss renowned), gender balance, and nationality." The authors group this selection process, with faculty commitment, among the two aspects they believe drive the program's success.
