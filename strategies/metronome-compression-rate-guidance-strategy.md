# Use a metronome during CPR training to guide chest compression rate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 7 studies (3 causal, 2 review, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 7 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because chest compression rates exceeded guideline recommendations under both teaching techniques, the article recommends rate guidance independent of teaching method. It states that "a metronome should be used independently of the teaching technique, as it was shown to significantly improve the ability of students to produce a chest compression rate close to the ideal." The strategy targets the rate variable specifically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A metronome or audio pacing device available during practice
#### Constraints
- Addresses compression rate only; the study did not test the metronome itself in this trial, citing other studies

### Target Learners
- medical students
- CPR trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- chest compression rate within guideline-recommended range
