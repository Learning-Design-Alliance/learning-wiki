Use a CFT-based epistemic stance to inform the future design and practice of MOOCs and online learning¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The article proposes that a CFT-based epistemic stance should inform the future design and practice of MOOCs and online learning in general. It states that the authors "discuss how a CFT-based epistemic stance may further inform the future design and practice of MOOCs and, in a similar manner, online learning in general". The strategy builds on the value of diverse perspectives afforded by the MOOCocracy culture while adding CFT's epistemic reframing.
