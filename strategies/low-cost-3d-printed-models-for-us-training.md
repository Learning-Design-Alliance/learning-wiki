# Use a low-cost 3D-printed simulator to reduce financial barriers in ultrasound-guided procedural training

## Description
The article recommends leveraging low-cost, 3D-printed models as "a cost-effective alternative to commercial models, reducing financial barriers in medical education", noting the custom simulator cost approximately USD 40 versus approximately USD 1,000 for comparable commercial trainers. This may facilitate adoption in resource-limited training settings.
