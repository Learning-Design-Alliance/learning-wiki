# Use longitudinal interim assessment data, modeled with seasonal trends, to monitor progress of students, classrooms, and schools within and across school years

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a modeling approach for interim assessment data that respects their seasonal structure. It notes that "These tests are often used to monitor the progress of students, classrooms, and schools within and across school years," and argues that models should capture both within-year gains and losses and year-to-year growth rather than ignoring one or the other.
