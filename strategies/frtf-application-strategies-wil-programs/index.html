Apply the FRTF as curricular support, as a student and program assessment tool, and to map academic learning outcomes to workplace-relevant language¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article names three planned applications of the FRTF: "as curricular support, as an assessment tool both at the student and program level, and as a tool for integration between WIL experiences and academic curriculum." Concretely, the FRTF can anchor WIL preparatory curriculum and workplace reflections, collect employer or student feedback via its behavioral statements for student self-assessment and program gap analysis, and link course or program learning outcomes to talents employers recognize, with mapping exercises potentially revealing curricular gaps.
