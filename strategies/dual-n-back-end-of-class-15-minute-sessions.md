# Use dual n-back training sessions of about 15 minutes, four days per week, at the end of language classes to expand learners' phonological memory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe: "The experimental groups performed the task four days per week for 6 weeks" and "were instructed to complete the training sessions at the end of class for 15 minutes," using the freely available Brain Workshop software on classroom desktop computers. One session consisted of 15 rounds of the n-back, and teachers were first trained on a round of 2-back before introducing it to participants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to desktop computers in class; teachers first explained the task and completed a demonstration round of 2-back before learners practiced until confident
#### Constraints
- Tested only with adult beginner-level EFL learners aged 20 to 30 over a six-week course; the article does not report effects on L2 learning outcomes themselves

### Target Learners
- adult EFL learners at the beginner level

### Target Learning Goals
- phonological memory capacity expansion supporting L2 learning
