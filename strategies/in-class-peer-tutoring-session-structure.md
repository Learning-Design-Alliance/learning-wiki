# In-class peer tutoring structured as 30 minutes of note comparison, feedback and doubt clarification after the teacher's explanation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The implemented strategy embedded tutoring within the 50-minute English class: the first 20 minutes were the teacher's explanation, and in the last 30 minutes each tutor monitored their tutee through comparison and complementation of class notes, explained basic concepts, and dispelled doubts. The stage lasted 11 weeks, and the teacher strategically seated tutors and tutees close together to monitor them. Tutors were recruited from students scoring above 80, prepared the topics in advance, took notes, and provided feedback on tutees' doubts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors attend classes, make class notes, accompany and support their tutee during practice exercises, explain topics seen in class and clarify doubts
- Tutor preparation in advance of the topics to be seen in each session
- Teacher accommodation of tutors and tutees in the classroom so they are close enough to monitor
#### Constraints
- The article states the need for timely training of tutors prior to implementation and for identifying tutor profiles to make the model more efficient

### Target Learners
- Last-year high school students with low English performance (grades below 65)

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved grades in the English learning unit
- Clarified understanding of class topics through note comparison and feedback
