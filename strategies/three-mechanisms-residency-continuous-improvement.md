# Use three continuous improvement mechanisms: annual Advisory Council meetings, program surveys, and weekly leadership meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Faculty and program leaders engage in collaborative, solutions-oriented continuous improvement, modeling the learning orientation expected of residents and mentors. The report states Claremont used "three main mechanisms to collect data and engage in continuous improvement: (1) annual Advisory Council meetings, (2) program surveys both during and at the end of each semester, and (3) weekly leadership meetings." Program leaders, instructors, and clinical faculty advisors also monitor candidates' academic, social, and emotional well-being using formative assessments, surveys, frequent check-ins, and course exit tickets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Inclusion of all faculty, including adjunct instructors and clinical faculty advisors, in program meetings in a nonhierarchical way
#### Constraints
- Described as practiced at one small program; the report presents this as a case description rather than a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- teacher residency program faculty and leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- program quality improvement
- responsiveness to resident needs
