# Maintain Optimal Classroom Temperature

## Description
The temperature in a workspace affects engagement levels and overall productivity. The ideal temperature range for effective learning in reading and mathematics is between 68º and 74º. Teachers need to be able to control the temperature in their own classrooms, or at least small blocks of classrooms, to maintain a comfortable learning environment. Ensuring appropriate temperature levels enhances student engagement and learning.

## Design Implications
