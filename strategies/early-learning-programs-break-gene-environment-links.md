# Improve children's access to interventions, such as high-quality early learning programs, that can break reinforcing gene-environment links

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because gene-environment correlations tend to match and reinforce children's genetic dispositions, a child genetically at risk for poor educational outcomes tends not to receive the stimulating, supportive parenting that could help. The authors argue this makes it "important to improve children's access to interventions that may be able to break reinforcing links between genes and environments, such as high-quality early learning programs". The strategy targets children whose home environments are less aligned with their genetic propensities for educational success.
