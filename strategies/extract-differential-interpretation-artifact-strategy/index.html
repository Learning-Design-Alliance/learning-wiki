Use latent variable models to extract differential interpretation as a methodological artifact from measurement models¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy uses certain latent variable models to separate a methodological artifact—differential interpretation of vague quantifiers—from measurement models that are intended to be purely substantive. The article states that its analyses "illustrate how a researcher might use certain latent variable models to extract a methodological artifact—differential interpretation—from measurement models intended to be purely substantive." It is a methodological recipe for survey researchers rather than an instructional intervention.

Context¶
