# Universities should build structured SDL programs and PsyCap support schemes into faculty development for interdisciplinary teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical implications recommend that faculty development centers offer "modular, goal-setting-based workshops tailored to interdisciplinary teaching contexts" with digital learning portfolios and micro-credentials, and that institutions integrate structured reflection, SDL journals, and resilience training into performance review systems. It further recommends PsyCap support schemes run jointly by counseling centers and faculty affairs offices, including semester-based resilience workshops, peer coaching groups, and confidence-building counseling for interdisciplinary teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support from faculty development centers, counseling centers, and faculty affairs offices
- Embedding the initiatives in broader professional development policies for sustainability
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are grounded in a cross-sectional, self-report study of STEM lecturers in selected regions of China, which the authors state may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- University STEM lecturers

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing competency in interdisciplinary teaching
- Strengthening psychological capital (self-efficacy, resilience, optimism) through self-directed learning
