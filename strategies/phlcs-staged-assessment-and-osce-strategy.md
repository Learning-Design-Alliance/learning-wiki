# Integrate the PHLCS into staged medical education assessment and OSCEs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the PHLCS across medical training stages to support competency-based development. It states the scale "can be applied in Objective Structured Clinical Examinations to evaluate medical students’ communication strategies in simulated patient encounters," and that "PHLCS-informed workshops for residents and practicing physicians can help improve SDM and patient-centered communication." Ongoing instructional assessment with the tool is proposed as a route to progressively improve students’ health literacy competencies.
