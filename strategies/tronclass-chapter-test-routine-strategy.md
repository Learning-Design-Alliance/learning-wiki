# Time-limited chapter tests with randomized item order and delayed answer release

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable assessment routine on the platform: teachers upload chapter videos before class and post reflection questions; after each chapter students take a time-limited test of “10 multiple-choice and judgmental questions” with “a time limit of 5 min”. Each student gets a different order of questions or options, “Only scores, but no correct answers, were released after the tests”, and answers with explanations are published after the whole chapter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must upload chapter videos and reflection questions before class, and the platform must auto-score tests and calculate the error rate and distribution of options for each question.
#### Constraints
- Applied to self-learned simple chapters such as Disease Overview, Child Growth and Development, and Newborn Care; difficult chapters such as systemic diseases and fluid therapy were taught offline.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- memorization, comprehension, and analysis ability
- independent learning of course chapters
