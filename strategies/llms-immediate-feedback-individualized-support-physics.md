# Use LLMs as learning supports offering immediate feedback and individualized learning experiences, not as replacements for human instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that LLMs underperform students on physics exams yet remain capable assistants, the authors recommend using ChatGPT and BingChat to help students and teachers during learning and teaching activities, "particularly by offering immediate feedback and individualized learning experiences." They also note LLMs can produce practice and test materials and give students access to materials and tailored feedback regardless of location or socioeconomic situation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accuracy and reliability of LLMs in specialized fields like physics need to be improved, and platforms and tools need to be created to ease classroom integration
#### Constraints
- LLMs are not yet capable of fully replacing human intellect in physics teaching, given their limited capacity for high-level application problems

### Target Learners
- Vietnamese high school physics students
- physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- physics learning with immediate feedback and personalized learning experiences
