# Score self-diagnoses against both expert ideal knowledge and novice-perceived knowledge using RDS, SDS, and RSD columns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An implementable grading recipe: for each student, first diagnose the quiz solution yourself (RDS), record how the student perceives their own correctness (SDS), and then judge the quality of the student's diagnosis (RSD). Comparing these columns reveals whether the student identified and correctly explained their own mistakes. The article used this structure to score introductory physics students' work on a rollercoaster normal-force problem in its self-diagnosis studies.
