# Explicit or Direct Instruction

## Description
Explicit (direct) instruction is a teacher-led, highly structured approach in which goals, expectations, and content are stated clearly, new material is presented in small steps with [Practice](../elements/practice.md) after each step, and skills are modeled before learners attempt them. It follows a predictable sequence — activate prior knowledge, model, guided practice with feedback, independent practice — and deliberately reduces ambiguity about what is to be learned and how success is judged.

## Design Implications

Explicit instruction works because it manages working memory load during initial acquisition: novices lack the schemas to search unstructured problem spaces productively, so unguided or minimally guided approaches tend to underperform for them [Kirschner et al.'s argument that minimal guidance overloads novice working memory.](../claims/example-problem-sequences-reduce-cognitive-load.md) [+S]. Its effectiveness depends on the full sequence being present — modeling alone, or explanation without guided practice and feedback, does not produce the gains seen in the complete cycle [Rosenshine's principles emphasize short segments with student practice after each.](../claims/part-task-practice-reduces-load-for-novices.md) [+M].

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear learning goals and success criteria stated to learners in advance
- [Demonstration](../elements/demonstration.md) or modeling of the target skill, ideally with narrated reasoning ([Think-Aloud](../elements/think-aloud.md))
- High-frequency questioning and checks for understanding ([Check-In](../elements/check-in.md)) during guided practice
- Immediate, task- and process-focused correction [Feedback is most effective when directed at the task and process rather than the person.](../claims/feedback-most-effective-at-task-and-process-levels.md) [+S]
- Deliberate sequencing from simple to complex, with [Chunking](../principles/chunking.md) of content into manageable steps [Chunking reduces working memory load.](../claims/chunking-reduces-working-memory-load.md) [+S]

#### Constraints
- For learners with substantial prior knowledge, high guidance becomes redundant and can depress performance — the expertise-reversal pattern [Guidance that helps novices can hurt more knowledgeable learners.](../claims/expertise-reversal-effect.md) [~S]
- If modeling is skipped and instruction is explanation-only, learners may follow stated rules without forming usable procedures
- Overly scripted delivery can reduce learner engagement and autonomy; pairing with brief [Activation](../elements/activation.md) of prior knowledge and opportunities for [Self-Explanation](../elements/self-explanation.md) mitigates passivity [Self-explanation improves conceptual understanding.](../claims/self-explanation-improves-conceptual-understanding.md) [+M]
- Less suited to ill-structured, open-ended goals (e.g., creative design, discussion-based inquiry) where no single expert procedure exists to model

#### Implementation Variability
- **Scripted programs** (e.g., Direct Instruction / DI curricula) specify teacher wording and pacing precisely
- **Explicit teaching frameworks** (e.g., Archer & Hughes) specify the structural sequence but leave wording to the teacher
- **Gradual release ("I do, we do, you do")** embeds [Fading](../elements/fading.md) of support as competence grows [Fading support promotes transfer of responsibility.](../claims/fading-support-promotes-transfer-of-responsibility.md) [+M]
- **Hybrid designs** open with explicit instruction, then hand off to [Case-Based Learning](../elements/case-based-learning.md) or inquiry once core procedures are secure

### Target Learners
- Novices and low-prior-knowledge learners, who benefit most from reduced search and clear procedure [Unguided approaches disadvantage novices specifically.](../claims/example-problem-sequences-reduce-cognitive-load.md) [+S]
- Younger learners and learners with learning difficulties; meta-analytic evidence in special education favors explicit formats [Direct instruction is effective for students with learning disabilities.](../claims/part-task-practice-reduces-load-for-novices.md) [+S]
- Advanced learners, for whom the same guidance should be reduced or removed [Guidance that helps novices can hurt more knowledgeable learners.](../claims/expertise-reversal-effect.md) [~S]

### Target Learning Goals
- Procedural and skill acquisition: decoding, computation, grammar, lab technique
- Well-structured conceptual content with a canonical expert strategy
- Foundational knowledge intended to later support inquiry, [Case Studies](../elements/case-studies.md), or transfer tasks

### Instructions
1. State the goal and success criteria; connect to prior knowledge ([Activation](../elements/activation.md), [Advance Organizers](../elements/advance-organizers.md)).
2. Model the skill in small steps with narrated reasoning ([Demonstration](../elements/demonstration.md), [Think-Aloud](../elements/think-aloud.md)).
3. Guide practice with frequent prompts, checks, and immediate corrective [Feedback](../elements/feedback.md) ([Coaching](../elements/coaching.md)).
4. Fade support through [Fading](../elements/fading.md) into independent [Practice](../elements/practice.md), ideally spaced over time [Spaced repetition improves retention.](../claims/spaced-repetition-improves-retention.md) [+S].

## Examples
- **National Institute for Direct Instruction (NIFDI)** — implements the Engelmann-style DI programs (e.g., *Reading Mastery*) with scripted lessons, choral responding, and continuous assessment ([https://www.nifdi.org](https://www.nifdi.org))
- **Rosenshine's Principles of Instruction** — widely adopted school frameworks (e.g., UK Education Endowment Foundation guidance) built on modeling, questioning, and guided practice
- **Explicit phonics programs** such as *Jolly Phonics* and *Sounds-Write* — systematic, teacher-modeled letter–sound instruction [Phonics instruction outperforms whole-word approaches for generalization.](../claims/phonics-instruction-outperforms-whole-word-for-generalization.md) [+S]
