# Spread OPS-English teaching throughout the school year rather than compressing it into six months

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The researcher recommends implementing OPS-English activities across the whole schooling year instead of the compressed six-month schedule, because "the duration of six months given is too short to finish all the 10 units in the text book". Spreading the units out gives teachers and students more flexible time, prevents rushing through the syllabus, and reduces pressure on teachers, particularly in lower-band secondary schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need longer, more flexible time allocated to teach the ten units
#### Constraints
- The researcher frames this for secondary schools which are in the lower band

### Target Learners
- Form One secondary school students in lower-band Malaysian schools

### Target Learning Goals
- English speaking ability and vocabulary development
