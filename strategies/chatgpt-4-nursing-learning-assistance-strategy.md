# Use ChatGPT 4.0 as a multilingual auxiliary learning-assistance tool for nursing education, with user quality review and institutional regulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying ChatGPT 4.0 as learning assistance for nursing students, since its "big data analysis and rapid text feedback can help students consolidate and expand knowledge points while completing MCQ exercises." The strategy pairs use with safeguards: users must "take part in the quality review process of the content that ChatGPT generates," institutions must create policies such as forbidding ChatGPT during exams, and users should avoid inputting private information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- User participation in quality review and error correction of generated content
- Institutional policies from schools, hospitals, and publishers governing ChatGPT use
#### Constraints
- The article states regulatory mechanisms and supervisory bodies are still needed and high-quality prospective randomized controlled trials are required to validate actual effectiveness

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- nurses preparing for licensure examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- consolidating and expanding professional medical and nursing knowledge
- preparing for nursing licensure examinations
