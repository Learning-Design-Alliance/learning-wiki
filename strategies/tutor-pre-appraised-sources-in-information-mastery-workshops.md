# Add tutoring on pre-appraised sources such as UpToDate to information mastery workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial findings, the authors recommend that information mastery curricula include tutoring on pre-appraised resources such as UpToDate, because such sources "seem to be more rewarding and faster, so more applicable in the daily practice" and "can enhance lifetime learning competencies among physicians". The workshop format used a one-hour lecture on information mastery principles, self-learning PowerPoint slide-shows on PICO question formulation and keyword choice, ten minutes of familiarization with each resource, and answering PICO-formatted clinical scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training materials such as self-learning slide-shows demonstrating how to use each resource
- Formulated PICO-format clinical scenarios with definite answers for practice
#### Constraints
- The authors note limited time for learning, practicing, and searching, and that unfamiliarity with searching PubMed Clinical Queries despite equal prior training might explain its low retrieval rate

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents
- first-time users of clinical information resources

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient retrieval of relevant evidence to answer clinical questions
