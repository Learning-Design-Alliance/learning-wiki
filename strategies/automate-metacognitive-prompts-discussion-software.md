# Automate metacognitive prompts in discussion board software based on critical thinking keywords

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a future research and design recommendation, the article proposes building a more intelligent discussion system that automatically delivers prompts to students while they write their posts. The system would be "based on key critical thinking words" and would give students prompts such as metacognitive questions during composing. This translates the study's finding that instructor probing improved discourse into a software-embedded design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programming prompts into the discussion board software triggered by key critical thinking words in student writing
#### Constraints
- The article presents this only as a future recommendation for research, not as an implemented or tested system

### Target Learners
- students participating in asynchronous online discussions

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognitive reflection during online discussion writing
- critical thinking and argumentation
