# Provide an always-available automated assistance module for immediate answers to routine student queries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper proposes an automated assistance module available at all times to answer questions about studying or living in Australia. Students are often hesitant to ask questions in live sessions or public forums due to communication or language issues, and emailed queries arriving in bursts can take "up to one or two days for the student to receive a reply". The automated system provides immediate answers instead, freeing human advisors for issues requiring intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sustainable solution for information storage, to avoid duplicating storage of similar questions submitted in different languages
#### Constraints
- Some issues or problems will always require human intervention

### Target Learners
- international students hesitant to ask questions in live sessions or public forums

### Target Learning Goals
- resolving questions about studying and living in the host country
- reducing feelings of isolation
