Repeat flipped-learning studies across diverse subject areas and contexts before generalising effects¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's own future-research recommendation is that generalisation requires replication beyond the single course and institution studied. It states that "in order to generalise the effects of FL, this study needs to be repeated and conducted in diverse academic subject areas", and suggests future work focus on whether FL improves student-teachers' practical skills such as creating and editing digital videos, given the role of video technology in flipped classrooms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Validation of the conceptual framework across different FL contexts

Constraints¶

  • The present study took place at one educational institution, in one specific course, with a purposive (biased) sample

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service teachers in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • educational technology skills such as creating and editing digital videos

Related Strategies¶

  • Instructors redesigning higher education courses should implement flipped classrooms as an effective pedagogical practice while attending to subject-specific and pre-class-engagement conditions
  • Future research should replicate the model in diverse educational domains and use experimental and intervention methods
  • Extend meta-analytic synthesis of flipped learning to additional outcomes, samples, and regions
