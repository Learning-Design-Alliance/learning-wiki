# Closed-trajectory exercises with non-geometric objects to approach centripetal acceleration and force

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes an exercise in which learners try to produce closed trajectories with VELOBJECT, ACCOBJECT and NEWOBJECT. Geometric commands are ineffective, so learners must combine velocity commands with SLOWDOWN and SPINUP, and with acceleration objects they discover that "an equal and opposite acceleration will not stop the object but leave it in a state of constant linear or angular speed, according to Newton's First Law". This progressively approaches centripetal acceleration and, for NEWOBJECTs, centripetal force.
