# Deliver targeted, periodic DDH education programs for family physicians emphasizing lesser-known risk factors and the ineffectiveness of double diapering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend recurring education for family medicine physicians that targets identified knowledge gaps. They state: "We suggest more targeted and periodic education programs for family medicine physicians, including in-service training, congresses, symposiums, and guidelines to address knowledge gaps regarding lesser-known risk factors, diagnostic processes, and treatment methods, with a particular emphasis on the ineffectiveness of double diapering." Their survey showed congress attendance, in-service training, and specialty training were associated with higher knowledge scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered periodically and repeatedly, since knowledge gaps persist among practicing physicians
- Should target the specific gaps the survey identified: lesser-known risk factors, diagnostic processes, treatment methods, and the double-diapering misbelief
#### Constraints
- The article's supporting evidence is correlational survey data from Istanbul family physicians and may not generalize to all of Türkiye, as the authors state in their limitations

### Target Learners
- Family physicians working at family health centers

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of DDH risk factors, diagnostic processes, and treatment methods
- Correct screening-program practices and abandonment of the double-diapering misbelief
