# Host a researcher-industry networking event at a shared venue with pre-distributed project catalogues to create a forum for direct technology-transfer dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SMARTHEP meets Industry @ CERN event brought twenty-five companies from Italy and France into direct contact with ESRs, after distributing detailed catalogues of ESR activities and projects that enabled targeted one-on-one networking sessions. The article states this event format provided "a practical forum for direct dialogue between researchers and industry, which can be adopted for future industrial/academic network collaborations".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed catalogues of ESR activities and projects distributed to participating companies before the event
- A dedicated budget line item for co-organising events, conferences and schools
