Emphasize the inductive way of teaching grammar while varying techniques to students' interests and achievement levels¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (3 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that EFL teachers vary their teaching techniques according to students' interests and achievement levels, with more emphasis on the inductive way of teaching grammar. It also encourages teachers and instructors to attend intensive training courses held by the Ministry of Education to become aware of modern ways of teaching English and to apply them in class.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers attend intensive training courses held by the Ministry of Education to become familiar with modern teaching methods

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is drawn from a study conducted in Jordan; the author calls for further studies in other regions and on other syntax areas

Target Learners¶

  • EFL teachers and instructors at school and university levels in Jordan

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English grammar achievement

Claims¶

  • Inductive grammar teaching produced significantly higher post-test grammar achievement than deductive teaching among Jordanian elementary-stage EFL students [+W]
  • Inductive grammar teaching produced significantly higher post-test grammar achievement than deductive teaching among Jordanian university EFL students [+W]
  • The review concludes the inductive method has proved effective for grammar teaching in most reviewed university- and school-level cases, whereas the deductive method has not [+W]
  • Majorities of students believed inductive instruction more effective for simple structures and, conversely, for difficult structures [~W]
  • Review synthesis: explicit teaching matters, but its advantage over other approaches varies by context and structure [~W]
  • All student groups preferred explicit over implicit grammar instruction [-W]
