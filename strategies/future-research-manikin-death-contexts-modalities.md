# Future research should evaluate paediatric manikin death across contexts, professions and modalities, including simulated parents and virtual reality

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendations: future studies should evaluate manikin death in multiple contexts, professional groups and simulation modalities; evaluate actors or simulated patients depicting parents to increase emotional depth and provide communication skills education; and evaluate augmented or virtual reality simulation, which the authors suggest may offer a psychologically safer space for less experienced staff for whom live simulation might be overwhelming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research designs evaluating participant experience and performance across varied professional groups and modalities
#### Constraints
- The review found only three studies, all in acute emergency settings with qualified doctors and/or nurses, none in palliative or end-of-life scenarios

### Target Learners
- paediatric and neonatal healthcare professionals
- less experienced staff via virtual modalities

### Target Learning Goals
- developing experience with paediatric death
- communication skills with families
