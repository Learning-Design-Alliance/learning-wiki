# Develop SJT test content in the culture and language where the test is administered and confirm psychometric properties within an actual selection procedure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the pilot's limitations, the authors advise that "For future assessments, it is advised to develop the test content in the culture and language where the test is administered and to confirm the psychometric properties within an actual selection procedure." This strategy addresses the uncertainty about cultural differences in scenario content and the volunteer-sample motivation gap observed when a North-American test was transplanted into the German context. It applies to rater-based selection tools being adapted across countries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Test scenarios relatable to local test takers and aligned with the goals of the local education system
- Psychometric evaluation embedded in a real high-stakes selection context rather than a volunteer pilot
#### Constraints
- The pilot's moderate interrater agreement and lack of OSCE correlation may partly reflect the imported scenario content and non-high-stakes volunteer setting

### Target Learners
- medical school applicants

### Target Learning Goals
- assessment of personal characteristics in admission selection
