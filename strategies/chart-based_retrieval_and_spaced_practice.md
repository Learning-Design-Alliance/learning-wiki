# Chart-Based Retrieval and Spaced Practice

## Description
A simple classroom exercise to demonstrate the effectiveness of spaced practice using a chart. Learners copy a chart from memory, highlight what they recall, and then complete the chart using notes or textbooks, highlighting the new additions. This process is repeated over several days, demonstrating increased recall and retention. The key is to have a discussion with students about why they should use this sort of learning strategy over cramming the night before the test. Also, like most of the learning strategies, pointing out to students that these steps can easily be applied to a wide range of topics across most classes. This isn’t a strategy that is specific to psychology class.  It works with math, English, history, science, etc.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creating a chart with relevant information. Minimal class time spread over several days. Student commitment to repeating the exercise.
#### Constraints
- May require initial preparation of the chart. Effectiveness relies on student participation and reflection. May not be suitable for all learners or subjects.
