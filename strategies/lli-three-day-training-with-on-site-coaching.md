# Provide LLI interventionists with 3-day formal training including follow-up on-site coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The summary recommends that LLI Interventionists receive formal 3-day LLI training from Heinemann Publishing. This consists of "2 days of in-person training, followed by one day of on-site coaching throughout the year", pairing initial training with distributed coaching support during implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Heinemann-provided training and an on-site coach during the implementation year
#### Constraints
- Training is specific to the LLI program and its publisher

### Target Learners
- struggling readers in grades K-2 taught by trained interventionists

### Target Learning Goals
- faithful implementation of the LLI tutoring model
