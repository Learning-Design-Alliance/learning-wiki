# Employ CAI as an alternative or supportive strategy for teaching English grammar, with teacher training in developing textbook-based CAI packages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the CAI group's gain exceeded the CIM group's, the authors recommend that the investigated instructional strategy may be employed as an alternative or supportive strategy in teaching and learning English grammar in schools and open schools. They further recommend that school teachers should be given training on development of text book based CAI packages, so individualized self-instructional CAI can supplement or replace conventional instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in developing textbook-based CAI packages
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one quasi-experimental study of a single grammar topic with 89 eighth-standard students in Matriculation schools

### Target Learners
- Eighth standard students in schools and open schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning English grammar (Voice)
