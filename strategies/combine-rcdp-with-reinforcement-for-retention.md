# Combine RCDP with additional reinforcement methods to sustain long-term skill retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because RCDP improved immediate but not one-year forceps skill performance, the authors recommend pairing it with ongoing reinforcement to prevent skill decay. They note that "intermittent learning has been shown to aid in the consolidation of long-term memory" and that repeated CPR training every three months using RCDP improved long-term memory, and conclude that additional methods may be needed for long-term retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to periodic booster or refresher simulation sessions after initial RCDP training
#### Constraints
- The study tested only a single one-year retest interval, chosen for cost and cohort-scheduling reasons, and did not assess clinical patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- Obstetrics and gynecology residents learning forceps delivery skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term retention of forceps-assisted vaginal delivery skills
