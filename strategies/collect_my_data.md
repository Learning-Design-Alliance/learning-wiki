# Collect My Data

## Description
Learners choose a category of items to collect data on, creating tally charts to record observations, and then graph the data to visualize findings. This reinforces data collection, organization, and representation skills, while also creating a tangible keepsake of the trip.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paper, pencil, and materials for creating tally charts and graphs.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the availability of items in the chosen category. May require assistance with graphing for younger learners.

### Personalization
