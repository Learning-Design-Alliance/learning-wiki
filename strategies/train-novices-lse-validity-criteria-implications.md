# Train Fibroscan novices to understand the clinical implications of the LSE validity criteria before independent clinical use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's summary section gives an implementable recommendation for centres adopting Fibroscan: novices should both understand why the validity criteria matter diagnostically and complete a minimum of observed scans before working independently. It states the study "should inform other UK National Health Service centres that prior to using Fibroscan in clinical practice, novices should be trained to understand the clinical implications of the LSE validity criteria, and should undertake a minimum of 10 observed scans on patients prior to using the Fibroscan independently."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must cover the three manufacturer validity criteria and their diagnostic implications, plus a minimum of 10 observed scans on patients
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is derived from a single-centre retrospective study; disease-specific and probe-specific LSE cut-offs still require prospective validation in UK clinical practice

### Target Learners
- novice Fibroscan operators in NHS liver services

### Target Learning Goals
- adhering to LSE validity criteria to ensure diagnostic accuracy of Fibroscan
