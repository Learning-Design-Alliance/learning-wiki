# Individualized concentration training using q-bank questions to extend concentration time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend one-on-one training in which, when a student's attention is about to wane during question practice, the instructor encourages the student to do five more questions or spend five more minutes on the task. Repeated sessions are expected to extend the student's concentration time and build a self-applied strategy. This follows from the finding that concentration was the only study strategy correlated with Step 1 performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor available for one-on-one sessions with the student during q-bank question practice
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a single-cohort correlational study; the authors state the study should be replicated in other cohorts

### Target Learners
- medical students preparing for licensing exams

### Target Learning Goals
- improving concentration skills for exam preparation and test taking
