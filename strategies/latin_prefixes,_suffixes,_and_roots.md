# Latin Prefixes, Suffixes, and Roots

## Description
Direct teaching about the meaningful parts of words begins with the most common inflections, but then extends to prefixes, suffixes, and roots of Latin origin. This is especially helpful in grades four through eight to help students develop a larger vocabulary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Word lists, morphological charts, direct instruction
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit and systematic instruction
