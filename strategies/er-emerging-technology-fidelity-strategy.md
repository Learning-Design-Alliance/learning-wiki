# Enhance escape room simulation fidelity with AR, VR, AI, and real-time adaptive data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (3 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that medical and nursing faculties teach with "augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and artificial intelligence (AI) to make real feel with advanced simulators in ERs", and leverage sensor-based and wearable devices tracking responses such as heart rate so facilitators can adjust difficulty in real time. It reports that smart algorithms twisting puzzles based on real time have been shown to improve engagement, citing the technology-enhanced simulation literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- collaboration with technology, cognitive, psychology, and health professionals for continued design development
- testing the technology in training sessions to see how well it works
#### Constraints
- Research on long-term outcomes of ER-based interventions, especially for quality care and clinical performance, still needs exploration per the review

### Target Learners
- nursing and medical students
- health professionals in immersive training environments

### Target Learning Goals
- decision-making skills
- skill and knowledge retention in simulation settings
- engagement
