# Use situational communication exercises, normal speech, ad hoc grammar explanations, and echo-expansion correction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article lists classroom techniques that its experiment found sufficient for statistically significant skill improvement: situational communication exercises such as language games, interviews, dialogues, and skits; speaking to students normally, slowing down and simplifying speech when the instructor felt necessary; giving short ad hoc grammatical explanations when students asked or appeared to need them rather than by course design; and correcting students by a personalized echo-expansion technique. Each was reported "sufficient for significant skill improvement" in the experimental program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An environment which maximized students' participation in the English language, with attempts to take the learner's mind off the task of learning the language.
#### Constraints
- Findings come from one limited experimental research project; questions about assisting untrained teachers, introducing reading and writing, oral-skill evaluation, and accent reduction were not dealt with.

### Target Learners
- adult second language learners in immersion classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- structural and communication skill improvement in English
