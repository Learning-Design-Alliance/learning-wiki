# Design empathy-reliant inclusion programs around communication and contextual information as key mechanisms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's analysis of communication and contextual information carries design implications for STEM inclusion efforts: "with implications for programs that rely on empathy to develop more inclusive STEM academic spaces." Because faculty empathy emerges through dialogue and can be blocked by dis-communication, programs should attend to how faculty gather contextual information about students and how messages are received, not only to faculty intentions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must support meaningful two-way communication, since empathy development requires communication between participants
#### Constraints
- Findings come from two participants at one private research university and are not meant to be generalizable
