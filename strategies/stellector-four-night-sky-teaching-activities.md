# Four laser-guided night sky activities for teaching observational astronomy concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose four activities using the Stellector: (1) the celestial sphere and geolocation, showing the apparent movement of stars due to Earth's rotation and tracing celestial circles and cardinal points, including simulation of other geographic latitudes; (2) the ecliptic and the seasons, showing the Sun's yearly path relative to the stars; (3) constellations and asterisms, tracing constellation lines, borders and asterisms; and (4) the planets, showing their differential movement relative to the fixed stars, including retrograde motion. As the authors state, "we propose four activities that explore basic concepts of observational astronomy."
