# Provide career development support for diverse pathways, especially non-academic careers, through skills workshops, alumni networks, and funded internships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Survey respondents cited a lack of formal instruction to prepare for career paths other than professors or research scientists, including a lack of experience in real-world skills like coding, scientific writing, machine learning, and project management. The article recommends skills-based workshops developed with input from working professionals, formalized alumni networking events each semester or year, and a widely publicized, financially-supported internship or research experience geared to student interests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops developed with input of professionals actively working in those fields
- Networking events widely advertised and open to all students and alumni
- Financial support for internships that would otherwise be unpaid or low-pay
#### Constraints
- Development of robust skill-building workshops can require significant effort and time commitment

### Target Learners
- STEM students and early career scientists from under-represented groups

### Target Learning Goals
- career preparedness for academic and non-academic careers
- job-search skills
