# Teacher training should prioritize logic-based instructional scaffolding and creative thinking stimulation to raise EFL learners’ self-efficacy and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that only the cognitive-oriented styles predicted engagement and self-efficacy, the authors recommend that teacher training programs "prioritize logic-based instructional scaffolding and creative thinking stimulation techniques when designing pedagogical interventions aimed at enhancing learners’ self-efficacy beliefs and engagement outcomes." This translates the SEM findings into a concrete design directive for pedagogical interventions in EFL higher education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers able to present information in a logical, organized, clear manner and to employ innovative, imaginative instructional approaches
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from undergraduate institutions; the authors caution that generalization to vocational colleges requires caution

### Target Learners
- Chinese university EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Enhancing self-efficacy beliefs and classroom engagement
