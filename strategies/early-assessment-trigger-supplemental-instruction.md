# Use early content-area assessments as triggers for supplemental instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose using performance on early, well-discriminating content-area assessments to trigger additional support. Because the first assessments of energy (Content Area #2) and oscillatory motion (Content Area #3) occur early in the course and provide good discrimination between final course grades, they write that "poor performance on these initial evaluations of these content areas might serve as a trigger for the implementation of additional tutorials or other supplemental materials." This makes ALPaCA's per-content-area scoring usable as an early-warning mechanism within the course.
