# Use an authentic-context interprofessional activity early in training to promote acceptance of interprofessional learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that the prescribing skills workshop, "where medical and pharmacy students were placed in an authentic context, was a promising activity to promote interprofessional learning". IPL is recommended to be initiated early in the training of healthcare professionals, and facilitators were directed to encourage interaction and communication between the medical and pharmacy students throughout the Expert and Jigsaw sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- the introduction of the concept and the programme to students is the most crucial step so that students from different backgrounds are willing to embrace learning together
#### Constraints
- implementation challenges include timetable difficulties, different programme lengths, distinct assessment methods, lack of administrator support, limited resources and student attitudinal factors
- the workshop could be modified slightly to engage the pharmacy students more

### Target Learners
- final-year medical and pharmacy students

### Target Learning Goals
- acceptance of interprofessional learning and collaborative prescribing skills
