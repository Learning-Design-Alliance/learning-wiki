# Give more instructional weight to reversible cycles other than the Carnot cycle to improve understanding of Carnot's theorem

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that Thermodynamics textbooks and instruction attach more importance to cycles besides the Carnot cycle, such as the Stirling cycle, because students fail to generalize Carnot's theorem beyond the Carnot cycle. It states it is "necessary to attach more importance to other cycles besides Carnot cycle in the Thermodynamics textbooks" and suggests "Stirling cycle could be an important supplement to Carnot cycle".

## Design Implications
