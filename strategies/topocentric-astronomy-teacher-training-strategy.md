# Use the topocentric didactic model of astronomy as the starting point for designing intercultural teacher-training devices for physics teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends beginning the design of intercultural training devices for physics teachers from the interrelation of astronomy-to-be-taught with indigenous, essentially topocentric astronomy with a local vision of celestial phenomena. It argues "el modelo didáctico topocéntrico resultará útil" because it describes most movements of bodies seen from the Earth's surface, reduces abstraction complexity, and is closer to students' prior ideas. Cultural astronomy both opens reflection on how knowledge is built socioculturally and validates other knowledge systems not customary in classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-training devices built on astronomy contents present in curricular designs or associated with other physical phenomena
#### Constraints
- Astronomy contents are often absent from science classes despite their curricular presence, per Gangui e Iglesias (2015)

### Target Learners
- in-service and pre-service physics teachers (docentes de física tanto en ejercicio como en formación)

### Target Learning Goals
- intercultural perspective in physics teacher training
- didactic design for cultural heterogeneity
