# University-nonprofit partnership model (NEON) delivering AI instruction while preserving school relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy pairs university faculty-delivered AI instruction with a national nonprofit's institutional infrastructure. The article states "This partnership was critical to enabling equitable and scalable AI education": because many high school educators have received little formal training in AI, the NEON model lets the university deliver instruction while preserving students' existing classroom relationships. NEON handles recruitment, coordination with school-based educators, and college-access alignment, and the bichronous structure supports consistent delivery across schools at scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A nonprofit partner providing recruitment, coordination, and college-access alignment infrastructure
- School-based co-teachers overseeing classroom implementation
- Supplemental infrastructure such as headphones and a secure AI sandbox where needed
#### Constraints
- Courses offered at no cost to students, dependent on donations or school- and district-level subsidies

### Target Learners
- Students from underrepresented and low-income backgrounds in Title I and Title I-eligible schools

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy with ethical reasoning
- Postsecondary access and preparation
