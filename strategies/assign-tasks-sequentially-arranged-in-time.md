# Influence student study habits by assigning tasks sequentially arranged in time before exams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that teachers can shape students' preparation habits by scheduling practice tasks in a deliberate time sequence ahead of exams, using the platform's logged activity data to diagnose weaknesses prior to the exam. As the authors state, "small changes in the habits of the students could be influenced, by assignation of tasks properly arranged in time." This turns the platform's records into a feedback mechanism for teachers, not only for students.
