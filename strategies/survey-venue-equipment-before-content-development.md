# Survey the venue's equipment capabilities before developing content past the research phase

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors advise learning the presentation technology's capabilities and limitations early: "an appreciation for the capabilities of ETSU's planetarium equipment was crucial before we could develop the project past its initial research phase." Discovering that their Nightshade version was depreciated and that shorter coded scripts ran more reliably led them to split the show into four acts, which made visuals easier to edit and pair with narration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Hands-on access to the projection system, its control software, and knowledgeable staff such as a facility director
#### Constraints
- Technological hurdles such as depreciated software and unreliable long scripts constrained the show's audio and visual design

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate researchers
- Informal science education designers

### Target Learning Goals
- Technical production skills
- Adaptive project planning
