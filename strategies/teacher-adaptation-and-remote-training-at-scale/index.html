Strategies

Permit teachers to adapt and integrate programme materials in their own way in future trials, and consider remote delivery of training and support when scaling¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on the process evaluation finding that all teachers could implement the programmes but found the weekly content overloaded, the report recommends flexibility in future delivery. It states: "In future trials of the programmes, teachers should be permitted to use and integrate the materials in their own way, as they would in a normal teaching situation." For scale-up, it also recommends the Oxford team consider whether training and ongoing technical support could be delivered remotely rather than in person.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programme materials flexible enough for teachers to integrate into normal teaching; access to computers and/or tablets for the online games

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation arises from an efficacy trial with intensive developer support; a future trial at scale in more than one location is suggested to evaluate the programmes at scale

Target Learners¶

  • Year 2 pupils in primary and infant schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • numeracy
  • literacy

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide a programme manual plus mentoring and support before scaling up a teacher-developed physical activity intervention
  • Use virtual engagement and online training delivery to sustain and extend apprenticeship system operations during disruptions
  • When planning mentoring to support evidence uptake, plan regular meetings over time, choose an appropriate delivery approach, establish a mentor-mentee matching process, and consider combining mentoring with other KT interventions
