# Organized Online Resources

## Description
Storing lesson plan ideas, instructional websites, and electronic copies of worksheets using organizational sites such as Livebinders.com, or desktop applications such as Evernote can streamline the organizational process and increase efficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to organizational websites or desktop applications, digital resources, and a structured system for categorization.
#### Constraints
- Requires time to set up and maintain the system. Reliance on technology can be a challenge if there are accessibility issues.

### Affordances
- Centralizes digital resources, improves accessibility and organization, facilitates sharing and collaboration among educators, enhances efficiency in lesson planning.

### Personalization
- Allow students to create their own digital portfolios to organize their work. Offer digital organizational tools that cater to different learning styles (e.g., visual, linear).

- Enhances organization and accessibility, saving time and improving the overall efficiency of digital content use in education.
