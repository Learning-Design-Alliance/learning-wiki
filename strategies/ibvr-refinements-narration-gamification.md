# Refine IBVR modules with clearer narration, shorter sessions, and gamification to raise hedonic appeal

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose iterative improvements to IBVR to address its lower satisfaction and hedonic scores: "incorporating clearer, more vivid voice narrations, minimizing background noise, and offering shorter, focused training sessions", plus "integrating gamification elements, dynamic feedback, and personalized content". They also recommend eliminating repetitive demonstrations and ensuring instructional content is concise and targeted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to IBVR module development tools and the ability to revise narration, pacing, and content based on learner feedback.
#### Constraints
- These refinements are recommendations aimed at improving hedonic stimulation and identification; their effectiveness was not tested in this study.

### Target Learners
- Junior residents using immersive VR surgical training modules

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining engagement and satisfaction during immersive VR procedural skills training
