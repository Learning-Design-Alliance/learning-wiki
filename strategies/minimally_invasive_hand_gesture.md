# Minimally Invasive Hand Gesture

## Description
Using a minimally invasive hand gesture to secure student attention. This technique involves a simple, non-disruptive hand signal that prompts students to quiet down and focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires clear communication and practice to ensure students understand the meaning of the hand gesture. Consistency in using the gesture is essential.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if students are not properly trained on the meaning of the gesture. Some students may not respond to subtle cues.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Quickly and efficiently gain the attention of students with minimal disruption.

### Affordances
- Provides a silent and subtle method for gaining attention, reducing classroom disruptions. Can be used discreetly without interrupting the flow of instruction.

### Personalization
- The specific hand gesture can be chosen by the teacher and students together to create a shared understanding and sense of ownership.
