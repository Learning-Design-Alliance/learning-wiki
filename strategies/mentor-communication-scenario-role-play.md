# Use scenario-based role-play to prepare interns for communicating with mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Communicating with Mentors session prepares students for workplace communication by having small groups act out scenarios drawn from years of internship staffing experience, such as "you arrive for what you believed was a one-hour meeting with your mentor, but she only has 5 minutes now." Each team decides how to act out a productive or unproductive response, followed by instructor-led discussion of productive strategies. The session's stated emphasis is that students must be proactive and self-directed in getting the most out of their internship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of realistic mentor-interaction scenarios drawn from program experience
- Small groups with time to plan and act out responses (about 5-10 minutes per scenario)
