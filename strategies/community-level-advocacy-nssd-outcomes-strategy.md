## Description
The authors recommend intervention at the exosystem level, arguing their findings "point to the importance of advocacy, systemic-level change, and community-level interventions to ameliorate outcomes in youth." They list four concrete avenues: local policy changes for adequate housing access, expanded health insurance coverage, local reform to improve child safety, and development of free/low-cost health-related services, citing prior evidence that community-level strategies affect child development.
