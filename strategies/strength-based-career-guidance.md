# Strength-based guidance: recognizing and nurturing learners' inherent strengths rather than emphasizing deficits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that schools taking a personalized approach to career guidance use strength-based guidance, which 'focuses on recognizing and nurturing the inherent strengths and skills that each learner brings, rather than solely emphasizing areas for improvement'. This supports learners' self-esteem and encourages pursuit of areas of existing competence and passion. Learner wallets are positioned as technologies that can support strength-based guidance by surfacing trends and recommendations from individual learner data.
