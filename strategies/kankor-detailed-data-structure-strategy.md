# Record detailed, properly tagged Kankor data with standardized attributes and main-stream categories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author proposes a concrete data-recording structure so policymakers can generate accurate, drillable insights. Core recommendation: "Attributes with predefined domain values should have proper tags and should be divided into separate attributes", for example splitting Candidate's Result into field of study, institution, institution type, and location. He further recommends categorizing roughly 150 fields of study into main streams (Natural Science, Social Science, Health Science, Literature and Humanities, Islamic Education, Technical and Vocational Education), unifying terminology across provinces, and storing candidates' ranked field choices, score details, and available seats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The article assumes the Kankor Committee of MoHE, through its Kankor Management System, is capable of storing and producing the data; if detailed data generation costs extra, results should also be produced in spreadsheets with confidential data excluded.
#### Constraints
- The article states that in some provinces fields such as Languages, Mathematics, Physics, and History fall under Education while in others they fall under Sciences or Humanities, so unified transparent reports currently cannot be generated easily.

### Target Learners
- Kankor candidates (Afghan high school graduates seeking higher education)

### Target Learning Goals
- Enabling accurate descriptive and predictive analytics to improve admission settings and policy decisions
