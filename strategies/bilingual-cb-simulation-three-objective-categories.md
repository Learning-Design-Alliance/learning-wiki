# Structure bilingual cross-border simulation training around three learning-objective categories and reduce complexity to focus on cross-border communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training was conceived around three learning objective categories: "(a) proper and barrier-free communication during CB-EMS rescue operations, (b) emergency medical knowledge and (c) specific medico-legal aspects of CB-EMS between Germany and Poland." Thirteen bilingual scenarios with bilingual debriefing were led by an interprofessional instructor team with a bilingual course coordinator, in mixed groups with a 1:1 German-Polish ratio. Based on their evaluation the authors recommend reducing complexity of emergency medical objectives to focus on CB-communication in future trainings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bilingual scenarios and debriefing led by an interprofessional team including a bilingual course coordinator, with questionnaires translated through a multi-stage forward and backward translation process
#### Constraints
- The article states further research is highly needed to evaluate communication processes and intra-team interaction during bilingual simulation training and in cross-border rescue operations

### Target Learners
- German and Polish paramedics of ground and air ambulance services in border regions

### Target Learning Goals
- Barrier-free communication during cross-border EMS rescue operations
- Emergency medical knowledge
- Medico-legal aspects of cross-border EMS
