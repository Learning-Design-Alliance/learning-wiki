# Use active, collaborative problem-solving methods with concrete teaching aids rather than lecture-only instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that teachers replace teacher-centred presentation with active methods in which students face problems, think about solutions, and solve them with the teacher's help, using concrete aids. In the study's active condition, "teaching aids such as coloured balls and clothing were used", and students built bracelets from counters to grasp circular versus gyrate permutation before returning to the formula. The article states "it is suggested that teachers use active and collaborative methods, in order to solve maths problems."
