# Using real Hubble-constant data in guided case studies to build data-analysis skills in introductory astronomy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a strategy in which introductory students analyze real astronomical data to study Hubble's Law and cosmic acceleration. It pairs a hands-on model activity with "guided questions for three "case studies"" that show students "how to constructively deal with confusing points in the analysis of data." The explicit goal is skill in "analyzing data in terms of a model." Students prepare with a Lecture-Tutorial on the expansion of the universe before the activity.
