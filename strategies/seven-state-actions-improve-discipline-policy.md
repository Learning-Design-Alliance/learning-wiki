# Seven state-level policy actions to improve school discipline equity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief enumerates seven criteria state leaders should prioritize: set clear goals for decreasing exclusionary discipline and reducing disparities; provide state agency human resources for technical assistance; ban corporal punishment and limit restraints to trained staff and imminent-danger situations; ban zero-tolerance policies and exclusionary discipline for non-violent, minor, or young-learner offenses and discriminatory hair/grooming codes; fund positive discipline such as restorative justice; fund professional development in positive discipline; and publicly report disaggregated district-level discipline data.
