# Have users self-report competence-development activities instead of monitoring their devices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To obtain the activities of competence development, the article weighs two approaches: constant monitoring of all users' actions on their devices, versus users informing the activities themselves. Monitoring requires 'sophisticated techniques to detect the users' actions of competences development' and raises 'ethical issues involved in the worker's monitoring'. The authors therefore 'decided to use the approach in which the own users inform the activities that they performed to develop their competences', obtaining event participation via an Inform Participation option or the worker's Curriculum Lattes XML upload.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users must actively mark resources as used, inform event participation, and confirm interactions; counterpart people must confirm that interactions helped
#### Constraints
- Self-reporting depends on users remembering and bothering to enter activities; the article does not report data on the accuracy of self-reports

### Target Learners
- workers using mobile competence-management tools

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate recording of competence-development activities in trails
