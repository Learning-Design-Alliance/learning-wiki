# Pilot listening test recordings with equivalent audio content across modalities before main administration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researchers prepared "a set of audio and video recordings was prepared for each task by the researchers to ensure the best possible sound with a medium rate of speech delivery (130 wpm)" and pilot tested with students excluded from the main study. This strategy ensures recording quality and speech rate are appropriate before data collection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recordings should be pilot tested with students excluded from the main study
#### Constraints
- Speech rate was fixed at 130 wpm; other rates were not tested

### Target Learners
- ELT students taking listening comprehension tests

### Target Learning Goals
- Valid and reliable listening assessment
