# Faculty development workshops that contrast beliefs about race-conscious admissions with how holistic admissions actually functions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend educational initiatives that clarify how holistic admissions functions, moving beyond misconceptions about quotas or point systems. They suggest schools "create an interactive workshop that details what race‐conscious admissions is believed to be and what it actually is", sharing the history and definitions of affirmative action, holistic admissions and race-conscious admissions, and contrasting selection under each approach. Fact-checking and debunking techniques, reported as at least somewhat effective for changing vaccine beliefs, could be included.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires accurate information about how holistic admissions actually functions and the history and definitions of the three admissions approaches.
#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals

- understanding how race intersects with other aspects of applicant experience
