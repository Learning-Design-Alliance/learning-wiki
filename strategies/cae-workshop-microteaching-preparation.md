## Description
Both faculty instructors attended NASA Center for Astronomy Education two-day workshops before teaching, where they were introduced to Lecture-Tutorials, ranking tasks, think-pair-share questions, and simulations. The article notes "The instructors were introduced to Lecture-Tutorials, ranking tasks, think-pair-share questions, and astronomy simulations and practised their implementation in mock classroom situations." Microteaching events let participants role-play the parts of student and instructor, and developing PCK was explicitly introduced early on.
