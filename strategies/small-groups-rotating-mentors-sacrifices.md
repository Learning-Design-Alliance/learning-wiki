# Use small stationary groups with rotating mentors to discuss career and personal sacrifices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In this strategy, participants stay in small groups while mentors move through the groups, creating an intimate discussion environment in which mentors identify the sacrifices they made to reach their current positions. The article implemented it because past needs assessments showed underrepresented participants feel isolated when asked to make difficult familial and economic sacrifices for their STEM education. It addresses the finding that many mentors do not traditionally share personal sacrifices to maintain professional distance.
