Conduct large-scale, well-designed RCTs with multiple outcome indicators to evaluate new teaching methods¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that "more large-scale and well-designed RCT studies should be conducted to increase the frequency in the reporting of accurate results" and that learning outcomes "should not only include examination-based scores but also be evaluated from multiple perspectives". Future research should involve more indicators to comprehensively assess learning outcomes and psychological impact on students.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires well-designed RCTs reporting accurate results and multiple outcome indicators beyond examination scores

Constraints¶

  • The article notes current studies lack blinding of subjects and evaluators, and randomization methods are often unclear, reducing reliability
