# Iterate the 3D training tool with fixed viewpoints, step animations, instant feedback, and profession-tailored delivery formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on participant feedback, the article recommends improving navigation and learning support in the 3D tool: users "suggested additional fixed viewpoints", touchscreen or controller-based interaction, animations of specific surgical steps such as the femoral neck cut, and "instant feedback during tasks" to support learning and self-assessment. Delivery preferences differed by profession: medical students favored fully immersive apps while resident surgeons favored web-based formats.
