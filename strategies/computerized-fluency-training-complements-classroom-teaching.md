# Use computerized fact-fluency training to complement, not substitute, classroom teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends integrating educational technology into a well-rounded curriculum rather than relying on it alone: "Computerized trainings can complement but not substitute classroom teaching." It argues the strength of a computational fluency program such as XtraMath is that "it trains foundational skills while leaving strategy instruction to teachers." This positions home-based adaptive practice as one element alongside teacher-led instruction and teacher-facing supports such as e-learning resources about preterm birth.
