# Open an integrated speaking lesson with theme questions, hypothesis-building, key words, and lecturer–student discussion

## Description
The article describes a concrete implementation sequence for integrative teaching in a speaking lesson. First, "Lecturers provide the questions that had to do with the title or theme of the material to be discussed" as an introduction recalling concepts mastered through the four skills. Then "the students were asked to create hypotheses and determine the title of the theme in question and also determine the key words", followed by discussions between lecturers and students to transfer cognitive structure.
