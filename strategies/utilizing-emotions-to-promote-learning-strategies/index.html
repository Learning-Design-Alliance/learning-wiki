Strategies for utilizing emotions to promote learning: spark curiosity via knowledge gaps, foster mastery orientation, transmit enthusiasm, and time emotional events within lessons¶

Description¶

The review enumerates implementable strategies by which teachers can use emotions to facilitate learning and prevent emotions that compromise it. These include increasing the cognitive and motivational quality of instruction; as the article puts it, "to spark students' curiosity, teachers can create a knowledge gap and promote a sense of wonder through questioning and inquiry." Further strategies are fostering goal orientation towards learning and improvement (since performance orientation can lead to anxiety and anger), demonstrating enthusiasm to transmit enjoyment, being authentic in emotional expression, evoking positive emotional experiences at the start of class, and postponing unfavorable performance feedback until the end of class.
