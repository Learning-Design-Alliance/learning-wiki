# Schedule virtual simulation training before the flipped classroom course rather than after it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends deploying the OSCRS simulation and clinical observation module prior to the course rather than after completion. The authors state that "The OSCRS training and clinical observation module prior to the course should be a better model," because pre-course simulator use lets students encounter clinical scenarios before in-class case discussion, which the authors link to stimulating learning enthusiasm and enhancing learning effect and clinical practice ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Randomized or at least deliberate assignment of students to a pre-course simulation sequence
- Access to the OSCRS system and a clinical observation module before course delivery
#### Constraints
- Satisfaction differences between the timing groups were mostly not significant; the authors note the trend warrants further investigation in larger samples or across multiple semesters

### Target Learners
- Dental undergraduates in preclinical courses using virtual simulation

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving examination performance and clinical practice ability through early clinical exposure
