# Reader's Theater

## Description
Reader's Theater is a strategy that combines reading practice and performing by having learners read scripts created from books or stories aloud. Students perform by reading scripts without the use of costumes or props. Reader's theater can be used to involve students in reading aloud, enhance reading skills, increase confidence, improve fluency, and motivate reluctant readers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scripts that are fun to do with lots of good dialogue. Students do not memorize their parts; they always read from their scripts.
#### Constraints
- Boring scripts are no better than boring stories. It is important that students feel comfortable in the performance mode.
