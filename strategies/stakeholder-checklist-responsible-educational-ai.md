# Use a four-dimension stakeholder checklist to assess whether educational AI aligns with cognitive, pedagogical, emotional, and ethical principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper proposes a practical checklist (Table 1) to help key stakeholders—AI designers, students, and institutions—assess educational AI against the four dimensions of the framework. As the authors state, "we propose in Table 1 a practical checklist to help key stakeholders—namely AI designers, students and institutions—assess whether educational AI aligns with cognitive and pedagogical principles, according to the four dimensions studied in this paper". Checklist items include scaffolding reasoning and metacognition, preserving cognitive effort, communicating AI limits, minimizing monitoring, and redesigning assessments to reward authentic learning through peer review, oral defense, and iterative writing.
