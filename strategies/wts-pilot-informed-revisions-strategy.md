# Revise professional learning materials and structure in response to pilot feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes concrete revisions made for the 2024/25 efficacy study based on teacher and coach feedback: materials were housed in NMPED's Canvas platform, one-page overviews and vignettes were added for each practice, materials now "more explicitly describe how practices can apply to a variety of genres," supports for novice coaches were included, and materials were updated to "better support teachers to work as a team when a coach is not available." Clearer guidance emphasizes planning ahead and scheduling trainings in advance, and coach logs were revised with reminders for timely completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher and coach feedback collected during a pilot implementation to identify feasibility and usability problems
#### Constraints
- The one-page overviews were piloted only during spring implementation; genre applicability concerns arose because materials initially did not apply to all text genres

### Target Learners
- Grade 4-8 teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective implementation of evidence-based writing instruction practices
