# Integrate IVR as warmup or refresher training with self-guided or peer-to-peer debriefs followed by facilitated large-group debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete integration recipe proposed by the study's participants for exploiting IVR's scalability while preserving debriefing quality. Simple IVR scenarios with computer-based feedback serve as "a warmup for large-scale simulations or as refresher training in between large-scale simulations". Learners are trained to conduct self-guided or peer-to-peer debriefs, potentially followed by a facilitated debriefing in a larger group, and learners receive prompts to guide their debriefing or reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must be trained to conduct self-guided reflection or debriefing before unsupervised scenarios
#### Constraints
- Risk of educational blind spots if learners are enacting the scenario and/or debriefing on their own

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate learners and junior health professionals in emergency training

### Target Learning Goals
- Emergency management algorithms
- Clinical findings interpretation
- Verbal communication
