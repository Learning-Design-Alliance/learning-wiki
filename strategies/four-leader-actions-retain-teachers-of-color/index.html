Four leader actions to retain teachers of color: financial incentives, disaggregated data, diverse leader investment, and inclusive environments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The report closes with four recommended actions for state, district, and school leaders: value teachers of color through loan forgiveness, service scholarships, loan repayment and relocation incentives; collect and disaggregate recruitment, hiring, and retention data by race/ethnicity; invest in recruiting and developing diverse leaders committed to positive working conditions; and ensure curriculum and work environments are inclusive and respectful of all racial and ethnic groups.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coordination across school, district, and state levels, including budget authority for financial incentives and data systems capable of disaggregation by race/ethnicity.

Constraints¶

  • The report acknowledges these recommendations may seem straightforward but are difficult to put into practice.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers of color

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher retention, workforce diversity, equitable working conditions

Claims¶

  • Teachers of color face retention pressures including placement in high-poverty schools, pigeonholing into race-based roles, undervalued ideas, and an unrecognized invisible tax of extra duties. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Disaggregate engagement and performance data by subgroup and regularly evaluate and adjust support plans
  • Make retaining teachers of color a district priority: compensate the invisible tax and place teachers in cohorts to reduce isolation
  • Districts use financial, personnel management, and working-conditions strategies to recruit and retain teachers, including leadership compensation, mentoring, and collaboration time
  • Invest in principal retention strategies that address the invisible tax on leaders of color
  • Multi-pronged state investments to diversify and retain the educator workforce
