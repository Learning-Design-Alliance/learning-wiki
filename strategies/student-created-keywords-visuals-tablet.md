# Have students create their own keywords, definitions, and visuals with technology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study results, the authors recommend that "instead of learning with ready-made keywords, students can create their own keywords, definitions, and visuals via a tablet and use different mnemonic strategies together". They also recommend planning studies in which students with SLD and their typically developing peers participate together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to tablet devices for creating keywords, definitions, and visuals
#### Constraints
- This recommendation extends beyond what was tested, as the study used researcher-prepared materials

### Target Learners
- middle school students with specific learning disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- science concept learning and retention
