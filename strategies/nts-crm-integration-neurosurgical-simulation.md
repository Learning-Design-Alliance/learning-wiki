# Integrate immersive non-technical skills and crisis resource management training into neurosurgical simulation programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "future simulation programmes should prioritize the integration of immersive non-technical skills (NTS) training, including crisis resource management (CRM) scenarios specifically tailored to the neurosurgical context" to address simulation's limited replication of stress and consequence. It cites accumulating evidence that such programmes enhance team performance and reduce the incidence of error.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Crisis resource management scenarios specifically tailored to the neurosurgical context.
#### Constraints
- Assessing the real-world impact of NTS training is challenging due to the individualized nature of these competencies.

### Target Learners
- neurosurgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- non-technical skills
- crisis response and decision-making under pressure
- safer surgical care
