# Radical constructivist MARSGs require mindful development and instructor oversight to ensure learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For developers pursuing radical constructivist MARSGs—games affording flexible play without one objectively correct answer—the review recommends extra safeguards. The authors state that "while radically constructivist games are feasible, they will likely require more mindful development to afford this meaningful flexibility of play [59] or supervision from an instructor to ensure students are learning material appropriately [28]." Nuanced real-time feedback also works toward radical constructivism, and such games can leverage many framework elements but their applications must be nuanced. Because successful application requires an individualized, domain-dependent approach, radical constructivism is excluded from the general design-considerations figure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- More mindful development to afford meaningful flexibility of play, or instructor supervision such as a post-assessment verbal report with a human being
#### Constraints
- Most MARSGs employ quizzes as knowledge checks seeking objective correct answers, limiting radical constructivist application; the approach suits complex learning topics requiring internal working models, not rote memorization

### Target Learners
- learners in complex, higher-order domains such as computational thinking and managerial decision-making
- instructors supervising flexible-play MARSG sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- forming internal working models of complex material
- generating nuanced solutions rather than seeking one correct answer
