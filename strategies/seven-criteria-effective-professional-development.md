# Effective professional development is content focused, active, collaborative, modeled, coached, reflective, and sustained

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The chapter presents seven criteria for effective professional development identified by Darling-Hammond, Hyler & Gardner (2017), which future physics teachers need in order to orient themselves within different PD programs. Effective PD, as listed, "Is content focused", "Incorporates active learning utilizing adult learning theory", "Supports collaboration, typically in job-embedded contexts", uses models of effective practice, provides coaching and expert support, offers opportunities for feedback and reflection, and is of sustained duration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PD programs should be evaluated against all seven criteria, including sustained duration and job-embedded collaboration
#### Constraints
- The chapter reports that US science teachers rated the alignment of their PD experiences with elements of effective PD as only moderate (average about 50 on a 100-point alignment scale)

### Target Learners
- in-service physics and science teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving teaching practice through high-quality professional development
