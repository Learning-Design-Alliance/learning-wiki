# Develop educational projects promoting hygienic precautions and routine preconception CMV counseling by doctors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends educational projects as the practical route to cCMV prevention, since no effective vaccine or treatment exists. It states that "developing educational projects to promote effective hygiene is very important" and that "medical doctors must routinely counsel patients about CMV preventive methods and behavioral interventions before pregnancy." Health care professionals are positioned as critical disseminators of preventive information to women of childbearing age.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Doctors must be aware of CMV transmission routes and hygienic precautions themselves before they can counsel patients effectively
#### Constraints
- No effective vaccine or established therapeutic treatment for cCMV existed at the time of writing; hyperimmune globulin safety and efficacy were not established

### Target Learners
- Medical doctors, especially family doctors and general practitioners
- Women of childbearing age

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of CMV transmission routes and hygienic prevention practices
- Preconception counseling behavior
