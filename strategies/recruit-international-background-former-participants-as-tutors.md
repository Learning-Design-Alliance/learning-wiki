# Recruit former participants with international backgrounds as peer tutors for international students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends staffing peer-guided courses for international students with tutors who share their situation: all four tutors had an international background (Canada, Syria, USA) and all were former participants in the tutorial, recruited "since they showed interest in taking on the job and seemed to be well suited". Because of their comparable social roles, tutors "can act more empathetic with students’ worries", serve as role models, and use less technical language that contributes more to understanding.

## Design Implications
