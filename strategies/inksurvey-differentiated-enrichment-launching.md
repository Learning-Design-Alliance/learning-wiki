# Launch independent follow-up questions for faster workers to enable differentiated instruction and keep every student on task

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because InkSurvey lets the instructor "independently launch access to any question at any time", an instructor can start the whole class on a fundamental-concept question, then launch an enrichment question for students whose correct responses arrive early. By the time most students have responded, a subset will have considered the enrichment question, and the received responses can seed class discussion of it after the instructor addresses the original question's formative feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor ability to launch questions independently at any time within InkSurvey, and anticipation of the internet-access temptation to prepare engagement for faster workers
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as one way to address the classroom challenge of students' ready access to the internet during class; not all students will have individually experienced the enrichment question

### Target Learners
- University students in classes with mixed pacing, e.g., introductory physics

### Target Learning Goals
- Deeper understanding of fundamental concepts via enrichment questions; keeping every student on task
