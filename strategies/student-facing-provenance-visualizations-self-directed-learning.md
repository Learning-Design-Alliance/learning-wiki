# Share stepwise performance visualizations with students to support self-directed learning and reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Teachers in the evaluation suggested VisTA's visualizations could be shown to students themselves, helping them identify where they went wrong and reflect on their performance. The authors connect this to theories of self-directed learning where learners set their own goals, monitor their performance, and seek out feedback, and cite prior work showing student-facing performance visualizations prompted reflection and reduced gaming of the system. They propose expanding VisTA's design to include students in future studies.
