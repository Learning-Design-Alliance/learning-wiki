# Develop department-specific communication enhancement strategies, including virtual-reality emergency-scenario simulation

## Description
Because communication demands differ across departments, the article recommends that "hospitals should develop department-specific communication enhancement strategies." For the emergency department, where NCCCS scores were lowest, it proposes that "Virtual reality technology can be utilized to simulate various emergency scenarios, enhancing nurses' ability to adapt and communicate effectively in high-pressure environments." For pediatrics and geriatrics it recommends targeted training, e.g. communication with children and parents and listening to elderly patients' needs. Policy-level recommendations add incorporating caring ability and communication competence into performance evaluations with rewards for model nurses.
