# Weekly reading responses with randomized in-class sharing to foster discussion of historical literature

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A recurring active-reading routine: students write a thoughtful weekly response of about one page to the reading materials and class lectures, and each week a random selection of students is asked to share their responses with the class to foster a discussion. The strategy pairs a wide array of readings, spanning historical biographies to social science literature, with supplementary media such as audiobooks, films, and artwork. It operationalizes the course's premise that becoming conversant in history means developing a faculty for creatively processing the past in the present for a better future.
