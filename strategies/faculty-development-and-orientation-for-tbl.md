# Pair TBL courses with faculty development sessions and student orientation on TBL objectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that faculty development is essential because untrained faculty members may struggle to guide discussions effectively, limiting the potential benefits of TBL. Accordingly, "Faculty Development sessions were held immediately before TBL to ensure standardized instructions", and a faculty member provided a pre-course orientation to students explaining the importance of TBL and its learning objectives. The authors recommend that when conducting a TBL course, advantages and disadvantages be considered and faculty and students discuss comprehensive learning objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty members willing to embrace the principles of TBL rather than merely transmit knowledge
#### Constraints
- The article warns positive learning effects may be undermined if the psychological needs of medical students do not match the objectives of TBL

### Target Learners
- medical students
- faculty tutors

### Target Learning Goals
- effective facilitation of team-based clinical reasoning learning
