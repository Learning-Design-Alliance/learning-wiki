# Use Peyton's 4-step teaching approach during the guided teaching phase of skills practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the STALS practice component, the course adopts Peyton's 4-step teaching approach for the guided teaching phase before trainees move into free practice on the simulators. The article states "During the teaching phase, Peyton's 4-step teaching approach [4] is embraced", after which trainees use the training simulators, LapSim VR trainer, and animal organ perfusion simulators for programs including hand-eye coordination, visual navigation, dissection, and advanced energy operations.
