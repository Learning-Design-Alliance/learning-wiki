# Plan for possible points of infrastructuring, including breakdowns and anticipation work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This third IMPROV action area determines when infrastructuring activities could unfold. The article defines that "a point of infrastructuring is the moment when an infrastructure is visible to its users so that it can be brought to the fore to be improved." Breakdowns are classic points because infrastructures remain invisible while functioning; anticipation work complements them by envisioning needs or repairs in unknown futures, which matters for long-term sustainability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Points of infrastructuring are always local, embedded in practice, and significantly impacted by how infrastructure is perceived by users
#### Constraints
- Designing for future users can be challenging, causing frustration and attrition among participants

### Target Learners
- teachers
- project teams implementing innovations

### Target Learning Goals
- creating opportunities to negotiate changes with current infrastructures
