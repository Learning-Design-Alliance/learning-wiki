# Sequence summarizing instruction from explicit macrorule modelling on short texts to independent summarizing of curriculum texts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The programme's recipe combined "explicit teacher modelling, guided practice and independent practice of individual students or pairs of students". In the first part students learned to choose and create titles expressing main ideas, delete unimportant ideas, circle important words, and connect them into 1–2 sentence summaries. In the second part they rehearsed on textbook texts from science and social studies, first short texts twice weekly, then longer texts worked mostly independently with teacher accuracy checks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher one-day training, a manual with precisely described schedule of content, prepared texts, and student workbooks
#### Constraints
- Large differences in relative progress between classrooms of individual teachers, attributed to differences in teachers' engagement/effort

### Target Learners
- 4th grade primary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying deletion, generalization and integration macrorules to produce coherent summaries
