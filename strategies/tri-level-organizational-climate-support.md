# Foster a supportive organizational climate through a university-department-supervisor tri-level linkage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building a multi-level support system to mitigate burnout risks at the systemic level. At the university level, awards for "Excellent Supervisors" highlighting moral and benevolent leadership can role-model positive practices. At the department level, heads should foster open, inclusive, collaborative atmospheres through teaching rounds and case discussions. At the supervisor level, "teaching communities" provide platforms for exchanging ideas and sharing the burden of student support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-level coordination across university, department, and supervisor levels
#### Constraints
- Organizational-level support is one of the most significant protective factors against professional burnout among healthcare workers
