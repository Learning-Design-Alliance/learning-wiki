# Five classroom use cases deploying social robots for emotional learning, conflict mediation, and one-on-one support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each robot type is operationalised as a use case: exploring social issues through enacted scenarios ('What would you do?'), emotion regulation and physical proximity exercises including boundary-violation exploration, one-on-one settings from job interview training to sharing emotions with a trustworthy partner, externally guided scenarios encouraging pupils to push classroom-rule boundaries, and re-enactment of concretely observed classroom scenes. 'The key advantage across all five use cases is twofold': physical presence surpassing multimedia animations, and abstraction because robots are machines rather than humans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use case four requires ethical guardrails and clear guidelines, informing pupils about the experimental setting, and external coaches rather than classroom teachers to control the robot
#### Constraints
- Robots' enactment differs pedagogically and ethically from showing a video, though physical presence may be crucial in some educational contexts, though not necessarily in all cases

### Target Learners
- pupils between the ages of eight and fifteen in MindMatters schools

### Target Learning Goals
- emotional learning
- conflict mediation
- emotion regulation
- self-disclosure and trust
