# Integrate concrete communication strategies, metaphors, and practical implementation tips into e-learning for physiotherapists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy recommends that e-learning for physiotherapists on low back pain management explicitly teach communication skills rather than only guideline content, because guidelines seldom specify how to communicate. The article states: "Future educational interventions should be encouraged to invest on improving communication skills of physiotherapists and provide metaphors and analogies along with tips for practical implementation." The EEI's metaphors, reassurance tips, and pain-education videos were the content participants valued most.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must demonstrate how guideline recommendations can be applied in practice, e.g. metaphors, clinical videos, and simulated scenarios as used in the EEI
