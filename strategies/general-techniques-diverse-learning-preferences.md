# Use general teaching techniques and materials that cover the needs of learners with diverse learning preferences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
Following Ellis (2005), the article recommends that instead of matching language classes based on learners' learning styles, language teachers and syllabus designers can design activities that cover the needs of various language learners. This strategy is grounded in the study's finding that learning styles do not always affect language learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and syllabus designers should design activities covering diverse learner needs
#### Constraints
- Based on null findings for impulsivity and reflectivity specifically in reading comprehension

### Target Learners
- Adult EFL learners with diverse learning preferences

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- General language learning
