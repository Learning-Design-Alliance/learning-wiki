Form Interest Groups and Strengths-Based Groups in Science¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Interest groups can work well in science teaching 'if learners perceive value in participating in a given activity', wish to use individual talents, and persevere in making a success of collaborative endeavors. The digest also states that groups should not be formed based on needs only: highly talented or gifted science pupils should be grouped and given a challenging science curriculum.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learners must perceive value in the activity for interest groups to work well.

Constraints¶

  • Interest groups depend on learners' perceived value, desire to use individual talents, and perseverance in collaborative endeavors.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 science pupils, including highly talented or gifted pupils

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Engagement in collaborative science activities and a challenging science curriculum
