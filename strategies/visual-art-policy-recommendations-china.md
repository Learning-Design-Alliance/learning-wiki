# Policy recommendations: integrate visual art into formal curriculum and expand low-cost community activity centers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that policy-makers in developing countries "cherish the application of visual art activities by introducing it under a formal curriculum" so that qualified and trained teachers can integrate art into their subject matter. They further recommend that governments monitor the cost of out-of-school private training centers to eliminate social injustice, and that governments, non-government organizations, and communities work together to promote free or low-cost out-of-school training centers offering different extracurricular activities. They also urge shifting parents' mindset away from viewing spending money as their best effort with zero parent-child communication and relationship time.
