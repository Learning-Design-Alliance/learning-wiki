# Human-in-the-loop quality control for AI-generated educational content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that concerns about AI-generated content quality in educational tools be addressed by keeping humans in the loop: "implementing a human-in-the-loop approach for quality control is an effective solution." This involves more extensive post-editing and customization of AI-generated content before release, as textbook authors could quickly identify discrepancies between their knowledge and LLM-generated content while still finding AI a good starting point. The article also advocates explicitly annotating which parts are generated by AI, following the transparency obligations of the EU AI Act.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors or textbook authors available to post-edit and customize AI-generated content
- Explicit annotation of AI-generated portions of the tool
#### Constraints
- The article notes AI cannot always discern subtle differences or generate content that satisfies authors or instructors

### Target Learners
- higher-education students reading AI-enhanced textbooks

### Target Learning Goals
- trustworthy and accurate AI-supported reading support
