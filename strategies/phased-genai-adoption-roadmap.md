# Phased implementation roadmap for departments adopting GenAI personalization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review proposes a phased approach to GenAI personalization adoption with five phases: Foundation (Months 1–2: form working group with faculty, IT, legal, and students; conduct vendor vetting; draft policy template; identify volunteer faculty for pilot), Pilot (Months 3–6: deploy in 1–2 sections; instrument key metrics such as help latency, hint usage, and grading agreement; weekly check-ins; compare to control), Evaluation (Month 7: summarize process and outcome metrics; analyze equity by stratifying on preparation, language, and other factors; faculty/student debriefs; reach a departmental judgment), Scale (Months 8–12: expand to additional courses; institutionalize training, documentation, audits; continuous monitoring), and Sustain (Year 2+: annual policy review; vendor re-evaluation; longitudinal studies; share practices). The review also outlines resource requirements for a mid-sized CS department.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Working group with faculty, IT, legal, and student representation
- Vendor vetting before deployment
- Instrumentation of key process and outcome metrics during pilots
- Departmental judgment on whether benefits outweigh risks before scaling
#### Constraints
- The review states the aim is not to enforce hard thresholds but to encourage routine monitoring of process and outcome metrics and structured decision-making

### Target Learners
- Higher-education computer science students in departments adopting GenAI personalization

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained performance and quality maintenance across scaled GenAI deployments
- Evidence of learning benefits and equity monitoring
