# Discuss the Benefits of Positive Self-Talk

## Description
Openly communicating what self-talk is and how it can help. This involves explaining the psychological benefits of positive thinking and addressing skepticism about changing one's mindset. Discussions can include reviewing psychology and self-help resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to psychology and self-help resources. Facilitator with knowledge of the benefits of positive self-talk.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if learners are deeply entrenched in negative thinking patterns. Requires a skilled facilitator to address skepticism and promote open dialogue.

- Skeptical learners who need to understand the rationale behind positive self-talk.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- To educate learners about the impact of self-talk and motivate them to adopt a more positive mindset.

### Affordances
- Provides learners with a deeper understanding of the science behind positive thinking. Addresses common misconceptions and resistance to changing one's mindset. Encourages critical thinking and informed decision-making.

### Personalization
- Tailor the discussion to learners' specific concerns and questions. Provide examples that are relevant to their lives and experiences.
