# Structuring Lessons

## Description
A lesson structure maps the teaching and learning that occurs in class. Sound lesson structures reinforce routines, scaffold learning via specific steps/activities, optimise time on task and classroom climate by using smooth transitions, and stimulate and maintain engagement by linking lesson and unit learning through planned sequencing of teaching and learning activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to plan the sequence of activities and transitions within a lesson.
#### Constraints
- Overly rigid structures may limit flexibility and student autonomy.
