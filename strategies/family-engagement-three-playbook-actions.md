# Families partner with schools on attendance and grades through belonging, collaborative support, and joint monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The playbook recommends three implementable family actions: (1) build a community of belonging—talking with other parents and meeting with principals about family engagement, since high-quality engagement lays "the foundation for a healthy school culture rooted in trust"; (2) support student success by asking about collaborative strategies such as curriculum nights and guidance for home learning; and (3) monitor together by attending Local School Council meetings, reviewing school data including on-track categories, and asking about bright spots by student group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessible school data (e.g., To&Through data tool) and channels for family input such as LSC meetings
#### Constraints
- Written for Chicago Public Schools families; uses 'parent' per LSC seat designation

### Target Learners
- All K-12 students, via their parents and caregivers

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving attendance and grades through family-school partnership
