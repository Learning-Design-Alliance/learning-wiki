# Patience

## Description
Teaching higher-level thinking skills and analyzing mentor texts can be a new experience for struggling learners, and it doesn't come easily. Be patient and keep trying.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher commitment to providing patient and supportive instruction. A classroom environment where students feel safe and valued. Time for reflection and feedback.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming. Some students may require more intensive support than others. Requires careful planning to ensure that all students receive the support they need.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for struggling learners and those who lack confidence

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Foster a growth mindset, encourage persistence, promote self-efficacy, create a supportive learning environment, reduce anxiety and frustration

### Affordances
- Provides emotional support and encouragement. Creates a safe space for experimentation and risk-taking. Promotes a growth mindset by emphasizing the importance of effort and persistence.

### Personalization
- The teacher can provide individualized support and feedback. The teacher can adjust the pace of instruction to meet student needs. The teacher can celebrate student progress and effort.
