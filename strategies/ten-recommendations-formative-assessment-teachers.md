# Ten recommendations for teachers on effectively using formative assessment in the classroom

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The chapter closes with "ten recommendations for teachers about how to effectively use formative assessment in the classroom." They cover employing assessments built on a cognitive-developmental research foundation, engaging students in cognitively demanding tasks, informing students of learning goals, administering assessments frequently and informally, giving feedback as "constructive comments, not grades," supporting self- and peer assessment, using evidence from formal and informal FAs for diagnosis, preplanning probing questions and alternative instructional paths, leveraging prior knowledge, and collaborating regularly with other teachers.
