# Improve leadership skills of nurse coordinators to foster an evidence-based practice culture in primary health care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend developing the leadership capabilities of nurse coordinators as key agents for implementing a culture more favourable to evidence-based clinical practice, and creating a nursing research friendly work environment through increased communication, collaboration and a more active attitude towards workplace research. They state that "it would be highly desirable to improve the leadership skills for nurse coordinators, as they appear to be key agents in the implementation of a culture more favourable towards EBCP".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment of nurses and health managers at the highest level
- Strategies involving the whole primary health care team, taking nurses' characteristics into account
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design does not allow exploration of the directionality of the associations; leadership was explored only tangentially

### Target Learners
- nurse coordinators and managers in primary health care
- clinical primary health care nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- leadership for evidence-based clinical practice
- knowledge transfer into clinical practice
