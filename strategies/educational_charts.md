# Educational Charts

## Description
Educational charts are visual aids that present data in an accessible and understandable format, revealing patterns and stories behind the numbers. They are valuable tools for simplifying complex information, analyzing processes, and supporting arguments in presentations, handouts, and reports. Common types of charts include pyramid charts for visualizing hierarchies and flowcharts for illustrating processes. Effective charts use clear labeling, appropriate scales, and strategic colors to enhance readability and comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to charting software or tools. Implementation involves careful selection of chart types and integration of data. May require training in data visualization.
#### Constraints
- Poorly designed charts can be misleading or confusing. Inaccurate data can undermine credibility. Requires attention to detail and accuracy.
