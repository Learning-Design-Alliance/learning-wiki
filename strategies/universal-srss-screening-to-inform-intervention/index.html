Conduct universal mental health screening with the Student Risk Screening Scale to inform intervention¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The district implemented a universal screening process using the Student Risk Screening Scale (SRSS) to gather information about students' mental health and inform intervention. Between fall 2020 and December 2023 it "conducted 6,637 student screenings through Project Cal-Well." Screening is positioned as a Tier 1 universal practice feeding the district's MTSS approach.
