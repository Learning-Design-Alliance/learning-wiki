# Scaffolding Structure and Function in PBL

## Description
Scaffolding structure and function in project-based learning (PBL) involves providing students with the necessary tools to demonstrate their understanding of content by giving them specific functions and structures of the language. This includes offering sentence starters, stems, and creating lessons that scaffold how to justify answers or use specific language structures (e.g., "If ______, then ______").

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful analysis of project requirements, creation of appropriate sentence starters and stems, and explicit instruction on language structures.
#### Constraints
- May require additional preparation time and careful monitoring to ensure students are using the scaffolds effectively and not becoming overly reliant on them.
