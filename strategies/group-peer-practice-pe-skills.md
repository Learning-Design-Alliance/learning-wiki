# Have students learn physical examination skills in groups with peer observation, near-peer teachers, and simulated or real patients with feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Drawing on cited work, the article reports that "the students can more effectively learn physical examination skills by studying within a group than by studying individually", because group practice lets students observe and learn from others. It also notes near-peers can be effective teachers and that simulation training and feedback may influence skills outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
