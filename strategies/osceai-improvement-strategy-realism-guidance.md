# Future OSCEai updates should improve patient-behaviour realism and provide context-specific guidance during simulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future updates to OSCEai address the realism and usability limitations reported by students. For realism, they propose "fine-tuning a LLM to better simulate real patient behaviours, emotional responses, and variability in symptom presentation" and integrating virtual or augmented reality. For usability, they recommend "detailed, context-specific instructions during simulations, including visual aids, real-time feedback, and post-case questions to test and reinforce concepts", plus multidisciplinary scenarios for interprofessional education.
