# Prioritize future research on ChatGPT's efficacy in STEM subjects and its support for collaborative learning and mental health

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review's Future studies section sets an agenda for empirical work on ChatGPT in higher education. It proposes "Investigating ChatGPT's efficacy in certain subject areas, such as science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM), where individuali zed education and immediate feedback are critical," including comparing academic performance of students who use ChatGPT against those who do not. It also proposes studying ChatGPT's effects on group participation and collaborative work quality, and its role in student mental health support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should address the shortcomings of ChatGPT, particularly its lack of emotional intelligence and interpersonal interactions
#### Constraints
- The review notes the current evidence base is a rapid review limited to works published between January 2023 and July 2023

### Target Learners
- higher education students, including STEM students and international students

### Target Learning Goals
- individualized instruction, immediate feedback, collaborative learning, and mental health support
