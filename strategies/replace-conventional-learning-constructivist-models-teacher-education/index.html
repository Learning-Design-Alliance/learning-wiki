Strategies

Replace conventional lecture learning with constructivist models in teacher education to raise and equalize critical thinking¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that conventional learning produced the lowest critical thinking scores at every ability level, the authors recommend that teacher education programs abandon conventional learning and adopt constructivist models such as QASEE, with RQA as an alternative. They further recommend continual classroom implementation so students become accustomed to the model's activities, and suggest special courses developing critical thinking for pre-service teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learning models must include pre-class individual preparation activities requiring students to read and generate questions and answers

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one quasi-experiment at a single university over one semester

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service teachers in Indonesian teacher education programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • critical thinking skills

Affordances¶

  • Qasee Learning Model

Claims¶

  • Under conventional learning, pre-service teachers at all ability levels obtain the lowest critical thinking scores, with upper and middle groups statistically equal [+W]
  • The interaction between learning model and academic ability significantly affects pre-service teachers' critical thinking skills [+W]
  • The QASEE model equalizes critical thinking outcomes across upper, middle, and lower academic ability groups [+W]
  • Pre-service teachers taught with the QASEE model achieve higher critical thinking scores than those taught with RQA or conventional learning [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use the constructivist learning approach to improve academic achievement, especially at college and high school levels and across subjects other than religious instruction.
  • Organize in-service training programs and atelier studies at the education-region level to develop teachers' skills in applying constructivist learning models
