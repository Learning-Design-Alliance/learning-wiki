# Run student oral talks on announced topic lists to extend the curriculum and relieve prior-knowledge discrepancies in multidisciplinary courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course assigns oral talk topics (Table I) tied to each class's content, each tagged with one of three goals: emphasizing hardware and physics, digesting news with quantum knowledge, or exploring frontier fields beyond textbooks. The authors state that "the setting of oral talks also helps to relieve the discrepancy of students' prior knowledge and hence is very necessary for courses open to multidisciplinary students." Topics are announced "two or three topics in every class" rather than all at once, which "helps students gain better understanding of the meaning of the oral talk topics."
