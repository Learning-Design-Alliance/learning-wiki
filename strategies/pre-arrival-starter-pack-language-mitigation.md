# Provide a pre-arrival Starter Pack to mitigate the language barrier and prepare students in English-language Python terminology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For international outreach events where oral English is challenging, the report found the Starter Pack the most important mitigating measure: "Perhaps the most important mitigating measure was The Starter Pack which ensured that participants were familiar with key Python and astronomical terms in English before arrival." Thai instructors on hand in practical sessions also lessened the barrier, and an anonymous online forum (Mentimeter) let students ask questions. The Starter Pack was found to be a valuable tool for students who engaged with it beforehand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Provision of the Starter Pack materials (installation guide, introductory and advanced notebooks, and in 2019 video tutorials) before the school
#### Constraints
- Oral communication remained occasionally challenging even with written comprehension largely unproblematic

### Target Learners
- non-native-English-speaking secondary students attending international outreach events

### Target Learning Goals
- familiarity with key Python and astronomical terms in English
- readiness for hands-on sessions
