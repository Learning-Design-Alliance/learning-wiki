# Train teachers and prioritize brainstorming as a modern method for teaching English grammar

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational guidance bodies and school administrations address weak English grammar achievement by adopting modern teaching methods, with brainstorming as a named example, and that teachers be trained to implement brainstorming lessons correctly. In the study, "Male and female teachers were trained in the correct way to implement the steps of the lesson through the method of brainstorming" before the semester-long experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in the correct implementation of brainstorming lesson steps, as done in the study
#### Constraints
- The study applied the method only to English grammar content of the sixth-grade English book, part one, in the second semester of 2016-2017

### Target Learners
- Sixth-grade students in public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- English grammar achievement
