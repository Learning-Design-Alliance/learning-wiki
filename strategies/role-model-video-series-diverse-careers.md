# Role model video series reflecting the diversity of observatory careers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NRAO Role Model series is a video product that "currently reflects the diversity of jobs and experiences that are needed to run a national observatory." The article proposes expanding the series to include ngVLA staff, and Table 1 proposes role model videos specifically for grade school girls and URM students in all fields that support an Observatory, presented as part of educational sessions delivered by a diverse group of students and post-docs to raise visibility of both radio astronomy and diversity in STEM.
