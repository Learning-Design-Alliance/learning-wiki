# Optional campus workshop during semester break to address missing physical interaction and group interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address the disadvantages of no physical interaction with equipment and no direct group interactions, the author proposes hosting "an optional campus workshop during the semester break" where students visit the lab to assemble, align and operate all equipment quickly without real data, analysis or formal reports. The visit also lets students meet their online cohort, in-person students, and faculty, and serves as a graduate recruiting tool. The campus visit program will be implemented in the near future.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- campus access during semester break
- transfer of students to the physical lab for assembly, alignment and operation without the need for real data
#### Constraints
- addresses only items 1 and 2 of the disadvantage list; it does not restore the design-refine cycle or debugging opportunities

### Target Learners
- online Advanced Lab students, many of whom are adult learners with work and family responsibilities

### Target Learning Goals
- apparatus assembly and alignment
- cohort and community building
