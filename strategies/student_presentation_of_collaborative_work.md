# Student Presentation of Collaborative Work

## Description
After completing a collaborative problem-solving task, groups project their work and present their final responses to the class. Learners take turns explaining how they arrived at their answers and why they believe they accomplished the task of building the best answer they could. This practice enhances communication skills, reinforces learning, and builds confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projector or whiteboard, collaborative work samples, time for presentations
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be hesitant to present in front of the class. Requires a supportive classroom environment to encourage participation and constructive feedback.
