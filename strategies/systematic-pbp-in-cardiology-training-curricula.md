# Incorporate PBP systematically into academic/institutional surgical training curricula for novice CIED implanters

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is that "formal surgical training for CIEDs should be systematically implemented in academic/institutional curricula using a PBP approach", beginning at the start of an electrophysiologist's learning curve. The strategy pairs the simulation curriculum with metrics-based progression checks so trainees demonstrate a predetermined performance level before supervised real-patient implantation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Academic or institutional adoption of a simulation-based curriculum
- Instructor training in the PBP method and access to validated intraoperative metrics
#### Constraints
- Simulators are rarely utilized in contemporary cardiology training programmes, so adoption would require new infrastructure

### Target Learners
- Cardiology trainees and novice CIED implanters

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent, standardized surgical technique for cardiac device implantation across institutions
