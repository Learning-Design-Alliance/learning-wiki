# Augmented Reality (AR) for Medical Education and Training

## Description
AR is used to create models of the human body that let medical students learn anatomy in depth; provide more training opportunities for medical students with the help of simulations; and allow students to practice surgeries on virtual patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AR technology and tools.
#### Constraints
