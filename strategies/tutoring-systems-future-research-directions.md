## Description
The review closes by directing future work: "Future research should focus on bridging gaps in scalability, addressing ethical considerations comprehensively, and advancing AI models to support diverse educational needs." Specific directions include improving AI to operate autonomously within robot-based systems beyond adjunctive designs, establishing transparent guidelines for data collection, usage, and security, and exploring long-term impacts of human-robot interaction on student development, which the review says remain underexplored.
