## Description
The deck's considerations for developing policy content include recognizing that implementing emerging technologies and GenAI effectively takes time, planning to frequently update policies, and adapting instructional methods for AI integration. It notes "AI is not just a grading tool; it can be used to create learning experiences that develop creativity and critical thinking," and recommends introducing policies with a position statement.
