# Shared online reflective journals for the instructional team

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before each term, the team set up a private online reflective journal with a general prompt to evaluate how the class went, viewable and commentable by all members. Entries covered emotional state, expectations, reactions to instruction, and ideas for improvement. The article reports "The shared nature of the journals helped open dialog amongst the faculty, peer instructors and researchers" and served as a repository of elements of PCK and documentation of how views about teaching evolved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All team members record entries and view and comment on each other's entries
