# Teach geodesic behaviour through tool-based exercises with pre-prepared configure files

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article packages its GeodesicViewer features as ready-formulated student exercises, each with a mathematical explanation directed at the teacher and a configure file holding the final result for reproduction. Flexibility is built in: 'The exercises can be made either from scratch or a conﬁgure ﬁle can be prepared in advance, so that the student only has to change a few parameters.' Online tutorials for the interface can be worked through alone or with teacher guidance. This lets instructors scale scaffolding from open exploration to parameter-tweaking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the GeodesicViewer (freely available for Linux and Windows) and, for teacher preparation, the mathematical background of the metric and geodesic equations.
#### Constraints
- The mathematical explanations are directed to the teacher rather than the students.

### Target Learners
- students in a first course of general relativity

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the behaviour of null and time-like geodesics in various space-times
