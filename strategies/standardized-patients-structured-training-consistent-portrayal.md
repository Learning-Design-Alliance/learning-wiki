# Use standardized patients with structured training and consistent role portrayal across simulation sessions

## Description
The article describes using standardized patients including trained actors, community-dwelling older adults, and physiotherapists who received "written role description, supervised rehearsals with feedback, and observations of real therapist-patient interactions." Each clinical case was portrayed by the same SP across all sessions to ensure stability in behavioral expression, emotional tone, and clinical cues.
