# Use measured student expectations of TAs to tailor TA professional development and faculty supervision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using short-answer survey questions about TA support - such as "What one action can your TAs at <this institution> take to best support you in your classes (please be as specific as possible)?" - analyzed thematically, to directly inform TA professional development and faculty guidance and supervision of TAs. Because expectations differed by time period and demographics, the authors suggest such measurement could serve as a diagnostic tool for adjusting instructional practice from term to term or year to year, and for including all voices in teaching-improvement efforts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short-answer survey item administered to enrolled students, with thematic coding of responses
#### Constraints
- Non-response bias differs across demographic groups, which the authors report alongside themes as a limitation

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate engineering students; graduate TAs as the beneficiaries of development

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving TA-led instructional support, interactions, preparation, and inclusive classroom climate

- [Undergraduate engineering students most often expect TAs to facilitate high-quality interactions (42.9%), with preparation (37.6%) and care/hospitality (7.61%) less frequently cited](../claims/ta-expectations-interactions-preparation-hospitality-themes.md) [+W]
- [Within TA-expectation themes, students most often call for more contact with TAs (72.2% of interaction responses), clearer content presentation (50.5%) and guidance (41.7%) within preparation](../claims/ta-expectations-secondary-themes-contact-presentation-guidance.md) [+W]
- [Student expectations that TAs facilitate high-quality interactions peaked during emergency remote teaching (51.6%) compared with pre-COVID (30.7%) and post-COVID (42.7%) periods](../claims/ta-interaction-expectations-peaked-during-ert.md) [+W]
- [Expectations for TA preparation were higher before COVID (47.4%) than during (31.4%) or after (36.2%), and were most popular among Latino (43.5%), Black (41.2%), and female (40.7%) students](../claims/ta-preparation-expectations-time-and-demographics.md) [+W]
- [Non-response to the TA-support question was highest among international (16.1%) and Black (17.7%) students and lowest among other URM (3.23%), Asian/White (4.55%), and female (5.03%) students](../claims/ta-support-nonresponse-demographic-differences.md) [+W]
