# Differentiate Google Classroom use by study year: multimedia library for freshmen, source-supported analysis for second years, complex use for senior students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article assigns Google Classroom different didactic purposes by year. First-year students use it "with a propaedeutic purpose, as a multimedia library (without downloading works and sending it for correcting analysis to the teacher)", because topographic survey skills require in-class work with maps and geodesic instruments. Second-year students in Population Geography need access to dynamic record-statistics, supported by the teacher restricting the search field to reputable statistical organizations. Years III-IV and Master students work in a complex way: performing tasks in Docs, Sheets and Slides, sending them for checking, commenting on multimedia content and monitoring achievements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher curation of reference sources on the Information page for data-search tasks
- Multimedia library content including video demonstrating topographic survey algorithms
#### Constraints
- Google Classroom use "will in no way replace work with a map or field surveys" for first-year cartography training

### Target Learners
- first- to fourth-year Bachelor and first-year Master geography students

### Target Learning Goals
- topographic survey skills
- demographic data analysis
- complex task completion and achievement monitoring
