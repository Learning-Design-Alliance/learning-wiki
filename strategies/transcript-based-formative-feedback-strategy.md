# Use clinical transcripts as a time-saving route to formative feedback on clinical performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends evaluating students' clinical performance via verbatim transcripts rather than full video review, since transcripts "required significantly less time to evaluate when compared to faculty evaluating full videos" while showing good agreement with video scores. This frees faculty time for personalized feedback and more clinical encounters per student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accurate verbatim transcription with speaker annotation
- A validated rating scale adapted for text-only scoring
#### Constraints
- Text-only input misses tone, gestures, and nonverbal cues

### Target Learners
- final-year dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical behavior guidance
- formative feedback
