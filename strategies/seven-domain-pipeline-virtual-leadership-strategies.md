# Districts should map virtual-learning and equity actions onto the seven domains of a comprehensive, aligned principal pipeline

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report adapts the seven-domain "comprehensive, aligned" principal pipeline—leader standards, pre-service preparation, selective hiring and placement, on-the-job evaluation and support, principal supervisors, leader tracking systems, and systems and sustainability—to virtual contexts. For each domain it offers strategies, e.g., "Include equity considerations for the virtual environment in leader standards" and providing "authentic opportunities for candidates to demonstrate competencies relevant to equity-focused leadership in a virtual environment." Domains are aligned because they reinforce one another.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts update preparation, hiring, and placement systems given the increased emphasis on virtual learning
#### Constraints
- The report describes itself as an early effort at guidance based on an emerging research base

### Target Learners
- K-12 school principals and the district leaders who develop and support them

### Target Learning Goals
- equity-centered leadership of high-quality virtual learning
