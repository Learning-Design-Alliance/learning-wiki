# Recommendations for adopting instructional programmes in Saudi kindergartens

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study closes with recommendations for schools, teachers, practitioners, and the Ministry of Education. Developers should build Arabic instructional programmes for tablets, mobile devices and desktops; schools should involve parents via training and awareness sessions; teachers must be properly trained to independently use and troubleshoot the programmes; and schools must provide infrastructure so every child can be instructed using instructional programmes within the school building.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding the needs, expectations and requirements of kindergarten children, teachers and kindergarten schools before developing programmes
#### Constraints
- Caution is urged because the study is based on a small sample size of 41 children from a single province

### Target Learners
- Arabic-speaking kindergarten children aged 5-6

### Target Learning Goals
- emergent literacy skills, and future extension to basic science and mathematics concepts
