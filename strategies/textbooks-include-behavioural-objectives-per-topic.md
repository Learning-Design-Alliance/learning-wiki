# Include the behavioural objectives of each major topic at the beginning of each topic in mathematics textbooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable recommendation from the article's recommendations section: "Authors of Mathematics textbooks should include the behavioural objectives of each of the major topic at the beginning of each topic in their textbooks in order to encourage self-development among students." This extends the prior-presentation strategy beyond the classroom teacher to printed materials, so students encounter objectives before studying each topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objectives placed at the beginning of each major topic, formulated as specific, measurable behavioural objectives
