# Mirror Faces

## Description
Learners pair up, and one makes an emotional face while the other mirrors it, promoting recognition and understanding of facial expressions associated with different emotions. This activity enhances empathy and nonverbal communication skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires learners to be paired up. A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may feel uncomfortable making facial expressions in front of others. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Affordances
- Engaging and interactive, promotes active participation, and enhances understanding of the connection between facial expressions and emotions.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose emotions to portray or be assigned specific emotions. Can be adapted by using pictures or written prompts.

## Impact
