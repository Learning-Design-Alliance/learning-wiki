# Accessing Students’ Background Knowledge

## Description
This strategy involves intentionally activating and building upon learners' existing knowledge and experiences to enhance comprehension and engagement. Teachers can research students’ native countries, cultures, and educational systems and draw on students’ experiences to connect what they are reading to their own lives. Approaches include: asking students to speak with peers in their home language about a topic, using a graphic organizer, finding materials that will engage students and involve them in the learning process so that they find elements they can connect to and learn from, inviting students to share special traditions, holidays, and foods from their cultures throughout the year and across the curriculum, or asking students to interview their family members in order to learn more about their memories and experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need to research students' backgrounds and create opportunities for sharing. Requires sensitivity to students' comfort levels with sharing.
#### Constraints
- Risk of making assumptions or putting students on the spot. Requires time and effort to gather information and prepare culturally relevant materials.
