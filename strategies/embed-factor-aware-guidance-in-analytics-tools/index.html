Embed factor-aware guidance directly within analytics tools at the point of interpretation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends embedding contextual, factor-aware support inside analytics dashboards rather than leaving biasing considerations implicit. As the authors state, "Providing contextual cues at the point of interpretation can help prevent premature conclusions and promote more transparent, evidence-aligned decisions." They add that "Integrating such support into dashboards or training can strengthen institutional capacity for responsible analytics use." The strategy operationalizes FACTRIA at the moment of interpretation through conversational scaffolding.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A structured framework of relevant biasing factors (FACTRIA) to drive the prompts
  • A mechanism (e.g., a chatbot) that surfaces factors while the user examines the visualization

Constraints¶

  • The study was a proof of concept with 11 participants at one institution; the authors caution about automation bias and note FACTRIA may require adaptation across institutional contexts

Target Learners¶

  • Higher education stakeholders making evidence-based decisions from institutional analytics dashboards

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Context-aware, responsible interpretation of institutional data

Affordances¶

  • Factria Biasing Factors Framework
