# Teach economy of movement by finding an optimum camera perspective that minimizes repositioning during laparoscopic tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the trial showed the RCH is disadvantaged when constant camera movement is required, the authors recommend minimizing camera movements: "To minimize time loss from repositioning the optimum perspective should be found to encourage economy of movement." This allows continuous cutting and maximizes time efficiency. They also recommend emphasis on teaching appropriate needle handling technique because the needle frequently bent in the needle guidance task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of which task regions require visualization so a perspective covering them can be selected
#### Constraints
- Derived from novice simulation performance on E-BLUS tasks; the authors call for clinical-setting studies

### Target Learners
- novice laparoscopic trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- laparoscopic task efficiency and camera navigation
