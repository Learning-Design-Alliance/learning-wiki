# Adopt OER to make digital learning flexible and affordable for learners facing medical, financial, and daily life challenges

## Description
The article recommends using open educational resources in online learning, arguing that the COVID-19 pandemic pushed institutions worldwide to shift instruction digital and that OER can "make learning more flexible and affordable for those who face medical, financial, and daily life challenges." It treats Hilton's (2016) synthesis as collective evidence supporting adoption and as guidance on how OER can be used internationally.
