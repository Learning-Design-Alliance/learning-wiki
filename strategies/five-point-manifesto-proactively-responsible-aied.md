# Adopt a five-point manifesto for pro-actively responsible AIED: inform and challenge, expand the field of view, guide EdTech and policy, examine good intentions for misuses, and expand focus beyond dominant learners

## Description
The article closes by proposing that "The following 5-point manifesto provides a start towards the action needed": (1) Inform and Challenge — the Community "must play a pro-active role in disseminating and actioning its collective knowledge beyond its own Community's comfort zone"; (2) Expand the field of view, including raising questions about "data exploitation for life-long surveillance of individuals"; (3) Engage with, guide and hold to account the EdTech industry and endorsing policies, working towards "auditing mechanisms and examples of best practice"; (4) Examine good intentions for implications beyond intended applications, since "good intentions conceived in research labs often do not extend to mainstream uses"; (5) Expand focus beyond dominant learners to differently abled and disenfranchised learners, treating "different abilities as strengths". This is the article's own implementable action recipe for the field.
