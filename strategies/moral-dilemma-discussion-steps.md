# Moral dilemma discussion with structured steps

## Description
A teaching strategy in which the teacher confronts each student with a moral dilemma and gives them the opportunity to hear what other students say about the sample event. UNESCO (1986) defines five steps: definition of dilemma, students' request of answers, determination of students' positions, evaluation of ideas, and meditation on reasons leading to a decision.
