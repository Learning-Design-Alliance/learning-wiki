# Weekly individualized formative feedback on group work using three criteria

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each week tutors assess students on their group work and provide written individualized feedback before the next project, based on three criteria: Individual Understanding (how well the student develops their own understanding), Group Understanding (how well the group ensures all members understand), and Group Focus (how well the group manages discussion and use of ideas). "Each week, we assess students on their group work and provide them with feedback for improvement." The feedback names one participation strength and one area to work on, discusses group functioning, and has been critical to ensuring less computationally-prepared students take charge of writing programs while more prepared students tutor classmates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors who observe group work continuously and can write feedback weekly; assessment criteria communicated through rubric-like expectations; small groups whose dynamics tutors can see.
#### Constraints
- The sustainable personnel model is a single faculty member, a graduate student, and ten undergraduate learning assistants for a one hundred student section; the initial implementation with two faculty, a post-doc, and a graduate student was unrealistic for sustainability.

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students working in groups, including those with varied computational preparation

### Target Learning Goals
- group collaboration and communication practices
- reflection on one's participation in group learning
