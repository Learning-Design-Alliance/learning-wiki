# Four-part didactic sequence structure for curvature-based mechanics problems

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article structures each application example with a fixed sequence: "se proponen las siguientes secciones: i) planteamiento de la situación física, ii) introducción de la curvatura en la situación física, iii) análisis general del modelo incluyendo la curvatura y estudio de los casos particulares y iv) comentarios y recomendaciones didácticas". This recipe guides the teacher from a physical situation, through introducing curvature, to general model analysis with particular and limit cases, and closes with didactic commentary. Both worked examples (circular and parabolic motion) follow it, supported by geometric deductions and graphical modeling in Python.
