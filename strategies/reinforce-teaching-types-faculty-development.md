# Reinforce the named teaching types among faculty and track resulting changes in teaching distribution and learner ratings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a forward-looking application, the authors propose faculty development built on awareness of the six verbal teaching types, paired with systematic outcome measurement. They state that future work will examine "how the distribution of teaching types will change once the different types of teaching moments identified in this paper are reinforced among our faculty teachers," alongside a separately developed and validated daily and weekly learner rating scale. They also report anecdotal observations that residents attain endoscopic comfort sooner once teachers are aware of the classification.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty teachers must be made aware of the six teaching types; a validated daily and weekly learner rating scale is used to measure outcomes
#### Constraints
- The reported benefits of awareness are anecdotal; the planned studies were underway but not reported in this article

### Target Learners
- residents on endoscopy rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- rate of attainment of endoscopic competence ratings
