# Begin MI training with reflection and OARS-type skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends starting MI training with reflective-listening and OARS skills, because the Reflections-to-Questions ratio was the skill counsellors acquired fastest, with all three using as many reflections as questions after about six months. The authors write that this finding "favours MI training beginning with reflection or \"OARS\" (open questions, affirmations, reflections, summaries) type skills".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial MI workshop after which supervision can target OARS-type skills such as reflective listening
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on an exploratory study of three counsellors at a single quitline

### Target Learners
- counsellors new to motivational interviewing
- telephone quitline counsellors

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective listening
- open questioning and other foundational MI skills
