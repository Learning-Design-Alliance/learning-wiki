# Use the Recommendation's Policy Action Areas to translate AI ethics values into policy across education, data governance and other spheres

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The page recommends that policymakers use the Recommendation's extensive Policy Action Areas to move from principles to implementation. It states that these areas "allow policymakers to translate the core values and principles into action" with respect to "data governance, environment and ecosystems, gender, education and research, and health and social wellbeing, among many other spheres". For education stakeholders, the education and research action area is the direct entry point for applying the ethics standard to learning systems.
