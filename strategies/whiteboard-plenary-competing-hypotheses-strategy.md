# Whiteboard group work followed by plenary presentation to surface competing hypotheses about a correlation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable classroom strategy: organize students into small groups, give each group a whiteboard to carry out the data analysis, then have every group present its findings and hypotheses to the class. The authors report that groups "presented the data in various ways", which "led to a rich discussion, allowing for a deeper understanding of the interpretation of the data and the distinction between correlation and causation".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups of four or five members, one whiteboard per group, and allotted time for a plenary session.
