# Spaced Learning Through Activity Breaks

## Description
Breaking up learning or work into short chunks of time interspersed with activity breaks leverages the 'spacing effect,' a well-replicated phenomenon in psychology. This method enhances memory retention by giving the brain time to process information and retrieve it later. Activity breaks during these intervals can include physical movement or other engaging tasks, optimizing cognitive function and creativity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured schedule that incorporates short intervals of focused learning or work interspersed with brief activity breaks. Minimal to no equipment needed for most breaks.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to balance learning intervals and break times effectively. Individuals may need initial guidance to understand the benefits and adapt to this method.
