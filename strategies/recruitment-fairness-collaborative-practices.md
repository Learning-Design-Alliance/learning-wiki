# Demonstrate commitment to fairness and collaborative practices when recruiting districts and schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
This strategy directs researchers to show commitment to fairness and collaborative practices when recruiting districts and schools into impact studies. The brief names commitment to fairness and collaborative practices as one of the common factors that may influence districts' and schools' ability and willingness to participate in research.
