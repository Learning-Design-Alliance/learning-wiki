# Design training programs to nurture non-cognitive skills, especially for students with low GPA scores

## Description
The article's recommendations call on universities and teaching staff to act on non-cognitive skills: "Design training programs for students to nurture their various non-cognitive skills which help improve their academic performance, especially in students with low GPA scores." Other recommendations include holding training sessions for teaching staff on the importance of these skills, researching staff attitudes toward them, identifying students with academic problems to provide assistance, and studying the most efficient development strategies.
