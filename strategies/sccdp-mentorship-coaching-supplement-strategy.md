# Integrate structured mentorship and reflective coaching alongside technical competency programs to support psychological outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the SCCDP improved technical and performance outcomes but not self-efficacy, the authors recommend that "future adaptation programs should integrate structured mentorship and reflective coaching components to enhance psychological resilience and confidence in addition to technical competence". The strategy pairs competency-based technical training with continuous mentoring, accumulation of successful experiences, and psychological support during the transition from student to nurse.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous mentoring and psychological support delivered alongside educational programs
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single-institution, single-group study where self-efficacy change was not statistically significant

### Target Learners
- New nurses in their transition period from student to practitioner

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-efficacy, psychological resilience, and confidence in performing nursing tasks
