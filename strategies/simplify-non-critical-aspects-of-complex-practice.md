# Provide necessary learning resources and simplify non-critical aspects of complex practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 theoretical, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The second strategy is "Providing learners with necessary learning resources and simplifying non-critical aspects of complex practice." This reflects productive complexity, "a balance between engaging students with complex problems and preventing them from nonproductive practices that merely increase their cognitive load." Non-critical aspects, such as managing data generated during investigation, can be simplified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Necessary learning resources and facilities must be provided to promote productive complexity
#### Constraints
- Simplification applies to non-critical aspects only; critical complexity must be preserved

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry skills
- data analysis
