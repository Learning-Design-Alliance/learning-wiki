# Gradual Release of Responsibility in Multimodal Composition

## Description
Using the gradual release of responsibility model to support students in multimodal composition. This involves direct instruction on selecting and analyzing multimodal resources (e.g., websites, book trailers, blogs), followed by facilitated discussions in pairs or small groups, and then independent analysis of multimodal examples. This scaffolding approach aims to strengthen students’ interest in multimodal composition and develop recursive design skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples of multimodal resources, time for direct instruction and discussion, opportunities for independent analysis.
#### Constraints
- May require significant preparation to identify appropriate multimodal resources. Relies on active participation in direct instruction and discussions. Some students may need additional support to develop independence.
