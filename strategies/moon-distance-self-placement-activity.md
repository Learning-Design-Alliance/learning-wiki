## Description
To revisit knowledge of the Sun-Moon-Earth system, the teacher asks students to stand at the distance within the installation where they think the Moon would be. The article reports that 'Experience has shown that they will choose many different distance throughout the installation,' making scattered placements a visible prompt for discussion about scale.
