# Research should explore simulation's effects across diverse populations including developing countries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review calls for further research to explore simulation's effects "across diverse populations, including those in developing countries," to maximize its educational potential in health professions education. It also highlights the need to investigate how different educational systems and cultural contexts affect the development of critical thinking and reflection.
