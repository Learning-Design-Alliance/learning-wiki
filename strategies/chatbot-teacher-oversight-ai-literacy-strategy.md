# Implement custom AI chatbots as teacher-supported learning assistants with teacher oversight, curated databases, and AI literacy development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends deploying domain-specific chatbots as assistants that support and relieve, not replace, teacher expertise. It states that "Ensuring sustainable implementation therefore requires teacher oversight, contemporary adaptation of the curated database to the learning group, and fostering AI literacy skills among both teachers and students". It further argues that didactic utility hinges on empowering educators to configure and customize bots to their pedagogical objectives and student needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher oversight of chatbot use
- Contemporary adaptation of the curated knowledge base to the learning group
- AI literacy skills among both teachers and students
#### Constraints
- Plausible but incorrect outputs cannot be eliminated despite RAG; Socratic questions may be misleading or terminology inconsistent

### Target Learners
- secondary STEM students

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and effective use of AI learning assistants in subject-specific instruction
