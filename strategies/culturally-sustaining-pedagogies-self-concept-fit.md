# Support self-concept fit through asset-based and culturally sustaining pedagogies, identity-connection assignments, and affinity spaces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that teachers and institutions foster belonging by supporting self-concept fit: asset-based and culturally sustaining pedagogies that "explicitly include and highlight the value of the kinds of knowledge students bring from their home cultures" have repeatedly been shown to support ethnic-racial identity and school belonging among Black and Latinx students. Concrete practices include assigning students to notice connections between their identity and course content, publicly discussing socioeconomic diversity during school transitions, and providing institutional support for affinity spaces such as Black student unions and LGBTQIA+ student organizations.
