# Passion-based seminar program culminating in senior internships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
UCLA-CS uses a seminar program, driven by teachers' passions and partner availability, to expand course offerings despite small size and limited staff; topics range from robotics to Latin American film, and new courses such as Exploring Computer Science and AP Capstone began as seminars. The program culminates in a senior internship taught as an applied economics course that "foregrounds the value of students' aspirational capital", connecting students with mentors across the city and on the UCLA campus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers willing to add seminars to their workload
- partner organizations and mentors at internship sites
#### Constraints
- Adding a course to a teacher's workload can be a challenge, per the report

### Target Learners
- Middle and high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Student agency
- career exploration
- college and career preparation
