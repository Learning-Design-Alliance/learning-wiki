# Internet-based application for post-discharge adherence reminders and health education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
After discharge, parents used an app developed by Hunan Children's Hospital through which they could "receive reminders for taking medicine and follow-up, and receive adherence questionnaires", alongside health education materials and emergency operation videos pushed according to the child's disease progression. Medical staff could check records and extract follow-up information in the system. The authors credit this follow-up system with improving adherence and regular hospital visits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A hospital-developed app with medication and follow-up reminders, education content, and adherence questionnaires
- Access controls so only authorized persons could view system information
#### Constraints
- Described within a single-center Chinese tertiary hospital context; the article does not report the app's effect separately from the training

### Target Learners
- Children with epilepsy and their parents after discharge

### Target Learning Goals
- Medication adherence
- Emergency management capability
- Continued health education
