# Use importance–performance analysis to prioritise weak core competencies for curricular improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors use IPA to map students' perceived importance of competencies against perceived performance, so that items in the 'concentrate here' quadrant become top priorities. "Such a mapping process enables the prioritization of certain teaching strategies on the basis of students' perception of importance for competence and perceived performance." The results "can guide instructors in designing curricular activities focused on the weaker core competencies of the students at a practicum site for each level of care".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete both importance and performance ratings for the same competence items
#### Constraints
- The study used only students' self-perceptions; the authors note instructor evaluations should be added in future work

### Target Learners
- nursing students undertaking fundamental nursing practicum

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying and prioritising weak core competencies for improvement
