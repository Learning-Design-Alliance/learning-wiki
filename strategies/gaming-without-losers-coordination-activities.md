# Design coordination activities as games without losers so all children participate actively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend coordination exercise programs structured as games in which no child loses, so that every child — including those who dislike sports or are not good at them — enjoys and actively participates. The Droutability program embodies this: children move continuously in all directions responding to color and number commands, with no elimination or scoring against peers. The article states that "Programs with a high element of gaming without losers like the Droutability allow all children to enjoy the activity and participate actively, including those children who do not like sports".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Equipment and commands that keep all children moving simultaneously
