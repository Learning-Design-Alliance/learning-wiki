# Stepwise progression pathway integrating theoretical instruction, simulation, and supervised clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends a structured, stepwise progression integrating theoretical instruction, simulation, and supervised clinical practice. Foundational curricula such as the FRS and TTP provide the initial cognitive framework, followed by dedicated courses covering perioperative management. Simulation-based training including virtual reality platforms (dVSS, DV-Trainer), dry lab, and wet lab models plays a central role, with a recommended minimum of 20 hours of simulation. The clinical phase involves participation as a bedside surgeon in approximately ten procedures, followed by supervised console experience in at least ten cases, and structured follow-up of initial cases supported by mentorship.
