# Use structured SENSE-model debriefing to consolidate VR simulation learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The active experimentation stage uses "structured debriefing based on Ko and Choi's [24] SENSE model, which consists of five steps: Share, Explore, Notice, Support, and Extend." Debriefing questions move from emotional reflection ("What did you feel during the game?") through psychological support to application planning ("How do you think what you learned in this program will help in clinical practice?"). The authors credit this debriefing with promoting metacognition and confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructor-led structured debriefing time (30 minutes in this study) following the simulation and PBL sessions
#### Constraints
- The authors state the SENSE debriefing was provided only to the intervention group, so its effect cannot be isolated from the VR simulation itself

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in simulation-based education

### Target Learning Goals
- critical reflection, metacognition, and transfer of simulation learning to clinical practice
