# Assess executive functions at an early age to enable early detection and targeted work plans

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend evaluating children's executive function levels early in schooling as a basic cognitive factor, so that atypical development can be detected and specific work plans implemented. As they state, "Early detection of atypical development of executive functions can lead to the establishment of specific work plans aimed at improving development and preventing future educational problems." The strategy targets cognitive, behavioral, and emotional development integral to later academic success.
