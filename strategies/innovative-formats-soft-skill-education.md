# Innovative continuing education formats including patients and families, videos, remote classes, interactive games and smart devices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond CPR technique practice, the article states continuing education strategies "precisam incluir planos que possibilitem a aquisição de habilidades comportamentais aplicadas ao local de trabalho e recursos disponíveis, sem impor pressão excessiva". It lists innovation possibilities: including patients and families to empower professionals, integrating face-to-face meetings with videos and remote classes, using interactive games, and using smart devices such as cell phones/apps or watches. It also highlights active teaching methodologies, especially realistic health simulation, as contributors.
