# Instruct distributed weekly practice in ex vivo laparoscopic skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article instructs participants to practice at least once, preferably three times, for at least 15 minutes during each week of the training experiment, because distributed practice leads to more durable training effects than massed practice. The authors state that "even a small amount of practice shows to be beneficial" and that the significant effect was found despite limited training time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must meet a minimum practice criterion of one time a week for at least 15 minutes
#### Constraints
- Clinical duties, on-call hours, and additional tasks during the COVID-19 outbreak interfered with practice for some participants

### Target Learners
- surgical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- laparoscopic measurement skill acquisition
