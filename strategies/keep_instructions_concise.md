# Keep Instructions Concise

## Description
Avoid lengthy instructions, especially for learners with low English proficiency. Break instructions into smaller phases aligned with the task stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ability to break down complex instructions into smaller steps.
#### Constraints
- Over-simplification may omit necessary details.
