# Enriched distance-lab preparation scenario: a one-hour Skype session with OnLabs one to two days before the physical lab

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an educational scenario that keeps the conventional face-to-face tutorial and adds steps in which interested students attend a one-hour Skype session where the tutor presents microscopy principles and performs a complete microscopy procedure using OnLabs, after which students repeat the procedure alone in OnLabs' instruction mode and evaluate the experience. The session is scheduled "1-2 days prior to the physical lab", with advance e-mails covering connection steps and software download.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A skype-tutor to run the session, students' network connection to Skype for Business, and OnLabs downloaded on each participant's PC or laptop
#### Constraints
- Only students who responded to the survey invitation took the extra steps; the physical lab and its tutorial were provided to all students

### Target Learners
- distance-learning undergraduate students enrolled in Biology laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- familiarity with microscopy concepts and procedures before a physical laboratory experiment

- [Distance-learning students who chose to prepare with the OnLabs virtual lab, alongside a tutor-led Skype session, scored better on microscopy Pre-Tests in all three test-difficulty groups](../claims/onlabs-higher-pretest-baseline-all-groups.md) [+W]
- [The face-to-face tutorial equated Post-Test scores at low and medium difficulty, but With-OnLabs students scored higher in the high-difficulty group](../claims/onlabs-posttest-advantage-high-difficulty-only.md) [~W]
- [With-OnLabs students responded positively on an ARCS-based satisfaction questionnaire, with nearly all endorsing the Skype session and a majority wanting it before every lab exercise](../claims/onlabs-skype-satisfaction-arcs-positive.md) [+W]
