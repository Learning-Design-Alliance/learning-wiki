# Train students in autonomous learning skills before or alongside online-based instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "educators and institutions start training students‟ autonomous learning skills" because without them students struggle with study habits and time management needed for online learning. Teachers should act as facilitators who help students discover how their minds work and find strategies that work for themselves, rather than serving as the sole source of knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment and teacher initiative to develop learner autonomy over time
#### Constraints
- Learner autonomy skills are not automatic and need deliberate cultivation, especially in content-laden subject areas accustomed to lecture technique

### Target Learners
- University students transitioning to online or blended learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-directed study habits
- Time management
- Independent source evaluation and synthesis
