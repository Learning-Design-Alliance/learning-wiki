Strategies

Clarify assessment purpose, define skill expectations with rubrics and progressions, and calibrate on student work¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that districts first be clear about what they want to learn from their assessment system, aligning it with their instructional vision before designing it. Second, districts should clearly articulate the meaning of each future-ready skill and develop rubrics, learning progressions, "I can" statements, and classroom Look Fors to communicate expectations. Third, educators should regularly examine student work and calibrate to improve scoring consistency and build capacity.
