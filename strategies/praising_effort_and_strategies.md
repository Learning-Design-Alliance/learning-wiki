# Praising Effort and Strategies

## Description
Praising effort and strategies focuses on acknowledging the process and hard work learners invest in their tasks, rather than praising innate intelligence. By highlighting specific actions, effective strategies, and persistence, instructors can reinforce the idea that effort leads to success and that intelligence is not a fixed trait.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to be mindful of their language and focus on specific actions. Requires a shift from praising intelligence to praising effort.
#### Constraints
- Praise that is not genuine or specific can be ineffective. Over-praising can lead to complacency.

### Target Learners

- All learners, especially children and adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Cultivate a growth mindset, encourage effort, promote effective strategies

### Affordances
- Encourages learners to value effort and hard work. Reinforces the idea that skills can be developed through practice. Helps learners see mistakes as opportunities for learning.

### Personalization
- Feedback can be tailored to individual learners' specific efforts and strategies. Instructors can provide different types of praise based on individual needs and learning styles.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' responses to challenges and setbacks. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward effort. Measure the effectiveness of different praise strategies.

## Impact
- Research shows that praising effort and strategies can lead to increased motivation, improved performance, and a greater willingness to take on challenges. Learners who are praised for their effort are more likely to persist in the face of difficulty.
