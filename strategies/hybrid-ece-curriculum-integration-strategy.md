# Integrate ECE into dental curricula through hybrid models balancing simulation and real patient contact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that curriculum integration of ECE proceed "through hybrid models that balance simulation and real patient contact opportunities" as a feasible path for preparing dental professionals. It further suggests that blends of simulation, early patient contact, peer learning, faculty development, and guided support enable sustainable implementation, and that structured curricular integration with defined outcomes and assessments is necessary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured curriculum integration with defined outcomes and assessments
- Faculty development and guided support
#### Constraints
- RCT and multicentre designs may not be scalable across institutions with separate curricula, per the review's discussion

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical competence
- Confidence
- Patient-centered care
