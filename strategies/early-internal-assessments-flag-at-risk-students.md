# Use early internal course assessments to flag students at risk of underperformance on high-stakes exams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend leveraging the strong relationship between internal exam performance and NBME outcomes for early identification. They write that "early course assessments may be used to flag students who are at risk for underperformance on high-stakes exams", informing tailored remediation plans, resource allocation, and peer or faculty mentoring programs.
