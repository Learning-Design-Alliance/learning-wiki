# Co-design a multi-year vocational physics curriculum in which physics topics explain winery tools and equipment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the positive feedback of students and teachers, the authors recommend expanding context-anchored physics: they are designing learning paths such as mechanics in the winery (winepress), fluid mechanics (hydraulic press, decanting wine, vats' usage) and optics (grape refractometer), "in close collaboration with physics and vocational teachers in the same school", preparing and testing paths spread over two years introducing main physics topics through tools normally used in the winery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Close collaboration between physics teachers and vocational-subject teachers from the initial design stage
#### Constraints
- Developed for a viticulture/enology vocational institute; the authors note the first path showed the need to integrate activities with students' previous knowledge

### Target Learners
- Vocational secondary school students in agriculture and enology

### Target Learning Goals
- Physics topics (mechanics, fluid mechanics, optics, thermodynamics) learned through professional instruments
