# Use portfolio assessment as a complementary alternative alongside traditional timed writing assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article concludes that portfolio assessment "could be used as a complementary alternative along with traditional assessment to shed new light on the process of writing", supplementing one-shot tests with numerous drafts and writing assignments collected in the portfolio. It recommends investigating alternative ways of giving students grades in writing courses and notes that electronic portfolios add real-time interactive feedback requiring a new research agenda.
