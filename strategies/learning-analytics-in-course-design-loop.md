## Description
This strategy comes from the Festschrift paper by Yan, Lin and Kinshuk on self-paced online course learning design, aimed at open-university contexts. The introduction reports that the paper "argues strongly that learning analytics should be part of the “design loop” during course design, and provides a set of recommendations as to design strategies to use". The recommended strategies feature "diagnosis, learner profiling, continuous assessment, and adaptivity to individual differences among learners" prominently. The editors suggest Jim Greer would have endorsed these recommendations.
