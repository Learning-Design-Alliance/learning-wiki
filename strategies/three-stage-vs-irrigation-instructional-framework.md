# Three-stage instructional sequence: theoretical instruction, virtual simulation training, then phantom-head practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a three-stage instructional framework incorporating the virtual simulation system into preclinical curricula: "Theoretical instruction → Virtual simulation training → Phantom-head practice". The sequence moves learners from cognitive grounding through interactive simulation with real-time feedback to hands-on practice on physical models. The authors state this approach "has the potential to strengthen psychomotor integration, enhance procedural fluency, and support competency development in preparation for clinical practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the virtual simulation system on standard computer hardware; standardized theoretical instruction on irrigation prior to simulation training; phantom-head or 3D-printed model practice stations for the third stage
#### Constraints
- The framework is proposed based on a preliminary single-institution, single-group pre-post study; the authors state multi-center studies and long-term follow-up are needed before broader curricular claims

### Target Learners
- preclinical undergraduate dental students
- postgraduate dentistry trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- root canal irrigation procedural competence
- psychomotor integration and procedural fluency in endodontics
