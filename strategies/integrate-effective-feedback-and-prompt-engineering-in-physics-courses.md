# Integrate principles of effective feedback into AI feedback design and incorporate prompt engineering instruction into introductory physics courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students overwhelmingly preferred feedback from structured prompts — particularly those combining prompt-engineering techniques with principles of effective feedback — the article recommends integrating effective-feedback principles into the design and delivery of AI-generated feedback and teaching prompt engineering in introductory physics courses. The article states its implications include "integrating the principles of effective feedback in design and delivery of feedback through AI systems, and incorporating prompt engineering in introductory physics courses". This operationalizes the Feed Up/Feed Back/Feed Forward components within AI prompt templates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prompt templates must embed foundational techniques (instructions, clarity, role assignment, delimiters) together with explicit effective-feedback components
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one extra-credit assignment in a single large-enrollment course; students used AI platforms of their own choice

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students in large-enrollment courses

### Target Learning Goals
- prompt engineering competency
- obtaining and using actionable AI feedback on physics arguments

### Affordances
- Three Component Effective Feedback Framework
