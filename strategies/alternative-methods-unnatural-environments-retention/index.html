Employ alternative vocabulary teaching methods in unnatural foreign language environments to ensure retention efficiency¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article concludes that in foreign language settings without possibilities of social practice, vocabulary is easily forgotten, so "alternative methods should be employed in vocabulary teaching or learning in unnatural foreign language so as to ensure efficiency of retention". It notes these methods can be implemented collectively in a class by a teacher or individually by the learner himself.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Recognition that the learning environment lacks natural social practice opportunities
  • Willingness to adopt alternative methods beyond conventional vocabulary teaching

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one small experimental study with descriptive percentage analysis and no inferential statistics

Target Learners¶

  • foreign language learners in unnatural (non-immersion) environments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • long-term retention of foreign language vocabulary

Claims¶

  • Vocabulary knowledge measured after audiovisual instruction remained stable between week one and week two, with changes restricted to one or two words [+W]
  • Learners taught French vocabulary with an audiovisual method (listening to video pronunciation while following the written script) achieved higher learning success rates in week one than learners taught with the conventional method [+W]
  • Learners taught with the audiovisual method showed higher vocabulary retention in week two than learners taught with the conventional method [+W]
  • Control-group participants showed declining retention success between week one and week two, with 13 of 14 participants declining [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Combine implicit and explicit word instruction, ensure repeated exposures, and use interesting topics and delayed-feedback review to support vocabulary learning
  • Use authentic pedagogical video materials (e.g., TV5 Monde applications) as the basis for audiovisual vocabulary lessons
  • Use online audiovisual excerpts to introduce new vocabulary in an authentic target-language context, followed by transcript-based identification and communicative practice
