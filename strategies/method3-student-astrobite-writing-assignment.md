# Student writing assignment: authoring an Astrobites-style summary with peer editing and optional guest-post publication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Method 3, "designed with upper-level undergraduate or graduate classes in mind," has students write their own Astrobites-like article synthesizing one or more research papers for a specified audience, within a narrow word count and including 1-2 figures from the source paper with captions in their own words. Drafts are exchanged for peer editing guided by a provided rubric, and final pieces may be submitted to Astrobites as guest posts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The instructor decides "what audience to direct students to write for" and verifies each student's source-article selection
- A peer editing rubric guides student feedback, ideally in in-class review sessions
#### Constraints
- Entry-level students are directed instead to summarize already-summarized articles, since they "will not likely be able to read and summarize articles directly from the scientiﬁc literature on their own"

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate and graduate physics or astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- composition of concise, accessible summaries
- communication to audiences of different training or experience
- interpreting data visualizations
