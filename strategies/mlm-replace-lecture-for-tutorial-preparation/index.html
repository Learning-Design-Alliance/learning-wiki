Replace traditional textbook reading and lecture with multimedia learning modules as pre-class preparation for lecture-based tutorials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends using on-line MLMs as a replacement for the lecture in an inverted classroom strategy, particularly at small- to mid-sized institutions without graduate teaching assistants and no defined recitation sections. The author states that "even if learning gains had remained unchanged, the results would still suggest that MLMs are an effective replacement for the lecture in an inverted classroom strategy", since the MLM groups achieved their results using less in-class time.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Student preparation and buy-in are important for realizing significant gains

Constraints¶

  • Different populations can respond very differently to reformed instruction based on scientific reasoning ability, motivation, major, and general academic preparation

Target Learners¶

  • natural science majors in introductory physics at small- to mid-sized institutions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • preparation for in-class tutorial activities in electricity and magnetism

Claims¶

  • Students completing multimedia learning modules before tutorials achieve significantly higher normalized learning gains on the CSEM than students preparing via textbook reading and lecture [+W]
  • Students completing multimedia learning modules score between 8 and 9% higher on mid-term examinations covering electrostatics and electromagnetism than non-MLM students [+W]
  • MLM students report more positive perceptions of instructor clarity, class atmosphere, and effective use of class time than non-MLM students despite identical instructors and in-class activities [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Flip the classroom by assigning pre-recorded multimedia lectures rather than textbook readings as the pre-class material
