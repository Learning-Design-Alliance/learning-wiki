# Connect Writing to Real-World Applications

## Description
Instructors connect the importance and usefulness of writing to learners' lives and future careers. By demonstrating how writing skills are essential in various professions and fields, learners understand the relevance of developing effective writing skills. This may involve inviting professionals to speak to learners about the role writing plays in their lives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples of real-world writing, guest speakers from various professions, structured activities to connect writing to career applications. Requires awareness of learners' career interests.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to find relevant examples or guest speakers for all career interests. Learners may still struggle to see the relevance if the connections are not made explicit.

### Target Learners

- All learner levels, particularly effective for older learners considering career pathways

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance motivation, improve understanding of writing relevance, connect learning to real-world applications, foster career readiness

### Affordances
- Enhances motivation by demonstrating the real-world relevance of writing skills. Connects learning to career aspirations. Provides practical insights from professionals in various fields.

### Personalization
- Examples and guest speakers can be tailored to the learners' specific career interests. Writing tasks can be designed to simulate real-world writing scenarios in their chosen field. Instructors can provide individualized guidance on how to apply writing skills to their future careers.

## Assessment Evidence
- Increased learner motivation and engagement in writing tasks. Improved understanding of the real-world relevance of writing skills. Enhanced ability to apply writing skills to career-related scenarios.

## Impact
- Conveying the importance and usefulness of writing effectively helps learners understand that writing well plays a significant role in their success, regardless of their chosen profession. This approach increases motivation and engagement in writing tasks.
