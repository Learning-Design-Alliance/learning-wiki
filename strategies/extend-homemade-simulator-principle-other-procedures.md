# Extend the low-cost homemade simulator principle to other basic, highly structuralized, standardized surgical procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend applying the same low-cost homemade simulator principle to other procedures to enhance resident education. They conclude that their homemade simulator of CVCD for TIVAP implantation "is a cost-effective way of teaching first-year surgical residents" and state: "We believe the same principle can also be applied to other basic, highly structuralized, and standardized surgical procedures, such as pediatric hernia repairs, in the future to enhance resident education." The strategy rests on modular, affordable construction and standardized procedural steps.
