# Adopt blended teaching strategies that combine face-to-face and virtual-world modes.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests that shaping a sense of in-betweenness becomes an essential competency for future teaching in the post-Great Online Transition age, making online technology-based blended teaching regular. Lecturers in the study 'borrowed' strategies between environments, e.g., establishing specific objectives and tasks in Second Life and clarifying students' doubts, mirroring face-to-face practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need strong foundation skills to facilitate a smooth transition between different environments.
#### Constraints
- The article notes sporadic attempts to introduce technology had not produced substantial change to traditional methods.
