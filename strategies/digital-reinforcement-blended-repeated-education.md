# Blend repeated in-person education with digital reinforcement tools such as quizzes, video reviews and mobile reminders

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes extending repeated education through digital learning tools, noting that "blended learning approaches combining in-person education with mobile- or web-based reinforcement can enhance accessibility and retention". It suggests "offering post-session quizzes, short video reviews or mobile-based reminders may help extend the benefits of repeated education without requiring additional face-to-face time", and that "personalised learning analytics could further tailor the content to individual knowledge gaps".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In-person repeated education sessions already in place, to which digital reinforcement is added
#### Constraints
- The article cites supporting studies [19, 25] but does not test digital reinforcement itself in this study

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses
- nurses in high-demand clinical settings

### Target Learning Goals
- extended retention of infection control knowledge
- sustained behavioural adherence between sessions
