# Use SMS as a low-requirement support tool in learning processes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using SMS messaging as a support tool alongside regular instruction, because SMS requires minimal technology and is easy to apply, reaching large masses. The conclusion states that "SMS support can be used as a support tool in learning process", given that the SMS content was also available to control students in written form. It is implementable by sending course-aligned information messages on a schedule across the teaching period.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mobile phone infrastructure able to deliver SMS to all students, with delivery confirmation
#### Constraints
- The article's evidence comes from one seven-week university programming course with 40 students

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- supporting academic achievement in technical course content
