# Incorporate Crafts to Remember Positive Self-Talk

## Description
Using creative activities to reinforce positive self-talk, such as making crafts with positive affirmations written on them. These crafts serve as visual reminders and provide learners with a tangible tool to use when they need extra support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Craft supplies and instructions. Time for creative activity.
#### Constraints
- May not appeal to all learners. Effectiveness depends on learners' engagement with the craft and the meaning they attach to it.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Learners can choose crafts and affirmations that resonate with them. Crafts can be tailored to specific challenges or goals.
