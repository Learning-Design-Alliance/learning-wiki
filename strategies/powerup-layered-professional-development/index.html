Support PowerUp implementation with layered professional development: customized planning, product training, and ongoing data coaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The page describes a multi-part professional development model intended to equip educators to "implement PowerUp with fidelity to deliver successful student outcomes". A customer success team customizes training to school goals and timelines, covering implementation planning, product training, and data reviews and data coaching throughout the year, delivered on-site or live online, supplemented by the self-paced Lexia Academy eLearning platform.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training is customized in partnership with a dedicated customer success team based on each school or district's goals and timelines.

Constraints¶

  • Professional development offerings are provided by the program vendor (Lexia), so implementation support is tied to the commercial product.

Target Learners¶

  • middle and high school teachers of struggling readers
  • school and district literacy staff

Target Learning Goals¶

  • fidelity of program implementation
  • using progress-monitoring data to target instruction

  • Implementation Success Partnership with check-ins and training events for all staff
  • Layered professional learning and support for teachers implementing individualized software
  • Layered professional development for curriculum implementation (webinars, workshops, coaching)
  • Multi-format professional learning for curriculum implementation (on-site, virtual, blended, coaching)
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Assign a dedicated site expert and site-developed professional learning plan when implementing Imagine Language & Literacy
  • Offer optional virtual or on-site professional development tailored to administrators and educators
  • Support district implementations of adaptive reading programs with a milestone-based implementation plan and ongoing data-informed coaching
  • Support teachers to customize curriculum for local relevance and cultural responsiveness
  • Multi-layer coach training and support system: coach camp, paired mentoring, ongoing webinars, and coach-the-coach sessions
  • Deliver Getting Ready educator training and coaching through virtual formats
  • Provide multi-year professional development with instructional coaching before and during implementation of a comprehensive reading program
