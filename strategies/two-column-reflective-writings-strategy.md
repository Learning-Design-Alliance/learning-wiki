# Two-column reflective writings with guiding questions in the reflective learning group

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the reflective learning group, learning writings were implemented through two-column writings, chosen for two purposes: "to record either learning content or method" and "to record students‘ learning regarding their reactions and refl ections". Each week prospective teachers reported facts and questions gained about the discussed subject, prompted themselves with questions such as 'What did I see?', 'What did I learn?', 'What did I hear?', and analysed their educational lives to create reports. The researcher also posed structured reflection questions and gave regular positive and negative feedback on reports, lesson plans and concept maps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well organised preparation regarding the course
- Participants need to be knowledgeable about the approach, with expert support
- Regular feedback from the instructor
#### Constraints
- A minority of participants reported initial fears and adaptation difficulties because they had never encountered such an approach before

### Target Learners
- final-year prospective teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective thinking
- understanding one's own learning processes
- instructional planning
