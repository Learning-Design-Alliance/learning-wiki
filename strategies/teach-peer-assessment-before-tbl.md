# Introduce and teach peer assessment before implementing team-based learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because satisfaction with peer evaluation was the lowest-rated attitude dimension and did not improve after TBL, the article recommends that "peer assessment should be introduced and taught prior to implementing TBL", with thorough training to help students understand the feedback process. The authors argue peer assessment is a key TBL component for fair grading and feedback-giving skills in healthcare practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effective implementation requires thorough training to help students understand the feedback process
#### Constraints
- Many students dislike peer assessment, likely due to discomfort or lack of familiarity

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students new to team-based learning

### Target Learning Goals
- giving constructive peer feedback
- evaluating team members' contributions
