# Teach complex mathematics topics such as algebra with differentiation and train teachers via in-service seminars

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article’s Turkish conclusion recommends that concepts students perceive as complex, such as the algebra learning field, be taught with the differentiated teaching approach, and that teachers be made aware of it through in-service training seminars. It also recommends future experimental studies applying or comparing different differentiation techniques, since this study employed only the tiered teaching method.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher professional development on differentiated instruction before classroom application
#### Constraints
- The study was limited in student numbers and examined only the tiered technique in one topic and grade

### Target Learners
- primary and middle school mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- achievement in mathematics topics students find complex, especially algebra
