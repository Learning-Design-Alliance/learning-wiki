## Description
RIGID's evaluation phase extends beyond judging effectiveness to examining how and why outcomes occurred, analyzing instructional processes, learner interactions, and contextual conditions. The article states that "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which augments model outputs by retrieving relevant documents or knowledge, can further support this process by grounding AI-generated interpretations in relevant theoretical frameworks and contextual information." This produces explanations aligned with existing theories and sensitive to context, feeding the next analysis phase.
