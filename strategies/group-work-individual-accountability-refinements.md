# Refine group work by adding individual accountability: individual member work, re-implemented roles and rules, self or peer grading, and separated per-meeting tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
Because the first attempt "could not fully achieve our expectations", the authors plan new strategies for the upcoming year, listing "requesting individual work from each member, reimplement roles and rules for the best practice, let be work individually if like to, self or peer gradings, separated work for each meeting, discuss solutions in each meeting to the given set, separate time to value JEDI rules". These refinements target the mid-semester withdrawals and uneven role rotation reported in the survey data by adding individual accountability and per-meeting structure to the group format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing semester-long in-class group-work setup to refine
- Instructor time to reimplement roles, rules and per-meeting problem sets
#### Constraints
- The article states these refinements are planned, not yet tested; the first attempt did not fully achieve the authors' expectations

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in large introductory physics courses working in groups of 3-4

### Target Learning Goals
- individual accountability within group work
- fairer assessment of individual contributions
- effective use of JEDI ground rules within group meetings
