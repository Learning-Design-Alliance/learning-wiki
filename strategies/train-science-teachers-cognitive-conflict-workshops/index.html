Train science teachers in the cognitive conflict strategy through courses and workshops¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study recommends professional development for science teachers centered on the cognitive conflict strategy: "Holding training courses and workshops for science teachers on cognitive conflict strategy." It further recommends directing teachers to discover, diagnose and treat prevailing misconceptions using modern strategies and pre-diagnostic tests, and setting teacher and student guides for overcoming misconceptions through proper activities and guidance.
