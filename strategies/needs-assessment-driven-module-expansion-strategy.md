# Use a resident needs assessment survey to select e-learning module topics, then expand the module set based on demand and feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors used a Qualtrics needs assessment survey of pediatric residents to identify desired pulmonary topics before building their e-learning modules, choosing the four topics of interest "based on these results". After implementation, they gathered faculty and trainee feedback and planned changes accordingly. They state: "We also plan to create more pediatric pulmonary, case-based, interactive modules on topics of high demand, such as cystic fibrosis and basics of mechanical ventilation."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distribution of a topic-preference survey to the target trainee population before development
- A mechanism for collecting trainee feedback after implementation to guide revisions and new topics
#### Constraints
- The strategy was implemented at a single institution (UCSF and Oakland campus) within a pediatric pulmonology elective

### Target Learners
- pediatric residents
- medical students on pediatric electives

### Target Learning Goals
- standardized exposure to common outpatient pulmonary conditions
- diagnosis and management skills for common respiratory presentations
