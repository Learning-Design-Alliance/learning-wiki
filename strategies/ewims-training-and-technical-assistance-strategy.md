# Implement EWIMS through combined in-person and online training with ongoing technical assistance liaisons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article describes a blended professional development model for implementing EWIMS: "A combination of in-person trainings (regional training, early warning data tool training, and 3 site visits) and online trainings (early warning data tool refresher and Web-Shares) were provided." Technical assistance liaisons provide training and support throughout program implementation, sustaining the team-based early warning process over time.
