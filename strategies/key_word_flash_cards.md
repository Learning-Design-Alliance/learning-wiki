# Key Word Flash Cards

## Description
Create flash cards for the four mathematical operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division). On one side of each card, write the operation and its symbol. On the reverse side, list the key words associated with that operation, providing a reference tool for students to identify the correct operation to use in word problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Index cards, markers, list of mathematical operations and their associated key words.
#### Constraints
- Reliance on key words alone may not be sufficient for solving complex word problems. Students need to understand the context of the problem in addition to identifying key words.

- Elementary and middle school learners (Grades 3-8)

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve understanding of mathematical operations, enhance problem-solving skills, develop vocabulary related to math, reinforce memory of key math concepts

### Affordances
- Provides a visual and tactile tool to help students remember key words for mathematical operations. Promotes active recall and association between operations and key words.

### Personalization
- Allow students to personalize their flash cards with colors, images, or additional notes. Provide different levels of flash cards based on student abilities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe students' use of flash cards to identify key words and operations. Assess students' accuracy in solving word problems using the flash cards as a guide.

## Impact
- Increased problem-solving accuracy: Visual aids like flash cards can improve students' ability to identify the correct operation for solving word problems. Enhanced memory: Active recall and association of key words with operations promote memory and retention.
