# Use just-in-time teaching: pre-class electronic exercises whose responses structure the upcoming class session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
JiTT exercises cover material to be covered in the next class and are submitted electronically a few hours beforehand. Instructors review responses and use them to organize the upcoming session, presenting excerpts at the start of class as the basis for discussion and follow-up group exercises. Exercises often include a reflective question about what remains unclear, encouraging metacognitive reading practices and giving instructors insight into student preconceptions before class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Electronic submission via course management software, web form, or email by a pre-assigned deadline; instructor review of responses before class
#### Constraints
- Information from reading quizzes, an alternative, often comes too late, after students and faculty have moved on to the next topic

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in principles-level economics or physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- class preparation, metacognitive reading practices, surfacing preconceptions
