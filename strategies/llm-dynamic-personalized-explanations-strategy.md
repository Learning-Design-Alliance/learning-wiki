# Use AI chatbots to provide dynamic, personalized explanations that adapt to a student's specific misunderstandings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that medical chatbots can complement static commercial question banks: "they can provide dynamic explanations tailored to a student's specific misunderstandings, they can reformulate explanations when initial clarifications are insufficient, and they can connect concepts across different medical subjects in real-time." This strategy positions LLMs as adaptive explanation engines for exam preparation, with the article's caveat that variable performance across anatomical topics necessitates careful validation of LLM limitations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Chatbots trained on medical content that can generate explanations in real time
#### Constraints
- The article warns that incorrect AI-generated explanations could cause student misconceptions, requiring validation of LLM limitations

### Target Learners
- US medical students preparing for USMLE Step 1

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding of pre-clinical science concepts including gross anatomy
