# Student Reflection (Responsible Decision-Making)

## Description
This strategy focuses on using student reflection to leverage strengths and address challenges related to responsible decision-making, which is the ability to make constructive choices about one’s behavior and social interactions in an ethically responsible way.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of student strengths and challenges, targeted strategies, and student reflection.
#### Constraints
- Requires a supportive classroom environment and careful facilitation to ensure students engage thoughtfully with the reflection prompts.

### Target Learners

- Students who may have strengths and/or challenges in responsible decision-making.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop students' ability to make responsible and ethical decisions by leveraging their strengths and addressing their challenges.

### Affordances
- Helps students become more aware of their decision-making processes, consider the consequences of their actions, and make more ethical choices.

### Personalization
- Strategies are tailored to the individual student's strengths and challenges, using graphic organizers, visual representations, and real-life scenarios.
