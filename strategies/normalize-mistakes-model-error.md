# Normalize mistakes by instructors modeling error, saying I don't know, and inviting questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues discomfort and error are part of learning — "we remember better when we make mistakes" — and that instructors should model tackling the unknown. Tactics include telling students about one's own mistakes (embraced from Data Mishaps Night), letting students catch live-coding errors, proudly saying "I don't know", encouraging iteration with version control, and asking "what questions do people have?" instead of "are there any questions?" to lower intimidation.
