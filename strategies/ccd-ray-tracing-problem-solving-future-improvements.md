# Enrich practical sessions with ray-tracing software and 2D CCD cameras, and tutorials with a problem-solving approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors foresee computers helping students analyze measurements: a ray tracing software could help students understand light propagation through optical devices, and 2D CCD cameras could introduce concepts of numerical photography such as numerical aperture and resolution. For tutorials, a problem-solving approach could supplement traditional exercises, for example giving students a photograph of a known monument and asking them to infer the main properties of the objective lens that took it.
