Combine video-based reflection on one’s own teaching with reappraisal and mindfulness-based strategies to foster professional vision and well-being.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article suggests that training student teachers to use more reappraisal and put more distance between themselves and challenging classroom situations when reflecting on their teaching could help them cope with stress. Combining video-based reflection with reappraisal and mindfulness-based strategies is proposed as "a promising way to foster student teachers’ professional vision, as well as their well-being and stress-resistance".
