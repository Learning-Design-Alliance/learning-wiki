# Solve many problems and explain ideas to others to test understanding

## Description
The article recommends two complementary habits for testing understanding. First, 'Solve as many problems as you can', since problems reveal gaps: when you cannot 'explain the origins of an equation, it means you have to do some more reading and thinking'. Second, 'Talk to other people', because explaining an idea to a friend, colleague, or professor invites questions that force clarification, since 'It is very easy to fool oneself'.
