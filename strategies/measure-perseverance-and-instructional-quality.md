# Develop time-based measures of perseverance, instructional quality, and opportunity to learn

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends renewed effort to develop measures of each model variable, noting that 'measurements of perseverance and of instructional quality are practically nonexistent.' It suggests a general way of measuring opportunity to learn, i.e., actual time available to individual students given pacing, and proposes that perseverance be assessed by observing the amount of time pupils actively engage in learning.
