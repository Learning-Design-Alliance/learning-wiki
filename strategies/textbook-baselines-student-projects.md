# Use undergraduate-level review articles and standard textbooks as baselines for student research projects

## Description
The article recommends that undergraduates entering a research topic build on accessible entry points: it notes that Silbar and Reddy's article about neutron stars for undergraduates, together with the standard compact-star textbooks, "can be used as baselines for student projects on compact stars". Pairing a pedagogical review with authoritative textbooks gives students a scaffolded route into the primary literature.
