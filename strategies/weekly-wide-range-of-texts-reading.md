# Devote weekly intervention time to reading a wide range of texts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Alongside repeated reading of short passages, intervention classes should regularly expose students to varied texts. "Reading a wide range of texts counterbalances the limitations of repeatedly reading the same brief passage by exposing students to a variety of sentence structures and text topics." As students explore wider texts they meet unfamiliar words and syntax and their reading becomes more fluent; the panel suggests devoting some time each week to this activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Choose texts at the higher end of students' instructional reading level
- When possible, choose texts aligned to grade-level content or topics of high interest

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4–9 with reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- General reading fluency across varied sentence structures and topics
