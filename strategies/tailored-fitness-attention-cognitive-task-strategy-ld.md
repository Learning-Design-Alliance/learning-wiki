# Tailor physical activity interventions to individual sustained attention levels by pairing structured physical activity with attention-oriented cognitive tasks for children with LD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a tailored intervention strategy based on individual DAUF sustained attention levels, integrating physical activity with attention-oriented cognitive tasks. It suggests that "implementation of structured physical activities, such as team sports or aerobic exercises, paired with attention-oriented cognitive tasks, can improve both physiological and cognitive outcomes". The strategy targets children with LD, especially those with lower sustained attention, who the authors argue may benefit most.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual assessment of sustained attention levels (e.g., DAUF) to tailor the intervention
#### Constraints
- The article states this is based on cross-sectional predictive findings; causal relationships require methodical manipulation of fitness parameters in future studies

### Target Learners
- elementary school children with learning disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance in Chinese language and mathematics
- sustained attention and executive functions
