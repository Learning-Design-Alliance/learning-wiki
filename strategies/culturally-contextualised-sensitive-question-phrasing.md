# Culturally contextualised phrasing strategies for sensitive OB-GYN history-taking questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The programme replaced direct clinical terminology with locally acceptable phrasings when students take sensitive histories, e.g. asking about menstruation as "When was your last monthly cycle?" and post-coital bleeding as "bleeding after being intimate with your husband". Strategies were based on expert consensus among trainers, SPs and clinical faculty, literature review, and regional hospital practice, and were paired with training students in respectful, empathetic tone and emotional cue display by SPs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scripts written with emotional cues displayed for sensitive questions
- Students trained in respectful and empathetic tone with careful phrasing
- Briefing and debriefing emphasising religious and cultural norms
#### Constraints
- The strategy might vary based on the context of each scenario, and SPs are trained to answer accordingly

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate medical students in OB-GYN clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- culturally sensitive OB-GYN history-taking
- respectful communication about reproductive health
