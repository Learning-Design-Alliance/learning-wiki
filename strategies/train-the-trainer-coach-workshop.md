# Train-the-trainer workshop to build coach competency and self-efficacy before delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because high levels of trainer competency and self-efficacy are acknowledged drivers of implementation success, the process includes a train-the-trainer workshop with coaches on how to deliver the programme to their players. Educational strategies and support materials for coaches, players, caregivers and administrators are developed, and club administrators receive educational activities to build capacity to provide organisational and resource support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coaches who participated in end-user workshops lead the pilot teams
- Support materials meeting coaches' needs
#### Constraints
- Described for the I-PROTECT feasibility testing and season-long implementation

### Target Learners
- youth handball coaches
- club administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- competence and self-efficacy in delivering injury prevention training
