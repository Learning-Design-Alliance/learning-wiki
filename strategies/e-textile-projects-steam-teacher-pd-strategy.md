# Use wearable e-textile project design as a hands-on vehicle for STEAM teacher professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends engaging in-service teachers in designing e-textile STEAM projects during PD so they apply subject-matter knowledge through hands-on making. It states that "Engaging teachers in designing e-textile STEAM projects and allowing them to apply their subject-matter knowledge through PD could be a promising strategy for integrating STEAM more effectively into classrooms." The strategy sequences structured projects (circuits, sensors, Arduino programming) before an open-ended design task, embeds problem-based scenarios and engineering design stages, and closes with a project showcase integrating art, design, and technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to e-textile materials, tools, and facilitator support
- Teachers with interest in STEAM fields, particularly arts and crafts
#### Constraints
- Resource limitations such as time, funding, materials, and suitable learning spaces are the main implementation barrier reported by teachers

### Target Learners
- in-service K-12 science teachers
- early-career or inexperienced teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- STEAM integration
- electricity and circuitry understanding
- design-oriented thinking
- creativity
