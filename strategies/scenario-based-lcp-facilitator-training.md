# Scenario-based learner-centered facilitator training using APA's learner-centered psychological principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention training used scenario-based modules grounded in the APA's 14 learner-centered psychological principles (LCPs), which indicate that learning is social in nature and that social interactions are a key element in learning. The training included a substantial discussion component where intervention facilitators interacted with peers via online forums, fostering an online community of facilitators. Scenarios addressed student fears, course rigor, the online format, organization, time management, and peer interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators must have access to online discussion forums for peer interaction during training
- Training must occur before the start of the course
#### Constraints
- The model should be implementable by any available and committed staff member with a bachelor's degree, not necessarily a teacher

### Target Learners
- On-site facilitators in rural small schools supporting online distance learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Supporting student retention, motivation, and social interaction in online courses
