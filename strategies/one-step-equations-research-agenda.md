# A five-question research agenda for one-step equation learning and instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that researchers investigate five questions arising from the classification: whether Type 1 equations are easier than Type 2 to learn; whether equations without a special feature are easier than those with one; the best way to teach equations involving a decimal, a percentage, and a fraction; how to help students learn equations with the pronumeral on the right side; and why solutions involving a decimal or fraction challenge students.
