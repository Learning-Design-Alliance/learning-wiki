# Mobile Communication for Family Engagement

## Description
Utilizing text messages to inform families about their student’s progress, as well as general tips for supporting children’s learning. Schools experiment to find out which messages are effective for their population. Technology must be adapted to reach family members who do not speak English or have limited reading skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mobile communication platform, updated contact information for families, cultural sensitivity and translation services as needed.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on tailoring messages to the specific needs and demographics of the family. Some families may not have access to mobile devices or may be uncomfortable receiving texts from schools.

### Target Learners

- Schools, teachers, families (including low-income families and families of color)

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve student outcomes by informing and supporting families in their children's education.

### Affordances
- Mobile communication can be particularly effective for engaging families who may face barriers to traditional forms of communication. Texts are convenient and can provide timely information.

### Personalization
- Messages should be tailored for different populations, such as non-native English speakers, families with different cultural backgrounds, and those with students in special education. Focus on the individual student’s progress or contain steps families can take to support their child.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track student outcomes and correlate them with the use of mobile communication. Collect feedback from families to assess the relevance and effectiveness of the messages.

## Impact
- Studies show that texting families with information about their student's progress and tips for supporting learning can lead to improved student outcomes.
