# Provide active semester-long support to keep study groups active

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on reported obstacles (scheduling, non-responsiveness, lack of leadership), the authors recommend active support during the semester. They state "We are improving our system to automatically send reminder emails to improve this issue", suggest asking students to self-identify as leaders in the initial preferences survey, and propose slotting study group time into official course schedules so students can block off that time when signing up for courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An opt-in matching process with a preferences survey that can collect leader self-identification
- Course scheduling structures that can accommodate dedicated study group time slots
#### Constraints
- The article reports this in the context of a remote semester; the authors note further work is required to explore how conclusions hold with in-person instruction

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in large introductory courses, particularly students who struggle to initiate study groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining active, responsive study groups over a semester
