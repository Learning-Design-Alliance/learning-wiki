# Assign pre-session e-learning video viewing before hands-on procedural skills practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Provide learners with a short instructional video available for flexible, repeatable viewing on their own devices before a hands-on practice session, so foundational knowledge is acquired in advance. The article reports that fundus examination skills "were improved through high-dimensional learning and reflection such as application and analysis enabled by having the foundational knowledge through prior viewing of an e-learning video."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-designed video of similar content and images to the in-person instruction it replaces
- Scheduling lead time (48 hours in the study) before the practice session and assessment
#### Constraints
- Shown for one procedural skill (fundus examination) with medical students on simulators; authors call for validation with real patients and other populations

### Target Learners
- medical students
- medical interns

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural clinical examination skills
- diagnostic accuracy
- confidence and motivation
