# Implement SWH with layered supports across pre-, during-, and post-instruction phases plus sustained teacher professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SWH incorporates intervention methods and strategies to give students with disabilities added support across the instructional cycle. Before instruction, teachers plan to connect student ideas to broad topics or 'big ideas'; during instruction, teachers blend individual, small group, and whole class work so students share knowledge and reflect; students keep science notebooks or journals documenting their learning; and after instruction, teachers use multimodal representations for students to express what they learned. Treatment teachers received a week-long summer professional development program plus follow-up sessions and support throughout the school year.
