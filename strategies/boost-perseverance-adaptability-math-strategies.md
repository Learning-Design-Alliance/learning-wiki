# Integrate classroom strategies that boost perseverance and adaptability in math

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers "integrate strategies that boost students' effort and perseverance in facing challenges and adjusting their problem-solving strategies based on learning task characteristics". Suggested activities include pedagogical tasks that inspire students to think outside the box, collaborative tasks cultivating access to multiple and diverse viewpoints, transitions in classroom activities, constructive feedback, and regular reflective practices around mathematical mistakes to build mastery experiences that enhance self-efficacy. School psychologists are urged to design targeted interventions promoting perseverance and adaptability, particularly for students showing low self-efficacy or disengagement in math.
