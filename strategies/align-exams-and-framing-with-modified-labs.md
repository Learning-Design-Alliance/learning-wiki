# Align course assessment with modified labs by modifying semester exams and explicitly framing the long-term value of design work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their pilot experience, the authors recommend that instructors adopting microcontroller labs actively sell the approach to students and align assessment with it. They write that "In future semesters it will be essential to both remind students of the long-term value of engaging in real design work, and also modify semester exams to better incorporate the modifications we make to our labs." This addresses the observed student perception of the labs as tangential extra work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must communicate the long-term value of real design work to students
- Semester exams must be modified to reflect the lab modifications
#### Constraints
- Stems from pilot implementations in two sections; the authors present it as a lesson learned rather than a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- University Physics students in courses with modified, unconventional lab approaches

### Target Learning Goals
- Student motivation and engagement with design-oriented lab work
