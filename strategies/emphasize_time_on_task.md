# Emphasize Time on Task

## Description
Time plus energy equals learning. There is no substitute for time on task. Learning to use one’s time well is critical for all learners. Tutees need help in learning effective time management. Allocating realistic amounts of time means effective learning. How the tutor uses the tutoring session time helps to define time expectations for the tutee and can help them establish the basis for high performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured tutoring sessions, tutor guidance on time management.
#### Constraints
- Requires structured sessions, may not accommodate individual learning styles.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve time management skills, enhance focus and productivity, establish realistic expectations for learning.

### Affordances
- Improves time management, enhances productivity, establishes realistic expectations.

### Personalization
- Communicate to tutees the amount of time they should spend preparing for class. Expect tutees to complete their assignments promptly. Underscore the importance of regular work, steady application, self-pacing, scheduling. Divide tutor sessions into timed segments so as to keep on task. Don’t hesitate to refer students to Learning Strategist to help them with their learning skills.
