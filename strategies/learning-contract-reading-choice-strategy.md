# Use learning contracts in which students choose readings and commit to goals to build responsibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's learning-contract activity had students state specific objectives over a limited period: contracts are "written agreements between students and instructors" determining required assignments. Students chose stories of interest from ten reading passages with score allocation on the teacher's website, read, summarized, gave a personal response, and answered comprehension questions. Students rated contract items highly (e.g., 4.13 and 4.20 for realizing responsibility).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher's website hosting the reading passages with score allocation, and instructor agreement on assignment numbers and types
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one 12-week treatment with Thai EFL undergraduates; the authors recommend broader samples and comparison treatments before generalizing

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension and summary writing
- Responsibility and self-directed learning
