# Narrow the scope of quantum outreach and make quantum technologies relatable to everyday life

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the mixed outcomes, the authors recommend future outreach to 'narrow the scope of their outreach and focus on making quantum technologies more relatable.' They suggest explicitly linking quantum technologies to everyday life, current issues and personal experiences to foster greater interest, and focusing on one or two core concepts to reduce cognitive overload and promote deeper understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Outreach designers must identify one or two core quantum concepts and familiar everyday anchors to link them to
#### Constraints
- The article notes that excessive novelty can hinder engagement, while an appropriate balance between novelty and familiarity enhances learning

### Target Learners
- General public visitors at informal outreach settings such as festivals

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained interest and deeper understanding of quantum science and technologies
