# Use a game-based online course to foster meta competences as preparation for domain-specific practice tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The VirtuIng course used a multiplayer online game (EVE Online) in which students "work in groups to each find a strategic game objective", exercising communication, collaboration, critical thinking, creativity, and information literacy. This strategy prepared bachelor Civil Engineering students for an unfamiliar BIM practice task without prior domain instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multiplayer online game students must learn to operate
- Group work organized around strategic game objectives
- A follow-up practice task where meta competences can be applied
#### Constraints
- Pilot study with only five VirtuIng participants; the small group may have fostered a well-cohesive group unlike larger courses
- The practice task in VirtuIng has the character of a laboratory study without contextual connection to other course activities

### Target Learners
- bachelor students in Civil Engineering
- master students in Architecture

### Target Learning Goals
- communication
- collaboration
- critical thinking
- creativity
- information literacy
