# Integrate the VR camera navigation test into mastery learning programs so trainees practice until reaching the pass/fail standard

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend a mastery learning approach in which, per the article, "all trainees must keep practicing until they reach a predetermined pass/fail level of competence". The validated 70% pass/fail score serves as that standard: trainees use the simulator outside the operating room, review preparation and tools before practicing, and repeat the test until proficient, ensuring basic competency before clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a well-designed test with solid validity evidence and a credible evidence-based pass/fail standard as a pre-requisite for a successful mastery learning program.
#### Constraints
- A fixed number of sessions or training time will not ensure all trainees reach proficiency given individual learning curves; learners who consciously practice without achieving pass/fail scores should undergo a review before continuing psychomotor practice.

### Target Learners
- young surgical assistants and medical students with very limited laparoscopic skills

### Target Learning Goals
- achieving proficiency in laparoscopic camera navigation before entering clinical practice
