# Structured simulation practice session: teacher demonstration, group case practice, individual reporting, and peer-informed revision

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the experimental group's practical training, the session followed a fixed sequence: "The practical operational training was first explained and demonstrated by the training teacher, followed by selecting typical cases, assigning tasks, and allowing the group to freely discuss and practice." After completion, "the learning results were reported one by one, with other members listening to the report, and the training teacher providing guidance. Finally, the group members refer to the results of others to improve their answers." This combines demonstration, collaborative practice, individual reporting, instructor feedback, and peer comparison within 45-minute group sessions of five nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A working group of one head nurse, two senior national ultrasound trainers, and intensive care physicians; consistent instructors and content across conditions; groups of five practicing 45 min
#### Constraints
- Reported as implemented in the experimental arm of a single-hospital study of 40 nurses; the article did not isolate which session components drive the outcome

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses in advanced ultrasound training

### Target Learning Goals
- Integrating ultrasound practical operation skills with theoretical knowledge; image recognition and probe operation
