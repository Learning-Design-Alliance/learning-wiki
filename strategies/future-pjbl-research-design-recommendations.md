# Future PjBL research should use active control groups, random assignment, blinding, validated measures, and report replication details and intervention fidelity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future research overcome the shortcomings it encountered: "the lack of active control groups; the lack of randomly assigned participants; inappropriate blinding of participants, instructors, and evaluators; un-validated measures for the learning outcomes; or the lack of detailed information to replicate the study". It also advises researchers to provide detailed information about the fidelity of the intervention to key features of PjBL and to address the impact of student and teacher prerequisites.
