# Support clinician behaviour-change skill learning with a comprehensive training package beyond a one-off workshop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article attributes its above-typical post-training competence to a comprehensive, intensive training package: an initial two-day workshop with information provision, modelling, role play, self-reflection and feedback, one-day clinical shadowing with real-time troubleshooting, a one-day booster workshop roughly two months later, and at least monthly supervision and coaching. It argues that "a one-off workshop is insufficient to facilitate sustained clinician proficiency", so skill learning must be distributed across multiple evidence-based learning methods over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing supervision, coaching, and feedback following the initial workshop
- Experiential methods (modelling, role play, self-reflection, video feedback) alongside information provision
#### Constraints
- Only modest improvements were observed despite the comprehensive training package, per the article

### Target Learners
- Clinicians learning behaviour change counselling (oncology dietitians)

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained proficiency in motivational interviewing and behaviour change counselling skills
