Four strategic actions to advance SoTL integration in CSD¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on their examination, the authors identify "four primary areas where strategic action might effectively advance the integration of SoTL in CSD": increasing awareness of SoTL and its value, including a disciplinary culture change toward problematizing teaching; supporting those engaged in SoTL across the academic lifespan through funding, professional development, and mentorship; applying SoTL to support students as learners via evidence-based education; and engaging students as SoTL scholars. They propose expanding SoTL-specific programming such as symposia, institutes, and a reinstated conference topic strand.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Dedicated funding from ASHA, CAPCSD, sub-disciplinary groups, institutions, and departments, which is currently absent

Constraints¶

  • The article notes few SoTL studies reflect audiology-based content, with the vast majority focused on speech-language pathology

Target Learners¶

  • speech-language pathologists, audiologists, clinicians, researchers, and instructors across career stages

Target Learning Goals¶

  • evidence-informed pedagogy and disciplinary integration of SoTL in CSD

Claims¶

  • CSD instructors show uneven awareness of disciplinary SoTL supports, with the least awareness of financial supports [+W]
  • Most surveyed CSD instructors engage with SoTL, with two-thirds using it to inform teaching and about one-third conducting studies [+W]
  • Institutional (macro) sources currently provide the highest level of SoTL support in CSD, with programs (meso) providing the fewest [+W]
