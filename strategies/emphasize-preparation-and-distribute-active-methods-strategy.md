# Emphasize pre-session preparation and distribute active methodologies across the curriculum to sustain WSLA effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because pre-test preparation declined across successive WSLA sessions, the authors recommend that "educators proactively emphasize to students the critical importance of thorough preparation before engaging WSLA". They also propose that "a possible solution would be to strategically integrate different active methodologies across the curriculum to mitigate workload differences and avoid overloading." This strategy targets sustained engagement in active-learning courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-session content provision on virtual campus and a pre-test measuring student readiness
#### Constraints
- The additional preparation workload of active methods is a stated limitation; the decline was attributed to increased assignments, workload or burnout over the academic year

### Target Learners
- first-year medical undergraduate students in integrated basic science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained pre-class preparation
- maintaining engagement and performance across a sequence of active learning sessions
