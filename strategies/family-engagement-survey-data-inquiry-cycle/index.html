Use a family engagement survey within a data inquiry cycle to gather family feedback and drive changes to policies and practices¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A specific implementable routine for incorporating family input into decision-making. The brief says leaders can "use a family engagement survey in the context of a data inquiry cycle to gather feedback, make sense of data, and strategize how to make changes to policies and practices driven by family input," citing the Equitable Parent-School Collaboration Research Project (2015) and Williams & Benner (2015).
