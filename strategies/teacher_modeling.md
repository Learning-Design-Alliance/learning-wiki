# Teacher Modeling

## Description
Demonstrating writing strategies and techniques by showing students how you use them yourself. Students will be more willing to try new strategies if they see their instructor applying them. The teacher's own struggle with writing is important for students to see.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher willingness to share their own writing process. Time for modeling in class. A supportive and encouraging classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming. Some students may be intimidated by the teacher's writing skills. Requires careful planning to ensure that modeling is relevant and accessible to students.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All grade levels, particularly beneficial for struggling writers and those who lack confidence

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase student confidence in writing, promote risk-taking and experimentation, model effective writing strategies, demonstrate the writing process, encourage a growth mindset

### Affordances
- Provides a clear and relatable model for students to follow. Reduces anxiety by showing that even experienced writers struggle. Encourages experimentation by demonstrating that writing is a process of trial and error.

### Personalization
- The teacher can model strategies that are relevant to specific student needs. The teacher can share their own personal struggles and successes with writing. Students can be invited to participate in the modeling process.
