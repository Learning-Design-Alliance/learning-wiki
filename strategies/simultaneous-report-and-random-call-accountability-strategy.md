# Promote student accountability for team answers through simultaneous report and individual random call

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' third recommendation is to hold students accountable at team and individual levels: make teams' answers public through "simultaneous report, such as voting with colored cards or electronic devices", whose countdown "provides some element of focus and motivation to the discussion", and promote individual accountability via random call, citing evidence that randomly called students become more comfortable speaking and prepare more. The studied classroom used warm calling after team discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mechanism for simultaneous public reporting of team answers and a random-call procedure, ideally warm calling after team discussion
#### Constraints
- The accountability link is supported by cited literature and the authors' interpretation, not directly manipulated in this study

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in large active-learning team courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement and high-quality team discussion
