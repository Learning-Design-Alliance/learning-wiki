# Visual Reading Aids

## Description
Visual reading aids, such as reading guides, colored overlays, and tracking rulers, are tools designed to make text more accessible and readable. They help learners with visual difficulties, visual tracking problems, or those who struggle to maintain focus while reading. These aids can reduce visual stress, minimize distractions, and improve reading fluency and accuracy. Examples include reading guides that highlight one line of text, colored overlays that change the background color of the page, and tracking rulers that help maintain place while reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reading materials, visual reading aids (reading guides, colored overlays, tracking rulers). For colored overlays, a selection of colors should be available to determine the most effective hue for the individual. These can be implemented in both print and digital reading contexts.
#### Constraints
- Elementary-aged kids learning to read may find it difficult to use until they grasp the concept of word-by-word reading. High school students may be hesitant to use reading guides at school due to social stigma, but may adopt them for homework. The effectiveness of colored overlays depends on selecting the correct color for the individual, which may require a process of trial and error.

<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve reading fluency, reduce visual stress, enhance focus, support visual tracking skills

### Affordances
- Reduces visual distractions, supports visual tracking, increases reading rate, improves focus, and provides a low-tech, accessible solution for reading difficulties. Colored overlays can filter out rogue wavelengths of light that cause visual stress, stabilizing the image of the text.

### Personalization
- Visual reading aids come in various forms (reading guides, colored overlays, tracking rulers) and can be chosen based on individual preferences and needs. Colored overlays can be selected in specific hues and saturations that work best for each individual. Reading guides can be adjusted to highlight different amounts of text.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe improvements in reading speed, accuracy, and fluency. Monitor the frequency of losing place while reading. Collect self-reports from learners regarding their comfort and focus while using the aids. Track the usage and preference for different types of reading aids.

## Impact
- Research indicates that colored overlays can significantly improve reading speed and comprehension over extended periods. Eye Level Reading Rulers are used in approximately 60% of schools in England as an intervention strategy for students with reading difficulties. Studies have shown that visual stress can be remedied with colored overlays or lenses, leading to improved reading experiences.
