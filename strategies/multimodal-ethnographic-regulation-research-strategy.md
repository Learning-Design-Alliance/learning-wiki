# Adopt multi-level, multimodal and observational research designs to study integrated regulation of learning

## Description
The article recommends that future research adopt a multi-level and integrated perspective covering self-, co-, and socially shared regulation, adding SSRL to a literature dominated by SRL. It proposes collecting multimodal data from different channels — for example 'objective physiological and subjective self‐report data' such as 360-degree cameras, electro-dermal measures, heart rate and skin conductance — and triangulating objective and subjective sources. It further recommends ethnographic direct observation of healthcare teams in real-life or simulation settings, noting simulation-based research settings easily incorporate technology and suit analysing complex regulatory phenomena.
