# Use combined wiki and video recording of group presentations while highlighting advantages and making the activity fun

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators use the combined recording of group oral presentations and a class wiki, since the activity can influence students' satisfaction and course recommendation. Teachers should "highlight the advantages and usefulness of the activity" and "try to make the activity fun and, at the same time, to reduce students' stress because this is a barrier to their satisfaction."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Record presentations and upload videos to a wiki accessible to all students, with teacher feedback provided through the wiki.
#### Constraints
- Stress is a barrier to satisfaction, so the activity should be designed to reduce it; evidence comes from first-year undergraduates in an economics degree.

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- oral communication skills

## Claims

- [In a cross-sectional survey, attitude statistically fully mediated the associations of perceived relative advantages and enjoyment with satisfaction](../claims/attitude-full-mediation-advantages-enjoyment-satisfaction.md) [+W]
