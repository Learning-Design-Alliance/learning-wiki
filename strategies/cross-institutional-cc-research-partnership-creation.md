# Create collaborative cross-institutional research partnerships between four-year colleges and under-resourced institutions, facilitated by a professional researcher and a community college instructor

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends collaborative partnerships between 4YCs and under-resourced institutions, facilitated by a professional researcher and a CC instructor, with involvement from 4YC faculty for longevity. The partnership design is described as an iterative process between all parties, promoting equality, agency and partnership status, and research is treated as a job, with students compensated for their participation. The authors note each institution has its own funding opportunities and constraints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student researchers are compensated for their participation, either as an independent study or through paid opportunities
- Involvement from 4YC faculty to support the longevity of the program
#### Constraints
- Each institution has its own unique set of funding opportunities and constraints, and the details of how will be very specific to each institution

### Target Learners
- community college students from under-represented backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement in authentic STEM research
- transfer to four-year colleges and STEM degree completion
