# Normalize graduate training for a broad range of STEM careers with formal courses

## Description
The report recommends that graduate programs in particle physics should normalize training for a broad range of STEM careers with appropriate formal courses, rather than forcing students to resort prematurely to self-teaching or peer learning. Courses should provide strong grounding in particle physics and mathematics but also computation, statistics and instrumentation, aiming to benefit careers in physics, industry or education.
