# Calming Oral Sensory Input

## Description
Employing oral sensory input as a calming strategy involves engaging the mouth to provide sensory experiences that promote self-regulation. Activities include chewing snacks like bagels and fruit leather, using chewy pendants or bracelets, sucking thick smoothies through a straw, and blowing activities like blowing a feather across a table. Chewing provides proprioceptive input, while sucking and blowing can be calming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Oral sensory snacks (e.g., bagels, fruit leather), chewy pendants or bracelets, straws, feathers or pompoms.
#### Constraints
- Some children may have allergies or dietary restrictions. Chewing items should be safe and appropriate for the child's age and abilities.
