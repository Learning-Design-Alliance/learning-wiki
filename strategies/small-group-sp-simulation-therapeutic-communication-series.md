# Use small-group standardized patient simulation series focused on therapeutic communication within psychiatric-mental health nursing courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation recipe drawn from the study's procedures: run a semester-long series of SP scenarios in small groups of five to eight students who rotate so that at least two to three students interview each patient case, with debriefing after each interview including feedback from peers, the facilitator, and the SP. Cases targeted "therapeutic communication, establishing rapport, and eliciting a psychosocial history."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained standardized patients
- Clinical course time for scenarios and debriefing
- Cases covering relevant psychiatric conditions
#### Constraints
- The study used nine scenarios across one semester at one research-intensive university; generalizability is limited

### Target Learners
- pre-licensure Bachelor of Science in Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- therapeutic communication
- rapport building
- eliciting a psychosocial history
