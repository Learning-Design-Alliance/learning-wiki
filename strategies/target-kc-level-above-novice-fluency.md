# Target knowledge analysis at the component level just above where novices have achieved success and fluency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (3 theoretical), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
For course-level knowledge analysis, KLI recommends focusing on the component level at which novice students make errors. The article advises that "within a hierarchy of components, a knowledge analysis for a particular course may focus only on a single level that lies just above the level at which novices have achieved success and fluency." Lower levels (e.g., letter identification or multiplication) can then be treated as atomic under an empirical constraint about the target population.
