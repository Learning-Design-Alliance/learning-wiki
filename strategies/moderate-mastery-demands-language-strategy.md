# Avoid excessively ambitious mastery-goal demands in language learning and prioritize understanding and skill development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For language subjects, the article recommends that parents and teachers foster understanding and personal growth rather than escalate demands. The authors advise that "teachers and parents should avoid excessive demands and focus on fostering interest and personal growth in language learning", because at exceptionally high congruence the positive association between mastery-goal congruence and language performance weakened or reversed. They also suggest professional training can equip parents and teachers with the skills to support this approach.

## Design Implications
