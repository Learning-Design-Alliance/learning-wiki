# Provide templates and publicly posted talk moves to scaffold discussion and lower cognitive load

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The primer recommends giving students time to think and write responses before discussing, using a template or prompt sheet to scaffold thinking. A set of goals and prompts could be posted on the wall and made available on paper or online. Making talk moves visually and publicly accessible helps students develop stronger responses and "lower their cognitive load in these often challenging environments of dialogue with their peers."

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students benefit from time to think and write down responses before discussing in pairs or small or large groups
