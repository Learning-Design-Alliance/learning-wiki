# PEARLS-based structured debriefing after each pathophysiology simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each simulation ends with a debriefing structured on the Promoting Excellence and Reflective Learning in Simulation (PEARLS) script: facilitators first gather initial thoughts and reactions with open-ended questions, guide discussion of what went well and what did not, review key learning points, and conclude with final thoughts. In-depth discussion of medical management is saved for the didactic portion. All six fellows in cases 1 and 2 strongly agreed the debriefing allowed all learners to speak and addressed teamwork and communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A fellow leading the simulation plus faculty facilitators to run the debriefing; roughly 10 minutes of debriefing time within the session flow
#### Constraints
- Teamwork, leadership, and communication were discussed but not formally evaluated, so the curriculum's impact on these skills is unknown

### Target Learners
- pediatric critical care fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- reflecting on case performance
- teamwork and communication during resuscitation
- consolidating pathophysiology learning points
