# Ask the Right Questions

## Description
Before you hit “share,” what questions should you ask? You might share On the Media’s 11 guidelines. Listen to the whole show for more detail, and find additional chapters in the group’s clear and helpful “Breaking News Consumer’s Handbook”. Or consult the Newseum’s popular Believe It Or Not? lesson plan that walks teachers and learners through basic news literacy. Students learn to ask these six “consumer questions” when vetting a story: Who made this? How was this made? Why was this made? When was this made? What is this missing? Where do I go from here?
