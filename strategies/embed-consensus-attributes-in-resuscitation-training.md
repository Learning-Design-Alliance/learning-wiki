# Incorporate the consensus attributes into BLS, ILS, ALS and simulation-based resuscitation education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the expert-validated attribute framework to shape resuscitation education: "Incorporating these attributes into structured programmes such as Basic Life Support (BLS), Immediate Life Support (ILS) and ALS, as well as into simulation-based education, can enhance the realism, relevance and transferability of learning outcomes." It also states the framework "can support the alignment of educational content with expert-defined priorities, particularly with regard to NTSs and team-based performance", noting alignment with ERC Guidelines 2025 emphasis on human factors, team behaviour and cognitive load management.

## Design Implications

- Further empirical validation of the framework prior to operational implementation in assessment or selection

### Target Learning Goals

- Technical resuscitation competencies
