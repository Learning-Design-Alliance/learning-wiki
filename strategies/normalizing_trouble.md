# Normalizing Trouble

## Description
Normalizing struggle involves acknowledging that challenges and failures are a natural part of the learning process and a sign of growth. It shifts the mindset from viewing difficulties as negative to recognizing them as opportunities for improvement. The goal is for learners to accept challenges and failures as a chance to grow and do better. This approach encourages a Learner Mindset by reducing the fear of making mistakes and promoting resilience in the face of setbacks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a supportive classroom environment, explicit communication about the value of struggle, and opportunities for learners to reflect on their learning process.
#### Constraints
- May require a significant shift in mindset for both learners and educators. Requires ongoing effort to reinforce the message that mistakes are a natural part of learning.
