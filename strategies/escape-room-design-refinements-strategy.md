# Refine digital escape rooms with adaptive difficulty, clear feedback and instructions, and collaborative gameplay

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on pilot findings, the authors recommend design refinements for digital escape rooms in medical education: scaffolding and adaptive difficulty to prevent overload, brief tutorials or introductory stages to reduce initial confusion, real-time feedback and transparent scoring rules, optional time extensions or relaxation intervals, pre-game review resources, and collaborative elements such as group puzzles or interdependent roles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Escape room content aligned with students' current knowledge levels, with pre-game resources to refresh relevant concepts
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a single-institution qualitative pilot; the authors note further work is needed on whether preferences vary by learner characteristics

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and motivation while applying theoretical knowledge to clinical problem-solving
