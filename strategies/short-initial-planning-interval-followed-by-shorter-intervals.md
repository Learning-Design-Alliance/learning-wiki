# Optimal planning behavior in CPS: a short planning interval at the beginning followed by even shorter intervals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the interaction effects among the three planning indicators, the article identifies an optimal pattern of planning behavior in complex problem solving: "a short planning interval at the beginning of the process that is followed by even shorter intervals." This pattern combines an early planning phase with continued planning activities distributed across the process, and reflects the finding that the absence of an early planning phase can be partly compensated by continued planning activities later in the process.
