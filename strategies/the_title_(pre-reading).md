# The Title (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Learners are given the title of the reading and asked to form small groups to pool their knowledge about the topic. After the group discussion, each group shares their ideas with the rest of the class. This activity activates prior knowledge, encourages collaborative thinking, and prepares learners for the content they are about to read.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires only the title of the reading and facilitation for group sharing.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the clarity and relevance of the title. Some learners may have limited prior knowledge about the topic.
