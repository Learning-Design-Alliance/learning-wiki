Direct future RALL research toward the directions the review identifies for social robots in language education¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review closes by setting an agenda for subsequent work: "This review concludes with directions for future research on the use of social robots in language education." Researchers planning RALL studies should use these directions to choose targets — the review indicates the field targets "different languages, age groups, and aspects of language" with "different robots and methodologies," so future work should be positioned relative to that spread and to the motivation and social-behavior issues the studies raise.
