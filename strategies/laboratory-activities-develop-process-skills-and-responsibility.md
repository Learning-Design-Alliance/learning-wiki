# Use laboratory teaching approaches that develop scientific process skills and include activities in which students take responsibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's suggestions section recommends applying teaching approaches that develop scientific process skills within the laboratory and preparing activities for that purpose, and improving self-efficacy by "including activities to the learning environment during which students take responsibilities". It further suggests adapting research and inquiry to different lecture contents so students reach information themselves, and encouraging students to research and question rather than receive ready-made knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory environment with activities prepared to develop scientific process skills
- Activities during which students take responsibilities
#### Constraints
- Participants reported the approach is time-consuming and tiring, and accessing reliable resources was challenging

### Target Learners
- pre-service science teachers in university laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific process skills
- laboratory usage self-efficacy
