# Science Through Inquiry

## Description
Engage learners in hands-on, exploratory science activities that encourage questioning, discovery, and testing to develop new understandings. This methodology mirrors the processes used by real scientists, fostering creativity and innovation. Inquiry-based science education promotes a deeper understanding of scientific concepts through direct experience and investigation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to provide materials and resources for hands-on activities. Need time for learners to conduct experiments and investigations. Instructors should be knowledgeable about scientific concepts and processes.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming and require significant planning. May be challenging to assess learning outcomes. Some learners may struggle with the open-ended nature of inquiry-based learning.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, particularly effective for elementary and middle school students, as well as adult learners in science-based programs

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop scientific inquiry skills, foster creativity and innovation, enhance understanding of scientific concepts, promote hands-on learning and exploration

### Affordances
- Provides hands-on learning experiences, fosters creativity and innovation, and enhances understanding of scientific concepts. Inquiry-based science education allows learners to follow the same processes used by real scientists.

### Personalization
- Offer a variety of inquiry-based activities that cater to different interests and skill levels. Provide support and guidance to help learners develop their scientific skills.

## Assessment Evidence
- Evaluate learner scientific inquiry skills and critical thinking. Assess their ability to design and conduct experiments. Observe their collaboration and communication skills. Assess the quality of their proposed solutions.

## Impact
- Research indicates that inquiry-based science education enhances scientific inquiry skills, creativity, and understanding of scientific concepts. Hands-on learning experiences make science more engaging and meaningful.
