Use the multi-rater latent growth curve model when estimating growth from longitudinal multi-rater data¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends its model for researchers analyzing longitudinal data in which multiple raters evaluate psychological or social-emotional constructs. Rather than relying on a single rater, the approach removes unique rater variance at each timepoint and models growth of the shared perception. The authors state the model "shows promise for use by researchers who want to estimate growth based on longitudinal multi-rater data."
