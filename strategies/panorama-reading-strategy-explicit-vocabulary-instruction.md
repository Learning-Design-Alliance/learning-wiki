# Use the PANORAMA reading strategy to structure explicit vocabulary instruction in ESP reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
PANORAMA (Edwards, 1973) is an eight-stage reading strategy — Purpose, Adaptability, Need to pose questions, Overview, Read and relate, Annotate, Memorize, and Assess — that the authors used to deliver explicit vocabulary instruction in the EVI group. It organizes reading into pre-reading, whilst-reading, and post-reading stages, with each stage specifying concrete classroom actions such as posing questions before reading and summarizing or mind-mapping afterwards. In this study it served as the procedural vehicle for teacher-directed, explicit vocabulary teaching in ESP reading lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-led delivery with clear procedures and lesson plans, as the EVI class was “controlled and guided by the lesson plans”
#### Constraints
- The article evaluates PANORAMA only within explicit vocabulary instruction for ESP reading with polytechnic engineering students; no evidence is given for its effectiveness under implicit instruction or in other contexts

### Target Learners
- Non-English department university students in ESP contexts (e.g., Mechatronics engineering students)

### Target Learning Goals
- ESP reading comprehension
- Vocabulary knowledge in L2 content-area reading
