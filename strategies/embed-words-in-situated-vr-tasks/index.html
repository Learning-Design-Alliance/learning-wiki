Embed word learning meaningfully into situated VR tasks with social guidance and repeated exposure¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In the discussion the authors recommend redesigning VR language tasks so the linguistic component is embedded in the activity itself, for example by placing participants in a virtual old Finnish farm where "a character guide the child through a goal-directed task" providing corrective feedback, and allowing use of the assembled objects. They also recommend repeated exposure to the words and longer training periods, noting previous research found "VR learning may take up to four training sessions before being effective for vocabulary learning". They further suggest future studies manipulate cognitive load relative to engagement, for example by shorter sessions or letting learners set the pace.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The language element must be embedded in the task rather than presented after it

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • middle-school children learning L2 vocabulary in school settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • L2 vocabulary acquisition through embodied, situated learning

Affordances¶

  • Embodied Grounded L2 Word Learning Account

Claims¶

  • PC-based passive viewing yielded higher word recall than embodied VR assembly training in school-aged children [-W]
  • The PC-over-VR recall difference was significant only for children with higher sustained attention [~W]
  • Sustained attention, not learning condition, was the strongest predictor of children's word recall [~W]
  • VR behavioural measures (word gaze, assembly time, rotation) did not predict word learning outcomes [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed situated, context-triggered language learning activities in learners' everyday environments
  • Deliver neurocognitive training at low frequency within ecologically valid academic contexts rather than as isolated high-frequency sessions
