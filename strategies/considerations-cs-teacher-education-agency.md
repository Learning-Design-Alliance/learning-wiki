# Address teacher agency development in computer science teacher education programmes

## Description
The paper "provides considerations for computer science teacher education", recommending that teacher education attend to how teachers achieve agency when enacting a new subject. Because teachers' agentic decisions shape learners' agency, preparing teachers for the realities of teaching LCCS should include supporting their agentic processes within school figurations.

## Design Implications

### Context
