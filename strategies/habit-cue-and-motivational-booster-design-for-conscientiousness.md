# Design conscientiousness interventions around habit building with explicit contextual cues, and complement them with motivational boosters to maintain intention-habit alignment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on the finding that all reviewed interventions included habit building, the review recommends that future interventions (i) highlight the importance of contextual cues for developing and enacting habitual behaviors and prompt people to actively search for appropriate contextual cues to associate with the target behavior, and (ii) complement interventions with "motivational boosters" ensuring participants remain motivated to engage long term in conscientious behaviors, maintaining alignment between original intention and the established habit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must include habit-building activities in which repeated practice of target behaviors is paired with contextual cues so the cue generates the action impulse
#### Constraints
- The review notes habit formation may be neither necessary nor sufficient to sustain long-term behavior change, because habit-generated impulses compete with other impulses

### Target Learners
- general adults
- students

### Target Learning Goals
- forming and maintaining conscientiousness-supporting habits and behaviors
