Government furnishes secondary schools with computers for sculpture study and runs computer appreciation and programming seminars for sculpture teachers¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The article recommends two implementable actions to enhance sculpture education: equipping secondary schools with computers for sculpture study, and building teacher capacity. It states government "should organize seminars and workshops for sculpture teachers on computer appreciation and programming to help them function effectively in the classroom situation." These address the article's noted shortages of computers, software and maintenance in Nigerian schools.
