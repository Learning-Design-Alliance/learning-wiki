# Recruit project-team members on interest and prior project or research experience rather than specific technical skill sets, and onboard members from diverse technical backgrounds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PULSE-A's recruiting prioritizes interest and prior project experience over existing skills. The paper reports that "recruiting for particular skill sets can be time consuming and relatively unproductive" because the needed skills exceed most students' prior exposure, so "our recruiting focuses mainly on interest and prior experience in other projects or research work, whether technically relevant or not." The team also onboards students from diverse STEM and non-STEM backgrounds so members can learn from one another in a culture of collaborative, self-driven learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applicants are encouraged to participate in other organization projects and to self-study the skills for a chosen subsystem before applying
- An environment where members can continue to learn from one another
#### Constraints
- Recruiting for particular skill sets was found time consuming and relatively unproductive in this team's context

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students from STEM and non-STEM programs joining a student engineering team

### Target Learning Goals
- Develop capable, self-sufficient learners through individual project work
- Foster a culture of collaborative and self-driven learning
