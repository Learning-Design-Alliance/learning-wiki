# Future PA–stress–AP studies should use validated measurement tools and sample during truly stressful periods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's first and third future-direction recommendations. It calls for objective PA measurement where feasible, validated self-report tools like the IPAQ instead of single items, comprehensive stress assessment including objective measures like cortisol, and sampling that captures real stress: "If stress is a variable of interest, it should be measured towards the end of a semester (i.e., just before the examination period)". This addresses the methodological weaknesses the review found in included studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must measure PA, stress, and AP simultaneously with validated instruments
#### Constraints
- Objective PA measurement may be infeasible for large samples, in which case validated self-report tools like the IPAQ are recommended

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance under examination stress
