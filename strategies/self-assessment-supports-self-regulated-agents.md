# Self-assessment as a formative assessment practice that supports students to act as self-regulated agents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief presents self-assessment as the formative assessment practice supporting the agency feature: students interpret evidence of their own learning and make informed decisions about next steps. Teachers support this by co-constructing success criteria, providing protocols to interpret evidence and document self-assessments, offering different options to demonstrate learning, and curating classroom resources students can use independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need a clear understanding of the learning goals and success criteria when self-assessing so they can engage in determining their learning status.
#### Constraints
- Students may need teacher support initially, particularly when they are in the early stages of developing English language proficiency.

### Target Learners
- English learners, including students at early stages of English language proficiency

### Target Learning Goals
- Determining one's own learning status and making informed decisions about next steps in learning
