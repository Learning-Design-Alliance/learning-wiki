# Combine dialogued expository classes, laboratory practicals, guided discussion groups, seminars and debates in the Statistics discipline

## Description
The discipline's stated methodology systematizes students' knowledge of the topics and expands theoretical references through a mix of formats: dialogued expository classes, laboratory practical classes, discussion groups guided by roteiros, follow-up expository classes for clarification and final conclusion of the theme, seminar presentations and debates. The article lists "grupos de discussão norteados por rotei ros" and "apresentação de seminário e debates" among its teaching formats.
