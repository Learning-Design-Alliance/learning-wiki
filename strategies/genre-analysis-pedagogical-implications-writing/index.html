Pedagogical implications: teach writing through situational-cultural context, form-meaning association, and discourse community awareness¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

From its findings the article derives five pedagogical implications for genre-based literacy teaching: equipping learners to construct or comprehend meaning in its situational and cultural context; considering the association between form and meaning with suitable markers; using multiple ways for learners to comprehend and construct meaning; having learners consider the discourse community, highlighting the collaborative nature of literacy; and facilitating sub-specific literacy skills. As printed, the first is "Equipping the learners to construct or comprehend the meaning in its situational and cultural context resorting the principles of genre analysis".
