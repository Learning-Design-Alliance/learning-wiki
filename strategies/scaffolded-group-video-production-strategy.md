# Add a scaffolded group video-production project to conventional lectures to build health education competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention adds to identical theoretical instruction a team-based science popularisation video project structured by Kolb's four stages, with a special lecture on health communication, a mid-term targeted guidance session, and final structured evaluation. Students in groups of 3–5 "self-selected topics related to preventive dentistry" and produced "a 3–5-minute science popularisation video". Students themselves recommended more detailed tool tutorials early on, timely instructor guidance at each stage, and specific feedback after grading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time for guidance sessions and trained single-blind evaluators with a validated rubric; access to user-friendly video editing software
#### Constraints
- Limited student proficiency in video editing and in translating creative ideas into science communication works were the two primary challenges reported; outcomes were assessed only immediately post-course in a single centre

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical and dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge translation
- planning and design of science communication works
- health communication confidence
- professional identity
