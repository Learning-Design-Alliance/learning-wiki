# Implementation Success Partnership with check-ins and training events for all staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Lexia Implementation Success Partnership (ISP) is an implementation support model in which Lexia’s customer success team "held check-ins with school and district leaders, as well as Core5-specific training events for all staff". Support includes implementation planning, product training, and data reviews and data coaching throughout the year, with training events on-site at schools or live online. In the reviewed study, the district participated in this partnership.
