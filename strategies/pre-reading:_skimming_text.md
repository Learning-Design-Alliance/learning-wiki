# Pre-reading: Skimming Text

## Description
Pre-reading involves skimming a text to identify key ideas before reading from start to finish. It typically includes reviewing titles, introductions, summaries, headings, subheadings, study questions, and conclusions to create an overview that increases reading speed and efficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the text and a few minutes to skim through key sections.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for texts that require careful, detailed analysis. Effectiveness depends on the clarity and structure of the text.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Suitable for adult learners and students in higher education who need to quickly grasp the main points of a text.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase reading speed and efficiency, locate key ideas, and provide an overview of the text before detailed reading.

### Affordances
- Helps learners quickly grasp the main ideas of a text, improves comprehension, and supports efficient reading strategies.

### Personalization
- Learners can adjust the depth of their skimming based on their prior knowledge and the complexity of the text.
