Develop challenging mathematics teaching materials that trigger cognitive conflict alongside PBL implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In its recommendation section the article states that "problem-based learning should be developed in the field and can be use as an alternative option for the impleme ntation of learning mathematics" and that teaching materials should be made "in form of more challenging for students and inorder to exist trigger for cognitive conflict". The strategy is therefore to pair PBL implementation with deliberately challenging materials that provoke cognitive conflict.
