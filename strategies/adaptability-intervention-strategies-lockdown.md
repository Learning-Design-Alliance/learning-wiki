# Encourage students to adjust cognition, behavior, and emotion during lockdown learning, and to recognize their own adaptability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors offer preliminary, cross-sectional-grounded practical directions focused on adaptability and self-efficacy, the factors with the largest effect sizes. Educators might encourage students to adjust cognition (reframing new online tasks), behavior (seeking new online resources or asking for assistance), and emotion (minimizing negative feelings), and to recognize when they successfully regulate in the face of disruption to boost self-perceived adaptability.
