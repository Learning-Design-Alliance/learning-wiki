# Include high-fidelity clinical simulation in GBV training for undergraduate nursing students and registered nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend adopting high-fidelity clinical simulation, delivered as simulated video consultations, to train nursing students and registered nurses in GBV victim management. They state: "we recommend including a high-fidelity clinical simulation methodology for training undergraduate nursing students and registered nurses in GBV victim management." The strategy targets the gap between clinical theory and practice in GBV care and allows learning from errors without jeopardizing patient security.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained standardized patients
- Nurse lecturers trained in clinical simulation methodology
- Prior curriculum coverage of GBV so students can research and plan an evidence-based approach in pre-briefing
#### Constraints
- The article calls for more research to further analyze the usefulness of simulated video consultations; findings come from a small-scale qualitative study with limited transferability

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- registered nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- GBV victim management
- early detection of GBV through non-specific symptoms
- non-technical caring skills
