# Educators should assess and plan to enhance food literacy skills in children and adolescents

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that to ensure an adequate level of FNLIT, educators should assess and plan to enhance food literacy skills in children and adolescents. The authors argue that without a complete understanding of food, nutrition, and health, children cannot improve their eating habits and diet quality, and that interventions targeting FNLIT improvement may effectively improve children's eating behaviors, school academic achievement, and weight status. They also call on stakeholders including government, food manufacturers, health providers, educators, and the food industry to play their roles for greater reach and impact.
