A proposed but not yet classroom-evaluated extension: have students write and refine the measurement-application prompt themselves¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors propose a natural extension in which students write and refine the prompt for a measurement application themselves. They argue that deciding which quantities a measurement application should display requires students to identify in advance the relevant variables, the expected functional dependences, and what would constitute a meaningful comparison between measurement channels. This extension has not yet been evaluated in the classroom.
