# Teachers should diagnose students' prompting purposes and provide tailored, scaffolded prompt-engineering instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the three themes and activity-system qualities, the authors recommend that teachers learn students' emergent purposes and conditions for prompting so that "teachers can provide tailored instructions and guidance to facilitate students’ effective prompt engineering". More guidance is suggested for students who have no idea when or how to prompt, and less for students who can articulate clear writing plans for AI elaboration. Teachers are positioned to introduce the tension in a student's activity system that leads to system improvement and language learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need insight into individual students' tendencies, difficulties and strategies in prompting NLG tools
#### Constraints
- The authors note NLG tools providing more structured assistance are especially helpful when teachers have limited knowledge of NLG tools or face constraints in providing tailored guidance

- EFL secondary school students
