# Structure GenAI use guidelines as a scaffold progressing from what GenAI is, through affordances, to consequences and collective responsibilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that institutional GenAI guidelines be broader than academic-integrity rules and be layered to match students' mental models. Guidelines should progress "from awareness of what GenAI is and how it works, through its educational affordances and productivity implications, to its long-term consequences and the collective responsibilities it entails." This scaffolded design meets students at their current understanding while expanding it toward integrated, consequence-aware thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guidelines must address not only permitted use but why responsible use matters at technical, educational, ethical, and societal levels
#### Constraints
- The article notes current guidelines focusing narrowly on plagiarism, disclosure, and permitted use align with only one dimension of student conceptions

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students across mental model clusters

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible and ethical GenAI use; integrated consequence-aware reasoning about GenAI
