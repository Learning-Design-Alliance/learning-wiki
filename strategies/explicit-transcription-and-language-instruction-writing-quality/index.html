Systematically and explicitly teach transcription skills, vocabulary and oral language to support beginning writers' writing quality¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Drawing on the study's findings together with prior causal evidence, the article recommends that writing instruction for young children systematically address the skills supporting the writing process. It states that "high-quality instruction that supports development of children's transcription skills" would help automate transcription, allowing quality writing, and that the independent contribution of vocabulary suggests quality instruction on vocabulary and oral language, with strategies for attentional control and working memory such as planning using graphic organizers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instruction should attend to transcription skills (spelling, handwriting fluency), oral language, and executive function supports during writing instruction

Constraints¶

  • The article notes inferences on practical implications are limited due to the correlational nature of the present study

Target Learners¶

  • Young beginning writers in primary grades (kindergarten to Grade 2)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Written composition quality
  • Automated transcription (spelling, handwriting fluency)

Claims¶

  • Writing fluency completely mediates the relations of Grade 1 spelling and handwriting fluency to Grade 2 writing quality [+W]
  • Grade 1 vocabulary is directly related to Grade 2 writing quality over and above writing fluency [+W]
  • Kindergarten working memory is indirectly related to Grade 2 writing fluency and quality via Grade 1 spelling, while attentional control shows no direct or indirect relation [+W]
  • Grade 2 writing fluency is moderately and independently related to Grade 2 writing quality among beginning writers [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Assess and target discourse-level oral language and transcription skills to locate and address sources of writing difficulty
