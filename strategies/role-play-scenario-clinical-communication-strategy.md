# Use role-playing tasks and scenario-based exercises to build clinical communication skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends a dynamic teaching approach in which medical students practise clinical communication through role-playing tasks, scenario-based exercises, and similar activities. The authors state these activities help students "establish well-rounded clinical communication skills in preparation for their future clinical practice". One surgeon described instructing two students to act as patient and doctor, with the patient acting emotional to test how the doctor copes with delivering bad news.
