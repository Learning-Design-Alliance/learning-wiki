Offer non-native teachers remedial training sessions combining recognition and production drills via the Grammar/Intonation and Audio-Articulation Models¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that non-native speakers of English, especially teachers, be offered training sessions where they remediate pitch-pattern problems through recognition and production drills. The drills were prepared via the Grammar/Intonation Model (Cauldwell & Hewings, 1996) and Audio-Articulation Model (Demirezen, 2003), delivered as a six-class-hour PowerPoint-based session with audiovisual materials. The article also urges developing "a metacognitive and meta -phonological awareness" of differences between the mother tongue and target language regarding pitch patterns.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A multi-modal method combining auditory and visual aids presented simultaneously in real time, per the article's discussion of speech visualization technology

Constraints¶

  • The six-hour session was insufficient for significant production gains; the article suggests more remedial sessions are needed

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-service and in-service non-native teachers of English

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Remediating recognition and production problems with English pitch patterns and other prosodic features

Claims¶

  • A six-hour remedial training session significantly improved participants' recognition of If-clause pitch patterns (p < .05) [+W]
  • The remedial training did not significantly improve participants' production of If-clause pitch patterns (p = .094) [-W]
  • Turkish pre-service teachers of English showed low baseline competency, with approximately 80% failing to recognize the pitch patterns of If-clauses on the pre-test [+W]
  • Post-test production improved for Type 2 and Type 3 conditionals but decreased by almost 5% for Type 1 sentences [~W]
