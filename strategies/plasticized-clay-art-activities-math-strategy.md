# Use plasticized-clay creative art activities as a medium for developing preschoolers' basic mathematics skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating basic mathematics skill practice into creative art activities using plasticized clay, so children learn "by action, by observation, by classification and comparison with the real objects/things". The strategy positions hands-on molding and shaping as a medium for exhibiting knowledge and building conceptual ideas and positive attitudes toward mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervised sessions in which teachers monitor safe use of the clay
- Activities designed to cover each target skill, three activities per skill
#### Constraints
- Classification skill developed least in this implementation, so additional suitable activities may be needed for that skill

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged 3-4 years

### Target Learning Goals
- basic mathematics skills including observation, categorization, comparison, classification, ordering and measurement
