# Disseminate partnership information through blog posts, videos, and infographics

## Description
As one of its outputs, the LEAD Partnership plans to "Develop dissemination resources (blog posts, videos, infographics) to share information about the partnership, intervention, and relevant research". This makes multi-format public communication an explicit component of the partnership's work rather than an afterthought.
