# Align pre-clinical curriculum design with core TBL strategies supported by curriculum governance frameworks and faculty development programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is for medical schools to "align curriculum design with core TBL strategies supported by curriculum governance frameworks and faculty development programs". The authors present this as the route to "effective and sustainable scaling of TBL across pre-clinical programs", grounded in their synthesis of implementation practices across 37 studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional curriculum governance frameworks supporting TBL implementation
#### Constraints
- Gradual adoption may reflect resource constraints inherent in transitioning fully to a TBL-based curriculum

### Target Learners
- First- and second-year pre-registration medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational and biomedical sciences learning
- Teamwork and collaborative problem-solving
