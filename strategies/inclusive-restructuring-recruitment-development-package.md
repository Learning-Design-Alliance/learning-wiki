# Restructure physics departments so that inclusive policies and norms are packaged with recruiting and developing Black graduate students, postdocs, and professors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper endorses Charles Brown's call for physics departments to restructure so Black students, staff, and faculty are truly valued: restructuring 'should involve establishing policies and  norms that are inclusive of Black physicists, enhance their sense of belonging, and place value on their academic contrib utions', together with 'recruiting and developing more Black graduate students, postdocs, and professors' as one package with inclusion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Decision-makers such as admissions officers and department chairs must feel discomfort, look inward, and admit their decisions are skewed by racism and prejudice rather than blaming a shortage of talented students of color.
#### Constraints
- Recruitment and development in an inclusive environment must be 'a package deal'; the paper also reports faculty are 'far more aware of equity issues specific to white socioeconomically privileged women than of other groups', leaving them deficient on intersectional inequity.

### Target Learners
- Black graduate students, postdocs, and faculty in physics
- physics department members broadly

### Target Learning Goals
- belonging and valued contribution for Black physicists
- recruiting and developing Black physicists at every academic stage
