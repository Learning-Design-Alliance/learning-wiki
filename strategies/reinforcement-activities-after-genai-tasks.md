# Add reinforcement activities such as reflective exercises, spaced repetition, and active recall after technology-supported tasks

## Description
Because human memory is susceptible to natural forgetting, the article recommends that "additional reinforcement activities—such as reflective exercises [26], spaced repetition [27], and active recall techniques—may be necessary to solidify learning over time, regardless of the technology used." This strategy extends instruction beyond initial task completion to consolidate learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reinforcement activities must follow initial task completion, since retention requires instructional approaches beyond it
#### Constraints
- The article proposes this as a direction for future research rather than testing it empirically

### Target Learners
- higher education students using GenAI or search tools

### Target Learning Goals
- long-term retention of learned concepts
