# Develop self-assessment as a personal e-learning activity within hybrid health-science curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adding self-assessment to the hybrid learning environment because students reported difficulty assessing themselves: "Half of our student sample declared that they had difficulties in assessing themselves." Only a few knowledge capsules currently incorporate online self-assessment surveys, so the authors conclude "it seems necessary to develop self-assessment as a personal e-learning activity." They caution that requiring more self-assessment tests may increase students' time-management difficulties.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge capsules and the LMS must support embedded online self-assessment surveys as part of the learning sequence
#### Constraints
- The article notes self-assessment has yet to have a proven effect on students' performance, though it helps students analyse their own work; added tests may increase time-management difficulties

### Target Learners
- undergraduate midwifery and health professions students in blended/hybrid programs

### Target Learning Goals
- self-assessment and self-confidence
- analysing one's own work
- deep approach learning of health profession knowledge
