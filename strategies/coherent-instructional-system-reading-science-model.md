## Description
A research brief and technical appendix examine "the practices and conditions of a coherent instructional system, and where and how it had the most impact," using "a case study from a U.S. school district." The brief offers "a practical model that leaders can adapt in their own schools to help strengthen instruction across classrooms," addressing reading and science improvement together rather than as isolated initiatives.
