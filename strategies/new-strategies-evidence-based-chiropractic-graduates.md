# Chiropractic educators require new strategies to produce graduates who understand and deliver evidence-based health care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes that current chiropractic education is not extinguishing non-evidence-based beliefs, which "remain resilient to change over the 5 years of their education". It calls for new strategies for chiropractic educators so graduates understand and deliver evidence-based care and can integrate into mainstream health care, and recommends longitudinal cohort follow-up and more detailed survey questions on what students understand by 'advice'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must address beliefs that persist despite increasing knowledge across the program
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design limits causal inferences about individual knowledge progression; the article recommends following the same cohort over its educational progression

### Target Learners
- chiropractic students across all five years of pre-professional education

### Target Learning Goals
- evidence-based health care delivery and scope-of-practice understanding
