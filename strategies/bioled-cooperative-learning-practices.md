# Implement cooperative learning through four named practices and a balance of group and individual grades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
BioLEd students experience cooperative learning, whose characteristics include "students working in small groups", shared learning goals, and grades based on both individual and group work. Group-work challenges stem largely from intra-group dynamics, and are addressed via four practices: positive interdependence, face-to-face positive interaction, individual and group accountability, and group processing. A balance of group and individual grades was found to be crucial so students see their grade reflects personal effort regardless of peers' performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effort reports prepared by each student and turned in with select assignments
- Group meetings spaced roughly one-third and two-thirds of the way through the semester
#### Constraints
- Common issues include perceived unequal workload, lack of communication, and one member's irresponsibility delaying the entire group

### Target Learners
- undergraduates working in POI groups of pairs, triples, or up to 6-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- collaboration and group dynamics
- shared responsibility for learning
- individual accountability

### Affordances
- Bioled Two Semester Inquiry Research Model
