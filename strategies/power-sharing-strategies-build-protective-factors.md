# Adopt power-sharing and reciprocity strategies such as mentoring and cooperative learning to strengthen social bonds and build protective factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that prevention strategies rebuild naturally occurring social networks by using approaches grounded in power sharing and reciprocity. As the abstract states, "Strategies based on power sharing and reciprocity, such as mentoring and cooperative learning, are advocated to strengthen these social bonds and to promote the protective factors of support, high expectations, and participation." The strategy treats mentoring and cooperative learning as implementable mechanisms for delivering caring support, high expectations, and meaningful participation to youth, and pairs them with school-community linkages and communitywide collaboration.
