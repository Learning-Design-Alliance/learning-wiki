# Education Calendars

## Description
Education calendars are visual aids designed to keep students, parents, and teachers informed about important dates, events, and activities. By adding design flair to the calendar, it becomes a fun and useful tool that can be displayed in the classroom or shared via email. Organizing information clearly, with each week's focus identified using images, helps students quickly understand what's going on. These calendars enhance communication, ensure that nothing is missed, and support a well-organized educational environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to design tools and information. Implementation involves regular updates and distribution. May require training in design and communication.
#### Constraints
- Poorly designed calendars can be overlooked. Inaccurate or outdated information can cause confusion. Requires attention to detail and consistency.
