# Five-step methodology for introducing smartphone apps into exact-science courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a five-step methodology to involve university students actively during the semester: a) search for smartphone apps that allow calculations; b) once selected, identify the software characteristics; c) select the groups in which the new method will be tested; d) build a bank of problems solved by the course students; and e) apply a survey to students at the end of the courses. The author applied this sequence in three groups across two semester periods in different courses (Introducción a la Física, Estadística Básica, Estadística Inferencial).
