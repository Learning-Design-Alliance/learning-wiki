Use grammar correction symbols cumulatively across practice and editing to connect grammar instruction with real texts¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In X-Word Grammar instruction, students are given tasks on authentic readings that call their attention to a grammatical feature by finding and labeling it with grammar correction symbols. "To assist students in remembering the correction symbols, they are used cumulatively in practice and editing activities." Because the symbols are learned simultaneously with the elements they mark, feedback becomes narrow and specific to error types, and students gradually learn to state grammatical reasons for their answers and to analyze whatever they read. The article suggests this labelling may have made patterns more interactive and discernible for future use.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Symbols must be introduced as elements are presented and practiced, and used in both practice exercises and supplemental readings

Constraints¶

  • The article links this strategy to the X-Word Grammar system's smaller set of simply described rules; effectiveness was examined only in this three-month study with intermediate L2 students

Target Learners¶

  • L2 writers in pre-college or intermediate English programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reducing grammatical error in free writing and building independent grammatical analysis of texts

Affordances¶

  • Xword Grammar Sector Analysis Framework

Claims¶

  • L2 pre-college students taught X-Word Grammar showed larger improvement in verb construct accuracy than students taught traditional grammar, borderline significant [+W]
  • Sentence-pattern accuracy improved more under X-Word Grammar than traditional grammar, but the difference was not statistically significant [~W]
  • X-Word Grammar students improved significantly more on subject-verb agreement, borderline on tense, and not significantly on aspect/verb forms [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Guide reading with targeted questions that direct attention to grammatical, lexical and structural features needed for the writing task
