# Student Presentations: All About Me

## Description
Instructors begin the year having the learners do an 'All About Me' presentation, which involves sharing facts about themselves and including pictures or video. The instructor should also present first, in order to model what they're looking for. This also allows the learners to get to know the instructor on a more personal level. This can be modified to suit any age range or subject.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time needed to prep the presentation, access to presentation tools. This strategy will also require presentation time during class.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be hesitant to share too much about themselves for privacy reasons. Requires time and thought, and can be difficult to execute effectively without proper planning.
