# Use zipper models in topology- or quantum-physics-oriented courses and extend them to entanglement and color confinement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends zipper models as easier-to-handle versions of paper-strip representations for further topological topics: the paper stripe representation has already been extended to entanglement and color confinement, and zippers are especially appropriate for color confinement where three stripes are sometimes used. Implementation in a course may in the future provide more information about the suitability of the haptic representations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Zippers of the same model in different colors so parts can be recombined
#### Constraints
- The article states that classroom implementation is still future work and may only in the future provide information about suitability for supporting the development of quantum physics knowledge

### Target Learners
- Learners in topologically and/or quantum physics-oriented courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding entanglement, color confinement, and topological quantum concepts
