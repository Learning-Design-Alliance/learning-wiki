# Design LGBT competency training around intergroup contact and experiential learning rather than didactic knowledge transmission alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that trainings and continuing education programs move beyond purely didactic knowledge acquisition. It reports that programs "focused on intergroup contact and experiential learning have been shown to be particularly effective", and that educational opportunities providing "hands-on learning and actual contact and dialogue with the group or groups in question" lead to especially positive outcomes for cultural humility, competence, and acceptance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Provide opportunities for hands-on learning and actual contact and dialogue with the group or groups in question, not solely didactic elements of knowledge acquisition.
