# Reduce families' dependence on private education or expand access to high-quality learning resources for lower-income youth

## Description
The article draws a policy implication from its finding that private educational expenditure was the only significant mediator of the income–achievement association. It states that this finding "underscores the need for policies that reduce families' dependence on private education or expand access to high-quality learning resources for lower-income youth." The recommendation targets structural interventions aimed at reducing income-based educational stratification in Korea's institutionalized private education system.
