# Teacher-led networked learning: remixing peer-reviewed Open Source Physics templates and contributing models back to digital libraries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors describe a teacher-led professional development process begun in 2007 of immersing in the NTNU Java Virtual Lab and OSP discussion forums and networked learning with computational physicists, deriving or remixing community models as templates, and submitting customized models to the peer-reviewed OSP library and IOP Physics Education. They report being active contributors since 2009 with 10 of the 550 computer models shared worldwide through the OSP website for free, and encourage other teachers to customize the models and contribute back.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the free Easy Java Simulation toolkit, the OSP and NTNUJAVA communities and their digital libraries
#### Constraints
- OSP template derivation provided, in the authors' words, ‘guaranteed’ scientific validity in their models; submissions are peer-reviewed by Physics Professors

### Target Learners
- Physics teachers and teacher-researchers developing inquiry materials

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher professional development in simulation design and inquiry-based physics teaching
