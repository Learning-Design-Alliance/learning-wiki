# Incorporate structured reflection activities (group reflection and individual reflective journals) into service-learning subjects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies reflection as a practice that enhances service-learning effectiveness. Students "took part in a group reflection to discuss their problems and gains after each service activity, and they wrote individual reflective journals to report their service process and insights." This strategy is grounded in Kolb's experiential learning cycle, where reflective observation leads to transformed understanding of concepts applicable in future situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Service-learning subjects need structured opportunities for both group reflection after each service activity and individual reflective journal writing.
