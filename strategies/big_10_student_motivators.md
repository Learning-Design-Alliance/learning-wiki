# Big 10 Student Motivators

## Description
Ten research-backed strategies that encourage playful learning, innovative thinking, and problem-solving skills while engaging students in core subjects. These motivators include providing choice, fostering collaboration, integrating meaningful talk, encouraging problem solving, inquiry learning, risk-taking, unlocking creativity, developing empathy, teacher-student negotiation, and ensuring adequate recess and brain breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supportive classroom environment, flexible curriculum, resources for various activities and projects, trained educators who can facilitate student-led discussions and collaborations
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift from traditional teaching methods, may need additional training for educators, some students may struggle with the open-ended nature of certain activities

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student engagement and participation in activities. Evaluate the quality of student projects and presentations. Gather feedback from students on their learning experiences. Assess improvements in problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

## Impact
- These strategies prepare students for college, career, and life by developing skills valued by employers and educational institutions. They also promote a positive and supportive learning environment where students feel empowered to take risks and express their creativity.
