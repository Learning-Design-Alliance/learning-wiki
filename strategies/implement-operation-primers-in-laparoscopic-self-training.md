# Implement nodal point operation primers in self-educational simulation-based laparoscopic teaching programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend pairing instructional videos with a key-point primer in self-educational simulator training. Their conclusion states it "should therefor be implemented in self-educational simulation based laparoscopic teaching programs as it is a valuable tool in taking up a procedure". The recommendation rests on the trial's significantly higher composed knot score and shorter knotting times in the primer group.
