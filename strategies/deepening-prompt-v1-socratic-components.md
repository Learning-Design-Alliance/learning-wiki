# Deepening Prompt v1.0: a five-component Socratic tutoring prompt structure for evaluating AI tutors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Deepening Prompt v1.0 is the prompt structure the study designed to make Socratic tutoring principles explicit in the applied experiment. It "consists of five cognitive -control components: backcast thinking control, real -time self -observation, immediate confusion detection, spiral conceptual development, and serendipity elicitation." The prompt was administered to all 11 model conditions under the same topic and learner premise, so tutoring performance could be compared across transfer and non-transfer states.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered with a learner premise in which the learner cannot verbalize what they do not understand, so that confusion detection and strategy switching become measurable
#### Constraints
- The article states Socratic tutoring is only the first application candidate and generalization to other domains remains future work

### Target Learners
- Adult learners in one-on-one Socratic dialogue with an AI tutor

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding through dialogue, e.g. economics concepts such as comparative advantage and opportunity cost
