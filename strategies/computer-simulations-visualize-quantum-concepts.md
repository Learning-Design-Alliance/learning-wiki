# Use computer simulations and software visualizations extensively to build students' visualization of quantum mechanics concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Faculty recommendations and the reviewed literature support extensive use of interactive computer visualizations in quantum mechanics courses. The article records Albert's view that "Computer simulations and software applications of Quantum Mechanical concepts should extensively be used in order to furnish students' visualization of quantum mechanics", and reports Zollman et al. (2001) similarly recommending interactive computer visualizations, hands-on activities, and problem practice in recitation hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
