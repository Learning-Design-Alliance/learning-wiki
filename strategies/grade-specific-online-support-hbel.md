# Provide grade-specific online support and psychological interventions to reduce demographic differences in home-based e-learning behavior

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because junior and senior students showed poorer HBEL behavior and perceived health status predicted HBEL, the article recommends targeted support: "it is necessary for universities to provide them with added online support and resources, such as online internship training and guidance and career fairs", plus "providing timely and targeted psychological interventions to all students during the pandemic". It also recommends investing in educational network construction in less-developed regions to address province-level differences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Universities need awareness of students' grade level, perceived health status, and regional infrastructure to target support appropriately.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation addresses junior and senior students facing practicum, continuing education, and employment concerns, as stated by the authors.

### Target Learners
- junior and senior nursing and midwifery undergraduates
- students with poorer perceived health status

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining home-based e-learning behavior quality across grade levels and health statuses
