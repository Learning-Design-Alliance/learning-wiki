# Questioning Techniques to Address Errors

## Description
Strategic questioning is used to guide students to recognize their errors and correct their thinking, emphasizing conceptual understanding over rote memorization. Questions should focus on the student's reasoning, the reasonableness of the answer, and the underlying concepts. The teacher acts as a facilitator, guiding the student through the process of self-discovery and correction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skilled teachers who can ask effective questions and facilitate meaningful discussions. Base-10 blocks or play money (for math interventions).
#### Constraints
- Requires teachers to be skilled in questioning techniques. May be time-consuming, especially in large classes. Students need to feel safe to respond honestly.

- The types of questions and level of scaffolding can be adapted to meet individual learner needs. Some students may require more direct guidance, while others may benefit from more open-ended questions.
