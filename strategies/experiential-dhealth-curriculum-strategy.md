# Complement traditional teaching with experiential dHealth formats such as hackathons and capstone projects, covering fundamental technologies broadly before AI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because experiential rather than pure knowledge competences predicted EBM practice, the authors recommend that "traditional teaching of conceptual and theoretical knowledge could be complemented by novel and interdisciplinary formats to convey experiential knowledge, such as 'hackathons' or capstone projects". They further advise that "it is advisable to cover fundamental dHealth technologies broadly instead of focusing on AI exclusively", situating AI as an advanced topic after basic data literacy or IT infrastructure topics.
