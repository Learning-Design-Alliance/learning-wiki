Proactively co-plan library space use for inquiry and active learning with teachers and librarians¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the teacher–librarian misalignment on Use of Space and Time, the authors recommend that teachers and librarians "proactively collaborate on how to leverage the library resources, including the physical space." The idea is for the two groups to plan together not only what collections are needed but how the physical library supports inquiry and active learning. The article frames this as an opportunity arising directly from the significant perception gap the survey detected.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Willingness of teachers and librarians to engage in joint planning around library resources and the physical space

Constraints¶

  • Research to date shows teachers often plan individually based on standards and top-down directives

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers and school librarians

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supporting inquiry and active learning through library space and resources

Affordances¶

  • Frs Lf Three Metaconstructs Framework

Claims¶

  • Teachers rated Use of Space and Time significantly higher than school librarians (p < .001), with no significant administrator–teacher or administrator–librarian differences [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Guided Inquiry: immersing students in information seeking as a way of learning
  • Creating a Classroom Library
  • Leverage school-based librarians to create and deliver K-6 CS/CT lessons when classroom integration slows
