# Document formal and informal visual-inspection training methods to isolate high-quality practices as automation reduces learn-by-doing opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors advocate that "documentation of the methods through which both formal and informal training is provided" is needed to better isolate high quality training practices. The rationale is that visual inspection was historically learned by doing, but increasing automation in observational astronomy is decreasing those opportunities, so educators need evidence about the extent and form of training to provide. The article also poses the open question of how educators provide training when opportunities to perform visual inspection are diminishing.
