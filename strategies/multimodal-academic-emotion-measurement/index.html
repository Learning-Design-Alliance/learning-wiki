Combine subjective and objective multimodal measurement (self-report, facial expression, physiological data) to capture academic emotions during learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends measuring learners' academic emotions by combining subjective and objective methods, since most existing studies use only self-reporting. It proposes "a novel method to capture learners' learning behavior in various learning environments by combining multichannel video observation, physiological data, and facial expression data". Facial expression recognition software (e.g., FACET, FaceReader 7) was already used in some included studies, but expressions are only one expressive component of emotion and low-intensity emotions are easily suppressed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Use both subjective and objective measurement methods, combining self-reporting and physiological measurements such as facial expression and EEG

Constraints¶

  • Facial expressions are only one of the expressive components of academic emotions; faces not showing emotions do not rule out an inner emotional experience, and low-intensity emotions are easier to suppress

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students
  • high school students
  • college students
  • graduate students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • accurate measurement of academic emotions during learning
  • understanding how academic emotions change over the learning process

Related Strategies¶

  • Use unobtrusive AI facial expression recognition alongside qualitative interaction coding to study emotion regulation in collaborative learning
  • Use multimodal data channels combining subjective and objective measures to overcome single-channel constraints in SSRL research
  • Triangulate physiological data with other CSCL data streams ( such as video or logfile traces ) to uncover otherwise unidentifiable psychophysiological reactions
  • Use multimodal data and time-sensitive analyses to capture situational motivational processes
  • Supplement self-report questionnaires with process-oriented and online measures of motivation during learning
  • Research agenda: investigate affective dynamics with within-person, longitudinal, and multi-channel methods
  • Adopt integrative, longitudinal, multimodal deep-phenotyping designs combining self-report, behavioural tasks, ecological momentary assessment, and physiological measures
  • Integrate physiological, dialog, video, and self-report data to analyze collaboration at individual and group levels
  • Employ multimodal methods to capture the multifaceted feedback process
