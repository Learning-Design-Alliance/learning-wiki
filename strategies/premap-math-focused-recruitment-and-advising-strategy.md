# Prioritize math success and target recruitment of underrepresented students placing into Pre-Calculus and Calculus I

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' program recommendations: focus recruitment on underrepresented students placing into Math 120 (Pre-Calculus) and Math 124 (Calculus I), the group on which the program seems to have the greatest impact; treat math placement as a significant (though not sole) indicator of success; and make success in math classes a priority for academic advisers. Advising tactics include focus study skills development using math homework and exams, more frequent academic check-ins for students taking math courses, and referral to math study centers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination with structures such as the Office of Minority Affairs and Diversity at the University of Washington
#### Constraints
- Math placement should not be used as the sole indicator of success

### Target Learners
- underrepresented minority freshmen placing into pre-calculus or calculus I

### Target Learning Goals
- STEM retention and degree completion
- success in early math coursework
