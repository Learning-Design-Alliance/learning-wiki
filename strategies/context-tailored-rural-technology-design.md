# Design flexible and adaptive technology interventions tailored to the specific socio-technological context of rural areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future ICT interventions in rural settings be more flexible and adaptive, tailored to the specific socio-technological context, including students' competency levels, socio-cultural differences, and digital literacy. It calls for design opportunities that enhance the practical efficacy of live-streaming paradigms and alleviate local teachers' workload. This follows from observed mismatches between urban-designed content and rural students' foundations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design must account for rural students' foundational knowledge, societal educational awareness, and local teachers' capacity
#### Constraints
- Imbalanced or improperly utilized ICT resources and differences in digital literacy may exacerbate rather than reduce existing inequalities

### Target Learners
- rural high school students in remote China

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of streamed course content
- classroom engagement
- application of learned concepts
