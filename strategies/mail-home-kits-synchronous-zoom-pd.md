# Deliver hands-on teacher PD remotely by mailing equipment kits and streaming demonstrations synchronously

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
When the pandemic forced MIPEP online in June 2020, the program was 'taught synchronously via Zoom' with pre-recorded in-class demonstrations shown as videos, and 'shoe boxes with equipment for low budget demonstrations were assembled and mailed to each teacher'. Master teachers led evening discussions with live-streamed demonstrations and tips on engaging students, keeping the two-week structure while removing labs and facility tours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Synchronous video platform for live classes and discussions
- Mailed kits of low-budget demonstration equipment for each participant
- Pre-recorded demonstration videos to substitute for in-class demonstrations
#### Constraints
- No labs or facility tours could be included in the online format
- The rapid conversion is unlikely to represent the potential of a virtual version of the program

### Target Learners
- in-service high school physics teachers unable to travel to a residential program

### Target Learning Goals
- physics content knowledge
- hands-on engagement with physics demonstrations
