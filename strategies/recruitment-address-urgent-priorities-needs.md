# Address districts' and schools' urgent priorities and needs when recruiting them for impact studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy directs researchers to attend to the urgent priorities and needs of districts and schools during recruitment for impact studies. The brief lists this among the common factors that may influence districts' and schools' ability and willingness to participate in research, so recruitment approaches should engage with what districts currently consider most pressing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers must identify and respond to the urgent priorities and needs of the districts and schools they approach.
