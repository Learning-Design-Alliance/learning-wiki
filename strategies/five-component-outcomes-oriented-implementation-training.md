# Five-component outcomes-oriented implementation training for scaling evidence-based treatment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recipe for taking an EBT to scale: "Scaling up or outcomes-oriented implementation appears best accomplished when training incorporates: 1) practice-based learning, 2) fidelity coaching, 3) clinical assessment and outcomes-oriented treatment, 4) organizational skill-building to address barriers for agencies, and 5) linking clients to trained clinicians via an online provider roster." The NC CTP pilot combined these elements and used the resulting clinician performance and client outcome data to win legislative support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert consultation and coaching to fidelity, which the authors note is a costly clinical investment
- A public roster infrastructure to link clients to trained clinicians
#### Constraints
- The expert consultation needed to address treatment integrity monitoring can be a costly clinical investment for any program

### Target Learners
- Licensed community mental health clinicians and their agencies in underserved regions

### Target Learning Goals
- Delivery of an EBT with model fidelity
- Routine use of standardized assessment and outcomes monitoring
