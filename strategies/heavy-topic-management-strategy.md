# Manage the emotional weight of equity topics by co-facilitating with trusted colleagues, mixing lighter articles with heavier ones, and building stamina through repeated attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends reducing overall sensitivity when sessions centre on heavy topics such as human rights and systemic inequality: "facilitating with trusted colleagues and occasionally mixing lighter articles with heavier ones". Consistent co-facilitators who do not rotate help build participant trust over time, and repeated attendance is reported to help build stamina, counter isolation, and foster accountability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- consistent facilitation rather than a rotating model, in the authors' context
- a long-term commitment, with stamina and tolerance to discomfort
#### Constraints
- Sessions are run without encountering actively harmful remarks so far, but the authors recommend an exception protocol in case one occurs

### Target Learners
- STEM community members and session facilitators

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining engagement with difficult equity and history topics over time
