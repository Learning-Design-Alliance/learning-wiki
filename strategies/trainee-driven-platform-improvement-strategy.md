# Iterate digital simulation platforms using trainee suggestions: add haptic feedback modules, case-discussion content, scoring and login tracking, and a streamlined interface

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distills trainee feedback into concrete platform upgrades. Students asked to "enhance system compatibility and responsiveness, introduce scoring and login features for improved tracking and personalization, as well as streamline the interface to minimize scrolling", and those concerned that online platforms cannot replace hands-on experience "suggested incorporating virtual simulation modules with tactile feedback systems". The authors also plan cloud-based haptic access and richer case-discussion content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open-ended questionnaire feedback from trainees to surface usability and content gaps
#### Constraints
- Tactile training is not yet supported due to cost constraints

### Target Learners
- preclinical dental students using virtual simulation platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- improved engagement tracking, personalization, and clinical skill development in tooth preparation training
