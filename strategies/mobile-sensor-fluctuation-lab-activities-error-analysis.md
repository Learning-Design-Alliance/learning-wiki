# Laboratory activities based on mobile-device sensor fluctuations to teach error analysis and uncertainties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Laboratory activities in which students record the statistical fluctuations of mobile-device sensors, mainly the vertical acceleration, and analyze means, standard deviations, histograms and normality. The article states: "We propose here a set of laboratory activities to teach error analysis and uncertainties in introductory Physics laboratories based on the ﬂuctuations registered by mobile-device sensors." Apps such as Physics Toolbox Suite, Androsensor and PhyPhox register the data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mobile device with built-in sensors and an app able to communicate with them, such as Physics Toolbox Suite, Androsensor or PhyPhox, to "select the relevant sensors" and set "the duration of the time series and the sampling frequency".
- Delayed execution or remote access via wi-fi or browser, so the experimenter avoids touching or pushing the device once the experiment has started.
#### Constraints
- Magnetometer fluctuations, significantly influenced by motors or ferromagnetic material in the vicinity, do not follow normal distributions.
- The authors do not recommend studying pedestrian gait fluctuations as a function of alcohol beverage intake.

### Target Learners
- freshman Engineering and Physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- statistical treatment of measurement errors and uncertainties
- physical meaning of the mean value and standard deviation
- interpretation of histograms and distributions
