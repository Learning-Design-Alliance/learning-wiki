# Concept mapping instruction for reading comprehension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a procedure in which the teacher introduces concept maps through explanation and modeling, has students brainstorm related words around a central concept, and guides them to connect related concepts graphically using color pencils. Students then produce concept maps for short stories individually and share them with classmates. The teacher observes and helps with vocabulary as needed, and students add missed concepts after discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who models concept mapping and guides brainstorming around a central topic
- Short stories or texts for students to map
- Color pencils for categorization and revision
#### Constraints
- In this study the strategy was used with intermediate EFL learners aged 14 to 18 in a language school setting

### Target Learners
- Intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary development
