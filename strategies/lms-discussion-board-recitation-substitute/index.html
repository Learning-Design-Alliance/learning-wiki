Use LMS discussion boards as a low-anxiety alternative to oral recitation for L2 opinion expression¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Replace traditional classroom recitation with asynchronous discussion board activities where students "discuss, give opinion, and argue using the discussion board." Students reported they could compose responses carefully, check grammar before posting, and avoid the fear of real-time criticism that suppresses L2 learners' willingness to express opinions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An LMS with discussion board functionality and instructor moderation

Constraints¶

  • Students still preferred face-to-face dialogue for instruction and consultation

Target Learners¶

  • ESL university students
  • Shy or anxious second language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Expressing opinions and arguments in a second language
  • Participation in academic discussion

Claims¶

  • Students perceive discussion boards as lowering anxiety about expressing opinions in a second language [+W]
  • Anxiety in foreign language classroom learning experiences is linked with oral performance among Japanese EFL learners [+W]
  • Learners perceived face-to-face speaking tasks as more useful than asynchronous online tasks, citing lack of interaction and naturalness [-W]
