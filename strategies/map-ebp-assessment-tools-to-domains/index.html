EBP educators should map assessment tools to targeted EBP domains and develop tools for assessment gaps such as the apply step¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 review), q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that EBP educators "identify specific assessment tools (for formative and summative use) that provide accurate, reliable, and timely evaluation of the EBP education being provided and map these assessment tools to the EBP domains targeted". Where gaps exist, educators may need to develop new tools (e.g. for EBP step 4: apply) and evaluate their psychometric properties. It also recommends exploring innovative approaches such as objective structured clinical examination, standardised patients, and audio-recording in clinics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Any newly developed assessment tools require evaluation of their psychometric properties

Constraints¶

  • Innovative evaluation approaches should balance robustness with feasibility

Target Learners¶

  • Health professionals in EBP educational programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate, reliable evaluation of EBP teaching across all five EBP steps and outcome domains

Claims¶

  • Few EBP intervention studies used high-quality instruments, and no high-quality instrument covers all five EBP steps [+W]
  • Most evaluated EBP educational interventions teach only some of the five EBP steps, with critical appraisal (Step 3) taught most frequently [+W]
  • Instruments show great heterogeneity in measurement quality, with validity or reliability information lacking in many cases [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Train raters systematically and re-establish reliability and validity when applying ANTS in a new population or scenario
