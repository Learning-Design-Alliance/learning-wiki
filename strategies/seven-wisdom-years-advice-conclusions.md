# Seven advice-laden conclusions for remaining productive in the wisdom years

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes with seven pieces of advice distilled from the wisdom-years interviews: "(a) Do not retire, but if you do, retire to something, (b) follow your bliss, (c) work hard, (d) offset aging challenges, (e) be inspired by role models, (f) be a life-long learner, and (g) take heed of the universe conspiring." These conclusions are the article's own forward-looking recommendations for readers drawn from the interview stories.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The advice is grounded in the interview stories of seven highly productive wisdom-years participants rather than in experimental tests of the recommendations.
#### Constraints
- The recommendations derive from a small qualitative sample of exceptionally productive individuals and are not experimentally validated.

### Target Learners
- adults approaching or in retirement
- lifelong learners

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining productivity and engagement in later life
- lifelong learning
