# Use simulation-based education to address shortfalls in paediatric clinical placement opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes SBE as an additional educational strategy where paediatric clinical placements are limited by a lack of suitably qualified paediatric physiotherapy educators. SBE "creates a safe environment where clinical confidence and competence can be developed", allows learning experiences tailored to specific objectives set up on demand, and can guarantee consistent, diverse experiences including clinically uncommon scenarios, promoting more equitable learning for all students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios designed in consultation with university teaching staff and aligned to the unit's clinical domain modules
- Pre-session eLearning preparation by students
#### Constraints
- The article notes SBE carries a high cost, which is often a barrier to its implementation
- Students who receive SBE may overestimate their ability and be less realistic in self-evaluation in a simulated environment

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physiotherapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- Development of competency in paediatric physiotherapy practice prior to registration

## Claims

- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, undergraduate physiotherapy students' self-rated self-efficacy in paediatric assessment and management rose significantly from pre-test to post-test across all surveyed skills after simulation-based education](../claims/sbe-increases-paediatric-physiotherapy-self-efficacy.md) [+W]
- [Students were highly satisfied with SBE as a learning strategy, with 78.6% very satisfied, but rated promotion of self-directed learning lowest](../claims/student-satisfaction-sbe-paediatric-physiotherapy.md) [+W]
- [Clinical educators identify specific clinical skills—documentation, basic safety, manual handling, subjective assessment, and understanding the multidisciplinary team's role—as appropriate for instruction through simulation before placement.](../claims/sbe-suited-prerequisite-clinical-skills.md) [+W]
