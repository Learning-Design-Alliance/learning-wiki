# Allocate simulation groups so CALD students receive peer support while interacting with domestic students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that "simulation facilitators should consider how students are allocated to ensure that students from CALD backgrounds receive support from fellow CALD peers while also creating adequate opportunities for interaction with domestic students". This balances solidarity with inclusive interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- facilitator control over group composition in SBL activities
#### Constraints
- some CALD students experienced difficulties connecting with local students and fault-finding attitudes from domestic peers

### Target Learners
- health professions students from CALD backgrounds
- domestic health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- inclusive participation in SBL
- positive inter-peer interactions
