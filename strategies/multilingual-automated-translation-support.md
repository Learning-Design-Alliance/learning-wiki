# Offer multilingual automated translation in student support systems to close communication gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The paper proposes that the support system "might be also provided in different languages using automated translator, to reduce the gap of communication between students having different mother tongues". This would also provide a sustainable solution for information storage by avoiding duplicating storage of similar questions submitted in different languages. It particularly benefits students without strong English skills, since the online module does not rely on listening skills.
