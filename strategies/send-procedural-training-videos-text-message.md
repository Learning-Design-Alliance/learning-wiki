# Send procedural training videos via text message before practical sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Send short procedural training videos to learners via text message before practical sessions, emphasising that the videos are only for the intervention group and should not be shared with the control group, and that watching the videos will not affect exam or achievement scores. Students should be encouraged to watch each video repeatedly to consolidate theoretical knowledge and improve skills by seeing step-by-step process steps before proceeding to practical applications. Remind learners to review the videos before the OSCE exam to reinforce learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos must be sent before the intervention period so learners can review them repeatedly before practical sessions.
- Learners must be informed that watching the videos will not affect exam and achievement scores to encourage voluntary participation.
#### Constraints
- Adherence to the intervention could not be objectively verified, so intervention fidelity may have varied across participants.
- Students may share information through lectures and social interactions, which is a significant limitation that may affect the study's findings.

### Target Learners
- first-year nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor skills (blood collection, drug administration, dressing care)
