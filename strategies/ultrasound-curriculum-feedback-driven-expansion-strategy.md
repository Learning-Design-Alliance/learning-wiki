# Expand curriculum scope, increase hands-on practice time, and optimize scheduling in response to resident feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on resident feedback, the authors recommend that ultrasound curricula expand training scope, increase hands-on practice time, and optimize schedules. They state the "primary demands for expanding the scope of training, increasing practice time, and optimizing the schedule highlight residents' recognition of the value of ultrasound skills". They further call for widespread, application-oriented training accommodating the requirements of different medical fields, and future work on long-term outcomes and advanced training modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A feedback collection mechanism (structured questionnaire) after the curriculum
#### Constraints
- Feedback was collected from a single Chinese hospital's residents, and the authors state the study focuses primarily on training processes specific to China, limiting generalizability

### Target Learners
- Non-ultrasound-specialty residents in standardized training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Broader organ-system coverage
- Hands-on scanning proficiency
- Efficient integration of ultrasound training with rotation schedules
