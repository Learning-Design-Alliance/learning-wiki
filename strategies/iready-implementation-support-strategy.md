# Implement i-Ready with designated points of contact and Curriculum Associates professional development support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The page describes an implementation model in which no additional staff are required, but implementations do require points of contact to assist with initial set-up and disseminate communications, guided by i-Ready Partners who offer assistance throughout the implementation period and beyond. Curriculum Associates offers research-driven professional development for new, practicing, and advanced users, including six-hour on-site development days and an Online Educator Learning platform with digital courses available at any time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Points of contact for initial set-up and communication dissemination
- Professional development sessions with annual number varying depending on implementation length
#### Constraints
- On-site development days are six hours; session frequency depends on implementation length

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading achievement through individualized online instruction
