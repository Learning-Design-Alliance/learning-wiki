# Prioritize prospective MedDiet–AP studies with validated adult instruments, institutional AP records, and prespecified covariate roles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article sets out three research priorities for this emerging field. First, "prospective studies should assess MedDiet adherence at the beginning of an academic year and AP at its end", repeated over consecutive years with baseline attainment accounted for. Second, studies should make the association their main question, use instruments validated in adults, obtain AP from institutional records rather than self-report, and prespecify each covariate's causal role. Third, candidate mediators (cognitive function, sleep quality, perceived stress) should be measured longitudinally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prospective designs assessing diet at the start and AP at the end of an academic year, accounting for baseline academic attainment
#### Constraints
- The article states the current evidence is not sufficient to inform specific dietary recommendations aimed at improving AP

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance (grades, GPA, entrance scores)
