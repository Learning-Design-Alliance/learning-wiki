# Mindfulness Techniques for Managing Emotional Impulses

## Description
Incorporating mindfulness techniques helps students manage their initial emotional impulses and reactivity, resulting in improved self-regulation skills. It aids in developing the ability to monitor their reactions to new or challenging situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator awareness of self-regulation concepts and strategies. Time for mindfulness and guided practice.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle with mindfulness techniques, limiting its effectiveness.

### Affordances
