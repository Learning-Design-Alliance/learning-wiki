# Require rigorous ERCP training with sufficient case volume and referral of complex cases to experts to reduce post-ERCP adverse events

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that ERCP competence be maintained through ongoing training and adequate procedure volume, with complex cases referred to experts. It states that "Rigorous training and a sufficient case volume along with an adequate system of referral to experts for complex cases are required to reduce the incidence of PEP in practice." Endoscopists are advised to consider whether they have an adequate case volume before performing ERCP and to modify treatment strategies according to their expertise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Endoscopists maintain an adequate case volume to sustain ERCP technical skills
- A referral system to experts is available for complex cases
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a single prospective observational study in six South Korean centers, not a tested training intervention

### Target Learners
- endoscopy trainees
- less experienced endoscopists

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining ERCP technical skill
- reducing post-ERCP adverse events
