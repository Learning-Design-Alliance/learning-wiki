# The Best Part of Me Book

## Description
Have the class create a book in which each student tells one thing they like about themselves. Each student chooses one part of their body that they love and then photographs it. Students write the name of the body part and the reason they love it, promoting self-esteem and positive body image.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials for creating the book, camera for taking photos
#### Constraints
- May be uncomfortable for some students, requires sensitivity to body image issues
