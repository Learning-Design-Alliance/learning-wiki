# Engage a research partner with evaluation expertise when setting targets for research questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide specifies who can serve as the research partner in target-setting work: "The research partner could be staff within the organization who have expertise in evaluation, or an external organization, technical assistance provider, or consultant." The strategy is therefore flexible about the partner's position, requiring only evaluation expertise, and pairs the organization with that partner to set and use targets in evidence-building.
