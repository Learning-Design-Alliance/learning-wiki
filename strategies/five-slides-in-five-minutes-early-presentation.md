# Five-slides-in-five-minutes early presentation to assess and build intern understanding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Early in the internship, interns meet their research mentors, discuss their projects, and present their understanding in a "Five-slides in five-minutes" exercise. The article reports this "helped the research mentors to evaluate initial level and grasp of potential physics work by the student, and in parallel gave the students the space to get familiar with ideas and skills they would learn during the internship". One intern said putting the presentation together was very useful for working through their current understanding into a coherent narrative.
