# Provide cognitive guidance—structured environments and explicit instructional support—to help LLM learners move beyond pattern-matching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the base LLM defaults to a diligent but brittle surface learner, the authors recommend cognitive guidance for LLM-based learners. The article states: "These findings highlight the need for cognitive guidance—such as a structured environment and explicit instructional support—to help LLMs move beyond pattern-matching and develop more robust, human-like deep understanding." This translates the simulation's diagnosis into a design direction for building AI learning systems and for studying learning development over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A diagnosis of emergent learner profiles, e.g., via trap questions and longitudinal tracking
- Structured learning environments with explicit instructional support
#### Constraints
- Derived from simulation with LLM agents rather than human learners

### Target Learners
- LLM-based learner agents and AI systems being optimized for learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Deep, generalizable understanding rather than shallow pattern-matching
