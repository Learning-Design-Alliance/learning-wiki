# Offer in-course assessments so students can test clinical-technical skills before the OSCE

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend accompanying offers to manage training resources efficiently, specifically "in-course assessments in which students can test their clinical-technical skills during their learning process and before the OSCE assessment". They link this to deliberate practice requiring observation and feedback, and note a core prerequisite is students detecting their own weaknesses as a motivational factor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students' detection of their own weaknesses as a motivational factor; observation and feedback opportunities
#### Constraints
- Presented as a recommendation for discussion, not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students preparing for OSCE assessment

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical-technical skills development through deliberate practice and feedback
