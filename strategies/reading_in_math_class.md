# Reading in Math Class

## Description
Have students read articles from sources such as Math Horizons or Martin Gardner's Scientific American columns. When students get a feel for what writing about math can look like, they will be able to do it better themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to articles from relevant sources.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to suitable reading materials and may require guidance in selecting appropriate articles.
