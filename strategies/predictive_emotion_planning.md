# Predictive Emotion Planning

## Description
Learners anticipate emotions linked to upcoming activities. Identifying potential feelings (anxiety, boredom, excitement) allows pre-planning of coping strategies. This builds the emotional regulation process, improving focus and learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a schedule of daily activities and facilitator guidance. Learners need to reflect on their feelings and develop coping plans.
#### Constraints
- May not fully prepare learners for unexpected emotional challenges. Requires ongoing practice to become effective.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Proactively addresses potential emotional challenges, enhances self-regulation, and improves focus. Provides a framework for managing emotional responses.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by allowing learners to self-select activities and strategies. Flexible formats can include written plans, checklists, or discussions.

- Track the use of coping strategies in real-time scenarios. Monitor changes in emotional responses over time. Assess the clarity and relevance of learner-developed plans.

## Impact
- Improved self-regulation: Planning enhances the ability to manage emotions proactively. Enhanced focus: Better emotional management supports improved attention and learning.
