# Health-promotion parenting intervention combining trust-based coaching, motivational video, counseling with parents and home visits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention group received a packaged health-promotion program: the researchers "given a questionnaire and carried coaching relationship of 'trust'" continued with health counseling, watching videos about adolescent motivation, sessions with counseling teachers (BK) and parents, a post-test, and home visits for parents and teens. The non-intervention group received a basic health education video and home visit to anticipate Hawthorne effects. The strategy targets behavior change in parenting over eight months.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with counseling teachers (BK) to select respondents with the same criteria and school permission from education authorities
#### Constraints
- The article notes Hawthorne effects as a threat, addressed by giving the non-intervention group a basic health education video and home visit

### Target Learners
- senior high school students and their parents/families in Jakarta and Depok

### Target Learning Goals
- positive parenting practices to reduce adolescent brawl behavior
