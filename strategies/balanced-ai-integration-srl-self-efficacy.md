# Balance AI integration in SRL so technological advantages are harnessed without undermining student self-efficacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review calls for balanced AI integration in higher education SRL support, "ensuring technological advantages are harnessed without undermining student self-efﬁcacy". It recommends combining human and automated feedback, course structure, and teacher guidance to foster learner autonomy and emotional self-regulation, and urges future research to broaden and deepen investigations into AI's capacity to augment intricate SRL processes and strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course structure, teacher guidance, and a combination of human and automated feedback to foster learner autonomy and emotional self-regulation
#### Constraints
- Students with initially low self-efficacy often struggled to leverage AI tools effectively; lack of digital literacy poses obstacles

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- educators and policymakers

### Target Learning Goals
- self-regulated learning
- learner autonomy
