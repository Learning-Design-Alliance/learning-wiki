# Puppet Shows to Explore Emotions

## Description
Puppet shows can be used to act out different scenarios and explore the emotions of the characters involved. After the show, learners discuss the emotions displayed and how the puppets coped with them, promoting emotional understanding and problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires puppets and a stage or performance area. A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the chosen scenarios. Some learners may be shy about performing or using puppets.
