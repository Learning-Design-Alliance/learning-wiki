# Deliver pre-procedure patient education via an anytime-reviewable smartphone multimedia program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Replace or supplement printed instructions with a short (about 12 minutes) smartphone-accessible multimedia program combining images, sound, and information targeted at patients' concerns, delivered by QR code so patients can review it anytime. The article reports this improved embarrassment, bowel preparation, and satisfaction, and argues multimedia substitutes long textual descriptions with images and motions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Patients must have smartphones; content should be designed by a multidisciplinary team and pilot tested before deployment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one large medical center in Taiwan, so generalization to other settings needs multi-center testing; viewing frequency could not be verified

### Target Learners
- Adult patients preparing for colonoscopy, including older adults who may struggle with printed instructions

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate comprehension of preparation procedures
- Reduced embarrassment and psychological burden
- Higher satisfaction with care
