# Implement targeted interventions to develop nurses' therapeutic communication skills as a route to strengthening spiritual care competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that healthcare institutions carry out initiatives to raise nurses' therapeutic communication skill levels, on the grounds that higher therapeutic communication skills were associated with higher spiritual care competence. It states that "it is recommended that targeted interventions be implemented to develop nurses' communication skills and that similar research be conducted in different healthcare settings." The recommendation rests on correlational evidence, not a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions should target therapeutic communication skills as measured by instruments such as the TCSSN, and be evaluated against spiritual care competence measures
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and correlational from a single institution; the article itself notes findings are limited to the participating nurses and instruments, and recommends replication in different healthcare settings

### Target Learners
- practicing nurses in hospital settings

### Target Learning Goals
- therapeutic communication skills
- spiritual care competence
