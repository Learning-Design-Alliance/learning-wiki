# Use observation, questioning, experimentation, data association, and peer communication activities to develop generic science skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion recommends a set of concrete activities within P2BPS: observation tied to real-world contexts, mutual questioning with peers and teachers, experimenting (planning, designing, carrying out experiments, obtaining and processing data), associating data through classification and inference with worksheets, and communicating results orally and in writing through presentations and reports. The article states that "In P2BPS students can deve lop the ability to communicate orally and in writin g", and that activeness in finding answers arouses curiosity and motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for students to perform scientific-method activities themselves rather than only receiving lecturer presentations
- Worksheets or practice discussions for the data-association activities
- Presentation, report, or performance formats for the communication phase
#### Constraints
- The activities are described as implemented in general physics II at State University of Medan; the discussion's claims about motivation and curiosity are the authors' interpretation citing Septin et al. (2013), not separately tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate science students
- prospective physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- generic science skills
- scientific attitude
- oral and written science communication
