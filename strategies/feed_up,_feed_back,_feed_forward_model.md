# Feed Up, Feed Back, Feed Forward Model

## Description
The Feed Up, Feed Back, Feed Forward Model is a structured approach to providing effective feedback. It involves guiding the learner through three key questions: 1. Feed Up: Where am I going (what are my learning intentions?) 2. Feed Back: How am I going (what does the evidence tell me?) 3. Feed Forward: Where to next (what learning activities should I do to make better progress?) This model encourages reflection and helps learners understand their progress towards learning goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear learning intentions and success criteria, time for discussion and reflection, tools for evidence gathering
#### Constraints
- Requires a well-defined learning environment, may be time-consuming, depends on the learner's ability to self-reflect
