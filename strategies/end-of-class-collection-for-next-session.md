# Collect formative assessment at the end of class to inform the next session when instructors are uncomfortable modifying delivery in real time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For instructors intimidated by changing classroom delivery upon discovering lingering misconceptions, the article suggests collecting formative assessment at the end of a class and using the student feedback to inform instruction at the next class. This gives the instructor more time to contemplate and design a new strategy for refining student understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The instructor must still use the collected feedback to redesign subsequent instruction; the article stresses that without instructor response the process will not reach its full potential.
