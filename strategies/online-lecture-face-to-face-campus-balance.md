# Balance online delivery with maximised face-to-face engagement across a distributed multi-campus programme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For a distributed programme with student mobility across campuses, the article recommends prioritising online delivery for lectures and tutorials while maximising in-person engagement at partner campuses. Where necessary for practical components, "a modular delivery model was adopted", though "only two courses required this modular format, and only for a select set of lab sessions held 2–3 times per week". Short-term teaching staff mobility is an additional solution where feasible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student mobility periods across partner campuses must be accommodated in scheduling
#### Constraints
- National legislation caps online delivery at 60% of course hours and 35% of practical and research activities

### Target Learners
- Master's students studying across multiple partner institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining coherent advanced coursework while enabling mobility and hands-on practical training
