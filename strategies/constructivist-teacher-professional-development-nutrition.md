# Ground teacher professional development for nutrition education in constructivist principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article provided teachers eight hours of weekend professional development on the Michigan Model for Health Education curriculum, aligned with the three principles of constructivism. Teachers "debated and discussed lessons in light of their difficult teaching contexts," peer taught lessons to one another, connected new knowledge to prior practice, and shared and learned from one another. The authors suggest "it may be as important to base interventions aimed at teacher learning on sound theory as it is student learning."
