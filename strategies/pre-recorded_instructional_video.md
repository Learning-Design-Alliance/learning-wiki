# Pre-recorded Instructional Video

## Description
Pre-recording instructional videos in advance is a versatile teaching strategy. The production of video content can range from quick-and-easy to in-depth. There are many ways instructors can benefit from delivering course content in video format, including: students can access and rewatch videos freely, frees up synchronous class time for more interactive learning activities and instructors can reuse and re-edit recorded videos

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recording equipment, editing software, and a platform for hosting and sharing videos (e.g., Canvas, YouTube).
#### Constraints
- Time investment in video creation, technical skills required, and potential lack of real-time interaction.
