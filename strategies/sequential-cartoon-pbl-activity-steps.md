# Structure cartoon-supported PBL activities in sequential problem-solving steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific sequence of steps for implementing cartoon-supported PBL: feeling the problem, defining the problem, creating hypotheses, planning for data collection, data collection, reviewing and analysing data, and generalisation. Each step is scaffolded with specific cartoon-based tasks, such as writing down problems seen in pictures, formulating problems as question sentences, conducting research over four days, and responding to opinion questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cartoons on relevant topics displayed as posters in the classroom
- Students divided into groups before the experiment
- Example resources and keywords provided for data collection
#### Constraints
- Activities were designed by the researchers for the specific learning outcomes of the Science, Technology and Society domain

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade primary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- problem-solving skills
- understanding harms of technology products
