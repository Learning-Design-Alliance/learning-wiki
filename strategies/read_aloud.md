# Read Aloud

## Description
Encouraging learners to read questions and their own writing aloud helps leverage auditory processing strengths. Reading aloud allows learners to discern what good writing "sounds like," improving their comprehension and composition skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for learners to read aloud
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who are uncomfortable reading in front of others
