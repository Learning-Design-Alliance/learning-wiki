Advising strategies for students on academic probation: validation, just-in-time resources, goal setting, and mindset dialogues¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends four advising practices informed by STAR Lite findings: validating each student's stories and intersecting identities without judgment, providing just-in-time guidance and resources throughout the semester, helping with iSMART goal setting, and cultivating growth mindsets through meaning-making dialogues. Advisors should "humanize the advising experience by demonstrating care and compassion for students." These strategies target academic proficiency enhancement for probation students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Advisors trained to affirm intersecting identities and demonstrate care
  • Academic resources readily available throughout the semester

Constraints¶

  • Strategies are informed by findings from a single institution's program and are not generalizable

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate students on academic probation

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic proficiency
  • Return to good academic standing
  • Persistence toward graduation

Claims¶

  • Students completing all or extra STAR Lite components returned to good academic standing at higher rates than comparison students [+W]
  • Student engagement with the STAR Lite program increased across successive cohorts [+W]
  • First-generation STAR Lite participants returned to good academic standing at higher rates than first-generation comparison students [+W]
  • Male STAR Lite participants showed lower return-to-good-standing rates than male comparison students [-W]
  • STAR Lite program participation showed no statistically significant relationship with returning to good academic standing after one semester [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Teach an academic toolbox combining study strategies with self-regulation skills such as goal setting, time management, and anxiety coping techniques
