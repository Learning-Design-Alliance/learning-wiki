# Improve research designs, competence conceptualization, and teaching-method description in future research on teaching for democracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is a research agenda addressing three gaps. The article states: "Our study shows that there is still much to be gained in terms of research designs, the conceptualization of democratic competences, and the description of teaching methods." Researchers designing future intervention studies on democratic education should attend to stronger designs, clearer conceptualization of the knowledge, attitude, skill, and behavior components, and more complete descriptions of the teaching methods used.
