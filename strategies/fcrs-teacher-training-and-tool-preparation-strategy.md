# Teacher training and preparation recommendations for implementing the flipped classroom strategy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From the study's recommendations, teachers should be trained to use FCRS in modern courses, and educational stakeholders should encourage teachers to gain education qualifications exposing them to modern teaching methods that improve students' SRLSs. The article states "Teachers should have training to use FCRS in the teaching of students in modern courses" and notes the literature challenge of untrained teachers. Researchers should prepare needed tools for applying FCR, and teachers who could not create FCRS multimedia could transfer it from several free websites.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers have training to use FCRS
- Needed tools prepared to support assembly processes and learning environments for FCR
#### Constraints
- The literature notes challenges including low-quality video lectures and untrained teachers

### Target Learners
- university students
- postgraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- students' achievement
- students' self-regulated learning skills
