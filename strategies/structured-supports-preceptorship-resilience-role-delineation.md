# Structured supports including enhanced preceptorship, resilience training and clear delineation of educational versus therapeutic roles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends structured interventions to support both graduates and educators, including "enhanced preceptorship, resilience training and clear delineation of educational versus therapeutic roles". It further proposes structured psychological safety training, routine reflective supervision, formalised debriefing pathways, psychologically informed leadership and protected time for supervision, framed against upcoming Victorian WorkSafe Psychological Safety Laws requiring systematic management of psychosocial risks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordinated responses across both education and health service sectors
#### Constraints
- Organisational frameworks must delineate educational versus therapeutic responsibilities without educators assuming a counselling function

### Target Learners
- Early career mental health nurses
- Clinical nurse educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Work readiness and transition to practice
- Wellbeing and psychological safety
- Professional role clarity
