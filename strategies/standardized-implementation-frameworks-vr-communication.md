# Establish standardized implementation frameworks and conduct multi-center longitudinal studies on VR communication training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that realizing VR's full potential requires establishing standardized implementation frameworks and generating further evidence of long-term efficacy. It recommends multi-center longitudinal studies to assess long-term effectiveness across diverse socioeconomic contexts and to inform evidence-based guidelines for curricular integration. This responds to the finding that most included studies lacked long-term follow-up and came from high-income settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-center longitudinal studies assessing long-term effectiveness across diverse socioeconomic contexts
#### Constraints
- The review notes geographic bias, with 6/7 studies from high-income settings, and inability to disaggregate outcomes by discipline in the mixed-profession study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical communication competencies
