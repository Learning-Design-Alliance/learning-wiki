Strategies

Integrate creative drama activities into primary IT teaching, especially for hard-to-grasp topics like programming and algorithm creation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that creative drama outperformed comparison methods, the authors recommend that "creative drama activities, which provide considerable benefits, should be integrated" into the primary IT curriculum, and specifically that "employing creative drama shall enable to receive better learning outcomes" for programming and algorithm creation, which "students hardly grasp". They also suggest applying it to topics such as privacy, security, and ethical and social values.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher preparation: in-service training seminars and workshops so teachers of different branches "experience creative drama collectively"
  • Suitable working environments: "drama rooms in schools must be built to perform creative drama activities" with required tools and materials

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations extend beyond the tested unit to topics the study did not measure; the authors note the study can be recreated for different issues and grade levels

Target Learners¶

  • primary school students
  • pre-service and in-service teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • IT course achievement
  • problem solving and communication skills

Claims¶

  • Creative drama instruction gave the highest pre-test-adjusted post-test achievement of three groups in the IT unit \"Tomorrow's Technology\" among sixth graders, ahead of computer-based instruction and standard curriculum, in a significant ANCOVA group effect with no pairwise tests reported [+M]
  • In the creative drama group, students interacted more intensely, listened more diligently, and showed more harmonious and enthusiastic behavior than in the comparison groups [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use creative drama techniques across different grades and courses to support achievement and affective outcomes
  • Encourage and improve the use of creative drama activities in Social Studies teaching, with extra attention at the 4th-grade level
