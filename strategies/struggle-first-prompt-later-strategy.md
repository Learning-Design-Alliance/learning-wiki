# Require learners to attempt the task and answer pretest questions before granting access to ChatGPT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation recipe: present a problem that cannot be solved with prior instruction, have learners answer pretest questions with no corrective feedback, and only then open access to the GenAI tool with structured step-by-step prompts and comprehension questions answered in the learner's own words. The article states pretesting "encourages them to activate prior knowledge and think critically about potential solutions" before AI assistance, preserving productive struggle in AI-supported learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A task that cannot be solved with prior instruction alone, so pretest questions must be attempted before AI access
#### Constraints
- No corrective information is provided during the pretest attempts; participants who did not answer pretest questions were excluded

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in statistics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- retention and transfer of statistical analysis concepts
- productive struggle before AI-assisted problem solving
