# Threshold-based credit conversion mapping PIS-7 composite scores to engagement tiers and base credit units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
CLICS proposes classifying PLEs into engagement tiers by composite score: "noncreditable (<40), basic (40-59), meaningful (60-74), advanced (75-89), and very high (≥90) engagement tiers, each associated with a proposed base credit unit of 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1, respectively." Final CPD credit equals this unit multiplied by a jurisdiction-specific scale factor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Empirical calibration of thresholds and regulatory approval before any actual credit conversion
#### Constraints
- These thresholds are conceptual pending empirical calibration; tier labels refer only to engagement within a PLE

### Target Learners
- Physicians seeking CME/CPD credit through AI-mediated learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Translating reasoning-related engagement into creditable professional development
