# Educators should flexibly use PBL alone or combined with other methods, matched to teaching content and the learning situation, rather than adopting one mode universally

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that, given diversified teaching modes, teachers "can flexibly and organically use these methods individually or in combination", analysed against the learning situation, to form characteristic teaching methods with high trainee interest and suitable learning effect. It applies this to future cardiac surgery internship teaching, suggesting educators "consider using the PBL teaching method flexibly alone or in combination with other techniques depending on the content."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must analyse the learning situation and select content-appropriate methods
#### Constraints
- The article states the PBL-online model may not fully exploit its potential for obscure cardiac surgery knowledge points because students need more hands-on practice and on-site observation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate clinical medicine students in cardiac surgery internships

### Target Learning Goals
- adapting teaching mode to content and learner situation
- developing creative and application-oriented talents
