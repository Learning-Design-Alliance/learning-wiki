# Provide trained gatekeepers with easy access to referral resource information to support engagement in gatekeeper behaviors.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article notes that "once participants are trained, they must have easy access to information about where to refer the individual they are concerned about, or they may not be as likely to engage in gatekeeper behaviors." To address this concern, online training simulations such as VOC can easily direct participants to the location of campus, institutional, or local resources to link student veterans to needed support services. This strategy connects training completion to practical action by embedding resource information within the training environment.
