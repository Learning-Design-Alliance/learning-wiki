# Research why institutions are drawn to PBL constructs and what socio-political factors sustain neo-colonial uptake

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a way forward, the review proposes research avenues on the drivers of PBL adoption: "future research should analyze why people and institutions are drawn to PBL constructs, what they see as added value of using the term, how they take decisions regarding its adoption and implementation, and what forces guide these decisions." Such work should elucidate the global and local values underpinning curricula and the socio-political factors perpetuating neo-colonialist views and practices.
