# Break VR lectures into short sections with assessment questions and consider inserted break prompts to mitigate cybersickness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors structured their VR lecture into 5-minute sections each followed by an assessment question, allowing users to pause or take breaks. They suggest that "Inserting a prompt suggesting a break within the presentation may be a way to mitigate these negative effects" of cybersickness, since participants could otherwise complete the entire 32-minute lecture without removing the headset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR lecture divided into short sections with assessment questions
- A mechanism for prompting or allowing breaks
#### Constraints
- Longer duration of VR exposure is well known to increase frequency and severity of cybersickness symptoms

### Target Learners
- medical students using VR headsets for lectures

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural knowledge and technique for ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture
