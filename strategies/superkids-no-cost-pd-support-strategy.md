# Provide no-cost initial training, coaching visits, administrator overviews, and year-round support when implementing Superkids

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's implementation model bundles professional development and support at no charge: "Superkids teachers receive initial training, provided as either a half-day session or a 90-minute webinar," each teacher receives one complimentary coach visit, administrators receive a 90-minute session or webinar, and sustained phone and website support runs year-round for all school personnel involved in implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating teachers and administrators must engage with the initial training sessions and remain reachable through the year-round phone and website support.
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through grade 2 teachers and school administrators implementing the program

### Target Learning Goals
- Faithful implementation of the Superkids beginning reading curriculum
