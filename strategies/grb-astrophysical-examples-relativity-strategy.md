# Use astrophysical examples such as Gamma-Ray Bursts to complement idealized special relativity examples

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends bringing current research astrophysics into the relativity classroom. Astrophysical applications "complement idealized examples with real applications and very eﬀectively exemplify the role of a ﬁnite light travel time". Because many astronomical sources have relativistic outflows, the observed effects of special relativity are ubiquitous there, and the GRB variability example highlights the observer-definition issue while giving students a taste of a current research topic.
