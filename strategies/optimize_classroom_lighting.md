# Optimize Classroom Lighting

## Description
Maximize the use of natural lighting in classrooms. Research has shown that artificial lighting has negative impacts on those in schools while natural lighting has positive impacts. Natural lighting boosts the morale of teachers and students, reduces off-task behavior, and improves test scores. If natural light is limited, use full-spectrum artificial lights to simulate natural daylight. Consider installing lighting controls to allow teachers to adjust brightness and reduce glare.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Natural light sources, full-spectrum artificial lights, lighting controls
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to implement in existing buildings with limited windows. Requires careful design to minimize glare and heat gain.
