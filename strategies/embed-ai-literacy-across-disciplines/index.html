Embed AI literacy across disciplines by identifying intersections with existing content and using case studies, interdisciplinary modules, and collaborative projects¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For curriculum design, the chapter recommends a transdisciplinary approach in which institutions identify "points of intersection between AI and existing disciplinary content and developing strategies to seamlessly integrate AI concepts." Concrete tactics include incorporating AI-related case studies in existing courses, developing interdisciplinary modules exploring AI applications in specific fields, and creating collaborative projects applying AI concepts to discipline-specific problems. New AI-focused programs should cover all seven knowledge dimensions and progress from foundational to expert levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional adoption of a transdisciplinary approach to curriculum design

Constraints¶

  • The strategies are recommendations illustrated by examples, not tested interventions

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate and graduate students across academic disciplines

Target Learning Goals¶

  • integrating AI concepts with disciplinary content and balancing technical skills with ethical and sociocultural awareness

Related Strategies¶

  • Support faculty AI literacy through needs assessment, targeted training, teaching resources, and communities of practice
  • Integrate computational thinking into existing disciplinary teaching across grade bands rather than adding it on
  • Invest in dedicated instructional technology leadership roles and role-specific professional learning for AI literacy
  • Integrate AI literacy education as an integral component of first-year curriculum development rather than an add-on
