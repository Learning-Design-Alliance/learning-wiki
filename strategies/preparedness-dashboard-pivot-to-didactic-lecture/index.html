Use a real-time preparedness dashboard to let instructors pivot lesson plans when few students have completed pre-class work¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

CLICKSTREAM's lesson-level dashboard shows the percentage of students who have completed pre-class activities in a learning cycle (reading, comprehension activities, homework, downloads) in time for instructors to adapt. When most learners are prepared, instructors proceed with active, constructive, interactive lesson elements; when preparation is low, "The dashboard could signal this to an instructor in the hours before a lesson, and they could quickly pivot to the less engaging but more adaptive didactic lecturing." This is a step-loop adaptivity application of enriched trace data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Raw data must flow into CLICKSTREAM and be enriched in real time
  • Resources must be tagged with their role in the learning cycle and temporal alignment to the lesson

Constraints¶

  • The article presents this as an opportunity and prototype capability, not an evaluated intervention

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate course instructors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • matching lesson delivery to students' preparedness so students can acquire basic content knowledge
