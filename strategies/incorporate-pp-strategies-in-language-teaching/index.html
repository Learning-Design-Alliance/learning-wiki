Teachers should incorporate PP-based strategies (fostering enjoyment, building resilience) into language teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that PP interventions positively affect language performance and engagement, the review recommends that "teachers should incorporate PP-based strategies (e.g., fostering enjoyment, building resilience) into their teaching practices". It further recommends that teacher training programs be updated to equip educators with the skills to implement PP interventions effectively, and that future research design interventions tailored to specific language skills and psychological dimensions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training programs need to be updated to equip educators with the skills to implement PP interventions effectively.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes cultural factors may significantly influence the effectiveness of PP in language learning, and calls for culturally sensitive approaches.
