# Assess and target multiple achievement emotions, and raise intrinsic value, when supporting primary mathematics students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications for practice recommend that teachers and educational psychologists go beyond single emotions. Because the Mixed profile performed poorly despite moderate emotions, the authors argue "it cannot be assumed that a decrease in boredom alone, for example, would lead to improved mathematics achievement if enjoyment remains low", and that measuring only one emotion may miss at-risk students. They also recommend raising intrinsic value, for example "by, for example, providing choices, using appropriately challenging content, and increasing students' sense of belonging in the classroom", and suggest emotional interventions that build awareness and regulation of achievement emotions.
