# Four-session introductory teacher-training course on WxMaxima and Geogebra with video tutorials

## Description
The author proposes a four-session training course/seminar for secondary teachers covering WxMaxima (linear algebra; analysis and vector calculus) and Geogebra (2D; 3D). It comprises "21 v´ ıdeotutoriales con una duraci´ on de dos horas y media aproximadamente", thirteen videos on WxMaxima and eight on Geogebra, each session listing contents with linked video tutorials. Its goal is to give teachers the basic tools to adapt everyday classroom exercises and implement digital tools for solving and visualizing mathematical content.

- using WxMaxima and Geogebra to solve, visualize and teach secondary mathematics content
