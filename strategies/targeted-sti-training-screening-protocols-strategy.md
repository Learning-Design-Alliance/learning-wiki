# Targeted continuing education, structured sexual health training, and standardized screening protocols to strengthen STI prevention in primary care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends strengthening STI prevention and counseling in Saudi primary care through "targeted continuing education, structured training in sexual health, and standardized screening protocols." This responds to identified gaps in risk assessment and pervasive counseling barriers among surveyed physicians. The recommendation is grounded in the study's own findings on clinical exposure and training rather than a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programs should address the risk assessment gap and the multifaceted sociocultural and structural barriers the survey identified
#### Constraints
- The article notes the effectiveness of training likely depends on its structure, reinforcement, and practical components rather than its mere presence

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians in Saudi Arabia

### Target Learning Goals
- STI risk assessment
- STI prevention counseling
- STI testing practices
