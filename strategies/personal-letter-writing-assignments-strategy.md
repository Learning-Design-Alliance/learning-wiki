# Use personal letter-writing assignments in composition and literature classes because personal letters are expressive and intimate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper describes a strategy of assigning letter writing in college classes for nontraditional students, drawing on an instructor who has developed specific writing assignments for composition and literature classes. Its rationale is that "When personal, letters are expressive and intimate," and that letters are usually short, so the attention span required of students is not long. The paper presents this as viable practice rather than a tested intervention.
