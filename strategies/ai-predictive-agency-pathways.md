# AI-supported choice architectures that build agency while the teacher retains curricular scope

## Description
At point ②, the article proposes predictive models that give students "a selection of recommended next steps for their own personalised learning" while remaining within the teacher-assigned knowledge area; the algorithm builds a decision tree for learning pathways "but leaves the final step of the process, the decision itself, up to the student". Learning analytics built into the system could identify cognitive strengths and weaknesses and nudge students toward their own stated goals, enhancing perceptions of agency and control.
