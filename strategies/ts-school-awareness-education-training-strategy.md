# Raise awareness among health care providers, teachers, and parents of TS-related school challenges and provide education and training to support children with TS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that awareness among health care providers, teachers, and parents of potential school problems and the role of TS and co-occurring conditions would help support the child's education. They state "Additional education and training may be needed for parents, teachers, and peers to recognize tic symptoms and their impact on the child so that they can support children with TS in school," and point to Tourette Association of America resources for parents, teachers, and providers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learning Goals

- supporting school functioning of children with TS
