# Recruit diverse community-based standardized patients who act as credible experts on living with their condition in their community

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends interacting with diverse community standardized patients to reinforce that all patients are credible experts on the complexities of living with their condition in their community. Such standardized patients reflect not only case characteristics but the value systems of their cultural background and social group membership, and offer a unique perspective on student performance not provided by students or instructor evaluations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community- and condition-specific participation of diverse standardized patients; without it, authenticity and condition- and context-specific nuances are lost
#### Constraints
- Multiple individual factors beyond culture, including experiences, mental models, and beliefs, may also influence OSCE performance and its evaluation

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- reflecting on reactions to patient diversity
- communication skills for multicultural clinical encounters
