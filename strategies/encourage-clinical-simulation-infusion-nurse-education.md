# Use clinical simulation scenarios in health institutions to develop and assess nurses' infusion-therapy clinical judgment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that institutions adopt the validated scenarios and simulation-based education for nurses: "The use of clinical simulation should be encouraged in health institutions, in order to assess the impact on the clinical judgment of nurses, as well as the use of scenarios by nurse educators in corporate, continuing and permanent education services." The strategy pairs the scenarios with prior video lessons on the VHP model, pre-briefing and briefing, and formative plus summative debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity simulator, trained simulation support team, facilitator, and prior educational content such as the VHP video lessons delivered before application
#### Constraints
- Pilot evidence comes from a small convenience sample of eight nurses at one university hospital

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses in hospital adult care and infusion therapy units

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical judgment and safe, quality infusion therapy planning and management
