# Teachers should proactively nurture class norms supporting strategic resource use, e.g., by incentivizing and socializing use of the resource

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because intervention benefits were larger in classes where more peers used the Exam Playbook and where instructors offered credit for engaging with it, the authors recommend instructors actively shape supportive class climates. They write: "Teachers could proactively encourage and nurture the psychology of strategic resource use in their courses —such as by incentivizing the use of the Exam Playbook, by encouraging groups of students to work together on the Exam Playbook, or by incorporating self-regulated resource use into their teaching". This treats peer norms and instructor support as levers for translational effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor willingness to offer course credit or class time for resource use, and a platform through which students can access the resource
#### Constraints
- Bonus credit in this study did not influence the exam-performance outcome measure, and the credit-effect association was observational

### Target Learners
- College students in large introductory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated, strategic use of learning resources before exams
