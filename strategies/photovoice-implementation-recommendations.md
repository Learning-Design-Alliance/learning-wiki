# Before Photovoice assignments, integrate ethics training, scaffold teamwork skills, provide standardized consent templates for photographing minors, and align projects with learning outcomes and assessment rubrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article’s conclusion proposes four practical recommendations for implementing Photovoice in educational settings: "(1) integrate ethics training prior to photography assignments; (2) scaffold teamwork skills through structured preparatory activities; (3) provide standardized consent templates for photographing minors; and (4) explicitly align Photovoice projects with defined learning outcomes and assessment rubrics." These respond directly to the ethical and teamwork challenges students reported.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support including preparatory sessions on ethical photography and clear guidance on consent
#### Constraints
- The recommendations respond to challenges observed in a single-university Iranian context; the authors call for further research on ethical and cultural factors

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- ethical photography practice
- collaboration skills
- alignment of activities with learning outcomes
