# Provide 2-hour interactive training with audience-response analysis of participants' own items to improve SCT writing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports a 2-h interactive training session for junior faculty before they write SCTs, structured around a 58-slide presentation covering theoretical foundations, SCT structure, scoring principles, and "interactive analysis using a real-time audience response system (Wooclap®) of participants previously submitted SCTs, highlighting examples of high and low-quality items". This training significantly increased valid SCT production versus a reference teaching aid alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by trained medical education facilitators
- Participants first produce SCTs so their own items can be analyzed during training
