# Structured video-debriefing sessions using the SHARP debriefing tool added to simulation-based training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the intervention arm, HBB simulation practice sessions were video-recorded and reviewed in structured debriefing sessions run with two facilitators in a separate room. Teams of three (a birth attendant, a mother and an assistant) practised while being videotaped, then watched the recording with feedback from the participants in the scenario, the rest of the group and the facilitator. "Prior to the debriefing, the participants were asked to set learning objectives at the beginning of each practical session using the SHARP (Set learning objectives, How it went, Address concerns, Review learning points, Plan ahead) debriefing tool", and the OSAD tool guided facilitators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video-recording of simulation practice sessions, a separate debriefing room, and two debriefing leaders/facilitators in attendance.
#### Constraints
- The article states more research is needed to justify adding video-debriefing to standard HBB training in this context.

### Target Learners
- skilled birth attendants (nurses/midwives and clinical officers/doctors) in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- neonatal resuscitation knowledge and skills (bag-mask ventilation and OSCE-assisted resuscitation skills)
