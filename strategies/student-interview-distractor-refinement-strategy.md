# Refine flagged assessment items by rewording ambiguous distractors and using student interviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For flagged low-discrimination items, the authors recommend rewording ambiguous distractors — for item 18, distractors using "atmosphere" and "sky" interchangeably should be reworded — and replacing illogical distractors (item 28's "ocean") with plausible terms. Following Adams and Wieman (2011), they argue "the use of student interviews to delve into the students' thinking process" is a particularly important part of developing and validating FASI-type instruments, so distractors are empirically grounded in students' thinking.
