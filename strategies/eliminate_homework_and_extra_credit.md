# Eliminate Homework and Extra Credit

## Description
Eliminate the inclusion of homework and extra credit in final grades. Homework grades often lead to copying and reflect privilege. Emphasize practice and mastery to foster intrinsic motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alternative methods for assessing practice and engagement
#### Constraints
- May initially lead to lower homework completion rates
