# Use evidence-based education as the knowledge basis for teacher decision-making

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends evidence-based practice in education, defined as the approach "to base decisions on the best available evidence, in the sense of the best possible – in particular systematic! – use of existing knowledge and research." In the spirit of Hattie's observation that the greatest effects occur when teachers become learners of their own teaching, teachers should know whether an approach really increases understanding or motivation and be able to test this in their own classrooms. The article pairs this with a balanced view that research evidence must be weighed judiciously alongside other kinds of knowledge.
