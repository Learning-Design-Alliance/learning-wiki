# Use targeted strategies to balance student groups in collaborative programming activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on post-survey feedback on group work during the Jupyter Notebook activities, the authors conclude that targeted strategies are needed in later course iterations to balance groups. While 71% of students were satisfied with group work and positive comments highlighted mutual help, dissatisfied students reported unbalanced division of labour, differing skill levels, and one person doing most of the work. The authors recommend balancing groups equally to make more students comfortable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group work during JN activities; awareness of members' differing prior programming levels
#### Constraints
- The strategy addresses dissatisfaction reported by 12% of students; the authors note students could choose their own group members or be assigned by the instructor

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students working in groups of about three

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative Python data-analysis skills
