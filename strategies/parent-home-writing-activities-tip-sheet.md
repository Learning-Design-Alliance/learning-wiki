# Parents support elementary writing skills at home through everyday writing activities like writing stories, reviewing movies, and describing things around them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy, drawn from a tip sheet published by the National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, positions parents as deliverers of writing practice at home for elementary school children. The tip sheet is explicitly derived from an evidence synthesis: it is "based on the What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) practice guide". Parents are guided to embed writing in ordinary home routines "through activities like writing stories, reviewing movies, and describing things around them", so that children produce authentic writing for real purposes rather than only completing school assignments.
