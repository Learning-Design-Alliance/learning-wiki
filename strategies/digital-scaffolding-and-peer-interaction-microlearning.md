# Support less digitally savvy learners with targeted digital scaffolding and add collaborative activities to AI-enhanced microlearning

## Description
Based on the age-group difference and the reported lack of peer interaction, the article recommends two design actions. It states that "targeted digital scaffolding is necessary to support less digitally savvy learners while considering their age", and that designers should explore integrating collaborative activities and peer interaction features, alongside human-centred AI integration that helps rather than overwhelms learners and attention to content clarity and pacing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators should focus on human-centred AI integration and design quality, particularly content clarity and appropriate pacing
#### Constraints
- The perceived lack of social engagement was reported specifically for online sessions in this study

### Target Learners
- older and less digitally savvy adult learners in microlearning

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable learning outcomes and social/collaborative engagement
