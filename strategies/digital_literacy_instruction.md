# Digital Literacy Instruction

## Description
Digital literacy instruction involves teaching learners how to effectively use digital technology, communication tools, and networks to locate, evaluate, use, and create information. This includes understanding computer basics and effective internet search strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to computers, internet connectivity, and instructors with expertise in digital literacy.
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging for learners with limited prior experience, requires ongoing updates to keep pace with evolving technology, and may require significant time investment.

### Affordances
- Empowers learners to participate fully in a digital society, enhances employability, and promotes lifelong learning.

### Personalization
- Instruction can be tailored to specific skill levels and learning goals, with options for one-on-one or group training.
