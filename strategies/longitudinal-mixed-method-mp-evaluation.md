# Evaluate mental practice with longitudinal mixed-method designs in real clinical teams, and embed MP in organisational routines such as pre-surgical briefings

## Description
The article recommends that future research "should look at longitudinal mixed method evaluation designs and focus on real clinical teams", moving beyond immediate performance outcomes to uptake, retention and transfer of MP routines. Three reviews advocated prospective investigations using systematic ethnographic observations, follow-up self-reports and evidence of changes in local training methods, team routines or curriculum. The authors also urge evaluators to consider organisational support — time and space allocated to MP in pre-surgical routines and briefings — and the effects of sustained MP on surgical flow and patient care.
