# Evaluate integrated physics-biology curricula with assessments of cross-domain energy integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that evaluating the new physics curriculum for biology students "should include assessments of how well students can integrate energy concepts between physics and biology and between different scales, in comparison to students with a traditional, less integrated curriculum." This makes cross-domain integration an explicit outcome measure for curriculum development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An integrated curriculum and a traditional, less integrated comparison curriculum must both be available so integration outcomes can be compared.
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a recommendation for its own piloted physics sequence for biology majors at the University of Maryland, 2011-12.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology majors in introductory physics

### Target Learning Goals
- integrating energy concepts across disciplines and scales
