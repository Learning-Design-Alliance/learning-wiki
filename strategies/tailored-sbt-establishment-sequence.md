# Tailored, stakeholder-engaged establishment sequence for ED simulation programmes led by a local simulation champion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a priority hierarchy for establishing SBT in an ED: first tackle local issues such as resources and optimal time windows while engaging key stakeholders early, then identify team needs and intentions, then resource needs, and finally optimise by maximising enablers and minimising barriers. It states "a tailored approach is vital" and recommends "electing a local simulation champion who is enthusiastic and has the drive to lead such a project". Informal discussions with stakeholders during planning help surface local culture and barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engagement of key stakeholders from the beginning of programme planning
#### Constraints
- The article states a standardised framework may not be possible because countries and establishments have varying systems and cultures, so a tailored solution is required

### Target Learners
- Emergency department clinical staff
- Emergency medicine trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Establishing and sustaining simulation-based training and in situ simulation programmes in emergency departments
