Sequence the legal translation curriculum from human from-scratch translation to AI-assisted workflows, with strategic AI literacy from the outset¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A pedagogical principle for legal translator education: students should first develop legal translation methodology and core translation competences through human from-scratch translation before progressing to AI-assisted workflows. As printed: "Students should first develop legal translation methodology and the core translation competences through human from -scratch translation before progressing to AI-assisted workflows". Premature reliance on AI output may weaken competence acquisition and encourage uncritical acceptance. AI initially augments decision-making (source-text analysis, terminology research) before being used for drafting under human oversight, while strategic AI literacy — deciding whether and how to use AI — is present from the start.
