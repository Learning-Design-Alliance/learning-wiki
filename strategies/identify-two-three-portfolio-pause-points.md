# Identify 2-3 intentional pause points for collecting and reflecting on artifacts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before implementing portfolios, educators analyze the most meaningful engagement points during the year or unit and select a small number of moments to pause. The guide directs educators to "identify 2-3 points over the course of the year or unit of study where you will pause to intentionally collect and reflect on student artifacts." This keeps the pause a meaningful use of time rather than an afterthought.
