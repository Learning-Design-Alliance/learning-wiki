# Prioritize mid-year CBM literacy screenings, particularly LNF and LSF, within a multi-point progress-monitoring framework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy uses dynamic, curriculum-based measurement (CBM) tools at multiple points in the year rather than relying solely on fall screening. The article found mid-year LNF and LSF substantially improved identification of at-risk readers and states that "educators should prioritize mid-year assessments, particularly LNF and LSF, to maximize the effectiveness of literacy screenings." It further recommends teacher professional development on administering, interpreting, and using CBM data for instructional planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need training on how to administer, interpret, and utilize CBM data
- Resources for assessment materials, teacher training, and intervention programs
#### Constraints
- The study's assessments were administered at semester ends, which may limit the ability to capture ongoing learning progress within a semester

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten and grade 1 Arabic-speaking students
- Students at risk of reading difficulties

### Target Learning Goals
- Early word reading fluency and foundational literacy skills (letter naming, letter sounds, phoneme segmentation)
