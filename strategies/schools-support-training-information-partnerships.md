# Schools should consider parents' socioeconomic status, provide necessary information, and support training for effective technology-integrated partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's concluding recommendation directs schools to address the digital divide in partnership practices through three actions: considering parents' socioeconomic status, providing necessary information, and supporting training. As the review states, "To address this, schools must consider parents' socioeconomic status, provide the necessary information, and support training for effective technology-integrated partnerships." The strategy responds to the skills and usage gaps the review identified as limiting partnership benefits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must have the capacity to offer information and training support to parents.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation addresses families with limited access to technology or digital skills, as identified in the review's included studies.

### Target Learners
- parents, particularly those with limited technology access or digital skills

### Target Learning Goals
- developing digital skills for effective participation in family–school partnerships
