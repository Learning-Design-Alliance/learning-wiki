# Use tier 2 and 3 school-wide positive behavior supports alongside academic instruction to increase attentiveness and engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends, drawing on Benner et al. (2010), that students with or at-risk of EBD benefit from tier 2 and 3 of school-wide Positive Behavior Support and Intervention programs. These supports are intended to motivate students "toward increasing attentiveness and engagement along with controlling their behavior," complementing academic intervention given the negative impact of problem behavior on literacy outcomes.
