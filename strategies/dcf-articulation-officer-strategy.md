# Articulation officers can apply DCF to single-community-college transfer cohorts to measure college-specific challenges and support data-driven articulation decisions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that "Articulation officers deciding on course transferability can use our DCF methodology to narrow transfer groups to single community colleges to measure college-specific challenges and support data-driven articulation decisions". Because about 92% of admitted transfer students arrive from in-state community colleges, such subgroup analyses could reveal where preparatory gaps originate, although the power analysis implies sufficiently large group sizes are needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient enrollment per course and per community-college subgroup for reliable DCF parameter identification and statistical power
#### Constraints
- Detected effects concern A-grade achievement on first attempt, not graduation success; transfer and native graduation rates are similar (91% vs. 92%)

### Target Learners
- transfer students from community colleges

### Target Learning Goals
- data-driven course articulation and transfer support decisions
