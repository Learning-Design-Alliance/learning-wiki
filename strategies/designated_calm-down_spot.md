# Designated Calm-Down Spot

## Description
Creating a designated calm-down spot is providing a safe and structured space within the classroom where students can go to regain control over their emotions. The spot should be equipped with tools and resources that support self-regulation, such as stress balls, Theraputty, or visual aids with calming techniques.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designated space in the classroom, tools and resources for self-regulation, explicit teaching and modeling of how to use the space.
#### Constraints
- May be ineffective if not introduced and implemented properly. Relies on students recognizing and utilizing the space independently.
