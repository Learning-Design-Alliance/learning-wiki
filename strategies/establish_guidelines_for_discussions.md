# Establish Guidelines for Discussions

## Description
Establish clear guidelines for group discussions, emphasizing engagement, comfort with discomfort, speaking one's truth, and accepting non-closure. Examples include: Staying morally, intellectually, and socially engaged; acknowledging and expressing discomfort; being honest about thoughts and feelings; and accepting the lack of immediate solutions, emphasizing the continuous nature of understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator to introduce and reinforce the guidelines, willingness of participants to adhere to the guidelines, and a safe space for open dialogue.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if participants are unwilling to engage in good faith. Requires ongoing reinforcement and adaptation to address emerging challenges.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners of all ages and backgrounds engaging in discussions about sensitive or controversial topics.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Foster a safe and respectful environment for discussing sensitive topics, promote active listening and engagement, encourage honest expression of thoughts and feelings, and manage expectations for resolution.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured framework for productive dialogue, encourages empathy and understanding, minimizes conflict, and promotes a sense of safety and inclusion.

### Personalization
- Guidelines can be adapted to suit the specific needs and context of the group. Flexibility in implementing the 'pause of respect' or check-ins allows for individualized responses.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the quality of interactions, the level of engagement, and the degree of respect shown among participants. Collect feedback on the effectiveness of the guidelines in promoting productive dialogue.

## Impact
- Research suggests that clear guidelines and norms can promote more effective and respectful communication in diverse groups. Creating a psychologically safe environment can improve learning and engagement.
