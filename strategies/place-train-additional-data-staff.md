# Build agency data capacity by supporting, training, and placing additional data staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy described in the study is to strengthen education agencies' data-driven decision making by providing "the support, training, and placement of additional staff." Rather than relying solely on existing personnel, agencies receive trained data staff whose placement is intended to build capacity to collect, manage, analyze, and use data.
