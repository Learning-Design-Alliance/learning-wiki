# Include both LOTS and HOTS practice in communicative FL lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Following Song (2020), the article states that communicative-approach lessons 'must not only contain the introduction and practical dissemination of many LOTS-related exercises, but also clearly focus upon the introduction of attractive and original practice of HOTS'. Learners should offer personal opinions, assessments and evaluative productions, which the article calls the fastest and didactically best forms of upgrading language proficiency.
