# Follow pronunciation instruction with controlled practice, guided practice, communicative practice, and feedback at every step

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that pronunciation instruction "should be followed by controlled practicing, and then guided practice, and finally communicative practice and accompanying feedback in all steps (Celce-Murcia, 2010) in order to sustain a lasting change." This graduated practice sequence with feedback addresses the gap between test performance and spontaneous classroom use observed in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must provide consistent feedback during all practice phases
#### Constraints
- The study itself provided no feedback during the four-week retention period, which the authors identify as a main reason for students' failure to use sounds in practice

### Target Learners
- Adult EFL learners
- University students with fossilized pronunciation errors

### Target Learning Goals
- Automaticity in target sound production
- Transfer of learned pronunciation to spontaneous speech
