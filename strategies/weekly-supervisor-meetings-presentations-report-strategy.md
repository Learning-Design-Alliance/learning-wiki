# Require weekly supervisor meetings, mid-term and final oral presentations, and a final technical report to structure and evaluate the internship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program structures each internship with supervisory contact and staged deliverables. The article states that "The supervisor should meet his or her student personally at least once per week in order to ensure the best productivity of the trainee." All students must give a mid-term and a final oral presentation and write a technical report at the end of the stay; these documents are stored in the Education Office web archive, where they can be consulted.
