# Provide easily accessible, low-cost refresher training such as e-learning to maintain BLS/AED skills after initial training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study found marked skill decay within three months, the article recommends refresher training and specifies its design conditions: it "should be easily accessible and available at with no or low cost, making it more likely to be used by the general population". E-learning is named as a modality meeting these requirements and as increasingly popular. The article also calls for future studies to develop methods to improve performance among those who fail to reach adequate skill level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Refresher training addressing skill retention
- Accessibility at no or low cost to encourage use by the general population
#### Constraints
- The optimal timing for refresher training is not yet determined; current first-time refresher recommendations range from 6 to 24 months after initial training
- The optimal type of refresher training is stated to be undetermined

### Target Learners
- laypersons
- general population

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining BLS/AED skill retention over time
