# Provide virtual microscopy platforms with bilingual content, search, favorites, and instructor forums to support independent and guided learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete feature set for curriculum-specific virtual microscopy: bilingual (Persian/English) support, search functionality for finding tissue structures quickly, a favorites section for most-visited slides, schematic images, and public and private forums for instructor–student communication, integrated with the institutional LMS. These features "were specifically designed to address the educational needs of Iranian medical sciences students and to facilitate both independent and guided learning".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-curated slide libraries and laboratory images, and integration with an institutional learning management system.
#### Constraints
- The availability of Persian-language content is described as particularly valuable for novice learners still developing proficiency in English medical terminology; generalizability beyond the single institution studied is limited.

### Target Learners
- Medical, dentistry, and pharmacy students, particularly novice learners developing English medical terminology proficiency

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent and guided learning of histological slide interpretation
