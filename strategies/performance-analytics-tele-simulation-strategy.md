# Adopt performance analytics and tele-simulation to individualize simulation learning and extend access across campuses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In its future directions the article recommends that simulation centers implement performance analytics and tele-simulation: "implementation of performance analytics could further individualize simulation learning based on overall performance trends, and tele-simulation could create space for inter-campus center training across entities". The strategy pairs new technologies (VR, AI, tele-simulation) with the caution that simulation should complement, not substitute for, clinical experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital infrastructure for VR, AI and tele-simulation delivery
- Performance data streams to support analytics-based individualization
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as future direction, not a tested intervention
- Institutions must keep simulation a complement to clinical experience

### Target Learners
- Medical students and clinical trainees
- Remotely located learners at rural or inter-campus sites

### Target Learning Goals
- Individualized adaptive simulation training
- Extended access to simulation across campuses
