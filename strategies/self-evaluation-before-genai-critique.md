# Require a rubric-aligned self-evaluation before students receive GenAI critique

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete sequencing strategy: students first evaluate their own draft against explicit criteria, then receive AI-generated critique. The method states that "The reflective GenAI-supported condition required a brief rubric-aligned self-evaluation before students received GenAI feedback." This sequence requires learners to articulate an initial judgment and then compare it with external feedback, supporting internal feedback and evaluative judgment development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit rubric criteria and a structured self-evaluation prompt must be available before AI critique is delivered
#### Constraints
- The reflective condition's conceptual-learning advantage over direct GenAI feedback was positive but not statistically significant after adjustment

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate science students

### Target Learning Goals
- feedback uptake
- evaluative judgment
- self-regulated revision
