# Implement XR-based ultrasound training as partial replacement of standard training during a dedicated training day

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing the XR application "as a partial replacement of standard training during a dedicated ultrasound training day—either in small group sessions or as a blended, asynchronous component that students can complete in their own time". This approach leverages the application's capacity for autonomous, repeated practice with feedback to enhance student-directed learning and curriculum flexibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- XR hardware (HoloLens 2) and the Augmedicine application
- A morning lecture and group-based practical session preceding the XR component
#### Constraints
- Applicable in the early stages of training; the authors note XR cannot fully replace POCUS training as clinical competence also requires experience with real anatomical variation, artefacts, and patient interaction
- Further validation is required before broader implementation, including optimal training duration, long-term skill retention, and transfer to clinical practice

### Target Learners
- Medical students in early clerkship training

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic cardiac ultrasound image acquisition
- Independent practice with automated feedback
