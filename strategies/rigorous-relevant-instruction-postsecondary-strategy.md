# Provide rigorous and relevant instruction connecting academics to postsecondary options

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's sixth schoolwide recommendation increases engagement by providing students the skills needed to complete high school and introducing them to postsecondary options. It advises integrating academic content with career and skill-based themes through career academies or multiple pathways models, hosting career days, partnering with local businesses for internships and simulated job interviews, and giving students extra assistance and information about the demands of college.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing teacher professional development to expand knowledge and improve skills
- Partnerships with local businesses and postsecondary institutions
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- All middle and high school students, particularly those at risk of disengagement

### Target Learning Goals
- Engagement in learning, skills needed to graduate, and awareness of postsecondary opportunities
