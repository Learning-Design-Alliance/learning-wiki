# Creating special choice situations at the lesson so pupils choose aims, content, level, form and tempo of learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Within the 'I can learn' technology, the article recommends organizing pupil activity through choice situations: "a part of a lesson prepared by the teacher when the pupils have to choose a variant of coping with some task". Pupils choose not only aims but also content beyond the standard, the level, type and form of the task (written or oral, individual or group), the tempo, and the right to estimate their own results. A planning table (topic, aim, date, content, form, result, requirements, notes) structures the work, and sentence starters support personal aiming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher explains the requirements to the results of learning the topic and offers pupils choice; support is needed when pupils have difficulties.
#### Constraints
- Content choice applies only to material beyond the federal educational standard; the obligatory invariant elements remain fixed.

### Target Learners
- pupils with a positive but unstable attitude to learning at secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- personal goal-setting, reflection, independent organization of learning activity
