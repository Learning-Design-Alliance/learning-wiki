# Digital Tips for Quick and Thoughtful Peer Feedback

## Description
Using digital tools to facilitate peer feedback can make the process easier and more efficient. This involves students sharing their work digitally, such as posting a link, sharing access to a document, or snapping a picture, and receiving feedback through comments or face-to-face conversations. Incorporating digital citizenship discussions can also enhance the feedback process, particularly for older learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to digital tools (e.g., Google Classroom, Padlet, Seesaw), clear guidelines for providing feedback, and a structured process.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on students' digital literacy and their ability to provide constructive feedback. Requires initial training and modeling by the instructor.
