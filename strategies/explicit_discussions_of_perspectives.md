# Explicit Discussions of Perspectives

## Description
Facilitating structured conversations where learners explore multiple viewpoints and consider how different individuals might experience the same event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared discussion prompts or case studies, class time for discussion, and a skilled facilitator.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may dominate discussions. Discussions can be sensitive and require skilled facilitation. Learners may resist or reject perspectives that differ from their own.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, including K–12, higher education, and adult learners in professional development programs

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop social awareness, enhance empathy, improve understanding of different perspectives, and promote positive relationships.

### Affordances
- Provides opportunities to learn about different perspectives, encourages empathy and understanding, promotes critical thinking, and supports positive relationships.

### Personalization
- Discussions can be tailored to align with different age groups, cultural backgrounds, or interests. Support can be provided for learners who struggle with perspective-taking.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners’ participation in discussions. Review learners’ written or verbal reflections on different perspectives. Evaluate learners’ understanding of social issues and cultural diversity.

## Impact
- Structured discussions can enhance social awareness, promote empathy, and improve understanding of different perspectives. Explicit instruction can help learners develop their perspective-taking skills.
