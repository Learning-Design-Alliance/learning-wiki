# Introduce QIST concepts early through K-12 educator professional development and accessible conceptual materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that introducing students to QIST concepts early is critical to getting them excited about the topic and related careers, especially for students from diverse backgrounds, low-income families, or whose parents did not attend college. Recommended mechanisms include professional development for K-12 educators, incorporating QIST concepts into national K-12 standards and existing courses, and short courses focused exclusively on QIST concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership between postsecondary educators and K-12 educators, for example through the Quantum for All program
#### Constraints
- Without exposure at the K-12 level, students from underrepresented backgrounds will likely not pursue QIST-related courses at the college level

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, especially those from underrepresented groups

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of QIST concepts such as quantum key distribution; excitement about QIST careers
