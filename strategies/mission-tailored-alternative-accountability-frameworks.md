# Develop flexible, alternative accountability frameworks tailored to schools' missions rather than relying on uniform measures such as the SAT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Charter school leaders in the study recommended creating more flexible, alternative accountability frameworks tailored to schools' missions and the unique value they provide their communities. They argued that "using the SAT to evaluate schools focused on career and technical education (CTE) is not an adequate measure of success" and suggested that postsecondary outcomes, mission-based goals, and attendance data would better reflect the value and real-life benefits these schools provide. RIDE employees confirmed that efforts to develop such alternative models are underway.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Measures aligned to school mission, such as postsecondary outcomes, mission-based goals, and attendance data
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as a recommendation from some charter school leaders, not a tested result

### Target Learners
- Students in mission-specific charter schools, such as career and technical education schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Valid assessment of school quality across diverse missions
