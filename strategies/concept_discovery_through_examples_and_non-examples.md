# Concept Discovery Through Examples and Non-examples

## Description
Expose learners to a wide range of examples and nonexamples and allow them to discover the concept. Present multiple examples to the learner and inquire about shared characteristics of the examples. Through exploration and discovery (interactive activities), learners can acquire the concept. Then use questions and context-sensitive feedback, to correct any misconceptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a rich set of examples and non-examples. Needs interactive activities that promote exploration and discovery.
#### Constraints
- May be less effective for learners with limited prior knowledge. Requires careful design to guide exploration and prevent confusion.
