# Apply double-blind peer review to open educational resources before publication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends adapting the scholarly peer-review workflow to educational resources: submissions are reviewed anonymously by two stakeholders, an educator and an astronomy researcher, with identifying information stripped before review. The authors note that "astroEDU's form of peer-review is double blind and free from bias" and that, like peer-reviewed scholarly articles, the process provides a metric for the quality of educators' work. Reviewers know the editor but not the author, and the author does not know the reviewers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An editorial board and a pool of qualified reviewer peers (educators and domain researchers); an activity template that makes outcomes evaluable
#### Constraints
- The article notes earlier peer-review attempts for educational resources had varying success, and NASA Wavelength and CLEAN's panel-based review did not allow open submission

### Target Learners
- educators seeking quality-assured astronomy education activities

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific accuracy and educational value of published learning activities
