# Future research should clarify construct boundaries, strengthen content-validity evidence, and directly compare competing measurement models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that the field move beyond composite reliability evidence toward resolving what academic resilience instruments actually measure. It states that "Future research should further clarify construct boundaries, strengthen content-validity evidence, and directly compare competing measurement models." This agenda follows from the review's findings that many measures may be dominated by a broad general resilience factor while global-fit comparisons do not establish whether proposed subdimensions add differentiated value beyond composite scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Direct comparisons of competing measurement models within the same samples, plus content-validity evidence, are needed to resolve whether subdimensions provide explanatory, diagnostic, or predictive value
#### Constraints
- The review notes current global-fit evidence does not establish differentiated value of subdimensions, and formal measurement-invariance tests were not possible with available data

### Target Learners
- students in primary, secondary, and higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- improved measurement of adaptive academic functioning under adversity

### Affordances
- Academic Resilience Construct Framework
