# Future ultrasound training research in low-resource settings should add control groups, expand to advanced skills, and include midwives and nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose forward-looking directions: "Adding a control group would also aid in evaluating the efficacy of the intervention compared to real-time practice in the field without any formal teaching session." They further suggest expanding "the scope of the study to include advanced obstetric and gynecologic ultrasound skills" and extending "the study population... to midwives and nurses", hoping to inspire clinicians to develop and assess ultrasound teaching tools in developing areas.
