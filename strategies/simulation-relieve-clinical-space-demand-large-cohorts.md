# Use simulation and technology-based teaching to relieve clinical space demand in large cohorts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy emerging from the model's transformative learning sub-concept: where growing student numbers outstrip clinical placements, innovative teaching approaches including technology and simulation are suggested. As one educator put it, "simulation can be used to cut the demand for clinical space", with fundamental skills mastered in college laboratories. The article notes simulation has globally been recommended as a way to build skills within the college environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate infrastructure and resources to support transformative teaching methods at educational institutions
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of growing student numbers and unchanged clinical practice site capacity

### Target Learners
- nursing and midwifery students in colleges with large cohorts

### Target Learning Goals
- building clinical skills when clinical placement capacity is limited
