# Schedule online events on weekends, evenings or holidays to avoid clashes with participants' own online sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A scheduling strategy for online education and outreach: because most potential participants had their own online sessions, the authors found that "Planning of the events has to be done carefully" and that they "selected weekends, evenings or holidays for all our events". It was applied across all their 2020 online classes and workshops.
