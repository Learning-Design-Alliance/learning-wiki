Optimize consolidation through sufficient sleep, spaced and interleaved study, and rest periods between learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 9 studies (6 quant-synthesis, 2 causal, 1 review), q2–q4 · 5 of 9 report an effect size

Description¶

The review holds that consolidation can be optimized for enhanced learning and remembering. "Sleep is the most important means to consolidate, integrate and semanticize memories, which is supposed to generalize memories, foster insight, and create strong schemas." Spacing and interleaving of topics over time are given as reasons for pronounced effects on memory performance, and offline rest periods show consolidation-like effects; the review advises regular breaks with little new information and repeating information on separate days.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Enough sleep per night, especially after studying new information; breaks in which little new information is absorbed

Constraints¶

  • Effects of adding emotional value or targeted memory reactivation during sleep are small and specific and hence not easy to practice in daily life

Target Learners¶

  • adult learners
  • students in educational settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • memory consolidation
  • schema strengthening and semanticization

Affordances¶

  • Memory Phases Encoding Consolidation Retrieval Reconsolidation

Claims¶

  • Spaced Practice Improves Retention [+S]
  • Retrieval practice and spaced retrieval improve achievement and retention by medium amounts, while interleaving helps category learning only for some materials and blocking beats it for words [+S]
  • Distributed practice benefits L2 learning, and one review argues that spreading it over years can be worse than over months [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use elaboration, reactivation of prior knowledge, and desirable difficulty during encoding to build connected, detailed memories
  • Cramming
  • Sequence simpler desirable difficulties (retrieval, spacing, interleaving) during initial L2 vocabulary encoding, reserving generation for post-encoding consolidation
  • Apply retrieval practice (quizzes, peer teaching, flipped classroom) and monitor detail loss to balance gist and episodic memory
