# Improve elearning engagement by signposting content, linking it explicitly to assessment, and building students' self-directed learning skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the control-value lens, the article recommends concrete educator actions: clearer signposting within large elearning resources, explicit links between elearning content and assessment, emphasis of relevance to future professional practice, and focussed training to develop students' self-confidence with elearning technology and a more self-directed approach to learning. These target the control and value appraisals the authors identified as driving disengagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and curriculum developers must have control over resource structure and assessment alignment
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a qualitative study at one UK institution; the authors note findings are theoretically transferrable rather than generalisable

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students, especially school leavers ill prepared for independent learning

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement with elearning resources
- self-directed learning
