# Strengthen nurses' EBP competence and clinical decision-making through targeted education, access to organizational resources, and supportive policies

## Description
Based on its findings, the article recommends targeted strategies such as education, access to organizational resources and supportive policies to strengthen nurses' EBP competencies and CDM. It calls for regular training programs, hands-on learning opportunities and institutional policies facilitating access to research, and for organizational arrangements to minimize workload and improve working conditions. The authors state that "addressing barriers and fostering a culture of continuous learning can enhance patient care and nursing outcomes".
