Strategies

Set case-mix-aware proficiency expectations and personalized therapeutic endpoints: count procedures toward proficiency only within the nodule-size stratum being trained, and for large nodules target symptom relief rather than maximal volume reduction

The article recommends judging procedural proficiency within the nodule-size stratum actually treated, since a learning curve achieved on small or medium nodules does not guarantee performance on large ones.
