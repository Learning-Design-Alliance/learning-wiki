# Strategically employ AI tools, including chatbots, within active learning methodologies such as problem-based learning, flipped classrooms, and virtual labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that AI tools including chatbots be "strategically employed in active learning methodologies, such as problem-based learning, flipped classrooms, and online learning". In PBL, small groups tackle real cases in genetics, ecology, or biomedical research while interpreting AI-generated suggestions; in virtual labs such as Labster, AI-based feedback flags improbable methods or unexpected results; educators structure reflective checkpoints where learners articulate reasoning and evaluate AI-generated insights. Chatbots can scaffold genetics data analysis, ecology modeling, and lab protocols such as cell culture or PCR.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must effectively use the tools with guided reflection and collaborative projects, and embed transparent rubrics highlighting potential biases or missing perspectives
#### Constraints
- Students must frequently evaluate algorithmic outputs and their ethical implications rather than simply accepting them at face value

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biological sciences students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking, evidence-based reasoning, collaborative problem-solving, and critical evaluation of AI outputs
