# Teachers cultivate approach-oriented goals and help students manage AI-feedback anxiety in AI-assisted language learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's practical implications recommend that instructors actively foster adaptive motivational patterns in AI-assisted language education: they should "actively cultivate mastery-approach and performance-approach goals by providing clear guidance, constructive feedback, and encouragement that emphasizes growth and progress", while also helping students manage anxiety associated with AI-generated feedback so avoidance tendencies do not impede engagement. Training programs can equip teachers to integrate AI tools in ways that support rather than replace pedagogical interaction, and learners can receive structured guidance on interpreting AI feedback and setting realistic, approach-oriented goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need guidance on interpreting AI feedback and integrating AI tools into classroom instruction alongside pedagogical interaction

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
