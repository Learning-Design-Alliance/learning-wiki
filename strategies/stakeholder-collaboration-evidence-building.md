# Collaborate with stakeholders from the priority community throughout evidence building

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The checklist directs users to carry out the evidence-building process in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community. Rather than designing, refining, and testing solutions externally, teams engage community stakeholders as partners across the phases, beginning with solution design in Phase 1 and continuing through refinement, early evidence assessment, and validation.
