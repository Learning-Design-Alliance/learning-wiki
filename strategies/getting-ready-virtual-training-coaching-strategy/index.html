Deliver Getting Ready educator training and coaching through virtual formats¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's implementation model is a two-part virtual professional development strategy: educators first receive "initial training and ongoing coaching to build program capacity." Initial training covers adult-child interactions and relationships, family-school partnerships, and collaborative interactions, delivered "through live virtual training or video modules," followed by ongoing coaching sessions in which educators reflect on and support their approaches. No additional staffing is required; educators need internet access and video conferencing software.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators need access to the internet and video conferencing software

Constraints¶

  • Training must be followed by ongoing coaching sessions for the approach to be supported as described

Target Learners¶

  • early childhood educators in home visitation and center-based programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • family-school partnership skills
  • collaborative goal setting with families

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Layered professional development for curriculum implementation (webinars, workshops, coaching)
  • Support curriculum implementation with initial multi-day training plus ongoing onsite consultation during the school year
  • Implement Reading Edge with two days of initial teacher training plus monthly in-class coaching and peer support structures
  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Support PowerUp implementation with layered professional development: customized planning, product training, and ongoing data coaching
  • Combine summer professional development with school-year on-site coaching and provision of complete instructional materials
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Replace labor-intensive in-person coaching with a virtual professional learning community to scale professional development
