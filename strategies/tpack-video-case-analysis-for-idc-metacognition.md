# Use TPACK-based analysis of teaching video cases and design artifacts to build novice instructional designers' metacognition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete teaching activity in which educators guide pre-service teachers to analyze specific teaching video cases and instructional design components through the TPACK framework, then design, receive feedback, revise, micro-teach, and reflect. The article states that "TPACK-based analysis is the element of teaching the meta-cognition of instructional design that can help pre-service teachers better improve teaching design knowledge and teaching design skills." It was implemented across three iterative cycles in the experimental course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching video cases and design tasks aligned to the TPACK framework; structured feedback from instructors and peers
#### Constraints
- Studied with pre-service teachers in the early stage of professional development with no prior instructional design experience

### Target Learners
- pre-service preschool teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- instructional design knowledge, skills, and metacognition
