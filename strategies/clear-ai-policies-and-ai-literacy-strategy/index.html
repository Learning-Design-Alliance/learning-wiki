Institutions should adopt clear, prominently displayed AI policies and cultivate AI literacy for ethical, employability-oriented use¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that doctoral-granting institutions develop "clear and prominently displayed policies regarding ethical AI use and academic integrity", because participants reported differing or hidden course AI policies. Policies should "clearly demarcate appropriate AI usage" while accommodating diverse student approaches, and should pair with AI literacy education so students understand AI capabilities, limitations, and ethics—also an emerging employability skill. Instructors are advised to integrate AI tools into curricula while guarding against dependency that could undermine independent thinking.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional commitment to policy development and AI literacy education across disciplines

Constraints¶

  • The article notes it remains unclear whether AI integration could create dependency that undermines students' independent writing

Target Learners¶

  • doctoral students, faculty, administrators and policymakers in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • ethical AI use, academic integrity, AI literacy, workforce readiness

Related Strategies¶

  • Build ethical AI integration on four coordinated levels: faculty professional development, ethics curricula, clear institutional guidelines, and interdisciplinary updating
  • Strengthen institutional policies, ethics education, verification, disclosure, and data protection to support ethical GenAI use
  • Accrediting bodies and professional organizations should provide AI policy frameworks integrating academic integrity, data ethics, and equitable access, and programs should invest in faculty development
  • Institutions should respond to GenAI with clear governance frameworks and structured professional training programs for staff.
  • Integrate AI literacy, digital skill development, and technology-focused career planning into coursework, with targeted support for higher-anxiety groups
  • Prepare teachers to use AI expeditiously and provide AI-text detectors for evaluation, alongside updated academic-integrity rules
  • Provide AI literacy training, human-AI interaction opportunities, and ethical-use guidelines and policies for instructional designers
