# Use Career Development Events to develop transferable skills alongside career-specific preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "Agriculture teachers should consider participation in Career Development Events that not only prepare students for a specific career but also develop other transferable skills." CDEs embed skill development in authentic competitive contexts such as agricultural sales, supporting communication, teamwork, and problem solving alongside technical content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers dedicate at least six weeks (21-24 hours) to preparing a CDE team.
#### Constraints
- Participation levels for some CDEs, such as agricultural sales, may be low enough that state associations consider removing them.

### Target Learners
- Secondary school agriculture students

### Target Learning Goals
- Transferable skills including communication, teamwork, and problem solving
- Career-specific content knowledge
