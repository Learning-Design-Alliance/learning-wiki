# Embed employability skills and career awareness across the physics curriculum through a coordinated employer-engagement programme

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy embeds employability into the undergraduate physics curriculum via sessions on investigating career options, CV writing and interview skills, employer talks, workshops and visits, SEPnet-wide careers webinars, paid summer placements, an annual Expo, and an employer mentoring scheme. It responds to the Wakeham Review's recommendations on work placements, professional skills training and stronger higher-education-employer collaboration. A centrally-funded director leads employability advisors at each partner institution.
