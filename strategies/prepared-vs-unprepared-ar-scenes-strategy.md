# Use prepared-marker scenes for classroom AR and additional hardware sensors (gyroscopes, GPS, compasses) for unprepared out-of-classroom scenes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Rizov and Rizova, the article distinguishes "підготовленої" та «непідготовленої» сцени — prepared and unprepared virtual modelling scenes. For unprepared scenes, typically outside the classroom, determining and tracking the scene's state requires additional hardware such as gyroscopes, GPS navigators and compasses. For classroom use the article recommends preparing the scene, so position determination and tracking are done with reliable black-and-white markers of characteristic shape (square or circle).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepared scenes rely on reliable black-and-white markers of characteristic shape whose detection and tracking is specified by the software architecture
#### Constraints
- Unprepared (out-of-classroom) scenes require additional hardware — gyroscopes, GPS navigators, compasses — to determine and track scene state

### Target Learners
- higher education students using mobile AR in and outside the classroom

### Target Learning Goals
- engineering graphics and physics laboratory learning with AR
