# Leverage trauma champions and peer education to disseminate TIC principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Trauma champions are healthcare professionals already working within their organization who can lead cultural change and share TIC expertise through interdisciplinary peer education. Participants described champions as valuable in mentoring peers, modeling best practices, and starting conversations about TIC. Academic structures such as student mentorship and residency programs were also recognized as important avenues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identified clinicians with TIC expertise and positional influence within the organization
- Interdisciplinary meeting structures for peer education
#### Constraints
- The article notes TIC should not be confined to isolated clinics or individual champions alone

### Target Learners
- Prenatal clinicians
- Residents
- Nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Trauma-informed care competency
- Trauma-informed clinical practice
