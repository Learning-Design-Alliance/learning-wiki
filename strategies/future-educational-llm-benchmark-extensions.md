# Extend educational LLM benchmarking toward multilingual contexts, real classroom interaction data, and targeted training for identified capability gaps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors close by recommending next steps for educational LLM evaluation and development. The article states: "Future work includes extending to mul- tilingual contexts, incorporating real classroom interaction data, and developing targeted training strategies to address the capability gaps identified in this study." This positions the current benchmark as a first open, large-scale, learning-science-grounded instrument whose gaps (e.g., Assessment and Research center weakness, alignment inconsistency) should drive both data collection and model training efforts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A benchmark baseline (such as OpenLearnLM) against which targeted training strategies and new data sources can be evaluated
#### Constraints
- The recommendation follows from a seven-model evaluation on synthetic and adapted items; real classroom interaction data is named as future work, not yet incorporated

### Target Learners
- LLMs deployed in multilingual and authentic classroom educational settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving educational LLM capability in weak areas such as systematic judgment, multi-step analytical reasoning, and behavioral consistency
