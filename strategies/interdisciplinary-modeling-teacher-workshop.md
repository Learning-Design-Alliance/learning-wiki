# Interdisciplinary physics-mathematics modeling workshop for teachers designing interdisciplinary learning paths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
After the Department of Mathematical Sciences launched its plan in 2009, a workshop for teachers of physics and mathematics on modeling was performed in collaboration with the Physics Plan, followed by an updating course in which "selected topics, named in the same way in both disciplines, are discussed in order to design interdisciplinary learning paths". The purpose is to clarify these topics using tools from both physics and mathematics and to outline similarities and differences between the two disciplinary contexts. The authors believe the activity helps students acquire deeper insight into fundamental concepts and supports teacher professional development.
