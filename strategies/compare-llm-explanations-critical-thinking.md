# Use students' comparison of explanations from different AI models as an activity to build critical evaluation of AI-generated content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends enhancing critical thinking by "encouraging comparison and contrast of explanations from different AI models, prompting students to identify strengths, weaknesses, and potential biases in AI-generated content". This implementable classroom activity exploits the diversity in explanatory approaches the study documented across six LLMs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to multiple AI models whose explanations differ measurably
#### Constraints
- Potential misconceptions may arise from varying explanatory styles

### Target Learners
- Physics students at secondary or higher levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated scientific explanations
