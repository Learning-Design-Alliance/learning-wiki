# Use the MUSCAT for individual self-evaluation and team-based reflection to develop multidisciplinary collaboration capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the MUSCAT both individually and in teams: professionals self-evaluate, identify competencies needing improvement for a given high-risk caregiver case, and share results with the team. The authors state it "can be used for evaluation and capacity development of multidisciplinary approaches in training programs" and that "all team members may develop their multi-occupational collaborative ability by using this scale". Self-evaluation is expected to prompt reflection and skill upgrading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Team members from differing job categories complete the scale against a concrete high-risk caregiver case so that needed abilities can be clarified and shared
#### Constraints
- The MUSCAT was developed for self-evaluation of individual abilities; the article notes professionals' abilities may differ depending on job types or other related factors

### Target Learners
- Doctors, nurses, public health nurses, midwives, social workers, child welfare workers, kindergarten teachers, and nursery school teachers involved in early prevention of child abuse

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing multi-occupational collaborative ability through self-reflection and team discussion
