## Description
The article proposes three topics addressable with the app. For magnetic force, the student varies current, turn length and field to "facilmente visualizar o efeito das altera¸ c˜ oes dessas grandezas na for¸ ca magn´ etica". For magnetic flux, the user can manipulate four of the five parameters of Φ_B = NBLW cos θ. With the commutator disabled, the user observes how an external field influences the dynamics of a turn with magnetic dipole moment via τ = μ × B.
