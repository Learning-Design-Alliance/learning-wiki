Strategies

State and district leaders should collect information from mentors about how they chose activities and topics, to decide whether variation reflects teacher needs or insufficient mentor resources¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because mentoring activities and topics varied greatly across teachers, the report recommends that leaders gather evidence before acting: "state and district leaders might consider collecting additional information from a sample of mentors to learn about how they determined what activities and topics to address." If variation reflects new teachers' preparedness or needs, it may be helpful; if it reflects differences in mentors' training, guidance, or time, leaders "might consider investing in more supports for mentors."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to mentors or mentoring records for follow-up data collection

Constraints¶

  • The study cannot determine whether variation in mentoring was due to differing teacher needs or to mentors' capacity and skills

Target Learners¶

  • First-year and early-career teachers in district mentoring programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher retention
  • New-teacher instructional and noninstructional support

Related Strategies¶

  • Conduct a larger-scale, statewide study of mentoring features and retention to detect small or moderate relationships
  • Withdraw mentor support earlier than currently thought, and calibrate how closely mentors work with mentees
