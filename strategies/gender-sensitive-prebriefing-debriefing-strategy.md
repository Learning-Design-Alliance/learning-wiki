# Gender-sensitive pre-briefing and debriefing practices to support psychological safety in simulation-based assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that educators establish an informative, purposeful pre-briefing that clarifies the educational setting and social contract, and use debriefing to encourage students' emotional expression and build rapport, since "women are more sensitive to interpersonal relationships". They also recommend a fiction contract in pre-briefing outlining student roles so students can immerse themselves "without fear of judgment", and tailored support recognizing gender differences in learning preferences and communication styles.
