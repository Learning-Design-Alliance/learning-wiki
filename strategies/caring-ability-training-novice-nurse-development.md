# Integrate caring-ability training into novice nurse development with theory-practice curricula and senior-nurse mentorship

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding caring-ability development in early-career nursing education: "caring ability training should be integrated into novice nurse development, theory-practice curricula should be designed, and senior nurse mentorship with individualized guidance should be implemented." Concretely, this includes professional caring-ability courses, a mentor framework with personalized guidance and timely feedback, and optimized course designs increasing practical instruction such as clinical communication case analyses, conflict simulation exercises, and caring-ability enhancement workshops. The aim is to raise communication competence, which the regression linked to caring ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to add caring-ability courses and a mentor framework for new nurses
#### Constraints
- Rationale is cross-sectional correlational evidence; the article states it cannot confirm a causal relationship between caring ability and communication competence

### Target Learners
- novice and junior nurses in tertiary hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- caring ability
- clinical communication competency
