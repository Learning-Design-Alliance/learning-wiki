# Build structured feedback mechanisms, explicit team formation and clear workload expectations into active learning environments in public health education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on its perceptual findings, the review recommends implementation design changes: "the need for explicit team formation strategies, clear workload expectations and structured facilitation to optimise the student experience", and that "structured feedback mechanisms be built explicitly into active learning environments across all three modalities". It further recommends adapting approaches to local context and resource availability, and tailoring cases to local health challenges in online delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty training and manageable student-to-instructor ratios for online delivery
#### Constraints
- Challenges are compounded in resource-limited settings; findings may have limited applicability in low-resource educational settings

### Target Learners
- public health students at bachelor's and master's levels

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and participation
- equitable team participation
- professional competency development
