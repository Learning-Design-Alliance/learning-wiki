# Simulate the direct pathophysiological impact of COVID-19 on obstetric patients: symptom recognition, modified early warning systems, respiratory compromise, critical care collaboration, and pregnant-patient ACLS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond infection-control processes, the article lists key simulation considerations for the direct impact of the novel pathogen in obstetrics: recognition of symptoms of COVID-19 and appropriate diagnosis and workup, best use of a modified obstetric early warning system to detect maternal deterioration, approach to maternal respiratory compromise, collaboration with intensive care teams when critical care is needed, and simulation of advanced cardiac life support protocols in the pregnant patient. Each theme lends itself to repeated practice with several variations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation exercises developed with several variations that may be practised repeatedly
#### Constraints
- The article notes the disease course does not appear to be more severe in the pregnant population than in the general population, but the possibility of severe disease remains

### Target Learners
- obstetric teams
- critical care teams

### Target Learning Goals
- maternal deterioration detection
- maternal respiratory compromise management
- pregnant-patient advanced cardiac life support
