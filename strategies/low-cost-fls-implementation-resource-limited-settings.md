# Implement FLS-style simulation training with low-cost materials in resource-limited settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that countries with limited resources implement the fundamentals of laparoscopic surgery using low-cost materials: "implementing FLS using low-cost materials can provide a solution to this problem that hinders the training of young residents." They also encourage combining simulators: "we encourage the conjunction of the different simulators for a complete training when it is possible."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A low-cost trainer box with webcam-based visualization and low-cost consumables
#### Constraints
- High fidelity simulators and animal models offer more realistic situations and variability; the conjunction of simulators is encouraged only when possible

### Target Learners
- young surgical residents in resource-limited settings

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition and evaluation of technical laparoscopic surgery skills
