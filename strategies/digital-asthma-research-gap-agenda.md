# Future research agenda: detailed BCT-coded systematic review, primary qualitative examination of patient experience, and economic analysis within trials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose a three-part agenda to address gaps their metareview exposed: first, a detailed systematic review of currently available digital asthma interventions "detailing the presence or absence of BCTs"; second, examination of the primary qualitative literature on the patient perspective to inform future interventions; and third, greater emphasis on economic analysis within trials. This agenda responds to findings that interventions were poorly described, patient perspectives largely ignored, and no cost-effectiveness data existed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Primary trials must report intervention components and behavior change techniques for the proposed review to succeed
#### Constraints
- Applies to digital asthma self-management interventions; the authors note no firm conclusions on cost-effectiveness can be drawn without routine health care resource utilization data

### Target Learners
- adults and children with asthma
- researchers designing digital self-management interventions

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying active ingredients of digital self-management support
- understanding patient experience of digital asthma tools
