# Provide user-controlled intradural pressure and variable tear locations to tailor simulation difficulty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A trainee feedback section highlights that "the ability to control intradural pressure and tailor the difficulty level by creating dural tears in different locations throughout the simulated dura has been instrumental in refining my microsurgical techniques". Flow rate from a suspended saline bag, regulated with an IV roller clamp, sets intradural pressure, letting instructors scale task difficulty for learners.
