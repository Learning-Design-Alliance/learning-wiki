# Direct student research with remote sensing data along three directions, including dynamic maps of environmental change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article outlines three directions for student research work with Earth remote sensing data: territorial research aimed at solving specific regional problems such as monitoring natural and anthropogenic areas; compilation and refinement of cartographic materials and registration of the land fund; and detailed study of space images to identify atypical or unique objects and processes on the Earth's surface. Analyzing time-varying images of the same territory lets students create dynamic maps reflecting environmental violations, human-induced changes and deforestation.
