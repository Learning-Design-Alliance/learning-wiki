# Open a group tutoring project with brainstorming, snowball and related techniques at the first group meeting to make teaching dynamic rather than static

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable opening activity for group tutoring: the author integrated techniques from the pedagogy course into his personal method by introducing "brainstorming , snowball and related techniques" at the first group meeting of the project course. He reports students accepted the idea enthusiastically, liking to experiment with new forms of learning just as teachers experiment with new forms of teaching.
