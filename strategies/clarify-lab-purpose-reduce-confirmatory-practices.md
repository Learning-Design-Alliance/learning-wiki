# Clarify lab purpose, prompt critical consideration of model limitations, and emphasize uncertainty reduction to reduce confirmatory practices in experimentation-focused labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that confirmatory goals dominated student questionable research practices, the authors propose instructional responses in experimentation-focused labs. They suggest that "clarifying the purpose of the lab to students, asking them to think critically about the limitations of simple models, or constantly strive to reduce uncertainty, could affect student practices." The strategy targets the confirmatory framing students bring from verification-style labs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must explicitly communicate the lab's purpose since students may assume labs verify known results
#### Constraints
- The article states it is unclear whether instructional differences change the frequency of practices or only how often they are reported in lab notes; the proposals are possible influences, not tested interventions

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students in experimentation-focused labs

### Target Learning Goals
- responsible conduct of research
- authentic scientific practice
- evidence-based reasoning about models
