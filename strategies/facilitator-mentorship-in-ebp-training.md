# Pair EBP lectures and practice with facilitator mentorship and consultation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors conclude that mentorship is a key design ingredient of EBP training: facilitators "not only guide participants to complete research article searches and interpretations but also facilitate participants to solve technical difficulties encountered in actual practice." Based on prior findings that "mentorship programs represent an important tool in promoting EBP-related beliefs, knowledge, and acutalization," they state that "mentors and facilitators may be essential to designing EBP training programs." The program operationalized this as ten hours of consultation plus four mentored teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-trained evidence-based nursing facilitators, with a facilitator-to-participant ratio of 10 participants per facilitator group as implemented here
#### Constraints
- Barriers-facilitators scores stayed below 70 even after training, suggesting organizational and structural support beyond training may be needed

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses in hospitals, including those without prior EBP training

### Target Learning Goals
- applying research evidence in clinical practice, building EBP knowledge, skills, and beliefs
