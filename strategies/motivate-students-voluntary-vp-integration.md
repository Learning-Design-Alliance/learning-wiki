# Motivate students and lower access barriers when integrating voluntary virtual patients into a course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their implementation experience, the authors recommend that educators introducing virtual patients "should focus on stimulation of students’ motivation to participate in the project", starting the course with a dedicated pilot activity showing efficient software use, and making teachers enthusiastic promoters of the activity. They further suggest simplifying the login process as much as possible and enabling the VP application on mobile devices, since students reported the complex login and poor Internet access as barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated pilot learning activity to show students how to use the new software efficiently
#### Constraints
- Some students indicated problems accessing VPs because of Internet connection or computer access limitations

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- integration of virtual patients with instructor-led courses
