# Culturally appropriate Tier 1 attendance supports: recognize good and improved attendance, hold regular check-ins, and adapt school calendars

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends expanding Tier 1 supports when chronic absenteeism affects many students, since high rates may indicate Tier 1 supports are not designed to meet AI/AN students' needs. Recommended practices include recognizing good and improved attendance with low-cost and culturally significant items such as hand drums or beaded necklaces, holding regular morning check-ins so students feel seen and appreciated, and adapting school calendars so attendance at important cultural events is exempt rather than marked absent. Partnerships with local dentists and pediatricians can schedule appointments during school breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Positive reinforcement of attainable goals to build the habit of attendance
- Collaboration between school districts and tribal governments to negotiate calendar flexibility

### Target Learners
- AI/AN K-12 students in districts and tribal communities

### Target Learning Goals
- Regular attendance and feeling welcomed and known at school
