# ITPC-framed CL lesson sequence with warm-up, image-schema instruction, controlled practice and productive tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study applied the integrated text and picture comprehension (ITPC) model to frame instruction, with three main class activities: "warm-up, instruction with image schemas, and controlled practice", and added two productive tasks (a speaking task and a writing task) after instruction. Each class met once a week for approximately 90 minutes over 4 sessions. In CL lessons, gap-fill answers were given as image schemas and each preposition was taught with three examples illustrated by real-life pictures pointing to the image schema.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training was administered before the pilot study, and handouts, PowerPoint files and test instruments were amended after pilot feedback
#### Constraints
- Teacher-fronted time was the main input, and language fluency was not a focus of the treatment

### Target Learners
- Vietnamese first-year university EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- spatial and metaphorical meanings of English prepositions

### Affordances
- Domain Mapping Theory Prepositions
