# Structure sensory-room lessons in stages: affirmation-based opening, relaxation, activation exercises, and reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's lessons followed a recurring structure: at the beginning of every lesson adolescents talked about themselves while handing over massage balls, with affirmation used when working with pupils; a relaxation stage used visualization and breathing control exercises; an activation stage used interaction exercises to overcome psycho-emotional tension and increase trust; and each lesson ended with reflection, which the article calls a new formation for adolescents. The authors report that gradually adolescents started to speak more willingly and joined new activities with higher enthusiasm.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A group of classmates serving as a reference environment
- Sensory-room facilities supporting relaxation and tactile engagement
#### Constraints
- The article links this structure to its own 8-lesson program with 7th-grade pupils; generalization beyond this setting is not tested

### Target Learners
- adolescents working in class groups

### Target Learning Goals
- talking about oneself and upgrading self-evaluation
- overcoming psycho-emotional tension
- trust and cooperation within the group
