# Add instructor-designed prompt scaffolds, verification steps, and instructor oversight to structured AI-use implementations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on students' reported concerns, the article recommends two implementation supports for structured AI use. First, the mismatch between AI-generated solutions and in-class methods "suggesting that instructor-designed prompt scaffolds could improve alignment with course expectations". Second, concerns about AI accuracy "highlight the importance of verification steps and instructor oversight in future implementations". Students themselves suggested incorporating human oversight or instructor validation to improve reliability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-designed prompt scaffolds aligned with course methods
- Verification steps for AI-generated solutions
- Instructor oversight of AI use
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from student-reported concerns in a single-institution sample skewed toward engineering majors, which may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics students using generative AI tools

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate, course-aligned problem-solving support
- Trustworthy integration of AI into study practices
