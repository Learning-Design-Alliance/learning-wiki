# Introduce a rigid renewal system of the teaching certificate requiring natural-science exams, with retry opportunities and self-study support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To cure teachers' physics-phobia, the authors propose increasing the natural-science share and passing score of the teaching-certificate exam, increasing compulsory undergraduate science subjects, and introducing certificate renewal with rigid assessment. They argue short SPP workshops cannot raise teachers' scholastic ability, that step-by-step exercise solving is more effective, and that teachers cannot renew their license without passing the test, with retries allowed and self-study programs developed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A renewal system with rigid assessment of natural-science knowledge; programs encouraging teachers' self-study; allowance for disqualified teachers to retry
#### Constraints
- In Japan it is highly controversial whether to introduce a renewal system of the teaching certificate

### Target Learners
- current elementary and secondary school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- scholastic ability in natural science, especially physics
