# Targeted interventions to enhance clinical preparation, plus mentorship and education–practice collaboration, to bolster nurse interns' readiness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends targeted interventions that strengthen clinical preparation as the direct lever for practice readiness, since perceived preparation significantly correlated with readiness. The practice implications section names three concrete supports: "Providing ongoing professional development opportunities" for nurse interns, "Encouraging collaboration between nursing education programs and healthcare institutions", and establishing mentorship programs that "can provide invaluable support for nurse interns".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires coordination between nursing education programs and healthcare institutions to foster a seamless transition from education to practice.
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a cross-sectional perception study using purposive sampling; the authors call for larger randomized-sampling studies across different Saudi colleges.

### Target Learners
- Nurse interns in the internship year of baccalaureate nursing programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical preparation and readiness for professional nursing practice
