# Use a combined teaching model mixing PBL with lecture-based and digital teaching, adapted to the local setting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (7 for, 2 mixed, 2 against) · 6 studies (6 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 3 of 6 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review observes that PBL "is often combined with other teaching methods, such as LBL and digital teaching", and that digital PBL may outperform traditional learning or traditional PBL for improving skills. It concludes a combined teaching model can be used in education, particularly in limited-resource medical colleges, and that non-Western adopters should modify PBL rather than transfer it unchanged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must adapt adoption methods to their particular setting rather than lifting PBL unchanged from its Western environment of origin
#### Constraints
- The current application of PBL in surgical education has not been widely or thoroughly studied

### Target Learners
- medical students in limited-resource medical colleges
- nursing and dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills
- critical thinking
- learning efficiency
