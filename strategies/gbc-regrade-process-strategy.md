# Use a re-grade request process that requires students to identify their category and re-solve the problem correctly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GBC affords a simple re-grade process: if students feel their response was incorrectly categorized, they fill out a form stating the category they were placed in and the category they believe they should be in, or why the category should receive more points, and they must also solve the problem correctly. This puts the burden of proof on students to show their understanding instead of the instructor, and relieves some of the agony of assigning partial credit because instructors need not decipher difficult-to-follow solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students requesting a re-grade must fill out a form identifying their assigned category and the category they believe they should be placed in, and must solve the problem correctly.
#### Constraints
- In the authors' experience students are less likely to challenge a grade with this method, but whether this is because they understand their score or because effort is required of them has yet to be investigated.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in large science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- encouraging students to explain their reasoning and demonstrate understanding of exam material
