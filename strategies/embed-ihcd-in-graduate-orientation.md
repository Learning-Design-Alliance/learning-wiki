# Embed I&HCD content in graduate school orientation so students benefit without additional courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's curricular recommendation drawn from both its quantitative results and student reflections: "embedding I&HCD content in graduate school orientation allows students to benefit without enrolling in additional courses or participating in activities outside the academic curriculum." Students supported "the integration of I&HCD across educational and employment settings and requested more I&HCD experiences," reflecting that they wished they had encountered such content earlier in their careers. The strategy relies on the experiential learning format and coaches facilitating small-group activities that students credited for their learning.
