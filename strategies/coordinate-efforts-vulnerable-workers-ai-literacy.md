# Coordinate cross-sector efforts to address AI literacy gaps, focusing on vulnerable workers lacking foundational digital literacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that government agencies, policymakers, advocacy organizations, foundations, and non-profits "coordinate efforts to address AI literacy gaps, particularly focusing on vulnerable workers who lack foundational digital literacy skills." It proposes targeted training initiatives and awareness campaigns, and calls on state educational agencies to establish train-the-trainer programs, develop comprehensive materials, and offer micro-credentialing opportunities focused on digital and AI literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- coordination across government agencies, policymakers, advocacy organizations, foundations, and non-profits
#### Constraints
- access concerns to infrastructure and materials such as hardware, software, and WiFi often occur in certain geographic areas of the country

### Target Learners
- vulnerable workers who lack foundational digital literacy skills
- workers facing access barriers

### Target Learning Goals
- foundational digital and AI literacy
- equitable access to AI-enabled tools
