# Introduce additional recoil systems (sprinkler, swing, DC motor, atomic recoil) as deep thought-provoking exercises after the core explanation

## Description
Once recoil in firing systems is fully explained, the article recommends introducing other systems undergoing recoil "in the form of deep, thought-provoking exercises." Four systems are presented: a rotary lawn sprinkler (force from water pushing on sidewalls), a swing on a freely moving stand (component of gravitational force on the bob along the rod), a DC motor at start-up (interaction between rotor magnetic field and stator magnetic dipoles), and atomic recoil from spontaneous photon emission (disturbance in electrostatic force between nucleus and electron).
