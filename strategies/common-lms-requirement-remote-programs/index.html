Require one common school-wide learning management system for any program using remote learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In its instructional materials dimension, the blueprint issues a single, concrete operational directive: "Require all teachers to use one common, school-wide Learning Management System (LMS) for any program that will use a remote learning environment." This standardizes the platform through which content is delivered to students across synchronous and asynchronous opportunities, digital or in-person, while keeping the teacher as the driver of delivery.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructional materials must be aligned to adopted academic standards with proper scope, sequence, and rigor, and the teacher must be the driver of the delivery

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students and teachers in programs with any remote learning component

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Consistent delivery of standards-aligned instructional content across remote and blended environments
