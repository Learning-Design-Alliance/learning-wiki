# Future social presence research should use solid conceptualization, report instrument validity, consider media generalization, and use diverse measurement approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review endorses Cui's (2013) call that 'future research on social presence and the development of effective instrumentation to measure the construct [that] should (a) have a solid conceptualization of social presence, (b) clearly report instrument validity and reliability in published research', (c) take media concerns and generalization into consideration, and (d) assess social presence with diverse approaches. The authors chose this path over 'killing' social presence, resolving the confounding situation by disentangling the original theory instead.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers must adopt a solid conceptualization of social presence before selecting or developing instruments
#### Constraints
- The review warns future research is at risk if the confounding situation continues to exist

### Target Learning Goals
