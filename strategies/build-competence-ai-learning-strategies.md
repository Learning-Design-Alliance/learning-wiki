# Build students' competence in AI-enriched environments by choosing appropriate tools, giving clear guidance, establishing role models, and offering frequent encouragement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Given competence need satisfaction's highest centrality among correlates, the authors recommend that educators "may consider choosing appropriate AI tools, providing clear guidance, establishing role models, and offering timely and frequent encouragement" to satisfy students' competence needs. They add that selecting chatbots based on students' competencies is preferable, that observing proficient peers can enhance beliefs in one's own competence and increase effort, and that tertiary educators should foster students' sense of agency in human-AI interactions.
