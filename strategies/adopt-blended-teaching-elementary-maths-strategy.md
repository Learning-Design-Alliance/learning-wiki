# Adopt blended teaching in elementary mathematics and provide the resources and staff to support it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's results, the researcher recommends using blended teaching by elementary grade teachers and other grade levels because it "assist in supporting the achievement of the students and improves the quality of teaching", and recommends providing the resources and staff required for supporting its use. Implementation in the study required qualified teachers (ICDL and Intel certificates), available and ready computers and monitors, a computerized unit with activities and printed materials, and supervised instruction time for both methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers qualified through ICDL and Intel Certificate holding
- Availability and readiness of computers, monitors, and a computerized unit about Numbers below 9999, with activities and printed materials
- Supervised instruction time for both traditional and blended teaching methods
#### Constraints
- The study was limited to two schools in the Bani Kenanah directorate, first semester 2015/2016, one educational unit (numbers below 9999), third graders, and PowerPoint as the technology

### Target Learners
- third grade students
- elementary grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement in the numbers below 9999 unit
- improved quality of teaching
