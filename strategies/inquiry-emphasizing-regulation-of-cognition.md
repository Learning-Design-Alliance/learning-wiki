# Develop inquiry-based learning that explicitly emphasizes regulation of cognition, especially information management, monitoring, evaluating, and debugging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend building on the inquiry learning model by making regulation of cognition an explicit, maximized component. They state it is "necessary to develop a learning model by making the inquiry learning model as a specific basic model emphasizing on students to carry out maximally the process of regulation of cognition, especially towards the aspects of IMS, M, E, and D", to be achieved "through integration of possible strategies, such as cognitive conflict, non -routine concept presentation, or implementing portfolio -based performance". This follows from their finding that inconsistent impacts traced to differences in these regulation abilities.
