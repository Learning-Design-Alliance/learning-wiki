# Use construct maps to develop and test hypotheses about TPACK development and refine measurement tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that researchers use the construct maps to help develop and test hypotheses about how teachers' TPACK Confidence or Usefulness develops, to refine and improve evidence gathering, and to ensure tools provide appropriate opportunities to measure changes in proficiency pre- and post-intervention. The authors note the maps support efforts to "improve the validity and precision of TPACK survey tools."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers gather evidence using tools sensitive enough to identify differences between respondents at different proficiency levels
#### Constraints
- The maps describe TPACK Confidence and Usefulness for a 'teaching content' aspect only; further research is needed to develop maps for other TPACK components and contexts

### Target Learners
- Education researchers
- Practicing teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Investigating and measuring teachers' TPACK proficiency development
