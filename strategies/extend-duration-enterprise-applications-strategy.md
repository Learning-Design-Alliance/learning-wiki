# Extend workshop duration and emphasize practical, enterprise-relevant applications in future iterations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the IT-Tage implementation, the authors recommend that future workshops strengthen the emphasis on practical, enterprise-relevant applications, for example by incorporating a broader range of illustrative examples in the introductory sessions. They further suggest adapting the concept to specific industry domains such as finance, logistics or manufacturing, integrating it into corporate training programs, defining role-specific quantum computing skills, developing tools such as quizzes to assess learning success, and pursuing standardized certifications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshop leaders with complementary subject-matter specializations to cover the required breadth of knowledge
#### Constraints
- A single-day format tends to be too limited in duration, especially for participants with little or no prior knowledge

### Target Learners
- IT professionals, developers, architects and decision-makers in industry

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical application of quantum computing knowledge in enterprise contexts
