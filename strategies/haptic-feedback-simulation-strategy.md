# Add tactile/haptic feedback channels (vibration, force, thermal, vibrotactile pulsation cues) to simulation training, potentially integrated with existing AR/VR settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes haptic technologies as an alternative or complement to audiovisual feedback, noting touch "typically requires lower cognitive efforts as compared to audio/visual feedback". Roboticist solutions cited include vibration for directional cues guiding the trainee's arm, force stimuli such as an engineered fabric band squeezing the arm to code alert signals, fingertip vibration replicating arterial pulsations during palpation, and voice coil motors reproducing surface contact and roughness with thermal displays. The authors recommend investigating integrating haptics into existing AR/VR settings. Pilot studies in medical settings are cited as background, not re-analysed.
