# Write multi-qubit states stacked above one another to avoid tensor notation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For multi-qubit calculations such as teleportation, the article recommends writing the qubit states stacked above one another instead of using tensor notation: "Um eine Tensorschreibweise zu vermeiden, empfehlen wir, die QuBit-Zustände übereinander zu schreiben." This makes it iconically plausible how measuring qubits that initially form a Bell state projects the state. During the taught unit, the states were computed after every gate.
