# Use bilingual teaching strategies and encourage strategic L1 use in post-reading group discussions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that teachers use bilingual teaching strategies and encourage the strategic use of the L1 in the L2 classroom, specifically by allowing post-reading group discussions in learners' L1 before written recalls. In their study this let learners discuss longer, in greater depth, and access higher-order L1 reading strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners sharing a common L1
- Structured post-reading discussion followed by individual written recall
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a small cohort of fifteen intermediate-level learners; the authors state broad conclusions cannot be drawn

### Target Learners
- Intermediate-level L2 learners at university

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 reading comprehension
- genre awareness
- comprehension monitoring

### Affordances
- [Languaging Sociocultural L1 Reading](../theories/sociocultural-theory.md)

## Claims

- [Collaborative group discussion in general improved detail and comprehension of L2 texts relative to no discussion](../claims/group-discussion-facilitates-comprehension.md) [+W]
- [L1 discussion groups showed the greatest awareness of textual genre in written recalls](../claims/l1-discussion-genre-awareness.md) [+W]
- [L1 post-reading group discussions lead to greater mention of reading comprehension strategies and constituent elements in L2 written recalls than L2 discussion or no discussion](../claims/l1-discussion-greater-strategy-recall.md) [+W]
- [Only the L1 discussion group showed higher-order processing such as comprehension monitoring, autobiographical connections, and questioning textual reliability](../claims/l1-discussion-higher-order-processing.md) [+W]
- [L1 discussion groups spoke longer and produced more discussion words than L2 discussion groups](../claims/l1-discussion-longer-more-words.md) [+W]
