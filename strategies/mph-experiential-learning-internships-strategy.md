# Add experiential learning, internships and real-world attachments to MPH programs to link theory and practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Graduates proposed innovative course-organisation changes enabling students to engage with real public health work: "including internships and practicals, where they deepened competencies, through applying skills in projects identified by hosts". Suggested exposures included local public health departments, industry visits to detect hazards, and engagement with alumni and experts. Learning from academic and practical exposures should be integrated, e.g. via a capstone assignment applying skills to a public health scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Host organisations and projects must be identified for practical placements
#### Constraints
- Recommendations come from 36 respondents giving free comments; they differed by institution (p = 0.023)

### Target Learners
- MPH students in South Africa

### Target Learning Goals
- Applied public health competencies
- problem solving
- project management
