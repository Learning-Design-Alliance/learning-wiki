# Support a long-term community engagement program whose form best matches the partner institution, such as guest speaker programs, outreach seminar courses, internships, or joint events

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The whitepaper proposes that "Along­termcommunityengagementprogramshouldbesupported,withtheformchosentobest­matchthe partner institution." It enumerates example forms: a guest speaker program where astronomical society members host public viewing or planetarium shows; a seminar course for undergraduates on public understanding of science with outreach participation as a course requirement; internships or co-ops in which education students develop or present K-12 outreach events; and events programs at the intersection of the amateur astronomy and instructional communities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A partner institution and community organization able to host the chosen program form
#### Constraints
- The form should be chosen to best match the partner institution; some event programs may already be in place

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- education students
- K-12 students
- general public

### Target Learning Goals
- public understanding of science
- astronomy education
- practical outreach skills for education students
