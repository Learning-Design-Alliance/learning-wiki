# Student research project: topic selection, scaffolded reading of related Astrobites articles, and a paper or presentation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Method 2 asks students to "select a research topic and then identify and read several Astrobites articles related to that topic," then prepare a short written paper (4-10 pages) or a 5-10 minute presentation. Optional tasks include an annotated bibliography collected via Google Form and shared with the class, a concept graph of prerequisite curriculum concepts, and a research timeline reconstructed from source-paper citations. In advanced courses students read the original source papers, with Astrobites as scaffolding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Astrobites articles and their source papers
- Instructor guidance on the accessibility and level of detail expected of the paper or presentation
#### Constraints
- The article states this method "is perhaps best suited to the upper-level undergraduate course"

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate students, with adaptations from introductory undergraduate through graduate level

### Target Learning Goals
- synthesis of multiple sources into an original work
- interpreting data visualizations and statistical graphics
- reading comprehension
