# Incorporate NIHSS training into EM residency programs without delaying stroke-care metrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that NIHSS training be established within emergency medicine residency programs because its QI initiative "demonstrated that it is possible to incorporate NIHSS training within EM residency training without having the detrimental effect of prolonging stroke metrics". This prepares graduates who plan to practice in community settings, where EM physicians commonly use the NIHSS but neurology residents are often unavailable. The strategy pairs certification with supervised dual scoring to build scoring proficiency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- NIHSS certification for all residents and attendings (6-part 8-hour AHA online module)
- Paired scoring with neurology residents to allow side-by-side comparison and discussion of scoring differences
#### Constraints
- Generalizability is limited by the study's setting: an urban, Level I trauma center and public safety-net hospital with 24-hour neurology consultation
- More studies are needed to further investigate the relationship between training and stroke metrics

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine residents

### Target Learning Goals
- independent, timely NIHSS assessment of acute ischemic stroke in community emergency departments
