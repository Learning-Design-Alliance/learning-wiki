Conduct further simulation studies to map boundary conditions of the rank ANCOVA power advantage¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend extending the simulation program to identify when the rank transform approach's power advantage holds. They write it "maybe fruitful to conduct further simulation studies to determinethe boundary conditions" on combinations of skewness, heteroscedasticity, strength of relationship and sample size. They also suggest including unequal group frequencies and several degrees of group differences so empirical power curves can be constructed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Simulation designs varying skewness, heteroscedasticity, strength of relationship, and sample size in combination

Constraints¶

  • The present study's unequal-group condition had a total frequency of only 15, which the authors suggest may explain the absent power advantage there

Target Learners¶

  • Educational researchers analyzing pretest-posttest randomized control group designs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Appropriate choice between parametric and rank-transform ANCOVA for analyzing pretest-posttest data
