# Use best-practice video review with instructor explanation as formative feedback between simulation runs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Immediately after the first simulation, the instructor played a recording of the team that demonstrated the highest ACLS performance and explained effective aspects of the leader's role, assessment, CPR rotation, medication administration, defibrillator use, intubation, and communication. Teams then discussed their own roles and interventions with this feedback before rerunning the scenario. The authors report this "provided feedback and guide[d] the students through the process of self-reflection".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A recorded best-practice simulation video and an instructor who highlights effective aspects and observed issues
#### Constraints
- This study used the video of the best-performing team from the same cohort; generalization to other video sources is not tested

### Target Learners
- Nursing students in team-based simulation

### Target Learning Goals
- ACLS clinical performance, teamwork, and communication skills
