# Turn-around Time

## Description
Providing timely feedback is crucial for learners to reflect, correct, and extend their performance. Avoiding long delays between assignment submission and feedback delivery helps prevent content disconnection and student isolation, especially in online courses. Short turn-around times support a mentoring or coaching relationship, facilitating a more connected learning environment. Learners should be able to use feedback to influence their future work effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to prioritize grading and feedback, and to set clear expectations for turn-around times. Utilizing efficient grading methods and tools can help expedite the process.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for instructors with heavy workloads or large class sizes. Requires careful planning and time management to ensure timely feedback.
