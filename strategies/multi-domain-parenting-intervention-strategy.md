# Parenting and child-focused interventions should target multiple parenting behaviors and multiple domains of child functioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that accumulated parenting risk rather than any single behavior was linked to poorer child functioning, the authors recommend that "parenting interventions to prevent child maladjustment should target a variety of parenting behaviors such as providing parents with tools to create an organized and stimulating environment, and promoting the importance of consistency and warmth." They further recommend interventions reducing mothers' mental burden and parenting stress, and compensatory interventions targeting several domains of child functioning at once, citing social-emotional learning approaches shown to improve adaptive school behaviors, promote behavioral regulation, and reduce depressive symptoms.
