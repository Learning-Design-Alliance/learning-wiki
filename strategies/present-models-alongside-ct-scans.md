# Present 3D-printed anatomical models alongside corresponding CT scans to stakeholders for comparative evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's method of presenting CT scans and 3D-printed models together, showing "Screenshots of the CT scans showing coronary anatomy were displayed, followed by introduction of the model which was then passed around and examined." This allowed stakeholders to draw comparisons on effectiveness between the two modalities. A presentation lasting approximately one hour was given to researchers, with a similar drop-in session for clinicians.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both CT imaging data and physical 3D models available for the same cases
- A structured questionnaire to collect comparative feedback
#### Constraints
- The researcher administering the survey had no prior relationship with participants

### Target Learners
- clinicians
- cardiovascular researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating the educational and clinical value of 3D-printed anatomical models
