# Use text production as a vocabulary-retention intervention for mature adult (30–40-year-old) language learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends text production as an intervention measure for mature adult learners: "As adults learn a new language, especially analytically and by means of rules and their needs, text production could be a good intervention measure for learners in this age group." In the study, learners completed all three steps of text-oriented vocabulary exercises and produced a text at the end, which took thirty minutes longer than the control condition, and this group showed significant retention benefits at all four test intervals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must complete all three steps of the text-oriented vocabulary exercises and produce a text at the end of the learning phase
#### Constraints
- The benefit was demonstrated only for the 30–40-year-old group, whose experimental cell was small (n = 5); the article asks how the same positive impact could be achieved in other age groups

### Target Learners
- mature adult GFL learners aged 30 to 40

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of textually learned vocabulary in short, medium and long term

### Affordances
- [Kuhn Three Step Vocabulary Teaching Model](../designs/kuhn-three-step-vocabulary-teaching-model.md)
