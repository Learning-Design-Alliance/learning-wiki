Adopt through-year assessments that combine interim and summative functions to increase instructional relevance¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report describes through-year assessments as an innovation many states and vendors are developing and field-testing: students take short assessments over the course of the year, typically 3-5 times, whose results are either compiled into a single summative score or inform the content of a shorter year-end assessment. Educators and school leaders quickly receive during-year results that can inform instructional adjustments or identify students for additional supports. The report states "Through-year assessments, in effect, combine interim and summative assessments into a single, coherent set of assessments" and encourages further development so they serve instructional use while producing valid, reliable data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Results must reach educators quickly enough to adjust instruction during the year
  • Assessments must produce valid and reliable data for state and district leaders while serving instructional use

Constraints¶

  • As currently designed, statewide summative assessments are not intended for real-time instructional adjustments; the report says it seems worth exploring if or how they might be used in the ways educators seek

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in states adopting through-year designs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • grade-level content mastery
  • timely instructional adjustment
