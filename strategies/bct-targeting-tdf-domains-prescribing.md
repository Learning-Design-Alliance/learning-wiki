# Design prescribing-safety interventions around behaviour-change techniques matched to the relevant TDF domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that interventions to improve trainee doctors' prescribing pair behaviour-change techniques with the theoretical domains identified as relevant: interventions “could include a number of behaviour-change techniques that target these domains.” Table 3 maps example techniques to domains, including prompts, triggers, and cues for behavioral regulation; feedback about prescribing errors made for beliefs about capabilities; persuasive communication for beliefs about consequences; and rehearsal of appropriate prescribing behaviors for skills. Environmental changes such as reducing distractions and redesigning prescription charts are also recommended.
