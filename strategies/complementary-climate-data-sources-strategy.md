# Use districtwide surveys and school-developed measures complementarily at different stages of improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that large-scale districtwide surveys and school-developed tools be used for different purposes at different stages of school improvement. "While large-scale surveys work well to diagnose problem areas at schools, they may not be ideal tools for schools to assess the effectiveness of newly implemented strategies and interventions." Large-scale surveys typically measure perceptions, while school-developed tools assess presence of and exposure to school practices. States and districts can support schools by communicating when to use each source and how to develop practical school-level measures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- States and districts communicate to schools when to use different data sources and how to develop practical, school-level climate measures meeting their specific needs
#### Constraints
- Large-scale surveys may not be ideal for assessing the effectiveness of newly implemented strategies and interventions

### Target Learners
- middle school students
- school and district leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing school climate problems and evaluating climate interventions
