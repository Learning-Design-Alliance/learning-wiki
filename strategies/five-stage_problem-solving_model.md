# Five-Stage Problem-Solving Model

## Description
A five-stage model to help learners understand, identify, and resolve issues. This involves understanding the problem by framing it in their own words, describing any barriers preventing them from achieving their goal, identifying various solutions, trying out a solution while keeping accurate records of their thoughts and procedures, and evaluating the results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need to understand the five stages of the model and be willing to engage in self-reflection and evaluation.
#### Constraints
- May be too rigid for some learners. Requires learners to actively engage in self-assessment.
