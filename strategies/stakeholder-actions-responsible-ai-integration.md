# Stakeholder actions for responsible AI integration: funding, policy, organizations, departments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report closes with urgent actions by stakeholder to ensure AI integration into science education and research is responsible and generative. Funding bodies should support faster, higher-risk research, invest in open infrastructure, establish continuous 24/7 inference infrastructure for education, and require ethical and environmental accountability. Educational policy should integrate AI concepts within science disciplines rather than as isolated content, redesign assessment frameworks to emphasize reasoning, modeling, and explanation over easily automated outcomes, and ensure equity of access. Professional organizations should accelerate dissemination and build shared repositories; departments should embed AI literacy in teacher education and reframe researcher training.
