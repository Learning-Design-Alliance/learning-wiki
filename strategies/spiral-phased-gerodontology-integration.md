# Spiral, phased integration of gerodontology content across all curriculum phases with regular assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating gerodontology learning content across the whole dental curriculum rather than in one isolated course: "it is advisable to integrate the learning content into the curriculum in a spiral, and to administer regular assessments". Content on DOP as a vulnerable, growing population is placed in semesters 1–4 to reduce fear of contact; communication and oral hygiene practice for DOP enters phantom courses in semesters 5–6; and clinical training, interdisciplinary modules and outreach experiences dominate semesters 7–10.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination with core competencies of other specialties to ensure coherence and avoid redundancies
#### Constraints
- One standalone course (the mandatory interdisciplinary lecture) will not provide a comprehensive curriculum; crowded curricula required the LOs to be designed as easily adaptable

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate dental students in Germany across all study phases (semesters 1–10)

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for dental care of care‐dependent older people, including reducing fear of contact and developing treatment strategies
