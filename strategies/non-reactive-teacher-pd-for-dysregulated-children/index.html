Provide early-grades teachers professional development in non-reactive engagement with dysregulated children and emotionally supportive classroom practices¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that teachers of young children receive professional development to improve classroom climate and engage dysregulated children in non-reactive ways, because conflictual relationships persist over time and affect academic trajectories. It also promotes "emotionally supportive classrooms and learner-centered practices" so children feel safe and take academic risks, and identifies strategies for promoting self-regulatory skills before kindergarten entry.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers of young children in kindergarten and first grade classrooms

Constraints¶

  • The article notes causality cannot be ascertained due to omitted variable bias, such as variables related to teacher's own beliefs and competencies

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten and first-grade children, particularly boys and children with low self-regulation

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-regulation skills, school engagement, and academic competence in early elementary school

Related Strategies¶
