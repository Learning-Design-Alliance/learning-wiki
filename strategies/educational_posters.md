# Educational Posters

## Description
Educational posters are visual aids that add color and helpful resources to a classroom environment. They can inspire and remind students of key concepts, study tips, and learning strategies. By organizing information with different colored backgrounds or visualizing each type of learner with icons, educational posters can enhance understanding and memory retention. These posters serve as valuable tools for creating a visually stimulating and informative classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to design tools and printing resources. Implementation involves strategic placement and use. May require training in design and communication.
#### Constraints
- Overly complex designs can be distracting. Ineffective use of visuals can hinder understanding. Requires attention to detail and clarity.
