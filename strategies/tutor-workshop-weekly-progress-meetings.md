# Support tutors with a 2-day initial workshop plus weekly per-child progress meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Galaxy Math's professional development model combines "a 2-day initial workshop" with ongoing support: tutors "participate in weekly meetings with project coordinators to review the progress of each child." Training is provided through AIR, where implementers specify tutoring type, grade level, number of trainees, and location.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to AIR tutor training and project coordinators for weekly meetings
#### Constraints
- Training logistics require negotiating dates with a trainer

### Target Learners
- at-risk first graders

### Target Learning Goals
- fidelity and effectiveness of scripted math tutoring delivery
