# Use a triadic guiding-question checklist to design and reflect on adult learning activities across preparation, performance and reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teachers, trainers and adult educators use the guiding questions derived from the three concepts to design, develop and reflect on learning activities more effectively and in a more demand-oriented manner. The strategy operationalizes the integrated model by prompting attention to learner choice and needs, planning-monitoring-evaluation of learning processes, and reflection at every stage. It is offered as a pragmatic tool rather than a tested procedure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must work through questions covering all three concepts and all levels of the learning process: preparation, performance and reflection
