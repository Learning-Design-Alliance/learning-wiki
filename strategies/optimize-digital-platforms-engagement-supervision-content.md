# Enhance digital training platforms with interactivity, supervision, and expanded content coverage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on nurses' feedback and the authors' recommendations, digital training platforms should add interactive formats such as popular science videos to improve motivation and comprehension, strengthen supervision and instructor interaction to support learners who struggle with self-directed formats, and expand content beyond assessment to comprehensive pain management topics including interventions to improve educational continuity and clinical applicability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structures that support learners requiring more self-motivation in individual learning formats
#### Constraints
- Recommendations address limitations identified in the qualitative findings; the platform's current form lacks these enhancements

### Target Learners
- PICU nurses
- self-directed online learners in nursing education

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement, motivation, and comprehensive pain management competence
