# Evaluate efficiency and student satisfaction of a teaching-learning activity before permanently integrating it into the teaching repertoire

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators assess both efficiency and student satisfaction of each teaching-learning activity before adopting it permanently, using validated instruments such as the FLAS. It states it is 'crucial to evaluate both the efficiency and student satisfaction of each teaching‐learning activity before permanently integrating them into the teachers' repertoire.' The FLAS operationalises this by measuring students' flipped learning experience across three dimensions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated experience-assessment instrument and students who have participated in at least one flipped learning session
#### Constraints
- The article frames this in the context of nursing higher education and notes intervention studies are challenging when students can choose whether to attend class

### Target Learners
- university nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- informed decisions about integrating flipped learning into nursing education
