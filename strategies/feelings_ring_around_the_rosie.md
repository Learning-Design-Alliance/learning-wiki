# Feelings Ring Around the Rosie

## Description
During recess, learners play 'Ring Around the Rosie' while acting out different emotions. This combines play with emotional expression, helping learners connect emotions with physical movements and social interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an open space for playing 'Ring Around the Rosie.' A facilitator to guide the game and provide signals.
#### Constraints
- Requires physical mobility. Some learners may be uncomfortable expressing emotions through movement or social interaction.

### Affordances
- Combines play with emotional learning. Encourages kinesthetic learners and promotes emotional awareness through movement and social interaction.

### Personalization
- Emotions and end actions can be tailored to suit the group's needs and interests. Different variations of the song can be used.
