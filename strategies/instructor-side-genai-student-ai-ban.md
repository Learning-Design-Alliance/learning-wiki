# Use GenAI on the instructor side only, with students barred from AI tools during reasoning assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study used GenAI solely on the instructor side for instructional material development, while prohibiting student use of GenAI tools during in-class discussions and the final case-analysis assessment, so that independent clinical reasoning could be assessed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GenAI tools must be prohibited for students during in-class discussions and the final case-analysis assessment
#### Constraints
- Contamination bias was mitigated by scheduling groups in parallel time slots and an academic integrity agreement not to disseminate case details

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- independent clinical reasoning assessment
