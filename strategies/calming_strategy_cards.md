# Calming Strategy Cards

## Description
Calming strategy cards provide learners with reminders of emotional regulation strategies, such as squeezing a fidget or taking a mindfulness break, to use when feelings escalate or when they are losing control. These strategies can become part of students' long-term memory and support their emotional self-regulation through repeated use. When learners are calm, instructors should discuss feelings of anger and different options that are available for cooling down. Learners choose what strategies might suit them best, and practice these strategies during periods of mild to acute anger.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of pre-made or custom-made calming strategy cards. Requires discussion and practice to effectively implement strategies.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness may vary depending on the learner's emotional state and the appropriateness of the chosen strategy. Requires consistent practice and reinforcement.
