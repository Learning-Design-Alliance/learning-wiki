# Future mGBL research should avoid research to prove and embrace research to improve

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that future research involving mGBL instructional theories, models, or methods should avoid research to prove and embrace research to improve, citing Kozma's (2000) point that traditional experimental designs cannot accommodate the complexity of real-world situations. It advises being skeptical about research that compares a traditional approach to mGBL to prove effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Better tools for rapid mGBL development are needed to reduce the substantial instructor effort
#### Constraints
- Kozma (2000) suggests traditional experimental designs are not able to accommodate the complexity of these real-world situations

### Target Learners
- mGBL researchers
- instructional designers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving mGBL solutions up the S-curve of innovation and adoption
