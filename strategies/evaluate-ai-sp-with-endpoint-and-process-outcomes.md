# Evaluate AI standardized patient systems with both endpoint and process-sensitive outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that "Future evaluations of LLM-based simulated patient systems should therefore include both endpoint and process-sensitive outcomes." Diagnostic correctness should be complemented by measures of communication quality, history-taking completeness, information elicitation, checklist coverage, and consultation structure, especially in early clinical training where students are still learning how to conduct the consultation. Process improvements should also be evaluated alongside safeguards against over-reliance and AI-induced never-skilling for novice learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- process measures such as communication quality, history-taking completeness, and checklist coverage alongside diagnostic endpoints
#### Constraints
- the study's own comparator was structured non-LLM materials, so human-feedback comparisons remain untested; no delayed follow-up was included, so durability of gains is unknown

### Target Learners
- medical students in early clinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical interviewing performance
- communication quality
- diagnostic accuracy
