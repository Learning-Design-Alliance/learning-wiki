# Structure educational environments to facilitate role model identification and embed role models in a broader social network

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "educational environments need to be structured in ways that facilitate the identification of role models, for instance by fostering explicit reflection on role models." Given the link between role models and peer support, it further suggests that "medical programs may benefit from investing in peer mentoring, study groups, and social learning activities, thereby embedding the potential of role models within a broader social network."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Environments should provide structural opportunities for close contact with potential role models, as in apprenticeship-like, practice-based curricula
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on correlational associations; the authors call for longitudinal or experimental designs to clarify causal pathways before attributing outcomes directly to role models

### Target Learners
- medical students
- higher-education students across faculties

### Target Learning Goals
- sense of belonging, particularly peer support
- role model identification within the study program
