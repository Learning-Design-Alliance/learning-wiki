# Physik im Apollo: popular science talks on abstract topics interwoven with acrobatics and music at a local theater, with physicists or physics-connected artists on stage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Physik im Apollo puts physics on stage at the 550-seat Apollo theater in Siegen. The article states: 'Our concept is to educate the general public on abstract and difficult topics like Dark Matter in the UniverseorQuantum Computingby interweaving popular science talks with performing arts, in particular acrobatics and music.' An additional feature is that 'every artist on stage is either physicist or has a very close connection to physics'. After sold-out shows in 2023 and 2024, a third run was planned for fall 2025.
