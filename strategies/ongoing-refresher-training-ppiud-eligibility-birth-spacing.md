# Provide ongoing refresher training covering PPIUD eligibility and birth-spacing importance to sustain provider knowledge and reduce bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that PPIUD training interventions not be one-off: providers need "ongoing refresher training to ensure that providers increase and maintain their knowledge on the PPIUD", plus training content on social and medical eligibility for unmarried women and women with prior ectopic pregnancy, and emphasis on the importance of birth spacing during the first and ideally second postpartum year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must include social and medical PPIUD eligibility content and birth-spacing importance, not only counseling and insertion technique.
#### Constraints
- The article notes effectiveness of the intervention itself may be improved through ongoing and more in-depth training; recommendations rest on a descriptive panel analysis in six tertiary hospitals in Nepal.

### Target Learners
- Health providers in obstetrics and gynecology departments in low- and middle-income country hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintain accurate knowledge of IUD effectiveness and eligibility
- Reduce bias and misconceptions about who is eligible for PPIUD
- Understand the importance of postpartum birth spacing
