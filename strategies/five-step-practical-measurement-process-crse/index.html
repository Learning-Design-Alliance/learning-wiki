Five-step practical measurement process for improving CRSE mathematics instruction¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Figure 2 lays out an iterative process: identify an instructional goal, design an instructional strategy, identify a practical measure, implement the measure, and improve instruction. The worked example follows a teacher who wants to affirm students' racial and cultural identities by adding a reflective question about funds of knowledge at the start and end of lessons, then uses biweekly surveys and informal check-ins to decide whether to adopt, adapt, or abandon the strategy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The teacher makes time to analyze and reflect on the collected data regularly

Constraints¶

  • The process is illustrated with a single teacher-level example integrating identity affirmation into lessons

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 mathematics teachers refining CRSE instruction

Target Learning Goals¶

  • affirming students' racial and cultural identities in teaching and learning
  • continuous improvement of culturally relevant instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Leader supports for teachers using practical measures to advance CRSE
  • Plan and design backwards from a research-based learning goal to features, metrics, and measurement tools
