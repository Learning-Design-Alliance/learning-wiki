# Enhance virtual laboratory platforms with live experiment videos, mobile-friendly versions, and adaptive learning pathways

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback and best practices in simulation-based learning, the authors recommend three forward-looking enhancements for virtual laboratory platforms. Embedding high-quality video demonstrations of real laboratory procedures can bridge the gap between virtual and physical experiences; optimizing for mobile devices increases accessibility; and implementing adaptive features that tailor content and feedback to individual student progress can enhance engagement, address diverse learning needs, and promote mastery of laboratory skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing simulation platform with modular structure that can absorb new features such as video embedding and adaptive pathways
#### Constraints
- These are proposed future directions, not features evaluated in the study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students
- students in low-resource or remote settings

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement with laboratory procedures
- mastery of laboratory skills
- accessibility of virtual learning
