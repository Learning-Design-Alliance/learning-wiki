# Screen for SMA and nomophobia and build academic self-efficacy through mastery experiences, role models, feedback, and coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing schools routinely assess SMA and nomophobia to identify students at risk of reduced ASE and clinical performance, integrating screening into wellness programs. To strengthen ASE, it advises "emphasizing confidence development via mastery of challenging academic or clinical assignments, learning from competent role models, obtaining positive feedback, and managing anxiety". Hospital managers are advised to set clear smartphone-use guidelines, with preceptors modeling appropriate behaviors and offering mentorship, while educators deliver workshops on time organization, stress alleviation, and responsible technology usage.
