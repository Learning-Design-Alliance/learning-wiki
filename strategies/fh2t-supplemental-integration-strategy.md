# Deploy FH2T as supplemental practice with GM tools integrated into teacher instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using FH2T as a supplemental instructional tool for practicing foundational math skills, with the game usable independently by students. It states that "Ideally the related GM tools can be integrated into teacher instruction and classroom activities," and that "Professional development is part of a school/district license." Implementation therefore pairs independent student game play with teacher-led integration supported by license-included professional development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School/district license that includes professional development
- Student devices with a modern web browser
#### Constraints
- Evaluated as a supplement at nine 30-minute sessions per year; the article does not report outcomes for standalone or core-instruction use

### Target Learners
- Middle and high school students in introductory prealgebra or algebra

### Target Learning Goals
- Practice of foundational algebra skills such as expression manipulation and equation solving
