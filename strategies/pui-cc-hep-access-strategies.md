# Expand PUI and CC access to HEP research through flexible funding, scaled collaboration membership, and reassigned time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The whitepaper recommends a package of actions for funding agencies and large collaborations: DOE funding avenues for PUIs, affiliation with nearby non-PUI groups as a safety net, reevaluating fixed entry fees (an "entry fee structure that scales based on the number of Ph.D. holders"), low-cost non-time-limited "light" membership, travel support, remote service-task options, and allowing grant funds for reassigned time in NSF RUI. It argues these changes would directly address the teaching-load, funding, and membership barriers it documents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding agencies and experiment governing bodies must adopt the recommended changes; PUI faculty need national-agency funding for affiliation-based authorship.
#### Constraints
- The article notes NSF and DOE currently generally discourage using grant funds to buy out teaching duties, and existing no-cost membership forms often carry time limitations ill-suited to long-term collaboration.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students at PUIs and community colleges
- early-career HEP faculty and postdocs

### Target Learning Goals
- early exposure to particle physics research
- research skill building and career preparation in HEP
