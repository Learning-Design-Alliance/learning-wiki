# Best practices for RL in education: evaluate live with actual users at scale, look beyond STEM, and target under-served learner groups including children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Drawing on the systematic review, the authors distill best practices for implementing RL in education. Central recommendations are the "need for broader and more large-scale deployments and evaluations with actual users relative to only using simulated data", extending research beyond the STEM umbrella to test transferability of concepts, and expanding applications targeting children to support their overall learning process. They also caution that education poses unique challenges — limited interaction log data, partially unobservable psychological processes, multiple optimization goals, immediate reaction requirements, and interpretability of RL actions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluations should move beyond simulated users toward live interaction with actual learners
- Applications must address RL's substantial data requirements, e.g., environments that can record learner data in large quantities
#### Constraints
- The review notes methodological issues and the lack of simulations that adequately reflect the respective learning context as current limitations

### Target Learners
- adult and higher-education learners (current majority)
- children and school learners (recommended expansion)

### Target Learning Goals
- enhancing learning outcomes and student engagement through adaptive, personalized learning environments
