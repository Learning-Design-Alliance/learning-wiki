# Establish guest-lecturer mobility and train-the-trainer schemes bringing subject matter experts from industry and national labs into teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report recommends mobility schemes in which experts from industry, private companies, user facilities, and national laboratories serve as part-time lecturers, primarily in advanced, niche topics and skills, creating flexibility in course topics while ensuring relevant, up-to-date knowledge transfer. For experts without teaching experience it proposes "Train the trainer" programs: "programs that provide them with an overview of the latest education techniques and practice over a few days to ensure they can engage students". Sending organizations benefit from access to trained candidates for recruitment.
