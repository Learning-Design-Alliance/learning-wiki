# Videoconferencing-based simulation: distribute participants across locations to reduce on-site numbers while maximizing debriefing participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Videoconferencing technologies allow simulation scenarios with participants in different locations, decreasing the number of participants at any one site. The article states that experiential learning is limited with this approach, but opportunities for others to participate in the debriefing are maximized. It is proposed as one of the four social distancing categories, usable alone or in combination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videoconferencing technology connecting participants in different locations
#### Constraints
- Experiential learning is limited with this approach

### Target Learners
- health care teams unable to gather on site

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in simulation debriefing at scale
