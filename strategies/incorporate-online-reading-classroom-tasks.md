# Incorporate technology-based reading activities into classroom reading tasks and let students experiment with reading online while learning strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Despite the null results, the article recommends that practitioners incorporate technology-based reading activities, since real-life language use involves online reading, and that teachers teach reading strategies through online tasks. It states "these kinds of activities involving technology should be incorporated into classroom reading tasks rigorously" and that teachers can provide "classroom tasks where students can experiment with reading online".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom reading tasks should include technology-based activities reflecting real-life online reading demands.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is made alongside the finding that the tested online tasks did not improve test scores in this sample.

### Target Learners
- tertiary-level EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 reading comprehension
- metacognitive reading strategy use
