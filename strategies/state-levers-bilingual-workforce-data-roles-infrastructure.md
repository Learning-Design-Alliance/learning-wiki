# Three state policy levers: modernize educator data systems, analyze BLA role entry and retention conditions, and build sustained regional infrastructure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief proposes three state policy levers for creating enabling conditions for the BLA workforce. Lever 1 is to "Integrate California Department of Education (CDE), CTC, and local K–12 and educator preparation data to create a linked, longitudinal system that connects preparation, credentialing, assignment, and retention." Lever 2 is to analyze where BLA teachers are placed and examine the design of bilingual teaching roles to guide targeted incentives. Lever 3 is to sustain regional collaboratives and institutionalize the California State Educator Workforce Collaborative as the statewide alignment mechanism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State agency integration of CDE, CTC, and local K–12 and educator preparation data; regional and local access to timely, actionable workforce data
#### Constraints
- The brief notes declining enrollment and declining revenues require streamlining program costs and strategic cost sharing to communicate return on investment

### Target Learners
- Multilingual Learners in California K–12 bilingual instructional programs
- monolingual students in bilingual programs

### Target Learning Goals
- literacy and long-term academic outcomes through bilingual instructional models
- universal bilingualism and biliteracy under California's Global 2030 vision
