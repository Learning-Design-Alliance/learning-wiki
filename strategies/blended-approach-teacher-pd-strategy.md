# Use blended learning approaches for teachers' professional development in ICT integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adopting a blended approach to professional development to address the challenge of community building in online environments, since virtual communities struggle to develop the belonging, trust, and support that are prerequisites to learning. Blended programs give teachers opportunities for learning on the job and collaborating with other teachers, and can help teachers better understand and implement technology in their classrooms. The study's own blended model — combining face-to-face lab sessions with asynchronous and synchronous online learning — produced higher ICT knowledge gains than traditional training.
