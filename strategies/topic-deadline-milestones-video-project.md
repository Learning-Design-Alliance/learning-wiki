# Institute an early topic-registration deadline and announced milestones to counter procrastination on long projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students procrastinate on multi-week creative projects, the instructor set a topic deadline about 1.5 weeks after the project is assigned and announces milestones up to the due date, plus near-weekly reminders. The author reports that "I have now instituted a deadline for the topic (~1.5 weeks after the project is assigned) and announce milestones up until the due date," and that only three times in 18 semesters were videos late or missing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multi-week project timeline (about six weeks from assignment to due date in this course)
- Instructor willingness to remind students almost weekly
#### Constraints
- Students still tend to procrastinate despite reminders; the author reports procrastination can lead to a low-quality video

### Target Learners
- undergraduate non-science majors

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement with a self-directed research and production task
