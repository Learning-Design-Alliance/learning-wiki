# Adopt project-based learning to reduce continuous-assessment workload and increase time for reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose that where students report spending too much time on multiple continuous evaluation tasks, "a commitment to the project-based learning model (PBL), could fit in and solve this demand" for more time to reflect and apply new knowledge. They cite existing PBL, DIY and Maker experiences in other online universities that connected study plans with real social problems of interest to students.
