# Children's Literature for Math Instruction

## Description
Children's literature for math instruction is a strategy in which stories, picture books, or narrative texts are used to introduce mathematical ideas, contexts, or discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A text with a meaningful connection to the mathematical idea.
- Follow-up discussion, modeling, or problem solving that makes the math explicit.
#### Constraints
- Story context alone does not guarantee mathematical clarity.

### Target Learning Goals
- Increase engagement and make mathematical ideas more concrete and discussable.

### Instructions
- Read or revisit a text that contains a relevant quantitative or spatial situation.
- Identify the mathematical structure in the story.
- Extend into representation, explanation, or problem solving.
