Improve visibility, alignment, and concrete strategies of the Transforming Schools Framework and resources¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on interview findings, the report recommends improving Alaska's Trauma-Engaged Schools resources so that they are "visible, are aligned to other initiatives, and provide concrete strategies for translating concepts into action in a school or classroom setting." This responds to the finding that most schools were unaware of or not using the resources, and that educators often implemented trauma-consistent practices without recognizing them as such. The strategy targets state-level resource developers and district support staff.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Resource developers must act on interviewee suggestions to make the framework and resources visible and aligned to other initiatives

Constraints¶

  • Findings apply to the 271 responding schools and may not generalize to all Alaska schools

Target Learners¶

  • Alaska public school educators and school leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Implementation of trauma-engaged practices supporting students affected by childhood trauma
