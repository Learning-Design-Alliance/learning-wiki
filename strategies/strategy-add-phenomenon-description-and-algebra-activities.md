# Strengthen the ILS strategy with more activities on describing the physical phenomenon and on algebraic procedures from prior courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the residual postest errors, the authors recommend extending the strategy beyond memorized solution procedures: "especialmente las relacionadas con condiciones iniciales, procedimientos  algebraicos y descripción más profunda del fenómeno físico a estudiar". The aim is for students to connect the second-order ODE concepts with the pendulum phenomenon rather than only memorizing procedures, and to address the course from the analysis of physics situations as prior literature suggests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The existing ILS with pretest, content, learning activity and postest scenarios, into which the additional activities are incorporated
#### Constraints
- The recommendation addresses the observed limits of this implementation: residual incorrect-popular model and distractor selection above 30% and 15% on items 2–5

### Target Learners
- fourth-semester engineering students in the differential equations course

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual comprehension of second-order ODEs linked to the physical phenomenon
- improved algebraic procedures from previous courses

## Claims

- [After implementation, students still selected the incorrect-popular model and the distractor on questions 2–5 in percentages above 30% and 15% respectively, reflecting weak algebraic and physical prerequisites](../claims/residual-incorrect-model-distractor-selection-postest.md) [+W]
- [After the ILS implementation, all five questionnaire items moved to higher Bao concentration-score zones, with students shifting from random answering to the correct model](../claims/bao-vectors-postest-higher-comprehension-zones.md) [+W]
- [On the item asking students to identify the differential equation modeling the pendulum, correct-model selection rose from 70% (zone MM) to 94% (zone HH)](../claims/question-1-identification-mm-to-hh-94-percent.md) [+W]
