# Establish anonymous feedback channels and psychologically safe classrooms to support critical expression

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's managerial implications recommend institutional measures that lower the social risk of expressing critical views. Teachers should "inspire students to ask questions boldly, dare to question, and maintain an open attitude towards any viewpoint", and universities should create inclusive, psychologically safe classroom atmospheres where challenging viewpoints and evidence-based argument are institutionalised. For cultural constraints such as "saving face", the article specifically proposes that "an anonymous communication zone could be established: utilizing online platforms or school internal systems to create an anonymous feedback mechanism, enabling students to freely express their opinions, ask questions, or seek help without worrying about being judged or ridiculed by others".

### Context
