# Use the CFQT as a common language to plan educational activities, map qualifications, and formulate job requirements in quantum technology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends concrete uses of the CFQT for different actors: educators to plan educational activities and map objectives; individuals and learners to specify qualifications and identify needed training; companies to specify employee or team qualifications, identify gaps, formulate job requirements and compare candidates; and job seekers to map qualifications against job requirements. The framework is positioned as "an important step towards the standardization of QT education", with a certification scheme under development as a second step.
