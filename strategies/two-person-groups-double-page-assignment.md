# Assign students to two-person study groups with twice their share of pages to extract and cross-check conceptual indexes.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the second step of the Education Management Model, the teacher distributes an equal number of text pages among students so each two-person group covers twice its individual share. Students study their pages, extract conceptual indexes, and compare findings with a partner to "find confidence on the accuracy of his findings." Group sizes are determined based on the number of pages in each topic and sub-topic, observing the twice-number-of-pages-per-student rule.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher determines a fixed amount of course per two students
- Each student compares his extracted indexes with another student's to find confidence on accuracy
- Observing the twice number of assignment pages per student is a necessity for two-person discussion
#### Constraints
- This differs from ordinary participative learning procedures where students are divided into three-to-five member teams

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students
- Second-grade boy students in public elementary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Extraction of conceptual indexes of course content
- Precision and deep insight in subject matter
