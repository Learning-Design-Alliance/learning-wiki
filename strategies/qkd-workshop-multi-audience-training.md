## Description
The authors position the QKD workshop system as "an indispensable instrument for general training": the same desktop device serves as a practical course for undergraduate physics students through its quantum basis and optical information transfer, for electronics engineers and hardware programmers through pulse generation and synchronization tasks, and as a research field for algorithm developers and mathematicians via error correction and privacy amplification.
