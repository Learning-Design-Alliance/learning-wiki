# Let students sketch a voluntary research proposal in small groups and present it for bonus exam points

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In self-study time, students in small groups of up to four voluntarily sketch a short research proposal on a self-chosen topic investigable with lecture methods, and earn bonus points by presenting it against pre-announced criteria. The article states the goal is "kein vollst¨andig ausgearbeiteter Antrag, sondern eine Skizze", through which students deepen method knowledge and learn aspects of the research process such as turning an idea into a plan, literature search, method selection and time and cost estimation. Bonus points are capped at "maximal 10 %" of the exam's achievable points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supporting literature as a starting point, an introduction to research proposals, and a planning tool such as the Planungsf¨unfeck should be provided.
- Assessment criteria for the presentations must be announced in advance.
#### Constraints
- The allotted time is about one work day per student, deliberately tight to encourage task distribution within groups; the proposal is a sketch, not a full proposal.

### Target Learners
- master's students in a research-oriented lecture

### Target Learning Goals
- insight into research processes
- deepening knowledge of lecture methods
- teamwork in a scientific context
- presenting ideas to specialists
