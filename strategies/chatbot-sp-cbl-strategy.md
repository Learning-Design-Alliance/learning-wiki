# Use chatbot standardised patients combined with CBL to address limitations of traditional SP approaches.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends integrating chatbot standardised patients (CSP) with CBL methodologies to address limitations of traditional SP approaches by providing enhanced reproducibility, extensive coverage, standardised training protocols and personalised interactive mechanisms. This innovative hybrid model facilitates targeted knowledge delivery and consistent clinical scenario replication, showing significant potential for broader implementation in medical education.
