# Offer courses in either online or in-class form where equivalent outcomes are acceptable

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is that courses showing no performance difference between delivery forms may be offered flexibly. The author writes that the null result "can be a signal that some courses can be offered in alternative forms or can be done in either of the forms with the same results", while noting teachers' roles differ online. It is grounded in the study's non-significant ANOVA and t-test findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course content, aims and lesson number held equal across forms, as in the study where both groups had the same content and assignments
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one small cohort of final-year pre-service English teachers at a single university, so generalisation is limited
