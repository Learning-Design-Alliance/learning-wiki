# Racial Equity in PBL - Listen to the Voices of Students

## Description
Give students authorship in sharing and telling their own stories, which a good project allows them to do. Student voice matters. It creates agency and leadership that will help build the culture for PBL. Advancing youth voice is directly connected to advancing youth equity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom environment that values student voice; facilitator who is willing to listen and learn from students.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in power dynamics; facilitator must be comfortable relinquishing some control.
