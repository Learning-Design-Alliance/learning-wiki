# Dictation Software

## Description
Dictation software allows learners to speak their ideas and have them transcribed into text. This supports learners with strengths in auditory processing by allowing them to bypass visual cues involved with writing and focus on expressing their thoughts orally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to dictation software, training on its use
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all writing tasks, some learners may struggle with speaking their thoughts clearly
