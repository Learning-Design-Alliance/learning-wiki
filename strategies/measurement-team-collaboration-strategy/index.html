Have measurement team members collaborate to complete measurement tasks successfully¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief discusses how measurement team members can work together to complete measurement tasks successfully. Collaboration among staff and consultants is presented as the mechanism for carrying out the typical measurement tasks the brief describes, from making key measurement decisions to producing high-quality data.
