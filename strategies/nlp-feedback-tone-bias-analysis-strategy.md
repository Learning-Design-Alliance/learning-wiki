# Extend the evaluation system with NLP-based analysis of feedback tone and bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the Conclusion the authors recommend future extensions of the evaluation platform using natural language processing. They propose that NLP techniques could assess the emotional tone of student feedback, identifying positive, negative, or neutral sentiments, and could detect biased, repetitive, or irrelevant evaluations so that analysis emphasizes authentic and constructive input. They also suggest that after every evaluation process the system should provide constructive feedback to the evaluated lecturer highlighting areas requiring attention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- After every evaluation process, the system should provide constructive feedback to the evaluated lecturer highlighting areas requiring attention and offering suggestions for improvements.
#### Constraints
- This is stated as future work with the aid of an artificial intelligence system, not as an implemented or tested component of the current platform.

### Target Learners
- University lecturers in Nigeria

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying specific areas for improvement for each lecturer and ensuring authentic, constructive student input
