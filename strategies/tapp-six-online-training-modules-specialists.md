# Train school specialists through six online modules covering all stages of the TAPP process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
TAPP preparation uses six online training modules for school specialists covering all stages of the TAPP process and related content. Each module "includes a didactic component via PowerPoint, embedded video examples, guided notes, knowledge checks, final quizzes and a certificate of completion," giving specialists a structured, self-contained route into leading the consultation meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School specialists will receive six online training modules that cover all stages of the TAPP process and related content.
#### Constraints
- School specialists will need internet access.

### Target Learners
- School specialists leading TAPP (counselors, psychologists, instructional specialists, administrators, special education teachers)

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of the TAPP process and its stages for leading family-school partnership meetings
