# Use theory-driven framing of instructor talk as a first step before testing effects on student motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend organizing research on non-content instructor talk around specific motivational theories (immediacy, self-efficacy, task values) rather than purely data-driven categories, so that talk types map onto constructs that can later be linked to student outcomes. They state, "The novel framing we present here is a necessary first step before testing for the effects of non-content instructor talk on students' motivation-related beliefs and behaviors." The strategy is to identify forms of talk related to specific constructs rather than letting the data alone drive characterization.
