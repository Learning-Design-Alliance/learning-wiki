## Description
The article proposes that the subject of naturally occurring nuclear reactors be used as a jumping-off point for classroom discussions. It states the subject matter falls within NSE Standards for grades 9-12 and is also appropriate for college classes. The discussion topics it names are planetary energy sources, the geomagnetic field, and the value of debating and challenging current scientific thinking rather than treating science as an assemblage of facts.
