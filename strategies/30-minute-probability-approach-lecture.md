# Deliver a 30-minute lecture on the Probability Approach between two quizzes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific implementable teaching activity: administer a conditional quiz, deliver a 30-minute lecture on the Probability Approach, then administer a second quiz after a week. In the study, "the experimental class (N=33) received a 30-minute lecture on the Probability Approach" and learned "how to decide upon a conditional sentence type by judging the probability of realizing the condition in the result-clause." The control class was merely given answers and discussed incorrect questions without the approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two conditional quizzes administered before and one week after the lecture
#### Constraints
- Tested only with Chinese freshman English majors in a teachers college

### Target Learners
- freshman English majors whose native language is Chinese

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding English if-conditional sentences

### Affordances
- Probability Approach English Conditionals
