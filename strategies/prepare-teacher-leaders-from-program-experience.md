# Draw on a professional development program's experience preparing teachers to lead it when preparing teacher leaders to facilitate professional learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's approach is to derive insights for preparing teacher leaders from a program's own experience developing teacher-leaders: it "offers insights to help address these challenges based on the National Writing Project's experience preparing teachers to lead one of its professional development programs." The strategy treats an established professional development program as a source of practical guidance for building teacher leaders' facilitation capacity, addressing the identified challenges of unfamiliarity with strategies and weak facilitation skills.
