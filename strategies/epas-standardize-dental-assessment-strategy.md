# Use EPAs to standardize student performance expectations and assessment across dental schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing EPAs so that dental schools can set consistent expectations for graduates regardless of institution. It states "By implementing EPAs, dental schools could standardize student performance expectations, ensuring graduates meet consistent standards of practice regardless of their institution", arguing this could reduce variability in clinical competency and help students transition from classroom to professional practice. The authors also propose next steps: clearer EPA descriptions, standardized assessment methods, and faculty training to ensure consistent evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearer descriptions of each EPA and developing standardized assessment methods; faculty training would also be needed to ensure consistent evaluation
#### Constraints
- The proposed EPAs reflect one institution and one German region; individuals from different institutions may find different EPAs more important

### Target Learners
- Dental students in German university hospitals and clinical traineeships

### Target Learning Goals
- Consistent, competency‐based assessment of clinical skills and readiness for independent practice
