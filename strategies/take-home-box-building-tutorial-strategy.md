# Give course participants a tutorial for building their own low-cost training box to encourage independent practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At the end of the course, participants received a tutorial on producing their own laparoscopic training boxes from easy-to-find materials, deliberately extending practice beyond the supervised session. The article reports this encouraged "future independent practice", and many participants expressed interest in continuing to practise during the curricular year and at home.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training boxes constructible from easy-to-find, low-cost materials so residents can reproduce them outside the hospital

### Target Learning Goals
