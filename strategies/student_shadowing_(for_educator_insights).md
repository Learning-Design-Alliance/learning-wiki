# Student Shadowing (for educator insights)

## Description
Educators spend the majority of a school day shadowing a student to gain real-time qualitative data about their experiences. The key is to stay objective, making low-inference observations without interpretation or judgment. Notes are taken on body language, facial expressions, and interactions with teachers and fellow students. This raw data is then analyzed to pinpoint areas that need to be addressed, such as instructional practices or biases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student Shadowing Template, dedicated school day, objective observation skills
#### Constraints
- Requires significant time commitment, may be subject to observer bias, may not be representative of all students' experiences
