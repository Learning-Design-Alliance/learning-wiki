# Things to Consider as a Healthy Skeptic

## Description
Provide adolescents with opportunities to see the value of a healthy skepticism toward information they encounter in both online and offline contexts. Use curriculum as a springboard for introducing students to multiple perspectives and new ways of thinking about content. Use thinking prompts that move beyond the typical checklist and ask for evidence that supports their thinking. Adolescents also like working in small groups as they grapple with these issues, and then meeting with the whole class to exchange strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum that introduces multiple perspectives, thinking prompts, small group activities, and whole-class discussion.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation. Some students may need support to express their thinking and perspectives effectively.
