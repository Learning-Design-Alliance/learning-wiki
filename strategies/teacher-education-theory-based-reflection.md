# Teacher education should develop teachers' theoretical understanding and train theory-based reflection on practice

## Description
The article recommends that teacher education move beyond a theory–practice dichotomy and demonstrate how theory informs practice. It argues that the mechanism for turning experience into knowledge is reflection, and that reflection requires theory-based analysis because theory supplies the criteria driving reflection about experiences. As quoted support it cites Postholm (2008, p. 1721): "Teachers can reflect independently on their own by using theory as an analytical tool in this activity", enabling teachers to analyse, understand, and improve their practice. On this view research shifts from providing direct prescriptions to developing and testing theories that teachers can use as analytical tools.
