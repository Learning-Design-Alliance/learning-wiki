# Rethinking Accountability

## Description
Recognize that students did not choose the current situation and should not be unjustly penalized for its challenges. Balance generosity and high standards in grading practices. Offer empathy and flexibility in grading decisions. Modify assignments, adjust expectations, and conduct alternative assessments based on each situation. Prioritize keeping connected with students and cultivating hope. Place yourself in students’ shoes in all instructional decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open communication with students, flexibility in grading policies, access to alternative assessment methods.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant time and effort, may be challenging to balance with institutional grading requirements.
