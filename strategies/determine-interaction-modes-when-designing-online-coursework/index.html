When shifting to online delivery, first determine how students will interact with content, instructor, and peers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete design step for instructors moving instruction online: attend to students' experiences and decide in advance how students will interact with the content, with the instructor, and with other learners. The article presents this as what instructors "should attend to" when designing online coursework, grounded in the interaction types its cited study linked to minority students' success.
