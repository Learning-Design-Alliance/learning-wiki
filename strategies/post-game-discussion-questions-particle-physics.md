# Use post-game discussion questions to link game experiences to particle physics concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article supplies a set of questions with answers tied to game rules, intended "as topics for further conversation and discussion after playing the game," so that players in workshops or at home can "further refine and deepen the newly acquired knowledge of elementary particle interactions." Questions cover the once-only gravitational rule, the strong interaction as quark-only, weak-force lepton generations, electromagnetic charge requirements, neutrinos as least-interactive particles, neutrino oscillations, and annihilation conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator or discussion leader able to explain the physical background answers provided in the article
#### Constraints
- The questions presuppose that players have first played the game and encountered its rules

### Target Learners
- workshop participants in physics popularization settings
- players at home

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting game rules as physical principles
- differentiating quarks and leptons
- explaining weak and electromagnetic interactions
