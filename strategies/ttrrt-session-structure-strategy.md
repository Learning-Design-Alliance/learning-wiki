# Combine pre-learning, lectures, skill stations and simulated multi-disciplinary scenarios with structured debriefing for trauma team training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The TTRRT session recipe: participants complete prescribed web-based pre-learning to reach a common knowledge base, attend 7 hours of face-to-face training combining lectures on team dynamics and human factors, four skill stations (haemorrhage control and vascular access, tourniquet and pelvic binder, pleural decompression, surgical airway), and low-fidelity simulated team scenarios with six participants per simulation and observer feedback. Structured debriefing after each scenario targets "teamwork, communication, decision making, leadership and followership", aiming for about 20 minutes per scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-learning materials and a roles manual issued before the session so all participants share the same baseline understanding
- Mixed-discipline participant groups and low-fidelity simulation replicable in low-resource environments
- Debriefing adjusted to content with an aim of 20 min per scenario
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Multi-disciplinary trauma team members (medical, nursing) across tertiary, urban and rural hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Team-based trauma reception and resuscitation
- Technical life-saving procedures
- Non-technical teamwork and leadership skills
