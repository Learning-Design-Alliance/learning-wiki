# Establish a centralized database of anonymized simulation data accessible to global research groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article argues that as simulators become more available internationally, "it would be important to establish a centralized database of anonymized simulation data." Such a database should be accessible to research groups across the globe to develop more representative and generalizable models powering intelligent tutoring systems. The authors frame this democratization of data as essential as ITS adoption spreads.
