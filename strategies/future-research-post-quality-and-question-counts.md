# Future research: examine post content quality and instructor question counts rather than post quantity alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose that follow-up work explore "the content of the posts, as quantity does not assume quality," and investigate whether personalizing discussion posts reduces the number of questions instructors receive. They also recommend examining where participation is directed (instructor posts versus peer posts) when personalized versus nonpersonalized headings are used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to discussion-forum post content and question-forum logs for analysis
#### Constraints
- The current study measured only post quantity, not quality

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate online students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding engagement quality in online discussion forums
