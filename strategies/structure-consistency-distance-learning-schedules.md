# Create structure and consistency through schedules, regular communication, and clear instructions in distance learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To help students feel settled, the brief advises educators to "Create structure and consistency through lesson schedules, regularly scheduled communication, and clear, concise instructions." For virtual settings this means an online calendar showing lessons, start and stop times, and due dates; for nonvirtual settings, a paper version plus instructions for returning work. Teachers should communicate at the same time(s) every day using the same formats and tell students and caregivers how and when to reach them.
