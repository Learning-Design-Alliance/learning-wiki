# Professional development programs should prioritize strengthening teachers' reflective and goal-setting skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that teachers' lowest-rated skills were help-seeking and goal-setting and that their critical-level reflection was rare, the article recommends that professional development programs prioritize strengthening reflective and goal-setting skills to foster teachers' SRL development. It suggests future programs could incorporate targeted workshops, training, and ongoing reflective practice exercises to address these deficits, tailored to the needs and constraints of teachers and students in various settings. The recommendation rests on the article's own findings that teacher SRL deficits, particularly in reflection and goal-setting, appear to influence students' SRL development.
