# Creating a Digital Library Section for SEL Topics

## Description
A digital library includes a dedicated section of books focused on social and emotional learning (SEL) topics. This allows teachers and students to easily search for and find titles relevant to specific SEL skills, such as empathy, positive relationships, managing emotions, problem-solving, grit, perseverance, and perspective-taking. The library can be organized by topic, skill, or grade level, making it easier for educators to integrate SEL into their curriculum and for students to find resources that address their specific needs. This approach emphasizes the importance of SEL and provides a convenient way to access relevant literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a digital library platform, a collection of SEL-focused books, and a system for organizing and categorizing titles. Staff training may be needed to effectively curate and promote the collection.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing maintenance to ensure the collection remains current and relevant. Access to technology and digital literacy skills are necessary. May not address all aspects of SEL without complementary activities and discussions.

### Affordances
- Provides easy access to a wide range of SEL resources, supports curriculum integration, and enhances student awareness of SEL skills and topics. Facilitates a more comprehensive approach to SEL by integrating literature into various subject areas.

### Personalization
- The digital library can be tailored to the specific needs and interests of the school community by selecting titles that reflect diverse perspectives and address relevant social issues. Teachers can create curated lists based on grade level, reading ability, and SEL focus.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track usage of the SEL section, survey teachers and students about the usefulness of the resources, and analyze student work for evidence of improved SEL skills. Monitor the diversity and relevance of titles to ensure the collection meets the needs of the school community.

## Impact
- Research indicates that integrating SEL into the curriculum enhances academic performance, improves student behavior, and promotes positive relationships. A well-curated digital library section can support these outcomes by providing easy access to relevant literature.
