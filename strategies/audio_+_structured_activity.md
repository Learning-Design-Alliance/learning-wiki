# Audio + Structured Activity

## Description
Students engage with audio resources (music, podcasts, voice-recorded lectures) accompanied by a structured activity. This combines the advantages of audio learning with the benefits of active engagement and application of concepts. Audio resources are typically smaller in file size and easier to host, making them accessible to learners with limited bandwidth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creation or selection of audio resources and design of structured activities. Instructor must provide clear instructions and feedback.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of content or learners. Requires careful planning to ensure active engagement and understanding.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All learners, particularly those who prefer audio-based learning and those with limited access to technology or bandwidth.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Provide accessible and flexible content, promote active learning, facilitate understanding and application of concepts.

### Affordances
- Offers a low-bandwidth alternative to video, provides flexibility and accessibility, promotes active engagement with the material. The sound of a human voice can play an important role in learning.

### Personalization
- Structured activities can be tailored to different learning styles and needs. Audio resources can be selected based on learner interests and preferences.
