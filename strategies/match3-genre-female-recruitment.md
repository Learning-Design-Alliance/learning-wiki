# Build educational games in a genre with high female participation, such as Match 3, when targeting girls for STEM recruitment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
When the goal is recruiting girls into coding and physics, build the educational game in a genre girls already play. The authors note that "games like \"Candy Crush\" [9] and \"Bejeweled\" [10] have much higher female-player participation" and therefore designed Quantum 3 in the Match 3 genre, which "typically attracts a higher ratio of female players." Recruitment impact is to be measured later through a follow-up survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The chosen genre must genuinely attract the target demographic, as the Match 3 titles cited do
#### Constraints
- Impact on girls' interest had not yet been demonstrated at the time of writing; it will be measured through a follow-up survey

### Target Learners
- K-12 girls
- girls interested in coding and STEM

### Target Learning Goals
- Raising public interest in QCD and increasing interest in coding among girls
