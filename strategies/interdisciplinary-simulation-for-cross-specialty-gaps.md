# Adopt interdisciplinary simulation sessions to address curriculum gaps in topics spanning multiple specialties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend leveraging simulation with trainees from multiple specialties to fill gaps in topics neglected across resident curricula, stating that "many medical topics span multiple disciplines, presenting opportunities for further interdisciplinary education and simulation." They further suggest grouping residents from different specialties together to allow residents to learn from one another, which their own scheduling constraints prevented.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination across residency programs' didactic schedules
#### Constraints
- The authors note grouping residents across specialties was not possible due to each group's limited availability away from clinical duties

### Target Learners
- Residents in primary care specialties and OB/GYN

### Target Learning Goals
- Managing menopausal concerns and other topics spanning multiple disciplines
