# Use structural equation modeling and standardized assessments to test the SVR with deaf learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that researchers "employ structural equation modeling to test various hypotheses of reading development to examine the applicability of the SVR to reading and deafness," following designs used in the general field of reading such as latent variable models with multiple measures per construct. It further recommends standardized measures of decoding, language comprehension, and reading comprehension so outcomes can be compared to normative data and across investigations, and that studies be conducted within the appropriate target age range with a longitudinal component and thorough description of participant demographic characteristics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must be conducted within the appropriate target age range for the constructs under investigation and include a longitudinal component
#### Constraints
- Achieving adequate sample sizes presents an on-going challenge in conducting research with a low incidence population such as deaf learners

### Target Learners
- deaf and hard-of-hearing learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- phonological skills
- decoding
