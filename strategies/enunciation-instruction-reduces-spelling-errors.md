# Teach clear, accurate spoken English with painstaking enunciation to reduce spelling errors due to faulty speech

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools secure clear, accurate spoken English with painstaking enunciation, predicting this "will diminish the number of spelling errors due to faulty speech." It works by aligning students' pronunciation with approved forms so that spelling no longer encodes unapproved speech. The article applies it to the mispronunciation class of errors found in college students' themes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must recognize the need of securing clear, accurate spoken English with painstaking enunciation.
#### Constraints
- The article states greater care in pronunciation alone is a vain expectation for greatly reducing spelling difficulty, since a person sure of the correct sound must still ask how far accepted symbols fail to represent the sound.
