# Develop student AI literacy and institutional GenAI policies, guidelines and safeguards for responsible integration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions develop policies and formal guidance on GenAI use while building students' AI literacy. Students themselves asked that "A well-balanced usage guideline needs to be in place so that the benefits of the tech can be leveraged." Suggested actions include educational resources and workshops on GenAI's ethical and societal implications, prioritizing transparency, accuracy and privacy in tool development, and rethinking curricula to emphasize critical thinking, creativity, digital literacy and AI ethics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to policy development, workshops and curriculum revision; technical staff able to work on explainable AI models and data protection practices
#### Constraints
- Grounded in student perceptions from one cross-sectional survey; the study did not examine actual impact of GenAI on learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate university students

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy, responsible and ethical use of GenAI, critical evaluation of AI-generated content
