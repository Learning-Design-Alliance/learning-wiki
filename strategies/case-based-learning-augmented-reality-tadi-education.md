# Use case-based learning and interactive augmented reality simulations as substitutes for direct clinical contact in TDI education.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors conclude that dental students have limited clinical exposure to dental trauma cases and suggest "more innovative learning methods such as case-based learning and interactive augmented reality simulations as a substitute for direct clinical contact." This strategy addresses the practical constraint that real TDI cases are infrequently encountered during dental training, while still building the diagnostic and management skills needed for competent TDI care.

### Context

#### Constraints
- Effectiveness of these methods for TDI knowledge and confidence has not been directly tested in this study
