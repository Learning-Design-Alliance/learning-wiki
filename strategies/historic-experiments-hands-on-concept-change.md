# Use historic experiments on movement, repeated hands-on by students, to induce conceptual change

## Description
The article recommends easing beginners' difficulties in physics by discussing Gassendi's experiments and having students repeat them, for example observing falling bodies in a moving vehicle such as a car, bus, lorry or boat as an out-of-school experience. It states that "Even a single experiment can induce a concept change in students; most efficient are hands-on experiments". Recalling the historic elimination of naive beliefs is presented as easing students' own conceptual change.
