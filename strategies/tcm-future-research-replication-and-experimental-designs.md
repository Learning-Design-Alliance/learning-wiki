# Future research should replicate the model in diverse educational domains and use experimental and intervention methods

## Description
Based on the meta-analysis and review, the authors recommend that "future research needs to provide further replications of the model in diverse educational settings beyond physical education and test model hypotheses using experimental methods." They further identify the need for more longitudinal tests of the model and greater diversification to other educational domains such as math lessons and homework to confirm generalizability beyond the physical education context.
