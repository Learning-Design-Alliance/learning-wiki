# Train primary school teachers to extend and multiply early-astronomy programmes

## Description
UNAWE makes teacher training a central component because 'The effectiveness of reaching children is multiplied by a large factor through targeting their teachers.' Training is designed to build the confidence of elementary teachers, who often feel inadequate to teach STEM topics; after they are motivated, their excitement can be exploited to teach children language skills through space stories and math skills through solar system problems.
