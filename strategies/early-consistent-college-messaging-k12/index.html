Deliver clear, simple, consistent early messaging to K-12 students and parents about college affordability, preparation, enrollment, and completion¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Guidebook identifies early messaging as a hallmark of College Promise programs: "One of the features of College Promise programs is the clear, simple, and consistent messaging to K–12 students and parents about college affordability, preparation, enrollment, and completion." Such messaging builds a college-going culture, reaches families who may not have considered postsecondary education, and can incorporate Guided Pathways information during secondary school outreach and counseling. Section III directs teams to develop a core program message and adapt it for multiple audiences.

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate layered student support services across the transitions from middle and high school, into college, and through college
