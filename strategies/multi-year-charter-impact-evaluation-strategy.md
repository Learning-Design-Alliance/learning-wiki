# Evaluate charter school impacts across college enrollment, graduation, attendance, and suspensions over multiple years

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report models a multi-year evaluation approach: it "evaluates the impact of the Kauffman School on college enrollment, high school graduation, attendance, and suspensions during its first nine years of operation." This combines long-run educational attainment outcomes with near-term behavioral outcomes for a charter school, and the evaluation is conducted as an annual series of impact reports (with prior Year 6 through Year 8 reports also published) under an associated evaluation project spanning 2011–2024.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal data on enrolled students and a comparison group of similar students in the same city
- Ongoing evaluation capacity, as prepared for the sponsoring foundation over the school's operating years
#### Constraints
- Impacts are specific to one charter school in Kansas City, Missouri, serving a predominantly low-income, Black and Hispanic population

### Target Learners
- charter school students in grades 5 through 12

### Target Learning Goals
- college enrollment
- high school graduation
- student attendance and discipline outcomes
