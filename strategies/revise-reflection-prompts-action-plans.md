# Revise reflection prompts to explicitly require action plans and support revisiting past reflections

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' design implication for future GRF versions is that "the reﬂection prompts will be revised to explicitly ask students to make action plans", since action plans were less frequent than desired. They further recommend that future versions "create opportunities for students to re-read their past reﬂections" and work toward the same goal over multiple weeks, and suggest allowing students to give one another feedback as a subject of ongoing design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reflection prompts must explicitly require action plans rather than leaving planning implicit.
#### Constraints
- The online format let students complete weekly reflections without reviewing prior feedback or goals; the recommendation targets future versions of the tool.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- making concrete, actionable improvement plans
- sustained progress toward learning goals across multiple weeks
