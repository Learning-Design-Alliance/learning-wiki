# Focus equity-relevant research at the classroom and systems levels, treating teachers—not just technology—as drivers of change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy shifts attention from adapting technology to individual students toward how platforms can support teachers and change classroom and system practices. The report states experts "weighed in strongly as seeing the teacher—and not just the technology—as the driver of change where equity is concerned," citing Cohen, Raudenbush & Ball (2003) on transforming relationships among teachers, students, and resources. The K-12 platforms are deployed to whole classrooms, not as student-level interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research questions addressing whether improvements give teachers information and guidance they need to address equity
#### Constraints
- The report notes the nature of data needed may shift, and identity variables might be used only to establish population characteristics

### Target Learners
- K-12 classrooms and higher education courses using SEERNet platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- Achieving more equitable learning outcomes through classroom- and system-level change
