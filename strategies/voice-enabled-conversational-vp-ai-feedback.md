# Integrate AI-generated feedback with voice-enabled, conversational VP platforms using natural speech interaction

## Description
The article suggests that because the AI-generated feedback "is derived from the analysis of transcribed voice interactions between students and the SARI", the observed benefits "may extend to other voice-enabled VP platforms that facilitate natural conversational interactions". The strategy is to pair AI feedback systems with VP technologies that enable natural speech communication, integrating speech recognition and natural language processing, potentially expanding beyond social robotic platforms.
