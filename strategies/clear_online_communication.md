# Clear Online Communication

## Description
Establishing clear channels and rules of online communication with your students ensures that you are getting back to students reasonably quickly when they have questions and is an important component of establishing strong connections with students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors should be ready to answer questions reasonably quickly.
#### Constraints
- May be difficult to manage when instructors are busy

### Affordances
- Clarifies communication channels and expectations, facilitates timely responses, and provides feedback for improved communication.

### Personalization
- Instructors can establish a 'hotline' to reach out with questions.
