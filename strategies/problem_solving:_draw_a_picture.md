# Problem Solving: Draw a Picture

## Description
The 'draw a picture' strategy is a problem-solving technique in which learners make a visual representation of the problem. This intermediate step between language-as-text and the symbolic language of mathematics allows students to begin to think about the problem mathematically. Pictures and diagrams are also good ways of describing solutions to problems; therefore, they are an important part of mathematical communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paper, pencils, and a clear understanding of the problem-solving steps.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of problems. Some students may need additional support to create effective visual representations.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the accuracy and clarity of the visual representations. Assess the learners' ability to use the diagrams to solve problems correctly. Analyze the explanations of the solutions.

## Impact
- Visual representations improve problem-solving accuracy: Students who use accurate visual representations are more likely to correctly solve mathematics problems. Improved math achievement: Teaching students to systematically use visual representations leads to improvements in math achievement.
