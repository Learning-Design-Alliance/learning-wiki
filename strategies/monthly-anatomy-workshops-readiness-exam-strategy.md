# Continue offering monthly optional anatomy workshops and add a formative anatomy readiness exam before Step 1

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend sustaining the workshops as recurring optional review opportunities and adding longitudinal assessment. They state: "Based on the overwhelmingly positive feedback on these sessions, we plan to continue offering these workshops", having expanded topics to include neurology. They also plan a formative anatomy readiness exam covering workshop content, offered before the earliest Step 1 date, to measure long-term retention, plus follow-up surveys after Step 1 and clerkships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshop topics aligned with USMLE content outlines, course content, and essential-anatomy literature
- Advertising through newsletters, calendars, and word of mouth to sustain attendance
#### Constraints
- Long-term benefits are still to be determined; the authors can only speculate until longitudinal follow-up is completed
- Step 1 is pass/fail, which may affect students' perception of its importance

### Target Learners
- Medical students in the preclinical Foundations curriculum, particularly the Class of 2027

### Target Learning Goals
- Increase knowledge retention of key anatomical concepts
- Increase preparedness for clerkships and Step 1
