# Implement competency-based teaching, simulation-based learning and regular assessments for invasive pressure monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions address identified knowledge gaps in zeroing, levelling, waveform interpretation and the phlebostatic axis through structured education. It states: "Institutions should implement competency-based teaching, simulation-based learning and regular assessments to consolidate theoretical knowledge and practical skills." This is paired with standardisation of protocols and ongoing professional development to reduce heterogeneity in monitoring practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured education, protocols and simulation-based training resources, which the authors state are needed to improve accuracy and safety
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is a cross-sectional survey with a convenience sample distributed via the authors' networks, so selection bias may limit generalisability

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses, physicians and anaesthesia/operation technicians working in critical care, operating theatres and emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct zeroing and levelling of arterial transducer systems
- Arterial waveform interpretation
- Phlebostatic axis identification and safe practical handling of arterial lines
