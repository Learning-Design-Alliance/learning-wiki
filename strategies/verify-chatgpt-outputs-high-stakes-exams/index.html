Verify ChatGPT outputs against reliable resources and do not rely on it solely for high-stakes exam study¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends that although ChatGPT versions showed potential in answering licensing examination questions, learners and professionals should not depend on them alone for test preparation. The authors state it is "essential to handle their results with caution and verify them against reliable educational resources, especially in high-stakes exams such as health licensing exams", because LLMs often deliver false or unreliable data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Users must verify ChatGPT results against reliable educational resources before relying on them.

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is framed for high-stakes exams such as health licensing exams, where LLMs often deliver false or unreliable data.

Target Learners¶

  • medical, pharmacy, dentistry, and nursing students preparing for licensing examinations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • accurate medical and health knowledge for licensing examination preparation

Related Strategies¶

  • Use ChatGPT as a supplementary educational tool alongside traditional study methodology rather than as a standalone resource
  • Use LLMs cautiously as supplementary learning tools in dental education, with output verification and professional supervision
