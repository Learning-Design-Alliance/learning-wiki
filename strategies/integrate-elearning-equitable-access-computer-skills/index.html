Integrate e-learning into health professions training while ensuring equitable technology access and developing students' computer skills¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review's implication-for-practice section recommends that decision-makers integrate e-learning into health professions training programs, pairing it with two enabling conditions: "equitable access to technological equipment and internet connections" and developing students' computer skills. It further argues investment in e-learning implementation is an unavoidable necessity given its return in training quality.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Equitable access to technological equipment and internet connections for students
  • Development of students' computer skills

Constraints¶

  • Based on cross-sectional studies with author-developed instruments; the review urges caution in interpreting results

Target Learners¶

  • Health professions students (medical, dental, nursing)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Continuity of learning during disruptions
  • Effective participation in online learning

Claims¶

  • Negative perceptions of pandemic e-learning focused on internet access, health problems, and difficulty developing clinical skills online [-W]
  • Health professions students predominantly reported positive perceptions of e-learning during the COVID-19 pandemic in 7 of 12 studies [+W]
  • Satisfactory levels of acceptance of distance learning were reported in 3 of 4 studies during the pandemic [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • During initial evaluation, investigate legal compliance, equity, accessibility, and context-specific barriers
  • Pair technology access with targeted investments in infrastructure, staffing, and support, and pilot initiatives with dedicated budgets
  • Pursue inclusive digital strategies, educator professional development, and ethical AI governance for AI in adult education
