# Provide opportunities for both collaborative and individual learning to accommodate differing learning preferences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that most Grade 12 Thai EFL students preferred group activity and project orientations while a substantial minority preferred individual work, the authors recommend that educators acknowledge individual differences and vary instruction. They state that "providing opportunities for both collaborative and individual learning can help improve academic achievement and engagement" and that adopting flexible teaching strategies prepares students for diverse real-world scenarios. This strategy is grounded in the study's learning style distribution and its significant learning-style effects on GPA and QPT scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom instruction must include both group/project-based activities and individual learning activities
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on an observational, purposively sampled study of three Thai schools; the authors call for longitudinal and mixed-methods follow-up

- Grade 12 EFL secondary students

### Target Learning Goals
