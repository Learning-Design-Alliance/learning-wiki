# Provide ready-to-use lesson plans, self-study guides, and interactive network software to enable K-12 adoption of network science

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation for scaling network science in schools: "Urgently needed for teachers and students to adopt network science in their daily teaching and learning are resources such as: (1) detailed, modular, ready-to-use lesson plans that teachers can easily incorporate into existing curricula; (2) accessible, interactive online self-study guides for students; and (3) intuitive, interactive computer software tools for network drawing, visualization, analysis, and simulation." The authors report organizing a K-12 teacher workshop to develop lesson plans and exploring options for the other two.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination among researchers, educators, and software developers to produce and maintain the resources
#### Constraints
- The initiative is, per the authors, still far from completion and these resources are not yet fully available

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- K-12 teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Network science concepts and skills integrated into daily teaching and learning
