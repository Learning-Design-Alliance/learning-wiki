# Combine translation-based contrastive analysis with phonological-awareness instruction when teaching formulaic sequences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that teachers combine both tested instructions: "specify contrasts/similarities with L1 and also (if applicable) point out phonological similarity" to strengthen students' retrieval of the form and meaning of lexical phrases. This draws on the study's finding that both form-processing (PBI) and meaning-processing (TBI) produced learning gains. It is offered for pedagogic practice in EFL vocabulary teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need time to point out L1-L2 contrasts and phonological repetition patterns such as alliteration, assonance, rhyme and word repetition.
#### Constraints
- The study was small-scale, in one specific context, and lasted five weeks.

### Target Learners
- low-intermediate (B1-B2) tertiary EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of form and meaning of formulaic sequences (multiword expressions)
