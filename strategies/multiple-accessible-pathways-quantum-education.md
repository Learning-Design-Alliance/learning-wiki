# Provide multiple accessible pathways into quantum education, from short videos to courses to majors, at all institution types

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends developing information and pathways into quantum that meet students where they are, because students differed in whether they wanted short engagements such as videos and seminars or full courses, certificates, and degree programs. As the authors state, students "need information and accessible pathways that meet them where they are, ranging from videos to courses to majors". This is particularly important because most existing quantum courses are taught at PhD-granting institutions and are inaccessible to most US undergraduates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of formats (videos, seminars, courses, certificates, minors, majors) to match differing student schedules and backgrounds
#### Constraints
- Most undergraduate institutions cannot, at least in the near future, create courses introducing quantum beyond the physics curriculum, per the article

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate STEM students across disciplines
- Students at primarily undergraduate institutions, including HBCUs and MSIs

### Target Learning Goals
- Awareness of quantum concepts and quantum career pathways
