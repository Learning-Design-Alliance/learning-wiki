# Inhibition Support Strategies

## Description
These are strategies used to leverage student strengths in order to address challenges with inhibition, such as the ability to suppress attention to irrelevant input and to focus on pertinent stimuli or information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator must understand the principles of inhibition and have knowledge of each student’s individual strengths and challenges.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort to implement effectively. May not be effective for all learners in all situations.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Adaptable to various classroom and workplace settings. Can be tailored to individual needs and strengths. Scaffolded support can be provided as needed.

## Examples
- Let students know what order they will be called on to allow students to plan their response and wait until their turn. Graphic organizers and visual aids help students identify the most important information and focus in on it. Incorporate mindfulness techniques to help students develop their metacognitive skills and control their impulses.
