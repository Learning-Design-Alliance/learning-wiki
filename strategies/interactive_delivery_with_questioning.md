# Interactive Delivery with Questioning

## Description
Engage students through interactive discussions driven by questions and answers. Focus on personal interaction and immediate feedback, especially for struggling students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strong questioning skills, knowledge of student needs, and willingness to provide frequent feedback.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful management to ensure all students are engaged. May require additional resources to support individualized feedback.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Tailors questions to individual student needs and knowledge levels, providing personalized feedback and support.
