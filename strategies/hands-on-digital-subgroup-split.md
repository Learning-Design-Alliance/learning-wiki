# Split project work into hands-on and digital subgroups so participants follow their own interests

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
When learners develop creative projects, let them choose between a handcrafted and a digital artifact and split the group accordingly. The authors report this "enabled the participants to follow their own interests", letting them use their respective skill sets and approach the projects very enthusiastically; about one half worked with canvas and paint, the other half developed digital artwork.
