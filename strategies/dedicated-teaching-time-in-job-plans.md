# Include dedicated teaching time in consultant job plans to counteract time constraints

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that consultants who teach medical students in small group settings should have teaching formally recognised and time-protected in their job plans, since 78% reported inadequate time and 95% felt they "should have dedicated time for teaching". It cites GMC guidance that teachers "should have dedicated time in their job plans" to deliver educational responsibilities, and reports a desire to have teaching included in job plans to counteract time constraints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching must be specified within consultant job plans (supporting professional activities) at trust level
#### Constraints
- The article reports this as consultants' stated desire and GMC guidance, not as a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- medical students in small group clinical tutorials

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical education delivered by consultant clinicians
