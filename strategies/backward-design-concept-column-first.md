# Use backward design: complete the concept column first, mapping prior-knowledge, connecting, and application concepts before planning activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the Montebello project, teachers used a form of backward design (DiRanna et al., 2008) by beginning lesson designs with the concept column. First they identified the concepts students should possess as prior knowledge; next, the concepts students should explore to connect prior knowledge with the lesson concept; last, the concepts students should apply to demonstrate understanding. Only then did they plan the teacher and student columns.
