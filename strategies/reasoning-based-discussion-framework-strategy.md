# Develop a framework for skilled reasoning-based classroom discussion with discussion goals, discussable questions, norms, and talk tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q1`–`q4` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Critical Reasoning and Discourse Working Group reported that "many teachers, in any subject area, are not skilled in orchestratin g the kind of reasoning-based discussion required for discussing complex issues in science or society". It strongly recommended developing a framework including goals for discussion, planning for discussable questions, building norms for respectful and equitable discussion, and a series of talk tools for teachers to orchestrate skillful discussion with a large group of students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content training alone is insufficient; teachers need professional development to facilitate these discussions
#### Constraints
- The report notes the recommendation calls for the kind of detailed curriculum constructed for Exploring Bioethics

### Target Learners
- K-16 students
- science and social studies teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- critical reasoning through discourse
- evaluation of evidence
- role of science in a democratic society
