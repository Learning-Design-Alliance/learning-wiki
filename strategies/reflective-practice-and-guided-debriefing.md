# Reflective practice sessions, supervised role-play exercises and guided debriefing opportunities enable ANPs to process challenging encounters and develop empathy-based communication.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy holds that the inclusion of reflective practice sessions, supervised role-play exercises and guided debriefing opportunities could enable ANPs to process challenging encounters, build coping strategies and develop empathy-based communication. By preparing ANPs emotionally, in addition to clinically, programmes can enhance both practitioner well-being and the therapeutic alliance with patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reflective practice sessions and supervised role-play exercises
- Guided debriefing opportunities to process challenging encounters
#### Constraints
- Many trainee ANPs reported feeling emotionally overwhelmed when managing patients with personality disorder, citing uncertainty and frustration

### Target Learners
- postgraduate nursing students in Advanced Nurse Practitioner pathways
- qualified advanced nurse practitioners
