# Sixteen-point checklist of aspects to develop student motivation during the exhibit activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enumerates sixteen aspects to take into account to develop motivation in students during the exhibit, including "Despertar la curiosidad", generating a sensation of control, promoting responsibility, proposing goals with a moderate degree of difficulty, favoring independent learning, providing security and support, valuing effort and treating errors as part of learning, attributing success to controllable variables, insisting on the positive, realistic demandingness, being a good model, explaining the educational objectives, logical and ordered activities, varied capacities, fostering communication through group tasks, and applying content to situations close to the students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and monitors must attend more to the process than to the result, per the article's point that students need to see effort rewarded
#### Constraints
- The checklist is stated as design guidance for this extension activity; the article reports no test of the individual items

### Target Learners
- high-school (enseñanza media) students

### Target Learning Goals
- motivation for physics
- independent learning
- cooperative group work

## Claims

- [A holographic-exhibit extension activity was positively received by high-school students, with 100% reporting they liked the activity](../claims/holographic-exhibit-100-percent-liked-activity.md) [+W]

- [Students rated the exhibit's activities highly, with the initial lecture lowest at 78% of scores between 8 and 10](../claims/holographic-exhibit-quality-ratings-8-to-10.md) [+W]
- [No surveyed student decided against returning to future modules, and 15% offered criticisms and suggestions for improvement](../claims/holographic-exhibit-return-intent-and-suggestions.md) [+W]
