# Instructors and TAs should send the message that physics success is determined by effort and learning strategies rather than innate traits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that agreeing with the gender stereotype correlates with decreased CSEM performance for female students in the year-long calculus-based course, the authors recommend that instructors and TAs avoid propagating stereotypes in actions and statements and instead convey that "success in physics is primarily determined by effort and engaging in appropriate learning strategies rather than by something innate". They recommend professional development workshops help instructors and TAs encourage a growth mindset — that intelligence is malleable and can be cultivated with hard work and productive learning strategies regardless of gender or other characteristics.
