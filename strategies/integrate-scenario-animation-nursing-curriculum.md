# Integrate scenario-based animation videos into basic nursing course curricula as course material

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "Scenario-based animation videos should be integrated into the nursing education curriculum as course material," arguing such videos are instructive and build motivation and positive attitudes. They further suggest strengthening basic nursing courses with scenario-based animation methods and, in future work, integrating artificial intelligence, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, or Mixed Reality to enhance comparability and motivation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to devices and uninterrupted internet, as in the study's inclusion criteria; expert review and pilot testing of the animation before use
#### Constraints
- When more effective methods cannot be used, the authors state skill video training "should not be overlooked as a good alternative"

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate nursing students in fundamentals-of-nursing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor skill knowledge, e-learning motivation, and positive attitudes toward e-learning
