# Integrate AI-assisted virtual patient pre-class work with flipped classroom in-class discussion for clinical decision-making training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article implements a blended instructional model in which foundational content acquisition is shifted to pre-class e-learning and virtual standardized patient engagement, freeing classroom sessions for high-order learning activities such as synthesis of complex clinical data, inter-group debates, and simulation-based diagnostic and therapeutic decision-making. The AI component enables dynamic case generation, adaptive questioning, and automated feedback, while the flipped classroom structure supports targeted pedagogical interventions through intensive teacher-student discourse.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty training to transition educators from traditional lecturers to AI-literate facilitators
- AI platforms with virtual standardized patient capability and learning progress analytics
#### Constraints
- Single-centre trial with limited sample size may limit generalisability
- Relatively short follow-up period poses challenges in assessing long-term impact
- Relative contributions of AI versus flipped classroom components remain unclear

### Target Learners
- medical residents in reproductive medicine

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical decision-making
- theoretical knowledge in assisted reproductive technology
- self-directed learning
