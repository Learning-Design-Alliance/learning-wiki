## Description
The paper closes with a call to action urging AI researchers to become AI educators. Because K-12 students will search the internet to understand how AI works, researchers should "Think about how your research can be made into an easily available demo, resource, or activity that students and teachers can use and share". The authors also invite the community to reflect on the big ideas every K-12 student should know and on communicating with the public about AI advances.
