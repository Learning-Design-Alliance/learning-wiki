# Tailor physician leadership training program content to the specific needs and cultural aspects of each region

## Description
The article recommends that current physician leadership training programs be tailored to suit the specific needs and cultural aspects of each region, because the competencies valued by leaders differ across cultures and subgroups. The authors state that their data on regional and subgroup variation in competency ratings should help shape the content of leadership training programs to meet unique regional needs, and that further validity studies of the instrument with other samples and cultures are warranted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regional data on which leadership competencies local leaders value most, such as those generated by the cross-cultural survey approach used here
#### Constraints
- The authors note further validity studies of the instrument with other samples and cultures are warranted before generalizing

### Target Learners
- current and aspiring medical leaders in a given region

### Target Learning Goals
- leadership competencies valued in the trainees' cultural and regional context
