# Future SNA e-learning studies should examine two-mode networks and additional communication channels such as chat rooms, wikis, blogs and microblogs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that future research broaden the network structures and communication channels studied. It states: "Future studies should examine two-mode networks and communication channels like chat rooms, wikis, blogs and microblogs." This addresses the limitation that most existing work analysed one-mode forum networks, extending SNA to other e-learning interaction data types.
