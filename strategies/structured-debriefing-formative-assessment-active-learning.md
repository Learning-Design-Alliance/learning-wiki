# Use structured debriefing, formative assessment, and continuous feedback to consolidate learning in active learning activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 4 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Educators described reflective feedback and formative assessment as essential for consolidating learning. Structured debriefing after procedures helped students "engage in reflective thinking, correct mistakes, consolidate learning and turning the clinical experience into deeper and more sustainable learning." Continuous formative assessment including participation-based evaluation and symbolic grading incentives increased engagement. Educators also collected student feedback to refine instruction.
