# Set up frequent locally organized spirometry workshops for health workers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend establishing more frequent, locally organized spirometry workshops in Nigeria, arguing that even a one-day seven-hour combination of knowledge and practical training would likely have significant impact on participants' spirometry practice. They state: "There is need to set up more frequent locally-organized spirometry workshops since a one-day seven-hour effective knowledge and practical training would most likely have significant impact on participants' spirometry practice". The strategy includes a two-step competency model with a follow-up skills assessment (phase 2) and re-certification opportunities domiciled locally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified resource persons with formal spirometry training as faculty
- Funding for a local certification of skills course
#### Constraints
- The one-day course tested knowledge but did not test skill or competence; the phase 2 skills assessment was interrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- Health workers in Nigeria and other low- and middle-income countries who perform or interpret spirometry

### Target Learning Goals
- Competent performance, interpretation and quality assurance of spirometry
