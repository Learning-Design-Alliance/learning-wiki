# Extend mobile video availability beyond 1 week and support flexible viewing (streaming, closed captioning, technical support)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend longer video availability and student-suggested usability improvements for mobile skill-education videos. Because 1 week was too short to produce knowledge or skill gains, they "suggest that video clips be used for more than 1 week to improve the students' skills and knowledge competencies." Students additionally recommended online streaming instead of downloading and the addition of closed captioning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate user training and technical support for mobile-device use
- Sufficient intervention duration (beyond 1 week) for skill and knowledge differences to emerge
#### Constraints
- Findings are from one nursing skill at one nursing college with one posttest, limiting generalizability

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students learning clinical skills

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skill and knowledge acquisition via mobile-based video learning
