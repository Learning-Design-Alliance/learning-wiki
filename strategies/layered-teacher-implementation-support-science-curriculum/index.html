Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the evaluated trial, intervention teachers received a layered support package: "four days of preservice professional development to learn about the specific curriculum before the school year, a three-hour follow-up training before the curriculum was implemented, and access to a ‘science coach’ midway through teaching the unit that was tested." All teachers were also offered basic professional development related to the district’s new textbook. Beyond the study, more than 60 GEMS® network sites and centers provide ongoing training and support to teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to trained science coaches and network sites able to provide ongoing support
  • Time for preservice professional development before the school year

Constraints¶

  • The study does not provide information on the education or experience of teachers
  • Teachers in both groups were instructed to refrain from adding any activities to those present in their assigned curriculum

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school teachers implementing a new science curriculum

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Faithful implementation of a reform-based science curriculum
