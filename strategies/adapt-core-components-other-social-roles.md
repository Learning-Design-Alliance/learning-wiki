# Retain core empathy and supportive-communication components while adapting delivery to other social roles and contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends localizing the program by retaining its core interpersonal components while adapting delivery to new contexts. It states that "Core components of the program, particularly those related to interpersonal empathy and supportive communication, can be retained while adapting delivery methods to suit different contexts", suggesting extensions to workplace colleagues or teachers, reflective exercises about coworkers or students, and collaboration with schools, companies, and community-based organizations for sustainable delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intervention packages including user materials, a practical guide for program providers, and feedback training workshops
- Collaboration with schools, companies, or community-based organizations for localization
#### Constraints
- The current program is framed around the role of a friend; other-role extensions are proposed for future implementation, not yet evaluated

### Target Learners
- Adult public
- Workplace colleagues
- Teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Empathy and supportive communication toward people with cancer
- Stigma reduction in professional and institutional settings
