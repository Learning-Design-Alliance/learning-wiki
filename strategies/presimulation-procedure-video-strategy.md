# Show a video of the simulated procedure before hands-on simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before the simulation session, both groups were instructed on the methodology and assessment scale, and one group was additionally "shown a video of the simulated procedure". That group subsequently scored higher on global performance, execution and quality on the model. The article also reports that participants agreed "watching a video of the procedure prior to the simulation is important" (M = 4,53, SD = 0,507). This is a low-cost, easily replicated course-design step.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video of the simulated procedure (the authors supply one as a supplementary file)
- Time in the course schedule before the hands-on session
#### Constraints
- Tested in a single session with first-year residents with no prior laparoscopic experience; the video was shown in addition to, not instead of, the lecture and case videos

### Target Learners
- first-year general surgery residents with no prior laparoscopic experience

### Target Learning Goals
- technical performance on a first simulated laparoscopic appendectomy
