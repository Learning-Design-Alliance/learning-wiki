# Pursue two separate lines of enquiry: whether IVR improves learning in practice, and how IVR improves learning at the micro level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes a roadmap in which education researchers pursue "two separate lines of enquiry": (i) whether IVR improves learning in practice, using macro-level comparisons where IVR operates at maximal capacity and only training content needs to be valid; and (ii) how IVR improves learning, which demands identifying which subvariables constitute immersion, reducing them via factor analysis, and manipulating immersivity within an HMD for high experimental control.
