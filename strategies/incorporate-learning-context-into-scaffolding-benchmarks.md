## Description
The article recommends that future scaffolding benchmarks and alignment datasets should incorporate the learning context external to the chat, both as input to the chatbot and as grounding for its evaluation. It states: "We recommend that future scaffolding benchmarks and alignment datasets should incorporate the learning context external to the chat, both as input to the chatbot and as grounding for its evaluation." Rubrics should also measure the educational quality of student-driven interactions, and benchmarks should feature varied student behaviours such as low uptake and diverse learning contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Benchmarks must capture the student's side of the interaction and their learning context to evaluate situations resembling real-world use.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the Student Uptake metric is only a first step and does not capture whether uptake is productive for learning; techniques such as knowledge tracing and behavioural signals are cited as candidate methods, not yet validated for this purpose.

### Target Learners
- K-12 and higher-education students interacting with LLM tutor chatbots in self-study and platform settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective scaffolded assistance that adapts to diverse learning contexts and student-driven interaction patterns
