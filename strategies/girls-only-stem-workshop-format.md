# Run STEM outreach workshops exclusively for girls, taught and tutored entirely by female scientists and physics students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's workshop is delivered only to girls, with all tutors being female undergraduate and graduate physics students and the lessons taught by two female particle physicists. The survey supports the format: "The fact that it was only girls made me feel more comfortable expressing my doubts" drew 62% agreement, and 86% valued meeting other girls interested in science. The closing session invites a Chilean female scientist so the girls "can listen to the motivations and unique journey of a female scientist from a different field".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Female tutors previously trained in the workshop contents and detectors
- A closing talk by an invited female scientist from a different field
#### Constraints
- The article does not compare the girls-only format against a mixed-gender format, so the comfort benefit is self-reported rather than tested

### Target Learners
- High-school girls aged 15–16

### Target Learning Goals
- Confidence in STEM
- Comfort expressing scientific doubts
- Awareness of women's participation in science
