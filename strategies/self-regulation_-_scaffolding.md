# Self-Regulation - Scaffolding

## Description
Scaffolding can help learners learn how to manage their responses in an emotionally safe context. Self-regulation is the ability to alter and regulate our emotional and behavioral responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a facilitator.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find this activity to be limiting or not helpful.
