# Implement Exact Path with existing district staff supported by vendor professional development and Customer Success Managers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program is designed for implementation by a district's current teachers, school leaders, and district leaders, with guidance from Edmentum's dedicated Customer Success Managers, so "no additional staffing is required." Professional development is offered by the Professional Services Team, in alliance with subject matter coaches, focused on how to elevate instructional practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Edmentum's Customer Success Managers and the offered professional development opportunities for continuous learning.
#### Constraints
- Runs on all commonly used district devices and browsers without additional technology applications or devices.

### Target Learners
- K-12 learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics and English Language Arts standards achievement
