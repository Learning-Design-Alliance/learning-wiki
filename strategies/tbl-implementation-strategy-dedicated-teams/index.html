Implement TBL with dedicated teaching teams, systematic teacher training, and diversified assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend establishing a dedicated TBL teaching team including experienced TBL teachers and new teachers interested in TBL, selecting suitable course content, and providing systematic TBL training covering "teaching philosophy, methods and skills, and assessment feedback". Students should be reasonably assigned to groups with diversity in learning abilities and interests, and diversified assessment methods including self-assessment, peer assessment within groups, and teacher evaluation should be used. Teachers should flexibly adjust TBL teaching strategies based on student feedback and learning outcomes.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Dedicated TBL teaching team with experienced TBL teachers
  • Systematic TBL training for teachers covering teaching philosophy, methods and skills, and assessment feedback
  • Reasonable grouping of students ensuring diversity in learning abilities and interests
  • Diversified assessment methods including self-assessment, peer assessment, and teacher evaluation

Constraints¶

  • Teachers need to prepare in advance, including designing teaching units, preparing outlines for pre-study, test questions, and application exercises
