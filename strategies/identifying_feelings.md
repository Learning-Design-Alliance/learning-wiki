# Identifying Feelings

## Description
Learners identify, label, or match facial expressions and feelings. This can include categorizing feeling words or analyzing characters' feelings in literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources for identifying and labeling feelings, such as facial expression cards, lists of feeling words, or literary texts.
#### Constraints
- May require sensitivity to learners' emotional states and cultural backgrounds. Some learners may need additional support in recognizing and labeling emotions.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Increases emotional literacy and promotes understanding of emotional expressions.

### Personalization
- Activities can be adapted based on age, cultural relevance, and learning preferences.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' ability to accurately identify and label feelings in themselves and others. Review written or verbal responses for depth of emotional understanding.

## Impact
- Identifying and labeling feelings fosters self-awareness and emotional intelligence. Understanding one's own emotions is a foundational skill for social and emotional learning.
