# SOAP (Specific, Observable, Appropriate, Personal)

## Description
SOAP is an acronym that serves as a guide for teachers when selecting behaviors for students to self-monitor. Each letter represents a criterion to ensure the behavior is well-defined and suitable for the student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the SOAP criteria and the student's behavior and abilities.
#### Constraints
- The criteria require careful consideration and may not always be easy to apply, particularly for complex behaviors.
