# Use weekly scenario simulation with structured debriefing and operational skills training to develop new graduate nurses' competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention model combines weekly scenario simulation courses with teacher-guided debriefing and a linked operational skills session. Nurses role-play doctors, nurses, patients and family members in preset scenarios, then review experiences through "teacher‐guided debriefing using the 'reaction, understanding, summary and take‐home massage (RUST)'" method, compile key takeaways, and complete a related nursing-skill training session. This implementable recipe was delivered once a week for 2 months.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An expert team of clinical specialists to design scenarios and select content
- Teachers receiving standard training before the course begins
- Groups randomly assigned with role assignments and preparation tasks
#### Constraints
- Session duration varied from 35 to 70 min for simulation classes and 35 to 50 min for operational training, depending on the scenario theme

### Target Learners
- new graduate paediatric surgery nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- core clinical competence
- clinical decision-making
- teamwork and communication skills
