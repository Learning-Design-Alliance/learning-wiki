# Supplement game-based learning with reinforcement strategies such as periodic refresher sessions and adaptive difficulty to sustain patient safety competency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because performance gains from game-based learning diminished by two months, the article recommends implementing GBL supplemented with reinforcement strategies for surgical nurse patient safety training. "This study recommends the implementation of GBL supplemented with reinforcement strategies to enhance patient safety training among surgical nurses." The conclusion adds that future implementations should incorporate periodic refresher sessions and adaptive difficulty levels tailored to learners' experience, and calls for research on optimal reinforcement intervals and hybrid GBL-simulation models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial game-based learning program on patient safety risks
- Follow-up reinforcement (e.g., refresher sessions) after the initial training
#### Constraints
- The two-month follow-up provides only preliminary evidence of retention; longer-term assessment is needed, and the recommendation rests on a single-institution quasi-experimental study

### Target Learners
- Surgical nursing students and nurses in patient safety education

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining patient safety performance and reasoning competency over time
