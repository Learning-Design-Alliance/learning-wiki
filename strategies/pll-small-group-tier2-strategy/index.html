Deliver PLL as small-group Tier 2 instruction with one teacher per 4-5 students¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article specifies staffing requirements for implementing the 95 Phonics Lesson Library: it is best used in small group settings with 1 teacher to 4-5 students, as a Tier 2 intervention for students identified as Below or Well Below Benchmark. Implementation is supported by product training, customer success visits and coaching support, and requires no technology.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Small group settings with 1 teacher to 4-5 students
  • Complete 95 Phonics Lesson Library, virtual product training, customer success visits and coaching support

Constraints¶

  • No technology required; the program is materials-based

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary students below benchmark in phonics (grades 1, 4, and 5 in the reported studies)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Closing specific phonics skill gaps through targeted small-group instruction
