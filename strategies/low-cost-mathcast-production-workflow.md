# Low-cost mathcast production workflow: Wacom tablet, free drawing and screen-capture software, and YouTube hosting with analytics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends an inexpensive production method: the only purchase was a Wacom Bamboo tablet (approximately $80) so the instructor can use a stylus for on-screen sketches and writing, paired with two free Mac-supported programs, SketchBook Express and Quicktime Player, using Quicktime's "New Screen Recording..." to record a selected screen region. Videos are hosted free on YouTube, where privacy options control access and built-in analytics let the instructor "monitor views, demographics of viewers, playback locations, retention, comments, etc."
