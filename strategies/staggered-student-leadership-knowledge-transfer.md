# Manage annual student turnover by staggering leadership roles by year and maintaining redundant, accessible documentation platforms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To counter knowledge loss from graduation-driven turnover, PULSE-A pairs a year-based leadership pipeline with redundant documentation. The paper states that "the team typically prioritizes second and third year students taking on technical leadership positions, while advisory roles are reserved for fourth year students," giving graduating students a framework to focus on knowledge transfer. Documents and technical files are shared on GitHub and Google Drive for redundancy and access, with weekly journal documentation on Notion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Shared documentation platforms providing redundancy and easy access to all students (GitHub, Google Drive)
- Weekly journal documentation (maintained on Notion)
- A multi-year membership pipeline so continuing students perform technical development while graduating students transfer knowledge
#### Constraints
- The team has a steady turnover due to students graduating each year

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students on a multi-year project team with annual member turnover

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuity of technical knowledge across cohort turnover
- Knowledge transfer from graduating to continuing students
