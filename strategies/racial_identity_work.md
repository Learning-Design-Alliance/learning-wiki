# Racial Identity Work

## Description
Racial identity work involves examining one's own racial identity, confronting personal biases, and understanding how privilege and misinformation can affect work as an educator. This includes recognizing and addressing one's own privilege, learning about the racial identities of learners, and actively working against biases. By modeling this work for learners, educators show them how to advocate for themselves and disrupt systems of oppression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to be willing to examine their own racial identity, confront their biases, and take responsibility for their own education around race in America.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and commitment. Some educators may be hesitant to address issues of race and privilege.
