# Use TGT tournaments with team recognition tiers to motivate students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes teams' recognition as the stage where "students feel compelled to study physics because their group can be a super team with an average score of 50. It can be a great team with the mean score of 45. In addition, it can be a good team with the average score of 40". Teachers can implement tournament points pooled within teams and recognise teams at these printed average-score tiers.
