# Short Conversations (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Learners are given a sentence from the reading and engage in a conversation with a partner about the sentence. A rule is set that they cannot use more than three words each time it is their turn to speak. This activity encourages concise communication, critical thinking, and collaborative engagement with the text's content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires pre-selected sentences from the reading and clear explanation of the three-word limit rule.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find the word limit restrictive. The effectiveness depends on the relevance and clarity of the chosen sentence.
