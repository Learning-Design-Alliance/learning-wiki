# Explicitly Addressing Social Awareness

## Description
The approach involves specifically identifying student strengths and challenges related to social awareness and then employing aligned strategies to leverage those strengths to support learning. Social awareness, which includes understanding social norms and empathizing with others, is an essential element of emotional intelligence and social-emotional learning (SEL). Teachers model empathy, facilitate discussions about diverse perspectives, explicitly address emotions and social issues, and utilize current events and fictional characters to enhance understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need to identify student strengths and challenges, implement targeted strategies, and provide ongoing support. Professional development or training in social-emotional learning may be beneficial.
#### Constraints
- Requires a deep understanding of individual students and their social contexts, which can be time-consuming. Some students may be resistant to discussing social issues or emotions openly.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student interactions and communication skills to gauge their level of empathy and understanding. Collect student reflections on their experiences and growth in social awareness. Use formative assessments to track student progress and adjust strategies as needed.

## Impact
- Studies show that early social-emotional functioning is linked to future wellness and that social-emotional learning interventions improve academic outcomes, coping skills, and behavior.
