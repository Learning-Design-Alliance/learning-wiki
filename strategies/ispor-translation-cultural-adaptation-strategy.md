# Translate and culturally adapt an assessment instrument following the ISPOR Task Force process with originator approval

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports a concrete translation workflow for adapting the Dutch MAAS 2.0 assessment sheet into German, carried out "Based on the criteria of the ISPOR Task Force for Translation and Cultural Adaptation". The steps were: obtain permission from the original author, create two independent translations by the project team, develop a consensus version with cultural adaptation, and obtain the originator's approval of the consensus version.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Permission from the original instrument authors
- Two independent translations and a consensus version
- Originator approval of the final version
#### Constraints
- The back-translation step was omitted because the Dutch project manager knew German — a deviation the article states explicitly

### Target Learners
- medical educators and assessment developers

### Target Learning Goals
- valid cross-language adaptation of communication-assessment instruments
