# Use aesthetically striking, dual art-science images with the four-step Interdisciplinary Image Reading routine in astronomy lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends performing Interdisciplinary Image Reading on carefully chosen images to improve astronomy education. Selection criteria it demonstrates are images with "great potential within the Art and Science approach" and strong aesthetics that "might awake different feelings from its observers" and invite interpretations "going beyond a merely objective analysis". The routine moves learners from shape description, through technical content, to relational and interpretive readings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An image chosen for the routine should support both scientific and artistic discussion and be accompanied by detailed, well-written descriptions.
#### Constraints
- Results of the reading can vary with the analyst: the authors state the analysis "leaves room for different interpretations".

### Target Learners
- beginner students in astronomy

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting astronomical images
- connecting art and science perspectives
- avoiding alternative concepts from misleading imagery
