Manage TPS discussion challenges by having the instructor circulate among pairs and by using classroom response systems for evaluating responses and managing time¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article reports two implementation challenges of TPS: "to get the students to really be engaged during the discussion" and "the noise level during the discussion". As precautions it recommends that the instructor walk around amongst pairs during the activity and encourage students to focus on the concept tests and/or problems, and that classroom response systems be used for evaluating student results and managing instructor time in small and/or large groups.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor presence during pair discussion to keep students focused on the concept tests and/or problems

Constraints¶

  • Engagement and noise-level challenges were observed in the application procedure of this study

Target Learners¶

  • first-year university students in large or small class sections

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustained engagement during pair discussion
  • efficient in-class evaluation of student responses

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement think-pair-share through a three-step sequence: individual thinking, pair discussion, and whole-class sharing with instructor feedback
  • Think-Pair-Share (Turn & Talk)
  • Cold Call To Sample Pair Responses
