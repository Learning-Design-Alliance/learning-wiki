# Provide targeted, early-curriculum empathy development opportunities for disciplines with lower entry empathy such as Nursing and Pharmacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Where tightening admissions is not feasible, the article recommends curriculum-based empathy development. It states "Targeted empathy development opportunities for Nursing and Pharmacy students may be a more feasible way forward" and that "Implementing these early in the curriculum could help prevent declines in empathy over time". Pedagogical tools mentioned include perspective-taking exercises, communication training, and exposure to the arts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum space early in the training period for empathy-focused activities such as perspective-taking exercises, communication training, and exposure to the arts.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the current and anticipated future shortage of nurses and pharmacists is a likely disincentive to tightening admissions criteria in these disciplines.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate Nursing and Pharmacy students

### Target Learning Goals
- Empathy and empathic care intentions for person-centred patient care
