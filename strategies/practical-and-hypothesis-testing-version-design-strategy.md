# Design experimental comparisons that combine practical design alternatives with hypothesis-testing contrasts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends that researcher-practitioner collaboration proceed by "collaboratively designing experimental comparisons of different versions of MOOClets." These versions contain "both practical design alternatives of instructional interest and experimental contrasts designed to test alternative hypotheses about how people learn and interact with technology." This lets each experiment simultaneously evaluate designs instructors care about and test scientific hypotheses.
