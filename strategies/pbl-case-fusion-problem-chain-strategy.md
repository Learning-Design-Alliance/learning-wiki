# PBL-case fusion teaching via a problem chain around real clinical hematological disease cases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article integrates problem-based learning with case-based teaching "by developing a problem chain around real-world clinical hematological disease cases, with students as the main body and center of teaching activities". In the chronic myelogenous leukemia example, a film protagonist's story stimulates interest, then sequential questions (what additional laboratory tests are needed; which diseases are possible) guide students through diagnosis, bone marrow morphology at different stages, diagnostic techniques, and outcome analysis. The authors state this "strongly engages students in applying professional theoretical knowledge to practical clinical dilemmas, fostering their evidence-based thinking and problem-solving prowess".
