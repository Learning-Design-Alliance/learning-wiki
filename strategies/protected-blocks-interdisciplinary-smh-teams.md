# Schedule regular protected blocks for interdisciplinary meetings, planning, and case consultation, with structured reflection routines

## Description
The article recommends institutionalizing collaboration through organizational structures rather than ad hoc interactions. Schools can 'schedule regular, protected blocks during the school week dedicated to interdisciplinary meetings, collaborative planning, and case consultation' and implement structured routines such as guided debrief sessions, reflective discussion protocols, or peer feedback processes. This grounds the competencies—communication, reflective practice, shared accountability, coordinated teamwork—in routine, supported practice.
