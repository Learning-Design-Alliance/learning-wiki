# Make EPME training mandatory in post-graduate GP training programmes and consider permanent e-learning tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that regular training in the external post-mortem examination be intensified beyond undergraduate education: "Regular training in EPME should be intensified not only in undergraduate medical education but also be mandatory in post-graduate training." It further suggests e-learning tools, citing an online tool from the University of Muenster whose learning outcome was evaluated positively with a gain of confidence in conducting a real EPME, could be permanently implemented in post-graduate training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses on the EPME topic implemented as an obligatory component of post-graduate training programmes
#### Constraints
- The article notes an increment of practical experience is not always possible, and further longitudinal observational data are needed to establish whether course information translates into improved practice

### Target Learners
- GP post-graduate trainees in Germany

### Target Learning Goals
- Performing a complete and accurate external post-mortem examination and completing the death certificate
