# Use log-file data and stakeholder-access research to refine learning-analytics feedback

## Description
The article's future-research recommendations are concrete next steps for learning-analytics feedback design. "Besides using questionnaires, log-file data (e.g., mouse clicks representing visits of the JIT and VIZ-modules) can be used to examine whether a specific module or a combination of both modules is most beneficial for learning." Further research should examine which kinds of curricula and work domains are amenable to analytics-enhanced E-portfolios, and whether and in what cases a supervisor should have maximum access to feedback messages in order to stimulate learners' self-assessment and reflection.
