# Use a four-quadrant attitudes survey to give instructors actionable evidence for lab course transformation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using E-CLASS as a common evaluation tool for lab course transformation by comparing students' personal and expert-aligned attitudes with their perceptions of grading requirements and practice frequency. The survey "is designed to guide and assess lab course transformation by giving instructors actionable evidence about their lab course," and it supports evaluating students "at all stages of an undergraduate program" to assess the longitudinal impact of a lab sequence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration at beginning and end of semester to measure shifts; instructor access to grade and practice quadrant results
#### Constraints
- Initial results come from one institution's physics lab sequence; cross-institution use was planned but not yet reported

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in physics lab courses, introductory through advanced

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring and transforming student attitudes and beliefs about experimental physics
