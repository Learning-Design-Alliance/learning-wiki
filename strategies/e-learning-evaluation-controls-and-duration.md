# Use control groups with conventional education and study durations of at least 2 months when evaluating e-learning for oral health

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors draw two design recommendations from their review. First, because prior positive meta-analyses often used control groups with no education or education only at baseline, "control groups should include conventional education methods to examine the effectiveness of e-learning." Second, since behavior change takes roughly 66 days, "a study period of at least 2 months or more is required to encourage participants to improve their behavior using e-learning education," with continued follow-up after the intervention ends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Control groups must receive a conventional education intervention rather than no intervention or baseline-only education
#### Constraints
- The authors state most included studies lasted about 3 months and it is unknown whether participants continued using e-learning afterward

### Target Learners
- adults
- adolescents
- children

### Target Learning Goals
- oral health knowledge, attitude, and practice
- oral hygiene behaviors
