# Foster supportive peer environments and emotion regulation skills to support academic development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that universities move beyond curriculum delivery to foster supportive social-emotional environments. Educators "can facilitate this by integrating cooperative learning tasks that build peer networks", and social-emotional learning could be incorporated into orientation programs or general education curricula. Students are encouraged to cultivate peer relationships, engage in collaborative learning, seek help, and develop emotion regulation skills for managing academic pressure.
