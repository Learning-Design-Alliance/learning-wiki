# Regroup at-risk students (CC, EP, CO) from several classes into separate target-group sessions with interventions distinct from substance-specific content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For students the standard program did not reach, the article recommends designing separate prevention provisions. It states that "the cluster of the  current curious (CC) needs additional and different intervention" and that because only few at-risk students sit in one single class, youngsters from several classes should be summarized into a group and informed specifically. Teachers are assigned responsibility for identifying and grouping these at-risk students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers have to assume the responsibility for the identification and grouping of these at-risk students
#### Constraints
- Measures to increase self-esteem should be separated stringently from substance-specific contents for this consumption group

### Target Learners
- currently curious (CC)
- experimenter (EP)
- consumer (CO) adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
- self-esteem
- drug prevention for at-risk students
