# Stakeholder-consultative intervention development process following the MRC framework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a structured development process, informed by the Medical Research Council framework for developing and evaluating complex interventions, in which the intervention was formulated through empirical and consultative work with stakeholders. The activities included a review of psychosocial and educational interventions, telephone and postal surveys of UK practitioners, focus groups with young people with diabetes and their carers, observational work of consultations in three clinics, experimental role-play, three one-day stakeholder workshops, and local piloting with young patients. The article recommends that research of this kind be developed through consultation with patients, families, healthcare professionals and health economists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consultation with key stakeholders including patients, families and the multidisciplinary healthcare team
- Development phase activities completed before trial evaluation
#### Constraints
- Development activities are reported separately and are not evaluated within this protocol

### Target Learners
- paediatric diabetes healthcare teams and the young people and families they serve

### Target Learning Goals
- developing a feasible, acceptable communication skills intervention
