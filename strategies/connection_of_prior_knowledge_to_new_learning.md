# Connection of Prior Knowledge to New Learning

## Description
Building connections to prior understandings and experiences to facilitate new learning. This involves activating learners’ existing knowledge and linking it to new concepts. Connecting prior knowledge enhances understanding, retention, and transfer of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-assessments, discussions, brainstorming activities, and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires awareness of learner prior knowledge and experiences. Some learners may have gaps in their prior knowledge that need to be addressed.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All learners, especially those with diverse backgrounds and experiences

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance understanding, improve retention, facilitate transfer of learning, activate prior knowledge

### Affordances
- Enhances understanding by linking new concepts to existing knowledge. Improves retention by creating meaningful connections. Facilitates transfer of learning to new contexts.

### Personalization
- Activate learner prior knowledge through discussions, brainstorming, and pre-assessments. Link new concepts to learners’ existing knowledge and experiences. Provide opportunities for learners to share their own experiences and perspectives.
