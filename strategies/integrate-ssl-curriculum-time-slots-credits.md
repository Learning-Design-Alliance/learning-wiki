# Integrate SSL into the nursing curriculum by blocking out dedicated time slots and attaching credits to its utilisation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy addresses the time-constraint barrier by embedding self-directed simulation learning into the formal curriculum rather than leaving it as an optional, uncredited activity. The article recommends that "its integration into the curriculum by blocking out time for SSL and attaching credits to the utilisation of this modality by student nurses are recommended to enhance its use". Participants themselves recommended dedicated SSL time slots on the timetable and replacing some clinical hours with simulation-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum managers must reconcile SSL integration with statutory SANC requirements for education and training accreditation
#### Constraints
- At the host HEI SSL is not compulsory and bears no credit across undergraduate professional degree programmes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate student nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills acquisition
- self-directed learning
- lifelong learning
