# Teachers emphasize the value and joy of learning rather than relying on rewards or punishments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom messaging strategy in which mathematics teachers frame learning through messages highlighting the personal value and enjoyment of learning new things, rather than applying external pressure through rewards or punishments. The article recommends that "instead of pressuring students through rewards or punishments, what seems more reasonable is for teachers to rely on messages that highlight the value and joy that learning interesting things brings". It extends the same messaging to parents, optionally combined with autonomy-supportive practices such as providing rationales for demands or linking mathematical learning to life goals.
