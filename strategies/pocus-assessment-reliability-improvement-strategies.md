# Improve assessment reliability by training raters, screening video quality, and adding a not-applicable response option

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion proposes concrete fixes to raise inter-rater reliability of performance assessments: "developing a standardized program to train the raters on the assessment tool virtually or in-person", adding "three columns to select from (yes, no, or not applicable)", and including "a set of criteria to assess the quality of the videos" in future studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Raters currently received no training on the assessment tool; a standardized training program would need to be developed
#### Constraints
- Expert-novice comparisons for validity arguments have several inherent limitations, and recruiting medical students with expert-level ultrasound training was difficult

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- postgraduate trainees
- practicing physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- reliable assessment of point-of-care ultrasound clinical competency
