# Teach prompt structures and prompt literacy to all future organization members

## Description
Based on its scenario analysis, the article concludes that "all future organization members should acquire knowledge about prompt structures and develop prompt literacy." It cites Knoth et al. (2024) indicating more advanced prompt engineering skills are associated with higher‐quality LLM outputs, and Federiakin et al. (2024) on different levels of prompting knowledge relevant to varying extents across the scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prompt literacy extends knowledge of how to interact with AI, including transforming frame conditions into clear prompts
#### Constraints
- Different levels of prompting knowledge are relevant to varying extents in the scenarios

### Target Learners
- future organization members
- students in leadership education

### Target Learning Goals
- effective human–AI communication
- goal‐directed use of generative AI
