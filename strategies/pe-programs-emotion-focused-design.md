# PE teachers should design programs that develop emotional recognition, relationships and interpersonal communication alongside physical skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that PE programs attend to emotions, not only motor content. It states that "PE teachers should design educational programs that focus on the development of effective relationships, recognition of negative and positive emotions, and interpersonal communication, not only on developing physical sports skills, knowledge, techniques, and strategies". This follows from the finding that emotions relate to motivation, resilience, grades and activity habits.
