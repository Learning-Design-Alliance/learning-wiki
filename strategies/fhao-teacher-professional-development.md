# Multi-format teacher professional development for implementing the Facing History and Ourselves curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's professional development model combines an intensive Summer Institute, introductory workshops, online courses, and ongoing follow-up support. The report states that 'A Summer Institute provides an intensive five- or six-day seminar focusing on issues related to identity, violence, bigotry, power, and conformity,' in which teachers explore ways to apply the content and approaches to their own teaching. Follow-up classroom support continues during the school year in person, by phone and email, and on the website, supplemented by a lending library, guest speaker lists, videos, and technical assistance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In the evaluated study, each FHAO teacher had attended the FHAO Institute and had taught the FHAO curriculum for at least three years before the study
#### Constraints
- Professional development carries costs: Summer Institute fees of $650 for commuting and $900 for resident participants, online courses $300, workshops $35-$250

### Target Learners
- middle and high school teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- applying Facing History and Ourselves content and approaches to teaching history and ethics
