# Integrate escape room-inspired physical activity challenges with curriculum content in progressive phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy embeds gamified, time-bound escape room challenges combining moderate-to-high-intensity physical activity with academic content, delivered three times weekly in 50-minute sessions across four progressive phases. The article states "The activities were designed to integrate physical exertion with curriculum content in Italian language and literature through escape room-inspired challenges." Sessions pair a standardized warm-up, a core activity, and a cool-down, with tasks escalating from vocabulary relays to timed literary debates and HIIT-based problem-solving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and trained sports educators co-conduct sessions to ensure academic relevance and physical safety
- Standardized lesson plans and attendance logs to ensure uniform delivery
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as a 16-week pilot RCT in one Italian secondary school with class-level randomization of two second-year classes

### Target Learners
- secondary school students aged 14-16

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension and textual analysis in Italian language and literature
- teamwork, decision-making, and problem-solving
- stress regulation and executive function
