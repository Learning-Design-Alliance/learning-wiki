# Establish standardized, shared training scenario banks across institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Participants requested "a collection of standardized, case-based training scenarios" gathered from different hospitals that institutions could adapt, described as "a scenario bank we could draw from and adapt." The article identifies development of standardized training scenarios and support for demonstration videos as institutional and policy support needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- support for producing multimedia educational materials
- inter-institutional personnel exchange and educational networks
#### Constraints
- High cost of tabletop training tools was reported as a barrier to shared scenario-based training

### Target Learners
- infection control personnel
- physicians and nursing personnel

### Target Learning Goals
- scenario-based response preparedness
- reduced burden of creating training from scratch
