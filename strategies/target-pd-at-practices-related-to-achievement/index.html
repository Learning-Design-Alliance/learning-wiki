Strategies

Target PD at the instructional practices most strongly related to student achievement, such as reducing errors and imprecision¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study, q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that knowledge and most practice measures were not correlated with achievement while Errors and Imprecision was, the authors recommend future PD and research focus there. The snapshot states: "future research might focus on identifying PD that will improve this aspect of practice" and that research "might also seek to identify other aspects of knowledge and practice to target with PD that are more strongly related to improved student achievement".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • PD designs should target aspects of knowledge and practice demonstrably related to student achievement

Constraints¶

  • The study PD did not significantly improve Errors and Imprecision, so the recommended target was not achieved by the studied program

Target Learners¶

  • fourth-grade teachers
  • elementary school teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reducing errors and imprecision in teachers' math instruction
  • improving student math achievement

Claims¶

  • Teacher math knowledge and instructional practice were generally not correlated with student achievement, except Errors and Imprecision [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Target Errors and Imprecision and achievement-linked knowledge and practice in future content-focused PD
