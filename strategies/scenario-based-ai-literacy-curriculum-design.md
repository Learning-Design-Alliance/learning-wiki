# Use intentional, scenario‐based curriculum design with AI literacy embedded across subjects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article calls for "intentional, scenario‐based curriculum design in which AI literacy is embedded across subjects to prepare learners for AI's evolving roles, from a collaborative tool to an autonomous partner." Educators should derive required competencies from likely human–AI constellations and align learning outcomes, teaching methods, and assessment practices accordingly, drawing on the ED‐AI Lit framework's emphasis on critical evaluation, responsible use, and effective collaboration with AI systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Embed AI literacy systematically across curricula rather than treating AI solely as a technical add‐on
#### Constraints
- Further specification for real‐life, organization‐specific contexts, and future developments will be necessary

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- future organization members

### Target Learning Goals
- safe, critical, and empowering AI use
- calibrating trust and maintaining critical thinking
