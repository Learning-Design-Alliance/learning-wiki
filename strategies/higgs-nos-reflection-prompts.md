## Description
The article supplies a set of discussion prompts (Box 1) to accompany the Higgs discovery narrative, asking how theory guided experiments, why Higgs bosons cannot be observed directly, what was inferred, where creativity and social dimensions appeared, and whether scientists were fully certain. Its rationale is that "Teaching NoS is most effective when using an explicit-reflective approach." Adapted versions of the same prompts close the mystery box sequence.
