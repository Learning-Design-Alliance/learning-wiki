# Wraparound student supports: navigator, financial supports, and job placement assistance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond integrated instruction, I-BEST provides a bundle of non-instructional supports intended to keep students engaged. A career navigator provides, as the report lists, "intake, orientation, job readiness, mentoring, and job placement services." Students can receive Opportunity Grants covering up to 45 credits of tuition and up to $1,000 a year for books and supplies, plus tutoring, career advising, emergency child care, emergency transportation, and college success classes. ACE and Accelerating Opportunity implementations added partnerships with Workforce Investment Boards to connect students to employers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated I-BEST navigator (coach) available to students
- Funding for support services such as uniforms, transportation, licensure testing, clinical placements, and internships
#### Constraints
- I-BEST as studied in Glosser et al. (2018) did not include specific employment and job placement services

### Target Learners
- Low-income adult community college students in occupational training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Persistence and engagement in training through program completion
