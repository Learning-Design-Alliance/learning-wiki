# Hybrid AI question-development workflow: AI generation with standardized prompts, then expert review, psychometric validation, and continuous monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a structured integration framework in which AI systems first generate base questions using standardized prompts, followed by expert review and modification, psychometric validation, and implementation with monitoring. The authors state the framework "emphasizes the importance of human oversight while leveraging AI capabilities to enhance efficiency in question development," and describe a hybrid implementation model supported by continuous performance-metric feedback, regular content updates, and faculty development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mandatory expert review and modification of all AI-generated content, with multi-stage validation processes and regular updates to maintain alignment with current medical knowledge
#### Constraints
- The authors state AI-generated questions' current limitations in discrimination capability suggest their optimal use is in formative assessment rather than high-stakes testing

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students on urology clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing urological clinical competency across subspecialties
