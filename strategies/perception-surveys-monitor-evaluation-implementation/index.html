Use teacher and administrator perception surveys to monitor evaluation-system implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief demonstrates monitoring a district-wide evaluation overhaul by surveying the educators subject to it. It "draws on survey data from more than 19,000 teachers and nearly 800 principals and assistant principals to measure their views of REACH," tracking satisfaction, perceived practice change, fairness concerns, stress, and effort across years. This lets implementers detect declining enthusiasm and implementation problems early, as the author notes year two is "a crucial year for ironing out the details of implementation."

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to large numbers of teachers and school administrators willing to respond to perception surveys

Constraints¶

  • Perceptions are self-reported views, not direct measures of instruction or student outcomes

Target Learners¶

  • teachers
  • principals and assistant principals
