# Involve secondary-school students in building interactive components for outreach activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the 2024/2025 school year the project collaborated with a secondary school in Gallipoli (Lecce), where a group of students developed a custom card-reading system based on Arduino to be integrated into one of the room's stations. The authors state this collaboration "provided an opportunity to connect formal education with public engagement, and encouraged student engagement with applied physics and engineering."
