# Summarizing

## Description
Summarizing involves integrating important information from a text or problem to create a concise overview. In math, this means justifying the answer and evaluating the success of the plan for solving the problem. It also includes explaining the problem-solving strategies used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clear understanding of the topic and the ability to identify key information.
#### Constraints
- May require additional support for learners who struggle with identifying key information.
