# Test surgical simulators with novices and track learning-curve metrics before clinical translation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that the simulator "should next be tested with novices" and that markers such as stitch position, cerclage height, cerclage circumference, or destruction of the bulging amniotic sac could be evaluated to demonstrate a learning curve, followed by studying translation to the clinical setting. This defines a staged validation pathway from expert face validity to novice skill acquisition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulator suitable for both expert and novice users, with measurable performance markers such as those developed by Hall et al.
#### Constraints
- The current validation covered experienced obstetricians only; the effect of repeated simulator use on less skilled obstetricians has not yet been investigated.

### Target Learners
- novice obstetricians and gynecologists

### Target Learning Goals
- acquiring McDonald cerclage technique before operating on patients
