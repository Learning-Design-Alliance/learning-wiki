# Strengthen PK-20 partnerships through annual summits of policy makers, practitioners, and teacher education faculty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A specific implementable activity the article reports: New Hampshire state-level initiatives include "strengthening of PK-20 partnerships through annual summits of state-level policy makers, district- and school-based practitioners, and teacher education faculty." These summits bring the stakeholder groups together to support coherence across levels and layers of education and to build buy-in from all stakeholders for a context of innovation around performance assessments.
