# Implement mandatory structured ED rotations plus simulation-based, ward-based ECG training to build competence and confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that curriculum planners implement mandatory, structured emergency department rotations during both clinical years and internship, and that clinical educators develop simulation-based and practical ward-based ECG training modules. The authors state these should "build both competence and the self-confidence that our analysis shows is critically linked to performance," and also recommend national standardized ECG benchmarks and feedback mechanisms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated ED rotations available during clinical years and internship
- Supervised practice opportunities with positive reinforcement and mastery experiences
#### Constraints
- Evidence is cross-sectional and observational, so causal effects of rotations are not established; generalizability is limited to the four studied Ethiopian institutions

### Target Learners
- medical interns
- final-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- ECG interpretation competency
- recognition of critical cardiac conditions
- diagnostic confidence
