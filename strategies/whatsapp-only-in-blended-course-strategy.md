# Use WhatsApp mobile learning activities only within a blended course strategy that integrates face-to-face and mobile learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation directs teachers to embed WhatsApp activities inside a blended design rather than as a standalone tool. It states: "the researchers advise teachers and actors in mobile teaching and learning to use WhatsApp mobile application only to pursue learning activities in a blended course integrating both face to face learning and mobile learning." The recommendation rests on the study's finding that the blended group outperformed the fully in-class group on both achievement and attitudes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A blended course structure combining face-to-face sessions with mobile-mediated activity hours, with instructor presence in the mobile space
#### Constraints
- The article grounds this advice in a single course in one discipline and recommends replication in other online disciplines

### Target Learners
- university students, including nontraditional students

### Target Learning Goals
- course achievement and positive attitudes toward the learning process
