# Mentor-apprentice co-teaching with weekly full-team studio preparation to sustain active-learning implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each first-time instructor (the apprentice) is paired with an experienced co-instructor (the mentor), regardless of faculty rank. The apprentice leads a studio section and delivers roughly one-third to one-half of lectures, while "the mentor provides feedback to the apprentice on his or her implementation of the active learning pedagogies embedded in the curriculum." After a mentored semester the apprentice can become a mentor, expanding the cohort of faculty using active engagement. The full team, including TAs and Learning Assistants, meets each Friday for two hours to work through every upcoming studio question collaboratively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses are co-taught by two or more faculty members each semester
- The apprentice must lead one of the studio sections
- A two-hour Friday meeting of the full instructional team prepares the coming week's studios

### Target Learners
- faculty and graduate and undergraduate teaching assistants teaching reformed large-enrollment courses

### Target Learning Goals

- awareness of common student conceptual and problem-solving difficulties
