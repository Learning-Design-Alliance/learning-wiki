# Integrate bonded-retainer monitoring and maintenance knowledge and skills into dental curricula and postgraduate courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that knowledge and skills for monitoring and maintaining bonded retainers be built into dental education, because many practising dentists feel insufficiently competent and request further training. As the conclusions state, "Knowledge and skills regarding monitoring and maintenance of bonded retainers should be integrated into dental curricula and postgraduate courses." The discussion frames this as closing a gap in the undergraduate dental curriculum where these topics are insufficiently covered.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dental curricula and postgraduate courses must cover knowledge and skills related to bonded retainers, which the article says are currently insufficiently covered
#### Constraints
- The article focuses on bonded retainers only; how dentists handle removable retainers remains unclear

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- general dental practitioners in continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring and maintenance of orthodontic bonded retainers
- recognising unintentionally active bonded retainers and their side-effects
