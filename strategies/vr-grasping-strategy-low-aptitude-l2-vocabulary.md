# Use VR-based grasping of virtual referent objects to support vocabulary learning in low language aptitude learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's pedagogical outlook proposes that, with affordable VR devices, "vocabulary learning with VR objects could support LLA learners in an efficient way", allowing ubiquitous, accessible training "at any time in facilitation of multilingualism" and personalized programs that take a learner's aptitude and individual learning needs into account. The strategy operationalizes the study's AVOG condition: learners view and hear L2 words with referent objects and grasp the virtual objects during training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR environment in which learners can grasp virtual objects corresponding to the meanings of the to-be-learned words, as in the study's Deep Space cave
#### Constraints
- Findings are specifically applicable to the learning of L2 concrete words with meanings already well-understood in the learner's L1, per the article's stated limitation

### Target Learners
- Adult low language aptitude foreign-language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of foreign language vocabulary
