# Match students to educational approaches by assessing self-regulated learning beliefs and readiness for self-directed learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "future research into the differentiation of learning should focus on the matching of PT students to different educational approaches." Such matching would need to assess and address individual students' beliefs and illusions about self-regulated learning and determine their willingness or readiness to engage in SDL, using self-reported questionnaires as reported in medical and nursing education. The article observed that some students preferred IBL to SDL, with three students switching conditions.
