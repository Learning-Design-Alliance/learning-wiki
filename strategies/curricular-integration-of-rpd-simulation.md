# Embed simulation tools into official curricula as standardized preclinical modules and pursue multi-institutional validation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future iterations move beyond standalone use: "future iterations should explore embedding this tool into official dental curricula as a standardized preclinical training module." They further suggest structured integration modeled on successful simulation platforms in medical education, and multi-institutional collaborations to validate scalability, similar to collaborative frameworks used in Association for Dental Education in Europe guidelines for simulation-based training. They also propose incorporating haptic feedback and augmented reality for surveying and undercut analysis in future versions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured curricular integration and multi-institutional collaboration to validate scalability
#### Constraints
- The article notes a paucity of data on the efficacy of such training modules for dental students and calls for future studies on long-term effectiveness and clinical transferability

### Target Learners
- preclinical dental students
- dental education programs

### Target Learning Goals

- integration of simulation into preclinical curricula
