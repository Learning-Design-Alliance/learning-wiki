## Description
The article recommends that future work broaden how learners interact with textbooks beyond reading, using advanced human-computer interaction, smart interfaces, and generative AI. It states that "it is important for future work on intelligent textbooks to develop new modes of interaction between learners and textbooks, e.g., through drawing, sketching, highlighting (Kim et al., 2020), annotations, and conversations." Such modes would open new fronts in data analysis to better understand learners.
