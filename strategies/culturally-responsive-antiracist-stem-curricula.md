# Incorporate culturally responsive, anti-racist, and multicultural curricula in higher education STEM programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that practitioners "consider pushing for the incorporation of culturally responsive, anti-racist, and multicultural curricula within higher education STEM programs" to address race and racism at the Immediate Contexts level. It cites literature showing culturally responsive curricula positively impact achievement, academic efficacy, literacy skills, cultural identity, and ethnic pride among students of color, and that multicultural curricula benefit white students' ethnic identity, democratic attitudes, and intellectual engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula must reflect the racial and ethnic diversity of the student body and expose students to diverse voices and perspectives in STEM courses
#### Constraints
- The recommendation targets U.S. higher education STEM programs where POC have been absent from curricula for decades

### Target Learners
- Students of color in STEM higher education
- White students in STEM higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement, academic efficacy, cultural identity, and ethnic pride in STEM
