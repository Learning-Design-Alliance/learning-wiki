# Use post-reading group discussion to broaden thinking and strengthen comprehension and inference

## Description
Carried out after reading, group discussion aims at further activating students' cognition of the context. Because individuals' differences in life history lead to differences in memorized information, exchanging ideas on the same text broadens students' ways of thinking and background information. The teacher raises questions about the text, encourages group discussion and group reporters to exhibit results, and may have students raise their own questions or write a composition on the same theme, making them more active in seeking relevance in the material.
