# Sequence clinical learning experiences deliberately from skill-based through integrative experiences in the nursing curriculum

## Description
The review reports Nielson et al.'s recommendation that "clinical learning experiences should be incorporated sequentially into the nursing curriculum in this order: skill-based, case-based, concept-based interventions, focused direct client care, and integrative experiences". This scaffolding strategy structures the curriculum so that clinical learning builds progressively in complexity. It is one of three sub-themes clustered under the scaffolding-the-curriculum theme.
