Sponsor teachers to attend workshops and seminars on the effective use of the demonstration teaching method¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Among the article's recommendations, teachers should be sponsored by government and other educationally friendly stakeholders to attend workshops and seminars on the appropriate and effective use of the demonstration teaching method, so as to realize enhanced learning outcomes in chemistry among senior secondary school students in Kaduna state. This addresses the discussion's point that persistent failure in chemistry could be arrested if teachers were better equipped with effective strategies.

  • Senior secondary chemistry students (via their teachers)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Enhanced learning outcomes in chemistry through effective demonstration teaching

Claims¶

  • Female students taught by demonstration method scored higher than female students taught by lecture method [+W]
  • Male students taught by demonstration method scored higher than male students taught by lecture method [+W]
  • Students taught chemical bonding with the demonstration method scored higher on the post-test than students taught by lecture method [+W]
  • No significant difference in achievement between male and female students taught with the demonstration method [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide in-service training, workshops, seminars and conferences on ICT utilization for teachers
  • Adopt active-participation strategies such as CRAIS for mathematics teaching and train teachers through seminars and workshops
