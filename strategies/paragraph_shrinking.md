# Paragraph Shrinking

## Description
A PALS activity focused on identifying main ideas and summarizing paragraphs. Students take turns reading paragraphs and shrinking them to the most important points. The 'Coach' asks the 'Player' to skim the paragraph again and answer question a second time. Ask students to state the main idea in 10 words or less which will encourage them to monitor comprehension while taking turns reading.
