# Implement contemplative meditation practices across educational settings, including disadvantaged ones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that policymakers and practitioners bring meditation-based programs into the educational system as a school-based intervention for children's positive development. They suggest implementing "contemplative practices in various educational settings (Shapiro et al., 2015), including the most disadvantaged ones", so that research can identify the most appropriate meditation practice for specific populations and target domains such as relaxation, attention, empathy, and self-regulation.
