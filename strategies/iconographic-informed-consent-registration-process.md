# Iconographic informed-consent process during registration using visual flowcharts with minimal text

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a registration consent process that is "Roughly but adequately explanatory in purely visual iconography and flowcharts with almost no text," with additional plain-language English text available. It aims to fill in background knowledge about how information may be used, and addresses both scientific data used in research and demographic information used in population research about citizen scientists. Iconography should depict diverse scientists to disrupt the stereotype that science is done by white men.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consent must address both sets of data: science data used for research and personally identifying demographic data used for population research
- Icons should be designed to expand the variety of people shown in professional science
#### Constraints
- A link to terms of service or a brief text block alone is insufficient because some volunteers will not read text and some do not speak English

### Target Learners
- non-English-speaking volunteers
- volunteers with varying language skills and educational levels

### Target Learning Goals
- substantial understanding of what one is consenting to regarding data use and credit
