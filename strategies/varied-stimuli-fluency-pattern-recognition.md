# Provide varied, complex stimuli and novel contexts during fluency training so learners use pattern recognition alongside rather than instead of analytical processing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators implementing fluency teaching techniques "should provide ample opportunities to practice the targeted behavior across a variety of contexts with novel stimuli and situations of growing complexity". This guards against the risk that encouraging rapid pattern recognition promotes surface learning rather than deeper understanding of underlying physiology. The strategy pairs fluency-based repetition with stimulus variety to encourage both non-analytic recognition and analytical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a range of exemplar stimuli of growing complexity beyond the initial training set
#### Constraints
- The article notes encouraging pattern recognition "could potentially encourage more surface learning than a deeper understanding of the physiology behind ECG abnormalities" if varied practice is not provided

### Target Learners
- medical students
- novice clinical learners

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced pattern recognition and analytical reasoning in clinical interpretation
