# Reasonable Feedback

## Description
Reasonable feedback involves providing feedback that is manageable and not overwhelming for the student. This can be achieved by selecting a focus for feedback with each assignment, such as transitions or evidence. Another strategy is to provide a 'Glow and a Grow' comment, highlighting one area the student did well on and one area that needs improvement. Students can then refer back to these comments and goals before writing future assignments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to carefully select the focus of their feedback and provide balanced comments. Students need to reflect on and apply the feedback to future assignments.
#### Constraints
- May require teachers to limit the scope of their feedback. Students must be willing to engage with feedback and use it to improve.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Prevent students from becoming overwhelmed by feedback and encourage them to take meaningful steps for revision and growth as writers.

### Affordances
- Makes feedback more manageable and less discouraging for students. Helps them focus on specific areas for improvement.

### Personalization
- Teachers can select feedback focuses based on the assignment and the individual needs of the student. Glow and Grow comments allow for personalized praise and constructive criticism.
