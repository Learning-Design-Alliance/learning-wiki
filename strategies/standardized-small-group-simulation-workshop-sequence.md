# Run standardized 60-minute simulation workshops in small groups of 5 with a fixed sequence of introduction, demonstration, supervised practice, and feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each workshop session lasted approximately 60 minutes and "followed a standardized sequence: brief introduction, demonstration, supervised practice, and feedback." Sessions included faculty supervision and periodic feedback to tutors. The article reports this structure supported significant knowledge gains and high satisfaction across 2 academic years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained senior student tutors and faculty supervision with periodic feedback to tutors
#### Constraints
- Knowledge tests were workshop-specific and not validated psychometric instruments, so gains reflect immediate learning only

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills acquisition and teaching competencies for tutors
