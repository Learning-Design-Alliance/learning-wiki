Strategies

Target Errors and Imprecision and achievement-linked knowledge and practice in future content-focused PD¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3–q4 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the study's finding that the PD changed teacher knowledge and Richness of Mathematics but not student achievement, the report recommends that future research focus on PD improving the Errors and Imprecision dimension of practice, since it was the only teacher measure associated with student achievement. It also recommends identifying "other aspects of knowledge and practice to target with PD that are more strongly related to improved student achievement."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • PD designs must be identified that improve the Errors and Imprecision dimension, on which the study PD had no statistically significant impact

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation rests on one study in which the measured knowledge and practice dimensions were generally uncorrelated with achievement

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary teachers of mathematics

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reduce incorrect, unclear, and imprecise use of math in instruction
  • Improve student math achievement through teacher PD

Claims¶

  • The PD significantly improved the Richness of Mathematics dimension of instructional practice (23 percentile points) but not Student Participation or Errors and Imprecision in spring [+M]
  • Teacher knowledge and most practice dimensions were not correlated with student achievement; only Errors and Imprecision was (association −0.20) [+M]
  • Despite gains in teacher knowledge and practice, the PD did not improve student math achievement; treatment students scored 2 percentile points lower [+M]
  • The PD did not improve fourth-grade student math achievement; treatment students scored 2 percentile points lower on both measures [+M]
  • A 93-hour content-intensive math PD program improved fourth-grade teachers' math content knowledge [+M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Target PD at the instructional practices most strongly related to student achievement, such as reducing errors and imprecision
