Design daily school programmes that schedule meals, play and frequent short recreation periods alongside lessons¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Dr. Chaillou's proposed daily programme, presented at Section IX of the Congress on School Hygiene, takes account of the needs of the whole organism rather than lessons alone. The article reports that his plan "prescribes the hours for meals and for play as carefully as the hours for lessons," and that recreation periods occur at frequent intervals with none longer than one hour. It is a time-tabling recipe for schools, reported as advocacy rather than a tested result.
