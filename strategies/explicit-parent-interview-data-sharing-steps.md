# Add explicit parental interview, data sharing, and weekly progress sharing steps to existing field-based assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Family Collaboration Project extended the existing Pupil Impact Assignment by adding three explicit components: interviewing the student's parent, sharing baseline data with the parent, and sharing progress with the parent at minimum weekly. The article reports that "more explicit direction to interview parents and systematically discuss data and student progress was needed to establish real parent–teacher candidate experiences."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A field-based assignment already in place
- Mentoring teacher directed to support family connection
#### Constraints
- Completed over eight weeks within one clinical seminar course

### Target Learners
- Special education teacher candidates

### Target Learning Goals
- Building parent-teacher relationships
- Professional communication with families
