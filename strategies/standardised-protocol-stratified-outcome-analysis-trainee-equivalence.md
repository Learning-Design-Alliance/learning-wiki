# Standardised protocol plus stratified outcome analysis to demonstrate trainee equivalence to experienced clinicians

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that trainers use learning theories and structured supervision so that trainees can perform procedures with outcomes equivalent to experienced clinicians. Standardisation of the ET protocol "has been shown to reduce the differences in the success rate of the various clinicians", and controlling for co-variables limits the between-clinician range further. The unit analysed its own outcome data and shared the finding with patients that clinician grade did not influence cycle outcome.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate direct and indirect supervision of early procedures
- A standardised procedure protocol and unit outcome data collection
#### Constraints
- Not all variables can be accounted for, which can contribute to remaining differences between clinicians; findings reflect one unit's training culture

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate trainee fellows in assisted reproductive treatment

### Target Learning Goals
- Reaching clinical competence in embryo transfer with outcomes matching experienced clinicians
