# Use structured think-aloud sessions with a semi-structured moderator guide to capture student feedback for iterative AI tool refinement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy runs think-aloud sessions in which students verbalize thoughts and expectations while using an AI tool in real time, guided by a semi-structured moderator's guide covering preparation, session flow, and post-session reflection. Moderators use minimal prompts such as "What do you think?" and "What do you expect to happen next?" to capture authentic feedback. Sessions in the case study included a 5-10 minute introduction, a 20-30 minute main task, and a 10-15 minute post-task reflection, with insights documented for subsequent development cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured moderator guide to ensure consistency across sessions, prepared moderators familiar with the tool, and recording arrangements
#### Constraints
- The article notes concurrent verbal reporting can disrupt the natural flow of thought, and that maintaining a second recording device added complexity for students in this study

### Target Learners
- middle school students
- K-12 learners

### Target Learning Goals
- usability evaluation of AI learning tools
- student voice in tool refinement
