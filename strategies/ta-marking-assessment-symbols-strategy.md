# Mark assessments with visible symbols, identity checks, and integer scores

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A marking recipe for homework, assignments, quizzes and examinations. The guide instructs TAs to "Mark with a red pen/pencil" and avoid a color similar to the students' writing, to check that students write their name and student ID, and to place a tick for a correct solution, a cross for a wrong one, and a circle for an ambiguous or understandable solution before assigning the corresponding score. Empty sheets receive a long line across the paper and a zero. Scores are summed per item and written on the front page.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A provided solution key against which the corresponding score is assigned.
- A total score convention per assessment, here either 10 or 100 points unless another instruction is provided.
#### Constraints
- Half-point or fraction scores are avoided; the guide says to simply award an integer.

### Target Learners
- graduate teaching assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent and transparent assessment marking
