# Provide remote surgical feedback and virtual didactics when in-person training is restricted

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the context of COVID-19 restrictions on surgical training, the article recommends remote feedback and virtual teaching formats. It states that "written or verbal feedback can be provided remotely, either through faculty review of uploaded recordings or hosted video chat sessions", and lists virtual libraries, webinars, take-home box trainers, and interactive virtual mentoring modules as tools to preserve training opportunities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to video- and phone-based conferencing platforms with screen sharing, and faculty capacity to review uploaded operative recordings.
#### Constraints
- The article notes practice in a training center will always differ from real operating-room experience with actual patients, given patient-specific characteristics.

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees and residents
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of surgical skills training during restricted access to operating rooms
- remote feedback on operative technique
