# Consider Socio-Cultural Factors

## Description
Acknowledge that access to technology and digital literacy can vary based on socio-economic background, social environment, and cultural factors. Consider the digital divide and provide resources to bridge the gap. Offer affordable technology options, ensure access to internet and devices, and provide culturally relevant training materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of social and cultural factors, partnerships with community organizations, funding for technology and resources
#### Constraints
- Addressing systemic inequalities can be challenging, requires ongoing effort and commitment
