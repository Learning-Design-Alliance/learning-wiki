# Make students explicitly aware that LLM feedback may contain errors and provide mechanisms to flag erroneous feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because erroneous feedback went almost undetected by students, the article recommends that LLM-based feedback systems "make users explicitly aware that the generated feedback may contain errors and that it should not be accepted uncritically." It further recommends that "the system should provide simple mechanisms for students to flag potentially erroneous feedback, enabling continuous monitoring and improvement", alongside critical oversight by educators and initiation of a design cycle to improve the system for a given context.
