## Description
The article concludes that challenges to SBL implementation center on instructor knowledge and resources, and states "There is a serious need for a simulation-based training program for faculty members and students." This strategy directs stakeholders to plan, develop, and evaluate SBL while addressing the reported weaknesses in trained staff and facilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Findings come from one institution with a small sample and lack psychometric testing of the instrument, per the article's stated limitations

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- medical school faculty

### Target Learning Goals

- knowledge, skills, and attitude domains of learning
