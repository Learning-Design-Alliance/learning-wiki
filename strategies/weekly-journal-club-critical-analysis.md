# Weekly journal club with student-led critical analysis of bioinformatics research articles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the second half of the year, weekly journal club meetings have each student present a bioinformatics research article, which the group then critically discusses "for the methodology and research design". The authors report that students initially find it very difficult to analyse critically rather than simply summarise, but that with time and experience they improve their critical and presentation skills to an acceptable level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly meetings over the second half of the year, with each student presenting at least one article.
#### Constraints
- Early sessions are difficult: students initially summarise rather than critique.

### Target Learners
- postgraduate students new to the discipline's research literature

### Target Learning Goals
- critical evaluation of research methodology and design; presentation skills
