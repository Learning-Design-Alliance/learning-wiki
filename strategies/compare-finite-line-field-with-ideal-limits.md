# Compare the field of a finite line of charge analytically or numerically with the fields of an infinite line and a point charge to develop intuition about idealizations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom activity for introductory electromagnetism: the electric field of a finite line of charge is compared, analytically or numerically, with the fields of an infinite line and of a point charge. The authors report the analysis "can be performed in the classroom in just a few minutes" and believe it helps students develop physical intuition about electromagnetic idealizations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions are posed so students answer both without making numerical calculations, or the numerical version is supported by a spreadsheet analysis.
