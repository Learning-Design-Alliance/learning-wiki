# Prioritize longitudinal and interventional region-specific research with standardized PSMU and AP measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's authors recommend that future work move beyond the cross-sectional designs that dominate the current MENA evidence base. They state that "longitudinal and interventional studies with clearer reporting practices are warranted to advance understanding of this relationship," alongside region-specific research addressing the limited number of studies, inconsistent PSMU measurement, variable operationalization of academic performance, and lack of data on mediating mechanisms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized PSMU measurement and clearer operationalization of academic performance across studies
#### Constraints
- Current evidence is limited to cross-sectional designs, so causal inferences remain limited

### Target Learners
- university students in the MENA region

### Target Learning Goals
- establishing the causal relationship between problematic social media use and academic performance
