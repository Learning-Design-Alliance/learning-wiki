# Discussing Race with Students

## Description
This strategy involves creating a classroom environment conducive to complex conversations about race through activities that help students notice and celebrate similarities and differences. It emphasizes designing products in partnership with educators and racially diverse communities to facilitate these conversations, using multimedia tools to share diverse stories, and providing discussion guides developed with experts of color. Additionally, it suggests incorporating inclusive icons and creating feedback loops for continuous improvement, ensuring edtech solutions center learners of color.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a commitment to creating inclusive and equitable learning environments. Access to multimedia tools, diverse stories, and expert-developed discussion guides. A willingness to address and mitigate personal biases.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure respectful and productive conversations. May require additional training and support for educators and facilitators. Potential for discomfort or resistance from individuals with differing perspectives.

The Black Genius Profile captures Black learners’ genius using the language of the elements. Parents typically complete a Black Genius profile for their child(ren) for a number of reasons, including but not limited to: 1. as an advocacy tool when a child is being treated unfairly or being deprived of opportunities at school, 2. as a cultural tool to help the teacher learn how to be more responsive to the child’s culture when teaching 3. as a personal learning tool for the parent to learn more about the child’s interests, skills, expectations, strengths, and growth areas in order to support the child’s healthy development.
