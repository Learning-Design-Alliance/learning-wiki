# Establish TA-instructor and TA-student communication channels at the start of the semester

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has the TA open the term by contacting the instructor and, where applicable, the students. The guide advises that at the beginning of the semester, "the TA can introduce him/herself by sending a message containing contact information to the respective instructor", and for online classes post an introduction via the LMS. Announcements should be posted in both Korean and English because the student body is diverse and BSM classes are taught in English. Students' message inquiries should be answered promptly, with persistent cases forwarded to the instructor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The course uses a learning management system (the Canvas-based i-Campus platform) for announcements in online or multi-section settings.
#### Constraints
- Bilingual Korean-English posting is specific to institutions with a diverse, partly Korean-unfamiliar student body.

### Target Learners
- graduate teaching assistants
- undergraduate students in Basic Science and Mathematics modules

### Target Learning Goals
- smooth course administration
- timely resolution of student questions
