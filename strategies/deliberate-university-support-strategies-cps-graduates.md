# Universities can support CPS graduate retention through deliberate support strategies such as scholarships, test-free admission, billing and hold policy changes, and tutoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article highlights institutional actions that universities can take to bring in and retain CPS graduates. It notes that "Northern Illinois and Western Illinois Universities have created deliberate support strategies to bring in and retain CPS graduates, such as scholarships, test-free/optional admission, changes to billing and hold policies, and tutoring support," and reports both universities saw double-digit increases in enrollment and retention of CPS graduates even during the COVID-19 pandemic. The strategy targets institutional policy and practice rather than student behavior alone.
