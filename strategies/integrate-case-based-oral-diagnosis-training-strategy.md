# Integrate case-based learning, visual diagnostic training, and interdisciplinary teaching into medical curricula to strengthen oral diagnosis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that medical curricula strengthen oral health education by combining several educational approaches. They state that "Integrating case-based learning, visual training modules, interdisciplinary teaching, and short clinical rotations in oral medicine may enhance diagnostic competence and improve patient care." The recommendation responds to the identified gap: limited curricular exposure to oral diseases, separation between medical and dental education, insufficient visual diagnostic training, and exam-oriented learning that limits applied clinical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires curricular integration of oral health content into medical education rather than separate dental pathways
#### Constraints
- The article notes the effectiveness of such interventions has not yet been evaluated and calls for further research

### Target Learners
- Medical students and interns
- General practitioner trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnostic reasoning for oral diseases
- Recognition of systemic conditions with oral manifestations
- Appropriate referral decisions
