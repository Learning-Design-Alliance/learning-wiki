# Spaces that Foster Creativity

## Description
Traditional spaces designed in the industrial revolution were designed to maximize efficiency. For the 21st century, employers tend to look for teams that work organically in collaborative groups to solve problems. It would make sense to structure classrooms to reflect the needs of our global economy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of activity options such as art supplies, games, and toys. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the playful spaces.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve creativity, improve problem solving skills, enhance collaboration

### Affordances
- Promotes creativity and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred activities. Provide a variety of playful activity options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more fun.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that playful spaces can increase engagement, improve creativity, and enhance collaboration. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
