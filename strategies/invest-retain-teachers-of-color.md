# Invest in retention efforts for teachers of color that improve working conditions and provide personal and professional growth opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The third policy lever is to invest in retaining teachers of color through improved working conditions and opportunities for personal and professional growth. Proposed solutions include creating a reimbursement program aiding pre-service and in-service educators, pushing state leaders to allocate funding to specific retention efforts, and creating a task force. Minnesota's $6 million investment in 2022 and 2023 for affinity groups and other retention strategies prioritizing teachers of color is cited as a promising practice.
