Train instructors in learner-centered practices through modules emphasizing collaboration, interaction, and caring about students¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The researcher recommends that "instructors' training modules should be developed to define and stress the aspects of learner-centeredness." Modules should address individual and social learning needs, illustrate successful collaboration techniques using online resources, have instructors develop a collaborative activity, and stress interaction, knowing students, and making them feel valued.
