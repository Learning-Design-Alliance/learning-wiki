# Mitigate instructor cognitive load in online teaching with dual monitors, more breaks, and a second instructor

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because much non-verbal feedback is lost online, instructors must deliver the lesson while also scrolling through video and chat pages, which the authors say increases their cognitive load significantly. Their experienced mitigations are using multiple monitors — one displaying students' video streams, the other the instructor's own stream or slides — providing more breaks between lessons or reducing lesson duration, and having a second instructor available to assist, address questions, and provide additional feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors need access to a second monitor; the authors acknowledge this is subject to the affordance of the resources available to the instructors
#### Constraints
- The authors state there is no easy solution to the increased cognitive load; these measures relieve it only slightly

### Target Learners
- instructors teaching synchronous online classes

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining instructor awareness of student engagement and understanding
