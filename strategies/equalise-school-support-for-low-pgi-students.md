# Focus equality policy on equalising school support for students with lower EA-PGI rather than on residential-area interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that policy to reduce social inequality in achievement in Norway "should focus on tackling unequal support across schools for children with difficulties", because school-driven inequality is concentrated among students with lower EA-PGI. They suggest policymakers find ways to equalise opportunities between schools for students equally low on the EA-PGI distribution. They add that identifying residential factors as intervention targets might not greatly reduce achievement differences of social origin.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identifying how schools differentiate students with similarly low EA-PGI, since the specific school factors driving the interaction remain uncharacterised
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is grounded in the Norwegian egalitarian context with no tracking and nearly universal local public school attendance; generalisability is limited to participants of European ancestries

### Target Learners
- students with lower EA-PGI in comprehensive public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing school-driven differences in academic achievement
