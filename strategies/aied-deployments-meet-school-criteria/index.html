Design AIED deployments to meet school operational criteria such as support, stability, and availability¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To move AIED systems into regular school usage, the paper recommends that deployments satisfy schools' operational needs. It states that "AIED system deployments need to meet the criteria of schools – such as available technical support and troubleshooting, stability and continuous availability." Because research prototypes often fail these criteria, the paper suggests professional methods such as commercial spin-offs, noting universities can hardly deliver stable systems with continuous support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Continuous support structures and stable, reliable systems that function in everyday school use

Constraints¶

  • Research test prototypes developed as part of a PhD thesis often fail to meet these criteria, and profitability requires a larger clientele

Target Learners¶

  • school students and teachers in regular classroom deployments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustained practical use of adaptive tutoring technology in classrooms

Related Strategies¶

  • Tailor AI deployment to specific applications and stakeholder groups rather than applying a one-size-fits-all integration approach
  • Strengthen implementation scaffolding for new edtech rollouts with routines, guided practice, and change management grounded in observed workflows
  • Embed product rollout into existing routines with structured cadences, teacher champions, and stipends to accelerate adoption
