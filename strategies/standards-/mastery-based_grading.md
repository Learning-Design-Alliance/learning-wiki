# Standards-/Mastery-Based Grading

## Description
Standards-/Mastery-Based Grading emphasizes grading based on the level of mastery of learning objectives. Explicit criteria are developed and made transparent to students, ensuring grades reflect learning rather than gaming the system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear learning objectives, transparent grading criteria, systems for tracking mastery levels
#### Constraints
- May require significant restructuring of curriculum and assessments. Requires thorough development of learning objectives and criteria.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Ensure grades reflect mastery of learning objectives, provide clear grading criteria to students, reduce focus on accumulating 'points' rather than learning

### Affordances
- Ensures clear criteria for grades, promotes student understanding of learning goals, encourages mastery of content

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by setting personalized mastery goals for individual learners. Flexible assessments can accommodate different learning styles.

## Impact
- Clear learning objectives ensures that the criteria for grades are clear to students and that grades meaningfully reflect learning as opposed to a student’s ability to game the system by earning “points”.
