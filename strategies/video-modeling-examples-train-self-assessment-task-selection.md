# Train self-assessment and task-selection with video modeling examples before a self-regulated learning phase

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before independent task selection, students observe video models who perform a problem-solving task, then think aloud while rating invested mental effort, self-assessing performance by assigning one point per correctly solved step, and applying a task-selection rule to pick the next task. The study shows this "relatively simple intervention can help students gain more from self-regulated learning", improving both posttest performance and transfer of task selection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modeling examples must show the model writing out each problem-solving step while thinking aloud, with steps gradually built up on screen
#### Constraints
- The way video modeling examples are designed can have a significant impact on the success of the training

### Target Learners
- secondary education students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-assessment accuracy
- task-selection skills
- problem-solving performance under self-regulated learning
