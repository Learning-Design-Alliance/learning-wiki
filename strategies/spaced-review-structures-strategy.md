# Embed low-burden spaced review structures such as a 48-hour recap and a 1-week revisit with automated reminders

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors propose that "Future implementations could explore embedding simple, low-burden review structures—such as a 48-hour recap and a 1-week revisit—and providing automated reminders to prompt learners to consolidate key material". This strategy translates meta-analytic evidence on spaced e-learning into concrete course design for voluntary online clinical skills courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Automated reminders to prompt learners
- Availability of recordings and supplementary review materials on the platform
#### Constraints
- Postclass review was not an independent predictor of final scores in the adjusted model; its role was selective, characterizing excellent learners

### Target Learners
- physicians
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- long-term retention of clinical knowledge
- consolidation of key material after live sessions
