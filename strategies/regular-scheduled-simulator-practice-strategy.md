# Schedule regular, frequent simulator practice sessions supported by mandatory structured curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends organizing simulator training as regular, frequent practice rather than occasional longer sessions, noting high-fidelity simulators' motivational factor makes trainees "more likely to continue their practice on a regular basis", which is described as superior to occasional exercises at wider intervals. Institutions should allocate protected work time and attach structured assessment, since unassessed self-training goes unused.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mandatory curriculum with structured assessment of self-training results
- Allocated work time for self-training
#### Constraints
- The article reports self-training sites are used infrequently even when 2 or more working hours are made available, and that non-use is independent of simulator type

### Target Learners
- surgical residents in laparoscopic training

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition and maintenance of laparoscopic technical skills
