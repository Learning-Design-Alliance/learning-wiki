# Partner ARISS-style contact programs with digital citizen-science platforms to overcome infrastructural barriers to STEM outreach in underrepresented regions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that regional organisations partner with established outreach programs such as ARISS and use digital platforms to mobilise widespread participation. It states that "effective networking, shared goals and digital platforms can overcome infrastructural barriers that traditionally limit STEM outreach and space science education in Africa". Going forward, PACS e-Lab is developing a campaign for the adoption of ARISS experiments across institutions throughout Africa while maintaining exposure to robotic telescope projects.

## Design Implications
