# Design LLM-based edtech so educators can mediate harms, vet tools through independent regulators, and explore educator-led tool-building

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion sets out recommendations for educator-centered edtech development. The first is that "Edtech providers should design tools in a way that facilitat es educator mediation of LLM harms", building opportunities for mediation into tools to increase educator autonomy. The second calls for regulators to develop centralized, clear, and independent reviews of LLM-based edtech, leveraging existing organizations such as the What Works Clearinghouse to vet tools and create searchable repositories. The third proposes exploring how to entrust tool-building to educators themselves, who listed uses such as generating lesson plans aligned to curriculum standards.
