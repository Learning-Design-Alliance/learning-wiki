Pair Tier 2 intervention delivery with professional development workshops and in-class coaching visits¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a support model in which interventionists attended two five-hour professional development workshops covering lesson content, small-group management, and validated instructional practices, with opportunities to practice sample lessons and receive feedback. In addition, "each ROOTS group received between three and four in-class coaching visits" from former educators, facilitating an observation-feedback loop of direct observation followed by debriefing on delivery quality and implementation fidelity.
