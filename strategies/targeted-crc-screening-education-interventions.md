# Targeted educational interventions (lectures, smartphone applications, low-cost reminder tools) to improve trainee knowledge of screening ages and non-invasive modality follow-up

## Description
The article recommends focused education to close trainee knowledge gaps in colorectal cancer screening, stating that "Education interventions geared toward improving resident knowledge and screening guidelines will be important to improving awareness among trainees." It points to a smartphone application offering guideline information, a formal lecture on racial disparities, and a low-cost educational health maintenance card as interventions discussed in the literature, and to streamlining education on age-specific recommendations and follow-up protocols.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education must be delivered consistently across multiple specialties at the institutional level, which the authors identify as a continuing challenge
#### Constraints
- A cited lecture intervention improved endoscopic exam performance but produced no significant changes in rectal exams or fecal occult blood testing

### Target Learners
- surgical and non-surgical residents and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of USPSTF colorectal cancer screening initiation ages
- appropriate follow-up protocols for non-invasive screening modalities
