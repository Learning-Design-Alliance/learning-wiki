# Design future manikins with adjusted anatomy for Asian populations and simulated airway obstruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on observed limitations, the authors recommend future manikin designs for Chinese populations adjust thyromental distance dimensions, since current models' distances were longer than in Chinese patients, and add foreign-body obstruction features similar to Laerdal's Choking Charlie, as current models do not typically include such scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Population-specific anatomical data (e.g., thyromental distance cut-offs) to set manikin dimensions
#### Constraints
- Upper airway obstruction was not simulated in any of the studied manikins

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine trainees
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- difficult airway management
- foreign body airway obstruction removal
