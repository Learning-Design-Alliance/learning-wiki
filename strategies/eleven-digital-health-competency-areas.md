# Eleven proposed competency areas for the next generation of pre-licensure nursing informatics education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address identified gaps systematically, the review recommends expert-consensus research on new competencies and enumerates eleven areas. As printed: "New competencies should be considered in the areas of: (1) telehealth and virtual care delivery; (2) AI literacy, responsible use, and evaluation; (3) sensor data, wearables, and remote monitoring;" plus ethical/legal/regulatory frameworks, human-technology teaming, digital equity and inclusive design, risk management and security, ethics and governance leadership, innovation and implementation science, environmental sustainability, and digital therapeutics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research to elicit expert consensus on the next generation of competencies, building on existing frameworks
#### Constraints
- The review states these areas "should be considered", not that they are established competencies

### Target Learners
- pre-licensure (baccalaureate) nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- telehealth and virtual care delivery
- AI literacy and responsible use
- sensor data and remote monitoring
- digital ethics and governance
