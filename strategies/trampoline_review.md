# Trampoline Review

## Description
Trampoline Review is a kinesthetic learning strategy that involves learners jumping on a small trampoline or performing other motor activities while reviewing information. The combination of physical movement and mental rehearsal enhances memory consolidation and engagement. This method is particularly effective for learners who benefit from kinesthetic learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small trampoline or other safe space for motor activities, learning material to be reviewed, and supervision to ensure safety during physical activity
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for learners with physical limitations, requires careful supervision to ensure safety, may be disruptive in some classroom settings
