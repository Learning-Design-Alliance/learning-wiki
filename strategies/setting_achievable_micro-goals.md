# Setting Achievable Micro-Goals

## Description
Micro-goals involve breaking down larger objectives into smaller, manageable steps. This strategy supports consistent, incremental progress, which can foster a growth mindset and increase confidence. Achieving small wins repeatedly over time helps learners build momentum and develop a positive attitude toward learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful planning and breaking down of larger goals into smaller, achievable steps. Requires regular monitoring and feedback to track progress.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of tasks. Learners may need guidance in setting appropriate micro-goals.
