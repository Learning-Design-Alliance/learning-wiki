# Use MOOCocracy learning culture themes to inform digital course design and online learner engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy recommends that designers of massive open online courses and other digital courses use the MOOCocracy learning culture themes as a design resource. The commentary's stated purpose is that "key ideas, value, and future research implications are addressed in relation to MOOC design using the MOOCocracy themes", positioning the themes as a bridge between research on MOOC learning culture and practical course design decisions aimed at improving online learner engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the MOOCocracy themes as articulated in Loizzo and Ertmer (2016), and attention to the learning culture of the target online course
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in a response commentary on a single prior study rather than in new empirical evidence

### Target Learners
- online learners in massive open online courses
- digital course designers and instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- improving online learner engagement through course design

### Affordances
