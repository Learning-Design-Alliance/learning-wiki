# Develop structured, standardized training pathways for simulation technicians integrating technical and pedagogical domains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on convergence between technician self-assessments and manager perspectives, the article recommends structured professional development frameworks that integrate technical and pedagogical domains. It states that "the convergence of views between technicians and managers underscores the need for structured professional development frameworks that integrate both technical and pedagogical domains," and that findings can support "standardizing training, recruitment, and certification of simulation technicians." Manager-identified priorities (educational/didactic competencies, equipment maintenance, medical simulation) specify the content of such pathways.
