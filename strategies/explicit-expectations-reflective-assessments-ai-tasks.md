This strategy packages the article's three named activity types for integrating generative AI into an academic writing course. The article states: "These activities include setting explicit expectations for AI-assisted work, integrating reflective assessments to evaluate AI outputs, and designing tasks that strengthen students' critical thinking and academic integrity."
