# Leave some apparatus readings unadapted to promote collaborative work between blind and sighted students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Rather than adapting every reading, the authors deliberately left the on-screen frequency readout unadapted so the blind student must work with a sighted partner. They write: 'we did not do that in order to promote collaborative work with other student. In this case the blind student's partner should not be visually impaired.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mixed-ability student pair, with the partner able to read the computer screen
#### Constraints
- This specific choice applies to the frequency readout; other adaptations (audio node location, audio position reading) preserve the blind student's independent measurement role

### Target Learners
- mixed pairs of blind and sighted students in shared laboratory sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative experimental work
