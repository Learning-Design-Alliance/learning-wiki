# Adopt blockchain for education in developing countries through a World Bank-style Community of Practice where ministries and organisations run pilots together

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter recommends a community-of-practice model for developing countries, modelled on the World Bank's Blockchain for Education Community of Practice, described as "a hub for anyone interested in using blockchain for positive change in educational institutions". Members share insights from research and experimentation; educators and policymakers in public school systems are the key target demographic. The chapter proposes this be adopted in underdeveloped countries with the World Bank's EdTech Team, with participating organisations and Ministries of Education conducting pilots and contributing to accessible global public goods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating organisations and Ministries of Education work together to conduct pilots and contribute to accessible global public goods
#### Constraints
- The strategy is proposed specifically for underdeveloped countries, with support from the World Bank's EdTech Team

### Target Learners
- students in developing countries
- educators and policymakers in public school systems

### Target Learning Goals
- improving access to education and credential verification in developing countries
