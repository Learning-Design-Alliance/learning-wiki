# Run icebreaking activities before formal team activities to build trust and intimacy among newly formed teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before starting team activities, the article recommends adding activities that break down barriers and improve intimacy. It notes that "icebreaking can increase trust among members by improving understanding and connectivity through short activities before starting formal team activities." This is aimed especially at freshmen in a new environment with a rigid atmosphere, so they can participate with a comfortable mindset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Short trust-building activities conducted before formal team tasks begin
#### Constraints
- Most pertinent for newly admitted students unfamiliar with one another

### Target Learners
- Newly admitted medical (premedical) students

### Target Learning Goals
- Team trust, intimacy, and meaningful interaction
