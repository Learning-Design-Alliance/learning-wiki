# Incorporate cognitively rich physical activities as a promising avenue for supporting mathematics learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that interventions incorporating cognitively rich physical activities "may offer a promising avenue for supporting mathematics learning, a possibility that warrants further investigation." They suggest the complexity of the physical task may be a key factor differentiating the magnitude of EF benefits, and that coordinative, cognitively engaging activities may more effectively enhance EF than repetitive aerobic exercise. The recommendation is explicitly hedged pending longitudinal or experimental confirmation.
