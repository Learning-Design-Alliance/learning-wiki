# Travel Journals

## Description
Every student at Normal Park Museum Magnet, a K–8 school in Chattanooga, Tennessee, created a travel journal to chart their learning. These journals included not only charts, drawings, and graphic organizers, but also writing and reflection pieces that capture students’ learning about a topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires journals or notebooks for students to write in. Requires time for students to work on their journals.
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to integrate writing, drawing, and graphic organizers. Teachers need to provide guidance and support.
