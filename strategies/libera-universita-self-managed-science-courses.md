# Libera Università: self-managed semi-professional science course cycles outside institutional schooling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A cycle of self-managed courses held in the Cdcs, forming a true curricular cycle outside institutional school and university study. It offers "l'occasione per lo studio di livello professionale o semiprofessionale delle SN" to citizens who, for economic conditions, historical circumstances, work choices or other needs, could not attend university courses despite having interest and inclination for the subject. Enrolment and attendance of lecture cycles are expected to yield both conceptual assimilation and a technical repertoire potentially usable professionally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses programmed by designated holders (internal members or external staff) according to the Centre's Regulations
#### Constraints
- Serves citizens excluded from university study for diverse personal circumstances; not a substitute for institutional degrees

### Target Learners
- adult citizens without university education but with interest and inclination toward the natural sciences
- workers and unemployed people seeking professional qualification

### Target Learning Goals
- professional or semi-professional level study of the natural sciences
- a technical repertoire usable in professional settings
