# Adapt e-learning training with time estimates, blended learning, and peer mentoring to support communication-strategy delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on postdelivery interviews, physiotherapists recommended format adaptations: "giving participants an estimate of the time required to complete each level and additional e-learning training and blended learning (ie, small-group face-to-face coaching alongside E-SOLAS) to support delivery of the self-determination theory-based communication strategies during goal setting and action-planning activities." One physiotherapist also proposed training a designated clinician per primary care area via face-to-face training to act as a peer mentor supporting colleagues who completed E-SOLAS. The authors add that technical support should be in place to maintain user engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technical support must be available during training; six of 13 physiotherapists reported difficulty accessing online materials.
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a small qualitative sample (five interviewed physiotherapists) and were not tested in this study.

### Target Learners
- primary care physiotherapists
- health care practitioners in e-learning training

### Target Learning Goals
- delivering self-determination theory-based communication strategies
- group-based intervention delivery
