# Advise students to plan their study days, use breaks between lectures for independent study, prevent procrastination, and invest effort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's individual-level recommendation is that students should "plan their study days and to actively consider using breaks for independent study in their learning plan", and that "students should find strategies to prevent procrastination and invest effort into learning time". These recommendations follow directly from the multilevel findings that planning, breaks use, effort, and low procrastination independently predict daily study satisfaction. The strategy is framed as applicable to all students because the random-slope model for breaks use did not fit better than the fixed-slope model.
