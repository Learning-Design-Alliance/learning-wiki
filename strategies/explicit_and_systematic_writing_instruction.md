# Explicit and Systematic Writing Instruction

## Description
Explicit and systematic teaching of strategies, skills, and knowledge needed to be a proficient writer. Instruction should include modeling writing strategies and teaching learners how to regulate their use, such as how to monitor, evaluate, and adjust strategies as needed for particular tasks and goals. Explicit instruction is combined with extended writing practice for different purposes like recording, communicating, persuading, self-expression, and reflection.  Foundational writing skills such as spelling, handwriting, and keyboarding are taught to automaticity. Skilled writing requires planning and revising, with more accomplished writers spending about 50 percent of their writing time planning and revising text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured curriculum, modeling of writing strategies, time for extended writing practice, explicit feedback on progress
#### Constraints
- Requires significant instructional time, may be less effective without motivation or engagement
