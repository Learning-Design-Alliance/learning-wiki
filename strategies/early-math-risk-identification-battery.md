# Early identification battery for children at risk for long-term mathematics difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that children at risk for long-term mathematics difficulties can be identified before age four using an assessment battery including "cardinal knowledge, count list, enumeration, letter recognition, and executive function measures." Numeral recognition might substitute for letter recognition given their strong correlation (r = .71), though letter recognition may be more useful due to greater variance. This battery enables early identification of children likely to become delayed CPKs.
