# Key considerations and proposed strategies for better CBL implementation in applied physiology

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on facilitator-reported challenges, the authors propose a set of implementation strategies spanning training, facilitated learning, case construction, group discussion, reflection, assessment, and long-term impact. They state these "suggestions could help to construct a better CBL framework and empower students to become active participants for better engagement in CBL." Specific recommendations include continuous faculty and student training, interdisciplinary case construction, interactive live case presentations, clear group-discussion guidelines, reflection and feedback mechanisms, assessment rubrics, and continuous evaluation of CBL effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment to continuous faculty training and regular evaluation of CBL effectiveness with adjustments based on feedback and assessment data
#### Constraints
- The strategies are proposed by the authors based on their own implementation experience; they were not separately tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physiotherapy students
- physiology faculty facilitators

### Target Learning Goals
- active participation and engagement in case-based learning
- critical thinking and collaboration skills for clinical practice
