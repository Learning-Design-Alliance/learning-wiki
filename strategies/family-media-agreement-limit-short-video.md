# Families jointly agree to limit short video time and model balanced media habits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that parental usage duration exacerbates the negative usage–attention link, the authors recommend parents establish positive patterns of short video usage. They suggest "parents and children could agree to limit the time spent using short videos and adhere to it collectively, encouraging more outdoor activities and face-to-face communication". This targets the family microsystem rather than restrictive control alone.
