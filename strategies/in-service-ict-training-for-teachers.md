# Provide in-service training, workshops, seminars and conferences on ICT utilization for teachers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends ongoing professional development so teachers can handle ICT facilities effectively: "In-service training, workshops, seminars and conferences on the utilization of ICT should be organized from time to time to update the knowledge of teachers in our schools." This operationalises the abstract's recommendation that teachers go for in-service training on the best way of handling ICT facilities to impact knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment from government, school administrators, parents, NGOs and philanthropists to provide ICT facilities in schools and at home
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of secondary schools serving students with hearing impairment in Nigeria

### Target Learners
- Teachers of students with hearing impairment in secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective integration of ICT into teaching and learning
