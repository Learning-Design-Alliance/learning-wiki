# A bundle of pupil programs: weekly lecture courses for talented pupils, two-week internships with tutoring, Kinderuni for ages 8-12, and a CERN-anniversary video contest with a 3-day CERN visit as prize

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond RentaProf and Masterclasses, the group runs weekly lecture courses for talented and interested pupils on theoretical mechanics, quantum mechanics, or quantum computing; hosts mandatory two-week high-school internships in which 'Students at the age of about 15 years visit different working groups and mostly spend two days with each group'; offers Kinderuni for ages 8-12; and ran a contest 'for groups of pupils to create a short video or presentation related to CERN, its history and future, famous scientists, etc.' with a 3-day visit to CERN as the incentive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Working groups willing to host interns, staff to give weekly lecture courses, and prizes such as a 3-day CERN visit to incentivize the contest
#### Constraints
- Interns are about 15 years old and the visit day is organized in four 2h-slots; the contest was tied to CERN's 70th anniversary

### Target Learners
- talented and interested pupils
- high-school students around age 15
- very young pupils aged 8-12

### Target Learning Goals
- experience of university life and research
- physics topics from theoretical mechanics to quantum computing
- creative engagement with CERN's history and future
