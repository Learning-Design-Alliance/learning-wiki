# Integrate TBL with other teaching methods and modern educational technologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend integrating TBL with other teaching methods such as Problem-Based Learning and Case-Based Learning to "fully leverage the advantages of different teaching methods to achieve better teaching results". TBL can be integrated with LBL, using LBL's teaching resources and advantages to improve students' application and understanding of knowledge through TBL's teamwork and discussion. TBL can also be combined with modern educational technologies such as the flipped classroom and mobile learning applications; the authors report that flipped classroom combined with TBL enhances academic achievements and garners higher satisfaction rates.
