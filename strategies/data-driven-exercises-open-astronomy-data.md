# Integrate data-driven educational exercises using open data archives and community-developed tools into the undergraduate astronomy curriculum

## Description
The article proposes bringing students into direct contact with real-world data by leveraging community-developed infrastructure: open source web-based tools for stellar evolution and open data archives for galactic and extragalactic data. It states these developments "offer educators outstanding opportunities to bring s tudents directly into contact with real- world data". Specific exercises include inferring exoplanet properties, building an HR diagram from Tycho data, and determining the Hubble constant from Type Ia light curves.
