# Ensure communication and co-development with school leaders throughout edtech process rollout

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends creating space to bring school leaders into the iterative design of an edtech evaluation process and communicating its rationale across all levels and roles. Interviews showed principals often lacked depth of knowledge to support change management, with one noting "all of a sudden you have to go through this process now," and teachers largely misunderstood the process's purpose. Open communication and involvement opportunities are recommended to build buy-in and trust.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires time and structures for school leaders and educators to participate in solutioning and to receive clear rationale and timelines for the process.
#### Constraints
- The report frames this as a recommendation from first-year rollout learnings in one large district where schools previously needed no permission to use any tool.

### Target Learners
- District and school leaders, and classroom teachers in K-12 districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective, intentional use of approved edtech tools in teaching and learning
