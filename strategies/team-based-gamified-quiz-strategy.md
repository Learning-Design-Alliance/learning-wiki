# Use team-based gamified quizzes at the start and end of teaching sessions to complement traditional histology teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating gamification platforms such as Kahoot® into curricula as a complement to traditional teaching, with quizzes played in teams rather than individually and repeated at the beginning and end of sessions. The authors conclude that "New technologies, such as the introduction of Kahoot® or similar gamification platforms, could complement the more traditional teaching methods and provide additional scaffolding for student learning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quiz questions peer-reviewed for content validity by senior anatomists
- Supervisors available to discuss game results and give feedback
#### Constraints
- The authors state the effectiveness assumption "cannot be generalized without further research"; exam averages were not significantly changed

### Target Learners
- first-year medical and dental students
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- histology knowledge retention
- collaboration and engagement
