# Survey students' learning-style distribution to choose between flipped and face-to-face classroom formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that instructors assess the learning-style profile of their cohort before deciding on a teaching format, using an instrument such as the ILS. As they state, "The instructors may find it helpful to survey new students to identify the distribution of learning styles in their classroom and subsequently decide which classroom styles might be most appropriate for each student." Intuitive-sequential cohorts suit flipped formats; sensing-verbal cohorts suit face-to-face teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must administer a validated learning-style questionnaire such as the ILS before the course
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence comes from one small wire-bending study; the authors caution results may not generalize to larger classes, other disciplines, or other countries

### Target Learners
- fourth-year preclinical dental students
- health-professions students in practical skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- psychomotor skill acquisition (orthodontic wire bending)
- student satisfaction with instruction
