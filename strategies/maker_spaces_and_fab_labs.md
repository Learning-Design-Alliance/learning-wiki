# Maker Spaces and Fab Labs

## Description
Establish physical spaces where learners can invent, design, and build things using a variety of materials and tools, from wood scraps to technology. These spaces foster exploration, imagination, and collaboration, allowing learners to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems. Maker spaces encourage hands-on learning and creativity, enabling learners to see the practical applications of their knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a dedicated physical space with a variety of materials, tools, and equipment. Instructors need to be knowledgeable about different technologies and techniques and be able to provide guidance and support to learners.
#### Constraints
- Can be expensive to set up and maintain. Requires careful planning and supervision to ensure safety and prevent misuse of equipment.

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Offer a variety of projects and materials to cater to different interests and skill levels. Provide support and guidance to help learners overcome challenges and develop their skills.
