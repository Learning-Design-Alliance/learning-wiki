# Develop standardized, regular, and targeted CPR education for lower-level hospitals and professional groups with lower knowledge-based BLS competency.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that BLS education be 'standardized, regular, and targeted CPR education, particularly for lower-level hospitals and professional groups with relatively lower knowledge-based BLS competency'. This strategy addresses identified knowledge gaps in defibrillation and quality-control topics and variation across professional roles and hospital levels. The authors suggest regular refresher training, periodic assessment, simulation-based education, and strategic allocation of training resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular refresher training and periodic assessment as suggested by the authors
#### Constraints
- The article notes additional longitudinal or interventional studies should be conducted to assess the impact of these approaches on knowledge-based BLS competency and patient safety

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals including physicians, nurses, technicians, and other clinical staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge-based BLS competency including CPR, defibrillation, airway management, and resuscitation quality-control knowledge
