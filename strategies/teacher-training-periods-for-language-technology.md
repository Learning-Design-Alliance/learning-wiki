# Provide teachers with training periods on how, when, and where to use language-learning technologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes that technology alone does not improve language learning: "just the presence of computer technologies cannot facilitate language learning in general and vocabulary learning in particular". It therefore recommends that enough training periods be provided for teachers to know how, when, and where to use these technologies in teaching language skills, enabling more student-centered and interactive teaching environments.
