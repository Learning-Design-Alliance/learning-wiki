# Add virtual simulation to the online platform of blended clinical skills courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a forward-looking design recommendation, the authors propose that future blended-teaching designs incorporate virtual simulation teaching into the online platform. They state this addition "aims to address the limitations of the online platform in practical training and enhance the overall learning experience", complementing video-based online content with more interactive practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online course platform capable of hosting virtual simulation content
#### Constraints
- Positioned as future work; the study itself did not implement or test virtual simulation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in clinical skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- practical clinical skills training
