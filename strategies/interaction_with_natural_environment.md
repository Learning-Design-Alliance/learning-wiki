# Interaction with Natural Environment

## Description
Incorporating natural elements (views of nature, plants) into the learning environment can improve focus, well-being, and academic performance. Strategies include providing unrestricted views of nature from classrooms and introducing leafy plants into the classroom. Interaction with nature can be particularly helpful for kids with ADD and ADHD.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to views of nature or leafy plants. Integration of natural elements into the learning environment.
#### Constraints
- Limited availability of natural views or plants in some environments. Requires maintenance of plants.

## Assessment Evidence
- Measure focus levels, behavior, and academic performance. Track instances of sick leave and disciplinary events.

## Impact
- Students in schools with unrestricted views of nature tested higher in reading, math, and language arts (Uncapher, 2016). Introducing leafy plants into the classroom has a positive impact on students’ well-being and behavior (Uncapher, 2016).
