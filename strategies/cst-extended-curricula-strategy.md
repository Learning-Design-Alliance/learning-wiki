# Extend structure-based teaching to related chemistry curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors state CST "can be utilized in other curriculums, such as organic chemistry, medicinal chemistry and spectrum analysis", and that in organic chemistry it helps speculate chemical properties of structures while in spectrum analysis it demonstrates how to determine chromatographic behaviors and spectral characters. They believe CST can be popularized among chemistry courses whose knowledge is diverse, memorization-based and focused on structure-property relationships.
