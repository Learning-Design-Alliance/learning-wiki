# Open the lesson by eliciting prior knowledge and experiences about spaceflight, then orient toward the Saturn V

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The handout recommends starting the unit by asking students about their existing knowledge and experience of space travel — for example whether they know a (German) astronaut — and then steering the discussion toward the rocket used, the Saturn V. Supporting media include ISS livestreams, DLR Apollo 11 videos, the original 1969 launch broadcast, NASA HD footage, and the Project Apollo Archive photos; with sufficient time, students research Apollo and Saturn V online or prepare presentations. A water-rocklet building project is suggested as a cross-curricular or club extension.
