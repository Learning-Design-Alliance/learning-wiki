# Produce e-learning resources where patients contribute to the learning and scenarios are grounded in real life

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors state an intention to continue analysing patient-related feedback and to "produce more resources that encourage such reflections – such as learning resources where patients contribute to the learning or learning resources that use patient scenarios grounded in real life experiences". This builds on learner appreciation of patient videos and scenarios. It applies to e-learning for healthcare professionals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources should feature videos of patients speaking about their illnesses and interactive case-based patient scenarios, which learners commented on positively
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from users of a single e-learning resource (BMJ Learning) and may not be representative of users of other e-learning resources; only English-language reflections were analysed

### Target Learners
- GPs, GP trainees, and junior and senior secondary care doctors

### Target Learning Goals
- Practical clinical knowledge learners can use in day-to-day practice
- Patient-centred care
