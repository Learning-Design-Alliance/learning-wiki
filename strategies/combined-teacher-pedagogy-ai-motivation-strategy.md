# Combine teacher professional development, engagement- and well-being-focused pedagogy, and culturally responsive AI tools to sustain EFL motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its findings, the article recommends a combined approach: "the study underscores the importance of teacher training for competence, pedagogical strategies focused on student engagement and well-being, and culturally responsive AI-enhanced learning tools". It argues such a comprehensive approach can sustain and enrich students' motivation for English learning in Chinese higher education, and that educators should select AI systems that are transparent about training data and bias mitigation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in need-supportive pedagogy so that autonomy-supportive practices can function within hierarchical classroom norms
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that AI systems are not culturally neutral and that excessive reliance on AI may reduce meaningful teacher-student interaction

### Target Learners
- Chinese undergraduate EFL students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- English learning motivation
- student engagement
- psychological flourishing in language learning
