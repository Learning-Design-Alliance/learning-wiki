# For computer-based physics exercises with broadly distributed abilities, keep the task level as baseline, add flexibility, and retain a text-based completion option

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the survey finding that acceptance, working style and time needs varied with students' backgrounds, the authors recommend for future courses keeping the current exercise level "as the base line", designing "an enhanced flexibility to cover a wide range of students' abilities", and retaining "the option of successfully completing the course with text-based exercises exclusively". This lets each student choose among data-analysis and purely text-based routes to the same exercise-points threshold.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exercise points threshold (50%) must remain reachable through text-based exercises alone
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a single-course survey; the follow-up course was to be evaluated the same way for comparison

### Target Learners
- undergraduate bachelor physics students with broadly distributed programming experience and learning styles

### Target Learning Goals
- data analysis and interpretation of experimental physics data
