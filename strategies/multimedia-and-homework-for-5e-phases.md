# Use multimedia and real models alongside 5E activities, and assign homework for the enter and evaluation phases to save time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the Suggestions section the author recommends that teachers use "multimedia combining graphics, animations, simulations, sounds, colors, software s and video clips in the teaching environment, along with real models and shapes", and that because the 5E application phase takes long time, homework be given for the enter and evaluation phases to use time more effectively. Teachers should also be informed about the constructivist approach and student portfolios should supplement written exams in evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher familiarity with the constructivist approach; availability of multimedia resources and real models
#### Constraints
- The author states the application phase of the 5E model takes a long time and that some subjects may not suit the model

### Target Learners
- Secondary school physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- Physics concept understanding and motivation
