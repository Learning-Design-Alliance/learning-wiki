# Finger-Painting: Arts Integration

## Description
Integrating arts like fiction writing, dance, drama, graphic design, music, drawing, painting, or ceramics into all subjects helps learners learn and find their talents and interests. Arts integration fosters creativity, imagination, and deeper engagement with the curriculum. This approach is more common in elementary schools, but high school teachers can also use it to provide creative outlets and encourage learners to explore their strengths.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials and tools for various art forms. Instructor training and support.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources and training. Some learners may feel uncomfortable with art-based activities.
