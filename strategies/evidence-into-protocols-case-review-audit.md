# Integrate current evidence into protocols, case review, audit and quality-improvement activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Because evidence-based practice had the lowest PSC dimension score and nurses may rely on established procedures rather than direct literature searching, the article recommends incorporating updated evidence into "accessible protocols, audit standards, case discussions, and quality‐improvement activities" rather than only encouraging individual nurses to search the literature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires organisational infrastructure for updating protocols and running case discussions and audit activities
#### Constraints
- The dimension difference was small and does not indicate a clear competency deficit

- Evidence-based practice in patient safety
