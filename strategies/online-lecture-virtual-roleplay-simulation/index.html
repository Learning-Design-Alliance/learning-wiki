Pair online lectures with virtual role-play or simulation sessions to add experiential learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review describes combining online lectures with virtual role-play or simulation sessions, where "the online lecture was paired with virtual role-play or simulation sessions" so that "residents were able to apply their knowledge accordingly in case scenarios." In one study, psychiatric trainees attended a 2-hour virtual lecture on on-call skills and then participated in weekly 2-hour simulated on-call shifts covering 10 common scenarios over 10 weeks. The virtual role-play concept was credited as a useful technique to enhance interactive learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A videoconference platform supporting breakout rooms or scenario-based simulation
  • Case scenarios relevant to the skills being taught

Constraints¶

  • The virtual format can be awkward owing to the need for turn-taking, which affects interactivity especially when few residents are involved concurrently in a single case scenario

Target Learners¶

  • psychiatric trainees preparing for on-call duties

Target Learning Goals¶

  • on-call clinical skills
  • application of psychiatric knowledge in case scenarios
