# Extend AI tutor implementation across modules, expand question banks, and establish ethical oversight guidelines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing AI tools "over extended periods and across various modules" to evaluate long-term learning and retention; expanding and refining the question bank with exam-style items and clinical essays; capturing objective usage metrics such as login frequency and time spent; broadening application to clinical-phase content including simulated patient cases; and having institutional leaders "establish clear guidelines and quality assurance protocols to ensure the ethical use of AI."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robust study designs such as randomized within-cohort comparisons for future evaluation
- Institutional guidelines and quality assurance protocols for ethical AI use
#### Constraints
- Current findings are based on a single-module, single-institution implementation without baseline AI-exposure assessment

### Target Learners
- Medical students across pre-clinical and clinical phases

### Target Learning Goals
- Long-term knowledge retention
- Clinical reasoning and diagnostic decision-making
