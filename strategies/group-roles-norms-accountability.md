# Establish group norms and procedural roles, delegate authority, and monitor group dynamics to foster student accountability.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The essay recommends establishing group norms and procedural roles that support productivity and inclusion, noting that teamwork spans both cognitive and social domains. It provides tables of example student roles (e.g., Team Leader, Note-Taker, Researcher, Skilled Contributor) and example group norms for equitable participation, and recommends instructors monitor group dynamics and intervene when necessary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must model the group norms and interactions they hope to see in their students, as students may not inherently possess collaboration skills
#### Constraints
- If students cannot resolve conflict or address destructive behaviors, instructor intervention may be needed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- student accountability in group work
- inclusive participation
