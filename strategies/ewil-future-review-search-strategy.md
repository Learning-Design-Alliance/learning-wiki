# Broaden search terminology and bibliographic databases in future eWIL reviews

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that future systematic searches extend beyond the term "work integrated learning" combined with "digital", "online", "technology" and "virtual". It states that "Additional search terms such as, "work-based learning", "internship", "placement practicum" and "ICT" could be used in future research to ensure a wider scope and coverage of the literature", and that future studies should consider broader bibliographic databases such as Web of Science and Google Scholar, which this study avoided due to irrelevant results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future reviews should add terms such as work-based learning, internship, placement practicum and ICT, and consider broader bibliographic databases
#### Constraints
- The current review was limited to six psychology and education databases and publications from January 2000 to January 2016 in English

### Target Learners
- higher education students in work integrated learning

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the scope of technology-supported and technology-delivered WIL
