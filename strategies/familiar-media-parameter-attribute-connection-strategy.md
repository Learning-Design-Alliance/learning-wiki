# Use familiar media (zombie films and games) to connect model parameters to physical attributes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After students implement the SIZR model, the instructor discusses "different zombie movies, video games and television shows" and students "write down physical attributes that they believe describe the zombies in their favorite media," then discuss how to capture those attributes in the model. The authors state that through parameter variation or model modification "students gain a deeper understanding of the different terms in the model and how they affect the dynamics."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A basic implemented epidemiology model whose parameters (infection rate, destruction rate, transition rate) can be varied and mapped onto narrative attributes
#### Constraints
- Recommended for engaging contexts where learners share cultural familiarity with the scenario; the article illustrates it only with the zombie apocalypse unit

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding model terms and parameters
- connecting mathematical models to real or familiar systems
