# Evaluate simulation training beyond immediate quiz and feedback measures using the Kirkpatrick four-level model and interval assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (3 review, 1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
To reduce test–retest effects and capture deeper outcomes, the authors recommend alternatives such as using different validated questions for post-simulation assessment, interval linear assessments weeks later or at clerkship end to assess knowledge retention, or the Kirkpatrick four-level evaluation model, which involves four hierarchical stratas from student reaction through learning, behavior change, to patient outcomes. They suggest level 3 and 4 assessment could comprise interval OSATS and scenario-based simulations with an external professional evaluator and high-fidelity mannequin.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to validated alternative question sets, interval assessment opportunities during the clerkship, and OSATS or scenario-based assessment resources
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as future evaluation methodology; levels 3 and 4 were not assessed in the present study

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year medical students in obstetrics and gynecology rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge retention and clinical competence in normal vaginal delivery
