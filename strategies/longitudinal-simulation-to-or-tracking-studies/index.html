Prioritize longitudinal studies tracking trainees from simulation to real-world surgical performance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that future research prioritize longitudinal designs connecting simulation-based learning with operative outcomes. The authors state: "Future research should prioritize longitudinal studies that track the progression of trainees from simulation-based learning to real-world surgical performance, ensuring that these tools provide meaningful educational value beyond the simulated environment." This strategy addresses the sparse transferability evidence identified in the review.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Objective proficiency metrics to complement subjective assessments

Constraints¶

  • Current evidence base lacks standardized objective metrics, complicating widespread implementation

Target Learners¶

  • neurosurgery trainees
  • neurosurgery residents

Target Learning Goals¶

  • surgical skill acquisition
  • transfer of training to operative performance

Claims¶

  • Most reviewed simulation studies demonstrated face and content validity, but few assessed construct or predictive validity [+W]
  • Evidence for transfer of simulation training to operating-room outcomes was sparse in neuro-oncological simulation research [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop standardized simulation-based leadership curricula with longitudinal evaluation and multi-center randomized comparison of formats
  • Prioritize multicenter, randomized designs with standardized reporting and longitudinal follow-up in future surgical education research
  • Research agenda: standardize reporting, run comparative efficacy studies, and track longitudinal transfer to clinical practice
  • Combine objective metrics with validated subjective tools and audio-visual recording to contextualize interpretation of surgical performance data
  • Prioritize larger multi-center RCTs with standardized AR systems, uniform metrics, and long-term follow-up when evaluating AR surgical training
  • Validate automated video analytics and reach metric consensus before widespread adoption in surgical training
