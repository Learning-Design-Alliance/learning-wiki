## Description
The article's practical-effect section translates the chain mediation findings into implementable actions for nursing education: programs "could implement mentorship and peer-support initiatives to strengthen professional identity", incorporate workshops that build self-efficacy through skill development, and design engaging, interactive learning environments that encourage active participation, with the aim of improving academic outcomes and guiding support systems for nursing students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nursing programs with capacity to organize mentorship and peer-support initiatives, run self-efficacy workshops, and design engaging, interactive learning environments
#### Constraints
