# Structure high-gap RT competencies into progressive beginner, intermediate, and advanced learning phases

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementation recommendation from the Discussion: high-gap competencies "can be structured into progressive learning phases—beginner, intermediate, and advanced—ensuring a stepwise approach to competency development," while critical competencies such as ECMO and lung ultrasound are to be integrated into mandatory training modules and medium and low-gap competencies serve as core early-training skills. The recommendation derives from the survey's gap analysis and was not tested experimentally in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Gap-analysis data identifying critical, high, medium, and low-gap competencies to sequence the phases
- Structured training before competency evaluations, which 78.64% of surveyed departments reported providing
#### Constraints
- Derived from cross-sectional, self-reported survey data and not experimentally tested

### Target Learners
- Newly employed respiratory therapists
- RT students in entry-to-practice programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Stepwise development of high-gap procedural competencies
- Foundational competency in medium and low-gap core skills before advancing to specialized skills
