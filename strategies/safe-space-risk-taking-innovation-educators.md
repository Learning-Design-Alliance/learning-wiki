## Description
In the practical implications for innovation educators, the article recommends that educators "encourage students to take risks, try out new ideas, and be open to failing as normal parts of the learning process" and "create a safe space for students to look into unusual answers and new ideas without worrying about being judged." This operationalizes the model's supportive-environment activity, which experts ranked highly in the NGT phase.
