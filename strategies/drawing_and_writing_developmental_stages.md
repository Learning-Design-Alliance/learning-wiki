# Drawing and Writing Developmental Stages

## Description
Understanding the developmental stages of drawing and writing can help educators tailor activities to match a child's current abilities. These stages include random scribbling, controlled scribbling, lines and patterns, pictures of objects or people, and letter and word practice. By understanding these stages, educators can provide age-appropriate materials, expectations, and encouragement to foster a child's growth in both drawing and writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of the developmental stages of drawing and writing. A variety of art materials (crayons, markers, paper, etc.). A supportive and encouraging environment.
#### Constraints
- The timetables are approximate and do not apply to all. Sensory information may be too much for some children in this stage.
