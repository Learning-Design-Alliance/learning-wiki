# Implement structured group roles, open communication, and bias-awareness training to promote inclusivity in physics labs

## Description
The article recommends targeted interventions to mitigate implicit bias in physics laboratory settings: "Strategies such as implementing structured group roles, fostering open communication, and providing bias-awareness training for instructors can help mitigate the impact of implicit biases and create a more equitable learning environment." It also cites prior work suggesting such inclusive pedagogical strategies have shown promise in reducing gender disparities in STEM. The recommendation responds to the qualitative themes of perceived superiority, disregard for contributions, and fear of invalidation reported by some female students.
