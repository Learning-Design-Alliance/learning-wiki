# Take Action, Be Intentional

## Description
Create an atmosphere that enables teachers to meet the needs of all students. Know students to reach them and remember that when a student comes in hungry or tired from a challenging environment, he or she may not be in the best position to succeed. How we act and react will determine whether we reach that student and their peers. Create the environment and personify the culture, and reduce the empathic mindset gap between teachers and students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment to building relationships, awareness of student backgrounds, supportive school policies
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort, may be challenging in large classes or with limited resources
