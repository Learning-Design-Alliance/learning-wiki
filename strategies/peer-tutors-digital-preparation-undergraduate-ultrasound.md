# Use peer tutors and a deliberately planned digital preparation phase when delivering ultrasound training to undergraduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A delivery recipe for undergraduate ultrasound courses: peer tutors enable longer training sessions spread through undergraduate tuition, and "the digital preparation time using e-learning/online video courses was consciously planned and included as tuition in the study design" because students as "digital natives" use these formats often. In this design, peer tutors "reduced the group sizes from 6–14 students per teacher in preliminary studies [35] to 4–5 students".
