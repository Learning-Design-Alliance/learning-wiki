# Target older-volunteer training by assessed needs: workshops for awareness, activity-design practice, and monthly case meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the survey findings, the authors propose concrete training actions matched to the need patterns they measured: regular workshops to enhance service awareness whose "content must consider the age, educational level, and physical and mental conditions of older volunteers"; more opportunities to practice designing and leading dynamic and static activities; and monthly meetings focused on handling special or problem cases to improve consultation and referral competencies. They also recommend surveying volunteers with the OVCS so program content matches volunteer needs, and routinely reviewing competencies to assign tasks appropriately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community care centers conduct pre-employment or routine competency/needs assessments (e.g., with the OVCS) before allocating training and tasks
#### Constraints
- The authors note future studies should group volunteers by specific tasks, since division of labor is highly task-specific

### Target Learners
- older adult volunteers at community care centers

### Target Learning Goals
- service awareness, designing and leading health-promoting activities, consultation and referral competency
