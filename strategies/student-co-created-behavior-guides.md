# Student-co-created acceptable/unacceptable behavior guides for conflict management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy has students brainstorm and compile lists of unacceptable and acceptable methods of solving conflict, which are discussed, narrowed down, and typed up as a class behavioral guide. The article states: "The lists were typed up as the acceptable and unacceptable behavioral guide. The professors acted as facilitators and the group of students subsequently held each other accountable for managing conflict." The session also introduces negotiator personality styles from the Black Swan Group to normalize differing conflict approaches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professor facilitation and a class session devoted to conflict management
#### Constraints
- The article notes the three negotiator style list is in no way a comprehensive review of conflict management, only an easy-to-understand overview

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- Adult conflict-management skill
- Peer accountability for team behavior
