# Deploy smartphone-based labs as flexible, low-cost complements to traditional labs across classroom, home, and remote settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends using SmartIPLs as a complementary modality rather than a replacement: smartphones' built-in sensors and camera-based analysis support hands-on, inquiry-based experiments deployable "in conventional classrooms, dorm rooms, or fully remote environments, without the need for specialized infrastructure." This strategy extends learning beyond fixed lab schedules, supports open-ended inquiry and project-based learning, and increases accessibility where specialized studio classrooms or extra staffing are unaffordable. The COVID-19 pivot demonstrated the approach at scale, and post-pandemic studies cited in the review affirm continued value.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intentional, evidence-based pedagogical integration rather than passive screen use; access to appropriate apps and, where needed, low-cost kits
#### Constraints
- Device heterogeneity and sensor limitations affect measurement consistency; the review notes challenges of sensor variability, device compatibility, and classroom integration

### Target Learners
- undergraduate introductory physics students
- resource-constrained institutions
- remote learners

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry-based experimentation
- scientific process skills
- self-directed hands-on learning
