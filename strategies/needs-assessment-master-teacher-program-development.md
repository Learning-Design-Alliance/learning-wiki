# Develop education programs through needs assessment with educators, master-teacher co-development, and participant feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Fermilab's program development process begins by holding a needs assessment with educators to determine their needs and interests, then matching those needs with laboratory resources. A few master teachers are invited to help develop and conduct the program, and participant feedback is used to improve programs. The authors report that this process matters as much as the programs themselves.
