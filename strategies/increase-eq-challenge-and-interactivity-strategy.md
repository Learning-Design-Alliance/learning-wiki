# Increase challenge and interactivity in future versions of the Ethics Quarter

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on developmental feedback and fidelity findings, the authors recommend increasing the challenge of the EQ and implementing interactivity into the educational area, for example through common discussions or other participants' readable responses. It is known that social interaction plays a role in learning, and some managers indicated the contents could have been even more challenging. Future studies should also use more extensive user logs to understand what increases participation and investigate enactment of learned ethical activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Possibilities of including interactivity in the EQ should be evaluated in the future
#### Constraints
- The main concern seems to be lack of engagement with e-learning interventions

### Target Learners
- Nurse managers using e-learning interventions

### Target Learning Goals
- Deeper engagement with ethics e-learning content
