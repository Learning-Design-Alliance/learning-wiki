# Use complementary evaluation with two tools: a teacher-developed educational progress test and a recently standardized measure of educational effects.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The tenth step of the Education Management Model is complementary evaluation using two tools: one for measuring educational progress developed by the teacher in each course, and second, the recently standardized measure prepared based on conceptual indexes of the effects of family, information process models, participative learning models, individual personality learning models and behavioral learning models. This measure was standardized by receiving the views of 150 students who participated in applying the model in teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students and teachers use two tools for measuring educational effects
- The teacher-developed educational progress test undergoes contextual validity and assessor-agreement reliability examination
- The standardized measure was developed by receiving the views of 150 students
#### Constraints
- The standardized measure is prepared based on conceptual indexes of specific learning model families rather than general content mastery alone

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students
- Students across grades and educational levels

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring educational effects of each step of education management model in practice
- Measuring educational progress in each course
