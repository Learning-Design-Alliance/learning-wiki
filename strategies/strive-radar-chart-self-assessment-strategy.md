# Use STRIVE's weighted category scores and hexagonal radar chart for time-efficient HSC self-assessment and benchmarking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that HSC managers score their center on the 24 STRIVE items, apply the weighted scoring in which "Each category was assigned a total of 10 points", and plot the six category totals on a hexagonal radar scheme. This visual presentation is designed to let users "easily identify their strengths and weaknesses" and support benchmarking and continuous quality improvement in a time-efficient format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of the 24-item STRIVE checklist and application of the 2-point/3-point item weights within each category
#### Constraints
- The weighted scoring system was adopted based on consensus from the steering group rather than empirically derived weights

### Target Learners
- Heads and managers of healthcare simulation centers

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehensive institutional self-evaluation and identification of center shortcomings
