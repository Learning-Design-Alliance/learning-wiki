# Modeling Reflection

## Description
Share parts of the reflection brought them to another level of understanding as they worked together in a learning community. Reflection can bring the same spirit of community to your classroom, too.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be willing to share their learning.
#### Constraints
- Teachers who are not engaged in learning and reflection will find this challenging.
