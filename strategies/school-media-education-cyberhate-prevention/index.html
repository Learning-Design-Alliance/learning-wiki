School-based media education that builds coping skills and critical evaluation of online information¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that schools address cyberhate within whole-school policies on online risks and digital skills. It states that "teaching students and educators to evaluate the legitimacy and credibility of online information is critical to combatting cyberhate," and that increasing ethical media skills can help adolescents dissect cyberhate and understand other perspectives, reducing prejudice. Schools should also teach educators about online perils so they can support students' development into informed citizens.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Whole-school policies regarding online risks and digital skills across the curriculum include a discussion of cyberhate
  • Educators are trained about Internet risks including exposure to cyberhate

Constraints¶

  • Because most cyberhate exposure is accidental on common social media sites, it is nearly impossible to protect adolescents entirely from cyberhate

Target Learners¶

  • Adolescents in schools
  • Educators and school administrators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Evaluating legitimacy and credibility of online information
  • Coping with cyberhate exposure
  • Understanding differences between free speech and hate speech

Affordances¶

  • Routine Activity Theory Cyberhate Framework

Claims¶

  • Witnessing cyberhate is positively associated with cyberhate victimization among adolescents [+W]
  • The most common form of cyberhate victimization is being made jokes about because of religious affiliation or ethnic group [+W]
  • Existing digital citizenship and online safety frameworks were appraised as too broad and not addressing serious online risks [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop policies and pedagogical practices that create supportive, accessible, and welcoming digital spaces for adolescents
