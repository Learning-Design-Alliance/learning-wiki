Strategies

Adopt blended learning for Arabic language teaching with teacher training and computerized curriculum materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the study's results, the author recommends a package of implementation actions: "Adopting Blended Learning approach for teaching and learning Arabic language", holding training courses and workshops for teachers in school districts on implementing blended and e-learning, providing a conducive school environment, and curriculum computerization distributed to students with the textbook. The article frames these as following from the experimental group's outperformance on achievement and verbal creative thinking.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers receive training courses and workshops on how to implement blended and e-learning
  • Schools provide a conducive environment for application of this type of learning

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single small quasi-experiment in one grade and one textbook unit

Target Learners¶

  • Fifth grade Arabic language students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic achievement in the My Beautiful Language Textbook
  • Verbal creative thinking skills

Claims¶

  • Blended learning develops fifth graders' verbal creative thinking more than traditional instruction [+W]
  • Blended learning raises fifth graders' achievement in My Beautiful Language Textbook more than traditional instruction [+W]
  • Blended learning improves the flexibility skill of verbal creative thinking [+W]
  • Blended learning improves the fluency skill of verbal creative thinking [+W]
  • Blended learning improves the originality skill of verbal creative thinking [+W]

Related Strategies¶
