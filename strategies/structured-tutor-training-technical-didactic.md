# Provide substantive technical and didactic basic training for PAL tutors, including group leadership, role playing, feedback and learning-transfer didactics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that PAL tutors receive substantive training by experienced lecturers covering both technical skills and didactic teaching skills, since tutors teach fellow students at a similar study stage. It states "Training of tutors appears to be an essential prerequisite for the success of PAL" and specifies didactic content including group leadership, role playing, feedback and learning-transfer didactics, with procedurally structured, consistent presentation of skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Substantive training conducted by experienced lecturers to give tutors a competence advantage over their tutees
- Basic training in didactic teaching skills in addition to professional technical training
#### Constraints
- Only few published concepts for structured training approaches exist, so there is room for improvement in quality assurance of PAL teaching

### Target Learners
- Medical students acting as peer tutors in Skills-Labs

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical practical and procedural skills teaching competence
- Didactic teaching skills
