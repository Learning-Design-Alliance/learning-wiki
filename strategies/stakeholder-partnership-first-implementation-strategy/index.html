Getting-started sequence: build authentic partnerships, assess intervention-context fit, then budget for continuous revision¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article's concrete recommendation for researchers and practitioners beginning an implementation project. It prescribes a sequence: "education researchers and practitioners should first cultivate authentic partnerships with local stakeholders—including students, teachers, administrators, and families—to clearly understand and articulate local needs"; then collaboratively explore multiple intervention options and rigorously assess compatibility with the unique cultural, structural, and systemic characteristics of the context; and plan so as to explicitly budget time, resources, and personnel for continuous revision based on ongoing feedback.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partnerships must be authentic and formed before intervention selection; compatibility assessment must cover cultural, structural, and systemic characteristics

Constraints¶

  • The article cautions that even with simplified protocols, bridging the gap remains a significant undertaking requiring dedicated time, resources, and continuous attention

Target Learners¶

  • Students in the local school communities whose needs anchor the intervention selection

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Selecting interventions that fit local needs and sustaining them through adaptive revision
