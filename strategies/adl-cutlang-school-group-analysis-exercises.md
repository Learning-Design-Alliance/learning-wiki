# Structure analysis schools as short lectures followed by group implementation of a real ATLAS or CMS analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training design in which short lectures on LHC physics are followed by extended group work: after three days of lectures and short CutLang exercises, groups of 3-4 students were assigned an ATLAS or CMS analysis "with the task to implement the analysis in ADL, run it with CutLang over MC and get simple results".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monte Carlo signal and background events generated by facilitators or obtained from the ATLAS/CMS Open Data portal; a pool of ATLAS or CMS analyses to assign.
#### Constraints
- Exercises target simple results rather than full research-grade analyses.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students with no or little programming or analysis experience

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing and running a real LHC analysis end to end
