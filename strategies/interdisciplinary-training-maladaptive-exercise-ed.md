# Provide interdisciplinary training on maladaptive exercise for ED clinicians to build cohesive clinical approaches across professions.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given profession-related differences in attitudes and routines regarding maladaptive exercise, the authors recommend interdisciplinary training efforts. They state "interdisciplinary training efforts may help create a more cohesive and informed clinical approach." This addresses the finding that professional training background likely shapes views on the therapeutic role of physical activity in ED treatment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should address the differing attitudes and routines across professional groups identified in the study.
#### Constraints
- A recent study (Quesnel et al. 2025) found brief education improved self-efficacy but factual knowledge only improved moderately, with difficulties translating knowledge into clinical decision-making.

### Target Learners
- ED clinicians across professions

### Target Learning Goals
- Build clinical competence and confidence in managing maladaptive exercise
