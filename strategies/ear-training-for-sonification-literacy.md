# Provide context, description and repeated listening for sonifications, and introduce ear training from primary school

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because visual graph reading is a long-trained skill, the article argues sonifications cannot be expected to be understood without context and a description of the content, a function similar to a figure caption and legend, and may need to be listened to several times. It recommends basic and advanced ear training from primary to high school focused on recovering and interpreting information from the aural channel, explicitly not implying formal musical training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sonification, especially a complex one, needs a context and description of the content
- In most cases a sonification might need to be listened to several times, focusing on different aspects of the data
#### Constraints
- The proposed ear training does not imply formal musical training but a more general training focused on the aural channel

### Target Learners
- primary school students
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting information from the aural channel
- handling complex sonifications
