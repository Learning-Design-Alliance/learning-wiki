Select short, entertaining films and allocate four sessions per theme to sustain motivation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In the instructional reflection, the authors recommend adjusting time allocation and film choice: two hours covered films, questions, discussion and experiential activities, and one session covered blog publication and sharing. Because "Triggering the students’ motivation usually wastes too much time; therefore, the time spent watching the film was reduced," they judge that "Four sessions would be more appropriate," and that dull or long films bore students.
