# Involve students in real-life resuscitations and adopt competency-based assessments with regular practice rather than relying on course certifications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend actively involving students during patient resuscitations encountered in clinical duties, since real-life CPR experience correlated with better performance while observation alone did not. They also state "Training programs would be wise to adopt competency-based assessments along with regular practice to ensure proficiency and preparation in these areas rather than relying on certifications after training courses."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for students to administer compressions during actual resuscitations
- Competency-based assessment alongside regular practice
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that allowing students to perform CPR without ensuring BLS excellence may risk patient outcomes, and note many students feel unprepared and reluctant to participate

### Target Learners
- Medical students on clinical rotations
- Physician assistant students

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintain and improve BLS competency through clinical experience
