# Incorporate pathological findings and additional practice into pre-clerkship ultrasound training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student recommendations and identified barriers, the authors suggest integrating ultrasound images of pathological findings or simulated pathology into the OSCE assessment and adding practice sessions or self-directed practice time. They argue this could address the barriers of "lack of patients with indications for EFAST and the time constraints of often fast-paced clinical settings" and help students develop clinical decision-making skills when pathology is identified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources such as simulation technology or image libraries to represent pathological findings in the preclinical environment
#### Constraints
- The article notes incorporation of technology to simulate pathology is identified as a future direction to explore, not an evaluated intervention

### Target Learners
- Pre-clerkship medical students in POCUS training programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognition of pathological ultrasound findings and clinical decision-making when pathology is identified on EFAST
