# Extend the framework with Explainable AI techniques and multimodal signals in future work

## Description
The authors recommend integrating Explainable AI (XAI) techniques to enhance interpretability, so that XAI methods "could reveal which words and sentences most strongly influence cluster assignments and topic coherence". They further suggest incorporating multimodal signals such as audio and video cues, exploring cross-domain applications, and integrating large language models to facilitate brainstorming sessions in real time.
