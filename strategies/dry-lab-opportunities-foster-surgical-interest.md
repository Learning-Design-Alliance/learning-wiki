# Provide all medical students with opportunities to learn surgical skills in dry laboratory training to foster interest in surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends giving every student, not only aspiring surgeons, chances to practice surgical skills: "This indicates that providing all students with opportunities to learn surgical skills has significant educational value". The authors argue that even students without initial surgical aspirations may develop interest after experiencing skill improvement, and that such strategies can help address surgeon shortages in Japan.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a dry laboratory with instruments actually employed in surgical procedures during clinical training
#### Constraints
- The article links this strategy to the Japanese context of surgeon shortage; interest development is the authors' interpretation, not a measured outcome

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade medical students, including those undecided or aiming for non-surgical specialties

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical technical skills and interest in surgical careers
