# Integrate AI-based diagnostic analysis into preclinical coursework while keeping AI complementary to instructor-student interaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educators integrate AI-based analysis into preclinical coursework so students develop advanced diagnostic skills before clinical practice, noting that AI-powered convolutional neural networks have demonstrated high accuracy in detecting caries and occlusal abnormalities. It simultaneously cautions that "AI should complement, rather than replace, traditional instructor–student interactions" and calls for ethical frameworks addressing student autonomy and potential bias in AI-generated feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ethical frameworks for AI integration in education
- Stronger collaboration between dental educators, AI developers and clinical practitioners
- Cultivation of digital literacy among emerging dental professionals
#### Constraints
- Existing research on AI in dentistry exhibits notable deficiencies, impeding its reproducibility and practical implementation

### Target Learners
- dental students in preclinical education

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnostic skills
- clinical decision-making
