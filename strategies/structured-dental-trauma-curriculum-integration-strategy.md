# Integrate structured, interdisciplinary dental trauma training into vocational health curricula using modular, problem-based and digital formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that educational institutions implement a structured, interdisciplinary dental trauma training framework combining theoretical and practical components, tailored to each vocational discipline. Concrete formats named include problem-based learning modules for trauma-scenario decision-making, short certification programs with dental faculties, and digital micro-learning with scheduled refresher trainings. The authors argue these can be embedded without significantly increasing course loads.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Format should be modular and flexible so it fits existing vocational program loads
#### Constraints
- Authors note lack of accessible curricular documentation makes it unclear whether dental trauma education is currently and systematically included

### Target Learners
- Health Services Vocational School students across 16 auxiliary-healthcare programs in Turkey

### Target Learning Goals
- Emergency management of traumatic dental injuries, including avulsed and fractured tooth handling and referral decisions
