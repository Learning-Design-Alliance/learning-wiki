# Integration module in the curricular internship: fixed weekly multiprofessional team practice with families in a health territory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation of interprofessional education in which multidisciplinary student teams of at least two different backgrounds work weekly (80 hours per semester) in Family Health Strategy/NASF and school scenarios. Teams conduct home visits with families of complex needs, build and biweekly review an integrated care plan, guided by preceptors and tutors, following IPEC's community-centered and patient-centered principles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students always work in multiprofessional teams of at least two students from different backgrounds; teaching-service integration territory; preceptors and tutors supervise directly and indirectly
#### Constraints
- High demand for the FHS/NASF hindered the common start of internships, and frequent changes of preceptors due to precarious contractual bonds required renewed awareness-raising measures

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in nursing, nutrition, public health, physical education and biological sciences in the compulsory internship semester

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative teamwork competencies for interprofessional practice

### Affordances
- [Ipec 2016 Collaborative Practice Framework](../products/ipec-core-competencies.md)
