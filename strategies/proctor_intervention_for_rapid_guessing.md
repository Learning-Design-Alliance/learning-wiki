# Proctor Intervention for Rapid Guessing

## Description
When learners are flagged for rapid guessing during assessments, proctors should approach them, pause their test, and engage them in a low-pressure conversation about what’s going on. Students might be rushing through the test for any number of personal reasons, including confusion and anxiety. Proctors can take this opportunity to see what’s going on for the student and address the issue sensitively before encouraging them to resume the test.  The goal is to help students understand the importance of assessment, not to pressure them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time monitoring of assessment data, trained proctors who can engage in supportive conversations with learners, a non-judgmental approach.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and resources for proctor training, effectiveness depends on proctor’s ability to build rapport with learners.

- Proctors can tailor their approach to address the specific needs and concerns of each learner.
