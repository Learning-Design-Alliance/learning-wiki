# Combine immersive VR simulation with existing traditional teaching resources for venipuncture training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends introducing immersive VR simulation as a complement to, not a replacement for, current resources for teaching vacuum blood collection. It cites literature noting that studies comparing traditional methods with virtual simulators "did not present significant results but show that the combination of strategies, that is, virtual simulations with traditional methods, is indicated". The simulator is positioned for students beginning to study the topic and technique.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an immersive VR laboratory setup with a head-mounted display and gesture sensor
#### Constraints
- The article states studies comparing traditional methods with virtual simulators alone did not present significant results, so combination rather than substitution is indicated

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students beginning study of venipuncture

### Target Learning Goals
- acquiring vacuum blood collection technique and competencies for supervised practice
