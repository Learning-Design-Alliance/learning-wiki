# Fund and guide Grow Your Own programs to attract candidates of color into teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief lists Grow Your Own (GYO) programs among the research-based strategies states should use to target resources toward districts and schools struggling to recruit a diverse workforce. States should develop and expand GYO programming through legislation or state education agency priority, fund it at the state level, and provide state-led guidance, for example via scholarships or stipends to paraprofessionals completing licensure requirements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts need strategic approaches to building and supporting GYO programs, per the brief's recommendation to Ohio
#### Constraints
- Ohio only partially meets this criterion: it authorized a grant program that may include GYO support but has not invested in or guided the programs

### Target Learners
- prospective teachers of color including paraprofessionals and high school students
- districts and schools with hiring challenges

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce
