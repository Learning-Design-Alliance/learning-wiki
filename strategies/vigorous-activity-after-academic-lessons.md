# Schedule vigorous in-class exercise sessions after core academic lessons rather than before them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends timing more vigorous physical activity after, not before, mathematics lessons, based on the absence of math gains in the CARDIO group and teacher observations of post-session agitation. Three of four CARDIO teachers "described the students as agitated", and the article concludes it "may be more beneficial to schedule vigorous activity sessions after mathematics lessons". Calmer formats such as balance tasks are suggested for pre-lesson slots.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom routines that allow flexible placement of activity breaks relative to academic lessons
#### Constraints
- The arousal interpretation remains speculative because the study did not measure motivation, arousal or affective states

### Target Learners
- Primary school children in grades 2 and 3

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining engagement and concentration during subsequent mathematics instruction
