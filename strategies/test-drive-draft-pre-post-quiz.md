# Test-drive draft pre-/post-quizzes with colleagues and former students before first use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Once the synergistic development of the LOs and pre-/post-quiz is nearly finalized, the article recommends finding at least one person, such as a colleague or advanced student, to test-drive it and provide feedback on what they answered correctly versus incorrectly and how long they took. Jonsson regularly served as Cooksey's guinea pig, contributing to revision for general clarity and better focus on what is intended to be assessed; students who have taken the course are good beta testers, since discussing their incorrect answers can inform revision or replacement of questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one willing test-driver (colleague, advanced student, or former student) must be available before the quiz is first administered
#### Constraints
- Feedback may also lead to modifications of the LOs and course design, and revising or replacing questions increases the noise in longitudinal evaluation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate science students

### Target Learning Goals
- valid, unambiguous assessment of course learning objectives
