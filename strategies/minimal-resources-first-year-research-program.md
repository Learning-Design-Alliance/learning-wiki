# Keep physical resource requirements minimal when launching a first-year research program

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article advises that launching a first-year research program requires only modest physical resources: workspace with one table per group, access to lab equipment and computing facilities, and a small supply budget funded through lab fees. The authors report Loyola started with an average budget of $50 per group, later raised to $200 per group, and that a staffed machine shop providing a safety class and tool training can usefully supplement the course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supply budget, however modest, with a funding mechanism such as lab fees
- Access to lab equipment and computing facilities, which may be as simple as standard laptop computers
#### Constraints
- Some additional funds may be required beyond home-improvement-store materials

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- hands-on experimental design and construction skills
