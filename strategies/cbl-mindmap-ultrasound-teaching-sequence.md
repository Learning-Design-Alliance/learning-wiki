# Case-based ultrasound teaching sequence with pre-class case materials, in-class image progression analysis, and mind-map construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific teaching activity sequence for ultrasound residency: before lectures, instructors supply case studies, pertinent data and ultrasound images so trainees review anatomy and diagnostic knowledge, analyse cases, formulate and differentiate diagnoses, and build mind maps. In class, instructors explain how ultrasound images and clinical data change across the case progression, teaching concepts such as "one map for multiple diseases" and "multiple maps for one disease", then guide trainees to use mind maps to organize and reinforce material and foster clinical reasoning. Hands-on operational practice with attention to probe selection, pre-examination preparation and communication accompanies the sequence.

### Target Learning Goals

- clinical reasoning
