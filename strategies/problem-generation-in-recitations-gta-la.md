# Move problem modification and creation into recitation sections supported by trained GTAs and LAs to raise participation and motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking implementation recommendation is to relocate the modification and creation process into recitation sections for both algebra-based Physics 1 classes, relying on Graduate Teaching Assistants and Undergraduate Learning Assistants who are trained and discuss recitation strategies weekly. They report that "incorporating the method in recitation sections that are supported by GTAs and LAs has shown initial success in terms of participation and motivation", with anecdotal improvements in student attitudes toward physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained GTAs and LAs who discuss various recitation strategies weekly
#### Constraints
- Success so far is anecdotal; the authors state future research will have to investigate outcomes from classes using this implementation

### Target Learners
- algebra-based Physics 1 students in recitation sections

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in problem modification and creation, improved attitudes toward physics
