## Description
This strategy implements the review's call for flexible adaptation: instruction should be adjusted to individual learners' needs based on ongoing measurement of their expertise and cognitive load. The abstract states that "more advanced methods to measure expertise and cognitive load" are needed "so that instruction can be flexibly adapted to individual learners' needs." The review reports experimental studies illustrating these developments and offers guidelines for future research.
