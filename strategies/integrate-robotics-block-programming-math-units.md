# Integrate robotics with visual block programming into mathematics units on coordinates and integers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "educational authorities include robotics and programming in the educational context of sixth-grade students in the area of mathematics", especially units on coordinates and integers, where students receive immediate feedback when inserting coordinates or numbers into the robot. They advise starting from basic concepts such as sequences and loops and progressing to intuitive robots from fifth grade, using visual block programs such as Scratch, Blockly or mBlock.

## Design Implications
