# Include cartoon and problem-solving learning outcomes in curriculum and coursebooks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the findings, the article recommends that learning outcomes related to cartoons and problem-solving skills may be included in the curriculum and featured in coursebooks. It further suggests preparing supporting materials where cartoons are used and scrutinised, and sharing research data on humour and humorous elements with teachers through panels and symposiums.
