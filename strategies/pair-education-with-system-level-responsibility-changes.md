# Pair education with system-level changes to shift responsibility attitudes in perioperative teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the responsibility dimension of prevention attitudes did not improve with training alone, the authors recommend complementary organizational strategies: role clarification for MDRPI prevention within multidisciplinary teams, cross-department feedback mechanisms reporting postoperative MDRPI outcomes back to OR teams, and embedding MDRPI prevention into OR quality metrics and routine safety checks. The article states that "education alone may be insufficient to shift the responsibility construct unless it is paired with workflow and organizational changes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizational support for role delineation, feedback loops, and quality-metric integration
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single-group study in which the responsibility dimension showed no significant change (P = 0.157); the recommended strategies were not themselves tested

### Target Learners
- Operating room nurses
- Perioperative team members including anesthesia and ward/PACU staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Shared accountability for MDRPI prevention
- Sustained preventive behavior
