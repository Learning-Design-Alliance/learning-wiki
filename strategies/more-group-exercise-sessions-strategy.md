# Offer more or longer supervised group exercise sessions in home-based online rehabilitation, since participants wanted more

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that participants valued the supervised online group exercise as motivating and fun, and that “Several of the participants wanted more group exercise sessions per week or longer sessions.” A strategy for implementers of home-based online rehabilitation is therefore to schedule additional or extended physiotherapist-led group exercise sessions beyond the two 30-minute weekly sessions used in the pilot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A physiotherapist available to lead additional online group exercise sessions
- Videoconferencing capacity for the group
#### Constraints
- The pilot used two 30-minute exercise sessions per week; the effect of adding sessions was not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- Adult COPD patients in home-based online rehabilitation groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Increased exercise participation and physical activity maintenance
