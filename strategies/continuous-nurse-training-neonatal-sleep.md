# Provide continuous nurse training on neonatal sleep, following ANA guidelines, covering sleep-inducing positions and safe sleep measures

## Description
The article recommends that NICU nurses receive ongoing education about neonatal sleep because the studied nurses showed insufficient awareness of sleep stages, inducing factors and standard induction methods. It states "it is necessary to pay more attention to nurses training about neonatal sleep" and that "the trainings should follow American Nurses Association (ANA) guidelines and should be practiced continuously". The discussion cites prior training studies reporting improved nurse awareness after education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training content should cover sleep positions, sleep policies and safe sleep measures as the cited studies emphasize
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of neonatal sleep stages, safe sleep positions and sleep-inducing methods
