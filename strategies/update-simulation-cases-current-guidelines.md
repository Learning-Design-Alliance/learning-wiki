# Reference the most recent AHA and NCS guidelines and update simulation cases and frameworks to reflect clinical updates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends referencing the most recent AHA and NCS guidelines and updating the simulation cases and post-ROSC framework to reflect any future clinical updates, since these guidelines are periodically updated and the simulation reflects current guidelines at the time of implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of current AHA and NCS post-ROSC guidelines
#### Constraints
- Guidelines are periodically updated, so the simulation session may become outdated without revision

### Target Learners
- Simulation curriculum developers and facilitators

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining accuracy and relevance of post-ROSC simulation content
