# Spread innovative educational outreach ideas through social media, colleague networks and teacher professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes how the disco ball idea disseminated and recommends channels for sharing future innovations. Survey respondents reported hearing about the technique "through colleagues or social media posts referencing either the Cumming et al. (2024) Physics Education paper or the Rapson et al. (2024) article", and the authors conclude that demonstrations at teacher professional development events and national media promotion are also effective ways of spreading the word.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The idea should be simple enough that educators with a basic understanding of science can set it up and explain the results, with details available in scientific publications for those who want more
#### Constraints
- Survey-based evidence of dissemination channels may carry sampling bias, since the survey itself was shared via social media

### Target Learners
- schoolteachers
- informal educators
- astronomy education community

### Target Learning Goals
- adopting innovative solar observing and outreach techniques
