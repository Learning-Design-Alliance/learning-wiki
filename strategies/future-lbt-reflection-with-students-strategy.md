# Test learning-by-teaching of deliberate reflection with less-experienced learners and more practice sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their null results with residents, the authors recommend two adjustments for future work: give learners more practice with the procedure across multiple sessions with a shorter interval, and test the approach with medical students, since learning-by-teaching "seems to be particularly effective for students with little prior knowledge". They also suggest future studies include a reflection template against which participants' reasoning can be compared, and use qualitative studies to gain more insight into the reasoning process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning phase with multiple sessions and possibly a fixed study period, since residents may need more practice to automatize the procedure
#### Constraints
- The authors state they do not know whether a fixed study period would have helped, and that their study may have been underpowered

### Target Learners
- medical students with little prior knowledge
- general-practice residents

### Target Learning Goals
- adopting deliberate reflection as an autonomous diagnostic reasoning strategy
