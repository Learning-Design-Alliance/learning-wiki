# Salt-plus-ice extension experiment reaching about -18 °C, run as an optional lowest-temperature competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Second experiment keeping the same groups: students pour 500 mL of room-temperature water into a beaker, add about 10 ice cubes of approximately equal amounts across groups "so that possible differences cannot be associated to a different setup of the experiment", measure the water+ice temperature (about 0 °C), then dissolve salt while measuring. Once the temperature stops dropping, students may add more ice; the guide notes "You could even transform this part of the experiment into a competition: which group will reach the lowest temperature?"

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 400 g of ground ice
- Approximately equal amounts of ice added by all groups
- Same grouping of students as the first experiment
#### Constraints
- If groups cannot reach -18 °C, the guide suggests removing some water so the ice cubes touch the salt at the bottom; the drop is weaker if the ice floats on the surface without contacting the salt

### Target Learners
- GCSE students
- A-level students

### Target Learning Goals
- Explain the stronger temperature drop via the additional breaking of bonds between water molecules in ice
