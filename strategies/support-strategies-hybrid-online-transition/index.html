Provide frequent deadline reminders, easy instructor access, and technical support to ease undergraduate transition to hybrid/online learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends several support strategies for undergraduates transitioning to hybrid or online formats. These include providing "consistent reminders of upcoming assignments," giving students instructor and teaching assistant contact information, allowing first online quizzes to be taken twice to reduce submission problems, offering an "online readiness assessment," and providing "adequate online course management training and round-the-clock technical support."

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors must be accessible for timely feedback and communication

  • Some student fears and anxiety can be lessened with appropriate support and training, but instilling motivation among undergraduates may prove more challenging

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate students transitioning from traditional to hybrid or online course formats
