# Recruit students with essays instead of transcripts and recommendation letters to avoid biased measures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The author replaces transcripts and letters of recommendation with two short essays, citing studies showing that "letters of recommendation tend to reﬂect the gender biases of their writers" and that grades are also subject to bias. The first essay gauges interest and self-expression; the second asks students to expand on an Astronomy Picture of the Day blurb to gauge independent learning ability. Essays are followed by short interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Providing a list of leading questions to guide students through both essays
- Interviews at the high school to learn about prior research experience, coding skills, and personality
#### Constraints
- Selection still uses ordered criteria including seriousness, prior experience, age, gender, and ethnicity, with no student accepted or turned away solely on gender or ethnicity

### Target Learners
- high-school applicants

### Target Learning Goals
- access to authentic research experiences for diverse applicants
