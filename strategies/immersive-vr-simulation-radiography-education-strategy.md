# Incorporate immersive VR simulation into radiography education to build clinical preparedness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating immersive VR simulation, such as the X-Ray Pro VR Suite by Virtual Medical Coaching, into radiography curricula alongside or replacing traditional simulation. Learners practise routine and complex clinical scenarios in immersive environments with "immediate feedback and advanced scenario‐based training", including adjusting exposure factors to see effects on image quality that modern digital radiography usually conceals. The conclusion calls this "a scalable, standardised and cost‐effective solution". The strategy is grounded in the study's measured preparedness and performance advantages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to immersive VR simulation facilities such as the X-Ray Pro VR Suite
- Equivalent total simulation time and matched clinical scenarios between training methods
- Tutors willing to use innovative teaching methods within the VR environment
#### Constraints
- Findings come from two universities and a sample of 80, so smaller institutional differences may not have been detected
- Long-term impact on clinical performance, decision making, teamwork and career progression is not yet known

### Target Learners
- Newly qualified diagnostic radiographers
- Undergraduate radiography students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical preparedness
- Radiographic technical proficiency
- Confidence and adaptability in clinical settings
- Problem-solving in patient care
