Research on composite instructional designs should connect studies via a common theoretical model and may exploit machine-learning-based process data¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The editorial recommends that future research connect multiple studies through a common theoretical model, so that preparation-effect assumptions can be tested coherently and advance theory building. It also expects that developments in methodology, "especially in data collection and evaluation utilizing machine learning strategies", can give a more detailed picture of learning processes in complex instructional designs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A common theoretical model shared across studies, and an appropriate balance between ecological validity and controlled implementation when using machine-learning methods

Constraints¶

  • Current special-issue studies constitute independent efforts rather than a coherent test of preparation-effect assumptions

Target Learners¶

  • researchers and designers of multi-phase instruction

Target Learning Goals¶

  • systematic theory building on preparation effects in composite instructional designs

Related Strategies¶
