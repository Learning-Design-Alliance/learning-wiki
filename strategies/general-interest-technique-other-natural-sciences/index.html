Use the general technique for forming learners' interest in teaching other natural sciences¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends extending the general technique for the formation of learners' interest beyond physics: "The proposed general technique for the formation of learners' interest can be used in teaching other natural sciences (e.g., Chemistry, Biology, Geography, Ecology) for providing the sustainable development of education." The technique's components, the active means of training and active learning methods, are transferable because they connect educational material to life and rely on learners' own activity. The article also states that schools need to teach learners to acquire knowledge independently.
