# Systematically integrate practice-based modules targeting tactical intervention and performance evaluation into coach and teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends curricular enrichment with explicit practice-based modules for tactical facilitation and evaluative expertise. It proposes "The incorporation of a dedicated course such as 'Tactical Intervention and Performance Evaluation,' as proposed by the present study", alongside structured video analysis tasks, simulation-based coaching experiences, targeted micro-teaching with immediate feedback, and authentic assessment such as athlete-focused reflections, peer evaluations, digital portfolios, and game performance assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programmes should ensure systematic opportunities to practice and refine the reflective, tactical, and evaluative dimensions of game-based coaching
#### Constraints
- Rather than demonstrating causal impact, the findings highlight facilitation patterns that may condition the effectiveness of game-based implementation

### Target Learners
- pre-service coaches in coach education programmes
- pre-service physical education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- in-game tactical intervention
- reflective evaluation and performance analysis
- tactical questioning
