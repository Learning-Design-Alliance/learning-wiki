# Use realistic simulation scenarios of STEMI assistance, adjusted to the local health system, in undergraduate medical teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend developing a realistic simulation standard scenario of STEMI assistance, adjusted to the particularities of the Brazilian health system, because it "can be reproduced in other medical schools and used in the teaching process". Simulation is described as an active learning methodology that was used here for evaluation but can also be used in medical teaching, and as an interesting alternative for improving emergency competencies during the undergraduate course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A realistic scenario standard adjusted to the particularities of the local health system
- Integration of teaching with the urgency and emergencies network, including the "zero vacancy" transfer concept
#### Constraints
- The article notes some regional differences, such as availability of fibrinolytic agents in some Emergency Units, and suggests dividing the transfer item into two sub-items in such settings

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- recently graduated physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- STEMI diagnosis
- temporality of treatment
- assistance-flow and transfer management
- communication skills
