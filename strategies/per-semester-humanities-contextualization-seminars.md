# Schedule special contextualization seminars each semester, taught by economics, philosophy, geography, history and sociology professors, aligned with the science content in progress

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To give the science content its social, political, economic and historical contextualization, the proposal foresees that "Seminários especiais com professores de economia, filosofia, geografia, história e sociologia estão previstos em cada semestre para as respectivas contextualizações das matérias científicas". The seminars implement the CTS dimension of the curriculum, connecting each semester's scientific discipline with the historical and social circumstances of the technological products it underpins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Seminars are programmed in the semester in which the related scientific discipline is taught, e.g. thermodynamics together with the first industrial revolution
#### Constraints
- Offered as elective disciplines or complementary activities: "a título de disciplinas eletivas ou atividades complementares"

### Target Learners
- licenciatura students in science teacher education

### Target Learning Goals
- socio-political, economic and historical contextualization of science content
