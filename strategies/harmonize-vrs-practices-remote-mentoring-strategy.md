# Harmonize VRS training practices and use remote mentoring and multimodal simulation to broaden access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends harmonizing VRS practices and assessment methods internationally, and proposes concrete measures to widen access: "Remote mentoring should also be considered as it might help reducing costs and overcome geographic limitations while optimizing experts supervision time." It also notes VRS is "frequently combined with dry-or wet-laboratory simulation training and theorical learning courses" as complementary methods, and that on-machine supports like the VRM net platform remain valuable alternatives. The strategy targets program directors and curriculum developers designing ophthalmic surgical training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated instructors or remote mentoring arrangements, and simulator access for trainees, are needed for VRS programs to function as described.
#### Constraints
- Finding one dedicated instructor may be challenging as surgery teaching is time consuming for experts.

### Target Learners
- ophthalmology residents
- fellows
- junior surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- cataract and vitreoretinal surgical skills
- pre-patient surgical competence prior to operating room access
