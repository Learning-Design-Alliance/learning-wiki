## Description
The brief closes by offering "implications for funders and program implementers engaged in systems change," responding to growing philanthropic and community development interest in investing in systems change and to the difficulty of sorting through proliferating research on systems change success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funders and implementers actively engaged in education systems change efforts
#### Constraints
- Implications are derived from a literature review and community narratives rather than from a new experimental evaluation

### Target Learners
- Children and youth in communities undertaking systems change

### Target Learning Goals
- Guiding investment and implementation decisions in education systems change
