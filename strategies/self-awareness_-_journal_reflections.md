# Self-Awareness - Journal Reflections

## Description
This activity involves having learners think back on their experiences and outcomes, which helps provide them with a greater understanding of themselves. Learners can think and write about their experiences throughout a day or class period and look for patterns, as well as create and manage goals. Self-awareness is the ability to correctly identify your own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they impact behavior.
