# Multi-source intensive case study followed by a stakeholder conference

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For individual cases, the department combined school reports, social-visitor family histories, medical and psychological examinations, and then convened a conference of laboratory staff, teachers, principal, parent, and superintendent to discuss the case frankly and make treatment recommendations. The article states "These studies included a school report, personal and family history (investigated by a social visitor), medical examination, and psychological examination."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to a social visitor, medical examiner, and psychological testing staff, plus cooperation of parents and school staff
#### Constraints
- intensive studies were made only of such individual cases as could be reached by the department

### Target Learners
- individual exceptional children in public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing individual children's conditions and recommending treatment
