# Provide family-centred support services that empower families and reduce parental stress, including respectful school–parent cooperation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that findings on elevated conflict and reduced closeness in NDD families be used to motivate "better informed and more widespread family‐centred support" rather than blame. They argue support services should "empower families and reduce parental stress", which may help shape an everyday environment conducive to learning. They also point to high-quality, respectful cooperation between school staff and parents of children with autism as a lever for reducing parental stress and improving family well-being.
