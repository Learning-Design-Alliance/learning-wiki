# Tailor goals of care conversation teaching to discipline-specific needs and avoid formulaic approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Program directors who identified a curricular gap proposed approaches including "Online modules, podcasts, practical tips, workshops, simulation, clinical exposure, role modeling, mentorship". Their comments emphasized teaching "Tailored to discipline-specific needs" and warned that "a 'formulaic approach' may be counterproductive" because every situation differs, so techniques must flex to clinical context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Approaches must be adapted to each program's discipline-specific context and integrated into existing curricula rather than delivered uniformly.
#### Constraints
- These techniques remain program director proposals; the survey did not evaluate their effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- communication skills for goals of care conversations
