# Future CT-in-ELT research should prioritise inclusive, data-driven, longitudinal approaches integrating pedagogical experimentation and sociocultural perspectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends directions for future research on critical thinking in English language teaching, moving beyond descriptive bibliometric mapping. Its conclusion states that "Future research should prioritise inclusive, data driven, and longitudinal approaches that integrate pedagogical experimentation and sociocultural perspectives to strengthen CT development in diverse educational settings." The Discussion adds recommendations for mixed or more dynamic methodologies, including AI-assisted meta-synthesis, and for a pedagogical framework situating critical thinking as both a cognitive process and a social practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research designs that go beyond descriptive mapping to include classroom-based trials and cohesive technological pedagogical designs
#### Constraints
- The article notes its own analysis lacked gender-sensitive or sociocultural analysis due to insufficient granularity of current bibliometric tools

### Target Learners
- EFL learners in higher education
- English language teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Developing critical thinking through English language teaching
