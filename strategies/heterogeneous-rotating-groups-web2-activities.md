# Form heterogeneous student groups and rotate membership across Web 2.0 activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on interview complaints and field notes, the article recommends that teachers form groups as heterogeneous as possible and change group members for each new activity, citing Richards and Rogers on heterogeneous group formation. The article states "the teacher needs to form groups  more heterogeneous" and that this "can be done by changing the group members for each activity" to avoid unequal effort and lack of devotion to shared products.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher oversight of group composition
- Rotation of members across activities
#### Constraints
- There is no absolute solution because there is no way to please every student

### Target Learners
- adult EFL university preparatory students

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative vocabulary content creation
