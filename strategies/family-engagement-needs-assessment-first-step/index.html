Conduct a family engagement needs assessment as the first step in planning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The playbook prescribes a needs assessment as "an initial and essential step in developing a tailored family engagement plan." It uses surveys, focus groups, interviews, and analysis of existing data such as climate surveys and attendance records. Tools should be created collaboratively by school and district leaders, educators, and family engagement specialists, and data collected from students, teachers, families, leaders, and community partners so the plan fits the school's specific context.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tools must be comprehensive, culturally sensitive, and aligned with the school's objectives; administration must include a wide range of school community members

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students and their families

Target Learning Goals¶
