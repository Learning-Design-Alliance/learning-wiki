# Building a Cyprus Golden Record: mixed-group discussions of what to send to an alien civilisation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A bi-communal astronomy activity in which children from both communities were "split into mixed groups" and discussed what they would send to an alien civilisation as a representation of the whole island of Cyprus. The authors report this "allowed the children to get to know each others' cultures, and to discover the many similarities between them". The finished artefact, constructed by participating children, is shown in the article's Fig. 1.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mixed-group composition across the two communities
- a shared framing task (representing the whole island)
#### Constraints
- Designed for contexts where groups hold distinct but partly shared cultural identities

### Target Learners
- school children from both communities

### Target Learning Goals
- cross-cultural understanding
- discovering similarities between groups
- scientific curiosity
