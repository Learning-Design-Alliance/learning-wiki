# Distributed low-cost simulation training schedule for basic laparoscopic skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training recipe: sessions twice a week for 4 weeks, each lasting a maximum of 1 hour, practicing 4 FLS tasks with instructors present for feedback, at least 10 repetitions of peg transfer and pattern cutting, and suturing practiced "until a self-perceived improvement was noted." The schedule follows evidence that "skill retention is superior using a distributed training model (i.e., practice interspersed with periods of rest) compared with mass training model."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors present to provide feedback and supervise participants
- An instruction video demonstrating the tasks shown before the first session
#### Constraints
- Sessions limited to 1 hour each with a maximum overall time limit per session

### Target Learners
- medical students with limited laparoscopic experience

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of basic laparoscopic skills outside the operating room
