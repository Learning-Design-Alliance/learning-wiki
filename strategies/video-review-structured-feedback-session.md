# Structured post-procedure feedback sessions using video review with a senior surgeon

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
For live surgery, the review describes feedback facilitated by review of a videotape recording of the operation and a 60 min structured feedback session, during which technical deficiencies and possible errors were covered and instructions for improvement offered. This approach was associated with reduced procedure time and improved economy of movement and error scores in live cholecystectomy. The authors note this manner of provision may be resource intensive, so cost- and time-effectiveness must be considered.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learning Goals
