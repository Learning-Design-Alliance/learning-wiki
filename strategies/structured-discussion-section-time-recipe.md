# Structure discussion section time with a short objectives review, a structured activity, and follow-up review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's first recommendation urges instructors to use a backwards design approach to set discussion section goals, assessments, and activities, then give TAs a concrete time structure: "a short (e.g., 10 min) review of relevant learning objectives, 30 min of a structured activity designed to support learning the learning objectives, and end with 10 min of follow-up and review". Observations of sections and weekly pedagogy discussions are suggested to support TA instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors set discussion section goals and communicate structure recommendations to TAs
#### Constraints
- The study did not assess student learning; the article notes many recommendations have been previously shown to support understanding

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in large biology lecture courses with TA-led discussion sections

### Target Learning Goals
- content understanding
- sense of belonging
- engagement and attendance
