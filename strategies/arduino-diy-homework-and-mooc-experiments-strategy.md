# Use low-cost Arduino hardware for do-it-yourself homework experiments, online curricula, and emerging-country physics labs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose extending the low-cost Arduino approach beyond the project lab: university physics curricula in emerging countries with limited funding, experiments outside university walls, homework with do-it-yourself experiments using lent Arduino boards and simple sensors with tasks matching the lesson of the day, and online or MOOC-type curricula. They write it "could also be particularly useful in the case of an online education curriculum and MOOC type of approach", and that similar teaching in other universities could encourage new exchanges among physics teachers and students.
