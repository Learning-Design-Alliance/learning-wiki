# Implement UbD through collaborative teacher design groups with iterative evaluation meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy organizes UbD implementation by training teachers first, then forming small design groups that each produce unit plans for their courses, implement them, and share experiences at evaluation meetings after each implementation. The action plan was reshaped with the help of views gathered at the meetings, so each successive design benefited from the previous cycle. In this study the process ran across one semester for Reading and Listening & Speaking courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers are primarily trained about UbD before designing
- Groups design unit plans by sticking to current curriculum's objectives while enriching content via UbD's guiding principles
#### Constraints
- The study was carried out during the spring semester of the 2014-2015 academic year at one university's School of Foreign Languages

### Target Learners
- University EFL preparatory-class students

### Target Learning Goals
- Foreign language learning motivation
- Meaningful and transferable language learning
