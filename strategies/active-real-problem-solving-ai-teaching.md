# Teach AI through holistic, active, collaborative pedagogical strategies starting from real problem solving

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For meaningful learning of AI to take place, the review states AI teaching must use holistic, active, and collaborative pedagogical strategies in which real problem solving is the starting point of the learning process. This aligns with its broader conclusion that AI education can adopt a global, practical, and active approach in which project-based and contextualized work plays an important role, and with learning experiences built on constructionist ideas of building and creating intelligent devices.
