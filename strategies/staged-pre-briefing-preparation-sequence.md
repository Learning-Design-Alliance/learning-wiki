# Staged pre-briefing preparation before a simulation scenario (written material, online class, laboratory practice)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's pre-briefing sequence prepares students over roughly two weeks before scenario execution: written study material on PACU and pain care 15 days ahead, a synchronous online expository-dialogue class ten days ahead, and a laboratory practical class with equipment review and environment recognition two days ahead. The article states pre-briefing "aimed to provide guidance that would assist in the execution of the scenario and clinical judgment, increasing confidence in dealing with problems". Environment recognition is described as increasing participant safety, confidence, and autonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a simulation laboratory, high-fidelity mannequin and equipment, and time for students' individualized study of the provided material
#### Constraints
- Timings and sequence as implemented in this study's single testing day; the article does not report comparative evidence for this schedule

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical judgment for pain assessment and control
- confidence and psychomotor readiness before simulation
