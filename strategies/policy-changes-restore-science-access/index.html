Pursue policy, materials, and professional development changes to restore science as a core content area for all students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's closing recommendations target national, state, and local levels: change accountability systems so performance in science is an indicator; develop new standards-based instructional materials that "include both academic language and science objectives"; ensure district policies guarantee all students access to science curricula and curriculum guides provide time for inquiry-based science; and provide professional development in both science content and academic-language content at pre-service and in-service levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers highly qualified in both the content and language domains
  • District policies ensuring access to science curricula and time for inquiry-based science

Constraints¶

  • State educational codes may not mandate instructional time requirements for science

Target Learners¶

  • All students, regardless of language status or linguistic background

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Science content knowledge
  • Academic language development
  • College and career readiness
