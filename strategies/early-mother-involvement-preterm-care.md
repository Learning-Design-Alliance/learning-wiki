# Involve mothers in preterm infant care starting immediately after birth, from 30-60 minutes postpartum or after 24 hours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends beginning maternal role attainment through early mother-baby interaction and involvement in care. It states "The nurse is as soon as possible after 30 to 60 minutes after birth, involving the mother in caring for her baby", calling this a critical and sensitive period for bonding, and elsewhere says involvement "can be started after 24 hours post partum until the return of preterm infants from the hospital." Mothers then participate in measures preventing apnea, hypothermia, impaired nutrition and infection.
