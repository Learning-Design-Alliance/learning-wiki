# Target tutoring equity by prioritizing underserved students and using culturally reflective curriculum and formative data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's equity focus runs through all five profiled states: grant applications prioritizing rural, low-income, and students-of-color communities (Colorado); implementing tutoring in ways that affirm students' identities and solve longstanding inequities (Louisiana); curriculum reflecting students' cultural backgrounds (New Mexico); and prioritizing underserved students who may need more time to master skills or haven't received other academic supports (Tennessee). New Mexico's roadmap states that "Accelerated learning enables educators to connect unfinished learning with new ideas and new information, all while engaging with grade-level content and celebrating student cultural wealth."
