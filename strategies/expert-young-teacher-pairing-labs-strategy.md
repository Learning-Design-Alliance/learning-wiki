# Pair young teachers with expert teachers in laboratories for informal teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Since 2007 young teachers in initial training are invited to a full-immersion stage at the summer school, where 'usually an expert and a young teacher were engaged in laboratory for improving teacher practice'. Methodologies are discussed and selected with the teachers, and laboratory activities with students are supervised by a young and an expert teacher to fix correct methodology. The authors report this informal training is very effective, with some activities entering school practice and a small professional community forming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- About 4-5 expert teachers and 5-7 young teachers at the school
- Joint planning of activities between university and school teachers
#### Constraints
- Teachers are often obliged to interrupt the stage for school service; young teachers without permanent positions can be called for temporary jobs; few in-service teachers participate; in 2013 no teachers from the compressed Formative Active Training course attended

### Target Learners
- young and pre-service physics teachers in initial training

### Target Learning Goals
- laboratory methodology
- design of inquiry-based activities
