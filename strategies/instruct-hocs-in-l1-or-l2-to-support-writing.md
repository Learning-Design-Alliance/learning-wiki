# Provide instruction on higher order cognitive skills in L1 and/or L2 to support writing development for dual language learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a preliminary practical implication, the article recommends instruction on higher order cognitive skills — inference, perspective taking, and comprehension monitoring — in either language. Because the bifactor structure suggests a common skill across languages, "instruction in L1 or L2 would help develop higher order cognitions for dual language learners", and because these skills relate to writing quality, such instruction "would improve students' writing skills". The authors stress this is preliminary given the correlational design and note instructional studies targeting higher order cognition for dual language learners are scarce.
