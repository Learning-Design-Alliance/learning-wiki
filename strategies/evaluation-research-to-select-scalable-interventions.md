# Use evaluation research as a tool to determine which interventions are likely to produce desired outcomes and which organizations are ready to scale them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a concrete funder strategy: "Implementing this process can assist foundations in using evaluation research as a tool to determine which interventions are likely to produce desired outcomes, and to identify which organizations are ready to scale them." This pairs evidence review of interventions with assessment of organizational readiness before scaling.
