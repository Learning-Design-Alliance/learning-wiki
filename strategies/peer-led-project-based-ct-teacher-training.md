# Use peer-led, project-based professional development led by teachers in similar contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project supported educators seeing themselves as part of the region's computing future partly by "providing hands-on, project-based trainings on computational thinking, led by other K-8 teachers who work in similar contexts." For the first three years a Pittsburgh STEAM teacher with about 10 years of experience trained the Eastern KY teachers. A second supporting approach was having local industry representatives explain how computing skills matter in their sectors and how K-8 teachers play a role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers who are K-8 teachers working in similar contexts
- Hands-on, project-based training formats
#### Constraints
- Initially required importing an experienced outside trainer (a Pittsburgh STEAM teacher) before local teachers could take over

### Target Learners
- K-8 teachers in Eastern Kentucky

### Target Learning Goals
- Computational thinking teaching skills
- Seeing their role in shaping the region's future
