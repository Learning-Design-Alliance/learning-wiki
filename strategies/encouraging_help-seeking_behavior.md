# Encouraging Help-Seeking Behavior

## Description
Creating a classroom environment where students feel comfortable asking for help. Some students may find it difficult to ask for assistance, so it's important to foster a supportive atmosphere. Research suggests that students' varied responses to academic challenges highlight the need for educators to encourage help-seeking behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive classroom environment, explicit encouragement of help-seeking behavior, and accessible resources.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in classroom culture. Students may need explicit encouragement and support to feel comfortable asking for help.
