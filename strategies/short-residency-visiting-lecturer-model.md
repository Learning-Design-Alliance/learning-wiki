# Short-residency visiting-lecturer model for astronomy-specific teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy recruits Croatian astronomers working abroad and foreign scientists to deliver the astronomy-specific courses, while local physics faculty teach the non-astronomy courses required for the degree. Visiting lecturers 'will not need to spend a whole semester in Split, but rather a few weeks necessary for completing their course'. This keeps international expertise flowing into a department that currently has no professional astronomers, and the same visitors offer master projects in their home institutions abroad.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A network of Croatian and foreign astronomers willing to visit and teach, since the article notes there are currently no professional astronomers at the Department of Physics in Split
- Courses must be organised in blocks compatible with short visits
#### Constraints
- Visiting lecturers are present only for the few weeks needed to complete their course, not a whole semester

### Target Learners
- MSc students in astrophysics taught by international faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- astronomy-specific master-level coursework
- research projects and publishable results at international institutions
