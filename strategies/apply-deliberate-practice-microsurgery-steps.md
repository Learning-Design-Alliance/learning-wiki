# Apply deliberate practice in microsurgery by setting measurable goals, deconstructing skills, planning practice time, and seeking expert guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article prescribes four application steps: transforming abstract aspirations into "actionable, trackable goals," breaking complex procedures into smaller components, purposefully scheduling practice rather than relying on clinical execution, and integrating expert guidance through coaching or mentorship. Mentors foster long-term mentee-driven development, while coaches focus on short-term, evaluative skill improvement with a standardized question-based approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated practice time separate from patient care, guided by an instructor or mentor following a structured plan
#### Constraints
- Intrinsic motivation is crucial but insufficient; expert guidance must be systematically integrated

### Target Learners
- periodontists and implant dentists developing microsurgical competence

### Target Learning Goals
- precise needle entry/exit placement, suturing, and other component skills of microsurgery
