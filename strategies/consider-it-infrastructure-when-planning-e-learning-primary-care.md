# Explicitly consider local IT conditions and digital infrastructure when planning e-learning implementation in primary care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (3 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review argues that e-learning introduces determinants tied to its digital format and mode of delivery, since reliance on IT infrastructure distinguishes it from other learning interventions. It states this "underscores the need to explicitly consider information technological conditions and prerequisites when planning and implementing e-learning in primary care settings", citing firewall restrictions and variable mobile network coverage as reported barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Available and functional local digital infrastructure and internet access for learners
#### Constraints
- In resource-limited contexts, reliance on mobile devices and cellular data with variable network coverage and data costs hindered consistent engagement

### Target Learners
- healthcare professionals in primary care and community-based settings, including resource-limited contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent engagement with and access to e-learning interventions for infection management
