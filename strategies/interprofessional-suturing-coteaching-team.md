# Interprofessional co-teaching team pairing expert midwives with an obstetrician-gynecologist for suturing instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The workshop's teaching and evaluation team combined midwifery and surgical expertise: "2 community‐practicing certified nurse‐midwives (CNMs), 2 faculty CNMs, one obstetrician‐gynecologist, and an educational technology expert." The physician demonstrated ideal surgical techniques while midwifery faculty demonstrated skills up close and assisted students at their desks. The article states this co-teaching approach models effective interprofessional communication and fosters interprofessional trust and cooperation among professions with shared clinical responsibilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one expert midwife and one expert surgeon or obstetrician-gynecologist on the teaching team
#### Constraints
- Additional research is needed to identify how to meet all learners' needs in an interprofessional education setting

### Target Learners
- student nurse-midwives
- medical students
- obstetric interns
- family practice residents and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- perineal repair technique
- interprofessional collaboration and understanding of roles
