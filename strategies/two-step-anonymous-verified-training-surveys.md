# Two-step pre- and post-event surveys with anonymous verification to collect learning-experience metrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors implemented "a two-step survey process, circulated among participants before and after the training events". Pre-event feedback lets educators tailor their approach to each group's needs, while post-event surveys assess effectiveness and identify parts requiring revision. To ensure response rates while maintaining anonymity, submitting the survey generates a return code entered into the Indico system to confirm completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An anonymous verification process linking survey completion to event registration (a return code entered into Indico)
#### Constraints
- All registered participants, including those who did not attend or complete the module, are encouraged to complete the whole survey, which can dilute post-survey responses

### Target Learners
- training-event participants in High Energy Physics

### Target Learning Goals
- continuous improvement of training material and events
- tailoring explanations to participant needs
