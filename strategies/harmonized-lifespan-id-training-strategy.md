# Harmonize ID training between and within countries and across the psychiatrist lifespan from CAP to old age psychiatry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend harmonized ID training within and between countries to promote harmonized care despite contextual differences, and that ID training "should not be limited to CAP training" but should facilitate transitions between child and adolescent psychiatry, adult psychiatry, and old age psychiatry as life-expectancy of people with ID increases. They also stress cooperation with other professionals and including people with ID, families, and carers as stakeholders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination between national training bodies and across psychiatric subspecialties
#### Constraints
- The authors acknowledge differing economic power, service organization, and societal expectations across countries

### Target Learners
- psychiatry trainees across child, adult and old age specialties

### Target Learning Goals
- continuity of competent psychiatric care for people with intellectual disabilities across the lifespan
