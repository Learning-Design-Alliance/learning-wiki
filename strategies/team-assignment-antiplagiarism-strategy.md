# Replace individually submitted homework with team assignments of 3 to 5 students to make policing plagiarism redundant.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy restructures weekly homework so that students "must team up with 3 or 4 fellow students and hand in one copy of the assignment solutions per team", turning would-be copiers into collaborators. Teams self-organize during the first two course weeks using the icampus Q&A board, report their membership for a team number, and the instructor allows flexible team rearrangements during the semester rather than using rigid icampus team management. Assignments are distributed weekly via the instructor's own teaching-materials web-server.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams of 3 to 5 members handing in one solution set per team
- A team-formation period of about two weeks at the course start
- A tool such as icampus Q&A for students to find or recruit teams
- Fast grading turnaround, with assignments returned within a week
#### Constraints
- Occasional team-change requests arise when members stop participating or teams want to merge
- The instructor deliberately does not interfere with how teams divide their work

### Target Learners
- first-year university students in large General Physics classes

### Target Learning Goals
- physics problem solving
- teamwork and cooperation skills
