# Use composed PRE–POST–EXP process model views to give feedback on rework, order, and time performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For each objective the authors compose a view of three models — the student's PRE and POST executions and an expert model — applying activity, case-selection, and collapsing filters. The rework view shows which activities the student repeated; the order view shows activities performed in the wrong order or not at all; the performance view highlights activities "performed too slowly" and transitions that took too long, benchmarked against average expert times. These views let instructors focus deliberate practice on specific difficult steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Event logs from tagged videos for PRE, POST, and expert executions
- A discovery algorithm and filtering options (Celonis was used)
#### Constraints
- Checking activities are excluded in the rework view and well-performed phases are collapsed or excluded in the order view, for readability

### Target Learners
- medical students learning surgical procedures in simulation

### Target Learning Goals
- avoiding rework, executing activities in the correct order, and matching expert time performance
