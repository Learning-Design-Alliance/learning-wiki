# Use ChatGPT-4o as a supplementary educational tool rather than an autonomous clinical decision-support system in non-English contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that ChatGPT-4o "may be best used as a supplementary educational tool rather than an independent clinical decision-support system in non-English contexts." This strategy recommends limiting the model to supportive roles in dental education while its performance parity with human specialists is not yet established across all languages, given the patient safety risks of relying on insufficiently reliable tools in clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Human oversight and correction of AI-generated responses in educational settings
#### Constraints
- The model is currently insufficiently reliable for clinical decision-making in the Chinese healthcare context, with a 16% performance deficit relative to OMS specialists

### Target Learners
- dental undergraduate students, OMS graduate students, and clinicians in non-English-speaking contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and effective use of AI-assisted study support for dental licensing exam preparation
