# Immersing awarded students in a year-long scholarship program with monthly meetings, observatory visits and community outreach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy is a follow-on award used by MAES 2018: the fifteen finalists received CNPq PIBIC-Jr (ICJ) scholarships for 2019 and took part in monthly meetings at the Observatório Astronômico do Ifes Guarapari and in state parks and historic centers. According to the article, awardees improved their projects, attended lectures on astrophysics, astronomy, astronautics and cosmology, and joined public sky-observation actions, immersing them in scientific congresses and community-facing outreach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding from foment agencies to sustain the scholarships, which the authors report was unavailable in 2019
#### Constraints
- The article reports the scholarship award was discontinued in 2019 because funding could not be secured

### Target Learners
- High-school students in public and federal state schools (Espírito Santo, Brazil)

### Target Learning Goals
- Scientific research initiation in astronomy
- communication of project work to diverse public audiences
