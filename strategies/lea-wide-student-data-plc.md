# Establish an LEA-wide student learning data PLC and schedule data study with school leaders

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For building districts' data processes for school improvement, the guide recommends that leaders "Establish an LEA-wide student learning data professional learning community (PLC) to study student performance trends and identify and address challenges" and "Establish an LEA-wide schedule for LEA and school leaders to study student learning data to develop a shared understanding." Additional actions include identifying an LEA team to coordinate sharing key data, engaging in routine dialogue about school leaders' data-analysis needs, and developing targeted professional learning to build leaders' data-analysis capacity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systems for collecting, analyzing, and sharing school performance data; district capacity to provide data-analysis professional learning
#### Constraints
- Addressed to district-level leaders supporting school improvement planning

### Target Learners
- District leaders and school leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- Data literacy for school improvement planning and monitoring student learning trends
