# Team projects in which students discover quantum concepts (teleportation presentation and engaging Grover-algorithm video)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two group projects of three students each anchor the second half of the course. For teleportation, teams prepare a presentation addressing provided questions, determining what gates comprise each box in a diagram and programming the circuit on the Quantum Composer; each team "essentially works on their own to discover the concept of teleportation with some prompt questions from us". For the Money or Tigers project (Grover's algorithm for four qubits), teams create an engaging video presenting the puzzle in any way they choose, and the authors report students "have come up with very nice and creative ideas".
