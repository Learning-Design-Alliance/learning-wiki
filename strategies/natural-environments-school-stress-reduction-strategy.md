# Use natural environments in educational activities as a feasible intervention for reducing children's stress

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend organising education so that children spend time in natural environments, arguing that genuine education outside the classroom is already common and spreading in Denmark and other Western countries. They write that "using natural environments in educational activities could be a feasible intervention for reducing stress and thereby more widely enhancing wellbeing among children". The strategy is framed as practically implementable within existing school practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools positioned in close proximity to natural environments and teachers and classes regularly performing education outside the classroom
#### Constraints
- Direct transfer of findings to children not accustomed to education outside the classroom or residing in urban areas may not be possible

### Target Learners
- primary school children in schools near natural environments

### Target Learning Goals
- stress reduction and wellbeing enhancement
