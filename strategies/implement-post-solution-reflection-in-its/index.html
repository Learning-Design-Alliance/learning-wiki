Implement post-solution reflective activities (reflection questions with feedback) in intelligent tutoring systems¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The studies support adding post-solution reflective activities such as reflection questions after problem solving in intelligent tutoring systems like Andes. Reflection questions with feedback improved learning over no reflection, and canned feedback sufficed: the studies "support the practice of implementing post-solution reflective activities in intelligent tutoring systems, but call into question the need for natural-language processing techniques." Implementable as scripted reflection questions followed by canned feedback.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reflection questions posed after problem solving, with feedback provided on student responses

Constraints¶

  • Human tutor feedback with multi-exchange, dialectic character was not significantly more effective than canned feedback, so expensive NLP support may be unnecessary

Target Learners¶

  • students solving physics problems in intelligent tutoring systems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conceptual understanding and problem-solving ability

Claims¶

  • Post-solution dialogues in physics tutoring most prominently serve explaining conceptual knowledge and integrating it with strategic problem-solving knowledge [+M]
  • Frequency of post-solution discussions, abstraction from the current problem, and tutor initiation predict transfer gains in Andes [+M]
  • Reflection questions with feedback produce more learning than no reflection questions, but canned feedback and human tutoring do not differ significantly [+M]
  • Human tutors adapt post-solution discussions to students' ability levels and current-problem performance [+M]
