# Use the tools as facilitated conversation starters that produce artifacts, not as a checklist exercise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide prescribes how teams should work with its tools: "Completion of the tools should not be a goal in and of itself; use of this guide is not a checklist exercise. Instead, we encourage using the tools as conversation starters." Working through the prompts creates artifacts that document discussions and lead to further discussion. General facilitation recommendations include engaging a skilled facilitator who can help participants examine assumptions, resolve differing viewpoints, and allow all team members to participate, plus setting norms, timelines, and manageable priorities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A cross-agency or cross-district team with diverse perspectives, since having a team increases the diversity of ideas and thus the value of the tool
#### Constraints
- If LEAs will use some tools, the SEA process may need to be completed first, e.g., modifying tool 2 to reflect the state's framework

### Target Learners
- SEA cross-agency teams
- LEA district teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Aligning agencywide approaches to evidence-based intervention selection
