# Assign take-home copies of modelling videos for periodic review over two months

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends giving participants take-home copies of modelling videos to review at home over two months, which produced stronger and more sustained self-efficacy gains than workshop viewing alone. The authors state that "homework assignments focusing on contemplation and preparation can also further enhance self-efficacy," even when participants watch less often than recommended.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants receive take-home video copies and periodic review prompts
#### Constraints
- Average actual viewings were well below the recommended four; benefit may come from possession as a reminder rather than review itself

### Target Learners
- adults preparing for weight loss behaviour change

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained self-efficacy for weight management
