Strategies

Edit target FSs into extended paragraphs, including learners' own texts, as a low-time cued-output activity integrated into a writing course¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The strategy asks learners to edit target FSs into example paragraphs and paragraphs they wrote themselves, choosing items by function and appropriateness, with feedback via a model example. The article describes it as belonging "at the freer end of cued output" and requiring only about five minutes of class time, so it can run alongside other paragraph-editing activities without displacing syllabus objectives.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learners must first be familiarized with the target FSs (Stage 1 activities preceded editing in the study).
  • Target items must be visible during the lesson, e.g. written on the board after a gap-fill check.

Constraints¶

  • The article notes the constraints of the paragraphs "did not allow for entirely free use of the target items."
  • No fixed time was allotted; activities were carried out alongside other paragraph editing tasks.

Target Learners¶

  • First-year undergraduate EAP students at approximately CEFR B1

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Use of academic formulaic sequences in learners' own paragraph writing

Claims¶

  • Control-group use of in terms of reflected copying of a single instructor example rather than function-based selection [+W]
  • The control group showed no significant change in target FS use between midterm and final [+W]
  • Editing target FSs into paragraphs significantly increased treatment-group learners' use of those FSs between midterm and final papers [+W]
  • The treatment-group gain persists when the familiar item for example is excluded from the analysis [+W]
  • Before the editing stage, treatment and control groups did not differ significantly in target FS use [+W]
  • Stage 2 activities appeared to reinforce in addition to as a chunk and to shift use away from the less genre-appropriate thanks to [+W]
