# Move beyond explorative expert-novice comparisons to hypothesis-driven designs investigating application of evidence-based pedagogical principles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that research on professional vision shift from explorative expert-novice comparisons to controlled, hypothesis-driven designs examining how teachers learn to apply evidence-based pedagogical knowledge. It argues explorative comparisons are of limited value because teacher education programs offer sparse opportunities for deep learning of scientific pedagogical knowledge, so experienced teachers may not master principles novices should learn. The author also challenges the reacting-teacher conception, arguing careful lesson planning makes classroom complexity predictable and enables flexible reaction.
