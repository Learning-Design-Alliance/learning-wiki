# Let students choose their own writing topics to build confidence and increase communicative language ability.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To aid EFL learners to have freedom during the writing process, the teacher lets students choose their own writing topics. The article argues that "the more stude nts interest in their own topic the more communicat ive language ability increase, because they choose the topic that they have knowledge about." Students who struggle to select topics can brainstorm a list and choose one they are interested in and have enough knowledge about.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students have some knowledge of their chosen topic
#### Constraints
- Some students have difficulties selecting topics and need brainstorming support

### Target Learners
- EFL college students

### Target Learning Goals
- increased confidence in writing
- greater fluency in expressing ideas
