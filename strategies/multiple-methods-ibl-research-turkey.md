# Encourage Turkish science educators to conduct more IBL research using multiple research methods rather than a single paradigm

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on their finding that IBL research in Turkey is dominated by quantitative quasi-experimental studies, the authors recommend that "Turkish science educators to direct their interest more on to the IBL studies in Turke y with more focus on using multiple methods rather than relying on only one major r esearch paradigm." They also note the renewed science curriculum encourages IBL use and that IBL is needed for a scientifically literate future population, pointing to EU projects (PATHWAY, PRIMAS, SAILS) encouraging IBL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research attention to IBL beyond efficacy-focused intervention studies, including integration studies and qualitative and mixed-method designs
#### Constraints
- IBL is not yet widely used in Turkish educational studies, and no study has examined how IBL could be effectively integrated into teaching science

### Target Learners
- science educators and researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- inquiry-based science teaching
- scientific literacy
