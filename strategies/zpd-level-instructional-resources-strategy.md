# Report a zone of proximal development level with aligned instructional resources for foundational skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For students not ready to read passages, the assessment's Phonological Awareness and Phonics & Word Recognition domains each offer within-domain adaptivity so reported data point toward a ZPD level within a skill progression. "Each step in the Phonological Awareness and Phonics & Word Recognition progressions is mapped to best practice instructional materials made available by a variety of research-based institutions," allowing teachers to select instructional emphases matched to the reported level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Within-domain adaptive measurement to locate the student's ZPD level in each progression
#### Constraints
- Applies to students who cannot yet read passages; each skill assessed aligns to CCSS en español through grade 2

### Target Learners
- Pre-K to grade 3 students not yet ready for oral passage reading

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational decoding skills and language comprehension supporting future reading fluency
