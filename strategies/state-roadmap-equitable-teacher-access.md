# Five-step state roadmap for equitable access to experienced teachers: examine data, diagnose root causes, set goals, implement data-based policies, and monitor with transparency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (8 for, 1 against) · 6 studies (6 associational), `q2` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report proposes a five-step roadmap for state and district leaders: establish data systems and examine disparities; determine whether disparities are within- or between-district and engage teachers on root causes; set clear goals and interim measures; implement evidence-based policy solutions; and commit to ongoing data transparency and accountability. As the report states, "States should examine their data for patterns of disparities, as we did in this analysis." Recommended policies include principal pipelines, Grow Your Own and residency programs, differential pay, earlier hiring timelines, and mentoring for novice teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State commitment to collecting, disaggregating, and publicly reporting teacher equity data by race
- Meaningful stakeholder engagement to inform goal-setting
#### Constraints
- Whether disparities are greater within or between districts varies by state, so policy solutions must match the local pattern; within- versus between-district differences are just one potential cause among many, such as urban or rural location

### Target Learners
- Black students in P-12 schools
- students of color and students from low-income backgrounds in high-need schools

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable access to experienced and certified teachers
- reduced teacher turnover in high-need schools
- college readiness opportunities through advanced coursework access
