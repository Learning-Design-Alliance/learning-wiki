# Vocational education programs should let trainees experience and reflect on authentic errors, e.g. via error management training and negative knowledge-diaries

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 theoretical, 1 quant-synthesis), `q1`–`q4` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "vocational education programs should give trainees opportunities to experience authentic, complex and challenging errors, to learn from them and to thus build up negative knowledge". Error management training, in which errors are encouraged and consciously utilized as part of learning, is an interesting approach with shown beneficial effects especially for complex tasks. Practically, trainees could keep negative knowledge-diaries, whose "what not to do" statements could be compiled into a guidebook to catastrophe.
