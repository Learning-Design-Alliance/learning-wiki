# Monitoring Progress

## Description
Monitoring progress involves regularly assessing advancement towards goals. This includes evaluating whether you are on schedule, identifying any deviations from the action plan, and making necessary adjustments. Regular monitoring helps maintain focus and ensures timely corrective action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time for regular review and assessment. Access to tools for tracking progress, such as calendars, spreadsheets, or project management software.
#### Constraints
- Over-monitoring can lead to stress and micromanagement. Infrequent monitoring can result in missed opportunities for corrective action.
