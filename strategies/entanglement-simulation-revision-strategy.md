# Revise the Entanglement simulation to target the difficulties found: a coefficient-changing view, help texts on factorization and basis conversion, and a Challenges view

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the identified difficulties, the authors plan specific simulation revisions: a new view where students can "change the coefficients in an entangled state to explore the transition between maximal and non -maximal entanglement", help texts showing how to convert between a sum of terms and the factorized form for a product state and how to translate a state from the Z to the X basis, and a "Challenges" view with multiple challenges targeting the difficulties found. Future work will elicit underlying reasons for the difficulties and evaluate the revisions with pre- and post-tests and student interviews.

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate quantum physics students
- advanced undergraduate quantum mechanics students

### Target Learning Goals
- distinguishing maximal from non-maximal entanglement
- factorizing product states and converting between Z and X bases

## Claims

- [Many students incorrectly believe all entangled states are maximally entangled, showing perfect correlations or anticorrelations along all common measurement axes, and this persists after revised-simulation use](../claims/entanglement-maximally-entangled-misconception.md) [+W]
- [Students incorrectly state that product states are entangled because they cannot be factorized, due to difficulty converting a sum of two-particle terms into a product of single-particle states](../claims/factorization-product-state-misconception.md) [+W]
- [Some introductory students incorrectly state that particles in a product state must have definite spin values rather than being in a superposition of spin states](../claims/product-state-definite-spin-misconception.md) [+W]
- [Students incorrectly attribute properties of entangled states to product states, linked with difficulty translating a state from the Z to the X basis](../claims/product-state-entangled-properties-misconception.md) [+W]
