Encourage students to post comments on video feedback to complete the feedback loop and foster dialogue.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends encouraging students to post comments in response to generic video feedback, either as a basis for discussion in a following class or as a pre-requisite to a subsequent assignment. This completes the 'feedback loop' and provides what the authors call an important 'missing link' in the feedback dialogue process between staff and students. The approach aligns with evidence that online feedback augmented by further dialogue provides the greatest benefit to future learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students need encouragement or requirements to post comments, as they were not required to do so during the pilot.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • University students engaging with feedback

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Developing feedback as a dialogue between staff and students
  • Encouraging active student engagement with feedback

Claims¶

  • A structured feedback-and-reflection cycle (pre-lesson plan review, immediate verbal comments, written feedback, take-up time, and open-ended questioning) supported the mentee's reflective development [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Plan and structure video feedback around signposts and feed-forward points before recording.
