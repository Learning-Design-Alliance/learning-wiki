# Calm-Down Corner

## Description
Dedicated, positive space where learners practice emotional regulation skills. Stocked with tools like emotion charts, fidget toys, and social-emotional books to provide a safe place to recenter and refocus. It supports the ability to self-regulate and return to learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a dedicated space and a variety of emotional regulation tools. Facilitator guidance is needed to help learners use the space effectively.
#### Constraints
- May be stigmatizing if not implemented carefully. Requires clear expectations and guidelines for use.

### Personalization
