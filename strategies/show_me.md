# Show Me

## Description
Use slides or the board to provide another way for learners to get the information or directions. This provides reinforcement, but also allows time for students to catch up if they missed something in the spoken lecture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should be able to create slides or write clearly on the board to enhance comprehension and avoid cognitive overload.
#### Constraints
- Visuals need to be high quality. This also depends on whether students are self-aware enough to notice that they missed something in the spoken lecture.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance comprehension, improve retention, support learners who face auditory processing challenges

### Affordances
- Provides multiple exposures to key concepts through the permanent visual. Students can take their time to grasp information.

### Personalization
- Provide graphics that are as clear as possible and match the learning goals. Also allow students to provide input into the types of visuals that best support their learning.
