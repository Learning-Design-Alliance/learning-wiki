# Practitioners situate their MSL design problem in the dimensional space and consult related MSL systems' evidence to refine designs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends a concrete procedure for practitioners adopting or creating an MSL design: use the ten-dimension analysis to locate where the design problem's constraints and parameters lie, then examine related MSL systems. As printed: a practitioner "can use our analysis to situate the dimensional space where the constraints or parameters of his or her design problem lie, and look at relevant design and research-based evidence of other related MSL systems to refine her own design."
