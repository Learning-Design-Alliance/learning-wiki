# Design physics courses with UDL by examining the why, what, and how of teaching to find student barriers, then adding supports and options

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends instructors first "examine the why, what, and how of our teaching while looking for the barriers our students frequently encounter," asking "Why should students care about this topic?", "What do students find challenging about this topic?", and "How do students show their understanding of this topic?" They then identify or develop strategies to provide support and options. The article's example is an instructor examining her coverage of momentum by connecting it to students' interests, major discipline, and future careers, then adding explicit mentions of these connections in class to provide one option for motivation.
