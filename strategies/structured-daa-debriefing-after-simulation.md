# Use structured debriefing after simulation scenarios to consolidate transferable prevention actions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each simulation session in the program paired 40 minutes of scenario execution under time pressure and sterility constraints with 20 minutes of structured debriefing following a Description–Analysis–Application (DAA) framework: reviewing key events and decision points, examining why actions succeeded or failed with reference to checklists and guideline principles, and translating learning into specific actions for future OR practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators, standardized observer checklists, and a structured debriefing guide
#### Constraints
- Debriefing quality was not itself measured as a study outcome; fidelity measures were formative only

### Target Learners
- Operating room nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- Transfer of prevention skills to clinical practice
- Teamwork and communication
