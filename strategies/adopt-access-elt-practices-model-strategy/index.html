Adopt Access Program ELT practices as a model for English language programs in Pakistan¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that the ELT practices used in the Access Program be researched thoroughly and taken as a model: "Now the ELT practices used in Access Program should be researched thoroughly to take them as a model for all English Language Programs in Pakistan and for suggesting further improvement in the Access Program itself." The recommendation rests on their conclusion that Access practices are significantly better than public school practices as proven by their effect on learning outcomes. It is a forward-looking design recommendation, not a tested manipulation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Further research into Access ELT practices before adoption, as the authors state the practices should first be researched thoroughly

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is specific to English Language Programs in Pakistan and includes improving the Access Program itself

Target Learners¶

  • Socio-economically disadvantaged youth aged 13–20 enrolled in English language programs in Pakistan

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English linguistic competence and performance across listening, speaking, reading and writing

Claims¶

  • After one year, Access Program students significantly outperform public school students who had also qualified for the program on a diagnostic English test [+W]
  • Access and public school students showed no significant difference in English scores before treatment [+W]
  • Access Program students improved by about 24 points on the diagnostic test over one year [+W]
  • The complete sample showed a significant overall pre-to-post gain of about 16 points [+W]
  • Public school students improved by only about 8 points over the same year [+W]
