# Integrate staff training into the organisation's own schedule and LMS, simplify the program, and assess home readiness before implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the implementation failure, the article recommends embedding future training in existing organisational systems, simplifying it, and assessing readiness first. "Strategies such as having the IMPETUS-D training integrated into the organisation's training schedule and available on the organisation's learning management system, or implementing a more simplified version such as targeting only nurses or implementing it in a stepped approach may have helped overcome some of these barriers and increased uptake." It also suggests future work should assess individual care homes' readiness to implement with input from managers, staff, residents and families, and target homes ready to implement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of care homes' readiness to implement before deployment
- Coordination with the organisation's existing training schedule and LMS
#### Constraints
- The article notes many challenges have been reported in other nursing home-based studies and strategies to address common barriers need further evaluation

### Target Learners
- Nurses working in nursing homes
- Personal care workers in nursing homes

### Target Learning Goals
- Dementia-specific palliative and end-of-life care knowledge and skills
