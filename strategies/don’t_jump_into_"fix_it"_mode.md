# Don’t Jump Into "Fix It" Mode

## Description
Resist the urge to immediately find solutions. Sometimes, it’s more useful to just listen and understand what’s wrong. Students might not even want you to fix the problem. After you understand the problem, you can talk with the student about possible solutions and what support they may (or may not) want from you.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Patience, active listening skills, ability to offer guidance without dictating solutions
#### Constraints
- Requires resisting natural problem-solving instincts, may be challenging in time-constrained situations
