# Three integrated strategies pairing public health with clinical teaching: faculty partnerships, digital prompts, and data aggregation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes three strategies integrating public health perspectives with clinical teaching practice: "pairing clinical preceptors with public health professionals in structured teaching partnerships"; embedding health literacy decision-support prompts within the electronic Mini-CEX platform, such as a reminder window prompting teach-back verification; and incorporating de-identified, aggregated OHL-Mini-CEX data into departmental annual OHL reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured evaluator training emphasizing teach-back, de-jargonization, and empowering guidance domains
- Phased implementation supported by digital assistance tools and pilot programs
#### Constraints
- Digital prompts are a compensation while faculty competence is still being cultivated

### Target Learners
- clinical preceptors
- pediatric residents

### Target Learning Goals
- OHL-oriented assessment competency
- standardized teach-back verification
- institutional health equity accountability
