# Guide medical students to use ChatGPT cautiously with cross-checking, disclosure, and teacher oversight

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's future-perspectives and discussion sections recommend careful, supervised integration of ChatGPT into medical education. Citing a systematic scoping review, it advises that medical students use ChatGPT cautiously, "cross-checking information with reliable sources and disclosing AI-generated content in their work". Teachers are positioned to "guide students on the effective and ethical use of ChatGPT, assess its reliability, and explore mixed assessment methods" while considering the impact on traditional assignments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher oversight during implementation
- Cross-checking of AI-generated information against reliable sources
- Disclosure of AI-generated content in student work
#### Constraints
- Known drawbacks include the potential for fabricated, incorrect, or biased information
- Limited training periods and the possibility of different answers to the same question depending on phrasing
- Good performance on a specific test may not indicate abilities for general and reliable medical education usage

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Medical educators and teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective and ethical use of AI tools in medical study and assessment
