# Use autonomy-supportive intervention behaviors in physical education to foster a self-determined motivational climate

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article lists concrete teacher behaviors drawn from intervention programs designed to enhance intrinsic motivation and build a supportive motivational climate in physical education. These include "using informal language that is flexible and not controlling; allowing independent and critical thought" and "rational discussion with students about the value of respecting the thoughts, feelings, and behaviors of others", as well as providing interesting learning opportunities, allowing independent work time, and permitting student initiative. Through such behaviors teachers can foster an autonomy-supportive climate and facilitate a more self-determined motivational approach in students.
