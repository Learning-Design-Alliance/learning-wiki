# Offer additional debriefing and practice opportunities to students who find simulation-based training stressful

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that for students who experience simulation training as stressful, "educators might consider offering additional debriefing and practice opportunities to help them benefit from SBT". This responds to the survey finding that 0.66% of participants indicated the training undermined their confidence and increased tension and fear in facing clinical problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal debriefing sessions at the end of each scenario
- Instructor feedback and access to the simulator for repeated practice
#### Constraints
- Applies to the minority of students who report reduced confidence, increased tension or fear after simulation, as identified through course evaluation

### Target Learners
- medical students who find simulation stressful

### Target Learning Goals
- confidence in facing clinical problems
- benefiting from simulation-based training
