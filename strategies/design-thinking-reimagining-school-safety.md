# Use a design thinking process with Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test stages to reimagine school safety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends design thinking as a human-centered, recursive framework for transforming complex adaptive systems: "A recursive process, design thinking has several distinct stages: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test." Teams spend considerable time in the Empathize problem space gathering information from community members before defining the problem, then generate many possible strategies before prototyping and testing. The process draws on the National Equity Project's Liberatory Design principles by centering equity and shifting who holds power and agency in the design process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assemble a team including individuals with different areas of expertise representing various groups, with varying degrees of positional and social power.
#### Constraints
- The guide notes the process is not linear and teams need to move between stages more than once, and that there will be unintended consequences to any solution or change effort.
