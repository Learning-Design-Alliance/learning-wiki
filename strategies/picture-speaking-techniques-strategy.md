# Use structured picture-based speaking techniques such as picture dictation, spot the difference, and picture sequencing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article details specific pictorial-aid techniques for oral practice, including picture dictation, in which a student "describe a picture that a student cannot see" and draws it; spot the difference with partner pictures; and sequencing pictures to tell a story in the correct order. Each technique was implemented in one of the six treatment sessions and paired with pronunciation and vocabulary support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need suitable picture sets and should experiment with and test resources before classroom use
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that using too much picture material at one time can cause boredom and frustration when technology does not meet learners' cognitive level

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners in private language institutes

### Target Learning Goals
- speaking fluency
- describing, comparing and narrating in English
