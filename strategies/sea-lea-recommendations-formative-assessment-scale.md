# Recommendations for state education agencies and districts implementing formative assessment at scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief closes with recommendations for SEAs — build on existing relationships with LEAs to identify systems ready for change; identify and extend partnerships yielding thought partners and content expertise; develop a flexible implementation plan meeting local contexts, planning for scale at the outset; and leverage discretionary grant opportunities for LEA implementation resources. For LEAs: emphasize formative assessment is not "another" initiative; provide constant and consistent messaging about its relationship to improved teaching and learning; engage in deep learning at all levels with collaboration, peer feedback, and self-reflection; and focus on building coherence within the district.
