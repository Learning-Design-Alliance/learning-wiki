# Include unpublished studies and feasibility data when reviewing school mindfulness research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future syntheses of school-based mindfulness research locate both published and unpublished studies (e.g., doctoral theses), since these "often contain supplementary information that could be valuable and could introduce new approaches to this specific research field." It also recommends systematically assessing feasibility—acceptability to teachers and students and implementation within daily school routine—when new interventions are introduced, as a source for improvement and adaptation.
