# Four suggested classroom applications of Astrobites: reading assignments, written summaries, in-class presentations, and a website discussion forum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose four novel curricular applications of Astrobites to supplement existing classroom examples. Reading assignments use the site as pre-lecture material to increase immersion and reinforce lecture concepts; written summaries ask students to write Astrobites-modeled summaries of research papers; in-class presentations ask students to describe a paper to peers, with classmates reading the site's summary beforehand; and a discussion forum uses the site's commenting feature so students can formulate responses after background research, potentially drawing in Astrobites authors and researchers worldwide.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need access to the Astrobites archive of brief, informative, and accessible summaries of recent astrophysics research to select papers and topics of interest
- Participation in reading assignments could be evaluated through in-class discussions or by asking students to formulate follow-up questions based on the reading
