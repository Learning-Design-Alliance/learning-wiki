# Give the face-to-face feedback a more prominent role in order and emphasis when delivering the doktormitSDM module

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants' improvement recommendations consistently concerned the face-to-face feedback component. Following Likert ratings and free-text comments, "the face‐to‐face feedback was considered most supportive," and recommendations "quite consistently suggested a more prominent role for face‐to‐face feedback with regard to both order and emphasis of the components." The revised module accordingly reorders and reweights components, with one summarizing comment suggesting to "present the manual last and begin with the feedback."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained communication analyst able to deliver MAPPIN'SDM-based individual feedback on the trainee's own recorded consultation
#### Constraints
- Recommendations come from a small pretest sample of ten doctors and await testing in the ongoing randomized controlled trial

### Target Learners
- specialist doctors in clinical practice

### Target Learning Goals
- acquiring shared decision-making communication skills
