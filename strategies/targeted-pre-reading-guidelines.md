# Three guidelines to encourage completion of targeted pre-class reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors report "We have found three guidelines helpful to encourage and facilitate completion of this reading." Reading is targeted to the upcoming class ("For a 90-minute class we assign at most 12-15 pages of reading; the average is 8-9 pages."), online quizzes give a direct incentive ("these qu izzes counted for 7.5% of a students total mark"), and students are reminded of the importance of reading throughout the term.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online reading quizzes are due a few hours before class begins
- Pre-reading must be targeted and specific to only the upcoming class rather than generic assignments
#### Constraints
- Quizzes consist of one or two questions designed to take only a few minutes if the reading was completed

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- completion of targeted pre-class reading
- preparation for in-class activities

- Deliberate Practice Framework Upper Division
