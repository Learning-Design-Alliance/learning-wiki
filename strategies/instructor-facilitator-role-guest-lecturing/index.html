Instructor acts as facilitator in student-centered guest lecturing: select speakers, set expectations, monitor, but avoid dominating each stage¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article assigns the instructor a critical but bounded role in the student-centered approach. The instructor identifies appropriate guest speakers, sets clear expectations of students on each step, and closely monitors students' behavior and activities, yet should "avoid getting into a dominant role in each stage except selection of subject matter experts". The stated aim is that the instructor act as a facilitator creating an environment where students freely build deep understanding through close involvement in the guest lecturing process.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor capability to identify appropriate guest speakers and set clear expectations for each step of the approach

Constraints¶

  • Dominance is limited to speaker selection; the instructor steps back in other stages to preserve student ownership
