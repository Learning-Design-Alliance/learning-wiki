# Open-label placebo administration protocol: honest disclosure plus standardized four-point rationale before high-stakes assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's procedure gives students placebos without deception using four standardized statements: placebos are inactive substances with no medications but placebo effects may still be powerful; the body may automatically respond, like Pavlov's dogs; a positive attitude may help but is not necessary; and participants taking placebos must take them faithfully. Pills were labeled openly and taken twice daily for 14 days before an exam, with contact time matched to controls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participant-provider interaction and contact time held similar across groups
- research assistant blind to assignments
- written informed consent and ethical approval
#### Constraints
- Pilot study with small sample; authors call for replication with a bigger sample, a covered placebo condition, and informing participants after baseline assessment

### Target Learners
- university students preparing for exams

### Target Learning Goals
- test anxiety reduction
- self-management skills
