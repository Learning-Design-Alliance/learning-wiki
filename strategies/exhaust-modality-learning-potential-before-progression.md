# Exhaust the learning potential of one training modality before progressing to other modalities or supervised surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that "the learning potential of one modality should be exhausted before learners progress to further training using other modalities or supervised surgery," based on their finding that simply adding training procedures improved skills in both groups. Repeated practice on 3D-printed models produced significant score gains from the first to the second procedure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate time per training procedure (the authors used 2½ hours for two procedures, noting 90 min vs. 60 min per procedure may explain differences from prior studies)
#### Constraints
- The authors note additional practice beyond two procedures might have affected results

### Target Learners
- novice surgical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition of basic surgical drilling skills
