# Use the validated scale to identify communication training needs, design targeted workshops, and evaluate communication interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend deploying the validated scale as a practical tool for communication skill development in clinical palliative care. In their words, "Educators and supervisors can use the scale to identify training needs, design targeted workshops, and evaluate the effectiveness of communication interventions." Administrators may also incorporate it into professional development or quality improvement initiatives, and it supports structured self-assessment and reflection by healthcare providers in emotionally charged interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The scale must be administered to healthcare staff working in palliative or oncology care contexts
#### Constraints
- The authors note that applying the tool in community or comprehensive hospitals may require content adjustments due to differences in infrastructure and human resources

### Target Learners
- Healthcare staff in oncology palliative care, including doctors and nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- Palliative care communication skills, empathy, and emotional regulation
