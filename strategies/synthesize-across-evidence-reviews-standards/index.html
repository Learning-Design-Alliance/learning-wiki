Consult multiple evidence reviews and attend to their differing standards when synthesizing evidence for decisions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because reviews differ in how they assess elements such as attrition and in how they conceptualize effectiveness, decision makers synthesizing evidence across multiple program evaluations should attend to each review's goals and standards. The article notes "it can be difficult for decision makers to synthesize the body of evidence when faced with results from multiple program evaluations," making awareness of cross-review differences a practical step.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to the goals and standards documents of the reviews being synthesized.

Constraints¶

  • Differences in effectiveness conceptualization across reviews limit straightforward synthesis.
