# Select diverse, word-internal-variable example words to illustrate GPCs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that phonics instructors "illustrate GPCs with a sufficiently large number of example words as well as examples with high word-internal variability," such as practicing the "e" – /ε/ mapping with bed, get, and hen, where the critical vowel is surrounded by varied consonants. Additional strategies include illustrating the same GPC in different word positions and selecting contrastive example word pairs. Future work should use orthographic distinctness measures and weigh learning benefits against instructional time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection must balance diversity benefits against the instructional time needed to practice with examples
#### Constraints
- The validity of contrastive example-word pairs remains to be assessed relative to potential benefits of interleaved learning
