# Build face-to-face relationships between blog authors and introductory students early, assess student needs before launch, and include hands-on blog features

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
From the pilot assessment the authors recommend: developing face-to-face relationships between blog authors and participants near the start of implementation (through class visits, social events, lab help, office hours, or tutoring), because they "noted a correlation between increased student participation and established face-to-face relationships." They also call assessing students' needs vital for setting the blog's focus, and suggest hands-on features such as computer simulations or at-home demo instructions, mirroring the hands-on character students praised in labs and problem sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Blog authors able to interact in person with introductory students (visits, labs, office hours, or tutoring)
- Pre-instructional needs assessment such as the CLASS, periodic surveys, and an initial query of student wishes
#### Constraints
- Impact of hands-on features on motivation was not formally assessed in this pilot; upper-level majors' own learning benefits also warrant formal assessment

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students (Aviation majors)

### Target Learning Goals
- student motivation
- sense of relevance and confidence in physics

## Claims

- [Student blog comments progressed from surface-level to substantive over the semester, and commenting frequency tracked established face-to-face relationships with authors](../claims/blog-comments-surface-to-substantive-face-to-face-effect.md) [+W]
- [Post-instruction interviews confirmed students read the blog more than they commented, citing time constraints, and reported interest rose most when material applied to aviation](../claims/interviews-read-more-than-commented-aviation-relevance.md) [+W]
- [Periodic in-class surveys showed improved self-confidence in all identified skills, with lab and class activities cited far more often than the blog](../claims/in-class-surveys-confidence-improved-lab-and-activities-cited.md) [~W]
