# Provide transparent, inspectable evaluation trails in AI-teaching benchmarks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participant feedback indicated that primary engineering frictions came from multimodal synchronization under strict latency constraints and ambiguous LLM-judge feedback. The authors argue these self-reported concerns about evaluator noise align with the near-zero judge–human correlation, and that "this highlights the critical need for transparent, in- spectable evaluation trails in future benchmarks."
