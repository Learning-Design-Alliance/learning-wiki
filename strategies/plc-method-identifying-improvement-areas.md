# Incorporate a method for identifying areas of improvement into PLC models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that "professional learning community models could be enhanced if these models incorporate a method for identifying areas of improvement," since PLCs are means for school improvement rather than goals in themselves. This addresses their observation that the assumption that schools need improvement is not linked to a method for identifying what should improve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PLC implementation must link the assumption of needed improvement to an explicit identification method
#### Constraints
- The paper offers this as a conceptual recommendation, not a tested procedure

### Target Learners
- teachers and administrators in school-based PLCs

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying and improving professional practice
