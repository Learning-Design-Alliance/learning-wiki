Use a VFT as the field-study alternative when real field trips are blocked by cost, logistics, safety or permission barriers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that when teachers cannot take students on a real field trip, they plan Biology lessons around a VFT instead: "the in tegration of technology, through a VFT enables all students to explore the places virtually , without the need to consider the issues of cost and other logistical aspects that a real field trip poses". The strategy addresses barriers the article enumerates from Nafishah (2007): planning workload, accommodation and food arrangements, education-department permission, parental permission, student safety, cost, and limited attendance. The conclusion urges educators to find such teaching alternatives and for responsible parties to build teacher and student preparedness for ICT-integrated approaches.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A VFT module equipped with a photo gallery, video and clear explanation so students can explore and achieve the desired objectives
  • Teacher and student preparedness to use ICT-integrated teaching and learning approaches

Constraints¶

  • Recommended for topics where field study is the suggested curriculum activity and a real trip is impractical; the article does not claim VFT replaces the real experience in every context

Target Learners¶

  • Secondary Biology students whose teachers cannot conduct a real field trip

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Achieving field-study learning objectives, such as understanding the mangrove swamp ecosystem, without travelling to the location

Claims¶

  • The VFT group outperformed the conventional group on all three item types, while the conventional group improved only on objective items [+W]
  • A significant Group x Time interaction indicates the VFT group's overall achievement improved more from pre-test to post-test than the conventional group's [+W]
  • Students taught with a VFT module show significantly higher overall achievement gains than students taught with conventional methods for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on the essay item for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on objective items for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on structured items for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
