# Invest in ongoing educator professional development and an assessment community of practice for POG success

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief states the success of a POG will likely hinge on the ongoing professional growth of educators tasked with facilitating POG competency learning, and that investing in ongoing PD is crucial with dedicated funding allocated for educators and leaders. PD should emphasize core POG competencies and offer practical strategies for integrating these skills into teaching. Establishing an assessment community of practice through regular meetings, online collaboration tools, and shared repositories could drive assessment innovation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated funding should be allocated to support educators and leaders; the community of practice relies on regular meetings, online collaboration tools, and shared repositories of resources.
