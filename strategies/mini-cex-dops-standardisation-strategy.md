# Standardise scoring and use cross-evaluation to reduce mini-CEX/DOPS subjectivity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To address inconsistent standards, the authors recommend uniform instructions: specialists develop grading standards and demonstration videos, educators watch and simulate scoring, experts guide item evaluation, and scoring standards are marked on the forms. They also "suggest a 'cross' evaluation in which different educators evaluate one student to reduce subjectivity".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert-developed grading standards, demonstration videos and assessor training
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of a single Chinese tertiary hospital with hierarchical feedback culture

### Target Learners
- clinical nursing educators

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent, accurate formative scoring
