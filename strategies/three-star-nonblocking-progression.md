# Use a three-star efficiency rating that rewards skillful play without blocking progression

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Rate levels with up to three stars for efficient play, but never gate progression on the star count. The article states: "By providing a system that rewards efficient play but does not block progressing to the next level if a player is doing poorly, we avoid discouraging players that simply aren't as skilled." Earning three stars requires avoiding wasted moves and unnecessary swaps, which encourages thoughtful action and replay of levels.
