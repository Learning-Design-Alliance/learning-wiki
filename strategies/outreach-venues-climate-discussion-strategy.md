# Incorporate climate change discussion into existing astronomy public outreach venues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article notes that astronomers already lead public outreach through planetaria (reaching over 146 million people worldwide), public talks, national parks, and libraries, and recommends using these existing entry points to add climate change discussion. It highlights freely downloadable NASA Data Visualization Studio videos and the film Dynamic Earth, now screening at over 175 planetariums, as ready-made climate-relevant visuals for planetarium domes.
