Use the construct map for progress monitoring of CBPL and HQIM implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide positions the construct map as a practical tool for measurement and progress monitoring: instructional leaders can diagnose system strengths and gaps, plan targeted supports, and run continuous improvement cycles using the map's dimensions and sub-constructs. Dimension 1, for example, calls for leaders who "Provides opportunities to diagnose system strengths and gaps, and plan targeted supports for CBPL and HQIM implementation" and who "engage in data-driven continuous improvement cycles". The strategy is to score sub-constructs over time and act on the results.
