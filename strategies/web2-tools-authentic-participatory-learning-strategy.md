# Use Web 2.0 tools (wikis, blogs, social networks, sharing services) to provide authentic, participatory learning experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends drawing on Web 2.0, or social software, in education: wikis, blogs and microblogs, social networks, social bookmarking, news feeds, photo/audio/video sharing and slide-sharing services. It argues that "Web 2.0 provides authentic learning experiences for students and encourages global awareness, creativity, innovation, critical thinking, active participation and collaboration", and that through such tools teachers experience and apply new pedagogical models centered on learners rather than teaching.
