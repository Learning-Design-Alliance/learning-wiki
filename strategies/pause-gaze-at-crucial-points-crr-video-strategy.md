# Pause the eye gaze at crucial points of CRR videos (holding the focus spot while playing the audio explanation) instead of showing a continuous loop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Student feedback on the EYE-ECG video suggested a concrete modification: several students proposed "pausing eye gaze (as opposed to pausing the entire video track) at crucial points instead of showing a continuous loop", i.e. holding the focus spot in place while the corresponding auditory explanation plays. This would give learners more time to retrace and comprehend the visual information being referred to. The authors call this an exciting strategy to be investigated as the next step in a follow-up study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a video player or production format that can hold the gaze focus spot stationary while the audio commentary continues
#### Constraints
- Proposed by students and endorsed by the authors as a follow-up modification; it was not tested in the present study

### Target Learners
- medical students learning ECG interpretation from self-paced digital videos

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of expert visual search patterns during ECG interpretation
