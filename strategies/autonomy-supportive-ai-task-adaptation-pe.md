# Use AI to support autonomy-supportive task adaptation in physical education rather than algorithmic exercise prescription

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that AI help practitioners adapt "intensity, task complexity, group size, feedback frequency, and recovery options without turning exercise into algorithmic prescription or coercive monitoring". This implements the self-determination and ecological-dynamics reasoning that sustained engagement depends on autonomy, competence, and relatedness, and that exercise should remain a psychologically safe, motivationally sustainable context for practicing regulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Human judgment and student choice remain primary
- Feedback should scaffold self-regulation rather than replace student judgment
#### Constraints
- Recommendations should not become coercive exercise prescriptions

### Target Learners
- university students in physical education classes and campus sport

### Target Learning Goals
- emotion-regulation practice through embodied activity
- motivationally sustainable participation

### Affordances
- Human Technology Environment Framework Emotion Regulation
