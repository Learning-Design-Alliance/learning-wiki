# Use mixed methods (HLM plus exploratory case study) to characterize factors behind digital skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article pairs a quantitative hierarchical linear model with a qualitative exploratory case study so that broad factors explaining test performance can be interpreted alongside in-depth accounts of student behaviour. The authors state "A mixed methodology was used to explore these factors and provide a more detailed analysis," with the case study providing "a more detailed analysis" of high and low performers' technology use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized skills measure for the statistical model, and access to subsamples of high- and low-performing students for qualitative analysis.
#### Constraints
- The authors note the findings "should be further investigated" before being used to design school activities.

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students (10th grade)

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying and characterizing factors that develop students' digital skills
