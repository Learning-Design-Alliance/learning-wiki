# Use clearly defined, feature-backed explanations with tutor final control when presenting forecasting signals in goal-setting dashboards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's case study points to a deployment recipe for predictive signals in tutoring: show recommendations alongside explanations that cite interpretable features such as ability, difficulty, and consistency, keep definitions and data sources transparent, and let tutors retain final control of the goal value. Tutors "generally prefer to take final control of the specific goal value" yet were "most influenced by the third explanation variant that provided additional feature-backed evidence." The article recommends testing explicit forecast-to-recommendation mappings as future work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explanations must reference clearly defined features whose data source (weeks of personalized student data) is transparent to the tutor
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that accurate week-ahead predictions might not determine appropriate goals; using a predicted declining value as a goal recommendation may not be optimal for student motivation and requires further scrutiny

### Target Learners
- college tutors and K-12 students in online math tutoring

### Target Learning Goals
- calibrated weekly practice goal setting
- tutor-supported effort regulation
