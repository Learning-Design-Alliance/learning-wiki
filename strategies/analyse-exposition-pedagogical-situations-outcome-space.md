## Description
The article identifies three pedagogical situations paralleling the interview discussions: 'The senior-junior research discussion, as in supervision of new research students or assistants', 'The oral examination of senior undergraduates by their teachers or tutors', and 'Exposition and discussion in lectures and seminars'. In each, one party speaks of an object of knowledge for the benefit or critical consideration of the other, and the forms of exposition offer different affordances for the listener's constitution of a knowledge object.
