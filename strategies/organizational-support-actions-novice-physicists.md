# Organizational actions to help physics novices navigate careers: ubiquitous support, untold rules, values-aligned opportunities, and data tracking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enumerates concrete actions organizations can take to help novices navigate their careers. These include making "resources and support services as ubiquitous as possible", helping novices "understand and navigate the actual landscape of a research career by telling the untold rules, including information about non-academic careers", engaging in organization-wide learning about in situ experiences of BIPOC people, focusing on opportunities aligned with novices' values and interests, tracking and visualizing key data, and confronting oppression directly since neutral policies are insufficient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations must commit to organization-wide learning about BIPOC people's experiences and to tracking, frequently reviewing, and visualizing key data
#### Constraints
- The article states neutral policies are insufficient and that outward-facing DEI without internal change amounts to organizational gaslighting (NASEM, 2021)

### Target Learners
- senior graduate students
- postdoctoral scholars

### Target Learning Goals
- career progression and retention in physics despite structural barriers
