# Sequenced skillfulness assessment and ethics sign-off across the training pathway

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a linear sequencing strategy: learners complete the full Skillfulness Assessment at programme entry to establish a developmental baseline, course-relevant components are embedded into assignments, rubrics, deliberate-practice activities and peer/community of practice feedback throughout theory courses, and the Code of Ethics is introduced in the second theory course. At the end of the final theory course, learners repeat the full Skillfulness Assessment and either sign the Code or propose revisions needed to enable alignment as a readiness checkpoint before practicum.
