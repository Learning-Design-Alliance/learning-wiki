# Use reinforcement strategies including interdisciplinary priority verbalization, bedside tools, and integrated sepsis alert technologies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To overcome knowledge deficits and cognitive overload, the article proposes teams use reinforcement strategies leveraging interdisciplinary team facilitators and advanced technological solutions. These include team-based training via interdisciplinary simulation, teaching on daily rounds, verbalizing priority sepsis interventions, interdisciplinary bedside tools such as checklists and pocket cards, and integrated technologies including sepsis alerts and electronic medical record chart reminders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires engagement of the interdisciplinary team and, for technology-based alerts, integration with early warning systems or the electronic medical record
#### Constraints
- Screening tools integrated within early warning systems may have limited awareness and utilization because these systems are disabled within the ICU to reduce alarm fatigue

### Target Learners
- ICU registered nurses
- Interdisciplinary critical care teams

### Target Learning Goals
- Sepsis identification
- Prioritization and implementation of priority sepsis interventions
