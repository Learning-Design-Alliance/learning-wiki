# Proposed research agenda: wider survey, teacher-training follow-up experiment, and advanced-course comparison

## Description
The article closes by proposing three research lines: a survey with a wider sample chosen by variables such as initial teacher training, degree, experience and school socioeconomic context; a follow-up study after the introductory course comparing two groups taught by a trained teacher using ICT versus an untrained teacher without ICT, measuring results, acceptance, motivation, satisfaction and perceived learning; and, if significant gains appear, an advanced course and a comparison between teachers with basic versus advanced mastery. The author writes that "pr´ acticamente cualquier iniciativa formativa ser´ a bien acogida por la comunidad educativa".
