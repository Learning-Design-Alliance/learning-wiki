# Run structured debrief sessions after each lesson module during teacher camps so teachers can reflect, adapt lessons, and surface needed adjustments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During the teacher camp, debrief sessions were intentionally placed throughout the week after each lesson module so teachers could discuss their perspectives with each other and with the instructors. Debriefs were structured so teachers shared key concepts learned, how they would modify lesson modules to meet their students' needs, and lingering questions; at week's end, teachers also shared additional resources and strategies to support student success. The authors report that the debrief sessions "not only enabled the teachers to reﬂect on their learning of the material, but also helped us identify adjustments for future iterations of the lessons," serving both teacher learning and curriculum improvement.
