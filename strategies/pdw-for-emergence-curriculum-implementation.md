# Provide professional development workshops for teachers implementing an emergence theory-based curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends Professional Development Workshops as the vehicle for training teachers in the emergence theory-based curriculum. PDWs "facilitate the use of new instructional practices and allow teachers to develop and construct knowledge," and should train teachers in lesson-plan implementation, deepen their understanding of ASD, and facilitate colleague collaboration. Teachers in the study requested additional in-services and time to develop lesson plans within the group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All teaching staff exposed to the curriculum through a PD in-service session, augmented with emergent-based activities and games
#### Constraints
- The study's teacher feedback did not differentiate between treatment and control groups, so no between-group conclusions could be drawn from the meeting notes

### Target Learners
- teachers of middle school students with ASD

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing emergence theory-based lesson plans
- understanding ASD cognitive deficits
- collaborative lesson planning
