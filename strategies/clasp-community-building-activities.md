## Description
The article identifies community building as a potentially explanatory CLASP feature: "At Next University there is specific attention played to community building with activities throughout the semester that promote learning more about their classmates." The authors note this aligns with research on self-efficacy, identity, and belonging factors for persistence in STEM. Because CLASP students actively engage in model construction and application, they have more opportunities to talk about science and use scientific tools in a social environment with instructors and peers.
