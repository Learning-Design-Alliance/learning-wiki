## Description
The review's forward-looking pedagogical recommendation is to integrate AI-powered feedback while balancing it with human feedback, so that immediate, customized automated feedback is combined with teacher input to optimize writing skill. The article "highlights the pedagogical implications of Integrating AI-powered feedback" and "suggests directions for future research, particularly in balancing human and automated feedback to optimize writing skill."
