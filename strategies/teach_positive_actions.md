# Teach Positive Actions

## Description
Education on positive actions for a healthy body (nutrition, exercise, and sleep), intellect (thinking, decision-making, problem-solving), self-management (managing time, energy, emotions), getting along with others (treating others fairly, kindly, respectfully), being honest (taking responsibility, not blaming others), and continual self-improvement (setting and achieving goals).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum or resources that teach positive actions, classroom activities to practice positive actions, opportunities for students to set and achieve goals
#### Constraints
- May require specific training or resources, students may need support in developing self-management skills.
