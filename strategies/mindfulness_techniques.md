# Mindfulness Techniques

## Description
Incorporate mindfulness techniques to help students manage their initial emotional impulses and reactivity. These techniques involve focusing on the present moment with awareness and acceptance. Examples include deep breathing exercises, body scan meditations, and mindful observation of thoughts and feelings without judgment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a quiet space, willingness to practice mindfulness, guidance from a trained instructor or audio resources.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find mindfulness techniques challenging or uncomfortable at first. Requires regular practice to be effective.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners who are highly reactive to stimuli or have difficulty managing their emotions.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Manage emotional impulses, reduce reactivity, increase self-awareness, promote relaxation and focus.

### Affordances
- Provides a tool for managing immediate emotional responses, promotes self-awareness and acceptance, reduces stress and anxiety.

### Personalization
- Mindfulness techniques can be adapted to individual learner preferences and needs. Some learners may prefer guided meditations, while others may benefit from simple breathing exercises.
