# Return graded post-class assignments with extensive narrative feedback to build foundational knowledge and critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Post-class formative and summative work—case studies with patient care plan documentation, research question formulation, recorded physical examinations, critical appraisals—is graded and returned with detailed comments. The article states each assignment is "graded and returned to the student with extensive narrative feedback, which allows the student to improve foundational knowledge and critical thinking skills."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments emphasizing analysis, synthesis, and evaluation of student performance
- Faculty capacity to provide extensive narrative feedback on each graded assignment
#### Constraints
- All post-class activities in the described curriculum are graded

### Target Learners
- online graduate health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- analysis and synthesis in applied nutrition tasks
