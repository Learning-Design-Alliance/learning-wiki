# Collect qualitative data on learners' experiment-design decisions and actions to understand the processes underlying scaffold effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In their future-directions discussion the authors recommend that "Future studies should also include qualitative data regarding learner decisions to understand their rationales behind their experiment designs and qualitative data about learner actions in designing experiments." Such data would, in their words, provide "richer insights in the processes underlying the results." They also raise investigating more sophisticated scaffolding models that remain one step ahead of the advancing learner.
