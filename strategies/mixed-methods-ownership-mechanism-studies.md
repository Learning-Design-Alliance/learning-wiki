# Combine repeated quantitative survey administration with qualitative interviews and project artifacts to identify mechanisms supporting project ownership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking research strategy: continue implementing E-CLASS and POS in the same lab courses each spring to increase sample size and sensitivity to inter-course differences and confounders, while analyzing post-project interviews with students and instructors and collected project artifacts, including weekly free-response surveys. The stated aim is identifying mechanisms coupling views about experimentation with ownership, described as "an important step toward developing effective pedagogies for physics lab courses that prioritize students' sense of project ownership as a learning outcome."

## Design Implications

#### Constraints
- Current analyses were not yet sensitive to inter-course differences or potential confounders such as prior project experience
