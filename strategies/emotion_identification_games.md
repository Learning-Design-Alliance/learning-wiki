# Emotion Identification Games

## Description
These activities involve various games and exercises to help children recognize and understand different emotions. Examples include making emotion faces and having children guess the feeling, using puppets to act out situations and identify emotions, playing an emotion walking game, and singing an emotion hello song. Such games make learning about emotions interactive and engaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Props such as puppets, emotion cards, or art supplies; space for movement and interaction; facilitator to guide activities and provide feedback
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be shy or uncomfortable expressing emotions; games may be oversimplified for some children; effectiveness depends on facilitator's skill
