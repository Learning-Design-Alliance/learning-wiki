# Map It Out!

## Description
Before a trip, children map the route, calculate mileage, estimate travel time, and track progress along the way. This promotes map reading, measurement, and problem-solving skills, enhancing their understanding of distance, time, and direction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Map, ruler, and calculator.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to a map and reliable information about distances and routes. May require assistance with calculations for younger learners.
