# Optimizing School Facilities

## Description
Improving the learning environment by addressing factors like acoustics/noise, air quality, lighting, temperature, and space. These factors impact teacher and student outcomes, including health, behavior, engagement, learning, and achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires financial investment, planning, and coordination to implement facility improvements.
#### Constraints
- Improvements may be costly and time-consuming. May require advocacy to prioritize facility improvements in budget allocation.

### Affordances
- Creates a more comfortable and conducive learning environment. Reduces stress, absenteeism, and aggression, while increasing engagement and productivity.

### Personalization
- Facility improvements can be tailored to specific needs, such as adjusting temperature controls, improving lighting, or reconfiguring classroom space.
