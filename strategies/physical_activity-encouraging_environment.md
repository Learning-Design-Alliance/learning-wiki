# Physical Activity-Encouraging Environment

## Description
Design school environments that encourage physical activity by including playground equipment, walking trails, and bike racks. Classrooms can be arranged to promote movement between activities. Scheduling classes in different rooms encourages students to walk between them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schoolyard space, equipment, infrastructure, scheduling flexibility
#### Constraints
- Requires financial investment. May need to address safety and accessibility concerns.
