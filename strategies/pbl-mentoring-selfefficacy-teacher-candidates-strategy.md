# Foster deep learning approaches in teacher education through problem-based learning, peer mentoring, and self-efficacy training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that deep approaches and strong support profiles accompany better TQT-PE outcomes, the authors propose concrete curricular methods. They recommend that "integrating problem-based learning into curricula could foster deep learning approaches", for example having candidates design inclusive physical activity programmes. They also suggest that "Establishing structured peer mentoring systems could also enhance academic self-efficacy and peer support", with senior students or alumni mentoring juniors, plus reflective practices, case studies, and self-efficacy training built on mastery experiences, peer observation, constructive feedback, and stress-management techniques.
