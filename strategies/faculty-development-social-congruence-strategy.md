# Equip faculty and peer tutors with tools to foster social congruence with and between students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends extending PAL insights beyond peer-tutors: “there may be advantages in equipping faculty with tools to foster a greater degree of congruence with and between their students”. It also suggests PAL may be utilized in wellbeing, personal development and medical humanities curricula, and that appreciating camaraderie and shared experience may help tutors cultivate more effective environments.
