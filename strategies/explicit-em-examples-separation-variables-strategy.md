# Include more explicit E&M-context examples of separation of variables and boundary conditions to improve transfer

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend giving students more opportunities to see separation of variables and boundary conditions used explicitly to solve E&M problems, so that the generality of these techniques is conveyed and reflected in higher CUE scores. OSU moved the Mathematical Methods Capstone and Central Forces Paradigm to the Spring term of the junior year to enable this, and the effect will be tested in a later publication. The article states this rearrangement "gives us an opportunity to test whether the inclusion of more examples where separation of variables and boundary conditions are explicitly used to solve E&M problems can impart the generality of the techniques to the students".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses must provide explicit E&M-context practice with separation of variables and boundary conditions, since exposure only in quantum contexts did not transfer.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness of the schedule change has not yet been evaluated; the article defers discussion to a later publication.

### Target Learners
- junior- and senior-level undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- applying separation of variables to electrostatics problems
- selecting and applying boundary conditions in E&M
