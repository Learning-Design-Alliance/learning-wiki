# Teach by live coding instead of slides

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Instructors write and run code live in front of learners rather than presenting slides. The article reports that live coding is "more convincing", lets instructors respond to "what if?" questions, and facilitates lateral knowledge transfer, meaning people "learn more than we realized we were teaching them by watching us work". It places more burden on instructors than a pre-packaged slide deck, but most find it more fun.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors willing to carry the extra preparation and improvisation burden of coding live
#### Constraints
- It puts more of a burden on instructors than a pre-packaged slide deck

### Target Learners
- Scientists learning programming in hands-on workshops

### Target Learning Goals
- Programming skills and incidental tooling and workflow knowledge gained by watching how an expert works
