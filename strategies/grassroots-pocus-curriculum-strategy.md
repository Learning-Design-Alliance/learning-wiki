# Implement a grassroots PoCUS curriculum in South African medical schools and junior-doctor training facilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that South African medical schools and facilities training junior doctors "work towards implementing a PoCUS curriculum at the grassroots level (including undergraduate students) in order to equip new trainees with robust PoCUS skills in a healthcare landscape that will increasingly demand them". This responds to their finding that training is widespread but credentialling uptake remains persistently low, and that most core competencies are currently taught informally in the work environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilities training junior doctors (interns and community service medical officers) would need to adopt the curriculum alongside existing supervised PoCUS education in the ED
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a recommendation for further development, not an intervention it tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- interns and community service medical officers

### Target Learning Goals
- robust foundational point-of-care ultrasound skills
