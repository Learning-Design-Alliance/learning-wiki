# SEAs can use turnaround competencies and BEIs across selection, mentor matching, supervisor coaching, and leader pipelines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief reports several state uses of turnaround leadership competencies — training entire school leadership teams, competency-aligned professional development, and exploring a micro-credential — and Robinson recommends that states develop policies encouraging competency use for turnaround leader selection and development, support principal supervisors to coach turnaround principals using competencies as guideposts, build pipelines of turnaround leaders in advisory roles, and disseminate resources for using BEIs to identify mentors and match leaders with mentors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration with identified turnaround leaders to develop and disseminate BEI resources for districts and schools
#### Constraints
- The recommendations are addressed to state education agencies developing and sustaining turnaround leaders

### Target Learners
- State education agency staff, principal supervisors, and district leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- Recruiting, retaining, and developing turnaround education leaders
