# Sequence questions in Dirac notation before equivalent questions in the position representation to scaffold measurement-probability reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose that proficiency with Dirac notation can support learning of measurement probabilities in the position representation. They found students sometimes took advantage of Dirac-notation questions to answer position-representation questions, and suggest that "if students become proficient in using Dirac notation, they may even become more adept at determining probabilities of measuring observables in position representation" when questions in Dirac notation precede those in position representation. This is an implementable sequencing recommendation for upper-level quantum mechanics instruction.
