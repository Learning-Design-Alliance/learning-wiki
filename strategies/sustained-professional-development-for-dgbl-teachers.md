# Provide prolonged, sustained professional development so in-service teachers can deliver digital game-based learning effectively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends creating professional development opportunities for in-service teachers to develop the Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge required to integrate DGBL such that student engagement is prolonged and intensified. It notes that although 14 hours of professional development were offered, "only roughly five hours were devoted to preparing teachers to deliver the learning content digitally", so more intense efforts are needed to build teacher confidence with digital delivery modes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops should also address perceived barriers teachers face when using technology in their classrooms
#### Constraints
- Not all professional development time in the study was devoted to pedagogy, which the authors identify as a shortfall

### Target Learners
- in-service secondary agricultural education teachers
- pre-service agricultural education teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- effective integration of digital game-based learning
- student-centric pedagogy
