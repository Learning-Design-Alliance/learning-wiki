# Adopt a hybrid TBL-PBL approach that extends TBL duration and adds clinical reasoning prompts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend wider-scale implementation consider a hybrid approach utilising the strengths of both TBL and PBL, since students found the two formats qualitatively different with advantages and disadvantages to each. Specific further modifications they propose include "extended duration of TBL to allow more time for clinical reasoning; and prompts to facilitate clinical reasoning during the TBLs." This addresses the identified deficiency in clinical reasoning opportunity within TBL problem-solving while retaining TBL's preparation, feedback and small-group advantages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Extended TBL session duration to allow more time for clinical reasoning
- Prompts to facilitate clinical reasoning during TBL sessions
#### Constraints
- The authors state wider-scale implementation with further modifications is needed before a final decision on teaching methods

### Target Learners
- medical students in pre-clinical years

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- basic science knowledge application
- collaborative problem solving
