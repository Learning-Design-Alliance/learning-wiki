Use statistical adjustment to separate school effects from family resources and prior learning when rating schools¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report describes a strategy in which a state education agency, here the Louisiana Department of Education working with Mathematica, uses a statistical model on administrative data to estimate each school's contribution to long-term student success. This enables the state to "identify its high schools’ contributions to students’ long-term success separately from other factors, such as family resources and students’ learning before high school."

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partnership between the state education agency and an analytical team
  • Access to statewide longitudinal student outcome data
