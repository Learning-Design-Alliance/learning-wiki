# Five policy questions for unifying, funding, and improving California's ECE system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's Takeaways section sets forth five questions for policymakers: moving from "a patchwork of disconnected programs to a more unified ECE system"; increasing availability of high-quality, full-day programs; sustainably funding ECE through a stable revenue source; improving quality and supports, including improving the county-led QRISs statewide; and improving data systems for strategic decision-making. Specific mechanisms named include investing in full-day Head Start and state preschool, blending and braiding funding sources, adding preschool funding to the Local Control Funding Formula, and creating incentives for QRIS participation.
