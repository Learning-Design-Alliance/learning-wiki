# Keep smartphone e-learning videos short and ensure good Internet access so they stream smoothly on any device

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A practical implementation strategy drawn from the study's questionnaire findings: because "half of the participants were dissatisfied because the videos did not play quickly and smoothly", the authors recommend keeping each video short and that "educators [provide] good Internet access for students to learn with e‐learning materials without any inconvenience". This addresses device and network variability across schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Smartphone-accessible delivery and adequate Internet infrastructure in the learning setting
#### Constraints
- Videos that do not stream smoothly reduce satisfaction with the materials

### Target Learners
- Dental hygiene students in universities, junior colleges and technical schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Learning dental treatment procedures outside class time via mobile devices
