# Read-Alouds

## Description
Read-alouds involve an educator reading a text aloud to learners, often pausing to model effective reading and thinking strategies such as questioning, inferencing, and using context cues. This approach enhances narrative skills, connects text elements to background knowledge, builds vocabulary, develops understandings of story structures, supports developing connections between print elements, encourages high levels of understanding, teaches the reading process in a meaningful context, models fluency, and motivates learners to read. Discussions are supportive and engaging, allowing learners to extend their worldview and develop critical thinking skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to appropriate reading materials, time for reading and discussion (15-20 minutes per session), and facilitation skills to guide discussions.
#### Constraints
- Learners may have varying levels of interest in the selected texts. Effective implementation requires skilled facilitation to guide discussions and ensure engagement.

### Affordances
- Models effective reading strategies, supports comprehension, enhances vocabulary, promotes critical thinking, connects learning to personal experiences, and motivates learners to engage with literature.

### Personalization
- Texts can be chosen based on learner interests and experiences. Discussions can be tailored to different levels of understanding. Scaffolding can be provided through prompts and sentence starters, especially beneficial for English language learners.
