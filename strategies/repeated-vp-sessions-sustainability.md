# Schedule repeated virtual patient training sessions to achieve sustainable knowledge effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends repeating virtual patient training because knowledge gains from a single session faded by the follow-up test. The authors state that "Repeated training sessions with virtual patients are proposed in order to achieve sustainable educational effects", and they propose research on the impact of repeated sessions over a course of several months on the durability of acquired knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- sessions repeated over a course of several months
#### Constraints
- drawn from a "rather small sample of resident psychiatrists with similar work experience and expertise"

### Target Learners
- resident psychiatrists

### Target Learning Goals
- durable retention of PTSD knowledge

## Claims

- [In one group measured before and after a single virtual patient session, knowledge scores were higher at the follow-up test several weeks later than at baseline, but the difference was not statistically significant](../claims/vp-knowledge-gain-not-sustained.md) [+W]
- [In one group of resident psychiatrists, scores on a knowledge test covering PTSD diagnosis, clinical management and basic communication skills were higher immediately after one virtual patient session than before it](../claims/vp-session-immediate-knowledge-gain.md) [+W]
