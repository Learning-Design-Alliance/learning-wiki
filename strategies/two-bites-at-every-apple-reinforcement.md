## Description
The intermediate Scientific Computing with Python course serves a heterogeneous student group with only the "Two Bites at Every Apple" approach described as suited to it. The article states: "This involves presenting an activity that addresses multiple audiences from diﬀerent perspectives at the same time, revisiting key concepts in multiple contexts to deepen understanding, and is powerful for the mixed audience of students this course targets."
