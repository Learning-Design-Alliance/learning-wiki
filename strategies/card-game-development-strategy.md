# Develop educational card games by selecting key terms, adapting familiar rules, pilot testing, and expert validation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article documents a development procedure: assess current instruction and needs; select key terms from the reference material (90 terms per topic, duplicated for 180 cards); design cards with terms on all four sides and a topic icon; derive rules from familiar games; pilot test with previous classes and revise the rules; then have subject matter experts review and validate the game. The researcher reports that "comments and feedback from the SMEs were positive."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the reference material used for instruction so the term set matches what students are taught
- Access to subject matter experts for review and validation before classroom use
#### Constraints
- The article recommends teachers come up with their own set of terms based on the reference material they use, and formulate other rules so students can play in a different manner

### Target Learners
- college biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- reinforcing factual and conceptual knowledge of biology topics
