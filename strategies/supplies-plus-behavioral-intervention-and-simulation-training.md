# Combine provision of supplies with a behavioral intervention (supportive supervision, opinion leaders, reminders, audit, feedback) and simulation-based training to improve pre-eclampsia care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a two-pronged improvement strategy for pre-eclampsia care in Kinshasa: strengthening provider knowledge and enhancing supply availability. It points to a cluster randomized controlled trial in Kinshasa and Lusaka in which "a combination of provision of supplies with a behavioral intervention including supportive supervision, the use of opinion leaders, reminders, audit, and feedback, improved the diagnosis and management of maternal conditions such as syphilis". It further recommends "evidence-based capacity-building strategies such as simulation-based training" proven effective in other LMICs.

#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of pre-eclampsia prevention, diagnosis, and management, including magnesium sulfate administration and toxicity monitoring
