Adopt integrative, longitudinal, multimodal deep-phenotyping designs combining self-report, behavioural tasks, ecological momentary assessment, and physiological measures¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review's future-directions section recommends that progress depends on "the adoption of integrative, longitudinal, and multimodal designs that assess regulation competencies, neurophysiological processes, and social well -being outcomes within the same adolescents". Deep-phenotyping would allow regulation to be examined as a dynamic capability unfolding across contexts and developmental time rather than a static attribute. The review also suggests machine-learning and predictive modelling to integrate multimodal indicators and identify latent profiles of risk and resilience.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Studies must combine self-report, behavioural tasks, ecological momentary assessment, and physiological measures within the same adolescents

Constraints¶

  • The review notes current evidence rarely links neurophysiological measures to everyday social functioning, and cultural representation remains uneven with sparse research from South Asia, Africa, and the Middle East

Target Learners¶

  • at-risk adolescents aged 10 to 18, especially trauma-exposed and socioeconomically disadvantaged youth

Target Learning Goals¶

  • identifying regulatory profiles and mechanisms linking regulation competencies to social well-being and resilience

Affordances¶

  • Integrative Regulation Competence Framework Risem

Claims¶

  • Neurophysiological measures are markedly underrepresented in research on at-risk adolescent regulation and, when used, are deployed as adjunctive indicators concentrated in specific clinical risk profiles [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Research agenda: investigate affective dynamics with within-person, longitudinal, and multi-channel methods
  • Study effort regulation in real-world, personally relevant contexts over longer timeframes, including via ecological momentary assessment
  • Use multimodal data and time-sensitive analyses to capture situational motivational processes
  • Adopt an integrative methodological toolkit using machine learning and computational models to capture SR's multi-level, temporal, and social dynamics
  • Use multimodal data channels combining subjective and objective measures to overcome single-channel constraints in SSRL research
