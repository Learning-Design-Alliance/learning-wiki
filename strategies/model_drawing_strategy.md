# Model Drawing Strategy

## Description
Teaching fraction and percentage word problems using a model-drawing strategy. This cognitive strategy places emphasis on explicitly teaching students how to draw a schematic diagram to represent the qualitative relations described in the problem, and how to formulate the solution based on the schematic diagram.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pencil and paper, or whiteboard and marker, along with explicit instruction of the model-drawing strategy
#### Constraints
- Learners must have foundational drawing skills

### Affordances
- Visually organizes qualitative relations of the word problem and allows learners to better formulate solutions. Cognitive offloading of quantitative information

### Personalization
- Adjust the complexity of the model drawing and word problems based on learner ability
