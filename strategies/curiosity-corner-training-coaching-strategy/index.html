Implement Curiosity Corner with two initial training workshop days plus ongoing onsite coaching and support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's professional development model combines initial training with sustained support: professional development is provided through a combination of onsite workshops, email and phone support, online resources, and onsite observation and consultation. Two initial training workshop days are required to begin implementation, and in-school coaching and online support may be arranged afterwards.
