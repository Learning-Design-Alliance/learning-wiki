## Description
'Meaning-making' is the second Influence in the article's adapted Community of Inquiry Framework, an overlap of the three Presences through which tutors can promote SoB. The article offers "guidance illustrated by examples" for each Influence, leading to particular suggestions concentrating on the promotion of a sense of belonging. Tutors apply this guidance when facilitating meaning-making among online learners.
