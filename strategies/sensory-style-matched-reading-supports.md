# Match reading supports to learners' diagnosed sensory learning styles (visual, auditory, kinesthetic)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
After diagnosing learners' sensory styles with Reid's (1987) questionnaire, the teacher had each learner read ESP texts using their own style: visual learners used "glosses, covering visual clue, boldness of words, Italic forms, headings", auditory learners read aloud, listened to audio, or had background music, and kinesthetic learners moved their hands, wrote information down, walked, or role-played the text. The article suggests ESP reading materials be equipped with various visual, audio and kinesthetic aids such as audio and visual glosses.
