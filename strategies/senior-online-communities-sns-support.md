# Direct older new SNS users to senior-focused online communities offering social support and courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that people advising senior citizens point older, new social media users toward established online communities that provide social support information and instruction about Internet and SNS use. It names AARP, SeniorNet.com, SeniorPlanet.com, and SeniorSite.com as examples, arguing such resources "may also be useful" given older new users' vulnerability to Internet investment and health misinformation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advisors or helpers aware of senior-focused online communities and their support resources
#### Constraints
- Older, new users may be particularly vulnerable to Internet investment and health misinformation, so guidance should accompany access

### Target Learners
- older adults (senior citizens) new to social networking sites

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and effective adoption of social networking and Internet use
