Adopt ethical safeguards and pedagogically grounded frameworks when deploying GenAI assessment tools¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This strategy directs educators and policymakers to pair any GenAI assessment deployment with explicit ethical safeguards and a pedagogically grounded framework. The paper highlights "the need for ethical safeguards and pedagogically grounded frameworks" alongside GenAI's engagement and workload benefits. Implementation should address academic integrity, algorithmic bias, and transparency risks identified in the paper's critical review before tools are used for high-stakes evaluation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ethical safeguards must accompany GenAI tool deployment; frameworks must be grounded in pedagogy rather than technology alone

Constraints¶

  • The paper offers this as guidance for responsible future integration, not as a validated procedure

Target Learners¶

  • higher education computer science students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academically integrity-preserving assessment practice

Claims¶

  • Safeguards in university AI policies are sparse and unevenly covered [+W]
  • Human-AI relationship concerns persist across all CAI generations while academic integrity and data privacy are emerging ethical concerns [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt a roadmap for ethical CAI implementation in education
  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
  • Establish comprehensive institutional AI governance frameworks including task forces, roadmaps, and transparent policies
  • Address core ethical challenges—data privacy, algorithmic fairness, and transparency—when deploying AI-driven personalized learning
  • Adopt LLMs in higher education only alongside organizational readiness, ethical guidance and professional development
  • Deploy agentic AI in education with transparency about agentic decisions, human oversight and institutional responsibility
  • Adopt a clear pedagogical approach focused on critical thinking and fact-checking strategies when integrating LLMs into curricula
  • Operationalize abstract ethical frameworks as concrete LLM agent personas with demographic cues and value prioritizations
