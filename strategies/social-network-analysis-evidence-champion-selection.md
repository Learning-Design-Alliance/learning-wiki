# Use social network analysis to identify influential staff as Evidence Champions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The delivery team used social network analysis surveys with all teaching staff to map and measure relationships and determine which staff had most influence in sharing ideas for practice, then recommended two to three potential Evidence Champions per school. The report states the intervention team recommended that "at least one Evidence Champion came from the senior leadership team" so teachers saw leadership support, with others chosen from staff colleagues looked to for advice. A one-off cost of £2500 per RLC covered this analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- surveys with all teaching staff to determine which members of staff had most influence in terms of sharing ideas for practice
#### Constraints
- the £2500 per RLC social network analysis was not considered part of the intervention itself

### Target Learners
- primary school teachers and senior leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- effective selection of staff to lead research engagement in schools
