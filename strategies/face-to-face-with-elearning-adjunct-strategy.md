# Deliver NIPT education through face-to-face sessions supported by e-learning for refreshing knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on questionnaire and interview findings, the article recommends that NIPT training be delivered face-to-face because of the opportunity to discuss and ask questions, with online resources used to access up-to-date information and refresh knowledge. The authors state that whether training is face-to-face, e-learning or blended, "it is likely that training on NIPT will need to be incorporated into the mandatory training offered by individual hospital's antenatal Screening Coordinators", with a cascade approach through the National Screening Committee and Screening Coordinators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face sessions allowing questions and discussion
- E-learning content kept up to date as NIPT develops
#### Constraints
- Interviewees raised concerns about whether health professionals would complete online training and about absorbing information online without the opportunity to ask questions

### Target Learners
- midwives and other health professionals offering Down syndrome screening
- antenatal Screening Coordinators

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining up-to-date knowledge of NIPT and its care pathway
- supporting informed choice in counselling pregnant women
