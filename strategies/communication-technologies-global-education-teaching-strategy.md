# Use communication technologies (newspapers, television, video, news blogs) in teaching global-education attainments to raise attention, achievement and retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends designing learning environments that integrate communication technologies into teaching of global-education content, because such environments address multiple sensory organs and sustain attention. It states "it is important to increase interest in global issues and to draw attention and to design learning environments that allow communication technologies to be utilized at this point and to integrate these environments into the learning process". The strategy was implemented through 11 lesson plans pairing unit topics with newspaper clippings, television, video and news blogs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course plans and activities prepared in advance and expert-evaluated
- News content relevant to students' lives and global issues
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one semi-experimental study in a single Samsun secondary school; the article notes some prior studies found no significant difference for technology-supported teaching

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade secondary school students in social studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement and retention of global-education attainments; attention to global issues; intercultural awareness
