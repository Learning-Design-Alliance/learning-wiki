# Adopt a one-page Event Participation Code of Conduct distributed before and at meetings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
At its Fall 2019 meeting the section adopted an Event Participation Code of Conduct based on AAPT's but "kept to one page to encourage people to read the policy", referring readers back to AAPT's original document for definitions. It was posted on the website under meeting information, emailed to all pre-registered participants, and displayed as a poster near the registration table for walk-in registrants. The stated purpose is reinforcing expectations of professional conduct and creating an atmosphere of safety for minoritized populations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A parent organization's fuller policy document to reference for definitions and language; distribution channels (website, email, registration-table poster)
#### Constraints
- The section had no reported incidents in the current officers' memory, so the code's effect on incidents is untested; the authors plan to iterate with a clearer reporting system

### Target Learners
- Section meeting attendees, including walk-in registrants

### Target Learning Goals
- Setting and reinforcing norms of professional conduct and safety at professional meetings
