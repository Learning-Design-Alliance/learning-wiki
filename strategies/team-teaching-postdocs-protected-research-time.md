# Increase protected research time for anatomy faculty through team teaching and postdoctoral teaching positions

## Description
The article proposes structural staffing changes to free anatomy professors' time for scholarly work: making content-heavy courses team taught and implementing additional postdoctoral teaching positions that could lighten the teaching load. It notes "previous suggestions 1 for making content‐heavy courses team taught may provide anatomy professors with additional research time" and that postdoctoral fellows could help lighten teaching loads, though freed time may shift toward mentoring.
