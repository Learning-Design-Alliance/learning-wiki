# Summarize a multi-step procedure into a short acronym to prevent information overload and aid memorization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To avoid information overload and simplify the explanation, the module's designers summarized the inhalation procedure into four steps and defined the acronym ASMA, whose letters correspond to the Italian sentences for each step (shake the canister, breathe out, press the canister, take 5 deep breaths). This chunking strategy pairs each step with a memorable verbal label displayed with images and gestures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The procedure must be reducible to a small number of steps
- A meaningful acronym in the learner's language
#### Constraints
- The interaction is in Italian, since the experimentation will be conducted in Italy

### Target Learners
- children aged 6-11 with asthma

### Target Learning Goals
- memorizing the sequence of inhalation steps
