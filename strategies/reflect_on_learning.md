# Reflect on Learning

## Description
Student reflection on learning, particularly when done collaboratively, is critical for moving knowledge of content and strategies into Long-term Memory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured reflection time, prompts for reflection.
#### Constraints
- May feel tedious, requires careful facilitation.
