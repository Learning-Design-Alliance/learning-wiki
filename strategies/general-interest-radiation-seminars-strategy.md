# Offer general-interest seminars and popularized informational material on radiation and nuclear issues for students and the public

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose developing and disseminating informational material, in popularized form, and general-interest seminars on radiations and nuclear issues, because students' knowledge comes mostly from informal sources and their survey found "a positive attitude towards the general interest seminars". They link this to social learning connecting the educational system with the wider community.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Seminars must be a non-traditional but valid way to be informed, as the article describes students' preference after secondary school
#### Constraints
- The article notes a more systematic and representative survey is needed for secure assessment of Greek students' and public knowledge

### Target Learners
- technology-oriented undergraduate students
- general public

### Target Learning Goals
- differentiating ionizing and non-ionizing radiation
- understanding beneficial applications and risks of radiation and nuclear energy
