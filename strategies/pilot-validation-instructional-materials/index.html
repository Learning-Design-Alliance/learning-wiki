Validate multimedia instructional materials with student feedback before deployment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Before the main study, the researchers invited students to try the instructional materials and used their feedback to revise them. The article states "five English majors and five English non-English majors were invited to try the instructional materials" and "Feedback from the students was used as the reference for editing and revising the instructional materials."
