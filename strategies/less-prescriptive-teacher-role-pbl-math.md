# Teachers should be less prescriptive and allow students to decide how to carry out mathematics activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that as teachers implement problem-based instructional strategy, they should be less prescriptive and allow students to decide on how to carry out the mathematics activities themselves. This contrasts with the PBIM condition where the teacher prescribed the line of research and assigned tasks and roles to individual members of small groups, which produced lower achievement than PBIS.
