# Use chat-based GenAI as a partner for students to practice argumentation and scientific claims, especially in larger courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q3` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend using chat-based GenAI as a practice partner for argumentation skills. They state the study "has important implications for future research and practice on using chat-based GenAI as a partner for students to practice the knowledge and skills connected to argumentation and scientific claims, especially in larger courses or broader audiences". The strategy positions the AI as a conversational partner supporting learners' argumentation practices rather than as an authority.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A chat-based GenAI tool available to students as an interaction partner
#### Constraints
- GenAI's tendency to provide definitive answers unsupported by evidence must be managed

### Target Learners
- Students in larger courses or broader audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- Argumentation
- Evaluating scientific claims
