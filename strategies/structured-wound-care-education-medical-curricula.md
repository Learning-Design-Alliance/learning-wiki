# Integrate structured, evidence-based wound care education—including hands-on and interprofessional components—into undergraduate medical curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes there is "an obvious need to integrate structured wound care education into undergraduate medical curricula to address current knowledge gaps among medical students," and that policymakers and curriculum developers "should prioritise evidence‐based wound management training, including hands‐on experience and interprofessional approaches." It also recommends early exposure to wound care in clinical practice.
