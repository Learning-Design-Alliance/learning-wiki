# Formalize simulation as a core ACGME requirement for EM residency training and track simulation use in hours in future surveys

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's future-directions section recommends formalizing simulation's role in EM curricula: it notes it "has recently been suggested that simulation be formalized as a core ACGME requirement" and that this survey may mobilize engagement in that initiative. It also recommends future surveys measure simulation in hours rather than sessions per year to enable longitudinal comparison.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further research linking programmatic and regional differences to educational outcomes to prioritize funding and equipment distribution
#### Constraints
- Whether increased simulation alone drives better educational results, or which curriculum elements are effective, remains unstudied per the authors

### Target Learners
- EM residents
- Residency program directors

### Target Learning Goals
- Standardized simulation curricula in EM residency training
