# Use Cultural Astronomy questions and naked-eye sky experiences as strategic entry doors for astronomy teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends taking "las ingeniosas preguntas que se hacían y aún se hacen distintas culturas" as strategic entry doors for teaching, within a historical-epistemological approach that shows how humanity built current scientific models and that knowledge is always "situated". Because naked-eye astronomy "requires little or no instruments", sky experiences can be worked with observationally and experientially in formal classrooms.
