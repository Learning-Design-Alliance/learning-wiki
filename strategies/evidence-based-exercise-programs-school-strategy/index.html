Prescribe intensive, multi-component physical activity integrated into regular classes to maximize learning outcomes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends implementing evidence-based physical education programs in school settings and optimizing effects by combining multi-component exercise schemes with regular classes. It states "the concomitant prescription of 'gross motor tasks' and more intensive (and appropriate) physical activities is also recommended in an attempt to maximize students' learning outcomes."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Physical activities should be more intensive rather than merely more frequent to achieve greater academic gains

Constraints¶

  • Positive effects are not uniform across subjects and appear higher for mathematics skills

Target Learners¶

  • School-aged students across education levels

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Overall academic achievement, especially mathematics

Claims¶

  • Greater exercise intensity, not merely increased frequency, is associated with larger academic gains [+W]
  • A significant overall improvement in academic achievement was detected after physical activity programs compared with controls [+W]
  • Effects of school-based physical activity on academic performance are not uniform and appear higher for mathematics skills [+W]
  • Performance in mathematics tests significantly improved after school-based physical activity interventions compared with control groups [+W]
  • School physical activity interventions did not significantly improve language, reading, or composite academic scores compared with controls [-W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Design physical activity interventions as continuous regular activity sustained over several weeks
  • Embed 30-minute bouts of structured physical activity, especially open-skill team games, into primary-school routines to support short-term memory
