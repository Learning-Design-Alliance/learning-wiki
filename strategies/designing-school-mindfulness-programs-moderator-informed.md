# Design school mindfulness programs using combinations of mindfulness activities, matched to adolescent developmental stage and delivery agent

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend applying their meta-analytic moderator findings to future program design, stating the findings "can be used to inform future designs and applications of mindfulness interventions in the school setting." The strategy is to design school mindfulness programs informed by the moderators the meta-analysis identified: delivering during late adolescence (15–18), combining various mindfulness activities, and deliberately choosing between an outside facilitator and trained educators/teachers.
