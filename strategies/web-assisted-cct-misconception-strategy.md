# Use web-assisted conceptual change texts with visualizations to prevent and remediate science misconceptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend combining conceptual change texts with web devices: "Through the use of web devices such as pictures, video, animation and simulation of especially concrete and difficult to reach concepts can decrease the formation of errors." They also recommend three-stage tests for simultaneous, objective diagnosis of conceptual errors and suggest visualizing science textbook texts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diagnostic pre-study to identify target conceptual errors in upper grades
- Web-assisted texts prepared from identified errors
#### Constraints
- Authors note conceptual change texts work "even if they don't have a success of 100%"

### Target Learners
- Elementary and secondary science students

### Target Learning Goals
- Replacing misconceptions with scientifically correct concepts

## Claims

- [Conceptual error reduction was more durable in the web-assisted group (61%) than the control group (3.8%) three months after instruction](../claims/web-assisted-cct-durable-error-reduction.md) [+W]
- [Web-assisted conceptual change texts reduced 8th graders' conceptual errors in water chemistry more than classic conceptual change texts (65% vs. 14% error reduction)](../claims/web-assisted-cct-greater-error-reduction.md) [+W]
- [Web-assisted conceptual change texts produced significantly higher three-month permanency test scores than classic texts (U = 67, p = 0.011)](../claims/web-assisted-cct-significant-permanency-advantage.md) [+W]
- [Web-assisted conceptual change texts produced significantly higher three-stage post-test scores than classic conceptual change texts (U = 103, p = 0.038)](../claims/web-assisted-cct-significant-posttest-advantage.md) [+W]
