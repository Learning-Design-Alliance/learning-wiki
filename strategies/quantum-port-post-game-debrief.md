# Post-game debrief with guiding questions connecting game rules to real quantum teleportation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
After a few rounds of gaming sessions, a debrief session should be conducted to inform the player about the real science involved. The debrief begins with guiding questions (e.g. what is special about the black wires, what happens if only white wires are built) and establishes connections between the gaming rules and quantum teleportation, including that classical communication is required so faster-than-light communication will not occur, and that quantum teleportation offers security by quantum physics rather than by encryption.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The debrief may go into a diagrammatic explanation, a more detailed mathematical explanation, or both, depending on the technical background of the player
#### Constraints
- The moderator should highlight that some gaming mechanics are not translatable to physical reality, such as the Entangler representing Alice's state rather than the entangling effect between Alice and Bob

### Target Learners
- general public with varying technical backgrounds

### Target Learning Goals
- mapping game mechanics onto the actual quantum teleportation protocol, including measurement, Pauli correction, classical communication, and communication security
