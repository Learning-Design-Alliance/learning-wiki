Provide a structured first-year Getting Started Experience plus year-long administrator training when adopting a comprehensive curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

With adoption of Bridges in Mathematics, schools and districts receive "a comprehensive Getting Started Experience for all teachers and instructional leaders during the first year of implementation." The full experience includes "workshops delivered by Bridges educators, self-paced online courses, and informational videos," plus a year-long training program for administrators, and curated professional learning materials to support ongoing implementation.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Adoption of Bridges in Mathematics by schools or districts.

  • instructional leaders
  • administrators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective implementation of the Bridges curriculum

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training plus follow-up courses and coaching for program implementation
  • Support curriculum implementation with initial multi-day training plus ongoing onsite consultation during the school year
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Provide virtual and on-site professional learning on the digital platform and grade-specific curriculum training
  • Provide initial training plus multi-year in-classroom coaching when implementing Carnegie Learning Algebra I
  • Provide welcoming schools with more ongoing training, time for reflection, and targeted support when integrating displaced students and staff
  • Use a full-time program facilitator plus multi-mode professional development to sustain comprehensive reform implementation
  • Support first-year adopters of a comprehensive curriculum with quick-start overviews, editable materials, and curated course plans
