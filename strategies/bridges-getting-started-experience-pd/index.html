Provide a structured first-year Getting Started Experience plus year-long administrator training when adopting a comprehensive curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

With adoption of Bridges in Mathematics, schools and districts receive "a comprehensive Getting Started Experience for all teachers and instructional leaders during the first year of implementation." The full experience includes "workshops delivered by Bridges educators, self-paced online courses, and informational videos," plus a year-long training program for administrators, and curated professional learning materials to support ongoing implementation.
