# Expand deliberate practice with periodic feedback on a virtual simulator to target higher-order mechanical ventilation skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that residents performed poorly on complex applied competencies despite the course, the authors recommend that "educational strategies focusing on higher-order competencies need to be emphasized and reinforced, such as by expanding deliberate practice on the simulator and offering periodic feedback during training". They also call for further research on more intense deliberate practice with feedback and simulator-based assessments with minimal passing scores and skills-retention testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a virtual MV simulator for deliberate practice
- A support structure for questions and feedback during practice
#### Constraints
- The article did not measure how many hours residents spent practicing, so dose is untested

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents learning mechanical ventilation

### Target Learning Goals
- Higher-order MV competencies such as adjusting ventilator parameters to correct asynchrony and implementing lung-protective ventilation in ARDS
