# Use simulation technologies and skills labs to accelerate learning when operative exposure is reduced

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that where duty hour restrictions reduce operative exposure, training employing simulation technologies and skills labs can partially compensate. It states that "Training that employs simulation technologies and skills labs could serve as a partial remedy to accelerate learning in the setting of reduced operative exposure". The authors note this approach has not been studied in the literature with respect to open cases, so its recommendation is prospective rather than evidence-based for open surgery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
