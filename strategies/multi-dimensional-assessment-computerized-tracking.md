# Use multi-dimensional assessment with computerized tracking to capture learning process in flipped classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A future-directions recommendation: because the flipped classroom model emphasizes the learning process rather than the outcome, the authors suggest researchers "design multi-dimensional assessment methods to assess the various aspects of the learners' growth, with a computerized system to track, manage, and identify the assignments, examinations, and learning situations of the students." This addresses the study's own limitation that the learning process was not recorded for in-depth analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computerized system to track and manage assignments, examinations, and learning situations
#### Constraints
- The article offers this as a suggestion for future studies, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- learners in flipped classroom courses

### Target Learning Goals
- assessing various aspects of learners' growth beyond outcome tests
