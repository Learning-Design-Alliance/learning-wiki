# Motivate students with both the carrot of selling knowledge's benefits and the stick of potential failure with attendant consequences.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The essay recommends preserving both motivators: instructors should sell the beauty and benefits of knowledge and abilities while students remain aware that failure carries consequences. The authors argue "Motivating students requires both selling the beauty and benefits of knowledge and abilities in an area ( the carrot), and awareness of potential failure with the attendant consequences ( the stick)," and that the customer model removes the stick because students shift blame for failure to the instructor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers empowered as gatekeepers with control over academic rigor and learning quality
#### Constraints
- The stick is an ineffective motivator for the student who believes they can simply shop for a different instructor who will pass them

### Target Learners
- college students in challenging science and engineering courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained study effort toward rigorous grade goals
