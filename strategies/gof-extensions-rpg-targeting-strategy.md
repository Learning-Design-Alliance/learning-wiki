# Extend the Gate of Fate to spatial targeting games and role-playing games where antagonists respond to players' quantum reflexes

## Description
The article outlines implementable extensions of the GOF beyond Tetris. In a Mölkky-like targeting game, a shooter emits a magnetic particle through a Stern–Gerlach-like field, and “By changing the field parameters through the GOF, the probability distribution of particle trajectories can be reshaped, thereby increasing the likelihood of reaching the desired upper target.” In RPGs, an encounter is encoded as a superposition of favorable and unfavorable outcomes (mushroom vs. Goomba), and manipulation biases measurement toward the favorable outcome without guaranteeing it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A game outcome that can be encoded as a superposition of favorable and unfavorable alternatives whose amplitudes the player can manipulate before measurement
#### Constraints
- Manipulation increases the probability of a favorable result without guaranteeing a favourable result

### Target Learners
- video game players developing quantum intuition

### Target Learning Goals
- biasing outcome probabilities toward desirable states via quantum operations
