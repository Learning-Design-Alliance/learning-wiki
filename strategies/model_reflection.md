# Model Reflection

## Description
Students need to encounter reflective role models. Modeling reflection can involve reflecting on a workshop session. Teachers can share journal excerpts. The journal excerpts can model evidence of good listening skills, probe for clarity and understanding, ask thoughtful questions, and share metacognitive thinking. Through experience and continuous modeling, the class begins to learn how to use the habits of mind in reflective conversations, which strengthens the transfer to the internal voice of reflection.
