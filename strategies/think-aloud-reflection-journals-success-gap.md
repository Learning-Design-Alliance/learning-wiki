# Use think-aloud protocols and reflection journals to bridge the gap between perceived and actual success

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address discrepancies between students' perceptions of success and their actual academic proficiency, the authors recommend that "teachers should incorporate think-aloud protocols and reflection journals, requiring students to justify their decision-making steps during problem-solving". Evaluating how a solution was reached, rather than just what the solution is, is intended to mitigate the illusion of knowing and align outcome expectations with true capabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must justify their decision-making steps during problem-solving, not only report answers
