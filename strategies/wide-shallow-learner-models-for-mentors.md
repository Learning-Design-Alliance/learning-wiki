# Design intelligent mentors around wide-and-shallow learner models rather than narrow-and-deep ones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that intelligent mentoring systems should build broad yet shallow understanding of the learner and current situation to select pedagogical strategies and respond in a motivational, emotionally-aware way. They state: "we hypothesis that for intelligent mentors‘wide and shallow’ learner models would be preferred to‘narrow and deep’", and believe the wide approach is likely to have significantly higher payoff than adding a single tutor-like competence at a time. This is a forward-looking design direction, not a tested result.

## Design Implications

- connecting learning with real-world experience
