# Shadow screen performance in which students choreograph particle physics concepts with their bodies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students divided into two smaller groups devise, perform, and film a short performance piece behind a paper screen with an overhead projector casting their shadows, avoiding self-consciousness. The article notes that "Ideas that it would be challenging to include in a drawing can be explored in a performance, by using the movement and interaction to express and discuss activity in the particle world." Students allocate roles such as direction, sound, performance, and camera, and choose props, sounds, and projected background images to reinforce the chosen concept.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A paper screen, overhead projector, and filming equipment; editing was done by the artist, and in future by the art teacher, with students advising on filters and effects
#### Constraints
- New concepts can be introduced at this point only to particularly able groups or older participants, and editing depends on the abilities of the group

### Target Learners
- Key Stage 4 students aged 14 to 16 in the British education system

### Target Learning Goals
- Expressing and discussing particle world activity such as transformation and interaction of particles through movement and choreography
