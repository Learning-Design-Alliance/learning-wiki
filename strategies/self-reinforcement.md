# Self-Reinforcement

## Description
Self-reinforcement involves individuals selecting a reinforcer and rewarding themselves for reaching or exceeding a set criterion. This strategy actively involves students in their learning environment, teaching a beneficial skill for independent adult living and often creating greater changes than teacher-imposed rewards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher and students must set a clear and objective goal. Students must select a reinforcer. Procedures for evaluation must be determined. Student must administer the reinforcer.
#### Constraints
- What is reinforcing for students will vary according to factors such as age, personality, culture, and gender.

### Personalization
- Teachers can involve the student in the process of choosing a reinforcer by asking the student to suggest possible reinforcers or by providing a reinforcement menu for the student to choose from or rank order.
