## Description
The review enumerates the lesson components of Reading Recovery's daily tutoring sessions: "fluency instruction, letter and word identification skills, phonemic and orthographic awareness instruction, reading a new, challenging book with scaffolded teacher support, and daily progress monitoring of independent oral reading." This recipe pairs skills instruction with scaffolded authentic reading and daily monitoring of independent oral reading.
