# Require repeated benchmark performance with spaced repetition to ensure reliable skill acquisition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states that achieving proficiency on the simulator "should also include requirements for repeated benchmark performance to ensure reliable skill acquisition and promote retention through spaced repetition." It cites the FLS curriculum requiring two consecutive repetitions followed by ten non-consecutive repetitions for Peg Transfer and Intracorporeal Knot Tying as an example. No universal standard exists for defining proficiency based on number or temporal spacing of repetitions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future definitions of repetition requirements should be informed by evidence on both learning and retention
