# Establish student-led peer-based suicide prevention clubs using Sources of Strength

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The district partnered with Two Feathers Native American Family Services to train staff and students in Sources of Strength, described as "a peer-based mental health and suicide prevention resiliency program." Student-led SOS clubs were then established on secondary campuses as the district's suicide-prevention mechanism within Tier 1 universal supports.
