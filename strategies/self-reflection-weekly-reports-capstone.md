# Add a self-reflection section to weekly reports to counter corrective-feedback-only marking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the CT-inspired systematic iterative marking procedure is designed only to tell students what they have done wrong, a student who made no mistakes but was unsure would see no substantial comments. The author proposes adding a self-reflection section to the weekly reporting so students can indicate reduced confidence in a specific section and ask questions, with additional feedback comments addressing these.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly reporting mechanism (Google or Microsoft Forms capturing rubric elements, scoring points, penalties, and common feedback statements)
#### Constraints
- Corrective feedback is used only in a negative sense, so error-free but unsure students receive no substantial comments

### Target Learners
- undergraduate capstone students submitting weekly reports

### Target Learning Goals
- seeking help and expressing uncertainty about their research work
- receiving actionable feedback
