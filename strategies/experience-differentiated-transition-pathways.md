# Differentiate transition curricula by prior experience level, offering abbreviated foundational modules and extended advanced sessions for experienced practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that "transition curricula may benefit from differentiated, experience-based pathways, including abbreviated foundational modules and extended advanced application sessions for experienced practitioners." This responds to their finding that experienced physicians reported limited learning gains, and that curricula targeting SP-specific gap areas were most effective when highlighting novel system constraints rather than generic robotic fundamentals.
