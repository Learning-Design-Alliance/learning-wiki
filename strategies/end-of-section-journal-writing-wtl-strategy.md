# Use end-of-section journal writing as a writing-to-learn activity during a science unit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy asks students to write a journal entry as a writing-to-learn activity at the end of each section of a science unit, organizing what they know in their own words rather than copying the teacher. In this study, “The experimental group students were asked to write a journal as a WTL activity at the end of each section for five weeks,” with journals checked and accepted the next day, and writing at home was permitted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher- or teacher-prepared instruction sheet for journal writing distributed and explained at the start
- Journals checked and accepted by the teacher/researcher the day after writing
#### Constraints
- Studied only in a fourth-grade science unit (“Illumination and Sound Technologies”) over five weeks in one state school

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement in science unit content
- positive attitudes toward science
