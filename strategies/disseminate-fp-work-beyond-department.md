# Encourage FP groups to present and publish beyond the department to extend project impact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To maximize the impact of first-year research, the authors recommend that groups be encouraged to present their work outside the department, at venues such as a local AAPT meeting, an undergraduate research symposium, or Society of Physics Students regional or national meetings. Depending on the project, faculty and research groups could consider publication in an undergraduate research journal or in journals such as The Physics Teacher or American Journal of Physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completed projects of presentable quality, and faculty willing to support external presentation or submission
#### Constraints
- Publication depends on the nature of the project

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific communication
- professional dissemination of research
