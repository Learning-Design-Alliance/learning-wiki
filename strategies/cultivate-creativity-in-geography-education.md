# Cultivate students' creativity in geography education to support achievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that creativity partially mediates the link between family capital and geography achievement, the authors call on geography educators to emphasize creativity and higher-order geographic thinking in curricula and educational goals. They state that "these findings should prompt a deeper consideration of the importance of creativity in shaping student success in geography" and urge teachers to "continue to promote internal cognitive development".
