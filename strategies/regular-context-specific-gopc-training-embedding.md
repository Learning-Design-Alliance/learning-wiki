# Prioritize regular, context-specific communication training with conversation guides and available technologies to embed GoPC conversations in practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's concluding recommendation is that embedding Goals of Patient Care conversations requires ongoing, context-specific training integrated with organizational supports. It states that "When clinicians and organizations prioritize regular, context-specific communication training, which promotes the use of conversation guides and available technologies, Goals of Patient Care conversations are more likely to be embedded in practice." The strategy links training delivery to system-level embedding rather than treating training as a one-off event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous learning opportunities and overcoming existing barriers to training
- Leadership recognition and involvement, provider training, and support to manage systems and roles across disciplines
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on overcoming existing barriers, providing continuous learning opportunities, and embedding training into clinical practice

### Target Learners
- registered health professionals in acute care settings

### Target Learning Goals
- conducting effective, patient-centered Goals of Patient Care conversations
