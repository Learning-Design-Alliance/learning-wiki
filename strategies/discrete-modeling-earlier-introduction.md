## Description
The article recommends using discrete modeling to introduce students to meaningful modeling earlier, since its mathematics is lower-level than the differential-equations route. The authors write that 'this technique can be used to introduce students to meaningful modeling problems at an earlier age,' with classroom activities such as the bungee-cord vibrating-string experiment built from inexpensive, readily available equipment.
