# Foster pre-service teachers' empathy, teaching motivation and inclusive education efficacy to improve attitudes toward inclusion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the supported mediation paths, the article recommends that teacher education institutions address the affective and motivational components of pre-service teachers' development, not only knowledge and skills. It states that higher education institutions and educators "should pay attention to the cultivation of pre-service teachers' teaching motivation and the formation of inclusive education efficacy" and attend to their emotional needs. The recommendation follows directly from the finding that empathy works through these two organism variables.
