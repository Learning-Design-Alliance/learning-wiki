# Integrate interactive e-learning modules into dental curricula while combining them with practical demonstrations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend integrating interactive, case-based e-learning modules into dental curricula to improve diagnostic accuracy, knowledge retention, and communication skills, while cautioning that e-learning should not stand alone. They state that "practical demonstrations should be included with e‐learning modules to better address complex areas and positional errors," and suggest enhancing instructor support and structured guidance for students who find self-directed learning overwhelming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured, standardized case-based content aligned with curriculum learning outcomes
- Calibrated supervisors and validated checklists for assessment
#### Constraints
- Findings are from a single-center pre-post study without a control group; self-directed e-learning overwhelmed some students; VDC does not fully capture non-verbal cues and empathetic engagement

### Target Learners
- dental undergraduate students, particularly in oral radiology

### Target Learning Goals
- radiographic interpretation and reporting compliance
- patient communication skills
