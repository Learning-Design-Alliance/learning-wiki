# Adapt anatomy teaching strategies to regional and institutional contexts and strengthen teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review concludes that anatomy teaching strategies must be adapted to regional and institutional contexts and that teachers need support: "Teaching strategies need to be adapted to regional and institutional contexts. It is therefore necessary to support these teachers to better meet the contemporary demands of anatomy education." The discussion reports that resource-limited institutions rely on conventional approaches, and that content taught varies by healthcare profession. The review also cites calls to strengthen teacher training and consolidate the academic training of anatomy teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of the institution's human, material and financial resources when selecting pedagogical approaches
#### Constraints
- The review's results cannot be generalized to all healthcare professions, since studies were largely conducted in medical schools with lower representation of other professions

### Target Learners
- anatomy teachers and program leads in healthcare professions education

### Target Learning Goals
- context-appropriate anatomy instruction across health professions programs
