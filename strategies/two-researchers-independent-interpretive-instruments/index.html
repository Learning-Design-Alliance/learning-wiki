Use two researchers as independent instruments for interpretation instead of one researcher confirming the other's reliability¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy replaces conventional single-coder reliability checking with collaborative interpretation. The study uses "two researchers as independent instruments for interpretation in education policy evaluation", and rather than "using one researcher to confirm the reliability of the other", it draws on each researcher's positional reflexivity to cogenerate themes and theory. It is implementable in qualitative evaluation projects analyzing interview or other qualitative data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Two researchers with distinct perspectives willing to engage in positional reflexivity during analysis
