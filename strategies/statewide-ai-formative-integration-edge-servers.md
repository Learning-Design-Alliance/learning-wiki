## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendation is directed at state education authorities: "The study recommends state-wide curriculum integration of AI-formative tools and the deployment of offline-capable Edge AI servers to ensure instructional continuity in resource-constrained environments." It pairs curriculum-level adoption of real-time AI formative assessment with infrastructure provisioning so that feedback tools keep working where connectivity is unreliable.
