# Shadowing (Behavior Adjustment)

## Description
An adult follows a child closely, within arm's reach, to provide constant direct supervision and prevent unwanted behaviors (e.g., biting) before they occur. The adult remains unobtrusive, ideally ensuring the child doesn't feel singled out or that their play is being adjusted. The goal is to keep the class safe while the child develops alternative, more appropriate behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated adult (preferably the same one), close proximity to the child, and consistency in shadowing.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant adult effort and dedication. Could be perceived negatively if not implemented carefully (child feels singled out).
