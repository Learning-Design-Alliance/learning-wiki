# Debrief learners on the mechanics of interpreter use: short phrases, frequent pauses, and speaking directly to the patient or family member

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During post-case debriefing, the faculty observer reviews critical actions and then explicitly teaches the standard mechanics of working with a medical interpreter. The article specifies "the need to use short phrases, pause frequently for interpretation, and speak directly to the patient (or family member)," alongside probing questions about the differential diagnosis and how the learner typically works with an interpreter. A tip sheet for clinical interpreter use is provided as an appendix for educators.
