# Extend competency-based progress testing to higher-level formats (key feature, situational judgement, OSCE) and richer feedback, with continued student involvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's outlook recommends continuing development of the formative competency-based progress test with students actively included, focusing on feedback for participants, online administration, and additional question and exam formats. The authors state the focus will be on "the expansion to include other questions and exam formats (e.g. key feature questions, situational judgement questions and OSCE) that are able to represent the basis of competency at a higher level than is possible with MCQs", since the MCQ format captures only the knowledge component of competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student involvement in continued test development
- Feedback formats that make performance differences across subject groups and competency areas actionable for students
#### Constraints
- MCQs assess only the knowledge components of competencies, not skills at a higher level

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- longitudinal self-assessment of competency-related knowledge during medical study
