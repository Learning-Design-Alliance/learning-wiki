# Create structured opportunities for positive risk-taking and scaffold the transition period to mitigate transient engagement costs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that stakeholders "create structured, supportive opportunities for youth to engage in positive risk-taking behaviors (e.g., enrollment in challenging academic coursework, leadership roles in extracurricular activities, and prosocial peer advocacy)". Because initial adoption of positive risk-taking may temporarily reduce academic engagement, it further recommends "providing targeted scaffolding and executive function support during the transition period" and helping adolescents connect risk-taking skills to academic learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adults guide sensation-seeking tendencies toward positive rather than suppress all risk-taking
#### Constraints
- Educators should be aware that initial adoption of positive risk-taking behaviors may temporarily reduce academic engagement

### Target Learning Goals
