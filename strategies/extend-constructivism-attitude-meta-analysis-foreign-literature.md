# Repeat and extend meta-analysis of constructivism's effect on attitudes to include foreign publications

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that future synthesis work on constructivism and student attitudes not be restricted to a single national literature. They state: "We suggest that the research be repeated and extended in such a way as to include literature that incorporates foreign publications." This follows their observation that the present study "was limited only to academic studies conducted in Turkey", and they argue that meta-analysis of studies from different countries would strengthen the results. The strategy is therefore to broaden the geographic and linguistic scope of evidence synthesis on this topic.
