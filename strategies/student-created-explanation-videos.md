# Mentoring students to create their own explanatory videos to nurture knowledge creators and peer discourse

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teaching activity in which teachers mentor students to produce their own videos explaining concepts to peers, positioning students as knowledge creators rather than consumers. The chapter states that "teachers can also mentor students to create their own video to explain to other students their creative ideas and facilitate discourse in the process". Local competition platforms such as the annually held School Digital Media Awards organised by MOE-ETD serve to promote students' technological creations.
