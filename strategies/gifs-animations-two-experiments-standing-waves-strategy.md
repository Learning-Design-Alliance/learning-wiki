# Use gifs, animations and two simple experiments to make pressure and air-displacement profiles of tube harmonics visible

## Description
To make the topic clearer than textbook treatments, the authors recommend three moves: building experiments, using apps, applets and gifs, and discussing interesting related topics. They state: "Sugerimos o uso de gifs e animac ¸˜oes e a utilizac ¸˜ao de dois experimentos que permitem a visualizac ¸˜ao dos perﬁs de variac ¸˜ao de press ˜ao e deslocamento de ar para os modos harmˆonicos de vibrac ¸˜ao." ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two tablets or smartphones with the free apps installed, a semi-open glass tube, speaker, microphone on a rod, and styrofoam balls; the speaker needs power around 10 W for the displacement visualisation.
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated for a semi-open tube; extending to other geometries such as rooms or corridors is stated as future work.

### Target Learners
- mid-level and higher-education physics students in schools with limited laboratory infrastructure

### Target Learning Goals
- distinguishing air displacement from pressure variation and seeing the temporal oscillation of standing sound waves in a tube
