# Deliver dedicated lymphoedema lectures and clinical exposure sessions, including cancer-context teaching by oncologists or nurse consultants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants and authors propose concrete teaching activities: dedicated lectures on lymphoedema recognition and management, teaching in the cancer context delivered by an oncologist or clinical nurse consultant, and real-life clinical exposure. One participant noted "there'd be value in having lectures, like at least, like a lecture about it or, guidelines out there on how to manage and recognise lymphoedema". Two participants described a need for greater clinical exposure to appreciate its significance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to clinicians with lymphoedema expertise to deliver teaching
- Clinical placements where interns can encounter lymphoedema cases
#### Constraints
- Blanket precautions (e.g. avoiding blood pressure or cannulation on at-risk limbs) are not supported by expert consensus in favour of personalised risk-reduction strategies

### Target Learners
- medical interns
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- recognising and managing lymphoedema
- appreciating the significance of timely lymphoedema care
