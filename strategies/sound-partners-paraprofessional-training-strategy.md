# Train paraprofessionals to deliver Sound Partners lessons using a local trainer plus manual and video-based professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's staffing model uses local trainers to prepare paraprofessional tutors: "A local trainer (reading specialist, teacher, or special education teacher) will train the local tutors (educational assistants and paraeducators)." Professional development is provided through the Voyager Sopris Learning website, which "provides a training manual and training videos" including clips of a trainer using main lessons with a student; internet access is required for the training materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access for training materials
- A local trainer who is a reading specialist, teacher, or special education teacher
