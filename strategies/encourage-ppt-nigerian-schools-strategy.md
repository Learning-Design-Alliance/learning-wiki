# Equip Nigerian schools with PowerPoint presentation facilities and train teachers to use them for technology-based subjects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students taught with PowerPoint outperformed chalkboard-taught peers and that the medium benefited high, medium and low achievers while being gender friendly, the article recommends that Nigerian schools adopt PPT, especially for technological and practically oriented courses. It states this "could be achieved if government and other education stakeholders co uld provide IWB (PPT) to schools with adequate infrastructure and training of teachers on its usage".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Government and education stakeholders provision of ICT facilities and adequate infrastructure
- Training of teachers on the usage of the technology
#### Constraints
- The article calls for further empirical studies on different subjects and at different levels to provide a sound basis for integration

### Target Learners
- Junior secondary school students in Nigerian public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic Technology and other technological or practically oriented subjects
