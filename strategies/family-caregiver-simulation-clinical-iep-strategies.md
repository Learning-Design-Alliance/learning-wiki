# Use simulations, dedicated clinical time, and interprofessional education to teach family caregiver collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends specific educational strategies: simulations that include family caregivers "replicates real-world scenarios and allows students to practice communication and collaboration skills in a controlled environment"; enhancing clinical rotations with dedicated time to interact with and learn from family caregivers; and interprofessional education bringing nursing students together with other healthcare professionals to learn holistic approaches that situate family caregivers as essential team members.
