# Include peer and near-peer mentoring structures such as a bridge buddy system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends opportunities for peer and near-peer mentorship, in which senior students mentor incoming students in groups or clusters, providing community and helping new mentors support each other. It cites the Fisk-Vanderbilt bridge buddy system, where incoming students are paired with more established students, noting participants reported increased comfort discussing difficult issues such as harassment, department politics, and imposter syndrome with peers rather than faculty mentors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program structure should create opportunities for senior students to mentor incoming students as the program progresses
#### Constraints
- The ideal scheme will depend on the specifics of the institution

### Target Learners
- incoming bridge program students
- more senior students acting as mentors

### Target Learning Goals
- sense of belonging
- retention in the program and field
