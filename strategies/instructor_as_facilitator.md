# Instructor as Facilitator

## Description
Reducing the power differential between instructor and students by acting more as a facilitator than an instructor fosters a nurturing environment. Providing questionnaires about interests empowers students, enhancing intrinsic motivation. Allowing students to bring in their own reading material and present it provides them with an opportunity to interact with and share culturally relevant stories, thoughts, and ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness of the instructor to relinquish some control, time for student-led presentations and activities, questionnaires or surveys.
#### Constraints
- May require significant adjustments to traditional teaching methods, can be challenging to manage if students are unprepared or unengaged.
