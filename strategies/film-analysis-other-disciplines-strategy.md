# Extend the film-analysis approach to other disciplines such as astronomy, biology and mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors state that in addition to physics, this approach to teaching basic concepts at the non-major introductory level "can be applied in many other disciplines", and Table 6 lists possibilities with example films: Astronomy (Armageddon, Contact, Deep Impact), Biology (Planet of the Apes, Spiderman), and Mathematics (Contact, Pay It Forward, Pi, A Beautiful Mind). They add that the possibilities are really unlimited, naming archaeology, chemistry, computer science, engineering, forensic science, history, law and philosophy. The authors are also writing a physical science textbook incorporating the concept, complete with an instructor's CD of analysis notes and a tutorial on analyzing scenes.
