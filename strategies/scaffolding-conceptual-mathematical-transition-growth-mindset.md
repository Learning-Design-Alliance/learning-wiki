# Provide scaffolding at the conceptual-to-mathematical transition and use growth-mindset activities to sustain persistence after setbacks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that students struggled and lost confidence at the transition from conceptual modules (m1-m6) to numerical problem-solving modules (m7-m10), the article recommends two interventions: improving instruction on the difficult module and conducting growth-mindset activities. The article states "instructors need to provide more scaffolding to facilitate students in this transition" and that "Conduct activities that develop a growth mindset, which has been shown to be beneficial for student success" could boost confidence. It also suggests messaging students that setbacks are normal and the key is not giving up.
