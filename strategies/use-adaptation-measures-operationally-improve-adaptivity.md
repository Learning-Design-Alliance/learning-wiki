# Use test adaptation measures in operational settings to identify adaptation difficulties and suggest processes for improving adaptivity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends using its three adaptation measures as operational diagnostic tools: after a test form runs, compute the measures from examinee-level test information to locate where adaptation is weak and to guide improvement. The abstract states the measures can "identify difficulties with adaptation and to suggest processes for improving adaptivity." This is an implementable process for assessment teams maintaining adaptive test batteries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to examinee-level test information from the operational test
- The three adaptation measures must be computed for the test in question
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated so far on simulations of adaptive, multistage, and fixed form tests rather than documented operational deployments in the available text

### Target Learners
- examinees taking adaptive, multistage, or fixed form tests

### Target Learning Goals
- improving the adaptivity of assessments so they better match individual examinees
