# Provide delayed explicit and metalinguistic feedback after communicative tasks, using board presentation and whole-class explanation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates a concrete feedback procedure: after each learner finishes a retelling, the teacher presents the learner's simple past tense errors on the board, corrects them explicitly, and provides metalinguistic explanations to the whole class. This delayed, whole-class treatment produced oral accuracy gains equal to online recasting by the final session, and the authors suggest it can benefit the entire class even when only some students participate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Errors are collected during the task and addressed after learners finish
- Metalinguistic explanations must be comprehensible to the learners
#### Constraints
- The article notes students lacking specialized vocabulary and grammar awareness may not be able to make use of metalinguistic feedback

### Target Learners
- low-intermediate adolescent EFL learners in intact classes

### Target Learning Goals
- oral accuracy and explicit knowledge of the targeted structure
