# Use a dynamic practice session with mediated assistance, group ZPD, and collaborative engagement prior to dynamic speaking assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study used a three-class dynamic practice session prior to each DSA, in which learners practiced CRA-based dialogues "first with MA, then with a new arrangement of the GZPD and, finally, with CE". This practice sequence prepared learners for the dynamic speaking assessments and was credited with producing new, more implicit forms of mediated assistance during the DSAs themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dialogues based analogously on learners' own learning situations
- A sequence moving from explicit to implicit forms of mediated assistance
#### Constraints
- Because of the low level of learners a less structured mediation was not possible

### Target Learners
- First-year university L2 learners

### Target Learning Goals
- L2 speaking development
- Self-repair of spoken errors
