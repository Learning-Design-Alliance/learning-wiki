# Budget extra schedule time for scripting and coding in media-production projects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors found that after five months of research, coding the show in the Nightshade scripting language was "the most labor-intensive part of the project," especially for students new to programming. They advise: "if you create your own planetarium show, plan a little extra time into the schedule for troubleshooting code!" Expert mentorship from the Planetarium Director was critical in navigating the computational challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to experienced technical mentorship and realistic schedule buffers for learners new to the platform
#### Constraints
- Steep learning curve when show creators lack prior experience with the platform's scripting language

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate research students
- Novice programmers

### Target Learning Goals
- Coding and scripting skills
- Project management
