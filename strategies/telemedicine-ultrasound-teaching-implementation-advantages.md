# Adopt telemedicine and e-learning formats for ultrasonography teaching where flexibility, accessibility and cost savings matter

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends distance-learning formats as an adjunct for teaching ultrasonography, especially in places that are difficult to access where no schools or universities exist. It lists organizational advantages over classroom lessons: an "Environment free from stressful factors and without judgment", live updates, easy and uniform dissemination of teaching resources, temporal and spatial flexibility for students, and greater accessibility. It also highlights monetary savings, since professionals in rural areas otherwise travel to major centers for training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access for live video classes; attention to each student's particularities, since some students may not adapt to online teaching because of low levels of interaction or a preference for textbooks over screens
#### Constraints
- The need for internet access in order to have live video classes may be a problem for some locations; reading books on an electronic device is not always a pleasant experience for the learner

### Target Learners
- medical students
- residents
- physiotherapy students
- nurses
- professionals in rural or resource-limited areas

### Target Learning Goals
- ultrasound skill acquisition
- image interpretation
- retention of ultrasound knowledge
