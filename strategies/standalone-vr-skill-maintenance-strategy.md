# Use standalone VR for repeated self-directed skill maintenance between certification cycles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends scalable, self-directed VR practice to support skill maintenance between conventional simulation opportunities, given recertification courses attended only once every 3 years. Because the application runs on commercially available hardware without internet or specialized infrastructure, the authors state findings "may be applicable across a range of clinical environments." Future work should integrate VR with conventional SBT and compare directly with mannequin-based simulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must already hold certification and foundational knowledge of the resuscitation algorithm; pre-certification novices were excluded
#### Constraints
- Educational impact (skill retention and transfer) not yet examined; no comparator study; guideline-specific adaptation needed outside Japan

### Target Learners
- already-certified perinatal healthcare professionals with limited resuscitation or simulation opportunities

### Target Learning Goals
- maintenance of neonatal resuscitation proficiency between formal recertification courses
