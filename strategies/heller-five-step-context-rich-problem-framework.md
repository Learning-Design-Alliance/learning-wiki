# Use a five-step framework for context-rich problems: focus on the problem, describe the physics, plan the solution, execute the plan, evaluate the answer

## Description
Heller and colleagues formalized problem-solving tasks into "a coherent framework" instantiated as a stepwise strategy for context-rich, real-world problems (e.g., "you are a stunt driver jumping a series of buses"). Students (1) focus on the problem, (2) describe the physics, (3) plan the solution, (4) execute the plan, and (5) evaluate the answer. The framework "makes explicit use of conceptual knowledge in steps 1 and 2 and connects that knowledge directly to representing the problem mathematically in step 3."
