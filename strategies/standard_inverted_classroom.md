# Standard Inverted Classroom

## Description
Learners are asked to go through lecture videos and other study material that are prerequisites for the next class. Usually, all the aspects of a topic are converted into video lectures. The class time is reserved for practicing the concepts studied at home and to improve the students’ understanding in various ways like a one-to-one interaction with the teacher.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creation of comprehensive video lectures and study materials.
#### Constraints
- Dependent on learner compliance with pre-class work, may not suit all learning styles.
