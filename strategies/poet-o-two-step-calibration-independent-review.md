# Use a two-step calibration and independent review process with standardized training for peer reviewers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "All faculty reviewers should receive standardized training and/or debriefing to ensure reliable and consistent assessment regardless of reviewers." The pilot used a two-step process: a calibration step where all reviewers rated the same lesson and inconsistent items were discussed in a debriefing, followed by an independent review step with two randomly assigned reviewers per lesson. Only one debriefing session per lesson format was needed to reach the defined consistency threshold of 90% agreement within one point of the mode.
