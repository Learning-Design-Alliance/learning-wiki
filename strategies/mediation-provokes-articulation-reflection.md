# Mediation suggesting courses of action that provoke articulation and reflection to lead groups toward more refined solutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article states that "The objective of the mediation is to suggest courses of action that provoke articulation and reflection, and thus lead to more refined solutions." An artificial mediator implements this by intervening in the ongoing group interaction once a meta-cognitive conflict is detected, prompting members to articulate and reflect. This is a concrete intervention recipe the MArCo prototype embodies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computational system able to analyse the ongoing interaction and detect meta-cognitive conflicts before suggesting courses of action
#### Constraints
- Applied to conflicts inherent to group problem solving in planning interactions

### Target Learners
- Groups of learners working on planning tasks collaboratively

### Target Learning Goals
- Articulation, reflection, and refinement of the group's solution to the task
