# Align course experiments and student activities with the China Rank Certificate Test of Computer Software

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building the certification examination into the course itself: lab experiments draw "programming questions from the China Rank Certificate Test of Computer Software, and all students must complete the questions", so students "will advance into the actual combat" of the test. After the course, students are required to take the annual programmers or software designers test, and stronger students receive extra training and are encouraged into contests such as ACM, Baidu star, TopCoder and Oracle Think Quest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to past certification-test programming questions for lab exercises
- A requirement that all students complete the certification questions
- Training and encouragement structures for students with strong learning ability
#### Constraints
- The strategy is tied to the Chinese certification system; the article reports no measured pass-rate improvement

### Target Learners
- undergraduate computer science majors taking object-oriented programming

### Target Learning Goals
- passing the China Rank Certificate Test of Computer Software and building comprehensive programming ability
