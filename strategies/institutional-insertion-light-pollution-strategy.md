# Institutional insertion strategy: network-based academic projects, municipal working groups, national diagnosis, training courses, databases and normative work for light pollution

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To insert light pollution academically, the article leverages UTN's federal structure of more than 30 academic units to channel integrative projects on atmospheric pollution through a network of working groups. Presentation at UTN rectorado jornadas produced inclusion of CL as a work line in PROIMCA (Proyecto Integrador para la Mitigación de la Contaminación Atmosférica). Proposed activities include forming human resources, organizing "grupos de trabajo comunales o municipales, con objeto de plantear estrategias zonales de mitigación", national-level diagnosis of CL levels, training courses and paper presentations in atmospheric-pollution scientific meetings, a national database, and normative work citing CIE 126 (1997), OTPC (2001), CONAMA (1997) and the Cataluña pilot plan.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination of efforts is important because effects in a zone have implications beyond the local; a genuine solution implies a common strategy
#### Constraints
- Zonal mitigation strategies must consider division into environmental-interest zones and lighting classes by road, since installations vary by site

### Target Learners
- professionals of diverse origin or areas
- municipal and communal working groups
- university teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- building capacity to diagnose and mitigate light pollution
- integrating light pollution into atmospheric-pollution curricula and research
