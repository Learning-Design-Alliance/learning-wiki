Implement Glogster and cooperative learning differentiated instruction through staged group inquiry with tiered, choice-based products¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's implementable recipe: teachers present a many-sided problem from textbook themes, students generate questions that become subtopics investigated in small groups of four or five, groups divide tasks and gather resources, then synthesize findings into glogs. Stage 4 lets groups plan presentations and choose glog formats, so presentations "were all differentiated b y content and product". Teachers act as facilitators and planners, and evaluation uses rubrics accounting for creativity and content gained during inquiry.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training in cooperative learning structures (Jigsaw I, Group Investigation, STAD, Numbered Heads Together, Think Pair and Share), Glogster, and differentiation by content, process, and product

Constraints¶

  • The article reports few teachers need more time to plan for differentiating classes, and some teachers found it difficult to differentiate content, process, and product due to diverse learner abilities

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 8 EFL and Science learners of low socioeconomic status in Lebanese public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Research and oral presentation skills; inquiry and synthesis of findings on a topic

Affordances¶

  • Zpd Tomlinson Di Framework Glogster

Claims¶

  • Teachers identified time demands, class size, and curriculum coverage requirements as the main obstacles to differentiating instruction [-W]
  • Post-treatment survey and pre-treatment interviews indicated a significant positive change in Science and English teachers' perceptions of differentiated instruction [+W]
  • Nearly all surveyed teachers confirmed the Glogster model was effective in increasing students' achievement and enhancing differentiated instruction [+W]
  • Experimental teachers reported the Glogster model, tiered assignments and scaffolding were effective for struggling students, though assessment demanded much effort [+W]
  • After the treatment, 100% of English teachers and 85% of science teachers reported completely changed perceptions of employing differentiated instruction [+W]
