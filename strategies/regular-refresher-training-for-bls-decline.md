# Use regular refresher training to counter significant decline in BLS knowledge and skills at 3 months

## Description
The article states that the observed decline in knowledge and skills at 3 months highlights the need for regular refresher training. This strategy addresses skill and knowledge decay following initial BLS training by scheduling periodic retraining sessions. The authors note this in their discussion of retention findings.

## Design Implications

- Initial BLS training must have been completed before refresher sessions are scheduled
