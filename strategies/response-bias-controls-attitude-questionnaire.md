# Control response bias in attitude self-assessment questionnaires using balanced scales, mixed forward/reverse scoring, and pre-testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To minimize response bias in the attitude section, the article adopted three controls: balanced five-level response options, forward and reverse scoring, and pre-testing. The authors state they "designed forward and reverse scoring to avoid midwives answering questions without reading them carefully," with items 1-7 scored positively and items 8-19 scored negatively. Pre-test results consistent with the interview findings were taken as evidence of small deviation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attitude items must be scorable in both directions
- A pre-test sample must be available before formal administration
#### Constraints
- The authors note self-assessment may still produce positive bias in attitude scores and suggest future reverse-expression items or qualitative integration

### Target Learners
- practicing midwives

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate self-report of attitudes toward liberal birthing position
