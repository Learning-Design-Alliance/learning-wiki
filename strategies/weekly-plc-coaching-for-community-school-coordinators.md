# Build coordinator capacity through a structured weekly professional learning community with personalized coaching and targeted assets-and-needs-assessment training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy organizes new coordinators' professional learning around a weekly PLC with a structured scope and sequence, one-on-one coaching, and collective problem-solving, supplemented by targeted training in conducting assets and needs assessments. The report states that "To help coordinators grow into their roles, Lynwood offered a well-structured, weekly professional learning community (PLC), personalized coaching, and collective problem-solving." The PLC established consistent goals, routines, and practices while coordinators learned to make tailored decisions for their own campuses, and a district community school coordinator provided coaching and professional development across sites.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A system-level position (district community school coordinator) to lead the PLC and coaching; time for weekly meetings; training content covering listening campaigns, administrative data sources, survey design, data analysis, and presenting findings
#### Constraints
- Described in the context of a district scaling 16 new coordinator positions at once with dedicated grant funding for staffing

### Target Learners
- Newly hired community school coordinators in a K-12 district

### Target Learning Goals
- Coordinator capacity to lead community school implementation, conduct needs assessments, and integrate services
