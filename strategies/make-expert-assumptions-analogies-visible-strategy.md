# Make expert assumption-making and analogy use visible to students through curricula informed by expert practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes integrating the characterized expert practices into physics curricula so that processes that are typically invisible become learnable. The authors state that "Elucidating aspects of theorists’ problem-solving practices that instructors can integrate into undergraduate and graduate curricula will allow teachers to better leverage expert approaches for supporting student learning." Because theorists deliberately sought out or constructed analogies, the article indicates analogy use is a skill students can practice, and it offers suggestions for research avenues encouraging more expert-like engagement with assumptions and analogies.
