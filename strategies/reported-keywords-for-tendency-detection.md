# Use reported author keywords rather than statistically derived keywords for research tendency detection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends leveraging keywords already reported by original researchers as a reliable indicator of research tendencies, avoiding computationally intensive statistical keyword extraction. The authors note that "a statistical keyword extraction method was not necessary since the keywords had already been reported by the researchers themselves," citing Conway (2010) that frequent words characterize texts better than derived keywords.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Source articles must provide readily available author keyword lists
#### Constraints
- Not applicable to text types that do not provide a readily available list of keywords

### Target Learners

- research methodologists

### Target Learning Goals
