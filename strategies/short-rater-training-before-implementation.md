# Train raters in the use of the SHORT (e.g., via webinars, e-learning with videos, or live-debriefing ratings) before implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the SHORT's reliability and validity results depend on raters familiar with the instrument, the authors recommend that "specific training in the use of the SHORT should be provided to all raters before its final implantation". They suggest approaches modeled on the DASH team's webinar training, as well as e-learning using videos or followed by live-debriefing ratings to assess raters' familiarity with the scale. A detailed rater guide is provided to support this process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the SHORT scale and its detailed rater guide
- A quality control process and rater familiarity with the assessment tool
#### Constraints
- The study's G and D study results are limited to contexts where raters have a similar level of expertise to the paper's authors

### Target Learners
- Novice and experienced raters of healthcare simulation debriefings

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate, reliable use of the SHORT rating scale
