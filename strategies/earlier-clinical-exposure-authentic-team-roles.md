# Provide earlier clinical exposure with authentic, defined roles for undergraduates in clinical teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a culture shift making undergraduate students legitimate members of clinical teams with defined roles. Students "should be involved in authentic, practice-based learning of all aspects of patient care, learning not only from senior clinicians but also by shadowing the internship doctors inhabiting the role they are being prepared for". This gives students direct experience of internship's actual demands before qualification.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A culture shift in the clinical space granting students legitimate team membership with defined roles
#### Constraints
- Requires students not being under constant pressure from high-stakes assessments so they have time to spend in the clinical space

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the responsibility of the intern role
- authentic practice-based learning of patient care
