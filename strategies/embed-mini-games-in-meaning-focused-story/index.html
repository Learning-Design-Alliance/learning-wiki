Embed controlled grammar mini-games in a meaning-focused story read in class¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study embedded mini-games in a mystery story based on the early history of Coca-Cola, read and discussed in class, so that controlled practice was embedded within meaning-oriented L2 use. Practice sentences were drawn from the mystery story, learners were introduced to the activities in class, and had opportunities to practise further at home, with practice behaviour logged.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reading and discussion activities concerning a mystery story in which mini-games are embedded

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Secondary education learners of L2 English

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Acquiring constraints on grammatical features of English

Affordances¶

  • Mini Games For Focused L2 Practice

Claims¶

  • Mini-game-supported controlled L2 practice improves accuracy and response times on a timed grammaticality judgment test relative to a control group [+W]
  • Mini-game-supported controlled L2 practice transfers to written productive use, with higher accuracy on a written discourse completion test [+W]
  • Time spent on mini-game practice predicts oral elicited imitation accuracy for learners aware of the grammar focus [+W]
  • Transfer from mini-game practice to spoken production is smaller than transfer to written tasks [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use oral elicited imitation tasks with automatic speech recognition as meaningful spoken practice
  • Embed metalinguistic corrective feedback briefly within communicative tasks without disrupting communicative flow
