# Promote physical activity through a multilevel approach combining school, family and community interventions with policy and environmental changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
For promoting activity among students who might have a disadvantaged background, the article endorses "a multilevel approach to promoting physical activity", combining school-based interventions with family or community involvement and pairing educational interventions with policy and environmental changes. It points to guidelines recommending community-wide health education campaigns, school-based physical education, social support in community settings, and active transport such as walking and bicycling to school.
