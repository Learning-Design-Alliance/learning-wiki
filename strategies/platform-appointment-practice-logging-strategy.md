# Platform-based flexible practice scheduling with logged attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An implementable tactic for skills practice: the training base publishes available practice times on a learning platform, and nurses book sessions around their work schedules. "Nursing staff could appoint practice time on the learning platform based on their work schedule," and nurses log in and scan a QR code during practice so the backend records each person's practice time and frequency. The article says this video-supported, flexible access respects nurses' time and encourages self-directed learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning platform with appointment publishing, QR-code check-in, and backend logging
- On-site guidance staff taking turns to ensure practice quality
#### Constraints
- The article links the strategy to reduced instructor burden and resistance, but its outcome evidence comes from the system as a whole, not this component in isolation

### Target Learners
- In-service hospital nurses balancing training with clinical work

### Target Learning Goals
- Adequate deliberate practice frequency for nursing operation skills
- Self-directed learning
