# Institutions and supervisors should improve doctoral students' well-being and engagement rather than using fear and stress as motivators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that universities and supervisors support doctoral students' mental well-being and engagement to promote academic success, instead of scaring students with dropping out. It lists concrete supervisor practices: "(1) checking students' mental health status regularly, (2) maintaining enthusiasm for learning but with regular breaks, (3) improving connections and mutual support among doctoral students, (4) providing sufficient resources and in-time support to doctoral students in their academic journey, and (5) improving the curriculum, and training according to students' needs". It also calls for training supervisors and teaching staff in how to support students' studies and emotions, and for parents to provide emotional support.
