# Schools should clearly communicate the animation course's aims and promote the potential benefits of participation to increase family sign-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A recruitment recommendation drawn from the trial's process findings: "To increase the proportion of families who sign up to the animation course, it is important that schools clearly communicate its aims and promote the potential benefits of participation." This addresses the very low take-up observed in the trial, where most eligible families did not attend. The report also concludes that introducing the programme more generally may be difficult where schools are not committed to parental engagement or have difficulty delivering activities out of hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must be committed to parental engagement and able to deliver activities out of hours
#### Constraints
- Recruitment of schools was itself difficult; the report suggests scale-up may be hard where these commitments are absent

### Target Learners
- families of primary-aged pupils in disadvantaged areas

### Target Learning Goals
- parental engagement in children's learning
