# Pair intervention evaluation with cost-effectiveness analysis to guide scaling to all students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project pairs impact evaluation with cost-effectiveness analysis so administrators can implement programs that impact the most students at the lowest cost. The authors argue that knowing, for example, that faculty mentoring is critically important is insufficient without knowing "how costly an intervention would be to scale to all students who enter UMBC." This component is intended to inform UMBC and other universities about best practices in promoting retention at the lowest possible cost.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cost data must be collected alongside effectiveness estimates for each intervention
#### Constraints
- UMBC's existing successful scholarship programs can serve only a limited number of students due to cost

### Target Learners
- University administrators designing STEM retention programs
- entering STEM-intending undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- STEM retention and graduation at lowest net cost
