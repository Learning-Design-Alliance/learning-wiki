# Revise proposals by removing dead wood, avoiding generic opening statements, writing short clear sentences, and playing the critical reviewer of your own draft

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete revision recipe: create an outline, write a quick complete draft, then spend the remaining time revising. The article advises to "play the role of 'critical reviewer' to your own proposals as you revise it", remove everything not relevant, avoid generic statements that tell the reviewer nothing new, replace filler phrases, limit jargon and abbreviations, avoid typos, and break large text blocks with text boxes or figures. Feedback from colleagues outside the field is part of the loop.

#### Requirements
