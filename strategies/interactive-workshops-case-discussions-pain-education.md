# Interactive small-group workshops with case study discussions as the educational format for changing nursing practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention's educational component consists of workshops defined as "an interactive activity aimed at promoting learning about a pre-set subject or topic", conducted partly in working groups and partly as free discussions. Each workshop lasts 180 minutes and includes 15 RNs from the intervention ward; the first covers pain anatomy, physiology and pain analysis through case study discussions, the second covers treatments for cancer-related pain with case study discussions. The article states that interactive learning in small groups and learning from personal experience have proved winning blueprints for change.
