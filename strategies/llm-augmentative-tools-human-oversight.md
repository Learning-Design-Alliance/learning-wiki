# Deploy healthcare LLMs as augmentative tools under human oversight rather than autonomous decision-makers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on persistent clinical reasoning and prioritization limitations, the article recommends positioning LLMs as "powerful augmentative tools rather than autonomous decision-makers," whose knowledge recall can enhance clinical decision-making when combined with human oversight and critical evaluation. It states any future educational or clinical use should occur under institutional governance with human professional accountability preserved for all patient-facing decisions.
