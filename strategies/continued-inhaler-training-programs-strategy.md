## Description
The article's closing recommendation is that education must continue on both sides of the clinical encounter: "It is necessary to continue promoting training programs for both patients and healthcare personnel in inhalers and inhalation techniques." The survey's identified knowledge gaps serve as the diagnostic basis for designing such programs, and the authors state the findings will allow implementation of educational and awareness interventions for more effective, personalized disease management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programs should target the specific knowledge gaps the survey identifies, such as critical inhalation steps and device selection criteria
#### Constraints
- Survey data reflect self-perceived knowledge and may not reflect respondents' behavior in actual clinical practice

### Target Learners
- pulmonologists
- primary care physicians
- nurses managing COPD
- COPD patients

### Target Learning Goals
- correct inhalation technique for pMDI and DPI devices
- appropriate inhaler device selection for COPD patients
