# Discussion (Pre-Reading)

## Description
In this pre-reading activity, the instructor prepares several sentences expressing different opinions about the reading topic and places them around the room. Learners then choose the opinion they agree with the most and stand near it. The group of learners agreeing with each opinion prepares reasons for their choice. Finally, learners interact with those from other groups, sharing their opinions and reasons, fostering critical thinking and discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a clear space for learners to move around, pre-prepared sentences with various opinions, and facilitation to guide discussions.
#### Constraints
- May require careful facilitation to ensure respectful and productive discussions. Some learners may be hesitant to share opinions publicly.

- Sentences can be tailored to different proficiency levels. Group sizes can be adjusted based on class size and dynamics.
