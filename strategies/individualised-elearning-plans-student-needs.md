# Recommend individualised eLearning educational plans matched to students’ demands and needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that eLearning provisions be tailored to individual students, arguing that modality preference, personality, and learning style shape engagement and achievement. It states that "individualised eLearning educational plans should be recommended depending on the student’s demands and needs", grounded in findings that shy students worried about interacting synchronously while asynchronous-preferring students showed GPA improvement. The recommendation is framed for medical colleges delivering synchronous and asynchronous online teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires information about students’ modality preferences, personality types, and learning styles to tailor provision
#### Constraints
- Grounded in cross-sectional self-report data; the authors call for future interventional research to overcome interaction barriers and enhance performance and understanding

### Target Learners
- Clinical and pre-clinical medical students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining engagement and interaction during online lectures
- Supporting academic achievement (GPA) during eLearning
