# Use curvature and radius of curvature as bridging concepts to strengthen interdepartmental math-physics coordination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its conclusions the article recommends using accessible key concepts to connect apparently disconnected topics: "se recomienda utilizar conceptos clave y relativamente fácil de entender como lo es la definición geométrica de curvatura o aún más intuitivo, el radio de curvatura, para fortalecer o establecer un puente entre temas aparentemente inconexos". It further states that transversal didactic sequences between mathematics and physics require strengthening interdepartmental relationships and operationally matching topics to treat them simultaneously, which it calls an enormous logistical challenge.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
