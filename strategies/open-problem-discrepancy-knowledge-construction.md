# Use an open problem with discrepant data to scaffold student knowledge construction in the laboratory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A laboratory strategy in which students analyze data from a familiar experiment that deviates from the textbook model while the instructor scaffolds the resulting knowledge construction: "As students attempt to reconcile this discrepancy, the instructor can provide guidance in the process of validating data, modeling, and experimental design." The exercise was used in a junior level advanced laboratory course with over 200 students during a 5 year span, emphasizing scientific writing, experimental techniques, and critical thinking about data supporting models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An open problem whose data deviate from the textbook model, requiring accuracy beyond a typical 8-bit laboratory oscilloscope
#### Constraints
- The instruction described above is a fruitful but time intensive endeavor; a shorter alternate exercise saves course time but does not allow practice in modeling and experimental design

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics laboratory students
- senior design students

### Target Learning Goals
- constructing new knowledge
- validating data
- modeling
- experimental design
- critical thinking about data supporting models
