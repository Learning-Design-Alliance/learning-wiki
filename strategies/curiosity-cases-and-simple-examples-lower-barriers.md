# Cultivate curiosity and lower psychological barriers by opening each project with real-world motivation cases and simple accessible AI examples

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At the base of the curriculum pyramid, curiosity and self-motivation are intentionally cultivated in the general introduction and at the start of each project module. The general introduction contrasts the limits of theory-based thermal modeling with AI solutions and presents real-world cases comparing salary levels, career path diversity, and professional ceilings between traditional ME graduates and those with AI expertise. The article states that "simple accessible examples are provided to demonstrate that implementing AI methods can be easy and straightforward, thereby reducing psychological barriers to learning and encouraging active participation."
