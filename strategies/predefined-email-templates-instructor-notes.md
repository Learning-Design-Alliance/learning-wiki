# Support instructors during execution with predefined email templates and instructor notes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that designers "provide predefined email templates covering a range of possible replies to trainees" and provide instructor notes detailing actions and inputs requiring instructor assistance. This reduces instructor workload during the exercise and serves as a guide for novice instructors and a refresher for experienced ones.
