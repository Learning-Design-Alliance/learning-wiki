# Offer trainees feedback-seeking opportunities with people who can serve as coaches aligned to their career aspirations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that programs create opportunities for trainees to seek feedback from people who can act as coaches in light of career aspirations, since residents approached high-effort feedback as goal-oriented connection rather than performance review. As they conclude, "If systems are put in place that offer trainees opportunities to seek feedback from people who can serve as coaches in light of their career aspirations, they may be more likely to actually pursue and value such opportunities."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback providers whose goals align with the trainee's developmental goals; longitudinal relationships rather than one-off context-specific feedback.
#### Constraints
- Study limited to one context (simulated team training), one learner group (pediatric residents), one institution; generalizability may be limited.

### Target Learners
- residents
- medical trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- feedback-seeking
- self-directed learning
- continuous skill improvement
