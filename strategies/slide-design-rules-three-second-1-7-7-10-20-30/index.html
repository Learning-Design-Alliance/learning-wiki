Apply slide design rules to limit text and enhance visual impact¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends several slide design rules: "the three-second rule, i.e., information on each slide should be limited to the amount that the audiences can understand within three seconds"; "the 1-7-7 rule, i.e., each slide should contain no more than seven lines of text and no more than seven words per line"; and "the 10/20/30 rule, i.e., 10 slides should be presented in 20 minutes with no font smaller than 30 points." These rules are grounded in Miller's (1956) finding that short-term memory processes about seven chunks of information.
