# Offer adaptive learning pathways tailored to learners' prior ultrasound experience level

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that novices gained more knowledge while experienced learners improved less, the authors recommend differentiating future course iterations by experience. They suggest "novice learners may benefit from additional foundational modules covering ultrasound basics before advancing to more complex RUSH protocol applications," while experienced learners receive advanced case-based exercises. Instruction should start with straightforward tasks and gradually increase complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Information on learners' prior ultrasound course experience
- Modular course structure allowing differentiated pathways
#### Constraints
- This is a future-directions recommendation, not a design feature tested in the reported course

### Target Learners
- Novice ultrasound learners
- Experienced ultrasound learners

### Target Learning Goals
- RUSH protocol knowledge acquisition
- Engagement and knowledge retention across experience levels
