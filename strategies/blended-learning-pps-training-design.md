# Design PPS training as blended learning with short, concise online modules and in-person workshops focused on role-play skills practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's evaluative synthesis found blended learning delivery was well received, with online materials providing "good theoretical preparation for the in-person training days". Future programmes should keep online modules short and concise and reserve face-to-face time for skills development via role play, particularly explaining explanatory models, with opportunities to discuss difficult cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational e-learning elements paired with in-person workshops focusing on skills development via role play
#### Constraints
- The review notes current time constraints and pressures on health services may prevent development and implementation of a comprehensive PPS training programme; e-learning lasting up to 7 hours was reported as extensive and time consuming

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary care healthcare practitioners, including GPs, GP residents and medical specialists

### Target Learning Goals
- communication skills for PPS consultations
- confidence and self-efficacy in managing PPS
- providing plausible biopsychosocial explanations for symptoms
