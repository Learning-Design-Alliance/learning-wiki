# Involve preservice teachers in constructing and analyzing their own low-cost experiments as simultaneous physics learning and teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends having future teachers themselves experience device construction, definition of experimental conditions, measurement, data analysis, and discussion of limitations, rather than using a previously designed activity. The authors state that when devices are built by preservice teachers, "experimentation simultaneously becomes a strategy for learning Physics and a teacher-training experience for their future professional practice," developing competencies later adaptable for teaching children and adolescents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly structured meetings to develop the work
- Bibliographic review of prior experimental proposals to define operating principles and select materials
- Attention to boundary conditions, variables, uncertainties, and model limitations despite low cost
#### Constraints
- Low cost should not imply reduced scientific rigor; simple systems require careful attention to limitations of the model used

### Target Learners
- preservice physics teachers in initial teacher training

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental investigation skills
- integration of physics concepts with environmental issues
- pedagogical planning of contextualized experimental activities
