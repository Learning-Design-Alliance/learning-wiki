## Description
The reviewer closes by noting, via Ernest Horn's introduction, that Watkins' method is intended only as a supplement rather than a replacement for broader reading experiences. The proposed method 'is not supposed to displace instruction in literary appreciation and extensive reading from printed materials.' The strategy is therefore to treat direct command-based reading exercises as one strand within a wider program that retains literary and extensive reading.
