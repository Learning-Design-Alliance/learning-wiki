# Incorporate institution-level benchmarking, collaboration process measures, and delayed or performance-based assessments in future CBLC evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend strengthening future evaluations of the CBLC by moving beyond student-level, immediate post-test outcomes. They state that "Future evaluations should incorporate predefined institution-level benchmarking metrics, process measures of inter-university collaboration, and delayed or performance-based assessments", alongside prospectively recording reasons for non-completion and conducting attrition analyses. This addresses the present study's inability to compare individual observer programmes or assess retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prospective recording of reasons for non-completion and attrition analyses where feasible
- Collection of institution-specific identifiers to enable programme-level comparison
#### Constraints
- The present evaluation could not assess programme-level standardisation because observer participants were recorded as a single aggregated group

### Target Learners
- Third-year physiotherapy students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Evaluation of inter-university collaborative learning
