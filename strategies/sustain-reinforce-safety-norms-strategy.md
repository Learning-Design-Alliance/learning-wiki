# Sustain and reinforce restrictive catheter-use norms beyond the project period, using education as a platform for norm dissemination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that quality improvement efforts continue after the funded project phase so that restrictive catheter use becomes part of hospital culture. It argues staff education and training can serve as a platform to disseminate and reinforce new norms and expectations, especially when clinical leaders use them to demonstrate commitment and foster interprofessional collaboration. The recommendation rests on the observed small-to-moderate changes and the finding that only a minority of staff IDs matched across surveys.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continued training, education and policy reinforcement after the formal project phase ends
#### Constraints
- Cultural change is a slow process that could not be completed within the short time frame of the QI programme

### Target Learners
- nurses and physicians in acute care hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and restrictive indwelling urinary catheter use
