# Implement teaching-focused workplace-based peer teaching placements with mentorship, repetition, and role modeling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors provide practical recommendations for anatomy educators implementing similar placements: define structured learning objectives; assign a mentor for continual feedback; provide pedagogical training materials; establish safe spaces; "Build in repetition, and nonteaching time" so students teach the same session multiple times; and have staff "Role model effective and professional practices", including modeling uncertainty to foster intellectual humility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentor allocation
- structured learning objectives
- pedagogical resources
- repeated teaching sessions with nonteaching preparation and reflection time
#### Constraints
- Placement was elective and oversubscribed; authors caution against mandating participation due to community dynamic risks

### Target Learners
- undergraduate veterinary students on workplace placements

### Target Learning Goals
- peer teaching skills
- communication
- professionalism
- tolerance of uncertainty
