# Use playing analytics to understand and adapt game-based learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter recommends that future empirical studies of digital game-based learning employ learning analytics adapted to DGBL, which it says leads to "the emergence of the playing analytics". Through playing analytics, data could be used to develop adaptive systems embracing machine learning for education in the next generation of educational games for secondary education and other educational levels. Research should also account for game components such as Learning Mechanics and Game Mechanics and for disciplinary frameworks.
