# Strengthen women's practical computer knowledge, avoid stereotype-threat-prone situations, and teach functional attribution patterns

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the finding that stereotype threat leads to internal attribution of failure among women, the authors recommend three practical implications: "(a) to strengthen the practical computer knowledge of women, for example, by training them in handling technical difficulties, (b) to avoid stereotype threat-prone situations, such as direct information about comparably better achievements of men in a relevant test, and (c) to strengthen the self-confidence of female students in the computer domain, by teaching them functional and self-protective attribution patterns."
