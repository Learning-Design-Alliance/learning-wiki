# Heterogeneous jigsaw grouping based on GPA level, gender and place of origin

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A concrete grouping recipe for jigsaw work: compose jigsaw groups so that students of different achievement levels, genders and backgrounds work together. The article states "the jigsaw method was implemented with the heterogeneous grouping strategy", basing grouping on third-semester GPA divided into four levels (90–100, 80–89, 70–79, and 69 points and below), gender, and place of origin (in or out of the province). The authors report this strategy helped develop cooperative spirit and teamwork.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to students' prior grade data and demographic information before grouping
#### Constraints
- Grouping criteria were drawn from a specific Chinese nursing-program context (third-semester GPA, provincial origin)

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in mixed-ability classes

### Target Learning Goals
- cooperation and teamwork skills
- peer learning and knowledge sharing
