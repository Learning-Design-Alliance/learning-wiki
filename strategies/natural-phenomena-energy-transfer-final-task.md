# Close the learning path with a research task in which students select a natural phenomenon suitable as a renewable energy-transfer source

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The final part of the second year was dedicated to "considering energy transfer between mechanical or electromagnetic devices and natural phenomena," and students carried out research on which natural phenomena can serve as suitable sources of renewable energy. This task connects the laboratory concepts of resonance and efficient energy transfer to real-world economic, social, environmental and technological implications that the article notes are rarely discussed at school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior laboratory work on resonant systems and energy transfer in mechanical and electrical systems
#### Constraints
- Implemented with a very small group of selected students in an optional extracurricular setting

### Target Learners
- Upper-secondary science students (age 15-18) in physics deepening laboratories

### Target Learning Goals
- Apply understanding of resonance and energy transfer to evaluate natural phenomena as renewable energy sources
