# Analyzing Projects for ELL Support

## Description
Analyzing project-based learning (PBL) projects through the lens of English Language Learners (ELLs) involves identifying the language skills, vocabulary instruction, speaking and listening scaffolds, and language functions needed for students to succeed. This includes determining the skills students will need in terms of language, planning vocabulary instruction, scaffolding speaking and listening skills, and considering the functions of the language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thorough analysis of project tasks, understanding of ELL students' language levels, and planning for targeted language support strategies.
#### Constraints
- Requires additional time and effort in project design, and might necessitate ongoing adjustments based on student progress and feedback.
