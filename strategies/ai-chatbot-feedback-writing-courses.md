# Provide timely automated feedback through an AI chatbot during EFL writing practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
This strategy embeds an AI chatbot into writing courses so that learners receive immediate machine-generated assistance on their drafts rather than waiting for instructor feedback. The article motivates it by the challenges of providing feedback on academic writing at scale and by evidence that ChatGPT-integrated instruction raised learners' writing motivation relative to conventional instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing assignments must be structured so that chatbot feedback can be obtained and acted on during drafting and revision
- Students need orientation on prompting the tool and evaluating its suggestions
