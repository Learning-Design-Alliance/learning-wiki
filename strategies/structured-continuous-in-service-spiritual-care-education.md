# Implement structured, continuous in-service education programmes on spiritual care practices for nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends enhancing nurses' spiritual care competency through "structured, continuous in-service education programmes on spiritual care practices", supported by its finding that in-service education participation was significantly associated with higher competency scores. It further recommends integrating spiritual care modules into preservice and in-service programmes and strengthening institutional support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programmes should be structured and continuous, addressing spiritual care practices, and consider sociodemographic influences on competency
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-institution descriptive cross-sectional study, which the authors state limits generalisability to other healthcare settings

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses at cancer care institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Spiritual care competency: assessing and implementing spiritual care, communication, referrals, attitudes towards patient spirituality
