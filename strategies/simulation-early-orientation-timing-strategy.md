# Hold the patient experience simulation early in orientation, before interns know the staff and location

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Interns recommended keeping the disorientation and anxiety of the exercise by running it early in orientation. One resident commented, "I think doing it early before people know the staff and know the location [is important]." Interns also suggested enhancements such as taking away patients' cell phones, establishing IV access, and clarifying the participant patient-family relationship and history coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduling within the first week of residency orientation, before interns become familiar with ED staff and layout
#### Constraints
- The pain-simulation component lacked credibility for participants and may need rethinking

### Target Learners
- incoming EM interns

### Target Learning Goals
- empathy
- orientation to the patient and family experience of ED care
