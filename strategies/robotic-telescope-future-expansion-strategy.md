## Description
The article's future directions recommend strengthening links with schools worldwide by continuing to provide access to LCO's expanding global network, and developing new facilities next to existing ones. The article states "FT continues to work with schools and teachers worldwide strengthening links with schools from Europe and further afield". For the NSO, it looks forward to the likely development of a 4-metre telescope owned by LJMU located next to the Liverpool Telescope, envisaged to run alongside the existing LT providing even more opportunities for researchers and educators alike.
