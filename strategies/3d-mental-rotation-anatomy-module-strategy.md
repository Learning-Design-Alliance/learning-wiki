# Introduce anatomy learning involving 3D mental rotation of anatomical structures and combined 2D pictures with 3D models to support low-spatial-ability dental students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends supplementing anatomy education for students with low spatial ability by introducing an anatomy module involving 3D mental rotation of anatomical structures, or learning with 2D anatomical pictures and 3D anatomical models. It frames this as potentially improving dental students' spatial ability, noting that "students with low visual-spatial ability might have difficulties in a gross anatomy course, although the correlations were weak."
