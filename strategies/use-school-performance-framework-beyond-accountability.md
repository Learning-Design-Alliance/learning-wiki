## Description
The article recommends that education officials use the framework's information "in a variety of contexts—beyond federal accountability—to better identify and understand student and school needs and make more informed decisions." The strategy treats outcomes, impacts, and processes data as decision-support inputs for state and district officials rather than solely as accountability ratings.
