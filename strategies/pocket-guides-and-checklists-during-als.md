# Use pocket guides or checklists during advanced life support and emphasize evidence-based post-resuscitation interventions in ERC training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given the low overall adherence to ERC standards found in the survey, the authors propose that "the use of pocket guides or checklists during advanced life support might be a suitable way to improve performance with the aim to improve survival after cardiac arrest". They also recommend that course instructors and the ERC point out the importance of evidence-based interventions such as therapeutic hypothermia more prominently in training programmes and teaching material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors and course directors must emphasise the importance of evidence-based interventions during courses; training material must cover them explicitly
#### Constraints
- Currently there is no firm evidence that pocket guides increase the quality of resuscitation; one study recommends them for preoperative evaluation and crisis checklists improved simulated operating-room crisis management

### Target Learners
- Emergency physicians attending ERC-ALS provider and refresher courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct application of guideline-recommended resuscitation steps, including post-resuscitation hypothermia
