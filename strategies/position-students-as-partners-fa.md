# Position students as partners in instruction and assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This line of action recommends engaging students as active partners in formative assessment, including involving them in developing rubrics, justifying answers, analyzing solutions, and participating in peer and self-assessment. The synthesis statement holds that "Effective formative assessment strategies only work if students are engaged in the learning." Engaging students in rubric design also encourages valuing tasks without a single correct approach.
