# Quiet Signals

## Description
Quiet signals are predetermined cues or actions used by teachers to quickly and effectively quiet a noisy classroom. They include methods like flicking the lights, ringing a call bell, raising fingers, or using call-and-response techniques. When consistently implemented, these signals help establish a predictable routine for managing classroom noise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent implementation, clear explanation of the signal to students, practice and reinforcement of expected behaviors.
#### Constraints
- May lose effectiveness if overused, some signals may not work for all students, requires initial training and reinforcement.
