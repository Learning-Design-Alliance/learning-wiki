# Integrate AI literacy instruction into study programs alongside responsible tool design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors argue that fostering responsible AI usage extends beyond tool design because students will continue using general-purpose LLMs regardless. They recommend that institutions integrate AI literacy directly into existing study programs, teaching students to 'criticallyevaluate AI outputs' and recognize the long-term value of cognitive engagement with automated answers, treating responsibility as both a system-side and learner-side challenge.
