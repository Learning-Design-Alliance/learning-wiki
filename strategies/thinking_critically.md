# Thinking Critically

## Description
This strategy encompasses questioning, reflection, and evaluation to understand, solve problems, and make informed decisions. Critical thinking skills are essential for adults in their communities, education, and workplaces. It includes identifying assumptions, organizing, categorizing, interpreting, inquiring, analyzing, evaluating, summarizing, synthesizing, and making decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction in critical thinking skills, opportunities for discussion and reflection, tasks that require analysis and evaluation
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners from educational systems that do not focus on critical thinking. Requires intentional integration into instruction.
