# Provide institutional support for educational technology in schools: ministry provision, expert training, and a utilization manual

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the Ministry of Education provide innovations of educational technology in all schools, especially middle and secondary schools, and that experts and specialists be recruited to give training on innovations of educational technology. It further recommends developing "An educational techniques utilization manual ... to present the rules of selecting the content educational techniques as well as the rules to be considered before, during and after utilization." These strategies address the low availability and skill levels the study found in practicum schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ministry of Education provision of innovations in schools
- Recruitment of experts and specialists for training
#### Constraints
- Schools that provide such modern technologies lack some potentials and resources

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers (student teachers)
- in-service teachers in middle and secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- effective utilization of educational technology innovations and techniques
