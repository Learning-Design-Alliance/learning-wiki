# Deliver RGR instruction via incremental self-paced online professional development completed before and after instruction begins

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The RGR professional development model spreads a small time commitment across the implementation timeline: "PD includes five self-paced, interactive online courses (approx. 6 hours)" with the first two completed before instruction begins and the remaining three after instruction has begun. A 6-hour in-person session is an alternative. This incremental design supports "ease of use" and minimal disruption to teaching schedules.
