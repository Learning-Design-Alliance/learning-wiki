# Design experiments contrasting direct learner offloading versus indirect transience reduction by the presenter

## Description
The article recommends that future research should explore the benefits of allowing an active role for the individual in choosing how and when to reduce transience during complex learning tasks, either through direct offloading or through avoiding transience. It suggests contrasting direct offloading on the part of the learner versus indirect offloading via transience reduction by the presenter, examining impacts on learning of otherwise transient information.
