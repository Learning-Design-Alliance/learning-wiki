# Teachers present and summarize chemistry topics with concept maps and teach students to construct their own maps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that chemistry teachers present and summarize concepts and topics using concept maps, since maps "clearly defined a central idea and allows one to figure out the links among the key ideas more easily". It further recommends students be taught how to construct concept maps on their own in chemistry and other subjects, as a tool for understanding relationships between ideas and making recall and review more efficient.
