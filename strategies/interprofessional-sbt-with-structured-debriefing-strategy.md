# Structure each in situ simulation session as short interprofessional scenario practice followed by extended structured debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A session recipe for in situ simulation in clinical environments: keep the simulated scenario brief and interprofessional, then devote comparable or greater time to a structured debriefing. The article's sessions each ran 30 to 50 minutes, "with 15–20 minutes of interprofessional SBT followed by 15–30 minutes of structured debriefing". This structure let on-duty physicians and nurses practise typical postnatal care challenges and reflect on team performance without leaving the clinical area for long.
