# Combine online and role-playing formats in an inverted classroom for smoking cessation training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend combining the particular strengths of the two tested formats: the online course offers time efficiency and student preference, while the seminar yields higher self-confidence and better SP ratings. Following the inverted classroom model, factual knowledge is transferred online and can then be trained and applied in a subsequent attendance phase. Because both modules were well examined and proven effective, they might be adjusted and combined into an inverted classroom course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
