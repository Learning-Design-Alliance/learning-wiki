# Session-design adjustments suggested by student feedback: shorten or split the manual, add facilitators, schedule the session earlier, and keep facilitator synthesis pauses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Free-text survey responses yielded concrete adjustments for implementing the program session: shortening the manual or dividing the activity into two sessions, adding facilitators for more student–faculty interaction time, and delivering the session earlier in the course so students can revisit the material. Respondents reported that "Pauses for the facilitators to synthesize key points greatly enhanced the learning experience". These are the authors' and respondents' recommendations for future implementations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate facilitator availability and course scheduling flexibility to move the session earlier
#### Constraints
- The rapid pace and time demands of the course limited out-of-class program use, with only 20.5% accessing the program after the session

### Target Learners
- medical students in organ-systems physiology courses

### Target Learning Goals
- grasping complex gas exchange concepts and preparing for examinations
