# Provide at least three repeated practice sessions with expert-answer feedback when training novice raters on standardized assessment scoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structuring standardized assessment training around repeated scoring practice with feedback after each session. Its findings show that "accuracy reached its peak at the third session" and that "one or two practice sessions may be insufficient for novices to achieve proficiency". The strategy applies regardless of whether the lecture is delivered online or offline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to video-recorded patient cases for repeated scoring practice
- Expert-rated answers and feedback provided after each practice session
#### Constraints
- Findings are limited to FMA-LE scoring accuracy on video-recorded cases and may not generalize to in-person administration or other instruments

### Target Learners
- Novice physical therapy students learning standardized clinical assessment scoring

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate and reliable scoring of the Fugl-Meyer Assessment lower extremity motor domain
