Adopt an integrative methodological toolkit using machine learning and computational models to capture SR's multi-level, temporal, and social dynamics¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that research on SR in education move beyond single-tradition methods toward a toolkit combining advanced computational approaches with process-oriented measures such as trace data, log files, eye tracking, and multimodal analytics. It argues: "We further argue for an integrative methodological toolkit, leveraging advanced machine learning and computational models, to capture the multi-level, temporal, and social dynamics inherent in SR." This is presented as crucial for an ecologically valid understanding of SR.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Digital learning environments and learning analytics that enable highly detailed real-time assessments of Learning Activities and SRL processes

Constraints¶

  • Connections between fine-grained cognitive paradigms and broader educational SRL models remain somewhat limited, per the article

Target Learners¶

  • Researchers and designers studying learners across educational contexts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring and modeling self-regulatory processes as they unfold dynamically during learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Research agenda: investigate affective dynamics with within-person, longitudinal, and multi-channel methods
  • Use multimodal data channels combining subjective and objective measures to overcome single-channel constraints in SSRL research
  • Broaden co-regulation methodology using trace data, server logs, episodic coding and multilevel modelling
  • Advance the use of structural-configurational methods such as SNA and ENA toward empirically testable models
  • Model assets and threats to availability by blending inter-individual self-reports with process-oriented intraindividual online measures
  • Leverage self-regulated learning measurement methods in feedback research
  • Build research programs combining studies optimized for internal validity with studies optimized for ecological validity
  • Adopt integrative, longitudinal, multimodal deep-phenotyping designs combining self-report, behavioural tasks, ecological momentary assessment, and physiological measures
  • Decompose aggregate models of group learning using fine-grained behavioural observations
  • Use log data from digital assessment to reconstruct solution processes and identify validity threats, disengagement, and technical obstacles
