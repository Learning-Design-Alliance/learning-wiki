# Structured frameworks can guide effective and ethical LLM integration in nursing education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reviews frameworks proposed to navigate LLM use: the IDEE framework offers "a structured approach for integrating AI into general education settings by identifying desired outcomes, determining appropriate levels of automation, ensuring ethical considerations, and evaluating effectiveness"; a SWOT analysis of ChatGPT integration identifies advantages and challenges; a delegation-framework model guides ethical LLM use; and Goktas et al developed guidelines for GPT-4.0 using the prompt learning method. Collectively these support structured, ethical adoption.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Frameworks require continued refinement and adaptation as the technologies evolve
#### Constraints
- The IDEE framework was not specific to nursing education
- Adoption of theoretical models varies across institutions
