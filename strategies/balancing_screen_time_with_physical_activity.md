# Balancing Screen Time with Physical Activity

## Description
Promote a balance between screen time and physical activity by encouraging children to use screens for active purposes, such as making nature documentaries, videoing sports skills, or playing video dance games. It’s essential to strike a healthy balance between screen time, physical activity, homework, socializing, and sleep.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of healthy screen time habits, access to appropriate resources, support from parents and peers.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful monitoring and balance. Needs consistent reinforcement of healthy habits.
