# Multi-level strategy for strengthening EBM implementation: individual workshops and protected time, institutional access and culture, and system-level public awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a multifaceted approach across individual, institutional, and system levels to facilitate EBM implementation. At the individual level, continuing EBM education through workshops and training should target non-specialists, and protected time should be allocated for EBM activities. At the institutional level, improving access to EBM information sources through subscriptions and IT support, fostering supportive organisational culture, EBM mentoring programmes, and journal clubs are recommended. At the system level, greater public awareness and digital networking platforms for EBM collaboration are proposed.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- Routine application of EBM in clinical decision-making
