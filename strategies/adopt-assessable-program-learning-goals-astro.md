# Adopt formal, assessable program-level learning goals mapped to specific courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report recommends that all academic departments adopt formal program-level learning goals aligned with expected graduate outcomes, encompassing the content knowledge and skills all majors should leave with. Goals "should map to specific (ideally multiple) courses within the major requirements and should be phrased in such a way that they can be easily assessed" internally and by external reviewers or accreditors. Appendix C provides example well-phrased goals drawn from the survey data.

## Design Implications
