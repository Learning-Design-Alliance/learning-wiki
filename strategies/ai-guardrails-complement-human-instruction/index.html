Set clear AI guardrails positioning AI as a complement to human instruction with age-appropriate progressions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's fourth use case describes how Indian Prairie SD's governance was grounded in 'a belief statement and set of principles that positioned the use of AI as a complement, not replacement, to human instruction.' Guidance covered privacy-aligned high school access to generative tools like ChatGPT and Google Gemini, structured learning progressions where younger grades engage with AI ethically and analytically before direct tool use, and explicit teaching about AI limitations, biases, and critical evaluation of outputs—operationalizing Recommendation 4.
