# Use classroom demonstrations (water tank, fire extinguisher on cart) to teach recoil via unbalanced forces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends two simple classroom experiments to explain recoil through unbalanced forces. First, a freely moving water tank with a hole in one side recoils because "the pressure decreases at the hole and thereby the total force acting on the left wall becomes less than the right one." Second, a student holding a fire extinguisher on a cart or bicycle is propelled opposite to the CO2 flow, "demonstrating a classroom implementation of the rocket engine." These demos make the unbalanced-force mechanism tangible.
