For the full study, use additional vocabulary tests, varied podcast types, and qualitative methods for subjective learning progress¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

Based on the pilot's weaknesses, the author plans concrete changes for the full case study (September 2012 – July 2013): "another vocabulary test will be used as well as more tests each time (cf. Nation & Webb, 2011) and different types of podcasts (e.g., videopodcasts)". In addition, "the subjective learning progress of the students will be focused using qualitative research methods". This strategy responds to the critical VKS results and the unexplained convergence of the groups.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Willing Italian teachers participating with their pupils, which the author reports was difficult to find

Constraints¶

  • The pilot showed most students did not create the podcasts due to lack of time and receiving no grade, so the full design must address this liability

Target Learners¶

  • Beginning learners of Italian as an L3 in the Austrian school context

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Lexicon acquisition in Italian as a foreign language

Claims¶

  • At the end of the pilot, experimental-group students more often wrote two-column lists and sample sentences, while control-group students more often copied words from the textbook [~W]
  • After the pilot study the control group still scored higher on the VKS, but the gap between the groups narrowed [~W]
  • At the start of the school year the two groups differed significantly in three vocabulary learning strategies [~W]
  • The Vocabulary Knowledge Scale used in the pilot showed very high internal consistency (α = .96) [+W]
