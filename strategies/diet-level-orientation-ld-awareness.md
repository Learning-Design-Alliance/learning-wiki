# DIET-level orientation to help teachers identify and combat learning disabilities early

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that because CAI enhances the achievement of LD students, orientation be organised at the District Institute of Education and Training (DIET) level so that primary and high school teachers become aware of learning disabilities and can identify and address them at an early stage. The authors frame this as a way to reduce wastage and stagnation in schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers at primary and high school level receive orientation through DIET-level programmes
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is extrapolated from a single Class XI English study; the authors argue effectiveness for LD students should extend to other backward students

### Target Learners
- primary and high school teachers as orientation recipients; LD and other backward students as ultimate beneficiaries

### Target Learning Goals
- early identification and remediation of learning disabilities
