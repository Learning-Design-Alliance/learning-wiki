# Schedule top-up or refresher sessions after short SBE workshops to maintain knowledge gains

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because knowledge gained in a short simulation workshop declined significantly by three months while confidence persisted, the authors recommend scheduling repeat sessions to maintain and build on initial gains. They write: "we recommend "top-up" sessions in the future to maintain and further build on the gains achieved," noting this aligns with literature indicating that repeating teaching over time produces more durable learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial SBE workshop producing measurable knowledge gains to refresh
- Follow-up assessment (e.g., at three months) to identify decay
#### Constraints
- Derived from a pilot with nine voluntarily participating students at a single school; the recommendation follows the observed three-month knowledge decline (p = 0.0104 vs. immediate post-workshop)

### Target Learners
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of clinical specialty knowledge and skills over months
