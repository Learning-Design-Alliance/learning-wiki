# School-based interventions promoting growth math mindset and academic grit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend school-based interventions from primary level that teach that the brain "grows stronger with use", reframe mistakes as brain-building, discourage fixed-math messages like "not everybody can be good at math", and present successful mathematicians as those who loved and devoted themselves to math. Classroom practices include collaborative communities, individual growth-oriented feedback, multiple assessments allowing revision, valuing effort in grading, and questioning students' effort and strategies.
