# Live Writing Demonstrations

## Description
Instructors demonstrate the writing process in real-time, in front of learners, to make the thinking and decision-making involved in writing visible. Using a projector or similar tool, the instructor composes a piece of writing while verbalizing their thought process, revisions, and considerations. Learners observe, critique, and provide input, turning the writing process into a collaborative and transparent activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projector or similar display tool, pre-planned writing task, instructor preparedness to verbalize their thought process. Requires a classroom environment that encourages participation and critique.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant instructor preparation and real-time adaptability. May be challenging for instructors who are uncomfortable writing in front of others. Learners may become passive observers if not actively engaged.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learner levels, particularly effective for secondary and higher education students learning specific writing skills or genres

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Demystify the writing process, enhance understanding of writing strategies, improve learners' writing skills, promote collaborative learning

### Affordances
- Provides a clear and immediate view of the writing process, allows learners to see revisions and decision-making in action, promotes active engagement and collaboration, builds confidence by demystifying writing

### Personalization
- The writing task can be tailored to the learners' current needs and interests. The level of instructor support and guidance can be adjusted based on learner skill levels. Learners can focus on different aspects of the writing process based on their individual challenges.

## Assessment Evidence
- Improvements in learner writing quality as evidenced by better structure, clarity, and use of techniques observed in the demonstration. Increased learner participation in writing activities and peer feedback.

## Impact
- Live writing demonstrations help learners understand how to approach writing tasks, revise their work, and prioritize concision. By seeing the instructor's weaknesses and struggles, learners become less defensive about their own work and more open to criticism.
