Train teachers in the “SQP2RS via WTL” strategy and give students practice opportunities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the study’s results, the authors recommend that “training programs should be offered to train teachers on usi ng the “SQP2RS via WTL” strategy, students should b e provided with opportunities to practice the strategy, and mo re studies should be conducted on different variabl es and different populations.” The recommendation follows directly from the experimental group’s significantly higher adjusted post-test mean. It targets curriculum designers, supervisors and teachers of reading in EFL contexts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training programs covering the strategy’s six steps and their WTL integration
  • Classroom opportunities for students to practice the strategy

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is one quasi-experimental study in Bethlehem district with a purposive sample of two schools

Target Learners¶

  • 10th grade EFL students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • The “SQP2RS via WTL” strategy produced significantly higher 10th-grade English reading comprehension scores than traditional teaching [+W]
