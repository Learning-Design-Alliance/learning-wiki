# Balanced assessment strategies integrating objective testing with oral, practical, and competency-oriented evaluation approaches

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that, from a curriculum development perspective, assessment in competency-based dental education should not rely on a single format. It states that "balanced assessment strategies integrating objective testing with oral, practical, and competency-oriented evaluation approaches may provide a more comprehensive assessment of students' knowledge, clinical reasoning, communication abilities, and professional competencies." This follows from the finding that students preferred oral and practical formats while staff preferred test-based examinations. The recommendation is also framed through backward curriculum design, where learning objectives, teaching activities, and assessment strategies are intentionally developed in an integrated manner.

## Design Implications

#### Constraints
