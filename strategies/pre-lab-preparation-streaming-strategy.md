# Perform all preparative biological steps in advance and stream them to students to minimize in-lab time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before the student session, staff prepare the plasmid and RNA samples and characterize transfected cells; these activities are then "presented to the students and discussed in streaming in order to minimize the time necessary for the experimental activity performed in presence". This keeps the in-presence session short, reduces SARS-CoV-2 transmission opportunities, and allows sequential student streams. The article notes that if COVID-19 restrictions are abandoned, these preparative activities can instead be performed by students under teacher supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff capacity to perform bacterial transformation, plasmid isolation, cell transfection and RNA extraction before the class
- Streaming or recorded presentation of the preparative activities to students
#### Constraints
- Under COVID-19 restrictions students do not hands-on perform the preparative steps; the article states these can become student-performed once restrictions are abandoned

### Target Learners
- university students in applied biochemistry and pharmacology laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding PCR/RT-PCR workflows
- quality control of nucleic acid preparations
