# Use online platforms to connect eastern and western universities for cross-regional interprofessional teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 associational, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete implementation strategy for addressing regional disparities in educational resources: deliver interprofessional education synchronously across geographically distant institutions using a free online teaching platform, under the guidance of an experienced teacher. The article reports that "the interprofessional OFST connected universities from eastern and western China, enabling cross-regional collaboration under the guidance of a national master teacher", and chose Ding Talk "for its automatic screen recording function". Students from resource-rich and resource-limited institutions shared disciplinary perspectives in mixed teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A reliable online classroom platform with features such as screen recording
- Teacher training to familiarise instructors with the model
- Recruitment across institutions with informed consent
#### Constraints
- The article notes it provides only indirect evidence on educational equity and did not directly assess equity-related metrics such as resource access or long-term academic outcomes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students at universities in both resource-rich and resource-limited regions

### Target Learning Goals
- interprofessional collaboration
- broadened disciplinary knowledge
- equitable access to quality instruction
