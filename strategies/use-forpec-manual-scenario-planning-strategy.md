# Use ForPEC and its Operational Manual to plan and elaborate simulation scenarios for training students and health professionals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes with an explicit recommendation: "it is recommended to use ForPEC and its Operational Manual for planning and elaborating scenarios" aimed at training and qualifying students and health professionals in the skills needed for their performance, with consequent benefit for patient safety. The strategy involves completing the 91-item form with manual guidance, piloting the built scenario, and debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the ForPEC form (version 1.3) and its Operational Manual (version 1.2)
- Completion of a pilot/test of the built scenario
#### Constraints
- Elaboration was reported as time-consuming due to the need for detailing
- the paper format limited registration space

### Target Learners
- students and health professionals in medicine, nursing, physiotherapy and other healthcare specialties

### Target Learning Goals
- technical and non-technical clinical skills
- patient safety
