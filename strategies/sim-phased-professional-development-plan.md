# Phased SIM professional development plan: initial planning days, monthly site PD and coaching, monthly leadership-team sessions, and annual program-specific training with coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review prescribes a concrete PD schedule for adopting SIM: 2-3 days of planning activities; site professional development of 2-3 days per month combining PD and coaching; Literacy Leadership Team PD and work sessions of 1 day per month; and Xtreme or Fusion Reading PD of 5 days per year plus coaching. This schedule supports the phased adoption in which staff work through planning, implementing, and sustaining a literacy improvement initiative over three to five years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time and scheduling capacity for monthly site PD, coaching, and leadership-team work sessions
#### Constraints
- Xtreme or Fusion Reading PD is specific to the targeted programs at 5 days per year plus coaching

### Target Learners
- secondary content and support teachers
- school leadership teams

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher implementation of SIM routines and learning strategies
