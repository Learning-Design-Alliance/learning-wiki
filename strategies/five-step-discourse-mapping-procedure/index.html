Five-step discourse mapping procedure for representing a contested knowledge debate¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper offers a implementable recipe for social cartography: choose the debate to map; select the widest range of texts and translate their defining rhetorical characteristics; identify the range of positions on chosen ontological axes; locate and interrelate textual communities while "resisting all modernist urges to box-in or lay down a grid"; and field test the map with the communities involved, re-mapping as desired. The author presents this "cookbook" fashion process as an oppositional postmodern strategy that translates across interacting sites of material inscriptions.
