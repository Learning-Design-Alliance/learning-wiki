# Review the evaluation plan and possibilities for further research when funding is restored

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's authors planned for continuation: it "reviews the evaluation plan and possibilities for further research if funding is restored." This treats a disrupted evaluation as resumable, preserving the design documentation so future research could build on the existing treatment-control framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Restoration of funding for the program and study
#### Constraints
- The program and study were scheduled to conclude in 2009 absent restored funding

### Target Learners
- Middle and high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating a program connecting school experiences to aspirations for adulthood
