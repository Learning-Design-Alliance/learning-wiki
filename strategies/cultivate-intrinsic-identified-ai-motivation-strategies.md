# Cultivate identified and intrinsic AI motivation through curriculum integration, activities, workshops, and real-world AI applications

## Description
Because introjected regulation dominated the AI motivational system but is not the most optimal form of motivation, the authors recommend that "educators are encouraged to implement strategies that help students nurture their identi ﬁed and intrinsic motivation." Concretely, they propose incorporating AI-related content into curriculum design, organizing AI-related activities and workshops, and introducing real-world applications of AI, listing specific activities such as AI knowledge competitions, project-based AI learning, AI-powered games, and AI ethics debates, and embedding topics like natural language processing, machine learning, and AI ethics into non-technical subjects.
