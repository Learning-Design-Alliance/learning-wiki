# Monitor modifiable risk factors for poor academic performance during distance education and support students through university counselling services

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends ongoing monitoring of distance education's impact on student well-being and academic outcomes, targeting the modifiable variables it identified as compromising performance, such as concentration impairment and depressive symptoms. It states the need "to refine educational offerings and meet students' psychological needs by implementing psychological interventions based on the modifiable variables". University counselling services are assigned a fundamental role in supporting students facing emotional-psychological distress, focusing on individual vulnerabilities.

## Design Implications
