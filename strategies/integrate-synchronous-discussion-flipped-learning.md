# Integrate synchronous discussion tools into online flipped learning environments to maximize engagement and self-regulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that integrating synchronous online discussion tools (e.g., BigBlueButton) into online flipped learning environments creates a new interactive learning experience. In this design, students watch pre-recorded lectures before class, then participate in real-time discussions during synchronous sessions. The combination produced the highest engagement and self-regulation outcomes in the study. The authors state this "contributes to the online flipped learning approach by adding a new perspective, namely synchronous online discussions."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a video-conferencing discussion tool integrated with the LMS (e.g., BigBlueButton within Moodle)
- Instructor preparation of pre-class video lectures and discussion prompts
- Rubric-based assessment of discussion participation to ensure quality contributions
#### Constraints
- Effects were demonstrated in a quasi-experimental design with undergraduate math students; generalizability to other populations is not established
- The interaction effect on engagement was small-to-medium and observed power was below .80 (.66 and .67)

### Target Learners
- Preliminary undergraduate students in basic math courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Student engagement (behavioral, emotional, cognitive)
- Self-regulation
