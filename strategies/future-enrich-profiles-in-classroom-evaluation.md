# Future system development: enrich learner profiles with language background, attentional variability, and neurodiversity, and run in-classroom evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors state a forward-looking agenda for extending FACET beyond the current sparse-prompt prototype. Their "future vision is to enrich learner profiles with additional dimensions such as language background, attentional variability, and neurodiversity (e.g., ADHD, dyslexia)", and they plan "large-scale, in-classroom evaluations using outputs tailored to real learner profiles." They also intend to extend teacher agents with operators and motivational strategies grounded in educational theory and embed full curriculum specifications into prompts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher agents extended with theory-grounded operators and full curriculum specifications in prompts
#### Constraints
- Current prototype used intentionally minimal prompting and was not tested in authentic classrooms

### Target Learners
- diverse K-12 students including those with neurodiversity such as ADHD and dyslexia

### Target Learning Goals
- authentic, individualized instructional design validated under real classroom conditions
