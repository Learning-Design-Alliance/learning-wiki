# Revise the dental curriculum to better address cancer-treatment complications and add periodic practical visits to head and neck cancer hospitals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend revising the dental curriculum so that it better addresses oral complications of cancer treatment, complemented by periodic practical hospital visits that expose students to real cases. They state, "We recommend revising the current curriculum to better address cancer treatment complications, thereby enhancing students' knowledge and awareness. Periodic practical visits to head and neck cancer hospitals may also be beneficial." This responds directly to the low good-knowledge rates the survey measured, particularly among pre-internship students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The strategy targets dental students before and during internship, where the article locates the knowledge gap
#### Constraints
- The article notes the survey was not designed to identify curriculum gaps, and recommends a qualitative study to determine the appropriate stage for introducing this knowledge

### Target Learners
- Senior dental students (years 5 and 6)
- Dental interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of oral complications of head and neck cancer therapy
- Oral care and health management for cancer patients
