# Strengthen ICU eye care through optimised protocols, clear standards, multidisciplinary collaboration and enhanced training programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that clinical managers improve eye care for critically ill patients by system-level and educational actions together. It states that "future efforts should focus on improving protocols and standardised procedures for eye care in critically ill patients, establishing clear standards and enhancing multidisciplinary collaboration mechanisms" and that there is a need to "strengthen training programmes and encourage continuing education". Clinical nurses are advised to enhance their own eye care learning and attend to ocular complication risks. The recommendation rests on the survey's findings that training experience and material consultation were the primary factors associated with competence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programmes and continuing education opportunities must be available to ICU nursing staff
- Hospital protocols and standardised procedures for eye care must be established
#### Constraints
- The article notes eye care implementation in China requires a doctor's orders, and eye care is not included in the scope of hospital nursing core policies

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses
- critical care nursing staff

### Target Learning Goals
- eye care knowledge for critically ill patients
- prevention of ocular complications in ICU settings
