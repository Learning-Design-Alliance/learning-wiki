# Use a two-step debriefing process: immediate written self-debrief followed by a large group debrief

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After a series of studies, the team's solution for debriefing students who play VGS at their convenience is a two-step process: students complete an immediate written self-debrief referring to their individualized summary report, then within two weeks participate in a facilitated large group debrief using the same questions. The authors state they "determined our solution: a two-step debriefing process".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive an individualized summary report of their decisions at the end of the game
- Group debrief questions are the same as the questions on the self-debrief
#### Constraints
- The first year the VGS was introduced without a required debrief, and the focus group study suggested students wanted to reflect after the experience

### Target Learners
- nursing students in didactic courses

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on simulation experience
- exposure to other perspectives through group discussion
