# Use brief pre-review training videos emphasizing scale meaning, consequences of scoring, and careful criteria reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend specialized peer reviewer training delivered as a brief video just before reviewing. The video teaches the meaning of each rating scale value, the definitions of minor, moderate, and major weakness, reverse coding, how anchoring bias from academic grading habits inflates scores, and why minor score differences affect funding. It frames reviewers' role as consequential and asks them to read criteria carefully.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video explicitly teaching scale value meanings and the consequences of inaccurate scoring, administered close in time to the review task
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated only rating scale interpretation and application with limited, concrete information; the authors state results probably reflect a best-case scenario and moving to abstract judgments of quality and merit would likely decrease reliability

### Target Learners
- Grant peer reviewers, novice and experienced

### Target Learning Goals
- Correct selection of rating scale values
- Increased attention to review criteria
- Reduced anchoring bias from academic grading habits
