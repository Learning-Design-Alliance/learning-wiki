# Match passage difficulty to students' English proficiency and check readability before teaching academic content-area reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that English teachers measure the difficulty level of academic passages and adjust them to students' English proficiency, using online readability software to match passages to learners. It states "the levels of passages' difficulty should be adjusted to the levels of students' English proficiency", otherwise passages become incomprehensible and lessons boring and stressful. It also recommends assessing students' English proficiency before the academic year to plan instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of students' English proficiency levels before instruction
- Use of online readability software to gauge passage difficulty
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of EFL academic content-area reading at the tertiary level

### Target Learners
- non-English department tertiary students in EFL contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- comprehension of academic English passages
