# Combine cognitive stimulation with racial socialization by selecting enriching activities that highlight African American art, history, and culture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy proposes that enriching home learning activities for African American adolescents should also serve racial socialization, so that a single activity supports both cognitive development and racial identity. The article states that "Reading books, visiting museums, and engaging in cultural activities that highlight African American art, history, and culture may serve the dual purpose of cognitive stimulation and racial socialization" and that "Activities that combine racial socialization and cognitive stimulation may be even more beneficial for fostering academic achievement." It is offered as a direction for future research and practice rather than a tested manipulation.
