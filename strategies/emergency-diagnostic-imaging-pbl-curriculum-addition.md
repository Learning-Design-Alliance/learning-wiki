# Include emergency diagnostic imaging via problem-based learning simulation in the clerkship curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because diagnostic imaging documentation correlated only weakly and non-significantly with clerkship performance, the authors suggest a curricular addition: "the inclusion of emergency diagnostic imaging using a problem-based learning simulation in the curriculum may be warranted." The rationale is that students are less likely to have direct clinical experience with emergency diagnostic imaging compared with informed consent and resuscitation situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problem-based learning simulation scenarios for emergency conditions such as brain hemorrhage, tension pneumothorax, and cardiac tamponade
#### Constraints
- This is an author recommendation based on a non-significant correlation, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- Medical students during clinical clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- Emergency diagnostic imaging documentation and differential diagnosis
