# Pair AI-supported PBL sessions with brief learner orientation and a short postsession debrief

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because simulated group-process behaviors varied across sessions, the article recommends that "implementation may therefore benefit from brief learner orientation (expectations for participation, justification, and turn-taking) and a short postsession debrief to normalize reflection and calibrate engagement." This strategy addresses the observed heterogeneity in interactional formative dimensions (AP, CC, SR) by making participation norms explicit before the session and consolidating reflection afterward, complementing the AI environment's own regulatory scaffolding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short orientation covering platform workflow, tutor and peer roles, and participation expectations, plus a brief debrief after the session.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the orientation in its own trial was considered part of the intervention package and had no time-matched counterpart in the control condition, so its isolated contribution was not established.

### Target Learners
- Medical interns using AI-supported PBL environments

### Target Learning Goals
- Participation and turn-taking norms
- Reflection and engagement calibration
