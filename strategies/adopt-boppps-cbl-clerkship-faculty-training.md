# Incorporate the BOPPPS-CBL method into clinical clerkships with faculty training to ensure effective execution

## Description
The article recommends that educators adopt the combined BOPPPS-CBL approach in clinical and dental programs. It states that "educators could incorporate the BOPPPS-CBL method into their clinical clerkship, with faculty training to ensure effective execution", and that policymakers might advocate its inclusion in national educational standards. The authors also suggest the method could extend into other clinical disciplines such as orthodontics or prosthodontics, advancing teaching practices in healthcare education more broadly.
