# Expressive Outlets

## Description
This strategy encourages learners to express their emotions through journaling, drawing, or talking out loud. By externalizing their thoughts and feelings, learners can gain perspective, separate facts from emotions, and reflect on how to handle problems effectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Journal, art supplies, or private space; facilitator (optional)
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be hesitant to express emotions; requires a safe and supportive environment; effectiveness depends on learner's willingness to engage

### Affordances

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred expressive medium; provide prompts for reflection; encourage creative expression
