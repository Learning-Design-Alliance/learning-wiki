# Daily Class Schedules

## Description
Structuring the school day with specific time allocations for various subjects and activities. This strategy involves creating a consistent, workable schedule that maximizes instructional time and caters to students' energy levels and needs throughout the day. Schedules may include morning work, language arts, reading groups, specials, math, science/social studies, and recess, strategically arranged to optimize learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding of subject mandates, knowledge of student needs and energy levels, and ability to allocate time effectively for each subject.
#### Constraints
- May require adjustments based on unforeseen events or student progress. Can become rigid and less adaptable if not regularly reviewed and revised.

### Target Learners

- Elementary grade students, particularly in classrooms where effective time management is crucial

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Maximize instructional time, optimize student engagement, cater to student energy levels, provide structure and predictability

### Affordances
- Helps students anticipate the day's activities, reduces time wasted on transitions, and allows for focused instruction in each subject. Enables teachers to plan effectively and address all curriculum areas.

### Personalization
- Schedules can be adapted to accommodate varying subject priorities and student needs, such as incorporating breaks or activities to address attentional challenges. Flexible pacing within subjects can further tailor the experience.
