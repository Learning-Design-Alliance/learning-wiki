# Use physical design probes to elicit customer feedback on subjective requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Design probes are simple mockups or samples given to the customer so they can try something physical and give feedback, which the article says is instrumental when requirements are subjective. In the fidget cube case, "The team used a set of simple taped together blocks of wood as an initial design probe to quickly assess which one the customer liked the best," leading to iterations on edge curvature and finally a clear-resin print so the whole cube lit up. The authors argue this iterative process would not happen in a self-ideated project, so students miss learning to use design probes.
