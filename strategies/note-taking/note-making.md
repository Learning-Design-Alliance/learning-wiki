# Note-Taking/Note-Making

## Description
Note-taking/note-making involves students taking notes on key points from a lesson and then making notes by writing their own reflections, reactions, and personal connections to the material. This strategy encourages active engagement with the content and helps learners integrate new concepts with existing knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured guidance for note-taking, time for reflection and note-making, and a format for recording both types of notes (e.g., divided paper, digital document).
#### Constraints
- Requires clear guidance on note-taking and sufficient time for thoughtful reflection. Effectiveness depends on learners' ability to make meaningful connections.
