# Use consensus-derived teamwork competencies to develop a European curriculum for community-based interprofessional team specialists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking plan is that the consensus competencies feed curriculum development: "These competencies will subsequently be used to develop an EU Curriculum to adequately prepare a new cohort of healthcare professionals specialised in Community-Based Interprofessional Teamwork." The curriculum will be tested through four pilot programs in Ireland, Italy, Greece, and Poland and released on an Open platform to support implementation in other countries. A supporting appendix of competencies aims to support an online Community of Inquiry platform to be developed in parallel with the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Competencies aligned with European skills classification and crediting systems (ESCO, EQF, ECVET) to ensure transferability
#### Constraints
- The curriculum and CoI platform are planned next phases of the TEAMCARE project, not yet evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- Health and social care professionals specialising in community-based interprofessional teamwork

### Target Learning Goals
- Person-centred, interprofessional teamwork competencies for community-based care
