# Regular reminder tags after the intervention to sustain positive behaviour change in pain management

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
To maintain possible positive behaviour following the intervention, the article plans reminder tags set up on a regular basis to inform nurses about the most recent guidelines for pain management. This follows the article's statement, citing Ajzen, that persuasive communication theory can also be used to change a form of behaviour, addressing the stated risk that positive improvement in behavioural change may not be sustained.
