# Complement open robot inquiry with written reports, presentations, or parallel structured tasks to make grading feasible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the open, exploratory robot exercise has a variety of possible solutions, the authors warn that 'the diﬃculty to deﬁne criteria to grade a student's performance, due to the unstructured na- ture of the activity' is a caveat. They recommend complementing the activity with a written report or a presentation, and note that parallel structured activities such as sensor calibration or building a speedometer could simplify grading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher willingness to invest extra preparation work, which the authors state this activity requires
#### Constraints
- The grading difficulty stems from the unstructured nature of the activity and the variety of possible solutions, as stated by the authors

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- communication of experimental findings
