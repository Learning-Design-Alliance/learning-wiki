# Implement a systems change approach addressing five factors simultaneously for AI and digital equity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Digital Promise works with educational leaders to implement a systems change approach to both AI and Digital Equity. The brief states that "five factors must be simultaneously addressed: leadership; resources and policies; consistent access; AI literacy and professional development for teachers; and design of powerful learning experiences for students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simultaneous attention to leadership; resources and policies; consistent access; AI literacy and professional development for teachers; and design of powerful learning experiences for students
#### Constraints
- Requires work with educational leaders as the implementing actors

### Target Learners
- students
- teachers
- educational leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- powerful learning experiences with AI that are safe, equitable, and meaningful
