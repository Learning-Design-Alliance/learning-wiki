# Place mannequins and self-study resources in each community midwife's practice setting with virtually proctored practice sessions by peer mentors to reduce dropout at scale-up.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For scaling and sustaining the intervention when trainees are scattered at distant locations, the article recommends embedding practice materials locally instead of repeatedly gathering trainees centrally. The study states that "the study recommends the placement of mannequins and self-study resources in each CMWs’ practice setting with virtually proctored practice sessions by peer mentors," and the conclusion suggests weekly proctored practice under peer supervision in a blended modality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mannequins and self-study resources available at each practice setting
- Peer mentors able to virtually proctor practice sessions
#### Constraints
- The article notes shorter two-day trainings, as participants requested, may lead to further dropouts, as occurred on the last training day with the fewest participants

### Target Learners
- Community midwives working at distant, independent birthing centers

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained neonatal resuscitation skills retention
