# Use OBC RFP templates with market validators and ESSA evidence tiers as efficacy criteria

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation provides OBC RFP template language in which applicants demonstrate efficacy through one or more criteria: holding "strong (Tier 1), moderate (Tier 2) or promising (Tier 3) evidence under ESSA" for the district's target student population, showing a track record of achieving payment outcomes with a supporting theory of change, or earning alignments, badges, or certifications. Districts can adopt this template language directly in their own RFPs to make evidence a scored procurement criterion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- RFP definitions for efficacy, digital equity, and accessibility must be available when rating the indicator
#### Constraints
- The certification criterion applies only "If applicable" and is one of several alternative efficacy routes, not a sole requirement

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, with particular focus on key subgroups such as English learners and students with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Procuring edtech with demonstrated efficacy for the district's target population
