# Full-day standardisation workshops to consolidate quality-control procedures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After experts consolidated per-instrument quality-control (QC0) procedures, two full-day dedicated workshops presented the procedures to everyone involved in an instrument's operations. The paper calls the workshops "a key stage of the QC0 project" and states that "Without the workshops, the standardisation of the procedures would be impractical." Their informal character encouraged questions to iron out every doubt.
