# Use job lists from position descriptions as a novel approach to developing EPAs.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review highlights using job lists from position descriptions as a novel and previously undocumented approach to developing EPAs, potentially providing other education programs with additional guidance. Position descriptions are an accessible resource for professionals and link to learner expectations, as learners will reference these documents when investigating job opportunities.
