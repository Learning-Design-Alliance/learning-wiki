# Teachers can generate customized electronic contour maps by deleting selected layers and objects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a practice in which teachers create digital contour maps by removing layers or objects whose knowledge they want to assess, e.g., leaving only 2-3 layers such as "land - sea", or deleting specific objects and signatures for a particular lesson. Teachers can cumulate variants into a personal library of contour maps fitted to their school's educational process, and maps can be filled electronically or printed.
