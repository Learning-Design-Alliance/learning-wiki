# Pre-Writing with Graphic Organizers

## Description
Students use graphic organizers to plan and structure their ideas before writing drafts, aiding in idea generation and organization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Graphic organizers and writing prompts.
#### Constraints
- Organizers should be used for note-taking, not as a replacement for the writing piece itself. Most writing time should be spent drafting.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those who struggle with organizing their thoughts before writing.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Facilitate brainstorming, organization, and structuring of ideas before writing, improving the coherence and quality of written work.

### Affordances
- Provides a visual framework for organizing ideas, reduces cognitive load during the writing process, and promotes structured thinking.

### Personalization
- A variety of organizers can be used, depending on the writing task (e.g., outlining, clustering, concept mapping).
