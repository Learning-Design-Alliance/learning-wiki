# Form design teams by building trust and relationships and prioritizing meaningful representation before project kick-off.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy governs the first activity phase, forming design teams. It has two parts: build trust and relationships, by establishing shared understanding and goals around equity and meeting community members before project kick-off; and prioritize meaningful representation, by providing "a wide range of stakeholders: students, alumni, teachers, administrators, parents, community partners", resisting rushing the process, and positioning youth and adults as equally valuable design partners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Establish shared understanding and goals around equity before the project begins
- Meet community members before project kick-off
- Provide a wide range of stakeholders and resist rushing the process
