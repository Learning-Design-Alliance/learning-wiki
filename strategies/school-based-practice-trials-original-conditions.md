# Implement coordinated practice trials in schools that let candidates complete a published test version under original conditions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete preparation activity: baccalaureate schools offer coordinated practice trials with original tasks of all task types outside of teaching time, officially supported by the cantonal education department. Practice trials "enable future candidates to complete a published test version under original conditions and thus prepare how to approach the real test". Complementary trial runs were organized by medical students at the University of Fribourg.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to published original test versions and all task types
- Coordination via schools, outside of teaching time
- Official support from the education authority
#### Constraints
- Primarily reached candidates from the implementing canton; candidates from other cantons benefited less

### Target Learners
- baccalaureate students preparing for a high-stakes scholastic aptitude test

### Target Learning Goals
- familiarity with test format and conditions to improve aptitude-test performance

## Claims

- [Coordinated practice trials with original tasks in Fribourg baccalaureate schools reduced the mean score gap between French-speaking Fribourg candidates and German-speaking candidates from 0.39 to 0.05](../claims/practice-trials-reduced-fribourg-gap.md) [+W]
- [French-speaking candidates from outside Fribourg benefited less from the school-based practice trials than Fribourg candidates, with their gap to German speakers shrinking only from 0.48 to 0.39](../claims/outside-fribourg-smaller-benefit-practice-trials.md) [~W]
- [The first intervention improved both French-speaking groups, with Fribourg candidates benefiting more than other French speakers, and after the second intervention the two French-speaking groups no longer differed reliably](../claims/differential-benefits-across-french-groups.md) [+W]
- [Free additional original tasks of the type 'concentrated and careful work' in all languages further reduced the gap between German-speaking candidates and French-speaking candidates from outside Fribourg to 0.24](../claims/free-original-tasks-reduced-outside-fribourg-gap.md) [+W]
