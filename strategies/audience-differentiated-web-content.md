# Differentiate web science content by audience: interactive scenarios for novices, reference organization for experts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The site reaches two audiences with different organization: interactive modules and experiments for novices and children, who prefer experiences that keep them ”engaged until they achieve some payoﬀ”, and an encyclopedia of FAQs plus a detailed glossary for experts and adults, who ”often prefer formal organization and ready access to information”. Content is structured in multiple ways to engage a range of learners in a variety of meaningful ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scientific content structured in multiple ways at the site to engage a range of learners in a variety of meaningful ways
#### Constraints
- A kiosk exhibit version for museums, science centers and planetaria is still in development to reach people who do not have access to the Internet

### Target Learners
- novices and children
- experts and adults

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement with black hole physics and astronomy
- ready access to specific information about black holes
