# Take students with telescopes to accessible non-formal sites where they observe, plan and transmit what they learned

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a concrete activity recipe: students accompany the teacher to sites outside school such as hills and beaches in Guarapari, where they assemble and use school-acquired telescopes, learn mounting, instrumentation, pointing and sky observation in practice, and bring a study plan mapping planets, stars and constellations. Because these outings attract visitors who interact and dialogue, students come to lead the work: "Nesses espaços fora da escola utilizados para observação do céu, os alunos recebem muitos visitantes que interagem e dialogam, fazendo-os assumir a condução do trabalho." The strategy is supported by higher-education institutions through lectures, orientations and telescope loans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher orientation and planning of study plans before outings; institutional support (IFES, UFES/PPGCosmo, Núcleo Cosmo-Ufes) providing equipment loans, lectures and orientations
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that non-formal spaces must not be escolarized, so school practices should not simply be repeated in these settings

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- practical telescope skills; sky mapping; communicating astronomy to visitors
