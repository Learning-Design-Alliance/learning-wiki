# Pursue explanatory then prescriptive research on the rationale behind teachers' design decisions to strengthen design implications

## Description
The review recommends a staged research agenda following the descriptive work of Bennett et al.: first develop "a more explanatory and then prescriptive study to explore the rationale behind design decisions and strengthen implications". This strategy treats descriptive accounts of teachers' design work as a foundation whose implications must be strengthened by explaining why design decisions are made before prescribing practice.
