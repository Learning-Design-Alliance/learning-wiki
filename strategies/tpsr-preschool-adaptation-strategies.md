# Adapt TPSR for preschoolers using concrete language, visual aids, stories, and other subject areas to explain responsibility goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that preschool teachers and physical educators adapt TPSR strategies to young children's cognitive and language development. The program leader introduced "a story about family, decisions, and leadership with the aim of clarifying these concepts", used visual aids and examples, and drew on other subject areas to give meaning to self-direction and leadership. Less complex tasks, such as assigning a leader in a small group on a specific task and engaging children in discussions, replaced typical level III and IV strategies like assigning group leaders to organize and lead practice.
