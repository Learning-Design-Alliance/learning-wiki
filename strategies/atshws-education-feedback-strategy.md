# Use the ATSHWS in surgical nursing education for competency assessment, targeted feedback, and skills reinforcement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating the ATSHWS into nursing education to assess competency and guide skill development. It states the tool "can be used not only to assess competency but also to guide targeted feedback and skill development" and that structured skills reinforcement modules could help students who repeat courses. Implementation strategies named include embedding the ATSHWS into undergraduate nursing curricula, surgical orientation programs, and periodic competency audits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Observers must be trained on item definitions and scoring criteria before implementation
- Assessments conducted individually in a standardized setting
#### Constraints
- Current evidence covers psychometric validation only; links to infection outcomes are planned, not yet established

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- students repeating courses who may need remedial training

### Target Learning Goals
- competent, protocol-adherent surgical handwashing
