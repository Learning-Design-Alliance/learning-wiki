Strategies

Embed routine evaluation into all training and education programmes to determine effectiveness.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review notes that very few studies included an evaluation component and recommends that routine evaluation needs to be embedded into all training and education programmes. This allows quality assessment and improvement of interventions specific to enhancing the knowledge and skill of SBAs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Evaluation components should be built into training programme design.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Skilled birth attendants
  • Programme facilitators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Programme quality assessment and improvement

Claims¶

  • Education focused on perineal tear assessment and classification only is associated with increased perineal tear detection rates. [+W]
  • Education and training programmes increase knowledge and skills of skilled birth attendants in perineal anatomy, assessment and classification of perineal tears. [+W]
  • Education programmes including perineal tear prevention training are associated with reduced severe perineal tear rates. [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use short 2-hour to 3-hour workshops with didactic and practical elements for perineal anatomy education.
  • Embed verification routines and cognitive forcing functions into AI-supported learning tasks
