# When evaluating curriculum materials, consider additional influential factors beyond curricular design features

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that explanations of curriculum effects on student learning should not rest on design features alone: alongside the hypothesized design-based explanation, the authors "discuss additional influential factors to consider." This strategy directs evaluators and designers to treat curriculum material design as one influence among several on opportunities to learn and achievement, and to examine those further factors when interpreting curriculum comparison results.
