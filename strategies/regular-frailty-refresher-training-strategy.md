# Provide regular, periodic refresher training on frailty rather than one-time orientation programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the graded association between recency of frailty education and KAP scores, the authors recommend that healthcare institutions provide regular, periodic refresher training to help sustain competence, creating sustained learning platforms that foster an organizational learning culture. They state that "our data cannot identify an optimal training interval" and suggest nursing administrators allocate protected time for education and integrate training participation into performance evaluation systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support including protected time for education and integration of training participation into performance evaluation systems
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional data cannot identify an optimal training interval or establish that training causes higher competence

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses in tertiary hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining frailty-management knowledge, attitudes, and practices
