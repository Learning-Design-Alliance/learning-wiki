# Extend virtual patient work to pairs or groups, locally or online

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend extending virtual patient training beyond individual use: "Future studies should also include the impact of working with the VP in pairs or groups, either at a local level or online." This positions collaborative virtual patient sessions as a next implementation direction, alongside a planned randomized controlled trial examining cognitive outcomes of several VP cases and actual patient outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- several VP cases for the planned trial program
#### Constraints
- proposed as a future direction; pair or group use was not evaluated in this study

### Target Learners
- resident psychiatrists

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative clinical reasoning and decision making
