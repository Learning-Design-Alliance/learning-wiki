# Student Shadowing (Teacher/Coach Observation)

## Description
A teacher or coach shadows a student to gain insights into their experiences and identify areas for improvement in teaching practices. This involves observing the student's behavior, engagement, and interactions throughout the school day, then reflecting on the observations to inform instructional adjustments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated time for shadowing, willingness to observe and reflect on student experiences, and a commitment to making instructional adjustments based on observations.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming, requires careful observation and reflection, and may not capture the full picture of a student's experience.
