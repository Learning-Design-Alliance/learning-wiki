# Expand affordable, accessible Makaton training and embed it in undergraduate professional education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends increasing training opportunities and structured support so SLTs can use Makaton confidently and consistently. Participants suggested reducing costs, offering more online and higher-level training, integrating Makaton into undergraduate healthcare curricula, and raising public awareness. The authors argue this requires "a national framework that embeds AAC within curricula, ensures equitable access across provinces".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers and resources aligned with Makaton South Africa’s six-level training structure
- Institutional or government support to reduce training costs
#### Constraints
- The article notes the study sample was small, urban-concentrated and self-reported, so rural and under-resourced settings need further research

### Target Learners
- Speech-language therapists
- Allied health professionals and LSEN educators
- Parents and caregivers

### Target Learning Goals
- AAC competence and confidence
- Consistent Makaton implementation across therapy, home and school settings
