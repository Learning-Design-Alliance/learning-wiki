# Use hybrid AI feedback combining directive guidance with reflective prompts in large-enrolment and online courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends hybrid AI feedback, which blends explicit corrective guidance with reflective prompts, as a practical response to the challenge of timely, personalised feedback at scale. The authors state that "hybrid AI feedback offers a practical means to address the enduring challenge of delivering timely, personalised feedback in large-enrolment and online courses", accommodating varying learner readiness without requiring instructors to gauge each student's metacognitive development. In the trial, hybrid feedback produced the highest revision rate (27.5%) while maintaining comparable confidence and outcome quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI-generated feedback engineered with a fixed three-part structure (Summary, Strengths, Suggestions for Improvement) with only the Suggestions segment varying by condition
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in a single course with relatively high baseline performance, which may limit generalizability and create ceiling effects

### Target Learners
- higher education students in large-enrolment or online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- revision of authored resources
- balancing immediate improvement with metacognitive growth
