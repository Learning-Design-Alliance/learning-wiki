# Embed the 3R-EC Framework in teacher education assignments such as reflective writing, discussion groups and video journaling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend teaching the 3R-EC Framework explicitly and embedding it in teacher education coursework and internships. They suggest it 'can be introduced as an underlying mechanism that might be embedded in assignments to change the beliefs of teachers about the nature of knowledge and processes of knowing, like reflective writing, discussion groups, video journaling, to name a few', and that it 'could be taught explicitly and be expected to be implemented in assignments, across coursework, and during teaching internships'.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
