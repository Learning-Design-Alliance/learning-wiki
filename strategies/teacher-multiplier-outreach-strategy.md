# Target teachers as the primary audience to multiply public reach, combined with free planetarium shows, media appearances and real-time alerts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's outreach strategy deliberately "targeted primarily teachers, because of the mulYplicaYve eﬀect", layering teacher workshops and credit-bearing courses with free media-ready products: the free 'Totality' planetarium show in multiple formats, downloadable animations, eclipse viewers (over a million distributed, including 100 cards for each of Texas's 8,800+ public schools via a Buc-ee's grant), media briefings, and email alerts. The survey-supported result was an average downstream reach of over 500 people per respondent.
