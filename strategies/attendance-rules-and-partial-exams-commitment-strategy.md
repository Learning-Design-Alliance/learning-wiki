# Use compulsory attendance and multiple partial exams as commitment structures for less conscientious or less open students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that universities introduce "compulsory attendance rules and the opportunity for students to take multiple exams, each one in partial fulfilment of the knowledge requirements," instead of a single overall exam. These settings serve as commitment strategies against procrastination for less diligent students and provide short-term deadlines that help students lacking intellectual curiosity maintain study effort.
