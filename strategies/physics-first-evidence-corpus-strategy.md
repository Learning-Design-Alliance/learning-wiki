# Build an evidence base for Physics First with larger-scale learning studies, longitudinal follow-ups, and required-physics control groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a research agenda for making Physics First evidence-based: "a data corpus must be built that includes larger scale studies on student learning in ninth-grade physics courses," plus studies following students into subsequent chemistry and biology courses, longitudinal studies, and data from schools requiring physics of all 12th-graders to control for selectivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Larger-scale studies across more schools, including schools that require physics of all 12th-graders as a selectivity control
#### Constraints
- The authors state their own sample size is somewhat small and schools volunteered via a listserv, raising teacher self-selection concerns

### Target Learners
- high school physics students in 9th and 12th grade

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of mechanics and kinematics
