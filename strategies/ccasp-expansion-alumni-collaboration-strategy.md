# Continue and expand CCASP tracks and yearly alumni dinners to support interprofessional collaboration

## Description
The authors state that ESO will continue to develop the CCASP programs and strongly recommend them to oncologists and other physicians involved in the care of cancer patients. The article notes these programs "favor interprofessional collaborations through yearly alumni dinners organized for the current and former participants." Future plans include "the launching of CCASP in gastrointestinal cancers and the development of CCASP in prostate cancers and gynecological cancers."
