# Practice Changing Negative Thoughts into Positive Ones

## Description
Challenging learners to reframe negative thoughts into positive affirmations. This involves providing examples of negative statements and asking learners to transform them into more positive ones. Can be done with made-up examples or real-life situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples of negative statements. Facilitator to guide the activity.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who are deeply entrenched in negative thinking patterns. Requires a supportive and non-judgmental environment.
