Use online audiovisual excerpts to introduce new vocabulary in an authentic target-language context, followed by transcript-based identification and communicative practice¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend extending audiovisual materials beyond listening comprehension to vocabulary development: instructors provide a partial or full transcript of a dialogue, students identify unfamiliar vocabulary items and decipher meaning from conversational context, and a follow-up activity requires students to use the new items in a communicative exercise. The article states that "audiovisual excerpts can be used to introduce new vocabulary items in an authentic target-language context," leveraging the accessibility and diversity of online media.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to online audiovisual materials matching learners' proficiency level
  • Transcripts (partial or full) of the selected excerpts

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • postsecondary foreign language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • L2 vocabulary development
  • communicative language use

Claims¶

  • The experimental group's advantage persisted on a delayed posttest one week later, with scores 8.19% higher than the control group and a small-to-medium effect (d = −0.40) that was not statistically significant [+W]
  • Second-semester French learners who received four weeks of daily audiovisual-media listening activities scored 14.18% higher than the control group on an immediate listening posttest, a medium-to-large effect (d = −0.68) that did not reach statistical significance [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Introduce authentic listening materials to lower-level learners by simplifying the task, grading the text, and staging the listening
  • Employ alternative vocabulary teaching methods in unnatural foreign language environments to ensure retention efficiency
  • Listening and reading for specific information
  • Apply authentic materials in teaching listening comprehension and have students practice with them independently
