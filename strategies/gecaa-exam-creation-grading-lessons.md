# Apply GeCAA's exam-creation lessons: pre-solve every problem, test numerical stability, and synchronize graders with problem writers before grading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors list exam-quality practices "pertinent to any teacher who is involved in creating exams": "have individuals solve each problem before finalization, to catch potential mistakes; test tasks involving approximations or curve-fitting for numerical stability", and hold a mandatory meeting between graders and problem writers for each question before grading begins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- "before grading begins, hold a mandatory meeting between the graders and the problem writers for each question, to synchronize the grading scheme and reduce bias".
#### Constraints
- During GeCAA grading, "two errors in the official solutions of the theory round were discovered", and an alternative student solution not in the official marking scheme required editing the scheme and notifying graders.

### Target Learners
- competition organizers and teachers creating problem-solving exams

### Target Learning Goals
- fair, reliable, and bias-reduced grading of multi-step numerical exams
