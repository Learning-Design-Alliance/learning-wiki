# Use commercially available CAVI programs to enhance learners' vocabulary

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications recommend that "language teachers may use such commercially available CAVI programs to enhance learners’ vocabulary", noting that computer programs present all materials for language items so teachers need not spend time preparing vocabulary materials. The study also suggests training instructors to exploit corpus tools, dictionaries software and computer technology resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and students need training to learn to use computers, and hardware and software must be affordable.
#### Constraints
- The article states there are financial barriers to affording CALL hardware and software because university administration does not spare an appropriation, and computers cannot handle unexpected situations due to technological barriers.

### Target Learners
- pre-intermediate and intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- English vocabulary learning and pronunciation
