# Develop online courses with fixed timeframes, individual feedback, and a minimum of personal interaction (blended forms)

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors derive three design recommendations from their interviews: strengthen extrinsic motivation through fixed timeframes and external support of self-regulation; strengthen intrinsic motivation through individual feedback such as corrected homework or embedded multiple-choice questions; and prefer "hybrid forms with a minimum of personal interaction instead of purely online courses". This means a shift toward blended learning, at least for difficult or important subject matters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fixed timeframes for working through topics and external support of self-regulation
- Mechanisms for individual feedback, e.g. submitted and corrected homework assignments or embedded multiple-choice questions
#### Constraints
- Based on a small qualitative sample of ten students at one university, which the authors state limits generalisability

### Target Learners
- medical students in semesters 7 to 10

### Target Learning Goals
- ECG interpretation and other clinically relevant elective content
