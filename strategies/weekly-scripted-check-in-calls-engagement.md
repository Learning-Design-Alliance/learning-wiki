# Use brief scripted weekly check-in calls to enhance parental engagement without providing therapeutic advice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To address poor parental engagement, which the article notes can compromise preventative parenting interventions, intervention-group parents receive "a 5-min, weekly phone call from a researcher" for at least 8 weeks, with the total number of calls matching the number of modules selected plus one. Research staff follow "a standard script and will not provide individual advice or therapy". The stated aims are "to enhance parental engagement and ensure that parents progress smoothly through their allocated program until completion". Control parents receive matching calls to match the factsheets received.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Calls must follow a standard script and provide no individual advice or therapy
- Call frequency must match the participant's allocated program content
#### Constraints
- Calls provide an opportunity to ask questions about study procedures but explicitly no therapeutic support

### Target Learners
- parents participating in self-directed online parenting programs

### Target Learning Goals
- program engagement, completion and retention
