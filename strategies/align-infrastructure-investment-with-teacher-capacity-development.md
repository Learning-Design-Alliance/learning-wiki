# Align infrastructural AI investment with sustained teacher professional capacity development in vocational institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that collective teacher-perceived AI capability is the transmission mechanism through which institutional readiness relates to student AI literacy, the article recommends that system-level policy align infrastructure spending with sustained professional capacity development. The abstract states the findings "identify collective teacher capability as its central transmission mechanism, and underscoretheneedtoaligninfrastructuralinvestmentwithsustainedprofessional capacity development." This implies readiness investments alone are insufficient without building teachers' collective capability to enact AI-supported pedagogy.
