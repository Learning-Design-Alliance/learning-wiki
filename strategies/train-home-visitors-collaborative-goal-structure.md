# Provide home visitors training and a structure for collaborative goal setting with attainable, child-focused goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that programs train and structure home visitors' goal-setting work so that all caregivers are consistently engaged in collaborative goal setting, goals include child-focused as well as parenting aims, and goals are attainable within the program's scope. It points to the Getting Ready Intervention as a promising structured model in which educators and family members build relationships, establish mutual goals, and monitor and evaluate the child's progress. The authors state this structure "may improve the effectiveness of the home-based component of Head Start."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Home visitors need professional development to consistently and routinely engage all caregivers in collaborative goal setting
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from an exploratory single-program study whose results the authors say should be interpreted with caution

### Target Learners
- low-income preschool children and their caregivers in Head Start home-based programs

### Target Learning Goals
- children's development and school readiness
- caregivers' capacity for parenting
