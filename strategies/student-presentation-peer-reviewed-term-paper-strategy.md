# Student presentations plus instructor-peer-reviewed term papers to build technical writing, reviewing skills, and academic-honesty awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each student prepares a 15-minute presentation on a nanoengineering topic of interest and writes a term paper that classmates peer-review before final submission. The authors state that "a student based peer review process is developed which helps them to improve technical writing skills, as well as address the ethical issues of academic honesty while reviewing". Reviewing also exposes students to "a totally different topic presented by their colleague" and prepares them for academic and industrial peer review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small class size enabling in-class peer review, with class size "restricted to fifteen students"
- Student topics relating to nanoengineering and nanotechnology, with societal-impact topics encouraged
