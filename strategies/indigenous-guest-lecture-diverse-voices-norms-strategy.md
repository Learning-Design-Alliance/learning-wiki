# Open the course with a local Indigenous cultural perspective lecture and reinforce diverse voices with scientist life stories

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To establish a norm valuing diverse perspectives, the course opened with a lecture by "a member of the local Tohono O'odham Native American Nation" on their cultural beliefs of the Solar System, Milky Way, and other celestial objects, tied into the first unit on human and cultural connections to the sky. The norm was reinforced throughout the semester through stories of scientists such as Cecilia Payne-Gaposchkin and the systemic barriers she faced, acknowledging that curricula focused only on dominant Western perspectives can imply other cultures' beliefs are not valued.
