# Descriptive Feature Chart

## Description
A descriptive feature chart is a graphic organizer with horizontal and vertical axes used to develop a descriptive model of an organism. It displays the features of the organism in a structured way, such as body parts, number, color and shape, position, and appearance. It is commonly used in science instruction to help students organize and analyze observations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-designed chart with appropriate categories (e.g., Body Parts, Number, Color and Shape, Position, Appearance); materials for observation (e.g., specimens, books, images); pens and paper or digital tools
#### Constraints
- May require significant initial teacher guidance to ensure students understand how to use the chart effectively; can be time-consuming to fill out all details

- Can be used in whole class, pairs, small groups, or individually; adaptable for different types of organisms and details
