# Remote group project built on individual extensions of standard kit experiments with cross-group peer review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A revised group project approach optimized for remote delivery: every student first individually designs and performs a personal extension of a standard kit experiment, then groups are formed from students extending the same standard experiment, with "each had to do a different extension". Final reports follow a journal-letter template and are reviewed by the instructor and by student peer reviewers from other groups; extra-credit extensions leverage 24/7 access to the kits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students must possess the experiment kits so each can perform the standard experiments and an individual extension
- Synchronous lab sessions with Zoom breakout rooms to preserve collaboration
#### Constraints
- Designed for pandemic lockdown conditions in which students could not collaborate in the same manner as onsite
- Peer reviews were graded for helpfulness and professionalism

### Target Learners
- undergraduate introductory physics students in fully remote laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- learning how to establish research goals (LG-12)
- designing, evaluating feasibility of, and improving custom scientific experiments (LG-13)
- technical writing and communication of scientific results (LG-11)
