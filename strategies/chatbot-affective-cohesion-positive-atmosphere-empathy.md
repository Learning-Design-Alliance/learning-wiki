# Support affective cohesion through positive atmosphere and empathy formation in chatbot dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy cultivates emotional bonds among team members through mutual comprehension and empathy. Sub-guidelines include easily achievable pre-task activities to provide opportunities for success, praise time where members exchange positive feedback, occasional encouraging feedback tied to individual performance, empathetic responses when learners share emotions, summarizing learner input to show understanding, and prompts encouraging agreement and empathy toward teammates' opinions. The chatbot should also deliver encouragement alongside help responses and offer perspective-taking messages during team conflict.
