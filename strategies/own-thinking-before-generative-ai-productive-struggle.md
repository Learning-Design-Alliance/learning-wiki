# Have learners bring their own thinking before using generative AI to enable productive struggle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Under the Foster Metacognition practice, the framework recommends encouraging "learners to bring their own thinking and ideas to the table before using generative AI, allowing them to engage more deeply in their own thinking and learning." It also advises creating a safe space for risk-taking and mistakes with productive feedback and multiple attempts, and keeping content appropriately challenging with new strategies offered.
