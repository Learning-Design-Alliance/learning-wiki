# Scale UNAWE through language-matched mini-MOOCs for teachers and mobile Astrobus delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To expand UNAWE's reach, particularly in Africa, the article proposes innovative scalable delivery modes: making 'courses and demonstration videos for teachers' (mini-MOOCs) 'matched to the language and culture of the targeted country', and extending the mobile Astrobus pioneered by UNAWE-Tunisia, which transports a small telescope, a mini-planetarium and an exhibition to reach children in remote villages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- courses and demonstration videos matched to the language and culture of the targeted country
- for the Astrobus, a small telescope, a mini-planetarium and an exhibition
#### Constraints
- future ambitions are contingent on the availability of sufficient funds

### Target Learners
- teachers in remote areas
- children in remote villages

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher training in astronomy education
- access to astronomy activities for remote communities
