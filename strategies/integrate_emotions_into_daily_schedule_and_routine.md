# Integrate Emotions into Daily Schedule and Routine

## Description
Integrate the teaching of emotions into daily schedules and routines. When reviewing the daily schedule with students, ask them to predict the emotions they might experience. Explore if Math makes them anxious, tired, or bored. Ask if Recess makes them nervous because they don't always have someone to play with. Spend a few minutes anticipating what emotions might emerge, and help students think about how they will manage those emotions. Write them down so that you can remind them to use those strategies in the moment.
