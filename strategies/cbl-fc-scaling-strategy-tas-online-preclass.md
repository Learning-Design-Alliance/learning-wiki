# Scale CBL-FC to larger classes using online pre-class materials, teaching-assistant facilitation, and cross-department faculty collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose adaptations for settings with larger classes or limited faculty. Pre-recorded lectures, videos, and digital readings can move knowledge acquisition online; trained teaching assistants or senior students can "help moderate group discussions, assist with case analysis, and provide feedback"; and faculty from different departments can co-develop interdisciplinary cases and co-facilitate discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online platforms for pre-class learning
- Trained teaching assistants or senior students to support facilitation
#### Constraints
- Recommended in response to scalability limits the authors state; not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical imaging students in larger classes

### Target Learning Goals
- imaging interpretation
- diagnostic reasoning
