# Foster autonomy and curiosity-driven learning by embedding metacognitive processes and self-regulation elements into education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's implications section recommends a fundamental shift in educational practice toward nurturing autonomy and curiosity-driven learning. It states this "can be done through the inclusion of metacognitive processes where learners an reflect on their own thinking, monitor their understanding, and adapt their strategies", with institutions promoting metacognitive awareness so learners plan their own learning path and assess their progress. The strategy responds to the study's finding that pandemic-era digital self-regulated learning habits persisted after the emergency passed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational institutions must acknowledge and embrace the transformation toward autonomy and curiosity-driven learning
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on survey evidence from 206 pre-service teachers in only two countries, a limitation the authors state

### Target Learners
- EFL pre-service teachers in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning of a foreign language
- Metacognitive awareness and learner autonomy
