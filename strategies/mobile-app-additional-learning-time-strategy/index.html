Integrate mobile language learning apps into language teaching so students gain additional learning time outside school¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The project's main idea was that integrating mobile devices in language teaching practice would give students "valuable additional learning time outside school, something that could improve their language learning efficiency". The strategy pairs regular classroom lessons with app-based study outside school, using a tool students find easy and simple to use. In this study the strategy was associated with significant test gains despite very low actual usage.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An app students find easy and simple to use that works smoothly on their devices
  • A language test or other measure to evaluate effectiveness before and after

Constraints¶

  • Actual use was sporadic and short (about once per week, 10–15 minutes), and the app did not become a regular habit

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students studying foreign languages

Target Learning Goals¶

  • foreign language proficiency

Claims¶

  • Students using the busuu mobile app alongside lessons improved language test performance (+2.2%) while the control group declined (-3.1%), a statistically significant post-measurement difference (p=.013) [+M]
  • Actual app use was sporadic and short — about once per week for 10–15 minutes, roughly 120 minutes total — which the authors say cannot explain the significant difference between groups [~M]
  • Experimental group motivation was significantly higher in 3 of 5 sub-categories, but no correlation was found between test results and motivation [+M]
  • Students found busuu easy and useful especially for vocabulary and writing, but 73.3% thought it helps without being the ultimate tool, and language learning apps were largely unknown to them [~M]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt mobile messaging applications, especially WhatsApp, across English-language teaching
  • Integrate pronunciation-focused technology such as language learning apps and mobile-assisted pronunciation training into the CLT curriculum
  • Design m-learning apps for offline access, cross-device synchronization, context-awareness, personalized paths, and responsive support services
