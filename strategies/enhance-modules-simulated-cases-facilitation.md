# Enhance self-learning modules with simulated patient cases and instructor facilitation where available

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend future enhancements: combining the module with a patient video of a clinical case, using it as a small-group collaborative exercise, discussing cases from learners' own rotations, and adding a simulated exercise of managing a patient with obesity before and after the session. If offered in class, "facilitation by an instructor knowledgeable and experienced in the area of obesity medicine would be ideal."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- For in-class use, an instructor with obesity medicine expertise; for self-learning use, no expertise is needed
#### Constraints
- The learners did not apply the module in a simulated patient scenario, so application was not demonstrated

### Target Learners
- Medical students in clinical years
- primary care residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying evidence-based recommendations to patient cases
- moving up Bloom's taxonomy from describing to applying
