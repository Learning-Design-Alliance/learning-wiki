Provide extensive initial training plus ongoing coaching, with a teacher-level facilitator, when adopting a whole-school reform model¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Implementing the Talent Development High School model relies on building staff capacity: "Extensive professional development is provided, both initial training and ongoing coaching." Adoption also carries a staffing requirement that "An additional teacher-level facilitator is required," while "No technology is required," making the implementation strategy personnel- and training-based rather than technology-based.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An additional teacher-level facilitator is required

Constraints¶

  • The summary states no technology is required, so the strategy presumes investment in people and training rather than tools

Target Learners¶

  • high school teachers and staff implementing the model

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful implementation of the Talent Development High School model

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Use a full-time program facilitator plus multi-mode professional development to sustain comprehensive reform implementation
