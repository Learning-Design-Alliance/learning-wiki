# Providing Graphic Organizers or Guided Notes

## Description
During lectures, providing students with graphic organizers or guided notes helps them pull out the important points and follow the structure of the lecture. This supports students with challenges related to processing speed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation of structured graphic organizers or guided notes aligned with the lecture content.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time to prepare. Effectiveness depends on the relevance and clarity of the provided materials.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Learners with challenges related to processing speed or note-taking skills.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve note-taking, enhance information processing, and facilitate comprehension during lectures.

### Affordances
- Reduces cognitive load by providing a structured framework for note-taking. Helps learners focus on key information and relationships.

### Personalization
- Customize the graphic organizers or guided notes based on individual learning preferences and content complexity.
