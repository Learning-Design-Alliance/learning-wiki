# Add post-training support measures and competency-refresh strategies to sustain effects and enable real-world implementation of change-agent actions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because training alone did not translate into deployed actions, the article recommends building competency-updating strategies into post-training activities: "La mise en place de stratégies d'actualisation des compétences dans le cadre d'activités post-formation apparaît essentielle". It also calls for evaluating trainings "intégrant ce type de soutiens longitudinaux ou mobilisant des approches plus contextualisées menant à l'application en contexte réel".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Post-training activities that refresh competencies and provide longitudinal support, since following and understanding a training is not sufficient for applying skills in real contexts
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in an observational summative evaluation with a small convenience sample and 16-participant attrition; the authors state results must be interpreted with prudence

### Target Learners
- Health and social service professionals trained as change agents

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining perceived competence and skills over time and translating change-agent intentions into real-world actions
