# Four recommended foci for new academic success definitions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on feminist literature, the commentary recommends supplementing existing success recommendations with four foci: "(1) personal dispositions, (2) diverse mentorship models, (3) workload and wellbeing strategies, and (4) failure narratives". Personal dispositions such as confidence and self-efficacy should be prioritised in future professional development of female academics; mentorship models should be informed by diverse experiences and embedded in evidence-based institutional policies; extreme workload should be challenged rather than promoted; and institutional support is needed when failures occur, especially for marginalised groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional mentorship policies that are evidence-based and informed by diverse experiences
- Greater prevention measures promoting work/life balance, mental health and wellbeing, and reduced publication pressures
#### Constraints
- Support for developing personal dispositions has been minimal in traditional academic training, per the commentary citing Sousa & Clark (2018)

### Target Learners
- female academics
- early career researchers
- scholars from marginalised groups
