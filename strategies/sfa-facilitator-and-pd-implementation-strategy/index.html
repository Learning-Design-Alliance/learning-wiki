Use a full-time program facilitator plus multi-mode professional development to sustain comprehensive reform implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

SFA assigns a program facilitator who "provides support for program implementation, manages central data collection and review, and serves as a coach for teachers." Professional development combines onsite workshops, email and telephone support, just-in-time online resources, and onsite observation and consultation, with about three days of workshops per teacher to begin and a strong focus on regular peer support structures within the school.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Identification of a program facilitator, usually an assistant principal, Title I Coordinator, or other existing staff member
  • About three days of workshop participation per teacher to begin implementation
  • Facilitator access to the internet-based data center

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school teachers and administrators implementing the program

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective implementation of cooperative learning-based reading instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Designate a Spalding coordinator and provide 45-70 hours of formal teacher training with ongoing mentoring to support implementation fidelity
  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Provide a structured first-year Getting Started Experience plus year-long administrator training when adopting a comprehensive curriculum
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Support curriculum implementation with initial multi-day training plus ongoing onsite consultation during the school year
  • Pair program adoption with two days of initial training and weekly coaching meetings
  • Provide extensive initial training plus ongoing coaching, with a teacher-level facilitator, when adopting a whole-school reform model
  • Pair new instructional practices with sustained in-class coaching after initial professional development
