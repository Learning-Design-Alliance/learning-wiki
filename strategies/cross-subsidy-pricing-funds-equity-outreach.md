# Cross-subsidy ticket pricing to fund outreach to underserved populations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
A funding strategy in which a public-facing program raises ticket prices for its existing, relatively privileged audience and uses the proceeds to serve populations lacking the same privileges. After noting local attendees' tech-sector advantages, the authors write: "The decision was made to raise ticket prices, and to use the proceeds to do outreach to populations that did not have the privileges enjoyed by the then-current attendees."

### Context
#### Requirements
