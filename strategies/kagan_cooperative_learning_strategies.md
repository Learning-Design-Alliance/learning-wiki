# Kagan Cooperative Learning Strategies

## Description
Implement Kagan Cooperative Learning Strategies to encourage active participation and engagement in learning. These strategies include: Gallery Walk (learners examine and discuss various items or prompts displayed around the room), Two Stray, One Stay (small groups discuss a topic, then two members 'stray' to other groups to share their findings while one member 'stays' to explain to new visitors), Round Robin (each member of a group takes turns sharing their ideas or responses), and Simultaneous Round Table (each group member writes their response to a prompt on a shared paper, passing it around until everyone has contributed). These small changes can easily be implemented in any learning environment to promote collaboration and active learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear instructions and prompts for each activity. Space for learners to move and interact. Willingness to facilitate cooperative learning activities.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure activities align with learning objectives. May be challenging to manage in large classrooms or with learners who struggle with collaboration.
