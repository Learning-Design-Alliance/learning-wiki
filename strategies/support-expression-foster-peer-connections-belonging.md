# Support diverse authentic self-expression and foster interpersonal peer connections to cultivate belonging in physics courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on interview findings that discrepancies and lower belonging were linked to not feeling personally known by physics peers and to pressure to alter gender expression, the authors recommend "supporting students’ diverse and authentic self-expression and fostering interpersonal connections among peers" as important steps toward cultivating stronger belonging. Students in the study contrasted physics with courses built around personal discussion, suggesting course structures that create opportunities to know peers personally could address these dynamics.

## Design Implications
