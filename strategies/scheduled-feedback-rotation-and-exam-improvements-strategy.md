# Improve standardised nurse training through scheduled feedback mechanisms, planned rotation schedules, standardised examinations and supportive communication culture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on combined quantitative gaps and qualitative explanations, the authors propose improvements across Training Programme, Examination, Activities, Clinical Instructors and Motivational Strategies. Key actions include scheduling instructor feedback "on the first week of the rotation and the last week of rotation in each clinical department", plotting clinical rotation schedules before training, having the nursing department administer examinations, and creating "a work culture that promotes open communication between the nurse trainees and the clinical instructors".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nursing department coordination of instructor schedules and examination administration
#### Constraints
- Adding nutrition, gastronomy and health science content is subject to approval of the nursing department

### Target Learners
- Trainee nurses in standardised training
- Clinical instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- Aligning self-perceived and instructor-evaluated competency
- Reducing trainee transition stress and improving feedback timeliness
