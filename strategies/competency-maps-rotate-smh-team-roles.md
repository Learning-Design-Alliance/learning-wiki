# Use competency maps to rotate differentiated team roles such as facilitator, equity advocate, and data integrator based on expertise

## Description
For day-to-day practice, the article recommends interdisciplinary teams use competency maps to intentionally differentiate collaborative roles—facilitator, equity advocate, data integrator—so that leadership rotates 'based on situational expertise rather than professional discipline'. The article illustrates this with a school psychologist leading data integration, a school social worker acting as equity advocate, and a school counselor facilitating communication across partners.
