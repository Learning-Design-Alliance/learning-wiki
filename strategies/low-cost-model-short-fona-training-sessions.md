# Conduct short, simple skill-training sessions on a low-cost self-made model where full-scale simulators are unavailable, embedded in recurring refresher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that institutions without access to a full-scale simulator run short and simple FONA skill sessions on an easily accessible, low-cost self-made model: "short and simple training sessions can be effectively conducted using an easily accessible, low-cost model, even in institutions without access to a full-scale simulator". The authors add that one-off training is not sufficient and "Training should also aim to implement refresher courses at regular intervals", with mastery learning named as a suitable concept for this high-stakes low-frequency skill.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Embedding the simple-model training in a didactically aligned, recurring training concept with regular refresher sessions
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center study with anaesthesiology residents only and a short three-month follow-up

### Target Learners
- anaesthesiology residents
- all healthcare professionals involved in airway management

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining FONA/cricothyrotomy procedural skill
