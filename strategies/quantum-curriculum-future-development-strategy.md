# Plan multi-institutional evaluation and extend the curriculum with more simulations, collaborative activities and conceptual diagnostics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors plan "multi-institutional studies in the coming year to assess the educational effectiveness of the resources in helping students from diverse backgrounds to learn introductory quantum mechanics". They will extend the resources with further simulations (e.g. on the Aspect experiments, decoherence and quantum information), produce HTML5/Javascript versions, develop a second set of more exploratory and collaborative activities promoting student discussions, and build a community of users with exemplars and a forum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group observation sessions where students collaboratively work with the simulations will be used to optimize the new collaborative activities
#### Constraints
- Current evaluation has been limited to simulations and activities and mostly to a single institution

### Target Learners
- students from diverse backgrounds studying introductory quantum mechanics

### Target Learning Goals
- introductory quantum mechanics learning gains
- collaborative sense-making with simulations
