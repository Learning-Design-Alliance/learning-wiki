# Use CME platforms and NTP training to train both public and private doctors on standard TB diagnosis and management guidelines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends strengthening the public-private mix model and expanding training: "Strengthening of currently implemented public private mix model along with improvement in the trainings of public and private practitioners is highly recommended to control TB in Pakistan." Because CME was the main knowledge source for both sectors, the authors propose CME platforms as a delivery channel for training private providers on standard guidelines, since only 31% of doctors were NTP-trained.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training aligned with NTP standard diagnosis and management guidelines
- CME platforms accessible to private-sector doctors
#### Constraints
- Private sectors are mainly unregulated in Pakistan and private doctors face lack of motivation and non-availability of time required for training, per the article's discussion

### Target Learners
- Public and private sector MBBS doctors in high TB burden urban settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Standard-guideline TB diagnosis and management, including contact screening and counseling
