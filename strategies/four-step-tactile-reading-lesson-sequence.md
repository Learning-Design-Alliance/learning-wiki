# Four-step tactile reading lesson sequence: matching, finger spelling, word reading, sentence reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental lessons followed a fixed four-section sequence: "1. Matching raised words. 2. Finger spelling raised letters. 3. Reading raised words. 4. Reading raised sentences." The control group received the same structure with non-raised materials (matching words, finger spelling letters, reading words, reading sentences), so the sequence itself was held constant while the tactile embossed materials varied. Sessions lasted 35 minutes once a week over six weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Raised/embossed word and sentence materials, consistent weekly 35-minute sessions, and parallel lesson structure across comparison groups
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a small class-one group at one special school over six weeks

### Target Learners
- pupils with hearing impairment in class one

### Target Learning Goals
- reading words and sentences
