## Description
As the Collaborative's work continues, the brief states it is "vital to disseminate insights and refined practices and recognize significant accomplishments." R15CC will share Bright Spots documenting the region's family and community engagement best practices, making accomplishments visible and spreading refined practices beyond the Collaborative.
