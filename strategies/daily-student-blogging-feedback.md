# Encourage daily student blogging as a feedback channel on lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that student participants write daily blog posts about the lessons, with an appropriate blogging medium set up. Students commented on each day's lessons in blogs, so problems were "quickly brought to our attention," and blogs also revealed that students especially enjoyed interacting with the scientists behind the lessons. The author notes many students write more freely behind a screen than aloud in class.
