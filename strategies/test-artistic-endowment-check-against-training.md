# Test school children's artistic endowment and check test results against subsequent training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editorial recommends that schools systematically assess children's artistic endowments, following the lines Seashore suggested for musical ability in "Psychology in Daily Life," and then validate the tests longitudinally: "a careful checking of the results of such tests on the basis of subsequent training." The rationale is conserving creative artistic talent for society and developing pupils' potentialities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Artistic-endowment tests administered in schools, on the lines suggested for musical ability by Seashore
- Follow-up of tested children through subsequent training to validate the test predictions
#### Constraints
- The editorial notes such an extensive study of school children's artistic endowment is what is "urgently needed," implying it does not yet exist

### Target Learners
- school children

### Target Learning Goals
- identification and development of artistic endowment and creative potential
