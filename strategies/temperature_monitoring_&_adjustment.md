# Temperature Monitoring & Adjustment

## Description
Monitoring the temperature of a learning environment and making adjustments to maintain an optimal temperature range can improve learner focus and reduce distractions. Being in an environment that is too cold can lead to distractibility and decreased Attention, while an environment that is too warm can cause decreased concentration due to discomfort and drowsiness. The ideal temperature range for effective learning in reading and mathematics is between 68º and 74º.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Temperature monitoring tools (thermometers, sensors), HVAC systems with sufficient control, understanding of optimal temperature ranges for learning, ability to adjust temperature based on feedback.
#### Constraints
- Achieving ideal temperature for all learners in a shared space can be challenging due to individual preferences; older buildings may have limited temperature control capabilities; reliance on technology for monitoring and adjustment.

## Assessment Evidence
- Measure learner focus and engagement levels under different temperature conditions, track performance metrics (e.g., test scores) in relation to temperature, survey learners about their comfort levels and perceived impact on learning.

## Impact
- Research indicates that maintaining an optimal temperature range (68º-74º) enhances engagement levels and overall productivity, including student achievement. Studies show a correlation between comfortable temperatures and improved test scores and reduced off-task behavior.
