Transportable tutoring protocol with structured tutor training and supervision¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article concludes the tutoring protocols were transportable across sites and scalable, provided tutors are trained as in the study: tutors receive a full day of training, practice implementing procedures alone and with each other during the subsequent week, conduct a session with a supervisor who provides corrective feedback, study (not read) the tutoring scripts, and meet with fellow tutors and the supervisor every 2-3 weeks to address problems. Fidelity checks sampled audiotaped sessions and found mean percentages of essential lesson points addressed above 98 percent at both sites.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Part-time tutors trained over two full-day sessions with scripted lessons and ongoing supervisor meetings

Constraints¶

  • Scaling is conditioned on tutors being trained as done in the present study

Target Learners¶

  • Third-grade students with math difficulties

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Automatic retrieval of basic math facts

Related Strategies¶

  • Intensive trainer-led observation and feedback model for tutoring professional development
  • Certify tutors before tutoring begins and provide professional development to supervising school staff
  • Train paraprofessional tutors with one initial day of training plus two two-hour booster sessions
  • Sustained professional development model combining training days, site visits, observations, and ongoing coaching
  • Provide LLI interventionists with 3-day formal training including follow-up on-site coaching
  • Implement formal peer tutor training programs under faculty supervision within simulation curricula
