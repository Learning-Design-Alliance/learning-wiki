## Description
In its implications for research, the article states that "Future research should explore the potential of artificial intelligence (AI)-driven educational tools and decision-support systems to enhance nurses' knowledge and adherence to PICC care guidelines". It suggests AI-powered simulations, adaptive learning platforms, and real-time clinical alerts could provide personalized and efficient training addressing the knowledge gaps identified, and also proposes integrating nursing engineering research toward automated monitoring systems and smart PICC care devices.
