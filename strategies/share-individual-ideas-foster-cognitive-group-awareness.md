# Share individually generated ideas before and during group brainstorming to foster cognitive group awareness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In hybrid brainstorming, the group phase can be scaffolded by making all individually generated ideas visible to group members before discussion, rather than sharing only ideas produced during the session. The platform "facilitated cognitive group awareness by sharing all individually generated ideas among group members", and participants were instructed to review others' ideas and discuss ways to expand on interesting ones. This supplies associative cues that unlock idea categories individuals would not reach alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shared digital workspace where individually generated ideas can be displayed to all group members, plus time for reviewing and discussing others' ideas
#### Constraints
- Tested with newly formed triads of higher education students on a sustainability business-idea task; group-phase flexibility did not increase despite originality gains

### Target Learners
- higher education students working in small groups in hybrid brainstorming settings

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing originality of collaboratively and individually generated ideas; broadening idea categories

### Affordances
- Dual Pathways To Creativity Model Dpcm
