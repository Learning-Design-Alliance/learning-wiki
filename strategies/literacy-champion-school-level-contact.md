# Appoint a Literacy Champion as school-level contact to support A2i rollout

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This implementation strategy calls for identifying a 'Literacy Champion' who "serves as the main school-level contact and resource as teachers begin the A2i rollout". The role is typically filled by a teacher on special assignment (TOA/TOSA), coach, or grade-level lead, and is described as a critical role required for implementation, giving teachers a readily available resource during the A2i adoption process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A critical role required for implementation is the identification of a 'Literacy Champion'
- No additional staff are required beyond the Literacy Champion and a main district-contact
#### Constraints
- Stated as an implementation requirement in the program description; the review reports no outcome data testing this staffing role

### Target Learners
- Pre-K through Grade 3 students in schools adopting A2i

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading at or above grade level by the end of 3rd grade
