Form project groups so each student works with at least one friend¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that teachers forming project groups place each student in a group with at least one friend they like to work with. In this study the researchers gave students the chance to be grouped with a close friend when forming eight heterogeneous groups of 4 or 5, which the authors report prevented disagreement while determining group members and increased motivation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher-controlled heterogeneous group formation with student friend preferences taken into account

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is offered for group-work implementations similar to this 7th grade mathematics study

Target Learners¶

  • 7th grade secondary school students working in project groups

Target Learning Goals¶

  • collaboration and teamwork during project work

Related Strategies¶

  • Collaborative Group Work in PBL
  • Alternate student work forms: small groups, brainstorming, and oral presentation in project work
