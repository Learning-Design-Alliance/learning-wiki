# Calming Combinations

## Description
Combining two or more calming sensory strategies can create a more effective and personalized approach to self-regulation. Examples include playing in a tactile bin while listening to white noise on headphones, sitting or lying with a weighted blanket while looking at sensory bottles, chewing gum while working on a fine motor task, and rhythmic rocking with calming breathing techniques. Experimentation and trial and error are key to finding the best combinations.
