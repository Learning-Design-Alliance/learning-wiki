# Prompted Source Evaluation

## Description
Pair strategy instruction with written prompts to guide students toward independence. Prompts can ask students to systematically look for evidence that supports and refutes key claims. To examine relevance and accuracy, have students consider the quality of the content. To determine reliability and perspective, they should consider the author and his or her agenda in relation to his or her affiliations. Cross-checking claims between multiple sources can help adolescents recognize ideas they might otherwise ignore, weigh the usefulness (and reliability) of these ideas against what they previously believed to be true, and consider that new ideas may actually be more accurate than their original thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Written prompts, examples of sources, and a framework for cross-checking claims.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort. Some students may need additional support to understand and apply the prompts effectively.
