Gradually shifting leadership responsibility to students through peer coaching and cross-age tasks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

In the TPSR leadership program, responsibilities in planning and executing the program are gradually shifted from program leaders to young people so they take on leadership and responsibility. From the 15th week students were assigned tasks and given opportunities to develop leadership behaviors through peer coaching and leading activities; students led groups of two or three peers, provided feedback such as helping others and controlling misbehavior, and prepared plans for lessons they would lead. The article links this experiential approach to Dewey's learning by doing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students are given the chance to lead together with peer groups during the activities
  • Leaders are asked to provide feedback and to make plans for the courses they will lead

Constraints¶

  • One interviewed student reported the program had not contributed much to his leadership skills, saying he was timid and had to know the group first

Target Learners¶

  • high school athlete students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • leadership behavior
  • peer coaching
  • taking responsibility
  • self-confidence

Affordances¶

  • Tpsr Five Levels Responsibility Model

Claims¶

  • Deeper co-leadership emerges when staff support lets partners progressively take on responsibility, but recedes when staff carry the process [+W]
  • Students in the TPSR leadership program reported gains in self-confidence, taking responsibility, cooperation, and leading behaviors in post-program interviews [+W]
  • The TPSR leadership program significantly improved leadership behavior scores (initiation of structure and consideration) on the LBDQ compared with the control group [+M]
  • A 16-lesson TPSR leadership program significantly improved personal and social responsibility scores of sports high school students compared with a control group [+M]
