# Three-pronged intervention framework: personalized digital learning, emotional support, and engagement enhancement for EFL teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a practical framework with three components: leveraging digital technologies through "intelligent leveled reading systems, AI-powered speech interaction tools, and adaptive vocabulary training platforms"; integrating emotional intelligence cultivation via affective text analysis, situational role-playing, and reflective writing; and combining flipped classrooms with gamified teaching to create an "input-interaction-feedback" learning cycle with pre-class micro-lectures, discussions, and digital badge rewards. The authors state it aligns with Self-Determination Theory's core elements of autonomy, competence, and relatedness.
