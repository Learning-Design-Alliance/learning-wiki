# Infuse social psychological concepts into existing pre-service courses and professional development rather than new standalone courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that teacher educators adapt social psychological principles into classroom practices, evaluate whether they transfer from laboratory to classroom, and disseminate findings to practitioners. Rather than a standalone applied social psychology course, which over-saturated programs are unlikely to adopt, it suggests using parts of existing courses and professional development workshops, connecting concepts to teachers' own experience to leverage the self-reference effect. Small demonstrated interventions can earn latitude for larger ones.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher educators must adapt principles into actual classroom practices and evaluate which applications transfer from laboratory to classroom
#### Constraints
- The review calls pre-service coursework infusion only a partial solution, since it cannot reach the many teachers already practicing

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers
- in-service teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding and applying social psychological principles such as cognitive dissonance, intergroup bias, and social perspective taking in classrooms
