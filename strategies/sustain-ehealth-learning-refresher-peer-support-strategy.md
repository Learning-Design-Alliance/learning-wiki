# Sustain program effects through refresher sessions, peer support, and integration into routine clinical practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Limitations section, the authors recommend strategies to sustain long-term effects: "Continuous support and counseling through regular refresher sessions can help reinforce learning and maintain patient engagement," "integrating the program into routine clinical practice is essential, involving health care providers in the ongoing monitoring," and "Providing peer support and fostering a shared responsibility for health management can also contribute to sustaining the program's impact." They also call for extended follow-up periods and future studies to increase male participation.

## Design Implications
