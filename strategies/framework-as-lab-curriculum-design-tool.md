# Use the modeling framework as a curriculum design tool to generate lab variants emphasizing different modeling stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The framework can repurpose standard equipment to design activities emphasizing model construction, articulation and testing of assumptions and limits of validity, testing broader sets of predictions, or limitations and refinement of measurement tools. The article states that "the framework oﬀers a way to generate various alternative pendulum labs that emphasize additional aspects of the modeling process that go beyond measuring a particular parameter or physical constant". For example, video analysis of a pendulum surfaces measurement-model limitations such as camera position, angle, and lens distortion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standard lab activity whose physical system and measurement tools can be analyzed within the framework
- Instructor decisions about which modeling stage to emphasize
#### Constraints
- The article notes the listed alternatives do not constitute an exhaustive list of possibilities

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in introductory or upper-division labs

### Target Learning Goals
- model construction
- articulating assumptions and limitations
- testing predictions
- refining measurement models
