# Use the PEARLS Healthcare Debriefing Tool with paired otolaryngology faculty and nurse educator facilitators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Postsession debriefing uses a reflective model structured according to the PEARLS Healthcare Debriefing Tool, beginning with individual reflections followed by review of objectives. The authors "recommend that an otolaryngology faculty member and an experienced nurse educator be available for the debrief discussion due to the complex and rare nature of these clinical scenarios."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators complete a standardized orientation to the scenario content and debriefing structure before the simulation day to ensure consistency across sessions
