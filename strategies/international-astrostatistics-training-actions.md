# International-level actions: collaborate with international astrostatistics organizations, develop statistical standards and a standard graduate curriculum, and recruit internationally trained astrostatisticians

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that a CASCA astrostatistics chapter or working group collaborate with international organizations such as the AAS WGAA, ASA AIG, and IAU B3 Commission, potentially hosting an internationally open astrostatistics conference in Canada. They also advocate developing statistical standards for astronomy, including publication policies for statistical requirements in major journals, and helping develop "a standard curriculum in astrostatistics at the graduate level through collaboration with international organizations". They further propose leveraging international connections to recruit postdocs and faculty trained in these areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formation of a CASCA astrostatistics chapter or WG as the vehicle for international collaboration
#### Constraints
- International-level programmatic risks are less easily mitigated by Canadian-based astronomers and statisticians alone

### Target Learners
- Graduate students and postdocs in astronomy seeking advanced statistical training

### Target Learning Goals
- Graduate-level astrostatistics curriculum and statistical reporting standards in astronomy research
