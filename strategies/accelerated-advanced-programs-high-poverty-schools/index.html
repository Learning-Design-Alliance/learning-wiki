Offer high achievers in high-poverty schools accelerated and advanced programs, plus explicit support for college-knowledge and academic-behavior skills¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To close the performance gap that persists despite equivalent growth, the report recommends that high-poverty schools provide their top students the accelerated and advanced coursework standard in wealthier schools, and go beyond academics to teach Conley's college-readiness dimensions. It specifies attention to academic behaviors such as self-management, time management and persistence, and contextual skills and awareness such as college culture, affording college, and admission procedures, noting this programming may require additional resources.
