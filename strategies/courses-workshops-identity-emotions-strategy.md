# Organize courses and workshops that strengthen academic identity and positive emotions and control negative emotions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation directs senior educational managers to act on the regression findings by running structured courses and workshops. It states: "it is recommended that senior educational managers at the university facilitate the improvement of academic identity, positive emotions, and academic enthusiasm while also controlling negative emotions in students by organizing relevant courses and workshops." It further recommends fostering vibrant cultural, social, and academic environments and updating curricula with more practical courses and student involvement in decision-making.

## Design Implications
