## Description
The authors recommend that "future research should explore the longevity of educational interventions and the use of digital decision‐support tools and simulations to improve assessment accuracy and inter‐rater agreement". This strategy is forward-looking: the study itself did not implement digital decision support or simulation, and the authors flag it as an untested direction for addressing residual inconsistencies in ICDSC screening.
