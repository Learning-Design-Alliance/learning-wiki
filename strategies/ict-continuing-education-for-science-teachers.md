## Description
A significant proportion of surveyed teachers express a need for continuing education to use ICT effectively and appropriately. "A significant proportion of them ex- press the need for continuing education in order to better understand how to use ICT effectively and appropriately." The article frames this training as an essential lever for building teachers' confidence, improving mastery of digital tools, and promoting balanced use of simulations and real experiments, calling it a prerequisite for exploiting ICT's potential while preserving hands-on activities.
