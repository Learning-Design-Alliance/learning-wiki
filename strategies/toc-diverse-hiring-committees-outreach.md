# Diverse hiring committees and intentional recruitment practices including HBCU/MSI outreach and higher-education partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants proposed hiring committees that include teachers of color as core members and advisors who tap existing TOC networks, arguing administrators 'need purposeful intention and commitment to diversity and anti-racism with systemic policies and practices.' Additional recommendations include visiting HBCUs, MSIs and other institutions to build district brand recognition, and partnering with higher education to create school-to-career pipelines with student teaching opportunities.
