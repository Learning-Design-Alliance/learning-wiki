Future research should widen samples, respondents and institutional comparisons for O&M and social adjustment studies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In its Future Study Directions section the article calls for expanding research beyond the current resource-limited Islamabad sample: "The future studies should expand the horizon of research on the same parameters and also take a bigger sample size for future research." It further recommends gathering views from siblings, trainers and peer fellows to validate findings, catering for discrepancies in O&M training across special education setups, and exploring the mediating effect of different institutions for policy making.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Future studies should address the resource and access limitations that confined the current data collection to Islamabad-based institutions

Constraints¶

  • The current study's convenience sample of 125 children from Islamabad and Rawalpindi limits generalisability across Pakistan, as the authors state

Target Learners¶

  • visually impaired children in special education institutes across Pakistan

Target Learning Goals¶

  • standardised O&M training procedures in the national curriculum
  • policy making informed by wider evidence

Claims¶

  • The study sample was predominantly male and drawn mostly from public special education institutes in Islamabad [+W]
