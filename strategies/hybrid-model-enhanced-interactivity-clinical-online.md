# Adopt a hybrid model and enhance interactive online elements to bridge the gap between online and face-to-face clinical teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because online teaching could not fully replace face-to-face teaching for clinical practice abilities, the authors recommend a hybrid model in which online and face-to-face teaching complement each other, and future improvements focusing on enhancing interactive elements in online teaching platforms. They specifically suggest incorporating artificial intelligence based standardized patients, three-dimensional anatomy tools, virtual simulation classrooms, and image interpretation systems to support clinical skill development, and implementing small-group online teaching for simpler skills training such as catheterization, abdominal puncture, and lumbar puncture.
