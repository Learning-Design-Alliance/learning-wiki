# Connecting Concepts to Procedures

## Description
Conceptual understanding must precede and coincide with instruction on procedures. Learning is supported when instruction on procedures and concepts is explicitly connected in ways that make sense to learners and are iterative. Conceptual foundations lead to opportunities to develop reasoning strategies, which in turn deepens conceptual understanding; memorizing an algorithm does not. Examples of explicitly connecting procedures and concepts can be found in the Additional Resources section.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Demands thorough preparation, creative instructional strategies, and access to resources that effectively illustrate connections. Requires educators to deeply understand both concepts and procedures.
#### Constraints
- Conceptual understanding and instruction can be time-consuming, resource-intensive, and may not be suitable for topics with limited real-world applications.
