# Use science fiction, with care, as a route for teaching modern physics concepts such as relativity to young learners

## Description
A strategy, attributed to Krauss (1998) and Stannard (1991), of using science fiction to teach modern physics. The article reports Krauss 'argues that, with care, it may be productive to use science fiction to teach physics', and notes Stannard applied this approach by writing about relativity for 11 year olds, offering an accessible route into counter-intuitive physical ideas.
