# Distribute educational kits with in-service teacher training through local institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Photonics Explorer rollout pairs material distribution with teacher professional development: "Science teachers will receive this material along with an in-service training course from a local institution." The kit will be translated into 7 languages and handed out free-of-charge to science teachers at teacher training courses, ensuring teachers can apply the inquiry-based framework rather than receiving equipment alone.
