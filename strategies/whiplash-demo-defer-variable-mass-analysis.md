# Demonstrate the solution's validity limit in class via the rope's whip-lashing, and reserve the full variable-mass analysis for intermediate mechanics courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends two concrete classroom moves. First, the validity limit of the energy solution can be shown empirically: "This fact can be demonstrated to students in the classroom, by observing some whip-lashing behavior of the rope before its whole length goes over the pulley." Second, the teacher "must leave the complete analysis of the problem, from the point of view of a variable mass system, for students in intermediate courses of mechanics", keeping the beginning-student treatment to the analogy-based methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A uniform flexible rope and a small frictionless pulley for the demonstration
#### Constraints
- The complete variable-mass analysis is deferred to intermediate mechanics courses

### Target Learners
- beginning physics students
- students in intermediate courses of mechanics (for the deferred analysis)

### Target Learning Goals
- connecting analytical validity limits to observable physical behavior
- recognizing limits of Newton's second law forms
