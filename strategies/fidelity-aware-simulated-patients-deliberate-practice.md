# Use fidelity-aware simulated patients as a low-risk complement to supervision for deliberate psychotherapy practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors position the system as supporting deliberate practice — repeated, goal-directed exercises with immediate feedback in controlled environments — rather than replacing supervision. The system "is intended to support deliberate practice by enabling experimentation, reflection, and immediate feedback in low-risk settings", offering unlimited practice where standardized patients and supervision are resource-intensive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback aligned with established fidelity criteria (ACT-FM dimensions)
- Clinically plausible patient behavior grounded in real transcripts
#### Constraints
- Intended as a complement to traditional supervision, not a replacement

### Target Learners
- Psychotherapists in training
- Practicing psychotherapists

### Target Learning Goals
- Deliberate practice of ACT interventions
- Experimentation with alternative therapeutic responses
