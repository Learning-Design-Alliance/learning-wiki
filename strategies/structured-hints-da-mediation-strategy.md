## Description
The article demonstrates a step-by-step hint procedure during DA mediation, illustrated in Figure 1 with four graduated hints (H1–H4) for a vocabulary item about 'permeability.' Hints progress from simple rejection (H1) through contextual clues (H2, H3) to explicit answer revelation (H4). The article states that "through some preordained hints, the researchers tried to dynamically assess every single item of the pretest."
