# Deploy VolumetricOR across four proposed application areas for surgical learning and training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes '4 areas of application to further validate the potential and impact of VolumetricOR': training junior physicians so they 'acquire procedural knowledge in an authentic way before going to the operating room', extending professionalization for residents when on-site training is limited, using machine-readable three-dimensional geometric data as 'a tool for quality assessment and control', and giving recruitment candidates an immersive insight into operating-room work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Site-specific volumetric capture based on the hospital's own operating room infrastructure and workflows, plus validation studies to establish potential and impact.
#### Constraints
- In later, more experienced career stages, the added value will probably drop, as physicians will have sufficient on-site experience.

### Target Learners
- junior physicians
- residents
- surgical assistants and nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural knowledge acquisition
- communication of new techniques and workflows
- realistic insight into operating-room tasks and working situations
