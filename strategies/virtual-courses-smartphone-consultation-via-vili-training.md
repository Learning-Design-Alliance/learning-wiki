# Use open free virtual courses and real-time smartphone consultation with specialists to train and support first-level providers in VIA/VILI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For health professionals working in first-level care, especially remote areas, the authors propose two support mechanisms: an open free virtual course on VIA/VILI, and smartphone photography with real-time specialist consultation when in doubt during the procedure. They note the smartphone approach "could be useful in low-resource settings, although more research is needed to stablish a solid recommendation."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the Geneva Foundation for Medical Education and Research virtual course; for smartphone consultation, a reachable specialist in real time
#### Constraints
- The smartphone approach is explicitly flagged as needing more research before a solid recommendation

### Target Learners
- health professionals working in first-level care, especially in remote areas

### Target Learning Goals
- competence in performing VIA and VILI cervical cancer screening
