# Draw Time When Felt Emotion

## Description
Learners draw pictures of a time when they felt specific emotions and share with classmates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials for drawing. The facilitator requires a prompt.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be introverted and prefer not to participate.

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance emotional expression, improve self-assessment, promote empathetic understanding

### Affordances
- Engages learners in self-assessment, helps them consider different types of emotions.

### Personalization
- The facilitator can offer several prompts for students. Learners may be introverted or extroverted and the facilitator can consider those preferences.

- The facilitator can observe how the learners perform. Ask learners to do an individual reflection of how they used emotions and what they learned from the activity.
