# Co-op structure with three mandatory internships, including an extended eight-month placement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program alternates class semesters with paid work placements. Internships "account for at least 30% of the program", giving graduates about a year of relevant experience by graduation. The first internship follows the fourth semester and students are encouraged to seek classical software development roles; the second and third are combined into an extended placement after the fifth semester, meeting industry preferences for longer placements and enabling international internships. Industry interviewees reported internships are "less beneficial if limited to only four months" and that longer internships should be privileged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paid work placements arranged with employers
- A two-credit course (Opportunities in Quantum Science and Technology) placed before the eight-month internship to prepare students for opportunities
#### Constraints
- The extended placement is described as less common at the university

### Target Learners
- undergraduate QIS students from the third year onward

### Target Learning Goals
- professional integration and workplace experience in quantum and classical software development
