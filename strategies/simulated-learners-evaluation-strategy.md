# Use simulated learners to scale up formative evaluation and component validation, without replacing summative studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Simulated learners are software systems or agents designed to reflect the population of intended learners and scaled up to provide as large a sample size as desired, addressing bias and small sample sizes in experimental evaluations. The authors state they "can help with formative assessments of learning environments or in validating specific components of a system". They cite Vanlehn et al. (1994) as a survey of prospects and Matsuda et al. (2014) as a recent example.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Avoid overfitting the simulated learner to the learning environment
#### Constraints
- Overfitting may lead to unrealistic learning predictions
- Cannot replace full summative evaluation and studies of educational impact; students and system may behave in unanticipated ways in real situations

### Target Learners
- researchers evaluating adaptive learning environments

### Target Learning Goals
- formative evaluation and component validation of learning environments
