# Specialized facilitator training for implementing RCDP-based simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that instructors implementing RCDP receive specialized simulation training beyond traditional immersive simulation preparation. Instructors "received specialized simulation training on skills specific to the implementation of RCDP-based SBME, such as giving real-time, rapid feedback and guiding deliberate practice and overlearning through facilitation of multiple rounds of the same simulation." As part of this training, instructors facilitated RCDP simulations and received expert feedback on their facilitation effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All facilitators received similar simulation instructor training covering simulation design, debriefing, and methodology
- Opportunity to facilitate RCDP simulations and receive expert feedback on facilitation
#### Constraints
- At the implementing institution, all faculty were members of the anesthesiology department at a single institution

### Target Learners
- simulation facilitators and instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- delivering real-time rapid feedback
- facilitating deliberate practice and overlearning
