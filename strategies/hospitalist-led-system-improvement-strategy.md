# Hospitalists should lead system-level improvement activities for procedural care and training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each procedure section includes a System organization and improvement domain directing hospitalists to leadership actions: "Lead, coordinate, and/or participate in institutional efforts to standardize the use and availability of kits and equipment used to perform" the procedure, plus quality improvement programs monitoring performance and supervision of procedural competence. The emergency procedures section adds establishing ongoing multidisciplinary training and consolidating emergency equipment in accessible locations.
