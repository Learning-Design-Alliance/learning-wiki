# Curating Resources

## Description
Curating involves selecting and arranging readings, videos, and other resources to scaffold concepts. This helps students build their understanding step by step, with carefully chosen materials that support each stage of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires careful selection and organization of resources, and clear communication of the learning path.
#### Constraints
- May limit student autonomy if not done carefully. Requires instructors to invest time in resource selection and organization.

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Scaffold learning, provide relevant resources, facilitate deeper understanding of concepts

### Affordances
- Provides a clear learning path, ensures access to relevant and high-quality resources, and supports progressive understanding of concepts.

### Personalization
- Can be personalized by allowing students to choose resources, providing varied formats, and offering resources at different levels of difficulty.
