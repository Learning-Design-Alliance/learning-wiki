## Description
The article describes how teachers can evaluate designs using "unit evaluations and questionnaires, verbal and written reflections from students, data from digital platforms, and assessment data". Evaluating a design not only improves it but helps teachers understand how to design for learning in their specific context. This complements general knowledge from research and best practices.
