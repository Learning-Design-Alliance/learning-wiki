# Run time-limited contests with small prizes that reward participation over performance to elicit posting in small online communities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that online community managers "consider the use of contests as a low-cost and effective way to generate participation, especially contests that reward participation over performance." In the case study, all but 1 of 6 contests centered on posting, used token prizes such as a water bottle, and the four most popular threads were prize threads. Contest formats included staff-favorite judging, first-time-poster drawings, and a shared healthy-snack idea thread.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time-limited contest structure and small prizes or rewards to offer winners
#### Constraints
- Some contests discouraged members who did not meet goals; a repeated contest near program exit drew only 4 replies

### Target Learners
- adult participants in an Internet-mediated walking program

### Target Learning Goals
- increased posting and participation in online community forums
