# Clarification of Vocabulary, Symbols, and Language Structures

## Description
Supporting learners to construct meaning from words, symbols, and numbers using different representations. This involves providing clear definitions, explanations, and examples of complex terms and concepts. Clarifying language structures helps learners understand the relationships between ideas and improve comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear and concise explanations, visual aids, glossaries, and translations.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of vocabulary and concepts to ensure relevance and appropriateness. Some learners may find simplified language to be condescending.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those with language disabilities, English language learners, and learners encountering new or complex material

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve comprehension, enhance vocabulary acquisition, support understanding of complex concepts, clarify language structures

### Affordances
- Enhances understanding of complex terms and concepts. Supports vocabulary acquisition. Improves comprehension of language structures.

### Personalization
- Provide definitions, examples, and visual aids to clarify vocabulary and concepts. Use simplified language and sentence structures. Offer translations and glossaries for English language learners.

- Assess learner understanding of vocabulary and concepts through quizzes, tests, and discussions. Monitor learner use of clarified language structures in their own writing and speaking. Gather feedback on the effectiveness of clarification techniques.

## Impact
- Improved comprehension: Research indicates that clarifying vocabulary and language structures can significantly improve comprehension, particularly for learners with language disabilities and English language learners.
