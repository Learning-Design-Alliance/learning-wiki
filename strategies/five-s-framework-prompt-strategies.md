# Use the Five S framework to scaffold students' prompt writing with generative AI in chemistry education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Five S framework is a prompt-engineering scaffold for educators and students using generative AI, comprising five strategies: "set the scene", "be specific", "simplify language", "structure output", and "share feedback". In the reviewed study A1, chemistry teacher candidates performed exercises using ChatGPT and were informed about the Five S framework, then evaluated their prompts and answers. The review presents it as a solution to the risk of copying unverified AI-generated answers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need opportunities to evaluate their prompts and the AI's answers, as in study A1 where students evaluated their prompts and answers after using ChatGPT.
#### Constraints
- The review reports it as a response to accuracy and reliability risks in chemistry education contexts; the article does not print evidence of its effectiveness beyond the study's survey-based evaluation.

### Target Learners
- pre-service chemistry teacher candidates
- middle and high school chemistry students

### Target Learning Goals
- prompt writing skills
- reflective and critical use of generative AI
- chemistry problem solving
