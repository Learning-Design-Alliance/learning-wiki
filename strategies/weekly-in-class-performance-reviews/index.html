Strategies

Review application usage and performance statistics with students in class at least weekly¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The author recommends that teachers using self-directed computer applications implement and lead an in-class performance or measurement review of student output on at least a weekly basis. In the study, usage and performance data were presented in Excel to all group members as a sort of "scoreboard" to help learners stay focused and motivated. The author states this process provides immediate feedback, maintains learner focus and motivation, and gives the teacher insights from continuous data analysis.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The teacher must have access to usage and performance data from the application to present to students

Constraints¶

  • The author asks how results would differ without the frequent in-class reviews, noting the reviews were not experimentally isolated as a factor

Target Learners¶

  • first-year undergraduate English learners in Thailand
  • adult learners of English not living in a native English speaking country

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English language proficiency
  • self-directed learning habits
  • self-efficacy and motivation
