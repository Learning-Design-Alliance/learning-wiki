## Description
The article recommends that modern technological educational programs be supported by outdoor activities, arguing that students’ susceptibility to environmental issues may only be increased by strengthening their link to nature. It suggests such combined programs can be practiced on students and examined at different levels of the educational hierarchy, balancing outdoor activities with technological education.
