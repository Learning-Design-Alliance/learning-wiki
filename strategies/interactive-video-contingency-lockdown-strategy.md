# Adopt interactive educational videos when face-to-face meetings are not possible

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes interactive educational videos as a contingency strategy for situations where face-to-face teaching is not possible, such as during pandemic lockdowns. The authors state that 'when face-to-face meetings are not possible (e.g. during lockdowns due to the COVID-19 pandemic), a switch to online interactive educational videos could be made without fear of falling short of necessary programme outcomes.' This recommendation is grounded in the finding of no significant difference between video and demonstration outcomes.
