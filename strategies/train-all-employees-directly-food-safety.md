# Provide food safety training directly to all employees, including non-managerial staff, rather than relying on train-the-trainer filtering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that every employee working in any aspect of hot and cold self-service food bars participate in additional food safety training focused specifically on food bar issues, because the study found managerial employees' knowledge was not effectively distributed to non-managerial employees. It states: "All employees who work in any aspect of the hot and cold self-service food bars within the grocery stores should be required to participate in additional food safety training."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be delivered directly to non-managerial employees rather than filtered through managers
#### Constraints
- Recommendation arises from a purposively selected, non-probabilistic sample whose results the authors state cannot be generalized to a larger population

### Target Learners
- managerial and non-managerial grocery store food bar employees

### Target Learning Goals
- essential food safety knowledge for hot and cold self-service food bars
