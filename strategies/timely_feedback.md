# Timely Feedback

## Description
Timely feedback involves providing feedback as soon as possible after the learner demonstrates proof of learning. This approach helps the learner connect the feedback with the action and reinforces learning in a confident manner. When feedback is delayed, the moment is lost and the learner may not make the connections between the feedback and their work.

## Design Implications
