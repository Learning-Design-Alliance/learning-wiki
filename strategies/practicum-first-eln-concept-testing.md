# Test and evaluate strengthened research-data documentation concepts first in a teaching practicum, then transfer the short-cycle lessons into research-group workflows.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose piloting more strongly structured lab-book concepts in the practicum before applying them to research: "Aufgrund der abgeschlossenen Praktikumseinheiten sind dabei kurze Lern- und Innovationszyklen möglich". Experiences from these short cycles are then fed into the longer-term, more complex documentation of research in the working group, aided by computer-science concepts such as Entity-Relationship diagrams. This links curriculum innovation directly to the group's research data management.
