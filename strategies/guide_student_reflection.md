# Guide Student Reflection

## Description
Teachers use many strategies to guide students through a period of reflection. These include discussions, interviews, questioning, and logs and journals. Discussions are about problem-solving processes. Invite students to share their metacognition, reveal their intentions, detail their strategies for solving a problem, describe their mental maps for monitoring their problem-solving process, and reflect on the strategy to determine its adequacy.
