# Co-develop a dementia care competency framework with persons living with dementia, care partners and health professionals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that the review findings be used to focus planned co-development of a holistic competency framework for dementia care training. The authors state they "will also utilize the findings of this review to focus the planned co-development of the final competency framework with persons living with dementia, care partners and health professionals", and that such a framework, when co-developed, could align educational efforts with the aspirations and goals of individuals living with dementia.
