# Life Back Then

## Description
Learners pick a time in history and a character who lived then, researching and presenting this to the class. This activity encourages learners to view an unfamiliar world through the eyes of the people who lived in it, fostering historical empathy and understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources for research, a space for presentations and discussions, and time for individual reflection.
#### Constraints
- Learners may rely on stereotypes or lack access to accurate information about the past. It requires careful guidance from the instructor to ensure accurate and thoughtful analysis.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who are studying history and want to connect with the lived experiences of people from the past

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote historical empathy, develop research skills, encourage perspective-taking, improve communication skills

### Affordances
- Promotes historical empathy by exploring the lives of people from the past. Develops research skills by investigating different time periods and cultures. Improves communication skills through presentations and discussions.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose a time period and character that resonate with their interests and values. The level of research and presentation can be adjusted to their abilities.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assess the depth of research, the accuracy of representation, and the level of empathy demonstrated in the presentation. Monitor changes in learners' attitudes towards different historical periods and cultures.

## Impact
- Historical empathy has been shown to promote tolerance, understanding, and a sense of connection to the past. Researching different time periods also enhances historical knowledge and critical thinking skills.
