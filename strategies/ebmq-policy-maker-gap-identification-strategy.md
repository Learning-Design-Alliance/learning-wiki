# Administer the EBMQ to help policy makers identify knowledge, practice and barrier gaps and improve EBM uptake

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the validated EBMQ as a large-scale gap-identification tool: administering it lets policy makers locate weaknesses in doctors' EBM knowledge, practice and barriers and target improvement efforts. The article states that "Information gathered from the administration of the EBMQ will assist policy makers to identify the level of knowledge, practice and barriers of EBM and to improve its uptake in clinical practice."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated instrument such as the EBMQ must be available in the respondents' language of understanding
#### Constraints
- The authors caution the findings are not generalizable beyond the recruited cohort

### Target Learners
- primary care physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- identify gaps in evidence-based medicine knowledge and practice
- improve uptake of evidence-based medicine in clinical practice
