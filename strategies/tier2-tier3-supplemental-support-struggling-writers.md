# Supplement whole-class SRSD instruction with intensive Tier II or Tier III support for struggling writers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because whole-class SRSD instruction produced smaller, non-significant gains for struggling writers at maintenance, the article recommends additional support for these students. It states that "the smaller growth seen for struggling writers indicates a potential need for more intensive intervention, such as would be provided in Tier II or Tier III supplemental instructional time in a response to intervention (RtI) model." Teachers are encouraged to carefully monitor students' written expression progress throughout instruction and provide opportunities for practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers monitor students' written expression progress throughout instruction and provide opportunities for practice
#### Constraints
- Gains for struggling writers at maintenance were not statistically significant (d = 0.57, p = .070)

### Target Learners
- Struggling writers and students with disabilities in Grade 5 and 6

### Target Learning Goals
- Persuasive writing elements and written expression skills
