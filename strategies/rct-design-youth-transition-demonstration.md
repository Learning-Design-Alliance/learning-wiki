# Use a randomized controlled trial design to evaluate a transition-services demonstration for youth with disabilities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes how the Vermont DVR supported a rigorous evaluation by relying on "a randomized controlled trial design, recruiting 803 participants." Random assignment of enrolled youth to LLC or a control group allowed the evaluation to estimate program impacts on service use, postsecondary education, and employment up to two years after enrollment. This strategy suits state vocational rehabilitation agencies seeking credible evidence on transition programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Capacity to recruit a large participant pool (803 in this demonstration) and randomize between treatment and control
#### Constraints
- Impacts were measured only up to 24 months after enrollment, while most youth were still attending high school

### Target Learners
- high school students with disabilities in transition programs

### Target Learning Goals
- college and career readiness outcomes
