# Differentiated support for university students according to personality profile

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's professional implications recommend tailoring support to personality profiles: students high in neuroticism and anxiety and low in self-esteem "will need teacher support, receiving positive feedback after successful outcomes" and programs for anxiety and stress management; highly extraverted students may need help with distractions; students low in conscientiousness may need an external regulatory context promoting self-regulation; highly open students involvement in routine practices; highly agreeable students training in assertiveness. Teachers can also organize activities flexibly according to students' affect and performance needs.
