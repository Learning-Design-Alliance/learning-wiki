# Combine Visual Clot with ROTEM and merge quantitative data into the visualization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participants' perceptions, the article recommends integrating quantitative information into the Visual Clot platform. Several interview participants said they could benefit from combining Visual Clot and ROTEM when interpreting coagulation assays, and 16 of 23 survey respondents stated that such a combination would help them manage complex hemostatic situations. The authors write that "An integral facet of advancing the technological framework informed by the results of this study lies in the prospect of merging quantitative data into the Visual Clot platform and presenting this merged information in a consolidated interface."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A consolidated interface that aligns quantitative and qualitative data, for example by direct overlay of numerical values onto the Visual Clot visualization or parallel juxtaposition of a complementary graphical representation
#### Constraints
- The recommendation responds to the identified lack of a quantitative component and is grounded in single-center user perceptions at a university hospital

### Target Learners
- anesthesia care providers managing perioperative coagulation, especially clinicians inexperienced in coagulation management

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate interpretation of viscoelastic coagulation tests and timely targeted coagulation treatment decisions
