# Use particle-in-a-box visualization to connect nodal structure to energy states before applying the model to real molecules

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The lesson strategy has students use Maple's plotting functions to visualize particle-in-a-box energies and wavefunctions, providing 'the necessary background and understanding of the relationship between higher energy states and their corresponding nodal structure.' Students then apply the model to a chain of hydrogen atoms and to cyanine dyes, comparing computed molecular orbital nodal structures to the expected particle-in-a-box wavefunctions, and approximate peak absorption wavelengths from the HOMO-LUMO gap.
