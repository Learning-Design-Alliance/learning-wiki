# Use classroom comparison of video analysis and smartphone sensors to discuss experimental and numerical concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends presenting the same experiment analysed by both video analysis and smartphone sensors so students can compare the two techniques. It states that "the comparison between these two techniques offers an opportunity to discuss not only concepts related to the specific experiment but also with the experimental and numerical aspects including their pros and cons", turning the tools' complementary strengths and limitations into teaching content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experiments suited to being analysed simultaneously with both tools
- Access to smartphone sensors and video analysis software such as Tracker
#### Constraints
- The comparison should address the tools' pros and cons, since sensor-derived and numerically derived quantities differ in noise and accuracy

### Target Learners
- high school students
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- concepts of the specific experiment
- experimental and numerical aspects of measurement
