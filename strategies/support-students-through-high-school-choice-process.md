# Provide students consistent GoCPS messaging, accessible school information, experiential learning opportunities, and adult support throughout the choice process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's Insights and Implications sections enumerate what students need from educators and platforms during high school choice: easy linkable information on GoCPS searchable on phones, up-to-date details on offerings, chances to hear from current students, generative adult discussions scaffolding school comparisons, consistent and correct messaging about ranking and extra requirements, and adult help completing supplemental requirements. The authors state students need "Emotional and concrete support" in this stressful process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated staff time and school-based support for the search and application process, as the NLCI schools provided through the 6to16 curriculum and counselors
#### Constraints
- The authors note not all elementary and middle schools have the level of support the studied schools had

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade students choosing high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- navigating school search, application, and selection processes
