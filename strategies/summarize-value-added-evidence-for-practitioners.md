## Description
Because researchers have presented value-added evidence in varied ways, the brief's strategy is to highlight and summarize recent studies in a consistent format for practitioners. It states: "This brief highlights and summarizes three recent IES studies," aiming to help practitioners draw lessons from data that is otherwise "challenging" to interpret.
