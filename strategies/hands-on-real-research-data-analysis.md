## Description
The ATLAS outreach program's central strategy was giving students access to the same data scientists use: "students can explore the same particle collisions that thousands of scientists around the world use in their research." Students reconstructed decays of short-lived particles with lifetimes around 10^-10 seconds using detector data, with guidance from physicists, as a first step toward Higgs-event analysis. The article identifies exploring particle collisions and contact with physicists among the subjects that particularly interested students and the public.
