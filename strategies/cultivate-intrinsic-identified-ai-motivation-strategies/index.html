Cultivate identified and intrinsic AI motivation through curriculum integration, activities, workshops, and real-world AI applications¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Because introjected regulation dominated the AI motivational system but is not the most optimal form of motivation, the authors recommend that "educators are encouraged to implement strategies that help students nurture their identi ﬁed and intrinsic motivation." Concretely, they propose incorporating AI-related content into curriculum design, organizing AI-related activities and workshops, and introducing real-world applications of AI, listing specific activities such as AI knowledge competitions, project-based AI learning, AI-powered games, and AI ethics debates, and embedding topics like natural language processing, machine learning, and AI ethics into non-technical subjects.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires curriculum space and instructor capacity; the article suggests working with teaching and learning centers to train and equip university instructors with AI skills.

Constraints¶

  • The article frames these as discussion-based recommendations, not tested interventions; the study itself was an exploratory cross-sectional survey.

Target Learners¶

  • university students in AI-enriched courses, including non-STEM majors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • identified and intrinsic motivation to learn AI
  • understanding the value and real-world applications of AI

Claims¶

  • Intrinsic motivation was strongly associated with identified regulation, and introjected regulation with external regulation, in the AI motivational network [+W]
  • Introjected regulation is the most central node in university students' AI motivational system [+W]
  • Within SDT, intrinsic motivation predicts intended effort most strongly, followed by identified regulation, with introjected regulation much weaker [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Build students' competence in AI-enriched environments by choosing appropriate tools, giving clear guidance, establishing role models, and offering frequent encouragement
