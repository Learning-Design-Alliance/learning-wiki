# Develop a wider variety of CCS covering diverse oral diseases and transition to mobile platforms to broaden access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on students' strong emphasis on understanding diverse oral pathologies within limited clerkship hours, the authors propose "developing a wider variety of Computer-based Case Simulations covering different types of diseases and optimizing the allocation of clerkship time". They further suggest transitioning CCS from desktop to mobile platforms (building on their Bilibili video-based learning and potential App development) to enhance accessibility during clinical rotations, and integrating more AI-assisted tools to address current limitations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional development of simulation scenarios and optimization of clerkship time allocation
#### Constraints
- Motivated by constrained clerkship hours and the need to cover diverse oral pathologies

### Target Learners
- non-dentistry medical undergraduates in stomatology clerkships

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding diverse oral pathologies
- clinical diagnostic and therapeutic thinking
