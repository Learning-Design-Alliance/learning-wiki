# Extend OSCEs with self-reflection, improvement planning, and peer feedback questions to make skills assessment learner-centered

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second edition revised the objective structured clinical evaluations by adding 5 questions that prompt providers to self-reflect on their performance, formulate a plan for improving behaviors or practices for the next resuscitation, and receive feedback from peers or facilitators. This made the OSCEs more learner-centered and usable as both summative and formative evaluation, addressing frontline complaints that the assessments were cumbersome, confusing, and potentially biased.

### Context
