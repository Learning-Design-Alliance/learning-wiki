# Implement targeted error-mitigation strategies—expert-reviewed benchmark validation, clinician oversight, and continuous updating—when using LLMs in neurosurgical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given the models' limitations on image-based and interpretative questions, the article recommends targeted error-mitigation strategies: "systematic validation against expert-reviewed benchmarks, integration of clinician oversight, and continuous updating with validated clinical data" to safely leverage LLM strengths in neurosurgical education and clinical practice. It also recommends that implementation guidelines emphasize the complementary role of these tools rather than their use as primary educational resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systematic validation against expert-reviewed benchmarks
- Integration of clinician oversight
- Continuous updating with validated clinical data
#### Constraints
- The article states LLMs remain supplementary rather than definitive solutions, and their reliability in image interpretation falls well below clinical standards

### Target Learners
- neurosurgery residents
- medical educators
- clinicians using LLMs in training contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- safe integration of LLMs into neurosurgical training and assessment
