# Screen Readers

## Description
Screen readers convert text into spoken audio, enabling learners to listen to written material. This strategy benefits learners with strengths in auditory processing by allowing them to access information through their preferred modality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to screen reader software, training on its use
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all types of text, some learners may prefer visual reading

### Affordances
- Leverages auditory strengths, provides an alternative to traditional reading, enhances comprehension

### Personalization
- Offer training on screen reader software, allow learners to adjust reading speed and voice preferences
