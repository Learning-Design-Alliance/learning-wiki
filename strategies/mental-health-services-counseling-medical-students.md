# Provide mental health services and periodic counseling to support medical students' self-esteem

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
Based on the finding that one in four medical students showed low self-esteem and that mental health conditions negatively affect it, the authors recommend that issues affecting students' mental health be discussed and addressed in medical schools. They state that "Strategies to improve students' self-esteem are vital in educational systems" and that "the role of mental health services is imperative", alongside periodic counseling and coping strategies to boost student morale.
