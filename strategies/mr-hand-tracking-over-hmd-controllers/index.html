Prefer mixed reality with hand tracking over head-mounted-display controllers for gesture-simulation training¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 review), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends developing mixed reality environments using hand tracking so students physically practice gestures, rather than using HMD controllers. It states it "would be better to establish an MR environment, using hand tracking and physically practicing the skills, rather than using the HMD controllers", reducing learner burden from unfamiliar environments and enabling experiences similar to reality.
