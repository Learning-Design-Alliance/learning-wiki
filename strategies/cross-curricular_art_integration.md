# Cross-Curricular Art Integration

## Description
Integrating art with other subjects like science and history to enhance learning experiences. This includes activities such as leaf rubbings for plant identification, painting fish stamps for science, and historical propaganda creation and song analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Art supplies, coordination with art teachers, and subject-specific content.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to integrate art meaningfully with other subjects.
