# Search ADS by first author and year, then filter by field and refereed status to locate a specific paper

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For finding a specific assigned paper, the guide recommends the SAO/NASA Astrophysics Data System (ADS), whose powerful search functions support filtering by author, abstract, title, and body text. The demonstrated workflow is searching first-author name and year (using the caret to force first-author matching), then using sidebar filters such as astronomy subject and refereed versus non-refereed status to narrow to the desired paper.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the ADS database at ui.adsabs.harvard.edu
#### Constraints
- ADS is described as an automated system collecting published sources, unlike arXiv where anyone can upload; author-name ambiguity may require filtering and context clues

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- locating specific scientific papers independently
- distinguishing peer-reviewed from non-refereed work
