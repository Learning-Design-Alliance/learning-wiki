# Link instructional videos and checklists to a QR code for on-demand student access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Upload self-created demonstration videos and supplementary materials (instructional sheets, checklists) to a shared drive and link them via a QR code freely accessible to students. The article notes QR codes' "speed, readability, and enormous storage capacity of text, images, and URLs" as primary benefits, enabling students to revise techniques as needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A QR code linked to videos uploaded to an accessible platform such as Google Drive
#### Constraints
- Videos must be validated before being made accessible to the community

### Target Learners
- 2nd-year MBBS pharmacology students

### Target Learning Goals
- Independent revision of practical skills
- Learner autonomy
