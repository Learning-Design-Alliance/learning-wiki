# Announce assessment results with privacy protection and a bounded inquiry period

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After marking, the TA tabulates results in a spreadsheet and may post an announcement in the LMS if the instructor allows. The guide stresses privacy: "instead of listing all the names with their scores, perhaps listing student IDs and their score might provide some privacy." Regular weekly assessments may take online inquiries, while midterm and final examinations should use offline inquiries. Students are told there is a time limit for inquiries, otherwise the TA may end up explaining repetitively throughout the semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor permission to post results in the LMS.
- A declared inquiry window so explanations do not repeat all semester.
#### Constraints
- Score changes after the inquiry period must be reported to the instructor and students; unresolved disputes are escalated to the instructor with a picture of the answer.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- graduate teaching assistants

### Target Learning Goals
- transparent feedback on assessment results
- student privacy protection
