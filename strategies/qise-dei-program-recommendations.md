# Leverage QISE program development to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in engineering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames new QISE programs as an opportunity to address equity: "QISE presents extraordinary opportunities to work towards rectifying issues of inclusivity and equity that continue to be pervasive within engineering." Its recommendations include equitable and inclusive learning environments, partnerships between community colleges and four-year institutions to remove transfer barriers, opportunities at minority serving institutions, industry roles in promoting diversity, and demystifying the field rather than emphasizing its exotic attributes to attract a larger, more diverse student population.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Deliberate attention to career trajectory examples, mentoring relationships, and varied initial exposures to engage a broad range of potential future quantum engineers
#### Constraints
- The article notes that upskilling programs are unlikely to increase workforce diversity because the fields contributing to QISE are not gender, racially, or ethnically diverse

### Target Learners
- students from underrepresented groups
- transfer students from two-year colleges
- students at minority serving institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- sense of belonging and self-efficacy in QISE
- recruitment and retention of diverse cohorts
