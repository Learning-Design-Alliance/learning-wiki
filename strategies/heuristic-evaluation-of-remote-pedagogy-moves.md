# Use a heuristic evaluation method to examine how educators' pedagogical moves relate to the technologies in use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article offers a heuristic evaluation method educators can use to reflect on their remote instruction: examining how their pedagogical moves connect to the technologies they are using. The abstract states the paper introduces "a heuristic evaluation method to focus on how those moves might relate to the technologies they are using." It is intended as a practical tool educators can apply while facilitating remote learning, building on the heuristic method of Nacu et al. (2018).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must be able to identify their pedagogical moves and the technologies they use so the relationship between the two can be evaluated
#### Constraints
- The method is heuristic and practice-oriented rather than an empirically validated instrument

### Target Learners
- Educators facilitating remote learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving educators' instructional practice in remote environments
