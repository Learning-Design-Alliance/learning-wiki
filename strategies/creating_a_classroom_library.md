# Creating a Classroom Library

## Description
Setting up a classroom library involves acquiring books and materials, organizing the shelves, creating labels, and making the space cozy. This approach encourages learners to find joy in selecting and reading books they will love. Organization can be based on genres, topics, themes, Accelerated Reader (AR) levels, or guided reading levels. Book bins can be used to display books by their covers, increasing the likelihood of learners selecting them. Involving learners in deciding categories can enhance engagement and ownership.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A collection of books, shelving or bins, labels, and space to create the library. Requires initial setup and ongoing maintenance.
#### Constraints
- May require significant initial investment to build a diverse library. Requires ongoing maintenance to ensure the library remains organized and engaging.
