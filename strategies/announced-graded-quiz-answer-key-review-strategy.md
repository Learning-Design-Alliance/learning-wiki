# Program announced, graded fill-in-the-blank quizzes with immediate in-class answer-key review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concrete quiz recipe: quizzes were announced in the syllabus and reviewed at the first class, administered at the start of quiz class periods, and graded with feedback returned promptly. After each quiz "the instructor displayed the answer key on a screen and answered any questions about the quiz" before continuing with application and review questions. This combines retrieval practice with immediate feedback, which the article identifies as a moderator of quiz effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quizzes must be scheduled and announced in advance so students know when quiz conditions occur
- An answer key must be displayed and reviewed with students before the class proceeds
#### Constraints
- The article notes feedback as a moderator was identified from prior reviews, not manipulated in this study; quiz grade contingencies were not varied and may matter

### Target Learners
- college students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- retrieval practice of assigned course readings and improved exam performance
