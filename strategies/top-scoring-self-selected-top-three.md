# Top scoring: students self-select their top three ideas for a fluency-independent creativity rating

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the final step of the structured task, top scoring (Silvia et al., 2008) is applied: rather than rating every idea a student produced, "the method of top scoring has been developed to assure a fixed number of ratings for every student and a fluency-independent measure". Students themselves select and label their three most creative ideas after the administrator defines a creative idea as original, complete, and easily applied in practice. This keeps the rated set comparable across students who generated different numbers of ideas.
