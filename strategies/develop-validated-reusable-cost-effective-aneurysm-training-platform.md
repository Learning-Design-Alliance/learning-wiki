# Develop and validate a reusable, cost-effective, high-fidelity training platform and build homogeneous assessment tools for aneurysm clipping simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward-looking recommendation is that future research should focus on "developing and validating a reusable, cost-effective, high-fidelity training platform" and that "there is a need to build homogenous assessment tools and validate the role of simulation training in education". It suggests combining the OSAACS and SAMBA scoring systems and combining technological methods, for example creating a mixed environment using augmented reality technology and incorporating dynamic flow on VR systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A consensus on assessment methods should be reached to compare different studies
#### Constraints
- Implementing hands-on training is limited by simulator availability, supply chain, replacement of head micro-anatomy when ruptured, the training site's economic situation, and mentorship availability

### Target Learners
- neurosurgery residents
- neurosurgeons in training

### Target Learning Goals
- microsurgical aneurysm clipping skills
- patient safety in aneurysm surgery
