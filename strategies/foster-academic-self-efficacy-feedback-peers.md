# Fostering students' academic self-efficacy through feedback, encouragement and peer-success modeling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Given the strong association of self-efficacy with achievement, the authors recommend that "Educators can foster students' academic self-efficacy by providing frequent positive feedback, encouragement and guidance." They add that group activities allow observing peers succeeding, which motivates imitation, and that parents can nurture self-efficacy by engaging in academic activities, praising deserved efforts and giving honest feedback on failure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and parents delivering frequent feedback, encouragement and guidance; group activities where peers can observe success
#### Constraints
- The article notes sources of self-efficacy can differ across cultures and further research is needed on which factor matters most for Lebanese adolescents

### Target Learners
- Secondary school adolescents

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic self-efficacy and intention to achieve good grades
