# Use real-time feedback devices during CPR skills training to correct compression quality in real time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (6 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
During resuscitation training, give learners a feedback device that displays compression depth, rate, and chest relaxation so they can correct technique as they perform. The article states the device displays these parameters, "allowing for correction of quality of CCs in real time", and its randomized cross-over simulation study found significant improvements in depth, rate, and relaxation when the device was used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A feedback device displaying depth, frequency, and relaxation in real time; brief instruction and device familiarization before practice
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from simulated conditions with 2-min compression cycles, not real resuscitation; participants were inexperienced trainee doctors

### Target Learners
- trainee doctors
- novice resuscitation providers

### Target Learning Goals
- high-quality chest compressions meeting guideline parameters
