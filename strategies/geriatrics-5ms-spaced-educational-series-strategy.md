# Deliver the Geriatrics 5Ms modules as individual sessions in a spaced longitudinal educational series to promote knowledge retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a future implementation in which the workshop modules are delivered "as individual sessions as part of a longitudinal educational series." They argue the Geriatrics 5Ms serve as "a unique organizing principle that can be easily applied to a spaced educational series and further promotion of clinical knowledge retention." This format would allow expansion of modules (e.g., distinct Multicomplexity and Matters Most modules) and additional patient-centered practical exercises such as more deprescribing exercises in the Medication Module.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thematic integration across sessions, which the authors note "can be challenging"; institutional scheduling support for longitudinal delivery
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a future direction, not an evaluated implementation; the authors also call for a more robust knowledge assessment and analysis by training level

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents in primary care
- Adaptable to medical students, nurse practitioner or physician assistant trainees, and practicing clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- Retention of core geriatric clinical knowledge over time
- Expanded depth in individual 5Ms domains such as deprescribing and prognostication
