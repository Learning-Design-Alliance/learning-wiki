# Prioritize operational-setting protocols, longitudinal expertise tracking, team dynamics, and real-time monitoring as next steps for educational neuroimaging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review identifies areas ripe for further research: establishing viable study designs and methods for operational settings, since studies embedded in realistic clinical tasks are scarce; examining team dynamics, as one included study showed it was feasible to use wireless functional neuroimaging to measure workload and team engagement in simulated clinical settings; conducting longitudinal studies tracking brain activation changes to individualize and optimize training; and developing sophisticated and affordable wearable devices for live patient care environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robust methodology for sample size considerations with respect to the research question, which the review says is needed as the field matures
#### Constraints
- The review notes logistical impediments remain an important barrier to combining measurement modalities, and that team dynamics in fluid clinical settings are quite complex

### Target Learners
- medical trainees and health professionals in simulation-based and clinical settings

### Target Learning Goals
- expertise development
- teamwork and crisis event management
- neurocognitive engagement monitoring
