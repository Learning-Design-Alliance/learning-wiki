# If It Happened to You...

## Description
This activity enhances empathy and emotional regulation by presenting students with scenarios from YA novels or short stories, stripped of character names and page numbers, and asking them to discuss how they would approach the situation from their own perspectives. They can prepare skits, short stories, poems, or presentations to share their ideas and justify their choices. Afterwards, they read the original scene and discuss the character's approach versus their own, focusing on the motivations behind their decisions and how emotions affect behaviors. This fosters introspection, dialogue, and empathy, while also revealing personal biases and perspectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of appropriate YA novel or short story. Preparation of 'skeleton scenarios' without character names or page numbers. Facilitation of group discussions and presentations.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of scenarios to ensure relevance and sensitivity. Success depends on the instructor's ability to facilitate meaningful discussions and create a safe space for sharing.
