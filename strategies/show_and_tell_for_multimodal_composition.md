# Show and Tell for Multimodal Composition

## Description
Devoting a few minutes each week for learners to show the effects of different multimodal combinations or tell about their multimodal composition process creates a space akin to an author’s chair in writing workshops. Learners explain their design decisions and receive constructive feedback from their peers. These learner-led discussions generate concrete examples of the various processes that can be used for multimodal composition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time for show and tell sessions. Needs a structured format for learners to present their work and receive feedback.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who are hesitant to share their work or provide feedback. Learners may need support in providing constructive feedback.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Encourage learners to reflect on and articulate their design decisions. Provide constructive feedback to peers. Generate concrete examples of multimodal composition processes.

### Affordances
- Promotes metacognition and self-reflection. Enhances peer learning and collaboration. Provides opportunities for authentic communication and feedback.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by allowing learners to choose the multimodal combinations they want to showcase. Feedback protocols can be customized to meet the needs of different learners.
