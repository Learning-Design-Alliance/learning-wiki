Strategies

Socialize PBL with teachers and provide easy guidance before using PBL-based teaching material¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on interviews and field findings, the article recommends that "teachers must understand the teaching material Civic education based PBL deeply for more effective learning, need to be socialized about PBL before learning." Teacher feedback and suggestions were made into a lesson plan so applying the learning is easy. The teacher plays a facilitator and guide role, and understanding of PBL greatly affects learning quality.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training or socialization in PBL before the lesson begins

Constraints¶

  • Students were still difficult in reviewing the problems without teachers' help

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school teachers
  • fourth-grade elementary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective implementation of PBL-based civic education learning

Claims¶

  • Nine experts rated the PBL-based civic education teaching material eligible for learning, with an average final score of 92% [+W]
  • A teacher assessment of the PBL-based material in the limited test rated all aspects 85%, declared eligible for learning [+W]
  • Civic education teaching material based on PBL improved fourth-grade students' learning outcomes in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study [+W]
  • Mean student test scores rose from pretest 62.46 to posttest 81.21 after using the PBL-based civic education material [+W]
