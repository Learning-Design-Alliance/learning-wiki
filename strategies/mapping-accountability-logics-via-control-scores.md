# Map accountability logics across countries by computing school-level external control scores in key school functions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article offers a systematic approach for mapping accountability logics: survey school principals on who holds significant responsibility for assessment policies, hiring teachers, choosing learning materials, and deciding budget allocations; code each item external (1) or internal (0); compute country-average external control scores; and classify countries as consistently external, mixed, or consistently internal. The authors state their "findings provide a systematic approach for the mapping of accountability logics across countries."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires principal self-report survey data on responsibility for assessment, human resource, curriculum, and budget decisions
- Requires classification criteria such as the article's absolute 10%/90% thresholds, relative ±1.5 SD cutoffs, or subjective distributional break points
#### Constraints
- Country-level findings need careful interpretation because five sub-national entities were included and some countries' response rates did not support reliable estimates
