# Structured 12-hour simulator-based ultrasound training program in individual hands-on sessions over six weeks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The FESIM III training program consisted of an introductory 90-minute seminar, two tutor-supervised 30-minute introductions, then individual one-hour sessions twice weekly totaling 12 hours of self-training. The training "focused on the correct acquisition of standard planes as an essential basis for any sonographic assessment", and trainees could also learn standard planes in pathologic cases. Progress was checked with standardized tests every two weeks under examination conditions with simulator aids removed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One-hour sessions twice weekly over six weeks with access to the US Mentor simulator
- An introductory seminar plus supervised introductions before self-training
#### Constraints
- The number of trial participants had to be kept limited because one simulator was shared with a parallel study

### Target Learners
- US novices such as medical students
- residents and Ob/Gyn specialists in basic care

### Target Learning Goals
- second-trimester standard plane acquisition
- extended prenatal screening protocol skills
