# Civic education teachers foster an open classroom climate to prevent negative emotions and increase enjoyment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on the finding that an open classroom climate relates to more enjoyment and less anxiety and boredom, the article recommends that teachers co-create a supportive emotional climate. It states: "Schools and teachers can try to improve the democratic climate during discussions by discussing current events (Siegel-Stechler, 2021), fostering student-teacher relationships, and students' collective efficacy for changes at schools (Maurissen et al., 2018)." The abstract notes fostering openness "can prevent the experience of negative emotions and increase students' experience of enjoyment."

## Design Implications
