Prioritize high-quality RCTs with standardized assessment tools and subjective outcomes in future flipped classroom research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend that "Future studies should prioritize high-quality RCTs with standardized assessment tools and explore subjective outcomes such as student satisfaction". They also recommend "Multicenter and cross-cultural research" to validate and generalize results. This addresses limitations of the current evidence base, which used self-designed, non-standardized measurement tools and focused only on objective outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Standardized assessment tools for theoretical and skill outcomes
  • Multicenter and cross-cultural study designs

Constraints¶

  • Current findings are limited to Chinese populations and objective outcomes; generalizability to other educational or cultural settings remains limited

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate clinical medicine students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Theoretical knowledge
  • Practical clinical skills
  • Student satisfaction and engagement

Claims¶

  • Publication bias is present in the flipped classroom meta-analysis evidence base [-W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Limit pre-class video length to 20 minutes to reduce student burden
  • Future flipped-classroom research should be well-designed, sufficiently powered RCTs with low risk of bias reported per CONSORT
  • Future active-learning research should use randomised controlled designs with larger samples and standardised outcome measures
