# Provide pre-assessment training to raters before video-based quality assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In its discussion of limitations, the article recommends training raters before they score videos. It states that "literature suggests that pre-assessment training for the raters could contribute to more consistent results in video-based quality assessment", and that despite the tool's simplicity, such training could potentially elevate ICC scores by fostering consensus when assigning scores to specific criteria from the same video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A training or explanation session on how to use the assessment tool and when to assign specific scores, delivered before raters begin scoring
#### Constraints
- In this study no such training session was given, so the recommendation is prospective rather than tested here

### Target Learners
- surgical residents and surgeons acting as video-assessment raters

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent and reliable scoring of surgical video quality
