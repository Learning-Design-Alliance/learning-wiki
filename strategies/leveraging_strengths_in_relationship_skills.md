# Leveraging Strengths in Relationship Skills

## Description
This method involves an educator identifying specific learner strengths and/or challenges related to relationship skills and uses or justifies aligned strategies to leverage learner strengths to support learning. This involves the ability to form and maintain healthy connections with others by communicating, cooperating, and managing conflict effectively. It also requires the capability to resist inappropriate social pressure and offer or seek help when appropriate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of learner strengths and challenges related to relationship skills. Implementation of aligned strategies to leverage strengths. Observation and documentation of the impact of the strategies.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful identification of learner strengths. May require time and effort to implement strategies effectively. Success depends on understanding individual needs.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who struggle with relationship skills. Appropriate for elementary through adult learners.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve learners' ability to form and maintain healthy relationships, communicate effectively, cooperate, and manage conflict. To help learners advocate for themselves, develop good listening skills, and compromise.

### Affordances
- Focuses on strengths to address challenges. Supports the development of crucial social and emotional skills. Promotes positive relationships and effective communication.

### Personalization
- Strategies can be tailored based on the learner's strengths and challenges. Different activities and supports can be used based on individual needs and age levels.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assessments should focus on identifying specific strengths and challenges related to relationship skills. Teachers can observe learners’ interactions with peers and adults to assess their communication skills, cooperation, and conflict management abilities. Reflection questions may be helpful to the learner to consider their own interactions and skills.

## Impact
- Learners show improvements in academic performance and social development when educators focus on their strengths and foster relationship skills. Research: Sung, Y. Y. & Chang, M. (2010). "Which Social Skills Predict Academic Performance of Elementary School Students?" I-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, 3(3), 23-34.
