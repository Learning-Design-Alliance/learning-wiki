# Create a List of Positive Self-Talk Statements

## Description
Generating a collection of affirmative statements for learners to read and internalize. This list serves as a resource to counter negative thoughts and promote a positive mindset. Learners can choose from pre-made lists or create their own personalized affirmations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a list of positive affirmations or the ability to create one. Time for reflection and personalization.
#### Constraints
- May feel superficial if not accompanied by genuine reflection and effort. Some statements may not resonate with all individuals.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Offers a tangible tool for combating negative thoughts. Allows learners to personalize their self-talk, making it more meaningful and effective. Promotes self-awareness and reflection.

### Personalization
- Learners can select statements that resonate with them or create their own based on their needs and experiences. Lists can be tailored to specific challenges or goals.

## Assessment Evidence
- Learners' ability to recall and use affirmations in challenging situations. Self-reported changes in mindset and behavior.

## Impact
- Research suggests that affirmations can positively impact self-esteem and reduce stress. Having a personalized list can empower learners to actively manage their thoughts and emotions.
