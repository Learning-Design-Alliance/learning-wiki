## Description
Based on the findings, the article recommends that "Physics teachers in Abuja should adopt GITM in the teaching of light and sound waves in Abuja secondary schools and they should be given adequate training on how to use the method". It further recommends that policy makers, school administrators and curriculum planners consider GITM when planning, designing and reviewing the Abuja secondary school physics curriculum, especially in the sub-topics of light and sound waves.
