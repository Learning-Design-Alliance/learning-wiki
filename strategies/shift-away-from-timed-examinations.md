# Shift assessment away from inauthentic timed examinations toward more scientifically authentic evaluation activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a comprehensive change in assessment style in large introductory STEM courses: moving away from a dominant reliance on timed examinations, which the authors associate with gendered performance differences, toward evaluation using more scientifically authentic activities. The authors write: "The best approach may be a wholesale shift toward evaluation using more scientiﬁcally authentic activies and away from a dominant reliance on inauthentic timed examinations." As an interim measure while courses retain timed examinations, they recommend supportive interventions such as values-affirmation and mindset interventions, delivered online at scale across a wide range of disciplines.
