Integrate AI literacy across grades and subject-area learning rather than confining it to electives¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that educators 'integrate AI literacy within the topics they already teach, such as math and ELA.' Because library media and computer science courses are typically electives not mandated for all students, and computer science courses 'disproportionately enroll white males,' integrating AI literacy into core subjects promotes AI literacy for all students, particularly females and students of color. Cross-disciplinary integration also lets AI literacy become 'a value-add to disciplinary learning and not an add-on to an already overscheduled school day.'

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate computational thinking into existing disciplinary teaching across grade bands rather than adding it on
  • Integrate data and analysis into existing K-2 teaching practices through classroom graphs such as birthdays and word walls.
  • Integrate metacognitive strategy instruction across content areas and across before, during, and after phases of teaching
  • Integrate AI assistants with existing curricula and teaching practices rather than replacing instruction
  • Integrate computational thinking across K-12 subject areas rather than treating it as computer-science-only
