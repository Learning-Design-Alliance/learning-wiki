# Use a web-based role-playing game to present clinical communication scenarios to preclinical health-professions students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests the potential use of the digital game "as a media for presenting clinical scenarios for students, in a lecture-based class." The strategy gives preclinical students, who lack patient contact, a vicarious context for dentist-patient interaction before clinical training begins, and can be delivered fully online during disruptions to on-site education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios scripted and evaluated by senior clinicians or educators for clinical relevance and discussion value
#### Constraints
- The study does not show that students with greater changes perform better in actual clinical communication later

### Target Learners
- preclinical undergraduate dental and medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the context of clinical dentist-patient interaction
- motivation and self-efficacy for learning communication skills
