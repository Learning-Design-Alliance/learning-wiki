# Experienced faculty can gain agency in restrictive contexts by mentoring newer instructors within the same local context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In her restrictive military institution, Cleopatra recognized her context-dependent expertise and acted as a resource for newer instructors, with a new instructor shadowing me, and watching my class to see how the labs go and how the discovery exercises go. The article presents this mentoring as a supportive leadership influence available where formal positions of power are out of reach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recognized, experienced local teaching status within the same institution as the mentees
#### Constraints
- Drawn from one subject's self-reports in a restrictive military institution; the article presents it as an interpretation of her case, not a tested intervention

### Target Learners
- newer physics faculty
- tenure-track physics instructors

### Target Learning Goals
- navigating local institutional context
- developing physics teaching practices
