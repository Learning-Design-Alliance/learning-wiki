# Apply mind mapping in mathematics teaching through overview, preview, inview, and review stages

## Description
The article describes four application stages for learning with mind mapping: "Overview is a comprehensive review of a topic durin g the learning process started", followed by "Preview, early reviews are a continuation of the ov erview", then "Inview, in-depth review sutau which is the core of the learning process", and finally "Review is a review carried out before the end of ja mpelajaran". Teachers can use these stages to structure topic study with mind maps, including a Master Mind Map summarizing a semester's topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher delivers content and learning objectives before students construct mind maps
- Students work in groups to create mind maps of studied material
#### Constraints
- For very simple materials, the preview step can be skipped so it goes directly to the Inview step

### Target Learners
- junior high school mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
- mastering mathematical concepts
- mathematical reasoning
