# Four institutional strategies for improving blended learning in medical imaging education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the practical implication section the authors recommend that educational institutions enhance student satisfaction and behavioral intention by improving perceived ease of use and perceived usefulness through four strategies: introducing VR and AR technologies to provide near-real operational experiences; providing educators specialized technical training and resources such as teaching guides and best practice cases; enhancing teacher-student communication via regular information sessions, expert lectures, and interactive platforms; and continuously improving course content based on regular satisfaction surveys and feedback collection with incentive mechanisms for student participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support including technical training for educators and adequate resources
- Technological infrastructure capable of supporting online platforms and simulation equipment
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of medical imaging education at a single institution; the authors note generalizability requires validation in other contexts

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical imaging students
- Medical imaging educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved satisfaction, engagement, and practical skill mastery in blended medical imaging courses
