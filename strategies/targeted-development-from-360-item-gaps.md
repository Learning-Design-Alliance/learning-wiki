# Use 360° competency assessment results to target professional development at low-scoring behavioural items

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using item-level MCAP 360° results to design individual professional development programs. Behavioural items receiving a mean score less than five suggest the manager "would benefit from further training", and the rich per-manager information "supports the supervisor to design an individual professional development program, targeted at both specific behavioural items highlighted for development" and items contextual to the organisation and role. The authors also recommend further emphasis on implementing an evidence-based approach to management in HSM professional development curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of the MCAP 360° assessment by self and colleagues
- Supervisor engagement with low-performing managers to identify reasons for lower competency scores
#### Constraints
- Self-assessment is recognised as a potentially inaccurate method of assessing competence; the study measured subjective perspectives only

### Target Learners
- Middle-level health service managers and their supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthening evidence-informed decision-making, resource management and healthcare environment knowledge competencies
