# Plan for regional sustainability from the start with a two-year transition to regional ownership

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The blueprint prescribes a sustainability timeline for regional pathways work. Its regional sustainability components are to "Plan for sustainability from start," provide "2-yr foundational support that transitions to regional ownership and leadership in YR3-4," and build "State infrastructure that fosters sustainability." This pairs with school-system capacity outcomes: capacity to sustain pathways focused on expanding opportunities for learners and capacity to apply the co-research and co-design process to regional challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Foundational support during the first two years
- state infrastructure that fosters sustainability
#### Constraints
- Regional ownership and leadership are expected only in years three and four, after the foundational support period

### Target Learners
- learners in regional pathway programs

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained access to pathways opportunities across a region
