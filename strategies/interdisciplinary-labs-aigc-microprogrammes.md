# Structured cross-disciplinary mechanisms: interdisciplinary laboratories and 'AIGC + discipline' micro-programmes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address literacy disparities between disciplinary clusters, the article proposes two strategies: "interdisciplinary laboratories where STEM learners lead technical development while humanities and social sciences learners contribute to scenario design and ethical assessment", and 'AIGC + discipline' micro-programmes that embed technical application units into humanities curricula and technological-ethics modules into STEM pathways. It also recommends embedding AIGC across the learning cycle, from foundational concepts in general education to discipline-specific applications in upper-level courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured collaboration mechanisms between science and social science education
- Curricular inclusion of modules on ethical analysis and validation of AIGC outputs
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a single-institution design with non-random sampling, which the authors state may limit generalizability and introduce selection bias

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students across disciplinary communities

### Target Learning Goals
- AIGC literacy: using and evaluating AI-generated content
- motivational efficacy (value, skill, usage)

## Claims

- [AIGC literacy (using and evaluating) differs significantly across communities, with science-dominant students outperforming humanities and social sciences peers](../claims/community-differences-aigc-literacy.md) [+W]
- [Motivational efficacy differs significantly across the four disciplinary communities, declining from science-dominant to humanities and social sciences](../claims/community-differences-motivational-efficacy.md) [+W]
- [Motivational efficacy partially mediates the negative effect of moving from science to humanities communities on Using AIGC, with skill efficacy the largest indirect path](../claims/mediation-community-using-aigc.md) [+W]
