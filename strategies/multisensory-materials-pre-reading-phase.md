# Use colourful, attractive, and purposeful multisensory materials at the pre-reading phase to draw students' attention to the reading text.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Elementary school English teachers can use colorful, attractive, and purposeful multisensory materials which hit visual, auditory and kinesthetic learning styles at the pre-reading phase to draw students' attention to the reading text and motivate them to do reading tasks. This strategy involves touching, feeling, and having a product outcome and succeeding together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A variety of literacy and visual materials provided to extend exposure beyond classrooms
#### Constraints
- It is difficult to convince pupils to stay in their places when colorful and attractive multisensory materials are first presented

### Target Learners
- elementary school English learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reading motivation
