# Use flexible, context-specific BLS training strategies — mobile learning, small-group simulation, train-the-trainer, routine refreshers and remote supervision — in conflict settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend resource-efficient interventions tailored to conflict settings: "leveraging mobile-based educational tools, conducting targeted simulation exercises in small groups, establishing train-the-trainer programs to build local capacity, and adding short BLS refreshers into routine clinical workflows", plus remote certified trainers via online platforms. They also recommend integrating BLS into medical and nursing curricula and requiring certification before graduation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified trainers available remotely when in-person training is not possible
#### Constraints
- Designed for wartime conditions with limited resources; the article states these are practical ways to maintain skills under challenging conditions in Gaza

### Target Learners
- Doctors and nurses in conflict-affected hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Basic life support knowledge and skills
- Trauma and cardiac life support certification
