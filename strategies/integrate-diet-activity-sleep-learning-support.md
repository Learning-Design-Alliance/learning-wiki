# Integrate a healthy diet, physically active lifestyle, and adequate sleep to support brain development and learning in school-age children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that schools and parents support learning by integrating multiple health behaviors rather than targeting diet alone. It states that "integrating a healthy diet, physically active lifestyle, and adequate sleep may provide optimal circumstances for brain development and learning", since diet, physical activity, and sleep appear related to cognition partly independently of each other but with modest effect magnitudes. The recommendation rests on the review's synthesis of observational and intervention evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
