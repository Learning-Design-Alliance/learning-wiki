# Provide accessible caregiver resources to reinforce phonics at home

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Family Support Hub gives families instructional materials, comprehension prompts, and QR codes linking to short video lessons that model decoding strategies, so caregivers can reinforce phonemic awareness, phonics, and fluency at home. The article states these tools "empower families to reinforce phonemic awareness, phonics, and fluency skills at home, strengthening the school-home connection and boosting students' motivation and confidence in reading."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Families need access to the Family Support Hub and devices to scan the QR codes linking to video lessons.
