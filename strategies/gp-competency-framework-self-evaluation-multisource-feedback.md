# Use the tertiary-hospital GP competency framework for self-evaluation and multisource feedback in medical education and quality improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (2 associational, 1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends applying the constructed framework in medical education, general practice research and quality improvement, with two concrete assessment uses: "self-evaluation by GPs or multisource feedback by others who work with GPs". This addresses the reported absence of competency evaluation tools for GPs in tertiary hospitals in China.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The framework's indicators must be applied in the context of tertiary hospital general practice departments
- Further evidence from implementation in real practice settings is needed to establish effectiveness
#### Constraints
- Different indicators may be deemed more or less relevant depending on the stakeholder audience
- Nurses and patients were not represented among the experts

### Target Learners
- General practitioners in tertiary hospitals in China

### Target Learning Goals
- Professional competency self-assessment
- Multisource competency feedback
