# Demonstrating Caring

## Description
Demonstrating caring is a powerful way to build positive relationships with learners. When your actions and words communicate that you sincerely care for your learners, they are more likely to want to perform well for you and enjoy coming to school. Demonstrate you care by inquiring about aspects of their personal lives, standing by the door and welcoming learners as they enter the classroom, empathizing with learners, listening intently to learners, and expressing gratitude for their accomplishments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must care about and be sincere when interacting with their learners.
#### Constraints
- Must make sure to be equitable with all learners and avoid the appearance of having favorites.
