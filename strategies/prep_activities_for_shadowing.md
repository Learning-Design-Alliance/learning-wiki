# Prep Activities for Shadowing

## Description
This involves creating learning goals for the shadowing day, choosing a student to shadow, and coordinating with the student, their parents, and school staff to ensure a smooth and meaningful experience. It also includes questioning one's own assumptions about students and the school environment to approach the shadowing experience with an open mind.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for planning and coordination, a clear shadowing protocol, and willingness to question one's own assumptions.
#### Constraints
- Requires effort to coordinate with students, parents, and staff. May be challenging to set aside preconceived notions and approach the experience with a truly open mind.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Educators, school leaders, and product designers who are planning to shadow a student and want to maximize the value of the experience.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Prepare educators for a meaningful shadowing experience, set learning goals, choose an appropriate student to shadow, coordinate logistics, and foster an open mindset.

### Affordances
- Ensures the shadowing experience is focused and purposeful. Helps educators identify their learning goals and select a student who can provide valuable insights. Facilitates communication and coordination with key stakeholders. Encourages an open and unbiased approach to observation.

### Personalization
- Educators can tailor their learning goals, student selection criteria, and coordination efforts to align with their specific needs and interests.
