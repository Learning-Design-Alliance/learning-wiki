# Package of policy and teaching recommendations to reverse the decline in physics enrollment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article closes with twelve recommendations spanning schooling and higher education: "Community colleges and state colleges should give more full-time positions instead of hiring adjuncts for teaching those courses"; banning calculators in elementary schools to build hand-calculation skill; reducing teaching load; using instructor evaluations as input data rather than the sole job-extension criterion; subject-degree requirements for math and science teachers; and more interdisciplinary courses linking physics to daily life and other sciences.
