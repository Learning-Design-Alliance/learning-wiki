# Institutional screening, prevention and gender-oriented mental health support for students in wartime universities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that Ukrainian higher education institutions implement measures for monitoring, prevention and treatment of student mental health problems during war. They write that "there should be implemented measures for timely monitoring of students' mental conditions, prevention of mental health disorders, screening for mental health problems, and the implementation of modern interventions", including digital prevention tools and online interventions adapted to students' wartime needs. At policy level they propose government funding for screening, psychosocial support and group programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment: the authors state universities should prioritize students' mental health as a strategic objective and increase funding for psychological support cabinets
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from an associational cross-sectional study; the authors state the design does not confirm a direct cause-and-effect relationship

### Target Learners
- university students in Ukraine during wartime, with targeted support for female students

### Target Learning Goals
- protecting academic performance and human-capital development by reducing mental health burden
