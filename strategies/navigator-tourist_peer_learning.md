# Navigator-Tourist Peer Learning

## Description
Pair struggling learners ('tourists') with learners who have mastered the content ('navigators'). Empower navigators to teach a lesson or skill to tourists. Navigators ensure tourists understand directions, clarify confusion, and encourage them to stay on task. Tourists ask clarifying questions and collaborate with navigators to answer problems. This model fosters peer learning, strengthens communication skills, and supports academic mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires identification of learners with mastery, careful pairing of learners, clear role definitions, and monitoring of group dynamics.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure all learners are engaged and supported. Needs clear expectations to prevent misuse of power or off-task behavior. Requires ongoing feedback and guidance.
