# Enhance systematic guiding and training of students throughout the PBL course, with attention to critical thinking dispositions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that PBL tutors and curriculum managers recognise that students' critical thinking dispositions influence PBL outcomes, and support students across the whole course rather than only at the start. They state that "Systematic guiding or training of students, which would help them to acclimatize to PBL pedagogy and learning methods, should be enhanced throughout the whole course", and that tutor training should be improved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors and curriculum managers who recognise the potential influence of critical thinking dispositions on PBL outcomes
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a correlational study at a single institution; the authors note the 102-student sample is limited

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in PBL curricula

### Target Learning Goals
- acclimatisation to PBL pedagogy and learning methods; improved PBL performance
