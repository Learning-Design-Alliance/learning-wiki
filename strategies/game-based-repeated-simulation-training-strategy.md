# Game-based simulation training with repeated sessions for surgical instrument recognition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training strategy in which trainees repeatedly practice instrument recognition and instrument use across consecutive simulation sessions, with gamified scoring, timed challenges, and post-session dashboards providing motivational feedback. The article notes that 'interactive game-based learning is an emerging technology, which can improve residents' motivation and engagement in simulation-based learning.' In the trial, trainees completed three sessions per module, and performance improved significantly with repetition on both PeriopSim modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulation platform with gamification elements such as scoring and timed challenges, repeated practice sessions, and performance dashboards shown after each session
#### Constraints
- Transference of skills from simulation training to the real neurosurgical operating room had not yet been evaluated
- The total-score algorithm was weighted toward time saved to encourage anticipation of correct instruments

### Target Learners
- postgraduate year 1 neurosurgery residents
- novice surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- surgical instrument recognition and anticipation
- application of instrument knowledge during simulated procedures
