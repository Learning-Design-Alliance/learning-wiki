# Encouraging Self-Advocacy

## Description
Self-advocacy involves understanding one's needs, knowing what support might help, and communicating those needs to others to promote independence and problem-solving. This can involve direct instruction, models, and practice opportunities. Anonymity can help overcome barriers to asking for help. Educators can foster self-advocacy by teaching students about their racial identity and privilege, and by confronting their own biases. The goal is to empower students to disrupt systems of oppression for themselves. Specific strategies include establishing goals, and recognizing available resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time for self-reflection, opportunities for communication, willingness to address personal biases.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners to identify their needs or communicate them effectively. Requires educator to be aware of and address their own biases.
