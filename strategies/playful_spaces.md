# Playful Spaces

## Description
Bringing fun back into learning by creating playful spaces. Play helps teams work together effectively and creates meaningful learning engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of activity options such as games and toys. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the playful spaces.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Increase collaboration, improve creativity, promote fun

### Affordances
- Promotes collaboration and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred activities. Provide a variety of playful activity options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more fun.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that playful spaces can increase engagement, improve creativity, and enhance collaboration. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
