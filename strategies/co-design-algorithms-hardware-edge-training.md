# Train researchers in the co-design of algorithms and hardware to enable data-reduction decisions earlier in the processing pipeline

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research direction stemming from SMARTHEP aims to transition real-time AI from traditional computing clusters to the edge of enhanced experimental detectors, embedding ML directly into sensor electronics. The article states "training is needed on the co-design of algorithms and hardware, enabling data reduction decisions to be made even earlier in the processing pipeline"; many SMARTHEP PIs now pursue this in the EPIGRAPHY COST Action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training on the co-design of algorithms and hardware
#### Constraints
- Edge algorithms face strong constraints on latency and the available computing resources

### Target Learners
- PhD students and researchers in machine learning for high-energy physics

### Target Learning Goals
- Deploying ML on edge hardware such as FPGAs and GPUs
- Making data reduction decisions earlier in the processing pipeline
