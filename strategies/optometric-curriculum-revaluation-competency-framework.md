# Revaluate optometric academic training and create a national competency framework to support professional regulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends revaluating academic training in optometry in "number of years and content" and creating "a competency framework within the scope of national optometric practice", with possible expansion paths based on academic and professional training, as fundamental to advocating regulation of the profession in Portugal. The recommendation is grounded in the survey's findings that performed competencies are reductive and training needs concentrate in advanced and special-population areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A competency framework within the scope of national optometric practice, with possible expansion paths based on academic and professional training
#### Constraints
- The article notes "the need for further study of the subject"

### Target Learners
- Portuguese optometry students and trainees
- Practising optometrists undertaking postgraduate and continuous education

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical procedure competencies across the full internationally stipulated optometric scope of practice
- Confidence in performing advanced and special-population procedures
