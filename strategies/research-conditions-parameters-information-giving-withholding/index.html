Pursue research yielding conditions and parameters for when to use information giving versus information withholding interactions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 6 studies (2 causal, 2 review, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 qualitative), q2–q4 · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article's forward-looking recommendation is a research strategy: treat Cognitive Tutor experiments comparing forms of interactivity as partial answers to the assistance dilemma, and "emphasize the need for further science to yield specific conditions and parameters that indicate when and to what extent to use information giving versus information withholding forms of interaction." Designers and researchers should seek these conditions rather than assume one interaction form is universally best.
