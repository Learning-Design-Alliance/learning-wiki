# Fifteen recommendations for establishing and improving audio-visual administration in vocational and adult schools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on his interview data, the author recommends that Wisconsin Vocational and Adult Schools should "recognize that audio-visual services require the administration and supelwision of trained personnel" and "develop operational plans for audio-visual administration and supervision." The fifteen-point list spans staffing, policies, planning, training, facilities, records, and state leadership. It translates the study's eight documented deficits into concrete administrative actions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained personnel must administer and supervise audio-visual services
- Operational plans for audio-visual administration and supervision must be developed
#### Constraints
- Recommendations are grounded in one state's vocational and adult schools and address the deficits found there

### Target Learners
- instructors and audio-visual coordinators in vocational and adult education schools

### Target Learning Goals
- effective administration and supervision of audio-visual services and instruction

## Claims

- [Vocational and adult education schools in Wisconsin provided almost no administration or supervision of audio-visual functions for evening school programs](../claims/almost-no-av-administration-evening-school-programs.md) [+W]
- [Audio-visual coordinators in the surveyed schools lacked sufficient time and no organized procedures existed for previewing, evaluating, and selecting materials](../claims/av-coordinators-insufficient-time-no-selection-procedures.md) [+W]
- [The surveyed schools had no materials purchase policies, no long-range equipment planning, and budgets not based on planned needs of frequency-of-use and amortization](../claims/no-av-purchase-policies-or-long-range-planning.md) [+W]
- [Audio-visual in-service training was casual and unplanned, no administrative plan existed for materials production, and there was virtually no state-level audio-visual leadership](../claims/unplanned-av-in-service-and-no-state-leadership.md) [+W]
