# Use infographics in geography lessons where visual and information content are given together, especially where achievement and attitudes are low

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Infographics can be effectively and widely used in geography lessons in different grade levels and learning areas when visual and information are to be given together" and specifically suggests using them "in cases where achievement and attitudes of students in geography lesson is low". It further calls for studies to increase the use of infographics in geography syllabuses and textbooks and for nationwide studies in different branches.
