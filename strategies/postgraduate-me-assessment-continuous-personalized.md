# Assessment domain: direct, continuous, personalized assessment and feedback, including self-assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This domain covers all ways to test end users and formalize their knowledge gain. "Seventeen articles described assessment as most effective when used in a direct, continuous, and personalized way", and self-assessment was also identified as an important part of learning. The review also highlights assessing user experience and satisfaction, and continuing to provide the software and associated tools after the course ends.
