# Pay Attention, Pause, Paraphrase

## Description
Pay Attention, Pause, Paraphrase is a structured conversation strategy for small groups or pairs to promote active listening. Learners are assigned numbers, and only one learner speaks at a time. Others listen without formulating a response, focusing solely on understanding. After the speaker finishes, the listener pauses to paraphrase what was said before offering their own thoughts. Discussion sentence starters can support learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Requires careful facilitation to ensure equal participation, may feel unnatural at first
