# Match each study subject to the candidate intervention most likely to benefit them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that evaluators use its Bayesian approach to consider multiple candidate interventions simultaneously within one trial, matching each study subject with the intervention most likely to benefit that individual given their subgroup. This turns evaluation from estimating a single average effect into an allocation problem that learns what works for whom as data accumulate.

## Design Implications
