# Provide continued professional development, mentorship, and repeated navigation training for oncology nurse navigators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends continued professional development and institutional support for nurse navigation, noting that "these findings highlight the importance of continuing professional development, mentorship, and clear career-progression pathways for ONNs." Training frequency was associated with competency: nurses trained once scored significantly lower than those trained twice or three times, while twice-versus-thrice showed no significant difference. The strategy targets strengthening expert navigation and care coordination competencies, which showed the lowest mean score and strongest satisfaction associations respectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured navigation-related training programs and institutional support within the employing cancer center
#### Constraints
- The evidence is cross-sectional; the article states these associations should not be interpreted as causal effects, and the benefit of a third training session was not distinguishable from two

### Target Learners
- Oncology nurse navigators, particularly those early in the role

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthening expert navigation, care coordination, and other core navigator competencies
