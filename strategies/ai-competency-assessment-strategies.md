# Assess AI competencies continuously with formative quizzes, peer review, reflective journals, real-world summative projects, and portfolios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter recommends continuous evaluation of AI competencies to track student progress across the framework's dimensions. Formative strategies include regular quizzes on AI concepts, peer reviews of AI project proposals, and reflective journals. Summative assessments should "reflect real-world AI challenges," such as capstone projects with ethical and societal impact analysis, comprehensive exams, and industry-partnered projects. "Encouraging students to document their AI journey through portfolios can provide a holistic view of their developing competencies," supplemented by showcases and symposiums.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alignment of assessment tasks with the framework's knowledge dimensions and proficiency levels
#### Constraints
- Presented as recommended practice; the chapter reports no empirical evaluation of these assessment approaches

### Target Learners
- higher education students developing AI and data literacy

### Target Learning Goals
- demonstrating technical, ethical, and collaborative AI competencies
