# Redesign PBL practice with theory-based ingredients: peer feedback, concept maps in study teams, smaller sub-cohorts, and personalized task selection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes concrete redesigns for each principle: incorporating structured peer feedback on cognitive, social and motivational contributions; splitting PBL groups into study teams of three to four students who collaboratively prepare concept maps for discussion; splitting large cohorts into smaller sub-cohorts and creating student-staff partnerships and professional learning communities; and offering personalized curricula in which students analyze needs, set goals and select tasks and resources with autonomy-supportive support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and students need training; coaching and scaffolding by teachers is needed for self-evaluation and goal-setting
- Peer feedback is known to improve functioning for students who initially make poor contributions only under certain conditions
#### Constraints
- The paper states personalized learning still lacks a widely accepted definition and further evidence is needed; concept-map effectiveness might be dependent on the context in which they are used

### Target Learners
- health professions students in large-cohort PBL programs

### Target Learning Goals
- deep learning
- social connection and commitment
- self-directed learning
- engagement and ownership

- Self Directed Learning Principle Pbl
