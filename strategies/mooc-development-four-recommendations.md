# Four practical recommendations for MOOC developers: budget time, test materials, correct errors, and be responsive on discussion boards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors close with explicit advice for other MOOC developers: (i) "do not underestimate the time it takes to develop materials---this four week course took three years to complete"; (ii) test materials multiple times before release, watching every video, reading every transcript, trying every problem, and selecting alpha-testers from the target audience; (iii) "be prepared to correct your course as errors are found---no course is error free"; and (iv) "be responsive on the discussion boards," since limited staff participation is annoying to learners. These are implementable process recommendations drawn from the authors' own three-year development experience.
