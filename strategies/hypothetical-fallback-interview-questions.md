# Hypothetical fallback questions for unexperienced course features

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For features students have not encountered, the protocol instructs switching to a hypothetical framing. If the student says the course has not included real world topics, the interviewer asks how inclusion of such topics would impact learning; likewise for racial and cultural connections, and for feeling welcomed, the interviewer can ask what could be done or to reflect on a time the student did not feel welcomed in a course. This preserves data even when a feature is unused.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interviewer must first ask the direct question and establish the feature was not experienced before using the hypothetical

- Capture student perceptions of course design features they have not directly experienced
