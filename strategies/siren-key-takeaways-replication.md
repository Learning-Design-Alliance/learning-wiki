# Key takeaways for institutions adopting short-term team-based research programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
For institutions seeking to adopt similar short-term research experiences, the article recommends "establishing clear project goals early, ensuring a balance between structured training and open-ended inquiry, and leveraging peer mentors as an essential layer of support." Because the program "utilized low-cost equipment, modular workshops, and a peer-mentorship framework, it is both adaptable and replicable," the authors present SIREN as a practical, scalable approach for resource-limited institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear project goals established early
- A balance between structured training and open-ended inquiry
- Peer mentors integrated as an essential support layer
#### Constraints
- Some participants recommended mid-program check-ins or additional time for project development to enhance collaboration across groups

### Target Learners
- community college undergraduate students in STEM disciplines
- students at teaching-focused institutions with limited research access

### Target Learning Goals
- broadened access to authentic undergraduate research experiences
- transferable STEM skill development
