# Integrate structured CBCT interpretation training into undergraduate and postgraduate dental curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends targeted education in CBCT interpretation and clinical decision-making under diagnostic uncertainty, particularly for less experienced clinicians. It notes that "structured training in three‐dimensional imaging is essential for dental students" and that the European Society of Endodontology advocates integrating CBCT interpretation into curricula so future clinicians can "interpret it critically and apply it judiciously". This strategy responds to the finding that students reported greater difficulty with CBCT despite its additional information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluators or students need prior familiarity and minimum proficiency, as in this study where students underwent curricular training including theoretical instruction and supervised pre-clinical exercises
#### Constraints
- The article notes continuing professional education remains the primary training method for CBCT interpretation (69.2%), so curricular integration is not yet the norm

### Target Learners
- final-year dental students
- endodontic postgraduate trainees
- less experienced clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- CBCT image interpretation
- endodontic diagnosis
- treatment planning under diagnostic uncertainty
