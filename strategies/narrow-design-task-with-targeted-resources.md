# Narrow the design task and provide targeted structure–function resources to enable discipline-driven technology design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
After Y1 showed that a broad design challenge made it difficult for teachers to support students in modeling biological relationships, the team concluded that "a more constrained design task, supported by targeted (biology and engineering) structure–function resources, would better enable students to engage in discipline-driven technology design." Y2 specified a digging robot and provided detailed resources about four digging animals, explicit instruction on analyzing structures and functions, a narrower range of mechanisms, and additional design documentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Targeted biology and engineering structure–function resources matched to the narrowed task
- Explicit student instruction on analyzing animal structures and functions
#### Constraints
- Streamlining ED features was necessary to keep overall unit duration from becoming unwieldy

### Target Learners
- middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- discipline-driven technology design
- structure–function analysis
