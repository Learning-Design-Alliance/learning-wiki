# Use CW-FIT group contingency game format with teams, points, and rewards for appropriate behaviors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3`–`q4` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
CW-FIT is a classroom management strategy combining direct instruction of three target skills (gaining the teacher's attention, following directions, ignoring inappropriate peer behavior) with a group contingency game in which teams of 2 to 5 students earn points on a variable schedule and receive rewards for meeting point goals. The article recommends it as an "efficient, relatively easy to implement, and low-cost" procedure for improving on-task behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 2-hr training workshop, modeling of procedures for 2 to 3 sessions, and weekly feedback from building coaches
- A timer beeping every 2 to 3 min on a variable schedule and a team point chart
#### Constraints
- Less than 50% of teachers continued implementation after coach support ended
- Seven of 66 responding teachers stated the timer and giving points was distracting to their teaching

### Target Learners
- elementary school students in general education classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- class-wide on-task behavior
- appropriate classroom behavior skills
