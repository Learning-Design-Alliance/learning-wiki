# Implement LMS, Moodle teaching, instructor personal webpages and virtual labs to improve blended learning quality in Ghanaian universities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its evaluation of key indicators across the three universities, the review recommends that "the implementation of LMS, moodle teaching, creating personal webpages of instructors and virtual labs will improve the quality of blended learning in Ghanaian universities". It targets the postgraduate level first and subsequently the bachelor's level, projecting full blended learning at the three universities in less than two academic years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional investment in infrastructure and capacity to operate an LMS and virtual labs
#### Constraints
- The review projects the two-year timeline only for the three public universities it analyzed

### Target Learners
- postgraduate students, then bachelor's-level students, in Ghanaian universities

### Target Learning Goals
- quality blended learning delivery combining online and face-to-face modes
