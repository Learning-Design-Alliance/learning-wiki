# Direct Instruction: Genre-specific Writing Strategies

## Description
Direct instruction of genre-specific writing strategies involves explicitly teaching the characteristics and conventions of different writing genres to enhance students' understanding and skills in both reading and writing. Teachers introduce a specific genre (e.g., argumentative writing), build on students' existing knowledge, and explain how the six traits of writing apply to that genre. This approach often includes modeling, gestures, and turn-and-talk activities to reinforce learning. Students then apply these strategies to write their own pieces within the genre.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher knowledge of various writing genres and effective teaching methods. Access to examples of different genres and materials for writing activities.
#### Constraints
- May be less effective if students lack foundational writing skills. Requires careful planning and delivery to keep students engaged.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- K-12 students, particularly those in middle and high school, who need explicit instruction in writing genres

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance understanding of writing genres, improve writing skills, apply knowledge of genre conventions, reinforce key concepts through modeling and practice

### Affordances
- Provides a structured approach to understanding and applying genre conventions. Enhances writing skills through direct instruction, modeling, and practice. Reinforces learning through varied teaching methods such as gestures and discussions.

### Personalization
- The teacher can adjust the level of instruction and complexity of the writing tasks based on students' needs and abilities. Students can also have some choice in the topics they write about within the assigned genre.

## Assessment Evidence
- Assessment can include student writing samples, participation in discussions, and completion of genre-specific writing tasks. Success is measured by students' ability to effectively apply genre conventions in their writing.

## Impact
- Explicit genre instruction has been shown to improve students' writing skills and understanding of literary elements. This approach helps students become more confident and effective writers by providing them with a clear framework and specific strategies.
