# Treat de-escalation as moment-by-moment co-construction using mutual trade-offs and turnings rather than refusals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review’s synthesis implies that teachers can steer loaded moments toward de-escalation by attending to how the moment is co-constructed as it progresses. The mechanisms it names are “mutual trade-offs, turnings, or refusals,” with trade-offs and turnings offering paths away from escalation while refusals mark a party’s rejection of the negotiated move. Teachers and students together shape whether the moment escalates.
