## Description
The review provides "suggestions for designing training scenarios" using AR, derived from its exploration of "the limitations of current AR systems and their efficacy in training". The strategy is to build AR training scenarios on the approaches the review found effective for learning and feedback, while heeding the limitations the authors identify for current systems.
