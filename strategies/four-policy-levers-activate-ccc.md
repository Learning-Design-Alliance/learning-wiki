# Activate the CCC through four complementary policy levers: education, credentialing and quality assurance, financing and incentives, and measurement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article outlines a nursing-led policy pathway that "can activate the CCC through four complementary levers: education, credentialing and quality assurance, financing and incentives, and measurement within a learning system." Education integrates interprofessional simulation modules into prelicensure and continuing programs, substituting part of clinical hours where quality standards are met, with faculty investment in scenario design, debriefing, and psychological safety. Financing uses the national fee schedule to recognize training costs and credentialed staff, with time-limited add-on payments for early adopters. Measurement pairs process indicators (time to intervention, adherence to safety checks) with outcome measures (adverse events, throughput, patient experience) on institutional dashboards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Financing and incentives are described as "essential for adoption"
- Faculty capacity in scenario design and debriefing warrants investment
- Institutional policies should recognize CCC scope within clear supervision frameworks
#### Constraints
- Aligning incentives with safety and quality standards is stated to help ensure that scaling does not outpace capability
