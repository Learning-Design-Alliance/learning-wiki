# Select AVG systems for curriculum implementation by clinical utility, patient-friendly capability, and cost-effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
When choosing gaming systems for the DPT curriculum, the authors weighed three criteria: "clinical utility, patient-friendly capability (no training or background required), and cost-effectiveness". They selected an older system (Nintendo Wii) alongside more current systems (Xbox One Kinect and Nintendo Switch) so clinics and patients could transition AVG plans of care to affordable home exercise programs without extensive physical space. Games were chosen to vary gross and fine motor requirements, cognitive levels, and interests.
