# Electricity and Circuit Tasks

## Description
Electricity and circuit tasks involve investigating how electricity flows in simple circuits using tools like electric testers and puzzle boards. Learners identify complete circuits and determine the flow of electricity between different points. These activities enhance understanding of basic electrical concepts and build problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Electric tester, circuit boards, wires, bulbs, batteries
#### Constraints
- Requires access to specialized materials. Safety precautions are necessary when working with electricity.
