# Use process-oriented preceptor assessment of clinical reasoning when students use AI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that preceptors evaluate not only final clinical outputs but also students' reasoning processes when AI is used. It suggests "incorporating process-oriented assessment criteria—such as critical evaluation of AI outputs, integration with evidence-based practice, and transparency in AI use—can provide a more accurate assessment of clinical judgment." Students may be asked to explain how AI-informed suggestions were appraised and justify modifications based on patient needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preceptors available to evaluate students' clinical reasoning processes during AI-supported tasks
#### Constraints
- Assessment should ensure that AI use complements, rather than replaces, professional judgment

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- new graduates in clinical practice settings

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical judgment
- critical evaluation of AI outputs
- evidence-based practice integration
