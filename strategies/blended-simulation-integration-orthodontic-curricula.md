# Integrate simulation technologies with traditional teaching through blended approaches tailored to specific clinical tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that educators integrate simulation-based methods into orthodontic curricula through practical strategies rather than replacing traditional teaching. It states that "A blended learning approach that combines simulation technologies with traditional teaching methods, such as typodonts alongside lectures and clinical shadowing, can provide students with a well-rounded educational experience." Simulations should be tailored to specific tasks such as bracket placement or wire bending, with investment in cost-effective high-fidelity tools, faculty training programs, repeated practice opportunities, and assessment mechanisms such as performance metrics integrated into VR simulations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty training programs to help educators implement and adapt the tools for diverse student needs
- Investment in cost-effective yet high-fidelity simulation tools
- Ongoing assessment and feedback mechanisms, such as performance metrics integrated into VR simulations
#### Constraints
- The review notes variability in simulation fidelity, software quality, and immersive features across studies, underlining the need for standardized tools and reporting standards

### Target Learners
- orthodontic students in undergraduate and preclinical education

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills such as bracket placement and wire bending
- clinical preparedness and confidence for treating patients
