# Undergraduate leadership and iterative refinement of program design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Compass runs a flat, consensus-based decision process in which any community member may attend any meeting and every decision requires consent of everyone present. Undergraduates who went through the summer program as freshmen serve as coordinators and "use their ﬁrst-hand experience to improve it", interviewing teacher applicants, shaping the theme, recruiting, fundraising, and coordinating logistics. The article reports that this "allows for more efﬁcient and effective improvements than could be made by graduate students working alone", and that the summer program improved across five iterations from eight hours per day of class to five hours plus community-building activities.
