# Use participatory adult-learning formats (group discussion, reflective learning, role play, structured case discussions) alongside didactic talks in clinician PMH training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 review, 2 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training combined didactic talks with interactive adult-learning methods: "group discussions, reflective learning, role play, and structured case discussions were used along with didactic talks." Each didactic session was followed by a reflective and problem-solving exercise using standard case vignettes, and talks were pre-recorded by experts to ensure uniformity. The authors attribute the knowledge gains partly to these methods, noting didactic teaching and case-based discussions are effective for improving PCP knowledge of common mental disorders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators trained in perinatal psychiatry; standard case vignettes; pre-recorded expert talks for consistency
#### Constraints
- Structured feedback was not collected after all training sessions

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians / medical officers

### Target Learning Goals
- Applied knowledge of perinatal mental health identification and management
