# Design guided play environments that balance child-initiated curiosity with social scaffolding

## Description
The article describes guided play as a play-based educational setting that dynamically incorporates both individual and social learning: 'learning in guided play scenarios balances child-initiated curiosity and social scaffolding 2 often in enriched learning environments'. The teacher carefully designs environments with tools children are likely to explore and learn from, and applies scaffolding where appropriate. This implements the Janus-faced balance between exploitation of known knowledge and exploration.

## Design Implications

### Context
