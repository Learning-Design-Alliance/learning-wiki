# Use AI as a creative writing partner that maintains flow and opens a space of possibilities

## Description
The article describes using Transformer AI as a tool for creative writing: the student writes a first paragraph, the AI continues with the second, and so on. The AI writing partner helps maintain a flow of words and takes the story in unexpected directions to which the student must respond. Generating alternative continuations may help students see creative writing as an exploration of a space of possibilities rather than a linear progression.
