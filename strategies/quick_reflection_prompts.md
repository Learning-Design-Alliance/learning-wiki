# Quick Reflection Prompts

## Description
This strategy involves using short, focused prompts at the end of a class period or learning experience to encourage learners to articulate their ideas and deepen their understanding. These prompts can be jotted down and shared out loud or used for larger writing assignments. The goal is to allow quiet thinkers to articulate their ideas and for entire groups to share ideas and make meaning of content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time at the end of a class period or learning experience; specific reflection prompts.
#### Constraints
- May require clear instructions and a supportive classroom environment to ensure honest and thoughtful responses. Some learners may need more time to process their thoughts.
