# Support transfer of implicit game-based CT knowledge into explicit, usable knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because students in GBL environments tend to acquire implicit knowledge that does not transfer to far-transfer assessments, the authors recommend that "more efforts should be made to assist students in transferring the implicit knowledge into the explicit knowledge that they can use to solve problems in the real world." They also note that a longer gameplay period may be required for significant gains on transfer tasks, since three 40-minute sessions per level was relatively short.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Deliberate instructional support that helps students articulate implicit in-game CT knowledge explicitly
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from a null competency result in a short, one-group exploratory study; the authors note a longer gameplay might be required for significant gains in such a transfer task

### Target Learners
- middle school students learning computational thinking through games

### Target Learning Goals
- applying computational thinking competency to real-world, far-transfer problems
