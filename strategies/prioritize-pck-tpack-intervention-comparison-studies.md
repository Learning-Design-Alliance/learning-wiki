# Prioritize intervention and comparison studies on PCK/TPACK effects in mathematics classrooms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its mapping, the review recommends that future studies investigate PCK and TPACK as classroom interventions rather than surveys. It notes that "no study has investigated the difference between teachers with PCK skills and non-PCK skills and what the reflection of this is on their students", and calls for empirical studies — surveys, case studies, interventions — on teachers' attainment of content or pedagogical knowledge. The strategy directs researchers toward comparative designs that measure student outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires empirical designs with comparison groups or pre/post measurement rather than surveys without comparison
#### Constraints
- The review found most existing mathematics studies were surveys without comparison or qualitative work, so the evidence base for designing such interventions is thin

### Target Learners
- mathematics teachers at primary, secondary, and university levels

### Target Learning Goals
- improving student mathematics learning, attitudes, and conceptual understanding through teacher PCK and TPACK
