# Use validated competency frameworks to guide curricular training strategies and evaluation, and invest in further framework creation across other nursing areas

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that validated competency frameworks "are influential in curricular training strategies", guiding teachers in developing programs within the curricular matrix throughout the course and in the evaluation process, and serving "as indicators of curriculum performance" for monitoring the impacts of curricular changes. They further recommend that "The study suggests the investment in further research to help in the creation of competency frameworks as proposed, not only in the area of urgency, but also in other areas of nursing teaching and research."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Frameworks must be embedded in the course pedagogical plan with previous and simultaneous theoretical and practical context at each level of training progression.
#### Constraints
- The number of experts participating in the study was reduced relative to the number invited, and snowball sampling reaches the most visible people, preventing generalization of the results.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- nurses in emergency care training

### Target Learning Goals
- development and evaluation of ABC emergency-care competencies and curriculum improvement
