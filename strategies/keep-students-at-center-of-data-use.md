# Keep students' lived experiences at the center of data use and ensure data reflect students' lives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy warns that data users can become absorbed in trends and patterns tied to policies and programs and lose sight of the students they aim to serve. The brief advises users to "keep the context of the lived experiences of your students front and center" and, citing Safir and Dugan (2021) and Warner (2021), to ensure they have data that reflect the lives of their students. It supports the brief's broader equity focus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data collections should include data that reflect the lives of the students served
