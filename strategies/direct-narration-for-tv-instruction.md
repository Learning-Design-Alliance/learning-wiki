# Use direct narration by an instructor talking directly to trainees when producing television instruction.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's findings suggest that when producing television instruction, direct TV narration in which the instructor talks directly to the trainees is the most effective method of presentation. Animated sound film also seemed effective, but combining dramatic action with narration was not as effective. Producers of televised lessons should therefore favor direct address over dramatized formats.
