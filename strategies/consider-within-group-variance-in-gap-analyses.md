# Consider sources of within-group variance when comparing performance between groups of students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors recommend that researchers investigating gender or performance gaps examine demographic slices (e.g., major, course level) that contribute to within-group variance rather than only comparing aggregate groups. Their findings show the gender gap varied significantly and sometimes disappeared for specific subpopulations such as BFY physics majors. As they write, "our ﬁnd- ings underscore the importance of considering sources of within-group variance when comparing performance be- tween groups of students".
