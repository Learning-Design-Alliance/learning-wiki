# Design E-learning for adoption by involving target users throughout development, catering to diverse learning preferences, and integrating the tool department-wide

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the SUS and interview findings, the authors recommend that E-learning designers "include target users in every step of the design and development process," build features catering to a diversity of learning preferences, and involve the entire department by incorporating the tool into the educational infrastructure and daily workflow.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Target users involved in every step of the design and development process
- Department-wide buy-in with integration into the educational infrastructure and daily workflow
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that generalizability may be limited because not all E-learning tools are the same

### Target Learners
- E-learning designers in medical education
- medical and physician assistant students in clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- adoption and sustained use of E-learning tools
- documentation and sharing of clinical learning experiences
