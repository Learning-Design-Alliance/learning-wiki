# Position AI-SPs as preparation, reflection, and supplementation for real patient contact rather than replacement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article explicitly states that "AI-SP should be used under supervised conditions as a supplement and a preparation tool for real patient contact, not as a replacement." Its value lies in "providing safe, controllable practice opportunities before contact with real patients; providing structured reflective support after contact with real patients; and providing scalable supplementary solutions in resource-limited environments." This positioning addresses risks of overreliance, incorrect learning, and privacy disclosure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervised conditions for use
- Positioning as an auxiliary tool rather than a replacement
#### Constraints
- Learners may become accustomed to AI interactions that are 'simpler' than real patients, leading to insufficient adaptability

### Target Learners
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for real patient communication
- Reflective learning after clinical contact
