# Develop next-generation virtual communication-training tools that integrate natural language processing, nonverbal cue detection, and conceptual and psychological fidelity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that future virtual simulation tools move beyond current limitations by integrating natural language processing and advanced AI/ML capabilities, capturing nonverbal communication, and designing for conceptual fidelity (scenarios consistent with reality) and psychological fidelity (provoking emotional responses like reality), not only physical resemblance. It concludes that "Addressing these gaps requires future innovations that integrate natural language processing and other advanced capabilities to enhance both the realism and educational value of virtual simulations." It also recommends standardized tests to evaluate beneficial effects before fully integrating tools into training plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effective natural language processing systems and natural conversation flows integrated into the tools
- Ability to capture nonverbal communication such as gestures and emotions
#### Constraints
- The review notes realism and authenticity require more than physical fidelity, and that conceptual and psychological fidelity factors were not considered in its own review

### Target Learners
- health care students
- practicing health care professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- realistic patient-centered communication
- empathic communication
- shared decision-making
