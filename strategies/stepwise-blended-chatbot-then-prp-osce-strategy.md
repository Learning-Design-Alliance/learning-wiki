# Stepwise blended learning: train clinical reasoning with the AI chatbot first, then practice procedural and communication skills through PRP or standardized patients, with structured debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the complementary strengths observed in the pilot RCT, the authors propose a stepwise blended model in which students "initially train their clinical reasoning with the chatbot and subsequently practice procedural and communication skills through PRP or with SPs". They further recommend integrating "a structured debriefing session with peers or tutors following the chatbot training" to bridge the AI's strength in clinical reasoning and the human interaction needed for communication and professionalism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A functioning scenario-based chatbot with automated feedback; access to peer role-play or standardized patients for the subsequent stage; peers or tutors available for debriefing
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a proposal for future implementation; the pilot tested each method separately, not the blended sequence, and findings are preliminary and hypothesis-generating

### Target Learners
- medical and Korean medicine students in clinical skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- patient education
- procedural and communication skills for OSCE
