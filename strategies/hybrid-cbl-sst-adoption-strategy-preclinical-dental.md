# Adopt hybrid CBL-SST-simulator teaching for preclinical clinical-reasoning and communication goals while retaining LBL for foundational knowledge

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends the integrated, simulation-based approach as "a powerful strategy to prepare future dentists for the demands of modern clinical practice", while noting it is "not replacing LBL for foundational knowledge". The strategy targets the higher levels of Miller's Pyramid (Shows How and Does) that lectures cover less well, and the authors suggest extending it to Prosthodontics and Orthodontics and adding VR and AI-powered virtual patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulators and standardized patients, which the authors note make the model resource-intensive
- Assessment aligned to higher competence levels, e.g. process-oriented tools like OSATS
#### Constraints
- Pilot evidence only (N = 20, single cohort, single institution); same-cohort contamination risk; only Kirkpatrick Levels 1 and 2 assessed; cost-effective implementation strategies remain to be explored

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate dental students in preclinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical thinking and treatment planning
- doctor-patient communication
- theory-practice integration
