# Validate district-level opportunity measures against a common assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's title names its method: "Validating the SEDA measures of district educational opportunities via a common assessment". The strategy is to check an existing district-level composite measure by comparing it with scores from a shared, common assessment, which can confirm achievement scores while exposing inconsistencies in derived growth estimates. This is how the authors structure their validation study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a common assessment whose scores can be compared with the district-level measures being validated
#### Constraints
- Differences may emerge for derived growth estimates even when achievement scores are supported

### Target Learners
- K-12 students across United States school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring district educational opportunity accurately
