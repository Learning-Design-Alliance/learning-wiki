Train tutors with an initial week plus twice-weekly hour-long follow-ups supported by structured manuals¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's implementation strategy pairs structured manuals with sustained training: tutors "receive a week of initial training, followed by two hour-long follow-ups each week." Tutoring is provided through AIR via a training request process in which implementers indicate tutoring type, grade level, number of trainees, and location, then negotiate dates with a trainer.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tutors who are either teachers or paraprofessionals
  • Structured manuals with lesson guides and materials

Constraints¶

  • Training is provided through AIR and requires scheduling negotiation with a trainer

Target Learners¶

  • at-risk fourth graders taught by trained tutors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • fractions achievement
