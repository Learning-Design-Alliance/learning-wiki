# Strengthen the appeal of medical teaching careers by amplifying motivational factors and addressing demotivational factors such as lack of economic incentives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends strategic initiatives targeting both sides of the motivation ledger for future medical educators: amplifying factors like passion and contribution, and directly addressing demotivators — above all inadequate economic compensation — to attract and retain well-suited candidates and support their professionalization. It frames this as necessary given faculty shortages in Mexico and other low- and middle-income countries, and also calls for addressing the factors that hinder women from pursuing the same paths as men.
