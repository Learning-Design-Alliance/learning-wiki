Teach reading passages by explicitly discussing genre features alongside the standard pre-, while- and post-reading phases¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's experimental treatment kept the usual three-phase reading course (pre-reading, while-reading, post-reading) but added explicit genre work: teachers activated background knowledge, clarified the main idea, identified text sections, and discussed "collocations, genre types, lexical and grammatical features as well as the similarities and differences among the exposed texts". Both groups received the same passages, credit hours and topics over 10 sessions.
