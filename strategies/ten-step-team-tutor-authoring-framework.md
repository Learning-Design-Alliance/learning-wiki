# A generalized framework for authoring a team tutor: shared terminology plus ten authoring steps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the Surveillance Tutor, a shopping mall team tutor, and a three-person search-and-destroy tutor in progress, "a generalized framework is offered for creating team tutors." Its first element is the terminology ontology; the other elements are ten steps for authoring a team tutor, adapted and expanded from eight steps in Bonner et al. (2016a), beginning with analyzing an existing team task to define the characteristics to be adapted for tutoring or team training. The framework is intended to aid future authors in any software tool, not only GIFT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing team task that requires tutorable skills and enables assessment, with measurable outcomes determined early in development
#### Constraints
- Based on authoring in GIFT and the authors' own tutor projects; formal evaluation of the case-study tutor was still ongoing

### Target Learners
- developers and researchers authoring team tutoring systems

### Target Learning Goals
- team skills including communication, coordination, and backup behavior

### Affordances
- Team Tutor Terminology Ontology
