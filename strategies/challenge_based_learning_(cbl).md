# Challenge Based Learning (CBL)

## Description
Challenge Based Learning (CBL) is a customizable framework that provides an efficient and effective way for learners to solve real-world challenges. It fuels collaboration to identify big ideas, ask thoughtful questions, and identify, investigate, and act on challenges. CBL aims to help learners gain deep subject area knowledge and develop the skills necessary to thrive in an ever-changing world. The CBL framework includes three interrelated phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to real-world challenges, collaborative tools and resources, and a supportive learning environment that encourages inquiry and problem-solving.
#### Constraints
- Requires a significant time commitment and may necessitate a shift in traditional teaching practices to fully integrate the framework.
