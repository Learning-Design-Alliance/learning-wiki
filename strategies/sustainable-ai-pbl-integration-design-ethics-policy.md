# Pursue sustainable AI integration in PBL through attention to design, ethics, and policy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on its synthesis, the review recommends that sustainable AI integration in education be pursued through design, ethical, and policy measures. The article states the review contributes "practically by highlighting design, ethical, and policy implications for sustainable AI integration in education." This strategy directs implementers to address the ethical concerns (data privacy, algorithmic bias, educator autonomy) alongside technical design when adopting AI-enhanced PBL.
