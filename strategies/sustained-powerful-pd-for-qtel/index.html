Provide sustained, powerful professional development with professional learning communities for QTEL implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Walqui cautions that schools and districts must give teachers time to explore and experiment with the recommended practices, preferably through sustained professional development and professional learning communities. She argues such learning must weave theory and pedagogical practice together so teachers know how to deliver instruction purposefully. A key implementation lesson is that although teachers can readily add productive activities after training, more than a few hours of professional development are needed to make deep and coherent changes to long-held instructional beliefs and practices.
