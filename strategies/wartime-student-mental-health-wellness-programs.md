# Universities in conflict zones should implement mental health screenings, accessible counseling, support networks, and curricular adaptations to reduce academic pressure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that universities develop and implement activities and programs supporting student mental health during wartime, including "regular mental health screenings, offering accessible counseling services, and creating support networks". It also suggests adapting curricula to reduce academic pressure and provide flexibility for students facing war stressors. As an example, Ternopil National Medical University established a mental health center, conducts mental health support trainings, and included an elective Mental Health Gap Action Programme component in its curriculum.
