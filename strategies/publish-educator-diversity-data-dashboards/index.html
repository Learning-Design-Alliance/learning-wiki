Publish school-level educator race/ethnicity data and program-completer and retention data on state dashboards¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends that state education agencies, as keepers of state data systems, make educator workforce data publicly available and easily accessible so stakeholders can make targeted workforce decisions. Massachusetts "posts school-level data on the racial demographics of the educator workforce on the state website" and posts candidate enrollment data for preparation programs, but the brief recommends adding program-completer racial breakdowns and "data on the retention rates of educators of color through a dashboard, on individual school reports cards, or through a state-developed report."
