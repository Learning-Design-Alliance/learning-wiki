# Use a voluntary statewide climate survey to track climate and identify schools needing support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
State education agencies can administer a voluntary school climate survey to any school in the state and use the resulting scores longitudinally to monitor climate trends and flag schools needing additional support. In this article, PDE's survey served both functions, and its multi-year data enabled analysis of pandemic-era changes in climate perceptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent survey administration across multiple years by the same schools to support longitudinal comparison.
#### Constraints
- Voluntary participation yields a small, non-representative sample, limiting generalization of findings.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- school climate monitoring and improvement
