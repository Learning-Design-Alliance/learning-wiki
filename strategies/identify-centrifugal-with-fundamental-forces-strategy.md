# Ask students to identify the centrifugal force with one of the four fundamental forces to trigger critical thinking about fictitious forces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For students convinced of the reality of the centrifugal force, the instructor asks them to identify it with one of the four fundamental forces. Because students are also convinced only four fundamental forces exist, their inability to classify it 'allows for critical thinking about the nature of this force.' The instructor then reminds them that centrifugal and Coriolis forces are relevant only in a non-inertial frame.
