# Classification Tasks

## Description
Classification tasks involve sorting objects or concepts into categories based on shared properties. Learners develop and justify their classification schemes, demonstrating their understanding of the properties and relationships between objects or concepts. These tasks can be adapted for various subjects, from classifying rocks based on observable properties to classifying candies based on their attributes. Tasks may include binary classification systems or classification trees, depending on the complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Objects to be classified, classification criteria, tools for observation (e.g., magnifying glass), recording sheets
#### Constraints
- May require a variety of materials. Success depends on the clarity of the classification criteria.
