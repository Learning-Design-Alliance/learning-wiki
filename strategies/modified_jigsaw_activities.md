# Modified Jigsaw Activities

## Description
The jigsaw strategy involves dividing a task into subtopics, assigning one to each group member. Students then work to become subtopic experts, sharing new knowledge with their groups. For peer teaching, you can group students together as you normally would, but give each tutor-tutee pair the same subtopic to master. Working side-by-side, they can discuss and finish activities, presenting new knowledge to their team together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires clear division of tasks into subtopics, structured activities, and teacher guidance. Students need to be trained on how to give effective feedback and provide constructive criticism.
#### Constraints
- May require additional planning and coordination to schedule activities. Requires careful planning and monitoring to ensure both students are actively engaged and benefiting from the activity.
