# Blend VR and IP simulation within a curriculum, matching each modality to learning objectives and training stage

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
For future curricula the article proposes treating VR and IP simulation as complementary rather than competing, matching each to different learning objectives or training stages, with VR valuable for independent practice and reinforcement after initial IP training. The article writes that "a blended approach might leverage the strengths of each method for different learning objectives or at different stages of training".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to both VR and IP simulation modalities within the curriculum
#### Constraints
- the initial development and technology costs of VR should be weighed against the long-term benefits

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical management skills such as sepsis recognition and management across training stages
