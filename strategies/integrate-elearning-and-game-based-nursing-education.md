# Integrate e-learning and game-based approaches into nursing education to leverage the strengths of both methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that because e-learning "should not be seen as a replacement for traditional teaching methods but rather as a complementary option for educators", both e-learning and game-based approaches be integrated into nursing education. It also recommends that educational and medical center administrators be familiarized with innovative approaches like gamification, and that the IPSE model be adopted as a framework for retraining ICU nurses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrators and educators familiar with both e-learning platforms and gamification tools
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single three-group trial with 45 ICU nurses on organ donation candidate care in Iran

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses
- Nursing education planners and administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge and attitudes toward care of organ donation candidates
- Continuing education for healthcare teams
