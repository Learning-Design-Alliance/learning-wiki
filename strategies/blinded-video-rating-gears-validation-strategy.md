# Validate the stepwise assessment tool by having blinded experts rate endoscopic videos of resident and expert cases step by step using GEARS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors outline a concrete validation plan for the inventory-based tool: "Experts will be recruited and asked to rate the endoscopic videos of resident (PGY3-5) and expert cases using the GEARS tool to assess each step", with experts blinded to level of training. To minimize time burden, participants evaluate only two steps at a time until each step has been assessed, and video access is provided by a secure email link. Ratings draw on a prospective database of RARP cases maintained by the team.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A prospective database of RARP cases with endoscopic video
- Recruited experts blinded to trainee level
- Secure video access and staged rating (two steps at a time) to limit time burden
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a planned next stage; validation results are not yet reported

### Target Learners
- urology residents PGY3-5 performing RARP

### Target Learning Goals
- stepwise intraoperative RARP competence and tool validation evidence
