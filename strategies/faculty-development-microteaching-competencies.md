# Strengthen core microteaching competencies — structured teaching, learner engagement, reflection, feedback, and role modelling — through faculty development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review concludes that structured teaching, learner engagement, reflection, feedback, and role modelling "consistently appeared across the identified models as core microteaching skills," and recommends that "clinical educators and training institutions should strengthen these competencies through faculty development and context-sensitive application of clinical teaching models." The strategy targets the cross-cutting skills embedded in all eight mapped models, treating them as teachable competencies developed via faculty development programs integrated with routine clinical teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training institutions able to mount faculty development initiatives and support educators during routine clinical care
#### Constraints
- The review presents this as a recommendation from convergence across mapped models, not from a tested faculty-development intervention

### Target Learners
- clinical educators and faculty in health professions education

### Target Learning Goals
- structured teaching
- formative feedback
- reflective practice and role modelling
