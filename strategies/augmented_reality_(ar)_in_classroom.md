# Augmented Reality (AR) in Classroom

## Description
Augmented reality can be used to turn an ordinary class into an engaging experience. AR technology provides virtual examples and adds gaming elements to support textbook materials, making classes more interactive and helping students better remember information. Students can scan elements of a book and receive text, audio, or video tips from teachers, or they may find useful information about the course, a teacher, or other students which could lead to better communication.
