# Hold workshops developing emotional and spiritual intelligence to support academic achievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's authors recommend implementing workshops that develop students' emotional and spiritual intelligence as a route to better academic outcomes. They write that "It is possible to augment academic performance and status, as well as education quality, in students by implementing workshops to escalate different types of intelligence and their applications," and repeat the recommendation in the discussion. This is an author recommendation, not an intervention the review tested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workshops must target the emotional and spiritual intelligence constructs measured in the included studies (e.g., EQ-i 2.0, MSCEIT, SQ-5R, SSI domains)
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on correlational evidence only; the review did not test any workshop intervention
- Most included studies were conducted in Islamic countries, many in Iran, which the authors state as a limitation of generalisability

### Target Learners
- university students
- high school students
