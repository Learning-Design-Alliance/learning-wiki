# Give feedback against final-standard learning outcomes from day one, supported by a workshop to interpret early failing grades

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Rather than grading easy-to-hard across the semester, all feedback from day one is given with respect to the learning outcomes as it would be on the final day. To avoid negative self-confirmation, a workshop helps students interpret early 'bad' grades, emphasising that qualitative feedback matters more than a grade indication, since the rubric has only one failing grade: <5.5.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A workshop to help students interpret early low grade indications
#### Constraints
- The authors report this may heighten sense of urgency and, with proper guidance, enhance intrinsic motivation — an experiential, not tested, claim

### Target Learners
- second-year BSc students

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting formative feedback
- maintaining motivation under high standards
