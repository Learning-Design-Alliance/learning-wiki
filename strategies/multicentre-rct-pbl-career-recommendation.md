# Conduct multi-centre, multi-country randomised controlled trials with adequate confounding adjustment to clarify PBL-career associations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 3 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that the ideal way to clarify the conflicted results on PBL and career choice would be through a multi-centre, multi-country randomised-control trial with a large study population. It further calls for new longitudinal studies with good power that adequately adjust for confounding variables, noting that evidence has only been conducted in a small selection of the countries that use PBL in their medical education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Large study population with good statistical power
- Adequate adjustment for confounding variables
- Multi-centre and multi-country design
#### Constraints
- Current evidence is limited to a small selection of countries using PBL, with multiple studies from the same medical schools (three at University of New Mexico, two at McMaster, two at Harvard)

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- career choice formation in medical education
