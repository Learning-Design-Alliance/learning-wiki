# Integrate frequent in situ self-regulated simulation training with in-facility champions into routine health systems as continuous quality improvement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends sustaining clinical skills through frequent self-regulated in-facility simulation rather than one-off refresher courses. Its conclusion states that "frequent self-regulated training, motivated by well-trained and motivated in-facility champions using improved technological tools, is possible in low-resource settings, and can be easily integrated into the existing health system as part of continuous quality improvement". Learning corners in the workplace environment plus team debriefing are presented as supporting knowledge sharing, retention, teamwork, and confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-trained and motivated in-facility champions to coordinate and motivate peers
- Innovative training tools available in the workplace
- Quarterly mentorship and supportive supervision visits
#### Constraints
- The article notes barriers to and facilitators of sustained effects and scalability should be explored before wider scaling

### Target Learners
- healthcare providers in labour wards in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of neonatal resuscitation skills over time
