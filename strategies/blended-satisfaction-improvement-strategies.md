# Improve blended learning satisfaction by fostering community, higher-order-thinking activities, and usable LMS functions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes practical implications for three audiences based on its findings. For students, "efforts should be made to create an open and pleasant learning community, that fulfills students' emotional needs and provides a sense of belonging". For teachers, it recommends designing meaningful activities — online discussion, group work, critical reflection, and peer assessment — so students develop higher-order thinking. For platform developers, "LMSs should provide students with enough useful and easy-to-use learning functions to improve acceptance among students".
