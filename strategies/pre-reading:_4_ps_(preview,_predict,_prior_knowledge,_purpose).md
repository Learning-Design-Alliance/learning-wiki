# Pre-reading: 4 Ps (Preview, Predict, Prior Knowledge, Purpose)

## Description
The 4 Ps of pre-reading include previewing the text, predicting the content, activating prior knowledge, and defining the purpose for reading. Previewing involves taking a quick look at the text. Predicting means using clues to figure out what information the reading will provide. Activating prior knowledge involves recalling what one already knows about the subject. Defining the purpose involves understanding the author's intent, which helps in comprehending the text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the text and a few minutes to engage in the 4 Ps of pre-reading.
#### Constraints
- May require guidance for learners who are new to the 4 Ps approach. Effectiveness depends on active engagement with the text.
