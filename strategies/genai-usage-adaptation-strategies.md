# Students adapt GenAI usage through input-related, output-related, personal and user-awareness strategies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies four categories of user strategies for integrating GenAI into various situations: input-related, output-related, personal and user awareness strategies. Input-related strategies include 'prompt engineering, repeated questioning, and initiating a new request when facing difficulties'; output-related strategies center on verifying outcomes, e.g. via web search, and giving feedback. Personal strategies include delegating tasks to GenAI to minimize mental load, and user awareness centers on handling GenAI with caution, particularly with sensitive data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users must understand the nature of GenAI, including its capabilities and limitations, to make informed decisions regarding its application
#### Constraints
- Strategies were identified from a small sample of eleven part-time students in Germany

### Target Learners
- part-time university students using GenAI in study and work

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and responsible use of GenAI tools for academic and professional tasks
