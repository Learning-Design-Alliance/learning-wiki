# Develop learning environments in schools, higher education, and nonformal settings that address naive conceptions about viscosity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "more learning environments within school science curricula, higher education, and nonformal science education settings (e.g., science centers, scientific TV shows) could be developed and implemented that address na ¨ ıve conceptions about a fluid’s viscosity" and enable individuals to develop a deeper, scientifically backed understanding of viscous behavior. The recommendation rests on the survey finding that naive conceptions are prevalent among adults while the topic is hardly represented in curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning environments should explicitly address naive conceptions such as confusions between viscosity, density, and stickiness
#### Constraints
- The topic is currently hardly represented within school science curricula or higher education curricula, which the authors identify as the reason such environments are needed

### Target Learners
- school students
- higher education students
- adults in nonformal science education settings

### Target Learning Goals
- developing a scientifically backed understanding of the viscous behavior of fluids
- overcoming confusions between viscosity, density, and stickiness

## Claims

- [Adults around the globe hold naive conceptions about the viscous behavior of fluids, consistent with those found in prior qualitative research with children and students](../claims/adults-globally-hold-naive-conceptions-viscous-behavior.md) [+W]
- [Participants self-evaluated their knowledge of natural science in general significantly higher than their knowledge of the viscous behavior of fluids](../claims/self-evaluated-science-knowledge-exceeds-viscosity-knowledge.md) [+W]
- [Adults also hold naive conceptions about temperature and pressure effects on viscosity and about the viscosity of water](../claims/temperature-pressure-water-viscosity-conceptions.md) [+W]
- [The most prevalent adult naive conceptions entangle viscosity with density and stickiness, with 67.0% assuming more viscous fluids are denser](../claims/viscosity-density-stickiness-entanglement-adults.md) [+W]
- [Naive-conception scores correlate weakly negatively with self-evaluated knowledge about natural science and about viscosity](../claims/weak-negative-correlation-self-evaluated-knowledge-viscosity.md) [+W]
