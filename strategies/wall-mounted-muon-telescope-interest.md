# Mount a continuously counting cosmic-ray detector on the classroom wall to raise interest in physics among students and visitors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A small, permanently running detector hung in a school physics lab makes invisible cosmic rays visible as a live count display. The article reports that such a display "works well at increasing the horizon s of mind not only of young people but of all who see it", and that several school groups have since built their own telescopes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A compact telescope of three GM counters in coincidence with a display of the number of counts, built by student groups
#### Constraints
- GM counters themselves cannot be made by students and must be supplied by an institute

### Target Learners
- high school students
- school visitors and staff

### Target Learning Goals
- interest in physics and science in general
