# Implement SDM EPAs through longitudinal workplace training integrated with EBM curricula and supervisor role modeling

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing the SDM EPAs in existing competency-based workplace curricula, arguing that "Longitudinal workplace training is necessary to bridge the transfer gap between learning and sustainable application in clinical practice". The EPAs can be used as a tool for observing the SDM process in clinical encounters and providing feedback, may guide competence assessment, can be incorporated into supervisors' continuing education since adequate role modeling is essential, and should be integrated into postgraduate EBM curricula because residents learn complex skills optimally when integrated with medical expertise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual learning processes should be the starting point to prevent using the EPAs as a box-checking exercise
#### Constraints
- Most prior SDM training interventions were single sessions whose sustainable application diminished over time, motivating the longitudinal approach

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents
- clinical supervisors

### Target Learning Goals
- routine application of shared decision making in clinical practice
