# Train school staff in emotional intelligence skills and embed them in the school curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that school personnel be trained in emotional intelligence skills to address students' academic problems, and that these skills be built into schooling structures. Specifically, they recommend that 'School Principals, counsellors, Psychologist and Subject teachers should be exposed to Emotional intelligence skills' and that 'Emotional intelligence skills should be inculcated into the school curriculum', with seminars, workshops and symposium organised to train teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training covers emotional self-awareness, self-management, social awareness and relationship management
- Organisational support via seminars, workshops and symposium for teachers
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a single small quasi-experimental study of 40 students

### Target Learners
- junior secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- positive locus of control
- academic competence and self-efficacy
