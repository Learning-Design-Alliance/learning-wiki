# Favor interactive, thorough AI literacy programs over brief informational texts to shape appropriate reliance on LLMs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the null over-reliance effect and the significant under-reliance increase, the authors recommend that schools considering scalable brief texts or brochures instead provide more interactive and extended interventions. They state that "more interactive and thorough interventions might be needed to durably and meaningfully change the way students interact with these tools" and caution that brief texts may invite excessive skepticism toward AI outputs rather than appropriate reliance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs would need to be more extensive than short explanatory texts and to develop selective, rather than generalized, dismissal of AI advice.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is preliminary: the findings come from a small sample (36 participants) and would need replication in larger studies before guiding practice.

### Target Learners
- High school students using LLM-based tools in education

### Target Learning Goals
- Appropriate reliance on LLM advice: reducing over-reliance without inducing under-reliance
