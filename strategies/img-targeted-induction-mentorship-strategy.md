# Provide targeted induction, interview preparation and structured mentorship for international medical graduates applying to surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends targeted support to mitigate barriers international medical graduates face in UK surgical selection, including limited mentorship, unfamiliarity with NHS systems and lack of preparation for UK-specific assessments. It states that "Targeted interventions, such as NHS-specific induction programmes, focused interview preparation courses and structured targeted mentorship" might mitigate these challenges and support equitable access to training posts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programmes must address familiarity with NHS practices, clinical communication expectations and interview formats, and preparation for assessments such as the MRCS examination and portfolio-based interviews
#### Constraints
- The article frames these as interventions that might mitigate challenges; their effectiveness was not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- International medical graduates applying to UK higher surgical training

### Target Learning Goals
- Navigating the ST3 selection process and enhancing equitable access to higher surgical training
