# Use survey findings on PHC professionals' HPV knowledge gaps and concerns to update curricula and guide postgraduate training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends an educational response to its findings: "The study results can be utilized to update curricula for medical and nurse students, and to guide postgraduate training of PHC personnel by the NIPH and the Cancer Registry of Norway." The strategy is targeted updating of professional knowledge of HPV infection, vaccination and cervical cancer, with particular attention to GPs, whose knowledge and use of NIPH sources were lowest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Updating should be carried out or guided by the NIPH and the Cancer Registry of Norway, which the article names as bodies that can "guide postgraduate training of PHC personnel".
#### Constraints
- Selection bias cannot be ruled out because "only one third of the eligible professionals participate" in the survey underlying this strategy.

### Target Learners
- public health nurses
- general practitioners
- medical and nurse students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of HPV infection, HPV vaccination and cervical cancer
