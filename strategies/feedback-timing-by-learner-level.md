# Adjust feedback timing by learner level: give feedback during the resuscitation to novices and after the resuscitation to senior learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that "feedback given after a resuscitation to a senior learner allowed for more autonomy. In contrast, feedback given during a resuscitation was perceived as better for a novice learner." This implements the graduated-responsibility theme: an attending described minimally intervening and giving feedback at the end of the case for residents running things smoothly, while interjecting comments during the case for residents who had not run traumas before.

## Design Implications
