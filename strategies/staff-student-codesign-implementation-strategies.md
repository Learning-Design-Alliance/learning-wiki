# Involve school staff and students in planning, and use workshops, parental meetings, researcher support and materials to optimise adherence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol prescribes a set of implementation strategies to optimise adherence: workshops with school staff to plan delivery, parental meetings, continuous research support, and materials including "Physical activity inspirational material" and digital audiobook subscriptions. Workshops focus on feasible implementation strategies and on enhancing students' and teachers' involvement, and an initial intervention delivery plan is established at the end of each workshop. The approach responds to the article's argument that involving school staff and students in planning facilitates transfer of evidence into practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The school headteacher is asked to provide facilities and extra time/hours for school staff to perform the intervention
#### Constraints
- Teachers tend to identify time constraints as a major barrier to implementing physical activity programmes, per the article's background

### Target Learners
- adolescents in grade 8 (age 14–15)

### Target Learning Goals
- adherence to and sustainable delivery of a school-based physical activity and homework support programme
