# Provide lecturers with professional development and concrete pedagogical-didactic guidance for physically activating teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because lecturers' uncertainty about implementation is a major barrier, the article recommends strengthening lecturers' knowledge, sensitivity, and acceptance and enabling competent realisation through further training in higher education didactics. The article states: "professional development for lecturers appears as a key component for successful implementation." It notes this complex ability "cannot be built up by reading a book alone but also requires participation in further training in higher education didactics". Lecturers also explicitly expressed a desire for concrete methodological and pedagogical-didactic guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address implementation uncertainty, lack of methodological and didactic knowledge, social norms around sitting, and lack of time
#### Constraints
- The article notes the ability to teach with physical activity is a complex skill lecturers still need to acquire

### Target Learners
- university lecturers

### Target Learning Goals
- competence to integrate physical activity into teaching in a goal-congruent way
