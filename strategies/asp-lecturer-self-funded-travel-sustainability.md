# Lecturers cover their own travel so raised funds maximize African student attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A sustainability strategy the article attributes to ASP: "the dedication of the international lecturers, most of whom cover their travels from external sources" allows funds raised by the IOC "to be used to maximize the attendance of African students." Most lecturers stay about one of the three weeks on average, and full bursaries are provided to selected students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- International lecturers able to fund their own travel from external sources
#### Constraints
- Most lecturers stay for only one of the three weeks on average

### Target Learners
- African physics students selected across African countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to high-quality fundamental physics training
