# Apply a contextual perspective when enabling educator learner-support roles in online education

## Description
The article recommends approaching learner support in online education contextually rather than generically, responding to Nacu et al.'s (2018) guidelines "to enable educators to fulfill learner support roles in online education from a contextual perspective". In practice, educators and designers would adapt support roles to their learners' circumstances and environment, a need the article ties to pandemic-era online instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators' support roles should be analyzed against the specific context in which online learning occurs, per the guidelines the article responds to.
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is argued, not empirically tested in this article; the available text reports no evaluation.
