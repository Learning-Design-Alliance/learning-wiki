# Develop targeted, systematic delineation training programs addressing the errors identified through structured assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends building targeted and systematic training programs that specifically address the common delineation errors its competition identified, emphasizing dynamic approaches for physiological tumor motion, multimodal imaging use in anatomically complex regions, and disease-specific spread patterns for lymphatic tumors. It also calls for "ongoing education and the standardization of training protocols for clinicians, especially for those in the early stages of their careers."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Error data from structured expert assessment to target program content
- Standardized contouring guidelines and delineation atlases for dissemination
#### Constraints
- The study did not directly rectify the identified errors; programs are a recommended next step, not a tested intervention in this article

### Target Learners
- young radiation oncology clinicians in the early stages of their careers

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate and consistent target volume delineation
- appropriate handling of tumor motion and multimodal imaging integration
