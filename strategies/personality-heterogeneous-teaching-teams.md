# Compose teaching teams heterogeneous in personality so different traits serve different teaching skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teaching teams deliberately mix attending physicians with different personality profiles, because different traits facilitate different teaching skills. It states: "Teaching teams could ensure quality of teaching by creating heterogeneous teams in terms of personality of attending physicians". It also suggests individual attending physicians reflect on the role of their personality and specialty in their teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires knowledge of attending physicians' personality traits and the teaching skills each trait supports, as measured by instruments such as the SETQ and a Big Five inventory.
#### Constraints
- The supporting associations are observational and specialty-dependent; the article notes what works in one specialty does not necessarily work in another.

### Target Learners
- residents in residency training

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical supervision and teaching quality in residency training
