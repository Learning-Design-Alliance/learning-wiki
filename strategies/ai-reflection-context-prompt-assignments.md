# Context-prompt reflection assignments in which students elaborate on a concept, apply it to a novel problem, and receive AI feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes reflection assignments as an extension of the same context-prompt framework: students elaborate on their understanding of a specific physics concept, apply it to a novel problem, and receive AI feedback on the completeness, coherence, and correctness of the elaboration. The authors present this as partially automating the resource-intensive process of reflection, while noting that in the present paper reflection is best understood as the next pedagogical extension rather than an equally implemented classroom component.
