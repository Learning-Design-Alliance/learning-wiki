# Advise community college students to enroll in developmental mathematics in their first semester before continuing astronomy coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend formal academic advisement so that students enroll in developmental mathematics early and then continue with astronomy coursework, since research shows early enrollment in developmental mathematics improves academic performance and persistence. Advisement is framed as especially beneficial for students choosing astronomy to fulfill science requirements who "may not be aware of the fundamental mathematical applications." Without adequate advisement from institutional agents, students may misjudge their need for remedial instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to formal academic advisement or institutional agents who can guide students' remedial mathematics and science course sequencing
#### Constraints
- The study's data were localized in New York State and may not be generalizable to all community college students

### Target Learners
- community college students, particularly those needing mathematics remediation
