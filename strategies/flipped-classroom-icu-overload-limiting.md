# Flipped classroom: pre-class self-paced preparation followed by interactive problem-solving sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends the flipped classroom as an overload-limiting format: "Core concepts and essential foundational knowledge are studied by the learner at their own pace before class, using videos, gamification, virtual reality, quizzes, and learning platforms such as Moodle", followed by in-class interactive problem-solving. Spreading learning through advance preparation optimizes classroom time with the teacher and limits overload risks during active sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must prepare in advance using provided pre-class materials such as videos, quizzes, and online course platforms

### Target Learners
- ICU trainees including physicians in postgraduate training

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational critical care knowledge, freeing class time for problem-solving
