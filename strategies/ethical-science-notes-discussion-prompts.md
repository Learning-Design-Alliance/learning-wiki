# Use the article's numbered notes on principles of ethical science to initiate discussion about the responsibilities of being a scientist

## Description
The article supplies a numbered list of principles, procedures, and practices of ethical science — including analyzing data objectively before hypothesizing, labeling speculation clearly, considering issues from all angles, and treating science as a logical rather than democratic process. It states these "should become part of a young scientist's early training" and that science teachers may find them useful for initiating discussion and debate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select and frame the numbered notes as discussion prompts rather than facts to memorize
#### Constraints
