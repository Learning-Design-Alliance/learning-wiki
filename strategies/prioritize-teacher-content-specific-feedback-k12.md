# Prioritize teacher-provided, content-specific feedback in K-12 settings, and build students' capacity to give peer feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the moderator results, the article recommends that feedback be delivered by teachers rather than peers and be content-specific, since "Content -specific feedback seems to provide the most positive impact on academic achievement." It also recommends future work on "how to better equip students to provide feedback to their peers" and more quantitative studies of feedback in college and university settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher with mastery and knowledge of subject matter is likely best equipped to provide content-specific feedback that improves understanding.
#### Constraints
- The current structure of the school system and time constraints in a typical school day may limit how much time a teacher can devote to individual teacher-to-student feedback.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- pre-service teachers
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- effective use of formative feedback

## Claims

- [Content-specific feedback shows the strongest effect (g = .91); praise is moderate (g = .42); objective feedback is weak and non-significant (g = .13)](../claims/content-specific-feedback-strongest-effect-type.md) [+M]
- [Feedback effects are significant at the K-12 level (g = .55) but non-significant in higher education (g = -.01)](../claims/feedback-k12-significant-higher-education-null.md) [+M]
- [Feedback produces a moderate, statistically significant positive overall effect on academic achievement (Hedges' g = .40)](../claims/feedback-moderate-positive-overall-effect-achievement.md) [+M]
- [Teacher-to-student feedback shows a significant positive effect (g = .41) while student-to-student feedback is non-significant (g = .32)](../claims/teacher-feedback-significant-peer-feedback-null.md) [+M]
