# BOOKMATCH: Scaffolding Independent Book Selection

## Description
BOOKMATCH is a tool for helping students select just-right books for independent reading. It introduces various criteria that influence book selection, such as book length, ordinary language, organization, prior knowledge, manageable text, appeal to genre, topic appropriateness, connection, and high interest. Students use these criteria to select books, discuss and evaluate their choices, and become more aware of their personal preferences as readers. This approach helps students become independent and responsible for their book selections during independent reading time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a classroom library, the BOOKMATCH poster, student comment forms, and an online discussion forum. Requires dedicated class time for book selection and discussion.
#### Constraints
- May require significant time investment for initial setup and training. Effectiveness depends on consistent application of the BOOKMATCH criteria.
