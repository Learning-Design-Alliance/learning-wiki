# Gradual Increase of Independent Reading Time

## Description
Start with short independent reading sessions and gradually increase the amount of time as students build reading stamina. Begin with sessions as short as five minutes for young children and gradually increase the time as students become more focused and engaged.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated time for independent reading, a timer to monitor reading sessions, and a plan for gradually increasing reading time.
#### Constraints
- Requires consistent implementation and monitoring. Some students may still struggle to focus even with gradual increases in time.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Build reading stamina, increase focus and engagement, and establish a consistent independent reading routine.

### Affordances
- Prevents distraction and disengagement, builds reading stamina gradually, and establishes a positive association with independent reading.

### Personalization
- Adjust the pace of increasing reading time based on individual student needs and progress. Provide support and encouragement for students who struggle to focus.
