# Extend PBL research and implementation to other student levels, larger samples, and longer assessment periods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is to broaden PBL implementation and evaluation beyond the present setting: "Future research might need to focus on the implementation of PBL at different levels of students, such as primary school, secondary school and pa rt-time students." They also call for a larger sample and assessments spanning the whole course rather than one semester, and for more comprehensive conceptualization and measurement of problem solving beyond accuracy and quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessments should be done not only for one semester but throughout the whole course
#### Constraints
- Present findings cannot be generalized to other settings, given the small samples at a premier Malaysian Technical University for only one semester

### Target Learners
- primary school students
- secondary school students
- part-time students
- undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- problem solving skills
