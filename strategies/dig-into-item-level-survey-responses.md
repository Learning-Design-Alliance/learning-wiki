# Dig into item-level survey responses: examine how students and teachers answer specific questions as a starting point for learning and responding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief's tip instructs leaders to "dig into your data! Take a look at how students and teachers are responding to specific survey questions," asking what people are saying about the culture and climate of the school. It frames scores as "a starting point," with learning more and responding as what matters to improve teachers' and students' experiences and outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to item-level survey response data for students and teachers
#### Constraints
- The brief treats scores as a starting point rather than sufficient; responding to what the data reveals is presented as the necessary follow-through

### Target Learners
- K-12 students and teachers whose experiences the survey captures

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding and improving school culture and climate
