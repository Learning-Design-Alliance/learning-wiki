# Math Art

## Description
Math Art involves creating drawings and designs that explore mathematical concepts and patterns. This strategy can involve using stencils to create geometric shapes, identifying patterns and relationships within the artwork, and exploring numerical relationships through visual representation. By visually representing mathematical concepts, learners can enhance their understanding and make math more engaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Drawing supplies (paper, stencils, colored pencils, etc.). Mathematical concepts to be explored.
#### Constraints
- May require teacher guidance to connect the art to specific mathematical concepts. Can be time-consuming.

### Target Learners

- Elementary students, specifically first graders.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Explore mathematical concepts, enhance pattern recognition, and make math more engaging through visual representation.

### Affordances
- Visual representation of mathematical concepts makes abstract ideas more concrete. Engaging and fun way to explore math. Can be used across a range of math topics including geometry, number relationships, and patterns.

### Personalization
- Students can use their own interests and preferences to create unique math art. Activities can be adjusted based on the students' abilities and skill levels.
