# Connect force, motion, and iterative processes during instruction to facilitate computational thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
From the essay findings, the authors recommend that the concepts of force, motion, and iterative processes be connected during instruction to facilitate computational thinking. The article states these concepts "should be  connected during instruction", because observing how students make the connections requires questioning them while they engage in computational modeling. Successful students employed local rules, where only the net-force rule needs changing for new problems.
