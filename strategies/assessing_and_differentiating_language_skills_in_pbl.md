# Assessing and Differentiating Language Skills in PBL

## Description
Assessing and differentiating language skills in project-based learning (PBL) involves evaluating not only content knowledge but also language proficiency. As educators scaffold and teach vocabulary, structure, and function of the language, they should assess these skills to determine further support needed. Through assessment, teachers can decide how to differentiate instruction, whether through products or processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing assessment of language skills, flexible instructional strategies, and a commitment to meeting diverse student needs.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing assessment and adjustment, and may necessitate additional resources or support to effectively differentiate instruction.
