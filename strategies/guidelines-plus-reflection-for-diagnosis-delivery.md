# Provide clinicians with best-practice guidelines for delivering a dementia diagnosis and opportunities to reflect on their practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practice recommendation is that "Clinicians should be provided with guidelines on best practice in delivering a dementia diagnosis and be given more opportunities to reflect on their practice in doing so." The workshop implementing this combined didactic guideline content with reflective discussion and critique of videoed feedback sessions, and was followed by improved self-efficacy and self-reported practice change.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to published best-practice guidelines such as SPIKES-D
- Structured opportunities for reflection and discussion among clinicians
#### Constraints
- The article notes the intervention did not include the experiential activities that prior reviews identified as important in communication-skills training

### Target Learners
- clinicians in multidisciplinary memory clinics

### Target Learning Goals
- improving communication self-efficacy and practice in delivering dementia diagnoses
