# Regulate conflict through group characteristics, communication processes, and task complexity that maintain exchange

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report derives conflict control principles from three levers: group characteristics, communication processes, and task demands. It states that 'Self interest and selfesteem are generatedor degenerate in relation to variousgroup characteristics (open-closed,presence of a third rarty,problem- solving norms, and power differentialamong memters), communication processes (formal-informal, verbal-non-verbal,and time lap of response), and the complexity of the taskrequirements.' Each lever is argued to maintain the mutual perception of advantages of continuing exchange between opposing parties.

- members of groups, dyads, and disputing parties in educational and organizational settings

### Target Learning Goals
- conflict regulation and avoidance of aggression
- maintaining self esteem and social interest during dispute resolution
