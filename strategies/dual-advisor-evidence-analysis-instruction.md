# Dual-advisor model pairing content and methodology advisors in evidence analysis instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the article's 15-week online graduate course, "students are assigned both a content advisor who is knowledgeable in their field of practice and a methodology advisor versed in evidence analysis and statistical methods". Content advisors help students identify contingencies in treatment, exposure, or diagnostic measurement that may affect results, and provide rubric-based feedback on the extraction framework; methodological instructors advise on field structure, data forms, and feasible analyses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advisors must be available for detailed feedback; the article reports instructor involvement is intensive at the extraction stage and relatively light during synthesis as students become self-reliant
#### Constraints
- The article notes very few students create an adequate data extraction form on their first attempt, so iterative feedback is needed

### Target Learners
- graduate health sciences students

### Target Learning Goals
- creating adequate data extraction frameworks and conducting sound meta-analytic synthesis
