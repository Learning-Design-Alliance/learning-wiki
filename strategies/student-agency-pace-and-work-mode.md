# Promote student agency by letting students decide the pace of their work and whether to complete it independently or collaboratively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends supporting students "to decide about the pace of their work and how they best complete it, whether independently or collaboratively," defining agency as people's capacity to take action, craft and carry out plans, and make informed decisions. Students reported that flexibility to complete assignments on their own schedule helped them manage home and school life, and some preferred working independently in quieter spaces.
