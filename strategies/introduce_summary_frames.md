# Introduce Summary Frames

## Description
Learners read a section of text to themselves before verbally summarizing the passage to a partner or completing sentence frames. Examples of sentence frames: "_____ wanted _____, but ______, so ______.” for problem/solution; "_____ happens because ______.” for cause/effect.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prepare appropriate texts for summarizing.
#### Constraints
- Learners may rely too heavily on the sentence frames.
