# Verbal Repetition

## Description
Verbal repetition is a strategy where learners repeat information verbally to reinforce understanding and retention. This can include turn-and-talk activities, where learners explain concepts to a partner, or checking for understanding of skills through verbal explanations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for learners to verbalize their understanding, structured prompts or questions
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for learners who are uncomfortable speaking in front of others
