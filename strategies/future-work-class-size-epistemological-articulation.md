# Investigate class size and articulate epistemological features when studying attitudinal shifts

## Description
The article's forward-looking research agenda identifies two key issues for understanding positive attitudinal shifts. First, since all reported Modeling Instruction sections were capped at 30 students, future research should probe "whether the positive attitudinal shifts continue in classes two to three times the size of those reported here". Second, the authors call for continued articulation of "the epistemological features of classroom and instructional environments", noting that documentation of curricular features addressing epistemological issues is crucial to understanding their impact on student attitudes.
