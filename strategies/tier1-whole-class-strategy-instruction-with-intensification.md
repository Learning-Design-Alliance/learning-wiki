# Whole-class Tier 1 strategy instruction with intensified small-group follow-up for nonresponders

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing the intervention as whole-class, Tier 1 instruction integrated into the general curriculum and pacing guide, with more intensive small-group intervention for students who do not respond. They write that the intervention "may best serve the needs of all students (but particularly those with LD) if it also offers more intensive small group intervention that focuses on the modified elements most critical to improved performance," mirroring how RTI intensifies instruction for Tier 1 nonresponders. They also recommend positioning the final assessment no later than 1 month before the school year closes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research-validated teaching strategies delivered by general education teachers with prior professional development
#### Constraints
- Many students with LD did not reach mastery (70%), so the authors state more intensive intervention may be necessary; the study cannot identify the intervention as a research-validated package for immediate implementation

### Target Learners
- All students in inclusive fifth and sixth grade math classrooms, particularly students with LD who do not respond to the general intervention

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving math problem-solving performance toward grade-level proficiency
