## Description
As part of the STEM for GIRLS in Ethiopia initiative, the authors organised in 2019 "the very first workshop for teachers to discuss why it is important to bring more girls into STEM". The workshop targets teachers of grade 9 and 10 girls, positioning teachers as intermediaries who can support girls' participation in STEM.
