# Invest in social structures and cross-disciplinary standards to strengthen the DLP research community

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations are to "Invest in a social structure to bridge, deepen, and sustain community connections" and to "Converge on cross-disciplinary standards and resources to support onboarding and reuse." It also identifies gating factors: limited awareness of existing platforms with research infrastructure and the lack of permanent scientific structure such as journals, societies, and conferences. The authors argue that even small bridging events can have meaningful impact on network connectivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community-building mechanisms such as workshops or networks that connect otherwise separate groups of researchers
#### Constraints
- The article notes the subfield currently lacks a dedicated journal, conference, or scholarly society serving the broader community

### Target Learners

- platform developers
- research funders and network organizers

### Target Learning Goals
