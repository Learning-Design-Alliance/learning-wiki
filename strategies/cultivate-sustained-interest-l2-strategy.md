# Design engaging L2 instruction and positive classroom climate to cultivate learners' sustained interest in the target language

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that consistency of interest predicted L2 achievement, the article recommends that "L2 instructors could design engaging instructional activities, foster a positive classroom climate and establish strong student rapport, thereby enhancing learners' sustained interest in and passion for the target language". The strategy targets the interest dimension of grit rather than effort, aiming to boost L2 motivation and engagement and ultimately improve outcomes. The article also advises cultivating a growth language mindset and self-efficacy, and incorporating learner well-being as a key educational objective in higher education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional activities must be engaging and supported by positive classroom climate and strong student rapport
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single correlational/SEM study of first-year English majors at one Chinese university; the CI-achievement link was not experimentally manipulated

### Target Learners
- University L2 learners
- first-year English majors

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained interest and passion for the target language
- L2 motivation and engagement
- L2 achievement
