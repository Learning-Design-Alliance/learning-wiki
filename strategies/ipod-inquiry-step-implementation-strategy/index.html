Implement the four iPod inquiry steps with stated teacher principles¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article gives implementation principles for each step. For invitation, textbook design should let students feel the interaction between science and life experiences, and teachers should avoid too much guidance so students' thinking is not limited. For prediction, teachers should tell students the prediction is not a test and identify misconceptions. For operation, students perform actual operations and record findings directly. For discussion, teachers should encourage every student to express and share ideas so peers with stronger abilities can lead others.
