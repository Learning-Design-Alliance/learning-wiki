Integrate quantitative and qualitative approaches in future cardiac arrest simulation feedback research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends that "future research needs to integrate quantitative and qualitative approaches" to gain the necessary insights into both technical and non-technical skill acquisition recommended in resuscitation guidelines. This strategy responds to the review's finding that non-technical aspects of cardiac arrest management are underreported and that qualitative methodologies are underused. It directs researchers to combine quantitative performance measures with qualitative exploration of participant experiences.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research designs must combine quantitative outcome measures with qualitative methods to capture both technical and non-technical skill acquisition

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • resuscitation education researchers and simulation educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • technical and non-technical cardiac arrest management skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Future HFS trials for practising nurses need larger samples, rigorous randomised design and standardised, fully reported measures
  • Future research should use standardized validated outcome measures, longitudinal follow-up designs, and methods linking learner gains to patient-relevant outcomes
  • Prioritize rigorous RCTs with retention tests and transfer measures before expanding distance simulation programs
  • Use both qualitative and quantitative research designs, including longitudinal follow-up, to clarify which aspects of residency training best prepare doctors
