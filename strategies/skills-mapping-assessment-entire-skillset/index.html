Use skills mapping and assessment to articulate individuals' entire skillsets from formal and informal experiences¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As a skills-first alternative to traditional talent development, the report describes career navigation that "utilizes skills mapping and assessment, where a large emphasis is placed on identifying, understanding, and articulating an individual's entire skillset (including technical, essential, and transferable skills) that may be gained from a variety of experiences (formal and informal)." It contrasts this with traditional practices that rely primarily on past job titles and formal degrees, and pairs it with skills-based job matching that allows flexible pathways across industries or roles.

Context¶

  • Digital tools such as digital wallets and ePortfolios to create adaptable, sharable, and verifiable skill profiles; staff and client access to relevant technologies

Target Learners¶

  • workers and jobseekers with skills acquired outside traditional academic routes (e.g., on-the-job experience, military service, gig work, caregiving)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • identifying, communicating, and demonstrating skills; skills-based career planning and job matching
