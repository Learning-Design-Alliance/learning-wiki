# Ad-hoc pathway with repeated in-classroom expert lessons for deeper engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For 2018/19 ASI tested an ad-hoc pathway in which the ASI expert visited the school four times: theoretical lessons on infrared spectroscopy and MATISSE, guided group analysis of VIR-Dawn data with only "light" support, PowerPoint preparation, and a final visit to ASI HQ where students presented their work. The authors report this "more detailed and longer experience involved the students more deeply, leading them to a better understanding of how a research program is conducted", and that it was "greatly appreciated by both teachers and students".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Long-term availability of an expert to deliver multiple in-school lessons and accompany the final presentation
#### Constraints
- The paper states this pathway was not evaluated quantitatively; the 2019/20 expansion was suspended due to the COVID-19 emergency

### Target Learners
- upper secondary (high-school) students

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the full cycle of a research program, including paper structure, peer review, observation selection, and oral presentation
