# Investigate the effectiveness of each individual working-memory strategy separately in future research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the Suggestions for Further Research section the authors recommend that subsequent studies analyze how effective each individual strategy is by investigating students' response to each one, since the present study implemented the strategies as a set. They also propose examining how strategies demanding auditory processing promote retention, and determining correlations between poor WM indicators and overall language achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies should isolate student responses to each strategy rather than bundling them
#### Constraints
- The present study could not attribute the effect to any single strategy

### Target Learners
- undergraduate beginner EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- vocabulary retention and retrieval in English
