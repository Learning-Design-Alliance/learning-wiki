# Adapting an existing medical-student simulation tool for PA students through minimal, faculty-consulted changes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
To extend eCREST to physician assistant students, the authors adapted the tool in consultation with PA faculty. As they report: "We adapted eCREST for PA students, in consultation with PA faculty, which involved ensuring the case description referred to PAs not just general practitioners (GPs)." Adaptations were minimal: reframing case introductions for PAs and selecting existing cases that best fit the PA curriculum. This strategy rests on the assumption, supported by the study's comparison results, that PA students approach clinical reasoning similarly to medical students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consultation with PA faculty to ensure case framing fits the professional group and curriculum
#### Constraints
- The article notes no clinical reasoning resources had previously been identified as targeted toward PAs, so transferability had to be established empirically

- diagnostic decision-making
