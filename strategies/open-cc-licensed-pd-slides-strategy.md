# Share professional development slides openly via short links and a Creative Commons license

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The TAN project makes all professional development materials openly accessible: slides for every session are posted at a shared short link, and the presentation itself is licensed for reuse. The deck states the information "is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License and may be adopted, remixed, or used as inspiration for your own innovation efforts". This supports rural teachers with limited access to in-person PD in reusing and adapting the materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Slides must be collected at a stable shared URL and an attribution guideline provided
#### Constraints
- Reuse requires attribution under the CC BY 4.0 terms

### Target Learners
- K-8 teachers, especially in rural settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Teaching computational thinking concepts in K-8 classrooms
