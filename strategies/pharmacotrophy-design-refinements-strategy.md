# Refine competition-based tournaments by adding group-stage matches, spaced post-event quizzes, student question-writing and collaborative formats

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose organisational refinements to competition-based learning tournaments grounded in their experience and cited research. They suggest that “a revised could involve increasing group stage matches and reducing knockout stage”, that “involving students in question development could promote experiential learning”, that collaboration “should be encouraged through collaborative questions”, and that offering Kahoot! questions after the event could support spaced education. They also propose presenting questions in an amphitheatre format with teams answering collectively to preserve anonymity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher time to prepare questions and animate matches; a question bank covering the target curriculum.
#### Constraints
- Face-to-face matches lack anonymity, which the authors note requires caution to avoid a stressful atmosphere that could decrease fun, motivation and engagement.

### Target Learners
- pharmacy students in exam revision periods

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained motivation to revise
- knowledge acquisition across the curriculum
