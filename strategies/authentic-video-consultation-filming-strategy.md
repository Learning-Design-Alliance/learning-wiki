# Authentic video consultations filmed to show both doctor and patient simultaneously

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The VP case embeds video films of a patient encounter produced in an authentic clinical setting to visualize good communication and clinical skills. The article states: "Video films were produced in an authentic clinical setting with a family doctor and a standardized patient." The camera viewed both the family doctor and the patient simultaneously in order to visualize the interaction in all parts of the patient encounter, and the content was created in congruence with the Calgary-Cambridge Guides and the PRACTICAL training model.
