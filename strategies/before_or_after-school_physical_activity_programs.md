# Before or After-School Physical Activity Programs

## Description
Offer structured physical activity programs before or after school, such as morning exercises, intramural sports, or opening school facilities for recreational use. For younger students, create intramural or informal sports programs. For high school students, facilitate competitive sports or informal sports programs. Provide diverse options like yoga, jump rope classes, or access to the gym, swimming pool, or playground. Aim to encourage physical activity just for fun without competitive pressure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision, facilities (gym, pool, playground), equipment, scheduled time
#### Constraints
- Requires resources and staffing. May not reach all students due to transportation or scheduling constraints.
