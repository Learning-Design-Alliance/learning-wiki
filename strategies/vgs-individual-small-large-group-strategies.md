# Offer VGS as individual, small group, or large group activities depending on learning goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes three teaching strategies for VGS in curriculum: assigning it as an individual activity completed at the learner's convenience, working through it in a small student group in person or by web-conferencing to develop team decision-making, and playing it as a large group activity with audience polling or group discussion. The authors write "One common strategy is to assign the VGS as an individual activity which students complete on their own at their convenience within a specified time frame".
