## Description
As homework, the article pairs control questions that vary the solved problems (earlier/later start, angled motion) with a superficially unrelated challenge problem solvable by the same two ideas. The article states: "In order to reinforce students' understanding of the solution explained above, you can offer them control questions on the following page." The challenge problem asks for a puck's final speed on a wedge with µ = tan θ.
