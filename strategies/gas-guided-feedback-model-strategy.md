## Description
A structured post-simulation feedback recipe recommended by the Best Practice Standards and applied in this study. The article states that "The Gather-Analyze-Summarize (GAS) model ... is applied for guided feedback, which consists of three stages: information gathering, problem analysis, and summarization". Instructors first build trust and open dialogue, then lead group discussions in which students reflect on their own and peers' performance, analyze decisions, and summarize lessons and areas for improvement. ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors who evaluate learning-objective achievement by observing performance and providing guided feedback; feedback sessions focused on safety principles and trust before discussion
#### Constraints
- The article reports gains in satisfaction and guided reflection/feedback perceptions but no significant difference in self-confidence, and notes feedback must be provided in a standardized and effective way to have the most educational benefit

### Target Learners
- midwifery students in simulation laboratory courses
- nursing students in simulation-based training

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on clinical performance
- clinical reasoning and internalization of knowledge and skills
