# Storage Areas

## Description
Organization is a key component of successful learning environments. A de-cluttered learning environment is free from distractions and allows the focus to be on learning. Students can move quickly through the space and easily access resources needed for various learning purposes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of storage options such as shelves, bins, and cubbies. A designated space for group work and discussion. A welcoming and supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- May require additional resources. Some learners may be distracted by the changing storage arrangements.

### Affordances
- Promotes organization and reduces stress. Creates a more welcoming and inviting learning environment. Encourages a love of learning.

### Personalization
- Allow learners to choose their preferred storage options. Provide a variety of storage options. Allow learners to bring personal items from home to make the space more comfortable.

- Observe learners' engagement in learning activities. Collect feedback from learners on the comfort and appeal of the learning environment. Track changes in learners' attitudes toward learning.

## Impact
- Research suggests that organization can increase engagement, reduce stress, and improve academic performance. Studies have shown that learners are more likely to engage in activities when they feel comfortable and supported.
