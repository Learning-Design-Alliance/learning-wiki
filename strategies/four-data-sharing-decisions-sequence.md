# Work through four data-sharing decisions: what data to share, how to organize it, what documentation to include, and where to share it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide organizes data sharing as a sequence of four decisions education researchers should work through: which study data to share, how to organize the data, what documentation to include, and where to share the final dataset. As the guide puts it, it covers "deciding which study data to share, how to organize the data, what documentation to include, and where to share their final dataset." Each decision area is paired with a checklist in the guide.
