# Train delivery staff and monitor fidelity to the explicit session schedule during one-to-one reading interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The evaluation found the intervention was delivered by teaching assistants after two days of training, but that adherence needed oversight: "There is a tendency for some staff to stray away from the explicit schedule and this is likely to reduce the programme's impact". The report concludes that regular monitoring will increase fidelity but may also increase cost, and that full training and support is required for all relevant staff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two days of initial training for all staff delivering the intervention, plus ongoing monitoring and support
#### Constraints
- Regular monitoring increases fidelity but may also increase cost

### Target Learners
- Year 7 weak readers at the primary-secondary transition

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension and fluency
