Agile pedagogical strategies: interactive notebooks, bootcamps, and sustained educator professional learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because multi-year degree redesign is slow, the paper recommends agile strategies to bridge skills gaps: interactive notebooks that let students reproduce and modify end-to-end ML workflows without weeks of software setup, and intensive workshops and bootcamps such as short courses at MRS meetings. For educators, it recommends sustained professional learning communities such as AI Book Clubs, noting teacher development 'requires iterative, practice-based training rather than one-off workshops.'
