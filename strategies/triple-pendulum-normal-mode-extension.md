# Triple-pendulum normal-mode prediction and observation extension

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
An extension in which students predict the normal modes of three coupled pendula, then excite and observe them to test their predictions. "We currently ask students to predict the normal modes of the set of three coupled pendula as shown in Fig. 5. They are then asked to excite these modes and to observe them to see if they match their predictions." The article reports "Our students are all able to find the normal modes and can show that the relative amplitudes of the bobs match those of their predictions."
