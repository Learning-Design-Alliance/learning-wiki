# Use a collaborative, work-in-progress writing template shared between clinical and simulation teams

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To manage the complexity of designing realistic relational skills in preprogrammed dialogue, the working committee created "a collaborative and work-in-progress writing template as a shared working tool". The final template includes the nurse-patient dialogue script, quizzes, feedback, green and red labels, guidelines for branching, and stage directions for voice-over actors. It allowed the script to be written in the manner of a choose-your-own-adventure book and evolved over time as a guide for both clinical and VP teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The clinical inventors must be skilled in MI themselves so the quality of the simulation depends on their expertise and teamwork.
#### Constraints
- The authors state they had to build their own method because of insufficient guidance on creating and translating preprogrammed interpersonal interactions.

### Target Learners
- VP simulation developers
- educators responsible for continuing professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- codevelopment of narrative simulation content rooted in relational skills
