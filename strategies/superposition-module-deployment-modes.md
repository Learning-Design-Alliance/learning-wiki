# Deploy the module in full, partial, or flipped modes with a minute-by-minute 50-minute timing plan

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article specifies a 50-minute implementation: 5-minute prediction-first warmup, 10 minutes instructor-led on Activities 1–2, 12 minutes paired work on Activity 3 with debrief, 10 minutes Activity 4, 8 minutes Activity 5, and a 5-minute exit prompt. Two compressed modes are supported: in the partial mode Activities 1–3 are in class and Activity 5 plus one concept check are homework; in the flipped mode "Activities 1 and 2 are completed before class using the pre-reading and simulator, so that class time focuses on Activity 3 and the statistics discussion in Activity 5."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Optional challenge items may be assigned as homework; the ordered-operations extension is always outside class
#### Constraints
- Compressed modes preserve the coherence/mixture distinction as the in-class centerpiece

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate quantum mechanics students in time-constrained settings

### Target Learning Goals
- quantum superposition and measurement reasoning
