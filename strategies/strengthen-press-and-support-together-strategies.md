# Schools should pursue strategies that strengthen social support and academic press together rather than choosing one reform approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that schools strengthen both conditions simultaneously, challenging "either-or" proposals for school reform. Observed support strategies include "creating smaller, more personalized learning environments for students, such as schools-within-schools", tutoring, parent education, and community mentoring. Press strategies include setting and communicating high expectations, professional development toward challenging work, incentive systems, and assessment systems used to improve instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Efforts to raise standards and accountability must be accompanied by strong social support for students
#### Constraints
- Supportive environments alone may not lead to meaningful academic gains without academic press

### Target Learners
- sixth and eighth grade students in urban elementary and middle schools

### Target Learning Goals
- reading and mathematics achievement
