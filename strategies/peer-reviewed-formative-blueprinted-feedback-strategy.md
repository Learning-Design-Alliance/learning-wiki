# Use blinded peer review of blueprinted feedback from formative e-assessment to encourage reflection before summative assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the conclusions the authors propose that reflection may be encouraged by providing a formative e-assessment where the blueprinted feedback would undergo blinded peer-review with the purpose of getting peer recommendations on how to improve performance. They state this would provide the opportunity for collaborative interaction and motivate students to spend time analysing the reports prior to a summative end of term e-assessment which could then test the same objectives but using different questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formative e-assessment producing blueprinted feedback, and a mechanism for blinded peer review
#### Constraints
- Proposed as a possibility by the authors; not tested in this study

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection on strengths and weaknesses and mastery of learning objectives
