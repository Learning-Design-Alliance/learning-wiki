# Encourage other university-supervised groups to establish similar robotics activities and extend programming logic to all projects in future iterations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that other university-supervised groups replicate the exhibition model and that future iterations add programming throughout. They write: "Our hope is that the great results presented here encourage other groups, supervised by university teachers, to establish similar activities in order for science to be disseminated among the young students." They also expect implementation of programming logic for all projects and more sophisticated chassis and structures in future activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities should be established by groups supervised by university teachers, mirroring the PIBID supervision structure
#### Constraints
- not all prototypes had micro-controller, most were architected so that we had a dynamic and autonomous physical structure driven by electrical and electronic circuits

### Target Learners
- high school students in Brazilian public schools
- undergraduate teacher-education (PIBID) fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- dissemination of science among young students
- programming logic across robotics projects
