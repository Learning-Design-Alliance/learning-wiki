# Sequencing familiar everyday tasks to teach order of instructions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The webinar suggests having students sequence routine tasks such as getting dressed, making a pizza, school procedures like hallway and lining-up routines, drawing a picture, and brushing your teeth. These activities make visible how the order of a sequence affects the outcome, building the algorithmic insight that instructions must occur in a specific order.
