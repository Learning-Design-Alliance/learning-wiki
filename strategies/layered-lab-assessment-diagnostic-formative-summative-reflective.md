# Layered assessment strategy combining diagnostic pre-tests, formative observation, immediate summative assessment, and delayed reflective assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes assessing whether learning objectives were achieved through a layered sequence: diagnostic assessment including pre-tests before and during the lab, formative assessment using passive and active observational techniques such as assistant questioning, a summative assessment "immediately following the conclusion of the laboratory" with qualitative and quantitative components, and a secondary reflective assessment six months later. Pre-tests, solutions, example questions, and the full assessment are included in the supplementary documentation, making the strategy reproducible for other instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supplementary pre-tests with solutions and example questions
- A laboratory assistant present to conduct formative questioning during the lab
- A follow-up contact point with students roughly six months after the lab for the reflective component

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate laboratory course students
- Instructors of upper-division inorganic chemistry or materials science laboratories

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating achievement of laboratory learning objectives
- Assessing retention and transfer of skills over time
