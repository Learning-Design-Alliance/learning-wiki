# Creating Questions

## Description
Creating Questions is a strategy where learners generate questions about what they are listening to. The instructor stops at intervals during a speech, documentary, or story, and learners write down one or two questions about what they just heard. This encourages learners to actively listen for points of confusion or curiosity. Modeling is important, as learners are typically trained to seek answers rather than identify gaps in understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured intervals during instruction, modeling of effective question formulation
#### Constraints
- May require a shift in mindset for learners accustomed to seeking answers rather than asking questions, some learners may struggle with question formulation
