# Reconsider pedagogical warnings that dismiss visual arguments in undergraduate mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests that lecturers' warnings that pictures are not rigorous may install a sociomathematical norm that leaves students dismissing visual arguments regardless of source, a norm that differs from practicing mathematicians' more flexible stance. It recommends exploring "the range of sociomathematical norms researchers and undergraduates act in accordance with in relation to visual arguments" and whether the differences are desirable, noting students respond to the "heavy warning" while missing visual arguments' potential for providing insight and suggesting results.
