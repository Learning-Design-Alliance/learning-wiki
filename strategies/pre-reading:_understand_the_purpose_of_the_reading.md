# Pre-reading: Understand the Purpose of the Reading

## Description
Understanding the purpose of pre-reading is to increase the capacity to comprehend material before starting to read. By building a conceptual framework, learners can better integrate new details and evidence they encounter in the text. This involves thinking about what one already knows about a given topic, predicting what will be read, and increasing interest in the text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the text and a few minutes to engage in pre-reading activities.
#### Constraints
- May require guidance for learners who are new to pre-reading strategies. Effectiveness depends on active engagement with the text.
