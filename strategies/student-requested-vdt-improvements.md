# Extend VDT learning with longer sessions, more oral/maxillofacial cases, remote access and SECTRA interface improvements, per student feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From the open-ended survey items, the article reports that "A few students requested improvements such as longer session times, inclusion of more oral/maxillofacial cases, the ability to access the software remotely, and interface improvements for the SECTRA table". The discussion likewise identifies the needs of increasing the hours on the table, online remote access, improving the SECTRA interface, and implementing more clinical cases dedicated to the maxillofacial and oral regions, which the authors suggest might have positive learning outcomes for dental education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing VDT sessions and case libraries to extend, plus software infrastructure enabling remote online access
#### Constraints
- The improvements are based on a small number of student requests in a 24-resident cohort, not on tested outcomes

### Target Learners
- postgraduate dental residents in oral surgery and orthodontics

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical oral anatomy
- maxillofacial and oral region case analysis
