# Refine tactile properties of 3D-printed simulators by adjusting photopolymer resin formulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends improving tactile feedback by "improving the simulation of tissue adhesion and detachment forces, refining the thickness and resistance of the simulated gum, and incorporating a visual demarcation between the attached gingiva and mucosa." These modifications are feasible because Polyjet technology allows fine-tuning of material properties during printing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Polyjet multimaterial printing with adjustable photopolymer resin presets
#### Constraints
- Material innovation is still needed to better mimic the elasticity, adhesion, and resistance of human tissues

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- postgraduate dental students
- expert oral surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- haptic realism in surgical simulation
- tissue handling skills
