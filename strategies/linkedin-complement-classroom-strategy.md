# Use a LinkedIn group community to complement classroom learning rather than replace it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study recommends that educators use a LinkedIn group community to complement classroom learning activities and avoid mere reliance on these platforms to support students, develop their skills, and build communities. Since the LinkedIn group did not influence teaching and learning perception, community, or skills development, it should supplement rather than substitute face-to-face teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must set expectations that the LinkedIn group complements rather than replaces classroom learning
#### Constraints
- The LinkedIn group did not significantly affect teaching and learning perception, community, or skills development

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- Student engagement
- Student satisfaction
