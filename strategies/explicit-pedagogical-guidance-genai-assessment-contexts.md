# Provide explicit pedagogical guidance on effective and ethical GenAI use within specific assessment contexts, beyond prohibitions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Instructors should provide "explicit, pedagogical guidance beyond prohibitions, helping students explore effective and ethical GenAI use within specific assessment contexts." The strategy directs teaching effort towards shifting students away from outsourcing and towards reallocation, tailoring guidance to the demands and permissions of each assessment rather than relying on blanket bans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor time to design context-specific guidance for each assessment
#### Constraints
- Applies to unsupervised, after-class assessment contexts in higher education as studied

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- effective and ethical GenAI use in assessments; shift from cognitive outsourcing to reallocation
