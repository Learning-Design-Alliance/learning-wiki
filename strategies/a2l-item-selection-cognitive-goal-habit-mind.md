# Choose and sequence items by first targeting a cognitive goal, then varying the targeted habit of mind

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy guides teachers selecting and sequencing items. The report recommends that "teachers first decide which cognitive goal to target within any particular topic or subtopic" and then, to maintain interest and motivation, "vary the targeted habit of mind". Particular habits suit particular goals, so the pairing of goal and habit shapes which items are chosen for a lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to items varied in style, difficulty, and purpose, most of which yield a range of responses.
#### Constraints
- Particular habits of mind are more suitable for certain cognitive goals than others, and a particular habit can advance more than one cognitive goal.

### Target Learners
- high school physics teachers
- college physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- conceptual development
- problem solving
- student motivation
