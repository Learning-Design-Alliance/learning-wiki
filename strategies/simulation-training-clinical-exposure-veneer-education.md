# Strengthen simulation-based training, clinical case exposure, and complication management in undergraduate prosthodontic education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends expanding preclinical simulation exercises, diversifying esthetic restorative case exposure, and strengthening instruction in technically demanding procedures and complication management, because theoretical knowledge alone "may not be sufficient to generate perceived clinical readiness". It cites evidence that supportive supervision, structured feedback, and simulation-based education facilitate the transition from theoretical learning to clinical application.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires supervised clinical learning environments with structured feedback and opportunities for patient management
#### Constraints
- Recommended based on perceived preparedness data from a single institution; actual clinical competence was not objectively assessed

### Target Learners
- Fifth-year undergraduate dental interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical preparedness and self-confidence in porcelain laminate veneer treatment
- Technically demanding prosthodontic procedures and complication management
