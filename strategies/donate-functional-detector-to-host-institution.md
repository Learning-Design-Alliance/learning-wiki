# Donate the functional detector built during the course to the host institution for future training and research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
At the end of each course the working detector built by the students stays behind as a gift to the host. The article states that "after each course, a functional detector remained at the visited institution as a donation to be used by local professors to facilitate the training of future students and to support and enable local research activities." This converts a one-off workshop into a durable local capacity for hands-on instrumentation training and research.
