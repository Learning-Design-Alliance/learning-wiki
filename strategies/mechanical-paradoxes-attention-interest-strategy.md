# Use mechanical paradoxes and scientific games to capture student attention and increase interest in studying physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using paradoxes — instruments with surprising properties that seem not to follow the laws of physics — together with scientific games during lessons. Careful observation of the experiments shows the phenomena are fully consistent with the laws of physics, overcoming the initial sense of wonder, and students beginning mechanics are attracted by paradoxes and helped by the teacher to explain them with Newton's laws.
