# RPP researchers can support practitioner partners through concrete non-research supports such as fundraising support, edtech procurement, and building a lesson database

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy names specific supportive activities researchers took on in the NSF-funded Tough As Nails project beyond their research role. The slides list Quinn's example of "listening, coaching & affirming", Emi's example of "fundraising support", and other supports including "edtech procurement support", "building a lesson database", "supporting a TV show pilot", and sharing social capital. These are implementable activities a research team can adopt to support practitioner partners directly.
