# Map e-learning resources to learning outcomes and clinical presentations to identify content gaps and guide development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The consortium matched existing contents with learning outcomes for basic medical education and, since 2014, mapped clinical cases to clinical presentations. Through this mapping the authors report they "were able to recognize that there were some clinical presentation topics in which clinical cases were not available", enabling targeted content development that built a repository covering most basic learning outcomes in clinical presentations and clinical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A defined set of learning outcomes for basic medical education
- Faculty contributors with expertise across subject areas to fill identified gaps
#### Constraints
- Applied within the consortium's portal-based e-learning format for basic medical education in Korean medical schools

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students at participating Korean medical schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Coverage of basic medical education learning outcomes
- Clinical presentation-based case browsing and clinical reasoning
