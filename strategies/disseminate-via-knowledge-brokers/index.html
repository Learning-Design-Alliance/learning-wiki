Strategies

Disseminate research to practitioners through knowledge brokers and accessible formats such as short videos and social media threads¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because classroom teachers rarely seek out research, the report recommends reaching education's knowledge brokers—individuals in school systems who facilitate the exchange of research-informed knowledge, serving formally as instructional coaches or informally as teacher-leaders. The CREATEd Team at the University of Delaware is building a Knowledge Broker Network whose members will engage in professional learning, share practices, and collaborate on connecting research and practice. Bite-sized formats like social media threads and short video content are suggested to bridge the gap between journal articles and classroom practice.

Requirements¶

  • The idea of knowledge brokering is less prevalent and less researched in education, per Rycroft-Smith (2022)

Target Learners¶

  • Instructional coaches, teacher-leaders, and other knowledge brokers supporting classroom teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supporting practitioners' use of evidence in policy and classroom practice
