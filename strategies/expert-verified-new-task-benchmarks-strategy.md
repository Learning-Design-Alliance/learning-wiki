# Build new frontier-model benchmarks from new hard tasks with expert verification and adequate curation resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends replacing near-saturated, defect-ridden closed-ended benchmarks with a new style of evaluation. It states the field "calls for a new style of benchmark for frontier models, built from new hard physics tasks and adequately verified", noting such a benchmark "will likely require substantial financial resources to achieve high-quality curation". It suggests nonprofit organizations with rigorous processes or open competitions in the style recently adopted in mathematics as possible data-collection mechanisms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Substantial financial resources for high-quality curation
- Expert verification of tasks and reference solutions
#### Constraints
- The article notes this does not follow that frontier models are capable of end-to-end physics research; agents made considerably less progress on open physics problems than on mathematics

### Target Learners
- benchmark developers
- AI evaluation organizations

### Target Learning Goals
- objective measurement of frontier model physics capability
