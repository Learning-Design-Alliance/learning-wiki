# Pitch Forms to cautious adopters as a time-savings and instant-feedback tool, and let them experience it firsthand

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Technology coaches in the study describe concrete tactics for encouraging Forms adoption among “cautious adopter” teachers. Three coaches initially “sell” Forms from a time-savings angle because data is captured automatically, and one coach emphasizes student demand for instant feedback. A Scottish coach’s maxim is that “one of the best ways to get teachers to want to use Forms is to give them a Form” during training, after which roughly half of staff ask how it was created.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers have access to Forms, ideally district-supported; training time is limited, so experiencing a Form must substitute for formal sessions
#### Constraints
- Coaches reported cautious adopters balk when technology does not work seamlessly the first time, discouraging future use

### Target Learners
- teachers and school staff adopting classroom technology

### Target Learning Goals
- technology adoption for formative assessment and instruction
