# Use fit-for-purpose workplace-based assessments reflecting holistic outcomes instead of constant high-stakes theory assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "a programme of fit-for-purpose, workplace-based assessments that reflect holistic outcomes" should make clear that spending time in the clinical setting, familiarising with the healthcare system and learning from patients and the interprofessional team equips students to succeed. This replaces assessment pressure that currently rewards individual academic knowledge over clinical engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Workplace-based assessment programme aligned with holistic outcomes
#### Constraints
- Current high-stakes assessments encourage seeking study time and tutorials over clinical experience

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical engagement and system familiarisation
- learning from patients and interprofessional teams
