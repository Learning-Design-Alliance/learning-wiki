# Interest Inventories

## Description
Interest inventories are surveys used to assess students' areas of interest. These can be open-ended, where students generate their own topics, or closed, where students select from a predetermined list. The inventories are used to form learning clubs based on shared interests. This motivates students by allowing them to explore topics they are passionate about and connects learning to their background knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interest inventory forms (open or closed), class time for students to complete the inventories, and a system for organizing students into groups based on their interests.
#### Constraints
- May require careful structuring to ensure alignment with learning objectives. Some students may struggle to identify their interests or may choose topics that are not conducive to learning.
