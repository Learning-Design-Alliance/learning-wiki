Implement the own-it, learn-it, share-it guidelines through the shift to digital learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The commentary proposes examining how each component of Lee and Hannafin's framework can be realized when learning moves online, stating that it "examines how each of these frameworks might be implemented with the “shift to digital” learning." It treats digital learning environments as a setting in which ownership, learning, and sharing activities must be redesigned rather than directly transferred from face-to-face practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Digital learning environments in which ownership, learning, and sharing activities can be redesigned for online delivery

Constraints¶

  • The commentary addresses implementation considerations rather than reporting tested digital implementations

Target Learners¶

  • students in student-centered digital learning environments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • student engagement in student-centered learning

Claims¶

  • Lee and Hannafin's framework holds that learners in student-centered classrooms need autonomy, scaffolding, and authentic audiences to enhance engagement [+W]
  • Owning, learning, and sharing knowledge need to be reinterpreted in light of cultural differences when the own-it, learn-it, share-it framework is adopted internationally [~W]
  • Commentators recommend contextual adaptations of the Own It, Learn It, Share It framework [~W]
