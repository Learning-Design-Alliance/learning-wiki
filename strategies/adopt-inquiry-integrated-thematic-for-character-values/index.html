Strategies

Adopt inquiry-based integrated thematic instruction and a supporting national curriculum to promote primary students' character values¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on its finding that the inquiry-based integrated thematic group outperformed the thematic group on character education, the article recommends: "It is suggested for primary school teachers to use an inquiry-based integrated thematic model to improve the learning outcomes of the students' character values or affective aspects, and the learning outcomes of the knowledge and skills in general." It further recommends that the Ministry of Education formulate a comprehensive inquiry-based thematic learning curriculum with additional tools, with each subject devised based on inquiry suited to the surrounding context.
