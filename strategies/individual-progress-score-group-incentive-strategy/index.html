Use STAD-style individual progress scores with translated group scores as grade incentives in cooperative learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 review), q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article notes that in terms of CL techniques to improve performance in accounting classes, 'the most popular technique is grade incentive,' which 'involves computing student exam grades based on a combination of individual exam performance and the average exam performance of group members.' In this study, individual progress score was derived by deducting the base score (an average of previous quiz scores) from the current quiz score, then translated into group scores; groups or individuals with the highest progress score in each unit received one extra point added to their final semester score in accounting.
