# Start each face-to-face class with a verification quiz or Q&A to link online preparation to classroom activity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends beginning F2F sessions with a quick verification quiz or a 5–10 minute Q&A session to check learners' understanding of online content. These initial activities serve as "entry tickets for the classroom activities" that support the link between online and F2F sessions and promote motivation to come to class prepared.
