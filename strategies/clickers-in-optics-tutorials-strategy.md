# Use clickers in tutorial sessions to activate students before or after exercises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the parallel tutorial sessions, which otherwise follow a traditional exercise-and-correction format, clickers are used to make students active. Each professor defines his or her own clicker questions, asked at the beginning of an exercise to check students' intuition about the expected result, or at the end to check how many students solved the problem correctly.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professors must author their own clicker questions tied to the tutorial exercises
