# Weekly Thinking Science lesson routine for pre-service teachers: work the lesson as students, then metacognitive discussion, activity design, and written reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the Spring 2011 section, each weekend students worked through one Thinking Science lesson as students, followed by substantially metacognitive discussion on the following Monday. Students also identified the main learning goal of the lesson, created additional aligned activities for early-finishing pupils, and wrote reflections on the nature of the learning in each lesson. The long-term goal was for the class to facilitate Thinking Science lessons with 5th grade students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the Thinking Science lesson materials and class time for weekly lessons plus Monday discussion
#### Constraints
- The planned facilitation with 5th grade students did not materialize because of logistics

### Target Learners
- pre-service elementary education majors

### Target Learning Goals
- scientific reasoning ability
- ability to facilitate reasoning-focused lessons with elementary pupils

### Affordances
- Thinking Science Intervention Lessons
