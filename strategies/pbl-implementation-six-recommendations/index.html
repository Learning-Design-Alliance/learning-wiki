Six literature-based recommendations for implementing project based learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report's literature review distilled six recommendations for facilitating PBL: student support with time and self-management, regular teacher support through networking and professional development with crucial senior-management backing, high-quality group work ensuring equal agency, balancing didactic instruction with independent inquiry, assessment emphasising reflection and self and peer evaluation, and student choice and autonomy. As the review states, "pupils need to be effectively guided and supported through the PBL process". The Learning through REAL Projects programme incorporates all six to some degree.

Target Learning Goals¶

  • independent and group learning skills
  • pupil ownership and control over learning
  • effective group work

Claims¶

  • PBL was generally delivered with fidelity but required substantial management support and organisational change [~M]
  • The trial found a statistically significant negative literacy impact for FSM-eligible pupils, treated with caution [-W]
  • The process evaluation found PBL developed pupils' oracy, communication, teamwork and research skills [+W]
  • A year of Learning through REAL Projects had no clear impact on Year 7 pupils' literacy attainment [-W]
