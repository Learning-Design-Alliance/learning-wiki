# Assign interactive video pre-class viewing to reduce in-person laboratory hours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that nursing educators use interactive educational videos as a pre-class preparation strategy, allowing students to watch skill demonstrations at home before attending laboratory sessions. The authors state that 'Watching interactive videos before coming to class allows students to better prepare for the class, thus reducing time for lectures and providing more time for practice and feedback.' This strategy was implemented by having the experimental group watch the video at home and attend six-hour rather than eight-hour laboratory sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have access to the video via a learning management system before the laboratory session
- Faculty must produce and verify the video content in advance
#### Constraints
- Evaluated at a single institution in Oman on a single nursing skill; authors recommend randomised controlled trials and multi-institution studies before wider generalisation

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in fundamental skills laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Fundamental nursing skill acquisition with reduced face-to-face instructional time
