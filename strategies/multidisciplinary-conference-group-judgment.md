# Use structured multi-disciplinary conferences whose returns are synthesized to replace dogmatism with unbiased group judgment on contested educational topics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The reviewed sex-education conference method pooled contributions from educators, teachers, biologists, psychologists, psycho-analysts, sociologists, physicians, and physical directors. The reviewer presents this method as indicative of trustworthiness, letting "dogmatism, prejudice, and unsupported personal opinion give way to the unbiased group judgment of a large number of conferees." It suits contested topics requiring impersonal evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Conferees whose special interests and abilities fit them to contribute from widely separated viewpoints to a subject of general concern.
#### Constraints
- The method addresses subjects of general concern where personal bias is prevalent; the reviewer urges readers to assume a similarly impersonal attitude in evaluating the report.

### Target Learners
- general educators and specialists contributing to curriculum decisions

### Target Learning Goals
- developing balanced, evidence-based objectives for controversial curriculum topics
