# In-ward staff-led education (self-learning, journal clubs, educational rounds, library, telegram channel) to build clinical competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The nurses chose self-learning, journal club and in-ward short educational rounds as education methods, with subjects suggested by the nurses and sessions presented inside the ward by all team members including staff nurses. Programmes were registered to the continuous medical education programme so nurses could benefit from the hours, a small library with current nursing references was prepared, and a telegram channel shared educational programmes and films. The impact statement says educational programmes presented by the nursing staff are useful in increasing their competencies and satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurse involvement in selecting educational subjects, a head-nurse-planned timetable, and registration of sessions in the continuous medical education programme.
#### Constraints
- The ward did not have a library, so one had to be prepared; the article notes the research environment was a single surgical department with limited nursing staff.

### Target Learners
- staff nurses in hospital departments

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competency
- continuing professional education
- self-confidence
