# An interaction design procedure for designing, developing and implementing collaborative learning environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes an interaction design procedure for designing, developing, and implementing electronic collaborative learning environments. It responds to the observation that designers design such environments according to their own constructivist interpretations of what collaborative learning is and what it should achieve, and that students use them sometimes enthusiastically but often in a perfunctory way. The procedure is one of the three contributions the article presents and tests through its projects.
