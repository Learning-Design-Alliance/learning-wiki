# Future work: design UI for practical subjects and set e-learning lesson duration from the e-learning environment rather than conventional class lengths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's future-works section proposes two directions: considering how UI should be designed for practical learning such as music, painting, and carpentry, where learners need knowledge of using tools; and analyzing the e-learning environment, including the order of examples and exercises, because "duration of e-learning classes must be based on e-learning environment" rather than traditional class lengths. It also calls for examining the relation between UI and e-learning dropouts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The order of examples and exercises through the course should be specifically determined
#### Constraints
- Conventional class durations are based on traditional educational environments and may not transfer to e-learning

### Target Learners
- learners of practical subjects such as music, painting, carpentry
- e-learning course designers

### Target Learning Goals
- tool-use skills in practical domains
- appropriate pacing of e-learning lessons
