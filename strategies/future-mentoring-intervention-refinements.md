# Refinements for future mentoring interventions: extend duration, embed in curricula, train mentors, activate mentees, assess quality, and use richer reflective assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article's recommendations section proposes six refinements for future mentoring interventions in medical education. These include extending duration and continuity, since "Reflective habits require time to develop", embedding mentoring in structured curricula such as portfolio reviews, more intensive mentor training per AMEE guidance, mentee activation strategies, qualitative assessment of mentoring quality, and combining self-report questionnaires with richer data sources such as learning diaries and narrative reflections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal mentoring relationships spanning multiple clinical rotations to allow reflective habits to develop
#### Constraints
- The 8-week intervention in this study may not have provided sufficient continuity or depth to yield measurable gains beyond the clinical experience itself

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students in clinical rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- self-reflection and insight
- self-regulated learning
- clinical competence
