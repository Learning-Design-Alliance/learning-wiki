# Schools lacking ethnic diversity with small Latino representation should implement programs recognizing and empowering Latino culture and identity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a near-term alternative to structural change, the authors recommend that "schools that lack ethnic diversity and have a small Latino representation should be implementing programs geared toward recognizing and empowering Latino culture and identity." Such programs are proposed to help Latino students connect more to their school and promote educational success where declining same-ethnic representation poses the highest threat to belonging and achievement. The recommendation is grounded in the study's finding that low-diversity schools showed the steepest negative slopes for representation decline.

## Design Implications
