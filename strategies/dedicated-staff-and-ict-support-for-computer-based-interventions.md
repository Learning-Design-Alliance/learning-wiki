# Provide dedicated staff and better ICT support in schools implementing computer-based interventions like Units of Sound

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the trial's process evaluation, the report concludes that "Schools need dedicated staff and better ICT support in their school if they are to implement UofS." Technical issues with installing the programme and accessing online tests were the most common barriers reported by teaching assistants, and coaches reported that technical problems stemmed from a lack of technical support in the school. Coaches also felt that the modelling they provided during face-to-face training was important for achieving good implementation fidelity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School technical support available for installing and running the programme and online tests
- Staff released by senior management to complete online training before implementation
#### Constraints
- In this trial, inconsistent DA coach support occurred because schools would not allow visits, and staff turnover led to cancelled sessions and untrained replacement staff

### Target Learners
- Struggling readers at the primary-secondary transition in secondary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading ability
- Spelling ability
