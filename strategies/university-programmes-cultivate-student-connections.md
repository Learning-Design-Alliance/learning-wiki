# Universities should prioritise programmes that cultivate social connections among students during the transition to university

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that higher education institutions, many facing retention challenges and poor student health during transition, prioritise "the development and implementation of programmes such as academic cohort programs, learning communities or social groups and organisations designed to cultivate connections among students who share common interests and identities". It further points to social group interventions such as Groups4Health and peer-led social support interventions for first-year students as implementable options.
