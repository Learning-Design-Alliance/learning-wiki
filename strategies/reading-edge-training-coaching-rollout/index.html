Implement Reading Edge with two days of initial teacher training plus monthly in-class coaching and peer support structures¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's professional development model combines an initial workshop with ongoing classroom-embedded support: "Two days of initial training is followed by in-class coaching about once a month." Support is provided through onsite workshops, email and telephone support, just-in-time online resources, and onsite observations and consultation, with "a strong focus... on the development of regular peer support structures within the school."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Two days of workshop participation per teacher to begin implementation
  • classroom teachers deliver the program within existing Language Arts/Reading time

Target Learners¶

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful implementation of cooperative team reading practices
