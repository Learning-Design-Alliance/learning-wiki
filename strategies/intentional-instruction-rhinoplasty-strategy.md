# Provide intentional instruction through direct observation, verbalization of maneuvers, preoperative nasal analysis, and structured feedback during rhinoplasty training.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review proposes intentional instruction as a future direction to enhance rhinoplasty learning. Recommendations include: simulation models providing opportunities for constructive feedback and evaluation via direct observation by attendings or formal review of surgical videos; attending surgeons verbalizing maneuvers and notable decision-making points during operations; performing preoperative comprehensive nasal analysis between attending and trainee; utilizing Gunter diagrams for cartilage graft strategy and planning; and offering rhinoplasty educational opportunities including didactic lectures, journal clubs, cadaveric labs, and conferences. The authors emphasize that "intentional feedback and instruction allow for continual revision of techniques for improvement of techniques and ultimate aesthetic results."
