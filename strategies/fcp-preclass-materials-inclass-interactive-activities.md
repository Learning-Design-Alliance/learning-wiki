# Provide pre-class materials (videos, slides, modules, worksheets) with assessment, then use class time for interactive individual and group activities with the teacher as facilitator

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementation of the FCP provided the experimental group with a list of websites, videos, lecture notes, PowerPoint slides, and course modules to watch and read before class, plus assessment materials to test understanding while viewing. During face-to-face class time, students engaged in interactive activities done individually or in groups with the teacher acting as a facilitator. This moves content acquisition ahead of class and concept engagement into class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need internet access and adequate computer facilities to view pre-class materials
- Teachers may need to prepare additional materials and schedule extra activities for students lacking internet access or struggling with basic materials
#### Constraints
- Students without internet at home could not always access lessons; some felt helpless without immediate feedback while studying alone

### Target Learners
- college freshmen in college algebra

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding of challenging algebra topics (factorization, rational expressions, radical operations, applied problems)
