# Predict and warn students at risk to assist teacher management and prevent selective ignoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes using the EGWO-MLP model to predict and warn students, assisting teachers in managing and helping students. It states the prediction results "can enhance self-learning awareness of students and improve the teaching quality of teachers", help teachers reflect on teaching performance, and "can prevent teachers from selectively ignoring students with a poor foundation, resulting in the unfairness of the teaching process". It also suggests school decision-makers use such mining to monitor and regulate factors affecting teaching quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School management systems must make available the information affecting students' learning, which the article says is currently used mainly at a primary stage with simple queries and statistical tables
#### Constraints
- Online learning achievement prediction mainly relies on structured data, difficult to deeply mine learners' states and emotions

### Target Learners
- students at risk of poor achievement, including those with a poor foundation

### Target Learning Goals
- timely teacher support to improve students' academic performance and teaching quality

### Affordances
