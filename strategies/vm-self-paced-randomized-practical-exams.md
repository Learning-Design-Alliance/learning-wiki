# Administer practical exams through the virtual microscope so students can self-pace, revisit slides, and receive randomized slide sets with electronic grading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a practical exam mode with several hundred exam-only slides, faculty peer review of image quality, administration on student laptops over the school wireless network with honor code and proctors, randomized slide assignment, and electronic grading tools including single-click credit and importable performance reports. Because "each student is working on his or her laptop during exams, they can self-pace and revisit slides they are unsure of," which the authors call "a significant enhancement over traditional microscope or kodachrome-based exams that forced students into a timed lockstep for each question."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty peer review and approval of slide image quality before exam inclusion
- Student laptops and the institution's wireless network
- Faculty proctors and a student honor code for exam security
#### Constraints
- A pool of several hundred exam-only slides with restricted access is needed
- Slide randomization requires multiple digitized examples of the same tissues

### Target Learners
- medical students taking histology practical examinations

### Target Learning Goals
- assessment of histology structure identification
