# Use a simulated Geiger-counter experiment to tie experiments to statistical analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose replacing or supplementing the physical Poisson-distribution and radioactivity experiment, previously performed with a Geiger counter, with a simulation that lets students generate many trials, record each trial's result to a file, and treat the data with a data analysis tool. This exposes students to both the idea of simulating a physics experiment and the practice of statistical analysis of repeated measurements.
