# Monitor tutor performance and conduct, with schools implementing their own oversight mechanisms, to secure pupil benefit

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To ensure pupils derive maximum benefit from external tuition, the evaluation recommends ongoing monitoring of tutors by both the tuition provider and the participating schools. The report states "it is recommended that the Tutor Trust continues to put additional mechanisms in place to monitor tutor performance and conduct and that schools also implement such mechanisms". It further suggests more formal feedback from schools could be introduced to increase the consistency of tutor quality.
