# Peer Observation

## Description
Peer observation is a collaborative process where teachers observe each other's classroom practices to improve teaching and learning. It is designed to be non-judgmental and supportive, focusing on providing constructive feedback and encouraging reflection. The process typically involves a pre-observation meeting to discuss goals, the observation itself, and a post-observation meeting for feedback and reflection. The goal is to foster a culture of shared inquiry and continuous improvement among educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires commitment from teachers and school leadership, dedicated time for observations and feedback sessions, and a culture of trust and respect.
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging to implement due to time constraints, resistance to being observed, and the need for careful pairing of colleagues. Success depends on creating a safe and supportive environment for honest feedback.
