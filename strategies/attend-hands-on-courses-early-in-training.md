# Schedule hands-on skills courses early in the training pathway, before trainees accumulate substantial procedure experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that caecal intubation rate benefited only trainees with fewer than 70 pre-course procedures, the authors recommend that hands-on training courses "should be undertaken early in the training pathway". The strategy positions a standardised, faculty-led hands-on course with performance-enhancing feedback at the novice stage, where basic-principles teaching can expedite procedural completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by accredited trainers at accredited training centres
- trainees must have ongoing training lists available after the course to consolidate learning
#### Constraints
- The PICI benefit applied regardless of experience, so early timing is supported specifically by the CIR data; the study was non-randomised and observational

### Target Learners
- Novice procedural trainees early in their training pathway

### Target Learning Goals
- Procedural competence and completion-based performance metrics in colonoscopy

## Claims

- [The unassisted caecal intubation rate showed a significant step-change improvement only in the least experienced trainee group (< 70 pre-course procedures), not in the 70-140 or > 140 groups](../claims/cir-step-change-only-early-trainees.md) [+W]
- [Trainees whose caecal intubation rate improved after the course had performed significantly fewer pre-course procedures (median 98 vs 122, P = 0.006) and had a lower pre-course CIR (median 36% vs 56%, P < 0.001)](../claims/cir-improvers-fewer-pre-course-procedures.md) [+W]
- [Pre-course improvement in caecal intubation rate slowed with increasing experience, averaging 4.2, 3.6 and 1.7 percentage points per 10 procedures in the three experience groups](../claims/pre-course-cir-gradient-slows-with-experience.md) [+W]
