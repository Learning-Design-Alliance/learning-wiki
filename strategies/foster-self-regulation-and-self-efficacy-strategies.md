## Description
Based on the path-model findings, the article recommends that educators prioritize strategies building students' self-regulation and self-efficacy. As the authors conclude, "The findings underscore the necessity for educational strategies that foster self-regulation and self-efficacy, as these constructs emerge as pivotal in propelling academic success." The strategy targets the two constructs the model identified as most central to achievement, directly or as mediators.
