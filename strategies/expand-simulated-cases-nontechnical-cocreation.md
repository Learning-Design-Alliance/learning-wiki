# Expand contextualised simulation to non-technical skills and co-create future cases with students as partners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's results and qualitative feedback, the authors recommend broadening the remit of contextualised simulation activities and involving students in case design. They suggest "patient videos or live, online 'patient' interactions with actors could be used to help develop students' patient communication skills", an area not covered, and that "future case development could potentially rely quite heavily on collaboration and co-creation with students as partners in the process".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case scenarios with patient histories, radiographs and photographs to provide contextual background for each activity
#### Constraints
- Communication skills were not covered in the current iteration of the cases; expansion is a suggestion for future development, not a tested manipulation

### Target Learners
- 4th- and 5th-year undergraduate dental students

### Target Learning Goals
- Patient communication and other non-technical skills
- Confidence in areas of knowledge identified by students through reflection
