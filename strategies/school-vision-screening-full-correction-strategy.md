# Incorporate regular visual acuity and accommodative function assessment and full myopia correction into school vision screening

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that schools and parents implement regular visual acuity and functional assessments, including binocular AF and AA, and ensure myopic children receive full, timely spectacle correction with regular follow-up. They suggest "incorporating visual function assessment such as AA and AF into routine vision screening to establish a more comprehensive visual health evaluation system" within a home-school collaborative framework. The recommendation rests on the study's cross-sectional associations, not on a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires vision screening capacity in educational settings, including accommodative facility and amplitude measurement and subjective refraction to detect undercorrection.
#### Constraints
- The cross-sectional design prevented determining causal relationships; results come from Chinese-speaking primary school children in grades 2–5 and may differ in other age groups or language contexts.

### Target Learners
- Primary school children (grades 2–5)

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining visual status that supports classroom learning and academic performance
