# Sorted, statement-by-statement instructor reports as formative assessment for lab courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A formative-assessment reporting strategy in which each E-CLASS statement's class results are compactly displayed and ranked by fractional agreement with experts, alongside participation summaries, overall class scores, expectations plots, and comparisons with similar-level classes. The authors state that their "standard presentation of results was primarily designed to give actionable feedback that instructors could use to improve their courses." Reports are delivered as PDFs at semester's end so instructors can identify which aspects of experimental epistemology their course may be influencing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Post-survey class data and a comparison group of students in similar-level classes to provide a baseline for judging whether results are typical
#### Constraints
- Epistemology categories were omitted from the initial survey version, limiting its utility for broadly contrasting the epistemological impact of different curricular approaches

### Target Learners
- instructors of undergraduate physics laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying gaps between student and expert views of experimentation to guide iterative course improvement
