# Write Alouds

## Description
Write-aloud lessons, also known as modeled writing, involve an experienced writer (typically the teacher) verbalizing their thought process while writing. This metacognitive strategy helps learners understand the decisions and processes involved in constructing different types of texts. The instructor explicitly demonstrates how writers construct various kinds of texts by verbalizing the internal dialog they use as they write, explicitly demonstrating metacognitive processes. These sessions are brief (10-15 minutes) and focused on specific aspects of writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor expertise in writing, chart paper or document viewer for writing demonstration, prepared writing examples. Requires a structured lesson plan and clear articulation of thought processes.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming to prepare effective write-aloud lessons. Learners may become passive if the modeling is not engaging. Effectiveness depends on the instructor’s ability to articulate their thought processes clearly.

### Affordances
- Makes the writing process transparent, provides explicit instruction in cognitive strategies, supports independent writing skills, offers real-time modeling and feedback

### Personalization
- Teachers can adapt the type of writing modeled based on learner needs and challenges. The level of detail and complexity can be adjusted to suit different skill levels. Flexible formats allow for individual, small group, or whole class instruction.

## Assessment Evidence
- Improvements in learner writing quality as evidenced by better structure, clarity, and use of techniques modeled. Increased metacognitive awareness demonstrated through self-reflection and peer feedback.

## Impact
- Research demonstrates that students improve their writing ability when cognitive strategies are demonstrated for them in clear and explicit ways. Modeled writing helps learners see the forms and functions of writing as they observe and participate in writing events directed by knowledgeable writers.
