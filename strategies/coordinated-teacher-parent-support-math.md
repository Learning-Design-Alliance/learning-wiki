# Coordinated teacher–parent support practices to strengthen self-efficacy, buoyancy, and mathematics performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical implications recommend paired actions by teachers and parents. Teachers are encouraged "to provide opportunities for student decision-making, support their choices, and show genuine interest" to strengthen perceived teacher support, while parents maintain regular communication with mathematics teachers and discuss math-related challenges and achievements. The rationale is that coordinated efforts characterized by consistent encouragement, targeted support, and open communication foster buoyancy, strengthen self-efficacy, and improve mathematics performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
