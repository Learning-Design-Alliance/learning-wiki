# Assume Support

## Description
Be aware that families’ attitudes and beliefs about education may vary, and it’s a mistake to assume that all students have parents who promote school engagement. Logistics related to online learning (consistent internet access, reliable devices, quiet spaces) are also highly variable. An empathetic practitioner should assume that all families and students are doing the best they can with what they have in the current situation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open communication with families, flexibility in expectations, access to resources and support services.
