# Resolve intuitive inconsistencies by connecting the phenomenon to broader intuitions via analogies, blends, microscopic mechanisms, causal stories, reframing, and multiple perspectives

## Description
Once a student has articulated the conflict, the article enumerates strategies a teacher might use: analogies to familiar phenomena, chained analogies forming a 'conceptual blend', explanations via more intuitive microscopic phenomena, building a 'causal story' or chain of cause-and-effect, and reframing or recategorizing an idea. In all cases the goal is to show the phenomenon is coherent with the student's broader intuitions, and examining a problem from several angles widens the range of possible responses.
