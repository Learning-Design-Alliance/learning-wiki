# Develop standardized DHN training curricula that integrate interpersonal soft skills with digital competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that interpersonal and digital competencies are closely intertwined in patient-centered DHN support, the authors propose that DHN training should combine both. They argue a standardized training curriculum may be necessary to ensure role clarity and quality of care, given varying levels of digital, clinical, and communicative competencies across health care professions, while curricula may need adaptation to professional backgrounds and health system contexts. As the authors write, "we propose that effective DHN training should integrate both interpersonal (soft) skills and digital competencies, as these are closely intertwined in enabling patient-centered support and safe, competent use of DMHAs." The strategy responds to participants' consistent emphasis on specific training for the DHN function.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must address role-specific tasks and both interpersonal and digital competencies
- Curricula may need adaptation to specific professional backgrounds and health system contexts
#### Constraints
- The authors note the optimal training pathway might differ across countries and settings

### Target Learners
- digital health navigators in outpatient mental health care
- health care professionals taking on the DHN function

### Target Learning Goals
- patient-centered support for digital mental health app use
- safe and competent use of digital therapeutics
- recognizing and escalating warning signs
