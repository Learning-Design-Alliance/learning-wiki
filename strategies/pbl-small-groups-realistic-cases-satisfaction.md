# Design PBL courses around small group size and realistic case scenarios to support student satisfaction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Drawing on prior work on healthcare students' satisfaction with small-group active learning, the article recommends that course organizers attend to the structural features that drive PBL's popularity. It reports that "Several important points were reported to be the key players in achieving such popularity including small group size, realistic case scenarios," and concludes that organizers should focus on these factors when designing and constructing the courses. This is an implementable design recipe for PBL sessions in clinical medical education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL courses must be organized with small groups and realistic case scenarios as the satisfaction-driving features
