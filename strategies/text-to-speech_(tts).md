# Text-to-Speech (TTS)

## Description
Text-to-speech (TTS) technology converts written text into spoken words, enhancing accessibility and comprehension for individuals with dyslexia, visual impairments, or other learning differences. It involves highlighting text on a digital device, and then using software or an app to read the text aloud. Users can adjust the voice, speed, and pitch to suit their preferences. Devices include computers, tablets, and smartphones with TTS capabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) with text-to-speech software or app. Digital text that can be highlighted and read aloud. Headphones or speakers for audio output.
#### Constraints
- Comprehension may be reduced if the synthesized voice is difficult to understand. May not accurately convey tone or emotion. Not all digital content is compatible with TTS technology. Reliance on TTS may hinder the development of decoding skills for some individuals.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students and adults with dyslexia, visual impairments, or anyone who struggles with reading.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve reading comprehension, support independent learning, enhance access to digital content, and increase vocabulary development.

### Affordances
- Reduces the cognitive load associated with decoding and reading, allowing individuals to focus on comprehension. Provides an alternative means of accessing written information for those with visual impairments. Improves vocabulary development by exposing individuals to new words in context.

### Personalization
- Users can select different voices, adjust the speaking rate, and choose whether to highlight words as they are read aloud. The technology supports diverse devices and platforms, including computers, tablets, and smartphones. Languages and accents can be selected based on learner needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Measure reading comprehension with and without TTS. Evaluate the speed and accuracy of reading aloud. Gather feedback from users on their experience and perceived benefits. Track improvements in reading skills and vocabulary development over time.

## Impact
- Research shows that TTS technology improves reading comprehension and reduces reading fatigue for individuals with dyslexia and visual impairments. Enhances access to digital content and supports independent learning.
