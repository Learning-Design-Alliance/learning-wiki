# Integrate computational thinking across K-12 subject areas rather than treating it as computer-science-only

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper recommends teaching computational thinking across subject areas in K-12 schools because it is "a skillset for solving complex problems, a way to learn topics in any discipline, and a necessity for fully participating in a computational world." Integration complements, not replaces, computer science expansion: where computer science is not yet offered, embedding computational thinking in existing disciplines empowers educators and students, and schools already teaching coding benefit from weaving it across disciplines. Suggested classroom activities include creating websites and apps for authentic users, analyzing and communicating with data, and creating computational models of complex systems.
