# Combine video viewing with post-viewing activities to test comprehension and encourage productive use of technical vocabulary

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Following Tarnopolsky's constructivist blended learning approach, the article proposes that post-viewing activities are as important as the viewing activity itself. The study implemented this by having students complete a five-minute pre-test, watch a ten-minute mini-lesson, complete a 25-minute test, and fill in a five-minute feedback questionnaire.
