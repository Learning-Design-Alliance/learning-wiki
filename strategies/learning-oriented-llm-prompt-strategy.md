# Design learning-oriented LLM prompts around guided questions, step-by-step support, reflection, and a thinking partner rather than direct answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Drawing on the discourse of the learning- and skill-oriented creator groups, the article's Figure 1 synthesis recommends that effective learning prompts combine G1 and G2 practices: guided questions, step-by-step support, reflection, and using ChatGPT as a thinking partner, while avoiding prompts that request direct answers. G1 creators modeled this orientation, e.g., "Your goal is to use AI as a wise tutor who guides you with questions, not as a vending machine that just spits out the answer." The strategy treats prompting as a learnable practice that keeps the learner cognitively engaged.
