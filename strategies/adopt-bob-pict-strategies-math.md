# Adopt BOB, PICT or BO-PICT instructional strategies to improve mathematics achievement and student motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers embrace BOB, PICT and BO-PICT whenever achievement, positive attitude and motivation are priorities, and that school administrators hold seminars and workshops on the BO-PICT strategy so teachers can adopt it for effective classroom instruction. It further recommends ministry-level campaigns and in-service training to enable teachers to use varied instructional practices in lesson delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher in-service training to build capacity in using multiple instructional practices.
#### Constraints
- Evidence is limited to one mathematics topic (compound proportion and rates of work) over three weeks in Kenyan secondary schools.

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
- positive attitude towards mathematics
- motivation for learning
