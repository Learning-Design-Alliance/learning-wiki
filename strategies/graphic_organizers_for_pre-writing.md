# Graphic Organizers for Pre-Writing

## Description
Students use graphic organizers to plan and structure their ideas before drafting a written piece, jotting down notes rather than writing complete sentences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates, writing prompts.
#### Constraints
- May be seen as an extra step and could be ineffective if students are not shown how to use them properly.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those who benefit from visual planning tools

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Organize thoughts, structure writing, develop ideas

### Affordances
- Provides a clear framework for writing, enhances organization, and reduces writing anxiety.

### Personalization
- Learners can select the type of organizer that best suits their writing task (e.g., mind map, outline).
