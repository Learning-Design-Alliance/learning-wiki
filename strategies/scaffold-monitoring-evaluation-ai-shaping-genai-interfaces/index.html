GenAI learning interfaces should actively scaffold monitoring, evaluation, and AI-shaping behaviors adolescents may not bring to the interaction on their own¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on the finding that students' interactions were request-dominated with near-absent monitoring and evaluation, the article recommends that "the results point toward GenAI learning interfaces that actively scaffold the monitoring, evaluation, and AI-shaping behaviors that adolescents may not yet bring to the interaction on their own." This means interface-level supports prompting learners to make comprehension needs visible, check AI responses against their goals, and impose scaffolding constraints such as requesting hints rather than answers. The article frames this as informing "scaffolds for more agentic and epistemically proactive GenAI use."

Target Learning Goals¶
