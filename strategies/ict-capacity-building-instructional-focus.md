# Train teachers and tutors on instructional use rather than device mastery, and include school-level storage and maintenance training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends capacity-building that focuses on improving instruction rather than on mastering hardware, and warns that adopting ICT components takes substantial time and repetition. It states that "Training at the school level on proper storage and maintenance can have huge cost-effectiveness impacts, as this will decrease loss of or damage to devices." The PRIMR team was struck by the length of time and repetition teachers and tutors needed to adopt the ICT components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time and repetition for teachers and tutors at all levels to build capacity on hardware, software, and instructional implementation
#### Constraints
- The article notes the strategy emerged from the Kisumu County pilot context and should be applied with caution in other contexts

### Target Learners
- classroom teachers and TAC tutors in Kenyan primary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- effective classroom implementation of ICT-supported literacy instruction
