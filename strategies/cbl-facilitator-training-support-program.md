# Comprehensive facilitator training and support program before and during CBL implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before implementation, facilitators underwent a two-week training program covering CBL principles, roles, and hands-on workshops simulating CBL sessions; during the semester they received regular mentoring, weekly debriefing meetings, and a peer support network. The article states facilitators "were trained for the critical analysis of clinical cases, formulating relevant questions that trigger critical thinking, and how to guide discussions to maximize students' learning." Selection criteria included at least five years of applied physiology teaching experience and prior experience with interactive methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Volunteer physiologists with expertise in applied physiology and prior experience with interactive teaching methodologies
- Training delivered by multidisciplinary education experts, including a senior professor with more than fifteen years of CBL implementation experience
#### Constraints
- Adequate or persistent facilitators' training is required to ensure their ability to effectively guide CBL, manage group dynamics, and provide constructive feedback (Table 6)

### Target Learners
- physiology faculty facilitators of case-based learning sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- effective CBL facilitation, group-dynamics management, and constructive feedback
