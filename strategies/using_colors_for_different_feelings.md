# Using Colors for Different Feelings

## Description
Learners use different colors to represent different emotions in art, which creates associations between emotions and visual cues, enhancing emotional expression and awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to art supplies (e.g., crayons, paint, paper). A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Requires learners to be comfortable expressing emotions through art. Requires a supportive and inclusive environment.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Early childhood and elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Enhance emotional expression, improve emotional awareness, promote positive associations with emotional expression

### Affordances
- Connects emotional expression with a creative outlet (art). Promotes a visual and tangible approach to learning about emotions.

### Personalization
- Colors can be chosen by learners or assigned based on cultural associations. Different art mediums can be used.

- Connecting emotions with visual cues can enhance emotional learning and regulation. (Source: Art Therapy and Emotional Regulation - https://www.arttherapy.org/)
