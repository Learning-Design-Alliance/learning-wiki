# Standardise outcome measurements and isolate individual instructional design features in future research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's forward‐looking recommendation is that "future research should incorporate standardised outcome measurements to enable between study comparisons and directly isolate and compare instructional design features". It argues addressing these gaps and standardising methodologies will advance training, offer future recommendations and improve clinical practice and patient safety. It also notes a meta‐analysis may become feasible as randomised controlled trials accumulate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Future studies must standardise outcome measurement and directly isolate instructional design features between groups
#### Constraints
- Meta‐analysis was not possible due to heterogeneity of study design, outcomes, modalities, features and assessment methods

### Target Learners
- healthcare providers training in ultrasound‐guided regional anaesthesia

### Target Learning Goals
- evidence‐based simulation curriculum design for regional anaesthesia
