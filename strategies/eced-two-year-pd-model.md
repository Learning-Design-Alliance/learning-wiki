# Two-year professional development model combining institutes, site visits, and remote monitoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ECED's professional development spans both program years: a three-day teacher institute covering orientation, creating learning targets, pacing guides, unit frameworks, assessment blueprints and plans, and a vertical alignment map; four three-day site visits per year with large-group, department, and individual coaching; and remote monitoring with feedback on curriculum documents and monthly phone check-ins. The second year, optional, allows refinement and builds capacity to sustain the work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both years include the three-day institute, four 3-day site visits per year, and remote monitoring and feedback
#### Constraints
- The second year of professional development is optional

### Target Learners
- high school math teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- creating learning targets and assessments
- building a repertoire of high-yield instructional strategies
- sustaining curricular work
