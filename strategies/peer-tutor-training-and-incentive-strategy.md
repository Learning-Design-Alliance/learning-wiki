# Train peer tutors in a pre-session workshop and sustain quality with feedback questionnaires and incentive awards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before implementation, peer tutors received a two-hour workshop held half a month before the sessions covering the theory and process of PA-PBL, core competencies such as "case facilitation techniques (e.g. open-ended questioning, structuring thought processes)", feedback strategies, and team-collaboration methods, plus experience sharing by distinguished prior tutors with real-case reviews. Quality was then sustained through "a continuous feedback and incentive mechanism": regular student-evaluation questionnaires yielding personalized improvement suggestions, and end-of-semester Outstanding Peer Tutor awards combining honorary recognition and material rewards.
