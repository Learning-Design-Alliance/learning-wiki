# Use women scientists as role models sharing life stories with girls

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The strategy organises interactions between girls and women scientists so that women share their professional experiences together with their life stories of how they became scientists, serving as role models, while girls share their own life stories, future plans, and challenges. The article reports this was implemented with almost 1000 girls, with many activities focused on astronomy.

## Design Implications

### Context

- Settings where girls can also share their own stories and challenges
