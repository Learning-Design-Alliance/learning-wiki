## Description
A future-research recommendation from the article: to assess the design process itself rather than only its quiz outcomes, the author proposes that researchers "record student interactio ns to look for diﬀerences in the way that the students approach solving the design problems", which "would be a more direct assessment of the design process itself". The author also raises testing whether a longer interval between the lab activity and the quiz would change results.
