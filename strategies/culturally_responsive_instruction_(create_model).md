# Culturally Responsive Instruction (CREATE Model)

## Description
This strategy entails providing instruction that empowers students intellectually, socially, emotionally, and politically by using cultural referents to impart knowledge, skills, and attitudes. It involves making standards-based content accessible by incorporating relatable aspects of students' daily lives, such as language, prior knowledge, and extracurricular interests. Teachers use student-centered stories, vocabulary, and language while infusing hip hop, sports, and other student interests without seeming fake.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need to understand students' cultures and be willing to adapt their teaching methods. Requires ongoing reflection and adjustment.
#### Constraints
- Risk of stereotyping or appearing insincere. Requires time and effort to develop culturally relevant materials.
