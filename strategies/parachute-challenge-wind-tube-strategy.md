# Parachute design challenge in a DIY wind tube following the engineering design process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Using the Tubora DIY wind tube (a fan and transparent plastic tube), groups receive identical materials (paper, tulle, cotton, umbrella fabric, plastic bags, string, masking tape) and must design the most efficient solution to lift a too-heavy stuffed-animal 'skydiver' out of the tube. Students "proceed through the engineering design process, which involves defining the problem, brainstorming potential solutions, creating prototypes and testing them in a wind tunnel", complete a record sheet, and draft front and top view sketches to document and share.
