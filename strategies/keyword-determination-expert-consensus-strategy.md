# Select keywords for partial captions by expert-teacher consensus on script importance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a keyword determination procedure adapted from Guillory (1998) and Park (2004): five experienced teachers watched the films, read transcripts, and underlined words important for understanding sentences. The researchers processed the selections and found that "above 80% of the keywords were selected by all five teachers", choosing nouns, verbs, adjectives, and high-rate words from the full script captions. This yields keyword captions that reduce reading load while keeping meaning-critical words.

## Design Implications
