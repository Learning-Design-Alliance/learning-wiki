# Teachers should periodically test all pupils in a wide variety of mental and physical traits to gain intimate knowledge of individual qualities and weaknesses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 4 studies (4 associational), `q2` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "every teacher should, at intervals, test all the pupils in his grade in as great a variety of traits, mental and physical, as possible." Such testing yields "intimate knowledge of and correct insight into the individual qualities and weaknesses" of pupils, explaining difficulties and removing obstacles that would otherwise remain mysteries. The article's own battery shows this can be done without extensive apparatus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tests conducted at intervals on all pupils in the grade
- A variety of simple mental and physical measures chosen from an available list
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- grammar-grade pupils in a single teacher's classroom

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing individual strengths and weaknesses to adapt grading, special interests, and educational processes to individual needs
