# Support school data use through intermediary instructional officers rather than directly

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that DRE's instructional support work "with school staff through the AIOs, who will serve as intermediaries," providing assistance from basic help understanding test score and gain results to helping staff "formulate and conceptualize questions about student performance and then design inquiry strate- gies to pursue them." Area offices include data analysts, and DRE staff provide them with data and training rather than working directly with schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Area Instructional Officers and their staffs prepared to show school staff how data can improve instruction; dissemination systems for data to the AIOs
#### Constraints
- Depending on AIO staff backgrounds, this may require more DRE staff members than the plan describes

### Target Learners
- Principals, teachers, and area instructional staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Use of data, including standardized test results, to improve instruction
