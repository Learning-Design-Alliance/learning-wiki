# Clinical teachers adapt teaching to include more active learning activities and visual technology, assessed with STRICT

## Description
The article recommends that clinical teachers and educators use STRICT feedback to adjust their teaching. The conclusion states "clinical teachers might adapt their teaching practices to include more active learning activities and utilize visual technology to facilitate clinical learning efficacy". Clinical educators may apply the scale to assess how these practices are implemented in current clinical settings.
