## Description
The authors propose that future iterations of the A-SPIRE model could incorporate AI tools within its practical stages, noting that "Future iterations of the A-SPIRE model could incorporate AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT for message drafting, AI analytics for audience insight) within its practical stages, while emphasizing ethical oversight and human judgment." They caution that risks such as misinformation, over-reliance on technology, and erosion of interpersonal communication skills necessitate a balanced approach where AI serves as an adjunct rather than a replacement.
