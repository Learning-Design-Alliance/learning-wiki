# Include a directly-observed clinic-based assessment of the bHIT as a specific component of EPAs for evaluating dizzy patients

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that competency-based curricula include direct observation of the bHIT within dizziness-related EPAs, since the current Otolaryngology EPA permits competence judgments without direct bHIT observation while the neurology EPA mandates it. It also suggests rater training, more raters, single competent-or-not judgments, and repeated measurements over time to improve reliability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Entrustable professional activities formulated to require direct observation of the skill
#### Constraints
- Findings may not generalize beyond Otolaryngology residents because of convenience sampling at one review course

### Target Learners
- Otolaryngology and other residency trainees assessing dizzy patients

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate performance and interpretation of the bedside head impulse test
