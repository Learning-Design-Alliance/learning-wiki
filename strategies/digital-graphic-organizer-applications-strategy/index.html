Use digital graphic organizer applications for collaborative concept mapping¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3–q4 · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article highlights technology applications featuring graphic organizers that "have opened the door for collaborative opportunities providing a platform for students to work on their project in real-time in partners or even groups." Applications include Popplet, Bubbl.us, and Educreations, which increase technology skills and science knowledge.
