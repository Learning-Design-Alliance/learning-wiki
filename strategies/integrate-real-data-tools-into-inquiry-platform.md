## Description
The authors recommend embedding the data-analysis backbone into an online teaching and learning environment: "SalsaJ is now a backbone project to integrate tools in an online teaching and learning platform, STOCHASMOS". This integration is presented as improving incorporation into learning environments designed for reflective inquiry and guided inquiry-science teaching in classrooms.
