# Partner with existing teacher-led active classrooms and evaluate them empirically to build high-school evidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The essay's future-directions section recommends that researchers "work with these existing classrooms and empirically evaluate their efficacy using cognitive and psycho-emotional assessments, as well as learning outcomes", building on Ontario high schools where classroom physical activity, especially in mathematics, is already led by teacher champions. It pairs this with qualitative surveys and focus groups involving high school teachers and administrators to understand barriers to implementation and inform the development of effective classroom-based programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires identifying existing high-school classrooms where teacher champions already incorporate physical activity and are well-informed of the link between physical activity and academic learning.
#### Constraints
- The essay notes this existing work "is being led only in specific schools and classrooms by teacher champions", so the starting pool of classrooms is limited.

### Target Learners
- high school students (adolescents)

### Target Learning Goals
- academic learning outcomes
- adolescent cognitive and psycho-emotional functioning
