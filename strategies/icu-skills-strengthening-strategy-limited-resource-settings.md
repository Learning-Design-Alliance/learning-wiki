# Strengthen ICU nursing skills through targeted training, clinical guidelines, SOPs, mentorship, and access to learning resources

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a bundle of supports for ICU nurses in limited resource settings: "innovative educational interventions on evidence based ETS recommendations, clinical guidelines coupled with adequate support like mentorship, support systems like guidelines and SOPs be in place", plus learning resources such as journals, computers and internet to encourage use of evidence-based information. It rests on the finding that training improved knowledge but practice remained poor, so education must be paired with guidelines and workplace support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should reflect needs and be conducted according to established protocol, as the article cites studies showing low knowledge and practice can be improved this way
#### Constraints
- The study's findings cannot be generalized to the whole Tanzania because nurses in referral hospitals outside Dar es Salaam were not included

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses in limited resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based endotracheal suctioning knowledge and practice
- Adherence to clinical practice guidelines
