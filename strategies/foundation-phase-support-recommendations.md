# Extend Foundation Phase support through nutrition, motor apparatus, emotional stimulation, home-language attention, music, and parent awareness of television

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From the findings the author recommends concrete measures for improving Foundation Phase education: "The National Feeding Scheme should be extended to all learners not receiving adequate nutrition (including fresh running water) at school and home"; motor abilities should be improved by providing appropriate apparatus in schools; specific measures should stimulate emotional status and self-images; children whose home language differs from the medium of instruction should receive special attention; classical music should be introduced; programmes should reflect multiple intelligences and learning styles; and parents should be made aware of the detrimental effects of extended television watching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quality intervention programmes and adequately trained, dedicated teachers, which the article names as contributory factors towards programme success
#### Constraints
- The author recommends focused interventions aimed at poorly-performing schools, not forcing all schools to participate

### Target Learners
- Foundation Phase learners (Grades 1-3), especially deprived learners

### Target Learning Goals
- School readiness
- numeracy, reading and writing
- conceptual development
- emotional and motor development
