## Description
The article's recommendation list pairs curricular and assessment changes with experiential activities to build student interest in physics. It calls for a high school curriculum with "more conceptual understanding," testing that includes multiple choice questions alongside descriptions and essays, and activities where "Class demonstrations and hands-on experience with lab equipment will help to develop interest." It adds semester-long and interdisciplinary projects and student involvement in research through neighboring universities as ways to attract students to science.
