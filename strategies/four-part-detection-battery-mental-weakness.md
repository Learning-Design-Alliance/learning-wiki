# Detect suspected mental debility in children via a four-part test battery plus medical examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy operationalizes Major's recommendation that detection of mental debility be carried out through a short, structured battery: "the chief tests being (1) mathematical problems; (2) questions involving discrimination; (3) tests of school learning, and (4) various tests by the psychoanalytic method," followed by "an elaborate outline for medical examination." The review presents this as a procedure usable beyond specialists, since the method "can be applied by the capable parent and teacher."
