# Use topic modeling of student project corpora to inform context-specific resources supporting open inquiry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose their methodology — applying BERTopic topic modeling to a corpus of student project abstracts, then testing topic distributions across contextual variables — as a reusable approach for other fairs and open inquiry settings. They argue findings can "inform designers and teachers on what kind of resources would be most relevant to support students in open inquiry processes", with resources designed taking into account context-specific opportunities and constraints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A corpus of student project materials (e.g., titles, keywords, abstracts) from the fair or open inquiry setting being studied
#### Constraints
- The authors state the list of topics is specific to this study's context and may vary in other contexts, so they propose the methodology rather than generalizing the results to any SEF

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- problem finding and topic selection in student-driven projects
