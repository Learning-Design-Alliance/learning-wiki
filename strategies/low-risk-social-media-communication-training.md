# Use a low-risk social media platform as a safe space where scientists at all career stages practice science communication

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The project treats Instagram as a training ground for science communication, a skill the article says is not included at any stage of formal physics education. It states that "The low-risk environment of Instagram encourages exploring creative ways to share science through low-risk elements", equipping scientists at all career stages rather than aiming to make them professional communicators. The authors link this skill to job markets, grants and stakeholder communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive, low-risk platform and team structure where early career researchers can create and refine public-facing science content
#### Constraints
- The stated goal is not for team members to all become professional science communicators but to equip the majority with career-relevant communication skills

### Target Learners
- scientists at all career stages, particularly early career researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- communicating complex research topics effectively to non-expert audiences
