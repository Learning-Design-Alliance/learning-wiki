# 5-Minute Writing Conferences

## Description
This strategy involves conducting brief, targeted conferences with each student to provide personalized feedback on their writing. Prior to the conferences, teachers read student essays, record a score from the rubric, and note one strength and one area for improvement. Students reflect on their papers and choose a growth area to discuss. During the five-minute conference, the student shares their self-assessment, and the teacher provides targeted feedback. This approach allows for more effective communication and relationship-building compared to written comments alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires significant class time (three 55-minute periods for a class of 30). Teachers must prepare notes on each student's essay in advance.
#### Constraints
- Time-intensive, requiring careful management and preparation. May not be suitable for all class sizes or schedules.

### Affordances
- Provides individually targeted instruction, allows students to ask questions, and fosters a stronger teacher-student relationship. Improves student understanding of rubrics.

### Personalization
- Students choose a specific growth area to focus on during the conference, tailoring the feedback to their individual needs.
