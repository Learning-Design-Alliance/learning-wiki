# Vary test types in grammar classes to reduce monotony and foster learner autonomy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the null finding, the article recommends that teachers not rely on a single test format. It states teachers "can concentrate and embark upon more tests in order to change the serious and boring mood of grammar classes", and that using cloze passages with grammatical deletions adds diversity, encourages students to be more autonomous and self-confident, and can also support reading comprehension and vocabulary exercises at the students' level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers willing to try new teaching and testing techniques rather than routine approaches
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a study showing no advantage for either format, not that varied testing raises grammar achievement

### Target Learners
- EFL pre-university students

### Target Learning Goals
- grammar knowledge
- learner autonomy
- reading comprehension
