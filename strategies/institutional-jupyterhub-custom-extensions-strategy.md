# Combine custom JupyterLab extensions on an institutional JupyterHub to give students a bespoke, ready-to-use computing environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes combining multiple JupyterLab extensions to 'provide a bespoke, ready-to-use programming and computing environment for students or researchers of a given discipline'. Its paradigmatic example is EPFL's NOTO service, built on JupyterHub, where the jupyterlab-mol-visualizer extension is preinstalled and listed in the launcher. The authors conclude that 'Institutional JupyterHubs thus provide an effective means of bringing extensions like jupyterlab-mol-visualizer ... to a large number of users'.
