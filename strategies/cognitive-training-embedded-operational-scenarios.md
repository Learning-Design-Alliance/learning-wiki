# Cognitive performance training embedded in operational scenarios under fatigue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends shifting cognitive training from isolated drills toward conditioning relevant to mission demands. It states that training sustained attention, reaction time, and working memory "is most likely to be successful when embedded within operational scenarios, especially those that require cognition under fatigue and in moments of stressful demands". It also targets metacognitive load management, teaching crews to recognize when workload has exceeded appropriate decision margins and to redistribute duties or simplify procedures without external assistance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Repeated feedback on limiting performance factors, extrapolated from elite performance frameworks, rather than scenario repetition without context
#### Constraints
- Presented as a translational recommendation from elite performance domains, not a spaceflight-tested result

### Target Learners
- astronaut crews during pre-flight preparation and in-flight maintenance

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained attention, reaction time, working memory, cognitive load management, situational awareness
