# Use a short validated self-report rubric longitudinally to track SDL competency development and target students for remediation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends administering the five-item SDL self-assessment rubric repeatedly across the pre-clerkship phase so that "repeated observations of students indicating patterns of performance" serve as more reliable assessments than a single timepoint. Longitudinal use lets institutions identify students needing remediation and evaluate whether educational strategies foster SDL skill development. Planned refinements include adding a pre-course baseline administration and refining proficiency levels and anchors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration at defined time points (the article suggests once or twice per academic year through the end of the pre-clerkship phase)
#### Constraints
- Self-assessment may not represent actual skillset, as most populations undertaking self-assessment tend to overestimate their skills; the article plans a faculty version to compare self- and external ratings

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- monitoring growth in self-directed learning competency
- identifying students for remediation or assistance
