# Collect facility-level workload data to distribute human resources efficiently and maintain competence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends collecting facility-level data on delivery workload per skilled birth attendant, because workload translates into experience and competence. The conclusion states such data "could allow for a more efficient distribution of limited human resources within a health system and improve quality of care". The study found workload ranged from 0 to 184 deliveries per SBA and was associated with vignette scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Surveillance or facility data on births per facility
- Staffing counts of skilled birth attendants per facility
#### Constraints
- Workload estimates are likely underestimates where facilities serve women outside the surveillance area

### Target Learners
- Skilled birth attendants and health system planners in district health systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintenance of professional delivery care skills through adequate practice volume
