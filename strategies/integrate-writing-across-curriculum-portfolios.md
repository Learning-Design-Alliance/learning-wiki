# Integrate writing across the curriculum and maintain portfolios to reposition writing as integral to classroom culture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes how the teacher-researcher Patricia integrated writing across the curriculum and across multiple activities so that writing became integral to the culture of the classroom. Writers' Workshops were integrated across core curriculum like social studies and the sciences, as well as into field trips and other non-classroom activities. Students maintained a portfolio where they stored their published work and self-selected pieces from their journals.
