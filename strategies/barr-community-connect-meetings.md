# Community Connect meetings to coordinate out-of-school resources for students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
BARR includes "Community Connect meetings ... designed to implement comprehensive strategies to address the needs of the students who need resources outside of school for enrichment and support." This extends the model's data-and-relationships work beyond the school day by connecting students and families to external enrichment and support resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination by the BARR coordinator, who works with school staff, administrators, and parents/guardians to support the needs of all students.
