# Extend the sphere-versus-disk illumination analysis to images of Venus and the Earth

## Description
The article recommends using the model's results to interpret illumination effects on other celestial bodies in the classroom. Because Venus's internal orbit makes "the relative positions of Venus, the Sun, and the Earth produce phases on the planet equivalent to the lunar phases", its convex terminator is the easiest planetary one to observe. The same analysis is applied to the Apollo 8 Earthrise photograph, where the authors infer "the Earth phase in this picture would be waning gibbous Earth", checkable against a lunar calendar.
