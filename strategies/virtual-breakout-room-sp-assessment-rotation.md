# Administer a rotating virtual breakout-room SP assessment with 15-minute interviews and 5-minute feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a logistically specific virtual administration: students were divided into rotating groups, each student placed in a breakout room with an SP and a facilitator on Zoom. "The allotted time was 20 minutes, with 15 minutes for the interview and 5 minutes for feedback," and a designated facilitator sent a wrap-up notification one minute before the interview ended. Feedback covered clinical, cultural, and communication aspects structured by the checklist. All 15 Advanced-course students completed sessions in approximately 1 hour using this rotation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video platform with breakout rooms
- SPs and facilitators assigned to each breakout room
- Written and verbal instructions distributed within 1 week before and on assessment day
#### Constraints
- Administered virtually due to the COVID-19 pandemic; the authors view the virtual format as enhancing accessibility rather than as a limitation

### Target Learners
- medical and dental students in Medical Spanish courses

### Target Learning Goals
- conducting a medical interview in Spanish with a standardized patient
- receiving formative feedback to improve Medical Spanish communication skills
