# Deliver a mental imagery session combining relaxation, teacher-guided mentalization of each protocol step, and independent rehearsal within time matched to conventional review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's MI intervention is a concrete implementable recipe: a 5-minute relaxation session, 10 minutes in which the teacher mentalizes each of the 7 steps of the procedure with participants, then 30 minutes of individual mental rehearsal of the whole procedure, with the paper protocol available for consultation. The article states "The 'MI' group (a) benefited initially from a 5-min relaxation session including relax seating, body relaxing, deep breathing in a calm environment." Total time (45 min) was identical to the control group's review time, isolating the imagery component.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An explicit protocol of the surgical procedure (here 7 steps) presented beforehand via presentation, commented video demonstration, and paper document
#### Constraints
- Protocol timing and structure come from a single pilot study; the article notes the best way to implement MI in surgical skill courses remains debated in the literature

### Target Learners
- Novice surgeons learning a new operative technique

### Target Learning Goals
- Mental rehearsal of a multi-step technical surgical procedure prior to physical practice
