# Concrete strategies for building inclusion in hybrid cohorts: induction photos at lectern, leaving audio/camera on after class, extended on-campus breaks, and proactive communication about exam disruptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants proposed implementable practices to counter isolation in hybrid delivery: meeting the hybrid group during induction week and placing "a copy of their photos and names beside the desktop" so lecturers can match faces to names; lecturers leaving audio and camera on after class so online students can join peer chatter between classes; extending lunch or breaks on on-campus days for in-person interaction; and faculty proactively informing students when exam connectivity problems affect the whole group.
