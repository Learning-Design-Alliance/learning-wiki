# In future test-enhanced learning studies, give the control group an interim assessment using cross-over designs and add feedback with spaced repeat testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that future studies give control groups an interim assessment via cross-over designs to avoid disadvantaging controls, and study the addition of feedback and testing frequency. They state: "Further randomized controlled studies assessing the frequency of testing and the addition of feedback are needed to optimize the test-enhanced increase in student performance in a realistic educational setting."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-over design so all students receive the intervention; careful consideration of the nature, source and timing of feedback as suggested by Veloski et al.
#### Constraints
- If the effect is confirmed, control students are disadvantaged; a second assessment within less than 2 h showed no added value, so spacing and feedback require investigation

### Target Learners
- medical and biomedical science students in ongoing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- optimizing test-enhanced learning gains on formal examinations
