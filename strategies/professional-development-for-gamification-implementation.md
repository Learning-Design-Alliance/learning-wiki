# Provide professional development to equip educators with gamification implementation skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review states that "Professional development for educators is essential to equip them with the skills necessary for implementing gamified learning effectively", noting that many teachers lack experience or confidence with gamification techniques, which can impede successful integration. It also reports that many teachers find it challenging to use gamification in the classroom and struggle to incorporate elements such as feedback, points, and rewards into their teaching methods.
