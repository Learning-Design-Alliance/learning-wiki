# Author ITSs without programming by enabling non-programmers to create, curate, and sequence instruction through authoring tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends lowering the skills and time required to author ITSs by providing authoring tools that let non-programmers formalize and visualize their knowledge, a goal adapted from Murray's shell-tutor vision. GIFT operationalizes this through policies, tools, and methods enabling authors to create ITSs without knowledge of instructional design principles or software programming, curate content representing instructional domains, and sequence content by hierarchical or dependency relationships. Associated objectives include ease of use, rapid prototyping, collaborative authoring, and rapid integration of external environments to reduce development time and cost.
