# Target pedagogical change efforts at R1 institutions: train PhD students and postdocs bound for R1 faculty careers, reach professors who train graduate students at top R1 departments, and build collaboration channels between teaching-focused and research-focused institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The model yields three suggestions premised on the rationale that "R1 institutions represent a critical lynchpin for faculty pedagogical change". "First, pedagogical training initiatives should target PhD students and postdocs who are likely to become professors at R1 institutions. Second, initiatives should target professors who train graduate students at top R1 institutions." Third, more channels for collaboration and learning between teaching-focused and research-focused professors should be prioritized, since teaching-focused institutions are more susceptible to pedagogical change but cannot spread practices via PhD training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Focus limited time and resources on R1 institutions as the population-level leverage point
- Identify trainees who are likely to become professors at R1 institutions
#### Constraints
- The article notes existing programs like IRACDA and FIRST IV do not specifically target individuals likely to pursue R1 careers
- Change at this scale is a slow, multi-generational process

### Target Learners
- Graduate students and postdoctoral scholars likely to become tenure-track faculty
- Professors who train graduate students at top R1 institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Adoption of evidence-based instructional practices by the future professoriate
