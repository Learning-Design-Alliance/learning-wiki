# Novelty Sounds

## Description
Using novel sounds such as wind chimes or rain sticks captures young students’ attention. Puffing up cheeks and having students follow suit as if they are popping a marshmallow in their mouths can also quiet a class. Filling an empty Windex bottle with lavender mineral oil and labeling it "Quiet Spray,” or blowing magic “hush-bubbles” for a similar impact, can also be effective.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to novelty items such as wind chimes, rain sticks, empty spray bottles, lavender mineral oil, and bubble solution. Minimal preparation time is needed.
#### Constraints
- The novelty may wear off over time, requiring teachers to introduce new sounds or methods periodically. This technique may not be as effective with older students.
