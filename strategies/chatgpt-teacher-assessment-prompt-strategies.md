# Teachers can use ChatGPT to generate open-ended question prompts, quality rubrics, and formative assessment prompts aligned with learning goals

## Description
Drawing on Herft (2023), the article recommends specific teacher uses of ChatGPT: creating "prompts for open-ended questions that align with the learning goals and success criteria of the unit of instruction", generating "quality rubrics that clearly and concisely explain exactly what students need to accomplish", and creating "prompts for formative assessment activities that provide ongoing feedback to inform teaching and learning". These are implementable prompt-based recipes for assessment practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
