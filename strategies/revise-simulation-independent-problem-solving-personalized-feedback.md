# Revise maternity simulation to include independent problem-solving, real-life scenario variables, and personalized instructor feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend concrete revisions to simulation design based on the study findings: revising scenarios to "provide more opportunities for independent problem-solving skills" and to "incorporate more real-life factors and variables into the scenario". They further recommend skilled, encouraging simulation instructors who provide valuable guidance and feedback for more individualized teaching, and more opportunities for reflection and self-assessment to build self-confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skilled and encouraging simulation instructors who can provide participants with valuable guidance and feedback
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a single nursing school in the UAE with a small qualitative sample, which the authors state may limit applicability to other nursing programs

### Target Learners
- Male undergraduate baccalaureate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skills and decision-making in maternity and neonatal care
- Self-confidence and independent problem-solving
