# Incorporate telelactation standardized patient cases into primary care medical curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that structured virtual standardized patient encounters for clinical lactation be built into primary care training. They state that "Teaching clinical lactation through telelactation cases can be used to teach medical trainees and should be incorporated into primary care medical curricula," noting telesimulation reduces costs such as supply costs and clinical station rental compared with in-person simulation. The trial showed both delivery formats produced positive sustained self-efficacy change.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured virtual telemedicine standardized patient encounters with trained standardized patients
- Asynchronous pre-work to prepare learners for the videoconference-based session
#### Constraints
- The evidence base is a pilot trial with high dropout conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic, which the authors state could limit generalizability to other sites or learners

### Target Learners
- medical trainees
- primary care residents

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical lactation and breastfeeding medicine skills
