# Integrate 20-minute physical activity breaks into twice-weekly 60-minute science lessons alongside nutritional education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe is to run "2 weekly lessons of sciences of the duration of 60 min in which a classroom-based physical activity lasting 20 min was integrated", with the science content being nutritional education. A typical active classroom session combined active breaks between and within learning activities, learning activities involving movement, working at benches, standing desks, on the floor or in combination, and learning outdoors. Sessions were supervised by certified physical education teachers and evaluated before and after the 12-week program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified physical education teachers to outline, supervise and carry out the evaluations and programs
- Students able to complete a moderate-to-vigorous aerobic exercise session
#### Constraints
- The study ran in a single public high school in southern Italy, so the authors caution findings may not extend to pupils at other schools or with different backgrounds

### Target Learners
- high-school students aged 14–15

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- study skills
- physical health and weight status
