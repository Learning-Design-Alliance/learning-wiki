# Prioritize discussion of the theory behind quantum hardware in QISE theory courses to prepare students for entry-level industry positions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The report recommends that undergraduate programs "emphasize the theoretical foundations of quantum hardware within QISE courses to better prepare students for entry-level positions in the quantum industry." It argues that many introductory QISE courses currently focus solely on abstract theoretical concepts with limited time on the theory underlying experiments and devices, so shifting course focus to include discussions of the theory behind hardware components would better align QISE education with industry needs.
