# Use scheduled interactive on-line courses as a scalable strategy for building health research capacity in resource-limited settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends on-line distance learning as an efficient, cost-effective and scalable alternative to on-site training for health research methodology in resource-limited settings. It emphasizes scheduled group e-learning sessions, in which "the participants could log-in and discuss the course material with other participants and facilitators", a format it notes has previously performed better than on-demand formats. The recommendation rests on the trial's finding of similar 3-month knowledge gains across formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Broadband internet access and basic computer skills, which the study verified for participants before the courses
#### Constraints
- The study could not accurately assess per-person costs for each format; higher satisfaction on instructor-interaction items suggests faculty-trainee interaction needs strengthening on-line

### Target Learners
- Indian biomedical scientists and clinicians with graduate or postgraduate degrees and research experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Biostatistics knowledge and application
- Research ethics knowledge and ethical analysis skills
