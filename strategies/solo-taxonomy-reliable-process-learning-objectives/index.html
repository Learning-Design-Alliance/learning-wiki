Use cognitive taxonomies such as SOLO as reliable processes for constructing learning objectives that are epistemic aims¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends cognitive taxonomies as reliable processes teachers can use to frame the depth and structure of knowledge targeted in learning objectives. It explains: "The SOLO taxonomy classifies students’ learning into stages from simple (unistructural : one idea/concept; multistructural : several independent ideas/concepts) to more complex ( relational : several integrated ideas/concepts; extended abstract : generalized ideas/concepts that go beyond material presented) levels of understanding." Dr. Jones uses it to move students from multistructural to relational objectives across a unit.
