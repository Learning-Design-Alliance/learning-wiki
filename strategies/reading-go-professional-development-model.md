# Sustained professional development model combining training days, site visits, observations, and ongoing coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's professional development model provides school staff with 10 days of professional development given over two years, plus 6-8 site visits offering tailored professional development, tutor observations, and coaching. It also includes ongoing data analysis and progress monitoring, classwide assessments for student selection, and provided materials such as training manuals and leveled book sets. The page states that the combination of training plus daily program implementation "significantly enhances pedagogical knowledge and skills."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must nominate at least three staff members, one of whom serves as program coordinator
- A two-year minimum partnership commitment
#### Constraints
- Training is spread over two years rather than delivered as a single block

### Target Learners
- school staff members: teachers and paraprofessionals serving beginning readers

### Target Learning Goals
- pedagogical knowledge and skills for accelerating literacy
- delivery of structured one-on-one tutoring
