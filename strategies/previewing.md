# Previewing

## Description
Prepare learners for upcoming tasks or materials. Previewing is one of the simplest and most flexible strategies. This can be used for all students, especially those with trouble staying organized, impulse control problems, or difficulties paying attention. Tailor what you preview and when according to the students’ needs - then follow through.
