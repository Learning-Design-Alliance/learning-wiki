# Incorporate remote cloud-accessible quantum experiments to extend experimental-skills opportunities to students at less-resourced institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes remote, cutting-edge quantum experiments as one option "to teach relevant experimental skills to a wide set of students," since hands-on quantum experiences are resource-intensive and unavailable at many institutions. Oqtant lets students control an ultracold-atom apparatus, receive and analyze real data, and practice skills like data analysis and parameter optimization without local hardware. The strategy targets broadening participation in quantum information science and engineering education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A reliable internet connection during class sessions and support materials such as lab guides and instructor background resources
#### Constraints
- The remote platform cannot provide hands-on skills such as aligning lasers, and some instructors would use remote experiments only where in-person labs are not an option

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students at institutions without physical quantum experiments

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental skills relevant to the quantum industry
- engagement with authentic quantum experiments
