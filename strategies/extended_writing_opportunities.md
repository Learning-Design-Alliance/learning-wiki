# Extended Writing Opportunities

## Description
Extended writing opportunities involve providing learners with regular practice in writing to enhance their skills. This can be implemented through daily writing exercises focusing on specific skills such as sentence fluency, or by integrating chat features in digital products that allow for written communication with authentic audiences in real time or asynchronously. Such activities aim to improve processing speed, genre knowledge, and provide primary language support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time for writing practice, access to digital tools with chat features (for digital implementation), clear prompts and skill-focused tasks.
#### Constraints
- May require significant time allocation, learners may need scaffolding to engage effectively in extended writing tasks, some learners may find open-ended writing tasks challenging.
