# Support GLBCC adoption with professional development, authentic-problem curricula, and higher-order assessment

## Description
The article's forward-looking recommendations for putting the GLBCC model into practice at scale. The Conclusion states the implications "extend to educational practice through the need for comprehensive professional development programs, curriculum reforms that incorporate authentic problem-solving contexts, and assessment strategies that evaluate higher-order thinking skills". It also calls for attention to systematic cognitive conflict, structured generative activities, and instructional scaffolding, and proposes future research on other physics topics and 21st-century variables.
