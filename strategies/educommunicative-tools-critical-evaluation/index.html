Use educommunicative tools — the Critical Media Literacy Framework, the dialogic–critical method, and critical pedagogies — to build students' critical evaluation of information¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends specific educommunicative tools for the classroom: the Critical Media Literacy Framework (Kellner and Share, 2019), the dialogic–critical method in educommunication (Bermejo Berros, 2021), and critical pedagogies. These tools "allow students to identify, question, and evaluate the veracity of information and understand the context in which it is produced," incorporating dialog as an educommunicative tool. Practices should prompt students to reflect on who is behind news, its intentions, ideology, sources, and how to compare it, addressing clickbait and incidentally received information.

  • Experts note these approaches require moving beyond a purely technological focus in teacher training; the article reports current teacher training shortcomings hinder such integration
