# Six-session teaching sequence for translation technology education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A configurable module for a translation technology course spanning six sessions of approximately two hours each. Sessions progress from establishing and recovering a workflow, through comparing evidence for decisions, investigating a quality rule, mapping data and choosing an AI configuration, proposing a bounded improvement, to demonstrating transfer and defending decisions. A core route requires no AI subscription while an optional route introduces provider comparison.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor should reduce text length and technical depth where students have limited CAT experience
- Both routes should carry equivalent credit
#### Constraints
- This is a planning assumption, not an observed teaching duration
- The sequence has not been validated through classroom study

### Target Learners
- translation technology students, including undergraduates with limited CAT experience

### Target Learning Goals
- workflow competence
- evaluative judgement
- technological agency
- transfer across CAT environments
