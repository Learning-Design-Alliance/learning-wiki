# Embed the Long COVID core competencies in training and professional development to standardize practices and improve patient outcomes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend implementing the 13 consensus-based competencies in curricula and professional development for primary care clinicians, arguing that alignment with ACGME domains facilitates integration into existing education structures. They state that "Implementing these competencies in training and professional development can enhance care quality, standardize practices, and improve outcomes for patients facing complex, multisystem health challenges." Training should also emphasize flexibility, collaborative learning with patients and caregivers, and relational care given the evolving science.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curricula must remain competency-based and outcomes-focused, and incorporate stakeholder perspectives including lived experience
#### Constraints
- The authors note the competencies' transferability to contexts with varying levels of specialty access and institutional resources has not yet been evaluated

### Target Learners
- Primary care clinicians (PCPs)
- Medical educators developing continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- Competency-based Long COVID care in primary care settings
