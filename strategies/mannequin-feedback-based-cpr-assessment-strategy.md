# Assess CPR competence with mannequin performance trials using real-time feedback devices, before and after training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 2 review), `q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's method combines a theoretical questionnaire with practical mannequin trials: candidates perform 2 minutes of CPR without feedback, then with feedback to check compression depth and frequency, and again after training with feedback visible only to the instructor. A smartphone application connected to the mannequins via Bluetooth provided "visual feedback of resuscitation maneuvers at both training times," enabling objective measurement of skill rather than self-report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mannequins connectable to feedback software (a smartphone application via Bluetooth) capable of measuring compression depth and frequency
#### Constraints
- Post-course testing occurred immediately after training; the design does not establish long-term retention

### Target Learners
- newly licensed physicians

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate chest compression depth and frequency
- effective CPR performance over 2 minutes
