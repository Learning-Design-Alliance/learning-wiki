Use Curriculum Associates professional learning: introductory course, on-site development days, and free OEL digital courses¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Curriculum Associates' professional learning for Magnetic Reading is centered on developing educators' capacity, across ranges of experience, to implement key instructional moves. It includes a course introducing the program's fundamentals and key practices, tailored support offerings on specific instructional moves, and on-site development days of six hours. For continued training, educators can use "free professional development through its OEL platform" with digital courses on product features and implementation best practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • On-site development days are six hours, with the annual number of sessions varying depending on implementation length

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 3-5 educators implementing Magnetic Reading

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Implementing key instructional moves of the Magnetic Reading program

Related Strategies¶

  • Tiered professional learning for Magnetic Reading Foundations implementation
  • Implement Magnetic Reading with designated points of contact supported by i-Ready Partners, with no additional staff required
  • Layered professional development for curriculum implementation (webinars, workshops, coaching)
