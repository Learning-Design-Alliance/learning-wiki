# Combine diverse assessment categories (practical structure identification, oral tests, open-ended questions) rather than relying on MCQs alone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends diversifying anatomy assessment by combining test categories: it states "This approach consists of combining various categories of tests to evaluate students, such as timed practical tests to identify structures, oral tests for understanding concepts, and open-ended questions to develop students' critical reasoning." The review reports this combination provides a more comprehensive evaluation and influences comprehension, long-term memorization and critical thinking. It also cautions that timed practical tests can underestimate students' real abilities due to time limits and stress.
