# Prepare industry-tailored resumes that translate HEPA skills into generic competencies and avoid physics jargon

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors advise early career scientists to convert a CV into a resume suitable for each target industry such as finance, defense, tech, or software, considering roles titled Engineer, Data Scientist, Algorithm Developer, Quantitative Analyst, or Research Scientist. They write that "focusing on skills that generalize and core competencies that are relevant to the job search are of far more use" and that physics jargon tied to previous research should be strictly avoided, emphasising instead publications via link, teamwork, and generic descriptions of distinctions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful attention to studying the company profile, past hires, culture of embracing STEM graduates and PhDs, and chances of career advancement
#### Constraints
- Very few industry job requisitions come with a PhD Physicist requirement in the title

### Target Learners
- HEPA PhD students and postdocs seeking industry jobs

### Target Learning Goals
- Job-search communication skills
- Resume preparation
