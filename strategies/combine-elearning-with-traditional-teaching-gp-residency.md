# Combine E-learning with traditional teaching methods so each benefits from the other

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating E-learning technologies into traditional GP residency teaching: clinical teachers give residents guidance and structure by systematically describing dermatological conditions, while E-learning provides instant stimulation via authentic clinical cases from practice. The authors conclude that "E-learning programmes and traditional teaching methods should be combined to be of benefit for each other", with residents valuing dialogue and discussion in traditional sessions and the uniform, always-available E-learning format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical teachers providing guidance and structure; E-learning providing authentic clinical cases and instant stimulation
#### Constraints
- Further research is required to evaluate implementation of E-learning programmes in parallel to regular teaching programs, per the article

### Target Learners
- first-year GP residents in postgraduate general practice training

### Target Learning Goals
- linking dermatological theory to clinical practice; acquiring and retaining dermatological knowledge
