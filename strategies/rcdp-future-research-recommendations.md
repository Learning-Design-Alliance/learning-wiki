# Future RCDP research should assess long-term effects, dose-response across sessions, and standardized protocols with larger randomized samples

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend: repeated studies with long-term assessment to determine sustainability of RCDP effects; studies analyzing effects by number of training sessions and difficulty levels with standardized content and assessment; applying RCDP to protocols beyond adult resuscitation validated with randomized controlled groups and larger samples; and exploring effectiveness in diverse populations across countries. They state future RCTs "could address these limitations and strengthen the causal inferences".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Randomized controlled designs with larger sample sizes and standardized content, difficulty, and assessment methods
#### Constraints
- Current findings come from fourth-year nursing students in South Korea and may not generalize to other educational or clinical settings

### Target Learners
- Nursing students and healthcare professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- Resuscitation and standardized-procedure skill acquisition and retention
