# Story retelling with familiar picture books to practice sequencing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Students put a story in the correct order using an enclosed worksheet, with suggested books including The 3 Pigs, The 3 Bears, If You Give a Mouse a Cookie, The Napping House, and There Was an Old Woman who Swallowed a Fly. A Going on a Bear Hunt YouTube retelling is also offered, connecting narrative comprehension to algorithmic sequencing.
