# Consider AI-focused training and define AI literacy for both physics professors and students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the discussion, the authors recommend that the physics community consider “what kinds of AI-focused training could be most beneficial, both to professors and students, as well as what AI literacy might look like within the domain of physics”. They argue such guidance would help the community thoughtfully and critically adapt AI to its needs, positioning the study’s framings and practices as a first step in that direction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guidance should help the physics community to thoughtfully and critically adapt AI to its needs.
#### Constraints
- The underlying study is a single-institution qualitative case study whose results the authors view as more of an existence-proof than a generalizable framework at this time.

### Target Learners
- physics professors (university faculty)
- physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- AI literacy and critical, thoughtful use of generative AI in physics
