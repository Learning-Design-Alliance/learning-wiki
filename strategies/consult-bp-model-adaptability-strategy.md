# Adapt the CONSULT-BP bias-mitigation training model to other clinical domains where bias affects decisions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend extending the CONSULT-BP model beyond hypertension, noting it is readily adaptable to other diseases such as COVID-19, in which testing decisions are emerging as subject to bias. They position the protocol as a roadmap for others designing bias education and training models for healthcare professionals, and note the CDC recommends bias awareness and mitigation skills to address the COVID-19 public health crisis.
