## Description
The article argues that universities could benefit from 'framing EdTech support in terms of embedding representations to first build, then guide, technical and pedagogical knowledge and skill.' Guiding principles may then motivate faculty to acquire and assemble competencies in context-sensitive ways for instructional decision making.
