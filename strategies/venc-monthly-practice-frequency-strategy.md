# Encourage at least weekly virtual simulation practice until competence, then at least monthly for retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health professionals use the virtual simulations frequently after initial training: "Health professionals should be encouraged to use it at least weekly using the automated reminders built into the app to support deliberate practice until competence has been achieved, and monthly thereafter." Monthly use in this study corresponded to approximately 3 scenarios per month, 200 within-scenario actions, and 40 minutes in the app, based on median logged use of participants reporting monthly use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the vENC app on a mobile device; automated reminders built into the app
#### Constraints
- The recommended frequency of virtual refresher training had not been previously established; the association is observational and case-scenario score correlations with use were not significant

### Target Learners
- nurses and midwives caring for newborns in low-resource settings

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of essential newborn care and neonatal resuscitation knowledge and skills
