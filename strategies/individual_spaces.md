# Individual Spaces

## Description
Creating designated areas within the classroom to serve specific functions, such as independent work, collaborative projects, or mini-lessons, can promote student engagement. By strategically arranging seating to match the learning activity, instructors can maximize student focus and participation. Different seating configurations support different types of instruction. For instance, independent work areas encourage focus, collaborative setups promote communication, and large-group arrangements facilitate discussions.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- Classroom space, adaptable furniture (desks, tables, chairs), planning for space allocation, clear communication with students
