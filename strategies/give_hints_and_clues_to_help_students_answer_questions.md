# Give Hints and Clues to Help Students Answer Questions

## Description
Teachers should do more delving and rephrasing for learners for whom they have high expectations. When a learner is struggling, provide appropriate hints and clues. Give sufficient clues to low-performing learners to build their self-esteem and encourage them to do better in the future. Be careful to not give so many hints that you are basically giving the learner the answer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must be able to provide the right scaffolding to support learners.
#### Constraints
- Teachers may give too many hints, which makes it obvious to the learners that they are struggling.
