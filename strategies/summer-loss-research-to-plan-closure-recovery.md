# Use summer learning loss research to help educators, policy makers, and families plan for and address impacts of extended school closures

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends drawing on the established research base on summer learning loss as an analog for understanding the consequences of the COVID-19 pause in schooling. It states that this research "can offer insights to help educators, policy makers, and families understand, plan for, and address" the potential impacts of the extended pause when students return to school. The strategy is therefore to treat seasonal learning patterns as an evidence-based planning tool for post-closure recovery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to research on summer learning loss and seasonal learning patterns to translate findings into planning
#### Constraints
- The brief frames this as addressing potential impacts, not measured COVID-specific effects

### Target Learners
- K-12 students returning to school after COVID-19 closures

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement in mathematics and reading following an extended interruption in schooling
