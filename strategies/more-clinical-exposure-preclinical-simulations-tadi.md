# Incorporate more clinical exposure and preclinical simulations to improve dental students' TDI management knowledge and confidence.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that dental curricula include more undergraduate and postgraduate clinical exposure and preclinical simulations to address the gap between average knowledge and low confidence in TDI management. They specifically suggest "exploring creative learning modes such as virtual reality, simulation clinics and other clinical components can help to advance knowledge and confidence levels in TDI management." The study's finding that 83.9% of students reported low confidence despite average knowledge supports this recommendation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dental curricula must include dedicated TDI modules with preclinical simulation components
#### Constraints
