# Embed inductive vocabulary expansion in the regular reading program rather than replacing it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools accelerate vocabulary growth by enhancing their existing reading programs rather than adding separate instruction. It states it is "feasible for schools to accelerate student vocabulary growth in an inductive fashion that also improves student reading comprehension", and that the study is "suggestive of how student vocabulary acquisition and reading proficiency can be accelerated through the enhancement of their regular reading programs".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers use the same district-adopted basal reading series and apply the model to a committed set of stories during the school year
#### Constraints
- Effects were demonstrated over an 18-week, single-school-year implementation in grades 3-5; multi-year cumulative effects remain untested

### Target Learners
- Elementary students in grades 3-5, including low-SES and minority students

### Target Learning Goals
- Accelerated vocabulary acquisition
- Reading comprehension proficiency
