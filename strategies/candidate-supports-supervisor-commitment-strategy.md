## Description
The article identifies candidate areas for future policy and leadership research rather than demonstrated interventions: psychological-capital-focused activities such as resilience training, coaching, goal-setting workshops, and leadership development; empowerment-supportive practices including meaningful participation in decision-making and recognition; and professional-development investments, mentoring systems, and workload adjustments. The authors state these "warrant testing in longitudinal or intervention studies" rather than claiming effectiveness.
