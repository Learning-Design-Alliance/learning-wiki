# Involve students in the full publishing process, including journal selection, copyright and plagiarism discussion, contribution definition, and responding to referee feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The final course step was getting the document into publishable form, which the author calls an exciting moment since very few students had published before. Students weighed journal quality, audience and ethics, preferring open-access or non-profit journals, and targeted the review at beginning researchers. The process yielded educational side-benefits: discussion of "copyright and plagiarism as well as defining each individual's contribution", familiarization with publisher guidelines, and feedback from referees and editors that students had to address in the resubmitted version.
