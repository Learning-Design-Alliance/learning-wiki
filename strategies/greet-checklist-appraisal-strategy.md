# Use the GREET checklist with its E&E paper to appraise the completeness of reporting in published EBP educational intervention studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The validated use case for the GREET checklist is appraising published studies: readers indicate whether and where each checklist item is reported, using response options of fully reported, partially reported, not reported or not clear, with the E&E paper available for clarification. In the validation study, participants completed this appraisal in supervised small-group sessions, taking a mean of 77 minutes (SD 23). The checklist can also structure interpretation of relevance and identification of possible biases for educators applying research to teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the GREET checklist and E&E paper
- Familiarity with the E&E paper's explanations and verbatim examples for each item
#### Constraints
- Validation tested appraisal of reporting rather than use of the checklist to guide writing a paper; tested with non-expert users from one tertiary institution, with no medical or nursing students

### Target Learners
- Tertiary health professional students
- Researchers and educators appraising EBP educational intervention literature

### Target Learning Goals
- Appraising completeness of reporting of EBP educational interventions
