# Badges

## Description
Badges are a digital way to acknowledge learner work. Learners can receive a badge if they achieve certain levels of success on assignments, or if they do additional work, such as submitting a draft or sharing notes with another learner. Badges may be displayed to other learners in the class as a means to encourage competition or to demonstrate the variety of badges which can be earned.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Use of badge-making tools (e.g., Credly, OpenBadges). Clear criteria for earning badges. Integration with learning management systems or digital platforms.
#### Constraints
- May lead to a focus on earning badges rather than learning. Requires careful design to ensure badges represent meaningful achievements. May not be motivating for all learners.

### Affordances
- Provides visual recognition of accomplishments. Encourages learners to strive for specific achievements. Fosters a sense of accomplishment and pride.

### Personalization
- Badges can be tailored to individual learning goals and achievements. Learners can choose which badges to display to showcase their accomplishments.
