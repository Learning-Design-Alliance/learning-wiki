# Provide a video-based hardware demonstration for students to watch before a VR session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
After the pilot showed lower ease-of-use ratings, the team developed a video-based demonstration of the VR hardware for students to watch at their own leisure prior to a VR session, replacing or supplementing the in-person hardware demonstration. The authors state: "We have thus developed a video-based demonstration for students to watch at their own leisure prior to a VR session." This addresses the finding that young students cannot be assumed to navigate VR hardware confidently in a medical training setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video content demonstrating the VR hardware, available to participants before the session
#### Constraints
- This approach is currently being evaluated in a new VR project; its effect on ease-of-use ratings is not yet demonstrated

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical and nursing students new to VR hardware

### Target Learning Goals
- operating VR simulation hardware confidently before clinical scenario practice
