Just-in-time training to minimise skill decay before clinical performance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Just-in-time training delivers refresher training immediately before a clinical procedure. The review reports it was "feasible and clinically valuable" and suggests it "could be a feasible strategy to minimise skill decay before clinical performance".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery of refresher training immediately before the clinical procedure is needed

Constraints¶

  • Only a few of the included studies examined just-in-time training
