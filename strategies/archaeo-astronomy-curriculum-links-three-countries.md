# Link archaeo-astronomy to science curricula through four common key areas: astronomy, geology, biology and chemistry

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends implementing archaeo-astronomy by targeting the common key areas it identified across English, German and Portuguese curricula. It lists them as: "Astronomy: Light and shadow and seasonal changes of the path of the Sun; Geology: Stratigraphy; Biology: Archaeo-botany; Chemistry: Effects of industrial and technological development". In England these map to KS1-KS4 programme statements; in Germany to Sachkunde and Sekundarstufe I topics; in Portugal to the four science themes and the non-disciplinary Project Area.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of the national or state curriculum, since Germany's 16 states each have autonomy and 160 different schoolbooks are in use.
#### Constraints
- In Germany it is difficult to give a detailed list of curriculum links because teachers need not strictly follow topic contents; in England schools are not required to teach citizenship as a subject.

### Target Learners
- students from primary (KS1/Grundschule/1st Cycle) through secondary (KS4/Sekundarstufe I/3rd Cycle)

### Target Learning Goals
- Earth and its place in the Universe
- stratigraphy and geology
- archaeobotany and ecology
- effects of human activity on the environment
