# Body Scan Meditation Check-In

## Description
Students participate in a guided body scan meditation to bring calmness and awareness into the classroom. This involves focusing attention on different parts of the body to notice sensations and promote relaxation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quiet space, a guided meditation script or recording, and time for the meditation (5-10 minutes).
#### Constraints
- Some students may struggle to focus during the meditation. The effectiveness of the activity depends on creating a calm and supportive classroom environment.

### Affordances
- Provides a structured way to practice mindfulness and relaxation. Can be easily integrated into the classroom routine. Promotes self-awareness and emotional regulation.

### Personalization
- The length and complexity of the meditation can be adjusted based on the age and experience of the students. Can be adapted to focus on specific areas of the body or specific emotional states.
