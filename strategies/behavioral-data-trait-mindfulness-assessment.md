# Use behavioral data alongside self-report measures when assessing trait mindfulness in adolescents

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that future studies assessing trait mindfulness should consider the use of behavioral data to understand underlying elements not captured by self-report questionnaires. It notes that individuals scoring high state mindfulness may not necessarily disclose high trait mindfulness, which might be linked to distinct interpretations of questionnaires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combining self-report with behavioral data
#### Constraints
- Self-report nature of instruments based on dispositional models may have implications, in addition to social desirability

### Target Learners
- Adolescents in secondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate assessment of trait mindfulness
