# Nighttime Blue Light Reduction

## Description
Reducing exposure to blue light from digital screens before bed can improve sleep quality and quantity, which enhances learning by preventing disruptions to sleep patterns and storage processes. Strategies include limiting screen time before bed. American teens spend an average of nine hours per day using digital media, leading to concerns about sleep disruptions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of screen time habits and their impact on sleep. Commitment to reducing screen time before bed.
#### Constraints
- Potential difficulty in reducing screen time due to dependence or necessity. May require alternative activities before bed.
