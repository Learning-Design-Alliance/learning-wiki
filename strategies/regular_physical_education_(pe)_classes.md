# Regular Physical Education (PE) Classes

## Description
Implement consistent PE classes to help students burn off energy and be active, improving their ability to focus in class. Guidelines recommend 150 minutes of PE weekly for elementary school students and 225 minutes for middle and high school students. Provide multiple activity choices and cycle through different sports and activities to encourage participation and exploration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduled class time, trained PE teachers, appropriate facilities and equipment
#### Constraints
- Requires dedicated resources and facilities. May not appeal to all students if activities are not varied and engaging.
