# Promoting a Growth Mindset

## Description
Promoting a growth mindset, particularly in mathematics, encourages learners to believe that intelligence is not fixed. Feedback that values effort and encourages persistence, rather than focusing solely on innate ability, can show students that there is always room for them to improve their skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A focus on the learning process rather than just the end result.
#### Constraints
- If praise is not genuine or specific, it may be ineffective.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->

### Affordances
- Reinforces the value of effort and perseverance. Encourages learners to embrace challenges and see mistakes as opportunities for growth. Promotes a positive self-image and reduces fear of failure.

### Personalization
- Feedback can be tailored to individual learner efforts.
