# Use Kotter's 8-step and COM-B models within iterative action-research cycles to co-design EPA implementation improvements with expert and stakeholder panels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article used Kotter's 8-Step Change Management Model and the COM-B model to design implementation strategies, with COM-B providing "a lens to analyse whether barriers reflected limitations in capability, opportunity or motivation". Six five-week action-research cycles involved enacting plans, gathering user data, analysing data, and co-designing improvements with an expert panel and a stakeholder panel. This approach enabled multiple revisions to be executed across the pilot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated implementation team resources, including an EPA consultant and fellow within a medical education unit
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in one Australian metropolitan health service in the first implementation year before EPAs were mandatory and without an e-portfolio

### Target Learners
- Prevocational medical trainees (interns, PGY1)

### Target Learning Goals
- Entrustable professional activity completion and workplace-based assessment participation
