# Use educational discussion and debate to form ecological and moral convictions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article treats debate and educational discussion as methods creating a situation of cognitive dispute, arguing that knowledge obtained in the collision of opinions has "a higher measure of generality, durability and flexibility". It states that "The use of the discussion at the lesson is particularly effective for the formation of ecological and moral convictions, because it promotes an intensive mental activity, develops skills of critical judgment and reflection". Debates on themes such as "Technical progress and ecology" were also run in extracurricular work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Topics must be current, socially significant and connected with the problems of youth

- senior secondary pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- critical judgment
- argumentation in a foreign language
- ecological and moral convictions
