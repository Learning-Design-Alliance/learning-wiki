# Design TTXs with assessment in mind so that log data capture the learning process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that TTX creators consider assessment goals during the design phase, because a thorough design of learning objectives and activities is crucial for automated assessment to work; otherwise the log data may be less accurate in capturing the learning process. Building milestone structure and activity alignment into the exercise from the start enables higher-quality analytics in the post-TTX phase, supporting timely cluster-differentiated feedback.
