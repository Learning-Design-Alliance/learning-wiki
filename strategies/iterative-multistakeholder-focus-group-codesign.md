# Co-design pedagogical frameworks through iterative multi-stakeholder focus groups with member checking and expert review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a three-phase participatory process: focus group 1 collected stakeholders' wishes, focus group 2 served as a member check confirming the principles, and focus group 3 invited experts to review the framework. The authors note that "Focus group 2 served, in turn, as a type of member check, that is, informant feedback or respondent validation". Literature grounding was added between rounds, and expert feedback was incorporated to increase ecological validity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants representing the main stakeholder groups; sessions in local languages; recorded sessions with written consent
#### Constraints
- Expert review required English-proficient experts and international video conferences

### Target Learners
- students, teachers, school directors, parents, university students and STEM professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- learning environment design principles relevant to key stakeholders
