# Add a case observation with feedback component to supportive supervision rather than relying entirely on register review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends redesigning supportive supervision so that it strengthens clinical skills rather than only record-keeping. Because supervision in the study focused on register review, the authors suggest that "supportive supervision may be more effective if it includes a component of case observation with feedback, rather than relying entirely on register review," while noting the feasibility of this in settings with few consultations needs assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors would need to observe HEWs conducting sick child consultations and provide feedback, requiring sufficient consultation volume
#### Constraints
- The article states the feasibility of case observation with feedback in a setting of small numbers of consultations needs to be assessed

### Target Learners
- Health Extension Workers at rural health posts

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving assessment and classification skills in iCCM case management
