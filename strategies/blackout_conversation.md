# Blackout Conversation

## Description
Students transform conversational text selections into poetry by blacking out sections to reveal only key terms and phrases, focusing on the meaning, nuances, and silences of each character's dialogue. Working in pairs, students read aloud the text, then independently examine their character's subtext—what they're really saying, hiding, feeling, expressing, or withholding. After blacking out the 'background noise,' they reread the remaining words and phrases with their partner, highlighting the essence of the conversation. This exercise aims to foster social awareness, empathy, and relationship skills by encouraging active listening, reflection, and deeper understanding of others' experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Text selections featuring dominant character dialogue. Blackout markers or pens. Facilitation of paired and independent reading activities.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of texts and sensitivity in interpreting character emotions. Success depends on students' willingness to engage deeply with the text and their partners.
