# Use a simple, non-photorealistic IVR model for layout-knowledge learning experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educational IVR experiences for layout knowledge do not require photorealistic detail: "for the domain of layout knowledge learning, a remarkably simple IVR model is enough". In the study, the SketchUp model omitted most surface textures and details like ceiling beams, hanging lamps and sculptures, yet the IVR group achieved the highest delayed recall. The authors note further studies are needed on the effect of model detail level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An IVR environment with a walkthrough path, teleport points, and stereoscopic HMD display, as used in the study
#### Constraints
- The finding applies to layout knowledge learning in a complex building; the article states further studies are needed to clarify the effect of the detail level of the three-dimensional model on layout learning

### Target Learners
- first-year architecture and design students

### Target Learning Goals
- spatial layout knowledge of complex buildings
