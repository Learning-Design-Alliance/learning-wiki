# Cultivate student advocates as an indicator and driver of scaling-up

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As an area of improvement, the article recommends growing the learner side of the community: "we find that having more students3 advocates to be an useful indicator of scaling–up as evident in projects like Scratch ([Resnick et al., 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1592761.1592779)) and GeoGebra (Hohenwarter & Fuchs, 2004) to be useful projects for which EJS can learn from, in terms of scaling–up." The strategy is to build advocate communities around the models, learning from how Scratch and GeoGebra scaled through their user communities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A community of users who can remix and re-release models and resources
#### Constraints
- Presented as an area for improvement rather than an achieved outcome of the project

### Target Learners
- Students as advocates and community members

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained community-driven creation and reuse of learning models
