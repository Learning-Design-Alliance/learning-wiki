# Let students discover telescope drift-scan imaging by switching tracking off

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As an unplanned enrichment of the lab, some students discover that switching the telescope tracking off lets the Moon drift naturally across the CCD while they capture images. The author reports this as an example of student excellence rather than an instructed technique, so the strategy is to leave room for such student-initiated variation during observing sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A telescope whose tracking can be switched off, and open observing time in which students may experiment.
#### Constraints
- Reported as occasional examples of student excellence, not a standard instructed procedure for all groups.

### Target Learners
- non-major undergraduate astronomy students

### Target Learning Goals
- experimental problem-solving during observations
