# Schedule intermediate student-instructor meetings halfway through extended computational projects

## Description
A scheduling strategy for extended project work: set up "meetings between students and the instructor together with a teaching assistant halfway through the duration of the computational project". The article gives three purposes: preliminary feedback that helps prevent students from getting lost in details, an opportunity to ask about subject matter, programming and expectations, and an intermediate deadline that encourages an early start.
