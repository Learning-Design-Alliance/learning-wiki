# Use RJ strategies such as proactive circles to manage classrooms and establish norms beyond discipline

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Experts described teachers managing classrooms using RJ strategies such as "proactive" circles to discuss subject matter. When teachers used these methods and encouraged students to share perspectives, it "helped establish norms for the classroom that were useful for dealing with issues beyond discipline." RJ can also be used to teach classroom content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers encourage students to share their perspectives during circles
#### Constraints
- Holding conferences or circles takes more teacher time initially than sending a youth to the office

### Target Learners
- K-12 classroom students and teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Classroom norm setting
- communication skills
- classroom content learning
