# Engage community constituents through multiple input methods as the first step in POG development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that engagement of community constituents be "a critical first step in developing a POG," with leaders actively seeking input on the desired knowledge, abilities, and attributes of graduates. States and districts have used surveys, focus groups, public feedback periods, community forums, listening tours, and digital platforms like ThoughtExchange to gather community feedback on what students should master upon graduation.
