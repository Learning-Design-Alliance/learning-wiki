# Barometer: Taking a Stand on Controversial Issues

## Description
In this activity, learners express opinions by standing along a continuum labeled with 'Strongly Agree' and 'Strongly Disagree.' After taking a stand, learners explain their positions, referring to evidence and examples. This strategy facilitates open discussions about controversial issues, encouraging perspective-taking and evidence-based reasoning. The activity concludes with a debrief, where learners reflect on how the discussion changed or reinforced their opinions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom space where learners can stand along a continuum, 'Strongly Agree' and 'Strongly Disagree' signs, reflective journals, and prompts that call for agreement or disagreement.
#### Constraints
- May lead to outbursts if not managed carefully, requires learners to be open-minded and respectful of others' opinions, and may not be suitable for highly sensitive topics.

### Affordances
- Provides a visual representation of opinions, encourages learners to take a stand and defend their positions, promotes listening and perspective-taking by hearing diverse viewpoints, fosters self-reflection on personal beliefs.

### Personalization
- The topic and continuum statements can be tailored to the curriculum or learner interests. Variations like the 'Forced Decision' and 'Post-It Notes Barometer' can accommodate different learning styles and group dynamics.
