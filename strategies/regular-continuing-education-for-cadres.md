# Provide regular continuing education programmes for health cadres, especially younger cadres, to maintain competency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that educational programmes be continued regularly for health cadres so competency is maintained for monitoring pregnant women's risks, with particular emphasis on younger cadres, since age was negatively associated with endline competency and intervention-group knowledge faded after two months. The recommendation rests on the trial's findings of fadeout and the age predictor rather than on a tested schedule of booster sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Periodic learning sessions to keep knowledge accurate and updated
#### Constraints
- The 3-month observation period could not establish long-term impact

### Target Learners
- Community health cadres, especially younger volunteers

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained competency in monitoring high-risk pregnant women
