# Personalize the learning environment and instructional process to foster belonging

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's fifth schoolwide recommendation creates a personalized learning environment that fosters belonging and a climate where students and teachers know one another and can offer academic, social, and behavioral encouragement. Suggested structures include small learning communities, team teaching, smaller classes, extended time through schedule changes, and extracurricular participation. The panel rated the evidence moderate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structural changes such as small learning communities, team teaching, or smaller classes
- Schedule changes creating extended classroom time
#### Constraints
- Schoolwide reforms may take years to produce measurable improvements, even if implemented effectively

### Target Learners
- All students in middle and high schools, especially schools with high dropout rates

### Target Learning Goals
- Sense of belonging, reduced anonymity, and increased school engagement
