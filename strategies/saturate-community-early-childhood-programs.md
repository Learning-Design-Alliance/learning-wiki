# Saturate a community with early childhood programs so kindergartners enter classrooms surrounded by ready peers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes North Carolina's implementation approach: it "attempted to saturate a community with its early childhood programs so that a child would enter the kindergarten classroom surrounded by peers who are ready to learn and a teacher who would not have to remediate others." The strategy works through high program penetration, so that classroom peer environments reinforce and embellish early gains rather than requiring remediation of unprepared classmates.
