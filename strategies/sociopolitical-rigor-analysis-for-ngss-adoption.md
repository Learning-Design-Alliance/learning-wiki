# Use the sociopolitical rigor framework when adopting standards such as NGSS to identify where rigor could be increased

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that educators apply its rigor framework when new standards are adopted. It states this analysis "can be an insightful process as states adopt the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)", and that "By considering the sociopolitical backing for the educational standards many US science teachers are required to use, one can gain an understanding of the areas in which rigor could be increased within one's own context." This supports purposeful planning and delivery of instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must have access to the standards' sociopolitical backing and the four-quadrant framework
#### Constraints
- The framework does not analyze instruction or assessment rigor, so application is limited to curriculum-level analysis
