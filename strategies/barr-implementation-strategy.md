# Implement BARR with a dedicated coordinator, weekly teacher-team meetings, multi-year professional development, and intensive coaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The page specifies implementation requirements: a school selects a BARR coordinator who "will manage, coordinate, and integrate the BARR model", and "Teams of teachers need to meet weekly using the BARR process." Schools receive two days of in-person professional development annually within a sequenced multi-year plan, plus a dedicated coach providing structured weekly coaching sessions, quarterly on-site visits, and an average of more than 200 hours per year of support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A staff member selected as school coordinator for the BARR model
- Weekly teacher-team meetings using the BARR process
- Two days of in-person professional development annually through a sequenced, multi-year training plan
- A dedicated BARR coach with structured weekly coaching sessions and quarterly on-site visits
#### Constraints
- No specific technology required

### Target Learners
- K–12 students, with evaluated implementations in grades 7–12

### Target Learning Goals
- Math achievement and broader student outcomes supported by relationship-building and data use
