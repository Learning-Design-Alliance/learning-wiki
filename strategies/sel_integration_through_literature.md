# SEL Integration through Literature

## Description
Integrating social and emotional learning (SEL) into language arts curricula involves using literature to explore emotions, build community, handle conflict, and counter bias. Books with strong characters solving problems can help students connect to stories and learn problem-solving strategies. Discussions prompted by the literature enable students to access their own backgrounds, emotions, and relationships. It also emphasizes distinguishing conflict from violence and prompts reflection on character feelings and conflict resolution. Furthermore, integrating SEL lessons into reading curriculum can connect learners to the universality of literature, its power to transport them to different experiences, and connect with others.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to appropriate literature, structured lesson plans, and facilitated discussion.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of literature to ensure relevance and appropriateness. Success depends on the instructor's ability to facilitate meaningful discussions and create a safe space for sharing.
