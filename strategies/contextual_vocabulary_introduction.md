# Contextual Vocabulary Introduction

## Description
Introducing vocabulary words within the context of a story or reading passage allows learners to understand their meanings in relation to the surrounding text. This approach involves selecting words that are important for understanding the text or that can add depth to the learners' insight about the situation. By discussing the author's choice of words and their impact, learners can develop a richer understanding of both the vocabulary and the text itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful selection of texts and vocabulary words. Facilitation of discussions that encourage learners to analyze the author's use of language and its impact on the story.
#### Constraints
- Requires instructors to be skilled at facilitating discussions and guiding learners to make meaningful connections between vocabulary and text. May be challenging to find texts that offer a wide range of vocabulary opportunities.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, especially those in upper elementary through high school, who need to develop a deeper understanding of how vocabulary contributes to the meaning and impact of texts.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance vocabulary acquisition, improve reading comprehension, and foster appreciation for author's craft by introducing words in meaningful contexts.

### Affordances
- Helps learners understand the nuances of word meanings and how they contribute to the overall message of a text. Promotes critical thinking and analysis of authorial choices.

### Personalization
- Instructors can select words based on their relevance to the text and the specific needs of their learners. Discussion questions can be tailored to encourage learners to make connections between the vocabulary and their own experiences.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track learner participation in discussions, analysis of vocabulary in written responses, and demonstration of understanding of the author's use of language. Monitor improvement in reading comprehension and vocabulary acquisition.

## Impact
- Learners demonstrate a deeper understanding of vocabulary and texts, improved critical thinking skills, and a greater appreciation for the power of language. By understanding the context in which words are used, learners can retain and apply new vocabulary more effectively.
