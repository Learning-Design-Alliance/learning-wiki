# Watch, write, and talk about movies as a structured sequence for raising multicultural awareness in teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a four-day structured sequence in which trainee teachers watch a multicultural-themed movie, individually write reflections on a Movie Evaluation Scale, then participate in a whole-group debate on the movie's reflections on their teaching. The researcher gives feedback, reads anonymous phrases, and asks "drilling questions" to build new cognitive structures. This watch-write-talk cycle was implemented over 22 total hours across three movies chosen from a National Education Ministry recommended list.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Movies must relate to multiculturalism and its effects on education
- Individual writing must precede group discussion to help build independent ideas
- Researcher feedback and drilling questions between sessions
#### Constraints
- Tested only with trainee teachers in Turkey over four days
- One-group pretest-posttest design without control group

### Target Learners
- Trainee teachers (pre-service)

### Target Learning Goals
- Multiculturalism awareness and perception
