# Structured cross-platform training curriculum combining generalised exposure and platform-specific proficiency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends a structured, cross-platform training curriculum for robotic surgery with two components: "a generalised cross-platform training exposing trainees to the nuances and ergonomic differences between platforms, and secondly, platform-specific proficiency training on the system used by their home institution". It proposes starting with VR simulation for basic skills then advancing to higher fidelity dry- or wet-lab models, to build adaptive expertise across systems while maintaining patient safety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of both generalised cross-platform exposure and platform-specific proficiency training
- Initial use of VR simulations for basic skills, then advancing to higher fidelity dry- or wet-lab models
#### Constraints
- The review notes further research is required to clarify how cross-platform training can be systematically integrated, ideally via prospective randomised controlled trials

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees in early robotic training

### Target Learning Goals
- transferable robotic surgical skills across platforms
