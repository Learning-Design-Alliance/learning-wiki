# Trainer education for SBL should explicitly include building trust and giving feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' design recommendation from the study: educational training for SBL trainers should give building trust and giving feedback a more explicit place, since trainers otherwise underemphasize feedback and trust is absent from established design principles. They state that "Building trust needs a more explicit place in the design of SBL programs" and that trainer development should highlight feedback's importance, alongside team skill development, crisis management, and learning from mistakes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainer development programs with room for pedagogical and facilitating skills beyond classroom facilitation training
#### Constraints
- Derived from trainer perceptions only; the authors call for mixed-method studies to evaluate multi-layered complexities of educational practices

### Target Learners
- Physician trainers and subject-matter experts facilitating SBL in CPD

### Target Learning Goals
- Facilitation, feedback, and trust-building skills for simulation trainers
