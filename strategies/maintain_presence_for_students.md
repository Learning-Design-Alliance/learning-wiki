# Maintain Presence for Students

## Description
Teachers should make themselves available for short talks with students before and after school or between classes. Teachers needs to show that they are doing this tough work with the students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who is aware of the language being used in the classroom, who is willing to intervene when necessary, and who has the skills and resources to address the situation effectively.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to address in the moment, may require additional support from administrators or parents, and may not always be effective.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe the frequency and severity of abusive language in the classroom, assess student understanding of the impact of their words, and gather feedback from students about the safety and respectfulness of the learning environment.

## Impact
- Research suggests that addressing abusive language in schools can create a safer and more respectful learning environment, promote positive social-emotional development, and improve academic outcomes.

## Examples
- A teacher hears a student use a homophobic slur and immediately says, 'I don't allow that term in my classroom. I need to talk with you after class about that.' The teacher then meets with the student to discuss the impact of their words and to foster empathy for those who are hurt.
