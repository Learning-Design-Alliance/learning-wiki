# International practicum placements at partner astronomical research institutions for astronomy undergraduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RTU places its astronomy students' practicum at international partner institutions to give them authentic research experience abroad. The article states "The Practicum of the students will be held at the National Astronomical Research Institute of Thailand and the Chiang Mai University wherein RTU has international linkages and inks collaborative efforts to train faculty and students in the field of astronomy and space science". The practicum is intended to instill significant and relevant research experience, and the university plans to benchmark and link with other international astronomical research institutions or space agencies in succeeding years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- International linkages and collaborative agreements with foreign astronomical research institutions or space agencies
#### Constraints
- The article states plans are for succeeding years; current linkages named are only the Thai institutions

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate astronomy students, plus faculty trained through the same collaborative efforts

### Target Learning Goals

- Astronomy and space science skills development
