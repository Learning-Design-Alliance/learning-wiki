# Professional development in asset-based pedagogies and adult SEL to build culturally affirming environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief argues educators need skills to create culturally affirming environments, build relationships with students, and support academic success, plus mindsets geared toward anti-racism. It recommends professional learning especially in "asset-based pedagogies (e.g., culturally responsive practices); diversity, equity, and inclusion; adult social-emotional learning (SEL) or adult mindsets; and strategies and approaches for evidence-based, equity-focused, systemic SEL for students," supported by robust funding for structures such as professional learning communities and induction or mentoring programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robust funding for districts to create evidence-based professional development structures
- Educator competency requirements covering equitable practices such as cultural competency and positive discipline
#### Constraints
- Tennessee's current PD policies lack clear equity-centered goals addressing adult mindsets and SEL, per the brief

### Target Learners
- All adults in schools, and through them P-12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Educator capacity to support students' well-being and academic success through culturally affirming environments
