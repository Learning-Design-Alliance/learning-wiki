# Visualize contact forces with slow-motion, mass-proportional area representations to teach Newton's 3rd Law

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
To close the observed 3rd Law learning gap, the authors propose new design features in the collision carts model: "clearer and slow motion visualization of contact forces during collision and larger mass having proportional larger area representation", with forces shown "as equal, opposite and acting on different bodies even for masses that are different". This targets the difficulty that forces during a collision are invisible in real demonstration equipment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An EJS-based collision carts model whose Evolution Page implements the spring impact-force equation (3) and display of contact forces on each cart.
#### Constraints
- These features are design ideas the authors "will test out in future research lessons"; their effectiveness is not yet tested in this article.

### Target Learners
- Secondary and junior college physics students learning Newton's 3rd Law

### Target Learning Goals
- Understand that contact forces during collision are equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, and act on different bodies
