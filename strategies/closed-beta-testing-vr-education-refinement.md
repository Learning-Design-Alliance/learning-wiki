# Use closed beta-testing with anonymous postsession questionnaires to refine VR educational software

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates a closed beta-testing strategy in which students attempt at least one full clinical scenario in a 1-hour session with unlimited attempts, then complete an anonymous questionnaire. The authors state the beta test "provided an opportunity to identify elements for future improvements," including refinement of interactive elements and consideration of a multiplayer design. Feedback on difficulty with interactive elements directly guided software development toward the final product.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants attend an in-person testing session and complete an anonymous postsession questionnaire; ethics approval is obtained
#### Constraints
- The prototype evaluated differs from the final product; voluntary convenience sampling may introduce selection bias
