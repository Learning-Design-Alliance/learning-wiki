# Line Awareness Activities

## Description
Activities focused on improving line awareness, which is crucial for writing legibly within a given space. These activities involve tracking lines, copying letters on lines, and using visual cues to promote consistent line use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lined paper, visual cues (e.g., highlighters, bold lines), graph paper, and structured practice sessions. Can be implemented in classrooms or at home.
#### Constraints
- May not address underlying issues such as visual perception difficulties or fine motor weakness. Relies on consistent practice and reinforcement.

### Personalization
- Activities can be tailored to individual line awareness difficulties or learning styles. Visual cues and tools support consistent line use.
