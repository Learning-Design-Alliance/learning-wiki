# Detector calibration project exploring statistical fluctuations and threshold plateaus

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Students use muonic to calibrate the scintillation detectors themselves, gaining deeper understanding of the physical principles involved. Two calibration activities are described: evaluating trigger rate versus averaging time to explore statistical fluctuations and determine the measurement time needed for a robust rate estimate, and analysing single-channel trigger rate versus threshold to find the plateau of stable rates. For each threshold setting approx. 5 minutes of data were collected in the example shown.
