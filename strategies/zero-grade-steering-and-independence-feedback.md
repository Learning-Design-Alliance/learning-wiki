# Use a low or zero Participation Grade, explained immediately, to steer grade-motivated students, and use the grade in advanced labs to foster independence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For the student exclusively focused on getting the right result, the article states that "Receiving a bad grade (a 0% should not be discouraged since it can be psychologically very effective , while bearing little impact on the final grade) right at the end of the session accompanied by an explanation of the reasons for the bad grade can work wonders". In advanced labs, the Participation Grade gives quick quantitative feedback to guide and encourage the development of independence, particularly for students who perform well under guidance but struggle to work independently.
