# Support students as whole people through non-traditional homework, grade-interpretation discussions, and rubric-based self-reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Compass courses implement whole-person support through homework that requires "attending ofﬁce hours and meeting with the department's student support staff" and through discussions in which students interpret grades as "academic measurements" and read about stereotype threat, self-handicapping, and motivation. As of spring 2012, students submit a weekly reflection evaluating their behavior in a traditional course with a rubric including "persistence, intellectual courage, and mental resourcefulness", tracking growth with holistic measures complementary to grades.
