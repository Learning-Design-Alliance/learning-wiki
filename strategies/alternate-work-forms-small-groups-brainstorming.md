# Alternate student work forms: small groups, brainstorming, and oral presentation in project work

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends varying the forms of students' work, noting that "engineering through means of small groups, brainstorming, and oral presentation in students' projects work reasonable well". It supports this with cited investigations in which tasks included a user role-playing exercise, proper brainstorming techniques, and problem solving methodologies, after which participants were able to design an innovative product. The strategy is offered as part of fostering creative and critical thinking while designing a product or solving an open-ended problem.
