Extend problem-based learning with role-play cases and interactive whiteboards¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article describes extending the problem-based learning model used in medical schools to overcome certain of its limitations through two innovations: "an innovative form of medical case for role-playing called 'the deteriorating patient'" and "the use of interactive whiteboards." Changes in scaffolding strategies and discourse patterns in response to these innovations were analyzed.
