Strategies

Prioritize robotics education investment at primary and secondary levels and favor prototype-creation and inquiry-driven teaching modes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study, q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends incorporating intelligent-robot education into school curricula to improve students' literacy in "programming control, human-computer interaction, algorithm programs, neural networks, and intelligent ethics", and investing more in creativity promotion at primary and secondary stages. It further recommends teaching modes that "assist in prototype creation and inquiry-driven learning" as most beneficial for enhancing student creativity.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Robotics curriculum must be incorporated into the school curriculum with attention to programming control, human-computer interaction, algorithms, and intelligent ethics literacy

Constraints¶

  • The senior-secondary level showed no significant creativity effect in the meta-analysis, while the secondary school level is described as particularly critical for innovation ability development

Target Learners¶

  • primary students
  • junior secondary students
  • senior secondary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • student creativity
  • creative thinking
  • practical innovation ability

Claims¶

  • Inquiry-driven teaching yields the largest creativity effect among teaching methods, followed by design-based and project-based teaching [+M]
  • Robotics education most strongly enhances practical innovation ability, and the combination of creative thinking plus practical innovation ability shows the largest effect [+W]
  • Robotics education significantly boosts creativity of primary and junior secondary students but shows no significant effect on senior secondary students [+W]
  • Teaching programs such as hands-on creation, experimental verification, and process participation are exceptionally effective at enhancing student innovation ability [+W]
  • Robotics education has a significant positive above-moderate effect on student creativity (SMD = 0.576) [+W]
