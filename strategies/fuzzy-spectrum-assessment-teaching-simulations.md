# Use fuzzy logic assessment on a spectrum rather than a binary scale to recognise student potential in teaching simulations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose applying fuzzy logic to academic assessment itself, so that multiple performance factors are graded continuously instead of pass/fail. They write that "fuzzy logic could be used to assess teaching simulations, where multiple factors such as communication skills, adaptability, and content knowledge are evaluated on a spectrum rather than a binary scale", allowing recognition of potential that traditional grading systems might overlook. This extends the study's predictive use of fuzzy logic into formative assessment practice.
