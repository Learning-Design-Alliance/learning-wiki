Involve trainees in the conception, design, implementation and improvement of medication reconciliation quality improvement programs¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends house staff involvement in conception, design, implementation and improvement of the medication reconciliation process as a vital step to ensure compliance and usability. Direct care providers such as residents can provide invaluable input and regular feedback that can improve practical aspects of the medication reconciliation process resulting in better compliance and more accurate reconciliation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Regular feedback mechanisms so residents remain an active part of improving medication reconciliation
  • Hospital administrators and policy developers working closely with resident representatives

Constraints¶

  • Challenges include tight resident schedules, rotation at several health care institutions, and limited financial resources

Target Learners¶

  • Medical residents (house staff)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Quality improvement participation
  • Medication reconciliation process design

Claims¶

  • Involving residents in developing and implementing medication reconciliation quality improvement initiatives improved compliance and reduced adverse drug events [+W]
  • Experiential education interventions for medical residents showed little effect on medication reconciliation competence and skills [-W]
