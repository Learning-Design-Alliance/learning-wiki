# Reduce mobile phone addiction as a modifiable behavioral target to enhance nursing students' self-directed learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends intervening on mobile phone addiction to strengthen self-directed learning, since the SEM showed addiction mediates part of alexithymia's negative effect on SDL. It states that "reducing alexithymia may enhance SDL by decreasing mobile phone addiction, which underscores mobile phone addiction as a modifiable behavioral target for clinical interventions." It further suggests positive interventions including increasing physical activity, strengthening social support, enhancing family cohesion, and cultivating professional identity to mitigate mobile phone addiction.
