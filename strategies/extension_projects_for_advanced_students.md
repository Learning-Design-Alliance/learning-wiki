# Extension Projects for Advanced Students

## Description
Challenge advanced learners with real-world extension projects that apply their knowledge in meaningful ways. Pool select learners from different classes to complete these projects. Provide opportunities for learners to collaborate in teams, analyze data, and develop creative solutions. This approach allows advanced learners to deepen their understanding, develop problem-solving skills, and engage in higher-order thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires identification of advanced learners, access to real-world projects, and resources aligned to project topics.
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure all learners are engaged and supported. Needs clear expectations to prevent off-task behavior. Requires ongoing feedback and guidance.

- Observe learners’ engagement, participation, and problem-solving skills within projects. Collect data from project deliverables, presentations, and peer evaluations. Track changes in performance following implementation of extension projects.

## Impact
- Advanced learners are challenged and deepen their understanding, while also motivating other learners to work harder to qualify for future projects.
