# Design curriculum materials that integrate concordancing programs and corpus-based activities into language programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests that material developers and curriculum designers should "design materials integrating corncordancing programs and corpus-based activities in language programs in order to promote language learning, especially vocabulary learning." This is an implementation recommendation directed at those who produce textbooks and course materials, extending the classroom-level finding to the curriculum-design level. It is grounded in the study's finding that corpus-based activities significantly improved verb-noun collocation recognition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Material developers need access to corpus tools and expertise to embed concordance-based activities in course materials
#### Constraints
- The recommendation extrapolates from a small study of 30 pre-intermediate learners; the authors call for research with more proficient learners and different learner backgrounds

### Target Learners
- EFL learners at pre-intermediate level

### Target Learning Goals
- Vocabulary learning, especially verb-noun collocation recognition
