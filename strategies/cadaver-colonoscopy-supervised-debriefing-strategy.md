# Supervise cadaver-based colonoscopy training with skilled endoscopist feedback and debriefing, since the model lacks objective performance metrics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that cadaver-based colonoscopy sessions include live supervision and debriefing because, unlike virtual reality devices, the cadaver model "does not provide objective quality measurement tools" with educational value. In the study, investigators performed complete supervision including live feedback and a debriefing session with open evaluation during each participant's colonoscopy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision by skilled endoscopists to guarantee the educational value of the model
#### Constraints
- The model lacks objective quality measurement tools such as patient's pain level, percentage of mucosa visualized, or amount of air insufflated

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Residents
- Fellows in gastrointestinal endoscopy

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe acquisition of initial endoscopic skills in a mistake-tolerant environment
