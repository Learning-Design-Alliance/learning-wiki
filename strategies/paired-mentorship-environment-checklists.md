# Paired mentee and mentor checklists to prompt critical thought on the research group environment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
As future work, the authors plan mentorship checklists prompting critical thought on the research group environment: "We will design two checklists: one aimed at the mentee level, i.e. early career researchers such as students and post-docs, and one aimed at the mentor level, i.e. faculty, civil servants, PIs, etc." The checklists will work in tandem with similar questions on either side to compare results for a future IRB study on the checklist's efficacy. The mentee checklist probes feelings of safety, value, and being heard; the mentor checklist probes equitable communication and group structure, ideally prompting introspection on inherent power dynamics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation into the developed mentorship training as a measure to constantly check in throughout a mentoring relationship, in combination with mentoring compacts
#### Constraints
- Efficacy of the checklists is not yet established; a future IRB study is planned

### Target Learners
- early career researcher mentees (students and post-docs)
- mentors (faculty, civil servants, PIs)

### Target Learning Goals
- self-assessment of mentoring relationship quality
- awareness of power dynamics in mentor/mentee relationships
