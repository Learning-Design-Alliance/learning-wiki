# Deliver a brief online role model letter that normalizes belonging concerns, frames academics as an investment, and exemplifies overcoming challenges

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 5-minute online activity in which students read a letter from a similar, successful graduate student. The letter (a) normalizes poor performance and feelings of not belonging, (b) directs students to think about time and effort spent on academics as an investment for the future, and (c) serves as an example that similar others can overcome challenges to succeed. It was delivered via an online survey after Exam 1 in large courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A role model perceived as competent and of the same gender or ethnic group, successful in an area of mutual interest; direct contact is not necessary
- The letter must communicate that the role model experienced challenges, since unattainable achievements can produce discouraging social comparison
#### Constraints
- The intervention was compared with a nonsubstantive control group
- It is not clear which incorporated approach (role model, belonging, attribution) is responsible for the effects

### Target Learners
- female 1st-year college students in introductory STEM courses

### Target Learning Goals
- academic performance (course grades)
- persistence (reduced failure and withdrawal rates)
