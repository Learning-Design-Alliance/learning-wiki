# Scaffolded guidance fading in VR surgical rehearsal: visible ideal-target guidance for the first five evaluated attempts, then deactivated

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the evaluated simulation stage the student first rehearsed unevaluated for familiarization, then completed 10 evaluated attempts. For "the first five attempts to visualize the guidance of the ideal target area and volume (evidenced in blue colour in the VR scenario), while the guidance was deactivated in the following five attempts". Real-time multiplane displays let the operator check target progress and accuracy during practice, and each session's outcome was recorded and reviewed with a clinical academic, supporting a progressive fade of external support toward criterion-based independent performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulator with defined target volumes, real-time multiplane progress display, and software assessment criteria (95% target progress, 90% accuracy, each with 10% tolerance)
#### Constraints
- Reported here for a single resident on hard-tissue stages only (ostectomy, apicoectomy, retro preparation)

### Target Learners
- postgraduate endodontic residents

### Target Learning Goals
- precision in osteotomy, root-end resection and retrograde cavity preparation
- self-monitored accuracy without visual guidance
