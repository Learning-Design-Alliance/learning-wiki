# Use ChatGPT as a supplementary tool for grammar, writing, and conversational practice so teachers can focus on higher-order thinking skills, while integrating human interaction to offset emotional detachment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends integrating ChatGPT into the classroom as a supplementary aid rather than prohibiting it: "ChatGPT serves as a supplementary tool for aiding grammar,writing,andconversationalpractice", allowing teachers "to allocate their attention toward higher-order thinking skills and moreintricateareasoflanguagetraining". Because interactions with ChatGPT lack emotional connection, educators should integrate human factors such as peer conversations and teacher-led interactions to provide a more well-rounded learning experience. Teachers can also use ChatGPT's data and insights to monitor student development and adjust instructional strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must acknowledge the limitations of the tool and complement ChatGPT interactions with human interactions
#### Constraints
- Inaccurate or misleading AI feedback reduces learner confidence and requires extra verification effort; internet stability may influence engagement with the tool

### Target Learners
- undergraduate EFL students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- English grammar, writing, and conversational practice; higher-order thinking skills; learner autonomy
