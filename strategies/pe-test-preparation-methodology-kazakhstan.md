# Methodology for preparing ninth-grade students for school qualifying standards and Presidential physical fitness tests

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the detected regularities and experimental results, the authors developed a preparation methodology whose components they enumerate: "a) the use of game like method as the main one in terms of student motivation development; b) improvement of the students' independent work; c) rationalization of the sequence content of planning documents and providing cumulative effect as regards the implementation of the program material; g) effective use of different forms of physical training (in this case, three PE classes per week + two section classes per week + independent physical exercises); e) the use of student achievement cards." The authors state this methodology was tested through the pedagogical experiment and can be used in modern teaching practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effective use of PE planning forms
- Cumulative effect in implementation of program material during each lesson and a quarter
#### Constraints
- Even holding three classes per week is insufficient to hit the qualifying standards of the Presidential tests

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade secondary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- reaching school qualifying standards and Presidential physical fitness test levels in stamina, speed-strength and strength
