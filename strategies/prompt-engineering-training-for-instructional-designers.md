## Description
The article suggests that biases in ChatGPT-generated content can be mitigated by training instructional designers in effective prompt engineering. It cites the recommendation that "good engineering prompts clearly articulate the objective of the prompt, provide context, and suggest examples of desired outputs." Prompt engineering can also extend to supporting students' help-seeking, remediating tasks, and practicing new skills.
