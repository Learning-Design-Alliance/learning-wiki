# Use four assessment types — computer-based, tutor-, self- and peer-assessment — within e-Learning scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
User requirements indicated that teachers "would like to make use of four diverse types of assessment in particular: computer-based assessment, tutor-assessment, self-assessment and peer-assessment". Scenarios sought to maximise the system's potential to support formative assessment strategies including collaborative assessment, a significant challenge where traditional summative, teacher-centred assessment is the norm.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instructions on what and how to assess for peer-assessment to avoid unhelpful comments
#### Constraints
- Traditional summative and teacher-centred forms of assessment are the norm in compulsory schooling

### Target Learners
- secondary school students and teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- formative assessment and assessment for learning
