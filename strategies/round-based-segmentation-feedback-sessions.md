# Round-based segmentation practice with 30-minute one-on-one expert feedback sessions after each round

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study structured segmentation practice into five rounds of increasing case counts (cases 1–10, 11–25, 26–50, 51–76, 76–100), with feedback after each round. As the article prints it, "After each round, a 30-min one-on-one feedback session between the expert and the reader was held online, using computer screen sharing." The expert reviewed three to five cases per session, highlighting non-overlapping segments and pointing out systematic errors and potential improvements for the next round. The authors conclude that continuous feedback after an initial introduction might provide a good balance between cost-effectiveness and segmentation accuracy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An expert radiologist available to provide one-on-one feedback sessions between rounds
- Segmentation software that can display non-overlapping segments between reader and reference
#### Constraints
- Feedback sessions were held in Swedish with native Swedish speakers, and no reader had prior feedback from the expert, so transfer to other settings is not established

### Target Learners
- radiology residents
- medical students (novices)

### Target Learning Goals
- manual prostate MRI segmentation accuracy
