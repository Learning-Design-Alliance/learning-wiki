# Pair live online tutorials with asynchronous tools — case reports, model answers and tutorial recordings — for consolidation and reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program combined synchronous live tutorials with asynchronous resources: after each tutorial students summarised the case and submitted a case report, and 'model' answers and video recordings were made available the following day. The article states this combination "aimed to support different learning styles, increase learning flexibility and provide an opportunity for consolidation and reflection outside of the live teaching sessions". Model answers served as worked examples showing how to summarise pertinent positive and negative points and the reasoning behind differentials, investigations and management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-developed case scripts and model answers aligned to the existing curriculum
- An online portal for case report submission and access to recordings
#### Constraints
- Psychological wellbeing effects of synchronous versus asynchronous settings were not studied in this program

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students studying remotely across multiple time zones

### Target Learning Goals
- history summarisation and clinical reasoning
- consolidation and reflection on case material outside live sessions
