# Embed verification routines and cognitive forcing functions into AI-supported learning tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend designing learning environments that make evaluation an explicit part of AI-supported work. They propose that "Instructional activities and AI tools should make verification a routine part of task completion by prompting students to explain their reasoning or justify why they may agree or disagree with an AI-generated recommendation." They further suggest embedding cognitive forcing functions, such as requiring students to commit to an answer before viewing the AI recommendation, identifying evidence for or against a suggestion, or completing a brief verification step before submitting a final response.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI-supported tasks in which students receive AI recommendations they may accept or reject
- Prompts or interface steps that elicit student reasoning or justification
#### Constraints
- The article frames cognitive forcing functions as a direction for future educational systems, citing prior work that they can reduce overreliance, rather than as a tested intervention in this study

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students using AI assistants during problem-solving

### Target Learning Goals
- reflective evaluation of AI-generated recommendations
- selective and appropriate reliance on AI assistance
