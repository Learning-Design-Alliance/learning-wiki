Strategies

Implement FCI in university EFL writing classes and provide instructor development and institutional support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on their findings, the authors recommend implementing FCI in writing classes as an effective way of improving Iraqi EFL learners' writing skills, and urge "a reconsideration of universi ty funds and structure to enable such a teaching method at universities". They also call for more conferences and opportunities for EFL instructors to acquire knowledge regarding FCI and similar approaches, and for a more prominent, directed use of technology among university students.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional funding and structure that enable flipped teaching methods
  • Professional development opportunities for EFL instructors on FCI

Constraints¶

  • The creative method had not yet been followed in university writing classrooms in Iraq

Target Learners¶

  • university EFL students
  • students of other disciplines

Target Learning Goals¶

  • EFL writing skills
  • learner autonomy and engagement

Affordances¶

  • Flipped Classroom Instruction Model

Claims¶

  • Flipped classroom instruction produced significantly better post-test writing scores than traditional instruction among Iraqi EFL sophomores [+W]
  • Iraqi EFL learners held predominantly positive attitudes toward flipped classroom instruction [+W]
  • Before the FCI treatment, the experimental and control groups showed no statistically significant difference in writing ability [+W]
