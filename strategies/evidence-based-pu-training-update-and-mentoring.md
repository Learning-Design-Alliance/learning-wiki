# Update undergraduate and in-service PU training to include evidence-based practice, evaluate effectiveness periodically, and have senior nurses guide new graduates

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends updating existing training programs to include evidence-based practices for pressure ulcer management, evaluating training effectiveness periodically, and pairing experience with novice support: "senior nurses should be encouraged to guide newly graduated nurses." It also recommends practical training on real patient cases alongside theoretical knowledge to support clinical decision-making, and that health institutions organise in-service training and clinical practice opportunities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Health institutions must organise in-service training programs and develop clinical practice opportunities; universities must update undergraduate curricula.
#### Constraints
- The article calls for intervention studies evaluating the impact of educational programs, since these have not yet been carried out in this setting.

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- newly graduated nurses
- practising nurses in direct patient care

### Target Learning Goals
- evidence-based pressure ulcer management practices and clinical decision-making
