# Analyse students’ reflective writing to reveal learning bottlenecks and support needs in online communication training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends gathering insights into learning processes through reflection to support student development. “Gathering insights into learning processes facilitated through reflection is crucial in supporting students through their development, revealing learning bottlenecks or identifying where students may benefit from additional support”. Instructors can use thematic analysis of reflective texts to identify challenges, skill gaps, and emerging insights not covered by formal objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students compose written reflections in response to guided prompts during the course
#### Constraints
- Qualitative thematic analysis is time-intensive; the study sampled reflections from 55 of 114 completing students due to scope limitations

### Target Learners
- medical students in e-learning courses

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying learning bottlenecks
- tailoring instructional support

## Claims
