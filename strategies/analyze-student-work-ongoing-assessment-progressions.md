# Train teachers to analyze student work as ongoing assessment using research-based learning progressions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes OGAP's core instructional strategy: teachers are trained "to analyze student work as an ongoing assessment, helping them recognize strategies and misconceptions to guide instruction in real-time and over time." This strategy rests on research-based learning progressions and is supported by research-based item banks and frameworks across the core domains of K–8 mathematics, enabling teachers to adjust instruction based on the strategies and misconceptions evident in student work.
