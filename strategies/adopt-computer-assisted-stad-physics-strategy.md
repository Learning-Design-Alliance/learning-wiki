# Expose physics students to computer-assisted STAD cooperative instruction to improve performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its findings, the article recommends that "Physics teachers should expose their students to computer-assisted STAD cooperative instructional strategy so as to improve their performance in physics." It further recommends that ministries and stakeholders organize workshops on computer-assisted cooperative learning, that teacher education programs prepare teachers in such innovative strategies, and that instructional designers develop relevant computer-assisted instructional packages for Nigerian schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in the package and cooperative strategy; computer laboratory facilities; available CALP-style packages
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one quasi-experimental study in two Nigerian public schools; the article itself notes prior mixed findings for STAD versus conventional methods

### Target Learners
- secondary school physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- physics achievement and retention
