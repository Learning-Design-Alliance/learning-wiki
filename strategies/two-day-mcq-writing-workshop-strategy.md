# Two-day interactive MCQ-writing workshop combining checklist consensus, revision of faculty-authored items, and small-group item construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention was a two-day full-time workshop for 25 newly joined faculty. On day one, "theoretical backgrounds were discussed along with the revision of the MCQs construction checklist criteria and consensus was achieved regarding the checklist items"; faculty's own pre-workshop MCQs were revised against the agreed checklist. On day two, participants in small groups of three to four wrote five MCQs in their specialties using the checklist, which were then discussed, corrected and edited with participants' agreement. After the workshop, new items were reviewed at departmental level and non-conforming items modified or deleted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quality MCQ construction checklist agreed by consensus among participants
- Participants bring their own items for revision and write new items in their own specialties
- Departmental-level review of post-workshop items against the checklist
#### Constraints
- The article states further workshops would be needed for other assessment tools such as short answer questions and OSCEs, and that workshops must be conducted at broader scales in different contexts

### Target Learners
- newly joined faculty members (demonstrators through professors) teaching basic science courses

### Target Learning Goals
- writing high-quality single-best-answer MCQ items following standard construction guidelines
