# Develop preventive and educational initiatives aimed at optimizing university students' sleep habits, including sleep-hygiene education sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend preventive and educational initiatives to optimize sleep habits among university students, given the strong influence of sleep on academic performance and health. They note that many factors related to sleep problems are modifiable without pharmacological therapy, and that "a single one-hour session on education in sleep hygiene improves both the quantity and quality of sleep", citing prior intervention work. The strategy targets the sleep problems documented in this sample, where 56.6% showed poor sleep quality.
