# Deliver spaced-education refresher training via daily SMS prompts and participant-initiated IVR calls on simple phones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe: send a daily SMS prompt asking if the learner is ready; when the learner texts back, the system immediately calls with recorded audio questions; the learner answers by keypad and hears detailed explanations after each answer, correct or incorrect. Learners may "answer between 0 to 4 questions a day based on their preference and availability", and questions repeat until answered correctly twice before retirement. A printed copy of course content is provided on completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 3-hour orientation for participants on using the system
- A telephony contract allowing free texts and unlimited system calls to participants
#### Constraints
- Requires adequate cellular network coverage; rural participants reported more reception issues

### Target Learners
- health workers with simple mobile phones and low literacy

### Target Learning Goals
- refresher training on family planning clinical content
