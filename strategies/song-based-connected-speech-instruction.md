# Use English verbal songs as a pedagogical tool for improving connected speech in EFL instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends incorporating English verbal songs into EFL instruction to improve learners' connected speech production. Over 18 sessions, one song was worked on per two sessions, focusing on phonological aspects rather than grammar or lexis. The authors conclude that "Songs could be a promising replace for the traditional methods of teaching pronunciation such as listening and repeating individual words in isolation."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Songs should be preselected based on criteria such as popularity, familiarity, amount, and speed of speech delivery.
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted with adult intermediate male learners aged 18-25 at a single language institute in Tehran; the authors note further research is needed before generalizing.

### Target Learners
- Adult intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Connected speech production in reading-aloud and speaking modes
