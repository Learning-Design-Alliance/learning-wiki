# Design medication safety continuing education around career-stage needs, self-reflection, communication training, and RCA practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's discussion translates its five themes into design recommendations: education that encourages self-reflection and vigilance to prevent complacency with routine; SBAR communication training to improve interprofessional teamwork; education building independent learning of pharmacological actions rather than only basic drug information; simulation-based, case-based, and flipped learning for root cause analysis skills; and a clinical ladder education system with incentives and organizational support. The authors state "systematic educational programs should be designed to ensure that appropriate education is continuously provided to nurses with different levels of clinical experience."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous provision of education across career stages
- Organizational atmosphere and adequate economic support, with incentives for participation
#### Constraints
- The study's recommendations rest on qualitative needs identified in one tertiary hospital in one country; needs may differ by clinical setting

### Target Learners
- novice through proficient clinical nurses
- nursing educators and hospital education planners

### Target Learning Goals
- medication safety competence
- interprofessional communication
- root cause analysis skills
- self-reflective attitude toward medication safety
