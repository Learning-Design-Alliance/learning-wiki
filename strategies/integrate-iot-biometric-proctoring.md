## Description
The review recommends combining Internet of Things sensing and biometric verification with AI-based proctoring to strengthen security, scalability, and operational efficiency. It reports that "IoT Monitoring Enables real-time, multimodal cheating detec -tion using sensors" while noting privacy concerns and environment-dependent accuracy, and that advanced security techniques with IoT enhance authenticity and data integrity using biometric/fingerprint methods but involve costly hardware and user resistance due to invasiveness.
