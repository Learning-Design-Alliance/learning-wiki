# Collaborative COPD education combining specialist-led training events, spirometry interpretation training, clinical audit with individual benchmarking, and a mobile app

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (5 for, 4 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multi-component continuing education strategy in which pulmonologists, clinical pharmacologists and GPs jointly deliver "periodic (every 6 months) and interactive face to face meetings" including "training session on interpretation of spirometry findings". Audit results from electronically extracted GP data are presented anonymously and discussed in meetings, then each GP receives an individual benchmarking report. A COPD mobile app lets GPs view their own indicator values in real time and receive remote updates via email.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating GPs must use electronic archives from which quality-of-care indicator data can be automatically extracted and audited
- Specialist involvement (pulmonologists and clinical pharmacologists) to deliver updated diagnosis and management content
#### Constraints
- The article notes the effect "may vary across GPs and indicators of COPD quality of care"
- Participating GPs were already involved in regular clinical research activity and performed better at baseline than the national comparator, limiting generalizability

### Target Learners
- General practitioners in primary care

### Target Learning Goals
- COPD diagnosis including spirometry interpretation
- Guideline-concordant pharmacological management of COPD
- Preventive care registration (vaccinations, BMI, smoking)
