# Emotion Charades

## Description
Emotion Charades involves a student acting out a scenario that makes them feel a certain way, while the rest of the group guesses the emotion being portrayed. This activity promotes emotional literacy and recognition of emotional triggers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a set of pre-written scenarios or a whiteboard to generate ideas. A facilitator to guide the activity and ensure respectful interactions.
#### Constraints
- Some students may feel uncomfortable acting out emotions. Requires a supportive and inclusive classroom environment.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Develop emotional literacy, recognize emotional triggers, promote nonverbal communication skills

### Affordances
- Enhances emotional vocabulary, promotes empathy, and offers a creative outlet for emotional expression. Supports social awareness by encouraging students to consider different perspectives.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by providing different scenarios or focusing on specific emotions. Learners can choose scenarios relevant to their experiences.
