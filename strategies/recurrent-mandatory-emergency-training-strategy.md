# Run emergency training as a regular, recurrent, mandatory course with more scenarios and virtual patients kept available as refreshers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants' free-text responses and the follow-up survey repeatedly recommended making the emergency training a regular, recurrent and mandatory course for all staff, offering more scenarios, and keeping the virtual patients available for individual refreshers. The article reports that implementation of a regular, recurrent, and mandatory course for all staff was suggested, and in the follow-up 28 respondents mentioned repetition of training, for example that yearly repetition is required to ensure replicability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional scenarios and, per some suggestions, extended or multi-day training formats
#### Constraints
- The article reports these as participant suggestions, not as tested design modifications

### Target Learners
- all medical and nursing hospital staff, including new staff before independent duty

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of emergency preparedness over time
- sustained confidence and team performance in pediatric emergencies
