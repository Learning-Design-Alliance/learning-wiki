# Integrate TIC training into existing organizational structures such as team meetings and onboarding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Prenatal clinicians identified existing institutional structures that could be leveraged to integrate TIC training more effectively. These include mandatory team meetings, onboarding processes, and residency curricula. One participant noted "We all have mandatory meetings that are built into our year" as an opportunity for TIC training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing organizational meeting structures and onboarding processes that can serve as delivery channels for TIC training
#### Constraints
- Requires organizational commitment to prioritize TIC within already scheduled structures

### Target Learners
- Prenatal clinicians
- Healthcare administrators
- Residents and students

### Target Learning Goals
- Trauma-informed care competency
- Trauma-informed communication skills
