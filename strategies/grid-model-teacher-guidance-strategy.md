# Provide teachers with detailed guidance, orientation and feedback practice when implementing the Grid Model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends supporting teachers who implement the Grid Model with guidance and orientation, because observed implementation difficulties centered on evaluation and feedback rather than the model itself. It states that "teachers must give the necessary feedback to the students in order to carry out the Grid Model" and that "A detailed guidance or orientation may be provided the teachers implementing the model in order to guide and evaluate the students' tasks appropriately." The study itself trained experimental-group teachers on the model and ran pilot schemes before implementation.
