# Use a country grading system in applicant selection to contextualise achievements and increase diversity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Since 2023, LEAPS selection incorporates a country grading system that accounts for applicants' nationality and university affiliation, considering biases and disparities in access to education, research opportunities, and financial constraints. Countries are evaluated on developmental and educational factors including Human Development Index, STEM representation, gender and diversity representation, and scientific publications. The stated aim is to "provide a fairer way to contextualize candidates' achievements and increase diversity in the program".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data sources for country-level indicators such as the Human Development Index, OECD STEM scoreboard, UNESCO reports and Scopus publication counts
- Selection guidelines for supervisors covering eligibility, confidentiality, effective interviews, and reducing intrinsic bias
#### Constraints
- The authors state future monitoring is required to evaluate the effect of its implementation on the diversity of LEAPS participants

### Target Learners
- Applicants from countries with less internationally recognised academic systems or fewer research opportunities

### Target Learning Goals
- Fairer evaluation of applicant achievement in context
- Increasing geographic diversity of program participants
