# Collect and analyze students' pre-inquiries before class and use the selected meaningful pre-inquiries to structure science lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy has the teacher gather students' own questions about an upcoming unit, select with experts those closely related to the curriculum, and run question-resolution lessons built around them. The article recommends that "teachers should not only analyze teaching materials such as curriculum and textbooks before class, but also understand and analyze student's thoughts by using student's pre -inquiries", reconstructing class to suit students' level and needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A questionnaire or other means of collecting students' pre-inquiries before the unit
- Consultation with science curriculum specialists or fellow teachers to select meaningful pre-inquiries
#### Constraints
- The supporting study was conducted with elementary school students in South Korea on the 'Acid and Base' unit

### Target Learners
- elementary school 6th grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- science process skills
- science-related affective domain
