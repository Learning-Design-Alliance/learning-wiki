# Close the feedback loop: have residents bring evidence of addressing prior developmental needs and have the CCC evaluate whether performance improved

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that CCCs "complete the feedback loop by reflecting on feedback provided to residents after the previous meeting and reviewing whether residents have acted on it to improve their performance". Residents should provide evidence that previously identified developmental needs have been addressed. This builds on the developmental approach to assessment the article advocates, in contrast to CCCs that focus only on identifying struggling residents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient time between meetings for residents to collect evidence of performance improvement
- Feedback delivered to residents as soon as possible after each meeting
#### Constraints
- Residents in this study considered the 2-month span between receiving feedback and handing in information for the next meeting too short to collect evidence of improvement

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- acting on feedback to improve clinical performance
