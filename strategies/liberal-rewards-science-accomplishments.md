# Reward and celebrate student science accomplishments liberally with positive feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues the goal of student science projects is to build not only the ability to do good science but "a passion and love and confidence for doing it," and that this goal "is best served with liberal and ample rewards and positive feedback for all levels of success." It contrasts the prominent celebration of athletic wins with the minimal recognition of state science fair placements, and suggests trophies, plaques, banners, yearbook or newspaper coverage, and celebratory meals.
