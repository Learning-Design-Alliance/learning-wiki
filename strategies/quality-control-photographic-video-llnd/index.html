Incorporate quality control measures with standardized photographic or video-based assessments using predefined anatomic landmarks¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends incorporating quality control measures such as standardized photographic or video-based assessments employing predefined anatomic landmarks to ensure consistent and objective validation of surgical technique during learning curve assessment. This addresses the finding that none of the included studies described the use of intraoperative or postoperative audit protocols to verify the completeness of the dissection, unlike the JCOG0212 trial which employed photographic verification.

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt cluster-randomised or stepped-wedge trial designs with blinded, video-based assessment and pre-defined process and patient endpoints in future NTS training research.
