# Conduct focus groups to hear firsthand from multiple partners and build shared understanding beyond the numbers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Focus groups let leaders "hear firsthand from multiple educational partners and create an environment in which participants can build on each other's ideas." Qualitative data from focus groups helps leaders see beyond the numbers and better understand what is really going on and how to help. They differ from listening sessions in being smaller, more intimate, and more focused on specific outcomes and predetermined questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predetermined questions focused on specific outcomes, distinguishing them from open-ended listening sessions
#### Constraints
- Smaller and more intimate than listening sessions; suited to specific outcomes rather than open-ended sharing

### Target Learners
- Groups of students, families, staff, and community members holding unique perspectives on a topic

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding problems deeply before selecting solutions
- gathering qualitative data to complement quantitative indicators
