# Integrate EBM training into clinical care and spread it across residency rather than relying on a single workshop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the null workshop effect, the authors recommend broader, longitudinally distributed and clinically integrated EBM education. Their program built a curriculum enhancing faculty competencies, providing separate PGY-1 and PGY-2 workshops for reinforcement, and piloting an evidence-based educational prescription tool that walks residents through EBM steps while answering clinical questions at the point of care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development to enhance EBM competencies
- Point-of-care tools such as the educational prescription to connect learning with clinical questions
#### Constraints
- The authors note integration into clinical practice has not been confirmed in randomized controlled trials, and changing secondary resources may require different teaching methods

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents
- Faculty teaching EBM

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying evidence at the point of care
- Reinforcing and consolidating EBM skills across residency
