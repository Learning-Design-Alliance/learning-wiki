# Hide information in early representations to reduce cognitive load before introducing more complex ones

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the Lecture-Tutorial's first representation, lensing objects are deliberately hidden behind black boxes. The authors state that "Restricting the amount of information displayed in Figure 3 protects students from needing to simultaneously engage in two novel tasks, namely reasoning about the bending of light rays and processing the RdR." This restriction reduces cognitive load so students can construct understanding of the relation between light bending and spacetime curvature before applying their full cognitive resources to the reduced-dimensional representation.
