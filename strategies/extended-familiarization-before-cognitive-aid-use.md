# Provide longer training and familiarization before deploying an interactive cognitive aid in simulated resuscitation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Participants in the pilot study expressed the need for more practice before using the app in a scenario: "it also emerged that longer training and familiarization with the app before the simulated scenario would have been beneficial to interact more efficiently and effectively with the app." The authors commit to incorporating this into the design of the upcoming randomized controlled trial protocol, having found that insufficient familiarization contributed to difficulties interacting with the screens flow and to operating the tablet while leading the team.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time must be allocated for hands-on familiarization with the device before high-stakes use
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of a single-center simulation pilot with pediatric residents; effectiveness of the adjustment will be tested in a future RCT

### Target Learners
- pediatric residents and other clinicians learning to use a new cognitive aid

### Target Learning Goals
- efficient and effective interaction with an interactive cognitive aid during resuscitation
