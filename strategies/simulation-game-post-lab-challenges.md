# Use game-like simulation challenges as post-lab application tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Post-lab questions extend the experiment with open-ended application tasks in the same simulation environment, such as the PhET electric field hockey game in Experiment 1, where students place positive and negative charges to steer a charge to a goal without hitting barriers. The manual instructs students to "Repeat the procedure till you get the goal" and to attach screenshots for at least two difficulty levels, then discuss electric repulsion, attraction and the electric field. This turns post-lab time into exploratory, goal-directed practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A browser-based simulation game with difficulty levels; screenshot capture for lab reports
#### Constraints
- Used as a post-lab extension attached to the lab report, not as the core measured procedure

### Target Learners
- undergraduate introductory physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- applying electric force and field concepts to solve a goal-directed challenge
