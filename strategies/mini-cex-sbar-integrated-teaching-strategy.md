# Combine modified Mini-CEX assessment with SBAR structured communication in experimental health assessment teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes an integrated teaching method in which "an modified Mini-CEX combined with the SBAR teaching method was adopted, and the teaching process included classroom scenario simulation and clinical apprenticeship." Students work in groups of six on clinical cases, use SBAR for structured information exchange, and receive assessments and feedback from faculty pairs. The strategy spans pre-class preparation, in-class simulation with standardized patients, and clinical probation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty with case-writing skills, classroom management ability, and clinical nursing experience
- Standardized patient training and a clinical case bank
#### Constraints
- The study could not disentangle the independent instructional effect of each component

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- health assessment skills
- structured communication
- clinical reasoning
