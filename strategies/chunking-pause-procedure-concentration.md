# Chunking lecture content and using the pause procedure to improve student concentration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For curriculum planning, the authors recommend two simple faculty techniques: chunking lecture content and utilizing the pause procedure. Chunking replaces a one hour lecture with four mini-lectures separated by three short intervals that may be student interaction, a quiz, or a written assignment. The pause procedure involves two minute pauses every twelve to eighteen minutes during the lecture. The authors cite prior work indicating these methods helped hold students' attention and improved recall.
