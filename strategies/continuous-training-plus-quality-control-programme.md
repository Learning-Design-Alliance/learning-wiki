# Pair continuous refresher training for microscopists with a laboratory quality control programme that assesses the periodicity of new training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend, based on their finding that a single refresher course improved knowledge and smear quality but not diagnostic accuracy, that laboratories institute "a continued programme training for technicians, combined to a quality control programme to assess the periodicity of new courses." Quality control is presented as a way to supervise diagnosis and determine when new training is needed, rather than relying on one-off courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory infrastructure to run periodic quality control on microscopy diagnosis
- Investment in regular training and qualification of microscopists
#### Constraints
- Training alone did not significantly improve sensitivity or specificity in this study; adequate equipment, reagents, good working conditions and motivated personnel are also needed

### Target Learners
- Laboratory technicians and microscopists in malaria-endemic peripheral hospitals and health centres

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained competence and diagnostic accuracy in malaria microscopy
