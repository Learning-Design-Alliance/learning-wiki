# Precede non-obligatory e-learning with face-to-face education that explains the subject's importance to enhance autonomous motivation and participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because e-learning as a remote method can lack relatedness, the article recommends providing learners with a meaningful rationale so they identify with reasons to learn, which raises autonomous motivation and participation. Concretely, it proposes face-to-face education prior to an e-learning module explaining the importance and relevance of the topic, integrating e-learning with face-to-face instruction in a blended design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face session must explain the importance and relevance of the e-learning subject to influence learners' attitude
#### Constraints
- The article frames this as a promising approach whose effect on participation still needs study; it reports no direct test of the blended format

### Target Learners
- medical residents
- postgraduate clinicians in antimicrobial stewardship programmes

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in non-obligatory e-learning
- appropriate antibiotic prescribing
