# Counsel students on clerkship scheduling and provide extra academic support early in the year

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because students who take a clerkship earlier in the year are at a disadvantage on shelf exams, the review advises that clerkship directors counsel students when creating schedules and provide extra academic support during early-year clerkships. As the review states, "Clerkship directors can counsel students on this when they are creating their schedules". Students may also place specialties they are highly interested in later in the year.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires clerkship directors to have some input or influence over student scheduling
