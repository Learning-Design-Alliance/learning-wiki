# In public physics talks, establish vivid contact, invite audience perceptions, and give simple answers to typical quantum questions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For presenting particle physics to the general public, the author reports that hints at the basics of the scientific method are usually fruitful, and describes his own practice: "I try to establish a vivid contact with the audience and to encourage the visitor to voice his or her own perception." Because the description of nature by humans is a human enterprise, dogmas do not exist in science but consensus has to be established. The article also recommends giving simple, direct answers to typical questions rather than retreating into mystery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A presenter willing to treat audience views and expectations as legitimate contributions to a human enterprise of describing nature
#### Constraints
- The author notes the outcome depends on the very public at least as much as on the skill of the presenter, and that pop science gibberish often rules in spheres remote from direct experience

### Target Learners
- general public
- taxpayer audiences of high-energy physics outreach

### Target Learning Goals
- voicing and refining one's own perception of science
- curiosity about scientific endeavor
