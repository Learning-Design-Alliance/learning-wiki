# Provide timely mental health detection and comprehensive educational support for pupils with depression, addressing shared precursors rather than single risk factors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on its findings, the review recommends that "families, clinicians, and educators should engage in comprehensive approaches that can address modifiable, shared precursors for both depression and poor attainment (such as school absence), rather than targeting single risk factors in isolation." It argues that because the negative association presents during the school years rather than only in adulthood, "Timely detection and intervention during school is therefore critical," and that future work should test whether detection and treatment of depression symptoms in pupil populations improves educational outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evidence-based interventions for children and adolescents with depression, and research testing whether treatment improves educational outcomes
#### Constraints
- The review notes further research is needed to establish mechanisms before interventions can be precisely targeted

### Target Learners
- children and adolescents with depression symptoms in school settings

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining educational attainment among pupils experiencing depression
