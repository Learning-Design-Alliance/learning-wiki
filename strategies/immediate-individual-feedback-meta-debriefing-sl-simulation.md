# Immediate individual trainer feedback plus group meta-debriefing after each simulated debriefing practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
After each roughly 10-minute simulated debriefing, the trainer delivers about 20 minutes of individual feedback focused on the session objective and general debriefing skills as a meta-debriefing, covering moves such as "plus-delta, advocacy and inquiry, useful expressions in specific situations, open-question, intentional silence, honest speaking, making notes of learners' response." Waiting trainees prepare and reflect in another Zoom room, and the session closes with group debriefing of common insufficient points such as excessive support and perfunctory reaction.
