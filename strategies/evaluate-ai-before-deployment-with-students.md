# Evaluate AI systems before deploying them with students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that edtech teams evaluate their AI models and outputs before going live with students, because failures like the LAUSD "Ed" chatbot and BuzzFeed's AI-first pivot show the costs of deploying without robust evaluation. The authors write: "Evaluate before you deploy with students." Buyers are increasingly asking vendors to demonstrate how well their AI actually works.
