## Description
The article recommends implementing "some form of semi-supervised, or even unsupervised, algorithm to analyse each student's past and current learning performance and/or mastery data" to suggest learning pathways or dynamically adjust task demands. Positioned at point ① of the model, this maintains a balance between task demand and cognitive quality of instruction, minimising frustration and enhancing the likelihood of successful learning outcomes.
