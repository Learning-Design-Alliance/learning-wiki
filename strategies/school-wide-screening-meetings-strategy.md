# Pair the individual e-learning program with school-wide organizational responses such as screening meetings to identify children in need of support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the program "can be used as a training program within the school", with administrators organizing screening meetings where schoolteachers, staff, and administrators share information to identify children in need of support. The authors note that in a national survey, 80% of Japanese elementary schools held meetings about children with concerns but only 13.5% held screening meetings to identify children in need.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administrator involvement to organize screening meetings or other organizational responses within the school
#### Constraints
- The study assessed individual teachers' understanding and behaviors, not the organizational response of the school as a whole

### Target Learners
- Elementary schoolteachers and school administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying children of parents with mental illness in need of support
