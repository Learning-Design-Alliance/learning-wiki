# International resident fellowship model for developing shared online pathology teaching content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A specific, implementable staffing strategy: a pathology resident is awarded an international fellowship to spend dedicated time (6 weeks in this case) at partner universities developing e-learning module content under expert supervision. The article reports that "a pathology resident from Québec spent 6 weeks in France and Switzerland to develop the contents and build the module on an e-learning Moodle platform under the supervision of two dermatopathologists", with financial support from CFQCU. The experience is then transferred to the next international fellow, who will create lung and breast pathology modules.
