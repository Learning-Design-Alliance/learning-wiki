# Reorganize workshop modules in response to students' dynamic requirements as the semester unfolds

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends dynamically adjusting GenAI workshop modules during a design studio semester. In Case-II the authors observed that "The level of integration of cutting-edge tools remained consistent when workshop outcomes were not continually measured or adapted to align with the evolving needs of the students throughout the semester." They therefore emphasize reorganizing workshop modules in response to students' dynamic requirements, and in Case-III scheduled workshops in response to ongoing observations and insights from semi-structured interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing observation of students and measurement of workshop outcomes throughout the semester
#### Constraints
- The recommendation arises from observations in architecture design studios with third-year students

### Target Learners
- architecture design studio students

### Target Learning Goals
- proficiency with GenAI tools
- integration of GenAI into the design process
