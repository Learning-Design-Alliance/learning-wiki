# Use formative quizzes, writing portfolios, and peer-feedback rubrics rather than relying only on a final examination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that in the evaluation phase, student learning should be monitored using formative quizzes, writing portfolios, and peer-feedback rubrics rather than relying only on a final examination. Continuous feedback enables instructors to adjust teaching activities and provide additional support to students showing low engagement or slow progress.
