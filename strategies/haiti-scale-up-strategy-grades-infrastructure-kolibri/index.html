Scale-up strategy: add grades 3 and 4, expand solar and connectivity with local capacity-building, and adopt Kolibri for offline access¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The presentation's future plans lay out an expansion strategy: "Content: Adding 3rd grade (2023-24) & 4th grade (2024-25)"; "Infrastructure: Expansion of solar power & internet connectivity (pending funding); local capacity-building for infrastructure management; learning from infrastructure solutions in similar settings"; and "Technology: Kolibri, Learning Equality's open-source, offline-first digital platform." Scaling also means "Expanding to additional partner schools," supported by core partners including APA, MSN, Blue Butterfly, Digital Promise, EDC, and Learning Equality.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Pending funding for infrastructure expansion; local capacity-building for infrastructure management; partner school networks

Constraints¶

  • Infrastructure expansion is explicitly pending funding

Target Learners¶

  • Early grade primary students in rural Haiti, expanding from grades 1-2 to grades 3-4

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Continued access to mother-tongue digital science content as grades expand
