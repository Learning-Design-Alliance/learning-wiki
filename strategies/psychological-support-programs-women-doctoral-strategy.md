# Implement psychological support programs, foster intrinsic motivation, integrate psychological assessments, establish support networks, and make tailored curricular adjustments for women doctoral students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article translates its findings on protective psychosocial factors into practical implications for doctoral programs. It states: "The practical implications include implementing psychological support programs, fostering intrinsic motivation, integrating psychological assessments, establishing support networks, and making tailored curricular adjustments." These actions target the protective functions of psychological capital and academic motivation identified in the quantitative and qualitative phases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs should strengthen the protective factors the study identifies: self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation, within the four approaches of optimism, hope, resilience, and self-efficacy.
#### Constraints
- The study is a cross-sectional, single-institution study of 108 women in one Doctorate in Education program in Lima; the article states limitations and future projections are discussed.

### Target Learners
- female doctoral students

### Target Learning Goals
- mental health and well-being during doctoral study
- academic performance and persistence

## Claims

- [Among 108 female doctoral students, 36% show low psychological capital, 34% medium academic motivation, and 37% high university academic performance](../claims/female-doctoral-students-levels-psycap-motivation-uap.md) [+W]

- [Intrinsic Motivation toward Accomplishment is the positive core where most female doctoral students converge, with 64% at high level and 0% at medium](../claims/intrinsic-motivation-accomplishment-positive-core.md) [+W]
- [A gap exists between academic motivation (rated higher) and UAP (rated lower) among female doctoral students](../claims/motivation-uap-gap.md) [+W]
- [Binary logistic regression shows psychological capital and academic motivation (and specifically self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation) are protective: higher scores decrease the probability of non-high UAP](../claims/psycap-motivation-protective-logistic-regression.md) [+W]
- [CHAID analysis identifies a risk profile (low/medium intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation plus low/medium self-efficacy) and a protective profile (high self-efficacy) for UAP](../claims/risk-protective-profiles-uap-chaid.md) [+W]
