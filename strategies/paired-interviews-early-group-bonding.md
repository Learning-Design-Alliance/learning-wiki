# Use paired interviews early in a group's life to build connections and surface participant information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide recommends paired interviews in the early stages of a group so members start connecting quickly. It states that "Paired interviews are a great way to get people talking to each other, to elicit a lot of valuable information about participants, and to make sure that each person has at least one 'buddy'." The exercise should be repeated in subsequent meetings with changed topics and pairings so everyone converses with everyone else.
