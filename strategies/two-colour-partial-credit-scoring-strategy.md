# Colour-coded partial-credit scoring that keeps points in jeopardy during collaboration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Scoring differentiates work by pen colour: full credit for correct answers in the individual-phase colour, half credit (or another decreased value) for correct answers amended in the collaborative colour, and half credit deducted when a correct individual answer is crossed out for an incorrect collaborative one. This makes the decision to amend an answer consequential, which the authors describe as brokering decision-making in a peer situation and keeping points in jeopardy for amended answers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two visually distinct pen colours per student
- Collection of individual-phase pens before collaboration so phase attribution is unambiguous
#### Constraints
- The scoring format can be varied; the article notes offering full credit in both phases could make the assessment more formative

### Target Learners
- secondary school students taking written summative assessments

### Target Learning Goals
- careful evaluation of peer suggestions before revising one's own answers
