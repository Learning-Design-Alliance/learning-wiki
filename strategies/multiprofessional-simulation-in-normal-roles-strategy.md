# Use multi-professional simulation training with participants working in their normal team roles to strengthen emergency competency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementation strategy is to let participants with different professional experience train together in their normal function, on the authors' postulate that they benefit most this way. The courses combined algorithm presentation, CRM essentials, scenario practice on high- and low-fidelity mannequins, standardized patients and immediate debriefing. The authors conclude that "Implementation of simulation training strengthens the professional competency" based on improved three-month self-assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical algorithms presented before scenario training
- Debriefing with immediate feedback after each scenario
- Multi-professional tutor teams supervising scenarios
#### Constraints
- Benefit was demonstrated only for self-perceived competency, not objectively measured skills or outcomes

### Target Learners
- midwives
- obstetricians
- anaesthesiology and neonatology staff in multi-professional teams

### Target Learning Goals
- coordinated management of obstetrical emergencies
- team communication
- self-confidence
