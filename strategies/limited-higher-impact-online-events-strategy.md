# Replace an over-long online programme with a limited set of higher-impact public events run by small organising groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that the ten-week 2020 camp was too long, IWA restructured eIAYC 2021 into a more limited set of higher impact events: a seminar series of seven one-hour sessions every two weeks, a 36-hour hackathon in July 2021, and a virtual reunion weekend for former participants. This lets the team split into three small organising groups focused on each event while reaching a larger audience, especially young people who had not attended an in-person IAYC; sign-ups to eIAYC 2021 reached more than 400.
