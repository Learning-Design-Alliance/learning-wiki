# Deploy five affect-aware support bots matched to novice programmers' support needs in informal communities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose five automated, affect-aware Reddit bots aligned with the five identified support-need categories: ConceptBot (topic explanations), PathBot (learning paths), DebugBot (technical debugging help), SupportBot (emotional challenges), and CelebrationBot (achievement reinforcement). They state: "we propose five automated intelligent affect- aware bots aligned with the key support needs identified in our study." Each bot responds to textual trigger cues with context-sensitive interventions, and bots integrate with one another when issues reflect conceptual misunderstanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- novice programmers seeking help in informal online communities such as r/learnprogramming

### Target Learning Goals
- timely emotional and cognitive support aligned with learners' emotional states
