# Integrate happiness training into teacher professional development programs to reduce burnout and support teaching effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that educational institutions adopt positive psychology-based training models within professional development, stating "Integrating happiness training into professional development programs for teachers could significantly reduce burnout and improve teaching effectiveness". It further suggests policymakers prioritize teacher well-being, since healthier teachers create more supportive learning environments that may benefit student engagement and outcomes. The recommendation rests on the study's own pretest–posttest–follow-up gains and narrative themes.
