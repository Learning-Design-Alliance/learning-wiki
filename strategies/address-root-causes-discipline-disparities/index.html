Identify and address the root causes of discipline disparities in schools flagged as disproportionate¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that, given persistent disproportionality in exclusionary discipline, educators and policymakers work to "identify and address the root causes of these discipline disparities". It supports this by applying the MSDE definition of disproportionality to administrative data to identify and describe disproportionate schools, giving practitioners a way to locate schools where disparities concentrate before intervening.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administrative school discipline, enrollment, and student demographic data sufficient to apply the state's disproportionality definition and identify disproportionate schools.

Constraints¶

  • The snapshot analyzes Maryland data through 2017/18 and does not itself evaluate any intervention to reduce disparities.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in Maryland public schools, particularly Black students and students with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reducing exclusionary discipline (out-of-school suspensions and expulsions) and eliminating subgroup disproportionality in school removals

Related Strategies¶

  • Restorative Circles
  • Empathic Discipline
  • Restorative Approaches
  • Political Education
  • Restorative Practices
  • Replace exclusionary discipline with restorative practices and implement data-driven attendance monitoring
  • Address digital well-being with empathy, root-cause understanding, asset framing, and tech-free spaces
  • Districts should track rates of lost instruction from suspensions and conduct recurring public reviews of discipline disparities
