# Select modular tutoring system lessons and games most relevant to the target writing task

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When using a modular intelligent tutoring system for a task it was not originally designed for, select the subset of lesson videos and games most applicable to that task. The article notes that "the modular aspect of the Writing Pal affords selecting videos and games based on the nature of the task", and the study selected nine videos and three games from four modules, with the majority of lessons discussing evidence because source-based writing requires selecting appropriate evidence from sources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The tutoring system must be modular, allowing educators to utilize only the modules they deem necessary
#### Constraints
- The range of games appropriate was limited because many games rely on the player having seen all of the lessons in the module

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- source-based writing
- evidence selection and integration
