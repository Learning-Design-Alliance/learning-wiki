# Deliver infographic-based CPR education through a messaging group with scheduled nightly content and discussion time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The infographic intervention sent educational content on CPR skills as infographics to a Telegram group over four nights at 8 PM each evening, with an hour for discussion and questions, administered by the first author. The 8 PM timing was chosen based on the nursing students' suggestions. This strategy pairs visual content delivery with immediate peer and instructor discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A messaging platform group, an administrator, and scheduled content releases with reserved discussion time.
#### Constraints
- Timing and format were tailored to nursing students' schedules and suggestions in this single-institution study.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- BLS knowledge retention
