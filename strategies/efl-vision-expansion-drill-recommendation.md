# Incorporate vision-expansion speed-reading drills into EFL instruction and teacher training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that EFL instructors motivate learners to use vision-expansion drills to raise reading speed, and that policy-makers embed such drills in teacher training courses. They argue the drills "can be very handy for college-going students" and help learners "save time and achieve greater success", based on the 39 percent speed gain with no comprehension loss observed in their experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Drills such as Drills A to N from Cutler (1993) and training sessions for learners
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted in the Saudi EFL context with a small group of 50 students of above intermediate proficiency; the authors call for testing on larger groups, over longer durations, and with different proficiency levels

### Target Learners
- EFL undergraduate students
- college-going language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reading speed improvement in English as a foreign language
