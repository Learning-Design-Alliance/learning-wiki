# Replace board-work by a few selected students with device-based submission of all students' problem solutions for whole-class real-time feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In a high school Algebra I class, the instructor had students perform their work on Tablet PCs and submit solutions, reviewing all answers from the class to fix misconceptions before students leave class and practice on their own at home. Previously only "2 or 3 students" showed solutions on the board due to time constraints. Projecting selected submissions lets the class compare alternate approaches and realize there may be multiple correct mathematical methods within Algebra I.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student needs a pen-enabled device for submitting handwritten problem solutions
- The instructor must quickly review all submissions during class
