# Deploy DeepSeek in medical education as a virtual mentor, PBL case tool, and adaptive question bank

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's practical contributions recommend institution-level ways to integrate DeepSeek into medical education: building socially interactive AI platforms such as a virtual mentor system for role-playing in group learning with immediate feedback, embedding DeepSeek into problem-based learning modules where instructors upload anonymized real-world case data for students to generate diagnostic hypotheses, and using it as an adaptive question bank that dynamically adjusts question difficulty based on response history. It states: "a virtual mentor system based on DeepSeek could be developed to simulate real-time clinical scenarios through dialogue, enabling students to engage in role-playing during group learning and receive immediate feedback to enhance collaborative learning outcomes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support for socially interactive AI platforms and instructor involvement in curating case data
#### Constraints
- The article notes DeepSeek's interactive features may not fully reflect the common characteristics of other AI systems, and the sample is primarily Chinese medical students, limiting external validity

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- self-efficacy
- exam preparation
- collaborative learning
