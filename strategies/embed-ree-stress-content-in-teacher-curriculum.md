# Embed REE stress management content in the religious education curriculum so students acquire stress-management skills for themselves and their future students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend "a stress management intervention strategy with an REE-driven curriculum" because the activities of schools are directed by the curriculum. They state that "the religious education program curriculum for undergraduate students could be improved to include REE stress management contents," furnishing students with skills for managing stress as future teachers of religious studies and preparing them to pass such skills on to their own students. The recommendation rests on the trial evidence that REE reduced and sustained lower stress in participants.
