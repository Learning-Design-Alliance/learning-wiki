Student-led conferences in which students present their cumulative work, reflect, and set goals with families and advisors¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Gateway holds student-led conferences midway through the school year. The report states these conferences "allow students to formally share their cumulative work across the semester with their parents or guardians and teachers" and that they "help students build agency and self-advocacy while also encouraging self-reflection and metacognition." Advisors scaffold the presentations with sentence stems, and students highlight what they are proud of, what they found challenging, and how they have grown.

Context¶

Requirements¶
