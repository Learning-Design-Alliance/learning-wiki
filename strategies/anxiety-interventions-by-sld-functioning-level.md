# Tailor anxiety interventions to SLD functioning level: test-anxiety reduction for high-functioning students, coping and self-perception support for typical-functioning students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends differentiating support by functioning level within SLD. It states that "for typical-functioning SLD students, interventions focusing on maintaining their adaptive coping strategies and fostering a positive self-perception may be particularly beneficial", while "for high-functioning SLD students, interventions aimed at reducing test anxiety may be more appropriate". This follows directly from the group-specific mediation patterns the study found.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators or clinicians must be able to recognize the spectrum of functioning within SLD, since the authors note these classifications may not be easily used formally in practice.
#### Constraints
- The classifications may not be easily used in practice formally; the study is cross-sectional and relied on self-report measures, precluding causal inference; SLD subsample sizes were small.

### Target Learners
- Chinese undergraduate students with high-functioning SLD
- Chinese undergraduate students with typical-functioning SLD

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing anxiety-related barriers to academic performance in reading and general university coursework
