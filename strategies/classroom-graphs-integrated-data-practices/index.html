Integrate data and analysis into existing K-2 teaching practices through classroom graphs such as birthdays and word walls.¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The webinar recommends embedding data practices into routines primary teachers already run, rather than adding standalone lessons. Its 'Classroom Graphs (birthdays, word walls, etc.)' segment frames everyday classroom artifacts as data displays. It states the primary-grade focus is "How to teach data through your current teaching practices" and "How to integrate data and analysis into various content areas."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Existing classroom routines (e.g., birthday charts, word walls) that can be rendered as graphs

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-2 primary grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • data collection and representation across content areas

Related Strategies¶

  • Use a culture-themed children's book to generate data for K-2 glyph and graph activities.
  • Integrate computational thinking into existing disciplinary teaching across grade bands rather than adding it on
  • Integrate AI literacy across grades and subject-area learning rather than confining it to electives
