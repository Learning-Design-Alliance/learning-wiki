# Cultivation of Understanding and Respect Across Languages and Dialects

## Description
Using translations, descriptions, movement, and images to support learning in unfamiliar or complex languages. This involves providing learners with multiple ways to access and understand content in different languages and dialects. It promotes cultural awareness and respect for linguistic diversity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Translations, descriptions, movement, images, simplified language, bilingual resources, and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to accurate translations and culturally appropriate materials. Some learners may feel uncomfortable speaking in a new language.
