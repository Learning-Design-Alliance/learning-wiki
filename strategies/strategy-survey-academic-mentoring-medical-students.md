# Use learning-strategy survey findings to guide academic mentoring and learner support in medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that examining students' learning strategies can provide a practical basis for academic mentoring: "Academic monitors may use this information, particularly in the early phases of medical training, to guide students in developing appropriate learning-management skills and in selecting learning strategies that align with the educational demands of the medical curriculum." This positions strategy assessment as an implementable mentoring activity grounded in the study's survey instrument.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A survey instrument capturing learning strategies and study behaviors, such as the study's 20-item questionnaire
#### Constraints
- The findings are exploratory rather than causal and come from a single institution with a low response rate

### Target Learners
- medical students in early phases of training

### Target Learning Goals
- learning-management skills
- selection of effective learning strategies
