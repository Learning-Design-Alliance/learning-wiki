# Structure faculty development programs around the eight competencies to improve outcomes and resource efficiency

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends identifying the eight competencies as the foundation for planning teacher development programs, since the authors believe the competencies "can be developed and trained". It notes that in the healthcare field, faculty development programs are rarely structured around competencies, and that prioritizing these competencies can enhance effective use of financial resources. The review also proposes recognizing these competencies during recruitment, before hiring, and enabling professors to self-assess and seek constant development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding the competencies is needed to design programs with measurable outcomes, reinforce teaching adherence, optimize enhancement timelines and reduce institutional costs
#### Constraints
- The authors note no studies have assessed the effect of training most of these competencies in health education teachers

### Target Learners
- healthcare faculty
- education managers and institutional leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- teacher development toward educational excellence and better student outcomes
