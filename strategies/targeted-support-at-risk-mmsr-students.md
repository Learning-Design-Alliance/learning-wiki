# Target male, older, low-SES, and non-Greek-home-language students for individualised motivational and metacognitive support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend identifying students in the minimal motivation and metacognitive self-regulation profile and providing differentiated support to demographic groups at elevated risk. They write that "emphasis should be placed on maintaining male students' motivation and metacognitive self-regulation" and suggest "providing individualised assistance to students from low-SES backgrounds and those who do not speak Greek at home to prevent them from becoming unmotivated, metacognitively unregulated, and, subsequently, low-performers".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers able to identify students with minimal motivation and metacognitive self-regulation
#### Constraints
- Grounded in observational profile associations, not causal evidence, as the article notes

### Target Learners
- male adolescent students
- students from low-SES backgrounds
- students not speaking the school language at home

### Target Learning Goals
- motivational beliefs
- metacognitive self-regulation
- prevention of underachievement
