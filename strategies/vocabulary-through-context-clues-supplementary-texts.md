# Teach vocabulary through context clues in supplementary reading passages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article advocates using supplementary text passages to develop EFL learners' vocabulary by teaching vocabulary through context. This means looking for clues in the sentence that might tell the reader something about the meaning of an unfamiliar word. The article cites Walters (2006) reporting improved reading comprehension when ESL students were shown strategies for deriving meanings of unfamiliar words from context clues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supplementary reading passages containing unfamiliar words in meaningful sentence contexts
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence cited is a small study of 11 ESL students aged 17 to 47 reported by Walters (2006), not the present study's own data

### Target Learners
- Iranian female second-grade high school EFL learners
