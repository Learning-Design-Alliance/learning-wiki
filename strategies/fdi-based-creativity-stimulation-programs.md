# Develop creativity stimulation programs for young children that build on the field independence cognitive style

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that, because FDI is critical in the link between children's Gf and DT, "stimulation programs aimed at promoting DT based on the field independence cognitive style could be developed since the early ages." Knowing that boys' higher field independence increases DT is described as useful to tailor such programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs target the field independence cognitive style as the lever for promoting divergent thinking
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and cannot support causal claims about program effects

### Target Learners
- school-age children (ages 6–10)

### Target Learning Goals
- divergent thinking
- analytical thinking
- creativity
