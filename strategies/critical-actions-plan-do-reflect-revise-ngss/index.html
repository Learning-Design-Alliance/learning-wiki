Eleven critical actions executed through a Plan-Do-Reflect-Revise cycle¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Domain 2 enumerates eleven critical actions leaders take to implement the NGSS: aligning policies, allocating funding, assessing the system, building a shared vision, designing and implementing professional learning, developing communication strategies, engaging families and key stakeholders, focusing on equity and accessibility, forming implementation teams, selecting and aligning curriculum and assessments, and using data and research to monitor and improve. The article specifies that "Leaders implement all of these critical actions using a continuous improvement cycle of Plan-Do-Reflect-Revise."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Actions are informed by the six leadership knowledge areas and focused on the six system drivers.

Constraints¶

  • Actions must be continuously informed by data on their influence on system drivers and progress toward sustainable implementation.

Target Learners¶

  • Education leaders at state, district, and school levels implementing the NGSS

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Planning and executing standards implementation
  • Continuous improvement of implementation plans

Affordances¶

  • Four Domain Framework Leading Ngss Implementation

Related Strategies¶

  • Design professional learning for leaders using a 5E learning cycle
  • Action Research
  • Evaluate & Reflect then Sustain & Scale: analyze pilot data against targeted goals and share results with critical partners to plan expanded use
