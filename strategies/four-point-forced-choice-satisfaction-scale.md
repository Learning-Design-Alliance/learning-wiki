## Description
The article assessed participant satisfaction using a 4-point Likert scale (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Agree, Strongly Agree). This scale was "intentionally chosen to eliminate a neutral midpoint, thereby encouraging participants to provide more definitive feedback and facilitating clearer interpretation of satisfaction levels". It is a concrete evaluation instrument for simulation-based training programs.
