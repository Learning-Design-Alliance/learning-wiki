# Support teachers with in-service training and positive self-efficacy for implementing 5E inquiry activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's implications section recommends building teacher capacity for 5E inquiry implementation. It notes that "Science teachers usually have difficulties in organising inquiry-based teaching activities in their classes and in forming the stages of the 5E teaching model", so in-service training may be offered. It further recommends that teachers themselves hold positive attitudes and high self-efficacy toward 5E and inquiry activities, and that teacher-education curricula be redesigned so prospective teachers frequently implement such activities. It also suggests real-life examples and small-group work to hinder misconception formation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- hindering misconception formation and memorisation-based perceptions of science
