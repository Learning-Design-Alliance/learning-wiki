# Refine team-building guidelines and pre-class materials when implementing FC-TBL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend two concrete refinements for future FC-TBL implementations: improving how learning teams are composed and improving pre-class materials. They state they "plan to further explore and refine team-building guidelines in our teaching practices to enhance motivation" and aim to "fine-tune pre-class materials, such as videos and micro-courses, to better engage students". This responds to their finding that GPA-only grouping omitted communication and collaboration skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators willing to iterate on group composition criteria and pre-class media over an extended period, as the authors note optimization will necessitate sustained, dedicated efforts
#### Constraints
- The article notes that essential skills such as communication, presentation and collaboration were not factored into the original GPA-based group composition criteria, and that the type and duration of video materials were not thoroughly explored

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- student motivation and engagement in pre-class preparation
- teamwork skills
