# Reuse a well-designed remote escape room across courses and successive course editions to amortize its high creation effort

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends treating a remote educational escape room as a reusable asset: "the escape room is easily reusable in another course on the same topics or in successive editions of the same course and this way many students can benefit from its advantages". This offsets the creation effort, which the authors estimate at 30-40 hours for the escape room versus 8-12 hours for an equivalent lab session. Remote delivery also lets students participate at their preferred timeslot and retry the experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A good design and integration with the rest of the course materials and activities; the escape room must address topics shared across the courses or editions in which it is reused.
#### Constraints
- Creating the escape room in the first instance is complex and requires notably higher teacher time and effort than traditional hands-on activities such as computer lab sessions.

### Target Learners
- higher education students in database and data engineering courses

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement and motivation in database courses
- knowledge of database concepts
