# Allowing Think-Time and Re-Reading

## Description
Allowing plenty of think-time and time for re-reading provides students who need more time to process information the space they need to fully understand the material. This supports deeper comprehension and reduces frustration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexibility in pacing and scheduling. Creation of a supportive and non-pressured learning environment.
#### Constraints
- May require adjustments to the overall pace of instruction. Can be less effective if students do not use the time productively.

### Personalization
