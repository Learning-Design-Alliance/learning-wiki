# Use learner-led open-ended debriefing after each standardized patient encounter

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After each completed patient encounter, the small group and faculty expert entered the exam room for a 5-minute feedback session using "a learner-led, open-ended framework". The learner first self-assessed what went well and what could be improved, then the standardized patient gave feedback from the patient's perspective, followed by peer and faculty feedback and review of the performance checklist. A whole-group 20-minute debrief on key takeaways concluded the session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A debriefing guide and a completed performance checklist to structure faculty feedback
- 5 minutes per encounter plus 20 minutes for the whole-group session
