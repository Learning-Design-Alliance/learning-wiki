# Use a consultancy protocol in which each leader presents a problem of practice for structured peer discovery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Collaborative used a consultancy protocol in which "each leader had the opportunity to invite the team to think expansively about a specific family engagement problem of practice." Through a structured process, the team engaged in rigorous discovery discussion that supported the presenting leader in developing next steps, developing leadership while generating actionable guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured process
- A peer team willing to engage in rigorous discovery discussion
- A named problem of practice brought by each leader
