# Schedule mid-term interviews with experts and affected lay people to steer the social impact analysis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Within CDS, two interview instruments are scheduled by mid-term. Experts are invited by the teaching staff to act as mentors during 2 hours discussing the device and potential innovative solutions, providing a highly technical perspective on the device's maturity and potential. Each group must also find and schedule an interview with a lay person affected by the Challenge, such as a nurse, doctor or patient for a Health challenge, enriching the challenge from a social, non-technical perspective.
