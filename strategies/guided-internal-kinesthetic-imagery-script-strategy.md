# Two-minute guided mental imagery script with internal perspective and kinesthetic emphasis immediately before a procedural simulation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a brief, supervisor-led imagery session delivered immediately before the simulated procedure. Supervisors "read aloud a standardized script on PVC insertion to participants with their eyes closed", with the script "designed to promote an internal perspective (first-person view) while emphasizing kinesthetic sensations". It requires only a script and supervisor time, making it a low-cost addition to simulation workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized script adapted for the target procedure; participants have seen an instructional video beforehand to equalize baseline knowledge
#### Constraints
- The article notes the control group did not perform an attention-matched neutral task, so benefits may partly reflect enhanced immediate focus

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students
- novice and non-novice learners of invasive procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural skill performance in peripheral venous catheter insertion
- mental readiness and procedural confidence
