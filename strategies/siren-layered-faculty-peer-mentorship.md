# Layered mentorship combining faculty advisors with near-peer mentors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
SIREN assigned each student team a faculty advisor and a peer mentor with prior research experience, a layered mentorship approach that "combined faculty guidance with peer support." Advisors directed research goals, technical development, and project milestones, while peer mentors offered day-to-day support, helped troubleshoot technical challenges, and fostered collaboration within and across teams. The article credits this dual approach with reinforcing skill development, encouraging autonomy, and promoting a sense of community.
