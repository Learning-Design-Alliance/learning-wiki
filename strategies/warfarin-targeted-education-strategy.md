# Target warfarin education through structured programs, interactive e-learning modules, regular workshops, and case-based training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend targeted educational interventions to close the specific knowledge gaps the survey documented, particularly for high-risk drug and food interactions. They state that "Future initiatives should focus on structured educational programs, introducing interactive e-learning modules, regular workshops, and case-based training, as well as promoting multidisciplinary teamwork to enhance nurses' competency in warfarin counseling and patient care." The regression findings support this by linking health education exposure and anticoagulant training to higher knowledge scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured educational programs and confidence-building initiatives, since self-confidence was a significant positive predictor of knowledge
#### Constraints
- The study is limited by reliance on self-reported data and a convenience sampling approach, which may impact generalizability

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses managing patients on anticoagulant therapy in hospital settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Knowledge of warfarin–drug and warfarin–food interactions and competent patient counseling on warfarin therapy
