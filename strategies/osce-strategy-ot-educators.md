# Use OSCE as a strategy to enhance occupational therapy students' clinical skills and prepare them for practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Occupational therapy educators and clinical mentors should consider using OSCE as a strategy to enhance occupational therapy students' clinical skills and prepare them to become competent occupational therapists." OSCE provides "a risk-free environment for learners to practice skills as well as make decisions," encouraging students to try new things and learn from errors, and serves as a stepping stone for transitioning to real occupational therapy practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-designed OSCE with clinical stations simulating real clinical situations; expert educators and clinical mentors for supervision and feedback
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-university pre-post study in Saudi Arabia; the article notes limited external validity and calls for studies at more institutions with more simulated scenarios and extended timeframes

### Target Learners
- occupational therapy university students in clinical-based courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical competency skills
- professional identity development
- self-efficacy
