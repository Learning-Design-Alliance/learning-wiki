# Use A/B/n testing embedded in natural learning environments to evaluate EdTech design features at scale

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends A/B/n testing as particularly suitable for evaluating different designs of educational technology tools 'situated within authentic education contexts'. A key advantage is its ecological validity, 'as tests are carried out in natural learning environments and can be embedded within the curriculum, allowing the educational experience to continue after the test ends'. This approach enables large-scale implementation without disrupting regular teaching activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment at the school or user level, with only one design feature varying between partitions.
#### Constraints
- Remote data collection limits the ability to pinpoint teachers' classroom behaviours when interacting with the dashboard.

### Target Learners
- Learners in early-years classrooms in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating the comparative impact of educational technology design features on learning outcomes
