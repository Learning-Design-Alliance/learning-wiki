# Policy recommendations for protecting clinical education during health system reforms: protected teaching time, aligned evaluation, teaching-linked compensation, and embedded educational objectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on participants' accounts, the authors recommend that health system reforms explicitly consider educational priorities rather than assuming service-oriented improvements automatically support education. Recommended considerations include "Protecting dedicated time for clinical teaching within faculty workloads", aligning performance evaluation systems to acknowledge both service and educational roles, recognizing teaching contributions within compensation and incentive structures, and embedding educational objectives within policy design and evaluation frameworks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional and policy commitment to aligning incentive and evaluation structures with educational roles
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from faculty perceptions at a single university with a relatively small sample; the article notes findings are based on perceptions rather than objective educational outcomes

### Target Learners
