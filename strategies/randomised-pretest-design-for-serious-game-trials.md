# Design future Serious Game versus traditional teaching trials as randomised studies with pre-tests and learning-time data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 4 studies (4 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that, given the established non-inferiority of EMERGE, "future studies should be randomised in order to decrease validity threats". They further advise including a pre-test to separate previous performance from intervention-attributable learning, gathering data on self-directed learning time outside sessions, comparing the type and amount of feedback across formats, and using an assessment format unfamiliar to both groups (e.g. standardized patients) to avoid software-familiarity advantage.
