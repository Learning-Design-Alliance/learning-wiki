# Trainee-selected, program-screened and trained mentors sustained through monthly staff contact after residential programming

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the residential period, each trainee selects a mentor who is then screened and trained by the program. The mentor is supposed to meet with the trainee a minimum of four hours per month during the one-year post-residential phase, while program staff maintain at least monthly contact with the trainee and mentor. This strategy extends program support into the community transition period when dropout risk remains high.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentors must be screened and trained by the program; minimum four hours of mentor contact per month; staff maintain at least monthly contact with trainee and mentor
#### Constraints
- The mentoring phase lasts one year following the residential period

### Target Learners
- Youth ages 16 to 18 who have dropped out of or been expelled from high school

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement and completion of high school diploma or GED after residential programming
