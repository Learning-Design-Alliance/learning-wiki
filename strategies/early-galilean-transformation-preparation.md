# Emphasize Galilean transformation early to prepare students for the conceptual leap of SRT

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Some researchers cited by the review recommend teaching Galilean transformation before SRT, arguing that "a stronger emphasis on Galilean transformation would help students to appreciate the profound consequences of the principle of relativity". Because Galilean transformation involves no counterintuitive concepts, it can be learned at an early age, and the later incompatibility of the light postulate with it should lead students to recognize the necessity of a new theory of relative motion.
