# Emphasize scientific and engineering practices — modeling and conducting experiments — so students understand and apply core science content rather than memorize it

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that with NGSS adoption, high school trajectories become "more engaged" by "delving into modeling and conducting experiments to completely understand the content knowledge and apply it to everyday problems." Allocating time to the rationale behind core sciences is presented as "an imperative part of keeping students engaged in the sciences and to foster natural inquiry." The authors state the hope that scientific and engineering practices will be further emphasized under NGSS.
