# Scaffolding

## Description
Scaffolding is a support technique to help learners manage their responses in an emotionally safe context. It involves providing temporary support tailored to the learner's needs and gradually removing it as they become more competent. Scaffolding in self-regulation might involve providing structured prompts, modeling self-regulation strategies, or breaking down tasks into smaller, more manageable steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires awareness of individual learner needs and emotional triggers, ability to provide tailored support and guidance, a safe and supportive learning environment.
#### Constraints
- If scaffolds are not gradually removed, learners may become dependent on them. Requires careful observation and adaptation.
