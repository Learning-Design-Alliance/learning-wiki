# Provide sample keywords and prompt guidance so students ask short, simple questions to the AI tool

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During the pilot, observations revealed that students had difficulty asking questions to the AI. The authors responded by embedding sample keywords on the web page and instructing students "that they should ask short, simple questions (prompts) to the artificial intelligence and that they could get better answers in this way." A user manual and instructional videos were also distributed to ensure proficiency with the platform. This strategy operationalizes the AI component of the learning environment and mitigates initial difficulties in forming effective prompts.
