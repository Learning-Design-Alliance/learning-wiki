# Efficiently test school report card design changes at scale before adopting them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents its study as a template for education agencies considering changes to school report card design: rather than adopting a design on intuition, policymakers can run comparative usability tests of design variants and measure usability, understanding, findability, and satisfaction. The study "illustrates how policymakers and practitioners in other states can efficiently test school report card design changes at scale."
