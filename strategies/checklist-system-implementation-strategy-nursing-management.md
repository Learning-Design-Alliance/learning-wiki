# Adopt the checklist-based competency system in pre-employment and ongoing professional development with resource and leadership support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that "Hospital administrators may consider incorporating this system into pre-employment training programs for new nurses and establishing it as a core tool for ongoing professional development among in-service nurses." They specify two supports: resource support, including expert panels to adapt and localize the checklist and standardized head-nurse training, and leadership support elevating the system to a strategic level in quality and safety management. Integration with routine quality control and performance evaluation is proposed for sustainability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resource support (expert panels, standardized head-nurse training) and leadership support from nursing management are stated as necessary for successful implementation.
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-center quasi-experimental study; the authors recommend multicenter investigation before broad generalization.

### Target Learners
- new nurses in pre-employment training
- in-service critical care nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- ongoing professional development
- continuous learning and self-improvement
