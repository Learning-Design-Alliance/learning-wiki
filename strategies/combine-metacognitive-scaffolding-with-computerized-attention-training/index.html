Combine metacognitive scaffolding with computerized process-based training of executive attention to boost cognitive and neural benefits in young children¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy pairs social scaffolding and metacognitive strategies with computer-based process-based training of executive attention. The article notes that "no prior studies have examined the effect of combining these two training strategies" and tested the combination with typically developing preschoolers at cognitive and brain levels. The authors conclude that "metacognitive scaffolding boosts the influence of process-based training on cognitive efficiency and brain plasticity related to cognitive control."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires a computerized process-based training protocol for executive attention plus adult scaffolding that embeds metacognitive strategies during training

Constraints¶

  • Evidence comes from typically developing preschoolers; the abstract does not establish generalization to other ages or populations
