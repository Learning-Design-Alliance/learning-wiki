# Connecting New Vocabulary to Texts

## Description
This approach involves integrating vocabulary instruction directly with texts to enhance understanding and retention. It includes identifying key words within a text (Tier One, Tier Two, and Tier Three), providing brief explanations, and selecting words that align with the themes and concepts of the text. Teachers also consider students’ existing knowledge and choose words that offer more precise or complex ways of expressing ideas they already understand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to appropriate texts, teacher preparation to identify and explain key vocabulary, and instructional time for vocabulary activities.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of words to avoid overwhelming learners, and instruction must be engaging and meaningful to ensure retention.
