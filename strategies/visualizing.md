# Visualizing

## Description
Visualizing involves creating a mental image or representation of a problem or text to aid in understanding. This can include drawing a picture, diagram, table, or other representation to help solve the problem.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to tools or materials for creating visual representations, such as paper, pencils, or software.
#### Constraints
- May require additional support for learners who struggle with visualizing abstract concepts.
