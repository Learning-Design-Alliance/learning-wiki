# Shadowing to Address Behavior Problems

## Description
Shadowing can be used as a direct intervention to address behavior problems in the classroom. An adult shadow closely follows a child exhibiting harmful behaviors (e.g., biting) to prevent incidents before they occur. The adult stays within arm's reach, providing constant direct supervision without drawing special attention to the child. This approach aims to break the cycle of negative behavior and keep the classroom safe while teaching the child alternative strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained adult shadow, administrative and parental support, and clear protocols for intervention.
#### Constraints
- Requires dedicated resources and staffing, can be labor-intensive, and may not address the root causes of the behavior.
