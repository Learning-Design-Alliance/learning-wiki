## Description
The article recommends that universities address student well-being holistically even when well-being initiatives do not directly improve GPA. It states that "Universities should consider implementing targeted mental health services, stress management programs, and physical activity promotion initiatives tailored to student needs." The recommendation rests on the high observed prevalence of low physical activity and psychological distress in the sample rather than on demonstrated GPA effects.
