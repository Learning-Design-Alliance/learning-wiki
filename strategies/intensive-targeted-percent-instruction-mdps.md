# Provide intensive, targeted instruction on percent problem solving for students with mathematics difficulties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study, `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that interventions for students with MD-PS include intensive instruction on percent content, because percent treatment effects were not sustained at delayed posttest and treatment did not moderate growth over time. As they state, "This could include intensive instruction (i.e., small group instruction, sustained and elaborated instruction) for students with MD-PS to master this content." The recommendation responds directly to the study's null and non-sustained percent findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction that is sustained and elaborated rather than brief, delivered in small groups where possible
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a six-week intervention in which percent effects were not maintained at delayed posttest; the authors note more intensive attention to percent problem solving and the supports students require to sustain gains over time may be needed

### Target Learners
- Seventh-grade students with mathematics difficulties in problem solving

### Target Learning Goals
- Percent problem solving within proportional reasoning
