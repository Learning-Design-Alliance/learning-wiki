## Description
The brief situates its findings for educators and families weighing school entry age, noting that an early advantage of entering older may fade in later grades. The article's framing — "New research suggests an early advantage may fade in later grades" — implies decisions about delayed entry should rest on growth trajectories through the early grades, not only entry-year status.
