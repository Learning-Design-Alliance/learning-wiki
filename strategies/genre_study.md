# Genre Study

## Description
Genre study is an approach to teaching literature that involves exploring the characteristics and conventions of different literary genres. It typically involves students reading a variety of texts within a specific genre, discussing the elements that define that genre, and often writing their own pieces in that style. This method promotes a deeper understanding and appreciation of literature by helping students recognize the unique features of various genres and how they shape the reader's experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a variety of books representing different genres. Printable bookmarks, genre characteristics handouts, book review handouts, website access
#### Constraints
- May require significant time and resources to gather materials. Can be challenging if students lack foundational reading skills. May need to address misconceptions about genre categories.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Identify literary elements of common genres, experience and develop interest in literature, improve comprehension by interpreting, analyzing, synthesizing, and evaluating written text to categorize text into literary genres

### Affordances
- Enhances reading comprehension and analytical skills. Develops an appreciation for diverse literary forms. Improves writing skills through genre-specific practice. Connects in-school literacies to out-of-school experiences.

### Personalization
- Teachers can select genres relevant to students' interests and cultural backgrounds. Students can choose books within a genre that appeal to them. Assignments can be adapted to suit different learning styles.

## Assessment Evidence
- Student participation in discussions, completion of book reviews and bookmarks, ability to identify genre characteristics in texts, quality of contributions to group charts

## Impact
- Research indicates that students who engage in genre studies have a better understanding of literary elements and are more successful in writing across different genres. Genre studies also promote cognitive development by allowing students to apply similar skills and strategies across various types of literature.

## Examples
- Students are assigned a genre to explore, use printable bookmarks to record evidence that their book fits the assigned genre, complete a book review, and share summaries of the books they read with their classmates. Conducting studies of multiple genres can help students achieve a better understanding of their characteristics.
