# Vary reflection timing between in-the-moment and future-focused reflections

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the reflect step, students engage in reflections at different time scales. The guide recommends "in-the-moment" reflections such as exit tickets to capture immediate insights, alongside retrospective or future-focused reflections such as data-driven goal setting to examine the learning progression over time. This variety lets students capture both immediate insights and longer-term progression.
