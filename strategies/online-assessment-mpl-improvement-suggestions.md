# Improvements to online assessment and MPL drawn from student feedback: equation editors, detailed quiz feedback, pre-posted video lectures and more project guidance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on student feedback, the authors propose concrete modifications for future MPL and online-teaching implementations. As printed, "The online quizzes can be improved with the help of equations’ editors and training the students on how to use them"; quiz feedback should be more detailed, chapter-referenced and immediate, supported by a large question pool; video lectures should be posted in advance so live sessions allow questions; and the mobile application project should come with more guiding materials to strengthen self-learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large pool of questions should be provided to avoid students who finish the exam earlier sharing questions and answers with other students
#### Constraints
- Students had concerns with interaction due to online assessment and teaching

### Target Learners
- senior-level undergraduate engineering students in online or pandemic-disrupted courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clearer conceptual understanding from online quizzes and feedback, more interactive live sessions, and stronger self-learning in project work
