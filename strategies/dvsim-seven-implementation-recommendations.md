# Seven implementation recommendations for educators deploying virtual environments in teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors give educators seven recommendations: bridge gaps in traditional clinical training when live patient access is limited; adopt blended learning with pre-simulation preparation and post-simulation debriefing; replicate real-world clinical settings with responsive avatars and adaptive scenarios; expand exposure across multi-therapeutic contexts such as pediatrics, geriatrics, and mental health; collaborate with practicing professionals to align content with best practices; use tiered difficulty levels; and continuously refine simulations with student and instructor feedback. The article recommends educators "adopt a blended learning approach by integrating pre-simulation preparatory materials (e.g., readings, case reviews) and structured post-simulation debriefings".
