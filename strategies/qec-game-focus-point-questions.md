# Use guided focus-point questions during play to direct attention to redundancy–noise trade-offs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article closes with a non-exhaustive list of focus points educators can pick from, framing questions that should "help build an intuitive sense of the stakes of error correction in com munication channels and the interplay between information redundancy and noise levels." Questions probe why the Noise rolls a die rather than swapping automatically, what happens if the Receiver can observe whether marbles were exchanged, how changing the Messenger-to-Noise ratio or the Noise Value affects each team, and, for the adversarial variant, whether higher Noise Values are always good.
