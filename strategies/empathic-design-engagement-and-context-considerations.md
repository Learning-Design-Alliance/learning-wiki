# When applying empathic design, consider delivery mode (synchronous and asynchronous), four dimensions of learner engagement, and three features of learning context

## Description
Respondent Xie extends empathic design with considerations for digital delivery. The editorial reports that Xie "highlights both synchronous and asynchronous delivery modes while also introducing considerations for applying empathic design to learner engagement (i.e. behavioral, cognitive, affective, and social) and learning context (i.e. physical environment, technological, and social features)". Designers should therefore analyze engagement and context dimensions when empathically designing for digital learning.
