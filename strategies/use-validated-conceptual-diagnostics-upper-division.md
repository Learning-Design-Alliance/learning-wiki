# Use validated conceptual diagnostics alongside traditional exams to assess upper-division course transformation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend research-based conceptual assessments as complements to traditional exams, which generally target calculational skills while the assessments are largely focused on conceptual underpinnings. Such an assessment allows comparisons across academic years and institutions, provides instructors insights into conceptual difficulties, and helps assess pedagogical reforms. The CURrENT itself is the working example, and the authors report it is discerning measurable differences between traditional and transformed courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- upper-division physics students in junior-level electrodynamics

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring conceptual understanding and the effectiveness of transformed pedagogy
