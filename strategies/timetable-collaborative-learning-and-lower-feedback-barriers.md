# Timetable collaborative learning into the internship and redesign feedback mechanisms to lower the barrier to commenting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implications section proposes three practical directions for clinical teaching bases: timetabling collaborative learning, redesigning feedback collection, and targeting preceptor training at participatory skills. It states collaborative learning "could be timetabled into the internship itself (e.g., a fixed weekly discussion slot)" and that feedback collection could be separated from assessment with written responses after each round.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires institutional scheduling capacity to embed fixed collaborative sessions within rotation schedules
- Requires a feedback mechanism that separates feedback collectors from intern assessors and provides visible follow-up
#### Constraints
- Recommendations derive from a descriptive, cross-sectional, single-region, all-female associate-degree sample; the scheduling explanation for low participation remains untested

### Target Learners
- Final-year associate-degree nursing students on clinical internship

### Target Learning Goals
- Collaborative learning and teamwork skills
- Quality of clinical teaching and student feedback processes
