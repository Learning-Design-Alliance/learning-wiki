# Incorporate life-meaning reflection and spiritual care training into nursing intern management to strengthen competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that schools and hospitals design targeted interventions to improve nursing intern students' spiritual health, including "life meaning reflection and spiritual care training courses," aiming to cultivate good interpersonal relationships, clarify life and career goals, and help students correctly understand themselves. The rationale is that spiritual health strengthens the implicit foundation of ability and thereby promotes core competency development.
