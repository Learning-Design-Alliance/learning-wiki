# Establish team ground rules—such as listening to others' opinions and controlling tone—to encourage discussion and team functioning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends creating an environment that encourages student discussion during team activities by establishing "ground rules," defined as "shared rules established within a team to perform tasks." Specifically, it suggests rules such as listening to the other party's opinions and controlling the tone of voice during discussions. It states this helps the team function effectively, prevents free riders, and improves team performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicitly shared rules agreed upon within the team before or during task work
#### Constraints
- Grounded in the interpersonal culture of one Korean medical school cohort

### Target Learners
- Medical school freshmen in team-based classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Participation in discussion and effective team functioning
