# Rotate students’ written questions among peers so they assess and discuss them to feed the dialogue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
As a second implementation purpose, the article proposes that students’ written questions "could be rotated among their peers in the small working group". Students assess their peers’ questions, search for misconceptions, and discuss these in small groups to feed the dialogue and elaborate on their thinking. The article states this approach is not time consuming for tutors and is suitable for large cohorts.
