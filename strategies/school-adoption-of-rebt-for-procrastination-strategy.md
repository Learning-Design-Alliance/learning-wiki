# Schools should adopt REBT through trained counselors and teachers to reduce student academic procrastination

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' practitioner recommendation: "Teachers and guidance counselors could strive to adopt REBT which is capable of reducing academic procrastination of in-school adolescents." They urge that "adequate provisions should be made by the government and educational authorities for the regular application of rational emotive behavioral therapy by experienced educational psychologists and guidance counselors," and that Secondary School Boards "should recruit additional counselors to schools who could assist in preventing academic procrastination among students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experienced educational psychologists and guidance counselors trained in REBT
- Government and educational authorities providing provisions for regular REBT application in schools
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in a single quasi-experimental study in Nigerian secondary schools

### Target Learners
- in-school adolescents in secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- reducing academic procrastination
- engaging actively in academic tasks

### Affordances
- Rebt Disputing Irrational Beliefs Framework
