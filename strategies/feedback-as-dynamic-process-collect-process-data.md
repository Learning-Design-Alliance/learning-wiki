# Conceptualize feedback as a dynamic process and collect data that directly investigates that process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second step urges researchers to treat feedback not as a static treatment but as an unfolding process, and to gather data aimed at that process itself. The article states "it is essential to conceptualize feedback as a dynamic process and collect data that directly investigates this process". As an agenda item it implies longitudinal or within-process measurement designs rather than pre-post outcome comparisons alone.
