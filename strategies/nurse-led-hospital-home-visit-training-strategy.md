# Nurse-led multi-session training combining a hospital session with repeated home visits and follow-up phone calls

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention delivery strategy combines a 40–50 minute hospital training session with home visits of approximately 30–45 minutes after discharge, plus 8–10 phone calls per participant to plan visits and address questions. "The researcher asked each participant 12 questions that were prepared based on the content of the training, and behavioral changes were investigated. If deemed necessary, training was again provided."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by a trained nurse researcher; home visits within the patient's locality; telephone contact to schedule and follow up
#### Constraints
- Home visits and physiological follow-up were limited by the autumn–winter season, ending BMI and waist circumference measurement in early autumn

### Target Learners
- patients with coronary artery disease discharged from a cardiology clinic

### Target Learning Goals
- behavioral change in CAD self-care
- control of modifiable risk factors
