# Blend VSO with offline practical teaching using the BOPPPS model for high-risk laboratory skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe: combine offline scenario-based practical teaching with online VSO reinforcement using the BOPPPS model. Students form groups before class, complete pre-class VSO or video assignments with online preparation checks, present challenges encountered, then receive in-class instruction addressing those issues followed by guided practical projects. The authors recommend that "future improvements should focus on enhancing simulation fidelity, implementing tiered teacching design, adopting blended learning approaches, and incorporating emergency scenario simulations" to improve skill transferability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the VSO platform with adequate technological infrastructure
- group formation and pre-class online preparation checks
#### Constraints
- Generalizability is limited to institutions with adequate technological infrastructure and learners with comparable baseline laboratory skills; effectiveness in non-laboratory scenarios remains unvalidated

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical laboratory technology students

### Target Learning Goals
- pathogen isolation and culture skills
- laboratory biosafety and aseptic awareness
