# Allow Think-Time and Re-reading (Processing Speed)

## Description
Allowing plenty of think-time and time for re-reading for students who might need more time to process. This strategy involves providing extended pauses for reflection and multiple opportunities to review content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of student's processing needs, flexible pacing, and intentional pauses during instruction
#### Constraints
- May require adjustments to lesson planning, some students may not use the additional time effectively, and might not address underlying processing speed challenges

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Support processing speed, improve comprehension, and promote thoughtful reflection

### Affordances
- Reduces time pressure, allows for deeper processing, promotes self-regulation, and supports individual learning pace

### Personalization
- Think-time and re-reading opportunities can be tailored to student needs, with options for structured reflection prompts and varied review methods.
