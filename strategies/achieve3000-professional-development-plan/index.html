Description¶

Achieve3000's implementation model pairs the digital program with site-specific professional development. The article states that "Achieve3000 works with each customer to develop a plan that identifies the targeted students and outlines professional development and implementation services." This makes planning of targeted student identification and PD services part of program adoption.
