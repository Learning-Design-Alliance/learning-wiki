# Teachers can make desirable peer norms salient by explicitly acknowledging students who express mastery goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that peer achievement goals function as descriptive norms that shape individual achievement goals, the article recommends that teachers harness social norms to support mastery goal adoption. The concrete activity is for the teacher to acknowledge explicitly, in class, when one or several students express mastery goals, thereby making those desirable peer norms salient to the whole class so that classmates treat them as descriptive standards of what is typically valued.

## Design Implications
