# Include library representatives on curriculum and budget committees to align board prep resource decisions with student needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on interview findings, the article recommends formal library representation in governance. Directors stressed "having formal representation on curriculum or budget committees was crucial to ensuring alignment between student needs and resource decisions," and partnerships such as advisory task forces helped "turn annual budget battles into collaborative processes." Libraries excluded from governance learned of decisions only after they were made.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal governance structures that include library directors in budget or curriculum decision-making
#### Constraints
- Findings are limited to COM institutions and self-reported director perspectives

### Target Learners
- medical students in colleges of osteopathic medicine programs

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained institutional access to board exam preparation resources
