# Repeat exposure to word meanings via a digital dictionary instead of rereading the entire story when the goal is vocabulary enrichment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
For vocabulary enrichment, the article suggests that repeated activation of a word's dictionary meaning within one story reading may be sufficient, without rereading the whole story. The authors write that "there is no need to repeat the entire story reading several times, and perhaps repeated exposure to the word's meaning by the dictionary will be sufficient for this purpose", noting that short and focused repetition may require fewer cognitive resources and that full rereading can be demanding and boring for some children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The e-book must include a dictionary that provides oral word explanations that children can reactivate
#### Constraints
- If the goal includes story comprehension, the authors state it is necessary to test whether this strategy is suitable; this was not examined in this work

### Target Learners
- Young children, especially those with low language and literacy ability such as children from low socioeconomic status

### Target Learning Goals
- Expressive vocabulary (explaining new words)
