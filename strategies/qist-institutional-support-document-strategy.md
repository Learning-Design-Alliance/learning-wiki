## Description
To win administrative support, the article recommends demonstrating program benefit with evidence and building lasting structures. It recommends "the development of a PowerPoint p resentation or "glossy" PDF with employment ﬁgures and other data" developed closely with industry, supplemented with localized pilot data. At the departmental level the motto is "just do it": use free teaching slots or non-approval programming to demonstrate that QIST can be popular, and put systems and structures in place that outlast individual faculty.
