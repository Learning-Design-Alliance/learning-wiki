# Social Awareness Instruction with Current Events

## Description
This strategy involves using current events and fictional characters to explicitly teach social awareness. Students learn to understand different perspectives, empathize with others, and discuss social issues, including social justice. This activity directly connects learning to real-world applications and enhances learner autonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to current events news and a teacher to facilitate discussion. Can be integrated into social studies, language arts, or advisory periods.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality of the selected news articles and the teacher's ability to guide discussion. Some students may be sensitive to certain social issues.

### Personalization
- Current events can be selected to match students' age, interests, and cultural backgrounds. Teachers can provide background information and scaffolding to support students who are unfamiliar with social issues.
