# Prioritize dental trauma education in the nursing curriculum, delivered by dentists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends adding dental trauma education to nursing students' formal training, because surveyed students' knowledge of diagnosis and treatment of dentofacial injuries was insufficient and 70% were willing to attend an educational program. The authors state that "dental trauma should be prioritized in the curriculum" and that an educational program "can be provided by dentists" to enhance knowledge of students and nurses working at emergency departments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education should target management of dental traumatic injuries, particularly avulsion, transport and cleaning procedures
#### Constraints
- The article recommends additional education during and after formal education; it did not itself test an educational intervention

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- nurses working at emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- emergency management of dental traumatic injuries, including avulsion, storage media and cleaning procedures
