# Use exposure-based desensitization to numerical symbols to address the number-processing pathway of math anxiety

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the number-processing pathway, the authors propose exposure-based therapy in which individuals are repeatedly exposed to numerical information so that they "become desensitized to numerical symbols". They point to Supekar and colleagues' finding that children with high MA showed lower amygdala activity after an intensive math tutoring program as a promising approach in this direction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Repeated, structured exposure to numerical symbols and tasks over time
#### Constraints
- The desensitization mechanism is the authors' proposal; the supporting tutoring evidence is from a cited prior study with children, not tested here

### Target Learners
- children with high math anxiety
- adolescents and adults needing desensitization protocols

### Target Learning Goals
- symbolic number processing fluency
- reduced anxiety responses to numerical stimuli
