# Design structured, competency-based, stage-specific transition programmes for new nurse managers with mentoring by experienced managers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structured training programmes for newly appointed nurse managers, tailored to career stage and individual needs, including mentoring and coaching by experienced nurse managers and academic staff. It states that "Orientation and ongoing training programmes, including mentoring and coaching by experienced nurse managers and academic staff, will facilitate smoother role transitions". Programmes should be hierarchical and differentiated by career stage, delivered in-house, face-to-face and short-term, with flexible modular online follow-up for longer-term development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training should be customised according to individual needs and integrated into the organisational culture
- Trainers should have academic knowledge and field experience
#### Constraints
- The article notes further research is needed to examine the effectiveness of specific training interventions

### Target Learners
- Newly appointed nurse managers with 0–24 months of managerial experience

### Target Learning Goals
- Managerial competencies in unit management, personnel management and care management
- Risk management, leadership, human resources management and conflict management
