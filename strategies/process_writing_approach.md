# Process Writing Approach

## Description
Includes many related activities, including a greatly increased quantity of writing (only some of which is completed to publication) and a focus on writing throughout the course, along with mini-lessons on embedded skills. Modeling of writing by instructors. It is a professional development model as well, and results seen in students’ writing are correlated to teachers’ training in the approach. Instructional activities of sentence combining and inquiry are part of the approach.
