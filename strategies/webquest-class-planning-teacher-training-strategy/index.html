Integrate WebQuests into teachers' class planning with teacher training¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend, in light of their results, that technologies such as WebQuest become "a part of teachers' class planning" and that the Jordanian ministry of education "make use of computers in schools appropriately", while noting that "teachers needs training for this type of technology". The strategy treats WebQuests as a vehicle for affective-domain objectives, since the article notes the focus on the cognitive domain may shift attention away from developing values and attitudes.
