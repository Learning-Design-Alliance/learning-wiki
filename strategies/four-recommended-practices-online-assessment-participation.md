# Use four recommended practices — multiple email reminders, multiple in-class announcements, and participation credit for pretest and posttest — to maximize online assessment participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article identifies four practices instructors can use to motivate students to complete online low-stakes assessments: "multiple email reminders, multiple in class announcements, participation credit for the pretest, and participation credit for the posttest." In the second semester all instructors were advised to use them, and interview confirmation of use was included in the model. The practices were focused on improving CBT participation and were modeled as a cumulative count from 0 to 4.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must actually implement the practices during the semester; the article confirmed use via instructor interviews
#### Constraints
- Practices were modeled only for the CBTs, not the paper assessments
- The context may not represent physics classes at other institutions

### Target Learners
- introductory physics undergraduates

### Target Learning Goals
- complete low-stakes research-based assessments (concept inventories and attitudinal surveys)
