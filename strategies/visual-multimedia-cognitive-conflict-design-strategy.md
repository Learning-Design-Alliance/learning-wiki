# Align educational media design with students' visual learning preferences by embedding interactive visual elements and cognitive conflict

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructional designers and educators "adopt a learner-centered approach that embeds these strategies into technology-based learning tools", meaning generative learning with cognitive conflict. Materials should be structured to promote analysis, evaluation, and logical reasoning, and media design must align with the prevalent visual modality by incorporating interactive visual elements that support cognitive engagement. The strategy is a forward-looking recommendation grounded in the survey's needs analysis, not an action tested in this study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article states the study is limited to a descriptive survey within a specific regional context; the strategy's impact on learning outcomes remains unmeasured
