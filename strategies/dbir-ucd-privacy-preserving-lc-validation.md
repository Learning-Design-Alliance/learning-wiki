# Co-design and validate context-aware AI through DBIR blended with user-centered design in privacy-preserving enclaves

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's application, testing, and validation strategy is to bring stakeholders together to co-design the LC and implement it in AI instructional tools using a design-based implementation research approach combined with user-centered design. "we propose a design-based implementation research (DBIR) framework blended with user-centered design (UCD)." Testing occurs by integrating LC-based AI tools into the OpenStax Assignable platform and studying engagement and outcomes via SafeInsights, with students and instructors engaged as active partners, particularly on ownership, hosting, and the privacy–informativeness tradeoff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engage a diverse group of stakeholders—particularly students and instructors—as active partners in a User-Centered Design process
#### Constraints
- All research and pedagogical interventions are conducted within SafeInsights' privacy-preserving data enclaves; the LC presents significant privacy risks that must be addressed intentionally

### Target Learners
- K-12 and college learners reached through the OpenStax platform's national reach

### Target Learning Goals
- Validating that context-aware AI supports deep learning and reduces equity gaps
