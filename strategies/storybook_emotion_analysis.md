# Storybook Emotion Analysis

## Description
This strategy involves using storybooks to explore and discuss the emotions of characters. Learners guess how characters are feeling based on the narrative and visual cues, then discuss the reasons behind these emotions. This activity promotes empathy and helps learners understand the connection between events, feelings, and expressions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of storybooks; facilitator to guide discussion; classroom setting or reading space
#### Constraints
- Requires careful book selection to ensure emotional content is appropriate; discussions may be superficial without skilled facilitation
