# Augment existing standard laboratory experiments with smartglasses rather than building new setups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose that "The basic idea of a holo.lab scenario is to augment existing standard experiments widely used in laboratory classes in Physics", noting "This has the advantage of being easily transferable also to other laboratories at diﬀerent universities." The strategy retrofits established apparatus with sensor feeds and holographic overlays instead of replacing the experiment, and the authors expect "more complex experiments will beneﬁt even more from augmentation."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of new sensors into the software framework and availability of smartglasses
#### Constraints
- The article names the programming effort for integrating new sensors and "the relatively high price for the Development Edition of HoloLens" as limiting factors for rapid implementation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in physics laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- connecting experimental measurements with theoretical principles in real time
