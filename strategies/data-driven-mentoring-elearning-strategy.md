# On-site personalized mentoring based on healthcare workers' own consultation data, eLearning programs, and continuous monitoring of CDSA data trends to track skill development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendations for improving clinical skills of CDSA users. It states that "Strategies for skill improvement, such as on-site personalized mentoring based on HWs' own data and direct consultation observations, as well as eLearning programs, are crucial", and identifies the 'companionship' model of strong, regular supervision as the best approach, though largely absent at primary care level in Africa. It also proposes giving a supervising person at the district hospital access to a dashboard with the retrospective data-pattern comparisons, so nurses in a flagged health center can be pointed to instructional videos or hands-on refresher training, and highlights continuous monitoring of data trends to assess the impact of interventions such as eLearning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- eLearning success depends on fulfilling key conditions, such as active engagement, adequate technological support, and integration with hands-on practice
#### Constraints
- eLearning programs have often yielded results below expectations in Africa; the companionship supervision model is largely absent at primary care level in Africa

### Target Learners
- healthcare workers in primary health centers
- district health management teams

### Target Learning Goals
- improving accuracy of clinical measurements
- sustaining high-quality clinical skills over time
