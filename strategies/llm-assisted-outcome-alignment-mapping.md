# Elicit an outcome tree from a domain expert, then generate outcome–item alignment mappings with an LLM and have the team verify them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For non-formal e-learning systems that lack formulated outcomes, the article's procedure is to facilitate explicit outcome formulation by a domain expert starting from the system's highest-level goal, reusing existing domain structures, then map outcomes to verifying items with a language model: 'we used an LLM to produce the initial alignments', with the mapping further checked and edited by the product team.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A domain expert formulates the outcome tree, and the product team reviews and edits the LLM-generated mappings.
