# Use ANN-based adaptive feedback to provide scalable, process-oriented real-time feedback at scale in higher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 theoretical, 1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends automatic adaptive feedback in simulations as a way to give large student cohorts elaborate feedback that would otherwise require instructor effort. It states that such feedback offers "scalable, elaborate, process-oriented feedback in real-time to high numbers of students in higher education". The strategy is implementable in simulation-based learning environments where learner responses can feed a neural network that generates adaptive feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation-based learning environment; artificial neural network infrastructure for automatic feedback generation
#### Constraints
- In the reported study the benefit appeared for justification quality, not diagnostic accuracy

### Target Learners
- Pre-service teachers in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
