# Intensive Instruction/Active Engagement

## Description
Intensive instruction involves active learner engagement and plenty of time on task. Students learn more when they are active, that is, not just listening or watching, but applying "focused, sustained effort on the content or task. Intensive instruction requires a high degree of learner attention and response and frequent instructional sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to design engaging activities and provide frequent opportunities for practice. Requires allocation of sufficient time for active engagement.
#### Constraints
- May be challenging to maintain high levels of engagement over extended periods. Some learners may require additional support to stay focused.
