Integrate AI literacy education as an integral component of first-year curriculum development rather than an add-on¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that AI literacy education be built into first-year curricula as a core component, because the mediation results indicate course support translates into psychological benefits through students' AI proficiency. The authors state that "AI literacy education should be an integral component of first-year curriculum development, rather than merely an add-on tool training," and suggest teachers enhance AI mastery through demonstrations, clarified task requirements, explained guidelines, and progressive AI learning activities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor capacity to provide AI demonstrations, clarify task requirements, and explain AI-use guidelines

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single-institution cross-sectional self-report study; the authors call for longitudinal and multi-institution studies before firm policy recommendations

Target Learners¶

  • First-year university students in AI-integrated courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI proficiency including tool use, information verification, and understanding AI guidelines
  • Academic adjustment and sense of competence

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed AI literacy across disciplines by identifying intersections with existing content and using case studies, interdisciplinary modules, and collaborative projects
  • Integrate AI into nursing curricula through AI-supported case discussions, structured clinical learning activities, and AI literacy training tailored to user groups
  • Sequence the legal translation curriculum from human from-scratch translation to AI-assisted workflows, with strategic AI literacy from the outset
  • Integrate career adaptability cultivation and AI literacy with career planning in higher education curricula
