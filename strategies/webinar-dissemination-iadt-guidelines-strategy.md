# Use webinars for widespread dissemination of dental trauma guidelines to practising dentists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes webinars as the dissemination vehicle for the IADT guidelines, especially under pandemic conditions where regular direct contact between teachers and learners is restricted. It argues that "webinars are interactive, easy to use, they reach a large audience, are repeatable and are inexpensive, if compared with traditional courses". Accordingly, the Italian societies SIOCMF and SIMEUP developed a webinar on dental trauma in the pediatric population, made freely available online.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional sponsorship and an online platform hosting the webinar (SIOCMF and SIMEUP websites in the article)
#### Constraints
- Proposed in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic, when regular direct contact between learners and teachers is not possible

### Target Learners
- practising dentists
- dental undergraduate and postgraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of emergency management of avulsed teeth
- adherence to IADT guidelines
