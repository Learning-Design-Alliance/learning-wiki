# Future research agenda for meta-debriefing: context-specific frameworks, enablers and challenges, cultural influences, and meta-debriefer faculty development needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes "4 main areas for future research" because the science and art of meta-debriefing itself remain underexplored and current understanding is derived mostly from collective experience and evidence extrapolated from the debriefing literature. The areas are: developing specific meta-debriefing frameworks that take varied professional, cultural, and environmental contexts into account; seeking meta-debriefers' perceptions of enablers and challenges; researching cultural influences on meta-debriefing practice worldwide; and clarifying best practice for faculty development needs as practitioners transition from expert debriefers to meta-debriefers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article notes gaps: meta-debriefing practice and its recommendations remain underexplored, and the explicit role of meta-debriefing in fostering adaptive expertise is yet to be appropriately explored

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Building an evidence base for meta-debriefing practice
- Adapting meta-debriefing to local cultural and professional contexts
