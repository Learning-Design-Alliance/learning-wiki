# Incorporating Self-Testing

## Description
Reframing the concept of testing involves using frequent, non-graded tests as progress checks to ensure students don’t fall behind. Teaching students to self-test while learning new information helps them remember and use what they've learned, realizing when they need to study a topic in greater depth. This approach shifts the focus from grades to learning, reducing stress and promoting active participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources for self-testing (e.g., quizzes, practice problems), time for self-testing during class.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset from grades to learning, may not be suitable for all learning styles.
