## Description
The review proposes Facebook usage guidelines that educators can adopt to encourage social media adoption in their teaching. The guidelines are offered as a practical output of the review, intended to help educators use Facebook as an educational instrument while managing the limitations the review identifies. As the review states, "Facebook usage guidelines that can be adopted by educators to encourage social media adoption are proposed."
