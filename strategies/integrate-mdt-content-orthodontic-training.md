# Integrate TMJ, myofunctional, and periodontal-orthodontic multidisciplinary content into residency training and target continuing education at undertrained practitioners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose reducing observed competency gaps by embedding multidisciplinary content — TMJ assessment protocols, myofunctional therapy techniques, and periodontal-orthodontic combined treatment approaches — into orthodontic residency programs, and by prioritizing high-quality continuing education for private-practice and primary-care clinicians with limited access to advanced training. This recommendation follows directly from the finding that TMJ and myofunctional competencies scored significantly lower than other domains and that educational attainment was the strongest predictor of composite competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residency training programs and continuing education providers able to incorporate multidisciplinary (TMJ, myofunctional, periodontal) content
#### Constraints
- Recommendation is based on findings from a Chinese cross-sectional sample with non-probability sampling; the authors call for further validation across diverse populations and healthcare systems

### Target Learners
- Orthodontic residents
- Private practice clinicians
- Primary care practitioners

### Target Learning Goals
- TMJ assessment
- myofunctional therapy techniques
- periodontal-orthodontic combined treatment competencies
