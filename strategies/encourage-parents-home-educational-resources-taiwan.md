## Description
The article proposes that educators and policy makers in Taiwan improve eighth-grade math achievement by working through the home: "they can encourage parents to purchase more educational resources for children and improve the home learning environment". This follows from the finding that home possessions and parent education significantly predicted achievement. The article also recommends future research on how parents' education background affects achievement so that appropriate supports or interventions can be implemented.
