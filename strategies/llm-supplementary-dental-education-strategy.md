# Use LLMs as a supplementary educational tool in dental education and exam preparation, with reliability evaluated per specialty

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that large language models serve as an auxiliary resource in dental education and exam preparation, based on their measured accuracy on DUS oral pathology questions. It concludes that "LLMs can serve as an important auxiliary tool in dental education and exam preparation", while cautioning that "the levels of reliability, currency, and clinical relevance of the models be evaluated more comprehensively in future studies". Because different models excelled on different question types and topics, the article advises that an AI program may not be equally useful across all fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluate the reliability, currency, and clinical relevance of the chosen model for the intended specialty before use
#### Constraints
- Performance was measured only on Turkish-language DUS oral pathology questions; the article states inaccuracies from language translation may have contributed to errors, and generalization is limited by the question set

### Target Learners
- dental students
- dental specialty trainees preparing for the DUS examination

### Target Learning Goals
- oral pathology knowledge
- clinical decision-making skills
