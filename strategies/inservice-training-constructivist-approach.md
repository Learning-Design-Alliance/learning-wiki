# Provide in-service training for teachers in the constructivist approach to support knowledge retention

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because the constructivist approach failed to secure retention in control group 2, the article suggests "there may be some drawbacks in implementation of constructivism" and that "teachers need some in-service training." It recommends teachers receive in-service training about the constructivist approach's basic philosophy and steps, since lack of such knowledge may explain the retention failure.
