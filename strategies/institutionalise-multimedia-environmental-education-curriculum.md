# Institutionalise multimedia-based environmental education from elementary through higher secondary levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's educational implications call on institutions, funders, teachers and policy makers to embed multimedia-based environmental education across schooling. It recommends that "environmental education should be included in their curriculum for the learner's long-term benefits", that educational institutions organize workshops and conferences to develop multimedia study materials on environmental issues, and that teachers use recent models of teaching and innovative instructions in environmental education classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding agencies providing funds for such programmes and activities
- Teachers, students, educationists, policy makers, curriculum framers, and administrators working at the grass root level
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single quasi-experimental study with no control group in one Indian locality

### Target Learners
- Elementary to higher secondary students
- Pre-service teachers
- University students

### Target Learning Goals
- Environmental sustainability awareness and eco-friendly citizenship
