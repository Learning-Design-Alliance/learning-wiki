# Complete a full design journey with divergent-then-convergent tools to build creative confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The aptitudes (design doing) stage builds participants' creative confidence by equipping them with design tools deliberately selected from a wider toolset, starting with divergent thinking tools to explore the problem space and closing with convergent tools to establish team consensus. To experience the impact of design thinking, participants must 'complete a full design journey, from uncovering problems to devising solutions'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A design thinking process must be followed, with tools deliberately selected from a wider toolset to fit different implementation and application contexts.
#### Constraints
- Benefit realizations differ according to designers' level of expertise, which the literature establishes as creating unique sets of characteristics distinguishing levels among individuals.

### Target Learners
- organizational participants without a professional design label
- participants in design thinking schools such as Potsdam and Stanford

### Target Learning Goals
- creative confidence and trust in one's own creative skills
- shifting from a static mindset to evolving mindshifts
