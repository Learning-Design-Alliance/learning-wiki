# Develop integrated domestic violence educational programs combining communication skills and ethico-legal content

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that medical educators develop integrated educational programs dealing with various cases of DV, integrating DV education with communication skills training and ethico-legal content. They state that "DV recognition and treatment is an integral component of medicine and should be taught in an integrated way, including patient safety, professionalism, ethics, laws, and communication skills". They also recommend giving students more experience encountering DV patients in clinics or simulated environments and focusing on learners' perspectives and the patient-centered interview.
