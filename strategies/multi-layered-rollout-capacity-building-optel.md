# Multi-layered, multi-year rollout combining guidance resources, webinars, workshops, and training-of-trainers to build local capacity for a new assessment tool

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The OPTEL rollout was planned as a multi-layered, multi-year endeavor whose initial priority was guidance that was compliant with law, clear and digestible, and informed by evidence. Supports included the OPTEL User Guide, FAQs, scripts for parent discussions, two listening sessions on LEA needs, spring 2024 statewide webinars, in-person workshops, and training-of-trainer materials, with sessions "designed to be highly engaging and practical for participants."
