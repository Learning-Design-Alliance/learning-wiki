# Support tertiary educators to dialogue safely with colleagues about student feedback and teaching failure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the conclusion the authors endorse Mackenzie's call to make "time and space for dialogue about our teaching failures", suggesting tertiary educators be "actively supported and encouraged to connect with colleagues with whom they can dialogue safely and constructively around student feedback". They argue this could support educators' wellbeing, normalise failure-based learning, and provide professional development that overcomes the solitariness of academic life.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional provision of time and collegial structures for dialogic engagement with student feedback
#### Constraints
- The authors note the "twin constraints of funding and time poverty" as challenges to implementing this strategy

### Target Learners
- Tertiary educators
- Early-career faculty

### Target Learning Goals
- Wellbeing in response to negative student evaluations
- Learning from teaching failure
