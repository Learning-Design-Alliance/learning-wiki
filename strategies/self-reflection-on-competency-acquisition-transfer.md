# Reflecting on one's own competency acquisition to support transfer into professional practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends building explicit reflection on competency acquisition into tutor training. "The reflection on one's own competency acquisition can support the promotion of corresponding competencies in the qualification programme and their transfer into the professional practice later." This is implemented in the fourth module through collegial advice, collegial visits, final reflection, portfolios and a final report, and the tutors themselves serve as models for implementing the CanMEDS roles.
