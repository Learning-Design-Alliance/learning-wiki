# Enrich feedback mechanisms with hierarchical taxonomies and mid-stream injection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that improving student-driven adaptation "will require both a richer, hierarchical feedback taxonomy and mechanisms to inject feedback more directly into the in-flight prompt (e.g., mid-stream updates)". Future work also includes "expanding the feedback schema, integrating tags mid-prompt, enriching user profiles with additional signals (e.g., past performance, sentiment)".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mechanisms to increase engagement beyond the sparse tag usage observed (about 9.5% of turns)
#### Constraints
- The current five-level tag set applied at the end of each turn lacked the granularity and immediacy needed

### Target Learners
- STEM students interacting with generative AI tutors

### Target Learning Goals
- Real-time personalization of AI responses
- student agency in shaping AI behavior
