# Implement a grading policy that rewards preparedness and engagement rather than correct responses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
Based on the author's experience that successful peer instruction depends on pre-class reading and in-class engagement, the study "a grading policy was implemented, which rewarded preparedness and engagement rather than the selection of correct responses". This removes the penalty for wrong initial answers, encouraging students to arrive prepared and to participate fully in discussion before and after the revote.
