# Approve the course through integrative final projects applied to concrete engineering problems of the local specialty and region

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposed approval method replaces generic assessment with "trabajos finales totalizadores, que sean de aplicación concreta", chosen according to the engineering specialty and the zone where the course is taught. Group projects exercise scientific research methodology (bibliographic search, state of the art, open questions, hypothesis generation). Regional emphasis at UTN San Nicolás includes Paraná river flood prognosis, pollutant dispersion in industrial complexes, and agricultural environmental problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects must require gathering information from specialized centres, such as river height series and specific ENSO and astronomical forcing indices.
#### Constraints
- Practical work should be adapted to the engineering specialties of the students and to the region where the course is delivered.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students of all specialties in a regional campus

### Target Learning Goals
- applying atmospheric physics to concrete regional engineering problems
- practicing scientific research methodology
