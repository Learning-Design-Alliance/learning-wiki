Support intervention teachers with biweekly Teacher Learning Community meetings alongside scripted curriculum materials¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

During the study, "Researchers conducted biweekly Teacher Learning Community meetings with intervention group teachers", at which researchers facilitated discussion of practices that worked well in previous lessons and aspects of the curriculum that were problematic. Teachers also received curriculum materials including detailed lesson plans, quasi-scripted lesson guides, overhead transparencies, worksheets, homework assignments, and all necessary reading materials. The curriculum itself was not modified as a result of these meetings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researchers or facilitators available to convene meetings biweekly
  • Full curriculum materials provided to teachers

Constraints¶

  • In the reviewed study the curriculum was not modified as a result of these meetings

Target Learners¶

  • intervention teachers delivering the vocabulary curriculum

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful implementation of vocabulary lessons

Related Strategies¶

  • Weeklong initial teacher training with biweekly follow-up meetings for delivering the fraction intervention
