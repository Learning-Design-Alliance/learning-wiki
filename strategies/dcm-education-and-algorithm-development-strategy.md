# Engage higher education providers and develop DCM training and a diagnostic algorithm for physiotherapists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends improving DCM education from the start of clinicians' careers and developing practical decision support. It states that “Engaging with higher education providers could improve awareness of DCM from the outset of a clinician's career”, and reports that participants “expressed a strong interest in the development of a DCM algorithm to assist with its assessment, diagnosis, and management”. Training needs concentrated on diagnostic clinical tests, assessment symptomology, screening questions and non‐surgical management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training content should target the areas respondents most frequently identified: diagnostic clinical tests, assessment symptomology, screening questions and non‐surgical management.
#### Constraints
- The article notes existing international guidelines are either poorly understood or deemed unhelpful by MSK physiotherapists, and evidence for non‐surgical management recommendations is weak.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physiotherapy students
- Early‐career and practising UK musculoskeletal physiotherapists

### Target Learning Goals
- Recognising and screening for degenerative cervical myelopathy
- Selecting and interpreting clinical tests for DCM
- Non‐surgical management and referral pathways for DCM
