# Structure cooperative learning tasks in heterogeneous groups to build social cohesion and student-to-student relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that teachers purposefully and carefully mixed students and used multiple-paired activities to maximise student-to-student interactions. Team building, heterogeneous teams, and cooperative games were a cross-nation feature of most observed HPE classes, with activities serving as "just a vehicle for learning another's social values and fostering social cohesion".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of students' group dynamics when dividing the class
- changing partners across activities to widen interactions
#### Constraints
- The authors caution that cohesion teaching must address social conditions that privilege and marginalise groups, not merely integrate

### Target Learners
- 13–15-year-old compulsory HPE students

### Target Learning Goals
- social cohesion
- cooperation
- personal and social responsibility
