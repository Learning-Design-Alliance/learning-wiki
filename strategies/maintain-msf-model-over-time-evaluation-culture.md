# Maintain the 360° MSF evaluation model over time to internalize an evaluation culture and minimize rating bias

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the multisource evaluation process be sustained across measurement cycles rather than applied once: "the assessment process should be maintained over time to familiarize participants with the procedure and thereby minimize the bias". Repeated use is expected to help participants internalize an evaluation culture, reduce inflated ratings, and reinforce reliable assessment that improves professional practice and patient care quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Management commitment and a communication plan informing authorities, supervisors, teams, and union representatives about the model
- An evaluation culture that reduces attitudinal barriers and phobias toward assessment
#### Constraints
- The study notes ceiling effects persist even with control measures such as randomly chosen raters based on interaction analysis

### Target Learners
- health professionals in hospitals undergoing continuous professional development

### Target Learning Goals
- nontechnical competency development and self-identification of learning needs through repeated multisource feedback
