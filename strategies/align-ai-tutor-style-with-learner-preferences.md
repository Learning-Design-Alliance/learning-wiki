# Align AI tutor response style with learners' personal learning preferences, e.g., via presets or learning preferences over time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that learners bring divergent expectations and preferences into AI-mediated education, the authors recommend that "Neuroadaptive and regular AI tutors, especially those intended for general audiences, may benefit from being aligned in style with learners' personal learning preferences, for instance, by offering presets or learning user preferences over time." This strategy treats response style (narrative versus structured, Socratic versus direct) as a designable parameter matched to the learner's disposition rather than fixed by the system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mechanism for capturing or eliciting learner style preferences (e.g., presets or preference learning over time)
#### Constraints
- Grounded in qualitative interview findings from one study; the article does not test preference alignment empirically

### Target Learners
- general-audience learners using AI tutors with divergent learning dispositions

### Target Learning Goals
- readability, engagement, and fit of AI tutor response style to learner expectations
