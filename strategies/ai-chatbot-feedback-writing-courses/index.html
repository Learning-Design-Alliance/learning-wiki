Provide timely automated feedback through an AI chatbot during EFL writing practice¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

This strategy embeds an AI chatbot into writing courses so that learners receive immediate machine-generated assistance on their drafts rather than waiting for instructor feedback. The article motivates it by the challenges of providing feedback on academic writing at scale and by evidence that ChatGPT-integrated instruction raised learners' writing motivation relative to conventional instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Writing assignments must be structured so that chatbot feedback can be obtained and acted on during drafting and revision
  • Students need orientation on prompting the tool and evaluating its suggestions

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • EFL university students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic essay writing
  • Revision skills

Claims¶

  • ChatGPT-integrated instruction produced significantly higher post-test writing motivation scores than conventional instruction among EFL learners, with a medium effect size [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Collaborative Human-AI Revision Design embedding prompt engineering across drafting, feedback, and rewriting phases
  • Integrate AI feedback as a supportive resource within process-oriented drafting cycles, with explicit guidance on critical evaluation
  • Revision Cycles
  • Embed AI in guided accept/reject/justify workflows, teach digital critical thinking explicitly, and integrate institutional authorship standards
