Strategies

Add explicit parental interview, data sharing, and weekly progress sharing steps to existing field-based assignments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Family Collaboration Project extended the existing Pupil Impact Assignment by adding three explicit components: interviewing the student's parent, sharing baseline data with the parent, and sharing progress with the parent at minimum weekly. The article reports that "more explicit direction to interview parents and systematically discuss data and student progress was needed to establish real parent–teacher candidate experiences."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A field-based assignment already in place
  • Mentoring teacher directed to support family connection

Constraints¶

  • Completed over eight weeks within one clinical seminar course

Target Learners¶

  • Special education teacher candidates

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building parent-teacher relationships
  • Professional communication with families

Claims¶

  • Explicit direction to interview parents and share data was needed because implicit direction to consider parental needs did not establish substantive parent interaction [+W]
  • Intervention group candidates rated advocacy for evidence-based practices significantly higher than comparison peers [+W]
  • Teacher candidates completing the Family Collaboration Project scored significantly higher on the joining (collaboration) subscale of the PTRS than comparison peers [+W]
  • Intervention group candidates rated maintaining positive relationships and ongoing collaboration significantly higher than comparison peers [+W]
  • No significant difference was found between groups on the communication subscale of the PTRS [-W]
  • Intervention group candidates provided more detailed narratives about collaborating with parents while comparison group candidates primarily mentioned classroom teachers [+W]
