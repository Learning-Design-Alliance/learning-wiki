# Collaborative Problem-Solving with Sticky Notes

## Description
Learners individually solve a problem on sticky notes, then share and discuss their responses in small groups. The groups then synthesize the best parts of each individual response to create a 'best possible answer' on a common page. This strategy encourages accountability, participation, and the defense of one's thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sticky notes, problem-solving pages, common space for group answers, time for individual work and group discussion
#### Constraints
- May require careful management to ensure equal participation and prevent dominant students from overshadowing others. Students may need guidance in providing constructive feedback and resolving disagreements.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Increases accountability by requiring individual contributions, promotes teamwork and collaboration, facilitates peer learning and support, encourages critical thinking through discussion and defense of answers

### Personalization
- Can be adapted by varying group sizes, adjusting the difficulty of the problems, or modifying the roles within the groups. Teachers can also strategically group students based on their individual needs and learning styles.

- Students improve their collaborative abilities and mathematical reasoning. The structured process helps students engage with the material more deeply and learn from their peers, leading to a more thorough understanding of the concepts.
