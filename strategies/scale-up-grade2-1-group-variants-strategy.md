# Scale up the grade-2 intervention using 1-group variants, and include small-group instruction in pandemic-response toolboxes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation from the trial results: "we would therefore deﬁnitely recommend a scale-up of interventions in grade 2 but would seriously consider only scaling up 1-group variants of the intervention, as the 1-1 variant is much more expensive." They further recommend rolling out scale-up with a design enabling further study, being more reluctant about grade-8 scale-up because impacts fade out, and policymakers considering the intervention for future pandemics since its small-group nature is robust to lockdown situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scale-up rolled out with a design that enables further study of the impacts of the intervention
#### Constraints
- Effects would probably still be smaller if implemented more broadly, due to sample selection as well as other issues

### Target Learners
- Low-achieving pupils in mathematics in grades 2 (and cautiously grade 8) in public school systems

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving mathematics competencies of the 20% lowest achievers
