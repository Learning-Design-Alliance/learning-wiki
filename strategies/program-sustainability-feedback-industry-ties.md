# Sustain a new program through stakeholder feedback structures and active industry partnerships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends mechanisms for keeping a new program relevant: a QIS program committee meeting at least once per semester with department representatives, a pedagogical advisor, the internship department and one student representative per cohort; monthly meetings with teaching staff to foster course cohesion; and dedicated industry partnership management. Following student feedback, some courses were removed and replaced or moved between semesters. Partnerships let faculty incorporate software development kits or libraries into courses and, in some cases, offer students exclusive access to quantum hardware.
