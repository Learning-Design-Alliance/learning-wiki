# Design generative production tools with originality feedback, divergence-promoting generation, and deliberate friction anchored to documented mechanisms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article anchors design implications to specific documented mechanisms rather than general principles: originality feedback addresses the invisibility of the output distribution, divergence-promoting generation targets the single shared generate-and-check script, and deliberate friction is positioned where the process data shows it would bite. The authors state that friction "can be funded by time the loop already spends idle," referring to the large share of generation time spent dead waiting. This is a forward-looking design recommendation, not a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the platform to see the distribution of users' artifacts, since only the platform sees it in the current regime
#### Constraints
- The implications are anchored to observed mechanisms in one course deployment; no intervention was tested in this study

### Target Learners
- users of vibe-coding and generative production platforms, including students and small-business builders
