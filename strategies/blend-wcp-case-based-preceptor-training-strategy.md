# Design blended preceptor education by combining web-based modules with face-to-face case-based learning and planning logistics in the initial design phase

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health care organizations and course developers prepare preceptors through blended programs that pair asynchronous web-based pedagogy modules with facilitated face-to-face case discussion. The authors advise: "For course developers, it is important to consider learning styles, learning needs, approaches, and possible platforms in the initial design phase, and logistic issues, such as Wi-Fi connection, servers, and physical classrooms, should be taken into consideration." They also recommend ongoing support such as recognition and compensatory systems to encourage preceptors to take up the role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Early involvement of target preceptors in development to identify learning needs and verify content
- Facilitators (nurse educators) for the case-based sessions
- Logistics: Wi-Fi connection, servers, and physical classrooms
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-institution, single-group study; the authors recommend randomized controlled trials in a variety of health care settings for stronger evidence

### Target Learners
- Registered nurse preceptors in acute hospitals, community hospitals, primary care, and higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Pedagogical readiness for preceptorship
- Clinical teaching and assessment skills
