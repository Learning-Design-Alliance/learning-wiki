# Recommend students read quality books as one possible way to improve mental health

## Description
In the Discussion the authors state that "One possible way to improve mental health for students may be recommend students to read quality books", positioning school counselors as key actors since improving students' mental health is a key concern for them. They also call for future studies on the effectiveness of reading quality books on students' other psychological characteristics, follow-up studies on durability of self-esteem gains, and studies at different educational levels.
