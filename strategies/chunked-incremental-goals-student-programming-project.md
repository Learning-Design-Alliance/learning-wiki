# Divide a demanding programming project into incrementally more complex chunks with small self-set goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
One intern reports managing frustration during the internship by setting small goals while learning Python, advising students who become discouraged "to divide their project into chunks and measure progress in a comparable manner". He progressed from a one-dimensional harmonic oscillator, to a planet orbiting a star, to a whole galaxy, finding that working on incrementally more complex and challenging goals made progress more visible and the project scope seem less overwhelming.
