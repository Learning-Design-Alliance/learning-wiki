# Instructors can facilitate activation and sharing of known shared resources when students do not spontaneously activate them

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's instructional implication is that instructors who pay attention to how students share resources can deliberately prompt resources known from the literature. As the authors put it, instructors "could facilitate both activation and sharing of known (from the literature) shared resources if students don't spontaneously activate them on their own." This builds directly on the shared-resources analysis of group problem solving in upper-level E&M.
