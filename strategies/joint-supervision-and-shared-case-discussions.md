# Interprofessional learning opportunities such as joint supervision, shared case discussions and collaborative learning forums support ANPs to develop expertise while benefiting from specialist knowledge.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy holds that promoting interprofessional learning opportunities such as joint supervision, shared case discussions and collaborative learning forums supports ANPs to develop their own expertise while benefiting from the knowledge of mental health specialists. These initiatives can bridge the confidence gap while fostering a team-based approach to complex care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Joint training initiatives involving ANPs and mental health specialists
- Multidisciplinary case reviews to improve decision-making and care coordination
#### Constraints
- Limited research on the long-term impact of ANP training programmes on clinical preparedness and patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- qualified advanced nurse practitioners
- mental health specialists
