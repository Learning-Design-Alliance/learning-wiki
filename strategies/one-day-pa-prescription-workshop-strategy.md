# Use short one-day lecture-plus-practice workshops as a continuing education strategy to build physicians' PA assessment, counseling and prescription skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is a free, one-day (8-h) certification workshop combining conference-style lectures with a hands-on practical session. "The workshop included a 4-h theoretical and 2-h practical sessions." In the practical component physicians in pairs practice physical examination, fitness tests, risk assessment, and write a standardized PA prescription for a colleague. The authors conclude this "short course is an effective continuing education strategy" for teaching PA assessment, counseling and prescription to MDs in Latin America.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content based on ACSM, EIM and international PA guidelines, delivered as three lecture modules plus supervised practical practice with a pre/post 20-question test for certification
#### Constraints
- Only immediate knowledge gain was evaluated; the impact on actual clinical practice "is unknown" per the authors, and a volunteer self-selection bias is possible

### Target Learners
-  practicing physicians (MDs) in Latin America, especially general practitioners and primary care providers

### Target Learning Goals
- physical activity assessment, brief behavioral counseling, and exercise prescription knowledge and skills
