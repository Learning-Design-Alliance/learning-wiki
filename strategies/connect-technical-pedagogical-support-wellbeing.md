# Support teachers' digital competence development through operationalisations connecting technical and pedagogical knowledge while protecting educator well-being

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The preface reports that institutions can support teachers' digital competence development through "practical operationalisations that connect technical and pedagogical knowledge, make digital possibilities across modes of delivery more explicit, and should also protect the well-being of educators." This responds to evidence that institutions commonly address technical issues while pedagogical support is often missing from training. The recommendation comes from a review of emergency remote teaching studies applied to Australian universities.
