# Use the platform's rubrics and checklists so students assess their own and peers' abstracts throughout the project stages.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The researcher organized and posted copies of attachments like checklists, rubrics and documents to Edmodo as they were needed for the participants to use. Each participant accessed the rubrics and checklists on Edmodo to assess the work of own, peers and small groups, and they were allowed to assess their own and other small groups' abstracts throughout the project stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher must procure copies of attachments like abstract checklist, peer review checklist, collaboration rubric, discussion checklist, and presentation rubric and post them to the platform as they are needed.
- Students must be trained on the criteria of using the abstract checklist to help them in writing their abstracts.
#### Constraints
- The attachments were used within a single Research Methods course on abstract writing, so their usefulness for other language tasks was not examined.

### Target Learners
- university EFL students

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed learning
- self-evaluation and peer review
