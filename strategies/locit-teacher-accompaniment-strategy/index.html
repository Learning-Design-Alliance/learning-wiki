Accompany content teachers with a CLIL-specialist teacher using the LOCIT structure of lesson observation and critical incident feedback¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

LOCIT (Lesson Observation followed by the critical incident technique) pairs content teachers with an expert CLIL teacher who observes classes and gives immediate oral feedback to guide pedagogical support. The article reports that this accompaniment methodology "was successful and allowed self-management of teach ing in class using a foreign language", letting teachers recognize strengths and weaknesses and continue reflecting on their practice through immediate oral feedback and personalized advice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A teacher specialized in CLIL available to accompany and observe content teachers
  • Agreed accompaniment with immediate oral feedback after observed classes

Constraints¶

  • Reported in the context of one institution's first-semester Nursing program with six teachers

Target Learners¶

  • Content teachers of higher education programs developing CLIL instruction

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher development in CLIL methodology
  • Self-management of teaching in class using a foreign language
