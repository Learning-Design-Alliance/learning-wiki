# Deliver theory-based oral health e-learning through pretravel orientation before long-term overseas assignments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a concrete delivery recipe for oral health education of overseas workers: before leaving for the assignment location, participants receive access to a theory-based e-learning program, with the leaflet distributed to both groups in the trial. Participants suggested that "the materials could be widely used by public organizations to disseminate information about oral health and include it in pretravel orientation, as in this study," alongside a request for an easy-to-access medium available at any time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access credentials (URL code and passwords) must be distributed to participants via e-mail before departure, as done in the study
#### Constraints
- Recruitment of eligible long-term overseas workers may be limited (in this study by COVID-19), and a longer posttest collection period is advised to account for time differences and internet availability

### Target Learners
- Japanese overseas cooperation volunteers and similar long-term overseas workers in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learning Goals
- oral health self-care prior to and during long-term stays abroad, including fluoride toothpaste use and prevention of dental caries and periodontal disease

### Affordances
- Imb Model Oral Health Elearning Framework
