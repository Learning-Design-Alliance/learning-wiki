# Increase guideline awareness through regular IADT guideline review, clinical decision support apps, and gamification features

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends that GDPs regularly revise the IADT guidelines and use resources such as the Dental Trauma Guide, and that guideline content be integrated into mobile applications. It states that "Integration of gamification features could be used to further enhance the educational features of such apps and promote continued learning by encouraging user engagement, enjoyability, and motivation", and that IADT endorsement of apps would ensure content reliability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing dental trauma applications must be endorsed by the IADT to ensure reliability of content, per the review.
- Continued translation of the IADT guidelines and DTG is required so all GDPs have equal access.
#### Constraints
- Existing dental trauma applications have been found to be of moderate to low quality, per the review citing Walia et al. 2024.

### Target Learners
- general dental practitioners
- newly qualified dentists

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of traumatic dental injury management in children
- confidence in managing traumatic dental injuries
