Incorporate language pedagogy methods such as suggestopedia into teacher-training curricula¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the study's findings, the author recommends that language pedagogy be incorporated into the curriculum of teacher training colleges and universities so that student-teachers benefit from the latest methods used in teaching English language. The recommendation rests on the study's result that suggestopedia improved listening skill relative to conventional methods, and on the article's diagnosis that conventional or traditional methods in Nigerian schools are not student-centered or humanistic.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher-training colleges and universities would need to add language pedagogy content covering current methods to their curricula

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation derives from a single quasi-experiment limited to two schools in Borno State, Nigeria

Target Learners¶

  • student-teachers in teacher training colleges and universities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • knowledge of current English language teaching methods, including suggestopedia

Affordances¶

  • Suggestopedia Method

Claims¶

  • Students taught listening with suggestopedia outperformed students taught with conventional methods on a posttest of English listening ability [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Recommendations: involve parents in listening education, balance all language skills, and add listening training courses to teacher education
