# Universities can encourage student physical activity through awareness campaigns, sports facilities, and PA offerings during examination periods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review's fifth future-direction recommendation targets practice: encouraging students to be more physically active "by awareness raising campaigns through lecturers and tutors as well as investments in the sports association and sports facilities at the campus". Following the Okanagan Charter, this approach is said to strengthen student health by forming long-term health habits, with concrete options including offering sports courses during the examination period and providing mobile health interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional investment in sports associations and campus sports facilities
#### Constraints
- Especially in high-stress examination phases PA decreases, so the review notes PA may not execute its potential as a stress-buffer for students during those periods

### Target Learners
- university students

### Target Learning Goals
- stress management and health habits supporting academic performance
