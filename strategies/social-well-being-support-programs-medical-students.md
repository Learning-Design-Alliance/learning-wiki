# University programs that strengthen students' social well-being to support academic motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's findings, the authors recommend that universities implement two categories of interventions: social well-being enhancement ("creating student support groups in universities, holding workshops on communication skills and emotional intelligence") and targeted motivational interventions grounded in a social approach ("pair mentoring systems between students with high and low social well-being", cooperative learning integrated into curricula, and community service participation). Policy-level recommendations include optional socio-academic skills courses, integrated counseling centers, and periodic social health monitoring.
