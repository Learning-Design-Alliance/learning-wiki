# Use accessible graphite-on-paper experiments as investigatively oriented activities in Electricity courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends an experimental activity whose ease of assembly supports student engagement and an investigative approach: "a facilidade de montagem do experimento, possibilita o maior engajamento dos alunos e converge para uma abordagem mais investigativa". Instructors implement it by having students deposit graphite layers, measure electrical quantities, fit linear models by least squares, and compare their thickness estimate with a reference technique where available. The authors state the relative simplicity allows adaptation for High School.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simple equipment (pencil, paper, multimeters, power supply); measurement and data-analysis tasks structured so students apply Ohm's Law themselves
#### Constraints
- The article notes earlier proposals for High School lacked statistical treatment consistent with Higher Education; overly closed scripts risk limiting reflection and creativity

### Target Learners
- Higher Education students in Electricity courses
- High School students (with adaptations)

### Target Learning Goals
- Consolidating understanding of voltage, current and resistance
- Connecting theory to everyday materials
- Developing scientific skills through investigation
