# Project-Based Learning (PBL) in Math

## Description
Project-Based Learning (PBL) is a teaching method where learners gain knowledge and skills by working for an extended period to investigate and respond to an engaging and complex question, problem, or challenge. In math, PBL involves tackling authentic, real-world problems, allowing learners to apply mathematical concepts in practical ways. Key components include real-world problems with no single solution, learners working in groups and being self-directed, and instructors serving as facilitators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engaging and complex real-world problems. Group work and self-directed learning. Instructors serve as facilitators. Access to technology and resources.
#### Constraints
- Requires significant planning and preparation. Can be time-consuming. May be challenging to align with specific standards. Relies on effective group work and self-direction.
