# Adapt GOSCE case scenarios to the specific sensory, cultural, and individual challenges of the target recruitment population

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend designing simulated recruitment cases around the specific barriers the target participants face, noting "this methodology is highly adaptable to a wide range of sensory, cultural, and individual challenges" and that "GOSCEs are highly adaptable to the specific recruitment needs of a clinical trial." Cases were developed from a literature review of facilitators and barriers to recruitment plus a focus group of five research staff, then revised until expert consensus was reached. The lowest-performing case (hearing impairment) illustrates how case choice surfaces remediation needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases developed from a literature review of recruitment facilitators and barriers and staff focus groups
- Expert review of cases until consensus is reached
#### Constraints
- Generalizability is limited by the sample and setting and potential limitations of the evaluation methods

### Target Learners
- research staff recruiting older adults to clinical trials

### Target Learning Goals
- structural competency
- culturally appropriate recruitment communication
