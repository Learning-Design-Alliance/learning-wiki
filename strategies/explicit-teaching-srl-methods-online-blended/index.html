Provide explicit teaching of SRL methods as structured guidance for regulating learning in online and blended settings¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that educators give students structured guidance on regulating their own learning through explicit instruction in SRL methods. As the authors write, the findings "highlight the need of giving students structured guidance on how to regulate their learning processes successfully through the provision of explicit teaching on SRL methods". The strategy treats SRL skills — goal setting, planning, monitoring, and strategy use — as teachable content rather than assumed prerequisites, and is grounded in the meta-analysis's moderate overall effect of SRL interventions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors or course materials must explicitly teach SRL methods rather than assuming students already possess them

Constraints¶

  • The article notes SRL intervention effectiveness may depend on course format and factors such as access to supplementary materials and technical infrastructure

Target Learners¶

  • online and blended learners across primary, secondary, high school, and undergraduate levels

Target Learning Goals¶

  • self-regulation of learning processes
  • improved learning outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Use computer-based instruction with observation, collaboration, and semi-structured guidance, plus online metacognitive guidance, to enhance SRL in mathematics
  • Teach less-proficient EFL learners metacognitive strategies, effort regulation, and help-seeking, supported by goal-setting training
  • Guide adult students through self-regulated learning skills within the course of study using educational technology
  • Teach subzone classification explicitly as a teachable, assessable cognitive skill in clinical curricula
  • Universities should provide SRL training and consider reducing the length of breaks between lectures, using web-based instruction to give flexible attendance
  • Support SRL in the clinical context with a dual approach: improve individuals' goal setting, reflection skills and SRL confidence while creating a learning environment that lets students use their skills
  • Design web-based metacognitive training with structural reflection, social interaction, and self-awareness activities
