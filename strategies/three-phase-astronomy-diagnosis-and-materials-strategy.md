# Three-phase intervention: diagnose teachers' and pupils' representations with open-ended questionnaires, then co-design didactic materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose a concrete research-and-development plan for Argentine astronomy education. Phase one uses "Cuestionarios y entrevistas a los docentes y futuros docentes de la enseñanza primaria" to detect representations before and after formal instruction; phase two investigates pupils' representations that obstruct learning; phase three is joint design by teacher educators and researchers of innovative didactic materials grounded in those representations. They argue multiple-choice is inadequate because mere reading of the correct option can help the respondent, and free justifications reveal the respondent's logic.
