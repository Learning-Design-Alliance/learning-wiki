## Description
Among future refinements, the authors plan to add components of peer instruction as a teaching approach to future iterations of the course "to make the conventional lectures more interactive among students". This is stated as a forward-looking design intention for the Hybrid Quantum-Classical Systems course, alongside implementing surveys to evaluate the course's reception and effectiveness with regard to the defined learning outcomes.
