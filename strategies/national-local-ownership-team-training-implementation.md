# Implement large-scale team training through local ownership with ongoing central support and multi-level backing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distils lessons for large-scale implementation of team training. It concludes that 'Local ownership and delivery of the team training programme, with ongoing support from a central organisation, looks to be an effective implementation strategy.' Further named lessons are recognition as a national programme supported by national institutions, support at multiple levels continually renewed, simulation quality that engages all participants, provision of evidence, and an exhaustive ongoing communication strategy to maintain senior management support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Support is needed at multiple levels and must be continually renewed and reinforced; an ongoing communication strategy is essential to maintain senior management support.
#### Constraints
- Lessons derive from a country of five million with a single government; the extent to which the programme can be scaled to larger populations remains to be tested.

### Target Learners
- operating theatre teams across hospital systems

### Target Learning Goals
- large-scale implementation of multidisciplinary team training
