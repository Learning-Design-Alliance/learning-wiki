# Eight tangible instructor actions to shift physics culture toward neuro-inclusivity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes eight concrete classroom actions, stating “we propose the following 8 tangible actions that can shift physics culture toward being more neuro-inclusive.” They include offering multiple means of feedback about understanding, modeling how to “search for clues” in problem statements, avoiding skipping steps when modeling problem-solving, modeling question asking, providing rubrics allowing multiple solution pathways, validating different ways of solving problems, verbalizing that students may not understand material right away, and providing examples of scientists who do science in non-normative ways.
