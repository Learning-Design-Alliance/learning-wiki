# Record sessions with consent, data-protection communication, disclosure norms, and detailed notetaking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The recording tips sheet advises that recording 'can capture participants' exact words and preserve important details, supporting accurate analysis.' If recording, facilitators should gather consent and offer alternatives such as switching to written notetaking, 'Communicate data protections' like anonymization and secure storage, set disclosure norms acknowledging confidentiality cannot be fully prevented, and use a detailed notetaking protocol alongside recording.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly explain why you are recording and let participants know they can leave at any time
#### Constraints
- Facilitators 'cannot fully prevent other participants from sharing information outside the session'

### Target Learners
- Staff facilitating or recording community engagement sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- Record sessions ethically while protecting participant privacy
