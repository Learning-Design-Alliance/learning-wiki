# Use visualization and side-by-side comparison of mathematical models of datasets to press and refine learners' definitions of mode

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy engages learners in constructing mathematical models of datasets on a virtual number line and comparing models of contrasting datasets (one with a repeated value, one without) side-by-side. The authors report that "Alex considered his definition of mode as insufficient only after he developed mathematical models and visualized both models side-by-side," and that such models may help PSMTs and K-12 students develop more sophisticated schemes for operating with data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks must include scenarios at odds with the learner's prior definition, such as datasets with no repeated values
- An interactive tool allowing manipulation of individual data elements
#### Constraints
- Some individuals may need additional models that further press their definition of mode

### Target Learners
- preservice mathematics teachers
- middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- defining and identifying the mode of a dataset
