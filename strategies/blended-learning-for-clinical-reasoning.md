# Combine e-learning with face-to-face practice exchange groups to develop clinical reasoning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because e-learning alone did not improve clinical reasoning, the authors recommend improving this aspect "by setting up blended learning in the form of face-to-face teaching by practice exchange groups". This strategy pairs the knowledge gains of interactive e-learning with interactive reasoning practice the platform lacks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An e-learning component delivering theoretical knowledge plus face-to-face sessions for reasoning practice
#### Constraints
- The article notes recent meta-analyses found no difference between e-learning and traditional learning overall, and heterogeneity of programs complicates interpretation

### Target Learners
- anesthesia residents
- medical residents

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- theoretical clinical knowledge
