# Institutional interventions on emotional regulation and learning strategies to mitigate digital hoarding's effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors' practical recommendation for higher education institutions is to treat digital hoarding as a diagnosable, technology-linked anxiety-related problem and to respond organizationally. They advise that "universities could implement diagnosis and interventions focused on increasing personal (i.e., emotional regulation) and academic resources (learning strategies) as organizational plans to decrease the levels of health impairment processes in their students". The intervention targets the resource side of the JDR model, building students' personal and academic resources to counter the burnout pathway.
