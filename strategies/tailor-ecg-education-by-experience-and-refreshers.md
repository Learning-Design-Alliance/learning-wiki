# Tailor ECG education by experience level and schedule regular refresher courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that less experienced nurses gained most from the SDL package while nurses with more than 11 years gained least, the authors recommend tailoring educational interventions to different experience levels and adding "regular refresher courses and assessments" to sustain skills acquired through SDL and e-learning programs. They also suggest SDL approaches could extend to other critical diagnostic areas in nursing curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SDL or e-learning infrastructure accessible within nurses' working schedules
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in a single-site, one-group study of 50 nurses without long-term follow-up

### Target Learners
- critical care and cardiology nurses across experience levels

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained competency in ECG arrhythmia interpretation
