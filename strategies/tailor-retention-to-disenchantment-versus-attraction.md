# Tailor STEM retention strategies to whether students leave from disenchantment or attraction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that distinguishing disenchantment from attraction matters for retention practice: "Understanding factors that make students feel disenchanted with STEM fields may help inform what retention and support strategies can best prevent students from perceiving these fields negatively", while understanding attraction factors "can help educators learn why students might perceive non-STEM fields as appealing". Educators wanting to retain attraction-type leavers might re-frame STEM fields to seem similarly appealing. The authors note it remains an open question whether positively-framed attrition should be discouraged at all.
