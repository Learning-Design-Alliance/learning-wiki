# Kirkpatrick-based four-level evaluation roadmap for empirically validating AI-SP effectiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes a testable evaluation framework based on Kirkpatrick's 4-level model to guide future empirical validation. Level 1 measures learner satisfaction through questionnaires and usage logs (1–3 months); Level 2 measures knowledge and skill improvement using the SEGUE Scale, JSPE empathy scale, and pre-post test design (3–6 months); Level 3 measures behavior transfer through OSCE and clinical supervisor evaluation comparing AI-SP training vs control groups (6–12 months); Level 4 measures patient outcomes including satisfaction and communication-related medical error rates (>12 months).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Self-designed questionnaires and system usage logs
- SEGUE Scale and JSPE empathy scale
- OSCE and clinical supervisor evaluation
- Randomized controlled trials comparing AI training groups and human training groups
#### Constraints
- This is a proposed roadmap rather than presented empirical data

### Target Learners
- Medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Communication skills
- Empathy
- Self-efficacy
