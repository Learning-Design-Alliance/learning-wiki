# Analyse incorrect answers to key feature questions to identify and remediate specific misconceptions in clinical reasoning teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 associational), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that beyond aggregate scores, educators should systematically examine frequent incorrect answers to key feature questions. It states that "incorrect answers to key feature questions have rarely been analysed although they may reveal specific shortcomings in teaching that could be easily remedied" and that "a more thorough assessment of misconceptions and frequent errors appears promising".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Key feature items with recorded student responses
- Error frequencies calculated across learning and exam phases
#### Constraints
- Error analysis in this study was descriptive and limited to internal medicine cases

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying and correcting clinical misconceptions
- improving diagnostic and treatment decisions
