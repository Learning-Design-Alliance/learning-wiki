# Use flipped classrooms with pre-class reading and in-class group problem-solving for building-design topics

## Description
For the building-design portion of the course, the authors "chose a flipped learning approach: students are required to have read a chapter on the topic at hand before class." Class time is then spent mostly on solving Fermi questions and group activities rather than lecturing, with only the introductory urbanization lecture delivered traditionally.
