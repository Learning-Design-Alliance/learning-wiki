# Creating A Sense of Belonging

## Description
Learners who feel like they belong in school are more motivated, engaged, and ultimately show higher performance. Cues of belonging can have a powerful impact on learners. Cues such as including representations of all learners are simple to implement and will improve your classroom environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- This requires intentionality on the part of the instructor to create an inclusive and welcoming environment.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming and require constant upkeep to be effective.
