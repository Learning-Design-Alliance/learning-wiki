# Use awareness-focused selection tools and strengthen teamwork, leadership, and awareness in the medical curriculum

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that advanced medical students scored low on awareness and moderately on teamwork and leadership, the authors recommend that medical educators consider assessing awareness at selection and checking curricular coverage of the GAP-test competences. They note "a similar test like the GAP-test might be a helpful selection tool for medical students" and that situational judgement tests, which require awareness, have predicted selection-centre performance in postgraduate medical education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further investigations should determine whether awareness should be included in selection or trained in curriculum units, as the authors state
#### Constraints
- The authors state the study would have to be repeated with newly selected medical students to determine whether awareness is low from the start or decreases during training
