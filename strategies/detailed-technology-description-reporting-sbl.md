# Provide detailed descriptions of applied technology and pedagogical considerations when reporting SBL research to enable comparison and synthesis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review concludes that descriptions of the applied technology and pedagogical considerations are pivotal for comparing or synthesizing research results. It found that "the technology applied was diversely described in the studies" and that studies using digital technology in TESs generally provided richer descriptions than those using HPSs. Researchers should report technology features, functions, and integration into learning activities to support validity and transferability assessments.
