# Use flashcards and letter-sound correspondence teaching to support early word reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review describes flashcards as tools usable freely inside and outside the classroom that help learners focus on words they are struggling with, breaking each word into letter sounds and phonetic segments and blending them to read the word. Teachers should teach "letter sound correspondence and letter names" before reading, including a sounding strategy to differentiate similar sounds, and pair flashcards with pictures so learners know what the word means.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must learn letter-sound correspondence, differentiating letter sounds and letter names across their home language and English
#### Constraints
- Learners from homes where languages other than English are spoken struggle to display correct letter sounds and blend letters, sometimes saying sounds in isiXhosa and misreading words

### Target Learners
- Foundation Phase learners (Grades R–3)

### Target Learning Goals
- Word reading accuracy and fluency
- Vocabulary acquisition
- Phonemic awareness and blending
