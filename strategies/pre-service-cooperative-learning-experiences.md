# Provide prospective teachers with cooperative learning experiences during pre-service training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because teachers without cooperative learning experience are not expected to apply the method in their own classes, the article recommends creating learning environments where prospective teachers develop cooperative learning experiences during teacher education. Embedding methods like the adapted Jigsaw in pre-service laboratory courses lets student teachers experience modern teaching methods in their subject area, supporting their professional development before in-service practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher education programs that integrate cooperative learning into methodology courses
- Learning environments in which prospective teachers personally experience cooperative techniques
#### Constraints
- The article notes that a teacher who has no experience with cooperative learning is not expected to apply this method in his/her class

### Target Learners
