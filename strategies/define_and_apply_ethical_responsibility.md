# Define and Apply Ethical Responsibility

## Description
Define responsibility and ethical responsibility. Give students specific tools such as graphic organizers or methods for creating visual representations to use as they consider the ethical and moral responsibilities of their decisions. For example, depending on age level you could have students create if/then decision trees on the board or use sticky notes to create an option wall. Read current events, discuss if the decisions made are ethical, and evaluate those decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A definition of responsibility, a variety of graphic organizers or methods for creating visual representations, and current events.
