Enroll teachers in advanced computer courses for technology-supported language teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends enrolling teachers in advanced and professional computer courses demonstrating ways of using technology to facilitate listening and reading comprehension. It argues that the computerized method based on the ADDIE model "may address the barriers of time constrains, ways of teaching and learning language in general and listening and reading comprehension in particular". The recommendation follows the study's finding that teachers can be comfortable with computer programs, and the authors state the generation gap between teachers and students can be overcome by teachers' training.

Claims¶

  • A computer program based on the ADDIE model produced significantly higher listening comprehension post-test scores than traditional teaching among Jordanian ninth graders [+W]
  • A computer program based on the ADDIE model produced significantly higher reading comprehension post-test scores than traditional teaching among Jordanian ninth graders [+W]
