# Tailor the emotional tone of AI feedback to the type of task

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on their finding that task type (creation vs moderation) influenced how students engaged with feedback, the authors recommend that designers "Design feedback that adjusts its tone and emotional content based on the task's nature to enhance engagement and effectiveness". This implements the study's broader implication that educational technologies should consider both cognitive and emotional aspects of learning, using motivational phrases, praise and strategically used emojis to foster emotional connection and peer accountability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feedback systems must be able to distinguish task types (e.g., content creation vs peer review moderation) and vary tone accordingly
#### Constraints
- The article reports the enriched feedback lengthened comments substantially (mean word count 278.6 vs 190.9), and some students complained feedback was too long

### Target Learners
- higher education students in large online or blended courses

### Target Learning Goals
- engagement with AI feedback
- reduced negative emotional responses to feedback
