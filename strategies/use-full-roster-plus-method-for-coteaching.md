# Policymakers and model developers should use the Full Roster-Plus Method to account for co-teaching in value-added models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that policymakers addressing co-teaching in value-added models adopt the Full Roster-Plus Method, which treats co-teachers as teams with each teacher receiving equal credit for co-taught students. Unlike the Full Roster Method, the improved method ensures "all students contribute equally to the calculation of the relationship between student characteristics and achievement," avoiding the extra weighting of co-taught students. The article's empirical check found very small differences between the methods in District of Columbia Public Schools data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data identifying co-taught students and their multiple teachers
- A value-added model estimating the relationship between student characteristics and achievement
#### Constraints
- The article's empirical evidence on the method comes from one district's data; the available text reports no evidence from other settings

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in co-taught classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Fair and accurate attribution of achievement growth to individual teachers in co-taught settings
