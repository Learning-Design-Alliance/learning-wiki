# Support teachers with interventions that stimulate adoption of approach-oriented goal profiles and recognize maladaptive multiple-goals profiles early

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
From a practitioner's point of view, the authors recommend "it might be beneficial to support teachers with intervention measures that stimulate the adoption of approach‐oriented profiles" and stress "the importance of early recognition of this maladaptive goal profile by educational practitioners". They further suggest classroom practices that primarily promote social comparison and competition will diminish students' willingness to invest effort, and that focusing on learning and individual progress instead might be more successful.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
