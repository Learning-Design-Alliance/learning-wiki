Embed opportunistic RCTs into planned policies or programs¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's central strategy is to recognize moments when a district is already planning a policy or program change and embed a randomized controlled trial into that rollout. It argues that "Opportunistic RCTs can generate strong evidence for informing education decisions—with minimal added cost and disruption," making rigorous evaluation feasible within routine district operations.
