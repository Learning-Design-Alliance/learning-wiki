# Three consciousness-raising text techniques: Recall, Reconstruction, and Bolding-Underlying

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article details three concrete operations learners perform on paired original and re-write texts. In Recall, students compare a changed re-write with the remembered original and highlight significant sections; in Reconstruction, students listen to the teacher dictating the original while reading a gapped re-write and "manipulate language to uncover the main patterns"; in Bolding-Underlying, students attend to bolded and underlined parts of an unchanged re-write. All three aim to draw attention to language points in context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An original text and a prepared re-write version
- The original text is covered while students work with the re-write
#### Constraints
- The techniques were implemented with pre-intermediate learners aged 11–16 in groups and pairs, not individually

### Target Learners
- young EFL learners aged 11–16

### Target Learning Goals
- noticing and hypothesising about target grammatical structures
