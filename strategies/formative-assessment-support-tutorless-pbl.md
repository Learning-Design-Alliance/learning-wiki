# Support tutorless PBL with ongoing formative assessment and daily feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (1 for, 4 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that tutorless PBL be accompanied by frequent formative evaluation so instructors can monitor and adjust group development. The authors state: "We agree with their argument that ongoing and immediate formative assessment is valuable in tutorless PBL." In their own course this took the form of daily reports on every small group discussion, formative evaluation and feedback every day, and prior guidance to all students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Daily formative evaluation and feedback during tutorless sessions
- Sufficient guidance provided to students prior to PBL
#### Constraints
- The study setting was four SGDs in a week session with pre-clinical students; the authors note a curriculum with reduced SGD frequency would be required for typical PBL scheduling

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students in pre-clinical PBL

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical problem-solving introduction
- self-directed learning
- group collaboration
