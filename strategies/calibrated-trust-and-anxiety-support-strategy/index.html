Target calibrated trust and anxiety alongside AI literacy instruction when supporting existing generative AI users¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because trust occupies the pivot of the modeled chain, the authors recommend that AI literacy instruction aim "not only at skills but at calibrated trust" — teaching what generative models do well, where they fail, and how to verify output. They further recommend low-stakes, scaffolded encounters such as supervised practice, worked examples, and explicit normalization of uncertainty to ease anxiety, and transparency features in student-facing tools that make reasoning and limitations legible.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learners must already be users of the tool; the authors state the study provides no basis for recommendations about encouraging first-time uptake

Constraints¶

  • Rests on cross-sectional associations among existing users; the authors note literacy instruction alone should not be assumed to work for anxious students

Target Learners¶

  • University students who already use ChatGPT

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Calibrated reliance on generative AI and sustained academic use

Affordances¶

  • Capability Cognition Behavior Serial Model

Related Strategies¶

  • Teach AI literacy as epistemic calibration and design assessment that preserves visible spaces for human reasoning and ownership
  • Prioritize trust calibration over trust maximization when evaluating explainability and other design factors
