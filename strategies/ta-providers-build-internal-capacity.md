# Technical assistance providers should plan to work themselves out of a job by building districts' internal capacity

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy directs external providers and districts to design partnerships so district staff gradually take over the work. The report recommends that "TA providers should plan to 'work themselves out of a job,' by building districts' internal capacity to continue the work beyond their contract together." Partnerships were designed with heavy initial collaboration followed by progressively less support as district staff took the reins on elements such as professional development or designing PLCs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An explicit goal of the partnership must be building the district's internal capacity to implement the standards
#### Constraints
- The report notes ongoing funding is often important, if not required, to sustain these improvement efforts

### Target Learners
- District staff and administrators who will sustain standards implementation after external funding ends

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining district capacity for standards-aligned mathematics instruction beyond a funded initiative

### Affordances
- Strategic Ta Provider Partnerships
