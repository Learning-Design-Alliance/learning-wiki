# Good Tutee-Tutor Contact

## Description
Regular contact between tutees and tutors is part of the tutoring process, increasing tutee motivation and involvement. Tutor interest helps tutees keep on working and get through rough times. Getting to know your tutee enhances their intellectual and emotional commitment to learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regularly scheduled tutoring sessions, tutor willingness to engage with tutees personally.
#### Constraints
- May be time-consuming for tutors, requires strong interpersonal skills.

### Affordances
- Enhances motivation and involvement, fosters a sense of connection and support, promotes emotional commitment to learning

### Personalization
- Tutors can share past experiences, values, and attitudes to connect with tutees. They can also get to know tutees by name and treat them as individuals with real lives, asking about their well-being.
