# Varying Levels of Choice in Assessments

## Description
Give students a choice about the areas of focus in a paper or project. For in-class or online essay questions, each student generates a designated number of questions, and the teacher selects one of those questions for the student to answer. Choice on homework problems of different difficulty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear guidelines for project expectations and grading criteria. Resources and support for different assessment formats.
#### Constraints
- Requires more planning and preparation from teachers. Some students may struggle to make effective choices.

### Affordances
- Enhances motivation by allowing students to choose assessment formats that align with their strengths and interests. Fosters creativity and ownership of learning. Provides personalized learning experiences.

### Personalization
- Tailor project options to learner interests and skill sets. Vary levels of support and scaffolding based on individual needs.
