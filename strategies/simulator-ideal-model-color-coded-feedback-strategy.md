# Use simulator modules with predefined ideal models and color-coded visual feedback for standardized self-assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes simulator tooth preparation modules that "allow students to compare their preparations with predefined ideal models, providing color-coded visual feedback and enabling standardized assessment of parameters such as taper, occlusal reduction, and finish line standardization". This supports self-assessment and independent learning, reducing reliance on instructor-led feedback and offering unlimited practice potential in a low-risk environment.
