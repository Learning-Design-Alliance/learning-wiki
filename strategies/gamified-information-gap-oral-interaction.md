# Use gamified information gap activities with time pressure and forced output to promote L2 oral interaction practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article implements information gap activities, digital or paper-based, in which learners must exchange and negotiate information under time constraints to emulate real interaction. The rationale is that "the motivation and 'forced output' engendered by interactive games such as PàN would encourage learners to practice language" through "systematic repetition" and "negotiation with their interlocutors". Sessions of roughly 25 minutes ran biweekly over four weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An interactive environment that promotes interaction among interlocutors
- Tasks requiring exchange of information and negotiation toward a shared outcome
- Time constraints to emulate the pressure of the digital countdown
#### Constraints
- One month of such practice produced no significant pronunciation improvement on judge-rated comprehensibility, fluency, or overall pronunciation in this study

### Target Learners
- intermediate-level second/foreign language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- oral interaction skills
- fluency
- negotiation of meaning
