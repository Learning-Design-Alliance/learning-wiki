Apply the 10-minutes-per-grade homework time guideline, adjusted modestly for remote learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper recommends a common guideline that total homework across subjects be 10 minutes per night per grade level, e.g. a maximum of 30 minutes for a third grader but 120 minutes feasible for a high school student. Because remote learning shifts more work asynchronous, "the 10 minute per night may need to be adjusted slightly" to include former school self-study time, while focusing only on assignments with the strongest learning purpose.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must prioritize assignments with the strongest learning purpose given limited synchronized instruction time

Constraints¶

  • For lower grades, time formerly blocked as desk work time cannot be directly transitioned to homework, and the guideline still generally applies

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary through high school students in remote or hybrid learning

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Managing total independent-work load across subjects
