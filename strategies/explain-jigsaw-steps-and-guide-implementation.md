# Explain the jigsaw technique's aim and steps to students beforehand and guide them during implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the study's results and student opinions, the article recommends that before applying the technique "The aim and steps of this technique should be clearly told to the students before starting to apply this technique", and that "the teacher should guide the students in the implementation phase of this technique". This addresses student-reported disadvantages such as difficulty telling subjects and confusion from misunderstandings. The article further suggests teachers could be informed about the implementation step by step and that the technique be used in appropriate subjects at different times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher guidance during the implementation phase of the technique
#### Constraints
- The article states the technique should be used "in appropriate subjects at different times" and that student motivation should be kept at a maximum level parallel to the teacher's willingness

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade secondary school students in social studies classes

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic success in social studies units, plus communication, cooperation and participation skills
