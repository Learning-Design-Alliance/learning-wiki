# Early-semester mandatory tutoring intervention for lowest-scoring students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The strategy is to administer a short prerequisite-skills survey on the first day of class, invite the lowest-scoring students into mandatory tutoring beginning the first week, and bring them to proficiency on deficit topics before the first exam. Students meet twice weekly in small groups of up to three to foster social connections, work through ALEKS-generated worksheets, and take a Final Quiz requiring 80% to pass, with retakes after further review.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated initial survey to identify students needing the most help
- Experienced tutors whose availability students can schedule around
- Instructors and tutors must maintain frequent contact, as communication is key
#### Constraints
- Expansion must be completed slowly so adjustments can be made; careful instructor and tutor selection is needed to ensure program goals are met

### Target Learners
- Developmental mathematics students placing in the lowest third on placement testing

### Target Learning Goals
- Remediation of prerequisite arithmetic deficits within the first month of class
- Transferable study strategies and collaboration skills

## Claims

- [Scores on a four-problem arithmetic survey given on the first day of class positively correlated with final course grade](../claims/arithmetic-survey-score-correlates-with-final-grade.md) [+W]
- [ITS program completers earned an average final course grade of 2.18 on a 4.0 scale (SD = 1.388, N = 39)](../claims/its-completers-average-course-grade-2-18.md) [+W]
- [Most ITS participants (95.3%) continued tutorial support after the intervention, and the authors observed improved attitudes toward learning](../claims/its-participants-continued-tutorial-support-attitude-change.md) [+W]
