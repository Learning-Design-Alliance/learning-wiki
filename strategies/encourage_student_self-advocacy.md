# Encourage Student Self-Advocacy

## Description
Empowering students to become self-advocates involves helping them understand their strengths and challenges, and providing them with a voice in their learning. By understanding what works best for them, students can confidently advocate for the support they need. Strategies include creating a classroom culture where students can comfortably ask for help, even anonymously. Self-advocacy equips learners to effectively communicate their needs and find solutions to problems they encounter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to resources for students to explore their strengths and challenges, a supportive classroom environment, and strategies to help students articulate their needs.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort from both students and educators to cultivate self-awareness and effective communication skills. Students may initially resist taking responsibility for their learning or feel uncomfortable sharing their needs.

### Affordances
- Enhances students' ability to recognize their strengths and needs, encourages them to take responsibility for their learning, and prepares them for success in various environments by promoting independence and problem-solving skills.

### Personalization
- Instructors can adjust the complexity of self-advocacy strategies based on students' developmental stages and learning needs. Anonymity options can be provided to students who may be uncomfortable asking for help in public.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe students' willingness to participate in discussions, request help when needed, and articulate their learning preferences. Measure students' ability to set goals, solve problems, and advocate for themselves.

## Impact
- Research indicates that self-advocacy skills lead to improved academic outcomes and success in post-secondary education, the workforce, and civic life. Students who self-advocate are better equipped to navigate challenges and make informed decisions about their learning and lives.
