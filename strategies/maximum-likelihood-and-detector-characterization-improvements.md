# Future iterations should add maximum-likelihood reconstruction and detector characterization

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' stated forward-looking improvements to the activity: implementing a Maximum Likelihood method for reconstruction, and thoroughly characterizing detector performance so students can adequately interpret and correct their experimental data. The article states "Implementing a Maximum Likelihood method [21] would enable the recovery of smoother Wigner functions, and a thorough characterization of detector performance, in particular detector clearance and common mode rejection ratio, would enable students to interpret and correct their experimental data adequately."
