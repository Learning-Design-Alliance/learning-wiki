Strategies

Include students in diverse educational, creative, scientific-research activities and continuous pedagogical internship to form polylingual professional competence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's concluding recommendation is that "The decisive condition for the formation of the future teacher's professional competence on the basis of polylingual approach is the inclusion of students in a variety of educational, creative, scientific and research activities in university pedagogical process, in the course of continuous pedagogical internship." This follows from the finding that the existing system of psycho-pedagogical training leads to predominantly low and insufficient formation levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • University teachers should pay more attention to forming a conscious, positive attitude of future specialists to professional competence on the basis of polylingual approach

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • students of pedagogical specialties in universities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • formation of professional competence for teaching subjects in Kazakh, Russian and foreign languages

Claims¶

  • Future teachers show low motivation for polylingual subject teaching, with 73.2% saying it is unnecessary and 87.2% not self-learning subjects in a second language [+W]
  • Few future teachers master operational competencies for polylingual teaching, from 17% able to plan to 7% able to analyze and evaluate results [+W]
  • Initial formation levels are predominantly low and medium in both experimental and control groups, with the high level absent in the experimental group [+W]
  • Future teachers' knowledge of the polylingual approach is superficial, with only 27% recognizing its essence and 13% knowing CLIL methodology and technologies [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed research methods and creative activity across physics studies to strengthen competency formation
  • Support competence formation through activity in cognition, self-reliant design work, convergence with real knowledge processes, and continuity between levels
