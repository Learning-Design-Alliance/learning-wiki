# Shift from targeted optimization to configurational coordination when allocating resources to raise achievement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the three fsQCA pathways, the authors recommend that universities "avoid one-size-fits-all teaching management practices and instead allocate limited educational resources to the most appropriate driving models." Institutions should match interventions to student groups, course types, and learning stages—for example, pairing teacher conscientiousness and supportiveness with behavioral efficacy training such as time management workshops and goal-setting mechanisms for freshman transition periods and foundational courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of which configurational pathway applies to a given student group, course type, or learning stage
#### Constraints
- Derived from a single-institution cross-sectional fsQCA study at an agricultural university in North China

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving academic achievement
