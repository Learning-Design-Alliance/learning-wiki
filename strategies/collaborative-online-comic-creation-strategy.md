# Integrate collaborative online comic creation into Mandarin vocabulary learning activities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that collaborative learning, especially CSCL, should be applied in daily activity because it is significantly beneficial for students' learning activity, learning behavior, and learning products. Students work in small groups in a computer lab, with one computer per group, to create online comics on www.toondoo.com. Students communicate and negotiate before making group decisions about the story.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a computer lab with internet-connected computers (18 sets in this study) with Simplified Chinese language input enabled on keyboards
#### Constraints
- The article notes that internet connection stability and sufficient time duration are necessary for online activity

### Target Learners
- elementary school students learning Mandarin as a foreign language

### Target Learning Goals
- Mandarin vocabulary learning
- Chinese character writing
- collaborative story creation
