# Build collaborative bridges between R1 institutions and R2/PUI/CC faculty

## Description
The report regards as central the building of collaborative bridges between faculty and experimental programs at R1 institutions and those at R2 institutions, Predominantly Undergraduate Institutions, and Community Colleges. It recommends expanding benefits of faculty collaboration and research opportunities across the broad spectrum of academia with equivalence of opportunity and appropriate recognition, and conducting a study of new collaboration models addressing teaching loads, student training, and funding availability.
