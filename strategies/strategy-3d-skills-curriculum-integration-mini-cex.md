# Integrate the 3-D skills formative assessment into the main curriculum and pair it with formative tools such as the Mini-CEX

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend incorporating the 3-D skills formative assessment into the main curricula "to ensure no student is disadvantaged" by the voluntary out-of-hours format, and suggest the model "may be effectively paired with formative tools such as the ‘Mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise’" to assess learners before 3-D part 1. Future work will test the model on clinical placements and other formative assessment tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional capacity to run formative OSCE events with trained near-peer tutors
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated at a single institution, four weeks before a year three summative OSCE in undergraduate medicine

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical examination skills
- equitable access to formative assessment

## Claims

- [The short-term formative impact of the 3-D skills model was replicated with a larger sample, showing high student satisfaction, improved confidence and a mean increase of ~15% in checklist scores.](../claims/cl-3d-skills-short-term-formative-replication.md) [+W]
- [Third-year medical students taught with the 3-D skills model at a formative OSCE achieved significantly higher summative OSCE station pass rates than the rest of the year cohort, with a higher median grade banding of educational significance.](../claims/cl-3d-skills-summative-osce-improvement.md) [+W]
