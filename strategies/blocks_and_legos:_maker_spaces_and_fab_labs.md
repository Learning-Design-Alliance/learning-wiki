# Blocks and Legos: Maker Spaces and Fab Labs

## Description
Maker spaces and fab labs provide learners with opportunities to invent, design, and build things. These spaces encourage exploration, imagination, and collaboration, mirroring the principles of play. Learners can create jewelry, build guitars, design robots, or sew their own clothes. Maker spaces can integrate technology or use simple materials like wood scraps and soda bottles. This hands-on approach allows learners to apply theories in practical ways and solve real-world problems, fostering deeper engagement and learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated maker space or fab lab with necessary tools and materials. Instructor guidance and support. Collaboration opportunities.
#### Constraints
- May require significant resources to set up and maintain. Some learners may need more guidance and support.
