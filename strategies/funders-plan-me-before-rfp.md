# Funders should develop learning goals, plan their M&E approach, and communicate expectations before releasing an RFP and awarding grants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief recommends that, before releasing a request for proposals and awarding grants, funders "develop their learning goals, plan their measurement and evaluation (M&E) approach, and communicate expectations to prospective grantees." This front-loads evaluation planning so grant-funded solutions can generate usable learning evidence from the start.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funders must act before the request-for-proposals and grant-award stage, setting learning goals and an M&E approach and communicating expectations to prospective grantees.

### Target Learners
- Education funders preparing grant programs for learning solutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Structuring measurement and evaluation to determine whether a solution works, for whom, and in what context
