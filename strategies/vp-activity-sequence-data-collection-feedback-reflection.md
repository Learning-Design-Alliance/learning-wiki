# Structure VP learning activities around data collection, didactic questions, and author-formulated formative feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The vpNAM prescribes an implementable activity sequence: the student is first introduced to the patient and the aims of the learning activity, then collects relevant data to frame an understanding of the patient's needs and capture the patient's story. In Layer 2, "this process is supported by didactic questions that simulate reflection and make thinking skills visible." After completing the encounter, the student receives formative feedback formulated in advance by the case author and uses it "to reflect and to identify further knowledge and learning needs." The activity also incorporates core drivers of learning in medical simulation: scenario-based learning, task deliverables, trigger events, data collection guidance, feedback, and reflection.
