# Use teaching approximations (role play, microteaching) to scaffold pre-service teachers' pedagogical reasoning

## Description
Forkosh-Baruch and colleagues propose simulations of practice as a way to cope with pre-service teachers' lack of authentic experiences. As the editorial summarizes, teaching approximations are "simulations of practice (role play, microteaching) that are less complex than real practice but offer opportunities for scaffolding pre-service teachers pedagogical reasoning." Approximations should also be developed for responsible use of algorithm-driven software, with algorithms disclosed.
