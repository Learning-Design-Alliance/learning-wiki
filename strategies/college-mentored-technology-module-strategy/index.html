College-mentored technology module with step-by-step virtual lab handouts and in-class troubleshooting¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article describes an outreach program in which college fellows create and implement technology-rich science lessons in collaboration with the classroom teacher. After the pre-quiz, mentors gave brief introductions to series and parallel circuits, provided step-by-step instructions for building circuits in the online module, and circulated to troubleshoot issues, which gave them "one-on-one time with the students". This freed the teacher to address individual student needs rather than the entire class.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • College mentors selected with relevant tutoring or teaching experience
  • Step-by-step handouts for the online module, with identical questions and equation sheets for both groups
  • Teacher collaboration in lesson implementation

Constraints¶

  • An initial difficulty connecting circuits in the app required a brief explanation of where to click on the screen

Target Learners¶

  • high school physics students, particularly young women in an all-female school

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conceptual understanding of circuit design
  • interest and motivation in physics

Claims¶

  • Teachers described an ideal technology coach as prepared, patient, observant, and using current research-based methods [+W]
