# Videos (Pre-Reading)

## Description
A short video relating to the topic of the reading is shown. Discussion questions are prepared based on the video content. After watching the video, learners discuss what they saw with a partner. This approach leverages visual media to engage learners, activate prior knowledge, and provide a context for the reading material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to a relevant video and pre-prepared discussion questions.
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the video. Some learners may be distracted by the visual media.
