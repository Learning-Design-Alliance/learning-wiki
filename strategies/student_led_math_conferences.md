# Student Led Math Conferences

## Description
Throughout the year, teachers can have personal math conferences with the students. Students bring their formative assessments to the conference and reflect on their progress. The teachers and students work together to find areas that need strengthening and write a personal goal related to specific academic concepts. Students may decide to bring their math journal to their math conference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formative assessments, time for personal math conferences, and a way for teachers and students to work together to find areas that need strengthening and write personal goals.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to have personal math conferences with each student.
