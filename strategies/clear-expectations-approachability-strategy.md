# Communicate clear goals and expectations and maintain instructor approachability to reduce assessment-related anxiety and support motivation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Across both assessment styles, students identified clarity of instructor goals and expectations as a major influence on academic-related anxiety and motivation. The article reports that instructors who utilized feedback-based formative assessments typically provided more clarity surrounding their goals and expectations, and that reassurance and approachable instructor demeanor influenced whether students sought feedback. The authors recommend that clear communication and instructor approachability can cultivate stronger teaching presence, which may enhance motivation, engagement, and quality of learning outcomes.
