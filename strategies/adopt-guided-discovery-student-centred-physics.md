# Adopt guided discovery and other student-centred strategies for teaching physics concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that physics teachers move away from expository, teacher-centred delivery toward strategies in which students actively perform mental processing tasks such as observing, classifying, measuring, predicting and inferring. It states that "Guided discovery and other student-centered teachin g strategies should be adopted for teaching various concepts in physics" so students engage in activities supporting meaningful acquisition and transfer of scientific knowledge. In the study this was implemented through researcher-prepared lesson packages on heat energy transfer delivered by trained teachers over six weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in the strategy; the study trained research assistants for one week with detailed lesson packages
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one non-randomized study of senior secondary physics students in one Nigerian local government area

### Target Learners
- senior secondary school physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- transfer of physics knowledge to new concepts and situations
