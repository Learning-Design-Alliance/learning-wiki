# Use authentic reading texts drawn from students' subject areas to motivate reading engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that reading teachers use authentic texts drawn from the students' subject areas, stating this will lead to motivation and reading engagement and sustain students' interest in the reading event. In the study, passages were taken from introductory Biological Sciences and Agriculture textbooks, selected so that both topic and language were familiar to the students.
