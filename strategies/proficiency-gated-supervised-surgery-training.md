# Proficiency-gated training combining a short theoretical module with supervised surgeries until operative competence is certified

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before operating on trial patients, both MDs and ACs — none previously trained in mesh repair — completed a training program consisting of a 1-day theoretical module followed by hands-on supervised surgeries whose length varied with each trainee's needs. Competence was assessed by senior consultant surgeons using the Groin Hernia Operative Performance Rating System, and trainees had to reach a defined performance threshold before operating independently. The article states the hands-on module "lasted 1 to 3 days, depending on the need of each trainee to reach proficiency."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment by senior consultant surgeons using the Groin Hernia Operative Performance Rating System, which grades competence on a scale of 1 to 5.
#### Constraints
- The article reports this training was designed for a similar study in Ghana and that trainees needed a 4 or higher overall performance score to qualify for this study.

### Target Learners
- medical doctors and associate clinicians without prior mesh-repair training

### Target Learning Goals
- performing anterior mesh inguinal hernia repair to a certified proficiency standard
