# Use coaching over time to review assessment portfolios away from the clinic and set personal action-oriented goals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
When staff are familiar with coaching techniques, the article proposes using coaching over time to review an endodontic portfolio of assessment data from multiple clinical and academic observations, away from the clinical environment. This facilitates student reflection on present capabilities and progress and enables the student to set personal, action-orientated goals, for example against the ESE undergraduate curriculum guidelines or national accreditation requirements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Large assessment data sets that students otherwise find difficult to assimilate when agreeing learning goals
- A coach with a dental background is advantageous to help the student understand the context behind clinical issues
#### Constraints
- This type of coaching has, as yet, only been reported in medicine

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and postgraduate endodontic students

### Target Learning Goals
- Longitudinal professional development and willingness to take on more complex cases
