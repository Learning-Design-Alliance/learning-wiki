# Teachers and parents should provide sustained academic and emotional support to raise mathematics achievement, interest, and well-being

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that parents set aside time to assist children because "it increases their self-confidence and motivation to learn, improving mathematics achievement", without needing to teach the material themselves. It advises teachers to understand students' varying abilities, vary learning approaches and models, select attractive learning media, and use technology-based learning media to reduce stress; and suggests schools provide briefings or training for teachers and parents on maintaining students' interest and encouraging them academically and emotionally.
