# Summaries and Recaps

## Description
Reinforce and clarify concepts, making sure information is both understood and remembered. Break content into smaller pieces, explain a part of the concept, then do a quick recap using different words, images, or examples. Include interactive activities lets students apply what they’ve learned in practical ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools for creating summaries and recaps. A clear understanding of the key concepts.
#### Constraints
- Summaries and recaps can be ineffective if not well-designed. Overuse of summaries and recaps can become repetitive.
