# Train teachers in mixed-skill classrooms to manage conflict and evaluate both instructional practices and interpersonal tone

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
As a policy implication, the article recommends that "Teachers charged with implementing higher order instructional strategies in mixed-skill classrooms should also receive training in how to manage conflict with students," and that evaluations should cover both classroom-level instructional practices and the interpersonal tone of individual interactions. Programs such as complex instruction in detracked classrooms are named as proven models for this balance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development in conflict management for teachers using higher order instruction in mixed-skill classrooms
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is observational and specific to elementary math; causality was not established

### Target Learners
- Elementary school teachers in mixed-skill classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining higher order instruction while maintaining supportive teacher–student relations
