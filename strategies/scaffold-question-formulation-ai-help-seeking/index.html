Scaffold question formulation and active help-seeking in AI-supported tutoring systems¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on observed differences in inquiry behavior, the article recommends that "AI-supported tutoring systems may benefit from scaffolding mechanisms that en- courage students to formulate questions and engage in more active help-seeking behaviors." This targets students who under-ask or rely on confirmation-seeking, and environments that encourage "reflective inquiry and pro- ductive help-seeking rather than repeated confirmation-seeking or excessive procedural delegation."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The AI system must log or detect students' inquiry patterns so that scaffolding can encourage question formulation where help-seeking is passive or confirmatory.

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation arises from a study where the two sessions differed in both programming topics and conceptual complexity, and the authors state findings should be interpreted cautiously.

Target Learners¶

  • First-generation and novice CS2 students interacting with AI assistants

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Productive help-seeking and reflective inquiry during programming problem solving

Related Strategies¶
