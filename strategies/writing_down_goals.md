# Writing Down Goals

## Description
Writing down goals makes them tangible and helps prioritize them. This practice allows for reflection on what one wants to achieve and why, setting due dates for completion and evaluation. It aids in prioritizing goals by assessing their importance and planning accordingly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time to reflect on and articulate goals in writing. Access to writing materials or digital tools is needed.
#### Constraints
- The act of writing goals alone does not guarantee achievement; it requires consistent effort and monitoring.

### Personalization
- Learners can write goals in a format that resonates with them—be it bullet points, mind maps, or detailed plans. Different tools like journals, digital documents, or visual boards can be used.

## Assessment Evidence
- Regular review of written goals can indicate progress and areas needing adjustment. Success is measured by the completion of specific goals within the defined timeframe.

## Impact
- Research indicates that writing down goals significantly increases the likelihood of achieving them. Tangible goals serve as constant reminders and motivators, enhancing focus and commitment.
