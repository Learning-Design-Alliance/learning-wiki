# Give all trainees a standardized video-based introduction to the technique before hands-on training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Before any hands-on practice, all participants in both arms receive the same video introduction to suturing and knot tying, and trained student tutors give a standardized introduction to the box trainer, shoebox, and instruments. The protocol states: "All students receive a standardized video-based introduction to the suturing and knot tying technique at the start of the study", and students may refer to the video throughout participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A recorded demonstration video and trained tutors providing a standardized introduction to the training modalities and terminology
#### Constraints
- The video introduction is held constant across arms, so it is not itself an experimental manipulation

### Target Learners
- laparoscopic novice medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic suturing and knot tying technique
