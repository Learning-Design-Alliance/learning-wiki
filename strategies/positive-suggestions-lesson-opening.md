# Open lessons with positive suggestions, instrumental music and motivating sentences to remove learning obstacles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that teachers' first task is to encourage students and make them ready to learn in the critical first minutes of a lesson. Teachers "can give positive suggestions to remove learning obstacles" such as fear of failure or public humiliation, boredom, and a sense of no benefit from learning. "All the hindrances should be replaced by positive suggestion by playing instrumental music with motivating stories or sentences at the beginning of the lessons."
