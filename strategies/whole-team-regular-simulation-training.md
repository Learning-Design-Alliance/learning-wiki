# Advocate regular simulation training for the whole delivery team, including senior staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that simulation-based episiotomy training be offered regularly to the entire delivery team rather than only to juniors, because 'both obstetricians and midwives could benefit' and 'Senior accoucheurs may particularly benefit in some certain procedures.' The course mixes anatomical theory with hands-on practice in a sandwich design, and the authors state that 'Operative obstetric simulation training with appropriate instructions should be advocated.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An experienced senior consultant trainer
- a low-cost replicable simulation medium such as the paper pads
- pre and post assessment to measure progress
#### Constraints
- Generalisation is limited by voluntary recruitment, small sample size, tertiary teaching hospital settings, and lack of long-term follow-up

### Target Learners
- obstetricians
- midwives
- senior perinatal care providers

### Target Learning Goals
- mediolateral episiotomy technique
- reducing sub-standard incision parameters
