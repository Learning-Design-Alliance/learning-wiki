# Weigh stated considerations before adopting computerized adaptive testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The publication is framed as guidance for education agencies considering computerized adaptive testing: its title, "Measuring Up: Considerations for Adopting Computerized Adaptive Testing", signals that adoption decisions should be preceded by weighing considerations rather than adopting CAT outright. The strategy is for decision-makers to review such considerations — including the claimed validity and reliability potential of CAT relative to non-adaptive tests — before implementation. The page itself provides only the framing and a brief overview, not the considerations in detail.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adopters need information about CAT's mechanism and its claimed validity and reliability advantages relative to non-adaptive tests before deciding to adopt
#### Constraints
