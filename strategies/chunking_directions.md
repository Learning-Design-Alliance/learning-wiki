# Chunking Directions

## Description
Breaking down directions into smaller, more manageable sections to support sustained attention. This strategy involves providing information in bite-sized pieces to prevent overwhelm and improve engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of student's attentional capacity, careful planning of instruction, and clear communication
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning to ensure logical flow, might not address underlying attentional challenges, and some students may prefer the full set of directions upfront

- <!-- TODO -->

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Students who struggle with sustained attention and following lengthy directions

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Improve sustained attention, reduce cognitive overload, and increase comprehension of multi-step instructions

### Affordances
- Reduces the amount of information processed at once, provides clear stopping points, supports memory, and enhances engagement

### Personalization
- The size of the chunks can be adjusted based on individual student needs and attention spans. Use of visual cues and reminders can be incorporated.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student's ability to follow directions when presented in smaller chunks. Assess comprehension through task completion and verbal feedback.

## Impact
- Research indicates that chunking information supports working memory and improves task completion for students with attentional challenges.
