# Implement mandatory transition simulations in curricula to address first-month accident rates.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that VET institutions introduce mandatory "induction simulations" in the final semester that "mimic the pressure and lack of supervision of a real workplace, testing students' ability to identify hazards under stress." This strategy directly targets the first-month vulnerability identified in the accident data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VET institutions introduce mandatory induction simulations in the final semester
#### Constraints
- Experimental designs implementing simulation-based safety modules are needed to evaluate the long-term impact on real injury outcomes

### Target Learners
- vocational students transitioning to employment

### Target Learning Goals
- hazard identification under stress
- refusing unsafe work
