# Use representative optical system simulations alongside geometric and physical optics to teach Fourier optics

## Description
The article recommends illustrating Fourier optics instruction with computer simulations of complete optical systems, combining Fourier-optics methods with ray-tracing and exact Maxwell solutions so students see "not only the physical principles but also real world applications of optical science and engineering". Three representative examples span birefringent crystal propagation and diffractive optical elements, showing how the same simulation package supports diverse optics topics.
