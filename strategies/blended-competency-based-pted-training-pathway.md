# Blend simulation with traditional apprenticeship under a competency-based milestone framework for PTED training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a dual-pathway approach to close the PTED simulation implementation gap: first, "developing a structured, blended training model to strategically integrate simulation with traditional apprenticeship"; second, guiding this blended model with a competency-based framework in which progression is determined by achievement of predefined skill milestones rather than time spent, given the variability in clinical experience among PTED surgeons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined skill milestones must be established so progression can be determined by achievement rather than time-based training
#### Constraints
- The article did not investigate the specifics of how different simulation types should be implemented, noting subsequent investigations should quantify their influence on learning efficiency

### Target Learners
- Novice surgeons learning PTED, including postgraduates, residents, and fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- Achieve proficiency in percutaneous transforaminal endoscopic discectomy skills while maintaining patient safety
