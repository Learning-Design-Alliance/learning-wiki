# Run an international cross-campus group histology project in which mixed campus groups complete an e-workbook and deliver a group presentation on an assigned GI tract region.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students from two campuses are assigned to mixed groups, each given a GI tract region (e.g. oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, anal canal), and collaborate remotely to complete an e-workbook and a 10-minute group presentation on normal histology, a chosen pathology, and its structural-functional consequences. The article reports students worked "in international groups to collectively complete an e‐workbook on a region of the GI tract" and that communication platform choice was left to students. Peer assessment of presentations was incorporated, and all groups heard presentations on all GI regions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students, regardless of location, must be able to access the same virtual microscope material throughout the project
- Joint introductory lecture delivered physically or via videoconferencing
- Course convenor contact details available at both campuses for support
#### Constraints
- Working across different time zones was reported as the main difficulty
- Unequal workload between campuses occurred when coursework deadlines in other modules clashed, which the authors accommodated in later years

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate BSc students on biomedical sciences and medical physiology programmes at two international campuses

### Target Learning Goals
- Histology and histopathology of the GI tract
- Groupwork skills of time management, group dynamics, and communication
- Presentation skills
