Five prioritized improvements for AI tutoring prototypes: instant core-strategy layer, curriculum tags, dynamic geometry, step-specific buttons, automated wrong-book¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on field findings and participants' explicit requests, the authors list five improvements: a dual-mode structure where the "Answer + Core Idea" layer loads instantly; explicit junior-high syllabus tags; automatic highlighting of auxiliary lines synchronized with text; localized step-specific follow-up buttons such as "Explain this step"; and automated wrong-book generation organizing captured problems by knowledge point for spaced weekend practice.
