# Incorporate Advance Organizers into primary poetry lessons and teacher preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that the AO strategy "may be incorporated in teaching poetry in primary schools" and that "Teachers need to be encouraged to use AO in the learning of poetry in English to enhance achievement". It further recommends that Curriculum Support Staff (TAC tutors) assist teachers in preparing and using AO, that AO be made part of poetry content as ICT is integrated into the primary curriculum, and that teacher training institutions include AO strategy in their content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained on how to use the AO (the study used a teachers' induction manual)
- Support from Curriculum Support Staff (TAC tutors) for preparation and use of AO
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is one quasi-experimental study in Nakuru North Sub-county, Kenya

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils learning poetry in English

### Target Learning Goals
- improved achievement in poetry and in English Language at KCPE

### Affordances
- Advance Organizers Prior Knowledge Bridging
