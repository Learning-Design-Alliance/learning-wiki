# Switchable dome design moving from purely perceptual to quantitative representation after usability evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking plan is to first deploy the dome in tactile format showing position and apparent magnitude, evaluate it with tablets of Cassiopeia, the big and small dipper and other summer constellations, and then insert all tablet information directly into the dome. There, "by means of a suitable switch, to switch from a purely perceptual representation to a more quantitative one, providing information on apparent distances and brightness." This implements the two-cognitive-planes framework in a single artifact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A suitable switch mechanism on the dome to toggle between the two representations.
#### Constraints
- The switch design is planned only after evaluating and discussing the usability of the initial setup and stimuli.

### Target Learners
- festival and planetarium visitors, both sighted and BVI

### Target Learning Goals
- accessing both perceptual appearance and quantitative astronomical data of stars
