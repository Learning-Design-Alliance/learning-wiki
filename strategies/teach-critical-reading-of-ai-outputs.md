# Teach students to read AI critically and discuss the difference between fluency and thought

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends valuing voice over polish and treating AI uncertainty as pedagogical opportunity. Students "should be taught to read AI critically, to see that algorithmic fluency optimises for what is likely rather than what is thoughtful". The article also recommends discussing the limits of AI, the genericity of its outputs, and the difference between fluency and thought, which it says shifts the classroom from surveillance to dialogue and helps students see why AI cannot do the real work of academic writing.
