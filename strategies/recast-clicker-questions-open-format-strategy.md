# Recast multiple-choice clicker questions as open-format questions requiring written justification to expose faulty reasoning behind correct answers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In a freshman calculus-based Physics I course, the instructor recast traditional clicker questions as open-format InkSurvey questions, adding a request for justification. Students must "justify their answers in writing" and work through equations on the tablets during class rather than simply selecting an option. Responses revealed students who reached the correct conclusion with faulty reasoning, such as constant-velocity thinking, which may not have been apparent without the open-format question.
