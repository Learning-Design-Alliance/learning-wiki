# Use multiple-surface analyses with TIPER-style tasks (Ranking Tasks, Conflicting Contentions, What, if anything, is Wrong Tasks) to challenge student ideas about the Ampère-Maxwell law

## Description
The article recommends building exercises around the fact that different surfaces bounded by the same curve yield the same magnetic field circulation via different current terms. It states that "Exercises such as Ranking Tasks, Conflicting Contentions, or What, if anything, is Wrong Tasks? are ideal for challenging their ideas about the different features of the AM law." Suggested tasks include ordering circulations across three surfaces and evaluating statements claiming the field's cause depends on the chosen surface.
