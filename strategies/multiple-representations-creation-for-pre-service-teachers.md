# Have pre-service teachers create multiple representations of science concepts, with AI's multimodal support, in science content courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Observing that participants generated more diverse representations when acting as teachers, the authors recommend that science content courses for pre-service teachers "actively encourage pre-service teachers to create multiple representations of science concepts to become more confident learners and successful educators." AI's ability to engage with multi-modal data makes this goal more achievable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses must provide opportunities to generate varied representation types (e.g., pictures, cartoons, analogies, graphs)
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that over-reliance on AI risks reducing creativity, critical thinking, and originality among pre-service teachers

### Target Learners
- pre-service elementary teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- representational competence in communicating science concepts
