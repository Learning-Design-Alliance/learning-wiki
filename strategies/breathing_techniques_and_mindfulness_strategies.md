# Breathing Techniques and Mindfulness Strategies

## Description
Teaching breathing techniques and mindfulness strategies equips students with tools to manage challenging situations. These techniques help students become more aware of their emotional and physical states, enabling them to respond thoughtfully rather than react impulsively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit teaching of breathing techniques and mindfulness strategies, consistent practice and reinforcement.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing practice to become effective. Some students may initially resist or find it difficult to engage with these techniques.
