Adopt brain-based learning methods and teacher workshops in chemistry and science teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article closes with recommendations directed at ministries, curriculum organizers and teachers: apply BBL-based models because of their importance for achievement, organize syllabus and textbook content using brain-based learning principles, and run professional development so teachers can use BBL in classrooms. Its final recommendation is to "Use of BBL method in teaching chemistry and science." The recommendations follow directly from the study's significant ANCOVA result and the authors' interpretation that varied, enriched activities meet individual differences and raise self-confidence.

  • Teacher training: the article recommends "conduct workshops for teachers in the filed of BBL and apply it in the classroom"

Constraints¶

  • Grounded in one quasi-experiment in one girls' school in Tafila, Jordan, covering specific chemistry topics

Target Learners¶

  • 9th-grade science and chemistry students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement in chemistry and science

Affordances¶

  • Brain Based Learning Theory Principles

Claims¶

  • A brain-based learning teaching-learning program produced statistically significantly higher chemistry achievement than traditional teaching for 9th-grade female students [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Classroom strategies from brain-based learning: positive emotion, student choice and responsibility, and the joy of success
  • Organise seminars, conferences and workshops on modern instructional strategies such as Round-robin for chemistry teachers
  • Design ICT-integration teacher training that is subject-oriented and workshop-based over a long period
