# Islamic Geometry Exploration

## Description
Exploring Islamic geometry with students centers on exploration, joy, and solidarity. Art lends itself in exploring geometry standards such as symmetry, tessellation, congruency, similarity, and composition. Students are invited to go on a virtual field trip to the Mosque of Sultan Barquq in Cairo to identify patterns and geometries. Questions are facilitated during this exploration to pique curiosity and connect art to mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Images of Islamic art, virtual field trip to the Mosque of Sultan Barquq in Cairo, and guidance to facilitate exploration and questioning.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and facilitation to ensure students make meaningful connections between art and mathematics.
