# Structured pre-simulation briefing, hands-on practice, and model-based debriefing sequence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article prescribes a session sequence: a pre-simulation briefing that explains objectives, safety policies, and software tools; supervised hands-on delineation practice; then a debriefing using established frameworks. It states "The debriefing sessions employ the established frameworks, i.e., the GAS (gather, analyze, and summarize) model and the PEARLS (promoting excellence and reflective learning in simulation) model."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators able to apply the GAS, PEARLS, SNAPPS, and Pendleton feedback models
#### Constraints
- Session scheduling must not coincide with daily clinical practice, per the logistics section

### Target Learners
- radiation oncology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- delineation skills
- reflective practice
- clinical decision-making
