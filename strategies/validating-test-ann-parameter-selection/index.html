Choose ANN parameters with a validating test instead of typical statistical criteria such as AIC or BIC¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends selecting the appropriate parameters for the ANNs using a validating test rather than typical statistical criteria such as AIC or BIC. The authors present this as a distinctive feature of their approach within cognitive diagnostic modelling. It supports the goal of achieving robust and accurate classification of latent attribute profiles.
