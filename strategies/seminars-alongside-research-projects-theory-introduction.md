# Run regular seminars alongside research projects to introduce theory in a passive format

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the pilot summer programme, while students completed research-style tasks, "regular seminars were held, allowing the relevant theory to be introduced in a more passive format". After these were completed, students regularly met with an assigned project supervisor to plan and solve a research activity. This combines passive theory introduction with supervised research work.
