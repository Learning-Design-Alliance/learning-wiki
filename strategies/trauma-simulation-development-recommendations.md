# Develop trauma simulation training with objective automated evaluation, undergraduate inclusion, combined fidelity levels, and pre-hospital settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the gaps identified in its scoping review, the article recommends several directions for trauma training design: developing automated, objective evaluation that uses information simulators can gather directly; including trauma training in medical students' education; classifying simulation modalities by training focus; and including pre-hospital settings. The review states that objective information from simulations "provides high-quality feedback, which allows them to see the impact of their actions during the simulation", supporting skill learning and performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation methods must be prepared to gather the information necessary for automated evaluation of trainee performance
#### Constraints
- The review notes the search was limited to English-language articles published from 2010 onwards and to certain simulation terms, which may not have gathered a complete representation of trauma training

### Target Learners
