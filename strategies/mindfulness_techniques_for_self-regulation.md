# Mindfulness Techniques for Self-Regulation

## Description
This approach incorporates mindfulness techniques to help students manage their initial emotional impulses and reactivity. Practicing mindfulness can promote self-awareness and emotional regulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Quiet space; facilitator (optional); mindfulness prompts
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for some students to focus; requires consistent practice; effectiveness depends on student engagement
