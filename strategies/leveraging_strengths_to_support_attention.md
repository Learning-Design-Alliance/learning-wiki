# Leveraging Strengths to Support Attention

## Description
This strategy focuses on identifying a learner's strengths and using those strengths to support their attention during learning. It recognizes that all learners have varying degrees of strengths and challenges related to attention, and the most effective way to address these challenges is by focusing on and leveraging their strengths. Strategies include preferential seating, graphic organizers, visual aids, and chunking directions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of the learner's strengths, and strategies for leveraging those strengths to support attention.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to identify individual strengths. Strategies may need to be adapted over time as the learner's needs change.
