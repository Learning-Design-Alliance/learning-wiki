# Strengthen undergraduate and postgraduate ASD training for physicians using interactive workshops, case-based discussions, and ECHO Autism-style models

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends changing undergraduate and postgraduate training to address inadequate physician ASD knowledge, since Polish medical schools dedicate only "2–5 h" to ASD across five years and postgraduate programs include no or one ASD didactic. It points to evidence that interactive workshops and case-based discussions improved diagnostic accuracy and access, and highlights the ECHO Autism model of interactive specialist meetings as promising for Polish primary care physicians.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systematic solutions obliging physicians to constantly acquire high-quality knowledge on the complexity of care for ASD patients, which the article says are currently lacking
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge of ASD etiology
- ASD diagnostic criteria and screening tools
- comprehensive longitudinal care and support for autistic patients and families
