Create small controlled presentation mini-sets as a near-term community benchmark¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To address the corpus gap, the survey recommends "creating small, controlledpresentation mini-sets, consisting of 5–10 minute talks per speaker with slide markers, emphasis annotations, and diverse accent coverage, as a near-term community benchmark." Section 7 elaborates the target composition: 20–30 speakers from 5+ L1 backgrounds delivering technical talks with timestamped slide boundaries, phone-level transcriptions, and expert prosody ratings, enabling head-to-head system comparison and shared evaluation campaigns.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Slide markers, emphasis annotations, phone-level transcriptions, expert prosody ratings, and diverse L1 coverage across speakers.

Constraints¶

  • The survey notes the datasets it curates and recommends are limited in scale; large-scale presentation corpora with diverse accents and domains would strengthen evaluation.

Target Learners¶

  • L2 English presenters across diverse L1 backgrounds

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Enabling evaluation of coaching systems on all five presentation-skill dimensions

Related Strategies¶

  • Calibrate coaching thresholds per L1 cohort and avoid penalizing identity-marking accent features
  • TTS-based coaching pipeline: anchor and target exemplars, chunked recording, alignment, and focused drills
