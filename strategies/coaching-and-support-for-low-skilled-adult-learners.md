# Provide support, feedback, guidance, and coaching along the way for low-skilled adult learners

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report closes by warning against assuming low-skilled adults can self-direct: many "have had negative experiences with learning in the past, and haven't developed some of the skills needed to direct their own learning." It recommends that designers provide "support, feedback, guidance, and coaching along the way" as key to these learners' success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology products should provide "anytime, anywhere learning, and giving adults flexibility and control to learn at their own pace."
