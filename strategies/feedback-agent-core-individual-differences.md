# Place the feedback agent at the core of the feedback process, attending to individual differences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The fifth step centers the person delivering or enacting feedback — the feedback agent — in models of the feedback process. The article states that "placing the feedback agent at the core of the feedback process, with particular attention to individual differences, is of utmost importance". This shifts attention from feedback as a message toward the agent's characteristics and variability as determinants of feedback effects.
