# Co-design AI support tools with educators through structured design thinking workshops

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A co-design strategy in which vocational lecturers and researchers collaboratively specified the AI support tool through structured design thinking workshops. "These workshops, typically lasting 90 minutes with 4-6 educator participants, employed design thinking methodologies to systematically work through problem definition, ideation, and solution specification." Participants evaluated candidate solutions against feasibility, impact, user value, and alignment with values before selecting an AI chatbot, and established critical boundaries to maintain academic integrity and prevent dependency.
