Five state policy actions to sustain and scale teacher residencies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends that the state: (1) "Fund resident and mentor stipends with state matching grants that increase the district match over time to support sustainability"; (2) provide seed grants to EPPs launching residencies with high-quality features; (3) invest in technical assistance capacity through Education Service Centers, including ESC Fellows and possible multidistrict regional residency consortia; (4) establish pipelines for mentor teachers and residency clinical faculty connected to state workforce priorities; and (5) provide targeted support for partnerships preparing teachers for high-need assignments and locations, modeled on the Teacher Incentive Allotment structure.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • State funding, guidance, and technical assistance infrastructure, including Education Service Centers

Constraints¶

  • High-quality residency features are most difficult to establish and sustain in smaller, under-resourced, and more rural districts

Target Learners¶

  • teacher residency candidates
  • mentor teachers
  • educator preparation programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • building a strong, stable, and diverse teacher workforce

Related Strategies¶

  • Policy recommendations for sustaining and expanding teacher residencies
  • Use Teacher Residency Grant Program funds, braided with other sources, to recruit and retain a diverse teaching workforce
  • State policy levers for sustaining yearlong, stipended clinical preparation: require or incentivize full-year stipended clinical experience, fund partnerships, and reduce testing fees
  • Sustain teacher residencies through blended federal and state funding, including TQP grants, ESSER funds, and dedicated state investments
  • State actions to strengthen and sustain a funded residency pathway: clarify goals, upgrade data systems, adjust grant parameters, offer transition grants, and pair new LEAs with experienced IHEs
  • Build high-retention, supportive pathways into teaching for candidates of color
  • Sustain and expand residency pathways through maintained state grant funding, financial aid, apprenticeship structures, and technical assistance
