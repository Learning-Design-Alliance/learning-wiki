# Share the VR in a dome: pause, clicker-question, and branch on mastery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using a fullsphere movie (VR360) in a planetarium dome rather than headsets, so the presenter can "specify which half of the sphere your audience is looking at". The presenter can "pause the movie, ask questions using a clicker system, display the results, and move on if the subject is mastered or explain more if items are not understood", making the dome show an interactive, mastery-checked group lesson rather than passive viewing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A planetarium dome with fullsphere (VR360) playback, a clicker response system, and a presenter to pause the movie and branch on mastery
#### Constraints
- Supporting learning results are "preliminary results which will be expanded in the next year"

### Target Learners
- high school students
- planetarium and museum audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- three-dimensional science concepts such as magnetism and eclipse geometry
