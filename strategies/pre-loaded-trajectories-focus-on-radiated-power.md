# Provide pre-loaded trajectories so students can focus on radiated power rather than approximating motion with controllers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CUBE includes selectable pre-defined charge trajectories in its menu. The authors' rationale is that "We have provided some "pre-loaded" trajectories to make it easier for students to focus on the radiated power (as opposed to waving the controllers around attempting to approximate sinusoidal oscillation, for example)." Uniform circular motion is one selectable trajectory that makes the non-relativistic versus relativistic power difference easy to see.
