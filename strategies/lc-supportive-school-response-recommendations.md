# Box 4 recommendations: believe and validate CYP with Long Covid, raise awareness, and offer a range of tailored adaptations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's Box 4 sets out recommendations for schools and healthcare professionals supporting CYP with Long Covid: "Show that you believe, understand and are willing to help CYP with LC attempting to return to school", raise awareness of Long Covid among the school community, communicate regularly with CYP about coping, and offer a range of adaptations that can be tailored to individual and changing needs. Specific adaptations listed include reduced timetables and/or prioritisation of fewer subjects, rest/'time-out' passes, allowing lifts to alleviate physical impacts of moving around school buildings, considering technology such as artificial intelligence-based robots, briefing school staff, and consulting staff on their experiences and support needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School staff must be advised of CYP who have Long Covid and be aware of the adaptations available to them
#### Constraints
- The article notes a need for further research on appropriate educational and social adaptations for CYP with Long Covid

### Target Learners
- Children and young people (aged 5–18) with Long Covid

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining engagement with school and learning while managing persistent symptoms
- Minimising adverse educational, social and mental health impacts of Long Covid
