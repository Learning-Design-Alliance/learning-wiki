# Stakeholders should foster workable strategies to improve EE teaching and learning across disciplines and place GIS use in national education objectives

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "stakeholders in Nigerian education system – students, teachers, parents, school management, policy makers, politicians, entrepreneurs, government and non-governmental agencies should foster and awaken workable strategies that can improve teaching and learning of environmental education across disciplines". Additionally, "the crux of working with GIS within the local environment must be considered for placement in the national education objectives", so Nigeria can join the technology-driven use of GIS for personal, social, educational, and spatial development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination among students, teachers, parents, school management, policy makers, politicians, entrepreneurs, and government and non-governmental agencies
#### Constraints
- EGIS is not currently feasible for adoption into the teaching-learning process in Nigeria

### Target Learners
- Junior secondary school pupils in Nigeria

### Target Learning Goals
- Environmental education across disciplines for sustainable development
