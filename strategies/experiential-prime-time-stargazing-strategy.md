# Present star maps as an experiential, hands-on event with time fixed a few hours after sunset and seasons organizing the sky

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that the Ojibwe and D(L/N)akota Sky Star Maps "are best presented by transforming the discussion into an experiential, hands-on event". The discussion is simplified by fixing the observing time as a few hours after sunset, called 'prime time' for stargazing, and by organizing the sky by the four seasons: facing south shows the current season's stars, the previous season sets in the west and the following season rises in the east. The authors note this highly visual, holistic and cooperative learning environment is more consistent with a traditional native learning style (Cleary and Peacock 1997).
