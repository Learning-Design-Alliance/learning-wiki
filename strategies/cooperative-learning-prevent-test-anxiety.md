## Description
Based on the finding that students' anxiety is strongly connected to performance anxiety in tests, the article recommends preventing this documented cause of stress "by promoting creative interactions through cooperative learning of STEM subjects", noting this was tested in chemistry teaching classes. The strategy targets the assessment-centred anxiety detected in the students' forma mentis networks rather than anxiety about STEM subjects themselves.
