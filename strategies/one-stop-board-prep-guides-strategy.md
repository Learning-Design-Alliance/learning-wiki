# Aggregate board exam prep resources into one-stop student-facing guides, portals, or boot camps

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends consolidating all available board exam prep resources in one place regardless of funding unit. Directors who developed "LibGuides, resource portals, or “board prep boot camps,” that consolidate all available resources in one place" reported these initiatives were "widely credited with raising student awareness and boosting engagement." This strategy addresses the discovery problem when resources are funded across departments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires coordination across funding units so the guide covers library and non-library funded resources alike
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness is self-reported by directors; the study did not measure student learning outcomes from these guides

### Target Learners
- medical students in colleges of osteopathic medicine programs

### Target Learning Goals
- effective discovery and use of institutionally provided board exam preparation resources
