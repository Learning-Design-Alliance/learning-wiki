# Self-Regulation - Mindfulness Techniques

## Description
Incorporate mindfulness techniques to help learners manage their initial emotional impulses and reactivity. Self-regulation is the ability to alter and regulate our emotional and behavioral responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a facilitator.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find this activity to be limiting or not helpful.
