# Policy strategies to personalize learning: competency-based progressions, phased implementation, networks, and interoperability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper's policy section recommends that districts support personalized learning plans, equitable technology access, personalized professional learning, phased implementation, and networks; that states promote competency-based progressions, broader definitions of success, and innovation investments; and that national organizations address data interoperability. It notes that districts can "organize work in phases, and leverage networks," and highlights Project Unicorn's goal of a seamless, secure, and controlled exchange of data between applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher leadership development; flexible federal, state, and local policies; waivers from seat-time and other regulations; sustained local leadership
#### Constraints
- Competency-based learning at scale challenges the basic architecture and organization of schools; barriers may arise as programs mature; mountains of rules and traditions still inhibit work at scale; some measures of school quality are still in early development

### Target Learners
- K-12 students across district, state, and federal policy contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- Mastery of critical knowledge, skills, and dispositions needed for success in college and career
