# Develop grading rubrics that credit deeper expert-like analysis even when the final answer is incorrect

## Description
Because partially correct responses imply different levels of expertise, the article recommends objective grading schemes that distinguish types of incorrect responses. Rubrics should "favor students who provide better responses involving deeper analysis" similar to experts "although the answers are incorrect". Rubrics can be built by expert theoretical analysis of the problem plus categorizing student responses by quality of conceptual analysis and decision making.
