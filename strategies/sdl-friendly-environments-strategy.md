# Healthcare institutions should prioritize SDL-friendly environments including protected learning time, access to digital tools, and mentorship programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the review's findings, the authors recommend that "institutions should prioritize SDL-friendly environments—such as providing protected learning time, access to digital tools, and mentorship programs." The review reports that mentorship programs pairing junior staff with experienced healthcare workers support goal-setting, access to resources, and constructive feedback. It also suggests hospitals encouraging open communication and offering digital learning platforms enable healthcare professionals to pursue SDL while balancing clinical responsibilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutions must provide protected learning time, access to digital tools, and mentorship programs to create SDL-friendly environments.
#### Constraints
- The review notes challenges such as information overload and technical issues must be addressed to optimize digital tool use, and that in resource-constrained settings, availability of digital tools and reliable internet access may limit effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in clinical settings, including junior staff and experienced healthcare workers

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-directed learning and continuous professional development
