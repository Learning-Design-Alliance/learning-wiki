# Use cooperative learning settings such as studios to develop LAs' teamwork skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because teamwork was the learning category consistently identified least, the authors recommend attending to teamwork in LA programs: "Including cooperative learning in classroom setting (such as studios) appears an effective way to develop team work skills both for the students in studios [16] and for the LAs facilitating studios." The recommendation follows their finding that studio contexts designed for group interaction elicited teamwork learning from all interviewed studio LAs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom settings such as studios where LAs facilitate student group work
#### Constraints
- The authors label the underlying data preliminary, drawn from LAs' self-reported interview responses

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering learning assistants
- students in large-enrollment STEM courses

### Target Learning Goals
- teamwork and group-facilitation skills
