# Embed discussion and high-quality explanations within explicit, step-by-step mathematics instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends combining explicit instruction with opportunities for problem-solving discussion and high-quality explanations. It reports that Fuchs et al. (2016) "combined explicit instruction with high-quality explanations, resulting in improved content understanding, particularly for students with limited working memory," and suggests researchers "embed discussion within step-by-step instruction."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intervention must provide step-by-step modeling and opportunities for practice with targeted feedback, as prior reviews found for explicit instruction
#### Constraints
- The article states explicit instruction by itself may not allow students to benefit from problem-solving discussions with teachers and peers

### Target Learners
- students with mathematics difficulty or disability in grades 4 through 12

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics problem solving
- fractions understanding
- general mathematics skills
