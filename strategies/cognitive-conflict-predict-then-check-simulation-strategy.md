# Induce cognitive conflict by having students predict, check predictions against simulations, and reconcile mismatches with guidance

## Description
The article recommends inducing "cognitive conflict" so students realize there is some inconsistency in their reasoning, then providing appropriate guidance and support. In the measurement QuILT, students first predict what they expect in various situations, then check their predictions using simulations. If prediction and observation do not match, students reach a state of cognitive conflict, and the QuILT provides guidance to reconcile the difference so they can build a good grasp of the relevant concepts.
