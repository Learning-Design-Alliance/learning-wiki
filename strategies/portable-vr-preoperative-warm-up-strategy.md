# Deploy portable low-cost VR simulation for ubiquitous learning and preoperative warm-up before surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using a portable, low-cost simulator such as SIMISGEST-VR for "preoperative warm-up exercises anytime, anywhere (ubiquitous learning)", including at home the night before surgery or in the operating theater on the day of surgery. It cites studies indicating that preoperative warm-up exercises performed for at least 15 min before the procedure improve soft-tissue handling during cholecystectomy, bimanual skill, efficiency and smoothness of movement, and depth perception, while reducing mistakes and operating time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A portable, low-cost simulation solution, since the large size and elevated costs of market VR simulators prohibit their use in the operating theater
#### Constraints
- The warm-up benefit is reported from studies the article cites; the article's own validated warm-up model using 3D reconstructions of patient images is still under development

### Target Learners
- practicing surgeons

### Target Learning Goals
- preoperative psychomotor readiness
- basic psychomotor skills in minimally invasive surgery
