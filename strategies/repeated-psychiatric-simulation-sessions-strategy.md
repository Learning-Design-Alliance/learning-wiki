# Schedule repeated simulation sessions across the psychiatric attachment and extend the program to other clinicians

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A recommendation, grounded in participant feedback and author argument, to provide recurring simulation training during the psychiatric attachment rather than a single exposure, and to extend training to nurses and allied health professionals. "Most participants reported that the program could be improved by having further simulation training during their psychiatric attachment", and the authors conclude that further development of simulation-based learning within a psychiatric setting is justified.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators, actors, and simulation equipment available for repeated sessions across the rotation
#### Constraints
- Grounded in participant feedback and author argument rather than a tested comparison of training doses

### Target Learners
- house officers on psychiatric rotations
- nurses and allied health professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- managing psychiatric emergencies
- retaining emergency management knowledge and experience through repetition
