# Publish national data on ALS uptake, completion, delay, exemption and any association with ARCP capability sign-off to evaluate the current system.

## Description
If policymakers remain unconvinced, the article argues the least they should do is evaluate the current system properly. Foundation schools or NHS England should publish data on uptake, completion, delay, exemption and any association with ARCP capability sign-off. Five years after ALS was removed from the mandatory foundation curriculum, the authors state we still cannot confidently describe the consequences.
