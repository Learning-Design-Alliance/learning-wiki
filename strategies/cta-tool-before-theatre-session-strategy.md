# Use a multimedia CTA tool prior to operating theatre sessions to build a cognitive foundation and shorten the learning curve

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that learners use a CTA tool such as IFINCTA before attending theatre: "Using this tool prior to attending the operating theatre will provide learners a sound understanding of the technical steps and the decision-making involved in this procedure." They argue this reduces the initial steep phase of the learning curve and allows more effective utilization of operating theatre training time in an environment of reduced training hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An accessible, web-based CTA tool covering the target procedure's steps, decision points and errors
#### Constraints
- Transfer validity to operating theatre performance has not yet been analyzed; the recommendation rests on simulation-based cognitive outcomes

### Target Learners
- novice surgical trainees and medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- procedural knowledge and decision-making before live operative experience
