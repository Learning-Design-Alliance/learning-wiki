Strategies

Use authentic pedagogical video materials (e.g., TV5 Monde applications) as the basis for audiovisual vocabulary lessons¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article demonstrates a concrete procedure using instructive videos with pedagogical references from TV5 Monde's "Enseigner le français avec TV5 monde" website, which teaches French as a foreign language "by means of pedagogical methods and in an amusing way". Two C1-level applications of about 15 minutes each were selected; their pronunciations were transformed into written texts of around 1200 words each, from which 30 unknown, relatively difficult words (mostly nouns and verbs) were selected for testing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to videos with accompanying written transcripts
  • Selection of unknown, relatively difficult target words from the transcripts

Constraints¶

  • Materials used were at C1 instruction level on the topics of terrorism and wars/conflicts

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate and graduate students of French language teaching

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning and retaining difficult French vocabulary

Claims¶

  • Vocabulary knowledge measured after audiovisual instruction remained stable between week one and week two, with changes restricted to one or two words [+W]
  • Learners taught French vocabulary with an audiovisual method (listening to video pronunciation while following the written script) achieved higher learning success rates in week one than learners taught with the conventional method [+W]
  • Learners taught with the audiovisual method showed higher vocabulary retention in week two than learners taught with the conventional method [+W]
  • Control-group participants showed declining retention success between week one and week two, with 13 of 14 participants declining [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Employ alternative vocabulary teaching methods in unnatural foreign language environments to ensure retention efficiency
  • Video Based Instruction
