# Close the workshop with open questions in particle physics, confronting students with the limits of the Standard Model

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
At the end of the session students sit again in a circle and a discussion introduces open questions: the Standard Model particles discussed during the workshop make up only about 4.6% of the matter-energy content of the universe, with the majority attributed to Dark Matter and Dark Energy. The article reports student reactions vary — most are excited and interested, some disappointed, and one student got upset, almost angry, not being able to accept the revelation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both parts of the workshop presented as a whole before the closing discussion
#### Constraints
- Reactions vary and some students may react negatively to the revelation

### Target Learners
- Key Stage 3 students (12-13 years old)

### Target Learning Goals
- Awareness of open questions in particle physics, dark matter and dark energy
