# Ongoing Feedback and Reflection

## Description
Learning management systems (LMS) such as Google Classroom, Canvas, and Schoology allow teachers to conduct formative and summative assessments during PBL. Using tools like Google Slides and Adobe Spark to share work or Microsoft’s Flipgrid to engage students in weekly reflections encourages students to give each other constructive feedback and share their wins, challenges, and next steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to LMS and assessment tools, commitment to providing regular feedback, guidance on constructive criticism, reliable means to create a safe space for discussion.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming for both teachers and students, potential for negative feedback, requires effective facilitation and clear protocols.
