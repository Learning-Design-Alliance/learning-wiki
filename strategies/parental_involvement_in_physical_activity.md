# Parental Involvement in Physical Activity

## Description
Engage parents by providing details about the benefits of physical activity and encouraging their participation. Parents can promote physical activity at home by taking walks or doing family activities focused on moving. They can also volunteer to supervise before or after-school programs or lead classroom exercise breaks. Active parental support increases the likelihood of program success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Communication with parents, volunteer opportunities, resources for promoting activity at home
#### Constraints
- Requires active parental participation. May be challenging to engage all parents due to time constraints or other factors.
