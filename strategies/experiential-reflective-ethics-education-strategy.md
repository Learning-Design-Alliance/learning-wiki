# Prioritize experiential and reflective ethics education over didactic approaches in nursing curricula and continuing professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that patient rights education is associated with higher PCC competency and that certified post-graduation ethics training was rare (7.1%), the article recommends that "Nursing curricula and in‐service training programs should prioritize experiential and reflective ethics education over didactic approaches". It also suggests structured case discussions, ethics rounds, and reflective practice sessions at the practice level. The authors cite evidence that didactic training may not translate into improved patient-centred behaviours.
