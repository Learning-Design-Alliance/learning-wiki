# Use a three-part partner agreements intervention of individual reflections, partner agreement forms, and partner reflection forms to scaffold equitable group dynamics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention gives students an active role in preempting and resolving problematic group dynamics themselves, aiming to encourage cooperative group work. It comprises a one-time individual reflection assignment inspired by values affirmation, partner agreement forms completed collectively each time students enter a new group that require "an explicit conversation about how work would be split or shared," and collective partner reflection forms completed when students return to the same group. Forms carried no grade points and were completed at the start of class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must complete the forms at the start of class before proceeding with lab activities, with TAs requiring completion
#### Constraints
- The article notes some students may choose not to make maximal use of the forms since they carried no points, and this was deliberate as part of what the study tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory physics lab courses working in groups of two or three

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable division of lab labor, group communication, and norm setting
