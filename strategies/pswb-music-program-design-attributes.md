# Design school music programs for PSWB with small at-risk groups, private settings, sufficient length, wellbeing focus, and participatory delivery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
From critical reflection analysis 2, the article recommends programs recruit groups of more than four and less than ten students, target at-risk participants, engage students in the program's purpose, avoid classroom delivery, use private settings, run at minimum between seven and 12 weeks, focus on wellbeing rather than curriculum, target the needs of a specific group, employ a quality facilitator, use a participatory and democratic approach, and foster the appropriation of music as a PSWB resource.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator skilled in engaging participants and using music to address PSWB goals; students must understand the purpose and value of the program
#### Constraints
- The article states following each recommendation will not guarantee benefits, and it may often not be possible to follow each recommendation in the real world

### Target Learners
- at-risk secondary school students
- mainstream school students

### Target Learning Goals
- psychosocial wellbeing
- social connection
- self-efficacy
- engagement
