# Propaganda Technique Identification

## Description
Learners are taught about propaganda techniques and then asked to identify the use of these techniques in online advertising. They reflect on how the interactive medium of online reading can increase or decrease the power of a particular technique.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples of online advertisements and instructional materials on propaganda techniques.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness depends on the learners' prior knowledge of propaganda techniques, and they may require guidance in identifying and analyzing them.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Increase awareness of propaganda techniques and their use in online advertising, and to understand how the interactive nature of online media affects their impact.

### Affordances
- Enhances critical thinking and media literacy skills by enabling learners to recognize and analyze persuasive techniques used in advertising.

### Personalization
- The complexity of the propaganda techniques and the advertisements selected for analysis can be adjusted based on the learners' skill level.
