# Select match-grade, double-polished Airsoft spheres for drag-measurement labs seeking better accuracy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For teachers implementing the radar drag-coefficient lab, the article recommends choosing Airsoft spheres that are "match grade" and "double polished" to eliminate the seam and maintain tighter dimensional and mass tolerances. The 200 mg double-polished sphere produced an SEM of 0.9% versus 3.2% for the seamed 112 mg sphere in the reported measurements.
