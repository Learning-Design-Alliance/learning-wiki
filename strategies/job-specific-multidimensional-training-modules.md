# Develop job-specific, multidimensional training modules differentiated by occupational role

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes developing curricula "differentiated for distinct occupational groups" covering competencies "from technical knowledge to communication and emotional regulation." This strategy responds to the finding that current programs primarily target physicians and nurses while neglecting transport staff, environmental services, and administrative teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- guaranteeing protected training time, offering incentives, linking training to career advancement
#### Constraints
- Conducted during healthcare labor disputes, which precluded participation of some frontline professionals such as medical residents

### Target Learners
- infection control personnel
- physicians and nursing personnel
- administrative support personnel

### Target Learning Goals
- role-specific response competencies
- communication and emotional regulation
- critical care capacity
