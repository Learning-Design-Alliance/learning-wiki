# Offer virtual office hours at varied times plus an asynchronous discussion board and chat option

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends establishing virtual office hours held "at different times of the day" so students in different time zones can connect, noting these live one-on-one or few-on-one sessions let students ask questions after reflecting on material and homework. It also recommends an asynchronous discussion board on the learning management system (e.g., Blackboard or Canvas) that keeps a record of discussions, and offering office hours via virtual chat instead of video for students whose internet connections do not reliably support video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning management system with a discussion board feature and a videoconferencing or chat tool
#### Constraints
- Students are spread across time zones and some have internet connections that cannot reliably support video interfaces

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students studying remotely from home

### Target Learning Goals
- getting questions answered after independent work on material
- peer and instructor discussion of challenging content
