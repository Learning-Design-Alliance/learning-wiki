# Assign courses to faculty based on training and expertise, with active researchers teaching graduate courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that 'Course assignment to faculty should be done based on their training', matching instructors to introductory or advanced courses according to where they teach best, and that core graduate courses be taught by faculty with a PhD in the related area. Graduate faculty 'relatively active in research' should be given preference because active researchers can make graduate courses more interesting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignment processes that match faculty training to course level, with core graduate courses taught by faculty holding a PhD in the related area.
#### Constraints
- The article notes faculty and administrators are also equally affected by the pandemic and may not be ready to make drastic changes.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- quality of physics course instruction and graduate teaching
