Shift attendance measurement from seat time to engagement, participation, and student outcomes in distance and hybrid learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that states rewrite attendance laws and regulations so engagement can be tracked through competency-based tasks rather than seat time. It states that 'the role of attendance in a hybrid, student-centered learning system shifts from time spent in class to engagement, participation, and student outcomes.' This strategy is offered so attendance policy supports student-centered distance and blended learning rather than enforcing time in physical classrooms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • States must rewrite attendance laws and regulations to allow tracking of student engagement through competency-based tasks

Constraints¶

  • The report notes many states currently need such rewrites, implying existing regulations assume in-person seat time

Target Learners¶

  • k-12 students participating in distance, hybrid, or student-centered learning

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained engagement, participation, and competency-based progress during disrupted schooling

Related Strategies¶

  • Competency Based Progression
  • Competency Based Learning
  • Use course grades data reports to identify and reach out to students who most need different supports, similar to ninth-grade on-track teams
