# Implement CIRC through heterogeneous four-member groups reading, discussing, presenting and summarizing a shared text

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a concrete CIRC lesson sequence: the teacher forms heterogeneous or mixed-ability groups of four, gives each group a suitable text or clipping, students read and work together to find the main idea and write suggestions, groups present their results, and the teacher gives support or solutions before making a summary with the students. In the study this cycle was repeated over four meetings with the experimental group. The authors recommend teachers "should use various and appropriate strategies or techniques such as CIRC in the process of teaching reading comprehension".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of four students from heterogeneous or mixed-ability groupings
- A text or clipping suitable with the topic
- Group presentation of results and a jointly made summary
#### Constraints
