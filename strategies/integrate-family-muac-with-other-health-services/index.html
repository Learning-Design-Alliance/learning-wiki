Integrate caregiver-led child nutrition monitoring with other health services to increase sustainability¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends embedding Family MUAC with two-way SMS into existing health service touchpoints so caregivers already visiting facilities can be enrolled and followed up efficiently. Healthcare workers "suggested that integrating Family MUAC supported by two-way SMS with other health services would lead to greater sustainability," for example enrolling women attending PMTCT services. This positions the intervention within routine care rather than as a standalone program.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Health facilities with existing maternal and child health service contacts through which caregivers can be enrolled

Constraints¶

  • The article reports this as healthcare worker perception and recommendation, not as a tested effect

Target Learners¶

  • Caregivers of children aged 5–12 months in western Kenya

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained caregiver skill in measuring and reporting child mid-upper arm circumference

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed new solutions into existing operations and procedures to sustain and scale them
  • Provide universal well-baby screening and home-visit services regardless of income, following Victoria, Australia's model
