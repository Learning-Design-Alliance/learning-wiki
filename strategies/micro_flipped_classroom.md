# Micro Flipped Classroom

## Description
Short video lectures are distributed as study material along with short assignments. The rest of the lecture and assignments are conducted during the classroom time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creation of concise video lectures and targeted assignments.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for complex topics requiring more in-depth pre-class preparation.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Suitable for learners who benefit from focused, bite-sized content and immediate application.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Introduce key concepts succinctly, reinforce learning through short assignments, facilitate in-class application and discussion.

### Affordances
- Keeps pre-class material manageable and focused, allows for immediate reinforcement in class.

### Personalization
- Short assignments can be tailored to different skill levels.
