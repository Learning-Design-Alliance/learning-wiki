# Invest in facilitator development and interdisciplinary collaboration to scale VR-based simulation learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article suggests that investing in facilitator development is essential for scaling VR-based learning, and that collaborative development and delivery of simulations across professional programs supports facilitation quality. The article reports that "Educators who had completed a basic facilitation course reported feeling more competent in guiding reflections and applying feedback/feedforward principles", and that interdisciplinary collaboration among facilitators was perceived as enriching and resource-efficient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A basic facilitation course for educators
- Collaborative development and delivery of simulations across educational programs
#### Constraints
- VR simulations require more time and personnel compared to traditional lectures

### Target Learners
- Educators and facilitators in higher healthcare education

### Target Learning Goals
- Professional development in facilitation, feedback, and guided reflection
