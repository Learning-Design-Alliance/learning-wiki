# Deliver virtual labs synchronously via live video conference and post recordings to the LMS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A delivery strategy in which the lab class is fully web-enhanced through a learning management system: live sessions are run over video conference, and recordings are added to the LMS so students can also work asynchronously. The manual states that a "Learning management system (LMS) was used to fully web enhance the lab class" and that "Virtual labs were delivered by using live video conference technology and recorded lab sessions were added to LMS." The class was tested with both synchronous and asynchronous virtual delivery methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An LMS, live video conference technology, and recorded lab session videos
#### Constraints
- Tested with virtual delivery methods (synchronous and asynchronous) in an introductory physics lab

### Target Learners
- undergraduate introductory physics students in remote or virtual courses

### Target Learning Goals
- participation in laboratory learning experiences remotely
