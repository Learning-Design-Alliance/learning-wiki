## Description
A dialogue recipe for the requirement extraction phase: 'after the instructor states an initial goal, the AI assistant runs a structured dialogue to elicit the four pedagogical grammatical elements'. For each component the assistant asks guidance questions, checks whether responses are specific enough to translate into design constraints, and proposes a small set of candidate options to support refinement through selection and revision, granting instructors maximum authority to shape the design.
