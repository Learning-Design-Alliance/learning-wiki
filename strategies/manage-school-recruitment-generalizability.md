# Actively manage school recruitment to protect the generalizability of impact studies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The guide treats recruitment as a design task, not an afterthought: researchers should be "managing school recruitment" as one of the key steps for making impact study findings generalizable. Recruitment management is positioned between sample selection and the assessment of sample-population differences in the guide's sequence of recommended actions.
