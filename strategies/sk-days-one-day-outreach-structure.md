# Run one-day mathematics outreach events as a structured program of workshops, talks, and problem-solving sessions for female students aged 7-17

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SK Days are one-day events held at colleges and universities, organized with AWM sponsorship, consisting of "a program of workshops, talks, and problem solving sessions for female students aged 7-17". The activities are designed to encourage young women to continue studying mathematics and to learn about educational possibilities. The article details the day's structure: registration, an introduction to the AWM, a presentation on Kovalevsky's life and obstacles, icebreakers, then small-group activity sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A hosting institution with rooms, volunteers, and a registration process with parental forms
#### Constraints
- Events are designed for female students aged 7-17

### Target Learners
- female students aged 7-17

### Target Learning Goals
- continue studies in mathematics
- learn about educational possibilities
