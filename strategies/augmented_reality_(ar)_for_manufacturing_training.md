# Augmented Reality (AR) for Manufacturing Training

## Description
AR provides step-by-step instructions while a trainee is practicing, using AR headsets to provide step-by-step instructions while a trainee is practicing. With hands-free devices, new hires can use images and videos as a part of their initial training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AR headsets (e.g., smartglasses), AR software, stable network connectivity, trained instructors.
#### Constraints
- High initial setup costs, requires technical expertise, software must be updated regularly.
