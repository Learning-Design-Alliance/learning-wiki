# Assign group science projects with English oral presentations as CLIL implementation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's procedure: form groups of 4-5 students after a pretest, assign each group a project in mathematics, physics, biology, or chemistry, have them "prepare a paper and power point for oral presentation," give a week for preparation, then hold group presentations with question-and-answer sessions and lecturer comments, all in English.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pretest to establish English ability and distribute students into groups
- One week preparation time
- All presentations done in English
