# Conclude online tutoring sessions with a prompt for students to identify and explain what they learned

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend that online tutoring sessions conclude with a prompt for students to identify what they learned and explain how they know they learned it. This gives students the opportunity to think about their own mathematical understanding and gives tutors a window into student thinking. The recommendation arises from the finding that some students spontaneously identified specific learning outcomes even within an unstructured postsession prompt, suggesting that consistent progress monitoring may engage students in thinking about their own learning.
