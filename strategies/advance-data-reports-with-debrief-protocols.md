# Send EQUIP data reports in advance of debriefs and use discussion protocols to guide data conversations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To support teachers reviewing vulnerable data about their own practice, researchers deliberately emailed data reports before debriefing conversations, giving teachers time to process results. Discussion protocols guided conversations to highlight successes and areas for improvement, and debriefs focused on future brainstorming of teaching changes to bring hope and optimism rather than dwelling on shortcomings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time between data delivery and discussion
- A discussion protocol for the debrief conversation
#### Constraints
- Recording lessons may feel disruptive to some teachers and students, and video may not capture every interaction

### Target Learners
- teachers and instructors reviewing their own EQUIP data

### Target Learning Goals
- productive data sense-making and instructional improvement
