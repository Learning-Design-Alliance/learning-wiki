# Support collaborative learning activities through sharing, problem-solving scaffolding, social regulation, and immediate feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy covers the chatbot's pedagogical support during collaborative learning: promoting sharing via visualization tools and real-time shared workspaces; enhancing collaborative problem-solving with step-by-step task guidance, example deliverables, and follow-up questions; supporting social regulation by helping teams set and display achievement goals, plan, check fair role distribution, and reflect on plan adherence; and providing immediate feedback when teams request help, including referencing open educational materials when the chatbot cannot answer directly.
