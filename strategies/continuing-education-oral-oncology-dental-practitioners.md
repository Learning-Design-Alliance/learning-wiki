# Promote continuing education programs, workshops, and certifications on managing oral complications in cancer patients for practicing dentists

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beyond undergraduate curricula, the authors recommend post-qualification provision: continuing education programs, workshops, and certifications focused on managing oral complications in cancer patients. They write that "Given the knowledge gaps identified among dental students and interns, continuing education programs, workshops, and certifications focusing on managing oral complications in cancer patients should be promoted," with the stated aim of helping practicing dentists stay updated with advancements in oral oncology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of continuing-education offerings on oral oncology for qualified dentists
#### Constraints
- Generalizability of the underlying evidence is primarily limited to Saudi Arabia and similar Middle Eastern contexts, per the authors' own scope statement

- Dental interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Up-to-date management of oral complications in cancer patients

## Claims

- [Only 24.2% of surveyed Saudi senior dental students and interns demonstrated good knowledge of oral complications of cancer therapy in a cross-sectional survey (n = 506)](../claims/24-percent-good-knowledge-oral-complications-cancer-therapy.md) [+W]
- [Specific knowledge items showed very low correct-response rates, including effects of high-dose therapy on child development (17.2%) and pre-radiotherapy oral care benefits (15.8%)](../claims/lowest-scoring-knowledge-items-cancer-therapy.md) [+W]
- [Dental interns demonstrated higher knowledge of oral complications of cancer therapy than senior dental students (29.7% vs. 21.6% good knowledge, p < 0.05)](../claims/interns-exceed-students-oral-complications-knowledge.md) [+W]
