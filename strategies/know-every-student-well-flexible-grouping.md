# Know every student well through many adults, multi-year teacher loops, and flexible small-group instruction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A central quality of the case study schools is that every student is known well by many teachers, not just one. Teachers probe into each student's interests, home background, parents, culture, learning style, and academic strengths and weaknesses, and schools tap the community to personalize instruction. Schools use very little whole-class teaching: students work in flexible small groups or learning stations on personalized lessons, with further differentiation within groups, and Montgomery Center loops middle school subject teachers with the same students for three years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structures ensuring many adults know each student, not just one teacher
#### Constraints
- The schools use very little whole-class teaching and no grading on a curve or teachers working in isolation

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in high-poverty public schools
- including English learners and students in special education

### Target Learning Goals
- personalized instruction reaching high achievement and equity for every student
