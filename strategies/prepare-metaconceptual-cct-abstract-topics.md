# Prepare conceptual change texts enriched with metaconceptual activities for mathematics and science topics containing abstract concepts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q4` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the results, the author recommends that "conceptual change texts which are enriched with metaconceptual activities related to all subjects of mathematics and science courses containing abstract concepts can be prepared", so that learning becomes more meaningful and permanent and misconceptions are surfaced. The strategy extends the tested symmetry materials to other abstract topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of the topic's common misconceptions from the literature before writing the texts
#### Constraints
- The study tested the approach only with symmetry among teacher candidates; other topics are extrapolation

### Target Learners
- Students in mathematics and science courses encountering abstract concepts

### Target Learning Goals
- Meaningful and permanent conceptual learning; surfacing and correcting misconceptions
