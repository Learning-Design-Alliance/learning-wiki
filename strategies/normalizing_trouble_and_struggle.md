# Normalizing Trouble and Struggle

## Description
Creating a classroom culture where challenges and failures are seen as opportunities for growth and learning. This involves acknowledging struggles, encouraging students to embrace challenges, and emphasizing that problem-solving skills are explicitly taught and transferable across settings. Reframing mistakes and struggles as essential parts of the learning process helps students develop resilience and a growth mindset.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom environment that values effort and growth, explicit instruction in problem-solving strategies, and opportunities for students to reflect on their processes.
#### Constraints
- Requires a shift in mindset for both teachers and students. May take time to establish a classroom culture where students feel safe sharing their struggles.
