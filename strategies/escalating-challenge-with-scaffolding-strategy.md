# Deliberate escalation of challenge across years, with scaffolding and retrospective communication of purpose

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The programme designs a deliberate escalation of challenge across the degree: a first-year short group project with a defined outcome, a second-year semester-long self-designed experiment, and a final-year independently managed year-long project with an external stakeholder. When second-year students found the workload unmanageable, staff responded by "offering one-on-one coaching where needed", adding scaffolding and resources, and reducing unnecessary barriers, while communicating the purpose behind design choices and sharing retrospective feedback from previous cohorts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Challenges must be paired with support such as scaffolding, resources and coaching where workload becomes unmanageable
- Communication of the purpose behind design choices, including retrospective feedback from earlier cohorts
#### Constraints
- The balance was not always right: some second-year students experienced the workload as unmanageable despite the escalation design

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- resilience and independence in tackling open-ended problems
- forward planning and time management
