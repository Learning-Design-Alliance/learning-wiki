# Test, track and extend readiness support programs for disadvantaged children

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors close with recommendations for future work building on this study: effectiveness "should be tested with various samples", children whose readiness was supported "should be tracked throughout primary school to investigate the sustainability of the program", and new studies should examine joint effects of support programs and variables such as gender, age and time spent at the institution. They also recommend that institutions use such results to support an equal start to first grade for disadvantaged children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support to compare results across samples and track children longitudinally
#### Constraints
- Recommendations arise from a single-institution study of 40 children

### Target Learners
- Disadvantaged children in institutional care approaching first grade

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustained primary school readiness and equal start to first grade
