# Integrate multi-component simulation-plus-counseling strategies into midwifery curricula to strengthen respectful maternity care

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend combining wearable simulation with structured counseling as a curricular strategy: "Integrating multi-component educational strategies into midwifery curricula may strengthen both emotional and cognitive dimensions of respectful maternity care." The strategy pairs an experiential wearable birth simulation with weekly facilitated counseling on communication, empathy management, and academic progress, and the authors add that integrating simulation with broader educational and institutional strategies may support more consistent privacy-protective behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured counseling component delivered by trained facilitators alongside the simulation, with validated scenarios and standardized debriefing
#### Constraints
- Behavioral change in privacy-protective practices did not occur to the same extent as attitudinal change; findings come from a single institutional and cultural setting and lack long-term outcome data

### Target Learners
- final-year midwifery students
- nursing and health professions students in obstetric education

### Target Learning Goals
- empathy
- privacy protection awareness
- respectful maternity care
