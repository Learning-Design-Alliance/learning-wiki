# Convene cross-sector expert panels to sustain policy-relevant evidence during data disruptions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a webinar that “brings together academic and government experts” to discuss how education research can keep offering meaningful information to policymakers and practitioners after COVID data disruptions. The approach pairs researchers with government experts to address measurement, missing-data stopgaps, federal data collection, and remote equity interventions. It functions as a knowledge-sharing mechanism rather than an empirical study.
