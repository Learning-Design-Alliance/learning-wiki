# The Pencil Pledge

## Description
The Pencil Pledge is a classroom management technique that helps create a positive learning environment by promoting respect for materials and self-regulation. It involves having students commit to using pencils responsibly, taking care of classroom supplies, and maintaining a respectful attitude towards learning. This approach encourages students to be mindful of their behavior and to take ownership of their learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a clear and concise pledge. Regular reinforcement and reminders of the pledge. Consistent implementation by the teacher.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if not consistently enforced. Some students may not take the pledge seriously. Requires ongoing effort from the teacher to maintain.
