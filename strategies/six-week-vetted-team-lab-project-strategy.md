# Structure a short team experimental project within a lab course, with vetted proposals replicating published experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The redesigned course gave students six weeks to complete an experimental project in teams of four, requiring them to research an existing experiment, design apparatus and procedure, predict results with a model, carry out the experiment and analysis, and report findings. To ensure success in the short time, students had to choose an experiment already published in an undergraduate-level journal, and instructors vetted projects for feasibility and ambition at the proposal stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experimental labs in the first six weeks teaching technical skills and easing the transition to the open-ended project phase
- Instructor vetting of project proposals for feasibility and ambition
#### Constraints
- The short timeline was itself reported by students as a challenge, including coming up with an appropriate research question

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate engineering physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- modeling
- design of experiments
- teamwork
- technical skills with apparatus and data analysis
