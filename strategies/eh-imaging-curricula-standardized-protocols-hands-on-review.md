# Incorporate endolymphatic hydrops imaging into radiology curricula with standardized assessment protocols and hands-on case review

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends formalized training for hydrops MRI interpretation: structured training or mentorship programs led by experienced radiologists, and incorporation of EH imaging into existing head-and-neck curricula "particularly with standardized assessment protocols and hands-on case review", to enhance diagnostic reproducibility among radiologists at various stages of training. It grounds this in the newly certified radiologist's low intraobserver agreement and notes residents often lack formal training and hands-on experience despite high awareness of advanced imaging terms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Centers that perform hydrops assessments with experienced radiologists available to lead mentorship or structured training
#### Constraints
- Hydrops imaging is not yet routinely integrated into clinical practice in many countries, remaining primarily a research topic

### Target Learners
- radiology residents
- newly certified radiologists

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate and reproducible interpretation of endolymphatic hydrops MRI scans
