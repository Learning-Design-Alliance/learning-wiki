# Localize scenario-based emergency training with local faculty, translated materials, and debriefings in the local language

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An implementable recipe for transferring simulation-based emergency training to a new setting: recruit local senior staff as faculty, translate validated learning materials into the local language, align scenarios with local clinical guidelines, and hold debriefings in that language. The article states: "Another strength was our contextual approach, including the use of Swahili in learning materials and debriefing sessions", and the authors believe the training "is transferable to other health facilities that aim to improve their PPH-management".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validated learning materials in the clinical domain that can be translated into the local language
- Local senior clinicians willing to serve as facilitators and operators after a preparatory course
- Alignment between training content and local institutional guidelines for the emergency
#### Constraints
- The strategy was evaluated in one zonal consultant hospital; the authors call for randomized controlled trials in countries where this training is not yet widely established

### Target Learners
- Maternity staff in low-resource hospital departments

### Target Learning Goals
- Prevention and management of postpartum hemorrhage in local clinical context
