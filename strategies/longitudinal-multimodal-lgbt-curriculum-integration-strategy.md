# Integrate LGBT health systematically across undergraduate and postgraduate curricula using multi-modal interactive methods and validated competence assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that LGBT health be "systematically integrated into undergraduate and postgraduate curricula, with explicit learning objectives and a minimum number of dedicated instructional hours". It further recommends moving beyond lectures to interactive methods such as case-based discussions, role-play, standardized patient encounters, e-learning modules, and patient narratives, and incorporating LGBT-DOCSS-JP-based assessment into program evaluation.
