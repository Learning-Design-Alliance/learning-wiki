# Place a simple '0/1' label above the measurement icon to cue both measurement-convention camps to interpret measurement as in the Z basis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because courses differ on whether unspecified measurement defaults to the Z basis or is meaningless without a basis, the authors recommend, for reusable curricular materials, that "a simple “0/1” above the measurement icon appears to adequately cue students accustomed to either convention to treat the measurement as in the standard (Z) basis." The notation avoids introducing the word 'basis' while remaining interpretable to both camps. This emerged from v1.0 interviews and was beta-tested across variants in v2.0.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment or curriculum materials that will be reused across courses with differing measurement notational conventions
#### Constraints
- Educators should be mindful that both notational conventions coexist, and students may bring the opposite convention from prior courses or online materials

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory quantum computing courses

### Target Learning Goals
- interpreting measurement operations in quantum circuit diagrams
