# Expand the science-based comparative culture approach to non-science faculties in modified form as material for 'physics for all'

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The authors report expanding the experimental course to students in the Faculties of Education, Arts and Letters, Economies and Law in a modified form, planned to start in 2007. They argue that science-based comparative culture is ideal material for 'physics for all' because evidence-based decision making, a key concept in scientific literacy, can be studied by using the five senses. They hope a variety of materials used in this approach will be developed for the future.
