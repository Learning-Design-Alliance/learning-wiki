# Self-Evaluation in Writing

## Description
Self-evaluation involves teaching students how to review their writing to decide if changes are needed. By modeling when to stop and evaluate writing and providing specific questions to ask about each paragraph, students can learn to identify areas for improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Writing assignment; facilitator; self-evaluation checklist or questions
#### Constraints
- May be challenging for some students to self-assess; requires consistent practice and feedback; effectiveness depends on student engagement
