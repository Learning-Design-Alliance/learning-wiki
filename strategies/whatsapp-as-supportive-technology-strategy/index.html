Use WhatsApp as a supportive technology alongside traditional instruction, with careful attention to message timing¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article concludes that WhatsApp use in the education process should be encouraged as a supportive technology: it "has the potential of a natural educational technology and the qualities to contribute to education as a supportive technology." Implementation should send short expert-authored information messages concurrently with the curriculum, include supportive images, and take precautions on timing and group discipline. The authors list follow-up needs: researching other courses, minimizing drawbacks, testing other multimedia elements, experimentally verifying the qualitative data, consulting educators, and repeating with other grades.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Messages timed to avoid disrupting lessons; students informed of the group's purpose

Constraints¶

  • It is early to know WhatsApp's impact on education; findings come from one subject, one grade, and one school context

Target Learners¶

  • Secondary education students with smartphone access

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving academic success in subject-matter courses through mobile social learning support

Claims¶

  • Supporting traditional instruction with WhatsApp information messages produces significantly greater achievement gains than traditional instruction alone in 10th-grade literature [+W]
  • Student success scores increased in both the traditional and the WhatsApp-supported environments [+W]
  • Most experimental-group students wanted similar WhatsApp applications used in their other courses [+W]
  • Students reported that learning took place unconsciously from following WhatsApp messages without deliberate revision [+W]
  • Students reported that information messages sent with supportive images increased their interest and helped recall [+W]
  • A minority of students raised negative opinions about the timing of posts and redundant messages within the group [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adopt mobile messaging applications, especially WhatsApp, across English-language teaching
