# Brief professional development plus sequenced curriculum units with videoed implementation to support Pre-K teachers' engineering teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention model pairs two short professional development sessions with structured, sequenced curriculum enactment: "All intervention teachers received two 2.5-hour professional development sessions to provide background information about the EDP and a challenge that demonstrated the approach of teaching integrated STEM problem solving." Teachers then received units in sequence on a predetermined schedule and recorded each implemented unit activity with a provided camera, enabling fidelity coding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research-team-provided video cameras and unit delivery on a predetermined schedule
- Comparison teachers continue business-as-usual STEM activities while both groups implement Unit 8 for outcome comparison
#### Constraints
- Piloted with Head Start Pre-K teachers in central Massachusetts; PD was only two 2.5-hour sessions

### Target Learners
- Pre-K and early childhood teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- engineering design process knowledge
- integrated STEM teaching practice
- teaching self-efficacy
