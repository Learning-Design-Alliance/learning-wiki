# Use arctan rather than tan⁻¹ notation to prevent inverse/reciprocal confusion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After a student mistook tan⁻¹(1/y²) for a reciprocal and derailed a continuity problem, the author recommends avoiding the inverse-function superscript notation when named arc functions exist. He reports warning students in class that arctan is safer, and argues that "good notation is golden and in this case it would result at least in students not confusing the inverse function with the reciprocal of the function." He criticizes textbooks and calculator keys that default to the f⁻¹ style.
