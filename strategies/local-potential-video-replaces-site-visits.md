# Use local-potential-integrated science videos to replace costly, risky, and inefficient site visits

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating local potentials into science learning video, motivated by seven documented problems of visiting an actual MHP plant: inefficient allocated time, high transportation cost, high safety risks, unpredictable weather, difficulty handling students, students paying less attention, and students being easily distracted. The video "gives realistic presentations about an MHP plant without the students having to visit it," and students may replay it many times at any time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The video must be developed for natural science learning and validated by experts, containing natural science concepts integrated with the local potential
#### Constraints
- A limitation in the use of learning video is the lack of hands on approach to the topic of MHP plant compared to directly visiting the plant; the authors suggest comparing or combining the two methods in further studies

### Target Learners
- junior high school students in regions with local potentials such as MHP plants

### Target Learning Goals
- improving students' SPS and concept mastery
