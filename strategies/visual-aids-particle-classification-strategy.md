# Use visual aids and particle-meaning classification activities when introducing orientational particles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's experimental treatment combined teacher-led classification of target particles by meaning with visual aids to "crystalize the students' understanding of PVs." Students worked with the concepts of particle functions, thinking deeply about particles and their meanings rather than memorizing whole phrasal-verb translations. This strategy is drawn directly from the article's reported procedure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher preparation classifying particles by meaning and function; availability of visual aids
#### Constraints
- Treatment in the study lasted only 25 minutes per group, and the author notes results may be applicable mainly in laboratory-like situations

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- learning meanings of orientational particles in phrasal verbs
