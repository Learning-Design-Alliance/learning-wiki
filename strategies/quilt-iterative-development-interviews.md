# Develop tutorials iteratively through one-on-one think-aloud interviews and classroom refinement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
QuILT development is a cyclical iterative process. Preliminary tutorials are drafted from documented common difficulties, then "implemented in one-on-one interviews with student volunteers, and modiﬁcations are made". "After such one-on-one implementation with at least half a dozen students, tutorials are tested and evaluated in classroom settings and reﬁned further." In the case studies, each tutorial underwent 1.5-hour think-aloud interviews with 6-7 student volunteers before classroom administration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One-on-one think-aloud interviews with student volunteers, with hints provided as appropriate
- Modifications based on interview feedback before classroom administration
