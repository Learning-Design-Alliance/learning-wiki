# Start early: foster a positive math climate at home and school to prevent the distal avoidance pathway

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the direct/distal pathway, the authors argue intervention must "start early and target the socio-cultural climate in which the child is embedded", since anxious attitudes may be transmitted from parents or teachers and early failure can spark a vicious spiral of avoidance. They advocate fostering a positive learning environment at home and in school, ideally with interventions in primary school that may show compound effects downstream and reduce the later need for desensitization protocols.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combined effort of schools and parents; early timing in primary school
#### Constraints
- The avoidance interpretation of the direct pathway is untested in this study; the authors propose testing it in younger children where the direct effect should be weaker or nonexistent

### Target Learners
- children in primary school
- parents and teachers as climate-shaping agents

### Target Learning Goals
- positive attitudes toward mathematics
- sustained engagement with math courses and number-reliant activities
