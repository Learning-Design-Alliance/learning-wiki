# Protocolize the prebriefing with a fiction contract, a preplanned written briefing script, and a case-design checklist

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The panel's top-priority improvement actions target prebriefing quality: setting a fiction contract for students and lecturers, providing a preplanned briefing script covering simulation objectives, scenario type, available materials, and assessment descriptors with a precise time limit, and giving lecturers a prebriefing checklist for setting up each clinical case. The article states panellists "suggested creating a fictional contract for students and lecturers and providing preplanned briefing script". This responds to student and lecturer reports of incomplete prebriefings that left little time to grasp the scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A consistent, preplanned, standardized written briefing script including orientation to the simulation experience, environment, available resources, and assessment tool descriptors
#### Constraints
- Recommended for simulation-based experiences with high psychological stakes, where facilitator preparation establishes a psychologically safe learning environment

### Target Learners
- postgraduate interprofessional simulation participants

### Target Learning Goals
- preparation for integrated simulation scenarios
- reduced unnecessary physiological tension and anxiety
