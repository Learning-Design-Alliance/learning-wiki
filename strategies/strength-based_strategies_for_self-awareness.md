# Strength-Based Strategies for Self-Awareness

## Description
Educators identify and support students by recognizing their strengths related to self-awareness and using these strengths to address challenges. Self-awareness includes identifying emotions, recognizing strengths and limitations, and understanding how one's actions impact others. Strategies involve identifying feelings, using journal reflections, estimating and predicting outcomes, and leveraging resources like the Learner Variability Navigator. The goal is to enhance students' understanding of themselves, their emotions, and their capabilities, fostering a growth mindset and improving academic and social outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires understanding of students' strengths, access to reflection prompts, vocabulary wheels, and resources like the Learner Variability Navigator. Educators need to facilitate activities and provide guidance on self-reflection.
#### Constraints
- May require time to identify individual student strengths and tailor strategies accordingly. Some students may struggle to reflect on their emotions. Activities need to be carefully chosen to avoid triggering or alienating students.
