# Seven-question scaffold with AI problem solving, game-based learning, and systems thinking to support proactive decision making

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes a scaffold of seven questions to encourage a proactive strategy in decision making about environmental change, linking pedagogical approaches to different questions. Three approaches are elaborated: AI problem solving following four steps from formulating the problem to executing a program that visualizes results; game-based learning in which students "assess the challenges presented in the game, think about and discuss the simulated future scenarios, make a decision, and observe and reflect on the result"; and systems thinking to handle stakeholder trade-offs and complexity.
