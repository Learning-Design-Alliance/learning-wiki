Implement SEL systemically as a public health approach with shared vision, adult SEL, aligned programs, and continuous improvement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that sustainable SEL requires supportive infrastructures and systemic efforts with core features: developing a shared vision integrating SEL with academic learning, building professional development infrastructure, establishing SEL learning standards, adopting evidence-based programs, integrating SEL into all school goals, communicating with parents, coordinating with mental health services, and establishing a staff learning community using data. It frames SEL as a universal Tier 1 intervention within a multi-tiered system of support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Effective teacher and staff training and administrative support are essential for effective SEL implementation and sustainable system change
  • A principal's active support for implementation of SEL programming is essential

Constraints¶

  • It is common for schools to adopt SEL curricula without providing essential initial training, ongoing coaching and mentoring, or technical support

Target Learners¶

  • all students in PreK–12 schools
  • school leaders and teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • social and emotional competencies
  • supportive school climate
  • equitable student outcomes
