# Train minority students' academic literacy alongside tool use, and incorporate collaborative study into their learning process

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that when computational tools are introduced to minority students, instructors must also train academic literacy skills such as "describing problems concretely for others to understand or making notes of learning experiences for future reference". It further recommends that "collaborative study should be incorporated in Tibetan students' learning process", given students' demonstrated preference for teamwork. The authors plan follow-up guidance on properly recording learning experiences with Mathematica.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must make an effort in training students' academic literacy alongside providing educational tools
#### Constraints
- The article states providing educational tools or techniques alone is far from sufficient to enhance minority students' learning processes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics students from ethnic minority groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of quantum mechanics
- Proficiency with computational tools
- Academic literacy skills
