# Blend tablet-assisted video training with traditional classroom training for CHWs with fewer years of formal education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the education–improvement relationship appeared only in the classroom-trained group, the authors propose supplementing traditional training with tablet-assisted training for CHWs with fewer years of formal education. They write that "it might therefore be useful to adopt a blended style learning program". Video also allows learners to rewind difficult segments and progress at their own pace, which classroom teaching does not permit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tablets pre-loaded with locally made videos in the local language, plus a half-day digital literacy orientation
#### Constraints
- Recommended on the basis of a pilot trial with a non-significant interaction term (beta −0.32, p = 0.10)

### Target Learners
- Community health workers in low- and middle-income countries, particularly those with fewer years of formal education

### Target Learning Goals
- Refresher training on clinical knowledge and skills, such as pneumonia iCCM care
