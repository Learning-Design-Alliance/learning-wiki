# Future directions: cross-component comparisons and multilingual embeddings for course overlap

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors outline extensions to CourseGraph in the discussion. They state that "Cross-wise comparisons between different course components, such as relating prerequisites to learning outcomes, could be explored," and that "multilingual embedding models [24] may enable comparison of courses offered in different languages." They also note that distinguishing full from partial overlap would better reflect course equivalence but requires overlap-extent annotations that were unavailable, and that future work can explicitly capture differences in pedagogical design or complexity between courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Annotations of the extent to which two courses overlap to support full-versus-partial overlap classification
#### Constraints
- Binary overlap/no-overlap formulation was driven by limited labeled data

### Target Learners
- university students in international mobility programs

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate cross-institution course equivalence assessment
