# Implement TBL for chest radiograph interpretation using pre-class recordings, readiness assurance tests, and the 4S framework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific implementable TBL session structure for radiograph interpretation. Students 'independently reviewed three short, recorded lectures (10 min each)' before class, then completed an iRAT followed by a tRAT in teams of 4–5, received immediate feedback, and worked through application exercises using the '4S' framework (significant problem, same problem, specific choice, simultaneous reporting) with whiteboards and simultaneous team reporting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-class recorded lectures and resource list for preparation
- Randomly assigned teams of 4–5 students
- Software platform supporting readiness assurance testing, immediate feedback, and simultaneous reporting
#### Constraints
- Total student time-on-task was not equivalent between groups and was not formally measured

### Target Learners
- physician associate students in didactic training

### Target Learning Goals
- chest radiograph interpretation
- recognizing normal and pathological findings
