# Adopt web-based training as continuing professional education to build nurses' EBP competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that healthcare organizations adopt web-based training as a method of continuing professional education to enhance the competencies required for implementing evidence-based practice among nurses. They further recommend establishing "the organizational infrastructure and mentoring necessary to support EBP and research utilization" and developing "creative strategies... to promote a culture of clinical inquiry". The recommendation rests on the study's observed knowledge, skills, and attitude gains.

## Design Implications
