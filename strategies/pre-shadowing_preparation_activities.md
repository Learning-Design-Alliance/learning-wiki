# Pre-Shadowing Preparation Activities

## Description
Pre-shadowing preparation involves several activities to set up a meaningful shadowing experience. These activities include: (1) creating learning goals for the Shadow Day to focus observations; (2) choosing a student who can help meet those learning goals; (3) coordinating with the student, parents, and staff; (4) questioning assumptions to approach the shadowing day with beginner's eyes. Examples of learning goals include understanding student engagement, college and career readiness, or experiences of English Learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time to dedicate to preparation activities (approximately 1 hour), templates or worksheets for goal setting and assumption questioning, and coordination with the student, parents, and staff.
#### Constraints
- May require additional time and effort, potential for logistical challenges in coordinating with students and staff, and dependence on the willingness of students and parents to participate.

### Personalization
