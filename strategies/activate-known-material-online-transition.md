## Description
The article identifies activation of known material as a major design consideration for online courses, drawing on Ke's (2016) systematic review of purposeful learning in game play. It states that "Activation of known material" is among "the major issues to be taken into consideration when designing online courses" and discusses how it can be brought to life in an online format. Instructors making a fast transition to online teaching are advised to take this theme into consideration.
