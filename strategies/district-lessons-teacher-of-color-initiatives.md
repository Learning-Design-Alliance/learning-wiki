# District lessons for launching teacher-of-color support initiatives: safe space, clear goals, buy-in, and relationship time

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In its Implications section, the report distills advice from initiative participants for other districts. Contributors emphasized "building and establishing a safe space where people can be their authentic selves" as integral; an Admiral Squad educator advised "Define your goals. What makes your district special?" and to "Try to get buy-in"; and a SPECTRA teacher recommended creating space and time to meet together and identifying stakeholders with connections who can bring in human capital.
