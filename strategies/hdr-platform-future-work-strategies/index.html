Planned extensions: expanded user studies, expert-in-the-loop RLHF, hybrid retrieval, and hand-tracking-based assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors lay out four forward-looking directions for the platform. They plan "controlled user studies to compare the outputs of the proposed solution with those of traditional HDR brachytherapy training methods," engagement of board-certified professionals to assess clinical realism, hybrid knowledge retrieval for rare edge cases plus Expert-in-the-Loop refinement via RLHF, and integration of hand-tracking sensors with AI-driven kinematic analysis for automated quantitative assessment of procedural accuracy.

Target Learners¶

Target Learning Goals¶

  • quantitative assessment of procedural accuracy and spatial precision
  • educational impact evaluation

Related Strategies¶

  • Future improvements: real-world user studies, advanced student modeling, and spaced repetition
