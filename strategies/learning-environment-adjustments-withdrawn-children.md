# Adjust learning environments to support withdrawn children's participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that withdrawal is entangled with achievement, the authors recommend that teachers create optimal learning conditions for withdrawn children. These "may include learning environments with fewer participants, more flexible dates and contexts for assignments and presentations, and more supportive and consistent student–teacher communication". The strategy targets the participation barriers that social anxiety creates in oral and group-based academic activities.
