# Use everyday objects as minimal setups for smartphone mechanics experiments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends very limited setups for dynamics experiments with iMecaProf: circular motion can be studied "using a swivel chair, pendulum using the smartphone charge cable and oscillation by putting the smartphone into a plastic bag attached to an elastic rubber". This makes practicals achievable without laboratory equipment since the smartphone stays in the user's hand or in cheap everyday items.
