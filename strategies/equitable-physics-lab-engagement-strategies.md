# Promote gender equity in physics labs through structured role rotation, intentional group composition, instructor facilitation, and bias awareness training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a set of instructional strategies to prevent informal gendered task division and support belonging in physics laboratories. It states "structured role rotation should be implemented so that all students gain experience and confidence in each aspect of lab work", alongside attention to gender balance in group composition, active monitoring and redistribution of participation by instructors, faculty/TA bias awareness training, and fostering a culture of respect that addresses physical and social barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must monitor group dynamics and intervene to redistribute participation when necessary
#### Constraints
- Recommended based on this single-institution study and the broader literature; the article calls for future research on intervention effectiveness

### Target Learners
- non-physics STEM majors in introductory physics laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable participation across laboratory roles
- comfort, confidence, and sense of belonging in STEM labs
