# Pair daily SMS quiz questions with linked readings and online courses to stimulate self-study in mobile continuing medical education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that mCME interventions use daily SMS multiple-choice quizzes as a stimulus for uptake of study behaviors when paired with access to relevant readings and online courses. The authors state: “In mCME interventions, daily SMS quizzes can effectively act as a stimulus for uptake of study behaviors when paired with access to relevant readings and online courses.” They found performance on daily quizzes most consistently predicted endline test performance, which “strongly supports the strategy of using SMS quiz questions to motivate study behaviors.”
