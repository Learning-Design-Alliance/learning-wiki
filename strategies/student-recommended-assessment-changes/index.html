Offer test corrections and retakes, diverse performance-based assessment options, and peer-teaching opportunities so students can learn from mistakes and demonstrate understanding¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Students in the focus groups recommended concrete assessment changes: test retakes or corrections with partial credit, more open-ended questions instead of multiple choice, and diverse options such as projects, presentations, and essays. They also suggested having responsibility for teaching their peers as a way to learn and demonstrate knowledge. One student praised a psychology teacher whose test corrections require identifying the topic, explaining why the answer was missed, correcting it, and writing how to remember it.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must offer structured test corrections or retakes with partial credit, which students report few teachers currently offer.

Constraints¶

  • Students recognize tests are an unavoidable aspect of their education; the strategy supplements rather than replaces testing.

Target Learners¶

  • high school students in mainstream public high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning from mistakes and improving understanding
  • demonstrating knowledge through multiple formats
  • developing planning, self-direction, and communication skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Offer candidates multiple ways of demonstrating competence, crediting prior teaching experience and using performance-based alternative assessments
