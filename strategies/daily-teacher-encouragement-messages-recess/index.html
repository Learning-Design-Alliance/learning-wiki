Daily brief teacher encouragement messages before recess to promote moderate-to-vigorous physical activity¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The encouragement treatment had classroom teachers deliver "a brief message, encouraging them to make an active choice to engage in MVPA during recess" daily over a 6-week period before morning and lunch recess, preceded by a teacher-facilitated discussion of MVPA benefits. The study's results suggest this low-cost strategy raised children's confidence to be active without adult prompting, outperforming adult modeling of games.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom teachers willing to deliver the message daily before each recess over six weeks
  • An advance discussion of the benefits of MVPA with students

Constraints¶

  • The encouragement school had also introduced a new recess practice (yard aides prompting students to play after 10 minutes), so encouragement alone may not have yielded the same significant results

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • increased moderate-to-vigorous physical activity during recess
  • reduced dependence on external encouragement to be active

Claims¶

  • Teacher's encouragement is more effective than modeling by a competent adult at raising children's adult encouragement barrier self-efficacy for recess physical activity [+W]
