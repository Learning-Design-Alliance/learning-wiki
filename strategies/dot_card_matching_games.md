# Dot Card Matching Games

## Description
Learners match dot cards with equal quantities but different arrangements, or match dot cards to corresponding numeral cards. Advanced learners play 'Make 10' match, where they find pairs that total 10. Matching equal quantities with different representations helps reinforce the concept that numbers can be represented in multiple ways. Matching dot cards with numerals solidifies the connection between visual representations and symbolic numbers. 'Make 10' match supports the learning and recognition of number pairs that add up to 10, an important skill for developing number sense and mental math strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dot cards, numeral cards (optional), counters (optional).
#### Constraints
- May require careful preparation of card sets to ensure clear matches. Some learners may need guidance to understand the matching criteria.
