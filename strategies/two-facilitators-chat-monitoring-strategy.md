# Run faculty development workshops with at least two facilitators so one can present while others manage chat and logistics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors ran each session with at least two facilitators and "strongly recommend this strategy during its implementation elsewhere." One primary facilitator presents content while others monitor the chat for questions and share documents, ensuring smooth transitions between activities. When Workshop 2 ran with only one facilitator, monitoring participant comments in the chat became more difficult, and the facilitator had to defer questions to the end of the session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least two facilitators per session, with the primary facilitator rehearsing and testing all software ahead of time
#### Constraints
- With a single facilitator, chat questions must be batched and addressed at the end of the session

### Target Learners
- Health professions educators attending faculty development workshops

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective facilitation of interactive virtual teaching sessions
