Provide detailed descriptions of applied technology and pedagogical considerations when reporting SBL research to enable comparison and synthesis¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 review), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review concludes that descriptions of the applied technology and pedagogical considerations are pivotal for comparing or synthesizing research results. It found that "the technology applied was diversely described in the studies" and that studies using digital technology in TESs generally provided richer descriptions than those using HPSs. Researchers should report technology features, functions, and integration into learning activities to support validity and transferability assessments.

Claims¶

  • A trend toward blended educational approaches combining synchronous and asynchronous learning activities emerged after 2019 in SBL for critical thinking [+W]
  • A shift across time occurred in SBL technology used to enhance critical thinking, from human-patient simulators before 2018 to computer-based simulations after 2018 [+W]
  • Realism of technology is a prominent enabler of critical thinking development in technologically supported SBL, mapped to the information quality dimension of the D&M IS Success Model [+W]
  • The most frequently reported challenge of virtual simulation teaching is its lack of realism, alongside operational complexity, English-language demands, cultural mismatch, and adverse physical reactions [+W]
