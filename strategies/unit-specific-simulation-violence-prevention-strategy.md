# Integrate unit-specific simulation training into regular workplace violence prevention programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend developing different simulations for specific medical units, integrating simulation training into the hospital's regular workplace violence prevention program with periodic updates, and building an online course combining text, real-case videos, and interactive processes for flexible learning. They state it is "recommended to develop different simulations for specific medical units in the future, such as emergency, psychiatric, maintenance institutions, home care, internal medicine wards, surgery wards and outpatient clinics". This extends the tested 3-h course toward a sustained, differentiated prevention program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A hospital workplace violence prevention program into which simulation can be integrated
- Periodic updating to meet the latest needs
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the discussion based on a single-center quasi-experiment; unit-specific variants were not themselves tested

### Target Learners
- Nursing staff across medical units including emergency, psychiatric, and surgical settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Workplace violence prevention strategies
- Coping skills for aggression events
