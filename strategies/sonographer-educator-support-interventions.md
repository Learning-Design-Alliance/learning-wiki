# Targeted interventions to support sonographer teachers: competency-framework review, feedback workshops, protected time, and sonographer educator roles

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the needs assessment, the authors propose targeted educational interventions matched to the six identified themes. These include reviewing ACGME milestones and EPAs with sonographers, updating them on individual fellows' progress, feedback workshops with role play to address hierarchy, dedicated protected time for sonographer teachers, and didactics on adult learning theory and just-in-time teaching. They also suggest creating sonographer educator leadership roles within echocardiography labs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires programs to allocate protected time for sonographer teachers and to share competency-based evaluation frameworks (ACGME milestones/EPAs) with sonographers.
#### Constraints
- The authors note the sample was a convenience sample of medium to large academic centers, so findings may not be generalizable to all centers.

### Target Learners
- Pediatric cardiac sonographers teaching pediatric cardiology fellows

### Target Learning Goals
- Echocardiography teaching skills, competency-based fellow evaluation, feedback and communication skills
