# Train pre-clinical students as chronic-disease health educators via structured curriculum, shadowing, and faculty oversight

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors offer their experience as "a blueprint and model for the development of comprehensive medical student health educator programs at other SRFCs." The recipe combines a structured training course using small-group interactive discussions and formal didactic sessions covering pathophysiology, medications, complications, and health coaching strategies; months of shadowing experienced students; gradual assumption of an own patient panel; and weekly faculty review of laboratory results and monthly quality improvement reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty facilitators and an endocrinologist for weekly review; selection of students based on commitment and rapport-building ability
#### Constraints
- The model was developed and evaluated at a single clinic and its findings may not generalize to other settings

### Target Learners
- first-year and second-year medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- chronic disease management
- patient counseling and adherence coaching
- care coordination for underserved patients
