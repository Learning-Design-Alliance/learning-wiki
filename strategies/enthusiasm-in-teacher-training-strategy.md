# Reflect affective components of teaching, especially teacher enthusiasm, in teacher training and professional development

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Because Grade 5 teacher-reported enthusiasm slowed the decline in class-level mathematics interest, the article argues that "the importance of affective components of teaching, such as the teacher's enthusiasm, need to be reflected in teacher training". It further recommends that didactic and pedagogical strategies for enhancing shared perceptions of teacher support be discussed and reflected throughout teachers' professional development and education programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher education programs must treat teacher motivation and enthusiasm as a component of professional competence
#### Constraints
- The enthusiasm effect was found shortly after the school transition and did not extend to changes in perceived teacher support

### Target Learners
- early adolescents in mathematics classrooms after transition to secondary school

### Target Learning Goals
- maintaining class-level mathematics interest
