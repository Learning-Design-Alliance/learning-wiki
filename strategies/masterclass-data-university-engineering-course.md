# Use masterclass measurements with LHC data as tutorials in a university introductory particle physics course for engineering students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that masterclass measurements can be repurposed as tutorial work in formal higher education. At the Faculty of Electrical Engineering at University of Žilina, an optional first-year Master-degree course pairs two hours per week of lectures on the Standard Model, accelerators and detectors with two hours per week of tutorials that "rely on CMS and ATLAS masterclass measurements with LHC data". The course was inspired by demand for skilled engineers at particle physics laboratories and engineering students' interest in modern physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lectures covering the Standard Model, accelerators and detectors
- Access to CMS and ATLAS masterclass measurements with LHC data
#### Constraints
- The course is optional in the curriculum and offered to 1st-year students of the Master degree programme

### Target Learners
- 1st-year Master degree engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- introductory particle physics
- data analysis with real LHC data
