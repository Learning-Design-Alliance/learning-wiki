# Sustain and resource statewide PBIS scale-up with emphasis on advanced-tier implementation support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on finding that only a minority of trained schools implemented all three tiers with fidelity, the authors recommend that "statewide scale-up efforts need to continue to find ways to support PBIS implementation." State technical assistance successfully helped most schools implement Tier 1 with fidelity, but more work is required for advanced tiers, and a key barrier is "the resources to provide ongoing intensive coaching and feedback" across a geographically large state whose regions contain schools often located several hours apart.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing intensive coaching and feedback resources; regional organization of technical assistance; adherence to the PBIS Implementation Blueprint
#### Constraints
- Implementing all three PBIS tiers with fidelity is difficult and requires widespread commitment throughout a school; geographic spread of schools complicates coaching delivery

### Target Learners
- Schools and districts statewide implementing or scaling PBIS, spanning elementary, middle, and high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Building school capacity to implement all three PBIS tiers with fidelity to reduce disciplinary exclusions
