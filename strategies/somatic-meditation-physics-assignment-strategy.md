# Integrate a somatic meditation and contemplation assignment into physics courses to connect theory with lived experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors encourage colleagues to "consider implemen ting contemplative practices into their curricula," noting students were overwhelmingly openminded and the practice "did not contribute signiﬁcantly to our grading burdens ince credit was awarded on completion." The strategy is a guided somatic meditation followed by contemplation of course-relevant phenomena, with a written reflection afterwards.

## Design Implications

### Context
