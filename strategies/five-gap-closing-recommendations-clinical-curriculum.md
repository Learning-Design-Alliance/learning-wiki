# Five improvement recommendations: refined modules, more CPX scenarios, evaluator training, video debriefing, and contingency planning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From the identified curriculum gaps the authors derive recommendations: develop refined modules for diverse traditional modalities, add CPX scenarios for diseases commonly encountered in clinics and emphasize patient-physician interaction, train evaluators "to increase inter-rater reliability", retrain procedures with direct feedback via performance-video debriefing, and prepare contingency plans and accommodations for disabled examinees. These respond to stated gaps including module scarcity, inconsistent evaluator scoring, and written feedback's limits.

## Design Implications
