# Extend clinical simulation laboratory operating hours, including after-hours and weekend opening, to improve student access for SSL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 5 studies (5 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy responds to the finding that restrictive laboratory hours block self-directed practice during students' only free time. The article recommends that "the operating hours of the clinical simulation laboratory at the host HEI be extended to 17:00 between Monday and Friday and consideration be made to open the facility over weekends". Participants suggested weekend operation and longer weekday hours would enable self-practice, and cited literature supports increased operating time and after-hours sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory staffing and management capacity to support extended and weekend opening
#### Constraints
- Current schedule operates Monday to Friday, 07:30–16:00, which participants said makes free-time access very difficult

### Target Learners
- undergraduate student nurses

### Target Learning Goals
- self-directed clinical skills practice
- repeated practice and refinement of clinical skills
