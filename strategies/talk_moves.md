# Talk Moves

## Description
Talk moves are sentence frames we supply to our learners that help them express ideas and interact with one another in respectful, academically appropriate ways. From kindergarten all the way through college, learners can benefit from explicit instruction in the skills of summarizing another person’s argument before presenting an alternate view, asking clarifying questions, and expressing agreement or partial agreement with the stance of another participant. Talk moves can be incorporated into any of the other discussion formats listed here.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a set of sentence frames or talk moves, a discussion topic, and clear instructions for learners.
#### Constraints
- May feel artificial or restrictive to some learners, requires careful modeling and practice to be effective.
