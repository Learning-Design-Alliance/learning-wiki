# Offer domestic study away alongside study abroad and community-engaged learning to broaden intercultural access

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that educators expand the portfolio of intercultural program options beyond international travel. Given the diversity of cultures within the United States, the authors "urge educators and students alike to consider new types of programs and models in addition to study abroad and community-engaged learning", positioning domestic study away as a viable opportunity that can reach students underrepresented in study abroad. The strategy rests on reviewed evidence that domestic programs attracted male and minoritized students who had declined study abroad.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must provide academic credit in return for participation, per the review's working definition of study away
#### Constraints
- The review found study away is often ill-defined and understudied, so programs should be clearly defined and their outcomes assessed

### Target Learners
- postsecondary students, including those underrepresented in study abroad

### Target Learning Goals
- intercultural development
- psychosocial growth
- attitudes and values
