# Step through three Kepler's-law problems live in KSP during a single lecture period

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lecture demonstration sequence in which the instructor launches a rocket into orbit on the classroom projector and steps through three questions: orbit shape, orbital speed, and orbital period. Students watch an orbit elongate as eccentricity increases, watch speed change with radius via the game's readout, and compare two vessels' periods. The author states he has performed these demonstrations during a single hour-long lecture period of college-level introductory courses for nonmajors.
