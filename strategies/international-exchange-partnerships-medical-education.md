# Strengthen and extend international university partnerships to ease students' access to clinical and scientific exchanges abroad

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that German and American medical schools maintain and grow transatlantic alliances, arguing that exchange programs "facilitate the often gruesome process of application and credentialing that students undergo when trying to expand their exposure to other institutions". It documents existing partnerships such as LMU Munich with Harvard University, Heidelberg's 4th-year US rotations, Rostock with East Tennessee State University, and Technical University of Munich's course with Weill Cornell Medicine faculty. The authors are hopeful such prospects will grow, fostering intercultural collaboration and advancement of healthcare.
