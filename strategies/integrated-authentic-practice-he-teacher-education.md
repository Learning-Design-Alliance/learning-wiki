# Integrate pedagogic-didactic competence, content knowledge and socio-emotional skills in authentic teaching practice within health education teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that health education teacher education avoid developing competence domains in isolation and instead create opportunities for student teachers to apply multiple domains together with real learners. As the authors write, "the aim should be to create situations where student teachers can apply pedagogical and didactic competence, content knowledge and socio-emotional skills with real students in authentic situations". This rests on their argument that the fundamental domains are interconnected and develop reciprocally, so guided and reflective teaching practice is crucial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Opportunities for guided and reflective health education teaching practice
- Attention to the surface, deep and implicit structures of teaching practices in teacher education
#### Constraints
- The article notes perceptions that knowledge-based processing alone may not enable trainees to deal successfully with practical situations

### Target Learners
- student teachers of health education
- teacher education programme designers

### Target Learning Goals
- integrated professional competence across fundamental domains
- practical teaching performance in authentic situations
