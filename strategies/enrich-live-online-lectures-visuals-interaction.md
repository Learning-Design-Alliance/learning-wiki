# Enrich live online lectures with visuals, varied instructor communication, and interactive structures to sustain attention and positive emotion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's recommendations section advises that live online instruction avoid monotony and plain lecturing. It states that "lessons enriched with visuals, graphics, and infographics increase students’ interest and motiva- tion" and that instructors should "provide clear examples, use tone of voice and body language effectively, and foster student interaction." Students in the interviews reported that text-heavy slides made concentration hard, while visual explanation and instructor questions directed attention to the class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with pedagogical, technical, and communicative competencies for the online environment
#### Constraints
- Plain lecturing often leads to attention loss, per the article's own recommendation section

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in synchronous online higher education courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained attention, positive emotions, and engagement during live online lectures
