# Carefully vet freely available video resources for clinical correlation and procedural detail rather than assuming all supplemental video content is comparably rigorous

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article underscores "the importance of careful vetting of video resources for teaching the knee examination, rather than assuming all supplemental video content is comparably rigorous." Citing Yoo et al., the authors note that only 58% of YouTube videos on knee stability tests met criteria for educational suitability and fewer than a quarter addressed limitations or precautions. Variability in freely available control video quality may have narrowed the observed difference between groups.
