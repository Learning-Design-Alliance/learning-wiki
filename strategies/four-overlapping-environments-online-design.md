# Design online courses around four overlapping constructivist environments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that online instructors design learning environments that advance and support active learning based on four overlapping environments: learner-centred, knowledge-centred, assessment-centred, and community-centred. This strategy directs instructors to focus on the goals, objectives, needs, and interests of the learner; use assessments to provide consistent and meaningful feedback; and promote connectivity and collaboration through integrated online activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must understand learning theory and adapt from a teaching role to that of coach and facilitator
#### Constraints
- The strategy was derived from experience in an introductory managerial accounting course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate business students in online or hybrid courses

### Target Learning Goals
- active learning
- meaningful engagement with course content
- collaboration and community building
