# Extend empathy mapping with case discussion into clinical teaching for senior students and newly qualified nurses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In their recommendations the authors propose that "the intervention could be applied in clinical teaching by integrating empathy maps with case discussion to guide senior nursing students or newly-qualified nurses to engage in perspective-taking and patient-centered assessments of patient demands". The goal is to enhance empathy, communication self-confidence, and nursing care quality beyond the classroom. They also recommend extending follow-up to clinical placements to examine medium- to long-term effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of empathy maps with case discussion in clinical teaching contexts
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness in clinical settings and other courses is unverified; the article states more research is needed on maintenance and generalization of outcomes

### Target Learning Goals
