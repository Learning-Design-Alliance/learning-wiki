# Place resources such as FAQs, introductory videos, and assignment rubrics in an easily accessible course location

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends instructors pre-consider students' needs in the design process and place resources such as "frequently asked questions, introductory videos, and assignment rubrics in an easily accessible location on the course delivery platform." This avoids unnecessary student questions and lets students find critical course information at ease, implementing pedagogy of care in ERT design.
