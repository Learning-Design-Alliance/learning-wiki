# Use interactive learning activities (polls, questions) to stimulate higher engagement in social media learning communities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study recommends that educators include creative and interactive posts that foster discussions, collaborative and active learning in their LinkedIn group strategies. Interactive learning activities such as polls and questions asking students to answer and provide opinions generated higher engagement rates than informative content. This strategy supports instructional design that stimulates engagement in online group communities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must include interactive learning activities that foster discussions and request students' opinions
#### Constraints
- Only two types of learning activities (informative and interactive) were tested, limiting understanding of other activity types such as gamification

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate students

### Target Learning Goals
- Student engagement
- Student satisfaction
