# Calming Movement

## Description
Using calming movement involves engaging in repetitive and rhythmic vestibular input to promote relaxation and self-regulation. Activities include rocking, swaying, gentle swinging, Row, Row, Row Your Boat activities, and gentle back and forth movement on a scooter or therapy ball. These movements can help a child reset when they are overstimulated, overwhelmed, or dealing with tantrums.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rocking chair, exercise ball, scooter board, therapy ball.
#### Constraints
- Activities should be safe and appropriate for the child's age and abilities. Supervision may be necessary.

- Provides a way to engage the vestibular system, helping to reduce anxiety and promote emotional regulation. Offers a movement-based way to manage sensory experiences and emotions.

### Personalization
- The type of movement can be tailored to individual preferences and needs. Some children may prefer rocking, while others may prefer swinging.
