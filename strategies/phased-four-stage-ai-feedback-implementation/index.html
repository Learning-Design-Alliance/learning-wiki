Implement AI-assisted report feedback through a four-stage phased pathway beginning with mature low-level tools¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes a staged progression: Stage 1 deploys commercially available tools for structural and presentational feedback; Stage 2 introduces AI-supported intermediate-level checking under human oversight, beginning "with high-frequency human oversight of all AI outputs, scaling back only as evidence demonstrates acceptable reliability and security"; Stage 3 has staff provide high-level feedback with enhanced tools; Stage 4 integrates all feedback with clear attribution to support student feedback literacy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional procurement, VLE/LMS integration, staff and student training, assessor-curated knowledge bases, security measures, and clear human oversight protocols

Constraints¶

  • Scaling back oversight is permitted only as evidence demonstrates acceptable reliability and security

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students and academic staff assessing complex reports

Target Learning Goals¶

  • timely multi-level feedback on complex written reports
  • student feedback literacy development

  • Implement an AI-Peer-Teacher hybrid integrated feedback model staged across drafting, revision, and polishing phases
  • Three-stage hybrid workflow: API batch review, identification of doubtful cases, focused conversational teacher review
  • Implement AI in assessment as a hybrid support tool for low-stakes, formative stages while humans retain final grading authority
