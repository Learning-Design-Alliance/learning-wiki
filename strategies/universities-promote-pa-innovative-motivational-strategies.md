# Universities should promote physical activity through innovative motivational teaching strategies such as gamification

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend that educational institutions in the university context promote physical activity through innovative strategies: "Educational institutions in the university context should target and promote PA throughout innovative strategies in order to lead young adults to improve their immediate and longer-term physical health." They also suggest future studies use two-factor analysis comparing a gamification group with a traditional-teaching group that also has a motivation strategy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional support for innovative teaching methodologies in university physical education contexts
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single quasi-experimental study with students from sport and health sciences, which the authors state makes generalization to the whole young adult population difficult

### Target Learners
- college students (university young adults)

### Target Learning Goals
- increasing physical activity levels
- improving cardiorespiratory fitness and physical health
