Description¶

Nagurney's response offers a practical perspective on using the themes from Ke's (2016) games research to support rapid transition to online formats. The editorial notes Nagurney "elaborate[s] on how the lessons learned from the gaming perspective can be readily applied through a gamification lens, when a quick shift to an online modality becomes necessary."
