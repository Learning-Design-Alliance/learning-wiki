Strategies

Introduce constant acceleration by saying velocity increases by a fixed amount each second (e.g. 10 m/s per second in free fall) instead of stating a constant acceleration of 10 m/s²

The article proposes starting the academic discussion of free fall by telling students that "la velocidad aumenta cada segundo en 10 m/s", rather than that the acceleration is constant at 10 m/s².
