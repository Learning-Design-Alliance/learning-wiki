# Leverage students' lived experiences and support systems in course reform, beyond framing activities in interesting contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on Sam's case, the authors recommend that future reforms "should leverage the opportunity to allow for students to bring their rich lived experiences into the classroom." They argue that framing activities in contexts students find interesting or career-related "is only part of the solution": Sam's engagement also depended on her own lived experience of wound healing and on her peer network. They add that students are "capable of making connections to physics if there is sufficient support and intersection between their interests, coursework, and support systems."
