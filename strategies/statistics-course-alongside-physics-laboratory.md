# Pair the physics laboratory course with an introductory statistics course taken before or simultaneously

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because students entering the laboratory course usually have no previous experience with R and cannot be expected to self-learn it, the author recommends scheduling support: "The best synergy might be achieved when s tudents take an introductory course in statistics before the physics laboratory c ourse or simultaneously with it." This positions statistical background as the prerequisite that makes adopting a command-line analysis tool feasible in the laboratory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quick introduction course in R, since students cannot be expected to self-learn it
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is stated as a possibility ('might be achieved'), not a tested arrangement

### Target Learners
- first-year university students in an introductory physics laboratory

### Target Learning Goals
- statistical data analysis and graphing of experimental data
