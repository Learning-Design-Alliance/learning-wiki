# Employ combined Pre-question and genre-based instruction in EAP reading for science students

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that teachers of EAP reading activate background knowledge through discussion, diagrams and questions while also teaching the generic structure of texts, the register of Biology, cohesive devices and rhetorical functions. It states that activating background knowledge and raising awareness of generic features can lead to positive attitude to EAP reading. The combined strategy was the most effective for attitude in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Four weeks of instruction using subject-relevant reading passages and a structured weekly teaching plan
#### Constraints
- The strategy did not significantly improve reading comprehension achievement in this study

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduates in universities of science, technology and agriculture

### Target Learning Goals
- positive attitude to EAP reading
- identifying text structure and main ideas
