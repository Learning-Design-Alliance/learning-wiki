# Use multimedia modules with audio-video clips to develop speaking skill in teacher education

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends introducing multimedia modular approaches in colleges of education for developing student-teachers' communication skill. The strategy involves preparing structured multimedia modules with audio and video clips, graphics, animation and sound, organized into units of one hour each. The article recommends that colleges provide spacious multimedia language laboratories, train teacher educators in multimedia use, and organize workshops on preparing multimedia presentations and editing audio-video clips.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Well-equipped multimedia language laboratory with multimedia materials, language learning software and internet facilities.
- Teacher educators trained in utilizing multimedia equipment.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that many colleges lack adequate infrastructure, with devices that are 'dusted and outdated'.

### Target Learners
- B.Ed. student-teachers
- Teacher educators

### Target Learning Goals
- Speaking skill in English
- Communication skill
- Techno-pedagogic competence
