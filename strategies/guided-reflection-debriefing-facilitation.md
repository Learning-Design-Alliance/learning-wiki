# Facilitate debriefing as guided reflection linking theory to practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the debriefing stage, the facilitator reiterates the simulation objective, invites students to reflect on what they experienced, encourages highlighting positive aspects and alternative behaviors, discourages destructive criticism, and prompts discussion of communication and teamwork competencies. The article states that "professors/facilitators must encourage students to share experiences and build new knowledge", identifying emotions, removing doubts, and encouraging "integration between theory and practice". The scenario's debriefing script operationalizes this with main questions and a synthesis covering communication, collaboration, personal presentation, comprehensive care, medication safety, allergy recognition and decision-making.
