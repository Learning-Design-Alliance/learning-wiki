# Overnight open-collaboration derivation homework (Sommerfeld price): consult anyone, cite sources, write up like a scientific article

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a homework problem for the XL category, the article assigns the derivation of the general impedance of the GIC including the general operational-amplifier equation, submitted overnight after the Olympiad. Students are explicitly allowed outside help — "You can use Internet, you can consult professors in physics and electronics anywhere in the world, you can work together" — and must write the result up like a scientific article with acknowledgements and citations, checking whether the problem has been solved before. The best solution wins the Sommerfeld price; otherwise the solver receives co-authorship on the published solution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Algebra practice sufficient to derive the general formula; overnight submission to the olympiad email; a written report crediting consultations, books, articles and websites.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the difficulty is that students are tired after the Olympiad, and that the prize sum is given only personally the next morning.

### Target Learners
- university students
- high-school students with practice in algebra

### Target Learning Goals
- analytical derivation with non-ideal component models
- scientific writing and citation practice
