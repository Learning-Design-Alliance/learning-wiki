# Tiered Vocabulary Instruction

## Description
Tiered vocabulary instruction categorizes words into three tiers based on their frequency and utility. Tier One words are basic and commonly known, rarely requiring instruction. Tier Two words are high-frequency words for mature language users and are ideal for direct instruction. Tier Three words are low-frequency and domain-specific, best learned within specific content areas. Selecting Tier Two words for explicit teaching enhances learners' overall language ability by providing more precise and mature ways of expressing ideas they already understand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful selection of Tier Two words from texts and content areas. Direct instruction and activities that promote active use and understanding of these words.
#### Constraints
- Requires instructors to be knowledgeable about vocabulary tiers and able to identify appropriate words for instruction. May need to adapt instruction to account for learners with varying levels of prior knowledge.

### Target Learners

- All learners, particularly those in upper elementary through high school, who need to develop a more sophisticated vocabulary.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Expand vocabulary, improve language proficiency, and enhance comprehension by focusing on high-utility words.

### Affordances
- Focuses instruction on the most useful words, allowing learners to express ideas with greater precision and maturity. Helps build a robust vocabulary that supports reading comprehension across different subjects.

### Personalization
- Instructors can select words based on the specific needs and interests of their learners, as well as the content they are studying. The depth of instruction can be tailored based on learner proficiency.

## Assessment Evidence
- Track learner use of Tier Two words in writing and speaking. Assess comprehension of texts containing Tier Two words. Monitor engagement and participation in activities focused on vocabulary development.

## Impact
- Learners show improved vocabulary, enhanced reading comprehension, and greater confidence in expressing themselves using precise language. Direct teaching of Tier Two words provides learners with a strong foundation for academic success.
