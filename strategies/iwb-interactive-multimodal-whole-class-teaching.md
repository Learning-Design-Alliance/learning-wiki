# Use interactive whiteboard technology to support interactive, multimodal, student-centered whole-class teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend using IWB-integrated multimodal presentation to promote a learner-centered pedagogy in which both teacher and students are learners, for example generating common topics and finding answers jointly from the internet. They report that "the more the students interacted with the MPS, the more they became adept in using its functions and features", and that teacher-student interactivity promotes attention to instruction and participation in classroom discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with adequate subject matter content knowledge, instructional material production capability, instructional competency, and classroom management skills, since the tool cannot compensate for deficits in these
#### Constraints
- The MPS "is not a magic tool for improving ESL student academic success"; it was "simply a tool that improved the teacher's innate and teaching capability in the ESL classroom"

### Target Learners
- elementary school students learning English as a second language

### Target Learning Goals
- English vocabulary learning
- student engagement and participation in whole-class lessons
