# Develop virtual clinical simulations through the five Contextualized Instructional Design stages with expert nurse participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes developing the simulation through the Contextualized Instructional Design stages of "análise, design, desenvolvimento, implementação, avaliação" (analysis, design, development, implementation, evaluation). In the Analysis stage, ICU nurses met online to prioritize the theme from actual adverse events; a narrative literature review then grounded the content; design and development produced the clinical guide, storyboard, and audiovisual script; implementation used free tools (H5P, Youtube, Canva, Shutterstock); evaluation was the judges' content validation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participation of practising ICU nurses to select the priority theme
- A narrative literature review to ground risk-factor and prevention content
- INACSL guidelines for the clinical guide
#### Constraints
- The authors note the time factor for completing all planned stages and the small sample size as limitations

### Target Learners
- Nurses
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Prevention of adverse events in intensive care
- Patient safety practices
