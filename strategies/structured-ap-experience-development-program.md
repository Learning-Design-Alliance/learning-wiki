# Institute strategies that turn the assistant principal experience into a structured program for developing school-leadership skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This strategy converts ordinary on-the-job assistant principal work into deliberate leadership development. Rather than treating the AP role as informal apprenticeship, the organization "instituted a number of strategies that effectively turn the assistant principal experience into a structured program for developing the skills required to serve as a school’s top administrator." The publication presents this as KIPP's distinctive addition to district-style talent pipelines.
