# Use indoor simulation for first-time HEC education and annual refreshers while retaining outdoor training for aspects simulation cannot reproduce

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend finding "the ideal balance between indoor simulation and real outdoor training", based on the provider's needs and staff experience. For a beginner attending HEC education for the first time, and for the annual required refresher course, indoor simulation is described as an ideal choice, while dynamic winching and the effects of noise, altitude and adverse environmental conditions remain difficult to train indoors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An indoor simulation facility able to reproduce the relevant HEC manoeuvres
#### Constraints
- Indoor simulation cannot completely replace outdoor training; dynamic winching, noise, altitude and adverse environmental effects are difficult to simulate

### Target Learners
- HEMS providers and crew members
- Beginners attending HEC education for the first time

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining currency of HEC skills
- Fulfilling EASA recurrent training requirements
