# Brief lectures on intellectual history illustrated with films, records, and print excerpts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The teaching strategies section prescribes a sequence of brief lectures, each on one intellectual or artistic development and each illustrated with named films, records, slides, or print excerpts. Lecture 7, for instance, covers the origin and development of Expressionism as a reaction against Realism's neglect of inner reality and as an incorporation of influences from psychology, sociology, and science, illustrated with films and slides such as Metropolitan Seminars in Art: Expressionism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- The guide advises teachers not to attempt all suggested material but to make a judicious choice of activities for each objective

### Target Learning Goals
