# Use a comprehensive exhibition of original instruments and documents as an education, teaching, and research event

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the Einstein centenary 1979 in Berlin and the Michelson Colloquium 1981 in Potsdam, the authors prepared a comprehensive exhibition at the Astrophysical Observatory Potsdam. The article states "This was designed as an education, teaching, and research event to make visible all still existing instruments and publications for national and international visitors". The exhibition included the cellar where Michelson performed his 1881 experiment and original documents showing the support of the AOP director and Hermann von Helmholtz.
