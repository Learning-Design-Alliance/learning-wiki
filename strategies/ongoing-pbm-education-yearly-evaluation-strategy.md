# Recommend ongoing delivery of the PBM education programme to interns with yearly evaluation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the observed knowledge gains, the authors state: "We will recommend to hospital administration the ongoing delivery of our education programme to interns and yearly evaluation thereof." They position the dedicated intervention as "an important strategic instrument to enhance medical competence among interns" and a means to enact PBM governance, and state future direction will focus on ensuring the attained knowledge is applied to practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Healthcare providers should support and strengthen further activities in PBM
#### Constraints
- Future studies should focus on validation of one unique multiple-choice questionnaire based on expert opinion applying questionnaire design methodology

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
