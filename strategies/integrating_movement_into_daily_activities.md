# Integrating Movement into Daily Activities

## Description
Integrating movement into daily activities involves incorporating physical activity into routines and tasks that are typically sedentary. This approach aims to increase overall physical activity levels without requiring dedicated workout sessions. It includes simple changes such as taking the stairs, walking during breaks, and engaging in active household chores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Minimal to no equipment needed. Requires awareness and intention to incorporate movement into daily routines.
#### Constraints
- May require initial effort to identify opportunities for integrating movement. Some individuals may need to adapt activities to accommodate physical limitations.
