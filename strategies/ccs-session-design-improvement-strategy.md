# Spread simulation scenarios across multiple sessions, provide preparatory materials in advance, assign structured observer roles, and increase direct participation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking design recommendations for community care simulation activities. The authors state that "Future activities should therefore consider spreading scenarios across multiple sessions, providing preparatory materials in advance, assigning structured observer roles, and increasing opportunities for direct participation." These changes are proposed to reduce fatigue and anxiety while strengthening engagement and perceived support, addressing the organizational and pedagogical limitations students reported.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-designed scenarios with established learning objectives and a high-fidelity simulation laboratory and debriefing room
#### Constraints
- Session duration varied slightly among groups according to scenario progression

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students

### Target Learning Goals
- Community care competencies including communication, health education and health promotion
