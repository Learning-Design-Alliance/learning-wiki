# Pace software usage in short sessions with sufficient time between sessions, and follow each session with discussion of what students learned

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Practitioner-recommended practices for using the reading software, drawn from informal user evaluations reported in Appendix B. The article recommends that "students can use these programs 20 to 30 minutes a session - two to three times a week - for six to eight weeks in conjunction with other methods of instruction" and that "program usage should be paced to allow students sufficient time between sessions to absorb the material." Teachers follow up each Workout session by asking students to name familiar and new content, discuss scores, and identify problem areas.
