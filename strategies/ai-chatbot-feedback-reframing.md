# AI chatbots to moderate feedback loops and reframe self-reflection away from success versus failure

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes using LLM-based chatbots, sideloaded with an appropriate set of background data files in a safe and closed environment, to help students moderate the reinforcing loop between perceptions of control and maths anxiety at point ④. The chatbot "can help to reframe these reflections to consider how an individual has developed in multiple ways" and reduce the dichotomous focus on success versus failure; feedback must be carefully supervised so unhelpful self-talk does not dominate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful supervision of this type of feedback so that unhelpful self-talk does not dominate the process
- Sideloaded background data files restricting the chatbot to appropriate information
#### Constraints
- The article notes chatbots are still far from perfect, and that smaller sideloaded models run on prosumer-level local hardware rather than massive cloud infrastructure

### Target Learners
- Students experiencing maths anxiety in classroom mathematics activities

### Target Learning Goals
- Emotional self-regulation
- Retrained notions of failure and success
