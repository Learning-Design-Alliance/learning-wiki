# Build a voluntary mentor community in which participants become mentors and feedback into the training system

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The HSF/IRIS-HEP training community of mentors is voluntary-based and reflects the vision that "participants become mentors at diﬀerent levels in the pyramid and feedback into the system". Community building is central: it builds and improves training material, incorporates new ideas, makes connections, and acquires new mentors for sustainability and scalability. Mentor profiles are advertised on the program web page with links to professional details, and the community has expanded to related fields like Nuclear Physics and Computing.
