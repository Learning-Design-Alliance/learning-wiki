# PLaNS (Play Learning and Narrative Skills)

## Description
The PLaNS approach supports writing skills through guided play and includes practical resources to help with lesson planning. It focuses on building stories together to inspire young writers. PLaNS involves building stories together to inspire young writers through structured play and guided narrative activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured play activities and narrative prompts. Instructor guidance and support. Collaborative learning environment.
#### Constraints
- May require significant planning and preparation. Some learners may need more scaffolding and support.
