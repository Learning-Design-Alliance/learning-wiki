# Combine face-to-face teaching with the VI, using pharmacists as educators, to deliver the best learning outcomes on clinical vancomycin use

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the finding that "pharmacists have the greatest level of knowledge on clinical vancomycin use," the authors recommend pairing the VI with in-person teaching delivered by pharmacists. This strategy targets the identified profession-specific knowledge gaps and was explicitly proposed in the discussion as the way "to deliver the best learning outcomes for health professionals in this area."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to pharmacists able to act as educators on clinical vancomycin use
#### Constraints
- The authors note VI uptake was relatively low despite broad advertisement, highlighting the challenge of noncompulsory e-learning delivery

### Target Learners
- nurses
- doctors
- pharmacists in hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- safe and effective clinical use of vancomycin including dosing, administration, and monitoring
