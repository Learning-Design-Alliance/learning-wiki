# Use simulated patients actively in debriefing and assessment, and role play for empathetic perspective-taking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends specific implementable uses of dramatization techniques. The simulated patient "participates actively in the activity and, in the debriefing process, permits interactivity in the learner's reflection", and "needs to be engaged in the assessment of the activity". Role play "allows the learner to empathetically experience the role of the patient, relative and/or of another professional, in an active, involving and dynamic manner", supporting clinical competence and effective communication. Feedback from the simulated patient "contributes and enriches the teaching-learning process".
