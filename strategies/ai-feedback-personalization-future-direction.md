## Description
As a forward-looking design recommendation, the authors propose augmenting Jigsaw-based cooperative learning with artificial intelligence feedback. The article states: "Future work will explore the integration of AI-based feedback systems to personalize instruction and further improve learning outcomes." The intent is to tailor support to individual students within the collaborative structure, addressing the varied outcomes observed in the networks course and building on the formative assessment data already collected through quizzes and evaluations.
