# Staged institutional implementation: audit interactions, map to teaming levels, build AI literacy, co-design, and evaluate iteratively

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter recommends institutions translate the teaming framework into practice as a staged, capacity-building process rather than a technical deployment challenge. "A practical starting point is for institutions to audit existing teacher-AI interactions within their platforms and workflows and map them onto the five levels of teaming to identify their current baseline." This mapping then guides design decisions such as upgrading transactional tools towards situational or operational teaming, structured professional development aligned with AI literacy frameworks, human-centred co-design with teachers, and robust evaluation cycles using process analytics and teacher reflections.

#### Requirements
- Institutional capacity for professional development aligned with AI literacy frameworks such as the UNESCO AI CFT, and human-centred design processes involving teachers
