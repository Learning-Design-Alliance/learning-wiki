# Combine accurate AI labor-market information, employability-building activities, and growth-oriented career support for students facing AI employment threat

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that universities help students develop "a more accurateappraisal of AI-related career change" by providing occupation- and major-specific information about which tasks are exposed to automation and which human capabilities remain important. It further recommends combining career counseling with "AI literacy training, digital skill development, interdisciplinary projects, internships, and opportunities to apply AI tools in profession-speciﬁc tasks," and using growth-oriented feedback, examples of successful skill transitions, structured reflection on learning progress, and individualized development plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Career education and counseling infrastructure in higher education able to deliver occupation-specific AI information and employability-building activities
#### Constraints
- The moderation results suggest growth-oriented support is particularly useful for students experiencing stronger AI employment threat; the article notes these interventions may strengthen resources but reports no intervention test

### Target Learners
- College students, especially those reporting high AI employment threat, low perceived employability, and weaker growth mindset

### Target Learning Goals
- Realistic appraisal of AI-related employment change
- Perceived employability
- Growth mindset reinforcement
- AI literacy and transferable skill identification

### Affordances
- Ai Threat Employability Mindset Moderated Mediation Framework
