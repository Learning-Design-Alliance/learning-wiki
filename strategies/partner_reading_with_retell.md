# Partner Reading with Retell

## Description
A PALS activity aimed at improving reading fluency and summarization skills. Students take turns reading aloud and then summarizing what they have read. The higher-performing reader serves as a model, while the lower-performing reader practices fluency and comprehension. Partners alternate roles and provide feedback to each other.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate reading material, trained students, timer, quiet space
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on proper pairing and training. Students need to provide constructive feedback.
