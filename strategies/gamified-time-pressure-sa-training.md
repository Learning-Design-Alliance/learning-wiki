# Use gamified time pressure and team-based competition to simulate clinical urgency during situational awareness training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A concrete activity recipe: place interprofessional teams in a realistic virtual scene, require accurate identification and documentation of patient safety hazards under strict time constraints, and use team-based competition and a red-flashing final-minute countdown to heighten stress. The article states that "gamification, including time constraints and team-based competition, introduces pressure and engagement mechanisms intended to simulate aspects of clinical urgency and support the training of SA under realistic conditions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A hazard-embedded realistic scene, a central timer, and team-level documentation tools (shared digital notebook) so performance can be compared and debriefed.
#### Constraints
- The article frames these as engagement and pressure mechanisms intended to simulate clinical urgency; their effect on learning outcomes has not been empirically tested.

### Target Learners
- interprofessional teams of healthcare students and professionals

### Target Learning Goals
- situational awareness under time pressure
- error detection
- team engagement
