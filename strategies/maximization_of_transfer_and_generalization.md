# Maximization of Transfer and Generalization

## Description
Supporting the application of learning to new contexts. This involves providing opportunities for learners to practice and apply their knowledge in different situations. Maximizing transfer and generalization enhances the relevance and durability of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case studies, real-world examples, and opportunities for practice and application.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful selection of examples and activities to ensure relevance and appropriateness. Some learners may struggle to transfer their learning to new contexts.

### Affordances
- Enhances application of knowledge to new contexts. Improves transfer of learning to different situations. Increases the relevance of learning. Promotes durability of learning.

### Personalization
- Provide opportunities for learners to practice and apply their knowledge in different situations. Offer case studies and real-world examples. Encourage learners to reflect on how their learning can be applied in their own lives.
