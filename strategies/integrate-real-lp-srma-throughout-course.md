# Integrate real learner-performed SR/MA projects throughout online research methods courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that online SR/MA courses integrate real learner-performed SR/MA projects as a long-term assignment running across all modules. Trainees actively conduct their own real SR/MA as part of the course while receiving expert feedback, which the article states enhances learning outcomes and provides hands-on experiential learning grounded in the learners' subject area.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert trainers with discipline-specific knowledge to provide individualized feedback
- Virtual research teams for collaborative work
#### Constraints
- Requires longer-term commitment as the project spans the entire course duration

### Target Learners
- Medical and health students
- Clinicians

### Target Learning Goals
- Conducting real systematic reviews and meta-analyses
- Research synthesis skills
