# Use randomized physics exercises to introduce probability concepts in a physical context

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends using stochastic physics exercises to introduce basic probability theory concepts such as expected value, variance and density function of a random variable in a physical context. These exercises "enable comparison between stochastic and deterministic solutions" and encourage exploration of different probability distributions. Students can extend the method to similar systems to develop problem-solving skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students and teachers familiar with elementary probability theory and mechanics
#### Constraints
- The stochastic viewpoint is not widespread for projectile motion problems and is basically not present at high schools and physics competitions

### Target Learners
- high school students in specialized mathematics classes
- university students who took basic probability theory

### Target Learning Goals
- introducing expected value, variance and density function
- comparing stochastic and deterministic solutions
- developing problem-solving skills
