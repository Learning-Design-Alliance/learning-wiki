# Pre-planned scripts and teleprompter use to achieve conversational, personalized narration

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A production strategy for implementing the personalization, signaling, and embodiment principles: all spoken text is written and revised in advance to simplify phrasing, then delivered via teleprompter so instructors can speak conversationally while gesturing toward visuals added in post-production. The authors found speaking naturally while interacting with imaginary visuals is difficult without such preparation, and that a teleprompter was invaluable, though shifting gaze between teleprompter and on-screen points can be difficult to manage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully written and simplified scripts
- A teleprompter during filming
#### Constraints
- Turning away from the teleprompter to point something out and returning gaze to the proper point in the text can sometimes be difficult to manage

### Target Learners
- MOOC video audiences

### Target Learning Goals
- conversational, personalized narration with humanlike gestures supporting learning
