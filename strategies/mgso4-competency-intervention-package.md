# Implement simulation-based refresher drills, visual job aids, mentorship champions, and stocked emergency kits for MgSO4 readiness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 5 studies (2 review, 1 causal, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's conclusion recommends four interventions: "1. Structured refresher training: Implement simulation-based drills to reinforce skills and improve emergency readiness. 2. Visual job aids: Provide laminated dosage charts and wall-mounted SOPs… 3. Mentorship systems: Establish 'MgSO4 champions' and conduct regular peer-led debriefings… 4. Facility preparedness: Ensure emergency kits are available, stocked with antidotes like calcium gluconate." Together these aim to reinforce provider confidence, enhance protocol adherence, and improve maternal and neonatal outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facility commitment to stocking antidotes and monitoring tools
- designation of clinical champions and integration of drills into routine workflows
#### Constraints
- Recommended for resource-limited EmONC settings; impact on clinical outcomes not yet quantitatively evaluated

### Target Learners
- EmONC providers in low-resource maternity facilities

### Target Learning Goals
- emergency readiness
- protocol adherence
- confidence in administering magnesium sulfate
