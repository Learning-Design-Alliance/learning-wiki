# Expand school-climate improvement programs as a lever for raising school performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because climate is a factor schools can act on directly, the report recommends expanding programs designed to improve school climate as a route to broader school success. It frames climate as "a malleable factor that schools or districts are able to manipulate," in contrast to fixed student demographics, and points to federal initiatives such as the Safe and Supportive Schools program as existing vehicles.

## Design Implications

### Context
