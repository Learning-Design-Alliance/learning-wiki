# Research agenda: measure intent versus actual design practices and study design support for under-resourced contexts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The editorial identifies gaps in the literature where more support can guide transitions to digital, beginning with "Measuring intent versus actual design practices" (Whitford 2020). It also calls for contributions from parts of the world whose research is rarely found in English publications, learning designs that turn contextual weaknesses such as limited ICT infrastructure or hurricanes into achievable outcomes, designs that change cognitive constructs, and policies promoting professional development for the teaching profession.
