# Add a professor team to a course review game to heighten healthy competition

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The activity adds an extra team of three professors closely related to the course topics, competing against the student teams. The article states "This format significantly increases healthy competitiveness, as students are highly motivated to outperform their instructors." The professor roster changed across editions, drawing instructors from related courses such as Field and Waves and Electromagnetism and Optics. The main course professor always acted as moderator/presenter rather than playing.
