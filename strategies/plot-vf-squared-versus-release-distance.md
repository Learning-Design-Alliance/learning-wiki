# Plot vf squared versus release distance d to verify the prediction equation over a range of inputs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because the final cart speed is proportional to the square root of the release position d, the authors recommend transforming the data for linear-style fitting. They write: "a plot of (vf)2 versus d might be the best way to see that the prediction equation is valid over a range of inputs." The release position on the ramp is the quantity that can easily be varied in this lab.
