# Combine course-specific group walk-in sessions with individually scheduled tutoring hours

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The center allocates its evening hours between group and individual formats: ten hours of course-specific walk-in sessions are scheduled weekly "according to demand projected from consideration of assignments, graded reviews, and historical demand," and ten hours are individualized sessions booked on an online calendar. The article reports this mix became necessary in the first week, when demand exceeded what scheduled one-on-one sessions alone could serve. Individual sessions let tutors review cadet work, identify weak areas, and build rapport.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projected demand data (assignments, graded reviews, historical demand) to schedule walk-in topics
- An on-line calendar for first-come, first-served individual sign-ups
#### Constraints
- Group sessions above roughly 5-6 cadets require shifting to board modeling rather than individual diagnosis

### Target Learners
- undergraduate cadets in first and second year quantitative courses

### Target Learning Goals
- course-specific quantitative problem solving and exam preparation
