# Deliver gymnastics training accompanied by non-verbal rhythmic music in 16-week sessions for children with ASD and DS

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a concrete training recipe: gymnastics activities performed to a non-verbal violin waltz with a 3/4 rhythm, administered "for 16 weeks, followed by 2 days a week, 1.5-2 hours under educator control". Activities include oscillations with music rhythm, stretching, trampoline work, balance-board walking, ball throwing and catching, and exercise-ball movements. The authors chose non-verbal music so children "can be more easily focused on the rhythm of the song".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An educator controlling the sessions; children with sufficient mental capability to adapt to the training program
#### Constraints
- The program was evaluated only in children with ASD aged 9–14 and DS aged 8–13 without existing health problems

### Target Learners
- children with autism spectrum disorder
- children with Down syndrome

### Target Learning Goals
- static and dynamic balance
- coordination
- flexibility
- loco-motor development
