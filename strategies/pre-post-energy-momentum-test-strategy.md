# Administer the energy and momentum test as a pre-/post-test to diagnose difficulties before instruction and evaluate instructional interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using the developed 25-item test both before and after instruction. It states the test "can be administered as a pre-/post-test to assess the difficulties prior to instruction, as well as those that remain uncorrected following a particular type of instructional intervention", and that it "can be used to compare the understanding of energy and momentum concepts in courses employing different instructional designs and strategies". Some classes in the study administered it both ways for exactly this purpose.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administer as a 50-minute post-test or pre-test; combine with think-aloud interviews and per-semester item analysis to judge item quality
#### Constraints
- Pre-test scores depend on class type and on whether instruction on linear kinematics/dynamics had already occurred, since some questions can be answered without energy or momentum concepts

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus- and algebra-based physics students

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing conceptual difficulties with energy and momentum
- evaluating the effectiveness of instruction
