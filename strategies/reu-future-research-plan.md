# Future REU research should survey students nationally who could not attend or chose not to apply, and interview students and site directors to identify best practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors lay out a research agenda to optimize REUs for students with limited research experience. They state their preliminary results "inform our future work, which will generate recommendations for REU directors and the NSF," including interviewing REU students and site directors on best practices and nationally surveying undergraduates who were accepted but could not attend or chose not to apply. They also plan further study of mentor–student relationships in enculturating inexperienced students as confident physics researchers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to students who were accepted to REUs but could not attend, or who wanted to apply but chose not to, a population absent from the present sample
#### Constraints
- The present sample of graduate students at a competitive R1 program cannot speak to students who faced barriers significant enough to prevent attendance

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students, particularly minoritized students and students with few research opportunities

### Target Learning Goals
- access to undergraduate research experiences and enculturation into physics research careers
