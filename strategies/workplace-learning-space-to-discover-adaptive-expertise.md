# Use workplace learning with ill-structured, complex, authentic tasks and 'space to discover' to foster adaptive expertise

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's closing recommendation is that learning or work-related tasks must be "ill-structured, complex, authentic, and either be new to the individual or new to the world", and that learners or professionals need 'space to discover'. These requirements are described as optimal in (simulated) work environments where learners or professionals have guided possibilities to work on tasks calling for adaptive expertise and adaptive performance. The authors suggest further research into the relation between workplace learning and the development of adaptive expertise.
