# Future direction: more dynamic adaptation of virtual patients to contextual factors for a diverse, adaptable learning experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Looking beyond the pandemic, the authors state that "a more dynamic adaptation of virtual patients to contextual factors is desirable", which they argue would help in extreme situations and "provide in general a more diverse and adaptable learning experience for learners". They sustain the international collaboration to further expand the VP collection as a source for deliberate practice of clinical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VP systems and authoring workflows capable of adapting case content to contextual factors
#### Constraints
- Current VPs are static and cannot be rapidly or automatically adapted

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Health profession students

### Target Learning Goals
- Deliberate practice of clinical reasoning across varied contexts
