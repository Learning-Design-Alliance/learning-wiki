# Use feedback-based case-series training of roughly 75 prostate mpMRI cases early in residency, across specialties

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes structured, feedback-based interpretation training in which trainees read a difficulty-balanced sequence of cases with immediate feedback on answer correctness, cumulative performance, and graphical progress summaries after each submission. It concludes that "most learning within 75 cases" makes an empirical benchmark of ~75 cases achievable early in training by both radiology and urology trainees, regardless of prior imaging experience, informing certification standards and early-residency curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A case sequence balanced for difficulty and maintaining the original PI-RADSv2.1 distribution
- A platform providing immediate feedback on correctness and cumulative performance after each submission
- Embedded reference resources (PI-RADSv2.1, PI-QUALv2, EPE grading materials) accessible during interpretation
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated routine cases only and did not test complex pathologies or incidental extraprostatic findings
- Results support readiness for supervised reporting, not expert-level proficiency
- Long-term skill retention was not addressed, so durability of the plateau remains unknown
- Participant numbers were modest, particularly among urology trainees (n = 4 vs n = 10)

### Target Learners
- radiology trainees (residents and fellows)
- urology residents without prior imaging experience

### Target Learning Goals
- prostate mpMRI interpretation competency: PI-RADSv2.1 classification, PI-QUALv2 image quality scoring, EPE grading, and readout efficiency
