# Pair talent identification with at-risk student detection to avoid unbalanced resource distribution

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
To address the ethical concern that focusing only on talented students may cause teachers to prioritize them and overlook struggling students, the article reports that "an at-risk student detection model has been developed alongside the TalentPredictor model in this research". Deploying both models together lets teachers identify students above or below average and provide appropriate support to each group. This pairs early talent identification with early risk identification so neither group is neglected.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both the talent prediction model and an at-risk student detection model must be available to teachers.
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a mitigation of a stated ethical concern, not as an evaluated intervention outcome.

### Target Learners
- secondary school students, including at-risk students

### Target Learning Goals
- balanced teacher support for both talented and at-risk students
