Offer full-group or small-group follow-up sessions for staff needing aid in implementing the learning system model¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Among the report's recommendations drawn from the innovation diffusion experience is that staff members who request or require help implementing the learning system model be supported through follow-up sessions. The report recommends that "The use of full group or small group follow -up sessions for those staff members requesting or requiring aid in the implementation of the learning system model Should be considered by the Director of the Division of Instructional Learning Systems." This extends the workshop's individualized support structure beyond the workshop week itself.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A director or leader with authority to schedule and sanction the follow-up sessions for division staff.

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is addressed specifically to the Director of the Division of Instructional Learning Systems and concerns implementation of this learning system model.

Target Learners¶

  • state department of education coordinators and curriculum development specialists

Target Learning Goals¶

  • implementing the learning system model in divisional responsibilities

Related Strategies¶

  • Support effective data use by offering targeted recommendations and strategies to state officials, district-level staff, and system developers
