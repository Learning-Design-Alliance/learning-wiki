# Develop Cultural Awareness

## Description
Developing cultural awareness as an educator includes building empathy for diverse learners, intentionally recognizing how one's own identity intersects with learners' identities, and creating an awareness of how the environment can impact learners' sense of belonging. This involves self-reflection, training, coaching support, and peer collaboration to identify explicit and implicit bias in oneself, in the curriculum, and in broader systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment to self-reflection and ongoing learning. Access to training, coaching, and peer support.
#### Constraints
- Can be challenging to confront personal biases. Requires sustained effort and support.
