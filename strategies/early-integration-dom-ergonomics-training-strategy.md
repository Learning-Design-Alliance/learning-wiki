# Integrate microscope-based experiential ergonomics training early in undergraduate dental curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend embedding experiential, microscope-based ergonomics courses early in dental education, on the grounds that the course improved knowledge, attitudes, and posture and synchronized competence with conviction. They state the findings justify "its early integration into dental curricula to promote long-term professional health", because ergonomic habits are difficult to build through lectures alone and MSD onset can occur during undergraduate training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Successful implementation requires consideration of equipment and facility costs, access to microscopes, ongoing device maintenance, and faculty training to guide experiential learning effectively
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single-group pretest-posttest study without a control group; long-term retention and comparison with other aids such as loupes remain untested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in preclinical training

### Target Learning Goals
- ergonomic posture habits and musculoskeletal disorder prevention

## Claims

- [Students' agreement that ergonomic practice reduces musculoskeletal pain increased post-course for all assessed body regions](../claims/attitudes-toward-ergonomics-pain-prevention-improved.md) [+W]
- [Better post-course postural performance correlated strongly and negatively with belief in ergonomics' effectiveness for pain relief (r = -0.624)](../claims/dea-score-negative-correlation-belief-ergonomics-efficacy.md) [+W]
- [Dental undergraduates' ergonomic knowledge scores rose from pre-test to post-test after a microscope-based experiential ergonomics course, in one single-group pre-post study (p < 0.001 for all three knowledge items)](../claims/dom-experiential-course-improved-ergonomic-knowledge.md) [+W]
- [Participant satisfaction was exceptionally high, with 100% satisfied with course content and all satisfaction means significantly above the neutral midpoint](../claims/high-satisfaction-microscope-ergonomics-course.md) [+W]
- [Post-course postural performance was favorable, with a mean overall Dental Ergonomics Assessment score of 1.45](../claims/post-course-dea-postural-performance-favorable.md) [+W]
