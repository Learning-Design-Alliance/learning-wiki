# Use this Toolkit: Mitigating Racial Bias in Edtech

## Description
This toolkit is designed to guide educational technology (edtech) companies in identifying and mitigating racial bias in the design and development of their products. It emphasizes that without rigorous and intentional oversight, AI and machine learning in edtech can amplify existing racial biases in school systems and introduce new ones through algorithmic design. The toolkit offers actions to take at every stage of product design and development, advocating for racial equity and a commitment to changing education for all students, especially those the system is not designed for.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment to racial equity, willingness to adopt new design and development practices, and openness to feedback from Black and Brown students and teachers.
#### Constraints
- Implementing the toolkit requires significant effort and resources. May not solve all problems related to racial bias in edtech. Relies on the ongoing commitment of edtech companies to prioritize equity.
