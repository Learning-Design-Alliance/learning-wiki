# Require: process-based assessment through metacognitive rationales for accepting or revising AI suggestions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The third framework component shifts assessment from final products to process. The authors argue that grading the essay or code is insufficient in the age of GenAI and that 'students should be required to submit a brief rationale explaining why they accepted or revised the AI’s suggestions, thereby evidencing their critical engagement'. A passing grade should require evidence that the student critically evaluated AI suggestions, justified revisions, and verified the final output.
