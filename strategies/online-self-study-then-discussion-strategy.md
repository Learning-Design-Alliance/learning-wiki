# Structure online health-professions learning as focused self-study followed by small-group discussion, rather than lecture followed by discussion

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a learning method for health professions education in which students first study written material individually (allowed to "take notes and underline the learning materials while they studied") and then discuss in online breakout groups of three or four before testing. The authors state this study "presented a learning method that could improve learning outcomes, that is, focused self-study followed by discussion", and expect it to generalize to online team-based and project-based learning in medicine and dentistry.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a video-conferencing platform with breakout rooms for group discussion
- Self-study time with written learning materials preceding the discussion
#### Constraints
- Experimental group was limited to dental students; the authors call for studies with other health professions before generalizing
- The learning topic was a non-health topic (criminal procedure law) unfamiliar to dental students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- health professions students

### Target Learning Goals
- verbatim recall of learning material
- transfer and application of material to new situations
