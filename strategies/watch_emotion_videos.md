# Watch Emotion Videos

## Description
Share helpful videos that explains big emotions to kids. Reinforce topic using different medium while providing simple steps to get students thinking about their own emotional regulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to video and time for debrief.
#### Constraints
- Requires thought about appropriate video. Some students may need different sensory activity.
