# Create small wins to motivate faculty during pedagogical change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The strategy recognizes faculty who implement flipped teaching in at least 40% of their teaching sessions through public acknowledgment and tangible rewards. Drawing on Kotter's "creating short-term wins", the article proposes presentation at faculty meetings and conferences, acknowledgment in the university newsletter, a best-teacher award, and public praise, stating these activities "will also encourage the ‘so-called reticent’ faculty members to also join the change effort".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sponsor support for the recognition initiatives
#### Constraints
- The article reports sponsors responded positively but the rewards were not yet evaluated

- medical school faculty

### Target Learning Goals
