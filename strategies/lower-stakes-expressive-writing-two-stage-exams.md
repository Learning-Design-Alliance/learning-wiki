# Use instructor-led strategies to lower test anxiety — lowering exam stakes, expressive writing, and two-stage exams — which are likely to benefit all students, especially URM students at some institutions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study found test anxiety negatively associated with exam scores at every institution type, the authors recommend strategies instructors can use to mitigate it. They write that "strategies instructors can use to mitigate test anxiety are likely to benefit all students, and especially URM students at some institutions," naming lowering the stakes of exams, expressive writing, and two-stage exams. They also cite a meta-analysis of 56 interventions showing skill-focused, behavioral, and cognitive strategies reduce test anxiety, while noting less work has tested these in biology or STEM classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Reduce test anxiety to support exam performance
