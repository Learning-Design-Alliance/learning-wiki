# Provide a prerequisite flowchart so time-limited students can study a reduced unit sequence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module ships a flowchart of learning outcomes (Figure 1) mapping the minimum recommended prerequisites for each of the nine units, enabling abbreviated pathways. The text says: "The units are best studied in numerical order. However, for those with limited time, Figure 1 shows the minimum recommended prerequisites for each unit." The authors caution that "A few references and examples may have to be skipped over, but the core content should still be understandable."
