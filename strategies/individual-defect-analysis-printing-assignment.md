# Individual printing assignment with defect identification and dimensional analysis

## Description
After the FDM and SLA labs, each student designs and prints a part to investigate the limiting attributes of a chosen AM process. The article states: "Following the FDM and SLA labs, we asked each student to design a part, or an assembly of parts, with aim to investigate the limiting attributes of a chosen AM process or combination of processes." Students report findings against structured questions covering defect identification and analysis, dimensional comparison of measured values to nominal CAD dimensions across five repeated measurements, and process redesign possibilities. The article notes students measured each key dimension five times and reported statistics relative to nominal values.
