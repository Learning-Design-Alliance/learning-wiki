# Sustain and deepen state investments in high-retention teacher pathways and streamline licensure requirements

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends policymakers institutionalize investments in high-retention pathways such as service scholarships, teacher residencies, and Grow-Your-Own programs, which "produce well-prepared teachers who stay in the profession at higher retention rates." It also recommends streamlining licensure by "allowing candidates to demonstrate their competency through coursework-based options and the rigorous performance assessment already required," and providing financial support such as the Golden State Teacher Grant Program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained (not one-time) state funding; financial supports that defray candidates' living and preparation costs
#### Constraints
- The report notes many pathway programs have been supported with one-time funds, and residency candidates struggle financially

### Target Learners
- Prospective teacher candidates, including candidates of color and rural community members

### Target Learning Goals
- Building a stable, fully prepared teacher workforce to support student learning
