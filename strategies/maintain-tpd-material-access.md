# Enable former TPD cohort members to maintain access to TPD materials and community of practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following Philipsen et al. (2019), the article recommends "enabling former TPD cohort members to maintain access to TPD materials, allowing them to refer to them as needed in their practice." The pandemic-era case shows this access, together with networking opportunities, was highly valued by faculty attendees when a program was redesigned for digital delivery.
