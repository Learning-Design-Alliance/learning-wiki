# Use pharmacy-context laboratory experiments such as measuring diffusion coefficients, fluid viscosity, and thermal properties of pharmaceutical materials

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The review recommends laboratory experiments as "another effective way to teach physics in the context of pharmacy." Specific examples it prints are "measuring the diffusion coefficients of drug molecules, analyzing fluid viscosity, and investigating the thermal properties of pharmaceutical materials." These hands-on experiences are said to reinforce theoretical concepts and prepare students for real-world challenges in research and clinical practice.
