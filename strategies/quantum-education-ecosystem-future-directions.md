# Prioritize six coordinated future directions: equity, evidence-based design, curriculum coherence, workforce alignment, educator support, and infrastructure sharing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review proposes shifting from isolated initiatives to coordinated ecosystem-level strategies, listing six priorities: "Equity and inclusion", "Evidence-based design", "Curriculum coherence", "Workforce alignment", "Educator support", and "Infrastructure sharing". These directions emphasize designing quantum education as a connected, adaptive system rather than a collection of independent efforts, to build a sustainable, inclusive, globally distributed quantum workforce.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination across academia, industry, and government agencies rather than isolated programs
#### Constraints
- Hands-on experimental training remains largely confined to well-funded institutions, limiting infrastructure sharing

### Target Learners
- K-12 through graduate learners and workforce participants in quantum education

### Target Learning Goals
- Quantum literacy, workforce preparedness, and progression across educational stages
