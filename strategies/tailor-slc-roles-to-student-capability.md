# Distribute SLC group roles and tasks according to each member's capability so students exchange information

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that within SLC group work, roles and tasks be matched to individual students' capabilities so that members can exchange information with one another. It reasons that because "not all students have the same ability", tailoring the distribution of roles lets each member contribute and share information among group members, supporting both conceptual understanding and independent learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must know or assess the capability development of each student before assigning roles
#### Constraints
- The article states this adjustment is needed because students differ in abilities such as understanding certain physics phenomena

### Target Learners
- secondary vocational school students in heterogeneous groups

### Target Learning Goals
- independent learning
- collaborative information exchange
- physics conceptual understanding
