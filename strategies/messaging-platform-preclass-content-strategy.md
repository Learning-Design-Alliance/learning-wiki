# Deliver pre-class content and practical-session videos through messaging platforms (Eitaa and Telegram) to enable non-attendance preparation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable tactic for flipped and blended courses: the teacher prepares educational videos, pamphlets, and clips and shares them with students via the Eitaa and Telegram messaging platforms, which students use at home before class. The article used this to deliver "non-attendance educational content" and to provide practical-session videos before clinical skills sessions, supporting preparation without requiring physical attendance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-prepared educational videos and pamphlets
- Students' access to the messaging platforms Eitaa or Telegram
#### Constraints
- Described for medical students with technology access; the article notes students may not always complete required homework before class

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- pre-class preparation for flipped sessions
- preparation for practical skills sessions
