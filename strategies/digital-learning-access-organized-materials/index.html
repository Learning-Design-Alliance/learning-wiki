Strategies

Increase access to digital learning so materials are organized and accessible online¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends increasing access to digital learning, which "feels natural to students and also helps them organize information and complete their assignments." It acknowledges the benefits of digital learning are nuanced while citing an evidence review showing technology can sometimes enhance student learning experiences. Students valued having all learning materials compiled online rather than digging through backpacks and paper.
