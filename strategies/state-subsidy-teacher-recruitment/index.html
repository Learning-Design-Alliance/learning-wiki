Recruit the rural teaching profession through state subsidies, supported by survey evidence and arguments from other professions' recruiting methods¶

Description¶

The notice reports that Hertzog's monograph, having documented rural teaching conditions, "proposes plans for recruiting the profession through state subsidies." The strategy is to attract teachers into rural schools through state-funded subsidies, with the proposal backed by supporting data and by arguments drawn from how other professions recruit their members. The recommendation belongs to the monograph as characterized by the notice.
