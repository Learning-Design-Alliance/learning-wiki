# Students calculate spacetime sector models themselves using a simplified method requiring only elementary mathematics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A simplified calculation of sector models, extended from curved surfaces to the 1+1-dimensional case, presumes knowledge of the Minkowski metric. Using this method students can calculate sector models on their own using elementary mathematics only, enabling them to study other curved spacetimes when given their metric. The calculation starts from the Schwarzschild metric and constructs each sector as a symmetric trapezium.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be familiar with the Minkowski metric
#### Constraints
- The approximations of the simplified method are discussed in paper II

### Target Learners

- pre-service physics teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- calculating sector models from a given metric
- studying curved spacetimes independently
