# Develop an AI-powered lecture assistant (AILA) that automatically detects opportune moments for retrieval practice intervention

## Description
Building on the observed benefits, the authors propose developing an AI-powered lecture assistant (AILA) that 'can automatically detect a favorite moment for intervention' during live teaching. Stated future directions include 'automatic quality control of the LLM-generated retrieval practice questions', studying AILA's impacts on students' long-term retention in diverse teaching environments, and investigating instructor perceptions and engagement with such a system during course preparation and live teaching.
