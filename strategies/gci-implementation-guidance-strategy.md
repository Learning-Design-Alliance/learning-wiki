# Implementation guidance for educators adopting GCI approaches in engineering courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors distill their first-time implementation into concrete guidance for educators. "Essential principles include starting with single activities, making pedagogical rationale explicit, providing substantial scaffolding, ensuring psychological safety, timing activities realistically, documenting collective artifacts, and most critically, aligning assessment with pedagogy." The alignment principle is emphasized because, if evaluation remains purely individual, students rationally prioritize individual preparation over collective learning. The strategy presumes instructors can structure two-session activities with defined AI consultation points and reflection artifacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment must be aligned with collective pedagogy; substantial scaffolding and psychological safety must be provided for group reasoning phases.
#### Constraints
- Guidance derives from a single-institution, two-course first-time implementation in Qatar; the authors call for randomized controlled trials and standardized protocols before generalization.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students
- instructors implementing AI-augmented collaborative activities

### Target Learning Goals
- productive integration of GenAI into collaborative learning
- strategic AI use rather than dependency
