# Use group training for the computing-experiment stage and individual multi-level tasks for remaining stages

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends organizing the computing-experiment stage of modeling tasks as group work, because individualized computing experiment on real tasks is nearly unimplementable given restricted school machine time. For the remaining stages, an individual form of training with multi-level tasks is used to organize pupils' independent activity. Classes are also carried out using step-by-step formation of the digestion process.
