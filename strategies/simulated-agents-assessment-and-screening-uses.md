# Extend simulated teaching practice to student assessment and teacher screening uses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond training, the authors propose two implementable uses: assigning simulated sections to students, where guiding agents to a solution may reveal the user's own understanding of material, and using EducaSim as one step in screening teachers for online courses. Because current asynchronous screenings are "vulnerable to Generative AI simply doing the work", tuning agents with personality randomness could yield non-uniform experiences with more distinct markers, such as time spent per utterance, for potential dishonesty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment and screening uses presuppose that interaction quality reflects the user's understanding, which the authors frame as a possibility based on prior human-to-human work
#### Constraints
- The screening use case is speculative: the article presents it as a potential step, not a validated measure

### Target Learners
- CS1 students; applicants to teach online courses

### Target Learning Goals
- Gauging understanding through teaching; evaluating online teaching readiness
