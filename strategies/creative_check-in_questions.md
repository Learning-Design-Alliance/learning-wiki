# Creative Check-In Questions

## Description
Instructors ask thought-provoking questions to encourage reflection and emotional awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A list of thought-provoking questions and time for reflection and discussion (5-10 minutes).
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness of the activity depends on the quality of the questions and the willingness of students to participate.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- All ages, adaptable for elementary through college students

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Encourage reflection, promote emotional awareness, foster creativity, build a positive classroom environment

### Affordances
- Provides a quick and easy way to check-in with students. Encourages reflection and emotional awareness. Promotes creativity and imagination.

### Personalization
- The questions can be adapted to focus on specific emotional categories or to address specific classroom needs.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe student participation in answering the questions. Assess the thoughtfulness and specificity of students' responses.

## Impact
- Acknowledging emotions calms the amygdala and reduces the intensity of felt emotions, helping individuals feel more in control. Promotes emotional literacy and awareness.
