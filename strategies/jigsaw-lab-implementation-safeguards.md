# Implementation safeguards when applying jigsaw in laboratory courses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
From their conclusions the authors recommend practical safeguards for jigsaw in laboratory settings: groups "should be given sufficient amount of time and the required opportunities should be provided for pre -preparation", missing tools should be completed beforehand, members' responsibilities should be made explicit, and non-fulfilling members should be detected so precautions can be taken. They also advise against jigsaw in very crowded classes because of class-control difficulty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient time and opportunities for pre-preparation
- Complete tools and materials before the experiment
- Monitor and address members who do not fulfill responsibility
#### Constraints
- Not recommended for very crowded classes due to difficulty controlling the class

### Target Learners
- university science-education students in laboratory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- effective laboratory practice
- shared group responsibility
