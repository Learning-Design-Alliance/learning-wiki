# Phase 3D VR co-creation with scaffolding, preparatory communication training, and analytics-based predictive support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's forward-looking design recommendations for immersive co-creation: educators should adopt "phased implementation models, beginning with lower-complexity 3D VR tasks and gradually increasing cognitive demands as learners gain fluency", supported by guided practice, real-time feedback, structured collaborative tasks, built-in collaborative prompts, guided help-seeking channels, and adaptive feedback mechanisms. Learning analytics on student-generated behaviors can enable predictive models that scaffold engagement and self-regulation. Preparatory training in communication and co-regulation strategies is also advised.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparatory training in communication and coworking strategies before immersive co-creation tasks
- Support systems such as built-in collaborative prompts, guided help-seeking channels, and adaptive feedback mechanisms within 3D VR platforms
#### Constraints
- The article states insufficient familiarization time with 3D VR may exacerbate the novelty effect and limit engagement with higher-level cognitive strategies

### Target Learners
- Senior high school students in distance or hybrid learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Collaborative knowledge co-construction
- Self-regulated learning in immersive environments
