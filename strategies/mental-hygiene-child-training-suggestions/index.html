A printed set of practical mental-hygiene suggestions for training and protecting children¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The conference's printed program gave, in the report's words, "concrete practical suggestions in brief form for the protection of mental health." The listed suggestions are implementable home- and school-level practices: training children in cleanliness, healthful sleep in fresh air, varied wholesome activities and interests, regular occupation suited to ability with alternation of work and rest, attention to the present situation, protection from frights and shocks, and training by example.
