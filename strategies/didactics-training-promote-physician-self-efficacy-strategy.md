# Offer low-threshold, accessible didactics training and early teaching involvement to promote physicians' teaching-related self-efficacy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that medical faculties and university hospitals create an environment promoting teaching-related self-efficacy, e.g., "by offering low-threshold, accessible but mandatory didactics training", and that young doctors be involved in teaching at an early stage to gain first teaching experience alongside medical skills. They also suggest initial course offerings during medical studies to prepare students for didactic activities as instructors or peer teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Subject-specific didactics training offerings before the first teaching session, with students' daily tasks relieved during training
#### Constraints
- The supporting evidence is cross-sectional and correlational; the authors state interventional study designs are needed to clarify causal relationships and which training content is most conducive

### Target Learners
- young physicians at university hospitals
- medical students as prospective peer teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- teaching-related self-efficacy
- didactic skills
