# Support teachers to customize curriculum for local relevance and cultural responsiveness

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article outlines an example research project, Customizing OpenSciEd materials to students' local contexts, identities, and funds of knowledge, combining professional learning strategies with design heuristics. The project "would involve teachers who have experience teaching OpenSciEd units and who wish to develop customizations to meet specific needs of their students or community contexts", with developers and researchers co-designing supports and studying outcomes such as equitable engagement and integrity of implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Customizations must preserve OpenSciEd's distinctive principles; substantial pedagogical design capacity is needed because designing coherent NGSS-aligned materials is demanding
#### Constraints
- Materials designed at scale target a national audience; teachers and districts lack the time and effort for local customization without supports

### Target Learners
- Underrepresented learner populations in STEM, including students whose funds of knowledge differ from national curriculum defaults

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable student engagement, classroom participation, cultural responsiveness in science
