# Intelligent tools should dynamically process student activity to summarize group and individual contributions for educators

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For adoption at scale, the editors recommend intelligent tools for educators with limited time and limited cognizance of comprehensive assessment practices: "Intelligent tools that dynamically process student activities, summarize both overall group work as well as individual student contributions and recommend best practices can support these stakeholders in maximizing the efficacy of learning in groups." This targets teachers and administrators as the supported stakeholders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools that dynamically process student activities and produce summaries and recommendations usable by educators
#### Constraints
- Educators generally have both limited time and limited cognizance of comprehensive assessment practices

### Target Learners
- students working in groups, indirectly supported via their educators

### Target Learning Goals
- assessment and management of group learning
