# Cultural Responsive Practice

## Description
Culturally responsive practice involves engaging students in the learning process through an understanding of how their identity and perspective is shaped by their culture and community experiences. It encompasses many different aspects of a person’s identities including native language, gender, and able bodiedness as well as aspects that may be less visible such as mental health and learning disabilities. Understanding different cultural frameworks can provide insight into how and where implicit bias shows up in classrooms and curricula and help teachers push back on dominant narratives that have historically and systematically excluded students. Culturally responsive practice taps into students’ funds of knowledge and builds cognitive and emotional capacity for productive struggle, supporting them in becoming independent learners.
