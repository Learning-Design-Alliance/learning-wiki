## Description
The commentary recommends that institutions apply and extend insights from Bennett et al.'s research to build faculty design capacity. It frames this as a systems view of "obtaining the faculty design talent needed to rapidly deploy or scale up digital learning." The two named practices are professional development and strategic faculty hiring.
