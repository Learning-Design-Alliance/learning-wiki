# Structure seminar talks and fights on different problems with feedback carryover

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on their seminar experience, the authors recommend that in future seminars each student gives a talk and a short physics-fight presentation not about the same, but about different problems, so more problems are covered and the exchange between students is intensified. Students were able to include feedback from their first presentation when taking the stage a second time. Training fights will mainly be performed right before the GPT, with more free discussions about possible approaches instead.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduling must allow talks and fights between semester start and the GPT date
#### Constraints
- IPT 2021 tasks may not yet be published when the seminar starts, so earlier years' problems can be used

### Target Learners
- university physics students in tournament preparation seminars

### Target Learning Goals
- preparing multiple tournament problems
- incorporating peer feedback
- discussing open problems
