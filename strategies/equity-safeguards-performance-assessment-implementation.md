# Safeguard equity in performance assessment systems through non-negotiables, clear rubrics, normed scoring, multiple pathways, and inclusive design

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report describes concrete equity strategies states used: creating "non-negotiables" about expectations and quality to counter perceptions that performance assessments are less rigorous; providing clear rubrics to students to reduce grading bias; developing standard practices to norm and score tasks; maintaining multiple pathways and diverse demonstrations of knowledge to avoid a gatekeeper mentality; and connecting with communities historically marginalized by assessment practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear rubrics shared with students, standard norming and scoring processes, and capacity building to ensure equitable access to high-quality performance assessments.
#### Constraints
- The report notes some states observed that performance assessments did not guarantee equity.

### Target Learners
- All students, with special attention to language learners, students with disabilities, and communities historically marginalized by assessment practices

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable opportunities to demonstrate knowledge and skills through performance assessment
