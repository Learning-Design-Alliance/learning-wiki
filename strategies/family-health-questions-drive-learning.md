# Use real family health questions as the driver for intern literature retrieval and response

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the SFHC model, family members "could upload real case information, laboratory test sheets, inspection reports, and other data of the ill relatives, and initiate consultations and raise disease-related questions". Interns are then guided to "independently retrieve literature, diagnosis and treatment guidelines, and clinically relevant data" and answer family questions in the group, with the guidance team correcting biased answers. This strategy grounds learning in authentic, demand-driven tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A WeChat group platform connecting interns, family members, and a guidance team
- A guidance team able to intervene and correct inaccurate intern answers
#### Constraints
- All family-provided case information must be anonymized and consent obtained

### Target Learners
- General surgical nursing interns

### Target Learning Goals
- Literature retrieval skills
- Clinical knowledge application
- Doctor-patient communication
