# Advise students to maintain a large set of learning goals or a journaling approach to sustain reflection app use through placement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that "students with a large set of goals or who used a journaling approach stayed active longer" and concludes "certain types of use are more conducive to self-regulated learning and should be advised." This strategy recommends steering students toward SEC-related goals or reflective journaling rather than logging only clinical procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need learning goals whose urgency persists beyond the introductory placement phase
#### Constraints
- Students using the app only for clinical-skill goals lacked urgency and quit; the finding comes from a single optional-use field trial

### Target Learners
- nursing students on clinical placement

### Target Learning Goals
- socioemotional competence development
- sustained reflective learning

## Claims

- [SEC-focused app use was limited (7 of 15 students) but involved students used the app longer and reported greater benefits](../claims/sec-focused-use-limited-but-longer-and-greater-benefit.md) [+W]
