# Offer basic-skills mathematics enrichment to students below SAT-M 600 before college-level mathematically intensive coursework

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Regarding SAT-M as a diagnostic, the authors recommend that students testing below roughly 600 who intend to major in a mathematically intensive subject "should be oﬀered enrichment in the basics of high school level mathematics before continuing on to colle ge level work". They suggest intensive summer preparation programs or a gap year focused on raising SAT-M related capabilities, modeled on existing programs for incoming students from underrepresented groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized math diagnostic scores available before students begin college-level work in mathematically intensive fields
#### Constraints
- The article notes the underlying cause of the score-GPA correlation is unresolved; whether intensive intervention can elevate low-scoring students' performance is an empirical issue in need of actual data

### Target Learners
- prospective undergraduate STEM majors with SAT-M below ~600

### Target Learning Goals
- basic high-school-level mathematics readiness for college physics and mathematics coursework
