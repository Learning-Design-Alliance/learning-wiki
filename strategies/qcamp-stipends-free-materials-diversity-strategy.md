# Provide participant stipends and free materials to remove financial barriers and reach socioeconomically diverse participants

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To encourage socioeconomic diversity in a summer outreach program, QCaMP sent materials to participants free-of-charge and paid each participant a stipend, which made up the majority of the program's costs. The authors reason that "financial stress disproportionately affects the underrepresented communities we hope to reach with this program," so prospective participants otherwise face choosing between summer jobs and camps that could prepare them for better opportunities. Stipend amounts were chosen to be comparable to a one-week teacher salary in New Mexico for teachers and a half-week at minimum wage for students. This strategy directly serves the program goal of reaching participants across gender, racial/ethnic, regional, and socioeconomic diversity.
