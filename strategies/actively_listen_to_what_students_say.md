# Actively Listen to What Students Say

## Description
Give your full attention and listen to both a student’s words and tone of voice. State in your own words what you think you’ve heard and then confirm by asking, “Is that what you’re telling me?” That simple question gives students an opening to correct any misunderstanding and shows respect for their feelings and perspective.
