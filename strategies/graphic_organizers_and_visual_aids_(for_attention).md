# Graphic Organizers and Visual Aids (for Attention)

## Description
These tools help learners identify the most important information and focus on it. Graphic organizers structure content visually, making it easier to discern key points and relationships. Visual aids, such as diagrams and color-coded notes, also support attention by presenting information in an accessible and engaging manner. This strategy is particularly effective for learners who benefit from visual supports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Graphic organizer templates, visual aids (e.g., diagrams, color-coded notes), digital tools (optional), and instructor guidance.
#### Constraints
- Requires initial instruction on how to use the tools effectively. Some learners may find the visual structure too rigid or overwhelming if not tailored to their needs.
