# Support deep CI implementation through capacity inventories, gradual-release coaching, structured collaboration opportunities, and student voice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's implications section recommends actions for intermediaries and school system leaders pursuing networked continuous improvement. Intermediaries may use a capacity inventory process before teams join a network and periodically thereafter, use coaching grounded in a gradual release of responsibility to promote school-site ownership, and facilitate collaboration through convenings, role-alike meetings, spotlight protocols, and knowledge management systems. At the school level, using student voice in root cause analyses and inquiry cycles is described as a promising way to gain insight into students' assets, needs, and context, and sustainability planning should promote local ownership and ongoing knowledge sharing.
