# Structured managerial training programmes and a centralised IQN skill-assessment framework as policy recommendations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends two implementable actions: structured training for healthcare managers and administrators, and a centralised framework to assess and manage IQN specialty skills. It states "there is a clear need for structured training programmes for healthcare managers and administrators", focused on understanding diverse international nursing backgrounds, and that the framework "should provide guidance on the assessment of qualifications, orientation programmes, mentorship opportunities and cultural integration initiatives". The framework is also proposed to reduce administrative burdens on managers, drawing on Western Health's structured overseas-recruitment guidance.

## Design Implications
