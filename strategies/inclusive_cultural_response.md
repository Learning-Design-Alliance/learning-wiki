# Inclusive Cultural Response

## Description
Inclusive Cultural Response involves addressing gaps in cultural learning and representation by adding and including texts and making changes in units of study. This cultural response addresses historical omissions by including authors, perspectives, and experiences traditionally marginalized to make content more relevant and engaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Diverse and representative materials, willingness to revise curriculum, ability to facilitate inclusive discussions.
#### Constraints
- Can be seen as tokenism if not implemented thoughtfully, requires ongoing effort to stay current with diverse voices and perspectives, may be challenging to address all cultural gaps within a single course.
