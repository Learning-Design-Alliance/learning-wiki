# Adopt blended learning combining e-learning with traditional classrooms for medical education amid crises

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's practical recommendation is that "policymakers in higher education should consider adopting blended learning, which includes a mix of online (e-learning) and offline (traditional classrooms) to harvest the benefits of both approaches to learning." Educators are also advised to redesign courses to fit e-learning and ensure more engagement with students. This strategy responds to the finding that e-learning alone is limited for practical medical courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional capacity to deliver both online platforms (e.g., Blackboard) and traditional classroom sessions
#### Constraints
- Stated for medical education contexts where practical courses and clinical sessions require conventional learning

### Target Learners
- medical students in higher education

### Target Learning Goals
- sustaining quality education during disruptions while preserving practical skill development
