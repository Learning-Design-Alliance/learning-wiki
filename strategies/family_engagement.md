# Family Engagement

## Description
Family engagement is a practice in which families and school personnel collaborate to support and improve the learning and development of children. It involves behaviors and practices in the school environment, such as parent/guardian-child and parent/guardian-teacher communication, roles, and responsibilities at home and school. Effective engagement requires thoughtful planning, mentoring, open communication, and modeling high expectations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commitment from school personnel, active participation from families, and effective communication channels
#### Constraints
- Requires time, effort, and resources to develop and maintain. May be challenging to engage all families.
