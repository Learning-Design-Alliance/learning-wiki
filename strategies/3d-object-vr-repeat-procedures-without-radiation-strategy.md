# Use interactive 3D object-based VR to let students repeat radiation-involved procedures without exposure and with feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
For procedures involving radiation exposure or limited instructor availability, the article recommends interactive VR simulation as a practice medium. "Replicating these interactions through VR simulation can reduce radiation exposure during dental radiography training." The strategy is to build the procedure from 3D objects so learners can manipulate equipment, repeat the entire process or specific steps as needed, and receive feedback on correct and incorrect actions, complementing mannequin and peer practice where individualized feedback is difficult.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR content with user–object interaction (HMD and controller) rather than passive 360° video, since 360° videos "are designed for passive experiences with minimal interaction"
#### Constraints
- Providing expensive equipment to every dental institution is challenging, per the Background; the study evaluated user experience only, not clinical competence

### Target Learners
- dental, dental hygiene, and other health-professions students in preclinical procedure training

### Target Learning Goals
- repetitive practice of clinical procedures involving radiation exposure or equipment constraints
