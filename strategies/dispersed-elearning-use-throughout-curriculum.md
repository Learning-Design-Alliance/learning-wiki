# Use e-learning modules in a dispersed manner throughout the curriculum rather than concentrated before assessments

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that practice resources be used in a frequent manner throughout the curriculum rather than concentrated shortly before assessments. The authors observed that e-learning modules were used in a dispersed manner throughout the curriculum and that this pattern was associated with passing, whereas practice tests used largely shortly before the test showed no significant association. They suggest practice tests "if used, like the e‐learning modules, in a more frequent manner throughout the curriculum" might contribute more.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning modules available across the curriculum timeline
#### Constraints
- The contribution of more frequently used practice tests is a suggestion, not a tested result

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- pharmacotherapy knowledge and prescribing skills
