## Description
The article proposes "una secuencia de actividades did´ acticas" for implementing the model as an educational tool: conceptual exploration of the physical and mathematical principles before programming, incremental step-by-step development starting from a simple system, systematic parameterized experimentation, design of interaction matrices for predefined behaviors such as grouping, segregation or pursuit, quantitative analysis of emergent patterns, model extension with new features, and a final project applying the model to a real-world phenomenon such as flocking, cellular dynamics or social phenomena.
