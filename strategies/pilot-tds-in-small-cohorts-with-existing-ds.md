# Adopt TDS by piloting in smaller cohorts within institutions that already use traditional double scoring

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends a phased adoption path for institutions considering TDS. It states that "Best practices for adoption include piloting TDS in smaller cohorts before scaling up" and identifies institutions already running traditional double scoring as well positioned, since TDS then requires minimal adjustments and the follow-up practice reduces operational burden. The article also cautions that implementation may face resistance to change and requires faculty training.
