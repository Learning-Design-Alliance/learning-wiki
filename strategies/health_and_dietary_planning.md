# Health and Dietary Planning

## Description
Tasks involve studying the food pyramid, learning about daily requirements, and planning food for one day. They promote an understanding of health and dietary needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Food pyramid chart, dietary information, planning materials
