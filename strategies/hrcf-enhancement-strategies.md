# Enhance HRCF with instructor sense-making support, automated issue prioritization, and instructor response interfaces

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the null first-use result and the null large-enrollment results, the authors recommend enhancements that support instructors in interpreting weekly feedback and acting at scale. Concretely, "HRCF may incorporate automated clustering and prioritization of recurring issues or use AI to generate helpful action plans that would support instructional improvements based on the received feedback." They also propose an interface where instructors respond directly to students' feedback, since students' motivation to give thoughtful feedback depends on believing it will be valued, and recommend evaluating these improvements via field experiments.

### Target Learning Goals
