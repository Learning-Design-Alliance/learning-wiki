# Structured pre- and post-simulation assignments to consolidate clinical reasoning learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before each simulation session, students complete a preparatory task aligned with scenario objectives, such as developing an interview script or planning assessment and treatment strategies. After the session, a reflective exercise is assigned within 48–72 hours that prompts students to identify key clinical information, consider the patient's perspective using a biopsychosocial approach, and select evidence-based interventions. Over time, students are also encouraged to reflect on their own CR processes and interpersonal strategies.

## Design Implications
