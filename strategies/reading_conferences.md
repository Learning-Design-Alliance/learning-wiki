# Reading Conferences

## Description
Reading conferences involve one-on-one interactions between an instructor and a learner during independent reading time. The instructor asks questions, provides targeted feedback, and guides the learner in practicing comprehension strategies tailored to improve their genre knowledge and overall reading skills. These conferences allow for individual support, identifying strengths and learning gaps, and providing opportunities for practice in authentic contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires dedicated time for one-on-one conferences during independent reading. Instructor needs to be trained in conducting effective reading conferences, asking targeted questions, and providing appropriate feedback. Digital tools or reading materials suitable for independent reading at various levels.
#### Constraints
- Can be time-consuming for the instructor, especially in large classes. Requires careful planning and organization to ensure all learners receive adequate support. May not be suitable for learners who are uncomfortable with one-on-one interactions.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Improve reading comprehension, provide individualized feedback, identify learning gaps, enhance genre knowledge

### Affordances
- Provides personalized learning experiences, allows for immediate feedback, and supports the development of comprehension strategies. Instructors can adapt their instruction based on individual learner needs and progress. It supports the development of learner independence and self-regulation in reading.

### Personalization
- Can be adapted to different reading levels and learning needs by adjusting the complexity of questions and comprehension strategies used. Can also be delivered using different modalities (e.g., in-person, video conferencing) to accommodate different learner preferences and access.

## Assessment Evidence
- Observe improvements in reading comprehension skills, track progress in genre knowledge, monitor the application of comprehension strategies, and analyze learner responses during conferences to gauge understanding and engagement.

## Impact
- Personalized reading support: Research shows that one-on-one reading conferences can lead to significant improvements in reading comprehension and motivation, particularly for struggling readers. Targeted feedback: Studies indicate that providing specific and actionable feedback during reading conferences helps learners develop effective comprehension strategies and build confidence in their reading abilities.
