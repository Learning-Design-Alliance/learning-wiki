## Description
The article adopts, for the use of new media in centers, the strategies it attributes to Martin-Moreno (1994): "A search for a correspondence between building and furniture designs", a new conception of the student work-place, "Redistribution of work areas through mobility of the furniture", student participation, and consideration of the urban dimension. These accompany the design conditions for technology-rich spaces.
