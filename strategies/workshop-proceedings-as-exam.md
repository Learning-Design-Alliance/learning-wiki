# Use a workshop with published, refereed proceedings as the exam form for an advanced research course

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes and documents an assessment format in which students' examined work takes the form of talks delivered at a workshop and written up in a published proceedings book. The author states that "The workshop with published proceedings book was, as a matter of fact, the innovative form of exam that I proposed for the advanced research course." Contributions were refereed, giving students an authentic peer-review experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An organized workshop venue with a published proceedings book
- A referee pool to review the contributed papers
#### Constraints
- Described for an advanced research course in astrophysical dynamics at graduate level

### Target Learners
- graduate students
- senior researchers
- motivated undergraduate students with good background in physics and mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- communicating research results
- writing refereed scientific contributions
