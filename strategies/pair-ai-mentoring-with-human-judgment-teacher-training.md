# Pair AI-generated mentoring with human judgment when scaling teacher education

## Description
The editorial proposes using GAI to scale teacher training while insisting that AI support be combined with human expertise. It reports that AI-generated mentoring in simulated classroom environments can provide "feedback aligned with educational theory" but "lacks human experts' nuanced and balanced insights", so AI-based mentoring must be paired with human judgment to address the complexities of classroom practice.
