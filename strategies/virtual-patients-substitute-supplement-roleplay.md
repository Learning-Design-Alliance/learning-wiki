# Use linear virtual patients as a substitute or supplement to role-play, not as the sole method

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors conclude that linear virtual patients focused on the patient's perspective could serve as a substitute when holding real-time classes is not possible or when a student is absent, and that mixing the methods during classes could make them more engaging. However, they state they cannot recommend virtual patients as the sole alternative since satisfaction was significantly higher in the role-play group. Students themselves suggested virtual patients could serve as a supplement to role-playing.

## Design Implications
