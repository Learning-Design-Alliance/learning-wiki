# Structured Discussion Techniques

## Description
Facilitating productive discussions requires clear expectations, modeling, sentence starters (particularly for English language learners), and routines. These techniques ensure that effective discussions can occur among learners even without direct instructor supervision. Structured discussions promote engagement, respect, and deeper understanding of the material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires training in facilitation techniques and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may feel constrained by structured techniques. Requires ongoing monitoring and adjustment.
