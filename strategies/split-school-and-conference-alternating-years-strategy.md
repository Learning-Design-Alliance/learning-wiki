# Permanently split the school and conference components of a biennial training event and alternate them across host countries in different years

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report states that combining a two-week school with a conference in a single three-week event often led to confusion about whether the event was a school or a conference, and that the COVID-19 pandemic forced the two components to be held separately. Going forward, the authors state that "the school and the conference parts of ASP will be permanently split and organized in diﬀerent countries in alternating years", which they say will also allow more African countries the opportunity to host an ASP event.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The school and conference must be organized as distinct events in different countries and alternating years.
#### Constraints
- The three-week combined arrangement often led to confusion whether the event was a school or a conference.

### Target Learners
- African university students
- African physics researchers and early career physicists

### Target Learning Goals
- Intensive training in fundamental and applied physics
- Dissemination of physics research through a dedicated conference
