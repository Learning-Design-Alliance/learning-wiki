# Use a green-yellow-red cone signal system to calibrate instructor interventions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Adopted from colleagues at the University of Hannover, each student group is provided with three cones: green, yellow, and red. Students display green when working smoothly, yellow when encountering problems or troubleshooting scenarios, and red after persistently attempting to resolve a problem without success. "This visual cue allows instructors to tailor their interventions based on the students' expressed state, for example avoiding seeking interaction with students too often when they feel they are working smoothly on a process."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each group of students must have access to the three cones and display them during experimental work
#### Constraints
- There are occasions when students prefer to troubleshoot independently, even when encountering difficulties

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students working in small groups in a physics laboratory

### Target Learning Goals
- independent troubleshooting and decision-making
- self-reflection on experimental process status
