# Deliver social isolation and loneliness interventions for older people as group activities with educational or support input, targeted at specific groups

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's synthesis supports delivering interventions as group activities that include an educational or support input and that "target specific groups" of older people, rather than generic one-to-one support, advice and information, or health-needs assessment. Nine of the ten effective interventions in the review were group activities with an educational or support input, making targeted group formats the approach the evidence favours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group activity format with an educational or support input
- Targeting of specific groups of older people
#### Constraints
- Evidence base limited to quantitative outcome studies from 1970 to 2002, mostly conducted in the USA and Canada, with design, methods, quality and transferability varying considerably

### Target Learners
- older people at risk of social isolation or loneliness

### Target Learning Goals
- alleviating social isolation and loneliness
