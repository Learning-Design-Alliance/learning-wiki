# Graphic Organizers as Thinking Tools

## Description
Students interact with content by organizing chunks of it into graphic organizers in groups, pairs, or individually, then comparing their results. This helps learners grapple with content, work with similarities and differences, and engage in critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to graphic organizer templates and content.
#### Constraints
- Requires time and effort to structure content into organizers effectively.
