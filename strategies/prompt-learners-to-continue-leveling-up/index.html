Use design interventions that make the levels system visible and prompt continuation to encourage learners to level up¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because learner awareness of the levels system was relatively low, Duolingo added design elements giving learners "a visual stand-in for how the levels system works" and a pop-up with a redirect button shown upon finishing a level, prompting learners to start the next level in the skill. These interventions directly operationalize the finding that completing higher levels has measurable learning value, and A/B tests showed they increased lessons completed beyond the required foundational level and overall studying activity.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A platform with a multi-level skill structure whose levels carry learning value
  • Measurement infrastructure to detect changes in leveling behavior

Constraints¶

  • Grounded in the Duolingo gamified self-directed platform context; the article does not claim generalization to other platforms

Target Learners¶

  • Self-directed learners on gamified online learning platforms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Increased practice at higher difficulty levels of previously studied material
