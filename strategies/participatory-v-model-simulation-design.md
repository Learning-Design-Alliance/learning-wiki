# Participatory, V-model systems-engineering design process with end-user engagement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The development approach engages clinical end-users throughout an iterative prototype cycle. The authors write, "We followed participatory [32], [33] and V-model approaches to systems engineering [32] to identify the appropriate mode of delivery for intubation": requirements are established with end-users, specified into component modules (laryngoscopy, vital sign monitoring, endotracheal tube insertion), implemented, then tested in reverse order from lower-level modules through integration, system and acceptance testing with clinical domain experts.
