# Use a multiple-baseline across-classrooms observation routine to monitor on-task behavior around classroom activity breaks

## Description
The study evaluated on-task behavior with a multiple-baseline across-classrooms design, observing students before and after Energizers activities. As printed: "A multiple-baseline across-classrooms design was used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Energizers on on-task behavior." Teachers or researchers can adopt this observe-before/observe-after routine across classrooms to judge whether activity breaks change on-task behavior.
