# Build low-cost 3D anatomy models using open-source files, Blender, and Sketchfab to maximize accessibility

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that educators create 3D digital learning resources without technology training by combining free open-source anatomical files, the free Blender creation suite, and the free browser-based Sketchfab platform. This approach, the authors state, was chosen so "the creation and distribution of the model was performed at no or low cost, and with a procedure that avoided difficult technological tasks."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time investment to find open-source files and learn Blender basics
- Internet-connected device for student access
#### Constraints
- More comprehensive models with unique features exist but at cost to students

### Target Learners
- Educators without technology-related training creating resources for their students

### Target Learning Goals
- Supplement traditional textbook studying with accessible 3D anatomy resources
