# Before-and-after design with randomly selected after-only control group to separate CPD effects from mere-measurement effects

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To test the instrument's validity and sensitivity to change, the protocol uses "a before-and-after study design" in which half of participants, randomly selected, complete the evaluation instrument both before and at the end of the CPD activity, and half complete it only after. "Comparing results from participants who completed the instrument before and after the CPD activity and those who completed it only after the activity will allow us to explore the mere-measurement effect." All participants are contacted three months later by phone to collect self-reported clinical behaviour for estimating predictive validity. The design also supports paired t-tests of intention change and multiple-group analysis contrasting family physicians and specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants active in clinical practice for six months after the indexed activity and reachable by phone at three months
#### Constraints
- Self-reported behaviour is used as a proxy because chart audit or standardized simulated patients are not feasible in this project

### Target Learners
- family physicians
- medical specialists

### Target Learning Goals
- detecting construct change in response to CPD activities
