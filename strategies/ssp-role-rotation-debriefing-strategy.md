# Use student standardized patients with role rotation and multi-source debriefing in scenario-based skills training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's intervention used Student Standardized Patients (SSP) in small groups of 5-6, with nurse and SSP roles "assigned by lottery before class and were rotated across different cases". Before class, students analyzed cases online; during class they performed skills training and humanistic care practices; after class, instructors, students, and SSPs jointly participated in debriefing focused on Mini-CEX performance, identifying common errors and formulating improvement suggestions. SSPs were coached to use standardized phrases and modeled patient and family behaviors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SSP recruitment, training, and deployment; small groups of 5-6 students; case materials distributed one week before sessions
#### Constraints
- The authors note that using student peers as standardized patients may introduce potential bias in the portrayal of clinical symptoms and signs, possibly influencing clinical reasoning development

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in practical skills courses

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical skills
- nurse-patient communication
- empathy
- clinical reasoning
