# Incorporate time-matched active control conditions and adequately powered designs when evaluating serious games as instructional add-ons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future evaluations of serious-game add-ons use time-matched active comparators and adequately powered samples. It states that "future studies should incorporate time-matched comparators (e.g., case-based discussion or alternative non-game activities) to isolate the specific contribution of the serious game and support causal inference", and calls for "larger, adequately powered studies with longitudinal follow-up". This strategy addresses the confound of unequal instructional exposure in the pilot design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must match total instructional time between game and control conditions
#### Constraints
- The source study itself did not use a time-matched active control, so its null between-group finding cannot isolate game-specific effects

### Target Learners
- Medical trainees in simulation-based education research

### Target Learning Goals
- Airway management knowledge and clinical reasoning
