Foster self-determined motivation at the start of a learning cycle to support later achievement¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Since the self-determined profile related to the best achievement, and motivation measured at the start of a teaching cycle predicted end-of-cycle outcomes, teachers should act early in an instructional cycle to support self-determined forms of motivation (e.g., supporting autonomy and intrinsic interest rather than controlled forms). The article reports that "Path analysis showed that the self-determined profile was related to the best level of achievement," with motivation assessed "at the beginning of a 10-week gymnastics teaching cycle."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Motivation must be assessed early enough in the cycle to identify students not in the self-determined profile before performance outcomes accrue.

Constraints¶

  • Evidence is limited to 10-week gymnastics cycles in high school physical education; the article's available text does not print specific intervention techniques.

Target Learners¶

  • high school physical education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving performance, effort, and grades

Affordances¶

  • Sdt Motivational Profiles Framework

Claims¶

  • The self-determined motivational profile is related to the best level of achievement in physical education [+W]
  • In Study 1 (N=215), motivation measured at the start of a 10-week gymnastics cycle predicted performance assessed at its end [+W]
  • In Study 2 (N=210), the profile-achievement link held while controlling initial performance and using effort and grade as outcomes [+W]
  • Students' motivation in physical education combines into three distinct profiles: self-determined, non self-determined, and moderate levels of both types [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Teachers should consciously invest in involvement behaviors—showing interest and emotional support—to foster student connectedness and self-determined motivation
