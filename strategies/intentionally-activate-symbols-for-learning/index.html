Intentionally activate symbols so they assist transformative learning¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Following Boyd and Myers's priority of identifying symbols critical to the learning undertaken, the article recommends deliberately activating symbols in embodied creative work. As printed: "A goal of Chormmunity would be to intentionally activate symbol(s), thus letting them help in our learning", while pursuing fuller conscious awareness and integration and accepting the multiply-languaged nature of our membership in life.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Participants able to work with image, symbol and metaphor in non-verbal modalities
  • A facilitative stance that accepts symbols operating pre- and suprarationally

Constraints¶

  • Playing with images does not necessarily call symbols to the explicitly cognitive realm

Target Learners¶

  • adult learners in transformative education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • making frames of reference more inclusive, discriminating, open, and reflective

Related Strategies¶

  • Addressing Biases in the Use of Language and Symbols
  • Participate in the creative reconstruction of a language that foregrounds human kinship with nature, using postmodern science fiction as a generative site
