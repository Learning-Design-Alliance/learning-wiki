# Train both teachers and students in cooperative learning before implementation, with sufficient time to master interpersonal skills and accountability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that to promote effective implementation of cooperative learning, both teachers and students in Vietnamese higher education institutions would need to undergo a training course in this kind of learning and be involved in as much relevant teaching and learning as possible. The study's own six-session training programme was judged insufficient, as some students still disliked uncooperative or irresponsible group members due to 'a lack of individual accountability and dysfunctional interpersonal and social skills.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both teachers and students undergo a training course in cooperative learning before implementation
- Students should have a longer period of time to expose their personal and social skills as well as to recognise the extent of their individual accountability in learning
#### Constraints
- The six-week training programme in this study involved only six training sessions, which may not have been sufficient for students to master skills and understand their accountability for learning in groups.

### Target Learners
- teachers and students in Vietnamese higher education institutions

### Target Learning Goals
- developing interpersonal and social skills for cooperative group work
- understanding individual accountability in group learning
