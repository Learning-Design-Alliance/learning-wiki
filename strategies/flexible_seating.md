# Flexible Seating

## Description
Flexible seating involves providing learners with a variety of seating options and allowing them to choose where they sit based on their individual needs and preferences. This approach fosters collaboration, empowers learners to think about how they work best, and creates a more engaging learning environment. Options may include couches, chairs, bunk beds, canoes, five-gallon buckets, crate seating, and pillows on the floor. By giving learners a choice in their learning space, instructors can help them become more strategic about their choices and create a space that supports their individual learning styles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Variety of seating options (couches, chairs, buckets, pillows), flexible bookshelves, large round or rectangular tables, willingness to throw out traditional schooling equipment
#### Constraints
- May require initial investment in alternative seating options. Requires careful management to avoid distractions or disruptions. May not be suitable for all learning environments or tasks.
