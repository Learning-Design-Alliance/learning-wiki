# Pace and vary gamified activities, and offer self-directed options, to mitigate learner fatigue

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review recommends that "educators can carefully pace gamified activities and ensure they are varied to maintain student interest", for example by "incorporating a mix of game elements—such as quizzes, interactive simulations, and collaborative challenges". It also suggests providing "opportunities for self-directed learning within the gamified framework" so students can take breaks and choose activities aligned with their interests and energy levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must have access to a mix of gamified activity types and the flexibility to schedule them
#### Constraints
- Learner fatigue arises when gamified elements are not managed properly, per the reviewed studies

### Target Learners
- EFL learners in gamified courses

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and motivation in language learning
