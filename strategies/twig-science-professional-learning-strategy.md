# Provide virtual and on-site professional learning on the digital platform and grade-specific curriculum training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The publisher supports implementation with Customer Success team assistance plus "various virtual and on-site professional learning for teachers and administrators." Sessions cover how to use Imagine Learning digital platforms and Twig Science curriculum training, with the curriculum training varying "by grade level and district needs." This makes professional development an implementation component rather than an optional add-on.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and administrators must have access to the Imagine Learning digital platforms being trained on.
#### Constraints
- Curriculum training content varies by grade level and district needs, so it is not a single standardized sequence.

### Target Learners
- Teachers of Grades K–8 science
- School administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective use of the Twig Science curriculum
- Operation of Imagine Learning digital platforms
