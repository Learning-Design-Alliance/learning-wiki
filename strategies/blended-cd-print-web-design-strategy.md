# Adopt a blended learning design combining interactive multimedia CD-ROM modules, printed booklets, and web-based discussion forums and activities with occasional face-to-face support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' forward-looking design recommendation from the study's findings: provide learning modules through interactive multimedia CD-ROM, support them with printed booklets, and run discussion forums and activities through the World Wide Web, with occasional face-to-face support for discussion and clarification of doubts. The article states that "adoption of a blended learning design strategy" along these lines "will be more beneficial". It is grounded in the study's achievement and learner-preference findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning modules provided through interactive multimedia CD-ROM
- Printed booklets as support
- Discussion forums and activities through the World Wide Web
- Occasional face-to-face support for discussion and clarification of doubts
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a single 60-student experiment in one introductory computing module at IGNOU

### Target Learners
- distance learners in open universities
- certificate-level higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- introductory computing content (computer networks)
