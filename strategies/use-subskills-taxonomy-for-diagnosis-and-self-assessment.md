# Use the unified subskills taxonomy to diagnose learners' strengths and weaknesses and to build self-assessment tools

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that teachers and teacher-researchers use the operationalized subskill list for diagnostic purposes, because subskills support pinpointing learner strengths and weaknesses. It also suggests designing self-assessment tests or tools based on the taxonomy, since students themselves view their academic reading as multidimensional. The strategy applies the compiled common and exclusive subskill lists directly in classroom assessment practice.
