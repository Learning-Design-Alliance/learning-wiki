# Provide a manageable, easy-to-use e-learning offer and enable prompt practical application in small groups with instructor feedback

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' design recommendation for maximizing knowledge transfer from e-learning to practical application: "it is important to provide a manageable, easy-to-use e-learning offer and to enable the prompt practical application of the knowledge in small groups with feedback from the instructor." In this study the e-learning was deliberately limited to about one hour of learning time, completion was communicated as obligatory in advance, and application followed promptly in small simulation groups. The authors also recommend checking that all students have the technical means to use digital formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-learning platforms should provide feedback on progress to students and teachers, and technical device access must be ensured in advance
#### Constraints
- The article notes personnel and time requirements and costs of creating e-learning modules must be considered

### Target Learners
- medical students
- midwifery trainees
- young doctors in further training

### Target Learning Goals
- transfer of theoretical knowledge into practical action competence for obstetric emergencies
