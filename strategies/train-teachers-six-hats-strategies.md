# Train teachers to use six-hats strategies in their teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study recommends teacher training in six-hats strategies as a route to developing thinking and improving education. Its stated recommendation is "the need to train teachers to use the strategies of the six hats in the teaching of their importance in the development of thinking and improve the education process". This follows from the study's finding that the six-hats program outperformed usual teaching, and from the article's argument that teaching thinking changes the teacher's role from transmitter of knowledge to designer and coordinator of learning activities.
