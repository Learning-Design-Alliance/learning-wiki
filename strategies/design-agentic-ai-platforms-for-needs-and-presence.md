# Design agentic AI learning platforms to support psychological needs and social-presence affordances

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the validated model, the article states that "AI learning platform developers can gain insights from this model to design Agentic AI-enabled learning platforms" and that instructors can draw insights for introducing effective agentic AI interventions. The strategy is to build in supports for autonomy, competence, and relatedness (e.g., self-paced learning, progress monitoring, step-by-step explanations, empathetic feedback) and for interactivity, information sharing, and collaborative environment, since these were the antecedents of motivation and social presence in the model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Platform features must actually be experienced by learners as supportive of autonomy, competence, relatedness, interactivity, information sharing, and collaboration
#### Constraints
- Recommendations rest on perception-based cross-sectional data; the article notes conclusions refer to learners' perceptions, not objective academic performance measures

### Target Learners
- higher education students using AI-supported online learning

### Target Learning Goals
- learning motivation
- social presence
- learner engagement
