Integrate discussion-based methods into a mandatory basal curriculum when it cannot be changed¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

For teachers constrained to a basal reading program, the article recommends implementing Book Club, Literature Circles, or Instructional Conversations as part of the existing curriculum rather than replacing it wholesale. The author states "if teachers cannot change the mandatory basal curriculum, they can implement Book Club, Literature Circles, or Instructional Conversations as part of their curriculum to help enhance EFL students’ reading comprehension." The article further advises that "at least some of the instructional time should be spent on discussion; a teacher’s lecturing can alternate with peer-led discussions."
