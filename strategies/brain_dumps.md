# Brain Dumps

## Description
Brain dumps involve learners writing down everything they can recall about a specific topic within a set time. This strategy tests knowledge, generates ideas, and creates longer-term retention. Collaboration can be added by having learners compare notes to identify gaps and misconceptions, and research the answers together.
