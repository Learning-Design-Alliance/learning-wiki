# Choose the unit of analysis for learning research empirically by examining stabilities and dynamics in data

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends treating the choice between individual and group/unit-of-analysis perspectives as an empirical question rather than 'a matter of a priori theoretical commitments.' Researchers should examine behavioral and discourse data for clustering, persistence, resistance to perturbation, and transitions, and attribute cognition 'at the grain size(s) at which there are observable stabilities and coherent dynamics in cognitive activity.' The authors hope this 'will enable greater communication and collaboration between cognitivists and those who model cognition as situated and/or distributed.'
