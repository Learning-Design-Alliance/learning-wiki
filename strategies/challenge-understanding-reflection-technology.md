# Three-phase challenge-understanding-reflection technology for forming critical thinking

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article presents an algorithm for forming critical thinking among students based on the educational development technology of critical thinking developed by American educators J. Steele, K. Meredith and Ch. Temple. Step 1 is a challenge phase challenging existing knowledge and activating motivation; Step 2 is understanding while working with new information; Step 3 is reflection, the creative processing and analysis of the studied information.
