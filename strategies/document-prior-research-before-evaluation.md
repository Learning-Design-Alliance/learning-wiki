# Document prior research on a solution, starting from community-needs assessment efforts, before planning future evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that organizations systematically document previous research on their solution to inform future evaluations. The recommended sequence starts with a question about efforts that organizations have made to understand the needs of the priority community they intend to serve through their solution, and then proceeds to populating the Prior Research Table with that documentation.
