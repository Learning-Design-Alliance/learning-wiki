# Include regular refresher or booster courses to maintain and advance resuscitation and teamwork competencies

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because the study observed that resuscitation skills and patient safety attitudes improve significantly after initial training but decline over time, the authors recommend regular refresher courses. The article states that "the observed decline in skills over time supports the inclusion of regular refresher courses to maintain and advance these vital competencies", and that enhancing teamwork behaviors should be integrated into booster training rather than focusing only on individual resuscitation skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Booster content covering resuscitation skills, leadership, teamwork skills and patient safety attitude
#### Constraints
- No definitive evidence exists on the most effective timing or frequency of booster courses; the article calls for future research on optimal timing, frequency and cost-effectiveness

### Target Learners
- junior resident physicians
- healthcare providers

### Target Learning Goals
- retention of resuscitation skills
- teamwork behavior maintenance
