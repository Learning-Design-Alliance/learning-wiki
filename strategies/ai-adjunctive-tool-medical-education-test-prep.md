# Use AI language models as adjunctive tools in medical education, especially for test preparation or knowledge reinforcement

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that AI can and should assist in medical education, especially for test preparation or knowledge reinforcement. The authors state that "large language models may have a valuable role in medical education and examination preparation" while emphasizing that AI "remains a tool - not a replacement." In orthopedics, where physical skill and human judgment are central, the role of AI must be complementary, not competitive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI should be deployed in knowledge-retrieval and test-preparation contexts rather than in clinical decision-making or procedural care
#### Constraints
- Responsibility for diagnosis, treatment decisions, and procedural care must remain with trained physicians

### Target Learners
- medical students
- orthopedic residents preparing for specialist certification

### Target Learning Goals
- knowledge reinforcement
- examination preparation
