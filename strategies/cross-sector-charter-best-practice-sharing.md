# Facilitate cross-sector collaboration so charter and non-charter high schools share best practices on their respective strengths

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that Chicago's charter and non-charter high schools collaborate around best practices given their complementary strengths. It suggests non-charter schools that improved course performance and Freshman OnTrack rates may have insights on "strong academic behaviors and mindsets, such as grit and study habits," while charters with strong test-score growth and college enrollment records may share college-access strategies. Sharing practices across both sectors is offered as one way to strengthen school options and student outcomes citywide.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Willingness of both charter and non-charter schools to engage in collaboration around identified best practices
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on observed sector-level differences in outcomes, not on evaluated collaborations

### Target Learners
- High school students in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic behaviors such as attendance, study habits, and grit
- College readiness and college enrollment
