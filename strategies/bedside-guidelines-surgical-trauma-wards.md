# Provide standardized evidence-based bedside wound-care guidelines in surgical and trauma wards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals "Provide accessible evidence‐based protocols in surgical and trauma wards to assist early‐career staff." This targets the predominantly young, early-career workforce (77.0% aged 21–30, 55.6% with 1–5 years experience) managing high volumes of surgical and traumatic wounds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessible evidence-based protocols available at the point of care
#### Constraints
- Developed for surgical and trauma ward contexts in a tertiary referral setting

### Target Learners
- Early-career nurses and midwives in surgical and trauma wards

### Target Learning Goals
- Evidence-based wound-care protocol adherence at the bedside
