# Supporting Self-Regulation

## Description
Identifying and supporting students who have learning strengths or challenges related to self-regulation. Self-regulation involves the ability to alter and regulate emotional and behavioral responses. It addresses how to leverage student strengths to support learning, rather than focusing solely on deficits. Strategies include scaffolding, journaling, and mindfulness techniques to help students manage their responses in emotionally safe contexts and build metacognitive awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification of student strengths and challenges, implementation of strategies like scaffolding, journaling, and mindfulness techniques, and assessment of student reflection.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful observation and understanding of individual student needs. Strategies may not be effective for all students. Requires time and effort to implement and monitor effectively.
