# Pursue active curricular innovation and transformation to maintain and improve medical postgraduates' mental health and academic performance

## Description
The authors state: "We need more active curricular innovation and transformation to maintain and improve medical postgraduates' mental health and academic performance." They argue the COVID-19 pandemic may represent an enduring transformation in medical education, with uncertainty about how long the situation will persist and possible periods of recurrence. The strategy is to adapt curricula proactively rather than treat pandemic-era arrangements as temporary, given that offline courses shifted online with reduced practical operations and less teacher-student interaction.
