Nine-step Learning Together implementation procedure for a course unit¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a concrete implementation recipe used in the treatment group: "the lecturer applied the following eight steps (1) the lecturer organized the learning materials and identified the objectives of the subject matter, (2) the lecturer introduced the structure of the lesson, and raised the outcomes expected, (3) the lecturer formed groups" and subsequent steps through students studying materials, helping each other learn, presenting their understanding of the entire unit, and the lecturer assessing understanding through whole-class presentations. The process was repeated once for each of the eight units.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Both lecturers and students would need to undergo a training course in this kind of learning, per the article's conclusion.
  • Groups must be formed and students moved to assigned groups before materials are delivered.

Constraints¶

  • The procedure was implemented in 100-minute weekly sessions over eight weeks with the same lecturer teaching both groups on different weekdays.

Target Learners¶

  • first-year tertiary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • psychology subject knowledge
  • achievement and knowledge retention

Affordances¶

  • Five Basic Elements Cooperative Learning

Claims¶

  • Students taught with the Learning Together technique retained significantly more psychology knowledge on a delayed test than lecture-taught students [+M]
  • Students taught with the Learning Together technique achieved significantly higher psychology posttest scores than students taught by lecture-based teaching [+M]
  • The two groups did not differ significantly in age or psychology pretest scores before the treatment [+M]
  • Evidence on cooperative learning in Asian contexts is mixed relative to Western findings [~W]
