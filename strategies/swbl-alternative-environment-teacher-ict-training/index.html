Use SWBL as an alternative learning environment to equip pre-service teachers with technology-application knowledge and skills¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends SWBL as an alternative to traditional teaching for pre-service teachers' ICT preparation, arguing that teachers need knowledge and skills before applying technology in classrooms. It states SWBL "can be used as an alternative learning environment to TTs for pre-service teachers to be equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills to apply technology in their classrooms effectively." The recommendation rests on the achievement and satisfaction findings of this study.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • teachers and candidate teachers trained to use such technologies effectively, as the article states is important for the FATIH project

Constraints¶

  • the article calls for similar studies at different school types, classes, education levels, periods and with more students before generalization

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • ICT course knowledge and skills for future classroom technology integration

Claims¶

  • Pre-service teachers who learned via SWBL reported high satisfaction with the environment [+W]
  • Pre-service teachers who learned ICT spreadsheets via a Semantic Web based learning environment achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those receiving traditional teaching [+M]
  • Pre-service teachers liked SWBL's facilitating learning, anywhere/anytime access, just-in-time learning, ease of use, meaningful learning and reliability, but disliked reduced classroom communication and Internet dependence [+W]
