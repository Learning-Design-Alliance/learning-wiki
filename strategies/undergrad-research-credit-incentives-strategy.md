# Institutionalize undergraduate research with credit, faculty load credit and summer funding as central to student satisfaction and recruitment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The department made research a structured program: supervision of undergraduate research counted as one course for faculty, students could take research for credit, a research experience was required in each emphasis program, and summer funding let students work on research. The authors list benefits including student satisfaction, department atmosphere, fliers, brochures and resumes, and recommend students work 'for two semesters and one summer'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty incentive of course credit for supervising research
- Research-for-credit course option for students
- Summer funding for student research
#### Constraints
- Before the intervention, research was almost non-existent in the department; the authors note much 'busy work' is involved for faculty

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics majors

### Target Learning Goals
- Research skills and engagement in physics
