# Report and control visual sophistication and targeted discrete emotions in emotional design research

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that researchers improve operationalisation and reporting of emotional design manipulations. It states "future studies should explicitly report and, where possible, control the level of visual sophistication when investigating emotional design effects", since variation in sophistication may confound cross-study comparisons. It further recommends that "Future research should also explicitly report which discrete emotions are targeted by the design manipulations and clarify their theoretical relevance" to enhance interpretability and comparability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must include visual samples of manipulated materials and describe colour, shape, anthropomorphism and sophistication parameters explicitly.
#### Constraints
- The review notes most prior studies did not specify which discrete emotions were targeted, so findings could not be categorized by specific emotional outcomes.

### Target Learners
- researchers and designers of instructional multimedia

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent operationalisation of emotional design to support learning outcomes
