# Run a controlled comparison of traditional cybersecurity instruction versus ChatGPT-augmented exploration, assessed with the Cybersecurity Concept Inventory

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose an experiment using "the cybersecurity concept inventory for a control class" taught various cyber concepts traditionally, compared with a group taught similar ideas but also prompted to use ChatGPT to explore them more deeply, with depth facilitated by assignments asking for more detailed challenges. This is a forward-looking proposal; the article reports no participant data from such a comparison.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A usable concept inventory (the article notes the Cybersecurity Concept Inventory is currently being validated) and assignments designed for more detailed challenges in the cybersecurity space.
#### Constraints
- Proposed only — no such study or participant data is reported in the article.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate cybersecurity students in a control group and a ChatGPT-augmented group

### Target Learning Goals
- cybersecurity concepts measured by the Cybersecurity Concept Inventory, explored at greater depth with ChatGPT
