# Require only a small number of supervised practice readings (7-17) for tuberculin skin test reading competency when training is properly structured

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's findings directly informed a change in Brazil's national training requirement: the former minimum of 80 readings was replaced by 7 to 17 readings depending on the number of discrepancies. The authors conclude that "a competência técnica para leitura da prova tuberculínica pode ser alcançada com o número reduzido de práticas supervisionadas, a partir da capacitação adequadamente estruturada." International guidelines similarly recognize competence with few supervised practices under standardized protocols and qualified supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training must be properly structured and conducted in controlled environments following standardized protocols with qualified supervision
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single operational observational study; the article calls for longitudinal studies tracking professionals over time to further corroborate the findings

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals (nurses and nursing technicians/assistants) being trained in tuberculin skin test reading

### Target Learning Goals
- Technical proficiency in measuring induration and classifying tuberculin skin test results within 2 mm of an expert reference
