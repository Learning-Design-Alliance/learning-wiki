# Design professional development resources mapped to practitioner motivations rather than career stages

## Description
The article recommends that departments, organizations and institutions draw on the three personas to design supports for physicists' public engagement, because needs mapped better to motivations than to career stages. Resources include searchable activity lists, science communication training, community-building opportunities, networking avenues, discussion spaces to elevate informal physics, and advocacy for funding. The personas informed the first JNIPER initiatives launched in Fall 2022.
