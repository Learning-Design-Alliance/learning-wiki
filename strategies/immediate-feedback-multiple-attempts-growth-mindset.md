# Immediate computer-graded feedback with multiple attempts at full credit to promote growth mindset

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
On the edX platform, students answer numerical and symbolic questions that are computer-graded, 'allows students to get immediate feedback, correct incorrect answers and try again (each question has multiple attempts allowed)'. Essays are self-graded against an instructor rubric and 'solutions are also instantly available', which the authors believe 'will help them learn the material more effectively' and 'helps students develop a growth mindset with respect to this material'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A platform supporting computer-graded numerical and symbolic input with multiple attempts, plus instructor-provided rubrics for essay self-grading
#### Constraints
- Implemented on the edX platform; the flipped and online formats differ only in instructor availability

### Target Learners
- massive open online course learners
- flipped-classroom students

### Target Learning Goals
- master quantum mechanics problem-solving
- develop a growth mindset toward quantum material
