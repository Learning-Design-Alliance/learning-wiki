Realign: deploy Socratic withholding protocols that delay direct answers for conceptual or diagnostic queries¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The second framework component has institutions deploy wrapper applications governed by system prompts that implement a withholding protocol. Purely factual queries may still receive direct answers, but conceptual or diagnostic queries trigger Socratic Mode: 'offering hints in decreasing order of abstraction rather than immediate answers', typically by first requesting the learner's own explanation, then providing a hint, and only later revealing a fuller answer if needed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Wrapper applications driven by speciﬁc system prompts that govern the tool's behavior and implement a withholding protocol that delays direct answer delivery for conceptual or diagnostic tasks.

Constraints¶

  • Major AI providers may not restrict their general models, so institutions deploy wrappers rather than altering the underlying models.

Target Learners¶

  • students in institutions deploying educational AI wrappers
  • novice learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • guided discovery
  • schema construction through hints rather than answer delivery
