# Deliver Six Hats training through repeated subject-embedded sessions with hat-specific reading responses

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training program embedded the Six Hats Model directly in subject content: students read curriculum units and then "expressed what he or she had learned while reading, depending on the color of that specified hat and its specific meaning at that stage." The program ran 29 sessions over six weeks with a total of 5.25 hours per week, and students were first trained by the researchers on the rules of applying the model and the meaning of each hat.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior researcher-led training on the rules of the Six Hats Model and the meaning of each hat
- Curriculum units suitable for hat-based responses, chosen to be free of linguistic bias
#### Constraints
- Developed and validated for gifted seventh-grade Arabic language students in Jordan

### Target Learners
- gifted seventh-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- creative thinking
- academic achievement in the Arabic language
