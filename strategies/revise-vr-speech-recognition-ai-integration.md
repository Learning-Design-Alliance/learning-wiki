# Revise virtual patient speech recognition via extended AI integration before further comparative evaluations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends fixing the speech recognition layer of virtual patient systems as the highest-priority design action: the most significant limitation was "the insufficient speech recognition preventing the virtual patient from receiving a fair comparison to the standardized patient", and the authors state that "a revision of the speech recognition is, therefore, urgently needed, which could be achieved through the extended integration of AI". Log data show most errors were system errors, especially short acknowledgments misread as questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The recognition layer must distinguish acknowledgments such as ehm or ok from new questions and handle free-form question wording
#### Constraints
- It is not possible to determine exactly to what extent the speech recognition problem influenced the overall evaluation of the virtual patient

### Target Learners
- medical students in psychiatric education

### Target Learning Goals
- natural conversational flow in simulated patient interviews
