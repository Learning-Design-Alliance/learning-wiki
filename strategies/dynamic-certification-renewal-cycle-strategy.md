# Sustain certification through dynamic renewal, multi-channel feedback, and a training–certification–motivation–development cycle

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Looking ahead, the article recommends "implementing regular qualification review and renewal systems, integrating multiple feedback channels such as student evaluations, and forming a virtuous cycle of “training –- certification –- motivation –- development”". Department managers likewise suggested that "Assessment standards and methods should be regularly updated alongside advancements in clinical technology and educational theory," and participants suggested incorporating student satisfaction scores as an evaluation criterion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to periodic recertification and feedback integration so standards track evolving clinical technology and educational theory.
#### Constraints
- The article states the current hospital-based, institution-specific design may limit cross-institutional standardization and that broader adoption requires contextual adaptation and multi-center validation.

### Target Learners
- Certified clinical nursing preceptors and hospital nursing education administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Continuous maintenance and advancement of clinical teaching competency and teaching quality

## Claims

- [94.5% of surveyed nursing interns were relatively satisfied with teaching quality and 95.5% with their preceptors after certification implementation](../claims/intern-satisfaction-94-5-teaching-quality.md) [+W]
- [Certified preceptors' open-ended feedback most frequently cited standardized teaching processes (49.0%), knowledge consolidation (46.2%), and professional recognition (37.2%), with 62.8% calling for stronger pre-assessment training](../claims/open-ended-feedback-standardization-and-training-needs.md) [+W]
- [Certified preceptors reported high satisfaction with the certification system, with every questionnaire item scoring above 4.7 on a 5-point scale (Cronbach's α = 0.936)](../claims/preceptor-certification-satisfaction-above-4-7.md) [+W]
- [The teaching ward rounds component had a lower pass rate (83.9%) than the theoretical teaching component (93.3%), indicating interactive teaching skills as a weakness among preceptors](../claims/ward-rounds-pass-rate-lower-than-theoretical.md) [+W]
