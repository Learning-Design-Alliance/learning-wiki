# Timely Peer Review

## Description
This strategy involves conducting peer reviews at multiple points in the writing process, including before the assignment is due, on the day it is due, and after it has been graded. This tells learners that the writing process is ongoing and that feedback can be used to drive improvement and growth, and supports a cycle of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured time for peer review at multiple points in the writing process, a clear understanding of the assignment goals, and a supportive classroom environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful planning and scheduling to ensure that peer reviews are conducted at appropriate times. May be challenging to implement in classrooms with limited time.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Middle school, high school, and higher education

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Reinforce the iterative nature of the writing process, promote continuous improvement, and enhance learners' ability to use feedback to drive growth

### Affordances
- Reinforces the idea that writing is a process and that feedback is valuable at all stages. Encourages learners to see peer and teacher feedback as a tool for continuous improvement.

### Personalization
- The timing and frequency of peer reviews can be adjusted based on the learners' needs and the nature of the assignment. Different partners can be used for each round of review.

## Assessment Evidence
- Tracking of revisions made by learners based on peer feedback. Comparison of drafts before and after peer review to assess the impact of feedback on the quality of writing.

## Impact
- Adding subsequent peer reviews to revisit the same assignment with the same or different partners the day the assignment is due and again after it’s been graded encourages the writing process is never over, and improving products that have been graded is an opportunity for students to see peer and teacher feedback can be used to drive their improvement and growth.
