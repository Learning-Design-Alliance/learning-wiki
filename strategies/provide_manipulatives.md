# Provide Manipulatives

## Description
Learners are provided with manipulatives to help make complex concepts concrete. These tangible aids enable learners to physically interact with the material, making abstract ideas more accessible and understandable. Manipulatives can be especially helpful in math and science, where visualization and hands-on exploration are crucial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate manipulatives, clear instructions on how to use them, and a supportive learning environment.
#### Constraints
- Requires preparation time for gathering and distributing manipulatives. Some learners may become distracted by the manipulatives if not used effectively.

- Learners who struggle with abstract concepts, tactile learners, and students who benefit from hands-on activities.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Provides tactile experiences, enhances visualization, improves problem-solving skills, and makes learning more engaging.

### Personalization
- Use a variety of manipulatives to suit different learning styles and content areas. Offer options for using manipulatives individually or in small groups.
