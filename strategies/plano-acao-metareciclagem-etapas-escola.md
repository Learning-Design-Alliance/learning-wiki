# Structured metarecycling action plan: introductory sustainability session, two-week disassembly practice, and four-week device construction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article details an implementable action plan for bringing metarecycling into physics teaching. Students first attend an introductory session on sustainability concepts using audiovisual materials. Then "realizou-se prática de 2 semanas, com encontros de 2 horas cada, em que dispositivos eletrônicos descartados, como computadores e celulares, foram desmontados para identificar e separar componentes reutilizáveis". A subsequent four-week phase with weekly three-hour sessions has students design and build chargers or small energy sources from recovered materials using basic electronics tools. Activities ran mainly in the counter-shift of classes and in the school's Novo Ensino Médio curriculum disciplines.
