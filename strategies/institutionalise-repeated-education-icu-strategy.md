# Institutionalise brief repeated education as annual training or competency-based assessment in high-risk units

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "Nurse educators, infection control teams and hospital administrators should consider institutionalising such programmes as part of annual training requirements or competency-based assessments, particularly in high-risk units such as ICUs". It frames repeated education as "a cost-effective strategy for meeting regulatory expectations and ensuring clinical competence among nursing staff". The recommendation rests on the study's sustained knowledge and performance gains over 3 months.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Brief, structured sessions embedded within clinical workflows to fit time and staffing constraints
#### Constraints
- Supported by a one-group pretest–posttest study without a control group; the authors call for controlled or randomised designs to confirm causal impact

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses
- nursing staff in high-risk clinical units

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained infection control competency
- protocol compliance in MDRO prevention
