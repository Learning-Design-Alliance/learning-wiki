# Develop materials that explicitly distinguish expansion-contraction from flexibility and mass change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that future studies develop materials emphasising the distinction between flexibility/mass change and expansion/contraction, since these were the most persistent alternative concepts. It states: "For future studies, development of materials which emphasise the distinction between the concepts of flexibility and mass change and the concepts of expansion and contraction are suggested."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials should address the specific alternative concepts identified in this study.
#### Constraints
- Based on post-test findings from a single quasi-experimental study with 56 students.

### Target Learners
- 5th grade elementary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Distinguishing expansion-contraction from flexibility and mass change
