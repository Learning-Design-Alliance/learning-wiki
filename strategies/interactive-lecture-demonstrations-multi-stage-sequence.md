# Use interactive lecture demonstrations: a multi-stage sequence from personal experience through model comparison to metacognitive reflection

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
ILDs, developed by Sokoloff and Thornton and extended at the University of Maryland PERG, require students to relate concepts to existing mental models and carry out demonstrations likely to challenge those models. Students record prior experience, describe competing models, use experiments to evaluate them, resolve differences, and reflect metacognitively. The article states this systematic process is universally applicable across disciplines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully constructed worksheets focused on a single conceptual topic; a demonstration or experiment that can challenge students' initial models
#### Constraints
- The economics inflation ILD risks leaving students believing only steepness of a graph matters for measuring rates of change; a follow-up exercise addressing percentage change relative to the starting base may be needed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory physics or economics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- confronting misconceptions, building metacognitive skills, connecting concepts to prior experience
