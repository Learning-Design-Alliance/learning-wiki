# Develop national cross-profession guidelines and quality standards for professional training and clinical management of children with selective mutism

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends developing national cross-profession guidelines and quality standards to promote consistent, accountable and equitable delivery of evidence-based SM interventions across the UK. It states: "To promote consistent, evidence‐based care, we recommend development of national cross‐profession guidelines and quality standards for professional training and clinical management of children with SM." This responds to respondents' reports of inconsistent local provision and absent care pathways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guidelines must span professions, since no single profession leads SM provision and no NICE guidelines for SM exist
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on an exploratory survey with a non-representative sample

### Target Learners
- NHS, Local Authority and Health and Social Care therapists supporting children with SM

### Target Learning Goals
- consistent evidence-based clinical management of selective mutism
- clear professional accountability and care pathways

## Claims
