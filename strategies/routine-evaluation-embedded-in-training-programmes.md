# Embed routine evaluation into all training and education programmes to determine effectiveness.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review notes that very few studies included an evaluation component and recommends that routine evaluation needs to be embedded into all training and education programmes. This allows quality assessment and improvement of interventions specific to enhancing the knowledge and skill of SBAs.
