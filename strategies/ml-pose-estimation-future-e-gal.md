# Enhance gesture-aided e-learning platforms with automated pose estimation, real-time feedback, and wearable sensors

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's future-work recommendations for gesture-aided e-learning platforms: incorporate machine learning models for pose estimation and gesture quality assessment to support or replace the instructor's subjective rating or provide real-time feedback to the student; use wearable motion sensors instead of webcams for more learner mobility; and add participant-requested features such as avatar customization, seeing oneself during the lesson, and immediate feedback. The article states: "Future platforms could incorporate machine learning models for pose estimation (e.g., [41,42]) and/or quality assessment [43] of the performed gesture."

### Target Learning Goals
