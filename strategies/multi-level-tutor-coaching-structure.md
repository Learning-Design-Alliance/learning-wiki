# Multi-level coaching structure for tutors: internal school coach plus program Coaching Specialist

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Math Corps sustains tutoring fidelity through two coaching layers: a school-appointed Internal Coach provides "monthly coaching and feedback that focuses on ensuring high fidelity to the program" plus on-demand scheduling support, while an assigned Coaching Specialist provides additional regular coaching to both the tutor and the school staff member and supports data-driven decisions. Initial training occurs before each school year, with mid-year starts permitted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An Internal Coach providing 6-9 hours of coaching per month per tutor
- A Program Manager and a Coaching Specialist hired by Ampact
