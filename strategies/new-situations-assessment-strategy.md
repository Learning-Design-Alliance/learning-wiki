# Assess with new situations and start from real-life problems rather than repeating lecture tasks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In both Science Foundation courses the author's team started instruction with real-life problems rather than abstract concepts, and in exams "students are not asked to repeat tasks from lectures but given new situations that they need to have mastered the taught materials to solve". This aligns assessment with the goal of transferable process skills rather than memorization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course materials must start from real-life problems, not abstract concepts
- Exam items must be novel situations solvable with the taught material
