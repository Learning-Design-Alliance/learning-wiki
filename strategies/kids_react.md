# Kids React

## Description
Perform an online image search for the words “school lunch,” and project the results or create handouts with the images from the search results. Have students focus their attention on one image at a time. Ask them to make a face or display thumbs up, down or in the middle to convey their emotional reaction to the image. Chart their reactions. As a class, discuss the assumptions they might have been making when reacting to these images. Have kids write in their notebooks or journals about some of the assumptions a person might find themselves making based on a simple online image search.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online images, chart, and notebooks or journals.
#### Constraints
- Requires careful facilitation. Some students may need additional support to identify their assumptions and biases.
