# Support faculty e-learning adoption through institutional support, faculty development, ownership policies, and a learning-outcome-mapped shared repository

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends multi-level institutional action to remove barriers to faculty use and sharing of e-learning resources: providing institutional support and faculty development programs, adopting "institutional policies to support medical faculties to resolve such issues" of ownership and intellectual property, and building "a more comprehensive, better-organized resource repository" through collaboration among medical schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration among medical schools to overcome resource constraints
- Faculty development programs on e-learning, including training for constructing multimedia assessment items
#### Constraints
- The authors note challenges and needs on e-learning resources may differ across countries, so some findings may not generalize beyond the Korean context

### Target Learners
- Medical faculty members in undergraduate medical education

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective use and sharing of e-learning resources in medical teaching
