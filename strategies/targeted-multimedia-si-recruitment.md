# Advertise SI with short, targeted multi-media messages based on student input to increase attendance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To raise session attendance, the authors advertised SI "with a series of short, targeted messages delivered to students via multiple media", including hallway posters with student testimonials and subtitled interview videos on hallway and coffee-machine televisions. Message content was developed in brainstorm sessions with students to verify the right tone of voice, following the findings and advice of Allen et al. (2019).
