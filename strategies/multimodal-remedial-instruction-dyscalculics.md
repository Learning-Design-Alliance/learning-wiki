# Deliver remedial instruction through blackboard work, group work, visual explanations and hands-on experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends differentiated remedial instruction for dyscalculic learners: "Remedial instruction for this study included different features such as blackboard work, group work and activities through visual explanations and hands-on experiences." The treatment lasted one week (7 days x 45 minutes) and was followed by significant score gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured, well-organized remedial programme with individual attention and immediate corrective feedback
#### Constraints
- Evidence base is a single-group study without a control condition

### Target Learners
- dyscalculic primary-level students

### Target Learning Goals
- arithmetic computation and broader mathematical ability
