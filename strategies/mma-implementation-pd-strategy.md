# District implementation and professional development support for My Math Academy

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Age of Learning offers a structured implementation plan (discovery meeting, comprehensive plan, integration and launch meetings, regular communication cadence, and impact reports) plus professional development including "initial setup and a turnkey and effective implementation" training, ongoing coaching on using student data for instructional decisions, and open office hours with a Customer Success team.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- No extra staffing is required from the school or district side; partnership with Age of Learning is optional for implementation planning
#### Constraints
- Training plans are customized to the district timeline set forth by the District

### Target Learners
- Early elementary students and their teachers and administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective implementation of adaptive math learning and data-informed instruction
