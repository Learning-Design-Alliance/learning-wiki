# Discussion Web

## Description
A discussion web is a graphic organizer that enables students to examine both sides of an issue before agreeing on a conclusion, promoting deeper processing of texts through meaningful conversational discussion. This strategy involves students thinking of individual ideas based on their knowledge of the text, then working as a pair to record, discuss, and resolve their perspectives before meeting with another pair of partners to share these ideas. The foursome then nominates a speaker to present this information to the entire class. The discussion web promotes active engagement, allowing students to examine various points of view within a social setting, encouraging argumentation of an open-ended question, and developing critical thinking abilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to texts, a discussion web graphic organizer (paper or digital), structured activity guidelines, and facilitation to introduce the approach and guide initial discussions.
#### Constraints
- Requires explicit instruction and modeling. May be time-consuming. May not be suitable for students who struggle with collaboration or have difficulty expressing their ideas verbally.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Suitable for students from elementary to adult learners, adaptable for a variety of grade levels and content areas, such as mathematics, science, social studies, and literature.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Enhance critical thinking, promote collaborative discussions of texts, allow for examination of various points of view within a social setting, encourage argumentation of an open-ended question, and develop listening, talking, reading, writing, viewing, and visually representing abilities.

### Affordances
- Stimulates and extends students’ thinking, builds and supports social learning communities, enables collaborative sharing of ideas, alternative perspectives, and problem solving, allows students to revisit, question, and clarify text ideas, and promotes higher cognitive abilities.

### Personalization
- Adaptable to meet the developmental level of different age groups. Can be modified for different forms of writing, such as persuasive arguments and discussion pointers. Can be used to brainstorm and prepare students for debates about a variety of topics.

## Assessment Evidence
- Monitor the quality and depth of student responses in the discussion web. Evaluate the level of critical thinking demonstrated in the responses. Assess the effectiveness of group collaboration through observation and participation.

## Impact
- Meaningful conversational discussion has enormous value to the learning process, as it stimulates and extends students’ thinking (Cramer, 2004) while building and supporting social learning communities (Fish, 1980). Discussion enables collaborative sharing of ideas, alternative perspectives, and problem solving during learning (Almasi, 1996).
