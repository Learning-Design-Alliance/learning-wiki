# Standardize a multi-parameter clinical examination protocol through hands-on training, instructional videos, and a bedside pocket card

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The REDUCTION protocol structures fracture assessment as a fixed checklist of 15 midfacial or 14 mandibular parameters (e.g., swelling, malocclusion, bony step-off, tongue blade bite test), each scored present, absent, or not testable, with fracture probability rated 0–10 before and after. Standardization was achieved by training: "Standardization was ensured through hands-on training, online instructional videos, and a bedside pocket card with the illustrated clinical parameters." This strategy suits emergency departments training varied assessor levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Physicians complete the checklist of 15 midfacial or 14 mandibular parameters before re-rating fracture probability on the 0–10 scale
#### Constraints
- Parameters unassessable due to severe swelling, hematoma, or patient consciousness are scored as not testable; interns showed a decline in assessment accuracy with the protocol

### Target Learners
- medical interns in training
- junior and senior residents
- consultants in emergency departments

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate clinical assessment of maxillofacial fractures
