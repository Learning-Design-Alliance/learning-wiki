# Boomeranging technique: argue both sides of a topic before drafting an argumentative essay

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Within the blogfolio procedure, students start with an assumed stance and generate supporting arguments, then take "an adversial stance on the same topic and argue against their very ideas", and finally juxtapose the pro and con ideas for logicality and convincingness. The stated purpose was "to tap their ways of knowing, both connected and separate". It precedes drafting, peer commenting, face-to-face debate, and revision in the weekly cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students post initial individual ideas on their blogs before peer negotiation begins
- Access to peers' blog posts for subsequent reading and commenting
#### Constraints
- Implemented as step 3 of the 13-step blogfolio procedure with EFL tertiary students; not tested as a stand-alone technique

### Target Learners
- EFL tertiary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Generating arguments and counterarguments
- Activating both connected and separate ways of knowing
- Building more logical and convincing argumentative essays
