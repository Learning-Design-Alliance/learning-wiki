# Set an instructional objective of students using the appropriate perspective (deterministic or probabilistic, localized or delocalized) at the appropriate time.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (7 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 4 studies (2 associational, 2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose an instructional objective "for students to use the appropriate perspective (deterministic or probabilistic, localized or delocalized) at the appropriate time." Students should learn when classical deterministic versus quantum probabilistic descriptions apply. The article cautions the goal "seems to require a level of metacognitive awareness that students may not have at the introductory level."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must explicitly address the distinction between classical and quantum uncertainty, since few students demonstrated that ability spontaneously.
#### Constraints
- The goal seems to require a level of metacognitive awareness that students may not have at the introductory level.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in introductory modern physics courses

### Target Learning Goals
- distinguishing classical uncertainty from uncertainty inherent to quantum systems
- selecting the appropriate perspective for a given physical context
