# Use portraiture as a contemplative classroom technique in which learners create their own stories of life events

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Portraiture is implemented as the prime contemplative technique: "the teacher uses portraiture to assist learners to create their own stories of life events." The article defines it as "the detailed description and reporting of whatever happens in our daily life," which allows monitoring of previous mistakes to function better in similar future situations. In the treatment, each session included 15 minutes of discussion using portraiture alongside reading instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who implements portraiture to assist learners in creating their own stories of life events, integrated with reading texts of general interest
#### Constraints
