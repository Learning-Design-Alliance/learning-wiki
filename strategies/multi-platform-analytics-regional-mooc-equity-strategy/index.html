Study regional MOOC providers with multi-platform analytics to address equity and access questions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend using their multi-platform learning analytics approach to study regional MOOC providers, stating the findings "suggest the potential of this methodological approach to study and learn from regional MOOC providers, particularly around the questions of equity and access in the global MOOC ecosystem." The strategy involves combining data across global and regional platforms under a common format.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A common data format and comparison script agreed across platforms

Constraints¶

  • Data exchange between platforms may be restricted by privacy and security concerns, so the procedure avoids it

Target Learners¶

  • Regional MOOC providers and researchers studying equitable access

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding equity and access in the global MOOC ecosystem

Claims¶

  • Regional provider Edraak attracts younger learners, more females, and learners with lower education levels than global MOOC providers [+W]
  • Edraak learners value local courses because the courses cater to their interests and learning needs [+W]
  • Multi-platform comparison without exchanging data addresses privacy and data security concerns [+W]
