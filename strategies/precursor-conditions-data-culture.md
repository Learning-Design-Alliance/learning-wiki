# Establish precursor conditions — school vision, collaboration, equity focus, and supportive culture — to nurture a data-use culture

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The analyzed texts frequently discussed contextual aspects of schools that must be in place to establish and nurture a culture around data, which the authors label precursor conditions. The paper states "These conditions include the need for a common school vision that describes the school's mission and operating principles, frequently invoking a perspective of enhancing education for all students." Other conditions include connection to other initiatives, authority of groups examining data, understanding of the change process, collaboration, focus on equity, cultural proficiency, school culture, and deep, sustained professional development connected to educators' work contexts.
