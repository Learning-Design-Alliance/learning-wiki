# Make audio-visual programs a regular feature of preschool activities and supply program content to teachers as guide books

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends organizing non-formal preschool activities around the themes of audio-visual programs and supporting teachers with printed materials. It states that "such programs should be a regular feature in preschools so as to help enhance the language development process of the young children", and that where sequenced material and textbooks are absent, program content can be printed with activities and supplied as guide books so teachers can prepare. Daily three-hour non-formal preschool activities can be based on the program's specific themes and recapitulated in varied ways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers prepared in advance via printed guide books of program content and activities
- A two-day orientation and training for the preschool teacher
#### Constraints
- Recommended in the context of the non-formal preschool component lacking a structured and prescribed syllabus

### Target Learners
- preschool children in non-formal preschool settings

### Target Learning Goals
- language development
- attention span
- interest in learning activities
