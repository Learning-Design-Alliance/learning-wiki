# Literature/Social Stories

## Description
Using literature or social stories to explicitly teach relationship skills involves selecting or creating narratives that model positive social interactions, communication strategies, and conflict resolution techniques. These stories provide learners with vicarious experiences, allowing them to observe and analyze characters' behaviors and outcomes. By engaging with these narratives, learners can develop empathy, perspective-taking skills, and a deeper understanding of social dynamics. Social stories, in particular, are designed to provide clear and concise information about social situations, helping learners to navigate complex social interactions and develop appropriate responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful selection or creation of literature or social stories that model positive social interactions and communication strategies. Facilitation and guidance to support learners' understanding and analysis of the stories. Opportunities for learners to discuss the stories and share their own experiences and perspectives.
#### Constraints
- The effectiveness of this strategy depends on the quality of the selected literature or social stories and the learners' willingness to engage with the narratives. There is a risk that some learners may not fully understand or internalize the lessons from the stories or that the stories may not accurately reflect their own experiences.

### Affordances
- Provides learners with vicarious experiences that model positive social interactions and communication strategies, fosters empathy and perspective-taking skills, and increases understanding of social dynamics. This can lead to improved social skills, stronger relationships, and increased participation in social activities.

### Personalization
- The selected literature or social stories can be tailored to the specific needs, interests, and cultural backgrounds of the learners, ensuring that they are relevant and meaningful. Learners can also be given opportunities to discuss the stories and share their own experiences and perspectives, fostering a deeper understanding and connection.
