# Use industry role- and task-level KSA data to build student career awareness and align QISE curricula with workforce needs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends using its role-level and task-level data in QISE education: role data can build "general awareness of career options" for students and educators, while task-level data is specific enough to inform instruction, since prior work shows educators need a more thorough understanding of industry KSAs to develop relevant course goals and learning objectives. The authors anticipate that continued data collection on more roles and tasks may reveal "emergent themes that could suggest new units, courses, or programs in QISE education."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continued collection of role- and task-level interview data, which the authors state is preliminary.
#### Constraints
- The dataset is skewed toward quantum-expert roles, and relationships between KSAs and different company types are not yet elucidated.

### Target Learners
- STEM students exploring QISE careers
- QISE educators designing courses and programs

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness of QISE career options
- alignment of course learning objectives with industry-needed quantum KSAs
