# Validate predictions within an intervention pipeline and include downstream policy recommendations

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy directs ML4Ed researchers to treat prediction as one component of an intervention pipeline and to validate downstream effects; six participants "highlight the importance of validation in the translation from prediction to interventions". It includes adding policy recommendations and causal testing of interventions, a practice the authors note is standard in quantitative subfields of economics but rarely referenced in ML4Ed papers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An intervention must be in mind for prediction work to add value, and additional data-driven methods are required to validate the full intervention pipeline.
#### Constraints
- Many ML4Ed papers do not reference the need for causal A/B testing of downstream interventions, even when they invoke policy-driven motivations.

### Target Learners
- education researchers
- machine learning researchers
- education policymakers

### Target Learning Goals
- evaluating the efficacy of downstream interventions such as financial aid programs
- bridging the gap between prediction and intervention
