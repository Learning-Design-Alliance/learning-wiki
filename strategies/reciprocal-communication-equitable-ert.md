# Establish reciprocal communication with learners to understand their contexts and create equitable online experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends establishing reciprocal communication with learners in ERT design, which allows teachers to understand the contexts learners go through and create equitable online experiences that consider learners' particular learning needs. This helps teachers identify digital pedagogy practices that support rather than impede learning, and to determine challenges learners face such as technology access and unreliable Internet.
