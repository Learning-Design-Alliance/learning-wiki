# Three-part learning support: contextualized real-world problems, hot topics, and question-and-answer

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The FOCUS learning support strategy divides weekly one-hour sessions into three sections: connections between mathematics and the real world using technology and culturally relevant problems, mini-instruction of hot topics addressing common misconceptions via alternative approaches, and Socratic-style question-and-answer time. The article states "The learning support model was a three-part approach generalizable to any mathematics course". Time was allocated roughly 50% connections, 30% hot topics, and 20% question and answer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- Developmental mathematics students in co-requisite college coursework

### Target Learning Goals
- Connecting abstract mathematical concepts to real-world relevance
- Addressing misconceptions and critical concept attainment
