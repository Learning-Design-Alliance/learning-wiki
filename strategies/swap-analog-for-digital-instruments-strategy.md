# Replace analog instruments with digital ones in a laboratory exercise when analog-reading uncertainty is not part of the intended lesson, and teach analog-reading error in a separate exercise.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports that reading uncertainty of analog instruments was not part of the Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering curriculum, so "analog instruments were swapped with their digital counterparts". Teaching materials for a laboratory exercise on the separate Electrical Measurements course were altered to educate students on accuracy problems related to reading analog instruments, keeping the two lessons distinct.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A different course, Electrical Measurements, carries the lesson on analog instrument reading accuracy.
#### Constraints
- The swap applies where analog reading uncertainty is outside the exercise's intended lesson and curriculum.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in professional study of electrical engineering

### Target Learning Goals
- accurate power factor measurement without the confound of analog meter reading error
