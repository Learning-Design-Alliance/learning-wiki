# Adapt the 5CardsGame format to other healthcare education contexts, including digital versions for remote and self-paced practice

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend extending the game's underlying principles beyond surgical technology education: in nursing education a similar game could support patient assessment and care planning, and in residency programs it could focus on differential diagnosis and treatment planning. They also suggest the "format could also be adapted to create digital versions, allowing for remote learning and self-paced practice", and propose integrating game-based approaches into surgical technology curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous refinement and expansion of the game scenarios and card sets would be necessary to ensure comprehensive coverage
#### Constraints
- The recommendation rests on a pre-experimental study without a control group; the authors call for randomized controlled trials before full establishment of efficacy

### Target Learners
- nursing students
- medical residents
- healthcare professionals in continuing education
- surgical technologist students

### Target Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- differential diagnosis
- care planning
- updating best-practice knowledge
