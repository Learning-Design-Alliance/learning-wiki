# TCM course explicit goals for teacher learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The article enumerates the TCM course's explicit goals: "a) enhance teacher classroom management skills and to improve teacher-student relationships; b) assist teachers to develop effective group and individual behaviour plans to enable proactive rather than reactive classroom management; c) encourage teachers to adopt and promote social and emotional regulation skills; and d) encourage teachers to strengthen positive teacher-parent relationships with all parents". These goals define what teachers are expected to learn and apply in practice, and the trial's process evaluation examines how teachers translate them into classroom strategies.
