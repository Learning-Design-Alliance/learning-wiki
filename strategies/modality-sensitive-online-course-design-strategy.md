# Modality-sensitive online course design: structured templates for COC and synchronous elements embedded in asynchronous lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends designing online learning environments around each modality's strengths rather than applying uniform expectations. In practice, it states "this means providing structured design templates for COC courses and incorporating synchronous interactions into asynchronous lessons to enhance instructional coherence across modalities." It also advises stakeholders to prioritize meticulously designed courses over unprepared instructor-generated content, and to strengthen clarity and structural organization of instructional information to boost perceived usefulness and continuance intention.
