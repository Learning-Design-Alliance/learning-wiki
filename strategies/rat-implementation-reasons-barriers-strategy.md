# Use thematic analysis of implementers' reasons to anticipate facilitators and barriers when planning RaT programs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review reports a thematic analysis, attributed to Al Achkar et al. (2017), that identified "five main reasons for implementing RaT programs": teaching is part of residents' role; learners desire formal training; regulatory bodies require it; programs improve residents' education; and they prepare residents for current and future roles. It also lists five reasons for not implementing: lack of time and energy; lack of expertise and resources; newness of the program; limited access to students; and undesired instruction. Program planners can use these lists to anticipate local facilitators and barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Awareness of local resources and expertise, since the review notes instruction expertise is not equally distributed among residency programs
#### Constraints
- The review notes resources and expertise to lead instruction are not distributed equally among residency programs

### Target Learners
- medical residents
- residency program directors
