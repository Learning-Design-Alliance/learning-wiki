# Four-step REBOA training programme: theory, basic skill training, interventional radiology practice, and high-fidelity simulation with debriefing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The programme is a combined theoretical and practical training sequence divided into four parts: a didactic theoretical introduction with question-and-answer discussion; repeated basic skill training on a purpose-built mannequin; one day of supervised real patient vascular access in an interventional radiology laboratory; and high-fidelity team simulation of a refractory-VF cardiac arrest scenario followed by semistructured debriefing. The authors state it "is based on validated educational models for skill training of other procedures performed by physicians from a variety of specialties".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an interventional radiology department for supervised patient practice, with consultant approval of each operator
- A purpose-built cannulation mannequin and a simulation facility with video recording for debriefing
#### Constraints
- Practice in an interventional radiology lab can be difficult where the service is lacking or regulations are strict; the authors propose specialised structured courses as an alternative

### Target Learners
- board-certified anesthesiologists and paramedics on a physician-staffed prehospital emergency medical service

### Target Learning Goals
- safe, competent team performance of REBOA during simulated out-of-hospital cardiac arrest resuscitation
