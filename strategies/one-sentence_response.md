# One-Sentence Response

## Description
When instructors ask a question, rather than having students raise hands, every learner writes a sentence to answer the question. This ensures that all learners actively engage with the question and encourages writing about math. The sentences allow learners to articulate their ideas in a clear and succinct manner, fostering active participation and engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A specific question, time for writing, and a method for collecting responses (e.g., paper, digital platform).
#### Constraints
- May not allow for in-depth exploration of concepts, requires clear and focused questions, and may require additional discussion to address misconceptions.
