Plan multiple intelligence implementations for longer periods, since effects grow with implementation duration¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study, q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the moderator finding that effect sizes rise from 0,868 (3 weeks and less) to 1,606 (7 weeks and more), the authors recommend that designers and researchers consider duration when planning multiple intelligence implementations. The article states: "It should be considered that the effect of multiple intelligence practices on success may increase when the imple mentation period becomes longer."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementations must be based on multiple intelligence theory and measured against a control group with traditional instructional practices

Constraints¶

  • The finding comes from Turkish experimental studies with implementation periods between two and 14 weeks; the article frames the duration effect as something that may increase, not a guaranteed optimum

Target Learners¶

  • elementary students
  • secondary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement across multiple school courses

Claims¶

  • The effect of multiple intelligence practices on achievement increases as the implementation period becomes longer [+W]
