# Provide Easy-Access Educational Tools

## Description
Providing easy-access educational tools involves making a variety of resources readily available to students to support their learning. This includes manipulatives, graphic organizers, audio recordings, magnifying paper, highlighters, choice boards, learning menus, and timers. By having these tools easily accessible, students can independently manage their learning and advocate for their needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires educators to gather and organize a variety of learning tools, make them easily accessible to students, and provide instruction on how and when to use each tool.
#### Constraints
- Requires resources for acquiring and maintaining the tools. Some students may need additional support to learn how to use the tools effectively.
