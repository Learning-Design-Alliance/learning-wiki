# Teach graduate students to analyze standardized concept tests in targeted parts rather than as a whole

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In the motion and force unit, graduate students are given data tables of student responses to FMCE questions and asked to build models of student reasoning about specific content, and to decide why certain nearly identical FMCE questions are not analyzed. The article states the purpose is "to help the GSs practice and understand the ways in which standardized tests are useful when analyzed in part, rather than analyzed as a whole (such as with a normalized gain)." Results are described as encouraging, with students accurately connecting student reasoning to curricula and the research literature.
