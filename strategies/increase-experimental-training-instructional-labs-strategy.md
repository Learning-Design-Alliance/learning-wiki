# Increase experimental skill development opportunities within existing instructional lab courses to prepare students for quantum industry positions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report recommends increasing opportunities for experimental skill development within existing lab courses, noting that many of the experimental skills needed for non-PhD quantum positions overlap with general experimental skills valuable across career paths and "are already part of the learning goals of existing lab courses, which makes this recommendation achievable with minimal additional resources." This strengthens preparation for quantum industry positions while also benefiting other career paths.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing lab courses whose learning goals already include relevant experimental skills
#### Constraints
- Achievable with minimal additional resources, per the report, because skills already overlap with existing lab course learning goals

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in lab courses

### Target Learning Goals
- general and quantum-industry-relevant experimental skills
