# Implement Amira with no special staff qualifications, roughly two hours of initial teacher training plus a one-hour follow-up data deep-dive

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The summary states that "No special qualifications or certification are required to implement Amira." Teachers typically receive 2 hours of in-person or remote training covering Amira Assessment, Amira Practice, logging in, and reports, followed by a 1 hour follow-up "data deep-dive" training after teachers and students have used Amira a few times to explore using the data to guide instruction.
