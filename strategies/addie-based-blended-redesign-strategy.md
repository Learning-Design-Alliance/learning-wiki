# Use the ADDIE model to redesign an underused lecture-based course into a blended digital format meeting five quality criteria

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An implementable redesign recipe: form an interdisciplinary team of lecturers, didactics, and digital-teaching experts plus student assistants; analyze the current teaching situation and time budget; then design, develop, implement, and evaluate. The article states "The ADDIE model describes a process for course development that is divided into five steps: analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation." The redesign reduced lecture time to 135 min to create room for an interactive seminar while keeping total student time unchanged, and defined five quality criteria including flexible competence-based digital teaching, patient-oriented insights, interactive learning, doctor–student exchange, and clinical experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interdisciplinary team including didactic and digital-teaching expertise
- Critical evaluation of existing content to remove redundancies and select central content
- Approval by the study commission for hour-neutral restructuring
#### Constraints
- The authors note developing the concept required considerable effort, which they justify as an investment in the discipline's future

### Target Learners
- medical students in clinical modules

### Target Learning Goals
- flexible competence-based theoretical knowledge transfer
- active participation through case-based seminars
- patient-oriented clinical insight
