# Recruit volunteer implementers first to generate small wins, models, and critical mass for change

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To expand a solution beyond initial adopters, the article describes recruiting and implementing with volunteers first so they generate tangible benefits, provide models, and build critical mass and social pressure for change. Participants described "recruiting and implementing with volunteers ﬁrst to generate tangible beneﬁts, provide models, and build critical mass and social pressure for change", for example presenting a Socratic Circles toolkit to voluntarily interested teachers before wider rollout.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Volunteers must be willing to invest planning and preparation time and perceive likely success and greater benefits to students
#### Constraints
- The article cautions that those who volunteer first may not be the ones who would most benefit from implementing the solution

### Target Learners
- Teachers expanding a new practice across classrooms and schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining and spreading solutions such as Socratic Circles and compact writing assignments
