# Run school support campaigns using the technique compiled from many successful campaigns for increased school support

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The notice describes Alexander and Theisen's handbook for school superintendents and boards of education, which "gives the technique used in many successful school campaigns for increased support." It compiles campaign organization practices: organizing a campaign staff, identifying who is to be reached, selecting arguments and examples, and preparing and circulating material for effective publicity, with examples of good publicity material and a bibliography.
