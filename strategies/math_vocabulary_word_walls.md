# Math Vocabulary Word Walls

## Description
Create a class word wall displaying mathematical terms and vocabulary to support working memory and develop language skills. Model how to identify and highlight key mathematical words in word problems that prompt addition or subtraction. Brainstorm with students all the potential words that signal addition or subtraction, emphasizing conceptual understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Math word problems, markers or highlighters, space for a word wall in the classroom, and posters or cards for creating the word wall.
#### Constraints
- May not be effective if students lack foundational mathematical knowledge. Requires ongoing maintenance and updating of the word wall.

### Target Learning Goals

### Affordances
- Supports learners in identifying key information in word problems. Visual aids support working memory and vocabulary development. Promotes active engagement in problem-solving.

### Personalization
- Adapt the complexity of the word problems based on student level. Allow students to contribute to the word wall with terms relevant to their learning.

- Observe students' ability to identify key words in word problems. Assess students' use of math vocabulary in explanations and problem-solving.

- Improved math problem-solving: Research suggests that explicit instruction in math vocabulary enhances comprehension and problem-solving skills. Increased working memory: Visual aids like word walls support memory and understanding of mathematical concepts.
