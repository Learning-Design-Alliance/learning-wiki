# Visual Prompts for Essay Writing

## Description
Using artwork along with life lesson statements can help adolescents write essays. With an image attached, students have plenty to say about the statement. Students gaze at the photo, ask themselves what it’s about, and make up a life lesson that fits. This method involves an 11-minute essay with specific instructions for timed writing, guiding students to connect the visuals with their own experiences and beliefs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Royalty-free photos, life lesson statements, and instructions for the 11-minute essay.
#### Constraints
- May not be suitable for all learners or writing tasks, and requires thoughtful selection of visuals.
