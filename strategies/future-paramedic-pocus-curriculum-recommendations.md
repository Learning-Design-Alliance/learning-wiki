# Prioritize thoracic sonography and image optimization, add clinical attachments, and pursue certification in future paramedic POCUS curricula

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on observed weaknesses and evaluation results, the authors recommend that future training "prioritize certain sub-areas, such as thoracic sonography or image optimization", since poor image optimization was a common error and thoracic views outperformed abdominal ones. They further recommend that "Future curricula should focus on consolidating practical skills through clinical attachments in emergency or ultrasound departments, and should emphasise POCUS for prehospital use, preferably under qualified supervision", and that curricula undergo certification procedures by professional associations for quality assurance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Qualified supervision for prehospital-use training
- Institutions or committees of professional associations engaging with paramedics on implementation strategies
#### Constraints
- The article states no field trial involving POCUS use on patients was implemented, so safety, practicability, and transferability to real-world use still need evaluation

### Target Learners
- paramedics in training and continuing education

### Target Learning Goals
- practical POCUS skills, image optimization, and realistic self-assessment for prehospital use
