# Gradually increase irrelevant propositions within word-problem materials to boost solution accuracy for children with MD

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (3 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 theoretical), `q3`–`q4` · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "curriculuminstructionthatincludes solvingproblemswithgradualincreases in irrelevantpropositionsmay be an effectiveprocedure forboostingsolution accuracy in childrenwith MD." Materials progressed from four-sentence problems with one irrelevant sentence to eight-sentence problems with five irrelevant sentences across 20 lessons, directing children to discriminate relevant from irrelevant information and avoid number grabbing. The materials-only condition using this procedure was as effective as some overt strategy conditions on the experimental measure.
