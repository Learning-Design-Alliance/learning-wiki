# Use small shared-meal conversations among teachers, students, and a facilitator to surface student perspectives on assessment and strengthen relationships

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project organized teams of four — a teacher, two high school students, and a non-educator facilitator — to share a meal and discuss how a test or assessment "might be redesigned so that everyone in the class will feel successful in it." Each participant then completed a 30-minute individual interview to share perspectives. The report suggests such informal conversations may be a model of strengthening relationships and mutual respect between teachers, students, and other adults.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Grant or district funding to pay for meals and for the project team's efforts to organize the conversations and surface key findings
- Arrangement of conversations by project team members and scheduling of follow-up individual interviews
#### Constraints
- Concrete change is difficult to enact because there is not a lot of room in the curricular pacing, as teacher-coaches perceived

### Target Learners
- high school students
- middle school students

### Target Learning Goals
- healthy risk-taking and exploration in the classroom
- student-teacher relationships and mutual respect around assessment
