# CASCADE competition: motivated pupils prepare educational physics content and present it to their peers

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
CASCADE fosters the interest that Masterclasses enhance by having motivated pupils create educational content on physics topics and present it to peers, reaching a few hundred teenagers through the participants themselves. "The scheme is to have the interested and motivated pupils to prepare educational content on (particle) physics topics and present it to their peers." Before 2020 it was a competition of three-member teams giving recorded talks judged on content and presentation quality; after 2020 it became an individual video competition judged on content and attractiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pupils already interested and motivated, e.g., by prior Masterclass experience
- A jury evaluating both content and presentation or video quality
#### Constraints
- The in-person team-talk format was no longer doable after 2020 and was replaced by online videos

### Target Learners
- interested and motivated secondary pupils

### Target Learning Goals
- deepening particle physics knowledge
- science communication to peers
