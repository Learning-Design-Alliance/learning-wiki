# Redesign assessment so fluency is not enough: process logs, staged drafts, in-class composition, and oral defences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's first recommendation is to reclaim epistemic labour through assessment redesign. Because students turn to AI to meet expectations that "prioritise fluency over originality and polish over intellectual risk", the response is to design assessments for which fluency is not enough. The article specifies that "Process logs, staged drafts, in-class composition, and oral defences make the work of thinking visible, and reframe writing as a process rather than a deliverable."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessments designed so that situated judgement, ethical reasoning, or genuine voice is required, which the article says AI cannot meaningfully complete
