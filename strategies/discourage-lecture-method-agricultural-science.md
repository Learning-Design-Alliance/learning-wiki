# Discourage the continuous use of the conventional lecture method in agricultural science teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that secondary school teachers be discouraged from the continuous use of the conventional lecture method in teaching agricultural science, because the method made students perform poorly on the ASAT tasks compared with the demonstration method. This strategy targets a reduction in lecture-only instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alternative activity-oriented methods such as demonstration must be available to teachers as a replacement.
#### Constraints
- The study measured achievement on five specific agricultural science topics over two months; broader curricular effects were not tested.

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary students

### Target Learning Goals
- Agricultural science achievement
