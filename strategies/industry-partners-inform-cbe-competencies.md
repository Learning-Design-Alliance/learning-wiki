# Engage industry partners to inform curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and develop new programs in response to employer input

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief reports that "Industry partners informed curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and new programs were developed in response to employer input." This strategy makes employer input a direct input into which competencies programs teach and whether new programs are launched, aligning CBE offerings with workforce skills gaps. It is presented as a feature of how the consortium's CBE programs were built.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Industry partners willing to advise on needed competencies
- A mechanism for translating employer input into new or revised programs
