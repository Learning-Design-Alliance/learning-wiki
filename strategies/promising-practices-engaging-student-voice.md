# Engage student voice through safe sharing spaces, assumption removal, active listening, co-creation, and co-designed action plans

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief lists promising practices for engaging student voice to inform school climate: create safe and supportive spaces for sharing with consent and confidentiality, remove previous assumptions about student experiences, listen with intention using active listening skills, engage and support students as leaders and co-creators, and co-create an action plan with students in which actions are led by students and grounded in youth participatory action research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Obtain consent for recording, be transparent about use of responses, ensure confidentiality, and provide students tools, information, and skill-building to develop as leaders
