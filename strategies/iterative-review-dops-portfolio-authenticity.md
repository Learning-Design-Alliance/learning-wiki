# Use iterative draft review, real-time demonstration, DOPs and regular check-ins to keep portfolio and practical assessment robust against GenAI

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A strategy for work-based and portfolio assessment in which trainers demonstrate techniques directly, ask learners to explain concepts back, use competency tests and direct observations of practice (DOPs), review iterative drafts discussed with the learner, and hold regular check-ins. Because the learner demonstrates knowledge, understanding and laboratory skills in real time, the assessment is both authentic and robust, and it would be difficult to demonstrate expected outcomes without the expected competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The trainer must discuss each piece of evidence with the learner to ensure they can interpret data and complete case-study tasks in real time, since GPT-4o or later can read and analyse images with text
#### Constraints
- Any portfolio evidence containing plagiarism, the use of GenAI platforms (including ChatGPT) or collusion will not be accepted for the final assessment (verification)

### Target Learners
- pre-registration biomedical science trainees completing the IBMS registration training portfolio
- laboratory apprentices and trainees

### Target Learning Goals
- demonstrating HCPC Standards for Proficiency and laboratory competency authentically
