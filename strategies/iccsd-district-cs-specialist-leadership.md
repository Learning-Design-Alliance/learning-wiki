# Appoint a district-level CS/CT leadership position to center and prioritize computing as a curricular offering

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In Year 2, ICCSD appointed a district-wide CS Specialist to support teacher professional development and integration, which the district's reflection identifies as its second key decision: to center leadership and prioritize CS and CT as a curricula offering. The position supported implementation of PLTW at five schools during the 2020-21 academic year.
