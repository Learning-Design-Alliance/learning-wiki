# Teachers respond to individual differences and preferred learning styles during science lessons

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on the study's results, the authors recommend that teachers deliver science instruction that responds to students' individual differences and preferred learning styles rather than a single style. The article states: "Teachers are also recommended to focus on individual differences among students and respond to their preferred learning styles in the course of science lessons." The authors additionally recommend workshops training teachers in these methods, and advise curriculum developers and textbook authors to account for learning styles and brain-part characteristics in the materials they develop.
