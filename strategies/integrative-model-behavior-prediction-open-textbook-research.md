# Research teacher behavior toward open textbook adoption using the integrative model of behavior prediction

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The commentary proposes that future research apply the integrative model of behavior prediction (Fishbein & Ajzen) to study how teachers can be supported to use open textbooks. It states that "future research needs to probe a variety of issues relating to how teachers could be intentionally and effectively use open textbooks to empower the digital learning in the 21st century." The strategy directs inquiry toward teacher beliefs and intentions as determinants of open textbook use.
