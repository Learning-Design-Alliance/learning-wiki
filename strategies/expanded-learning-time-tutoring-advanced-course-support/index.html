Support struggling advanced-course students with expanded learning time and intensive tutoring¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To ensure advanced courses have a safe, equitable, positive learning environment, the brief recommends "Support for students who may need additional time to build skills for AP courses, such as expanded learning time programs (summer school, after-school, winter session) or targeted intensive tutoring." This addresses the beware-of that enrollment without a goal of student experience and success leaves students merely enrolled rather than successful.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programs must give students additional time to build skills for AP courses rather than only enrolling them

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students needing additional skill-building time before or during AP courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Success and belonging in advanced STEM coursework
