# Flexible extensions: edge-geometry variables and slow-motion video for advanced course levels

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The activity can be adapted to different course levels without changing its central idea. In a more advanced version, "instructors may invite students to explore additional variables such as lip sharpness or edge curvature, or to compare the observations with a simple inertial–capillary interpretation". Students may also record slow-motion smartphone videos to compare the visible stream shape near the lip and distinguish partial sticking from clean ejection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The core guided comparison of flow velocities and surface treatments can be completed in a single lab period
#### Constraints
- Slow-motion recordings are optional and not essential for the core version of the experiment

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students
- students in more advanced courses

### Target Learning Goals
- exploring edge geometry effects on flow separation
