# Nursing managers should tailor caring support to profile-specific needs while fostering conditions that sustain learning and vitality

## Description
The article recommends that nursing managers tailor support to different caring profiles while creating conditions that sustain learning and vitality. For the low profile, workplace caring warrants particular attention through a consistent preceptor or mentor and regular one-to-one communication. For the moderate profile, support should be strengthened at organisational and unit levels. For the high profile, the emphasis shifts towards maintaining the existing caring environment and promoting continued development.
