Establish AI-specific evaluation and accountability systems using independent audits, role-based dashboards, and privacy and equity safeguards¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends districts collaborate with states and partners to build evaluation and accountability systems integrating independent audits of AI tools for accuracy, bias, accessibility, and explainability, plus role-specific dashboards and embedded safeguards. It suggests "consider collaborating with independent evaluators to assess AI-enabled edtech before larger-scale adoption, require third-party validation aligned with established frameworks." Third-party validations offer a common language for developers and leaders comparing options.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Processes and timelines for periodic re-evaluations
  • District-level review processes to flag risks in the procurement and use of tools

Constraints¶

  • Building rigorous evidence about a single edtech solution requires massive resources, time, and coordination; even existing evidence can be difficult for districts to access, interpret, and apply

Target Learners¶

  • PK16 students in districts adopting AI-enabled edtech

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Ensuring AI-enabled tools are trustworthy, safe, effective, and supportive of positive student learning outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Establish AI-specific accountability systems with iterative playbooks, family guides, and continuous evaluation cycles
  • Concentrate AI-edtech adoption priorities through collaborative networks that share tools, policies, and evaluation practices
  • Build state AI evaluation on the ESSA evidence framework and integrate results into the existing education evidence base
  • Design AI literacy tools with transparency in development and establish district policies for vetting AI tools
  • Adopt a systems approach to AI integration rather than spotlights or gaps
  • Use shared frameworks, cross-district collaboration, and state-level policy alignment to scale AI-enabled edtech
  • Audit AI vocal-assessment systems for bias, transparency, privacy, and recording-context robustness before evaluative use
