# Use LLM-generated lessons within a human-AI collaborative workflow where educators review and refine AI drafts

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A workflow in which AI-generated lesson content serves as a foundational draft that educators and instructional designers iteratively review and refine, with AI handling repetitive, time-intensive drafting tasks while human expertise ensures pedagogical alignment. The article states that LLM-generated lessons' "effectiveness is maximized within a human-AI collaborative workflow" and that educators "play a crucial role in reviewing and refining AI-generated content." Designers reported the AI drafts "can be used after a few rounds of modification."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators or instructional designers review and refine the AI-generated draft content before publication
- Human verification of academic references, since generated references were often unreliable
#### Constraints
- The workflow was evaluated only through lesson designers' qualitative judgments, not through experiments measuring tutor learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- novice human tutors teaching middle school mathematics online

### Target Learning Goals
- effective tutoring practices
- high-quality scenario-based lesson content
