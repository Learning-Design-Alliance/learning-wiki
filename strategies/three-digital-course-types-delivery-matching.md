# Categorize digital medical courses as knowledge-, skill-, or problem-based and match each to its delivery format

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a three-way categorization of digital medical courses by learning objective, with each type matched to a delivery mode: "knowledge-oriented courses suited for asynchronous e-learning", skill-based courses in hybrid designs combining online preparation with in-person OSCE practice, and problem-based courses using synchronous interactive discussion platforms. Educators are advised to "align each course with the most suitable delivery format" when planning digital curricula. Stage-specific examples are given: PBL and OSCE-oriented modules for interns, ACLS-based simulations and case-driven synchronous discussions for PGY trainees.
