# Engage trainees in planning the next workshop and post year-round mentoring opportunities

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond the workshop itself, the article describes sustaining engagement by inviting student participants each year to help plan the subsequent BPDM workshop, infusing it with ideas from trainees currently experiencing computer or data science education, and posting additional mentoring opportunities such as conferences, training, internships, and workshops. The authors state these opportunities allow participants to develop additional skills that can help improve their engagement in the discipline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An ongoing annual workshop structure that returning participants can help plan
- a channel for posting conferences, training opportunities, internships, and workshops
