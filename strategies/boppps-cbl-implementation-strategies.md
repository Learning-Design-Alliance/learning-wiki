# Phased implementation, rigorous case development, faculty development, and balanced assessment when adopting BOPPPS-CBL

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend four implementation strategies for educators adopting BOPPPS-CBL in protocol-driven domains: "phased implementation may help students adapt to active learning" via scaffolded cases progressing from simple to complex; rigorous case development with content validity verification; faculty development in facilitative teaching that emphasizes "questioning over telling"; and balanced assessment combining individual accountability with collaborative learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Investment in authentic case development with content validity verification
- Faculty training in facilitative teaching techniques
#### Constraints
- The substantial faculty time required (estimated 8 hours of training plus 9 hours of instruction per cohort) should be weighed against learning gains; no formal cost-effectiveness analysis was conducted

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students
- health professions students in protocol-driven domains

### Target Learning Goals
- infection control competencies
- active learning engagement
