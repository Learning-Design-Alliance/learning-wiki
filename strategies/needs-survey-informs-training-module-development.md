# Use a needs survey of current counselors to tailor the development of training modules on SCI physical activity counseling best practices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Before building training for the SCI physical activity counseling best practices, the authors surveyed counselors in the implementation countries about their knowledge, skills, confidence, barriers and needs. They state insights are needed "to tailor the development of these tools to the context in which they will be implemented". The "descriptive findings ... have been used to tailor the development of training modules on SCI PA counseling to professionals' challenges to improving their knowledge, skills, and confidence levels".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Survey dissemination in partnership with an expert panel and professional organizations to reach the target counseling professions
#### Constraints
- The authors note no validated survey was used because the study explored knowledge, skills, and confidence for the newly developed best practices, and the relatively small sample means results should be approached with caution

### Target Learners
- counselors of adults with spinal cord injury in Canada and the Netherlands

### Target Learning Goals
- improve counselors' knowledge, skills and confidence in SCI physical activity counseling

### Affordances
- Sci Pa Counseling Best Practices Framework
