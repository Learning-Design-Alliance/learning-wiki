# Weekly supervisor meetings with fortnightly thesis updates to keep short-duration research on track

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because research time in a one-year MSc is very limited, supervisors spend one-on-one time with each student weekly, often in the computer laboratory to encourage communication. Students are asked to "build the thesis as they go and send the supervisors an updated version every two weeks". The authors report this keeps students focused and organised and lets supervisors monitor progress on methodology, analysis and literature review.
