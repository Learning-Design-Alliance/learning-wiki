# Use simultaneous remote coaching to reduce regional disparities in surgical education where instructors are scarce

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends remote coaching as a viable option for surgical education in geographically dispersed settings. It argues "The development of this training method could remove bridge geographical gaps in surgical education, especially in local medical settings where instructor shortage is a concern", saving travel time and money, and concludes remote laparoscopic training may be a valuable tool in reducing regional disparities. Standard internet connections and commonly available platforms sufficed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standard internet connection and commonly available online platforms
- An initial investment in training equipment, which can be reused for subsequent suture ligation training
#### Constraints
- Technical barriers have been reported as a disadvantage
- Remote training may not be as precise as on-site training
- Small sample size limits generalizability; consistency across different instructors was not confirmed

### Target Learners
- Surgical residents at rural and remote-island hospitals

### Target Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic surgical skills acquisition despite geographic distance from expert instructors
