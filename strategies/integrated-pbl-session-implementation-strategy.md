# Implement a two-stage integrated PBL process: pre-class preparation, in-class group case discussion, self-study, and next-class group reporting

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's implementable recipe runs: instructors prepare PowerPoint, cases and handouts delivered "at least a week before the class"; students in groups of 8 or 9 preview and clarify unfamiliar concepts; in class they read case materials, discuss within groups led by an appointed group leader, raise, define and analyze problems, identify key issues and set learning goals; instructors give only brief guidance without detailed answers; students then self-study, and each group's speaker reports solutions in the next class before instructor summary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained PBL lecturers with appropriate qualifications and titles
- Small study groups of 8 or 9 students
- Materials delivered at least a week before class
#### Constraints
- The article notes the number of teachers in Chinese medical colleges cannot meet the requirement of implementing PBL teaching for all undergraduates

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- Raising, analyzing and solving problems
- Independent learning
- Connecting basic and clinical medicine knowledge
