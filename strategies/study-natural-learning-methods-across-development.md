# Study natural methods of learning and run experiments to determine how learners pass from beginner's crude efforts to expert performance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instead of teaching expert methods at once, educators should make 'a study of natural methods of learning' and carry out experiments to find what methods best move learners from crude beginner efforts to accurate expert performance. It specifies studying learning processes in adults, in children at different developmental stages, and in animals of different grades of intelligence.
