# Limit flipped classroom video duration to around 20 minutes and use opening quizzes

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article cites Hew's recommendation that "limiting the total duration of video clips in flipped classrooms to around 20 minutes is appropriate, and that using quizzes at the beginning of each class will make the flipped classroom more effective." This strategy addresses the risk that students perceive pre-class videos as a time burden, which can hinder effective participation.
