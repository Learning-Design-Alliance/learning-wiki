# Masterclass workshops in developing countries through collaboration with the African School of Fundamental Physics

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
IMC collaborates with the African School of Fundamental Physics and Applications (ASP) to bring masterclasses to new audiences. When ASP was held in Kigali, Rwanda in August 2016, IMC associates delivered a day-and-a-half teacher workshop and three short student workshops, including short ATLAS and CMS masterclass measurements for schools with little or no technology support. "Thus, approximately 150 students and 20 teachers in Rwanda were exposed to masterclasses and had a chance to work with LHC data." The team also ran a CMS masterclass in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and an ATLAS masterclass for ASP graduate students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership with a local host (ASP, NCRST in Namibia) to organize workshops
- Short masterclass measurements adapted for schools with little or no technology support
#### Constraints
- Some host schools have little or no technology support, requiring shortened measurements teachers can bring to their students

### Target Learners
- high school students and teachers in Rwanda, Ethiopia, and Namibia; graduate students attending ASP

### Target Learning Goals
- working with authentic LHC data
- teacher capacity to run masterclass measurements locally
