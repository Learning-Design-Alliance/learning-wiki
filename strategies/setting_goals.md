# Setting Goals

## Description
Lessons have clear learning intentions with goals that clarify what success looks like. Lesson goals always explain what students need to understand, and what they must be able to do. Having clear learning goals helps the teacher to plan learning activities and it helps students understand what is required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to define clear learning intentions and success criteria before the lesson.
#### Constraints
- Goals must be communicated effectively to students to be beneficial.
