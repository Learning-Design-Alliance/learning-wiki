# Recommend weekly blended MOOC-HFS sessions during internship training, prioritizing procedural topics such as corneal refractive surgery

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on a questionnaire of the 16 trained teachers, the article reports that "Most teachers considered corneal refractive surgery the most suitable topic for blended learning (Fig. 3A) and recommended weekly use during internship training (Fig. 3B)." This strategy directs programs to schedule blended MOOC-HFS sessions weekly during clinical internships and to prioritize operation-oriented topics where simulation adds the most value.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in the blended MOOC-HFS protocol
- Topics suited to simulation, such as corneal refractive surgery
#### Constraints
- Teacher recommendations come from a small, non-blinded sample (n = 16) and should be interpreted with caution

### Target Learners
- Ophthalmology graduate students in optometry clinical internships

### Target Learning Goals
- Procedural skill acquisition in refractive surgery
- Regular reinforcement of theory-practice integration
