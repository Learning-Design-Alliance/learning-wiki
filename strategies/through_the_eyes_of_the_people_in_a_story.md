# Through the Eyes of the People in a Story

## Description
Learners pick a character from a short story or book and relive the main events from that character’s point of view. This encourages consideration of the character’s emotions and thoughts about others and the events around them, promoting a deeper understanding of the story and perspective-taking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A short story or book, a space for individual reflection and group discussion, and time for role-playing or presentation.
#### Constraints
- Learners may struggle to understand the characters' motivations or may impose their own biases on the interpretation. It requires careful guidance from the instructor to ensure accurate and thoughtful analysis.

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- All learners, particularly those who are studying literature and want to deepen their understanding of characters and themes

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->
- Promote perspective-taking, enhance comprehension of literature, encourage emotional intelligence, improve communication skills

### Affordances
- Enhances comprehension of literature by exploring different characters' perspectives. Promotes emotional intelligence by considering characters' emotions and motivations. Improves communication skills through discussion and role-playing.

### Personalization
- Learners can choose characters that resonate with their interests and values. The level of analysis and interpretation can be adjusted to their abilities.
