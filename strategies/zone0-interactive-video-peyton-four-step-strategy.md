# Deliver zone 0 preparatory self-directed learning through an interactive video structured on Peyton's 4-step approach

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's zone 0 session is an implementable recipe: an approximately 30-minute interactive video built in Stornaway.io that follows Peyton's four steps — "Uninterrupted demonstration", "Step-by-step explanation", "Guided decision-making" with branched options and immediate corrective feedback, and "Hands-on practice with feedback" using a QCPR-equipped task trainer — followed by a supervised competency assessment. Learners control pace, decide at critical points, and may repeat the video as many times as desired.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A branched-narrative video platform (Stornaway.io) and a task trainer with automated real-time feedback (Laerdal QCPR)
- An instructor present for technical support and to supervise the competency assessment
#### Constraints
- The study could not disentangle the effect of interactive video from approximately 30 additional minutes of time-on-task

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students without prior CPR training

### Target Learning Goals
- CPR basic life support competence
- procedural skill retention
