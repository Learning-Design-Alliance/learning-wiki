## Description
The article recommends using simulation and human factors training to replicate adverse events "with the explicit aim of extracting learning points and developing recommendations to help prevent future recurrence". Identifying individual or recurrent patterns of adverse events enables remedial training approaches to be integrated into regular simulation programmes to assist dissemination of learning.
