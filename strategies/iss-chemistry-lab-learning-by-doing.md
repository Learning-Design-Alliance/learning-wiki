# Learning by doing through authentic space-science experimentation in the chemistry lab

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The G.B. Grassi high school implemented a learning-by-doing approach (Dewey, 1916) in which students refined pilot projects in the Chemistry Lab at TRL 4, then engineered experimental proposals through PBL & PrBL and the Engineering Design Process. Students chose simple chemical transformations using common substances, noting physical properties and preparing solutions. They annotated observations in forms drawn up under teachers' guidance, developing organization and problem-solving abilities under a constructivist methodology. The strategy connects curricular chemistry and astronomy knowledge to authentic research proposal development for a real space context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A well-equipped chemistry laboratory at school
- A technical tutor with chemistry expertise
- Motivated pupils familiar with the competition regulation
#### Constraints
- The reactions' protracted duration excluded ground-based drop facilities like the Einstein-Elevator
- Projects were limited to low technology readiness level (TRL 4) chemical reactions

### Target Learners
- Last-year secondary school pupils (ages 17-19)

### Target Learning Goals
- Chemistry experimentation skills
- Problem-solving abilities
- Organization skills
- Understanding of microgravity effects on chemical reactions
