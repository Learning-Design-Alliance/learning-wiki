# Establish a dynamic assessment and early intervention mechanism using learning motivation as a key monitoring indicator

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Given the mediating role of learning motivation, the article recommends continuous monitoring rather than solely focusing on burnout outcomes. It proposes brief electronic questionnaires at the mid-point and end of rotations to assess motivation and leadership perceptions, drawing on the Academic Motivation Scale and the Paternalistic Leadership Scale. When motivation declines significantly, educational management should activate an early warning and support system involving timely communication, academic tutoring, and psychological support.
