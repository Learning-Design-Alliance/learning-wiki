# Diversify learners' virtual clinical exposure with alternative virtual learning experiences such as virtual patients and virtual OSCEs

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The commentary recommends diversifying learners' virtual clinical exposure by devising alternative virtual learning experiences. A virtual patient (VP) simulates real-life clinical scenarios in which learners emulate the role of health care providers; its affordances are "active learning in contrast to passive observation, personalised exercises in a nonthreatening environment, and performance assessment with opportunities for reflection and feedback". A virtual OSCE is also proposed as an alternative.

## Design Implications
