# Provide structured, sustained reading intervention across all grade levels, with speed-focused practice for moderate intellectual disability

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend establishing structured and strategic reading intervention for all students irrespective of disability label, sustained throughout schooling rather than limited to primary level. For students with moderate intellectual disabilities, "the focus of their intervention could be on improving reading speed, as those students performed weaker in the rapid reading test." In secondary school, structured intensive reading instruction can be combined with more complex literacy instruction such as writing, comprehension, and reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evidence-based instructional materials and clearly defined learning objectives with direct instruction
- Early identification of reading difficulties to enable early preventive support
#### Constraints
- Students with intellectual disabilities often do not receive frequent, intensive and comprehensive reading instruction, especially in segregated special education schools or self-contained classes

### Target Learners
- students with intellectual disabilities
- students with special educational needs
- students without special educational needs

### Target Learning Goals
- reading fluency and speed
- vocabulary
- sentence and text comprehension
