# Policymakers should choose between the full roster and full roster-plus methods for co-teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper advises policymakers facing co-teaching in value-added systems to rely on the full roster method or the full roster-plus method, since the first two options are impractical and the last two are empirically similar. As the paper frames it, "Policymakers have four options for addressing co-teaching in a value-added model," of which the last two are the workable choices. This gives model developers a concrete decision rule when configuring teacher accountability systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A value-added model that must handle jointly taught classrooms
#### Constraints
- Applies to value-added accountability contexts; the two preferred methods are empirically similar, so the paper does not rank them
