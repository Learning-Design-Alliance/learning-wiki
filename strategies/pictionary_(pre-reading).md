# Pictionary (Pre-Reading)

## Description
Key words from the text are selected, and the class is divided into two or three groups. A learner from each group comes to the whiteboard and draws the word without using letters or numbers. Their teammates try to guess the word. This activity makes learning fun, reinforces vocabulary, and activates prior knowledge through visual representation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a whiteboard, markers, and pre-selected key words from the text.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may be hesitant to draw. Effectiveness depends on the relevance and difficulty of the words.
