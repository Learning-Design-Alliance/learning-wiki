# Assess and boost trainee motivation before surgical training sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends assessing motivation before training and actively increasing it beforehand. As printed: "This might include the assessment of motivation by the use of appropriate methods before training sessions or even efforts to increase motivation before training courses, such as the stimulation of curiosity, prospects of reward, or setting individual goals for the attendees". The authors also suggest emphasizing the value of the educational activity and letting trainees select their own strategies, especially if these enhance motivation for the training itself.
