# Rotate learners through junior and senior interprofessional roles across simulation scenarios

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each simulation team of 5-7 learners fills primary nurse, charge nurse, otolaryngology primary call provider, and chief resident roles, with additional participants in structured observer roles. Learners "rotated through junior and senior roles across sessions, allowing them to observe the scenario from different vantage points" and practice communicating across levels of responsibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios run concurrently in separate rooms, each staffed by 2 trained facilitators, with a 15-minute debrief after each scenario

- Practicing communication across levels of responsibility
