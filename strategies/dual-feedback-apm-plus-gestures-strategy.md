# Provide trainees with dual feedback from both APM and surgical gestures to cover different aspects of technical and procedural skills

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend combining the two data types for trainee feedback: "Another way could be to provide the trainees with feedback from both APM and surgical gestures." They argue trainees "could benefit from dual feedback which covers different aspects of technical skills and procedural skills", since APM give cumulative movement-based scores while gestures describe the pattern of actions throughout the procedure.
