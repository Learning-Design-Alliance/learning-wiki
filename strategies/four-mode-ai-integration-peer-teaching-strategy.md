# Integrate AI into structured peer teaching in four modes with training and quality safeguards

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a specific implementation recipe: AI was integrated into peer teaching in four modes — "(1) Presession preparation", "(2) During sessions", "(3) Case-based learning", and "(4) Postsession" self-directed study. All participants received a 2-hour training session on effective AI use, "including prompt formulation, critical evaluation of AI outputs, and recognition of AI limitations". Faculty review of AI interactions was conducted weekly, with approximately 10% of interaction logs sampled for factual accuracy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 2-hour training session on effective AI use
- weekly faculty review of AI interaction logs for factual accuracy
#### Constraints
- No custom fine-tuning, institutional configuration, or API integration was applied; the model was used in its publicly available standard configuration

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- peer teachers (senior students)

### Target Learning Goals
- orthopedic clinical knowledge
- clinical reasoning
- case-based discussion
