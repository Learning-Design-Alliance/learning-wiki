# Integrate ChatGPT into hands-on skills training through pre-practice preparation, in-session tablet consultation, and post-practice reflection, capped at about 5 minutes per 30-minute session

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This strategy structures AI assistance around, rather than instead of, hands-on practice. Trainees used ChatGPT for pre-practice preparation reviewing anatomy and procedural steps, consulted a dedicated tablet with ChatGPT at each training station during practical sessions for immediate clarification, and engaged post-practice to analyze performance and identify improvements. "To maintain an optimal balance between AI assistance and practical training, ChatGPT interactions were limited to an average of 5 min per 30-minute practice session." A faculty-curated repository of over 100 common trainee questions equipped ChatGPT to answer queries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 15-question diagnostic quiz to identify individual knowledge gaps for personalized content generation, a dedicated tablet at each training station, and a faculty-curated repository of over 100 common trainee questions
#### Constraints
- ChatGPT interactions were limited to an average of 5 min per 30-minute practice session to avoid compromising hands-on practice time

### Target Learners
- Refresher doctors in a 12-week CT-guided puncture skills training program

### Target Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge of anatomy and puncture techniques, real-time procedural problem-solving, and self-directed performance improvement
