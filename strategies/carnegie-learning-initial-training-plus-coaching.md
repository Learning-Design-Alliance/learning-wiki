# Provide initial training plus multi-year in-classroom coaching when implementing Carnegie Learning Algebra I

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Implementation of the curriculum calls for professional development beyond initial training. "At minimum, teachers were expected to receive three days of initial training", and the program "recommends four or more days per teacher of in-classroom support and instructional coaching in at least the first two years of implementation." Training amounts varied widely in the large-scale effectiveness study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At minimum three days of initial training per teacher.
#### Constraints
- The amount of training and professional development teachers received varied widely during the RAND Corporation large-scale effectiveness study.

### Target Learners
- High school teachers implementing the curriculum

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective implementation of the blended algebra curriculum
