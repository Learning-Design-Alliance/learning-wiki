# Revise an online study group based on participant feedback: consolidate groups, drop low-rated methods, and provide references in advance

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
After the first OSG cohort, the authors implemented feedback-driven changes for the second cohort (November 2013 to August 2014): consolidating participants into one large study group, dropping the less popular journal club, adding facilitators, making references more accessible and manageable in length, and providing reference lists two weeks in advance. This is a concrete recipe for iterating an online CPD design based on evaluation data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluation data (survey ratings and open-ended feedback) from a prior cohort to identify what to change
#### Constraints
- The article reports these changes were made but further evaluation is needed to ascertain whether they improve perceived or actual knowledge

### Target Learners
- Practicing psychiatrists
- Geriatric psychiatry residents

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved engagement and interactivity in online CPD
- Exam preparation and knowledge consolidation
